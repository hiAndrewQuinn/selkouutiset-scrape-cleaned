## Spot electricity

![Electricity pylons in a snowy landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11201922)

Open image viewer

Spot electricity does not have a fixed price. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Energy Authority predicts that there will be more spot electricity users. About 300,000 Finns used spot electricity at the turn of the year.

Spot electricity is an electricity contract where the price of electricity changes every hour according to the market price on the electricity exchange.

A fixed-term electricity contract ends, or has ended, this year for about 800,000 customers. After that there is hardly any alternative to spot electricity.

There are queues at electricity companies' customer service. People are now asking a lot about contracts, prices, billing and saving electricity.

## Homelessness

![Markku Korhonen's legs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10410853)

Open image viewer

The corona time made the situation of homeless people worse. Photo: Mari Jäntti / Yle

The corona time has increased the number of homeless people, says the Diakonissalaitos. Because of corona restrictions, many services for homeless people were closed.

There are currently about 3,900 people without a home in Finland. 18 percent of them are under 25 years old.

Today, Monday, is Homeless Night. Events are being organised in different parts of Finland.

## Agriculture studies

![grain, agriculture](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3076,x_0,y_572/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-6002157b1ef25e9a52/1471278951)

Open image viewer

Fewer people are studying in the agriculture field than before. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

Agriculture studies interest fewer people than before.

In 2015 there were almost 1,100 primary applicants for vocational training in the field. This year there were fewer than 750 applicants.

One reason is the shrinking of the age groups.

The field fears that the reputation of agriculture may also reduce interest in the field. Students in the field think that the news says too many negative things about agriculture.

## Zsar bankruptcy

![Empty shopping street at Zsar Outlet Village in Vaalimaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5068,h_2851,x_0,y_261/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6435255e4a7e26e123f/1581941464)

Open image viewer

The Zsar shopping street has been almost empty since Russia attacked Ukraine and tourism from Russia to Finland decreased. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

The Zsar outlet village, located on the eastern border, has filed for bankruptcy. The shopping village is near the Vaalimaa border checkpoint in Kymenlaakso.

The outlet village opened in 2018. It attracted mainly Russian tourists to shop there.

The business has been loss-making from the start.

## Swedish Prime Minister

![Ulf Kristersson stands in front of a speaker's podium and smiles. A blue wall can be seen behind him.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7353,h_4128,x_0,y_172/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-101985963468fa82c788/1665568792)

Open image viewer

Sweden's new Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson is the leader of the Moderate Party. Photo: Jonas Ekströmer / EPA

In Sweden the era of the Social Democrats ends after 8 years.

Sweden's new Prime Minister is the leader of the Moderate Party, Ulf Kristersson.

The Swedish Riksdag, that is, the parliament, voted on the matter on Monday.

Kristersson announced on Friday that 4 centre-right parties have agreed on government cooperation. In addition to the Moderate Party, the government includes the Christian Democrats and the Liberals.

The Sweden Democrats, the election winner, are left outside the government.

Sweden's new government starts work on Tuesday.
