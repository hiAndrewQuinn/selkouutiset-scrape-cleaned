## Strengthening the eastern border

![At the Salla border station, a sign reminds people in several languages that access to the border zone without permission is prohibited.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5666,h_3187,x_0,y_127/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-954841627e6598839f0/1652450801)

Finland wants to strengthen its eastern border. A Yle survey says that many MPs want better border fences on the border between Finland and Russia.

Interior Minister Krista Mikkonen says that Finland has already decided to strengthen border control. For example, the number of border guards will be increased. In addition, Finland is buying surveillance aircraft.

Finland wants to strengthen its eastern border, so that Russia cannot disturb Finland with the help of migrants.

## Charger

![USB-C cable.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2069,h_1164,x_477,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-966284629f2862b22a6/1664881076)

Open image viewer

USB-C charger connector. Photo: Jakub Porzycki / AOP

All small devices in the home can soon be charged with the same charger.

In the European Union area, all devices will get a USB-C connector. The same charger must fit, for example, phones, tablets, digital cameras and headphones.

This is meant to reduce electronic waste. Less waste is created when people do not need to buy a separate charger for every device.

The European Parliament and the European Council must still approve the reform. After that, device manufacturers will have 2 years to carry out the reform.

## Animals and the elderly

![The rooster of the summer henhouse at the Kullero service home walks around the yard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4680,h_2632,x_0,y_126/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9644526298b3f343a21/1654175104)

Open image viewer

Photo: Juuso Stoor / Yle

Animals bring joy to the elderly. That is why animals are nowadays welcome in many places where elderly people are cared for.

For example, the elderly care home Kullero has a flock of hens all summer. The service home Kullero is in Tervola, in Northern Finland.

Many residents of the Kullero service home remember hens from their own childhood. The hens bring dear memories to mind. That is why many elderly people get to hold them in their laps.

Raisa Viik of elderly care in Tervola says that animals have many good health effects. They bring good cheer to the elderly and reduce depression.

Also the functional ability of the elderly improves when they potter about with animals, Raisa Viik says. At the Kullero service home the elderly look after the hens and collect the eggs every day.
