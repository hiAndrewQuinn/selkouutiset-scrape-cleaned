Prime Minister Petteri Orpo says that Europe must still support Ukraine a lot, which is fighting a defensive war against Russia. Orpo also said on Yle's Prime Minister's interview hour that Europe must have its own strong defence. In Orpo's view, Europe cannot wait for the USA to take care of Europe's defence.

Prime Minister Orpo said that Russia is not a threat to Finland right now. However, Russia's actions are difficult to guess in advance, said Prime Minister Petteri Orpo.

## Fire in a Spanish nightclub

![Map of Spain showing the city of Murcia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1080,h_607,x_0,y_311/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117965865195f6900345/1696161736)

Open image viewer

Map of Spain showing the city of Murcia. Photo: Laura Merikalla / Yle

In Spain at least 13 people died when a fire broke out in a nightclub. The accident happened in the city of Murcia in south-east Spain. 4 people were injured in the fire.

An alarm about the fire came at about 6 o'clock local time. However, rescuers only got into the building many hours later.

The mayor of Murcia declared a 3-day period of mourning in the city.

Helsinki-Vantaa airport security screening staff will walk out of their workplace next Thursday. The walk-out will interrupt security screening for half an hour on Thursday afternoon. The purpose of the walk-out is to show opposition to the policy of the Finnish government.

The trade union Pro and the Industrial Union are also organising walk-outs next week.

## Miina Sillanpää

![The Finnish flag flutters on a flagpole.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4154,h_2337,x_0,y_214/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4482345a27ae2854b8e/1512550066)

Open image viewer

Finnish flag. Illustrative image. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

Today, Sunday, is a flag day, that is, the Finnish flag was raised on flagpoles at offices and in house yards. On Sunday it was flown in honour of Miina Sillanpää. At the same time, respect was shown for civic participation.

The Electoral Act came into force in Finland on 1 October 1906. Both men and women got the same political rights in Finland.

Miina Sillanpää was elected as an MP in 1907. Miina Sillanpää was later Finland's first female minister.
