# Sunday 11.12.2022 (radio)

Housing costs. Finland's NATO membership. Ukraine. Moon exploration. Weather forecast.

## Rising housing costs

![Two apartment blocks in sunny winter weather.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5557,h_3126,x_0,y_182/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-763480600593542fdc7/1610984572)

Housing costs are rising, because many things are getting more expensive. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

The rise in prices is also raising housing costs in right-of-occupancy flats and city rental flats.

Many things have become much more expensive. For example, heating, repair work and loan interest rates have become much more expensive. That is why housing costs will rise a lot next year, also in many right-of-occupancy flats and city rental flats.

In right-of-occupancy flats, costs are evened out between different buildings. That is why fees also rise in many new right-of-occupancy buildings.

## President Niinistö answered people's questions

![President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö answers the audience's questions at the Yle Radio 1 presidential question time on Sunday, 11 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5206,h_2928,x_0,y_220/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10465176395c0c62744c/1670758809)

Open image viewer

President Niinistö answered people's questions on the radio. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

President Sauli Niinistö answered people's questions on Sunday in a live Yle radio programme. People asked a lot about Ukraine, Russia and NATO.

President Niinistö said that he is sure that Finland will become a NATO member, even though Hungary and Turkey are delaying the approval of Finland into NATO.

Niinistö was also asked about the president's contacts with Russia. Niinistö said that there has been no reason to talk with the Russian president since May.

President Niinistö estimated that it will take a long time before relations between Finland and Russia return.

The presidential question time can be listened to on Yle Areena.

## Russian attacks on Ukraine

![Workers repaired power lines destroyed in a Russian missile strike in Odesa on 7 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3900,h_2193,x_0,y_375/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-104650063959ca4cc6c1/1670749473)

Open image viewer

Russia is destroying Ukraine's electricity network with attacks. Photo: Oleksandr Gimanov / AFP

Russia has continued attacks on Ukraine's electricity network. For example, the city of Odesa is almost completely without electricity. That is why the situation in the city is difficult.

Russia's new attacks have destroyed 2 important power plants in Odesa.

Ukraine says that Russia's attacks on the electricity network are a war crime, because the attacks destroy civilians' chance of a normal life.

## Exploring the Moon

![SpaceX's rocket carried the Dragon spacecraft into Earth orbit on 15 September 2021, where it spent three days.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_27/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-85560161468565476e1/1632011914)

Open image viewer

A Falcon 9 rocket carries the research equipment. Photo: EPA/Inspiration4/John Kraus

Researchers have sent new research equipment to the Moon. The equipment is carried by a rocket that left from the Cape Canaveral Space Centre in the USA.

The rocket was sent by the Japanese company ispace. This is the first time that a private company has sent research equipment to the Moon.

The rocket takes a capsule near the Moon, which will land on the Moon. The capsule has a robot that studies the surface of the Moon. The capsule also has a rover that can move on the surface of the Moon.

## The weather

![Monday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12684277)

Open image viewer

On Monday evening a snowfall area will reach Finland.

On Monday in the north and in Ostrobothnia it is clear during the day, so the sun can shine.

In the southern and central parts of the country snow starts to fall in the evening and during the night. A lot of snow will fall, more than 10 centimetres. The wind is strong and the wind piles the snow into drifts.

The temperature is between -5 and -15 degrees, and in the northern part of the country between -15 and -30 degrees. The wind makes it feel colder.
