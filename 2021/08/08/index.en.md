The number of corona infections is growing.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare said on Sunday that there are over 1,300 new corona infections. The figure includes infections from the whole weekend.

There are 5 new corona deaths. In total, 991 people have died in Finland because of corona.

There are 100 corona patients in hospital care.

## Mecca's pilgrims

![In the picture are men in white robes bending down.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11877762)

Open image viewer

Corona safety distances have been observed in Mecca. Photo: AOP

Saudi Arabia is again letting pilgrims in from abroad into Mecca. Because of corona, Saudi Arabia's borders have been closed for over a year.

Pilgrims may again go to Mecca if they have been vaccinated against corona. Pilgrims must also first be in quarantine, that is, they cannot join the crowd at the start of the journey.

Mecca is the holy city of Muslims, and many pilgrims visited it before corona.

## Electric scooter accidents

![Two red electric scooters.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_95/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5751875d1ca7123a17f/1562158973)

Open image viewer

An electric scooter driver must follow the traffic rules for cyclists. Photo: Jasmine Nedergård/Yle

In Finland electric scooters are being talked about again.

Doctors in the Helsinki and Uusimaa region hope that electric scooters will be banned from traffic at night at the weekend.

Most electric scooter accidents happen at night. In addition, the driver often injures themselves badly if they are drunk. That is why doctors hope that electric scooters are not used on weekend nights.

The police remind that an electric scooter may not be ridden on the pavement. Driving while drunk is also banned.

In addition, it is good for an electric scooter driver to wear a helmet. A driver may not take anyone on board. Electric scooters are ridden alone, the police remind.

## End of the Olympics

![Mira Potkonen smiles in the ring.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3457,h_1945,x_0,y_108/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8381316109059914c54/1627981272)

Open image viewer

Boxer Mira Potkonen did well at the Tokyo Olympics. Photo: Lehtikuva

The Tokyo Olympics have ended.

The closing ceremony of the Olympics was held on Sunday. Boxer Mira Potkonen carried the Finnish flag at the closing ceremony.

In Tokyo Potkonen won a bronze medal in women's boxing. The 40-year-old Potkonen ended her sports career at the Tokyo Olympics.

Finland won 2 medals in Tokyo in total. Swimmer Matti Mattsson also won a bronze medal. Mattsson was 3rd in the men's 200 metre breaststroke.
