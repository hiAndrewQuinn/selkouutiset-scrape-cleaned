# Tuesday 23.10.2018 (radio)

Finns have less debt than people in many other countries. There is a dispute about harassment at the Ministry for Foreign Affairs. The Ministry for Foreign Affairs will start investigating harassment. The world's longest bridge has been opened in China. Animal videos are good for people.

## Household debts are reasonable

![household debt graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10470063)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics

In Finland there has been a lot of talk about people having a lot of debt. But there is less debt than in many other countries.

Economist Lauri Uotila says that the amount of debt is reasonable. Compared to income, there is more debt than ever, but the cost of paying off the debt is small. Loan interest rates are low. In addition, loan periods are much longer than, for example, in the 90s.

The expert says that Finns must still learn to save. Many do not save any money for a rainy day.

## There has been harassment at the Ministry for Foreign Affairs

![Ministry for Foreign Affairs](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10469675)

Open image viewer

The Ministry for Foreign Affairs in Helsinki. Photo: Jyrki Valkama / Yle

There is a dispute about harassment at the Ministry for Foreign Affairs. Employees say that the employer does not do enough about cases of harassment.

There has been harassment at the ministry and at missions abroad. The employees' organisation says that not all cases have been handled properly. The chief shop steward says that the ministry does, however, have internal guidelines for investigating.

Most of the harassment at the Ministry for Foreign Affairs is inappropriate behaviour. There is also sexual harassment.The civil servants' organisation JUKO did a survey in the spring. It revealed about 50 reports of harassment.

## The Ministry for Foreign Affairs launches an independent investigation into harassment

![Anne-Mari Virolainen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10394422)

Open image viewer

Minister Anne-Mari Virolainen. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The Ministry for Foreign Affairs will start investigating harassment. Foreign Minister Timo Soini and Minister for Foreign Trade and Development Anne-Mari Virolainen announced it during the day.

The Ministry for Foreign Affairs will carry out an independent review of how the ministry handles harassment cases.

The ministers say that it is important to investigate the matter to protect the legal rights of the parties.

## The world's longest bridge was opened in China

![Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau bridge](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10465261)

Open image viewer

The bridge was opened a couple of years late. Photo: Chen Xianyao / VCG / Getty Images

The world's longest sea bridge has been opened in China.

The main bridge is 22.9 kilometres long. In addition, part of the route goes under the sea, and the bridge connects 2 artificial islands.

The bridge connects Hong Kong and Macau and the city of Zhuhai in mainland China. At the same time, it shows how the Chinese government wants to tie the special regions ever more strongly to mainland China.

The bridge is designed to withstand earthquakes, super typhoons and collisions with big ships.

## Animal videos are good for people

![An alpaca's head.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10464128)

Open image viewer

The alpaca is especially effective at relieving stress. Photo: Unsplash

Watching animal videos is healthy.

Brain researcher Heini Saarimäki says that animal videos relax the viewer's brain. The viewer's stress decreases and joy increases. Saarimäki says that positive moments affect how the whole brain works. After watching an animal video, a person learns better and works more efficiently.

People also help other people more after they have watched animal videos.
