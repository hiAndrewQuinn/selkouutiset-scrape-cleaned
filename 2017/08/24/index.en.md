# Thursday 24.8.2017 (radio)

Police and refugees clashed in Italy. Belgian authorities warned about the leader of the Barcelona terrorist attack. The NBI has arrested more people because of the Turku stabbings. Finns are keeping to speed limits better than before. School peace was declared in Helsinki.

## Police and refugees clashed in Rome

![Police clear Piazza dell&#39;Indipendenza square in Rome on 24 August 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9796047)

Police clear a square in Rome. Photo: Angelo Carconi / EPA

Italian police and a group of refugees have clashed in the centre of Rome.

The police tried to evict the refugees from a square where they were living without permission.

The refugees threw stones and bottles at the police. The police used water cannons.

The square is near Rome's main railway station, Termini.

The roughly 100 refugees have come from Eritrea and Ethiopia. Most of them have been granted asylum in Italy.

## Belgium warned about the leader of the Barcelona attacks

![A police operation in Ripoll on 18 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9786581)

Open image viewer

Police patrol in Ripoll. Photo: Robin Townsend / EPA

Already a year ago, the Belgian authorities warned Catalonia about the imam who is considered the leader of the Barcelona terrorist attacks.

The imam worked at the mosque in the town of Ripoll in Catalonia. Catalonia is a large region in northern Spain, where Barcelona is also located.

The imam had tried to get work at a Belgian mosque. However, he was considered too radical and unqualified.

The Catalan authorities did not tell the Spanish authorities about Belgium's warning.

17\. August: in the terrorist attack carried out in Barcelona, 15 people died and over 100 were injured.

## NBI: More arrests over the Turku stabbings

![Memorial candles at the Turku market square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9796046)

Open image viewer

The Turku stabber was radicalised quickly, says the National Bureau of Investigation.  Photo: Jaakko Stenroos / AOP

The National Bureau of Investigation says that it has arrested more people who are suspected of being linked to the Turku stabbing case.

One of the people arrested has been released. The other has been detained.

At the moment, four people are being detained because of the Turku stabbings.

The main suspect is suspected of two murders committed with terrorist intent.

The National Bureau of Investigation says that the Moroccan man had become radicalised only in August. The man's behaviour and way of dressing had changed quickly.

## Finns are keeping to speed limits better and better

![A speed camera monitors speeds.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9720373)

Open image viewer

All speed cameras are now in use. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finns are keeping to speed limits better than before.

There are 120 automatic speed cameras in Finland. They were all switched on on Wednesday morning.

In one day, the cameras detected speeding 4,000 times. The number is 20 per cent lower than a year earlier.

The police say that all the automatic speed cameras will stay switched on for the time being.

## School peace was declared in Helsinki

![Isac Elliot performs in Kaisaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9795182)

Open image viewer

Isac Elliot said that he too has sometimes been bullied.  Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

School peace has been declared in Finland today.

School peace was declared in Helsinki in several different languages. There were many schoolchildren at the event, and the main performer at the event was Isac Elliot.

School peace aims to prevent bullying. The declaration of school peace encourages pupils to be good friends to others.

This year the theme of school peace is the right to be and to dream.
