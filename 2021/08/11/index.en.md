Budget negotiations. Corona vaccinations for lower secondary school pupils. Corona measures at workplaces. Popularity of ready meals. The weather.

## Budget negotiations

![Centre Party chairman, Minister of Finance Annika Saarikko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83045260e836e9638b2/1625831623)

Finance Minister Annika Saarikko leads the budget negotiations. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

Negotiations on the state budget have started. The budget tells how the state uses money.

The budget proposal for next year is made at the Ministry of Finance. Many people are interested in how the budget proposal shows, for example, measures against unemployment and climate change.

Minister of Finance Annika Saarikko will present the budget proposal later this week.

The whole government will negotiate about the budget in September. After that, Parliament still has to approve the budget.

## Corona infections

![A nurse takes a nose-and-throat swab at the Mehiläinen drive-in testing station in Helsinki on 2 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_320/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82808860df14bbd7050/1625561394)

Open image viewer

A man in a corona test. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The number of corona infections is growing.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare said on Wednesday that there are 844 new corona infections in Finland.

The number of corona deaths is also growing. In total, 995 people have now died in Finland because of corona. That is 11 more than a week ago.

## Corona vaccinations

![A young person gets a corona vaccine at the vaccination point at Energia Areena in Vantaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12051522)

Open image viewer

Photo: Seppo Samuli / Lehtikuva

Corona vaccinations for lower secondary school pupils are starting at schools. Work at schools has started in many municipalities on Wednesday.

For example, in lower secondary schools in Vantaa corona vaccinations were started straight away, and in the coming days corona vaccinations for lower secondary school pupils will also start in Helsinki and Espoo.

Lower secondary school pupils, that is, 12–15-year-olds, can usually decide for themselves whether they take a corona vaccination.

## Corona measures

![Two women greet each other at an office with an elbow greeting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-779790603d06e060179/1614612490)

Open image viewer

It is still good to wear a mask at the workplace. Photo: Tiina Kokko / Yle

It is good to continue corona measures at workplaces. This is what the Finnish Institute of Occupational Health says.

At workplaces one must remember that not everyone has yet received a second corona vaccination. That is why, for example, it is good to continue remote work.

If remote work is not possible, at the workplace one must protect oneself against corona. Employees may, for example, continue to use face masks.

## Ready meals

![Ready meals](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10566504)

Open image viewer

Ready meals are now being sold a lot. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

Ready meals are now popular. This is what the retail sector's organisation says.

Ready meals were sold 20 percent more in April, May and June than a year ago.

At the same time as Finns buy more ready meals, they make less food themselves. For example, sales of potato crisps have risen, but sales of potatoes have fallen.
