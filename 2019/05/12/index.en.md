# Sunday 12.5.2019 (radio)

Finland celebrated mothers. Ohisalo is the only candidate for leader of the Greens. Police are again investigating the activities of neo-Nazis. A thunderstorm raged in North Karelia.

## Mother's Day was celebrated in Finland

![Awarded mother Helena Nordberg and President Sauli Niinistö talked after the group photo at the Mannerheim League for Child Welfare's national Mother's Day celebration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10779462)

Finland has once again celebrated mothers. Mother's Day in Finland is held every year on the second Sunday of May. A big Mother's Day celebration was held in Helsinki, and President Sauli Niinistö was also there. At the celebration, 35 mothers received a medal of honour.

The medal recipients have raised children, for example on their own. They have been single parents. Foster mothers were also honoured. They have acted as mothers to children whose own parents have not been able to look after them.

In Finland, Mother's Day is celebrated, for example, by eating together. Some mothers also get breakfast and presents in bed.

## Maria Ohisalo the only Green leadership candidate

![Maria Ohisalo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10779314)

Open image viewer

Maria Ohisalo is the only candidate for the new leader of the Greens. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

MP Maria Ohisalo is very likely to become the new leader of the Greens. The Green party announced today that Ohisalo is the only candidate who has signed up for the Greens' leadership race. The deadline for candidates to sign up ended yesterday, Saturday.

The party now has an interim leader, Pekka Haavisto. The Greens' new leader will be chosen at the party congress in Pori in mid-June.

Maria Ohisalo is from Helsinki. She is 34 years old.

## Police again investigate neo-Nazis

![A scarf with the logo of the Nordic Resistance Movement at a demonstration in Tampere on 21.10.2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10534187)

Open image viewer

The logo of the Nordic Resistance Movement on a neck scarf. Photo: Yle

The police are starting again to investigate the activities of neo-Nazis. Helsingin Sanomat reports that the authorities are starting a criminal investigation into the neo-Nazi organisation the Nordic Resistance Movement.

The authorities suspect that the movement is continuing its activities, even though its activities have now been banned. However, the police suspect that the movement has published material on a website.

Last autumn the court decided that the activities of the Nordic Resistance Movement are not legal. The decision will still be heard in Finland's Supreme Court.

The National Police Board wanted to ban the activities, because the resistance movement spreads, for example, hate speech about immigrants, sexual minorities and Jews.

## Thunderstorm raged in North Karelia

![Lightning in the sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10516204)

Open image viewer

Thunder rumbles and the sky flashes with lightning in Finland, especially in summer. Photo: Keijo Salokangas / Yle

There have been severe thunderstorms in North Karelia, in eastern Finland. A thunderstorm on Saturday evening cut the power for thousands of people when trees fell onto power lines.

In the violent storm, many trees also fell onto roads and railway tracks. The North Karelia rescue service had dozens of jobs on Saturday evening because of the violent thunderstorm.
