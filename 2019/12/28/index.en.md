# Saturday 28.12.2019 (radio)

A devastating bomb attack has taken place in Mogadishu. NASA has unveiled a new Mars rover. The heat is still troubling Australia. The weather in Finland is cloudy.

## Bomb attack in Mogadishu

![Photo from the accident site in Mogadishu, where a devastating car bomb exploded on 28 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11136501)

A devastating car bomb exploded in Mogadishu. Photo: Abdirazak Hussein Farah / AFP

A bomb attack has been carried out in Mogadishu, the capital of Somalia.

Dozens of people have been killed and injured. At least 90 people have died. Many of the dead are students.

The car bomb exploded during the morning rush hour at a busy junction. There were many people there.

The perpetrator of the attack is not known. Many bomb attacks take place in Mogadishu. The terrorist group al-Shabaab has carried out a large part of the attacks.

## Mars rover

![Jessica Samuels explained the mission of the Mars 2020 rover to the media during a tour of NASA's Pasadena laboratories in California on 27 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11136372)

Jessica Samuels explained the mission of the Mars 2020 rover to the media during a tour of NASA's Pasadena laboratories in California on 27 December. Photo: Robyn Beck / AFP

The US space agency NASA has unveiled a new rover. The rover will set off for the planet Mars perhaps next July. The journey to Mars takes about 7 months.

The rover is the size of a car. It has 6 wheels and, for example, 23 cameras. In one day the rover can move about 200 metres.

The purpose of the rover is to study Mars. It will look for signs of life on the surface of Mars. The rover collects rocks from Mars, for example.

NASA hopes that in the future it can send people to Mars. The rover has equipment on board, for example, that can make oxygen.

## Heat in Australia

![Burned house and car](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11132803)

Open image viewer

House destroyed by fire in Balmoral, New South Wales, on 23 December. Mick Tsikas / EPA Photo: Mick Tsikas / EPA

The oppressive heat continues in Australia.

In the state of South Australia it has been over 40 degrees. In Melbourne, for example, it may be even hotter next week.

In the east of Australia there have been severe bush fires. The fires have destroyed land in an area bigger than the Northern Ostrobothnia region in Finland. At least 8 people have died.

Australia's animals are also suffering because of the heat. Koalas, for example, are suffering. Up to 30 per cent of koalas in the northern parts of New South Wales may have died in the bush fires.

## Finland's weather

![Cars driving on bad winter roads in Hämeenkyrö on 23 December 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11131610)

The weather in Finland is cloudy. In Southern and Central Finland it is dry, and in the north a little snow will fall on Sunday morning.

The wind is quite strong, and there are storms in the Lapland fells.

Driving conditions turn poor in the east and north because of snowfall.

Next week, as the year changes, the weather will be a little colder. Sometimes the sun will shine, but at other times a little rain or snow will fall too.
