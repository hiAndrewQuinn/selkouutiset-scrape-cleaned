# Sunday 14.7.2019 (radio)

9 people have died in a plane crash in Sweden. Cool weather has led to more last-minute departures to the south. A person with a spinal cord injury may be able to walk again with a new implant. There has been a wide power cut in Manhattan. The royal couple of the tango market have been chosen.

## A small plane crashed in Sweden

![Map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10877164)

Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

A serious plane crash has happened in Sweden near Umeå. The small plane was carrying 9 people. Everyone died in the accident.

The plane fell during the climb after take-off and dived into the ground.

There were parachutists on the plane. In the late afternoon there was still no information about the cause of the accident.

## Cool weather sends Finns on holiday to the south

![On the busiest day of June, Helsinki-Vantaa had 74,000 air passengers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10876817)

The summer has been a record-busy one at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. In June more than 2 million passengers moved through the airport.

Cool weather has increased last-minute departures to holiday destinations in the south. Travel agencies say that only scattered places are left for the end of July.

In many travel agencies sales picked up only after midsummer. Last summer's heatwave made Finns hope that the weather in Finland would be good this year too. Now, however, it has been cool.

## A person with a spinal cord injury may regain the ability to move

![David Mzee can now walk even without the electrical stimulation given by the implant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10876949)

David Mzee can now walk even without the electrical stimulation given by the implant. Photo: Hillary Sanctuary / EPFL

A person with a spinal cord injury may regain the ability to move with a new implant.

In Switzerland a device has been developed that has helped patients walk again. The device sends an electrical impulse that controls the muscles of the leg.

Wheelchair patients have been able to walk after rehabilitation. Already 8 patients have received the implant, which is connected to the spinal cord.

The research team is also developing the implant for Parkinson's disease. The device has been made to work in animal tests.

## There was a wide power cut in Manhattan

![Manhattan, power cut](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10876926)

Open image viewer

Manhattan in New York during the power cut. Photo: JASON SZENES/EPA

In the United States there has been a wide power cut in Manhattan, New York. The power cut began on Saturday evening and lasted about 5 hours. As many as over 70,000 customers were without electricity.

Broadway shows and concerts had to be stopped. In restaurants, light came from mobile phone lamps. The metro stopped running, and taxi traffic came to a halt. The National Guard was asked to direct traffic.

The cause of the outage is being investigated. The cause may have been a transformer fire.

## The tango royalty has been chosen again

![Pirita Niemenmaa (right) was crowned the new tango queen and Johannes Vatjus the new tango king in the final at the Seinäjoki Tango Market on 13 July](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10876872)

Open image viewer

Pirita Niemenmaa (right) was crowned the new tango queen and Johannes Vatjus the new tango king in the final at the Seinäjoki Tango Market. Photo: Timo Aalto / Lehtikuva

The royalty of this year's Seinäjoki Tango Market has been chosen.

The new tango queen is 34-year-old Pirita Niemenmaa from Seinäjoki. The new tango king is Johannes Vatjus. The 38-year-old Vatjus comes from Haapavesi.

There were 6 performers in the final. In total more than 400 singers took part in the competition.

More than 100,000 friends of tango visited the Tango Market this year.
