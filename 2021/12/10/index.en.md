Finland's military aircraft. Corona infections. The Nobel Peace Prize. Afghan refugees. Weather.

## Finland's military aircraft

![f35 features](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12225234)

Open image viewer

Finland is buying F-35 military aircraft. Photo: AOP/Stella Pictures

The government has decided which aircraft will be bought for the Finnish army.

Finland is buying American F-35 aircraft.

Finland considered the matter for a long time and carefully, because the deal is big.

Finland is buying 64 military aircraft, and they cost about 10 billion euros.

The deal also brings jobs to Finland, because parts of the aircraft are also made in Finland.

The number of corona infections is growing. On Friday it was reported that there are almost 1800 new corona infections. That is more than ever before in one day.

The total number of corona infections in Finland is already more than 200 000.

On Friday it was also reported that there are 15 new corona deaths. In total, 1421 people have died of corona in Finland.

## The Nobel Peace Prizes

![Both winners of the Nobel Peace Prize, Maria Ressa and Dmitri Muratov, face photo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5670,h_3189,x_0,y_381/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8645266160194223828/1633688208)

Open image viewer

Maria Ressa and Dmitri Muratov. Photo: Jérôme Favre / Sergei Chirikov / EPA

The Nobel Peace Prizes have been awarded in Oslo, the capital of Norway.

The Peace Prize was awarded to the journalists Maria Ressa and Dmitri Muratov.

Ressa and Muratov have worked for freedom of speech. They want everyone to have the chance to get correct information.

Ressa and Muratov come from countries where the work of a journalist is difficult and even dangerous.

Maria Ressa is a Filipino journalist, and she is the director of the news website Rappler. Dmitri Muratov is the editor-in-chief of the Russian newspaper Novaya Gazeta.

## Afghan refugees

![Afghans at the border going to the Pakistani side.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_125/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8768676188d5ae486bd/1636357620)

Open image viewer

Many people have fled from Afghanistan. Photo: EPA-EFE

EU countries are taking in refugees from Afghanistan. 40 000 people from Afghanistan will be taken into EU countries.

A group of EU countries agreed on this matter this autumn.

Most Afghan refugees will get to Germany. Germany will take in 25 000 refugees in total.

Finland is also part of the agreement. Almost 1000 people from Afghanistan will come to Finland.

## Weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12225819)

Open image viewer

The beginning of December has been cold across the whole of Finland. In southern Finland it has not been this cold at the start of December for more than 80 years.

Now the weather is warming, that is, the temperature rises. Next week the temperature is above zero in almost the whole country.

On Saturday the temperature is mostly between -1 and -6 degrees, and in south-west Finland the temperature is between 0 and +2 degrees.

On Saturday it snows in Lapland, but in the south it rains or sleets. Among the rain there is also freezing rain. That is why the driving conditions on the roads are poor.
