# Friday 7.4.2017 (radio)

A terror attack has probably happened in Stockholm, the capital of Sweden. Finns too are shocked by the events in the neighbouring country. The police are increasing surveillance in Helsinki because of the events in Stockholm. The USA has fired missiles at a Syrian army airfield. Sunday is election day. There are more than 60,000 different tree species in the world.

## The Stockholm attack

![Stockholm terror attack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9554343)

The attack happened in central Stockholm. Photo: AOP / Swepix

A terror attack has probably happened in Stockholm, the capital of Sweden. At least 3 people died in it. There are many injured.

The attacker drove a lorry into people in central Stockholm. This happened on Friday afternoon, and that is why there were many people in the centre.

The Swedish police are investigating the matter.

## Helsinki police

The police are increasing surveillance in Helsinki because of the events in Stockholm. Police officers can be seen in places where there are many people. For example, around Helsinki railway station there are more police officers than usual.

The Finnish authorities are following the situation in Sweden and how it affects Finland.

## The war in Syria

![Shayrat air base.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9552243)

Open image viewer

An aerial photo of the airfield where the USA fired missiles. Photo: US Department of Defense / EPA

There is a new situation in the war in Syria. The USA has fired missiles at a Syrian army airfield. The missiles were fired from American warships that are in the Mediterranean Sea.

President Donald Trump says that he ordered the strike because Syria has used chemical weapons. They killed ordinary Syrians, also small children. That is why Trump wanted to destroy the airfield from which the attack with chemical weapons started.

The airfield is near the city of Homs. No people live near the airfield.

## Finland's municipal elections

![The party leaders at a debate on 6 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9552021)

Open image viewer

The party leaders had a discussion on television on Thursday. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Here in Finland the municipal elections are getting closer.

The last big election debate was on television on Thursday. The party leaders discussed, for example, daycare centres. Many Finns are worried about whether children sometimes have too little space in daycare.

In the municipal elections, people are chosen who decide on the municipality's matters, for example daycare centres. Election day is Sunday.

## The world's tree species

![A birch forest in spring.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8064493)

Open image viewer

For example, many birches grow in Finland. Photo: Arja Lento / Yle

Researchers have made a list of the world's tree species. There are more than 60,000 different tree species.

35 tree species grow in Finland. Common tree species in Finnish forests are spruce, pine and birch.

The most different tree species are found in Brazil. Almost 9,000 tree species grow in Brazil. More than 4,300 of them are tree species that do not grow anywhere else.
