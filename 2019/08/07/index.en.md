# Wednesday 7.8.2019 (radio)

There is work in the technology industry. Schools are starting. You must be careful at pedestrian crossings. The interest rate on student loans is low. The weather is cloudy.

## The technology industry is doing well

![Meyer Turku shipyard's large crane.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10510273)

The technology industry is doing well in Finland. The technology industry means, for example, the metal industry and the IT sector.

Many Finns work for a technology company.

A lot of technology products are exported from Finland to other countries.

Companies in the technology industry have been able to take on new people to work for them.

## Pupils' summer holidays end

The summer holiday of many pupils ends this week. Schools start at slightly different times in different municipalities.

In Rovaniemi in Northern Finland, the pupils went to school already today.

In many places school starts tomorrow, on Thursday.

In Turku in Western Finland, the pupils only go to school next week.

## You must be careful at pedestrian crossings

![-](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10357172)

Open image viewer

A pedestrian can end up in a dangerous situation at a pedestrian crossing. Photo: Liikenneturva

Pedestrians say that at pedestrian crossings they often end up in a dangerous situation.

In a survey, Finns were asked about road safety. 40 per cent of pedestrians said that they had been in danger or in an accident at a pedestrian crossing.

Some respondents said that they are afraid of crossing the road on a familiar route. For example, crossing the road on the way to school can be dangerous.

Accident statistics show that road safety at pedestrian crossings has improved.

Now it has been 3 years since any child died in an accident at a pedestrian crossing.

## A student should take out a student loan

![Student financial aid application form](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10642525)

Open image viewer

Students can get a student loan. The interest rate on student loans is now low. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The interest rate on student loans is now low.

A student can get a student loan if they study at a university, a university of applied sciences or a polytechnic.

The state guarantees the student loan.

If a student studies quickly and gets a degree, they also get a student loan reimbursement from the state.

That means the state pays part of the student loan to the bank.

A student should take out a student loan, because the interest rate is low and the state pays the student loan reimbursement.
