## Overconsumption

![Earth, nature, consumption, overconsumption, energy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10848657)

Open image viewer

Thursday is Overconsumption Day. Illustrative image. Photo: AOP

Today, Thursday, is the world's Overconsumption Day. Overconsumption means that the world's people use up natural resources more than the earth can sustain.

On Overconsumption Day people have used the amount of natural resources that the earth can sustain for the whole year. So people use the whole year's natural resources in about 7 months.

The oil company Neste has a good result.

Neste's result in the spring was 750 million euros. A year earlier the result was 465 million euros.

Neste's turnover in the spring was over 7 billion euros. A year earlier Neste's turnover was about 3 billion euros.

Neste's share price rose on the Helsinki stock exchange.

## Corona and the sense of taste

![A child is being given a coronavirus test. There are people queuing behind them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3999,h_2249,x_0,y_416/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98467462d9179e78d3a/1658394739)

Open image viewer

Corona testing. Photo: AFP

People's sense of smell and sense of taste get disturbed when they catch corona. For many people the sense of taste disappears for a while after the corona infection.

A large study says that about 5 percent of people still suffer from problems with the sense of smell and taste 6 months after a corona infection. Most people get back their sense of taste at the latest 3 months after the corona infection.

Many foreign tourists have visited Finland in the summer.

In June, foreign tourists visited Finland 4 times as many as in June 2021. Statistics Finland says that the number of domestic tourists grew by 8 percent.

However, in June there were fewer tourists in Finland than in June 2019. The corona epidemic began in 2020.

The South-Eastern Finland Police and the Regional State Administrative Agency continue investigating what caused the locking of the gate of an animal transport lorry to fail and the pigs to fall out in Lappeenranta. Illustrative image. Photo: Yle/Erica Vasama

In Lappeenranta on Wednesday pigs got away. 8 pigs escaped from the slaughterhouse's lorry. 1 pig died when it hit a car on the road.

Later all the pigs were caught. 1 pig was slightly injured. The other pigs are in good condition.
