# Sunday 7.11.2021 (radio)

Fire on a cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland. There was an earthquake in Rovaniemi. Police are investigating the Houston music festival. A train derailed in Sweden.

## Cargo ship fire in the Gulf of Finland

A fire broke out on a cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland during the night. The fire started in the ship's engine room.

The ship's workers put out the fire. The fire was dangerous, because the ship's cargo includes flammable and poisonous substances.

The ship sails under the Panama flag. The ship is on its way from Russia to France. No oil got into the sea from the ship.

There was an earthquake in Rovaniemi on Saturday. The earthquake was near Santa Claus's workshop. The tremor was about 5 kilometres under the ground.

The strength of the earthquake was 1.5, that is, the tremor was rather small.

In Finland there are dozens of earthquakes every year. Earthquakes in Finland are usually small.

## Crush at the Houston rap concert

![Police sit on a closed street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_493/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-876660618774d11ef39/1636267300)

Police guarded the festival area in Houston on Saturday. Photo: Alex Bierens de Haan / AFP

In the USA police are investigating a music festival where at least 8 people died on Friday. People died at the festival in Houston, when panic broke out during the concert of rapper Travis Scott.

In total more than 300 people were injured at the festival. 11 people's hearts stopped.

Houston police say that someone may have injected other people with drugs or medicines.

There were 50 000 people at the festival on Friday. The second day of the festival was cancelled.

## Train crash in Sweden

A train has derailed in Sweden. The train carried ore and had almost 40 wagons. Nobody was injured in the accident.

The train derailed in northern Sweden in Norrbotten county, north of Jällivaara. Train traffic between Jällivaara and Kiruna may be interrupted for perhaps 3 days.

The train tore down 3 power lines, and repairing the damage from the accident costs a lot of money.
