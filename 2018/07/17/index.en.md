# Tuesday 17.7.2018 (radio)

The police are pleased with the security measures at the Helsinki top-level meeting. President Sauli Niinistö will meet Russian President Vladimir Putin in August. There will be changes to train traffic in the Helsinki region on Wednesday. There are forest fires in Sweden. In Turku it was measured 33.3 degrees on Tuesday.

## Security measures succeeded

![Police in Esplanade Park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10306423)

Open image viewer

Police in Helsinki on 16.7.2018. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

The police are pleased with the security measures at the Helsinki top-level meeting. The meeting of the US and Russian presidents went calmly on Monday.

The top-level meeting was a big job for the Finnish police. The police had to look after the security and traffic of the meeting.

Many police officers came to work voluntarily from their summer holidays. There were police officers in Helsinki from all over Finland.

The police had only 2 weeks to prepare for the Helsinki top-level meeting. That was a very short time, because around the world the security for top-level meetings is usually planned for many months.

## The presidents meet

![Putin and Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10310069)

Open image viewer

Vladimir Putin and Sauli Niinistö also met last summer. Photo: AOP

President Sauli Niinistö will meet Russian President Vladimir Putin next month. Niinistö will visit Russia on 22 August.

Niinistö met Putin on Monday, when Finland hosted the top-level meeting of the Russian and US presidents in Helsinki. The August meeting had already been agreed earlier.

President Putin has thanked Niinistö for organising the top-level meeting.

## Changes to train traffic

![Commuter train.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9770692)

Open image viewer

Changes to commuter trains are due to track work. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There will be changes to train traffic in the Helsinki region on Wednesday.

P and I trains running from the airport through Myyrmäki to Helsinki will only go as far as Pasila. A and L trains will not stop at Ilmala.

These changes will continue until 4 August. The changes are caused by track work.

There are no longer changes on commuter trains that come to Helsinki from the Kerava direction. They are now running normally.

## Forest fires in Sweden

![Map of forest fire areas](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10308706)

Open image viewer

There are forest fires in different parts of Sweden. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

There are forest fires in Sweden, Finland's neighbouring country. Fires are hard to put out, because nature is dry.

There are fires in as many as 80 places in different parts of Sweden. The fire has also burned houses, and people have been moved away from dangerous areas.

Firefighting work continues all the time. There are too few firefighters and firefighting equipment, because there are so many fires.

Norway has sent 6 helicopters to Sweden to help with the firefighting. Italy has sent 2 firefighting aeroplanes, which will arrive in Sweden on Tuesday evening.

## A hot Tuesday

Tuesday has been a very hot day across Finland. In Turku it was measured at 33.3 degrees. The hot weather will also continue for the next few days.

The weather is now so hot that a heatwave warning is in force across almost the whole country.
