# Sunday 19.11.2017 (radio)

A large part of Finns want to raise carers' wages. Zimbabwe's president Robert Mugabe has been removed. The British royal couple have been married for 70 years. A person lives longer if they own a dog.

## Wages in the municipal sector

![A kindergarten teacher lifts a child in the yard of a daycare centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5010028)

A large part of Finns want to raise carers' wages. The information comes from new research, in which Finns were asked their opinion on wages in the municipal sector.

About 60 percent of respondents wanted to raise the wages of childcare workers and nurses. Municipal childcare workers earned about 2300 euros a month last year. A nurse's wage was about 3000 euros a month.

About 50 percent of the respondents also wanted to raise the wages of cleaners.

Many respondents thought that the salaries of municipal managers and city managers are too high. In the respondents' view, managers' salaries could be lowered.

## Zimbabwe's president

![Robert Mugabe](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9938639)

Zimbabwe's president Robert Mugabe has been removed.

Mugabe was removed on Sunday. The decision was made by Mugabe's own party.

The Zimbabwean army had already removed Mugabe from power earlier this week. The army ordered that Mugabe must not leave his home.

Mugabe is 93 years old. He led Zimbabwe, in Africa, for almost 40 years.

The Queen's husband, Prince Philip, is 96 years old.

## The meaning of the dog

![A pensioner and a dog in an autumn forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9905412)

Open image viewer

It is nice for people to be outdoors and move about together with a dog. Photo: AOP

A person lives longer if they own a dog. This is what a Swedish study says.

The researchers followed people's health records for as long as 12 years. The researchers wanted to know whether dog owners have fewer heart and blood vessel diseases than others. The researchers also found out whether there are fewer deaths among dog owners.

The researchers noticed that dog owners fell ill less often with heart and blood vessel diseases than others. Fewer dog owners also died.

The dog brought health especially to people who live alone. If a person lives alone with a dog, they have about 30 percent lower risk of dying than a person who lives alone without a dog.

The researchers say that this needs to be studied more. But it seems that a person is healthier and lives longer if they have a dog.
