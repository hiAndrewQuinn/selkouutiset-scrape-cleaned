The number of coronavirus patients has grown in Finland's hospitals.

There are now a little over 700 coronavirus patients in hospitals. That is about 50 more coronavirus patients than on Monday.

On Wednesday it was reported that there are 29 new coronavirus deaths. In Finland there are now a total of more than 2100 deaths linked to the coronavirus.

On Wednesday it was also reported that there are more than 4500 new coronavirus infections in Finland.

## The end of corona restrictions in Sweden

![Coronavirus testing place in Sweden.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5112,h_2875,x_0,y_198/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9123876203694894446/1644390806)

Coronavirus testing place in Sweden. Photo: Johan Nilsson / AFP

Corona restrictions have ended in Sweden, Finland's neighbour country. Sweden no longer has any coronavirus restrictions.

For example, big sports events and cultural events are allowed again.

The Swedish government decided to remove the coronavirus restrictions because most Swedes have already received their coronavirus vaccines.

## A suggestion for corona support for schools

![Education Minister Li Andersson at a press conference in Helsinki on 9 February 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4392,h_2470,x_0,y_122/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91242862037b701c2db/1644395408)

Open image viewer

Education Minister Li Andersson wants to support schools because of the corona. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Finland's Education Minister Li Andersson suggests corona support for schools. Andersson says that the corona has harmed teaching. That is why the damage must be repaired.

Andersson suggests that early childhood and basic education receive 65 million euros in support. Andersson also suggests 30 million euros of support for vocational education.

Andersson says that the harms of the corona time must not fall mostly on children and young people.

## Weak ice

![A snowmobile and skiers on the sea ice.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_10,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90020561dc326589adc/1641821738)

Open image viewer

It is fun to go out on the sea ice, if the ice is surely strong enough. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The ice situation in the Gulf of Finland is bad. Moving on the ice is now dangerous.

Sea rescue chief Jyrki Lehto says that nobody should go onto the ice.

The ice situation is bad because of the snow. In recent days a lot of snow has fallen, and there is a thick layer of snow on top of the ice.

Now the wet snow is melting the ice from above, and at the same time the sea water is melting the ice from below. That is why the ice is weak.

## The weather

On Thursday the weather is dry.

In the west of the country it is clear, elsewhere there is changing cloudiness.

Cloudiness is heaviest in Lapland. Snow showers will spread to Lapland during the day.

It is 1-10 degrees below zero, and in the south and west of the country it is a few degrees above zero.

At the weekend the weather gets colder.
