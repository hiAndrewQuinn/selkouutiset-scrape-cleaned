# Thursday 24.9.2020 (radio)

Finland is tightening travel rules for many countries. The number of corona infections in Finland keeps growing. At the University of Oulu they are studying whether ear infections can be prevented with a person's own bacteria. The autumn apple harvest has ripened. The weather is warm.

## Travel restrictions

Travel restrictions are coming back, for example on traffic between Finland and Sweden. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

The travel rules will change again on Monday.

The government has decided to tighten the rules, for example for traffic from Estonia, Sweden and Norway. When a traveller returns to Finland from these countries, it is a good idea to stay at home in quarantine for 2 weeks.

However, residents of Northern Finland may handle matters on the Swedish and Norwegian side without quarantine. In addition, from the start of October, people may go to work from Estonia and Sweden on the Finnish side without quarantine.

Finland is tightening the rules because the number of corona infections has grown in many countries.

Finns can now travel freely to only 6 European countries. Finns can return home without quarantine from Latvia, Lithuania and Poland, for example.

The number of corona infections in Finland keeps growing.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, reported 91 new corona infections on Thursday.

During the last 2 weeks, 460 more corona infections have been reported than during the 2 weeks before.

## Ear infections

![A female doctor examines a child's ear.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10432146)

Open image viewer

Many children suffer from ear infections. Photo: AOP

The University of Oulu has started a study on children's ear infections. The study will find out whether ear infections can be prevented with a person's own bacteria.

The bacterium Streptococcus salivarius lives in the human throat. It produces defence proteins against harmful bacteria. Researchers intend to find out whether a good bacterium can fight bad bacteria.

If ear infections can be prevented, children's health will improve and parents' absences from work will decrease.

The autumn apple harvest has ripened.

Finnish apple growers compete for customers with foreign growers.

Apple farms have invested in training and developing their operations. The amount of the harvest and the quality of the apples have improved.

Apple growers know that apples must also look good. They say that customers buy apples with their eyes.
