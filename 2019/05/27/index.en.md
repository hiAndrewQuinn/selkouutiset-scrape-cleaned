# Monday 27.5.2019 (radio)

Finns elected their representatives to the EU Parliament. Right-wing populists won more seats in the EU Parliament. Finns are celebrating the ice hockey world championship. All euro banknotes have now been redesigned.

## EU Parliament elections

![Support in the European elections](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10803045)

Open image viewer

Photo: Hilppa Hyrkäs, Seppo Suvela / Yle

Finns have chosen their new representatives to the EU Parliament.

The largest party in the election is the National Coalition. The National Coalition gets three seats in the European Parliament. This is the same number as before.

The Greens are the only party that gets more seats in the Parliament. The Greens had one seat before. Now the party has 2 seats.

The Centre lost 1 seat and keeps 2 seats.

The SDP and the Finns Party got 2 seats each in the Parliament. The RKP and the Left Alliance got 1 seat each.

Voter turnout in Finland was about 43 per cent.

## More power for small groups

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10804623)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

In the European Parliament, representatives belong to different groups.

The largest groups in the European Parliament are the EPP, which represents the centre and the right, and the Social Democrats. Until now these two groups together had more than half of the seats in Parliament. Now the number is less than half.

The small and medium-sized groups in Parliament are growing, and they get more power.

The Greens will gain the most new seats in Parliament. Nationalist populists were elected to Parliament a little more than before.

The European Parliament has 751 representatives in total.

## Finland is the ice hockey world champion

![TOP Lankinen and friends](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10803164)

Open image viewer

Photo: AOP

Finns are delighted that the Finnish team won the ice hockey world championship.

Last night 10,000 people celebrated Finland's team's victory in Helsinki's Market Square.

The team returned to Finland in the afternoon, and the celebrations continue today.

Finland won the final yesterday evening 3-1 against Canada.

The Finnish ice hockey national team has now won the world championship 3 times in total.

## All euro banknotes have been redesigned

![100 and 200 euro banknotes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10804125)

Open image viewer

Photo: Maik Guedes / European Central Bank / Lehtikuva

The redesigned 100 and 200 euro banknotes will come into use tomorrow in all euro countries.

These banknotes are the last of the new euro banknotes. New euro banknotes of 5, 10, 20 and 50 euros have already been introduced earlier.

There is no 500 euro banknote in the new set of euro banknotes.

The new euro banknotes contain a lot of high technology. Because of this they are harder to forge than the old banknotes.

The old euro banknotes can still be used.
