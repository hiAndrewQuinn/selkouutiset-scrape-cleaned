# Monday 26.9.2022 (radio)

Border traffic from Russia. Italy's election winner. School shooting in Russia. Yle News School. Weather.

## Border traffic from Russia

![Map showing the Vaalimaa, Nuijamaa and Imatra border stations.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12638278)

Open image viewer

There has been busy traffic at Finland's border crossing points.

Traffic from Russia to Finland is still busy.

Over the weekend more than 17,000 Russians came across the border from Russia into Finland. On the Russian side there has been a 5-kilometre queue to the Finnish border.

Many Russians have come to Finland since Russia started calling men into the army. Russia needs soldiers in Ukraine, where Russia started the war 7 months ago.

Many Russian men have travelled abroad, because they do not want to be forced into war. The Russian newspaper Novaya Gazeta reports that in recent days over 260,000 men have left Russia.

## Italy's election winner

![Light-haired woman in a white suit smiles with a "Grazie Italia" sign. In the background a large poster reads Fratelli d'Italia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12638345)

Open image viewer

Giorgia Meloni may be Italy's next prime minister. Photo: Riccardo De Luca / AOP

There have been parliamentary elections in Italy. Italians voted in the parliamentary elections on Sunday.

The winner of the election is the party named Brothers of Italy. It is a far-right party. The leader of the party is Giorgia Meloni.

Meloni may become Italy's new prime minister. At the same time she will become Italy's first female prime minister.

Many minorities are worried about Meloni's victory, because Meloni wants a Christian and conservative Italy.

For example, immigrants and gay people fear that their rights will be reduced in Meloni's Italy.

## School shooting in Russia

![Izhevsk city on a map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1012808633187cd1cd0d/1664190589)

The school shooting took place in the city of Izhevsk. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

A school shooting has happened in Russia.

At least 13 people died when a former pupil of the school began shooting people. Among the dead are 7 children.

In addition, about 20 people were injured. Most of them are also children.

The shooting took place in the city of Izhevsk. It is about 1,000 kilometres from Moscow.
