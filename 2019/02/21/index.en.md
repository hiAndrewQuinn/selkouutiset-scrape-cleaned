# Thursday 21.2.2019 (radio)

Anne Berner can continue as minister. Alcohol consumption has grown. Finns are buying snus in Sweden. The ski world championships have started. On Saturday the driving conditions are bad.

## Minister Berner

![Anne Berner](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10657704)

Open image viewer

Transport and communications minister Anne Berner. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Anne Berner can continue as minister. The decision was made by Berner's party, the Centre Party. Berner is the minister of transport and communications.

Berner's work led to a discussion when news came out that she may also get an important job at a bank. Berner will be elected to the board of the Swedish bank SEB in March. Many people asked whether a minister can also work for a bank.

On Thursday, however, SEB said that Berner will only start the new job at the beginning of June. Elections will be held in Finland in April, and after the elections the government will change. That is why Berner will not be a minister and work for the bank at the same time.

## Alcohol consumption

![Lonkero](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10071854)

Open image viewer

Long drink, or lonkero, is a favourite drink of many Finns. Photo: Hartwall

Alcohol consumption has grown. In 2018, Finns drank more alcohol than in 2017. Consumption grew by 1 percent.

The new alcohol law is visible in the figure. The law gave grocery shops permission to sell stronger alcoholic drinks than before. Today, grocery shops may sell drinks with up to 5.5 percent alcohol.

Because of the law, sales of strong beer and strong long drinks grew. Last year, five times more strong long drinks were sold than a year earlier.

## Snus imports

![snus tin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4970,h_2797,x_32,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-7481858932d8d73f69/1486040551)

Open image viewer

Snus tins. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finns also bought a lot of snus last year. Snus is a tobacco product. Snus is not smoked. It is put in the mouth, under the lip.

Finns buy snus when they visit Sweden. Sweden is the only EU country where snus may be sold.

Last year, Finnish travellers brought more than 17 million snus tins into Finland. That is more than the year before.

## Ski world championships

The ski world championships have started. The competition venue is Seefeld in Austria.

The Finnish skiers did not do very well on the first day of the competitions.

However, new chances will come, because the world championships continue until the end of next week.

## Sunshine and rain

![Slushy ground and a person walking](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9946540)

Open image viewer

The streets are slippery when the sun turns to rain. Photo: Eveliina Matikainen / Yle

The weather varies. On Friday there is frost across the whole country. Friday is also a sunny day.

On Saturday the weather warms up, so the frost gets weaker. Rain moves across Finland, and the driving conditions are bad on Saturday.

At the weekend, traffic will be busy, because the ski holiday ends in Southern Finland and starts in Central Finland.
