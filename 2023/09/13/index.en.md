Russia's president **Vladimir Putin** and North Korea's leader **Kim Jong-un** have met in Russia. North Korea's leader Kim Jong-un said that North Korea supports Russia's fight in Ukraine.

Russia's president Vladimir Putin said that Russia will help North Korea build a satellite. Russia wants to buy weapons from North Korea, because Russia is using up a lot of weapons and ammunition in the war in Ukraine. North Korea wants to get new weapons technology and food aid from Russia.

The cooperation between North Korea and Russia worries many Western countries.

## Bird flu

![Foxes in cages at a fur farm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5464,h_3073,x_0,y_2463/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115666164db31c7dc180/1699893863)

Open image viewer

Bird flu can spread quickly on fur farms. Photo: Janne Järvinen / Yle

Finland is increasing measures against bird flu. The new regulations concern fur farms that have foxes or raccoon dogs.

The Food Agency has ordered that all foxes and raccoon dogs must be killed if bird flu is found on a fur farm. The order concerns about 115 000 animals. Earlier, the Food Agency already gave the same order for fur farms that have minks.

The aim is to protect people's health. Bird flu may also start to spread to people, if it is allowed to spread among animals for a long time. That is why minks, foxes and raccoon dogs must be killed on fur farms if they have bird flu.

## Honey harvest

![Oulu beekeeper Pertti Harmaala stands next to beehives, wearing beekeeper's protective clothing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2267,x_0,y_112/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11708216500256eca12a/1694509305)

Open image viewer

Beekeeper Pertti Harmaala and beehives. Photo: Päivi Seeskorpi / Yle

The honey harvest of the summer has been smaller than usual. Two things were bad for the honey harvest. Early in the summer it was rather dry. In late summer it was cool and rained a lot.

For example, Oulu beekeeper **Pertti Harmaala** says that the honey harvest will be about 10–20 percent smaller than in previous years. In Northern Finland, some beekeepers had to start winter feeding of their bees in July, because honey no longer built up in the beehives.

A small honey harvest and higher costs may raise the price of honey in shops.
