# Saturday 19.9.2020 (radio)

People can now travel from Sweden and Norway to Finland without border checks. Corona infections in Finland continue to grow. Many young people have caught corona from a restaurant at night. Even a little exercise improves health. On Sunday it rains in the north, and in the south it is dry.

## Travel is becoming easier

The new travel rules have come into force.

People can now travel from Sweden and Norway to Finland without border checks. Passengers can also freely come to Finland from Germany, for example.

Finland estimates that the corona situation in these countries is so good that restrictions are no longer needed.

However, restrictions continue for many countries. Passengers cannot freely come to Finland from countries where there are many corona cases.

## Corona infections are increasing

![Corona testing at the Meilahti drive-in station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3455,h_1943,x_0,y_162/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7078665f35167fee7e7/1597659846)

The number of corona infections is growing in Finland. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, reported 64 new corona infections on Saturday.

During the last 2 weeks, over 300 more corona infections have been reported than during the 2 weeks before.

In total, about 9,000 corona infections have been reported in Finland.

## Restaurant operations

![Lasse Lehtonen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11553706)

Open image viewer

Diagnostics Director Lasse Lehtonen. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

In Finland there is a debate about how restaurants operate.

Many young people have caught corona at a restaurant at night. This is what Lasse Lehtonen, Diagnostics Director of the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district, says.

Lehtonen hopes that the sale of alcohol in restaurants is banned at night. Although young people often do not get seriously ill from corona, they can pass the corona on to their grandparents, Lehtonen says.

The operations of restaurants have been restricted in many countries. For example, Estonia said on Thursday that it is banning the sale of alcohol in restaurants at night.

## Exercise and health

![A woman listening to headphones while lying on the sofa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3939,h_2216,x_0,y_412/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7014685f18024e13d1c/1677582738)

Open image viewer

Health always improves when you get up from the sofa. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Even a little exercise improves health. This is what a new Finnish study says.

Researchers say that just 10 minutes of everyday physical activity a day improves health. Everyday physical activity is, for example, getting up from your desk or the sofa and walking to get a coffee.

Even a small change in habits is good for heart health. A beginner does not need to walk 10,000 steps a day straight away.

It is good to remember, however, that the benefits of exercise grow when you move more.

On Sunday it will rain in Northern Finland. In Southern Finland it is dry, that is, no rain falls.

On Sunday the temperature in the north is under 10 degrees, and in the south about 15 degrees.

On Monday the weather warms up a little. In the south it may be as warm as 17 degrees.
