# Thursday 27.5.2021 (radio)

There are fewer corona infections than before. The prosecutor is demanding a 13-year prison sentence for Niko Ranta-aho. A Kone building burned in Hyvinkää. Norppalive is closed.

## Fewer corona infections than before

![Corona test](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11938325)

Open image viewer

Corona test. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

There is good news about corona.

There are a little fewer new coronavirus infections in Finland than before. There are 98 new infections in Finland. Infections are also fewer elsewhere in the world than before.

People in Finland have been vaccinated at a good pace. Already 50 percent of people over 16 have received the first dose of the vaccine.

People have been taking a lot of corona tests. The origin of coronavirus infections can now be traced well. People also need less hospital care than before.

However, the authorities say that people must still follow corona restrictions.

## Trial over drug crimes

Helsinki District Court in Ruoholahti on 19 February. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

A trial over drug crimes has begun in Helsinki.

The prosecutor is demanding a 13-year prison sentence for Niko Ranta-aho. He is charged with 4 aggravated drug offences. Ranta-aho says that he is not guilty.

A total of 15 people have been charged.

Niko Ranta-aho was already given 11 years in prison in April for drug offences he committed earlier.

## Fire in Hyvinkää

The roof of an industrial building was in flames on Thursday afternoon. The police are investigating the cause of the fire. Photo: Central Uusimaa Rescue Department

There was a big fire in Hyvinkää. The roof of Kone's research centre, a lift company, caught fire on Thursday afternoon. There was a lot of smoke in the air because of the fire.

People had to leave the building that caught fire. People also left buildings that were close to the fire. No one was injured in the fire.

Putting out the fire will take a long time.

## Norppalive closed

![Saimaa ringed seal on a shore stone](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80831060a606b2e52f0/1621493498)

Saimaa ringed seal seen through binoculars from a boat. Photo: Tarja Nyyssönen / Yle

The Norppalive of the environmental organisation WWF has been closed.

A video camera filmed Saimaa ringed seals on Lake Saimaa. The picture was shown live on the internet. The picture showed the seals lying on a rock.

The location of the camera was revealed to some people, and people have disturbed the Saimaa ringed seals. That is why the camera was closed earlier than planned.

WWF says that next year the camera may be put in a new place.

The Saimaa ringed seal is a rare small seal. Saimaa ringed seals are found only in Lake Saimaa. Saimaa is Finland's biggest lake.
