## The Finnish economy shrank in the spring

![An aerial photo of the Kamppi shopping centre and Narinkka square in May 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7007825f1586302e8aa/1595248092)

The Finnish economy has perhaps come through the corona situation better than many other European countries.

Statistics Finland says that Finland's gross domestic product shrank by about 5 per cent in April to June compared with April to June last year. Gross domestic product is the value of all the goods and services produced in Finland.

In many other European countries the gross domestic product shrank more than in Finland.

## Bus ticket prices may rise

![Buses in the central square of Tampere.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10663430)

Open image viewer

Buses in the central square of Tampere. Photo: Tuomas Hirvonen / Yle

The number of passengers on public transport has fallen a lot because of the corona situation. Public transport includes, for example, buses and trains.

Because fewer people travel on public transport, the ticket money is not enough to cover the costs of public transport. That is why many cities may have to raise bus ticket prices next year.

For example, in the Helsinki area, Tampere, Turku, Oulu and Jyväskylä, public transport has had only about half the usual number of passengers from January to July.

## Children are healthier now

![a doctor examines a child's ear](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10432114)

Open image viewer

A doctor examines a child's ear. Photo: AOP

The number of children getting flu and other respiratory illnesses has fallen a lot this year.

Because of the corona situation, people are careful. For example, people keep their distance and wash their hands often. That is why many illnesses do not spread easily now.

Researchers at the University of Eastern Finland say that children now have fewer respiratory illnesses and, for example, ear infections than before. Children have been to the doctor much less than usual.

## Face mask recommendation for ships

![people leaving a ship at the west terminal](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9482328)

Open image viewer

Passengers at the port. There may be a lot of people at the port. Then it is a good idea to use a mask. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Shipping companies in Finland have given a face mask recommendation.

Shipping companies recommend that ship passengers use a face mask when passengers cannot keep safe distances.

For example, at the port, when passengers go onto the ship or off it, it is often cramped. Then it is a good idea to use a face mask.
