# Monday 16.11.2020 (radio)

There is heavy congestion at dental care. Finland's coronavirus situation. Some people are sometimes ashamed of their work. Iraqis are applying for citizenship. Vastaamo's service for victims of the data breach. Rainy weather.

## Dental care is very busy

![Patient being treated at a dentist.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11601409)

Open image viewer

You can get into the dentist quickly if the problem is bad. Photo: Heini Holopainen / Yle

Dental care is now very busy because of the coronavirus.

In the spring, when the coronavirus began to spread, dental clinics only took on patients who were in a hurry to get treatment.

Dental check-ups and other routine treatments were postponed.

The situation is bad across the whole country.

The worst situation is in the capital region. In the capital region almost 200,000 people are still on a waiting list.

They are waiting to get dental treatment.

## Some people are sometimes ashamed of their work

![Shop assistant Linda Väyrynen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_546/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7347515f97ff7c00ea4/1603797402)

Open image viewer

Young people and low-paid workers may sometimes be ashamed of their work. Photo: Markku Pitkänen / Yle

Yle investigated whether people feel ashamed of their work.

Especially young people and low-paid workers are sometimes ashamed of their work.

They feel that their work is not valued, because the pay is low or because the employer has a bad reputation.

One in four respondents said that at some point they have been ashamed to say what work they do.

More than 1,000 people answered Yle's survey.

Of them, 70 percent said that they are not ashamed of their own work.

## Iraqis are applying for Finnish citizenship

![Migri's new service point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10638968)

Open image viewer

Migri says that Iraqis are now the largest group applying for Finnish citizenship. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The Finnish Immigration Service, Migri, says that Iraqis are now applying for Finnish citizenship the most.

Iraqis submit the most citizenship applications.

Previously, Russians submitted the most applications.

Finnish citizenship can now be applied for by those asylum seekers who have received international protection and who came to Finland in 2015.

## Vastaamo service for data breach victims

![Psychotherapy centre Vastaamo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_287/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7354675f996f137c042/1605111263)

Open image viewer

An unknown perpetrator carried out the data breach on Vastaamo. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The psychotherapy centre Vastaamo has opened a service in which customers can order their own patient data.

A customer can make an inspection request.

The customer is identified with online banking codes or a mobile certificate.

The customer then receives copies of their own data by protected email or by letter.

An unknown perpetrator carried out the data breach on Vastaamo and stole a lot of patient data. The perpetrator has not yet been caught.
