## Exercises in local defense

First, about defense.

This week, local defense exercises have been conducted in various parts of Finland.

Local defense exercises have been held in various parts of Finland. Colonel **Vesa Helminen** says that the Huittinen exercise was a success.

## Russian drone in Finland

Then about drones.

A drone has been found in the Pellingi archipelago in Porvoo.

The Border Guard says it is Russian-made. The Border Guard has not yet confirmed whether the drone has been used by Russia.

The Ministry of Defense says the drone is now under investigation. The investigation will determine whether Finnish territory was violated.

A fisherman found a drone in Pelling on Tuesday. The drone may be a surveillance drone that did not contain explosives.

## Gender-neutral salary model

Now salary news.

A new model has been created in Finland that assesses the complexity of work.

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health's model compares tasks and salaries. The goal is that, for example, men and women receive the same salary for the same job.

The new model allows workplaces to compare whether different tasks are equally demanding.

## Finland's tallest wooden building

Finally, about wooden construction.

Finland's tallest wooden building has been completed in Keilaniemi, Espoo. The Keilaniemi Gate has 13 floors.

The building is environmentally friendly. Its carbon footprint is half that of a similar house built of concrete.

The carbon footprint measures the total amount of greenhouse gas emissions.

Architect **Arndt Heinzmann** says the green roof continues the view from the beach.

Keilaniemi Gate is the tallest wooden building in Finland. It is also one of the tallest wooden buildings in Europe.
