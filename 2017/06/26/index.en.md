# Monday 26.6.2017 (radio)

Finland interests tourists. The prime minister is visiting China. Finland is deporting Kurds to Turkey. A pizzeria was burned down in Espoo. People are fairly satisfied with speed limits. Midsummer passed peacefully.

## Finland interests Chinese tourists in particular

![Tourists in Senate Square in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8711515)

Open image viewer

Finland now interests Chinese tourists in particular.  Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Finland interests foreign tourists.

Overnight stays by foreign tourists in Finland grew in January–April. There were about 25 percent more overnight stays than a year ago.

The number of Chinese tourists in particular is growing, says the tourism industry organisation MaRa.

Russians are also travelling to Finland again, because the value of Russia's money, the rouble, has risen.

## Prime Minister Sipilä is visiting China

![Chinese President Xi Jinping](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9551640)

Open image viewer

Chinese President Xi Jinping visited Finland. Now Finland's Prime Minister Juha Sipilä is visiting China. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä has met Chinese President Xi Jinping in Beijing.

China wants to continue cooperation with Finland, says President Xi.

Xi visited Finland in April, and Sipilä thanked him for the visit. Sipilä said that Xi's visit is one of the highlights of Finland's 100th anniversary year.

## Finland is deporting Kurds to Turkey

![Serhat and Demir applied for asylum in Finland. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9650985)

Open image viewer

Serhat and Demir are being deported from Finland to Turkey.  Photo: Jari Kovalainen

Finland is sending back Kurds who have been imprisoned by Turkey. The Kurds were imprisoned in Turkey for political reasons.

The Finnish Immigration Service does not consider the Kurds victims of persecution, Yle News reports.

In Turkey, the Kurds may be sent to prison for many years. They also say that they have been tortured in prison.

## A pizzeria was burned down in Espoo

![A fire started in the pizzeria on Sunday evening; police say it was a petrol bomb attack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9689953)

Open image viewer

The fire destroyed the pizzeria completely. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

A pizzeria was set on fire in Kyyhkysmäki in Espoo on Sunday evening.

Police suspect that two men threw a petrol bomb at the pizzeria's window. Police have caught two men.

There were about 8 customers inside the pizzeria when it caught fire. Their lives were in danger, says the pizzeria's owner.

The pizzeria was destroyed in the fire.

## Finns are satisfied with speed limits

![A variable speed limit sign beside a motorway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6784249)

Open image viewer

The speed limit on motorways is now at most 120 kilometres per hour. Photo: Yle

Most Finns are satisfied that on motorways you may drive at most 120 kilometres per hour.

Yle did a survey, according to which 70 percent of people are satisfied with the current speed limits.

Almost 30 percent said in the survey that they want higher speed limits on motorways.

The Finnish Transport Agency says that the speed limits will not be raised. If cars drive faster, noise increases, roads wear out and more petrol is needed than before.

## Midsummer passed peacefully

![A man holding a breathalyser](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6728940)

Open image viewer

You may not steer a boat while drunk. At Midsummer, police and the coast guard breath-tested people driving cars and boats. Photo: Juha Korhonen / Yle

Midsummer in Finland was quite peaceful, police say.

Police say that the reason was probably the cold weather.

Police caught about 160 drunk drivers and drunk boaters, that is, people who drove a car or a boat while drunk.

No one died in the Midsummer outgoing and return traffic, even though there were many cars on the road.

At least six people drowned at Midsummer.
