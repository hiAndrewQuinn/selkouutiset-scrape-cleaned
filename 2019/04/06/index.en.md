# Saturday 6.4.2019 (radio)

There has been a big climate march in Helsinki. There are severe floods in Iran. Is spring a time of love? Saturday was a warm day.

## Helsinki climate march

![ Participants in the climate march gathered at Senate Square in Helsinki on 6 April 2019 ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10726239)

There has been a big climate march in Helsinki. About 10,000 people were part of the event.

People march because they are worried about global warming. They demand that politicians make decisions that slow down global warming.

Participants in the march reminded people that the climate must not warm by more than 1.5 degrees. If the climate warms more, for example storms, floods and heat will increase on Earth.

The climate warms because of carbon dioxide emissions. Emissions come, for example, from traffic and industry. In the opinion of the marchers, politicians must take global warming seriously.

## Floods in Iran

![Aerial photo of the flooded area of the city of Pol-e Dokhtar on 2 April. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10726160)

Flood water has risen onto the streets of cities in Iran. Photo: Aziz Babanejad / AFP / Lehtikuva

There are severe floods in Iran. 70 people have died. More than 80,000 people have been moved to safety from the flood area.

The cause of the floods is heavy rain. It has been raining for 3 weeks already.

Iranians criticise President Hassan Rouhani. In the opinion of Iranians, the president has handled the situation badly.

## Spring and love

![A man and a woman stand facing each other in a park and kiss each other](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_423/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5502365c922e84501be/1598167782)

Open image viewer

Spring brings light, but does it also bring love? Photo: © Eugenio Marongiu

Now in spring the amount of light grows. Many people say that spring and summer are also times of love. But is it so?

Docent Sonja Koski says that people form couples and have babies in all seasons. Culture has an effect, however. In Finland Midsummer is an important celebration, and earlier many people got married precisely at Midsummer. Lots of babies were then born in March. That is no longer the case, Koski says.

Light, however, affects the brain through the eyes. Professor Timo Partonen says that because of the light the brain produces more serotonin. It is a substance that makes us cheerful and social. That is why we sleep less and become interested in other people.

Maybe spring is a time of love after all. At least a big Finnish dating service says that it has more customers in spring than in winter.

## The warmest day of spring

![Dandelions.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10150568)

Open image viewer

The dandelion is a spring flower. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Saturday has been a sunny day. Saturday was also the warmest day of spring so far.

In Pori, in Western Finland, 15 degrees of warmth was measured on Saturday.

In the next few days, however, the weather will turn colder. Rain, sleet and snow will fall in different parts of Finland.
