# Sunday 12.12.2021 (radio)

Tornadoes caused much damage in the USA. Train traffic between Helsinki and St Petersburg started again. Kimi Räikkönen's Formula One career ended. Selkosanomat is now more modern and colourful than before. The weather is grey.

## Tornadoes caused destruction in the USA

![A man looks at the ruins of a residential building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_18/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89094361b58fe54291e/1639288927)

In the USA, tornadoes have caused big damage.

In the middle of the country there was a strong storm on Friday, which produced perhaps about 30 tornadoes, that is, whirlwind storms.

Almost 100 people have died and dozens are missing. Most of the dead are in the state of Kentucky.

The tornadoes have knocked down trees, cut off electricity and torn off the roofs of houses.

President Joe Biden has promised help to the disaster areas. Biden says that this may be one of the worst tornado disasters in the history of the USA.

## Allegro trains are running again

src="https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5685611"

Open image viewer

Allegro trains run between Helsinki and St Petersburg. Photo: Yle / Raine Martikainen

Passenger train traffic between Finland and Russia has been opened again. The first Allegro trains between Helsinki and St Petersburg left this morning.

The train connection has been cut for almost 2 years. The connection was cut in March 2020 because of the corona pandemic.

The Allegro train now runs between Helsinki and St Petersburg every day. There are 2 train services a day in both directions.

Kimi Räikkönen's career as a Formula 1 driver has ended. Räikkönen drove his last Formula 1 race today in Abu Dhabi.

Kimi Räikkönen is now 42 years old. Räikkönen's Formula 1 career lasted about 20 years.

Räikkönen won the world championship once, in 2007.

## Selkosanomat has been renewed

![Two women and one man look at a screen. In the background is a poster that reads Selkosanamat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12224504)

Selkosanomat layout designer Heidi Ahlström, editor-in-chief Petri Kiuttu and journalist Kaisa Kaatra prepare the December issue of the paper. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

Selkosanomat, Finland's only newspaper in easy-to-read Finnish, has been renewed.

The first renewed paper came out in September. The paper is now more modern, clearer and more colourful than before.

![Cover of the Selkosanomat paper in close-up after the renewal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_516/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-883856619f6acd2a203/1638522958)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

Selkosanomat's editor-in-chief Petri Kiuttu says that the content and layout of the paper were meant to bring it up to date. At the same time the online paper was also renewed.

\- In the printed paper we now have more background material like that and longer articles. So the news focus is online, Petri Kiuttu says.

New news is written for the online paper every week. The printed paper comes out 8 times a year. In addition, Selkosanomat comes out in Swedish under the name Lätta bladet.

What kind of things does Selkosanomat tell about?

\- We have exactly the same topics in our easy-to-read papers as in other newspapers, that is, we have Finland, the world, sport, and then we also have theme stories, which are longer pieces that tell about people, matters and phenomena, editor-in-chief Petri Kiuttu says.
