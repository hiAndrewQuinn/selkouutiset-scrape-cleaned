# Wednesday 19.8.2020 (radio)

Finland is bringing back travel restrictions to several countries. Flights from Skopje will probably end by the end of next week. The cost of face masks can be reduced in taxation. School peace has been declared again. In Finland the snow season has become shorter.

## Travel restrictions came back

![Two passengers walk with their luggage at Helsinki-Vantaa airport on 7 August 2020. They are wearing face masks in front of their faces.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1384,h_779,x_203,y_294/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7064895f2e70454b317/1607756526)

You can still travel freely from Finland to, among others, the Baltic countries, Italy, Hungary and Slovakia. Photo: Arttu Timonen / Yle

Finland is bringing back travel restrictions to several countries. Interior Minister Maria Ohisalo told about the decision in the morning.

Border control between Schengen countries will be brought back from Norway, Denmark, Iceland, Germany, Greece and Malta.

At the external borders of the Schengen area, restrictions will be brought back from Ireland, San Marino, Cyprus and Japan.

The government recommends that people do not travel to risk countries. In addition, a 2-week quarantine is recommended when people come to Finland from these countries.

It is still possible to travel to Finland, for example for work trips and for family reasons.

There is a special arrangement on the land borders with Sweden and Norway. People living in border areas have the chance to cross the border, so that everyday life is made easier.

## Traficom decides on the Skopje flights

![Passengers arriving in Finland from Skopje in North Macedonia at Turku airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11500880)

Open image viewer

Under the law, only an authority may decide to suspend flights. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The Finnish Transport and Communications Agency Traficom is making the decision on suspending the Skopje flights. There have been 3 flights from Skopje in North Macedonia to Turku and 43 corona infections in total.

The law says that the decision belongs to civil servant level. Traficom still needs a statement from the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

The Skopje flights will probably end by the end of next week.

## Masks for commuting can be deducted in taxation

![A person wearing a respirator on the Helsinki metro on a hot Friday at the end of June 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_256/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6938825ef5e7d3e6bd0/1595158942)

Open image viewer

The cost of buying a face mask for public commuting transport can be deducted in taxation. A mask is not a compulsory protective item, but it is recommended. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The Tax Administration has decided that the cost of face masks can be deducted in taxation.

The deduction applies to trips between home and work in public transport. Costs that you have paid yourself are deducted in taxation as part of the costs of commuting.

The deduction is 2 euros per working day.

## School peace has been declared again

![Basketball training in the gym of Tiistilä school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4290,h_2413,x_0,y_362/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6431085e46de8a201d5/1581703246)

Illustration photo. Photo: Janne Lindroos / Yle

School peace has been declared again today. It applies to all Finnish pupils, pre-school children and students.

The message of the school peace declaration is that a school must be a safe place. The motto of school peace is "hand in hand".

School peace was first declared in Finland 30 years ago.

## Snowy winters have become shorter

![Heavy snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3884,h_2185,x_0,y_407/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6672165ea1223009f54/1643793806)

Open image viewer

Emissions must be reduced if snowy winters are to be kept. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

In Finland the snow season has become shorter, says a new doctoral study. In addition, the depth of the snow has decreased over large areas in recent decades.

Between 1961 and 2014 the snow cover became thinner the most in Southern and Western Finland. There was a decrease of about 2–4 centimetres per decade. The reason is that in winter more water falls as rain than before.

Snow and rain have also increased in winter in Northern Finland.

The study predicts that the ice cover of the Baltic Sea will get thinner in the coming decades. Reducing emissions is the most important factor.
