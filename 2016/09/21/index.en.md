## Niinistö wants to stop extremist movements

![President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö speaking at the UN General Assembly on 21 September 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9183085)

Open image viewer

President Niinistö spoke at the UN General Assembly. Photo: Yle

Finland's President Sauli Niinistö says that violent extremist movements must be stopped. Niinistö gave a speech at the UN General Assembly in New York.

Niinistö said that, for example, racism and violent extremism are destructive ideologies and they must be stopped.

The President thinks it is really important that the world tries to find solutions to the reasons why people set out on the move.

Niinistö told the assembly that equality between the sexes is also important for Finland and it must be promoted.

## The court remanded a member of the Resistance Movement in custody

![Jesse Torniainen, suspected of aggravated involuntary manslaughter (on the right in the picture), in court with his lawyer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9181656)

Open image viewer

The court remanded a member of the Resistance Movement in custody for the assault at Asema-aukio.  Photo: AOP

The Helsinki District Court has remanded Jesse Torniainen, a member of the Nordic Resistance Movement, in custody. Torniainen was remanded in custody for the assault at Asema-aukio.

Torniainen is suspected of assault and aggravated involuntary manslaughter.

The Resistance Movement is a neo-Nazi organisation. The organisation handed out leaflets outside Helsinki railway station a week and a half ago, and Torniainen was there.

It is suspected that Jesse Tornianen is the man who assaulted the 28-year-old man. The victim died about a week later.

In police questioning, Torniainen has said that he had no part in the incident at Asema-aukio. He has not said anything else in the questioning.

## Driving an Uber taxi breaks the law

![The Uber taxi service in China.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9179724)

Open image viewer

Uber taxi fares may have to be given to the state. Photo: AOP

The Helsinki Court of Appeal says that driving an Uber taxi is illegal activity. The court has ordered fines for two Uber taxi drivers. In addition, the Uber drivers must give all their fares to the state.

The court thinks that the Uber activity was professional and that a taxi licence is needed for it. One driver has to pay the state about 3000 euros in fares and the other over 12,000 euros.

Dozens of similar Uber taxi cases are being investigated in Finland.

## An animal rights party was born in Finland

![a mink in a cage](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7811269)

Open image viewer

The animal rights party wants animals not to be used, for example, as clothes for people.  Photo: Oikeutta eläimille

Finland has got a new party. It is the Finnish Animal Rights Party.

The Animal Rights Party works for animal rights. The party wants to change Finland so that animals are not used as people's means of production, food, entertainment or clothes.

According to the Animal Rights Party, other parties write nicely about animal rights, but they do not really promote animal rights.

The party collected supporters so that it became an official party. The Finnish Animal Rights Party got 5400 supporters.
