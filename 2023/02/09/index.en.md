# Thursday 9.2.2023 (radio)

The victims of the Turkish earthquake. The President of Ukraine in Europe. Young people and vitamin D. Wind power. The weather.

## Victims of the earthquake

![Rescue workers carry a man who was found in the rubble.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688912)

Open image viewer

Rescue work in Turkey continues. Photo: Esra Hacioglu Karakaya / AOP

In Turkey and Syria the number of victims of the earthquake is rising. More and more dead are being found all the time as the rescue work goes on.

On Thursday it was reported that about 20,000 people died in the earthquake. Most of the dead are in Turkey.

In addition, many Turks lost their homes because of the earthquake. Therefore about 50,000 Turks can move into hotels in the Antalya area.

Antalya is a popular holiday destination, but in winter the hotels are closed. Now the hotel workers have been called to work, and the hotels will be opened for people who are without a home.

## President of Ukraine

![The plenary hall of the European Parliament, with many people.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688913)

Open image viewer

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy spoke to the European Parliament in Brussels. Photo: Lehtikuva

The President of Ukraine, Volodymyr Zelenskyy, has visited Europe.

On Thursday Zelenskyy spoke to the European Parliament in Brussels.

Zelenskyy said that Ukraine is defending European values in the war against Russia. He thanked Europe for its support.

In Brussels Zelenskyy also took part in a meeting of EU leaders.

The Ukrainian president's visit to Europe began on Wednesday.

Then Zelenskyy met the British Prime Minister in London, and after that the leaders of France and Germany in Paris.

Zelenskyy told the leaders of these countries that Ukraine now especially needs military aircraft.

There has been a war in Ukraine for about a year already. Russia attacked Ukraine last February.

## Vitamin D

![A vitamin D jar and a hand taking a tablet from it.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688914)

Open image viewer

Vitamin D supplements are recommended for all children. Photo: All Over Press

In Finland a large part of young people get too little vitamin D. This is what a new study by the University of Eastern Finland says.

In Finland it is recommended that all 2-18-year-olds take vitamin D supplements, that is, for example, vitamin D tablets.

However, about 30 percent of young people do not take vitamin D supplements. The researchers of this matter want to change this.

Some vitamin D is also obtained from food, for example from dairy products, fats and fish.
