# Monday 23.4.2018 (radio)

A big pulp mill is planned to be built in Kuopio. There are prisoners in Finland who had violent extremist thinking. It is hard for a Finn to decide whether to greet other people or not. Britain has a new little prince.

## Pulp mill

![The site of the softwood pulp mill.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10172795)

Open image viewer

A big pulp mill may be built at this place in Kuopio. Photo: Antti Karhunen / Yle

A big factory is being planned for Kuopio. The Finnish company Finnpulp plans to build a pulp mill in Kuopio.

Finnpulp said on Monday that the Chinese company Hengan International will invest money in the mill. With the money, planning work on the mill can begin.

If the mill is built, it will be the world's biggest softwood pulp mill. The mill will make pulp from softwood, for example pine. Pulp is used for making paper.

Finnpulp estimates that the mill will be ready in 3 years at the earliest.

## Prisoners' extremist thinking

![Riihimäki prison](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10036198)

Open image viewer

There is extremist thinking in Finnish prisons. Photo: Timo Leponiemi / Yle

There are prisoners in Finland who may get involved in terrorist activity.

The Criminal Sanctions Agency investigated the matter in prisons. It found more than 100 prisoners who had violent extremist thinking. Some of the prisoners were interested in Islamism and some in the far right.

Extremist thinking can spread in prison. That is why the Criminal Sanctions Agency thinks it is important to find the prisoners who may follow the bad example of other prisoners.

## Greeting people

![Anja Salmi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10169468)

Open image viewer

Anja Salmi says that she often says hi to people. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

It is hard for a Finn to decide whether to greet other people or not. Yle asked people's opinions about greeting and got more than 600 answers online.

A large part of those who answered wished that people would greet each other more often. Many said that it is nice abroad because people greet each other.

But some of those who answered were pleased that Finns leave each other in peace. In Finland you do not need to be super sociable all the time.

Finns are most annoyed when someone does not answer a greeting. Finns also want to be greeted in customer service situations. Some who answered praised bus drivers with an immigrant background. They often greet passengers.

## A new prince

![Royalist supporters in London.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10173204)

Fans of the royals waited for news of the royal baby outside a London hospital. Photo: Daniel Leal-Olivas / AFP

Britain has a new royal. Prince William and Duchess Catherine had a baby on Monday.

The baby boy was born in a London hospital. Mother and baby are doing well, the British court says.

The new prince is William and Kate's third child and Queen Elizabeth's sixth great-grandchild. The little prince's name will be announced later.
