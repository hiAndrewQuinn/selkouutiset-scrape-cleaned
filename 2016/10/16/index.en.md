# Sunday 16.10.2016 (radio)

The Syria negotiations will continue on Monday. An important climate agreement was made. New medicines for cancer. The ground was white in Lapland.

## The Syria negotiations will continue on Monday

![Representatives of the United States, Russia and several of Syria's neighbouring countries negotiated about the possibilities of a ceasefire in Syria. Ministers around a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9233239)

The United States, Russia and Syria's neighbouring countries negotiated about the situation in Syria on Saturday in Switzerland. Photo: Jean-Christophe Bott / EPA

The situation in Syria was negotiated on Saturday evening in Lausanne, Switzerland.

The negotiations ended without promises of a ceasefire. However, the negotiators said that a ceasefire is possible.

The foreign ministers of Russia and the United States, Sergei Lavrov and John Kerry, discussed ending the violence in Syria.

The negotiations will continue on Monday without the foreign ministers.

In eastern Aleppo the situation is already extremely bad. Food and drinking water may run out soon.

## An important climate agreement was made

![a man repairs cooling equipment in Ahmedabad, India, in May 2015](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9232471)

Open image viewer

A man repairs air conditioners in Ahmedabad, India.  Photo: Divyakant Solanki / EPA

Almost 200 countries accepted an agreement on Saturday that slows down climate warming.

The countries agreed that hydrofluorocarbons, or HFC compounds, will be reduced. They are used in fridges and air conditioners. They are very powerful greenhouse gases and warm the climate.

The United States and the EU countries will reduce the use of HFC compounds by 10 percent by the year 2019.

## New medicines for cancer

![a mole on the skin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9231761)

Open image viewer

Skin cancer can look like this. Photo: AOP

Medical researchers have developed new medicines for treating cancer.

The medicines can prevent cancer from coming back. They are based on the body's own defence system, that is, the immune system.

They can be used to treat, for example, melanoma, lung cancer and breast cancer.

Petri Bono, the leading chief physician at Helsinki University Central Hospital, says that in the future the new medicines may perhaps replace chemotherapy.

## The ground was white in Lapland

![snow on the ground in Inari](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9233469)

Open image viewer

There was snow on the ground in Lapland on Sunday morning. Photo: Jonne Järvinen / Yle

It has already snowed in Lapland. In places the ground had got a thin cover of snow.  However, people only talk about the first snow when there is at least 1 centimetre of snow.

The snow may melt quite soon, because temperatures will stay above zero next week, in Lapland too.

So the weather will continue fairly similar.
