# Saturday 16.2.2019 (radio)

The dispute over the border wall continues in the USA. Nigeria is moving its elections to next week. The Finnish film Hölmö nuori sydän has won the best youth film prize at the Berlin Film Festival. The streets are slippery now.

## Trump's border wall

![Donald Trump](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10649586)

Open image viewer

President Donald Trump. Photo: Jim Lo Scalzo / EPA

The dispute over the border wall continues in the USA.

President Donald Trump wants to build a wall on the border between the USA and Mexico. However, the president has not received enough money from Congress to build the wall. Congress decides on state funds.

On Friday Trump decided that the USA has a national emergency. Trump's reason for the emergency is that criminals and drugs are coming into the USA from Mexico. With the emergency, the president gets more power over state financial matters, and so he can get more money for the wall.

The Democratic Party disagrees with the president. The Democrats say that there is no real emergency in the USA. In the Democrats' view, Trump's decision on the emergency is illegal. It is possible that the matter will still go to court.

## Nigeria's elections

![Muhammadu Buhari](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10643816)

Open image viewer

Nigerian President Muhammadu Buhari at an election event. Photo: EPA

In Africa, Nigeria is moving its elections forward by a week. Nigeria was supposed to hold its presidential and parliamentary elections on Saturday.

However, on Saturday Nigeria said that the elections are only a week away. The election arrangements are not yet ready.

Nigerians fear violence during the elections. The terrorist organisation Boko Haram has carried out attacks in Northern Nigeria for 10 years.

Nigeria is a big country. It has about 190 million inhabitants.

## Film award

![Rosa Honkonen and Jere Ristseppä in the film Hölmö nuori sydän](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10612453)

Open image viewer

Rosa Honkonen and Jere Ristseppä star in the film Hölmö nuori sydän. Photo: Tuffi Films / Uwa Iduozee

A Finnish film has won an award at the Berlin Film Festival in Germany.

The film is called Hölmö nuori sydän. It was the best youth film at the festival. About 60 films made for young people were shown at the festival.

Hölmö nuori sydän is about young people from East Helsinki. The young people fall in love, and a girl becomes pregnant.

The film was written by Kirsikka Saari, and it is directed by Selma Vilhunen.

The Berlin Film Festival is an important international film event. It is held every year.

## Slippery streets

![A man on a slippery street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10649857)

Open image viewer

The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns of slippery streets. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The streets are slippery now. There is a lot of ice on the streets.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns especially pedestrians. A pedestrian may slip and fall on an icy street. That is why people must be careful when they are outdoors.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute also warns of strong wind in Northern Finland. The wind may disturb skiers, for example.

Otherwise the weekend's weather for outdoor activities is good. The weather is quite warm, and the sun is shining.
