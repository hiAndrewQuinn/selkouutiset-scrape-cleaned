# Monday 14.8.2017 (radio)

Train traffic stopped because of a strike. The storm damage is being assessed. The famous Big Ben falls silent because of renovation. This week's weather is quite warm.

## Train traffic stopped because of a strike

![VR passenger trains in the Helsinki rail yard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9766272)

Open image viewer

VR estimates that train traffic will return to normal on Wednesday.  Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

A train drivers' strike has begun in Finland. The strike began at 6 pm. It stops almost all train traffic. Only the Allegro and Tolstoi trains going to Russia run normally.  The strike ends on Tuesday at midnight. It is estimated that train traffic will return to normal on Wednesday.

VR estimates that over a thousand train services must be cancelled because of the strike. The train drivers are holding the strike because they oppose the Finnish government's plan to open passenger traffic to competition.

During the strike there are more buses and taxis on the move than normal. Traffic may be congested, for example near Helsinki on Tuesday morning and afternoon. The authorities say that it is a good idea to go to work, for example, by bike or by bus.

## Kiira's damage is being assessed

![Roof panels that have come loose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9772875)

Open image viewer

In Helsinki, Kiira tore the roof off a building.  Photo: Petri Kejonen / Yle

The damage from the weekend storm is being assessed in different parts of southern Finland. The storm Kiira destroyed a lot of forest, for example in Uusimaa and Kymenlaakso. Experts say that it is still too early to give the exact amount of damage to trees. It can be estimated better at the end of this week.

In some areas the ground is wet, and that hinders repairing the storm damage.

Kiira also destroyed a lot of power lines. In Kymenlaakso alone, the damage to the power network is over 100,000 euros. Because of the damage, people have started calling insurance companies. Their customer services have been busy.

## London's Big Ben falls silent for several years

![The British flag Big Ben](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9535733)

Open image viewer

Big Ben's renovation begins on Monday.  Photo: Hannah McKay /EPA

In London, the famous clock of the parliament building, Big Ben, falls silent for several years. The clock will be repaired and the work will take four years.

Big Ben is at the top of the Elizabeth Tower. Big Ben's chimes are familiar around the world, because the broadcasting company BBC has used them in its broadcasts.

## Summer weather expected this week

![Weather maps from Tuesday to Thursday, 15-17 August. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9774004)

Open image viewer

The holidays are coming to an end, but the weather is summery.  Photo: Yle

The weather this week is quite warm. Yle meteorologist Seija Paasonen says that temperatures this week are higher than normal for this time of year.

Daytime temperatures may be 23 degrees in the southern and central parts of the country and 20 degrees in the north. Early in the week there will be only a little rain, but at the end of the week there may be a little more rain.
