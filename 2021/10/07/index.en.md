## Nobel Prize in Literature

![Nobel Prize in Literature winner Abdulrazak Gurnah.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12132919)

Open image viewer

Nobel winner Abdulrazak Gurnah. Photo: Brigitte Friedrich / AOP

The Nobel Prize in Literature goes to Abdulrazak Gurnah this year.

Gurnah is 72 years old. He was born in Africa, in Tanzania.

Gurnah came to Britain as a refugee more than 50 years ago. Gurnah has also written a lot about the life of refugees.

Gurnah has been a professor of literature at a British university. He writes in English.

## Earthquake in Pakistan

An earthquake has happened in Pakistan. Pakistan is a neighbouring country of India.

At least 20 people died in the earthquake. More than 200 people were injured. The earthquake also damaged houses.

Rescue work is difficult, because transport and phone connections to the earthquake area are poor.

## Party support

![September 2021 party support graphic. The National Coalition is in the lead.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12130473)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

In Finland there have been no big changes in party support.

This is what Yle's new support poll says. In the poll, people were asked which party they support.

The largest party in the poll is the National Coalition. Its support is about 21 per cent.

The second largest party in the poll is the SDP, and the third largest is the Finns Party.

The SDP's support is a little over 19 per cent, and the Finns Party's support is a little under 18 per cent.

The National Coalition and the Finns Party are opposition parties, that is, they are not in government. The SDP is the party of Prime Minister Sanna Marin.

Among the government parties, support for the Greens has fallen, but support for the Left Alliance has risen.

The support figures for all parties can be found on Yle's website.

Yle's Teksti-TV turns 40. Teksti-TV's service began on 7 October 1981.

People can read news and sports results, for example, on Teksti-TV's pages.

Finns today have many kinds of smart devices, but Teksti-TV is still popular. Every week more than a million Finns read Teksti-TV's pages.

## Friday's weather

The weather is windy and rainy.

On Friday the rain area moves across the northern part of the country from west to east.

In Northern Lapland the temperature is close to zero, and the rain is sleet or snow. The driving conditions may therefore be poor.

In the southern part of the country the weather is dry and warm. The temperature is close to 15 degrees.

On the west coast the sun may shine a little.
