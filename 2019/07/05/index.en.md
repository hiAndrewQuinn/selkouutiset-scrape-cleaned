# Friday 5.7.2019 (radio)

The European Commission has visited Finland. The Red Cross wants countries to bring their citizens home from the al-Hol camp. A UN report criticises Venezuela. The citizens' initiative to improve the protection of the Saimaa ringed seal is moving on to Parliament. The Ruisrock festival has started in Turku.

## The European Commission in Finland

![The Finnish government and the Commission's guests in a group photo at the House of Estates](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10864148)

The European Commission has visited Helsinki today, Friday. Finland started its presidency of the European Union this week. The EU Commission usually visits right away in the new presidency country.

In the morning the commissioners met members of the Finnish government at the House of Estates. After that the commissioners moved to the Presidential Palace, where the programme included lunch with President Sauli Niinistö.

The day's programme ended with the commissioners visiting Parliament. There they met the Grand Committee, which decides on EU matters, and the Speaker's board.

## The Red Cross and the al-Hol camp

![Women in the back of a truck](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10798220)

Open image viewer

According to the Red Cross, there are 100,000 people in al-Hol and the camps around it. Photo: Antti Kuronen / Yle

The International Red Cross asks countries to bring their citizens home from the al-Hol camp in Syria. The camp holds family members of Isis fighters. There are also Finnish women and children in the camp.

The Red Cross says that conditions in the al-Hol camp are terrible. People should be returned to their home countries as soon as possible. The organisation warns that those who are left in the camp will face great difficulties.

As an example, it mentions violence against political opponents and activists, which has included torture and killings.

## Protecting the Saimaa ringed seal

![A Saimaa ringed seal in April 2019 on Lake Saimaa south of Puumala.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10744956)

Open image viewer

A Saimaa ringed seal in April 2019 on Lake Saimaa south of Puumala. Photo: Ismo Marttinen

Citizens want to improve the protection of the Saimaa ringed seal.

A bill on protecting the Saimaa ringed seal, that is, a citizens' initiative, has received more than 50,000 signatures. The citizens' initiative can therefore be discussed in Parliament.

The citizens' initiative says that gillnet fishing must be banned for the whole year if seals live in the area. At present, gillnet fishing in the Saimaa ringed seal's habitats is banned only in spring.

This week at least 4 seal pups have already died in Saimaa. The pups had become entangled in fishing nets.

Saimaa ringed seals are found only in Finland.

## Ruisrock gets going

![Audience at the Ruisrock stage in 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10385737)

Open image viewer

The Ruisrock audience in front of the stage in 2018. Photo: Juuli Aschan / Yle

The 50th Ruisrock festival started today, Friday, on Ruissalo in Turku.

The festival of the anniversary year is sold out in advance. A total of 105,000 visitors are expected at Ruissalo over the weekend.

Ruisrock is one of Finland's oldest festivals.
