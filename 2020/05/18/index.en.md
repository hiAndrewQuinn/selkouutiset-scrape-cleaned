## Finnair is adding flights in July

![Finnair aircraft at Helsinki-Vantaa airport in May. The planes are on the ground because of the coronavirus pandemic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_419/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6726665eb52ff57d5bf/1588933643)

Finnair is adding flights because many countries are easing corona restrictions. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The airline Finnair is adding some flights from the beginning of July.

Finnair's planes will fly to big European cities in July.

In addition, Finnair will also start flying some long-haul flights to Asia.

Finnair estimates that in July it will fly about a third of its flights.

## Many pensioners want to work

Kurt Lindberg does freelance jobs. Photo: Tommi Penttinen

Many pensioners want to work.

Statistics Finland says that over 100,000 pensioners are working.

In addition, there are as many as 30,000 more pensioners who want to work.

Kurt Lindberg has been retired for many years. He does freelance jobs, for example garden work.

*Kurt Lindberg says: I intend to carry on for as long as calls come in and I am taken on for work. It is nice to work and meet people. If I end up lying on the bed bottom, then that's where I will be.*

## Young people can also be in a risk group

![Eetu Louhe sits in a kitchen chair and edits photos on his laptop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_88/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6756045ebcffca9c66c/1589445088)

Open image viewer

Eetu Louhe is in a risk group because he has asthma. Photo: Kimmo Gustafsson / Yle

Young people can also be at risk because of the coronavirus.

There are many young people who have a serious underlying illness. They belong to the risk group for the coronavirus.

An illness does not always show on the outside, and it can be hard for others to understand that a young person is ill.

The coronavirus can be dangerous if a person has asthma, for example, or if they have had an organ transplant.

## Trotting races start

![Harri Koivunen drives a trotting horse on a training track.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4644,h_2612,x_0,y_239/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6773875ebfd7525881a/1589630865)

Open image viewer

Harri Koivunen drives a trotting horse. Photo: Paula Collin / Yle

Horses and drivers can compete now that the corona restrictions on trotting races have been eased.

Trotting races are usually run many times a week. Now they can be held 3 times a week.

However, no audience is allowed in the stands.

Trotting races were given a special permit to start already now, before other sports.

From the beginning of June, other sports will also be allowed to start competitions and matches.

## Sales of menthol tobacco end

![cigarette butt on a rock](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10849006)

Open image viewer

Menthol tobacco may no longer be sold. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

The ban on selling menthol tobacco comes into force the day after tomorrow, on Wednesday.

The new Tobacco Act says that cigarettes or rolling tobacco that have a smell or a flavour may no longer be sold to consumers.

This refers, for example, to additives with a menthol flavour.

In Finland, all other flavours in tobacco have been banned since 2016.
