## The border between Poland and Belarus

![Migrants at the border between Poland and Belarus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1799,h_1007,x_0,y_187/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-877728618a9faa503ba/1636475222)

People at the border between Belarus and Poland. Photo: EPA-EFE / Leonid Scheglov

The European Union and Belarus are in a dispute.

The reason for the dispute is a large group of people who are trying to get from Belarus to Poland. Poland is a member state of the European Union.

Poland says that the situation at the border between Poland and Belarus is a danger to the whole European Union. Poland says that Belarus is deliberately sending people towards the European Union. Among the group there are people, for example, from the Middle East and Africa.

The European Union thinks that Belarus is trying to disturb EU countries that have criticised Belarus's poor human rights situation.

## Finland's corona situation

![Sanna Marin speaks to the media.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8506426136fc56cbf95/1630993902)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin's government is negotiating about the corona situation on Wednesday. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finland's corona situation has worsened. There are now more corona patients in hospital care than before.

The number of new corona infections is also high. Corona is now spreading in particular to people who have not received corona vaccinations.

About 80 per cent of over-12-year-olds have received corona vaccinations. That was the government's goal, so that all corona restrictions can be ended.

However, it is possible that the government will continue the corona restrictions for restaurants because of the poor corona situation. The government is negotiating on the matter on Wednesday.

## Denmark's corona situation

![Mette Frederiksen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4172,h_2347,x_1191,y_909/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6525575e6a132e7b72b/1584010290)

Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen told that the corona pass will be used again in Denmark. Archive picture. Photo: Liselotte Sabroe / EPA

The corona situation in Denmark is also worsening. There are many new corona infections in the country.

Because of the poor corona situation, the corona pass is being brought back into use in Denmark.

Denmark ended corona restrictions in September. At that time the use of the corona pass was also ended.

## Pensioners working

![Patient and nurse.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_131/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-860985615687adeb5f4/1633066214)

Open image viewer

Pensioners are helpful, for example, in the health care field. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Many pensioners want to work. This is what a survey by the pension institution Keva says.

The survey says that about 25 per cent of pensioners are interested in working. About 10 per cent of pensioners already work regularly.

Many pensioners want to work for money. In addition, work gives the pensioner a feeling that they are useful.

The problem, in the view of many pensioners, is that suitable work is difficult to find.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12181076)

Open image viewer

Tuesday morning was the coldest of the autumn. In Kittilä in Lapland, 22.2 degrees of frost was measured. On Tuesday, however, the weather has warmed, and snow and rain showers have moved across Finland.

On Wednesday it is above zero across almost the whole country, in the south even up to 10 degrees. In the west the sun may shine a little, but otherwise it is cloudy. In the east and south a little rain falls.
