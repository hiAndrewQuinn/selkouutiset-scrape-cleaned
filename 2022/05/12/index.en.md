Finland's security policy is changing. Finland will probably apply for membership of the military alliance NATO.

President Sauli Niinistö and Prime Minister Sanna Marin told their own opinion on NATO membership on Thursday. They want Finland to join the military alliance NATO.

The President and the Prime Minister say that it is good for Finland to apply for NATO membership quickly. Niinistö and Marin say that NATO membership will improve Finland's security.

Finland may apply for NATO membership perhaps as early as next week. Before that, Parliament will deal with the matter.

Finland began to consider NATO membership when Russia attacked Ukraine in February. NATO has 30 member countries, which defend each other.

## Picture of the black hole in the Milky Way

![Plasma ring and shadow](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12443511)

Open image viewer

Black hole in the middle of the Milky Way. Photo: EHT collaboration

Researchers have got a rare picture. The picture is of a black hole, which is in the middle of the Milky Way.

The gravity of black holes is so big that not even light can escape from them. That is why they look black.

Black holes are often in the centres of galaxies, that is, star systems. The Milky Way is the galaxy in which our own Sun and Earth are located.

It was difficult to get a picture of the black hole in the Milky Way. It took the 8 best radio telescopes in the world, which are located in different parts of the globe.

Researchers from many countries, also from Finland, took part in the work. The picture of the black hole in the Milky Way was presented on Thursday in Washington, USA.
