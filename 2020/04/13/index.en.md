# Monday 13.4.2020 (radio)

Spain starts carefully opening its economy today. Police have had to break up gatherings of young people in Pori. There are now more than 3,000 confirmed coronavirus infections in Finland. Blood donors are needed during the corona epidemic too.

## Spain is lifting its orders

![Police handing out respiratory masks at Atocha station in Madrid.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11304114)

Spanish police handed out respiratory masks at Atocha station in Madrid on Monday. Photo: Javier Soriano / AFP

Spain starts carefully opening its economy today. Spain has suffered a lot from the corona crisis.

For example, some workers at factories and building sites are going back to work. Two weeks ago, Spain closed all activity that is not essential.

Much of the movement restrictions still remain in force. Spain has about 47 million residents.

In Denmark, children under 12 return to school on Wednesday. Day care centres will also open. The Danish government believes that the spread of the coronavirus is under control.

Denmark wants to get the economy going again.

In Austria, some shops may open tomorrow. The government's permission applies to smaller shops; larger shops will stay closed until at least early May.

Restaurants and hotels may open in mid-May.

## The police have broken up gatherings of young people in Pori

![Police cars parked in Pori.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10531140)

Open image viewer

Police cars in Pori. Photo: Juuso Kuusisto / Yle

The police have had to break up gatherings of young people in Pori, in Satakunta.

The police say that as many as 100 young people have been gathering on many evenings in Pori. There have been arguments and threats in the group. The police have broken up the group 3 times. A lot of police officers were needed.

The police have also had to step in to young people's gatherings in Ostrobothnia.

The police say that guardians must make sure that children do not take part in gatherings.

Unnecessary hanging around in public places must be avoided. Gatherings of more than 10 people are banned because of the coronavirus.

## There are more than 3,000 coronavirus infections in Finland

![Ward for testing and treating coronavirus patients at the Turku University Central Hospital, Tyks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11302937)

Open image viewer

Ward for testing and treating coronavirus patients at the Turku University Central Hospital, Tyks. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

There are now more than 3,000 confirmed coronavirus infections in Finland. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that there have been 90 new infections since yesterday.

There may be more infections, because not everyone has been tested.

The number of infections is highest in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district, both in numbers and in relation to the population.

59 people have died of the disease caused by the coronavirus. The number has risen by 3 since yesterday.

## The Blood Service needs donors

![Blood donation](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9995372)

Open image viewer

Photo: Eveliina Matikainen / Yle

The Finnish Red Cross Blood Service reminds people that blood donors are needed during the corona epidemic too.

Even though non-urgent operations are not being carried out, hundreds of blood donors are still needed every day.

The Blood Service says that coming to donate blood is safe. Donating blood does not weaken your resistance.

The Blood Service says that it is a good idea to book a donation time in advance. Then donating goes smoothly.
