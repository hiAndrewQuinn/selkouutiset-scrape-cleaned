# Sunday 24.1.2021 (radio)

President Niinistö. Arrests in Russia. Estonia's government. Chinese mine accident. Snowfalls.

## Russia's situation

![Police hit a protester with a baton, with police and protesters in the background](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3909,h_2199,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-765657600d62a42311d/1611496942)

Police used harsh methods against demonstrators in Russia. Photo: Maxim Shipenkov / EPA

Finland's President Sauli Niinistö says that the political situation in Russia is serious. Niinistö wrote about the matter on Twitter.

In Niinistö's opinion, the foreign ministers of the EU countries must think together about how to react to the situation in Russia. The foreign ministers of the EU countries have a meeting on Monday.

There were big demonstrations in Russia on Saturday. The police arrested more than 3,000 demonstrators.

The demonstrators wanted to support the opposition leader Aleksei Navalnyi. The police arrested Navalnyi a week ago.

Navalnyi is a critic of President Vladimir Putin. Last summer an attempt was made to kill Navalnyi with poison.

## Estonia's government

![Kaja Kallas, who is rising to become Estonia's prime minister, photographed on Sunday 24 January 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2736,h_1539,x_0,y_286/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-765655600d5c0971b82/1611488364)

Open image viewer

Kaja Kallas is Estonia's new prime minister. Photo: Siim Lõvi / Estonian Public Broadcasting

Finland's neighbour Estonia is getting a new government. The ministers of the government were announced on Sunday.

The prime minister of the new government is Kaja Kallas. Kallas is Estonia's first female prime minister.

Kallas belongs to the Reform Party. Kallas's ministers are from the Reform Party and the Centre Party.

Estonia's previous government resigned because of a suspicion of corruption. The prime minister of the previous government was from the Centre Party, which is suspected of corruption.

## Chinese mine accident

![Various work machines and rescue workers in a barren landscape](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3774,h_2123,x_0,y_195/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-765587600cfb5066f33/1611463658)

Open image viewer

Rescuers dug shafts through which food and medicine were brought underground. Photo: All Over Press

Miners have been rescued from deep underground in China.

The men were in a gold mine where an accident happened 2 weeks ago. In the accident more than 20 miners were trapped underground.

China has now managed to rescue 11 miners. They were almost 600 metres deep.

Rescuers made contact with the men a week ago. After that, food and medicine were successfully delivered to them through the shafts.

Mine accidents are common in China.

## Fire in Salo

![Traces of a fire in an apartment block in Salo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11753680)

Open image viewer

The fire was on the top floor of the apartment block. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

There has been a serious fire in Salo, in Southern Finland. 2 people died in the fire.

The fire was in a flat on the top floor of the apartment block, on the 4th floor. The residents of the other flats in the stairwell were moved to safety.

The dead were an old woman and an old man. In addition, 1 person was injured in the fire.

The police and the rescue service are investigating what the cause of the fire was.

Snowfalls continue.

On Monday it will snow on the coast of Western Finland and in Northern Finland.

On Tuesday it will snow in Lapland. In addition, new snowfalls will come on Tuesday towards the eastern and southern parts of the country.

The weather is mild, which means that in most of the country there is no frost. There is frost only in Lapland.
