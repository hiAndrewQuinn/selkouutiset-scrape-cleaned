# Tuesday 16.1.2018 (radio)

Yle asked Finland's MEPs about Finland's net payment to the European Union. Electricity bills have become more expensive. Osuuspankki's balance errors have been fixed. The name of the maternity package will not change.

## Finland will lose support because of Brexit

![The euro sculpture in front of the central bank](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10023842)

Britain leaving the European Union reduces the EU's budget. The reduction is more than 10 billion euros. So the regional support and farm support that Finland gets may fall.

Yle asked Finland's MEPs about the payment shares.

The MEPs of the National Coalition Party, the Social Democrats and the Greens accept that Finland's net payments will grow. However, they demand conditions. They demand that EU funds must be used efficiently. They also demand that support is reduced for those countries that go against the EU's rule of law principle. For example, not all countries take care of refugees.

The MEPs of the Finns Party, the Left Alliance and the Centre Party oppose increasing the net payment. The MEP of the Swedish People's Party and one Centre Party MEP are still thinking about their position on the net payment.

The electricity bill has become on average 20 euros more expensive

![Doing laundry.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9809583)

Open image viewer

Last year Finns paid about 20 euros more for electricity than in 2016. Photo: AOP

Last year Finns' electricity bill grew by an average of 20 euros a year. The rise is caused by electricity transmission, which became more expensive. The rise was just under 4 percent in houses heated by electricity and 6 percent in other households.

The price of electrical energy fell, and so the consumer's electricity bill did not rise more. Finland's electricity production fell slightly last year. Consumption, on the other hand, rose a little.

Electricity imports from Sweden fell, and electricity was imported from Russia at the same rate as before. In the winter season, electricity must be imported from neighbouring countries when consumption is high.

## Osuuspankki balances are in order again

![ The OP Group logo in a window ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9621826)

Open image viewer

A technical fault took money from customers' accounts. The fault has now been fixed, and the account information is correct. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Osuuspankki says that all errors in customers' balances have been fixed.

On Monday, customers' accounts showed a smaller balance than in reality. The fault was caused by an error in the bank's information system. The cause is being investigated.

## The maternity package keeps its familiar name

![Maternity package, year 2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9494505)

Open image viewer

The maternity package has lots of necessary items and clothes for a baby. Every pregnant woman or adoptive parent living in Finland gets the package. The package has more than 50 products in total. Photo: Kela

The name of the maternity package will stay. In November, Kela did a survey about a new name for the package. People could also support the current name. Kela considered whether the name of the maternity package should take all families into account better. Kela also considered whether the current name discriminates against fathers.

Almost 30,000 name suggestions came in. Almost 60 percent of those who answered wanted the name of the maternity package to stay the same. The suggestions were delivered to Minister of Family Affairs and Social Services Annika Saarikko. Saarikko says that, based on the results, changing the name is not on the agenda.

Besides the name maternity package, votes went to, for example, baby package, family package, parents' package, love package and first package. Also "vauvakka", baby box, paternity package and "vaaviloota" (baby crate) got support. The name of the maternity package is in the Maternity Grants Act. So only Parliament can change the name of the package, Kela says.
