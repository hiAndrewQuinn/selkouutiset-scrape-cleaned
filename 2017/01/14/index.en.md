# Saturday 14.1.2017 (radio)

President Sauli Niinistö is worried about nasty behaviour on social media. Cold winter weather is causing trouble for refugees in Southern Europe. At the Venla gala, prizes were given to last year's best TV programmes. 18-year-old Joni Saarinen is successful in downhill skating.

## The President hopes for better behaviour

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9404185)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

President Sauli Niinistö is worried about nasty behaviour on social media. The President thinks it is really sad that many people try to insult other people on purpose.

Niinistö hopes that people will behave better. It is not very difficult, the President says. He also says that people themselves suffer if they insult others.

However, Niinistö believes that most Finns feel fairly comfortable in today's Finland.

President Niinistö talked about these things on television on Saturday, when he was a guest on the programme Ykkösaamu.

## Asylum seekers suffer from the cold

![The frozen Danube in Belgrade, Serbia. The Danube last froze more than 15 years ago. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9396760)

It has been cold in the Balkans. The picture is from Belgrade, Serbia. Photo: EPA / Andrej Cukic

The UN, or United Nations, is worried about asylum seekers in Europe's cold winter. The UN thinks that European countries do not take good enough care of the people who are trying to get to Europe.

For example, 2 Iraqi men and a Somali woman have been found dead in Bulgaria. They maybe died of the cold.

In Greece and the Balkans, too, the winter has been cold and a lot of snow has fallen. Many asylum seekers live in cold tents.

## Prizes for TV programmes

![Arman Alizad ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9403886)

Open image viewer

Arman Alizad is Finland's best TV presenter. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finland's best television programmes of last year have received prizes. The prizes were given at the Venla gala on Friday.

The best drama series was Sorjonen. It is a crime series that has been shown on Yle's channels. The crime series Sorjonen also won other prizes at the Venla gala. Ville Virtanen and Anu Sinisalo act in the main roles of the series. They got the prizes for best actor and best actress.

TV viewers also chose their own favourite programme. The viewers thought that the best TV programme of the year was the hospital series Syke. It has been shown on Yle's channels.

Viewers voted Arman Alizad the best TV presenter of the year. Alizad is originally from Iran. He has done TV work for a long time. Now Alizad has his own programme on the TV channel Nelonen.

## Victory for a Finnish skater

![Joni Saarinen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9404149)

Open image viewer

Joni Saarinen was the best junior in a downhill skating World Championship round in France. Photo: Samo Vidic/Red Bull Content Pool

And finally, a happy sports news item.

18-year-old Joni Saarinen has been successful in downhill skating. Saarinen won the juniors' World Championship round, which was held now for the first time. The competition was in Marseille, France.

In downhill skating, skaters race at high speed down a track that also has bends and jumps. A skater's speed can be more than 80 kilometres an hour.
