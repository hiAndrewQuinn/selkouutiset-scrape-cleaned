# Saturday 16.7.2022

The National Coalition Party is still the most popular party. Biden criticised Saudi Arabia over the murder of journalist Khashoggi. Forest fires are raging in Spain, Portugal and France. The Athletics World Championships started. The weather is rainy.

The National Coalition Party is still the most popular party in Finland. In the Helsingin Sanomat survey the National Coalition Party's support is 23.9 percent. The National Coalition Party's support fell a little from the previous survey.

Second is the SDP. The SDP's support is 19.9 percent.

The third most popular party is the Finns Party. The Finns Party's support is 14.7 percent.

US President Joe Biden has visited Saudi Arabia.

Biden spoke in Saudi Arabia about the murder of journalist Jamal Khashoggi. The US information says that Saudi Arabian crown prince Mohammed bin Salman approved Khashoggi's murder. The Saudi journalist Khashoggi was murdered at the Saudi Arabian consulate in Istanbul in 2018.

## Heat in Southern Europe

![Smoke clouds rise above Fuengirola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1536,h_864,x_0,y_492/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98276362d19b41622a0/1657904088)

Open image viewer

Smoke from terrain fires rose above Fuengirola on Friday. Photo: Jyri Kataja-Rahko / Yle

Heat and forest fires still plague Southern Europe.

Thousands of people have fled forest fires in France, Spain and Portugal. In these countries there have been many days of over 40 degrees.

In Portugal and Spain over 300 people have died because of the heat. In south-western France over 11,000 people have fled forest fires.

## Athletics World Championships

![Wilma Murto clears the bar in the pole vault qualifying at the Oregon World Championships.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3925,h_2207,x_0,y_48/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98281562d22bfdc9c58/1657945200)

Open image viewer

Wilma Murto clears the bar in the pole vault qualifying at the Oregon World Championships. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The Athletics World Championships have started in Oregon, USA.

Finland's Krista Tervo and Silja Kosonen made it to the hammer throw final. Krista Tervo was 3rd in the qualifying and Silja Kosonen was 7th. 12 women got into Sunday's final.

Wilma Murto got into the pole vault final. The final is between Sunday and Monday night. 15 athletes got into the final.

On Saturday evening 6 Finns compete in the Athletics World Championships.

The Athletics World Championships continue until 24 July. A total of 36 Finnish athletes take part.

The rain weakens overnight. Still it rains in almost the whole country. Only in Southern Lapland is it dry.

On Sunday it rains most in Eastern and Northern Finland. In places there is thunder. During the day it is only 9 degrees in some places. The highest temperature is about 19 degrees.
