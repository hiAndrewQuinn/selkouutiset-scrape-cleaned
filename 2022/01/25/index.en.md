In the regional elections the day before yesterday, 21 regional councils were chosen.

Information about the values of the councils is already available. The values have been looked into from the answers to Yle's election compass.

Most regional councils are right-liberal.

The most conservative council will be in South Ostrobothnia, and the most liberal in West Uusimaa.

The regional councils are responsible for social and health services and for rescue services.

## Corruption

![The Finnish flag flies in Helsinki in December 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4695,h_2641,x_250,y_313/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7573455fe359db8b2df/1608737205)

Finland has very little corruption, that is, bribery, says an organisation that monitors corruption.

Finland, Denmark and New Zealand each got 88 points in a new report.

100 points means that there is no corruption at all. Zero points means that there is an extremely large amount of corruption.

Last year Denmark and New Zealand shared the top places. Now Finland gained 3 points and reached the shared top place.

Next come Norway, Singapore, Sweden, Switzerland, the Netherlands, Luxembourg and Germany.

In the list of 180 countries, the weakest was South Sudan, which got 11 points. Just above it come Somalia, Syria, Venezuela, Yemen, North Korea and Afghanistan.

The United States has now fallen out of the top 25 for the first time.

## Speaker of Parliament

![Anu Vehviläinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3482,h_1959,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-872210617904ccc5cff/1635321081)

Speaker of Parliament Anu Vehviläinen is leaving her post.

Vehviläinen of the Centre Party has been Speaker for a year and a half. She will no longer stand for election.

Vehviläinen hopes that the Centre Party's parliamentary group will propose MP Matti Vanhanen for the post.

Vanhanen says that he is ready for the post.

A new Speaker will be elected at the beginning of February.

## Safety park

![Artist's impression of a hall with cars and people.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90619461efa9dd67af1/1643096705)

Open image viewer

Artist's impression of the safety park being built in Kotka. Vehicles of various kinds can be driven into the warehouse hall, for example. Photo: FCG Finnish Consulting Group Oy

Europe's first port logistics safety park is being set up in Finland. The Kotka city council decided on the matter yesterday.

The safety park will be built in Kotka's Kantasatama in Kymenlaakso. In the safety park, authorities, students and port workers can practise dangerous situations.

Finland already has two construction-sector safety parks, in Espoo and Oulu. Thousands of trainees visit the parks.

The purpose of the parks is to improve occupational safety. Accidents happen most in the port and logistics field, right after the construction field.
