# Monday 6.12.2021 (radio)

Independence Day. The President couple. Independence Day events for the poor. Demonstrations. Frost.

## Independence Day

Finland has celebrated Independence Day.

Because of corona, the celebrations have been different from usual.

For example, the reception at the Presidential Palace was cancelled. This year nobody saw the president couple shaking hands with guests.

However, Yle made a programme from the Presidential Palace. In the programme, President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio were interviewed.

The president couple spoke, for example, about the corona time. The President said that during the corona time we have learned to value everyone's work. The president couple also thanked health care workers.

The programme also met a war veteran and a Lotta. They defended Finland in the war more than 70 years ago.

Veterans are former soldiers. Lottas are women who helped the army, for example as nurses and cooks.

Finns respect veterans and Lottas, because they helped Finland to stay independent.

Events for poor people were also held on Independence Day.

In Helsinki the traditional Independence Day event for poor people was this time held outdoors at Rautatientori because of corona.

The Independence Day event for poor people is a tradition that was started by Veikko Hursti. He wanted everyone to have the chance to celebrate Independence Day.

This year an Independence Day event for poor people was also held in Tampere.

One of the Independence Day demonstrations in Helsinki. Photo: Tuomas Rimpiläinen / Yle

On Independence Day there were also student processions in different parts of Finland.

The students marched, for example, in central Helsinki. At the Presidential Palace the students greeted President Sauli Niinistö.

In Helsinki there were also demonstration processions. The demonstrators were completely against each other, for example, about immigration.

The police made sure that the different demonstration processions did not meet each other.

## Frost

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12218740)

Open image viewer

Independence Day has been celebrated in frosty weather. Across the whole country there has been frost between 10 and 30 degrees. On the morning of Independence Day, 35 degrees of frost was measured in Muonio in Lapland.

The frost continues in the coming days. On Tuesday and Wednesday the frost in the south is between 10 and 20 degrees, and in the north between 20 and 30 degrees.
