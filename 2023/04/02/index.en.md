## Parliamentary elections

![Petteri Orpo raises his hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690199)

Open image viewer

The chairman of the National Coalition Party, Petteri Orpo, was happy about the election victory. Photo: Lehtikuva / Antti Aimo-Koivisto

Sunday has been election day in Finland.

Finns have voted in the parliamentary elections. 200 MPs will be elected to Parliament.

The election result is fairly even. There are no big differences between the big parties.

The most votes went to the National Coalition, the Finns Party and the SDP. Each of them got about 20 percent of the votes.

The National Coalition and the Finns Party will get the most MPs in the new Parliament. They are now opposition parties, that is, they are not in government.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin's party, the SDP, is the third largest party in the new Parliament. So it seems that Finland's government will change.

The parties will start negotiating a new government right after the election. The negotiations will be led by the National Coalition, because it is the largest party in the new Parliament.

## Tornadoes in the USA

![A car that has been wrecked in a storm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690201)

Open image viewer

Tornadoes have caused a lot of damage in the USA. Photo: Peter Zay / AOP

There have been tornadoes, that is, whirlwind storms, in the USA. There have been many dozens of tornadoes over the weekend.

Almost 30 people have died because of tornadoes. There are many injured.

The tornado season begins in the USA in April. Tornadoes form in thunderstorms, and there have been many thunderstorms in the USA this year.

## Palm Sunday

![Children in Easter costumes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690202)

Open image viewer

Palm Sunday willow-branch children. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

Palm Sunday has been celebrated in Finland. The Finnish Palm Sunday tradition is virpominen, which means children going round with willow twigs.

Virpojat are children who go round, for example dressed as Easter witches, and use willow branches to tap relatives and neighbours. The virpojat give willow branches and get chocolate eggs, for example, as payment.

Palm Sunday is celebrated one week before Easter.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690203)

At the start of the week the weather is dry, that is, no rain or snow falls. The sun shines across most of the country.

The nights are cold, but in the daytime the temperature rises a little above zero across the whole country.

At the end of the week the weather gets warmer, and Easter is spent in spring weather.
