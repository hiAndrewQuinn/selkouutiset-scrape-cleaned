# Monday 27.3.2023 (radio)

Advance voting. Election debate in easy Finnish. Digital skills of older people. Poor driving conditions. The weather.

## Advance voting

![Voting booths.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690040)

Open image viewer

Advance voting places are open until Tuesday 28.3. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Advance voting in the parliamentary election has been popular. On Monday afternoon it was reported that already 1.2 million people have voted in advance.

Advance voting continues on Tuesday. Then it is still possible to vote at some of the advance voting places, which are, for example, in libraries and shopping centres.

The actual election day for the parliamentary election is on Sunday, that is, on 2 April.

## Clear-language election debate

![Two men and two women.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690037)

Open image viewer

Tuukka Lukinmaa, Petri Strömberg, Janica Hirvenkivi and Mohor Banerjee discuss election themes in plain language. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

The Clear News election debate has been published. The clear-language election debate was broadcast on TV1 today, on Monday.

People who also use plain language in their own lives took part in the election debate. They discussed election themes in plain language with journalist Tuukka Lukinmaa.

The programme talked, for example, about Finland's security and how Russia's attack on Ukraine has affected Finns.

One theme of the debate was Finland's money shortage. The debaters considered where the state can save, and where, in the debaters' view, the state must not save.

The programme can still be watched on Yle Areena. It can be found on Areena by searching for "Eduskuntavaalit 2023 selkosuomeksi", which means "Parliamentary elections 2023 in plain language".

## Digital skills of older people

![Päivi Topo outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_40/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-952974627a7396ae90f/1726820285)

Open image viewer

Ombudsman for Older People Päivi Topo. Photo: Patrik Molander / Yle

Older people need support with digital skills.

This is what the Ombudsman for Older People, Päivi Topo, says. The task of the Ombudsman for Older People is to promote the rights of older people.

Statistics Finland says that a large part of older people have never used the internet. In 2021 using the internet was unfamiliar to about 30 percent of older people aged between 75 and 89.

Many people contact the Ombudsman for Older People, because digital services in particular cause problems.

This week it snows on many days. The most snow falls during the early part of the week, in the east and south.

It snows so much that driving conditions on the roads are poor. Warnings about poor driving conditions have been issued in different parts of the country.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690039)

Open image viewer

The weather forecast says that on Tuesday cloud cover is heavy in the southern and central parts of the country, and in places snow falls.

In the north the weather is fair, and in Lapland the sun shines.

In the south the temperature is close to zero. Elsewhere on Tuesday there is frost of 1–10 degrees.

On Wednesday snow also falls in the north. In the east the temperature may rise above zero, elsewhere there is still a little frost.
