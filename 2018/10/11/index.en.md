# Thursday 11.10.2018 (radio)

Prime Minister Sipilä intends to ask Parliament whether it trusts the government's employment policy. The launch of a space rocket failed in Kazakhstan. A hurricane has caused damage in the USA. Finns do not drink alcohol very often. The weather is getting warmer.

## The government's employment policy

![Prime Minister Juha Sipilä, Labour Minister Jari Lindström and the chair of the National Coalition parliamentary group Kalle Jokinen arrived to meet the media in the State Hall of Parliament before Question Time](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10452820)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä.

The government will not withdraw its proposal on the dismissal law. With the new law the government wants to make it easier to dismiss employees in small firms. Trade unions oppose the bill.

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä now intends to ask Parliament whether it trusts the government's employment policy. Making dismissals easier is part of the employment policy, Sipilä says. Parliament will vote on the government's confidence next week.

The government hopes that the trade unions' protests will decrease if Parliament supports the government. Trade unions have, for example, organised strikes against making dismissals easier.

## The launch of a space rocket failed

![Launch of a Soyuz rocket on 11 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10451675)

Open image viewer

A Soyuz rocket was launched on Thursday. Photo: Bill Ingalls / NASA

The launch of a space rocket has failed in Kazakhstan. Kazakhstan lies between Russia and China.

A Soyuz rocket was launched into space from Kazakhstan. Soon after the launch, an engine fault was noticed. Because of this, the rocket's crew had to make an emergency landing.

There were 2 crew members on the rocket. They did not die. They were saved by using the capsule, which had a parachute.

One crew member was American and the other was Russian. They were on their way to the International Space Station.

## Hurricane in the USA

![In the picture, there are signs, boards and other collapsed debris in front of a building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10451241)

Open image viewer

Hurricane Michael broke houses. Photo: Dan Anderson / EPA

A hurricane has caused damage in the USA. Hurricane Michael hit Florida, for example. The winds of the storm blew almost 70 metres per second.

Hurricane Michael broke houses, knocked down trees and covered streets under water. At least 2 people died. Over 700,000 people have been without electricity.

It will take many months to repair the storm damage.

## Drinking alcohol in different countries

![Bottles of Koskenkorva in an Alko shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9763523)

Open image viewer

Finns do not drink alcohol every day. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finns do not drink alcohol very often. A new study compares alcohol use in 8 European countries.

The study says that 13 per cent of Spaniards drink alcohol every day. In Italy and France about 10 per cent of adults drink alcohol every day.

People in the Nordic countries do not drink as often. Only 2 per cent of Finns, Swedes and Norwegians drink alcohol every day.
