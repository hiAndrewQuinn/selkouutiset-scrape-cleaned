# Friday 11.6.2021 (radio)

Travel to Estonia may become easier. Corona restrictions on restaurants are also being eased in the Helsinki region. Advance voting abroad was slow. The European Football Championship starts today. The weather.

## Ship traffic to Estonia

![Tallink ship at sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_206/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5595765cc83acbd141d/1556626154)

A Tallink ship at sea.

Finland may be opening commuter traffic to Estonia. Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the government will discuss the matter next week.

The Estonian Prime Minister Kaja Kallas has written an open letter to Prime Minister Marin about ship traffic to and from Estonia. The Estonian prime minister hoped in the letter that the travel restrictions on ship traffic between Finland and Estonia would be eased. Easing is hoped for because many Estonians work in Finland, but their families live in Estonia.

Finland already allows commuting trips between Finland and other EU countries. However, commuting trips are allowed only by air. Commuting between Finland and Estonia is mainly done by ships.

Corona restrictions are easing in the Helsinki region. The restrictions are easing because the corona situation is better than before.

From today, restaurants in the Helsinki region may stay open until 11 pm. In restaurants, it is now also allowed to dance and sing karaoke.

Gathering restrictions may be eased perhaps next week.

## Advance voting abroad

![Municipal elections 2021, voting ballot slip.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2580,h_1451,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81439660b71933c6558/1622613143)

Open image viewer

The actual election day for the municipal elections is the day after tomorrow. Photo: Jouni Koutonen / Yle

Fewer people took part in advance voting abroad in the municipal elections than in the previous municipal elections.

The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says that about 2,700 people voted abroad at Finland's diplomatic missions. In the previous elections, 9,500 people voted abroad.

Finns living abroad also had the opportunity to vote by post. It is not yet known how many people did so.

Advance voting was organised in about 70 countries. In addition, about a hundred crew members voted on ships.

The actual election day for the municipal elections is the day after tomorrow, on Sunday.

## Football European Championship

The Finnish national football team is known by the name Huuhkajat. Photo: Getty Images

The European Football Championship starts today.

The opening match of the European Championship is Turkey–Italy. It is played in Rome at about 10 pm Finnish time.

Finland is also taking part in the football European Championship for the first time.

Finland plays its first match against Denmark. The match is played in Copenhagen tomorrow.

The European Football Championship lasts a month. 24 countries take part in the tournament.

## Weekend weather

On Saturday there will be rain in the western part of the country.

During the day the rain spreads to an area that stretches from the southern part of the country to Lapland. In the east it is still dry during the day.

The temperature is between 20 and 25 degrees, but it is cooler in the rain.

It gets cooler across the whole country on Sunday.
