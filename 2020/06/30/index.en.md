# Tuesday 30.6.2020 (radio)

Finns are already buying holiday trips to warm destinations. A large forest fire is being put out in Muhos. Helsinki is adding warning signs at its beaches. Storm Päivö brings heavy rain and strong wind.

The TV Clear News has a summer break and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Sun holidays

![Stalis Beach, Crete, Greece.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9565400)

Open image viewer

Greece is the favourite destination of Finns. Photo: AOP

Holiday trips to warm destinations are selling well again. Thousands of Finns intend to go on holiday abroad, now that travel restrictions are being eased after the spring of corona.

For example, Aurinkomatkat will start package holidays to Greece and Croatia at the beginning of August. Demand has been brisk. Aurinkomatkat says that on a monthly level the number of travellers is in the thousands.

Another large tour operator, TUI Finland, says that autumn holiday destinations are now selling a lot. Now people are asking for trips, for example, to the Canary Islands.

At the moment the Finnish government does not recommend travel anywhere other than the Baltic countries, Norway, Denmark and Iceland. However, tour operators believe that the recommendations will be eased further in early July.

## Muhos forest fire

![Forest fire in Muhos.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6946105efa29a0e1635/1593453018)

Thick smoke rose from the forest fire in Muhos. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

A large forest fire has been put out in North Ostrobothnia. The fire broke out on Monday in Muhos, near Oulu.

The fire spread to an area of about 250 hectares before it was contained. The fire spread quickly because the terrain was bone dry.

The Muhos forest fire is unusually large. The Forest Centre says that the largest forest fires in Finland are usually under 100 hectares.

Extinguishing work after the fire may continue until perhaps tomorrow.

## Warning signs for beaches

![Lifebuoy on a beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6436745e4ab5697c27d/1581955335)

Open image viewer

Helsinki wants to improve the safety of its beaches.

Safety is to be improved because several drowning accidents have happened at beaches this summer. For example, 3 people have drowned in Hietaniemi in Helsinki in the past couple of weeks.

New signs warning swimmers about dangerous places will be put up on Helsinki's beaches this week. The signs warn, for example, of strong currents and of places where the water suddenly gets deep.

The signs have a picture of the dangerous place and a warning text in Finnish, Swedish, English, Somali and Arabic.

## Summer storm

![Man under an umbrella in pouring rain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7385297)

Open image viewer

It has rained a lot today. Photo: Paula Koskinen / Yle

The summer storm has passed over Finland today. Storm Päivö has brought strong rain and wind to the southern, central and eastern parts of the country.

For example, in eastern Uusimaa, in places as much rain has fallen as normally falls in the whole of June.

In South and North Savo and in North Karelia, the wind may be dangerously strong during the evening and night. The rescue service warns that it is not worth going out on the water now.
