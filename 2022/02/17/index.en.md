The number of Russian soldiers has grown at the Ukrainian border. The USA and the NATO military alliance estimate that Russia has brought thousands of new soldiers to Ukraine's border.

NATO estimates that Russia is doing the opposite of what it says. Russia said on Tuesday that it intends to reduce the number of soldiers at Ukraine's border. Russia has more than 100,000 soldiers at the Ukrainian border.

People around the world still fear that Russia intends to attack Ukraine. Russia demands that Ukraine does not join the NATO military alliance.

## The price of petrol

![Petrol price tower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5144,h_2893,x_136,y_292/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9131556204e9630ec20/1644489165)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

The price of petrol has risen. The price per litre is often about 2 euros.

The price of petrol is pushed up by the fact that the price of petrol on international markets has risen.

Taxes also affect the price of petrol. About 60 percent of the price of petrol in Finland is various taxes.

Petrol is now really expensive. That is why the government is considering how the situation of motorists could be eased.

One means could be to raise the commuting-cost deduction in taxation. If an employee can deduct more commuting costs from wage taxes, it compensates for the rise in the price of petrol.

## A storm in Europe

![Strong wind on the Baltic Sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_287/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6646465e96f68ea64e7/1586952105)

The wind has been strong in Europe. Photo: Yle / Stefan Paavola

There is a severe storm in Europe.

For example in Germany, train traffic was cut off in much of the country because of the storm. There have also been problems in road traffic and air traffic.

In Poland the storm has broken houses. It is reported from Poland that people have even died in the storm.

## Drought in the USA

![Forest fire in California.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_464/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83151860edcb4e854f3/1626196864)

Open image viewer

In recent years drought has also caused forest fires in the USA. Photo: Josh Edelson / AFP

The western parts of the USA are suffering from drought. Researchers estimate that the area has not been this dry for more than 1,000 years.

The drought has already lasted more than 20 years, and it will probably continue for many years yet.

The western parts of the USA have had drought periods before too, but climate change increases them and makes them worse.
