# Sunday 26.11.2017 (radio)

A volcano is erupting on Bali. Two died in a car accident. Christmas streets open. Cruel treatment behind a cheese. A poisonous alga is spreading.

## A volcano is erupting on Bali

![Boys played football as the Agung volcano spewed ash on Bali on 26 November.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9948921)

Open image viewer

Boys played football and the Agung volcano spewed ash on Bali on 26 November. Photo: Made Nagi / EPA

A volcano called Agung has had a series of eruptions. The volcano is in Indonesia, on the island of Bali. The volcano has spewed ash as high as four kilometres. There are many Finns on the island of Bali.

On Sunday, the Indonesian authorities raised the volcano warning to the highest level, which is the so-called red level.

The volcano's previous big eruption happened in 1963. At that time, over a thousand people died in the eruption.

## A driver drove into a tree and two people died

![A tree broken by the force of the crash](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9948967)

Open image viewer

The car hit a tree and the tree broke. Photo: Lore Korpelainen / Yle

Two people have died in a traffic accident in Pori. According to the police, a car went off the road on Saturday evening. The car was going too fast when it went off the road and hit a tree. The car broke in two.

According to the police, the two passengers in the car died. The man suspected of being the driver had been drinking alcohol. He was not seriously injured in the accident. He is suspected of crimes.

## Christmas streets open

![The opening of the Christmas street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8473933)

The opening of the Christmas street in Helsinki in 2015. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

Christmas streets were opened on Sunday in different parts of Finland. Christmas streets were opened in places like Helsinki, Tampere, Jyväskylä and Vaasa.

One of the oldest Christmas events in Finland began at Senate Square in Helsinki in the afternoon.

Santa Claus brought greetings from Korvatunturi, and the city's mayor switched on the Christmas lights on Sunday afternoon.

## The milk for Parmesan cheese comes from abused cows

![Parmesan cheeses](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9948861)

Open image viewer

Parmesan cheeses. Photo: EPA

An animal protection organisation has published video footage filmed in Italy. It shows that cows are treated very badly.

The videos were filmed on nine farms. The milk produced on the farms is used, for example, to make the famous Parmesan cheese. The cheese is often eaten with pasta.

The cows were starving. Some of the cows were lame, and they never got outside. The animals in poor condition have to stand in their own dung because the cowsheds were not cleaned.

## A poisonous alga is taking over Finland

![The dinoflagellate alga can cause bioluminescence, which colours the sea a glowing turquoise at night.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7413158)

Open image viewer

The dinoflagellate alga glows. Photo: Daniel Eriksson / Handout / Lehtikuva

A poisonous alga is taking over the Baltic Sea. The name of this alga is the dinoflagellate (Alexandrium). It releases a dangerous nerve poison. The alga glows turquoise in the sea in late summer.

The authorities say that around the world this alga has caused the deaths of seabirds, mammals and even people.

In Finland, the alga was seen for the first time in the 1970s. In recent years, the dinoflagellate alga has been seen in more and more places in the Baltic Sea.
