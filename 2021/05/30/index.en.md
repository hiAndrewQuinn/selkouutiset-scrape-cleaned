## Corona vaccines

![Corona vaccine bottles in Germany](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_214/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-801437608fc72bf32ae/1620131000)

Finland is now getting plenty of corona vaccines. Photo: Markus Matzel / AOP

Finland will get a record number of corona vaccines next week. Over 350,000 corona vaccine doses will come to Finland. That is the largest number in 1 week so far.

Many corona vaccines will also come to Finland in the following weeks. Because so many vaccines are now available, vaccinations are progressing well in Finland.

44 percent of Finns have now received the first dose of the corona vaccine, that is, over 2.4 million people.

Today, Sunday, is the 5th advance voting day for the municipal elections.

Over 430,000 people have voted, that is, almost 10 percent of those entitled to vote.

The advance voting period for the municipal elections is 2 weeks. The last advance voting day is 8 June. The actual election day is Sunday 13 June.

More information about the municipal elections: Municipal elections 2021 in clear language

Yle is organising the Clear Debate, a municipal election debate in plain Finnish, on 4 June.

## Job advertisements

![Joensuu city's renewed job advertisement in Kauppalehti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9149625)

Open image viewer

People hope that municipalities will give clearer job advertisements. Photo: Janne Ahjopalo / Yle

Many people think that job advertisements are unclear. A new study shows this.

Job seekers want to know how much the work pays. However, in many job advertisements the pay is not stated clearly.

Especially in job advertisements in the municipal sector, information about the job and the pay is often unclear. People say that they want to know the pay.

## Municipal energy companies

![Etelä-Savon Energia's Pursiala power plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11940204)

Open image viewer

Many municipalities in Finland have their own energy company. Photo: Esa Huuhko / Yle

Municipal ownership of energy companies is decreasing. Many municipalities may sell their municipal energy company to private equity investors so that the municipality gets money.

Many municipalities are considering selling an energy company, because municipal finances are weakening.

Sales of energy companies may pick up perhaps in the autumn. That is when municipalities prepare their budgets for the following year.

## Name changes

![Close-up of a calendar, with days of the week and names.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8414915)

Open image viewer

The picture shows a name-day calendar. About 15,000 Finns applied for a name change last year. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

More and more Finns want to change their first name or surname than before. Last year about 15,000 Finns asked for a name change. Applications for name changes have increased somewhat in recent years.

A common reason for changing a first name is that a person changes their call name into their official name.

A common reason for changing a surname is returning to an old surname after a divorce.
