# Wednesday 30.9.2020 (radio)

Economic policy has been debated in Parliament. The government is restricting restaurant opening hours. Female genital mutilation is to be banned more clearly. Lonely Planet thinks Rovaniemi is the best winter holiday destination in Europe.

## Economic policy was discussed

![LKS 20200930 Prime Minister Sanna Marin (SDP) speaks in the plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki on 30 September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11571699)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin in Parliament on Wednesday. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Parliament, that is, the Finnish parliament, has discussed economic policy.

The opposition parties think that the government's economic policy is bad. The opposition thinks the government has no plan for how Finland's debts will be managed. The government has taken on a lot more debt because of the corona crisis.

The government says that the opposition does not have a good alternative to the government's economic policy.

Parliament will vote on Friday on whether it still has confidence in the government. The government parties have more MPs in Parliament than the opposition parties. That is why it is fairly certain that the government will win Parliament's confidence and stay in power.

## Restaurants are being restricted

![Bar counter of the restaurant Harry's](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5124,h_2882,x_0,y_8/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7248535f747466b3e92/1602001689)

Open image viewer

Restaurants must stop selling alcohol at midnight, that is, at 24:00. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

The government has decided to restrict the opening hours of restaurants. The reason is that the coronavirus has spread in restaurants, especially at night.

So the government orders that restaurants may not be open at night. Restaurants must stop selling alcohol at 24:00, and they must close at 01:00.

The new opening hours will come into force in a week, on 8 October.

The restaurant sector is worried about jobs. The industry's organisation fears that during the corona times there will be about 20,000 fewer jobs in restaurants than usual.

## Mutilation is not accepted

![A separate law to ban mutilation in Finland divides opinions, but everyone agrees that the keys to a solution are in information, education and open discussion.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10230189)

Open image viewer

Mutilation means cutting off part of a girl's genitals. Photo: Juha Tuominen / Yle

Finland wants to ban the female genital mutilation of girls more clearly than now. In mutilation, part of a girl's genitals is cut off.

Mutilation is already a crime in Finland today. A girl may also not be taken abroad for mutilation.

Parliament received a citizens' initiative, that is, a proposal from ordinary people, that the mutilation of girls must be clearly banned in the text of the law. The Constitutional Law Committee of Parliament agreed, and the law is going to be changed as soon as possible.

## Rovaniemi has been noticed

![Father Christmas feeding a reindeer](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4521545a3b5305f23e9/1607003269)

Father Christmas can be found in Rovaniemi.

The best winter holiday destination in Europe is the Finnish city of Rovaniemi. This is the view of the travel media Lonely Planet.

Lonely Planet writes that Rovaniemi is the official city of Father Christmas. In addition, a tourist can experience snow and reindeer in Rovaniemi.

Lonely Planet does remind, however, that in Finland there is frost in winter, so tourists must bring warm clothes on their trip.
