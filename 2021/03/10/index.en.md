# Wednesday 10.3.2021 (radio)

There will be a mask requirement on trains. People are breaking coronavirus quarantine. Vegetarian food divides opinions. The police are introducing body cameras.

## Mask requirement on trains

![Ski-holiday traffic at Helsinki railway station on 19 February 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11829861)

Most passengers have wished for a mask requirement on trains. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

There will be a mask requirement on trains in Finland. The rail company VR has decided that everyone must wear a mask on trains. The mask requirement begins tomorrow, Thursday. Without a mask on the train, you may only go without one for health reasons.

VR says that in a customer survey more than 65 percent of passengers wished for a mask requirement on trains.

There is a mask recommendation on the HSL commuter trains in the Helsinki region. HSL has not made a mask compulsory, but the wish is that everyone wears a mask.

## Quarantine is being broken

![A woman in quarantine works remotely from home in Porvoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6549055e723cd19f036/1584545299)

People in quarantine must stay at home. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

Some Finns do not follow the coronavirus quarantine. This year the police have met several Finns who have not stayed at home, even though they were ordered into quarantine.

If a person has a coronavirus infection, they must stay in quarantine. The police have met quarantine breakers in connection with emergency call-outs. The police say that there are clearly more quarantine breakers this year than last year.

Only a few quarantine breakers are caught. That is why it is not known how many Finns do not follow the quarantine.

## Finns disagree about vegetarian food

![A vegetarian meal portion in a workplace canteen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_432/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-57907577620a039d42/1467359574)

Young people and women want more vegetarian food added. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Finns have different opinions about vegetarian food. Yle asked Finns whether vegetarian food should be added, for example in schools and other publicly supported meals.

The result was evenly split. About 45 percent of Finns support offering more vegetarian food. The same amount, about 45 percent, oppose adding vegetarian food. 10 percent do not know their view.

Women and young people are the most in favour of adding vegetarian food. Men and older people are the least in favour of adding vegetarian food.

## Body cameras for police across Finland

![Police body cameras](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_506/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6251035dfb9841e987e/1579608566)

Police body camera. Photo: Sasha Silvala / YLE

The police are introducing body cameras across Finland. A body camera records the view in front of the police officer.

It increases the safety of the police. People are calmer when the camera is on. If, for example, someone behaves badly or is violent, it is recorded on the camera.

The police have already tried body cameras in the Helsinki and East Uusimaa police departments. The experiences have been good, and now body cameras will also be purchased for the other police departments in Finland.
