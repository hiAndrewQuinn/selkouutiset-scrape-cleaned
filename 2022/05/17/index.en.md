The Finnish presidential couple is visiting the neighbouring country Sweden.

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio travelled to the Swedish capital, Stockholm, on Tuesday morning.

King Kaarle Kustaa and Queen Silvia of Sweden received the presidential couple at the Royal Palace in Stockholm.

The visit comes at an interesting time. Finland and Sweden are applying together for membership of the military alliance NATO.

On Tuesday it was also reported that President Niinistö and Swedish Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson will travel to Washington on Thursday. They will discuss Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership with President Joe Biden. The USA is the biggest country in NATO.

## Finland's NATO application

![MPs in the plenary hall of Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_15/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9562406283974f5f682/1652791182)

Open image viewer

Parliament voted on the NATO application on Tuesday. Photo: Juha Kivioja / Yle

Finland's NATO application is ready. Finland will submit its membership application to NATO at the same time as Sweden.

President Sauli Niinistö and Finland's government officially approved the NATO application, after Parliament voted in favour of the application.

Parliament voted on NATO on Tuesday. 188 MPs were of the opinion that Finland should apply for NATO membership. 8 MPs were of the opinion that Finland should not go to NATO.

Before the vote, Parliament debated the matter. Most MPs estimated that NATO will increase Finland's security. NATO countries defend each other if any NATO country is attacked.

## Bicycle accident

![Map of the site of the accident in Kankaanpää.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12449126)

Open image viewer

In Kankaanpää in Western Finland a bicycle accident has happened. A man of about 60 died in the accident.

The accident happened in a tunnel that runs under the road. In the tunnel there is a concrete post, that is, a big piece of concrete. It prevents cars from getting into the tunnel.

The man rode into the tunnel on a bicycle and crashed into the concrete post. The accident happened at night, and there were no lights on in the tunnel.

The man was found in the morning, but he had already died.
