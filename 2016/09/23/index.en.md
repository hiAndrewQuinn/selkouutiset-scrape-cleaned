# Friday 23.9.2016 (radio)

The police want a new unit to investigate hate speech. The leaders of the Finns Party say that the party's politicians have no links to extremist movements. The French state is building a refugee camp. In Siberia, a 3-year-old child was lost in the forest for 3 days. Dogs also guard Finland's borders.

## The police tackle hate speech

![National Police Commissioner Seppo Kolehmainen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8948570)

Open image viewer

Seppo Kolehmainen. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

The police want to set up a unit that searches for and investigates hate speech on the internet.

Police chief Seppo Kolehmainen criticises people for how they  write on social media. People do not understand that freedom of speech also includes responsibility for your words.

Often hate speech is the first step and violence the second, Kolehmainen says.

## Terho: No links to extremist movements

![Sampo Terho](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9137379)

Open image viewer

Sampo Terho. Photo: Yle

The leaders of the Finns Party say that the party's politicians have no links to extremist movements.

Member of Parliament Sampo Terho says that, for example, MP Olli Immonen's link to neo-Nazis is an old story that has been talked through. Helsingin Sanomat wrote that members of the Finns Party have links to at least 5 far-right groups.

Extremist movements are talked about a lot in Finland now because two weeks ago a young man was assaulted at a neo-Nazi demonstration. The man got a bad injury and died. On Saturday, demonstrations against racism will be held in Helsinki and many other cities.

## The French state is building a refugee camp

![Refugees on a street in Paris. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9184704)

Open image viewer

Tents on the streets of Paris. Photo: Juha Nurminen / Yle

In France, thousands of refugees live in tents on the streets of Paris.

Many asylum seekers have come to France in the summer, and all the reception centres are full. That is why the French state is building a refugee camp in Paris. The camp's buildings have room for 400 people to sleep, and the camp will also have a health centre.

The French state will probably have to build more refugee camps. For example, in the town of Calais, a camp that people have built themselves, without permission, will be taken down. 9,000 asylum seekers live there.

## A little boy survived 3 days in the forest

![Tserin Dopchut](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9187619)

Open image viewer

Tserin Dopchut. Photo: Russia's Ministry of Emergency Situations

In Siberia in Russia, a 3-year-old child was lost in the forest for 3 days and survived.

3-year-old Tserin went into the forest, perhaps following a dog. He had no coat or hat, and only one chocolate bar as food. There are bears and wolves in the forest.

Tserin was searched for for 72 hours before he was found. He slept under the trees.

## Over 200 dogs work on Finland's borders

![German shepherd](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9187312)

Open image viewer

German shepherd Edi works on the border. Photo: Yle Pyry Sarkiola

Over 200 dogs guard Finland's borders. Most of them are German shepherds.

The dogs search the forest for people who have come across the border without permission. A dog finds people with its nose, with its sense of smell. A dog's sense of smell is very precise. A dog can follow one person's tracks even when many people have walked in the forest.
