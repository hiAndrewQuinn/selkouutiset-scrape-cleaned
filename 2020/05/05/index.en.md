## Corona restrictions are being eased

![A child looks for something to read on a library shelf.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8712976)

Open image viewer

Libraries are opening already this week. Photo: Jaana Polamo / Yle

The Finnish government has decided to ease the restrictions that have been given because of the coronavirus.

The libraries will be opened first. Already this week, people can go to the library to borrow books.

Schools will open next week. However, the government recommends that upper secondary schools, vocational schools and universities continue distance teaching until the end of the term.

The limit on gatherings of 10 people is in force until the end of May. After that, up to 50 people may gather.

Restaurants and cafés, theatres and museums, and sports and hobby facilities may open their doors in June.

However, the government recommends that people still work from home if it is at all possible. People over 70 years old must also stay at home for now and avoid meeting other people.

## The corona epidemic continues

![Washing hands in a toilet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6454635e4fa9e08caed/1582279368)

Washing hands is still important. Photo: Rami Moilanen/ YLE

Even though many restrictions are being eased, the coronavirus epidemic is not over. The government says that people must still remember to wash their hands often, cough into their sleeve and keep a distance from other people.

If the number of corona infections grows quickly, restrictions may be tightened again.

In Finland, over 5,400 coronavirus infections have been confirmed so far. During the past day, 85 new infections were confirmed. In Finland, over 240 people have died from the coronavirus.

## Ukrainians for spring jobs

![Ukrainian strawberry pickers at Holopainen's farm in Lemi, 2019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10862632)

Open image viewer

These Ukrainians picked strawberries in Lemi last summer. Photo: Kari Saastamoinen/ Yle

Finland and Ukraine have agreed on seasonal workers for the spring. Finland wants Ukrainians for spring jobs on Finnish farms.

Ukraine and Finland have agreed that Ukrainians in Finland will have the same rights to healthcare as Finns. In addition, Ukrainians have been promised work for at least 3 months.

1,500 seasonal workers will be taken into Finland from abroad, mainly from Ukraine. So far, 600 of them have come.

There is also work for Finns, because a total of 4,000 workers are needed for spring jobs. During the harvest season and the berry-picking time, many more workers will be needed.

## Returning tax returns

![A woman examines a tax return](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10766836)

Open image viewer

A tax return must be returned if you have made corrections to it. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Today is the first return day for tax returns.

A tax return must be returned if you have made corrections to it.

The return day for your own tax return is marked on your tax return.

The Tax Administration has pre-filled the tax return and sent it by post. It can also be found in the online OmaVero service.
