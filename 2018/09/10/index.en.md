# Monday 10.9.2018 (radio)

In Sweden the election victory went to the Sweden Democrats. Finnish politicians believe that good cooperation with Sweden will continue. Goods from foreign online shops are not safe. Autumn rains are coming to Finland.

## Support for the Sweden Democrats grew

![Sweden Democrats leader Jimmie Åkesson campaigned in Malmö, with the party's balloons attached to a railing in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10397012)

Leader of the Sweden Democrats Jimmie Åkesson. Photo: Johan Nilsson / EPA

There has been an election in Sweden. On Sunday, Swedes elected a new parliament.

The election victory went to the Sweden Democrats. The party's support rose a lot.

The main election theme of the Sweden Democrats was immigration. The party does not want immigrants to come to Sweden.

The Social Democrats are still Sweden's largest party, but the party's support has fallen. That is why it is uncertain whether Stefan Löfven can continue as Sweden's prime minister. Löfven is a Social Democrat.

The parties may find it difficult to agree on Sweden's new prime minister and government. The left and the right got almost equal numbers of votes. There is no clear winner.

The left and the right do not want to cooperate with the Sweden Democrats.

## Finland and Sweden cooperation

![Jussi Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10394404)

Open image viewer

Finland's Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finnish politicians have commented on the Swedish election.

Finland's Prime Minister Juha Sipilä was surprised that immigration was such an important theme in the Swedish election.

Finland's Foreign Minister Timo Soini believes that the good cooperation between Finland and Sweden will continue. The cooperation will continue even though Sweden's government changes, Soini says.

Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö agrees. All Swedish parties support defence cooperation with Finland, Niinistö says.

## Dangerous goods from online shops

![Inadequate products examined by Tukes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10396836)

Open image viewer

Goods from foreign online shops are rarely safe. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Goods from foreign online shops are not safe. This is what Tukes says. Tukes is an agency that supervises the safety of goods.

Tukes carried out a test on the online shops' websites. It ordered over 30 different products, for example from AliExpress and eBay. Only one product was safe.

Tukes is worried about the situation. For example, all children's toys were dangerous, and chargers posed a risk of fire.

People themselves are responsible for the safety of their purchases if they order goods from outside the European Union.

## Autumn rains are coming

![Umbrella.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10395280)

Open image viewer

An umbrella is needed in the coming days. Photo: AOP

Autumn rains are coming to Finland. On Tuesday and Wednesday a lot of rain may fall.

Yle meteorologist Kerttu Kotakorpi says that in the coming days perhaps as much rain will fall as during the last month.

The weather is autumnal also because the wind is strong in the coming days.
