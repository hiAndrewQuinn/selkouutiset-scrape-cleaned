# Tuesday 23.11.2021 (radio)

Corona recommendations. Corona infections in children. Corona deaths in Europe. Weather.

## Corona recommendations

Corona recommendations are getting stricter in different parts of Finland. The reason is that the corona situation is getting worse.

For example, people are recommended to wear a mask. Wearing a mask is also recommended for everyone who has had a corona vaccine.

In the Helsinki region, people are recommended to wear a mask, for example, on buses and in shops.

In the Turku region, everyone is recommended to wear a mask outdoors too, if they are close to other people.

Pupils in sixth grade and above are also recommended to wear a mask at schools in the Helsinki region and in the Tampere region.

## Corona infections in children

![Face-to-face teaching in a classroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8192,h_4608,x_0,y_858/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-766096600f37938ab4d/1652168136)

Corona infections in children are increasing.

A large part of the new corona infections are in people under 20. Last week more than 2600 corona infections were found in people under 20.

Children under 12 cannot yet get the corona vaccine. Among children over 12, corona infections are most common in those who have not had the corona vaccine.

A child usually gets a corona infection at home. Corona infections come less often, for example, from hobbies.

Often children get a corona infection from their own parents, if the parents have not been vaccinated against corona.

Corona illness is often mild in children. However, some children get serious breathing difficulties because of corona.

## Corona deaths

![Nurses turned a corona patient on intensive care onto their back at the Etterbeek-Ixelles hospital in Brussels, Belgium, on 27 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11618870)

A corona patient is being treated in Belgium. Photo: Stéphanie Lecocq / EPA

The number of corona deaths in Europe is growing. This is what the World Health Organization WHO says. WHO fears that as many as 700 000 people in Europe may die of corona this winter.

The number of corona deaths is also growing in Finland. On Tuesday, 5 new corona deaths were reported. In total, more than 1200 people have died of corona in Finland.

## Weather

Open image viewer

On Tuesday morning the first snow fell in southern Finland. The ground was white in a large part of southern Finland.

On the coast, that is, near the sea, there is not yet any snow cover. However, that is normal. For example, last year Helsinki got snow cover only at the end of December.

In the north, Tuesday started in very cold weather. On Tuesday night in Utsjoki in Lapland, the frost was as much as 29 degrees.

On Wednesday the weather is cloudy and dry across a large part of the country. In the middle of the country it will snow a little in some places.

In the south the temperature on Wednesday is above zero, elsewhere it is frosty.
