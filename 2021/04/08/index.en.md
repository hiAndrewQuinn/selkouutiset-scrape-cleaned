Verdicts in the big drugs case

Verdicts have been given in the big drugs case.

The Helsinki District Court sentenced the main culprits of the so-called Katiska tangle to long prison sentences. The verdicts came for, among other things, aggravated drug offences.

Janne Tranberg's sentence is almost 13 years, and Niko Ranta-aho's sentence is 11 years.

In addition, 7 other accused people were sentenced. They received unconditional prison sentences of at least 8 years.

The Katiska affair is one of Finland's biggest drug cases, in terms of the amount of drugs and the criminal proceeds.

## The director general of the National Audit Office is removed from office

![Tytti Yli-Viikari to be heard by the Grand Committee.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_402/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-792392606ed9be0139e/1621417528)

The director general of the National Audit Office, Tytti Yli-Viikari, is being suspended from office for a fixed period.

Parliament's Office Committee proposes that Yli-Viikari be suspended from office for the duration of the criminal investigation.

The National Bureau of Investigation is investigating the director general's actions as head of the office. Yli-Viikari has been much in the public eye because of unclear use of money.

A decision by a plenary session of Parliament is needed for any dismissal of the director general.

## Finns have committed to restrictions

![VR staff hand out face masks to passengers at Helsinki Railway Station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11836084)

Open image viewer

VR staff hand out face masks to passengers at Helsinki Railway Station. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Finns have committed to corona restrictions better than other countries. An international study covered 11 countries at the end of last year.

The study looked at, for example, compliance with safe distances and the recommendation to work remotely. In Finland, these were seen as less restrictive of life.

In addition, Finns have trusted the national leadership more than people in other countries.

Higher education, older age and being a woman all increased the likelihood of following the restrictions.

## Very few abortions in Finland

![A woman in front of the women's diseases outpatient clinic of HUS in Helsinki, 16 October 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11607139)

Open image viewer

A woman in front of the women's diseases outpatient clinic of HUS in Helsinki. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finland still has the fewest abortions in the Nordic countries.

In Finland, just under 8 abortions per 1,000 women of childbearing age were carried out the year before last. In total, 8,700 terminations of pregnancy were carried out in a year.

Sweden has the most terminations. The number was more than 16 cases per thousand.

In Finland, almost all abortions are carried out with medicines.

Finland has the strictest abortion legislation in the Nordic countries.

## Romani National Day

![Romani flag](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7914315)

Open image viewer

Románaleavga, the Romani flag. Photo: Wikipedia

Today, Thursday, Romani National Day is being celebrated.

The Ministry of the Interior has recommended that flags be raised generally in honour of the day.

Romani National Day began in London in 1971. At that time the first international Romani congress was held.
