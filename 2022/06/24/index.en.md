Ukraine. US weapons aid. Afghanistan. Midsummer. Weather forecast.

## Ukraine

Ukrainian troops have to withdraw from Sjeverodonetsk in Eastern Ukraine. This is what the governor of the Luhansk region says. Russia has been shelling the area for months.

The governor says that the city of Sjeverodonetsk has been destroyed into ruins. Russia has taken most of the city.

The governor says that the Russians are now advancing towards Lysytshansk, which is the city next to Sjeverodonetsk.

## US weapons aid to Ukraine

![Green military vehicle that works as a rocket launcher.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_997,h_560,x_1,y_5/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97432362b4772bb353c/1655994490)

The USA is sending more weapons aid to Ukraine.

The USA is sending 4 rocket launcher systems and other weapons and war equipment to Ukraine. The value of the new aid package is about 450 million dollars.

The aim is that Ukraine can better prevent the advance of Russian troops in Ukraine. The USA has also given rocket launchers to Ukraine earlier.

## Afghanistan

The earthquake broke many buildings in Afghanistan. Photo: EPA

Many people have died and been injured in the earthquake in Afghanistan. There was a big earthquake on Wednesday in the eastern part of Afghanistan.

Afghan authorities say that at least 1000 people have died and several thousand people have been injured because of the earthquake. About 10,000 houses have been damaged.

The UN has sent aid to Afghanistan, for example tents, food and medical supplies.

Corona vaccines have saved many lives. This is what a new study says.

The researchers studied how corona vaccinations helped prevent deaths over 12 months, that is, 1 year.

185 countries took part in the study. The researchers estimate that corona vaccines saved almost 20 million lives last year.

## Midsummer

Open image viewer

Emilia Hellsten, Taru Jäntti and Meri Frantti have put the traditional Midsummer wreath on their heads. Photo: Antti Lähteenmäki / Yle

Finns are now celebrating Midsummer. Friday is Midsummer Eve and Saturday is Midsummer Day.

Many Finns have travelled from the city to their summer cottages. There was a lot of traffic on Thursday and Friday. Now on Friday evening the traffic has quietened down.

For many Finns the summer holiday begins at Midsummer.

Midsummer is also the day of the Finnish flag. The flag is raised up the flagpole on Midsummer Eve at 18:00 and is lowered only on Midsummer Day at 21:00. Midsummer Night is the only night when flags are flown.
