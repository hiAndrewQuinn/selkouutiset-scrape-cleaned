## Government negotiations

![Petteri Orpo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_80/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11105806459f6be36c8c/1683617530)

National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The goal of the parties in the government negotiations is that in the future 100,000 more people will work in Finland than now. Government formateur Petteri Orpo says that Finland's economy will improve a lot if there are more jobs than before.

Petteri Orpo has said earlier that the parties agree that the state must save a lot of money.

The National Coalition, the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats are taking part in the government negotiations.

In Sudan more than 700,000 people have fled the fighting. About 150,000 people have gone abroad from Sudan.

The fighting between the Sudanese armed forces and the RSF forces began in mid-April. At least hundreds of people have died in the fighting.

## Eurovision semi-final

![Käärijä and his dance group on the Eurovision stage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4480,h_2520,x_0,y_464/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11104576459311cba3b3/1683604364)

Open image viewer

Käärijä and his dance group on the Eurovision stage. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The Eurovision semi-final is today, Tuesday, in Liverpool.

Finland is represented in Eurovision by Käärijä with the song Cha cha cha. Käärijä is the favourite in the semi-final. Käärijä performs as the last singer in the semi-final.

In tonight's semi-final, 15 countries compete, of which 10 countries get into the song contest final. On Thursday there is the 2nd semi-final.

The 1st Eurovision semi-final begins in the evening at 22:00. Yle broadcasts Eurovision live on television, radio and online.

The Eurovision final is in Liverpool on Saturday.

## Seal Live

![Venla, a Saimaa ringed seal, lounges on a rock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1716,h_965,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107240363ea546fa12b8/1676301726)

Open image viewer

Venla, a Saimaa ringed seal, lounges on a rock. Illustrative image. Photo: WWF Suomi / Ismo Marttinen

The Seal Live of the environmental organisation WWF is open again. The Seal Live camera films the Saimaa ringed seal. In Seal Live you can follow the life of the Saimaa ringed seal on a shore rock by Lake Saimaa.

Seal Live is part of WWF's Nature Live service. The images from Nature Live's cameras show, besides the Saimaa ringed seal, the Baltic ringed seal, the osprey and the Lapland owl.

You can watch Nature Live at https://wwf.fi/luontolive.

The weather is mostly dry, and in places the sun shines. On Wednesday in the daytime, a little rain may fall in Kainuu, North Ostrobothnia and South-West Lapland.

The night temperature is between 0 and +6 degrees. On Wednesday in the daytime the temperature is between 12 and 18 degrees, and in North Lapland between 6 and 12 degrees.
