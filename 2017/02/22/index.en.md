# Wednesday 22.2.2017 (radio)

Amnesty warns about hate speech. Banks must know their customers. Reception centres are being closed. Skylarks are already in Finland.

## Amnesty warns about hate speech

![US President Donald Trump, Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan and and Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orban.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9472657)

US President Donald Trump, Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan and Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orbán. Photo: Saul Loeb / AFP, Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva, Attila Kisbenedek / AFP

The human rights organisation Amnesty International says that politicians' harsh speeches are dangerous for human rights. Amnesty says that last year there was more hate speech than in many decades.

Amnesty's report says that among politicians, Donald Trump especially talked a lot about threats. The leaders of Turkey and Hungary, for example, also give harsh speeches that go against human rights. For example, 36 countries have sent asylum seekers back to countries where they are threatened by violence or even death.

Amnesty criticises Finland, because last year Finland, for example, tightened the conditions for family reunification.

## A bank must know its customers

![A Sampo Bank key code list on top of a computer keyboard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5002815)

Open image viewer

Banks watch, for example, unusual transfers in online banking. The bank tries to prevent, among other things, money laundering, in which the illegal origin of money is hidden.  Photo: Yle

Banks now ask customers for a lot of information.

Everyone living legally in Finland gets banking services and online banking codes. The law came into force at the start of the year. People who have got asylum or a residence permit in Finland also get online banking codes. They can prove their identity with a foreigner's identity card.

The law says that banks must know every customer, because online banking interests criminals and terrorists. For example, a bank investigates if suspicious transfers start to appear on bank accounts

Over six (6) million online banking codes are already in use in Finland.

## Reception centres are being closed

![Reception centre Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8342333)

Open image viewer

1100 accommodation places are being cut from reception centres. Photo: Jussi Vierimaa /SPR

The Finnish Immigration Service is closing 11 reception centres. They are in different parts of Finland. The number of places drops by over a thousand (1000).

Four (4) reception centres for adults and families and seven (7) centres for minors are being closed. The Immigration Service says that there are too many places and keeping them is expensive.

Last year over 22,000 accommodation places were cut. If more people arrive, places can be added.

## Spring is already in the air

![Skylark.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5326596)

Open image viewer

A month from the skylark to summer, half of six from the chaffinch, says an old proverb. It is also said that one swallow does not make a summer. Photo: Luonto-Liitto

The first skylarks have already flown to Finland. The skylark has been seen as a sign of the coming summer. An old proverb starts with the words "a month from the skylark to summer".

The bird organisation Birdlife Finland says that skylarks have been seen in South-West Finland on the island of Utö and in Nakkila. Skylarks have also been seen in Paimio, Pori and Kotka.

Snow buntings and stock doves have also arrived on the south coast and in Western Finland. The first gulls have already made it inland.
