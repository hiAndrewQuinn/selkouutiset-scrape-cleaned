# Tuesday 20.11.2018 (radio)

The ceiling of a school collapsed in Loviisa. Spain wants to change the EU withdrawal agreement with Britain. More police are needed. Metals can be recovered from ash. Employment increased. Over 2,700 new upper secondary graduates.

## The school ceiling collapsed in Loviisa

The interior ceiling of a school in Loviisa has collapsed.

The ceiling collapsed during the night between Monday and Tuesday. The school was empty and nobody was injured. The school is only 4 years old and is located in the centre of Loviisa. The cause of the collapse is not yet known.

Interior ceilings have already failed in three schools within a few weeks.

## Spain wants to change Britain's EU withdrawal agreement

![Gibraltar](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10517557)

Gibraltar is causing friction between Spain and Britain. Photo: Matt Cardy / Getty Images

Spain wants the agreement on relations between the EU and Britain not to cover Gibraltar. The small area of Gibraltar is located at Spain's southern tip, but it belongs to Britain.

Spain cannot block Britain's EU withdrawal agreement alone. The other EU countries consider the agreement good.

## Police: more police officers are needed

![Police car](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10279344)

Open image viewer

There are now about 7,000 police officers. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Police want more police officers in Finland. The Federation of Police Unions says that Finland needs 1,000 more police officers. There are now about 7,000 police officers. The police say that about 100,000 emergency tasks are left undone every year, because there are not enough police officers.

## Recovering metals from ash

A method for recovering metals from ash has been developed at the University of Eastern Finland. The method may be used at waste incineration plants. Valuable metals can be recovered when waste is burned.

## Water levels are still low

![Water in Lake Pyhäjärvi is low in Tammela.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10391716)

Open image viewer

The water levels are very low. Photo: Eeva Hannula / Yle

The water levels of lakes are very low. The water levels are low because there has been little rain. The weather will stay dry and the water levels may fall further. Low water is a problem for water pipes, which are left exposed. Fish are also harmed when the water falls in shallow lakes. Some wells may also dry up.

## Unemployment fell

![A woman washes dishes at the Mikkeli unemployment offices.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10405271)

Employment has increased. At the end of last month 43,000 more people were working than a year ago.
There were 42,000 fewer unemployed people than a year ago.
