## Uniper

![Uniper's logo, with a power plant chimney visible in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8256,h_4644,x_0,y_651/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98488262d95856337ee/1658485938)

Open image viewer

The German state will in future be a minority owner of Uniper. Photo: Robin Utrecht / AOP

Finland and Germany have agreed on how the German company Uniper will be rescued.

Uniper is a German energy company, but its largest owner is the Finnish company Fortum. Fortum belongs to the Finnish state.

Now the German state is buying a 30 percent share of Uniper. At the same time, Fortum owns a smaller part of Uniper than before. The German state also puts money into Uniper.

Uniper got into trouble when the price of gas rose. The war in Ukraine raised the price of gas. Uniper pays a lot of money for gas, but Uniper's customers paid the same amount for gas as before.

Now Uniper is allowed to raise prices when it sells gas to customers.

A bucket overflowing with cloudberries. Photo: Anja Kaarret / Yle

There are now a lot of cloudberries in the nature of Northern Finland. A cloudberry is a berry that grows in bogs. At this time of year people pick a lot of cloudberries, especially in Southern Lapland and North Ostrobothnia.

This is the 3rd year in a row when there are a lot of cloudberries in Northern Finland.
