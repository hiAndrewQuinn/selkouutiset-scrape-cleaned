## President Martti Ahtisaari

![Martti Ahtisaari received the Nobel Peace Prize in Oslo in December 2008.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4256,h_2394,x_0,y_379/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1186953652d296dd174b/1697458657)

Former President of Finland Martti Ahtisaari has died.

Ahtisaari died in Helsinki today, Monday. He was 86 years old.

Ahtisaari became President of Finland in 1994. He was the SDP's presidential candidate. Ahtisaari was president until 2000.

During Ahtisaari's presidency, Finland turned towards the West. For example, Finland joined the European Union.

Martti Ahtisaari did peace work around the world for many decades. Ahtisaari's work was noticed, and in 2008 he received the Nobel Peace Prize.

The justification for the prize said that Ahtisaari had an influence, for example, on Namibia becoming independent and on the peace agreement for the Aceh province in Indonesia.

Finns remember Ahtisaari with affection. President Sauli Niinistö says that Martti Ahtisaari had a big heart.

The life work of President Martti Ahtisaari is being honoured in many ways.

For example, there was a flag at half-mast at the Presidential Palace on Monday. On Tuesday Parliament will hold a moment of silence in memory of Ahtisaari.

Later the Finnish state will arrange a funeral for Ahtisaari. The day of the funeral is not yet known.

## Getting citizenship

![Interior Minister Mari Rantanen at a press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3619,h_2035,x_0,y_102/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1186974652d2d84065b6/1699539222)

Interior Minister Mari Rantanen told how getting citizenship will change. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Getting Finnish citizenship is becoming harder. Interior Minister Mari Rantanen told about the government's plans on Monday.

The government's plans include, for example, a citizenship test for everyone who applies for Finnish citizenship.

The citizenship test will include questions about Finland's political system, history and the rules of society.

In the citizenship test, an immigrant shows that they have understood the values of Finnish society, says Interior Minister Rantanen.

The government is also planning many other tightenings. In future, an applicant may get Finnish citizenship, for example, only after living in Finland for 8 years. Today the time is 5 years.

The applicant must also prove that they have enough income to live in Finland. Interior Minister Rantanen has not yet said the exact amount. However, the intention is that Kela benefits will no longer be counted in.
