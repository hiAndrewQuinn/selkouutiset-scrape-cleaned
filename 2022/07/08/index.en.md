## Shooting incident in Japan

![Three Japanese people read a newspaper on the street about the death of a former prime minister.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5713,h_3213,x_0,y_123/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97984162c81c979e64b/1657281820)

Open image viewer

Japanese people read in the newspaper about the death of a former prime minister. Photo: Kimimasa Mayama / EPA

Japan's former prime minister Shinzo Abe has died.

Abe died after he was shot at an election event. There are elections in Japan on Sunday.

The shooter was an about 40-year-old man. The police arrested the man. The man did not try to flee.

The police say that the man did not like Abe's politics.

Shinzo Abe was Japan's prime minister for many years. Abe stopped as prime minister 2 years ago because of health problems. Even after that he was an important politician in Japan.

Shootings are not frequent in Japan. That is why Abe's death has shocked the Japanese.

The incident has also shocked world leaders. Finnish President Sauli Niinistö and Prime Minister Sanna Marin are also sad because of Abe's death.

## Ukraine war and grain exports

![A farmer threshes the crop in a field in Kramatorsk, Ukraine, in a field where a crater, which is suspected to have been caused by an air strike, has formed earlier.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3999,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97944762c71f19e21e5/1657251739)

Open image viewer

Traces of war in a Ukrainian field. Photo: Miguel Medina / AFP

Russia's war in Ukraine may cause famine. Because of the war, food may run out for millions of people in different countries.

This is what the UN, that is, the United Nations, says. The UN estimates that 50 million people may soon suffer from hunger.

There is a shortage of grain around the world, because grain cannot be obtained normally from Ukraine. Ukraine is an important grain producer.

A large part of Ukraine's farmland is now in Russia's hands, and Russia is preventing grain exports from Ukraine abroad.

The war has continued in Ukraine for over 4 months already. Russia attacked Ukraine at the end of February.

## Young people and the future

![At the youth fair, lots of people between the mass tables.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5719,h_3216,x_6,y_438/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9464426267b9b3599dc/1650965115)

Open image viewer

Young people believe in the future. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

Finnish young people believe that the future is good. The information is from a survey by the Children and Youth Foundation.

Over 50 percent of young people say that their belief in a good future has grown in recent years. Only 10 percent of young people think that well-being will decrease.

Finnish young people now fear war the most. This is the first time for a long time that young people fear war the most.
