# Sunday 11.9.2016 (radio)

The government saves on care for the elderly. Many airlines have banned the use of Samsung's new phone on planes. The USA has remembered the terrorist attack on the World Trade Center. Finn Tuomas Kaario has swum across the Strait of Gibraltar.

## Care for the elderly

![A nurse beside the bed of a resident of a service home.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9128719)

Open image viewer

The number of carers for the elderly is planned to be reduced. Photo: Ere Ek / Yle

The government saves on care for the elderly. For example, the number of carers is planned to be reduced. The number of carers goes down in old people's homes and in some assisted living.

Municipalities will save 25 million euros next year, because there do not need to be as many carers as before.

Senior inspector Elina Uusitalo from Valvira is worried. Valvira is an agency that supervises social and health care. Uusitalo fears that the elderly will not get enough care if the number of carers is reduced. For example, carers may not have time to take the elderly outdoors, Uusitalo says.

Minister of Family Affairs and Social Services Juha Rehula is not yet saying more about the matter. More information on the matter will come when the state budget is public and everyone can read it. The budget tells how the state uses money next year.

## Battery fault in the new Samsung

![A phone in a hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9158060)

Open image viewer

The battery of the Samsung Galaxy Note 7 can heat up and catch fire. Photo: Jannis Mattar / EPA

Many airlines have banned the use of Samsung's new phone on planes. For example, Finnair and SAS say that Samsung's new phone must not be used or charged during the flight.

The name of Samsung's new phone is Galaxy Note 7. The phone's battery has a fault that can make the battery really hot. The phone can even catch fire.

Samsung has stopped selling the new phone. The Galaxy Note 7 will come back to the shops when the battery fault has been fixed. Customers can return the phone if they have already bought a Galaxy Note 7.

## Memorial ceremony for the WTC attack

![An old man down on one knee holds his hand on top of a low wall. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9159293)

Open image viewer

At the site of the terrorist attack there is a memorial with the names of all the victims of the attack. Photo: Justin Lane / EPA

The USA has remembered the terrorist attack on the World Trade Center. The attack happened exactly 15 years ago, on 11 September 2001. About 3000 people died in the attack.

The attackers were terrorists from the Islamist organisation al-Qaeda. They flew planes into the towers of the World Trade Center in New York.

Presidential candidates Hillary Clinton and Donald Trump also took part in the memorial ceremony for the terrorist attack. They took a day's break from the election campaign because of the memorial ceremony.

## A long swim

![Open-water swimmer Tuomas Kaario trains, among other places, at Hormajärvi in Lohja.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7322750)

Tuomas Kaario swims really long distances. Photo: Yle

Finn Tuomas Kaario has swum across the Strait of Gibraltar. The swim from Spain to Morocco was about 18 kilometres.

Kaario says that the swim was hard, because the waves at sea were high. The waves were as high as 2 metres. The swim took about 5 hours.

Kaario has also swum longer distances. For example, he swam across the Gulf of Finland from Estonia to Finland 2 years ago. That swim was about 60 kilometres, and it took over 26 hours.
