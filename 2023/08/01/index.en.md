# Radio Clear News Tuesday 1.8.2023

Bird flu. Day-care centres. Swedish security. Weather.

## Bird flu

![A mink on fur farm run by Markus Sjöholm from Uusikaarlepyy has risen to the mesh to look outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6272,h_3528,x_1146,y_776/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106577063d7911bcbe13/1690899827)

Bird flu has spread from birds to minks, which live on fur farms.

The authorities now order that all minks be put down on the fur farms where there is bird flu. That means the minks will be killed.

According to researchers, the minks must be put down, because in minks the bird flu can change so that it becomes dangerous for people.

50,000 animals on fur farms must be put down because of bird flu.

## Day-care centres

![A child makes a sand cake at the edge of a sandpit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114887864c3a815ce51d/1698245500)

The government wants a larger share of children to attend day-care centres. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

The government wants more children in day-care centres. The aim is that fewer children are looked after at home and more in day-care centres.

The government wants mothers to go to work. Mothers cannot go to work if they look after children at home.

Many day-care centres already have too few staff. If more children come, day-care centres will need many more staff.

The trade union of workers thinks that the government has not thought about where more staff will come from.

For example, the government intends to reduce the support that is paid to adults who study. It is harder for adults to study to become day-care workers if they no longer get financial support for studying.

## Swedish security

Sweden is starting to monitor its borders more strictly, because Sweden's security has weakened.

Recently, Qurans have been burned in Sweden, and many Muslim countries have become angry with Sweden.

Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson says that Sweden will not let in people who want to harm Sweden.

According to Prime Minister Kristersson, people who burn Qurans misuse freedom of expression.

In future, Sweden intends to ban the burning of the holy books of religions at demonstrations.

In Finland a law on religious peace is already in force. That is why, for example, the Quran or the Bible may not be burned at a demonstration.
