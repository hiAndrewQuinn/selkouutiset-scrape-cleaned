# Saturday 14.10.2017 (radio)

Researcher Toni Alaranta says that Finland must consider whether it still makes sense to support Turkey's bid to join the EU. The Prosecutor General is worried about racist hate speech. More than 600 towns around the world have held walks against human trafficking. The forest fires in Northern California have claimed 36 lives. The mushroom harvest is not good despite the rain.

## Researcher: Turkey's EU membership must be reconsidered

![Toni Alaranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9031622)

Open image viewer

Senior researcher Toni Alaranta of the Finnish Institute of International Affairs follows Turkish politics closely. Photo: Yle

The researcher from the Finnish Institute of International Affairs says that the European Union must reconsider its relationship with Turkey. Researcher Toni Alaranta said on Yle's morning TV that Finland must also consider whether it still makes sense to support Turkey's efforts to join the EU.

Turkey's human rights and press freedom have been in the spotlight, because Turkey has sentenced a Finnish journalist to prison. The reason for journalist Ayla Albayrak's sentence was that she worked in the Kurdish areas of Turkey.

Toni Alaranta says that an improvement in Turkey's human rights has been awaited for many years.

## Prosecutor General warns about hate speech

![Raija Toiviainen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9881741)

Open image viewer

Prosecutor General Raija Toiviainen. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

Prosecutor General Raija Toiviainen is worried that racist hate speech is increasing. The constitution says that everyone in Finland has freedom of speech. However, Toiviainen says firmly that hate speech has no protection under freedom of speech.

Toiviainen says that racist hate speech increases social exclusion and even feeds terrorism. Hate speech isolates its targets from the rest of society. Then people become bitter and are vulnerable to crime and extremist movements, Toiviainen says.

## Human trafficking walks have also been held in Finland

![Walk against human trafficking in Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9883647)

Open image viewer

The walk against human trafficking in Turku started from the Turku Cathedral. Photo: Sannimari Lehtilä / Yle

More than 600 towns around the world have held walks against human trafficking. The aim is to spread information about human trafficking and to collect money for work against it.

In Finland, the Walk for Freedom event has been held in six (6) places, for example in Joensuu, Turku, Jyväskylä and Helsinki.

The event is run by the A21 organisation, which works worldwide against human trafficking.

## Dozens of people have died in the California fires

![A landscape covered in ash after the fire. In the foreground is a pick-up truck destroyed by the fire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9883510)

Open image viewer

The forest fires in California are the worst in the state's history. Photo: John G. Mabanglo / EPA-EFE

In Northern California, USA, at least 36 people have died in the forest fires. More than 9000 firefighters are still fighting the fire. The forest fires have raged for five (5) days already. The fires are the worst in the state's history.

So far, almost five (5) times the area of Helsinki has burned in the fires.

About 90 000 people have had to flee from the wine-producing area of California. Almost 6000 buildings have been destroyed.

## The rain did not bring a good mushroom harvest

![Funnel chanterelles in an autumn forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9210755)

Open image viewer

The funnel chanterelle does not even get scared of frost. You can often find funnel chanterelles in December, if there is no snow. Photo: Juha Kemppainen / Yle

This autumn's mushroom harvest has been weaker than usual, says the Natural Resources Institute Finland. Senior researcher Kauko Salo says, for example, that very few cep mushrooms have been found.

Russula and chanterelles have been found in the forests in reasonable amounts. The chanterelle harvest has in places even been good.

The harvest season of most edible mushrooms is nearly over or has ended. However, you can still pick funnel chanterelles until the snow comes.
