# Friday 31.1.2020 (radio)

Finnair is cancelling all flights to China. Britain's departure from the EU takes effect on Saturday. The pollen season has begun in southern Finland. The snow situation in January has been unusual.

## Finnair cancels flights to China

![Finnair plane landing at Stockholm Arlanda airport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3895,h_2191,x_0,y_207/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-43915859f70104170f0/1509371759)

Finnair is not flying to China in February. Photo: Mostphotos.com / Stefan Holm

Finnair is cancelling all flights to China. Finnair's flights to China end next week.

Flights have been cancelled until the end of February. Flights to Guangzhou, Nanjing and Beijing's Daxing airport have been cancelled until the end of March.

Finnair's flights to Hong Kong continue as normal.

Finnair is ending flights to China because of the coronavirus. The coronavirus is spreading quickly. The virus was discovered in December in Wuhan in China, and since then the virus has killed over 200 Chinese people.

The coronavirus causes symptoms similar to flu. Symptoms include, for example, fever and cough.

## Brexit takes effect

![A man raises flags on a flagpole](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11184886)

Open image viewer

In February Britain will no longer be an EU country. Photo: Neil Hall / EPA

Brexit, that is Britain's departure from the European Union, is finally a fact. Britain leaves the European Union on Saturday night, that is, as soon as 1 February begins.

At first no big changes will happen, because Britain will follow the EU's rules until the end of this year. That is why people can travel to Britain as before, and trade continues as normal.

Next year obstacles to trade may come up, if the EU and Britain do not complete a new trade agreement by the end of the year. Negotiations on a new agreement may be difficult.

The Brits voted for leaving the EU already in 2016. The Brits want to reduce immigration from EU countries to Britain, for example.

Pollen is already in the air

## The pollen season has begun.

![Catkins of a hazel bush.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11185899)

Open image viewer

A hazel bush is already flowering in southern Finland. Photo: Anna-Mari Pessi / University of Turku

The University of Turku issued a pollen bulletin on Friday, that is, on the last day of January. Usually the first pollen bulletin of spring is issued much later, only in March or April.

This spring's first bulletin warns allergic people about pollen from the hazel bush. The hazel bush has already started to flower in southern Finland, because the weather is warm.

In northern Finland it is a proper winter, that is, there is snow and frost. That is why the pollen season has not yet started in the north.

## Little snow and a lot of snow

![Snow-clearing equipment has been buried in snow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10616390)

Open image viewer

A snow shovel is needed now in the north, not in the south. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The snow situation in January has been unusual.

Helsinki has not had snow cover on a single day. This is the first time in the measurement history that Helsinki had no snow on any day in January.

At the same time there is a lot of snow in Lapland. On Thursday this week there were 111 centimetres of snow in Kittilä in Lapland. That is a January snow record for Kittilä.
