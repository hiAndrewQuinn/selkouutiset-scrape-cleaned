Recep Tayyip Erdogan will continue as Turkey's president. Erdogan won the second round of the Turkish presidential elections on Sunday. Erdogan got just over 52 percent of the votes.

Opposition candidate Kemal Kilicdaroglu got about 48 percent of the votes. He says that the election was unfair.

Erdogan has already led Turkey for 20 years. He will also be Turkey's president for the next 5 years.

## Plain Speaker of the Year

![Plain Speaker of the Year finalist.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_84/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-112070464733d0f1bd6e/1685274236)

Open image viewer

Selkovantaalainen. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

The Plain Speaker of the Year award has been given.

This year the award went to the Selkovantaalainen newspaper. Selkovantaalainen is the 1st plain-language local newspaper in Finland.

Selkovantaalainen started to come out in autumn 2022. The paper is delivered once a month to all homes in Vantaa.

The winner of the Plain Speaker of the Year award was chosen by the Non-Discrimination Ombudsman, Kristina Stenman. The Plain Speaker of the Year award can be received if someone has done work for plain language in Finland.

Today, Monday, is International Plain Language Day. The Plain Language Centre estimates that more than one in ten Finns need plain language.

More about the winner of the Plain Speaker of the Year award will be told in the TV plain-language news tomorrow, Tuesday

## Mount Everest

![Mount Everest in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5754,h_3236,x_0,y_137/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1044273638f417004907/1670332816)

Mount Everest. Photo: Design Pics Inc / Alamy/All Over Press

Today, Monday, it is 70 years since a human first climbed to the top of Mount Everest.

New Zealander Edmund Hillary and Nepali Tenzing Norgay climbed Mount Everest in 1953.

Mount Everest is the world's highest mountain. The mountain is in the Himalayan range on the border of Nepal and China. Its height is about 8,850 metres.
