# Sunday 18.10.2020 (radio)

Hunting accident. Car accident. Slovakia's coronavirus tests. Coronavirus research. Marjatta Väänänen. The weather.

## Hunting accident

![Cyclist in Urho Kekkonen National Park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11600915)

Open image viewer

The hunting accident happened in a national park with beautiful cycling routes. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

In Lapland a hunting accident has happened. A hunter accidentally shot a cyclist, who died.

The cyclist was 30 years old and came from the Helsinki area. He was cycling in the Lapland nature together with 4 other people.

The police say that the hunter probably shot at the cyclist by accident. The police are investigating the case.

In Finland there are 10–20 hunting accidents a year. However, it is unusual for someone to die in a hunting accident.

## Car accident

![Kiire XL Tuire Joutsenvuo at the Pasila police station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3076,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-6211157d7e367188e1/1473766624)

Open image viewer

The police tried to stop a car that was driving the wrong way on Ring Road III. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

A car accident has happened in Espoo. Two people died in the accident.

The cause of the accident was a car driving the wrong way on Ring Road III. The police tried to stop the car, but it carried on and crashed into another car.

In the other car a man and a woman died. In addition, a man who was driving the car the wrong way was taken to hospital.

The police are investigating the case.

## Slovakia's coronavirus tests

![Yle journalist Pekka Viinikka having a coronavirus test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7037845f22a599b96ce/1596106284)

Open image viewer

Slovakia plans to test everyone over 10 years old for the coronavirus. Photo: Sauli Antikainen / Yle

Central European Slovakia plans to test the whole country for the coronavirus.

Slovakia is testing everyone in the country who has turned 10 years old. The test is free for Slovaks.

Slovakia has as many residents as Finland, but more coronavirus infections have been reported in Slovakia than in Finland.

## Coronavirus on the skin

![A researcher working in a laboratory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11362717)

Open image viewer

Researchers have a lot of work to do when they study the coronavirus. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The coronavirus survives on human skin for a long time. Japanese researchers say that the coronavirus can remain active on human skin for up to 9 hours.

The new information emphasises that good hand hygiene is important. Hand sanitiser affects the virus in just 15 seconds.

## Marjatta Väänänen has died

![Marjatta Väänänen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11600829)

Open image viewer

Marjatta Väänänen. Photo: Pentti Palmu / Yle

Finnish politician Marjatta Väänänen has died. Väänänen was 97 years old.

Marjatta Väänänen's party was the Centre Party. Väänänen was for a long time an MP in Parliament.

Väänänen was also a minister in many governments in the 1970s and 1980s.

## Frost at night

![First snow fell at Äkäslompolo in Kolari on 17 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11600818)

Open image viewer

Snow has fallen in Lapland, and there is night frost across the whole country. Photo: Aku Häyrynen / Lehtikuva

At the weekend it snowed in the north and rained in the south.

On Monday there will still be rain in Central Finland and Eastern Finland.

On Tuesday it does not rain, but the weather is cold. The temperature is below 5 degrees in almost the whole country.

At night there may be frost. That is why the roads may be slippery in the morning, in the south too.
