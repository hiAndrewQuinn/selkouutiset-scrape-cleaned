## Ilkka Kanerva has died

![Ilkka Kanerva looks away from the camera in a black and white photo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2194,h_1234,x_5,y_76/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-942695625886f4521f1/1649993419)

Ilkka Kanerva (28. 1.1948 – 14.4.2022) Photo: Kalle Mäkelä / Yle

Member of Parliament Ilkka Kanerva has died. Kanerva was a National Coalition MP. He was an MP for over 46 years, longer than anyone else in Finland has been an MP.

Ilkka Kanerva was also a minister in three (3) Finnish governments. In this electoral term, Kanerva was chair of the Defence Committee in Parliament. Ilkka Kanerva lived in Turku. He was also a member of Turku City Council.

Ilkka Kanerva has also been involved in many sports organisations. For example, he was chair of the Finnish Sports Federation and vice-chair of the Olympic Committee.

Ilkka Kanerva died on Thursday of a serious illness. He was 74 years old.

## Russian warship Moskva

![Russian flagship Moskva.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_625/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-94218862578ceff08b5/1649904957)

The Russian Black Sea Fleet's important ship Moskva. The picture was taken in Sevastopol in July 2016. Photo: AOP

Russia's important warship Moskva has sunk. Moskva was in the Black Sea.

Ukraine says that Ukraine hit the ship Moskva with two missiles. Russia denies that Ukraine hit the ship. Russia says that there was a fire on board, which spread to an ammunition store.

Moskva was an important warship for Russia. It was 185 metres long. The Moskva carried many missiles. The sinking of the warship Moskva is a big loss for Russia.

People threw stones near the Al-Aqsa mosque. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

There has been violence near the Al-Aqsa mosque in Jerusalem. Israeli police say that a large group of masked men went into the mosque and set off fireworks there.

The group also threw stones towards the Western Wall, which is a holy prayer place for Jews. People also threw stones at Israeli police. The police fired rubber bullets at people. About 150 people were injured in the violence.

There were many people praying at the Al-Aqsa mosque when the violence began. Right now the Christian Easter, the Jewish Passover festival and the Muslim holy month of Ramadan are being celebrated.

## Good Friday

![Painting of Jesus](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10118459)

Open image viewer

Painting of Jesus in Iitti church. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

Today Good Friday has been celebrated. On Good Friday, the crucifixion and death of Jesus at Golgotha are remembered.

On Good Friday in Lutheran churches, a word service is held, but no mass or Eucharist. On Good Friday the liturgical colour in church is black.

On Good Friday church bells are not rung, and hymns are usually sung without accompaniment in church.
