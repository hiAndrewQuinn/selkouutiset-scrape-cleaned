## Olkiluoto nuclear power plant

![Control room of the Olkiluoto nuclear power plant unit OL 3 of Teollisuuden Voima Oyj.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10661881)

Control room of the new power plant. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The new Olkiluoto nuclear power plant may soon be put into use.

The Radiation and Nuclear Safety Authority says that the nuclear power plant is safe. The task of the Radiation and Nuclear Safety Authority is to make sure that nuclear power plants are safe.

Permission from the Finnish government is still needed before the nuclear power plant can be put into use.

The new nuclear power plant is 10 years late. There are already 2 nuclear power plants at Olkiluoto. Olkiluoto is an island in Western Finland.

## The USA's tariff plans

![ Assembly line marriage point at the Uusikaupunki car factory. At this point the car body and the drivetrain are joined.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10654740)

Open image viewer

Cars are made at the Uusikaupunki car factory and exported to the USA. Photo: Valmet Automotive

The USA is planning tariffs on European cars. If the USA imposes large tariffs on European cars, it will also affect Finland's car industry.

The Uusikaupunki car factory makes cars for the German carmaker Daimler. The cars are mainly exported to the USA.

If a car has to carry a large tariff, the price of the car may rise in the USA by more than 5,000 dollars. Then fewer cars are bought.

That may mean that car production in Uusikaupunki also decreases, and jobs may disappear from the car industry.

That is why the car industry hopes that the USA will not impose new tariffs.

## Smoking during pregnancy

![A woman smokes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10232381)

Open image viewer

Some women smoke during pregnancy. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

New information has been obtained about smoking during pregnancy.

A child is more likely to get ADHD if the mother smokes during pregnancy. A child with ADHD finds it hard to concentrate and is hyperactive.

A study by the University of Turku says that nicotine increases the risk of ADHD. Nicotine enters the blood of the mother and the foetus when the mother smokes during pregnancy.

About 12 percent of Finnish mothers smoke at the beginning of pregnancy. 7 percent of mothers continue smoking for the whole pregnancy.

The new Moomin series starts on television and on Yle Areena on Monday. The series is a collaboration between Finns and Britons.
