# Thursday 23.11.2017 (radio)

Shops believe that Finns will buy a lot this Christmas, especially online. The Good Christmas Spirit collection has started. Koreans are making a documentary about how families with children are helped in Finland. The authorities want schoolchildren to drink low-fat milk.

## Christmas shopping

![People in a shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9371775)

Shops are expecting Finns to spend a lot of money on Christmas. Shops believe that Finns will buy a lot this Christmas, especially online.

The Nordea bank has asked people how much money they plan to spend this Christmas. Respondents plan to spend almost 500 euros on Christmas shopping. Of this sum, about 300 euros goes on Christmas presents, and 200 euros goes on other Christmas shopping.

## Christmas collection

![Sari Huovinen interviews Anne Laurinsilta and Milla Kalliomaa at Helsinki Central Station on 21 November.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7643207)

The Good Christmas Spirit collection continues until Christmas. Photo: Yle

The Good Christmas Spirit collection has started.

The organisers of the collection are the Mannerheim League for Child Welfare, the Finnish Red Cross and Yle. They collect money for poor families with children.

The goal is for 30000 families to receive a 70-euro gift card. Families can use the gift card to buy food for the Christmas table.

## Korean documentary

![The film crew and Jaakko and Miika at the Maretarium in Kotka](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9944700)

Open image viewer

Jaakko, Miikka and the Korean film crew. Photo: Ville Pisto / Yle

Many Asian countries are interested in how families with children are helped in Finland.

This week a Korean film crew is visiting Finland. The Koreans first visited schools in the Helsinki area. In the Koreans' view, Finnish schools feel like home.

From Helsinki the film crew travelled to Kotka. There the Koreans met 66-year-old Jaakko Väisänen and 12-year-old Miikka. Jaakko and Miikka got to know each other through the adult buddy activity. The Koreans found it interesting to see how an old man and a young boy can be friends in Finland.

The film crew's documentary about Finland will be shown on South Korean television in December.

## Low-fat milk

![Valio milk](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7402017)

Open image viewer

Schools now drink low-fat milk. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen/Yle

In the authorities' view, low-fat milk is healthy. That is why the state gives schools support only for buying low-fat milk. The authorities say that milk fat is not good for children.

Some municipalities disagree. For example, in Vöyri, schoolchildren can choose whether they drink low-fat milk or light milk. Light milk contains some fat.

Because of the decision, the municipality of Vöyri gets less money from the state, but the schoolchildren are happy. At Vöyri lower secondary school, 90 percent of pupils choose light milk.
