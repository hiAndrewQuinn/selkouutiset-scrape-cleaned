# Monday 1.4.2019 (radio)

Terrorism has been financed through tax fraud. The EU wants to extend the life of household appliances. The upper limit for contactless payment is rising. Today April Fools' jokes have been told.

## Financing of terrorism

![Fighter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10713236)

Open image viewer

Finnish foreign fighters have obtained money, for example through tax fraud.  Photo: Yle / Uutisgrafiikka

Terrorism has been financed in Finland through business activity and economic crimes.

Yle's MOT programme has investigated the financing of terrorism together with other European public broadcasters.

Finnish foreign fighters, for example, have set up shell companies, that is, companies that do no business. With the help of these companies, they have committed tax fraud before leaving the country. Money has also been raised through donations. Similar ways of financing have been used all over Europe.

The police estimate that more than 80 people have left Finland for the conflict areas in Syria and Iraq in recent years. Some of them have joined the terrorist organisation ISIS.

## Durability of household appliances

![Old general-purpose household appliance in use.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10714642)

Open image viewer

This general-purpose machine works well, even though it is more than 40 years old. Photo: Yle

The EU wants to improve the durability and lifespan of household appliances.

The EU requires that all new electrical devices must be repairable. Spare parts must also be available for them.

The EU is preparing a directive on the matter. It may come into force in 2021.

## Upper limit for contactless payment

![Payment card used at a shop checkout.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7735709)

Open image viewer

When a customer pays by contactless payment, they do not need to type in the card's PIN code. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The upper limit for contactless payment rises this spring. Until now the upper limit has been 25 euros. Now it rises to 50 euros.

Contactless payment means that the customer's payment card is read wirelessly at the shop checkout. The card is held close to the payment terminal at the checkout. The customer does not need to type in the card's PIN code.

## April Fools' Day jokes

![Screenshot.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10716449)

Open image viewer

The Eastern Uusimaa police say in an April Fools' tweet that the new pink and metallic surface of uniforms improves the police's work safety. Photo: Twitter

Today April Fools' Day has been celebrated.

1 April is always April Fools' Day. On that day people make April Fools' jokes, that is, try to fool people into believing some silly thing.

April Fools' jokes are made by ordinary people, but also, for example, by newspapers, various companies and authorities.

For example, the Helsinki police said today that they have acquired a drug mule. The police say that with a very good sense of smell the mule finds grass on people. In Finnish the word ruoho can also mean marijuana.

The City of Tampere, meanwhile, says that the Turku funicular, that is, a slope lift, will be brought to the Pispala ridge. The funicular will be painted pink, and a place on its side is reserved for the logos of Tampere's ice hockey teams.
