# Sunday 5.5.2019 (radio)

Parties are discussing Finland's new government. The Muslim holy month of Ramadan begins. Yle is starting to make news in plain Swedish too. Hämeenlinnan Pallokerho is the Finnish ice hockey champion.

## Parties discuss

![SDP chairman Antti Rinne and Centre chairman, Prime Minister Juha Sipilä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10768324)

Parties are discussing Finland's new government. SDP chairman Antti Rinne is meeting the leaders of the other parties.

The SDP is leading the talks between the parties, because it won the elections 3 weeks ago. The SDP is now the largest party in the Finnish Parliament.

Rinne met some of the party leaders on Sunday, and he will meet the rest of the party leaders on Monday.

Rinne may say on Tuesday which parties the negotiations will continue with. Then it will become clear which parties may be part of Finland's new government.

## Ramadan begins

![Man reads the Quran](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10224095)

Open image viewer

Prayer and quiet reflection are part of Ramadan. Photo: Mohammed Badra / EPA

The Muslim holy month of Ramadan begins in different parts of the world. In Finland Ramadan begins on Monday.

In some countries Ramadan already began on Sunday. The start of Ramadan is counted from how the moon can be seen in the sky.

Ramadan includes fasting. During the fast Muslims do not eat or drink while the sun is in the sky.

Ramadan ends on 4 June.

## News in plain Swedish begins

![Marit af Björkesten](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8847173)

Open image viewer

Director Marit af Björkesten. Photo: Kimmo Räisänen / Yle

Yle's plain-language services are growing. Yle is starting to make news also in plain Swedish, that is, in easy Swedish. News in plain Swedish begins on the radio next autumn.

Yle's Swedish-language director Marit af Björkesten says that news in plain Swedish helps, for example, people with intellectual disabilities who speak Swedish to follow the news. News in plain Swedish is also a good help for everyone who is learning Swedish, af Björkesten says.

Yle's news broadcasts in plain Finnish began on the radio in 1992. Yle has had news in plain Finnish on television since 2015.

## HPK is Finnish champion

![HPK Kärpät](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10767719)

Open image viewer

HPK (orange shirts) beat Kärpät (white shirts). Photo: Tomi Hänninen

There was a big celebration in Hämeenlinna on Sunday. The people of Hämeenlinna are celebrating the new Finnish ice hockey champion.

This year the Finnish ice hockey champion is Hämeenlinnan Pallokerho, that is, HPK. HPK played the final games against Oulu's Kärpät.

The exciting last final game was played on Saturday evening. After 3 periods the game was tied at 1-1. In extra time HPK's Markus Nenonen scored the winning goal, and HPK won the game 2-1.

After the game the people of Oulu were sad, but in Hämeenlinna the celebration began.
