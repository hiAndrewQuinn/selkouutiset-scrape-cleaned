The price of a home depends a lot on where the home is.

Home prices are rising most in the centres of big cities. Homes are getting more expensive, for example, in Turku and Oulu. This is the estimate of the technology company Reaktor.

On the Yle News website there is a map where you can look at home prices in different areas. The address is yle.fi/uutiset.

## Eurovision final without Finland

![pianist and singer](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9603940)

Open image viewer

The band Norma John, that is, Lasse Piirainen and Leena Tirronen. Photo: Ilmari Herranen / EPA

Finland did not get into the final of the Eurovision Song Contest. Finland competed for a place in the final on Tuesday in Kyiv, the capital of Ukraine.

The band Norma John, that is, Leena Tirronen and Lasse Piirainen, performed Finland's song. The song was not among the 10 songs that go on to the final.

The final is on Saturday.

## Interview with the King of Sweden

![King Carl Gustaf](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9603791)

Open image viewer

King Carl Gustaf of Sweden. Photo: Yle

The King of Sweden, Carl Gustaf, hopes that the Swedish language will survive in Finland. The king says that a shared language unites people.

The king gave an interview to the Finnish-language newsroom of Swedish Television. He wanted to congratulate the Finns now that independent Finland turns 100.

Finland was part of Sweden for over 600 years. The countries' relations are still very close today.

## Celebrations for the Norwegian royal couple

![The royal couple sit side by side on chairs](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9603734)

Open image viewer

Queen Sonja and King Harald of Norway. Photo: Lise Aserud / EPA

Birthdays are being celebrated in Finland's neighbouring country Norway. King Harald and Queen Sonja of Norway both turn 80 this year. They are now holding a joint birthday celebration.

On Tuesday there was a gala dinner. There were many royals and other important guests at the gala dinner. Finland's president Sauli Niinistö and his wife Jenni Haukio were also at the celebration.

## Presidential Palace open

![the Presidential Palace](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8520785)

Open image viewer

People cannot usually get into the Presidential Palace. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The Presidential Palace is open to the public this week. The doors of the Palace opened on Wednesday, and the Palace is open every day until Saturday.

People can go and see the important rooms of the Palace. The staff of the Presidential Palace answer people's questions. People should set aside about an hour for the visit, the Palace says.

The event is part of the celebrations of Finland's 100 years.

## Museum of the Year chosen

![City Museum](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9603146)

Open image viewer

The renovation of the Helsinki City Museum was finished a year ago. Photo: Helsinki City Museum

The Helsinki City Museum has been chosen as Museum of the Year. The museum's big renovation was finished a year ago.

The Helsinki City Museum got the prize because the people of the city were allowed to take part in renewing the museum. In the museum you can play, try things and touch things, the judges of the competition praised.
