## A senior military person was fined

![Markus Päiviö / Colonel / Officers' court case / Court of Appeal 01.05.2019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5598705ccac40a23dba/1556880017)

The Helsinki Court of Appeal has given a ruling against a senior military person. The court gave a fine to Markus Päiviö. Päiviö is the former commander of the Karelia Air Command.

Päiviö was fined because he behaved badly during an exercise in 2017. For example, Päiviö shouted insults at the mayor of Nurmes. The exercise was a voluntary exercise, which the mayor also took part in.

The court says that Päiviö misused his own position as a superior and insulted the honour of others. That is why Päiviö was fined 3,000 euros in total.

## Finns prefer fast food

![Hamburger](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10404229)

Open image viewer

Photo: Elisa Kinnunen / Yle

Finns often eat at fast food places. The restaurant industry organisation says that sales at fast food places grew by about 7 percent at the start of the year.

The popularity of fast food grows as people move to cities and hurry increases. For example, you can get pizza or a hamburger quickly.

Fast food is not always unhealthy. For example, there are more vegetarian meals at fast food places than before.

Fast food has not reduced the popularity of other restaurant food. Finns eat more at restaurants than before, and the growth continues.

## A skate container teaches skating

![A man skating](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10145476)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

A skate container is a new way to get children and young people moving. At the skate container young people get free skating lessons and can borrow equipment.

The skate container is one of the ideas that Helsinki received from its residents. The city will carry out 10 ideas. The ideas get money for 3 years.

Young people have liked the skate container, and many have started a new hobby of exercise.

## The Crown Prince of Japan is coming to Finland

![Princess Mako in a light brown coat and dark brown hat at Tokyo airport with her parents and sister.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9616695)

Akishino and Kiko (on the left) are coming to Finland soon. Photo: Kimimasa Myama / EPA

Important guests are coming to Finland from Japan next week.

The Crown Prince of Japan, Akishino, and the Crown Princess, Kiko, will visit Finland for 4 days. They will get to know Finnish culture, nature and health care.

This is Akishino's first visit abroad since he became Crown Prince. Akishino is the brother of Japan's new emperor, Naruhito.

## Lower secondary school holidays move in France

![The temperature rose to almost 37 degrees in Godewaersvelde in Northern France on Monday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10846253)

Open image viewer

France is now unhealthily hot. Photo: Philippe Huguen / AFP

Hot weather has upset the summer holidays of lower secondary school pupils in France. The final exams for lower secondary school pupils were supposed to be at the end of this week. The summer holiday starts after the exams.

However, the weather is so hot that the exams have been moved to next week. Only after that will French lower secondary school pupils go on their summer holidays.
