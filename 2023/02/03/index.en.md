# Friday 3.2.2023 (radio)

Industrial pay deal. Data leaker caught in France. New kind of treatment room. Ransunraitti in Tampere. Weather.

## Industrial pay deal

![Two men with microphones in front of them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688821)

Open image viewer

Representatives of employees and employers negotiated in Helsinki on Friday. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

A solution has been found to the pay dispute in industry. The negotiators for employees and employers reached a bargaining result on Friday.

The bargaining result will be described in more detail if the employee and employer organisations officially approve it. The organisations will consider the matter at the weekend.

If the bargaining result is approved, there will be no new strikes in industry. Because of the pay dispute there was a strike in industry this week, when over 10,000 employees stopped work on Wednesday and will return to work only on Saturday.

## Data leaker caught in France

![A man's face and a sign reading Vastaamo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688819)

Open image viewer

25-year-old Julius Kivimäki may be the perpetrator of the Vastaamo data breach. Photo: Europol, Juha Kemppainen / Yle, graphic: Aalto Puutio / Yle

A Finnish man who may have carried out a data breach in Finland has been caught in France. The Finnish police have ordered that the man must be arrested.

The police suspect that the man broke into the information system of the psychotherapy centre Vastaamo a few years ago. The man stole patients' medical records and may have tried to extort money using the information. The case is big, because over 33,000 people's medical records were stolen in the data breach.

The Finnish police hope that France will hand the man over to Finland as quickly as possible. The police want to interrogate the man soon.

## New kind of treatment room

![A hospital treatment room with a lot of equipment.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688789)

Open image viewer

The Raptor room at Helsinki's Siltasairaala hospital. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Helsinki's Siltasairaala hospital has a new kind of treatment room. The treatment room will be put into use in the coming weeks.

The idea of the treatment room is that the patient does not need to be moved from place to place.

All procedures can be carried out in the same place, from examinations all the way to surgery. This is important for patients who have a lot of bleeding, for example because of a traffic accident.

The treatment room is known as the Raptor room. There are still only a few similar Raptor rooms in the world.

## Ransunraitti in Tampere

![Ransu Karvakuono.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10437245)

Open image viewer

Ransu Karvakuono got his own street in Tampere. Photo: Jussi Mansikka / Yle

Tampere has a new street. The street's name is Ransunraitti.

The street is named after Ransu Karvakuono. He is a puppet character in the children's programme Pikku Kakkonen. Ransu Karvakuono has appeared in Pikku Kakkonen for 45 years already.

Pikku Kakkonen is now made at the address Ransunraitti 1. The address is Mediapolis, where Yle programmes are also made.
