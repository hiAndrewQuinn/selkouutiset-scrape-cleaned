# Tuesday 17.3.2020 (radio)

The government has decided on new measures because of the coronavirus. VR is cutting traffic and starting change negotiations about layoffs. Entrance exams for universities of applied sciences are being cancelled.

## Precautions tighten because of the coronavirus

The government has told about new measures and decrees because of the coronavirus.

People should not travel abroad now. Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto said that this is a very strong message to everyone.

Finns in other parts of the world are recommended to return to Finland. The return of Finnish citizens is secured. After returning, they must be in quarantine for 2 weeks.

Border traffic will be restricted, and border checks will be brought back to Finland's internal borders.

Goods traffic, important commuting and other important traffic will be secured so that society does not stop.

The police will start monitoring public gatherings, because gatherings of more than 10 people are banned.

The government says that the medicine situation in Finland is good. Even so, sales restrictions have already come into force at pharmacies.

The restrictions also apply to health care supplies and protective equipment. This is meant to prevent these supplies from being exported out of the country.

The government also told about the jobs in critical sectors. That means sectors whose workers' children can go to school. Face-to-face teaching at school applies to grades 1 to 3.

The list of critical sectors includes, for example, health care, police, rescue services, the border guard, the army and early childhood education teachers. Water supply, waste management and food supply are also included, for example.

## VR cuts trains and lays off staff

![P commuter train](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11259935)

Open image viewer

Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The railway company VR is cutting long-distance and commuter traffic in mid-April. Passenger numbers have fallen by about 60 percent in recent days.

The cuts do not apply to traffic in the Helsinki region.

In addition, VR is starting co-operation negotiations about layoffs, which apply to all employees.

The layoffs will come in several stages this year. Personal layoffs may last for a maximum of 90 days.

Many other companies have already reported layoffs as a result of the coronavirus.

## Corona changed the entrance exams for universities of applied sciences

![A woman studies and writes on a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2811,x_0,y_367/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-320743586e045193418/1483605269)

The universities of applied sciences are cancelling the entrance exams for the first spring joint application. Because of the coronavirus and the state of emergency, international students cannot take part in entrance exams in Finland.

The universities of applied sciences are now using selection based on certificates and selection tasks when choosing students for courses taught in a foreign language.

The results of the first spring joint application will be published as normal by 5 June at the latest.

In the first application there are 3,200 study places for courses taught in a foreign language, and there are 11,000 applicants for them.
