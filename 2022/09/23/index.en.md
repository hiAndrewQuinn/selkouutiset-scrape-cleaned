# Friday 23.9.2022 (radio)

Russians' trips to Finland. Russians crossing the border. The Russia–Georgia border. Travel agency Detur. Weather forecast.

## Russians' access to Finland

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin at the press conference after question time in the State Hall of the Parliament House in Helsinki on 22 September 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_466/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1011620632c73722e4de/1663857626)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the government wants to end Russians' travel to Finland. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The government wants to end Russians' trips to Finland. The aim is to prevent Russians from travelling through Finland to elsewhere in Europe.

The government has asked officials to find out how the arrival of Russian tourists can be prevented. The officials are examining the Schengen rules and Finnish law.

Russians' travel to Finland may perhaps be banned on the basis of the EU's Schengen rules. Russians' entry may perhaps be restricted because Russian tourism to Finland harms Finland's international relations.

## Russians at the Finnish border

![Cars coming from Russia in a queue at the Vaalimaa border station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5536,h_3114,x_0,y_81/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1011927632d8928c41e1/1663929284)

Open image viewer

Traffic at the Nuijamaa border station. Photo: Kalle Purhonen / Yle

The number of Russians crossing the border has increased in South-East Finland. The number of border crossers has also grown at the eastern border in Niirala in Lapland.

The reason may be Russia's mobilisation on Wednesday. Many Russian men may want to leave Russia.

Yesterday, Thursday, almost 6,000 Russians came to Finland. The number almost doubled compared with last week's days. The number of arrivals is growing, but there is no congestion at the border yet. Finland is monitoring the situation at the border stations.

## The Russia–Georgia border

![A young man detained at protests in St Petersburg. He holds his hands up. Many police are visible in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3749,h_2108,x_0,y_78/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1011705632d3f7d2d05e/1664211929)

Open image viewer

Police have detained protesters in Russia who oppose the mobilisation. Photo: Anatoly Maltsev / EPA

Border stations between Russia and Georgia have become badly congested. Border crossing can take up to 12 hours. The reason may be Russia's mobilisation. Many Russian men may leave Russia so that they do not have to go to war.

Georgia is one of a few countries where Russians can travel without a visa. That is why border traffic to Georgia has become congested.

On the Russian side there are queues of cars several kilometres long to the border station.

## Travel agency Detur

![Travel agency Detur Finland Oy on Eerikinkatu in Helsinki, 17 January 2011.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3837,h_2153,x_0,y_229/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1011983632d918c06f38/1663930854)

Open image viewer

Detur is suspending the organisation of trips. Photo: Kimmo Mäntylä / Lehtikuva

Travel agency Detur is suspending the organisation of trips. Detur is cancelling all trips for the rest of the year. Passengers who are at holiday destinations abroad will be brought back to Finland by the end of September.

Detur may have financial problems. Hotels have demanded payments from Detur's customers for overnight stays, even though the customers have paid for the whole holiday. Hotels have demanded payments from customers in Turkey, Rhodes and Crete.

The Competition and Consumer Authority says that people do not need to pay for their accommodation to the hotel, because people have already paid for the accommodation to Detur.
