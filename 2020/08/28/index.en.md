## The nuclear power plant starts operating in 2022

![Olkiluoto 3 nuclear power plant](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4784,h_2691,x_0,y_52/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5978065d9c8cd9f1823/1573223366)

The new nuclear power unit at Olkiluoto will start operating in 2022. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

In Eurajoki in Western Finland, a new nuclear power plant has already been under construction for 14 years.

Now the owner of the nuclear power plant, Teollisuuden Voima, says that the nuclear power plant will start producing electricity in 2022.

The construction of the nuclear power plant has been delayed a great deal. It was supposed to be ready in 2009.

When the nuclear power plant is completed, there will be 3 nuclear power units in Eurajoki.

Then there will be 5 nuclear power plants in Finland in total.

## Little rye is coming from Finnish fields

![A rye field](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2646,h_1487,x_0,y_83/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-2092554f5b3707e85b/1425388409)

Open image viewer

Rye grows tall. A lot of rye is eaten in Finland, in rye bread. Photo: Yle / Seppo Sarkkinen

This year there will be a poor grain harvest in Finland.

The Natural Resources Institute says that rye in particular will be much less than last year.

Last year 183 million kilos of rye were harvested from Finnish fields. This year only 70 million kilos of rye will come.

So little rye will come from Finnish fields that it will not be enough for consumption.

In Finland, for example, a lot of rye bread is eaten.

Bakeries now have to bake rye bread from foreign rye.

## Donald Trump is officially the candidate

In the USA, President Donald Trump is now officially the Republican Party's presidential candidate.

Trump accepted the candidacy in front of the White House in Washington.

He also gave a policy speech. In the speech Trump stressed the achievements of his own presidential term. He also spoke about the other candidate in the election. Trump said that the Democrats' candidate Joe Biden is a radical leftist.

The US presidential election will be held in November.

## A polar bear and a man died

![A polar bear looks at the camera](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9530542)

Open image viewer

A polar bear does not often attack people. Photo: Andy Rouse / AOP

Another strange and sad news from Norway.

In Svalbard in Norway a polar bear attacked a man. The man died.

The man had been on a tent trip at a campsite.

The polar bear also died, because it had been shot.

A polar bear does not attack people very often. The last time was almost 10 years ago.
