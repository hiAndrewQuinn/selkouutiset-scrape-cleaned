# Saturday 3.11.2018 (radio)

Pekka Haavisto is the new Green League chair. A storm has killed people in Italy. All Saints' Day was celebrated on Saturday. The arctic fox may disappear completely from Finland and other Nordic countries.

## Green League chair

![Pekka Haavisto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10491206)

Green League chair Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Pekka Haavisto is the new Green League chair. The Green League chose a new chair on Saturday.

Haavisto is an MP in the Finnish Parliament. He clearly won the Green League chair election.

Haavisto was also Green League chair before, more than 20 years ago. Haavisto has also been a minister and the Greens' presidential candidate.

The new chair's first important task is the election campaign. The parliamentary elections are already in 5 months' time.

The Green League had to choose a new chair, because the former chair, Touko Aalto, fell ill with depression. Aalto said last week that he is leaving the post of chair.

## Italian storm

![Firefighters move a tree that has fallen on the road in Liliana in northern Italy. The tree fell onto a car, killing two people.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10491177)

Open image viewer

Firefighters are moving fallen trees. Photo: EPA

There is a strong storm in Italy. The storm has lasted for many days.

At least 20 people have died in the storm. Many of them were trapped under trees when the storm winds knocked trees down. Lightning killed 2 people on Friday.

Many roads in Italy are blocked, because they are under water and mud. The streets of the famous city of Venice, for example, are also under water.

Meteorologists forecast that the storm will continue in Italy all weekend.

## All Saints' Day

![Memorial candles on a grave](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8417449)

Open image viewer

On All Saints' Day, candles are taken to graves. Photo: Nina Keski-Korpela / Yle

All Saints' Day was celebrated on Saturday. On All Saints' Day, Finns remember dead relatives and friends. That is why candles are taken to graves.

All Saints' Day is an old festival. Nowadays many Finns celebrate Halloween before All Saints' Day.

Halloween has come to Finland from America. Children especially like Halloween, the festival of pumpkins and ghosts.

## Arctic fox

![Arctic fox](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10491187)

Open image viewer

The arctic fox has a beautiful white coat. Photo: AOP

The arctic fox may disappear completely from Finland and the other Nordic countries. There are only about 250 arctic foxes left in the Nordic countries. They live in the northern fell areas.

Arctic foxes have not made dens in Finland for over 20 years. The last time an arctic fox made a den and had pups in Finland was in 1996. Each year, 5 to 10 arctic foxes wander through Finland.

In the past arctic foxes were hunted, because the arctic fox has a beautiful white coat. Arctic foxes are no longer hunted, but now climate warming threatens them.
