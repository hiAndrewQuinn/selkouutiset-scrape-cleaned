# Sunday 22.3.2020 (radio)

Lapland's ski resorts are closing. There are more than 100 new coronavirus infections a day in Finland. Finnair is bringing Finns home from abroad. A medicine for the coronavirus is being sought.

## Lapland's ski resorts are closing

![Skiers on a chairlift at Ylläs in Kolari.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11269492)

Skiers on a chairlift at Ylläs. Photo: Aku Häyrynen / Lehtikuva

The big ski resorts in Lapland close their slopes today. Ylläs, Levi, Luosto, Olos, Pallas and Ounasvaara close their slopes this evening. Ruka and Pyhä close their slopes tomorrow, on Monday evening.

The ski resorts originally planned to close the slopes in about a week. However, the closing was brought forward, when it was reported yesterday that many Finns had gone to Lapland to ski.

The ski resorts do not want any more tourists to come to Lapland now, because then coronavirus infections may also increase.

The government has said that people must now stay at home as much as possible. Gatherings of more than 10 people are also banned.

## More than 100 new coronavirus infections

![A sign leading to the Malmi coronavirus health centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11269992)

Open image viewer

Coronavirus health centre in Malmi, Helsinki. Photo: Eleni Paspatis / Yle

THL, the National Institute for Health and Welfare, has published new information on the number of coronavirus infections in Finland. THL says that there are now more than 620 infections in total. More than 100 new infections have come in a day.

There are 43 patients in hospital care. 12 patients are in intensive care. So far one person has died of the coronavirus.

Most of the coronavirus infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district.

## Finnair is bringing Finns home

![Finnair plane in the air.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_115/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5114885ba4cba435583/1572976411)

The airline Finnair has added flights so that Finnish travellers can get back home from holiday destinations abroad.

During the weekend Finnair has arranged 11 extra flights from Málaga in Spain to Finland.

More flights have been added for Tuesday. Then the flights leave from Las Palmas and Tenerife in the Canary Islands, and from Antalya in Turkey.

## A medicine for the coronavirus is being sought

![Coronavirus samples being tested in a laboratory in Stuttgart.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11269900)

Open image viewer

Coronavirus samples are being tested in a laboratory. Photo: Arnulf Hettrich / AOP

A medicine for the coronavirus is being sought in great haste. A medicine is being sought perhaps in hundreds of studies in various parts of the world.

One possible medicine has been developed in Turku, Finland.

The Turku company Faron Pharmaceuticals has developed the medicine Traumakine. The medicine was originally meant for a lung disease in which the patient has breathing difficulties. The coronavirus can also cause pneumonia and breathing difficulties.

The pharmaceutical company Faron says that in its studies the Traumakine medicine has been effective. However, the medicine must be tested more before it can be used.

There is still no medicine or vaccine against the coronavirus.
