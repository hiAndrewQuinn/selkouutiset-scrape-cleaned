## Ukraine

![A firefighter looks up at the ruins of a building, helmet on his head.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12359260)

Russia has carried out strikes on Kyiv. Photo: Roman Pilipey / EPA

The war in Ukraine continues.

In Ukraine's capital Kyiv the authorities have imposed a ban on going outside. During it, people may not go out.

The ban starts on Tuesday evening, and it lasts 35 hours. The reason for the ban on going outside is that Russia is bombing Kyiv.

The UN says that more than 3 million people have fled Ukraine because of the war. About half of the refugees are children.

Most Ukrainians have fled to neighbouring countries. Most of Ukraine's refugees are in Poland.

The war in Ukraine began on 24 February, when Russia attacked Ukraine.

Students on their way to a matriculation examination in Tampere. Photo: Miikka Varila / Yle

The spring matriculation examinations have begun. The first exam was the mother tongue reading skills test.

On Tuesday the Finnish as a second language exam and the Swedish as a second language exam were also held. They are for students who have a mother tongue other than Finnish or Swedish.

The matriculation examinations are being reformed this spring. All students must take at least 5 subjects. Until now 4 subjects have been enough.

The purpose of the reform is that students learn many things and get a good general education. Student organisations oppose the reform.

## A stomach bug

![Hands holding a stomach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9950330)

Open image viewer

Norovirus causes a stomach bug that starts with stomach ache. Photo: Elin Willows / Yle

There is a strong stomach bug going around in Finland. The cause of the stomach bug is norovirus.

Norovirus spreads to children and adults. Symptoms come quickly.

The first symptoms are stomach ache and nausea. After that the patient starts vomiting and often also gets diarrhoea. Some patients also have a fever. The symptoms last 1–3 days.

Norovirus spreads easily. The best way to prevent norovirus is to wash your hands.

## Streets

![Brush of a street sweeper.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5336447)

Open image viewer

Now is the time to clear the winter gravel off the streets. Photo: Mari Siltanen / Yle

Cleaning of the streets has begun. In Helsinki, for example, some streets are already so dry that cleaning can start.

There is a lot of work this year. There has been a lot of snow and ice on the streets in winter. That is why gravel, that is, crushed stone, has often been spread on the streets.

Cleaning streets in spring is important, because street dust rises into the air from the streets.

On Wednesday the sun shines across almost the whole country, but in Western Lapland it is cloudy.

The temperature in Southern and Central Finland is between +4 and +8 degrees, and in the north between -5 and +5 degrees.

Later in the week it is also dry and sunny. From the west, however, a few showers may come.
