# Sunday 23.12.2018 (radio)

Tsunami in Indonesia. Blood pressure medicine removed from sale. A busy day for Christmas tourists in Lapland. More snow will fall at Christmas.

## Serious tsunami in Indonesia

![Map showing the centre of the tsunami.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10570311)

Open image viewer

The tsunami was created between Java and Sumatra. Photo: Yle News Graphics

There has been a serious tsunami in Indonesia. The tsunami waves reached the shore on the islands of Java and Sumatra in the Sunda Strait. The tsunami happened on Saturday evening. According to the authorities, the waves were about 2 metres high.

Many people have died in the tsunami, at least 220. At least over 800 have been injured. The tsunami was apparently caused by a volcanic eruption.

Shortly before the tsunami, a volcano named Anak Krakatau erupted in the area. It is suspected that the eruption caused landslides on the seabed. Underwater landslides have created the tsunami.

Residents say that the tsunami struck suddenly. It could not be warned about.

## More than 700 Finns in Indonesia

![Aerial photo of the tsunami's destruction.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10570440)

Open image viewer

The tsunami destroyed many houses. Photo: Azwar Ipank / AFP

Finnish authorities are investigating whether any Finns have died in the tsunami. On Sunday during the day, it was believed that no Finns had died in the tsunami.

However, there are many Finns in Indonesia. Over 700 Finns have told the Ministry for Foreign Affairs that they are travelling in Indonesia.

The tsunami area has not been popular with tourists. The area is now in its rainy season. That reduces the number of tourists.

## Blood pressure medicine was removed from sale, but patients were not told

![medicine jar](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10010895)

Open image viewer

In Finland, a total of 2 blood pressure medicines have been taken off sale this year. Photo: AOP

The Finnish Medicines Agency has removed one blood pressure medicine from sale, but the agency has not announced the removal. The Medicines Agency Fimea gave an order to pharmacies in October. The order was not announced to patients or the media, for example.

Fimea says that the amount of the medicine was small. Fimea does not say which company brought the medicine into Finland.

Fimea says that if the medicine is used for a long time, that is, for many years, the risk of getting cancer may grow. According to the agency, the risk is very small. The blood pressure medicine was taken off sale because one ingredient of the medicine was not pure enough.

## A busy day for Christmas tourists in Lapland

![A Sami-language caption: the first charter of this season, 30.11.2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10536890)

Christmas tourist season is now very busy in Lapland. Photo: Vesa Toppari / Yle

Today was a very busy day for Christmas tourists in Finland. More than 90 aircraft landed at the airports of Rovaniemi, Kittilä, Ivalo, Kuusamo and Enontekiö. More than 60 of them came from abroad.

Many Christmas tourists came to Finland from Britain. The newspaper Lapin Kansa reported, for example, on the busy tourist day.
