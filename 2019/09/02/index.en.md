# Monday 2.9.2019 (radio)

The postal strike has started. Hurricane Dorian is raging in America. Yle's TV news turns 60. Teemu Pukki is doing well in the English Premier League. The Read Together network teaches Finnish to immigrants.

## Postal strike

![Delivery box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10951061)

Open image viewer

The post does not run normally during the strike. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

The postal strike has started. Postal workers are on strike until Wednesday.

The reason for the strike is that the working conditions of about 700 workers are changing. That means, for example, lower pay for the workers.

During the strike the post does not always run normally. Therefore, for example, Kela says that it is now a good idea to submit applications online.

## Dorian storm

![Satellite image of Hurricane Dorian moving over the Bahamas on 1 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10949783)

Open image viewer

Satellite picture of storm Dorian. Photo: AFP

Storm Dorian is raging in America.

On Monday Dorian hit the Bahamas. Dorian is a hurricane, that is, a tropical storm.

The wind in storm Dorian has blown at as much as 97 metres per second. The rain is also heavy. The sea level has risen so much that many houses have been left under water.

The storm has caused a lot of damage in the Bahamas. It is not yet known how many people have died.

Storm Dorian is now moving towards the USA. Next it will hit Florida.

In the USA people have prepared for the storm. They have bought food to store and covered the windows of their houses.

## TV news

![Yle television news studio on 26 December 2018, the set and stage props on the last evening before moving to temporary premises. News anchor Piia Pasanen is in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10571683)

Piia Pasanen is a popular news anchor on TV news. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Yle's TV news turns 60. Yle's television news operations began in 1959.

To mark the anniversary, Yle's website has newsreels from different years. The old newsreels tell a lot about the world, Finland and the lives of Finns over 60 years.

The anniversary article is called Elämäsi uutiset (Your News of Life). In the article you can look at the news from your own birth year, for example.

The article and the newsreels can be found at yle.fi.

## Teemu Pukki

![Teemu Pukki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10950967)

Open image viewer

Footballer Teemu Pukki. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

Finnish footballer Teemu Pukki is doing well in the English Premier League. Now Pukki has been chosen as the Premier League's player of the month.

Pukki plays for Norwich. Pukki has scored almost all of Norwich's goals this season.

Pukki is happy that he was chosen as player of the month. "A great honour," Pukki wrote on Twitter.

## Read Together

![Soili Harjula](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10951030)

Open image viewer

Soili Harjula has been chosen as the Volunteer of the Year for the Read Together network. Groups gather every week in different parts of Finland. Photo: Marko Tammi / Yle

The Read Together network teaches Finnish to immigrants. There are courses in different parts of Finland.

On the courses a large group of volunteers teaches immigrants the Finnish language and things about Finland. One of them is Soili Harjula, who was chosen as Volunteer of the Year.

You can read an interview with Soili Harjula on the Clear News website at yle.fi/selkouutiset.
