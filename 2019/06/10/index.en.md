# Monday 10.6.2019 (radio)

Tick vaccination is recommended for all children over 2 who move in nature in risk areas. Finland may start making synthetic petrol and diesel from surplus factory gases. Buying food online continues to grow. A holiday can be a risk for a relationship.

## Tick vaccination recommended for young children in risk areas

![Satu Väistö and her children in the garden in Muhkuri, Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10796849)

All of the children in Satu Väistö's family in Turku are vaccinated against ticks, because they spend their summer cottage time in Kustavi. Ticks have also been seen in the home yard. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

An expert recommends tick vaccination for all children over 2 years old who move in nature in risk areas. Such areas include, for example, the coastal region and the Turku archipelago.

About one per cent of the ticks in risk areas spread tick-borne encephalitis, that is, TBE. Only vaccination helps against it.

The communicable disease statistics show that tick-borne encephalitis was detected last year about as often as in 2017. There were 79 cases, mostly in known risk areas. The most cases were in Parainen.

Ticks also spread another disease, Lyme borreliosis. There is no vaccine against Lyme borreliosis, and it is treated with antibiotics.

## Synthetic petrol reduces traffic emissions

![Metsä Group's Kemi mill.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10798518)

Open image viewer

Metsä Group's Kemi mill. Photo: Antti Ullakko / Yle

Finland may start making synthetic petrol and diesel from surplus gases from factories. The gases can be captured, for example, from the chimney of a pulp mill.

Synthetic petrol can greatly reduce traffic emissions. Finland intends to halve traffic emissions by 2030.

A researcher says that there is no longer any need to dig fossil fuels out of the ground. Now carbon dioxide that is already in the air can be recycled. The LUT University says that a factory for synthetic fuels must be built in Finland.

Synthetic fuels may become a big business around the world within 10 years. Wärtsilä from Finland is taking part in planning a pilot plant in the USA.

## Online food shopping grows

![Patrik Vähähyyppä in the picture](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10809914)

Open image viewer

Patrik Vähähyyppä delivers shopping to homes almost every day. Customers include, for example, families with children and older people. Photo: Heini Holopainen / Yle

Buying food online continues to grow. At Kesko, for example, online food sales grew by 100 per cent in the early part of the year compared with the previous year.

However, the share of online food sales in the whole of Finland is still small, only 0.4 per cent.

Customers now have several options for ordering food. There are also more shops than before in the countryside, from which food is delivered to homes or to a pick-up point.

In growth centres, big shop chains can build efficient warehouses for online food sales. Then picking is efficient, and transport can be outsourced.

## A holiday is not always a time of happiness in a relationship

![hand in hand](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_60/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5235285bed305f7d0a4/1542271148)

Open image viewer

A holiday can be a hard time for a relationship if too many expectations are attached to it. Photo: AOP

A long-awaited and lovely holiday can strain a relationship. Sometimes the crisis is so bad that it ends in a separation.

Experts say that a lot of expectations are loaded onto a holiday. Still, they may not be shared with the other person.

Often the holiday starts after hard work, and the person is tired.

The nature of the relationship must also be taken into account. A distant relationship does not turn close in an instant.
