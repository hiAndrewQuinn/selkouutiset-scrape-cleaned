# Friday 21.1.2022 (radio)

President Niinistö talked with President Putin. The USA talked with Russia. The corona isolation guidance changed. The Paper Union's strike at power plants must end. Weather forecast.

Finnish President Sauli Niinistö has talked with Russian President Vladimir Putin by phone.

The presidents discussed the situation in Ukraine and security in Europe.

President Niinistö said that he is worried about the situation in Ukraine. Niinistö said that peace is needed in Europe. Niinistö also said that Finland decides for itself on Finland's security policy.

## The USA and Russia talked

Also US Secretary of State Antony Blinken and Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov have talked about the situation in Ukraine. Blinken and Lavrov met in Geneva, Switzerland.

Russia has demanded that the military alliance NATO does not expand eastwards. Lavrov says that the USA promised to give written answers on the matter next week.

US Secretary of State Blinken says that the USA warns Russia not to attack Ukraine. Blinken says that the USA will respond to an attack if Russia attacks Ukraine.

## New corona isolation guidance

![A positive result of a home corona test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_135/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90457261ea77dc997db/1642756977)

Open image viewer

A positive result in a corona home test. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The isolation practice for corona disease is changing. Isolation of corona patients is almost completely being abandoned in Finland. The reason is that isolation no longer prevents the coronavirus from spreading.

The new guidance is that a person isolates themselves if they have corona disease. A corona patient must also tell other people they have met that they have been exposed.

The new guidelines will be introduced in the Helsinki and Uusimaa, Pirkanmaa, Central Finland, North Ostrobothnia and North Savo hospital districts, and in Turku.

## The Paper Union's strike at power plants

![UPM Kaukas power plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4606,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89816161d2a3ba9bac4/1643269728)

Open image viewer

UPM's Kaukas power plant in Lappeenranta. Photo: Kari Saastamoinen / Yle

The Paper Union must end its strike at the power plants of the forest company UPM's factories. The Helsinki District Court has ordered that the strike must end, because the operation of the power plants is very important for people and the environment.

The District Court says that the strike may be dangerous for people and the environment if the power plants do not work.

The court's decision means that the Paper Union must end the strike at UPM's power plants, which are in 6 places in Finland.
