In the opinion of Finland's Prime Minister Juha Sipilä, Finland must pay more development aid.

According to Prime Minister Sipilä, the development aid that Finland pays has had to be cut, because Finland's economy has been put in order.

## The climate is warming

![Sunbathers on the beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10382671)

Open image viewer

Sunbathers at the Hietaniemi beach in Helsinki. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

It has been hot in Finland this summer. The exceptional heat tells us about the future climate.

The climate will continue to warm. It is possible to predict future temperatures on the basis of climate models. According to a very positive forecast, Helsinki in July 2080 will resemble Brussels, the capital of Belgium.

According to the forecast, the average temperature in Helsinki in July 2080 may be 18.7 degrees. That is 1.5 degrees more than now.

## Diabetes is becoming more common

![Equipment used in the treatment of diabetes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9981476)

Open image viewer

Diabetes care equipment. Photo: Tuulia Thynell / Yle

According to a recent study, diabetes is increasing quickly. The diabetes study found the disease in young people.

In the 1990s, people who got diabetes were often in their fifties. Now people who get diabetes can be only 25 years old.

Many factors increase diabetes. Diet affects a lot how much fat builds up in the liver. Sweet drinks such as juices and soft drinks increase the risk of diabetes.

According to the study, eating whole grains keeps the disease at bay. Whole grains are found, for example, in bread. Coffee may also help prevent illness.

## Cannabis as medicine

![Cannabis flower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10358562)

Open image viewer

Cannabis flower. Photo: Jon Santa Cruz / AOP

Cannabidiol contained in cannabis can work as an antipsychotic medicine.

Cannabidiol is a chemical compound. Cannabidiol is not an intoxicant. According to the study, cannabidiol has fewer side effects than current antipsychotic medicines.

The researchers scanned the brains of 33 young people. The young people experienced anxious and psychotic symptoms.

The young people were given cannabidiol, and it reduced abnormal activity in three parts of the brain. These three parts of the brain are linked, for example, to the onset of schizophrenia.
