## Finnair flight attendants and stewards strike

![Finnair plane at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. There is snow on the ground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4252,h_2391,x_0,y_96/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-915541620bb711a2406/1733249897)

Flight attendants and stewards are angry, because Finnair intends to cut jobs. Photo: Markku Ojala / AOP

Finnair's flight attendants and stewards are organising a walkout on Sunday, that is, they will stop work. The walkout concerns flights departing from Helsinki-Vantaa airport.

The cabin crew walkout at Finnair starts on Sunday at 3 p.m. and ends on Monday at 3 p.m. The reason for the walkout is Finnair's plan to cut staff.

Finnair said this week that it wants to outsource cabin services on the routes to Thailand and North America. Because of this, the work of about 450 people at Finnair may end.

## Rescue of Tapio Lehtinen

![Tapio Lehtinen sailing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_210/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10363496378925ed7377/1668846219)

Open image viewer

Tapio Lehtinen. Photo: Lehtikuva / Markku Ulander

Finnish sailor Tapio Lehtinen has been rescued from a maritime emergency. Tapio Lehtinen's sailing boat sank on Friday in the Indian Ocean.

Lehtinen was sailing alone. He was taking part in a solo sailing race.

Lehtinen had a rescue raft, and he reached safety on it for the night. Another competitive sailor, the South African Kirsten Neuschäfer, reached Lehtinen on Saturday morning. A cargo ship also came to the scene and took Lehtinen on board.

## Dog registration

![Microchipped litter of border collies](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-787913605b43208241c/1616594043)

Open image viewer

Dog registration prevents abuses in dog breeding. Photo: Marko Melto / Yle

Dog registration becomes compulsory in Finland at the start of next year. The dog register is kept by the Finnish Food Authority.

Information on all dogs in Finland will be collected into the dog register. The dog's name, date of birth, breed and the details of the dog's identification microchip will be recorded in the register.

Dog registration becomes compulsory, because registration prevents abuses in dog breeding and sales. The aim is that compulsory registration of dogs will also reduce the abandoning of dogs, because the owner of the dog can be found.

![Smoke rising from the city of Kyiv.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1677,h_943,x_0,y_173/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1027554636004deb2427/1667237138)

There are power cuts in large areas of Ukraine. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky says that the power is out, for example, in the capital Kyiv and in the Odesa region, and in more than 10 other areas.

Perhaps about half of Ukraine's electricity network is broken because of Russian missile strikes.

## Men's Day

![Men in a hot tub.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_66/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97752862c057d7ef886/1656774762)

Men's Day is celebrated in many countries. In Finland it is not an official day. Photo: Jussi Mankkinen / Yle

On Saturday, the international Men's Day has been celebrated in many countries.

49 percent of Finns are men. The most common men's names are Juha and Juhani. Statistics Finland says this on Saturday, because it is Men's Day.

Men's Day is quite a new day. It has been celebrated since 1999. In Finland, Men's Day is not celebrated officially. Men's Day is not in the calendar, for example.
