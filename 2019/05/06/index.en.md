# Monday 6.5.2019 (radio)

41 people have died and several have been injured in an air accident in Moscow. The Arctic Council meeting begins in Rovaniemi, Lapland. The spring joint application shows a rush for popular courses. There are shortcomings in Helsinki's rescue services.

## Over 40 people died in an aircraft fire in Moscow

![An Aeroflot Sukhoi Superjet-type aircraft burned fiercely on the runway at Moscow's Sheremetjevo airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10768538)

An Aeroflot Sukhoi Superjet-type aircraft burned fiercely on the runway at Moscow's Sheremetjevo airport. Photo: Russian Investigative Committee

In Moscow more than 40 people have died and several have been injured in an emergency landing and fire of an aircraft.

The Aeroflot aircraft was on its way to Murmansk on Sunday. Soon after take-off the aircraft had to make an emergency landing back at the airport.

There were 78 people on board.

The airline says that the aircraft's engines only caught fire on landing.

## Arctic Council meets in Rovaniemi

![Arctic Council](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9613467)

Open image viewer

The Arctic Council has 8 member countries. In addition, indigenous peoples' organisations take part in the meeting. Photo: Arktalaš ráđđi

The Arctic Council meeting begins in Rovaniemi, Lapland.

The Arctic Council has 8 member countries. They include, besides the Nordic countries, Canada, Russia and the United States. In addition there are 6 organisations that represent indigenous peoples.

On Tuesday a declaration will be signed at the meeting. There has been dispute about it, because the United States wants climate issues removed from the declaration.

Finland is the chair of the meeting. Finland wants the declaration to mention meteorological cooperation between Arctic countries. It helps to make better weather forecasts.

Finland's term has lasted 2 years, and the next chair is Iceland.

Russia's Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov and the United States' Foreign Minister Mike Pompeo will meet at the meeting.

## Joint application: popular fields are crowded

![Statistics graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10735548)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mikko Airikka | Yle

Popular courses are crowded in the spring joint application.

More than 72,000 students applied for a place in upper secondary education. About half applied for vocational studies and half for upper secondary school.

Dental care was the most popular target of application. About 4 times as many students applied for it as there are starting places.

Other favourites are medical technology services, theatre and dance, and pharmacy and other medicine supply.

Upper secondary schools had 3,000 more starting places than first-choice applicants.

## Helsinki rescue department faces a conditional fine

![Helsinki City Rescue Department fire engine at traffic lights.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10751835)

Open image viewer

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There are shortcomings in Helsinki's rescue services. The Regional State Administrative Agency, AVI, has imposed a conditional fine on the city of Helsinki. The fine is 20 million euros.

The shortcomings are especially in northern Helsinki and north-eastern Helsinki.

AVI says that in practice the fire brigade does not arrive at the fire scene quickly enough. The Ministry of the Interior says that the target time is 6 minutes.

60 percent of Helsinki's areas do not meet the target time.
