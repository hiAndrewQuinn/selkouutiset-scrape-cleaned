# Friday 22.9.2017 (radio)

A stabbing happened in front of Parliament. Girls are taken abroad for circumcision. Copying a bank card can become expensive. Outpatient care is popular in mental health care.

## Incident in front of Parliament

![Police officers and an ambulance in front of Parliament in Helsinki on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9846465)

Police and an ambulance in front of the Parliament building on Friday.  Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

A stabbing has happened in front of the Parliament building. Parliament's head of security says that 2 young foreign men came to sit on the steps of the Parliament building and both stabbed themselves in the stomach. Both men had the yellow identity card of a reception centre, which means they are apparently asylum seekers. The incident caused no danger to other people, even though a plenary session of Parliament was about to begin.

The police came quickly. An ambulance took the men to hospital.

## Girls are taken abroad for circumcision

![a razor blade](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9845718)

Open image viewer

Circumcision is dangerous and painful for girls.  Photo: AOP

Finnish girls are taken from Finland abroad for genital mutilation, or circumcision. Female circumcision is an old tradition in which part of the genitals is cut off and, for example, the labia may be sewn together. A new report says that the procedures are done in Egypt, Somalia, Syria and Iran. Doctors say that female circumcision is dangerous and causes health problems. It is also illegal in Finland.

## Copying a bank card can be a surprise

![Money and payment cards](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8842293)

Open image viewer

Bank card details are enough for shopping.  Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

A bank card must be kept carefully at the workplace too. The bank card details of a Lidl shop employee had been copied down while the wallet was in the staff changing room. Someone had used the details to play online games for almost 1,000 euros. The bank card was never missing at any point.

The bank thought that the employee had been careless, and the bank did not pay the money back. The Consumer Disputes Board said that banks have not told customers enough about the copying of card details. That is why the customer only has to pay 150 euros herself, and she gets the rest of the money back from the bank.

## Outpatient care is popular in mental health care

![The silhouette of a man in a dimly lit corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9843604)

Open image viewer

Outpatient care is based on the use of medicines.  Photo: Allan Swart / AOP

In Finland, a lot of outpatient care is used in psychiatric care. In outpatient care, a person lives at home and visits a doctor regularly. Outpatient care is cheaper because the treatment is based on medicines. Living at home is nicer than living in a hospital. In the old days you had to stay in a psychiatric hospital for almost a year at a time; now hospital care lasts a few months thanks to medicines.

A researcher says that mental health care can be as good in outpatient care as in hospital, but some people definitely need hospital care.
