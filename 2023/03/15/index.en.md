## Olkiluoto 3 nuclear power plant

Olkiluoto 3 nuclear power plant is important for Finland's energy production. Photo: Katja Halinen / Yle

The third reactor at the Olkiluoto nuclear power plant is producing electricity again. The trial operation of the third reactor at the nuclear power plant was started on Wednesday morning.

There was a long pause in the trial operation, because the plant's valves were repaired. The energy company TVO said on Wednesday that the reactor's production will be raised, so that on Wednesday evening the reactor runs at full power.

The third reactor at the Olkiluoto nuclear power plant will begin regular electricity production in mid-April. The start of electricity production has been postponed many times.

## Oysters and food poisoning

![Opened oysters and lemon on a serving plate.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-675915820816b1a60e/1677756613)

Open image viewer

Oysters are a fine delicacy. Oysters are eaten raw. That is why people can become ill from them. Photo: Yle/Sini Sovijärvi

Almost 200 people fell ill from oysters in Finland in February. Many people fell ill because they ate oysters in a restaurant. There were many cases in the Helsinki region.

Health authorities are investigating the matter. The authorities found norovirus in oysters. Norovirus was found in many batches of oysters. Oysters come to Finland from different countries.

Oysters are eaten raw. That is why they may contain bacteria or viruses, which make a person ill.
