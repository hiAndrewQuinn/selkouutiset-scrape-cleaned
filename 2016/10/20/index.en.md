# Thursday 20.10.2016 (radio)

A ceasefire began in Aleppo, Syria. The final debate between the US presidential candidates was heated. The police are worried about the clown phenomenon. Finland has collapsed in the world football rankings.

## The Aleppo ceasefire began

![A doctor examines a Syrian man's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9242415)

Open image viewer

Humanitarian aid is needed in Aleppo.  Photo: Sergei Chirikov / EPA

The fighting in Aleppo, Syria, has paused for a while. The ceasefire lasts 11 hours every day for 4 days. The UN has not yet been able to send aid to East Aleppo, because the Syrian government has not given permission for it. On Friday the UN will try to transport people who are wounded or sick out of East Aleppo.

Russia and the Syrian government stopped the air strikes already on Tuesday.

The situation, especially in East Aleppo, is serious, because at least 250,000 people live in the middle of the bombing.

## Trump may not accept the result of the presidential election

![Hillary Clinton and Donald Trump.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9241649)

Open image viewer

Clinton and Trump debated on television for the last time.  Photo: Gary He

In the United States the presidential candidates have debated on television for the last time. The Republican candidate Donald Trump says that he may not accept the election result if he loses the presidential election. The Democratic candidate Hillary Clinton says that Trump belittles democracy with his speeches.

Experts say that the debate was fairly matter-of-fact. Trump and Clinton debated hard about, for example, abortion, guns and immigration. In a survey by the American news channel CNN, viewers considered Clinton the winner of the debate.

Clinton is also leading the opinion polls.

The presidential election will be held in less than 3 weeks.

## The police want to put clowns in their place

![Clown](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9242150)

Open image viewer

Clowns are threatening children in Finland too.  Photo: AOP

The police are worried about clowns who are scaring children in different parts of Finland. On Wednesday people dressed in clown costumes scared children in the Tuusula and Järvenpää areas. They also had some kind of knives with them and apparently a chainsaw. Threatening clowns were also moving around in Helsinki.

The police are investigating the cases as illegal threats. The police consider the cases serious, even though the perpetrators may think of it as just a joke. The police ask parents to step in if they notice children planning a similar prank.

Scaring people in clown costumes started in the United States. It is not known how it all began.

## Finland's football ranking collapsed

![Hans Backe](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9219505)

Open image viewer

Hans Backe is the head coach of Finland's national football A team.  Photo: Tomi Hänninen

Finland has collapsed in the world football rankings. The international football federation Fifa has dropped Finland to place 101 on its list. Finland has never before been so low in the Fifa ranking. Finland fell as many as 17 places from the previous list.

Ahead of Finland there are now, for example, countries like Malawi, Kyrgyzstan and Syria.

The success of Finland's men's football team has been very poor in the World Cup qualifiers. Finland has not won a single game yet.

The number one country in the world football ranking is Argentina, and right behind it come Germany and Brazil.
