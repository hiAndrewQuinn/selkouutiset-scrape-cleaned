# Sunday 13.1.2019 (radio)

Police have arrested 3 men in Helsinki who may have committed sexual crimes. Party leaders support adding more police officers. Over 20 people died in a mine accident in China. The Girls' Lions won bronze at the World Championships. The week starts in wintry weather.

## Police suspect a sexual crime in Helsinki

![Police sleeve badge on the sleeve of a blue jacket](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3076,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-6210957d7e30b8d314/1677069540)

The police suspect that a sexual crime was committed in East Helsinki. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

The police have arrested 3 men in Helsinki who may have committed sexual crimes. The police suspect that the men sexually abused a child. The men have a foreign background.

The acts may have taken place in East Helsinki. The police suspect that the crimes were committed over a period of 2 months.

The police are investigating the matter.

## Party leaders want more police

![Motorcycle police officer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10594345)

Open image viewer

The number of police officers in Finland has decreased. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Finland's political parties support adding more police officers. The leaders of almost all the parties in Parliament think that Finland needs more police officers.

In Parliament, that is, the Finnish parliament, there are 9 parties. 8 party leaders support adding more police officers. The leader of the National Coalition does not take a position on the matter.

The other party leaders support the proposal of the Police Board. The Police Board has proposed that 650 more police officers be hired in Finland. There are now about 7,200 police officers in Finland.

## A mine tunnel collapsed in China

![Inside a mine tunnel](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7303110)

Open image viewer

A mine tunnel. The photo is not from China.  Photo: Yle / Siru Päivinen

A serious accident has happened in China. More than 20 workers died when the roof of a mine tunnel collapsed. The accident happened in the Shaanxi area in Northern China.

The cause of the accident is not yet known. The mine is private, which means that its owner is not the state. Especially in private mines, workplace safety in China is poor.

## Finland won World Championship bronze

![The Girls' Lions at the 2019 World Championships.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10594174)

Open image viewer

The Girls' Lions celebrate the bronze medal. Photo: Pasi Mennander / Leijonat.fi

Finland has been successful in ice hockey. Finland won bronze in the under-18 girls' World Championships.

So Finland was the 3rd best country in the tournament. Canada won the World Championship gold, and the USA won the silver.

Finland won the bronze medal game against Russia. Finland won the game 3-0.

The Finnish team, the Girls' Lions, played the bronze medal game well. Finland's hero was goalkeeper Erica Jaskari. She did not let the puck into the Finnish goal even once.

President Sauli Niinistö has also congratulated the Girls' Lions. The president and the president's spouse, Jenni Haukio, sent a congratulatory letter to the Girls' Lions.

The under-18 girls' World Championships were held in Japan.

## The weather is wintry

![A man shovelling snow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10594387)

The week starts in wintry weather. During the early part of the week, snow falls in different parts of Finland.

In the south it will be a few degrees below freezing on Monday and Tuesday. In the north it will be about 10 degrees below freezing early in the week.
