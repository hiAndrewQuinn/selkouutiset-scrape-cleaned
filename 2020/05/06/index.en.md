## Seasonal workers can come to Finland

![A woman picks strawberries at Rossi Tarhot's pick-your-own farm](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11337985)

Open image viewer

Berry farms need many seasonal workers. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Finland has decided that seasonal workers from EU countries can come to Finland to work.

Seasonal workers are needed, for example, on berry farms and in gardens. Berry farms need many workers in spring and summer.

In addition, Finland will take in 3,000 workers from outside the EU.

In addition to foreign workers, more workers are still needed from Finland.

For example, asylum seekers can do seasonal work until the end of October, even though their application is still being processed.

In addition, the Finnish government promises unemployed people extra financial benefits if they go to seasonal work.

## Older people are now encouraged to go outside

![An elderly person with a rollator and a carer outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11320893)

Open image viewer

The corona guidelines for elderly people will be changed. Now older people are being encouraged to go outside. Photo: Frida Lönnroos / Yle

Health authorities intend to change the corona guidelines for people who are just over 70 years old.

Older people may meet other people.

It would be good to meet outside and keep a 2-metre safety distance. This way, for example, grandparents and grandchildren can meet.

Older people are being encouraged to leave home and go outside.

A new set of guidelines will be ready soon, says the newspaper Ilta-Sanomat.

Until now, it has been recommended that people over 70 years old stay at home.

## Aurinkomatkat is cancelling trips

![Beaches fill up in Italy during the Ferragosto holiday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7416068)

Open image viewer

Aurinkomatkat has cancelled all trips until the end of July. Photo: Petri Burtsov / Yle

The travel agency Aurinkomatkat is cancelling many trips.

Aurinkomatkat is cancelling all trips that leave in July.

In addition, the company is cancelling all trips between July and October to Calabria in Italy and to Greece.

## Researchers found a strange gene variant

![Eye examination](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5499297)

Open image viewer

An eye doctor examines eye pressure. Photo: Heikki Kiseleff / Yle

Finnish and American researchers have found a strange gene variant in Finns.

The gene variant is such that it protects people from glaucoma, which is an eye pressure disease.

The strange gene variant is found in about 8 per cent of Finns.

The gene variant is more common in Finland than anywhere else in the world.

The researchers' results may help to find new treatments for eye pressure disease.

Eye pressure disease can cause blindness.
