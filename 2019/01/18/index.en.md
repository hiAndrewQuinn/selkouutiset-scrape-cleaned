# Friday 18.1.2019 (radio)

There are more places in municipalities for quota refugees than are needed. The police chief told the government about sexual crimes. Many workplaces are already thinking about summer holidays. Singer Danny is ending his tours. Iivo Niskanen is Athlete of the Year.

## Places for quota refugees in municipalities

![Refugee tent camp](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6253143)

Open image viewer

Syrian refugees in a camp in Jordan. Photo: Jamal Nasrallah / EPA

Fewer quota refugees come to Finland than there are places for them in municipalities.

Finland's refugee quota is 750 people, but this year about 1,300 municipal places are on offer. For example, the municipality of Orimattila says that it expected 20 quota refugees last year. However, only 3 came.

## The government again spoke about sexual crimes

![Juha Sipilä and Kai Mykkänen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10602772)

Open image viewer

Ministers Kai Mykkänen and Juha Sipilä. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The Finnish government has again spoken about suspected sexual crimes in Oulu and Helsinki.

The police chief also told the government statistics on how many children are sexually abused in Finland. The police say that adults often lure children and young people into sex on the internet. That is why more police are needed to monitor the internet, the government says. The government is also investigating whether Finland can begin to interpret international asylum agreements in a new way.

The police suspect that in Oulu and Helsinki, for example, some immigrants have sexually abused and even raped children.

## Workplaces are already thinking about summer

![Sunset in San Sebastián, Spain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10601307)

Open image viewer

Sunset in Spain. Photo: Javier Etxezarreta / EPA

At some workplaces, summer holidays are already being considered.

Employees and the employer discuss when each employee can be on holiday. Holidays are decided at different times in different fields. In some factories, holidays are agreed already in January or February; in shops and the transport sector, in April. The staff of many day-care centres only find out in May when they are on summer holiday. If people only know the timing of their holiday shortly before it starts, it is hard for them to plan a holiday with their family.

Many people have a fixed-term job, and so they may not get a summer holiday at all.

## Danny's tours are over

![Close-up of Danny.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_171/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5366025c3dfdc6f3e0b/1547566573)

Singer Danny has done a long tour: he has sung in 34 concerts in different parts of Finland. One concert is still left, in Helsinki on Friday evening.

Danny, who is 76 years old, says that this is his last tour. However, Danny is not stopping singing, and he will soon release a new song. He also plans to perform at some concerts and festivals, even though he no longer has the energy for long tours.

## Iivo Niskanen is Athlete of the Year

![Iivo Niskanen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10603465)

Open image viewer

Iivo Niskanen. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

Cross-country skier Iivo Niskanen was chosen as Athlete of the Year on Thursday evening.

Niskanen was chosen because he won the 50-kilometre cross-country skiing event at last winter's Olympics. Niskanen is Athlete of the Year for the third time. He was also chosen as Athlete of the Year last year and in 2014.

The Athlete of the Year choice was announced on Thursday evening at a party that was shown on television. Right after the party, Niskanen flew overnight to Otepää in Estonia, where he will compete in a race on Sunday.
