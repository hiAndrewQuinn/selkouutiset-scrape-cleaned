# Friday 31.3.2023 (radio)

Turkey approved Finland's NATO membership. Finland thanks NATO countries. EU acts against pay discrimination. There were big problems in train traffic. Weather forecast.

## Finland's NATO membership

![In the Turkish parliament they discuss and vote on ratifying Finland's NATO membership.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_272/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10927276426048b8e38e/1680213241)

Finland's membership of the NATO military alliance is confirmed.

The Turkish Parliament approved Finland's NATO membership late on Thursday evening. The Parliament's decision was unanimous. 276 MPs were present. They all voted for Finland's NATO membership. No one voted against.

Turkey was the last NATO country to approve Finland's membership. Now all NATO countries have approved Finland's NATO membership.

## Progress of NATO membership

![Turkish President Tayyip Erdogan](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7602,h_4262,x_0,y_628/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-109240564256ecb1653e/1680175041)

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. Photo: Emin Sansar / AOP

The Turkish Parliament approved a law by which Turkey approves Finland joining NATO. Next the law goes to Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. President Erdoğan must approve the law. It can take many days.

After that the NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg will invite Finland to become a full member of NATO.

The accession agreement will be deposited at the US State Department in Washington.

President Sauli Niinistö thanks all NATO countries. Niinistö says that Finland is a strong member of NATO. Niinistö says that Finland is committed to the common security of NATO countries.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin also thanks all NATO countries for their support for Finland.

Many countries congratulate Finland. For example, the Swedish Foreign Minister, the Norwegian Prime Minister and the Estonian Foreign Minister congratulate Finland on joining the NATO family.

## Pay discrimination

![EU flags in front of the EU Parliament building in Brussels.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4950,h_2784,x_0,y_277/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-104610363933c705402d/1670595869)

EU Parliament building in Brussels, Belgium. Photo: Dmitry Rukhlenko / AOP

The EU Parliament has approved new rules on pay transparency.

Employees will have the right to know the basis of their own pay. Employees will also have the right to know what other employees are paid for equal work. New rules will also apply to job advertisements. For example, job titles must be gender-neutral.

The aim is to reduce pay discrimination, which happens especially on the basis of gender. The changes will be made to Finnish law within 3 years.

## Problems in train traffic

![People at Tampere railway station waiting to get onto buses.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4962,h_2778,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10932876426ece8837d7/1680272861)

Open image viewer

People waited for a bus in Tampere because trains did not run. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

Train traffic has been cut off for a long time on Friday on the main line. The reason is a fault in the railway's electrical system between Tampere and Parkano.

Because of the fault there were many problems for trains running between Tampere and Helsinki, and for trains running north from Tampere.

The train company VR carried thousands of people by bus. That is why for many people the journey was lengthened by several hours.
