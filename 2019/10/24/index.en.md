# Thursday 24.10.2019 (radio)

Nokia's expectations have worsened. Major redundancy negotiations in North Karelia. The ceasefire was broken in Syria. Enni Mustonen is the new Clear Language book patron.

## Nokia has difficulties

![Nokia's premises in Espoo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11034564)

Nokia's head office is in Espoo. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Many companies have published their interim results on Thursday. Interim results tell how a company has done this year. Many people are interested in the Finnish technology company Nokia.

Many were surprised when Nokia said that business is going worse than before. Nokia says that the end of the year is worse than Nokia expected. Next year also looks worse than Nokia expected.

Nokia has suspended its dividend payment, that is, it will not distribute money to its owners. The development and sales of the new 5G internet technology have been difficult.

## Redundancy negotiations in North Karelia

![Emergency sign in front of the central hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10195340)

Open image viewer

In the redundancy negotiations, ways to save money are being looked for. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Major redundancy negotiations in health services are starting in North Karelia.

The negotiations concern more than 7000 employees in the social and health sector, for example in health centres and hospitals. The fire service and ambulance services are also included in the major redundancy negotiations in North Karelia.

In the redundancy negotiations, ways are being looked for for how municipalities can save money. Employees may be dismissed, that is, they will lose their jobs. Some employees may have to take forced leave, that is, they will have to be without work for a while.

## The ceasefire was broken in Syria

![Map of the border between Syria and Turkey](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11019568)

The Turkey-Syria crisis began two weeks ago. Photo: Leena Luotio / Yle

The Kurdish forces in Syria say that Turkey is breaking the ceasefire in Northern Syria. The Kurdish forces in Syria say that Turkey attacked three Kurdish villages towards on Thursday.

The Kurds say that the villages are in an area that the Kurdish forces left. The ceasefire agreement stated that the Kurds would leave the area.

## New Clear Language book patron announced

![Writer Tuomas Kyrö sits on stage with a microphone in his hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9906284)

Open image viewer

Tuomas Kyrö was the Clear Language book patron for 2017-2019. Photo: Kaisa Kaatra/Selkokeskus

The new Clear Language book patron will be the writer **Enni Mustonen**. Mustonen's book \_Paimentyttö \_will be published in Clear Language in two years, that is, in 2021. Mustonen's book will be written into clear language by the clear-language writer **Tuija Takala**.

A Clear Language book patron is a Finnish writer whose book is allowed to be written in clear language. Last year and this year the Clear Language book patron has been the writer **Tuomas Kyrö**. Tuomas Kyrö's book *Mielensäpahoittaja* will be published in clear language at the Helsinki Book Fair this week.
