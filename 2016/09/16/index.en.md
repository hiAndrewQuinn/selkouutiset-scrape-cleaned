# Friday 16.9.2016 (radio)

The police are needed less at reception centres. The heads of the centres say that there are no gangs in them. Hillary Clinton continues her election campaign in the USA. More rescue dogs are needed.

## The police have few tasks at reception centres

![Police cars near the Otanmäki reception centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9161246)

The police are needed at reception centres less often than before.  Photo: Jonna Karjalainen / Yle

The police say that police officers are needed less than before at reception centres. The number of call-outs has clearly gone down. In August, the police had only 620 tasks that were related to asylum seekers. As recently as January, there were over 2100 tasks.

The police say that the situation is now fairly calm. The police are usually needed if a person is causing trouble while drunk or there is a quarrel between asylum seekers. The police are also needed if some asylum seeker's things have been stolen.

Asylum seekers do not trust the police, which makes the tasks harder to handle. Abroad, the police are not as trustworthy as in Finland.

## No information about asylum seeker gangs

![An asylum seeker in a reception centre. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9153997)

Open image viewer

There is no information about asylum seeker gangs.  Photo: Kay Nietfeld / EPA

The reception centres say that there are no asylum seeker gangs in the centres. Yle asked the heads of reception centres whether residents have formed gangs or taken part in criminal activity.

The heads of the reception centres say that people in reception centres form their own groups, but no criminal activity has been noticed.

Former national police commissioner Mikko Paatero said last week that there are gangs in reception centres and some people drift into the world of crime.

There are over 100 reception centres in Finland in total.

## Hillary Clinton returned to the stage

![Hillary Clinton](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9171078)

Open image viewer

Hillary Clinton spoke to her supporters on Thursday.  Photo: Reuters

In the United States, presidential candidate Hillary Clinton has continued her election campaign. She was on sick leave because of pneumonia. Clinton had to leave the memorial ceremony for the New York terrorist attacks early because she fell ill. Now she says she is fine. Clinton admits that it was hard to tell the public about the illness.

Clinton's opponent, the Republican Donald Trump, has accused Clinton of keeping her health information secret. Trump himself told more about his own health on Thursday. A doctor says that Trump takes medicine but is otherwise healthy.

In the US election, the health of the presidential candidates has become a big issue, because the job of president is hard.

## Rescue dogs are much needed

![Inca the dog practises searching in the forest](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9166713)

Open image viewer

Inca will become a rescue dog when it grows up.  Photo: Adam Bellgrau / Yle

More rescue dogs are needed in Finland. Rescue dogs are used more than before in searches for people, and that is why more dogs are needed. Last year, rescue dogs got almost 250 call-outs.

Rescue dogs help the police and other authorities when a person has got lost, for example in a forest. The dogs search for the person either by smell or by tracks.

Training a rescue dog is difficult. The training lasts at least 2 years. The dogs are also tested before they get permission to work as rescue dogs. The test really tests whether the dog finds a person or not.
