## The Uusikaupunki car factory

The Uusikaupunki car factory is hiring more workers. The factory needs 400 new car builders.

Cars are being bought a lot now. That is why the Uusikaupunki car factory needs new workers quickly. The aim is for the workers to start in about 3 months' time.

The car factory needs new car builders in all departments.

## Restaurant restrictions

![A person drinks beer in a restaurant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11564879)

The sale of alcohol is being restricted because of the coronavirus. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The government will continue the coronavirus restrictions on restaurants until the end of February.

Restrictions are decided in the regions. The restrictions are stricter in regions where the coronavirus situation is bad.

For example, in the Helsinki, Turku and Tampere areas, restaurants must stop selling alcohol already at 22:00.

## Finland's coronavirus infections

![THL director Mika Salminen at the press conference of the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health and THL in Helsinki on 29 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11619831)

Open image viewer

Mika Salminen, director of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

188 new coronavirus infections have been reported in Finland on Thursday. This year a total of about 15,500 coronavirus infections have been reported.

The figures are smaller than in many other countries. THL director Mika Salminen says that Finns have followed the coronavirus guidelines well.

Salminen also says that Finland has a world record for the coronavirus app. Already 2.5 million Finns, i.e. half of Finns, have downloaded the Koronavilkku alert app to their phone. The app gives an alert if the phone user has been close to a person who has a coronavirus infection.

Salminen does, however, warn about large family celebrations. Large family celebrations are not a good idea, because the coronavirus has often spread at family celebrations.

## The stabbing in France

![Map of France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11619947)

Open image viewer

The stabbing happened in Nice, in southern France. Photo: Harri Vähäkangas / Yle

A stabbing has happened in France. 3 people died in the stabbing.

The stabbing happened in a church in the city of Nice. A man attacked the church and stabbed people with a knife.

The attacker killed a woman and a man in the church. He also injured one woman so badly that she died a little later.

The mayor of Nice says that the stabbing was probably a terror attack. The police caught the attacker, and he is now in hospital.

The Muslim Council of France condemns the Nice attack. In the view of the Muslim Council, the act was wrong.

At the weekend the south wind will strengthen and bring warm and humid air to Finland.
