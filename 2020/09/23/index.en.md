# Wednesday 23.9.2020 (radio)

Finnish construction companies have been cheated. President Halonen: school violence must be addressed. Travel agencies are now organising trips in Finland. Charging points for electric cars are being built slowly in apartment buildings. Weather forecast.

## Finnish construction companies have been cheated

![Mini excavator](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11547956)

Open image viewer

For example, a mini excavator like this one was stolen. Photo: Ari Haatainen

Estonian criminals have cheated many Finnish construction equipment companies. An Estonian criminal gang ordered work machines and equipment for Finnish building sites.

The criminal gang ordered excavators, pumps, cranes and tools for the building sites, for example. The members of the criminal gang applied for the machines or equipment and sold them.

The police say that the Estonian criminal gang has obtained millions of euros through fraud.

## President Halonen: bullying at school must be addressed

![Tarja Halonen standing in a park in front of a tree.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5254,h_2955,x_0,y_172/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7055155f2ab338085de/1596634736)

Open image viewer

President Tarja Halonen is worried about bullying at school. Halonen was President of the Republic of Finland from 2000 to 2012. Photo: Arttu Timonen / Yle

President **Tarja Halonen** says that school violence must be addressed. There is less bullying at schools than before, but bullying is nastier than before.

For example, pupils bully some pupil and film the bullying on video. The video is spread for others to see.

Yle asked Halonen what she thinks about bullying at school. President Halonen says that experts, parents and children must work together so that bullying at schools stops.

## Trips in Finland for Finns

![The ski slope of Levi at the end of September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_208/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7222665f6b2544a1f64/1600857529)

Open image viewer

Trips are being made to Levi, for example. In winter you can ski at Levi. Now in September there is still grass at Levi. Photo: Pekka Viinikka / Yle

Travel agencies are starting to organise trips in Finland for Finns. Because of the corona situation, Finns do not want to travel abroad.

The travel agency Aurinkomatkat is starting to organise trips to Lapland and Kuusamo. In Lapland there are many hotels without customers, because no foreign tourists come to Finland.

Lapland's tourism companies are waiting for Finns to travel in Lapland more than before.

## Few charging sockets for cars have been built

![Managing director Jukka Voutilainen, Novaka Oy, at an electric car charging point in Lappeenranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7224745f6b503691e2b/1600869261)

Open image viewer

An electric car needs a special socket, because a large electric current runs through during charging. Photo: Kari Saastamoinen / Yle

Housing companies have been slow to change their electricity poles so that they suit electric cars. The ordinary electric sockets in parking spaces cannot cope with an electric car charging all the time.

Setting up charging points is progressing slowly in housing companies.

In some housing companies charging points cannot be built, because the majority of residents oppose the work.
