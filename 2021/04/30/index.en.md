## Peat producers' May Day march

![Motor march and lorry protest by peat industry operators in front of the Parliament House on Mannerheimintie in Helsinki on the morning of May Eve, 30 April 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11908800)

Open image viewer

Peat producers drove lorries up to the Parliament House. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Peat producers have protested in Helsinki. In the morning, peat producers drove over one hundred (100) lorries up to the Parliament House.

Peat producers say that the government's support package for the peat sector is too small.

Peat producers hope that Finland will continue and develop the use of peat.

## Rental company for people with reduced work capacity

![People with intellectual disabilities do not get enough use of their training in working life.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5356223)

Open image viewer

People with reduced work capacity can be, for example, disabled people. Photo: Antti Eintola / Yle

The government is setting up a temporary work agency for people with reduced work capacity. The agency will place people with reduced work capacity in jobs at other companies. The aim is that people with reduced work capacity get jobs for a long time.

The aim is also that some of them may be able to stay on at the company where they have got in as temporary workers.

People with reduced work capacity are people whose ability to work has decreased. People with reduced work capacity can be, for example, disabled people, people with long-term illnesses, or people who are recovering from a serious illness.

## The popularity of audiobooks and e-books

![A woman looks at an audiobook on her mobile phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11906361)

Open image viewer

You can listen to audiobooks on a smartphone, for example. Photo: Ella Tommila

The popularity of audiobooks has grown a lot during the corona time. The popularity of e-books and e-magazines has also grown a lot. Audiobooks, e-books and e-magazines can be listened to or read online, for example on a phone or a tablet.

Borrowing audiobooks from libraries has grown by about 40 percent during the corona time.

In Finland, the libraries' e-services are being renewed. The aim is that audiobooks and e-books can be borrowed from one service.

## The euro area economy

![Euro statue.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10656834)

Open image viewer

The euro sign. Photo: Anna Karismo / Yle

The economic situation of the euro area has worsened. The euro area economy has sunk into a new recession. The production of the euro area, that is, gross domestic product, shrank by 0.6 percent at the start of the year, after the end of last year.

Gross domestic product of the euro area has now shrunk for the second quarter in a row. Then it is said that the economy is in recession.

The euro area consists of those European countries whose currency, that is, money, is the euro. There are 19 countries in the euro area.

## Celebrate May Day safely during corona

![Havis Amanda statue in Helsinki on 20 April 2021, wearing a white student cap and a face mask.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11903687)

Open image viewer

The Havis Amanda statue traditionally gets a white student cap for May Day. Now the statue has also got a face mask. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Today, Friday, and tomorrow, Saturday, May Day is being celebrated. Officials remind Finns to celebrate May Day safely with regard to corona.

The police, municipalities and health authorities recommend that May Day is celebrated only with close family and friends, but not in big groups.

Officials hope that people will not gather in city centres, so that the coronavirus does not spread.
