# Wednesday 19.10.2016 (radio)

The majority of Finns support a minimum wage law. A travel agency of people smugglers has been uncovered in Europe. VR is adding train services in December. The Pokemon game excites many people, but cities do not know how to make use of the players' enthusiasm.

## Finns support a minimum wage

![A construction worker spreads mortar on a building site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6305449)

Open image viewer

A minimum wage means the lowest wage. It is the smallest possible wage that must be paid for some work. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

A large part of Finns say that Finland must get a law on minimum wages. A minimum wage is the smallest possible wage for some work.

In Yle's survey 63 percent of respondents supported a minimum wage law. For example, young people think that the law can bring more jobs. Of those under 25, 75 percent of respondents supported a minimum wage.

The Central Organisation of Finnish Trade Unions, SAK, says that a minimum wage is not a good thing. The law could lead to more and more people getting only the minimum wage. The Confederation of Finnish Industries, EK, also says that the law is not needed. Now wages are set in collective agreements.

The respondents say that 10 euros an hour is a suitable minimum wage. That is about 1,600 euros a month.

## Europol found a fake travel agency of people smugglers

![Men wearing life jackets on the edge of a ship. Behind them a grey navy vessel. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9239294)

Africans left adrift by people smugglers were rescued last year in the Mediterranean onto an Italian navy ship. Smuggling people who are trying to get to Europe is a billion-euro business for criminals.  Photo: Giusepe Lami / EPA

The European police agency, Europol, has carried out a big operation against people smuggling. 52 countries took part in the operation, and more than 300 people were arrested in it.

In the operation, raids, that is, surprise inspections, were made on brothels, homes, airports and reception centres. In Greece a fake travel agency was uncovered that was really organising people smuggling.

Almost 530 victims of smuggling were identified.

Europol says that people smuggling networks often come from Nigeria, Asia and Eastern Europe.

## VR is adding train services in December

![A train at a station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9197445)

Open image viewer

VR will publish new timetables in December. They have more train services than before and more frequent stops at intermediate stations. Photo: Tiia Hemminki / Yle

The Finnish railway company VR says that train travel has become more popular. The number of journeys in commercial traffic has risen by seven (7) percent this year.

VR is adding hundreds of train services to different parts of Finland in December. VR's new timetables come into force in December.

More services are coming to Ostrobothnia, Lappeenranta and Imatra. In addition, many trains will start stopping more often at intermediate stations.

## Pokemon promotes local travel

![A little boy plays Pokemon Go.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9239157)

Open image viewer

Pokemon Go has got many mobile phone users hooked on the game. However, cities have not known how to use the game in tourism. Photo: Heli Kaski / Yle

The popular game Pokemon Go has increased enthusiasm for local travel. Pokemon is a mobile online game that you can play on a smartphone.

During the autumn many families have made small trips to nearby cities because of the game. Popular places for Pokemon hunting are, for example, Helsinki and Tampere.

Only a few Finnish cities have made use of the players' enthusiasm in tourism. Yle's survey says that of Finland's 15 largest cities, only Turku has properly taken the players into account.
