# Friday 27.4.2018 (radio)

The leaders of South Korea and North Korea have met. National Veterans' Day was held on Friday. Internet addiction is a common problem. Oulun Kärpät are the Finnish ice hockey champions.

## The Koreas

The leaders of South Korea and North Korea have met. South Korean President Moon Jae-in and North Korean leader Kim Jong-un met at the border between the countries, on the South Korean side.

The leaders said that a time of peace is starting on the Korean peninsula. The aim is to get nuclear weapons out of the area. The countries also plan to make a peace treaty.

There was a war between South Korea and North Korea in the 1950s. After the war the countries did not make a peace treaty.

Korea split into 2 states 70 years ago.

## Veterans' Day

![A war veteran's decorations, badges, medal, cross, memorial cross](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9585862)

On the day of celebration many veterans wear decorations on their chests. Photo: Carolus Manninen / Yle

Here in Finland National Veterans' Day was held on Friday. The Veterans' Day celebration was at Finlandia Hall in Helsinki. President Sauli Niinistö also took part in the celebration.

Veterans' Day is held in honour of Finland's war veterans. The war veterans served in the Finnish army during the Second World War in 1939–1945.

Last year about 15,000 war veterans were alive. Most veterans are already over 90 years old.

## Internet addiction

![A child using a smartphone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10167891)

Open image viewer

Even a child can develop an internet addiction. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Internet addiction is a new problem in Finland too.

Internet addiction means that a person follows the internet all the time. The person feels that they have to do so.

Internet addiction causes, for example, depression and sleep problems. In addition, it harms, for example, learning.

Internet addiction is hard to notice. For example, at a workplace it is much harder to notice internet addiction than alcohol addiction.

Psychologists have more and more patients who suffer from internet addiction. Many of the patients are children and young people.

## Champions Kärpät

![Veini Vehviläinen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10180515)

Open image viewer

Goalkeeper Veini Vehviläinen lifts the trophy. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

One more piece of sports news: The new Finnish ice hockey champion is Oulun Kärpät. The champion was decided on Thursday evening when Kärpät beat Tampere's Tappara.

Kärpät and Tappara played 6 final games. Kärpät was the better team. Kärpät won 4 final games and Tappara 2.

Kärpät won the last final game 1 - 0. The scorer of Kärpät's winning goal was Aleksi Mäkelä.

Kärpät's goalkeeper Veini Vehviläinen had an important role in the final games. In the last final game he did not let a single goal into Kärpät's net.
