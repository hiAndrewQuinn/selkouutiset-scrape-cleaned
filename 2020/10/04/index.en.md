# Sunday 4.10.2020 (radio)

The number of corona infections is growing. Protesters accuse the police of harsh methods. There was a strong storm in Southern Europe. Yle Kielikoulu teaches Finnish and Swedish.

The number of corona infections is growing in Finland.

On Sunday 147 new infections were reported. The total number of corona infections in Finland is about 10,500.

There are big differences between the areas. There are more corona infections in the Helsinki and Uusimaa area than elsewhere.

## Helsinki demonstration

![A police officer spraying a protester.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11578009)

Open image viewer

The police used pepper spray against the protesters. Photo: Reader's photo

The police are investigating what happened at the demonstration in Helsinki on Saturday. The demonstration was against climate change.

The protesters say that the police used too harsh methods against them. The police used pepper spray when the protesters were sitting in the street. Pepper spray irritates the eyes and skin.

The police think that the protesters disturbed traffic. The protesters did not obey when the police told them to leave the street. That is why the police decided to use pepper spray.

The police intend to investigate the events carefully.

## Storm in Europe

![The storm brought down the bridge over the Vésubie river in Roquebillière, France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11578166)

This bridge broke in the storm in France. Photo: Sebastien Nogier / EPA

There has been a strong storm in Southern Europe. The storm raged at the weekend in France and Italy.

At least 2 people died because of the storm. In addition, people went missing in the storm.

Rescuers have been searching for the missing, but the work has been difficult, because many roads and bridges were broken by the storm.

A lot of rain fell in the storm. About 50 centimetres of rain fell in a day, which is as much as normally falls in a year.

Finally, information about Yle Kielikoulu. Yle Kielikoulu is a new way to learn Finnish.

In Kielikoulu, Yle's Finnish-language programmes are watched with Finnish subtitles. The subtitles show what is said in the programme.

If the viewer clicks on any word in the subtitles, they get a translation of it. In this way the viewer also understands difficult words.

The viewer can choose which language they want the translations in. There are 6 languages: Arabic, Somali, Russian, Estonian, Swedish and English.

Besides Finnish, Yle Kielikoulu also teaches Swedish. Then Yle's Swedish-language programmes are watched with Swedish subtitles.

Swedish words also have translations. Yle Kielikoulu translates Swedish words into 25 languages, for example Kurdish, Persian and Thai.

More information about Kielikoulu can be found on Yle's website. The address is yle.fi/kielikoulu.
