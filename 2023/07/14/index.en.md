# Radio Clear News Friday 14.7.2023

Parliament. Minister Purra. Housing prices. Street gang.

## Parliament

![Plenary hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4999,h_2811,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1131012648c4c350d7cf/1686917217)

The opposition parties demand that Parliament meets soon. Parliament is now on summer holiday, but in the opposition's view Parliament must still meet.

In the opposition parties' view, Parliament must deal with the actions of the ministers. In the opposition's view, Parliament must also consider whether the government can function.

The reason is that the Finns Party chair, Minister Riikka Purra, wrote racist texts online many years ago. They became public a little while ago, when the media wrote about them.

Speaker of Parliament Jussi Halla-aho decides whether Parliament meets during the summer holiday or not. On Friday afternoon Halla-aho said that he is considering the matter.

## Minister Purra

Finance Minister and chair of the Finns Party Riikka Purra says that she has not considered resigning from the post of minister.

Purra's old racist texts have been discussed in Finland all week. The media abroad have also written about the texts.

The Finns Party is a party that criticises immigration. Purra says that the party will not apologise for that.

## Housing prices

![A real estate agent shows an apartment in a block of flats that is for sale.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_312/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105859163c00b5e12858/1700130030)

Open image viewer

Apartment viewing. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Estate agents say that the fall in housing prices will stop soon.

Housing prices have fallen a lot during the year. Now estate agents say that the fall may have stopped.

Flats are now bought much less than normal, because banks are raising housing loan interest rates. When housing loan interest rates rise, people do not dare to take out a housing loan.

## Street gang

![In close-up there is a sign on a red brick wall. The sign says "Oikeustalo" (courthouse).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4027,h_2266,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114070564acebfb64942/1689054466)

The court has given the leader of a Helsinki street gang an eight-year prison sentence.

The leader of an East Helsinki street gang is a 24-year-old man. He has, for example, committed a serious violent crime and aggravated robbery. He has also used drugs.

18 young men were sentenced in court. They all belong to an East Helsinki street gang. Many have committed violent crimes and robberies.
