# Wednesday 2.10.2019 (radio)

The school attacker in Kuopio acted alone. Post's chief executive Heikki Malinen is resigning. Prime Minister Johnson has presented his latest Brexit plan. You can follow the life of the lake salmon live on WWF's Nature Live.

## Police continue investigating the Kuopio school attack

![Flags at half-mast at the Herman shopping centre in Kuopio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11000961)

Flags at half-mast at the Herman shopping centre in Kuopio. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The police have been investigating the school attack carried out in Kuopio on Tuesday.

The police estimate that the attacker acted alone. The reason for the act, that is, the motive, is not known. The attacker is a student at the vocational college, and his fellow students were in the classroom.

The police fired 2 shots to stop the attacker, who did not obey the police. The suspected attacker was wounded by the shooting.

The police suspect that the Kuopio school attacker also started a fire in the shopping centre. The suspect did not shoot with his weapon, which was an air pistol.

1 person died and 10 were injured on Tuesday, when a man attacked a school classroom in Kuopio. The attacker used a large bladed weapon.

## Malinen of Posti resigns

![Heikki Malinen, chief executive of Posti Group, next to a parcel locker at the head office of Posti Group in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_265/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5817375d4937fe12384/1565079692)

Open image viewer

Posti's chief executive Heikki Malinen is resigning. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

Posti's chief executive Heikki Malinen has resigned. He says that now is a good time to change the chief executive.

Malinen will not be paid a separate severance payment.

Malinen's salary has been much discussed. Malinen's salary and fees had risen by 65 percent in 4 years. He received almost one million euros a year. At the same time, Posti has wanted workers to be paid less than now.

Malinen had been Posti's chief executive since 2012.

## Prime Minister Johnson presented the UK's Brexit plan

![British Prime Minister Boris Johnson at the Conservative Party conference on 30 September 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11000593)

British Prime Minister Boris Johnson assures that after Brexit, Britain and the EU will have a better relationship. Johnson has presented his latest Brexit plan to the Conservative Party.

Prime Minister Johnson said that there will be no border checks on the Northern Ireland border. Johnson assured that Brexit, that is, Britain's exit from the EU, will certainly happen at the end of this month.

## Lake salmon on Nature Live

![Brown trout in an underwater camera image.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1435,h_807,x_135,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5954755d94563490a16/1570002727)

Open image viewer

Screenshot from an underwater online broadcast in the Hiitolanjoki river. A brown trout is swimming in the picture. Photo: WWF

You can now follow the life of endangered fish in a live broadcast. The environmental organisation WWF has launched a new Nature Live, which follows lake salmon. The lake salmon is even more endangered than the Saimaa ringed seal.

The camera is in South Karelia, in the Hiitolanjoki river. The only wild lake salmon in Finland live in the Hiitolanjoki river.

Earlier, the Nature Live pictures have shown an osprey, a adder and the Saimaa ringed seal.

The fish camera of Nature Live is at luontolive.wwf.fi.
