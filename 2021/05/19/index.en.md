The Centre Party's parliamentary group has given a warning to two of the party's MPs. The MPs who receive the warning are **Tuomas Kettunen** and **Hannu Hoskonen**. The reason is that they voted against the EU recovery package.

The Centre is a government party. That is why all the Centre's MPs had to vote for the recovery package.

Hannu Hoskonen was also removed from his post as chair of Parliament's Environment Committee.

## The government won a vote of confidence

![Parliament voted on the government's confidence. Interpellation vote on the decisions made at the government's mid-term retreat. 19.5.2021](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_351/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80791260a4f26fd472d/1643286654)

The votes were split 106–75. 18 MPs were absent. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

Parliament has voted on the government's confidence. The government won a majority of Parliament's confidence. The voting result was 106–75. Confidence means that the government can continue its work.

The vote of confidence in the government was held because the opposition parties, the National Coalition, the Christian Democrats and the New Alternative Movement, made an interpellation about the framework budget session.

The opposition parties do not like that the spending limit is being exceeded and that employment decisions have been moved to the autumn.

## Low birth rate could slow economic growth permanently

![Two prams outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5317055c1763e605a05/1545036909)

Open image viewer

Economic growth slows down if the birth rate does not grow. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

A low birth rate permanently weakens Finland's economic growth. A new study says that the change in population structure, that is, Finns getting older, will permanently slow economic growth. The slowdown starts in about 20 years.

Economic growth will slow down permanently if the birth rate does not rise, immigration does not grow and the employment rate of immigrants does not rise.

The study was carried out by the Research Institute of the Finnish Economy, Etla. Etla says that political measures must be taken in Finland for the situation to improve. For example, working careers must be lengthened and increases in taxation must be evened out.

## The corona vaccination certificate comes into use

![Close-up of a computer screen showing the Kela Omakanta website.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4539,h_2553,x_0,y_234/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6169595ddfa5e62fe1a/1574938873)

Open image viewer

Omakanta service can be accessed on a computer and a phone. Photo: Ilkka Klemola / Yle

A corona vaccination certificate is being introduced in Finland next week. The certificate of the corona vaccination can be obtained from the online health information service Omakanta starting next Wednesday.

The certificate will appear in Omakanta within 5 days of a person receiving the corona vaccination. The certificate can, for example, be printed or shown from a phone.

Later in the summer, the common EU corona certificate will come into use. It will be introduced perhaps in July.
