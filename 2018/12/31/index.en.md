## Only a few municipalities lower the tax rate

![Men and women sit at tables in the city council chamber.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8460072)

Open image viewer

Municipal councils and city councils decide how high the municipal tax is. The picture shows the Kouvola city council. Photo: Kirsi Partanen / Yle

Only 2 municipalities on mainland Finland can lower the municipal tax next year. The other is Kustavi, and the other is Kitee, which was previously a crisis municipality.

The municipal tax of 46 municipalities rises at the turn of the year. It applies to half a million Finns.

Municipal costs have grown more than expected. Tax revenue from residents and companies has turned downwards.

Many municipalities are taking on debt, saving on spending and postponing investments.

The average income tax rate of municipalities next year is just under 20 percent. There are 311 municipalities in Finland.

## Sipilä warns about hate speech

![ Juha Sipilä ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10567516)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä says in his New Year's greeting that spreading hate speech is a crime in Finland.

Sipilä refers to the sex crimes in Oulu. They were aimed at minors, and the suspects have a foreign background.

The prime minister says that everyone who comes to Finland must follow the country's laws and respect the integrity of other people.

Sipilä stresses that the individuals, not population groups, are the ones who committed the suspected crimes in Oulu.

## The euro turns 20

![Euro statue in Frankfurt](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10576427)

Open image viewer

When the euro's cash came into use, 12 countries were part of the common currency. Photo: Anna Karismo, Yle

The euro turns 20 years old tomorrow, New Year's Day.

The common currency was at first only an accounting currency. The banknotes and coins were introduced 3 years later.

Currently 19 EU states belong to the official euro area.

The euro has faced many difficulties. First the financial crisis hit, and then came Greece's debt problems. Now there is tension over Italy's debt.

The crises are caused, for example, by the fact that the euro area lacks a common fiscal policy.

## New Year is stormy in Finland

![Fireworks in the sky over Sydney.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10576829)

Open image viewer

Sydney's fireworks in Australia. Photo: Brendan Esposito / EPA

The world is moving into a new year again.

In some Pacific islands the year changed already at 12 o'clock Finnish time. The residents of the atoll state of Kiribati were the first to celebrate the year 2019.

Many cities hold a big firework display to mark the New Year. Instead of fireworks, the city of Espoo is holding a laser light show.

In Finland citizens may set off fireworks only from 6 pm to 2 am at night. In the centres of many municipalities and cities, fireworks may not be set off at all.

Several rescue services have recommended not firing rockets because of stormy weather. A wind of 14 metres per second is considered the safe wind limit for fireworks. Gusty and strong wind can steer the rockets in the wrong direction.

A winter storm may also cause power cuts.
