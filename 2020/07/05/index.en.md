## Immigrant background and school results

![Footballers are important role models.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11431709)

Open image viewer

Encouragement and role models are important for children with an immigrant background. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

Immigrant background affects children's school results in Finland. Children of immigrant families often do worse at school than children of Finnish families.

For example, there is a big difference in children's reading skills between children with an immigrant background and other children. The difference is bigger in Finland than in many other countries. This is shown by the PISA studies.

Researchers say that there are many reasons for the situation, for example teachers' attitudes. If a teacher does not encourage a child with an immigrant background, the child does not believe that they can succeed.

You can read more about this on Yle's website. The address is yle.fi.

## Poor treatment in the cleaning sector

![Window cleaning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11433984)

Open image viewer

A cleaner's work is hard, and the treatment can be poor. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Workers are treated badly in the cleaning sector. Helsingin Sanomat writes about this.

In the cleaning sector, for example, working days are too long. Workers do not always even have days off. Poor treatment of workers is common in the cleaning sector, Helsingin Sanomat writes.

Many immigrants work in the cleaning sector. They do not always know their rights. Many also do not dare to complain about poor treatment, because they are afraid of being fired.

## The number of mosquitoes varies

![Close-up of a mosquito sucking blood.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11433949)

Open image viewer

Mosquitoes suck blood from people. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

There is a big difference this summer between the number of mosquitoes in Northern Finland and in Southern Finland.

There are many mosquitoes in Northern Finland. Mosquitoes develop in puddles of water, and there were many puddles in the north after the snowy winter.

In Southern Finland there are few mosquitoes now. There was little snow in the winter, and the spring was dry, so there were no puddles of water.

The number of mosquitoes interests Finns every summer, because small mosquitoes are a big nuisance for people. Mosquitoes suck blood from people.

## Bottas wins F1 race

![Bottas](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11434193)

Open image viewer

Bottas crossed the finish line first in Austria. Photo: Getty Images

Finnish Valtteri Bottas has won the Formula One race in Austria.

The Austrian race was the first Formula One race after the corona break.

Valtteri Bottas is a 30-year-old Formula One driver. He drives a Mercedes. Bottas has a chance to win even the world championship, if the season goes well.

The next Formula One race will be run in a week.
