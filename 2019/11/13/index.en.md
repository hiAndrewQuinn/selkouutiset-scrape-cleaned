# Wednesday 13.11.2019 (radio)

Negotiations on the postal strike continue. A payment default may make it harder to get insurance. Dangerous tattoo inks have been sold in Finland. Fewer antibiotic medicines are being sold. Many pensioners work.

## Negotiations on the postal strike

![Postal workers' demonstration in Ilmala](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5150,h_2897,x_9,y_418/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6110145dcaaaae7bbbc/1573563112)

The postal strike continues. Representatives of the employees and the employer negotiated today, but no solution was found for the strike. The next negotiations will be on Friday.

The strike began on Monday. If no agreement is reached on working conditions, the strike may last until early December.

Postal workers are on strike because Posti wants to move some of the employees to another collective agreement. After the move, they would receive lower pay.

The postal workers' union PAU has announced that it may extend the postal strike from 2 weeks to 4 weeks. Now about 9,000 employees are on strike, but the number may grow later.

## A payment default may make getting insurance harder

![A pile of bills](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_864/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-5941957a1cb774888d/1470221297)

A payment default may make it harder to get home insurance or car insurance.

An insurance company does not have to grant voluntary insurance if the company does not trust the applicant's ability to pay.

Almost 400,000 Finns have a payment default entry in their credit record.

One payment default does not usually prevent getting insurance, but you may have to negotiate about it with the insurance company.

In addition, the insurance may be narrower, or the insurance premiums may have to be paid in advance.

## Tukes found dangerous tattoo inks

![A person being tattooed with a tattoo machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7000,h_3937,x_0,y_536/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5184465bcf0e36b7530/1540296332)

The Safety and Chemicals Agency, Tukes, has found several tattoo inks that are dangerous to health.

Tukes tested 20 tattoo inks, of which almost half contained dangerous substances. These substances may cause cancer or make it harder for children to have children.

Tukes has contacted the companies that sold the inks and ordered them to stop selling the dangerous inks.

## Fewer antibiotic medicines are sold

![Misuse makes antibiotics ineffective.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-39140564db53e09605/1447933248)

Fewer antibiotic medicines are sold in Finland than before.

In pharmacies, over 30 percent fewer antibiotics are sold now than 10 years ago.

Last year people bought antibiotics 2.3 million times. In Finland, antibiotics can only be bought from pharmacies.

## Many pensioners work

![An elderly person's hands holding coins.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_75/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4743715aba0300bdf66/1683557210)

More and more Finnish pensioners are working.

About 10% of pensioners work part-time or full-time.

Pensioners go to work because they want something to do. They also want to earn more money.

Women usually care for people, clean, or work in shops. Most men do transport work or look after property.
