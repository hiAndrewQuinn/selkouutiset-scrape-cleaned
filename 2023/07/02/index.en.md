President Biden. Minister Rantanen. Food shopping. The weather.

## President Biden

US President Joe Biden is coming to Finland. Biden will visit Finland on Thursday, 13 July.

Biden is coming to Finland from Lithuania, where the NATO summit of the military alliance is being held. Biden is coming to Finland as the guest of President Sauli Niinistö.

President Niinistö is hosting a summit of the USA and the Nordic countries in Helsinki. The summit includes Presidents Biden and Niinistö, as well as the prime ministers of Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Iceland.

## Minister Rantanen

![Interior Minister Mari Rantanen (Finns Party).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3242,h_1824,x_0,y_149/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1136906649fcde610e3c/1688232232)

Interior Minister Mari Rantanen. Photo: Lehtikuva

Finland's Interior Minister Mari Rantanen says that she does not believe in conspiracy theories, that is, secret plans. Rantanen belongs to the Finns Party.

Rantanen has drawn attention because she has talked about population replacement on social media. Population replacement means a conspiracy whose aim is to bring other groups of people, for example Muslims, in place of Europe's white inhabitants.

Now Interior Minister Rantanen says that population replacement and other conspiracies are not true. Rantanen believes in numbers and statistics.

The Finns Party chair Riikka Purra also says that the Finns Party does not believe in conspiracy theories. Purra says that the Finns Party talks about bad immigration policy and its consequences.

## Food shopping

![A person bends down to take something from the ready-meal shelf in a grocery shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_321/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97266162b08ee143f26/1700546346)

Open image viewer

Customers are now looking for cheap products. Photo: Aalto Puutio / Yle

Finns buy different products in grocery shops than before. The reason is that the price of food has risen.

A new survey says that about 50 per cent of respondents do not buy expensive brand products in grocery shops. A year ago, only about 30 per cent of respondents left expensive brand products on the shop shelf.

Customers now buy more of the shops' own brands, for example Pirkka and Rainbow products. Shops' own brands are usually cheaper than other products.

The survey also says that especially young women choose cheap products in grocery shops. Older men look at price tags less.

## The weather

![Monday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113731064a195c70f41d/1688311275)

The week starts with rainy weather. Rain, thunder and strong wind are forecast for almost all of Finland.

On Monday and Tuesday the temperature is over 15 degrees in the south and over 10 degrees in the north.

The weather is cool, but the swimming water in lakes is warm. In many places the water is warmer than the air.

In many lakes the swimming water is as warm as 23 or 24 degrees.
