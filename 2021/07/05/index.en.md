## Guidelines for treatments

![Carita Vuorenpää and Lotta Lantto, surgical nurses, in the operating theatre of the Anaesthesia and Surgery Department K at Jorvi Hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4450,h_2503,x_0,y_299/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7023905f1ac488a6261/1609212989)

Open image viewer

Common guidelines are needed for the whole of Finland on how expensive treatments are used. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Doctors hope for guidelines on what treatments they may offer patients. Many treatments are expensive.

According to the Finnish Medical Association, guidelines are needed that apply to the whole of Finland. Nowadays a patient's place of residence can decide how quickly they get into a treatment.

New kinds of treatments keep coming, for example new cancer treatments are very expensive. Common guidelines on their use are needed, so that people are equal in different parts of Finland.

## Travel to Norway

![Motorhomes on the road in Norway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81461560b7587764681/1622629291)

Open image viewer

Finns like to travel in Norway. Photo: Sara Wesslin / Yle

Finns can now travel to Norway easily. Norway lets Finns in without restrictions, because Finland's corona situation is good.

Almost all Finns may travel to Norway without quarantine and without a coronavirus vaccination certificate. Only residents of the Kainuu area in Northern Finland still have to follow restrictions.

## Young people to the army

![Finnish Defence Forces basic training in Santahamina. A female person in the army.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_619/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7589825ff30512ac0fc/1609764691)

Open image viewer

Military service is compulsory for men in Finland, but voluntary for women. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

About 12,000 young people started military service on Monday. In the army young people learn, for example, how to use weapons.

This time young people get a coronavirus vaccination in the army.

Young people start military service in July or January. They are in the army for at least 165 days.

Military service is compulsory for men in Finland. Women can go into the army if they themselves want to.

If a young person does not want to go into the army, they can do civilian service. Civilian service can be, for example, work in some organisation.

## Unhealthy heat

![An elderly person having coffee at the Jaala service centre in Kouvola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6020,h_3386,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7342275f969c8082a5b/1640765076)

Open image viewer

Many elderly people do not eat and drink enough when the weather is hot. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

In Finland the weather is now hot and dry.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute forecasts that hot air will come to Finland from Siberia also this week.

Hot weather already strains the health of many people. For example, many elderly people do badly in heat. An elderly person's body can dry out in hot weather.

Elderly people may also fall and injure themselves if they run out of strength because of the heat.
