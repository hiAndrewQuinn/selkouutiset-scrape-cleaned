# Wednesday 27.5.2020 (radio)

The coronavirus can fly far when someone coughs. There are now enough respirators in Finland. The collective agreements for restaurants and hotels will be changed temporarily. Drug deaths are growing. Weather forecast.

## The coronavirus flies from a cough

![Audience in the stalls of a concert hall](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4326,h_2433,x_281,y_158/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6121425dce6f745b02c/1573810598)

Indoors, for example in a concert hall, the coronavirus can spread from coughing or talking. Photo: Sini Ojanperä / Yle

In Finland, computer modelling has been used to study how the coronavirus spreads when a person coughs. The study found that the coronavirus can spread up to 4 metres away, if a person coughs indoors, for example in a shop.

The coronavirus can also spread into the air when a person talks.

For example, in a restaurant or at a concert or at work, people may talk a lot.

## There are enough respirators in Finland now

![man wearing a protective mask](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11335665)

Open image viewer

Face mask. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Three Finnish companies have started making respirators in Finland. The Finnish companies make surgical protective masks and face masks.

Finnish companies also make protective suits. Their production started earlier.

When the coronavirus situation began, there was a shortage of all protective equipment, that is, there was not enough protection. Now protective equipment is being made in Finland, and it is also being imported to Finland from abroad. Now there is enough protective equipment.

Hospitals and health centres need about one million (1,000,000) pieces of protection every day.

## Restaurant working conditions made more flexible

![restaurant counter and menu](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11338976)

Open image viewer

Restaurant. Photo: Rami Moilanen / Yle

The collective agreements for the tourism and restaurant sector are being changed because of the corona situation. Shift rosters can be made faster than usual, and the timing of holidays can be changed.

Shift rosters can now be published with a notice period of one week for one week. Some of the employees' holidays can be moved later.

The collective agreements for the tourism and restaurant sector are being changed for the duration of the corona situation. It is not yet known whether there will be many or few customers in restaurants and hotels in the summer. Therefore it is hard to agree on shifts and holidays.

## Drug deaths grew

![Subutex seized by Customs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10113742)

Open image viewer

Subutex that Customs has found. Photo: Customs

Drug deaths have grown in Finland in a few years.

In 2017, about 6 people per 100,000 died from drug poisoning in Finland. The number of drug poisoning deaths rose by more than 20% in five years.

The opioid that causes the most drug poisoning deaths is often known by the brand name Subutex.
