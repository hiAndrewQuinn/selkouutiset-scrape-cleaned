# Wednesday 31.8.2022 (radio)

Mikhail Gorbachev has died. The government is discussing the budget. The nurses' strike is postponed. The weather is cool.

## Mikhail Gorbachev

![Soviet leader Mikhail Gorbachev smiles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4944,h_2781,x_0,y_600/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88673861a7776d4b17f/1661922789)

Mikhail Gorbachev. Photo: All Over Press

The last leader of the Soviet Union, Mikhail Gorbachev, has died. He was 91 years old.

Mikhail Gorbachev became leader of the Soviet Union in 1985. He reformed the Soviet Union, which in the end broke apart in the early 1990s.

In 1990 Gorbachev received the Nobel Peace Prize. Gorbachev received the prize because he had an influence on the end of the Cold War between the Soviet Union and the USA.

## Budget talks

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin arrived at the government's budget talks at the Säätytalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1001357630f0af18f9f5/1661930291)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin arrived at the government's budget talks at the Säätytalo. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The government is discussing the state budget on Wednesday and Thursday. In the talks, the state's income and spending for next year will be agreed.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the government must support people's purchasing power. People have less money available than before, because the prices of goods and services have risen. Prime Minister Marin says that families with children in particular must be supported.

The government is considering, for example, ways to make the price of electricity lower than it is now. People spend a lot of money on their electricity bills. Petrol also costs a lot.

## Nurses

![Placard held by Tehy protesters reads: Is this right? Nurse 2,546 € per month, building engineer 3,396 € per month. Pay gap 850 €!](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_408/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-93691662470a1a7f591/1648828169)

Tehy demonstration in Turku in April. Photo: Kimmo Gustafsson / Yle

The authorities are postponing the nurses' strike in Turku and Helsinki by 2 weeks. The nurses were supposed to go on strike on Tuesday in intensive care in Turku and in home care in the city of Helsinki. Now the strike may start on 20 September.

Attempts are being made to solve the nurses' labour dispute under the leadership of the National Conciliator. The nurses are demanding higher pay.

## Housing loans

In July, people borrowed less money from banks for housing than a year earlier. The Bank of Finland says that new housing loans were taken out 23 percent less in July than in July 2021.

People took out housing loans from banks worth 1.4 billion euros.

Housing loans now have to pay more interest than at the start of this year. The interest rate on a housing loan is 1 percentage point higher than at the start of the year.

## The weather

Cloudy sky. Photo: Seija Paasonen / Yle

The weather on Thursday is fairly cloudy. In places a little rain falls, but mostly it is dry. In many places it is windy.

During the day it is 8 to 15 degrees. At night there is frost in places in Northern and Western Finland.
