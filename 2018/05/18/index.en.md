# Friday 18.5.2018 (radio)

Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö proposes that women's access to the army could be limited. Olli Rehn is the new governor of the Bank of Finland. The new children's hospital has been completed. The church will not start marrying people of the same sex. The results of the spring matriculation exams have come out.

## Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö spoke about women's military service

![Jussi Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10076079)

Open image viewer

Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö (Blue Reform) Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö proposes that women's access to the army could be limited to save money.

In an interview with Lännen Media, Niinistö proposes that women's access to military service could be stopped, for example for a year. The alternative is to reduce the number of women. Military service for women is voluntary.

Niinistö is aiming for savings that could be 2 - 4 million euros.

Both the opposition and the government parties have rejected Niinistö's proposal. The Ministry of Defence is looking for ways to save money. Savings are being looked for, for example, in crisis management work.

## Olli Rehn has been appointed governor of the Bank of Finland

![Olli Rehn](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10211862)

Open image viewer

Olli Rehn will start as governor of the Bank of Finland in July. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Olli Rehn is the new governor of the Bank of Finland. President Sauli Niinistö appointed Rehn as governor on Friday.

The government proposed Rehn, who is a member of the Bank of Finland's board, for the job. Rehn has also been Minister of Economic Affairs and the European Union's Commissioner for Economic Affairs. The term starts in July and lasts 7 years.

The current governor of the Bank of Finland, Erkki Liikanen, is retiring.

## The children's hospital is ready

![the new children's hospital](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10209777)

Open image viewer

The new children's hospital in Helsinki. Photo: Matti Snellman / HUS

The new children's hospital has been completed. The hospital building has been officially handed over to the Hospital District of Helsinki and Uusimaa.

The hospital will get its first patients this autumn. The children's hospital serves the whole of Finland. The treatment of many difficult children's illnesses has been concentrated in the hospital, especially children's heart surgery and organ transplants.

In the new hospital, many things are better than in the old one. There is less office space and more treatment space. In addition, children's parents can stay overnight in the hospital.

## The church will not start marrying gay and lesbian couples

![A gay couple as a cake decoration](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9486117)

Open image viewer

The equal marriage law did not reach the church. Photo: AOP

The Evangelical Lutheran Church will not start marrying couples who are of the same sex. The General Synod decided on the matter by 59 votes to 46.

The Bishop of Porvoo, Björn Vikström, proposed that the initiative on marriage be sent to the Bishops' Conference for further preparation. Vikström's proposal lost in the vote.

The equal marriage law came into force in Finland in March 2017.
