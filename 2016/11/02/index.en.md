# Wednesday 2.11.2016 (radio)

The NBI suspects Finnish men of preparing a terrorist act. Fewer people are in the church. Supo is worried about foreigners' land purchases in Finland. Russia declares a short ceasefire in Aleppo. The Finlandia nominees for children's and youth books were announced.

## The NBI suspects Finnish men of preparing a terrorist act

![The headquarters of the National Bureau of Investigation in Vantaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9267963)

Open image viewer

The National Bureau of Investigation has investigated the preparation of terrorist acts and recruitment. Photo: Kimmo Brandt / AOP

The National Bureau of Investigation suspects that five Finnish men have prepared a terrorist act.

The National Bureau of Investigation, or KRP, started investigating the matter in summer 2015, and it has now completed the investigation.

The NBI says that four suspects travelled to Syria and perhaps took part in the activities of terrorist groups. One suspect has died in Syria and one suspect has not been reached. He is suspected of murder.

The suspects say that they are innocent.

The Finnish National Bureau of Investigation got help with the investigation from Sweden and the United States.

## Fewer people are in the church

![A priest in the church hall preparing for a service](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5664901)

Open image viewer

Not as many people go to church as before. Photo: YLE

The membership of the Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland is falling.

However, according to a survey commissioned by the Church Research Institute, many Finns believe in God and pray.

According to the survey, Finns also value the church's charity work.

In 2015 about 73 percent of Finns belonged to the Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland. The number has fallen in recent years. In 2012 more than 77 percent of Finns belonged to the church.

People leave the church especially in big cities. Especially older people belong to the church.

Many new religious communities have been registered in Finland. More than half of the new communities are Islamic.

## Supo is worried about foreigners' land purchases in Finland

![Supo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8394747)

Open image viewer

Supo thinks that foreigners are making suspicious property deals in Finland. Photo: Vilma Hannén / Yle

The Security Intelligence Service is worried that foreigners are making big land purchases in Finland, that is, buying a lot of plots and buildings.

Foreigners have bought a lot of land especially in Eastern Finland, near the Russian border. The Security Intelligence Service thinks that big land purchases can be a security risk.

The Security Intelligence Service warns that in a crisis situation a foreign landowner could close transport routes and house foreign troops in Finland.

The Ministry of Defence will make a report on the security risks of land purchases next spring.

## Russia declares a ceasefire in Aleppo

![A man walks along a street full of rubble; around him collapsed houses and hanging wires; at the end of the street a fire is smoking. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9255764)

Open image viewer

Air strikes have destroyed the city of Aleppo. Photo: Zouhir al Shimale / EPA

Russia is declaring a ten-hour ceasefire in Aleppo, Syria, on Friday.

The aim is that civilians and rebel fighters can leave East Aleppo. East Aleppo is held by the rebels.

According to Russia, the Syrian government has accepted the ceasefire.

## The Finlandia nominees for children's literature announced

Singer and actress Vuokka Hovatta will choose the best children's book of the year. Photo: Stefan Bremer

Six books are competing for the Finlandia Prize for children's and youth literature.

The Finlandia Prize for children's and youth literature will be announced on 24 November. The prize is worth 30,000 euros.

The winner will be chosen by singer Vuokko Hovatta.
