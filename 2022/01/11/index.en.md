# Tuesday 11.1.2022 (radio)

Coronavirus is spreading quickly. The Supreme Court overturned Johanna Vehkoo's conviction. The President of the European Parliament David Sassoli has died. A pig's heart was transplanted into a human for the first time.

Over 50 per cent of people in Europe will soon get the omicron corona infection. This is how Hans Kluge, the European director of the World Health Organization WHO, assesses it. He says that over 50 per cent of Europeans will get infected within 2 months.

More than 7 million new corona infections were recorded in Europe during the first week of this year. More corona patients have also ended up in hospital than before.

## Johanna Vehkoo

Journalist Johanna Vehkoo (left) and lawyer Martina Kronström arriving at a session of the Rovaniemi Court of Appeal in August 2020. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

The Supreme Court has overturned journalist Johanna Vehkoo's conviction. The Supreme Court considers that Vehkoo did not damage Junes Lokka's honour on Facebook.

The Court of Appeal earlier sentenced Vehkoo to fines for defamation. The Supreme Court overturned the Court of Appeal's judgment and freed Vehkoo from punishment.

The Supreme Court is of the view that Vehkoo's writing criticised above all Junes Lokka's activities, not personal characteristics.

Sassoli had been President of the European Parliament since 2019.

## USA and Russia

The USA and Russia negotiated on European security on Monday.

Russia said that it does not intend to attack Ukraine. The USA said that the military alliance NATO can in future also take in new countries as members.

Russia does not want NATO to expand eastwards. The USA and Europe have accused Russia of preparing an attack on Ukraine.

## Organ transplant

![A surgeon holds a pig's heart used in the operation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6008,h_3379,x_0,y_192/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90038761dd15ae78941/1641879593)

Open image viewer

Surgeon Muhammad Mohiuddin holds a pig's heart used in the operation. Photo: EPA

In the USA, surgeons have carried out an operation in which a pig's heart was transplanted into a man. The pig's heart was transplanted into a 57-year-old man.

The pig's heart was genetically modified before the operation. This was the first time that a pig's heart was transplanted into a human.

Doctors say that the man is doing well. However, it is not yet known whether the man will recover from the operation.

In the USA, 110,000 patients are currently waiting for an organ transplant. There are not enough human hearts for all patients.
