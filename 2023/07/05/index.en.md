## Orpo in Tallinn

![Kaja Kallas and Petteri Orpo shake hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_380/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113833764a53df5ee72b/1688550971)

Estonia's Prime Minister Kaja Kallas greets Finland's Prime Minister Petteri Orpo in Tallinn on Wednesday. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Finland's new Prime Minister Petteri Orpo has visited Estonia. Orpo met Estonia's Prime Minister Kaja Kallas in Tallinn.

The prime ministers said that Finland's and Estonia's relations are now stronger than before. Both countries are now members of the NATO defence alliance.

The prime ministers of Finland and Estonia hope that Sweden also gets into NATO as soon as possible. Sweden applied for NATO membership at the same time as Finland, but the NATO countries Turkey and Hungary have not yet approved Sweden's membership.

Orpo and Kallas also said in Tallinn that support for Ukraine will continue.

## World heat record

![A thermometer shows that it is more than 40 degrees outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_569/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11020426441331ee06c9/1688570121)

Photo: Siegra Asmoel/imageBROKER/Shutterstock/All Over Press

On Monday a new world heat record was set. This is reported by the US meteorological agency.

The average temperature of the whole planet was a little over 17 degrees on Monday. The previous record was measured 7 years ago. Then the record temperature was a little under 17 degrees.

For example, it is especially hot at the moment in North Africa, where the temperature has been almost 50 degrees.

There are many reasons for the high temperatures. The climate of the planet is warming, because people burn oil, gas and coal.

In addition, a natural El Niño phenomenon has started in the Pacific Ocean. It warms the planet for a few years.
