## Unemployment is falling

![Construction worker shovels sand into a concrete mixer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9642224)

The number of unemployed people has gone down.

In June this year there were clearly fewer unemployed people than a year ago.

The number of long-term unemployed has fallen the most. They are unemployed people who have been without work for more than a year.

Now many long-term unemployed people have found a job. The Ministry of Employment and the Economy says that there are now over 20,000 fewer long-term unemployed people than a year ago.

The number of unemployed is falling, because Finland's economy has improved.

## Work tasks for young people

![Hamza El-Issaoui, employed through the Micro-work project, works under the guidance of Ari Reiman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9733053)

Hamza El-Issaoui has found short jobs with a new mobile app. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

In Espoo, jobs are offered to young people in a new way.

A young person can download a mobile app called Work Pilots. Companies announce work tasks in the app. A young person says if they are interested in a work task.

In the mobile app you can find short jobs, that is, work tasks of a few hours. The young person gets 10 euros an hour in pay. Tax details can be reported easily in the app.

Short jobs give a young person a chance to get to know work in different fields. This helps when a young person is thinking about which field they want to study.

Espoo's trial continues until the end of the year.

## Thai berry pickers

![Thai berry pickers gathering blueberries in the forest](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6775273)

Open image viewer

Thai people pick blueberries and lingonberries in Finnish forests. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Fewer berry pickers will come to Finland from Thailand this summer than before.

Earlier, about 3,500 Thais picked berries in Finnish forests. This summer there are about 2,500 of them.

The newspaper Savon Sanomat says that the Thai authorities have warned Thais about Finland.

The authorities have warned that berry pickers get very little money in Finland and that working conditions are poor.

Thai berry pickers first came to Finland in 2005.

## Cold stone

![Beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7383788)

Open image viewer

Lake water is warming late this summer. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

Jaakko has once again thrown the cold stone into the water. An old tradition says that swimming waters start to cool on Jaakko's name day, that is, on 25 July.

You could say that this year Jaakko fails. The waters are not cooling, they are only now starting to warm up.

Because of the cold weather, lake water has been colder than usual. However, in the coming days the weather will warm up, Ylen meteorologist Seija Paasonen says.

The temperature will rise to between 20 and 25 degrees across the whole country. Therefore the swimming waters will also warm up.
