## Haavisto thought for a long time

![Pekka Haavisto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10474852)

Open image viewer

Pekka Haavisto wants to become chair. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

MP Pekka Haavisto has held a press conference and explained his decision to run for chair of the Greens.

Haavisto says that he thought about the decision for a long time. According to Haavisto, the Greens' Maria Ohisalo is the best option for leader. But Ohisalo is not running for chair.

The Greens will choose a successor to chair Touko Aalto on Saturday. Aalto has left the post because of severe exhaustion. Haavisto says that Touko Aalto's departure was a hard time for the Greens.

## More speed cameras

![An automatic police surveillance camera monitors traffic on a street in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6452857)

Open image viewer

A speed camera, that is, a camera on a pole, in Helsinki. Photo: Yle

Helsinki may get many more speed cameras. A speed camera is a camera on top of a pole.

A speed camera takes a picture of a car that is driving too fast. At the moment there is only a little camera surveillance in use in Helsinki.

The city will soon discuss a proposal according to which according to which perhaps 70 new cameras will be installed in the city.The cameras are wanted in places where many accidents have happened and where residents have asked for monitoring.

Cameras are wanted, for example, on busy streets and near schools.

## The environment is important at ski resorts

![Downhill skiers at the Ruka ski resort in Kuusamo on Friday](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10441613)

Open image viewer

Downhill skiers at the Ruka ski resort in Kuusamo. Photo: Harri Lindfors / Lehtikuva

Ski resorts are now competing with environmental values. For example, the Ruka ski resort has set the goal of being carbon neutral by 2020.

That means that Ruka's activities might not change the amount of carbon in the atmosphere.

Ruka is making very big investments. Ruka wants to reduce driving and to accommodate people efficiently.

## The tick is dangerous

![A tick in close-up.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10258966)

Open image viewer

The tick spreads Lyme disease. Photo: Artyom Geodakyan / AOP

Diseases spread by ticks are a big threat to health all over the world.

It is estimated that in 2050 over 30 percent of the world's people will have some disease spread by ticks.

The most common and best-known disease spread by ticks is Lyme disease.

Ticks may carry several different diseases, which they spread with one bite. According to a study, 65 percent of people who got Lyme disease also got other diseases from the tick bite.

If a person has many diseases spread by ticks, the person's immune defence can weaken. Because of that, the person can also get other diseases.
