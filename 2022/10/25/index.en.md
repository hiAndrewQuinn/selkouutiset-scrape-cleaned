# Tuesday 25.10.2022 (radio)

Municipalities start free flu vaccinations for risk groups. UPM made a good result. Britain's Prime Minister changed. The weather is cloudy.

## Flu vaccinations

![Sign reading FLU VACCINATIONS with an arrow pointing to the vaccination point. Below it the text OULU.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-883091619de74d4bbe0/1637738596)

Many municipalities will start free flu vaccinations for people in risk groups at the start of November. Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

People in risk groups will soon get a free flu vaccination. Many municipalities will start vaccinations at the start of November.

You get the vaccination free of charge if you are, for example, over 65 years old. Pregnant women and children under 6 years old also get a free flu vaccination.

In the autumn the authorities also recommend a new corona vaccination for some people. The new corona vaccination is worth taking if you are over 65 years old or belong to another corona risk group. You can have it if more than 3 months have passed since your previous vaccination or corona disease.

Corona and flu vaccinations should be taken at the same time.

Forestry company UPM has made a good result. Between July and September UPM made a profit of almost 780 million euros. This is almost 360 million euros more than in the same period last year.

CEO Jussi Pesonen says that UPM's result was better than ever before.

Britain's Prime Minister has changed. King Charles appointed Rishi Sunak to lead the country.

Sunak said that he will fix the mistakes made by Liz Truss. Sunak said that he must get Britain's economy in order.

Liz Truss said last week that she is resigning as Britain's Prime Minister.

## Aviation emissions

![A Delta Airlines plane landing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4998,h_2806,x_0,y_111/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-102357763525f9274c50/1666343108)

Open image viewer

A Delta Airlines plane landing at Tokyo's Haneda airport. Photo: Kimimasa Mayama / EPA

The countries of the world have agreed how to try to reduce emissions from aviation. The agreement was made at the UN. The aim is that emissions from flying will be much lower in 2050 than they are now.

Attempts are being made to reduce flight emissions, for example with new technology and new fuels.

After the lifting of corona restrictions, people have flown much more than during the corona time. Flying is still expected to increase.

Aircraft release a lot of carbon dioxide into the air. This speeds up climate change.
