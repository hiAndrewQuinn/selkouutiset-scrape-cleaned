## President Niinistö

![Finnish President Sauli Niinistö gives a speech at the UN General Assembly in New York.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-856771614a990fca845/1632278871)

Finland's President Sauli Niinistö has spoken at the UN, that is, the United Nations meeting. The UN General Assembly is in New York.

The theme of Niinistö's speech was climate change. Niinistö said that people face a climate emergency. That is why, for example, the use of oil and coal must be reduced and the use of clean energy must be increased.

President Niinistö also raised the situation in Afghanistan. Niinistö said that other countries must not forget the women and girls of Afghanistan. Women's position has worsened in Afghanistan since the Taliban took power.

## Virtual easy-read book fair

![Two women sit on a stage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12110832)

Open image viewer

Authors Enni Mustonen and Tuija Takala. Photo: Selkokeskus

The easy-read book fair was held virtually on Wednesday. The organiser was Selkokeskus.

The authors Enni Mustonen and Tuija Takala also took part in the fair. Mustonen was chosen as an easy-read book ambassador in 2019, and that is why Takala has made an easy-read version of Mustonen's book Paimentyttö.

A new easy-read book ambassador was also presented at the fair. She is the Swedish-language author Merete Mazzarella. Next, an easy-read version will be made of one of Mazzarella's books.

The Seesam award for easy-read literature was also presented at the easy-read book fair. This time the award went to the author Tapani Bagge.

## Screen time for toddlers

![A child looks at a tablet screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9326254)

Open image viewer

Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Lots of screen time can slow a child's language development. The information comes from a study at Tampere University Hospital.

The study included children who were 1.5 or 2 years old. If the child and the child's parents spent a lot of time with television and other devices, the child's vocabulary was small. Lots of screen time also slowed down learning to talk.

Vocabulary was also small among toddlers who were read few books.
