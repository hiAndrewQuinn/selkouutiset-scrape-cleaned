# Radio Clear News Sunday 23.7.2023

Forest fires on Ródos island. Spanish elections. Rovanperä's victory. Panorama ride at Linnanmäki. Weather.

## Forest fires on Ródos

![Tourists were evacuated from the path of raging forest fires on the Greek island of Ródos on 22 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_110/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114632064bcb4c927ea4/1690125492)

Tourists had to leave the hotels that the fire was approaching. Photo: Lefty Damian / Anadolu Agency / AOP

The fires continue on the Greek island of Ródos. Ródos is a popular holiday destination, and there are also many tourists on the island.

Forest and land have been burning on Ródos for 6 days already. At least 30,000 people have been moved to safety from areas that the fire is approaching.

Many thousands of tourists have also been moved away from the forest fire area. Among them are also Finnish tourists.

The Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs says that there are at least 700 Finnish tourists on Ródos. The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says that the Finnish tourists are safe.

Holiday flights from Finland to Ródos have been cancelled at least on Sunday and at the start of the week. Flights to Ródos have also been cancelled in many other countries.

## Spanish elections

![A man in a white shirt waves in the middle of a crowd.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4533,h_2549,x_0,y_207/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114439464b6a80238746/1689692367)

A People's Party election event. Photo: Raquel Manzanares / EPA

Spain held parliamentary elections on Sunday.

Before the election, the People's Party has been number one in opinion polls. The People's Party is a conservative party.

It is estimated that after the election the People's Party could form a government with the Vox party. Vox is a far-right party.

In Spain there has been fear that some people will not have the energy to go and vote because of the hot weather. The hot weather is now affecting the whole Mediterranean area.

Finnish rally driver Kalle Rovanperä has won the Estonia World Rally Championship.

The victory was important for Rovanperä. After the win he clearly leads the rally world championship.

After the race Rovanperä said that he likes Estonia and likes to compete there. Rovanperä has already won the Estonia World Rally 3 times in a row.

Kalle Rovanperä, who is 22 years old, won the rally world championship last year.

## Linnanmäki Panorama

![Firefighters lift people from the Panorama by crane.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_1,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114611564babc1551b6e/1689959536)

Open image viewer

The Panorama ride is working again. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The Panorama ride at the Linnanmäki amusement park is working again. Linnanmäki said about this on Sunday.

The Panorama ride is a viewing tower. It is 53 metres high.

A fault happened in the ride on Friday, and the ride stayed up. Almost 20 people waited for about 2 hours until they could get back down and out of the ride.

Linnanmäki says that no customer was in danger on Friday. The fault in the ride was not serious.
