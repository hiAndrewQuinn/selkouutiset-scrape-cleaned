# Saturday 3.8.2019 (radio)

Police have arrested hundreds of demonstrators in Moscow. The UN warns of terror attacks. There has been a boating accident in the Turku archipelago. Christian Democrat chairman Sari Essayah has got breast cancer.

The demonstrators demand, for example, free elections.

## UN warning about terror attacks

![Rescue workers on Rue de la Loi in Brussels at Maelbeek metro station after the explosion on 22 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8760897)

Open image viewer

Rescue workers in Brussels after the terror attack at Maelbeek metro station on 22 March 2016. Photo: Olivier Hoslet / EPA

The UN warns that new terror attacks may be coming. A UN report says that several new attacks may be carried out, perhaps before the end of the year.

Fighters who were part of the extremist organisation ISIS are a big threat. About 30,000 fighters who had travelled from abroad belonged to ISIS.

About 6,000 people travelled from Europe to join ISIS forces. The report estimates that perhaps 2,000 former ISIS fighters have returned to Europe.

Fewer terror attacks have been carried out in Western countries than in 2015 and 2016. However, the threat of attacks is still high.

## Boating accident in the Turku archipelago

![Accident boats in the picture between rescue units.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10907152)

Open image viewer

The boats involved in the accident are in the picture, between the rescue units. Photo: Western Finland Coast Guard

A boating accident has happened in the Turku archipelago.

A motorboat and a sailing boat collided near Seili island.

The Coast Guard says that 2 people have been seriously injured and 4 people less seriously. The seriously injured are in a critical condition. The injured have been taken to Turku University Central Hospital.

## Sari Essayah has breast cancer

![Sari Essayah](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10906953)

Open image viewer

Sari Essayah. Photo: AOP

The chairman of the Christian Democratic Party, Sari Essayah, has got breast cancer. The party's KD magazine says that Essayah will take 2 weeks' sick leave for cancer treatment. Essayah is also considering whether she will still stand as party chairman.

The Christian Democrats hold their party congress in Oulu at the end of August. At the congress the party chairman will be elected.

Sari Essayah is also an MP. She hopes she can continue her work as an MP in Parliament in September, when the session begins.

## Pointti city festival

In Helsinki the three-day city festival Pointti has been held. The Pointti festival was organised by Yle and the City of Helsinki.

At Pointti people have been able to meet the makers of programmes and take part in making them.

Clear News was at Pointti today, Saturday. Many thanks to everyone who came to meet us!
