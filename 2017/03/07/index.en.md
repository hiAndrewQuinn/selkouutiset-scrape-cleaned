## Many Finns do not know which party to vote for

![Which party interests those who do not state their position the most?](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9494250)

Photo: Yle News Graphics

Many Finns do not yet know which party they will vote for in the municipal elections. The municipal elections are held in a month. In Yle's party support poll, only 55 percent of voters said which party they plan to vote for.

In the poll the most popular party is the SDP, which is supported by about 21 percent of voters. The second most popular party is the Centre Party and the third most popular is the National Coalition Party. After them come the Greens, the Left Alliance and the Finns Party.

Support for the parties has stayed almost the same during this year.

## Young Finns trust the future

![A young man walks down the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9494861)

Open image viewer

Good relationships and a job are important to young Finns. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Young Finns see the future as bright. 83 percent of young people believe that their own future is good and bright.

But only a little over half believe that Finland will do well in the future. Even fewer, only 25 percent, believe in a good future for the world.

Good relationships and a job are important to young people. Young people also hope that equality and the state of the environment will improve. Young people's attitude towards immigrants is mostly positive.

The new survey studied young people's values and attitudes and thoughts about the future

## The US President has issued a new entry order

![Donald J. Trump](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9491058)

Open image viewer

President Donald Trump's new order bans citizens of six countries from coming to the USA. Photo: Shawn Thew / EPA

US President Donald Trump has issued a new entry order. The new order bans citizens of six countries from coming to the USA.

Citizens of Iran, Libya, Syria, Somalia, Sudan and Yemen may not come to the USA. Citizens of Iraq are no longer on the ban list.

But everyone who has a permanent residence permit for the USA may come to the USA.

## The use of sleeping pills has decreased in Finland

![Pills](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8398341)

Open image viewer

About 200,000 Finns use sleeping pills every day. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The Finnish Medicines Agency says that sleeping pills are now used about 30 percent less than at the beginning of the 2000s.

The biggest reason for the change is that patients get more treatments that do not use medicines than before.

About 200,000 Finns use sleeping pills every day.

## Fishermen think there are too many cormorants

![A cormorant flying at Lupinlahti in Hamina.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6755208)

Open image viewer

There are about 136,000 cormorants in Finland. Photo: Raine Martikainen / Yle

Fishermen on the west coast want to reduce the number of cormorants.  The cormorant is a large bird. There are maybe about 136,000 cormorants in Finland.

The fishermen have applied for permits to hunt cormorants. They also want to destroy cormorant eggs. The fishermen say that chasing cormorants from one shore to another does not help.

The fishermen say that there are fewer fish in the sea because the cormorants have eaten too many of them.
