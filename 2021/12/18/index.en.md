# Saturday 18.12.2021 (radio)

THL recommends a corona home test for arrivals from abroad. Operations have to be moved in hospitals. Crimes increased last year. The Car Union's Operation Snowflake has started. The weather gets colder.

## THL recommends a corona home test for people coming to Finland

![A person takes a corona rapid test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3886,h_2186,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89300261bb2e7982af7/1640681797)

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare THL recommends that everyone who comes to Finland from abroad takes a corona home test. A home test does not need to be taken if the passenger is tested for corona already at the Finnish border.

If the corona home test result is positive, the person must go to an official corona test. THL hopes that corona home tests will find corona infections that would otherwise not be noticed.

## Operations have to be moved in hospitals

Operations must be moved to a later time in hospitals in different parts of Finland. Operations have been moved to a later time in all 5 university hospitals. The most operations have been moved in the Tampere hospital.

The reason is that there are many corona patients in hospitals. The intensive care places in hospitals are full.

Since October, about 200 major operations have been cancelled and moved.

## Crimes became known to the authorities more than before

![A police car drives in the dusk. Photographed from behind.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_312/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8721156178ef6163aa9/1635315637)

Last year more than 686 000 crimes and offences became known to the authorities in Finland. Photo: Sasu Mäkinen

Last year the authorities learned of more crimes than before. Almost 20 per cent more crimes became known to the authorities last year than a year earlier.

Property crimes, drug crimes and traffic crimes increased the most. Sexual crimes and homicides also increased.

Assaults, on the other hand, were fewer last year than in 2019.

## Operation Snowflake has started

![Cars driving on a road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4997,h_2812,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89185861b88669be188/1681532814)

The road service of the Car Union has started its Christmas campaign again. The name of the campaign is Operation Snowflake. The Car Union's volunteers help drivers who get into trouble on the road.

At Christmas time people drive a lot by car. People drive a lot, for example, to the shop or to the cemetery.

Operation Snowflake continues until 2 January.

## The weather gets colder

Next week the weather will be much colder than now. In the east and north the temperature falls to between -15 and -35 degrees. In the south and west the frost next week is between 5 and 15 degrees.

On the coast and in eastern Finland it may snow at times. Elsewhere in Finland only a little snow falls next week.
