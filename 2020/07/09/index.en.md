# Thursday 9.7.2020 (radio)

There are already more than 12 million corona infections in the world. In Finland people are discussing how quickly police must arrive after an alarm. A Finnish man died in a diving accident in Norway. A man accidentally fired a gun in a Helsinki restaurant. Stormy weather is expected in Finland on Saturday.

The TV Clear News has a summer break and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

The coronavirus is still spreading around the world.

More than 12 million coronavirus infections have been found in the whole world. About 550,000 people have died because of the coronavirus.

The coronavirus is now spreading especially in America. Most corona infections are in the USA and Brazil.

The corona situation in Europe is calm. That is why travel restrictions for Finns are being eased.

From next Monday Finns may travel to many European countries without quarantine. Finns no longer have to stay at home for 2 weeks when they return from Greece, Italy or Germany, for example.

## Police response time

![Police car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3200,h_1800,x_0,y_170/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6955685efd7e7212e8b/1593871150)

The police are to be given guidelines on how quickly they must be on the scene after an alarm. Photo: Isto Janhunen / Yle

In Finland people are discussing how quickly the police must arrive when they receive an alarm.

A working group of the National Police Board proposes that the time may be different in different parts of Finland. The working group's proposal is that in large cities the police must arrive within 11 minutes. In the countryside the time may be longer, even up to 55 minutes.

The Ministry of the Interior is now starting to assess the working group's proposal. Police matters belong to the Ministry of the Interior.

The aim of the Finnish government is that the police receive clear guidelines on how quickly they must be on the scene after an alarm.

## Diving accident

![Map showing Fiskebøl.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1080,h_608,x_0,y_81/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6973695f0648365d927/1594247303)

The accident happened in the village of Fiskebøl in Norway. Photo: Yle

A diving accident in which a Finnish man died has happened in Norway. The accident happened on the coast of Northern Norway.

A Finnish diver, about 60 years old, died in the accident. The diver was taken out of the water, but could no longer be saved.

It is not yet known what caused the accident. The Norwegian police are investigating.

## Shooting in a restaurant

![Police car parked in front of the Pataässä restaurant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_536/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6973675f062995686cf/1594239453)

Open image viewer

The shooting happened in central Helsinki. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The police have reported on a shooting at a Helsinki restaurant. The police say that the shooting was an accident.

A young man was in the restaurant on Wednesday evening, and he had a gun in his trouser pocket. The gun went off by accident. The bullet hit a customer of the restaurant, who was taken to hospital.

After the shooting the man tried to hide the gun. He did not have a permit to keep the gun.

The police are investigating the case.
