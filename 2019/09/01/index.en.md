## Wage subsidy helps the unemployed only a little

The Finnish government is trying in many ways to create new jobs.

The wage subsidy is one way. An unemployed person who finds it difficult to get a job, for example because of an illness or little education, may receive a wage subsidy.

A wage subsidy means that the employer receives part of the wage from the state.

After a wage subsidy period, some unemployed people have got a permanent job.

Researchers say, however, that the wage subsidy is not a very effective way to reduce unemployment. The reason is that the wage subsidy goes to those unemployed people for whom it is most difficult to find suitable work.

## 6 people died in a helicopter accident

![Map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10948809)

Open image viewer

The helicopter accident happened near Alta in northern Norway. Photo: Source: NRK.NO. Map: Tanja Ylitalo / Yle

A serious helicopter accident has happened in Norway. 6 people died yesterday when a helicopter crashed to the ground.

There was a pilot and 5 passengers on the helicopter. The passengers were about 20-year-old Norwegians. They were travelling by helicopter to a music festival.

The helicopter crashed to the ground on a mountain in northern Norway.

## St Petersburg has Europe's tallest skyscraper

![In the foreground a green yard and a dilapidated house, in the background the Gazprom tower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10924257)

Open image viewer

The skyscraper in St Petersburg has a pointed top. Photo: Nikolai Gontar

In St Petersburg, Russia, there is Europe's tallest skyscraper.

The skyscraper is the head office of the Russian state gas company. It is 462 metres high.

The company wanted to build the skyscraper right in the city centre. But the residents of St Petersburg opposed the company's plan.

The company then built the skyscraper 10 kilometres from the centre of St Petersburg.

## Mira Potkonen won the European gold medal

![LEHTIKUVA Mira Potkonen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10948869)

This picture is from the 2018 European Championships. Mira Potkonen also won the European championship then. Photo: Lehtikuva

Boxer Mira Potkonen won the European championship yesterday. She won the final bout against a French boxer.

All the judges were of the opinion that Potkonen was better.

Mira Potkonen boxes in the women's 60 kilogram category. In October she boxes at the World Championships.

## Tomorrow may be hot weather

Tomorrow, Monday, a few showers and thunder showers will come to Finland.

The day is nevertheless sunny in a large part of the country. It may be even 25 degrees warm.

After tomorrow the weather will cool.
