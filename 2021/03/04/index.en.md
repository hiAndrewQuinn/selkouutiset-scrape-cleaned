# Thursday 4.3.2021 (radio)

The coronavirus situation in Finland is getting worse. Ever younger patients are being admitted to intensive care. The pace of vaccination will speed up next month. Finland's women skied to bronze. The Finnish Cat Association awarded heroic cats.

## Finland's coronavirus situation

![Coronavirus drive-in test in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2672,h_1503,x_0,y_246/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7521505fd0797134283/1608205118)

The coronavirus situation in Finland has worsened quickly.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that during the last week more coronavirus infections were found than at any other time during the epidemic. A total of about 3,900 infections were found.

Today about 750 new infections have been found. In addition, 5 new coronavirus deaths have been reported.

## Coronavirus patients

![HUS tower hospital in Meilahti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11397352)

Open image viewer

Meilahti Hospital in Helsinki. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

More younger coronavirus patients are now coming to hospital than before.

For example, in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district, a record number of young coronavirus patients were in intensive care last week. Last week, about 30 percent of HUS's coronavirus patients were under 40 years old.

In Turku too, more young coronavirus patients have been in hospital care than before.

About 2/3 of intensive care patients are men.

## Vaccinations

![Russian Sputnik V vaccine in a vial.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11683141)

Open image viewer

Russian Sputnik-coronavirus vaccine. Photo: Sergei Karpukhin / AOP

The pace of vaccination will speed up in Finland next month.

Chief physician Taneli Puumalainen of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that from the beginning of April more coronavirus vaccine will come to Finland. From April, about 160,000 doses a week of the Biontech and Pfizer coronavirus vaccine will be received.

The European Medicines Agency is currently assessing 2 new coronavirus vaccines. One is the vaccine from the pharmaceutical company Johnson & Johnson, the other is the Russian Sputnik.

## World Championship bronze

Finland has won bronze in the skiing World Championships in Oberstdorf, Germany.

The women's relay team was 3rd, that is, won bronze. Jasmi Joensuu, Johanna Matintalo, Riitta-Liisa Roponen and Krista Pärmäkoski skied in the team.

This was Finland's 3rd medal. Earlier in the competition, Finland had won 2 silvers.

## Heroic cats

![Günther, who lives in Myllypuro, Helsinki, is one of the four heroic cats awarded in 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3566,h_2006,x_0,y_184/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-780457603f5f2bc784f/1614765929)

The Finnish Cat Association has awarded the title of heroic cat to 4 cats. Thanks to heroic cats, a person's life has been saved or they have been spared a serious illness.

For example, the heroic cat Tassu noticed its owner's serious illness. The cat Viipuri, on the other hand, prevented a fire, and the cat Kombo prevented water damage in a flat.

The cat Günther, for its part, tells when its owner Liisa Kantanen's blood sugar drops too low. Günther sits in front of Kantanen, stares hard into her eyes and meows as loudly as it can. Günther does not stop until Kantanen goes to the kitchen to get a glass of juice.
