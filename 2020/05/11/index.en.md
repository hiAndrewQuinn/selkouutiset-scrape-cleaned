# Monday 11.5.2020 (radio)

The Ombudsman criticises the police. People are satisfied with remote work. Corona restrictions are being lifted in European countries. An egg can already be seen in the osprey camera in Paltamo.

## The Ombudsman criticises the police

![police road block on Highway 1](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7476,h_4205,x_0,y_783/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6588545e7f45cbbf003/1632825665)

The police monitored traffic at the Uusimaa border, which cut the region off from the rest of the country. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

The Ombudsman criticises the police. According to the Ombudsman, the police have given fines without a reason.

The Uusimaa border was closed in April because of the corona epidemic. The police monitored the border. The police gave people fines when they tried to cross the Uusimaa border.

The Ombudsman says that crossing the Uusimaa border was not punishable under the law. Therefore the police were not allowed to give fines to people who crossed the border.

## Finns are satisfied with remote work

![A man uses a laptop computer outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_156/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6668495ea012d4e5225/1596178515)

Open image viewer

The corona spring has kept a million Finns working remotely in their homes and yards for almost a couple of months. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

An online survey has been made about remote work.

Most people think that they can concentrate well on work at home. There are fewer disturbances at home than at the workplace.

However, more than half of the respondents miss their colleagues. More than a million Finns have worked remotely this spring, which means doing work at home.

5,450 people answered the online survey.

## Many countries are easing restrictions

![A quiet street in France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11327628)

Open image viewer

The restaurant street in Paris, which is normally full of people, is deserted. Photo: Juha Nurminen

On Monday, some corona restrictions have been removed in many European countries.

In Spain, people can meet their families and friends again. Restaurant and café terraces may also open, but they may only take in a limited number of people.

Schools will open at least in Norway, Switzerland and the Netherlands. In France, for example, hairdressers and bookshops will open. People may also move outside.

## An egg in the Paltamo osprey camera

![Miila the osprey with an egg a couple of days old in the Paltamo osprey camera. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1367,h_769,x_0,y_23/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6733355eb8da6998032/1589172935)

Open image viewer

Miila the osprey with an egg a couple of days old in the Paltamo osprey camera. Photo: Vesa Hyyryläinen / Paltamo osprey camera

The osprey, that is, the fish hawk, can be seen in the nest cameras of the Osprey Foundation. The osprey is a large bird of prey that lives on lakes and at sea.

The Osprey Foundation's nest cameras are in Paltamo, Janakkala and Ylläslompolo. WWF's Luontolive also has an osprey camera on Lake Saimaa. From the camera images you can watch the life of the osprey.

In the Paltamo osprey camera in Kainuu, the osprey pair Miila and Penna can be seen. Miila and Penna moved back to Paltamo from Africa before May Day. Now there is already 1 egg in the nest.
