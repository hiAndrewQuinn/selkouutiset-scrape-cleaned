# Monday 4.1.2021 (radio)

A lot of corona tests will be done at Helsinki-Vantaa airport this week. There are 4 new corona deaths in Finland. About 12,000 young people started military service. A fire destroyed the service building of the Levi ski resort. Guitarist Alexi Laiho has died. Winter frost weather continues.

## Corona tests at the airport

![A passenger goes for a corona test, registering at the City of Vantaa point at the airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_171/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7047515f280ab5471f5/1610107752)

A lot of corona tests will be done at Helsinki-Vantaa airport this week. The plan is to test the passengers of about 30 aeroplanes.

Many passengers are now coming to Helsinki from countries where the corona situation is bad. That is why passengers are guided straight from the aeroplane to a corona test.

A corona test is not compulsory. However, passengers usually want a corona test when one is offered.

## Finland's corona figures

![A woman wearing a face mask walks on the street in Lahti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11694795)

Now the corona figures for Monday.

There are 4 new corona deaths in Finland. A total of 565 people have died of corona in Finland.

There are also 147 new corona infections in Finland. About 37,000 corona infections have been found in Finland in total.

## The army has started

![Conscript in close order drill](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11012221)

Open image viewer

In the army young people learn, for example, how to use a weapon. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

About 12,000 young people started military service on Monday. In the army, young people learn, for example, to use weapons.

Young people start military service in January or July. They are in the army for at least 165 days.

Military service is compulsory for men in Finland. Women may join the army if they want to themselves.

If a young person does not want to go to the army, he can go to civilian service. In civilian service, the young person does not need to use weapons. Civilian service includes work, for example, in organisations.

## The fire in Levi

![A 1,000 square metre service building burned in Levi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1696,h_954,x_0,y_11/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7588935ff2548aa5cf5/1609716928)

The service building burned at the Levi ski resort. Photo: Iiro Rajala

There has been a big fire on Levitunturi in Lapland. Nobody died or was injured in the fire.

The fire destroyed the service building of the Levi ski resort. The damage may be worth more than 2 million euros.

The cause of the fire was possibly a fault in an electrical device.

The operation of the Levi ski resort continues as normal.

## Guitarist Laiho died

![Children of Bodom's Alexi Laiho plays the guitar.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11723284)

Open image viewer

Alexi Laiho. Photo: Niklas Meltio / Lehtikuva

Guitarist Alexi Laiho has died. Laiho was 41 years old.

Alexi Laiho played the guitar in the band Children of Bodom. The band played metal music.

Music magazines chose Laiho as the best guitarist in the world in 2009 and as the best metal guitarist in the world in 2011.

Laiho had had health problems for some time. Laiho died at home in Helsinki last week.

Winter frost weather continues on Tuesday across the whole country.

In the south on Tuesday it is a few degrees below zero, otherwise the temperature is between -5 and -15 degrees.

Frost weather continues on Wednesday too, and in the south a little more snow may fall.
