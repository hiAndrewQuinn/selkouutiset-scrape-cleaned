# Saturday 24.8.2019

Sari Essayah will continue as chair of the Christian Democrats. Metro trains have not been able to stop at Rautatientori metro station. The main prize of Eurojackpot went to Siilinjärvi in Finland. E-cigarettes can be dangerous. The weather is sunny.

## Christian Democrats' party leader

![Sari Essayah](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10932968)

Open image viewer

Christian Democrat party leader Sari Essayah. Photo: Juha Tamminen / AOP

The Christian Democrats have chosen a party leader. Sari Essayah continues as party leader. The party leader was elected at the Christian Democrats' party congress in Oulu.

Sari Essayah has been the Christian Democrats' party leader since 2015.

Before the party congress, Essayah considered whether she could continue as party leader, because she got breast cancer this summer. However, Essayah has recovered well from the cancer surgery, and she is able to continue working.

## Helsinki metro traffic

![Rautatientori metro station flooded in Helsinki on 23 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10935264)

Open image viewer

Water got into the Rautatientori metro station on Friday. Photo: Ulla Malminen / Yle

There are changes to Helsinki metro traffic at the weekend. Metro trains will not stop at the Rautatientori metro station.

The reason is the heavy rain on Friday. Water also got into the metro's technical rooms.

The shops in the station tunnel are open as normal.

On Friday metro traffic was cut off in central Helsinki because of the heavy rain.

## A 90 million euro win

![Eurojackpot balls in close-up.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10936648)

Open image viewer

This week the correct Eurojackpot numbers were found on a coupon that was played in Siilinjärvi. Photo: Veikkaus

In the small Finnish municipality of Siilinjärvi there are now 50 millionaires.

The reason is Eurojackpot, the European lottery. This week's main prize of Eurojackpot went to Siilinjärvi in Finland. The main prize is 92 million euros.

The winnings go to 50 people who played Eurojackpot together. The place where it was played was the K-Supermarket in Siilinjärvi.

Finns have had luck with Eurojackpot before too. The main prize has come to Finland 16 times already. Eurojackpot started in 2012, and 18 countries take part.

## The danger of e-cigarettes

![People smoking e-cigarettes in New York.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10936703)

Open image viewer

E-cigarettes are popular today. Photo: Alba Vigaray / EPA

E-cigarettes can be dangerous.

In the USA about 200 illnesses are being investigated, the cause of which may be e-cigarettes. Many of those who have become ill are young.

Now Americans are also investigating a death. The patient died of a lung illness whose cause the doctors have not found. The patient used an e-cigarette.

The World Health Organization, WHO, has warned about e-cigarettes. WHO says that e-cigarettes are harmful to health.
