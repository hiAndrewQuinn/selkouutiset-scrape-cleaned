# Radio Clear News Thursday 4.5.2023

Interest rates. Food sales. Strike warning in social services. Young adults. The weather.

## Interest rates

![ECB president Christine Lagarde at a press conference in Frankfurt on 4 May 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4697,h_2642,x_0,y_85/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11087636453b884c2988/1705950237)

European Central Bank president Christine Lagarde. Photo: Ronald Wittek / EPA

The European Central Bank, ECB, is raising interest rates. The ECB said on Thursday that it will raise its key interest rates by 0.25 percentage points.

With interest rates, the ECB fights inflation, that is, the rise in prices. The ECB has been raising rates since last summer.

The ECB's interest rate decision also affects the interest rates of many mortgages. Many households' expenses grow when the mortgage interest rate rises, and paying off the loan is more expensive than before.

## Food sales

![Meat products at a meat counter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_241/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1106082644bbbe7ba16f/1687350913)

For example, the price of meat has risen a lot. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Food sales have fallen. In particular, fish and meat, as well as vegetables and fruit, have been sold less than before at the start of the year.

People buy less food because food prices have risen a lot. In the early part of this year, food cost about 17 percent more than a year earlier.

## Strike warning in social services

![A man sits in a wheelchair at a table in a service housing unit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_122/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6255615dfccb2796b49/1576848611)

Open image viewer

There may be a strike in some service housing units at the end of May. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

Strikes may begin in the social services sector. Employee organisations warn of strikes in private social services.

If strikes begin, they will affect some day-care centres and round-the-clock service housing units. The strike does not concern all social services.

Workers are planning strikes because they want more pay. If they do not agree on pay, the workers will go on strike, that is, stop work for a while, from 23 May.

## Young adults

![A white village hall, a red log barn and a church tower in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4618,h_2593,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1101070643fa8996fdd3/1681893780)

It can be nice to live in a small place. Photo: Anne-Maria Niskanen / Yle

Many young adults want to move to the countryside.

Research director Tuomas Kuhmonen of the University of Turku says that small places now interest young people.

In a small place it is easy to combine work, free time and family life. Remote work gives good opportunities for this.

Small places want new residents. However, basic services must be in order for new residents.

Research director Tuomas Kuhmonen reminds that school, day care and healthcare are important services for young families.

It is also important that the place has cheap rental flats.

## The weather

![Friday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11088446453cea7319fc/1683214015)

The weather continues cold. At night the temperature goes below freezing across the whole country, and the daytime temperature also stays below +10 degrees.

Friday is mostly dry, with water and sleet showers mainly in the east.

The weekend also starts with cold weather. On Saturday the temperature is between 5 and 10 degrees across the whole country.

Gradually, however, the weather will warm up, and next week the temperature may rise to about 15 degrees.
