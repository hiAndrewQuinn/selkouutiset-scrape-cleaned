# Sunday 5.2.2017 (radio)

The Joint Responsibility fundraising campaign has started. Human rights awards have been celebrated in Kyyjärvi. Runeberg Day was celebrated on Sunday. The Finnish film "Tom of Finland" has won an important prize. Yle's name machine tells which names were popular in different times.

## Joint Responsibility fundraising

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9404185)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö opened the Joint Responsibility fundraising campaign. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The Joint Responsibility fundraising campaign has started. On Sunday President Sauli Niinistö spoke on television and opened the Joint Responsibility fundraising campaign.

During the Joint Responsibility campaign, money is collected for a good cause. The Joint Responsibility campaign is held every year.

This year money is being collected against human trafficking. In human trafficking a person is forced to work without a decent wage. He or she is not allowed to decide about their own affairs. Human trafficking is illegal, but it also exists in Finland. The victims are often foreigners.

The Joint Responsibility campaign also gives help abroad. This year it helps, for example, young Syrians who have fled the war to Jordan, which is a neighbouring country of Syria.

## Kyyjärvi's celebration

![Noormuhammad Noori at the Kyyjärvi reception centre, with social instructor Päivi Peltokangas and head instructor Terttu Niskanen in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9443194)

In Kyyjärvi in Central Finland there was a celebration on Sunday. The reason for the celebration was that Kyyjärvi received 2 human rights awards in December.

The organisations Amnesty and Sadankomitea gave the awards to Kyyjärvi, because Kyyjärvi has been a good place for asylum seekers.

## Runeberg Day

![Johan Ludvig Runeberg](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9340964)

Open image viewer

Finland's national poet Johan Ludvig Runeberg. Photo: Svenska litteratursällskapet i Finland

Runeberg Day was celebrated on Sunday. The writer Johan Ludvig Runeberg is Finland's national poet.

Runeberg was born in 1804. He was born in Pietarsaari and later lived in Porvoo. Both cities are Swedish-speaking, and Runeberg also wrote in Swedish.

Runeberg's most famous book is "The Tales of Ensign Stål". The book begins with a poem, whose name in Swedish is "Vårt land", that is, in Finnish "Maamme" ("Our Land"). The poem became Finland's national anthem.

## Tom of Finland

![Dome Karukoski](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8816804)

Now for newer culture. The Finnish film "Tom of Finland" has won an important prize. The film won the prize at the Gothenburg International Film Festival in Sweden.

![Tom of Finland paintings](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9400764)

Open image viewer

Touko Laaksonen, that is "Tom of Finland", made homoerotic drawings. Photo: Petra Ristola / Yle

The director of the film is Dome Karukoski. The film tells about the life of the artist Touko Laaksonen. Laaksonen is known around the world as Tom of Finland. He made homoerotic drawings of men with large muscles.

The film comes to Finnish cinemas at the end of February.

## Finns' first names

Open image viewer

Yle's name machine tells when a name was in fashion. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Do you want to know which first names Finns have given their children at different times?

On the Yle News website there is a new name machine. It tells when a name was in fashion.

The name machine covers the years from 1900 to 2016. Throughout that whole time the most popular first names are Matti and Anna.

Yle's name machine can be found at yle.fi/uutiset.
