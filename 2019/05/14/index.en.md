# Tuesday 14.5.2019 (radio)

Many EU election candidates are also members of municipal councils. Far fewer roads are being resurfaced this year than before. Alternative treatments are also used for health. About 1.2 million people lived alone in Finland last year. Finland's Eurovision representative Darude is trying to reach the Eurovision final today.

## Many EU candidates are also municipal councillors

Many EU election candidates are also members of municipal councils, the Association of Finnish Local and Regional Authorities' survey says.

About 270 candidates are seeking a seat as an MEP, that is, a Member of the European Parliament. About 30 per cent of them are also municipal councillors.

Some of the EU election candidates were also running in this spring's parliamentary elections. 15 of them were also elected to Parliament.

A total of 13 MEPs will be elected from Finland to the European Parliament.

Advance voting for the EU elections starts tomorrow, Wednesday. The actual election day is Sunday 26 May.

## There is less money for road work than before

![Asphalt work in the centre of Hämeenlinna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10782002)

Open image viewer

Asphalt work in the centre of Hämeenlinna. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Far fewer roads are being resurfaced this year than before. About 1,500 kilometres of resurfacing work will be done.

The Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency says that this summer only the busiest roads will be repaired. There is no money to repair smaller roads. That is why speed limits must be lowered on many roads.

This summer's resurfacing work has already started. The first road works have already been done in Southern Finland.

## Alternative treatments are used quite a lot

![Zone therapy](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5388841)

Open image viewer

Zone therapy is being given here. Photo: Pasi Takkunen / Yle

Health is also treated with alternative treatments.

Alternative treatments are treatments that are not given in hospitals. They have not been studied in the same way as treatment that a doctor gives.

Alternative treatments include, for example, various herbs, aromatherapy and zone therapy.

About 20 per cent of Finns have used some kind of alternative treatment.

## 1.2 million Finns live alone

![Apartment blocks in the harbour of Lappeenranta](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10416819)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

About 1.2 million people lived alone in Finland last year.

Statistics Finland says that the number grew by 2.5 per cent in a year. Already over 40 per cent of all households were homes of one resident.

Last year the number of two-person households grew, but the number of larger households fell.

## Darude competes in Eurovision

![Darude and Sebastian Rejman at rehearsals in Tel Aviv.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10781323)

Finland's Eurovision representative Darude, that is Ville Virtanen, is trying to reach the Eurovision final today.

In the evening semi-final in Tel Aviv, Israel, 17 countries perform, of which 10 will go through. Darude and singer Sebastian Rejman will perform in the semi-final with number 3.

The broadcast starts at 22.05 on Radio Suomi and at 21.25 on TV 2.

The second Eurovision semi-final is on Thursday and the Eurovision final on Saturday.
