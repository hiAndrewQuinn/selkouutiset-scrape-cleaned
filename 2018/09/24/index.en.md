# Monday 24.9.2018 (radio)

A big police operation. A church fire. A feared caterpillar. Child welfare.

## The big police operation is over

![Investigators and police in the Turku archipelago on 22 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10418712)

Investigators and police in the Turku archipelago. Photo: Kirsi Kanerva / Lehtikuva

The big police operation in the Turku area has ended.

According to the police, the operation was prepared from February onwards. The police arrested people in connection with the operation.

The reason for the operation is that the police suspect money laundering. With money laundering, criminals try to hide that the money has been obtained through crimes.

Interior Minister Kai Mykkänen says that the investigation is exceptional and that it was carried out according to plan. The investigation may last well into next year.

## More information about the church fire

![Smoking ruins of the Kiihtelysvaara church.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10420078)

Open image viewer

The Kiihtelysvaara church is now a heap of ruins. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

The fire at the Kiihtelysvaara church is becoming confirmed as deliberate arson.

On Monday new evidence has been obtained, because the ruins cooled down and could be examined. Kiihtelysvaara is located in Joensuu, in Eastern Finland.

Police dogs have examined the fire site. The police say that flammable liquids may have been used to set the church on fire.

The church was almost 270 years old. It is not yet known who may have set the church on fire.

## A feared pest spreads

![Nun moth - Lymantria monacha (lat.)](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10419887)

Male nun moth. Photo: Didier Descouens / CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Climate change is bringing feared caterpillars to Finland's forests. The climate is warming, and the nun moth, which is one of the forest pests, has spread quickly northwards.

The nun moth is a moth that is called the "white pest". A recent study found that the pest has good chances of spreading widely in Finland.

According to the study, nun moth eggs can survive a frost of 29 degrees. The nun moth caterpillars cause

In Europe, large forest damage is sometimes caused, because they eat the leaves and needles of trees. In Finland the nun moth has so far caused damage only in small areas.

## Social workers are tired

![A child plays with modelling clay.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10105134)

Open image viewer

Child welfare is hard work. Photo: AOP

According to a study, social workers in child welfare get very tired at work. The workers are prone to anxiety, because the work involves hard pressure.

Social workers would need support, but their supervisors do not have enough time to guide the work. Child welfare workers decide whether a child can stay in the family or whether the child is placed somewhere else.

Child welfare workers also bear responsibility for a wrong decision. At worst, a wrong decision can mean the death of a child.
