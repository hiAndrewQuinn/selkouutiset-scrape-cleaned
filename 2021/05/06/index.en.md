## The EU recovery package

![Petteri Orpo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4502,h_2532,x_0,y_373/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-770137601beb5905438/1619533863)

In Finland the debate about the EU recovery package continues.

The National Coalition said on Thursday that most of the National Coalition's MPs support the recovery package. Parliament will vote on the recovery package next week.

The government wants Finland to approve the recovery package. However, the government needs support from the opposition party, the National Coalition, in Parliament for the recovery package to be approved.

The other big opposition party, the Finns Party, intends to vote against the recovery package. In the view of the Finns Party, the recovery package moves money from Finland to Southern Europe.

The EU recovery package is 750 billion euros. With the package, the European Union intends to support the economies of EU countries, so that the EU gets through the corona crisis.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that there is 1 new corona death in Finland. There are 280 new coronavirus infections.

Corona vaccinations have progressed well. Already more than 1.8 million Finns have received a corona vaccination.

New information shows that corona vaccinations also work against variants of the coronavirus, that is, the variants.

## Party support figures

![April 2021 party support poll. The National Coalition has risen past the Finns Party to become the largest party, and the SDP is third.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11916015)

Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

The National Coalition is now the most popular party. This is what Yle's new opinion poll shows.

In the opinion poll, people were asked which party they intend to vote for in the municipal elections.

In the poll the National Coalition got the most support, about 20 percent. The support for the National Coalition has grown especially in Helsinki and other big cities.

Behind the National Coalition comes the Finns Party. In the previous poll, the Finns Party was still in first place.

The SDP gets almost as much support as the Finns Party. The support for both parties is about 18 percent.

The support figures for the National Coalition, the Finns Party and the SDP are close to each other. The competition for the place of the biggest party is fierce until the municipal elections. The municipal elections will be held on 13 June.

The support figures for all parties can be found on Yle's website.
