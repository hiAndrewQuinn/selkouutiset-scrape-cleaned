# Sunday 3.3.2019 (radio)

Forest felling. Stabbing. Biowaste. Ski competitions. Weather information.

## The opposition got angry at the prime minister

![Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10654793)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The opposition parties, the Greens and the SDP, got angry at Prime Minister Juha Sipilä today.

Sipilä said that the Greens and the left-wing parties want Finland's forests not to be logged at all. Sipilä said that this means many factories will close and work will end.

The chair of the Greens, Ville Niinistö, said that Prime Minister Sipilä is lying. Niinistö said that the Greens do not demand that forest felling be stopped.

SDP deputy chair Sanna Marin said that Prime Minister Sipilä's speech is not true.

The Greens and the SDP say that the amount of forest felling can be agreed. The Centre Party, the prime minister's party, thinks that forests can be cut more.

**Correction: Ville Niinistö is not the chair of the Greens. Ville Niinistö is a Green MP.**

## Maggots eat biowaste

![Black soldier fly larvae on apples](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10362815)

Open image viewer

Black soldier fly larvae eat a huge amount and grow quickly. Photo: Eeva Hannula / Yle

Insects are to be used as eaters of biowaste. In Finland, research is being done into everything that black soldier fly larvae eat.

Black soldier fly larvae eat a really large amount of food. Once the larva has hatched, it grows up to a thousandfold in 3 weeks.

In the study, the larvae have already eaten ordinary food waste, livestock manure and sludge from a wastewater treatment plant.

The larvae of the black soldier fly can eat more than half of all waste.

The ski world championships ended today. In the last competition, the men skied 50 kilometres.

Norway's Hans Christer Holund won. The Finns did not do very well.

Finland got only one medal at the world championships. Iivo Niskanen got bronze in the 15-kilometre race.
