RKP. SDP. Finland–Sweden athletics match. The weather.

## RKP

RKP chair Anna-Maja Henriksson in an interview with Yle on Saturday. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

Taking part in the government has been difficult for RKP. This is what the party chair Anna-Maja Henriksson says.

Henriksson tells Yle in an interview that within RKP there has been discussion even about whether the party will leave the government. Henriksson hopes that the government will have peace to work and can start the autumn's work.

RKP has argued in the government especially with the Finns Party. The old racist writings of Finns Party ministers have worsened relations between the parties.

This week the government finished its statement on racism. In the statement, the government says how it intends to fight racism. The statement is a joint text of all the government parties.

Parliament will deal with the government's statement next week. Henriksson hopes that RKP's MPs will then support the government.

SDPP general secretary Mikkel Näkkäläjärvi (left) and chair Antti Lindtman (right). Next to Lindtman is first deputy chair Nasima Razmyar. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The SDP party congress has continued in Jyväskylä.

On Saturday, the party congress elected the general secretary and the deputy chairs. SDP's new general secretary is Mikkel Näkkäläjärvi, and the first deputy chair is now Nasima Razmyar.

SDP got a new chair already on Friday, when Antti Lindtman was chosen for the chair's post.

Lindtman will now lead SDP's work together with the general secretary and the deputy chairs. SDP is the biggest opposition party, that is, the party is not in the government.

## Athletics match

![Elmo Lakka clears a hurdle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3543,h_1992,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1003182631494b542375/1662293244)

Athletes from Finland and Sweden meet in an international match every year. The match is held in turn in Sweden and Finland. This picture is from last year. Photo: Matti Raivio / All Over Press

Athletes from Finland and Sweden compete in the match this weekend. The athletics match is held this year in Stockholm.

The Finland–Sweden match is an old tradition. The first time the match was held was almost 100 years ago, in 1925.

Women athletes joined the match 70 years ago. The athletics match between Finland and Sweden is held every year.

In recent years Sweden has been better in the match. Finnish women last beat Sweden in 2015. The previous win for Finnish men came in 2018.

You can follow on Yle's channels how the match goes this year.
