# Monday 17.8.2020 (radio)

The corona situation is bringing more members to unemployment funds. There are queues for corona tests. Moving from cities to the countryside increased. Matka-Vekka cancelled autumn holiday flights. The weather.

## Many new members in unemployment funds

![A benefit form for earnings-related allowance is being filled in.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11452127)

Open image viewer

If you become unemployed or laid off, you can apply to an unemployment fund for earnings-related allowance. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Many new people have joined unemployment funds this year. Many Finns have joined an unemployment fund because many companies are doing badly.

More Finns than before are afraid that their work may end or that they may be laid off. If work ends, you can get money from an unemployment fund, that is, earnings-related allowance.

Tens of thousands of new people have joined unemployment funds this year.

## Queues for corona tests continue

![Taking a corona test at Armila health centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3624,h_2039,x_0,y_340/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7089865f3a72ed9b102/1597666653)

Open image viewer

At many health centres you can take a corona test while sitting in a car. Photo: Silva Nieminen / Yle

Corona testing is still congested in Finland. The situation is especially bad in the Helsinki area, where people have to wait 2 or 3 days to get a test.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, is looking for ways to speed up getting a test.

THL is also studying the possibility of a home test. If the corona test can perhaps be done at home, it will speed up getting the test result.

## The corona era has increased moving to the countryside

![A woman jogging in a rural landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11497995)

Open image viewer

Some people have wanted to move to a quieter place, perhaps because in the city it feels cramped because of the corona situation. Photo: Peter Erik Forsberg / AOP

The corona situation has increased the number of Finns moving from cities to more spacious municipalities. This year more people have moved from cities to places that have fewer houses and people.

Many people who have wanted to move to a rural municipality for a long time have perhaps now done so because of the corona situation.

Most Finns still move from small municipalities or the countryside to cities, however.

## A travel agency cancelled autumn regional flights

![Kuopio airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7311823)

Open image viewer

Matka-Vekka's holiday flights from Kuopio airport and 11 other airports have now been cancelled. Photo: Tina Lundan / Yle

The travel agency Matka-Vekka is cancelling holiday flights from regional airports. Matka-Vekka is cancelling autumn flights from 12 regional airports to Spain, Portugal, Bulgaria, Italy and Greece.

Matka-Vekka is cancelling holiday flights from regional airports because corona infections have increased in many countries.

On Tuesday the weather is mostly dry, that is, it does not rain. In the west there may be showers of rain and thunder showers. Mostly it is partly cloudy, that is, at times there are clouds and at times the sun shines.

The temperature on Tuesday is between 17 and 23 degrees, and in Lapland between 8 and 18 degrees.
