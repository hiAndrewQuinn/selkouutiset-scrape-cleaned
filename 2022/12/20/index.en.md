## Drains during a power cut

![Running water tap.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6048,h_3402,x_0,y_331/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-842429611a6b1694319/1669615403)

Open image viewer

A tap must not be left running during a power cut. Photo: Juho Liukkonen / Yle

During a power cut you must be careful with water. This is what the Finnish Rescue Sector's Central Organisation says.

The organisation says that a water tap must not be left running during a power cut. If a lot of water is drained into the sewer during a power cut, the water may start to flood.

The reason is that water travels to wastewater treatment plants by electric pumps. Wastewater cannot move if the pumps stop during a power cut.

Everyone should know what to do if the power goes out. Instructions in Easy Language for power cuts can be found on the Easy Language Centre's website. The address is selkokeskus.fi.

## Finnish woollen socks for Ukraine's president

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyi has received Finnish woollen socks.

Zelenskyi said that he received the woollen socks from a Finnish girl, Henriikka. Henriikka is 14 years old.

Henriikka decided to help Ukrainians when Russia started the war against Ukraine. Henriikka knits and sells woollen socks. She donates the money to help Ukraine.
