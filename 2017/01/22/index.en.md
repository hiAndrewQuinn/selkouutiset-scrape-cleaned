# Sunday 22.1.2017 (radio)

Train traffic. Popular parties in local politics. Wildfires in Chile. Israeli settlements. The World Rally Championship.

## A big disruption in train traffic has been fixed

![Passengers waiting for a train.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9270065)

Open image viewer

Trains were late on Sunday because of a data network fault. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

There were big problems in train traffic on Sunday.

In the morning, train traffic was up to two hours late because there was a fault in the data communication system. The situation was bad because there were also faults in the backup systems.

According to the Finnish Transport Agency, such a big fault is rare. It is not yet known why the fault happened in the system.

The system was being repaired all day, and traffic will return to normal in the evening.

## Survey: big parties are best at municipal matters

![Espoo City Council](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9379970)

Open image viewer

Espoo City Council has 75 members. The biggest parties are the National Coalition Party, the Greens and the SDP. Photo: City of Espoo

Citizens have rated how well the parties know municipal matters.

According to the survey, the National Coalition Party, the SDP and the Centre Party know municipal matters best.

The survey was done by the Foundation for Municipal Development, and about 1,000 people answered it.

Finland will hold municipal elections on 9 April. The residents of a municipality vote on which politicians get into the municipal council, that is, to decide on the municipality's matters.

Everyone who can vote in the municipal elections will get a letter in the post.

## Wildfires in Chile threaten to spread

Chile has fierce wildfires.

A state of emergency has been declared in Chile. In Southern and Central Chile there are 20 different fires that threaten vineyards, factories and towns.

40 houses have already been destroyed and about 200 people have had to leave their homes.

It is very warm and windy in Chile, and that increases the fires.

## Israel expands settlements

![The Palestinian village of Isawiya and the Israeli settlement of Almon in East Jerusalem.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9373851)

Open image viewer

A Palestinian village and an Israeli settlement in East Jerusalem. Photo: Atef Safadi / EPA

Israel continues building settlements in East Jerusalem, which belongs to the Palestinian territory of the West Bank.

Israel plans to build more than 550 new homes in East Jerusalem.

The deputy mayor of Jerusalem says that Israel can now continue building in the settlements because Donald Trump became President of the United States.

The United States is Israel's most important ally, but the previous president Barack Obama did not accept the building of settlements in the Palestinian territories.

In December, the UN Security Council also passed a resolution that forbids building settlements in the Palestinian territories.

## Finns are a rally nation

![Jari-Matti Latvala's Toyota in action at the Monte Carlo World Rally. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9417240)

Finn Jari-Matti Latvala got a good start to the World Rally Championship. Photo: Lehtikuva

Many Finns are now following rally. The World Rally Championship started in Monte Carlo.

Rally driver Jari-Matti Latvala was second today. He is Finnish. The race was won by Frenchman Sebastien Ogier.

The rally world champion is decided when the rally has been driven in 11 different countries. One World Rally Championship event is also driven in Finland.
