# Monday 1.3.2021 (radio)

Finland is in a state of emergency. Restaurants will be closed for 3 weeks. There are 8 new coronavirus deaths in Finland. A weak earthquake has happened in Kuusamo. The TV series The Crown won Golden Globe awards in the USA. February was colder than usual. Sunny and warm weather continues.

## Finland in a state of emergency

![ Sanna Marin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11815011)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin spoke about the state of emergency on Monday. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The fight against the coronavirus continues.

On Monday the government stated that Finland is in a state of emergency because of the coronavirus.

During the state of emergency, coronavirus restrictions can be tightened. For example, restaurants can be ordered to close. The government has made a proposal on this to Parliament.

The government's proposal is that restaurants will close a week from Monday and stay closed for 3 weeks. However, they may sell takeaway food, that is, take-away portions.

The plan is to close restaurants that are open to the public. For example, the canteens of workplaces and care homes may stay open.

Sign for a Mehiläinen drive-in coronavirus test in Rovaniemi. Photo: Perttu Ruokangas / Yle

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare has again reported new coronavirus figures.

There are 8 new coronavirus deaths in Finland. In total, 750 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

There are almost 400 new coronavirus infections. The total number of coronavirus infections in Finland is now about 58,000.

About 370,000 Finns have been vaccinated against the coronavirus at least once.

## Earthquake in Kuusamo

![Earthquake in Kuusamo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11815057)

Open image viewer

Kuusamo is in Northern Finland. Photo: Yle

A weak earthquake has happened in Kuusamo in Northern Finland. The tremor happened on Sunday evening.

The earthquake did not cause any damage. People in Kuusamo noticed the tremor because the houses shook and the windows rattled for about 10 seconds.

Earthquakes do not happen often in Finland. They are always also very weak.

## Golden Globe awards

Golden Globe awards for film and television have been handed out in the USA.

The award for best television series went to the TV series The Crown. The series is about the British royal family.

The series' actors Emma Corrin and Josh O'Connor were also awarded. They play Princess Diana and Prince Charles in the series.

## February weather

![Hoarfrost: moisture that frost has frozen onto tree branches.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11783614)

Open image viewer

There were many frost days in February. Photo: Marja-Liisa Kämppi / Yle

The weather has varied recently.

February was colder than usual. At the beginning of February there were severe frosts in Finland.

At the end of February, however, it was warm. In many measuring places, even the February temperature record was measured.

Sunny and warm weather continues on Tuesday.

At night there is frost in most of the country, but in the daytime the temperature rises above zero in the south and central parts. In Lapland there is frost.

In the middle of the week the weather will get colder again in the south too.
