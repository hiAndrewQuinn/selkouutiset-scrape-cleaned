# Sunday 30.4.2017 (radio)

May Day has begun. Havis Amanda was given a student cap. Many customs belong to a Finnish May Day. May Day itself is the celebration day of workers. Valtteri Bottas won the Russian Grand Prix of Formula One.

## The students' celebration

![Students washing the Havis Amanda statue on May Day Eve in central Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9589711)

May Day has begun. On Sunday May Day Eve was celebrated. May Day itself is on Monday.

In Finland May Day is an important celebration for students. Students have organised big events this May Day Eve too.

For example, in Helsinki students have put a student cap on Havis Amanda's head. Havis Amanda, also called Manta, is a statue in the Market Square in Helsinki. Every year it gets a student cap on May Day Eve at 6 p.m. This year too the Market Square was full of students.

University and polytechnic students walk around on May Day wearing a student cap. Many also wear overalls, that is the outfit of a student union. The outfit shows what subject the student is studying.

Many older Finns also wear their old student cap on May Day.

## May Day traditions

Many customs belong to a Finnish May Day. May Day has been celebrated in the same way as today for about 100 years.

![A glass of sima on a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7965600)

Open image viewer

Sima is drunk on May Day. Photo: Yle

The May Day treats are doughnuts and tippaleipä. The May Day drink is sima. It is made from water, farin sugar and lemon. Finns also drink a lot of alcohol on May Day.

The May Day decorations are the May Day streamer and balloons. A big and colourful balloon is the best thing about May Day in the opinion of many children.

## Workers' celebration

![May Day marchers in Helsinki in 1983.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7965647)

Open image viewer

May Day march in 1983. Photo: Martti Juntunen / Yle

May Day, that is 1 May, is the celebration day of workers. Workers' May Day is celebrated all around the world.

Workers' May Day began in the USA in the 1800s. It spread quickly to Europe too. At that time workers demanded better working conditions, for example an 8-hour working day.

Today political parties organise May Day celebrations. The parties also organise May Day marches. Earlier, very many people took part in the May Day marches, but nowadays fewer.

May Day is a spring celebration for many. The problem, however, is that a warm spring usually does not start yet at May Day in Finland.

This year too, May Day Eve has been cold. May Day itself is a little warmer and sunnier in Southern Finland.
