## China and Ukraine

![The presidents of Russia and China.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2999,h_1683,x_0,y_56/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91069861fd254ca2587/1643980153)

Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping. Photo: Alexei Druzhinin/TASS/All Over P

Ukraine asks China to condemn Russia's "barbarism", that is, its brutality, because Russia attacked Ukraine. Ukraine says that China should join the western countries in condemning Russia.

China has not condemned Russia's attack on Ukraine. China has also stayed away from international demonstrations against the war.

US President Joe Biden spoke on the phone with China's leader Xi Jinping on Friday. Biden said that supporting Russia in the war in Ukraine would be costly for China.

## Ukraine's parties and media

![Volodymyr Zelenskyy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_153/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-92803462300ac4bc6cc/1647316772)

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. Photo: Ukrainian presidential office / AFP

Ukraine bans the activity of 11 parties. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy told about the matter in a video address last night.

Britain's broadcaster BBC says that some of the parties have links to the Russian state.

Ukraine has also blocked the activity of 1,500 media outlets.

Ukraine says that media platforms have spread Russian propaganda. An estimated 15 million people followed them.

## Kela reimbursements

![Blood pressure being measured on an anonymous person at a doctor's.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6021,h_3386,x_0,y_195/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89789861cf08d68d871/1640958267)

Open image viewer

Photo: Esa Huuhko / Yle

Kela reimbursements for private health care will be cut from the beginning of next year. Social and health minister Hanna Sarkkinen says that reimbursements for doctors' fees will not, however, be removed completely.

The cuts apply, besides doctors' fees, for example to physiotherapy and laboratory tests.

The total amount of Kela reimbursements will fall by about 30 percent. The savings will fund staffing levels in elderly care services where care is given around the clock. Then there must be at least 7 care workers for every 10 elderly people.

Network company Nokia filed the most national patent applications in Finland last year. Nokia made over 220 applications in total to the Finnish Patent and Registration Office.

A patent is a time-limited exclusive right to benefit from some invention.

The second most patent applications were filed by the Technical Research Centre VTT, and the third most by the Aalto University Foundation.

## Spring equinox

![Morning of the spring equinox in Hesperia Park in Helsinki on 20 March 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9519517)

Open image viewer

Morning of the spring equinox in Hesperia Park in Helsinki on 20 March. Photo: Teemu Salonen / Lehtikuva

The spring equinox was on Sunday.

At the spring equinox the centre of the sun moves from the southern sky to the northern sky. Day and night are roughly equally long everywhere on Earth.

As spring goes on, days get longer and nights get shorter in Finland and elsewhere in the northern hemisphere.
