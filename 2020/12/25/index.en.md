## The EU and Britain trade deal

![Michel Barnier and Ursula von der Leyen on the stage](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5208,h_2930,x_0,y_269/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7574595fe5a20a73b01/1608884811)

The EU's Brexit chief negotiator Michel Barnier and European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said yesterday that a trade agreement with Britain has been reached. Photo: EPA / Francisco Seco

EU countries have thanked the EU's new trade agreement with Britain. For example, the leaders of Germany, France and Italy say that the agreement being reached is good news.

The agreement was reached yesterday, on Christmas Eve. The agreement concerns trade relations between the EU and Britain, and also fishing, for example, after the turn of the year. According to the agreement, free trade of goods between the EU and Britain will continue, but British products must meet the EU's rules on health and safety.

The agreement is over 2,000 pages long. Its details are now being gone through in EU countries and in Britain. After that, the agreement still has to be approved.

Minister for European Affairs Tytti Tuppurainen says that the Finnish Parliament will be called back from its Christmas break to deal with the agreement before the turn of the year.

## Christmas for the police and rescue services

![Text ESPL on the side of a fire engine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10549251)

Open image viewer

Rescue services have put out fires during the Christmas period. Photo: Esa Huuhko / Yle

Finns have mostly spent Christmas quietly.

The police's tasks have mainly been about drunk drivers and domestic alarms. The rescue service has put out some fires.

In Porvoo in East Uusimaa a semi-detached house caught fire during the night. 8 people were evacuated from the house. In Joroinen in South Savo a detached house was already on fire on Christmas Eve morning. One person died in the fire. In Riihimäki in Kanta-Häme a fire also broke out in an apartment block on Christmas Eve morning. About 20 people were evacuated from the building.

Fires have also been put out during the Christmas period in, for example, Kaarina in the Turku area, Utajärvi in North Ostrobothnia, and Jyväskylä in Central Finland.

## Finns' hopes for next year

![Bar chart of a survey for Finns](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11714810)

Open image viewer

Photo: Hilppa Hyrkäs / Yle

Finns' hopes for next year are brighter than those of other EU citizens.

57 per cent of Finns believe that next year will be better than this year. In other EU countries, on average 35 per cent believe in a better next year.

Only 10 per cent of Finns believe that next year will be worse than this year. In EU countries, about 30 per cent believe in a worse next year.

In Finland the survey was carried out by Taloustutkimus. Juho Rahkonen, research manager at Taloustutkimus, says that Finns' optimism, that is, their bright expectations, is perhaps due to the coronavirus epidemic not having been as severe in Finland as elsewhere in Europe.
