# Saturday 4.3.2017 (radio)

The government and the opposition have argued about the social and health care reform. The Lahti World Championships have been a success. Saturday was Lost Animal Day. The rainforest is not as untouched as has been thought.

## The social and health care reform causes disagreement again

The government and the opposition have argued about the sote reform, or the social and health care reform. The party leaders were in a municipal election panel discussion.

Antti Rinne of the SDP said that freedom of choice must be left out of the sote law completely. Freedom of choice means that everyone can choose which sote centre they want to be a customer of. A sote centre has, for example, a health centre.

The opposition says that freedom of choice helps big companies and goes against the interests of patients. The government stressed that freedom of choice will be in the law and that changes may still be made to the proposal in spring.

## The Lahti World Championships get praise

![The World Championships stadium bowl photographed from the ski jump](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9491368)

Open image viewer

This is what the World Championships stadium bowl looks like. The picture was taken from the ski jump towards the stands. Photo: Tomi Hänninen / Yle

The Nordic Ski World Championships in Lahti have been a success. The World Championships have received praise from Finland and abroad. All the skiing events could be held at the championships. The technology has also worked well.

It is now already certain that a part of the skiing World Cup, the Salpausselkä Games, will be held in Lahti next winter. Other major championships are also possible. The organisers believe that the World Championships could be in Lahti again in maybe 15 or 20 years. Before that there could be a World Cup competition every year.

The Lahti World Championships end on Sunday.

## Saturday was Lost Animal Day

![Dogs stay at the lost animal shelter for a couple of days.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7720178)

Open image viewer

Many municipalities have temporary homes for lost animals. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Saturday was the national Lost Animal Day. It is held every year on the first Saturday of March.

The Helsinki Animal Protection Society says that the position of lost animals must be improved in the new animal welfare law.

The society says that Finland must get a national database of animals. Then it is easier to find a lost animal and to hold people who abandon animals responsible.

## The Amazon rainforest is not untouched after all

![An aerial picture of the Amazon region.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6468061)

Open image viewer

The huge Amazon River flows through the Amazon rainforest. Photo: Marcelo Sayao / EPA

Trees were planted in the Amazon rainforest in South America already thousands of years ago. The rainforest is not as untouched as has been thought.

A study says that the indigenous people shaped the forest. It can still be seen in the rainforest today. The rainforest has a lot of useful tree species that are more common than other species. Such species are, for example, the Brazil nut and cacao. The species were used a lot for food and in building.

The researchers compared different forest maps of over 3000 areas in the Amazon. The researchers believe that by observing trees you can find ancient settlement areas that have not been known before.
