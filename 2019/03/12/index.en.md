# Tuesday 12.3.2019 (radio)

Politics interests young people. There were technical problems in the mother tongue matriculation exam. The consumer ombudsman wants stricter rules for telephone sales. Air pollution causes premature deaths. North Korea voted in its parliamentary elections.

## Youth barometer

![A large demonstration against climate change was held in front of Parliament House on 6 March. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10684032)

Politics interests young people more than before. A new survey says that there are now more young people interested in politics than at any time in the last 20 years.

Of the young people who answered the survey, 61 percent said that they are at least somewhat interested in politics.

The survey also says that young people's experiences of uncertainty and insecurity have increased. Young people are especially worried about climate change, the world political situation and terrorism.

On the other hand, young people's belief in their own ability to make a difference has grown.

The Youth Barometer measures the values and attitudes of 15–29-year-olds.

## Matriculation exams

![Electronic matriculation examinations](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8791076)

Open image viewer

Technical problems disturbed the mother tongue matriculation exam writing. Photo: Markus Heikkilä / Yle

The matriculation exam has had technical problems in different parts of the country.

The matriculation exams began today with the mother tongue reading test. At 2 upper secondary schools, taking the test was delayed by more than 2 hours because of technical problems.

The Matriculation Examination Board says that the problems will be taken into account when the exam is marked.

About 40,000 students take part in the spring matriculation exams.

## Telephone sales

Open image viewer

The consumer ombudsman wants to tighten the rules for telephone sales.

Consumer Ombudsman Katri Väänänen wants telephone sales to require the consumer's permission in advance. The EU Commission also supports prior consent.

Marketing by email or text message already requires the consumer's permission.

## Air pollution

![Factory chimneys against the evening sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6879398)

Open image viewer

Air pollution causes more deaths than was previously thought.

A new study says that air pollution causes about 790,000 premature deaths in Europe every year. In the whole world, air pollution causes 8.8 million premature deaths.

## North Korea's elections

![People queuing to vote in Pyongyang.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10681809)

Open image viewer

North Koreans queued to vote in the Sunday parliamentary elections. Photo: Ed Jones / AFP / Lehtikuva

Parliamentary elections were held in North Korea last weekend.

The voter turnout was 99.99 percent, according to North Korea's state news agency KCNA.

There were about 700 constituencies in the election. In each of them there was 1 candidate approved by the state. Each candidate got 100 percent support, KCNA says.

North Korea's parliament meets perhaps a couple of times a year. The parliament approves the budget and other decisions made in advance.
