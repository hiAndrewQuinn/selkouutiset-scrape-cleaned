## Young people and food

Initially, the topic is young people and food.

Finnish youth eat too much meat and too few vegetables.

Almost 90 percent of boys eat too much meat and other meat products.

One in three girls also eats too much red meat.

## Finnish Association for Developmental Disabilities

Then the topic is the Finnish Association for Developmental Disabilities.

The Finnish Association for Developmental Disabilities may cease to operate.

The reason is cuts to STEA grants. The government wants to reduce financial grants to social and health organizations.

The Finnish Association for People with Developmental Disabilities says it could lose a large portion of its funding. This would also put many services, such as Tikoteekki and Papunet for people with speech disabilities, at risk.

The funding for the Clear Finnish Centre may also run out. The Clear Finnish Centre is the only clear Finnish expert in Finland.

The Finnish Association for the Developmental Disabilities may be closing down. The association has been operating since 1952.
