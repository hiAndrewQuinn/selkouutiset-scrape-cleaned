# Thursday 14.4.2022 (radio)

THL drops mask recommendation. A major strike threatens the municipal sector after May Day. Entry of foreign students to Finland will be made easier. Roads will be repaired less than planned. Jari Kurri buys Jokerit. Weather.

## Mask recommendation

![People wore face masks in central Helsinki on 25 January 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_206/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90651261f0142cbd0d1/1643211467)

The general mask recommendation is ending.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, says that it is still good to wear a mask in public indoor spaces and on public transport. It is good to wear a mask if you suspect a corona infection or if you have respiratory symptoms.

THL says that wearing masks by the whole population no longer has an effect in the current epidemic situation.

THL's line does not apply to workplaces, which decide on the matter themselves.

Regional mask recommendations may also still be in force. For example, the broad mask recommendation is still in place in the Helsinki region.

## Municipal sector strike

![Balloons and a sign reading "You have to get by on your pay!"](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_3,y_447/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9348386242c9c9bd422/1648544455)

Open image viewer

The municipal sector strike threatens to spread after May Day. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

The municipal sector strike in the Helsinki region is being postponed.

The strike was to begin on Tuesday after Easter. However, Labour Minister Tuula Haatainen has postponed the start of the strike by 2 weeks.

The municipal sector now faces a major strike after May Day, unless agreement on the working conditions and pay of the municipal sector is reached before then. In May, more than 81,000 employees from 10 different cities will go on strike.

The municipal sector strike affects, for example, the operation of schools and day-care centres.

## Foreign students

![Students Dhanushi Srinivasan and James Afolaran in the library of the Hervanta campus of Tampere University.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_341/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90319561e6a04605b6a/1642505376)

Open image viewer

Finland wants to make it easier for foreign students to come to the country. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

Foreign students' entry into Finland will be made easier.

The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment says that in future foreign students will get a residence permit straight away for the whole study period. Applying for work after graduation will also become easier.

The relief also applies to the student's family members.

## Road repairs

![A machine spreads new asphalt on the road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10859970)

Open image viewer

A machine spreads new asphalt on the road. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

Roads will be repaired less this summer than was first planned.

The Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency originally said that it would resurface about 2,700 kilometres of roads this year. Now the agency says that about 2,300 kilometres of roads will be resurfaced.

Road maintenance is being cut, because the price of energy and of bitumen, a raw material of asphalt, has risen a lot.

## Jokerit

![Jari Kurri.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_243/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-942475625803d987792/1650016117)

Open image viewer

Jari Kurri owns Jokerit. Photo: Jussi Nukari/Lehtikuva

The ice hockey club Jokerit is passing entirely into the ownership of Jari Kurri.

Kurri already owned 60 percent of Jokerit, and now he is buying the remaining 40 percent from Norilsk Nickel Harjavalta Oy. NN Harjavalta belongs to the Russian Nornickel group.

Jokerit left the Russian KHL ice hockey league when Russia attacked Ukraine.
