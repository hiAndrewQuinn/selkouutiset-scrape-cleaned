# Friday 7.5.2021 (radio)

Gathering restrictions continue in much of Southern Finland. Australia's borders remain closed because of corona. Selkosanomat asked parties questions about municipal election topics. Yle has decided to keep the Pasila link tower. Snow fell in Eastern Finland on Friday.

## Gathering restrictions in Southern Finland

![Students in Kaivopuisto park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11910773)

Open image viewer

A group may have at most 6 people. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Gathering restrictions continue in much of Southern Finland. Gatherings of more than 6 people are banned until the end of May.

In Southern Finland there is still so much corona that the gathering restrictions cannot be lifted. The gathering restrictions are in force, for example, in the Helsinki region.

Even at small gatherings, people must remember safe distances and good hygiene.

## Australia's borders closed

![The city of Melbourne in Australia eased corona restrictions on 22 November 2020. People were, among other things, allowed to go outside without a mask again.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11675864)

Open image viewer

There are very few coronavirus infections in Australia. Photo: EPA / Erik Anderson

Australia's borders remain closed because of corona. Australia's borders may remain closed until the end of next year.

Australia's borders closed already a year ago. Now foreigners cannot travel to Australia, and Australians cannot travel out of Australia.

Because of the strict restrictions, Australia's corona situation is good.

## Selkosanomat and LL-Bladet

The municipal elections are approaching. Municipal election day is 13 June.

There is a lot of information about the elections. For example, Selkosanomat, together with the magazine Leija and Yle, has asked questions to the parties.

The questions concern, for example, paid work for people with intellectual disabilities.

From Selkosanomat you can read what the parties answered. The answers can also be found in Clear Swedish on the LL-Bladet website.

Selkosanomat and LL-Bladet are clear-language newspapers, which can also be read online.

## Pasila link tower

![Yle's link tower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5343577)

Open image viewer

The Pasila link tower is 146 metres high. Photo: Seppo Sarkkinen / Yle

Yle has decided to keep the Pasila link tower in Helsinki.

TV broadcasts now work over fibre-optic connections. Therefore the link tower is not really needed any more. However, people know the link tower, which is part of the Helsinki landscape.

The link tower is 146 metres high. Its maintenance costs about 100,000 euros every year.

A lot of snow has fallen in Eastern Finland. For example, in Ilomantsi more than 20 centimetres of snow fell.

Because of the snowfall, driving conditions were poor on many roads on Friday.
