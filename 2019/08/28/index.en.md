## Porvoo shooting case

![Police cars in the yard of the Eestinmäki day-care centre early on Sunday morning, 25 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_264/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5857805d63904fa9b04/1566806170)

2 men shot at police in Porvoo at the weekend. Photo: Lehtikuva

The police demand that the district court hold 2 men who are connected with the shooting in Porvoo in custody. The police say that the men may have tried to murder police officers. The men first shot at police in Porvoo and then during a car chase.

The police say that the men may have fired several dozen shots at police.

The district court will hold a hearing tomorrow. At the hearing the district court will decide on custody.

2 police officers were injured in the case.

## Light cars may not come into traffic

![Light cars being test driven at the Auto 2018 event in Helsinki on 9 November 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10942463)

Open image viewer

Light cars look like ordinary cars. Their driving speed is limited. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Finland may have to change the law on light cars. The European Union does not accept light cars.

A light car is a car that looks like an ordinary car, but is driven more slowly than an ordinary car. A light car can be driven at a speed of 60 kilometres an hour.

A light car can be driven by a 15-year-old who does not yet have a proper driving licence.

But now it will probably happen that light cars do not come into traffic.

The EU thinks it is too dangerous for 15-year-olds to be able to drive a light car.

## Greta Thunberg in New York

Swedish climate activist Greta Thunberg has arrived in the USA, in New York.

She travelled for 2 weeks across the Atlantic by sailing boat, because she opposes flying.

16-year-old Greta is taking part in big climate meetings in the USA and Chile.

## Easy-to-read author Seija Niinistö-Samela received the Tietopöllö award

![A woman in front of a bookshelf leafing through a book](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10941857)

Open image viewer

Seija Niinistö-Samela writes non-fiction in easy-to-read language. Photo: Merja Windia / YLE

Easy-to-read author Seija Niinistö-Samela has received this year's Tietopöllö award.

The Tietopöllö award is given to an author who writes good non-fiction for children and young people.

Seija Niinistö-Samela has published 7 non-fiction books in easy-to-read language. She has written, for example, about dogs, robots, physics, energy and Finnish nature.
