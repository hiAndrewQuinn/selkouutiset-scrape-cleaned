# Monday 3.7.2017 (radio)

There has been a bad bus accident in Germany. Finland has made new trade agreements with China. Many young foreigners want to do voluntary work in Finland. Many municipalities are starting to organise employment services themselves. June was colder than usual.

## A bus accident in Germany

![A forensic investigation team worked at the accident scene on the A9 motorway on 3 July. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9703198)

There has been a bad bus accident in Germany. 18 people died in the accident. 30 people were injured.

The accident happened in Bavaria, in southern Germany. A tourist bus crashed into a lorry and caught fire. The bus burned completely.

All the bus passengers were German. Many of them were pensioners.

## Trade between Finland and China

![Prime Minister Juha Sipilä (left) met Chinese President Xi Jinping in Beijing on 26 June 2017. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9703182)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä met Chinese President Xi Jinping in Beijing in June. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Finland does a lot of trade with China. New trade agreements were made last week, when Prime Minister Juha Sipilä visited China. Leaders of Finnish companies were also on the trip.

The new agreements increase Finland's exports to China by as much as a billion euros. Important agreements were made, for example, in the energy sector. Finns will, for example, build a district heating network in China's capital Beijing.

## Foreigners in voluntary work

![Young people restoring the windows of the Pitsku culture church.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9702915)

Open image viewer

These young people are learning to repair old windows. Photo: Yle/Saija Nironen

This summer there are many young foreigners doing voluntary work in Finland. About 300 young people have come from abroad to do voluntary work in Finland.

Young people have come from many different countries in Europe, Asia and America. They take part in camps where, for example, buildings are repaired or events are organised. At the camp the young people get food and a place to stay.

Finland now interests many because of safety. Young people do not want to go, for example, to France, where there have been terrorist attacks. This is what the Allianssi organisation, which handles youth exchanges, says.

Finland also interests young people because of its clean nature and silence. Some young people also want to travel to the home country of the Moomins.

## Municipalities' employment services

![Job hunting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7705287)

Open image viewer

Municipalities want to help with looking for work. Photo: Laura Valta / Yle

Many municipalities are starting to organise employment services themselves. They help people find work or education. Now employment services are organised by TE Offices.

This is a trial with about 20 municipalities taking part. One of them is, for example, Turku, where new premises are being renovated for the city's own employment services.

In Turku, the city's own employment services are meant for people under 25 who have been unemployed for a long time.

## A cold June

![Long hair blowing in a strong wind.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9702161)

Open image viewer

At times June was like autumn. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

June was colder than usual. In the far north the average temperature was 8 degrees, in the south 13 degrees. The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that June is this cold only once in 10 years.
