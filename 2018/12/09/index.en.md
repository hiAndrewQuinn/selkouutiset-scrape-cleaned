# Sunday 9.12.2018 (radio)

In the future, a small speeding offence will bring smaller fines than now. 20 million people suffer from hunger in Yemen. The police arrested more than 1700 protesters in France. One person died in a fight at the Kerava station. White reindeer interest British people.

## Speeding fines

![Bicycle police in Turku write fines for traffic offences.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5283412)

In the future, a small speeding offence will bring smaller fines. Photo: YLE / Petra Ristola

In the future, a small speeding offence will bring smaller fines than now. The law changes in summer 2020.

After that, the fine will be 100 euros if a driver drives a little too fast in a 60 zone. If the speed limit is over 60, the fine is 70 euros.

Today the fine is at least 140 euros. A small, or minor, speeding offence is at most 10 kilometres per hour over the speed limit.

## Crisis in Yemen

![Yemenis walk past a building destroyed by an air strike.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10536866)

Open image viewer

There is a war in Yemen. Photo: Yahya Arhab / EPA

The people of Yemen do not have enough food. About 20 million Yemenis suffer from hunger.

There is a war in Yemen, in which 100,000 people have already died. In the war, the government of the country and the rebels are against each other. They are now negotiating for peace in Sweden.

Yemen is in the Middle East, on the Arabian Peninsula.

## Arrests in France

![Riot police arrest a protester in Paris.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10545835)

Open image viewer

Police arrested protesters in Paris on Saturday. Photo: Lucas Barioulet / AFP

The police have arrested protesters in France. The police arrested more than 1700 people.

On Saturday there were protests in many cities in France. In the capital, Paris, protesters set cars on fire and broke windows.

This was already the fourth weekend that there were protests in France. People are protesting against President Macron.

The protesters think that life in France is expensive. They got angry when Macron's government raised the petrol tax.

## Fight at Kerava

![Map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10545762)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics

Early on Sunday morning there was a mass fight at the Kerava railway station. Many people were involved in the fight.

One person died in the fight. The person was found at the scene of the fight. The victim was over 50 years old.

The police have arrested one man and one woman. The police suspect that they killed the victim.

The police say that such serious mass fights are not usual.

## White reindeer

![Pia Tuukkanen offers reindeer rides to her Airbnb guests through her own company.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10521475)

Tourists like reindeer. Photo: Yle

White reindeer are the new stars of Lapland. Many pictures of white reindeer have been published in the British media. They interest people, and that is a good thing for tourism in Lapland.

Researcher Jouko Kumpula says that white reindeer are actually lazy. They are calmer and sleepier than reindeer of the usual colour.

However, the tourist business thanks the white reindeer. They are rare, and that is why they interest tourists.
