## Cut in electricity tax

![Electric heater.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8635843)

Open image viewer

Electricity tax has a big effect if a house has electric heating. Photo: Jyrki Utriainen / Yle

The price of electricity is falling, because electricity tax is being cut.

Normally, 24 percent tax must be paid on electricity. This winter the electricity tax is only 10 percent.

For example, a resident of a detached house saves about 100 euros a month if the house has electric heating.

The low electricity tax is in force from the start of December to the end of April.

This is how the state wants to help people who have difficulty paying electricity bills.

Russia's war in Ukraine has raised the price of electricity across Europe.

## Road lights

![Snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10010921)

Open image viewer

Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

Some of the road lights may have to be switched off, if electricity runs short in winter.

If electricity has to be saved, some of the road lights can be switched off between 7 and 9 in the morning and between 16 and 19 in the evening.

Electricity is needed most in the morning, when people wake up. Electricity is also used a lot in the evening, when people come home from work. That is why electricity may run short at these times.

Road lights will not be switched off in areas with a lot of traffic. The lights are also meant to stay on at junctions and in the morning near schools.

## December weather forecast

![Lahti city library in a winter landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10114379)

Open image viewer

There is frost in December this year. Photo: Emilia Malin / Yle

December may be a little colder than usual. This is what Yle's meteorologist Elias Paakkanen forecasts.

In mid-December it may be 3 to 6 degrees colder than usual. That means the daytime temperature will be below freezing also in southern Finland.

Also during Christmas week there may be a little frost, and the snow may stay on the ground in the south too. Now in southern Finland there is 1 to 10 centimetres of snow.
