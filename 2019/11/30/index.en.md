# Saturday 30.11.2019 (radio)

The party leaders of the government parties will discuss the Posti mess in the coming days. The Winter War began 80 years ago. Sexual minorities experience violence. The Tokyo Olympic stadium is ready.

The party leaders of the government parties will discuss the Posti mess in the coming days. SDP's Sirpa Paatero, Minister for State Ownership Steering, resigned from the government yesterday. The state owns Posti.

Prime Minister Antti Rinne has promised to give the government partners an account of what has happened.

Besides the SDP, the government includes the Centre Party, the Greens, the Left Alliance and the Swedish People's Party (RKP).

## Winter War began 80 years ago

![Ski patrol on Märkäjärvi lake in February 1940.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11091750)

Open image viewer

A Winter War ski patrol at Märkäjärvi. During and after the Winter War, about 430,000 Finns lost their homes and had to evacuate. Photo: SA-kuva

Today, Saturday, it is 80 years since the Winter War broke out.

The war broke out when the Soviet Union attacked Finland early in the morning of 30 November 1939. The Red Army bombed 16 places. Among them were Helsinki, Viipuri, Turku and Lahti, for example.

Researchers say that Finns were better at fighting in winter than Russians were. Finns also knew the terrain well.

However, Finland lost 11 per cent of its land area in the war, as well as Viipuri. Viipuri was Finland's second-largest city.

More than 26,600 Finns who were engaged in military operations died in the Winter War. In addition, about 1,000 civilians died.

The Finnish state leadership has honoured the anniversary of the Winter War. A wreath was laid at the Winter War National Memorial. The memorial is in Kasarmintori in Helsinki.

105 candles were lit at the memorial: one for each day of the war.

## Sexual orientation exposes people to violence

![School health survey 2019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11091503)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

One in 4 lower secondary school pupils who belong to a sexual or gender minority experiences physical violence at home.

Over 40 per cent of minorities also experience psychological violence at home.

23 per cent experienced bullying at school. Among other people of the same age, 6 per cent experienced bullying at school.

The figures come from the school health survey for lower secondary school pupils. It asked for the first time about minorities' experiences of violence and bullying.

## Tokyo's new Olympic stadium

![Tokyo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11096163)

Open image viewer

The new stadium is being prepared for very hot weather. Photo: AFP / Lehtikuva

Construction of the Tokyo Olympics is in the final stretch.

The Olympics will be held in Tokyo, Japan, next summer. The builders say that the central stadium is now ready. The stadium can hold 60,000 spectators.

The new National Stadium will host the opening and closing ceremonies, athletics competitions and football matches.

Building the stadium cost about 1.27 billion euros.

The design of the stadium takes the fierce summer heat into account. The stadium roof is designed so that wind can get inside the stadium.

The stadium also has 185 electric fans.
