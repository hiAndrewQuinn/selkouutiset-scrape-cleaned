## Drug deaths are being investigated

The number of drug deaths has grown strongly in Finland in recent years. Every year children under age also die from drugs. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The Safety Investigation Authority is starting to investigate drug deaths among young people.

206 people died of overdoses the year before last. 76 of them were under 25. That is the highest proportion in Europe.

The investigation includes accidental drug deaths. Violent deaths are excluded from the investigation.

The investigation does not deal with questions of guilt, for example. The aim is to find recommendations with which drug deaths can be prevented.

The study includes, for example, the police, the Youth Research Society and the universities of Helsinki, Tampere and Turku.

## Thai pickers

![Thai berry pickers worked in Kärsämäki in September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11865275)

Open image viewer

The information on the exploitation of the pickers is based on a recording that MOT has obtained. In it, Finnish berry industry players and Thai authorities go through the 2020 picking season. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

New information has been obtained about the treatment of Thai berry pickers. Finnish authorities found out about the exploitation of the pickers already 2 years ago, says Yle's MOT programme.

Thai authorities said that the berry company Polarica paid the pickers too little wages. In addition, the company charged the pickers an unreasonable amount of costs. MOT's enquiry says that Polarica's activities were nevertheless not intervened in.

At present the police and the Border Guard are investigating 2 berry companies in Finland. In addition, a Thai company that sent pickers to Finland is under investigation. It has also sent pickers to the berry company Kiantama.

## A warm year

![Family enjoying the warmth.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5987,h_3362,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99415162f8ec36311ff/1660480855)

Warm weather attracted swimmers into the water in August 2022. Photo: Ext-hans-peter Dhuy / Yle

Last year was warmer than usual, says the Finnish Meteorological Institute's statistics.

Finland's average temperature was 0.9 degrees higher than the 1991–2020 average. The last time a year was warmer than this was in 2020.

August was record-breakingly warm in part of the country.

The highest temperature of the year was 32.9 degrees. It was measured at Pori airport in Satakunta on 28 June. The lowest temperature of the year, -35.7 degrees, was measured on 8 January at Enontekiö airport in Lapland.

## Saving electricity

![Electricity consumption, extension lead.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10805411)

Open image viewer

Everyone should save electricity. It affects one's own electricity bill and ensures there is enough electricity everywhere in Finland. Photo: AOP

In Finland 10 percent less electricity was consumed in December than in December 2021. The figure takes into account the effect of temperature on consumption, says the grid company Fingrid.

Fingrid estimates that saving electricity has had a significant effect on there being enough electricity in Finland.

The saving measures were especially significant in the third week of December, when there was a severe cold spell in Finland.
