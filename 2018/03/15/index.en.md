# Thursday 15.3. 2018 (radio)

The icebreaker strike is called off. Bottled water contains microplastics. The maternity package is being renewed. There is a lot of snow on roofs in eastern Finland. VR made a record profit. Ford continues testing cars in Finland. A wolf was moving around in Vaasa at night.

## There will be no icebreaker strike

![The icebreaker Urho in harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10100693)

Open image viewer

Photo: Risto Koskinen / Yle

The icebreaker strike is called off.

The ships that break the ice on the surface of the water, that is icebreakers and multipurpose icebreakers, will not go on strike, because the dispute about the work has been settled.

The sides in the labour dispute have accepted the settlement proposal given by National Conciliator Minna Helle.

Plastic particles are being studied

![water bottles](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9958684)

Open image viewer

Small particles of plastic have been found in bottled water. An international study reports this. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

The World Health Organization is studying the health effects of small plastic particles, or microplastics.

Microplastics are very small particles of plastic that you cannot see with the naked eye. The World Health Organization is studying microplastics because they have been found in bottled water.

It is not yet known whether microplastics affect people. That is why the matter is being studied.

A new maternity package

![New items in the maternity package.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10116741)

Open image viewer

The maternity package now also has a plate, spoons, felt slippers and mitten holders for the baby. Photo: Petra Ristola / Yle

The maternity package is being renewed.

The maternity packages now have more things than before. New baby items include, for example, felt slippers, a plate and spoons.

Kela will start handing out the new packages in May. The mother can choose whether she takes the package or a cash grant.

Maternity packages have been given to mothers and babies for 80 years.

A lot of snow on roofs

![The larger than usual amount of snow in Lapland can also be seen on roofs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10012053)

Open image viewer

There is a lot of snow on the roofs of houses. Photo: Raimo Torikka / Yle

In the eastern part of Finland there is a lot of snow on the roofs of houses.

In the North Karelia area there is more snow than normal on roofs. The roofs of big hall buildings may not hold if there is too much snow. Owners of big halls should remove snow from the roofs.

At least one roof has already collapsed because of the heavy snow.

The roofs of detached houses hold the weight of the snow better.

VR improved its profit

![a train at a station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9531250)

Open image viewer

VR says that train travel has increased. Photo: Varpu Helpinen / Yle

The railway company VR has made a better profit than ever before.

The company's operating profit more than doubled from the year before and was now more than 110 million euros.

Train travel increased by 8 per cent. Last year people made about 127 million journeys.

Ford tests cars in Finland

![The picture shows the logo of carmaker Ford. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9542776)

Carmaker Ford continues testing cars in Finland.

The cars are tested in Rovaniemi, in the village of Sonka.

This winter almost 200 cars are being tested. Different car fuels are also being studied.

A wolf seen in Vaasa

![Wolf](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8913022)

Open image viewer

The wolf in the picture lives in Ranua Wildlife Park. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

A wolf was moving around in the centre of Vaasa at night.

The police say that the wolf walked in the centre of the city of Vaasa at two o'clock last night.

Several people saw the wolf.

A police patrol went and chased the wolf further away from the centre.
