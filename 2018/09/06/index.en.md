## The Greens' support has fallen the most

There are only small differences in the support for the big parties.

Yle's new survey says the 3 biggest parties are within 2.5 percentage points of each other. The support of the opposition party SDP has fallen slightly and is now 20.3 percent. The National Coalition has also dropped. Its support is 19 percent. The Centre's support is 17.8 percent.

Tuomo Turja, research director at Taloustutkimus, says that the news is the Greens' support, which is 12.6 percent. The party chair, Touko Aalto, was chosen last June. At that time the support was over 15 percent, and last autumn it was almost 18 percent.

## Young Muslims' identity has been studied in Finland

![Crescent moon symbol on the roof of a mosque.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10003582)

Open image viewer

Photo: Michael Reynolds / EPA

Young Muslims do not think Islam is part of Finnishness. This is what researcher Teemu Pauha says.

Pauha has studied the identity of young Muslims in Finland. Pauha says that young Muslims think Christianity is part of Finnishness. That is why young Muslims feel in Finland that they are foreigners.

Abroad, Finland's young Muslims often feel that they are Finns. They say that Finnishness also includes, for example, honesty and reliability. In young people's opinion, these unite Finnishness and Islam.

Finland's young Muslims take a critical attitude towards Islamic customs that exist only in their parents' home countries.

## Tobacco smoke is dangerous during pregnancy

![Tobacco burning between fingers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10110886)

Open image viewer

Tobacco smoke is especially harmful to pregnant women. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Tobacco smoke is dangerous during pregnancy. The father should also consider quitting smoking when the mother of the family is expecting a baby.

A new study says that a smoke-free environment is important during pregnancy. If there is tobacco smoke in the home, a woman can have a miscarriage and lose the baby.

The researchers compared smoke-free families with families where the man smokes. Miscarriages were 17 percent more common if the man smoked and the woman had to breathe tobacco smoke. The study was done in China.

## Patients soon get into the new children's hospital

![New children's hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10354396)

Open image viewer

The new children's hospital will open in stages during September. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

The opening of the new children's hospital has been celebrated in Meilahti, Helsinki. Patients can get treatment at the hospital during September.

The hospital treats patients from all over Finland. Many demanding illnesses, such as organ transplants and heart surgery, will be treated in the new children's hospital.

Construction of the hospital began in 2014. A public fundraising drive raised almost 40 million euros. Construction cost about 170 million in total.
