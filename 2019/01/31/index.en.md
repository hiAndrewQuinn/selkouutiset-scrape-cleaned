# Thursday 31.1.2019 (radio)

The government parties disagree about elderly care. Fraudsters posed as police. Children and young people move too little. Finns play digital games. The weather is getting colder.

## The government parties disagree

![A carer holds an elderly person by the hand](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10623774)

Open image viewer

Care homes have a recommendation on how many carers there must be. The recommendation does not have to be followed. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

In Finland, a lot has recently been said about the poor care of the elderly. The elderly have been poorly cared for in care homes because there are too few carers.

The government parties in Finland disagree about how to try to improve elderly care. The Centre and the Blues say that there must be a law that sets out how many carers there must be. The National Coalition does not think a law is needed.

At the moment care homes only have a recommendation on how many carers there must be.

## Fraudsters go to prison

![Police warning sticker.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10034545)

Open image viewer

The police remind people that you do not need to tell your online banking codes to anyone. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

4 men will go to prison because they posed as police officers.

The men looked for phone numbers from directory enquiries for people whose first names sounded old. Then they called the people and said that a police officer would call. The men got the online banking codes and PINs from the elderly people. They took money from the elderly people's bank accounts.

The men got over 800,000 euros in money.

The police remind people that a real police officer never calls and asks for online banking codes.

## Children and young people need to move more

![FC Kuusysi P9 team training in Lahti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10620988)

Open image viewer

Small children move a lot. Teenagers no longer move as much. Photo: Lauri Rautavuori / Yle

Children and young people move too little, a study shows.

In the study, 3,000 children had a meter that measured how much they moved. Only a third of children and young people moved briskly for at least an hour every day.

The results of the study also showed that exercise is no longer as important to children and young people. Experts say that this is worrying.

Children and young people should move briskly for at least one hour every day.

## Finns play on their mobile phones

![A man and a woman tapping on their mobile phones.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10598277)

Open image viewer

Finns of all ages play on their mobile phones. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

Finns play quite a lot of digital games, for example on their mobile phones.

40 percent of Finns play at least sometimes.

Teenage boys play the most. Half of adult men play games, and a third of women.

## The weather is getting colder

![Winter morning, winter, frost, sun, hoarfrost, weather](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10621996)

Open image viewer

Even more snow is falling in Southern and Western Finland. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

The weather is getting colder again. Tomorrow, Friday, snow will fall in Southern and Western Finland.

There is frost across the whole country. In Southern Finland the frost is about 10 degrees, and in Northern Finland more than 20 degrees.
