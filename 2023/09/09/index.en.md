## Earthquake in Morocco

![Debris of a damaged house is being cleared in Marrakesh.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_429/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116983364fc52715ab37/1694330031)

Many buildings collapsed in the earthquake. Photo: Jalal Morchidi / EPA

A disastrous earthquake has happened in Morocco. The earthquake happened on Friday evening in the central parts of Morocco.

A lot of people have died in the earthquake. The Moroccan authorities said on Saturday afternoon that over 1000 people had died in the earthquake and over 1200 had been injured.

Experts say that the number of dead may rise a lot, when more information is received from the earthquake area.

The centre of the Moroccan earthquake was 70 kilometres from the city of Marrakesh. The earthquake was the strongest in the area in 120 years.

Morocco can ask for help through the EU.

Prime Minister **Petteri Orpo** has expressed condolences to the victims and relatives of the Morocco earthquake.

The president **Sauli Niinistö** has also sent condolences to the king of Morocco for the victims of the earthquake.

## Sexual offences

![Two hands holding each other. In the background a couple in a bed can be seen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1799,h_1007,x_0,y_150/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115228764cd43adc900f/1691173880)

Sex must be voluntary. Photo: Aleksandr Davydov / Mostphotos

Reports of sexual offences made by Finns have increased. At the beginning of the year a new law on sexual offences came into force.

For example, rape was defined more strictly than before. It is possible that because of this, reports of sexual offences have also increased.

The new law says that sex must be voluntary. A person commits rape if they force another person into sex. For the police the situation is sometimes difficult. The police say that from people's statements it is often hard to find out whether sex was voluntary.

## Coffee company Meira

![Kultakatriina packages on a conveyor belt at the Meira factory in Vallila.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6044,h_3399,x_0,y_269/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90025761dc3f4b1a144/1641825815)

Open image viewer

Kultakatriina is Meira's best-known coffee brand. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Coffee company Meira is starting change negotiations. Meira wants to lay off all employees for 1–2 weeks. Meira has 180 employees.

Meira's director **Raimo Sinisalo** says that the problem is especially the price of coffee in shops. Shops often want to sell coffee on offer. Then the coffee must be cheaper than normal.

Meira says that the economic situation at the end of the year is uncertain, because sales on offer have increased.
