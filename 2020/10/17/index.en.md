# Saturday 17.10.2020 (radio)

In Finland people are talking about the coronavirus risk in sport. A terrorist murder has taken place in France. Homeless Night is held on Saturday. The first snow has fallen in Lapland. The new Road Traffic Act does not give dates for changing tyres.

## Sports' coronavirus risks

![Break in play on a floorball court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10666188)

Open image viewer

In team sports, players are close to each other. Photo: Yle

Authorities will say next week in more detail which sports carry a high coronavirus risk.

On Thursday the government recommended that, for example, team sports be stopped in areas where the coronavirus epidemic is accelerating. Sports organisations do not understand why ball games, for example, are dangerous in the coronavirus era.

Sport and the coronavirus are being talked about a lot now. On Friday it was reported that the famous ice hockey team Oulun Kärpät is going into quarantine. One player on the team has a coronavirus infection. Kärpät's next games will be played later.

## The terror attack in France

![French police in front of the Bois d'Aulne school on 17 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11600230)

Open image viewer

A man murdered a teacher near the school. Photo: Yoan Valat / EPA

A terrorist murder has taken place in France.

An 18-year-old man murdered a teacher near a school. The man also cut off the teacher's head.

The teacher was a history teacher. In class he showed cartoons of the prophet Muhammad.

The murderer was killed when police shot him. Police have also detained other people who may be connected to the murder.

The murder has shocked the French. President Emmanuel Macron says that the murder was an Islamist terror attack.

## Homeless Night

![Homeless Night, Yle TV1](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-3531056168b4caaa20/1444318030)

Open image viewer

The Homeless Night event reminds us that not everyone has a home.

Homeless Night is held on Saturday. Homeless people do not have a home.

At the end of last year there were about 4,600 homeless people living alone in Finland. In addition, about 260 families had no home of their own.

Most homeless people live in the Helsinki area. That is why food and health advice is being provided for homeless people in Helsinki on Saturday.

Homeless Night is held every year.

During the night between Sunday and Monday, it may also snow in the south. Otherwise, rain or sleet falls over most of the country.

## Winter tyres

![Studded tyres are usually inflated a little extra in anticipation of frost.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10461198)

Open image viewer

The weather decides when winter tyres are needed. Photo: Raimo Torikka / Yle

Drivers can change their car to winter tyres when the weather requires it.

The new Road Traffic Act no longer gives dates for changing tyres. Studded tyres can be used whenever they suit the weather.

The actual period of use for studded tyres is from the beginning of November to the end of March. But a driver does not need to change to winter tyres if the weather and road conditions stay good.

The new Road Traffic Act came into force in June.
