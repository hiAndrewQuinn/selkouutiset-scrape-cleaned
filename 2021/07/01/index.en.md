## Corona infections in Finland

![A man in a yellow protective suit and respirator sprays disinfectant from a device on his back onto the station platform. In the background are trains painted grey and red, and a man in a yellow high-visibility vest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3938,h_2215,x_0,y_240/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82606760d9bf475e803/1624913982)

THL says that it is not a good idea to travel to Russia now. The picture shows a train station being cleaned in Moscow. Photo: Anton Novoderezhkin / AOP

The number of new coronavirus infections has grown in Finland. Coronavirus infections have come to Finland from St. Petersburg, Russia.

Many Finns caught coronavirus when they were in St. Petersburg watching the football European Championship matches. Already about 400 Finns have got corona because of the trip to St. Petersburg.

Many of the travellers' coronavirus infections are of the delta variant of the coronavirus. The delta variant spreads easily. At least 50 Finns have already got coronavirus infection from a person who has been in St. Petersburg.

The National Institute for Health and Welfare now hopes that Finns will not travel to Russia.

## Changes to the client fees act

![Rollator and chairs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8166547)

Open image viewer

In assisted living, older people get a lot of care and help. Photo: Yle

The client fees act has changed. There were changes to the client fees act on 1 July.

The changes affect, for example, older people's fees in assisted living. Assisted living suits older people who need a lot of help and care.

Until now the fees for assisted living have been different in different municipalities. Now the law sets the fees more clearly, and the differences between municipalities end.

## Cash

![Money, banknotes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11499084)

Open image viewer

Cash is now used less. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finns use cash less than before. The use of cash, that is, banknotes and coins, has decreased quickly. This is what a survey by the Finance Sector says.

Less than 10 percent of those who answered the survey said that they usually pay for their daily purchases in cash. Most Finns pay by card.

## Finnish weather

The weekend will be very warm. Photo: Yle

June was also unusually warm in Finland. Never before has a June this warm been measured as this year.

Sunny and warm weather also continues in the coming days. The heat limit, that is, 25 degrees, is exceeded in almost the whole country. At the weekend it is even up to 30 degrees.
