# Saturday 27.6.2020 (radio)

Complaints were made about student admissions. Thunderstorms caused damage. The berry harvest may be good. There are few new corona infections. The weather is warm on Sunday.

The TV Clear News has a summer break and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Complaints about student admissions

![Students in a university library.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11408285)

Open image viewer

Students in the university library. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Not all Finns are satisfied with student admissions to higher education this spring.

Because of the exceptional circumstances, higher education institutions and universities selected many new students based on their certificates. The institutions could not hold normal entrance exams because of corona restrictions.

About 100 complaints about student admissions have been made to the Parliamentary Ombudsman. The Ombudsman is investigating whether the higher education institutions and universities acted according to the law when they selected many students based on certificates.

## Thunderstorm gusts knocked down trees

![Trees fallen on a power line.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_788,h_443,x_0,y_261/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6935455ef5a47d20132/1593156795)

A thunderstorm knocked trees onto power lines. The picture is from Uimaharju's Ukkola in Joensuu. Photo: PKS Sähkönsiirto Oy

There have been strong thunder gusts, that is, small storms, in Eastern Finland. The thunder gusts began on Friday evening.

The wind gusts have knocked down trees and caused power cuts. Electricity companies have been repairing power lines in Eastern Finland on Saturday. Trees also fell on some summer cottages in Joensuu. Lightning from the thunder set some buildings on fire.

On Saturday the thunder gusts have moved a little south from Eastern Finland.

## Forest berries are flowering well

![Bog bilberry (hilla).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8931505)

Open image viewer

Bog bilberry, also called hilla. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The berry harvest may be good. Many forest berries are flowering a lot now. Forest berries are flowering well, even though there was no snow in Southern Finland in the winter. For example, the bilberry harvest may be small if snow does not protect the bilberry plants from frost.

The Natural Resources Institute reports that the bilberry has survived the winter fairly well. Lingonberries are flowering a lot, and bog bilberries (hilla) are flowering very much.

Warm weather helps the flowers of the berries to be pollinated, but drought may make the berries smaller.

The number of new corona infections has stayed small in Finland.

On Saturday 7 new corona infections were reported in Finland. In the past week, that is in 7 days, 56 new corona infections have been reported.

In total about 7,200 corona infections have been reported in Finland.

## Weather

On Sunday the weather is dry over a large area, that is, it does not rain. The sun also shines.

In the southern part of the country there may be a few showers.

The daytime temperature is between 25 and 30 degrees. In the north the temperature is between 16 and 22 degrees.
