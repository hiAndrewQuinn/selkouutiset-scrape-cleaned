## Drone attack on Russia

![Smoke rises from Pskov airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2200,h_1237,x_0,y_299/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116467964ef20501cda1/1693408359)

Smoke rises from Pskov airport after the drone attack. Photo: Pskov regional governor's office

A drone attack has been carried out on Russia. The attack is the biggest drone attack on Russia during the whole war in Ukraine.

The attack was made on Pskov airport. It is near the Estonian border.

Russia says that in the attack, 4 transport planes were destroyed or damaged. The Belarusian news channel Nexta, however, says that in the attack a total of 7 planes were destroyed or damaged.

Ukraine has not commented on the attacks in any way.

## Wagner leader was buried

![A picture taken from high up of the cemetery where Prigozhin is buried. A guard in the middle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5359,h_3014,x_0,y_430/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116443564eef1d28db69/1693381198)

There were many police officers and soldiers at the Porokhovskoye cemetery in St Petersburg when Jevgeni Prigožin was buried. Photo: Olga Maltseva / AFP

The head of the mercenary company Wagner, Jevgeni Prigožin, has been buried. He was buried on Tuesday in a private ceremony in St Petersburg.

Prigožin died last week in the plane crash.

It has been suspected that Russian president Vladimir Putin is behind the crash, since Wagner troops led by Prigožin rebelled against Russia's military leadership at midsummer.

## Abortion becomes easier

![A hand holds a one-button and a four-button medicine blister pack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_356/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116033964e48aa2d18fa/1693482026)

Open image viewer

Most terminations of pregnancy in Finland are done with medicines. Photo: Retu Liikanen / Yle

The new abortion law takes effect in Finland in autumn.

After that, a pregnancy termination no longer needs to be justified if the pregnancy has lasted less than 12 weeks.

The change speeds up access to treatment. It also increases a woman's right to decide about her own body.

In Finland, about 7000 abortions are carried out each year. Most of them are done with medicines.

![Tays Emergency Acuta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3648,h_2052,x_0,y_508/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105299563a97de8f2314/1672052824)

Young people think that health care is one of the most interesting professional fields. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

A new study has asked young people what they think about different professional fields.

According to young people, the most interesting professional fields are health care, tourism and restaurant business, and the arts. Least interesting to young people are church work, cleaning and public administration.

According to the study, young people's interest in different fields is influenced by values, gender and parents' educational background.

More than 6000 young people answered the survey for the youth future report this spring

## Expansion of the National Museum

![Tower of the National Museum.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114202064afd27d624c3/1729852621)

Open image viewer

The National Museum gets an extension. Photo: Sonja Sajavaara / Yle

A major renovation will begin next month at the National Museum in Helsinki.

The National Museum's main building, which is over a hundred years old, will be thoroughly renovated. An extension will also be built for the museum. About 90 percent of the new premises will be built underground.

The National Museum will be closed in mid-October. The construction work will last 3 years.
