# Sunday 12.7.2020 (radio)

Finland's economy is slowly improving. The police are investigating the senior residence fire. Slot machines are opening again. Bicycle sales continue to be busy. The weather.

The TV Clear News has a summer break. It will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Finland's economy is slowly improving

![Cargo ships at the Vuosaari harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5286,h_2973,x_0,y_363/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6622735e8c38133414c/1586247883)

Finland's economy is slowly recovering from the corona situation. Economic experts say that Finland's economy is recovering more slowly than the economies of other European countries.

Many Finns are now saving money and not buying many goods or services. Many companies are also saving money and not buying new machines, for example, or developing new products.

When this happens, the economy does not grow, and unemployment may increase.

## Police investigate the senior residence fire

![Senior housing block damaged by fire, aerial view in Jyväskylä on 11 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11444723)

Open image viewer

The senior residence and the neighbouring building were badly damaged in the fire. Photo: Hannu Rainamo / Lehtikuva

The police have started to investigate the fire at the senior residence in Jyväskylä. The police are examining the fire site and talking with the residents.

The senior residence and another apartment block next to it burned on Friday evening. The police are investigating in particular why the fire spread so quickly to the other building.

The police say that the fire at the Jyväskylä senior residence may have started by accident.
The investigation of the fire may take many months.

## Slot machines open again

![Slot machines in the supermarket floor of Iso Omena in Espoo, which were out of use on 24 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11312452)

Open image viewer

Slot machines were closed in March. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Slot machines will open after the corona break next Wednesday. Slot machines are, for example, in shops and petrol stations. Slot machines have been closed all spring because of the corona situation.

However, the number of slot machines will be reduced this year. The gambling company Veikkaus is removing about 8,000 slot machines. Slot machines are being reduced so that gambling harms decrease.

Many people feel that they are forced to play slot machines. That is why slot machines are being reduced.

## Bicycle trade continues to be busy

![Bicycles for sale.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7177828)

Open image viewer

Many more bicycles have been sold this year than before. Photo: Mikko Tuomikoski / Yle

Bicycle trade continues to be busy. In June almost 70% more bicycles were sold than in June last year. Before June, in the spring, 36% more bicycles were sold than in the spring of last year.

Cycling has increased a lot now, because many hobbies ended in the spring because of the corona situation.

Cycling has grown in popularity in earlier years too, but this year it has grown a great deal.
