# Monday 8.7.2019 (radio)

Power has changed hands in Greece. The Conscripts' Union proposes that women should also do military service. The summer fruit harvest will be good. The Kaustinen folk music festival has started.

## New Democracy won the Greek election

![Kyriakos Mitsotakis](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10864855)

The centre-right New Democracy party of Greece returned to power in Sunday's election, led by Kyriakos Mitsotakis. Photo: Orestis Panagiotou / EPA

Power has changed hands in Greece. Greeks voted in the parliamentary election on Sunday. The centre-right New Democracy party won about 40 percent of the votes.

The party will get 158 of the 300 seats in parliament. The party is returning to power.

The Greek government is currently led by the left-wing Syriza party. Syriza lost and got a little over 30 percent of the votes.

Prime Minister Alexis Tsipras has congratulated Kyriakos Mitsotakis, who became the new prime minister.

New Democracy has promised to cut Greeks' taxes. Many Greeks are tired of having less money than before because of taxes and government savings.

Greece's economic situation has been bad for a long time. Greece's problem is that the state has a very large amount of debt.

## The Conscripts' Union proposes military service for women too

![Recruit Meri Nuutinen, a woman, prepares for military service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10867550)

Open image viewer

Recruit Meri Nuutinen prepares for military service. Photo: Antti-Jussi Korhonen / Yle

The Conscripts' Union proposes that women should also do military service. Women's military service is currently voluntary in Finland.

The Conscripts' Union also suggests that physical and psychological tests be carried out already at the call-up assessments. The Conscripts' Union says that recruits should be taken from the whole age group, regardless of gender.

The union's chair Matias Pajula says that military service is unfair in many people's opinion.

New recruits started their service in the army today. There are more than 12,000 new recruits. Almost 10,000 start in the Army and about 1,700 in the Navy.

The service starts with an 8-week basic training.

## The plum harvest will be a record

![A plum tree with plenty of unripe fruit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10857831)

Open image viewer

The plum harvest will be a record-breaking large one. Photo: Simo Tommola

The summer fruit harvest will be good.

Plums in particular will be record-breakingly plentiful. That is why growers should remove up to half of the unripe plums from the trees. The danger is that plum trees use too much energy to grow the fruit. Then they may not survive next winter. Branches may also break.

The apple harvest will be average. Unripe apples should also be removed from the branches.

## Folk music sounds again in Kaustinen

![Audience filling the arena in Kaustinen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10306532)

Open image viewer

Folk music sounds again in Kaustinen. Photo: Raila Paavola / Yle

The Kaustinen Folk Music Festival has started in Central Ostrobothnia.

During the week there are 1,600 concerts and about 4,000 performers. This year too, both domestic and foreign top artists will be seen on stage.

The summer festival has 3 themes: internationalism, rhythm and the Savo region.

A lot of visitors are expected at the Kaustinen Folk Music Festival this year. Last year the target was 47,000 visitors, and it was reached.
