# Wednesday 17.3.2021 (radio)

Helsinki region commuter trains will not run on Thursday and Friday. Mandatory coronavirus tests will begin at Helsinki's ports and at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. The EU Commission proposes a digital coronavirus certificate. 3 new coronavirus deaths have been reported in Finland. Finns are satisfied with Prime Minister Sanna Marin. There will be sunny frosty days at the end of the week.

Helsinki region commuter trains will not run on Thursday and Friday. The reason is that the train drivers will not come to work.

The train drivers think that VR is breaking agreements. That is why they are stopping work for 2 days.

The train drivers will return to work on Saturday.

## Mandatory coronavirus tests for passengers

![Signs reminding people about coronavirus preparedness and safe distances at a border control point in Helsinki's West Harbour on 23 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11832992)

Open image viewer

In Helsinki's ports, passengers must take a coronavirus test when they arrive in Finland. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Mandatory coronavirus tests will begin at Helsinki's ports and at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. The coronavirus tests apply to all passengers who come to Finland.

However, a passenger does not have to go for a coronavirus test if they have a certificate of a negative test result or that they have had the coronavirus.

Mandatory coronavirus tests begin tomorrow, Thursday, and will continue until the end of March.

## EU plans a coronavirus certificate

![Close-up of Ursula von der Leyen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1972,h_1109,x_0,y_99/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7831296048f12310682/1615394884)

Open image viewer

EU Commission President Ursula von der Leyen spoke about the digital coronavirus certificate. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

Matters of travel during the coronavirus time are also being considered in the European Union.

The EU Commission proposes a digital coronavirus certificate for Europe. The digital coronavirus certificate is meant to make travel between EU countries easier.

The digital coronavirus certificate says that a person does not spread the coronavirus. A person who has had a coronavirus vaccination, or a negative test result, or who has had the coronavirus can get the certificate.

The digital coronavirus certificate will be voluntary and free. It will come into use perhaps in June.

3 new coronavirus deaths have been reported in Finland. In total, 804 people have now died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

On Wednesday it was also reported that there are 842 new coronavirus infections. About 69,000 coronavirus infections have been found in Finland in total.

Coronavirus vaccinations are progressing. More than 11 percent of Finns have already received at least one coronavirus vaccine.
