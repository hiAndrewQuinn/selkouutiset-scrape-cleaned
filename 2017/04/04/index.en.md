# Tuesday 4.4.2017 (radio)

A young man is suspected of the Russian metro attack. Police and demonstrators clashed over the deportation flights to Afghanistan. Parliament argued about the social and health care reform. NHL ice hockey players will not be seen at the Winter Olympics.

## The terror investigation is progressing in Russia

![People laid flowers at Sennaya metro station in St. Petersburg on 4 April. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9545554)

In Russia, yesterday's terror attack on the metro in St. Petersburg is being investigated. A 22-year-old man is suspected of the attack. He comes from Kyrgyzstan, but is a Russian citizen. The man himself died in the explosion. The man was perhaps helped by a young woman.

Russian media say that both perpetrators perhaps have links to extremist Islam. Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov thinks that the terror attack is not connected to Russia's military actions in Syria.

In total, 14 people died in the metro explosion. In addition, almost 50 people are in hospital.

## Deportations to Afghanistan were strongly opposed

![People in front of the Pasila police building. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9545534)

Open image viewer

The demonstrators blocked the police cars from moving.  Photo: Yle

The police are investigating a clash between demonstrators and the police in Pasila, Helsinki. Almost 50 people opposed the deportations of asylum seekers to Afghanistan. The people tried to stop police cars from getting out of the garage of the Pasila police building. The police had to use force to move the people out of the way of the cars. Later a demonstration was also held at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. There everything went peacefully.

The police want to correct false information about the deportations. The police say that only adults were sent back to Afghanistan and none of the people was pregnant. There were 10 people on the flight and the flight went peacefully.

The demonstrators think that it is not safe to send people back to Afghanistan.

## The reform of social and health services is under way in Parliament

![Juha Rehula](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9545842)

Open image viewer

Minister of Family Affairs and Social Services Juha Rehula Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Parliament has discussed the big reform of social and health services, that is, the sote reform. There is a dispute between the government and the opposition about freedom of choice. Freedom of choice means that everyone can choose which place they go to for treatment. The opposition says that the reform does not improve doctor services and only brings money to big health companies.

Minister of Family Affairs and Social Services Juha Rehula promised in Parliament that the sote reform will be taken forward calmly.

Parliament votes on confidence in the government on Wednesday.

## NHL ice hockey stars will not be seen at the Winter Olympics

![Patrik Laine's Winnipeg plays against New Jersey.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9510471)

Open image viewer

The new top star Patrik Laine will not be seen at the Winter Olympics.  Photo: AOP

The world's best ice hockey players will not be seen at next year's Winter Olympics in South Korea. The North American professional league NHL says that it does not want to take a break in the middle of the season and let players go to the games. The decision means that only ice hockey players who play in Europe will be seen at the Winter Olympics. Many NHL star players think the decision is bad.

Finland has many players in the NHL. Finland has usually done well when Finnish NHL players have been in the team.
