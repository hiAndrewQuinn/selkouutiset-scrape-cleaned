# Friday 18.10.2019 (radio)

A big demonstration has been held in Catalonia. In Northern Syria, an attempt is being made at a ceasefire. A record-long flight will be made at the weekend. Topping up HSL travel cards will soon also be possible online.

## Demonstration in Catalonia

![Demonstrators in Barcelona.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11027126)

Open image viewer

Barcelona was full of demonstrators on Friday. Photo: Alejandro Garcia / EPA

A big demonstration has been held in Catalonia. Catalans were also on strike on Friday and did not go to work.

The demonstration was held in Barcelona, the capital of Catalonia. Hundreds of thousands of people took part in the demonstration.

There have been many demonstrations in Catalonia since Catalan leaders were sentenced to prison. The leaders demand independence for Catalonia.

Catalonia is part of Spain. However, Catalonia has its own language, culture and flag. Many Catalans want Catalonia to leave Spain and for Catalans to get their own state.

## Syria ceasefire

![Mike Pence and Recep Tayyip Erdoğan](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11026116)

Open image viewer

US Vice President Mike Pence and Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan negotiated a ceasefire in Turkey. Photo: EPA

An attempt is being made at a ceasefire in Northern Syria. No fighting takes place during the ceasefire.

US Vice President Mike Pence told about the ceasefire in Turkey on Thursday. The idea is that the ceasefire lasts 5 days.

Turkey and the Kurds have been fighting in Northern Syria. Turkey attacked Northern Syria a little over a week ago. In Turkey's view, the Kurdish soldiers in the area are terrorists.

The ceasefire has not started well. News agencies report that the fighting has not completely ended.

## Record-long flight

![Alan Joyce and Lisa Norman](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11026506)

Open image viewer

The Australian airline Qantas is trying out record-long flights. Photo: Dean Lewins / EPA

A record-long flight will be made at the weekend. The Australian airline Qantas is flying directly from New York to Sydney, Australia.

The plane leaves on Friday and arrives on Sunday morning. The journey takes 19 hours.

There are 40 passengers and crew on the plane. Everyone's condition and well-being will be monitored during the flight.

By the end of the year, Qantas will decide whether it will start such long flights. Qantas started flights from Australia to London last year. The flight to London takes 17 hours, so the weekend flight is still 2 hours longer.

## HSL travel cards

![A passenger stamps a ticket](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10759674)

Open image viewer

Soon you will be able to load a travel card yourself online. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Loading travel cards for the Helsinki Region Transport, that is, HSL, is becoming easier. Topping up travel cards online will soon also be possible.

HSL says that travel cards can be topped up online at the latest at the beginning of next year.

Loading can be done on HSL's website. Before loading, the customer must identify themselves.

Customers have long hoped that topping up travel cards would become possible online too.
