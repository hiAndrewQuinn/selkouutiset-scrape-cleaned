## Corona situation in Finland

![Monitoring screen of the intensive care unit of Turku University Central Hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4927,h_2771,x_0,y_402/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89224661b9a74dd13f2/1639732039)

The number of corona patients in intensive care has decreased. Photo: Arash Matin / Yle

The corona situation in Finland is starting to calm down.

The omicron variant of the corona virus caused many corona infections, but the worst time is already over.

Health authorities say that there are now fewer corona patients in intensive care than for a long time.

On Thursday there were 32 corona patients in intensive care. The last time there were so few corona patients in intensive care was in the middle of November.

On Thursday, 6,800 new corona infections were reported in Finland.

## Party support

![Party support poll for January 2022. The National Coalition Party remains the most popular party.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12298151)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

Support for the Finns Party has fallen.

This is what Yle's party support poll shows. In it, people were asked which party they support in the parliamentary elections.

The largest party in the poll is the National Coalition Party. Its support is about 21 per cent.

The second largest party is the SDP, whose support is 19 per cent.

The Finns Party is in third place. The party's support has dropped more than 3 percentage points since the previous poll and is now 15 per cent.

Political researchers estimate that in the next parliamentary elections, only the National Coalition Party and the SDP will compete for the place of the largest party.

The support figures for all parties are at yle.fi.

## Bang in Kouvola

![Hornet taking off](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7360,h_4140,x_0,y_770/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7271235f7d5a13e2371/1602052263)

Open image viewer

A bang was heard in Kouvola when an army Hornet fighter jet flew faster than the speed of sound. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

An unusual situation happened in Kouvola on Thursday morning. A loud bang was heard in the city.

In the afternoon, an explanation was given. The army said that its aircraft had flown over the Kouvola area.

An army Hornet fighter jet flew over Kouvola. It flew faster than the speed of sound, and that is why a bang was heard in Kouvola.

The army says the aircraft had a surveillance task. The army does not say more about the matter.

## The weather

On Friday, a lot of snow will fall in the west and north. In the west it is also very windy. In the east it is cloudy.

In the south and west the temperature is between -2 and -10 degrees, in the north and east between -10 and -15 degrees.

Snowfall continues at the weekend. The weather also gets milder, that is, the frost weakens.

On Saturday the temperature rises to +1 degree in the west. The coldest is in the east, where it is 8 degrees of frost.
