# Monday 12.12.2022 (radio)

There is a storm in southern Finland. The number of foreign tourists is smaller than before Covid. A lot of Christmas post is being sent this week. It also snows on Tuesday.

## Winter storm

![Cars on a winter road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12683994)

There is a winter storm in southern Finland. Lots of snow is falling and the wind is strong.

On the roads, driving conditions are very bad in places because of the snowfall. The police say that on the roads you must drive slowly. It is best to avoid overtaking.

At least the rescue services of the City of Helsinki and Päijät-Häme say that you should not drive a car at all on Monday evening, unless there is an important reason.

Some train services have been cancelled because of the storm. Finnair says that aircraft are running behind schedule. Flights may also be delayed tomorrow, on Tuesday.

The storm may knock down trees onto power lines. Because of this, the power may go out.

Some ski resorts closed their slopes because of the storm.

## Foreign tourists

Fewer foreign tourists visit Finland than before Covid. This year there have been 30 percent fewer foreign tourists in Finland than in 2019. In particular, there are fewer Asian tourists in Finland than before.

A lot of tourists from abroad are expected in Lapland at Christmas time. Many people from Europe are coming to Lapland this year. More people from the USA are also travelling to Lapland than in previous years.

## Christmas post

Finns send a lot of Christmas post and Christmas parcels before Christmas.

Posti (Finland Post) estimates that people will send the most parcels this week. Already last week Posti delivered a total of 1.5 million parcels. This is clearly more than in an ordinary week.

## Frost record

![View from Utsjoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11165199)

Archive picture from Utsjoki. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

On Monday morning the coldest temperature of the winter was measured in Lapland. At Tulppio in Savukoski there was a frost of 33.6 degrees. At many other measuring points in Lapland too, the frost was more than 30 degrees.

On Tuesday more snow will fall across much of the country. In Lapland it is dry.

In Lapland there is about 20 degrees of frost. Elsewhere in the country the frost is about 10 degrees. The weather is windy, so it feels very cold.

Driving conditions are bad or very bad in southern Finland, and also in many places in Central Finland. At sea there is a storm or the wind is strong.
