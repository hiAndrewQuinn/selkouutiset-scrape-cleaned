## Children's corona infections

![A municipal worker from Naples cleans surfaces in a nursery in Naples at the start of the school year on 10 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12141480)

The corona virus is spreading among children in different parts of Europe. Photo: Ciro Fusco / EPA

New corona infections are now found most among children in Finland.

Most infections are found in children under 10. The second most infections are found in children and young people aged 10 to 19.

Elsewhere in Europe too, the corona virus is now spreading especially among children. That is why in many countries the corona restrictions for children are much stricter than in Finland.

For example, in Denmark children will be moved to distance learning next week, Belgium is bringing the Christmas holidays forward, and in France there is a mask requirement for those over 6 years old.

## Christmas party season

![Decorations in a Christmas tree.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7660097)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

Restaurants' hopes for the Christmas party season have collapsed. Most restaurants and tourism companies believe that the Christmas party season will be bad.

A new survey says that about 70 per cent of companies believe that sales will be lower than in 2019. At that time Christmas parties were held as usual, because the corona pandemic had not yet begun.

At first the restaurants expected that this year's Christmas party season could even be the best ever. The expectations collapsed when workplaces were again recommended to work remotely. Remote work is recommended because of the bad corona situation.

## Return from Syria

![Al-Hol refugee camp](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10802194)

Open image viewer

Women and children at the al-Hol camp in Syria. Photo: Antti Kuronen / Yle

Finland has brought home a Finnish mother and her 4 children from Syria. The mother and the children had earlier been in the al-Hol camp in Syria.

Finland has now brought home 35 Finns from Syria in total. Most of those brought home are children.

About 10 Finns are still in camps in Syria.

## Slippery roads

![walking on a slippery road](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-74421588e29545c13b/1578575440)

Open image viewer

The pavements were slippery today. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Today the conditions have been very slippery across the whole country. Drivers have been warned about the poor driving conditions, and pedestrians about slippery pavements.

On slippery roads there have been runoffs and crashes. For example, in Lohja a car and a van collided nose to nose in the morning, and the drivers were taken to hospital. On Highway 5 near Kuopio 2 lorries crashed in the early hours of the morning, so that traffic to the north was blocked for many hours.

At Helsinki-Vantaa airport, air traffic was stopped on Friday evening for a few hours because of the bad weather.

## Sunday's weather

The weather forecast says that tomorrow too there will be light snowfall or freezing rain.

In the Helsinki and Turku regions heavy snow may fall in the evening.

The daytime temperature on Sunday is between +1 in the south and -5 in Lapland.

The driving conditions are poor tomorrow too across the whole country.
