# Sunday 1.3.2020 (radio)

3 new coronavirus infections have been found in Finland. 130 people have to go into quarantine, that is, they may not leave home. The situation at the border between Turkey and Greece is getting worse. It is getting warmer in the south.

## New coronavirus cases

The coronavirus continues to spread. 3 new coronavirus infections have been found in the Helsinki region.

The authorities told about the new infections on Sunday.

The new coronavirus patients are relatives of coronavirus patients who had previously got the infection in Italy.

6 coronavirus cases have been found in Finland so far in total.

## People in quarantine

![Helsinki Mayor Jan Vapaavuori.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_291/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4560715a5774f909bd2/1515681084)

One of the new coronavirus patients is a schoolchild. He is in good condition and may stay at home. However, he may not leave the home.

The authorities want to stop the spread of the coronavirus. That is why the child's school friends and football friends also have to go into quarantine, that is, they must stay at home.

In total about 130 people now have to stay at home. Teaching for the children will be arranged at home.

The authorities are telling the child's friends about this. The coronavirus patient is a pupil at the Viikki Normal School in Helsinki. He plays football in the HJK Töölö team.

## Information about the coronavirus

Information about the coronavirus is available on the website of THL, the National Institute for Health and Welfare. THL also explains the coronavirus in plain language.

Information about the coronavirus in plain language

THL says that good hand hygiene is now important. Hands must be washed with water and soap, for example every time you come in from outside.

The symptoms of the coronavirus are similar to the symptoms of flu. Symptoms include, for example, coughing and fever.

## Crisis at the EU border

![Clash at the Greece-Turkey border on 29 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11234819)

Open image viewer

Thousands of people are at the border between Turkey and Greece. Photo: Ozan Kose / AFP

The situation at the border between Turkey and Greece is getting worse. There are already thousands of people at the border who want to get into Greece, that is, into the EU side. Greece is not letting them across the border.

Turkey said on Saturday that it will no longer stop people at the border. Greece thinks that Turkey is even helping people who are trying to cross the border illegally.

The situation at the Turkey-Greece border is getting worse because of the war in Syria.

The worst battles of the Syrian war are now in the Idlib area, which is close to Turkey. As many as a million Syrians are trying to escape the fighting.

Turkey says that it cannot take in more refugees. There are already more than 3 million Syrians in Turkey. Turkey wants the new refugees to go somewhere else.

## It is getting warmer in the south

In the north, cold winter weather continues.
