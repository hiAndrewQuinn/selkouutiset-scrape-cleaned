## Children's hobbies

![Children's exercise / Eevi and Hilda / Viikki branch 28.01.019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_172/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5394175c504628c9231/1549534764)

Open image viewer

Children's indoor exercise continues. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

Children's and young people's hobbies continue in Southern Finland. The Regional State Administrative Agency for Southern Finland says that exercise and hobby facilities will stay open for children.

A week ago the government recommended that children's hobbies also be banned indoors for some time. Adults' hobby activities are already banned. The reason is the poor corona situation.

According to many experts, however, exercise hobbies are important for children. Banning hobbies does more harm than good.

Former President of Finland Martti Ahtisaari has recovered from corona disease. Ahtisaari got a corona infection in December. Now he is already well.

Martti Ahtisaari was President of Finland from 1994 to 2000. Ahtisaari has done a lot of peace work, and he received the Nobel Peace Prize in 2008.

## Bus accident

![An overturned coach on the snowy roadside, an ambulance and a rescue official.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_137/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90195661e15310aa78d/1642156925)

Open image viewer

The bus finally fell into the ditch. Photo: Ari Welling / Yle

There has been a bus accident in Salo in Southern Finland. 2 people were injured in the accident.

A double-decker bus was involved in the accident on Friday morning. The bus was on its way from Helsinki to Turku.

The accident happened when the bus was driving to a stop. Then it left the road and fell into a ditch.

There were a driver and 11 passengers on the bus. Nobody was seriously injured, because many passengers were wearing seat belts.

The police are investigating the accident.

## Queen of Denmark

![Queen Margareeta of Denmark smiles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5479,h_3070,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90143261e01c0166fa7/1642077366)

Open image viewer

Queen Margareeta II of Denmark. Photo: Keld Navntoft / EPA

The Danish queen was celebrated in Denmark on Friday. Margareeta II has been Queen of Denmark for exactly 50 years.

Margareeta became Queen of Denmark when her father, King Fredrik, died in January 1972.

Margareeta is popular in Denmark. A survey by a Danish newspaper shows that over 70 per cent of Danes like the queen a lot.

On Saturday the sun shines across a large part of the country.
