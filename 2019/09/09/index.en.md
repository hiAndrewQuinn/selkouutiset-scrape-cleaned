# Monday 9.9.2019 (radio)

Patient safety at the children's hospital is worrying. Mediation reduces court cases about child custody. British Airways pilots are on strike. Rivers and streams are being opened for migratory fish. It is warm on Tuesday.

## Patient safety at the children's hospital is worrying

![The new children's hospital was opened in early September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10428215)

Open image viewer

The children's hospital is located in Helsinki. Photo: Tuomas Uusheimo

There may be problems at the new children's hospital. Nurses and parents say that patient safety could be at risk.

At the children's hospital, for example, a piece of ceiling board fell onto a breathing machine. Nurses say that children are sometimes tied to their beds, because there are not enough nurses.

Nurses and parents have told Yle's MOT programme about the situation at the children's hospital. The director of the children's hospital says that the problems will be fixed.

## Mediation reduces custody court cases

![A mother gives a kiss to her child.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9974261)

Open image viewer

In mediation, matters concerning a child are agreed between parents who have separated. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Mediation has reduced court cases about children's custody. In mediation, parents who have separated agree on how the child's life will be arranged, and no court case is needed.

Every year, about 30,000 children's parents separate in Finland. About 2,000 children's custody becomes a dispute, where a court's decision is requested. Half of these are already mediated voluntarily, without a court case.

## British Airways cancelled many flights because of a strike

![A view from Heathrow airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10961750)

Open image viewer

The dispute over pilots' wages has been going on for 9 months. Photo: AOP

Pilots at the airline British Airways have started a two-day strike. The pilots are on strike on Monday and Tuesday. The reason for the strike is a dispute over pilots' wages.

Because of the strike, British Airways has cancelled almost all of the company's flights from British airports, that is, about 1,700 flights. British Airways flights to Finland have also been cancelled today and tomorrow.

## Room made for migratory fish in rivers

![Vaalimaa river, Miehikkälä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10909274)

Open image viewer

Vaalimaa river in Miehikkälä. Photo: Yle / Vesa Grekula

Rivers and streams in Finland are being opened for migratory fish. In the rivers and streams there are many ruins of old dams, which prevent migratory fish from swimming and breeding. The ELY Centres and landowners are removing the obstacles.

The work started on Monday in south-eastern Finland. In Savonkoski, a route for the fish is being dug past a dam. It will become easier for migratory fish to travel from the Russian border to the rapids of Luumäki in South Karelia.

## Weather is warm on Tuesday

![Plum tree with an unusually large number of fruit in summer 2019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10952371)

It will be unusually warm in Finland on Tuesday. Warm air is coming from the south-east. The temperature is between 20 and 25 degrees. Normally it is not this warm in September. After Tuesday, the weather will cool down again.
