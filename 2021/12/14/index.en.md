# Tuesday 14.12.2021 (radio)

Prisons have a shortage of guards. Norway tightens corona restrictions. Corona infections in Finland. President Niinistö talked with President Putin. The deadline for candidates in the regional elections ended. Weather forecast.

## Shortage of prison guards

![Turku prison guard leaning on the bars.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7304315f88339083557/1602762388)

The shortage of guards is greatest in big prisons. Photo: Saana Sjöblom-Hasselblatt / Yle

There is a shortage of guards in prisons. The reason is that places for training prison guards were cut about 10 years ago. Now there are not enough qualified applicants for the job of prison guard.

Many substitutes who do not have enough training work in prisons. Prison guards say that the work is heavier, because there are too few guards. Teaching the substitutes adds to the work of prison guards.

The shortage of guards is greatest in big prisons, for example in Sukeva, Turku and Helsinki.

## Norway's corona measures

![The border between Finland and Norway at Utsjoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2275,h_1283,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88604361a5f28d13f34/1638265561)

Norway is tightening corona measures at the border. Photo: Sara Kelemeny / Yle

Norway is tightening corona restrictions. All people who arrive in Norway must have a corona test. The reason is the Omicron variant of the corona virus, which is spreading quickly in Norway.

Norway also bans serving alcohol in restaurants and bars for a month. The aim is to reduce gatherings of people.

In Norway the number of Omicron infections has doubled in a week.

## Regional elections

![Pirkanmaa wellbeing services county office at Finmedi 5 in Tampere](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3590,h_2019,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8517996139f7e992507/1637674032)

Finland will have 21 wellbeing services counties. Photo: Kirsi Matson-Mäkelä / Yle

The deadline for candidates in the regional elections has ended. The candidate deadline ended on Tuesday afternoon. More than 10 000 candidates were registered for the regional elections. No party got its candidate lists full.

Finland's first regional elections will be held on 23 January. In the elections, representatives are chosen to the regional councils. The regional councils decide on the services of the wellbeing services counties.

Finland will have 21 wellbeing services counties. The wellbeing services counties will organise social and health services, and fire and rescue services, from March onwards.
