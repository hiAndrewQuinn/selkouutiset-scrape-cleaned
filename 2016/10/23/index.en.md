# Sunday 23.10.2016 (radio)

Finland now exports more weapons to the Middle East than to other regions. Motor insurance will change next year. People who were trying to get to Europe have been rescued in the Mediterranean. Alcohol has been completely banned in Iraq. Timo Kaartinen wants to save the Banda language.

## Finland's arms trade

![The prime minister's interview hour at Kesäranta on 23 October. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9247408)

Prime minister Sipilä thinks that Finland takes human rights issues into account when it sells weapons. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

Prime minister Juha Sipilä says that Finland's principles in the arms trade have not changed. Finland still considers carefully whom it sells weapons to.

A new report says that Finland now exports more weapons to the Middle East than to other regions. Finland has sold weapons, for example, to Saudi Arabia, which is involved in the war in Yemen.

Finland sold weapons worth 99 million euros last year.

## A new kind of motor insurance

![A crashed car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9237481)

Open image viewer

If a driver causes crashes, he has to pay a high price for motor insurance. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / AOP

The law on motor insurance will change next year. The price of the insurance will no longer depend on the car, but on the driver. A bad driver will pay more for insurance than a good driver.

The new law no longer sets how big the bonus, that is, the discount, can be. An insurance company may give a big bonus to a customer who has not made any mistakes in traffic. The bonus can be given for all the customer's cars.

The insurance companies are not yet saying what kind of motor insurance they plan to sell next year. Motor insurance is compulsory for a car owner.

## Rescue operations in the Mediterranean

![A Turkish border guard looks out to sea through binoculars.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8928354)

Open image viewer

The authorities monitor traffic in the Mediterranean. Photo: Sedat Suna / EPA

Over the weekend people have been rescued in the Mediterranean from small boats in poor condition. They were trying to get to Europe.

The Italian authorities say that 2,400 people were rescued in the Mediterranean on Saturday. 12 people died, 4 of them were children.

This year more than 3,000 people have drowned or gone missing in the Mediterranean while trying to get from North Africa to Europe.

## Iraq's alcohol ban

![A man sells fruit juice at a stall in Baghdad, Iraq.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9247147)

Open image viewer

The oldest juice kiosk in Baghdad. Photo: EPA/ALI ABBAS

Iraq has banned alcohol completely. Alcohol may not be made or sold.

The Iraqi parliament justifies the ban with the Islamic faith. If someone breaks the ban, he will get big fines.

Until now, beer and an alcoholic drink that tastes of anise have been made in Iraq.

## The Banda language

![Professor of social and cultural anthropology Timo Kaartinen (in the middle) in the village of Banda Eli in 2009.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9237300)

Timo Kaartinen with the Banda people. Photo: Elina Grundström

The Finn Timo Kaartinen is the Agricola of the Banda people. Agricola founded the Finnish written language 500 years ago; Kaartinen is now founding the Banda written language.

The Banda language is spoken by about 5,000 people. They live on the Banda Islands, which are part of Indonesia.

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9240221)

Photo: Yle News Graphics

Many young Banda people nowadays use the Indonesian language, but people also want to preserve the Banda language. That is why a Banda written language is also needed, and it is Kaartinen's big project. He is doing it together with Banda people and other Indonesians.

The Banda people value professor Timo Kaartinen. You can see it from the fact that nowadays many Banda boys are named Timo.
