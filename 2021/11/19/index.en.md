## Austria's corona vaccine requirement

![Alexander Schallenberg.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7019,h_3948,x_0,y_197/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-86537061641f37995c2/1633951590)

Austrian Chancellor Alexander Schallenberg said that the corona vaccine will become compulsory. Photo: Christian Bruna / EPA

The corona vaccine will become compulsory in Austria. Austria is the first EU country where people must take the corona vaccine.

Everyone in Austria must have the corona vaccine by early February at the latest. Austrians will get a fine or even a prison sentence if they do not take the corona vaccine.

In addition, a corona lockdown starts in Austria next week. During it, Austrians must stay at home. They may only go out to buy food and medicine. The corona lockdown in Austria lasts for no more than 20 days.

The new Minister of the Environment, Emma Kari, and the new Minister of the Interior, Krista Mikkonen. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Some of the ministers in Finland's government have changed. The reason is that minister and Green party leader Maria Ohisalo went on maternity leave on Friday.

Emma Kari has joined the government in Ohisalo's place. She is the new Minister of the Environment.

The Green Krista Mikkonen is now Minister of the Interior. Until now, Mikkonen has been Minister of the Environment.

Green party leader Maria Ohisalo will return to work next summer.

## Young people's housing

![Rental agent Taru Voipali of Vuokraturva Oy shows a flat to a possible tenant in Kaleva, Tampere](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4915,h_2765,x_0,y_169/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83409460f80b36c9a0e/1626868788)

Open image viewer

Many young people have found their own flat during the corona time. Photo: Kirsi Matson-Mäkelä / Yle

Young people in Finland move out from their parents at a younger age than before.

Last year about 15 per cent of young people under 30 lived with their parents. 20 years ago about 30 per cent of people under 30 lived together with their parents.

The corona time has made it easier for young people to become independent. In many cities there are now more free rental flats than before the corona time.

The figures are from a review by the credit institution Hypo.

## Outdoor skating season

![a man skating](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8470024)

Open image viewer

Photo: Katja Halinen / Yle

The outdoor skating season has started in the Helsinki region. People can skate at many artificial ice rinks in different parts of the Helsinki region.

The artificial ice rink in Martinlaakso in Vantaa opened on Tuesday. On Thursday the artificial ice rink in Oulunkylä in Helsinki opened.

The Ice Park at Rautatientori in Helsinki will not open this winter. However, Helsinki has 9 artificial ice rinks where you can skate for free.
