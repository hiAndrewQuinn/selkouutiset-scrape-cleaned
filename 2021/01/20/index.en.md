# Wednesday 20.1.2021 (radio)

Joe Biden has started as President of the USA. There are 444 new corona infections in Finland. About 62,000 people have received the corona vaccine in Finland. Netflix has a record number of new subscribers.

## Power has changed hands in the USA

![Joe Biden took part, together with his wife Jill, in a memorial event for the victims of corona on the eve of his inauguration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11745393)

Joe Biden took part, together with his wife Jill, in a memorial event for the victims of corona on the eve of his inauguration. Photo: Shawn Thew / EPA

Power has changed hands in the USA. The new President, Joe Biden, takes the oath of office on Wednesday in the capital, Washington.

Donald Trump is not taking part in Biden's swearing-in ceremony. He left the White House earlier.

Trump gave a speech at the Andrews military base near Washington. Trump said that he achieved a lot of good as president. Trump also wished good luck to the administration of Joe Biden.

After the speech Trump travelled to Florida. Trump is the first US president in over 150 years who was not present when the next president began the job.

Earlier in the day Trump, among other things, pardoned several people who had been convicted of crimes or were suspected of them.

## There are over 440 new corona infections in Finland

444 new corona infections have been found in Finland, says the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

278 of the infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district. There are 35 infections in the North Savo hospital district and 30 in Southwest Finland.

## About 62,000 people in Finland have received the corona vaccine

![Health care staff in Rovaniemi being vaccinated against corona.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7640346006f5de21380/1611304420)

In Rovaniemi the city's health care staff are being vaccinated. Photo: Jarmo Honkanen / Yle

About 62,000 people have received the corona vaccine in Finland. The information from THL may not be accurate.

The most corona vaccines have been given in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district, where about 17,000 people have received the vaccine. In the Southwest Finland, North Ostrobothnia and Pirkanmaa hospital districts about 5,000 people have received the vaccine.

The most people vaccinated are 50 to 59 year olds. The next most vaccinated are 40 to 49 year olds.

## Netflix's subscriber count rose

![A woman scrolls through Netflix offerings on a tablet in a cafe in Istanbul.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11460734)

Netflix has more new subscribers than ever before.

Netflix gained 8.5 million new subscribers between October and December. During the whole of last year Netflix gained 37 million new subscribers. Netflix now has over 200 million subscribers in total.

Netflix is a streaming service where you can watch TV series and films.
