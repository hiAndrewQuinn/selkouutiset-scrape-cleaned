# Monday 6.3.2017 (radio)

The race for the chair of the Finns Party has started. Finns think the economic recession is over. 12 countries won medals at the Lahti World Championships. It may snow heavily on the south coast.

## Sampo Terho is running for leader of the Finns Party

![Sampo Terho](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9493577)

Parliamentary group chair Sampo Terho held a press conference on Monday. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The race for the chair of the Finns Party has started.

Sampo Terho, chair of the Finns Party parliamentary group, said on Monday that he is running to be the party's new chair.

MP Leena Meri is also running for chair. Member of the European Parliament Jussi Halla-aho has not yet said whether he plans to run for leader of the Finns Party.

Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö is not entering the race for chair; instead he supports Sampo Terho. Speaker of Parliament Maria Lohela also says that she supports Sampo Terho.

The current chair Timo Soini said on Sunday that he will give up the chair's job in June. Soini led the Finns Party for 20 years.

## Finns believe in economic growth

![Yle recession survey](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9490637)

Open image viewer

In Yle's survey most respondents say that the recession is over.  Photo: Yle News Graphics

Finns think that the economic recession is now over.

In Yle's survey about 60 percent of respondents thought that the recession is over. Almost as many said that they have money left over to save.

A recession means that the economy has shrunk for 6 months.

Men especially were hopeful about new economic growth.

Juho Rahkonen, research director at Taloustutkimus, says that more jobs have come especially in fields where a lot of men work.

## 12 countries won World Championship medals

![Matti Heikkinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9493029)

Open image viewer

Finland's fifth medal was brought by Matti Heikkinen, who won bronze in the 50-kilometre race. Photo: Tomi Hännnen.

Finland got 5 medals in total at the Nordic Ski World Championships in Lahti.

Finland's 5th medal came on the last day of the championships, on Sunday. Matti Heikkinen then won bronze in the men's 50-kilometre race.

Norway won the most medals at the World Championships. Norway got 18 medals in total. Germany was second with 11 medals in total and Russia third with 6 medals. Finland was 5th in the medal table.

A total of 12 countries won medals.

## More snow is on the way

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9493315)

Open image viewer

On Tuesday it snows in the south, on Wednesday in the north too Photo: Yle

Snowfall is expected in Finland on Tuesday.

The snowfall comes first to Southern Finland. During the night between Tuesday and Wednesday, over 10 centimetres of snow may fall on the south coast. Later the snowfall will spread north too, says Yle meteorologist Joonas Koskela.

On Tuesday it is below zero in the whole country. It may be 5-10 degrees below zero. The night is cold. In Lapland it may be as cold as minus 30 degrees. The weather gets milder at the end of the week.
