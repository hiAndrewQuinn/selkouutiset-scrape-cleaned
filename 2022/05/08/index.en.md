## Medals for mothers

![President Sauli Niinistö and his spouse Jenni Haukio at the national Mother's Day celebration at the Säätytalo in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12436178)

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio took part in the Mother's Day celebration. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

On Sunday Mother's Day was celebrated. Mother's Day is an important holiday in Finland.

This year 30 mothers received a medal. President Sauli Niinistö awarded the medals to mothers who have done important and good work as raisers of children.

The medals were given to mothers at the Mother's Day celebration in Helsinki. President Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio also took part in the celebration.

Medals were given to many different kinds of mothers. Among them this year there were also mothers with an immigrant background.

## Nato debate

![Chair of the Left Alliance Li Andersson on A-Talk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9446626261b172d206c/1650569631)

Open image viewer

Chair of the Left Alliance, Li Andersson.

The parties continue the discussion on the military alliance NATO. Finland will soon decide whether it applies for NATO membership.

The Left Alliance, a party in the government, dealt with the NATO issue on Saturday. The Left Alliance decided to continue in the government also in the case that the government supports NATO membership.

The biggest government party, the SDP, will deal with the NATO issue next Saturday. Then the SDP will say whether it thinks Finland should apply for NATO membership.

The other parties have already said that they are ready to support Finland's NATO application.

Finland is considering NATO membership because Russia has started a war in Ukraine. In many people's opinion, Finland's own security will improve if Finland becomes a NATO member.

## Opening of the Eurovision

![Winner of the Eurovision, Italy's Måneskin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5258,h_2958,x_0,y_550/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80982560aa059bc6689/1621755390)

Open image viewer

The Eurovision is held in Italy, because Italy won the song contest last year. Photo: Robin Van Lonkhuijsen / EPA

The opening of the Eurovision is celebrated today, Sunday evening.

The Eurovision song contest is held this year in Italy, because Italy won the Eurovision last year. The competition venue is the city of Turin.

40 countries take part in the Eurovision this year. Finland's representative is The Rasmus. The band performs the song Jezebel.

The song contest has 2 semi-finals. They are on Tuesday and Thursday. From each semi-final the best countries get into the final, that is, the final competition.

The final is next Saturday. Then it will become clear which country wins the Eurovision this year.
