# Friday 18.2.2022 (radio)

The government supports Finns because of the rise in petrol prices. Russia is holding war exercises again. The situation in Ukraine is discussed at the Munich Security Conference. A suspended prison sentence was handed down for harassing a journalist. The Lions have reached the Olympic final. Weather forecast.

## Support for Finns because of energy prices

![Petrol pump handles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-915905620cd8321e94d/1645169027)

Rising petrol prices add to Finns' costs. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

The government has announced new support measures for Finns. Support measures are needed, because the price of energy has risen a lot. For example, driving a car to work has become more expensive, because the price of petrol has risen. That is why the right to deduct commuting costs is raised from 7,000 euros to 8,400 euros.

The mileage allowance is also raised from 25 cents to 30 cents. Mileage allowances are paid to a person who uses their own car for work.

There will also be relief for agriculture. The real estate tax on agricultural production buildings will be removed this year, if the EU gives permission. The changes are temporary.

Russia is holding war exercises on Saturday. Russia says that it is holding missile exercises, which are led by President Vladimir Putin.

The Russian Defence Ministry says that during the exercises ballistic missiles and cruise missiles will be fired. Russia's air and space forces, missile troops, navy and units of the southern military district take part in the exercise.

Russia has not said in which areas the missile exercises will be held on Saturday.

## Sentence for harassing a journalist

![Johan Bäckman on the left and Jessikka Aro on the right.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-916759620e7b4a1f692/1645169576)

Open image viewer

Johan Bäckman (left) and Jessikka Aro (right). Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle, Bengt Östling / Yle

The Supreme Court has given a verdict in the stalking case of journalist Jessikka Aro. The court sentenced docent Johan Bäckman to a suspended prison sentence.

The court says that Bäckman stalked Aro. Bäckman insulted Aro, for example in social media, and sent Aro several messages. Bäckman did not like articles written by Aro that told about Russian information influence.

The Supreme Court sentenced Johan Bäckman to 60 days of suspended prison sentence.
