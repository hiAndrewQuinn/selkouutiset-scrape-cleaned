## Russia attacks, Ukraine holds on

![Soldier and a dog.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_89/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-921096621b412b8245c/1645955068)

A Ukrainian soldier looks at a column of smoke rising from an oil depot near Kyiv on Sunday morning. Photo: Alisa Yakubovich / EPA

Russia's attack on Ukraine has continued for the fourth day.

The capital Kyiv is still in Ukrainian hands. Instead, it has been reported today that Russian forces have advanced to Kharkiv, Ukraine's second largest city. However, it is unclear whether the city is in Russian or Ukrainian hands.

Russia has apparently hit a natural gas pipeline in Kharkiv. In Kyiv Russia has hit an oil terminal. Ukraine says that Russia has also bombed residential areas.

## Weapon aid to Ukraine

![Antti Kaikkonen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4762,h_2673,x_0,y_99/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9197376217505959edb/1645695303)

Open image viewer

Defence Minister Antti Kaikkonen told what kind of help Finland is giving Ukraine. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Many European countries have promised weapon aid to Ukraine. For example, Portugal, the Netherlands, France and Belgium have promised to give weapons to Ukraine.

Germany, too, promises to give Ukraine 1,000 anti-tank weapons and 500 anti-aircraft missiles. Germany says that the weapons will be delivered to Ukraine as quickly as possible.

Finland, for its part, has decided to give Ukraine 2,000 bulletproof vests and helmets, and 2 field hospitals.

Finland also accepts that old German guns will be taken from Estonia to Ukraine. Estonia needs the permission of Finland and Germany to export the guns.

## Avoid travel to Russia

![Border markers between Finland and Russia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8470133)

Open image viewer

Finland and Russia border markers. Photo: Heikki Haapalainen / Yle

Finland's Ministry for Foreign Affairs urges people to avoid all travel to Russia. The ministry also urges people to leave the border zone between Russia and Ukraine, because the security situation there has worsened.

The ministry says that the risks of travelling and of payments have grown in Russia.

Because of the economic sanctions, it may be impossible, for example, to pay with foreign payment cards or to withdraw cash from ATMs.

## The Rasmus heads to Eurovision

![The Rasmus on stage after winning the UMK22 final.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2500,h_1406,x_0,y_139/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-921038621a98056edfe/1645916176)

Open image viewer

The Rasmus represent Finland at the Eurovision Song Contest in Turin. Photo: Miikka Varila / Yle

Finland has chosen its representative for the Eurovision Song Contest.

Finland will be represented by the band The Rasmus. The band's song "Jezebel" won the New Music Competition in the evening.

The New Music Competition had a record audience. 1.9 million people watched it on television.

The Eurovision Song Contest will be held in Turin, Italy, in May.

Russia is not allowed to take part in the competition. The European Broadcasting Union EBU has excluded Russia from the competition, because Russia attacked Ukraine.
