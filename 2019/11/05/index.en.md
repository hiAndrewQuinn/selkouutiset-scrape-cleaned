# Tuesday 5.11.2019 (radio)

The health care company Mehiläinen is growing, as it buys Pihlajalinna. Driving on slippery roads is now often practised on a simulator. The Father of the Year award will be given to 3 fathers this year. Lingonberries may help with problems caused by obesity. A new early-winter frost record was measured in Lapland.

A big health care company is being created in Finland. A big company is created when 2 companies merge.

The companies are Mehiläinen and Pihlajalinna. They have agreed that Mehiläinen will buy Pihlajalinna. The price of the deal is about 360 million euros.

After the deal, Mehiläinen will have 25,000 employees. Mehiläinen may become the largest health care company in the Nordic countries.

## Practising driving on slippery roads

![A young person drives in a driving simulator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10488386)

Open image viewer

You can practise driving on slippery roads in a simulator. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

The practice of driving on slippery roads has changed.

New drivers no longer have to practise on a training track how a car behaves on slippery roads. The Driving Licence Act allows practice also on a simulator, that is, a teaching device.

Driving instructors say that a simulator does not give a true picture of driving on slippery roads. For example, a student only notices the effect of speed in a real situation.

Because of the change in the law, the number of customers at training tracks has fallen. Some training tracks have even been closed.

The Father of the Year award will be given to 3 fathers this year.

The winners of the award are Hermanni Hyytiälä, Petri Jaakola and Jukka Lagerblom. They have managed to combine work and family.

The winners of the award have also been safe fathers in difficult situations. For example, Hermanni Hyytiälä looks after 4 children on his own, because the children's mother died of cancer.

On Sunday, Father's Day is celebrated in Finland. Father's Day is always on the second Sunday of November.

## Health effects of lingonberries

![Lingonberry](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11051604)

Open image viewer

Lingonberry is a superfood. Photo: AOP

Lingonberry is a really healthy berry. This is what a University of Tampere study says.

The researchers noticed that lingonberries may help with problems caused by obesity. Lingonberries prevent, for example, cholesterol and blood sugar values from rising.

It seems that lingonberries from Northern Finland are even healthier than lingonberries from Southern Finland. The reason may be that summer nights are longer and lighter in the north than in the south.

However, researchers do not yet know which substance in lingonberries improves health.

## Another new frost record

![Ice crystals in a window](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1727,h_971,x_0,y_100/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5232205bebf9c663a81/1542191724)

The cold weather continues. In Lapland, another new frost record for this early winter was measured.

In Sodankylä, on Tuesday morning, it was 28.3 degrees of frost. The previous record was measured on Monday.

In Finland it is now colder than usual for early November.
