# Friday 1.6.2018 (radio)

School end-of-term celebrations are held at the weekend. Fewer residence permits were granted to Finland than in 2016. Many weather records were broken in May. School end-of-term celebrations can be enjoyed in beautiful weather.

## More than 76,000 students graduated from vocational training this year

School end-of-term celebrations are held at the weekend.

More than 76,000 students have completed a vocational qualification. Of them, 55,000 have completed a basic vocational qualification.

You can search for the names of people who graduated from vocational training on the Yle website, through the Amiskone tool. Amiskone is at yle.fi. The list is being compiled as the schools send in their information.

Around 27,000 students will celebrate their matriculation tomorrow.

## The number of residence permits fell

![An asylum seeker by the window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9398980)

Open image viewer

The number of residence permits fell last year. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Fewer residence permits were granted to Finland last year than in 2016.

The European Migration Network says that almost 34,000 people received a first residence permit. That is about 6,000 fewer people than in 2016.

In particular, residence permits granted on the basis of international protection fell. The drop was 2,500 residence permits.

## May was a record warm month

![A man jumps from a diving tower at the Helsinki Swimming Stadium.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10234371)

Open image viewer

The Helsinki Swimming Stadium has been good for jumping: May was record-breakingly summery. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Many weather records were broken in May.

May was the warmest May in Finland's measurement history. The average temperature in May was generally 4 to 5 degrees higher than the long-term average. The average temperature for the whole country was 11.6 degrees. That is half a degree more than in 1963.

May had a record number of hot days: there were 14. Normally there are 3 hot days.

The highest measurement in May was 29.6 degrees. It was measured in Kemiö, at Helsinki-Vantaa Airport and in Utti in Kouvola.

The sun shone record-breakingly much in May in the southern and central parts of the country. In many places the sun shone more than 100 hours longer than usual.

The heat has also warmed the surface waters. The waters are now even warmer than during the whole of last summer.

## Beautiful weather for spring celebrations

![The weather maps for Friday, Saturday and Sunday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10234068)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

School end-of-term celebrations can be enjoyed in beautiful weather.

Saturday is dry and even hot in parts of the country.

North of Oulu the temperature on Saturday is 10 to 20 degrees.

Heat is forecast up to the height of Jyväskylä, and up to Oulu about 20 degrees.

On Sunday cooler air and rain will start to flow north. In the south and centre of the country it is still warm, and the heat threshold may be exceeded right in the far south.

On Monday and Tuesday the temperatures will fall even in the south, to 10 to 15 degrees.

Early in the week the rain will spread from the north to the rest of the country. In Lapland it may even snow sleet on Monday.

The rain will reach Southern Finland by Tuesday at the latest.
