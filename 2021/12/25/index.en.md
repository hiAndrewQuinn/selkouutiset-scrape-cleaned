Finns have been celebrating Christmas calmly. For example, it has been quiet and peaceful in central Helsinki.

However, the police have been needed in homes across Finland, because people have behaved in a disruptive or violent way.

The police have also been needed on the roads. On the roads, the problem is drivers who are drunk.

## Flight cancellations

![A display board showing flight schedules.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8192,h_4608,x_0,y_780/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89631161c6a9981e38b/1721400246)

Open image viewer

Photo: John G. Mabanglo / EPA

A lot of flights have been cancelled around the world over Christmas.

For example, on Friday and Saturday more than 4,500 flights were cancelled around the world in total. In addition, many flights have been delayed.

The reason for the cancellations is the omicron variant of the coronavirus, which spreads easily. So many pilots have caught corona or are in corona quarantine and cannot get to work.

## Space telescope

![The James Webb space telescope is launched from French Guiana.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5249,h_2952,x_0,y_759/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89632161c7167a07ce2/1640438744)

The space telescope's journey began in South America. Photo: JM Guillon / ESA

A large telescope has been launched into space from South America. The James Webb telescope was launched into space from French Guiana on Saturday.

The telescope will travel 1.5 million kilometres from Earth. After that, it will start to explore space.

Europe, the USA and Canada built the James Webb telescope together. The construction cost 9 billion euros.

## Sauna

![A sauna with a water pail and ladle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5312,h_2988,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7537005fd63aae472a0/1607875384)

Open image viewer

Photo: Sirkka Haverinen / Yle

Sauna is part of Christmas for many Finns. Researchers say that sauna is also good for health.

Professor Lasse Viinikka says that sauna is the hottest place a person goes to voluntarily.

According to Viinikka, it is hard to say how often you should go to the sauna. The most important thing is that sauna feels nice. Then it is probably healthy too, Professor Viinikka says.

Professor Jari Laukkanen, on the other hand, says that sauna bathing lowers the risk of dying from heart and blood vessel diseases. Sauna bathing, for example, lowers blood pressure. In addition, sauna bathing can reduce memory diseases, Professor Laukkanen says.

However, researchers remind that a person with a heart disease, for example, should discuss sauna bathing with a doctor.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12246705)

Open image viewer

The weather for Christmas is wintry.

It has snowed especially in Western Finland. For example, in Vaasa 40 centimetres of snow fell on Christmas Eve. However, the snowfall is gradually weakening.

There is also frost across the whole country. On Sunday and Monday there is frost of about 5 to 15 degrees in a large part of the country.
