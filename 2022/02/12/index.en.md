## Finns out of Ukraine

![Soldiers loading weapons onto a lorry.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_432/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-914416620785d44bf8c/1644660314)

Ukraine is preparing for Russia's attack. Photo: Sergei Supinsky / AFP

Finland's Ministry for Foreign Affairs has given instructions to Finns who are in Ukraine.

The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says that Finns should leave Ukraine right away.

The Ministry for Foreign Affairs also says that nobody should travel from Finland to Ukraine right now.

The reason for the Foreign Ministry's instructions is the poor security situation in Ukraine. The USA has warned that Russia could attack Ukraine in the coming days.

Other countries, such as Norway, Estonia, Britain and the USA, also urge their own citizens to leave Ukraine.

## Demonstrations in Helsinki

![Demonstrators marching on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_501/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9144426207ad705b074/1644670419)

Demonstrators in Helsinki on Saturday. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Several demonstrations are being held in Helsinki over the weekend. The demonstrations are about the corona.

On Saturday the demonstrators opposed the requirement that some workers in social and health care jobs must have a coronavirus vaccination.

On Sunday the "For Friendship" demonstration will be held. It opposes all corona measures.

The Convoy Finland movement also continues its demonstrations over the weekend. Convoy Finland has already been protesting against corona restrictions for a week.

The police have said that the demonstrations went peacefully on Saturday.

## Sign language day

![Woman signing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11531077)

Open image viewer

Sign language is based on movements of the hands. Photo: Valeria Borovskaya / Yle

Sign language day was celebrated on Saturday.

In Finland there are almost 14,000 sign language users. A large part of them are deaf, that is, people who cannot hear. Sign language is based, for example, on movements of the hands.

In Finland there are 2 sign languages. They are Finnish Sign Language and Finland-Swedish Sign Language.

The law sets the status of sign language in Finland. The law says that deaf people have the right to learn their own language and use it freely.

Finns have also taken part in the Beijing Winter Olympic competitions on Saturday.

Finland was fourth (4th) in the women's relay cross-country race. The team had 4 skiers, and each skied 5 kilometres.

The gold in the women's relay went to Russian skiers. Germany got silver, and Sweden got bronze.

In women's ice hockey Finland beat Japan. Finland scored 7 goals in the game, Japan only 1 goal.

The win means that Finland got into the semi-finals. Finland's next game in the women's tournament is against the USA.

## Weather

On Sunday the weather is rainy. Snow and sleet fall in a large part of the country.

The temperature in the south is a little above zero. That is why some of the precipitation may be water.

On Monday a new rain area will reach Finland. It will bring rain to the southern and central parts of the country.
