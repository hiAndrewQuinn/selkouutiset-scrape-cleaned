# Monday 8.10.2018 (radio)

Researchers warn about climate warming. Much forest is being cut in Finland now. Nature is good for children. The Economics Nobel Prize has been awarded to two American researchers. In Finland, many women who have undergone female genital cutting are being treated. Media company Sanoma is starting co-operation negotiations.

## Climate warming is a big worry

![Graphics.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10439941)

Open image viewer

Climate warming causes a lot of harm to nature and to people. Photo: Mikko Airikka | Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Researchers warn about climate warming..  Researchers say that emissions into the air must be reduced quickly so that the climate does not warm too much. Warming causes a lot of harm to nature and people. The use of oil, coal and peat as fuel must stop, the researchers say.

## Forests are now being cut at a record pace

![Piles of timber at a felling area in Vihti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10395627)

Open image viewer

The price of pulp is now high. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Much forest is being cut in Finland now. The record felling is due to rising demand in industry. The price of pulp has also risen to a record high.

Climate researchers consider it bad that so much forest is being felled in Finland. Forest cleans carbon dioxide gas from the air and reduces climate warming.

## Good childhood experiences of nature help as an adult

![Children on a large rock in forest-elf costumes, with shaman drums.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9191985)

In many schools, classes are held outdoors in nature. Researchers have noticed that when a child enjoys nature, it helps them as an adult to make environmentally friendly consumer choices. The schools' curriculum encourages learning in nature and teaching a sustainable way of life.

## Economics Nobel to Nordhaus and Romer

![Pictures of William D. Nordhaus and Paul M. Romer projected onto a cloth.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10445593)

The Economics Nobel Prize has been awarded to two American researchers. The Economics Nobel Prize goes to **William Nordhaus** and **Paul Romer**. Nordhaus has studied the interaction between climate and the economy, and Romer the importance of inventions for the economy.

## Circumcised women are treated in Finland

![A nurse prepares a patient for surgery.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10324911)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

In Finland, many women who have undergone female genital cutting are being treated. Obstetricians meet more than 100 women a year who have undergone female genital cutting. They are usually immigrants. The doctors want to tell families that the cutting is harmful to women. During childbirth, the cutting may require treatment. Doctors are hoped to receive more training for meeting immigrant women who have undergone female genital cutting.

## Sanoma in co-operation negotiations

![Sanoma House photographed from outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7842843)

Open image viewer

1,750 people work at Sanoma now. Photo: Yle

Media company Sanoma is starting co-operation negotiations. The work of 80 people may end or be reduced. The co-operation negotiations concern the newspaper Helsingin Sanomat, the magazine unit Sanoma Lifestyle and the children's and youth magazine unit Sanoma Kids.
