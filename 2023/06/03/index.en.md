# Radio Clear News Saturday 3.6.2023

School leaving celebrations. Indian train accident. Kaija Saariaho has died. Wedding loans. Estonia's stamp about Finland. The weather.

## School leaving celebrations

![Three students stand in front of their school with their certificates in their hands and caps on their heads.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3636,h_2045,x_0,y_18/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1124217647af8a45a369/1685780761)

New matriculated students Julianna Vataja, Silja Yli-Hinkkala and Saara Palomäki from Tampere Lyceum upper secondary school. Photo: Anna Sirén / Yle

In Finland the end of the school year has been celebrated.

On Saturday about 25,000 new matriculated students graduated. They got a student cap at the upper secondary school's leaving celebration.

About 45,000 students graduated from vocational institutes. In many vocational institutes the celebrations were already held on Friday.

The school year has also ended for basic education. About 62,000 young people completed their basic education studies this spring.

## Indian train accident

![Destroyed train carriages, with rescue workers around them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3908,h_2198,x_0,y_319/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1124173647aab2b67b4a/1685789810)

Open image viewer

The accident completely destroyed train carriages. Photo: Lehtikuva

A serious train accident has happened in India. About 300 people died in the accident and about 1000 were injured.

The accident happened on Friday. The accident involved 2 passenger trains and 1 freight train.

Many train accidents happen in India. However, Friday's accident was the worst train accident in more than 20 years.

## Kaija Saariaho has died

![Kaija Saariaho.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_243/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1124090647a07c8debd4/1685719204)

Open image viewer

Composer Kaija Saariaho. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

Finnish composer Kaija Saariaho has died. Saariaho was 70 years old. She had brain cancer.

Saariaho died on Friday in Paris, where she lived. Saariaho was born and grew up in Helsinki.

Saariaho is a well-known composer around the world. She composed over 100 musical works, for example 5 operas.

## Wedding loans

![A woman shows a wedding dress.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_642/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1083887640c8e6f8015f/1685790242)

Open image viewer

At wedding fairs you can get to know, for example, wedding dresses. Photo: Cecilia Juuti / Yle

Finns often take out loans for weddings. Many take wedding loans, even though interest rates on loans have risen.

For example, at Danske Bank it is said that a wedding loan is often between 5000 and 10,000 euros.

Wedding planners say that Finns now spend more money on wedding celebrations than before. Weddings costing over 20,000 euros have become more common.

## Estonia's stamp about Finland

![Screenshot of a stamp on the website of the Estonian public broadcaster ERR.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1311,h_737,x_0,y_94/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1124222647afab434731/1685781351)

Open image viewer

Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Estonia has issued a stamp whose theme is Finland. The stamp celebrates Finland joining the defence alliance NATO.

Estonia thinks that Finland's NATO membership also improves Estonia's security. Co-operation between Estonia and Finland is also strengthened when both countries are in NATO.

Estonia joined the defence alliance NATO in 2004. Finland became a NATO member 2 months ago.

## The weather

![Saturday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1124317647b521bddc94/1685803600)

Open image viewer

The weather is cooler than usual for early June.

Especially the nights are cold. On the night between Friday and Saturday there was frost in places, even in the south.

The weather is still cool, but the coldest air is moving away from Finland.

At the start of the week the temperature in the south may already be almost 20 degrees.
