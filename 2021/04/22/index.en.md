## The EU stops buying the Astra Zeneca vaccine

![Astra Zeneca vaccine bottle.18.3.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3736,h_2102,x_0,y_41/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78592460545a6e33c3a/1616141024)

Astra Zeneca vaccine bottle, 18.3.2021. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The EU no longer buys Astra Zeneca corona vaccines.

The Union had the chance to buy another 100 million vaccine doses.

The European Commission says that Astra Zeneca has not met the conditions agreed with the EU. Deliveries have been late all the time.

In addition, there are suspicions about blood clots linked to the vaccine.

The EU has not yet decided whether it will take legal action against Astra Zeneca.

## Rental flats are demolished with state support

![A half-demolished house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10777427)

Open image viewer

The house is being demolished. Photo: Isto Janhunen / Yle

Rental flats have been demolished in Finland with state support. Thousands of rental flats have been demolished in recent years.

The state can pay up to 90 percent of the demolition costs if there are not enough tenants for rental flats in the area. This is often the case in municipalities that are losing population, where people are moving away.

For example, in Polvijärvi in North Karelia, even houses from the 1990s have been levelled to the ground. The same houses were built with state grants.

## Rent increases are small

![Kitchen of a rental flat taken out of use.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7971606080140f91b29/1619006720)

The rise in rents in privately financed flats has slowed down. Anyone can rent a privately financed flat if they meet the terms of the rental agreement.

Statistics Finland says that across the whole country, rents rose by only 0.1 percent in January to March compared with the end of last year.

In the capital region rents rose slightly, and elsewhere they stayed at the same level.

Compared with a year earlier, rents rose in the capital region by 0.9 percent at the start of the year. Elsewhere in Finland there was a rise of 0.6 percent.

The most expensive rents were in Helsinki: on average 21 euros per square metre. The cheapest rents were in Kouvola: just under 11 euros per square metre.

## Online bullying is common, young people say

![A girl lies on a sofa looking at a mobile phone screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_583/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5147505bb761c322dc8/1680004032)

The Child Welfare League asked young people about online bullying and harassment in an online survey in autumn 2020. More than 1,100 answers came in. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Online bullying and online harassment are familiar to young people.

The Mannerheim League for Child Welfare made a survey for young people. In the survey, young people took online harassment seriously.

Young people encounter online harassment almost every day. Young people want adults to give them information on the subject, as well as support in bullying situations.

The most common form of bullying online is nasty comments, name-calling or insults. The most serious are death threats, rape threats and sexual harassment.

More than two thirds of respondents felt that everyone can have an influence in a bullying situation to stop the bullying.
