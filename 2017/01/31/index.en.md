# Tuesday 31.1.2017 (radio)

The Defence Forces no longer hire Finns who also have Russian citizenship. Almost 400 children are waiting for an asylum decision. Trump fired the attorney general. Collecting sports shoes interests young people.

## The Defence Forces do not hire Finnish-Russian dual citizens

![The Defence Forces' Local Defence West 1 exercise in Laajasalo, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9433703)

Open image viewer

Finnish-Russian dual citizens cannot have a military career.  Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The Defence Forces no longer hire people who have both Finnish and Russian citizenship. A security check is done on all job applicants. If the check reveals Russian citizenship, the person is not hired by the Defence Forces.

Citizenship is also taken into account in the training of conscripts. A conscript is not put in tasks where they can see important military matters.

The new practice is based on a secret instruction.

Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö says that there is no such instruction in the Defence Forces. But he says that the aim is to prepare a law that limits the chances of dual citizens to get military jobs.

## 400 children and young people are waiting for an asylum decision

![An asylum seeker uses a mobile phone in a corridor](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9365710)

Open image viewer

Many of the underage asylum seekers are 16-17 years old.  Photo: Kay Nietfeld / EPA

Almost 3,500 children and young people have applied for asylum in Finland. They have come to Finland without parents, that is, without a mother or father.

There are few small children, because most underage asylum seekers are 16 or 17 years old. The Finnish Immigration Service tries to decide the asylum cases of children and young people quickly.

At the moment, however, almost  400 underage asylum seekers are waiting for a decision. The processing of cases is slowed down by age tests, which find out whether the applicant is really under 18, that is, underage.

## Trump fired the attorney general

![Attorney General Sally Yates ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9432868)

Open image viewer

The President fired the US attorney general.  Photo: Saul Loeb / AFP

The decisions of US President Donald Trump cause a lot of opposition. American politicians and former president Barack Obama oppose Trump's decision that bans citizens of 7 Muslim countries from travelling to the USA. American diplomats and the attorney general also oppose the decision.

That is why President Trump fired the attorney general. Diplomats have also been told to resign if they do not accept the President's decision.

## Collecting sports shoes is young people's new hobby

![Shava](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9431204)

Open image viewer

Shava Sofy from Helsinki collects sports shoes. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

Collecting sports shoes has become a popular hobby around the world. Now the hobby has also spread to Finland. Sports shoes, or sneakers, are collected by young men and boys. Shava Sofy from Helsinki writes a shoe blog online about his hobby.

The young man already has 100 pairs of sports shoes. The hobby takes a lot of money. The most valuable shoes are on display in the young man's living room.

The popularity of sports shoes can also be seen in shops. Shops have been opened in Finland that focus only on selling expensive sports shoes.
