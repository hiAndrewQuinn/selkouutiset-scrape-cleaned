# Thursday 13.6.2019 (radio)

The new students for vocational education and upper secondary schools have been selected. Two oil ships were attacked in the Gulf of Oman. There are big demonstrations in Hong Kong. 17 - Sytten is a new Norwegian web series about immigrant youth.

## Joint application selections

![Ventilation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9091280)

Open image viewer

65,000 applicants got a study place. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

The new students for vocational education and upper secondary schools have been selected. Almost 65,000 applicants got a study place in the spring joint application.

A small share of the applicants did not get a study place. 4,000 applicants were left without a place in vocational education, and 3,000 applicants were left without an upper secondary school place.

Upper secondary schools arrange extra applications, however, and vocational education has continuous admission. In continuous admission, an applicant can also apply to study outside the joint application.

## Attack on oil ships

![The Norwegian shipping company's tanker Front Altair burning in the Gulf of Oman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10830433)

Open image viewer

An oil ship caught fire in the Gulf of Oman. Photo: IRIB / EPA

Two oil ships were attacked in the Gulf of Oman. The Gulf of Oman is close to the Persian Gulf.

One of the ships was Norwegian, and the other was Panamanian. At least one of the ships caught fire in the attack. The crews of the ships were rescued.

Attacks on ships have also been carried out in the area before.

## Hong Kong protests

![Protesters have built barricades in Hong Kong.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10827244)

Open image viewer

Protesters on the streets of Hong Kong. Photo: Jerome Favre / EPA

There are big protests in Hong Kong. Protesters have fought with the police. On Wednesday more than 70 people were taken to hospital.

The protesters are angry because of a new law plan. If the law comes into force, Hong Kong may hand over people suspected of crimes to mainland China.

Hong Kong is a special administrative region of China. Hong Kong has, for example, its own laws. The residents of Hong Kong also have freedoms that mainland Chinese do not have. Hong Kong residents want to hold on to them.

## A series about immigrant youth

![Cast of the series 17 - Sytten](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10825664)

Open image viewer

The main character of the series Sytten, Abdi, and his friends. Photo: Bendik Stalheim Møller / NRK

A new Norwegian web series for young people has come to Yle Areena. The name of the series is 17 - Sytten.

The series tells the story of a 17-year-old boy named Abdi, son of a Somali mother. Abdi lives in a suburb of Oslo, the capital of Norway.

The series was made together with immigrant youth. That is why the series is believable, say Helsinki residents Mohamed Hassan and Abdirashid Mahamed. They are the same age as Abdi in the series.

Yle asked Mohamed and Abdirashid to watch the series. The boys say that the series contains the same kinds of things as normal life. The filming location also reminds them of Kontula in Helsinki, where they live themselves.

Mohammed and Abdirashid think that more series are needed that tell about the lives of immigrant youth.
