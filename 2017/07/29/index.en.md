# Saturday 29.7.2017 (radio)

Children are being abducted abroad more than before. Posti is suffering from congestion. North Korea did another missile test. Role-players are gathered at Ropecon.

## Child abductions

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9746321)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics

The number of child abductions in Finland has grown.

Last year, a total of 46 children under 16 were abducted from Finland to other countries. That is the largest number in 10 years.

Child abduction means that a child has been taken away from their permanent country of residence without the guardian's permission. Usually one of the parents abducts the child.

Child abduction was criminalised in Finland in 2006, which means that abducting a child is a crime under the law.

In recent years, most children abducted from Finland have been taken to Sweden, Spain and Estonia.

## Congestion at Posti

![The Posti logo in front of an office building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7966774)

Open image viewer

Posti is no longer the only company that delivers letter mail. Photo: Yle

Posti has become badly congested during this year. Posti says that the congestion is caused by the opening up of letter services last year.

Now, besides Posti, more than 10 other delivery companies can also deliver letters. If the other companies deliver letters to the wrong addresses, consumers often return the letters to the post.

Posti says that during the early part of the year, tens of thousands of letters have been returned to it that are the responsibility of the delivery companies. Because of this, the congestion at the post has grown all the time.

You may put back into a postbox only the letters that Posti has delivered. To return other letters, you must ask the delivery company for advice.

## North Korea's missile test

![Men walk past a TV screen showing Kim Jong-un's face.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9747741)

Open image viewer

North Korea's leader Kim Jong-un says that the missile test was a serious warning to the United States. Photo: Kimimasa Mayama / EPA

North Korea has done a new missile test.

In the evening, North Korea launched a missile that flew about 1000 kilometres and then fell into the waters off northern Japan. North Korea says that it tested an intercontinental missile and that the test went very well.

This was the 11th missile test that North Korea has done this year.

France wants the UN Security Council to impose new sanctions on North Korea because of the missile test.

## Ropecon

![a woman dressed in feathers and furs](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9748037)

Open image viewer

Barbara Dubrovskaja came to the Ropecon role-playing event from Estonia. Photo: Linda Manner / Lehtikuva

Role-playing fans have gathered at the Ropecon event at the Helsinki Exhibition and Convention Centre. Thousands of eager players take part in the event over the weekend.

At Ropecon you can, for example, play card or board games or take part in a larp, that is, a live role-playing game. A larp is almost like theatre, because in it the players dress in costumes and act out different game characters.

This year, popular game themes include, for example, vampires and the world after the end of the world.

Ropecon has been held since 1994.
