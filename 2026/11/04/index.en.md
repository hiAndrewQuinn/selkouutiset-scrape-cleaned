## Baptism as church member

Next, news from the Evangelical Lutheran Church.

In future a child can be baptised as a church member even if it has no godparents.

A godparent is a witness to the child's baptism and a friend of the child.

Until now, the child being baptised has had to have at least one godparent who belongs to the church. The parish priest decides whether a child may be baptised without a godparent.

The church is afraid that a child will remain without baptism if no godparent is found.

A child can be baptised even if it has no godparents. The Church Assembly decided on the matter.

The matter is still being dealt with by the Church Board.
