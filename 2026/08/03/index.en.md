## Tax refunds of over a billion euros

Next, information about tax refunds.

Today, tax refunds will be paid to over 2 million Finns.

You can get tax refunds if you have paid too much tax. Last year, Finns paid a total of over a billion euros in too much tax.

More than 300,000 Finns paid too little tax. They will have to pay the missing taxes, or mätky.

Today, tax refunds will be paid to over 2 million Finns.

The Tax Administration warns that there are currently many scam messages circulating related to tax refunds.

## Finnish specialty stores compete with Temu

Finally, news about cheap fashion.

Large Asian online stores and Finnish specialty stores sell a lot of the same clothes.

For example, Cybershop, which specializes in a different style, sells clothes that can also be ordered cheaply from Temu or Amazon.

The CEO of Cybershop says that they buy clothes from the same Chinese manufacturer as Temu. However, they cannot sell clothes as cheaply as Temu.

The CEO says that they check the quality of every garment, unlike Temu.

Cybershop's turnover has decreased due to Temu. The CEO says that Temu affects all stores:

– It doesn't matter if you sell buckets or nuts. No matter what you sell, you can find it at Temus, and it's definitely cheaper.
