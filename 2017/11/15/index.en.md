# Wednesday 15.11.2017 (radio)

Prosecutor General Matti Nissinen answered charges in court on Wednesday. The police are demanding that the court remand in custody a man suspected of killing his own child. A dangerous level crossing in Raasepori must be closed. In southern Africa, in Zimbabwe, there is a coup. Rap artist Cheek's career is ending.

## The Prosecutor General in court

![Matti Nissinen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9932129)

Matti Nissinen Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

There has been a rare trial in Finland today. Finland's top prosecutor is suspected of a crime. Prosecutor General Matti Nissinen answered charges in court on Wednesday.

According to the charges, Nissinen ordered, for example, training for his own workplace. The training was organised by the company of Nissinen's brother. That is why fines are being demanded for Nissinen. He is suspected of misconduct in office.

Nissinen has said that he did not commit a crime on purpose. But Nissinen admits that he was careless.

## The Porvoo stabbing goes to court

![a playground](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9929622)

Open image viewer

The killing of the child happened in the Lyseo playground in Porvoo. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The police are demanding that the court remand in custody a man suspected of killing his own child. The court hearing is on Thursday.

The police suspect that a 38-year-old man stabbed a child. The child was 3 years old. The stabbing happened in a playground in Porvoo. The child died of the injuries in hospital.

The police started questioning on Tuesday.

## A dangerous level crossing must be closed

![A devastating accident happened at the level crossing in Raasepori on 26.10.2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9902890)

Open image viewer

The dangerous level crossing in Raasepori. Photo: Western Uusimaa Rescue Department / Police Department

The dangerous level crossing  in Raasepori must be closed. The authorities announced this on Wednesday.

The level crossing must be closed because it must be improved. Now it is extremely dangerous. A level crossing is a crossing of a railway and a road. At the Raasepori level crossing, trains go at 120 kilometres per hour.

At the end of October there was an accident at the level crossing in which four people died. A passenger train crashed into an army vehicle.

## The study grant will soon be reformed

![banknotes in a wallet](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9755384)

Open image viewer

Photo: AOP

The study grant will be reformed at the beginning of next year. Then parents' income will no longer affect the study grant of an independent 18-19-year-old. The reform applies to upper secondary schools and vocational schools.

At present, the study grant gets smaller or is not paid at all when the parents' combined annual income is at least about 61,000 euros.

## Zimbabwe's president was removed from power

![Zimbabwe's President Robert Mugabe inspecting a guard of honour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9932214)

Open image viewer

Zimbabwe's then President Robert Mugabe inspected a guard of honour on 25 August 2017. Photo: Aaron Ufumeli / EPA

In southern Africa, in Zimbabwe, there is a coup.

The army has detained President Robert Mugabe and Mugabe's wife. According to the army, Mugabe is no longer Zimbabwe's president.

Zimbabwe's President Mugabe says that he is in good health, but he is not allowed to leave his home. Mugabe has ruled the country for more than 40 years. Now in Zimbabwe there is a fight about who will be Mugabe's successor.
