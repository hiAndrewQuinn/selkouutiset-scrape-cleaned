# Monday 1.6.2020 (radio)

Restaurants and many other places opened. The police are questioning women who came to Finland from Syria. Sales of new cars fell a lot. Curfews in dozens of cities in the USA. The heat limit was broken in Finland.

Many places have opened today, as corona restrictions have been eased. For example, restaurants, theatres, libraries and sports facilities have opened.

Safety distances must be kept in all places. Fewer people are let into restaurants and swimming halls than before, so that safety distances can be kept.

Hands must also be washed or hand sanitiser used.

## Finns from al-Hol

![Map of Syria, Raqqa, al-Hol camp](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10921861)

Open image viewer

The al-Hol camp is in north-east Syria. Photo: Leena Luotio / Yle

The police are questioning women who returned to Finland from the al-Hol camp in Syria yesterday. The police are investigating whether the women have committed crimes or taken part in terrorism. Yesterday, 3 mothers and 9 children came to Finland from al-Hol.

The police say that the women travelled to Syria 6 to 8 years ago. They have been in different areas of Syria. The situation of each woman is being investigated separately.

The police have collected information about the women, because they have been in the Isis area. The police will question the women within 2 weeks.

Sales of new cars in Finland have fallen a lot.

Sales of new cars fell by 50% in May compared with May last year. About 5,000 new cars were sold.

Car salespeople say that trade in new cars is slow, but sales of used cars have picked up. The number of cars sold will remain much lower than before for the whole year.

## USA riots

![Burned and burning cars on a street](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11379349)

Open image viewer

Burned and burning cars on the street. Photo: Shawn Thew / EPA

Dozens of cities in the USA are imposing curfews because of riots. About 40 cities are bringing in a curfew.

There have been widespread riots in the USA. The rioting started when a Black man died in police custody last week. The rioters say that the police treat Black people badly.

Police have arrested over 2,500 rioters in the USA over the weekend. 4 people have died in the riots.

## Heat

![People celebrating the hot day at Hietasaari in Vaasa today.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3977,h_2237,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6835935ed4ffdab82a4/1591017517)

It has been a warm day in Western Finland today. In Satakunta the heat limit was broken. That means the temperature was over 25 degrees. In Niinisalo the temperature rose to almost 27 degrees.

The heat limit was broken in Finland now for the first time this year.

On Tuesday it is warmest in Lapland, about 20–25 degrees.
