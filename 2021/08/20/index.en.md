## Soldiers to Afghanistan

![Parliament House.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5460,h_3071,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7846326050572bc0341/1619533056)

Parliament House. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Finland is sending soldiers to Afghanistan. The soldiers will help and protect when Finns are brought from Afghanistan to Finland. The soldiers are going to Kabul airport.

A large part of the MPs were of the view that soldiers must be sent to Afghanistan.

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says that the Taliban are already using harsher methods in Afghanistan than before. The USA says that people must be brought out of Afghanistan quickly.

About 50 people have already been brought from Afghanistan to safety in Finland. They are Finnish citizens, they have permanent residence permits, or they were working at the Finnish embassy.

The evacuation of people from Afghanistan continues all the time.

The court has decided that Afghan asylum seekers cannot be returned from Finland to Afghanistan.

The court banned the deportation of 12 Afghans in total.

In addition, the court will quickly deal with the appeals of about 90 Afghan asylum seekers.

## Fish deaths

![Dead fish at Pyhäjärvi in Säkylä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83266760f16681e2e11/1626433389)

Open image viewer

Many dead fish were found on the shores of Pyhäjärvi in Säkylä in July. Photo: Tarja Hiltunen / Yle

In Pyhäjärvi in south-west Finland, many fish died in July. There were tens of tonnes of dead fish on the shores of the lake. Most of the fish were ruffe.

The investigations found 3 causes of the fish deaths. Many fish had an infection that was caused by a bacterium.

In addition, the fish had parasites. A parasite is a small organism that harms a fish. There was also less oxygen in the lake than usual.

The weekend weather is rainy.

On Saturday it rains especially in Southern Finland and Central Finland. In Lapland it rains less, but the weather is cloudy.

On Sunday it does not rain as much as on Saturday. Especially in Eastern Finland, however, a little rain still falls.

During the day it is about 15 degrees. If the sun shines, it may be almost 20 degrees. In Lapland it is under 10 degrees in many places.
