# Friday 15.1.2021 (radio)

You can again travel freely from Finland to Estonia. Flights from the British Isles and South Africa to Finland have not yet started. Final-year upper secondary students in the Helsinki area may again take part in in-person teaching. There are already over 30 million corona infections in Europe. Lukas Hradecky is Athlete of the Year. The frost weakens at the weekend.

## Estonia's travel restrictions

![A sign for the entrance to the Silja Europa floating shopping centre at the Tallinn port.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_267/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7560235fdcc2f137cf7/1608303791)

You can again travel freely from Finland to Estonia. Photo: Jan Erik Kooli

Estonia has changed its travel restrictions. For example, you can again travel freely from Finland to Estonia.

A passenger no longer needs to show a negative corona test result when travelling from Finland to Estonia. Nor does the passenger need to say the reason for the trip.

Estonia has eased travel from European countries where there is not much corona.

## Flights from Britain

![Aeroplanes at London's Gatwick airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11708154)

Open image viewer

Flights from Britain to Finland are still cut off. Photo: Ben Stansall / AFP

Passenger flights from Britain, Ireland and South Africa to Finland have not yet started. The flights have been stopped until at least 25 January.

Finland is trying to stop the mutated coronavirus from spreading in Finland.

The mutated coronavirus is dangerous, because it spreads easily and quickly. There is a lot of the mutated coronavirus on the British Isles and in South Africa. That is why flights from Britain were stopped already before Christmas.

## In-person teaching for final-year students

![Electronic matriculation examinations](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11542461)

Open image viewer

Final-year students take the matriculation examinations in the spring. Photo: Markus Heikkilä / Yle

New information has been given about upper secondary schools in the Helsinki area. Final-year upper secondary students in the Helsinki area may again take part in in-person teaching.

Final-year students have their matriculation examinations this spring. Many final-year students think it is hard to prepare for the matriculation examinations in distance teaching.

Final-year students in the Helsinki area may choose for themselves whether they take part in in-person teaching or distance teaching.

This applies only to final-year students. Otherwise the upper secondary schools in the Helsinki area continue distance teaching because of corona.

## Corona infections in Europe

![A woman wearing a mask, safety glasses, rubber gloves and a plastic apron takes a sample from a driver through a car window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11730333)

There are already over 30 million corona infections in Europe. The figure also includes corona infections in Russia.

About 40,000 corona infections have been found in Finland. On Friday 318 new corona infections were reported in Finland.

## Athlete of the Year

![Hradecky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11728471)

Open image viewer

Athlete of the Year Lukas Hradecky is a goalkeeper. Photo: AOP

Lukas Hradecky is Athlete of the Year. Sports journalists chose the Athlete of the Year on Thursday evening.

Lukas Hradecky is the goalkeeper of the Finnish national football team. He also plays for the German team Bayer Leverkusen.

Hradecky is happy that the Athlete of the Year has a foreign background. Hradecky grew up in Turku, but was born in Bratislava, Slovakia.

## The weekend weather

![Children on a sledding hill in sunny frosty weather.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11730583)

Open image viewer

On Friday there has been hard frost in Southern Finland. At the weekend the frost weakens.

On Saturday it is still cold in the south. In the central part of the country a little snow will fall.

On Sunday the cloudiness increases and in most of the country the frost is under 10 degrees.
