## Unemployment benefit

![At the door of an employment office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11514762)

Open image viewer

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

In Finland people are talking about unemployment benefit. Unemployment benefit means compensation that is paid to a person because of unemployment.

Many politicians support shortening earnings-related unemployment benefit. The amount of earnings-related unemployment benefit depends on the pay that the person received before becoming unemployed.

Researchers say that the period of earnings-related daily allowance should be shortened. Unemployed people return to working life faster if they receive earnings-related unemployment benefit for a shorter time than now.

Shortening the earnings-related daily allowance is one way to save money. There are no decisions on this yet.

The new Parliament has to think about how Finland's money will be enough in the future too. The work of the new Parliament begins next week.

## Via Crucis Easter play

![Actors at Senate Square in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690346)

Open image viewer

The Via Crucis Easter play is a tradition around Senate Square in Helsinki. Photo: Tanja Heino / Yle

The Via Crucis Easter play can be seen again in central Helsinki. The play is performed outdoors on Saturday evening around Senate Square.

Via Crucis has been part of Helsinki's Easter for 25 years. It was not performed during the corona period.

The Via Crucis Easter play tells of the last days of Jesus. This year Jesus is played by actor Panu Haavisto.

## International Romani Day

![Main image of the Romano mirits series, two Romani women and a man.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7479905fc4e2cc9bacd/1606738786)

Open image viewer

Many Romani people wear traditional clothes. Photo: Romano mirits / Yle

International Romani Day was celebrated on Saturday. The theme of the day is Romani culture. Romani people live in many countries.

Romani people have lived in Finland for over 500 years. Today there are about 10,000 Romani people in Finland. Some of them still speak the Romani language.

Many of Finland's Romani people dress in a traditional way. Romani women wear, for example, large skirts.

Romani people have been in Finland for a long time, but they still experience discrimination. During Romani Day the position of Romani people is also considered.

International Romani Day came to Finland's calendar in 2014.
