# Radio Clear News Friday 9.6.2023

Government talks. The Ukraine flood. Shortage of workers in the technology industry. Finnish language in Estonian schools. The weather.

## Government talks

The government talks could be finished at the end of next week. This is what Petteri Orpo, the leader of the government talks, says.

On Friday, Orpo said that the parties are still negotiating some difficult issues. The National Coalition, the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats are involved in the government talks.

Orpo says that the parties still need to negotiate, for example, about social and health services. It is especially difficult to decide on the money that the services need.

## The Ukraine flood

![A rescue worker lifts an old woman from a boat to the shore.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5524,h_3093,x_0,y_345/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11267096481b4cb5f399/1686322850)

Rescue work in the flood area in Ukraine continues. Photo: Mykola Tymchenko / EPA

People have died in the flood in Ukraine. The number of dead is at least over 10. People have also gone missing because of the flood.

The reason for the flood is that the big Kahovka dam broke on Tuesday. That is why the Dnepr river is flooding. Ukraine says that Russia destroyed the dam.

Rescue work in the flood area continues. Finland is also sending help to the area, for example food and supplies for cleaning drinking water.

## Shortage of workers

![Two people work in a laboratory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5868,h_3300,x_0,y_367/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1125900648070df6c5e5/1686139180)

Open image viewer

There are many kinds of jobs in the technology industry. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

More workers are needed in the technology industry.

For example, there are too few welders and machinists. More experts in automation, robotics and artificial intelligence are also needed.

Many technology companies say that they cannot grow their business, because they do not have enough workers.

It is estimated that over 100,000 new workers will be needed in the technology industry in the coming years.

## Finnish language in Estonian schools

![The Finnish language textbook used in Estonian lower secondary and upper secondary schools, opened.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1083,h_608,x_0,y_90/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-112287364785486901f1/1685607879)

Open image viewer

Studying Finnish is now popular in Estonia. Photo: Rain Kooli / Yle

The popularity of the Finnish language is growing in Estonian schools. In many Estonian comprehensive schools and upper secondary schools, pupils now choose Finnish as their second foreign language.

Many pupils choose Finnish, because they no longer want to study Russian. The popularity of the Russian language has fallen a lot, since Russia started the war in Ukraine.

Estonians think that Finnish is easy to learn, because Estonian and Finnish are related languages. That is why the languages are quite similar.

Still 30 years ago, many Estonians knew some Finnish, because they watched a lot of Finnish television. Today the most popular foreign language in Estonia is English.
