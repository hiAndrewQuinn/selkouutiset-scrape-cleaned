## May Day Eve

The May Day celebrations have started.

Today, Sunday, is May Day Eve. Tomorrow, Monday, is May Day. May Day is always on 1 May.

May Day Eve is especially a holiday for students. The best-known event of May Day Eve is at Helsinki's Market Square. There the statue of Havis Amanda, also called Manta, has once again got a student cap.

Manta gets a student cap every May Day Eve. This year Manta got its cap from students of Aalto University. Statues in many other cities also get a student cap on May Day Eve.

The student cap is part of Finnish May Day. Many Finns celebrate May Day with a student cap on their head. The student cap shows that its owner has passed the matriculation examination.

## Crime and accident

![On the map are Kärsämäki and Vuokatti, and the place of a crash is marked.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1106435644d2bab61d48/1682779237)

The crime happened in Vuokatti and the traffic accident in Kärsämäki. Photo: Leena Luotio / Yle

Over the weekend, a crime and a traffic accident happened in Northern Finland, and they are connected to each other.

An about 30-year-old man killed a woman in a hotel in Vuokatti. The woman was also about 30 years old.

After the crime, the man left the hotel by car and was in a traffic accident. The man died in the accident.

Before the man was in the accident, he told another person about the crime. The police are still investigating why the man committed the crime.

The International Red Cross has sent help to Sudan, in Africa.

A Red Cross aeroplane took 8000 kilograms of aid to Sudan, for example supplies for hospitals.

Life for Sudanese people is hard and dangerous, because there is fighting in the country. The fighting between the Sudanese army and the RSF forces began in mid-April.

## Hot April

![A thermometer shows that the temperature is 38 degrees.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3654,h_2055,x_0,y_85/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1106336644cdfa9177f6/1682860526)

April has been unusually warm in different parts of the world. It has been warm in Southern Europe, for example.

For example, in Spain the temperature in April has been as high as 40 degrees. Usually in Spain it is really hot only in August.

High temperatures have also been measured in Asia. For example, in Bangladesh it has not been this hot for 60 years.

In the capital of Bangladesh, Dhaka, it was 40 degrees in mid-April.

## The weather

On Sunday, which is May Day, the weather in Finland turns dry, which means the rain stops.

The sun may also shine on May Day. The temperature rises to about 10 degrees in the south, and it is a little colder in the north.

After May Day the weather gets colder. On Tuesday the temperature is below 10 degrees across the whole country. In Southern and Central Finland it will rain on Tuesday.
