# Friday 5.3.2021 (radio)

The government is planning more coronavirus measures. In restaurants, dancing and singing are not allowed now. Many Estonians have been ordered this week to return from the Finnish border to Estonia. There is already road dust in the air. On Saturday it is windy and snow will fall.

## More coronavirus measures

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin at the government press conference in Helsinki on 5 March 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11823072)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin told about new coronavirus measures on Friday. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The government is planning more coronavirus measures. The government proposes to Parliament that parts of the emergency powers act are brought into use.

The aim is that, for example, in health care, treatments that are not urgent can be postponed. In this way more workers can be found to look after coronavirus patients, if the coronavirus situation becomes difficult.

The plan is that the changes will come into force next Thursday and continue until the end of April.

## Coronavirus restrictions for restaurants

![Microphone and mixing desk](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78120460412fb5ab8d5/1614884836)

Open image viewer

Karaoke has ended for now. Photo: Joni Takalo / Yle

Coronavirus restrictions for restaurants have been tightened on Friday.

In restaurants, people may no longer dance or sing. For example, karaoke is banned.

Customers must also sit in their own place. However, they may go to fetch food and use the toilet or go out for a cigarette.

The new restrictions for restaurants will continue until the end of June. However, they can be ended earlier if the coronavirus situation improves.

The government also intends to close restaurants completely for 3 weeks. The restaurants are planned to be closed next week.

Almost 50 Estonians have been ordered this week to return from the Finnish border to Estonia.

A certificate of coronavirus vaccination or a coronavirus test is not enough to enter Finland. Estonians may come to Finland only if there is a necessary reason, for example family in Finland.

The strict rules will continue for another 2 weeks.

## Spring road dust

![Gritting sand on a street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10743123)

Open image viewer

In spring a lot of road dust rises into the air from car roads. Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

There is already road dust in the air.

Road dust is created when car tyres grind asphalt and sand. This happens in spring, when snow and ice melt off the car roads.

The road dust season has already begun in the Helsinki region.

Road dust is dangerous for health. Asthma sufferers and people with heart disease in particular suffer from road dust.

## Saturday's weather

On Saturday it is windy and snow falls across a large part of the country. In the western part of the country the weather gets milder and some of the rain is sleet or water.

On Saturday the temperature is between -1 and -10 degrees, in the west between 0 and +4 degrees.

On Sunday the snowfall decreases and there is frost across the whole country.
