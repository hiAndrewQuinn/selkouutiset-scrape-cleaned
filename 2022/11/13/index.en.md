The US Senate remains under Democratic control.

The matter was decided in the midterm elections, when the vote count in the state of Nevada was completed in the morning Finnish time. The Democrats win 50 seats in the US Senate.

That is enough for a majority, because in a tie the vote of the vice president decides. Vice President Kamala Harris is also a Democrat. That is why the Democrats win the majority in the US Senate.

## Pipe bursts in Helsinki

![A lonely scooter in a puddle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_198/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10334316370b174a490c/1668330915)

Water flooded the streets from pipes that broke. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Several pipe bursts, that is, large water pipes broke, happened in Helsinki on Sunday. Water flooded the streets and houses from the broken pipes. The pipe bursts also caused problems for traffic, because water flooded the streets. Many houses were left without water.

There were 5 pipe bursts in total in the city centre of Helsinki and in other districts.

Water pipes in Helsinki are old. That is why water pipes often break.

## Number transfers

![Mobile network base station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5027,h_2828,x_0,y_259/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6696485ea9898c728b8/1588169495)

Open image viewer

A mobile network base station relays calls. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Finns are changing their mobile phone operator more than before. Number transfers have been made this year almost 13 percent more than last year.

A number transfer means that a person changes their own phone number to another operator.

Mobile operators say that the reason is inflation, that is, rising prices. That is why many Finns are also looking for a cheaper mobile subscription from another operator. The competition between operators for customers is also fierce.

## Father's Day

![Father's Day card](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_640,h_360,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4421515a057677df668/1510307500)

Open image viewer

On Father's Day fathers and grandfathers are remembered. Many children like to draw a card for their father.

Father's Day has been celebrated in Finland on Sunday. On Father's Day fathers and grandfathers are celebrated. Father's Day is a flag day.

There are about 1.3 million fathers in Finland.

In Finland Father's Day is always on the second (2nd) Sunday of November. Father's Day is then also celebrated in Finland's neighbouring countries Sweden, Norway and Estonia, as well as in Iceland.
