## Assault at a demonstration

![A memorial event at Asema-aukio in Helsinki on 18 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9176387)

The police are investigating an assault that took place at a neo-Nazi demonstration a week ago on Saturday.

At the demonstration, a 28-year-old Finnish man was beaten so badly that he later died in hospital. The man himself did not take part in the demonstration, but was there by chance.

The demonstration was organised by the Nordic Resistance Movement. The police suspect that the attacker is one of the movement's members. The Nordic Resistance Movement opposes, for example, immigration.

In Finland, people are now talking about whether violent organisations should be banned.

## VR and people with intellectual disabilities

![Chairman of Me Itse ry Sakari Lehto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9176916)

Open image viewer

Chairman of Me Itse ry Sakari Lehto. Photo: Petri Aaltonen

People with intellectual disabilities are angry with VR. People with intellectual disabilities think that VR discriminates against them, because a person with an intellectual disability must buy a separate ticket for their assistant on the train. That means that a person with an intellectual disability must buy 2 tickets for a train journey.

For example, the assistant of a person with a physical disability may travel on the train for free, and a visually impaired person may take a guide dog on the train for free. The chairman of Me Itse ry, Sakari Lehto, does not understand why VR treats people with intellectual disabilities differently. Me Itse ry is an association run by people with intellectual disabilities themselves.

## Russia's elections

![A woman walking a dog puts a ballot paper into a ballot box. Voting booths can be seen behind, and a picture of Putin on the wall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9176468)

Open image viewer

Russians voted on Sunday. Photo: Maxim Shipenkov / EPA

Russia has had parliamentary elections. The winner of the elections is a party called United Russia. The party supports President Vladimir Putin's policies.

Putin says that the people trust the United Russia party. That is why, Putin thinks, the party won the elections.

Most Russians did not vote in the elections. Only 48 percent of adults went to vote. Many Russians do not bother to vote, because there is no real alternative to Putin's party.

## The Immigration Service's backlog

![The Immigration Service's sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9097100)

Open image viewer

The Immigration Service is busy. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

The Immigration Service has received many applications for family reunification. That is why handling the applications takes a long time.

Many applications have come because many asylum seekers have got permission to stay in Finland. Now their family members can apply for a residence permit.

The Immigration Service has not got more workers to handle applications for family reunification.

## Electronic matriculation exam

![Electronic matriculation exams](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8791088)

Open image viewer

The electronic matriculation exam is answered on a computer. Photo: Markus Heikkilä / Yle

A new era has begun in matriculation exams. On Monday, students answered the geography exam electronically. The students wrote their answers on a computer.

This was the first electronic matriculation exam. In spring 2019, all matriculation exams will already be done on a computer.
