# Thursday 8.11.2018 (radio)

The European EPP party group has held a meeting in Helsinki. Father of the Year awards have been given out. The SDP is the most popular party in Yle's poll. German band Rammstein will perform in Tampere next summer.

## Stubb lost

![Manfred Weber (left) and Alexander Stubb at the EPP party meeting in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10498325)

The European EPP party group has held a meeting in Helsinki. At the meeting the EPP chose its own candidate for the most important post in the European Union.

The EPP's candidate is the German Manfred Weber. The EPP wants Weber to become the President of the European Commission.

The Finn Alexander Stubb competed with Weber for the candidacy. Stubb clearly lost to Weber.

The EPP party group includes centre-right parties from different European countries. At the Helsinki meeting they prepared for the European Parliament elections, which will be held next year.

## Fathers of the Year

src="https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5343864"

Open image viewer

Kais Zaya is Father of the Year. Photo: YLE / Markku Sandell

Father of the Year awards have been given out. This year the award was given to 3 fathers. They received the award for their work for children and fathers.

One of the Fathers of the Year is Kais Zaya. He has coached children in football and organised club activities. Zaya, who is from Turku, was born in Iraq, and he also works for the integration of immigrants.

The other Fathers of the Year are Risto Tuominen from Lempäälä and Topi Linjama from Joensuu. Tuominen has led fathers' groups at child health clinics. Linjama has helped firms understand what family-friendliness in the workplace means.

Father's Day is celebrated in Finland on Sunday.

## Party support

![Party support poll chart](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10496755)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics

Yle has again asked Finns which party they support.

In the October poll the most support goes to the SDP, that is, the Social Democrats. The SDP is an opposition party, that is, it is not in government.

Almost 23 per cent of respondents support the Social Democrats.

Support for the government party, the National Coalition, has risen to just over 20 per cent. Support for the government party, the Centre, has fallen to under 17 per cent.

The support figures for all parties can be found on Yle's website. The address is yle.fi.

## Rammstein to Finland

![Rammstein in Norrköping, Sweden, in 2013.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10498277)

Open image viewer

Rammstein performed in Sweden in 2013. Photo: AOP

The German band Rammstein is coming to Finland next summer. Rammstein will perform in Tampere in August.

There were 32,000 tickets for the Tampere concert. They sold out in 10 minutes.

Rammstein plays metal music. The band's performances are always a big show. Fire is an important part of Rammstein's show.
