# Tuesday 15.11.2016 (radio)

Asylum seekers are leaving for other EU countries. The National Bureau of Investigation (KRP) has completed the preliminary investigation into terrorist crimes, of which two Iraqi brothers are suspected. In Russia the economy minister is suspected of having been paid bribes. Barack Obama is perhaps visiting Europe for the last time as US President. The flu season has started early.

## Asylum seekers are leaving for other EU countries

![Asylum seekers](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9135302)

Open image viewer

Many asylum seekers have left Finland. Photo: Mikko Savolainen/Yle

Asylum seekers have left Finland for other European countries.

Almost 2,500 asylum seekers have left for elsewhere in Europe this year. If an asylum application was first submitted in Finland, the application must also be processed in Finland.

That is why many European countries have asked Finland to take back asylum seekers. This year Finland has received almost 900 requests to take back an asylum seeker.

However, this is difficult. Sometimes an asylum seeker who has left Finland disappears completely.

## The NBI's investigation into terror suspects is complete

![Facade of the NBI building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8754880)

Open image viewer

The National Bureau of Investigation completed the investigation. The prosecutor decides on the court case. Photo: Kimmo Brandt / AOP

The National Bureau of Investigation (KRP) has completed the preliminary investigation into two Iraqi men who are suspected of terrorist crimes.

The men are suspected of 11 murders near the city of Tikrit. They are also suspected of aggravated war crime. The men are 23-year-old brothers.

The police say that the men were working in the terrorist organisation ISIS. The case will go to court if the prosecutor decides so.

## Russia's economy minister has been arrested

![Russian Economy Minister Aleksei Ulyukayev.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8747556)

Open image viewer

Russian Economy Minister Aleksei Ulyukayev. Photo: Yuri Kochetkov / EPA

In Russia the economy minister is suspected of having received large bribes.

Economy Minister Aleksei Ulyukayev gave the state-owned oil company Rosneft permission to buy more than 50% of another oil company, Bashneft.

In Russia it is suspected that Ulyukayev received bribes of more than 2 million dollars from the deals. Russia has not said who gave the bribes.

Economy Minister Ulyukayev has been arrested. He could end up in prison for 15 years.

## Obama visits Europe, perhaps for the last time as president

![Barack Obama smiling](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9206511)

Open image viewer

President Barack Obama. Photo: EPA/JIM LO SCALZO

US President Barack Obama is visiting Europe.

He arrived in Greece on Tuesday. Obama will meet Greek leaders in Athens. Among other things, they will discuss Greece's large debts and refugees.

From Greece, Obama's trip continues to Germany. Obama's visit to Europe may be his last before power changes hands in the USA. The Republican Donald Trump will start as US President in January.

## The flu came early

![A woman is given a flu vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9291998)

Open image viewer

A vaccination can protect against flu. Photo: Juha Korhonen / Yle

The winter flu season has started very early. Some who have caught flu are even in hospital care.

Many, however, are ill at home. The disease causes a high fever and perhaps also a runny nose, sore throat and aches.

Flu has not yet spread widely, that is, it has not become a flu epidemic, says the National Institute for Health and Welfare (THL).

1.5 million doses of seasonal flu vaccine are being distributed to health centres. The free vaccines are meant for risk groups, that is, for example, sick and elderly people.
