# Friday 25.11.2016 (radio)

In Finland, people are talking about a firm owned by Prime Minister Juha Sipilä's relatives. In Salo, a factory that makes LED lights is being closed. Many shops have had Black Friday. Soon it is time to send Christmas post. Polar night has begun in northern Lapland.

## The Prime Minister

![Prime Minister Juha Sipilä at the Terrafame plant on 14.11.2016](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9316551)

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. Photo: Jonna Karjalainen / Yle

In Finland, people are talking about a firm owned by Prime Minister Juha Sipilä's relatives. It got a big order from Terrafame, which is a state firm.

Terrafame was previously known as the Talvivaara mine. The mine has had big problems, and it has received money from the state. Most recently, 2 weeks ago, the government decided to give Terrafame 100 million euros.

Some people now ask whether it is right that the Prime Minister's relatives do business with a firm that has received money from the state.

Prime Minister Sipilä says that he has not had any knowledge of his relatives' actions.

## LED factory

![Oulu-based Valopaa Oy's ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7146397)

Open image viewer

LED lights. Photo: Pekka Loukkola / Yle Oulu

In Salo, a factory that makes LED lights is being closed. The owner of the factory is Hella Lightning Finland. It is closing the Salo factory in Finland and will start making LED lights in Romania.

The factory's workers are sad. Because of the factory closing, 165 people will lose their jobs. The factory will be closed gradually by the end of 2018.

## Black Friday

![A queue in an XXL shop on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9317051)

Open image viewer

Black Friday brought a lot of customers to the shops. Photo: Yle

Many shops have had Black Friday. The shops have sold goods much cheaper than usual.

The cheap prices have brought really many customers to the shops. In online shops, Black Friday started already in the night. Black Friday interests young people, under 35-year-olds, the most.

Black Friday comes from the USA. In Finland, many shops have cheap prices the whole weekend. They have a Black Weekend.

## Christmas post

![A Christmas post envelope and letters](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7722970)

Open image viewer

Christmas cards to places outside Europe and North America must be sent already on 1.12. Photo: Mari Siltanen / Yle

Christmas is approaching, and soon it is time to send Christmas post.

If you want to send a Christmas card within Finland or to the Nordic countries, you must send it by the 14th of December at the latest. To Europe, Canada and the USA, the card must be sent a little earlier.

To other countries, that is, outside Europe and North America, cards must be sent already on the 1st of December. Then they will arrive before Christmas.

If you want to send a Christmas parcel within Finland, you must send it early in Christmas week. Far from Finland, the parcel must be sent already on the 7th of December.

## Polar night

![Polar night](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5817435)

Open image viewer

During the polar night, it is quite dark even during the day. Photo: YLE

The winter polar night has begun in northern Lapland.

Polar night means that the sun does not rise. During the polar night, it is dark or dim even during the day.

Polar night comes to northern Finland every winter. In the very north, in Utsjoki, the sun will rise next time only on the 16th of January.
