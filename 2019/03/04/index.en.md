## Another big data leak from the banking world

![a large bundle of banknotes](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10615916)

Open image viewer

Photo: AOP

A huge network of shell companies has been revealed in the banking world. A shell company is a company that exists legally but is not operating.

A new large data leak reveals how a group of Russian businessmen created a network of shell companies. Hundreds of billions of euros have moved through the network.

The origin of the money is unclear, and the money may also be linked to financial crimes and money laundering.

Yle's MOT programme says that at least 700 million euros of the money has ended up in the accounts of the banking group Nordea. The data leak says that there have been hundreds of suspicious shell companies among Nordea's customers.

MOT is the only media outlet in Finland with access to the data leak material.

## Cardiac rehabilitation is important

![A woman's heart function is studied with a computer](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8050202)

Open image viewer

Pirkko Laitinen had a so-called biological bypass operation.

Cardiac rehabilitation improves the outlook for heart patients after hospital treatment. However, a new study says that too few people are referred to rehabilitation.

Only 15 percent of patients need hospital treatment for heart symptoms within 5 years if they have received cardiac rehabilitation. Without rehabilitation, 35 percent end up back in hospital.

In Finland only about 40 percent of heart patients get rehabilitation.

## Germany revokes citizenship of ISIS fighters

![Masked fighter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9736747)

Open image viewer

Masked fighter. Photo: FBI / EPA

Germany intends to revoke the dual citizenship of ISIS fighters. German citizenship can be taken away from those who take part in the fighting of the terrorist organisation ISIS. The new law does not apply to ISIS fighters who have already been imprisoned.

## The Reform Party won the Estonian election

![Kaja Kallas at the speaker's podium.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10671930)

Open image viewer

Kaja Kallas in the European Parliament the year before last. Photo: Egert Kamenik / EU2017EE

In Estonia, the centre-right liberal Reform Party has won the parliamentary election. The Reform Party has been in opposition.

The current prime minister's party, the Centre Party, fell to 2nd place. The populist EKRE rose to 3rd place.

The leader of the Reform Party, Kaja Kallas, received the largest number of votes in the election: more than 20,000 votes. She will probably become Estonia's new prime minister, and at the same time Estonia's first female prime minister.

The election turnout was 63 percent.

## Cottage sales pick up again in spring

Prices of lakeside cottages have generally fallen for many years. There has been too much supply. The peak price was in 2011.

However, there are big differences in prices. That is why the price of a good cottage may rise even now.

Last year, 200 more cottages were sold than the year before.
