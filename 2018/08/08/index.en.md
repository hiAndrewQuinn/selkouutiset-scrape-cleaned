# Wednesday 8.8.2018 (radio)

The proposal for the state budget is ready. The number of victims of the Indonesian earthquake has grown. Finland needs more treatment places for asylum seekers who have mental health problems. Chinese people are interested in the Finnish Nightmares comic.

## The state budget is being planned

![Finance Minister Petteri Orpo presents his budget proposal for 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10343471)

Finance Minister Petteri Orpo talked about the budget proposal on Wednesday. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

The Ministry of Finance has made a proposal for the state budget. The budget tells what Finland will spend money on next year.

The Ministry of Finance proposes, for example, that TE Offices get more money next year. That way TE Offices can help the unemployed better.

The Ministry of Finance also wants to spend money, for example, on repairing the railway between Helsinki and Tampere. The aim is that the journey between Helsinki and Tampere takes less than an hour. Then many people's trip to work will become faster.

The government will discuss the budget proposal at the end of August.

## The number of victims in Indonesia grows

![Local residents search for survivors in the ruins of a collapsed building in the northern part of Lombok, Indonesia, on 6.8.2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10340448)

Open image viewer

Rescuers are searching for people in the ruins of houses on the island of Lombok. Photo: EPA-EFE/ADI WEDA

Indonesia's authorities have given new figures about the earthquake on the island of Lombok. The earthquake happened on Sunday.

The authorities said on Wednesday that more than 130 people died in the earthquake. The number may even grow. Rescuers are still searching for people in the ruins of homes, schools and mosques.

More than 150,000 people have had to leave their homes because of the earthquake.

## Depression threatens asylum seekers

![A man looks out of the window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9892404)

Open image viewer

Photo: Seppo Sarkkinen / Yle

Finland needs more treatment places for asylum seekers who have problems with mental health.

Finland now has only one ward where asylum seekers' mental health problems are treated. The ward has 20 places, and it is full.

The Finnish Immigration Service hopes for more money so that it can open a second ward too.

The most common mental health problem among asylum seekers is depression.

## Matti conquers China

![a comic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10339733)

Open image viewer

Photo: Karoliina Korhonen

The Finnish comic Finnish Nightmares is successful around the world. The comic is about a Finn called Matti.

Matti is shy, and social situations are hard for him. Matti does not want to be close to other people.

Now Chinese people are also interested in Matti. That is surprising, because in China there are a lot of people everywhere.

Maybe Matti shows Chinese people that it is quite OK to want peace and your own space. So says Karoliina Korhonen. She is the creator of the Finnish Nightmares comic.

Because of Matti, there is even a new word in the Chinese language. It is jingfen. It means a spiritual Finn, that is, a person who hopes that other people stay far enough away.
