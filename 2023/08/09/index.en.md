## Schools start

The autumn term of schools is about to start.

In big cities schools start today or tomorrow. Today schools have started in Tampere, Lahti, Jyväskylä and Kokkola, for example.

About 55,000 pupils are starting the first year of comprehensive school this year.

The police say that they will monitor traffic near schools during the first school weeks. The police will pay special attention to the speed of cars and to stopping in front of pedestrian crossings.

The damage from Storm Sylvia was smaller than feared in advance.

Yesterday's storm knocked down individual trees, but no widespread forest damage has yet been reported, the Forest Centre says.

However, the strong wind caused hundreds of call-outs for rescue services. For example, in the Helsinki area the rescue services had about 400 call-outs yesterday, caused by the storm.

## Hot July

![An aircraft sprays water over a forest fire, smoke lit by orange light.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4851,h_2728,x_0,y_1111/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114878964c388f272b5f/1691495711)

An aircraft put out a forest fire on Ródos at the end of July. Photo: Mirrorpix / MEGA

July was the hottest month in the world's measurement history. The average temperature was 0.33 degrees higher than the previous record, which was July 2019.

This is reported by the EU's Copernicus service, which monitors climate change.

In July there were heatwaves in the Mediterranean area, in the United States and in China, for example.

In July a new record was also measured for sea temperatures.

## Shooting stars

![A meteor shower in the night sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_570/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115195264cccc01a8e03/1691143237)

Open image viewer

A shooting star in the night sky. Photo: Zoonar GmbH / AOP

This week you can see shooting stars in the night sky. The shooting stars come from the Perseid meteor shower, says the astronomical association Ursa.

Ursa says that the most shooting stars can be seen on Sunday before dawn. Then up to about 60 shooting stars per hour can be seen.

Ursa says that the Perseid shooting stars are sometimes very bright. Some may leave a trail in the sky for a moment, and the colour of some may look reddish.

You can see shooting stars with the naked eye, binoculars are not needed.

## Tove Jansson Day

![Tove Jansson in front of the Finnish flag](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1940,h_1091,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8378086107c3e905ec5/1627898988)

Open image viewer

On Tove Jansson and Finnish Art Day, flying the flag is recommended.

Today Tove Jansson and Finnish Art Day is celebrated.

This day is Tove Jansson's birthday. Jansson was a visual artist and writer, best known for the Moomin characters. Jansson died in 2001.

Tove Jansson and Finnish Art Day has been celebrated since 2020. The Ministry of the Interior recommends flying the flag in honour of the day.
