# Monday 5.2.2018 (radio)

Paula Risikko is the new Speaker of Parliament. The president's spouse and baby have already gone home. A lot of snow has fallen in Moscow. Runeberg Day was celebrated on Monday. Marjo Niemi got the Runeberg Prize for literature.

## Speaker of Parliament

![Paula Risikko](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10059023)

The new Speaker of Parliament, Paula Risikko. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Parliament has chosen a new Speaker. Parliament is Finland's legislature, and the Speaker leads the work of Parliament.

The new Speaker is Paula Risikko. Risikko's party is the National Coalition Party.

Risikko is 57 years old, and she has long experience of politics. Risikko has already been in Parliament as an MP for 15 years. Now Risikko is Minister of the Interior, but she is leaving the minister's job because of the Speaker's role.

Risikko has a doctorate in health sciences. She comes from Seinäjoki, in Ostrobothnia.

Then new news about the president's baby.

Mrs Jenni Haukio has gone home with the baby from the Women's Hospital in Helsinki. Haukio is the spouse of President Sauli Niinistö. Haukio gave birth to a baby boy on Friday.

President Niinistö took the baby and the mother home on Monday afternoon. Mrs Haukio told journalists that she is doing well.

## Snowfall in Moscow

A lot of snow has fallen in Moscow, the capital of Russia. In a couple of days, about half a metre of snow fell in Moscow.

A lot of snow clearing is now being done in Moscow so that the city's streets can be opened.

On Monday schools were closed, because small children could not get through the thick snow. The snow also disrupted traffic, and hundreds of flights were cancelled.

## Runeberg Day

![Johan Ludvig Runeberg](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9340964)

Open image viewer

Johan Ludvig Runeberg lived in the 1800s. Photo: Svenska litteratursällskapet i Finland

Runeberg Day was celebrated on Monday. The writer Johan Ludvig Runeberg is Finland's national poet.

Runeberg was born in 1804. He was born in Pietarsaari and later lived in Porvoo. Swedish is spoken in both cities, and Runeberg also wrote in Swedish.

Runeberg's most famous book is The Tales of Ensign Stål. The book begins with a poem whose name is Vårt land in Swedish, or Maamme (Our Land) in Finnish. The poem became Finland's national anthem.

## The Runeberg Prize

![Writer Marjo Niemi received the Runeberg literature prize for her novel Kaikkien menetysten äiti in Porvoo on 5 February](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10059441)

Marjo Niemi got the Runeberg Prize. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

On Runeberg Day the Runeberg Prize has also been awarded. It is a literature prize that has been named after Johan Ludvig Runeberg. The prize is worth 10,000 euros.

This year writer Marjo Niemi got the Runeberg Prize. Niemi got the prize for the book Kaikkien menetysten äiti (The Mother of All Losses). The book is about the difficult relationship between a mother and a daughter. In the book Niemi asks, for example, why it is so difficult for Finns to touch another person.
