# Sunday 16.4.2023 (radio)

Parties consider answers to the government sounding-out questions. Fierce fighting is taking place in Bahmut. There is also fighting in Sudan. The spring migration of birds is on schedule. The weather is dry.

## Government sounding-out

![Petteri Orpo gave the government questions to the parliamentary parties in a short meeting on Friday morning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_433/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10991996439095aa5b05/1681459605)

Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The parliamentary parties are now considering answers to the questions from National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo. Based on the answers, the National Coalition, as the election winner, will choose the parties with which government negotiations will be started.

The National Coalition is looking for parties to form a government with that want to reduce the state's spending. Petteri Orpo expects the parties' answers to the questions by Tuesday.

Petteri Orpo estimated earlier that the governing parties will be known around May Day.

## Fighting in Bahmut

![Ukrainian army medics evacuate a wounded soldier from the front line near Bahmut.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1099816643bab7f48010/1681632230)

Photo: Aris Messinis / AFP

Fierce fighting is taking place in eastern Ukraine, in the city of Bahmut.

Ukraine says that the city is still under the control of Ukrainian soldiers. Russia said on Saturday that

Wagner mercenaries have got closer to the city of Bahmut from the south and the north.

Ukraine and Russia have been fighting for control of Bahmut for several months.

## Fighting in Sudan

![Smoke rises over the Sudanese capital Khartum.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4618,h_2593,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1099690643a7ec198879/1681555193)

Open image viewer

There is also fighting in Sudan, in North Africa. In Sudan the army and paramilitary forces are fighting, and they may be trying to seize power. At least 56 civilians have died and over 600 people have been injured in the fighting during the weekend.

There is fighting in different parts of Sudan. Internet connections have been closed. UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres demands that the fighting must stop immediately.

## Sleep at night

![An anonymous young person scrolls on their phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_202/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107896763fe02505766f/1677591292)

Open image viewer

Many Finns sleep too little at night. THL estimates that Finns also sleep less than before.

The reason is that people use smart devices and watch television more and more. It is hard to get sleep if you use smart devices late in the evening. Too little sleep causes a lot of harm to human health.

## Spring migration of birds

![Two birdwatchers on the seashore follow the spring migration with telescopes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5476,h_3080,x_0,y_114/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-955506628209c5e1a18/1652690157)

Open image viewer

Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

Many birds are now flying to Finland. The spring migration of birds was late earlier, but now the same number of birds have come to Finland as normally at this time in April.

This week, for example, swallows have come to Finland. Swallows were surprisingly seen already in northern Finland, in Inari and Suomussalmi.
