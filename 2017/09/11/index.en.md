# Monday 11.9.2017 (radio)

Hurricane Irma has weakened, but it is not over yet. Companies are interested in developing solar power and biogas. Finland's only sugar factory needs more farmers. Large murals are coming to ten cities this autumn. Matti Pohto was a hero of Finnish-language literature who has been forgotten.

## Hurricane Irma is weakening

![A satellite image from the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) of Hurricane Irma moving over Florida on 10 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9825717)

A satellite image of storm Irma in Florida. Photo: NOAA / EPA

In the USA, Hurricane Irma has weakened. It is moving north in Florida.

On Sunday the storm still did a lot of damage. It was impossible to go outside, and water rose into the streets and houses. Over 4 million homes are without electricity.

Before Florida, Irma passed over islands in the Caribbean Sea. For example in Cuba, Haiti and on the island of St. Martin, it knocked down trees and broke houses. Almost 30 people died.

Irma was one of the strongest storms in world history.

## Solar power and biogas attract interest

![A man fills up with biogas](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9314290)

Open image viewer

Biogas into the tank. Photo: Markku Ojala / AOP

Renewable energy, such as solar power and biogas, now interests companies.

Many companies are applying for money from the state that helps them develop solar panel and biogas systems. The state gives money because Finland must reduce exhaust gases and the use of oil.

Biogas is made from waste and plants, for example tree branches. Biogas is Finnish, and that is why using it also brings jobs.

Finland has about 3,500 biogas cars; Sweden already has 50,000.

## The sugar factory needs more farmers

![Farmer Matti Pere](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9824354)

Open image viewer

Matti Pere started growing sugar beet this year. Photo: Jari Kärkkäinen / Yle

Finland's only sugar factory, Sucros, is trying to attract more people to grow sugar beet.

The European Union is freeing sugar production this autumn, which means every member state may produce as much sugar as it wants. 50 farms have started growing sugar beet this year. Sucros hopes that next year there will be 100 new farms.

If there are not more sugar farmers, Finland's only sugar factory may have to close.

## Murals are popular

![Millo paints a mural in Jyväskylä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9811496)

Open image viewer

A mural is being made in Jyväskylä. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

Many large murals are now being made in cities. In September, wall art will be finished in more than ten cities.

Murals are usually made by professional artists, and that is why they are often better than graffiti. There is always permission for murals and the artist gets paid for them.

The mural is usually paid for by the owner of the building. Sometimes the painting can be paid for by a company or an association, for example.

## Matti Pohto, a hero of libraries

![a man with a backpack, a book in his hand, next to a water sled](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9814403)

Open image viewer

There are no photographs of Matti Pohto. The artist Tapani Lemminkäinen imagined he looked like this. Photo: Johannes Wiehn.

Matti Pohto is a hero of Finnish literature who has been forgotten.

In the early 1800s, Matti Pohto walked thousands of kilometres around Finland. He collected books. 4,000 Finnish-language books were needed to replace those that burned with Finland's largest library in Turku 190 years ago. Matti Pohto managed to collect almost all of the 4,000 books. Only a few were missing when he died.

Matti Pohto collected books even though he was poor and could not go to school as a child.
