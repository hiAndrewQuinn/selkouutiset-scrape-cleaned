## Kela benefits

Finally, news about Kela benefits.

The amounts of Kela benefits differ greatly between municipalities.

Kela benefits include, for example, housing allowance, basic pension and unemployment security.

Last year Kela paid the least in benefits in Inkoo and Siuntio. The sum of benefits was a little under 2 000 euros per resident.

Kela paid the most in benefits in Rautavaara in North Savo. The sum of benefits was a little under 4 000 euros per resident.

In Helsinki the sum was about 3 000 euros per resident.

The amounts of Kela benefits differ greatly between municipalities.

The reasons for the differences in benefit sums are residents' ages and the number of unemployed people in the municipalities.
