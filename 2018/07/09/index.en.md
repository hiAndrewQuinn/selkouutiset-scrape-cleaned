# Monday 9.7.2018 (radio)

The rescue operation in Thailand continues. In Japan, 1.5 million people have left their homes because of floods. Finland's border control is getting tighter. A warm weather period now begins.

## More boys rescued in Thailand

![Soldiers and police carry a boy rescued from the cave onto a rescue helicopter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10295687)

After being rescued, the boys have been taken to hospital by helicopter.

In Thailand, 4 more boys have been rescued from the cave. Thai authorities say that a total of 8 boys have been rescued from the cave.

Yesterday the first four boys were brought out of the cave. Today it was reported that they are doing well. The rescue is being attempted quickly, because new rain is feared in the cave area. New rain could raise the water level in the cave system. Then the rescue would again be very difficult.

18 divers will bring the boys out. Each boy will be accompanied by 2 rescue divers. The distance through the cave is 3.2 kilometres. The boys will be taken to hospital to recover.

12 boys and a coach were trapped in the cave more than two weeks ago, when water filled part of the cave.

## Terrible floods in Japan

![Rescue workers carry a person on a stretcher next to a house that has tilted over.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10295489)

The floods and landslides in Japan have been terrible. Photo: Jiji Press / EPA-EFE

There have been severe floods and landslides in Japan. It is estimated that at least 100 people have died.

The floods and landslides came because a huge amount of rain has fallen in Japan. More than 1.5 million people have been taken from their homes to safety. Tens of thousands of people are taking part in the rescue work.

## Border checks are getting tougher

![Passengers at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10295332)

Open image viewer

Passengers at Helsinki-Vantaa airport are checked more carefully than usual because of the visit of Trump and Putin. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Finland is tightening border control because of the visit of the presidents of the USA and Russia.

The authorities propose that Finland introduces so-called internal border checks also for the Schengen area. This means that a border check will also be carried out on people who come to Finland from Schengen countries. The checks will last a few days.

The internal border checks are meant to stop people from entering Finland who could threaten security. The Schengen area includes 26 European countries. Normally there are no border checks between them.

## Warm summer weather in Finland

![Monday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10294723)

Open image viewer

Monday's weather map showed that the weather is warming up. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

July is warming up. Warm air is coming to Finland from the west. On many days the temperature will rise above 25 degrees, which means a heatwave. It is also fairly warm at night. Towards the end of the week the temperature may rise close to 30 degrees.

However, the weather is not just sunshine. This week, for example, rain will come to Southern Finland. Flashes of lightning may also be seen in the sky, because thunder showers may be part of it. However, not much rain will fall. Towards the end of the week it may rain elsewhere in the country too.

It now looks as though next week will also be warm. The sea water will warm up.
