## Life expectancy of Finns

![A four-day-old baby boy, born prematurely, at the Women's Clinic on 20 October 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5926,h_3333,x_0,y_627/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1189260653274af892fa/1709120310)

A newborn baby. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The life expectancy of Finns turned downwards last year. According to researchers, the life expectancy of Finns became half a year shorter last year.

Life expectancy has not fallen so much in Finland for a long time.

Researchers say that it is difficult to know why life expectancy became shorter. The cause may be heart and blood vessel diseases. The cause may also be loneliness.

More information about this will be available in the future.

## Winter in Ukraine

![A woman in Kyiv cooks food in a dark kitchen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4955,h_2787,x_0,y_130/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1030195636771a03c924/1697800468)

A home in Kyiv in October 2022. There are many power cuts in Ukraine. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

This winter is difficult in Ukraine. It may be more difficult than last winter. Russia has destroyed Ukraine so that the heating and electricity do not work well in many cities.

Ukrainians have repaired some of the heating and electricity, but not everything.

The UN, the United Nations organisation, says that the power plants work much worse than before the war.

Researchers say that Russia is trying to destroy the power plants and heating plants in the cities. Russia hopes that soon Ukrainians will no longer have the strength to fight.

## Alcohol

![Crates of alcohol bottles being lifted into a basket.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2267,x_0,y_468/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117046764ff0544dad8c/1694434689)

Open image viewer

Alcohol may be ordered from the shop to your home next year. Photo: Isto Matias Janhunen / Yle

Finns may soon be able to order alcohol to their homes, if the government's plan comes true.

In future, people will be able to order alcohol to their homes, for example from a shop, a restaurant or an Alko store.

Alcoholic drinks with up to 8 percent alcohol may be ordered to your home.

Today alcohol can be ordered from abroad, but not within Finland.

The government is also considering whether wine may be sold in grocery stores in the future. Today wine may only be sold in an Alko store.

## New art exhibition

![People looking at a painting by Claude Monet in an art museum.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5987,h_3367,x_0,y_161/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1187665652e918078eea/1706189686)

Picture from the new art exhibition at Ateneum. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

A new art exhibition opens today, Friday, at the Ateneum Art Museum in Helsinki.

The exhibition has art by the Impressionists from the 1800s and the early 1900s.

Impressionism is an art style that started in France. Impressionists often painted outdoors.

Impressionism also influenced some Finnish artists. Finnish artists began to make more colourful paintings.

The exhibition has paintings by famous foreign artists and paintings by Finnish artists.
