# Friday 15.11.2019 (radio)

Negotiations on the postal strike have continued. Game accidents happen most on the roads in November. The Guinness world record for sauna bathing belongs to Finland again. It has snowed a lot in France. In Finland, there is snow only in the north.

## Strike negotiations

![Heidi Nieminen (left), chair of the Posti and logistics sector union PAU, and Tuomas Aarto, managing director of the service sector employers Palta ry, continued mediation of the postal strike at the National Conciliator's office in Helsinki on Friday 15 November 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11070339)

The postal strike was negotiated on Friday. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Negotiations on the postal strike have continued. The employer and the employees negotiated all day on Friday. They said that the negotiations have moved forward a little.

Many Posti employees went on strike on Monday. They have not done any work during the whole week. The reason for the strike is a dispute, for example about pay.

Because of the postal strike, parcels are moving more slowly than usual, and letters are late by even many weeks.

Negotiations on the postal strike will continue on Sunday.

## Game accidents

![Roe deer in the forest](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10178822)

Open image viewer

A roe deer may run in front of a car. Photo: Paula Koskinen / Yle

It is worth driving carefully on the roads now. Game accidents happen most in November.

A game accident is when a car collides with a large animal. For example, an elk or a roe deer may run out of the forest in front of a car.

Last year, more than 12,000 game accidents happened in Finland. Most of the game accidents happened in Southern Finland.

## Sauna record

![Haaga Helia](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2241,h_1261,x_0,y_1362/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6120395dcd7dcf5c100/1573749503)

Open image viewer

There were 101 different nationalities in the sauna. Photo: Rinna Härkönen / Yle

The Guinness world record for sauna bathing belongs to Finland again. Students of the Haaga-Helia University of Applied Sciences made the record.

The idea of the record is to gather people from as many countries as possible in the same sauna. The record had been held for a time by China, where people from 99 different countries sauna'd at the same time.

Finland was now better than China. Here, people from 101 countries were in the sauna. They sauna'd at Suomenlinna, in Helsinki.

The official Guinness judge Glenn Pollard confirmed Finland's world record.

## Snowfall in France

![Alps in France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10881247)

Open image viewer

A lot of snow has fallen near the Alps. Photo: AOP

It has snowed a lot in France. The most snow fell near the Alps mountains. As much as 30 centimetres of snow fell in one day.

The heavy snowfall has caused a lot of harm. At least one person has died because of the heavy snowfall. In addition, the snowfall cut electricity for thousands of people.

The snowfall has also disrupted traffic in France. Authorities stopped trains, because trees fell onto the railway tracks.

## Weekend weather

![woman shovelling snow](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11032883)

Open image viewer

It is already winter in Lapland, but in Southern Finland there is still no snow. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Here in Finland, there is snow only in the north. In the north the snow also stays on the ground, because it is frosty in Lapland all weekend.

In Central Finland, sleet or snow will fall on Sunday. The temperature is at zero.

Winter still isn't coming to Southern Finland. The temperature is above zero during the day. In the south, drizzle may come at the weekend, that is, rain may fall very lightly.
