# Saturday 11.7.2020 (radio)

A senior residence burned in Jyväskylä. Parking is more and more often charged for in cities. Domestic beaches and nature destinations are now popular. It is 25 years since the Srebrenica massacre. The weather.

The TV Clear News has a summer break and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Senior residence burned in Jyväskylä

![Fire in an apartment block at the Palokan Ilona senior residence in Jyväskylä on 10 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11444150)

The fire at the senior residence was put out all night and on Saturday too. The building was built in 2018. Photo: Timo Laulainen / Lehtikuva

There has been a big fire in Jyväskylä. The fire started first in one apartment block and spread from there to the neighbouring apartment block.

The fire started in a senior residence where many old people live. Firefighters took all 170 people out of the buildings to safety. Nobody was injured.

The fire in the senior residence in Jyväskylä started late on Friday evening. The residents of the buildings cannot go back home, because the buildings were badly damaged in the fire.

## Parking is more and more often charged for

![Parking payment machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10975423)

Open image viewer

Paid parking spaces in the centre of Tampere. Photo: Jussi Mansikka / Yle

In more and more cities, you must pay to park your car at the roadside.

City traffic planners say that there are better parking spaces for customers of shops and offices when parking in city centres is paid.

In many cities, parking spaces have been or are being changed to paid parking. For example, the City of Turku intends to change more than 1,000 parking spaces to paid parking, so that drivers can more easily come to the centre, for example to go shopping.

## Domestic cottages and nature destinations are popular

![People at Yyteri beach in Pori in July 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9055006)

Open image viewer

Yyteri is a popular large beach in Pori. Photo: Carolus Manninen / Yle

Finns are travelling a lot within Finland this summer because of the corona situation. Travel companies say that many Finns stay on their domestic trips perhaps longer than before.

Many hotels and cottages by the shore are full in July. Many people also visit nature destinations, for example national parks.

In some towns there are fewer tourists than before, because big summer events have been cancelled. There are now very few foreign tourists in Finland.

## Srebrenica massacre 25 years ago

![People commemorating those killed in the Srebrenica massacre in Sarajevo, the capital of Bosnia and Herzegovina, on 9 July 2020. A 25th anniversary commemoration of the Srebrenica massacre has been held in Bosnia and Herzegovina. During the Bosnian war in July 1995, Bosnian Serb forces killed about 8,000 Bosnian Muslim men.

President **Sauli Niinistö** has sent Finland's greetings to the commemoration.
