# Friday 3.5.2019 (radio)

Snowfall made roads slippery. Plants suffer from the cold. The housing market is splitting. A strong storm hit India. Hungary is keeping asylum seekers hungry. There is a dispute in Parliament over seating arrangements.

## Snowfall made roads slippery

![Pedestrian in the snowfall in Hämeenlinna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10765099)

Snowfall has made roads slippery. There have been collisions in Eastern Finland, and cars have skidded off the road. A lot of snow has fallen in Eastern Finland, 10 centimetres. It has also snowed in Southern Finland. That is rare in May, when spring is well advanced.

## Cold affects plants

![Cherry blossoms.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10203758)

Open image viewer

The cherry tree blooms for a short time. It is sensitive to cold. Photo: Lena Nelskylä / Yle

A quick cooling of the weather can damage plants. There may be frost at night.

Cherry trees and other plants that bloom for a short time may lose their crop because of the cold. Some plants have grown quickly, because last week it was warm. Plants can freeze.

## Housing sales are splitting

![Flat for sale in Lappeenranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10416817)

Open image viewer

Tighter loan conditions have affected housing sales. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

Housing sales are splitting. In popular cities flats sell well. Popular cities include Helsinki, Turku and Tampere, for example.

In many places the time for selling flats has become longer. Housing sales are also slowed by tighter loan conditions. If you need a housing loan, you must have savings in order to get a loan.

## A strong storm hit India

![People look at the stormy sea at Puri beach in India.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10764910)

Open image viewer

People gathered on the beach to watch the stormy sea in Puri, India, on Thursday. Photo: Dibyangshu Sarkar / AFP

A strong storm has hit India. More than a million people have been taken to safety away from the storm's path on India's north-east coast. The storm has caused a lot of damage. From India the storm moves to Bangladesh. After the storm there may be heavy rain and floods.

## UN: asylum seekers were not given food in Hungary

![Migrant by the barbed-wire fence erected on the Serbia-Hungary border.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10078865)

Open image viewer

Migrant at the Serbia-Hungary border. (The person in the picture is not connected to the case.) Photo: Sandor Ujvari / EPA

Hungary is keeping asylum seekers hungry. The UN human rights commissioner says that about 20 asylum seekers have been kept hungry in Hungary. The UN says that Hungary is breaking international laws, because it does not give food to asylum seekers waiting for a decision on being sent back.

## Dispute over seats in Parliament

![Parliament's Secretary-General Maija-Leena Paavola (left), Speaker Antti Rinne and President Sauli Niinistö at the Speaker's podium at the opening of the parliamentary session in Helsinki on Thursday 25 April](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10754894)

Open image viewer

The Finns Party's seats have become a dispute. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Parliamentary parties have a dispute about the seating arrangement of the new Parliament. Many parliamentary group leaders want the Finns Party to be moved from the middle of the chamber to the right edge. The Finns Party does not want its seats to be moved to the right edge of the chamber.

No agreement was reached on the matter today, Friday. Negotiations will continue in Parliament on Tuesday.
