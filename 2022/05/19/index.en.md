The Republic's President Sauli Niinistö and Sweden's Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson have met USA President Joe Biden in Washington.

The visit discussed, for example, what kind of security the USA will give Finland and Sweden before the countries' NATO membership takes effect.

## Natural gas deliveries

![Gasum's gas station in Räikkölä, Imatra.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-956185628384edd74b2/1653110889)

Open image viewer

Natural gas pipes. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Finland is preparing for difficulties in natural gas deliveries. The reason is that natural gas from Russia may soon end.

The Ministry of Economic Affairs says that companies have prepared for the situation in different ways. Some companies can, for example, replace the gas with another fuel.

The gas company Gasum estimated on Wednesday that natural gas from Russia to Finland may end on Friday evening or Saturday. The reason is that Finland refuses to pay for gas in roubles.

## Corona absences

![A woman looks at a thermometer and a corona symptom assessment service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6646575e9709e98df02/1586956909)

The corona situation is not over. Many Finns still catch corona. A sick person must not go to work. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

Corona disease still causes many absences from workplaces. This is what Kela says. Many applications for infectious disease daily allowance are made to Kela.

This year more than 120 000 Finns have already applied for infectious disease daily allowance. Infectious disease daily allowance is being applied for a record number this year.

The corona situation has eased, but the corona situation is not over. During the pandemic Kela has already compensated employees' corona absences by as much as 200 million euros.

## Children's swimming skills

![Swimming school in the children's pool of a swimming hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5371,h_3021,x_0,y_277/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6869795ede2ee10b1b7/1591619698)

Open image viewer

Children at swimming school. Photo: Paula Collin / Yle

Children's swimming skills have perhaps weakened during the corona time.

The reason is that because of corona restrictions, swimming halls were closed for a long time and swimming schools were not organised.

The Finnish Swimming Teaching and Lifesaving Association has asked municipalities about the situation of swimming teaching. Almost 40 per cent of municipalities estimate that corona restrictions have weakened children's swimming skills.

Only a little over 10 per cent of municipalities were able to fully organise swimming teaching for schools, even though swimming halls were closed because of corona.
