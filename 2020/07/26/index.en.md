# Sunday 26.7.2020 (radio)

The Helsinki stock market is starting to recover from the corona crisis. On Sunday, 5 new corona infections were reported. In Germany people were injured when a car drove into a crowd in Berlin. The raspberry harvest will be good. The week starts in warm weather.

TV Clear News has a summer break and it will continue on 17.8.
Radio Clear News is made every day, also in the summer.

## The Helsinki stock market

![The Helsinki stock exchange building](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10006651)

Open image viewer

The Helsinki stock exchange building. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The Helsinki stock market is starting to recover from the corona crisis. On the stock market, shares of companies are bought and sold.

Share prices fell in the spring, but now share prices have started to rise. Prices are rising because people are daring to buy shares again.

People trust the economy, because many Finnish companies have come through the corona crisis better than was thought in the spring.

There are many reasons. The corona situation has improved in the summer. In addition, the state has given support to companies whose business has suffered because of the corona crisis.

## Corona infections around the world

![A barber cuts a customer's hair outdoors.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11465939)

Open image viewer

An American man has a face mask at the barber's because of the corona danger. Photo: AKM Images / AOP

In Finland, only a few new corona infections have been found recently. On Sunday, 5 new corona infections were reported.

In total, about 7,400 corona infections have been found in Finland so far. The most corona infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa areas.

In the world, the number of corona infections is growing quickly. More than 16 million corona infections have already been found in the whole world. More than half of the world's corona infections are in North America and South America.

## Car drives into crowd in Germany

![Police are investigating a car that drove into a crowd.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11466091)

Open image viewer

The police are investigating a car that drove into a crowd in Berlin. Photo: Tobias Schwartz / AFP

In Germany people have been injured when a car drove into a crowd. 3 people got serious injuries. In total, 7 people were injured.

The car hit people in Berlin, the capital of Germany. The police have arrested the driver of the car.

The case was probably an accident. The police believe that the cause was speeding. The driver was driving too fast right in the centre of Berlin.

The police have not found political or religious reasons for the incident.

## Raspberry harvest in Finland

![Raspberries in boxes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5005535b5f23f1e4cce/1532961894)

Photo: Thomas Hagström

The Finnish raspberry harvest is starting to ripen.

On the berry fields of Southern Finland, raspberries are already being picked. In Central Finland too, picking will soon get going.

The organisation of berry growers says that this summer there will probably be a lot of raspberries.

The weather in the coming weeks will decide how good the raspberry harvest in the end is.

## The weather at the start of the week

![A woman walks in the yard of a summer cottage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10078228)

Open image viewer

In the coming days holidaymakers will get both sunshine and rain.  Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The week starts in warm weather. Monday is a warm and sunny day in most of Finland.

However, rain will come to Finland from the west. The rain will start in Western Finland already on Monday, and it will spread to the rest of Finland on Tuesday.

The temperature is a little over 20 degrees, but in the rain it is cooler.
