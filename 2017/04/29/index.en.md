# Saturday 29.4.2017 (radio)

The leaders of EU countries had a meeting about Britain's exit from the EU. A serious traffic accident happened in Ostrobothnia. There is bad weather on May Day. Bad weather also bothers birds. Tampere people have celebrated the ice hockey Finnish champion KalPa.

## The EU meeting

![European Council President Donald Tusk in front of the press.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9589107)

European Council President Donald Tusk in Brussels on Saturday. Photo: Julien Warnand / EPA

The European Union has held an important meeting in Brussels. The leaders of the EU countries have agreed how they want to handle Britain's exit from the EU. Britain has decided to leave the EU, and long and difficult negotiations lie ahead.

It is important for the European Union that the rights of EU citizens in Britain are secured. 3 million citizens of EU countries live in Britain.

The EU also wants movement on the border between Ireland and Northern Ireland not to become too difficult. Northern Ireland is part of Britain and will be outside the EU when Britain leaves the EU.

## The traffic accident in Ostrobothnia

A serious traffic accident has happened in Ostrobothnia in Western Finland. 2 people died in the accident.

The accident happened on Friday evening. A train crashed into a car at a crossing between Vaasa and Laihia. There are no barriers or lights at the crossing to warn drivers about the train.

The police are investigating the accident.

## May Day weather

![Tulip leaves in the snow](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9577808)

Open image viewer

May Day weather is not warm this year. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

May Day is celebrated over the weekend. On Sunday it is May Day Eve, and on Monday it is May Day.

Finns celebrate May Day a lot outdoors, but this year May Day at least starts in bad weather. The weather is rainy and cold, and the wind is strong.

Even though the weather is cold, students celebrate May Day in a familiar way. For example, in Helsinki the Market Square is full of people on May Day Eve at 6 p.m. At that time students put a student cap on the head of the Havis Amanda statue. That is how May Day starts.

More May Day news in Sunday's Clear News.

## Birds' nests

![A swan building a nest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9588052)

Open image viewer

A swan is building a nest on the shore. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Windy weather is bothering birds. The wind is raising the surface of the sea around Helsinki in the Gulf of Finland.

When the sea water rises high, it breaks the nests of many birds. For example, many swans, gulls and ducks build a nest on the shore. They have to build a new nest when the first nest is broken.

## Tampere celebrations

![Pekka Saravo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9589116)

Open image viewer

Tampere has celebrated the ice hockey Finnish champions. Photo: Yle

There were big celebrations in Tampere on Saturday. The people of Tampere are celebrating because Tappara, a team from Tampere, is the Finnish ice hockey champion.

The celebrations were held in Tampere's Central Square. A large number of Tappara fans were there.

The Finnish ice hockey champion was decided on Thursday, when Tappara beat the team KalPa from Kuopio in the final match. Tappara was also Finnish champion last year.
