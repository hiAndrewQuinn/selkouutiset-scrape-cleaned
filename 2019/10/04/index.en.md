# Friday 4.10.2019 (radio)

Decision-makers are subjected to a lot of hate speech. Police believe that the Kuopio school attack is not linked to terrorism. Denying the Holocaust is not a human right, says the European Court of Human Rights. Driving conditions are very bad in places.

## Decision-makers become targets of hate speech

There has been a study about hate speech.

One in 3 municipal decision-makers and almost one in 2 MPs have been the target of hate speech. Most decision-makers think that hate speech is more common than before.

In the study, hate speech means speech that is degrading or threatening. Hate speech is also speech that is based on intolerance.

Many decision-makers say that hate speech has affected their work quite a lot. For example, one in 2 municipal decision-makers trusts unknown people less than before.

Politicians with an immigrant background are especially easily targeted for harassment. Hate speech is most common on social media.

The man suspected of the Kuopio school attack has been jailed. The police suspect the man of murder and of attempted murder of 9 people. The police believe that the attack is not linked to terrorism.

The man suspected of the attack is still in hospital. The man will be moved to prison later.

The police confirm that the woman who died in the attack is a citizen of Ukraine. The woman and the man suspected of the act studied at the Savo Vocational College.

## ECHR: Denying the Holocaust is not a human right

![A German train carriage used to transport Jews to concentration camps during the Second World War was photographed on a misty day at the Yad Vashem Holocaust memorial museum in Jerusalem, Israel, on 26 January.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8637929)

Open image viewer

This German train carriage was used to transport Jews to concentration camps during the Second World War. Photo: Abir Sultan / EPA

Denying the Holocaust is not a human right. This is what the European Court of Human Rights says.

In 2010, German politician Udo Pastörs gave a speech in which he doubted whether the Holocaust had really happened. He received a conviction in court in Germany.

Pastörs appealed the conviction to the European Court of Human Rights. Pastörs claimed that his freedom of speech had been violated. Now the European Court of Human Rights unanimously decided that Pastörs' appeal was unfounded.

The Holocaust was the genocide of Jews carried out by Germany during the Second World War.

First snow fell in eastern Finland in the morning. First snow also fell in Jyväskylä, for example, in Central Finland. First snow means that there is at least 1 centimetre of snow on the ground at 9 in the morning.

In northern Lapland, first snow fell last Sunday.

Driving conditions are bad or very bad in some places. The road is slippery in eastern and central Finland, so you must drive carefully.
