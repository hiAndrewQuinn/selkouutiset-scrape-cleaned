# Sunday 18.9.2016 (radio)

Leo-Pekka Tähti won a gold medal at the Rio Paralympics. Russia says that the United States is putting the Syrian ceasefire at risk. Finland supports the education of women and girls in developing countries. Air rescuers search for lost people to help the police.

## Leo-Pekka Tähti won gold in Rio

![Leo-Pekka Tähti T54 class 100m Rio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9175558)

Leo-Pekka Tähti has now won gold at the Paralympics 4 times in a row. Photo: Paralympic Committee

Finland has won a gold medal at the Rio Paralympic Games. Leo-Pekka Tähti won gold in Rio in the 100-metre wheelchair race.

Leo-Pekka Tähti has now won gold at the Paralympics 4 times in a row. Tähti won his first Paralympic gold in Athens in 2004.

The closing ceremony of the Rio Paralympics is held in Brazil on the night between Sunday and Monday. Finland got 3 medals in total from the games - 1 gold medal, 1 silver and 1 bronze.

The Paralympics are always held after the Olympics. Disabled athletes compete at the Paralympics.

## The United States bombed the Syrian army

![A meeting of the UN Security Council in New York. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7743404)

Open image viewer

Russia wanted the UN Security Council to meet to discuss the US air strike in Syria. Photo: Justin Lane / EPA

The UN Security Council has held an emergency session because of the situation in Syria. Russia wanted the session to be called, because Russia thinks the United States is putting the Syrian ceasefire at risk.

The United States carried out an air strike in Syria on Saturday evening. Over 60 Syrian army soldiers died in the strike and over 100 were wounded.

The United States says that the strike was made against the terrorist organisation ISIS. But the United States says that the strike may have hit Syrian army troops by mistake.

The Syrian ceasefire started a week ago on Monday evening. Russia says that the ceasefire has already been broken at least about 200 times.

## Finland gives less money than before to development aid

![A Nepalese boy drinks from a well.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5385857)

Open image viewer

With Finland's development aid, wells have been built in Nepal, for example. Photo: Ensio Karjalainen / Yle

Finland gives about 800 million euros to development aid this year. The sum is about 200 million euros smaller than last year.

Finland gives aid, for example, to Afghanistan, Nepal, Tanzania, Somalia, Syria and the Palestinian territories. Most of the development aid goes to humanitarian aid and disaster prevention.

With Finland's support, for example, clean water, washing facilities and toilets are provided for millions of people.  In addition, Finland wants to support especially entrepreneurship and the education of women and girls.

Most Finns think that development aid is important. They believe that development work can prevent a refugee crisis from arising.

## Air rescuers search for lost people

![Lake and forest scenery photographed from the air, a plane's wing at the top.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9171617)

Open image viewer

Searching for a missing person can be easier from the air than from the ground. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

If you go to an unfamiliar forest, it is a good idea to take a white bucket and matches with you. If you get lost, a white bucket can be seen from far away. With matches, you can light a campfire whose smoke guides the helpers to you.

This is the kind of advice the Finnish Air Rescue Society gives. There are over 1000 volunteer air rescuers in Finland. Air rescuers are often called to help the police or the rescue services.

At this time in autumn, air rescuers are kept busy by lost berry pickers and mushroom pickers.
