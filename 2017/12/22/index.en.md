# Friday 22.12.2017 (tv)

Many Finns travel abroad during the Christmas holiday. Most of Finns' Christmas trees are grown in Finland. Strömfors in Loviisa is the 2018 village of the year in Uusimaa. The film "95" tells about 1995, when Finland became ice hockey world champion. On Saturday driving conditions are bad.
