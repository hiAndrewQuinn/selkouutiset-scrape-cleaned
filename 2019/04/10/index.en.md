# Wednesday 10.4.2019 (radio)

The election law was broken during advance voting in Oulu and Jyväskylä. A cyberattack was carried out on the vaalit.fi website. Inequality among Finns threatens to grow. Researchers have photographed a black hole in space.

## Election law was broken

![Advance voting in the parliamentary elections.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10722691)

Open image viewer

According to the election law, everyone who has reached the voting place before it closes is allowed to vote. Photo: Perttu Ruokangas / Yle

The election law has been broken at advance voting places in Jyväskylä and Oulu. In the evening election officials turned dozens of people away from advance voting places at Kuokkala in Jyväskylä and at the main library in Oulu.

For example, in Jyväskylä people were queuing shortly before 8 pm to vote in advance when an election official came to say that they would no longer have time to vote. The official said that voting ends at 8 pm, when the advance voting place closes. Many people then left the queue.

Also in Oulu the voting queue was cut off at 8 pm, when some people did not get to vote.

The election law, however, says that everyone who has reached the voting place before it is closed is allowed to vote.

Advance voting ended yesterday. Now you can only vote on election day itself, that is, next Sunday.

## Vaalit.fi website was attacked

![hand on a laptop keyboard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10673889)

Open image viewer

A cyberattack was carried out on the Ministry of Justice's vaalit.fi website. Photo: AOP

A cyberattack has been carried out on the parliamentary election results service system. The National Bureau of Investigation is investigating the attack.

The NBI says that the attack was made on the Ministry of Justice's vaalit.fi website. The system is not connected to voting or to counting votes. It is connected to reporting results. The attack therefore cannot affect the result of the election.

The attack was carried out early on Sunday morning.

## Inequality threatens young people

![Hooded young man, brick wall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10652694)

Open image viewer

According to experts, education is the best way to prevent young people from being marginalised. Photo: Tommaso Altamura / AOP

Inequality among Finns threatens to grow in the coming years.

In a new survey, social and health care experts say that they are especially worried about young people, families with children and people who have many problems in life.

Experts say that education is the best way to help young people. That is why they support free upper secondary school and vocational school. Families with children, on the other hand, need home help.

Social workers also say that quick loans and indebtedness are now a big problem.

## Black hole was photographed

![Black hole](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10732487)

Open image viewer

The first image of a black hole in space was obtained through cooperation of an international research team. Photo: EHT collaboration project

Astronomers have for the first time succeeded in photographing a black hole in space.

The image was created through cooperation of an international research team. Eight radio telescopes in different parts of the globe were needed for it. The observations were made 2 years ago. After that they were put together into one picture.

The picture shows a black hole that is located in the M87 galaxy. It is about 55 million light-years away from Earth.
