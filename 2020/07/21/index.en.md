EU leaders have made an agreement about money.

EU leaders agreed on a big aid package that will be used to deal with the coronavirus crisis. European countries need help, because the coronavirus crisis has brought economic problems to EU countries.

EU leaders decided that the countries of the European Union will get 390 billion euros in support and 360 billion euros in loans. In total, 750 billion euros of money will be used.

EU leaders argued about how big a part of the aid package is support and how big a part is loans. The Southern European countries wanted support, which does not have to be paid back. The Northern European countries supported loans, which must be paid back. The compromise was that about half of the aid package is support and half is loans.

EU leaders are happy that the argument was settled and the agreement was finally reached. The negotiations in Brussels lasted more than 4 days.

## Corona around the world

![A coronavirus test queue in Barcelona.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11456138)

People are queuing for a corona test in the city of Barcelona in Spain. Photo: Enric Fontcuberta / EPA

The coronavirus is spreading around the world.

In Brazil in South America, more than 80,000 people have already died of corona. The country has more than 2 million corona infections. Only the USA has more corona infections and corona deaths than Brazil.

In Europe, there is bad corona news from Spain. There the number of corona infections is growing again.

The corona situation in Spain was already quite good because of strict corona restrictions. New corona infections have come after the corona restrictions were eased. Now, for example, people in the city of Barcelona are again being told to stay at home.

## Crayfish season

![A crayfish hunter holds a river crayfish in his hands](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6247868)

Open image viewer

Now crayfish are being caught. Photo: Juha Vauhkonen / Yle

Crayfish season has started in Finland. Crayfish are caught in the waters until the end of October.

Maybe there will be a lot of crayfish this summer. The crayfish have grown well, because the waters warmed up quickly at the start of summer.

You may catch crayfish only with the permission of the owner of the water area. In addition, you must pay a fishing management fee.

A lot of crayfish are eaten in Finland, and many people hold crayfish parties. The crayfish are cooked by boiling them.
