# Thursday 1.6.2017 (radio)

The Nordic royals and president came to congratulate 100-year-old Finland. A special coin was issued in honour of Finland's anniversary year. Finland's competitiveness is improving. June began in cold weather.

## The neighbours came to congratulate 100-year-old Finland

![The Nordic heads of state wave on the balcony of the Presidential Palace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9643120)

The neighbours. From the left, King Harald and Queen Sonja of Norway, Queen Margrethe of Denmark, Finland's president Sauli Niinistö and his wife Jenni Haukio, Iceland's president Guðni Thorlacius Jóhannesson and his wife Eliza Jean Reid, and King Carl Gustaf and Queen Silvia of Sweden. Photo: Timo Korhonen / AOP

The kings and queens of Norway, Denmark and Sweden and the president of Iceland are on a rare joint visit to Helsinki. The guests came to congratulate Finland on its 100th birthday.

In the morning President Sauli Niinistö and his wife Jenni Haukio received the guests in the courtyard of the Presidential Palace. Many ordinary people also came who wanted to see the royals of the neighbouring countries. The guests waved to the public from the balcony of the Presidential Palace.

From the Presidential Palace the guests walked to Helsinki City Hall, where they had lunch. The guests also watched the All of Finland Dances event at Helsinki's Market Square from the balcony of the City Hall. At the event people danced, for example, humppa.

In the afternoon the guests visited the Hanaholmen cultural centre and also stopped for a moment at the Sibelius Monument.

In the evening President Niinistö offered the guests a gala dinner at the Presidential Palace.

## A special coin in honour of the anniversary year

![Commemorative coin 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9644090)

Open image viewer

The special coin has a mosaic-like map of Finland. Photo: Mint of Finland

The Mint of Finland has issued a special coin in honour of Finland's anniversary year. The special coin is a 2-euro coin.

The special coin has a mosaic-like map of Finland.

A total of 2.5 million coins have been issued. At the shop till it is now worth looking carefully to see if the special coin is among your change.

## Finland's economy is improving

![The Finnish flag on a pole.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8045542)

Open image viewer

Finland's economy is now growing faster than Sweden's economy. Photo: Pasi Takkunen / Yle

Finland's economy is now doing well. Finland's economy grew faster than Sweden's economy during the first part of the year.

Finland's competitiveness has also improved. Finland rose to 15th place in a new comparison that measured the international competitiveness of 60 countries. Before, Finland was in 20th place.

At the top of the comparison are Hong Kong, Switzerland and Singapore. Norway, Denmark and Sweden are also still ahead of Finland.

## The beginning of June is freezing cold

![temperature map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9643036)

Open image viewer

At the weekend it gets warmer in the south, but elsewhere it is still cold. Photo: Yle

The weather is very cold, even though it is already June. Yle meteorologist Seija Paasonen says that the beginning of June is this cold only a couple of times a century.

For example, in the southern part of the country the temperature today and tomorrow is about 10 degrees, when at this time of year it is usually about 17 degrees.

In the north it is even colder. In Lapland there may also be sleet and snow.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that May and April were colder than usual this year. But March was warmer than usual.
