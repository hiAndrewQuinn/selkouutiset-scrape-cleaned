## Defence equipment

![The Ministry of Defence building, photographed from Eteläinen Makasiinikatu](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5664,h_3186,x_0,y_469/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81073460accf5137f22/1621938310)

The Ministry of Defence building in Helsinki. Photo: Terhi Liimu / Yle

Finland is sending more defence equipment to Ukraine, the Ministry of Defence says.

The ministry does not say more about the content of the aid, the delivery method or the delivery schedule.

The needs of Ukraine and Finland's own resources have been taken into account when delivering defence equipment.

At the end of February, Finland sent Ukraine, among other things, 2,500 assault rifles and 1,500 single-use anti-tank launchers.

Today it is a month since Russia began its attack war on Ukraine.

## Children

![A lot of blankets and mattresses in a corridor. Two small children sleeping.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12363709)

Open image viewer

Children who fled Ukraine slept at the railway station in the border town of Przemysl in south-east Poland. Photo: Dominik's photo diary

More than half of Ukraine's children have had to leave their homes during the war, says the UN children's agency Unicef. 4.3 million children are now on the run.

Before the war, there were an estimated 7.5 million children in Ukraine.

The UN says that 1.8 million children have fled the country and 2.5 million have become refugees inside the country.

## Corona

![A vaccine is put into a syringe.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_369/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88489561a0e8e6b8045/1637935415)

THL recommends a fourth vaccination for people aged 80 and over and for those living in care homes. Photo: AFP

The corona epidemic is not yet easing in Finland. The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health and the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, say this.

There are still many corona patients in hospital care, and the need for intensive care has stayed high.

Chief physician Otto Helve says that new wide-ranging restrictions are, however, not topical.

THL now recommends that municipalities offer a fourth vaccination to people aged 80 and over and to those living in care homes.

## Food packages

![Shopping trolleys with a carton of milk, bread, eggs and a pack of Nyhtökaura oat drink](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3024,h_1701,x_0,y_607/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90525161ed7cbcc4f81/1642954040)

Open image viewer

According to the Finnish Food Authority, food safety must not be put at risk, even though packaging labels can be relaxed. Photo: Outi Parikka / Yle

The Finnish Food Authority is granting relief on food packaging labels. The reason is availability problems caused by the war in Ukraine.

Packaging labels may not fully tell what the food contains.

Because of the relief, ready packaging materials will not go to waste.

## Selkosanomat

![Selkosanomat and Lätta bladet newspapers side by side.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12374315)

The newspaper Selkosanomat and its Swedish-language version Lätta bladet have received the Salli journalism award. The award was given for the renewal of the newspaper and website.

The goal is better accessibility.

The renewal was made in cooperation with students of Metropolia University of Applied Sciences.

The Salli journalism award was given by the Helsinki Metropolitan Area Journalists association.

The prize is 1,500 euros.
