# Radio Clear News Wednesday 28.6.2023

Minister Junnila. Election voting. Youth crime. Home care for elderly people. The weather.

## Minister Junnila

![Minister of Economic Affairs, Vilhelm Junnila of the Finns Party, after the plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki on 28 June 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1135640649c20073ee73/1688117301)

Minister of Economic Affairs, Vilhelm Junnila of the Finns Party, after the plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki on 28 June 2023. Photo: Eeva-Maria Brotherus / Lehtikuva

The Finns Party's Minister of Economic Affairs, Vilhelm Junnila, has been given Parliament's confidence. In practice, Parliament voted on whether Junnila can be Minister of Economic Affairs.

Junnila was given Parliament's confidence in the vote, with 95 votes to 86. There were 3 empty votes. 15 MPs were absent from the vote.

The Greens, the Left Alliance and the SDP demanded that Minister of Economic Affairs Vilhelm Junnila resign. The reason is Junnila's speeches linked to the far right. Junnila also took part in an event that had a connection to the far right.

## Election voting

![Plenary hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4999,h_2811,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1131012648c4c350d7cf/1686917217)

A person's gender, age and education have a big effect on the choice of party in elections. Illustrative photo. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Åbo Akademi has studied the voting in the parliamentary elections. The study says that more than 30 per cent of working-age men voted for the Finns Party in the elections.

A person's gender, age and education have a big effect on the choice of party. About 50 per cent of men voted for the Finns Party or the National Coalition. Among women, the most supporters were for the SDP.

The Finns Party was the most popular party in all other age groups, except people over 65.

## Youth crime

![Child Ombudsman Elina Pekkarinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5304,h_2983,x_0,y_2305/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1135284649b123219b7f/1694531019)

Open image viewer

Child Ombudsman Elina Pekkarinen. Photo: Rinna Härkönen / Yle

The Child Ombudsman criticises the new government's plans for how youth crime is fought. Child Ombudsman Elina Pekkarinen says that the crime of young people must be fought more in advance.

Pekkarinen stresses that poverty in families with children must be reduced. In Pekkarinen's view, the new government's programme does not reduce poverty in families with children, but increases it.

The police say that under-15s commit more crimes than before.

## Home care for elderly people

![An elderly person's hand with an alarm pendant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_312/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11336506493fd189c90e/1698058728)

Open image viewer

Home care for elderly people has decreased. Illustrative photo. Photo: Juha Kemppainen / Yle

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, is worried that elderly people get less home care than before. Home care for elderly people has now decreased for the first time, even though there are more elderly people than before.

Especially in Helsinki, nurses visit elderly people's homes less than before. THL is afraid that some elderly people will be left without care.

The weather is dry at night. On Thursday during the day, in many places there will be rain and thunder showers, especially in Eastern Finland and Northern Finland.

The night temperature is between 10 and 16 degrees. On Thursday the daytime temperature is between 21 and 27 degrees. In North Lapland it is warm in places, below 20 degrees.
