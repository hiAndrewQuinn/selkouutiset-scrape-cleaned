# Sunday 11.9.2022 (radio)

There were elections in Sweden. The Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant was taken off the electricity grid. Nurses oppose a law that forces them to work during a strike. Elderly people should move a lot. The weather is dry.

## Sweden's elections

Swedes voted in parliamentary elections on Sunday. In some places there was a long queue of people waiting to vote. Voting ended at 21:00 Finnish time.

A survey says that the election is close. Magdalena Andersson of the Social Democrats and Ulf Kristersson of the Moderate Party are competing for the post of prime minister.

The survey also says that the Sweden Democrats will get a lot of votes in the election. The Sweden Democrats are a party that opposes immigration.

## Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant

![Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_570/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99950963078237467bd/1661436602)

Open image viewer

Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant. Photo: Sergei Ilnitsky / EPA

In Ukraine the Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant was completely taken off the electricity grid. The energy company Energoatom took unit 6 of the nuclear power plant off the electricity grid.

Zaporizhzhia is Europe's largest nuclear power plant. In the war, Russia and Ukraine have fought near the nuclear power plant. Bombs have fallen near the nuclear power plant.

Ukraine said earlier that people should leave the area near the nuclear power plant. Because of the war it is not safe near the nuclear power plant. The area of the Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant is under the control of Russian soldiers.

## Nurses

![Intensive care corridor at Oulu University Hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2850,h_1603,x_0,y_106/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-922554621f4c321ae15/1646220029)

Open image viewer

Intensive care corridor at Oulu University Hospital. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

The government is planning a law, on the basis of which some nurses would have to go to work at hospitals during a strike too. The government has not yet decided on the law. The purpose of the law is that patients receive treatment also during a strike.

The nurses' organisations Tehy and Super oppose the law. The nurses' organisations say that in practice the law would completely prevent nurses' strikes.

Nurses intend to go on strike soon in many hospitals. Nurses demand more pay.

## Elderly people's exercise

![Nordic walking.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10758507)

Open image viewer

Elderly people should move a lot. Illustrative image. Photo: Ari Welling / Yle

Elderly people should move a lot. Research shows that a lot of exercise reduces illnesses in elderly people. Exercise can be light.

Exercise reduces the risk that an elderly person dies of heart disease and vascular disease.
