# Saturday 18.7.2020 (radio)

Restaurants filled up on Friday evening. In the corona spring people ate fast food. The number of drug drivers is growing. Artist Jope Ruonansuu has died. Dry weather continues in a large part of the country.

The TV Clear News has a summer break. It will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

In central Helsinki there were many people yesterday evening. Many people came to the central restaurants and nightclubs who wanted to party now that corona restrictions have eased.

Restaurant corona restrictions eased on Monday. Now restaurants and nightclubs may be open again until 4 am. When the restrictions were in force, alcohol sales had to stop at 1 am. Restaurants may now also let in a full number of customers. Everyone must still have their own seat.

Restaurants say that many more people were out in the evening than usual for this time of summer. The police say, however, that the evening passed fairly calmly.

## In the corona spring people ate fast food

![French fries.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_432/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5079575b9107fa3b8a5/1536231609)

Open image viewer

Finns ate a lot of fast food in the spring. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Fast food has been popular with Finns during the corona spring.

For example, Kotipizza set a record result in May. Its sales grew by about 17 per cent from last year.

Hesburger and Fafa's also say that sales went well during the exceptional circumstances of the corona spring.

During the spring, restaurants were allowed to sell food only for takeaway, for example to be taken home.

Many domestic fast-food chains are planning to expand. If the plans come true, dozens of new fast-food restaurants will come to Finland in the coming years.

## There are many drug drivers

![Police doing a quick drug test on a car driver.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6993605f0ec0c332afc/1594802461)

The police are doing a drug test on a car driver. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

The number of drug drivers in road traffic is growing. In the first part of the year, more drug drivers than drunk drivers were caught in traffic.

The police are particularly worried that the number of young drug drivers, under 25 years old, is growing.

For example, in South-West Finland about 180 young people have been caught this year driving under the influence of drugs. That is about 80% more than last year.

## Jope Ruonansuu has died

![Jope Ruonansuu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6444415e4d271e2e5a3/1582114782)

Open image viewer

Jope Ruonansuu. Photo: Yle

Artist Jorma Olavi Ruonansuu, known as Jope Ruonansuu, has died. He was 56 years old.

Jope Ruonansuu was an actor, comedian, impersonator and musician. He is remembered for many radio and TV programmes, for example the TV2 sketch show Jopet Show.

Jope Ruonansuu has received the Huumori-Emma humour award in the music industry and the Venla award for best TV performer. He received the award for Entertainment Artist of the Year 2 times.
