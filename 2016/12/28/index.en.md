# Wednesday 28.12.2016 (radio)

There is more flu than usual now. An attempt is being made to get a pause in the war in Syria. Bird flu threatens Finland's white-tailed eagles. Big New Year celebrations will be held in Helsinki. New Year's night is one second longer than usual.

## Flu

![A woman lies on a red sofa under a black blanket with a tissue on her face.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8627243)

Flu is now troubling many people. Photo: Soila Ojanen / Yle

Many Finns have caught flu. Flu is a worse illness than an ordinary cold. Patients have a high fever, for example.

There are a great many flu patients in the Helsinki area and in Lapland. Many hospitals are crowded.

Nurse Riitta Lepistö from Lappeenranta hospital says that a lot of flu patients come every day. All the beds are full in the evening.

There is more flu now than usual this early in winter. Cold medicine has run out in many pharmacies.

## A pause in the fighting in Syria perhaps

![A child holds a tin can in their hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9361567)

It is important for Syrians that the fighting ends. Photo: Ammar Safarjalani / AOP

Turkish media have reported that a ceasefire may begin in Syria. The ceasefire may begin during the night between Wednesday and Thursday.

If a ceasefire begins, the Syrian government and the rebels will stop fighting. After that, peace talks may perhaps begin.

Media reports say that Turkey and Russia have agreed on a ceasefire. In the Syrian war, Russia has supported the Syrian government and Turkey has supported the rebels.

## White-tailed eagles and bird flu

![A flying white-tailed eagle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9088318)

Open image viewer

The white-tailed eagle is a big and beautiful bird. Photo: AOP

Bird flu threatens Finland's white-tailed eagles.

Bird flu does not spread to humans, but it kills birds. This winter at least 3 white-tailed eagles have already been found dead from bird flu. The illness may threaten dozens of white-tailed eagles.

The white-tailed eagle almost completely disappeared from Finland in the 1970s. The bird was protected, and now white-tailed eagles are again along the whole coast of Finland. Last summer white-tailed eagles had over 400 chicks.

## New Year at Töölönlahti

![Fireworks against a dark sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9246813)

Big New Year celebrations will be held in Helsinki on Saturday.

The party venue is at Töölönlahti. Building the stage began on Tuesday. On Saturday, for example, Saara Aalto will perform on the stage.

Next year independent Finland will turn 100 years old. That is why the New Year celebrations are bigger than usual. There will also be a big firework display at Töölönlahti.

The Töölönlahti celebration is open to everyone.

## The leap second

![Atomic clock](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9348741)

Open image viewer

The leap second is important to researchers. Photo: AOP

New Year's night this year is one second longer than an ordinary night.

The reason is the leap second. It is an extra second that is added to time all over the world. This happens just before the clock in Finland shows 02.

The leap second makes sure that clock time keeps in step with the way the Earth turns. This is important to researchers.

However, a second is such a short time that ordinary clocks do not need to be changed.
