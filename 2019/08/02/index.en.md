# Friday 2.8.2019 (radio)

The government wants Finns to cycle and walk more. Many people have drowned in Finland this year. Saudi women may now travel without a man's permission. There are many big sporting events in Finland at the weekend. Clear News is at the Pointti festival on Saturday.

## More cycling and walking

![A cyclist on a cycle path.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10286729)

Open image viewer

The government promises money for building cycle paths. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The Finnish government wants Finns to cycle and walk more. The goal is that by 2030 Finns will cycle and walk 30 per cent more than they do now.

Cycling and walking do not pollute the air. Therefore they help to fight climate change.

The government intends to give more money for building cycle paths.

## Many drowned

![Father and child wading in the water, the father pulling the other child in a swimming ring.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10896508)

Parents must watch their children at the beach. Photo: Anniina Wallius / Yle

Many people have drowned in Finland this year. So far 64 people have drowned. Almost all of them were men.

The summer has been sad, because more children have drowned than usual. Already 6 children under 15 years old have drowned this summer.

Lifeguards say that parents must watch their children at the beach all the time.

## More freedoms for Saudi women

![A Saudi woman behind the steering wheel of a car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10629495)

Open image viewer

Saudi women are already allowed to drive, and now they can also travel without a man's permission. Photo: Ahmed Yosri / EPA

In Saudi Arabia women have been given more freedoms. Women may now travel without a man's permission.

A Saudi woman over 21 years old can decide herself to apply for a passport and travel abroad. She does not need a man's permission.

The last time Saudi women's rights were discussed was a year ago. Then they got the right to drive a car.

## Lots of sporting events

![Jari-Matti Latvala at speed in Moksi 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10905160)

Jari-Matti Latvala drives in the Jyväskylä rally. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

There are many big sporting events in Finland at the weekend.

In Lappeenranta there are the Kaleva Games. They are the Finnish Athletics Championships.

In Jyväskylä there are car competitions. The Jyväskylä World Rally is the big event of Finnish motor sport.

The weekend is also important for horse sport fans. In Lahti the Kuninkuusravit (King's Trot) is held. There it will become clear which are the fastest Finnhorses. The Finnhorse is an old Finnish horse breed.

## See you at Pointti!

In Helsinki there is a festival for Yle's friends. The Pointti festival is a joint event of Yle and the City of Helsinki. Pointti is held in front of the Oodi library.

At Pointti the audience can meet the makers of programmes and take part in making them. The audience can also try, for example, what it is like to commentate on a sports competition or to forecast the weather.

Clear News is also taking part in Pointti on Saturday from 10 to 14. Come and meet us then. Clear News journalists and Nasib Naseri, a journalist from the Asioilla series, will be there.

See you on Saturday at Pointti!
