## New ship orders for Finland

![Meyer's Turku shipyard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9222034)

Open image viewer

Turku shipyard. Photo: Meyer Turku

Finland's marine industry is doing well now. The Turku shipyard has again received a new order to build a ship. The shipyard is building 2 new passenger ships for the American company Royal Caribbean Cruises. The ship uses new technology, which is good for the environment. For example, almost no smoke comes out of the chimneys of the new ships, which reduces air pollution.

In Turku, 10 ships are being built in total. There will be 500 more jobs, and in total almost 15,000 employees will build ships.

The marine industry says that it now needs many new employees, such as welders and engineers.

## Child marriages are a big problem

![Afghan bride](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9222089)

Open image viewer

12-year-old Afghan bride. Photo: Farahanaz Karimy/EPA

Today International Day of the Girl is celebrated. Girls' lives are still difficult, especially in Africa and Asia.

The organisation Save the Children says that too many girls get married too young. Girls then do not get to go to school and become pregnant, which is dangerous for girls' health. Young girls are married especially in Afghanistan, Yemen, India and Somalia.

The organisation says that the best place for girls to live is Sweden, and the second best place is Finland.

## Samsung stops making its new smartphone

![Galaxy Note 7](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9160238)

Open image viewer

Samsung Note 7. Photo: Jannis Mattar / EPA

The South Korean company Samsung is stopping the production of its new smartphone completely. Production of the Galaxy Note 7 smartphone is being stopped completely because of serious safety problems.

The phone has often caught fire, or the phone's battery has overheated, even though attempts have been made to repair the batteries.

Samsung says that it is stopping the sale of the new phone. People are asked to stop using the Note 7 phone at once, to avoid harm.

For the first time, Samsung has had to withdraw a phone from sale. Samsung's share price has fallen a lot on the stock market.

## Even a little exercise is good for a child

![A girl jumps for distance.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8993733)

Open image viewer

Children must move for at least 2 hours a day. Photo: Seppo Haavisto / AOP

It is worth children moving at least a little every day. Even a short exercise break a day reduces the amount of fat in children and improves their fitness. A study by the University of Eastern Finland reveals that the changes are big in 6–8-year-old children.

It is good for children to move gently every day for at least 2 hours. In addition, it is good to do heavy exercise, which makes you properly sweat, for at least an hour a day.

Light exercise means, for example, walking and calm play in the sandpit. Heavy exercise includes climbing and running.
