## Maybe a ceasefire in Syria

There are attempts to get a ceasefire in the Syrian war. During a ceasefire, there is no fighting.

The USA and Russia have agreed on a ceasefire. They want the ceasefire to start on Monday and last a week.

The aim is that the Syrian government and the opposition stop fighting each other for the time of the ceasefire. In the Syrian war, Russia supports the Syrian government and the USA supports the Syrian opposition.

## The fire in Bangladesh

![Crying women carry pictures of people who died when a factory collapsed. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9158083)

There has been a bad fire in Bangladesh in Asia. Over 20 people died in the fire. The fire was in a factory that makes packaging materials, for example for clothes.

The clothing industry is important for Bangladesh. But safety in the factories is poor, and there are many accidents and fires.

## Compensation to employers for maternity leave

![A pregnant woman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9154472)

Open image viewer

A company has costs when an employee goes on maternity leave. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The government is planning compensation for an employer whose employee goes on maternity leave. The plan is that the employer gets 2500 euros when the employee's maternity leave starts. The compensation may start to be paid from the beginning of next year.

With the compensation, the government wants to support companies that have many female employees. Many employers say that the government's plan is good. Not all employees agree. For example, the employee organisation STTK says that bigger actions are needed for equality.

In Finland, people have long talked about family leave being a bigger burden on women's employers than on men's.

## Food gets dirty on the floor right away

![Pick-and-mix sweets on a shop shelf.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7166288)

Open image viewer

Do you dare to eat a sweet if it falls on the floor? Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Food starts to get dirty as soon as it falls on the floor. This is what a new American study says.

Many believe that bacteria do not have time to get onto food if the food is on the floor for less than 5 seconds. But the study showed that on the floor, bacteria get onto food right away. Bacteria get onto moist food the fastest, because bacteria move with moisture.

But it is not very dangerous to eat food that has fallen on the floor. If you pick the food up off the floor right away, there are not yet very many bacteria on it.

## Silver for Finland

![Marjaana Heikkinen at the Rio Paralympics](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9157981)

Open image viewer

Marjaana Heikkinen threw the javelin 18.42 metres in Rio and won a silver medal. Photo: Paralympic Committee

Finland has won its first medal at the Rio Paralympics. Disabled athletes compete at the Paralympics.

Finland got a silver medal when Marjaana Heikkinen was the second best in the women's javelin. The winner of the women's javelin competition was Zou Lijuan from China.

Marjaana Heikkinen uses a wheelchair because she cannot walk. She competes in a class where the competitors sit when they throw the javelin.
