# Tuesday 2.2.2021 (radio)

Anu Vehviläinen continues as Speaker of Parliament. MPs do not support mandatory corona vaccination. There is a snowstorm in the USA. Here in Finland it is beautiful winter weather.

Finland's parliament, the Eduskunta, has chosen a Speaker. The Speaker leads the work of Parliament.

Anu Vehviläinen continues in the role of Speaker. Vehviläinen is from the Centre Party.

Parliament officially starts its work for this year tomorrow, Wednesday. Then President Sauli Niinistö opens the parliamentary session.

## Finland's corona figures

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that 302 new corona infections have been found in Finland. In total, almost 46,000 corona infections have already been found in Finland.

On Tuesday it was also reported that there are 7 new corona deaths. A total of 684 people have died of corona in Finland.

## No mandatory corona vaccination

![Corona vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7638236006c35dac5d3/1612781627)

Open image viewer

Finland is not planning for corona vaccination to become compulsory. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

MPs do not support mandatory corona vaccination.

Yle asked MPs whether the corona vaccination should be compulsory for everyone in Finland. More than 90 percent of respondents said that mandatory corona vaccination is not needed.

In the respondents' opinion it is better to give the corona vaccine to people who want it themselves. Most Finns want the corona vaccine.

MPs say that people need information about the corona vaccine. In the MPs' opinion, compulsion does not encourage anyone to take the vaccine.

The question was asked of MPs because they decide what is laid down in Finnish law.

## Snowstorm in the USA

![A woman pulls a child on a sled in the middle of a snowstorm in New York.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11767308)

Open image viewer

On the east coast of the USA half a metre of snow has fallen. Photo: Justin Heiman / AFP

There has been a snowstorm in the USA. Snow has fallen on the east coast of the country, as much as half a metre.

In New York and New Jersey a state of emergency was declared because of the snowstorm. Authorities asked people not to travel unnecessarily. Schools were also closed and corona vaccinations were stopped.

The snowstorm has also badly disturbed traffic in the USA. For example, many flights have been cancelled.

Here in Finland it is beautiful winter weather.

On Wednesday it will snow in the eastern part of the country. Elsewhere the weather is dry.

In the west the cloudiness on Wednesday varies. In Lapland the sun shines.

The frost is between 10 and 15 degrees. In the clear areas of Lapland the frost sharpens on Wednesday in places to 25 degrees.
