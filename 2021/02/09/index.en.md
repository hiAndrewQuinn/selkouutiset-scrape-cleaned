## Loneliness

![Man at the edge of a field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11654831)

Open image viewer

The coronavirus has increased people's loneliness. Photo: Eleni Paspatis / Yle

People are lonelier than before. The reason is the many restrictions that exist because of the coronavirus. People see each other less than before.

In Finland, almost every 3rd person is lonely. The Finnish Red Cross says that especially people over 65 are lonelier than before the coronavirus. Many people with low incomes, without a family and without work are also lonely.

Taloustutkimus studied in January how the coronavirus pandemic has increased loneliness.

## Origin of the coronavirus pandemic

![A guard tries to stop a photographer from taking pictures in front of the Wuhan Institute of Virology.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11779835)

Open image viewer

A guard tries to stop a photographer from taking pictures in front of the Wuhan Institute of Virology. Photo: Roman Pilipey / AOP

Researchers do not know which animal the coronavirus passed from to humans.

The World Health Organization, WHO, has investigated the start of the coronavirus pandemic in Wuhan, China. So the investigation has not been successful.

However, researchers know that the virus did not come out of a laboratory.

The coronavirus may have passed to humans from a bat. However, there are not many bats in Wuhan. WHO has also studied raccoon dogs and cat-like animals.

Researchers say that the virus may have spread from frozen meat. However, this is not certain information. WHO continues its studies.

## Bird flu

![Whooper swans in icy water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3072,h_1728,x_0,y_250/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7634916005935f82eb7/1610982789)

Open image viewer

Whooper swans. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

A whooper swan with bird flu has been found in Helsinki. The swan was found in Eläintarhanlahti bay. The bird was dead when it was found.

Earlier, pheasants with bird flu were found in Janakkala in Kanta-Häme.

Bird flu kills a large part of the birds that catch bird flu. Bird flu can spread from birds to chickens.

Bird flu does not spread easily to humans. Because of this, the risk of bird flu for people is really small.
