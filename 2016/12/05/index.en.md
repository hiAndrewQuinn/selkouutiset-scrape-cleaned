# Monday 5.12.2016 (radio)

Finland's Independence Day is celebrated on Tuesday. Waiter students help at the Presidential Palace reception. The police are investigating the Imatra shooting. Singer Saara Aalto came to visit Finland. A Chinese-language magazine has been published in Finland for Chinese tourists.

## Independence Day

![The flag of Finland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9229020)

Open image viewer

Independent Finland turns 99. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

The biggest celebration of Independence Day is at the Presidential Palace. President Sauli Niinistö has invited about 1800 guests to the Presidential Palace.

Finns are interested in the guests of the Palace reception. Many watch the Palace reception on television. Every year the Palace reception gets more viewers than any other programme.

Finland became independent 99 years ago, on the 6th of December 1917. Finland became independent from Russia, which Finland was part of for about 100 years. Before that, Finland was part of Sweden for over 600 years.

Next year Finland has a jubilee year, when independent Finland turns 100.

## The Palace reception's trainees

![Students of the Lapland tourism college leaving for the Palace reception in 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9334708)

Open image viewer

The students are looking forward to their Independence Day work at the Presidential Palace. Photo: Marjukka Talvitie / Yle

The students of the Lapland tourism college are going to have an exciting Independence Day. 20 students are travelling from Rovaniemi to Helsinki. The trip takes them to the Presidential Palace.

The students help with serving at the Palace reception. They are restaurant waiter students and get to do a work placement at the Presidential Palace.

The students are happy. The Presidential Palace looks great on a CV, they say.

## Investigations continue in Imatra

![Candles on the pedestrian street in Imatra.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9335054)

Open image viewer

At this place, a man killed 3 women. Photo: Ville Toijonen / Yle

The police are continuing to investigate the Imatra shooting. A man shot 3 women in the centre of Imatra at the weekend.

On Monday, the police questioned the man who is suspected of being the shooter. However, the police do not yet know why the man killed the women. The reason may be mental health problems.

## Saara in Finland

![Saara Aalto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9336345)

Open image viewer

Saara Aalto at Helsinki airport on Monday. Photo: Timo Korhonen / AOP

Finnish singer Saara Aalto has made it to the final of the X Factor programme in Britain. The final is next weekend in London.

On Monday Saara Aalto came to Helsinki. A big crowd of fans was waiting for Saara at the airport. Saara Aalto got a big star's welcome.

Saara told her fans that she will perform in the evening in Helsinki's Senate Square.

## A Chinese-language magazine

![A Chinese tourist taking a picture at Ivalo airport in spring 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8738936)

Open image viewer

Chinese tourists at Ivalo airport in Lapland. Photo: Pirita Näkkäläjärvi / Yle

A Chinese-language magazine, Santa24, is now also published in Finland.

The magazine is made for Chinese tourists. The new magazine tells about services and also, for example, about Finnish education and design.

The themes of the first issue are Lapland and Helsinki. They interest Chinese tourists the most.

The Chinese-language magazine comes out quite rarely. The summer issue comes out in May next year and the winter issue in November. In addition, the magazine will get Chinese-language web pages.
