# Thursday 9.1.2020 (radio)

Iran's crashed plane caught fire before it fell. New evacuation orders were issued in Australia. In Finland 90 people drowned last year. A record number of women have leading roles in Hollywood's hit films.

## Iran's crashed plane was on fire before the dive

![Parts of the crashed plane were examined near Tehran airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11149132)

There is new information about Iran's plane crash.

The passenger plane caught fire before it fell to the ground. The plane also tried to turn back to the airport. Iranian investigators say this.

The investigators say that a technical fault came up soon after take-off. The investigators do not say what the technical fault was. However, the plane did not send an emergency message.

So far no signs have been found that the accident is linked to the conflict between Iran and the United States.

The accident happened on Wednesday and 176 people died. The plane was on its way from Tehran in Iran to Kyiv in Ukraine.

## Warning of new bushfires in Australia

Fears are growing of new bushfires in Australia.

From Friday it is going to be a very hot day in the eastern parts of Australia. The temperature can rise above 40 degrees. The wind is also strong.

Hot and windy weather makes fires worse. There are already almost 150 fires in the bush in Australia at the moment.

The authorities say that people should leave their homes in some areas. There is a state of emergency in the state of Victoria.

At least 26 people have died in the Australian bushfires. Thousands of people have lost their homes.

## 90 people drowned in Finland last year

![A lifebuoy on the riverbank.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11149496)

Open image viewer

Between May and August, 56 drownings were recorded. Photo: Linus Hoffman / Yle

In Finland 90 people drowned last year. In 2018, 94 people drowned, and in 2017 there were 83 drownings.

The Finnish Swimming Instruction and Life Saving Association says that many more men than women drowned. More people drown in summer than in other seasons. Between May and August there were 56 drownings.

Most people drown when they go swimming or boating. In winter 6 people fell through the ice, and there were 3 diving accidents.

## Women have more leading roles in hit films

![Little Women, directed by Greta Gerwig, will have its Finnish premiere at the end of January.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11149728)

Little Women, directed by Greta Gerwig, will have its Finnish premiere at the end of January.

Women get the leading role in an international hit film more often than before.

A study shows that women played 40 per cent of the leading roles in last year's hit films. In 2011, for example, a woman had the leading role in only 11 per cent of hit films.

Equality has not yet been achieved in Hollywood. For example, women have only about 30 per cent of all speaking roles.

Most of the female leads in films, in turn, are white women.
