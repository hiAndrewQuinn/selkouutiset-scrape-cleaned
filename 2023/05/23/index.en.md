# Radio Clear News Tuesday 23.5.2023

Government negotiations. Traffic accidents. Tram for Vantaa. Flights in France. The weather.

## Government negotiations

![Government negotiators at the chairman's table at the House of Estates.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5147,h_2895,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1117519646b58c62c373/1684757211)

Government negotiations are held at the House of Estates in Helsinki. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Government negotiators will discuss immigration and climate matters in the coming days. Government formateur, National Coalition's Petteri Orpo, says that the Finns Party wants to discuss these matters.

Negotiations on other matters will be suspended. Immigration and climate matters are the most difficult issues in the government negotiations. For example, the Finns Party and RKP strongly disagree on immigration.

Petteri Orpo's goal is that the government programme is ready by Midsummer at the latest.

Finland's roads claimed 8 lives in April. 3 of them were in a passenger car and 5 in a van when the accident happened.

In traffic in April there were almost 170 accidents in which someone was injured. Statistics Finland says that over 200 people were injured in accidents in total.

## Tram for Vantaa

![A tram line planned for Vantaa in an illustration on Kielotie in Tikkurila.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1280,h_720,x_0,y_59/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-108776464188a33aa491/1684850299)

Open image viewer

A tram line planned for Vantaa in an illustration on Kielotie in Tikkurila. Photo: City of Vantaa

Vantaa will get a tram. Vantaa city council decided late on Monday that a tram line will be built in the city.

Building the tram line costs about 600 million euros.

The route runs from Helsinki-Vantaa airport to Mellunmäki metro station.

Building the tram line starts, according to the plans, in autumn 2024. The tram is supposed to run in Vantaa in 2029.

## Flights in France

src="https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-42317531972b8c1dea/1394194877"

Open image viewer

France bans short domestic flights. Illustrative image. Photo: Reuters TV

France bans people from short domestic flights.

France bans flight routes that can be travelled by train in less than 2.5 hours. The ban is part of France's climate law.

The ban ends flights between Paris and, for example, Nantes, Lyon and Bordeaux. The law says that trains must run between these cities often enough. More people must also fit on the trains than before.

The weather is dry and sunny in a large part of the country. In the evening it rains in Lapland.

It is over 20 degrees on Wednesday. In the south it may be a heat wave, that is, the temperature may be over 25 degrees. In the north it is warm in places, about 15 degrees.
