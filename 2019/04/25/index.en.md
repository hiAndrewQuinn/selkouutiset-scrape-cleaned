# Thursday 25.4.2019 (radio)

President Sauli Niinistö opened the parliamentary session in Parliament. News Class Day has been celebrated at Yle. Students of adult basic education at Helsinki Adult Education Centre have looked into matters of basic education for adult immigrants.

Today on Thursday it is News Class Day at Yle. Plain-language news also has new journalists today. They will soon tell about matters of basic education for adult immigrants.

These are the first parliamentary sessions of the new Parliament. Finns elected the new MPs in the elections about 2 weeks ago.

## News Class Day

So today on Thursday at Yle it is News Class Day. Schoolchildren take part in news work and tell about things that are important to them.

Students of adult basic education at Helsinki Adult Education Centre are making plain-language news. Here in the studio are Ibrahim Nanai, Mohamad Nanai and Mahamoud Ali Husein.

(Ibrahim)

We wanted to find out about matters of basic education for adult immigrants. We went to the National Agency for Education and interviewed education councillor Katri Kuukka. We talked, for example, about how people cope with studying.

(Mohamad)

I asked Katri Kuukka whether there could be fewer foreign languages if you take more Finnish courses. Katri Kuukka said that foreign languages are also needed. In adult basic education there are already many Finnish courses, and also foreign languages, for example English, are important to the students.

(Ibrahim)

Adult basic education lasts perhaps 3 years. I asked whether, in Katri Kuukka's opinion, you can learn everything properly in such a short time. Kuukka said that a good teacher is important. Different students come to adult basic education. The teacher must recognise what the student already knows and how much time the student needs. The student's own motivation is also important, so that they can study well.

(Mahamoud)

In adult basic education the days are long. I asked what Katri Kuukka thinks, whether the student still has the energy to do homework after a long day. Kuukka said that studying is hard when everything has to be done in a foreign language. It is important that there are breaks during the school day. The school must give opportunities to rest.

**Thank you, Mahamoud Ali Husein, Ibrahim Nanai and Mohamad Nanai!**
