# Radio Clear News Saturday 22.4.2023

President Niinistö. Centre chairman Saarikko. Traffic monitoring. Wildfire risk. The weather.

## Niinistö interview

![President Sauli Niinistö as a guest on Ykkösaamu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_198/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-110271064438a5dd38f3/1682148088)

The discussion about Russia's actions in sea areas continues. President Sauli Niinistö believes that Russia is examining the northern sea areas.

In an interview with Yle on Saturday, Niinistö said that Russia is interested in, for example, telecommunications cables and electricity cables on the seabed.

According to the President, cables are very important for many countries. That is why it is good to think about how they can be defended.

Nordic TV companies reported this week on the movements of Russian ships in the Baltic Sea and the North Sea. Russia wants to know how it can damage cables, for example during a war.

## Saarikko carries on

![Annika Saarikko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3996,h_2247,x_4,y_410/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10927036425da1c2615e/1680202408)

Centre chairman Annika Saarikko continues to lead the party. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Centre chairman Annika Saarikko continues in the post of chairman. Saarikko told about this on Saturday.

The Centre lost in the parliamentary elections in early April. The party lost 8 MPs' seats.

That is why many have asked whether Saarikko will step down from the party chairmanship. Saarikko herself says that she wants to take responsibility for the party.

The Centre lost support in the election, especially to the Finns Party. In Saarikko's view the political battle between the Centre and the Finns Party has only now begun.

## Traffic monitoring

![Motorcycle police officer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5567,h_3131,x_0,y_255/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4998485b580f0670bf7/1619596414)

Police are monitoring traffic. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The police are monitoring traffic more closely than usual during the weekend. The police are especially monitoring how young people drive in traffic.

The police remind young people that too high a driving speed often leads to a dangerous situation. In that case the driver may get a driving ban. For example, in a summer job it can be a disadvantage for a young person if they are not allowed to drive a car.

## Wildfire risk

![Blue smoke rises from the terrain. A person walks near a campfire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2568,h_1444,x_0,y_239/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1100194643d211fcab67/1681728024)

A terrain fire broke out in Ylöjärvi last Monday. Photo: Juha Kokkala / Yle

The risk of forest fires and grassland fires is high in southern Finland, says the Finnish Meteorological Institute. That is why you must not light a fire outdoors, for example a campfire.

The terrain now catches fire easily, because it is dry. In southern Finland it has not rained during April.

## The weather

![Sunday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11028916443f773570d1/1682176011)

On Sunday the weather becomes cloudy. In the afternoon in the central parts of the country a little rain or sleet may fall.

On Sunday evening the rain increases. In the north it snows, in the south it rains.

The rain stops by Monday afternoon. On Monday the weather is still cloudy, but in Lapland and in the south the sun may shine a little.

In the coming days the temperature in the south is about 10 degrees, and in the central part of the country about 5 degrees. In Lapland the temperature rises only a little above zero.
