## Hacking

![On the screen of a computer in dim, greenish light, a figure using the machine is visible from the head and back and hands on the keyboard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11616713)

The number of hacking cases has grown a lot. Photo: Sascha Steinbach / EPA

The police manage to solve only a small part of the hacking cases.

Last year the police investigated about 1500 hacking cases. Only 4 percent of the cases were solved.

The number of hacking cases has grown a lot. The police say that behind them are often big foreign criminal gangs, but there are also domestic perpetrators among them.

Hacking of big companies gets a lot of publicity, but the police say that most often the target of hacking is ordinary citizens.

## The Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant

![Nuclear power plant on the opposite bank.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_1,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9976416303513231ba1/1661162074)

Open image viewer

Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant. Photo: Ed Jones / AFP

Ukraine says that Russia has again bombed the Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant. Russia again blames Ukraine for the bombings.

Ukraine's state nuclear power operator Energoatom says that the risk of a radioactive leak at the power plant is high.

The Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant is in Ukraine in an area that Russia has occupied. Russia has used the power plant as a base.

On Thursday a dangerous situation arose at the power plant, when the plant briefly lost connection to the electricity grid.

## Grain ships from Ukraine

![A cargo ship moves at sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2339,h_1315,x_5,y_48/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99138062ef44f5d2b45/1659848606)

Open image viewer

A cargo ship left the port of Odesa in the early part of August. Photo: EPA

So far 44 grain carrier ships have left Ukraine's ports. The ships have gone to 15 different countries.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy says that Ukraine has now exported one million tonnes of agricultural products under the grain agreement. The goal of the shipments is to reach 3 million tonnes a month, Zelenskyy said.

Ukraine, Russia, Turkey and the UN agreed on Ukraine's grain shipments in July in Istanbul.

## Finland's Nature Day

![Aerial photo of a heart-shaped pond in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10922880)

Open image viewer

In the middle of the forest there is a heart-shaped pond. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Today Finland's Nature Day is celebrated.

On Finland's Nature Day, for example, trips to nearby nature are organised, trees are hugged, people exercise in nature or nature work parties are held. The purpose of the day is to strengthen people's relationship with nature.

Finland's Nature Day is also a flag day.

## Venetian evenings

![A cottage shore lit by fires. Photographer: Derrick Frilund](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5871195)

Open image viewer

Fires light up the cottage shore as the evening darkens.

This weekend Venetian evenings are also celebrated in Finland. They are always celebrated on the last weekend of August, when the summer boating and cottage season ends.

Venetian evenings are also a celebration of water, fire and light. The Venetian weekend often includes colourful lanterns, bonfires and fireworks.
