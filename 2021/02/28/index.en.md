# Sunday 28.02.2021 (radio)

Yle asked about remote work. Finland has won silver in the skiing World Championships. An iceberg has broken off from Antarctica. In Finland, Sahara sand is being studied in snow. On Sunday, Kalevala Day was celebrated.

## Remote work does not suit everyone

![Photo collage of remote work](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11812539)

Open image viewer

Remote work at home or at a cottage suits many people but not everyone.

Almost one million Finns have done remote work for a year. Yle News asked its audience how the remote work year has changed their lives.

Almost 180 answers came in.

Some of those who answered said that remote work is too lonely. Remote work also requires new skills from managers. The worst management is when an employee is forgotten at home. This is what Jari Hakanen, research professor at the Finnish Institute of Occupational Health, says.

Experts who are also used to working independently enjoy remote work the most.

## Finland won silver in the skiing World Championships

![Joni Mäki and Ristomatti Hakola celebrating the world championship silver.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11813984)

Joni Mäki and Ristomatti Hakola brought Finland the second medal of the competition. Photo: Lehtikluva

Finland has won silver in the skiing World Championships. The medal came in the men's final of the team sprint.

Ristomatti Hakola and Joni Mäki brought Finland the second medal of the competition.

Norway won the freestyle race. The Russian ski federation's team took bronze, that is, third place.

The competition is held in Oberstdorf, Germany.

## A large iceberg broke off from Antarctica

![The Antarctic peninsula.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11198536)

Open image viewer

View of the Antarctic peninsula. Photo: AOP

A large iceberg has broken off from Antarctica. It is almost 1,300 square kilometres in size.

The break happened near the British Halley Research Station, which is closed for the winter. The station was moved already in 2017 because of the risk of breaking off.

Icebergs breaking off are common in Antarctica. This time it was an unusually large iceberg.

## Kalevala Day was celebrated on Sunday

![Old leather-bound Kalevala books.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11233822)

Open image viewer

The Kalevala can be found on the bookshelves of many homes. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

On Sunday Finnish culture day, that is, Kalevala Day, was celebrated.

It is also a flag day.

The Kalevala is Finland's national epic. In the 1800s, Elias Lönnrot collected folk poems, from which he put together the Kalevala.

People from 2 lands, Kalevala and Pohjola, appear in the Kalevala.

The first edition of the Kalevala was published in 1835.
