# Sunday 16.07.2017 (radio)

Business is going well. The police have shot 2 men during the weekend. A generous person is happy. Coffee reduces the risk of dying too early.

Business is going well in retail. Finland's economy has improved, and Finns now spend more money than in bad times.

For example, more trips and cars are being sold now. In addition, Finns, for example, eat at restaurants more often than before.

## Police use of firearms

![Police officer with a gun in its holster](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9724962)

Open image viewer

The police very rarely use firearms in Finland. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

During the weekend the police have used a gun 2 times.

During the night between Saturday and Sunday, the police shot a man in Vaasa, in Western Finland. The police say that the man had a gun and came towards the police. The man did not obey the police. The police shot the man in the stomach, and the man was taken to hospital.

On Saturday morning the police shot a man in Herttoniemi, Helsinki. The man had a knife and ran towards the police. The man did not obey the police. The police shot the man in the leg.

The authorities are investigating both cases.

It is not usual for the police to have to use a gun in Finland. The Police University College says that the police fired 122 shots with a gun in 2003–2013. The figure also includes warning shots, which are not fired at a person.

## The generous are happy

![Person putting a banknote into a collection box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9719028)

Open image viewer

Giving makes people happy. Photo: Yle

A generous person is happy. A new Swiss study says that it is happier to give to others than to collect for oneself.

The study had 2 groups. The researchers gave the members of the groups money.

The members of one group had to be selfish and use the money for their own benefit. The members of the other group had to be generous and use the money for the benefit of others.

At the end of the study, the generous group was happier than the selfish group. People became happy when they helped others.

The researchers say that happiness does not need big deeds. A person becomes happier already by helping others a little, or by intending to be generous.

## Coffee is healthy

![Coffee break.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9677742)

Open image viewer

Coffee does good. Photo: AOP

One more good piece of news for coffee lovers. The risk of dying too early is smaller if you drink coffee.

2 new big studies have been made on coffee. One was made in the USA and the other in Europe. In both studies it was noticed that coffee drinkers have a smaller risk of dying early than others. A suitable amount of coffee is 3 cups a day.

However, researchers do not know what in coffee is healthy. The answer is not caffeine. Decaffeinated coffee works just as well as normal coffee.
