# Wednesday 23.2.2022 (radio)

Western countries condemn Russia's actions in Ukraine. Finland's tourism sector believes in a better summer. Many dogs are being bought. The UMK is on Saturday.

## Sanctions on Russia

![Joe Biden speaks at the White House.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4339,h_2440,x_0,y_2/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9175066210491cd8148/1645234520)

Western countries condemn what Russia is doing in Ukraine.

The USA and the EU have imposed sanctions on Russia. US President Joe Biden says that Russia will no longer get money from the West. The sanctions are because Russia has ordered army troops into Eastern Ukraine.

Finland's Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that Finland strongly condemns Russia's actions.

## Emergency in Ukraine

![People fetch water into buckets from a well in a park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1968,x_0,y_165/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91912462160e25a9f3d/1645612676)

Open image viewer

Residents fetched water for storage in Luhansk in Eastern Ukraine on Wednesday. Photo: Anatolii Stepanov / AFP

Ukraine declares a state of emergency in the country. The reason is the threat of a Russian attack.

The state of emergency lasts 30 days, and it can be extended. The state of emergency makes it possible, for example, for cars to be checked.

Ukraine has also approved a bill that gives ordinary people the right to carry weapons. Ukraine has also called up reservists to service. The service time is up to a whole year.

## Tourism

![Winter seaside scenery at Suomenlinna in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5421,h_3049,x_0,y_568/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7895196061a9ed7c1dc/1677074132)

People in the tourism sector believe that foreign tourists will come to Finland again in the summer.

Because of the corona, there have been only a few foreign tourists in Finland. Lapland is an exception. In Lapland in December there were almost as many tourists as before the corona.

Most foreign tourists come to Finland at present from Europe.

## Dogs are being bought

![A small lion dog in close-up.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_260/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91848062148e6b3c3dc/1645514787)

Open image viewer

Small breeds have puppies more slowly than big ones, because they have only a few puppies at a time. Photo: Ville Välimäki / Yle

People are now buying a lot of dogs. Corona time has increased people's loneliness, and many buy a dog for company.

Puppies in particular are in short supply. You may have to wait a year, or even 2 years, for a puppy.

Dogs cost more than before. The prices of popular pedigree dogs have risen by as much as 30 percent in 3 years. For example, a chihuahua now costs more than 20 percent more than in 2018.

## UMK final in easy language

Soon the representative of Finland for this year's Eurovision Song Contest will be chosen.

The winner of the New Music Competition, UMK, gets into the Eurovision Song Contest. The winner will be decided in the UMK final this Saturday.

This year the UMK final will also be commented on in easy language. The UMK final can be followed in easy language on Yle Areena.
