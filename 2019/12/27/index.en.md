# Friday 27.12.2019 (radio)

Helsinki is raising doctors' pay. There has been a plane crash in Kazakhstan. New Year's fireworks have gone on sale. Floyd Mayweather is the highest-earning athlete of the 2010s.

## Helsinki tries to ease the shortage of doctors

![Hospital corridor](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11093903)

Open image viewer

Meilahti hospital. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

There are too few doctors in Helsinki's health centres.

Now the city of Helsinki is trying to ease the shortage of doctors. From January, Helsinki will pay health centre doctors 1,000 euros a month more in salary. The extra money will be paid to doctors for at least half a year.

The city had already given all doctors a 250-euro pay rise earlier. This rise is permanent.

The purpose of the pay rises is for more doctors to come to work at Helsinki's health centres than now. It will be quicker to get treatment at a health centre if there are more doctors.

## Plane crash in Kazakhstan

![Rescue staff worked at the accident site in Almaty on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11135147)

Open image viewer

Rescue staff worked at the accident site in Almaty on Friday. Photo: AFP

There has been a serious plane crash in Kazakhstan. A plane fell to the ground in the morning in the city of Almaty. 12 passengers died and over 50 were injured.

There were almost 100 people on board, so many of them survived the accident.

The cause of the accident is not known. Information about the accident is somewhat unclear.

The plane was on its way to Kazakhstan's capital, Nur-Sultan, formerly Astana. Kazakhstan is in Central Asia.

## New Year's fireworks go on sale

![Shooting fireworks in a winter landscape](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11135200)

Open image viewer

Over this decade, about 10 to 30 people each New Year's Eve have suffered eye injuries needing hospital treatment because of fireworks. Photo: Sari Gustafsson / Lehtikuva

New Year's fireworks go on sale in shops from this Friday.

However, fireworks, that is rockets, may not be fired yet. Rockets may be fired from 6 pm on New Year's Eve, that is, next Tuesday. Rockets may be fired at night until 2 am.

When firing rockets, you must wear safety glasses. Every year many people suffer an eye injury because of rockets.

Nearly half of Finns want to ban rockets completely. A citizens' initiative to ban rockets is currently in Parliament. The citizens' initiative has received over 65,000 signatures.

## Floyd Mayweather is the highest-earning athlete

![Floyd Mayweather](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11135326)

Open image viewer

Boxer Floyd Mayweather. Photo: Getty Images

The business magazine Forbes has made a list of the athletes with the best pay.

Boxer Floyd Mayweather is the athlete who earned the most money in the 2010s. Mayweather earned about 820 million euros in 10 years. Mayweather has already retired from boxing.

Second on the list is footballer Cristiano Ronaldo, and third is footballer Lionel Messi.
