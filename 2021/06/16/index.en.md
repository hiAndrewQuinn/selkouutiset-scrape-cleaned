## Students and schoolchildren

![Students celebrating Vappu at the market square by the river in Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10760507)

Open image viewer

Student life in the corona times has been quiet. Photo: Hanna Holopainen / Yle

The corona situation has affected students and schoolchildren. The National Institute for Health and Welfare and Kela have studied how students have coped.

More than 50 percent of students say that loneliness has increased during the corona times. The loneliest are 18–22-year-old women who study at university.

The corona times have also affected lower secondary school pupils. Many lower secondary pupils have experienced anxiety. About 33 percent of girls and 10 percent of boys have experienced anxiety.

## Service sector workers

![Prisma checkout workers and customers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_356/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-788883605ddb5bb8ad7/1616767424)

Open image viewer

Service sector workers meet many people, and so health worries them. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The corona times have frightened many service sector workers. The Finnish Institute of Occupational Health has done a study on the matter.

36 percent of service sector workers say that they have been afraid of whether they will stay healthy. The most fear has been among retail workers.

In service sector jobs, for example in shops, workers meet many people. That is why many service sector workers are afraid that they may catch corona from a customer.
