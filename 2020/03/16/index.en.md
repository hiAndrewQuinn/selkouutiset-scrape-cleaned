# Monday 16.3.2020 (radio)

Finland is bringing in the Emergency Powers Act because of the coronavirus. The borders will be closed, schools will be shut and public gatherings will be banned.

## Finland brings in the Emergency Powers Act

![Li Andersson, Katri Kulmuni and Sanna Marin at the government's information event on 16 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11260448)

Finland is bringing in the Emergency Powers Act. The Emergency Powers Act gives the authorities the possibility to restrict citizens' basic rights.

The Council of State, that is, the government, and the President of the Republic have today together stated that because of the coronavirus the situation in Finland is so exceptional that the Emergency Powers Act must be brought into use.

This is the first time in Finnish history that the Emergency Powers Act has been brought into use. Parliament still has to approve the matter.

## Citizens' lives are restricted in many ways

![Upper secondary pupils in a lesson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10998716)

Many new restrictions are being brought in in Finland because of the coronavirus. The restrictions are meant to prevent the spread of the coronavirus in Finland.

Finland intends, for example, to close the borders to foreign countries. However, goods traffic across the borders will continue.

Educational institutions will be closed and teaching must be arranged in some other way, for example as remote teaching. Day care centres, however, will stay open. This way, parents of small children who work in important fields, such as health care, can still go to work.

People over 70 are asked to avoid contact with other people. In addition, visits by outsiders are banned, for example in elderly care homes and hospitals.

Museums, theatres, libraries and, for example, sports and hobby facilities will be closed. Public gatherings of more than 10 people are also banned.

Shops, pharmacies and shopping centres will stay open. However, people must not stay in these places without need.

The restrictions are in force for one month.

## The number of coronavirus infections is not known

![A nurse and a breathing machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11259184)

Open image viewer

A nurse and a breathing machine. Photo: Anni Reenpää / Lehtikuva

At least 272 coronavirus infections have now been found in Finland. THL, the National Institute for Health and Welfare, says that there may really be many more infections.

The real number of infections is no longer known precisely, because not all suspected infections are tested any more. The test is only done for patients who are seriously ill, who belong to risk groups or who are health care staff.

In Finland the first coronavirus patient has been put into intensive care. Until now, coronavirus patients have had a mild illness.

The coronavirus can cause breathing difficulties, in which case a patient may need intensive care. Breathing difficulties occur only in a small part of the patients.
