## Illegal work is increasing in Finland

![Cleaning equipment on a cleaner's trolley](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8273560)

Open image viewer

For example, illegal work occurs in the cleaning sector. Photo: Sini Ojanperä / Yle

Illegal work has increased in Finland in recent years. Illegal work is done most in construction, restaurants and the cleaning sector. Subcontracting companies cause the most problems.

Illegal work means that a worker's work permit or residence permit has shortcomings. A worker may be in the country legally or illegally. Citizens of EU countries do not need a work permit. That is why those who do illegal work are usually people who come from outside the European Union.

If illegal work is discovered, it often leads to a fine. It is given to both the worker and the employer. If the person is in the country illegally, they also risk being held in custody or being removed from the country.

In Finland, 166 employers were sentenced last year.

## Isis retreats from Raqqa

![SDF fighters in Raqqa on 1 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9884317)

Open image viewer

An SDF fighter in Raqqa at the beginning of October. Photo: Bulent Kilic / AFP

In Syria, the escape of Isis fighters from the city of Raqqa has ended.

A group of fighters from the extremist group left the city and took civilians with them as human shields. This is what the Syrian forces say, which are supported by the Americans and other allies. Now the Kurdish-led Syrian forces continue fighting to finish off the remaining Isis fighters.

Isis captured Raqqa in 2014, in the chaos of the Syrian civil war. Raqqa was the capital of the caliphate, the Islamic state, proclaimed by Isis.

## Yösyöttö won an award

![A man holding a child in his arms.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9382558)

Open image viewer

Petteri Summanen plays the lead role in the film Yösyöttö. Photo: Marek Sabogal / Solar Films

The new Finnish film Yösyöttö has won an award at a film festival in America, in New York. The film received the award for best Nordic film. The award was given at the Nordic International Film Festival.

Yösyöttö is a comedy about a man who is left alone with a baby. The film was directed by Marja Pyykkö.

Yösyöttö has been in the headlines in Finland because of a contract dispute. The film has been removed from Finnkino's theatre schedule. The reason is a dispute between the production company and the distribution company over how the box office takes are shared.

## The autumn holiday week is quite rainy

![Pedestrians in windy and rainy autumn weather on 3 October in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9864463)

Open image viewer

During the autumn holiday week it often rains and is very windy. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

In the coming autumn holiday week, it will rain more than it shines.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that the worst weather may be on Monday in Northern Lapland. There, snowfall is disturbing road traffic. On Tuesday it may be sunny in Southern and Central Finland.

On Wednesday and Thursday the weather will probably get cooler, and it may rain in the southern parts of the country. Next weekend, a low-pressure system may bring some rain to Finland.

School autumn holidays are in the coming week and the week after that.
