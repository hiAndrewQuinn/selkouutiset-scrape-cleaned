# Saturday 2.10.2021 (radio)

Municipal finances. Restaurants. Motorists. Elokapina. Estonian border control. Weather forecast.

## Municipal finances

![Savonlinna town hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9256650)

Open image viewer

Municipalities are now considering how the economy will hold up. The picture shows the Savonlinna town hall. Photo: Paavo Koponen. Yle

Municipal finances will tighten next year. During the corona era municipalities have received a lot of extra support from the state. Corona subsidies helped municipal finances a lot last year and also this year.

Now the state's corona subsidies end, and municipalities must again save on expenses. State aid to municipalities will also shrink. Municipalities will receive about one billion euros less in state aid next year than this year.

The Association of Finnish Municipalities estimates that about 60 municipalities will raise municipal tax next year.

Restaurants are busy again, because restaurant restrictions were eased on Friday. Restaurants' opening hours were extended, and in restaurants people may again dance and sing.

For example, in Helsinki and Tampere the restaurants were busy on Friday. People were in a party mood.

The restaurants expect Saturday evening to be busy too.

## Motorists

![\'Stop the punishment of motorists!\' protest march passes Parliament House and the Pikkuparlamentti on Mannerheimintie.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-861693615847f6c21d4/1633183362)

Open image viewer

Police monitored the motorists' protest. About 200 motorists drove slowly past Parliament House and sounded their horns. Photo: Roni Rekomaa /Lehtikuva

Motorists have protested in Helsinki on Saturday.

Motorists protested against taxes on driving. Motorists say that taxes on driving must not be raised.

About 200 cars drove in front of Parliament House, where the motorists sounded their horns. The motorists wanted to remind Parliament that a car is needed for getting around in Finland.

The environmental movement Elokapina has continued its demonstrations in Helsinki.

Elokapina wants the government to declare a climate and environmental emergency in Finland.

Saturday was the fourth (4th) day that Elokapina demonstrated in Helsinki. Elokapina says that it will continue the demonstrations until next Friday.

## Estonian border control

![Fence on Estonia's eastern border](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_267/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-861415615704f6319df/1633092944)

Open image viewer

Fence on Estonia's eastern border. Photo: Jan Erik Kooli

Estonia has increased border control. The reason is that migrants are coming to the Baltic countries from Belarus without permission. Belarus is directing migrants to the borders of EU countries.

Estonia has increased border control on the southern border against Latvia and on the eastern border against Russia.

Estonia says that migrants who tried to travel to Finland illegally have been caught at the port of Tallinn.
