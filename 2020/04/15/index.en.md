# Wednesday 15.4.2020 (radio)

The isolation of Uusimaa. Coronavirus infections in Finland. Intensive care. WHO. The weather.

## The Uusimaa border is open

![Police commander Lasse Aapio being interviewed, and conscripts warming themselves at a campfire on the Uusimaa border.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11306600)

Police and conscripts stopped monitoring the Uusimaa border today. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The isolation of Uusimaa ended today. Everyone may move across the Uusimaa border again.

The Finnish government said in the morning that the corona epidemic is under control.

There is no longer any reason to stop people from moving freely.

However, Prime Minister Sanna Marin reminded people that everyone must still avoid travelling within Finland.

The Prime Minister said that now is not the right time to go to a summer cottage.

Other corona restrictions remain in force.

## There may be tens of thousands of infections

![Coronavirus testing at the Terveystalo Leppävaara medical centre in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11287324)

Open image viewer

There may be tens of thousands of coronavirus infections in Finland. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

There are now more than 3,200 confirmed coronavirus infections in Finland.

Health authorities estimate that there are actually many tens of times more infections.

Health authorities have studied the matter in Helsinki and the capital region.

Many people have had the disease caused by the coronavirus in a mild form or with no symptoms at all.

## Intensive care places are enough

![A nurse handles a breathing machine at Meilahti Tower Hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11275156)

Open image viewer

Breathing machine in an intensive care ward. Photo: Anni Reenpää / Lehtikuva

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health says that the intensive care places in Finland's hospitals are enough to treat coronavirus patients.

The coronavirus causes an illness called COVID-19.

The illness can cause breathing difficulties. In that case, the patient needs intensive care in hospital.

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health estimates that because of the corona, there have been fewer patients in Finland's hospitals than was first expected.

## The World Health Organization is in the news a lot now

![The headquarters of the World Health Organization in Geneva.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11185060)

Open image viewer

The headquarters of the World Health Organization is in Switzerland. Photo: Kim Petersen / AOP

Finland intends to give more money to the World Health Organization WHO.

Finnish Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says that Finland supports the work of the health organisation.

Haavisto says that the work of the health organisation is important right now.

Apart from Finland, many other countries have also said that they support the World Health Organization.

The United States disagrees. The United States is not giving the health organisation any money at all now.

US President Donald Trump says that the World Health Organization has failed in handling the coronavirus.

The US decision is a hard blow, because it is the health organisation's biggest funder.
