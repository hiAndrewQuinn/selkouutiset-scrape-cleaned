# Monday 5.4.2021 (radio)

Corona vaccinations. Car accident. Earthquake. The weather.

## Corona vaccines

![A nurse giving a corona vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_600/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-77159160215f7b805b1/1612802577)

A nurse giving a corona vaccine. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

More than 970,000 people in Finland have received the first dose of the corona vaccine. Later this week, the vaccine will already have been given to one million people.

THL says that a total of 4.5 million people in Finland can get the vaccine. Corona vaccination is given to people over 16 years old.

About 200,000 vaccines may now come to Finland each week.

There are many corona infections in India. India has had over 100,000 corona infections in a single day. Only the USA and Brazil have had this many corona infections in a day.

India has had almost 13 million corona infections in total. 1.3 billion people live in India.

## Landslides in Indonesia

![A neighbourhood covered in mud.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11870193)

Open image viewer

Destruction caused by a flash flood in the eastern parts of Flores island. Photo: BNPB / AOP

Water is flooding in Indonesia and East Timor.

At least 90 people have died and many people are missing. Water and earth have covered roads and people's homes. Entire villages have been submerged.

It rains a lot in Indonesia at this time of year. The rain causes many floods and landslides.

## Car accident in Suomussalmi

![A crashed car in a ditch about ten kilometres north of Suomussalmi, between Pesiönlahti and Alajärvi, where a head-on collision of two cars took place on Easter Monday, 5 April 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11870484)

Open image viewer

A crashed car in a ditch about ten kilometres north of Suomussalmi, between Pesiönlahti and Alajärvi, where a head-on collision of two cars took place on Easter Monday, 5 April 2021. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

There has been a serious car accident in Kainuu. 8 people were injured in the crash. At least 3 people were seriously injured.

The accident happened in Suomussalmi. Two cars were in the crash. There were 4 people in each car.

It was a head-on collision, that is, cars travelling in opposite directions crashed into each other.

The driving conditions were poor.

![Round bales in a field behind a birch grove.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_337/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6077885dbfd47cd2c89/1572853491)

There was a small earthquake in South Ostrobothnia on Sunday.

Iltalehti reports that loud noises were heard in Alajärvi, Lappajärvi, Soini and Vimpeli in the evening. It has now emerged that an earthquake caused the sounds.

## The weather

![A dog walker in spring in Kaivopuisto park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11869403)

Open image viewer

A dog walker in spring in Kaivopuisto park. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The weather this week is rather rainy.

On Tuesday and Wednesday, a little rain, sleet or snow will fall in many places. Even in Southern Finland, there may be some sleet.

During the rest of the week, more rain will fall than at the start of the week.

Across the whole country it is frosty at night, and the road may be slippery. That is why it is not yet worth changing to summer tyres on your car.
