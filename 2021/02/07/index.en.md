# Sunday 7.2.2021 (radio)

Rental flats. Plastic waste. Coronavirus infections. Bishop. Joint Responsibility collection.

## Rental flats

![Jätkäsaari](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10147118)

Open image viewer

Demand for rental flats has fallen because of the coronavirus. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

There are now more rental flats free than before.

Because of the coronavirus situation, many students have stayed in their home towns. Commuting for work has decreased. More rental flats have also been built.

The rise in rents has slowed down. Rents for some flats have fallen, because there are more rental flats on offer than before.

Most coronavirus infections happen in Uusimaa.

## Plastic waste

![Packaging plastic in a collection container](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11473349)

Open image viewer

Plastic waste. Photo: Heli Mansikka / Yle

Companies will be responsible for paying compensation for plastic waste and cigarette butts.

In the EU, a law has been made that companies which make plastic packaging or tobacco must pay compensation to municipalities for cleaning up the waste. This is called *cost responsibility*.

In Finland, it must now be decided what kind of compensation companies have to pay. Experts say that in Finland it is hard to calculate how much plastic waste or how many cigarette butts there are in different municipalities. The EU's guidelines are also unclear.

## Finland's 3rd female bishop

![Mari Leppänen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11777373)

Open image viewer

The new bishop of the Turku Archdiocese, Mari Leppänen, was ordained into office in Turku Cathedral on Sunday. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

Mari Leppänen has been ordained as the new bishop of the Turku Archdiocese. A *diocese* is an administrative area of the church, led by a bishop.

Mari Leppänen is 42 years old. She is the first female bishop of the Turku Archdiocese. She is the third female bishop in Finland.

Mari Leppänen was ordained as bishop at a festive service in Turku Cathedral. Mari Leppänen says that the church must look to the future.

## Joint Responsibility collection

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11775182)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö opened the Joint Responsibility collection on Sunday. Photo: Office of the President of the Republic

The church's Joint Responsibility collection started today, on Sunday.

This year, the Joint Responsibility collection is collecting money to help elderly people with little money. The Joint Responsibility collection reminds us that people's incomes fall a lot when a person retires. Poverty can then surprise a person.

This year the Joint Responsibility collection also remembers elderly people in crisis areas of the world.
