# Monday 3.6.2019 (radio)

The new government's programme has been released. The new government will have 19 ministers. Construction of the Raide-Jokeri light rail has started. It rained a lot in May.

## Government programme

![Pekka Haavisto, Juha Sipilä, Antti Rinne, Li Andersson, Anna-Maja Henriksson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10812927)

The programme of the new government is ready.

5 parties will be in the new government. The party leaders told about the government's plans on Monday.

The goal of the government is to create more jobs in Finland. In this way, the Finnish economy will be put in order.

The party leaders also said that the new government intends to spend more money than the old government. Government spending will grow by over one billion euros a year.

The government intends to give more money, for example, to education. For example, upper secondary schools and vocational schools will be free.

Pensioners are also promised more money. The smallest pensions will rise by 50 euros a month at first.

The government is raising money, for example, by raising some taxes. For example, the tax on alcohol, tobacco and petrol will rise. Then their prices will also rise.

## Ministers

![The Government Palace](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8711900)

Open image viewer

The Finnish government meets in this building in Senate Square in Helsinki. Photo: Yle

The new Finnish government will have 19 ministers. On Monday the party leaders said how the parties will share the ministerial posts.

The largest party, the SDP, gets the post of prime minister. SDP party leader Antti Rinne is Finland's next prime minister. The SDP gets 7 ministerial posts in total.

The second largest party in the government, the Centre, gets 5 ministerial posts. The Greens get 3 ministers in the government. The Left Alliance and the RKP each get 2 ministerial posts.

Antti Rinne hopes that the new government can start work soon.

## Raide-Jokeri

![Raide-Jokeri on a map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10810974)

Open image viewer

The new line will be built between Keilaniemi and Itäkeskus. Photo: Seppo Suvela / Yle

Construction of a new light rail line has started. Building work began on Monday in Helsinki and Espoo.

The name of the new line is Raide-Jokeri. It will be built between Itäkeskus in Helsinki and Keilaniemi in Espoo.

The construction work will take 5 years. The construction work will cause changes to traffic.

## A rainy May

![Summer rain](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10265046)

Open image viewer

Photo: AOP

It rained a lot in May.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that May was rainier this year than usual. The rainfall was between 40 and 100 millimetres in different parts of Finland.

In Central Finland and Eastern Finland, it rained more than twice as much in May as in a normal May.

Now at the beginning of June the weather looks completely different. On Wednesday the weather will begin to warm up, and by the end of the week the temperature may rise to close to 30 degrees.
