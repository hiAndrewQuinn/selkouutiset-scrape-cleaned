## Nurses' unions

![Outdoor view of the T-building of Turku University Central Hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4818,h_2710,x_0,y_391/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-839438610cf8d683414/1628240377)

A strike at Tyks begins on 6 September, if no agreement is reached before then. Photo: Arash Matin / Yle

The nurses' unions Tehy and Super have issued 2 strike warnings in 2 days. Today, Friday, the nurses' unions issued a strike warning to Turku University Central Hospital, Tyks.

Yesterday, Thursday, the nurses' unions issued a strike warning to Kanta-Häme Central Hospital.

With the strike warnings the nurses' unions want to make the contract negotiations more effective. If no agreement is reached in the contract negotiations, a 4-day strike will be held in September at Tyks and a 1-day strike at Kanta-Häme Central Hospital.

The National Conciliator has invited the nurses' unions to negotiations on Sunday.

## Trips abroad

![A beach strand surrounded by tall hotels.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_628/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6465315e54d29118219/1582617301)

Open image viewer

The Canary Islands are Finns' favourite destination in winter. Photo: /All Over Press

Finns dare to travel abroad again. People dare to book trips also a long time in advance again. Travel agencies are already selling many trips to the south for next winter.

The travel agency Tjäreborg expects that the sales of trips for next winter will be as good as before corona. The most popular destination of Finns in winter is the Canary Islands. Other favourite destinations of Finns are Madeira, Cape Verde and Thailand. However, demand for trips to Thailand is lower than before.

Statistics Finland's survey also showed that Finns no longer fear corona much.

S-Group is increasing the use of robots in food home deliveries. S-Group has tested delivery robots in Espoo. Small robots deliver shopping from Alepa stores to people. S-Group will also bring the delivery robots to Helsinki and Vantaa.

Other shops are also preparing for demand for home delivery to grow. The K-chain, that is, Kesko, has built a warehouse in Helsinki where robots collect ordered purchases.

The Norwegian online grocery shop Oda also intends to expand its operations in Finland.

## Producer prices of grain

![Max Schulman dug up the ground to show how moisture can be found under the dry surface.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5363,h_3016,x_0,y_295/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99486462fb550ea110b/1660805225)

Open image viewer

Max Schulman grows rapeseed, among other things. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Producer prices of grain have risen a lot since last year. Statistics Finland says that the producer prices of grain have more than doubled since last year. The producer price is the price that a farmer gets for grain.

There are many reasons for the rise in producer prices of grain. One reason is that the harvest was small in Finland last year. The price of grain is also raised by the fact that many countries have severe drought.

Producer prices are also raised by the rise in the price of energy and the rise in production costs.
