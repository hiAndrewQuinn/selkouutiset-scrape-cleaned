# Thursday 1.9.2022 (radio)

The budget was completed. Finland restricts visas for Russians. The EU also restricts visas for Russians. THL updated its corona vaccination recommendation. August was unusually warm. Friday's weather.

## Budget

![Press conference on the government's budget talks at the Government Palace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4545,h_2556,x_0,y_136/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-100215063109114ca836/1729006659)

The government has completed the state budget for next year, that is, the budget plan.

In the budget proposal the government aims to respond to the energy crisis and improve Finns' purchasing power.

Households are going to get tax relief and direct support for next winter's electricity bills. The government also hopes for EU action to calm the rise in electricity prices.

The government supports families with children, for example with an extra child benefit in December. In addition, early childhood education fees will get a permanent reduction.

The total of the budget is over 80 billion euros.

## Visas to Finland for Russians

![Crowd queues at Finland's visa centre. In the foreground a man reads his mobile phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3999,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99687462ff8ebcd9fac/1660915475)

Finland is granting even fewer visas to Russians.

From today Finland will accept visa applications from Russians at 500 a day, when previously it accepted a thousand a day.

Visas are easier to get for people who travel from Russia to Finland because of family, work or study. Getting a tourist visa is now harder.

Because of the war in Ukraine, Finland wants to restrict holiday trips to Finland by Russian citizens, or trips through Finland to elsewhere in Europe.

## EU visas for Russians

![Russia's and the EU's border in Imatra.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8434051)

Open image viewer

Russia–EU border in Imatra. Photo: Vilma Hannén / Yle

The EU also intends to restrict visas for Russians.

EU foreign ministers have agreed that the EU will freeze the agreement that made it easier for Russians to get a visa.

The agreement on simplified processing of visas was brought into use between the EU and Russia in 2007.

Russia says that freezing the visa agreement will have consequences for the EU.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare recommends new corona vaccinations.

THL recommends that this autumn everyone over 65 and people who belong to risk groups get a new corona vaccination.

THL says that the new vaccination is needed, even if a person has already had corona vaccinations or has had the corona disease many times.

A person can go for a vaccination when 3 months have passed since the previous vaccination or corona infection.

## August heat

![People enjoyed a sunny day at the Hietaranta beach in Helsinki on 21 June 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4999,h_2811,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97320462b1c92040e22/1655818822)

This summer was warmer than usual. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

August was unusually warm in almost the whole country, says the Finnish Meteorological Institute.

There were almost 20 hot days. The last time there were this many hot days in August was in 2006.

The highest temperature in August was 31.7 degrees. It was measured in Pori on 16 August.

The whole summer was also warmer than usual.
