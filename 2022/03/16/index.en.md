# Wednesday 16.3.2022 (radio)

Ukraine and Russia negotiate. Civilian victims of the war. Many refugees come to Finland. The joint application has begun. Young Finns' faith in the future. Weather.

## Ukraine and Russia negotiate

![Volodymyr Zelensky and Denys Shmyhal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_33/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9291126232045d1e38d/1647445187)

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyi and Prime Minister Denys Shmyhal. Photo: Office of the President of Ukraine

Ukraine and Russia have continued negotiations about a ceasefire.

Russia says that the conflict may perhaps be resolved if Ukraine stays outside military alliances. Russia says that Sweden or Austria may serve as a model, as they are neutral but have their own armies.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyi has rejected the proposal. He says that Ukraine needs security guarantees, which will protect the country in the future.

## Civilian victims of the war

![Smoke rises above Kyiv.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-92868862318d6a7a574/1647445811)

Open image viewer

Russia has heavily shelled Ukraine's capital Kyiv in recent days. Photo: Aris Messinis / AFP

The UN says that at least 600 civilians have been killed in Ukraine because of Russia's military actions. At least 46 of the dead are children.

The UN says that in addition at least 1,100 civilians have been wounded. However, the UN believes that the real figures are much higher.

The great majority of civilians have died in Russian rocket strikes and missile and air strikes.

## Support for refugees.

![Passengers arriving at Länsisatama.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9763280)

Open image viewer

Tallink Silja has so far brought at least 4,000 Ukrainian refugees from Tallinn to Finland. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

Hundreds of refugees are now coming to Finland from Ukraine every day. Tallink Silja says that its ships have brought, through aid trips alone, about 4,000 war refugees so far.

A service point for Ukrainian refugees has been opened at Helsinki's Länsisatama. At the service point it is possible to leave, for example, an asylum application or a residence permit application with the police.

In Helsinki's Vallila a separate aid centre for Ukrainians has been opened. There, information is given, for example, about coming to Finland and living here.

The joint application to universities has begun.

This time it is for Finnish- and Swedish-language education at universities of applied sciences and universities.

Almost 53,000 starting places are on offer. There are about 150,000 applicants.

## Young people and the future

![A silhouette-like picture of a woman holding a bird in her hand. Blue sky and a brightly shining sun in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3872,h_2178,x_0,y_411/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8517546139eeee80812/1631186842)

Open image viewer

Finnish young people believe that their own and Finland's future is bright. Photo: Igor Goncharenko / Alamy / All Over Press

Finnish young people see the future as bright.

80 percent of young people and young adults under 30 see their own future and Finland's future as bright.

Young people think the future of the world does not look as good. Only 38 percent are hopeful about the future of the world.

The survey was carried out last year, long before Russia attacked Ukraine, so worry about the war does not show in the results.

Young people are worried about climate change. However, they believe that they can influence environmental matters with their own choices.
