# Tuesday 9.5.2017 (radio)

Politicians are discussing the doctor reform. A change in how Finns drink alcohol. Ahtisaari is stepping down as chair of CMI. Finnair is hiring more staff.

## Health reform goes to Parliament

![medical orderly](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9602263)

Open image viewer

Finnish health care is changing dramatically.  Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Going to the doctor may change a lot in Finland. Politicians have started to discuss a proposal in Parliament that will change a lot, for example, going to the doctor or the dentist.

In the future the doctor may be, for example, in a private company or in a company that the region has set up. The reform means that many new health companies will come to Finland. Critics say the proposal is such that health companies can avoid taxes.

People also fear that the reform will cost Finns very much and that health care in Finland will become a mess. Critics also say that people who live far away in the countryside will get worse services than people in the cities.

If Parliament approves the reform, it will come into force in 2019.

## Getting drunk is decreasing in Finland

![A bottle of spirits going from hand to hand](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9506514)

Open image viewer

Getting drunk is decreasing in Finland.  Photo: AOP

Finns no longer drink as much alcohol at one time as before. According to the authorities, 19 percent of men now get very drunk at least once a month.

In 2008 the same figure was 27 percent.

Women's drinking has also decreased. 6 percent of them now drink a lot once a month. In 2008, 9 percent of women wanted to do the same.

This is the first time a study has found that heavy drinking, or binge drinking, has decreased.

## Ahtisaari steps down from leading CMI

![President Martti Ahtisaari received the Nobel Peace Prize in 2008.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9211780)

Open image viewer

Martti Ahtisaari received the Nobel Peace Prize in 2008.  Photo: Valdrin Xhemaj / EPA

President Martti Ahtisaari is leaving the post of chair of the peace organisation CMI. Ahtisaari founded CMI after he finished his work as president of Finland.

Ahtisaari wants to stop being chair in the autumn, but he plans to continue working in the organisation. He hopes that the new chair will be MP Alexander Stubb. Stubb plans to accept the task.

President Ahtisaari has received the Nobel Peace Prize. He turns 80 in the summer.

## Finnair grows and hires

![Flight simulator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9541599)

Open image viewer

Finnair is growing fast and hiring more employees.  Photo: Rinna Härkönen / Yle

The Finnish airline Finnair is hiring more employees. Finnair wants 350 new flight attendants and stewards for the cabins of its planes. Finnair also says that it will hire more pilots in the autumn.

Finnair is now growing strongly. According to Finnair, it has hired 1000 employees during the year.
