# Sunday 2.7.2017 (radio)

There are big differences between regions in how quickly possible buyers are found for a home with an online ad. Canada has turned 150. Europe is discussing the refugee situation in the Mediterranean. It is hot in Eastern Europe. In Finland the week starts with fresh weather.

## Online housing ads

![](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5720152)

Open image viewer

Buyers are interested in apartments in big cities. Photo: YLE / Jani Aarnio

Many people sell a home with the help of an online ad. But there are big differences between regions in how quickly possible buyers are found for a home with an online ad.

Yle studied the matter from the material of the Oikotie online service. In some regions housing ads were online for a week or two weeks, in some regions for over 2.5 years.

Buyers are more interested in apartments in big cities than in detached houses in the countryside. That is why, for example, in Helsinki and Turku, ads usually do not stay online for long.

## Canada's celebration day

![People celebrate Canada's 150 years in Ottawa on 1 July. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9701428)

Open image viewer

Canadians have celebrated their independence. Photo: Chris Roussakis / AFP

Canada has turned 150. Canada became independent in 1867. Before that, Canada belonged to Britain.

Canadians celebrated their independence on Saturday. The celebrations included, for example, concerts and big fireworks.

Canada's Prime Minister Justin Trudeau and Britain's Crown Prince Charles also took part in the celebrations.

Charles represented Britain's Queen Elizabeth. Elizabeth is also the Queen of Canada.

## The refugee situation in the Mediterranean

![Asylum seekers in the port of Augusta in Sicily.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9004504)

Open image viewer

Asylum seekers in an Italian port last year. Photo: Petri Burtsov / Yle

Europe's big countries have discussed the refugee situation in the Mediterranean. The interior ministers of France, Germany and Italy are taking part in the discussions.

Many asylum seekers come from Africa to Europe across the Mediterranean. Most come to Italy. This year over 80,000 people have already come to Italy across the Mediterranean.

Italy has said that it cannot handle the situation alone. Italy wants other countries to also open ports to rescue ships.

All the EU countries will discuss the refugee situation together on Thursday in Tallinn.

## Hot Eastern Europe

![People sheltering from the scorching sun in Athens on 1 July 2017. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9701421)

Open image viewer

Greeks look for shelter from the sun. Photo: Simela Pantzartzi / EPA

It is now hot in Eastern Europe. People have died because of the hot weather, at least in Bulgaria.

In Greece and Romania too, the temperature has risen above 40 degrees. On Friday it was over 45 degrees in Greece.

It has not been quite so hot on the Greek islands. In the coming days, the temperature will drop a little in the rest of Greece too.

## Cold Finland

![Bubbles on the surface of the water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9667223)

Open image viewer

The Finnish summer is fresh. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

Here in Finland the week starts with fresh weather.

Monday will still be warm in Eastern Finland, but elsewhere the temperature will be below 20 degrees. On Wednesday the weather will get colder in the whole country.

Rain and thunder will liven up Finns at the start of the week. On Monday it will rain in the west, and on Tuesday there will be rain in a large part of the country.
