## The central bank supports the euro

![Olli Rehn photographed at the Bank of Finland premises on 11 June 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_325/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5699105cff98d14cca8/1587545407)

The Governor of the Bank of Finland, Olli Rehn, says that the member states of the EU must co-operate better.

Rehn said at the Bank of Finland's press conference that the European Central Bank is doing everything possible to keep the euro standing.

For example, the Eurosystem of banks is buying a very large amount of bonds because of the coronavirus situation. The European Central Bank is also giving banks long-term funding.

## Yle's information: police have arrested Onni Sarmaste

![A picture of Onni Sarmaste.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11309179)

Open image viewer

Onni Sarmaste. Photo: Onni Sarmaste

According to Yle's information, the police have arrested the businessman Onni Sarmaste. The company of Onni Sarmaste has sold a large batch of poor-quality protective equipment to Finland.

Yle reported last week that the police had frozen more than 2 million euros of Sarmaste's money. Now the police have frozen about half a million (0.5 million) euros more from the account of Sarmaste's company. Freezing means that the money cannot be used then.

The National Emergency Supply Agency has said that the masks that Sarmaste's company delivered to Finland are not suitable for hospital use.

## The corona deaths at a care home are being investigated

![A flag at half-mast at the Kallionsydän care home of the health care company Attendo in Kiuruvesi on 5 April 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11308298)

Open image viewer

A flag at half-mast at the care home. Photo: Jorma Kärkkäinen / Lehtikuva

The Eastern Finland police have started a preliminary investigation into the coronavirus deaths at the Kiuruvesi care home.

The police are carrying out the preliminary investigation together with the Regional State Administrative Agency for Eastern Finland. The Regional State Administrative Agency for Eastern Finland supervises matters at the Kiuruvesi care home.

At the care home in Kiuruvesi, 11 residents have died in the spring of the disease caused by the coronavirus. The number of deaths is more than 30% of the care home's residents.

## Don't go to crowds to watch birds

![Birdwatchers in a bird tower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5467,h_3075,x_0,y_253/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6561855e74ed9f65f00/1584722552)

You may visit a bird tower if there are not many people there at the same time. Photo: Yle/Mikael Kokkola

The spring migration of birds is now busy. The bird enthusiasts' association Bird Life Finland gives Finns new instructions on watching birds.

Because of the coronavirus situation, places where many other people are watching birds must be avoided.

At popular bird-watching places there may be many people. A safe distance must be kept from other people. It is not a good idea to stay in a bird tower for long if there are other people there. It is also good to let others have a turn to go into the bird tower in peace.
