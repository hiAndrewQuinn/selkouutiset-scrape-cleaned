# Monday 13.7.2020 (radio)

Many corona restrictions eased on Monday. No new corona deaths have been reported in Finland. The winner of Poland's presidential election is the current president, Andrzej Duda. A fossil animal species has been named after the Finnish band Nightwish.

The TV Clear News has a summer break and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Corona restrictions are easing

![Beach on Rhodes in Greece.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11412333)

Open image viewer

From Finland you can already travel to the beaches of Greece, for example. Photo: AOP

Many corona restrictions have eased.

Finns can now travel freely to a large part of the countries of Europe. Finns can travel, for example, to holiday destinations such as Greece, Italy and Germany. After the trip you no longer need to stay at home in quarantine.

Travel restrictions have also eased for many countries outside Europe. Work trips are now allowed, for example, to Japan, China and Thailand.

Restrictions on restaurants have also eased. Restaurants may now stay open freely. They may also let in a normal number of customers. However, all restaurant customers must have a seat.

No new corona deaths have been reported in Finland. So far 329 people have died of corona in Finland.

On Monday 1 new corona infection was reported. About 7,300 people in Finland have caught the corona infection.

## Poland's president continues

![Andrzej Duda meeting local residents in Zamość, Poland, on 10 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11444309)

Open image viewer

The president of Poland, Andrzej Duda. Photo: Wojtek Jargilo / EPA

Presidential elections have been held in Poland.

The winner of the election is Poland's current president, Andrzej Duda. Duda will continue as president for the next 5 years too.

Duda is a conservative politician. He does not accept, for example, the rights of homosexual people.

Many EU countries have been worried that under Duda Poland has moved in an undemocratic direction. Duda's side has gathered power to itself.

Duda's opponent in the presidential election was Rafał Trzaskowski. Trzaskowski has wanted, for example, to improve Poland's relations with the European Union.

The election was fairly even. The winner, Duda, got about 51 per cent of the votes, and Trzaskowski got about 49 per cent.

## Honour for the band Nightwish

![Nightwish's Tuomas Holopainen and Marco Hietala at a presentation of a new album in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6362645e313e79a3992/1580285682)

Open image viewer

Members of Nightwish include Tuomas Holopainen and Marco Hietala. Photo: Tapio Laakkonen / Yle

The Finnish band Nightwish has received an unusual honour.

A fossil, that is, an ancient animal species, has been named after Nightwish. The name is Tanidromites nightwishorum.

American researchers gave the name to a crayfish species that lived about 150 million years ago, in the time of the dinosaurs.

The music of the Finnish band Nightwish is popular abroad. Nightwish is happy that an ancient animal species has been named after the band.
