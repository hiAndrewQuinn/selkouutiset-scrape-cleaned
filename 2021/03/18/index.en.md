The European Medicines Agency says that the Astra Zeneca coronavirus vaccine is safe and effective.

The Medicines Agency has not found evidence that the Astra Zeneca coronavirus vaccine increases cases of blood clots.

Many countries have suspended coronavirus vaccinations with Astra Zeneca, because they have feared side effects.

Finland has been using the Astra Zeneca coronavirus vaccine all the time.

## The origin of coronavirus infections

![A man in a cap stepping into a car. He has a pen and a large sheet of paper with columns in his hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11843076)

Open image viewer

A World Health Organization researcher in Wuhan, China. Photo: Alex Plavevski / EPA

The World Health Organization, WHO, may have found out how the coronavirus came to humans.

WHO has carried out studies in Wuhan, China, where the world's first coronavirus cases were.

WHO says that the coronavirus first passed from bats to other animals. The virus passed to wild animals that were kept on farms in China.

On the farms there were, for example, civets, hedgehogs and pangolins. They were sold at markets as food for people, and in this way the virus got into humans.

WHO will publish a report on the studies in the near future.

## Commuter trains are standing still

![1980s-built Eil-model commuter train carriage in Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5326,h_2996,x_0,y_316/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7493215fc7cc59686e0/1606929645)

Open image viewer

Photo: Samuli Holopainen / Yle

Helsinki region commuter trains will not run on Thursday and Friday.

The train drivers are not at work, because they are protesting against VR. The train drivers think that VR wants to make working conditions worse.

Bus traffic feared traffic jams while the commuter trains were standing still. However, there have not been any serious traffic jams.

Commuter trains will start running again on Saturday, when the train drivers return to work.
