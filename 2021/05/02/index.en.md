# Sunday 2.5.2021 (radio)

The world's corona situation is bad. In Finland the number of new coronavirus infections is decreasing. Israel has held a day of mourning. Many Finns now take out a mortgage alone. One person died in a fire in Seinäjoki. The weather is cool.

## The world's corona situation

![Workers in protective gear look after patients lying in hospital beds.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11911387)

The number of corona patients is growing in India. Photo: Prakash Singh / AFP

The world's corona situation is getting worse.

The World Health Organization, WHO, says that the number of coronavirus infections and corona deaths has been growing for many weeks already.

The corona situation is really bad in India and Brazil. The corona situation has also worsened quickly in Iran and Turkey.

Across the world, more than 3 million people have already died because of corona. More than 150 million people have caught the coronavirus.

## Finland's corona situation

![Syringes used for corona vaccination at a vaccination point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5389,h_3031,x_0,y_480/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7911706065ba3562bb3/1617279617)

In Finland the number of new coronavirus infections is decreasing. On Sunday it was reported that there are 132 new coronavirus infections. That is the lowest figure for many months.

The number of new coronavirus infections in Finland has been decreasing for some time. In total, more than 87,000 coronavirus infections have been found in Finland.

A little over 30 percent of Finland's residents have received at least one corona vaccination.

## Israel's day of mourning

![ In Israel, dozens of people are reported to have died in a crush at a Jewish religious event. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5760,h_3240,x_0,y_297/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-800505608b591e8b5fc/1619745307)

Open image viewer

An accident happened at a religious celebration on Friday, in which 45 people died. Photo: EPA/DAVID COHEN

A day of mourning has been held in Israel.

Israelis have remembered the people who died in the accident on Friday.

The accident happened at a religious celebration in the city of Meron. Tens of thousands of people came to the celebration.

Panic broke out in the crowd, and people were trampled underfoot. 45 people died.

## Mortgages

![Keys on top of mortgage papers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10998654)

Open image viewer

Many people buy a home alone. Photo: Kimmo Hiltunen / Yle

Many Finns now take out a mortgage alone.

For example, about 50 percent of OP's mortgages are already loans for one person. Other banks also say that mortgages for one person are increasing.

The reason for this is that many now live alone. That is why they also pay off the mortgage alone.

In the Helsinki region, the number of one-person mortgages is reduced by the fact that many people rent their homes. One person's salary is not always enough to buy a home in the Helsinki region.

## Fire in Seinäjoki

![Fire engine of the South Ostrobothnia Rescue Department.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10897931)

Open image viewer

Seinäjoki is in South Ostrobothnia, in Western Finland. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

In Seinäjoki in Western Finland there has been a fire. 1 person died in the fire.

The fire completely destroyed a residential building, an outbuilding and a sauna. The dead person was found in the residential building.

It is not yet known what caused the fire. The police are investigating.
