## Sanna Marin

![Sanna Marin (SDP) at a press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3983,h_2241,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116893164f9b8389e5fe/1694087281)

Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Former prime minister and SDP MP Sanna Marin is leaving parliament.

Marin is asking to resign from her post, because she has been appointed as a strategic adviser to the Tony Blair Institute.

The institute is an international foundation that advises governments and political leaders.

The institute is based in London and operates in more than 30 countries.

Replacing Marin in parliament will be 37-year-old researcher and doctor of health sciences Lotta Hamari from Hämeenkyrö.

## Finland's most popular parties

![In the latest party support poll, SDP is at the top (21.4%), the National Coalition Party second (21.2%) and the Finns Party third (18.5%).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2544,h_1431,x_0,y_189/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116819064f83611217ff/1693988587)

Open image viewer

Photo: Nanna Särkkä / Yle

SDP and the National Coalition Party are Finland's most popular parties.

Yle has carried out a new opinion poll, according to which 21.4 percent of Finns support SDP. Support for the National Coalition Party is almost as big. 21.2 percent of Finns support the National Coalition Party.

The popularity of the third biggest party, the Finns Party, has fallen a little and is now 18.5 percent.

The fourth biggest party is the Centre. Support for the Centre rose and was 11.5 percent.

## Electricity production

![OX2 Metsälamminkankaan wind farm in Vaala.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5360,h_3015,x_0,y_549/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116162464e70974254dc/1694341507)

Open image viewer

Photo: Petteri Löppönen

The government wants to double Finland's electricity production during the next 15 years.

Environment and climate minister Kai Mykkänen says that electricity production must be reliable when renewable energy is used more than before.

For example, wind power production varies a lot depending on whether it is windy a lot or a little.

The government also wants more nuclear power plants to be built in Finland.

## Drowning figures

![A wooden cross placed between the shore stones under the west end of the Bastvik bridge (Muulo bridge).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6119,h_3441,x_9,y_277/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9459936266817039134/1650886757)

Open image viewer

Fewer people drowned in Finland in the summer than before.

The Finnish Swimming Instruction and Life Saving Association says that in June, July and August a total of 40 people drowned.

Usually during the summer, on average 65 people drown.
