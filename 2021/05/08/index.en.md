## Police and racism

![victim of police violence, illustrative image](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5946,h_3345,x_0,y_307/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-801979609132bdaee10/1620130422)

Open image viewer

The National Police Board says that everyone must be treated equally. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

Police sometimes behave badly towards minorities.

The matter came to light when Yle interviewed police officers. The police said that they have seen some police officers speak in a racist way.

Police officers have usually received only light punishments for racist speech. In recent years, however, 2 police officers have been dismissed because of racist writings.

The National Police Board says that the police are doing everything possible so that minorities can still trust the police.

## India's corona situation

![A body is carried on a cart in Kolkata.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3543,h_1993,x_0,y_66/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8040026096470762bcc/1620461419)

Open image viewer

People in India are dying of corona all the time. Photo: Piyal Adhikary / EPA

India's corona situation is getting worse.

India set a sad record when more than 4,000 Indians died of corona in one day. More than 400,000 new coronavirus infections were found in a day.

Corona is now spreading quickly in rural India. In big cities, for example New Delhi and Mumbai, the corona situation has calmed down a little.

India has almost 1.4 billion inhabitants.

## Homework mentors

![A lesson at Eskolanmäki school in Kouvola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7223545f6b392194a25/1633344119)

Open image viewer

Homework goes better with a homework mentor. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

Homework mentors are being trialled in Finland. The experiences are good.

Homework mentors help comprehensive school pupils do their homework. The homework mentor and the schoolchild meet remotely, that is, through a computer.

Families have said that children's test grades have improved with the help of a homework mentor. The Finnish language skills of immigrant children have also improved.

The homework mentors have received training from the Mannerheim League for Child Welfare. It has trained about 30 homework mentors.

## Mother's Day

![Finnish flag on a pole.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11060883)

Open image viewer

Mother's Day is a flag day. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

On Sunday, mothers are celebrated, so it is Mother's Day time.

In Finland, Mother's Day is always on the second Sunday of May. Mother's Day is also celebrated then in many other countries, for example Estonia, Denmark, Germany and the USA.

Mother's Day is a flag day in Finland.
