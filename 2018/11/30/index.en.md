# Friday 30.11.2018 (radio)

They are trying to stop the spread of measles in Ostrobothnia. The environment is the theme of the president's Independence Day party. Buying homes becomes digital. Sauna is healthy.

## Measles in Ostrobothnia

![A child who has measles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10532230)

Open image viewer

Measles causes red spots on the body. Photo: AOP

One child has got measles in Ostrobothnia, in Western Finland. The child may have got measles in the Middle East, where the family was travelling.

Measles is a dangerous illness for a small child. At first the patient has a high fever, and in the end red spots come on different parts of the body.

Officials estimate that about 300 people may have been infected. Now the officials are trying to reach them.

This is important, because in Ostrobothnia there are families who have not taken vaccines. That is why measles can spread in Ostrobothnia.

Elsewhere in Finland almost all people have got a vaccine against measles. The vaccine gives good protection and stops measles.

## Castle party

![Presidential Palace in September 2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9876354)

Open image viewer

The Presidential Palace is getting ready for the Independence Day party. Photo: Kai Jaskari / Yle

Finland's Independence Day is next week. Preparations for the party are being made at the Presidential Palace. The palace party is the most important event of Independence Day.

This year the theme of the party is Our Shared Environment. The president has invited Finns who have done good deeds for the environment.

The theme can also be seen in the food served at the party. Guests get, for example, vegetables and Finnish fish.

## Digital home sales

![Share certificate for a flat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6840233)

Open image viewer

Paper share certificates may already be very old. Photo: Sami Tammi / Yle

Buying homes is changing. Until now, the buyer of a flat has got a paper share certificate. The share certificate shows who owns the flat.

At the beginning of next year, a new digital system comes into use. At first, all new flats will be sold and bought in the digital system. Gradually, old share certificates will also be moved to the digital system.

It is a new way to save time and money, because in the digital system deals can be made quickly and easily. Experts say the new system is safe.

## Healthy sauna

![Throwing water on the sauna stove](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10066286)

Open image viewer

This is how health improves. Photo: Petri Jauhiainen / Yle

Sauna is healthy. The universities of Eastern Finland and Jyväskylä have studied how sauna affects health.

Sauna bathing reduces deaths whose cause is a heart and blood vessel disease. Sauna is good for you, because for example it lowers blood pressure.

It is good to go to the sauna more than once a week. Sauna affects health in a positive way if you stay in the sauna for at least 10 or 15 minutes.
