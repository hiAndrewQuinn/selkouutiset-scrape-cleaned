## The USA has left Afghanistan

![The picture shows fireworks fired by the Taliban in Kabul. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12079303)

The Taliban celebrated the US departure. Fireworks were seen in the sky. Photo: AOP

The troops of the US and other Western countries have left Afghanistan. The last US army plane took off from Kabul airport at midnight.

The US and other Western countries evacuated more than 123,000 people from Afghanistan.

The US army was in Afghanistan for 20 years. Afghanistan is the longest war in US history.

About 4 million Finns, that is almost 72 per cent of Finns, have already received at least 1 corona vaccine dose.

Across the whole EU area too, the coverage of 1 corona vaccine dose is now over 70 per cent. Over 250 million citizens of EU countries have received at least 1 corona vaccine dose.

There are 617 new coronavirus infections in Finland.

## Estonia's new president

![Alar Karis, chosen as President of Estonia in 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2736,h_1539,x_0,y_141/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-848267612e0de9c339d/1630408248)

Open image viewer

Alan Karis. Photo: Priit Mürk / ERR

A new president has been chosen in Estonia. Estonia's new president is **Alar Karis**. He is the director of the National Museum of Estonia.

Alar Karis will start as President of Estonia on 11 October. The work of the current president, **Kersti Kaljulaid**, ends then. Kaljulaid was not a candidate to be president again, because she did not have enough support in parliament.

In Estonia the president is chosen by the Estonian Parliament. The public cannot vote for the president.
