# Tuesday 19.5.2020 (radio)

The government has agreed on new guidelines for restaurants. The restaurant industry organisation is not satisfied with the government's guidelines. Fewer people were killed or injured in road traffic in April than usual. Dogs can smell the coronavirus.

## The government made guidelines for restaurants

The government has agreed on new guidelines for restaurants.

Restaurants are now closed because of the risk of corona, but they may open their doors to customers at the beginning of June. New guidelines are needed so that the coronavirus does not spread in restaurants.

The new guidelines stress the cleanliness of restaurants. Customers must be able to wash or disinfect their hands when they come into the restaurant. Restaurants may not have buffet tables where customers take food themselves.

The government also wants to make sure that there are not too many customers in restaurants at the same time. Therefore restaurants must reduce their customer seats by 50 per cent.

The government also agreed that restaurants may be open from 6 to 23.

Until the end of May, restaurants may only sell takeaway food, that is, take-away portions.

## The restaurant industry criticises the guidelines

![Waiter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6850023)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

The tourism and restaurant industry organisation MaRa is not satisfied with the government's guidelines. MaRa says that the government wants to reduce restaurants' customer seats too much.

The government wants to reduce customer seats by 50 per cent. In MaRa's view, seats can be reduced by 25 per cent at most.

According to the organisation, there is not work for all employees if there are not enough customers.

## Few serious accidents in road traffic

![The picture shows an almost empty motorway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_419/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6613095e870674e8bae/1585913034)

Open image viewer

There has been little traffic because of the corona. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

In road traffic, fewer people have been killed and injured than a year ago.

In April this year, 12 people were killed in road traffic. That is 5 fewer than in April last year.

In April, about 100 fewer people were injured in road traffic than a year ago.

The reason is that there has been less traffic than usual this spring because of the corona. There has been little traffic everywhere in Finland.

## A dog can smell corona

![dog](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2899,h_1631,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6542475e70c80aec97b/1584449592)

A dog's sense of smell is remarkably good. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

Dogs can smell the coronavirus.

Researchers at the University of Helsinki say that dogs may be able to smell the coronavirus very accurately. A dog's sense of smell may even be more precise than a laboratory test.

In studies, dogs quickly learned to tell whether a urine sample contains corona or not. However, supporters say that the matter needs to be studied more.
