# Thursday 7.5.2020 (radio)

Yle party support survey. Finland's exports are getting harder. Restaurants want more support. Early potatoes will soon be in the shops.

## Party support survey

![Party support](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11337671)

Open image viewer

Yle's support survey. Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

Yle has again asked Finns which party they support. The most support goes to the SDP. 22% of respondents support the SDP.

The second most support goes to the National Coalition Party. Just over 18% of respondents support the National Coalition Party.
Third most support goes to the Finns Party, which just under 18% of respondents support.

The governing party SDP has risen to be the most popular party, ahead of the Finns Party. Experts say that the popularity of the SDP is perhaps due to the fact that the SDP is the party of Prime Minister **Sanna Marin**. Many Finns are satisfied with how Sanna Marin has led the government in the corona situation.

The support figures for all parties are on Yle's website, yle.fi.

## Exports are getting harder

![Outokumpu](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11040898)

Open image viewer

Finland exports steel abroad, for example.  Photo: Outokumpu

The corona situation is making Finland's exports harder. Buying goods and services has decreased a lot in various countries of the world. It is difficult for Finnish companies to sell machines or goods to other countries, for example.

The Research Institute of the Finnish Economy, Etla, says that Finland's exports may fall by more than 10% this year.

Finland may get new export opportunities from digital technology. Finland knows how to make many digital services.

## Restaurants want more support

![A glass of wine in the foreground and people in the background on a terrace](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11337930)

Open image viewer

Restaurants want more support because of the corona situation.  Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

Restaurants say that the support for restaurants is too small. On Wednesday the government said that 120 million euros of support will be given to restaurant businesses.

Restaurant companies say that the support is not enough to help all restaurants. Restaurant companies say that the support must help big companies more. Now the support helps small companies more.

Restaurant companies say that the support must be raised to 350 million euros.

## Early potatoes are ripening

![Early potatoes are taken from the field to be washed and put into cold storage, from where the potatoes are packed for customers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9739509)

Open image viewer

Early potatoes are taken from the field to be washed and put into cold storage. Then the potatoes are packed. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

Early potatoes will soon ripen in the potato fields. Finnish early potatoes will go on sale in about three weeks, that is, at the end of May.

The first early potatoes were planted in March. Potato growers hope for warm weather and some rain at times. Then the harvest of early potatoes will be good.

There will be enough early potatoes for the shops from the beginning of June.
