## Finns and Islam

![Muslim women on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10355416)

Open image viewer

Finns have a critical attitude towards Islam as a religion. Photo: AOP

Many Finns are against the Islamic religion.

An American research institute asked Finns about their attitudes. 62 percent of Finns say that Islam does not fit with Finnish culture and Finnish values.

The study was carried out in many countries in Europe. In Sweden, Portugal and France, people are not as much against the Islamic religion.

80 percent of Finns accept that their neighbour is a Muslim.

## Drivers speed

![Traffic monitoring camera](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9642222)

Open image viewer

A "tin policeman", that is an automatic speed camera, is effective. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Motorists speed a lot in Finland.

This year 350 000 motorists have received a fine or a warning for speeding.

Most have received a fixed penalty fine. It is either 140 or 170 euros.

Many motorists get a fine because the police monitor effectively.

## The president does not want gifts

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10355528)

Open image viewer

Finnish President Sauli Niinistö turns 70. Photo: Sakari Luhtala / Yle

Finnish President Sauli Niinistö turns 70 next week.

President Niinistö announced today that he does not want gifts. President Niinistö hopes that, instead of gifts, the money is given to charity.

The president says that money can be donated to two targets.

One is the Tukikummit foundation. It helps young people.

The other is the Laupeudentyö association. It helps all those with little money.

## A book about Kimi Räikkönen's life

![Kimi Räikkönen at the book launch event.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10356323)

Open image viewer

Kimi Räikkönen drives a Formula car. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finnish Formula driver Kimi Räikkönen tells his life story in a book.

In the book, Räikkönen tells how he became a racing driver. Räikkönen also says that he did not like school. He quit school and went out into the world alone.

Kimi Räikkönen is now 39 years old. He races in Formula races in a Ferrari car.

Kimi Räikkönen has won the Formula world championship once.
