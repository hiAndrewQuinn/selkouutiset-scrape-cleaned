The second week of the government negotiations has started. The National Coalition, the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats are taking part in the government negotiations.

The leader of the government negotiations, Petteri Orpo, says that the parties have also talked about the rules of the negotiations. The reason is that there has been nasty writing between the parties on social media.

For example, Finns Party politicians have written nastily about RKP. RKP chair Anna-Maja Henriksson reminded on Monday that negotiators must respect each other.

Aalto University has broken the law. That is what Deputy Chancellor of Justice Mikko Puumalainen says.

English has pushed out the Finnish language in some Aalto University courses. This is illegal, says Deputy Chancellor of Justice Puumalainen.

Puumalainen reminds that English must not be used in teaching so much that the use of Finnish in teaching ends.

## Plain Speaker of the Year competition

![Student working at Spesia Puoti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4548,h_2558,x_0,y_149/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6451845e4e953426ce8/1582210057)

Vocational institute Spesia is one of the finalists in the Plain Speaker of the Year competition. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

The finalists of the Plain Speaker of the Year competition have been published. The Plain Speaker of the Year competition looks for a person or organisation that has best promoted plain language.

3 competitors got into the final. The finalists are Vocational institute Spesia, the Selkovantaalainen newspaper and the Tampere University research project on plain Swedish.

Students at Vocational institute Spesia need special support. At Spesia, plain-language teaching materials are made, and plain language is also used in teaching.

Selkovantaalainen is the first plain-language local newspaper. It is delivered free to all homes in Vantaa. Plain-language information is important in Vantaa, where many languages are spoken.

The Tampere University research project on plain Swedish wants to improve the situation of people who need plain Swedish. It is important to remember plain Swedish too when talking about the need for plain language in Finland.

On Plain Language Day, that is 29 May, it will be known which finalist wins the competition and becomes Plain Speaker of the Year.

The weather is dry and in many places sunny.

On Tuesday the weather warms. The temperature in the middle parts of the country is about 17 degrees.

Later in the week the weather will warm more. The temperature rises close to 20 degrees.

On the coasts, near the sea, it is colder, because the sea water is still cold. The sea also cools the air.
