## Poverty in families with children

![Tiina Ristikari, research professor at Itla, child family poverty, expert, Saunalahti, Espoo, ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_144/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116031464e4865ab1ef2/1692698263)

Open image viewer

Research professor Tiina Ristikari of Itla. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

Poverty in families with children has increased in Finland. In two years, the number of poor children has grown by about 30 000 children. Almost 125 000 children live in a poor family.

Many poor families are worried about their finances. Prices and interest rates are rising, and for example unemployment is increasing.

The Children's Foundation's research professor at Itla, **Tiina Ristikari**, says that the government must notice the situation of families with children. Ristikari hopes that the government will not make cuts that make the finances of families with children harder.

## Death of Jevgeni Prigožin

![Ett foto av Wagnergruppens ledare Prigozjin med gevär i handen, fotot omgivet av röda nejlikor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5363,h_3017,x_0,y_42/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116296464e968781a0fb/1693018408)

Open image viewer

Many experts believe that Prigozhin's death was a murder. Photo: EPA

Russia confirms that the leader of the Wagner mercenary army, **Jevgeni Prigožin**, died in Wednesday's plane crash. Russia's investigative committee says that a DNA test confirms that one of the dead is Jevgeni Prigozhin.

Also, Russia's air transport agency says that the passenger list of the plane matches the dead who were found at the crash site.

The plane that carried Prigozhin and 9 other people crashed to the ground in Russia on Wednesday. Experts in Western countries believe that the fall was a murder.

President **Sauli Niinistö** challenges Finns to move and talk. The president took part in a walking event at Töölönlahti in Helsinki on Sunday.

At the event, people could walk and chat with President Niinistö.
