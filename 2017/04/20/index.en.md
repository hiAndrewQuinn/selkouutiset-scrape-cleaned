# Thursday 20.4.2017 (radio)

Fake oil has been sold in Finland. The police wrote many fines during intensified surveillance. Big riots in Venezuela. Aerobics interests fitness fans again.

## An oil scam was uncovered in Finland

![Motor oil oil.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9572694)

Open image viewer

Motor oil has been faked.  Photo: AOP

Car motor oil that is fake has been brought to Finland. The oil was really worse and cheaper than the package claimed. At least 1 Finnish trading company has already sold more than 13,000 litres of fake oil to customers in different parts of Finland. In addition, Customs has seized more than 16,000 litres of oil. The oil was brought to Finland from Estonia and Poland.

Customs suspects that more than 30 people and 4 companies have taken part in the scam. The case has now gone to the prosecutor.

The wrong oil can break a car's technology.

## The police wrote many fines during intensified surveillance

![A police officer measures speeds.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9572741)

Open image viewer

The police monitored speeding for 24 hours.  Photo: Kalle Ranta / Yle

The police wrote many fines in a big police operation. The police wrote almost 7,000 speeding fines during the intensified surveillance. The police say that the number was almost the same as a year ago. The police say that otherwise traffic went calmly.

The intensified surveillance began on Wednesday morning and ended on Thursday morning. The operation focused on speed monitoring, but at the same time 19 people were caught whom the police were looking for for other crimes. In addition, 30 other crimes came under police investigation, and their investigation continues after the operation.

The surveillance operation was organised at the same time in many European countries. Finland took part now for the 3rd time.

## Unrest in Venezuela

![Riot police behind their white car, demonstrators crouch behind a small shield in a street in Caracas](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9572616)

Open image viewer

Big riots in Venezuela.  Photo: EPA/MIGUEL GUTIERREZ

In Venezuela in South America, the demonstrations are getting more intense. On Wednesday the opposition organised demonstrations in different parts of Venezuela, demanding new presidential elections. At least 3 people died when demonstrators and police clashed. The president's supporters also took part in the clashes, and they used weapons against the opposition's supporters.

Almost 70 percent of people oppose Venezuela's president Nicolas Maduro, but he has not agreed to new elections.

## Aerobics interests fitness fans

![Aerobics exercises. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9571296)

Open image viewer

Aerobics came to Finland in the 1980s.  Photo: Yle

Dance exercise, that is, aerobics, is popular again in Finland. Aerobics came to Finland already in the 1980s, when actor Jane Fonda's workout videos spread to Finland.

At the beginning of the 2000s, heavy fitness workouts using weights were in fashion. Experts say that now people want to do exercise that combines beautiful movements and good music.
