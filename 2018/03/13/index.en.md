# Tuesday 13.3.2018 (radio)

Investing money in homes is popular. Record amounts of pulp are being sold from Finland abroad. The EU gives young people free Interrail tickets. Finns have started to eat insects. The prosecutor wants suspended prison sentences for many Kittilä decision-makers.

## Many invest money in homes

![The courtyard of a block of flats](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9479941)

Open image viewer

A rental flat is a popular investment. Photo: Terri Niemi / Yle

Investing money in homes is popular.

In ten years, tens of thousands of Finns have started to invest money in homes. People buy flats and rent them out. Rental flats are always needed in cities, and residents pay high rents.

Money is also invested in rental flats because interest rates are low. Interest-rate investments do not earn money well. In addition, many investors have bought flats with cheap borrowed money.

## Pulp sells well around the world

![Sheets of pulp at Metsä Fibre's Joutseno pulp mill.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10083465)

Open image viewer

Pulp is made, for example, in Joutseno. Photo: Jari Tanskanen / Yle

Finland now sells more pulp abroad than anything else.

Pulp is made from wood, and paper is made from pulp. Finnish pulp is sold most to Asia. There, pulp is made into, for example, toilet paper and kitchen paper, which Chinese and other Asian people use more and more.

People are also buying more goods than before on the internet, and that is why a lot of packaging paper and cardboard is used.

## Free Interrail tickets for young people

![Interrailers at a railway station in Germany.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10113830)

Open image viewer

With an Interrail ticket, young people can travel by train for a month. Photo: AOP

The European Union is giving as many as 30,000 18-year-olds a free Interrail ticket.

With an Interrail ticket you can travel by train for a month in 30 European countries. The ticket normally costs about 500 euros. The EU hopes that young people will get to know Europe and young people from other countries.

In a couple of years, perhaps all young people living in the EU who turn 18 will get a free ticket. But that will be expensive, because every year about 5 and a half million Europeans turn 18.

## 20 firms in Finland raise house crickets for food

![House crickets in a bowl and in a salad.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10109550)

Open image viewer

A portion of house crickets. Photo: Pekka Pantsu / Yle

Finns have started to eat insects.

For example, house crickets can be bought in shops and restaurants. This winter about 20 cricket farms have been started in Finland, and more are coming. An insect firm estimates that in a year, at least 10 times more crickets will be raised than now.

## Suspended prison sentences demanded for Kittilä decision-makers

![District prosecutors Katri Junnikkala-Heikkinen (left) and Sari Anttonen at the preparatory hearing on the charges of misconduct in office against Kittilä municipal decision-makers in the Regional State Administrative Agency building in Rovaniemi on 13 March](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10114799)

Open image viewer

The prosecutors in the Kittilä trial, which was prepared today. Photo: Kaisa Siren / Lehtikuva

In Lapland, 27 decision-makers of the municipality of Kittilä are accused of misconduct in office.

The court's prosecutors say that the Kittilä decision-makers prevented municipal manager Anna Mäkelä from doing her work. The decision-makers also dismissed the municipal manager. The prosecutor says that the dismissal broke many laws, for example the constitution.

The prosecutor demands that almost all the Kittilä decision-makers get a suspended prison sentence. The Kittilä trial begins in the autumn.
