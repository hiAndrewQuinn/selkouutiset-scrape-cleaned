## Sunila pulp mill

![Stora Enso's Sunila pulp mill in Sunila, Kotka.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4466,h_2512,x_0,y_120/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116363064ec8872a20f4/1693223238)

Sunila pulp mill is in Kotka. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

Forest company Stora Enso is closing the Sunila pulp mill. The mill is in the city of Kotka.

Stora Enso stopped the Sunila pulp mill in May. Now Stora Enso has decided that it will not restart the mill.

The pulp mill uses wood as its raw material. Stora Enso says that the price of wood has risen so much that making pulp at the Sunila mill is no longer profitable.

Closing the Sunila pulp mill is bad news for Kotka. The mill's 240 employees will be left without work.

## Siklatilat bankruptcy

![Siklatilat head office in Tupos, Liminka.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_515/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116715564f5c46c193a2/1693829238)

Open image viewer

Siklatilat's head office is in Liminka. Photo: Rami Moilanen / Yle

Bad news from the construction industry continues. Now the construction company Siklatilat is going bankrupt, and the company's operations will end.

Siklatilat has built, for example, homes, service homes, day-care centres and schools.

Siklatilat's finances have worsened, for example, because the prices of building materials and the interest rates on loans have risen.

Many of Siklatilat's employees have already been on forced leave, that is, they have had no work and no pay. Now they will lose their work completely.

## State property

![Railway track in sunny summer weather.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_3,y_372/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1126362648181a9dca4b/1686209232)

Open image viewer

The government wants money, for example, to improve train traffic. Photo: Johanna Lehtola / Yle

The government intends to sell state property. By selling the property, the government intends to raise 4 billion euros.

The government intends to use the money, for example, for train traffic, health care and the army.

In this way the government can carry out its plans without the state having to take on more debt.

However, experts disagree about whether it is worth selling state property. For example, shares bring profit to the state.

## Puppy in shop

![A puppy sleeps.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1558,h_876,x_1,y_100/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116706364f5a08f5ae8d/1693822155)

Open image viewer

A very young puppy was found in the shop. Photo: South-West Finland Police

In a shop in Turku on Monday there was an unusual situation. A small puppy was found in the shop.

The puppy was taken to an animal care home, and the police started to look for the puppy's owner using social media.

The police said on social media that the puppy is a female dog. The police did not know the breed of the dog. It could even be a polar bear, the police wrote about its white, fluffy snout.

More seriously, the police remind that leaving such a young dog in a shop can be an animal protection crime.
