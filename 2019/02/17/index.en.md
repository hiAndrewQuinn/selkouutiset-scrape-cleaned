# Sunday 17.2.2019 (radio)

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä says that the managers of care companies are responsible for mistakes in elderly care. A 15-year-old boy died at a ski resort. Finns are buying more clothes than before. A new treatment for leukemia patients is being introduced in Finland. A breeding ban has been declared for the Saimaa ringed seal.

## Elderly care

![Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10650500)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The discussion about elderly care continues. Prime Minister Juha Sipilä spoke about elderly care in a radio interview on Sunday.

The Prime Minister said that the managers of care companies are responsible for the mistakes. In many care homes, the elderly have received poor care. Sipilä thinks the care has sometimes been so bad that the police should investigate the matter.

The police will review the situation on Monday together with Valvira. Valvira is the agency that supervises the social and health care sector.

## Skiing accident

![Himos ski resort in Jämsä on 17 February](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10650495)

Open image viewer

Himos ski resort. Photo: Hannu Rainamo / Lehtikuva

The police are investigating the death of the boy at the ski resort. A 15-year-old boy died at the Himos ski resort on Saturday.

The boy died when he was skiing in the forest outside the official slopes. The boy went with his friends to a hill that is not marked as a ski area.

The hill is difficult for skiers. There are steep parts on the hill and a lot of snow.

The friends reported the accident. The police are investigating how the accident happened. The police do not suspect a crime.

## Clothing purchases

![woman looking at clothes](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10650483)

Open image viewer

Finns are buying more clothes than before. Photo: AOP

Finns are buying more clothes and shoes than before. Last year sales grew by about 2 percent.

However, people spend less money on clothes than before. Finns buy more clothes but at a lower price.

Clothing prices have gone down, for example because of online shopping. Competition on price is tough, and clearance sales are common.

## Leukemia

![Two lab nurses are using a CliniMACS machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2048,h_1152,x_0,y_42/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-42840659bb9136a3447/1518440297)

There are many kinds of treatment for leukemia today. Photo: Yle, Tero Kyllönen

A new treatment for leukemia patients is being introduced in Finland. Leukemia is a blood cancer.

The new treatment is based on gene technology. The treatment helps child patients.

A patient usually gets the treatment only once. It can help when other treatments do not help.

It is estimated that about 10 child patients a year need a new treatment. One treatment costs 300,000 euros.

## Saimaa ringed seal

![Saimaa ringed seal on the ice.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10008488)

Open image viewer

Saimaa ringed seal on the ice of Lake Saimaa. Photo: Miina Auttila / Metsähallitus

A breeding ban has been declared for the Saimaa ringed seal. The Saimaa ringed seal lives in the lake area of Saimaa, in Eastern Finland. It gives birth to its pups in February.

The Saimaa ringed seal looks after its pups in a nest that it builds in the snow. That is why the environmental organisation WWF hopes that people will not move around on Saimaa now and disturb the seals.

The Saimaa ringed seal is a Finnish animal. It is not found anywhere else in the world. There are only about 400 Saimaa ringed seals.
