## Corona infections in Finland

![Allan Pöyhönen is waiting for a vaccination; Sirkka Pöyhönen is just being given a shot.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5623,h_3163,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-769970601bb52fe9bae/1612431508)

A record number of new corona infections has been reported in Finland.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare said on Wednesday that there are 872 new corona infections. That is more than has ever been reported in one day before.

The number of corona patients in intensive care has grown. Now young corona patients under 40 years old are also ending up in hospital.

Good news is that corona vaccines work well. Few elderly people fall ill with corona, because the elderly have already been vaccinated against corona.

## Teaching during the corona era

![Katriina Raine teaches an expression lesson remotely.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11835343)

Open image viewer

Distance teaching demands a lot from the teacher. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

The corona era has been difficult for teachers.

A survey by the teaching profession's trade union says that about 30 percent of teachers have considered changing field during the corona era. The most teachers who have considered changing field are in basic schools and upper secondary schools.

Distance teaching has demanded a lot from teachers. However, most teachers think that the education field has got through the corona era well.

Teachers are also worried about pupils. Most teachers think that pupils have not learnt as much during the corona era as usual. Teachers think that pupils have also been tired and have been stressed.

Teachers hope that more teachers will be hired to schools.

![Voi e-scooter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3570,h_2008,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6936455ef5bd0fee9e3/1593163305)

An unusual traffic accident has happened in Turku.

A young woman who was riding an electric scooter died in the accident. The woman died when she got under a bus at a junction.

This is the first time in Finland that a driver of an electric scooter has died in a traffic accident.

The police are investigating the matter.

## Fires in southern Europe

![A firefighter put out a ground fire on Tuesday near Athens, in Varymbombi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12047052)

Open image viewer

A firefighter is putting out a ground fire near Athens. Photo: Orestis Panagiotou / EPA

Ground fires continue in the Mediterranean area.

Ground fires have burned forests and buildings. People have also died because of the ground fires.

In Greece there are ground fires around the capital, Athens. In Turkey there are ground fires in the southern part of the country.

Extinguishing work is hard, because the area is dry and hot. The temperature is over 40 degrees.
