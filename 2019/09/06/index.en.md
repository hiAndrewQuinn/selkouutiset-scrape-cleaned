# Friday 6.9.2019 (radio)

Finland is postponing the light car law. There are shortcomings in how day-care centres work. Immigrant doctors often have language problems. Robert Mugabe has died. A mountain collapsed in Norway.

## Light car law is postponed

![light cars at a car dealership yard in Vantaa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3072,h_1728,x_0,y_672/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5887905d7208c20cd2d/1567755104)

A light car is an ordinary passenger car whose top speed is limited to 45 kilometres per hour. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

Finland is postponing the law on light cars. Finland wanted to allow light cars in November. The law on light cars must be postponed, because the EU does not approve it.

The European Commission estimates that light cars are not safe for young drivers and other road users. Finland is moving the light car law back by one year.

Finland will make a new law so that the EU can approve it.

## Quality of early childhood education varies

![Two children play in a sandpit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10909547)

Open image viewer

Day-care centres are run under the Early Childhood Education Act. Photo: Jussi Mansikka / Yle

There are shortcomings in how day-care centres work. A new study says that many day-care centres do not do all the things that the Early Childhood Education Act requires.

For example, children are not read to every day in all day-care centres. Art or music lessons are rare in some day-care groups.

The biggest shortcomings are in the groups for children under 3 years old. Day-care centres are guided by the Early Childhood Education Act. Early childhood education means that children are taught different skills at day-care.

## Immigrant doctors have language problems

![Stethoscope in a doctor's hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4113,h_2314,x_0,y_244/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-65475580de70b87954/1529046208)

Open image viewer

There are about 2,000 doctors in Finland whose first language is not Finnish or Swedish. Photo: Alex Proimos

The language skills of immigrant doctors cause problems for patients and doctors. According to a study, many immigrant doctors do not speak Finnish well.

Language problems can put patient safety at risk. The patient does not always understand what the doctor means, or the doctor does not understand what the patient means. Because of the doctors' language problems, nurses have to check many things, and the nurses' workload grows.

There are about 2,000 doctors in Finland whose first language is not Finnish or Swedish.

## Robert Mugabe has died

![Robert Mugabe](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10958539)

Open image viewer

Robert Mugabe 1924-2019 Photo: Sven Simon / AOP

The former president of Zimbabwe, **Robert Mugabe**, has died. Mugabe was 95 years old. He was in hospital in Singapore for a long time. Robert Mugabe was president of Zimbabwe for almost 40 years.

Mugabe came to power in Zimbabwe in the early 1980s, but he was forced to resign in 2017, when soldiers seized power.

## Mannen mountain collapsed

![Helicopter. Mannen mountain in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10866108)

Open image viewer

Mannen mountain. Photo: Terje Pedersen / EPA

A huge landslide has happened on a mountain in Norway. A large piece has collapsed from the western peak of Mannen mountain in western Norway. Big rocks rolled and sand poured down the side of the mountain. Residents have had to leave the area, because it is dangerous there.

Mannen mountain in western Norway is about 1.3 kilometres high.
