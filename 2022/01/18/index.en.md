# Tuesday 18.1.2022 (radio)

The government has discussed the corona situation. The peak of corona infections may be in a couple of weeks. Corona weakens conscripts' ability to cope. Metsähallitus is looking for shepherds. The weather.

## Government discussed the corona situation

![Sanna Marin spoke to the media at the Säätytalo on 18 January.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5241,h_2948,x_0,y_285/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90346161e6e24c0da61/1643201597)

The government has negotiated about the corona situation.

The government discussed, for example, whether restrictions and ways of working should be changed because of the omicron variant of the coronavirus. The government also discussed changing the corona pass into a vaccination pass.

After the negotiations, Prime Minister Sanna Marin said that corona restrictions will continue until the middle of February. After that, the restrictions will be gradually abandoned.

Marin said, however, that restrictions on group hobbies for children and young people will end already in late January. Universities can then also switch to hybrid teaching, that is, they can give in-person teaching in addition to remote teaching.

## Infection peak at the turn of the month

![A corona patient with hands clasped in an isolation room, with a nurse in protective clothing beside him.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_522/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88950861b0adaf5de33/1639141839)

A corona patient in hospital. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The Institute for Health and Welfare estimates that the peak of corona infections may be ahead, at the turn of January and February.

THL says that testing and tracing can no longer slow the spread of infections. Instead, the focus must now be on promoting vaccinations and limiting situations where there is a high risk of infection. People must also be directed to home isolation if they have symptoms.

THL says that these measures are needed until the load on hospitals decreases.

## Conscripts' ability to cope is a worry

![Otto Pesonen's home album.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12273418)

Open image viewer

Corporals Junnola and Pesonen are in isolation in the gym at the Vekaranjärvi garrison because of a corona infection. Photo: Otto Pesonen's home album.

The Conscripts' Union is worried about the conscripts' ability to cope, because the corona situation in the military has worsened quickly.

The Conscripts' Union says that many conscripts, for example, have not been able to take leave for many weeks, because leave has been cancelled due to illness or quarantine.

During the early part of this year, about 2,200 corona infections have already been found among conscripts. During the whole of last year, conscripts had a total of about 1,350 corona infections.

## Metsähallitus is looking for shepherds

![A woman crouching with sheep.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2016,h_1134,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83255160f14a51e6586/1626426110)

Open image viewer

Elina Rinta-Lusa was a sheep shepherd for a week last summer at Punkaharju. Photo: Jouni Kirvesmies / Yle

Metsähallitus is again looking for shepherds for next summer. Shepherds are needed at 15 sites across the country.

A shepherd can rent a farm for a week from Finland's traditional cultural landscape. They look after the farm's sheep, cows or horses. The shepherd's duties include, for example, giving the animals fresh drinking water and taking them to a new pasture.

Registration for the Shepherd Weeks lasts for a week. Shepherds are chosen by lot during February.
