## Bomb attack in Turkey

![Crime scene investigators examining the explosion site on Istiklal shopping street in Istanbul.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3275,h_1844,x_0,y_62/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10335556371ad0396f8a/1668398330)

Turkish authorities are investigating the site where the bomb attack happened. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

A bomb attack has happened in Turkey. A bomb exploded on Sunday in the city of Istanbul.

6 people died in the attack. More than 80 people were injured.

Turkish police have arrested more than 40 people because of the bomb attack.

Turkey says that the PKK, that is, the Kurdistan Workers' Party, carried out the bomb attack. The PKK says that it did not carry out the attack.

Turkey, the European Union and the USA are of the view that the PKK is a terrorist organisation.

## Ukrainian President in Kherson

![Zelenskyy among soldiers in Kherson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2984,h_1678,x_0,y_72/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1033632637210c9b4782/1668419871)

Open image viewer

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy met Ukrainian soldiers in Kherson. Photo: Valentyn Ogirenko / REUTERS

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has visited the city of Kherson. Kherson is in Southern Ukraine.

Ukraine retook Kherson from Russia last week. It was a big victory for Ukraine.

Russia occupied Kherson at the very start of the war. Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

President Zelenskyy says that Russian soldiers committed war crimes in Kherson. Ukrainians are celebrating that the Russian army has left Kherson.

The residents of Kherson are happy, but life in the city is still difficult. For example, electricity and water are still cut off.

## Food prices

![Shopping trolleys in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11461537)

Open image viewer

Food is now more expensive than a year ago. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The price of food has risen. Food was almost 16 percent more expensive in October than a year earlier.

The price of flour has risen the most. Flour cost 46 percent more in October than a year earlier.

Eggs, coffee and butter, for example, are also much more expensive than before. They cost over 30 percent more in October than a year ago.

The price of food has risen, because food production is expensive. For example, fertilisers and energy are expensive because of Russia's war.

The football World Cup, that is, the world championship competition, begins at the weekend.

The competition is held in Qatar on the Arabian Peninsula. The weather in Qatar is so hot that the competition is held in winter.

Many people do not like the way Qatar has organised the football World Cup. Foreign workers have built the venues in such poor conditions that many have died and been injured.
