## 20 years since the terror attacks

![Presidents Clinton, Obama and Biden paying their respects to the dead at a memorial for the devastating terror attack 20 years ago in New York.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3822,h_2150,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-852765613cad7d0a8fc/1631366781)

In the USA the terror attacks of 11 September have been remembered. The attacks happened 20 years ago, in 2001.

The memorial for the attacks was held on Saturday in New York. President Joe Biden also took part in the memorial.

In the 2001 attacks the terrorists hijacked aeroplanes and flew them into the towers of the World Trade Center in New York. Thousands of people died in the attacks.

After the attacks the US began military operations in Afghanistan. The US said that the Taliban movement in Afghanistan had helped the perpetrators of the terror attacks. US soldiers only left Afghanistan this summer.

## Pirkanmaa District Court to Africa

![Empty courtroom](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2560,h_1440,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5976465d9c685d61e10/1570531497)

Open image viewer

Witnesses of the Pirkanmaa District Court are in Africa. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

The Pirkanmaa District Court is travelling to Africa from Finland for the second time.

The reason for the trip is crimes that were committed in the Liberian war. There was a war in Liberia about 20 years ago.

The court wants to hear witnesses in Africa. The witnesses may be able to tell more about what happened in the war.

The court is dealing with crimes that an African man may have committed during the war. The man may have murdered and raped people.

The case is being heard in a Finnish court, because the man now lives in Finland. The man himself says that he has not committed any crimes.

## Traffic accident in Kokkola

A traffic accident has happened in Kokkola in Ostrobothnia. 1 person died in the accident and 3 people were injured.

The accident happened on Friday evening, when a light van drove into a ditch. The police are investigating the accident.
