# Thursday 29.6.2017 (radio)

## The social and health care reform

Finland's government has problems with the social and health care reform. The "sote" reform means reforming social and health services.

An important part of the reform is freedom of choice. The idea is that everyone can choose a public or a private service, for example a medical centre. The owner of a public service is society.

The problem is how freedom of choice is arranged. The government has planned that society must set up its own companies for public services.

But members of the Constitutional Law Committee, which is important in Parliament, disagree. They say that society must not be forced to turn public services into companies.

The government plans to change the social and health care reform because of the Constitutional Law Committee's criticism. But the government is holding on to the reform being ready at the start of 2019.

## Environmental crimes

![Building waste in a pile. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9100755)

Open image viewer

For example, waste from a construction site must be disposed of correctly, even though it costs money. Photo: Tero Ylioja / Yle

More environmental crimes are reported to the police than before. In 2016, reports of environmental crimes grew by almost 30 percent.

Environmental crimes are usually committed by companies. For example, they take waste into nature because they want to save money. There are problems, for example, in the construction industry.

Police officer Christian Blomqvist does not believe that more environmental crimes are committed than before. But people report them to the police more often.

## Standard of living

![A grocery store](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9686705)

Open image viewer

Finns think that they have enough of everything. Photo: Orna Ben Lulu / Yle

Finns are satisfied with their standard of living. In a new survey, almost 90 percent of respondents said that they have enough money and property.

The survey was done by Taloustutkimus for Yle. Taloustutkimus research director Tuomo Turja wonders whether Finns are already too satisfied. Satisfied people do not bother to try harder. How will Finland's economy be made to grow then, Turja asks.

## Forest berries

![Lingonberries in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8319632)

Open image viewer

This summer a lot of lingonberries are growing in the forests. Photo: Petri Kivimäki / Yle

There will probably be a lot of wild berries this summer. A normal amount of bilberries is growing, but a lot of lingonberries are growing. There will probably be really a lot of cloudberries.

Berry plants bloomed late this year because the start of the summer was cold. After that the temperature has been good for insects, which pollinate plants by flying from flower to flower.

Of the summer's berries, the bilberry ripens first. In Southern Finland most bilberries ripen in the second half of July.
