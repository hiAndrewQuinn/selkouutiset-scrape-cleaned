# Thursday 10.8.2017 (radio)

Train drivers will go on strike next week. Dangerous level crossings are being taken out of use. A new school year begins. Cheese is being smuggled from Finland to Russia. An old prison camp is being studied in Lapland.

## Train drivers' strike

![VR trains in the rail yard in Pasila.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9502342)

Open image viewer

Because of the strike, perhaps about 1000 train services will not run. Photo: Timo Korhonen / AOP

Train traffic will stop next week because of a train drivers' strike.

The strike begins on Monday evening at 6 pm and ends during the night between Tuesday and Wednesday.

The strike affects long-distance trains, Helsinki commuter trains and freight trains. VR says that perhaps about 1000 train services will not run.

The train drivers are striking because they oppose the government's plans to open rail passenger traffic to competition.

## Dangerous level crossings

![an unguarded level crossing](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8809003)

Open image viewer

Some level crossings are really dangerous because they do not even have barriers. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

There are many dangerous level crossings in Finland. They are being taken out of use little by little, but the pace is slow.

There are over 3200 level crossings in different parts of the country. Fewer than 30 of them are taken out of use each year.

At a level crossing a road crosses a railway, and that is why the crossing is dangerous.

There are over 30 accidents at level crossings every year. This year, 6 people have already died in accidents.

## A new school year begins

![Young schoolchildren on their way to school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9076567)

Open image viewer

Drivers must remember to be especially careful, because there are again a lot of small schoolchildren in traffic. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Many schoolchildren's summer holidays ended this week. Today the school year began, for example, in Tampere, Oulu, Kuopio and in the Finnish-language schools in the Helsinki metropolitan area.

For example, in Tornio school already began on Tuesday.

In Turku, Vaasa and Mikkeli, schools only begin next week.

About 63,000 children start the first grade of comprehensive school this year.

## Cheese smuggling

![Cheeses in a smuggler's car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9767125)

Open image viewer

The Russian customs photos show how the cheese was hidden in the structure of the car. Photo: sztu.customs.ru

Cheese is being smuggled from Finland to Russia.

On Wednesday, Russian customs at the Nuijamaa border crossing caught a man who was trying to smuggle cheese into Russia. Almost 100 kilos of cheese had been hidden in the man's car.

Finnish cheese is wanted and rare in Russia.

Over 3 years ago, Russia banned imports of dairy products from EU countries. The ban was a response to the sanctions that the EU imposed on Russia because of the seizure of Crimea.

Because of the ban, an ordinary person may take at most 5 kilos of cheese into Russia.

## Wartime prison camps

![Hands sort through objects in a box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9768926)

Open image viewer

Archaeologists have dug up many kinds of objects, for example the remains of dishes and military supplies.

In Inari, Lapland, archaeological excavations have started at a German wartime prison camp.

During the Continuation War, Nazi Germany had over 200,000 soldiers in Lapland. They had perhaps 200 prison camps in the area.

Soviet prisoners of war were held in the camps. The prisoners were put to forced labour, for example chopping firewood or building roads.

Researchers estimate that there were perhaps about 30,000 prisoners in the camps. Thousands of them died, for example of hunger.

In the excavations, the researchers have found many kinds of objects, for example the remains of dishes and military supplies.
