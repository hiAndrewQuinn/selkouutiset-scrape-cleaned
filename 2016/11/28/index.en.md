## Finland's economy

![The gate of the Turku shipyard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9147155)

Open image viewer

The Turku shipyard now has a lot of work. Photo: Ari Welling / Yle

Finns trust the country's economy again. Statistics Finland says that Finns have not believed in the economy as much as now for many years. Many also believe that unemployment will go down.

One reason for the trust is good news from industry. For example, the Turku shipyard and the Uusikaupunki car factory have said that they need a lot of new workers.

## Housing prices

![A street in Kruununhaka, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9049009)

Open image viewer

Housing prices have risen the most in Helsinki. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Housing prices are rising in Helsinki and the surrounding area.

Statistics Finland says that old apartments in blocks of flats in the capital region were over 3 percent more expensive in October than a year ago. In Helsinki, housing prices rose the most, almost 5 percent.

Elsewhere in Finland, housing prices have fallen. Prices are falling the most in eastern Finland.

## Saara Aalto

Finnish pop singer Saara Aalto's success in Britain continues.

At the weekend, Aalto went through to the next round in the singing competition X Factor. Now Aalto is already among the 4 best singers. Next weekend one of them will drop out of the competition, and the other 3 will go to the final.

The final is on Saturday, the 10th of December. The winner of Britain's X Factor singing competition will be known on Sunday, 11 December.

## Care of premature babies

![A newborn child in an incubator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9009943)

Open image viewer

The care of premature babies is expensive, but important. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

Premature babies get good care in Finland.

Premature babies need care because they are born too early. Last year, it was decided in Finland that care is given even to babies who are born halfway through the pregnancy.

Finland spends on average 60,000 euros on the care of each premature baby.

## Chinese Christmas decorations

![Santa Claus wholesale trade China Yiwu International Trade Mart.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9311884)

Open image viewer

Chinese Christmas decorations are conquering the world. Photo: Mika Mäkeläinen / Yle

Most of the world's Christmas decorations are made in the city of Yiwu in China.

In Yiwu, for example, elf hats are made. Chinese Christmas decorations are cheap because the workers' pay is low.

The Christmas decorations are sold to the world in a shopping centre that is 30 times bigger than Itäkeskus in Helsinki.

Christmas decorations also come from Yiwu to Finland. But the traders of Yiwu also know that Santa Claus comes from Finland.

## School breaks

![Secondary school pupils running on a sand field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9101224)

Open image viewer

A comprehensive school pupil spends 2000 hours on breaks. Photo: Jani Ilola / Yle

In many schools, shared games and exercise are organised for pupils during breaks.

The pupils can lead the games themselves. Game leaders are trained for this.

Break-time activities are important, because during comprehensive school a pupil spends about 2000 hours on breaks.
