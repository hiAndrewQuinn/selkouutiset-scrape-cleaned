## Here are the new ministers

![Sampo Terho, Jari Leppä and Antti Häkkänen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9583617)

The government is getting 3 new ministers. The ministers have had too much work, and now their jobs are being shared out.

The Centre chose Jari Leppä as Minister of Agriculture and Forestry. The Finns Party chose Sampo Terho as Minister of Culture, Sport and European Affairs, and the National Coalition chose Antti Häkkänen as Justice Minister.

The President will appoint the new ministers to their posts next Friday.

## The development of the economy is seen as good

![Containers at the Vuosaari port.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9532893)

Open image viewer

The number of port containers can also tell of the economy picking up. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Finnish consumers' picture of the country's economy is the brightest in seven (7) years.

A little more than half of consumers believe that Finland's economic situation will improve in the coming year.

Belief in a fall in unemployment is the strongest in a decade. Statistics Finland studied the matter.

## Finns think a terror attack is likely

![Police officers guarding the site of the terror attack in Stockholm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9561194)

Open image viewer

Police guard the place in Stockholm where a terror attack took place. Photo: Maja Suslin / EPA

Yle has asked citizens about terrorism. The survey was carried out by Taloustutkimus.

54 percent think it is likely that a terror attack will take place in Finland within a year. Only 8 percent believe that an attack is unlikely.

20 percent say that attacks in Europe have changed their travel plans.

Terrorism experts say that the risk is possible, but Finland's situation is good. Finland has prepared well for attacks. Of the respondents, 56 percent say that the authorities have prepared well for attacks.

50 percent say that work against social exclusion and effective integration prevent terrorism.

## Veterans' Day was celebrated on Thursday

![Saara Kanerva.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9585156)

Open image viewer

105-year-old Saara Kanerva is reportedly Finland's oldest Lotta. She was assigned to be a dishwasher at a field kitchen for soldiers. The Lotta Svärd was a voluntary women's organisation that supported national defence in the years 1920-1944. Photo: Laura Valta / Yle

National Veterans' Day was celebrated on Thursday. This year's theme is "your Veterans' Day". It is meant to show that Veterans' Day is a celebration for everyone and for people of all ages.

There are about 22,000 war veterans still alive. The average age of war veterans is about 91.

The main celebration of Veterans' Day was held in Lahti.

## 100-year-old Finland received a greeting from Tartu

![Tartu Finnish school greeting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9583737)

Open image viewer

The Finnish school in Tartu has made a singing greeting for 100-year-old Finland. Photo: Yle

A large number of Finnish children and young people live in different parts of the world. They study the Finnish language and Finnish culture at Finnish schools.

As independent Finland now turns 100 years old, the Finnish schools send greetings to Finland on video.

The newest greetings come from the Finnish school in Tartu, Estonia.

You can watch the greetings from the Finnish school in Tartu on the Clear News website at yle.fi/selkouutiset and also on the Clear News Facebook page.
