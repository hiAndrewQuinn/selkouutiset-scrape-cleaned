# Tuesday 20.4.2021 (radio)

Forest giant Stora Enso plans to close the Veitsiluoto mill in Kemi. The police suspect several minors of murder in Helsinki. The tax authority's decision to hide information about the list of high earners is illegal. Manta has already been given a graduation cap.

## Stora Enso closes the Veitsiluoto paper mill

![Paper rolls inside the Veitsiluoto mill.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11892340)

Veitsiluoto mill. Photo: Pirkko Kukko-Liedes / Yle

Forest giant Stora Enso is cutting paper production sharply. The company plans to close the Veitsiluoto mill in Kemi in Finland.

In Sweden, the Kvarnsveden mill is planned to be closed. Demand for paper is weak, and both mills are making losses.

670 jobs will disappear in Veitsiluoto and 440 jobs in Sweden.

The Veitsiluoto mill is the northernmost paper mill in the world. It is also one of Europe's largest paper and board mills. It has been operating for almost 100 years.

Stora Enso plans to focus on packaging, construction solutions and biomaterials.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin has promised that the government will support the workers and the economy of the Kemi area.

The Paper Union says that the closure and the number of redundancies are the largest in the history of the industry.

## Minors suspected of murder

![Helsinki Central Railway Station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11892629)

Helsinki Central Railway Station. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The police suspect several minors of murder in Helsinki. A 24-year-old victim died at Helsinki Central Railway Station on Monday evening.

There was a brawl involving about 10 people at the station, and as a result one person died.

The victim died from stab wounds at the scene.

The police reached some of the suspects right after the incident. All the suspects are minors.

## The tax authority's secrecy was against the law

![Logo of the Tax Administration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8965460)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The tax authority's decision to hide information about the list of high earners is illegal. The Helsinki Administrative Court decided on this.

The tax authority must say who has asked for their own tax information to be removed from the list of the highest earners.

Last year, 4,600 people asked to have their information removed from the list. They referred to the data protection regulation.

Yle and other media appealed the tax authority's decision to keep the information secret. However, the Tax Administration did not agree to tell the media who asked for their names to be removed.

## Manta has already been given a graduation cap

![Students of the Swedish School of Economics' student union washed and capped the Havis Amanda statue on 20 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11892679)

Manta was given its graduation cap this year already before May Day. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The Havis Amanda statue, that is Manta, has already received a student cap. Manta is located at the edge of Helsinki's Market Square.

The capping duty fell to the student union of Hanken Svenska Handelshögskolan, the Swedish-language business school. Manta also received a face mask during the capping.

Traditionally, Manta is capped on May Day Eve, and hundreds of people follow the capping.

Last May Day, the statue was capped virtually because of the corona pandemic. The statue was also covered with a plywood fence. This year too, the statue will spend May Day safely inside fences.

Manta, over 100 years old, needs to be protected from handling that is too rough.
