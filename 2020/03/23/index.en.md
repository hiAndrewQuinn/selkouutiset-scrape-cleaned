# Monday 23.3.2020 (radio)

The government is preparing new restrictions. A total of 700 confirmed coronavirus infections in Finland. Municipal economies are suffering because of the coronavirus. The Tokyo summer Olympics may have to be postponed because of the coronavirus.

## New restrictions may come to Finland

![Empty restaurant in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11270039)

Open image viewer

The government is considering closing restaurants, for example, because of the coronavirus. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the government is preparing new restrictions because of the coronavirus. For example, restaurants could be closed and movement could be restricted further.

Interior Minister Maria Ohisalo says that the government will discuss the matter again this evening.

Last week, the emergency powers act was brought into force in Finland. The Ministry of Justice says that, for example, people in Southern Finland should not travel to other parts of the country unless it is necessary.

The restaurant industry is ready to close restaurants if the government orders it. The Tourism and Restaurant Services MaRa says that now we must think about health.

## At least 700 confirmed infections in Finland

![Coronavirus sample taking in Porvoo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11271805)

Open image viewer

Taking a corona sample. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

So far, a total of 700 coronavirus infections have been confirmed in Finland by laboratory tests.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare THL says that 50 people are in hospital care. 13 people are in intensive care.

So far, 1 person has died in Finland because of the coronavirus. THL says that many people have already recovered from the illness and gone home.

In the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district, the number of infections has grown. From Friday to Sunday, 184 infections were found.

Most of the covid-19 cases found in Finland have been mild, THL says.

## The economy of municipalities is suffering

![Coat of arms of Finnish municipalities in the corridor of the Municipal Building in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9672412)

Open image viewer

Coats of arms of municipalities. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The economy of municipalities is suffering a lot from the corona epidemic.

The corona costs municipalities at least 1.5 billion euros, but nobody knows the exact sum. Much of the costs of municipalities consist of employees' salaries.

The government promises extra help to municipalities that are in financial difficulties. Among other things, municipalities' tax revenues are decreasing because of the corona epidemic. This will show in the economy of municipalities after some time.

Municipal Minister Sirpa Paatero said that the situation of municipalities is being assessed right now. Paatero said that municipal employees may perhaps be moved to other tasks within the municipality.

## Postponing the Olympics

![Tokyo corona](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11264735)

Open image viewer

The coronavirus is about to postpone the Tokyo summer games. Photo: AOP

Japan says that the Tokyo summer Olympics may have to be postponed because of the corona epidemic. Japanese Prime Minister Shinzo Abe said this.

Until now, Japan's political leadership has assured that the Olympics will be held on the original schedule. The games were supposed to start on 24 July.

Yesterday the International Olympic Committee IOC assured that the games will not be cancelled. The IOC promised to decide within 4 weeks when the games can be held.
