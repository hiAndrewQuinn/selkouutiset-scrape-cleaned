## Gas pipeline leak

![A graphic shows on a map the BalticConnector gas pipeline, which starts from Inkoo and runs to Estonia, to Paldiski west of Tallinn.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3952,h_2223,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11834676523df94b477f/1696849981)

A gas pipeline, Baltic-Connector, runs between Finland and Estonia. Photo: Samuli Huttunen / Yle, Mapcreator, OpenStreetMap

There is new information about the leak in the gas pipeline between Finland and Estonia. The gas pipeline started leaking on Sunday.

Today, on Tuesday, the authorities found the place in the pipe where gas is leaking. There is a hole there, which someone may have made on purpose.

Prime Minister Petteri Orpo says that Finns do not need to worry. We have enough gas and energy, says Prime Minister Orpo.

President Sauli Niinistö has discussed the matter with the Secretary General of NATO, Jens Stoltenberg. The defence alliance NATO has promised to help investigate the matter.

## Finnair cancellations

![A Finnair plane takes off from the airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2875,h_1617,x_703,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1005590631b1ba07bfb1/1701790760)

Open image viewer

Finnair planes will not fly to Tel Aviv in Israel this winter. Photo: Tuomas Rimpiläinen / Yle

The airline Finnair is cancelling flights to Tel Aviv in Israel until the end of March.

The reason for Finnair's decision is the violent situation in Israel. Finnair says that safety is the most important thing.

At the weekend the Palestinian militant group Hamas attacked Israel. Since then Israel has attacked Hamas in Gaza.

The Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs hopes that Finns will not travel to Israel now.

## Work-based immigration

![A nurse is feeding an elderly person.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_426/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1045929639315f3d6c11/1670583857)

Open image viewer

A lot of foreign nurses work in the social and health care sector. Photo: Ari Haimakainen / Yle

Work-based immigration to Finland is busy.

It is estimated that this year about 18 000 foreigners will come to Finland directly to work. So they will already have a job waiting for them in Finland.

The number is almost as big as last year. Then more than 20 000 foreigners came to Finland to work.

Workers come from the Philippines, for example. Many Filipinos come to work in social and health care.

Many people from Russia, India and Thailand have also come to work in Finland. Most workers from EU countries have come from Estonia, Latvia, Romania and Poland.

## Aleksis Kivi Day

![The book Seven Brothers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2914,x_0,y_429/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-315829583ad319cba29/1696957498)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle/Jessica Edén

On Tuesday, Aleksis Kivi Day and the day of Finnish literature was celebrated.

Aleksis Kivi lived in the 1800s. He wrote the book Seven Brothers, for example. Seven Brothers came out about 150 years ago, in 1870.

To many people, Finnish literature begins with Seven Brothers. That is why Aleksis Kivi is Finland's national writer, and Aleksis Kivi Day is a flag day.

On Wednesday it is rainy and windy in almost the whole country. In the north it also snows.

On Wednesday the temperature is between 5 and 15 degrees, and in the north between -2 and +4 degrees.

On Thursday more rain will fall over a large part of the country.

In Lapland, as much as 30 centimetres of snow may fall during Wednesday and Thursday in total.
