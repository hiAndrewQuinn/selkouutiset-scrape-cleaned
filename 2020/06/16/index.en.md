# Tuesday 16.6.2020 (radio)

Finland's economy is weakening this year. Many unemployment benefit applications were made during the spring of corona. The anti-terrorism laws may be expanded. There are more wolves than last year. The Oscar gala is delayed.

## Economic forecast has darkened because of corona

![Empty shopping centre in Pasila, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11264977)

Because of the coronavirus, shopping centres have had only a few customers. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Finland's economy will weaken significantly this year, says the Ministry of Finance. The reason is the restrictions that are being used to try to hold back the corona epidemic.

The Ministry of Finance estimates that gross domestic product will fall by 6 per cent this year. Gross domestic product is a measure of domestic production in the national economy.

However, lifting the restrictions will boost the economy, especially in service sectors. The forecast says that the economy will start to recover slowly towards the end of the year.

The forecast is made uncertain by a possible second wave of the coronavirus.

## Many unemployment benefit applications

Many unemployment benefit applications were made during the spring of corona.

Kela received 186,000 new unemployment benefit applications in the spring. That is almost 100,000 more than at the same time last year.

The number of applications was high at the beginning of April. The number turned to rise again at the end of May.

Kela says that, in addition to corona, the number of applications was affected by new students graduating.

## Anti-terrorism laws may be expanded

![Women and children by a lorry.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_90/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6856185ed8fcd63d51a/1621400064)

Open image viewer

Al-Hol camp in Syria. Photo: Ahmed Mardnli / EPA

A new criminal offence of publicly inciting terrorist crimes may be added to Finnish law. The proposal comes from a working group of the Ministry of Justice.

Joining a terrorist group, that is, becoming a member, may in future be punishable. The working group proposes that the punishment be at most 2 years in prison.

The review of the terrorism laws is linked to the government's decision to bring Finns home from the al-Hol camp in Syria.

## There are more wolves than last year

![Wolf](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-33626158f7569366d16/1696250501)

Of the northern predators, the wolf is the most social animal. It prefers to live in a pack. Photo: Juha Laaksonen / Yle

There are now more wolves in Finland than last year. The Natural Resources Institute Finland, Luke, reports this.

The estimate says that in March there were 30 wolf packs in Finland. Of these, 24 packs were entirely on the Finnish side. 6 packs were on both sides of Finland's eastern border.

The total number of packs grew by about 25 per cent from last year.

The estimate says that the number of individual wolves in March was at most just over 240.

There were more packs than before, especially in western Finland. There were probably 18 packs there.

## The Oscar gala is delayed

![Media prepare for the start of the Oscar gala on the red carpet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_36/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6899425ee820db32b8b/1592271195)

The film industry's Oscar gala is being postponed by 2 months because of the corona pandemic. The publication date of the nominees is also delayed.

The Oscars will be handed out at the end of April, 8 weeks later than usual.

This is the 4th time in the history of the American Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences that the gala has had to be moved.

Next year the 93rd Oscar gala will be held.
