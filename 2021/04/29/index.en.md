# Thursday 29.4.2021 (radio)

Government negotiations. Peat producers' May Day march. EU coronavirus certificate. May Day celebration. May Day weather.

## Government negotiations

![The five-party government walks towards the Säätytalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11907738)

Open image viewer

Government ministers on their way to negotiations at the Säätytalo. Photo: Lauri Heikkinen / Government Office

The government has continued its economic negotiations today, Thursday.

Yesterday, Wednesday, the government already agreed on all the big issues. For example, the spending limit will be exceeded next year by 900 million euros, and in two years by 500 million euros.

The government also agreed, for example, on support money for peat production and on changes to earnings-related unemployment security.

Today, Thursday, the government has negotiated about even smaller details.

## Peat producers' May Day march

![Peat extraction at Kurjenneva in Seinäjoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10313072)

Open image viewer

Peat producers are worried about the future of the industry. Photo: Anna Wikman / Yle

Peat producers are holding a May Day march in Helsinki on Friday. The peat producers are worried about the future of the industry.

Yesterday, the government agreed that peat production will get support of tens of millions of euros. However, the use of peat is decreasing all the time. Many peat producers say that peat production is being phased out too quickly.

The peat producers' May Day march will disrupt traffic in Helsinki on Friday morning.

## EU coronavirus certificate

![A beach in Mallorca.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11841714)

Open image viewer

The EU wants a corona certificate so that people can travel. Photo: Cati Cladera / EPA

The European Parliament wants a corona certificate for the EU area. The official name of the certificate is *Covid-19 certificate*. The certificate will make travelling easier.

With a shared vaccination certificate, EU countries can open their borders for travel for the summer.

EU member countries still have to agree on what the corona certificate will be like and how it will be carried out technically.

## May Day celebration

![May Day at Ullanlinnanmäki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8858062)

Open image viewer

In the picture, May Day celebrations from before the corona time. Photo: Leila Oksa / Yle

Tomorrow, Friday, is May Eve. Usually, May Day is celebrated a lot outdoors and in big groups.

Because of the corona situation, May Day cannot be celebrated in the usual way.

Officials remind people that it is good to spend May Day calmly and in small groups. The recommendation is that people should not gather in city centres. The police will monitor May Day celebrations especially in the cities.

The police hope that parents will also remind young people of the May Day guidelines now, in the corona time.

## May Day weather

![Weather map for May Eve.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11908404)

Open image viewer

The May Day weather is cool, but in Southern Finland it will not rain.

The May Day weather is cool. On May Eve, Friday, cloudiness varies and in the central and northern parts of the country there will be snow showers in places.

In the southern part of the country it is mostly dry, meaning it will not rain.

The highest temperature of the day is between 5 and 10 degrees, and in the northern part of the country mostly between -2 and +5 degrees.
