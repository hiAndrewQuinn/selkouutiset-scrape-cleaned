# Sunday 12.2.2017 (radio)

Summer jobs. Haavisto as presidential candidate. Sign language. Winter feeding of birds.

## Summer jobs are increasing

![Sanni Wacklin is doing a summer work placement at Prisma.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8184814)

Many people get a summer job at a shop.  Photo: Petri Puskala / Yle

This year it may be much easier to get a summer job than last year. Finnish companies have said how many summer workers they plan to hire.

About 20 percent of companies plan to hire more summer workers than last year. It is estimated that there may be about 10,000 more summer jobs in Finland this year than last summer. That means there may be at least 100,000 summer jobs in total.

Now is a good time to look for a summer job. A summer job can be found, for example, in a shop, a factory, a building site, a hotel or a restaurant.

## Pekka Haavisto is the presidential candidate

![Pekka Haavisto was chosen as the Greens' presidential candidate at the party council meeting on 12 February 2017 in Lohja. Party council vice chair Katja Andrejev and chair Ville Niinistö congratulated Pekka Haavisto. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9456024)

The Greens unanimously chose Pekka Haavisto as their presidential candidate.  Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The Green party has decided that MP Pekka Haavisto will be the Green presidential candidate. The party held a meeting where everyone wanted Haavisto to be the presidential candidate.

Haavisto said on Saturday that he wants to be a presidential candidate. The Finnish presidential election will be held in January 2018, so there is not long left until then.

Two parties now have a presidential candidate. The first candidate is the Greens' Pekka Haavisto and the second is the Centre's Matti Vanhanen. Matti Vanhanen was Prime Minister of Finland many years ago.

## Sign Language Day in Finland

![Jarkko Köykkä, a sign language interpreter at Oulu's Tervatulli school, and special teacher Miia Kareisto show how to sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8669293)

Sign Language Day was held in Finland on Sunday. Sign language is used by deaf people. Sign language is spoken with hand movements and facial expressions, for example.

Many Sign Language Day events have been held in different parts of Finland. The cinema company Finnkino, for example, has shown the new film Onneli ja Anneli with a sign language interpreter.

## Continuing the winter feeding of birds is important

![Aki Arkiomaa is putting a fat ball on a feeder hanging from a branch. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9429676)

Open image viewer

Winter feeding of birds must continue, even though the weather is getting warmer.  Photo: Ghadi Boustani / Yle

Winter feeding of birds is worth continuing, even though the temperature is now rising. The bird organisation BirdLife Finland says that good winter food for birds is nuts and seeds. Nuts and seeds contain a lot of energy.

Yellowhammers, house sparrows and magpies also eat oats and barley. Bohemian waxwings and blackbirds also eat berries and fruit. Dry bread is not good bird food, because it has very little energy.

BirdLife Finland says that winter feeding of birds is good to continue as long as there is frost at night and snow on the ground. Only later in spring does feeding need to be stopped completely.
