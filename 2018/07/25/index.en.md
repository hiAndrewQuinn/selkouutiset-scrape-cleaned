# Wednesday 25.7.2018 (radio)

The police will investigate why there was a big forest fire in Western Finland last week. At least about 80 people died in the fires in Greece. The University of Helsinki is starting to teach Islamic theology. Finland's seasons have changed. The warm summer also affects the lives of birds. The heat continues.

## The police are investigating the forest fire

![Firefighting at the Pyhäranta forest fire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10316993)

Putting out a forest fire is hard work. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

The police will investigate why there was a big forest fire in Western Finland last week.

The police already know that forestry work was carried out in the forest before the fire started. The police are investigating whether the fire started because of the forestry work.

The forest fire in Pyhäranta lasted many days, and a large area of forest was destroyed. 150 people fought the forest fire.

## Many people died in the fires in Greece

![A ground fire burns on the hills bordering the shore.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6261795)

Open image viewer

Many people fled the ground fire to the seashore. Photo: Alexandros Vlachos / EPA

More people who died in the fires on Monday and Tuesday have been found in Greece. At least about 80 people died in the fires.

The number of dead may rise. There are still many people who have not been found after the fires.

The fires spread quickly in Greece, because Greece is hot and very dry. The fire burned forest, houses and cars.

## Teaching of Islam begins at the University of Helsinki

![University of Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9236198)

Open image viewer

Soon you will be able to study Islamic theology at the University of Helsinki. Photo: AOP

The University of Helsinki is starting to teach Islamic theology. The teaching begins in the autumn.

The university wants to increase knowledge about Islam. The studies will get to know the texts of Islam and Islam in practice.

Teaching in Islamic theology starts with bachelor's studies. Later the university may also start master's studies.

## Winters are getting shorter

![Nordic walker in winter frost in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10088802)

Open image viewer

In Southern Finland and on the Åland Islands, winter has become shorter. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finland's seasons have changed.

In Southern Finland and on the Åland Islands, winter has become shorter, says the Finnish Meteorological Institute. There, winter is now a month shorter than before.

The change is caused by climate change. If climate change continues, Southern Finland may have no winter at all. Climate change raises the temperature of the planet.

## Blackbirds are singing a lot this summer

![Blackbird.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7935117)

Open image viewer

The blackbird's song resembles the playing of a flute. Photo: Pirjo Käpylä, Uurainen

The warm summer also affects the lives of birds.

Blackbirds have been singing a lot this summer. A blackbird marks its territory by singing. The female sings to the chicks once they have hatched.

Many blackbird pairs have managed to nest three times already this summer, and that is why they have been singing a lot.

The blackbird's song sounds like the playing of a flute.

## The heat just continues

![Fan](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10311986)

Open image viewer

The heat does not end. Photo: AOP

The hot weather continues. There is heat every day in Southern Finland and Central Finland. In Lapland Thursday is a slightly cooler day, but at the weekend the heat returns to Lapland too.

Rain brings a little refreshment to the hot summer. In the north it rains on Thursday, and in the south it rains on Friday. The rain comes in showers, and it does not rain all the time.
