# Monday 10.4.2023 (radio)

Party negotiations. Fires. Paper newspapers. Return traffic. The weather.

## Party negotiations

![National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_230/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1033261636f569f28d7d/1668251860)

National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

The parties start negotiating a new government this week. The parliamentary elections were a week ago.

The winner of the election was the National Coalition. That is why National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo starts to lead the parties' negotiations.

Experts estimate that the negotiations will be difficult. The parties must negotiate, for example, on how the new government will save money.

Many parties are needed for a government, so that the government gets a majority in Parliament.

## Fires

![An apartment block where one window is broken and the wall around it is black.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690368)

Open image viewer

There was a fire in an apartment in Lahti. Photo: Petri Niemi / Yle

There have been many fires in Finland at Easter.

Because of the fire in Lahti, 6 people were taken to hospital. One of them was seriously injured.

The fire was in an apartment in an apartment block in central Lahti. The fire did not spread to other apartments.

The police are investigating what caused the fire in Lahti.

There was also a fire in Hämeenlinna, but no one was injured in it.

The fire destroyed part of the Ruununmylly school in Hämeenlinna. The police say that someone may have set the school on fire, possibly on purpose.

In addition, there was a fire in a detached house in Rantasalmi. One person died in the fire.

## Paper newspapers

![Newspapers (Länsiväylä) on the line at the Tuusula printing plant, 17.9.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_384/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5926655d8496b412f98/1569850883)

Open image viewer

Making a paper newspaper is expensive. Photo: Katriina Laine / Yle

Many Finnish newspapers have stopped the Sunday paper edition. It is one way for newspapers to save money.

Lecturer Katja Lehtisaari from the University of Tampere says that the paper edition has been doing badly for a long time. Newspapers have moved to the internet.

Stopping the Sunday paper edition is part of this development, Lehtisaari says.

Finns read a lot of newspapers, and a paper newspaper is part of many Finns' Sunday.

There is no more snow on the roads. Archive image. Photo: Mikko Tuomikoski / Yle

Today, on Easter Monday, the roads have been busy.

Many Finns spent Easter with relatives, and they have returned home during Monday.

Return traffic has been greatest on the big roads of southern Finland. Return traffic has been made easier because there is no more snow on the roads and the roads are already dry.

## The weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690369)

On Tuesday the weather is cloudy in southern and central Finland. In central Finland a little rain may also fall. In the north the weather is sunny on Tuesday too.

On Tuesday the temperature rises above 10 degrees in the south. In places it may be as much as 13 or 14 degrees warmer. In the north it is colder.

On Wednesday the weather gets warmer further in the south. In places the temperature may be as much as 15 degrees. In the north it is still colder, and in Lapland the temperature is around zero in places.
