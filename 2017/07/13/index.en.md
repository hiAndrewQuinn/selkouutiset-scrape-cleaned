# Thursday 13.7.2017 (radio)

Interior Minister Paula Risikko does not expect a large number of asylum seekers to come to Finland. China's best-known human rights activist has died. President Trump is visiting Europe again. Warm, dry weather is expected for the weekend. Greetings from the Finnish school in Cambridge.

## Fewer people are seeking asylum in Finland

![Migrants on board the Aquarius ship in port.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9712479)

A record number of refugees and migrants have come to Italy during the first part of the year. Photo: Carlo Hermann / AFP

Interior Minister Paula Risikko does not believe in a new asylum seeker crisis in Finland.

Risikko said on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme that at most about 8,000 asylum seekers will come to Finland this year. In 2015, a record year, more than 30,000 came.

Risikko says that Finland must still be prepared for the number of asylum seekers to grow.

Over the Mediterranean to Europe, 100,000 migrants and refugees have already come during the first part of the year. Most of them have come to Italy.

Risikko says that other EU countries must think about how they can help Italy.

## Nobel laureate Liu Xiaobo has died

![Liu Xiaobo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9722169)

Open image viewer

Liu Xiaobo fought for democracy and human rights. Photo: EPA

China's best-known human rights activist, Liu Xiaobo, has died. Liu died at the age of 61 of liver cancer.

Liu's illness was found while he was serving an 11-year prison sentence. At the end of June he was moved from the prison to a hospital.

Liu fought to improve democracy and human rights in China. He received the Nobel Peace Prize in 2010.

US President Donald Trump has arrived in France for a visit.

The trip to France is already Trump's second visit to Europe within a week.

## The weekend weather is warm

![Weekend weather maps](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9720854)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

Warm, dry weather is expected for the weekend.

Yle meteorologist Seija Paasonen says that already on Friday there will be dry weather in Southern and Western Finland. In the east and north there may be rain and thunder showers, but the temperature may rise above 20 degrees.

On Saturday and Sunday there will be dry weather in almost the whole country. The temperature will also rise above 20 degrees in almost the whole country.

It is worth enjoying the good weather of the weekend. The weather at the start of next week is unsettled again, as new rain comes from the west.
