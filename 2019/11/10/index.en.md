# Sunday 10.11.2019 (radio)

The postal strike may last about a month. Why is the Finns Party such a popular party? None of the 300 ISIS prisoners captured by Turkey are Finns. Expensive luxury products are bought second-hand online. Finnish singer Alma performs at Christina Aguilera's concerts in Britain.

## The postal strike gets longer

![Sara Luostarinen / postal worker / postal worker / Pasila 11.10.2019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5993385da09abca98ad/1570806510)

About 9,000 Posti employees go on strike on Monday. Photo: Jouni immonen / Yle

The postal strike may last about a month. The postal workers' trade union says that it is extending to 4 weeks the strike that begins tomorrow. Originally, the strike was supposed to last 2 weeks.

If no agreement is found in Posti's collective agreement negotiations, Posti's employees will be on strike until 8 December. That means letters and parcels will reach their destinations much more slowly than usual.

Tomorrow about 9,000 Posti employees will go on strike.

## Dissatisfaction is growing the Finns Party's popularity

![Emilia Palonen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9493069)

Open image viewer

Emilia Palonen. Photo: Yle

Researcher Emilia Palonen says that all kinds of people's dissatisfaction with other parties increases the popularity of the Finns Party. Everyone who opposes, for example, other parties' climate policy supports the Finns Party.

Researcher Emilia Palonen also says that opinions against foreigners are being said out loud more now, because the Finns Party is popular.

In Yle's new poll, the Finns Party was clearly the most popular party. It was supported by 23 percent of respondents. The support of the next most popular party, the National Coalition, was 6 percentage points lower.

## No Finns among Turkey's ISIS prisoners

![Turkish soldiers in their tanks in Northern Syria](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11014679)

Open image viewer

Turkish soldiers captured ISIS terrorists in Syria. Photo: EPA

None of the 300 ISIS prisoners taken by Turkey in Syria are Finns.

Turkey wants to send about 300 ISIS prisoners back to their home countries. The Turkish Interior Ministry told Yle that no one will be sent to Finland, because there are no Finns among these prisoners.

ISIS is a terrorist organisation that tried to set up its own state in Iraq and Syria.

## Expensive luxury goods are bought second-hand

![A Chanel shop window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11061001)

Open image viewer

A luxury bag can cost thousands of euros. Photo: imago images / Levine-Roberts/ AOP

Finns are buying more used luxury goods than before.

People buy expensive, second-hand luxury goods online. Many think that, for example, a used bag can cost a lot, because it can later be sold on at about the same price.

Earlier, people thought that only poor people buy second-hand goods. Now many think that it is smart to buy second-hand quality goods that last a long time.

Luxury goods are sold at online flea markets. There are also online shops that sell only second-hand luxury goods.

Finnish singer Alma performed in London at the weekend. Alma performed at the concerts before the main act, the American Christina Aguilera. Alma sings in a total of six of Aguilera's concerts in Britain and Ireland.

Recently, Alma has also written songs for the American Miley Cyrus, for example. Alma has also written songs for the film Charlie's Angels.
