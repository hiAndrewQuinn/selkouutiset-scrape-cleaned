In Finland almost 700 new corona infections have been reported on Tuesday.

In hospital care and intensive care there are about 100 corona patients in total.

The number of corona infections has grown so much that the areas of Helsinki, Turku and Tampere are already at the most serious level of the corona epidemic. That means strict corona restrictions can be introduced in those areas.

## Distance studies

![Distance learning, University of the Arts](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11298805)

Open image viewer

University of the Arts. Photo: Jussi Mankkinen / Yle

Many people are worried about distance studies during the corona era. For example, studying for a profession can be difficult remotely.

Mika Kuismanen of the Finnish Entrepreneurs organisation says that there are problems, for example, in the construction sector. Not all the work in the field can be learnt properly through distance teaching.

Kuismanen says that students may suffer from poor teaching for a long time. The effects can be felt throughout the whole working life, Kuismanen says.

Students, too, feel that they have not got enough support for their studies during the corona era. In an investigation by the Ministry of Education, more than 40 percent of students felt that their own professional skills had become weaker because of the corona era.

## Alvar Aalto

![Säynätsalo town hall](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8901820)

Open image viewer

Säynätsalo town hall. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Finnish architecture has been praised.

The American newspaper The New York Times has put the Säynätsalo town hall on its list of significant buildings. The list has 25 modern buildings that have been designed especially well.

The designer of the Säynätsalo town hall is the architect Alvar Aalto. Aalto is Finland's most famous architect. He died in 1976.

The Säynätsalo town hall was completed in 1952. The New York Times admires the design of the building and its materials, brick and Finnish wood.

The Säynätsalo town hall is in the Jyväskylä area. Aalto's works also include, for example, Finlandia Hall in Helsinki.
