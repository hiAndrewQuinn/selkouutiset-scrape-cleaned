## The cost of the corona epidemic

![Nurse Jenna Muurama works in the intensive care unit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4988,h_2806,x_0,y_522/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8669216167fe3c50ecd/1634893574)

Intensive care nurse at Turku University Central Hospital in October 2021. Photo: Arash Matin / Yle

Calculations have been made in Finland of the cost of the corona epidemic to society. The costs of corona have been estimated by Professor of Production Economics Paul Lillrank of Aalto University.

Lillrank says that the care of corona patients is dozens of times more expensive than normal care. However, the effects of the pandemic are complicated, so it is difficult to determine the exact price.

Lillrank has estimated the benefits and harms of corona care. The calculation includes, for example, how many human lives the care may save. The calculation says that so far more than 2000 lives may have been saved.

So far the total cost of corona may have been about 20 billion euros in the state budget.

## Corona infections at the shipyard in Helsinki

![Helsinki Shipyard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_556/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7317595f8d699a6c123/1660300206)

Open image viewer

Helsinki Shipyard. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Mandatory health checks are starting at the Helsinki Shipyard Oy shipyard. They have been ordered by the Regional State Administrative Agency for Southern Finland.

More than a thousand employees will be tested because of the corona epidemic.

The Regional State Administrative Agency says that extensive chains of infection have been found at the shipyard this autumn.

## Report on preventing school bullying

![A young person jumps over a backpack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-6259157e0f219d29cc/1474359987)

Open image viewer

Young people and children do not get enough help from adults with school bullying. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Methods used in schools against bullying will be studied next spring. The national education evaluation centre Karvi will investigate the effectiveness of the methods.

A similar evaluation has not been done in Finland before.

About half of school bullying never comes to the knowledge of adults.

## Dissolving Cannonball

![Cannonball vest from the police archive.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_657,h_370,x_0,y_10/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-869220616fbe63cecbf/1634713231)

Open image viewer

Photo: Police archive / Lehtikuva

Prosecutors are seeking to dissolve the motorcycle gang Cannonball and its subsidiary Squad 32.

This is the first time an attempt is made to dissolve a nationwide motorcycle gang. The prosecutor says that Cannonball is an organised criminal group.

Earlier in Finland the neo-Nazi organisation Nordic Resistance Movement and the vest gang United Brotherhood have been dissolved.

## Sakharov Prize

![Aleksej Navalnyj](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2086,h_1173,x_0,y_108/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-791621606b3ac570cdc/1617640179)

Open image viewer

Opposition figure Aleksei Navalnyj. Photo: AFP / Lehtikuva

Russian opposition figure Aleksei Navalnyi receives the European Parliament's Sakharov Human Rights Prize.

Navalnyi is currently in prison in Russia. Navalnyi is one of President Vladimir Putin's loudest critics.

Navalnyi was the target of poisoning last year. He was treated in Germany, after which he returned to Russia.
