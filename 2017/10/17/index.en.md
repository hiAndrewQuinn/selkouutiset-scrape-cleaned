# Tuesday 17.10.2017 (radio)

The police academy has too few good applicants. The Evangelical Lutheran Church received a plain Finnish award. The sun turned red. Tonight is the Night of the Homeless. Prince William is coming to visit Finland.

##

## The police academy needs more students

![A police dog.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9882684)

Open image viewer

The police academy wants more students. Photo: Yle

The police academy is short of good applicants. The number of student places at the police academy was increased this year. But 60 study places were left unused, because no suitable students were found for the school.

There is a shortage of Swedish-speaking students in particular. There will be an extra application round for the Swedish-language course in the autumn.

## An award for the Church for using plain language

![Two women and a man smile with a certificate.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9887750)

Open image viewer

Riitta Kuusi, Maria Sten and Sixten Ekstrand received the award. Photo: Kaisa Kaatra/Selkokeskus

The Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland has received an award for using plain language. The award is called Plain Speaker of the Year.

The Church got the award because it has produced material and services in easy Finnish and Swedish for many years. The Church has websites in plain Finnish and plain Swedish. The Church holds church services in plain language. There is also, for example, a plain-language order for baptisms and weddings.

## The sun was red

![A red sun in the sky over Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9886534)

Open image viewer

The sun glowed red on Tuesday during the day. Photo: Kimmo Porttila / Yle

The sun looked red today, especially in Southern Finland. The sun changed colour because Storm Ophelia, which raged in Ireland, blew particles from forest fires in Southern Europe and sand dust from the Sahara to Finland.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute said that a trail of dust could be seen in a satellite image. It went to Finland via the Netherlands and Sweden.

## The Night of the Homeless

![Mattresses on a rack in an emergency shelter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8617237)

Open image viewer

Homeless people in Turku can sleep in an emergency shelter.  Photo: Monica Forssell / Yle

Today is the Night of the Homeless. The night is part of the UN day against poverty and social exclusion. The Night of the Homeless reminds us that everyone has the right to a permanent home.

The number of homeless people in Finland has gone down. But at the end of last year, almost 7000 people in Finland were without a home. One in four homeless people who live alone has an immigrant background.

## Prince William visits Finland

![Prince William](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9886767)

Open image viewer

Prince William is coming to Finland next month. Photo: Oli Scarff / AFP

Britain's Prince William will arrive in Finland at the end of November. He is coming as the guest of President Sauli Niinistö. Prince William is second in line to the British throne, after his father, Crown Prince Charles.
