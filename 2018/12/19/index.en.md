# Wednesday 19.12.2018 (radio)

The number of low-income people grew. There is a wish to limit sales of energy drinks. The EU bans plastic products. President Niinistö received Christmas presents. Customs is training a second food dog. Wrestler Petra Olli is the history-maker of the year.

## Low-income people

![A man puts a banknote in his wallet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10348116)

Open image viewer

About 650,000 people lived in low-income households last year. Photo: AOP

The number of low-income people grew last year.

Statistics Finland says that about 650,000 people lived in low-income households last year. That is about 30,000 more people than a year earlier.

The low-income threshold for a one-person household was 14,750 euros per year last year.

## Energy drinks

![](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6603009)

Open image viewer

Energy drinks contain a lot of caffeine. Photo: Hanna Lumme / Yle

There is a wish to ban by law the sale of energy drinks to under-15s.

Minister of Agriculture and Forestry Jari Leppä and Minister of Family and Basic Services Annika Saarikko propose that caffeinated energy drinks may no longer be sold to under-15s. The ministers say that the sales ban must be agreed in the next government programme.

The ministers say that they are worried about children's health.

## Plastic single-use products

![plastic juice straws](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10228925)

Open image viewer

The EU bans plastic juice straws. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The EU has agreed to ban plastic single-use products.

The EU bans plastic single-use products that can be made from some other material. For example, plastic knives, forks and spoons, plastic plates, straws and cotton buds will be banned.

Single-use plastic products are to be banned because, for example, they litter the world's seas.

## The president's Christmas presents

![Sauli Niinistö and Jenni Haukio receive a Christmas tree donated by forestry students at the official residence of the president.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10562844)

The forestry students donated a Christmas tree and an oat sheaf to Mäntyniemi. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio have received Christmas presents from various organisations.

The president received a Christmas tree and a Christmas flower as gifts. The president also received Karelian pasties, a vegetable basket, a ham and pike as a Christmas fish.

In addition, the boys' choir Cantores Minores and a Lucia maiden sang to the president and his wife at Mäntyniemi.

## The customs food dog

![Aino the dog](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10254278)

Open image viewer

Customs' first food dog, Aino, started work on the eastern border last summer. Photo: Miina Sillanpää / Yle

Customs plans to train a new food dog. The work of a food dog is to find forbidden food souvenirs.

Customs' first food dog, Aino, started work on the Russian border last June. Aino has been very efficient. In half a year Aino has found almost 600 kilograms of forbidden meat and dairy products.

Customs wants a second food dog to start work at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.

## History-maker

![Petra Olli](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10478172)

Open image viewer

Wrestler Petra Olli. Photo: Jussi Nukari/Lehtikuva

The International Wrestling Federation has chosen wrestler Petra Olli as history-maker of the year.

Petra Olli won the world wrestling championship in October in Hungary.

Olli is the first Finnish world wrestling champion in more than 20 years. The previous one was Marko Yli-Hannuksela, who won world championship gold in Poland in 1997.

Petra Olli is also the first Finnish female wrestler who has won a world championship.
