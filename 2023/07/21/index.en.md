## Bird flu

![Rooster and hens in an outdoor enclosure.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_393/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97092362aae1e897332/1655366969)

A rooster and hens. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

In Finland they are trying to stop bird flu from spreading to hens. In nature, bird flu has already killed many birds.

Authorities have ordered that in some areas hens must be kept indoors. If the hens are outside, the area must be protected with netting.

The new rules apply to hen houses in Western Finland.

In addition, authorities remind everyone that dead birds must not be touched. If many dead birds are found in nature, it is good to tell the authorities about it.

## Riikka Purra

![A person in a black suit holds a folder in their hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_426/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114580864ba50da9a613/1689932217)

Many Finns want Riikka Purra to resign from her post as minister.

On Friday Parliament received a petition about Purra's resignation. The petition has about 120,000 signatures.

The signatories want Purra to resign because of old writings. Purra's old writings are racist.

Riikka Purra is the chair of the Finns Party. In the government she holds the post of Minister of Finance, that is, the minister of money.

## Electric scooter

![Electric scooter parked in the middle of a cycle path in Lutakko, Jyväskylä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4094,h_2303,x_503,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113730964a1958e9d7d8/1688311277)

Electric scooters have become more common.

Electric scooters are now also used a lot in small towns. In big cities electric scooters have been common for longer.

Hospitals in different parts of Finland have also noticed that electric scooters are becoming more common. They have received patients who have hurt themselves on an electric scooter.

Usually the one who has an accident on an electric scooter is a young man who has been drinking alcohol. The injuries in the accident are usually to the head.

## Crayfish fishing

![Crayfish that have changed their shells.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4624,h_2601,x_0,y_434/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83388060f7c5932453d/1627567181)

Open image viewer

Crayfish are a delicacy for many. Photo: Timo Leponiemi / Yle

Crayfish fishing has started today, Friday. As much as 6 million crayfish are caught from Finnish waters every year.

There are about 100,000 crayfish fishers in Finland. Crayfish may be caught only with the permission of the owner of the water area, and adults under 65 must also pay a fishery management fee.

Crayfish fishing continues until the end of October.
