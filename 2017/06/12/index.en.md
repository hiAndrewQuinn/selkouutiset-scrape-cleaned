# Monday 12.6.2017 (radio)

There is a government crisis in Finland. The party of France's new president has won the parliamentary election. Finland's airports will soon be emission-free. The thunderstorm season is starting again.

## The government is falling apart

![Juha Sipilä, Jussi Halla-aho and Petteri Orpo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9663501)

There is a government crisis in Finland. The crisis began when MEP Jussi Halla-aho was chosen as chair of the Finns Party on Saturday. Halla-aho is known as a politician who opposes immigration.

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä of the Centre and Petteri Orpo, the National Coalition's Minister of Finance, say that co-operation with the Finns Party will not continue. Sipilä and Orpo say, for example, that there is not enough common ground of values with the Finns Party. Sipilä said that the government's ability to work must be secured.

The Prime Minister will still talk with the parliamentary groups on Tuesday. After that it will become clear whether the Prime Minister asks for the government's resignation. It is not yet known which parties will be in the new government.

## The party of France's president won the parliamentary election

![President Emmanuel Macron and his wife Brigitte Trogneux vote in Le Touquet in northern France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9663608)

President Emmanuel Macron and his wife Brigitte Trogneux voted in Le Touquet in northern France. Photo: Christophe Petit Tesson / EPA

The party of France's new president has won the parliamentary election. Emmanuel Macron's party, Republic on the Move, and its centrist supporting party won over 32 percent of the votes.

The right-wing populist National Front won about 13 percent of the votes.

The second round of the election is in a week's time.

## Finland's airports are becoming emission-free

![An aircraft being refuelled at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9662576)

Open image viewer

An aircraft is being refuelled at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

Finland's airports will soon be emission-free. That means the airports do not cause pollution. The airport company Finavia will make the airports emission-free within a couple of years. Carbon dioxide emissions at Helsinki-Vantaa airport will be cut to zero already this year.

Finavia believes that companies must set an example, now that America has withdrawn from the Paris climate agreement.

At the airports, the switch is being made to renewable energy such as wind. The buildings are heated with ground heat, and the lighting is switching to LED lamps. In addition, airport buses will start using biodiesel made from waste.

In Finland it is believed that emission-free airports are good when competing for customers.

## Thunder must be prepared for again

![Lightning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9006878)

The thunderstorm season is starting again. There is thunder and lightning especially from May to September. Thunder and lightning are most common in July.

There are big weather differences between the regions: in Central Ostrobothnia there is the most lightning, and in Lapland and Åland the least. Strong thunderstorms can nevertheless occur anywhere. So last year most of the lightning struck Northern Finland. Thunder is also a weather phenomenon that is very difficult to forecast.
