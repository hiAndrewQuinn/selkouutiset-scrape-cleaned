## Kahovka dam breach

![Split image showing the dam before and after the destruction.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1125533647f64b6235d3/1686075043)

The Dnepr river is flooding because the Kahovka dam has been broken. Photo: Yle

In Ukraine and in the West, people are watching the effects of the Kahovka dam breach. The Kahovka dam broke early on Tuesday morning. Ukraine says that Russia destroyed the dam. In Western countries too, many people believe that Russia destroyed the dam. However, it is not known for certain who destroyed the dam.

Russia says that Ukraine broke the dam. The flood also harms Russian troops and their defences. That is why there is a small chance that Ukraine destroyed the dam. However, in Western countries more people believe that Russia broke the dam.

In Ukraine, thousands of people have fled because the Dnepr river is flooding. The UN estimates that the homes of about 16,000 people will be under flood water.

## Dnepr river flood

The Dnepr river flood is causing big problems for farming in Ukraine. Ukraine's Ministry of Agriculture says that tens of thousands of hectares of farm land will be under flood water.

The destruction of the dam also causes problems for watering crops.

Because the dam broke, the water level falls in some other areas. There may be problems watering crops in these areas. That is why the Kahovka dam breach has a very big effect on farming in Ukraine.

## Climate complaint

![Smokestacks in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_516/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6324815e21d78ae9962/1579281551)

Open image viewer

Environmental organisations want the government to do more to reduce emissions. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

The Supreme Administrative Court has rejected the environmental organisations' request to take up the climate complaint. The Supreme Administrative Court says that there are no matters in the climate complaint that can be appealed.

The environmental organisations Greenpeace and the Finnish Association for Nature Conservation asked for permission to appeal. They say that the government has not done enough climate action.

Because the Supreme Administrative Court will not take up the case, a big court case about climate matters will not start in Finland.

## Saimaa ringed seal

![Pup on a rock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2143,h_1205,x_184,y_550/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-112578664805c7fb4570/1689913757)

Open image viewer

The Saimaa ringed seal is endangered. Photo: UEF-NORPPATUTKIMUS

Two Saimaa ringed seals have been moved in Saimaa. The University of Eastern Finland says that the moves were successful. The female seal **Amalia** was moved from Pihlajavesi to Kolovesi. The male seal **Tuukka** was moved from Pihlajavesi to southern Saimaa.

The Saimaa ringed seal is an endangered animal, which means there are only a few left alive. About 450 Saimaa ringed seals live in Saimaa. The moves help the seals survive in Saimaa. The purpose is to keep the diversity of the seal population.

The moves also reduce the risk of the Saimaa ringed seal populations being separated. Saimaa ringed seals have been moved in Finland for the second time now.
