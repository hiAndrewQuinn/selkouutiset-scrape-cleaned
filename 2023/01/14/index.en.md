Electricity availability. Turkey and NATO. Alcohol taxes. Inactivity. School food. Weather.

## Electricity

![Mika Lintilä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5190,h_2919,x_0,y_361/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105934263c2702749914/1673687393)

Minister of Economic Affairs Mika Lintilä (Centre) praises Finns' electricity-saving effort. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Minister of Economic Affairs Mika Lintilä says that Finland may not need deliberate power cuts to ensure there is enough electricity. The price of electricity may also not rise any more.

Lintilä said on Yle's Ykkösaamu that uncertainty will continue, however, for as long as Russia attacks Ukraine.

Lintilä thanks Finns for saving electricity. In Lintilä's view the saving effort is still an important matter.

## Fighter jets, Turkey and NATO

![A fighter jet flies in the sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4316,h_2427,x_0,y_72/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105933463c26ba40d1a8/1673685990)

Romanian Air Force F-16 fighter in a NATO exercise over the Black Sea in 2021. Photo: Robert Ghement / EPA

The USA may sell F-16 fighter jets to Turkey. President Joe Biden's administration intends to ask Congress for permission for the sale. The newspaper Wall Street Journal reports this.

The paper's information is based on administration sources, but names are not given. The paper says that the aim is to get Turkey to approve Finland's and Sweden's membership of the NATO defence alliance.

Congress has considered selling weapons to Turkey for more than a year.

## Taxes on alcohol

![A customs officer carries alcohol products.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1679,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105851063bff36f850c8/1673524343)

Open image viewer

A customs officer lists alcoholic drinks ordered from abroad in the Customs warehouse in Helsinki in November 2022. Photo: Customs

The state loses about 100 million euros in taxes on alcohol bought from abroad through online shops. The Tax Administration says that only about one percent of the taxes on ordered alcohol is paid to Finland.

The corona era increased the ordering of alcohol from abroad. In 2021 almost 33 million litres of alcohol were ordered from abroad.

The state wants Finnish buyers to pay the taxes on alcohol from online shops. Previously attempts were made to get the taxes from foreign online shops.

Online shops have shifted the payment of alcohol taxes more to buyers.

## Inactivity

![Exercise recommendation pyramid for older people.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1200,h_675,x_0,y_366/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6567115e78b9dc0c4cd/1584970375)

Open image viewer

Photo: UKK Institute

The UKK Institute calculates that Finns' inactivity causes costs of over 3 billion euros every year. Lack of exercise increases the risk of type 2 diabetes and heart diseases.

The calculations include, for example, health care costs and losses of income tax.

The UKK Institute is a research centre for health exercise in Tampere.

## School food

![Food on a serving line.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_522/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-102568263592f2117686/1666789299)

Open image viewer

Photo: Petri Kuikka / Yle

School food is almost the same as before, even though prices have risen.

School menus have changed only a little. For example, instead of fresh fish, frozen fish is sometimes offered, but no raw ingredient has had to be dropped completely.

Municipalities, however, are paying more for food than before.

For example, in Tampere the price of school food has risen 7.3 percent and in Oulu 3.8 percent.
