## Advance voting for the regional elections

![A person examines regional election adverts in a snowy landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90246361e43844d2d21/1642346701)

Regional election adverts. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Advance voting for the regional elections ended yesterday. A little over a million people voted in advance, that is about 26 per cent of those entitled to vote.

The most lively voting was in the wellbeing services county of South Savo, where over 30 per cent of those entitled to vote went to vote. The least voting was in the wellbeing services county of Vantaa and Kerava, where the voting percentage stayed below 20.

Abroad, about 3,000 people voted in advance, the Ministry for Foreign Affairs says. More than half of the votes were cast in Spain. Advance voting was held in about 70 different countries.

The actual election day for the regional elections is on Sunday.

## Corona at workplaces

![A plush moose calf lies on a sofa at the Laaksolahti daycare centre in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5334,h_3000,x_0,y_222/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-880877619519c3135fd/1638376197)

Open image viewer

Daycare centres are preparing for an increase in sick leave. Photo: Janne Lindroos / Yle

Many workplaces are preparing for an increase in sick leave.

The wave of infections is being prepared for, for example, in schools and daycare centres. In Kuopio and Joensuu, for example, plans have been drawn up for how to act if there is no longer enough staff.

Absences of children and staff have already grown and caused extra work in many municipalities.

The police and rescue services are also preparing for corona absences.

Fire brigades and police stations are covering high sick leave, for example with overtime and by moving staff from one station to another. Managers are also being moved to shifts.

## Complaints to the Parliamentary Ombudsman

![Parliamentary Ombudsman Petri Jääskeläinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_262/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6679065ea2bfe65181e/1593744451)

Open image viewer

Parliamentary Ombudsman Petri Jääskeläinen. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

A record number of complaints were made to the Parliamentary Ombudsman last year.

In total, over 7,700 complaints were made. That is about 700 more than the year before last.

The most complaints were about health care, social care and the police. Over a thousand complaints were about matters that were somehow related to the corona epidemic.

Hautala has been a Vice-President of the EU Parliament since 2017. In total there are 14 Vice-Presidents in the Parliament.

The Maltese Roberta Metsola was elected President of the Parliament yesterday. The 43-year-old Metsola is the youngest President in the history of the EU Parliament. Her spouse is Finnish.
