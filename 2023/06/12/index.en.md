## Government climate policy

![The government talks continued at Säätytalo in Helsinki on 26 May 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11203776470b6c0459ab/1685633669)

The government talks are held at Säätytalo in Helsinki. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Large Finnish companies hope for support from the state for the green transition. It means a change in the economy towards sustainable growth and a low-emission society.

More than 30 per cent of companies are worried about how the climate policy of the new government can be predicted. This is what a survey by the OP Group, made by Taloustutkimus, says.

Large domestic companies have been pleased that the industrial policy of the current government has been active. 90 per cent hope that the new government will continue in the same way. 100 Finnish companies answered the survey in May.

The 7th week of the government talks has started at Säätytalo in Helsinki.

## Work loneliness

![A woman works at home on a computer. The silhouette of the woman can be seen against the window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_130/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-100210363108626b5aec/1665492551)

Open image viewer

Many people feel work loneliness when working remotely or in hybrid work. Photo: Marko Melto / Yle

Work loneliness is common in Finland. This is what a University of Vaasa study says.

More than 30 per cent of people who work fully or partly remotely say that they are lonely at work. They do not have the social relationships with their colleagues that they want.

Because of work loneliness, working can be difficult and the attraction of the job may fall. Burnout at work is also a threat, and some people change jobs.

Loneliness at work is reduced, for example, by shared breaks and good meetings with your own team.

## Silvio Berlusconi

![Silvio Berlusconi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4956,h_2787,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11282706486df26a9759/1686560628)

Open image viewer

Former Italian Prime Minister Silvio Berlusconi. Photo: Miguel Medina / AFP

Former Italian Prime Minister Silvio Berlusconi has died.

Berlusconi died in a hospital in Milan. He had heart problems and leukaemia, that is, blood cancer.

Berlusconi was 86 years old. He was Italy's prime minister several times in the 1990s and at the beginning of the 2000s. Berlusconi led the Forza Italia party, which is part of Italy's current government.

Berlusconi was also in the European Parliament.

Berlusconi's political career involved political scandals, sex scandals and court cases.

## Bumblebees

![Bumblebee](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_58/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4988575b4f09985e953/1597300795)

Open image viewer

Pesticides harm the lives of bumblebees. Photo: Matthias Hiekel / EPA

Pesticides against insects and plant diseases can weaken the memory of bumblebees.

This is what a study says. The study involved the universities of Helsinki and Oulu, the Finnish Environment Institute and the Natural Resources Institute Finland.

Pesticides may disturb how bumblebees learn colours. A large amount of pesticide also affects how well bumblebees are able to find food.
