# Wednesday 15.6.2022

A technical fault has disrupted the payment of wages. Mortgage interest rates are rising. The Sodankylä film festival has started. A record number of seal cruises are sailing on Lake Saimaa. The weather.

## Wage money did not arrive

![The Osuuspankki logo on the wall of the head office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5915,h_3327,x_0,y_499/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6324995e21d8f9dbfe1/1622021453)

The technical fault at Osuuspankki was fixed in the afternoon. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

Tens of thousands of people's wages were paid late today because of the bank's technical problems.

Many people were paid today, but the wage money did not reach the accounts. Kela benefits such as pensions, unemployment benefits or housing benefits also did not reach people's accounts.

The cause of the payment disruption was at Osuuspankki. Osuuspankki had a technical fault, because of which payments did not move to other banks. Osuuspankki fixed the fault in the afternoon.

OP says that wages and benefits will still show up in accounts during today.

## Mortgage interest rates are rising

![The graphic shows how the 12-month euribor rate, which was still negative at the beginning of April, has risen to 1.1 percent by 15 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2716,h_1526,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97042962a9a3773d754/1655285016)

Open image viewer

The one-year euribor rate is now rising fast. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle, graphic: Samuli Huttunen / Yle

Mortgage interest rates are now rising fast.

The most common reference rate for mortgages is the 12-month euribor. It rose above 1 percent today, that is, it has doubled in 2 weeks.

Banks believe that the one-year euribor will rise to 2 percent during this year and even higher next year.

Interest rates have been at zero for a long time. Now that rates are rising, it means that, for example, the interest costs of mortgages will grow.

## Sodankylä film festival started

![People walking into a blue and red circus tent where films are shown.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_301/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97036362a9a007c7335/1655284504)

In Sodankylä films are watched, for example, in a circus tent. Photo: Elina Ervasti / Yle

The Sodankylä film festival has started. The festival programme includes films and filmmakers from Finland and abroad.

During 5 days more than 100 films will be shown. The most popular screenings have traditionally been silent films, whose screening is accompanied by a live orchestra.

About 30,000 visitors are expected in Sodankylä.

## Seals interest people

![Saimaa ringed seal on a shore rock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80830860a6067dd7d03/1621493786)

Open image viewer

It is good to watch the Saimaa ringed seal with binoculars so that it is not disturbed. Photo: Tarja Nyyssönen / Yle

A record number of seal cruises will be organised on Lake Saimaa this summer.

During the corona time people have become enthusiastic about nature tourism, and that has also increased interest in the Saimaa ringed seal. People now want to see the seal with their own eyes.

Because seal tourism has grown, Metsähallitus and nature tourism companies have together agreed on rules for seal tourism. Cruise ships must not go too close to the seal. The seal is always watched from a distance with binoculars.

The number of Saimaa ringed seals has grown in recent years, but the species is still very endangered.
