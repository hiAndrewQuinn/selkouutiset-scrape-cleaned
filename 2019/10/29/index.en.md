# Tuesday 29.10.2019 (radio)

Early elections will be held in Britain. The Syrian and Turkish armies have fought battles in Northeast Syria. The Prime Minister of Lebanon is resigning. The body of ISIS leader Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi has been buried at sea. The number of drug deaths has grown.

## New elections in Britain

![Boris Johnson](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11041652)

Open image viewer

British Prime Minister Boris Johnson. Photo: Andy Rain / EPA

Early parliamentary elections will be held in Britain in December. Prime Minister Boris Johnson's government wants new elections. The Labour Party supports new elections.

In Prime Minister Boris Johnson's view, the elections will be held because the current Parliament cannot decide on Britain's EU exit.

Johnson wants the election day to be 12 December.

## Syria and Turkey fight in Northeast Syria

![Syrian army soldiers arrive in the town of Tal Tamr in Hasakah on 14 October 2019. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11019488)

Open image viewer

Syrian army soldiers arrive in the town of Tal Tamr in Hasakah on 14 October 2019. Photo: SANA / EPA

The Syrian and Turkish armies are fighting fierce battles in Northeast Syria. An organisation that monitors the war in Syria says that 6 Syrian soldiers have died near the town of Ras al-Ain.

Turkey attacked the Kurdish area of Northern Syria about 3 weeks ago. The Kurds have allied with the Syrian army. This is the first time that the Syrian and Turkish armies are fighting hard against each other.

Neither Turkey nor Syria has confirmed the fighting.

Lebanon's Prime Minister Saad Hariri is resigning.

In Lebanon there have been big demonstrations in recent weeks, in which the government has been opposed. On Monday, Hariri's government approved reforms that aim to improve Lebanon's economy.

## ISIS leader Baghdadi was buried at sea

![Screenshot from a video that is said to show ISIS leader Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10760490)

Open image viewer

Screenshot from a video that is said to show ISIS leader Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi. Photo: AL FURQAN ISIS MEDIA WING HANDOUT / EPA

The body of Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi, leader of the terrorist organisation ISIS, has been buried at sea. The US Department of Defense, the Pentagon, reports this.

Al-Baghdadi died on Saturday in an attack that the USA carried out in Syria.

The Pentagon says that al-Baghdadi's body was buried according to Islamic rules. The Pentagon did not say where the remains were lowered. Baghdadi's burial place is not wanted to become a pilgrimage site for ISIS supporters.

The body of Osama bin Laden, leader of the terrorist network al-Qaeda, was also buried at sea in 2011.

## Drug deaths in Finland

![Barbed wire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10508578)

Open image viewer

There were 21 more overdose deaths last year than the year before. Illustrative image. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

More people are dying from drugs in Finland than before. Last year, 188 people died from a drug overdose. There were 21 more deaths than 2 years ago. For example, in 2005, 72 people died from a drug overdose in Finland.

A large part of those who died are men. Drugs are the second most common cause of death for men under 40 in Finland. The most common cause of death is suicide.
