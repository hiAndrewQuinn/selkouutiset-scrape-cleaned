## Nato

![Should Finland join the military alliance NATO? Yes: 76%, no: 12%, don't know: 11%](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3839,h_2160,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9523176278fb3aaf342/1652095923)

Open image viewer

Photo: Eetu-Mikko Pietarinen / Yle

Yle's survey shows that support for the military alliance NATO has risen in Finland.

76 per cent of the respondents supported joining NATO. In March the supporters were 62 per cent, and in February 53 per cent.

The opponents of joining were 12 per cent. In February there were 28 per cent of them.

The uncertain have now turned to NATO's side.

Russia's attack on Ukraine in February has raised support for NATO in Finland.

## Victory Day

![President Vladimir Putin's speech in Red Square. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3024,h_1701,x_0,y_673/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9521326278c1e66b48b/1652081406)

Open image viewer

President Vladimir Putin's Victory Day speech in Red Square in Moscow.  Photo: Erkka Mikkonen/ Yle

Victory Day has been celebrated in Russia on Monday. It celebrates the victory over Nazi Germany in the Second World War in 1945.

President Vladimir Putin gave the Victory Day speech in Red Square in Moscow.

Putin repeated the claim that Russia protects its own citizens in Donbass, Ukraine. Putin did not mention the fighting elsewhere in Ukraine.

Russia calls the war in Ukraine a "special operation". Putin said that starting the operation was the right decision.

In his speech Putin did not declare war on Ukraine or a general mobilisation. The world had feared this in advance.

Mobilisation means that the armed forces and the rest of society are ordered to combat readiness.

## Europe Day

![The flag of the European Union is flying in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_319/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6732365eb7aa5ae5b19/1589097103)

Open image viewer

The Schuman Declaration, given on 9 May 1950, was an ambitious plan to secure lasting peace in Europe. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Monday was Europe Day, that is, the day of peace and unity in Europe. It is celebrated every year on 9 May.

Robert Schuman's proposal in 1950 was the beginning of today's European Union. Schuman put forward the idea of a new kind of cooperation, which can prevent war between the countries of Europe.

Schuman was the Prime Minister of France in the 1940s.

## Bicycle thefts

![Bicycle thefts increase in the autumn.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9758141)

Open image viewer

A lock like this arched lock is a better protection against bicycle theft than a wire lock. Photo: Antti Mikkola / Yle Rovaniemi

In Finland 50 bicycles are stolen every day. Thieves took more than 18 000 bicycles last year.

Insurance companies paid compensation of more than 11 million euros.

The increase of electric bicycles has raised the sums of compensation.

The locks of many bicycles are easy to break. Cheap wire locks can be cut in a few seconds.

## Tax return

![tax return 2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10493344)

Open image viewer

There are instructions for changing the pre-filled tax return. Photo: Sauli Antikainen / Yle

The first deadline for the tax return is on Tuesday.

If there is something to correct in the return, the changes must be reported by the deadline at the latest. The day is marked on the tax return.

If there is nothing to correct, you do not need to do anything.

The other deadlines are 17 and 24 May.
