# Radio Clear News Thursday 13.7.2023

US President Joe Biden in Finland. US and Nordic countries summit. The weather.

## President Biden in Finland

![Joe Biden and Sauli Niinistö smile as they look at each other.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114235464b0180bf0210/1689266030)

US President Joe Biden has visited Finland.

Biden met President Sauli Niinistö in Helsinki. The presidents said that the relations between the USA and Finland are very good.

President Biden said that Finland's membership of the NATO defence alliance is a good thing. Finland became a NATO member in April.

Biden said that Finland's NATO membership improves Finland's security. The USA promises to defend all of Finland.

President Niinistö thanked Biden. Niinistö said that Biden was strong support for Finland when Finland applied for NATO membership.

## US and Nordic countries summit

![President Joe Biden and the Nordic leaders in a group photo at the Presidential Palace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_423/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114213464afdfeb3cf8f/1689248551)

Biden met the political leaders of all the Nordic countries in Helsinki. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

President Joe Biden also took part in the US and Nordic countries summit in Helsinki. The leaders of the USA, Finland, Sweden, Denmark, Norway and Iceland took part in the summit.

At the summit they talked about security policy. Biden said that the Nordic countries are an important part of the NATO defence alliance. "We have the same values," Biden said.

The summit also talked, for example, about climate change. President Biden praised the Nordic countries' actions against climate change.

Biden said that the question is about saving the planet. There is not much time to save the planet, but cooperation is strength also in climate action, President Biden said.

The security of the US president has been looked after well in Helsinki.

There have been many police officers in Helsinki during Biden's visit. Police officers have also come to Helsinki from elsewhere in Finland.

The police have also cooperated with the US Secret Service. Secret Service agents have protected President Biden for the whole trip.

## The weather in Finland

Beautiful summer weather continues in Finland.

On Friday, in the southern and central parts of the country it rains from time to time. The temperature is between 20 and 25 degrees.

In Lapland and also near the eastern border it is rainy. In rainy areas the temperatures are colder.

The weekend is warm all over the country. In the south there may even be a heat wave, that is, the temperature may be 25 degrees or more.
