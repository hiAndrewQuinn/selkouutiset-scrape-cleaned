# Monday 6.1.2020 (radio)

Slowing down climate change is important to Finns. The Australian government promises help to repair the damage from the bushfires. About 12,000 young people started military service on Sunday. The weather in the coming days is warm and rainy.

## Finns' attitude to climate change

![Heavy traffic on Porkkalankatu in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10865538)

A bicycle is better for the climate than a car. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Finns are ready to act against climate change. This is what Yle's new survey shows.

Yle asked Finns how they feel about climate change. Almost all respondents said that slowing down climate change is important. Only 1 per cent of respondents thought that climate change does not need to be slowed down.

Finns are also ready to change their own ways of life because of climate change. Over half, that is 53 per cent of respondents, thought that every Finn must change their ways of life themselves, because climate change must be slowed down.

Changing ways of life can mean, for example, driving less or eating less meat. Traffic and meat production release carbon dioxide into the air, and that is the cause of climate change.

Climate change means that the climate is getting warmer. That is why, for example, storms, floods and droughts are increasing in different parts of the world.

Taloustutkimus carried out Yle's survey. About 1,000 people answered the survey.

## Australia's big bushfires

![Burnt forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11145703)

Open image viewer

Bushfires are plaguing Australians. Photo: Riku Roslund / Yle

Bushfires have burned large areas in Australia. The fire has already destroyed an area the size of Ireland.

The Australian government has promised to help repair the damage from the bushfires. The government promises over one billion euros for repair work.

During the weekend it rained in Australia and the temperature dropped. That made firefighting easier.

Meteorologists, however, forecast that by the end of the week the weather in Australia will be drier and hotter again.

## Young people's military service

![ New recruits arrive at the Santahamina garrison in January 2017. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11145615)

Open image viewer

Military clothing is used in the army. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

About 12,000 young people started military service on Monday. In the army, young people learn, for example, to use weapons.

Young people start their service in the army either in January or in July. They are in the army for at least 165 days.

Military service is compulsory for men in Finland. Women can join the army if they want to themselves.

If a young person does not want to join the army, they can do civilian service. In civilian service, the young person does not have to use weapons. Civilian service includes work in organisations, for example.

Wednesday is a windy and rainy day.
