## New restrictions for restaurants

![People eating in a restaurant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4378,h_2463,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7255415f75f61e5e37e/1613585362)

New restrictions will apply to restaurant opening hours in some areas of Finland.

These areas are Uusimaa, Southwest Finland, Pirkanmaa, Kanta-Häme and Ostrobothnia.

The restrictions start on Sunday.

Restaurants must stop selling alcohol at 22:00. Restaurants must close at 23:00.

## Hospitals prepare for intensive care patients

![A person being treated in an intensive care unit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11558645)

Open image viewer

Finnish hospitals have about 100 intensive care places in total. Photo: Caroline Blumberg / EPA

Hospitals are closely monitoring how the corona situation develops in Finland.

Hospitals want to make sure that there are enough intensive care places for the sickest.

Finnish hospitals can treat about 100 intensive care patients at the same time.

But in some areas of Finland, hospitals can treat only 5 intensive care patients at once.

Luckily, in the summer and autumn there have been only a few intensive care patients.

## White-faced sakis prefer traffic sound

![White-faced sakis in a tree in an acrylic tunnel.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11582764)

Open image viewer

A white-faced saki is in a box that makes a sound. Photo: Roosa Piitulainen / Aalto University

Researchers have studied what kind of sounds the monkeys at Korkeasaari like.

The white-faced saki monkeys at Korkeasaari got boxes in their cage that played different calm sounds.

The researchers were surprised when it turned out that the white-faced sakis enjoyed the box where traffic sound could be heard the most.

This research result can be used.

Suitable sounds can be offered to monkeys in zoos as enrichment. At the same time, their wellbeing increases.
