## Train connection to Sweden

Now news about train traffic.

A train connection has been opened from Finland to Sweden.

The train departs from Oulu and travels via Kemi and Tornio to Haparanda in Sweden.

The opening of the train route was celebrated yesterday, Monday.

The new train connection will cost approximately 2 million euros per year.

A large part of it is paid for by the state. The cities of Tornio and Oulu and the Maritime Lapland Development Centre also pay a part.

A train connection has been opened from Oulu to Haparanda in Sweden.

There are 2 train services every day.

Oulu Mayor **Ari Alatossava** hopes for more train services.

*"The current connections serve long-distance travelers. This could be extended to include daytime services, which would perhaps better serve the residents of the area."*
