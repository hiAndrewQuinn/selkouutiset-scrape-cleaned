## Party support

![Party support in June 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11427338)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

There have been no big changes in party support.

The new Yle survey shows that the SDP is still Finland's largest party. The SDP is the party of Prime Minister Sanna Marin.

The support for the SDP has fallen a little, however. About 22 per cent of respondents to the new survey support the SDP. In the previous survey, the SDP's support was about 23 per cent.

The support for the opposition parties has not changed either. The opposition parties are not part of the government.

The largest opposition party is the Finns Party. The Finns Party's support is now the same as in the previous survey, that is, 18 per cent.

The support figures for all parties can be found on Yle's website. The address is yle.fi.

## Russian vote

![Poster showing Putin, with a voter in a face mask in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11428768)

Open image viewer

The result of the vote was good for Putin. Photo: Maxim Shipenkov / EPA

Russians have voted on changing the constitution. The constitution sets out how the state works.

About 80 per cent of voters supported changing the constitution. The most important change is that Vladimir Putin can continue as president for a long time. Putin can be elected president until as late as 2036.

In Russia many people think that the vote on the constitution was not honest. For example, opposition leader Aleksei Navalnyi says that the vote was a big lie.

## New mosquito species

![A mosquito of the Anopheles daciae species.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1080,h_608,x_0,y_12/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6955605efd79d2e164e/1593670202)

Open image viewer

This mosquito may be able to pass malaria on to people. Photo: Lorna Culverwell / University of Helsinki

Researchers have found a new mosquito species in Finland. The new mosquito species may be able to spread malaria.

Malaria is a feverish disease that is found in warm countries. Malaria is not found in Finland.

Because of climate change, Finland's climate is warming. Therefore it is possible that new diseases will come to Finland in the future.

Finns do not need to worry, however. There is no malaria in Finland yet, and a person does not get sick from a mosquito bite here.
