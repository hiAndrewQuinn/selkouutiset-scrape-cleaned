# Saturday 7.11.2020 (radio)

Votes are still being counted in the US presidential election. Coronavirus has been found on mink farms in 6 countries. A new record number of coronavirus infections has again been recorded in Germany. October was unusually warm.

## Vote counting in the USA continues

Votes are still being counted in the US presidential election. Votes are still being counted in 5 states.

On the basis of the votes counted so far, Democratic candidate Joe Biden is in the lead. Biden has not yet said that he won the election. Biden said that all the votes must first be counted.

Counting the votes takes a long time, because during the coronavirus epidemic many ballots were sent by post in the USA. The result of the election may become clear during the weekend.

## Mink have coronavirus in many countries

![Two mink on a Danish farm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3059,h_1721,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7384935fa2c61e66274/1663129178)

Open image viewer

Mink on a Danish farm. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

In total, coronavirus infections have been found on mink farms in 6 countries. This is reported by the World Health Organization, WHO. Infections have been found in Denmark, Sweden, the Netherlands, Spain, Italy and the USA.

In Denmark the virus has spread from mink to people. 12 people have caught the infection. Denmark has decided that about 15 million mink are to be killed.

Britain has decided that people may not travel from Denmark to Britain. People living in Britain may come from Denmark to Britain, but they must stay in quarantine for 2 weeks.

## Record number of coronavirus infections in Germany

![Face masks are advertised in the window of a Berlin shop on Wednesday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5224,h_2939,x_0,y_310/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7357705f9a976873668/1603966923)

Face masks are advertised in the window of a Berlin shop in Germany. Photo: Hayoung Jeon / EPA

In Germany more than 23,000 new coronavirus infections have been confirmed in a day. The number is again a new record. Yesterday the daily number of infections in Germany rose above 20,000 for the first time.

France has also confirmed the largest number of infections since the coronavirus epidemic began. In France there are more than 60,000 new coronavirus infections.

In France there have been 1.7 million coronavirus infections in total.

In Finland, new information on coronavirus infections may perhaps not be available at the weekend. The reason is a technical fault.

## October was warm

![Tourists were paragliding in the Alps at the end of October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11635455)

On the edge of the Alps in Switzerland, people enjoyed paragliding and sunny weather on the last day of October, last week. Photo: Laurent Gillieron / EPA

October was very warm in Europe. Especially in Eastern Europe and Northern Europe it was warmer than usual for October. North of the Arctic Circle and over the Arctic sea area the temperature was clearly higher than average.

In Finland too, October was unusually warm. The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that October was 2.5 degrees warmer than usual across almost the whole country.
