## Many contacts have come in about the Laajasalo sex crime suspect

The police have received many contacts that relate to the Laajasalo sexual crime case in Helsinki. On Monday the police said that a man from Helsinki is suspected of abusing at least 12 underage girls. The police published the man's picture. After that, many more people have contacted the police and told them information about the man.

## Companies' expectations of economic growth weakened

![Electric building tools on a shelf in a hardware store.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10888195)

Open image viewer

There is a shortage of workers, especially in construction and service sectors. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

Companies' expectations of economic growth have weakened. Many companies say that growth in production and sales is being prevented by a shortage of good employees. There is a shortage of employees, especially in the service sector and construction. Companies say, however, that the economic situation is still quite good.
Companies' production, sales and number of employees grew in the early part of the year.

## Housing prices rise most in Helsinki

![A key and banknotes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10404761)

Open image viewer

Housing prices rose in Helsinki, Oulu and Turku. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The divergence of housing prices continues between the Helsinki region and the rest of Finland. In the Helsinki region, prices of new and old flats rose in April–June compared with last year. Prices also rose a little in Oulu and Turku. In the rest of Finland, housing prices fell or stayed the same.

## A Finnish company's prostate cancer drug immediately received a permit in the USA

![Orion sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7166296)

Open image viewer

Orion has developed a new drug together with another pharmaceutical company, Bayer. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The Finnish pharmaceutical company Orion has received permission to sell a new cancer drug in the USA. The new drug is for treating prostate cancer. The drug got a marketing authorisation very quickly, because it can help in treating a serious illness. Orion has developed the prostate cancer drug together with the pharmaceutical company Bayer. A marketing authorisation for the drug is also being sought in Europe and Japan.
