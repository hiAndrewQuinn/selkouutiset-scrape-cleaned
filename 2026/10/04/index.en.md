## What is greenwashing?

EU countries are introducing a new law aimed at reducing greenwashing of products.

Greenwashing refers to communication or advertising that exaggerates or misrepresents the environmental friendliness of a product.

For example, a product's packaging may say that the product is ecological, even though it is not. The product may also be advertised as being more environmentally friendly than before, but in reality, no change has occurred.

The EU's new greenwashing law prohibits false claims.

## Correct information for the customer

![Responsibly grown oranges.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3815,h_2146,x_0,y_767/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-15640836931406f07dbf/1765188826)

The new law applies to, for example, packaging labeling, advertising and other marketing.

The purpose of the law is to ensure that customers receive accurate information about the product. This makes it easier to compare and purchase the product.

If a product is said to be, for example, biodegradable or climate-friendly, the manufacturer must be able to prove that the claim is true.

The law also requires proof of claims that the product has been manufactured fairly and responsibly.

## Many packaging changes

![Swan label on the side of the dishwasher detergent packaging](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1570,h_883,x_10,y_5/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-9482542d01d237522/1412235830)

The familiar Swan label will also be seen in the future, as it meets the requirements of the new law. Photo: Yle/ Katja Solla

The new law will change the packaging of many products.

Packaging may no longer bear the manufacturer's own environmental labels. Instead, labels such as the Nordic Swan Ecolabel, the Organic Label, the EU Ecolabel and the Fairtrade Label will continue to be used, as they meet the legal requirements.

The purpose of the new law is to help customers find products in stores that are truly environmentally friendly. The buyer must be able to trust that the labels on the packaging are true.
