# Saturday 11.3.2023 (radio)

Marin's comments on giving fighter jets to Ukraine. Price rises. Driving conditions. Weather forecast.

## Marin's speech on giving fighter jets

![Finland's Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky held a joint press conference in the Ukrainian President's office in Kyiv.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_625/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1083592640b2a6406625/1678459127)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin met Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Kyiv on Friday. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

Prime Minister Sanna Marin has started a discussion in Finland about possible fighter jet aid to Ukraine. Marin said in Kyiv on Friday that Finland can assess handing over old Hornets to Ukraine.

Marin means Hornet fighter jets that Finland is taking out of use. Marin says that Ukraine hopes for fighter jets and therefore a discussion about the matter can take place in Finland.

Marin says that in Finland it can be discussed whether old Hornets can be given to Ukraine and what training Ukraine would then need.

## Marin's speech puzzles

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689317)

Open image viewer

President Niinistö was surprised by Marin's speech. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Prime Minister Sanna Marin's speech about giving fighter jets has surprised many people. President Sauli Niinistö says that Finland has not discussed giving fighter jets away.

Defence Minister Antti Kaikkonen also says that Prime Minister Marin has not spoken about the matter before.

Antti Häkkänen, chairperson of Parliament's Defence Committee, is also surprised by Marin's speech. Häkkänen says that giving fighter jets abroad is a sensitive question in international relations. That is why the matter must be discussed first in Finland.

## Rising prices

![TVs for sale on display in an appliance shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_258/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83417360f81d07e07e1/1626886496)

Open image viewer

The price of some televisions, for example, has risen a lot. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Some companies have raised the prices of goods a lot because of inflation. There are problems in the manufacture of many goods, and delivery times may be long. Shortage of raw materials has slowed down the manufacture of electronics and cars, for example.

Many prices are rising a lot, that is, inflation is fast. Economic experts say that some companies are taking advantage of the situation. Some companies have raised prices, because people are used to price rises. For example, the prices of some mobile phones and televisions have risen very much.

Experts say that prices have risen more than inflation. Companies may be testing how much prices can be raised.

Snowfall has disturbed traffic on Saturday in the southern and western parts of the country.

Snowfall began in the afternoon. In the evening and at night it snows a lot, that is, the snowfall is heavy especially in the east.

Many roads are slippery and visibility is poor because of the snowfall.
