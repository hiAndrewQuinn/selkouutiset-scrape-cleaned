# Sunday 17.11.2019 (radio)

Negotiations on the postal strike have continued. The flood continues in the city of Venice in Italy. Many people have visited the Moomin park in Japan.

## Postal strike negotiations

![Heidi Nieminen, chair of the Posti and logistics sector union PAU, arrives to continue mediation of the postal strike at the National Conciliator's office in Helsinki on 15 November 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11071626)

Negotiations in the postal sector have continued on Sunday. The negotiators have been discussing how the postal strike can be ended.

If the postal strike continues, support strikes will soon begin too. The support strikes will stop work in various sectors, because their workers support the postal workers' strike. Workers in the transport sectors intend, for example, to stop freight traffic in ports and on railways.

The postal workers are on strike because they have a dispute with the employer, for example about pay. The postal workers started the strike, that is, stopped doing work, a week ago. If the strike continues, letters and parcels will arrive much more slowly than usual.

## The Venice flood

![High tides returned to Venice on Saturday 16 November. Walkways are being built on Sunday 17 November.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11073131)

Flood water has risen everywhere in Venice. Photo: Andrea Merola / EPA

The flood continues in the city of Venice in Italy. The water level is 1.6 metres higher than usual.

On Tuesday the water level rose as high as 1.9 metres. Then the flood water covered 80 percent of the city.

The flood has caused big damage. The mayor of Venice says that the damage is in the billions.

The flood also disrupts tourism, which gives work to many Venetians. Many tourist sites have been closed because of the flood.

About 36 million tourists visit Venice every year. Now many have cancelled their trips to Venice.

The Italian state has promised help to the city of Venice and its residents.

## The Moomin park in Japan

![A woman hugs a Nipsu character](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1848,h_1040,x_0,y_98/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6121945dce811302b28/1573814633)

In Japan, adults too are interested in Moomins. Photo: Jenny Matikainen / Yle

Many people have visited the Moomin park in Japan. The park opened in the spring. Already in the summer, there were more than a million visitors in total.

The Moomin park in Japan is the first Moomin park outside Finland. The park is in the city of Hanno. It is about an hour's trip from the capital, Tokyo.

The Moomin park has, for example, a Moomin house. The park's museum also tells about the life of Tove Jansson. Tove Jansson wrote the Moomin books.

Many adults also visit the Moomin park in Japan. They watched Moomins on television when they were children. That is why they still like Moomins.

The Moomin park in Japan has one special feature. The park's signs and notices are in Finnish. The park's communications director Akiko Ikeda says that the Japanese do not understand Finnish, but Finnish-language signs look nice.
