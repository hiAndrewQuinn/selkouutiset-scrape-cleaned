# Sunday 25.4.2021 (radio)

Passenger limits on buses are being lifted. Matti "Fredi" Siitonen has died. The USA has recognised the Armenian genocide. New astronauts have arrived at the ISS. Municipalities are Movers of the Year. Unfortunately the archive is missing. A technical fault is being looked for at the moment. Hopefully the archive will be restored soon.

## Bus travel restrictions are being lifted

Passenger restrictions on public transport are being lifted. In the Southwest Finland area the restrictions were lifted yesterday, Saturday.

In the Uusimaa and Helsinki areas the restrictions will be lifted tomorrow, Monday.

## Matti "Fredi" Siitonen has died

![Matti &quot;Fredi&quot; Siitonen photographed in May 2012.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4142,h_2330,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-79861460847b48326ef/1619323678)

Open image viewer

Matti Siitonen (1942-2021) Photo: Kimmo Mäntylä / Lehtikuva

Singer and composer **Matti Siitonen** has died. Matti Siitonen was known by the stage name Fredi. He performed a lot in the 1960s and 1970s.

Matti Siitonen released more than 10 albums and several singles and compilation albums. He represented Finland twice in the Eurovision Song Contest. Matti Siitonen is also remembered for the singing group Kivikasvot.

Matti Siitonen died yesterday, Saturday. He was 78 years old.

## The USA recognised the Armenian genocide

![Joe Biden standing at a lectern giving a speech.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1969,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5929325d85a992497ff/1569040858)

US President \*\*Joe Biden \*\*has recognised the Armenian genocide. Biden is the first US president to recognise the Armenian genocide.

The Armenian genocide took place in 1915–1917. At least 1.5 million Armenians were killed then in the Ottoman Empire during the First World War.

Turkey does not accept that the USA recognised the genocide. Turkey does not want the events to be called a genocide.

4 new astronauts have travelled to the space station. Next week, 4 other astronauts who have been there for a long time will return to Earth.

## Municipalities are Movers of the Year

![Two skiers ski on a trail in Turku's Nunnavuori.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4128,h_2322,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-26898356a0b7d20435c/1641541541)

Municipalities maintain ski tracks, for example. Photo: Yle/Linus Hoffman

The sports organisation Suomen Latu has chosen the Mover of the Year.

The Mover of the Year is chosen from an organisation or company that has promoted people's movement in Finland. This year, municipalities have been chosen as Mover of the Year.

The corona situation has made many sports hobbies difficult, but municipalities have helped people to move outdoors. Municipalities make outdoor ski tracks and trails, and, for example, outdoor gyms.
