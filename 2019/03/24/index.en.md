# Sunday 24.3.2019 (radio)

There was a large rescue operation on the Norwegian coast. There were parliamentary elections in Thailand. An attempt was made to hit Foreign Minister Timo Soini in Vantaa. Migratory birds are returning to Finland.

## Cruise ship rescued in Norway

![The Vikin Sky cruise ship drifting towards the shore at Hustadvika in Norway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10704236)

The cruise ship was in trouble because of the storm. Photo: Frank Einar Vatne / EPA

There has been a large rescue operation on the Norwegian coast.

Rescuers have helped a ship whose engine broke down in the storm. The ship reached port on Sunday evening.

The ship is a large cruise ship with almost 1,400 people on board. At first, the passengers were taken to safety by helicopter.

The situation was serious, but the ship was not in danger of sinking, and the passengers were not in great distress. Nobody died.

The ship was at sea about 2 kilometres from the shore when its engine broke on Saturday. There was a strong storm at sea.

## Election held in Thailand

![Polling station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10704447)

Open image viewer

Thai people voted on Sunday. Photo: Diego Azubel / EPA

An election has been held in Thailand. Thai people voted in the parliamentary elections on Sunday.

The election will show whether the power of the military continues in Thailand. 2 parties are competing for the election victory. One supports a military government and the other opposes it.

It seems that the election victory will go to the party that supports the military government. The military's power in Thailand may continue.

## An attempt was made to hit the foreign minister

![A man in a Soldiers of Odin jacket, who tried to hit the Blues' Timo Soini, knocked to the ground and detained at the Korso country market in Vantaa on Sunday, 24 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10704559)

An attempt was made to hit Foreign Minister Timo Soini on Sunday. A man tried to hit Soini at the Korso country market in Vantaa. Security guards prevented the act.

Soini continued his election campaigning as normal. Representatives of many parties were at the Korso country market, because the parliamentary elections are already 3 weeks away.

It is very unusual for someone to attack a politician in Finland. Usually politicians can talk with people in peace.

## Migratory birds return to Finland

![Red-hatted Matti Maskulin is an active birdwatcher. There is plenty to look at during the spring migration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10704525)

Open image viewer

Birdwatchers follow the spring migration of birds. Photo: Antti Haavisto / Yle

Spring has come for birdwatchers.

Migratory birds are returning to Finland. They have spent the winter in warm countries.

Birdwatchers have already seen many different birds, for example skylarks and white wagtails.

There is, however, a big difference between Southern Finland and Northern Finland. When birdwatchers in the north see, for example, the first skylark of spring, birdwatchers in the south have already seen skylarks hundreds of times.

You can also listen to the spring migration of birds. In spring, birds sing a lot. Male birds in particular sing when they try to attract female birds.
