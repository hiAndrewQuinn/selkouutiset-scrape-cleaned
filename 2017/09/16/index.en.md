# Saturday 16.9.2017 (radio)

The British police have arrested a young man because of Friday's bomb attack. Minister Paula Risikko says that the police have prevented many attacks in Finland in recent years. Most Finns want President Sauli Niinistö to continue as president. Sweden's Crown Princess Victoria is visiting Finland. The world's oldest person, Violet Moss-Brown from Jamaica, has died.

The British police have arrested a man because of Friday's bomb attack. The man is 18 years old.

## The Finnish police have prevented attacks

![Paula Risikko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9836187)

Open image viewer

Minister of the Interior Paula Risikko. Photo: Yle

Minister Paula Risikko says that the police have prevented many attacks in Finland in recent years. Risikko is Finland's Minister of the Interior. The Minister of the Interior is responsible for, for example, police matters.

Risikko said in a Yle TV interview that the police also prevented an attack against politicians. Someone planned to attack political decision-makers about 5 years ago, Risikko said. The police have also prevented, for example, school shootings.

In Minister Risikko's opinion, the police have managed to prevent attacks through good cooperation. In Finland, cooperation between different authorities works well, Risikko says.

## Niinistö is the most popular presidential candidate

![President Sauli Niinistö on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme on Saturday 2 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9812565)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Yle

Most Finns want President Sauli Niinistö to continue as president.

Finland has a presidential election in January. Helsingin Sanomat asked almost 1,000 people whom they want as president. 68 percent of respondents supported Sauli Niinistö.

The other presidential candidates had far fewer supporters. Pekka Haavisto came second in the poll. 13 percent of respondents supported Haavisto.

The presidential election will be held on 28 January.

## Victoria is visiting Finland

![Princess Victoria with her family](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9723737)

Open image viewer

Crown Princess Victoria with her family in Stockholm. Photo: AOP

Finland has received an important guest from neighbouring Sweden. Sweden's Crown Princess Victoria came to Finland on Saturday.

Victoria is visiting the Opera in Helsinki. On Saturday evening she will see an opera called Autumn Sonata. The opera's text is based on a film by the famous Swedish director Ingmar Bergman. The singer of the opera's lead role is the Swede Anne Sofie von Otter. Von Otter is known around the world.

Crown Princess Victoria is visiting Helsinki because independent Finland turns 100 this year.

## The world's oldest person died

![tropical fruit in a bowl.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9476448)

Open image viewer

Good food helps you live long, said the world's oldest person. Photo: AOP

The world's oldest person has died. She was Violet Moss-Brown from Jamaica. She was 117 years old.

Moss-Brown was the world's oldest person for almost a year.

Moss-Brown gave 2 reasons for her long life. They were a clean life and good food.
