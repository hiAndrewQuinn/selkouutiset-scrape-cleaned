# Sunday 9.2.2020 (radio)

A storm is battering Europe. Switzerland bans discrimination on the basis of sexual orientation. Cheetahs are being moved from Africa to India. Music makers were awarded at the Emma Gala.

## A storm is battering Europe

![A car in floodwater.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3521,h_1981,x_156,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6404865e3ffbe4bb7ea/1581251605)

A severe storm has hit many parts of Europe.

Flights and train services have been cancelled in many countries. Bridges have also been closed. Ferry traffic between Norway and Denmark has been cut off because of the storm.

The storm is, for example, in Ireland, Britain, France, the Netherlands and Germany.

The storm has torn off roofs of houses and knocked down trees and power lines. In Ireland power was cut to 10,000 homes in the morning. In Britain flood warnings have been issued for many areas.

In Denmark and Sweden the wind has blown very hard. In Finland too the wind is strong at night and on Monday. Trees may fall and there may be power cuts.

## Switzerland bans discrimination against homosexuals

![Election poster on a street in Geneva.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11200027)

Open image viewer

Switzerland has voted on a law that aims to eradicate discrimination against sexual minorities. Photo: Fabrice Coffrini / AFP

Switzerland bans discrimination that happens because of sexual orientation.

There has been a referendum on the matter in Switzerland. Preliminary results show that over 60 per cent of Swiss people supported the change in the law. At present Swiss law bans discrimination on the basis of religion or ethnicity.

The change in the law was approved in the Swiss parliament already in 2018. However, critics of the law demanded that a referendum be held.

## Cheetahs are being moved from Africa to India

![A cheetah lies on a rock in a savannah landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11199597)

Open image viewer

A cheetah lounging in the morning sun in the Masai Mara in Kenya. Photo: Rex Features / AOP

Cheetahs are being brought to India from Africa.

A few cheetahs are being brought to India from Namibia. The aim is to test whether an African cheetah can live in India.

India once had many cheetahs. Then they were hunted to extinction. The last wild cheetah was seen in India in 1952.

The cheetah is a cat. It is the fastest land animal in the world, running at over 90 kilometres an hour.

There are cheetahs in Africa. In addition, about 40 cheetahs live in Iran, in Asia.

## Music makers were awarded at the Emma Gala

![JVG on the terrace of Warner](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3532,h_1987,x_0,y_535/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5679185cf63d435e948/1559641456)

JVG on the terrace of Warner in 2019. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Some of the best Finnish music makers of last year have been awarded at the Emma Gala.

The band of the year is JVG. JVG also received 3 other awards. The album of the year award went to Chisu.

The artist of the year is Lauri Tähkä. The audience was allowed to decide this award. Lauri Tähkä is also the male solo singer of the year. The female solo singer of the year is Ellinoora.

The newcomer of the year is Jesse Markin, who was born in Liberia.
