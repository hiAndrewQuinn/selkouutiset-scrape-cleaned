# Tuesday 18.4.2017 (radio)

Early elections in Britain. The Turkish referendum. Differences in the price of school food. Edelfelt's painting. Gardens are changing.

## Early elections for Britain

![Theresa May holding a press conference in front of her official residence on Tuesday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9569667)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Theresa May gave a rare announcement in front of her official residence in London.  Photo: Daniel Leal-Olivas / EPA

British Prime Minister Theresa May wants Britain to hold early parliamentary elections on 8 June.

The real election year is only in 2020. Parliament must approve the early elections in a vote on Wednesday.

Prime Minister May says that early elections are needed because of Britain's exit from the EU, that is, Brexit.

There is a dispute about the EU exit in the British Parliament, and the Prime Minister thinks the arguing makes Britain's position harder when the country negotiates the EU exit.

## Election fraud is suspected in Turkey

![Demonstrators with signs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9569994)

Open image viewer

People in Turkey have protested against the result of the referendum. Photo: Tolga Bozoglu / EPA

The EU wants Turkey to investigate the suspicions of election fraud.

Foreign election observers say that there was fraud in Sunday's referendum.

In Turkey people have also demonstrated against the result of the referendum.

In the referendum, 51 percent of Turks supported the new constitution, which increases the power of Turkey's president.

## School food is expensive in small municipalities

![Courgette and minced meat bake and vegetables on a plate](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9420042)

Open image viewer

Children get school food for free. Municipalities pay different prices for school food.  Photo: Kati Rantala / Yle

In Finland, small municipalities often pay much more for children's school food than big municipalities.

For example, little Puumala pays more than 8 euros a day for one pupil's school food. In Puumala the children get lunch and a snack.

School food is cheapest in Vantaa. In Vantaa, one pupil's school food costs about 1.7 euros a day.

In Finland, one pupil's school food costs on average 2.76 euros a day.

In big cities, one pupil's school food is often cheaper because a lot of food can be made at once.

## Edelfelt's painting was found in Russia

![An oil painting by Albert Edelfelt in a gilded frame.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9568497)

Open image viewer

There are two boys in Edelfelt's painting. Earlier it was thought that there were two girls in it.  Photo: Erkka Mikkonen / Yle

A painting by the famous Finnish artist Albert Edelfelt has been found in Russia.

Edelfelt painted the picture in 1881. The painting was thought to have been lost.

A Finnish art researcher found the painting with the help of the internet in a museum in the Russian city of Rybinsk. In the painting there are two boys who are nephews of Tsar Alexander II.

In Finland people hope that the painting can be brought to an exhibition in Finland some day.

## New plants in northern gardens

![Plantings in an allotment garden.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9073491)

Open image viewer

Many kinds of garden plants do well in Tornio in Lapland. Photo: Kyösti Vaara/Yle

Plants that did not do well in Finland's cold weather before are now growing in Finnish gardens.

The climate has warmed, and new plant species can be planted in gardens.

Growers of garden plants say that the change has happened over 20 years. In the future the climate will warm more and Finland can get a big harvest from gardens.

For example, fruit trees did not do very well in Northern Finland before. Now many apple trees grow, for example, in Rovaniemi.
