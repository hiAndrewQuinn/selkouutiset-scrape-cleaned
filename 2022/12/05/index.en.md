## Finland's NATO membership

![Finnish and NATO flags.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_500/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10421866388837d94603/1669891021)

Open image viewer

Finland's NATO membership is still waiting for the approval of 2 countries. Photo: PX_MEDIA

The government has submitted a proposal to Parliament on Finland's NATO membership. This means that Parliament can begin to deal with the decision on Finland joining NATO.

Finland's NATO membership is still waiting for approval from Turkey and Hungary. The other 28 NATO countries have already approved Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership.

Parliament needs time to handle the NATO decision before the election recess, which starts in March.

## Plastic bottles and milk cartons

![New, non-detachable plastic bottle cap.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4161,h_2340,x_0,y_112/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10351196375e43f610a2/1670223529)

Open image viewer

A new kind of plastic bottle, whose cap does not come off the bottle. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

Plastic bottles and milk cartons are being renewed. A new EU regulation says that the plastic cap must stay attached to the bottle throughout its use. The aim is to reduce the amount of plastic waste in the environment.

When the cap is attached to the bottle or carton, the cap also goes into recycling with the packaging. Plastic caps are a common plastic litter in nature. That is why the EU wants the caps to be collected.

New packaging will come into use in Finland already next year. The EU regulation says that new caps must be introduced at the latest the year after, that is, in 2024.

## Young people's road deaths

![A badly wrecked crashed car at the side of the road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_254/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-948237626bd35511cf6/1651233748)

Open image viewer

The cause of young drivers' accidents is often high speed. Photo: Rami Moilanen / Yle

More young people have died on the roads than usual. Almost 40 young people aged 17 to 24 have already died in traffic this year.

The cause of young drivers' road deaths is often excessive speed. Young drivers die in accidents twice as often as drivers of other ages.

## Tax refunds

![A person holds euro banknotes at a cash machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5200,h_2925,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1031165636a59177c096/1667914232)

Open image viewer

Tax refunds are now paid at different times to different people. Photo: LianeM / Alamy/All Over Press

The Tax Administration has paid tax refunds again on Monday to some Finns.

Most Finns received their tax refund in August or September. Some tax refunds are being paid now, in early December.

People's taxation is completed at different times. The new arrangement has been in use for 4 years now. Previously, all tax refunds were always paid at the beginning of December.

## Independence Day

![Schoolchildren, big and small, stand around a flagpole. One of the big schoolchildren is raising the Finnish flag up the pole, and the wind has blown the flag far from the pole.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3929,h_2211,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1043943638dd8e405e92/1670240673)

Open image viewer

Many schools celebrated Independence Day on Monday or last week. Photo: Laura Valta / Yle

Tomorrow, Tuesday, Finland celebrates Independence Day. Finland turns 105 years old.

Independence Day is a public holiday, so many shops, companies, banks and offices are closed. Grocery stores and shopping centres are, however, open.

Independence Day is celebrated in the traditional way again after 2 corona years. The best-known Independence Day celebration is the Presidential Palace reception. President Sauli Niinistö has once again invited many guests to the Presidential Palace.

Yle will show the Presidential Palace reception on television and on Yle Areena.
