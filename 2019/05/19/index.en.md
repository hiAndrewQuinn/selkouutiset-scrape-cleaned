# Sunday 19.5.2019 (radio)

Government negotiations have continued for 9 days. On Sunday, the Remembrance Day for the Fallen was held. The world's largest election ends in India. Kansanradio is 40 years old. Minetit and OVO Team won medals. The Netherlands won Eurovision.

## Government negotiations on day 9

![Antti Rinne arriving at the government negotiations.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10787519)

Open image viewer

SDP's Antti Rinne, who is leading the government negotiations. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The government negotiations have continued at Säätytalo in Helsinki for 9 days already.

The groups must have their proposals ready by Tuesday. At the weekend, economic questions, among others, were settled.

Many civil society organisations have reminded the negotiators about issues that are important to them.

Psychologists and psychology students handed over an online petition at Säätytalo this morning, which about 400 people have signed. The petition supports young people and children who feel anxiety about climate change.

## Soldiers buried on the Remembrance Day for the Fallen

![Cadets lowering an unknown soldier's coffin into the grave in Lappeenranta](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10791068)

Open image viewer

A cadet hands over the coffin of a fallen soldier. Photo: Kalle Purhonen / Yle

On Sunday the Remembrance Day for the Fallen was held.

President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö laid a wreath at the Hero's Cross at Hietaniemi Cemetery in Helsinki.

In Lappeenranta, unknown Finnish soldiers who were found in Russia have been buried. 50 of the fallen, who died in the Winter War or the Continuation War, were laid to rest in the grave.

They were found in searches over the last couple of summers, mainly in Tali and Taipale.

## India's giant election ends

![Followers of Mother Teresa queued to vote on Sunday in Kolkata.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10790796)

Open image viewer

Nuns who follow Mother Teresa queued at the polling station. Photo: Piyal Adhikary / EPA

The world's largest election ends today in India. Voting in India's parliamentary election lasted 6 weeks.

About 900 million people in India have the right to vote.

The counting of votes starts on Thursday, and the results are also expected then. The votes are counted by machine.

Opinion polls say that the Hindu nationalist BJP party will keep the top place.

## Minetit and OVO Team won medals

![Minetit at the 2019 World Championships](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10790899)

Open image viewer

Tampere's Sisu Minetit won silver in gymnastics. Photo: Carita Berg

The Finnish teams Minetit and OVO Team have won medals in the group gymnastics World Championships. The championships were in Cartagena, Spain.

Minetit won silver and OVO Team bronze. The Russian Madonna team won the world title.

Tampere's Sisu Minetit won the world title in 2015 and 2017. OVO Team from Espoo has won the title 7 times.

## Kansanradio turned 40

![Kansanradio presenters Olli Haapakangas and Jaana Selin, and a sound engineer in the studio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10790897)

Open image viewer

Kansanradio presenters Olli Haapakangas and Jaana Selin. Photo: Kimmo Hiltunen / Yle

Yle's Kansanradio programme has turned 40. Ordinary citizens took part in the anniversary broadcast, and so did President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö.

The programme has always discussed current issues. The programme has both humour and serious matters.

Kansanradio gets 10,000 contacts from its audience every year.

## The Netherlands won Eurovision

![The Dutch Eurovision representative Duncan Laurence kisses the winner's trophy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10790755)

Open image viewer

Duncan Laurence is delighted with the victory. Photo: Vyacheslav Prokofyev / EPA

The Netherlands has won this year's Eurovision. Duncan Laurence won the victory with the song Arcade. The contest was held on Saturday evening in Tel Aviv, Israel.

During the night the points were also announced for the countries that fell out of the final. Finland's song Look Away got 23 points and was the last in the semi-final.
