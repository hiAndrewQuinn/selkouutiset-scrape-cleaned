# Tuesday 11.6.2019 (radio)

Opposition parties criticised the new government's programme. Nokian Tyres is doing well in Russia. There have been thunderstorms in Germany. VR is renewing how train tickets are sold. Women over 70 need only 4,000 steps of walking a day.

## Parliamentary debate

![Prime Minister Antti Rinne in the plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki on 11 June 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10825717)

Prime Minister Antti Rinne in Parliament. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Parliament in Finland has debated the new government's programme.

The opposition parties criticised the government's programme. The Finns Party and the National Coalition said that the government's economic plans are bad. They suspected that the government will take on more debt for Finland.

The new government started work last week. The Prime Minister of the government is Antti Rinne of the SDP.

## Nokian Tyres

![A tyre in a press at Nokian factories.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9394049)

Open image viewer

Nokian Tyres also has a factory in Russia. Photo: Jussi Mansikka / Yle

The Finnish tyre company Nokian Tyres is doing well in Russia.

Nokian Tyres has a large factory in Russia. The factory makes tyres every day, enough to fill 50 lorries. About 60 per cent of the tyres are sold outside Russia.

The Finnish company has a good reputation in Russia. The safety of winter tyres in particular is important to customers.

Nokian Tyres has invested almost one billion euros in its factory in Russia.

## Thunderstorms in Germany

![Lightning in the sky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10792685)

Open image viewer

Lightning in a thunderstorm. Photo: Leonid Tit / AOP

There have been thunderstorms in Germany. The thunderstorms caused damage in Bavaria, in Southern Germany.

For example, hail fell on Lake Ammersee. The hailstones were 5 centimetres thick, that is, the size of a golf ball. People were injured when the hailstones hit tourist boats. Houses were also damaged.

## VR's ticket sales

![VR's new-model ticket vending machines will start to appear at stations this week.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10826076)

Open image viewer

VR says that the new ticket machines are easy to use. Photo: VR

VR is renewing how train tickets are sold. VR's machines, websites and phone app are changing.

VR says that the new machines are easier to use than the old ones. The screen of the machines is larger than before, for example.

New features are coming to VR's phone app. For example, it will send the customer a message if the train is late.

VR's new phone app will come into use in the coming days. However, the customer can choose for themselves when to try the new app. The old and new apps will be in use at the same time at first.

VR's new websites will be published in the autumn.

## Walking for older people

![Two seniors Nordic walking.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9883636)

Open image viewer

Older people do not need to walk 10,000 steps a day. Photo: Iuliia Mazur / AOP

Walking is healthy. Many people have heard that it is healthy for a person to walk 10,000 steps a day.

Now researchers have new information: for women over 70, about 4,000 steps a day is enough.

The information is important. Until now it has not been known how much older people should move.
