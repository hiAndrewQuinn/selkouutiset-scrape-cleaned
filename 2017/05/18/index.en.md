# Thursday 18.5.2017 (radio)

The names of the new upper secondary school graduates are known. Play and learn particle physics! There is a shortage of Islam teachers. City foxes bring joy and benefit. The weather is finally getting warmer.

## The new graduates are known

![A graduation cap among roses in the picture](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7998929)

Open image viewer

A graduate gets a white cap. Photo: Raila Paavola / Yle

The results of the spring matriculation exams have been announced. This spring about 26,000 young people who have finished upper secondary school get the white cap.

The best graduates got 8 laudaturs, which is the best grade. 6 students in 5 different schools got 8 laudaturs. Two of them went to school in Lohja and the others in Espoo, Pori, Helsinki and Kotka.

The names of all this spring's graduates can be found on Yle's website.

## A game teaches particle physics

![Lightneer brand director Peter Westerbacka, CEO Lauri Järvilehto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9615713)

Open image viewer

Peter Vesterbacka and Lauri Järvilehto's company made the game Big Bang Legends.  Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

A Finnish game company wants to teach particle physics to millions of people with a mobile phone game.

The game has been made together with top scientists. The idea of the game is that, for example, atoms, quarks, oxygen and carbon become familiar while a person has fun, that is, plays.

Professor Marcus du Sautoy says that games are the future of studying.

## More qualified Islam teachers are needed

![Suaad Onniselkä, Islam teacher, Vesala comprehensive school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9605670)

Open image viewer

Teacher Suaad Onniselkä.  Photo: Suaad Onniselkä

Schools have a shortage of Islam teachers.

In Finland there are only about 20 qualified Islam teachers who have trained as teachers at a Finnish university. There are 8000 schoolchildren who believe in Islam, and the number is growing fast.

Teacher Suaad Onniselkä says that school gives information about Islam. School does not make "good Muslims". For example, the teacher does not tell pupils which branch of Islam is the right one.

Most of the Islam teachers in Finnish schools are not qualified. Many are immigrants who, for example, do not know the curriculum of Finnish schools.

## City foxes do not cause much harm

![Foxes playing](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8084188)

Open image viewer

City foxes in Yle's yard in Helsinki. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Nowadays many foxes live in Helsinki and the nearby cities. "City foxes" also live in people's yards, for example under the terrace. Many people say it is nice to watch what the foxes do in the yard.

Experts say that foxes do not cause much harm in the city. Their den may smell bad. Remains of, for example, a squirrel or a duck are left in the fox's den, and they attract rats.

Foxes are useful when they eat rabbits, which there are too many of in many places.

## Warmth at last

![Daffodils blooming.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9608053)

Open image viewer

Spring picks up speed at the weekend. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

The weather is finally getting warmer. On Friday in many places in southern Finland it is over 20 degrees and the sun is shining. During Friday the rain stops in the north too.

At the weekend the sun shines in almost the whole country, and especially in the south it is as warm as in summer.
