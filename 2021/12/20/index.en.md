# Monday 20.12.2021 (radio)

Corona infections. Corona measures. Places at universities. Folkhälsan's Swedish course. Weather forecast.

## Corona infections

There are now many corona patients in intensive care. Photo: Arash Matin / Yle

Corona infections are now increasing quickly. During the weekend almost 6000 new corona infections have been found.

The number of corona patients in intensive care is also growing. There are about 60 corona patients in intensive care.

Intensive care needs a lot of nurses and equipment. Intensive care doctors are worried about how long intensive care places will still be enough.

The number of corona deaths is also growing. During the weekend about 40 people have died of corona.

Researchers fear that the Omicron variant of the corona virus will spread in restaurants. Photo: Ilkka Klemola / Yle

A group of researchers demands stronger corona measures from the government. For example, the researchers hope that restaurants will be closed for some time.

The researchers are worried about the Omicron variant of the corona virus. Because of the Omicron variant, corona is again spreading quickly in many countries. The researchers want to slow down the spread of Omicron in Finland.

## Places at universities

![Antti Kurvinen arriving at the press conference, with a mask on his face.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1918,h_1076,x_0,y_36/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89430861c04c57d686a/1641818146)

Open image viewer

Minister Antti Kurvinen told about the new places at universities on Monday. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

New study places are coming to universities. Next year there will be about 2300 new study places.

More students are being taken on in fields where more workers are needed.

Minister Antti Kurvinen said on Monday that the new study places will be especially for studies in social and health care. Places for studies in engineering and telecommunications will also be added.

One reason for the new study places is that the government wants to improve the skills of Finns. That is why the level of education must be raised.

## Folkhälsan's course

![People at a language course.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12240398)

Open image viewer

Here they practise working in a health centre in Swedish. Photo: Yle

Folkhälsan organises Swedish lessons for Russian-speaking immigrants.

Folkhälsan now has a Swedish course in Helsinki. If the course goes well, more courses may come.

Folkhälsan is a Swedish-speaking health sector organisation. It hopes that the course participants learn enough Swedish that they can study or work in the health sector.

Folkhälsan organises Swedish teaching especially for Russian speakers, because Russian speakers are the largest foreign-language group in Finland.

Folkhälsan hopes that some Russian speakers want to work in the health sector in Swedish.

## Weather

On Tuesday the weather stays cold and mostly dry across the whole country.

In many places it is also clear. In the east it may snow a little.

The frost varies between 10 degrees in the south and as much as 30 degrees in Lapland.

Snow will fall at the end of the week. Snow will fall during Christmas especially in southern and central Finland.
