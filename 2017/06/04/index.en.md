# Sunday 4.6.2017 (radio)

There has been a terror attack in London. President Sauli Niinistö condemns the London attack. Young people have celebrated the end of school calmly. The Finnish Army held a big parade in Helsinki.

## Terror attack in London

![Police help an injured victim of the attack into an ambulance.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9648764)

Open image viewer

Police take a person injured in the attack to an ambulance. Photo: Daniel Sorabji / AFP

A terror attack has taken place in London, the capital of Britain. The attackers killed 7 people. About 50 people were injured.

The attack happened late on Saturday evening in central London. The attackers drove a car into people on London Bridge. After that they stabbed people with knives in the Borough Market area.

There were 3 attackers. They died when the police shot them. The attackers were quite certainly Islamist terrorists.

## Niinistö condemns the attack

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9637490)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Finland's President Sauli Niinistö condemns the London attack. Niinistö says that Finland will never accept terrorism. Niinistö also says that democracy and freedom are stronger than hatred.

Niinistö is sorry for all those whose family members died in the London attack. Niinistö says that he and all Finns share in Britain's grief.

## A peaceful weekend

![Children standing outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9107253)

Open image viewer

School ended and young people celebrated. Photo: Antti Ojala / Yle

Here in Finland young people have celebrated the end of school. The police say that the partying was quite calm. One reason for this was the cold weather.

Young people celebrated in parks and on beaches, for example. The police monitored the partying. The police, for example, poured out alcohol that was found on young people.

Chief Inspector Sami Joutjärvi of the Eastern Finland police wonders about parents' behaviour. Some parents called the police when their children were outside drinking alcohol. Why do the parents not take care of their children themselves, Chief Inspector Joutjärvi asks.

## The army parade

![Defence Forces parade at Senate Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9648929)

Open image viewer

Armoured vehicles were also seen in the parade. Photo: Juha Vuohelainen / Yle

There was a big parade in Helsinki on Sunday. About 1,300 people took part in the parade, for example Finnish Army soldiers. Army armoured vehicles also took part in the parade, and Army aircraft flew over Helsinki's sky.

The parade was held on Sunday, because that was the Defence Forces' flag day, that is the Army's flag celebration. The flag celebration is always on 4 June, which is Marshal Mannerheim's birthday. Mannerheim led the Finnish Army in the Second World War more than 70 years ago. Mannerheim was born 150 years ago, that is in 1867.
