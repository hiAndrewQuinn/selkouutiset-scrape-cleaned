Forest companies met Prime Minister Sanna Marin. More than a billion euros in corona support has been paid to companies. Yle starts news for children. Weather forecast.

## Forest companies met Prime Minister Marin

![Ilkka Hämälä, CEO of Metsä Group (left), and Timo Jaatinen, managing director of the Finnish Forest Industries Federation, arriving at the Government Palace to meet Prime Minister Sanna Marin on Wednesday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11523521)

Ilkka Hämälä, CEO of Metsä Group, and Timo Jaatinen, managing director of the Finnish Forest Industries Federation, came to meet Prime Minister Sanna Marin on Wednesday. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The leaders of Finnish forest companies have met Prime Minister **Sanna Marin**. The forest companies and Prime Minister Marin have had a small argument about whether forest companies may close paper mills.

The leaders of the forest companies say that the meeting with Prime Minister Sanna Marin was good. The forest companies believe that the Finnish government will not make it harder for the forest industry to work.

The situation of the forest industry is much in the news, because the forest company UPM said last week that UPM will end the operation of the Kaipola paper mill in Jämsä.

## More than a billion in corona support has been paid to companies

![A person playing a mobile game at a game event in Bangkok in 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4267,h_2400,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7072275f329fc3bf94b/1597153363)

Open image viewer

Game companies, for example, have received corona support. Photo: Rungroj Yongrit/ EPA

This year Finland has given companies more than 900 million euros of corona support through Business Finland.

The companies that have received the most corona support are in trade, construction, the software industry and the game industry, tourism and restaurants, and the creative industries. About 30,000 projects applied for support to develop companies. About 70 percent of them got support.

The ELY Centres also give support to companies. This year they have given about 300 million euros of corona support.
