# Saturday 7.4.2018 (radio)

14 people died in a bus accident in Canada. Train traffic has been cut between Kouvola and Mäntyharju. The presidential couple's baby has been given a name. Finns use social media a lot. In the USA researchers have invented a device that can read thoughts.

## Bus accident in Canada

The accident happened near the town of Tisdale. Photo: Yle News Graphics

A bad bus accident has happened in Canada. 14 people died in the accident.

Many of the dead were young, because the bus was carrying a junior ice hockey team. The team's players are 16 to 21 years old.

The team was on its way to an ice hockey game when its bus crashed into a lorry.

## Train traffic cut on the Savo line

![Map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10149723)

Open image viewer

Trains were replaced by buses between Kouvola and Mäntyharju. Photo: Yle News Graphics

Here in Finland train traffic was cut on Saturday between Kouvola and Mäntyharju.

Trains have not been running because there has been a dangerous substance on the railway. The substance was the kind that catches fire easily.

The substance leaked onto the railway from a goods wagon. The wagon was not in service, but it started to move and broke.

The rescue service made sure that the substance did not catch fire. Passengers were taken by bus.

## Finns on social media

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10147032)

Open image viewer

Finns use social media services a lot. Photo: Laura Koivunen | Yle News Graphics

Finns use social media, or "some", a lot. A new Yle survey says that 90 per cent of Finns use social media.

On social media Finns use WhatsApp the most. Over 70 per cent of the people who answered the survey were WhatsApp users. WhatsApp messages have replaced text messages.

The second most used by Finns is Facebook.

There are differences between women and men in how they use social media. Women use Instagram much more than men. On Instagram users share pictures.

There are also differences between old and young people. Old people use Facebook and WhatsApp, but young people use many different social media services.

## A device reads thoughts

![A man with a device on his head that recognises words.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10149676)

Open image viewer

The device senses from the muscles of the neck and face what words a person is thinking. Photo: MIT Media Lab

Finally, science news from the USA. There, researchers have invented a device that can read thoughts. The device understands words that a person is thinking.

The device is put on the neck. The device follows the movements of the muscles of the neck and face. These small muscles move when a person thinks words. From the movements of the muscles the device understands what the person is saying in their thoughts.

Already today there are machines and services that people use by speaking. The new device may make it possible for people to use machines by thinking words.
