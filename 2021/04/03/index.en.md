# Saturday 3.4.2021 (radio)

Municipal election topics. Empty houses. Weak ice. The weather changes.

## Yle asked about municipal election topics

![Should people be allowed to be registered in two municipalities and pay taxes to both?](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11864667)

Open image viewer

Yle checked the parties' positions on the important questions in the municipal elections.

The pandemic period has pushed almost a million Finns to work remotely. Many people are thinking of spending more time at their summer cottage.

Yle asked about citizens' chance to be registered in 2 municipalities and pay taxes to both. The Centre Party, the Greens and the Swedish People's Party of Finland (RKP) supported dual municipality membership.

The SDP, the Left Alliance, the National Coalition Party, the Christian Democrats and the Now Movement do not support paying taxes in 2 municipalities.

Opinions were also divided on home care staffing ratios, that is, how many carers there must be per client. The SDP, the Greens, the Left Alliance and the Now Movement support the staffing ratio.

The National Coalition Party, the Centre Party and the Christian Democrats believe that the staffing ratio does not guarantee the quality of home care.

The chairman of The Finns Party, Jussi Halla-aho, wants to solve the question of home care by saving money from less important areas.

The municipal elections are in June.

## Finland has many empty houses

![An abandoned house in the middle of vegetation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2842,h_1599,x_0,y_1195/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7026785f1e8fba1058e/1595838465)

Open image viewer

Photo: Esa Huuhko/Yle

There are many empty detached houses in Finland. There are about 66,000 detached houses in Finland where no one has lived permanently for more than 7 years.

Almost half are in the countryside. However, some of them are used as holiday homes.

The most empty detached houses are in South Savo. They are usually old main buildings of farms.

## The ice is already weak

![A person who fell into the ice is rescued.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11847039)

Open image viewer

A person who fell into the ice is rescued. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

People who fell through the ice have been rescued in Helsinki and Pyhtää, among other places.

One of those who ended up in the water was skiing, and the other was walking on the ice.

The Gulf of Finland Coast Guard reminds people that it is no longer worth going onto the ice.

At sea you can already travel by boat, and the ice cover is thin.

## The weather becomes unstable

![Weather map: Saturday is the driest and sunniest of the coming days. Rain will fall on Sunday and especially on Monday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11869351)

Open image viewer

Saturday is the driest of the coming days. Photo: Yle Weather/Matti Huutonen

The good weather for outdoor activities at Easter is coming to an end. Already during the night, scattered showers will move east across Lapland.

On Sunday during the day, it will be fairly cloudy in North Ostrobothnia, Kainuu, Lapland and perhaps North Karelia. Some rain may also fall.

It will be sunniest in Southern and Southwest Finland, but there too the wind makes it feel colder.

On Monday a low-pressure system will hit Finland properly. A lot of rain will fall, and the wind will be strong.

In the south it will rain and even sleet, in the north rain and sleet, and in Northern Lapland snow. The rain is not continuous, though.

Yle meteorologist Matti Huutonen warns of poor driving conditions on Monday, especially in the north.

Although daytime temperatures are above zero, it may be frosty at night even in the south.
