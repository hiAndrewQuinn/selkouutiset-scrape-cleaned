## Cold weather raises blood pressure

![Storage worker dressed in winter gear at Valio in Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10365063)

Open image viewer

In cold weather you must remember to dress well. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

New information has been obtained in Finland about how cold weather affects health.

The study found that cold weather raises blood pressure a lot.

When blood pressure rises, the heart has to work hard. Then a sudden illness can strike.

Tiina Ikäheimo, who made the study, says that in cold weather it is really important to dress well and protect yourself from the cold. Blood pressure should also be monitored.

Cold affects health more than heat

## A club just for old men

![Velmut members sit in their club room.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10364365)

Open image viewer

A club for old men meets in Joensuu. Photo: Heikki Haapalainen / Yle

In Joensuu in Eastern Finland there is a club just for old men. It is meant for men over 65 years old.

You find company in the club, says Unto Rautiainen from Joensuu.

\- It's a great way to pass the time, and it's varied. You feel lonely, and it feels quite lonely to be in this flat, says Unto.

In Joensuu, old men are sought out and invited to join the club. Old people are often lonely.

## There is a shortage of nursery teachers

![Children's coat hooks in a day care centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10168494)

Open image viewer

There is a lot of work for nursery teachers in the capital region. Photo: Linda Manner / Lehtikuva

There is a serious shortage of qualified nursery teachers in the capital region.

In Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa, several hundred trained nursery teachers are needed.

Vantaa gave nursery teachers a pay rise of 145 euros. Even so, there is a serious shortage of workers.

Day care centres have many substitute nursery teachers. Not all substitutes have the right training.

## Defence cooperation with France

![Florence Parly](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10160125)

Open image viewer

French Minister of Defence Florence Parly is visiting Finland. Photo: Michel Euler / AFP

Finland and France have signed a bilateral defence agreement.

Finland has a similar agreement with Britain and the United States. France wants Finland to join the European defence project too. It is a project in which countries quickly step into crises.

France thinks Finland is well suited to sorting out crises. Finland will decide soon whether it joins.
