# Thursday 13.8.2020 (radio)

A recommendation on face masks was given. A recommendation on remote work was given. Corona infections have increased. The Turku shipyard is cutting staff. Investing in shares has grown a lot.

TV Clear News has a summer break. It will continue on 17.8.
Radio Clear News is made every day, also in the summer.

## THL recommends face masks

![Surgical face mask](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_544/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7037145f228e77e7d19/1596101517)

A face mask is recommended on public transport and whenever you have to be close to strangers without a safe distance. Photo: Jenni Joensuu / Yle

A recommendation on face masks has been given in Finland. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, recommends that people wear a face mask in areas where there are corona infections.

THL recommends that it is a good idea to wear a mask on public transport, that is, on buses, trams and trains. It is a good idea to wear a mask in places where strangers are close to each other.

The mask recommendation applies to Finns over 15 years old. You do not have to wear a mask, but it is a good idea to wear one.

## The government recommends remote work

![Sports newsroom news chief Janne Karinkanta and graphic designer Tanja Ylitalo in the newsroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4803,h_2702,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6773175ebfbe974177c/1589625423)

When there are only a few people in the office, the coronavirus does not spread easily. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

A new recommendation on remote work has been given in Finland. The government recommends that Finns do remote work in areas where there are corona infections at workplaces.

There are corona infections at workplaces now in the hospital districts of Helsinki and Uusimaa, Southwest Finland and West Pohja.

The areas where remote work is recommended may change if the corona situation changes.

## Corona infections have increased

![COVID-19 testing point signpost at the airport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3600,h_2025,x_0,y_3021/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7047535f280b654399d/1596460035)

Open image viewer

Corona tests are now done for everyone who has flu symptoms. Photo: Jari Kärkkäinen/ YLE

41 new corona infections have been reported in Finland on Thursday. The number of corona infections has risen now in August. There were fewer infections in July.

Some of the new corona infections have come to Finland from abroad, because Finns have travelled abroad.

More children are now going for corona tests than before, but no corona infections have been found in them.

## The Turku shipyard is losing work for many

![Meyer Turku shipyard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_337/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-422451599ad6aaea0e8/1588674503)

Open image viewer

Cruise ships are being built at the Turku shipyard. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The shipbuilding company Meyer's shipyard in Turku is cutting staff. Work ends for more than 160 people at the Turku shipyard. The shipyard builds cruise ships.

Many shipping companies are not ordering new ships now, because cruise travel has fallen a lot.

Many shipping companies have cancelled ship orders.

## Investing in shares is growing in popularity

![Stock market figures and money.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8852752)

Open image viewer

Many hope that they can buy shares cheaply, whose value will rise. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The number of share investors in Finland has grown a lot this year.

At least 33,000 Finns have started investing in shares this year.

The popularity of investing in shares has grown quickly, because a share savings account was introduced in Finland this year, and because many shares have fallen in value because of the corona situation.
