# Monday 27.11.2017 (radio)

In Indonesia, in Asia, people are preparing for a volcanic eruption. Roads can be slippery. The Finnish army has a big exercise this week. Britain's Prince Harry is getting married.

## The eruption on Bali

![Evacuation on Bali, people packing their things into a lorry on 27.11.2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9949893)

People on Bali are fleeing the volcanic eruption. Photo: Muhammad Fauzy / AOP

In Indonesia, in Asia, people are preparing for a volcanic eruption. The volcano Agung is erupting on the island of Bali.

Bali's international airport was closed on Monday. Over 400 flights were cancelled. But tourists are not in danger.

The authorities are moving residents away from near the volcano. Thousands of people on Bali have to flee the volcanic eruption.

## Slippery roads

![An icy road in a car's headlights](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9375728)

Open image viewer

The road can be icy in the morning, even if it is above zero during the day. Photo: Riina Kasurinen/Yle

Here in Finland, roads can now be slippery. Roads are slippery especially in the morning.

There were many accidents on Monday morning. The worst accident happened in Virrat. There one person died when a car and a lorry crashed into each other.

In Masku, a bus drove off the road and tipped into a ditch. There were schoolchildren on the bus. Nobody was badly injured.

In Kirkkonummi there was a pile-up. A pile-up means an accident that involves many cars. The pile-up in Kirkkonummi involved 5 cars.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute is warning drivers. The road surface can be below freezing and icy, even if the air temperature is above zero.

The Finnish army has a big exercise in Southern Finland this week.

About 9,000 people are taking part in the exercise. Military aircraft are also part of the exercise.

The army's aircraft are flying, for example, in the Helsinki area.
