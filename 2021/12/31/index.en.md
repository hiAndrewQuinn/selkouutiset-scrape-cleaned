# Friday 31.12.2021 (radio)

Record number of corona infections. Marin on corona vaccinations. Fireworks. The weather.

## Record number of corona infections

![People going down an escalator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5905,h_3321,x_0,y_614/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89731361cc6e2d9e035/1640788347)

There is a record number of new corona infections.

The Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, said on Friday that there are over 9,000 new corona infections. That is more than ever before in one day.

In addition, THL reported almost 2,000 corona infections from previous days. So on Friday, over 11,000 more corona infections were added to the statistics.

Most of the new corona infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa region.

THL also reported 10 new corona deaths on Friday. In total, over 1,500 people have died in Finland because of corona.

## Prime Minister's greeting

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin in the plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki on 28 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_159/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89691661cb28264fa4f/1640704148)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin has published a New Year's greeting.

In the greeting, the Prime Minister says that corona vaccinations are important. Corona vaccinations are the best way to fight corona, Marin says.

The Prime Minister hopes that everyone will get corona vaccinations. Marin reminds that by getting a corona vaccination, a person protects themselves, all their loved ones and the whole of society.

Corona vaccinations also help hospitals, because vaccinations reduce the number of corona patients in hospitals, Prime Minister Sanna Marin says.

## Sales of fireworks

![The fireworks sale started at the Tokmanni store in Ruoholahti, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89729661cc69a71084c/1640786385)

Today, Friday, is New Year's Eve, and a lot of fireworks have been sold. Rockets and other fireworks are part of New Year for many Finns.

Many people have bought their own fireworks, because public fireworks displays have been cancelled because of corona. Shops expect fireworks sales to reach a record this year.

Shops say that customers are now buying a lot of quiet fireworks. Not everyone wants to buy loud rockets, which scare animals.

Some shops no longer sell fireworks at all. They say that fireworks are bad for the environment.

Fireworks may be set off on New Year's Eve in the evening. Firing fireworks must stop at 2 am at night.

## New Year's weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12254217)

Open image viewer

The new year starts in frosty weather. On Saturday, New Year's Day, there is frost of 5 to 10 degrees across the whole country.

The weather also becomes drier. On Saturday no more snow falls. The sun can also be seen in the sky on New Year's Day.

Sunday is also a dry, frosty day. However, snowfall arrives in Western Finland during Sunday.
