## Raahe steel mill

![SSAB factory in Raahe.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_100/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-85523561446d9b91832/1631874598)

Open image viewer

Photo: Hannu Vallas / SSAB

Steel producer SSAB is starting change negotiations at the Raahe steel mill.

SSAB says that little steel is being bought now. That is why SSAB wants to reduce costs by at least 10 million euros a year.

The change negotiations concern about 2400 employees. It is not yet known how work will change and whether some employees will be left without work.

At the Raahe mill, SSAB makes, for example, special steel.

## Detached houses

![Ella Sälpäkivi's detached house construction site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3981,h_2239,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-86962461701c5e89a23/1634737294)

Detached house construction site. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Few detached houses are being built now.

Last year the building of about 6800 detached houses was started. This year the number may be 50 percent smaller.

The small-house industry says that detached houses have not been built this little for at least 50 years.

## Electricity price

![A phone whose screen shows an open Facebook group called &quot;Pörssisähkö&quot;, held in a man's hand. Open image viewer

Users of spot electricity follow the electricity price closely. Photo: Petri Karvinen / Yle

Spot electricity is now cheap. On Monday spot electricity has been almost free.

The cheap price of spot electricity may continue for several weeks. The price of spot electricity varies, as it follows the market price of electricity.

The market price of electricity is now low, because a lot of electricity has been produced because of strong winds and rain.

Also more nuclear power is becoming available, as maintenance work at nuclear power plants ends.

Homes that have an electricity contract for spot electricity use spot electricity.

Many homes have a fixed electricity contract, in which the electricity price does not vary.

## Pori tap water

![Flood water on the city streets in Pori.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3271,h_1840,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11763136511175479bac/1695626637)

Open image viewer

Damage to big water pipes could be seen on the streets of Pori. Photo: Rami Koivula / Yle

There have been problems with tap water in Pori. 2 big water pipes broke in the city.

Therefore the water pressure has been weak and the water may have looked dirty. The water utility says that the water can be drunk.

The water utility hopes that people of Pori do not wash laundry on Monday.

## The weather

On Tuesday the weather is dry almost across the whole country and cloudiness varies. Single showers are possible in Kainuu.

The weather is warm for the time of year. The temperature is between 11 and 18 degrees.

Yle's meteorologist Matti Huutonen says that warm weather continues throughout this week. The end of September is clearly warmer than usual.

Autumn has progressed several weeks more slowly than usual this year.
