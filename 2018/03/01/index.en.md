# Thursday 1.3.2018 (radio)

Tapio Luoma will be the new archbishop. The extension of the western metro will be late. A suicide prevention centre opened in Helsinki. The bears at Korkeasaari woke up from their winter sleep. There has been harsh winter weather in different parts of Europe.

## A new archbishop

![Tapio Luoma](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10066008)

Open image viewer

Tapio Luoma. Photo: Yle

The Lutheran Church of Finland is getting a new archbishop. Tapio Luoma will start work as archbishop in June. Then the current archbishop Kari Mäkinen will retire.

The archbishop leads the Lutheran Church of Finland. The church chose Luoma for the job of archbishop on Thursday.

Tapio Luoma is currently the Bishop of Espoo. The Lutheran Church has 9 bishops in Finland, but only one archbishop.

Tapio Luoma is 55 years old. He has a doctorate in theology. Luoma believes that Christians and Muslims can live in peace in Finland.

## The western metro

![Text about track damage on the timetable screen at Matinkylä metro station in Espoo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10086825)

Open image viewer

Matinkylä metro station in Espoo. Photo: Panu Tuunala / Lehtikuva

The extension of the western metro will be late. The new metro line is being built in Espoo between Matinkylä and Kivenlahti. The line will be 7 kilometres long.

The new metro line may not be ready until 2023. That is 4 years later than planned.

The new metro line will also be more expensive than has been believed until now. The metro line will cost almost 1.2 billion, or 1,200 million euros. Earlier it was estimated that the price would be 800 million euros.

## A suicide prevention centre

![a man on a road at night as a car approaches](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9520553)

Open image viewer

A person can get help if they have thoughts of suicide. Photo: Yle

A suicide prevention centre opened in Helsinki on Thursday. The centre gives free help to everyone. A doctor's referral is not needed.

The centre helps people who have thoughts of suicide or who have tried to kill themselves. The centre also gives help to these people's family members.

Almost 800 people killed themselves in Finland in 2016.

## The bears woke up

![In this picture they are already returning from their winter sleep](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10097121)

Open image viewer

The Korkeasaari bears came out of their winter den on Thursday. Photo: Mari Lehmonen / Korkeasaari

Spring has begun. The bears have woken up from their winter sleep at Korkeasaari Zoo in Helsinki.

The bears came out of their winter den on 1 March. They ran in the snow and slid down the hill. They also ate for the first time after the winter. The bears got, for example, apples and carrots.

The bears' winter ends when there is enough light outside. Then they wake up from their winter sleep. The hard frost does not bother the bears.

## Winter weather in Europe

![Snowfall has made traffic difficult in London.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10097476)

Open image viewer

Snowfall in London. Photo: Jeff Moore / AOP

There has been harsh winter weather in different parts of Europe.

A lot of snow has fallen in many countries. The snowfall has disrupted traffic from Sweden all the way to southern France.

For example, in Britain's capital London, hundreds of train services and flights have been cancelled because of the snowfall.
