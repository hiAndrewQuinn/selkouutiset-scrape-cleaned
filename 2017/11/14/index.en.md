# Tuesday 14.11.2017 (radio)

Four municipalities are setting up a hospital company with the private company Mehiläinen. Quite a lot of problems were found in concrete construction. The USA's new ambassador is Robert Frank Pence. Teemu Selänne got into the ice hockey Hall of Fame. You can grow mushrooms at home.

## A joint hospital for the municipalities and Mehiläinen

![The entrance to the Länsi-Pohja Central Hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9575362)

Four municipalities in Northern Finland will start treating sick people together with a private company called Mehiläinen.

Kemi, Tornio, Keminmaa and Simo decided on Monday evening that they will set up a joint company with Mehiläinen. The new company will start running the region's central hospital and other social and health services too. The agreement will be made for 15 years.

Municipalities in Finland have never before bought this much social and health services from a private company.

## Problems in concrete construction

![The construction site of the TYKS T3 hospital in November 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9922172)

Open image viewer

A hospital construction site in Turku.  Photo: Jouni Koutonen / Yle

Researchers found quite a lot of problems in Finnish concrete construction.

The construction industry ordered the study after poor concrete was found in different parts of Finland last year. For example, in Turku the building work on a hospital was delayed by almost a year, because the concrete was not good enough.

The study says that there are problems in all stages of concrete construction. At least the client, the concrete maker, the site designer and the builder must do better. There are the most problems with special concrete, which must withstand, for example, frost or salt.

## A new US ambassador to Finland

![US President Donald Trump.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9595955)

Open image viewer

President Donald Trump. Photo: Jim Lo Scalzo / EPA

The US president has chosen a new ambassador to Finland. He is businessman Robert Frank Pence.

Pence has studied law, and he has a construction company in Virginia. The company does, for example, renovations and alterations to office buildings, shopping centres and hotels.

Pence often gives money to presidential candidates in elections. He gave Donald Trump about 400,000 dollars in last year's election.

The new ambassador apparently has no old connections to Finland.

## Teemu Selänne becomes a Hall of Fame star

![Teemu Selänne being questioned by journalists](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9928137)

Open image viewer

Teemu Selänne.  Photo: AOP

Ice hockey player Teemu Selänne has been chosen for the Hall of Fame in America. Before Selänne, there has been only one Finn in the Hall of Fame, Jari Kurri.

Only the best ice hockey players are chosen for the Hall of Fame. Most of them are from Canada and the USA; 15 ice hockey players are from Europe.

## Mushrooms grow at home too

![Cultivated oyster mushrooms. Mushroom growing in containers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9919829)

Open image viewer

Oyster mushroom. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Growing mushrooms at home is a new hobby. For example, a mushroom called the oyster mushroom can be grown at home if you first buy a starter kit.

Mushrooms are good and healthy food. Many people like to grow them because much less is known about mushrooms than about plants and animals. It is exciting to find out new things.
