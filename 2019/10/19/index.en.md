# Saturday 19.10.2019 (radio)

In South America, in Chile, there have been violent demonstrations. Sales of e-cigarettes have decreased. The weekend is rainy.

## Brexit

![Brexit session in the Parliament building in London on 19 October. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11027993)

Open image viewer

The British Parliament continues dealing with Brexit. Photo: Uk Parliament / Jessica Taylor

The British Brexit mess continues. All of Europe was waiting for the British Parliament to decide on Brexit on Saturday. Brexit means Britain's leaving of the European Union.

However, Parliament once again decided to postpone the Brexit decision. The majority of Parliament wants the prime minister to ask the EU for more time to arrange the departure.

However, Prime Minister Johnson does not want to ask the EU for more time. He wants Britain to leave the European Union soon, already at the end of October.

Brexit has been a long and difficult process. The British voted for Brexit already 3 years ago, but politicians have not managed to finish the matter to the end.

## Protests in Chile

![A bus set on fire by demonstrators.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11027867)

Open image viewer

People became angry in the capital of Chile, Santiago, when the prices of metro tickets went up. Photo: Claudio Reyes / AFP / AOP

In South America, in Chile, there have been violent demonstrations. People have gone out into the streets in the capital of Chile, Santiago.

During the demonstrations, people have, for example, started fires. The police have used, for example, tear gas and water cannons.

The demonstrations started when the price of a metro ticket went up. Santiago has 6 million residents, and many of them are poor. In the city dwellers' view, life in Santiago is too expensive.

## Sales of e-cigarettes

![A man smoking an e-cigarette.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11022267)

Open image viewer

A man is smoking an e-cigarette. Photo: AOP

Sales of e-cigarettes have decreased.

In Finland there are many new e-cigarette shops. They say that they now have fewer customers than some time ago.

Many people do not dare to buy e-cigarette products, because in the USA some people have become ill or even died because of e-cigarettes. It is not yet certain what the cause of the illnesses is. One reason may be that people have used cannabis in their e-cigarettes.

People who smoke e-cigarettes breathe in vapour that contains nicotine. E-cigarettes do not produce smoke. That is why e-cigarettes have been advertised as being less dangerous than regular cigarettes.

However, the World Health Organization, WHO, has warned about e-cigarettes. WHO says that e-cigarettes are harmful to health.

## Weekend rain

The weekend has started with rainy weather. In Lapland it has snowed, elsewhere in Finland it has rained.

Sunday starts with fair weather, that is, the rain stops. In the south, however, new rain will come already on Sunday afternoon.

In southern Finland it is 10 degrees warm on Sunday. In Lapland there is a little frost.
