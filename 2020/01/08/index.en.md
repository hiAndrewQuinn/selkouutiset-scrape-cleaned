# Wednesday 8.1.2020 (radio)

Iran has made a missile strike on US bases in Iraq. Many airlines are avoiding Iran and Iraq. A Ukrainian passenger plane crashed in Iran. Prime Minister Marin visited Sweden. The first joint application round for universities has started. Sea rescuers had a lot of work last year.

## Iran's missile strike

![Map of Iran and Iraq](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11147596)

Open image viewer

Photo: Lasse Isokangas / Yle

Iran has made a missile strike on Iraq.

During the night Iran fired missiles at the Iraqi army bases at Ain al-Asad and in Erbil. American soldiers and other foreign forces are stationed at these bases.

There are also Finnish peacekeepers in Erbil. The Ministry of Defence says that the Finns are all right.

The Finns are in Erbil training Iraqis to fight against the terrorist organisation Isis.

![A passenger plane landing](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_628/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-41715059784e6209837/1529306682)

Open image viewer

Many airlines are now avoiding the airspace of Iran and Iraq. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Several international airlines have changed their flight routes because of Iran's missile strike. The airlines do not want to fly in the airspace of Iran or Iraq.

Finnair has also changed its route to Dubai. The old route went through Iran's airspace. Now Finnair flies through Saudi Arabian airspace for at least this week.

## Iran plane crash

![Parts of an aircraft at the crash site near Tehran airport in Iran on Wednesday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11147251)

Open image viewer

Parts of the aircraft at the crash site near Tehran airport. Photo: Rohhollah Vadati / AFP

A serious plane crash has happened in Iran.

A passenger plane of a Ukrainian airline fell to the ground near Iran's capital, Tehran. The plane was supposed to fly from Tehran to Kyiv.

There were about 180 people on the plane. They all died. Among the dead are, for example, citizens of Iran, Canada and Sweden.

The cause of the crash is not yet known.

Finland's Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Sweden's Prime Minister Stefan Löfven.

Finland's Prime Minister Sanna Marin has visited Sweden today.

Marin and Sweden's Prime Minister Stefan Löfven have talked about the plane crash in Iran and the tense situation between Iran and the United States.

On Friday Marin travels to Tallinn, where she will meet the Prime Minister of Estonia.

## Joint university application

![Chalkboard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5839979)

Open image viewer

The joint application for foreign-language degree programmes has started. Photo: Yle / Martti Juntunen

The joint application for universities started today.

Now the first joint application of the spring has started, in which people apply for degree programmes taught in foreign languages and for the University of the Arts.

The application has 4,700 starting places in total, in over 250 different programmes. The joint application lasts 2 weeks.

The second joint application for universities starts in mid-March. Then people apply for programmes taught in Finnish and Swedish.

## Sea rescue

![Volunteer sea rescue, Meri-Lapin meripelastajat](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10885031)

Open image viewer

Sea rescuers from Meri-Lapin. Photo: Arto Mattila

Volunteer sea rescuers had about 1,800 rescue and assistance missions last year. They set out on urgent rescue missions over 500 times.

In total, almost 3,800 people and over 1,400 vessels received help.

In most cases a pleasure boat got into trouble because the vessel had a technical fault or its maintenance had been neglected.
