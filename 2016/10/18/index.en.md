# Tuesday 18.10.2016 (radio)

The president of Lithuania says that Russia is aggressively showing its strength. More family day carers are needed. Schoolchildren have their autumn holiday. Museums are changing. Outi Ansamaa and Sini Sivonen, employees of the City of Oulu, received a prize for clear language.

## The president of Lithuania in Finland

Dalia Grybauskaitė. Photo: Kay Nietfeld / EPA

The president of Lithuania, Dalia Grybauskaitė, says that Russia is showing its strength when it took Iskander missiles to Kaliningrad.

The president of Lithuania is on a visit to Finland. She said in Helsinki that Iskander missiles are offensive weapons. President Grybauskaité said that Russia is openly and aggressively showing its strength to the Baltic countries and to the capitals of Europe.

Kaliningrad is a Russian military base, and it is located on the coast of the Baltic Sea, between Lithuania and Poland.

## Too few family day carers

![Children eat sausage on a forest trip](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9231484)

Open image viewer

Children in day care on a trip in the forest.  Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Finland needs more family day carers for children.

A family day carer looks after children in her own home while their parents are at work. A family day carer can have at most four children in her care. Many parents hope that a small child can get into family day care and does not have to go to a big day care centre.

However, there are too few family day carers, because a large part of them have retired. The work pays a small salary, and that is why it does not interest young people.

There are now fewer than 9,000 family day carers. For example, in the year '90 there were 30,000 of them.

## Museums are changing

![Time machine](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9234097)

Open image viewer

At the Helsinki City Museum you can try virtual reality. Photo: Maija Astikainen

Museums tell about history, but many museums want to do it in a new way. Otherwise museums will no longer interest people.

Usually a museum has old objects, photographs and texts. New kinds of museums have, for example, holograms, moving images and virtual reality. The Helsinki City Museum was renewed last spring, and it is really popular. You can also go there, for example, for coffee, to meet other people or to work.

The National Museum of Finland is also being renewed.

## Clear speakers of the year

![director hands negotiation agreement man woman paper meeting decision](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6615465)

Open image viewer

Some decisions of the City of Oulu are now easier to understand. Photo: Yle

Outi Ansamaa and Sini Sivonen, employees of the City of Oulu, have received the Clear Speaker of the Year prize.

Ansamaa and Sivonen changed the language of the city's decisions to be easier. The language of the decisions is now clearer, so they are easier to understand.

Ansamaa and Sivonen changed the decision texts with the help of the city's residents. They asked people for feedback and changed the texts the way people wanted.
