# Wednesday 28.10.2020 (radio)

The government discussed data breaches. Finnair made a loss. The coronavirus disease COVID-19 is being researched in Finland. Today's coronavirus figures. The Nordic Council honoured Finns.

## The government considered the data breach

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin arrived at the government's evening school at the Säätytalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11618106)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The government has been considering the data breach at the psychotherapy centre Vastaamo. The government has discussed, for example, how to help people whose patient data was stolen from Vastaamo.

The thief took the patient data of perhaps tens of thousands of people from Vastaamo. The thief has published the data on the internet. He demands money so that he stops publishing the patient data.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the victims of the data breach need support and help. Marin says that the government has considered, for example, whether victims could quickly get a new personal identity code.

## Finnair made a loss

![Finnair aircraft in May at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11491091)

Open image viewer

Finnair aircraft at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The airline Finnair has made big losses.

Finnair's result for July to September was almost 170 million euros in loss. Finnair's turnover fell by 89 percent, and the number of passengers fell by the same amount.

At the same time a year ago, Finnair made about 100 million euros in profit.

Finnair says that the poor result is due to the coronavirus pandemic and Finland's strict travel restrictions. Finnair says that its planes are flying half empty and many routes have been dropped altogether.

## Research on the coronavirus disease

![Test tubes at the drive-in coronavirus testing station at Messukeskus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11615208)

Open image viewer

Test tubes at a coronavirus testing station in Helsinki. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Research on the effects of the coronavirus disease is starting in Finland.

The Hospital District of Helsinki and Uusimaa is studying what effects the disease has on people in the long term.

The study is trying to include 300 patients who have had COVID-19. They will be followed for a year.

Based on earlier information, it has been assumed that the coronavirus disease may leave damage to the lungs and weaken a person's quality of life for a long time.

## Finnish artists were honoured

![Monika Fagerholm](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11617008)

Open image viewer

Author Monika Fagerholm. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The Nordic Council has given out cultural awards. Finnish artists won three awards.

The literature prize was won by Monika Fagerholm. She was awarded for the novel Vem dödade bambi?, i.e. Who Killed Bambi?

The music prize went to composer Sampo Haapamäki.

The children's and young people's literature prize went to the Finnish illustrator Jenny Lucander and the Swedish author Jens Matsson.
