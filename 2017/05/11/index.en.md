# Thursday 11.5.2017 (radio)

The National Coalition Party is still the most popular party. Ice is decreasing at the North Pole. Finland becomes chair of the Arctic Council. An uproar in the United States over the firing of the FBI director. Russians are travelling to Finland again.

## National Coalition Party still most popular

![Graphic of party support.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9605532)

Open image viewer

Yle's party support poll was carried out in April among a thousand Finns.  Photo: Yle News Graphics

The National Coalition Party is still Finland's most popular party, a new Yle poll shows.

The second most popular parties are now the SDP and the Centre Party. The Greens have gained the most support.

Support for the Finns Party has also grown. But support for the Left Alliance has fallen.

The National Coalition Party was also the most popular party in the municipal elections, which were held in April.

## Northern polar ice in danger

![As the climate warms, permafrost areas have started to melt.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7802422)

Open image viewer

Permafrost has started to melt in Greenland because the climate is warming. Photo: Nick Cobbing / EPA

Ice is decreasing at the North Pole. This winter there was less ice at the North Pole than ever before, says the US snow and ice centre.

The climate at the North Pole is warming as much as twice as fast as in the rest of the world.

Fishermen have noticed that new fish species have come to the Arctic Ocean from the south, for example mackerel.

90 researchers of the Arctic Council say that the ice at the North Pole may melt almost completely in summer as early as 2030.

## Finland started as chair of the Arctic Council

![the Arctic Council.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9344411)

Open image viewer

In the Arctic Council, countries discuss cooperation in the Arctic Ocean region. Photo: Yle

Finland has today started as chair of the Arctic Council.

The members of the Arctic Council are the northern countries of the world, that is, the Nordic countries, Canada, the United States and Russia.

In the Arctic Council the countries discuss, among other things, protecting the Arctic Ocean and its environment. They also discuss economic cooperation.

## Uproar in the United States over the firing of the BFBI director

![James Comey ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9604756)

Open image viewer

Preisdent Donald Trump fired FBI director James Comey. Comey led the investigation into the Trump administration's possible links to Russia. Photo: Shawn Thew / EPA

A big uproar has started in the United States because President Donald Trump fired the director of the federal police, the FBI.

The FBI has been trying to find out whether there are people in President Donald Trump's administration who have forbidden contacts with Russia. But Trump says that this was not the reason for firing FBI director James Comey.

The US Senate is also investigating whether Russia interfered in the US presidential election, which Trump won last November. The Senate has questioned, for example, Trump's former aide Michael Flynn.

## Russian tourists are coming to Finland again

![Finnish consulate in St Petersburg, an official sticks a visa into a passport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7348351)

Open image viewer

Visa applications from Russian travellers have overloaded Finland's consulate in St Petersburg. Photo: YLE

The number of Russian tourists in Finland is growing this summer. In St Petersburg, as many as over 50,000 Russians are waiting for a visa to Finland.

Russians apply for a visa at the Finnish consulate. The consulate has now received so many visa applications that people may have to wait as long as a month for a visa.

There is a danger that Russians will cancel their trip to Finland if they do not get a visa in time.

Many Russians want to travel to Finland in the summer, because travelling to, for example, Mediterranean countries is now too expensive.
