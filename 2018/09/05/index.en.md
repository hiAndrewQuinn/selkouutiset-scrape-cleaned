# Wednesday 5.9.2018 (radio)

Home care allowance prevents immigrant women from finding work. Alcohol consumption is decreasing. Economic growth is slowing. Finland wants a credit register. Many people move too little. There are errors in passports. A severe typhoon caused damage in Japan.

## Home care allowance can prevent employment

![Door of a TE Office service point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10071822)

Open image viewer

Employment has risen. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

The home care allowance prevents immigrant women from finding work. According to a study, immigrant women prefer to stay at home, because they are paid the child home care allowance.

More jobs have been created in Finland. Many Finns have got jobs. The employment of immigrants has not risen.

Finns' alcohol consumption has decreased. Alcohol consumption has decreased by 20% in ten years. A Finn usually drinks 1-4 beers at a time.

Researchers are worried that some people drink a lot of alcohol.

Economic growth slows a little

Economic growth has slowed across the whole world. The slowdown also affects Finland.

Finland wants a credit register

![Banknotes in a wallet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9755384)

Open image viewer

The purpose of the credit register is that people do not build up too much debt. Photo: AOP

Finland wants to set up a credit register. From the credit register, banks or quick-loan firms get information on a person's debts and income. A bank or quick-loan firm can notice that a new loan cannot be given if the person has a lot of debt.

The purpose of the credit register is that people do not build up too much debt.

Exercise is healthy

Many people move too little. Exercise is healthy. If you do not move enough, you can fall ill with heart disease, diabetes or cancer.

Researchers say that people nowadays sit a lot and move little.

Passports have errors

Open image viewer

There are passports in Finland that have an error. On the passport's watermark, the swan picture is upside down. If there is an error in a passport, you can apply for a new passport from the police.

The police say that there are few faulty passports.

A severe typhoon causes damage in Japan

![A cargo ship that was thrown against a bridge cut off traffic to Kansai airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10387252)

Open image viewer

A typhoon has caused serious damage in Japan. Photo: AFP

In Japan a very strong storm, a typhoon, has caused a lot of damage.

The typhoon has knocked down lorries and torn roofs off houses. Many homes are without electricity in Japan.
More than 10 people have died and almost 500 people have been injured.
