# Radio Clear News Saturday 15.7.2023

Speaker of Parliament. RKP. Bears. Bird flu. The weather.

## Speaker of Parliament Jussi Halla-aho

![Jussi Halla-aho photographed on 10.7.2023 in front of Pikku Parlamentti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5137,h_2889,x_0,y_301/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114089064ad25164eb87/1689068867)

Jussi Halla-aho photographed on 10.7.2023 in front of Pikku Parlamentti. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

Speaker of Parliament Jussi Halla-aho will not interrupt Parliament's summer holiday. Halla-aho justifies the decision, among other things, by saying that interrupting Parliament's summer holiday is very rare. In Halla-aho's view, there is no great hurry for Parliament to meet.

The opposition parties earlier demanded that Parliament must end its summer holiday early. The opposition wants Parliament to soon deal with the government's ability to function and the ministers' old, racist online writings.

RKP demands that all parties and the government's ministers condemn racism.

## Bird flu

![The eaten carcass of a black-headed gull that died of bird flu, on the breakwater of the Lahti harbour in July 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_245/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114077664ad01b7769f8/1689920018)

Open image viewer

Black-headed gull that died of bird flu on the breakwater of the Lahti harbour in July 2023. Photo: Markku Tuhkanen / Yle

Bird flu, a viral disease of birds, is killing gulls in Finland. Many birds have died of bird flu during the summer, for example in Salo, Lahti and Hauho in Hämeenlinna.

The Finnish Food Authority says that there is now a lot of bird flu in wild birds. Other animals also have more bird flu than before. Bird flu is found, for example, in animals on fur farms.

The authority says that bird flu can spread to humans. However, there is currently no great danger of this in Finland.

## Bears

![Brown bear walks on a green meadow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4525,h_2545,x_1,y_336/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114311664b26af865c2d/1689414540)

Open image viewer

Bear photographed in Eastern Finland on 4.7.2023. Photo: Olivier Morin / Lehtikuva

There are many more bears in Finland than about 20 years ago. That is why humans and bears meet in nature more often than before.

The Natural Resources Institute Finland says that in the 2000s there have been more encounters between humans and bears than before. Still, people see a bear in Finland only rarely. A bear has not killed a single person in Finland in the 2000s.

There may be a little under 2,000 bears in Finland.

## The weather

![Berry pickers in a field. A summer dry day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5389,h_3031,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1135154649ad5061c9b1/1687869675)

On Sunday the weather is dry all over the country. Cloudiness varies between clear and partly cloudy.

The night temperature is between 10 and 16 degrees. The daytime temperature varies between 18 and 27 degrees. It may be cooler in the rain.

The wind is mostly light or moderate.
