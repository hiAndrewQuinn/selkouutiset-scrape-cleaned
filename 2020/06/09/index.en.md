# Tuesday 9.6.2020 (radio)

Anu Vehviläinen is the new Speaker of Parliament. One more corona death has occurred. George Floyd's funeral was held in the USA. Many people of African background experience discrimination in Finland. A robot bus is being tested in Helsinki.

## New Speaker of Parliament

![The picture shows Member of Parliament Anu Vehviläinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11392511)

Anu Vehviläinen is the new Speaker of Parliament. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Parliament has elected a new Speaker. The new Speaker is Anu Vehviläinen.

The Speaker leads the work of Parliament, that is, the Parliament of Finland. The Speaker is the second most important person in Finland, right after the President.

Parliament elected a new Speaker because the previous Speaker, Matti Vanhanen, moved to a new job. Vanhanen is now Finland's finance minister.

The new Speaker, Anu Vehviläinen, is a 56-year-old politician. She has been a minister, for example. Vehviläinen's party is the Centre Party.

## Floyd's funeral in Texas

![In the picture ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11391270)

Open image viewer

George Floyd's coffin is carried into a church. Photo: Mario Tama / AFP

George Floyd's funeral has been held in the USA. George Floyd was buried in the city of Houston, Texas.

Before the funeral, a memorial service was also held. Thousands of people took part in the event.

The Black man Floyd died about 2 weeks ago because of police violence.

Floyd's death has shocked many Americans. They say that the police treat Black people worse than white people.

After Floyd's death there have been big demonstrations against racism in the USA.

## Discrimination against Black people in Finland

![Ufoka Eugene at a demonstration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2472,h_1391,x_0,y_127/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6862245eda7c494de8a/1591377029)

Open image viewer

Ufoga Eugene is fighting against racism in Finland. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Racism exists in Finland too. This is what a report by the Non-Discrimination Ombudsman says. The task of the Non-Discrimination Ombudsman is to intervene in discrimination.

The report says that 80 per cent of residents of African background in Finland have experienced discrimination. They have been treated badly because of the colour of their skin.

In Finland, for example, people shout at Black people in the city, in shops and on buses.

Racism also exists in schools and in working life. A person may even be left without a job because of the colour of their skin.

## Robot bus tests in Helsinki

![Robot bus Gacha in Pasila.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11390504)

Open image viewer

A driverless robot bus now runs in Pasila. Photo: Forum Virium Helsinki

A robot bus is being tested in Helsinki. A driver is not needed in the robot bus. A backup driver is on board for safety reasons.

The robot bus runs in the area of Pasila, the railway station and the exhibition centre. The robot bus also takes passengers. The ride is free.

The robot bus runs in the morning and in the afternoon. The robot bus runs in Pasila until 3 July.
