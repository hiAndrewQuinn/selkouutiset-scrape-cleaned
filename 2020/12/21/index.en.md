# Monday 21.12.2020 (radio)

## Flights from Britain to Finland were suspended

![Finnair aircraft waiting at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11708202)

Open image viewer

Finnair also has to cancel flights from Britain to Finland for the time being. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Air traffic from Britain to Finland ended today, Monday. The ban is in force for 2 weeks.

The reason is that there is a new coronavirus variant in Britain.

At the moment not even Finns can fly home from Britain. A separate repatriation flight for Finns, for example, is being considered.

On Sunday evening, passengers from London to Helsinki were sent for coronavirus tests. There were about 170 passengers in total. In Helsinki, the passengers were also given instructions for a stricter voluntary quarantine.

Any positive samples will be examined separately, and it will be established whether it is a mutated strain of the virus.

Many other countries have also decided to suspend passenger air traffic from Britain.

## Vaccinations may start in Finland after Christmas

![The picture shows the Pfizer Covid-19 vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11708690)

Open image viewer

The picture shows the Pfizer Covid-19 vaccine. Photo: Ariana Drehsler / AFP

The European Medicines Agency says that the Pfizer and Biontech coronavirus vaccine may be put into use. It is the first coronavirus vaccine to receive a permit for use in the European Union.

Earlier, the vaccine has received a permit for use in Britain and the USA, among other places. Vaccinations have already started there. The vaccine also has a permit for use in Mexico, Canada and Saudi Arabia, among other places.

Before vaccinations can start, the vaccine still needs approval from the European Commission. The Commission will decide on the matter soon.

If the approval process goes ahead, vaccinations may start in Finland after Christmas, that is, on 27 December.

At first, only a few vaccines will be available.

## Road deaths have decreased a little

![A police photo from the scene of a traffic accident](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_543/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7557245fdc78c087123/1608284579)

Open image viewer

A police photo of a traffic accident. Photo: Police

233 people died in road accidents in Finland last year.

Almost half of the fatalities in cars and vans were not wearing a seatbelt. The accident investigation boards estimate that a seatbelt could have saved 21 of them.

Compared with 2018, there were 4 fewer accidents and 13 fewer fatalities.

Pedestrian road deaths were the lowest in the whole history of investigations.

## Some schools have already gone on Christmas holiday

![Christmas tree decorations and a candle on the branches of a Christmas tree.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11201075)

Open image viewer

Many schools are already on Christmas holiday. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

The autumn term of comprehensive schools has ended today in many municipalities. Pupils have gone on Christmas holiday in Hämeenlinna, Jyväskylä, Lahti, Oulu and Turku, among other places.

In the capital region, for example, the autumn term mostly ends on Wednesday. In Kuopio, Rovaniemi and Tampere, teaching continues until Christmas Eve.

The spring term of schools starts across the whole country after Epiphany, that is, after 6 January.
