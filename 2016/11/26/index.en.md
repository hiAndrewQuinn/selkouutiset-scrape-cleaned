# Saturday 26.11.2016 (radio)

Cuba's former president Fidel Castro has died. Finland's finance minister Petteri Orpo warns that the state may have to save more money. The old icebreaker Tarmo is being repaired. Porridge is healthy.

## Castro

![Fidel Castro in Havana in 2006.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9318134)

Open image viewer

Fidel Castro. Photo: Alejandro Ernesto / EPA

Cuba's former president Fidel Castro has died. Castro was 90 years old.

Castro was the leader of Cuba for about 50 years. Castro's time began with the Cuban revolution in 1959. Castro was the leader of the revolution, who became the leader of all of Cuba.

Castro continued in the job of president until 2008. Then he gave the president's duties to his own brother Raúl Castro.

Fidel Castro was a communist. Castro's supporters said that he freed Cuba. In the opinion of his opponents, there was no political freedom in Castro's Cuba, because Castro did not accept different opinions. Many of Castro's opponents fled to the USA, which is Cuba's neighbouring country.

Relations between Cuba and the USA were cut off during Castro's time. They began to improve only in 2014.

## State debt

![Petteri Orpo at a plenary session of Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9318497)

Minister Petteri Orpo. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Finland's finance minister Petteri Orpo warns that the state may have to save more money. Orpo says that more savings are needed next year if the economy does not grow enough. Otherwise the state debt will continue to grow.

Finland's prime minister Juha Sipilä does not believe that more savings are needed. In Sipilä's opinion, the savings that have been made so far are enough.

## Tarmo

![Tarmo in Kotka's Kantasatama harbour](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9316284)

Open image viewer

The icebreaker Tarmo. Photo: Juha Korhonen / Yle

The old icebreaker Tarmo is being repaired. The ship will be moved from Kotka to Helsinki for the renovation. Tarmo will be repaired at Suomenlinna.

The icebreaker Tarmo is now a museum ship. It was built in England in 1907.

100 years ago, Tarmo was the most efficient and powerful icebreaker. It could go through ice 80 centimetres thick. Tarmo broke ice until 1970.

Tarmo's renovation costs almost a million euros. The renovation will be finished next year.

## Porridge

![A plate of oat porridge with a pat of butter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6245877)

Open image viewer

Oat porridge is many Finns' breakfast. Photo: Arja Lento / Yle

It is said that Finns love porridge. Everyone has eaten porridge at some point, and porridge has been on Finns' dinner tables for hundreds of years.

Researcher Marjukka Kolehmainen says that porridge is also healthy. Porridge has a lot of vitamins.

Porridge also has a lot of fibre if it is made from wholegrain cereal. Fibre helps to prevent, for example, diabetes and heart diseases.

Cereal researcher Kolehmainen says that rye porridge is the king of porridges. Rye porridge is really healthy. Oats are also a good porridge grain, Kolehmainen says.
