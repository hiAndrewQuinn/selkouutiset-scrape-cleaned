# Saturday 5.8.2017 (radio)

Ilja Janitskin, editor-in-chief of MV-lehti, has been arrested. In parts of Europe the weather is really hot. In Finland it is pleasantly warm. The brains of people in conversation seek contact, researchers say.

## Ilja Janitskin

![Ilja Janitskin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9194906)

Open image viewer

MV-lehti's editor-in-chief Ilja Janitskin. Photo: Yle

Ilja Janitskin, editor-in-chief of the Finnish MV-lehti, has been arrested. The Finnish police got the information from the international police organisation Interpol on Friday.

Janitskin was arrested in Andorra, which is between France and Spain. Janitskin has been living in Spain.

The Finnish police suspect that Janitskin has committed many crimes. For example, Janitskin may have incited hatred against a group of people, that is, spoken badly about some group. Janitskin's MV-lehti is known in Finland for insulting immigrants.

The Finnish police want Janitskin to be brought to Finland for questioning.

## Hot Europe

![People cool off in a fountain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9759728)

Open image viewer

Italians are trying to cope with the heat. Photo: Marco Bertorello / AFP

In parts of Europe the weather is really hot. The temperature has risen above 40 degrees, for example in Italy and in the Balkan countries.

The authorities warn that such hot weather is dangerous for health. People have had to go to hospital, and at least in Poland and Romania people have died because of the hot weather.

The authorities say that people must drink a lot of water and at least elderly people should stay indoors.

Weather forecasts say that the hot weather in Europe will continue next week too.

## The brains of people in conversation

![A brain image.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9234025)

Open image viewer

Brain waves start to follow the same rhythm when 2 people talk. Photo: AOP

Finally, something about the brain. Do you know what happens in the brain during a conversation? Researchers have revealed new and interesting information about it.

Researchers say that brain waves start to follow the same rhythm when people talk with each other. It almost looks as if the brains of the people talking are seeking contact with each other.

The study was done at the Basque Center on Cognition, Brain and Language. In the study, 2 test subjects talked with each other. They had measuring devices on their heads that measured their brain activity. With these, the researchers noticed that the brain waves of the people talking were seeking a common rhythm.

Now the researchers are interested in whether this information could help, for example, people studying foreign languages. Next, the researchers plan to find out whether brain waves also start to follow the same rhythm in a conversation in a foreign language.
