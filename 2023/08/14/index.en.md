## Security of ministers

![Government Palace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_196/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10132406332d795e46c2/1675336390)

The Prime Minister's office is in the Government Palace. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

In Finland, security measures have been increased for all ministries and ministers. For example, visitors are required to identify themselves more carefully than before at ministries. Visitor access control has also been stepped up. For example, visitors cannot get into all rooms in the ministries.

The personal protection of ministers in public places has also been increased. This means that there is often a bodyguard near ministers.

Security measures for ministries and ministers have been increased, because not all people may like the decisions of the new government. For example, decisions on cuts or savings may anger some people.

The National Coalition Party is asking Alexander Stubb to be its presidential candidate. The chair of the National Coalition Party, Petteri Orpo, says that Stubb received majority support in the party executive.

Alexander Stubb has been the chair of the National Coalition Party. He has also been prime minister.

Officially, the decision to appoint Stubb as the National Coalition Party's presidential candidate is made by an extraordinary party congress, if Stubb agrees to be a presidential candidate.

## Inflation

![Food purchases on a supermarket checkout conveyor belt.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5356,h_3013,x_0,y_285/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-776201602fa20cab68f/1706108089)

Open image viewer

Price rises speeded up in July. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Inflation, that is, the rise in prices, continues. Consumer prices rose by 6.5 percent in July compared with last July. Price rises speeded up slightly in July. In June the price rise was 6.3 percent compared with last June, so in July prices rose more than in June.

Interest rises on mortgages and consumer credit, as well as the rise in electricity prices, for example, have sped up inflation.

Not all prices rose. For example, the prices of petrol and homes fell compared with last July.

## Sweden's security

![Rasmus Paludan holds a burning Quran in his hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4012,h_2255,x_0,y_260/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106392463d13059461ae/1674653822)

Burning the Quran in Sweden angers many Muslims. Photo: Patrik Österberg / AOP

Sweden is still a safe holiday destination. This is what Finland's Ministry for Foreign Affairs says. The Ministry for Foreign Affairs is reassuring Finns, because the warnings from Britain worry Finns.

British authorities said at the weekend that terrorists were probably aiming to attack in Sweden, because Qurans have been burned in Sweden.

The Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs points out that Sweden's Security Service has not raised the rating that measures the threat of terrorism. The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says that Sweden is still a safe holiday destination.
