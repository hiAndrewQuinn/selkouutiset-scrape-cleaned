UN climate conference. Passports. Crown Bridges tram. Post for Father Christmas. The weather

## Climate conference

First, climate news.

The UN international climate conference has started in Antalya, Turkey.

This time the main theme of the meeting may be the electrification of the world.

That means that the use of clean electricity will be increased, for example in energy production, industry and transport. Then climate emissions will also decrease.

## Passports

Next, news about passports.

The police warn that there may be queues when renewing passports next year.

Queues are expected because the passports of more than a million Finns expire in 2027.

The police say that it is worth renewing a passport already in winter, before the summer holiday season.

## Crown Bridges tram

Next we tell about tram traffic.

In Helsinki a new tram route was celebrated at the weekend. The route connects the Laajasalo district with the city centre.

The route runs along the new Crown Bridges. One of them is Kruunuvuori Bridge, which is the longest bridge in Finland. It is over a kilometre long.

The new route has been called perhaps the most beautiful tram route in the world.

## Post for Father Christmas

Next, the topic is Father Christmas's post.

Finland Post has moved Father Christmas's postbox to London. The postbox is at the Christmas market in Leicester Square. You can leave a letter and gift wishes for Father Christmas in it.

**Isabella** wished for a wooden ice cream shop toy from Santa this year. **Talia** wished for money. **Sam** wished for happiness and a Lego gift card.

Finland Post brought Father Christmas's postbox to London, because Santa gets a lot of letters from Britain every year.

Not everyone knows the right address, however. Many send post to the North Pole, even though Father Christmas lives at Korvatunturi.
