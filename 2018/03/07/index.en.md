# Wednesday 7.3.2018 (radio)

The European Union and the USA are arguing about customs duties. The police have uncovered a criminal group that has brought drugs into Finland. An Australian family has found the world's oldest message in a bottle. Plain-language author Satu Leisko has written a new plain-language book about immigrants.

## A dispute over customs duties

![US President Donald Trump.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10103361)

Open image viewer

President Trump is planning customs duties. Photo: Olivier Douliery / EPA

The European Union and the USA are arguing about economic matters. The subject of the dispute is customs duties. The European Union plans to introduce customs duties if the USA does so too.

The USA plans to put customs duties on, for example, steel. Customs duties raise the price of European steel in the USA. President Donald Trump hopes that American factories will then use more American steel.

If the USA puts customs duties on European steel, the EU plans to put customs duties on some American goods. After that, their price will go up in Europe.

Firms suffer from customs duties, because they reduce trade between countries.

## Drug smuggling

![Drugs in a package held by the police](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10105375)

Open image viewer

The police found drugs in the Tampere area. Photo: Police

The police have uncovered a criminal group that has brought drugs into Finland. The group operated in the Tampere area.

The group brought a lot of dangerous drugs into Finland. It brought amphetamine, ecstasy and cocaine into Finland.

Finnish criminals transported the drugs together with Dutch criminals.

The Finnish police have got help from abroad while investigating the case.

## An old message in a bottle

![A 132-year-old bottle](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10105217)

Open image viewer

This bottle and message were thrown into the sea 132 years ago. Photo: Kym Illman / AFP

Then some news from far away in Australia.

An Australian family has found the world's oldest message in a bottle. The family found the old bottle on a sandy beach. Inside the bottle was a message that was written 132 years ago.

The message in a bottle was thrown into the sea from a German ship in 1886. The ship was studying sea routes.

The family has lent the message in a bottle to an Australian museum for 2 years. That way others also have a chance to see the rare old message.

## A new plain-language book

![Two women sit close together and smile](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10105177)

Open image viewer

On the left sits plain-language author Satu Leisko and on the right Olga Karatasiou. Photo: Minna Uusi-Eskola / YLE

Plain-language author Satu Leisko has written a new plain-language book about immigrants. The book is called "I Came to Finland". In the book, 9 immigrants tell how they came to Finland. They talk about their old home country and their new home in Finland.

The people interviewed in the book are from Russia, Turkey, Thailand, Greece, Hungary, Vietnam, Ivory Coast, Iraq and Jordan. In the book, the immigrants get to tell for themselves what they have experienced.

On the Clear News website you can watch an interview with Satu Leisko and Olga Karatasiou. Karatasiou is one of the people in the book. The interview can be found at yle.fi/selkouutiset.
