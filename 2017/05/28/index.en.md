# Sunday 28.5.2017 (radio)

Half a million employees get smaller holiday pay than before. Electric bikes are growing in popularity. Now is the time for the tick vaccination. Almost all migratory birds have come back. June begins in cold weather.

## Holiday pay got hundreds of euros smaller

![Ritva Hagström](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9635869)

Open image viewer

Ritva Hagström. Photo: Yle

Municipal and state employees have less money than usual this summer.

In the winter an agreement was made in Finland that reduced the holiday bonuses of municipal and state employees. That is why half a million employees will get less holiday pay for three years. For example, a cleaner's holiday bonus goes down by about 400 euros and a nurse's by about 700 euros.

The holiday bonus of employees in companies does not go down. Cook Ritva Hagström from Porvoo says that it is unfair when only some employees' holiday bonus goes down.

## People are buying many electric bikes

![Electric-assisted bikes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9635749)

Open image viewer

The battery of an electric bike is often attached to the frame. Photo: Yle

Finns have bought many electric bikes this spring. There are over 100 percent more electric bikes on the roads than last summer.

An electric bike has a battery and a small motor that helps, for example, going uphill. Pedalling an electric bike is lighter than an ordinary bike. You can go faster with it, and you can manage to ride long distances. Many people ride an electric bike to work.

For example, in the other Nordic countries, Germany and Holland there are already many more electric bikes than in Finland.

## Now is the time for the tick vaccination

![A young girl gets a TBE vaccination. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9635772)

Open image viewer

Nurse Kaisa Vaviolahti vaccinates Salla Keihäs. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

If you want to get a tick vaccination for the summer, you must get it soon.

The vaccination prevents encephalitis, which some ticks spread. The vaccination does not prevent another disease, Lyme disease, which some ticks spread. The vaccination also does not keep ticks off your skin. Only a small share of ticks spread diseases. Tick-borne diseases are most common in Åland, Parainen and Simo.

If you want to keep ticks off your skin, you must wear long trousers and a long-sleeved shirt when you move in the forest and on islands.

## Migratory birds have come back

![Nightingale](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9561778)

Open image viewer

Not all nightingales have come to Finland from the south yet. Photo: AOP

Most migratory birds have come to Finland, says the association BirdLife Finland. The flight of Arctic birds over Finland to the north is also almost over.

The migratory birds have come later than usual, because the weather has been so cold. But now it has been warmer for a couple of weeks, and the birds' migration is roughly on its usual schedule. But there are still few of some bird species in Finland, for example nightingales.

## The beginning of June is cold

![Dew drops left by the night shine in the morning sun ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6222239)

Open image viewer

Dew drops on the lawn. Photo: Sirkka Franttila

June begins in very cold weather.

Next week is also rainy. The air is as much as almost 10 degrees colder than usual. On Wednesday, the first day of June, it is about 10 degrees in southern Finland and 5 in the north. Temperatures like these are usual in April.
