## Bottled water

![A woman drinks from a water bottle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10933402)

Open image viewer

Photo: Pavel Rodimov / AOP

Sales of bottled water are growing around the world.

It is estimated that in 2030 twice as much bottled water will be sold around the world as now.

Bottled water also brings problems. For example, it increases the amount of waste, because a bottle deposit system does not exist in all countries.

Finns are not obliged to buy bottled water, because Finnish tap water is clean and good.

However, there are about 2 billion people in the world who do not have good tap water. This is what the UN, that is, the United Nations, estimates.

## Iran's police

![Women on the street with their hair covered.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690356)

Open image viewer

In Iran the hijab is compulsory for women. Photo: Fatemeh Bahrami / AOP

Iran's police will start to monitor women's clothing even more closely. In Iran women are forced to wear the hijab, that is, a headscarf, in public places.

Iran's police intend to use smart technology when they monitor women. Cameras will come to public places. With the help of the cameras the police will notice, for example, if there is a woman without a hijab in a car.

Many Iranian women break the dress rules. Last year there were big demonstrations in Iran, after Mahsa Amini died in the hands of the police. The police arrested Amini because her hijab was not on her head properly.

## Mikael Agricola

![Drawing of Mikael Agricola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690357)

Open image viewer

Mikael Agricola lived in the 1500s. Photo: Otava

On Sunday, 9 April, Mikael Agricola Day has been celebrated. The day is also the Finnish language day.

Mikael Agricola lived about 500 years ago. He is said to be the father of the written Finnish language.

Agricola's ABC book was published in 1543. It was the first book in Finnish.

The ABC book teaches, for example, the alphabet and gives instructions for writing Finnish. In addition, the book teaches the basics of Christianity.

Nowadays Mikael Agricola Day is a flag day.

## The weather

The sunny weather continues on Easter Monday. The temperature on Monday is about 10 degrees across the whole country.

On Tuesday warm and humid air comes to the south. There may be clouds in the sky, but the temperature may rise to as much as 14 degrees.

In the north it is a little colder on Tuesday. The temperature is below 10 degrees, but Tuesday is sunny in the north.
