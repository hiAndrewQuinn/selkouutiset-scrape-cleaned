Russia has widened its bombing in western Ukraine.

Russia has fired, for example, missiles close to Lviv airport. The missiles hit a place where aircraft are repaired.

Many Ukrainians fleeing the war have gathered in Lviv. Many journalists from different parts of the world have also come to Lviv.

Russia has also shelled the port city of Mariupol more. About 80 percent of Mariupol's buildings have been damaged. Ukraine's food situation

## Ukraine's food situation

![Farm work is being done in Ukraine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_52/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-915204620b5b9e10242/1644911638)

Open image viewer

Ukraine is an important food producer. Photo: Maxym Marusenko / AOP

The food situation is getting worse in Ukraine. The World Food Programme says that food production chains will soon collapse in Ukraine.

The activity of farms has become difficult. Transport does not work. Industry cannot make food products. The shelves of many food shops are empty.

The war in Ukraine has pushed up the price of food around the world.

## Ukrainian refugees

![Ukrainian refugees arriving at the Polish border.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6192,h_3483,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-926884622b1cca5f940/1647005527)

Open image viewer

More than 3 million people have fled Ukraine because of the war. Photo: Darek Delmanowicz / EPA

More refugees come to Finland from Ukraine every day.

Almost 8,000 Ukrainians have already applied for international protection from Finland. The Finnish Immigration Service says that the number of protection applications has grown a lot this week.

The UN says that more than 3 million people have already fled Ukraine because of the war.

## Sales of paperbacks

![A Finnish bookshop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6015,h_3388,x_0,y_188/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-7811358d132450d8fe/1490105042)

Open image viewer

It is nice to browse books in a bookshop. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Sales of paperbacks have decreased. Last year sales of paperbacks fell by about 20 percent compared with the year before.

Bookshops say that sales of paperbacks have decreased perhaps because travel has decreased. Many people buy paperbacks for a trip. Audiobooks have also reduced paperback sales. People listen to a lot of books.

Paperback sales may increase this year, if people start travelling more again.

## A happy Finland

![People enjoying the beach in sunny weather.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_0,y_429/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-927924622f504bb5f1d/1647337210)

Open image viewer

For example, nature makes Finns happy. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finland is the happiest country in the world. This is what the World Happiness Report says.

Finland has been the happiest country in the world for 5 years in a row. The other Nordic countries, that is, Denmark, Iceland, Sweden and Norway, are also happy countries.

Around the world corona has reduced happiness. This has happened especially in countries where there are many corona infections.

The least happy countries are those with war and poverty.

The weather is getting warmer.

On Saturday the weather warms across the whole country. In the morning some rain moves across Lapland, but for the most part the day is dry.

In the south and central parts of the country it is sunny, and the temperature rises to +10 degrees.

On Sunday the weather warms further.
