# Tuesday 2.3.2021 (radio)

The government has held an information event for students. There has been congestion in booking coronavirus vaccination appointments. Private gyms may take a small number of customers at a time. Wednesday is a dry day in Southern Finland.

## The government's information for students

![Jussi Saramo, Sanna Marin and Annika Saarikko](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11817150)

The government has held an information event for students.

At the information event, Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Ministers Annika Saarikko and Jussi Saramo answered students' questions.

More than 600 students asked questions. The students were worried, for example, about remote teaching. You cannot learn to weld or give injections remotely, the young people said.

The government said that it has reserved more money for teaching. The aim is to increase teaching hours when in-person teaching is possible again.

Young people were also very interested in mental wellbeing and coping during the coronavirus time.

Prime Minister Marin thanked young people and teachers for good work in a difficult time.

## Congestion in booking coronavirus vaccinations

![Coronavirus vaccination point at the Tennari sports hall in Lohja.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5691,h_3201,x_0,y_525/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-775265602d348b0a695/1613575813)

There has been congestion in booking coronavirus vaccinations in different parts of Finland.

Yle asked municipalities how the booking of coronavirus vaccinations has worked.

Many municipalities say that especially the telephone service has become congested. Because of the congestion, municipalities have hired more workers and opened new telephone lines.

You can also book a coronavirus vaccination appointment online.

Problems have also been caused by the fact that in some municipalities relatives cannot book a vaccination time on behalf of an elderly person. In some municipalities it is possible.

## Coronavirus restrictions for gyms

![A gym worker wearing a face mask cleans a piece of equipment.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-779806603d0a32760d5/1614613259)

A gym may have at most 10 customers at a time. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

More detailed information was given on Tuesday about the coronavirus restrictions in the Helsinki region.

For example, private gyms and other sports facilities may take a small number of customers at a time. The premises may have at most 10 customers at the same time.

Otherwise the premises are closed this week and next week.

On Monday it was still uncertain whether the premises could take even a small number of customers.

Wednesday is a dry day in Southern Finland.

Elsewhere a little rain will fall. In the middle of the country a little snow and rain will fall, and in the north there will be snow showers.

In the south and central parts of the country the temperature is between +1 and +5 degrees, in the north between +1 and -5 degrees.

On Thursday the weather gets colder, and there is frost across the whole country.
