# Monday 7.6.2021 (radio)

Coronavirus vaccines will also start to be studied in children. The number of new coronavirus cases is decreasing. Finland won silver at the ice hockey world championships. Advance voting in the municipal elections ends on Tuesday. Showers are becoming more common.

## Children's coronavirus vaccinations

![Coronavirus vaccine study at the Meilahti vaccine research centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4555,h_2562,x_0,y_114/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81595860ba165f13c2f/1622808490)

Researchers are studying children's coronavirus vaccinations. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Coronavirus vaccines will also be studied in children. The study is being done in many countries.

The universities of Tampere and Helsinki are taking part from Finland. They are studying how well the coronavirus vaccine protects children under 12 years old.

The researchers also want to make sure that the coronavirus vaccine is safe for children. That is why they will follow the children closely after they have been vaccinated.

If the study results are good, Finland could consider vaccinating all children.

## Ice hockey world championship silver

![The loss was a hard pill to swallow for the Finnish national team players.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11968519)

Open image viewer

The Finnish players were disappointed when they lost the world championship final. Photo: imago sportfotodienst/All Over Press

The Finnish ice hockey team has returned home from the ice hockey world championships. The championships were held in Riga, the capital of Latvia.

Finland won silver at the ice hockey world championships. The world champion is Canada.

The final match was exciting. The game ended 2–2 after regular time.

In overtime, Canada scored the winning goal and won world gold. Finland won world silver.

Silver, or second place, was a disappointment for the Finnish team, which is the previous world champion.

Finnish ice hockey fans were also sad, because the win did not come this time.

However, ice hockey fans gathered at Helsinki Market Square on Sunday evening. Some fans even climbed the Havis Amanda statue, even though that is forbidden.

## Advance voting in the municipal elections

![A voter in a voting booth at the advance voting place in Varissuo, Turku, on 1 June 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_312/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81657160bc8549a0484/1622967694)

Open image viewer

Advance voting place in Varissuo, Turku. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

Advance voting in the municipal elections ends on Tuesday.

Already more than a million people have voted in advance, that is, before election day. Election day for the municipal elections is next Sunday, 13 June.

In municipal elections, the voter does not need to be a Finnish citizen. Usually it is enough that the voter has lived in Finland for 2 years.

More information about voting can be found at selkokeskus.fi.

You can also watch the easy-Finnish municipal election quiz, Selkotentti, on Yle Areena.
