## A bomb exploded in Manchester

![Police patrolling near the Manchester Arena after the explosion on Tuesday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9627553)

Open image viewer

Police patrol at the Manchester Arena after the explosion. Photo: Paul Ellis / AFP

A terrorist attack has been carried out in the city of Manchester in Britain.

A bomb exploded late on Monday evening near a concert hall in Manchester. Over 20 people died and about 60 were injured. Some of the victims are children and young people.

There were a lot of people in the concert hall, because there was a concert by the American pop singer Ariana Grande. The bomb exploded when people were coming out of the concert.

Manchester police say that the explosion was a suicide attack. The police say they know who the attacker was. But the name is not yet being given, because the investigation continues.

President Sauli Niinistö and Prime Minister Juha Sipilä have expressed their condolences to the families and loved ones of the victims of the bomb attack. The president also says that Finland condemns all terrorism.

According to the Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs, there are no Finns among the victims.

## The woman kidnapped in Afghanistan is still missing

![A view of Kabul on 20 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9625205)

Open image viewer

There are many soldiers on the streets of Kabul. Photo: Ahmadullah Ahmadi / AOP

The Finnish woman who was kidnapped in Afghanistan on Saturday is still missing.

There is no information about where the woman is. It is not yet known who did the kidnapping.

In Afghanistan people believe that the kidnappers belong to Kabul's criminal gangs. It is thought that the kidnappers want money before they agree to release the Finnish woman.

## 20 percent of elderly people never do things online

![A senior with a tablet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9240670)

Open image viewer

Many elderly people do not have a device suitable for using online services. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Many old people have never done any business online.

The Union for Senior Services, Valli, asked elderly people how they use online services. Over 20 percent of those who answered said they have never used online services in any way.

The union is worried about how elderly people get digital services. For example, many elderly people do not have a device suitable for using online services.

80 percent of those who answered said that when they have problems with online services, they get help from their own children or other relatives.
