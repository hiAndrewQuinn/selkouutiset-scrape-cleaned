President Sauli Niinistö has visited Russia. Niinistö talked on Friday with Russian President Vladimir Putin.

The presidents talked, for example, about economic matters. President Putin hoped that travel between Finland and Russia will soon start again. Travel between the countries has been cut off because of corona.

The presidents of Finland and Russia last met before corona, in summer 2019. Since then they have talked only on the phone.

Inflation has grown stronger in the euro area, that is, in countries that use the euro.

Inflation, that is, the rise in prices, has been faster in October than for years. The last time prices rose this fast was 13 years ago.

In October prices have been about 4 per cent higher than a year ago. The price of energy has risen the most. Energy is now over 20 per cent more expensive than a year ago.

## Influenza

![A woman in a beanie sneezes into a paper tissue, with influenza viruses flying around her.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11972903)

Open image viewer

Influenza can also spread again when the corona restrictions end. Photo: TeamDAF / AOP

The influenza season is approaching. This winter the influenza season may be stronger than usual.

Last winter corona restrictions also prevented influenza. Now influenza can spread again, when corona restrictions end and people travel more.

Symptoms of influenza include, for example, high fever, cough and runny nose. In influenza the symptoms are much stronger than in an ordinary cold. Influenza can be a dangerous disease, for example for elderly people.

The best way to prevent influenza is vaccination. Influenza vaccinations have already started.

![Director Juho Kuosmanen poses for a photojournalist.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_203/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-869225616fc1aaa06a0/1634737543)

Juho Kuosmanen has directed the film Compartment No. 6. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The film *Compartment No. 6* has come to cinemas in Finland. The film is Finnish, and its director is Juho Kuosmanen.

*Compartment No. 6* has already won awards abroad. For example, it received the Grand Prix at the Cannes film festival.

*Compartment No. 6* is about a train journey in Russia. During the journey a Finnish student girl and a Russian man get to know each other. The film is in Russian.

The story of the film is from Rosa Liksom's book.
