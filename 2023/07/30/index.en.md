## Workplace deaths

![Sign reading: Construction site. Unauthorised persons are not allowed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_125/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1097834643649c74648c/1681279708)

Most workplace deaths happened last year in the construction and transport sectors. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

The number of workplace deaths in Finland nearly doubled last year compared with the year before. A total of 24 people died in workplace accidents last year.

Most workplace deaths happen to people over 50 years old.

Most deaths were in the transport and construction sectors.

Anna-Maria Teperi, research professor at the Finnish Institute of Occupational Health, says that workplace safety has improved especially in aviation and industry. Anna-Maria Teperi thinks that next, workplace safety must be improved especially in the social and health care sector.

## First-home mortgages

![A real estate agent presents an apartment in a block of flats that is for sale.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105859363c00b670510d/1707319055)

Clearly fewer loans have been applied for from banks to buy a first home than a year ago. The largest mortgage banks, OP, Nordea and Danske Bank, say that the number of first-home mortgages has decreased in the first half of the year.

Home prices have fallen, but people hesitate to buy a home because the economic situation is uncertain. Loan interest rates have risen, and the costs of living and housing have become more expensive.

## Bird migration

![A man stands on a bridge over a narrow river.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3327,h_1871,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114098164ad41fcde98b/1689076376)

Open image viewer

Kari Mäntylä ringed an Arctic skua 36 years ago, which still nests on an island in Merikarvia this summer. Photo: Markku Sandell / Yle

More precise information is now available about birds' migration routes than before. Birds' movements can be followed throughout the year with satellite trackers and identification rings.

This summer an Arctic skua is nesting in Merikarvia, which is reportedly the oldest bird ringed in Finland. Pori bird enthusiast Kari Mäntylä ringed the skua's chick 36 years ago, in 1987.

Mäntylä says that the bird winters on the west coast of Africa, around the Cape Verde Islands and Madeira. In summer it faithfully returns to Merikarvia to nest.

## Northern lights

![A form of northern lights, a red arc with green haze in the sky over Ikaalinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4789,h_2695,x_84,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114956264c62f290f838/1690709894)

Open image viewer

A red arc and green haze can be seen in the sky. Photo: Heidi Rikala

The astronomical association Ursa has given awards to Finnish star enthusiasts.

Star enthusiasts found a new northern lights phenomenon. They noticed that during bright northern lights a red arc and turquoise-green, hazy patches can be seen in the sky. The arc and the patches are caused by the stream of particles from the Sun, that is, the solar wind.

The Ursa Stella Arcti award went to Heidi Rikala from Ikaalinen and Eero Karvinen from Nurmes. Emma Bruus from Nurmijärvi and Lauri Kangas from Espoo received a Stella Arcti mention.
