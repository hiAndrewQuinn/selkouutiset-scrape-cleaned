A group of Finnish and European researchers says that European countries must cooperate more against the coronavirus.

The researchers say that fighting the coronavirus has not succeeded in Europe, because countries do not have a common plan.

The researchers say that European countries must act together and tighten coronavirus restrictions.

## Power cuts

![Trees fallen onto a power line.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10426365)

Open image viewer

Trees fallen onto a power line. Photo: Jari Tanskanen/Yle

Power cuts have decreased in Finland. There have been fewer power cuts than before, even though storms have increased in the 2000s.

Power cuts have decreased because a lot of power cables have been dug underground. Just 10 years ago, 20 per cent of power cables were underground. Now 50 per cent of power cables are already underground.

Most power cuts are in Central Finland and Eastern Finland. In these areas there are many overhead lines, that is, power lines on poles in the middle of forests.

## Christmas rush in shops

![People shopping for food.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8551196)

Open image viewer

Christmas rush in the shop. Photo: Juha-Petri Koponen / Yle

More people than before are going to grocery shops this Christmas. Grocery shops are very busy, because Finns are at home a lot at Christmas. Because of the coronavirus situation, Finns are not going to visit relatives, for example.

Shops are trying to reduce the crowds. Many shops have extended their opening hours. Some shops are open 24 hours a day, that is, around the clock.

Online shops have only a few delivery slots for food left before Christmas.

Finns have sent more Christmas cards than last year. The Post says that clearly more Christmas cards have been sent than last year.

Last year, Finns sent about 15 million Christmas cards.

The Post estimates that many people want to cheer up friends with a Christmas card, because this coronavirus year has been hard and meeting friends has decreased.
