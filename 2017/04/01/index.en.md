# Saturday 1.4.2017 (radio)

The Governor of the Bank of Finland thinks that the bad time in the Finnish economy is over. The entrance exam for nursing education is changing. On April Fools' Day, many funny news stories that are not true have been told.

## The Finnish economy

Governor of the Bank of Finland Erkki Liikanen.  Photo: Yle

The bad time in the Finnish economy is over. This is what the Governor of the Bank of Finland, Erkki Liikanen, said in an interview with Yle on Saturday.

But Liikanen reminded that the Finnish economy is growing slowly. That is why Finland's competitiveness must still be improved. If Finland's competitiveness is poor, it is not worth making goods in Finland.

Competitiveness has already been improved with the competitiveness pact. It brings 3 more working days a year, without wages rising.

## The entrance exam for nursing education

![A nurse at work](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8753696)

Open image viewer

The care sector is not suitable for everyone. Photo: Yle News Graphics

The entrance exam for nursing education is changing. With a new kind of entrance exam, they want to find the right people for the field. Nowadays students get into the education who are not suited to the care sector. That is why almost 10 percent of care sector students drop out of their studies.

4 universities of applied sciences tried the new entrance exam last autumn. The entrance exam tested how well the applicant can learn new skills. The exam had, for example, tasks in mathematics and language skills. The entrance exam also tested whether the applicant has a correct picture of care work and whether they surely want to go into the care sector. In addition, the applicant's social skills were tested.

The universities of applied sciences say that the new entrance exam for nurses worked well. This spring the new kind of entrance exam also comes for public health nurses, paramedics and midwives. 2 new universities of applied sciences are also joining. The goal is that the new entrance exam for the care sector is in use in all of Finland by 2020 at the latest.

## April Fool!

![Logs from Keitele Group's Alajärvi sawmill in January 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9423148)

The first day of April is April Fools' Day. This year too, many funny news stories that are not true have been told.

For example, the newspaper Maaseudun Tulevaisuus wrote about the wall that President Donald Trump plans to build on the border of the USA and Mexico. The paper's April Fools' story was that the wall will be built from Finnish wood.

On April Fools' Day the police said on Facebook that they are starting to use hate speech dogs. They smell when someone spreads hate online. A police pig also got onto Facebook, when the Helsinki police said that they are starting to use 5 police pigs.

On April Fools' Day, you could read on the Facebook page of the Finnish Centre for Easy Language that the Centre has set up a new easy language monitoring unit. It monitors whether the authorities use language that everyone can understand.
