# Saturday 9.2.2019 (radio)

An Attendo care home in Ostrobothnia was closed because of serious shortcomings. The Finnish Immigration Service's errors in asylum decisions have increased. Winter-hardy bees are being bred in Finland. The president played ice hockey for the climate.

## An Attendo care home was closed

![Attendo Pelimanni care home in Alavus](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10638881)

In Ostrobothnia, the operations of an elderly care home have been suspended because of serious shortcomings.

The Regional State Administrative Agency has suspended the operations of an Attendo care home in Alavus. Operations were suspended because the care home had several shortcomings. The care home had too few carers, and there were problems, for example, with medicine administration.

The care home has only been operating for a month. During that time, 6 residents have died there.

The operations of the care home have now been transferred to the responsibility of a joint municipal authority.

In Finland there has been a lot of talk recently about elderly care homes. People are worried that the elderly do not get good enough care in care homes.

## The Immigration Service also makes mistakes

![The errors of the Finnish Immigration Service in asylum decisions have increased.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10637450)

Open image viewer

The Finnish Immigration Service makes more mistakes in asylum decisions than before. Photo: Police

Last year, administrative courts sent 330 decisions back to the Finnish Immigration Service for reconsideration. The administrative court was of the view that the decisions contained errors of legal interpretation or of procedure.

The number of errors has grown since 2015. In that year, over 32,000 asylum seekers came to Finland.

## Winter-hardy bees

![Bees on a honeycomb.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10481379)

Open image viewer

Bees on a honeycomb. Photo: Mostphotos

In Finland, efforts are being made to breed bees that cope better with Finland's long winter.

Finland now has many queen bees that were imported from abroad. They do not always suit Finnish conditions. Some queen bees, for example, may lay eggs until late in the autumn. In that case there is a risk that the colonies use up their winter food already in the autumn.

The aim now is to increase the breeding of queen bees in Finland. The aim is to raise healthy and calm bees that cope well with the winter and produce honey well.

Last year there were about 3,200 apiaries in Finland.

## The president played ice hockey

![President Sauli Niinistö played with the Russian hockey legend Vyacheslav Fetisov..](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10639307)

Today in Helsinki, ice hockey has been played for the protection of the climate. The Save Pond Hockey tournament was played on outdoor rinks at Brahe Field in Kallio, Helsinki.

The president, Sauli Niinistö, also took part in the tournament. Niinistö played in a white shirt with the number 17. The president also scored a couple of goals in the tournament.

The tournament has been held in Helsinki for the 5th time. Climate-change tournaments of ice hockey will also be held during the winter in Mikkeli, Tampere and Oulu.
