## Ukraine

Ukraine is asking NATO countries for more weapons. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

Ukraine is asking Western countries for more weapons. Ukraine is asking NATO to give Ukraine heavy weapons.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy says that Ukraine needs aircraft, tanks and missiles to fight against Russia.

Ukraine says that Poland can give Ukraine fighter jets. Ukraine says that the USA no longer opposes this. Earlier, the USA opposed handing over fighter jets to Ukraine, because the USA had feared Russia's reaction.

## Parliamentary parties on refugees

![People queuing for a train.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-933889623fe979c262f/1648461249)

Open image viewer

Perhaps 80,000 refugees from Ukraine will come to Finland this year. Photo: Lehtikuva

All parliamentary parties say that Finland must take in refugees from Ukraine. Yle asked the parliamentary parties what they think about refugees from Ukraine.

Some parties are more ready to help Ukrainians than the refugees who came to Finland from the Middle East and Africa during the 2015 refugee crisis.

Many MPs estimate that Ukrainians will settle into Finland more easily, because many Ukrainians have a good basic education and language skills.

Up to 80,000 Ukrainian refugees may come to Finland this year.

## Workload in the public sector

![Computer keyboard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3072,h_1728,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-39578565431a206181/1492775933)

Open image viewer

About 30 percent of public sector employees feel that the corona time has added work. Photo: Photo: Yle/ Laura Mainiemi

The corona time has increased workload in many public sector jobs. This is what a survey study shows. About 30 percent of public sector employees say that the corona time has increased workload. The public sector means employees of the state and municipalities.

More than half of public sector employees think that their pay no longer matches the amount of work and the high demands.

Public sector employees do like their own work, however. 80 percent of public sector employees say that their motivation in their own work is good or fairly good.
