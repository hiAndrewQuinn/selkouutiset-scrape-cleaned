# Tuesday 17.12.2019 (radio)

The government has answered the opposition's interpellation. Being a journalist can be dangerous. Requirements for police school are tightening. High-definition television will be available across the country next summer.

## Government and opposition argue over al-Hol

![Wives of Isis fighters with children at the al-Hol camp in June (illustrative photo, the nationality of the woman is not known)](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11117606)

The government and the opposition have argued in Parliament about the interpellation. The opposition's interpellation concerns the Finns in the al-Hol camp in Syria.

The opposition is proposing a vote of no confidence in Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. The opposition criticised Haavisto's actions on whether Finns should be brought back to Finland.

On Monday, the government made a policy decision on al-Hol with 10 points. The government wants to bring the Finnish children from the camp back to Finland as soon as possible.

There is no obligation to help adults. Authorities decide each case separately. They then decide whether the child's best interests also require bringing the mother back home.

The vote on the interpellation and on the government's confidence will be held tomorrow, Wednesday. The new government's programme, on the other hand, was approved in Parliament today.

## Being a journalist can be dangerous

![Protesters demanding press freedom in Turkey.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11120752)

Open image viewer

Protesters in Istanbul in October 2017 demanded that the government free imprisoned journalists and lawyers. Photo: Erdem Sahin / EPA

Reporters Without Borders says that more and more journalists are being murdered because of their work, including in democratic countries.

In war zones, fewer journalists are murdered than before. This year, 49 journalists have been killed while doing their work. Last year, 87 journalists were killed.

Latin America and the Middle East are the most dangerous places for journalists at work. Almost 400 journalists are in prison. A third of them are in prisons in China. The worst situation in Europe is in Turkey.

Journalists are also being threatened in Finland more than before.

## Entry to police school gets tougher

![Police at a demonstration](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_99/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5945805d8df38279b78/1585218610)

Good writing skills are important for police. Now the requirements are tightening. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

The selection of students for police college is getting tougher. You cannot get into the Police University College if you do badly in the written exam.

The school has noticed that some students have writing difficulties and find texts hard to understand.

Good writing skills are important for police, because police documents are legally binding, that is, binding in court.

## The HD era arrives across Finland in the summer

![General view.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_325/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5826155d4d5d6f76254/1737974268)

Open image viewer

To watch broadcasts in HD, you need an HD television. With the reform of Yle's channel distribution, you can keep watching on an old device. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

HD picture, that is, high-definition video, will be available everywhere in Finland by next summer.

People do not need to buy new technology or reinstall their antennas. The television will not go dark, and programmes can be watched on old devices.

However, the HD picture is visible only on new devices. If a customer wants a better picture and sound than now, they can buy a T2 television or a new digital device.

Next April, 85 per cent of customers will receive HD. In the summer, HD will be visible everywhere with new devices.
