# Wednesday 13.6.2018 (radio)

The trial over the MV website has begun in Helsinki. The number of child welfare notifications has grown. Finns still trust the police strongly. The war in Yemen is getting worse.

## The MV website trial began

![MV publication founder Ilja Janitskin in Helsinki District Court on 13 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10251711)

MV magazine founder Ilja Janitskin in court. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

A big trial has begun in Helsinki today, in which freedom of speech and hate speech are being dealt with. The founder of the MV internet site, Ilja Janitskin, is the accused. The prosecutor wants Janitskin to be sentenced to more than a year and a half in prison.

A sentence is demanded because, according to the prosecutor, lies about a journalist were published on the MV site. In the prosecutor's view, the MV site also mocked Jews and dark-skinned people, for example because of their religion or skin colour.

There have been 90 criminal complaints relating to the MV site.

## The number of child welfare notifications has grown

![Onni Westlund looks at a river.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10219425)

Open image viewer

Many child welfare notifications are made nowadays. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

Not all children's lives in Finland are good. Some children have a hard time, for example when their parents get divorced.

When a child is having a hard time, child welfare can help. You can get help if a child welfare notification is made.

Far more child welfare notifications are being made than before. The notifications have even caused a backlog.

A notification can be made if a child is not, for example, in a school lesson. Now experts think that people also make child welfare notifications too easily.

## Finns trust the police strongly

![Police car and people on a pedestrian crossing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10242372)

Open image viewer

Finns' trust in the police has remained strong. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Finns' faith in the police is strong. According to a new study, 95 percent of Finns trust the police. However, according to the study, people increasingly think that there is corruption, that is, bribery, in the police, for example.

People were also asked what mistakes the police make. Answers included that the police could handle arrest situations and public disorder better than they do now, for example.

The police were also criticised for how they treat immigrants, for example.

## An important city was attacked in Yemen

![Yemeni soldiers in Marib.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10011878)

Open image viewer

Yemeni soldiers in Marib last January. Photo: Soliman Alnowab / EPA

The war in Yemen is getting worse. Forces led by Saudi Arabia have attacked forces of the Yemeni government in the important city of Hodeidah.

Hodeidah is a port city. About 600,000 people live there. A lot of aid for the Yemeni people has been brought through Hodeidah. Before the war, more than 70 percent of Yemen's food and fuel was brought in through Hodeidah.

Now the UN fears that the attack on Hodeidah will cause a catastrophe in Yemen.
