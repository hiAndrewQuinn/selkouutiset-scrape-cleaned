# Monday 12.12.2016 (radio)

Many disabled people feel that they are discriminated against. The bakery firm Vaasan is cutting workers. State support for firms is criticised. Saara Aalto came second in Britain's X Factor singing competition.

## Disabled people experience discrimination

![A man sits in a wheelchair.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9346857)

Open image viewer

You cannot get everywhere in a wheelchair. Photo: AOP

Many disabled people feel that they are discriminated against. The Non-Discrimination Ombudsman did an internet survey for disabled people.

Almost 500 disabled people answered the survey. About 65 percent of the respondents felt that they had sometimes been discriminated against during the last year.

The respondents felt that the reason for the discrimination was usually their disability. Disabled people felt that they are discriminated against the most at workplaces.

Kalle Könkkölä from the disability organisation Kynnys says that discrimination shows, for example, in that many places are difficult to get to in a wheelchair. Sometimes disabled people are also spoken to in an insulting way.

The Non-Discrimination Ombudsman did the survey, because the ombudsman's task is to prevent, for example, disabled people or foreigners from being discriminated against.

## Vaasan is closing bakeries

![A toast bread production line. A worker in white work clothes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5637100)

Open image viewer

Vaasan bakes bread in different parts of Finland. Photo: Yle

The bakery firm Vaasan is cutting workers. Vaasan says that about 200 people may lose their work.

There are fewer jobs because Vaasan is closing its bakeries in Tampere, Kuopio and Seinäjoki.

Vaasan has made bread in Finland for almost 170 years already.

## The state supports firms

![Viking Line's passenger ship Isabella.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5002519)

Open image viewer

For example, passenger ships get support from the state. Photo: Juha-Pekka Inkinen / YLE

In Finland, the state gives support to firms. Last year, the state gave firms almost a billion euros in different kinds of support.

Support has been given, for example, to ships, because the state wants to take care of jobs. Wind power plants have got support, because the state wants more clean energy in Finland.

Now the VATT Institute for Economic Research is criticising the support. The institute hopes that more money will be given to research and the development of new products. In the institute's opinion, that helps economic growth better than support.

## Saara Aalto did well

![Saara Aalto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9346823)

Open image viewer

Saara Aalto in Britain's X Factor final. Photo: AOP

Finland's Saara Aalto has done very well in a British singing competition.

Saara came second in the X Factor singing competition. The winner of the competition was the British singer Matt Terry.

British TV viewers chose the winner. Although Matt Terry won, many Britons fell for Saara.

Saara Aalto was happy that she got so far in the competition. I feel like a winner, Saara said on Facebook. Saara also thanked everyone who voted for her.

Saara Aalto comes from Oulu, in northern Finland. Oulu now wants to congratulate Saara. That is why the mayor of Oulu has invited Saara to visit Oulu as soon as she has time.
