Prime Minister Juha Sipilä says that Finland's economy has started to grow.

## Reception centres are being closed

![The entrance of the Finnish Immigration Service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9097101)

Open image viewer

The Finnish Immigration Service will decide in December which reception centres will be closed. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Reception centres are being closed in Finland.

There are now about 80 reception centres. Many of them will be closed because the number of asylum seekers is getting smaller.

About 17,000 asylum seekers now live in reception centres. However, only a few asylum seekers come to Finland any more. This year, only about 6000 new asylum seekers will come.

The Finnish Immigration Service will soon decide which reception centres will be closed. The Finnish Immigration Service will make the decision during December.

Finland's president Sauli Niinistö has talked on the phone with Donald Trump. Trump is the next president of the USA. Trump starts in the job of president in January.

Niinistö and Trump discussed relations between Finland and the USA. The topic of the conversation was also the jubilee year of Finland's independence. Independent Finland turns 100 next year.

Trump asked Niinistö to pass on his greetings to Finns. Trump said that Finns are a great people.

## The Day of Finnish Music was celebrated

![a choir performs on the steps of the cathedral](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9341368)

Open image viewer

A singing event in Senate Square on 8.12. Photo: Lenni Kuivalainen / Yle

Thursday was the Day of Sibelius and Finnish Music.

Sibelius is Finland's national composer. He is the most famous Finnish composer also abroad.

Sibelius was born on the 8th of December 1865. That is why the Day of Finnish Music is celebrated on 8 December.

On Thursday, a singing event was held in Helsinki's Senate Square in honour of Sibelius. At the event, people sang Sibelius's songs together. There were many hundreds of singers.

## Tobacco kills

![Cigarettes stubbed out in a snowy ashtray.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9089263)

Open image viewer

Even one cigarette a day can shorten life. Photo: Seppo Sarkkinen / Yle

Smoking is dangerous even if a person smokes only a little. Even one cigarette a day can shorten life. The information is from a new study.

In Finland, smoking among men has decreased. Women smoke as much as 30 years ago. 17 percent of men and 14 percent of women smoke every day.
