# Friday 11.8.2017 (radio)

Legal aid for asylum seekers will be investigated. The West Metro may start running at the end of September. The restaurant industry is doing well. Tero Pitkämäki reached the javelin final at the World Athletics Championships. On Saturday there will be thunder showers.

## Legal aid for asylum seekers

![A law book.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8857034)

Open image viewer

Asylum seekers need help with legal matters. Photo: Yle

Legal aid for asylum seekers will be investigated. Minister of Justice Antti Häkkänen announced this.

There may be problems in legal aid. Some say that lawyers even cheat asylum seekers.

Minister Häkkänen says that everyone must get good legal aid in different life situations. Money must not decide the quality of the help.

## The West Metro

![Tapiola station on 10 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9596697)

Open image viewer

Tapiola is one of Espoo's metro stations. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

The West Metro may start running at the end of September. Construction work on the metro is already finished, but now all the new stations are being inspected. There are 8 new metro stations.

When the West Metro starts running, you can get from Helsinki to Espoo by metro. The last new station is Matinkylä.

## Restaurant chains

![Restaurant Sandro in the restaurant area on the fifth floor of Kamppi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9767844)

Open image viewer

Finns often eat in restaurants. Photo: Berislav Jurišić / Yle

The restaurant industry is doing well. Already over 70 per cent of Finns eat regularly in restaurants.

Many new restaurants are being started.

Many new restaurants belong to a restaurant chain. Chain restaurants save money when they, for example, order large amounts of ingredients together.

Some customers think that the quality in restaurant chains is not always good. That is why a restaurant often tries to be different from the other restaurants in the chain. The customer does not always even notice that the restaurant belongs to a chain.

## Pitkämäki into the final

![Tero Pitkämäki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9769590)

Open image viewer

Tero Pitkämäki reached the javelin final. Photo: Franck Robichon / EPA

Finns have had good sports news. The news came from London, from the World Athletics Championships.

Tero Pitkämäki reached the javelin final, that is, the last round of the competition.

Pitkämäki's throw was almost 86 metres long. A throw of at least 83 metres was needed for a place in the final.

The javelin final is on Saturday.

## Thunder at the weekend

![lightning in the sky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9684945)

Open image viewer

On Saturday there may be lightning in the sky. Photo: AOP

At the weekend there may be strong thunderstorms. Rain, lightning and strong wind will come on Saturday evening and Sunday night.

Before the thunder the weather is hot. Yle meteorologist Toni Hellinen says that the temperature may rise to as much as 28 degrees on Saturday.
