## African swine fever

The first news item is about an animal disease.

African swine fever has been found in Finland. This is the first time the disease has been found in Finland.

African swine fever was found in wild boar piglets in Virolahti, Eastern Finland.

African swine fever is a serious disease that spreads easily. Pigs or wild boars die from the disease. There is no treatment or vaccine for African swine fever.

**Sirpa Kiviruusu**, head of unit at the Finnish Food Authority, says that African swine fever is greatly hindering the export of pork abroad.

African swine fever has been found in Finland. African swine fever is not contagious to humans.

## Ice cream kiosks

The next news item is about ice cream kiosks.

The hygiene of some ice cream kiosks is poor. A Yle survey shows that one in 10 ice cream kiosks is dirty. The premises and equipment of the ice cream kiosks are dirty or the food is handled incorrectly.

Some of the shortcomings of ice cream stands are easy to fix. Health inspector **Anne Peräniitty** says that, for example, ice cream scoops should be rinsed well.

The hygiene of some ice cream kiosks is poor. The majority of ice cream kiosks received a good rating in the inspection.
