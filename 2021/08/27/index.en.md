# Friday 27.8.2021 (radio)

Dozens of people died in the terror attack in Kabul. Finland's evacuations ended. Denmark is ending all corona restrictions.

## Terror attack in Kabul

![Map of the area around Kabul airport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12074321)

Open image viewer

Map of the Kabul airport area Photo: Pekka Sipilä / Yle, Lasse Isokangas / Yle

A terror attack was carried out near Kabul airport on Thursday.

2 bombs exploded. At least 13 US soldiers and more than 70 Afghans died in the attack. About 300 people were injured.

The Isis organisation carried out the attack. US President Joe Biden says that the US will take revenge for the attack.

## Evacuation ended

![Finnish troops took part in the evacuation operation at Kabul airport in August. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8462266126473403c5d/1629898865)

Open image viewer

Not everyone who wants to leave will be evacuated from Afghanistan before the end of August. The Ministry for Foreign Affairs has blurred the faces of civilians who were present in the pictures to protect their privacy. Photo: Försvarsmakten / Utrikesministeriet

Finland has stopped bringing people from Afghanistan to Finland.

Finland took in 330 people for safety. 250 of them were Finnish citizens and people who worked at the Finnish embassy.

The evacuation lasted 10 days.

Finland did not have time to help everyone get out of Afghanistan. People could not get to Kabul airport. Dozens of Finnish citizens and Afghans who worked for Finland were left in Afghanistan.

Finland is still trying to help the people who were left in Afghanistan.

## Consumer economy

![People at the Iso-Kristiina shopping centre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78566460534da34343c/1616072218)

People at the Iso-Kristiina shopping centre. Photo: Kare Lehtonen / Yle

Many people think that they have enough money. People's assessment of their own financial situation is better than ever before. People have been asked for their assessment since 1995.

People believe that they will have enough money next year too. People plan to buy various things. People believe that now is a good time to put money into savings or to borrow money from the bank.

People also believe that the Finnish state's economy is developing well. This is what Statistics Finland's survey says.

## Corona restrictions in Southern Finland

![Information sign outside the Helsinki City Theatre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4864,h_2736,x_0,y_388/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6800585ecb7bac772cd/1590393991)

Open image viewer

The Helsinki City Theatre is one of the theatres that are affected by the corona restrictions. Photo: Jari Pussinen / YLE

The corona restrictions will not change in Southern Finland. The Regional State Administrative Agency for Southern Finland is not changing the restrictions.

In theatre and concert auditoriums there must still be blocks. The 2-metre safety distance must be kept. The restrictions continue until 12 September.

## Corona restrictions in Denmark

![Mary poses for a selfie.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5760,h_3240,x_0,y_513/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8471046128baec17ed2/1630059299)

Open image viewer

Crown Princess Mary of Denmark (on the left) posed for a group photo with participants of the WorldPride event in Copenhagen on 21 August. Photo: Olafur Steinar Gestsson / EPA

Denmark is ending all corona restrictions. The restrictions end in 2 weeks, on 10 September.

Denmark says that the corona situation is good and that many people in Denmark have been vaccinated. Denmark will bring the restrictions back if many people have to go to hospital because of corona.
