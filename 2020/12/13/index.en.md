# Sunday 13.12.2020 (radio)

Negotiations between the EU and Britain. Coronavirus restrictions in Germany. Finland's coronavirus situation. Lucia bride. Shooting stars.

## Negotiations between the EU and Britain

![Boris Johnson and Ursula von der Leyen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11695276)

Britain's Prime Minister Boris Johnson and European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen. Photo: Olivier Hoslet / AFP

The EU and Britain intend to carry on negotiating a trade agreement after all.

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen and Britain's Prime Minister Boris Johnson will say in a joint statement that the negotiations will continue.

The EU and Britain have been negotiating the trade agreement for almost a year. The deadline for the negotiations was supposed to be today.

There is already pressure on the deal, because Britain's transition period after leaving the EU ends at the turn of the year. Then Britain will leave the EU's single market and customs union.

Germany is tightening coronavirus restrictions. The new restrictions will come into force on Wednesday.

Schools will move to distance learning, and most shops and businesses will be closed. Only essential shops, such as food shops and pharmacies, may stay open.

Germany has had a partial coronavirus lockdown for more than a month. However, the numbers of infections and deaths have only grown.

## Finland's coronavirus infections

![A line of cars at the joint coronavirus testing point of HUS and 9Lives in Koivukylä, Vantaa, at the Havukoski sports park on 1 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11681381)

The number of coronavirus cases is also growing in Finland.

In the last two weeks, over 6,000 coronavirus infections have been found in Finland in total. That is about 870 more infections than in the previous two weeks.

Almost 31,000 infections have now been found in total, and about 450 people have died of the coronavirus.

## Lucia bride

![Ingrid Enckell](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11695122)

Open image viewer

Lucia bride Ingrid Enckell. Photo: Ilkka Klemola / Yle

Today is St Lucia's Day.

Among the traditions of St Lucia's Day is the Lucia bride, who wears a crown of candles on her head. The Lucia bride brings light into the darkness of winter.

This year's Lucia bride is Ingrid Enckell from Helsinki. She was crowned in the evening at Helsinki Cathedral in Helsinki. Because of the coronavirus situation, no audience was let into the church and no traditional Lucia procession was held.

## Shooting stars

![Geminid shooting star.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9978138)

Open image viewer

Many shooting stars can be seen next night, if the sky is only clear. Photo: AOP

Many shooting stars can be seen next night.

The astronomical association Ursa says that up to 70 shooting stars an hour can be seen during the night between Sunday and Monday. The shooting stars come from a meteor shower called the Geminids.

However, Yle meteorologist Joonas Koskela says that the night is cloudy across the whole country. In places the cloud cover may break a little, but the shooting stars may well be hidden behind the clouds altogether, says Joonas Koskela.
