# Sunday 14.5.2017 (radio)

Sunday was Mother's Day. The president of France has changed. Portugal has won the Eurovision Song Contest. Speaking a foreign language strains the voice.

## Mothers have been celebrated

![The Grand Cross and breast star of the Order of the White Rose of Finland in a box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5963020)

Sunday was Mother's Day. Mother's Day is every year on the 2nd Sunday of May.

This year too, a group of mothers have received a decoration from the president. President Sauli Niinistö gave a decoration to 33 mothers. They got the decoration because they have done important work raising children.

![Lahja Lehtola, Inari.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9610404)

Open image viewer

Lahja Lehtola is already retired, but still looks after the reindeer of her own farm. Photo: Sirkku Savusalo / Yle

The people who got the decoration are from different parts of Finland. One of them is Lahja Lehtola. Lehtola lives in the north, in Lapland. She is the mistress of a reindeer farm.

Lehtola has 5 children of her own, who are already adults. For 30 years she also looked after children as a family day carer. In addition, Lehtola has raised 3 foster children, to whom she gave a safe home.

Lehtola and the other mothers received the decoration at a ceremony that was held in Helsinki.

## Macron started as president

The president of France has changed. Emmanuel Macron started work as president on Sunday. He won the French presidential election a week ago.

The new president's time began with a ceremony at the Élysée Palace in Paris. The Élysée Palace is the home of the French president.

The new president spoke to the French. Macron said that the world needs a strong France.

## Eurovision victory to Portugal

![Salvador Sobral celebrated his victory with his sister in Kyiv on Saturday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9612297)

Portugal has won the Eurovision Song Contest. The contest was late on Saturday in Kyiv, the capital of Ukraine.

Salvador Sobral sang Portugal's song. The song was calm and emotional, and Salvador sang it in Portuguese. The song was written by Salvador's sister Luisa.

Salvador Sobral was happy about the victory. He hoped that people will listen to music that has feelings in it.

Salvador Sobral also spoke up for refugees. The situation of refugees is a big problem in Europe, Sobral said.

## A foreign language strains the voice

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9601341)

Open image viewer

Many people speak a foreign language in a higher voice than their mother tongue. Photo: Yle News Graphics

Speaking a foreign language affects a person's voice.

Researcher Kati Järvinen says that people speak a foreign language differently from their mother tongue. Often, for example, the pitch of the voice rises. That is why the voice gets tired faster when a person speaks a foreign language.

Järvinen says that, for example, interpreters and language teachers can get voice problems, because they speak a foreign language a lot. That is why it is good to practise using the voice in a foreign language too.

Järvinen's study included people whose mother tongue is Finnish or English.
