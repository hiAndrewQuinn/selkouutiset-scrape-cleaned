# Wednesday 6.6.2018 (radio)

The leaders of the USA and North Korea meet next week. The Finnish government intends to improve children's rights in divorce. The police have been looking for drunk drivers on the roads. The number of wolves has grown in Finland. The presidential couple is moving to Naantali with their baby for the summer.

## Trump and Kim

![Capella Hotel on Sentosa Island in Singapore](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10240087)

The meeting place of Trump and Kim is the Capella Hotel on Sentosa Island. Photo: Grant Smith / AOP

The leaders of the USA and North Korea will meet next week, on Tuesday. US President Donald Trump and North Korean leader Kim Jong-un will meet in Asia, in Singapore.

The meeting place is on Sentosa Island, which is part of Singapore. It is believed that the meeting place is on the island for security reasons.

The subject of the meeting between Trump and Kim Jong-un is North Korea's nuclear weapons. The USA wants to agree on how North Korea will give up its nuclear weapons.

## Children of divorce

![A child's outdoor clothes on a bench.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9447605)

Open image viewer

Nowadays a child can lose important people after their parents divorce. Photo: Sanna Savela / Yle

The Finnish government intends to improve children's rights in divorce.

The government wants, for example, to make sure that a child's important relationships are kept even after the parents divorce. The government thinks it is important that a child can meet, for example, grandparents.

The government's aim is for the new law to come into force next spring. Parliament will decide on the new law.

## Drunk drivers

![A police officer has a driver take a breath test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9436193)

Open image viewer

The police have been looking for drunk drivers. Photo: Kalle Schönberg / Yle

On Wednesday the police monitored traffic more closely than usual. The police have been looking for drunk drivers, that is, people who drive a car while drunk.

There are many kinds of drunk drivers nowadays. Some have drunk alcohol, but some have used drugs. Young drunk drivers in particular have often used drugs.

Last year the police caught more than 17,000 drunk drivers in Finland.

## Wolves

![The family of Leena Latvala from Kurikka lives near the Jurva border. Wolf sightings have been made near the family home.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10241280)

Open image viewer

Wolves have been seen near the Latvala family's home in Ostrobothnia. Photo: Mirva Ekman/Yle

The number of wolves has grown in Finland. About 200 wolves now live in Finland. That is 10 percent more than last year.

The number of wolves has grown the most in Western Finland. In Ostrobothnia, for example, there are now twice as many wolves as a year ago. People have seen wolves even close to houses.

Wolves often live in a pack. One pack contains many wolves. Experts estimate that there are now 20 wolf packs in Finland.

## The presidential family

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10240624)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö has talked about life with a small baby. The president's baby boy, the son of President Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio, was born in February.

Niinistö told journalists on Wednesday that the family is living a happy and ordinary Finnish life.

The presidential couple is moving to Naantali with the baby for the summer. The president's summer residence, Kultaranta, is in Naantali.
