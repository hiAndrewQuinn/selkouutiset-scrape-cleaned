# Sunday 20.12.2020 (radio)

Al-Hol. A coronavirus mutation. Parcels in the post. Electric buses. Weather forecast.

## Al-Hol camp

![Children and women at the al-Hol camp.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1840,h_1035,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6358425e2ff51f0d4cb/1580281709)

Women and children at the al-Hol camp. Photo: Otso Reunanen / Yle

More people have returned to Finland from the al-Hol camp in Syria. Two women and six children of the women have come to Finland from al-Hol. Finland helped the mothers and children to Finland today, Sunday.

Earlier, Finland helped two orphaned children from the al-Hol camp to Finland. Mothers and children have also come to Finland from al-Hol, in cases where they arranged the trip to Finland themselves.

The police are carrying out an investigation of all adults who come to Finland from al-Hol.

## A new form of the coronavirus

![Protest against coronavirus measures in London.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11707815)

Open image viewer

Protest against coronavirus measures in London. Photo: Tolga Akmen / AFP

A new form of the coronavirus, that is, a mutation, has been found in Britain. The new coronavirus spreads more easily than the ordinary coronavirus. That is why it can spread very quickly to many people.

Strict coronavirus restrictions have been brought in in Britain so that the new virus form does not spread.

A new type of coronavirus has also been found in the Netherlands, Denmark and Australia.

## Parcels

![Parcels at the Helsinki post sorting centre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10532459)

Open image viewer

Parcels at the Helsinki post sorting centre. Photo: Markku Pelkonen / Yle

The Post and other transport companies are now carrying a very large number of parcels. People have ordered, for example, lots of Christmas presents from online shops.

The Post estimates that it is now carrying over 2 million parcels in 1 week. That is a record, that is, more than the Post has ever carried in one week before.

The numbers of parcels at the Post and other transport companies have grown quickly.

## Electric buses

Finland is introducing more electric buses. Electric buses will come into local public transport in big cities next year.

About 50 electric buses are now in use in Finland. At the end of next year there will be about 250 electric buses in Finland.

Electric buses are now in Helsinki, Tampere and Turku. Next year electric buses will also come to Lahti and Kuopio.
