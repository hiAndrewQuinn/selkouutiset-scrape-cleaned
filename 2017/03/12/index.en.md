# Sunday 12.3.2017 (radio)

Turkey and Europe are quarrelling. The Refugees of the Year 2017 have been chosen. Finland can offer tourists silence. It is 200 years since Book Matti was born.

## Turkey

![People wave Turkish flags in front of the Turkish consulate in Rotterdam. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9505195)

The relationship between Turkey and Europe is now bad.

Turkey is angry because speaking events of Turkish ministers have been banned in Europe. Turkey is especially angry with the Netherlands, which did not even let the Turkish foreign minister into the Netherlands. Turkey demands that the Netherlands apologise.

European countries do not like Turkish ministers holding political events in Europe. Many Turkish ministers have travelled to Europe because they hope for support from Europe's Turks for President Recep Tayyip Erdogan. In April Turks will vote on whether the president is given more power.

## Silence

![Forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9250315)

Open image viewer

Stress decreases in the forest. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Finland can offer tourists silence. Silence is a new trend in tourism. That is why nature, peace and silence are things that Finland is advertised with abroad.

For example, Elina Miettunen and Pauliina Myllylä's company offers tourists the peace of nature. The women do not organise activities, but passivities. The only activity is going into the forest and cooking food on a campfire.

Silence is a healthy trend. Studies show that in the forest stress decreases and blood pressure goes down.

## Book Matti

![The unveiling ceremony of Matti Pohto's memorial stone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9505265)

Open image viewer

Book Matti's memorial stone is in Helsinki. Photo: National Library

It is 200 years since Book Matti was born. He was born in March 1817.

But who was Book Matti? Matti's real name was Matti Pohto. He is called Book Matti because he saved Finnish-language literature. That happened after the great fire of Turku.

The city of Turku burned down almost completely in 1827. The library of the Academy of Turku also burned, and the fire destroyed the Finnish-language books that were kept in the library.

But Finnish-language literature was saved, because Matti Pohto collected Finnish-language books around Finland. Pohto collected almost 5000 books, which are now in the National Library.
