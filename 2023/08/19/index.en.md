## Zelensky in Sweden

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has made a surprise visit to Sweden. In Sweden Zelensky has met, for example, Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson and King Carl Gustaf and Queen Silvia.

The visit discussed the situation in Ukraine and Sweden's support for Ukraine. So far Sweden has delivered aid to Ukraine worth 2.2 billion euros.

Zelensky and Prime Minister Kristersson also said that Swedish armoured vehicles will in future be manufactured in Ukraine.

## Electricity price

![Loviisa nuclear power plant](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6563575e778067b59da/1584890064)

The annual maintenance of the Loviisa nuclear power plant begins on Sunday. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

Spot electricity is expensive during this weekend.

Today, Saturday, electricity was most expensive at 11 in the morning. At that time the spot electricity price was just over 26 cents per kilowatt-hour. Tomorrow, Sunday, electricity is even more expensive. At 8 in the morning and at 10 in the evening, spot electricity costs about 31 cents per kilowatt-hour.

The rise in the electricity price is because less electricity is available now than usual. The Loviisa nuclear power plant is being serviced, and a fault is being repaired in one reactor at the Olkiluoto nuclear power plant. Maintenance work is also being done at power plants in Sweden, so less electricity than usual is now being brought to Finland from there.

## Saturated fat

![Different cuts of meat at a supermarket meat counter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_298/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1111444645b7d224bbd0/1683720952)

Open image viewer

Meat and meat products contain saturated fat. Photo: Jari Pussinen / Yle

Finns eat too much saturated fat.

The most saturated fat comes from meat and meat products, and from milk and milk products.

According to health recommendations, the right amount of saturated fat is 10 percent of the total energy a person gets, says Mikael Fogelholm, professor of nutrition science at the University of Helsinki.

You can reduce saturated fat, for example, by buying low-fat or fat-free meat and milk products. Instead of meat, it is also worth eating more fish or plant-based proteins.
