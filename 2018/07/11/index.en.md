# Wednesday 11.7.2018 (radio)

The US president demands more money from Europe for military spending. There was a fault on Kela's online services during the day. Unconditional prison sentences in an art forgery case. The first heatwave warning in two years has been issued in Finland.

## Trump criticised Germany at the NATO meeting

![NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg and US President Donald Trump talk at a breakfast table in Brussels today.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10299880)

Trump criticised Germany right at the start of the NATO meeting. Photo: Brendan Smialowski / Lehtikuva

US President Donald Trump has arrived at the NATO military alliance meeting. In Brussels, Trump sharply criticised Germany. Trump said that Germany depends on Russian energy production and is therefore under Russia's control.

The USA demands more money from European countries for military spending. Funding has been increased, but only half of NATO countries will reach the agreed goal on time. There are 29 NATO countries.

Trump will continue from Brussels to Great Britain and then to Helsinki. He will meet Russian President Vladimir Putin in Helsinki next Monday.

## Art forgeries brought prison sentences

![An artist from Lempäälä who was charged with aggravated forgery at the Pirkanmaa District Court](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9401918)

Open image viewer

In the forgery case, paintings were sold, and one man painted 80 of them. Photo: Matti Vikman / Yle

The Pirkanmaa District Court has sentenced 4 people to unconditional prison terms in a large art forgery case.

The group sold, among other things, forged paintings by Akseli Gallen-Kallela and Reidar Särestöniemi. A total of 115 paintings were sold. The value of the sales was 1.3 million euros.

The paintings were sold from 2004 to 2014. More than 30 people and companies were deceived over 10 years. The convicted people must pay compensation totalling 1.2 million euros.

## Many Finns holiday on borrowed money

![On holiday](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10283706)

Open image viewer

Your own money is not always enough to fulfil holiday dreams. Then many people take out loans. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

14 percent of Finns finance their holidays with a loan, according to the Nordic loan barometer. Finns take out loans especially to buy a car and a motorbike, and for holiday trips. Money is also borrowed to renovate a home or a summer cottage.

Loans are taken out more often in our closest neighbouring countries. 22 percent of Swedes have financed a holiday with a loan, and 21 percent of Norwegians.

## The first heatwave warning of the summer has been issued

![Weather maps from Wednesday to Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10298925)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

The Finnish Meteorological Institute has issued the first heatwave warning of the summer.

Oppressive heat, almost 30 degrees, is coming to Southern Lapland and Central Lapland. It is also hot elsewhere. The weather in the coming days is warm over wide areas, but in the west and south there will be rain, which will cool the air.

The Yle meteorologist says that the most unstable days are Friday and Saturday. Next week the weather will keep warming up. At the start of the week, temperatures will be above 25 degrees in almost the whole country.

The last heatwave warning was issued in Finland in summer 2016.
