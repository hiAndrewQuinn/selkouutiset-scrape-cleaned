## Finland's debt

![State debt](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5807206)

Open image viewer

Finland's indebtedness worries the EU. Photo: YLE, source: Arja Lento / YLE

Finland's indebtedness is going over the European Union's limit values for the first time. Finland has gone into debt since the start of the corona pandemic so quickly that the EU may step in to deal with the development.

The Commission presents new rules this week for curbing indebtedness.

Member states can be divided into different categories according to their debt. Finland may no longer belong to the top group and may face closer monitoring than now.

The new rules may already be in force in 2024.

## Climate change

![Drought-stricken lake in Australia, dead trees on the shore.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10713367)

Open image viewer

Climate warming increases extreme weather phenomena, for example drought. Photo: Dean Lewins / EPA

The climate is still warming. The last 8 years have been the hottest years in statistical history. This is what the report of the United Nations, that is, the UN, says.

Researchers estimate that the world's average temperature has already risen by 1.15 degrees compared with the time before industrialisation.

The countries of the world have agreed that the climate must not warm by more than 1.5 degrees. Researchers say that the target no longer seems likely.

Exceeding the 1.5-degree limit causes much greater risks for people, living nature and ecosystems. The report warns that glaciers will melt, sea levels will rise and heatwaves will be record-breaking.

The UN's international climate negotiations have begun in Egypt.

## Ethnic discrimination

![Porthania building at the University of Helsinki's city centre campus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5020,h_2824,x_0,y_526/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7369115f9e78615578d/1733994379)

Open image viewer

Porthania building at the University of Helsinki's city centre campus. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Ethnic minorities experience discrimination in Finnish universities. The Ministry of Education's study concerned teachers and researchers at universities.

The study says that ethnic minorities experience discrimination almost twice as often as ethnic Finns. Career development is also weaker than for the majority population.

There are relatively few women and ethnic minorities in the top positions at universities. In addition, recruitment, that is, the selection of employees for work, is not transparent.

## Bears' winter sleep

![Brown bear at Korkeasaari, Ursus arctos arctos.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6927386)

Open image viewer

Bear at Korkeasaari in Helsinki. Photo: Korkeasaari archive / Mari Lehmonen

The bears at Helsinki's Korkeasaari zoo are now in winter sleep. The bears have built up fat stores during the autumn.

Bears can sleep for many months, the zoo says.

The zoo's bear enclosure is home to 2 female bears. For the past few weeks they have mostly been in their winter den.

The bears' winter sleep was interrupted last year already at Christmas, because a water pipe at the zoo broke.
