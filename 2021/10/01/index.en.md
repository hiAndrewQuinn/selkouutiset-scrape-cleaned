# Friday 1.10.2021 (radio)

Lifting corona restrictions. Paying corona subsidies. Interest rates on quick loans. Postal parcel prices. Weather forecast.

## Lifting corona restrictions

![Paula Klemola at Pirkkola swimming hall](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5231,h_2942,x_0,y_139/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8595276153354bf01ab/1632843171)

Lifting of corona restrictions has begun in Finland today, Friday. All gathering restrictions have ended. For example, swimming halls, cinemas and concert halls may again be entered without restriction.

However, general hygiene requirements, for example hand washing and the use of hand sanitiser, still apply at gathering places.

Restaurant restrictions have also ended in much of Finland. Restaurant restrictions are still in place in areas where the coronavirus is spreading.

## Corona subsidies

![Wine glasses in a restaurant](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-800999608bf7d6657d8/1619785735)

Restaurants, for example, have received corona subsidies. Photo: Birgitta Vuorela / Yle

Payment of corona subsidies to companies has ended. The last application round for corona subsidies ended on Thursday.

Companies have been doing better again. That is why companies applied for less subsidy than expected. About 700 million euros of corona subsidies have been paid to companies in total.

The National Audit Office of Finland has audited the distribution of corona subsidies. The Office says that errors were also made in the distribution. Some companies may have received subsidy even though the company was not doing so badly.

## Quick loan interest rates

![Someone applies for a quick loan on a mobile phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11032270)

Open image viewer

Quick loans are applied for, for example, by phone. Quick loans usually have a high interest rate. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Interest rates on quick loans may rise again. The Consumers' Association says that the interest rate on many loans may even double this month. The reason is that the temporary interest rate cap on quick loans has ended. The interest cap was 10 per cent.

Now the interest rate on some quick loans may rise again to 20 per cent. Quick loans may also be advertised to people again.

Quick loans had a 10 per cent interest cap and an advertising ban, because the corona situation worsened many people's financial situation.

## Postal parcels

![A postal worker sorts parcels into a parcel locker in Helsinki on 14 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11811511)

Open image viewer

A postal carrier puts postal parcels into a parcel locker. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Postal parcel prices have risen. Sending a parcel with Posti became 1 euro more expensive. This may affect the recycling of goods.

Many Finns sell and buy used goods online. For example, clothes are sold a lot on online flea markets. When goods are cheap, the postage may be too much for the buyer.

Parcels are also carried in Finland by the company Matkahuolto. Matkahuolto says that the number of parcels at Matkahuolto has increased since Posti's parcel prices rose.
