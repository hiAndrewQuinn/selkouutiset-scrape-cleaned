# Thursday 11.7.2019 (radio)

Finland is deporting citizens of Uzbekistan because of forged school certificates. The car factory in Uusikaupunki is hiring more workers. The government intends to increase the number of women's shelters. The number of people in the world is growing quickly.

## Uzbek construction workers to be deported

![Construction worker](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5756,h_3238,x_0,y_603/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5760355d23051d04b33/1562940535)

Finland is deporting citizens of Uzbekistan. The Finnish Immigration Service (Migri) has decided that almost 140 Uzbeks must leave Finland.

The reason for Migri's decision is that the school certificates of the Uzbeks are forged. They got jobs in Finland even though the certificates are not genuine.

Construction companies are worried about the situation. There are many Uzbek construction workers on construction sites in Uusimaa. Problems may arise for the companies when a large group of workers leaves the sites.

## More workers for the car factory

![The marriage point on the assembly line of the Uusikaupunki car factory. At this point the car body and the drivetrain are joined.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10654740)

There is plenty of work at the Uusikaupunki car factory. Photo: Valmet Automotive

The Uusikaupunki car factory is hiring more workers.

The number of jobs will grow, because a new additional line is being built for car assembly. The additional line will start in August. It will bring about 50 new jobs.

The Uusikaupunki car factory says that in the autumn it will also hire workers for various production tasks. There are jobs for about 400 people.

## More women's shelters

![Two beds and a cot in a family room of a women's shelter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10666877)

A women's shelter gives a place of safety if a family member hits. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

The government intends to increase the number of women's shelters. Shelters help people who suffer from domestic violence.

Domestic violence is a big problem in Finland. Last year almost 10,000 people suffered from domestic violence that was reported to the police.

For example, if the father of a family is violent, the mother and children must get into a shelter quickly. That can be difficult if the nearest shelter is full.

That is why the government intends to increase the number of family places in shelters, says Minister of Family and Basic Services Krista Kiuru. There are now about 200 family places in shelters, but the target is 250 places.

## The world's population is growing

![The Earth seen from the surface of the Moon.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10611384)

Open image viewer

We will soon be 8 billion here. Photo: AOP

The number of people is growing quickly. 200 years ago there were 1 billion people, and in 4 years there will already be 8 billion.

The number of people is growing fastest in Africa, south of the Sahara. There, in 30 years, there will be twice as many people as now. That is a big problem for food, because climate change harms agriculture in Africa.

By population, the largest country in the world is in Asia. It is now China. There are 1.4 billion Chinese. However, India will overtake China in less than 10 years.

In Europe the number of people is decreasing.
