## Football tourists' coronavirus infections

![Supporters of the Huuhkajat on their way to a European Championship match in St. Petersburg.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_564/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82577660d85b9aaef91/1624792005)

Open image viewer

Finnish football fans on their way to St. Petersburg. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

Coronavirus infections among football tourists are growing. For example, in Helsinki football tourists already had about 140 confirmed coronavirus infections on Saturday.

Finnish football tourists got coronavirus infections in St. Petersburg, Russia. They were there to watch the football European Championship matches. Finland had 2 matches in St. Petersburg.

More coronavirus infections may still be found. Not everyone who came back to Finland from St. Petersburg could be tested at the border. The authorities hope that all football tourists now go for a corona test in their home municipality.

## Rental cars

![Service desk of a car rental company at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5190,h_2921,x_0,y_195/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-41631659709ca84238a/1500552910)

Open image viewer

Car rental companies have many customers in the summer. Photo: Juha Kivioja / Yle

Many Finns rent a car in the summer. Rental cars are popular because many stay in Finland during the holiday because of corona.

Car rental companies say that rental cars may even run out. A large part of Finns start their summer holiday at Midsummer. That is why many cars are rented in the coming weeks.

Customers of car rental companies are now especially interested in electric cars. Car rental companies say that electric cars are booked quickly.

Monday is a dry day, no rain falls.

Monday is also sunny and warm. The temperature is between 20 and 27 degrees in almost the whole country.

The weather is also warm on Tuesday in a large part of the country. In Lapland, however, the weather gets a little colder.
