# Saturday 3.12.2016 (radio)

Elderly people in Finland get too many medicines. Asylum seekers held a demonstration in Helsinki on Saturday. A Finnish opera has been performed at the Metropolitan in New York. Finns are inventing new first names.

## Medicines for the elderly

![Jaakko Valvanne ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9332564)

Open image viewer

Professor Jaakko Valvanne. Photo: Yle

Elderly people in Finland get too many medicines. So says professor Jaakko Valvanne.

In the professor's opinion, the reason for bad care is often that doctors and nurses are lazy. It is easy to treat the elderly with medicines.

Doctors and nurses need more knowledge about elderly care, professor Valvanne says. Medicines must be reduced when that is best for the elderly person.

## Asylum seekers' demonstration

![A demonstration in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9332965)

Open image viewer

Hundreds of asylum seekers took part in the demonstration. Photo: Don Trammell

Asylum seekers held a demonstration in Helsinki on Saturday. About 500 asylum seekers and people who wanted to support asylum seekers took part in the demonstration.

The demonstrators opposed the Finnish Immigration Service returning Iraqis back to Iraq. Most of the demonstrators were Iraqis.

## Finns at the Metropolitan

![Kaija Saariaho.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9329121)

A Finnish opera has been performed at the famous Metropolitan opera house in New York. The opera's composer is Kaija Saariaho.

![Tamara Mumford as the Pilgrim, Eric Owens as Jaufré Rudel and Susanna Phillips as Clémence ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9329119)

Open image viewer

A sea of 40,000 LED lamps is created on the Metropolitan's stage. Photo: Ken Howard / Metropolitan Opera

The opera's text was written by the Lebanese-French Amin Maalouf. The text is in French, and the opera's name is L'Amour de Loin, in English Love from Afar. The opera's lovers live far from each other, with a sea between them. The story takes place 900 years ago.

Love from Afar brings the skills of Finnish women to the Metropolitan. It is the first opera by a woman composer at the Metropolitan in over 100 years. The opera performance is conducted by the Finnish conductor Susanna Mälkki.

## New first names

![Minna Saarelma-Paukkala](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9332621)

Open image viewer

Minna Saarelma-Paukkala knows Finnish names. Photo: Ilkka Klemola / Yle

New Finnish first names keep appearing all the time. Parents want to give their children unusual names. Unusual names are given more to girls than to boys.

Finns invent unusual first names in 4 different ways. They have been studied by Minna Saarelma-Paukkala, the head of the University of Helsinki's almanac office.

One way is to give a child a foreign name. Finnish children have got names such as Amour, Fathima and Prince, for example.

Old Finnish names can also be combined in a new way. This is how, for example, the name Kiia-Kesäkukka was born.

New names are also made on the model of an old one. For example, the old names Reeta and Reetta have been the model for the new names Eeta, Neeta and Meetta.

There are also completely new kinds of Finnish-language names. Often these names show that nature is important to Finns. A beautiful example is Aamuhelmi (Morning Pearl).
