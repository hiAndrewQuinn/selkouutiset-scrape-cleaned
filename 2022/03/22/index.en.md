# Tuesday 22.3.2022 (radio)

Fighting continues in Ukraine over Kyiv and Mariupol. The war affects Finland's economy. Employment has improved. There is a lot of street dust in the air. Weather forecast.

Fighting in Ukraine continues over control of the capital Kyiv and control of the port city of Mariupol. Russia has advanced closer to the centre of Kyiv.

Ukraine says that thousands of people have been evacuated from the city of Mariupol, that is, brought to safety. However, there are still about 300,000 people in Mariupol. There is a shortage of food and water in Mariupol.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy says that he wants to negotiate peace with Russian President Vladimir Putin.

## The war and the economy

![People in a shopping centre protecting themselves with face masks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_628/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-779524603ca2e7ac0ea/1614587240)

The war in Ukraine affects Finland's economy. Economic growth is slowing. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The war in Ukraine affects Finland's economy. Because of the war, Finland's economic growth is slowing. This is the forecast of the Pellervo Economic Research institute, PTT.

PTT forecasts that Finland's economy will grow by half (0.5) a percent this year and by about 1 percent next year. Economic growth is slowing because the war harms foreign trade, raises prices and changes the structures of the economy.

PTT also forecasts that the good development of employment will get worse.

## Employment

For example, restaurants have a shortage of workers. Photo: Emilia Åberg / Yle

Employment has improved. Statistics Finland says that in February there were 89,000 more people employed in Finland than in February last year.

The share of unemployed people has decreased since February last year. The unemployment rate, that is, the share of unemployed people in the labour force, was 6.7 percent in February. In February last year, the figure was over 8 percent.

The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment says that the number of open jobs in February was a record high. The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment estimates that the war in Ukraine may not reduce the shortage of workers in Finland.

## Street dust

![Street dust in central Rovaniemi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-79817060828052a30d6/1619165374)

In the spring sun, sand dries and blows into the air. Photo: Raimo Torikka / Yle

Spring has started and street dust is again worsening air quality in many cities. There is a lot of street dust now across the whole of southern Finland.

In winter, sand was spread on the streets. In the spring sun, the sand dries and blows into the air. Studded tyres on cars also loosen street dust from the asphalt into the air.

The street dust season is now at its worst. Air quality has been poor in many cities. The pollen season has also already started. Pollen and street dust cause symptoms for many people.
