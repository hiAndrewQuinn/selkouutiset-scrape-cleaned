# Wednesday 14.10.2020 (radio)

Minister Kiuru has won the confidence of Parliament. Face masks are recommended at workplaces in the Helsinki area. There are 4 new coronavirus deaths in Finland. In many countries there are thousands of new coronavirus infections a day. On Thursday the weather is cloudy.

## Minister Kiuru

![Family and basic services minister Krista Kiuru at a press conference on regulation concerning restaurants on 7 October](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11583550)

There was an important vote in the Finnish Parliament. MPs voted on whether they trust Minister Krista Kiuru. Minister Kiuru is responsible for Finland's health matters.

Kiuru won the confidence of Parliament and can continue as minister. The government parties, which have a majority in Parliament, voted for Kiuru. The opposition parties voted against Kiuru.

The opposition party, the National Coalition Party, said that Kiuru has handled the coronavirus measures badly. The National Coalition Party criticised, for example, the way the government told people about face masks in the spring. Finland recommended the use of face masks later than many other countries.

## Face masks for workplaces

![Helsinki Mayor Jan Vapaavuori.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_56/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7273815f7da0130441a/1602665412)

Helsinki Mayor Jan Vapaavuori. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

New coronavirus guidelines have been given to residents of the Helsinki area. Now it is recommended, for example, that face masks are used at all workplaces.

Helsinki Mayor Jan Vapaavuori says that the coronavirus is now spreading at workplaces. That is why, in Vapaavuori's view, face masks are necessary at workplaces.

In addition, it is recommended that parents use face masks in day care centres in the Helsinki area. It is good for a parent to wear a face mask when they bring or pick up a child from day care.

THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, says that there are 4 new coronavirus deaths in Finland. A total of 350 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

THL also said that there are 204 new coronavirus infections in Finland. The most new coronavirus infections are in the areas of Helsinki, Uusimaa and Vaasa.

## Coronavirus infections in Europe

![Coronavirus tests being carried out in a large hall in Spain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11532726)

Open image viewer

The coronavirus is spreading in different parts of Europe. In many countries there are thousands of new coronavirus infections a day.

For example, the Czech Republic said today that the country has more than 8,000 new coronavirus infections. That is why the Czech Republic is closing restaurants and schools.

There are also many new coronavirus infections and coronavirus deaths in Russia and Poland.

In Spain, bars and restaurants are being closed in the Catalonia region. Coronavirus restrictions were introduced in the capital, Madrid, already last week.
