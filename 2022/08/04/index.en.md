## Finland's NATO membership

![The US Capitol building and the square in front of it.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5922,h_3331,x_0,y_303/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98787562e298844daac/1659568919)

Finland's membership in the defence alliance NATO is closer again.

Now also the United States, that is, the USA, has approved Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership. The Senate voted on the matter in the USA on Wednesday.

The voting result was clear. 95 senators supported Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership, only 1 senator voted against. There are 100 senators in the Senate.

It is important for Finland and Sweden that the USA approved them for NATO. The USA is the biggest and most important country in the defence alliance NATO.

A total of 23 NATO countries have already approved Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership. NATO has 30 countries, and all of them must approve new member countries.

Finland and Sweden applied for NATO membership in May. The reason was that Russia attacked Ukraine. Finland and Sweden estimate that NATO membership will bring them security against Russia.

## Party support

![Yle's party support poll 08/2022](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3233,h_1814,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98997562ea5f2d39bb8/1659529458)

The National Coalition Party is Finland's most popular party. This is what Yle's support poll says.

In the support poll people were asked which party they intend to vote for. Over 23 percent of respondents intended to vote for the National Coalition Party.

The National Coalition Party is now an opposition party, that is, it is not in the government. The chairman of the National Coalition Party is Petteri Orpo.

The second biggest party in the support poll is the SDP. Over 20 percent of respondents intended to vote for the SDP.

The SDP is now a government party. Prime Minister Sanna Marin is the chairperson of the SDP.

Parliamentary elections will be held in Finland next spring. After the elections, the chairperson of the biggest party will probably become prime minister.

The support figures for all parties can be found on Yle's website.

## An extra child benefit

![Annika Saarikko speaks to the media.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_235/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99000062ea6a434bb30/1659580021)

Open image viewer

Minister of Finance, that is, the money minister Annika Saarikko. Photo: Sakari Piippo / Yle

Money minister Annika Saarikko proposes an extra child benefit for all families.

Saarikko proposes that families get 2 child benefits for each child in December.

In Saarikko's view families need support, because prices are rising. For example, the price of food has risen a lot.

Saarikko's proposal will quite certainly come true. It is estimated that the extra child benefit will cost the state over 100 million euros.
