## Berry picker visas

Finland has rejected most of the visa applications of berry pickers.

The reason for the rejection is the many problems with berry picking.

Berry pickers have been exploited. They have had poor conditions and low pay. There may have been human trafficking involved.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs estimates that the companies do not comply with the rules or workers' rights.

That is why Finland has rejected most visa applications from foreign berry pickers.

Finland has received approximately 2,200 applications. Of these, 1,400 applications have already been rejected.
