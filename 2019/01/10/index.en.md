Yle's survey says that support for the Finns Party has risen the most. In the new survey, support for the Finns Party is 10.2 percent. That is about 2 percentage points higher than in the previous survey.

Support for the SDP fell slightly and is now 21.2 percent. Support for the National Coalition rose and is 19.6 percent. Support for the Centre fell and is now 16.1 percent.

Support for the Greens is now 13.6 percent, for the Left Alliance 9.5, and for the Christian Democrats 4.2 percent. Support for the Blue Reform was 1.0 percent.

The support for the RKP in the survey was 2.5 percent. The survey was made for Yle by Taloustutkimus. Taloustutkimus later told Yle that there was a statistical error in the RKP support figure. It was caused by the fact that in some areas the number of respondents was too small.

## Online shopping is changing retail

![Postal parcels arrive from all over the world at the Tikkurila logistics centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10589990)

Online shopping is also a big business for Posti. Photo: Petri Vironen / Yle

Online shopping is quickly changing Finnish retail. Many international online shops already operate in Finland.

The value of online shopping grew by about 20 percent last year and is now over 4 billion euros. That is almost the same as the sales of 100 medium-sized hypermarkets. Yle collected figures from several sources.

Finns buy the most from the internet: travel, shoes, clothes, cosmetics and electronics.

## Girls in the north enjoy nature

![A girl and a dog on the bank of a river](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10586341)

Open image viewer

Nature and animals are important to girls in the north. Photo: Laura Valta / Yle

Nature has a big effect on the well-being of girls in Northern Finland.

Varpu Wiens, a researcher at the University of Oulu, has studied the well-being of girls in Northern Finland. Wiens says that winter is a hard time for some girls. In the dark northern winter, for example, school can be hard work. Many girls say that dressing and moving around are also difficult in winter.

Nature also brings joy to the girls. The girls feel that nature refreshes and calms them. Nature gives strength to the girls of the north.

Animals are important to girls in Northern Finland. The girls say that they can tell animals about their worries. Animals do not tell things to others, and they comfort the girls.

## Yle News in sign language is 25 years old

![Mikaela Tillander and Thomas Sandolm in the studio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10590736)

Open image viewer

The Yle News in sign language broadcast celebrates its 25th anniversary today.

The broadcast consists of news dispatches, which a deaf news anchor signs and a hearing journalist reads aloud in Finnish during the broadcast.

There are about 3,000 to 5,000 deaf people in Finland whose first language is sign language.

The sign language news team made a half-hour special broadcast to mark the 25th anniversary. It will be shown on television on Sunday at 11.05.

The broadcast can be watched on Yle Areena.
