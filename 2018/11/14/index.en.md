# Wednesday 14.11.2018 (radio)

Prices have risen a little. Lots of tourists are expected in Lapland at Christmas. Young people talk about relationships using different words from their parents. In Britain Prince Charles of Wales has turned 70. The sky is cloudy.

## Price rises

![A man smokes a cigarette.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10480622)

The price of tobacco has risen. Photo: Mari Kahila / Yle

Prices have risen a little. Prices in October this year were 1.5 per cent higher than in October last year. Electricity, petrol and tobacco have become the most expensive.

Some prices are now cheaper than a year ago. Children's day care and mobile phones have become the cheapest.

The figures are from Statistics Finland.

## Christmas tourists in Lapland

![Kittilä airport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10508360)

Open image viewer

Kittilä airport is ready to receive Christmas tourists. Photo: Finavia

A lot of tourists are expected in Lapland at Christmas. About 680 charter flights are coming to Lapland this Christmas. That is 15 per cent more than last Christmas.

Christmas tourists come from many countries. Most Christmas tourists come from Britain. Planes land, for example, at Santa Claus Village in Rovaniemi.

Traffic is growing most at Kittilä airport. The renovation of Kittilä airport will soon be finished, and the airport is now much bigger than before. The renovation cost 20 million euros.

## Young people's relationship vocabulary

![A young couple by the river bank.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10376135)

Open image viewer

Have we "started to be" yet? Photo: Pixabay

Language changes. Young people talk about relationships using different words from their parents, for example.

When two young people have "a thing" going, they are getting to know each other. At that stage young people meet in a way where other young people are also present.

"Säätö" means that young people already spend more time alone together. They may, for example, go for a coffee together, just the two of them.

"Aletaan oleen" means going out together. At that stage the relationship is already serious. Young people may, for example, tell their parents about the relationship and start having sex.

## Charles turns 70

![The British royal family watching the flypast from the balcony of Buckingham Palace during the RAF100 parade celebrations in London, 10 July 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10298257)

Prince Charles (left), Queen Elizabeth (centre), and Princes Harry and William (right). Photo: STR / EPA

In Britain Prince Charles of Wales has turned 70. The prince's birthday was on Wednesday.

Charles is the eldest child of Queen Elizabeth. Charles will become the ruler of Britain after Elizabeth. Queen Elizabeth is now 92 years old.

Princes William and Harry are Charles's children. The princes were born when Charles was married to Princess Diana.
