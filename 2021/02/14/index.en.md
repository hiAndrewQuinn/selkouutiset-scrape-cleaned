## Donald Trump

![Former US president Donald Trump.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11789597)

Open image viewer

The former US president Donald Trump was cleared of charges in the Senate. Photo: Tasos Katopodis / AFP

The former US president **Donald Trump** has been cleared of impeachment charges. The charges were brought because Trump urged people to go to the US Capitol on Epiphany. 5 people died in the storming of the Capitol.

The US Senate voted on the impeachment charge. 57 senators voted for the charge. 43 senators voted against the charge.

Because the Senate voted that Trump did not commit an impeachable offence, Trump could, for example, run as a candidate in US politics.

## Domestic travel

![Skiers on a slope](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10650536)

Open image viewer

Ski resorts must keep safe distances so that the coronavirus does not spread. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

The winter holiday season in Finland starts in a week. Domestic travel will become very busy, because you cannot travel abroad.

Most holiday cottages for rent have already been booked. VR has added more train trips to Lapland, because a lot of tickets have been sold.

Experts are worried about busy travel. The coronavirus situation is bad, and a new virus variant may spread. Experts recommend that people spend their skiing holiday in their home town.

## Grandparents

![Grandparents and two children](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9907893)

Open image viewer

A large part of grandparents keep in touch with their grandchildren almost every week. Photo: Pixabay

Grandparents take part a lot in the lives of their grandchildren in Finland.

A new study says that a large part of grandparents keep in touch with their grandchildren almost every week. 80 percent of grandparents take part in looking after their grandchildren, at least sometimes.

Nowadays, grandparents are perhaps expected to give a little more help than before.

## Shrovetide

![People on a sledding hill, enjoying a beautiful winter Sunday in Kaivopuisto, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11789611)

Open image viewer

On Shrovetide people go sledding downhill. There is now a lot of snow all over Finland, and sliding works well. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Today, Sunday, Shrovetide has been celebrated.

Shrovetide is a Christian tradition that is celebrated all over the world. According to tradition, on Shrovetide people prepare for fasting. In Finland people slide down hills and eat pea soup and Shrovetide buns.

Shrovetide is celebrated on Tuesday as well as on Sunday.

## Valentine's Day

![Chocolate heart](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5463084)

Open image viewer

You can give a friend a chocolate heart, for example. Photo: YLE / Arja Lento.

Today, Sunday, Valentine's Day is also celebrated.

In many other countries St Valentine's Day has been celebrated, that is, the feast of love. In Finland the theme of the day is friendship. Many people remember a friend with a message or a postcard, for example.

Valentine's Day is quite a new holiday in Finland. It got into the calendar in 1987.
