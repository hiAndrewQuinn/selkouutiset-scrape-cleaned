# Monday 11.3.2019 (radio)

Finland no longer attracts Estonian workers. The Uusimaa regional government took a position on the Helsinki–Tallinn railway tunnel. VR is buying locomotives from Switzerland. Bad driving conditions caused chain collisions in Oulu. Japan remembered the victims of a natural disaster.

## Estonian workers are now going home

![Estonians began moving to Finland in earnest at the start of the millennium, and Finland was for a long time a net gainer, but in 2017 more Estonians moved from Finland to Estonia than from Estonia to Finland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10682361)

Estonian workers have started to leave Finland. Estonians are now moving back to Estonia.

At the moment more people move from Finland to Estonia than from Estonia to Finland. The turning point came about 2 years ago.

Estonia's ambassador to Finland, Harri Tiido, says that the return migration is due to Estonia's economic growth. Estonia's economy is now growing faster than Finland's economy. Wages have risen, and at the same time the gap between Estonian and Finnish wages has become smaller.

Going home also attracts Estonians, because living costs are cheaper in Estonia than in Finland.

Ambassador Tiido says that, for example, the number of Estonian construction workers in Finland has fallen by more than 30 percent from its peak years.

## The regional government considered the Helsinki–Tallinn railway tunnel

The Uusimaa regional government has taken a position on the railway tunnel that is planned between Helsinki and Tallinn.

The regional government says that if the tunnel is built, it must run from Helsinki Central Station to Helsinki-Vantaa Airport. However, the regional government's line does not rule out other options.

There are 2 tunnel plans, and there have been several route options. For example, in the tunnel project of businessman Peter Vesterbacka, it is planned that the tunnel runs to the airport via Keilaniemi in Espoo.

## New locomotives for VR

![Illustration of a new diesel locomotive](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10682819)

Open image viewer

New diesel locomotives are being bought from Switzerland. Photo: VR Group

VR intends to buy new locomotives.

VR is buying 60 new diesel locomotives from the Swiss company Stadler. The value of the purchase is about 200 million euros.

The new locomotives are designed to work even in 40 degrees of frost. The design has paid attention, for example, to the fact that ice and snow do not build up on the locomotive's chassis.

The new locomotives will mainly be used for freight traffic.

## Chain collisions on Route 4

![Collision scene in Oulu on Route 4 on 11.3.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10682634)

A chain collision involving more than 20 cars happened on Route 4 in Oulu. Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

Today 2 large chain collisions happened on Route 4 in Oulu. Nobody died in the collisions, but 13 people in total were taken to hospital.

In the second collision 21 vehicles were involved, and in the other 5.

Driving conditions in the Oulu area have been bad.

## Japan remembered a natural disaster

![Old man at a memorial to the tsunami dead.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7868771)

Open image viewer

An old man at a memorial to those killed in the tsunami. Photo: Kimimasa Mayama / EPA

In Japan, a natural disaster from 8 years ago has been remembered at several commemorations.

In March 2011, Japan was struck by a strong earthquake, which was followed by a tsunami, that is, a huge wave. The natural disaster caused a nuclear accident at the Fukushima nuclear power plant.

Almost 20,000 people died in the disaster.
