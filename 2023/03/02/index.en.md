# Thursday 2.3.2023 (radio)

Yle's survey says support for the 3 largest parties is almost even. The dock workers' strike ended. A rail strike threatens to begin on 20 March. Fortum's result was worse than before. The weather is mostly dry.

## Party support

Finland's most popular party is still the opposition party, the National Coalition. In Yle's survey the National Coalition's support is almost 21 percent. The National Coalition's support has fallen.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin's party, the SDP, is in 2nd place in the survey. The SDP's support is about 20 percent.

The opposition party, the Finns Party, is in 3rd place. The Finns Party's support is 19 percent. The support of the SDP and the Finns Party has risen.

Parliamentary elections are in a month.

## Dock workers' agreement

![The Port of Hamina-Kotka in Kotka.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_333/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10800036400837287706/1677755896)

The strike of the port dock workers ends.

The Automobile and Transport Workers' Union approved the new working conditions for dock workers on Wednesday. Dock workers' pay will rise by 6.3 percent over 2 years. Workers will also receive 1,100 euros this month in addition to their other pay.

The bus strike, on the other hand, continues until next week's Friday, if the labour dispute is not settled before then. The labour dispute has been mediated again today, on Thursday.

## Rail strike

![VR train leaving Kuopio railway station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106621063d8cf980f889/1675154180)

Open image viewer

Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Locomotive drivers rejected the National Conciliator's compromise proposal for the labour dispute in the sector. At the same time the rail strike was postponed by 2 weeks, so that there is more time to mediate the dispute.

The locomotive drivers' strike begins on 20 March, if no agreement is found before then. Rail traffic will almost completely stop if the strike begins. Mediation of the labour dispute continues next Tuesday.

Fortum's result, however, improved during the last part of the year.
