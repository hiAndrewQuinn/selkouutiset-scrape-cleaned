# Tuesday 10.9.2019 (radio)

Jutta Urpilainen is a candidate for EU Commissioner. On Wednesday there has been bad news about the economy. Finland's 100-year-old Constitution has been celebrated. The weather is turning autumnal.

## Jutta Urpilainen is going to be a Commissioner

![Commissioner candidate Jutta Urpilainen was heard by the Grand Committee of Parliament at the Turku Europe Forum on Friday 30 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10964033)

Jutta Urpilainen may become an EU Commissioner, who will be responsible for international partnerships. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

Finland's Jutta Urpilainen is a candidate for EU Commissioner. Urpilainen is going to become the Commissioner who is responsible for international partnerships.

The new president of the European Commission, Ursula von der Leyen, said that Urpilainen was earlier a special envoy for peace mediation.

Urpilainen's job in the Commission is new. Among other things, Urpilainen is supposed to make a new Africa strategy for the EU.

Von der Leyen said on Monday that the Commission will have 13 women and 14 men. The Commission is the most equal in history.

The list of Commissioners is only a proposal. The European Parliament still has to approve the candidates.

## Tuesday was a day of bad economic news

![UPM Rauma logo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7502414)

Open image viewer

The fate of the Rauma paper mill worries the workers and the whole town. Photo: Iida Pakkanen / Yle

Forest company UPM plans to close a paper machine in Rauma. The machine makes paper for magazines.

Co-operation negotiations, that is, change negotiations, have started, and 179 people may lose their jobs. Rauma will keep 2 paper machines.

Many other companies have also announced change negotiations.

Forest company Stora Enso is starting change negotiations about layoffs at the Anjala paper mill in Kouvola. The Aktia bank plans to cut 110 employees.

The construction company Lehto has suffered losses, and the need for cuts may be 150 person-years. A person-year means the work of one full-time employee in one year.

## Finland's Constitution is 100 years old

![The 100th anniversary of the Constitution is celebrated in Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10963850)

Open image viewer

Parliament has celebrated Finland's Constitution, which turned 100. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

Today, Parliament has celebrated Finland's 100-year-old Constitution. The Constitution is the law of an independent state. It says who holds power in the country.

The Finnish Constitution says that state power belongs to the people, who are represented by Parliament.

The highest power was given to Parliament in 1919. Before that, there was a dispute about whether Finland would become a republic or a kingdom. The republicans won the dispute, and Finland became a republic.

## Summer weather turns into autumn

![A summer landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10892450)

Open image viewer

The summer weather has lasted unusually long. Now the weather is starting to turn towards autumn. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Tuesday has been the latest hot day in Finland's measurement history. In Lappeenranta, 26.2 degrees was measured in the afternoon. The old record was from 1947. Then it was one degree less.

From now on, the weather will become autumnal and maybe cooler. On Wednesday, it will be about 15 degrees in southern Finland. In the north, there may be even over 20 degrees in some places.

At the weekend, 3 low-pressure systems will pass over Finland. They will bring rain and wind. The temperature will be between 10 and 17 degrees.
