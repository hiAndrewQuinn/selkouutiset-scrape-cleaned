# Tuesday 25.9.2018 (radio)

The court has remanded 2 men who were caught in a police operation in the Turku area. OP is starting co-operation negotiations with head office staff. Pope Francis has visited Tallinn. The Consumer Agency has received many complaints about the Viagogo online service. The first snow has fallen in Lapland.

## Two men remanded in custody

![Start of the custody hearing, faces covered, and lawyer Tuomas Sunnari.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10422724)

The district court decided on remand on Tuesday. Photo: Marjatta Rautio / Yle

The court has remanded 2 men who were caught in a police operation in the Turku area.

The police suspect the men of money laundering and tax crimes. Money laundering is an attempt to hide that the money was obtained through crimes.

The men themselves say that they have not committed a crime. One man is an Estonian citizen, and the other is a Russian citizen.

The target of the police operation at the weekend was a company called Airiston Helmi. The operation was large, and it attracted a lot of attention.

## OP starts co-operation negotiations

![Head office of Osuuspankki on Teollisuuskatu in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10422518)

Open image viewer

OP head office in Helsinki. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

OP Group intends to organise employees' tasks in a new way. That is why OP is starting co-operation negotiations, that is, negotiations with employees.

The negotiations concern 6,000 employees. The negotiations concern OP's head office, but they do not concern the cooperative banks.

Co-operation negotiations often mean that some employees are made redundant. OP says that its co-operation negotiations are more about a new organisation than about layoffs.

With the help of the new organisation, OP wants to save 100 million euros a year. OP may, for example, reduce the work that it buys from other firms.

## The Pope visited Estonia

![The Pope in an open car in the middle of a crowd.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10420816)

The Pope came to Estonia from Latvia, where he visited on Monday. Photo: Alessandro di Meo / EPA

Pope Francis has visited Tallinn, in Estonia. On Tuesday the Pope held a mass at Freedom Square in Tallinn. A large group of Finnish Catholics also travelled to Tallinn and took part in the Pope's mass.

The Pope is the leader of the Catholic Church. He often visits various parts of the world. In the Baltics, the Pope visited Lithuania and Latvia as well as Estonia.

## Complaints have been made about Viagogo

![Screenshot of the Viagogo.com/fi website.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10418108)

Open image viewer

Screenshot of the Viagogo.com/fi website. Photo: Viagogo.com/fi

The Consumer Agency has received many complaints about the Viagogo online service. Viagogo sells entrance tickets to various events

People have complained to the Consumer Agency that Viagogo has taken a larger sum from them than it stated when they started buying the ticket. Viagogo also often sells tickets at a higher price than the official ticket seller.

Viagogo is a Swiss service. It has ended up in court in Switzerland.

## Snow fell in Lapland

The first snow has fallen in Lapland. Snow fell early on Tuesday morning at Kilpisjärvi. 2 centimetres of snow fell.

More snow may fall in Lapland on Wednesday. The day is rainy, and in at least part of Lapland the rain is snow or sleet.
