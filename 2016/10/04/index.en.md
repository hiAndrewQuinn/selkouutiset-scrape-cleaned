## Rich countries do poorly at taking responsibility for refugees

![An old man sits on a wooden box, with a boy playing and temporary shelters in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9207699)

Open image viewer

Refugees in temporary housing in Athens, Greece. Photo: Yannis Kolesidis / EPA

Only a small number of countries bear the responsibility for the world's refugees. The human rights organisation Amnesty says that ten (10) countries take care of 56 per cent of all of the world's refugees.

The most refugees are in Jordan, where there are 2.7 million of them. In Turkey there are 2.5 million refugees, and in Pakistan 1.6 million.

Amnesty says that the biggest problem is not the refugees themselves. The problem is that rich countries take in only a few refugees. Amnesty says that a country's wealth and population must be taken into account when responsibility for refugees is shared.

There are more than 21 million refugees in the world.

## Fujitsu and Ericsson are firing workers

![Fujitsu logo on the side of a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8587901)

Open image viewer

Fujitsu had co-determination negotiations last summer. Then almost 140 people were given notice. Photo: Mauritz Antin / EPA

The IT company Fujitsu is starting co-determination negotiations again. The company estimates that it will cut 400 jobs in Finland over three (3) years.

At the moment Fujitsu has 2,600 employees in Finland and the Baltics. Last summer the company gave notice to 140 employees.

Also the network equipment company Ericsson is cutting jobs. Co-determination negotiations concern 175 people in Finland. Most of them are in the capital area, in Kirkkonummi. Some cuts will also come in northern Finland, in Oulu. The Oulu unit is to be closed slowly.

Ericsson has 900 employees in Finland.

## Cars have a lot of IT

##

![Diagram graphics](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9203640)

Open image viewer

A "connected car" means that the car is connected to other road users and to real-time maps, for example, through the network. The car gets information, for example about traffic jams. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Many cars already have a touch screen. With the touch screen you can control, for example, windows and navigators.

Cars can also be on the information network and connected to the environment. Information about traffic jams, dangerous weather or road conditions travels in the network in real time between cars. You can also connect your own devices to the car's equipment.

New technology is now popular, and there is a big market for it. Many Finnish firms develop software and have done well in the market.

Last year more than six (6) million cars connected to the information network were made. In two years' time perhaps 12 million will be made, and in 2020 perhaps already 60 million.

## Research once again confirmed that berries are healthy

![Cranberries](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8342279)

Open image viewer

October is the time of cranberries. Photo: Kirsti Metsävainio

Berries contain substances that may prevent coronary heart disease. Earlier it was known that these substances may prevent diabetes, Parkinson's disease and heart attacks.

Berries contain, for example, anthocyanidins. They can be found, for example, in blueberries, cranberries and grapes.

An American study shows that the substances in berries lower the risk of getting some illnesses by half.
