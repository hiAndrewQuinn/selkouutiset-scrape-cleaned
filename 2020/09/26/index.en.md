# Saturday 26.9.2020 (radio)

Hybrid threats are part of everyday international politics. The net wealth of Finnish households grew. WHO warns about corona deaths. A statue of Juha Mieto has been unveiled.

## Finland cannot avoid hybrid threats either

![Teija Tiilikainen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11565116)

Open image viewer

Teija Tiilikainen. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Hybrid threats are part of everyday international politics. This is how Teija Tiilikainen assesses it. She leads the European Centre of Excellence for Countering Hybrid Threats.

A hybrid threat means that in some country, for example, harm, uncertainty or instability is caused.

Methods include spreading false information, various kinds of pressure, or attacks on information networks.

Tiilikainen says that the situation is confusing right now, when the US presidential election is soon. It is known from the 2016 election how Russia influenced online on behalf of Donald Trump. Trump was elected president.

Tiilikainen stressed that hybrid threats also threaten Finland.

Tiilikainen was a guest on Yle's Ykkösaamu on Saturday.

## Household wealth grew

![Buildings in Punavuori, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7040555f23ca8a01502/1624950612)

Open image viewer

Households have become wealthier while debts grow. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The net wealth of Finnish households grew quickly last year, Statistics Finland says. Net wealth means wealth after the household's debts have been deducted from its assets.

Households had financial assets, that is, for example cash, shares and fund units, of almost 332 billion euros.

Other assets were over 506 billion. Other assets include, for example, residential buildings and land.

During the year, financial assets grew by over 27 billion euros and other assets by almost 18 billion.

At the same time, households have more and more debt.

## WHO warns about corona

![Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4123,h_2319,x_0,y_238/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-43747659ec37043fce7/1508654141)

WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus. Photo: EPA/MARTIAL TREZZINI

The World Health Organization, WHO, warns about corona deaths. The number could rise to over 2 million across the world if infections are not kept under control.

Johns Hopkins University reports that almost a million corona deaths have so far been recorded in the world.

WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus rejects claims that the virus came from a laboratory.

The Director-General says that, based on research and science, the virus comes from nature.

93 new coronavirus infections have been reported in Finland

## Juha Mieto got a statue

![The Legend statue designed by sculptor Pekka Jylhä is unveiled on 26.9.20 in Kurikka.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_208/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7235025f6f2f8f1cfde/1601122286)

Open image viewer

The Legend rises to a height of just under 4 metres. The bronze Juha Mieto on top of it is 197 centimetres tall. The figure was modelled on the Salpausselkä Games of 1978. Photo: Merja Siirilä / Yle

The statue of the ski legend Juha Mieto has been unveiled. It was unveiled on Saturday in Kurikka in South Ostrobothnia. Mieto comes from Kurikka.

The statue called Legend was designed by sculptor Pekka Jylhä.

Mieto won 9 Olympic and world championship skiing medals.

His most famous defeat was the 15-kilometre race at the 1980 Lake Placid Winter Olympics. Mieto lost to Sweden's Thomas Wassberg by just 1 hundredth of a second.
