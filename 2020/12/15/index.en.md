## Vitamin D

![Vitamin D capsules from a jar onto a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1800,h_1013,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-1929954de0d5c3877d/1423838603)

Open image viewer

Vitamin D tablets are needed in Finland during the dark winter. Photo: Yle, Tero Kyllönen

The Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district, that is, HUS, recommends vitamin D for older people.

HUS says that everyone over 70 should take 20 milligrams of vitamin D every day.

Vitamin D may reduce the risk of respiratory tract infection, and that is important in the coronavirus time. A coronavirus infection can become very serious if the patient's airways also have other bacterial or viral infections.

Natural vitamin D comes from sunlight and from some foods, for example fatty fish and hen's eggs. In Finland, many people also need vitamin D tablets. For example, dark-skinned people and older people often suffer from a lack of vitamin D.

## The Foreign Minister's confidence

![Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto briefly left the plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11696950)

Open image viewer

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto has won Parliament's confidence. This means that Haavisto can carry on as foreign minister.

In the Parliament vote, the government parties voted for Haavisto. The opposition parties voted against Haavisto.

The question was whether Foreign Minister Haavisto acted wrongly in the al-Hol matter. Haavisto wanted to get Finnish children out of the Syrian al-Hol camp, where family members of Islamic State terrorists live. Haavisto also tried to move an official who disagreed with Haavisto on the matter to another job.

Last week Haavisto received criticism from the Constitutional Law Committee of Parliament because of the al-Hol case. Now the opposition party Finns Party wanted the whole of Parliament to vote on Haavisto's confidence.
