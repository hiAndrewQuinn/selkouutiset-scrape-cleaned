# Friday 13.7.2018 (radio)

The top-level meeting in Helsinki affects traffic. Border checks will also be added because of the top-level meeting. There was a stabbing in Joensuu. Jarno Kokko is the new tango king. The summit on Monday falls on a beautiful summer day.

## The top-level meeting closes the market square

![Suomenlinna ferry at the Market Square shore in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10298823)

Open image viewer

Helsinki's Market Square is closed on Monday because of the top-level meeting. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Traffic in central Helsinki will not run normally on Monday. The reason is security measures, when the presidents of the USA and Russia meet in Helsinki.

For example, Helsinki's Market Square is closed on Monday. There is no market trading, and you cannot move around the Market Square area normally.

There will also be many other changes to Helsinki's traffic on Monday.

## Checks are carried out at the borders

![Russian tourists queuing at the Nuijamaa border checkpoint for passport control.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9975757)

Open image viewer

During the summit, passengers will be checked more often than usual. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

Finland is also adding border checks during the Helsinki top-level meeting.

The checks are part of the security measures for the top-level meeting. Checks will be carried out especially at the ports and airports of Helsinki and Turku.

Not all passengers will be checked, but passengers should allow a little extra time. It is also a good idea to bring your passport.

The checks will continue until Tuesday.

## A stabbing happened in Joensuu

![Police car in front of a restaurant in central Joensuu on 13.7.2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10302541)

Open image viewer

The stabbing happened at the Ilona restaurant. Photo: Jouki Väinämö / Yle

A stabbing has happened in Joensuu in Eastern Finland. The stabbing took place in a restaurant. The Eastern Finland Police say that there were 2 perpetrators.

The attackers first stabbed 2 people in the restaurant's smoking area. When the restaurant's janitor went there, the attackers also stabbed him. Everyone was seriously injured.

The attackers managed to escape from the restaurant.

## Jarno Kokko is the tango king

![Tango king of 2018 Jarno Kokko](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10302489)

Open image viewer

Jarno Kokko won the men's singing competition at the Tango Market. Photo: Tuomo Rintamaa / Yle

The music event Tango Market is taking place in Seinäjoki. On Thursday a new tango king was chosen at the Tango Market. The new tango king is Jarno Kokko.

Jarno Kokko is a 37-year-old singer from Espoo. Kokko studied music at the Oulu Conservatory and the Sibelius Academy.

The Tango Market continues at the weekend. On Saturday is the women's tango singing competition. The winner of the women's competition will become the new tango queen.

The Tango Market is held every summer in Seinäjoki, Ostrobothnia. Finns like tango music. The Tango Market has been part of Finnish summers for more than 30 years.

## The weather favours the top-level meeting

![Weather map for the weekend and Monday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10304205)

Helsinki is getting ready for Monday's top-level meeting.

Yle meteorologist Joonas Koskela says that at least the weather on Monday will be good. Presidents Trump and Putin will meet in sunny Helsinki.

Monday is also a very warm day. In Helsinki it may be as much as 27 degrees. The nights are warm too.

It is a beautiful summer day that comes just at the right time, says Joonas Koskela.
