Interior Minister Krista Mikkonen already asked last week for a review of the security sector. Mikkonen asked the National Police Board for the review.

The review concerns how the police monitor the activities of security sector firms and individual guards.

On the basis of the review, further measures will be assessed.

The Interior Minister says that the death at Iso Omena in Espoo was shocking. A woman died at the shopping centre on Saturday in a situation where guards had apprehended her.

Mikkonen's request for a review concerned the whole security sector.

Education Minister Li Andersson also demands a review of the activities of the security sector.

There has been a lot of other news about the problems of the security sector this winter.

## Young forests

![A small forest plot where the first thinning has been done too heavily.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105567963b555474f7d2/1672830817)

Open image viewer

This is what a forest that is too sparse for cultivation looks like. The forest owner loses money from a second thinning, because it cannot be done in such a forest. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Young forests are being thinned illegally hard. The quality of cutting in cultivation forests has got worse every year across the whole country.

The Forest Centre investigated in more detail what the forests were like after the first thinnings in Central Finland.

Almost 60 percent were against the Forest Act, and in the rest there was something to criticise too.

No site met all the requirements of the forest management recommendation.

For example, a young spruce forest is worth thinning a little under 10 years after planting.

## Batteries

![Batteries](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6126871)

Open image viewer

Small devices and old batteries and accumulators can be taken for recycling free of charge. They can be taken to many shops, and the larger ones to municipal collection points. Photo: Arja Lento / YLE

Batteries and accumulators can start a fire if they are handled or stored incorrectly. Last autumn alone 5 such fires were recorded.

However, there are more lithium battery fires than in the official statistics.

This is how the Emergency Services College and Paristokierrätys, that is, Recser, estimate it.

A battery that has been without charging for a long time may short-circuit when it is charged again. It is worth taping the battery terminals together and taking batteries to collection immediately.

## Blood service turns 75

![Blood donor Marianna Hukanhauta talks with nurse Hanna Hill, who is in charge of the Oulu blood service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_17/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-95607362836f02e45ab/1652781024)

Open image viewer

Blood donor Marianna Hukanhauta talks with nurse Hanna Hill, who is in charge of the Oulu blood service. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

The Finnish Red Cross Blood Service turns 75 this year.

Blood began to be systematically collected in Finland during the war years. The Blood Service was founded in 1948.

The Blood Service delivers blood products and stem cell transplants to hospitals every day, around the clock.

A blood bus is also planned. Its purpose is to tour the capital region and workplaces. In this way donating blood is easier for volunteers.

In Finland 800 blood donors are needed every weekday.
