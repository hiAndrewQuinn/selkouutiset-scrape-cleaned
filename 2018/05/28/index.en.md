# Monday 28.5.2018 (radio)

The EU may ban a range of plastic single-use items. Girls have got better grades than boys in this spring's matriculation exams. An immigrant saved a child who nearly fell from a balcony in France. There have been more hot days in May this year than in previous years.

## The EU may ban plastic items

![Plastic juice straws.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10226828)

Open image viewer

Plastic juice straws are disappearing from Europe. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The European Union intends to reduce plastic waste. That is why the EU may ban a range of plastic single-use items. In the future, plastic juice straws, forks and cotton buds, for example, may be banned.

The EU wants to reduce plastic waste because plastic pollutes nature. Many pieces of plastic waste end up in the sea, where they harm sea animals.

## Girls do well in the matriculation exams

![A vegan student cap at Kallio Upper Secondary School.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10224292)

Open image viewer

The girls have reason to celebrate. Photo: Kari Ahotupa / Yle

Girls have got better grades in the matriculation exams than boys. Yle News compared the grades of girls and boys in this spring's matriculation exams.

Girls were better than boys in almost all subjects. Girls were clearly better in health education and psychology. Girls also did better than boys in the physics exam, for example.

However, boys were better in some subjects. Boys did better than girls in the English and advanced Russian exams. Boys were also better in chemistry and advanced mathematics.

## An immigrant saved a child

![President Emmanuel Macron and Mamoudou Gassama met at the Élysée Palace on Monday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10226212)

Open image viewer

President Emmanuel Macron met Mamoudou Gassama. Photo: Thibault Camus / AFP

A man saved a child in an unusual way in France.

A 4-year-old child was in danger of falling from a 5th-floor balcony. The child was already outside the balcony, but he was holding on to the balcony railing.

Then a Malian immigrant decided to save the child. Mamoudou Gassama climbed up the wall of the apartment block to the 5th floor and lifted the child onto the balcony.

French President Emmanuel Macron has promised Gassama an award for his good deed. Gassama will get French citizenship.

There is a video of the incident on the Yle News website. The video shows how Gassama climbs to the 5th floor.

## A hot May

![View from Seurasaari to Seurasaarenselkä on a beautiful day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10224882)

Open image viewer

May has been very warm. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

On Monday a new weather record was set. There have been more hot days in May this year than in previous years.

Monday was already the 13th hot day this month. On a hot day the temperature is over 25 degrees somewhere in Finland.

The old record was from 1984. Back then there were 12 hot days in May.

The warm weather will continue. In Southern Finland there may still be heat on Tuesday and Wednesday.

In Northern Finland there will be rain and thunderstorms on Tuesday. After that the weather will get colder.

During the end of the week the weather will get colder in the south too. At the weekend it may rain.
