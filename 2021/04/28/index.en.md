# Wednesday 28.4.2021 (radio)

The disputes among the governing parties have been settled. In Latvia, 8 people died in a fire. Traffic Safety warns about moose collisions. The weather is cool.

The disputes among the governing parties have been settled. On Wednesday, the governing parties said that they want to continue cooperating.

The governing parties have been arguing about state funds. The governing parties have disagreed, for example, about how much money the state can borrow.

In Finland, there has even been speculation in recent days that the government may not be able to continue its work. Now Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the government's work will continue.

The governing parties will tell more about the harmony on Thursday. Then it will be heard how the government plans to improve Finland's economic situation.

## Fire in Latvia

![Map of Latvia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11904975)

Open image viewer

Photo: Harri Vähäkangas / Yle

There has been a bad fire in Riga, the capital of Latvia. 8 people died in the fire.

The fire started early on Wednesday morning in a hostel. The hostel was illegal.

An attempt was made to carry out a fire inspection in the hostel in winter, but the hostel did not let the inspectors in.

The police have been keeping an eye on the hostel for a long time. Alcohol and drugs have been sold in the hostel. The hostel has also broken corona restrictions.

The Latvian police have started a criminal investigation because of the fire.

## Moose collisions

![Traffic sign warning of moose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9837561)

Open image viewer

A traffic sign warns of moose. Photo: Ian Shaw / AOP

Traffic Safety warns about moose collisions. The risk of a moose collision grows from May onwards.

Cars and moose often collide late in the evening, before or after sunset. Deer and roe deer are also a danger to cars.

Traffic Safety reminds drivers that in dangerous places they should drive slowly enough and concentrate on driving. Dangerous places include, for example, spots where a game fence begins or ends. A game fence prevents moose from getting onto the road.
