# Wednesday 4.12.2019 (radio)

Finland will get a new prime minister perhaps next week. Almost all Finns use Yle's services. Millions of people need help. A documentary film has been made about the northern lights. The weather is getting warmer.

## SDP

![SDP's Minister of Transport and Communications and Deputy Chair Sanna Marin in Parliament on 4 December](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11103571)

Sanna Marin has support to become prime minister. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finland will get a new prime minister perhaps next week. The previous prime minister, Antti Rinne, resigned on Tuesday.

The decision on the prime minister is made within the SDP. The SDP is the largest party in Parliament and in the current government.

Two politicians in the SDP want to become prime minister. They are Sanna Marin and Antti Lindtman.

Sanna Marin is Minister of Transport and Communications. Antti Lindtman leads the SDP group in Parliament.

Yle has found out how much support Marin and Lindtman have within the SDP. Marin has slightly more support for the post of prime minister.

Almost all Finns use Yle's services. 96 per cent of Finns use Yle's services every week.

Yle is a media company that has radio and television channels. Yle's TV channels include TV1 and TV2, and Radio Suomi is one of Yle's radio channels.

Yle's online services in particular have gained more audiences. Yle Areena and the Yle News websites are popular.

The popularity of television has fallen a little. Young people in particular now watch television less than before.

## Need for help

There are many people in the world who need help. Next year, almost 170 million people will need help. That is more than for a long time.

Diseases, wars and conflicts, and climate change are the reasons why help is needed in different parts of the world.

The UN, that is, the United Nations, asks rich countries to give help to the world's poor.

## Northern lights

![Northern lights shining in Lapland](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_253/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5455305c75467db9e39/1551189688)

Open image viewer

Northern lights in the sky. Photo: Vesa Vaarama

A new documentary film has been made about the northern lights.

The northern lights are light phenomena in the sky. In Finland, the northern lights are seen most in Lapland.

The new film tells how the northern lights are formed. It also tells why the northern lights can damage satellites and communications networks.

So a documentary film has been made about the northern lights. The film can be watched on Yle Areena, and it will also be shown on TV1 on Wednesday at 19:00.

## The weather

Warm air is flowing into Finland. In Lapland the temperature is below zero, and elsewhere it is above zero.

The weather is quite cloudy. In the east there will be drizzle. On Friday, rain will move across the southern part of the country.

The end of the week will be windy.
