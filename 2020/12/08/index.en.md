# Tuesday 8.12.2020 (radio)

Britain started coronavirus vaccinations. The coronavirus is spreading in Finland. Finavia is cutting staff. A record number of parcels went through the Post. Finns' trust in Yle has grown.

## Coronavirus vaccinations in Britain

![Margaret Keenan receives a coronavirus vaccine at Coventry hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11686342)

90-year-old Margaret Keenan received a coronavirus vaccination. Photo: Jacob King / EPA

Vaccinations against the coronavirus have started in Britain.

The first vaccine was given to 90-year-old Margaret Keenan. Keenan's birthday is next week, and she said that the vaccine was the best birthday present for her.

Britain has ordered 40 million doses of the vaccine from the drug companies Pfizer and BioNTech. First, healthcare and care home workers and people over 80 years old will be vaccinated.

## Finland's coronavirus situation

![A nurse helps another nurse put on a protective mask at the main health centre in Kuopio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11648121)

A nurse helps another nurse put on a mask. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

Many new coronavirus infections have been registered in Finland. About 360 infections have been registered today.

Varsinais-Suomi, that is, the Turku area, has now moved to the spread stage of the coronavirus epidemic. Of Finland's regions, only Eastern Savonia, Northern Savonia and Lapland are still in the basic stage. The other regions are in the accelerating or spread stage of the epidemic.

In Pieksämäki in Southern Savonia, many coronavirus infections have been found in an older people's care home. There are 26 infections in total. There are infections among both the staff and the residents of the care home.

## Finavia is making redundancies

![A Finavia sign at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11251862)

Open image viewer

Finavia has 21 airports in different parts of Finland. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The airport company Finavia reports redundancies and layoffs. Finavia says that it is making 110 people redundant. In addition, it is continuing extensive layoffs.

Finavia says that the cuts are due to the coronavirus crisis.

Finavia has 21 airports in different parts of Finland.

## The Post's parcel record

![Postal parcels on a conveyor belt](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10549198)

Open image viewer

The Post handled a record number of parcels last week. Photo: Anne Elhaimer / Yle

The Post has set a new record in handling parcels.

The Post says that 1.9 million parcels went through it last week. That is more than ever before in one week.

The Post says that Christmas shopping has moved even more online because of the coronavirus. In a survey by the Post, more than 2.5 million Finns intended to buy Christmas presents from online shops this year.

## Trust in Yle

![Yle's link tower in Pasila, Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9475401)

Open image viewer

Yle's link tower in Pasila, Helsinki. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Finns' trust in the Finnish Broadcasting Company has grown during the coronavirus year.

In a Yle survey, 71 per cent of respondents said that they trust Yle very much or quite a lot. That is 4 percentage points more than the year before. About 90 per cent also said that Yle has succeeded at its public service task at least quite well.

According to the survey, 96 per cent of people over 15 years old use Yle's services and content every week.
