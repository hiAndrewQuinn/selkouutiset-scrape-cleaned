# Friday 17.1.2020 (radio)

Rain has helped put out the forest fires in Australia. Finnish solar cookers are helping to protect forests in Kenya. Fewer babies are being born in China. This winter's weather is bad for berries. The darkest time of winter, the polar night, is over.

## Rain falls in Australia

![Environmental activist Ken Stewart in the yard of his house in the Budgong area of New South Wales.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11160912)

The fire did a lot of damage in Australia. Photo: Saeed Khan / AFP

It has rained in Australia at the end of the week. Australians have been waiting for rain for a long time, because the country has had serious forest fires.

Now the rain is helping to put out the forest fires.

The fire managed to burn large areas and thousands of homes. Over 20 people died in the fires. Australia's animals, for example koalas, have also suffered from the fires.

## Solar cookers save trees in Kenya

![A person lifts the lid of a pot that is in a solar cooker.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11164255)

Open image viewer

A solar cooker cooks food with the heat of the sun. Photo: Yle

Finnish solar cookers are helping in Kenya, in Africa. The solar cookers help to protect Kenya's forests.

Many of Kenya's forests have disappeared, because people need firewood to cook food.

A solar cooker, however, works without firewood. In a solar cooker, the heat of the sun cooks the food. It saves wood.

The solar cooker was designed in Finland, and Finns have donated solar cookers to Kenya.

## Fewer babies are born in China

![A mother and child on a bus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11162646)

Open image viewer

Chinese families do not have many children. Photo: AOP

Few babies are being born in China. Last year 14.7 million babies were born in China. So few babies have not been born for 70 years.

The number of people in China has still grown, however. Last year the population grew by almost 5 million.

There are now 1.4 billion Chinese people.

## The weather is not good for berries

![Lingonberry](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9829761)

Open image viewer

Next summer we will see how the berries have survived the winter. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

The weather in Finland has not been good for berries this winter. In a large part of the country the weather is warm and there is no snow.

Plants, however, need snow. Snow protects plants from frost. If there is no snow and a frost comes, the plants suffer.

It cannot yet be said how well garden berries will survive the winter. Lingonberries can stand bad weather a little better than bilberries.

## The polar night ended in Lapland

![Govva Njuorggámis 17.1.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10602827)

Open image viewer

The polar night can also be beautiful. Photo: Kirsti Länsman / Yle

The darkest time of winter, the polar night, is over.

The polar night also ended on Friday in Utsjoki, in Lapland. Utsjoki is the northernmost municipality in Finland.

During the polar night the sun does not rise in the sky at all. In Finland the polar night is in midwinter in Lapland.

In Utsjoki the polar night began on 25 November. The polar night ended in Utsjoki on Friday at 11.56. At that time the sun rose in the sky for a short while.
