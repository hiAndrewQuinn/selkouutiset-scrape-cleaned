# Tuesday 21.8. 2018 (radio)

Fewer people are receiving social assistance. The number of unemployed people is falling. The Finnish army wants vegetarian food. The police are monitoring driving speeds. 50 years since the occupation of Czechoslovakia.

## Social assistance

![Kela logo in a window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10286680)

Open image viewer

Students receive social assistance less often than before. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The number of people receiving social assistance has decreased.

Fewer people received social assistance in the summer than a year ago. According to Kela, the reason is that students receive social assistance less often than before. The law changed last year.

Although the Finnish economy is improving, still over 150,000 people receive social assistance. Many of them are unemployed.

Basic social assistance can be received by a person or a family whose money is not enough for the most important everyday expenses.

## Unemployment

![Man in the lobby of a TE Office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10114562)

Open image viewer

The number of unemployed people is falling. Photo: Emilia Malin / Yle

The number of unemployed people is decreasing. This is what Statistics Finland and the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment say.

Statistics Finland says that there were 183,000 unemployed people in July. That is 24,000 fewer than a year ago.

The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment also says that there are fewer unemployed people now.

Statistics Finland and the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment publish their own statistics on unemployment every month.

## Vegetarian food in the army

![Soldiers in a food queue in the army.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10363451)

Open image viewer

The Finnish army is increasing the eating of vegetarian food. Photo: Katriina Laine / Yle

More vegetarian food is eaten in the Finnish army.

Starting this autumn, the army will introduce a vegetarian food day once a week. Conscripts eat, for example, potatoes, pasta and soy, but no meat.

More vegetarian food is served because it is healthy, and because meat production affects climate change.

There are about 20,000 conscripts in Finland. Only 88 of them are vegetarians. Less vegetarian food is eaten in Lapland, but more in Helsinki. This is what a study says, which the army made last year.

## Traffic monitoring

![Tin police officer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10174545)

Open image viewer

The police are watching for speeding. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The police monitor traffic speeds around the clock.

The police will start monitoring tomorrow, Wednesday, at six in the morning. The monitoring will end on Thursday morning.

The police are visible in traffic and also monitor speeds with speed cameras in various parts of Finland. The police monitor most the places where accidents have happened, as well as schools.

The police also go to places where people often drive too fast.

## 50 years since the occupation of Czechoslovakia

![Protesters block Soviet soldiers from entering the offices of the Czechoslovak radio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10248364)

Open image viewer

Protesters block Soviet soldiers from entering the offices of the Czechoslovak radio. Photo: Ullstein Bild / AOP

Today it is exactly 50 years since the occupation of Czechoslovakia.

The occupation of Czechoslovakia began on 21 August 1968. Then troops from the Soviet Union and four other countries came to Prague, the capital of Czechoslovakia.

The people of Czechoslovakia had wanted freedom and reforms.

Today in the present-day Czech Republic, people laid flowers in front of the country's public broadcasting company. Some protested against the country's current prime minister.

Czechoslovakia no longer exists, because the country split into the Czech Republic and Slovakia.
