# Monday 11.6.2018 (radio)

The meeting of the leaders of the USA and North Korea begins in Singapore. Many pensioners have too little money. A test for search and rescue dogs was held in Vantaa at the weekend. In Hamina, thieves returned goods that they stole from a youth café.

## The Singapore meeting

The meeting of the leaders of the USA and North Korea begins in Singapore. The meeting is on Tuesday.

US President Donald Trump and North Korean leader Kim Jong-un will at first talk just the two of them. Only interpreters are present. After that, more people will take part in the meeting.

Trump and Kim will talk about North Korea's nuclear weapons. The USA wants North Korea to give up its nuclear weapons.

People around the world are anxious about how the meeting will go. Nobody can say what Trump and Kim think or what they intend to do. They are very unusual leaders.

## Poor pensioners

![A senior's legs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10244616)

Open image viewer

Pensioners often have a shortage of money. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Here in Finland many pensioners have too little money. The information comes from a new survey, whose respondents were pensioners.

In the survey, half of the pensioners said that they have difficulty making their money last. About 10 percent of pensioners said that they have big money problems. Some pensioners cannot even afford all their medicines.

The survey was carried out last autumn. There were almost 3,000 respondents.

## Rescue dogs tested

![A international training camp for rubble dogs is being organised in Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9042452)

A rescue dog at work. Photo: Yle

A test for search and rescue dogs was held in Vantaa at the weekend. Rescue dogs search for people, for example in the rubble of buildings.

In the test, the dogs had to find people who were hidden in the rubble. The dogs showed the right places by barking.

Good rescue dogs may be able to go abroad to help with rescue work, for example after an earthquake.

Here in Finland, rescue dogs search for people who have got lost in the forest, for example. There are almost 200 rescue dogs in Finland.

## The thieves regretted it

![A boombox, a bass guitar, a games console and an A4 note on top of them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10248881)

Finally, a strange crime story.

The thieves took goods from the Jaspis youth café, which is in the town of Hamina. The thieves took, for example, computers, a games console and a guitar.

The value of the goods was about 6,000 euros. That is a big sum for a youth café, and the young people were sad. Young people like to spend time at Jaspis.

The director of Jaspis wrote about the matter on Facebook. He hoped that the thieves would return the goods.

The surprise was great, however, when 3 rubbish sacks full of goods were found at the door of Jaspis. The thieves had returned the goods. A note was included in which the thieves apologised.

The police are also puzzled. It is very unusual for thieves to return goods.
