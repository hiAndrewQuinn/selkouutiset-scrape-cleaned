# Friday 5.11.2021 (radio)

The 80 per cent vaccination coverage is approaching. Companies cannot find enough employees. Pupils are absent from school more than before. More bears were hunted than last year.

## Corona vaccinations

![A young girl is being vaccinated.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3541,h_1991,x_1069,y_1459/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-875279618372f43c19d/1637060487)

A young girl is being vaccinated. Photo: AOP

In Finland 79.5 per cent of over-12-year-olds have received 2 corona vaccinations.

The authority says that an even larger part of young people have received the vaccination. Young people also have fewer corona infections than before.

Of Finns over 12 years old, 2 per cent have now received 3 vaccinations.

There are now 241 corona patients in hospital in total. Of them, 29 are in intensive care.

The authority is considering whether children under 12 should also be vaccinated in Finland.

## Aarnio and Vilhunen in the Court of Appeal

![Jari Aarnio whispers to his assistant in the courtroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1549,h_871,x_4,y_2/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8759026184dda770d83/1636097561)

Open image viewer

Jari Aarnio (Centre) whispered to his assistant Mikko Ruuttunen on Friday, the last session day of the Court of Appeal. On the right is Aarnio's other assistant, Riitta Leppiniemi. Photo: Tuomas Rimpiläinen

The Helsinki Court of Appeal will give its decision on the murder charges against Jari Aarnio and Keijo Vilhunen on 4 February. The hearing of the Vuosaari contract murder ended in the Court of Appeal today, Friday.

Last year the district court sentenced Aarnio to life imprisonment. The charge against Vilhunen was rejected.

Jari Aarnio is a former head of the Helsinki drug police.

The Swedish-Turkish criminal Volkan Ünsal was murdered in 2003 in Vuosaari, Helsinki. Four men have already been sentenced to life imprisonment earlier for the murder.

## Labour shortage

![Customers at the Iso Omena shopping centre in Espoo on 14 December 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11697787)

Customers at the Iso Omena shopping centre in Espoo on 14 December 2020. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Companies cannot find enough employees. A survey by Suomen Yrittäjät says that 40 per cent of companies suffer from a labour shortage.

The labour shortage is clearly greatest in construction and industry.

## Absences from school

![Corridor of Valkeala school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_9248,h_5202,x_0,y_1119/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-87504061827943246bf/1635941233)

Open image viewer

Corridor of Valkeala school. Photo: Juulia Tillaeus / Yle

Pupils are absent from school more than before.

In Finland's lower secondary schools there are over 4 000 pupils who are absent from school too much. This hinders studying. The reason for absence is often that the pupil has mental health problems.

Attempts are now being made to reduce absences with the help of a new trial. In the trial, workers have been hired to schools to support pupils.

## Bears

![Five bears march one after another towards the camera in autumn terrain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1026,h_577,x_0,y_257/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8645276160197f22695/1633695504)

388 bears were shot in Finland this autumn. The bear hunt ended at the end of October. 46 more bears were shot than last year.

The most bears were shot in Eastern Finland, because there are the most bears there. In North Karelia 198 bears were shot.
