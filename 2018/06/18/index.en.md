# Monday 18.6.2018 (radio)

President Niinistö arranged a discussion on security policy. Finland could become an important hub for telecommunications. There has been an earthquake in Japan. The air in the Helsinki region is clean. The weather turns rainy.

## Many people experience age discrimination

![An elderly man in a café.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10260782)

Open image viewer

Sometimes companies do not want to hire people over 55. Photo: Pixabay

Many Finns feel that they have been discriminated against because of their age.

Almost 20 percent of Finns have experienced age discrimination when they have applied for a job. It is hard for an applicant to get a job if they are old.

People over 55 have experienced age discrimination the most. Women have experienced age discrimination in job searches more than men.

It is important for Finland's economy that everyone of working age can get a job. That is why age discrimination is a problem.

The information comes from a new survey, which more than 2000 people of working age answered.

## Telecommunications through Finland grow

![Fibre-optic cable](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10242528)

Open image viewer

In a fibre-optic cable, information travels at the speed of light. Photo: Yle

Finland could become an important hub for telecommunications.

A new cable will connect Europe's and Asia's telecommunications, perhaps in just 3 or 4 years. The cable will run through Finland.

The plan is that the cable runs from Finland along a northern route to Asia. The cable is to be laid on the bed of the Arctic Ocean.

The cable will contain optical fibre. In optical fibre, information travels at the speed of light. That is the fastest speed there is.

The new cable could bring a lot of benefit to Finland. It could bring international companies to Finland, which would want to operate in a telecommunications centre.

## The ground shook in Japan

![People leave a commuter train in Osaka, Japan](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10260207)

Open image viewer

People leave the train after the earthquake. Photo: Jiji Press / EPA

There has been an earthquake in Japan. The ground shook strongly in Japan on Monday morning.

The earthquake had a magnitude of 6.1. The epicentre was close to the city of Osaka.

At least 3 people died in the earthquake. More than 200 people were injured.

## Helsinki's air is clean

![A café on Aleksanterinkatu in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10000906)

Open image viewer

The air in Helsinki is fresh, even in the centre. Photo: AOP

The air in the Helsinki region is clean. Residents were able to breathe good air in the capital region last year.

Measurements show that air quality in the capital region was good or satisfactory more than 90 percent of the time.

The Helsinki region also has problems with air quality at times. Street dust and cars pollute the air the most.

## Rain and wind on Tuesday

![A lake surface in the rain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10260658)

Open image viewer

The weather forecast promises rain. Photo: Kaisa Siren / AOP

Midsummer is approaching, and the weather is turning rainy.

On Tuesday it will rain and thunder will rumble in the south. The wind will also be strong. On Wednesday and Thursday it will rain in Lapland.

Yle meteorologist Joonas Koskela says that the rain will continue over Midsummer too, that is on Friday and Saturday. The temperature at Midsummer will be 15-20 degrees in the south and 10-15 degrees in the north.
