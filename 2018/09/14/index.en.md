# Friday 14.9.2018 (radio)

Hurricanes are causing destruction in the United States and Asia. A fire destroyed a candle factory. The grain harvest is uneven. Amur tigers are moving. The weather varies.

In the United States, Hurricane Florence has moved from the sea onto land.

Meteorologists have warned that the heavy rain brought by the hurricane may cause a bad flood. There may be as much as a metre of rainwater.

## Super typhoon Mangkhut in Asia

![A running man, waves hit the shore.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10405611)

Open image viewer

A man runs away from a big wave. The super typhoon has already caused strong winds. The photo is from Taiwan. Photo: AFP / Lehtikuva

In Asia people fear super typhoon Mangkhut. Super typhoon Mangkhut is the most powerful hurricane in the whole world this year.

It will hit the Philippines tomorrow, Saturday. The wind speed of super typhoon Mangkhut may be as much as 80 kilometres per second.

In the Philippines people fear that the super typhoon will cause a lot of destruction.

People are trying to strengthen houses. Rice farmers have harvested the rice crop. The super typhoon may ruin a very large part of the rice fields.

*There is a mistake in this news item. The wind speed of typhoon Mangkhut cannot be 80 kilometres per second; the correct wind speed is 80 metres per second.*

## Candle factory burned

![-](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10404236)

Open image viewer

The fire at the candle factory produced a lot of toxic smoke. Photo: Anssi Ketonen

There was a big fire in Salo, in Western Finland, today.

The fire started in a candle factory. Paraffin, which candles are made from, burned in the factory. The fire produced a lot of toxic smoke.

Nobody was injured in the fire, but the factory's production hall was destroyed.

## The grain harvest is uneven

![A combine harvester in a barley field](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10403418)

Open image viewer

A farmer drives a combine harvester in a grain field. He is threshing grain. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

Farmers in Finland have threshed almost all the grain fields.

Many farmers have found it hard to decide when to drive the combine harvester out to the field. The grain has ripened unevenly because the summer was so dry.

In Southern and Western Finland the soil is clay. Clay soil dries worse than humus soil. That is why the grain harvest is poorer in Southern and Western Finland.

## Amur tiger cubs are moving

![Amur tiger cubs at Korkeasaari in 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10405156)

Open image viewer

The Amur tiger cubs at Korkeasaari Zoo are already big. Photo: Mari Lehmonen / Korkeasaari Zoo

The Amur tiger cubs at Korkeasaari Zoo will soon move away from Helsinki.

The Amur tiger pair at Korkeasaari had three female cubs. The cubs are now 2 years and 4 months old, and it is time for them to move elsewhere. Suitable mates are being looked for them at some zoo, because it is hoped that they will have cubs of their own.

There are only about 500 Amur tigers in the whole world.
