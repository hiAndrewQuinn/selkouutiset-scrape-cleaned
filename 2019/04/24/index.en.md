# Wednesday 24.4.2019 (radio)

The refugees of the year come from Syria and Togo. The Finn Abroad of the Year works in Switzerland. Parliament has chosen its speakers. The singer of Iron Maiden will become an honorary doctor at the University of Helsinki.

## Refugees of the year

![Refugee man of the year 2019 Nourdeen Toure and refugee woman of the year Rand Mohamad Deeb.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10753171)

Nourdeen Toure and Rand Mohamad Deeb. Photo: Enni Kallio

Finnish Refugee Aid has chosen the Refugees of the Year.

The refugee woman of the year is Rand Mohamad Deeb. She comes from Syria, but now lives in Lahti. Deeb is 18 years old and will graduate from upper secondary school this spring.

Deeb wants to show other immigrants that learning the language and studying in Finland is possible. She also wants to help others, and she makes Finnish language teaching videos herself in Arabic.

The refugee man of the year is Nourdeen Toure. He comes from Togo in West Africa. Toure is 28 years old and lives in Helsinki.

Toure is a boxer. He says that exercise and hobbies help with integration. He hopes that he will someday win the Finnish boxing championship.

## Finn Abroad of the Year

![Petteri Taalas](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10349953)

Open image viewer

Petteri Taalas. Photo: Salvatore di Nolfi / EPA

Suomi-Seura has awarded the Finn Abroad of the Year. The Finn Abroad of the Year is Petteri Taalas, Secretary-General of the World Meteorological Organization (WMO).

WMO is the UN's special agency for weather, climate and water resources. The organisation works in Geneva, Switzerland.

Petteri Taalas has been Secretary-General of WMO since 2016. Before that he was the director of the Finnish Meteorological Institute in Finland

## Speakers of Parliament

![SDP chairman Antti Rinne was chosen as Speaker of Parliament, Juho Eerola (right) of the Finns Party was chosen as first deputy speaker, and Paula Risikko of the National Coalition was chosen as second deputy speaker.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10753350)

Paula Risikko, Antti Rinne and Juho Eerola. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The new Parliament has had its first plenary session today. New speakers were chosen at the session.

The Speaker of Parliament is, for the time being, the chairman of the largest party, that is, the SDP's Antti Rinne. The first deputy speaker is Finns Party MP Juho Eerola. The second deputy speaker is Paula Risikko of the National Coalition.

If Antti Rinne later becomes prime minister, Parliament will choose a new speaker.

## Honorary doctor of the University of Helsinki

![Bruce Dickinson](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9964630)

Open image viewer

Bruce Dickinson. Photo: Jussi Mankkinen / Yle

Bruce Dickinson, the singer of the rock band Iron Maiden, is being appointed an honorary doctor of the University of Helsinki. Dickinson receives the honorary doctorate because he has excelled in so many different fields.

Dickinson is a singer and songwriter. Dickinson's band Iron Maiden has sold over 90 million records. In addition, Dickinson is, for example, a pilot, radio presenter, author, film scriptwriter and fencer.

The university's promotion, that is, the celebration ceremony, is in a month. Dickinson cannot come to the ceremony, but he will receive the title in absentia.

The Faculty of Philosophy at the University of Helsinki appoints 9 honorary doctors in total this spring.
