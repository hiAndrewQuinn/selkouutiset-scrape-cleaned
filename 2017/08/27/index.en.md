# Sunday 27.8.2017 (radio)

Regular exercise is good for the brain. Crowberries are now also good food for Finns. The police have established the real name and age of the Turku stabber. Heavy rains are lashing Texas.

## Exercise improves the working of the brain

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9796595)

Open image viewer

When a person runs, the amount of blood in the brain grows by about 20 percent compared with sitting. Photo: Johanna Aulén / Yle

Exercise is very important for the well-being of the brain. Regular fitness exercise improves concentration, memory and children's ability to learn, says a new Swedish study.

When a person moves, blood circulation improves in the muscles and the brain. When a person walks or runs, the amount of blood in the brain grows by about 20 percent compared with sitting. The blood brings oxygen and nutrients into the brain, that is, the brain works better.

Anders Hansen, chief physician at the Karolinska Institute, says that fitness exercise, or a combination of fitness exercise and strength training, eases stress and memory problems, improves the ability to concentrate and slows down ageing.

Hansen says that a suitable amount of exercise is 45 minutes, 3 times a week. Studies seem to show that a bigger amount brings no extra benefit for the brain.

## Crowberries and bog whortleberries are already good for pickers

![Bog whortleberry](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6764653)

Open image viewer

The bog whortleberry looks a bit like the blueberry. Photo: Eelis Pulkkinen

Finns have learned to pick and use more unusual wild berries too. For example, the popularity of rowanberries, crowberries, honeyberries and bog whortleberries has grown.

This may be because people are now enthusiastic about wild food and so-called superfood, says special expert Rainer Peltola of the Natural Resources Institute.

Rowanberries, for example, contain more vitamin C and fibre than many other berries. Crowberries and bog whortleberries, on the other hand, contain a lot of polyphenols, which reduce the risk of falling ill with, for example, heart and blood vessel diseases, type 2 diabetes and memory illnesses.

Industry has also begun to use crowberries, for example. Juice is made from them, and they are used as an addition to other berries in jams and jellies.

## The identity of the Turku stabber is known

![The gate of the prison hospital of the Hämeenlinna prison on Wednesday 23 August 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9794053)

Open image viewer

The main suspect in the Turku stabbings is at the prison hospital in Hämeenlinna. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

The identity of the main suspect in the Turku stabbings has been confirmed.

The police say that they have established the man's name with the help of international police cooperation. The man's name will not be made public for the time being.

The man's year of birth has also been confirmed. He was born in 1994. The man himself said that he was born 4 years later, in 1998.

The man stabbed 10 people in Turku on Friday, a little over a week ago.

## Storm Harvey brought heavy rain to Texas

![Flood water on a street in Houston on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9799937)

Open image viewer

Flood water has risen onto the streets of the city of Houston in Texas. Photo: AOP

In the United States, Hurricane Harvey has weakened into a tropical storm.

Harvey brought heavy rain to the state of Texas. In places, more than a metre of water has fallen.

The rain has caused floods in the cities of Houston and Corpus Christi, for example. People have been evacuated to safety from the floods.
