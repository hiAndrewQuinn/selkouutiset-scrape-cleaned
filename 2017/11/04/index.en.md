# Saturday 4.11.2017 (radio)

Criminal record checks for elderly care work? A big military exercise. The US defence secretary is coming to Finland. Sex accusations against Kevin Spacey.

## People want elderly care workers' criminal records checked

![An old woman in a wheelchair near a bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9907936)

Open image viewer

Organisations for the elderly want to improve old people's safety and stop criminals from getting jobs in home care.  Photo: Pixabay

Finland is thinking about how old people's safety at home can be improved. In Finland, old people already often live in their own homes. They get help at home from home service workers.

Organisations for the elderly now want Finland to check whether home service workers have committed crimes, that is, whether they have a criminal record. The organisations think this would improve old people's safety. They want criminals not to get into old people's homes.

At present in Finland, criminal records are checked for people who work with children.

## A big military exercise in Finland

![Swedish soldiers training in Skövde on Thursday. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9835965)

Open image viewer

The Aurora 17 military exercise was held in Sweden this year. Finland's exercise is planned to be at least as big.  Photo: Bjorn Larsson Rosvall / TT / EPA-EFE

Finland plans to hold a big international military exercise. Finland's Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö says that Finland must practise how other countries can help Finland if, for example, there is a war.

Minister Niinistö hopes that many countries will take part in Finland's exercise. It is said that at least Sweden and the USA will take part. It is estimated that the big military exercise could be held, for example, in 2020.

Sweden had a big military exercise this year called Aurora 17. The aim is to make Finland's exercise at least as big as Sweden's exercise.

## The US defence secretary is coming to Finland

![James Mattis](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9916797)

Open image viewer

US Defence Secretary James Mattis.  Photo: Christian Bruna / EPA

The US defence secretary is coming to Finland next week. Defence Secretary James Mattis is coming to Finland for a meeting where, for example, security in Northern Europe will be discussed.

Many countries are taking part in the meeting. The Nordic countries, the Baltic countries, the Netherlands, Britain, Poland and Germany are taking part. Defence Secretary Mattis will also meet Finland's President Sauli Niinistö.

James Mattis is 67 years old. He is a former general. Mattis has been at war, for example, in Iraq and Afghanistan.

## Kevin Spacey faces sex accusations

![Kevin Spacey](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9909660)

Open image viewer

Kevin Spacey has played the US president in the series House of Cards.  Photo: AOP

People around the world are now talking a lot about a famous American actor. The big internet entertainment company Netflix no longer wants to work with actor Kevin Spacey.

Spacey has been a big star in Netflix's presidential series House of Cards. Many male employees of the series say that Spacey has sexually harassed them.

There are also more sex accusations. For example, the British police are investigating sexual allegations. It is also claimed that 30 years ago at a party, Spacey tried to get sex from a 14-year-old boy.
