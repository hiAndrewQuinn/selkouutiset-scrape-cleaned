# Wednesday 25.10.2017 (radio)

Terrafame wants to produce uranium. Finnair is still doing well. Alcohol drinking is clearly going down. The number of Saimaa ringed seals is growing.

## Terrafame wants uranium

![Terrafame's mining area in Sotkamo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9890178)

Open image viewer

Terrame, formerly Talvivaara, is in Sotkamo in Kainuu.  Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

The Finnish state's mining company Terrafame wants to get permission to produce uranium. Terrafame operates in Sotkamo in Eastern Finland. Terrafame digs ore out of the ground, from which nickel, cobalt and zinc are now produced. The ore also contains uranium, but it is not recovered.

Now the mine wants to start producing uranium from the ore too, because it wants to get more money. According to Terrafame, the uranium must be processed abroad. After that, it can be used as fuel in nuclear power plants.

Terrafame says that it wants to sell uranium only for energy production, not for nuclear weapons.

## Finnair is now flying well

![Finnair Airbus A350 XWB](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9675305)

Open image viewer

Finnair flies a lot to Asia, and that brings in a lot of money.  Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The Finnish airline Finnair is now doing really well. Finnair has released figures on its business in the summer and early autumn. Business has gone much better now than at the same time last year.

Finnair has been doing really well for a long time. Chinese and Japanese people especially like to fly to Helsinki with Finnair.

A large share of Asian passengers continue their journey from Helsinki to other parts of Europe. But some stay on holiday in Finland.

The Finnish state owns a large part of Finnair.

## Alcohol drinking is going down

![A man sits at a table with a beer bottle in front of him.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9848951)

Open image viewer

Finns drink less than before, but when they drink, they drink a lot at once.  Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Finns' drinking of alcohol continues to go down. Health experts say that Finns now drink 15 percent less alcohol than 10 years ago. Drinking has gone down especially among 15-29-year-olds.

Although Finns drink less alcohol, the style of drinking has not changed.

When Finns drink, they still drink a lot at once. In Southern Europe, for example, people drink alcohol more often than Finns, but not such large amounts at once.

## The number of Saimaa ringed seals is growing

![A seal on a rock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7906387)

Open image viewer

The Saimaa ringed seal sometimes likes to rest on a rock.  Photo: Juha Taskinen / SLL

The number of Saimaa ringed seals continues to grow. Metsähallitus, which manages state-owned land and waters, estimates that the number of seals has grown by 23 animals. Metsähallitus's estimate means that there are now about 380 Saimaa ringed seals.

The seal is endangered, and it has been protected for a long time. Metsähallitus thinks that the protection is working.

Protection means that, for example, fishing is restricted. Also, piles of snow are built on the ice for the seals in spring, so that they can make a winter den.

The seal gives birth to its pup in the winter den. Metsähallitus says that 83 seal pups were born this year.
