# Saturday 24.9.2016 (radio)

Demonstrations against racism have been held in Finland. There has been a big fire in Turku. Moose hunting has started. The number of children with a foreign background has grown in the Helsinki region. Finns have a lot of skin cancer.

## Demonstrations against racism

Demonstrations against racism have been held in Finland.

The biggest demonstration was in Helsinki. Over 15,000 people took part.

![Prime Minister Juha Sipilä (centre) at a demonstration against racism in Kuopio market square on Saturday 24.9.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9189787)

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä at the demonstration in Kuopio. Photo: Sakari Partanen / Yle

People have also marched against racism, for example, in Kuopio and Tampere.

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä also took part in the demonstration in Kuopio. The Prime Minister promised that the government will try to prevent violence by extremist movements.

Extremist movements are talked about a lot in Finland now because 2 weeks ago a young man was assaulted at a neo-Nazi demonstration. The man got a bad injury and died.

## The fire in Turku

![A fire engine in front of a factory hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9189599)

Open image viewer

Putting out the fire took a long time. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

There has been a big fire in Turku. The fire started in a building where buses are kept. The fire burned the building and 20 buses.

Nobody died or was injured in the fire, but the damage is worth millions of euros. The police have arrested a man who may have started the fire on purpose.

The bus company says that the fire does not affect bus services in Turku at the weekend.

## Moose hunting

![Moose hunting starts on Saturday 24.9.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9180027)

Open image viewer

Moose meat is good food. Photo: Yle

Moose hunting has started. Moose hunting started in a large part of Finland on Saturday.

This year hunters have got over 40,000 moose hunting permits. With one permit, you can shoot one adult moose or two young moose.

The moose is Finland's biggest animal. It can weigh over 600 kilos.

In Finland, there are moose collisions, in which a car and a moose crash into each other. That is one reason why moose are hunted.

## Multiculturalism is increasing

![Children swing in a playground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6285847)

Open image viewer

In Helsinki, many children have a foreign background. Photo: Yle

There are many children with a foreign background in the Helsinki region. Almost 20 percent of children under 7 have a foreign background, that is, both of the child's parents were born abroad. Most families with children come from Estonia, Russia and Somalia.

The number of children with a foreign background has grown fast. In 1990, less than 1 percent of children under 7 in the Helsinki region had a foreign background.

## The dangers of the sun

![Sunbathing and especially getting sunburned expose you to melanoma.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6556606)

Open image viewer

In the southern sun, you must protect your skin well. Photo: Pekka Sipilä / Yle

Finns love the sun. At this time in autumn, many are planning a trip to the south.

But too much sun is dangerous. Fair skin burns easily in the sun, and that increases the risk of skin cancer. Skin cancer is a serious disease.

A campaign against skin cancer will start in North Savo next year. The dangers of the sun are taught already in daycare centres, because a child's skin is sensitive to the sun.
