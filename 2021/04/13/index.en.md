# Tuesday 13.4.2021 (radio)

The USA plans to stop vaccinations with the Johnson & Johnson corona vaccine. There are 3 new corona deaths in Finland. Winter holiday trips are selling well. WHO recommends a ban on selling wild animals at markets.

## The Johnson & Johnson vaccine

![Close-up of a vaccine being injected into the upper arm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11879585)

Close-up of a vaccine being injected into the upper arm. Photo: Nic Bothma / EPA

In the USA, an authority recommends that the use of the drug company Johnson & Johnson's corona vaccine be stopped. The reason is a suspicion that the vaccine may cause blood clots.

In the USA, almost 7 million people have already received the Johnson & Johnson vaccine. 6 women have had a rare blood clot after vaccination. 1 woman has died. The authority now wants to study the vaccine.

Last week, the European Medicines Agency also started a study of the Johnson & Johnson vaccine. The Johnson & Johnson vaccine has been granted a marketing authorisation in the EU. However, the vaccine is not yet being given to people in EU countries.

## Corona infections

There are 286 new corona infections in Finland. There are a little more new infections than on Monday.

The most new infections are still in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district. In total, about 82,600 corona infections have been found in Finland so far.

There are 3 new corona deaths. A total of 182 people are now in hospital care because of corona. 38 people are in intensive care.

## Winter holiday trips

![A view of the beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11454821)

Open image viewer

People enjoyed the sun and sea in Famagusta, Cyprus, last June. Photo: Katia Christodoulou / EPA

People are already buying a lot of holiday trips. Finns have bought trips for next winter.

Many trips have been bought to Greece and the Canary Islands. People also want to go to places such as Thailand and the Maldives.

Only a few trips have been bought for the summer. People do not yet know how the corona epidemic will develop. It may not be possible to travel to many countries yet in the summer. For example, many Mediterranean tourist destinations have strict corona restrictions.

## Trade in wild animals

![Animal market full of people.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5760,h_3240,x_0,y_297/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7025875f1d9b0eb7087/1595775839)

The World Health Organization, WHO, says that the sale of live wild animals must be banned. WHO says that live mammals must not be sold at markets.

Wild animals spread diseases. Wild mammals in particular spread diseases.

WHO believes that the coronavirus was passed to humans from an animal. China has already reduced the trade in wild animals.
