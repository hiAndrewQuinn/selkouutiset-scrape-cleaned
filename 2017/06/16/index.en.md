# Friday 16.6.2017 (radio)

Police suspect that the building that burned in London had been badly renovated. Norway is ending heating with oil. Finland's border guards may carry submachine guns. Lake water is cleaned with mud balls. People in Helsinki ride city bikes a lot.

##

## Many people missing in London

![A burning apartment building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9669174)

Open image viewer

An apartment building burned in London on Wednesday. Photo: Pasi Myöhänen / Yle

Greater London police have started a criminal investigation into the tower block fire. Police suspect that mistakes were made in the renovation of the building. The mistakes meant that the fire was able to spread to different floors of the tower block.

Residents have also told the police that there was a lot of building waste in the building's corridors. At least 30 people died in the fire, but there may be more victims.

Many people are still looking for relatives and friends after the fire. Over 70 people are missing.

## Norway bans burning oil

![A street view in Bergen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9673661)

Open image viewer

Ordinary houses in Norway. Photo: AOP

Soon houses in Norway may no longer be heated with oil. Heating houses with oil is being banned, because burning oil produces a lot of greenhouse gases. Greenhouse gases warm the globe.

The Norwegian government says that houses should be heated, for example, with wood or with electricity that comes from a hydropower plant. Norway has a lot of electricity that comes from hydropower plants.

## Submachine guns for border guards

![A Russian driver is asked to pull over for an inspection.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8777352)

Open image viewer

Border guards take care of border security. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen/Yle

Finland's border guards may soon carry submachine guns. Border guards are getting permission to carry submachine guns, because Finland's security situation has become more dangerous.

The submachine guns will not be carried all the time; they will be taken in hand only in dangerous situations. At the moment, border guards usually carry only pistols.

## Lakes are cleaned with mud balls

![Bubbles on the surface of the water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9667224)

Open image viewer

People want to keep lake waters clean. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

Finland has a lot of lakes, and people want to keep their waters clean. Now a new way of cleaning water is being tried. Mud balls are dropped into the water. They are made, for example, from bacteria and fungi. The mud balls make the lake's useful microorganisms work.

## People in Helsinki use city bikes

![A city bike charging station. Instructions for using a city bike are available at hsl.fi/kaupunkipyorat. At the same address you can also register and check how many bikes there are at each station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9667266)

The city bikes are yellow. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

City bikes are very popular in Helsinki. Many people in Helsinki take a bike into use for the whole summer. People in Helsinki think that a bike is a handy way to get around the city.

There are a lot of city bikes. There are already 1,400 of them. Tourists also like to use city bikes.
