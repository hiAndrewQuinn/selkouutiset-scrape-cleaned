## Support for electricity bills

![Sanna Marin in front of microphones.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4847,h_2726,x_0,y_341/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105063063a079bda2b34/1671461336)

The government has presented support measures to the opposition parties to help citizens with expensive electricity bills.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the government is planning a one-off payment to help citizens. The aim is to get it to Parliament for handling at the beginning of January. In addition, the government is planning a price cap on electricity.

The price of electricity has now risen so much that many people's money does not seem to be enough to pay the electricity bill.

## UN nature conference

![Maria Ohisalo congratulates the Canadian representative.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3648,h_2052,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105035563a0356f65b7a/1671443869)

Finland's Environment and Climate Minister Maria Ohisalo took part in the UN nature conference in Canada. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

The UN nature conference has reached agreement on global conservation targets. The nature conference has been held in Montreal, Canada.

At the meeting it was agreed that 30 percent of the planet's nature will be protected by 2030. At present, about 17 percent of land areas and 10 percent of sea areas are protected.

Finland's Environment Minister Maria Ohisalo says that the agreement is a turning point in stopping the loss of biodiversity.

## Health centres

![Road sign directing to a health centre and a pharmacy, with the health centre behind it and cars in its car park in winter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_650/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90322861e6a6ac27b54/1642506780)

Open image viewer

Customer service at health centres has worsened in 2 years. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

Health centres are getting worse ratings from customers.

In the survey by the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, people say that it is harder than before to get health services. It is also difficult in general to get in touch with your own health centre.

Customers also say that it is rarer than before for them to be treated by the same doctor or nurse throughout their illness.

The situation is worst in Uusimaa, especially in Helsinki and Vantaa. Only in South Ostrobothnia was customer feedback now better than in the previous survey two years ago.

Almost 14,000 health centre customers answered THL's survey last spring. The survey was carried out in about 200 municipalities. THL carries out the survey every two years.

## Earthquake

![Map showing the location of the earthquake north-west of the city of Kotka.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105027663a02729f3997/1671440263)

Open image viewer

A small earthquake was detected in Kotka. Photo: Ilona Pesu / Yle

There has been an earthquake in south-eastern Finland. People woke up in the night at half past one because of the earthquake in Kotka.

A magnitude 1.8 earthquake was detected in Kotka during the night between Sunday and Monday. The tremor caused no damage.

Several earthquakes have been detected in the area in recent years. Last week earthquakes were detected in eastern Finland.
