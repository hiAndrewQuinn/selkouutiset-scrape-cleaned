# Saturday 7.7.2018 (radio)

The meeting of the presidents of the USA and Russia in Helsinki means big security arrangements. Confirmation school is more popular in Finland than elsewhere. Pori has Finland's first food park. A new service tells you when blueberries are ripe.

## Cyber security at the presidents' meeting

![A finger points at a laptop screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9096347)

Open image viewer

The meeting of Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin in Helsinki needs a lot of security arrangements. Photo: Ralf Hirschberger / EPA

The meeting of the presidents of the USA and Russia in Helsinki means big security arrangements. Cyber security is also important. This means protecting data and information security. It also means influencing through information, and communication.

Before the meeting, the conversations must be protected. The starting point is a room from which nothing can be seen or heard outside. Then it must also be known who is moving where. Security services prevent, for example, eavesdropping.

The USA and Russia, for their part, try to control how others communicate about the presidents' meeting.

## Confirmation camps are more popular in Finland than in other countries

![Confirmation camp participants singing](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6589604)

Open image viewer

Confirmation camps have not become less popular. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

In Finland, 80 percent of young people go to confirmation school or a confirmation camp. The number is higher than anywhere else. Many young people who do not belong to the church also join the church at confirmation school.

People even come from abroad to go to confirmation camp. Some go both to a confirmation camp and to a Prometheus camp, which is run outside the church. Youth culture and the company of friends explain the popularity of confirmation school. At camp, people still ask what faith means to a young person.

The membership of the Evangelical Lutheran Church has fallen by 10 percent in one generation. 71 percent of Finns, or 3.9 million people, belong to the church.

## Here is the new food park in Pori

![The food park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10290606)

Open image viewer

About a hundred plants have been planted in the food park so far. Photo: Carolus Manninen / Yle

Finland's first food park has been set up in Hevosluoto in Pori, Satakunta.

Near the Housing Fair area there is a park where anyone can pick fruit, berries and herbs. Behind the idea is a citizens' initiative.

But the park hopes that city residents will take part in looking after the park. People may pick the crop only for their own use and only when it is ripe.

## You can check if blueberries are ripe with a new service

![A man picking blueberries.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7342614)

Open image viewer

The online service "Mustikkaan?" (Blueberry picking?) tells where blueberries are ripe. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

A new service tells berry pickers about blueberries ripening. The service called "Mustikkaan?" (Blueberry picking?) follows temperatures to an accuracy of 5 days and 100 square kilometres.

The model uses weather statistics from the Finnish Meteorological Institute from 50 years. On the website you can follow how the crop develops in the whole country. The makers of the model say that it is based only on calculations. For example, the maps of the Natural Resources Institute are based on observations.

The service can be a good help, for example, for people at summer cottages. It can make you excited to go berry picking. At the moment the service is only on the internet. There is no mobile version.
