# Thursday 15.2.2018 (radio)

Finland has won a third Olympic medal at the Winter Games in South Korea. Nokia is starting co-operation negotiations (layoff talks) again. Attitudes towards unemployment are hardening. The search for gold in Finland is speeding up. Final-year upper secondary students have been celebrating with school-leavers' parties.

## Finland won a third Olympic gold

Finland has won a third Olympic medal at the Winter Games in South Korea.

Krista Pärmäkoski won bronze in the women's 10 kilometre cross-country skiing. Norway's Marit Björgen also got bronze, because they skied exactly the same time. Norway got gold and Sweden silver.

The medal is Pärmäkoski's second bronze. On Saturday she skied to bronze in the combined distance. Enni Rukajärvi won bronze on Monday in the women's snowboard slopestyle.

## Nokia has started co-operation negotiations

![Nokia's head office in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10076558)

Open image viewer

Nokia's head office in Espoo. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The technology company Nokia is starting co-operation negotiations again. Nokia is cutting about 350 jobs in its network business in Finland.

The need for cuts may apply to all of Nokia's workplaces in Finland, except Oulu. In addition, Nokia Technologies is starting co-operation negotiations, which will lead to about 75 jobs being cut.

Nokia currently has about 6 300 employees in Finland.

## Attitudes towards unemployment are hardening

![Susanne Ådahl](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10074200)

Open image viewer

In Finland, attitudes towards unemployment are hardening, according to a Yle News survey.

A majority of respondents say that the cause of unemployment lies in society. However, more than half of young people believe that unemployment is the fault of the individual, not of society.

Older age groups also think that attitude and work ethic decide whether you get a job.

## The Kittilä gold mine is expanding

![Gold bar](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10076542)

Open image viewer

Gold bar at the Kittilä gold mine. Photo: Agnico Eagle Finland Oy

The search for gold in Finland is speeding up.

Dozens of foreign companies are looking for gold, because demand and price for gold are rising around the world. Europe's largest gold mine, in Kittilä, Lapland, is expanding. The mining company has announced additional investments of 160 million euros. A mine shaft reaching more than one kilometre deep is being built for the mine. The company estimates that the mine can operate until 2034.

Six mines are now operating in Finland. There are 15 possible mines, and mineral prospecting is very active. Finland is the largest gold producer among EU countries. In addition, the traditions are long, because Lapland's gold mining turns 150 years old this year.

## Final-year upper secondary students celebrate the end of upper secondary school

![Penkkarit in Lappeenranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10077227)

Open image viewer

Here is some penkkarit fun from Lappeenranta. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

Final-year upper secondary students have celebrated the end of upper secondary school at school-leavers' parties.

In a large part of the country it was possible to drive around the streets in light frost. Posters on the sides of lorries commented on current issues, for example the presidential elections.

The school-leavers' parties start a month-long reading holiday. The written exams of the matriculation examination continue in mid-March.
