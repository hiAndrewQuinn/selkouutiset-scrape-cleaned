# Sunday 17.9.2017 (radio)

Finland has held a 100th anniversary celebration in the USA. The British police have arrested a second man because of Friday's bomb attack. The Finnish police had a car chase in Pirkanmaa. Finns and Russians often pick different mushrooms.

## Finland celebration in the USA

![President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio arriving at the Finnish Embassy in Washington for the Finland 100 gala.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9836751)

Finland has held a 100th anniversary celebration in the USA. Finland invited Americans to celebrate because Finland has been an independent country for 100 years. The celebration was held at the Finnish Embassy in Washington on Saturday.

The host of the celebration was President Sauli Niinistö. Niinistö told the guests that Finland and the USA are old friends. The president told journalists that Americans are interested in Finland's success. Finland has done very well in many studies that have compared different countries.

More than 100 guests took part in the celebration. Opera singer Camilla Nylund sang songs by Jean Sibelius for the guests. The party food was also Finnish. On the table there was, for example, salmon, mushrooms and blueberry ice cream.

## Arrest in London

![Police patrol in front of Parliament in London in connection with the metro bomb attack on 16 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9836478)

The British police have arrested a second man because of Friday's bomb attack. The police arrested the man in London. The man is 21 years old.

On Saturday the police arrested an 18-year-old man. The police suspect that the men are connected to the London bomb attack.

30 people were injured when a bomb exploded on the London Underground on Friday.

The terrorist organisation Isis says that it is responsible for the bomb attack. The British police are continuing to investigate the attack.

## A chase

![map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9836676)

Open image viewer

In Huittinen the police caught the car from which shots were fired at the police. Photo: Yle News Graphics

The Finnish police have had a chase in Pirkanmaa, west of Tampere.

On Saturday evening the police got a report about a car whose driver was possibly drunk. When the police found the car, shots were fired from the car at the police.

The car fled from the police, but the police caught the car. The police shot a man who had a weapon. The man was taken to hospital. The police also arrested the driver of the car.

No police officers were injured.

## Mushroom picking

![Honey fungus](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9833215)

Open image viewer

Russians think honey fungus is a good edible mushroom. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

Autumn is a good time to pick mushrooms.

Finns and Russians pick a lot of mushrooms, but they often pick different mushrooms. Finns think, for example, that the chanterelle is a very good edible mushroom. Russians do not like the chanterelle.

The chanterelle has no taste, says the Russian Alexander Volkov. Volkov still picks chanterelles because he has lived in Finland for many years already. But he has not learned to love the taste of the chanterelle.

Volkov loves the taste of honey fungus. Russians think honey fungus is a delicious edible mushroom. Finns do not use honey fungus.
