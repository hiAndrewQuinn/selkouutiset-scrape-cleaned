# Thursday 9.8.2018 (radio)

School has started in many municipalities. Finland was seen a lot in the media of different countries because of the Trump-Putin meeting. A new Moomin mug drove Moomin fans crazy. Almost 120,000 people have visited the Moomin Museum in Tampere. The weather forecast promises thunderstorms.

## School started

School has started in many municipalities.

Schools started on Thursday, for example in Helsinki. This autumn all comprehensive school pupils in Helsinki start studying a foreign language already in the first grade.

Last year about 14 percent of Finland's first-graders studied a foreign language. The government's plan is that in 2020 all schoolchildren in Finland start studying a foreign language already in the first grade.

## The media noticed Finland

![US President Donald Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin shook hands at a press conference in Helsinki on 16 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10310235)

Open image viewer

Trump and Putin met in Helsinki in July. Photo: AOP

Finland benefited from the presidents of the USA and Russia meeting in Finland at the beginning of July.

Because of the Trump-Putin meeting, Finland was seen a great deal in the media of different countries. The Ministry for Foreign Affairs and the City of Helsinki calculate that Finland was seen so much in foreign media that its value was more than 3 billion euros.

Most was written about the Trump-Putin meeting. The media also told a lot, for example, about Finlandia Hall.

The advertising campaign of Helsingin Sanomat was also noticed abroad. The theme of the campaign was freedom of speech.

## People queued for a Moomin mug

![Queuing for Moomin mugs at Iso-Kristiina](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10344644)

Open image viewer

Here people are queuing for Moomin mugs in Lappeenranta. Photo: Petri Kivimäki / Yle

A new Moomin mug has driven Moomin fans crazy. The mug was sold on only one day.

The mug was on sale on Thursday, 104 years after Tove Jansson was born. Tove Jansson, the creator of the Moomin books, was born on 9 August 1914.

The new Moomin mug was on sale in Iittala shops. Many people slept outside in front of the shop all night, because they wanted to get into the shop right in the morning.

Many shops quickly sold out of the mugs. Already on Thursday people were selling the mugs online. In the shop the mug cost 30 euros; online some people asked as much as 2,000 euros for one mug.

## The Moomin Museum is a success in Tampere

![The Moomin Museum in Tampere](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10073653)

Open image viewer

The Moomin Museum brings tourists to Tampere. Photo: Marko Melto / Yle

The Moomins are a success in Tampere too. The Moomin Museum in Tampere is really popular.

The Moomin Museum has been open for a year. Almost 120,000 people have visited the museum.

Visitors come to the Moomin Museum from many countries. Most foreign visitors come from Japan.

On Friday there are thunderstorms in different parts of Finland. It may rain a lot, and the wind is strong.
