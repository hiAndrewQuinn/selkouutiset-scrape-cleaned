# Tuesday 7.11.2017 (radio)

Finland has invited the USA to a military exercise in Finland. A new kind of concrete without cement is now being developed in Lappeenranta. Doping substances can also affect the human mind. Saara Aalto will represent Finland in the Eurovision Song Contest.

## Finland is planning a military exercise

![Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö speaks to the press.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9920453)

Finland's Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finland has invited the USA to a military exercise in Finland.

Finland's Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö made the invitation to US Defence Secretary James Mattis. Mattis has been visiting Finland.

Minister Niinistö has also told many other countries about the military exercise. There has been a meeting of Northern European defence ministers in Finland.

Finland plans to hold a big military exercise in 2020 or 2021.

## Alternatives to cement are being looked for

![Concrete casting under way](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7684019)

Open image viewer

Cement is an important part of concrete. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen/Yle

A lot of cement is used on building sites. Cement is part of concrete.

But cement is bad for the environment. When cement is made, carbon dioxide gets into the air, and carbon dioxide warms the climate.

A new kind of concrete without cement is now being developed in Lappeenranta. The new kind of concrete is made from industrial waste materials.

Concrete can also be replaced with wood in building. But only a few wooden apartment buildings have been built so far.

## Doping substances affect the mind

![A bodybuilder lifts dumbbells.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7040056)

Open image viewer

Doping substances grow muscles and mess up the head. Photo: Bilawal Arbab / EPA

Many people who work out use doping substances when they want bigger muscles. But doping substances can also affect the human mind.

Young men have even ended up in psychiatric hospital because of doping substances. Often they have used doping substances at the same time as intoxicants. Sometimes patients do not get completely better, even in hospital.

Strong and aggressive patients are difficult for nurses. Sometimes nurses even need help from the police.

## Saara Aalto is going to Eurovision

![Saara Aalto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9919881)

Open image viewer

Saara Aalto is a popular singer. Photo: Yle

Saara Aalto has been invited to represent Finland in the Eurovision Song Contest. Finland's entrant was now chosen for the first time without a competition.

3 new songs are being made for Saara Aalto for Eurovision. Saara Aalto will perform them at the Espoo arena in March, and one of the songs will be chosen for Eurovision.

The Eurovision Song Contest will be held in Portugal in May. Portugal is hosting the contest because it won the previous Eurovision.

Saara Aalto is known from the British singing competition The X Factor. Aalto came second in the competition last year.
