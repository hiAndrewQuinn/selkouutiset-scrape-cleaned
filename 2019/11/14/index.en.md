# Thursday 14.11.2019 (radio)

Finland has broken the European Convention on Human Rights. Postal workers get support from workers in other sectors. Finland has an important football match on Friday. The squat position is now difficult for school-age children.

## Criticism of Finland

![Sign of the Finnish Immigration Service in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11020296)

Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Finland has broken the European Convention on Human Rights. This is what the European Court of Human Rights says. Its job is to supervise that countries follow the human rights convention.

The court says that Finland broke the human rights convention in the case of an Iraqi man. The man did not get asylum in Finland, and he had to go back to Iraq. In Iraq he was killed. This happened in 2017.

The court says that Finland did not assess the situation well enough. That is why Finland must pay 20,000 euros in compensation to the man's daughter.

## Support for strikers

![Umbrella saying "strike" in big letters, with other strikers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11069572)

Open image viewer

The postal strike began on Monday. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Postal workers get support from workers in other sectors. The postal workers are on strike because they have a dispute with the employer, for example about pay.

The strikers get support, for example, from railway workers. Railway workers intend to stop freight trains next week on Tuesday evening, if the postal strike continues. Freight trains will stand still until Wednesday evening.

Stopping freight traffic is the railway trade union's way of supporting the postal trade union. Many other trade unions also intend to support the strikers if the postal strike continues.

An attempt will be made to find a solution to the postal strike on Friday.

Finland has an important football match on Friday. The Finnish men's team plays against Liechtenstein. If Finland beats Liechtenstein, Finland will qualify for the European Football Championship.

Finland has never yet qualified for an important football tournament. That is why the Friday evening match is a big event for Finns. The match is played in Helsinki.

## Squat training for children

![junior ice hockey player Sisu Tyni](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_536/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6112305dcbb64f0a43e/1573631833)

11-year-old ice hockey player Sisu Tyni practises the squat position. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

The squat position is now difficult for school-age children. In the squat position, the knees are bent.

Many children, for example, fall over when they try to get into the squat position. For example, 16 percent of fifth-grade boys cannot properly squat.

The squat position requires muscle strength. However, life is now so easy that getting into a squat is difficult for many.

The squat position is also difficult for many children who take part in sports. Experts say that sport is practised in too one-sided a way. More strength training is needed.
