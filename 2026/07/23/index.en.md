## Finnish Horse

Finally, animal news.

Fewer Finnish horses are being born than before.

In recent years, fewer than 1,000 Finnish horses have been born per year. That is a critical limit.

The risk is that the genetic diversity of the Finnish horse will decrease. This means that the horses will be too closely related to each other and foals will not be produced. The health of the breed may also deteriorate.

Fewer and fewer Finnish horses are being born.

Finnish horses are widely used as trotting horses.

Finnish horses were also used in the Finnish military. Over 100,000 Finnish horses served in World War II.
