# Saturday 3.6.2017 (radio)

Spring celebrations have been held in schools. Alcohol is no longer as important at young people's parties as it used to be. A new way of finding work for immigrants is being tested in Finland. Ireland gets a prime minister with an immigrant background.

## School spring celebrations

![Winners find it easy to smile.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9648048)

Happy schoolchildren at the spring celebration of Hansakallio school in Espoo. Photo: Tuomo Björksten / Yle

School summer holidays have started. The holidays began in comprehensive schools and upper secondary schools.

On Saturday, spring celebrations were held in schools. The spring celebration is a happy event especially for young people who have finished school.

This time about 58,000 young people graduated from comprehensive school. There are about 27,000 new graduates from upper secondary school. At the spring celebration the graduates received a white student cap.

## Young people's celebrations

![Police search a girl's bag for alcohol on the school leaving weekend in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6165283)

Open image viewer

Police have monitored young people's partying for many years now, when schools end. Photo: Yle

Many young people celebrate the end of school. Young people celebrate in parks and at home. However, they no longer drink as much alcohol as before.

The police say that young people's use of alcohol has decreased in recent years. The police monitor young people's partying when schools end.

Chief Inspector Marko Hiltula says that, in young people's opinion, drinking alcohol is no longer "pop". Young people know how to have fun without alcohol too.

## Work for immigrants

![Car factory](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9352397)

Open image viewer

Work has been found for immigrants at the Uusikaupunki car factory. Photo: Mikael Rydenfelt / Yle

In Finland a new way of finding work for immigrants is being tested. In the trial, companies give money for immigrants' training, and they get new workers for fields where there are now too few workers.

Authorities believe that in this way work can be found for about 2,500 immigrants.

The first experiences are good. Work has been found for immigrants, for example at the Uusikaupunki car factory, after a few months of training.

The trial lasts 3 years. The European Union also gives money to the trial.

## Ireland's new prime minister

![Leo Varadkar](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9647601)

Open image viewer

Leo Varadkar. Photo: Aidan Crawley / AOP

Ireland is getting a new prime minister. Leo Varadkar will become prime minister, because he was chosen as leader of the Fine Gael party. It is Ireland's largest party.

Ireland is getting a new kind of prime minister in Varadkar. Varadkar has an immigrant background. Varadkar's father moved from India to Ireland in the 1960s.

Varadkar is also gay, and he does not try to hide it. Varadkar will also become the youngest prime minister in Irish history. He is 38 years old.

Ireland is part of the European Union.

## Cold weather

School spring celebrations have been held on Saturday in cold weather.

On Sunday it is a little warmer than on Saturday. Monday is a rainy day, and in the north it may sleet or snow.

Later next week the weather will finally warm up all over Finland.
