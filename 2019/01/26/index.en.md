# Saturday 26.1.2019 (radio)

Authorities have suspended the operations of a care home in Kristinestad. The political parties have started to prepare for the elections. Viveca Lindfors won European bronze in figure skating. Icebreakers are already helping ships at sea. The Finnish Meteorological Institute is warning of hard frost.

## The care company is being investigated

![A man who needs crutches.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10529716)

Open image viewer

A care home must provide good care for the elderly. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

This week the authorities suspended the operations of a care home in Kristinestad in Western Finland. The authorities say that the care of the elderly at the Ulrika care home has been poor.

The care home belongs to a company called Esperi Care. Because Esperi Care has not given the elderly enough good care, the municipality has now taken responsibility for the care home.

Esperi Care admits that there have been problems at the care home. It promises to hire more staff.

The authorities are also investigating other Esperi Care care homes in different parts of Finland.

## Election campaigns start

![Deputy chairperson Sanna Marin at the SDP's election campaign launch.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10615632)

SDP deputy chairperson Sanna Marin. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The political parties have started to prepare for the elections. The parliamentary elections are held in April.

On Saturday, the Social Democrats and the Greens started their election campaigns. They promise voters, for example, that education at upper secondary level will be free for students if they come to power.

The Social Democrats, that is the SDP, and the Greens are opposition parties, which means that they are not in the government now. Which parties get into the government after the elections depends on the election result.

## Lindfors wins European bronze

![Viveca Lindfors](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10615413)

Open image viewer

Lindfors won a European Championship medal in figure skating. Photo: Kirill Kudryavtsev / AFP

Finland has won a medal in the European Figure Skating Championships. Viveca Lindfors brought Finland a bronze medal.

Lindfors finished in 3rd place in a tight competition. The winner and European champion of the competition is Sofia Samodurova of Russia. In 2nd place came Alina Zagitova of Russia.

Finland has not won a medal in the European figure skating championships for many years. The last time, Kiira Korpi brought Finland European silver 7 years ago.

The European figure skating championships are being held this time in Minsk, the capital of Belarus.

## Strength helps

![Icebreaker Otso](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10095588)

Open image viewer

Icebreakers now have a lot of work. Photo: Arctia Oy

There is already thick ice at sea. The icebreaker Voima left on Saturday to help ships in the Gulf of Finland. An icebreaker breaks, that is, cracks the ice so that ships can get through.

In the north, in the Bay of Bothnia, ships have needed the help of icebreakers for some time. The icebreakers Otso, Kontio and Polaris are helping ships in the Bay of Bothnia.

## The frost continues

![Winter landscape from Muonio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10605688)

Open image viewer

Lapland now has hard frost. Photo: AOP

The Finnish Meteorological Institute is warning of hard frost. The frost warning applies to the whole country on Sunday and Monday.

The night between Sunday and Monday is really cold. In the north the frost may be almost 40 degrees, and in the south as much as 25 degrees.
