## French presidential election

Voting in the presidential election has taken place in France today. The election chooses a president for the next 5 years.

In the second round of the election, Emmanuel Macron and Marine Le Pen are facing each other. Macron is a centre-liberal and the current president. Le Pen is the far-right candidate.

Ahead of the election, polls have predicted a win for Macron.

The election result will be known during Sunday evening and night.

## Insecurity in the service sector

![A cleaner mops the floor of a corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3820,h_2149,x_0,y_101/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-852647613b931ca56c3/1634018500)

Open image viewer

Cleaners, for example, face more aggressive behaviour than before. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

Many service sector employees feel unsafe.

Employees say that insecurity in the service sector has increased during the corona years. Customers' behaviour is now less friendly than before. The opinions of employees were asked by the temporary staffing company Barona.

Barona says that customers' threatening behaviour puts a strain on employees. That is why employees are offered, for example, conversation support, where they can go through unpleasant situations.

Customers' unfriendly and even threatening behaviour has also been noticed at Heka, which manages rental housing for the City of Helsinki. Because of this, Heka now offers employees training in which they practise self-defence skills and, for example, how to calm an aggressive person down.

## Letter post delivery

![A postal deliverer and a postbox.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9920661)

Open image viewer

Next year letter post may come even less often. Photo: Yle

Letter post delivery is becoming less frequent across the whole country.

If Parliament approves the proposal for a new postal act, letter post will in future be delivered only 3 times a week.

The new law affects only citizens' letters and cards that have a postage stamp. For example, business letters and invoices will continue as before.

The change in the law is due to the fact that ordinary letter post has moved more and more online.

The new law is intended to come into force at the beginning of next year.

## Snow cover thins

![Snow melts into water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10109350)

Open image viewer

An icicle melts into water. Photo: AOP

The snow cover has thinned considerably in various parts of the country.

Thanks to the warm weather, the snow cover has melted in places by 5–10 centimetres a day, according to the Finnish Meteorological Institute.

However, there is still a lot of snow, especially in Lapland. In Kittilä, for example, there is still over a metre of snow. In the central parts of the country too, there is snow in places, about half a metre.

On the western and southern coasts the snow has partly melted away completely.
