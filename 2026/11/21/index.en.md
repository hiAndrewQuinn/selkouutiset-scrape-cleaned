## Kela subsidies

Finally, news about Kela subsidies.

There are large differences in the amounts of Kela subsidies between different municipalities.

Kela's benefits include housing allowance, basic pension and unemployment insurance.

Last year, Kela paid the least in benefits in Inkoo and Siuntio. The amount of benefits was just under 2,000 euros per inhabitant.

Kela paid the most subsidies in Rautavaara in Northern Savo. The subsidy amount was just under 4,000 euros per resident.

In Helsinki, the subsidy amount was approximately 3,000 euros per resident.

There are large differences in the amounts of Kela subsidies between different municipalities.

The reasons for the differences in support amounts are the age of the residents and the number of unemployed people in different municipalities.
