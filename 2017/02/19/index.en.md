# Sunday 19.2.2017 (radio)

Two young people died in a crash in Sastamala. Many Spaniards want more help for refugees. Aki Kaurismäki got the best director award at the Berlin film festival. Kaisa Mäkäräinen won a bronze medal at the Biathlon World Championships.

## Two young people died in a crash

![Candles and wreckage beside highway 12 on the border of Sastamala and Nokia on Sunday. Two 17-year-olds died in a crash between a moped car and a van on Saturday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9468612)

There has been a bad crash in Western Finland. The crash happened in Sastamala on Saturday evening. Two 17-year-olds died in it, one girl and one boy.

The young people were driving a moped car. They died when a bigger car crashed into their small moped car.

The police have arrested the driver of the bigger car. The police suspect that he crashed into the moped car on purpose.

## There was a big demonstration in Spain

![Thousands of people march for immigrants and refugees in Barcelona in north-eastern Spain on 18 February 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9468414)

A demonstration for refugees in Barcelona. Photo: Alberto Estevez / EPA

In Spain many people want refugees to get more help.

In the city of Barcelona there was a big demonstration with about 160,000 people. They demanded that the Spanish government help asylum seekers.

2 years ago the Spanish government promised that Spain would take in over 17,000 asylum seekers. But Spain has accepted only about 1000 refugees.

## Aki Kaurismäki got the Silver Bear

![Director Aki Kaurismäki pictured with Simon Hussein Al-Bazoon (left) and Sherwan Haji (centre) at the Berlin film festival on 18 February. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9468308)

Open image viewer

Aki Kaurismäki (right) together with Sherwan Haji (centre) and Simon Hussein Al-Bazoon (left). Al-Bazoon and Haji act in Kaurismäki's new film. Photo: Ian Langsdon / EPA

Finnish filmmaker Aki Kaurismäki has received an award in Germany, at the Berlin film festival. Kaurismäki got the award called the Silver Bear. It is given to the best director.

Aki Kaurismäki got the award for the film The Other Side of Hope, which he directed. The film is about a Syrian man who does not get asylum in Finland. But the man gets a Finnish friend who gives him a job in a restaurant. The theme of the film is helping people.

Kaurismäki's film also competed for the main prize of the Berlin film festival, the Golden Bear. But the Golden Bear was won by a Hungarian film.

Aki Kaurismäki said in Berlin that he will stop making films. Kaurismäki has directed many films and won many awards. He turns 60 in April.

## Kaisa Mäkäräinen won a bronze medal

![Kaisa Mäkäräinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9468310)

Open image viewer

Biathlete Kaisa Mäkäräinen. Photo: EPA

Finland's Kaisa Mäkäräinen has won a bronze medal at the Biathlon World Championships. Mäkäräinen skied and shot well. She was third best in the race.

Mäkäräinen won the bronze medal in the mass start race. A mass start means that the competitors start skiing at the same time. The women ski 12.5 kilometres. During the race they stop 4 times to shoot at targets. Every time an athlete misses a target, she has to ski a penalty loop that is 150 metres long.

The Biathlon World Championships were held in Hochfilzen, Austria.
