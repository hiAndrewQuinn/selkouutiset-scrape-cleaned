## Russian actions in Ukraine

![Aerial photo of Mariupol with destroyed apartment blocks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4772,h_2682,x_526,y_848/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9586086289fc362f605/1653211936)

Russia destroyed the city of Mariupol and is now repairing it, so that it can bring Russian residents to the city. Photo: Andrey Borodulin / AFP

New information has come from areas that Russia has taken over in Ukraine.

Russia is removing Ukrainians from these areas and bringing Russians in their place. This is what experts from an American think tank say.

Russia is repairing the traces of war, for example in the city of Mariupol, so that it can bring tens of thousands of Russians to the city.

The experts say that Russia is breaking international agreements when it gives Ukrainians' homes to Russians.

Russia attacked Ukraine in February. The war has lasted almost 8 months.

## Helenius's defeat in boxing

![Deontay Wilder knocked down Robert Helenius.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4884,h_2747,x_0,y_443/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1021512634c047f1da23/1665926308)

Open image viewer

Deontay Wilder (on the left) knocked Robert Helenius to the floor. Photo: Getty Images

Finns have received bad sports news. Finnish boxer Robert Helenius lost an important fight in New York.

Helenius is a professional boxer. He had a heavyweight fight against the American Deontay Wilder.

Wilder knocked Helenius out in the very first round of the fight. Wilder's punch was so powerful that Helenius fell to the floor and lost the fight.

After the fight Helenius had a medical check. Helenius said that he is fine.

It is possible that Helenius's boxing career is now over. Helenius is already 38 years old, and because of the defeat it will be difficult for him to get into important fights again.

## Storm on Crete

In Greece on the island of Crete there has been a bad storm. The storm hit Crete on Saturday.

At least 2 people have died in the storm. They died in floods, which were caused by heavy rain.

Greek authorities say that residents and tourists must be careful because of the storm, also on the islands of Rhodes and Karpathos. The Greek islands are popular holiday destinations.

## Christmas tree for Senate Square in Helsinki

![A Christmas tree was taken to Senate Square in 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1200,h_675,x_0,y_35/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1021483634bc5d89b366/1665910360)

Open image viewer

Last year a Christmas tree was set up in Senate Square in sunny weather. Photo: Annamari Tolonen / Kaupunkiympäristö, Helsingin kaupunki

The city of Helsinki is looking for a Christmas tree for Senate Square.

Residents of the Helsinki region can offer the city a suitable tree, if one can be found on their own plot of land.

The city hopes that the tree is 12-20 metres high and bushy.

The city of Helsinki will accept suggestions until 11 November.

If a suitable Christmas tree is found, the city will cut it down and transport it.

## Weather

![Highest temperatures at the start of the week.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12660697)

The weather is clearly getting cooler in the coming days. Photo: Yle

On Monday the weather becomes dry and the rain decreases. In many places the sun shines. The wind is strong, however.

The temperature falls at the start of the week. On Monday the temperature is between 3 and 12 degrees.

On Tuesday the temperature is between 0 and 10 degrees. At night there may already be frost.

On Tuesday new rain will also come to Finland. In the north it may also snow or sleet a little at night.
