# Monday 3.5.2021 (radio)

There are problems with the participant contracts of reality TV. Restaurant restrictions may perhaps be eased. Complaints were made about how the corona situation has been handled. The safety of masks was studied. More electric car charging stations are coming.

## Problems for reality TV participants

![Mira Ahola speaks to the camera in Yle's premises.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7996046088eeda34494/1620193963)

TV studio. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

There are major problems in the participant contracts of reality TV programmes. The rights of participants are restricted a lot. The contracts even interfere with participants' basic rights, for example freedom of movement and freedom of speech.

An expert says that the participant contracts of reality TV programmes contain things that are unreasonable or unlawful for the participants. Production companies also do not always check the mental health situation of the participants.

At Yle's request, experts examined the participant contracts of the programmes Temptation Island, Big Brother and Au pairs.

## Restaurant restrictions

![A terrace in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4256,h_2394,x_0,y_253/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-797221608028325e0b5/1649939639)

Restaurant restrictions are being considered this week. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

In Finland the need for corona restrictions on restaurants is being assessed again. Yle has received information that the government may decide on Wednesday to ease restaurant restrictions.

The government is considering, for example, whether terraces and restaurants serving food may stay open longer than now.

If the government decides to ease restaurant restrictions, the easing may come into force as early as Friday.

## Complaints about the corona situation

![Corona vaccine bottle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7733986026526db1b0d/1613138171)

Open image viewer

Some of the complaints concern the use of the AstraZeneca vaccine. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

More than 80 complaints have been made to the Parliamentary Ombudsman about how the corona situation has been handled.

The complaints concern, for example, the order of vaccination, the distribution of vaccinations by region and the use of the AstraZeneca vaccine. The Ombudsman is examining whether the actions of the authorities have been lawful.

A complaint can be made to the Ombudsman if a person suspects that an authority has not followed the law or has not acted correctly.

## Chemicals in face masks

![Cloth mask.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11309280)

Open image viewer

There were not too many harmful substances in the masks. Photo: Globe Hope

The Safety and Chemicals Agency, Tukes, has studied the safety of face masks. Tukes studied cloth and fibre masks, because people have reported that some masks smell strange.

Tukes' study found that the masks did not contain too many chemicals harmful to health, meaning the masks were safe. However, some of the substances in the masks may cause mild irritation symptoms if there is a lot of the substance.

The Safety and Chemicals Agency Tukes studied more than 20 different cloth masks and fibre masks.

## Electric car charging network

![Tom von Bonsdorff, director responsible for K-Lataus, at a charging point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_564/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-797948608191c87d7fa/1619104364)

The electric car charging network in Finland is growing a lot this year.

Big retail chains are quickly opening more charging places for electric cars.

For example, about 1,000 new charging stations will be built at S-Group locations over 4 years. K-Group and Lidl are also building more electric car charging stations.
