# Saturday 24.9.2022 (radio)

Traffic from Russia to Finland. Criminal gangs' activity. Support for families with children. Forest marches. Weather forecast.

## South-East Finland border traffic

![Russian cars wait to enter Finland at the border between Russia and Finland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5349,h_3008,x_0,y_183/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1012383632ee1b1314ec/1664165085)

Traffic from Russia to Finland has become busier. There have been long queues of cars at the Nuijamaa border station. Photo: Sasu Mäkinen / Lehtikuva

Traffic at the South-East Finland border is being monitored in Finland. Traffic from Russia to Finland has become busier this week, because Russia began mobilisation on Wednesday, that is, men are being called to war. That is why many Russian men want to leave Russia.

The number of Russians arriving in Finland has almost doubled from last week. On Friday almost 7,700 Russians came to Finland. Many of them continue their journey to other European countries.

However, border traffic is still less than before the corona period.

## Criminal gangs' activity

![Illustration of a banned criminal gang](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3839,h_2160,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1011561632c5998602c3/1663941632)

Open image viewer

Members of disbanded criminal gangs wear red and black clothes, for example, because the gang's emblem was red and black. Photo: Eetu-Mikko Pietarinen / Yle

Disbanded criminal gangs have continued their activity in Finland. The criminal gangs United Brotherhood and Cannonball have been banned in Finland. The Court of Appeal has banned their activity.

A police expert says that criminal gangs' activity nevertheless continues, but the activity has changed. Gangs now operate, for example, in smaller groups, that is, cells.

Gang members no longer use the gang's name or emblems on their clothes. However, gang members wear clothes in the same colours that were in the gang's emblem.

## Child families' need for support

![Toys in a sandpit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4256,h_2394,x_0,y_93/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81080760acde8f4002c/1621943657)

Open image viewer

Many things are getting more expensive. That is why more families with children than before need support. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The need for support for families with children has increased. For example, electricity, food and loan interest rates have become more expensive. That is why families with children's costs have risen a lot.

More support than before is being sought from organisations that help families with children. From organisations you can get, for example, food gift cards and support for children's hobbies.

For example, SOS Children's Village gave hobby support to a total of 900 children in the whole of last year. This year SOS Children's Village had already given hobby support to 1,000 children by the end of August, that is, more than last year and in a shorter time.

## Forest marches

![Forest March 2022 environmental demonstration for forest nature and carbon sinks was organised in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5314,h_2989,x_0,y_243/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1012421632f110c77996/1664028993)

Open image viewer

About 1,000 people took part in the forest march in Helsinki. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Forest marches have been organised in Finland on Saturday. Forest marches are demonstrations that demand the protection of forests.

Forest marches were organised in Oulu, Joensuu, Jyväskylä, Raasepori, Tampere and Helsinki. The biggest forest march was organised in Helsinki, where about 1,000 people took part.

Participants in the forest march wanted to remind the government that forests are important and that protecting forests is important.
