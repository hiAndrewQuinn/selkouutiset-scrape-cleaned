## Corona aid to Latvia

![Breathing machine in the intensive care unit at Tyks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5841,h_3286,x_0,y_301/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7830616048cdcf77b25/1615384074)

Some corona patients need a breathing machine. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

Finland is sending corona aid to Latvia. Finland is sending 6 breathing machines to Latvia for the care of corona patients.

Latvia has asked other EU countries for help. Latvia needs help for hospital care of corona patients.

There are now record numbers of corona infections and corona deaths in Latvia. On Thursday Latvia said that there are almost 3000 new corona infections a day. There were 64 new corona deaths, which is more than ever before in one day.

## Corona restrictions for restaurants

![Sign: We check corona passes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-867938616a03d8b8ed4/1634338088)

Open image viewer

A restaurant avoids corona restrictions if it requires customers to have a corona pass. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Corona restrictions for restaurants continue in part of Finland. The government has decided that the restrictions are valid until the middle of November.

The restrictions apply to areas where the corona situation is poor.

Because of the restrictions, restaurants must stop selling alcohol at midnight, that is, at 24:00. They are also allowed to take fewer customers than usual.

However, a restaurant does not have to follow corona restrictions if it requires customers to have a corona pass. The corona pass shows that a person has protection against corona, for example through corona vaccinations.

## Corona vaccinations are progressing

![Niko Torikka gets a second corona vaccination, given by nurse Maroufian Pouye.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-87034561726c5b081ba/1639583307)

Open image viewer

Young people have also taken corona vaccinations because of the corona pass. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

Corona vaccinations are progressing in Finland. 78 per cent of over 12-year-olds have received 2 corona vaccinations.

A corona vaccination gives good protection against corona disease. Most patients in hospital care are corona patients who have not been vaccinated.

Also under-35s have started to take more corona vaccinations than before. The reason may be that many restaurants and bars require customers to have a corona pass. You get the corona pass once you have had 2 corona vaccinations.

## Smoking decreases

![Close-up silhouette of a person smoking a cigarette.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2202,h_1239,x_0,y_55/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6299095e1882948693d/1578666150)

Finns smoke less than before. The statistics of the Institute for Health and Welfare say that 12 per cent of adults smoke every day.

Smoking has decreased in Finland for a long time. 20 years ago about 30 per cent of men and about 20 per cent of women smoked every day.

A smoker usually smokes a little more than a pack of cigarettes a day. That is about 400 packs, that is, almost 4000 euros a year.
