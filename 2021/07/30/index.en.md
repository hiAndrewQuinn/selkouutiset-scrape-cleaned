# Friday 30.7.2021

Corona infections. Corona in the army. Traffic monitoring. Kalajoki forest fire. The weather at the weekend.

The TV plain-language news is on summer break.
The radio plain-language news is made every day, also in the summer.

## Corona infections

![Information sign for a corona test site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83672761028b1e8d516/1627556739)

The number of corona infections in Finland is still growing. On Friday over 700 new corona infections were reported.

Corona is now spreading at events and festivals, and in bars and restaurants. Corona infects people who do not yet have corona vaccinations.

So far about 66 percent of Finns have had the first corona vaccination. 34 percent of Finns have had both corona vaccinations.

There are many corona infections among young people. Many young people do not yet have corona vaccinations, and young people also go to bars and festivals a lot.

## Corona in the army

![Basic training of the Finnish Defence Forces at Santahamina](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5882,h_3309,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7589935ff305674855a/1609764267)

Open image viewer

Many young people have caught corona in the army. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Corona is also spreading in the Finnish army. During July about 300 corona infections have been found in the army.

Most corona infections are among the young people who are now in the army. The Finnish army is compulsory for men and voluntary for women.

The most corona infections are in the Karelia Brigade. There 100 people have caught corona.

Corona is spreading quickly also in the Kainuu Brigade. In a short time about 30 new corona infections have been found in the Kainuu Brigade.

Young people will be vaccinated against corona in the army as quickly as possible.

## Drunk drivers are being monitored

![A man blows into a breathalyser.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10193256)

Open image viewer

The police can find out with a breath test whether a driver has been drinking alcohol. Photo: AOP

Next week the police will monitor traffic more than usual. The police will check whether there are drunk drivers in traffic, that is, whether drivers have used alcohol or drugs.

The police monitor across the whole country that drivers are fit to drive. The police monitor drivers especially in places where there are many summer cottages.

July is ending soon, and many people's summer holidays are ending too. That is why many people are returning from their summer cottages to the cities.

## Kalajoki forest fire

![A ground fire that spread in Kalajoki in North Ostrobothnia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12040450)

Open image viewer

The Kalajoki forest fire area is 300 hectares. Photo: Glenn Hägg / Lehtikuva

The situation in the Kalajoki forest fire area has improved a little. It rained in Kalajoki, and that makes the extinguishing work easier.

Kalajoki is in North Ostrobothnia, about 130 kilometres south of Oulu.

The Kalajoki forest fire started on Monday. It is Finland's biggest forest fire in almost 50 years.

The extinguishing work in Kalajoki will continue for a long time.
