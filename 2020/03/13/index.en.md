## The spread of the coronavirus cannot be prevented

![HL's director general Markku Tervahauta (left) and director of health security Mika Salminen of THL at THL's information event about the coronavirus in Helsinki on 13 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11254992)

Open image viewer

THL is the highest health authority in Finland. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finland's health authorities say that the spread of the coronavirus can no longer be prevented.

The authorities say that now it is important that the virus does not spread quickly.

If a person has mild symptoms, they must stay at home straight away. If the symptoms are bad, they must first phone the health centre.

A coronavirus test is no longer done for everyone who has symptoms.

The test is done only for those who belong to particularly important groups.

In Finland 155 coronavirus infections have been confirmed so far.

## Many people now work remotely

![A woman reads an article on a laptop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11211587)

Open image viewer

Remote work can be done even outside, if it is warm enough. Photo: AOP

In Finland people are trying to slow down the spread of the coronavirus. There are many ways to do this.

Many employees have started to work remotely.

There is a lot of work that an employee can do at home on a computer.

The Finnish government also recommends that everyone who comes back to Finland from abroad stays in home quarantine for 2 weeks.

This applies to all people who return from a trip abroad. Children do not go to day care, schoolchildren do not go to school, and adults do not go to work.

## Many things are changing because of the coronavirus

![Female conscripts in the snow during shooting exercises.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11246603)

Open image viewer

The leave of female and male conscripts doing their military service is changing because of the coronavirus. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / AOP

Many things are now changing because of the coronavirus.

The timetable for the matriculation examinations is changing. The real subject exams of the matriculation examination will be held already in the second exam week.

The leave of conscripts is changing.

Conscripts are on leave in alternate weeks. They are on leave for one week and then they are at the garrison for 2 weeks.

Travel agencies have announced that they are cancelling trips in March and April.

Big events may not be held this spring.

All events where more than 500 people could come are now banned.

## The Winter War ended 80 years ago

![Winter war peace](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11255173)

Open image viewer

The Winter War ended 80 years ago. Then the flags were at half-mast, there was a day of mourning flag. Photo: SA-kuva

Church bells rang this morning.

The church bells are ringing, because today in Finland the end of the Winter War is being remembered.

The Winter War between Finland and the Soviet Union ended on 13 March 1940. Today it is 80 years since then.

The Winter War lasted 105 days.
