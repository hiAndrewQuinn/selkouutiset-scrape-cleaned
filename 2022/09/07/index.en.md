## Nurse staffing ratio

Most MPs want the nurse staffing ratio in elderly care not to be given up. However, many say that the reform can be postponed.

This is what Yle's survey shows. 131 MPs answered the survey. 118 said that the nurse staffing ratio must not be given up completely.

The law says that next spring there must be 7 nurses for every 10 elderly people. However, there are too few nurses.

In the opinion of many MPs, April comes too soon.

## Economic growth

![People in a shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_0,y_528/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-93599962458a187f122/1648725261)

Open image viewer

Because of inflation, the purchasing power of many people is weakening. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The bank Nordea forecasts that growth in Finland's economy will stop next year. High inflation, that is, the rise in prices, and rising interest rates weaken the economy.

Consumers' purchasing power is weakening, that is, people have less money to buy food, goods and services.

Europe's energy crisis also affects economic growth.

However, Nordea says that Finland is doing better than many other countries. For example, Finland already has a lot of environmentally friendly energy. In addition, Finland's employment is at a record high.

Nordea forecasts that the economy will still grow by 2.5 percent this year.

## Ukrainian children

![Ukrainian children read the Ella book translated into the Ukrainian language.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5349,h_3008,x_0,y_229/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9642896298a4345f879/1654171488)

Ukrainian children read the Ella book translated into Ukrainian. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

In total about 12,000 children who have fled Ukraine live in Finland.

The Ombudsman for Children says that in total 600 Ukrainian children in Finland are without a guardian. Some of them arrived alone and some, for example, with a relative.

Child Ombudsman Elina Pekkarinen says that in Finland expertise is now needed in treating children's traumas.

## Civilian service

![Reserve military police advance through the smoke during an exercise at the Army's largest reserve refresher exercise of the year 2014.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7976093)

Open image viewer

Reserve military police in an exercise. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

More and more reservists who have done military service are moving to civilian service. The biggest reason for the change is Russia's attack on Ukraine.

This year 3,300 applications for the transfer have already been made. Last year only 460 applications were made.

Interest in moving to civilian service was already growing at the turn of the year. However, the flood of applications reached the Defence Forces' regional offices only in March. Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

## Drug development

![Waist circumference being measured with a tape measure.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6990486)

Finnish researchers are starting to develop medicines for coronary artery disease, type 2 diabetes and fatty liver.

The European Union granted 6 million euros of funding for the international research.

Researchers from 9 countries take part in the drug development.
