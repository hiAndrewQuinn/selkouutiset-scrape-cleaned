# Radio Clear News Saturday 1.7.2023

Opposition criticism. Pride parade. Traffic accident in Kenya. French protests. Suviseurat. The weather.

## Opposition criticism

![Vilhelm Junnila speaks at the speaker's podium. On the other side of the river, behind Junnila, there is a line of people dressed in black.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_1,y_323/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1135364649bc4ef459b4/1688028318)

The opposition parties criticised the government in the case of Minister Vilhelm Junnila. The opposition parties are not part of the government.

The government did not demand that Junnila resign, even though Junnila's speeches have contained Nazi words. That is why, in the opposition's view, the government acted wrongly.

The Finns Party's Junnila decided himself to resign from the post of Minister of Economic Affairs on Friday.

## Pride parade

![Helsinki Pride parade at Senate Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3464,h_1948,x_0,y_360/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113718264a024363860a/1719193489)

Open image viewer

There were about 100,000 people in the Pride parade. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

A Pride parade has been held in Helsinki. People walked from Senate Square to Kaivopuisto, where a park party was held.

The Pride parade is the main event of Helsinki Pride week. This year as many as 100,000 people took part in the event.

Helsinki Pride is a big human rights event. Its theme is the rights of sexual minorities and gender minorities.

The Pride parade reminds people that everyone has the right to be themselves. Still about 50 years ago, homosexual acts were a crime in Finland.

## Accident in Kenya

A serious traffic accident has happened in Kenya in Africa. More than 50 people died and more than 30 people were injured in the accident.

The accident happened in Western Kenya. A lorry drove off the road and crashed into cars and pedestrians.

## French protests

The protests have continued for many nights. Photo: Christophe Simon / AFP

The protests have continued in France. In the night between Friday and Saturday, the police arrested more than 1,300 protesters.

Many French people are angry because of police violence. A 17-year-old boy died on Tuesday when a police officer shot him during traffic monitoring.

The boy was an Arab with an Algerian background. He was buried on Saturday.

## Suviseurat

![Suviseurat in Kauhava 2023, showing the event field on Thursday evening, 29.6.2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5565,h_3130,x_0,y_579/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1136800649ed1cc8e61b/1688130051)

Open image viewer

75,000 people have come to the Suviseurat. Photo: Suviseurat, photo service

The Suviseurat, the summer meetings, are held in Kauhava this weekend. The Suviseurat is a summer festival of the Laestadians.

The Laestadians are one of the revival movements of the Finnish church. The Laestadian revival movement is especially strong in Northern Finland.

In the view of the Laestadians, contraception is wrong. That is why their families are big.

The Suviseurat is also a big event. About 75,000 people take part in the event.

## The weather

![Sunday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113720064a03cab81d72/1688222932)

July has started with rainy weather.

For example, in the Helsinki area it rained more on Saturday than during the whole of June.

Yle meteorologist Anne Borgström says that June was unusually dry.

The rain continues on Sunday too. The temperature in the south is about 20 degrees, and in the north it is cooler.
