# Wednesday 6.11.2019 (radio)

Many organisations will get less money from Veikkaus in the future than now. Finland wants to build more wind power. Swedish Heritage Day was celebrated on Wednesday. There is already a lot of snow in Northern Finland.

## Organisations' money

![Slot machine in close-up. A woman's hand on the button.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10930570)

The money of many organisations is decreasing. The reason is that in the future they will get less money from Veikkaus than now.

The gambling company Veikkaus's income is decreasing, because it is giving up many gambling slot machines. Veikkaus's income will fall by as much as 200 million euros a year.

Veikkaus is giving up some gambling slot machines, because a lot has been said in Finland about the harms of gambling slot machines. Many people are addicted to gambling. They cannot stop playing on the machines, even though they want to stop. That is why in Finland it has been criticised that gambling slot machines are, for example, in shops.

## Wind power

![Wind turbines](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10997405)

Open image viewer

Electricity can be made cleanly with the help of wind. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

Finland wants to build more wind power. Wind power technology is now cheaper than before. Wind power is also clean.

However, in some parts of Finland, only a little wind power is being built. The reason is that wind turbines disturb the army's radars.

It is hoped that the government will solve the problem quickly. For example, new radars can be bought for the army in places where wind turbines do not disturb the radars. The government is investigating the matter.

Investigating is important, so that more wind power can be built across the whole of Finland.

## Swedish Heritage Day

![Road sign in Finnish and Swedish](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10450702)

Open image viewer

The languages of Finland are Finnish and Swedish. Photo: Esa Hiltula / AOP

Swedish Heritage Day was celebrated on Wednesday. Swedish Heritage Day, that is, Svenska dagen, is always on 6 November. On Swedish Heritage Day, Swedish, the second official language of Finland, is celebrated.

About 300,000 Finns speak Swedish as their mother tongue. Finland-Swedes, that is, Swedish-speaking Finns, live most in the coastal areas. Finland-Swedish culture is, however, important for the whole of Finland.

Many famous Finns are Swedish-speaking. For example, the writers Johan Ludvig Runeberg, Zachris Topelius and Tove Jansson wrote in Swedish. Also, for example, the mother tongue of President, Marshal Mannerheim was Swedish.

## Snow situation

![snow situation map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11053926)

Open image viewer

Photo: Matti Huutonen / Yle

There is already a lot of snow in Northern Finland.

The most snow is in Inari, in Lapland. There is already 35 centimetres of snow there. Usually in Lapland there is so much snow only in December.

The weather is also cold. Yle meteorologist Matti Huutonen says that in the north there may already be a permanent snow cover. The snow may only melt next spring.
