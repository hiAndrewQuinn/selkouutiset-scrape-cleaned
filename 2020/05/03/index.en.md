# Sunday 3.5.2020 (radio)

Corona restrictions are being reduced in Europe. In the USA, the coronavirus is spreading in prisons. In Finland, many people are baking now. Weather forecasts are less accurate.

## Corona restrictions are being reduced in Europe

![In the picture there is a view of the city of Barcelona.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11327678)

Open image viewer

A view of Barcelona in Spain. Photo: Pau Barrena / AFP

In many European countries, corona restrictions are being reduced. For example, in Italy from Monday people can meet relatives and pick up food from restaurants.

In Poland, Hungary and Slovenia too, some companies may open their doors next week.

In Germany, the government will decide on Wednesday whether schools and restaurants will open in Germany.

In Spain, home quarantine was eased on Saturday, and Spaniards can now go outside for exercise after 7 weeks.

In Finland too, the government has discussed on Sunday which restrictions can be lifted and when. Earlier this week, the government said that schools will open on 14 May.

## Many corona infections in US prisons

![A prisoner's hands at a prison window, under text that reads &quot;help, we matter&quot;.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11333245)

Open image viewer

A prisoner's hands at a prison window in the USA. Photo: Tannen Maury / EPA

In the USA, the corona epidemic is spreading quickly in prisons. In some US prisons, 80% of prisoners have fallen ill with the disease that the coronavirus causes.

In many US prisons there is little space, because there are many prisoners. The coronavirus spreads easily in them.

In the USA, many prisons are private companies. They do not want to give prisoners coronavirus tests, because tests cost a lot of money. There are 2.3 million prisoners in US prisons.

## Asphalt work has started

![Asphalt workers doing asphalt work in Koisjärvi village in Lohja.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6688525ea7f02879286/1588064374)

Asphalt work in Koisjärvi village in Lohja. Photo: Toni Määttä / Yle

Summer asphalt work has started in Finland. This summer, about 4,000 kilometres of road will be paved in Finland, which is much more than in many years.

The biggest asphalt job is on Road 4, where a section of road from the Orimattila junction to Vierumäki will be paved. The road section is about 50 kilometres long.

The price of asphalt has gone down, because the price of oil has gone down. If the price of oil stays low, a bit more asphalt work can still be done in Finland late in the summer.

## Many people are baking in Finland now

![hands kneading dough](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10436195)

Open image viewer

Here sourdough is being kneaded. No yeast is used in it. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

Finns are baking a lot now, because they have to be at home a lot. Finns have bought three times as much flour as usual. Yeast has also sold a lot in shops.

Many Finns are baking things like sweet buns (pulla) or pot bread (pataleipä), for example. Many share recipes and tell what they have baked. Many try baking something new, for example bread that is made with sourdough, which means without yeast.

Many people like baking. Baking is rewarding, when you get to eat bread or pulla that you have made yourself.
