## Mariupol

Fighting continues in Ukraine over the port city of Mariupol.

However, Russia has not shelled the port of Mariupol. The port is important for transport across the whole area.

Instead, Russia has shelled residential buildings, hospitals and schools in Mariupol.

There are still about 100,000 people in Mariupol who have not been able to leave the city. There are Russian soldiers around the city.

On Tuesday, more than 7,000 people were brought to safety from Mariupol.

The city of Mariupol has suffered major damage during the war. Russia attacked Ukraine a month ago.

There is new technology at the security check at Helsinki-Vantaa. Photo: Terhi Liimu / Yle

Here in Finland, the renewal of Helsinki-Vantaa airport continues. Now the airport's security check is becoming faster.

The security check has received new equipment. Now hand luggage can be checked without passengers having to take liquids or electronics out of their bag.

By summer at the latest, all flights and security checks will be in the same terminal. You can get to the terminal quickly and easily by train, bus or car.

## Corona research

![A woman in protective clothing squirts a sample into a test tube in a laboratory. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12363222)

Open image viewer

Corona is being studied a lot around the world. Photo: Ullstein Bild / AOP

New studies about the corona disease have been published.

A new British study says that the corona disease affects the brain. The corona disease damages, for example, the area of the brain related to smelling.

The brains of corona patients also shrink. The brain gets smaller because of the corona disease by as much as 2 percent.

The corona disease also affects thinking. After the corona disease, people cope worse than before with tasks that require complex thinking. That is what the British study says.

A new German study, on the other hand, says that the effects of the corona disease on the brain are likely to be corrected over time.

After the corona disease, a person may not have the energy to concentrate on things, they may have a headache and be tired. However, the problems gradually go away. The sense of smell also comes back, German researchers say.
