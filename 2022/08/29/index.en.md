## Companies' economic confidence

![Person bends down to take something from the ready-meal shelf in a grocery store.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_321/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97266162b08ee143f26/1700546346)

Especially the outlook for grocery stores has worsened. Photo: Aalto Puutio / Yle

Companies' confidence in the future is falling further. The business lobby group EK has asked companies what they expect from the future.

Especially the confidence in grocery stores, that is, food shops, has fallen. Expectations have also worsened in service industries.

Expectations have improved in the construction industry. Construction companies expect the situation to get better after a long decline.

## Mental health association crisis line

![Man at a computer with headphones and a microphone on his head.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3491,h_1963,x_0,y_256/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9260226228c4700a784/1646839929)

A crisis worker is on duty at the Crisis Line. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

More and more calls are coming to the Finnish Mental Health Association's crisis line.

The reason is that Finns are under more and more stress. For example, the corona situation and the war in Ukraine affect many things in people's lives. That is why they put stress on people.

The Mental Health Association says that in July almost 35,000 calls came to the crisis line. The crisis line cannot answer all the calls, because there are so many. In September, the service will also open in Ukrainian at the Crisis Line.

## Artemis 1 space flight

![An orange and white rocket stands ready for launch, lit up in the darkness.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7123,h_4006,x_0,y_89/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1000676630cbf8dee86f/1661780008)

Open image viewer

Artemis 1 spacecraft at the Kennedy Space Center. Photo: STELLA Pictures / Splash News

The US space agency has postponed the launch of the Artemis 1 spacecraft.

The Artemis spacecraft was supposed to leave the Kennedy Space Center in the USA on Monday to orbit the Moon. The launch had to be cancelled because of technical problems. The US space agency will say later when it will try the launch again.

The Artemis 1 spacecraft is uncrewed, which means there are no people on it. The spacecraft will study the conditions that astronauts will face on the trip to the Moon. There are also Finnish research devices on board. The plan is for people to be sent from Earth to visit the Moon again in 2024, after a 50-year break.

## Pea crop

![Pea field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1278,h_719,x_2,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83431160f928233194a/1626941708)

Open image viewer

Peas do not need expensive nitrogen fertiliser. Photo: Katja Halinen / Yle

The pea harvest will be very good this summer. The Natural Resources Institute Finland estimates that the pea harvest will be almost 90 million kilos.

Last year the pea harvest was under 43 million kilos. This year's harvest is twice as large as last year's.

Pea growing has increased in Finland. Farmers have increased pea growing, because peas do not need much expensive nitrogen fertiliser. The prices of nitrogen fertilisers have risen by as much as 300 percent because of the war in Ukraine.
