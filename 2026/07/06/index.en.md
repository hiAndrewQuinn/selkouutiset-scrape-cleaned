## Online fake doctors

First, news about internet health scams.

Finnish-language health scams are increasing online.

The video service YouTube has videos in which doctors give health advice.

The doctors look real, but they are made with artificial intelligence.

For example, the doctor in this video is not a real person.

Advice from fake doctors can be dangerous or not helpful. For example, the advice will not cure an illness.

Finnish-language health scams are increasing online.

Hoax videos are often made for older people, with topics such as memory problems, blood circulation, and cataracts.

## Wells

Now news about the wells.

This is the yard of **Olavi Nenonen**'s summer cottage in Pieksämäki.

Many wells in summer cottages may dry up this summer.

Olavi Nenonen checks to see if there is water in the yard well.

There is only a little water in the well.

Wells are drying up because lake levels and groundwater are low.

Water levels are tens of centimeters lower than usual.

In Kainuu, the groundwater is up to a meter lower than usual.

The reason is that there was only a little snow in the winter.

Groundwater accumulates in the ground from snow when the snow melts.

Many wells at summer cottages could dry up this summer. Some wells have already dried up.

Experts advise that you should monitor the condition of your own well.

## Visit of the Chinese Foreign Minister

Chinese Foreign Minister **Wang Yi** visited Finland on Sunday.

Wang first met with Foreign Minister **Elina Valtonen** in Helsinki.

Valtonen says he spoke to Wang about Russia's war of aggression in Ukraine. Valtonen said that Europe hopes that China will not support Russia.

After Valtonen, Wang met with President **Alexander Stubb** at Kultaranta in Naantali.

Wang did not give interviews to reporters.

Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi visited Finland on Sunday.

The visit is rare. The last time a Chinese foreign minister visited Finland was over 20 years ago.
