# Sunday 3.1.2021 (radio)

The Prime Minister on corona. Travelling. Norway. Earthquake. Lotto.

## Prime Minister Marin spoke about corona

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin and other party leaders of the government told a press conference in Helsinki on 17 December 2020 about the government's measures to raise the employment rate of people over 55.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11704694)

Finnish Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the government is looking into the need for stricter corona restrictions. The reason is a changed coronavirus, which can spread easily.

Marin spoke on the Radio Finland programme where the Prime Minister answers questions.

Prime Minister Marin hopes that Finland's vaccinations could go faster. Marin said that Finland is, however, only at the beginning of the vaccinations.

In Marin's opinion, there has been no failure at all in the vaccinations in Finland and elsewhere in Europe.

So far about 5,000 vaccine doses have been given in Finland.

The Prime Minister is worried that Finns have gathered in restaurants while travelling. In Marin's opinion, evenings have been spent in restaurants in a carefree way too.

## Many Finns are already booking trips for the summer and next winter

![An empty swimming pool area in Phuket, Thailand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11597613)

Open image viewer

Thailand is one of the Finns' favourite destinations. Photo: Lillian Suwanrumpha / AFP

Every 4th Finn plans to travel abroad as soon as the corona situation calms down. This is what a survey by Yle News, carried out by Taloustutkimus, says.

Half of the respondents do not plan to travel abroad.

Package holidays are now being booked actively both for the summer and for next winter.

Aurinkomatkat says that the favourite destinations for next summer are Greece and Turkey.

For next winter, destinations in the Canary Islands and Thailand have already sold well. Trips to Thailand have already been sold 10 times more than at the same time a year ago.

## The 6th dead person has been found in the Norway landslide

![Landslide area in Gjerdrum, Norway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11721831)

Open image viewer

The landslide area in Gjerdrum. Photo: Jaran Wasrud / NVE

2 more people who died have been found in the landslide area in Norway, Norwegian radio reports.

In total, 6 dead people have now been found in the area. 4 people are still missing.

The landslide in the municipality of Gjerdrum near Oslo happened on Wednesday morning.

About 1,000 people have been evacuated from the area. Almost 30 houses have been destroyed.

## Several earthquakes in Kymenlaakso

![Map of the Kouvola earthquake.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11722495)

Open image viewer

Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

A series of earthquakes happened in the early hours of the morning in Kouvola in Kymenlaakso.

In Koria there were at least 10 earthquakes during the night between Saturday and Sunday. The Institute of Seismology says that the strongest was 2.0 in magnitude.

The emergency centre received many reports of homes shaking and of bangs. No accidents or dangerous situations were observed.

Seismologist Tuija Luhta says that the earthquake in Koria was typical for Finland. Several earthquakes like this happen every year.

## Lotto is 50 years old in Finland

![Lotto balls in a drawing machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6944209)

Open image viewer

There are 40 lotto balls in the drawing machine. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Lotto turns 50 years old in Finland.

Veikkaus began selling Lotto tickets in December 1970. The first Lotto draw was shown on television on 3 January 1971.

In today's Lotto, the player chooses 7 numbers between 1 and 40.
