## Spring parliamentary elections

Li Andersson, Riikka Purra, Sanna Marin and Petteri Orpo were anxious about the election result on the election night of the parliamentary elections last April. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Many supporters of the Greens and the Left Alliance voted for the Social Democrats (SDP) in the parliamentary elections last spring. A new study by Åbo Akademi says that about 30 percent of the supporters of the Greens and the Left Alliance did vote for the Social Democrats in the last election.

One big reason voters switched was the great popularity of the SDP chair, Sanna Marin, says research professor Kimmo Grönlund.

The study also shows that in the spring elections the Centre Party lost support to the National Coalition Party and the Finns Party.

The Swedish People's Party and the Finns Party held on best to their older voters.

## Forest fires in Greece

![Black smoke on the horizon at sunset.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3391,h_1908,x_417,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114836164c25eea0f8f8/1690636882)

There have been many large forest fires in Greece in recent weeks. Photo: Thomas Silén / Yle

The large forest fires in Greece are starting to ease.

In the last two weeks there have been many large forest fires in Greece. Now they have mostly been brought under control, but firefighting work continues, Greek authorities say.

The largest forest fires have been on the islands of Ródos and Corfu and in central Greece. At least 5 people died in the fires, and thousands of people had to flee from the fire. In addition, 50,000 hectares of land and forest burned.

Greece has also been hit by a heat wave, during which temperatures rose as high as 46 degrees. The worst heat is also now starting to ease.

## Forest fires in Finland

![Forest burned in Kalajoki in summer 2021, photographed a year later.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_570/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97365562b301788d55d/1655899056)

Forest burned in Kalajoki in summer 2021. Photo: Juha Kemppainen / Yle

Finland is also preparing for large forest fires.

Juha Laitinen, a special researcher at the Emergency Services College, says that in Finland, for example, risk areas and the equipment needed to put out forest fires are being mapped. This is how preparations are made for the possibility that there may also be large forest fires here in the future.

However, this summer the forest fire situation in Finland has been normal.

![Ropecon event in Helsinki, Luka Kesto. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_68/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114920264c4d03b32d7d/1690621111)

Role-playing fans are gathering in Helsinki this weekend.

The Ropecon role-playing event started yesterday at the Messukeskus in Helsinki, and it lasts until Sunday. About 6,000 people take part in Ropecon every year.

At Ropecon different role-playing games are played, but its programme also includes lectures, panel discussions and workshops.

This year the theme of Ropecon is the past and the future. The theme can also be seen in what the audience wears.

Ropecon has been held for nearly 30 years.
