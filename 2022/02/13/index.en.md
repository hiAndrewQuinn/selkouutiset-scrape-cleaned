# Sunday 13.2.2022 (radio)

The Ukraine crisis. Corona restrictions for restaurants. Reading difficulties. The Olympic Games. Weather.

## The Ukraine crisis

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin arriving for talks at the House of Estates.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3936,h_2214,x_0,y_167/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90949461fa3b97cc565/1643789824)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin commented on the Ukraine crisis in a radio interview. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

Prime Minister Sanna Marin has commented on the Ukraine crisis.

Marin says that Finland can give financial support to Ukraine if Russia attacks Ukraine.

Marin did not say directly whether Finland can also give military aid to Ukraine. Marin said that the question is a difficult one.

Marin commented on the Ukraine crisis in a radio interview on Sunday.

People around the world fear that Russia will attack Ukraine. Russia has already moved soldiers close to Ukraine's border. Russia does not want Ukraine to join the NATO military alliance.

## Corona restrictions for restaurants

![A restaurant table; in the background a restaurant worker is an unclear figure.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4385,h_2466,x_735,y_686/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90703961f15b178b2ad/1643207557)

Open image viewer

Restaurants may be open for longer again. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The corona restrictions for restaurants change on Monday. Restaurants may be open for longer again, because the corona situation has improved a little.

From Monday, restaurants and bars must stop selling alcohol at 11 p.m. and close their doors at midnight.

If the corona situation develops in a good direction, the corona restrictions for restaurants may end completely at the beginning of March.

## Reading difficulties

![In the picture a woman moves her fingers over the text.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7652805)

Open image viewer

Photo: Niko Tuikka / Yle

Many Finns have difficulties reading and writing. Reading difficulty may affect more than 10 percent of Finns. This is what the University of Helsinki says.

There are different kinds of reading difficulties. For example, reading may be slow, or writing may be hard. Reading difficulty often runs in the family.

Reading difficulty is often noticed at school age. About 30 percent of Finnish children get support for learning at school.

## The Olympic Games

![Valtteri Filppula at the Lions' training.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4734,h_2662,x_0,y_10/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9124786203892e082b7/1644398923)

Finland's ice hockey team, the Lions, beat Sweden on Sunday. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

At the Beijing Olympic Games the men's relay was skied on Sunday.

Finland's team was sixth (6th) in the race. The team had 4 skiers, and each skied 10 kilometres.

The Russians won gold in the relay. Norway got silver and France got bronze.

On Sunday Finland's ice hockey team, the Lions, got an important win. Finland beat Sweden 4-3. The win means that Finland goes straight to the quarter-finals.

The game was exciting. Sweden already led 3-0, but Finland equalised. Finland's win was made certain in extra time. Finland scored the goal when 2 minutes of extra time had passed.

## Weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12315770)

Open image viewer

A new rain area will arrive in Finland on Monday. It will rain in the south and central parts. In the north it is sunny.

During the first part of the week, it is frosty only in the north. In the south the temperature is above zero.

The rain and above-zero temperatures in the early part of the week will melt snow in southern Finland. Because of the rain, driving conditions on the roads are poor.
