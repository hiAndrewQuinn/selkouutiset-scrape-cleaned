## Viking Grace ran aground

![The Viking Line passenger ship Viking Grace has run aground off Mariehamn in Åland on 21 November 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4395,h_2472,x_0,y_624/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7448535fb91f8c4bd39/1606390175)

Open image viewer

The ship drifted onto the shore. Photo: Niclas Nordlund / Lehtikuva

The Viking Grace ship has run aground in Åland. The ship was on its way to the port of Mariehamn, but it ran aground in strong wind.

The accident happened on Saturday afternoon, just after 2 o'clock. Viking Grace was on its way from Stockholm to Turku via Åland.

The ship company Viking Line said in the afternoon that there were a little over 400 people on the ship. Nobody was hurt when the ship ran aground.

This is the second time the ship company Viking Line has run aground in a short time. In September, the ship Viking Amorella ran aground near Åland.

## Jan Vapaavuori to lead the Olympic Committee

![Helsinki Mayor and candidate for chair Jan Vapaavuori at the public hearing of the candidates for chair of the Olympic Committee at the Finnish Sports Museum in Helsinki on 21 October 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11607071)

Open image viewer

Jan Vapaavuori. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The Mayor of Helsinki, **Jan Vapaavuori**, has been chosen as the new chair of the Finnish Olympic Committee. The chair was chosen on Saturday afternoon at the Olympic Committee's autumn meeting.

Jan Vapaavuori is 55 years old. He has been, for example, an MP for the National Coalition Party and a minister.

Jan Vapaavuori will start as chair of the Olympic Committee at the beginning of next year. The term is 4 years.

## Almost 500 new coronavirus infections

![The Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district area](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11659088)

Open image viewer

The area of the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

On Saturday, 469 new coronavirus infections were found in Finland. Most of them, over 340 infections, were found in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district.

The Helsinki area has moved to the stage of coronavirus spread, which means that infections are now spreading quickly in the area.

More than 21,000 coronavirus infections have been found in Finland so far.

## Many enquiries to debt advice

![A sign for financial and debt advice in a debt adviser's office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5438859)

Open image viewer

Contacts to debt advice have grown a lot. Photo: YLE

The coronavirus situation is causing financial difficulties for many Finns. Many people have lost their jobs or their work has decreased.

People's contacts to advice services have grown a lot. For example, the Takuusäätiö foundation says that there are now really many contacts to debt advice. Many contacts also come to the financial and debt advice of the legal aid offices.

However, Finns' debts have not been sent to debt collection much during the coronavirus situation. Many creditors understand that people may be in a difficult situation now.
