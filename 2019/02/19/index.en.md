# Tuesday 19.2.2019 (radio)

The police are investigating several cases related to elderly care. Many states are suing President Trump in court. Bernie Sanders wants to be the Democratic presidential candidate in the USA. The spring joint application starts today, Tuesday. The alcohol content of windscreen washer fluid will be limited.

## The police are investigating cases related to elderly care

![Rollators in a row.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8236789)

Open image viewer

The police have several cases related to elderly care under investigation. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The police have about 30 cases related to elderly care under investigation.

The reason for suspecting a crime is often death or injury caused by negligence. The police get reports, for example, from the relatives of elderly people.

If, for example, a service home looks after the elderly poorly, the supervisory authority Valvira investigates the matter. The police will look into the matter if a crime is suspected. The law says when it is a crime.

## States sue President Trump in court

![Donald Trump](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10649586)

Open image viewer

In the USA, 16 states are suing the administration of President Donald Trump. Photo: Jim Lo Scalzo / EPA

In the USA, 16 states have sued the administration of President Donald Trump. In the states' view, the president broke the constitution when he declared a national emergency.

Trump is trying to use the emergency to get money for a wall being built on the border with Mexico. Last week, the US Congress did not agree to give the wall as much money as President Trump wanted.

At the weekend, many civil society organisations also announced that they will challenge Trump's decision in court.

## Bernie Sanders wants to be a presidential candidate

![Bernie Sanders](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10184169)

Open image viewer

Senator Bernie Sanders wants to be the Democratic Party's presidential candidate in the USA. Photo: Michael Reynolds / EPA

Senator Bernie Sanders wants to be the Democratic Party's presidential candidate in the USA in next year's election.

Sanders strongly challenged Hillary Clinton in the Democratic primaries 3 years ago. He got a lot of support, especially from young people. In the primaries, Sanders spoke, for example, about free Nordic-style health care and education.

Bernie Sanders is 77 years old, and he is a senator for the state of Vermont.

## The spring joint application begins

![Aili Akkanen, who is studying to be a baker and confectioner, thanks Espoo's Omnia for fitting her upper secondary courses well with her vocational studies. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10652033)

You can now apply to upper secondary schools and vocational education. Photo: Pekka Koli

The spring joint application for education begins today, Tuesday. You can now apply to study at upper secondary schools and in vocational education.

If the school holds entrance exams, they are in April or May. There are almost 80,000 study places. More than half of the places are in vocational education, and the rest are in upper secondary schools.

You apply to study online at the address opintopolku.fi.

## The alcohol content of windscreen washer fluid will be limited

![Rainy windscreen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6339076)

Open image viewer

Soon, windscreen washer fluid with a lot of methanol will no longer be sold in Finland. Photo: Mari Siltanen / Yle

Soon, windscreen washer fluid with a lot of methanol will no longer be sold in Finland. The ban will come into force in May.

Methanol is a poisonous alcohol. Several people die every year because they drink windscreen washer fluid. One spoonful of windscreen washer fluid can make you blind.
