# Tuesday 25.5.2021 (radio)

The EU is imposing sanctions on Belarus. The supplementary budget is ready. Health services are moving online. The corona period worries children. Advance voting begins.

## Belarus

![A Belavia aircraft at the airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4720,h_2655,x_0,y_361/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81037660aba5ce2cae4/1621861939)

A Belarusian Belavia aircraft at Minsk airport. Photo: AOP

The EU is imposing new economic sanctions on Belarus. The EU also bans Belarusian airlines from entering EU airspace or EU countries' airports.

The reason is that Belarus forced a European aircraft to land in Belarus on Sunday. Belarus arrested an opposition activist who was on the aircraft.

Finnish airline Finnair is also stopping flying in Belarusian airspace.

## Supplementary budget

![Minister Vanhanen poses in front of the Government Palace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3983,h_2240,x_0,y_6/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80961960a8c96b015e3/1621674731)

Minister of Finance Matti Vanhanen says that the budget is one of the biggest in Finland.  Photo: Ilkka Klemola / Yle

The government has finished this year's third supplementary budget.

The supplementary budget increases state spending by just over 2 billion euros and reduces revenue by 200 million euros.

For example, the supplementary budget pays for vaccines and supports children, young people and companies. The supplementary budget will be handed to Parliament on Thursday.

## Health services

![Iida Niemi, a dietitian at Eksote, holding a remote appointment at a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2084,h_1172,x_0,y_456/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7676866013d108c1fae/1611913589)

Open image viewer

A remote appointment can help with many ailments.  Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

The corona period has quickly increased digital health services. For example, you can sign up for a corona test with an electronic form. The form directs you to a test if your symptoms give a reason for it.

Remote doctor visits have also increased a lot. For many matters you can talk with a doctor or nurse by video link, instant message or phone.

Doctors say that not all ailments can be treated at a remote appointment. People must also be able to get to a local appointment.

## Children's wellbeing

![Schoolchild studying remotely](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_351/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6610125e8608a4b1dd9/1585843364)

Open image viewer

Many young people feel that remote school has made it harder to cope at school. Photo: Pasi Takkunen / Yle

The corona situation is weakening children's wellbeing. Nearly 50 percent of children and young people are worried about their own mental wellbeing when the corona situation lasts a long time.

The organisation Save the Children has asked about how children and young people are coping. It interviewed children and young people aged 12–17.

Worry and stress are caused for children by, for example, remote learning and family finances.
More than 50 percent of children and young people say that remote learning has made it harder to cope in their studies.

## Advance voting

Advance voting for the municipal elections begins tomorrow, Wednesday. The advance voting period is 2 weeks. The advance voting period is now longer than usual, so that there is no congestion at the polling stations. The actual election day is 13 June.

You can get more information about the elections from the Ministry of Justice website vaalit.fi
