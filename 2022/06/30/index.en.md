NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg and Finnish President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Lehtikuva

The NATO defence alliance meeting has ended. The meeting was in Madrid, Spain.

At the meeting NATO invited Finland and Sweden to become NATO members. NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg said in Madrid that Finland's and Sweden's accession documents will be signed on Tuesday.

After that, all NATO countries' parliaments must still approve the accession documents. Then Finland and Sweden will be NATO members. That may still take several months.

## Turkey agreement

![Magdalena Andersson and Recep Tayyip Erdoğan shake hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3762,h_2116,x_0,y_224/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97572362bc0131a3b1e/1656488421)

Open image viewer

Swedish Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson and Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. Photo: Henrik Montgomery / EPA

In Sweden there is discussion about the agreement that Sweden and Finland made with Turkey. Turkey did not approve NATO membership negotiations with Sweden and Finland before the agreement.

In the agreement Sweden and Finland promise to fight against terrorism. Turkey wants cooperation against the Kurdish organisation PKK. The PKK is also considered a terrorist organisation by the European Union.

Now the Kurds in Sweden are worried about what the agreement practically means. About 100,000 Kurds live in Sweden.

Swedish Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson says that no one needs to worry if they are not really a terrorist.

## Savonlinna opera festival

![Olavinlinna](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4593,h_2584,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7017115f184fb1d3aa0/1595429253)

Open image viewer

Operas are performed at Olavinlinna. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

The Savonlinna opera festival starts on Friday. The Savonlinna opera festival is Finland's biggest opera festival.

The opera performances are seen in Olavinlinna, which is several hundred years old. This year, for example, the classic operas Aida, Carmen and Tosca are seen.

The Savonlinna opera festival was not held for 2 previous summers because of corona. Usually the opera festival is held every year.

The Savonlinna opera festival lasts for a month. About 70,000 people visit the opera festival every year.

A lot of festivals are held in Finland in the summer.

Timo Lang, a researcher at the Institute for Health and Welfare, says that at festivals people must take the weather into account.

Lang says that in hot weather festival guests must drink and eat enough. Light long-sleeved clothes protect from the sun. It is also good to bring a hat and sunglasses, Lang says.
