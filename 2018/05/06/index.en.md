# Sunday 6.5.2018 (radio)

The National Conciliator Minna Helle is receiving criticism. Chinese people have bought a lot of Finnish food online. The Eurovision song contest begins. Finland won its first game in the ice hockey World Championship. On Sunday a new spring heat record was measured.

## National Conciliator

![Matti Harjuniemi and Minna Helle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10193004)

Open image viewer

Chairman of the Construction Union Matti Harjuniemi and National Conciliator Minna Helle. Photo: Jukka Nissinen / Rakennusliitto, Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

In Finland people are talking about the work of National Conciliator Minna Helle. The job of the National Conciliator is to look for a solution when organisations of employers and employees argue, for example about wages.

The Construction Union, the trade union of construction workers, criticises Minna Helle. The chairman of the Construction Union says that Helle sided with the employers when wages were negotiated.

The Construction Union thinks the reason is that Helle has got a good job at the employers' organisation. Helle will start work as labour market director of the Technology Industries of Finland in June.

Helle herself says that she has done the work of National Conciliator correctly.

## Finnish food in China

![Pirkka, Fazer and Paulig](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9857695)

Open image viewer

Chinese people can buy Finnish food products online. Photo: AOP and Fazer

Finnish food products are popular in China. This is what the Finnish trading company Kesko says.

Kesko started selling food online in China in September. Kesko is working together with the Chinese online shop Alibaba.

Kesko says that Chinese people have bought a lot of breakfast products, berries and chocolate, for example.

## Eurovision

![Saara Aalto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10189988)

Open image viewer

Finland's Eurovision representative Saara Aalto. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Eurovision song contest begins in Lisbon. The opening party is on Sunday evening. The contest is held in the Portuguese capital, Lisbon, because Portugal won the Eurovision last year.

The Eurovision goes on all week. The first semi-final is on Tuesday. Finland is also taking part.

If Finland does well in the semi-final, Finland gets into the final, that is, the last round of the contest. The final is on Saturday.

Finland's representative in the Eurovision is Saara Aalto. She sings a song called Monsters.

## Ice hockey World Championship

![Sebastian Aho at the 2018 ice hockey World Championship.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10192751)

Open image viewer

Sebastian Aho scored 2 goals in Finland's game against South Korea. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

The ice hockey World Championship has also started. This year the championship is in Denmark.

Finland's first game was on Saturday. Finland beat South Korea. Finland scored 8 goals and South Korea 1 goal.

The World Championship goes on for another 2 weeks. Then it will become clear which country is the ice hockey world champion this year.

## Heat record

![Boaters set out to enjoy the sunny weather.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10193223)

Open image viewer

We are heading towards summer. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

Sunday has been a warm day. This spring's new heat record is 21.4 degrees. It was measured in Lahti.

The weather will also stay warm. Yle meteorologist Toni Hellinen says that on Monday it will be sunny and warm in the south. In the north it is colder, and snow may fall in Lapland.

After Monday the rain will also stop in the north, and it will be warm across the whole of Finland for many days.
