## The number of coronavirus infections has risen

![A nurse took a nasopharyngeal sample at the Mehiläinen drive-in testing station in Helsinki on 2 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82809160df14bdc4f97/1625233200)

Corona testing is being done. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Finland and other EU countries are already preparing for a third round of vaccinations in the corona pandemic.

Additional vaccinations may come if the current 2 vaccines do not protect people enough. Virus variants may also increase the need for a third vaccination.

Today 300 new coronavirus infections have been reported.

The growth is mostly due to infections among football European Championship tourists. More than 480 infections have come from the St. Petersburg matches in Russia. There have been 165 secondary infections.

The National Institute for Health and Welfare says that the coronavirus has also spread in restaurants.

## The National Coalition Party is at the top of Yle's poll

![Party support measurement carried out 14.6.–6.7.2021. The three largest parties are the National Coalition Party, the SDP and the Finns Party.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12011510)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

The National Coalition Party is the most popular parliamentary election party, according to Yle's new measurement. The National Coalition Party's support was 19.8 percent.

The second most popular was the SDP, at 19.6 percent. The Finns Party's support was 17.8 percent.

In the measurement the Centre got 13.5 percent support. The Greens got 10.4 percent, and the Left Alliance exactly 8 percent.

The support of the Swedish People's Party (RKP) was 4.5 percent and that of the Christian Democrats 3.3 percent. Movement Now got 1.9 percent support.

## Riikka Purra seeks to become chairperson

![Finns Party's first deputy chairperson Riikka Purra at her press event in Helsinki on 8 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4618,h_2598,x_0,y_447/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82982860e6b43eaca14/1625732211)

Open image viewer

Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

MP Riikka Purra seeks to become the chairperson of the Finns Party.

She is now the party's deputy chairperson. Purra held a press conference about her candidacy.

The next chairperson will be chosen at the party congress in August.

Earlier, MP Sakari Puisto from Pirkanmaa and Ossi Tiihonen from Lohja have registered for the contest.

The current chairperson of the Finns Party, Jussi Halla-aho, is no longer standing for election.

At the press conference Purra said that the party's goal is still to tighten immigration policy.

## Less road surfacing now

![Asphalt worker, machine operator Martin Myllärinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82943260e59af59edbd/1625660770)

Open image viewer

A road is being surfaced with an asphalt machine. Photo: Sini Järnström / Yle

The price of oil has risen. At the same time the price of bitumen, the raw material of asphalt, has risen.

Roads can be resurfaced less. Also, there is less funding than the year before. This year only 100 million euros is set aside for surfacing.

Only half as many roads are being asphalted compared with last year. Last year about 4,000 kilometres were surfaced. That was the largest amount in 15 years.

## Lots of blue-green algae in sea areas

![Blue-green algae on the water surface.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11418380)

Open image viewer

Water containing blue-green algae must not be swallowed or used as wash water or sauna water. Photo: Pirjo Ferin / SYKE

Warm and calm weather has increased the growth of blue-green algae. Blue-green algae is especially in sea areas, says the Finnish Environment Institute.

On the coasts of the Gulf of Finland, the Archipelago Sea and the Bothnian Bay there is an exceptionally large amount of blue-green algae.

In the lakes there is little blue-green algae.
