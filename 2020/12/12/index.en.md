## Coronavirus vaccinations in the USA

![A vial of the Biontech coronavirus vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3071,h_1727,x_0,y_48/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7449985fbb35e7344af/1606104691)

The Pfizer and Biontech coronavirus vaccine. Photo: EPA-EFE/BIONTECH SE

The USA is starting coronavirus vaccinations.

The USA's medicines regulator has given permission to use the coronavirus vaccine developed by the drug companies Pfizer and Biontech. President Trump says that coronavirus vaccinations will start within a day.

The coronavirus is spreading fast in the USA. In just under a week, more than a million people have caught the coronavirus, and more than 3,000 people are dying from the virus each day. In total, almost 300,000 people have already died of COVID-19 in the USA.

## The Communicable Diseases Act in Parliament

![Krista Kiuru in the plenary session of Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11694629)

Open image viewer

Family and Basic Services Minister Krista Kiuru presented the government's proposal on the Communicable Diseases Act to Parliament. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Parliament has dealt with changes to the Communicable Diseases Act today. The changes to the law are meant to curb the coronavirus pandemic.

If the bill is approved, the authorities and municipalities will be able to close, for example, private gyms and move patients from one municipality to another.

## Coronavirus in a care home

![Valona supported housing unit in Pieksämäki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5032,h_2831,x_0,y_393/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7536275fd4c73706d1d/1607780301)

Open image viewer

The coronavirus has put care home workers on sick leave or in quarantine. Photo: Matias Honkamaa / Lehtikuva

There is a severe shortage of skilled staff at an older people's care home in Pieksämäki. The shortage is due to coronavirus infections.

The care home has a little over 40 workers. Of them, 24 have caught the coronavirus. In addition, many workers are in quarantine because of the coronavirus.

Valona elderly services in Pieksämäki say that the difficult situation will continue for many more weeks.

## Natural ice in Northern Lapland

![Hanne Junnilainen skates on the ice of Kilpisjärvi, with Saana in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1910,h_1074,x_0,y_140/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7529855fd21b2ca281a/1607605145)

Open image viewer

Hanne Junnilainen skates on the ice of Kilpisjärvi. Photo: Ilkka Leinonen

In Northern Lapland, conditions for ski-touring-style skating on natural ice are now good. Skating on natural ice means skating on the ice of a sea, lake or river.

For example, in Käsivarsi Lapland there has been enough frost for the lakes to freeze. However, little snow has fallen, so the ice is clear as a mirror. Ice like that is good for skating.

For example, the ice of Kilpisjärvi is now very smooth and shiny. At this time of year Kilpisjärvi usually already has a lot of snow.

Although natural ice is already suitable for skating in the north, the ice elsewhere in the country may still be weak.

## The ice hockey SM-liiga continues

![Pelicans](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11694681)

Open image viewer

Lahti's Pelicans host TPS from Turku next week. Photo: AOP / Tomi Natri

The ice hockey SM-liiga continues next week.

The league was suspended at the start of December, when coronavirus restrictions were tightened in different parts of the country. Now the SM-liiga and the ice hockey clubs have decided that games will continue next Wednesday.

In December, 15 matches will be played. Because of the coronavirus restrictions, the matches will be played without spectators.
