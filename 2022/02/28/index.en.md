# Monday 28.2.2022 (radio)

A majority of Finns support NATO membership. Russia continues its attack on Ukraine. More than 420,000 people have fled Ukraine. Ukraine wants to join the EU immediately.

## Support for NATO membership

![Lead image.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12337137)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle, Graphics: Ilkka Kemppinen

A majority of Finns think that Finland should join the NATO military alliance. In a Yle survey, 53 percent of Finns support Finland joining NATO. Of the respondents, 28 percent oppose NATO membership and 19 percent are unsure of their opinion.

The Taloustutkimus poll was carried out last week, at the same time as Russia attacked Ukraine.

There are now far more supporters of NATO membership than before. In 2017 only 19 percent of Finns wanted Finland to join NATO.

Among the parties, the National Coalition has the most supporters of NATO membership. However, in almost all parties there are more supporters of NATO membership than opponents. Men support NATO membership more than women do.

## Russia's attack on Ukraine continues

![A destroyed armoured personnel carrier in Kharkiv.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-921392621c85d55da5c/1646036504)

Russia continues its attack on Ukraine.

A large number of missile strikes have been carried out in Kharkiv. Kharkiv is Ukraine's second largest city. Ukrainian authorities say that the missiles fired by Russia have also hit residential areas and civilian sites.

Ukraine says that dozens of people have been killed in the strikes. A curfew has been imposed in Kharkiv.

Many missile strikes have also been carried out in the city of Donetsk.

More than 420,000 people have fled Ukraine to other countries. The UN estimates this.

Poland says that more than 150,000 Ukrainians have come to Poland. People have also fled Ukraine to Hungary, Romania and Moldova, for example.

## Ukraine and the EU

![Volodymyr Zelenskyy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3262,h_1834,x_0,y_137/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-921430621c90bfd654d/1646040073)

Ukraine wants to join the European Union immediately. Ukrainian President Volodymir Zelenskyi demands that the EU give Ukraine membership right away.

Normally, joining the European Union takes many years. Zelenskyi said that Ukraine's aim is to be equal with all Europeans.

## S Group and Kesko

S Group is removing Russian products from its shops. About 50 products are being removed from the shelves of S Group's shops. Among them are foods as well as some sports equipment and clothes.

Kesko, too, is ending the buying of Russian products and the export of food to Russia.

Alko is suspending the sale of Russian alcohol.
