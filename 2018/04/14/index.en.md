## A strike on Syria

![A picture released by the French armed forces shows French Rafale fighter jets at the Saint-Dizier military base](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10160206)

Open image viewer

A picture released by the French armed forces shows French Rafale fighter jets at the Saint-Dizier military base late on Friday evening. Photo: AFP

The United States, Britain and France struck three targets in Syria last night.

The strike was made with several missiles. The Syrian army has said that nobody died. According to US President Donald Trump, it was a counter-strike. The aim was to weaken Syria's ability to use chemical weapons.

The operation was a response to last weekend's attack, which was made with chemical weapons in Douma.

## Niinistö was not surprised

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10160161)

Open image viewer

President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Yle

President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö has commented on the strike on Syria. The strike was not a surprise to President Niinistö.

He says that the strike was to be expected, because dramatic news has already been heard recently. According to Niinistö, last night's strike was a punishment and an advance warning.

## Soini: The strike was expected

Soini admits that he is worried about the situation getting worse.

## Children's allergies

![A baby.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10143765)

Open image viewer

Wet wipes have caused allergies in babies. Photo: AOP

Small children have more allergies than before. According to a study done in the United States, allergies can be prevented at home. The study found that wet wipes have caused allergies.

The researcher advises washing your hands before touching a baby and avoiding wet wipes. If you do use wipes, it is a good idea to wash the baby with water afterwards.

## Milk is of high quality

![A glass of milk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7998589)

Open image viewer

Finns like milk. Photo: Pekka Sipilä / Yle

Finns like to drink milk. Finnish milk is now of very high quality and it has few bacteria. In Finland over 95 per cent of milk belongs to the best class.

A farm must be careful about hygiene and take good care of its cows to get into the best class.
