## Serious accident in Raasepori

![Rescue personnel at the scene of the accident.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9901613)

Open image viewer

The level crossing in Raasepori has no warning lights and no barrier that comes down. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

In the morning, a serious accident happened in Raasepori in Western Uusimaa, at a level crossing of a road and a railway track. A Defence Forces transport vehicle and a passenger train collided.

4 people died in the accident. 3 of the dead were conscripts and 1 was a passenger on the train. 11 people were injured in the accident.

The Defence Forces vehicle and the conscripts were taking part in an exercise of the Nyland Brigade.

The cause of the accident is not yet known. The level crossing is unguarded. It has no warning lights and no barrier.

Flags were flown in mourning today at defence administration sites. Finnish flags had been lowered to half-mast.

## Nordea is laying off thousands of people

![Nordea's Nordea Campus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9901479)

Open image viewer

It is not yet known how many people Nordea will lay off in Finland. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Nordea bank is laying off thousands of people worldwide. The bank will cut 6000 employees over 4 years. Nordea explains the layoffs by saying that costs must be cut and efficiency improved. Nordea has almost 32000 employees worldwide.

The head of Nordea's operations in Finland, Ari Kaperi, says that it is not yet known how many people will be laid off in Finland. Kaperi says that Nordea will continue to reduce the number of branches in Finland.

## Shipping companies are increasing security

![Passengers arrive at the West Harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9763280)

Open image viewer

Shipping companies have tightened security. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

Shipping companies have tightened security. Passengers leaving Finland on a ship trip are now checked more often, although the checks are still done at random. Sometimes the identity and tickets of all passengers are checked. Sometimes only some of the passengers are checked.

The shipping companies say that one reason for tightening security is that there have been many violent attacks in Europe in recent years.

## Dry weather on Friday

![A snowy cityscape early in the morning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9901213)

Open image viewer

The first snow fell in Seinäjoki. Photo: Hanne Leiwo/Yle

A lot of snow has fallen in southern and central Finland. A lot of snow fell already last night, and that is why there were problems in the morning traffic.

Tomorrow, Friday, will be dry and sunny in parts of the country. Snowfall is coming to Northern Finland from the west. The temperature will be around zero degrees all over the country.
