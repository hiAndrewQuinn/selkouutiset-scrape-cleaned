## Extremist Islamic opinions in prisons

![An isolation cell in a prison](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9423659)

Open image viewer

Inside Vantaa prison. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

Finnish prisons have a new problem. In Finland there are prisoners who support Islamic extremism.

The authorities are worried about the situation. Elsewhere in Europe it has been noticed that people who carried out terror attacks have links to prisons. The authorities are now thinking about what happens in Finland when an extremist Islamic prisoner gets out of prison.

In Vantaa prison there is a project that is trying to solve the problem. The prisoners get guidance, and the authorities try to make sure that prisoners do not get dangerous ideas from other prisoners.

## Too few new babies

![A baby's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9422194)

Open image viewer

Fewer than 53,000 babies were born in Finland last year. Photo: AOP

In 2016, more people died in Finland than were born. This happened now for the first time. Before, only in the war years have more people died than been born.

Last year, a little under 53,000 babies were born in Finland. There were about 1000 more deaths.

However, Finland's population grew in 2016. The reason is that new residents moved to Finland from abroad.

## Koli's long ice road

![The Vaajasalo ice road has a speed and weight limit. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9393862)

Open image viewer

In Finland, in winter there are also roads on the ice. Photo: Jukka Eskanen/Yle

A long ice road has been opened in Eastern Finland. The ice road goes across Lake Pielisjärvi. The road is 7 kilometres long.

Drivers are very happy that they can drive across the lake. The ice road shortens the journey between the centre of Lieksa and the village of Koli by about 50 kilometres. The road is called the Koli ice road.

## Kustavi's free daycare

![Daycare children eating](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9419995)

Open image viewer

In the municipality of Kustavi, children can go to daycare for free. Photo: Jussi Kallioinen / Yle

Kustavi is trying out free daycare. Children under school age can be in daycare for free for 20 hours a week.

Kustavi hopes that new families with children will move to the municipality. Kustavi has 900 residents.

If the trial succeeds, the plan is to continue free daycare.

## Healthy habits of Helsinki's elderly

![A person walks alone on a pavement in autumn.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9379689)

Open image viewer

It is good for an elderly person to move enough. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Elderly people in Helsinki live more healthily than elsewhere in Finland.

People over 65 in Helsinki exercise more than other elderly people. They also eat a lot of vegetables, and only a few smoke.

Elderly people in Helsinki have one bad habit. They drink more alcohol than elderly people elsewhere in Finland.
