## Relations between Ukraine and Russia

![Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_95/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106955163e22a22aca70/1675766367)

President Niinistö says that there is no possibility of peace in the war in Ukraine right now. Photo: Markku ulander / Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö says that the situation in Ukraine is difficult. Russia has continued its attack on Ukraine for almost a year. Niinistö says that at the moment there are no possibilities for peace in the war.

Niinistö estimates that the war will leave a big mark on relations between Russia and Ukraine. The relations between the countries may be really difficult even after peace is someday achieved.

President Niinistö spoke about the matter in an interview with the news agency STT.

## Nato applications of Finland and Sweden

![Finnish Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson at a press conference in Stockholm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2226,h_1252,x_344,y_237/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107082763e4e1428964f/1675944592)

Finnish Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson met a short time ago. Photo: Daniel Ivarsson / Yle

In the interview President Niinistö also assessed the situation of the Nato applications of Finland and Sweden.

Niinistö estimates that Finland and Sweden will be members of the military alliance Nato at the latest in July, because that is when the Nato summit is. The Nato summit is an important meeting for Nato.

President Sauli Niistö estimates that if Finland and Sweden do not become Nato members by then, it cannot be known when they will become members.

## The Greens do not want to cut education

![Portrait of Maria Ohisalo, chairperson, Green League.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5921,h_3330,x_0,y_355/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106184463ca4b94a2f9b/1674202229)

Chairperson of the Greens Maria Ohisalo. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

The Green Party defends the funding of education in Finland.

Greens chairperson Maria Ohisalo says that the Greens cannot join a government that cuts money from education.

Ohisalo spoke at the Greens' party council meeting in Kouvola. Ohisalo says that also then the Greens will not be in the government, if the government wants to cancel climate measures.

## Earthquake in Turkey and Syria

![Rescue workers work in the rubble.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7682,h_4321,x_0,y_441/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107177763e7a3c8e3a01/1676193389)

Open image viewer

Rescue workers are searching for survivors in the rubble. Photo: Evrim Aydin / AOP

More than 25,000 people have died in the earthquakes in Turkey and Syria. The number may still rise as new information about the victims comes in.

The earthquake happened on Monday. Rescue workers are still searching for people in the rubble of houses.

The UN estimated that almost a million people urgently need food. Finland has sent emergency accommodation, tents and stoves to Turkey. Finland is also preparing aid for Syria.

## Wind power production record

![A wind turbine seen from below against a blue sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106688363da41a43a5b3/1684912683)

Open image viewer

Wind power is Finland's second most important form of energy production. Photo: Taneli Kärki / Yle

A new wind power production record has been set in Finland. Wind power produced about 1,900 gigawatt hours of electricity in January. That is 30 percent more than in October, when the previous wind power production record was set in Finland.

Many new wind turbines have been built in Finland. More wind turbines are also being built.

Wind power is Finland's second largest form of electricity production, right after nuclear power.
