# Monday 4.12.2017 (radio)

Prisoners' health care is being criticised. It is 100 years since Finland's declaration of independence. Saana fell is lit up blue. Light art is seen abroad in honour of independent Finland. The Finnish school in Reykjavik sends video greetings to 100-year-old Finland.

## Prisoners' health care is being criticised

![Helsinki prison.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9955180)

Open image viewer

Health care staff say that health care has become chaos in many prisons. Photo: Juha Kivioja / Yle

Prisoners' health care is being criticised. In an interview with Yle, many prison doctors and nurses say that the current management does not understand how prisoners are treated.

The National Institute for Health and Welfare is now responsible for prisoners' health care. Before, the Criminal Sanctions Agency was responsible for prisoners' health care.

Many permanent prison doctors have left their jobs. That is why there are often temporary doctors in prisons. Sometimes inexperienced doctors have, for example, prescribed opiate medicines to prisoners who use drugs. They are strong painkillers that affect the central nervous system.

## It is 100 years since the declaration of independence

![The Government Palace, the President's presentation hall](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9797344)

Open image viewer

The picture shows the President of the Republic's presentation hall, where the Senate met 100 years ago. Photo: Vesa Marttinen / Yle

Today it is exactly 100 years since the Senate of Finland made the declaration of independence. The Council of State, that is, the government, met in the morning. Representatives of the families of the members of the 1917 Senate were also at the celebration of the historic day.

The session was held in the same hall that was the Senate's session hall 100 years ago.

## Saana fell is now blue

![Saana fell lit up blue.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9962314)

Open image viewer

Saana fell in Lapland is a holy place for the Sámi. Photo: Christopher Lund

The celebration of 100 years of Finland's independence can already be seen today in Kilpisjärvi in Lapland. In the afternoon, Saana fell was lit up blue in honour of Finland. The festive lighting of the fell lasts over 30 hours.

The light work can also be seen in the neighbouring countries Sweden and Norway. The light work was made by light artist Kari Kola.

## Finland is also celebrated abroad

![The Colosseum lit in Finland's colours.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9951446)

Open image viewer

The Colosseum in Rome dresses in blue and white on Finland's Independence Day. Photo: Embassy of Finland in Rome

Light art in honour of Finland will also be seen abroad on Independence Day on Wednesday. Now the Christ statue in Rio in Brazil, San Francisco City Hall in the USA and the Holmenkollen ski jump in Oslo in Norway will also be lit blue and white in the colours of the Finnish flag. In Athens in Greece, the ancient Arch of Hadrian will be lit.

It was already known before that the Colosseum in Rome in Italy, Niagara Falls in Canada and the Globen arena in Stockholm in Sweden will get festive lights in honour of Finland.

Yle will show pictures of the festive lights from different parts of the world. You can follow the event on television, online and on social media.

## The Finnish school in Reykjavik greets its 100-year-old home country

![Pupils of the Finnish school in Reykjavik.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9962608)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

A large number of Finnish children and young people live in different parts of the world and study the Finnish language and Finnish culture in Finnish schools. Now that independent Finland turns 100, the Finnish schools are sending video greetings to Finland.

The newest greetings come from the Finnish school in Reykjavik, Iceland. You can watch the greetings on the Clear News website at yle.fi/selkouutiset, in the Finland 100 section.
