## Corona situation

![Mika Salminen visited Yle's Ykkösaamu in Pasila on 4 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_320/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-84999461331ed4e32a5/1638256512)

Director of the National Institute for Health and Welfare Mika Salminen. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

In Finland, more corona deaths have been reported. During the weekend, 16 people died because of corona.

More corona patients have also come to hospitals. There are already more than 300 corona patients in hospitals.

There is also crowding in emergency care, for example in the Helsinki area.

Mika Salminen, director of the National Institute for Health and Welfare, hopes that people will not go to emergency care because of minor cold symptoms. You can also stay sick at home when a home test shows a corona infection but the symptoms are small.

## Corona vaccinations

![People waiting outside a corona vaccination point run by Kymsote at the Hansa shopping centre in Kouvola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_574/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89651561c99e68ccdc9/1647518502)

Open image viewer

People are queuing for a corona vaccine in Kouvola. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

Corona vaccinations are progressing in Finland.

Almost one million Finns have already received 3 corona vaccinations. That means almost 20 per cent of people over 12 years old have received 3 corona vaccinations.

About 84 per cent of people over 12 years old have received 2 corona vaccinations.

Corona vaccinations give good protection against serious corona disease. That is why corona vaccinations also ease the situation in hospitals.

Corona affects transport around the world too.

For example, flights have been cancelled. During the weekend, more than 8,000 flights were cancelled around the world.

The reason for the cancellations is that many pilots and other aviation workers have caught corona and cannot get to work.

## Kela taxis

![Taxi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6078346)

Open image viewer

For many people, a taxi is the only way to get around to do their errands. Photo: Mirva Korpela / Yle

There will be changes for Kela taxi users next year. A customer can order a Kela taxi from 2 different numbers.

So in all areas there are 2 taxi companies that handle Kela rides. The customer decides for themselves which company they use.

The goal is that competition will improve the quality of Kela rides.

For example, in 2020 Kela paid almost 170 million euros in compensation for taxi trips.

## Nuoret Leijonat

![Finland beat Germany in the match.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4473,h_2504,x_0,y_186/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89641661c8dd9436d33/1640553878)

Finland's Nuoret Leijonat beat Germany on Sunday evening. Photo: Pasi Mennander / Suomen Jääkiekkoliitto

Finland has made a good start at the junior ice hockey world championships. Players at the junior world championships are men under 20 years old.

Finland's first game was against Germany. The Finnish team, Nuoret Leijonat, won the game 3-1. Finland's hero was Samuel Helenius, who scored 2 goals.

The junior ice hockey world championships are played in Canada. Finland's next game is against Austria.

## The weather

The weather warms on Tuesday, that is, the frost weakens.

In a large part of the country there is only a few degrees of frost. Only in Lapland is there still hard frost on Tuesday.

On Wednesday snowfall becomes more common, and the frost also weakens in Lapland.
