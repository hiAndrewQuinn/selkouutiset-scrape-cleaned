# Monday 9.3.2020 (radio)

The number of coronavirus infections in Finland is growing. Finnair is stopping flights to Rome. The price of oil is falling. People want to speed up work-related immigration. There will be rain on Tuesday.

## New coronavirus cases in Finland

![A coronavirus instruction taped to the window of the Haartman Hospital emergency department. The sign forbids entering the hospital without first consulting the healthcare staff by phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11240468)

There is a coronavirus instruction for patients on the door of the Haartman Hospital. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The number of coronavirus infections in Finland is growing. On Monday afternoon it was counted that there are 10 new coronavirus cases in Finland. In total 30 coronavirus infections have now been found in Finland.

About 700 pupils and teachers are in quarantine at a few schools. They are staying at home so that they do not spread the coronavirus.

All pupils have been given the instruction that they must not go to school if they have flu.

## Finnair cuts flights

![A Finnair aircraft at Kuusamo airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11242270)

Open image viewer

Travelling has decreased a lot. There are not enough passengers for the flights. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The airline Finnair is stopping flights to the Italian capital, Rome, because of the coronavirus. Last week Finnair stopped flights to Milan in Northern Italy.

There are many coronavirus infections in Italy. The Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs has advised that it is not a good idea to travel to Italy now.

Finnair is also cancelling other flights to different countries and cities, because all travelling has decreased because of the coronavirus.

Earlier, Finnair had already stopped flights to China, where the coronavirus began to spread.

## The price of oil fell

![A man lifting a petrol pump from its stand for refuelling.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5318884)

Open image viewer

The price of oil has fallen quickly because of the coronavirus. Photo: Uwe Zucchi / EPA

The price of oil has fallen a lot in a few days. Now, on Monday, the price of oil has fallen by about 30 percent, because Saudi Arabia is lowering the price of oil.

Oil consumption has decreased around the world because of the coronavirus, because travelling and movement have decreased and the economy has weakened.

Saudi Arabia is lowering the price of oil so that it can sell more oil. Other oil countries are also lowering the price, so that they can sell oil.

The oil countries did not manage to agree at the weekend on cutting production, because Russia did not want to reduce production.

## Work-related immigration is to be speeded up

![A young redheaded woman fills a coffee machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10663046)

Open image viewer

The restaurant sector wants to hire more workers from abroad, because there is a shortage of workers. Photo: AOP

The government wants to speed up the processing of work-related residence permits. For example, the restaurant sector has a shortage of workers, and workers are needed from abroad.

At the moment, processing work-related residence permits takes about four months. The government wants the time to be shortened to one month.

The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment is investigating how the permit process can be made faster.
