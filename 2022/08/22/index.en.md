## Ukraine's children

![Mikhailo, 5, waited in an air-raid shelter with a soft toy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_518/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9207266219ee788ea40/1645871901)

5-year-old Mikhailo was in an air-raid shelter in Kyiv last February. Photo: Daniel Leal / AFP

Many children have been killed or injured in Russia's attack on Ukraine.

The child rights organisation Save the Children says that so far over 900 children have been killed or injured in the war. The organisation says that on average 5 children are killed or injured every day.

The UN says that most of the child casualties have come when Russia has bombed residential areas of cities.

Millions of children have also been forced to flee their homes because of the war.

## Haavisto spoke to ambassadors

![Pekka Haavisto speaking about Russia's invasion war in Ukraine in the Pikkuparlamentti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5323,h_2994,x_0,y_177/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-997613630346946b985/1661159953)

Open image viewer

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Patrik Molander / Yle

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says that Russia's war of aggression in Ukraine may last for years. Haavisto says that neither Ukraine nor Russia currently has the means to end the war.

Haavisto also says that the escalation of the war, that is, its spreading or intensifying, does not currently seem likely, but it is still possible.

Haavisto spoke at a meeting of Finland's ambassadors in Helsinki.

The day after tomorrow, on Wednesday, it will be half a year since Russia attacked Ukraine.

## Turnip rape and rapeseed

![Rapeseed field in flower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9714889)

Open image viewer

A rapeseed field in flower. Photo: Pauliina Jaakkola/Yle

Turnip rape and rapeseed are cultivated in Finland more than before. The Natural Resources Institute Finland says that turnip rape and rapeseed have been sown about 20 percent more this harvest season than last year.

Turnip rape and rapeseed are oil plants. Protein-rich meal is also obtained from them, which is used as feed for livestock.

The cultivation of turnip rape and rapeseed has increased, because Russia's attack on Ukraine has raised the price of protein meal.

## The athletics European Championships

![Lassi Etelätalo with the Finnish flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4982,h_2802,x_0,y_275/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99742363029244668fe/1661113009)

Open image viewer

Javelin thrower Lassi Etelätalo. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

The athletics European Championships ended on yesterday evening in Munich, Germany.

Finland did historically well in the competitions. Finland got 4 medals in total from the European Championships.

The latest medal came in the evening, when Lassi Etelätalo won bronze in the javelin throw. Before that Wilma Murto won gold in the pole vault and Topi Raitanen in the steeplechase. Kristiina Mäkelä got silver in the triple jump.

The last time there were 4 medals was at the European Championships in Athens in 1982.

Athletics also interested Finnish TV viewers. The European Championships week reached about 3 million TV viewers.
