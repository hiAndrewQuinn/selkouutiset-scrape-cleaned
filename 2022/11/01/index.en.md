## Corona vaccinations

![Nurse holds a corona vaccine and needle in close-up.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4020,h_2258,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-100218563109b7fe3a24/1668858659)

Vaccination and corona disease give good protection against serious corona disease. Photo: Petri Niemi / Yle

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, has reported information on vaccine protection against corona.

THL says that if a person has had corona and has received a vaccination, they have very good protection against serious corona disease. Good protection lasts at least a year. Good protection may perhaps last even longer.

A large part of Finns have this kind of protection, that is, they have had corona and received a vaccination.

THL says that corona vaccinations will not be expanded. Fourth vaccinations are given only to elderly people and risk groups.

## Labour shortage

![Diosalyn Isnec and Jumer Ladores, who come from the Philippines, work as care assistants.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_384/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-100987763285cb65cc78/1725274107)

There is a great shortage of social and health care workers. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The growth of the labour shortage has slowed down. This is what a report by the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment says.

The shortage of labour is greatest in social and health care jobs. There is also a great shortage of kindergarten teachers and special needs teachers.

Jobs with too many workers available are general secretaries, tailors and seamstresses, journalists, and marketing and advertising specialists.

## Taxis

![Taxis queue for customers at Pasila station in Helsinki. Close-up of a taxi sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_225/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10254866358fe1b5365d/1667294994)

Open image viewer

In the city you usually get a taxi easily. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

It has become harder to get a taxi in small municipalities. Taxis do not want to wait for rides in small municipalities, because there are few customers. Taxis prefer to drive to busy cities, where taxis easily get many rides.

The reason is the new Taxi Act, which came into force 4 years ago. Taxis no longer have an obligation to be on duty in different areas.

Taxis can choose where they drive. Taxi drivers say that it is not worth it for them to be on duty in a small municipality, for example at night.

## A jubilee year for the Finnish Association for Persons with Intellectual Disabilities and TPS

![Aarne Intonen and TPS thank the Turku audience.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4636,h_2607,x_0,y_483/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1025830635981718c3ec/1666810279)

The Finnish Association for Persons with Intellectual Disabilities turns 70 years old this year. Turku Football Club TPS turns 100 years old this year.

The Finnish Association for Persons with Intellectual Disabilities and TPS are together organising an event where people are told about intellectual disability. The event will be held tomorrow, Wednesday, at Turku Hall, where the TPS–Kärpät ice hockey game is played. Discount tickets for people with intellectual disabilities have also been offered for the game.

The Finnish Association for Persons with Intellectual Disabilities also helps TPS to tell about the sports club's affairs in plain language.

## Winter tyre season

![Studded tyres, tyre change](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3386,x_0,y_320/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-662925817607edefab/1477928209)

Studded tyres in the car's boot. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Today, Tuesday, the winter tyre season has begun.

Cars must have winter tyres if the road conditions are slippery.

The winter tyre season starts at the beginning of November and lasts until the end of March. Cars can use studded tyres or friction tyres.
