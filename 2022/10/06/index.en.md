## Literature Nobel Prize

![Nobel winner Annie Ernaux.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12651695)

Open image viewer

Writer Annie Ernaux. Photo: CATI CLADERA / EPA-EFE / All Over Press

Writer Annie Ernaux will receive this year's Nobel Prize in Literature.

The Swedish Academy announced this on Thursday in Stockholm.

Ernaux is a French writer. Ernaux is 82 years old, and she has had a long career as a writer.

Ernaux has written a lot about her own life. She has dealt with, for example, her own marriage and abortion.

The Nobel Prize in Literature is the world's most important literary prize.

## Attack on a nursery in Thailand

![Entrance to a nursery.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2800,h_1575,x_0,y_52/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1017467633eaef1c73bc/1665052503)

Open image viewer

The attack took place at a nursery in the city of Nong Bua Lamphu. Photo: AFP

A serious attack has happened in Thailand. About 40 people died when a man attacked a nursery. Among the dead are more than 20 children.

The man had a gun and a knife. After the attack the man fled home. He killed his own family and finally himself.

The man was a former police officer. He was sacked from his job last year. He was dismissed because he used drugs.

## Party support

![Result of the party support poll for September: 1: National Coalition, 2: SDP, 3: PS, 4: Centre, 5: Greens, 6: Left Alliance, 7: RKP, 8: KD, 9: others, 10: Liike Nyt](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3233,h_1818,x_0,y_269/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1016867633d4779b1381/1664960717)

Yle's new party support poll says that support for the Centre Party has fallen. In the support poll people were asked which party they support.

Support for the Centre Party is now about 10 percent. The Centre has lost supporters to the Finns Party, whose support is 17 percent.

The Centre is a government party, but the Finns Party is an opposition party, that is, it is not in the government.

The biggest party in the poll is the National Coalition. Its support is about 24 percent.

The second biggest party is the SDP of Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Its support is about 19 percent.

Party support figures are now being followed closely, because there are elections in Finland in half a year.

In the elections representatives are chosen for Finland's parliament, that is, Parliament. After the elections Finland will also get a new government.
