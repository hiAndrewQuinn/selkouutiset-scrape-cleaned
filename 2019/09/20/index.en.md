# Friday 20.9.2019 (radio)

Google is investing money in Finland. People are marching for the climate around the world. India has donated a statue of Gandhi to Finland. The agility world championships are being held in Turku.

## Google

![Google CEO Sundar Pichai and Prime Minister Antti Rinne (SDP) at a joint press conference on 20 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10980874)

The technology company Google is investing more money in Finland.

Google has a data centre in Hamina. In May, Google said that it would invest 600 million euros in building a new data centre in Hamina.

Now Google says that it will invest another 600 million euros in Hamina. This raises Google's total investments in Hamina to over 2 billion euros.

In addition, Google plans to buy more wind power from Finland. Google is involved in two wind power projects, from which it will buy electricity for the Hamina data centre.

## Climate marches

![Climate demonstration in Stuttgart](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10981755)

Open image viewer

Climate demonstration in Stuttgart, Germany. Photo: Ronald Wittek / EPA

Millions of people all over the world have marched for the climate today. The marchers demand action from companies and decision-makers against climate change.

In Australia, at least 300,000 people took part in demonstrations in different parts of the country. There have also been demonstrations in Thailand, Indonesia, Japan and India.

Europe has also marched. In Germany, almost 500 climate demonstrations were organised today, for example, and in France too people are marching for the climate..

In New York in the USA, there is a major event in the evening where the Swedish climate activist Greta Thunberg will speak.

## Statue of Mahatma Gandhi

![Foreign ministers Subrahmanyam Jaishankar and Pekka Haavisto unveiled a statue of Mahatma Gandhi in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10982046)

Open image viewer

The statue of Mahatma Gandhi was unveiled on Friday afternoon. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Mahatma Gandhi, leader of India's independence movement, has received a statue in Helsinki. The statue is in Allotria Park in the Hermanni district.

The bronze statue is a gift from the Indian government to Finland.

This year marks 150 years since Mahatma Gandhi was born.

Mahatma Gandhi is famous, for example, for developing a way of resisting without violence.

## Agility world championships

![Jaakko Suoknuuti and Dao](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10981853)

Open image viewer

Jaakko Suoknuuti and Dao on the obstacle course. Photo: Paula Collin / Yle

The agility world championships have started in Turku.

About 500 dogs from 40 different countries are taking part in the agility world championships.

Agility is an obstacle course competition for dogs and their handlers. The dog is guided over different obstacles as fast as possible and without mistakes.

Doing well in agility requires good physical fitness from the dog and good teamwork with the handler.

13 dogs and their handlers are taking part from Finland. At least one medal is expected from the Finnish team.

The agility world championships last until Sunday.
