## Nokia lays off staff

![Pekka Lundmark speaking on stage. There is a Nokia logo in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5163,h_2890,x_0,y_215/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11882646530bb4017cb8/1697782326)

The Finnish technology company Nokia is laying off staff. Nokia said on Thursday that it will lay off perhaps 450 employees in Finland.

Nokia has employees in many countries. In two years Nokia plans to lay off perhaps 14 000 employees in different parts of the world.

Nokia is laying off staff because it is trying to make big savings in the coming years.

Nokia is in a difficult situation because its customers are not buying enough Nokia products. One reason is that interest rates have risen.

Nokia CEO **Pekka Lundmark** says that the situation is bad especially in the USA.

For Nokia's employees the layoffs are a shock. They are afraid that more layoffs will come.

Nokia has 86 000 employees in Finland and elsewhere in the world.

## Problems in vocational training

![A person wearing a welding mask is welding sheet metal together.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_229/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-118429065254bdb9a300/1727952667)

Companies say that there are problems in the teaching at vocational schools. This is what a new survey says.

Almost half of the companies say that at vocational school many students do not learn the skills that are needed at work.

That is a big problem, because companies need more workers who have completed vocational school.

According to business organisations, companies and vocational schools should cooperate more.

## Hero dogs

![A dog being petted by its owner.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5051,h_2842,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-102474463578ca834f08/1666682289)

Gin, the 2022 hero dog, rescued an elderly person who had got lost. Photo: Antti Ullakko / Yle

Hero dogs are being rewarded in Helsinki. 22 dogs receive the title of hero. In addition, 17 dogs receive a smaller award.

Hero dogs have done heroic deeds. They have rescued a person whose life was in danger.

Hero dogs have, for example, found people who have got lost. Some dogs have raised the alarm about a fire or a person's medical emergency.

Hero dogs will be rewarded in December at the Helsinki Dog Fair.

Hero dogs have received awards since the 1970s.
