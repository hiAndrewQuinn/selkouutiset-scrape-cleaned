## Law on patient safety

Nurses' strikes may be cancelled.

Nurses intended to go on strike, that is, stop work, in Helsinki's elderly home care next week. However, Minister Aki Lindén says that the strike may not happen.

Parliament will decide on Monday whether the new patient safety act will come into force. If the law comes into force, it will order nurses to work during a strike too. That is why strikes may be cancelled.

Nurses do not want the new law. They do not like that they can be ordered to work by law. Nurses are planning strikes, because they demand better pay.

## Queue for Elizabeth's coffin

![Queen Elizabeth's coffin lies in Westminster Hall in London.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1968,x_0,y_364/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-100887463244fbf75841/1663506339)

On Saturday the queue for the queen's coffin was 8 kilometres long. Photo: Lorena Sopena / AOP

Many Britons want to greet Queen Elizabeth for the last time. The queen died last week.

In London, the British capital, there is a long queue for the queen's coffin. On Saturday the queue was 8 kilometres long. People have been in the queue for as long as over 20 hours.

Nights in London are already cold. Authorities say that people queuing should bring warm clothes. Firefighters have also handed out blankets and hot drinks to people in the queue.

Queen Elizabeth's funeral is on Monday.

## Sense of smell after corona

![A man's face, cropped from the nose downwards.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10043978)

Open image viewer

Corona can affect the sense of smell. Photo: AOP

The sense of smell comes back after corona. This is what a new study shows.

Corona disease can affect the sense of smell. Many corona patients lose their sense of smell. After corona they cannot smell anything.

However, 90 percent of people who have had a mild corona disease get their sense of smell back. The sense of smell often comes back within 2 weeks.

Some corona patients lose their sense of smell for a long time. The sense of smell may only recover, for example, 2 years after the corona disease.

## Turku market square

![Market square photographed from Wiklund's window in 2019 and 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12626480)

Open image viewer

Turku market square during the renovation and now. Photo: Rosa Huuska / Yle, Minna Rosvall / Yle

The renovation of Turku market square is finished. The market square was officially opened on Saturday.

The renovation of the market square took 4 years. The renovation cost over 30 million euros.

In the renovation a car park cave was built under the square and trees were planted on the square. Some small jobs will still be done on the square in autumn.
