# Saturday 4.1.2020 (radio)

The new collective agreement for the technology industry has been approved. More worried notifications are made about elderly people than before. The situation in Iraq is tense. Lux Helsinki has begun.

## The technology industry agreement

![Collective bargaining between the Technology Industries of Finland and the Industrial Union in Helsinki on Thursday 2 January](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11144190)

The negotiations for the technology industry were successful. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The new collective agreement for the technology industry has been approved. Employers and employees approved the agreement on Saturday.

The new agreement raises wages. Pay will improve by over 3 per cent during 2 years.

The technology industry agreement covers about 100,000 employees. For example, they make IT and machines.

Other sectors follow the example of the important technology industry agreement and its pay rises. That is why the negotiations for the technology industry are followed closely.

## Worried notifications

![An old man's hands](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2681,h_1508,x_0,y_385/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4650335a85e2305f66a/1518723727)

Open image viewer

An elderly person does not always know how to say that they need help. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

More worried notifications are made about elderly people than before. The person who makes the notification is worried about an elderly person who may need help.

Anyone can make a worried notification. Usually the person who makes the notification is a relative, neighbour or friend of the elderly person.

The person making the notification is afraid that the elderly person cannot look after themselves. The elderly person may not know how to go shopping or cook food, for example.

When a municipality receives a worried notification, it must investigate the elderly person's situation. Help is arranged for the elderly person if it is needed.

## The situation in Iraq

![A funeral procession.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11144729)

Open image viewer

People in a mourning march in Baghdad on Saturday. Photo: Sabah Arar / AFP

The difficult situation in Iraq continues. On Saturday there was a big mourning march in the capital of Iraq, Baghdad.

The participants of the march mourned the Iranian general Qassem Suleimani. They also protested against the Americans.

The Iranian Suleimani died on Friday in a strike that the USA carried out in Iran's neighbouring country Iraq. Iran has said that it will take revenge for Suleimani's death.

The USA killed Suleimani because, in the Americans' view, he was dangerous. President Donald Trump says that Suleimani was planning attacks against Americans.

There are many American soldiers in Iraq. They are fighting against Isis terrorists.

## Lux Helsinki

![Lantern park](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11143910)

Open image viewer

There is now light art in Helsinki. Photo: LUX Helsinki

Lux Helsinki has begun. Lux Helsinki is a light art festival. It brings light into the middle of the dark winter.

Lux Helsinki presents 9 light artworks to the public in the centre of Helsinki. The works are on a route that runs from Senate Square to the Helsinki City Theatre.

In addition, Lux Helsinki includes 6 light artworks in different parts of Helsinki. You can see light art at the Stoa in Itäkeskus, for example.

Lux Helsinki continues until next Wednesday.
