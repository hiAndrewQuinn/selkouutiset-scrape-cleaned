# Thursday 17.12.2020 (radio)

Vaccinations against the coronavirus will start in EU countries at the end of December. French President Emmanuel Macron has caught the coronavirus. 12 new coronavirus deaths have been reported in Finland. Many municipal pensioners have already received their January pension. Finland's education minister has changed. The weather warms up at the weekend.

## Coronavirus vaccinations in the EU

Vaccinations against the coronavirus will start in EU countries at the end of December.

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen says that coronavirus vaccinations will start straight after Christmas.

Von der Leyen said that the European Union has bought so much coronavirus vaccine that there is enough for all residents of EU countries.

## Finland's coronavirus figures

![ Liisa-Maria Voipio-Pulkki and THL chief physician Taneli Puumalainen at the coronavirus situation briefing in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11702399)

Open image viewer

The authorities spoke about Finland's coronavirus situation on Thursday. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

More people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

On Thursday, 12 new coronavirus deaths were reported. In total, 484 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

On Thursday, 358 new coronavirus infections were also reported.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare is worried that older people have caught coronavirus infections more than a while ago. The coronavirus is especially dangerous for older people.

## Error in pension payments

![The head office of the pension institution Keva](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5963,h_3354,x_0,y_130/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-41721159785e1933bbb/1501060717)

Open image viewer

The pension institution Keva made a mistake in paying municipal sector pensions. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Many municipal pensioners have received their January pension too early. The error affects about 400,000 pensioners.

The pension institution Keva says that the January pension arrived in accounts already today, Thursday. That is why the pension will not be paid in January.

Now the tax office is finding out how the matter affects taxation. Kela, on the other hand, says that it will not claw back, for example, December's basic income support because of this.

The pension institution Keva says that it is possible to return the pension. Instructions can be found on Keva's website, the address is keva.fi.

## The education minister has changed

![ Education Minister Jussi Saramo at a press briefing at the Government Palace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11703771)

Open image viewer

Jussi Saramo is now carrying out the education minister's job. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Finland's education minister has changed.

Education Minister Li Andersson is going on parental leave. Andersson's first child is due soon. Andersson will return to the job of education minister next summer.

Jussi Saramo is now the education minister. Saramo is a 41-year-old MP from Vantaa.

Andersson and Saramo's party is the Left Alliance.

## Weather

In Lapland it has been almost 30 degrees of frost on Thursday. On Friday cloud cover increases and the frost weakens.

In the south and west it will snow and sleet on Friday, and the temperature is above zero.

At the weekend the weather is cloudy, and warmer air is flowing into Finland from the south-west.
