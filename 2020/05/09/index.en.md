# Saturday 9.5.2020 (radio)

Today is Europe Day. Finns' support for the EU has fallen. The coronavirus is spreading slowly in Finland. Corona infections in Finland. Corona causes stress. It may snow in Lapland.

## Finns' support for the EU has fallen

![Book market on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10776223)

Open image viewer

Finns' support for the EU has fallen slightly. Photo: Maija Salmi

It seems that the corona pandemic has affected how Finns feel about the European Union.

Finns' support for the EU has fallen.

Over 50 per cent of Finns view EU membership positively and 20 per cent negatively.

The survey was carried out by the employers' organisation Confederation of Finnish Industries. It estimates that the likely explanation for the change is that cooperation between EU countries has been rather poor during the corona pandemic.

## The coronavirus is spreading slowly in Finland

![people queuing for coffee](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11336306)

Open image viewer

People remember to keep a safety distance when they queue at a café. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Health authorities say that the coronavirus has spread in Finland much less than was estimated. Health authorities are constantly making different estimates of how the coronavirus is spreading in Finland.

Now it seems that the estimates have been wrong.

The spread of corona is being studied with antibody tests. According to the antibody tests, there are really very few infections in Finland.

The health authorities' estimates of the spread of the virus may not be quite realistic.

Making estimates is difficult, the health authorities say.

However, estimates must still be made, so that the Finnish government can make decisions about corona restrictions.

## Corona causes stress

![Three adults and four children in the picture. The photo was taken at Schiphol airport in the Netherlands. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11334179)

Open image viewer

Finnish parents are worried about how they will cope with the corona epidemic with small children. Photo: AOP

Researchers are carrying out international research on how the corona pandemic affects people.

Finns have said that they worry about the economy, loneliness and coping with small children.

The research is being done in 50 different countries.

It will continue for as long as the corona pandemic continues.

## Spring is progressing slowly

Spring is progressing slowly now. The weather is not really warming up.

Tonight there will be frost all over the country.

Tomorrow, Sunday, it will snow in Lapland. In Western Finland it will rain.

In Southern and Eastern Finland the weather is warmer.
