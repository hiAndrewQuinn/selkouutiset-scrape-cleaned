## Espoo bridge disaster

![Police and construction workers under the accident bridge.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7102,h_3994,x_1,y_488/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1112014645cb505c78d3/1683797532)

Several temporary pedestrian bridges in Tapiola, Espoo, because of construction work. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The pedestrian bridge in Tapiola, Espoo, that collapsed yesterday will be demolished over the weekend. The other similar bridges in the area have been closed, and they will be opened only once their safety has been confirmed, the City of Espoo says.

A teacher and more than 20 lower secondary school pupils were injured yesterday, when a temporary pedestrian bridge collapsed in Tapiola, Espoo.

Most of the injured have got home from hospital. The Hospital District of Helsinki and Uusimaa, HUS, says that 14 patients have already got home.

The Accident Investigation Board and the police have investigated the collapsed bridge. The Accident Investigation Board says that 2 transverse beams of the bridge fell, and after that the walking deck of the bridge collapsed.

## Remote schooling wore parents out

![A schoolchild at a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4718,h_2654,x_0,y_442/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6621315e8b3b736b01b/1586183086)

Remote schooling was also exhausting for children's parents. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

Children's remote schooling in the corona years wore out many parents, a new survey says.

In a survey by the University of Jyväskylä and the National Institute for Health and Welfare, over 30 percent of parents felt that they were responsible for children's teaching.

Parents of small schoolchildren and parents whose children have special needs spent the most time supporting remote schooling.

The survey was carried out in spring 2020.

## Finnish citizenship

![A passport in a person's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4242,h_2386,x_0,y_371/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8593276152f01b6b76e/1632827269)

Open image viewer

About 10,000 foreigners received Finnish citizenship last year. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

More people than ever got Finnish citizenship last year.

Statistics Finland says that about 10,000 foreign citizens who live permanently in Finland got Finnish citizenship. That is over 50 percent more than the year before.

Most Finnish citizenships were granted to Russians. Next came Iraqis and Estonians.

The average age of those who got citizenship was 29.

![Käärijä and dancers on a turquoise mat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2047,h_1151,x_0,y_204/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-111046964593a3c4b958/1683570139)

The Eurovision final dress rehearsal was held today in Liverpool, Britain.

Yle's Eurovision journalist Ville Vedenpää says that Finland's representative Käärijä performed confidently in the rehearsals and sang better than in Tuesday's semi-final. Finland's song Cha cha cha is one of the favourites of the contest.

The Eurovision final is tomorrow. Käärijä performs in the final with number 13.
