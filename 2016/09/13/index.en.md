## Newspaper delivery in the countryside is being discussed

![Newspapers on a table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8803475)

Open image viewer

People want newspaper delivery in the countryside to continue five days a week.  Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

In Finland, people are thinking about how people in the countryside will get newspapers in the future. People want delivery of paper newspapers to continue in the countryside five days a week.

The continuation of delivery is at risk because the postal service is doing badly. It wants to reduce deliveries. The Finnish Newspapers Association suggests that delivering the post in the daytime and delivering newspapers

in the morning be combined, and made into just one delivery. Then newspapers can be received in the countryside five days a week in the future too, says the Finnish Newspapers Association.

Posti likes the suggestion.

## Robots are suggested to help carers

![A carer pushes an elderly person in a wheelchair in a corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6087962)

Open image viewer

Robots are suggested to take part in care work.  Photo: Sari Gustafsson / Lehtikuva

Robot assistants are suggested for carers. The Finnish Business and Policy Forum Eva has studied whether robots can be used to help carers. Eva thinks that robots can replace 20 percent of carers' work.

Eva thinks that robot carers can be a reality in the next few years. Eva thinks robots bring efficiency to care work. In addition, carers can do demanding work with patients while robots do the carers' other tasks.

## A flood caused widespread damage in North Korea

![A picture of the city of Hoeryong, North Korea, on 11 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9162589)

Open image viewer

The flood in North Korea has taken the homes of 140,000 people.  Photo: Reuters

There has been a bad flood in North Korea. The aid organisation the Red Cross says that the flooding has caused a lot of damage. At least 130 people have died. It is estimated that 140,000 people have lost their homes. The water has flooded because there has been heavy rain in North Korea. The floods have destroyed whole villages.

## Two theatre men have died

![Kalle Holmberg](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9163251)

Open image viewer

Kalle Holmberg. Photo: AOP

Sad news has come from the Finnish theatre world. Director and theatre counsellor Kalle Holmberg has died. He was 77 years old. Holmberg was an actor and director in theatre, opera, films and television.  Holmberg died of an illness that lasted a long time.

![Pekka Valkeejärvi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9163768)

Open image viewer

Pekka Valkeejärvi.  Photo: Heidi Korsman-Hirvonen / AOP

Actor Pekka Valkeejärvi has also died. He acted in the theatre, in many Finnish films and, for example, in TV2's series Karjalan kunnailla. Valkeejärvi died of a serious illness. He was 58 years old.
