# Saturday 31.10.2020 (radio)

A severe coronavirus disease increases the risk of neurological symptoms. Almost half of Finns hope for online voting in the municipal elections. Actor Sean Connery has died. The importance of religion has decreased in Finland. All Saints' Day was celebrated on Saturday.

## Mild corona causes less damage than thought

![A nurse in protective clothing takes a sample on a ward for coronavirus patients at Turku University Hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11582514)

A nurse took samples on the coronavirus patients' ward at Turku University Hospital in April. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

A severe coronavirus disease, i.e. covid-19, increases the risk of neurological symptoms. Neurology studies the nervous system, i.e. for example diseases of the brain and muscles.

In a mild disease, on the other hand, neurological symptoms are rarer than was thought at the start of the epidemic.

A new international study says that the risk is of the same order as in influenza illnesses. The risk of brain infarction is also smaller than estimated.

In Finland, serious nerve damage has been very rare.

## Online voting for municipal elections gains support

![Sign for an advance voting place.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3386,x_0,y_320/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-7891358dd3ee620c3a/1490894685)

Open image viewer

Municipal elections will be held next spring. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Almost half of Finns hope that in the spring municipal elections people can vote online.

In a survey by the Association of Finnish Local and Regional Authorities, 46 percent supported the possibility of online voting.

Support is at the same level as in 2008, but greater than in the 2017 survey.

A researcher estimated that the coronavirus situation may increase support for online voting. On the other hand, recent news about data security may lower support for online voting.

Municipal elections are on Sunday, 18 April 2021. The term of municipal councils is 4 years.

## Sean Connery has died

![Sean Connery](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11624823)

Open image viewer

Sean Connery in the role of James Bond. Photo: AOP

Scottish actor Sean Connery has died. He was 90 years old.

Connery is known above all for the James Bond films, in which he played the agent Bond. He played in 6 "official" Bond films.

Later he also played in the film Never Say Never Again, made by a different production company.

In many people's view, Connery was the only real James Bond of the silver screen.

## All Saints' Day candles are lit

![Grave candles at a grave.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8417449)

Open image viewer

A candle on a grave is a popular All Saints' Day custom for remembering the dead. Photo: Nina Keski-Korpela / Yle

All Saints' Day was celebrated on Saturday.

Traditionally, candles, flowers and wreaths are taken to graves. About half of Finns take a candle to the graves, and about one tenth take part in an event in church.

All Saints' Day combines 2 celebrations: the memorial day of all saints, in memory of martyrs, and the memorial day of the dead.

In Finnish tradition, All Saints' Day has also been linked with the celebration of the harvest festival, i.e. kekri.
