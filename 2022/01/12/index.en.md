# Wednesday 12.1.2022 (radio)

Advance voting for the regional elections has started. THL proposes closing bars. Corona spreads in the military. The performers of the New Music Competition were announced.

Advance voting for the regional elections has started.

People can vote in advance until next week's Tuesday. In advance voting, you can choose the polling place yourself.

The election day for the regional elections is the 23rd of this month, that is, next week's Sunday.

## Bars

![The empty floor of a bar with bar tables, with a performance stage in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_627/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89719561cc46dacb099/1640778071)

Open image viewer

THL recommends closing licensed restaurants as soon as possible. These include bars, pubs and nightclubs. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

The Institute for Health and Welfare THL proposes that bars be completely closed. THL says that bars must be closed for at least 3 weeks as soon as possible.

THL says that bars must be closed because the coronavirus spreads very quickly. In THL's view, food restaurants do not need to be closed.

Ministers will discuss closing bars on Thursday. The law must be changed so that bars can be closed.

At the moment, bars and restaurants must close their doors at 6 pm. Alcohol sales must stop at 5 pm.

## Corona in the military

![Inspection of equipment in a vehicle hall at Parolannummi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-77136760211effea435/1612794228)

Open image viewer

Face masks are used, among other places, in the Armoured Brigade, indoors as well as outdoors, if a safety distance cannot be kept. Photo: Ville Välimäki / Yle

There are more corona infections in the military than before.

A total of almost 1,200 new corona infections have been found among conscripts and soldiers this year. The most new corona infections are in the Karelia Brigade, the Armoured Brigade and the Pori Brigade. In the Pori Brigade, dozens of new infections have been found during the early part of the week.

During the whole of last year, about 1,700 corona infections were found in the military.

## New Music Competition

![UMK22](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12267509)

Open image viewer

UMK22. Photo: Yle

Finland's representative in the Eurovision song contest is again chosen in the New Music Competition. There are 7 songs in the competition.

The songs are performed by 3 bands. The bands are The Rasmus, Cyan Kicks and Younghearted.

4 artists are also aiming for victory. The artists are Bess, Olivera, Tommi Läntinen and Isaac Sene.

The New Music Competition is at the Logomo in Turku at the end of February. The winner of the competition represents Finland at the Eurovision in Turin, Italy, in May.
