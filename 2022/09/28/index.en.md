## Baltic gas pipelines

![Large bubble caused by the Nord Stream 2 gas pipeline leak in the Baltic Sea near the island of Bornholm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1146,h_644,x_0,y_5/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10134126332fd5ba5c37/1664370911)

Gas is leaking into the Baltic Sea. Photo: Forsvaret, Danmark

German security authorities say that the second Baltic gas pipeline is probably unusable. The Germans say that the Nord Stream 1 gas pipeline may have been damaged so badly that it can no longer be used,

A total of 3 leaks have been found in the Nord Stream 1 and 2 gas pipelines in the economic zones of Denmark and Sweden. In both Denmark and Sweden it is believed that the leaks were caused on purpose.

The cause of the leaks is not yet known. Russia is suspected, but Russia has denied all accusations.

Denmark says that the leak points in the pipes will not be investigated for at least 10 days, because there is still a lot of gas left in the pipes.

## Russia's travel restrictions

![Russians at the Georgian border.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2901,h_1631,x_0,y_177/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10134046332f8e3b87ed/1664299162)

Open image viewer

Russian men and women cross the border from Russia to Georgia. Photo: ZURAB KURTSIKIDZE/ EPA-EFE/All Over Press

Russia intends to tighten travel restrictions.

The Russian government says that foreign passports will no longer be issued to people who have been called up for military service.

Thousands of people have fled Russia since President Putin announced a week ago that mobilisation would begin, that is, said that men will be called to fight in Ukraine. According to various estimates, as many as 200,000 people have left Russia in a week.

## Distance learning

![Students in an auditorium](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3399,h_1909,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-915913620cd8d0d31d7/1679748982)

Open image viewer

Many university students have not wanted to return to face-to-face teaching. Photo: Anna Wikman / Yle

More and more university students want to study the whole degree remotely.

The amount of online teaching at universities grew during the corona period. Now that schools have returned to face-to-face teaching, not all students want to return to campuses any more.

Students at smaller universities in the regions in particular have wanted to continue remote studies.

A job, family, friends and long distances may be the reason why students want to stay in their home regions and study remotely.

In Finnish road traffic 20 fewer people died in January–August than last year. In total about 140 people died in accidents, says the Accident Data Institute.

Of those who died, 12 were pedestrians and 12 were cyclists.

## Lightning

![Lightning appears over Rovaniemi, with the Kemijoki river in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1885,h_1063,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99585262fdd3eaee748/1660802082)

Open image viewer

Lightning flashed over Rovaniemi in August. Photo: Olli-Pekka Louniala / Yle

This summer there has been more lightning than usual.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that between May and September about 157,000 ground lightning strikes were observed in Finland. That is almost 20 percent more than the average of the previous 60 years.

There were thunderstorms especially in August, when about 87,000 ground lightning strikes were observed in Finland.
