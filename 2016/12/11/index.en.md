# Sunday 11.12.2016 (radio)

Sauli Niinistö is the most popular choice for president. A day of mourning in Turkey because of bomb attacks. A Coptic church attacked in Egypt. A new prime minister for Italy. The film The Happiest Day in the Life of Olli Mäki won a prize. Saara Aalto is still competing in X Factor today.

## Sauli Niinistö still the favourite for president

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9332898)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö has strong support from citizens. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Over half of Finns say in a Yle survey that President Sauli Niinistö is the best candidate also for Finland's next president.

The Yle survey listed 46 names, from which you could choose the five best candidates for the next president. The second most popular person for president was Pekka Haavisto of the Green party.

The presidential election is in about a year, in January 2018.

The President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö has not yet announced whether he wants to continue in the job.

Only Matti Vanhanen of the Centre Party has already announced that he is a candidate in the election.

## Turkey has mourned the victims of the bomb attack

![The funeral of police officers killed in the bomb attack in Istanbul on 11 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9346335)

Open image viewer

The police officers who died in the bomb attack were buried in Turkey. Photo: Sedat Suna / EPA

There has been a day of mourning in Turkey on Sunday.

Two bomb attacks were carried out in Istanbul on Saturday evening, in which 38 people died and over 150 were injured. Most of the dead were police officers.

13 people have been arrested because of the attacks. The Kurdistan Freedom Hawks have claimed the attack. It is a radicalised organisation that has carried out several other attacks in Turkey this year.

The previous big terror attack in Turkey was in June at Atatürk airport.

## Attack on a Coptic church in Egypt

![Soldiers guard the Coptic Christians' cathedral in Cairo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9346161)

Open image viewer

Soldiers have been guarding the Coptic church. Photo: Mohamed Hossam / EPA

A devastating bomb attack has also been carried out in Egypt.

The attack was carried out on a church of Christian Copts in Cairo.

At least 25 people died and dozens were wounded in the attack. The attacker is not yet known.

Egypt's Christian Copts have been persecuted for decades.

## A new prime minister for Italy

![Paolo Gentiloni ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9346069)

Open image viewer

Paolo Gentiloni was previously foreign minister. Photo: Giuseppe Lami / EPA

Paolo Gentiloni has been appointed as Italy's new prime minister.

He will form a new government in Italy. The previous prime minister Matteo Renzi resigned because he lost a referendum on changing the constitution.

The change to the constitution would have moved power from the regions to the country's government.

## Hymyilevä mies won a prize

![Jarkko Lahti](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9110233)

Open image viewer

Jarkko Lahti plays the lead role in the film Hymyilevä mies. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

The Finnish film Hymyilevä mies (The Happiest Day in the Life of Olli Mäki) has been chosen as the best European directorial debut of the year, that is, a director's first film, in the European Film Award competition.

The film was directed by Juho Kuosmanen. The film is about a Finnish boxer. The film Hymyilevä mies is also Finland's candidate for a prize at the Oscar gala.

Finland's Saara Aalto also made it to the very final of Britain's X Factor music programme yesterday. Today she is still competing with Matt Terry for the win of the whole competition.
