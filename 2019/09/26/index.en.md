# Thursday 26.09.2019 (radio)

The government hopes for more municipal mergers. Online shopping is becoming more popular. An EU project wants to ease Finns' fear of wolves. The Hunger Day collection has started. Today has been European Road Safety Day.

## Municipal mergers

![Municipal coats of arms on the walls of a corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10398912)

Open image viewer

Every municipality has its own coat of arms. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / AOP

The government hopes for more municipal mergers. Minister for Local Government Sirpa Paatero is starting a study on how the state can support municipalities merging.

However, the government does not want forced mergers. Instead, it hopes that municipalities will merge voluntarily.

The year 2009 was a record year for municipal mergers. Then so many mergers were made that the number of municipalities fell by almost 70.

Finland currently has 311 municipalities in total. Just over a hundred of them are towns.

## Online shopping

![a man shopping online](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10236651)

Open image viewer

You can buy clothes online, for example. Photo: AOP

Online shopping is becoming more popular.

In a Posti survey, 25 percent of respondents said they had bought some products online in the last week. 10 percent of respondents said they buy almost everything they need online. 20 percent of respondents believed that in 2025 they will do almost all their shopping online.

Music, entertainment, games, electronics and IT equipment are the things most often bought online.

## The wolf project

![A wolf in the evening sun.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10956150)

Open image viewer

Many people are afraid of wolves. Photo: Tuomo Björksten / Yle

An attempt is being made to improve Finns' attitude to wolves with a large EU project.

The project starts in October. It is led by the Natural Resources Institute Finland.

The project tries to find ways, for example, to protect farm animals from wolves even better. In addition, it aims to reduce people's fear of wolves and to stop the illegal hunting of wolves.

The estimated cost of the project is about 5.5 million euros.

## Hunger Day collection

![Hunger Day collection, Red Cross](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10417291)

Open image viewer

The Hunger Day box collection continues until Saturday. Photo: Markku Pitkänen / Yle

The Finnish Red Cross's Hunger Day collection has started.

The money raised is used to help people who suffer from natural disasters and conflicts in the world. The money is also used to help people who face sudden accidents here in Finland.

The theme of the collection this year is clean water. The Red Cross says that every day 800 children die because of a lack of clean water.

The box collection continues until Saturday.

## Road Safety Day

![-](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10357172)

Open image viewer

Cars must wait when a child crosses at a pedestrian crossing. Photo: Liikenneturva

Today, European Road Safety Day has been marked.

About 70 people are killed on European roads every day. In addition, several hundred people are injured in traffic every day.

In Finland, there are on average about 240 road deaths a year.

The goal of the day was that nobody dies in traffic today.
