# Wednesday 4.9.2019 (radio)

The post will soon run normally again. Aid work has started in the Bahamas. Britain's parliament is arguing about Brexit. Footballer Teemu Pukki has a funny name.

## The post runs again

![Helsinki Pasila postal centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10955237)

The post will soon run normally. Small delays may still happen.

The strike disrupted post deliveries early in the week. However, the postal workers called off the strike on Tuesday evening.

The strike began when Posti intended to change the working conditions of hundreds of workers, that is, in practice to reduce the workers' pay.

The workers called off the strike when Minister Sirpa Paatero decided that the working conditions would still be considered. The state owns Posti, and Paatero's job is to supervise state companies.

## Aid for the Bahamas

![Buildings destroyed in the storm in the Bahamas on 3 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10953862)

Open image viewer

Help is needed in the Bahamas after storm Dorian. Photo: Adam Stanton / US Coast Guard / EPA

Aid work has started in the Bahamas, in America. Storm Dorian caused major damage in the Bahamas earlier in the week.

At least 7 people died in the storm. The number of dead is probably higher.

Bringing aid to the Bahamas is difficult, because the storm also destroyed airports. Many areas of the Bahamas are under water.

The Finnish Red Cross is also sending aid to the Bahamas. A group of 6 people will leave Finland for the Bahamas.

## British MPs argue about Brexit

![Boris Johnson speaking in parliament in London on 25 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10952575)

Open image viewer

British Prime Minister Boris Johnson. Photo: Jessica Taylor / AFP

The Brexit dispute continues in Britain. In the British parliament, people have argued all Wednesday about how Brexit, that is, Britain's exit from the European Union, is to be handled.

Prime Minister Boris Johnson thinks that Britain must leave the EU at the end of October. Johnson wants to leave even though Britain and the EU are not able to make an exit agreement.

A law is proposed in parliament that would ban leaving without an agreement. The proposers think that the exit must be postponed if no agreement is made.

Prime Minister Johnson wants new parliamentary elections if parliament blocks Brexit by law.

## Teemu Pukki has a funny name

![Norwich's Teemu Pukki looking surprised.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10954379)

Open image viewer

Teemu Pukki. Photo: Stephen Pond/Getty Images

The most famous Finnish footballer at the moment is Teemu Pukki. Pukki plays for Norwich in the English Premier League.

The matches of the English Premier League are followed around the world. Therefore the name of Teemu Pukki has also become familiar to people in many countries.

Now Pukki's name makes people laugh. English people think it is funny that the Finnish word "pukki" can mean a horned animal, a billy goat, or a Father Christmas.

People have laughed the most in Malaysia, in Asia. In Malay, "pukki" is a rude word. It means a woman's genitals. This has been noticed, for example, on Facebook.
