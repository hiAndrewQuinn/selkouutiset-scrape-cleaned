## Respiratory illnesses

![A woman in bed blowing her nose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12686687)

Everyone should stay at home if they fall ill. Photo: Mostphotos

There are now many respiratory illnesses in Finland. Many people have fallen ill with coronavirus, influenza or RSV.

Viruses spread easily, because there are no longer any coronavirus restrictions. That is why everyone should stay at home if they fall ill.

You can protect yourself against coronavirus and influenza by vaccination. It is good to wash your hands often.

## Ukrainian refugees

Finland is ready to take Ukrainian refugees from Estonia. Finland can take 50 to 100 Ukrainians a week from Estonia.

Finnish authorities reported on the matter on Thursday.

So many Ukrainian refugees have come to Estonia that the situation in Estonia's reception system is difficult.

## Ukraine's president in the USA

![Two men shake hands, with a woman beside them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12686689)

Open image viewer

President Joe Biden received Ukraine's president in Washington. Photo: Lehtikuva

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyi has visited the USA. This was the first time that President Zelenskyi has travelled abroad during the war in Ukraine.

Zelenskyi met President Joe Biden in Washington. Biden said that the USA will give Ukraine a new support package of almost 2 billion dollars.

Ukraine will receive Patriot missiles, for example. They help Ukraine to defend the country against Russia's attack.

President Zelenskyi reminded Americans that the war does not concern only Ukraine. The war will decide what kind of world people live in in the future.

The war in Ukraine began 10 months ago. Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

## Holiday traffic

![Queue of cars in a dark, snowy evening.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2267,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-104758963988da26ebfe/1670942159)

Christmas traffic has started on the roads. Many Finns are going to spend Christmas with relatives.

It is estimated that traffic will be heaviest on Friday afternoon. There is especially a lot of traffic from the Helsinki region towards the north.

Return traffic is heaviest on St Stephen's Day. St Stephen's Day is on Monday this year. Then Finns return to the cities.

## Weather

![Snow heap and snow work on a city street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12686676)

Open image viewer

Photo: Kaisa Uusi-Kraapo

On Friday, snowfall will move across the south and central parts of the country. The precipitation may be rain in the south at first.

In the afternoon it snows in the east and north, and the sun shines in the west. There is frost across almost the whole country.

On Saturday, which is Christmas Eve, drier air flows into Finland. The weather also gets colder, and there is frost across the whole country.
