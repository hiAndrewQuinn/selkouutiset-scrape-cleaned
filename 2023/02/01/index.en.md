# Wednesday 1.2.2023 (radio)

Industrial Union strikes began. Parliament passed the Trans Act. The Sámi truth commission wants more time. January was warm. The weather is mostly cloudy.

## Industrial Union strikes

Strikes have started at some workplaces in the technology industry and the chemical industry. The strike lasts 3 days. In total over 10,000 people are on strike. The employees want more pay.

Later employees will go on strike also, for example, in shops and ports, if the labour disputes cannot be settled. In ports the strikes may begin in mid-February.

## Trans Act

![Trans people hugging in the public gallery of the parliamentary chamber.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3983,h_2241,x_0,y_241/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106704163da58ab536c1/1675254723)

Parliament has approved a new Trans Act. The Trans Act is the law on the basis of which a transgender person can change their legal gender.

Parliament approved the Trans Act by 113–69 votes. 13 MPs of the governing Centre Party voted against the law.

From now on a person can change their own gender by making a written application. Previously a psychiatrist's assessment was needed to change gender.

Gender can be changed when a person turns 18.

Transgender means a person who feels that they are in a body of the wrong gender.

## Sámi truth commission

![Minister of Justice, the Sámi Parliament's presidium, a Skolt Sámi representative and the truth and reconciliation commission gathered in the parliament hall of the Sámi culture centre Sajos.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4498,h_2531,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106672863da1e3708939/1675239052)

The Sámi truth and reconciliation commission wants more time. The government set up the truth and reconciliation commission in 2021 to investigate how the Finnish state has discriminated and still discriminates against the Sámi. The aim is also to increase discussion between the state and the Sámi

The commission now wants to continue its work for the years 2024 and 2025 as well. The commission wants to hear more of the Sámi people's views.

The Sámi are the only indigenous people in the European Union area.

## Warm January

![Winter landscape from a bridge and a partly melted river.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6196,h_3485,x_0,y_257/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106683263da3d2ecb399/1675247012)

January was very warm in parts of Finland. The January temperature was 2–5 degrees higher than usual across much of the country.

In Rovaniemi January was warmer than ever before since the late 1950s.

The average January temperature varied from about +1 degree in the south-west archipelago to about -10 degrees in Northern Lapland.
