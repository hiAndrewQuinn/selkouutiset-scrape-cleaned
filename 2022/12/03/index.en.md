## The Interior Minister is worried about gang violence

![Krista Mikkonen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5567,h_3131,x_0,y_267/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1043348638b0a7fc83f5/1671868115)

Interior Minister Krista Mikkonen is worried about youth crime and gang violence in Finland.

Mikkonen said in Yle's Ykkösaamu interview that, according to the police's estimate, there are about 10 street gangs in Finland, and about 200 people may belong to them. The gangs fight among themselves and also commit crimes.

The Interior Minister says that police are needed to fight gang crime. In fact, 700 new police posts are to be created in Finland by 2030, Mikkonen says.

Mikkonen says that there are many young people with an immigrant background in gangs and on their fringes. She says that integration must be improved, so that young people do not need to look for acceptance and their own place in gangs.

## Western Metro completed

![A metro sign showing the westernmost stations on the line: Stensvik, Esboviken and Sökö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4581,h_2576,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1043320638ae61dc36a5/1670047521)

The metro map now has 5 new stations. Photo: Lehtikuva

The capital region is celebrating the Western Metro. The extension of the Western Metro in Espoo was opened today.

The first train left Kivenlahti station early in the morning, shortly before five o'clock. HSL says that the train was full of passengers and the mood was lively. A train arrived at the other end of the metro line, in Helsinki's Vuosaari, shortly before six o'clock.

The extension of the Western Metro from Matinkylä to Kivenlahti is about seven kilometres long. It has five new stations: Finnoo, Kaitaa, Soukka, Espoonlahti and Kivenlahti.

After the expansion, the metro track in Helsinki and Espoo now totals 43 kilometres, and there are 30 stations in total.

## Pet food queue

![Asta the dog received food from a couple of bags at the pet food aid event in Malmi on 3.12.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2736,h_1539,x_0,y_169/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1043376638b39aa66483/1670069662)

Cats and dogs were given food aid for the sixth time. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

Food aid has been distributed to pets in Helsinki.

The animal welfare organisation Rekku Rescue organised the Pet Food Queue event in Malmi, Helsinki. About 300 pet owners came to get help.

The price of pet food has risen a lot over the past year. For example, in S and K-group shops the price of pet food has risen by about 5 to 6 percent since last year.

The tightening of pet owners' finances can be seen, for example, in the fact that animal welfare organisations receive more requests for help than before.
