# Tuesday 23.3.2021 (radio)

The threat of far-right terrorism has grown. Restaurant restrictions continue. There are more foreign companies in Finland than before. There has been a mass shooting in the USA.

The far right is more threatening in Finland than before. This is what the Security Intelligence Service says.

Some people in the far right want to carry out a terror attack. The far right has also planned a terror attack. People have acquired equipment that can be used to carry out a terror attack.

## Restaurant restrictions continue

![Restaurant customer seats taken out of use because of the emergency situation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_628/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7850926051bf212724c/1615978774)

Open image viewer

Restaurant customer seats taken out of use because of the emergency situation. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Restaurants and bars will stay closed for more than 3 more weeks. Restaurants are closed in a large part of the country.

The restriction was supposed to end at the end of this week, but the government has decided to extend it by 3 weeks. So restaurants will be closed until 18 April. The reason is the spread of the coronavirus.

The government is negotiating new corona restrictions on Tuesday evening. For example, the government is considering whether people's movement should be restricted.

## New corona infections

![Sign directing people to a drive-in corona testing site](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_725/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7869616058b06c0320a/1616425213)

There are 640 new corona infections in Finland. The most new corona infections are still in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district.

There has been 1 new death in Finland linked to the coronavirus. There are 302 people in hospital because of the coronavirus.

Almost 743,000 people have now received the first dose of the corona vaccine. More than 13 percent of Finland's population has received the corona vaccine.

There are more foreign companies in Finland than before. Almost 300 new foreign companies came to Finland last year. A year earlier, fewer than 250 foreign companies came to Finland.

Most foreign companies are from Sweden, Norway and Britain.

Foreign companies say that Finnish society works well. The companies also think that Finland is a trustworthy country.

## Mass shooting in the USA

![Several people are reported to have died after a gunman attacked a shop in Boulder, USA.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2048,h_1152,x_0,y_192/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78705660594a9775ee1/1616464918)

There has been a mass shooting in the USA.

A man shot people in a supermarket in the city of Boulder in the state of Colorado. 10 people died. One of the dead is a police officer, who was the first to arrive at the scene.

The man who shot people was himself seriously injured, but he did not die.
