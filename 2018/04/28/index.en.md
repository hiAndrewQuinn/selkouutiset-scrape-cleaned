# Saturday 28.4.2018 (radio)

President Niinistö thinks that the meeting of the leaders of South and North Korea was important. A Russian nuclear power plant is travelling on the Baltic Sea. A new big bus is being tested in Helsinki. Sauna is in fashion in Japan. Finland's under-18 ice hockey team has reached the World Championship final.

## Niinistö comments on the Korea meeting

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10183241)

President Sauli Niinistö in Yle's TV studio. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Finland's President Sauli Niinistö says that the meeting of the leaders of South Korea and North Korea was important. Niinistö thinks it is good that the Koreas are aiming for peace.

South Korean President Moon Jae-in and North Korean leader Kim Jong-un met on Friday. They said that the countries' aim is a peace treaty. The countries have not made a peace treaty since the Korean War, which was more than 60 years ago.

Niinistö commented on the meeting on Saturday on Yle's TV programme Ykkösaamu.

## A nuclear power plant travels on the sea

![St Petersburg's floating nuclear power plant Akademik Lomonosov](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9747336)

Open image viewer

The nuclear power plant set off from St Petersburg. Photo: Peter Kovalev / AOP

A rare vessel is now moving on the Baltic Sea. The vessel is not an ordinary ship, it is a nuclear power plant.

The vessel is Russian, and its name is Akademik Lomonosov. It is the world's only nuclear power plant that floats on water. It is 144 metres long and 30 metres wide.

The vessel's journey started in St Petersburg and continues from the Baltic Sea past Norway to the Arctic Ocean. In Murmansk the plant will be loaded with nuclear fuel. The vessel's destination is the town of Pevek in Siberia. There it will produce electricity.

Environmental organisations think it is a bad thing that Russia is transporting a nuclear power plant by sea.

## A new kind of bus

![Articulated bus](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10181993)

Open image viewer

Almost 130 passengers fit in this bus. Photo: Petri Saari

A new kind of bus is being tested in Helsinki. The bus is big, almost 130 passengers fit in it.

The new bus looks like a tram. The driver has their own cabin at the front of the bus.

Passengers can try the new bus for free on May Day. On May Day the bus will run in Helsinki from Elielinaukio to Kaivopuisto. The bus is easy to notice, it is big and green.

## The Japanese hobby of sauna

![Katsuki Tanaka, who has written works about sauna, has been named sauna ambassador in Japan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10177647)

Open image viewer

Katsuki Tanaka has written about sauna for the Japanese. Photo: Johanna Pohjola / Yle

Sauna is now in fashion in Japan. Young Japanese people are also interested in sauna. Sauna is a trendy hobby.

One reason for the sauna boom is the writer and illustrator Katsuki Tanaka. He has made books about sauna, for example the manga comic Sado, or The Way of the Sauna.

Tanaka's manga has also brought a new word into the Japanese language. When a Japanese person feels good after the sauna, they say "totonotta".

## Under-18 team to the World Championship final

![Finland's under-18 ice hockey team celebrating.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10183477)

Open image viewer

The players of the under-18 team celebrate their win over Sweden on Saturday. Photo: SJL

Sport: Finland's Pikkuleijonat (Little Lions) have reached the World Championship final. The Pikkuleijonat are ice hockey players under 18 years old.

Finland reached the final when the Pikkuleijonat beat Sweden on Saturday. If the Pikkuleijonat win the final, Finland will be this year's boys' world champion.

The final will be played on Sunday evening.
