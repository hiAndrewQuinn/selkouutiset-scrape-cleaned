## Austria's corona vaccination requirement

![Opponents of compulsory corona vaccinations at a demonstration in Austria.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4929,h_2759,x_0,y_513/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88198561990ab292df4/1637419904)

Open image viewer

In Austria people have demonstrated against the vaccination requirement. This picture is from a demonstration last November. Photo: Christian Bruna / EPA

Thousands of people in Austria have demonstrated against the corona vaccination requirement.

The Austrian parliament will vote next week on a general vaccination requirement, as the first country in Europe.

Everyone in Austria must have a corona vaccination from the beginning of February. If a person refuses the vaccination, they may get a fine.

In Austria many doubt whether the vaccination requirement is useful, when the omicron variant of the coronavirus spreads so quickly.

## Defence of Gotland

![On a map, the island of Gotland in the Baltic Sea, which belongs to Sweden.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12269317)

Open image viewer

Photo: Harri Vähäkangas / Yle

Sweden is strengthening its military forces on the island of Gotland.

Swedish public broadcaster SVT says that more soldiers and military vehicles have been brought to Gotland.

Sweden is strengthening Gotland's defence, because Russia has brought military ships to the Baltic Sea in recent days.

Relations between Russia and the West have long been tense because of the situation in Ukraine.

## Ghost flights

![Lufthansa aircraft at Frankfurt airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11806421)

Open image viewer

Lufthansa aircraft at Frankfurt airport. Photo: Armano Babani / EPA

Thousands of flights are being flown with empty aeroplanes in Europe.

Several European airlines fly empty planes in winter, because they want to keep their vital take-off and landing times at European airports. For example, Germany's Lufthansa intends to fly 18,000 ghost flights this winter, that is, flights without passengers.

The European Commission says that empty flights are harmful to the environment and the economy.

Advance voting for the regional elections abroad ends today. In Finland, advance voting continues until Tuesday.

You can also vote in advance at the weekend. There are 900 advance voting places in total. You can vote freely at any of them.

More than half a million advance votes have already been cast. The most eager voting has been in South Savo.

## Northern lights in the night sky

![Northern lights shine green over Saimaa in January 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_508/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90229061e2761f013b9/1697191039)

Open image viewer

The northern lights flared last night over Saimaa. Photo: Kalle Purhonen / Yle

This weekend you can see northern lights in the night sky.

Normally the northern lights are seen in the north, but now they can also be seen in Southern Finland. Last night northern lights were seen, for example, in Lappeenranta, Jyväskylä, Hamina, Rauma and Helsinki.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that space weather is now such that northern lights can be seen in the south also during the next night, that is, the night between Saturday and Sunday.
