# Saturday 8.4.2017 (radio)

The police arrested a suspect in the Stockholm attack. Crisis psychologist: fear may increase in Finland too. Checks on Finland's eastern border became stricter. Municipal elections on Sunday. Roma National Day. Bronze medal for the women's ice hockey team.

## The police arrested a suspect in the Stockholm attack

![A person sought by the Stockholm police](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9554572)

Open image viewer

A person sought by the Stockholm police Photo: AOP

The Stockholm police say that they have arrested one person who is suspected of Friday's terror attack. The man comes from Uzbekistan.

The police have not confirmed the man's possible Isis sympathies.

4 people died and 15 were injured when a man who had stolen a lorry deliberately drove into people in the very centre of Stockholm on Friday afternoon.

## Crisis psychologist: fear may increase in Finland too

![Candles and flowers](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9554814)

Open image viewer

Shocking attacks in close neighbouring countries can increase fear in Finland too. Photo: Maja Suslin / EPA

Crisis psychologist Soili Poijula estimates that the Stockholm terror attack and other attacks that have happened in nearby areas recently can increase the fear of terrorism in Finland too.

She says that for most people the best way to overcome fears is to find out the facts about what happened and try to continue everyday life as normally as possible.

## Checks on Finland's eastern border became stricter

![Nuijamaa border station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6138485)

Open image viewer

On Finland's eastern border, the papers of border crossers are checked more carefully than before. Photo: Yle

The papers of all border crossers are gone through more carefully than before on Finland's eastern border.

The more effective checking is due to a new Schengen border rule, which changed yesterday, Friday.

Now the information of all border crossers is checked against international registers. They show, for example, information about lost or stolen passports.

The goal of the change is to improve the security of the European Union.

## Municipal elections on Sunday

![Municipal election posters in Rovaniemi in April 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9551495)

Open image viewer

In Sunday's municipal elections, decision-makers are chosen for all of Finland's municipalities. Photo: Perttu Ruokangas / Yle

It is the last campaign day for the municipal elections. The chairs of the biggest parties are taking part in campaigning in Southern Finland. The chairs are going around, for example, shopping centres and market squares.

Election day is tomorrow. The polling stations open at nine in the morning and close at eight in the evening.

## Roma National Day

![A Roma woman stands on the stairs and leans on the railing](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9550911)

Open image viewer

Karita Palm from Turku in a traditional Roma woman's dress. Photo: Johanna Manu / Yle

Today, Saturday, Roma National Day is celebrated. It has been in the almanac since 2014.

It is estimated that there are more than 10,000 Roma in Finland. The Finnish Roma are a Finnish linguistic and cultural minority that has lived in Finland for almost 500 years.
