# Sunday 18.3.2018 (radio)

North Korea is being discussed in Helsinki. Russians have voted in the presidential election. The Turkish army has taken the Syrian city of Afrin. The Paralympics have ended in Pyeongchang, South Korea. The wintry weather continues.

## Representatives of the Koreas in Helsinki

![Choi Kang Il at Beijing airport on 18 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10121997)

A North Korean representative travelled via Beijing to the meeting in Helsinki. Photo: Reuters-TV

Representatives of North Korea and South Korea are holding talks in Helsinki.

The countries have not officially told about the meeting. But the media have reported that the Koreas are talking at least about nuclear weapons.

Nuclear weapons are one reason why the meeting interests the international media. North Korea has developed nuclear weapons, and that is why its relations with South Korea and the USA are bad.

People are trying to improve relations between the countries before North Korean leader Kim Jong-un and US President Donald Trump perhaps meet later this spring.

## Russians choose a president

![People putting ballot papers into a ballot box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10122005)

Open image viewer

Russians voted in the presidential election on Sunday. Photo: Igor Kovalenko / AOP

Russians have voted in the presidential election. Russians choose a president for the next 6 years.

There were 8 candidates in the election. Russia's current president Vladimir Putin was also a candidate.

In practice it is certain that Putin will win the election. Most Russians support Putin.

In Russia, many are already thinking about what will happen in the next presidential election. It is in 2024, and then Putin will no longer be a candidate.

## Turkey took Afrin

![Civilians who have fled the Afrin area.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10121712)

Open image viewer

People are fleeing the Afrin area. Photo: Aref Tammawi / EPA

News from Syria.

The Turkish army has taken the Syrian city of Afrin. Afrin is in northern Syria, quite close to Turkey.

Until now Afrin has been held by the Kurds. In Turkey's opinion, Kurdish fighters are terrorists.

A lot of people have fled the city of Afrin. In recent days about 200,000 people have left Afrin.

## The Paralympics ended

![Matti Suur-Hamari](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10119770)

Open image viewer

Matti Suur-Hamari won 2 medals at the Paralympics. Photo: Harri Kapustamäki / Paralympic Committee

The Paralympics have ended in Pyeongchang, South Korea. Disabled athletes from different countries competed in winter sports for more than a week.

Finland won 3 medals at the Pyeongchang Paralympics. Snowboarder Matti Suur-Hamari won one gold medal and one bronze medal. Skier Ilkka Tuomisto brought Finland one bronze medal.

Finland was in 16th place in the medal table. The best country was the USA.

The Paralympics were held in the same place as the Olympics in February. 670 athletes took part in the Paralympics.

## The wintry weather continues

![Highest temperatures from Monday to Wednesday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10122114)

Open image viewer

Spring cannot yet be seen in the temperatures. Photo: Yle Weather

The week begins in wintry weather.

On Monday the temperature in the south is about zero degrees; in the north it is a little below zero. On Tuesday and Wednesday it is below zero all over the country. Because of the north wind, the weather feels cold.

It snows on Monday in southern Finland. In northern Finland a lot of snow may fall in the middle of the week.
