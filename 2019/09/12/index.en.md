# Thursday 12.9.2019 (radio)

The ECB is cutting the deposit rate. Centre leader Katri Kulmuni demands that the government decides on employment measures. Matti Vanhanen has gone on sick leave. Too little is known about being Sami. Hunters still use lead shot.

## The ECB is cutting the deposit rate

![The European Central Bank in Frankfurt.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10967312)

Open image viewer

The European Central Bank in Frankfurt. Photo: Ronald Wittek / EPA

The European Central Bank, ECB, is cutting the deposit rate again. The aim is to improve the economy of Europe. The main steering rate stays at 0 percent.

The ECB says that interest rates will stay at their current level or fall, until the inflation target is reached.

The ECB predicts that the economy of the euro countries will grow more slowly than expected. This year's forecast for economic growth is now 1.1 percent, and next year's 1.2 percent.

## Kulmuni's policy speech

![Centre leader candidate Katri Kulmuni spoke before the leadership votes at the Centre's extraordinary party congress in Kouvola on Saturday 7 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10966390)

Open image viewer

Centre leader Katri Kulmuni. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The new Centre leader, Katri Kulmuni, has given an important speech, that is, a policy speech. She demands that the Finnish government decides next week on measures that will increase jobs.

Kulmuni says that the Centre is ready to do everything it can, so that the government's employment goal is achieved. Kulmuni believes that the most important task of the Centre in the government is to make sure that the economy develops well.

Katri Kulmuni was chosen as leader of the Centre last Sunday.

Speaker of Parliament Matti Vanhanen has gone on sick leave. He has had his kidney cancer removed.

Vanhanen has suffered from health problems. He was taken to hospital because of a heart rhythm disorder in November 2017.

In February, Vanhanen had a dizzy spell in the middle of an election event in Espoo.

## Sami people

![Gákti and silk](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7024089)

Open image viewer

Too little is known about the Sami in Finland. Photo: Vesa Toppari / Yle

In Finland, too little is known about the Sami. This is what the European Commission against Racism and Intolerance says.

The Commission says that, for example, Finnish schools must teach more about the Sami. The report says that more teaching can reduce the hate speech that the Sami experience.

The Sami hope that, for example, Finland will celebrate the Sami National Day more widely.

## Lead shot in hunting

![Game from the waterfowl hunting season.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7422808)

Open image viewer

Game from the duck hunting season. Photo: Mari Nupponen / Yle

Hunters of waterfowl still use lead shot.

Some hunters were found to have lead shot during inspections, and its use has been banned in Finland for more than 20 years. Lead shot is poisonous. For example, white-tailed eagles often die from lead poisoning.

The waterfowl hunting season started on 20 August. Inspections will continue for the whole hunting season.
