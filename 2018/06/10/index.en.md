# Sunday 10.6.2018 (radio)

Deputy chairs have been chosen at the party congresses of the National Coalition Party and the Centre Party. North Korean leader Kim Jong-un has arrived in Singapore. The Finnish army needs top e-sports players. Sewing LAN parties have been held in Finland. Roadside churches are open again.

## The National Coalition Party and the Centre Party chose deputy chairs

![Petteri Orpo and Jan Vapaavuori leave a press event together at the National Coalition Party congress.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10246190)

Chairman Petteri Orpo and Helsinki Mayor Jan Vapaavuori at the National Coalition Party congress. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Deputy chairs have been chosen at the party congresses of the National Coalition Party and the Centre Party.

Ministers Antti Häkkänen and Sanni Grahn-Laasonen will continue as deputy chairs of the National Coalition Party. Member of Parliament Mari-Leena Talvitie was chosen as the third deputy chair.

The deputy chairs of the Centre Party are the MPs Hannakaisa Heikkinen, Katri Kulmuni and Juha Rehula. Special adviser Riikka Pirkkalainen was chosen as the Centre Party's general secretary.

## Kim Jong-un and Donald Trump have arrived in Singapore

![TV cameramen at the meeting place.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10246705)

Open image viewer

TV cameramen in front of the St Regis hotel in Singapore on 9 June. Kim Jong-un and his entourage are staying at the St Regis. Photo: Wallace Woon / EPA

US President Donald Trump and North Korean leader Kim Jong-un have arrived in Singapore. Kim and Trump will meet on Tuesday in Singapore.

It is a historic meeting. The meeting is intended to discuss whether North Korea will give up its nuclear weapons.

Singapore, in South-East Asia, is the meeting place because the country has good relations with the USA. In addition, Singapore and North Korea have diplomatic relations.

## The army could not find enough good e-athletes

![ESL tournament in Hamburg.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10063108)

Open image viewer

Around the world, e-athletes fill entire stadiums. Photo: AOP

The Finnish army is looking for top e-sports players. In electronic sports, people play video games competitively. The games include, for example, strategy games and shooting games.

In Finland, e-sports is already the second most popular sports hobby among young men. Only ice hockey is more popular. There are about 40 professionals in Finland.

The Finnish Defence Forces Sports School looked for top e-sports players, but none of the applicants was good enough. The army needs e-athletes, because simulator training, for example, is increasing all the time.

E-sports also aims to become a demonstration sport at the 2024 Paris Olympics.

## The sewing LAN parties drew a good crowd

![A sewing event.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10246732)

Open image viewer

Pasi Heiskanen from Espoo managed to make at least a little pouch for an e-book, and the shirt he is wearing in the picture, at the sewing LAN party. Photo: Toni Määttä / Yle

Sewing LAN parties were held in Finland at the Ommel festival.

Usually LAN parties, that is, local network events, are gatherings where people get together to enjoy a hobby with their own computers. At the sewing LAN parties people came together to sew. Everyone brought their own machines, materials and fabrics to the event.

The LANs were in Espoo, and some people came from as far as Lapland. There were 40 people at the LANs. They sewed from Friday to Sunday, from ten in the morning until one at night.

About 2,000 people attended the Ommel festivals in total.

## Roadside churches are open again

![Vähänkyrö church](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10240094)

Open image viewer

Vähänkyrö church in Ostrobothnia is one of this summer's roadside churches. Photo: Tarmo Niemi/Yle

Roadside churches opened their doors on Sunday.

The roadside churches of the Evangelical Lutheran Church are near the main roads and are open until the start of school. Roadside churches can be stopped at for a break anywhere in the country. The northernmost roadside church in Finland is the Utsjoki church in Upper Lapland.

A total of 266 roadside churches are open this summer.
