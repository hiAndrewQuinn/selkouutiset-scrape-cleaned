# Thursday 20.2.2020 (radio)

People have been killed in a shooting in Germany. EU leaders are negotiating about EU funds. President Sauli Niinistö's health is good. The population of Sweden is growing. The inventor of the copy and paste command has died.

## Shooting incident in Germany

![Police at the scene of a mass shooting in Hanau, Germany.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11219211)

Open image viewer

The shooter attacked 2 hookah cafés in Hanau. Photo: Thomas Lohnes / AFP

People have been killed in a shooting in Germany. The shooter killed 9 people in Hanau, near the big city of Frankfurt.

The dead were immigrants. The shooting took place in 2 hookah cafés on Wednesday.

The shooter was a 43-year-old German man. After the act, the man shot himself. The police found the body in the shooter's home.

The police also found another dead person in the shooter's home. She was the shooter's mother. The shooter probably killed her too.

The shooting is being investigated as terrorism. German newspapers report that the shooter left a video message. The message was full of hatred towards immigrants.

## Meeting of EU leaders

![Sanna Marin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11219842)

Open image viewer

Finland's Prime Minister Sanna Marin is taking part in the meeting in Brussels. Photo: Ian Langsdon / EPA

The leaders of EU countries have gathered in Brussels. They are negotiating about the money of the European Union.

The EU's money is decreasing, because Britain no longer pays membership fees to the EU. Britain left the EU at the end of January.

EU countries disagree about what should be done in the new situation.

The rich EU countries want the EU to start saving. They do not want to pay bigger membership fees to the EU.

The poor EU countries do not support saving. They are afraid that their EU support will get smaller.

The negotiations between EU countries will be difficult.

## The president's health is good

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11219275)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö's health is good. A new medical statement says that Niinistö has no illnesses that get in the way of carrying out the president's duties.

In Finland, information about the president's health has been published for almost 40 years. The custom was started by President Mauno Koivisto.

Before Koivisto, Urho Kekkonen was Finland's president. At the end, President Kekkonen was so ill that he had to resign from the presidency.

## The population of Sweden is growing

![Street view of Stockholm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11202905)

Open image viewer

The population of Sweden and its capital, Stockholm, has grown. Photo: Frida Lönnroos

The population of Sweden, Finland's neighbouring country, is growing. There are now a little over 10 million Swedes.

In the last 10 years, the population of Sweden has grown by about a million.

The population of Sweden has grown especially because of immigration.

## Larry Tesler has died

![Larry Tesler](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11218692)

Open image viewer

Larry Tesler invented the copy/paste command. Photo: Wikimedia / CC BY 2.0

The computer science researcher Larry Tesler has died. The American Tesler was 74 years old.

Tesler had a long career in information technology. He worked for Apple, for example.

Tesler wanted to make using computers easier. For example, he invented the copy and paste command.
