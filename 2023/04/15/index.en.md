# Saturday 15.4.2023 (radio)

Olkiluoto 3 starts regular electricity production. MPs' election campaigns cost almost 33,000 euros on average. The retirement age rises in France. Sega plans to buy Rovio. The weather is dry.

## Olkiluoto reactor 3

![Olkiluoto nuclear power plant area in winter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4974,h_2797,x_0,y_879/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10848306410364dee9df/1678811512)

The Olkiluoto nuclear power plant's reactor 3 will start producing electricity at full power next night.

Teollisuuden Voima says that Olkiluoto's third reactor will start producing electricity at full power on Sunday night at 2 a.m.

The completion of Olkiluoto nuclear power plant's reactor 3 has been delayed a lot. The reactor was supposed to produce electricity at full power as early as 2009.

## The cost of election campaigns

![People queue on the Joensuu pedestrian street for the election tents of different parties, where food and coffee are offered.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_756/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1094696642ac5ac54440/1680525140)

Open image viewer

Photo: Marja-Liisa Kämppi / Yle

MPs spent on average about 32,600 euros on their election campaigns. Election campaign costs varied from a few thousand euros to almost 200,000 euros.

The information comes from Yle's election compass. Of the 200 MPs elected to Parliament, 119 answered the election compass question on the election budget, so the amounts of the campaigns will be specified later.

Annu Perälä, a researcher at the University of Vaasa, says that MPs spent a lot of money on campaigning. Perälä says that many people cannot put that much money into an election campaign. This is a problem for democracy.

## The retirement reform in France

![Riot police behind bins set on fire by protesters.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4565,h_2567,x_0,y_35/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1099643643a2d821dd1b/1681534470)

Open image viewer

Photo: Firas Abdullah / AOP

French President Emmanuel Macron has signed the law on the pension reform. The law says that people may retire in France at the age of 64. Previously the retirement age in France was 62.

The pension reform has been disputed a lot in France. On Friday there were riots in different parts of France, when people went onto the streets to oppose the pension reform. In Paris protesters set bicycles and rubbish on fire. The police arrested over 100 protesters.

French trade unions intend to organise big demonstrations on May Day.

## Rovio

![A drawn picture of a red, ball-shaped bird. The bird looks angry.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1110,h_624,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10806486401e5cfc0222/1698672474)

Open image viewer

Photo: IGDB/Rovio Entertainment

Japanese video game company Sega intends to buy the Finnish game company Rovio. This is reported by the newspaper Wall Street Journal. The paper says the purchase price is about 900 million euros.

Rovio is known for the Angry Birds game. In February Rovio said that it is negotiating purchase offers with many companies.

In March Rovio rejected the offer of the Israeli game company Playtika.
