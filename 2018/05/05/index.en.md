# Saturday 5.5.2018 (radio)

A pellet factory burned in Luumäki. 2 men died in a motorcycle accident in Kotka. It has been 200 years since Karl Marx was born. The police have shot a bear in Orivesi. Spring has come for Helsinki's city cows.

## Fire in Luumäki

![The pellet factory fire is being put out.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10192225)

Open image viewer

The fire destroyed the factory's sawdust store. Photo: South Karelia Rescue Department

There has been a big fire in Luumäki. The fire was at a pellet factory.

Nobody was hurt in the fire, but the fire caused big damage. The damage may be worth more than a million euros.

The pellet factory makes pellets from wood dust, that is, wood powder. Pellets are burned for heating.

In Luumäki the fire destroyed the entire sawdust store. In addition, the water used to put out the fire caused damage to the factory.

The owner of the factory estimates that making pellets will stop for 1 or 2 months.

Luumäki is between Kouvola and Lappeenranta.

## Marx 200 years

![On the wall, framed by large red flags, is a picture of Marx with the words 1818 - 2018 underneath. In rows of desks below the picture stand a group of party workers, mostly in black suits.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10191877)

Open image viewer

The Chinese Communist Party has celebrated the 200th birthday of Marx.

It has been exactly 200 years since the birth of Karl Marx, the German philosopher. Karl Marx was born on 5 May 1818.

Marx is the founder of communism. He wrote many important books about economics and politics. Marx's ideas were the basis for communist states, for example the Soviet Union.

There are no longer communist states in Europe, but in China, for example, power lies with the communist party. That is why China has celebrated Marx's 200th birthday. Chinese President Xi Jinping said that Marx is the greatest thinker in history.

To mark the celebration, China has donated a statue of Marx to the German city of Trier. Trier is Marx's birthplace, and on Saturday the 200th anniversary celebration of Marx was held there.

## Bear in Orivesi

![The bear was captured on the wildlife camera of Janne Pajula, a beekeeper from Orivesi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10192394)

Open image viewer

In Orivesi a bear moved very close to the houses. Photo: Janne Pajula

The police have shot a bear in Orivesi, near Tampere. The bear moved close to the houses after it hit a bus on Wednesday.

The police tried to scare the bear away from the houses, but it did not work. That is why the police had to shoot the bear.

The bear was perhaps less than 5 years old. It was a male, that is, a male bear.

## Cows to pasture

![The cows were let out to pasture at the Viikki teaching and research farm in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10192317)

Open image viewer

Spring came and the cows got out. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Spring has started for Helsinki's city cows on Saturday. The cows got out for the first time since winter at the University of Helsinki's research farm in Viikki on Saturday.

A big crowd of Helsinki people was watching as the cows ran and jumped for joy. The beautiful weather added to the joy of the cows and the watchers.
