## Parliamentary election

Advance voting for the parliamentary election continues until Tuesday 28.3. The actual election day is Sunday 2.4. Photo: Rinna Härkönen / Yle

The economy and climate change are important issues for Finns in the parliamentary election. This is what political researcher **Hanna Was** from the University of Helsinki says.

Many voters may choose a candidate who is worried about Finland's economy or climate change. For many voters, the candidate's ideas on Finland's security policy are also important.

Political researcher Hanna Was estimates that these themes, that is, the economy, climate change and security policy, affect Finns' voting decisions a lot.

The actual election day for the parliamentary election is Sunday 2 April.

## Attack on an MP

![A man attacks Ben Zyskowicz at Itäkeskus in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3024,h_1701,x_0,y_829/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1090477641eeb5eb5fda/1679750745)

Open image viewer

MP Zyskowicz was attacked while he was waiting for the metro. Photo: Reader's photo

MP **Ben Zyskowicz** was attacked in Helsinki on Saturday. Zyskowicz was waiting for the metro at Itäkeskus in Helsinki.

A man came up to Zyskowicz, insulted Zyskowicz's Jewish faith and threatened Zyskowicz's life. The man hit Zyskowicz in the face and knocked Zyskowicz to the ground. Guards caught the man.

National Coalition Party MP Ben Zyskowicz is a well-known MP. He has been an MP for a long time. It is very rare for an MP to be attacked in Finland.

## Ban on sales of combustion-engine cars

![Cars in a car dealership.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10100786328a353a6801/1663608715)

Open image viewer

The EU wants to ban new combustion-engine cars, because they pollute. Photo: Fanega Jorge González / Yle

The EU and Germany have reached agreement on a ban on the sale of combustion-engine cars. The EU bans the sale of combustion-engine cars in 2035. The idea is that new cars run, for example, on electricity or hydrogen.

Germany did not want to ban the sale of combustion-engine cars. Germany is a big car maker. There are many car factories in Germany. That is why the ban on combustion-engine cars is very important for Germany.

The EU promises Germany that new cars may have a combustion engine if the car uses synthetic fuel. Synthetic fuel is made, for example, from water and carbon dioxide.

## Summer time

![Clock out of order.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-5063356f13cf33a581/1534907163)

Open image viewer

The clock time will be moved 1 hour forward. Photo: Yle/Tero Kyllönen

Summer time begins tomorrow, on Sunday. The clock will be moved one hour forward at 3 o'clock on Sunday morning. Then the clock shows 4. The hour between 3 and 4 o'clock is skipped. That is why night trains and night buses are an hour late on Sunday morning.

Return to normal time takes place on the last Sunday of October.

Summer time begins and ends at the same time across the whole EU.
