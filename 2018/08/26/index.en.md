## Relatives report suicide plans to the police

![Police suicide-related callouts 2008-2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10371114)

Suicidal risk here means that a person is planning suicide. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

More and more people are asking for police help when they fear a close relative will take their own life.

People no longer feel ashamed of a relative's suicide plans, and they dare to ask the police for help. Often the person planning suicide disappears and does not, for example, go to work. Then the family or friends often report it to the police.

The police receive almost 100 percent more reports like this than 10 years ago. Suicides have decreased by about 20 percent in 10 years.

## Car emissions will be measured in a new way

![Cars on the road](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10370788)

Open image viewer

Car emissions will soon be measured in a new way. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

From the beginning of September, the carbon dioxide emissions of new cars will be reported with two different figures.

The new way of measuring emissions is even more accurate. The new tests take into account the car's weight and air resistance.

Car tax will also change. The prices and tax of the most polluting cars will rise.

## The Kuopio accident bus's brakes were in working order

![A coach crashed through the railing of a bridge onto the railway in Kuopio on Friday, 24 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10370443)

Open image viewer

The Kuopio accident bus. Photo: Timo Hartikainen / Lehtikuva

The condition of those injured in the Kuopio bus accident is stable, the hospital reports.

Three patients are in intensive care, and nine injured people are still in hospital in total. Still on Saturday morning, five patients were in intensive care. After the bus disaster, 19 people were taken to hospital. Four passengers died in the accident.

The investigators of the accident said on Saturday that the bus's brakes were in working order. The investigation continues.

## Sex educators for schools

![Two hands with the same picture tattooed on them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10368985)

Open image viewer

Knowledge increases sexual well-being, Sexpo believes. Photo: Takmeomeo / Pixabay

The Sexpo foundation wants a sex educator in every school.

Sexpo's experts say that the quality of sex education varies a lot in schools. Often the quality depends on the skills and attitudes of one teacher. Sexpo says that if young people do not get correct information about sexuality from adults, they get it from friends or the media.

The aim of the Sexpo foundation is to promote the sexual well-being of Finns.

## August is ending soon

![A lakeshore lit with lamps on Venetian Night.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10363517)

Open image viewer

In August the evenings are already dark but warm. Photo: Kokkola Tourism Oy

On the last weekend of August, end-of-season celebrations for boats and summer cottages have been held by the seashores.

In many places, the summer-ending celebration is called "Venetian Nights". Some Finns celebrate a festival called "Night of the Ancient Fires" on the last Saturday of August.

On Saturday evening, jetties and beaches were decorated with candles, lanterns and torches. Bonfires and fireworks were also watched in the dark and warm evening.
