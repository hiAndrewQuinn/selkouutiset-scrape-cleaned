# Thursday 1.12.2016 (radio)

The Guggenheim museum is not coming to Finland. Iraqi brothers are charged with mass murder. A Finns Party politician was convicted for a hate post. Small children watch a lot of television. At the Slush event, investors are looking for interesting firms.

## The Guggenheim museum is not coming to Finland

![An illustration of the building that won the Guggenheim Helsinki architecture competition.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9269693)

Open image viewer

A model picture of the Guggenheim museum Photo: Guggenheim Helsinki

The Guggenheim art museum will not be built in Helsinki. Helsinki City Council rejected the project during the night. The Guggenheim museum was meant to be built in Helsinki's South Harbour.

Building the Guggenheim museum has caused a lot of discussion, because it would have cost a lot of money. The museum was considered too expensive for Helsinki. The building would have cost at least 130 million euros.

An American investor founded the Guggenheim foundation almost 80 years ago, and there are currently museums in New York, Venice and Bilbao.

## Iraqi brothers go to court for mass murder

![The custody hearing of a terrorism suspect in Tampere](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8521822)

Open image viewer

The brothers are suspected of being ISIS fighters. Photo: Leena Peltokangas / Yle

Iraqi brothers are being charged with mass murders that happened in Iraq. Finland's top prosecutor, the Office of the Prosecutor General, has decided that the 23-year-old men will go to court. In the prosecutor's opinion, they took part in a mass murder that happened in Iraq in 2014.

Fighters of the terrorist organisation ISIS killed over 1000 people. The prosecutor says that the brothers were in the ISIS forces.

The men were arrested in Forssa a year ago.

## Politician convicted for a hate post

![Terhi Kiemunki at the Pirkanmaa District Court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9306700)

Open image viewer

Terhi Kiemunki Photo: Terri Niemi / Yle

Terhi Kiemunki, a politician of the Finns Party, has been convicted for her online writings. Kiemunki got a 450-euro fine for writing a hate post on her blog. She said that all of Europe's terrorists are Muslims. In the court's opinion, the post insulted followers of Islam.

Kiemunki is the assistant in Parliament of Finns Party MP Lea Mäkipää.

## Small children use media devices a lot

![Five-year-old Kaisla looks at an iPad; the family cat is also in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9323777)

Open image viewer

Children watch children's programmes on tablet computers. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

Small children watch a lot of television. A study says that 3-6-year-old children stare at the screen of a television, mobile phone or computer for almost 2 hours a day. 40 percent of the children use devices even more, that is, over 2 hours a day.

Children watch television and smartphones or tablet computers the most. The researchers say that using devices affects children's sleep. The amount of sleep may stay too small.

## The Slush event brings a lot of people to Helsinki
