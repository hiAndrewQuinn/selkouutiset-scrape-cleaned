# Tuesday 24.7.2018 (radio)

Heat is troubling the elderly. It is not a good idea to waste tap water now. There are fires in Greece. Japan has a record heat. A dam burst in Laos and water covered villages.

## Heat is troubling the elderly

![Nurse and patient in hospital](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10307076)

Open image viewer

For a sick elderly person, the heat is hard. Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

Hot weather, that is heat, has brought more patients to hospitals. The heat has lasted so long that many elderly people are unwell. Some people have already died because of the heat.

Chief physician Raimo O. Salonen of the National Institute for Health and Welfare says that the heat is increasing the number of deaths among the elderly. The heat is dangerous especially for elderly people who have, for example, a heart disease.

Chief physician Salonen says that elderly people must drink enough in the heat. The problem is that elderly people do not always feel thirsty.

Eating is also important, Salonen says. He recommends fruit and something salty to eat for the elderly.

## Don't waste water

![A man washes a car with a water hose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7246655)

Open image viewer

Washing a car with tap water is not a good idea now. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

Because of the hot and dry weather, Finns are using more water than usual.

Some water utilities are already asking people to use water carefully. It is not good to waste tap water on watering the yard or washing a car.

In detached houses, residents should only switch on the washing machine or dishwasher after 11 pm. Water use is more even when not everyone uses water at the same time.

## There are fires in Greece

![Burnt cars in Greece](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10320923)

Open image viewer

The fire also burned cars in Greece. Photo: Yannis Kolesidis / EPA-EFE

In hot Greece there are big fires. At least 60 people have died in the fires.

The fire spread quickly because Greece is dry. The hot weather and strong wind gave the fires more force.

There have been fires near the Greek capital, Athens. The fire has burned forest, houses and cars.

## Japan has heat

![The weather forecast promises a few degrees of relief from Japan's heat for Tuesday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10318864)

Open image viewer

A weather map of Japan shows the heat. Photo: Toni Hellinen / Yle

It is also hot in Japan. The hot weather has lasted about 2 weeks. At least 80 Japanese people have died because of the heat.

On Monday, Japan's new heat record of 41.1 degrees was measured.

## A dam burst in Laos

![Men stand on the roof of a house covered by flood water in Laos.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10319910)

Open image viewer

Water covered houses when the dam burst. Photo: EPA

In Laos in Asia there has been a major accident. A dam burst and a huge amount of water was released. The amount of water was 5000 billion litres.

At least 6 villages have been covered by water. They are in the Attapeu area of Laos. Hundreds of people are missing, and several thousand people have been left without a home.

There is not yet exact information about how many people died in the accident.
