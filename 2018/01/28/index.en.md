# Sunday 28.1.2018 (radio)

Sauli Niinistö has won the presidential election. Niinistö got more than 60 percent of the votes. The other candidates got far fewer votes. Niinistö will continue as president for another 6 years.

## Niinistö won the presidential election

Sauli Niinistö has won the presidential election.

Finns voted in the presidential election on Sunday. Almost 63 percent of voters voted for Niinistö.

Because Niinistö got more than 50 percent of the votes, a second round is not needed in the election. It is already clear now that Sauli Niinistö will continue as President of Finland for another 6 years.

## The other candidates got few votes

![The Greens' presidential candidate Pekka Haavisto and the voters' association's presidential candidate Sauli Niinistö shake hands at the presidential election night party at the House of the Estates in Helsinki on Sunday 28 January 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10045699)

Pekka Haavisto of the Greens got the second most votes. He congratulated Niinistö on his win.

There were a total of 8 candidates in the presidential election. 6 parties and 2 voters' associations had their own candidate.

Sauli Niinistö was the candidate of a voters' association. So he got more than 60 percent of the votes. The other candidates got far fewer votes.

The Green Party's candidate Pekka Haavisto got the second most votes. Haavisto got about 12 percent of the votes.

The support of the other candidates was clearly below 10 percent.

Laura Huhtasaari of the Finns Party got almost 7 percent of the votes. About 6 percent of the votes went to Paavo Väyrynen, who was the candidate of a voters' association.

The Centre Party's candidate Matti Vanhanen got about 4 percent of the votes.

The Social Democrats' candidate Tuula Haatainen and the Left Alliance's candidate Merja Kyllönen both got about 3 percent of the votes.

The Swedish People's Party's candidate Nils Torvalds got 1.5 percent of the votes.

## Niinistö has been in politics for a long time

![Sauli Niinistö and Jenni Haukio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9965882)

Open image viewer

Sauli Niinistö and Jenni Haukio. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Sauli Niinistö will soon start his second term as president. The term lasts 6 years.

Sauli Niinistö has been President of Finland since 2012. Before that he was an MP, a minister and the Speaker of Parliament.

Niinistö's party was the National Coalition Party. As president he is no longer a member of the party.

Sauli Niinistö turns 70 this year. He is married to Jenni Haukio. The presidential couple will soon have a baby.

Niinistö has 2 adult sons from his first marriage. Niinistö's first wife died in a car accident.

## Niinistö is very popular

![Presidents Niinistö and Trump at the White House.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10030451)

Experts have assessed Niinistö's popularity. The experts think that Finns like the fact that as president Niinistö has met many of the world's important leaders.

Finns also do not like to change president if the old president is a candidate in the election. So it was difficult for the other candidates to try to beat Niinistö.
