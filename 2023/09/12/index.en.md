## Wind power

At work sites the slowing of wind power is not yet visible. In 2024–25 new power plants will be installed in Finland in line with earlier decisions. Photo: Markku Sandell / Yle

No new wind power investment decisions have been made in Finland this year.

The reason is the rise in prices and interest rates. Projects no longer automatically pay off.

The Finnish Wind Power Association says that wind electricity has filled a shortage in the self-sufficiency of electricity production.

However, in the coming years new power plants with 3 300 megawatts will be built in Finland, for which decisions have already been made.

## Racism at universities

![The Otaniemi campus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113799664a405388b66b/1688471075)

Open image viewer

In a Yle survey, rectors identified hidden racism and structural racism at universities. Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

There is more racism at higher education institutions than before, says the Finnish Student Union federation. In recent times it has received more contacts related to racism.

SYL says that both Finnish and international students face racism in different parts of the country.

In the Yle survey, universities say that anti-racism has been promoted, but the work must continue.

Vaasa University rector Minna Martikainen says that the summer's debate about racism was necessary. However, Martikainen says that the continuation of the debate affects Finland's country risk.

The country risk affects, for example, how much people want to invest in Finland.

## Vocational training

![Hands cut a cable with pliers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3381,h_1901,x_0,y_176/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114051464ac0aaf1afac/1697274543)

Open image viewer

In Finland, vocational training does not guarantee jobs as well as in other OECD countries. Photo: Juha Kokkala / Yle

Vocational training is popular in Finland. However, from vocational schools it is harder to get work in Finland than in other industrialised countries.

The OECD, the organisation for economic cooperation and development, has published a report on education. It also shows that Finland's basic school has fewer compulsory lessons than the OECD countries on average.

Teachers' salaries in Finland are at a good level internationally. In early childhood education, that is, in day-care centres, salaries are worse.

## Swine fever

![Warning text about swine fever.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1004,h_565,x_0,y_118/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4600555a6d958c44dde/1694517625)

Open image viewer

Warning sign about swine fever. Photo: Evira

In Finland the Food Agency advises avoiding hunting trips to Central Sweden.

In Central Sweden 13 dead wild boars have been found, in which African swine fever was detected. Swine fever is a haemorrhagic fever that spreads among pig animals. The disease spreads easily, and there is no cure for it.

In Central Sweden an area of 100 000 hectares has been isolated, where no one may move. In addition, all pigs in the area will be killed.

Swine fever has not been detected in Finland. Swine fever is now also in Sweden for the first time.

African swine fever does not, however, spread to people.
