## The situation in Afghanistan

![A man grieves for people injured in the chaos outside Kabul's international airport on Tuesday 17 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_328/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8448476121fdcc43d7f/1629617749)

At Kabul airport in Afghanistan the situation is difficult. 7 Afghans have died in the chaos at the airport. People were crushed to death in the crowd.

At the airport gates there is a big crowd, because thousands of people are trying to get into the airport.

It is difficult for Finland, the USA and other countries to help their own citizens get out of Kabul, because people cannot reach the airport.

## The corona situation

![A restaurant terrace full of people in the centre of Mikkeli.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2108,h_1186,x_891,y_811/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81804060c0697983876/1626350722)

On Sunday 503 new corona infections have been reported in Finland.

Both of the 2 corona vaccinations have now been received by 46 percent of Finns. More than 70 percent of Finns have received one vaccination.

The employers' organisations, that is, the labour market organisations, encourage Finns to take a corona vaccine. The labour market organisations say that it is good for people to get the vaccine. Then corona restrictions can be reduced, and people have work, because firms and services are working.

## Shortage of doctors

![Chair of the Finnish Medical Association Tuula Rajaniemi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11714012)

Open image viewer

Chair of the Finnish Medical Association Tuula Rajaniemi. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

Doctors say that there are too few doctors in Finland. Doctors say that public health care in Finland is about 1,000 doctors short.

The chair of the Finnish Medical Association, **Tuula Rajaniemi**, says that doctors often have too much work. That is why many doctors do not want to work, for example, in health centres.

The Finnish Medical Association says that health centres and hospitals must get more doctors. The Finnish Medical Association says that more other staff are also needed to help the doctors.

## Sweden's prime minister is changing

![Swedish Prime Minister Stefan Löfven.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3979,h_2238,x_0,y_255/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-844864612224e60164d/1629629446)

Open image viewer

Swedish Prime Minister Stefan Löfven. Photo: Henrik Montgomery / TT

Sweden's prime minister changes in the autumn. Prime Minister \*\*Stefan Löfven \*\*said on Sunday that he is resigning, that is, ending his job as prime minister. Löfven will also resign from the post of chair of the Swedish Social Democratic Party.

Stefan Löfven has been Sweden's prime minister for 7 years. He will end his jobs as prime minister and party chair in November.

It is not yet known who will become Sweden's new prime minister and the party's new chair.
