# Friday 17.5.2019 (radio)

Government negotiations continue. The University of Helsinki is studying how well Russian speakers over 50 are doing in Finland. The European Parliament elections are in a week. The midnight sun has started in Utsjoki.

## Government negotiations

![Antti Rinne, SDP party leader and leader of the government negotiations, gave his comments to the media as the government negotiations continued at Säätytalo in Helsinki on 17 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10789230)

Antti Rinne is leading the government negotiations. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The parties have continued the government negotiations. SDP party leader Antti Rinne is leading the negotiations.

Rinne says that the negotiations are going well. In Rinne's view, the parties are already close to each other on economic matters too.

Centre party leader Juha Sipilä disagrees. Sipilä says that the parties' ideas about the economy are very different.

A total of 5 parties are taking part in the government negotiations. They are trying to form Finland's next government.

## Study of Russian speakers

![An older woman drinks coffee.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10776484)

Open image viewer

There are also Russian-speaking pensioners in Finland. Photo: E. M. Welch / AOP

The University of Helsinki is starting a study of Russian speakers in Finland. The university is studying how well Russian speakers over 50 are doing in Finland.

This is the first study of immigrants who are retired or will soon retire.

The university is sending questions to 3,000 Russian speakers in Finland. The researchers want to know what the respondents think, for example, about their own health and their financial situation.

Russian speakers are Finland's largest foreign-language group. Researchers think it is important to know how they are doing.

## European Parliament

![European Parliament](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10662785)

Open image viewer

The European Parliament has over 750 members. Photo: Patrick Seeger / EPA

The European elections will be held in a week, on Sunday.

In the European elections, citizens of the EU countries elect members to the European Parliament. The elections are held at the same time in all EU countries. The European Union has 28 member countries.

The European Parliament makes laws that apply to all EU member countries. Parliament also decides, for example, on the EU budget.

The European Parliament has over 750 members. There are 13 Finnish members in Parliament.

After the elections, members of Parliament join political groups. Each group has members from many different countries. However, they think about issues in the same way.

The European Parliament works in Brussels.

The European Parliament has 24 official languages. The debates of the Parliament are interpreted into the different languages.

## Midnight sun

![Midnight sun in Sápmi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10325076)

Open image viewer

The northern summer night is light. Photo: Maiju Saijets / Yle

The midnight sun has started in Utsjoki. This means that the sun is now in the sky over Utsjoki all the time, including at night.

Utsjoki is Finland's northernmost municipality. The sun will next set in Utsjoki on 28 July.
