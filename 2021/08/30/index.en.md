# Monday 30.8.2021 (radio)

Corona testing takes staff from health care. Finnair is calling people back to work. Many companies reward employees. Hurricane Ida caused a lot of damage in the USA. Weather forecast.

## Health centres

![Stethoscope on the doctors' desk](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5547,h_3120,x_0,y_286/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7722226023a4264c8da/1612949861)

Corona testing and corona contact tracing ties up many people's work. Photo: Marjo Pirilä / Yle

Corona testing and corona contact tracing put a big load on health care in Finland.

In many municipalities it is not possible to get an appointment at health centres, because the health centres are overcrowded. The reason is that staff have been moved to corona testing and corona contact tracing.

Chief physician **Esa Rintala** tells Yle that the crowding may get worse in the autumn, when the flu season starts. Rintala says that corona testing and corona quarantines may need to be reduced.

## Finnair's pilots

![Commercial pilot Olli Pöllänen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4256,h_2394,x_0,y_219/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8454906124c57573783/1629802570)

Open image viewer

Pilots practising in a simulator. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The airline Finnair is calling employees back to work. Many pilots and flight attendants have been laid off, that is, without work, because of the corona situation.

Air travel has started to increase again. That is why Finnair is calling pilots and flight attendants back to training, so that they can return to work.

Finnair has about 900 pilots. About half of the pilots have already returned to work.

## Rewarding employees in companies

![Hundred-euro notes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11056136)

Open image viewer

A reward can be money, for example. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Rewarding employees is increasing in companies. In many companies, employees have started to be rewarded with one-off bonuses. This means that the employee is given, for example, money.

Companies say that a one-off bonus is a quick way to thank a good employee. One-off bonuses are given in fields where there is tough competition for employees.

Companies may also offer other rewards. For example, they may offer food, a cinema ticket or a babysitter.

## Hurricane Ida in the USA

![Hurricane Ida rages in the United States.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3979,h_2238,x_0,y_37/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-847591612c46259b77e/1630291606)

Open image viewer

Hurricane Ida has caused a lot of damage. Photo: Patrick T. Fallon / AFP

A strong storm has caused a lot of damage on the south coast of the USA. The name of the storm is Ida. Hurricane Ida has cut the power for about a million Americans. For example, the city of New Orleans went completely dark, because the power was cut.

The storm has caused big floods. Many people's homes have been destroyed because of the storm.

The situation on the south coast of the USA is difficult for millions of people. Authorities are investigating the damage caused by Hurricane Ida.
