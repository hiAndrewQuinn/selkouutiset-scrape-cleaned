# Tuesday 4.5.2021 (radio)

Yle asked MPs for their opinion on dual municipal residence. The Bank of Finland is worried about large mortgages. There is congestion in passport applications. Checking the tax proposal. Garden saunas are popular.

## Dual municipal residence gains support

![A cottage for sale in Korppoo, Parainen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1280,h_720,x_0,y_120/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7052445f2a6d0db6b2d/1596616015)

A cottage is a popular place to work remotely. Photo: Miia Salo

Many people live in two places. During the corona time many people do remote work at a cottage.

Yle asked MPs for their opinion on dual municipal residence. In it, a person can live and pay tax, for example, in two municipalities.

74 percent of respondents supported dual municipal residence and 27 percent opposed it. A quarter did not know what to say.

Dual municipal residence is not yet possible in Finland. Minister of Local Government Sirpa Paatero says that the Finnish Constitution affects the matter. For example, a person can stand as a candidate and vote in only one municipality in elections.

## Large mortgages worry the Bank of Finland

![A light-coloured one-room flat with steel microwave and fridge doors.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_487/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78674660587ee51e6f0/1616413198)

Open image viewer

The Bank of Finland demands restrictions because many people have taken out a mortgage that is too large and too long compared with their income. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

The Bank of Finland is worried about large mortgages. The central bank says that Finns are borrowing more than before.

The bank warns especially about long mortgages. They are being taken out more than ever.

In addition, the average size of loans has grown.

More and more loans have been granted to people who have a lot of debt compared with their income.

The central bank demands a debt ceiling for households, so that too much money cannot be lent. The bank also demands a maximum length for mortgages.

## Congestion in passport applications

![Finnish passport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4961,h_2791,x_0,y_23/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6630395e8dde3b3fb98/1689742474)

Open image viewer

Photo: All Over Press / Ismo Pekkarinen

There is now congestion in passport applications.

The congestion is especially bad at the main police stations in the capital region. For example, the Helsinki police booking had a queue of 4 weeks last week.

Times are available more quickly at other police stations.

In Turku, Tampere and Oulu too, booking a passport appointment takes many weeks.

For example, in Kuopio, Lahti and Vaasa, you can get a time within 1 to 2 weeks. In Rovaniemi you can already book a passport this week.

The police say that passport queues may get longer towards summer. Many people are now dreaming of a trip, because during the corona year it has not been possible to go on a trip.

## Check the tax proposal

![Logging in to the OmaVero page on the Tax Administration website.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11466953)

Now it is worth checking whether there is still time to include, for example, the home office deduction in taxation. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

For some taxpayers, this Tuesday is the last day to change their tax return, that is, their tax proposal.

For the rest of the taxpayers, the deadlines are Tuesday 11 May and Tuesday 18 May.

Your own deadline is shown in the tax proposal or in the OmaVero service online.

## Garden saunas are popular

![A couple sits in front of a garden sauna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1453,h_817,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-801467608fcfce76116/1620037647)

Open image viewer

The dream of a garden sauna was born in 2019, when Vili-Pekka and Susanna Yli-Panula moved into their new home. Photo: Birgitta Vuorela / Yle

More and more homes have a separate sauna in the garden. The second corona spring has speeded up building in gardens.

Cottage saunas are also being renovated a lot now. Sales of sauna buildings have risen by as much as 30 percent.

However, the price of wood is rising, and it threatens to raise the cost of building.
