# Tuesday 7.2.2023 (radio)

The death toll from the earthquake in Turkey and Syria is rising. Finland is sending aid to the earthquake area. The foreign ministers of Finland and Sweden discussed Nato membership. Today was the first national preparedness day. The weather.

## The death toll from the earthquake rises

![People carry an injured person on a stretcher.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1280,h_720,x_0,y_66/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106889763e0901358011/1675682340)

Survivors are being searched for in the rubble of houses in the earthquake area in Turkey and Syria. Photo: Adsiz Gunebakan / AOP

The search for the victims of the earthquake continues in Turkey and Syria.

At least 5000 dead have already been found after yesterday's quakes. More than 20,000 people have been injured.

The death toll may still rise, because the earthquake destroyed thousands of buildings and there are still people in the rubble.

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan has declared a state of emergency in 10 provinces that suffered from the quake. The president has also declared a 7-day period of mourning in Turkey.

The president also said that 70 countries have offered help to Turkey.

## Finland sends aid to the earthquake area

![Collapsed buildings in Kahramanmaraş in Turkey, near the centre of the quake, on 7 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3658,h_2057,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106971363e24be23622b/1675775180)

Collapsed buildings in Kahramanmaraş in Turkey after the earthquake. Photo: Adem Altan / Lehtikuva

Finland is sending humanitarian aid to Turkey and Syria.

The Government says that aid worth one million euros will be sent to Turkey and Syria. The aid is sent through the Red Cross and the Red Crescent.

The money will be used to buy, for example, food and shelter, medical aid and support for people who lost their homes in the earthquake.

In addition, Finnish experts have left for Turkey today to help with rescue work.

## Nato ratification of Finland and Sweden

![Billström and Haavisto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_260/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106950463e21dde1acda/1675764952)

Swedish Foreign Minister Tobias Billström and Finnish Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Finland and Sweden will continue together on the way to becoming members of the military alliance Nato.

Foreign Ministers Pekka Haavisto and Tobias Billström met today in Espoo. They discussed the Nato process of Finland and Sweden.

The foreign ministers said that Finland and Sweden have taken Turkey's security concerns into account. The ratification, that is, the confirmation, of both countries' membership applications is now in Turkey's hands.

Finnish Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto hoped that Finland and Sweden will be Nato members by the July summit in Vilnius.

## National Preparedness Day

![Headlamp, matches, radio, water canister and food.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_534/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106941463e20bcabc60b/1675758766)

It is good to have water, food and medicines at home for three days. Photo: Lehtikuva

Today the first national Preparedness Day was celebrated.

The date of Preparedness Day, 7 February, that is 7.2, reminds us that every household should be prepared for at least 72 hours.

Preparedness means that it is good to have food, water and medicines at home, in case, for example, water or electricity is cut off and you cannot get to the shop or the pharmacy. It is also good to have, for example, a torch, first aid supplies, cash and a radio that works on batteries.
