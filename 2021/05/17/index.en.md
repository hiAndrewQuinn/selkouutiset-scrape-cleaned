# Monday 17.5.2021 (radio)

A new kind of nurse training starts in Tampere and the capital region. Finland's corona figures. VR is putting long-distance trains back into service. The Army is holding a big military exercise in Lapland. You can see Banksy street art in Mänttä.

## A new kind of nurse training

![practical nurse training](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9902929)

Open image viewer

A practical nursing student is training. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

A new kind of training is starting in Finland for nurses with an immigrant background.

The training focuses especially on learning Finnish or Swedish. The language is taught more than usual from the start of the studies. Teaching can start even from the basics, if there is a need.

The Tampere University of Applied Sciences and Metropolia in the capital region are part of the project. The training starts next January.

Finland has a severe shortage of nurses. This decade about 30,000 new nurses may be needed, as older practical nurses and registered nurses retire.

## Finland's corona figures

There have been 144 new coronavirus infections found in Finland.

Over the course of 2 weeks, about 2,800 infections have been found. That is about 300 fewer than in the previous 2 weeks.

There are two new corona deaths. In total, over 900 people have died in Finland during the pandemic.

Almost 38 percent of the population has received a corona vaccination.

## VR adds train services

![A VR train on the track.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9688684)

Open image viewer

VR believes that the number of train passengers will grow in the summer. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

VR is bringing new long-distance train services into traffic.

The services had been on a break, when corona reduced the number of passengers on the railways. However, VR believes that the number of passengers will grow again in the summer.

From this week, VR is putting 24 long-distance train services back into service. New train services will run, for example, from Helsinki to Vaasa, Turku, Oulu, Tampere and Jyväskylä.

## The Army trains

![Sniper of the Blues at the Rovajärvi exercise on 28.5.2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10226922)

Open image viewer

The Army is training in Rovajärvi in the coming weeks. Photo: Tapani Leisti / Yle

A big military exercise has started in Lapland. The exercise is the Army's biggest military exercise this year.

7,000 people are taking part in the exercise. About 350 soldiers from Sweden are also part of it.

The exercise areas are Rovajärvi, Sodankylä and Kittilä. The exercise may cause congestion in traffic in Northern Finland.

## Banksy street art

![Banksy work Love is in the air.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1500,h_844,x_0,y_16/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-806360609e7a6807b8b/1620998791)

Open image viewer

Banksy work Love is in the Air. Photo: Serlachius Museums

A rare art exhibition has opened in Mänttä. Works by the British street artist Banksy are on display at the Serlachius Museums in Mänttä.

Banksy is the best-known street artist in the world. In his art he criticises, for example, consumer society, the power of money and the art business.

This is the first time that Banksy's works have been on display in Finland.

The exhibition is open until October. Tickets are free, but because of the corona situation they must be booked in advance.
