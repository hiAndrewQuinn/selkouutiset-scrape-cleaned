# Thursday 23.2.2023 (radio)

Port strike. Weapons aid to Ukraine. Niinistö to the USA. Police University of Applied Sciences entrance exams. Drought in France. The weather in Finland.

## Port strike

![Men wearing high-visibility vests.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689319)

Open image viewer

The dock workers are now on strike, that is, they are not working. Photo: Janne Körkkö / Yle

The strike at Finnish ports continues. No solution to the dispute was found on Thursday.

The dock workers are on strike. They load goods onto ships and unload goods from ships.

The dock workers stopped working last week, because they demand more pay. The strike has stopped the freight traffic of the ports.

The dock workers' strike will be negotiated again on Sunday. If freight traffic is cut off for a long time, fruit, for example, may run out of shops.

## Weapons aid to Ukraine

![Tank in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689318)

Open image viewer

Tank. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Finland is giving more weapons aid to Ukraine. Finland is sending, for example, 3 Leopard tanks to Ukraine.

The tanks have a mine plough. They can remove mines from the terrain, which explode easily.

Finland also offers Ukrainians training in using and servicing tanks. The training can be arranged in Poland, for example.

Ukraine has especially hoped for tanks. Many other countries have also decided to send tanks to Ukraine.

Now a year has passed since Russia started the war in Ukraine. Russia attacked Ukraine on 24 February last year.

## Drought in France

![River with no water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689315)

Open image viewer

It has not rained in France for a long time. Photo: Coust Laurent / AOP

The weather in France is dry. The winter has not been this dry in 60 years. It last rained in France more than a month ago.

The French Government intends to restrict the use of water, so that there is enough water for everyone. The Government wants to start saving water even before summer.

In summer there may be severe heat in France. Last year was hotter in France than has ever been measured before.

On Friday the weather in Finland is mostly dry and quite sunny. In the north a little snow may fall.

The temperature on Friday is between -1 and -10 degrees, and the coldest is in the north.

The weekend has beautiful winter weather. The weather suits outdoor activities well at the weekend.
