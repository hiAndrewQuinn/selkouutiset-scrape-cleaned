## Sipilä told about his investments

![Prime Minister Juha Sipilä as a guest on Ykkösaamu on 10 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9345271)

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä was interviewed today on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme. Photo: Yle

Finland's prime minister Juha Sipilä has told in a Yle interview how he has invested money.

Many people have not known what kind of investments the wealthy prime minister has made.

The prime minister has invested money, for example, in a Nordea bank investment basket, which contains, among other things, firms' shares and loans. In an investment basket, the assets usually grow when the bank reinvests the money. However, no tax has to be paid on it if the money is not taken into use right away.

Sipilä says that all the necessary information about the investment basket is on Nordea bank's website. According to Sipilä, he has told about his investments as openly as he can.

## Train accident in Bulgaria

![A map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9345256)

Open image viewer

The accident happened more than 200 kilometres from the capital Sofia. Photo: Yle News graphics

A devastating train accident has happened in Bulgaria.

A freight train came off the rails, and two of its gas tanks hit a power line and exploded.

At least five people died and 25 were injured in the accident. In addition, at least 20 homes were destroyed in the explosion.

The accident happened in the village of Hitrino in north-eastern Bulgaria.

## France is extending the state of emergency

![A police operation in Paris in early December. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9345345)

Open image viewer

In France, the police's powers have been increased after the terror attacks. Photo: Etienne Laurent / EPA

The French government wants to extend the state of emergency.

The government proposes that the state of emergency be extended because of the elections next spring and summer.

If the French parliament and senate approve the proposal, the state of emergency will be extended for the 5th time already.

A state of emergency was declared in France in November 2015, when devastating terror attacks had been carried out in Paris.

During the state of emergency, the police have wider powers than usual, for example to arrest people.

## Nobel celebrations in Oslo and Stockholm

![Colombia's president Juan Manuel Santos received the Nobel Peace Prize at Oslo City Hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9345576)

Open image viewer

Colombia's president was honoured in Oslo. Photo: Haakon Mosvold Larsen / EPA

The Nobel Prizes have been awarded today.

Colombia's president Juan Manuel Santos received the Nobel Peace Prize in Oslo, the capital of Norway.

Santos has negotiated a peace agreement with the fighters of the left-wing FARC movement. The agreement ends Colombia's civil war, which lasted over 50 years.

The other Nobel Prizes, that is, the prizes for physics, chemistry, literature, medicine and economics, were awarded in Stockholm, the capital of Sweden.

The Nobel Prize in economics went to the Finn Bengt Holmström.
