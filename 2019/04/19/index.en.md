# Friday 19.4.2019 (radio)

A defeat in the parliamentary elections cuts party funding. The debt trap of credit cards also troubles people who are in work. A big pig barn fire has happened in Ostrobothnia. Young people and people over 70 have food choices that burden the environment the least.

## Centre's party funding falls sharply

![ Juha Sipilä at the election night event in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10738739)

A bad result in the parliamentary elections cuts party funding. The amount of party funding depends on the number of MPs.

The Centre lost 18 seats in Parliament. That is why the Centre's party funding falls by 2.6 million euros a year. It is not yet known whether the party will have to cut staff. Staff work at the party office and in district associations in different parts of Finland. The Centre, for example, is organising the coming European elections with the old party funding.

Election winners see their party funding rise. The SDP got 6 extra seats, and the rise in party funding is 900,000 euros a year. The Left Alliance got 4 extra seats, and party funding rises by 600,000 euros more. The Greens got 5 extra seats, and party funding rises by 740,000 euros.

The party funding of the Finns Party and the National Coalition also rises a little.

Party funding has been granted in Finland since 1967. Party funding is almost 30 million euros a year.

## Credit card debt trap troubles people in work too

![Woman puts a credit card in a payment terminal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10742626)

Open image viewer

Even people in work can find it difficult to get rid of credit card bills. Photo: AOP

The debt spiral from credit cards also troubles people who work.

New statistics show that women's debt problems have grown more than men's debt problems. Among female-dominated sectors, care sector workers' credit card bills are growing. In male-dominated sectors, for example logistics workers are in a risk group.

The Guarantee Foundation says that especially people on low incomes find it hard to get rid of credit card bills permanently. In addition to credit cards, instalment payments and other consumer loans burden their finances.

Many people have several credit cards. In that case the growth of debt is a big risk.

## 1,200 pigs burned in Vöyri

![Pig barn fire at Kullastået in Vöyri, map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10748527)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

A big pig barn fire has happened in Ostrobothnia. In the municipality of Vöyri, perhaps more than 1,200 pigs died on Thursday evening.

About 250 pigs were saved. The pig barn building was almost 4,000 square metres in size.

The cause of the fire is not known. The fire threatened to spread to other buildings, but spreading was prevented. A residential building and a machine hall are next to the pig barn.

## Young people's and elderly people's food choices are best for the environment

![salad](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10708185)

Open image viewer

Older people eat a lot of vegetable food. The reason may be that they see meat dishes more as festive food. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Food choices of young people and people over 70 burden the environment the least. Aalto University and the University of Eastern Finland have studied food choices of different ages.

For example, people over 70 eat the least red meat. Red meat includes beef, pork, lamb, reindeer and elk meat. The elderly also say that they prefer vegetable food.

The study shows that the food choices of middle-aged men with good incomes burden the environment the most.
