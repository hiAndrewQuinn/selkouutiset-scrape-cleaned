# Monday 31.5.2021 (radio)

The teachers' union OAJ has complained to the National Agency for Education. Denmark may have helped the USA spy in Europe. Outdoors a 2-metre distance is no longer needed. Summer jobs have increased. VR is adding trains to traffic.

## Special education at school

![Student in a Russian language lesson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6379225e37ead820189/1580723275)

The teachers' union OAJ has complained to the Parliamentary Ombudsman about the activities of the National Agency for Education.

The teachers' union OAJ says that the guidelines on pupils' special support are unclear and wrong. That is why teaching is given in different ways in different municipalities. OAJ says that children are in an unequal position.

OAJ has complained to the Ombudsman, because the National Agency for Education has not answered OAJ's questions about how supported learning must be arranged.

## Spying by the USA and Denmark

![Computer keyboard and text on a screen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11613824)

Open image viewer

Denmark may have helped the USA spy on European politicians. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Denmark may have helped the USA spy on European politicians and officials.

Swedish and Danish media have investigated that the US intelligence service may have eavesdropped on citizens of France, Germany, Sweden and Norway, and that Danish military intelligence helped the USA.

The countries that may have been spied on are angry about this and want an explanation.

## Corona restrictions

![Outdoor exercise at the Lappeenranta fortress.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10244334)

Open image viewer

For example, outdoor exercise can be organised again, since a 2-metre distance is no longer required outdoors. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

Changes are coming to the Communicable Diseases Act. The period of validity of corona restrictions will be extended. Now the restrictions are valid until the end of June, but they will be continued until the end of the year.

The restrictions of the Communicable Diseases Act concern restrictions and supervision of restaurants, passenger transport and other services.

The definition of close contact will also be changed. From tomorrow, Tuesday, the 2-metre safety distance must be kept only indoors. Outdoors a 2-metre safety distance is no longer required. This makes it possible to arrange events and exercise outdoors.

![An ice cream seller turns a scoop of ice cream.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6914835eeb83406c007/1643811506)

There are more summer jobs on offer than last summer.

There are now more summer jobs in the private service sector, industry, the transport sector and the public sector. Still, fewer summer jobs are on offer than before the corona time.

Some young people set up their own company, in which they work in the summer. Their own company can be, for example, an ice cream kiosk.

## Train traffic

![Tampere railway station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_108/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6991685f0dae86a24d8/1594732554)

Open image viewer

Train passenger numbers have grown again. Photo: Miikka Varila / Yle

Train company VR expects a busy travel summer on the railways.

That is why VR is adding train services on many routes. From this week VR is adding train services between Helsinki and Kajaani, Tampere and Turku, and Helsinki and Vaasa.

VR is also adding carriages to many trains, so that more passengers fit in them.

VR reduced train services in the winter. Now there are more passengers again, and trains are being added.
