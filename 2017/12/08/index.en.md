# Friday 8.12.2017 (radio)

Britain and the EU agreed on the terms of Brexit. The ground shook in the Oulu area. The police are looking for the Lauttasaari flag thief. Today flags were flown in honour of Finnish music. Greetings from the Finnish school in St. Gallen.

## A Brexit agreement was reached

![British Prime Minister Theresa May and Commission President Jean-Claude Juncker met early in the morning in Brussels.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9967527)

British Prime Minister Theresa May and EU Commission President Jean-Claude Juncker.  Photo: Olivier Hoslet / EPA

Britain and the EU have agreed on the terms of Britain leaving the EU.

In the negotiations they agreed, for example, on the position of EU citizens in Britain. According to the agreement, EU citizens who now live in Britain can continue to live, work and study there after Britain leaves the EU. The same also applies to British citizens who live in EU countries.

Britain's exit from the EU will take effect in March 2019.

## The ground shook in the Oulu area

![A graphic of the Oulu and Lumijoki area](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9967621)

Open image viewer

The centre of the earthquake was in Liminka, near Lumijoki. Photo: Yle News Graphics

There has been an earthquake in the Oulu area.

The earthquake happened on the night between Thursday and Friday, a little after midnight. The earthquake had a magnitude of 3.2

The earthquake shook houses, but did not cause damage.

The earthquake was the strongest in Finland since 2000.

Dozens of earthquakes are detected in Finland every year, but they are usually very small.

## The police are investigating the flag theft

![The Finnish flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8604000)

Open image viewer

You can be fined for damaging the Finnish flag. Photo: Pasi Takkunen/Yle

The police are starting an investigation into the Finnish flags stolen on Independence Day.

A total of 25 flags were stolen in Lauttasaari in Helsinki on Independence Day. The flags were taken from flagpoles in the yards of buildings.

A video in which Finnish flags are burned has now spread online.

The police are finding out who is burning the flags, and whether the flags came from Lauttasaari.

You can be fined for damaging the Finnish flag.

## The day of Sibelius and Finnish music

![ Jean Sibelius](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7793334)

Open image viewer

Jean Sibelius is Finland's national composer. Photo: Otava

Today, Friday, the day of Sibelius and Finnish music has been celebrated.

Jean Sibelius is Finland's national composer. He is the most famous Finnish composer abroad as well.

The 8th of December is Sibelius's birthday. Sibelius died in 1957.

In recent days, one work by Sibelius in particular has been heard a great deal. The Finlandia hymn, which Sibelius composed, has been performed a lot at Finland's centenary celebrations.

## Greetings from Switzerland!

![A boy and four girls sit around a table with cake plates in front of them. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9968853)

Pupils of the Finnish school in St. Gallen send greetings and congratulations to 100-year-old Finland.

A large number of Finnish children and young people live in different parts of the world and study the Finnish language and Finnish culture in Finnish schools.

Now that independent Finland turns 100, the Finnish schools are sending video greetings to Finland.

The newest greetings come from the Finnish school in St. Gallen, Switzerland.
