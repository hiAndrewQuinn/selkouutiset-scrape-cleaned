# Tuesday 28.5.2019 (radio)

Stora Enso is laying off workers. Many foreigners work in construction. Some Finns do no exercise at all. The police will monitor young people's celebrations at the weekend.

## Stora Enso

![Stora Enso's mill in Nuottasaari, Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10733749)

Stora Enso's mill in Oulu. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

The forestry company Stora Enso is cutting jobs. Stora Enso is laying off workers at its Oulu mill. Stora Enso is laying off 365 workers in Oulu.

Stora Enso makes paper at the Oulu mill. Stora Enso is closing one paper machine and starting to make packaging paper on another. That is why jobs will be lost.

Stora Enso is making changes at the Oulu mill because the world price of packaging paper is rising, but the price of other paper is falling.

180 workers will still remain at the Oulu paper mill.

## Construction

![Construction workers on a building site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10804760)

Open image viewer

There are foreign workers in construction. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

Many foreigners work in construction. About 20 per cent of construction workers are foreigners. Most workers come from Estonia.

Companies in the field say that it is hard to find Finnish workers for heavy construction work. Such work includes, for example, rebar fixing and heavy concrete work.

The Building Trade Union says that Finnish workers think too many workers are already coming from abroad.

There are almost 200,000 workers in construction. Most foreign workers are in Uusimaa, and the fewest are in Lapland.

## Exercise hobbies

![People Nordic walking.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7998468)

Open image viewer

Nordic walking is a popular hobby in Finland. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Some Finns do no exercise at all. About 20 per cent of Finnish adults have not done any exercise for at least a year.

About 80 per cent of Finns do exercise. Walking, cycling and swimming are popular forms of exercise.

The information is from a new survey.

The survey also says that many Finns want to start a new form of exercise. Women are interested in yoga, and men are interested in e-sports. E-sports means electronic sports, that is, playing video games.

## Celebration weekend

![Young people sitting on the grass.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8032299)

Open image viewer

Young people celebrate outdoors when school ends. Photo: Yle

This is the last school week of spring. At the weekend many young people will celebrate outdoors that school is ending.

The police intend to monitor young people's celebrations. The police will visit places where young people meet.

However, the police say that parents are responsible for their children and young people. The police hope that parents will answer their phones during the weekend. The caller may be the police if a young person has got into trouble.
