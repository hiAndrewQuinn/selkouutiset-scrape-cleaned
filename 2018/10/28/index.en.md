## An attack on a synagogue in Pittsburgh

![Pittsburgh police and other authorities continue to investigate the shooting at the synagogue.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10479727)

In the USA 11 people have died in an attack on a synagogue. The attack happened on Saturday in Pittsburgh.

Police have arrested a 46-year-old man who shot people in the synagogue. Police believe that the man carried out the attack alone.The man has written and shared violent messages on social media.

29 charges have already been brought against him. The death penalty is also possible. President Donald Trump says that Americans are mourning the mass murder of Pittsburgh's Jews. The Attorney General says that there is no place in the country for hatred and violence based on religion.

The attack has also been condemned in Europe.

## Pizza boxes may contain harmful chemicals

![Pizza in a box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10479082)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jari Kärkkäinen / Yle

Pizza boxes may contain chemicals that are harmful to health. In Denmark, measurements have been made of the chemicals in the boxes.

Perfluorinated compounds are used in the material. They stop grease from soaking through the box. Experts say that pizza should not be stored in the box.

The Finnish market leader Kotipizza and box maker DS Smith tell Yle that their boxes do not contain perfluorinated compounds.

But there are millions of other boxes from different countries on the Finnish market. Nobody studies the amounts of chemicals in the boxes.

## Not all patient records are safely stored

![a doctor holds his head with a stethoscope in his hand](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10390727)

Open image viewer

In the future patient records can be handed over to Kela for safekeeping. Photo: AOP

Perhaps even millions of old patient records are in places that are not known.

A private psychiatrist or dentist has been able to decide for themselves about putting patient records into an archive. Records have been left, for example, with the heirs when a doctor has closed their practice.

A provision on patient records is coming into the health and social services reform law. Kela will start receiving records and taking care of storing them. This is reported by Lännen Media, which includes 11 newspapers.

## The weather gets warmer from Tuesday

![A boy blows on a frozen window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5004449)

Open image viewer

When it is freezing you can see frost flowers on the window glass. Photo: Attila Balazs / EPA

Last night was the coldest of the autumn so far. In Muonio in Lapland it was minus 16.5 degrees. Tonight will be even colder.

On Monday the weather will still be dry in many places, which means it will not rain. There may be snow showers in Eastern Finland.In the southern and central parts of the country it will perhaps be a few degrees below zero, in Central Finland as much as 10. But the weather will get warmer in the south on Tuesday.

Further north the weather will get warmer on Wednesday. Then rain will start to flow into Finland.The first snow is not yet coming to the south.At the moment there is snow on the ground mainly in Central Finland, south of Oulu and in places in Lapland.
