# Monday 2.1.2017 (radio)

Poor concrete has been found in bridges. Finland's anniversary year began. The US vice president congratulated Finland. A man shot about 40 people in Turkey. In Scotland too, mothers now get a maternity pack. The week gets cold.

## Quality problems with bridges

![A lorry drives on a bridge in Tampere](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9384132)

Open image viewer

Cars can use the bridges without worry. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

Some new bridges are worse than was thought. Concrete with too much air in it was used to build the bridges.

Authorities have found poor concrete in at least 6 bridges. However, the bridges are safe to use.

Poor bridges may not stay in good condition for as long as they were planned. Bridges are normally used for about 100 years.

The authorities are continuing to examine the bridges.

## The anniversary year began

![President Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9382187)

Open image viewer

The President spoke on television and radio again on 1 January. Photo: Yle

Finland's anniversary year has begun. Independent Finland turns 100 years old.

President Sauli Niinistö also spoke about Finland's anniversary year on Sunday. Niinistö said that the anniversary year belongs to everyone. That is why we must help people who are doing badly. The President also wished everyone a good anniversary year.

The Finnish President speaks on the radio and television whenever a new year begins.

## The US vice president congratulated

![Joe Biden](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7812342)

Open image viewer

US Vice President Joe Biden. Photo: Michael Reynolds / EPA

Finland has also received 100th-anniversary congratulations from abroad.

US Vice President Joe Biden made a congratulatory video for Finland. He said that Finns have given the world a lot of good. He praised, for example, the equality of Finnish women.

"Congratulations Finland and another 100 years," Vice President Biden said.

## Attack on a nightclub in Turkey

![A man and two boys mourn beside a coffin covered with a flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9384124)

Open image viewer

About 40 people died in the Istanbul attack. Photo: Sedat Suna / EPA

In Turkey the new year began in a sad way.

A man attacked a nightclub in Istanbul, the largest city in Turkey. He shot people in the middle of New Year's celebrations.

About 40 people died, and about 70 people were injured. Many of them came from Arab countries, because there were many tourists in the nightclub.

The terrorist organisation Isis says that it carried out the attack.

## Scotland is also handing out maternity packs

![Baby in a box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9382880)

Open image viewer

The box of the Scottish maternity pack can also be used as a baby's bed. Photo: Scottish Government

Scotland has taken a model from Finland. In Scotland too, mothers now get a maternity pack.

In Scotland too the pack is free. The pack contains things that the baby and the baby's parents need.

The box of the Scottish maternity pack can also be used as a baby's bed. The pack also contains a mattress and a blanket.

Finland began helping mothers as early as 1938. Today the Finnish maternity pack changes a little every year. New items are added, and, for example, the colours of the clothes change.

## The weather gets colder

The week gets cold. On Wednesday in southern Finland it may be almost 20 degrees of frost, and in Lapland it may be as much as 35 degrees of frost.

At the weekend it is no longer as cold, because snow will fall.
