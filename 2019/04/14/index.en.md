# Sunday 14.4.2019 (radio)

The parliamentary elections have been held in Finland. The election result is even. The winners are the left-wing parties and the Greens. The Finns Party also did well. The biggest loser is the Centre. Sports: Finland won silver at the World Championship in women's ice hockey.

## Opposition parties did well in the elections

Elections have been held in Finland. Finns elected 200 MPs to the Finnish Parliament.

The election result is even. Of the big parties, the SDP, the Finns Party and the National Coalition got almost the same number of votes.

It can be said that the winners of the elections are the left-wing parties and the Greens. The SDP is perhaps the biggest party in the new Parliament. The Left Alliance and the Greens also get more MPs than in the previous elections 4 years ago.

The left-wing parties and the Greens have been in opposition. They have not been in government. That is why they may have got votes from people who have not been satisfied with the government.

## The prime minister's party lost the elections

![Juha Sipilä at the media results evening of the parliamentary elections in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10738941)

Open image viewer

Centre chairman Juha Sipilä lost the election. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The biggest loser of the elections is the Centre. The Centre is the party of Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. The Centre has been the biggest party in Parliament and in the government, but now the number of Centre MPs is falling.

The other big government party, the National Coalition, did better than the Centre. The National Coalition did about as well in these elections as in the previous parliamentary elections.

The third government party, the Blues, lost the elections. It does not get a single MP in the new Parliament. The Blues were formed about 2 years ago, when the Finns Party split.

The Finns Party has been in opposition in recent years. Now the Finns Party did well, and it becomes one of the big parties in the new Parliament.

## Parliament decides Finland's laws

![Parliament question time](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10667524)

Open image viewer

Parliament decides on Finland's matters. Photo: Yle

So there are 200 MPs in Parliament. MPs have important tasks.

MPs decide which laws there are in Finland. They also decide on what the state uses money for.

Parliament's decisions affect the lives of all Finns.

## Finland won silver at the World Championship

![USA world champion](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10739236)

Open image viewer

The USA beat Finland in the final. Photo: Lehtikuva

Still on sports: Finland has won silver at the World Championship in women's ice hockey. Finland lost the final to the USA.

The game was exciting. When the playing time ended, the score was 1 - 1. After that extra time began. The one who scores the first goal wins extra time.

Finland scored a goal, and Finns were already celebrating the win, but the referees did not accept the goal.

Extra time ended without a goal. A penalty shootout followed. In it the USA was better than Finland.

So the USA won gold at the World Championship and is world champion. Finland was left with silver.
