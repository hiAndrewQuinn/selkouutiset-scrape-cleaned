# Thursday 24.1.2019 (radio)

The Foreign Ministry's money was stolen by a scam. The number of unemployed is falling. There are protests in Venezuela. There is extreme heat in Australia. Frosty weather continues.

## The Foreign Ministry was scammed

![Ministry for Foreign Affairs](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10469675)

Open image viewer

A fraudster got 400,000 euros from Finland's Foreign Ministry. Photo: Jyrki Valkama / Yle

The National Bureau of Investigation is investigating a large scam. The Finnish Foreign Ministry has paid development aid grants into the wrong account.

The Foreign Ministry had received emails that looked as if they came from UN employees. The emails had been sent by a fraudster.

The Foreign Ministry paid 400,000 euros into the wrong account. Some of the money has been recovered.

## The number of unemployed is falling

![A worker fills a shop's drinks shelf.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10401167)

Open image viewer

Many people have found work in the retail sector. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

In Finland many unemployed people have got jobs.

There have been many new jobs in the retail sector. Young people have also found work well.

In December there were 146,000 unemployed people. There are now as many unemployed as there were 10 years ago.

## The situation in Venezuela is confused

![Juan Guaidó declared himself acting president of Venezuela.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10612059)

Open image viewer

In Venezuela, opposition leader Guaidó says that he is now leading the country. Photo: Miguel Gutierrez / EPA

In Venezuela in South America there have been serious clashes in recent days. Protesters have demonstrated against the country's president, Nicolás Maduro.

There have been tens of thousands of people at the demonstrations. The police have broken up the demonstrations with harsh methods, and many people have died in the clashes.

The protesters are tired of the president of Venezuela, who won the election by cheating. The economic situation in Venezuela is bad, and citizens' lives are miserable.

Opposition leader Juan Guaidó also opposes the president. The opposition leader says that he is now leading Venezuela.

## Heat is killing animals in Australia

![Dead horses in a dried-up riverbed](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10613331)

Open image viewer

Dozens of wild horses died of dehydration in Australia. Photo: EPA

There has been extreme heat in Australia for more than a month already.

Today in the city of Adelaide it was 47.7 degrees. That is the country's new heat record.

In nature, animals are suffering a great deal. A large herd of wild horses was found dead. They had died of dehydration. Many fish and bats have also died.

## Frost continues in Finland

![A frosty day in Rovaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10607569)

Open image viewer

This is how the bright colours of a frosty day looked in Rovaniemi. Photo: Perttu Ruokangas / Yle

In Finland the weather stays cold. There is frost across the whole country.

Tomorrow, Friday, it will snow in Southern and Central Finland. In Northern Finland the sun will shine and the frost may be 30 degrees.
