# Monday 22.5.2017 (radio)

A Finnish woman is still kidnapped in Afghanistan. The asylum seekers' demonstration continues. The US president is visiting Israel. Many differences in school grades. Use of cash machines is decreasing.

## Finnish woman still kidnapped in Afghanistan

![Two children in front of a house](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9626212)

Open image viewer

The guest house from which the woman was kidnapped in Kabul, the capital of Afghanistan. Photo: Jawad Jalali / EPA

The Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs is trying to free the Finnish woman who was kidnapped in Afghanistan on Saturday.

The ministry has not said what happened to the woman after the kidnapping.

The woman is an aid worker. She was kidnapped on Saturday from a guest house where other aid workers also lived.

Two people died when the kidnapping happened.

In Afghanistan it is estimated that the kidnappers belonged to a criminal gang, and they may demand a ransom.

Foreign Minister Timo Soini says that Finland does not pay ransoms.

Soini does not say what kind of cooperation Finland is doing to get the woman freed.

## The Railway Square camps got more time

![the Finland First camp on Helsinki's Railway Square](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9620243)

Open image viewer

Asylum seekers have been demonstrating on Railway Square since February. Photo: Yle / Mårten Lampén

The demonstrators' camps may stay on Helsinki's Railway Square after all.

The police say that the asylum seekers' camp and the camp of demonstrators who oppose immigration may stay on the square.

The City of Helsinki wanted the demonstrators off the square, because the area has been rented for several summer events.

Now the demonstrators, the police and the city have agreed together that both demonstrations will move to the edge of Railway Square and get smaller.

## Many differences in school grading

![Puistola comprehensive school, lower secondary.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9620441)

Open image viewer

Ninth-graders will soon get their comprehensive school leaving certificates.  Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

There are many differences between Finnish schools in what kind of grades teachers give in leaving certificates.

Leaving certificates are given in spring to pupils who finish comprehensive school. They are in the 9th grade.

In some schools teachers give pupils good grades much more easily than in other schools.

That can be unfair, because with a good leaving certificate it is easier to get into upper secondary school or vocational school.

## Use of cash machines is decreasing

![a man at a cash machine](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9625782)

Open image viewer

The number of cash withdrawal points has decreased.  Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Cash machines are used less than before.

There are fewer cash machines than before, and that is why many people withdraw a lot of money at one time.

Last year money was withdrawn from Otto cash machines a total of 123 million times.

15 years earlier, in 2001, money was withdrawn twice as often.

But a lot of money still moves through cash machines. Last year about 13 billion euros was withdrawn from the machines.

In 2001 a total of 17.5 billion euros was withdrawn from the machines.
