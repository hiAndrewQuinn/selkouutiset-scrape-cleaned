## Sri Lanka protests

![Security forces and protesters clash in Sri Lanka's capital Colombo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_700/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98001462c935ccc7c1a/1657353815)

Open image viewer

There have been many protesters on the streets of Sri Lanka. Photo: EPA-EFE

In South Asia, in Sri Lanka, the country's leaders are changing. Sri Lanka's president and prime minister announced on Saturday that they will resign.

Sri Lanka's leaders are resigning because there have been big protests in the country. People are angry, because the country's economic situation is bad. They say that the bad economic situation is the fault of the president and prime minister.

On Saturday the protesters forced their way into the president's official residence. The president fled.

Now the protesters are celebrating, because the president is resigning. The president intends to stop working on Wednesday.

## Wrong information about trains

![A group of people on the platform at Pieksämäki railway station. A man is just stepping onto the train.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5893,h_3314,x_0,y_344/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97165962ac7b67ec2ba/1655472662)

Open image viewer

At the weekend there were errors in the railway station announcements. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Passengers have received wrong information at some railway stations during the weekend. The reason was a fault in the information system.

Because of the fault, extra stopping places have been announced in railway station announcements. There may also have been wrong information on the display boards.

Passengers received wrong information, for example, about trains that run between Helsinki and Tampere.

## Tango royalty

![The finalists of the 2022 singing competition of the Tango Markets: Keijo Hietikko, Heta Halonen, Lauri Ketonen and Hanna Hirvonen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6611,h_3718,x_0,y_650/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97946762c7ac219e938/1657253265)

Open image viewer

There were 4 singers in the final of the Tango Markets. On the left Keijo Hietikko and Heta Halonen. Photo: Krista Luoma

This year's tango royalty has been chosen. The tango royalty were chosen at the Tango Markets in Seinäjoki on Saturday.

4 singers, 2 women and 2 men, got into the final of the singing competition. From them a tango queen and a tango king were chosen.

This year's tango queen is Heta Halonen, and the tango king is Keijo Hietikko. The judges agreed that Halonen and Hietikko were this year's best tango singers.

The Seinäjoki Tango Markets is a popular event in Finland. This year about 250 singers took part in the singing competition.

The strawberries have ripened. There are now many strawberries, because the weather has been warm.

The Fruit and Berry Growers' Association says that strawberries are good to buy now if you intend to freeze them.

Strawberries can also be obtained later in the summer, because some strawberry varieties ripen only during late summer.
