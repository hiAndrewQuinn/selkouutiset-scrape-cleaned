# Sunday 26.4.2020 (radio)

Finland's state of emergency. Corona infections in Finland. Veterans' Day is being marked differently. Is the North Korean leader ill? The weather stays cool.

## The state of emergency has lasted 6 weeks

![The picture shows Prime Minister Sanna Marin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11309092)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Finland has now been in a state of emergency for 6 weeks. Because of the corona epidemic, life has been restricted in many ways.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin said today that the Finnish government will assess the situation on 3 May.

So next Sunday, a week from now, the government will assess whether the state of emergency will continue.

## 190 people have died of corona in Finland

![A nurse in protective clothing takes a sample from a suspected corona case.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11293425)

There are now officially more than 4,500 coronavirus infections in Finland.

101 new infections were found.

190 people have died of the coronavirus in Finland.

## Veterans' Day is tomorrow

![The Finnish flag flutters in a yard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7304749)

Open image viewer

Veterans' Day is 27 April. On that day, Finnish flags fly on flagpoles. Photo: Hanna Lumme / Yle

Tomorrow, Monday, Veterans' Day is celebrated in Finland. It is a festive day in honour of the war veterans.

Because of the corona epidemic, Veterans' Day is being marked differently.

The President of the Republic, Sauli Niinistö, will publish a greeting on social media in the morning.

A virtual choir of more than 1,000 people will perform the Finlandia Hymn. The performance can be watched on the government's YouTube channel.

## Is the North Korean leader ill?

![Kim Jong-un sits at a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4252,h_2392,x_0,y_219/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6681375ea3b317218f7/1587786608)

Open image viewer

There are many rumours in the world about the North Korean leader. Photo: KCNA / EPA

There are many rumours in the world about what has happened to the North Korean leader Kim Jong-un.

Kim Jong-un has not appeared in public for a long time.

The last thing heard was that he may have had a major operation.

On social media people have already speculated that he has died.

The North Korean leader Kim Jong-un is only 36 years old. Many suspect that he is in very poor condition, because he has unhealthy habits and works too much.

## Spring is coming slowly

![Snow in Rovaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6665645e9edff63f055/1587470542)

Open image viewer

There is still a lot of snow on the ground and in the forests in Northern Finland. Photo: Antti Mikkola / Yle

April has been cool, and on many nights there has been frost. That is why the progress of spring has been slow.

In Southern and Central Finland spring started early, but now it is advancing at a completely normal pace.

The Finnish Environment Institute estimates that spring is late in Northern Finland.

There is still a lot of snow in Northern Finland. There will be snow until June.

In the north, very bad floods may come when the snow finally starts to melt.

## The weather stays cool

![May Day celebrations in Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11319504)

May Day will be celebrated next week. A year ago on May Day, this was the weather in Turku in Western Finland. Photo: Linus Hoffman / Yle

The weather stays cool.

Tomorrow, Monday, it will be sunny at first, but then it will become cloudy.

During the day, sleet and snow may fall in Eastern and Northern Finland.

Tonight there will be a little frost across the whole country.
