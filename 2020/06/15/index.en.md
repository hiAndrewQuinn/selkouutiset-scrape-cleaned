# Monday 15.6.2020 (radio)

The state of emergency is ending. Corona restrictions still apply. The corona situation is easing in hospitals. The police are investigating a tweet that mocked a Black man. The first strawberries are ripening.

## State of emergency ends in Finland

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Minister of Justice Anna-Maja Henriksson at the government press conference in Helsinki on 15 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11402754)

Minister of Justice Anna-Maja Henriksson and Prime Minister Sanna Marin said that use of the Emergency Powers Act will end. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The state of emergency in Finland is ending. The Finnish government said today that Finland no longer needs the Emergency Powers Act for exceptional circumstances, because the coronavirus situation has eased. Normal conditions will return in Finland on Tuesday.

The Emergency Powers Act for exceptional circumstances has been in force for 3 months. It gave the government more power to decide things, so that people could be protected.

Now the corona situation has eased, and the government is ending use of the Emergency Powers Act.

## Corona restrictions continue

![Linda, Veera, Nina and Tiina enjoy sparkling wine on the terrace of Mattolaituri in Helsinki on the evening of 5 June 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11389504)

Although the state of emergency ends on Tuesday, many instructions and restrictions are still in force in Finland because of the corona situation. For example, restrictions on restaurant customer numbers and opening hours still apply.

Travel restrictions also still apply. On Monday Finland opened its borders to the Baltic countries, that is, Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania, and to the Nordic countries of Norway, Denmark and Iceland. Movement to and from other countries is still restricted.

In Finland, people must still be careful, wash their hands and keep safe distances, so that the coronavirus does not spread.

## The corona situation is easing

![Espoo Samaria health centre works as a pandemic centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11262085)

The corona situation in Finland has continued to improve. No deaths due to the coronavirus have been reported in Finland on Monday.

Since yesterday, 4 new coronavirus infections have been reported. 22 patients are in hospital because of the coronavirus, that is 4 fewer than yesterday.

## Turtiainen's tweet leads to a police investigation

![MP Ano Turtiainen votes in the election of the Speaker at a plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11400494)

Open image viewer

MP Ano Turtiainen in Parliament. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The police are starting an investigation into a tweet by MP Ano Turtiainen. Ano Turtiainen posted a picture on Twitter that mocked a Black man who died in the USA after being restrained by police.

The Prosecutor General has ordered the police to investigate whether Ano Turtiainen broke the law when he mocked the Black man who died in the USA.

## Strawberries are ripening

![Ripe and unripe strawberries.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3981,h_2239,x_403,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6883215ee1d431eb8bc/1591858280)

The first strawberries are beginning to ripen. Strawberries that have grown protected under gauze have already gone on sale in Southern Finland.

For Midsummer, strawberries are already available from many strawberry farms in Southern Finland. Elsewhere in Finland, strawberries have ripened more slowly.

The main strawberry season begins at the turn of June and July.
