# Sunday 25.11.2018 (radio)

EU leaders approved the British withdrawal agreement. A grave memorial for Mauno Koivisto was unveiled. There are many air passengers in Finland. Syria demands that the UN investigate allegations of a gas attack in Aleppo. The USA and Mexico agree on asylum applications.

## EU leaders approved the British withdrawal agreement

![The European Council's announcement that the British EU withdrawal agreement has been approved.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10525218)

If the British Parliament does not approve the withdrawal agreement, Britain will have to leave the EU without an agreement. Photo: European Council, Yle News Graphics

Britain and the leaders of the EU countries have approved Britain's EU withdrawal agreement. The withdrawal agreement still has to be approved by the European Parliament and the British Parliament. The British Parliament may not approve the withdrawal agreement. There has been a dispute in the British Parliament about the agreement. The situation is difficult for Britain.

## President Koivisto's grave memorial was unveiled

![President Mauno Koivisto's grave memorial at the unveiling ceremony at Hietaniemi Cemetery in Helsinki on 25 November](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10525158)

Open image viewer

The grave memorial is made of black diabase granite. It was designed by the artist and photographer Perttu Saksa. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

President Mauno Koivisto's grave memorial has been unveiled at Hietaniemi Cemetery in Helsinki. The memorial is called Map. The memorial is a large black stone. The stone is flat on top. There are golden patterns on top of the stone.

Mauno Koivisto was President of Finland from 1982 to 1994. President Koivisto died last year.

## Many passengers at airports

![Helsinki-Vantaa airport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10347802)

Open image viewer

Helsinki-Vantaa is Finland's main airport. Photo: Elise Tykkyläinen / Yle

There are many passengers at Finnish airports. Over 20 million passengers have passed through Finnish airports this year. There are many more air passengers than last year.

Finland's busiest airports are Helsinki-Vantaa, Oulu and Rovaniemi. At the main airport, Helsinki-Vantaa, there are over 17 million passengers this year.

## Syria: the UN must investigate claims of a gas attack

![Map of Syria.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9475973)

Open image viewer

The rebels say that the regime staged the attack. Photo: Yle News Graphics

Syria is asking the UN to investigate whether a gas attack was carried out in Aleppo.

Syria says that on Saturday the rebels carried out a gas attack in the city of Aleppo, in which over 100 people were injured.

The rebels say that they did not carry out a gas attack. Syria asks that the UN investigate the matter.

## The USA and Mexico agree on matters concerning asylum seekers

![Metal fence.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10494529)

Open image viewer

Processing an asylum application can take a long time in the USA.

The USA and Mexico have agreed on matters concerning asylum seekers.

The USA and Mexico have agreed that asylum seekers must wait for their application to be processed on the Mexican side. Many people want to come to the USA from Mexico.

Processing asylum applications in the USA can take many years.
