# Saturday 21.1.2017 (radio)

The serial drowner was caught. People oppose Trump in demonstrations. A bus disaster in Italy. Travel to Turkey is decreasing. Finnish tourists are returning from Gambia.

## The serial drowner was caught

![Pekka Seppänen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9416473)

Open image viewer

Pekka Seppänen is called a serial drowner because he caused several people to drown. Photo: Eastern Finland Police

The police have caught the man who was sentenced to prison this week for several drownings.

The court sentenced Pekka Seppänen to more than 14 years in prison because he had drowned people and committed several other serious crimes.

But Seppänen was not at home when the police came to take him to prison on Tuesday.

The police got tips from the public about where Seppänen had been. Seppänen was caught in Joensuu. He was in a car driven by another person.

## Marches against Trump around the world

![Women hold signs with a cat and the text This pussy bites back](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9416427)

Open image viewer

Women marched around the world, also in Sydney. Photo: Dan Himbrechts / EPA

Demonstrations against the new President of the United States, Donald Trump, have been held around the world.

In the US capital Washington, as many as 200,000 people are taking part in a demonstration.

A demonstration was also held in Helsinki on Saturday.

Most demonstrations have been held for women's rights, and that is why the demonstrations have been called Women's Marches.

The demonstrators demand respect for human rights and freedom of religion.

In opinion polls, only 40 percent of Americans say that they trust Donald Trump.

## A group of young people died in a bus crash in Italy

![Italy Verona bus crash](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9416333)

Open image viewer

The bus was carrying teenage schoolchildren, and it was badly destroyed in the crash. Photo: Vigili del Fuoco / EPA

In Northern Italy, near Verona, 16 people have died in a bus accident.

In addition, about forty were injured. The accident happened around midnight.

On board the bus were teenage schoolchildren from Hungary.

The vehicle apparently hit a pole on the motorway and caught fire.

## Trips to Turkey have decreased

![Flowers in front of the Reina nightclub in Istanbul.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9382982)

Open image viewer

Unrest drives tourists away from Turkey. Photo: Yasin Akgul / AFP

Finns' travel to Turkey has decreased in recent years.

Travel to Turkey has fallen by 50 percent from the peak years. In the peak years, about 120,000 Finns travelled to Turkey a year, now only 60,000.

Finns' travel to Turkey has decreased because there has been unrest in Turkey.

However, Turkey is still Finns' third most popular travel destination. The most popular destinations are Greece and the Canary Islands.

## Finnish tourists are returning from Gambia

![A smiling Adama Barrow dressed in white waves his hand. There are several people around him.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9415073)

Open image viewer

Adama Barrow finally became President of Gambia when the previous president agreed to give up power. Photo: EPA

About 300 Finnish tourists are coming home today from Gambia, where unrest has been feared.

Some of the Finns have already arrived back home. They say that it was calm in Gambia.

Unrest was feared because Gambia's previous president Yahya Jammeh refused to give up power.

But last night Jammeh announced that he is giving up power because he wants to secure peace in Gambia.

The next president will be Adama Barrow, who won the election in December.
