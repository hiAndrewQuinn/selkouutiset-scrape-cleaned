# Wednesday 18.3.2020 (radio)

Day care centres stay open for all children. The emergency law guidelines may be tightened. There is now a shortage of blood donors. The joint application to universities began today. The Eurovision Song Contest has been cancelled.

## Schools closed - day care centres open

![Children playing in a day care centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11211424)

Open image viewer

Day care centres continue to operate despite the coronavirus. All children of all families have the right to go to day care, even though the government recommends home care for as many as possible. Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

The government has once again told about the consequences of the coronavirus. Schools and educational institutions have today mostly moved to remote teaching.

Day care centres stay open for all children. The government wants to secure parents' work and remote work.

The government also wants that the day care of children does not move to grandparents, who belong to a risk group.

However, the government recommends home day care if that is at all possible.

Education Minister Li Andersson hopes that municipalities will not charge day care fees for children who do not come to day care.

## Emergency guidelines may be tightened if restrictions are not followed

![Old age](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9957820)

Open image viewer

People over 70 may go outdoors despite the coronavirus epidemic, but they must be careful. Now all close contact with all people must be avoided. Photo: Viivi Sihvonen / Yle

The guidelines of the Emergency Powers Act may be tightened.

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health is ready to tighten the guidelines if people do not follow them.

In particular, the movement restrictions for people over 70 must be taken seriously. That is what Permanent Secretary Kirsi Varhila from the ministry says.

People over 70 can still go to the shop and the pharmacy, if they have an important matter there. They may also go outdoors, but not in a group. Varhila says that people must not stay or socialise with people outside the home.

The most important thing is to avoid all close contact with all people. The police monitor that no more than 10 people are gathered in public.

The aim is to protect risk groups, which people over 70 belong to.

The government and the authorities remind that common efforts to prevent the virus apply to all people. Only in this way is it possible to protect oneself and others.

## Blood service hopes for more blood donors

![About half a litre of blood is taken from each donor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10309323)

Open image viewer

There is now a shortage of blood donors. If you are not ill and have not been abroad much, it is worth going to donate blood, the Blood Service hopes. Photo: Maija-Liisa Juntti / Yle

There is now a shortage of blood donors. The coronavirus and emergency measures have reduced the number of blood donors.

The Finnish Red Cross Blood Service appeals to people and asks them to donate blood also in the coming weeks. There is a need for donors of almost all blood groups.

The Blood Service stresses that donating blood does not, for example, weaken immunity.

However, blood must not be donated when you are ill. Travelling in Europe leads to a 14-day ban on donating.

## The joint application has begun

![LAB Niemi campus](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4160,h_2340,x_0,y_215/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6515535e67457e6bc51/1583826377)

Lahti University of Applied Sciences. Photo: Meeri Niinistö / Yle

The joint application to universities has begun today. The application concerns courses in Finnish and Swedish that start in the autumn. The application period ends on 1 April.

The results of the certificate-based selections will be announced to applicants by the end of May at the latest. The results of the others will be announced by the beginning of July at the latest.

## The Eurovision Song Contest has been cancelled

![Eurovision 2020](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6537955e6f94b614d3a/1584370931)

The 2020 Eurovision Song Contest was due to be held in Rotterdam, the Netherlands, in May.

The Eurovision Song Contest will not be held this year. The reason is the danger of the coronavirus, says the European Broadcasting Union, EBU.

This is the first time that the Eurovision Song Contest has been cancelled in its 64-year history.
