# Saturday 8.12.2018 (radio)

Former Finnish President Tarja Halonen says that reuniting families improves the integration of immigrants. Annegret Kramp-Karrenbauer is the new chairperson of Germany's Christian Democratic Party. Protests have continued in France. On Saturday Finnish music day was celebrated.

## President Halonen

![Tarja Halonen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10545103)

President Tarja Halonen was a guest on the TV morning show Ykkösaamu on Saturday. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Former Finnish President Tarja Halonen says that reuniting families improves the integration of immigrants.

When a family moves together, integration succeeds better, Halonen says. In Halonen's opinion, one's own family can also supervise young immigrants better than the police. In that way crimes can also be prevented.

In Finland there has been a lot of talk about the sex crimes in Oulu, whose perpetrators were young men with a foreign background. Halonen reminded people that similar crimes are forbidden in all cultures.

Halonen was a guest on the television programme Ykkösaamu on Saturday.

Tarja Halonen was President of Finland from 2000 to 2012. She was Finland's first female president.

## Leader of the German Christian Democrats

![Annegret Kramp-Karrenbauer and Angela Merkel.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10486055)

Annegret Kramp-Karrenbauer (on the left) follows Angela Merkel (on the right) in the role of chairperson of the Christian Democrats. Photo: Omer Messinger / EPA

The German Christian Democratic Party, CDU, has got a new chairperson. The new chairperson is Annegret Kramp-Karrenbauer.

Before Kramp-Karrenbauer, the chairperson of the Christian Democrats was Angela Merkel. Merkel is also the Chancellor of Germany, that is, she leads the German government. Merkel still plans to continue as Chancellor.

The Christian Democrats are the biggest party in Germany. It is possible that the new chairperson of the party will someday become Chancellor. That is why the matter interests people outside Germany too.

## Protests in France

![Yellow vest protesters in Paris.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10545140)

Open image viewer

The yellow vests protest against President Macron. Photo: Sameer Al-Doumy / AFP

Protests have continued in France. The police have arrested hundreds of people in the capital, Paris, and elsewhere in France.

Many shops, museums and metro stations in Paris were closed on Saturday because of the protests. The Eiffel Tower was also closed.

There have been protests in France for many weekends now. The protesters say that President Emmanuel Macron is the president of the rich. They got angry when the government decided to raise the petrol tax. The government has already promised that it will not raise the petrol tax any more, but this has not calmed the protesters.

The protesters are known as the yellow vests, because many protesters are wearing yellow vests.

## Finnish music day

![Jean Sibelius](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9890474)

Open image viewer

Finland's national composer Jean Sibelius was born on 8.12.1865. Photo: Santeri Levas / Finnish Museum of Photography

On Saturday Finnish music day was celebrated. The day is celebrated on 8 December, because it is the birthday of Jean Sibelius.

Sibelius is Finland's national composer. He was born in 1865 and died in 1957.
