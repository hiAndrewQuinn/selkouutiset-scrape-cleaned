## Food insecurity

![A person shopping at a grocery store.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78927060604a9076f02/1616923335)

Open image viewer

Food insecurity means that a person does not have enough money to buy food. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Many workers in service sectors find it hard to make their money stretch to cover food.

The universities of Helsinki and Tampere made a survey for the members of the Service Union PAM. Of those who answered the survey, 65 per cent experienced food insecurity. Food insecurity means, for example, that because of lack of money, a person has to cut down their meals or even skip them.

According to the study, the worst situation is among young people, workers in the tourism and restaurant sectors, and men.

The survey was made in 2019, that is, before the corona pandemic. Most of the respondents were working.

## Winter storm

![Snow drifting on a road, in a photo taken by a reader.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1279,h_719,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90819861f5579c8aa40/1643469069)

Open image viewer

Storm Valtteri will bring more snow, especially to Southern and Central Finland. Photo: Susanna Salminen / Reader's photo

A strong winter storm has arrived in Finland. The storm has been named Valtteri.

Storm Valtteri blows a lot of snow into Southern and Central Finland. In addition, the wind blows at storm strength in places.

Driving conditions are poor in a large part of the country. In the Helsinki area, driving conditions can be very dangerous.

The storm continues until tomorrow.

## Film award for Finland

![Director Alli Haapasalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4920,h_2767,x_8,y_65/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90811761f421825180c/1643389464)

Open image viewer

Director Alli Haapasalo. Photo: Lehtikuva

A Finnish film has won an important award at the Sundance film festival in the USA.

The film Tytöt tytöt tytöt won the audience award in the international competition of the film festival. The film was directed by Alli Haapasalo.

The film follows the lives of three teenage girls. It is about sexuality, first love and everyday friendship.

Tytöt tytöt tytöt is the first long Finnish fiction film ever chosen to compete at the Sundance film festival. Sundance is the USA's most important film festival.

The film will next be shown at the Berlin film festival in February. The film will come to Finnish cinemas in April.

## Garden birdwatching

![Bramblings eating seeds on a bird table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10552085)

Open image viewer

You can spot birds from a bird table in your own garden. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

This weekend, birds in home gardens are being watched again.

The bird organisation Birdlife Suomi is organising the traditional garden birdwatching event. In the event, birds are watched for one hour in one's own garden or at some other place. Bird sightings can be reported on Birdlife's website.

Garden birdwatching lasts the whole weekend.
