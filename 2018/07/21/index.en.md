# Saturday 21.7.2018 (radio)

Forest fires are being put out in Northern Finland. There are fairly few berries in the forest. You can now catch crayfish. The weather stays warm.

## Forest fires are being put out in Northern Finland

![Forest fire at Ratsasvaara in Russia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10316443)

Open image viewer

The forest fire threatens to spread from the Russian side into Finland. Photo: Lapland Rescue Service

In Northern Finland, rescue service firefighters are trying to put out forest fires.

Forest is burning in Inari. The forest fire spread from Russia across the border into Finland.

In Salla, another fire also threatens to spread from Russia to the Finnish side. Smoke and sparks are drifting to the Finnish side with the wind.

Today the rain has helped a little to put out the fires.

Ordinary people have said that they want to help the firefighters. The Lapland Rescue Service says that the firefighters do not need help. The Rescue Service says that people must not go to the forest fire area.

## There are fairly few berries in the forest

![bilberry](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10312342)

Open image viewer

Now is a good time to look for bilberries in the forest. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

Fairly few berries are growing in the forests this summer. Because of the drought, fewer bilberries and raspberries are growing than before.

Now is the best time to look for bilberries and raspberries in the forest. Lingonberries will only ripen later.

You can look for a good bilberry spot on the internet. There is a website called marjahavainnot.fi. The website tells you where in Finland bilberries are now ripe.

## You can catch crayfish in lakes

![Marko Paloniemi test-fishes for crayfish in Lappajärvi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7421856)

Open image viewer

Crayfish are caught with a device called a crayfish trap. Photo: Pasi Takkunen / Yle

Today, Saturday, the crayfish season began. That means crayfish may be caught in lakes. This summer the crayfish have grown well, because it has been warm and the waters have warmed up quickly.

Finland's lakes have noble crayfish and river crayfish. River crayfish are most common in Eastern and Northern Finland.

The number of crayfish varies a lot. Crayfish plague is an unpleasant disease that can kill many crayfish.

Crayfish may be caught until the end of October.
