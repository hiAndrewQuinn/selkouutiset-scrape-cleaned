# Thursday 13.10.2022 (radio)

European air defence system. Russian attacks on Ukraine. Students return to campus. Finns' use of electricity. Weather forecast.

## European air defence system

![Jens Stoltenberg sits and listens to Finland's Defence Minister Antti Kaikkonen speaking to him.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2919,h_1641,x_0,y_170/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-102070863483095e5ee4/1667547190)

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg and Finland's Defence Minister Antti Kaikkonen are talking. Photo: Kenzo Tribouillard / AFP

The military alliance NATO has continued its meeting in Brussels on Thursday. Finland and Sweden also take part in the NATO meeting.

Finland and 14 NATO countries have agreed on a new European air defence system. Germany proposed the European air defence system. The aim is to improve European air defence and missile defence through joint procurement.

Defence Minister Antti Kaikkonen says that Finland takes part in the cooperation to some extent. For example, the Baltic countries and Norway are involved. Sweden is not taking part in the European air defence cooperation, at least not yet.

## Ukraine

Russia has continued attacks on Ukraine. On Thursday morning Russia again fired missiles at Ukraine's capital, Kyiv. Kyiv's authorities say that Russia fired rockets at an area that is built up densely.

Russia also attacked the city of Mykolaiv at night. All week Russia has hit many Ukrainian cities with missiles.

Many NATO countries have promised to give Ukraine more air defence systems.

## Students

![Coat hooks, with people in the lobby by them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5003,h_2804,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1011617632c72e34d3a3/1663858263)

Open image viewer

Students are once again meeting many other students. Photo: Risto Degerman

Students have once again been able to attend in-person classes at universities and universities of applied sciences. Most students have gladly returned to in-person teaching. For some students, returning to the university area, that is, the campus, is very exciting.

Students got used to studying alone. Many students lived in their home town. That is why it may feel strange to them to be in a new place, with many strangers around them. The situation is especially exciting for students who started studying during the corona time.

Kiia Kilponen, study psychologist at the University of Oulu, says that it can take a student many months to get used to the new situation.

## Electricity use

![Cosy decorative lamps in close-up.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4954,h_2787,x_0,y_224/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6609115e85d712b28ce/1648200387)

Open image viewer

Electricity can be saved, for example, by switching off unnecessary lights. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

Finns have reduced their use of electricity. Electricity use has fallen by almost 14 percent this autumn compared with last autumn. This is what the Energy Industries association says. It monitors energy matters in Finland.

Industry has also reduced its use of electricity. Industrial electricity use fell by more than 4 percent in September compared with September last year.

The Energy Industries association says that saving electricity is a good thing. Saving electricity helps to lower the price of electricity, when consumption goes down.
