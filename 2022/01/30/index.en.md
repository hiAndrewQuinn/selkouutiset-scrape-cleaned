# Sunday 30.1.2022 (radio)

Storm Valtteri. Winter storm in Sweden and the USA. Corona in Beijing. Rafael Nadal. The weather.

## Finland's winter storm

![A snowplough leaves snow in a large snow heap at Senate Square in front of the Cathedral in Helsinki during the Valtteri snowstorm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_236/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90829161f647c95e20f/1643530362)

Winter storm Valtteri has caused less damage than was feared in advance.

The storm cut electricity, but less than the electricity companies expected. At most, just over 3,500 households were without electricity.

Driving conditions have been very poor in places. Road traffic has nevertheless gone well, and no serious accidents have happened. There have been delays in rail and air traffic because of the storm.

The storm brought more snow, especially to Southern Finland. In places about 20 centimetres of snow fell within a day.

During Sunday the storm has gradually moved east.

## Winter storms in Sweden and the USA

![A man cleared snow from a pavement in Boston on Saturday 29 January.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5990,h_3375,x_0,y_112/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90822561f56f166cae3/1643474842)

A man sweeps snow from a street in Boston in the USA. Photo: Cj Gunther

Winter storms have also been elsewhere during the weekend.

For example, in Sweden a winter storm swept over the southern and western parts of the country. It knocked down trees, closed bridges and cut electricity to thousands of households.

In Malmö a construction crane collapsed in the storm wind late on Saturday evening.

On the east coast of the USA, the winter storm has cut electricity to over 100,000 households.

Snow has fallen over a wide area. For example, in the states of New York and Massachusetts, snow fell in places to over 60 centimetres.

Thousands of flights were cancelled because of the snowstorm.

## Corona in Beijing

![Corona testing in Beijing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4815,h_2708,x_0,y_191/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90772461f3b1a8ad9e0/1643360807)

Passengers sit for a corona test at Beijing International Airport. Photo: Sergei Bobylev / AOP

The Chinese capital, Beijing, has recorded the largest number of corona infections in 1.5 years.

Today, 20 corona infections have been reported in Beijing. China has now started mass corona testing in Beijing.

China has tried to suppress corona with harsh measures before the Beijing Winter Olympics. Despite this, infection clusters have been found in different parts of the country.

The Beijing Winter Olympics begin on Wednesday.

## Nadal made tennis history

![Rafael Nadal lifts a trophy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4817,h_2709,x_0,y_167/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90832161f6aa22c76f3/1643555480)

Rafael Nadal lifts the winner's trophy. Photo: Darrian Traynor / Getty Images

History has been made at the Australian Open tennis tournament.

Spain's Rafael Nadal won the men's singles. The final against Russia's Daniil Medvedev lasted over 5 hours.

The win made Nadal the all-time number one. He is now the only male player who has won 21 Grand Slam titles.
