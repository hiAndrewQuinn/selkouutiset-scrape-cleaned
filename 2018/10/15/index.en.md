# Monday 15.10.2018 (radio)

The number of criminal gangs has risen sharply. Asylum seekers under 18 are disappearing. What does a workplace without a boss sound like? There are now about 400 ringed seals. A royal baby for Britain.

## Criminal gangs have increased sharply

![On Monday Malmö police carried out a forensic investigation on Drottningsgatan, where the shooting happened.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10262176)

In Sweden the police have to investigate gang violence more than in Finland. Photo: Johan Nilsson / EPA

There are now a lot of criminal gangs in Finland. The police say that there are now about 90 criminal gangs in Finland. Still at the beginning of the 2000s, it was counted that there were 8 gangs in Finland.

The police say that street violence by gangs is still quite small. However, people fear that street violence will increase in Finland. In Sweden, for example, many shootings between criminal gangs have been seen.

## Underage asylum seekers are disappearing

![An asylum seeker without papers sits on a bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9539344)

Open image viewer

Underage asylum seekers are apparently disappearing because the processing of asylum is slow. Photo: Carolus Manninen / Yle

Thousands of asylum seekers under 18 have disappeared in Europe. Over the last 4 years about 220,000 asylum seekers under 18 have come to the EU and Norway alone. At least 10,000 of them have disappeared.

In Sweden, the number who have disappeared is about 60 school classes. In Finland almost 200 have disappeared. It is believed that minors disappear, for example, because the processing of asylum is slow and there is a lack of information.

## Firms without bosses are also emerging in Finland

![Employees in a meeting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10294255)

Open image viewer

There are already many firms in Finland that have decided to work without a boss. Photo: Mostphotos

In Finland a lot is now being discussed about bosses in the workplace. Firms with no bosses at all have also emerged in Finland. For example, in the IT field there are already many firms that have no bosses, that is, no managers.

Experts have also started to consider whether bosses are always necessary. People are thinking about whether employees can do the bosses' jobs themselves.

## Saimaa ringed seals are doing a little better than before

![Viljo is reportedly the largest Saimaa ringed seal ever found.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8998454)

Open image viewer

This is Viljo. He is a big seal. There are now about 400 ringed seals. Photo: Juha Taskinen

The number of Saimaa ringed seals has increased a little. The authorities estimate that there are now about 20 more seals than a year ago. The Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry says that there are now about 400 Saimaa ringed seals.

The number of seals has increased in recent years. 4 years ago the estimate for seals was 300. The Ministry thinks the number of seals has grown because the Saimaa ringed seal has been protected for years.

Protectors have, for example, made snow drifts on the ice for the seals in winter, when there has been little snow. The seal gives birth in winter, so the snow drifts are important nests for them.
