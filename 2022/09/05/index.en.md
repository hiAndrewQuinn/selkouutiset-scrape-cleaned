Britain's new prime minister is Liz Truss. She is currently Britain's foreign minister.

Britain's Conservative Party elected Liz Truss as its new leader. At the same time she will become Britain's prime minister. Truss's rival in the vote was Britain's former finance minister Rishi Sunak.

Britain's prime minister is changing, because Boris Johnson resigned in July. The Queen will appoint Liz Truss as prime minister on Tuesday.

## Famine in Somalia

![Small red three-wheeled scooter taxis on the streets of Mogadishu, the capital of Somalia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_457/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-954741627e4501216c7/1652443319)

Small red three-wheeled scooter taxis on the streets of Mogadishu, the capital of Somalia. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

Somalia is threatened by famine.

The UN warns that there will soon be famine in Somalia if the drought gets worse and food prices keep rising.

The UN estimates that as many as millions of people may need food aid in the Horn of Africa region. The countries of the Horn of Africa are Somalia, Ethiopia and Eritrea.

Very little rain has fallen in East Africa. The drought causes a shortage of food.

## State debt

The Finnish state is taking on 10 billion euros more debt.

On Sunday the government reported on the support that the state gives to electricity companies. The state lends money to electricity companies and guarantees their debts. The state itself takes on debt for the support.

In total the state is taking on almost 19 billion euros of debt this year.

## National parks

![Hiker stands on a small rock in Nuuksio National Park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80989860aa89b94c169/1621789657)

Open image viewer

Hiker in Nuuksio National Park in Espoo in May 2021. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

At the start of the year more people visited Finland's national parks than before the corona epidemic. Between January and July people visited the national parks a total of 2.2 million times. During the corona period even more people visited the national parks.

National parks are large areas where nature is protected. National parks are always open. You can go to a national park without an entrance fee.

At the start of the year Finland's most popular national parks were Pallas–Yllästunturi, Urho Kekkonen National Park, Nuuksio, Koli and Pyhä–Luosto. Finland has over 40 national parks in total.
