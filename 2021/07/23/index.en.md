The Olympic Games have been opened. The opening ceremony was held in the Japanese capital, Tokyo, on Friday.

Swimmer Ari-Pekka Liukkonen and shooter Satu Mäkelä-Nummela carried the Finnish flag when the Finnish team arrived at the stadium. The Finnish team has 45 athletes.

The Tokyo Olympics were supposed to be held a year ago already. However, they were moved because of the corona. Now the Olympics are being held, but because of corona there are no spectators.

In Finland the Olympics can be followed on TV2 and on Yle Areena.

## Corona vaccinations for school pupils

![A woman gets a corona vaccination.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4539,h_2553,x_0,y_404/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83309160f412098b03e/1633521111)

Open image viewer

Corona vaccinations for lower secondary school pupils may perhaps start straight away in autumn. Photo: /All Over Press

In Finland, corona vaccinations for school pupils are being planned.

Mika Rämet, director of the Vaccine Research Centre, estimates that corona vaccinations for lower secondary school pupils may start soon. Lower secondary school pupils means 12 to 15-year-olds.

Corona vaccinations for lower secondary school pupils may start at the same time as school starts. School starts in about 3 weeks.

Rämet thinks it is possible that school pupils will get corona vaccinations from school health care. Rämet says that a lower secondary school pupil can already decide for themselves whether they take a corona vaccination.

Mika Rämet also says that vaccinating children of primary school age, that is, under 12-year-olds, is being studied. If it is decided to vaccinate primary school children against corona, the vaccinations will start only early next year, that is, in the spring term of schools.

![A man is given a corona test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82110660c87b18bdba3/1623751620)

The number of corona infections is growing. On Friday it was reported that there are 412 new corona infections in Finland.

No new corona deaths have been reported in recent days.

Almost 65 percent of Finns have had the first corona vaccination, and 30 percent of Finns have had both corona vaccinations.

## Suspected crime by a postman

![Postman](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4214,h_2370,x_0,y_270/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-413991595e03bf6df55/1571807253)

Open image viewer

It is unusual for a postman to be suspected of a crime. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The police suspect that a postman committed a crime in Pori in Western Finland.

The postman left about 2,000 mailings undelivered. The mailings were found in a wardrobe.

The police say that the postman was young and inexperienced. He is no longer working at Posti.
