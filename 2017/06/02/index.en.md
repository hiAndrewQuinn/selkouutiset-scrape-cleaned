# Friday 2.6.2017 (radio)

The United States is withdrawing from the international climate agreement. Other countries are disappointed, but intend to continue the fight against climate change. Over 77,000 students graduated in a trade. There is still more than half a metre of snow at Kilpisjärvi.

## Trump does not want the climate agreement

![Donald Trump](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9646832)

Open image viewer

US President Donald Trump. Photo: Saul Loeb / AFP

The USA is withdrawing from the international climate agreement. President Donald Trump says that the USA is leaving the climate agreement, because it is unfair and too expensive for the USA.

However, Trump says that the USA may perhaps negotiate a new agreement that is better for the USA.

The international climate agreement is meant to slow down climate change. Climate change means the warming of the Earth's climate because of human activity.

Trump has said that he does not believe in climate change.

## Other countries are disappointed

![Emmanuel Macron](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9642695)

Open image viewer

French President Emmanuel Macron. Photo: Alexander Zemlianichenko / EPA

Other countries are disappointed with President Trump's decision to leave the international climate agreement.

For example, French President Emmanuel Macron says that Trump is making a big mistake.

Other countries intend to continue the fight against climate change. For example, the European Union, China and Canada say that they will continue to keep to the climate agreement.

The European Union has also said that the agreement will not be renegotiated.

More than 140 countries are part of the agreement.

The US states of California, New York and Washington also intend to continue measures to slow down climate change, despite Trump's decision.

## Many graduated in a trade

![Vocational school students studying in a workshop hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9091282)

Open image viewer

Many vocational school students completed their studies. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

Today, Friday, students who have now graduated in a trade have been celebrated in Finland. A total of more than 77,000 students graduated in a trade.

Most of those who graduated in a trade are adults. This is because nowadays it is common to change trade or to get further training.

On Yle's website there is an "amiskone" (a search tool), where you can look up the names of those who graduated. Included are students who have completed a basic, vocational or specialist vocational qualification.

## There is still snow in Lapland

![Large snowflakes drift in a snowy pine forest on 1 June in Purnumukka, Sodankylä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9644621)

Open image viewer

This is what 1 June looked like in northern Lapland. Photo: Sanna Safronova

In northern Lapland there is now more snow than usual at the beginning of June. For example, in Posio and Kittilä there has not been so much snow at the beginning of June since the 1960s.

In Kittilä there was 38 centimetres of snow on Friday morning and in Posio 9 centimetres. Normally at this time of year there is about one centimetre of snow.

At Kilpisjärvi there was 58 centimetres of snow in the morning. That is half a metre more than normal.

In Southern Lapland the snow has already melted.
