## Law on clothes recycling

![Example pile of clothes suitable for separate collection of textiles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4945,h_2782,x_5,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1097486643515eba82fd/1681211193)

The goal is that by 2030 textiles sold in the EU area will be recyclable, repairable and durable. Illustrative photo. Photo: Antti Ullakko / Yle

The EU is planning a new law that concerns clothes.

The goal is that by 2030 only clothes that can be repaired and recycled will be sold in the EU area.

In the EU, 12 million tonnes of waste from clothes is produced every year. Only about 20 per cent of clothes are recycled. The rest of the clothes are burned or they go to landfill.

There is serious violence in Sudan.

The UN warns that a full civil war may begin in Sudan. The war may also cause unrest in the countries neighbouring Sudan in Africa.

The UN gave the warning after more than 20 people died and dozens were injured in an air strike, which was carried out near the capital Khartoum. UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres condemned the strike.

The fighting in Sudan began in mid-April.

## Water mould

![The original Saimaa landlocked salmon in the hands of a worker at a fish farm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4069,h_2286,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113969464a7ed80d97a3/1688727025)

Open image viewer

Saimaa landlocked salmon. Photo: Luke

The Saimaa landlocked salmon is in trouble.

A fungal disease is killing large numbers of Saimaa landlocked salmon. The fungal disease is called water mould.

Water mould kills fish both in nature and in fish farms. In a fish farm, water mould can quickly kill a large number of fish. Because of the disease, the Saimaa landlocked salmon is even threatened with extinction.

Water mould also threatens another Saimaa fish, the Saimaa char. However, the char withstands water mould better than the landlocked salmon.

On Monday the weather is dry all over the country. The daytime temperature is between 19 and 24 degrees.

The weather is mostly dry and sunny for the whole start of the week. In Lapland and Western Finland there may be a heat wave, that is, the temperature rises above 25 degrees.
