# Tuesday 3.9.2019 (radio)

Minister Sirpa Paatero has intervened in the situation at Posti. Storm Dorian has caused a lot of damage in the Bahamas, in America. Cancer has become the most common cause of death in rich countries. Finland is the world's best nature travel destination.

## Post disputes

![Sirpa Paatero](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10952254)

Open image viewer

Minister Sirpa Paatero supervises state companies. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Minister Sirpa Paatero has intervened in the situation at Posti. The state owns Posti. Paatero's job is to supervise state companies.

Paatero says that the working conditions of Posti's workers are still being considered.

Posti has intended to change the working conditions of about 700 workers. If the working conditions change as the managers want, the workers' pay will fall.

The workers do not accept this and went on strike on Monday, that is, they stopped doing their work. Minister Paatero wants Posti's managers and workers to negotiate and agree on the matter.

Minister Paatero also wants the pay of Posti's managers to fall. In Paatero's view the managers have been paid too much.

## Damage in the Bahamas

![Aerial picture of an airport flooded by flood water in the Bahamas.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10953267)

Open image viewer

Water covers large areas after storm Dorian. Photo: Hunter Medley / US Coast Guard

Storm Dorian has caused a lot of damage in the Bahamas, in America. The storm destroyed as many as 13,000 homes.

Nobody yet knows how many people have died. The Bahamas' prime minister said that at least 5 people died in the storm.

Now storm Dorian is moving towards the Florida coast. In Florida and also elsewhere in the USA, the authorities have urged hundreds of thousands of people to go to safety.

## The most common cause of death

![A man receiving chemotherapy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10952929)

Open image viewer

A cancer patient receives treatment. Photo: AOP

Cancer has become the most common cause of death in rich countries. In rich countries cancer kills twice as many people as heart and circulatory diseases.

The situation is different in poor countries. There, heart and circulatory diseases are still the most common cause of death.

There are more heart and circulatory diseases in poor countries because of poor health care. Poor food and air quality also increase the risk of heart and circulatory diseases.

## The best nature travel destination

![A bear in Kuhmo on 22 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10952553)

Open image viewer

Finland's nature is beautiful. Photo: Anni Reenpää / Lehtikuva

The best nature travel destination in the world is Finland. This is reported by Lonely Planet, which publishes travel guides.

The choice of the best nature travel destination was made by a British travel agency. It compared more than 100 countries.

Finland came first because of its beautiful nature and good nature conservation. Finland also got points for the fact that 73 per cent of Finland is covered by forest.

Number 2 in the comparison was Sweden and number 3 was Brazil.
