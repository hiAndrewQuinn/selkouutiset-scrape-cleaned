# Monday 13.1.2020 (radio)

Fiskars is cutting staff. Drugs are becoming more common in Finland. A record number of organ transplants were carried out last year. The Taal volcano is erupting in the Philippines. A big forest fire in Australia has been brought under control.

## Fiskars is cutting

![Fiskars Norden series kitchen knives at a presentation event in 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11102175)

Fiskars is cutting jobs.

Fiskars is cutting about 220 jobs in different parts of the world in total. In Finland the closure may threaten 60 jobs.

The cuts are meant to reduce costs by about 20 million euros a year.

Fiskars makes household goods and garden tools, for example.

## Drugs are becoming more common

![Used drug syringe](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1383,h_778,x_537,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6068025dbbdafdaa465/1572592449)

Drugs are still more common in Finland.

A new study by the National Institute for Health and Welfare says that more than half of Finns under 35 know someone who uses drugs. Over 30 per cent also say that they have sometimes been offered drugs to use or buy.

Trying and using drugs has steadily become more common in Finland. Last year about 260 people died from drugs in Finland, which is more than ever before.

## A record number of transplants

![A woman holding an organ donor card and a wallet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8368921)

Open image viewer

Many Finns have filled in an organ donor card. Photo: Tuomas Hirvonen / Yle

A record number of transplants were carried out in Finland last year.

The Finnish Kidney and Liver Association says that over 450 transplants were carried out last year in total. The most were kidney transplants, about 300. The second most were liver transplants, about 60.

There were about 140 organ donors in Finland last year. The average age of the donors was just over 54 years.

About 570 people are constantly waiting for a new organ.

## The Philippines volcano eruption

![Taal volcano erupting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11154663)

Open image viewer

The Taal volcano is erupting in the Philippines. Photo: Ted Aljibe / AFP

An eruption has started in the Philippines. The Taal volcano is spewing ash and lava into the air.

Already over 20,000 people have fled the eruption. If the eruption gets worse, perhaps hundreds of thousands of people may have to be evacuated from the area.

Taal is about 70 kilometres from the Philippine capital, Manila. Manila's international airport has sometimes had to be closed because of the ash cloud.

Taal last erupted in 1977.

## Australia's bushfires

![Bush fires in New South Wales.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11150263)

Open image viewer

Australia is still burning. Photo: Dean Lewins / EPA

A big forest fire in Australia has been brought under control.

A big forest fire has raged in a national park in the state of New South Wales for almost 3 months. The fire spread over a total of 800,000 hectares.

The fire chief says that the fire will continue in a small area still. However, it looks like it will be possible to contain it.

Dozens of other bushfires are still burning. The Australian Bureau of Meteorology says that some of the fire areas may get rain this week.
