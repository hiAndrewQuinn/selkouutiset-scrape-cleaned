# Tuesday 28.03.2017 (radio)

Bosses and co-workers affect how well a person feels at work. Young people are less interested in Master of Science in Engineering degrees than before. The arrival of spring slows down this week. Kouvola is buying Finland's first daycare bus.

## Enjoying work

![In the picture, a boss gives feedback to an employee.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6948407)

Open image viewer

If the boss is good, the employee enjoys work. Photo: Raila Paavola / Yle

Bosses and co-workers affect how well a person feels at work. A study says that the most important thing is support from the manager, that is, the boss. About 40 percent of those who answered say that they get support from their boss when they need it.

A person feels well at work if they have a chance to influence their own work. Then they cope better and are sick less.

An employee's trust in work and enthusiasm for it can be lower if they have been unemployed at some time.

The study was done by the Research Institute of the Finnish Economy and the Labour Institute for Economic Research.

## Engineering degree

![Lotta Wallinmaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9523963)

Open image viewer

Lotta Wallinmaa studies at Aalto University. Photo: Joni Tammela / Yle

Young people are less interested in Master of Science in Engineering degrees than before, even though there is work in the field and the pay is good. Last year there were about 20 percent fewer applicants than 4 years ago.

Lotta Wallinmaa studies chemical engineering at Aalto University. Wallinmaa thinks it is good that students can try things themselves in the laboratory. The final thesis is done at some company, Wallinmaa says. She believes that it helps to find a job.

Finland needs engineers. That is why it is good if young people get excited about mathematics, physics and chemistry already at school.

## The arrival of spring

![Swans.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9533629)

Open image viewer

Spring is coming, but slowly. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The arrival of spring slows down this week. Yle meteorologist Seija Paasonen says that the weather is getting colder and that is why the arrival of spring slows down.

Paasonen says that in Åland, thermal spring already began in February. Thermal spring means the time when the average temperature of the day rises permanently above zero.

In Southern Finland, thermal spring began about 2 weeks earlier than usual. In the rest of the country, thermal spring has not begun yet.

Seija Paasonen says that the next few days will be cold. There will be frost at night in the whole country. In the north there will be frost during the day too. In the south the temperature can be a little above zero during the day.

After Friday the weather gets warmer again.

## Daycare bus

![Daycare bus in Kouvola](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9532730)

Open image viewer

The daycare bus also has a toilet and a story corner. Photo: Petri Niemi / Yle

Kouvola plans to buy Finland's first daycare bus. With the daycare bus, children can for example go on trips.

Daycare children have already tested the bus. The bus has, for example, a small kitchen, a toilet and a place to dry clothes. The tables and benches can be made into beds.

The maker of the bus is a Finnish company. It has already sold daycare buses to Sweden and Norway.
