# Radio Clear News Saturday 22.7.2023

Heat in Southern Europe. Sweden's relations with Muslim countries. Cough medicine disaster in The Gambia. Demonstration in Helsinki. Car accident in Seinäjoki. Weather.

## Heat in Southern Europe

![Tourists protecting themselves from the heat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3543,h_1992,x_0,y_240/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114614464bb682c6ddf8/1690003735)

Tourists try to protect themselves from the sun in Greece. Photo: Bougiotis Evangelos / EPA-EFE

Southern Europe is suffering from the heat. There is especially intense heat in the Mediterranean area.

For example, in Greece the temperature may rise to 45 degrees at the weekend. Authorities say that people should stay at home. Many tourist sites are closed because of the heat.

It is hot and dry in Greece, and there are also forest fires. On the island of Ródos, about 50 Finnish tourists have been moved away from the forest fires to another place to stay.

## Sweden and Muslim countries

![Protesters demonstrate against Sweden in Iraq.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4141,h_2329,x_0,y_46/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114618664bb8ca799420/1690979392)

Open image viewer

Demonstration against Sweden in Iraq. Photo: Lehtikuva

Relations between Sweden and Muslim countries are now bad. The reason is that Qurans have been burned and torn up at demonstrations in Sweden. The Quran is the holy book of Muslims.

Muslims have protested against Sweden in Iraq, Iran and Lebanon. In Iraq the Swedish embassy was set on fire.

Around 60 Muslim countries around the world have together demanded that the Quran must be protected.

In Sweden, burning holy books is a question of freedom of speech. The police cannot prevent Qurans from being burned, because the judiciary considers it part of freedom of speech.

## Dangerous cough medicine

![The headquarters of the World Health Organization (WHO) is shown. Open image viewer

The World Health Organization says that the cough medicines contained dangerous substances. Photo: Alamy / All Over Press

In Africa, children have died in The Gambia because of cough medicines. At least 70 children have died.

The cough medicines were brought to The Gambia from India. The cough medicines contained substances that damage the kidneys. That is why the children died.

The World Health Organization has found substances in the cough medicines that are used in antifreeze.

## Protest against savings

![Protest on the steps of Parliament House in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4416,h_2484,x_0,y_460/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114627664bbd7e88f235/1690035254)

A demonstration was held on the steps of Parliament House in Helsinki. The protesters protested against the government's savings plans.

In the protesters' view, the government's savings plans cause problems. The protesters say that people with long-term illnesses, disabled people and poor people will suffer from the savings.

## Accident in Seinäjoki

![Accident site on Ruukintie in Seinäjoki, 22 July 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_101/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114623964bbb16abd178/1690022279)

Accident site on Ruukintie in Seinäjoki. Photo: Timo Aalto / Lehtikuva

A serious car accident has happened in Seinäjoki in Finland. 2 people died in the accident.

The car drove off the road and hit a tree early on Saturday morning. The car was completely destroyed in the crash.

The driver of the car was driving very fast at the time of the accident.

Rain continues in some parts of Finland. The rain area is in the north on Sunday and in the middle of the country on Monday.

North of the rain area it is cool. The temperature is about 15 degrees.

In the south the sun may shine and the weather is warmer. The temperature is about 20 degrees.
