Jari Aarnio's conviction was overturned. Pensioners are doing well. Distribution of home tests to schools begins. The Minister for Family and Basic Services changed. Russia and China criticise NATO. Weather forecast.

## Jari Aarnio's murder conviction overturned

![Jari Aarnio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4301,h_2419,x_14,y_275/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-844960612346e565833/1629711494)

Open image viewer

Jari Aarnio. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The Helsinki Court of Appeal has overturned the life sentence of Jari Aarnio, former head of the Helsinki drug police. Earlier, the lower court, the district court, sentenced Aarnio to life imprisonment. Now the Court of Appeal has removed this life sentence.

The Court of Appeal says that Aarnio did not have a special duty to prevent the contract murder that happened in 2003. The Court of Appeal says that Jari Aarnio did not know that the victim was in mortal danger.

The Court of Appeal's decision means that Jari Aarnio can get out of prison.

## Pensioners

![Two people watching television.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1717,h_966,x_79,y_105/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90365461e7d11ae23d6/1642582464)

Open image viewer

Pensioners are doing well. Many would like to work a little. Photo: Ville Välimäki / Yle

Most pensioners are doing well. The pension company Ilmarinen has asked pensioners how they feel about their own well-being. 70 per cent of working-age pensioners feel that their own health and well-being is good.

Over 60 per cent of respondents say they could even go to work. Many pensioners would also like to work, for example, 2 days a week.

Over 40 per cent of working-age pensioners have sometimes done some work in retirement.

## Home tests for schoolchildren

![Corona home testing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_159/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90215761e18164638d4/1642169133)

Open image viewer

Corona home test. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

The distribution of corona home tests to schools will start soon. The National Emergency Supply Agency is distributing tests to municipalities and joint municipal authorities. They will distribute the tests to schools and to upper secondary school students.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare recommends that each pupil be given 5 corona home tests.

## Change of Minister for Family and Basic Services

![Aki Lindén at a press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_246/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90752761f2b75c7044b/1643300157)

Open image viewer

Aki Lindén. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The government's Minister for Family and Basic Services has changed. Minister Krista Kiuru went on parental leave on Friday. Kiuru is expecting a baby.

MP Aki Lindén became the Minister for Family and Basic Services in place of Krista Kiuru. Aki Lindén is an SDP MP in his first term. He is from the Varsinais-Suomi electoral district.

Krista Kiuru will return as minister again in September.

## Russia and China

![Olympic rings in the stadium at the opening ceremony of the Beijing Olympics.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_278/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91073061fd2d11add1e/1643982410)

At the Beijing Winter Olympics there are no spectators, and many countries' leaders are not there. Photo: Andreas Hillegren / Lehtikuva

Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping have met in Beijing. They signed a joint statement.

Russia and China say that they oppose the expansion of the NATO military alliance. Russia and China also criticise the role of the USA in Europe and in the Asia-Pacific region.

The Winter Olympics in Beijing were opened on Friday. Because of the corona situation, there are no spectators at the Winter Olympics, and the leaders of many countries will not travel to watch the Winter Olympics.
