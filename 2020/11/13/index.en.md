# Friday 13.11.2020 (radio)

4 new coronavirus deaths have been reported in Finland. Youth work gets more money because of the coronavirus crisis. In the Helsinki area it is advisable to avoid holding large celebrations. Coronavirus infections are growing around the world. It is difficult to find new workers for many sectors.

## Finland's coronavirus figures

4 new coronavirus deaths have been reported in Finland. THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, reported this on Friday. A total of 369 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

THL also said that there are 316 new coronavirus infections in Finland. Most new coronavirus infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa areas.

Almost 19,000 coronavirus infections have so far been found in Finland.

## Money for youth work

![Two boys look at a computer screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_84/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6519325e67d463a96a2/1583864089)

Open image viewer

Young people are to be helped with schooling in the difficult coronavirus times. Photo: Yle/Hedvig Sandell

Youth work is getting more money because of the coronavirus crisis. The regional state administrative agencies are giving more than 10 million euros to youth work across Finland.

With the extra money, youth workers will be hired for schools and educational institutions. Youth workers support children's and young people's schooling and learning.

The aim is that children and young people do not become marginalised because of the coronavirus crisis. It is important that schooling and studies continue as normally as possible.

## Guidance on private events

![Party refreshments on a tray.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_419/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6880735ee0c3d4b1302/1591788795)

Open image viewer

In the Helsinki area you may not invite more than 10 guests home now. Photo: Pasi Takkunen / Yle

In the Helsinki area it is not good to organise large private events because of the coronavirus.

The new guidance is that there may be at most 10 people at private events. That is why, for example, large parties should not be held at home.

The new guidance has been given because the coronavirus spreads easily at private events.

## Coronavirus infections around the world

![Sign announcing a face mask requirement on a pedestrian street in Delmenhorst, Germany.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11609553)

Open image viewer

The number of coronavirus infections has grown in Germany. Photo: Focke Strangmann / EPA

The number of coronavirus infections is growing around the world. For example, Germany and Russia reported record-high coronavirus figures on Friday.

Germany reported on Friday about 220 new coronavirus deaths and almost 24,000 new coronavirus infections.

Russia reported more than 400 coronavirus deaths and almost 22,000 new coronavirus infections.

So in Germany and Russia more coronavirus infections were reported in a single day than in Finland during the whole coronavirus era.

## Shortage of workers

![Security guard in front of their own shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3776,h_2124,x_0,y_12/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7410565fac3c6cdfd90/1605123240)

Open image viewer

There is still enough work for security guards in the coronavirus era. Photo: Markku Sandell / Yle

It is difficult to find new workers for many sectors. For example, more security guards are needed.

The coronavirus crisis has increased the number of unemployed, but not in all occupations. For example, security guards are needed during the coronavirus crisis too.

Security jobs are usually open for a long time. New workers do not come to the sector quickly, because security guards need good training.

More warehouse workers, as well as social and health care workers, are also needed, for example.
