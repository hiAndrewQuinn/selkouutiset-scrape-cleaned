# Saturday 20.4.2019 (radio)

Many MPs are also candidates in the EU elections. Researchers have started making new nutrition recommendations for the 2020s. Sea and lake ice is weakening quickly. Next week is warm.

## MPs also in the EU elections

Many MPs are also candidates in the EU elections, even though they were elected to Parliament. This also causes opposition, because there is only a little over a month between the parliamentary elections and the EU elections.

Researcher Kimmo Elo says that it is important to understand what a candidate is being voted in for.

Standing as a candidate cannot be restricted, because it is a constitutional right. Kimmo Elo says, however, that the Finnish Parliament can change the electoral system if it wants to.

The EU elections are held on 26 May.

## Nutrition recommendations for the 2020s are being made

![Lamb roast and vegetables on a plate.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10746948)

Open image viewer

The recommendations say that it is worth reducing the eating of red meat. Photo: AOP

Researchers have started making new food recommendations for the 2020s. The work is done together with the other Nordic countries.

The first nutrition recommendations, that is, food circles, came to Finland 70 years ago. The model came from America.

The new model recommends, among other things, fruit, vegetables and wholegrain cereals. The model advises reducing the use of red meat, sugar and salt. Fish, poultry and plant protein are recommended.

The new recommendations emphasise food that is sustainable. Food must not burden the environment too much.

## Ice is dangerously weak

![Melting ice surface at Vierumäki in Heinola on 29 March. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10737639)

Open image viewer

Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Sea and lake ice is weakening quickly also in the north.

At the moment there is still fast ice north of Vaasa. Fast ice is ice that does not move in the water but stays in place. In the Vaasa archipelago the ice is already weak.

Ice on the shores of the Bothnian Bay and Ostrobothnia is also weakening. Then even thick ice can give way.

April has been warmer than average. There is much less ice in sea areas than last spring.

The Finnish Swimming Instruction and Lifesaving Association says that last year 16 people fell through the ice and drowned. This year 3 people have drowned in the ice.

## Next week is warm

The wind strengthens on Sunday. Rain passes over Northern Finland. The temperature is about 10 degrees.

In southern Finland it is fairly sunny. The temperature can rise to 15 degrees.

On the second day of Easter, that is Monday, in southern and central Finland it is fairly sunny. In northern Finland it is cloudier.

Yle meteorologist Kerttu Kotakorpi forecasts that next week will be warm and sunny. The temperature can be close to 20 degrees.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute and rescue services warn about dry terrain. The risk of grass fires is high. The drought can continue for many days.
