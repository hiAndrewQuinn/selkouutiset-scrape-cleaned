## Corona infections in Finland

![Unmasked young adults at the Tikkurila festival.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4429,h_2491,x_0,y_187/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83526460fd5ce9b09d1/1627217569)

The number of corona infections is growing in Finland.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare said on Saturday that there are almost 800 new corona infections in Finland.

Many new corona infections have been found among young people. In the last 2 weeks about 30 percent of the new corona infections have been found among 20 to 29-year-olds.

Most new corona infections are in the Helsinki region. There are also many corona infections in the Tampere region and the Turku region.

## Corona test sites in Helsinki

![People queue standing for a corona test at the Terveystalo test point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12012906)

Open image viewer

There has been a crowd for corona tests this summer. Photo: Marjukka Talvitie / Yle

New corona test sites are being opened in Helsinki. The new corona test sites will be at Hartwall Arena and in Kamppi. They will open next week.

New corona test sites are needed, because there is a crowd for corona tests. In Helsinki you can now get a corona test time about 20 hours ahead.

The waiting time will get shorter when the new test sites open and the summer holidays of healthcare staff end.

## Trips to Estonia

![Corona certificate with vaccination details, printed from the Omakanta service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_219/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83156060ee7cf4d5328/1626242527)

Open image viewer

You get a corona certificate from the Omakanta service, whose address is kanta.fi. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Estonia now requires a corona certificate from travellers who come from Finland. The reason is that the number of corona infections has grown in Finland.

The corona certificate shows that the traveller has corona vaccinations, or a negative corona test result, or has had corona disease.

If a traveller does not have a corona certificate, a corona test and quarantine await them in Estonia.

## The price of coffee

![A worker collects coffee beans.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_630/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8372366104063a350e8/1627653910)

Open image viewer

Brazil produces more coffee than any other country. Photo: Igor do Vale / AOP

The price of raw coffee has risen.

Coffee that is sold in shops is made by roasting raw coffee. That is why the price of coffee may soon rise in shops too.

The price of raw coffee has risen because of bad weather. For example, Brazil may lose up to 15 percent of its coffee harvest, because it has been dry in the country and there has even been frost on the coffee plantations. The news is bad, because Brazil is the world's biggest coffee-producing country.

The price of coffee interests Finns, because Finns drink the most coffee in the world. Finnish shops often try to get customers by selling coffee at a special offer price.
