# Saturday 28.10.2017 (radio)

Whooper swans had only a few young this summer. The clocks will be turned to winter time tonight. Tuomas Kyrö is the new plain-language book sponsor. Spain is dismantling Catalonia's self-government.

## Whooper swans had very few young

![swans swimming](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7534160)

Open image viewer

Whooper swans have only a few young this year. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

The whooper swans' nesting seems to have failed this year. Birdwatchers in Northern Finland say that only a few young can be seen in the flocks of swans. Most of the birds are adult swans.

For example, birdwatcher Pirkka Aalto saw a big flock of whooper swans in Kemijärvi at the beginning of October. He counted that the flock had more than a thousand adult birds and only about 50 young. The young are easy to tell apart, because they are grey. Adult swans are bright white.

Birdwatchers say that the swans' nesting may have failed because of the cold and rainy summer.

Birdwatchers in different parts of the country are spotting swans this weekend. The aim is to find out how many swans there are in Finland now.

## The clocks will be turned tonight

![Clocks are turned to winter time](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9258269)

Open image viewer

We move to winter time by turning the clock back by one hour. Photo: Yle News Graphics

Finland moves to winter time tonight.

The clocks are moved back by one hour at four in the morning on the night between Saturday and Sunday.

Winter time is Finland's normal time. But in summer, summer time is used, because the EU countries have agreed so.

## Tuomas Kyrö becomes plain-language book sponsor

![A woman and a man sit on a stage, both holding microphones.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9906254)

Open image viewer

Leealaura Leskelä from the Plain Language Centre interviewed the new plain-language book sponsor, author Tuomas Kyrö, at the Helsinki Book Fair. Photo: Kaisa Kaatra/Selkokeskus.

Author Tuomas Kyrö is the new plain-language book sponsor. He was challenged to take on the role by the previous sponsor, author Eppu Nuotio.

Being the plain-language book sponsor means that Tuomas Kyrö's popular book Mielensäpahoittaja (The Grump) will be adapted into plain language. The book adapted into plain Finnish will be published in 2 years at the Helsinki Book Fair.

![Two women sit behind a table. A couple of books on the table in front.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9906267)

Open image viewer

Johanna Kartio has adapted author Eppu Nuotio's book Peiton paikka into plain language. Photo: Kaisa Kaatra/Selkokeskus

Of the books by the previous sponsor, Eppu Nuotio, Peiton paikka was adapted into plain language. The plain-language adaptation of the book was made by Johanna Kartio. The book has now been published at the Helsinki Book Fair.

A plain-language book sponsor's term is 2 years. The idea of the sponsorship is to make plain-language literature better known and to offer plain-language readers the same popular books that other people also read.

## Spain is dismantling Catalonia's self-government

![Spanish Prime Minister Mariano Rajoy](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9905762)

Open image viewer

Spanish Prime Minister Mariano Rajoy ordered that a new election will be held in Catalonia in December. Photo: Juanjo Martin / EPA

Spain has started to dismantle Catalonia's self-government.

Catalonia declared itself independent on Friday. Spain says that the declaration of independence is against the constitution.

Spain has dissolved Catalonia's regional parliament and regional government and ordered that a new election will be held in December. Spain has also dismissed the head of Catalonia's regional police and dozens of officials.

Spain's Deputy Prime Minister has been appointed to lead Catalonia.
