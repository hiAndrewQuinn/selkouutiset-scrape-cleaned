# Thursday 29.9.2016 (radio)

The price of food has gone down for 2 years already. Fewer text messages are sent than before. The fitness of Finnish schoolchildren is tested. At the Turku Book Fair, you can also get to know easy-to-read books. People in Kuopio fell in love with an Iraqi pie.

## The price of food

![Food in a birch-bark basket.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5291730)

Open image viewer

Food is now cheaper than 2 years ago. Photo: Yle

The price of food has gone down. The prices of food products have gone down for 2 years already.

PTT, that is, Pellervo Economic Research, believes that the fall in food prices will start to slow down. Some food products may even become more expensive. For example, the price of meat and milk has already risen a little elsewhere in Europe, PTT says.

But some food prices may still go down. For example, grain products may become cheaper, PTT believes.

## Text messages are decreasing

![Text message](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8941443)

Open image viewer

More text messages were sent before than now. Photo: Yle

Finns send fewer text messages than before.

New services have come to replace text messages. For example, SnapChat and WhatsApp are popular. Many think they are easy and quick to use, and they are free.

Experts say that sending text messages will not stop completely. Text messages are reliable. You can also use them when the internet does not work.

In addition, companies use text messages a lot. They need only the customer's phone number, and the message gets through.

## Fitness tests for schoolchildren

![Boys in the middle of a ball game at the Hämeenkyrö village school](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8592137)

Open image viewer

The Move project encourages schoolchildren to exercise. Photo: Kaisu Jansson / Yle

The fitness of Finnish schoolchildren is tested. In the Move project, the physical fitness of 5th and 8th grade students is tested.

The head of the project, Minna Paajanen, says that the test results are collected and kept. That way, information is gained on how fit Finnish children are.

The Move project also encourages schoolchildren to exercise. Schoolchildren must learn to take responsibility for their own fitness themselves.

## Easy-to-read books in Turku

![New easy-to-read books from 2015.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8398377)

Open image viewer

Many books have been published in easy-to-read language. Photo: Kaisa Kaatra / Selkokeskus

There is a book fair in Turku at the weekend. At the book fair, you can also get to know books in easy-to-read language. They are written in easy Finnish.

The programme of the Turku Book Fair includes, for example, a discussion about what happens when a book is adapted into easy-to-read language. Authors Eppu Nuotio and Johanna Kartio discuss this topic at the book fair on Sunday. Kartio has written an easy-to-read version of Nuotio's book Peiton paikka.

## An Iraqi treat in Kuopio

![Ahmed and Mehdi, Iraqi men, pie](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9200387)

Open image viewer

Ahmed and Mehdi bake pies for the people of Kuopio. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

And one more news item from Kuopio. People in Kuopio have a new treat, an Iraqi pie.

Iraqi meat pies and vegetable pies are sold in the Kuopio market hall. People in Kuopio like the Iraqi pie really a lot.

The makers of the pies are Ahmed and Mehdi. They are Iraqi asylum seekers. Baking brings variety to the men's lives.

You can read more about Ahmed and Mehdi on the Yle News website. The address is yle.fi/uutiset.
