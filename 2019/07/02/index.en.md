# Tuesday 2.7.2019 (radio)

Student flats are in great demand now. Fraudulent messages have been sent in Kela's name. May was the busiest month for mortgages since June 2012. The race for the Centre Party chair is becoming tight. The route of the Finnish victims of the avalanche in Northern Norway has been revealed.

## Student flats are scarce again

![Availability of student flats in some towns](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10856513)

Open image viewer

By far the most applications go to the Helsinki Student Housing Foundation (HOAS). Thousands of applications also go to the Tampere Student Housing Foundation (TOAS) and the Turku Student Union Housing Foundation (TYS). There are hundreds of applications for the student flats in Mikkeli (MOAS) and the Vaasa Student Housing Foundation (VOAS). Photo: Marko Melto / Yle

Many people are now looking for student flats, because information about the results of the joint application was received last week. Applications are flooding the housing foundations in different parts of Finland.

In Tampere about 4,000 applications will come in over the summer. In Helsinki a vacant flat is found for only about 20 percent of applicants.

The situation is easier in small towns.

The most wanted are one-room flats. However, in many towns there are mostly shared flats.

## Fraudulent messages have been sent in Kela's name

![Kela logo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10497406)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Kela warns that fraudulent messages have been sent in its name.

The fraudulent messages have come by email and text message. The fraudulent messages offer quick payday loans.

Kela says that this is phishing. It aims to gather personal information and bank codes.

Kela never asks for these by email, text message or phone.

## Many mortgages have been granted

![Keys to an apartment on a table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10810785)

Open image viewer

Photo: Kimmo Hiltunen / Yle

New mortgages worth 1.9 billion euros were granted in May. The Bank of Finland says that the sum was 130 million euros bigger than a year earlier.

Unsecured consumer credit of 290 million euros was taken out. That is more than ever before in a single month.

May was the busiest month for mortgages since June 2012.

## The race for the Centre Party chair is tight

![Statistical graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10853759)

Open image viewer

Photo: Harri Vähäkangas / Yle

The race for the Centre Party chair is becoming tight, according to a Yle survey. Yle sent the survey to Centre Party MPs and members of the party council.

Minister of Defence Antti Kaikkonen and Minister of Economic Affairs Katri Kulmuni are almost level. 29 percent of respondents support Kaikkonen and 27 percent support Kulmuni.

17 percent have not yet made up their minds.

The Centre Party chair will be chosen at an extraordinary party congress in September.

## The route of the Norway avalanche victims is known

![Police officers and Red Cross volunteers taking part in the search at Tamokdalen in Norway in January 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10857796)

Location data from the victim's sports watch revealed that the skiers' route ran in very steep terrain in places. Photo: Rune Stoltz Bertinussen / AFP

The route of the Finnish victims of the avalanche in Northern Norway is known. Last winter a group in Norway died, which included 3 Finns and 1 Swede.

The Norwegian report says that the group was moving in the mountains on a route that was different from the normal one. At the same time the group set off an avalanche.

The victims were buried under the snow mass. The avalanche carried them at a speed of over 70 kilometres an hour.

Researchers worked out the skiers' route using the location tracker of the sports watch.

The last missing person has still not been able to be searched for. Last week there was as much as 4 metres of snow in the area.

In this decade 64 people have died in avalanches in Norway. Most of the dead had been practising off-piste skiing in the mountains.
