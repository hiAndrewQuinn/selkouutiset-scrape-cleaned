# Saturday 10.3.2018 (radio)

The parties want better family leave. The police hope for more posts. Pictures help people understand speech. More and more metals are needed for the batteries of electric cars and solar panels.

## The parties want better family leave

![A mother and a newborn baby in hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10110758)

Open image viewer

A mother and baby in hospital. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

All the parties want to improve family leave.

Really few babies are born in Finland now. Last year as few babies were born as 150 years ago. All the parties think that this is a problem. In the future, Finland will have too few taxpayers.

Yle asked the parties what should be done about it. All the parties want to improve family leave. The parties want to pay mothers and fathers compensation so that they can afford to be away from work, at home looking after small children. Many parties also want to improve services for children. For example, they want to improve early childhood education, that is, teaching for 5- and 6-year-olds in day care centres.

## The police hope for more police officers

![Police officers at Narinkkatori in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10100309)

Open image viewer

Police cars in a line in Helsinki. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The police hope for more posts.

The number of police officers has been cut in recent years, and there are now about 7,200 of them. Police chief Seppo Kolehmainen says that Finland needs about 600 more police officers. Kolehmainen says that, for example, the new alcohol law and international terrorism add to the police's work.

The police officers' trade union says that it is a good idea to train more police officers.

## Pictures help people understand

![Crafts instructor Marja Hietanen shows an old photograph to elderly people sitting at a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10103788)

Pictures are sometimes used as an aid to speech.

Pictures help people understand speech and remember things better. They also help a child when the child is learning to talk. Pictures are also important in reception centres. Asylum seekers do not know Finnish straight away. Many of them cannot read and write. Then it helps when you can show a picture.

On the internet there is a website called Papunet.net, where you can download pictures to your own phone or computer.

## Metals are being searched for in the rock archive

![a man in a storehouse with rocks](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10111145)

Open image viewer

Ari Hevonkoski examines rock samples. Photo: Yle

A lot of batteries are used in the world today.

Batteries are needed, for example, for electric cars and solar panels. Batteries need a lot of metals: nickel, copper, cobalt and lithium. Metals are also being searched for in Finland. Many foreign mining firms want to study rock cores that have been drilled from Finland's bedrock. The rock samples are kept in the state rock archive, which is ten storage buildings full of rock samples in the municipality of Loppi.

If metals are found in a rock core, it may be worth opening a mine where the sample was drilled.
