## National Coalition Party most popular in municipal elections

![Petteri Orpo rejoiced at the National Coalition Party's success in the municipal elections on 13 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1980,h_1114,x_0,y_142/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82035760c668cce7b62/1623615861)

Today, on Monday, people in Finland have been talking about the results of the municipal elections.

The most popular party in the municipal elections was the National Coalition Party. The National Coalition Party is the largest party in big cities, for example in Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa and Turku.

In Tampere the National Coalition Party gained one more seat on the council, and it is now as big a party in Tampere as the SDP. In Jyväskylä too, the National Coalition Party and the SDP won the same number of seats.

In the whole of Finland the support for the National Coalition Party was 21.5 percent.

## SDP second most popular

The second most popular party in the municipal elections was the SDP. The SDP is the most popular in Tampere, for example, but the difference to the National Coalition Party is so small that the SDP and the National Coalition Party won the same number of seats on the Tampere council.

The SDP is also the most popular in Jyväskylä, but the SDP and the National Coalition Party won the same number of seats on the Jyväskylä council.

The SDP is the largest party in Kotka, Joensuu, Jämsä, Äänekoski and Heinola, for example.

In the whole of Finland, the support for the SDP was 17.5 percent. The SDP's election result was worse than in the previous municipal elections.

## Finns Party gained the most

![Finns Party chairperson Jussi Halla-Aho.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5997,h_3373,x_0,y_469/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82046860c6811c30627/1624267239)

Finns Party chairperson Jussi Halla-aho. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

In the municipal elections the Finns Party increased its support the most. The Finns Party gained more council seats in many municipalities.

The Finns Party gained 3 seats in Helsinki, 5 in Vantaa, 6 in Tampere and 4 in Turku, for example.

In the whole of Finland the support for the Finns Party was 14.5 percent. The Finns Party's support rose by 6 percentage points from the previous municipal elections. Many people expected that the support of the Finns Party would rise more.

## Centre has the most seats

![Annika Saarikko arrives at Pasila at Yle's Studio building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4176,h_2349,x_0,y_261/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82008060c642c90a608/1623606003)

Centre chairperson Annika Saarikko is also Minister of Finance. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

The National Coalition Party and the Finns Party won the most new seats in the municipal councils.

However, the Centre has the most council seats. The Centre is the largest party in Oulu, Kokkola and Kuopio, for example, and in very many small municipalities.

The support of the Centre in the whole of Finland was 15 percent. The support of the Centre fell a little from the previous municipal elections.

## Voter turnout was low

![Two early voters in voting booths.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5744,h_3231,x_0,y_332/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81752160bf412c132b0/1623147192)

In the municipal elections the support for the SDP, the Left Alliance and the Greens fell. They won fewer seats in many municipal councils than before.

The voter turnout in the municipal elections was quite low. About 55 percent of those entitled to vote went to vote.
