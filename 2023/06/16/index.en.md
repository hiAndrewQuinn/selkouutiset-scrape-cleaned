## Government programme

Finland is getting a new government. The programme of the new government was presented on Friday.

Four parties will be in the government: the National Coalition, the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats.

The party leaders of these parties said that the government wants to get Finland's economy into shape. That is why there are many savings in the government programme.

For example, housing benefit will be reduced. However, housing benefit for pensioners will not be reduced.

In addition, the government programme tightens immigration. For example, the conditions for permanent residence permits and Finnish citizenship will be tightened.

The government promises more money, especially for schools. The government wants to support children's learning.

More money will also go to the police. The aim is that more police officers will be hired.

Petteri Orpo will become Finland's next prime minister. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The new government will have 19 ministers. That is the same number as in the previous government of Sanna Marin.

Petteri Orpo will become prime minister. Orpo is the chair of the National Coalition.

The National Coalition won the elections this spring, and it became the largest party in Parliament. That is why Orpo got the job of forming a new government.

Orpo has a master's degree in political science. He has previously held the posts of Finland's finance minister and interior minister, for example.

Orpo is 53 years old. He has a home in Turku. Petteri Orpo has a wife, 2 children and 2 dogs.

## Bus accident in Canada

In Canada there has been a serious bus accident. At least 15 people died in the accident. 10 people were taken to hospital.

The accident happened when a bus and a lorry crashed into each other. The bus caught fire in the collision.

Canadian authorities say that most of the bus's passengers were elderly people.

It is hot in Finland now. On Friday the temperature rose close to 30 degrees in the south.

Hot weather is a strain on many people's health. Small children and elderly people in particular can suffer from the heat.

It is important to remember that in hot weather you must drink and eat enough.
