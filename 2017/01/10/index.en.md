## Posti moves into the care sector

![An elderly person drinks coffee. A glass of juice and a plate of sandwiches are also on the table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7378591)

Posti plans to take care services to places where they might otherwise disappear Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Posti is starting to work in the care sector too. Posti has bought a company called HR Hoiva, which offers home care services in 30 towns.

HR Hoiva's customers have been municipalities and private people.  The company has 190 employees.

Petri Kokkonen, the head of Posti's home services, says that in the future Posti will buy more care companies. In 10 years, more than 1000 carers will already be working in Posti's care services, Kokkonen says.

## The flu season is starting to ease

![A red-cheeked girl with a thermometer and a soft toy under her arms.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8621460)

Open image viewer

There have been many flu patients this winter. Photo: Päivi Meritähti / Yle

The peak of the flu season in Finland has  been reached. Although people in different parts of the country can still get the flu, the disease is slowly starting to ease.

This winter there have been many flu patients. Many people got a vaccine against the flu in the autumn, but the vaccine has not worked very well.

Influenza is a worse illness than an ordinary cold. Flu patients have, for example, a high fever.

## Students are not fit enough for work placements

![Waiter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6850023)

Open image viewer

Some waiter students do not have the strength to carry enough dishes. Photo: Yle

Vocational colleges are worried about students' poor fitness.

Teachers say that some young people are in such poor condition that they cannot even manage a work placement. The work placement is too hard for them.

Teachers think that one reason for the collapse in young people's fitness is a lack of everyday exercise. For example, young people do not even walk short distances, but go everywhere by car or moped.

However, there are big differences between young people. Some young people hardly move at all, while others exercise actively and are in good physical condition.

## Millions of people die from smoking every year

![Ash is tapped from a cigarette into an ashtray.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8921035)

Open image viewer

Low- and middle-income countries suffer the most from the harms of smoking. Photo: Seppo Sarkkinen / Yle

Soon 8 million people a year will die from smoking. The World Health Organization WHO and the US National Cancer Institute have done a study. According to it, the number of tobacco deaths will grow from 6 million to 8 million a year by 2030.

Low- and middle-income countries suffer the most from the harms of smoking. 80 percent of the people who smoke live in these countries.

The study says that the harms of smoking cost health care more than 1000 billion euros a year.
