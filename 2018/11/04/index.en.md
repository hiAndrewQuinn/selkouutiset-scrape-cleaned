# Sunday 4.11.2018 (radio)

There are more people in Finland with no income than before. More people have died in Italy because of the storm and flood waters. Finn Joona Sotala is the new world champion in e-sports. Married couples can improve their sex lives by talking and trying new things.

## People with no income

![Coins in a hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10111162)

Open image viewer

Many people have no income at all. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There are more people with zero income than before. Zero-income people have no income from which taxes are paid.

Last year there were about 160,000 zero-income people. Most of them are foreigners who, for example, have studied in Finland.

Many Finns are also zero-income. Many of them live on support money, from which no tax is paid. Some live on their parents' or partner's money.

## Floods in Sicily

![Damage caused by a flood on a street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10492033)

Open image viewer

The flood water left the streets in poor condition. Photo: Alessandro Fucarini / AFP

In Italy more people have died because of the storm and the flood waters.

At the weekend at least 12 people died on the island of Sicily. 9 of them died in a house that was under flood water. Children are among the dead. The water came into the house from a river whose water level rose quickly.

In Italy many people have died this week because of the storm. About 30 have died. In Italy there has been strong wind and rain all week.

## World champion in e-sports

![Joona Sotala](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10492098)

Open image viewer

World champion Joona Sotala. Photo: ESL

Finns continue to succeed in e-sports. Finn Joona Sotala is the new world champion in the video game Starcraft 2.

E-sports means electronic sports, which are played on a computer or game console. Starcraft 2 is a strategy game.

Joona Sotala took part in competitions in the USA. Sotala won the final match against a South Korean player.

Sotala's win is historic. He is the first world champion in the series who is not Korean.

## Sex in a relationship

![A man and a woman enjoying each other's company.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10491515)

Open image viewer

A couple from Kuopio appeared at the Helsinki sex fair. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There was a sex fair in Helsinki at the weekend.

A couple from Kuopio in Eastern Finland also appeared at the fair. The couple lead an ordinary family life, but also film erotic entertainment for adults.

A Yle journalist asked the couple how other married couples can improve their sex lives.

The couple's most important advice is talking. A partner cannot read minds. Sex is hard to talk about, but it is good to learn how. If partners do not talk, they do not know what the other wants.

The couple also advises other couples to try new things. In a long relationship sex is often the same. That can reduce the joy in a relationship. Partners have the right to try different things that they both like. They do not need to listen to other people's opinions.
