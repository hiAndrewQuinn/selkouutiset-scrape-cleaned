## Verdict in the Turku stabbing case

![Sea of candles and flowers reminds of the victims of the Turku stabbing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9790533)

Open image viewer

A sea of candles and flowers reminded people of the Turku stabbing victims last August. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

The court has sentenced a Moroccan man to prison for two murders committed with terrorist intent and for eight attempted murders.

The man stabbed people last August in central Turku. Two people died and eight were injured.

The verdict means that the court considers the stabbings to have been terrorist acts. The convicted man will be taken to Turku prison to serve his sentence.

## Ramadan has ended

![In Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, a woman had dressed her daughter in new festive clothes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10256837)

Open image viewer

In Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, a woman had dressed her daughter in new festive clothes. Photo: Ahmed Yosri / EPA

Muslims have been celebrating yesterday and today the end of the month of fasting, Ramadan.

During the past month, most Muslims fasted. They only drank and ate between sunset and sunrise. Prayer and quiet reflection are also part of the fast.

In the last days of the fast, Muslims prepare to celebrate Eid al-Fitr. The celebrations can last several days.

## Animals are treated badly

![A dog leans against its owner's legs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10256243)

Open image viewer

Authorities have to step in to deal with the treatment of animals more and more often. Photo: Marton Monus / EPA

People's wellbeing has got worse, and because of this, pets suffer too. Some people treat animals badly. Authorities see cases where the treatment is already really harsh.

Bad treatment of animals is caused, for example, by social problems and lack of care. Authorities made many inspection visits last year. During the visits, the condition of pets is checked.

A pet can be bought very easily, for example online. If problems arise with an animal, it is worth asking for help early.

## Young women are harassed

![woman being harassed](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9933489)

Open image viewer

Sexual harassment is common. Photo: AOP

38 percent of women and 17 percent of men have experienced sexual harassment in the last two years. Young women in particular are sexually harassed.

More than 50 percent of women under 35 have experienced harassment in the last two years. The most common forms of harassment are crude talk and inappropriate comments about the body and sexuality.

A new study says that women mostly face harassment from men, but men face harassment from both women and men.
