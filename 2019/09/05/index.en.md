# Thursday 5.9.2019 (radio)

Three people died in a car crash in Rauma. Veikkaus is cutting the number of slot machines. Support for the Finns Party has risen. Many government agencies have taken English names that don't tell people anything.

## Car crash in Rauma

![Scene of the fatal crash in Rauma on 5 September 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10957483)

Rauma crash site. Photo: Katja Halinen / Yle

There has been a bad car crash in Rauma, in western Finland. 3 people died in the crash.

The crash happened late on Wednesday evening. The people who died were young men.

The police say the car was driving too fast. It left the road, hit a ditch, crashed into trees and caught fire.

People came to the crash site, but they could not help the people in the car.

## Veikkaus removes slot machines

![Veikkaus slot machine](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10393569)

Open image viewer

There are many slot machines in Finland. Photo: Retu Liikanen / Yle

Gambling company Veikkaus is changing how it works. Veikkaus is a state-owned company.

Veikkaus runs Lotto, Fintoto and slot machines, for example. Its job is also to reduce the harms of gambling.

Veikkaus said on Thursday that it will cut slot machines, for example. About 3,000 slot machines will be removed.

In Finland, people have criticised the fact that there are many slot machines, and in many places, for example in shops. Slot machines cause addiction, so it is hard for many people to stop gambling.

After the changes, Veikkaus will collect less money for the state than before.

## Finns Party takes support from the Centre

![Jussi Halla-aho](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10956864)

Open image viewer

Jussi Halla-aho, leader of the Finns Party. Photo: Minna Raitavuo / Lehtikuva

Support for the Finns Party has risen.

In Yle's new poll, the Finns Party is the largest party. The Finns Party has taken support especially from the Centre. The Finns Party's support is now over 20 percent.

The National Coalition has the second most support, just over 17 percent. The Finns Party and the National Coalition are opposition parties, so they are not in the government.

The biggest government party, the SDP, is third in the poll. The SDP's support is about 17 percent. The SDP is the party of Prime Minister Antti Rinne.

The governing Centre party has lost the most support. The Centre's support is under 12 percent.

The figures for all parties can be found at yle.fi.

## No-harm Center?

![Screenshot from the website of Vaasa Central Hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10954488)

Open image viewer

No-harm Center looks after patient safety. Photo: Mirva Ekman / Yle

Finland's government agencies have name problems. Many agencies have taken English names that don't tell people anything.

For example, a patient safety centre was set up at Vaasa Central Hospital last week. It studies whether care is safe for patients in Finland.

However, many Finns do not know what the centre does, because its name is No-harm Center. The Institute for the Languages of Finland has also criticised the name.

After the criticism, it has been decided in Vaasa that the name No-harm Center will be changed, at least a little.
