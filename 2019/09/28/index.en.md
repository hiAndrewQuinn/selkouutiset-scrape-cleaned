# Saturday 28.9.2019 (radio)

More money will be added to the legal protection of asylum seekers. Food waste in the army has decreased. Dangerous speeding in Turku. News anchor Kari Toivanen has died

## More money for processing asylum applications

![Maria Ohisalo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10964110)

Open image viewer

Minister of the Interior Maria Ohisalo (Green) Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

Minister of the Interior and leader of the Greens, \*\*Maria Ohisalo \*\*, wants to strengthen the legal protection of asylum seekers. Ohisalo said on the TV1 Ykkösaamu programme that the government is proposing extra money for processing asylum applications. The aim is for the Finnish Immigration Service to get extra money totalling 26 million euros for the next two years.

Minister Ohisalo says that with the extra money, more interpreters and legal aid can be hired, so that asylum applications are processed faster.

## Food waste decreased in the army

![Conscripts fetch food from the serving line](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10642686)

Open image viewer

The army makes about 70,000 meals every day. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Less food is going into the bin in the army than before. Food waste in the army has decreased by 10% in three years.

The army makes about 70,000 meals every day. In a year, tens of thousands of meals are saved in the army, because waste has become smaller. At the same time, a lot of money is saved. A lot of work has been done in the army for three years to reduce food waste.

Food waste arises when the people eating it do not think the food is good, and it is thrown in the bin. Conscripts say that, for example, many of the products in field ration packs do not taste good or are not needed during outdoor exercises.

## Dangerous speeding in Turku

![Tyre marks on a main road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7395998)

Open image viewer

About 100 cars and motorbikes took part in the gathering. Photo: Tapio Termonen / Yle

Gatherings of cars and motorbikes have caused the police a lot of work
in Turku.

On Friday evening, many cars and motorbikes gathered in the Turku area. Drivers drove at high speeds and made dangerous overtakes in different parts of the Turku region. One driver fled from the police in a car and drove off the road. He was not seriously injured. There were also rear-end collisions during the gatherings. Not all the drivers had a driving licence.

The police gave 19 drivers fines or driving bans for dangerous driving. The police say that about 100 cars or motorbikes took part in the gathering in Turku on Friday.

## News presenter Kari Toivonen has died

![Kari Toivonen in 2005.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10995064)

Open image viewer

News journalist Kari Toivonen worked at Yle for almost 40 years. He retired in 2005. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Long-time TV news presenter **Kari Toivonen** of Yle has died. Toivonen died at home this Saturday morning, after a long illness. He was 77 years old. Kari Toivonen worked at Yle for almost 40 years. He retired in 2005.
