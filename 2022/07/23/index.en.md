## Grain shipments on the Black Sea

![The back end of a missile sticks out of a grain field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5110,h_2874,x_0,y_425/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98566962dbcd636ac1b/1659701683)

Open image viewer

A part of a missile in a grain field. Ukraine produces a lot of grain also in the middle of the war. Photo: Sergey Bobok / AFP

The price of wheat has fallen around the world. The last time the price of wheat was this low was before Russia attacked Ukraine.

During the war wheat has been expensive, because Russia has blocked Ukraine's grain shipments on the Black Sea.

On Friday Ukraine and Russia agreed that grain shipments on the Black Sea can start again. That is why the price of wheat is falling.

The agreement also includes the UN, that is, the United Nations, and Turkey. The UN hopes that Ukraine and Russia will follow the agreement.

The agreement is important, because Ukraine is a big exporter of wheat. Many countries need Ukrainian wheat.

Ukraine says that Russia has attacked the port of Odesa. Odesa is a Ukrainian port city on the shore of the Black Sea.

Ukraine's grain shipments are also meant to start again from the port of Odesa. Ukraine hopes that the UN and Turkey will make sure that Russia follows the new agreement on grain shipments.

## Caffeine

![A coffee cup in hand on the table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11522555)

Open image viewer

Coffee is the Finns' favourite drink, but too much is always too much. Photo: Kristiina Lehto / Yle

Many Finns get too much caffeine. The reason is that Finns drink a lot of coffee.

A person gets too much caffeine if they drink more than 2 cups of coffee at once. 2 cups of coffee contain about 200 milligrams of caffeine.

During one day it is good to drink at most 4 cups of coffee.

If a person gets too much caffeine, they can get, for example, heart palpitations and sleep problems.

## A new bird species in Finland

![Pygmy cormorant](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12548322)

Open image viewer

Pygmy cormorant. Photo: Photo: Pertti Renvall

A new bird species has been seen in Finland. A pygmy cormorant was seen in Siilinjärvi on Friday.

The pygmy cormorant looks like the cormorant, but it is smaller.

The pygmy cormorant has been seen in the Nordic countries before only once. That happened in Sweden over 100 years ago, in 1913.

## Heat in China

In China the summer has been hotter than usual.

The weather forecast says that this weekend the temperature in South China is well over 40 degrees.

The Chinese are afraid that hot weather will harm the country's cotton fields.
