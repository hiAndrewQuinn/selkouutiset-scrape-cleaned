## Tourist visas for Russians

![Pekka Haavisto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_106/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99488962fb5f6111c55/1660644139)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finland is reducing the number of tourist visas for Russians. The reason is Russia's attack on Ukraine.

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto said on Tuesday that in future Finland will grant Russians only about 100 tourist visas a day. Previously Finland has granted about 1000 tourist visas a day.

Finland will reduce tourist visas for Russians next month. The idea is that visas are given only to those Russians who want to come to Finland for family, work or study reasons.

## Rise in maintenance charges

![Apartment blocks in Jätkäsaari, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4223,h_2375,x_776,y_959/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98931862e8dc43b4dc9/1663760878)

Open image viewer

The rise in electricity and heating costs raises housing costs. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Maintenance charges rise in many housing companies this year. The Finnish Property Management Association estimates that maintenance charges will rise by about 10 percent this year.

Charges must be raised, because electricity and district heating have become much more expensive. Some housing companies have already prepared for the situation and raised the charges.

Some housing companies have not prepared for the situation. In many housing companies extra maintenance charges may have to be collected at the end of the year.

## Waterfowl populations

![Teal swimming in water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5121,h_2881,x_0,y_264/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80205460913e0436889/1620131404)

Open image viewer

Teal. Photo: /All Over Press

The number, that is, the population, of many waterfowl has decreased in Finland. Waterfowl populations have decreased gradually over a long time.

Waterfowl populations have been monitored in Finland for 37 years. For example, the breeding pair number of the wigeon has decreased by over 50 percent. The breeding pair number of the teal has decreased by about 20 percent.

One reason is that waterbodies have become very eutrophic. Eutrophication worsens the conditions of the birds.

## Grain harvest

![grain, agriculture](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3076,x_0,y_572/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-6002157b1ef25e9a52/1471278951)

Open image viewer

Grain threshing has already started in many areas. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

The grain harvest will be quite good this year. This is what the agricultural advisory centre Pro Agria estimates.

Threshing has started in Southern Finland, Satakunta, Southwest Finland, South Ostrobothnia and Ostrobothnia. The most threshed have been oats, barley and rye.

The harvest will succeed if the weather continues good.
