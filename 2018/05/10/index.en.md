# Thursday 10.5.2018 (radio)

A big cowshed burned in North Savo. Pensioners are hiring help with housework. Finns are donating their hair to cancer patients. The Eurovision continues in Portugal. Today is Ascension Day.

## Dozens of cattle died in a cowshed fire

![Cowshed fire in Siilinjärvi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10199137)

Open image viewer

The cowshed building was completely destroyed in the fire. Photo: North Savo Rescue Department

There has been a big fire in Siilinjärvi in North Savo. The fire started on Thursday morning in a cowshed, where there were cows and calves.

There were more than 100 animals in the cowshed. About 40 animals were saved. The cowshed building was completely destroyed in the fire.

The North Savo rescue department says that the fire may have started from a work machine.

## Pensioners now buy help for the home

![Tax deduction for household work can be claimed for labour hired for housework, for example.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5278076)

Open image viewer

If you buy cleaning help for your home, for example, you can deduct part of the costs in taxation as a household tax deduction. Photo: Mika Kanerva

Pensioners have increased their use of the household tax deduction.

In 2016 about 170,000 pensioners received the household tax deduction. Ten years earlier, about 60,000 pensioners received it.

The growth is due to, for example, the number of pensioners growing and pensions being bigger than before.

Experts say, however, that attitudes have also changed. Nowadays it is acceptable to hire an outside person to help with work at home.

The household tax deduction can be claimed for renovations, household work and care work.

## Wigs for children with cancer

![Hair.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10191406)

Open image viewer

Sanni Nieminen from Hausjärvi hopes that a sick child will get a wig made from her locks. Photo: Johanna Talasterä / Yle

Finns have become enthusiastic about donating their hair to charity.

A hairdressing salon in Helsinki started a campaign to collect hair last autumn. More than 100 kilos of hair has already been received as donations.

Finns' hair is sent to England, to the Little Princess Trust charity. The charity has wigs made from the hair, which are donated to poor child patients.

Usually a wig is needed by a child who has lost their hair because of cancer treatment, for example.

## The Eurovision continues in Portugal

![Alexander Rybak](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10199016)

Open image viewer

Norway's Alexander Rybak is one of the favourites to win the second semi-final of the Eurovision. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Eurovision song contest continues this evening in Lisbon, Portugal.

The second semi-final is next, with 18 countries taking part. From the second semi-final, 10 countries will advance to the final on Saturday.

Finland has already secured a place in the final. Finland's Saara Aalto got through from the first semi-final on Tuesday.

The second Eurovision semi-final starts at 22:00. You can listen to it on Radio Suomi or watch it on TV2 or Yle Areena.

## Ascension Day is a day off for many

![Spring sun shining](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10158566)

Open image viewer

On Ascension Day the sun shone across the whole country. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

Today Ascension Day has been celebrated.

Ascension Day is always 40 days after Easter. According to Christianity, Jesus ascended to heaven on Ascension Day.

Ascension Day is a holy day in Finland, which means that many Finns have a day off work then.
