# Monday 2.12.2019 (radio)

Finland is in a political crisis. A big climate meeting has started in Spain. Yle has started news in easy Swedish.

## Political crisis

![Antti Rinne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6177475de1178dd8745/1575032747)

Finland is in a political crisis. Many people demand that Prime Minister Antti Rinne resign.

Rinne has been criticised for how he handled the Posti dispute. There was a strike at Posti because of the dispute, and it lasted over 2 weeks. The state owns Posti, and according to many people, the government did not supervise Posti well enough.

Prime Minister Rinne is the chair of the SDP. The other parties in the government are considering whether they can still support the prime minister. The Centre Party, a government party, has already said that its trust in Rinne has weakened.

The situation of the whole government will become clear by Wednesday at the latest. Then Parliament will vote on whether it still trusts the government. The government cannot continue if it does not have Parliament's trust.

## UN climate meeting

![UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres speaks at the climate meeting in Madrid.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11098982)

Open image viewer

UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres. Photo: Cristina Quicler / AFP

A big climate meeting has started in Spain.

Almost 200 countries are taking part in the meeting. They are thinking about how climate change can be slowed down.

The biggest cause of climate change is carbon dioxide, which gets into the air from industry and traffic, for example. If the amount of carbon dioxide in the air grows, global warming will continue. Then droughts and storms, for example, will increase.

At the meeting in Spain, UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres said that people must act quickly. There is not much time left to stop climate change.

The UN, the United Nations, is organising the climate meeting. The meeting lasts 2 weeks.

## News in easy Swedish

![Woman in a radio studio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11099320)

Open image viewer

Camilla Andelin read the first Yle Nyheter på lätt svenska broadcast. Photo: Yle

Here is more about easy-language news.

Yle has also started news in easy Swedish. "Nyheter på lätt svenska" comes on the radio every weekday at 11.30. The channel is Yle Vega. The news can also be listened to on Yle Areena.

Svenska Yle's publishing manager Johanna Törn-Mangs says that Yle wants to serve all Finns. Finland is a bilingual country, and many Swedish speakers also need easy-language content, Törn-Mangs says.

In addition, easy Swedish news is useful for everyone who wants to learn Swedish. The texts of the news can be found online. The address is svenska.yle.fi/lättsvenska.

## Rain on the way

![Cars and traffic in snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11090828)

Open image viewer

From Tuesday evening, snow, sleet and rain will start. It is a good idea to drive carefully on the roads. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The weather early in the week is sunny and dry. On Tuesday evening, a rain area will spread over Finland and the weather will warm up. In the north it will snow, and in the south and central parts of the country the precipitation will be sleet and rain.
