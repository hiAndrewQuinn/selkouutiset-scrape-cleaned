# Tuesday 9.4.2019 (radio)

Most Finns are against local bargaining over working conditions. The police suspect a crime in the election campaign. Working dreams have changed. Zebrafish is suitable as a laboratory animal. Finnish Language Day.

## Majority of Finns oppose local bargaining

![Tapio Salomaa in Tikkurila.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_128/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5541215ca7543a41d95/1554469991)

Tapio Salomaa from Vantaa supports universal agreement, that is, that working conditions are agreed on generally. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

Flexibility in working life and local bargaining are again an election theme. In local bargaining, for example pay and working conditions are agreed at workplaces.

Most employees are against local bargaining. Yle made a survey about this. They do not want flexibility, even when the company is doing badly. 61 percent were of the opinion that wages should not be raised or lowered according to how the company is doing.

Many companies and politicians say that without local bargaining, Finland's employment rate will not rise to 75 percent.

Among the parliamentary parties, the National Coalition and the Centre have supported increasing local bargaining the most.

## Crime suspected in election campaign

![Marco De Wit](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10719419)

Open image viewer

Chairman of the Finland First party, Marco de Wit. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

The Helsinki police suspect a crime in the election campaign at Narinkkatori in Helsinki. The crime is suspected in the election campaign of the Finland First party at the weekend. The offence, at this stage, is incitement against a population group.

At least the party's chairman, Marco de Wit, and another candidate of the party are suspected of the crime.

The police say that the party's election campaigning has been hostile. In addition, the campaigning has aimed at discriminating against immigrants and sexual minorities.

## Working dreams are often nightmares

![Sleeper sees a nightmare](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6705301)

Open image viewer

A work nightmare can wake the sleeper from sleep. Photo: Yle

Finns' working dreams have changed. Yle asked online what kind of working dreams Finns have.

About half of adults have one or more nightmares at least once a month. The nightmares can be so strong that they wake the sleeper.

Nils Sandman, a sleep researcher at the University of Turku, is surprised that the tone of working dreams is so negative.

Nightmares have become more common since the 2000s.

## Zebrafish is a good laboratory animal

![Zebrafish in a tank.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6163260)

Open image viewer

Zebrafish in an aquarium. Photo: Yle / Petri Aaltonen

The zebrafish is a good laboratory animal when studying streptococcus bacteria.

Pneumococcus and group B streptococcus cause the most pneumonia and meningitis in small children. Pneumococcus alone causes the death of more than half a million children in the world every year.

The zebrafish has been studied in a doctoral dissertation at the University of Tampere. The zebrafish's immune defence is very similar to that of humans.

## Tuesday has been Finnish Language Day

![Detail from Mikael Agricola's Abckiria (1543).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6167149)

Open image viewer

The first Finnish book was published in 1543. Photo: National Library

On Tuesday Mikael Agricola Day, that is, Finnish Language Day, was celebrated. It is also a flag day.

Mikael Agricola was the father of the Finnish written language. Agricola's ABC book was published in 1543. The ABC book was the first Finnish book and a primer.
