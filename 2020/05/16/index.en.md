# Saturday 16.5.2020 (radio)

Children from Mediterranean refugee camps will come to Finland in the summer. Finns disagree about corona restrictions for people over 70. Many European countries are easing corona restrictions. It may be difficult to find summer work this year.

## Refugee children to Finland

![Children outside their tents at the Moria refugee camp on Lesbos](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3543,h_1993,x_0,y_299/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6415435e42cd81d1dd3/1581602104)

Children at the Moria refugee camp in Greece. Photo: Orestis Panagiotou / EPA

Refugee children will come to Finland from Greece next month.

25 children will come to Finland in June and the same number in July. The children come from refugee camps in Greece.

Every child will have a health check in Greece and in Finland. Everyone will also be given corona tests. In Finland, the children will have to be in quarantine for two weeks.

Finland has promised to take in a total of 175 asylum seekers from Mediterranean refugee camps. Priority goes to children who have no guardian, as well as single parents and their children.

## Corona restrictions for older people

![An elderly person with a rollator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_287/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6555275e73889ad29a8/1584630058)

Finns disagree about corona restrictions for older people. Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

Finns disagree about the corona restrictions for people over 70 years old.

In a Yle survey, young people support the restrictions for older people, and say that it is good to continue them. The majority of people over 65, on the other hand, oppose the restrictions. They think that if a person over 70 is in good condition, the same instructions are enough for them as for younger people.

The government has said that it is good for people over 70 to stay at home. They can go outside, but it is better if they do not meet other people.

## Europe is gradually opening

![Men wearing face masks hold a tape measure between them](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3072,h_1728,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6743225eba9a4ddd288/1591967968)

In many European countries, restrictions that were given because of the coronavirus are being eased.

For example, in Italy and France, people have been able to go to the beach today for the first time in a long time. Also in Greece, 500 beaches have been reopened.

Germany has eased traffic across the border to Austria, Switzerland and France.

In Germany, the football Bundesliga also continues today. The matches are played without an audience.

## Summer work may be hard to find

![Prisma Kaari 24h shop checkout](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10017818)

Open image viewer

The corona epidemic is reducing the number of summer jobs. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Employers are hiring fewer summer workers than last year. The number of summer jobs has fallen because of the corona epidemic.

For example, the S Group is hiring about 4,000 summer workers this year. Originally, they were planned to hire about 14,000.

Municipalities had a total of 45,000 summer jobs last summer, but this summer the number is smaller. Some municipalities cannot offer any summer work at all.

The TE Offices say that new summer jobs may still come up for applications, for example when restaurants open in June.
