# Saturday 22.12.2018 (radio)

In Britain, aircraft have been disrupted. Big cities want immigrants to get to work faster. Today was the shortest day of the year. Tomorrow it is frosty across the whole country.

## Aircraft disrupted in Britain

![Aircraft at London's Gatwick airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10564273)

Open image viewer

Gatwick is Britain's second biggest airport. Photo: Andy Rain / EPA

In Britain, the police may have found the people who disrupted aircraft. The police have arrested 2 people.

The police believe that they flew remote-controlled aircraft, that is, drones, to the airport.

A drone is dangerous, because it can hit an aircraft engine.

The big Gatwick airport has been closed on many days. Tens of thousands of passengers have not been able to fly anywhere.

Remote-controlled aircraft, that is, drones, may not be flown near an airport.

## Skills centres steer immigrants

![Pupils are in a maths lesson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10530060)

Open image viewer

Immigrants get to work quickly if they can study a profession in English. Photo: Tiia Korhonen / Yle

In Finland, big cities hope that immigrants will get to work faster.

City officials say that immigrants study Finnish for too long. Immigrants should be able to study a profession in English, the officials say.

For example, the city of Vantaa in Southern Finland is starting a new kind of vocational studies. Vantaa is starting 12 English-language courses at the Varia vocational college.

When the training is in English, it does not matter if the student does not yet know Finnish so well. Finnish can also be learned at work.

The big cities Espoo, Vantaa, Turku and Tampere are setting up a skills centre. The skills centre steers immigrants into training and work.

## Today was the shortest day of the year

![Winter solstice](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7723131)

Open image viewer

Today was the shortest day of the year. Tomorrow the day gets longer. Photo: Eelis Pulkkinen

Today, Saturday, was the shortest day of the year. The light part of the day was very short.

In Oulu the length of the day was 3 hours and 34 minutes. In Helsinki the length of the day was 5 hours and 49 minutes. North of the Arctic Circle the sun did not rise at all.

Tomorrow, Sunday, the day starts to lengthen. A week from now the light part of the day will be about 7 minutes longer.

## Tomorrow it is frosty across the whole country

![Cars on a road in snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10566944)

Open image viewer

The whole country now has wintry weather, snow and frost. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Tomorrow, Sunday, it is frosty across the whole country.

In Southern Finland the frost is about 10 degrees. In Northern Finland the frost is about 20 degrees.

In Lapland the frost may be as much as 30 degrees at night.
