# Friday 31.3.2017 (radio)

The National Coalition Party, the SDP and the Centre Party were almost equally popular in March. Taxi drivers protested against the government's transport reform. Finnish hobby horse enthusiasts have also been noticed around the world.

## Support for the parties

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9539222)

Photo: Yle News Graphics

The National Coalition Party, the SDP and the Centre Party are almost equally popular in a new party support poll.

In Yle's March poll the most popular party was the National Coalition Party, which was supported by a little over 19 percent of those who answered. But the SDP and the Centre Party were very close to the National Coalition Party.

The Finns Party had increased its popularity the most, and its support was a little over 10 percent.

About half of the people did not want to say which party they plan to vote for.

The municipal elections are in about a week. Advance voting has already begun.

## Taxi drivers' protest

![Taxi drivers' demonstration](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9540736)

Open image viewer

Taxi drivers fear that the government's transport reform will increase the grey economy.  Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Taxi drivers have demonstrated against the government's transport reform. The police say that about 200 taxi drivers took part in the demonstration in central Helsinki.

On Friday around noon, the taxis drove a loop along the streets of the centre from Senate Square to the Parliament House and from there to the Market Square. The demonstration went peacefully, but it caused traffic jams, the police say.

The taxis wanted to oppose the government's plans to make it easier to get a taxi licence and to free up pricing. Taxi drivers believe that the reform will increase the grey economy.

The bill goes to Parliament for discussion next week.

## Hobby horses

![Director Selma Vilhunen (left) and Alisa Aarniomäki. Hobby horses. Hobbyhorse Revolution.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9538210)

Director Selma Vilhunen has made a film about hobby horse enthusiasts. Alisa Aarniomäki got excited about hobby horses 10 years ago. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

A fun hobby has been invented in Finland, and it is getting attention around the world too. In the hobby, you ride a hobby horse, that is, a horse head on a stick.

Alisa Aarniomäki is one of the first hobby horse enthusiasts.

![Alisa Aarniomäki. Hobby horse enthusiasts. Hobby horses. The hobby horse Verna. Hobbyhorse Revolution.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9538209)

Open image viewer

Imagination is an important part of the hobby horse hobby. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

She says that riding a hobby horse needs muscles, good body control and of course imagination too. The upper body must stay still, but the rider's legs copy the horse's legs.

People also compete with hobby horses in show jumping and dressage.

10 years ago there were about 100 hobby horse enthusiasts in Finland. Now there are 10,000 of them.

The hobby is also spreading abroad. Alisa Aarniomäki sews hobby horses and she has sold them for example to Sweden, Estonia, the Netherlands and Germany.

Director Selma Vilhunen has made a documentary film about hobby horse enthusiasts. The American newspaper The Wall Street Journal and the British The Times have also written stories about Finnish young people and their hobby horses.
