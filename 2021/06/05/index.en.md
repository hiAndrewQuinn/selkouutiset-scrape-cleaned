# Saturday 5.6.2021 (radio)

The school year ended. The president congratulated. The corona situation has calmed down. The weather has warmed up.

## The school year ended

![Students sitting with caps in hand at the celebration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_368/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81641660bb6148a4015/1622893097)

Almost 25,000 young people held matriculation celebrations on Saturday. Photo: Tanja Heino / Yle

The school year has ended in Finnish schools. Celebrations have been held in comprehensive schools, upper secondary schools and vocational schools.

In many schools the celebrations have been small because of corona. Matriculation celebrations have also been held at homes, with only a small number of guests present at the same time.

This spring almost 25,000 new school leavers will graduate. They have now completed upper secondary school and can apply to universities of applied sciences and universities.

The new school year in Finnish schools starts in August. Pupils return to school in mid-August.

## President congratulated

![Sauli Niinistö visited Yle's Ykkösaamu in Pasila on 29 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_352/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81296560b1efa500df8/1622274952)

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

President Sauli Niinistö has congratulated pupils now that the school year is ending.

In a video greeting the president says that the corona time has been hard for pupils.

The president thanks pupils for having managed to study. The pupils have also encouraged each other, the president says.

President Niinistö also thanks teachers who have given good teaching during the corona time.

## Calm corona situation

![portrait](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4240,h_2385,x_0,y_331/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81582460b9fde7a9dfb/1622801945)

Open image viewer

Sandro Soro, Sofia Viitanen and Niko Paatero intend to enjoy their summer holidays. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

Pupils' summer holidays begin in a calm corona situation. There are fewer new coronavirus infections than before.

Chief physician Tuija Leino of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that people can now enjoy the summer. Outdoor hobbies can be pursued safely.

However, Chief Physician Leino says that people must remember the corona guidelines in the summer too. For example, it is not good to organise big gatherings.

Corona vaccinations also reduce corona cases. Corona vaccinations continue in the summer too.

Already nearly 50 percent of Finns have received the first corona vaccination, and over 10 percent of Finns have also received the second corona vaccination.

The corona vaccine gives good protection against corona. The protection is very good after a person has received the corona vaccination 2 times.

## Warm weekend

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11967677)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

The weekend is warm. In a large part of the country the temperature is between 20 and 25 degrees. In Lapland it is cooler, and the temperature is under 20 degrees.

The next week also starts in warm weather. On Monday the temperature is over 20 degrees in a large part of the country.

However, rain showers become more common at the start of the week. Thunder showers are also possible.
