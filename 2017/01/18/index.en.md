# Wednesday 18.1.2017 (radio)

The ministry wants cheaper cars. Queues at Kela. Municipalities have big differences in taxes. The Iraqi army took East Mosul. Axl Smith in court for voyeurism.

## The ministry wants to abolish car tax

![A car shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9409979)

Open image viewer

The Ministry of Transport and Communications wants to lower the prices of new cars. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The Ministry of Transport and Communications proposes that traffic taxes are cut by more than 1.5 billion euros.

The ministry wants to completely abolish the car tax that is paid on a new car.

This would lower the price of a medium-sized car by about 3,000 - 4,000 euros.

Instead, in the future people would pay a fee for using the roads. The fee would depend on how much you drive.

The ministry proposes that the fee is 540 euros a year on average.

The reform benefits those who drive little.

Transport Minister Anne Berner will present the transport report tomorrow, Thursday.

## Big differences in cities' taxes

![Euro coins on top of the Finnish flag](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9349569)

Open image viewer

The difference in taxes between cities can be as big as a month's salary, the Taxpayers' Association of Finland found. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

In Finland there are big differences in cities' taxes.

In some cities, people have to pay a lot more tax than in other cities.

For example, in Kokkola an average-income family pays almost 2,700 euros more in taxes in a year than in Helsinki.

However, living in Helsinki can be more expensive, because, for example, rents are expensive.

Cities can decide themselves how much municipal tax and property tax must be paid.

State tax is paid on the same basis everywhere in Finland. State tax is progressive, which means that the bigger your salary, the more tax you have to pay.

## Isis retreated from East Mosul

![Soldiers around a pallet full of ammunition boxes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9410370)

Iraqi government soldiers received ammunition in Mosul. Photo: Ahmed Jalil / EPA

Iraqi government forces say that the extremist group Isis has been driven out of East Mosul.

The army started its attack against Isis in October.

The army is now trying to take West Mosul from Isis. It is difficult, because more people live in West Mosul, and it is hard to move in the narrow alleys of the old town.

## Axl Smith to court for voyeurism

![TV presenter Axl Smith, accused of voyeurism, arrives at Helsinki District Court on 18 January.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9409563)

Open image viewer

Axl Smith did not answer journalists' questions when he arrived at Helsinki District Court on Wednesday. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

A trial began today, Wednesday, in Helsinki, in which the well-known presenter Axl Smith is suspected of secretly filming his sex partners.

The court suspects that Smith had hidden a camera in his bedroom and secretly filmed women with it.

A total of 32 women accuse Smith in court of voyeurism, defamation and spreading information that violates privacy.

In total, about 40 videos had been filmed in recent years.

Smith admitted some of the charges. He also denied some of the charges.

The prosecutor is demanding a suspended prison sentence and a fine for Smith. The trial will last 11 days.
