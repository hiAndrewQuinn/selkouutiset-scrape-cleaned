# Sunday 9.6.2019 (radio)

A lot of cruise ships are coming to Finland. The RKP chose its chairman. Venezuela opened its border with Colombia. Many holiday cottages have been booked. Whit Sunday is celebrated today.

## A busy cruise summer is coming

![Cruise tourists at Hernesaari harbour in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6643709)

A cruise ship at the quay at Hernesaari in Helsinki. Photo: Yle

A lot of cruise ships from abroad are coming to Finland this summer. More cruise ships are coming this year than before. 300 cruise ships will visit Helsinki this year. Helsinki has a new long quay for big cruise ships.

In the summer, Finns also go on cruises a lot. Finns travel a lot to Tallinn and Stockholm. There are many families with children on the ships in summer.

## Henriksson continues at the head of the RKP

![Anna-Maja Henriksson](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10741511)

Open image viewer

Anna-Maja Henriksson was re-elected. Photo: Yle

Justice Minister **Anna-Maja Henriksson** will continue as chairman of the Swedish People's Party, RKP.

Henriksson was re-elected for another term at the RKP party congress in Vaasa. **Sandra Bergqvist**, **Silja Borgarsdóttir Sandelin** and **Henrik Wickström** were elected as the RKP's deputy chairs.

## Venezuela's border was opened

![Thousands of Venezuelans queued on the Simón Bolívar bridge to get into the Colombian city of Cúcuta on Saturday, when the border between the countries opened after four months.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10822644)

Open image viewer

Thousands of Venezuelans queued on the Simón Bolívar bridge to get into the Colombian city of Cúcuta on Saturday, when the border between the countries opened after four months. Photo: EPA

The border between Venezuela and Colombia has been opened. The border was closed for four months, because Venezuelan president \*\*Nicolas Maduro \*\* ordered the border closed. There are very long queues at the Venezuela–Colombia border, because Venezuelans want to go to Colombia to buy food and medicines.

The political situation in Venezuela is difficult. A very large number of people have left Venezuela. Many people and many countries do not accept that Nicolas Maduro is Venezuela's president. Many people and many countries say that Venezuela's president is **Juan Guaidó**.

## Summer cottages booked for Midsummer

![Cottage on the shore of a lake.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9630822)

Open image viewer

It is hard to get a summer cottage for Midsummer now. Photo: Arja Lento / Yle

Finns have booked many summer cottages for Midsummer. Almost all cottages have been rented for Midsummer. In Northern Finland there are few cottages free over Midsummer. In Southern Finland there are not many cottages free.

Finns want cottages that are well equipped. A summer cottage must have electricity, a toilet, a shower and a dishwasher. A summer cottage must be pleasant.

## Today is Whit Sunday

![Pastor Marjaana Toiviainen of Oulunkylä parish serves communion at Helsinki Old Church.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6531639)

Open image viewer

Whit Sunday is an important day in the Christian church, along with Christmas and Easter. Photo: Yle

Today, Sunday, Whit Sunday is being celebrated. Whit Sunday is the day when the Christian church was founded. It is an important day of the church year. Whit Sunday is 50 days after Easter. In the Christian church, Whit Sunday is the feast of the Holy Spirit. In folk tradition, on Whit Sunday people predict the weather and look for a spouse.
