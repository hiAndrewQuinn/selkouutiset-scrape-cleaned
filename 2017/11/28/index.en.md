# Tuesday 28.11.2017 (radio)

Famous places will be lit with festive lights in honour of Finland's 100th birthday. Tobacco may have been brought to Finland from Russia with a drone. Finns like to shop at big supermarkets. It is good to drink 4 cups of coffee a day.

## Lights in honour of Finland

![Niagara Falls ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9951387)

Open image viewer

On Finland's Independence Day, Niagara Falls will also be lit with blue and white lights. Photo: Quentin Bargate / AOP

Finland's 100th anniversary will be visible in many countries next week. Many famous places will be lit with blue and white lights.

For example, the Colosseum in Rome in Italy and Niagara Falls in Canada will get festive lights in honour of Finland. At least Sweden, Portugal, the Czech Republic and Mozambique are also taking part in the event.

Yle will show pictures of the festive lights from different parts of the world. You can follow the event on television, online and on social media.

Independent Finland turns 100 on Wednesday, the 6th of December.

## Tobacco smuggling

![Drone](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9951587)

Open image viewer

Tobacco was smuggled with a drone like this. Photo: Finnish Border Guard

Tobacco may have been brought illegally to Finland from Russia in an unusual way. The authorities suspect that tobacco was brought across the border with a drone, that is, a small flying machine.

The authorities heard about it in July. When they investigated, they found a drone in the forest. Tobacco had been attached to the drone.

The authorities are continuing to investigate the matter.

## Popular supermarkets

![A woman at a bread shelf](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9502407)

Open image viewer

Big supermarkets have lots of goods. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Finns like to shop at big supermarkets. Sales in large shops have grown by about 4 percent this year.

Small shops are doing worse. Sales in small shops have fallen by as much as 5 percent.

The retail trade hopes that Finns will do a lot of Christmas shopping and that Christmas will raise sales.

## Healthy coffee

![A coffee cup on a table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9881436)

Open image viewer

Coffee is the Finns' favourite drink. Photo: AOP

Coffee is good for your health. This has been known for a long time. But now there is information about how much coffee it is good to drink.

A study on coffee was done at the University of Southampton in Britain. The university's researchers say that it is good to drink 3 or 4 cups of coffee a day.

If a person drinks this much coffee, he does not get ill, for example with cancer, as easily as a person who does not drink coffee at all. You can also drink more than 4 cups of coffee a day, but it does not add to the health benefit.

Coffee studies interest Finns, because Finns drink the most coffee in the world.
