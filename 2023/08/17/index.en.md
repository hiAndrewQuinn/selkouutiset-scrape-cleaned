## Sweden and terrorism

![ Susanna Trehorning, Charlotte von Essen and Ahn-Za Hagstrom sit down behind tables with microphones at a press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_1,y_313/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115835364de067cec98b/1692272740)

The Swedish police spoke on Thursday about the threat of terrorism. Photo: Henrik Montgomery / AFP

The threat of terrorism has grown in Sweden. That is what the Swedish police says. The Swedish police raised the threat assessment to level four from level three. The greatest threat is at level five.

According to the police, the reason is that Islamist extremist terrorists may attack Sweden. The threat has been growing for a long time.

For example, the terrorist organisation al-Qaeda threatened Sweden at the weekend. According to the Islamist extremist al-Qaeda, Sweden must be punished, because some people have burned Qurans in Sweden.

According to the Swedish police, many people wrongly believe that the Swedish state is against the religion of Islam.

The Finnish police say that in Finland the threat of terrorism has not changed.

There may also be terrorism in Finland, but the risk is not very high.

## Bird flu

![A dead young gull lies in grass where bellflowers grow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6238,h_3496,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114735964bfeb513d911/1690300543)

Open image viewer

Many birds have died of bird flu. Photo: Elina Ervasti / Yle

Bird flu must be brought under control quickly, say health authorities.

Bird flu is a disease that has killed many birds in Finland. Bird flu has also spread from birds to fur animals that live on fur farms.

According to health authorities, the risk that bird flu starts to spread in people grows in autumn.

The reason is that in autumn the normal human influenza virus starts to circulate. Then the risk grows that the bird flu virus and the human influenza virus combine

Then the disease may start to spread in people.

## Summer cottages

![A summer cottage in Karkkila.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2796,x_0,y_375/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1100271643d3ca621359/1681734872)

Few summer cottages are being bought now. In July, 30 percent fewer summer cottages were bought than usual.

One reason is that interest rates have risen and loans are more expensive than before.

In addition, people may not have the money to buy a summer cottage now, when almost all prices have risen.

There are more than half a million summer cottages in Finland.
