# Monday 22.7.2019 (radio)

The new president's party won in Ukraine. Many pigs died in a fire at a pig farm. Latvia asked Finland for help. People were killed in an air strike in Idlib. India sent a rocket to the Moon. The weather is warm.

## The new president's party won in Ukraine

![Victory party of Ukrainian president Volodymyr Zelenskyy's Servant of the People party on 21 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10888359)

Zelenskyy's party will get a majority in parliament. Photo: Tatyana Zenkovich / EPA

In Ukraine the new president's party has won the parliamentary elections. Parliamentary elections were held in Ukraine on Sunday. Ukraine's new president \*\*Volodymyr Zelenskyy \*\*organised the parliamentary elections, because many in the old parliament opposed him. In the new parliament Zelenskyy has many supporters.

## Many pigs died in a fire

![Pigs being moved from the fire site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10888100)

Open image viewer

Many pigs were in smoke. The pigs are being moved to other farms. Photo: Anne Elhaimer / Yle

There has been a fire at a large pig farm in Seinäjoki. There were 5,000 pigs in the pig farm. Many pigs died in the fire. Many pigs breathed poisonous smoke. A veterinarian will inspect the pigs. Pigs will have to be put down if they have breathed a lot of smoke. The pig farm fire probably started from a ventilation fan.

## Latvia asked Finland for diplomatic help

![An Iranian Revolutionary Guard boat moves around the Stena Bulk tanker.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10888446)

Open image viewer

The tanker has a crew of 23. One crew member is Latvian. Photo: Hasan Shirvani / EPA

Latvia is asking Finland for help in finding out about the Iranian tanker seizure. Iran has stopped a British tanker in the Strait of Hormuz. A Latvian sailor works on the tanker.

Latvia is asking that Finland's embassy in Iran help Latvia find out the situation of the Latvian sailor. Latvia does not have its own embassy in Iran. Finland is helping Latvia, because Latvia is an EU country.

## An air strike was carried out on Idlib

![graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8744594)

Open image viewer

Many people have died in air strikes. Photo: Niina Kalliainen / Yle graphics

An air strike has been carried out in Idlib in Syria. Perhaps 30 people died in the air strike. Many people were injured. Idlib is controlled by Syrian rebels.

Rescuers say that the air strike was carried out by the government of President Bashar al-Assad or by Russia, which supports President Bashar al-Assad.

## India sent a vessel to the Moon

![India's Chandrayaan-2 rocket.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10888507)

Open image viewer

The Chandrayaan-2 rocket left the Satish Dhawan Space Centre on Sriharikota island. Photo: ISRO / EPA

India has launched a rocket that flies to the Moon. The rocket carries a probe that will study the surface of the Moon. There are no people on the rocket. The rocket's journey to the Moon will take 1.5 months.

India is the fourth country to send a spacecraft to the Moon. Earlier, the USA, Russia and China sent spacecraft to the Moon.
