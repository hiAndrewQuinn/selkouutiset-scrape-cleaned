# Sunday 7.4.2019 (radio)

Many people have voted in advance in the parliamentary elections. Language training for immigrants was one theme in Yle's plain-language election debate. A pricey Moomin mug was sold on Sunday. There is pollen in the air.

## Busy advance voting

![Advance voting in Hämeenlinna](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10725463)

Open image viewer

Many people have visited advance voting places. Photo: Riitta Tapiomäki / Yle

Many people have voted in advance in the parliamentary elections. More than 800,000 people went to vote by Saturday.

Advance voting continues until Tuesday. Voting places are, for example, in shopping centres and libraries.

On election day everyone must vote at the place that is written on the voting notice. The notice came to your home by post.

Election day is in a week, on 14 April.

## Plain-language election debate: language training

![Man speaks into a microphone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10726985)

Open image viewer

Rezaei Amir Shah asked the parties the question from immigrant students. Photo: Yle

Language training for immigrants was one theme in Yle's plain-language election debate. 9 parties took part in the election debate.

The parties also received a question from the audience from students of Finnish. The students asked the parties: What kind of language skills must an immigrant have in order to get Finnish citizenship?

Almost all the parties' representatives were of the opinion that the current language requirement, that is, satisfactory Finnish skills, is good. Only Outi Alanko-Kahiluoto of the Greens disagreed. She said that language skills cannot be a condition of citizenship, because there is too little language training and it is often poor.

The parties were also of the same opinion that language training for immigrants must be improved and that there must be more language training.

You can watch the plain-language election debate on Yle Areena and on the Clear News website.

## Pricey Moomin mug

![Rare Moomin mug.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10726754)

Open image viewer

The buyer paid 6100 euros for this Moomin mug. Photo: Ville Välimäki / Yle

On Sunday a rare Moomin mug was sold. The mug was sold at an auction, that is, the mug went to the buyer who offered the most money for it.

The price of the mug rose high. The buyer paid 6100 euros for the mug. The mug is expensive because there are no other similar mugs.

The mug has a picture of Vilijonkka. However, a mistake happened at the factory when the mug was made. The mug shows 2 bottom halves of Vilijonkka, but no top halves at all. The mug nevertheless accidentally got the official Moomin stamp. An expensive mistake, you could say now.

## Pollen in the air

![Catkins hang on a tree.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8787708)

Open image viewer

Alder is already flowering in a large part of Finland. Photo: Maarit Siitonen

In a large part of Finland it is already pollen season. Many trees and bushes are flowering. Pollen comes into the air from them.

Pollen causes symptoms for allergic people: the nose runs and the eyes itch.

In the coming days the amount of pollen in the air will decrease, because rain will come to Finland. Rain cleans the air.
