# Monday 28.3.2022 (radio)

Russia continues its attacks in Ukraine. People believe that Finland's economy will develop badly. Mika Lehtimäki resigns as leader in the Olympic Committee. The Oscars were awarded in the USA.

## Ukraine

Russia continues its attack in Ukraine.

Russia has made missile strikes on many cities in Ukraine. Russia has attacked at least the capital Kyiv, Lviv and Kharkiv.

Ukraine says that it is suspending the evacuation of people from war zones. Ukraine's information says that Russia intends to attack the routes along which people are being evacuated.

The UN says that nearly 3.9 million people have fled Ukraine since Russia began its attack a month ago.

Russia says that no major results have been achieved in the negotiations between Russia and Ukraine. Russia and Ukraine are negotiating in Turkey this week.

## Finland's economy

![A hand pushes a Prisma shopping trolley. In the trolley are plastic bags and a pack of toilet paper.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4020,h_2258,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-932102623b11e561a04/1648038531)

People no longer believe that Finland's economy will do well in the future. The biggest reason for this is Russia's attack on Ukraine. In March, people had much less confidence in good economic development than in February.

In March, only 13 percent of people believed that Finland's economy would improve during the year. As many as 61 percent of people believed that Finland's economy is heading in a bad direction.

## Mika Lehtimäki

![Mika Lehtimäki looks down seriously.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3360,h_1890,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-934260624176d11ca48/1648457458)

Mika Lehtimäki. Photo: Lehtikuva

Mika Lehtimäki is resigning from his post as leader in the Olympic Committee. He was the head of the Olympic Committee's elite sports unit.

Yle Urheilu reported last week that Mika Lehtimäki received a warning in the autumn. The reason was that Mika Lehtimäki behaved badly towards women.

However, Mika Lehtimäki was allowed to continue as head of the Olympic Committee's elite sports unit. Now he is leaving the post straight away. Mika Lehtimäki says that he decided himself to stop working as a leader.

## Oscar awards

![The creative team of Coda on stage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6880,h_3870,x_0,y_7/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-93404962412e60c67e5/1648439805)

Coda, a music drama about a deaf family, was awarded the Oscar for best film of the year. Photo: Rob Latour/Shutterstock/All Over Press

The Oscar film awards have been handed out in Los Angeles.

The Oscar for best film went to the film Coda. The film is about a deaf family with a hearing child.

Jane Campion received the Oscar for best direction. She directed the film The Power of the Dog.

Will Smith received the Oscar for best actor. Jessica Chastain received the Oscar for best actress.

The science fiction film Dune won a total of 6 awards.
