# Thursday 9.9.2021 (radio)

The government has agreed on the budget and on climate emissions. Exhaustion in families with babies. Citizens' initiative on water. Yle is now 95 years old.

The government has agreed on next year's budget and on reducing climate emissions. There was a dispute about emissions between the Centre and the Greens, but the parties reached agreement.

Climate measures will reduce emissions by about 14 - 15 megatonnes by 2035.

Next year, for example, the taxation of tobacco will tighten. Regional travel discounts are secured until August next year. In 2023 a mining tax comes into use.

In the government's proposal, next year's budget expenditure is 63.2 billion euros.

The budget deficit is 6.9 billion euros.

## Families with babies

Exhaustion in families with babies varies a lot in different areas of Finland. The Institute for Health and Welfare has studied the matter.

Symptoms of exhaustion are found in 20 per cent of families with babies. Exhaustion can be as high as 40 per cent of families with babies.

Among mothers who have given birth, symptoms of exhaustion were most common in Kainuu. Exhaustion was least common in Kanta-Häme, Central Uusimaa and Southwest Finland.

Loneliness was also experienced most in Kainuu. Loneliness was least experienced in Central Uusimaa.

## Water

![A glass of water being filled from the tap.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11272078)

Open image viewer

For example, the city of Jyväskylä has previously considered privatising its water supply. Such a thing is now to be prevented. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

The citizens' initiative 'Vesi on meidän' ('Water is ours') has been approved unanimously in Parliament. The initiative aims to ensure that water supply is not privatised.

Parliament has not previously approved any citizens' initiative unanimously.

The authors of the initiative say that privatising water supply has caused problems around the world. The price of water has risen, its quality has worsened and the environment has suffered.

Over 89,000 people signed the initiative in a short time.

Next, detailed preparation of the legislation begins.

## Yle turns 95

Yle had its birthday on Friday. Yleisradio has turned 95 years old.

O.Y. Suomen Yleisradio – A.B. Finlands Rundradio broadcast its first radio programme in 1926 in Helsinki. The broadcast came from the studio at Unioninkatu 20 on 9 September.

Yle is owned by the state. Yle is financed by the Yle tax, whose collection began in 2013.

The newest reason for joy is Yle's placing in Taloustutkimus's brand study. It examined the reputation of different companies.

The streaming service Yle Areena was in 2nd place, right after Fiskars. In the ranking of Finnish brands, Yle was 5th.

The study covered almost 600 Finnish brands.
