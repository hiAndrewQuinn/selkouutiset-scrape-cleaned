## Transfer tax

![The empty living room of a new apartment for sale in an apartment block.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5903,h_3320,x_0,y_410/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117017464fea8985273a/1694415883)

The government intends to change the taxation of home sales. The change concerns the transfer tax that the buyer of a flat pays.

From next year, young people who buy their first own home will also have to pay transfer tax. Today a young first-time buyer does not pay transfer tax.

At the same time the government intends to lower the transfer tax on homes that are not first homes. Today the buyer pays 2 percent transfer tax on a flat. The government proposes that from next year the tax will be only 1.5 percent.

## Flights for Finns from Israel

![Rockets fired by Palestinians towards Israel from Rafah in the air.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2400,h_1350,x_0,y_237/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11854836527e4122b046/1697113331)

Open image viewer

It is not safe in Israel now. Photo: Said Khatib / AFP

Finns are going to be brought out of the crisis area in Israel. The reason is that the fighting in the area continues.

On Friday and Saturday flights will be arranged so that Finns can get out of the crisis area. There are about 200 Finns in the area.

Passengers pay for the flight themselves. The flight costs about 500 euros.

The Palestinian militant group Hamas attacked Israel last weekend, and Israel has carried out counterattacks on Hamas. Many people have died in the crisis.

In Finland, city dwellers are happy with their own neighbourhood. This is what a new survey says.

City dwellers are happy in quiet areas close to the centre. Noise and air pollution reduce satisfaction.

## TV series about Estonia

![A rescue helicopter lights up a rescue raft in the darkness. Drawing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7262,h_4085,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11835326523e7b7bb297/1696852866)

Open image viewer

The TV series about Estonia tells about a shocking accident. Photo: MTV

A TV series has been made about the Estonia car ferry accident. The series was commissioned by MTV.

The Estonia accident happened almost 30 years ago. More than 800 people died in the accident.

Estonia sank in the sea near Finland, when it was on its way from Tallinn to Stockholm. The TV series shows how the accident was dealt with in Finland, Estonia and Sweden.

The TV series focuses especially on the investigation of the accident, which lasted 3 years. The main character of the series is a young Finnish researcher.

The TV series about the Estonia accident is the most expensive TV series in Finland. It cost more than 15 million euros.

## The weather

![Friday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-118568365281cbd242c6/1697127641)

Thursday has been a rainy day. The rescue services in North Ostrobothnia in particular have had many jobs because of the rain.

Water has covered, for example, road underpasses. In addition, the wind has knocked down trees and cut electricity.

Friday is a dry day, so no rain is expected. The sun can also shine.

The temperature on Friday is between 0 and 11 degrees.

On Saturday new rain will spread over Finland.
