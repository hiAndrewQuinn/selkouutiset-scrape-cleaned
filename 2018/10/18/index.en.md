# Thursday 18.10.2018 (radio)

Smoking during pregnancy is more common in Eastern Finland than in the West. The founder of the MV publication, Ilja Janitskin, has been sentenced to prison. Singer Katri Helena receives a grandparent award. Dogs can recognise words.

## Smoking by expectant mothers varies by region

![Smoking during pregnancy / Media desk](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10459843)

Open image viewer

Smoking during pregnancy harms the development of the foetus. Photo: Yle

Many women smoke even though they are expecting a child. Over 12 per cent of expectant mothers smoke. Young people and those with little education smoke the most.

There are big differences between regions. In Eastern Finland smoking during pregnancy is more common than in the West. In Kymenlaakso 16 per cent of expectant mothers smoke. Kymenlaakso is an industrial area, and many women's partners also smoke. In Kymenlaakso only 33 per cent of women quit smoking in early pregnancy.

Elsewhere in the country about half quit smoking in early pregnancy. Maternity clinics help people to stop smoking.

## Ilja Janitskin was sentenced

![Ilja Janitskin (centre) in Helsinki District Court on 13 June](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10462650)

Open image viewer

Ilja Janitskin in court in June 2018. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Ilja Janitskin has been sentenced to prison. He is the founder of the MV publication.

The court gave Janitskin a prison sentence of one year and 10 months. The sentence was for 16 offences in total, which are linked to the activities of the MV publication and Uber Uutiset. The court says that, for example, the MV publication has published racist writings.

The court also gave a sentence to Johan Bäckman. Bäckman is known, for example, for his links to Russia. Bäckman was sentenced because, for example, he harassed a Yle journalist and insulted the journalist's honour.

## Katri Helena receives a grandparent award

![Katri Helena](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8509001)

Open image viewer

Family is important to Katri Helena. Photo: Yle

Singer Katri Helena receives the Grandparent of the Year award. It was given by the Association for Elderly and Neighbourly Services, Valli. Valli says that the example of Katri Helena Kalaoja is versatile. It shows that a grandparent can also have their own life, career and everyday life.

Katri Helena, who is 73 years old, says that she has a view on many things, because she is part of a chain of 4 generations. Katri Helena's mother turns 101 next month.

The award will be handed out next Saturday. That is Grandparents' Day.

## Dogs can recognise words

![A dog that is half golden retriever and half labrador lies in a brain scanner. In front of it are monkey and pig toys.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10461771)

Open image viewer

Eddie the dog took part in the test with toys. Photo: Gregory Berns

Brain scans prove that dogs can recognise words. It has usually been thought that a dog only reacts to tone of voice, gaze or gesture. However, many dog owners believe that a dog also recognises words.

American researchers studied the matter using magnetic resonance imaging. The study proved that dogs have at least a basic sense of words. However, the surprise was that dogs react to words in the opposite way to humans. Dogs react most to unfamiliar words.
