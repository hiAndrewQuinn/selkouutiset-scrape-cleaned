# Monday 5.10.2020 (radio)

Vaasa bans gatherings of more than 50 people. The Ministry of Finance has reduced its estimate of the weakening of Finland's economy. Bars and cafés are closed in Paris. The regional state administrative agencies have been awarded for clear language.

First, new information about the coronavirus.

The coronavirus is spreading quickly in the Vaasa hospital district. The city of Vaasa bans gatherings of more than 50 people. Almost 1,000 people are in quarantine in Vaasa.

In total, 164 new corona infections were found in Finland in one day. In the last 2 weeks, over 900 more infections have been found than before. Most infections are in the capital region.

One new death linked to the coronavirus has been reported in Finland. In Finland 346 people have now died because of the coronavirus.

## The Finnish economy

![Ministry of Finance forecast for the change in GDP.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11579025)

Open image viewer

Ministry of Finance forecast for the change in GDP. Photo: Samuli Huttunen / Yle

The Ministry of Finance has changed its estimate of the Finnish economy.

The ministry now says that the Finnish economy will shrink by 4.5 percent this year. In the summer the Ministry of Finance estimated that the Finnish economy would shrink by as much as 6 percent this year.

The Ministry of Finance estimates that next year the Finnish economy will grow by about 2.5 percent. This estimate of the ministry will only come true if the spread of the coronavirus stops.

## The clearest language of the year

![Award winners Sonja Tanttari and Taina Taskinen and Parliamentary Ombudsman Petri Jääskeläinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11579962)

The Clear Language of the Year award went to the working groups of the regional state administrative agencies for clear language training and website renewal. The winner was chosen by the Parliamentary Ombudsman Petri Jääskeläinen. The awards were handed out in the Little Parliament on 5 October 2020. The award was received by Sonja Tanttari and Taina Taskinen. Photo: Hanne Salonen / Parliament

The regional state administrative agencies have been awarded for clear language. The Clear Language of the Year award is given to working groups of the regional state administrative agencies that have done clear language training and renewed their websites.

A regional state administrative agency, for example, monitors that people's basic rights are not violated.

This year is the clear language theme year in the regional state administrative agencies. The agencies give clear language training to all their employees.

The regional state administrative agency also has a new website. The new website is clearer than the old one. The texts have been rewritten so that they are clearer than before.

The winner was chosen by the Parliamentary Ombudsman Petri Jääskeläinen. Jääskeläinen thinks it is important that the language of the authorities is clear.
