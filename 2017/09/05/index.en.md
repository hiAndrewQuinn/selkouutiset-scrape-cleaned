# Tuesday 5.9.2017 (radio)

Parliament went back to work. Asylum seekers demonstrated. In Sweden, mothers' patrols keep order on the streets. The Western Metro is still not ready. Many elderly people need help to go out safely.

## Parliament started work in the renewed building

![Statues in the Parliament's chamber unveiled on 05.09.2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9815873)

Open image viewer

The chamber of Parliament is now decorated with golden statues. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

Parliament has started the autumn session.

At the same time, MPs were able to return to the Parliament building, which had been under renovation for 2 years.

The Parliament building has been repaired so that it now looks the same as in the 1930s, when the building was completed. However, new and modern technology has been brought into the building.

The famous bronze nude statues in the chamber have also been renewed. They are now golden.

## Asylum seekers demonstrated

![Demonstration in Tornio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9815909)

Open image viewer

Asylum seekers protested against Finland's asylum policy in Tornio. Photo: Minna Aula / Yle

Asylum seekers have demonstrated in Tornio. The demonstration was against Finland's asylum policy.

Yesterday the police and demonstrators clashed at a reception centre in Jyväskylä.

The police came to collect an Afghan family, who are to be returned to Afghanistan. The demonstrators tried to stop the family from being taken away.

The police used force to end the situation.

Interior Minister Paula Risikko says that reception centres and the police must now think together about how such clashes can be prevented.

## Mothers' patrols keep order on suburban streets

![Five women in red vests; the vests say Nattvandring.nu](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9811503)

Open image viewer

Turkish mothers patrol the streets and parks of the Fittja suburb in Stockholm. Photo: Paula Tapiola / Yle

In Sweden, new ways are being sought to prevent immigrants from being pushed to the margins of society.

Good experiences have been gained from parents' patrols, which watch over the safety of young people on the streets.

For example, in the Fittja suburb of Stockholm, Turkish mothers go around the streets and parks on weekend evenings. Vandalism and petty crime have decreased, because the mothers' patrol knows the children and young people and their parents.

Many people say that one mother on the street gets young people in order more effectively than 10 police officers.

The patrols already operate in more than 300 places. About 45,000 adults take part in them.

## The start of the Western Metro may still be delayed

![Western Metro, Aalto University metro station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9344650)

Open image viewer

The Western Metro runs between Helsinki and Espoo. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

The start of the Western Metro once again threatens to be postponed.

Traffic on the metro line between Helsinki and Espoo was supposed to start at the end of this month. However, the metro's control system is not yet ready.

It is not yet known when the metro will be completely ready and when metro traffic can finally start.

## Many elderly people want company for a walk

![Elderly person sits on a bench](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9717302)

Open image viewer

Many elderly people need help to go out safely. Photo: Wasim Khuzam / Yle

Today the nationwide campaign "Take the Elderly Out" begins.

The Ageing Institute reminds that many elderly people need help to go out safely. That is why a walking companion is needed.

A short walk brings elderly people many kinds of joy. For example, it improves balance and refreshes the mind and memory.
