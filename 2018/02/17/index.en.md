# Saturday 17.2.2018

Ski holidays are starting. Pandas are going outdoors. People are protesting against the animal welfare law. Russia in the US elections. Emergency in Ethiopia. Kosovo turns 10.

## Ski holidays have started in Southern Finland

![Train passengers at Helsinki railway station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10079884)

Open image viewer

During ski holidays, train tickets must be booked in good time, because the trains are often full. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Many families have set off on a ski holiday at the weekend.

The ski holiday is next week in schools in Southern Finland.

On ski holidays people often travel to Northern Finland to ski and go downhill skiing. Many travel north by train.

Today a train left for Oulu, which was missing one carriage.

The train was full, and about a hundred people had to travel to Oulu without a seat. The journey took more than five hours.

The railway company VR says that one carriage was left off by mistake. VR pays compensation to passengers.

## The pandas got to go outdoors

![Panda Pyry in Ähtäri](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10080528)

Open image viewer

Pandas enjoy Finnish nature. Photo: Tarmo Niemi/Yle

The two pandas of the Ähtäri zoo have been outside for the first time.

The pandas came to Finland from China a month ago.

Today the public also saw the pandas for the first time. Hundreds of people were there.

The pandas played happily in the snow.

The pandas' names are Lumi and Pyry.

## People marched in Helsinki against the animal welfare law

![Tie-stall barn](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6901427)

Open image viewer

Animal rights activists oppose, among other things, tie-stall barns, where cows are kept tied up. Photo: Yle

Today there was a demonstration in central Helsinki against the new animal welfare law.

The demonstrators say that the law has many shortcomings. The law does not ban fur farming, nor the cramped conditions for cows and pigs.

A section of the law was also removed, which said that animals must have water at all times.

About a thousand people took part in the animal welfare associations' demonstration. The law is not yet in force.

## Charges against Russians in the US

![Former FBI director Robert Mueller leads the investigation into links between President Trump's election campaign and Russia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10042018)

Open image viewer

Robert Mueller has investigated how Russia interfered in the US presidential election. Photo: Michael Reynolds / EPA

In the United States, several Russians are accused of having interfered in the 2016 US presidential election.

Special prosecutor Robert Mueller has brought charges against 13 Russian individuals.

Three Russian organisations are also accused.

In the charges, Mueller describes in detail how Russia interfered in the election, for example on the internet.

President Donald Trump has earlier called Mueller's investigations pointless.

Russia, for its part, says that the charges are not true.

## Ethiopia declared a state of emergency

![Siraj Fegessa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10080616)

Open image viewer

Defence Minister Siraj Fegessa said that the state of emergency will last half a year. Photo: AOP

A national state of emergency has been imposed in Ethiopia.

The state of emergency lasts six months, and during it no protests may be organised.

The Ethiopian government declared the state of emergency because there is a lot of violence in the country.

## Kosovo celebrates its independence

![Map graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9407038)

Open image viewer

Kosovo's Albanian population wanted independence. They fought against the Serbs in 1996–1999. Photo: Yle

Kosovo is celebrating its independence day today.

Kosovo became independent from Serbia 10 years ago.

Kosovo's independence has now been recognised by 115 countries. Finland has also recognised Kosovo's independence.

Serbia and Russia, on the other hand, for example, have not recognised Kosovo.
