# Monday 5.11.2018 (radio)

Changes are proposed to the yt-law. Finns' English skills have got worse. Firms will start using the income register next year. Now it is Swedishness week.

## The yt-law

![Labour Minister Jari Lindström](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10465933)

Labour Minister Jari Lindström. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

A working group proposes changes to the Act on Co-operation within Workplaces, the yt-law. The working group told Labour Minister Jari Lindström about its proposals on Monday.

The yt-law sets the rules for yt-negotiations, which are the co-operation negotiations held in firms when working conditions change.

The working group says that the yt-law does not work well. Employees fear yt-negotiations, because they often mean dismissals. That is why the working group proposes that dismissal matters be moved to another law, out of the yt-law.

The new yt-law could be called the development law, the working group proposes. The name shows that the goal is the development of the firm's employees.

No decisions about the new law have been made yet.

## English skills

![A board with colourful teaching posters showing a clown and different numbers in English.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10410869)

Finns' English skills have got worse. A new study compared adults' English skills in different countries.

Finland came 8th. In earlier comparisons Finland has ranked better. The best country in this comparison was Sweden.

People are best at English in Europe, but English skills have improved the most in Africa.

## Income register

![Raija Kuri opens a folder placed on top of an office cabinet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5368278)

Open image viewer

Transferring salary information becomes easier when the new income register comes into use. Photo: YLE

Firms will start using the income register next year. The income register is a new system for salary information.

Cleaning firm director Monika Põldkivi says that the income register is simpler than the old system.

Salary information will go directly from the income register to Kela, for example. That is why, for example, information about a long sick leave no longer needs to be reported to Kela separately.

Põldkivi's firm is ready to start using the income register. However, a new survey shows that only half of firms have prepared as well.

That is why the authorities intend to share more information about the new system.

## Swedishness week

![Itämerenkatu](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9071140)

Open image viewer

Finland has 2 official languages. Photo: Kimmo Hiltunen / Yle

Swedishness week began on Monday. Then Swedish, which is Finland's second official language, is celebrated.

Tuesday is Swedishness Day. Swedishness Day, in Swedish *Svenska dagen*, is always on 6 November.

Almost 300,000 Finns speak Swedish as their mother tongue. Many famous Finns are Swedish-speaking. For example, Tove Jansson wrote the Moomin books in Swedish.
