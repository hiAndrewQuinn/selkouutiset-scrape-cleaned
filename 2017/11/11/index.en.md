# Saturday 11.11.2017 (radio)

The presidents of the USA and Russia have met in Vietnam. A big hall burned down in Rantsila. Father's Day is on Sunday.

## Trump and Putin meet

![US President Donald Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin met briefly at the APEC economic summit in Vietnam.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9927246)

The presidents of the USA and Russia have met in Asia, in Vietnam. There was a meeting of the economic organisation APEC in Vietnam.

Presidents Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin said that the war in Syria cannot be ended by military means.

The presidents think that a political solution is needed. They said that the war in Syria can only be ended by negotiating peace.

Russia and the USA support different sides in the war in Syria. Russia supports the Syrian government, and the USA supports the Syrian opposition.

The war in Syria has gone on for 6 years already. More than 300,000 people have died in the war.

## The Rantsila fire

![Firefighting at the scene of the fire in Rantsila. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9927143)

Open image viewer

The fire destroyed a whole hall in Rantsila. Photo: Arto Veräjänkorva / Yle

There has been a big fire in Rantsila in Northern Finland. The fire was in a hall owned by the company Kylmänen Food.

No one died in the fire. One person was taken to hospital, but he was not badly injured.

But the fire destroyed a lot of property. The hall burned down completely.

Kylmänen Food makes meat products and canned food. The fire burned hundreds of thousands of kilos of pork, reindeer meat and elk meat.

Machines and equipment also burned together with the hall. The damage may be worth millions of euros.

It is not yet known what caused the fire.

## Fathers of the Year

![Janne Nordman and family](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9920460)

Open image viewer

Father of the Year Janne Nordman in the middle of the family's children. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

Father's Day is on Sunday. Around Father's Day, the Father of the Year award is also given. This year the award was given to 3 fathers.

Antero Lindberg got the award because he has encouraged children to do sports. Lindberg has done voluntary work for more than 40 years. Many children have found a sports hobby thanks to Lindberg.

Janne Nordman also got the award. He is the father of a blended family. A blended family means that the parents already have children from previous marriages when they get married. There are 7 children in total in Nordman's family, and Nordman takes equally good care of all the children. Nordman is also involved in the work of an organisation for blended families.

Miika Niemelä is the third father who got the Father of the Year award this year. Niemelä has been involved in Icehearts activities. The idea of Icehearts is to help children and young people who need special support.

Father's Day is celebrated in many countries, but at different times. In Finland, Father's Day is always on the second Sunday of November. This is also done, for example, in Sweden and Estonia.
