# Monday 15.5.2017 (radio)

The discussion about Yle continues. Finns mourn President Mauno Koivisto. A new Finnish mobile game, Big Bang, teaches children particle physics. At the end of the week it is almost summer already.

## Yle must be independent

![Olli Mäenpää](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9613635)

Open image viewer

Professor Olli Mäenpää studied how Yle works. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The discussion about Yle continues in Finland. Professor Olli Mäenpää said on Monday that the way Yle works must be improved. Yle itself asked Mäenpää in the winter to study Yle.

Mäenpää studied, for example, a case in which the prime minister got angry at Yle. Prime Minister Sipilä did not like a news story about a company owned by the prime minister's relatives. Sipilä sent many messages about it to Yle.

Mäenpää says that Yle corrected the news story because the prime minister wanted it to. Mäenpää points out that Yle must not let anyone influence the news. Yle's task is to watch over the work of politicians, Professor Mäenpää says.

The freedom of newspapers, radio and television is very important in Finland. Politicians must not decide what is said in the news.

## Koivisto's book of condolence is opened

![Mauno Koivisto's memorial table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9611874)

Open image viewer

President Mauno Koivisto died on Friday. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Finns mourn President Mauno Koivisto.

Koivisto's book of condolence will be opened on Tuesday at the Presidential Palace. People can go and write their condolences in it.

Finland's current president Sauli Niinistö will write his condolences first. The book of condolence is on display until Friday.

People can also write their condolences online. The website opens and closes at the same time as the book of condolence at the Presidential Palace.

The news of Koivisto's death came at the weekend. Koivisto died on Friday evening in Helsinki. He was 93 years old.

Mauno Koivisto became president in 1982. He was president of Finland for 12 years.

## A mobile game teaches particle physics

![How do you teach particle physics to a child? With a mobile phone game, of course! Guests: Peter Westerbacka (left), brand director of Lightneer, the company that created the game Big Bang Legends, CEO Lauri Järvilehto and communications director Hanna Toivonen. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9613067)

Open image viewer

Peter Westerbacka (left), Lauri Järvilehto and Hanna Toivonen from the company Lightneer talked about the Big Bang game on Yle's morning TV.  Photo: Yle

A new Finnish mobile game, Big Bang, teaches children particle physics.

Adults think particle physics is difficult, but with the help of the game children learn about, for example, elements and atoms.

The makers of the game are Finnish experts who also made the game Angry Birds. They talked about the new Big Bang game on Yle's morning TV.

The makers of the game said that the children had fun when they tested the game. The children did not even notice that they were learning particle physics at the same time.
