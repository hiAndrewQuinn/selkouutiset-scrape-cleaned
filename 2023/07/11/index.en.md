## Sweden's NATO membership

![Erdogan and Kristersson shake hands while Stoltenberg stands in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6068,h_3413,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114056564ac2e0219082/1703613269)

The Turkish President, the NATO Secretary General and the Swedish Prime Minister met in Vilnius. Photo: Filip Singer / EPA

Sweden is becoming a member of the NATO defence alliance.

The matter became clear on Monday, when the Turkish president promised to approve Sweden's NATO membership. The intention is that the Turkish Parliament decides on the matter as quickly as possible.

The leaders of Turkey, Sweden and NATO negotiated on Monday in the Lithuanian capital Vilnius. Today, Tuesday, the NATO defence alliance summit began in Vilnius.

Turkey and Hungary are the only NATO countries that have not so far approved Sweden joining NATO. Now Hungary also intends to approve Sweden's NATO membership.

## Niinistö's assessment

![Sauli Niinistö speaks in front of microphones.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5117,h_2868,x_0,y_105/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114087364ad2209da39e/1689068086)

President Sauli Niinistö says that Sweden's NATO membership is very good news for Finland. Niinistö hopes that membership will happen soon.

Finland and Sweden applied for NATO membership at the same time last May. The reason was Russia's war of attack in Ukraine. Finland and Sweden estimated that they would be in a safer position if they joined the defence alliance.

Finland's NATO membership was already realised in April, but because of Turkey, Sweden's NATO membership has been delayed.

## Racism debate

![Finance Minister Riikka Purra in the plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki on 27 June 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4893,h_2752,x_0,y_368/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1136819649ee46c37db5/1688134777)

Finance Minister Riikka Purra. Photo: Eeva-Maria Brotherus / Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö says that the Finnish government should state its own position on racism. In Niinistö's view, it is wise for the government to say that it does not accept racism at all.

In Finland, there has been talk in recent days about racist writings that have been linked to Minister Riikka Purra. Purra is the chair of the Finns Party.

Purra apologised today, Tuesday, for old social media comments. Purra says that the comments are stupid, and that she no longer accepts them.

## Para athletics World Championships

![Leo-Pekka Tähti on a track in Tokyo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_231/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-848835612f5a99bc8b4/1630493435)

The para athletics World Championships are under way in Paris, France. The world championships continue until next Monday.

In para sport, athletes who have some disability compete. For example, track racers compete for speed in wheelchairs.

Many Finns can be seen in the para athletics World Championships. For example, Finland's track racers are world class.

Yle is showing the para athletics World Championships. The competitions can be watched, for example, on Yle Areena.
