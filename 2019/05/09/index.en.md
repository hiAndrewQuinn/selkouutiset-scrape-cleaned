# Thursday 9.5.2019 (radio)

5 parties are negotiating the government programme. The Bank of Finland wants a debt ceiling for Finns. In Oulu, 1 of the 8 men who raped and sexually abused a 13-year-old girl was sentenced to prison. The EU is more popular in Finland than ever before. Yle's Euro Election Machine has opened online.

## Parties negotiate Finland's goals

![Party leaders taking part in the government negotiations at Säätytalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10774552)

5 parties have started negotiations on the goals of the new government in Helsinki.

They are negotiating 8 big issues, for example how climate change can be stopped. The negotiations also consider how Finland's economy will be run in the coming years and how there will be enough jobs for Finns.

The negotiations are led by the SDP. The Centre, the Greens, the Left Alliance and the Swedish People's Party are also taking part.

The parties are trying to agree on a government programme, that is, a plan for how Finland will be run for the next 4 years. If the parties reach an agreement, they will together form Finland's next government.

## The Bank of Finland wants a debt ceiling

![A small house on top of banknotes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7972680)

Open image viewer

The Bank of Finland wants a debt ceiling for Finns. Photo: Anssi Leppänen / Yle

The Bank of Finland wants a debt ceiling for Finns. This means that the state sets how much debt a Finn can have.

The Bank of Finland says that housing loans, housing company debt and consumer debt must be included in the debt ceiling.

The Bank of Finland is worried that house prices are rising and that Finns are taking on too much debt. If Finns have too much debt, they may not be able to pay it back. They also cannot use money for spending. If people do not spend money, the whole Finnish economy suffers, the Bank of Finland says.

## Prison sentence for the Oulu sex crimes

![A man of Afghan background is suspected of serious sexual abuse of a child and aggravated rape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10751204)

Open image viewer

The man was sentenced to 4 years in prison. Photo: Mari Jäntti / Yle

In Oulu, a court has sentenced to prison one of the 8 men who raped and sexually abused a 13-year-old girl. The man is a 21-year-old asylum seeker from Afghanistan. The court ordered him to prison for 4 years.

The court said that the crime was serious, because the young girl could not say what she thought and could not make decisions very well.

In total, 8 foreign men used the girl for sex over many months. All the men came to Finland as asylum seekers or refugees. The other 7 men will go to court later.

## The EU is popular in Finland

![Celebrating Europe Day at Narinkkatori in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10774200)

Open image viewer

Europe Day has been celebrated today, Thursday, in Finland and elsewhere in the European Union.

The European Union is now more popular in Finland than ever before.

In a study by a Finnish organisation, 56 per cent of Finns said that Finland's EU membership is a good thing. 13 per cent said that it is a bad thing.
