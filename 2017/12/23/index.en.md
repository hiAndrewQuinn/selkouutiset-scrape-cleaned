# Saturday 23.12.2017 (radio)

Bad driving conditions have disrupted holiday traffic. A Finnair plane has had to make a stopover in Oslo. Gift cards are popular at Christmas. Christmas decorations have all kinds of history. It will be freezing on Christmas Eve.

## There was bad driving weather in the Christmas holiday traffic

![A car skids in winter conditions](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9900708)

The Christmas holiday traffic has been busy on Saturday. Bad driving conditions have disrupted traffic, and several small traffic accidents have happened.

Two people have been seriously injured. By the evening no other bad accidents were known.

## A crack in a window forced a flight to make a stopover

![The Finnair logo on a plane's tail.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9845256)

Open image viewer

The Finnair plane had to make a stopover in Norway because of a crack in a window. Photo: Russell Hart / AOP

A Finnair plane has had to make a stopover in Oslo. On Friday the plane was on its way from Manchester in Britain to Helsinki. But there was a crack in the window of the plane's cockpit, and the plane made a stopover in Norway.

The plane's windscreen has many layers, and the crack was in the outer layers. The cause of the crack is not yet known.

The plane's passengers spent the night in a hotel in Oslo. The passengers flew to Finland on Saturday.

## A gift card is a welcome Christmas present

![Santa Claus decorations on a department store sales table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9992147)

Open image viewer

A gift card is a good present both for the person who gets it and for the giver.

Gift cards are very popular Christmas presents. A gift card for a shop or a department store is a present that young people especially wish for. With the card you can buy exactly what you want yourself. The card is also an easy choice for parents, relatives and friends.

A gift card is often loaded with a few dozen euros. Some people give a gift card that can be worth several hundred euros. Then the gift card is used to buy, for example, a television or a computer.

Shops are also happy with gift cards. The cards reduce the exchanging of presents.

## Christmas decorations have all kinds of history

![A Christmas light.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9353234)

Open image viewer

A Christmas star in the window is a custom that came to Finland from Germany through Sweden. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Christmas decorations have a lot of history. For example, a star is hung at the top of the Christmas tree or in the window. The window star came to Finland after the Second World War. The star is connected to Christmas because of the so-called Star of Bethlehem.

Candles burn because they can drive away the darkness. In Finland, as late as the 1970s, Christmas trees had ordinary candles with a live flame. They caused many fires. There were electric candles in New York in America already at the end of the 1800s.

![Christmas decorations](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9944827)

Open image viewer

An old Christmas decoration, the flag string, is popular again. They sold out in the shops already in October. Photo: Viivi Sihvonen / Yle

This year the blue and white flag string has come back into fashion. The reason is the 100th anniversary of independent Finland. The flag strings sold out in the shops already in October. The flag string was also a popular decoration during the war years, because it was cheap.

The most important decoration is the Christmas tree itself. It came to Finland from Sweden at the end of the 1800s. At first there were Christmas trees only in wealthy homes. It became a tradition for the whole nation only in the 1900s.

## On Christmas Eve the weather is dry and freezing

![The snow situation in Finland on 23 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9993202)

This was the snow situation on the day before Christmas Eve. The situation may already be different at Christmas. Photo: Yle

Christmas Eve will be spent in dry, freezing weather in almost the whole country. In Lapland it may be 20 degrees below zero.

In the south-western part the temperature may be above zero.
