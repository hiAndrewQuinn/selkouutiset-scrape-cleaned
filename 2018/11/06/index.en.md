# Tuesday 6.11.2018 (radio)

Almost every 4th long-distance train in Finland is late. Many unemployment funds are cutting their membership fees. Flu vaccinations are already under way in many municipalities. Climate and the environment will feature at this year's Presidential Palace reception.

## Yle investigated train delays

![Atmosphere on a railway platform](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10492881)

Open image viewer

A train tracker that shows train movements and delays. Photo: Asmo Raimoaho / Yle

 Almost every 4th long-distance train in Finland is more than 5 minutes late. This is shown by data that Yle collected.

For example, the morning train from Turku to Helsinki carries many commuters. The train has been more than 5 minutes late almost every second morning. Not everyone makes it to work or to a meeting.

The reasons for the delays are track works and faults in the system that directs traffic. The biggest single reason is that another train is late. 90 per cent of Finland's rail network is single-track. Finland's delay figures are high compared with the rest of Europe.

You can see on yle.fi which trains are often late and by how much.

## Many unemployment funds' fees are falling

![Form for applying for unemployment benefit](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8332119)

Open image viewer

Improving employment is also reflected in unemployment fund fees. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Many unemployment funds are cutting membership fees. About 75 per cent of unemployment funds plan to cut fees next year. The reason is that employment has improved.

The Unemployment Funds' Joint Organisation says that membership fees fall by 10 per cent on average. About 2 million people belong to unemployment funds in Finland. If the monthly salary is 3,000 euros, the membership fee falls by 17 euros on average.

Fees fall the most in the Paper Workers' fund, by 39 per cent. In all sectors, unemployment fund fees do not fall.

## Flu vaccinations have started

![A child is given a nasal spray.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10493838)

Open image viewer

Here a child is being given a seasonal flu vaccine. The vaccine is in a nasal spray. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

 Flu vaccinations are already under way in many municipalities. In Helsinki, for example, vaccinations of risk groups are this week.

More children than before now get a free flu vaccination. Children get the vaccination up to the age of 6. Previously, only healthy children under 3 were vaccinated free of charge.

## The theme of the Presidential Palace reception is environment and climate

![Presidential Palace reception 2014](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10009264)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ari Lahti / AOP

Climate and the environment will feature at this year's Presidential Palace reception.

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio invite 1,700 guests to the Independence Day reception. Among the guests are Finns who have worked for the climate and the environment. War veterans and Lotta Svärd members are guests of honour.

The environment theme is seen in the Presidential Palace's food and decorations. Haukio's party dress also respects the theme: Aalto University is making the dress from ecological ioncell fibre. The raw material of the fibre is domestic birch.
