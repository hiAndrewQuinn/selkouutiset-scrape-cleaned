# Thursday 17.5.2018 (radio)

Health companies. Workers from abroad. Emissions from heavy traffic. The ruins of Turku. The heatwave is giving way.

## Terveystalo is expanding

![The Attendo sign](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10209153)

Terveystalo is buying part of Attendo. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

A really big health company is being born in Finland, as Terveystalo buys part of Attendo. Attendo is selling its health services. Attendo will continue its care services, for example care for the elderly.

The Finnish Competition and Consumer Authority still has to approve the deal.

## It will be made easier for foreign workers to come

![Reinforcing a concrete element.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6207614)

Open image viewer

There is a shortage of workers in the construction sector. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

Finland's economy is improving and companies are doing better.

In some fields there is a shortage of workers. That is why Finland is making it easier for people from outside the EU area to come to Finland to work. Before, the ELY Centre checked whether a company could hire a worker from outside the EU.

Now the ELY Centre has announced that there are many jobs for which a long check is no longer needed. For example, it is now easier to get work as a restaurant worker or a cleaner.

## Emission limits for big vehicles

![In the picture, a lorry drives on a rainy road](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7271042)

Open image viewer

The carbon emissions of big lorries and trucks must be reduced. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

The European Union is setting emission limits for big lorries and trucks. Heavy traffic must cut its carbon emissions by a third by 2030.

Car makers must now come up with ways to bring down the emissions of new diesel vehicles.

Sisu brand lorries are made in Finland. At the Sisu factory in Karjaa, people believe that the new limits are a good thing.

Vehicles are already being made electric or hybrid rather than diesel, says Sisu's CEO Timo Korhonen.

## You can go and see the ruins of Turku

![Turku from the 1300s is revealed under the floor of the Katedralskolan gym.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10079829)

Open image viewer

The ruins tell about life in the 1300s. Photo: Jouni Koutonen / Yle

Everyone can go and see the medieval ruins found in Turku.

Ruins from the 1300s were found under the gym of Katedralskolan when the school was being renovated.

The ruins tell about life in the Middle Ages.

You can join a free tour in June and July, in a small group led by a guide.

## The heatwave is giving way, but the warmth stays

![Young people playing volleyball on the beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10206999)

Open image viewer

The heatwave is giving way, but the weather stays summery. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The heatwave that has lasted almost a week will leave Finland during tomorrow, Friday.

 At the weekend, however, high pressure will start to strengthen again and then the weather will be sunny again.
