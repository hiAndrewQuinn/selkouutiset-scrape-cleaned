# Wednesday 14.3.2018 (radio)

Parliament has debated the social and health care reform. Finland is the happiest country in the world. British scientist Stephen Hawking has died. Skier Ilkka Tuomisto won a bronze medal at the Paralympics.

## The social and health care debate

![the Parliament chamber](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10117090)

Open image viewer

Parliament debated the social and health care reform. Photo: Yle

Parliament has debated the social and health care reform. "Sote" means social services and health services.

The government proposes freedom of choice for social and health services. It means that a person can choose for themselves where they get care. They can choose public or private services.

The opposition parties say that freedom of choice will be expensive and clients' fees will go up. The government says that freedom of choice will not raise prices.

The government wants Parliament to approve the social and health care laws in June.

## Happy Finland

![A man swims in a hole in the ice at Hernesaari in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10115791)

Finland is the happiest country in the world.

The information comes from the UN's happiness report. The UN, or United Nations, publishes the report every year. The report is called The World Happiness Report in English.

The UN studied how happy people are in more than 150 countries. The researchers looked into, for example, how long people live and whether there is corruption in the country. For the first time, immigrants were also included in the study.

Finland was the best country in the study. The other Nordic countries are also happy countries. After Finland came Norway, Denmark and Iceland.

## Stephen Hawking

![Stephen Hawking](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10115729)

Open image viewer

Stephen Hawking kept on working even though he had a difficult illness. Photo: Andrew Cowie / AFP

Then news from the world of science.

British scientist Stephen Hawking has died. He was 76 years old.

Hawking was a physicist. He studied space. Hawking also wanted to tell ordinary people what, for example, black holes in space are.

Hawking had a good sense of humour. He also took part in funny TV programmes, for example The Simpsons.

Hawking did a lot of work even though he had a bad illness. Because of the illness, Hawking got around in a wheelchair.

## A bronze medal for Finland

![Ilkka Tuomisto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9464698)

Open image viewer

Skier Ilkka Tuomisto. Photo: Lehtikuva / Handout

Finally, sport.

Finland has won a bronze medal at the Paralympics. Disabled athletes compete at the Paralympics.

Finland won a bronze medal when skier Ilkka Tuomisto was third in the 1.5-kilometre standing ski race. A Canadian skier also got a bronze medal, because he crossed the finish line at the same time as Tuomisto.

The winner of the race was a skier from Kazakhstan. He got a gold medal. The silver medal went to a Japanese skier.

The Winter Paralympics are in Pyeongchang, South Korea. The Olympics were also held in the same place in February.
