# Thursday 3.5.2018 (radio)

Summer holidays. Sauna. Air quality. Spring work. Heat in Pakistan.

**Moving the summer holidays**

![A schoolchild floats on a swimming mattress on a lake](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10168113)

Open image viewer

The summer holidays of comprehensive school and upper secondary school pupils now start at the beginning of June. Photo: Kirsi Matson-Mäkelä / Yle

Moving the school summer holidays would be a good thing for tourism and employment.

The Ministry of Employment and the Economy says that tourism income could grow by more than 200 million euros if the summer holidays of comprehensive and upper secondary school pupils start in the middle of June.

The ministry believes that families would travel more within Finland if the summer holiday were later in the summer. Then tourist destinations could stay open longer in August, and more foreign tourists would also come.

The Ministry of Employment and the Economy thinks that moving the summer holidays should be discussed in the next government negotiations.

**New health benefits of sauna**

![A sauna stove with a stone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8079249)

Open image viewer

Many people take a sauna whisk with them into the sauna. Photo: Tarja Nyyssönen / Yle

New information has been obtained about the health benefits of sauna. Frequent sauna use clearly reduces the risk of a stroke.

You must go to the sauna at least twice a week, so that the risk of a stroke goes down.

The more often you go to the sauna, the better.

Researchers believe that the benefit comes from the fact that sauna use lowers blood pressure. This has been studied for 15 years already.

**Finland has the best air quality in the world**

![Air quality meters.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5331465)

Open image viewer

Devices like these measure the amount of fine particles in the air. Photo: YLE / Ari Vihanta

People in Finland can breathe the best air in the world.

Finland's air quality is the best in the world, says the Finnish Meteorological Institute. Here, the air contains on average 6 micrograms of fine particles per cubic metre. That is the lowest figure for any country in the world.

Almost as few fine particles are in the air in Estonia and the other Nordic countries. The air quality is worst in the Middle East, the Far East and Africa. Here the air quality stays good, because there are few inhabitants and environmental protection is effective.

**Spring work cannot start yet**

![A tractor sowing in a field](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8859606)

Open image viewer

The fields are still too wet. Spring work is not possible yet. Photo: Timo Leponiemi / Yle

Farmers still have to wait before spring work starts. The fields are not yet ready for sowing.

There was a lot of snow in the winter, and a lot of rain has fallen in the spring. That is why the fields have dried slowly.

In most areas, however, spring is coming at the normal time. In the central and northern parts of the country, spring work can start about a week later than usual. Warmer weather is coming at the weekend.

**Pakistan's spring heat record of 50.2 degrees**

![People swimming](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10188707)

Open image viewer

In Pakistan last summer it was 50 degrees. Now 50 degrees has already been measured in the spring. Photo: EPA/ILYAS SHEIKH

Another shocking piece of news from Pakistan. There April was the hottest ever.

In the southern parts of Pakistan the temperature rose above 50 degrees. The highest temperature was 50.2 degrees.

That is the April heat record for the whole world. In Pakistan there are now fears that the summer will be a record-breaking hot one.
