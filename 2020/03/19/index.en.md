# Thursday 19.3.2020 (radio)

Finland has closed its borders. People aged 70 are being given more detailed instructions. Ski resorts may stay open. Tissues must not be put in biowaste. Yle is adding more Finnish music on its radio channels.

## Finland has closed its borders

![A fence on the Finland-Sweden border in Tornio, with the town of Haparanda in the background](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3527,h_1984,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6553285e7358b3f3db6/1584617819)

Finland has closed its borders because of the coronavirus. You cannot cross the border freely. Everyone must show identity papers at the Finnish border.

Only Finnish citizens and foreigners who live in Finland may enter Finland. Foreigners who have a job in Finland may also enter Finland. Foreign tourists may not enter Finland.

## Instructions for people over 70

![An elderly person walks along a corridor with a walker at the Myyrmäki elderly centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11252694)

Open image viewer

People over 70 must be protected well from the coronavirus. Photo: Eleni Paspatis / Yle

The government has made instructions for people over 70 because of the coronavirus. People over 70 must avoid other people, so that the coronavirus does not infect people over 70.

Municipalities are opening a telephone service, from which people over 70 get help if they need, for example, food from the shop or medicines from the pharmacy.

It is good for people over 70 to stay at home. They may go outside if they avoid other people.

People over 70 must be protected a lot from the coronavirus, because the coronavirus can be dangerous for people over 70.

## Do not put tissues in biowaste

![Rubbish in a waste bin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10602559)

Open image viewer

Put tissues in mixed waste. Photo: Paula Collin / Yle

Waste companies have given new instructions on handling and sorting waste because of the coronavirus.

Tissues must now be put in mixed waste. Tissues must not be put in biowaste, because viruses may spread more easily in biowaste than in mixed waste. For example, animals can spread biowaste.

## There are many people at ski resorts

Ski resorts in Lapland may stay open, even though gatherings are restricted in Finland.

The town centres are quiet now because of the coronavirus, but there are many people at the ski resorts in Lapland. The regional administrative agency says that the ski slopes may stay open.

Many restaurants at the ski resorts have closed, because people are not going to restaurants now. People are avoiding close contact, that is, they are not going close to other people.

Yle is adding more Finnish music on radio. Yle will start playing more Finnish music on its radio channels Radio Suomi and YleX.

Finland is now in exceptional conditions. Yle wants to create a feeling of closeness and continuity.

At the same time Yle supports Finnish music creators. Yle pays compensation to music creators when music is played on the radio.
