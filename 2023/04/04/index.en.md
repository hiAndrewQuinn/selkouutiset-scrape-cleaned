# Tuesday 4.4.2023 (radio)

Finland is now a NATO member. Sweden is happy about Finland's membership and waits for its own membership. Rejected ballots. Part-time work. The weather.

## Finland is a NATO member

![Flags of Finland, NATO and the United States.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_218/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1095301642c1deabb63c/1680612903)

The Finnish flag is now one of many at NATO headquarters in Brussels. Outside the headquarters 31 NATO country flags are flying. Photo: Johanna Geron / AFP

Finland is now a full member of the NATO defence alliance. At the same time NATO's security guarantees for Finland came into force.

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto sealed the membership when he handed over the accession documents to the USA in the afternoon in Brussels. The documents were received by Foreign Minister Antony Blinken at NATO headquarters.

After that Blinken declared Finland a NATO member. The Finnish flag was raised on the flagpole at NATO headquarters while the Finnish national anthem was played. So there are 31 flags at the moment.

The President of the Republic, Sauli Niinistö, and Defence Minister Antti Kaikkonen were also present at the ceremony.

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg said that it is an honour to welcome Finland to NATO. "Finland now has the world's strongest friends and allies," he said.

Russia's attack on Ukraine led Finland and Sweden to apply for NATO. Finland and Sweden had not been militarily allied until then.

## Sweden and NATO

![Billström and Haavisto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_260/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106950463e21dde1acda/1675764952)

Swedish Foreign Minister Tobias Billström visited Helsinki on 6–7 February. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Swedish Foreign Minister Tobias Billström says that Finland's entry to NATO is not a bitter day for Sweden. - On the contrary, we are happy for our brother country Finland, Billström said.

He also said that Finland will be a strong voice in NATO in favour of Sweden's membership. Finland has already ratified Sweden's membership today.

Billström said that Sweden has already done everything that NATO and Turkey have required. The NATO Secretary General said that he is completely sure that Sweden will get into NATO. Only the ratifications of Hungary and Turkey are still missing.

## Rejected votes

![A ballot paper is dropped into a ballot box at a polling place in Lempäälä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_3,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90524861ed7bef7bacc/1642953992)

The voter must write only the candidate's number in the middle of the open circle on the ballot paper, without any other marks. If the voter writes a wrong or messy mark on the ballot paper, they can ask an election official for a new ballot paper. In that case the spoiled paper is torn up in front of the election official. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

In the parliamentary elections on Sunday about 16,000 votes were rejected, Statistics Finland says. There were almost 2,000 fewer rejected votes than in the last parliamentary elections four years ago.

In total, just over 3 million people with the right to vote voted on Sunday.

Voter turnout was close to 71.9 percent.

## Part-time work

![Grocery shopping on a supermarket checkout belt.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5166,h_2906,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-776205602fa20e1c271/1613734998)

Part-time work is also done a lot in retail. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

More and more part-time workers did part-time work of their own free will last year, says Statistics Finland. A quarter of part-time workers want full-time work. A year before, almost a third wanted full-time work.

Last year just under a fifth of all employees did part-time work. Women made up 24 percent of part-time workers last year and men 12 percent.

Based on the number of employees, part-time work was done most in health services and social services.
