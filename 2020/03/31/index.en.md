# Tuesday 31.3.2020 (radio)

The government is continuing many restrictions because of the coronavirus. More than 303,000 people are already in co-operation negotiations. Sole traders can apply for support from municipalities because of the coronavirus. The Finnish Upper Secondary Students' Union wants to keep the graduation celebration despite the coronavirus. Winter speed limits will start to be removed this week.

## The restrictions continue

![Government meeting at the Säätytalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11276219)

The government is continuing many restrictions because of the coronavirus. The restrictions will last until 13 May.

For example, internal border controls, restrictions on gatherings and distance teaching in schools will continue. The restrictions will last at least a month longer than was previously said.

For public events and restaurants, the restrictions are in force until the end of May. The government is giving a decree today on closing restaurants across the whole country. However, food may still be sold to take away.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the restrictions have already started to work, but they must be continued.

## Now there are many co-operation negotiations and layoffs

The corona epidemic has affected workplaces. More than 303,000 people are already in co-operation negotiations, that is, negotiations between employers and staff. The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment says that more than 15,300 employees have been laid off.

These figures only show the increase in numbers. The notices cannot be separated into those caused by the coronavirus and those caused by other reasons.

Almost 3,300 employers have reported co-operation negotiations.

## Sole traders can get support

![The Neljä Vuodenaikaa restaurant in Tampere's Market Hall was closed on 25 March 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_141/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6577925e7c4d30089c5/1588749612)

Open image viewer

The Neljä Vuodenaikaa restaurant in Tampere's Market Hall was closed on 25 March. Photo: Miikka Varila / Yle

Sole traders can apply for support from municipalities because of the coronavirus. The support is 2,000 euros.

The support is granted for costs that come from the running of the business. A sole trader is an entrepreneur who has no other employees.

The condition for the grant is that the financial situation has got much worse. Turnover must also have fallen.

The support is discretionary, which means it is not given automatically. Support cannot be granted if the company was already in financial difficulties before the corona crisis. In addition, support is not given to a company that has tax debts.

## Holding graduation celebrations is uncertain

![Upper secondary school graduation ceremony.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8909465)

Open image viewer

Holding graduation celebrations is still uncertain. Photo: Kaisa Siren / AOP

The Finnish Upper Secondary Students' Union wants the graduation celebrations to be held despite the coronavirus. The union demands that the spring graduation celebrations, that is, the graduation days, are not cancelled.

The union wants the graduation day to be moved to one and the same day across the whole of Finland. The graduation celebrations could be, for example, in early autumn.

Organising the graduation days is still uncertain, because the situation is exceptional.

Some schools have considered organising virtual graduation days, for example. Virtual graduation days can be held online.
