# Sunday 16.9.2018 (radio)

Hurricane Mangkhut is now blowing in China. In the United States people are preparing for floods raised by storm Florence. The result of the Swedish election was confirmed. The autumn matriculation exams begin. During Mobility Week people are encouraged to leave the car at home. New fish species were found in the depths of the Pacific Ocean.

## Hurricane arrived in China

![Firefighters on a flooded street in Hong Kong.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10407209)

Open image viewer

Hurricane Mangkhut also brought floods to the streets of Hong Kong. Photo: Alex Hofford / EPA

Hurricane Mangkhut has passed Hong Kong and arrived in southern China. In Guangdong province almost half a million people have been evacuated out of the storm's path.

In the Philippines the hurricane claimed 50 lives. Most were killed in landslides.

The number of dead in the Philippines may still rise, because aid workers have not yet reached all the disaster areas.

## The United States prepares for floods

![A female Red Cross worker stands in the hall of an evacuation centre with her back to the camera, wearing a red jacket with the Red Cross logo. In the background two men sit on benches and a woman stands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10407177)

Open image viewer

Evacuation centres have been set up, for example in schools in the storm area. Photo: Nina Svahn / Yle

In the United States storm Florence threatens to raise powerful floods. In North Carolina as much as over a metre of rain has fallen.

Almost a million people are still without electricity. About 20,000 people have fled their homes to flood shelters.

More than 10 people have died because of the storm.

## Swedish election result is ready

![Stefan Löfven speaks to the media on the way to an election night party in Stockholm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10395724)

Open image viewer

Leader of the Swedish Social Democrats Stefan Löfwen. Photo: Jonas Ekstromer/ / AOP

The result of the Swedish parliamentary election has now been confirmed. The checking count of the votes did not bring changes to the support figures.

The Social Democrats got the most votes, over 28 percent. The Moderate Party got the second most votes. The Sweden Democrats were the third most popular party.

Voter turnout rose to over 87 percent.

## Autumn matriculation exams begin

![](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5573014)

Open image viewer

The first exam in the autumn matriculation exams is the mother tongue literacy exam. Photo: YLE

The autumn matriculation examination begins tomorrow, Monday. First up is the new mother tongue literacy exam.

The matriculation exams are now digital, which means the tasks are answered on a computer. Only the mathematics exam is still done on paper.

More than 37,000 candidates have registered for the matriculation examination this autumn.

## Give your car a day off

![a bicycle and public transport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9875169)

Open image viewer

During Mobility Week everyone can think about how they themselves get around and how it affects the environment. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

Mobility Week begins today. The international theme week aims to get people to think about how the way they travel affects the environment and society.

Many Finnish cities are offering public transport journeys free or at a reduced price for the week. Discounts are offered, for example, by the Joensuu region and the Lahti region, Oulu and Porvoo, Iisalmi and Vaasa.

The theme week ends next Saturday with the national Car-Free Day.

## New fish species found in the depths of the sea

![surf on a rocky shore](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10244477)

Open image viewer

The new fish species were found at a depth of over 7.5 kilometres. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

3 new fish species have been found on the floor of the Pacific Ocean.

The fish live at a depth of 7.5 kilometres. The fish are see-through and their bodies are very soft.

The new fish species have not yet been given names. For now they are called the pink, blue and purple Atacama snailfish.
