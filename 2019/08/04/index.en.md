# Sunday 4.8.2019 (radio)

Two serious shootings have happened in the USA, one in Texas and one in Ohio. Two people died in a boating accident in the Turku archipelago. London's Heathrow airport is cancelling flights. Estonian Ott Tänak won the Finland World Rally.

## Shooting in Texas

![Two women crying in the street in El Paso.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10907714)

Open image viewer

Texans are mourning the victims of the shooting. Photo: Joel Angel Juarez / AFP

There have been 2 serious shootings in the USA.

The first shooting happened in Texas. There at least 20 people died and almost 30 were injured.

The shooter fired at people in a Walmart department store. The department store had many customers, because it was Saturday.

The place is the city of El Paso, near the Mexican border. Among the dead are 6 Mexican citizens.

The police have arrested a 21-year-old shooter. He did not resist the police.

The act may be a hate crime. Before the act, a racist text appeared online. The text supports white power. The police suspect that the shooter wrote the text.

## Shooting in Ohio

![Map of Ohio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10907816)

The second shooting happened in Dayton, Ohio. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The second shooting in the USA happened in Ohio. There the shooter killed 9 people. Almost 20 people were injured.

The police say that the shooter also died. It is not yet known why he shot people.

The place is in the centre of the city of Dayton. There are many restaurants and bars in the area.

## Boating accident in the Turku archipelago

![Accident boats being towed to a harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10907703)

Open image viewer

The accident boats were taken to a harbour. Photo: Border Guard

A boating accident has happened here in Finland. 2 people died in the accident. In addition, 4 people were injured.

The accident happened in the Turku archipelago on Saturday. In the accident a motorboat and a sailing boat collided with each other.

The police do not yet know what caused the accident. The police do not believe that alcohol was the cause. There was no technical fault in the boats either.

The police continue their investigations.

## Strike at Heathrow airport

![Heathrow terminal](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10587421)

Heathrow airport in London is cancelling flights. On Monday and Tuesday almost 180 flights will be cancelled.

Heathrow is cancelling flights because of a strike by employees.

## Finland World Rally

![Ott Tänak in Jyväskylä in 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10907047)

Open image viewer

Ott Tänak was the fastest driver in Jyväskylä. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

The Finland World Rally, the big event of Finnish motor sport, has ended.

The winner was Estonian Ott Tänak. Tänak drives for Toyota. He also won the Finland World Rally last year.

This year the second fastest driver in the rally was the Finn Esapekka Lappi. He drives a Citroën. The third fastest driver was also a Finn, Toyota's Jari-Matti Latvala.

The Finland World Rally is driven in Jyväskylä. Therefore the competition is often called the Jyväskylä World Rally.
