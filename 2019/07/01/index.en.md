# Monday 1.7.2019 (radio)

Finland has started its EU presidency. The new taxi act has weakened customers' trust in taxi services. Flying a drone will require an exam in a year's time. There will be no restrictions on the use of electric scooters. The weather is about to change.

## Finland's EU presidency begins

![EU leaders at a round table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10854003)

The EU leaders' summit was held in Brussels on 20 and 21 June. Photo: Stephanie Lecocq / EPA-EFE

Finland has started its presidency of the European Union today.

One important matter is climate protection. Finland's goal is to reach a political agreement that the EU will be carbon neutral by 2050.

Finland's goal is also to reach agreement on financing and on the EU's next enlargement.

The presidency lasts until the end of the year.

## Taxis are not as popular as before

![Taxis at a taxi rank](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10856197)

Open image viewer

The taxi reform did not increase the popularity of taxis. Photo: Antti Lähteenmäki / Yle

The government will start to fix problems in taxi traffic, perhaps at the turn of the year. Possible changes to the law concern, for example, the safety of taxi traffic and its availability in different areas.

A survey by the Taxi Federation says that the new taxi act has weakened customers' trust in taxis. The taxi act came into force a year ago.

The new taxi act, among other things, freed taxi pricing and competition.

53 percent of respondents have a negative attitude to the current taxi act. Only 3 percent have increased their use of taxis, and 22 percent have reduced their use of taxis.

## Flying a drone will require an exam

![Flying drones will become more regulated from July 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10852639)

Open image viewer

Flying drones will become illegal without an exam. Photo: Ville Tiirikainen / Yle

Using a drone, that is, a robot aircraft, will require an exam in a year's time. A new EU regulation specifies this.

Every drone pilot must complete an online course and an online exam. In addition, drone pilots must register.

In Finland the number of drone hobbyists has grown in recent years. The Finnish Transport and Communications Agency estimates that there may be as many as 100,000 drones in Finland.

## No restrictions on electric scooters

![Electric scooters](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10852494)

Open image viewer

Electric scooters have been criticised, but no restrictions on their use are coming. Photo: Markku Pitkänen / Yle

No restrictions on the use of electric scooters are coming in Finland. In other parts of Europe, restrictions have been demanded, for example in Sweden and France.

In Finland the law and regulations allow light electric vehicles.

There are already about 2,000 electric scooters in Finland. The scooters can already be rented in Helsinki, Espoo, Tampere and Turku.

Only Tampere has drawn up separate guidelines for the scooters. There are instructions on parking and speeds in certain places. Elsewhere in the country, scooters have the same traffic rules as bicycles.

## The weather changes

![Rainfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10856171)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

The weather is about to change. From Tuesday the weather will cool down and become clearly more unsettled across the whole country.

On Tuesday the rain may be quite heavy in the southern part of the country. Thunderstorm showers will also occur. Further north the rain is more widespread.

The biggest change is in the southern and central parts of the country. On Wednesday temperatures will drop from heat levels and be 13–18 degrees.

It will be cool in the north all week. There the highest temperatures may stay at only about 10 degrees.
