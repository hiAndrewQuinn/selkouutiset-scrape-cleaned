## The Finns Party got an MP back

![Arja Juvonen arrived at the meeting of the Finns Party parliamentary group in Helsinki on 13 June](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9671523)

Open image viewer

MP Arja Juvonen returned to the Finns Party. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The Finns Party parliamentary group has grown by 1 MP. MP Arja Juvonen is returning to the Finns Party parliamentary group.

Juvonen left the group after the party conference. At the same time, about 20 MPs left the party and founded the New Alternative group. Juvonen herself did not join anything, but has now decided to return to the Finns Party.

The Finns Party moved into opposition after the government crisis. The group now has 17 MPs.

## The US entry ban on Muslims temporarily came into force

![Judges of the US Supreme Court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9651026)

Open image viewer

The Supreme Court allows the entry ban temporarily.  Photo: Shawn Thew / EPA

The US Supreme Court has given permission to restrict the entry of citizens of 6 Muslim countries. The entry ban partly prevents travel from Iran, Yemen, Libya, Somalia, Sudan and Syria to the USA. But the Supreme Court says that the ban does not apply to people who have, for example, family in the United States.

The ban lasts for 90 days. At the same time, the entry ban stops the reception of all refugees for 120 days. The decision is temporary, because the Supreme Court will make the final decision on the entry ban only in the autumn. The entry ban has been an important issue for President Donald Trump.

## The Finland First camp was taken down

![Police took down the Finland First demonstration in Railway Square in Helsinki on 26 June 2017. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9691108)

Open image viewer

Police arrested 4 demonstrators.  Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The anti-immigration Finland First camp in Railway Square in Helsinki has been taken down. The police and the City of Helsinki decided this, because the demonstrators have assaulted people. For example, at the weekend police received reports of 3 assaults.

Taking down the camp met resistance, and police arrested 4 demonstrators.

There has also been an asylum seekers' camp in Railway Square, which may stay there.

## Google got giant fines from the EU

![Competition Commissioner Margrethe Vestager.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9692321)

Open image viewer

The EU gave Google big fines.  Photo: Olivier Hoslet / EPA

The search engine company Google has received big fines from the European Union. Google has to pay fines of 2.4 billion, that is 2,400 million, euros for breaking competition rules. The fine is the biggest in the EU's history.

The EU thinks that Google has raised its own Google Shopping service to the top of the results in online searches. This has prevented fair competition.

Google does not accept the fine.

## A great honour for ice hockey player Teemu Selänne

![Teemu Selänne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9691227)

Open image viewer

Teemu Selänne.  Photo: Lehtikuva

Teemu Selänne, perhaps Finland's best-known ice hockey player, has been chosen for the hall of fame of the North American professional league NHL, the Hockey Hall of Fame. The honour is significant, because before only 1 Finn, Jari Kurri, has got into this hall of fame.

In his career, Teemu Selänne played 1,451 games in the NHL and scored 684 goals. In his career he won the championship, the Stanley Cup, once, in 2007.
