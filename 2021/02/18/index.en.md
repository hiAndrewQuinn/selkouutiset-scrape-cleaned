## THL wants bars in the capital region closed

![People wearing masks left the metro on 4 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_336/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-774078602a2d3ebef40/1614087305)

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, recommends that bars and nightclubs in the capital region be closed.

THL wants bars and nightclubs to be closed as soon as possible, for about 3 weeks. The coronavirus is spreading faster than before in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district. The coronavirus often spreads in bars.

THL also recommends that people living in the capital region do not travel to other parts of Finland.

About 244,000 people in Finland have received the first dose of the coronavirus vaccine. This is 14,000 more people than on Wednesday. A little over 70,000 people have received the second dose of the vaccine.

There are 614 new coronavirus infections. Most infections are clearly in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district.

There are 2 new coronavirus deaths in Finland. 137 people are in hospital because of the coronavirus, and 27 of them are in intensive care.

## Finnair's results

![A Finnair aircraft being moved at Helsinki-Vantaa airport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5403,h_3039,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7265885f7b2a40744c2/1601907334)

Airline Finnair's last year was bad because of the coronavirus.

Finnair's result was 600 million euros in the red, so Finnair has lost a lot of money. Finnair's planes had 76 percent fewer passengers last year than the year before.

The number of Finnair employees fell by more than 1,000 people last year.

The coronavirus has greatly reduced flying all over the world. Finnair believes that in the summer the number of air passengers will increase.

## Asylum seekers

Fewer asylum seekers have come to Europe than before.

More than 30 percent fewer people applied for asylum last year in the EU countries, Norway and Switzerland than in 2019. The biggest reason for this is the restrictions because of the coronavirus.

The most asylum applications were made by Syrians. Next came Afghans, Venezuelans, Colombians and Iraqis. These 5 groups made about 40 percent of all asylum applications.
