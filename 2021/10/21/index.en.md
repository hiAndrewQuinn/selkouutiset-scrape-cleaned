## The corona situation in Finland

![Digital corona pass and a phone at a bar counter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_508/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8678886169b5e83481c/1634355773)

With the corona pass, restaurants can operate normally also during a bad corona situation. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The corona situation is still poor in various parts of Finland.

For example, there are many corona patients in Helsinki's hospitals. The corona is now spreading among those who have not received corona vaccinations.

Because of the poor corona situation, the corona restrictions for restaurants are tightening in Uusimaa and South Ostrobothnia. Restaurants must stop selling alcohol at midnight, and they may take fewer customers than usual. The stricter restrictions come into force on Friday.

However, a restaurant does not have to follow corona restrictions if it requires customers to have a corona pass.

## Vaccinations of nurses

![A nurse vaccinating a woman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7988048)

Open image viewer

Corona vaccination is not mandatory for nurses. Photo: EPA

Nurses can spread corona if they have no corona vaccinations.

Yle has received information that, for example, at the service centre in Eurajoki an elderly person died this week who got a corona infection from a nurse. The nurse had not been vaccinated against corona.

The law now requires that a nurse has taken the important vaccinations if they care for weak patients. A nurse must have vaccinations against measles, chickenpox and influenza.

The law does not yet mention the corona vaccine. For example, the Director General of the Institute for Health and Welfare has demanded mandatory corona vaccinations for nurses too.

## Latvia's corona lockdown

![Latvia faces an almost month-long corona lockdown from 21.10.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_9/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-868614616e2bc765b86/1634610359)

Open image viewer

Most shops in Latvia are now closed because of corona. Photo: EPA/TOMS KALNINS

A corona lockdown has begun in Latvia. It lasts 4 weeks.

During the corona lockdown, restaurants and almost all shops are closed. Only grocery shops and pharmacies may be open.

Cultural events and sporting events have also been cancelled. People may not move outdoors at night.

Most Latvian pupils are now in distance learning. Adults are working remotely.

There are as many as 2000 new corona infections a day in Latvia. Latvia has 2 million residents.

Only 50 per cent of Latvia's residents have received 2 corona vaccinations.

## Storm warning

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12155266)

Open image viewer

A storm warning has been issued for the Helsinki region and all of Southern Finland.

A strong rain area arrives in Southern Finland. It rains heavily and the wind is strong.

In Helsinki the sea level may rise by almost a metre. At sea the waves may be up to 4 metres high.

The temperature on Friday in the south is about 5 degrees. In Lapland there is a little frost.

At the weekend the rains end and the weather gets colder.
