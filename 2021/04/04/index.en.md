# Sunday 4.4.2021 (radio)

The number of corona infections is falling. Children have more migraines than before. The Pope urges sharing corona vaccines with poor countries. Tropical forests are disappearing faster than ever.

There are 302 new coronavirus infections in Finland. Most new infections are still in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district.

The number of infections has fallen. Over 2 weeks there are about 1,600 fewer infections than 2 weeks before.

There are now fewer corona patients in the hospitals of the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district than before.

## Children's migraines

![A girl lies on a sofa looking at a mobile phone screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_583/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5147505bb761c322dc8/1680004032)

Open image viewer

Children's night sleep has become shorter and their amount of exercise has decreased. At the same time, screen time has increased. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Children have more migraines than before. A migraine is a severe headache.

Migraines in children have become much more common over the last 20 years. One reason is that children move and sleep less than before. Children also look at mobile phone or computer screens a lot.

Migraines are hereditary. A child often has migraines if their father or mother has migraines. Stress also increases migraines.

A child's migraine often starts in puberty. However, for some children, migraines start already before the age of 4.

## The Pope's speech

![Pope Francis of the Catholic Church.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11870021)

Open image viewer

Pope Francis. Photo: AFP

The Pope has given the traditional Easter speech.

Pope Francis said that corona vaccines must be shared with poor countries. The Pope also said that violence, for example in Syria, Yemen and Libya, must stop.

Pope Francis spoke in St Peter's Basilica in the Vatican. Up to 100,000 people usually attend the Pope's Easter mass. This time, because of corona, only about 200 people were present.

The Pope is the leader of the Catholic Church. 1.3 billion people belong to the Catholic Church.

## Tropical forests

![Forest cut down for palm oil plantations in Indonesia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11869993)

Open image viewer

Forest cut down to make way for palm oil plantations in Indonesia. Photo: Hotli Simanjuntak / EPA

Tropical forests are disappearing faster than before. Last year, people cut down 12 percent more tropical forest than the year before. Forest fires also destroy tropical forest.

The most forest was cut down last year in Brazil. Brazil also had many large forest fires.

In 2 countries, the development has been good. In Indonesia and Malaysia, forest destruction has decreased for many years. Both countries have decided that forests may not be burned as much as before.
