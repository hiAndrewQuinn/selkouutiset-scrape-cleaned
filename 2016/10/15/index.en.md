# Saturday 15.10.2016 (radio)

President Sauli Niinistö hopes that negotiations on the situation in Syria will continue. Black clothes will soon run out in Thailand's shops. Anger is a dangerous emotion. Karoliina Korhonen, the creator of the Finnish Nightmares comic, has received a prize.

## Niinistö spoke about Syria

![President Sauli Niinistö speaking in Santahamina on Saturday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9232583)

Finland's president Sauli Niinistö hopes that negotiations on the situation in Syria will move forward.

Over the weekend the situation in Syria is being discussed in Switzerland. The meeting includes, for example, Russia's foreign minister Sergei Lavrov and the USA's secretary of state John Kerry.

Niinistö does not believe that quick solutions will be found at the meeting. The most important thing is that the talks continue, Niinistö says.

Niinistö is worried about the bombing of the city of Aleppo. The bombs of the Syrian army and Russia kill not only soldiers but also ordinary people, Niinistö says.

The war in Syria has already gone on for almost 6 years.

## Thailand's grief

![Mourning citizens](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9232137)

Open image viewer

Thai people mourn the king in black clothes. Photo: Narong Sangnak / EPA

In Thailand black clothes will soon run out in the shops. Thai people are buying black clothes because the country is in a period of mourning. The king of Thailand, Bhumipol, died on Thursday.

The government of Thailand has warned that black fabric may run out. The government hopes that factories will start making more black fabric than usual.

The prices of black clothes have risen in Thailand. If a shopkeeper raises prices too high, he can get a fine or even go to prison.

Bhumipol was king of Thailand for 70 years.

## Dangerous anger

![A person in a hoodie ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8367797)

Open image viewer

Will he have a heart attack soon? Photo: Enja Heikkilä / Yle

Anger is a dangerous emotion. After a feeling of strong anger, the risk of a heart attack is 2 times higher than usual. The information comes from a large international study.

Hard physical strain also increases the risk of a heart attack. It is a bad thing if a person is also angry during physical strain. Then the risk of a heart attack is really high.

Anger and hard strain raise blood pressure and make the heart beat faster. That is why it is worth taking it easy.

## A prize for the creator of Finnish Nightmares

![A Finnish Nightmares comic panel.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9222144)

Open image viewer

A difficult situation for a Finn. Photo: Finnish Nightmares / Karoliina Korhonen

Comic artist Karoliina Korhonen has received a prize. Korhonen got the Lempi prize at the Kemi comics festival.

Korhonen makes a comic called Finnish Nightmares, that is, Finnish nightmares. In it, a Finn called Matti ends up in situations that are difficult for Finns. For example, he is in a lift or on a bus where there are also other people.

Matti's story started on the internet and on Facebook. A book about Matti has also come out. It tells in a funny way what Finns are like. The book has come out in Finnish and English.
