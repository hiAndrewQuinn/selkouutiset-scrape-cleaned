# Sunday 17.3.2019 (radio)

New information has been obtained about the New Zealand terror attack. People have died in Indonesia because of floods. A Moomin park has opened in Japan. Finland's Valtteri Bottas won the Formula One race in Australia. The driving conditions are bad because of rain.

## New information about the terror attack

![People at a memorial site](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10693781)

Open image viewer

People's grief continues in New Zealand. Photo: Matti Nousiainen

New Zealand's authorities have reported new information about Friday's terror attack.

50 people died in the attack. 40 people are still in hospital.

The people who died were of different ages. The youngest to die was 3 years old, and the oldest was 77.

The terror attack took place on Friday in the city of Christchurch. The shooter attacked 2 mosques.

New Zealand police believe that the shooter carried out the attack alone. The police have arrested the shooter.

The shooter is a 28-year-old Australian man. He is extreme right-wing and a racist.

## Floods in Indonesia

![Map of Indonesia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10693438)

Open image viewer

It has rained dangerously much in the city of Jayapura. Photo: Yle News Graphics

There are severe floods in Indonesia. About 60 people have died in the floods. There are many injured.

The cause of the floods is heavy rain. Because of the heavy rain, the flood water rose quickly. The water surged over houses in the city of Jayapura.

Indonesia is in Asia, and it is an island state. Indonesia includes thousands of islands.

## Japan's Moomin park

![Moomin Valley in Hanno, Japan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10693818)

Open image viewer

Japanese people love Moomins. Photo: Xinhua / AOP

A Moomin park has opened in Japan. It is the first Moomin park outside Finland.

Japan's Moomin park is in the city of Hanno, near the capital, Tokyo.

The Moomin park expects to get a million visitors a year. Visitors can see, for example, the Moomin house.

Finnish Moomins are popular in Japan. The Moomins are fairy-tale characters from Tove Jansson's books.

## Bottas's victory

![Valtteri Bottas celebrates on the podium.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10693469)

Open image viewer

Bottas was the best Formula One driver in Australia. Photo: AOP

Formula One car races have started again. The Formula One season started in Australia.

The winner of the race in Australia was the Finnish driver Valtteri Bottas. Bottas drives a Mercedes car.

Bottas led the race from the start. Bottas also drove the fastest lap of the race.

The next Formula One race is in two weeks' time, in Bahrain. The Formula One season continues until early December.

## Bad driving conditions

![traffic in winter weather](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10590139)

Open image viewer

In the north, more snow is falling. Photo: AOP

The driving conditions are now bad.

Rain will come to Finland on Sunday evening and on Monday. The rain is water or sleet in the south, and sleet or snow in the north.

Water, sleet and snow make the roads slippery. The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns motorists about dangerous driving conditions.

On Tuesday only a little rain will fall. On Wednesday the rain stops and the weather turns a little colder.
