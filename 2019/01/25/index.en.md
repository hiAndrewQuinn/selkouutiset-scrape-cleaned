## The Nordic climate policy

src="https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5531344"

Open image viewer

Carbon dioxide from smoke can be captured. Photo: YLE / Perämeri

The Nordic countries are increasing cooperation against climate change. The prime ministers and environment ministers of the Nordic countries, that is, Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Iceland, met in Helsinki on Friday.

The Nordic countries want to set an example to the rest of the world on climate matters. For example, they want to develop technology for capturing carbon dioxide. This can be done, for example, by capturing carbon dioxide from the chimney of a factory and burying it under the seabed.

Carbon dioxide warms the Earth's climate, which is why new technology is needed. The Nordic countries believe that they can export new kinds of technology to other countries too.

## Britons in Finland

![Finnish Immigration Service logo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10609367)

Open image viewer

Britons in Finland should now register with the Finnish Immigration Service. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Finland wants to make sure that British citizens can stay in Finland after Brexit too.

Brexit means Britain's exit from the EU. Britain leaves the European Union at the end of March.

About 5,000 Britons will need a residence permit in Finland if Britain leaves the EU without an agreement. No agreement has been reached yet.

That is why Finland is planning a law that gives Britons the right to continue staying in Finland at least until the end of 2020. Britons only need to register with the Finnish Immigration Service by the end of March.

## A new Moomin series

![Rosamund Pike, Taron Egerton, Moomin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10615166)

Open image viewer

Rosamund Pike has given the voice to Moominmamma and Taron Egerton to Moomintroll.Photo: Jussi Mankkinen / Yle

A new series has been made about the Moomins. The series was shown for the first time on Friday in Helsinki.

The series is new in many ways. For example, it is three-dimensional, so the audience sees the Moomins as 3D animation.

The series is a Finnish-British collaboration. In the English-language version, the Moomins are voiced by famous British actors.

The makers say that the new series does not spoil Tove Jansson's Moomin stories. The spirit of the stories is the same, even though the characters are three-dimensional.

## Hard frost

![A frosty day in Rovaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10607569)

Open image viewer

In the north it will be 20 to 30 degrees of frost at the weekend. Photo: Perttu Ruokangas / Yle

The weekend will be cold.

In the north there is hard frost for the whole weekend. The frost is about 20 to 30 degrees.

In the south, the coldest day of the weekend is Sunday. The frost may be about 20 degrees.

Next week the frost will ease, which means there will be fewer degrees of frost. On Tuesday and Wednesday there will be only a little frost in Southern Finland.
