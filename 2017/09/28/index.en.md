## Politicians' honesty

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9845049)

Open image viewer

Politicians ramble a lot, but they mostly tell the truth, says a new study.  Photo: Johanna Aulén / Yle

Politicians are more honest than people think, says a new study.

Researchers from the University of Turku checked the statements of Turku politicians in the last municipal elections. Fact-checking was used to find out whether politicians told the truth in their election programmes and in their answers to Yle's election compass.

The research results showed that, for example in election programmes, politicians rambled, which means they talked a lot and for a long time. However, their statements were mostly true.

## Cooperation between authorities

![The Vaasa emergency response centre starts on 25 November](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7639087)

Open image viewer

Finland's emergency response centre system makes cooperation between different authorities easier. Photo: Jarkko Heikkinen / Yle

Cooperation between authorities in Finland is at a high level.

International comparisons show that cooperation between Finnish authorities is efficient and flexible.

Cooperation is made easier, for example, by a shared emergency response centre system.

Police Inspector Marko Savolainen from the National Police Board says that in Finland the authorities must cooperate, because in a bad situation everyone needs the help and expertise of the others.

## Elina Brotherus's art exhibition

![Elina Brotherus exhibition, Carte Blanche PMU 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9854436)

Open image viewer

The visual artist Elina Brotherus has got a rare solo exhibition at the Pompidou Centre in Paris. Photo: Juha Nurminen / Yle

Finnish art can now be seen at the famous Pompidou Centre in Paris.

The photographic artist Elina Brotherus has got a rare solo exhibition at the museum.

Brotherus has made photographs and videos for the exhibition. Brotherus created the works together with the dancer Vera Nevanlinna.

The Paris audience praises Brotherus's works. They say that they are colourful and playful.

Brotherus's exhibition will also be seen in Finland next summer. The exhibition is coming to the Serlachius Museum in Mänttä in June.

## An audience ombudsman for Yle

![Sami Koivisto is Yle's first audience ombudsman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9854510)

Open image viewer

Audience ombudsman Sami Koivisto looks after the interests of the audience at Yle. Photo: Jussi Nahkuri / Yle Kuvapalvelu

Yle has appointed its first audience ombudsman.

Sami Koivisto will start in the job in November. Until now, Koivisto has been a social media journalist at Yle News.

The audience ombudsman looks after the interests of the audience. He also tells the audience what is done at Yle and why.

## Greetings from Limassol

![A smiling girl.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9856357)

Open image viewer

100-year-old Finland gets greetings from the Finnish school in Limassol.

A large number of Finnish children and young people live in different parts of the world, and they study the Finnish language and Finnish culture in Finnish schools.

Now that independent Finland turns 100, the Finnish schools are sending greetings to Finland on video.

The newest greetings come from the Finnish school in Limassol, Cyprus.
