# Sunday 17.5.2020 (radio)

Finnair is cutting domestic flights. A coronavirus infection was found at a school in Porvoo. The number of corona patients in hospitals has halved. The price of Norwegian salmon crashed. Young drivers are speeding.

## Finnair's domestic flights

![Finnair aircraft next to Joensuu airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4646,h_2613,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6298335e18728c5fcda/1685012012)

Finnair aircraft at Joensuu airport. Photo: Heikki Haapalainen / Yle

Finnair is cutting domestic flights at least for the summer.

Finnair will not fly to Joensuu, Jyväskylä, Kajaani, Kemi or Kokkola at all this summer. Finnair intends to decide in August whether it will fly to these cities next winter. Finnair says that the demand for tickets on these routes is also low in normal conditions.

Many domestic airports have had a flight break since March because of the coronavirus crisis. Finnair will start domestic flights little by little from July.

## Corona infection at a school

![Linnajoki school](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-102915319721fbe5d4/1394182711)

Linnajoki school in Porvoo Photo: YLE

A group of pupils and teachers in Porvoo have to go into quarantine.

One pupil at Linnajoki lower secondary school has tested positive for the coronavirus. 17 pupils and 4 teachers have been exposed to the infection.

All of them have been ordered to stay in home quarantine.

## The corona situation in Finland

![No entry sign in a laboratory where coronavirus samples are analysed, warning of the risk of infection.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11355463)

Open image viewer

Just over 100 corona patients are in hospital care. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Institute for Health and Welfare says that just over 6,300 coronavirus infections have now been confirmed in Finland. Since yesterday, about 60 new infections have been confirmed.

298 people have died in Finland from the coronavirus. Since yesterday, one new death has become known.

There are now just over a hundred corona patients in hospital, and about 30 of them are in intensive care. During the past month, the number of corona patients in hospitals and in intensive care has halved.

## The price of fish

![Salmon fillets at a fish counter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11075079)

Open image viewer

Salmon is now cheaper than usual. Photo: Goran Bogicevic / AOP

Fish is now cheaper than usual in spring.

For example, Norwegian salmon is now about 30 per cent cheaper than usual at this time of year.

The price of salmon has fallen because demand for it has fallen. The fall in demand is in turn due to the fact that because of corona restrictions, many restaurants and workplace canteens have closed their doors in different parts of Europe.

The prices of Finnish rainbow trout and wild fish have also fallen.

## Young reckless drivers

![Police monitor traffic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4998755b5835ade9f1f/1591769676)

Open image viewer

The police say that speeding has increased. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The police are worried about young drivers speeding in their cars. Over the weekend, the police have taken driving licences away from many young people, because they have been driving recklessly, that is, driving at a very high speed over the limit.

For example, in Halikko in Varsinais-Suomi, 2 young men were racing on a motorway. The speed of one of the cars was 220 kilometres per hour.

Speeds of almost 200 kilometres per hour were also measured in the Vaasa area and in Riihimäki in Häme.

The police have already said earlier that the corona pandemic has increased speeding in different parts of Europe.
