# Monday 13.9.2021 (radio)

The University of Tampere is cutting staff. Yle is changing its corona reporting. The police are inspecting car tyres. The Seesam award for easy-to-read literature goes to Tapani Bagge.

## University of Tampere redundancy negotiations

![Facade of the University of Tampere.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4452,h_2504,x_0,y_216/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-852427613b3a68ccbc8/1631271970)

Open image viewer

The redundancy negotiations at the University of Tampere begin in a week. Photo: Antti Eintola / Yle

The University of Tampere is starting large redundancy negotiations. The university intends to reduce 215 employees. The negotiations concern a total of 1,100 people.

The redundancy negotiations concern the University of Tampere's support services. Support services include, for example, administration, IT services and the library. The redundancy negotiations do not concern, for example, research or teachers.

The University of Tampere wants to save 14 million euros. The university also intends to outsource services.

## Yle's corona reporting

![Nurse Francis Agbehiadzie prepares a corona vaccine at the Helsinki Exhibition Centre. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_414/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-852325613b3152337a4/1631271310)

Open image viewer

Nurse Francis Agbehiadzie prepared a corona vaccine at the Helsinki Exhibition Centre on Friday. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

Yle is changing the way it reports on corona. Yle will no longer tell people every day on the radio and on television how many new coronavirus infections there are. The reason is that the number of new coronavirus infections is no longer very important information.

In future Yle will follow more closely how many people are in hospital because of corona. Yle also reports on the progress of vaccinations.

In future, the number of new coronavirus infections can be checked every day on Yle's website.

## Condition of tyres

![A police officer measures the tread depth of a car tyre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7458922)

Open image viewer

In addition to tread depth, the campaign urges attention to tyre air pressure. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

The police are inspecting car tyres this week.

The police say that bad tyres cause many collisions on the roads. The law requires that the tread depth of a summer tyre must be at least 1.6 millimetres. Tyre air pressure must be checked often.

Bad tyres must be replaced with new ones.

## Seesam award

![Tapani Bagge](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12096130)

Open image viewer

Tapani Bagge. Photo: Veikko Somerpuro

The Seesam award for easy-to-read literature goes to Tapani Bagge. Tapani Bagge has written 5 books in easy-to-read language. The award justification says that Bagge's books are clear. The books also have suspense, humour and warmth.

The Seesam award is given every other year. The award is given by the easy-to-read book working group of Selkokeskus.

## A bang in Helsinki

![The city skyline seen from Pasila on a February morning at sunrise.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-774074602a2d32d62d7/1613377342)

So far no explanation has been found for the loud bang heard in Helsinki late on Saturday evening. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

A loud bang was heard in Helsinki on Saturday. The cause of the sound is not known. The bang was heard in many different districts late in the evening.

People have suspected that the cause of the sound could be, for example, a meteor or an earthquake. The cause of the sound has not been found, however.
