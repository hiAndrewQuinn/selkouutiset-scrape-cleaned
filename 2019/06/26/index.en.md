# Wednesday 26.6.2019 (radio)

Finns think that military service should be compulsory for men only. Climate change is the most important issue for Finland's EU presidency. The Centre Party leadership race now has two candidates. Children in the countryside beat city children in motor skills. Heat is scorching Central and Southern Europe.

## Military service

![Which of the following best reflects your view on military service?](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10846382)

Finns think that military service should be compulsory for men, but not for women.

In a Yle survey, 65 percent of respondents said that military service should be compulsory only for men. 4 percent of respondents said that military service should be compulsory for women too.

However, more than 25 percent said that women too should have some kind of compulsory civic service.

## Finland's EU presidency

![Antti Rinne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10819744)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Antti Rinne. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Antti Rinne has told about Finland's goals during the EU presidency. Rinne said that climate change is the most important issue that Finland must raise.

Rinne says that Finland's most important goal is that the EU no longer burdens the climate with greenhouse gas emissions after the year 2050. In addition, Finland wants to emphasise social policy and education, for example.

Finland becomes the EU's presiding country at the start of July. The presidency lasts half a year.

## The Centre Party leadership race

![Centre Party parliamentary election candidate Katri Kulmuni in a press photo before the 2019 parliamentary elections.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10742831)

Katri Kulmuni Photo: Lehtikuva

2 candidates have now signed up for the Centre Party leadership race.

Minister of Defence and MP Antti Kaikkonen said last week that he is aiming to become chair of the Centre Party. Centre Party deputy chair and Minister of Economic Affairs Katri Kulmuni said today that she also wants to become chair of the Centre Party.

The Centre Party's extraordinary party congress is in September in Kouvola. At that time the party will choose a new chair to replace Juha Sipilä.

## Children's motor skills

![Children outdoors on a climbing frame at Niirala day-care centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9098725)

Open image viewer

Children on a climbing frame. Photo: Sakari Partanen / Yle

The motor skills of children in the countryside are better than those of city children.

A new study from the University of Jyväskylä examined the motor skills of children below school age. Motor skills mean, for example, balance, moving and handling equipment. A child needs motor skills, for example, when it runs, climbs or draws.

The study found the best motor skills in children who live in the countryside. Children in the countryside also spent more time outdoors than the others.

## Heat is scorching Europe

![Temperatures in Europe on 26 June 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10849655)

Open image viewer

Photo: Kerttu Kotakorpi / Yle

A heatwave is scorching Central and Southern Europe.

For example, 35 degrees has been measured today in France and 37 degrees in Berlin in Germany.

Tomorrow the most oppressive heat will ease a little, but after that the temperature will rise again. The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that, for example, in Spain and France the temperature may rise above 40 degrees at the end of the week.
