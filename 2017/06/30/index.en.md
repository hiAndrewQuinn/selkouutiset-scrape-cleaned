# Friday 30.6.2017 (radio)

Finland has joined a rapid reaction force led by Britain. Marriages of gay couples were approved in Germany. The asylum seekers' demonstration in Railway Square in Helsinki is ending. There will be a lot of strawberries this summer. The video blog A Year in Integration Training continues.

## Finland in a rapid reaction force

![Peter Hultqvist, Michael Fallon and Jussi Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9700355)

The defence ministers of Sweden, Britain and Finland met in Stockholm on Friday. Photo: Janerik Henriksson / Lehtikuva

Finland is doing a new kind of cooperation. On Friday Finland joined a force led by Britain.

It is a rapid reaction force. It is ready to act in crisis situations. The soldiers of the rapid reaction force can secure peace or give military help in a crisis situation.

Finland joined the rapid reaction force at the same time as Sweden. The other Nordic countries, the Baltic countries and the Netherlands are also part of the cooperation.

## Germany's marriage law

![Germany voted on gender-neutral marriage on 30 June. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9698591)

Open image viewer

The German parliament voted for gender-neutral marriage. Photo: Tobias Schwarz / AFP

Germany has approved gender-neutral marriage. The German parliament decided this on Friday.

Gender-neutral marriage means that gay couples can also get married. Gay couples also get the right to adopt children.

Marriage is also gender-neutral in Finland, which means that gay couples can marry in the same way as straight couples.

## The demonstration is ending

![The Finland First camp in Railway Square in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9620243)

Open image viewer

The asylum seekers' demonstration has continued in Railway Square in Helsinki since February. Photo: Yle / Mårten Lampén

The asylum seekers' demonstration in Railway Square in Helsinki is ending. On Friday police ordered that the demonstrators' camp must be taken down.

Police say that the asylum seekers have not done anything illegal. The camp has not caused any disturbance.

Police ordered the demonstrators' camp out of Railway Square for safety reasons. At the weekend there are many events and people in Helsinki.

On Monday the camp of immigration opponents was taken down from Railway Square. Taking down the camp did not go peacefully. The immigration opponents' camp was taken down because it caused disturbance.

## Strawberries are ripening

![Boxes of strawberries in a row.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9013658)

Open image viewer

The strawberry is a summer treat. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

There will be a lot of strawberries this summer.

Strawberries are ripening late because the start of the summer was quite cold. In Southern Finland most strawberries ripen after the middle of July.

But the strawberry season will last a long time. Fresh strawberries will still be available in August.
