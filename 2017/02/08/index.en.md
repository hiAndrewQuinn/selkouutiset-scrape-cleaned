# Wednesday 8.2.2017 (radio)

A dual citizen cannot become a spy or an army pilot. Kindergartens get a curriculum for the first time. Researchers suggest that someone who has been unemployed for a long time must, for example, study or work in an association. The state has bought an island in Oulu lake.

## A dual citizen cannot become a pilot

![Conscripts on the move.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8948492)

Open image viewer

Men in the army. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Yle has reported more details about Russian dual citizens in the Finnish army. An army officer gave Yle the new information.

The officer says that dual citizens of Finland and Russia are not taken on as, for example, spies, pilots and border guards. The army's intelligence unit has unofficially given such an instruction by email. The instruction breaks a law, under which all Finnish citizens must be treated in the same way.

The officer tells Yle that the army orders employees to break the law. But when this comes to light, the army says that the employee has made a mistake.

## Music and art in all kindergartens

![Children colouring in at a kindergarten.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7764996)

Open image viewer

Children colouring in at a kindergarten. Photo: Marianne Mattila / Yle

All children's kindergartens will get a curriculum next autumn. Until now only schools have had curricula. However, the years before school age are very important for a child's development.

A curriculum means that the same things are done in all kindergartens. Children must sing and play music, draw and make crafts with the children. Children must have space and time to play in peace. They must be outside every day, because there children can run and romp.

## Things to do for the unemployed

![A door of the TE services.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9420134)

Open image viewer

TE services handle the affairs of the unemployed. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Two researchers have considered how people who have been unemployed for a long time can be helped.

The researchers Heikki Hiilamo and Pasi Moisio suggest that the municipal social worker and the unemployed person agree together what the unemployed person will start doing. For example, the unemployed person can study or work in an association. In this way the unemployed person does not stay alone at home, but learns something new and perhaps also finds a real job. If the unemployed person does not want to do anything other than stay at home, he or she gets less money from the state, that is, less unemployment benefit.

Next, the government will consider whether the researchers' ideas are good and whether they will be put into use.

## The state bought a beautiful island

![Aerial photo of Ärjänsaari.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9448571)

Open image viewer

Ärjänsaari is in Lake Oulu in northern Finland. Photo: UPM

The state has bought Ärjänsaari. Ärjänsaari is in Lake Oulu in northern Finland.

The island will become a shared area for Finns, where everyone can go on an outing. On the island you can, for example, swim, fish, sleep in a tent and sit by a campfire. Ärjänsaari has long, light sandy beaches and a lot of forest. The nature of Ärjänsaari will be protected, that is, trees may not be felled and houses may not be built on the island.

The state bought the island from the big forest industry company UPM.
