## Turku's Christmas peace

![Announcement of the Christmas peace in Turku in 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11134044)

The Christmas peace is declared from the balcony of the Brinkkala House in Turku. Photo: Yle

Christmas has begun. For Finns, Christmas begins on Christmas Eve at 12 noon. That is when the Christmas peace is declared in Turku.

The Christmas peace is an old tradition. In Turku, the Christmas peace has been declared for about 700 years. It has been declared almost every Christmas. The last time the declaration was left out was 80 years ago, because at that time Finland was at war.

In the old days the Christmas peace lasted 20 days. If someone committed a crime during the Christmas peace, they received a harsher punishment than usual.

Nowadays the text of the Christmas peace is read from the balcony of the Brinkkala House, which is in the Old Great Square in Turku. The event is also broadcast on television, radio and online.

## Help for families

![Good Christmas Spirit campaign logo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9993588)

Open image viewer

The Good Christmas Spirit campaign has been held many times. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Many poor families with children have received help to buy food for Christmas. Help has gone to 28,000 families. The families received a 70-euro gift card, with which they can buy food.

The money was collected through the Good Christmas Spirit campaign. The campaign was organised by the Finnish Red Cross and the Mannerheim League for Child Welfare. The Good Christmas Spirit campaign has been held 23 times already.

## Clever reindeer

![Reindeer in Sevettijärvi, Inari](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4649,h_2615,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5292785c07ee415b997/1544023692)

The reindeer is part of Christmas. Reindeer help Santa Claus take presents to children in different parts of the world.

In stories reindeer even fly, but real reindeer are also fast. Researcher Anna-Kaisa Salmi says that a reindeer can travel up to 180 kilometres in a day.

Salmi is part of the University of Oulu's reindeer project. The project is studying when the reindeer developed from the wild reindeer. Wild reindeer are wild animals, but reindeer are used to people.

People have looked after reindeer for about 1,000 years. The reindeer herders have been the Sámi, who live in Lapland.

Researcher Anna-Kaisa Salmi says that the reindeer is an intelligent and social animal. The reindeer has learned what habits people have. That is why it can, for example, pull a sled and obey commands.

## The weather at Christmas

The Christmas weather is rainy. Near the sea on the coast it is raining, and elsewhere it is snowing.

On Christmas Day, that is Wednesday, it will snow in Central Finland. More than 10 centimetres of snow may fall.

On St Stephen's Day, that is Thursday, a little snow will fall in Eastern Finland and Ostrobothnia. On Thursday the weather will also get colder, and there will be frost across the whole country.
