# Friday 21.6.2019 (radio)

On Friday Midsummer Eve was celebrated. The meeting of the EU countries' leaders ended without decisions. The situation is getting tense around the Persian Gulf. Only 2 candidates are left in the leadership race of the British Conservative Party. Midsummer night. It is the time of magic.

## Midsummer Eve

![Midsummer bonfire in Mäntyharju in 2012](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6697685)

Open image viewer

This year midsummer bonfires could only be lit in the north. Photo: Juho Liukkonen / Yle

Today, Friday, is Midsummer Eve. The midsummer celebration has gone calmly.

Midsummer bonfires are only being lit in the north. In the south midsummer bonfires may not be lit because of the forest fire warning. The ground is so dry that fire can easily spread and set the forest alight.

The weather on Midsummer Eve has been varied. In some parts of the country it has been over 30 degrees warm, in some parts there have been heavy thunderstorms, and in Lapland it has been cold.

## EU countries' leaders

![Antti Rinne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10842573)

Open image viewer

Finland's Prime Minister Antti Rinne also attended the meeting of EU countries' leaders. Photo: Aris Oikonomou / AFP

The meeting of the EU countries' leaders has ended without decisions. The EU country leaders could not decide who will be chosen for the important jobs in the European Union.

The EU must choose, for example, a new president of the Commission, who leads the Union's work. The current president is Jean-Claude Juncker. The president is changing because the European Parliament elections were held this year.

The EU country leaders will have a new meeting in a week. They will then try to decide who will be chosen for the important EU posts.

## The situation in the Persian Gulf

![Airbus A321](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10812994)

Open image viewer

German airline Lufthansa is one of the airlines that are not flying over the Persian Gulf now. Photo: Susan &amp; Allan Parker / AOP

The situation is getting tense around the Persian Gulf.

Iran has shot down an American drone. There were no people in the drone.

The USA says that the drone was in international airspace. Iran thinks the drone was in Iran's airspace.

Many airlines have announced that they will not fly over the Persian Gulf now. Finnair is also avoiding flights in the area.

## British Conservatives

![Boris Johnson and Jeremy Hunt](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10842465)

Open image viewer

Boris Johnson or Jeremy Hunt will be the next Prime Minister of Britain. Photo: Dinendra Haria / AOP, Daniel Leal-Olivas / AFP, Tiina jutila / Yle

The leadership race of the British Conservative Party is moving forward.

Only 2 candidates are left in the race. They are former Foreign Secretary Boris Johnson and current Foreign Secretary Jeremy Hunt.

At the end of July it will become clear which of them is the new leader of the Conservative Party. The leader will also become Prime Minister of Britain.

The previous leader and Prime Minister, Theresa May, decided to resign, because she did not succeed in handling Britain's exit from the EU, that is, Brexit.

## Midsummer magic

![7 flower species on a pillow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10268774)

Open image viewer

Put flowers under your pillow, and you will see your future spouse in a dream. Photo: Kaisa Siren / AOP

Saturday is Midsummer Day. On Midsummer Day the weather is cooler than on Midsummer Eve.

Before Midsummer Day there is still Midsummer night. It is the time of magic. If you put 7 different kinds of flowers under your pillow, for example, you will see your future spouse in a dream. That is what people in Finland believed, at least in the past.

Happy midsummer to everyone!
