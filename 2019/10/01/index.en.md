# Tuesday 1.10.2019 (radio)

A violent attack has happened at the Savo Vocational College premises in Kuopio. A working group proposes an upper limit on borrowed money. The People's Republic of China turns 70. Police have shot a protester in Hong Kong.

## A serious violent attack has happened in Kuopio

![Shopping centre Herman, cordoned off by the police.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10999267)

The Herman shopping centre in Kuopio, cordoned off by the police. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

In Kuopio, a man has stabbed several people.

1 person died and 10 were injured. 2 people were seriously injured. The police have arrested the suspected perpetrator.

The incident happened at the premises of the Savo Vocational College in the Herman shopping centre.

The police say that the suspected perpetrator is among those seriously injured. 1 police officer was also lightly injured. The injured have cut wounds, but nobody is in mortal danger.

The police say that the perpetrator used a bladed weapon. He also had a firearm. The police used a firearm in the situation.

The suspected man is a native Finnish citizen.

## A working group proposes a debt ceiling

![Keys to a flat on a table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10810785)

Open image viewer

A working group proposes that the total of mortgages and other loans may be at most 4.5 times a household's income. Photo: Kimmo Hiltunen / Yle

A working group proposes that there should be an upper limit on people's borrowed money. If the proposal is carried out, debt may be at most 4.5 times a person's annual salary.

Debts include, for example, a mortgage, a consumer loan and a payday loan. Finns have a lot of debt, especially mortgages. The Ministry of Finance's working group wants a mortgage to be paid back in 25 years.

The aim of the working group's proposals is to reduce people's indebtedness.

## The People's Republic of China turns 70

![Soldiers march in Tiananmen Square before the wreath-laying ceremony on 30 September 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10996597)

The People's Republic of China turns 70 today, Tuesday.

Exactly 70 years ago, Mao Zedong declared that the People's Republic of China had been born.

China is celebrating the anniversary. In Beijing, for example, there has been a big military parade.

## There have been big protests in Hong Kong

![People on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10995932)

Open image viewer

The police and protesters clashed in Hong Kong on Saturday. Photo: Jenny Matikainen / Yle

There have been big protests in Hong Kong on China's anniversary.

The police have shot a protester in the chest in Hong Kong. It was the first time during the 4 months of protests in Hong Kong that the police fired live rounds at a protester. The protester was wounded.

Hong Kong's health authority says that 15 people have been injured in total in the protests. There have been clashes in different parts of the city, and tens of thousands of people have been on the streets. The police have fired tear gas and rubber bullets.

Many Hong Kong metro stations and a large shopping centre have been closed.

The protesters oppose China's power in Hong Kong.
