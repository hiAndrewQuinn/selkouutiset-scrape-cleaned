## Illegal votes

![Soldiers guard a polling station in occupied Mariupol.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4236,h_2378,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10129966332995cd13a5/1664260488)

Russian soldiers guard an illegal vote in Mariupol in Ukraine. Photo: AOP

Russia has organised illegal votes in Ukraine.

The votes are in areas that Russia has seized from Ukraine. The votes end today, Tuesday.

In the votes people have been asked whether they want to join Russia. Ukrainians say that the votes do not tell the truth.

Russia has, for example, forced people to vote. People have been told that they will be imprisoned if they do not vote.

Ukrainian refugees in Finland are also sad because of the illegal votes. "Ukraine is our homeland," they remind.

## Gas pipeline leaks

![Nord Stream gas pipeline leak points.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5444,h_3062,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-101344763330e743e7f0/1664290556)

Open image viewer

Photo: Riikka Tähtinen / Yle

There are leaks in the Baltic gas pipelines. Gas is leaking from 3 points.

In the Baltic Sea there are Nord Stream gas pipelines. They were built so that gas could be exported from Russia to Europe.

Now gas trade has stopped because of the war in Ukraine. However, there is still gas in the pipes.

Leaks in the Nord Stream gas pipelines were noticed on Monday and Tuesday. The gas pipelines are leaking in the sea areas of Sweden and Denmark.

Danish Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen says that the situation is unusual. The pipes have broken in many places at the same time.

Frederiksen fears that someone may have damaged the pipes on purpose. That is also feared in other countries.

## Food prices

![Person bends down to take something from the ready-meal shelf in a grocery store.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_321/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97266162b08ee143f26/1700546346)

Photo: Aalto Puutio / Yle

Food continues to become more expensive. This is what the Pellervo Economic Research Institute estimates.

The research institute estimates that the price of food will rise by about 11 percent this year.

Meat, dairy products and eggs become more expensive still. Their prices may rise by as much as 20 percent during the rest of the year.

## Easy-read books

![Easy-read books](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10711415)

Open image viewer

Easy-read books are borrowed from libraries more and more all the time. Photo: Terhi Varjonen / Yle

On Wednesday a seminar on easy-read books is being organised. The event is in the afternoon at the Oodi library in Helsinki.

Easy-read books suit everyone for whom other books are too difficult. At the Wednesday seminar the audience can meet easy-read authors.

For example, Ari Sainio will be present. He has worked with easy language and easy-read books for 30 years.

The seminar will also announce the winner of the Ilo writing competition. There are 135 easy-language stories in the competition.

The seminar is organised by Selkokeskus.
