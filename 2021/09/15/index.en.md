# Wednesday 15.9.2021 (radio)

EU Commission President Ursula von der Leyen thinks that the EU's corona measures have succeeded well. Finland is reducing corona testing. The metro and trams do not run in Helsinki on Thursday and Friday. The Jussi film awards are presented on Wednesday evening. The weather is dry, but cloudy.

The European Union's corona measures have succeeded well. This is how the EU Commission President Ursula von der Leyen assesses it.

Von der Leyen spoke in the European Parliament on Wednesday.

Von der Leyen said that the EU has given 700 million corona vaccines to its own residents. The EU has also donated millions of corona vaccines to poor countries.

Von der Leyen proposed that EU countries agree on a new health programme. The programme is needed so that new virus diseases can no longer spread around the world.

## Corona testing is decreasing

![A corona test is taken from a man](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82110660c87b18bdba3/1623751620)

Finland is reducing corona testing.

Finns no longer need to go for a corona test because of minor symptoms, if they have received 2 corona vaccine doses. Their symptoms are probably not corona, because the vaccines give good protection against corona.

Minister Krista Kiuru said on Tuesday that corona testing will now focus on elderly people and other risk groups. Corona tests are also given to people who have not yet received corona vaccinations.

Corona testing is being reduced so that health care workers have more time to treat other illnesses.

## Metro and tram traffic stops

![Tram on Mannerheimintie.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-85359161409255131c4/1631621840)

The metro and trams do not run in Helsinki on Thursday and Friday.

The metro and trams will stop because the drivers will not work for 2 days. In this way the drivers are protesting against the plans of the City of Helsinki.

The City of Helsinki has decided to set up a company for metro and tram traffic. The drivers will move into the service of the new company. They fear that their working conditions may then get worse.

The metro and trams will not run on Thursday and Friday, but services are not replaced with buses. Metro and tram traffic starts again on Saturday morning.
