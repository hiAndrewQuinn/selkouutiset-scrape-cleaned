## Corona restrictions for flights from China

![Airport staff hand out corona tests to arrivals from China. A passenger pulls a suitcase behind them and wears a protective overall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5500,h_3093,x_0,y_349/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105627663b6c949890b8/1672923606)

For example, Amsterdam airport already has restrictions in place. Photo: Hollandse Hoogte/Shutterstock/All Over Press

Finland is introducing restrictions for passengers who arrive in Finland from China. Passengers from China to Finland must have a certificate of a negative corona test. The test may be at most 2 days old.

There will also be a mask requirement for staff and passengers on flights from China. Finland will also start monitoring the amount of coronavirus in the wastewater of aircraft.

It has not yet been decided when the new rules will come into force. On Thursday the EU gave member states guidance to tighten corona restrictions on flights from China. The reason is that coronavirus infections have started to spread quickly in China.

## Russia's ceasefire offer

![Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky rewarded soldiers at the front line of the war in the city of Bakhmut on 20 December 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_63/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105164963a2f9c6df388/1671723088)

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy does not trust Russia. Photo: Handout / Office of the President of Ukraine

Russia promised to start a ceasefire in its attack on Ukraine on Friday. The ceasefire was supposed to begin in the morning and last until Saturday evening.

Ukraine says that Russia's ceasefire has not held on Friday. Ukraine says that Russia has shelled Bakhmut in eastern Ukraine.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy says that Russia wants a ceasefire so that Russia can strengthen its own troops.

## Problems in child protection

![Child Ombudsman Elina Pekkarinen stands in a press photo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_997,h_560,x_3,y_73/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99061762ebe01a96018/1670589603)

Open image viewer

Child Ombudsman Elina Pekkarinen. Photo: Mikko Vähäniitty

Child Ombudsman Elina Pekkarinen criticises Finland's child protection. One bad thing is, for example, that some of the children placed in care do not know who their own social worker is.

Child Ombudsman Pekkarinen is also worried that some children experience violence in foster care. Some children have also experienced punishments in foster care that are not legal.

Child Ombudsman Pekkarinen says that the problems in child protection have come to light in school health surveys. Pekkarinen says that the problems in child protection are also visible in other studies.

## Epiphany

![Christmas cards.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-104603663932828f3136/1670588541)

Open image viewer

On Epiphany many households pack away Christmas things. Photo: Juha Kokkala

Epiphany was celebrated on Friday. Epiphany ends the Christmas season for many people. For example, in homes the Christmas tree is taken away and the Christmas decorations are put into storage.

Epiphany is a public holiday in Finland, that is, for example, offices and banks are closed. However, many shops are open.

Epiphany is one of the oldest festivals of Christians. Epiphany has been celebrated even longer than Christmas. In many Christian churches, on Epiphany the Magi who came to see the Child Jesus are remembered.
