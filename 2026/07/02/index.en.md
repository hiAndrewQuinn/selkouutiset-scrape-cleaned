## Russia closes border crossings

In Finland, all other border crossings to Russia were closed in 2023.

Russia is closing border crossings on the borders with Finland, Estonia and Latvia.

## Study: Occupational health can be expensive for the state

Finally, some news about healthcare.

A study conducted in Sweden shows that access to a private doctor can increase the cost of public healthcare.

In Finland, many employees have access to a private doctor for occupational health care.

The study finds that in occupational health care, doctors easily refer patients for further examinations. Further examinations are carried out in public healthcare.

Therefore, public healthcare spending may increase.

In Finland, the major employee and employer organizations SAK and EK say that people's health is well taken care of in occupational health care.
