## Finland's corona situation

![Corona times: spaces at Kainuu Central Hospital have been divided, among other things, using a movable screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11455251)

The corona situation in Finland remains calm.

5 new corona infections have been found in Finland. THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, said on Monday that Finland now has 7,340 corona infections in total.

THL also said that there are no new corona deaths. 328 people have died in Finland because of corona.

## Corona dogs are being trained

![Kössi the dog sniffing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11358834)

Open image viewer

Kössi the dog has learned to find the coronavirus. Photo: Susanna Paavilainen

Dogs can smell the coronavirus. Dogs may find the coronavirus perhaps even better than laboratory tests do.

In a University of Helsinki study, dogs have learned to smell whether a patient's urine contains corona. The dog has smelled the corona even before laboratory tests have revealed the coronavirus.

The University of Helsinki study interests many countries. If the researchers succeed in training corona dogs, they may be of great help.

Corona dogs may perhaps find, for example at airports, people who have the coronavirus. Then the coronavirus cannot spread with the passengers.

## Face mask requirement in France

![Customer wearing a face mask in a department store in Paris.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11456145)

Open image viewer

The French must wear face masks, for example, in shopping centres. Photo: Alain Jocard / AFP

France is fighting against the spread of the coronavirus.

On Monday a new order came into force in France. People must now wear face masks in all public indoor spaces, for example in shops and banks. If a person does not have a face mask, they may get a fine.

Previously the French were required to wear face masks on public transport, for example on trains.

## The UAE space probe

![Tube-like rocket with a nut-shaped probe at its tip. Part of the cloudy surface of the Earth is visible.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11416511)

Open image viewer

Space probe. Photo: United Arab Emirates space agency

The first space probe of an Arab state has set off.

The United Arab Emirates space probe was named al-Amal, which means Hope. The space probe was launched towards space from Japan on Monday.

The space probe is on its way to the planet Mars. It will start orbiting Mars next February.

With the help of the space probe, researchers want to get information about the atmosphere of Mars.

The UAE also wants the space project to inspire young Arabs. Many young researchers are involved in the project, and many of them are women.
