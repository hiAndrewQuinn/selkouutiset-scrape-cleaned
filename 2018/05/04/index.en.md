# Friday 4.5.2018 (radio)

The Nobel Prize in Literature will be postponed to next year. The European Parliament wants to end animal testing for cosmetics across the world. The official portrait of President Niinistö has been unveiled. The first barn swallows and nightingales of spring have come to Finland. The record winners of Eurojackpot have been found.

## No Nobel Prize in Literature this year

![Nobel celebration at Stockholm City Hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10151984)

Open image viewer

The Nobel celebration was held at Stockholm City Hall in December 2017. Photo: Fredrik Sandberg / EPA

The Nobel Prize in Literature will not be awarded at all this year.

The Swedish Academy says that this year's literature prize will be awarded only next year, at the same time as next year's prize.

This is the first time since the Second World War that the Nobel Prize in Literature will not be awarded.

The decision is due to a crisis that has been shaking the Swedish Academy for many months. For example, the spouse of one Academy member is accused of many sexual harassments and data leaks.

## European Parliament wants to end cosmetics animal testing

![Mice used in animal tests in a terrarium.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5731300)

Open image viewer

Mice, for example, are used in animal tests. Photo: YLE/Mika Kanerva

The European Parliament wants to ban the testing of cosmetic products on animals.

Animal testing of cosmetics is already banned in the EU. Now the European Parliament wants the tests to be banned in the rest of the world too.

At the moment, about 80 percent of the countries in the world allow cosmetic products to be tested on animals. The European Parliament hopes that new kinds of test methods will be developed to replace animal tests.

## President Niinistö's portrait is finished

![President Sauli Niinistö at the unveiling of his portrait in the reception hall of the Government Palace](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10190731)

The official portrait of President Sauli Niinistö has been unveiled.

The portrait is a mosaic made up of 100 small pictures. 100 young artists took part in making it.

The portrait will be hung in the Government Palace, in the reception hall of the president. All the portraits of all Finnish presidents are there.

## Many migratory birds are arriving now

![Nightingale.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9698872)

Open image viewer

Nightingale. Photo: AOP

The spring migration of birds has been busy this week.

The bird organisation BirdLife says that this week the first barn swallows, nightingales, bluethroats and Blyth's reed warblers of spring have been seen in Finland.

Rarer birds have also arrived in Finland this spring. For example, a Eurasian penduline tit has been seen in Varkaus, a bird that has been spotted in Finland only 5 times before.

Some species that used to be rare are also becoming more common. BirdLife says that the hoopoe, for example, used to be rare in Finland. During the last two springs, however, many sightings of the bird have been made.

## The Eurojackpot winners of Loimaa have been found

![Eurojackpot coupons.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9567559)

Open image viewer

Finland's biggest ever gambling win was split into four parts. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

Finland's biggest ever gambling win has been claimed this week.

A Eurojackpot line was played in February at the Prisma in Loimaa, and it brought the winners about 90 million euros in total.

There are 4 winners in total. They waited almost 3 months before claiming their winnings this week at the Veikkaus head office in Helsinki.
