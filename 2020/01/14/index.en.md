# Tuesday 14.1.2020 (radio)

The foreign minister's actions are being investigated. Nokia is making redundancies. People have been detained in Iran. Many birds and butterflies are dying. The weather.

## The foreign minister's actions are being investigated

![Pekka Haavisto in Parliament on his way to be heard by the Constitutional Law Committee.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2690,h_1513,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6307135e1d789c183a6/1578989935)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

Parliament is investigating the actions of Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto.

Parliament's Constitutional Law Committee is examining whether the foreign minister has acted according to the law.

The inquiry concerns a decision that the foreign minister made about how Finnish people are brought back from the al-Hol camp. Some MPs think that the foreign minister may have acted wrongly in some way.

It is very rare for a minister's actions to be investigated in this way.

There are Finnish adults at the al-Hol camp who have joined the terrorist organisation Isis.

There are also children at the camp. 2 children have been brought to Finland from the camp.

## Nokia is making staff redundant

![The headquarters of the network company Nokia in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10323299)

Open image viewer

The headquarters of Nokia's network company is in Espoo. Photo: Aku Häyrynen / Lehtikuva

The Finnish network equipment maker Nokia intends to make many employees redundant.

Nokia will start co-operation negotiations next week.

180 employees may lose their jobs.

Nokia currently has about 6,000 employees in Finland in total.

## People have been detained in Iran

![A child's shoe on the ground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11153264)

Open image viewer

176 people died in the accident. Small children were also among them. Photo: Borna Ghassemi / AFP

In Iran the authorities have detained many people because of the plane crash.

The authorities say that the people who were detained may have been involved in the plane crash.

Last week a plane of a Ukrainian airline fell in Iran. Iran later said that Iranian soldiers had accidentally fired a missile at the plane.

There were 176 people on the plane and they all died.

The people came from many different countries.

## Birds and butterflies are disappearing

![A house sparrow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8385928)

Open image viewer

The house sparrow is a very endangered bird in Finland. That means the house sparrow may disappear completely. Photo: Maarit Siitonen

Birds and insects are constantly disappearing from Europe. Researchers say that biodiversity is declining. .

Birds are dying especially in areas where land is farmed.

Pesticides are used in farming. The poisons kill insects, and birds no longer have food.

Butterflies are dying because there are few natural meadows left.

Tomorrow, Wednesday, is an unusually warm day in Finland.

Tomorrow a winter heat record may be set in the south. Usually in winter people talk about frost records.

This winter has been unusually warm, that is, mild.

Tomorrow there will be frost only in Lapland.
