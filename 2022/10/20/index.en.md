Britain's Prime Minister is resigning. Prime Minister Liz Truss announced her resignation today, Thursday.

Truss started as Prime Minister in September. She has been Britain's Prime Minister for only 6 weeks.

Truss is the leader of the Conservative Party. The party no longer trusts a Prime Minister who tried to make unpopular economic reforms.

The Conservative Party may choose a new leader as early as next week. The party's new leader will also become Prime Minister.

## Olkiluoto 3

![General view of Olkiluoto 3. Reactor building number three of the nuclear power plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5832,h_3280,x_0,y_154/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-883916619f79055381c/1637841265)

Open image viewer

The Olkiluoto nuclear power plant. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

Finland will get more nuclear power, that is, atomic power, perhaps at the end of December.

At that point the new Olkiluoto nuclear power plant, Olkiluoto 3, will start to produce electricity regularly. This is the estimate of Teollisuuden Voima, the plant's owner.

Teollisuuden Voima also owns the older Olkiluoto 2 nuclear power plant.

Olkiluoto 3 has already been in trial operation. This week, however, faults were found in it. The faults will be repaired before the trial operation continues.

When Olkiluoto 3 starts to operate regularly, it will produce over 10 percent of Finland's electricity. The plant is important now, when there is an energy crisis in Europe.

## Queues at the children's hospital

![New Children's Hospital aerial photo, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4135,h_2326,x_0,y_213/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4981925b488d0444f65/1717408258)

Open image viewer

The children's hospital is located in Helsinki. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

There is a long queue at the Helsinki Children's Hospital for heart surgery.

There are now about 120 children in the queue. Many of them have been waiting for heart surgery for more than half a year.

Such a long waiting time is illegal. The care guarantee says that treatment must be accessed within six months.

The reason for the queue at the children's hospital is that there are too few intensive care nurses. More than 20 extra nurses are needed on the children's hospital's intensive care ward.

Although the children's hospital is in Helsinki, it treats children from all over Finland.

## Trips abroad

![Sunbathers on a sandy beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10224641)

Open image viewer

Finns like to travel to Mediterranean countries. Photo: Henryk T. Kaiser / AOP

Finns are travelling abroad a lot again. During the corona time, trips abroad were not made.

In the summer Finns made 4 times as many trips abroad as a year earlier.

Finns made the most trips abroad to neighbouring countries, Estonia and Sweden. After those, the most popular destinations were Greece and Spain.
