## Vocational training moves to workplaces

![A boy studying construction at a vocational school measures a board.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6557030)

Open image viewer

The government thinks that the best way to learn a trade is at a workplace. Photo: Yle

The government has agreed that vocational training will change. The government wants students to get more teaching at workplaces.

The intention is also that young people can apply for vocational training at any time. At the moment, people apply for vocational training in spring or autumn.

Vocational training will probably change next year.

## Schoolchildren made news at Yle

![News class journalists](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9580166)

Open image viewer

The day of the News Class pupils began early in the morning on radio and television. There were many cheerful journalists on Yle's Aamu morning TV show. Photo: Yle

On Tuesday News Class Day was celebrated at Yle.

In News Class, schoolchildren are journalists on television, on radio and online. Yle's journalists help the schoolchildren with the work of editing. The schoolchildren decide which topics the news is made about.

The news topics included, for example, summer jobs, school food and dance hobbies. The schoolchildren also interviewed President Sauli Niinistö. They asked the president about school bullying, for example.

The aim of Yle's News Class is to help schoolchildren understand how the media works. More than 300 schools have taken part in News Class. News Class Day was celebrated for the fourth time now.

## A Saimaa ringed seal used a nesting box

![A reed nest for a Saimaa ringed seal, partly under snow](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9580753)

Open image viewer

Reed was the building material when the nest was made for a Saimaa ringed seal. Photo: Juha Taskinen

There has been a new success in protecting the Saimaa ringed seal. This winter the Saimaa ringed seal has used a nest that a human has built. The materials of the nest were snow and reed, which is tall grass.

Normally the Saimaa ringed seal builds its nest from snow. It needs the nest for its pups, that is, the babies. That is why people want to help Saimaa ringed seals if there is not enough snow for a nest in winter.

Saimaa ringed seals live only in Finland.

## Children's digital devices

![A child practising maths on a tablet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6241791)

Open image viewer

Digital devices can also be used for many useful things. Photo: Yle

Many parents wonder how much a child should use digital devices. Studies say that children under school age sometimes use digital devices for almost 2 hours a day.

Non-fiction writer Satu Irisvik says that the time is not the most important thing. Parents must look at what the child does on the devices. The most important thing is that the content suits the child and the child does things, and does not just stare at the screen. For example, taking photos and playing games can be good things for a child to do, Irisvik says.
