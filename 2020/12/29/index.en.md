## Spanish vaccination register

![An elderly woman protected by a mask receives a vaccine from a nurse](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3504,h_1971,x_0,y_181/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7576095fe8528edfbc6/1609061101)

Open image viewer

Older people have already been vaccinated in Spain. Photo: EPA / Pepe Zamora

Spain is setting up a register in which the names of those who do not take the corona vaccine will be recorded.

Spanish health authorities say that the vaccine is voluntary, but the aim is for all Spaniards to be vaccinated.

The register will record that a person has refused a corona vaccine that was offered to them.

The register is not public, but Spain intends to share the information with other EU countries.

## Customs duties in Britain

![ Bentley is undergoing final checks at the factory in Crewe.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11572703)

Open image viewer

Bentley is a luxury car made in Britain. Their prices will also go up. Photo: AOP

From the start of next year many things will change, because Britain is no longer an EU country.

For example, if you shop online from Britain, you have to pay a customs duty on the goods.

Finnish Customs says that the customs duty raises the price of the goods by about 30 percent. The customs duty depends on which country the goods are shipped to the customer from.

The Customs website has a calculator, with which you can check how much customs duty there will be.

## Journalism is a dangerous job

![The coffin of a dead journalist is carried in Afghanistan](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3648,h_2052,x_0,y_188/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7531215fd27bad45426/1607629777)

Open image viewer

A woman journalist was buried in Afghanistan. ISIS killed a journalist who defended women's rights. Photo: Ghulamullah Habibi / EPA

Being a journalist can be dangerous.

The international journalists' organisation says that this year a total of 50 people have been killed in the world because they were journalists or media workers.

Countries dangerous for journalists include Mexico, India and Pakistan, for example. Journalists were killed because they wrote, for example, about dangerous criminals, corruption or environmental issues.
