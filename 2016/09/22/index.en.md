# Thursday 22.9.2016 (radio)

The Minister of the Interior wants to stop hate speech in Finland. The Finns Party is looking into its members' links to the far right. In the USA, riots against the police continue. The founder of Facebook wants to remove diseases from the world.

## The Minister of the Interior wants to put a stop to hate speech

![Paula Risikko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9073308)

Minister of the Interior Paula Risikko Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Minister of the Interior Paula Risikko says that the police must deal with hate speech more strictly than before. Risikko wants the police, for example, to increase the number of internet police officers.

In addition, the Ministry of the Interior and the Ministry of Justice are thinking together about whether laws must be changed.

Minister of the Interior Risikko thinks that with the current laws it is hard to stop hate speech, that is, speech that stirs up hatred against other people.

National Police Commissioner Seppo Kolehmainen promised on Wednesday to find out whether the Nordic Resistance Movement can be banned.

## The Finns Party's Timo Soini promises to look into the party people's far-right links

![Timo Soini](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9184182)

Open image viewer

Chairman of the Finns Party, Foreign Minister Timo Soini Photo: Yle

The chairman of the Finns Party, Foreign Minister Timo Soini, says that it is good to look into the links between the party's people and the far right. Soini thinks that no violence or threats should be accepted.

Helsingin Sanomat has found out that at least 5 far-right groups have links to the Finns Party. The party's members have links, for example, to neo-Nazis, that is, to the Nordic Resistance Movement. Timo Soini says that he did not know before about the party people's connections to such extremist groups.

A member of the Nordic Resistance Movement was remanded in custody on Wednesday because a young man was assaulted at the organisation's demonstration. The victim later died.

## Riots grow in the USA

![A picture taken from a helicopter, in which a group of demonstrators attack a police car. One demonstrator stands on the roof of the car and another stands on the bonnet and kicks the car's windscreen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9181625)

Open image viewer

Rioters and police have clashed in Charlotte.  Photo: CBS News

Big riots continue in North Carolina in the United States. People accuse the police of using too much force. The riots started in the city of Charlotte after the police shot a 43-year-old black man. The police say that the man had a gun in his hand. The man's relatives say that the man had a book in his hand.

The governor has declared a state of emergency in the city, and the National Guard has been called in to help. At least 1 person has been seriously injured in the clashes. The police say that the victim was injured by a bullet from another civilian.

The police's harsh treatment, especially of black people, has brought people out onto the streets many times in the last 2 years.

## The founder of Facebook donated a lot of money to research

![Facebook's founder and CEO Mark Zuckerberg and his wife Priscilla Chan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9183999)

Mark Zuckerberg and his wife Priscilla Chan Photo: EPA/ANDREW GOMBERT

Facebook's founder Mark Zuckerberg wants to get rid of all diseases in the world. For this reason, Zuckerberg and his wife Priscilla Chan are donating 2.7 billion euros to research. With the money, for example, a new research centre will be set up in San Francisco. Researchers are trying to get rid of serious diseases such as HIV, Ebola and the Zika virus.

Zuckerberg has earlier promised to donate a large part of his wealth so that the world is a better place for children to live.
