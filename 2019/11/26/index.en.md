# Tuesday 26.11.2019 (radio)

A solution to the postal dispute is in sight. Christmas parcels will arrive in time. The population of Finland is now growing only because of immigration. An earthquake has struck Albania. Many people fell and were injured on the slippery streets of Tampere.

## Postal strike

![Tuomas Aarto, managing director of the employers' organisation Palta, and Heidi Nieminen, chair of the PAU postal and logistics union, told reporters about the state of the talks as they left Palta's offices for the National Conciliator's office in Helsinki on 26 November.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11088533)

A solution to the postal dispute is in sight. The postal negotiators believe that a solution is close.

The negotiators will say more about the solution when the agreement is ready. When the dispute is settled, the postal strike will also end.

The postal strike has already lasted more than 2 weeks. The strike began on 11 November. Because of the strike, letters, for example, have not been delivered.

## Christmas parcels

![Parcel at the Posti sorting centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9610349)

Open image viewer

The parcels arrive well in time for Christmas. Photo: Yle

Domestic Christmas parcels are arriving in time, even though the postal strike has lasted a long time. This is what Posti says.

If a customer posts a Christmas parcel to somewhere in Finland by 20 December at the latest, the parcel will arrive by Christmas.

The postal strike has slowed down parcels that come to Finland from outside Europe the most.

## Finland's population

![Crowds of people in a shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10097979)

Open image viewer

Finland's population has grown thanks to immigration. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finland's population has grown a little this year. At the end of October, there were about 8,400 more people in Finland than at the start of January.

However, more Finns have died than have been born. There were over 6,000 more deaths than births.

The population has grown only because of immigration. 15,000 more people moved to Finland from abroad than moved from Finland abroad.

These figures therefore cover the period from the start of January to the end of October.

Finland's population is about 5.5 million.

## Albanian earthquake

![Traces of the earthquake in Thumane, Albania.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11087086)

Open image viewer

People were trapped under the rubble when houses collapsed. Photo: Malton Dibra / AOP

An earthquake has struck Albania in the Balkans. People have died in the earthquake. About 20 people have died.

The exact number of dead is not known, because people are trapped in the rubble. Rescuers are trying to find them. Rescue work is hard, because many apartment blocks collapsed in the earthquake.

The earthquake happened early on Tuesday morning. Its centre was 30 kilometres from Albania's capital, Tirana.

## Injuries in Tampere

![Tays](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10890572)

Open image viewer

The emergency department in Tampere was busy on Tuesday. Photo: Antti Eintola / Yle

Here in Finland, it has been slippery in the Tampere region. The streets were so slippery that many people fell badly.

About 60 people who had fallen were brought to the emergency department in Tampere on Tuesday. They fell and were injured because of the slippery conditions.

Many of those who fell even needed surgery. About 10 of the people who fell went to the operating theatre.

The reason for the slippery conditions was rain. Water froze on the surfaces of roads and streets.
