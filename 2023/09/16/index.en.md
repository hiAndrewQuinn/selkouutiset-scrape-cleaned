People are paying back their mortgages faster than before.

Banks say that problems in paying mortgages have not increased, even though interest rates have risen.

Instead, Finns are paying their mortgages faster. People are paying off mortgages with savings that they built up during the corona pandemic.

## NATO

![Finnish and NATO flags.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_500/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10421866388837d94603/1669891021)

Open image viewer

The Finnish flag and the NATO flag. Photo: PX_MEDIA

Finns have not discussed NATO enough. This is what professor **Tommi Koivula** of the National Defence University says.

Finland joined the military alliance NATO last spring. The decision to join NATO was made quickly, when Russia attacked Ukraine.

Professor Koivula says that it was a big change in Finland's history. However, the hurry was so great that the matter was not discussed very much.

For example, it is a new thing for Finland that Finland has a responsibility also for the security of other NATO member states. In history Finns have thought that Finland has responsibility only for its own security.

It is also a new and big thing that NATO's nuclear weapons are now part of Finland's security.

## Higher education

![Students studying in a library.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_150/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6216845df153652ff41/1576097038)

Open image viewer

56 000 students are applying to higher education this autumn. Photo: Shutterstock

More people are applying to higher education than a year ago.

This autumn 56 000 people are applying to higher education. That is almost 13 000 more than last autumn.

Higher education institutions are universities and universities of applied sciences.

The most competition is for places at universities of applied sciences where teaching is in English.

Many people who want to come from abroad to study in Finland apply for those places.

## Dangerous dog

![A dog sits on the floor of a studio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4891,h_2751,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11728796505487f28549/1694845292)

American Bully XL dog. Photo: AOP

Britain is banning a dangerous American dog breed. The dog breed's name is American Bully XL.

Britain banned American Bully XL dogs on Thursday. Earlier the same day, two American Bully XL dogs attacked a man. The man died.

Two other people have also died in attacks by similar dogs.

There are no American Bully XL dogs in Finland
