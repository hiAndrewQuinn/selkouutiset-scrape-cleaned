## Banning petrol engine cars

![Old petrol pumps in the yard of the Gulf bar and service station in Ruotsinkylä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_183/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7569375fe1f4385e19b/1608644469)

Finns are not excited about banning petrol engine cars. Photo: Rami Moilanen / Yle

Finns are not excited about banning petrol engine cars.

Yle asked Finns for their opinion.

Half of the respondents said that petrol engine cars should not be banned.

A little under 20 percent of the respondents supported banning the sale of new petrol engine cars from 2030.

Many EU countries are planning to ban the sale of petrol engine cars in the 2030s.

The aim is for emissions from traffic to fall.

## Corona and frost

![A woman in a bathrobe going to an ice hole for a swim.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4134,h_2325,x_0,y_407/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5392545c4f412c8b745/1548698112)

Open image viewer

Experts say that the coronavirus does not seem to be afraid of frost. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There has now been wintry frost weather in Finland.

It seems that frost does not affect the coronavirus.

Professor of virology Ilkka Julkunen says that changes of season have not shown up in the spread of the coronavirus anywhere in the world. Julkunen says that the coronavirus has spread in all kinds of weather. Viruses are not afraid of frost.

When the weather is cold, people are more indoors and close to each other. That also increases infections.

## Trump and social media

![Donald Trump's Twitter account on a phone screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11729852)

Open image viewer

Twitter closed President Trump's account completely. Photo: Olivier Douliery / AFP

In the USA there is a lot of talk about President Donald Trump and social media.

Twitter announced that it has permanently closed Trump's personal Twitter account. The company said the account was closed because Trump might incite people to violence.

Trump tweeted a lot. He had over 88 million followers on Twitter.

All of Trump's old posts have been hidden.

Many of Trump's supporters have started to use a social media service called Parler. On Parler the content is not monitored as closely as on Twitter and Facebook, for example.
