# Saturday 17.6.2017 (radio)

A new leader for the Greens. The strawberry harvest is good but late. A new national park in Eastern Finland. The world's only Moomin museum in Tampere.

## A new leader for the Greens

![Touko Aalto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9677113)

Touko Aalto from Jyväskylä was elected the new leader of the Greens on Saturday.  Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The new leader of the Greens is Touko Aalto from Jyväskylä.

Ville Niinistö, who led the Greens for 6 years, said in his farewell speech that education funding must be brought back to the level it was at before Sipilä's government.

Support for the Greens is now higher than ever before. In a new poll commissioned by Yle, over 15% of respondents supported the Greens.

## The strawberry harvest is good but late

![Ripening strawberries in a field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6215273)

Open image viewer

Strawberries are ripening later than usual this summer. The reason is the cold spring. But a good harvest is expected. Photo: Kirsi Lönnblad /Yle

There will be more strawberries this year than last year, berry growers estimate.

But the harvest is late because of the cold spring. In southern Finland the harvest will perhaps be one week late. Elsewhere in the country the harvest is almost 2 weeks late.

In part of the country the first strawberries will be ready by Midsummer. Most strawberries in Southern Finland will be ready in the second and third weeks of July.

## A new national park in Eastern Finland

![A landscape in Hossa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9675941)

Open image viewer

Landscapes of the new Hossa national park. Anyone can hike and admire nature in a national park. Photo: Anne Moilanen / Yle

A new national park has been opened in Finland, in Hossa.

Hossa is in Suomussalmi in Kainuu. The new national park has an area of about 11,000 hectares.

Hossa is known for its rock paintings. They are among the largest prehistoric rock paintings in Finland. Very old dwelling places have also been found there. They are almost 10,000 years old. The new national park is especially praised because accessibility has been taken into account in the park.

National parks are large nature conservation areas.  There are now 40 of them in Finland.

## The world's only Moomin museum in Tampere

![Japanese Moomin tourists in Tampere](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9676589)

Open image viewer

Japanese Moomin tourists Yoko Inaba and Fumika Nonaka went to the Moomin Museum as soon as it opened in Tampere on Saturday.  Photo: Heli Mansikka / Yle

The world's first Moomin museum has been opened in Tampere.

A large number of original Moomin pictures and stories made by Tove Jansson are on display there.

The museum is in Tampere Hall. It cost over 3.5 million euros. Many visitors are expected there from abroad too. Moomins are very popular especially in Japan and China.
