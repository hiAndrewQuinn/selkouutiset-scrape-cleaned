## Airports

![Ira Nyqvist prepares for a security check at Enontekiö airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_524/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1030963636a30d719537/1667903782)

Ira Nyqvist prepares for a security check at Enontekiö airport. Photo: Anneli Lappalainen / Yle

Many Finnish airports need more workers. There is a shortage of workers especially at Helsinki-Vantaa, and on the other hand at the small airports in Lapland.

For work, for example, security checkers, cleaners and machine and electrical fitters are needed..

The tourist season in Lapland will soon begin, and tens of thousands of tourists will arrive in the region.

Enontekiö airport in Lapland trains multi-skilled airport workers together with the Lappia vocational college. The training is part of the basic qualification in tourism.

Some of Enontekiö's students have already started work, for example at the airports of Ivalo and Kittilä.

## Housework

src="https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5276341"

Open image viewer

Never before has time-use measurement shown that men do as much housework as women. Photo: YLE / Juha-Pekka Inkinen

Men and women now spend almost the same amount of time on housework.

The total working time spent on housework was almost equal for the first time in the measurement history. Statistics Finland says that men now spend more time on housework and childcare than before.

Time spent on paid work, on the other hand, has decreased from before for both men and women.

The corona time may be behind the change.

![Finlandia Prize nominees with their books.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_101/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1032172636cbf7d5ccdf/1668071387)

One of them will receive the Finlandia Prize for literature on 30 November. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The nominees for the Finlandia Prize for fiction have been announced.

The nominees are Olli Jalonen's The Stalker Years, Marja Kyllönen's The Dead, Heikki Kännö's Human Twilight, Iida Rauma's Destruction, Sami Tissari's Krysa and Eeva Turunen's A Cultivated and Pleasant Person.

The Finlandia winner is chosen by the Bishop of Turku, Mari Leppänen. The winner will be announced on 30 November.

## Upper secondary school graduates

![Photo of a student cap and the leaves of a flowering plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3504,h_1971,x_0,y_169/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9562166283902ebff88/1653500429)

Open image viewer

Photo: Linda Tammela / Yle

The names of the autumn upper secondary school graduates have been published.

About 4,300 new graduates are now finishing, which is about 100 more than a year ago.

More graduates will still come, when candidates complete the rest of their upper secondary studies.

Many autumn graduates will get their graduation caps on 2 December. In many schools the capping ceremony is held in connection with the Independence Day celebration.

The highest number of laudatur grades this autumn is 8. 3 candidates reached it.

On Yle's website there is a graduates' search tool, where you can look at the names of new graduates at yle.fi.

Some names are missing from the tool, because they have refused to have their name published.
