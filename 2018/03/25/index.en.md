# Sunday 25.3.2018

Germany has arrested the former regional leader of Catalonia. In the USA young people demand stricter gun laws. Qantas opened a direct route from Perth to London. On Palm Sunday the virpojat were out and about. The weather is cold.

## Germany arrests the Catalan leader

![Carles Puigdemont](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10129201)

Open image viewer

Former regional leader of Catalonia Carles Puigdemont was just visiting Finland. Photo: Ahti Kaario / Yle

Germany has arrested the former regional leader of Catalonia, Carles Puigdemont. Puigdemont was caught when he came by car across the Danish border into Germany.

Puigdemont was arrested because Spain has issued an international arrest warrant for him. Spain suspects him of rebellion.

Puigdemont also visited Finland this week.

## Young people demand stricter gun laws

![Protesters gather in Washington to oppose US gun laws.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10132168)

Open image viewer

Protesters want US gun laws to be tightened. Photo: Michael Reynolds / EPA

In the United States, hundreds of thousands of people have demonstrated in favour of tightening gun laws. Demonstrations were held in more than 800 different places across the country.

Protesters want, for example, gun sales to be monitored more strictly and buyers' backgrounds to be checked better.

Tightening the laws has been demanded since a gunman attacked a school in Florida in February. The attacker shot 17 people dead.

Young people in particular are now demanding tighter gun laws, because they want to put an end to school shootings.

## From Australia to Britain without stopovers

![Qantas 787-9 Dreamliner.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10132428)

Open image viewer

Qantas flies from Perth in Australia to London in Britain in just over 17 hours.. Photo: Brent Winstone / EPA

The first direct route flight has been opened between Australia and Europe. The Australian airline Qantas flies between Perth and London.

The route is over 14 000 kilometres long. Its flight time is just over 17 hours. About 3 hours are saved, because the plane does not need to make a stopover.

The Perth-London route is the world's second longest passenger flight. A flight from Auckland in New Zealand to Doha in Qatar is about 30 kilometres longer.

Qantas flew between Australia and Britain already in 1947. Then the journey took 4 days, and several stopovers were made during it.

## Palm Sunday sets the virpojat in motion

![Virpojat at the door](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10132565)

Open image viewer

Virpojat are given sweets as payment, for example. Photo: Antti Haanpää/Yle

Many little virpojat have been out today. Virpojat are children who have dressed up, for example, as witches.

Virpominen means that children ring the doorbell and recite a short rhyme, which wishes health and luck. The children must be given sweets, for example, as payment.

The virpojat are out because today is Palm Sunday. It is a week until Easter.

## The cold tightens again

![Temperature maps.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10132638)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

The weather in the coming days is cold, says the Finnish Meteorological Institute.

The frost tightens during the early part of the week. Nights in particular are cold, and the frost may be over 10 degrees at night, even in Southern Finland.

The frost may also be present during the day across the whole country.
