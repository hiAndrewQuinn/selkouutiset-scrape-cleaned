## Finland's economy

![Name of a commercial bank on the wall of a building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3834,h_2156,x_0,y_390/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-95803062879a4c8f0f3/1653054901)

Finland's state economy will grow this year. That is how Handelsbanken assesses it. The bank says that Finland's economy will grow by 0.2 percent this year.

Many earlier estimates said that Finland's economy will not grow at all this year.

Prices are no longer rising as wildly as before. Handelsbanken estimates that inflation, that is the rise in prices, will slow this year to 5.8 percent. At the start of the year prices rose faster than this.

The reason for slower inflation is that petrol and electricity cost less than before.

## Immigration

![Moving boxes are being carried.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_583/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5038825b76b45c270e3/1534506796)

A record number of people moved to Finland last year.

Statistics Finland says that almost 50,000 people moved to Finland in 2022. In earlier years about 30,000 people moved to Finland.

More people than before moved to Finland, especially from Russia. About 6000 people came to Finland from Russia last year. Next, the most people moved to Finland from Sweden, India and Estonia.

## Fighting in Sudan

![People queue with luggage to get on a bus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5109,h_2873,x_10,y_356/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1118900646df449c3374/1684927610)

The UN again says that the fighting in Sudan must be stopped.

In Sudan's capital Khartoum the fighting continues, even though there is a ceasefire in the country. The Sudanese armed forces and the RSF forces agreed over the weekend that the ceasefire will last a week.

Hundreds of people have died in the fighting in Sudan. Most of the victims are civilians. More than a million people have fled their homes.

## Insect damage

![Bark beetle frass pile on a fallen spruce trunk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3024,h_1701,x_0,y_912/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83337560f5704193edb/1626699183)

Insects are causing more damage in Finnish forests than before.

New insects that destroy forests are spreading to Finland. Finland's current species are also moving further north. The reason is climate change.

Pohjola Insurance says that among insects, the bark beetle causes the most damage to trees. The bark beetle is a beetle that destroys spruces.

However, most of the forest damage is caused by storms.

## The weather

In the east it rains on Thursday during the day. In Lapland too there is a little rain in places. In Western Finland there are not many clouds in the sky.

The temperature at night is between 7 and 14 degrees. On Thursday the daytime temperature is between 10 and 18 degrees, and in the east in places about 20 degrees.
