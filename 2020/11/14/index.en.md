# Saturday 14.11.2020 (radio)

Interior Minister Maria Ohisalo has commented on violent crimes by young people. There are 244 new coronavirus infections in Finland. Experts have looked for reasons for Finland's good coronavirus situation. More than 3.3 million euros was collected on Nose Day.

## Interior Minister Ohisalo's comments

Interior Minister Maria Ohisalo has spoken about violent crimes committed by young people. Ohisalo was a guest on the TV programme Ykkösaamu on Saturday.

A lot has been written in newspapers about violent crimes by young people. The police estimate that more than 100 violent young people are moving around in Helsinki.

In Ohisalo's view, adults must make sure that young people do not become interested in a criminal life. The police have already received more money so that they can help young people better. In addition, more money is needed for child protection, Ohisalo says.

Among the violent young people there are also young people with an immigrant background. In Interior Minister Ohisalo's view, young immigrants must be brought into Finnish society. This succeeds better if racism and discrimination can be stopped, Ohisalo says.

## Finland's new coronavirus figures

There are 244 new coronavirus infections in Finland.

For some time now more than 200 coronavirus infections have been found per day.

A total of more than 19,000 coronavirus infections have been found in Finland.

In Finland the coronavirus has spread more slowly than in other European countries.

## Reasons for the good coronavirus situation

![People queuing for coffee.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11336306)

Open image viewer

Corona or not, Finns do not go close to others. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Experts have been thinking about why the coronavirus situation is better in Finland than in other European countries. Here are some reasons that experts have found:

Finland is on the edge of Europe, a bit like an island. There is little traffic from other countries here.

Finland also has few residents, and they are used to keeping their distance. People do not go close to others.

Remote work at home also suits Finns. In other countries people want to be in the same place as others.

One reason for Finland's good coronavirus situation is Kela. Kela pays compensation if an employee does not receive a salary because of quarantine. That is why people can really stay away from work if, for example, there is coronavirus in the family.

Finns also trust the authorities. That is why the coronavirus guidelines are followed here.

## Nose Day's good result

![Nose Day presenters rejoice beside a fundraising pot, with musicians in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_419/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7420095faf08c0b729d/1605306793)

The Nose Day collection has gone well. More than 3.3 million euros was collected on Friday.

Nose Day was celebrated on Yle's channels, for example on TV1. During the Nose Day programme viewers had the chance to donate money.

The money will be used for the benefit of children around the world. This year children are being helped in Africa, Asia and South America. Finnish aid organisations deliver the help.
