# Saturday 31.3.2018 (radio)

Three roofs have collapsed in North Karelia because of the snow. In southern and central Finland the roads are very bad on Monday, when many people are driving home from the Easter holiday. The summer cottage tax has risen quickly. Nobel prize winner Malala Yousafzai has visited her home region in Pakistan for the first time since she was shot there more than five years ago. The United States plans to ask almost everyone who applies for a visa to the United States about their use of social media.

## Too much snow on roofs

![The roof of an old shop collapsed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10141157)

The roof of a former shop collapsed in Joensuu on Saturday. Photo: Yle / Tapio Laakkonen

In North Karelia, three roofs have collapsed in total yesterday and today.

There is so much snow on the roofs that they cannot hold it. On Saturday the roof of a grocery store collapsed in Joensuu. On Friday the roof of a warehouse fell down in Nurmes and in Kontiolahti. Nobody was hurt in the accidents.

The rescue service recommends that people remove snow from roofs in Eastern Finland.

## Dangerous roads on Monday

![Snow blowing on the Turku road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10052669)

The Helsinki rescue service recommends that people return home from the Easter holiday already on Sunday.

Many people still have a day off on Monday, but then the roads in Southern and Central Finland will be very bad. A lot of snow will fall and the wind will be strong. In Southern Finland, as much as 20 centimetres of snow may fall on Monday. On the main roads you must drive slowly and carefully.

## Summer cottage tax rises

![A summer cottage by a lake shore.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10120164)

Open image viewer

The summer cottage tax is about 250 euros a year on average. Photo: Jari Hautala's home album

The summer cottage tax has risen a lot in the 2000s.

For example, in Raasepori and Porvoo you must pay property tax of about 500 to 600 euros a year for a cottage. The average for the whole of Finland is about 250 euros a year.

Municipalities have been collecting cottage tax for just under 30 years. Now the municipalities collect about three times as much as at the start.

## Malala visited home

![Malala Yousafzai](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10141080)

Open image viewer

Malala Yousafzai. Photo: Sashenka Gutierrez / EPA

Malala Yousafzai, the Pakistani winner of the Nobel Peace Prize, has returned to her home town.

Yousafzai is now in the city of Mingora for the first time since she was shot there. A Taliban gunman shot a 15-year-old girl in the head in 2012. Yousafzai was seriously injured.

Malala Yousafzai is known around the world for defending girls' education and other children's rights. She won the Nobel Peace Prize in 2014, younger than anyone else.

Malala Yousafzai is visiting her home country Pakistan for four days. In Mingora she visited her old home and met childhood friends.

## More information about visa applicants

![The US Embassy in Finland's visa instructions on a computer screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10141076)

Open image viewer

The United States is tightening visa rules. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The United States plans to require almost all visa applicants to give information about their use of social media.

The US State Department proposes that a visa applicant must give a list of the social media services they use. In addition, they will be asked for all phone numbers, email addresses and trips abroad over the last 5 years. The aim is, for example, to find supporters of extremist groups.

The new rule concerns an estimated more than 10 million people every year. Finnish travellers do not need a visa to go to the United States.
