## Stora Enso

![Stora Enso pulp and paperboard mill behind the Hartaanselkä water system in the morning sun.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4090,h_2300,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88007761935313a0a73/1643357438)

Stora Enso's Oulu mills. Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

Forestry company Stora Enso is starting change negotiations.

The negotiations will be held at Stora Enso's packaging material mills in Oulu, Varkaus, Heinola, Anjala and Inkeroinen. About 1,300 people are covered by the negotiations.

Stora Enso says that the negotiations may lead to employees being laid off. Laying off means that an employee is ordered to be on leave for a while and is not paid a salary for that time.

Stora Enso says that the change negotiations are due to variation in demand, caused by uncertainty in the markets.

## Inflation

![Large food packages in the Heino warehouse in Vantaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5791,h_3257,x_0,y_199/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6715455eb28a01019be/1588761157)

Flour has become 45 percent more expensive in a year. Photo: Eleni Paspatis / Yle

Inflation, that is, the rise in consumer prices, continued strongly in December. Prices rose by about 9 percent from the year before, the same amount as in November.

Statistics Finland says that inflation was accelerated especially by the rise in the price of electricity and the rise in interest rates. Electricity became 64 percent more expensive and housing loan interest rates 124 percent compared with the year before.

The price of food rose 16 percent. Flour, eggs and butter have become the most expensive in a year.

## Migrants

![The back of a Frontex border guard's jacket, from the European border and coast guard, reads &quot;Frontex&quot;.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89611561c46602d13f9/1640261888)

Open image viewer

Frontex is the common border and coast guard agency of EU countries. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

The EU area received considerably more migrants last year than the year before. The European border agency Frontex says that the number of migrants grew by over 60 percent from the year before.

Frontex says that about 330,000 border crossings were recorded at the EU's borders last year. About half of the border crossers came from Afghanistan, Syria and Tunisia.

According to Frontex, the most border crossings were made via the Western Balkans. The second most popular route ran via the Mediterranean.

## Athlete of the year

![Iivo Niskanen celebrates at the Sports Gala 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_30/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105884663c06cb9964bd/1673555168)

Athlete of the year Iivo Niskanen. Photo: Lehtikuva

Skier Iivo Niskanen is Athlete of the Year. Niskanen was honoured last night at the Finnish Sports Gala.

Sports journalists chose Niskanen as the best athlete of last year, because he won 3 medals at the Beijing Olympics. Niskanen won gold, silver and bronze in Beijing.

The men's ice hockey national team, Leijonat, was chosen as Team of the Year. Leijonat won gold at the Olympics and the World Championships last year.
