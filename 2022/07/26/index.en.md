## Gas

![Gas pipes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_657/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98054862cc05acd4b94/1658381799)

Open image viewer

Many European countries have used a lot of Russian gas. Photo: Jens Buttner / Lehtikuva

EU countries have agreed to reduce their gas use. EU countries intend to reduce gas use by 15 percent between August and March.

The European Union wants to save gas, so that there is enough energy also next winter. In winter, a lot of energy is used for heating.

Europe has bought gas especially from Russia, but now Russia has reduced gas sales to Europe. Gas has become a subject of dispute because of the war in Ukraine.

On Tuesday the EU countries agreed that each country decides on its own saving measures. The idea is that the savings especially concern industry, not homes.

In many European countries gas is used more than in Finland, for example for heating homes. In Finland gas is not of great importance.

## Patients from Ukraine

![Hospital sign](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7683886017fa275e123/1643610707)

Patients who were brought from Ukraine have also been treated in Finnish hospitals. Photo: Jarkko Heikkinen, Yle

Patients have been brought from Ukraine to Finland for hospital care. Some of the patients have been wounded in the war.

11 Ukrainian patients have received treatment in Finland.

The authorities have arranged transport for the patients. In addition, Finnish volunteers have transported patients from Ukraine to Finland.

## The price of petrol

![Petrol pumps.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_424/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9132026204ebb235662/1646217660)

Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

The price of petrol has fallen. At the start of the summer petrol was very expensive, but now the price is slowly falling.

Last week a litre of petrol cost about 2 euros 30 cents. Diesel cost about 2 euros 25 cents.

A week earlier the prices were about 5 cents more expensive.

## The corona situation in Australia

![A corona patient with hands crossed in an isolation room, a nurse dressed in protective equipment beside them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_522/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88950861b0adaf5de33/1639141839)

Open image viewer

A corona patient in hospital. Illustrative image. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The corona situation in Australia is bad. Over 300,000 new corona infections have been found in a week.

In Australian hospitals the situation is difficult. There are many corona patients, and at the same time many nurses have become ill with corona.

The reason for the difficult situation is a new variant of the coronavirus. It spreads quickly in Australia.
