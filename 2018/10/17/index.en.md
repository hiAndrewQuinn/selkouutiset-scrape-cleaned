# Wednesday 17.10.2018 (radio)

Strike again soon. Confidence in the government. Moving the clocks. Cannabis in Canada.

## Strike coming next week

![JHL logo on the wall of a building](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10461048)

Open image viewer

The Union of Public and Welfare Sector Employees (JHL) represents employees. Photo: Mesut Turan / Lehtikuva

Public sector employees are going on strike again.

This means that it will be very difficult to get food in schools, day-care centres and care homes. Cleaning will also stop, and some libraries will be closed.

The strike starts next Monday and lasts two days. Over 10,000 employees will join the strike.

The strike is linked to the dispute about dismissing employees. The government wants a change in the law that makes it easier for small firms to dismiss employees.

The employee unions oppose the government's bill. The government took the dispute to Parliament, which discussed it yesterday and voted today.

In today's vote the government has, as expected, won Parliament's confidence. The decision was made with 101 votes for and 73 against.

The result of the vote means that the government will now take the disputed law forward.

The vote was linked to a big dispute about dismissing employees.

## Winter time is coming soon

![Two alarm clocks, one set to summer time and one to winter time.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10400756)

Open image viewer

Finland has summer time and winter time. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

A large part of Finns want to stop moving the clocks.

Finland has summer time and winter time. In summer the clocks are moved one hour forward, and in winter one hour back. A large part of Finns want to live permanently in winter time.

The Ministry of Justice has carried out a big survey on the matter. The survey received almost 700,000 answers. About 52 per cent of the respondents supported changing to winter time.

Finland cannot decide by itself that the clocks will no longer be moved. The matter must be decided in the European Union.

The next time the clocks are moved in Finland is next Sunday. Then winter time starts and the clock must be moved one hour back.

## Canada legalised cannabis

![In Canada, for example in Markham near Toronto, cannabis is grown for medical purposes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10460629)

Open image viewer

In Canada, for example in Markham near Toronto, cannabis is grown as medicine. Photo: Warren Toda / EPA

Canada has legalised all use of cannabis. The first cannabis shops have opened in the country.

Cannabis is an intoxicating substance. It is banned in Finland.

Canada is the second country in the whole world to legalise cannabis. Before Canada, only Uruguay had legalised the use of cannabis for all purposes.

The age limit for using cannabis is at least 18 years. In Canada, a lot of cannabis is used.
