In Finland, work is still divided into men's work and women's work. The Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency warns of potholes and holes on main roads. ## Women's work and men's work

![Engineering student Anna Äijö and midwifery student Valtteri Markula.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10638391)

Engineering student Anna Äijö and midwifery student Valtteri Markula. Photo: Markku Pitkänen, Yle

In Finland the division into men's work and women's work still exists.

The division is especially visible in the largest fields of study. For example, more than 80 percent of students in social and health care are women. In technology studies, on the other hand, more than 80 percent of students are men.

In some occupations the division is even sharper. It is very rare for a man to choose the profession of midwife, or for a woman to choose the profession of fitter, for example.

In some fields, change has happened when women have applied to fields that used to be dominated by men. The share of women, for example, in the professions of doctor or lawyer has grown. Men, on the other hand, rarely apply to fields dominated by women.

## Winter has made holes in the roads

![A pothole in asphalt](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10144529)

Open image viewer

There may be potholes and holes in the roads. Photo: Dalbir Ram / Yle

The Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency warns motorists about roads in poor condition.

The Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency says that the asphalt surfaces of many roads are in poor condition, because the winter weather has been so variable.

Asphalt crumbles if there are cracks in its surface that let water in. When the weather freezes, the water freezes and expands, and at the same time it breaks off pieces of asphalt.

Even a small hole in the asphalt can break a car's tyre and rim, if you drive into the hole too fast, the Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency warns.

## On the train you can only buy a ticket by card

![A conductor getting onto a train carriage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10644179)

Open image viewer

After September, you can no longer buy a train ticket with cash on the train. Photo: Roger Källman / Yle

VR is ending cash sales of train tickets on long-distance trains.

Cash sales of tickets end in September. After that, a train ticket can only be bought on the train with a payment card.

On commuter trains, VR stopped ticket sales completely in summer 2017.

## Lake ice is weaker than usual

![Leo Laukkanen tried ice fishing with water up to his ankles at Kyyvesi. The catch remained a dream.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10634021)

Open image viewer

Leo Laukkanen went ice fishing at Kyyvesi. There was water up to the ankles on top of the ice. Photo: Anton Jussila

The ice situation on Finland's lakes is poor. The ice is weaker in many places than it usually is at this time of year.

There is only a little strong clear ice. There is a lot of water, slush and snow on top of it. Because of the slush, the clear ice cannot get stronger, even though there have been hard frosts at times.

The poor ice situation is especially a problem for fishermen. .

## Finns value Yle Areena

![Areena](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10553575)

Open image viewer

78 percent of Finns use Yle Areena at least now and then. Photo: Johanna Kannasmaa / Yle

Yle Areena is the most respected online brand in Finland.

Taloustutkimus carries out an annual survey of online brands. Yle Areena has long been number one among domestic online brands. In the new survey, Areena also rose to number one when international brands were included in the comparison.

About 150 domestic and foreign brands are included in the comparison.

78 percent of Finns use Yle Areena at least now and then.
