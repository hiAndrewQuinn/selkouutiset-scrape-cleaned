## Hungary's elections

![Viktor Orbán waves his hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1962,h_1098,x_0,y_122/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-937315624a669a076c4/1649043245)

Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orbán. Photo: Attila Kisbenedek / AFP

Parliamentary elections have been held in Hungary. Hungarians voted in the parliamentary elections on Sunday.

The winner of the election is the party of Prime Minister Viktor Orbán. Orbán's Fidesz party got about 53 percent of the votes.

The election result means that Orbán can continue as prime minister. Orbán has been Prime Minister of Hungary for as long as 12 years.

Orbán's opponents did badly in the election. It was difficult for Orbán's opponents to campaign, because Orbán's party controls Hungarian television, for example.

Hungary is part of the European Union. However, Orbán has received criticism from EU countries that Hungary does not uphold democracy.

## Sanctions against Russia

![A Ukrainian man in front of a mass grave in the city of Bucha in Ukraine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_136/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-937774624b080e1d0f8/1649084608)

Open image viewer

Mass graves have also been found in the Ukrainian city of Bucha. Photo: Sergei Supinsky / AFP

The European Union is planning new sanctions against Russia. The sanctions are a punishment for Russia, which has attacked Ukraine.

Next, the EU may reduce its purchases of oil and coal from Russia. The reason is Russia's actions in Ukraine.

Ukraine says that Russia has killed many ordinary Ukrainians who were not soldiers. Ukraine says that in the city of Bucha, for example, Russian soldiers shot people on the street.

Russia says that it is not guilty of these acts.

## Corona figures

![Practical nurse Janina Kinnunen in protective clothing with a corona patient.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_10,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90015261dc1ea0a80f2/1641816347)

Open image viewer

There are still many corona patients who need hospital care. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Over the weekend in Finland there were 74 deaths linked to the coronavirus.

Almost 12,000 new corona infections were confirmed over the weekend. The figure does not include infections confirmed with a home test. Therefore, the total number of new corona infections is much higher.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, says that from now on it will report corona information only once a week. THL will always report the new corona figures on Thursday.

## Popularity of electric cars

![Tesla electric cars charging in Norway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4792,h_2696,x_0,y_217/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-862659615c0f93e8442/1730535785)

Open image viewer

The price of electricity has not risen as much as the price of petrol. Photo: Tore Meek / EPA

Many electric cars are now being bought in Finland. The popularity of electric cars is growing, because petrol is now expensive. The price of petrol has risen more than the price of electricity.

The decision to buy may, however, be difficult, because the buyer may have to wait for a new electric car for as long as a year. One reason for this is that there is a worldwide shortage of car components, that is, parts. This also raises the price of the car.
