![General view](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_34/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5076485b8facf696abc/1536142691)

Safety distances must also be observed in indoor sports. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

New corona restrictions are coming to the Helsinki region.

Only 25 people may take part in indoor events. Outdoors, 50 people may be at events. Safety distances must be observed.

Because of the new restrictions, many indoor hobbies in which safety distances cannot be observed will end. For example, all team sports cannot be organised.

The new restrictions come into force in a week's time.

## Budget proposal

![Toilet cleaning is often wanted to be left to a professional.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5432161)

Open image viewer

For example, cleaning service invoices reduce taxes. Photo: YLE / Minna Rosvall

The proposal for the state budget is ready. The budget proposal tells how the state will collect and use money next year.

Minister Annika Saarikko presented the budget proposal on Thursday. For example, the aim is to get more customers for service sector companies through taxation. It is good for people to buy services when their invoices reduce the buyer's taxes more than before. Such services include, for example, care of children and elderly people, and cleaning.

In the budget proposal the state's debt will grow by about 6.5 billion euros next year. However, that is less than was estimated in the spring.

The whole government will negotiate about the budget in September. The budget comes into force when Parliament approves it.

## Afghanistan

![An Afghan soldier looks at an exploded car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3648,h_2052,x_0,y_217/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83578360ffcce13b7fe/1627376941)

Open image viewer

Fighting between the government and the Taliban movement is fierce in Afghanistan. Photo: Ghulamullah Habibi / EPA

The situation in Afghanistan is difficult. The extremist organisation Taliban is taking more areas as foreign soldiers leave Afghanistan.

Finnish soldiers have also been in peacekeeping work in Afghanistan for 20 years. Now they have left the country.

The Finnish government intends to bring to Finland also Afghans who have worked for Finland in Afghanistan. Many of them fear the Taliban.

It is not yet known how many Afghans Finland will evacuate, that is, bring out of Afghanistan.

Many countries, for example the USA, Germany and Britain, have already evacuated thousands of Afghan workers.
