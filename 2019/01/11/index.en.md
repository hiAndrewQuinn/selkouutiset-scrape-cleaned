# Friday 11.1.2019 (radio)

The economic situation is changing. Sweden may get a government. Young people gathered for a demonstration for the climate. Algae was found in the Gulf of Finland. The weather is sunny.

## The economic upswing has ended

![Loan calculator](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10592612)

Open image viewer

On Yle's website yle.fi there is an interest calculator. With it you can calculate how the interest on a loan raises costs. Photo: Eetu Pietarinen / Yle

The situation of the Finnish economy is changing.

Recently the economy has been doing well; there has been an upswing. Now economic experts say that the upswing has ended.

Many big things are happening in the world that affect the economic situation. For example, the fact that Britain is leaving the European Union affects the world economy.

However, economic experts have good news for people who have a housing loan. Banks are not raising the interest on loans now.

The interest rate may rise in a year's time. The interest rate has been very low for a long time.

## Sweden may get a new government

![The king opened the session of the Swedish Riksdag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10422551)

Open image viewer

Sweden has been without a government for 4 months. Photo: Claudio Bresciani / Epa

In Sweden the parties may have agreed on a new government.

The parties have a proposal that includes 2 parties, the Social Democrats and the Green party. The proposed Prime Minister is the chair of the Social Democrats, Stefan Löfven.

The parties will decide on the matter over the weekend. Sweden has been without a government for 4 months.

## Young people protest for the climate

![Schoolchildren protested against climate change in front of the Parliament House in Helsinki on 11 January.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10592055)

Open image viewer

Young people demand that politicians and business leaders make right decisions. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Young schoolchildren gathered today for a climate demonstration in Helsinki.

They gathered on the steps of the Parliament House and protested for the climate. They demanded that politicians and business leaders make decisions that are good for the climate.

The young people said that they will still be living on the planet in 50 years. They are tired of adults not getting anything done.

The young people plan to hold a similar demonstration every Friday.

## Blue-green algae was found in the sea

![blue-green algae in an ice hole](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10592088)

Open image viewer

Blue-green algae is usually in the sea only in summer. Blue-green algae can be poisonous. Photo: Syke

Blue-green algae has been found in the sea water of the Gulf of Finland. The algae was noticed when the ice was blue-green.

Blue-green algae is usually in the sea water in summer, when it is hot.

There are many kinds of blue-green algae. Some of them bloom in cool water and under the ice.

This winter the sea water has been much warmer than usual for winter.
