# Monday 16.12.2019 (radio)

The activation model increased applications for disability pensions. The Estonian president apologised for the interior minister's speeches. There has been unrest in India. A record number of people have died from drugs.

## Activation model increased applications for disability pensions

![Man in the lobby of a TE Office](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11119202)

Open image viewer

The aim of the activation model, which ends at the end of the year, was to prevent long-term unemployment and increase employment. Photo: Emilia Malin / Yle

An assessment of the effects of the activation model on unemployment benefits has been carried out.

The assessment shows that many more people applied for disability pensions because of the activation model. The number grew by over 1,600 people last year.

The aim of the activation model was to increase employment. The activation model did not apply to people who applied for disability pensions.

The activation model ends now at the turn of the year.

## Estonian president apologised for interior minister's interview

![Mart Helme](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10966306)

Open image viewer

Estonian Interior Minister Mart Helme. Photo: Valda Kalnina / EPA

Estonian President Kersti Kaljulaid has called President Sauli Niinistö.

Kaljulaid apologised for the speeches that Estonian Interior Minister Mart Helme gave on Sunday. Helme, the chair of Estonia's right-wing populist party, criticised the Finnish government and Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Helme doubted whether Marin is a good prime minister.

President Niinistö told Prime Minister Marin and the government about Kaljulaid's apology.

## Unrest in India

![A protester opposing the new citizenship law in Bangalore, India, on 14 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11119254)

Open image viewer

A protester opposed the new citizenship law in Bangalore, India, on 14 December. Photo: Jagadeesh NV / EPA

There have been large demonstrations in India.

At least 6 people have died in the demonstrations, and 10 have been injured. The protesters oppose the new citizenship law. The law has been accused of oppressing Muslims.

The law was passed last week. It may allow millions of illegal immigrants to get Indian citizenship. The law applies to Hindus and Christians who have come to India from Bangladesh, Pakistan and Afghanistan.

The administration of Prime Minister Narendra Modi says that the new law protects Hindus and Christians in India's neighbouring countries who are at risk of persecution. The law does not offer the same benefit to Muslims.

The Muslim party is taking the citizenship law to the Supreme Court.

## Record number of drug deaths

![Syringe and medicines](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11119142)

Open image viewer

Deaths of people aged 20–29 increased the most. Photo: Hernan Schmidt / AOP

A record number of people died from drugs in Finland last year. The number of deaths rose by more than 20 per cent. There were 261 deaths.

The number of drug deaths has grown for 3 years in a row. The deaths of under-30s grew the most. A large part of drug deaths are linked to the use of opioids.

More people also died from alcohol last year than the year before.

Many more people die of dementia and Alzheimer's than 10 years ago.
