## Corona crisis and equality

![A nurse in a corridor, New Children's Hospital, Helsinki, 8.7.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4974,h_2798,x_468,y_417/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5868905d68c5ca6c94f/1613564915)

Open image viewer

Many women have had a hard time during corona times. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

The corona crisis has affected women more than men. This is what researchers say.

Many women have lost their jobs because of the corona crisis. Jobs have ended, for example, in the tourism sector and in restaurants, where there are many female workers.

The situation is different in health care. There, workers have had a lot of hurry and stress during the corona times. A lot of women also work in health care.

In addition, many women have had a lot of work at home, because they have been doing remote work and looking after children at the same time. Mothers looked after children more than fathers, when schools were closed because of corona.

In Finland a project is under way that studies the effects of the corona crisis on equality between men and women. The National Institute for Health and Welfare leads the project.

## Coronavirus vaccination during pregnancy

![A man holds his hand on the bare stomach of a pregnant woman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11308088)

Open image viewer

Coronavirus vaccination is safe also during pregnancy. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

It is good for women to take the coronavirus vaccination also during pregnancy. This is what the chief physician of the National Institute for Health and Welfare, Hanna Nohynek, says.

A woman gets a serious coronavirus disease more easily when she is expecting a baby. That is why the coronavirus vaccination is beneficial, Nohynek says.

Around the world many coronavirus vaccinations have been given to women who are pregnant. The USA and Britain, for example, have done so.

Studies show that the coronavirus vaccination does not harm the foetus. The baby develops normally even though the mother gets the coronavirus vaccination.

In Finland, for example, Interior Minister Maria Ohisalo has said that she has had the coronavirus vaccination. Ohisalo is pregnant.

## Football European Championship final

![Wembley](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5844,h_3287,x_0,y_301/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83072960e9f272d50b8/1625944712)

Open image viewer

The football European Championship final brings many spectators to Wembley stadium. Photo: PA Wire/PA Images

Corona worries the football European Championship. Today, Sunday, England and Italy play the European Championship final, the winner of which is the European champion.

The final match is at Wembley stadium in London. Over 60,000 spectators can get into the stadium. That is the largest number of spectators at a football match in Britain during corona times.

Experts fear that corona will spread among the crowd at the stadium and in the streets.

The football European Championship is the biggest sporting event in Europe this year.
