# Tuesday 13.12.2016 (radio)

Finland has got over the recession. The police are resisted more than before. Picking up passports is getting easier. A British newspaper raves about the Finnish way of life.

## Finland has got over the recession

![Customers in a shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9349128)

Open image viewer

Private consumption and investments bring economic growth. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The Bank of Finland estimates that Finland has got over the economic recession.

The Bank of Finland says that total production will grow by 1.3 percent next year. In the two following years, growth will perhaps be 1.2 percent.

Growth is supported, for example, by people buying a lot of goods or services. Investments also support economic growth. Compared with other euro countries, however, Finland's growth is slow.

## The police's use of force has increased

![The FN 303 is part of police equipment in only a few European countries.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9348848)

Open image viewer

The police can use, for example, a riot gun that does not kill and that works with air pressure. Photo: Yle

The police are resisted more than before and in different ways. When orders are not enough, the police use force. This year the police have used, for example, handcuffs and tasers more often than before.

15 years ago, officials were violently resisted just under 900 times. Last year there were already over 1500 cases. Very often the person resisting an official is drunk.

What is new is that the police have to keep groups representing different views apart, for example at demonstrations.

## Picking up a passport is getting easier

![A Finnish passport on a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7247172)

Open image viewer

Next year every municipality will have a place where you can pick up a passport. Photo: Yle

Next year, a Finnish passport can be picked up from more places than before. You can then pick up your passport at Matkahuolto parcel points.

There are points, among other places, in K shops, Siwa shops, Valintatalo shops and R-kiosks. Next year every municipality in Finland will have a passport pick-up point.

Quick passports, express passports and identity cards can also be picked up at a Matkahuolto parcel point.

## A British newspaper praises the Finnish way of life

![A summer lake landscape and a lakeside sauna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9349067)

Open image viewer

In the story, the British newspaper raves about, among other things, going to the sauna. Photo: AOP

The British newspaper Evening Standard tells the secret of Finns' well-being. The newspaper writes that the Finnish way of life is simple but does not go to extremes.

The newspaper advises people to forget Danish hygge and look for well-being in Finnishness. In hygge, people value small everyday things and, for example, light candles.

The British newspaper says that the first step to Finnishness is an uncomplicated attitude to life. The newspaper says that people are well in Finland because they eat well here. In Finland, people eat fish, rye, vegetables and arctic superfoods such as lingonberries and cranberries. The Evening Standard also says that Finns have clear skin and bright eyes. That may be because of the sauna.

The newspaper's story reminds readers that Finland is at the top of the world in many statistics. Finland is safe and equal, we have only a little corruption, that is, bribery, and people trust each other.
