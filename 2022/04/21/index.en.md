# Thursday 21.4.2022 (radio)

Corona infections decrease. Passenger mask mandate ends. Skin cancer in older people. Spring floods.. Weather.

## Corona infections decrease

![Hospital staff at the corona intensive care unit of Oulu University Hospital prepare an X-ray device.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2530,h_1423,x_2,y_452/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-922563621f4c38b1a40/1646650394)

The need for intensive care for corona patients is also decreasing. Photo: Päivi Köngäs / Yle

The number of corona infections is decreasing.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that the number of corona infections has been decreasing for 3 weeks now. Corona infections have decreased across the whole of Finland.

The need for intensive care for corona patients has also decreased. Corona vaccinations give good protection against serious corona disease.

Because the number of corona infections is decreasing, there is no longer a general mask requirement. People can wear a mask if they want to.

## Passenger mask mandate ends

![Passengers with masks on their faces wait for a bus in front of Ratina in Tampere on 2 September 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_307/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8719096178014c757f1/1635254644)

Open image viewer

Passengers no longer have to wear a mask. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The mask requirement also ends in transport.

VR says that passengers no longer have to wear a mask on trains. However, passengers can still buy a mask on the train if they want extra protection.

Finnair also gives up the mask requirement. From next week, Finnair will no longer require passengers to wear a mask on the aircraft. The mask requirement does, however, still apply if the departure or destination country requires it.

In the Helsinki region, for example, people can already travel on buses and trams without a mask.

## Skin cancer in older people

![Precancerous skin lesion on a man's scalp.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_238/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4673705a941704046d2/1519654876)

Open image viewer

Skin cancer in older people can start from small skin changes. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Skin cancer in older people is often not noticed. This is what a study by the University of Oulu shows.

Old men in particular are in a risk group. They often have skin cancer that is not noticed.

Researcher Suvi-Päivikki Sinikumpu says that it is also a good idea for a doctor to check an older person's skin during a health check.

Treating skin cancer is more effective if the skin cancer is found in time.

## Spring floods

![A field covered by spring flood in Metsäkylä, Hamia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_640,h_360,x_0,y_76/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-94423962610a81eed32/1650526927)

Open image viewer

Water has risen, for example, onto fields. Photo: Kiira Ikavalko / Yle

The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns of floods in southern and western Finland. The flood warning is in force until next Monday.

The water in the rivers is now high. The water level will rise further as warm weather melts the snow.

Water has already flooded roads and fields in various parts of southern Finland. In the Lahti area, for example, small roads that have been under water have been closed.

In western Finland, in Ostrobothnia, the flood will worsen further at the weekend and next week. Roads and even buildings may be at risk, as water rises in the rivers of Ostrobothnia.

In the south it will still be sunny and warm on Friday.

In the north the weather cools and cloudiness is greater.

In the southern and central parts of the country the temperature is between 11 and 17 degrees, and in the north between 5 and 10 degrees.

At the weekend the weather cools. On Saturday the temperature is below 10 degrees in almost the whole country.
