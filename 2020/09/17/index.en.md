# Thursday 17.9.2020 (radio)

There was an autumn storm in Finland on Thursday. Corona infections in Finland are growing. Finnair plans to stop flights in Finland. Smoking increases the risk of serious brain haemorrhage. The weather calms down after the autumn storm.

## Storm Aila

![Clearing trees in Vaasa after storm Aila](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3984,h_2241,x_0,y_106/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7198585f630760e3f79/1600325617)

There has been a storm in Finland. The storm was named Aila.

The winds of storm Aila have blown hard, and a lot of rain has fallen in different parts of Finland.

Storm Aila cut off the power to many homes. On Thursday afternoon, more than 90,000 homes were without electricity. There were also problems with the phone networks.

Storm Aila has also disturbed traffic. The strong wind knocked trees onto the streets. Ferry trips were also cancelled because of the strong wind.

## Corona infections

The number of corona infections is growing in Finland.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, reported 49 new corona infections on Thursday. In Finland there are now about 8,800 corona infections in total.

THL is worried about the spread of the coronavirus in Finland. Recently many new infections have been found. The coronavirus is now spreading especially among young people.

## Domestic flights

![A Finnair aeroplane on the apron.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4695,h_2641,x_0,y_242/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7118525f452dd8e4c18/1625819192)

Finnair is stopping domestic flights. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Finnair is stopping flights in Finland. Finnair plans to stop flights to 5 airports next spring.

After that, Finnair will no longer fly to the airports of Joensuu, Jyväskylä, Kajaani, Kemi and Kokkola.

Finnair is stopping flights because it has financial problems. The reason for the financial problems is the coronavirus.

Because of the coronavirus, Finnair will fly only 70 or 80 flights a day in October this year. Last October, Finnair flew over 350 flights a day.

## Smoking

![A man smoking.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10780580)

Open image viewer

Smoking can cause a brain haemorrhage. Photo: AOP

New information has been obtained about how dangerous tobacco is.

Tobacco is often the cause when a serious bleed happens in the brain. This is what a new Finnish study says.

Smoking increases the risk of a disease called subarachnoid haemorrhage. It is a dangerous disease that kills up to 50 percent of patients within a few months.

A Finnish study proved for the first time that there is a link between smoking and subarachnoid haemorrhage.
