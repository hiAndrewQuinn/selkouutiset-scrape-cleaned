# Wednesday 3.6.2020 (radio)

The government is supporting Finland's economy because of the corona crisis. There are few new corona infections and deaths in Finland. A demonstration against racism was held in Helsinki. Thai berry pickers will not come to Finland this summer. VR is ending ticket sales on long-distance trains.

## Support for the economy

![Minister of Education Li Andersson, Minister of Finance Katri Kulmuni, Prime Minister Sanna Marin, Minister of the Interior Maria Ohisalo and Minister of Justice Anna-Maja Henriksson leaving the press conference on the government's fourth supplementary budget proposal in Helsinki, on 2 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11382967)

The government is supporting Finland's economy because of the corona crisis. The government promises 5.5 billion euros in new support money, so that Finland and Finns get through the corona crisis.

The money will be used, for example, for children and young people. For example, it is wanted to offer children free hobbies.

The situation of young people will improve, because for example over 4,000 new places to start studying will be added to universities.

The government also intends to help municipalities that have financial difficulties because of the corona. Without state support, municipalities now find it hard to run services for people.

The government is handing out a lot of support because of the corona crisis. Therefore Finland's national debt will grow a lot this year.

## Finland's corona situation

![THL sign on Mannerheimintie in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_339/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6782375ec3516ecc544/1618662483)

Finland's corona situation is still good. There are only few new infections and deaths.

On Wednesday the Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, reported one new death caused by the coronavirus. In total, 321 people have died in Finland because of corona.

About 6,900 people in Finland have caught the coronavirus infection.

## Demonstration in Helsinki

![Anti-racism demonstration held at Senate Square in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11383467)

Open image viewer

Protesters in Helsinki. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

A demonstration against racism has been held in Helsinki. Protests against racism are now taking place in various parts of the world.

The protests started in the USA. The reason for the protests is the death of the Black man George Floyd. Floyd died because of police violence.

Many Americans were angry, because in their view the police treat Black people worse than white people.

Now the anti-racism demonstrations have spread as far as Helsinki.

## Thai berry pickers

![Thai berry pickers carry berry baskets in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6777416)

Open image viewer

Berries may be left in the forest, because Thai pickers will not come to Finland this summer. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Thai berry pickers will not come to Finland this summer. The reason is that Thailand is not giving berry pickers permission to leave for Finland.

Thai berry pickers are important to Finland's berry companies. Thais have picked wild berries in Finland, for example bilberries and lingonberries.

Now the berry companies are in trouble. They have to think about how Finnish wild berries can be collected this year.

## Long-distance train tickets

![Pendolino on the railway track.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10366536)

Open image viewer

Tickets will soon no longer be sold on long-distance trains. Photo: Soile Laaksonen / VR Group

VR is ending ticket sales on long-distance trains. Ticket sales end on 15 June.

The reason is the corona situation. VR does not want long-distance trains to be too full at busy times.

Passengers can buy tickets for long-distance trains online and from R-Kioski kiosks. Next week VR will also open ticket sales points in Helsinki and Tampere.
