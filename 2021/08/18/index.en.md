## Officials to Kabul

![Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto at a press briefing on the current situation in Afghanistan](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2692,h_1514,x_234,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-842287611a434ea8350/1629117514)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Finland has sent 2 officials to Kabul airport in Afghanistan. The officials help when people are brought from Afghanistan to Finland. This is what Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says.

Haavisto says that almost 70 Finnish citizens have asked for help. They want to go from Afghanistan to Finland. Finland is trying to bring to Finland also the staff of the Finnish embassy in Afghanistan.

There are 673 new corona infections in Finland. THL says that there are 2 new corona deaths.

There are now 106 people in hospital care. Of them, 29 receive intensive care.

In the last 2 weeks there have been almost 2,700 more corona infections than 2 weeks earlier.

## The National Coalition's corona vaccinations

![Pharmacy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4746,h_2670,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6119925dcd6e6b7e167/1573745161)

Open image viewer

Pharmacy. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The opposition party National Coalition proposes to the government that corona tests be done in pharmacies in future too.

The National Coalition wants there to be more testing points in the future than now. In the National Coalition's view the tests must also be cheaper than now.

The National Coalition's proposal is told by its chairman, Petteri Orpo. He spoke at the National Coalition's summer meeting in Seinäjoki. In Orpo's view, Finnish pharmacies are able to make corona tests.

Orpo says that corona tests are done much more in the rest of Europe than in Finland.

## Bankruptcies

![Restaurant Juttutupa. 15.4.2021. In the foreground, glasses and a beer tap can be seen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4256,h_2394,x_0,y_233/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-794969607808b9ce1d9/1618484288)

Open image viewer

Restaurant Juttutupa. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

Fewer companies are going bankrupt than before.

Statistics Finland says that there were 1.6 percent fewer bankruptcies at the start of the year than at the same time last year.

Especially fewer restaurants and hotels went bankrupt than last year. Bankruptcies of restaurants and hotels fell by over 16 percent.

There were more bankruptcies than before in agriculture and in the construction sector.
