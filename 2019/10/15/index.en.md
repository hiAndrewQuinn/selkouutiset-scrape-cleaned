# Tuesday 15.10.2019 (radio)

More money for municipal health care. Construction slows down. The USA has imposed sanctions on Turkey. A student restaurant is giving up beef. Kela opened a clear-language website.

## More money for municipal health care

![Krista Kiuru ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11020558)

Open image viewer

Minister of Basic Services Krista Kiuru. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Municipalities will get more money for health care. The municipalities will get money from the state so that they can hire more doctors and shorten waiting queues.

The Government's Minister of Basic Services **Krista Kiuru** says that the state will give 70 million euros more to health care next year. Over four years, about 200 million euros will be given to the municipalities for health care.

## Construction decreases

![Apartment blocks under construction](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9839989)

Open image viewer

Construction starts for houses will fall by 16% next year, forecasts the Construction Industry Federation. Photo: Antti Ruonaniemi / Yle

Building new homes is slowing down. The Construction Industry Federation says that next year fewer houses will be started than this year.

The start of new apartment blocks will fall by 16% next year. Thousands of jobs will disappear from construction sites.

Only in the Helsinki area is construction not decreasing. In the Helsinki area, construction is busy next year too.

## Kurds launch a counterattack

![Turkish and Turkish-background Syrian fighters photographed near the Turkish border in Manbij.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11020371)

Turkish fighters in Manbij. Photo: Zein Al Rifai / AFP

In Syria, Kurdish forces have started a counterattack against Turkey in northern Syria.

Kurdish forces and Turkish forces have been fighting near the town of Ras al-Ain.

The USA has imposed sanctions on Turkey. The USA says that Turkey must stop its attack on Syria. The USA is, for example, ending trade negotiations with Turkey.

## Student restaurants give up beef

![A cow in a pasture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5093985b9a180715098/1536825385)

Open image viewer

Cows produce carbon dioxide. Photo: Touko Yrttimaa

The student restaurants of the University of Helsinki are stopping the use of beef.

Student restaurants will no longer serve food with beef next year. Also, beef will no longer be used in sandwiches or salads.

The student restaurants of the University of Helsinki want to reduce carbon dioxide emissions that come from raising cattle.

## Kela opened its clear-language website

![Kela logo in a window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5258,h_2958,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4965025b3b63b8b566b/1578589144)

Open image viewer

Kela. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Kela has opened a clear-language website. The clear-language website of Kela tells, in easy Finnish, about Kela's benefits, that is, the allowances that you can get from Kela.

The website tells, for example, about housing benefit, unemployment daily allowance, maternity allowance, paternity allowance, pensions and moving to Finland.

Kela opened the clear-language pages today, Tuesday 15.10., because today is the Day of Clear Language.

(You can explore the pages here: www.kela.fi/web/selkosuomi )
