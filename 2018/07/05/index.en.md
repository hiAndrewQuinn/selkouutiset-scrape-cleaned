# Thursday 5.7.2018 (radio)

The agency Fimea is taking some blood pressure medicines out of pharmacies. The SDP is the biggest party in a new Yle poll. Many journalists will come to Helsinki during the meeting of the presidents of the USA and Russia.

## Blood pressure medicines

![Measuring blood pressure](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9795585)

Open image viewer

Many people need medicine because of high blood pressure. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

The agency Fimea is taking blood pressure medicines out of pharmacies. The agency's job is to watch over the safety of medicines.

Fimea says that some blood pressure medicines may cause cancer. The medicines contain the drug substance valsartan, which is not pure. It contains foreign substances. The impure valsartan was made in China.

About 20,000 people in Finland use the blood pressure medicines that are now being removed. They can go to a pharmacy to exchange the medicines.

Yliopiston apteekki pharmacy says that customers do not have to pay anything if the new medicine is more expensive than the old one. But the customer must keep using the old medicine until they get a new one instead.

## Support for the parties

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10289410)

Open image viewer

The SDP is the biggest party in the poll, and support for the Finns Party has risen the most. Photo: Yle News Graphics

The Social Democrats, or SDP, is the biggest party in a new Yle poll. A little over 20 percent of people who answered the poll supported the Social Democrats. The SDP is an opposition party, which means it is not in the government.

The government parties, the National Coalition Party and the Centre Party, are behind the SDP. Support for the National Coalition Party is a little under 20 percent. Support for the Centre Party has fallen below 17 percent.

Support has risen most for the Finns Party. The Finns Party is an opposition party, and it wants to reduce immigration. Support for the Finns Party is now a little over 10 percent.

There are no big changes in support for the other parties. The exact figures are on Yle's website.

## The meeting in Helsinki

![A view from the Helsinki Market Square showing the Presidential Palace and the Cathedral.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9915511)

Open image viewer

Helsinki will be in the world's news pictures on 16.7. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Many journalists will come to Helsinki during the meeting of the presidents of the USA and Russia. Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin will meet in Helsinki on 16 July.

About 2000 foreign journalists will come to follow the presidents' meeting. Among them are big international TV channels and star journalists.

Media companies are already looking for good places to film in Helsinki. For example, companies are interested in Allas Sea Pool at the Market Square. They want to build temporary studios on its terrace. The official media centre will be in Finlandia Hall.

The City of Helsinki is pleased that Helsinki is being noticed. But the city will not wash the streets or plant more flowers because of the meeting. The most important thing for the city is to show that in Helsinki everything works well and quickly.
