# Monday 3.2.2020 (radio)

A big strike threatens shops. The government is setting up a climate fund. Founding a party is becoming easier. The sequel to the film Frozen is breaking audience records.

## Strikes

![Entrance to Prisma](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10539029)

A strike could close Prisma department stores for a couple of days. Photo: Mesut Turanb / Lehtikuva

Big strikes threaten shops and the property services sector.

The employees' union PAM says that the strike will start in two weeks. Perhaps about 50,000 employees will take part in the strike.

The strike initially concerns the S Group. Because of the strike, for example, all Prisma department stores in Finland will close for 2 days in week 8, which is the skiing holiday week in southern Finland.

Strikes in the forest industry are also continuing. Because of the strikes, for example, many paper and pulp mills are closed.

All the strikes are due to employers and employees not being able to agree on working hours and pay.

## Climate fund

The chairs of the government parties Anna-Maja Henriksson, Maria Ohisalo, Sanna Marin, Katri Kulmuni and Li Andersson talked about the climate fund. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The government intends to set up a climate fund.

Hundreds of millions of euros will be directed from the fund to various climate measures. The money for the climate fund will come from the returns of the state development company Vake.

The government's goal is for Finland to be carbon neutral in 15 years' time, that is, Finland will no longer add greenhouse gas emissions to the atmosphere. If Finland intends to reach this goal, Finland must reduce emissions and increase carbon sinks.

The government parties have today been discussing Finland's climate solutions together. The meeting continues tomorrow. Then employment will be discussed.

## Founding a party

![Supporter cards for registering the Blue Future party.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9868498)

Open image viewer

The Blue Future party collected supporter cards in 2017. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Founding a new party may be easier in the future than it is now.

The Ministry of Justice is preparing a bill which says that electronic supporter cards may also be collected for founding a new party.

At the moment only paper supporter cards are accepted. They need the signatures of at least 5,000 people before a new party can get into the party register.

Collecting electronic supporter cards may be easier and quicker. Experts say that after the reform the number of small parties in Finland may perhaps grow.

## Frozen 2

![The cartoon character Elsa dances in a swirl of snow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2955,h_1662,x_0,y_9/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6207515def5cd677667/1726754163)

The children's film Frozen 2 is breaking audience records.

Frozen 2 is a Disney animated film. It is the sequel to the film Frozen: The Icy Adventure.

The first Frozen film was already very popular, but the sequel has risen to become the most-watched animated film of all time in Finland.

Frozen 2 premiered in Finland on Christmas Day. So far over half a million viewers have already seen it.
