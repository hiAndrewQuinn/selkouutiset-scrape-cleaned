# Sunday 12.1.2020 (radio)

Trade unions are ready for strikes. Health care is becoming more unequal. Many people do not want to become leaders. Britain is angry with Iran over the treatment of its ambassador.

## Trade unions are ready for strikes

![The Paper Workers' Union in Hakaniemi, Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4305,h_2423,x_0,y_404/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-404983592d83e1dfcd1/1496155249)

The Paper Workers' Union in Hakaniemi, Helsinki. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Three big trade unions are ready for industrial action.

The Paper Workers' Union, the Industrial Union and the Pro Trade Union say that they can start a big strike if no agreement is reached on the collective agreements. The strikes will start on 27 January and last two weeks if no agreement is reached. If the strikes start, they will affect over 30,000 employees.

The biggest dispute in the negotiations is about the "kiky hours". Kiky hours are extra hours for which no extra pay is given. The employees' trade unions want to remove kiky hours from the collective agreements.

## Health care is becoming more unequal

![Juha Koponen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11153366)

Open image viewer

Juha Koponen. Photo: Yle

Ordinary health care is not enough to look after all Finns, because specialist hospital care takes up a lot of money and staff. Yle has interviewed the new head of the large insurance company Lähi-Tapiola, **Juha Koponen.**

Koponen says that public health care must be developed a lot so that it serves all people. Specialist hospital care works well in Finland, but it takes up a lot of money and labour, Koponen says.

In the insurance company this shows in that people are buying health insurance more than before, so that they can go to a private doctor.

## Many people do not want to be leaders

![Three people talking in an office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10744875)

Open image viewer

Leading a company is feared to be too demanding. Photo: AOP

Many people do not want to be a leader at work. According to a study, many highly educated people are afraid that they will fail if they become leaders. Many also think that the work of leaders is too heavy or demanding.

Many highly educated people avoid management positions. According to the study, only about 10 per cent of highly educated people want to be leaders.

## A British ambassador was detained in Iran

![A view of the site where the Ukrainian plane crashed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11151613)

Open image viewer

The place in Iran where the Ukrainian plane fell. Photo: Abedin Taherkenareh / EPA

A dispute has broken out between Britain and Iran over diplomatic relations. The dispute arose because Iran's police detained Britain's ambassador in Tehran for a short time on Saturday.

Britain's ambassador **Rob Macaire** took part in a memorial event held for the victims of the Ukrainian plane's fall.

Britain is angry that its ambassador was detained. Countries do not usually detain other countries' ambassadors.
