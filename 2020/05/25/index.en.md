# Monday 25.5.2020 (radio)

Summer cottages are selling well. Prisons are lifting corona restrictions. Drivers are speeding on the roads. In Lapland, floods are feared.

## Many summer cottages are being bought

![A summer cottage for sale on the lakeshore.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1969,h_1108,x_570,y_428/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6793895ec78bd2d4008/1590136202)

The cottages that sell best cost about 100,000 euros. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

People are now buying many summer cottages.

Estate agents expected that the trade in summer cottages would go quiet because of the corona. This did not happen. In Finland, about 20 per cent more summer cottages were sold in the early part of the year than in the same period last year.

However, regional differences are large. In Pirkanmaa, cottage sales were up to 80 per cent higher than last year. More summer cottages were bought than before also in Uusimaa, Päijät-Häme and Central Finland.

## Restrictions are being removed in prisons

![Gate of Vantaa prison opens](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5625875cd95d4c5c166/1557749212)

Open image viewer

Vantaa prison. Photo: Laura Hyyti / Yle

Restrictions that were put in place because of the corona epidemic are being removed in prisons. Restrictions are being removed a little from 1 June.

Prisoners may again use gyms, saunas and libraries. Prisoners may also work more than they do now.

The rule on prisoners leaving is still strict. At the moment, a prisoner may only leave the prison if the reason is extremely important. An extremely important reason can be, for example, the funeral of a close relative.

## Dangerous speeding on the main roads

![Police carrying out speed control.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9737585)

Open image viewer

Drivers have been racing at dangerous speeds for several weeks. Archive picture. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / AOP

Many drivers drove at high speed at the weekend.

The police say that drivers were driving at up to over 200 when the speed limit was 100 kilometres per hour. In Uusimaa, the police took the driving licences of 54 drivers. Some drivers lost their licences because they were drunk.

Speeding took place in cars and on motorbikes.

People have been driving too fast throughout the spring. The police believe that the corona times have increased speeding. Some people drive very fast because there is only little traffic on the main roads.

## Floods are expected in Lapland

![Flood watchers at Lainaanranta](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6801485ecba7869cda2/1590405499)

Water rose in Rovaniemi on Monday morning by about one centimetre an hour. Photo: Timo Metsäjoki / Yle

A serious flood is expected in Lapland.

There was a lot of snow in Lapland in the winter. At the weekend the weather was warm in Lapland too. Because of this, the snow is melting quickly.

When the snow melts, the rivers flood. A serious flood is expected at least on the Ounasjoki river in Kittilä and the Kemijoki river in Rovaniemi. The flood may be record-breakingly bad.

Flood water may wet houses. Some main roads may be under water.

In the centre of Rovaniemi, buildings are being protected. For example, the science centre Arktikum is now empty. A flood embankment has been built around the building.
