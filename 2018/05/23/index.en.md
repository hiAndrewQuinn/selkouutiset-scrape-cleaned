# Wednesday 23.5.2018 (radio)

The number of crime reports is falling. More wind power is being built. People should avoid eating cod. Outdoor strawberries are ripening. A little owl popped up in Lammi.

## The number of crime reports has fallen

![The wall of a Southwest Finland police station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9822048)

Open image viewer

Making a crime report can be difficult in the countryside, because many police stations have been closed. Photo: Kalle Mäkelä / Yle

The number of crime reports made by citizens has fallen in Finland. So it looks as if crime has decreased.

However, the matter is a little more complicated.

These days it is difficult to make a crime report, because the police station has been closed in many places. From a small place it can be a long way to the nearest police station. So crimes may happen, but no one reports them to the police.

The ageing of the population also reduces the number of crimes, because young men commit the most crimes.

## The tallest wind turbines in the Nordic countries are coming to Finland

![Wind turbines.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10219675)

Open image viewer

In Finland the state supports wind turbines a lot, because producing electricity with wind power is expensive. Photo: Remko de Waal / EPA

A new wind farm is being built in Finland. It is being built on the coast of the Bay of Bothnia in the municipality of Ii.

It will have the 5 tallest wind turbines in the Nordic countries.

The new wind turbines are more efficient than the old ones. That is why the new wind turbines can produce electricity without state support.

The state supports wind power a lot, because producing it is expensive. About 5 percent of Finland's electricity is produced with wind power.

## Cod should not be eaten, says an environmental organisation

![Baltic Sea cod.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10219975)

Open image viewer

People should avoid eating cod, says an environmental organisation. Photo: AOP

The environmental organisation WWF says that consumers must stop eating cod caught in the Baltic Sea.

It is not good to eat cod, because their numbers have collapsed. Cod are suffering in the Baltic Sea because the sea is in poor condition.

In addition, cod are overfished, says the environmental organisation WWF.

## The strawberry season comes early

![A strawberry.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10218963)

Open image viewer

Outdoor strawberries are already blooming in many places in Finland. Photo: Tuija Veirto / Yle

This summer, outdoor strawberries are ripening early.

In Finland, a lot of outdoor strawberries are grown in North Savo. There, the harvest season is expected to start at Midsummer.

In southern Finland, strawberries are ripening already in mid-June. The peak strawberry season is right after Midsummer.

## A little owl popped into a tree

![A little owl sits in a tree taking a nap](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10219381)

Open image viewer

Leila Uotila photographed a rare bird visitor. A little owl is sleeping there Photo: Leila Uotila

One more owl news item. A really rare visitor has been seen in Lammi in Hämeenlinna.

Birdwatchers have hurried to see the small little owl.

Leila Uotila was the first to spot the little owl. She photographed the little owl, which was taking a nap in a tree.

The little owl has been seen in Finland only once before, and that was already 7 years ago.
