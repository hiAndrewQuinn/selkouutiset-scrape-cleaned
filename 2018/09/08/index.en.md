# Saturday 8.9.2018 (radio)

There are elections in Sweden on Sunday. Finland has trained police officers for 100 years. The founder of the Chinese online shop Alibaba is retiring. Saturday was the 64th hot day of the summer.

## The Swedish election

![Jimmie Åkesson of the Sweden Democrats at an election meeting in Landskrona.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10382077)

Leader of the Sweden Democrats Jimmie Åkesson. Photo: Johan Nilsson / EPA

In Finland's neighbouring country Sweden there are elections on Sunday. Opinion polls predict that the election may change Swedish politics.

Sweden now has a government whose prime minister is the Social Democrat Stefan Löfven. In opinion polls, however, support for the Social Democrats has fallen.

Sweden's largest opposition party is now the Moderate Party. Support for the Moderate Party has also fallen.

In opinion polls, support for the Sweden Democrats has risen. The Sweden Democrats oppose immigration. Other parties do not want to cooperate with the Sweden Democrats.

It may be difficult for Swedish parties to agree on a new government after the election. The old left-wing parties or the old right-wing parties may not get a majority in parliament, if a large part of the seats in parliament go to the Sweden Democrats.

## The police training anniversary

![Police University College building from outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6814923)

Open image viewer

The Police University College is in Tampere. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

Finland has trained police officers for 100 years. The state police training school was founded in 1918.

To mark the anniversary year, the Police University College held an open-doors event on Saturday. The public could get to know police training.

The Police University College is in Tampere. The first police school was founded 100 years ago in Helsinki.

## Alibaba's founder

![Jack Ma.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10394367)

Open image viewer

Jack Ma founded Alibaba in 1999. Photo: Laurent Gillieron / EPA

Jack Ma, the founder of the Chinese online shop Alibaba, is retiring. Ma will retire next week, when he turns 54.

Jack Ma founded Alibaba in 1999. Now Ma is China's 3rd richest person. Ma's fortune is about 35 billion euros.

It is unusual for a Chinese leader to retire as young as Ma. Ma also intends to remain active. For example, he is interested in education, and he has previously worked as a teacher.

## The last hot day

![A man napping in the garden.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10394600)

Open image viewer

On Saturday it was perhaps the last hot day of the summer. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Saturday has been a warm day. The temperature rose above the heat limit, that is, above 25 degrees, for example in Rauma and Salo.

This summer there have been 64 hot days in total. That is close to the record, which is from 2002. Then there were 65 hot days.

Yle meteorologist Kerttu Kotakorpi says that Saturday was perhaps this summer's last hot day. Next week rain will come to Finland and the weather will get colder.
