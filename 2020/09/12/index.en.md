# Saturday 12.9.2020 (radio)

Annika Saarikko does not want to open the government programme. Finnish MEPs support sanctions against Belarus. Finnish TV series and films interest investors. A historic peace meeting between the Afghan government and the Taliban has begun.

## Saarikko wants to keep the old government programme

![Annika Saarikko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11541364)

Minister and the new chairperson of the Centre, Annika Saarikko. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The new chairperson of the Centre, Annika Saarikko, says that the government programme does not need to be changed. Saarikko is the Minister of Science and Culture.

Saarikko says that the corona has weakened the Finnish economy. However, Saarikko says that a new government programme is not needed. Saarikko says that the economy will be taken into account in the budget negotiations, that is, the budget session.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin said on Tuesday that the SDP is ready to renew the government programme.

The government's 2-day budget session starts on Monday.

## Finnish MEPs support sanctions

![Riot police blocked the protesters' way to Independence Square in Minsk, the capital of Belarus, on Sunday 6 September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5067,h_2850,x_0,y_269/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7159095f54dde1588a0/1599397399)

Riot police blocked the protesters' way to Independence Square in Minsk, the capital of Belarus, on Sunday 6 September 2020. Photo: AOP

Most of Finland's MEPs support adding sanctions against Belarus and Russia. This is what a survey by the news service Uutissuomalainen says.

Of the 14 Finnish MEPs, 13 support the idea that the EU adds sanctions against Belarus.

Only the representative of the Swedish People's Party, Nils Torvalds, doubted the effect.

Almost all MEPs also support sanctions against Russia, if the poisoning of the opposition leader Alexei Navalny is linked to the Russian leadership.

A German study says that Navalny was poisoned with the Novichok nerve agent.

Russia denies the claims about the poisoning.

## Investors are interested in Finnish films

![Kariluoto (Holopainen) and Sirkka (Marketta Tikkanen) in a tender embrace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5658,h_3183,x_0,y_292/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-43850959f1f4f469a91/1509029596)

A scene from Aku Louhimies's film The Unknown Soldier. Photo: Juuli Aschan

Finnish TV series and films interest investors and financiers. Financiers invest money in films if they have international success.

Interest from investors has grown because of the film The Unknown Soldier, which Aku Louhimies directed.

Financiers include, for example, the private equity fund IPR.VC. Behind it there is about 70 million euros of capital.

The first investment was the crime series Sorjonen. It was shown on Yle.

A new company, Aurora Studios, also invests in films. The company's first investment is Aku Louhimies's new film Odotus. It is due to premiere early next year.

## Historic peace talks have started

![Taliban negotiator Abbas Stanikzai at the opening ceremony of the talks in Doha on 12 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11541391)

Open image viewer

Taliban negotiator Abbas Stanikzai (sitting) at the opening ceremony of the talks in Doha on 12 September. Photo: Karim Jaafar / AFP

The peace meeting between the Afghan government and the terrorist organisation the Taliban has begun. It is the first peace meeting of its kind in history between the two sides.

The meeting started in Doha in Qatar.

The talks are difficult, and they may last many years. Both sides must make big concessions.

The Taliban want Afghanistan to be a state that follows a strict interpretation of Islam. The current Afghan administration supports a more liberal direction.
