# Sunday 18.12.2016 (radio)

At least 100,000 Finns have a superbug. There was a winter storm in Spain. Finland's Christmas will be white only in the north. Finnish boxers did well at the Helsinki boxing night.

## Superbugs

![Street food in Kolkata](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9353825)

Open image viewer

Finns often get a superbug on a trip to Asia. Photo: Piyal Adhikary / EPA

At least 100,000 Finns have a superbug. That is the estimate of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

Ordinary antibiotics do not help against a superbug. However, a superbug often goes away without medicines. The person does not even know that he or she has had a superbug.

Sometimes a superbug causes a really bad illness. In Finland, people do not often die because of a superbug. In the whole European Union area, superbugs kill 25,000 people every year.

Finns usually get a superbug abroad. There are a lot of superbugs in countries where too many antibiotics are used. Then the superbugs learn to resist antibiotics.

In some countries you can get antibiotics even without a doctor's prescription. For example, in India antibiotics are used really a lot. It is precisely in Asia that Finns have a big risk of getting a superbug.

## The storm in Spain

![Flooded streets in the city of Murcia in Spain on 17 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9360067)

Open image viewer

It has rained really a lot in Spain. Photo: Marcial Guillen / EPA

There was a winter storm in Spain at the weekend. At least 2 people have died in the storm.

It has rained really a lot. On the Mediterranean coast, 3 centimetres of rain fell in an hour. Rescue workers have rescued people from cars that were stuck in the middle of the water.

During Sunday, the rains in Spain have decreased.

## Christmas weather

This Christmas there will probably be snow only in the north. Photo: YLE / Pentti Kallinen

Here in Finland, Christmas will be white only in the north. In the south there will be no snow at Christmas.

Yle's meteorologist Anne Borgström says that in the south the temperature is above zero. That is why the snow melts away and no new snow falls.

## The Helsinki boxing night

And some sports news. There was a boxing night in Helsinki on Saturday. Finnish professional boxers did very well.

Finland's Edis Tatli beat Italy's Manuel Lancia. The win means that Tatli is still the European champion.

Eva Wahlström also won her fight and is still world champion. Wahlström is the best woman boxer Finland has ever had.

The Helsinki boxing night also saw a quick win. Finland's Robert Helenius hit hard and won his fight in 48 seconds.
