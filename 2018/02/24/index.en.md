# Saturday 24.2.2018 (radio)

Skier Iivo Niskanen won a gold medal at the Olympics. Estonia has celebrated its 100th anniversary. Finns have also celebrated Estonia. The freezing weather continues.

## Niskanen won gold

![Niskanen celebrates](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10091141)

Iivo Niskanen is an Olympic champion. Photo: Lehtikuva

Finland has won a gold medal at the Olympics. Skier Iivo Niskanen won the 50-kilometre ski race.

This was Finland's first gold medal at the Pyeongchang Olympics in South Korea. That is why Finns are really happy about Niskanen's victory.

Iivo Niskanen has received a lot of congratulations on social media. President Sauli Niinistö and Prime Minister Juha Sipilä also congratulated Niskanen on social media.

Over the 50 kilometres, Iivo Niskanen was clearly better than the other skiers. The silver medal and the bronze medal went to Russian athletes.

## Estonia celebrates independence

![Estonia 100 poster](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10090744)

Open image viewer

Estonia is now 100 years old. Photo: Yle

There is a celebration in Finland's neighbouring country Estonia. Estonia had its 100th anniversary on Saturday.

In Estonia's capital Tallinn, there was a lot of celebration programme on Saturday. In Tallinn, people saw for example a big military parade.

Estonia became independent exactly 100 years ago, on 24 February 1918. Before that, Estonia was part of Russia.

During the Second World War, in 1940, Estonia became part of the Soviet Union. That meant that Russia decided Estonia's affairs. When the Soviet Union broke up, Estonia became independent again in 1991.

Independence is an important thing for Estonians. Estonia has developed fast since Estonians have been able to decide their own affairs. Today Estonia is a modern and rich European country.

## Finland celebrates Estonia

![Yle's link tower lit up in the colours of the Estonian flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10090612)

Open image viewer

Blue, black and white shine on the Pasila link tower this weekend. Photo: Yle

Finns have also celebrated Estonia. In Finland, for example, flags were flown on Saturday. Flags were raised in honour of Estonia.

Estonia's colours, blue, black and white, can be seen in Helsinki all weekend. Yle, for example, has lit up the Pasila link tower in Estonia's colours.

Estonia is a dear neighbour to Finns. Finns also like Estonia because Finnish and Estonian are related languages.

In Finland's official **video greeting** to 100-year-old Estonia, President Sauli Niinistö and Prime Minister Juha Sipilä also speak Estonian.

## The frost is biting

The weather stays cold. The coldest day next week is Tuesday. Then it will be almost 20 degrees below zero in southern Finland. Because of the wind, the air feels even colder.

Later next week, it will be about 10 degrees below zero during the day. So March begins in cold weather.
