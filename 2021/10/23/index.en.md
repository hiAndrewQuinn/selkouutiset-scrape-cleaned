# Saturday 23.10.2021 (radio)

Food aid. Treatment of Alzheimer's. Harassment of schools and restaurants. Death during a film shoot. The weather.

## Food aid

![The Espoonlahti parish distributed food aid on Monday morning, 22 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11852620)

Open image viewer

Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The need for food aid has at least doubled during the corona time. About 200 000 people in Finland depend on food aid per year.

In addition to the unemployed, there are more and more young people and people who work among those who receive aid.

During the corona time, among others, low-income families with children have applied for food aid when school meals were not available.

Poverty researchers criticise the political atmosphere, which has perhaps become harsher than before towards low-income people.

## Memory illnesses

![Computed tomography image of the brain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7680,h_4320,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81723760be479abbab9/1623082994)

Alzheimer's disease degenerates the patient's brain. The degeneration can be seen in brain imaging studies. Photo: Mohamed Abdelrazek / AOP

Drug treatment for Alzheimer's disease must be started earlier than now. This is what professor of memory diseases Anne Koivisto says.

In Finland almost 15 000 new cases of memory disease at the dementia level are diagnosed every year. The most common is Alzheimer's disease. It can progress without symptoms for up to about twenty years.

Starting treatment as early as possible is important also for society.

At the stage of mild dementia, the cost of care is more than 2.5 times that of the early stage of the disease.

## Corona harassment

![Maria Ohisalo, dressed in a collared shirt and knitwear, in the studio of Ykkösaamu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8708456173bb3415227/1634998349)

Open image viewer

Minister of the Interior Maria Ohisalo. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Minister of the Interior and chairperson of the Greens Maria Ohisalo says that police have been needed in school yards to keep the everyday life of pupils safe.

Ohisalo was a guest on Yle's Ykkösaamu on Saturday.

Ohisalo said that police have been needed because corona critics have been in school yards.

Ohisalo also spoke up for entrepreneurs and the culture sector. For example, restaurants have been harassed when they have required customers to have a corona pass.

The corona pass is a certificate of a corona vaccine series, a negative test result or a corona illness that the person has had.

For example, restaurants and nightclubs can require customers to have a corona pass in areas where there are corona restrictions.

## Shooting on a film set

![Alec Baldwin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5170,h_2908,x_0,y_590/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-87024161722a4d658e0/1634880692)

Open image viewer

Hollywood actor Alec Baldwin. Photo: Angela Weiss / AFP

New information has been obtained in the USA about the death during a film shoot.

A 42-year-old woman died when an actor shot her with a gun used in films. The woman was the film's cinematographer. The director of the film was also injured.

The police say there was a real bullet in the gun. The film's assistant director thought the gun had only blank cartridges. He gave the gun to actor Alec Baldwin.

The police say that no one has been charged.
