## Annika Saarikko wants to be leader of the Centre Party

![Annika Saarikko](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11472107)

Annika Saarikko. Photo: Antti Lähteenmäki / Yle

Annika Saarikko is aiming to become the leader of the Centre Party.

Annika Saarikko has been on parental leave for about a year. She will continue her work as Minister of Science and Culture next week.

Right now the leader of the Centre Party is Katri Kulmuni. She wants to continue as leader. A survey made by Yle in July shows that Saarikko is a more popular candidate for leader of the Centre Party than Kulmuni.

The Centre Party will choose the party leader in Oulu in September.

## The number of corona infections in Finland has stayed the same

![A woman waves at a bus to stop in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11471267)

Open image viewer

The number of corona infections has stayed the same in Finland. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Last week, a total of 51 new coronavirus infections were found in Finland. The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health says that there were the same number of infections as at the end of June.

Most of the new infections were in the hospital districts of Helsinki and Uusimaa and Southwest Finland.

No infections were found in 14 hospital districts. There are 21 hospital districts.

In total, more than 7,400 coronavirus infections and 329 deaths linked to the coronavirus have been found in Finland.

## Very few tourists visited Finland in June

![The giant terrace of Senate Square in Helsinki in July 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_419/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7013145f17c27ca4c51/1617157080)

Open image viewer

Finns went out in June, but hardly any tourists were seen in our country. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Very few foreign tourists visited Finland in June. There were 93 per cent fewer foreign tourists in Finland than in June last year. The reason is the coronavirus.

In June there were almost as few tourists in Finland as in April and May.

Finns travelled in their own country a little more than in the spring. However, compared with June last year, the number of Finnish travellers fell by almost 40 per cent.

## NASA's Mars probe has set off

![A six-wheeled vehicle with cameras and other research equipment. The ground and sky are coloured by red dust. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11468998)

Open image viewer

This is how an artist imagines the Perseverance rover on Mars's surface, which is coloured red by iron oxide, under a dusty sky. NASA/JPL-Caltech Photo: NASA/JPL-Caltech

The US space agency NASA has launched a Mars probe. The probe set off from Florida on its journey towards the planet Mars.

On board the probe is a research station that looks for the answer to, for example, whether there was ever life on Mars.

There is also Finnish equipment on board the probe.
