Finland went into debt quickly during the corona crisis. However, the additional debt is average compared with other old EU countries.

Yle calculated the debt increase from Eurostat statistics.

During the worst corona period Finland took on about 5,200 euros of extra debt per inhabitant. Sweden and Denmark went into debt clearly less than Finland.

Belgium, France and Austria went into debt the most. In them debt rose by about 8,000 euros per inhabitant.

Finland has paid very low interest on the debt. This year the interest is about 700 million euros.

In next year's budget proposal the sum is double, 1.5 billion euros.

## Electricity consumption

![Electricity consumption meter of an iPhone charger. The consumption shows 0 watts.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5633,h_3168,x_1,y_208/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1005448631afb64a840f/1662713022)

Electricity cuts in Finnish homes are the last option if electricity runs short. Everyone can save electricity themselves. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Electricity use fell in September, says the grid company Fingrid. In Finland on average 7 percent less electricity was used than in September last year.

Finns have been asked to save electricity, because there may be a shortage of electricity in winter. Fingrid says that the EU is also aiming to reduce peak consumption by 5 percent.

At worst an electricity shortage could lead to electricity cuts. The cuts are at most 2 hours at a time.

Fingrid stresses that electricity cuts in homes are the last option.

## VR and electricity

![Kokkola railway station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4970,h_2796,x_0,y_274/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-43252859d1d98fd4ef4/1664443875)

Open image viewer

The rising electricity price is also hurting VR. Photo: Nathalie Lindvall / Yle

The continuing rise in the electricity price also shows in the finances of the railway company VR.

VR's trains use about 1 percent of Finland's electricity. Even in normal times the share of electricity in VR's costs has been about 10 percent.

In the coming winter the share of electricity in the trains' costs may be over 20 percent.

VR says that it is not yet intended to raise ticket prices. VR is considering, for example, whether freight traffic can be run at night.

## Nobel Prize

![Smiling Pääbo holds his hand on top of a skeleton's head.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12647741)

Open image viewer

Palaeogeneticist Svante Pääbo and the research subject. Photo: Karsten Möbius / Max Planck Institute

The Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine goes to Svante Pääbo, a researcher of the human genome and a biologist.

The Swedish Pääbo is rewarded for discoveries related to human evolution.

The Nobel Committee says that Pääbo has, among other things, succeeded in sequencing, that is, decoding, the Neanderthal genome.

He also discovered a human species that was not known before. This species is the Denisovan, who lived at the same time as modern humans like us.

The genome is still visible in us, among other things in the immune system's reactions, that is, the body's defence against inflammation.
