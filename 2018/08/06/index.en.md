# Monday 6.8.2018 (radio)

There has been an earthquake on the island of Lombok in Indonesia. Big cruise ships are bringing a lot of tourists to Helsinki this summer. Experts by experience in integration have been trained in Jyväskylä. There was an accident at the Linnanmäki amusement park. The Pori housing fair interested Finns.

## The earth shook in Indonesia

![building rubble in the city of Denpasar](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10339542)

Open image viewer

The earthquake did a lot of damage in Indonesia. Photo: Made Nagi / EPA

There has been an earthquake on the island of Lombok in Indonesia.

About 100 people have died in the quake. Several hundred are injured. Indonesia's authorities say that all the dead are Indonesians.

The quake was on Sunday evening. It was also felt on Bali, Lombok's neighbouring island, and on the Gili islands between Bali and Lombok.

After the quake, about 10,000 people have been moved away from Lombok.

## Cruise ships bring tourists

Big cruise ships are bringing a lot of tourists to Helsinki this summer. In total, half a million cruise tourists visit Helsinki.

For example, on Monday 3 cruise ships came to Helsinki. The ships were so big that together they have room for more than 11,000 passengers.

## Experts by experience in integration graduated

![Sika or siika? Strange differences in the Finnish language are learned on the classroom whiteboard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9084997)

Open image viewer

An expert by experience knows from their own life what integration is. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Experts by experience in integration have been trained in Jyväskylä. 7 people have graduated from the training.

An expert by experience has their own experience of integration. They have come to Finland as a refugee, learned the language and found their own place in Finland.

Experts by experience in integration can help for example in developing social and health services or in the social rehabilitation of refugees.

The youngest expert by experience is a 19-year-old upper secondary school student, and the oldest is a 41-year-old student of practical nursing.

## There was an accident at Linnanmäki

![Visitors on the Raketti ride at Linnanmäki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10340252)

Open image viewer

Accidents do not usually happen at Linnanmäki. Photo: Onni Ojala / Lehtikuva

There has been an accident at the Linnanmäki amusement park. A 9-year-old girl fell from a ride called Mustekala (the Octopus).

The girl was taken to hospital, but she was not badly injured.

The cause of the accident is not yet known. Linnanmäki says that there was no technical fault in the ride.

In Finland it is really unusual for an accident to happen at an amusement park.

## The housing fair had a large audience

![kitchen and dining room](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10336164)

Open image viewer

At the housing fair, people get to know Finnish building. Photo: Mari Kahila / Yle

The Pori housing fair interested Finns. About 120,000 people visited the fair.

The housing fair area had 28 detached houses and 1 terraced house, where Finnish building and living were presented. The fair area also had a service home for old people and a children's daycare centre.

The Pori housing fair lasted a month. It ended on Sunday.
