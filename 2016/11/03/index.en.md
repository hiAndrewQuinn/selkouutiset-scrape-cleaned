# Thursday 3.11.2016 (radio)

A pharmacy in Lahti may have sold wrong medicines. Many Finns want to live in the countryside. The SDP is now Finland's most popular party. At Seinäjoki hospital, patients are asked to stop smoking before surgery. Many charter flights are coming to Lapland in December.

## Wrong medicines

![The Renkomäki pharmacy in Lahti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9267893)

Open image viewer

The Renkomäki pharmacy in Lahti may have sold wrong medicines. Photo: Yle / Mika Halme

A pharmacy in Lahti may have sold wrong medicines. Wrong medicines may have been sold at the Renkomäki pharmacy.

The authorities say that nobody's health has been in danger because of the wrong medicines. However, the authorities are asking the police to investigate the matter.

The Renkomäki pharmacy has already got a warning before. Now its licence is going to be withdrawn, that is, the pharmacy may no longer be allowed to continue operating.

## The best place to live

![A view of Lake Vesijärvi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8980018)

Open image viewer

Many Finns want to live in surroundings like this. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

Many Finns want to live in the countryside. In a new survey 30 percent of respondents say that the best place to live is in the countryside.

Many residents of Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa also dream of living in the countryside.

Only about 10 percent of Finns want to live in the centre of a big city.

Professor Mari Vaattovaara says that many Finns want peaceful and safe surroundings. They think that such surroundings can be found in the countryside.

It is also important for Finns that nature is close to home.

## Support for the parties

![Party support graphic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9269334)

Open image viewer

The SDP is now the most popular party. The Centre Party and the National Coalition Party come after it. Photo: Yle News Graphics

A new survey says that the SDP, that is, the Social Democrats, is now Finland's most popular party. In the survey more than 21 percent of respondents supported the SDP. The SDP is an opposition party, that is, it is not in the government.

There are also changes in the support for the government parties. Support for the Centre Party fell below 20 percent in the survey. Support for the National Coalition Party rose to almost 19 percent.

Party support figures interest Finns now, because next spring there are municipal elections in Finland.

## Smoking among surgery patients

![A catheter valve procedure, or TAVI operation, at Tyks in October 2016](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9225784)

Open image viewer

A patient recovers from surgery better if he does not smoke. Photo: Jussi Kallioinen / Yle

Seinäjoki Central Hospital has got good results when patients have been asked to stop smoking before surgery.

If a patient is able to stop smoking about 2 months before surgery, the operation goes better and the patient recovers faster.

Doctor Hannu Puolijoki has now travelled from Seinäjoki to South Africa. He is telling hundreds of doctors about the Seinäjoki project at a big conference.

## Lapland's Christmas flights

![Ivalo airport in spring 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8738949)

Open image viewer

Many more tourists will come to Ivalo airport this Christmas than last Christmas. Photo: Pirita Näkkäläjärvi / Yle

Many tourists are coming to Lapland in Northern Finland in December. There is a lot of traffic at Lapland's airports then.

About 450 charter flights will come to Lapland's airports in December. For example, more than 90 charter flights will come to Ivalo airport.

Many tourists are also expected in Lapland after Christmas, when new regular scheduled flights to Lapland start from Central Europe.
