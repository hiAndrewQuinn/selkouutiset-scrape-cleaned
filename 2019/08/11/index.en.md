# Sunday 11.8.2019 (radio)

The police are investigating barber shops and hairdressers that take advantage of foreign workers. Finland's new government's first budget will be ready this autumn. The Muslim festival of Eid al-Adha has begun. Young wrestler Said-Magomed Tasukhanov has died.

## Undocumented workers are being exploited

![A barber's washing bench.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6889674)

An undocumented worker often gets too little pay. Photo: Elina Kinos / Yle

First, the matter of foreign workers.

The police are investigating many barber shops and hairdressers that take advantage of foreign workers. Exploitation can mean, for example, that a worker gets too little pay.

The victim of exploitation is often undocumented, that is, they do not have permission to be in Finland. An undocumented worker often does not dare to report the exploitation to the police.

Undocumented workers are also exploited in fields other than barber shops. For example, undocumented workers who get very little pay work in restaurants and in construction.

The information comes from Yle's MOT programme, and you can read more about the matter on Yle's website. The address is yle.fi.

## The budget is being prepared

![Antti Rinne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10911697)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Antti Rinne's government is making a budget for the first time. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finland's new government's first budget will be ready this autumn. The budget shows what the state will spend money on next year.

The budget proposal will be ready next week. After that the government still negotiates on the proposal before the budget is ready.

According to experts, the most important thing in the budget is how new jobs are created in Finland. The task is difficult, in the experts' view, because economic growth is slowing down.

## The Muslim festival has begun

![A family celebrating Eid al-Adha in Itäkeskus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10917870)

Open image viewer

Finland's Muslims also celebrate Eid al-Adha. Photo: Tanja Heino / Yle

The Muslim festival of Eid al-Adha has begun. The festival is linked to the pilgrimage, that is, the hajj, to Mecca in Saudi Arabia.

Eid al-Adha is also celebrated elsewhere. Finland's Muslims also celebrate Eid al-Adha.

Eid al-Adha lasts for many days. During the festival, for example, animals are slaughtered.

Many Muslims spend Eid al-Adha with their family. Good food and gifts for children are part of the festival.

## A young wrestler died

![Said-Magomed Tasukhanov.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10917771)

Open image viewer

Said-Magomed Tasukhanov died on Saturday. Photo: Helsingin Paini-Miehet r.y.

Finally, sad news from the world of sport: young wrestler Said-Magomed Tasukhanov has died.

18-year-old Tasukhanov died after a car accident.

The accident happened in Estonia, when Tasukhanov was returning to Finland. He had trained in Russia in the summer.

Tasukhanov was Finnish champion 5 times in youth categories. In the spring he won his first Finnish championship medal in the adult category.

Tasukhanov received Finnish citizenship 2 months ago.
