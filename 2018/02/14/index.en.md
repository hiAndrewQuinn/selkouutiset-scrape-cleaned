# Wednesday 14.2.2018 (radio)

Weakening basic security affects unemployed people, families with children and students. Efforts are being made to help upper secondary school students cope. Prince Henrik of Denmark has died. Finland's Olympic team is knitting a blanket for the baby of the Finnish presidential couple. Wednesday is Friendship Day.

## Basic security has weakened

![Twenty-euro banknotes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10032645)

Open image viewer

Unemployment benefit and study grant have fallen behind, because they have not been raised. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The weakening of basic security has affected especially unemployed people, families with children and students. Basic security benefits have been weakened by the fact that the benefits have not been raised for a long time. Child benefits and the study grant for university students are also smaller, because they have not been raised.

The benefits have now stayed the same for a long time, although many prices have gone up. For example, unemployment benefit or the study grant no longer covers as much as before.

## Upper secondary school students are helped to avoid exhaustion

![A lesson in a school classroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10007544)

Open image viewer

Studying in a group supports coping. Photo: Mostphotos

Efforts are being made to help young people at upper secondary schools cope. About 20 percent of girls and just under 10 percent of boys experience exhaustion at upper secondary school. Some upper secondary schools are trying to give students a ready-made timetable for the whole year.

Studying in the same group makes teaching easier, because the teacher and fellow students do not change so often. The teacher is also able to follow better how the pupils are learning.

## Prince Henrik of Denmark has died

![Prince Henrik](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10069337)

Open image viewer

Prince Henrik of Denmark was 83 years old. Photo: Keld Navntoft / EPA

Prince Henrik of Denmark has died. The Danish royal court announced the prince's death on Wednesday morning. The prince had been out of the public eye for a long time. He suffered from dementia, which is a memory illness. Prince Henrik of Denmark was 83 years old when he died.

## Finland's team knits a blanket for the presidential couple's baby

![Olympic team knitting project, blanket for the baby of the presidential couple](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10074622)

Open image viewer

The knitting of the blanket has received good attention from journalists following the Olympics. Photo: Lehtikuva

Finland's Olympic team is knitting a gift for the baby of the Finnish presidential couple. The team is knitting a blanket in Pyeongchang, South Korea, for the son of the President of the Republic, Sauli Niinistö, and Mrs Jenni Haukio. The idea of making the blanket came from skier Kaisa Mäkäräinen, who is an enthusiastic knitter.

Friendship Day is remembered with a card or chocolate

![Chocolate heart](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5463084)

Open image viewer

On Friendship Day, for example, heart-shaped chocolates are given. Photo: YLE / Arja Lento.

Today, Wednesday 14 February, Friendship Day is celebrated.
Celebrating Friendship Day in Finland is a fairly new custom. The day came into the calendar in Finland only in the late 1980s. There are different views about the origin of Friendship Day, or Saint Valentine's Day. The day is based on the story of a saint. Many people remember friends with a card, for example, or with heart-shaped chocolate.
