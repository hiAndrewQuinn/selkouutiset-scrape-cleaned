# Tuesday 16.2.2021 (radio)

Social assistance. Remote work. Coronavirus vaccinations. Staff restaurants. Weather forecast.

## Social assistance

![Kela card and money in a purse](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11238286)

Open image viewer

More social assistance was applied for last year than before. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The coronavirus situation has increased applications for social assistance.

The number of people receiving social assistance grew by 10 percent last year. In Uusimaa the need for social assistance grew even more.

Young women in particular now need social assistance. The coronavirus situation has reduced work especially in those sectors where young women work.

## Data security and remote work

![A man's hands on a laptop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_170/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6049005db6de437d1ca/1712246971)

Open image viewer

Scam messages have increased during the coronavirus time. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The coronavirus situation and tiredness from remote work increase data security risks.

The Digital Agency has asked Finns how they feel at work. Almost half of Finns say that this spring feels heavier than last year.

The Digital Agency says that stress and tiredness can increase mistakes, and data security can be put at risk.
Criminals take advantage of people's tiredness. For example, various scam phone calls and scam emails have increased.

## Coronavirus vaccinations

![Päijät-Häme wellbeing services coronavirus vaccination point at the Trio shopping centre in Lahti, a man walking inside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-771866602264483f647/1612866886)

Open image viewer

Coronavirus vaccination point. Photo: Juha-Petri Koponen / Yle

Coronavirus vaccinations for elderly people are going ahead in different ways in different parts of Finland. In some cities, vaccinations of elderly people over 80 started at the end of January. In other cities, vaccinations are only starting now.

Cities have received vaccine at different times. The vaccinations are also affected by which vaccine the cities have received. For example, one of the three vaccines is not given to people over 70.

A little over 210,000 people in Finland have now received the coronavirus vaccine.

## Staff restaurants

![Chef Jenni Klasila and head chef Jetro Luumi prepare food in the kitchen of Huoltamo Restaurant. 3.2.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_104/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-769456601a6c7c2ecc4/1612346405)

Open image viewer

Chef Jenni Klasila and head chef Jetro Luumi prepare lunch in a staff restaurant in Helsinki. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

Staff restaurants continue to be in a difficult situation. Many employees are working remotely and only a few people eat in staff restaurants.

Large staff restaurant companies say that only about 50% of their former number of diners come, compared with before the coronavirus.

Many staff restaurants are completely closed, because there are no customers. Some staff restaurants have lost their contract with the client company, because the company's employees are working remotely.
