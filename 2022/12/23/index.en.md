# Friday 23.12.2022 (radio)

Double child benefit. Shooting incident in France. Shortage of doctors. Father Christmas and Grandfather Frost. Flower shops. Weather.

## Double child benefit

![A child plays with a lorry in a playground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11272828)

Open image viewer

Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

On Friday the double child benefit was paid. A larger sum than usual has been paid automatically to everyone who receives child benefit.

The government decided in the autumn that the December child benefit will be doubled. The aim is to help families with children now, when prices have risen a lot.

The double child benefit for December is necessary for many families. It is estimated that about 130,000 children live in poverty in Finland.

A shooting incident has happened in France. At least 3 people died and 3 were injured in the shooting.

The incident happened in central Paris, the capital of France, on Friday.

The shooter is a 69-year-old pensioner. He is a French citizen. The police have arrested the shooter.

## Shortage of doctors

![An elderly person lies in a hospital bed, with a doctor standing beside the bed. Anonymous picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99484662fb4f07d5de3/1698854562)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mirva Ekman / Yle

There are too few doctors in Finland. There is also a shortage of doctors in big cities.

Economist Juho Ruskoaho estimates that Finland needs at least 3,000 more doctors. Otherwise, for example, there will not be enough doctors for health centres.

The Finnish Medical Association says that the situation is improving too slowly through training new doctors. In the Medical Association's view, all tasks that do not require medical training must be quickly removed from doctors' work.

## Father Christmas and Grandfather Frost

![Russian Grandfather Frost and Finnish Father Christmas stand side by side. Forest in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1597,h_897,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105242563a54f107a526/1671778549)

Open image viewer

Russian Grandfather Frost (on the left) and Finnish Father Christmas in 2018. Photo: Petri Kivimäki / Yle

The meeting of Finnish Father Christmas and Russian Grandfather Frost has been cancelled.

The meeting of Father Christmas and Grandfather Frost has been a fun tradition on the Finland–Russia border. The event was meant to promote cooperation between Finland and Russia.

The meeting of Father Christmas and Grandfather Frost is no longer organised because of the war in Ukraine. Russia attacked Ukraine and started the war 10 months ago.

## Flower shops

![Festive flower arrangement.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6946698)

Open image viewer

Photo: Elina Mäyrä / Yle

Christmas is an important time for flower shops.

There has been a big change in the trade. Finns now buy a lot of flowers from supermarkets.

Customers go to a flower shop when they need the florist's professional skills. From a flower shop, for example, people buy bouquets and flower arrangements.

The internet has also changed the work of flower shops. Many customers order flowers in advance online.

## Christmas Eve weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12686776)

Open image viewer

On Saturday, Christmas Eve, the sun shines in the southern and central parts of the country. In the north it is partly cloudy, and a little snow may also fall.

The temperature is below zero across the whole country. In Lapland the frost is as much as 10 to 20 degrees.

On Sunday, Christmas Day, the weather is frosty. The weather is very similar to Christmas Eve.
