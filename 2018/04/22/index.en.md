# Sunday 22.4.2018 (radio)

Home care for elderly people becoming more common is causing dangerous situations. Finland is getting an incomes register, a new database. The world's oldest person has died. False morels have already been found in different parts of the country.

## Home care for the elderly is faltering

![a carer holds an elderly person by the hand](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9935491)

Open image viewer

Home is not always the best place of care for an elderly person. Photo: AOP

Legal scholars say that home care for elderly people becoming more common has caused dangerous situations.

Yle interviewed doctors of law. They say that the dismantling of institutional care has already gone too far. For example, Urpo Kangas, emeritus professor of civil law, says that at worst it is a case of abandonment. People with memory disorders who live alone at home are in the greatest need.

Home care has too few workers, and the work is rushed. In addition, there are too few places in institutions. The law on services for the elderly was tightened 3 years ago.

The latest case to come to light was one where a person had returned home from an institution. The home carers did not remember to visit the client, and she was found dead after many weeks.

## Finland is getting an incomes register

![Banknotes and a payslip in a woman's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9860902)

Open image viewer

You can check your own wage income in the incomes register, but you cannot look at other people's information. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

At the beginning of next year Finland will get a new database, the incomes register.

Finns will be able to see their own wage income in it almost in real time. Kela and the Tax Administration, for example, will also be able to look at the information in the incomes register. Both authorities believe that the new register will make their work easier. It helps to prevent misuse.

The incomes register is also useful for the insurance sector. Real-time information improves the fight against the grey economy, where, for example, taxes are avoided.

## The world's oldest person has died

![A screenshot from The Asahi Shimbun news site about the death of Nabi Tajima at the age of 117.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10171961)

Open image viewer

A screenshot from the pages of the Asahi Shimbun newspaper about Nabi Tajima's death. Photo: Asahi.com

The world's oldest person has died.

A Japanese woman named Nabi Tajima died in hospital in Southern Japan. She was 117 years old. Tajima was born in August 1900. Tajima became the oldest known person when Violet Brown from Jamaica died last September. Brown was also 117 years old.

The world's oldest person is now believed to be Chiyo Yoshida. Yoshida, who is Japanese, is 116 years old.

## False morels have already been seen

![False morel](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8803082)

Open image viewer

You may be able to find false morels this big in a couple of weeks. Photo: Sini Ojanperä / Yle

False morels have already been found in different parts of Finland.

Mushroom expert Lasse Kosonen says that the mushrooms are still small. It will be a couple of weeks before the real false morel season starts. False morels already make a small fruiting body in the autumn, which waits for the snow to melt and for good growing conditions.

The false morel is a delicious edible mushroom, but it is poisonous. False morels must not be eaten without the right treatment. False morels must be boiled twice in plenty of water for at least 5 minutes. After that the mushrooms must be rinsed with plenty of water.
