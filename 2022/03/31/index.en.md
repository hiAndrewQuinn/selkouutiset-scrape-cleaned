# Thursday 31.3.2022 (radio)

Nurses' strike halts non-urgent healthcare. Contacts to the Non-Discrimination Ombudsman have increased. The number of foreign-language speakers is growing. Ukraine is trying to evacuate people from Mariupol. Weather forecast.

## Nurses' strike

![Nurses prepare to go into isolation rooms for corona patients.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90016661dc25d531441/1660319462)

The nurses' strike halts non-urgent care in 6 hospital districts. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

On Friday a nurses' strike begins. About 25,000 nurses will go on strike. The nurses' strike halts non-urgent hospital care in the 6 largest hospital districts.

For example, the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district HUS is cancelling many operations and other non-urgent treatments. The hospital districts inform patients of the changes by phone or text message

During the strike, only patients who need urgent care will be treated in hospital emergency departments. Births and cancer treatments will also continue during the nurses' strike. The nurses' strike does not affect health centres or occupational health care.

The nurses will start the strike on Friday morning, because no agreement has been reached on nurses' pay and work.

## Non-Discrimination Ombudsman

![Discrimination Ombudsman Kristina Stenman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3562,h_2004,x_353,y_561/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-79081760656b17bef44/1617259411)

Open image viewer

Non-Discrimination Ombudsman Kristina Stenman. Photo: Yle/Joni Kyheröinen

Contacts from Finns to the Non-Discrimination Ombudsman have increased. Most of the contacts concern discrimination because of disability or origin.

Non-Discrimination Ombudsman **Kristina Stenman** says that contacts about these matters have increased a lot in recent years.

Stenman says that the Non-Discrimination Act must be changed so that compensation can be ordered for a victim of discrimination. Non-Discrimination Ombudsman Stenman says that the powers of the non-discrimination authorities must also be increased.

Parliament will deal with the Non-Discrimination Act next autumn.

## Foreign-language speakers in Finland

![The Russian flag flutters in the wind against a blue sky with a few clouds in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3700,h_2083,x_0,y_665/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-340842591ec9309d960/1499830142)

Open image viewer

Russian speakers are the largest group of foreign-language speakers in Finland. Photo: Mostphotos

The number of foreign-language speakers in Finland is growing quickly. Last year, the number of foreign-language speakers grew by more than 25,000 people.

The growth is the biggest in 40 years.

The largest group of foreign-language speakers in Finland are Russian speakers. Among foreign-language speakers, the number of Russian speakers also grew the most last year.

Russia continues its attack on Ukraine. Russia has shelled Kyiv and Chernihiv, for example, even though Russia has said that it is reducing military activity in Ukraine.

The situation in the city of Mariupol is still difficult. Ukraine is sending 45 buses to Mariupol on Friday to pick up people. The International Red Cross is leading the evacuation.

It has been difficult to evacuate people from Mariupol, that is, to help people to safety, because Russia is besieging the city.
