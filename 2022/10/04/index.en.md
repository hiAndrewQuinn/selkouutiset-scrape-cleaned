## Nurses' pay

![A patient lies on their stomach and around them are several intensive care nurses.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1390,h_781,x_761,y_554/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89225461b9a756674ea/1644246113)

Nurses receive compensation for the difficult corona time. Photo: Arash Matin / Yle

An agreement has been reached on pay in the care sector. Employers and nurses' trade unions agreed on pay on Monday.

Nurses' pay will rise by a total of over 17 percent in 5 years.

The nurses' trade union says that a nurse's salary will rise to over 3,000 euros a month.

In addition, nurses treating corona patients will receive a 600-euro bonus. It is paid once.

Because the nurses' pay dispute is now over, nurses will continue working as normal.

Many nurses intended even to resign and quit nursing altogether if pay did not rise.

That is why everyone is pleased that an agreement has now been reached on nurses' pay.

## Young drivers

Young drivers are in danger in traffic. Young drivers have twice the risk of dying in traffic compared with others.

Every year about 40 young people die in traffic, and over 1,000 young people are injured.

Last weekend in Porvoo a traffic accident happened in which 2 young people died and 7 young people were injured. The police suspect that the 18-year-old driver was driving too fast.

## Charger

![USB-C cable.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2069,h_1164,x_477,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-966284629f2862b22a6/1664881076)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jakub Porzycki / AOP

All small devices in the home can soon be charged with the same charger. In the European Union area all devices will get a USB-C connection.

Device manufacturers get 2 years to carry out the reform, and the reform comes into force in 2024. By then the same charger must fit, for example, phones, tablets, digital cameras and headphones.

The reform makes people's lives easier. Also, less electronic waste is produced, when people do not need to buy a separate charger for every device.

The European Parliament approved the reform on Tuesday.
