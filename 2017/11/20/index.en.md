# Monday 20.11.2017 (radio)

Researchers swapped plastic for cellulose. A motorist hit a child. The Western Metro ran well. Hundreds of fake police officers. The German government has problems. Charles Manson has died.

## Researchers fight plastic waste

![A plastic bag photographed in the Red Sea, on the Egyptian coast.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8349512)

Open image viewer

Plastic in the sea is a big problem. Photo: Mike Nelson / EPA

Finnish researchers have developed a method that can reduce plastic waste in the seas. There is a lot of plastic waste in the world's seas, and it is a big problem.

For example, shower gel and toothpaste contain plastic beads. They scrub the skin, and that is why they clean. A new substance also cleans, but it also breaks down in nature.

The new beads are therefore not plastic beads but cellulose beads.

Researchers are looking for substances that can be used to make new material from, for example, recycled paper and used clothes.

## A motorist hit a child

A motorist hit a child who was crossing a pedestrian crossing in Helsinki. The motorist fled the scene. Police are asking for information about the motorist. The motorist hit the child on Sunday in Itäkeskus.

Police say that a 6-year-old boy was crossing a pedestrian crossing with his father. The car hit the child at high speed. He was taken to hospital for examinations.

## Many people got to work well on the Western Metro

![An artwork on the platform of Tapiola metro station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9937725)

Open image viewer

An artwork on the platform of Tapiola metro station. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

The Western Metro ran well on Monday morning, when people rode it to work. On Monday morning, people rode the Western Metro to work for the first time.

The Western Metro opened on Saturday. During the day, as many as 220 000 people travelled by metro. The Western Metro runs from Ruoholahti in Helsinki to Kivenlahti in Espoo.

## Police have found many fake police officers

![Handcuffs used by the police.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9927315)

Open image viewer

Posing as a police officer can be fraud. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

So far this year, the police have recorded more than 800 crime reports about fake police officers in Finland. A fake police officer is a person who poses as a police officer. Fake police officers are suspected of fraud, for example.

Fake police officers have, for example, asked people for their bank codes. The police say that they never ask people for their bank codes.

## Merkel's position is under threat

![Chancellor Angela Merkel, with CSU chairman Horst Seehofer in the background](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9939352)

It is difficult in Germany. Photo: Felipe Trueba / EPA

In Germany the government is negotiating, and there are now problems in the negotiations.

The Liberal party unexpectedly announced that it is leaving the negotiations. The end of the negotiations threatens Angela Merkel's fourth term.

Merkel is the chancellor of Germany. Merkel is seen as a guarantor that the European Union stays stable.

## Cult leader Manson has died

![Serial killer Charles Manson is brought before the court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9939078)

Open image viewer

Serial killer Charles Manson was brought before the court in December 1969. Photo: AOP

Serial killer Charles Manson was brought before the court in December 1969. In the United States, Charles Manson, the leader of a notorious cult, has died.

The group led by Manson murdered the wife of film director Roman Polanski, the actress Sharon Tate. The group also murdered six other people.

The murders took place in the late 60s. Manson was in prison for the rest of his life. Manson was 83 years old when he died.
