# Tuesday 8.10.2019 (radio)

Fortum is buying most of the large German company Uniper. A court case about forged paintings in Kuopio. There are ticks in the city too. A housing company sold 6 lamps and got 300,000 euros.

## Fortum gets most of Uniper

![Uniper power plant in Schkopau, Germany.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11010547)

Uniper has more than 10 coal-fired power plants in Germany. Photo: Mario Hommes / AOP

Fortum, the Finnish state's energy company, is buying more shares in the German energy company Uniper.

Fortum is becoming such a big owner of Uniper that Fortum can decide on Uniper's matters alone. Fortum has paid more than 6 billion euros in total for Uniper's shares.

Uniper is a large company that produces energy with coal, gas, and water and nuclear power plants. Fortum says that coal power plants will not be used in Europe for much longer, but gas will be used. Coal power plants will be closed soon, because they warm the climate.

## Court case about forgeries in Kuopio

![A work of art](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10194440)

Open image viewer

The police suspect that this picture is not a genuine Picasso painting. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

A court case about art forgeries has started in Kuopio.

A couple from Kuopio is charged with forging paintings. The prosecutor says that the couple printed pictures from a computer and lied that they were paintings by Pablo Picasso, for example. People have paid up to thousands of euros for the paintings.

The couple says that the pictures are genuine. They also say that if the pictures are forgeries, someone has tricked them. They had thought that the paintings were genuine.

The prosecutor is demanding that the couple be sent to prison for more than a year.

## Ticks in the city too

![Ticks in a laboratory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10882073)

Open image viewer

Ticks in a laboratory. Photo: Lab Santé Animale-Anses / Phanie / REX / AOP

Nowadays there are many ticks in the city too, for example in parks.

A tick is a small animal. A tick waits in the grass or in a bush for a person or an animal to walk past. The tick attaches to a person and sucks their blood. Some ticks carry a bacterium that can make a person ill.

There have been many ticks on islands and in forests, but researchers have also found many of them in cities, for example in parks. Ticks are everywhere where other animals are. A tick can attach to a person even late in the autumn. They only die when the frost comes.

A researcher says that people should always check for ticks on their skin after being outdoors. Ticks also easily attach to dogs.

## 300,000 euros to a housing company for lamps

![Paavo Tynell lamp](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3854,h_2168,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5952405d933ae9c090c/1569929988)

A Paavo Tynell lamp. Photo: Annmaris

A housing company in Tampere has received 300,000 euros for six light fittings, that is, lamps. The housing company sold the lamps at an auction.

The light fittings were designed by the famous Paavo Tynell. The beautiful lamps are made of brass, and some of them have flower shapes. Tynell designed the lamps in the 1950s for a cinema. Today the space is home to the Tampere Student Theatre.

The housing company will perhaps use the money for repairs to the building.
