## Corona vaccinations

![Sign on a queue number stand informing about corona vaccinations.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_409/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97834262c43c9f72cc1/1657027812)

4th corona vaccinations will be added in the autumn. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

Fourth corona vaccinations will be increased. The Institute for Health and Welfare THL is expanding the recommendation on who is given the 4th corona vaccination.

The new plan is that from 15 August the 4th corona vaccination is recommended to people who have turned 65. From the beginning of September the 4th corona vaccination is recommended to people who have turned 60 and to all 12–59-year-olds who belong to a risk group for serious disease.

THL told about the plan on Wednesday. THL will confirm the recommendation for the 4th corona vaccinations later in the summer.

## Antibiotics in care homes

![medicine jar](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10010895)

Open image viewer

The Ministry of Health wants to restrict the use of antibiotics. Photo: AOP

A new law restricts the availability of antibiotics in care homes. From August the care units may no longer keep antibiotics in medicine stores, because a permit is needed for storing medicines.

The reason for the restriction is that the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health wants antibiotics to be used responsibly. Doctors say that the change is bad, because it can slow down starting medication in care homes.

If an elderly person needs a course of antibiotics in a care home, they can now get it quickly. In future the medicines may have to be fetched from far away, or the elderly person must be sent to emergency care to get antibiotics.

## Registration of new cars

![A blue electric car charging at a car dealership.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_475/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-93609062459a7e7482a/1657187085)

Open image viewer

Component shortage hampers car trade, because fewer cars are obtained from the factory than before. Photo: Mimmi Nietula / Yle

The registration of new cars has decreased. About 25 percent fewer new passenger cars were registered in June than in June last year.

The registration, that is, putting into use, of new cars has decreased because of a component shortage. Car factories are unable to make enough cars, because they do not get enough components. Components are needed in the electronics of a car.

Car registrations have also decreased across the whole first half of the year, that is, January–June, by 23 percent. In January–June 43,500 new cars were registered in Finland.

## Day of Poetry and Summer

![Statue of Eino Leino](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3264,h_1836,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-29863559ab8de77486/1436203493)

The statue of Eino Leino is in Esplanadi park in Helsinki. The statue was sculpted by Lauri Leppänen.  Photo: Lotta Emanuelsson

On Wednesday Eino Leino's day was celebrated. The writer **Eino Leino** was born on 6 July 1878.

Eino Leino wrote many poems. That is why Eino Leino's day is also the Day of Poetry and Summer, that is, the summer day. Eino Leino's day and the Day of Poetry and Summer are flag days.

On Wednesday the Eino Leino Prize was also awarded. This year the Eino Leino Society gave the award to the poet and writer **Kaiho Niemi**.
