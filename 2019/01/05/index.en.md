# Saturday 5.1.2019 (radio)

Speaker of Parliament Paula Risikko says that permanent residence permits must be revocable. 5 teenage girls have died in an escape room fire in Poland. There are still homes without electricity in Åland. In Southwest Finland wolves have been driven out of a residential area. Finland plays in the final of the Under-20 Men's Ice Hockey World Championships.

## Risikko wants a stricter law on residence permits

![Paula Risikko](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10583166)

Open image viewer

Speaker of Parliament Paula Risikko. Photo: Yle

Speaker of Parliament Paula Risikko says that permanent residence permits must be revocable.

Risikko commented on President Sauli Niinistö's New Year's speech. In it the President referred to the suspected sex crimes in Oulu. All the suspects are quota refugees or asylum seekers.

Risikko says that people must be helped, but abuses must be prevented. Risikko said that if a person is a national threat, their residence permit must be possible to revoke. She says that the concept of a national threat must be added to the law.

Risikko was a guest on the morning show Ykkösaamu on Yle TV1.

## Five young girls died in a fire at an escape room in Poland

![An outdoor night view of an escape room in Poland. Police walk past an Escape Room sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10583079)

Open image viewer

Exterior picture of an escape room in Poland. Photo: Marcin Bielecki / EPA

5 teenage girls have died in a fire during an escape room game in Poland.

The accident happened on Friday evening in the city of Koszalin in Northern Poland. In addition, an adult man received serious burns.

The cause of the fire is not yet known. It is also not known why the girls could not get out of the room.

The Polish Minister of the Interior ordered that the fire safety of all escape rooms must be checked. There are more than 1,000 escape rooms in Poland.

An escape room game is a physical adventure game. Players solve problems in a locked room within a time limit.

Finnish escape room entrepreneurs and fire safety experts say that the safety of escape rooms is taken seriously in Finland.

## Åland still suffers from the storm Aapeli

![Fallen trees.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10579982)

Open image viewer

Fallen trees after the Aapeli storm in Ämnäs, in Finström municipality in Åland. Photo: Jonas Edsvik / Nya Åland / Lehtikuva

There are still homes without electricity in Åland because of the Aapeli storm. About 5,000 homes were still without electricity during the day.

In the remote villages, electricity may have to be waited for a couple of weeks. The authorities do not know exactly how many residents are cut off at the ends of the village roads. A lot of trees have fallen on the roads.

Many rural residents have moved to hotels in Mariehamn, because there is no electricity or water at home.

## Wolves came into the yard area

![Wolf.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9472398)

Open image viewer

A wolf in a wildlife park. Photo: Seppo Nykänen

In Marttila in Southwest Finland, wolves were driven out during the night. There was a pack of at least 5 wolves in the residential area.

The first sighting of the wolves was reported to the police as early as Friday afternoon. Some of the tracks were found in the yards of residential houses.

The wolves may have come close to the residential area chasing deer.

## Young Lions fight for gold
