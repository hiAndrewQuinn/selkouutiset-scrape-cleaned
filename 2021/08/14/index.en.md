## The Finns Party

Riikka Purra at the Finns Party party congress in Seinäjoki. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Finns Party has chosen a new chairman. The new chairman of the Finns Party is **Riikka Purra**.

In the party congress vote she got 774 votes, that is, many more votes than the other candidates. Sakari Puisto got the second most votes, with 252 votes.

The Finns Party's new chairman, Riikka Purra, says that she wants to make the party's line on immigration clearer. Purra says that the Finns Party must, for example, explain better why the party takes a critical attitude towards immigration.

Riikka Purra is 44 years old. She is also a Finns Party MP.

Fighting in Afghanistan is intensifying. The Taliban movement has taken more areas.

On Saturday morning the Taliban began a big attack on the city of Mazar-i-Sharif in the north of Afghanistan. The news agency AP reported on Saturday afternoon that the fighting is also approaching the capital, Kabul.

UN Secretary-General **Antonio Guterres** asks the Taliban to stop the attacks. Guterres says that Afghanistan may slide into chaos.

## Corona home tests

![Corona home test, a rapid test bought from Lidl in Finland](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2267,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-806037609e0da90a227/1620971132)

Corona home test packages. A test done at home does not give an official result. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

The sale of corona home tests has increased in Finland. Home tests are sold, for example, in grocery shops. Some shops have run out of the tests.

Shops estimate that a lot of corona home tests have been bought, because the corona situation has got worse again. People have also noticed that home tests are sold in shops.

However, a corona home test does not give an official test result.

## Heat on the planet

![Yellowed potato plants in a dry summer. The picture also shows common weeds such as pihasaunio, field pansy and fat hen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4160,h_2340,x_0,y_390/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8405026113498292c49/1628654478)

Finland too was hot and dry in July. Many plants turned yellow. Photo: Jouni Tanninen / Yle

July was really hot on the planet. US weather researchers say that July was the hottest month ever measured on Earth. Measurements have been made for 142 years.

The estimate of the US weather researchers differs from the results of European researchers.

European weather researchers say that July was the third hottest month ever measured on Earth.
