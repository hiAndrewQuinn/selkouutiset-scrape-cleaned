# Wednesday 28.6.2017 (radio)

Trade slows down in a cold summer. A computer virus is spreading. The men suspected of the pizzeria fire are to be remanded in custody. A police helicopter attacked in Venezuela. Fire safety of household appliances.

## Cold weather hurts the summer sales of shops and restaurants

![People at an ice cream kiosk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8978844)

Open image viewer

Ice cream sells when the summer is warm. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

It is quiet at ice cream kiosks and summer cafés, because the summer has been cold.

They are not getting enough money this summer.

The owners of shops and restaurants are also worried.

Only a few summer clothes, beach toys and garden products have been bought in shops. Less grill sausage has also been bought than last year.

Travel agencies, however, are satisfied. They have sold a lot of trips abroad this summer. Many Finns are going abroad because it is so cold in Finland.

## The computer virus Petya is spreading around the world

![A ransom message shown by Petya](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9694015)

Open image viewer

The malware is used to try to blackmail the computer's owner. The malware locks the computer and demands payment for removing the lock. Image: Finnish Communications Regulatory Authority

A bad virus, or malware, has spread to computers. The new malware is called Petya.

The malware has been used to carry out cyberattacks, especially on companies' computers.

With the malware, someone can lock a computer and demand money from the computer's owner.

Use of the malware started in Ukraine and Russia. It has also been detected in the United States and Western Europe.

The Petya malware has also been detected in Finland.

## Police ask for custody over the pizzeria fire

![A fire started in the pizzeria on Sunday evening; police say it was a petrol bomb attack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9689953)

Open image viewer

After the pizzeria fire, police caught two men. Police are now asking for them to be remanded in custody. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Police are asking for two men to be remanded in custody because of the Espoo pizzeria fire.

The fire is being investigated as aggravated criminal damage.

The fire started on Sunday evening in Kyyhkysmäki in Espoo, when a petrol bomb was thrown at the pizzeria's window.

The restaurant was destroyed in the fire.

## Police attacked with a helicopter in Venezuela

![In Caracas, the capital of Venezuela, the country's Ministry of the Interior was fired on from a helicopter and grenades were thrown at the Supreme Court building on Tuesday 27 June 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9694642)

Open image viewer

In Venezuela, a policeman says that he attacked the country's government buildings with a helicopter. Photo: Caraota Digital / Reuters

In Caracas, the capital of Venezuela, government buildings have been fired on from a police helicopter.

Grenades were thrown from the police helicopter at the Supreme Court building and the Ministry of the Interior was shot at, the Venezuelan authorities say.

Former police pilot Oscar Perez says in a video that he carried out the attack. Perez and a group of soldiers, police and civilians oppose the President of Venezuela.

Venezuela has been restless for a long time. There have been many demonstrations against the president there.

## A household appliance can cause a fire

![Rescue chief Jyri Silmäri approaches a fire protected by a fire blanket.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6391247)

Open image viewer

A fire blanket is a good tool for putting out a small fire. Photo: Paavo Koponen / Yle

Electrical devices in the home, household appliances, can cause a fire. For example, a toaster or a washing machine can catch fire.

The rescue service says that it is a good idea to put out a household appliance yourself if it catches fire. The fire brigade may not get there in time.

You can quickly put out a small fire, for example, with a fire blanket or a foam extinguisher.

The rescue service says that it is a good idea to keep household appliances clean and in good condition. Then they do not catch fire easily.
