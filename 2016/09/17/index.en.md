## Health is linked to voting behaviour

![People voting in a voting booth.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7914782)

Open image viewer

A person's state of health affects how actively they vote and which party they choose. Photo: Yle

A person's health has an effect on whether they vote in elections. Health also has an effect on which party they vote for. The information comes from a new study funded by the Academy of Finland.

The study says that healthy people vote more actively than sick people. Healthy people's voting activity is as much as 13 percent higher than others'.

If a person feels healthy, they are more likely to vote for right-wing parties. If a person has problems with their health, they are more likely to vote for left-wing parties or the Finns Party.

The study also says that poor health reduces a person's trust in political decision-makers.

## Britain will start the EU exit process early in the year

![ Theresa May talks with Donald Tusk](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9174724)

Open image viewer

President of the European Council Donald Tusk and British Prime Minister Theresa May.  Photo: Andreas Gebert / EPA

Britain will start the process of leaving the European Union perhaps at the beginning of next year. President of the European Council Donald Tusk spoke about it at the EU summit in Bratislava.

Tusk said that Britain is ready to start the process of leaving the EU perhaps in January or February. Tusk said that he got the information from British Prime Minister Theresa May.

Tusk and May talked last week.

## Police in EU countries carry out intensive traffic checks

![A police officer checking a driving licence](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8367328)

Open image viewer

The police will check how Finnish drivers drive in a big intensive check next Wednesday. The intensive traffic check is held at the same time in other European countries too.

The police keep an eye especially on drunk drivers and check the use of seat belts and mobile phones while driving.  The police also check whether pedestrians and cyclists obey traffic lights.

About 26,000 people die in traffic in EU countries every year, that is, about 70 people a day.

## Enjoy nature and sleep the night outdoors

![A father and mother with their two sons on a day trip on the Perhonjoki hiking trail. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8381050)

Thanks to everyman's rights, all people may move freely in nature. Photo: Juha Kemppainen / Yle

The organisation Suomen Latu encourages Finns to sleep outdoors on the night between Saturday and Sunday.

You can sleep in a tent or a lean-to, in your own yard or even on your balcony. The most important thing is to be outdoors and enjoy nature.

The Sleep the night outdoors campaign wants to remind Finns of everyman's rights, thanks to which you can move and also stay the night freely in nature.

Everyman's rights mean that all people may move in nature and, for example, pick berries and mushrooms in the forest without the landowner's permission. But you must not damage nature, and you must not go into anyone's yard.
