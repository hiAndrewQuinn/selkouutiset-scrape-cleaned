## Pensioners' livelihoods differ in the countryside and in cities

![Elderly person's hands holding coins.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_75/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4743715aba0300bdf66/1683557210)

Pensioners in the countryside more often have difficulty making ends meet than pensioners in cities.

40 per cent of pensioners in the countryside say that no money is left after compulsory expenses. In cities 33 per cent of pensioners answer this way.

Pensioners in the countryside are nevertheless just as satisfied with their lives as city pensioners. In addition, people in the countryside manage better on smaller incomes than in cities.

Almost 3,000 pensioners answered the survey.

## Iran's foreign minister has visited Finland

Iran's foreign minister says that there is currently no reason to negotiate with the USA about the nuclear agreement. The USA has withdrawn from the agreement, which is meant to stop Iran from developing a nuclear weapon.

The foreign minister is on a visit to Finland. He met President Sauli Niinistö and foreign minister Pekka Haavisto.

Other topics at the meeting included the situation in the Strait of Hormuz and the stopping of tankers.

## Diet affects climate change

![Poster of a healthy diet, the so-called food pyramid.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10384883)

Open image viewer

The government's report says that reducing the amount of meat clearly reduced the climate impact of diets. Photo: Marja-Liisa Kämppi / Yle

Diet affects the climate.

The government's report says that the impact can be reduced by changing the diet. The carbon stored in fields must also be looked after.

The project compared the average diet with 4 alternative diets.

Halving the amount of meat reduced the climate impact of the diet by 13 per cent. Reducing meat to a third reduced the impact by almost 20 per cent.

A fish-based diet already reduced the climate impact by about a third, and a vegan diet by 37 per cent.

However, none of the alternative diets fully met the recommendations for nutrient intake. The fish-based diet met the recommendations best.

## Serious forest fire on Gran Canaria

![The fire raging in the Valleseco area of Gran Canaria on 17 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10927531)

Open image viewer

The fire raged in the Valleseco area of Gran Canaria on 17 August. Photo: Desiree Martin / AFP

Thousands of people have been evacuated from the Gran Canaria holiday island in Spain. The forest fire is so fierce that it has not been put out.

600 firefighters are already fighting the fire. They have help from, among others, 5 aeroplanes and 11 helicopters. More help is expected.

The fire now raging is already the second on the island this summer. The new fire has already burned almost 3,400 hectares of land.

So far there have been no casualties in the fires.

Almost 14 million tourists visit the Canary Islands every year. In addition to Finns, the islands have for decades been popular with, among others, British and German tourists.
