# Monday 16.7.2018 (radio)

The presidents of the USA and Russia have met in Helsinki. The US presidential couple met the Finnish presidential couple at Mäntyniemi. The meeting brought many foreign journalists to Finland. The weather is hot all week.

## The presidents met in Helsinki

![Press conference of Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin at the Presidential Palace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10307777)

Presidents Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin in Helsinki. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The presidents of the USA and Russia have met in Helsinki. President Donald Trump and President Vladimir Putin talked at the Presidential Palace.

The presidents talked just the two of them, with only interpreters present. This is not usual. The presidents' talk lasted 2 hours.

After the talk, the presidents met journalists. The journalists wanted to know, for example, whether Russia had interfered in the US presidential election. In the USA, many people say that Russia influenced Donald Trump becoming president. Trump and Putin told journalists that this did not happen.

## The Trumps met the Niinistös

![Jenni Haukio, Melania Trump, Donald Trump and Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10306397)

Open image viewer

Jenni Haukio, Melania Trump, Donald Trump and Sauli Niinistö at Mäntyniemi. Photo: Kimmo Brandt / EPA

President Donald Trump came to Finland already on Sunday evening. His spouse, Melania Trump, was also with him.

The day of the US presidential couple began on Monday with breakfast at the home of the Finnish presidential couple. The Trumps visited Mäntyniemi, which is the official residence of the Finnish president.

The presidents of the USA and Finland talked for an hour. After the talk, the presidents commented on the discussion on social media. Niinistö said that the discussion was wide-ranging. Trump said that meeting Niinistö was an honour.

The spouses of the presidents also talked at Mäntyniemi. The theme of the talk was children and children's wellbeing. After the talk, Melania Trump thanked Jenni Haukio on social media for a good conversation.

## Finland got publicity

![Balcony of the press area in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10307702)

Open image viewer

The Finlandia Hall has been full of journalists. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

The meeting of the US and Russian presidents has brought a large number of journalists from many countries to Finland. They have also told millions of people about Finland, especially on social media.

The media centre for the meeting is at Finlandia Hall, and a sauna was brought to the yard of Finlandia Hall for the journalists. At least one journalist from the British BBC has told about the sauna on social media.

A journalist from New Zealand's morning television introduced a Finnish police officer on social media. In the journalist's opinion, Finnish police are friendlier and more fun than, for example, British police.

## The hot weather continues

![Temperature forecast for 17-19 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10307104)

Monday has been a hot day across Finland. In Turku, a new summer heat record of 32.2 degrees was measured.

Yle meteorologist Joonas Koskela says that the whole week will be sunny and hot.
