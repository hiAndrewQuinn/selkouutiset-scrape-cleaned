# Tuesday 20.6.2017 (radio)

Strong beer may come to grocery stores. World Refugee Day was marked on Tuesday. A terrorist act may have been planned in Helsinki. Extra kilos are dangerous to people's health.

## Alcoholic drinks in grocery stores

![Beer and long drinks on a shop shelf.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9515857)

Open image viewer

The amount of alcoholic drinks in shops will grow if the government's proposal is approved. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Strong beer may come to grocery stores.

The government proposes that shops may sell beer that has 5.5 percent alcohol. Now shops may sell beer that has at most 4.7 percent alcohol.

If Parliament approves the government's proposal, other drinks with 5.5 percent alcohol will also come to shops. These include, for example, alcopops, which contain alcohol and soft drink.

Parliament will discuss the government's proposal in the autumn.

## World Refugee Day

![People on the back of a truck.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9682183)

Open image viewer

There are now more refugees in the world than ever before. Photo: Zein al-Rifai / EPA

World Refugee Day was marked on Tuesday.

Last year there were almost 66 million refugees in the world. Over 22 million of them were refugees abroad. The most refugees were in Turkey, Afghanistan and Lebanon. In Europe, the most refugees were in Germany.

There are about 10 million people in the world who do not have citizenship of any country. That can mean that a person cannot, for example, go to school or see a doctor.

## A terror plan

![Concrete barriers put up in front of the Temppeliaukio Church, 19 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9680016)

Open image viewer

Police brought concrete barriers in front of the Temppeliaukio Church. Photo: Irene Stachon / Lehtikuva

A terrorist act may have been planned in Helsinki. The target of the plan may have been the Temppeliaukio Church.

Police had an operation near the Temppeliaukio Church on Sunday. On Monday concrete barriers were put in front of the church. No terrorist act happened.

Police are still investigating the matter.

## Dangerous extra kilos

![An illustration about obesity.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9679880)

Open image viewer

Image: Yle News Graphics

There are many people in the world who have extra kilos. About 30 percent of the world's people are overweight or obese. A person is called overweight if they are not very fat.

Extra kilos are dangerous to people's health. They are the cause of many diseases. Extra kilos cause, for example, heart disease and diabetes.

In 2015, about 4 million people died of diseases caused by extra kilos. Many of them were not even very fat, only overweight.

The situation in Finland is bad. Almost 65 percent of Finns are overweight or obese.
