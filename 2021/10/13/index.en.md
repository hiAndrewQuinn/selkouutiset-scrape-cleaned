In Finland they are considering who needs a 3rd corona vaccination.

Minister Krista Kiuru hopes for experts' opinion on the matter as soon as possible.

Some seriously ill people and some health care workers have already received 3 corona vaccinations.

Minister Kiuru says that all Finns can be vaccinated against corona 3 times, if it is necessary.

## Corona pass

![Mobile phone with a corona pass app on its screen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12038566)

Open image viewer

The corona pass is already in use in many countries. Photo: AOP

The corona pass is being introduced in Finland.

Parliament dealt with the law proposal on the corona pass on Tuesday. The corona pass will come into force on Saturday.

The corona pass shows, for example, whether a person has had corona vaccinations.

For example, restaurants do not have to follow corona restrictions if they require customers to have a corona pass.

For example, nightclubs in Helsinki can be open normally already at the weekend, if they check customers' corona passes.

The corona pass is thus an alternative to corona restrictions. Corona restrictions are in force, for example, in the Helsinki and Turku regions.

You can get the corona pass, that is the corona certificate, from the Omakanta service. The web address is kanta.fi.

Finland is trying to evacuate more people from Afghanistan.

Finland wants to bring about 50 more people from Afghanistan to Finland. Some of them have a Finnish passport or residence permit.

Finland brought more than 400 people to safety from Afghanistan already in August. They were Finnish citizens or Afghans who worked for Finland. They were in danger when the Taliban took power in Afghanistan.

Many countries have been transporting people to safety from the Taliban.
