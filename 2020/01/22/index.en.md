# Wednesday 22.1.2020 (radio)

The Helsinki police investigated over 30 honour violence cases last year. A new kind of virus is spreading in China. In Finland the parents of young children are happy with their lives. Most Finnish war veterans are now women.

## Honour violence

![The silhouette of a woman against a window](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5384728)

Open image viewer

Victims of honour violence are usually women. Photo: Yle

The police have reported information about honour violence in Helsinki.

The Helsinki police investigated over 30 cases last year that were linked to honour violence. Often the matter was assault, that is, the perpetrator, for example, hit the victim so that the victim was injured.

Honour violence was usually committed against a wife or a daughter. Often the violence was in immigrant families. The perpetrators of the crime thought that they were defending the family's honour.

In Finland assault is always a crime. However, the victim often does not dare to talk about the matter. That is why not all cases come to the attention of the police.

## Coronavirus

![A health centre in Wuhan and a woman wearing a face mask](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11160113)

Many Chinese people wear face masks when they try to avoid the coronavirus. Photo: Noel Celis / AFP

A new disease is spreading in China. The cause of the disease is a new kind of coronavirus. It causes symptoms similar to pneumonia.

In China almost 500 people have already caught the virus, and almost 20 people have died.

Most cases of illness have been in the city of Wuhan. The Chinese authorities say that people should not travel to Wuhan at the moment.

## Parents of young children

![Henna Kapulainen and Pasquale Romano have lifted their twins onto their shoulders.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11169881)

Open image viewer

The parents of young children are happy. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

In Finland the parents of young children are happy with their lives.

The information comes from a study by the National Institute for Health and Welfare. In the study, the parents of young children were adults who had at least 1 child under 7 years old.

Almost 90 per cent of the parents of young children feel that they have a good life. Children bring content to life.

The parents of young children also live more healthily than other adults of the same age. They watch less television and drink less alcohol than other adults.

In one thing the parents of young children fare worse than others. They sleep less.

## War veterans

![The annual meeting of the War Veterans' Union in Kuopio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5577301)

Open image viewer

A celebration of the war veterans 10 years ago. Photo: YLE

Most Finnish war veterans are now women.

The war veterans served the Finnish army in the Second World War. The Second World War ended 75 years ago.

In the war, the men were soldiers, and the women were, for example, nurses and cooks. Many women did important work in dangerous conditions during the war.

Almost 8,000 war veterans are still alive. They are already over 90 years old.

Just over half of the war veterans are now women. The reason is that women often live longer than men.
