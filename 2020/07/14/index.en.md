## The Jyväskylä fire

![Ruined roof structures of a burned building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5464,h_3074,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6987105f0c5f6c9bdcb/1594646493)

The Jyväskylä fire is being investigated.

The fire started last weekend in a senior residence in Jyväskylä where many old people live. However, nobody was injured in the fire.

Researchers want to know why the fire spread so quickly. The building is quite a new apartment block.

The fire started from a candle that was on a balcony. The fire was able to spread quickly in the apartment block, even from upper floors to lower floors. That is not usual.

Now it is being investigated whether there are structures in the building along which the fire was able to spread. The investigation may take a long time.

## Foreign Minister Haavisto's actions

![Pekka Haavisto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11442070)

Open image viewer

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

In Finland the actions of Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto are being investigated.

The investigation is linked to the al-Hol camp. The al-Hol camp is in Syria, and it houses family members of ISIS terrorists. Among them are also Finnish women and children.

In Finland there has been argument about whether Finns in al-Hol should be helped. The matter is still open, but Foreign Minister Haavisto has made some decisions on it.

Now it is being investigated whether Haavisto has acted correctly, or whether he has broken the law. The police have carried out a preliminary investigation, and now the matter is being examined by the Constitutional Law Committee of Parliament.

Foreign Minister Haavisto himself thinks that he has not broken the law.

## Corona infections

![A man washes his hands with liquid soap and water in the bathroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11287293)

The number of corona infections is growing in the world.

In the whole world there are already over 13 million corona infections. There are probably more corona infections in the world, but not all of them are found or reported.

Finland's corona situation is good. On Tuesday only 6 new infections were reported. In total, 7,301 Finns have caught the corona infection.

## Alko's sales

![Two boxes of wine in a man's hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11446812)

Open image viewer

Finns bought alcohol for home when restaurants were closed because of corona. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Alko's sales have grown this year. Finns have bought alcoholic drinks more than usual.

For example, in June this year Alko sold about 20 per cent more drinks than in June last year. Wines in particular were popular.

The reason for Alko's good sales is corona. During the corona crisis restaurants were closed, and people spent a lot of time at home.

Now restaurants may already be open freely. That is why Alko's sales may no longer grow much for the rest of the year.
