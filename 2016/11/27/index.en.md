## The Prime Minister

![Juha Sipilä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9319340)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä has commented on the discussion about the firm Terrafame. Sipilä says that he has not influenced matters between his own relatives and Terrafame.

Yle News reported on Friday that a firm owned by the Prime Minister's relatives has received an order from Terrafame. Terrafame is a state firm. It is buying goods worth 500,000 euros from Sipilä's relatives.

Terrafame has a mine in Talvivaara, from which metals are obtained. The mine has had problems, and it has received money from the state. Most recently, 2 weeks ago, the government decided to give Terrafame 100 million euros.

Prime Minister Sipilä has now asked the Chancellor of Justice whether he can take part in making decisions on Terrafame's matters. The Chancellor of Justice's task is to make sure that the authorities follow the law.

## Tiredness

![Insomnia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7534171)

Open image viewer

Many people think about work matters at night. Photo: Yle

Finns sleep too little. The information is from a new survey by Yle and the University of Helsinki. Almost 10,000 Finns have already answered the survey.

The survey's respondents sleep on average 6.5 hours a night. Sleep researcher Markku Partinen says that this is too little. It is good for an adult to sleep about 8 hours a night.

Many respondents say that they sleep badly because of stress. They think about work matters also at night. Over 50 percent of the survey's respondents say that they are often or always tired in the morning.

Sleep researcher Partinen is worried about the situation. More and more is demanded of people at work all the time, but they are already tired to begin with.

## Rosberg

![The top four arrive at the finish line at the 2016 Abu Dhabi GP.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9319532)

Open image viewer

Rosberg's world championship was secured in the Abu Dhabi race. Photo: AOP

Nico Rosberg is the new world champion of Formula drivers. Rosberg's win was secured in the Abu Dhabi car race on Sunday.

Nico Rosberg represents Germany, but Finns are also happy about the win of Rosberg, or "Ruusbär". Nico Rosberg's father is the Finnish Formula driver Keke Rosberg.

![Nico and Keke Rosberg](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9318712)

Open image viewer

Nico and Keke Rosberg. Photo: AOP

Keke Rosberg was the first Finnish Formula 1 world champion. He won the world championship in 1982.

Nico Rosberg represents Germany, because Rosberg's mother is German. However, Nico Rosberg also has Finnish citizenship.

## Advent

![Candle+advent](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6602913)

Open image viewer

The first Advent candle was lit on Sunday. Photo: Yle

Advent began on Sunday. The first Sunday of Advent begins Christians' preparation for Christmas.

Many Finns go to church on the first Sunday of Advent. Only at Christmas do more people go to church.

Advent lasts 4 weeks.
