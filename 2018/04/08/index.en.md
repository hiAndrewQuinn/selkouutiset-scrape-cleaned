# Sunday 8.4.2018 (radio)

A strike starts in the electrical sector on Monday. Finnair has flown directly from Helsinki to Beijing for 30 years. Roma National Day was celebrated on Sunday. The Civil War shows in history teaching in schools.

## Electrical sector strike

![Unfinished electrical work on a building site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10150167)

The electrical sector strike slows down construction work. Photo: Niklas Meltio / Lehtikuva

A strike starts in the electrical sector on Monday. About 2,500 workers will stay away from work right from Monday morning. The strike lasts a week.

The electrical workers' organisation, the Electrical Workers' Union, says that the strike slows down big construction projects. For example, the Ratina shopping centre in Tampere may not be finished on time. The Ratina shopping centre is meant to open during April, but because of the strike that may not succeed.

The electrical sector strike is starting because the companies in the sector and the workers are arguing about working hours and pay.

## Scheduled flight to Beijing

![A Finnair advertising postcard with a map of the world. It has lines showing Finnair's flight routes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10148695)

A Finnair advertising postcard from 1988. Photo: Finnair

Finnair has flown directly from Helsinki to Beijing for 30 years. The first flight was in June 1988. It was the first direct flight connection from Europe to the Chinese capital.

The shortest route from China to Europe goes through Helsinki. At first Chinese people flew through Helsinki to Europe's big cities, for example Paris.

Today many Chinese people stay in Finland for a holiday. Chinese people especially like Lapland.

## Roma National Day

![A close-up of a Roma woman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6858479)

The dress of a Finnish Roma woman includes a velvet skirt and a decorated blouse. Photo: Sini Salmirinne / Yle

Roma National Day was celebrated on Sunday.

About 10,000 Roma live in Finland. Roma have lived in Finland for 500 years.

The Roma have their own language, Romani. But many Roma no longer speak Romani.

The Roma also have their own culture. For example, respecting older people is important to the Roma.

## The Finnish Civil War

![Three young people in front of a brick wall](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10131397)

Open image viewer

There are bullet holes from the time of the Civil War in the walls of the school of Oona Pätäri, Siina Kuusela and Arvi Yrjölä from Tampere. Photo: Anna Sirén / Yle

In school history lessons there has been a lot of talk this spring about the Civil War.

The Civil War started in January 1918. The Red and the White Finns fought against each other.

The Civil War is the history of young people's own surroundings. That is why young people are interested in the Civil War.

The pupils of Tampere co-educational school see traces of the Civil War in their own school. There are holes in the school's walls. They are bullet holes. They were made when there was shooting in the building during the Civil War.

\- This building was held by the Reds and there were White prisoners of war here, says pupil Oona Pätäri.

The fighting in Tampere ended 100 years ago. The Whites captured the city from the Reds at the beginning of April 1918.

The victory in Tampere was important for the Whites. A month later, in May 1918, the Whites won the whole Civil War.

Almost 40,000 Finns died in the Civil War.
