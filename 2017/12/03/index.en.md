## The number of people who support immigration has grown

![Savo Vocational College is a pioneer in mapping the skills of immigrants](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9563441)

Open image viewer

There are now more supporters of immigration than opponents. Photo: Heli Laitinen

Finns now seem to have a positive attitude to immigration. The newspaper Helsingin Sanomat reports that almost half of Finns want more immigrants in Finland. In the newspaper's survey, 47 percent of those who answered said that more immigrants can come.

There are now fewer people who oppose immigration than people who support it. 41 percent of those who answered the survey opposed immigration.

Helsingin Sanomat reports that in 2010 there were clearly more opponents of immigration.

## The Finnish Red Cross helps people find a friend

![Glass jars with Christmas decorations inside and a wire handle. On the side of one jar there is text in Arabic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9952403)

Open image viewer

Before Christmas, you could do crafts with a friend, for example. Photo: Anniina Wallius / Yle

Many foreigners who have come to Finland have found a friend with the help of the Finnish Red Cross. The Finnish Red Cross runs the Finnish Buddy activity.

In it, a person who has received a residence permit and a Finnish volunteer can look for a friend to go for coffee or a walk with, for example.

The support of a Finnish friend is important especially when the person who has received a residence permit moves from a reception centre to their own home.

## The health and social services reform will be finished, says the Prime Minister

![Prime Minister Juha Sipilä ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9960612)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä  Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä says that the government will get the reform of social and health services finished on time.

Making the health and social services reform has been difficult, because many laws are connected to it.

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä said today, Sunday, that the government parties, the Centre Party, the National Coalition Party and Blue Reform, will get the reform done.

## Tonight you can see a supermoon

![The Moon](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9960398)

Open image viewer

On the night between Sunday and Monday you can see a supermoon. Photo: Ernie Wright / NASA Goddard's Scientific Visualization Studio

Tonight it is worth taking a peek at the sky. Between the clouds you may see a full moon that is a little bigger and brighter than usual. It is now supermoon time.

A supermoon means that the Earth, the Sun and the Moon are in a straight line, and in addition the Moon is at the point in its orbit closest to the Earth.

This is the only night this year when you can see a supermoon.
