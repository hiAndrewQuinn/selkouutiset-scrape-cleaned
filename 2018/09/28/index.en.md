# Friday 28.9.2018 (radio)

Many workers are on strike next Wednesday. Vocational school students are less satisfied with teaching than before. There was a big fire in Lahti. Many lower secondary school pupils do not eat school lunch every day. The weather varies at the weekend.

## Strike day on Wednesday

![Meyer shipyard in Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10139083)

Open image viewer

Not much work will be done next Wednesday. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Many workers are on strike next Wednesday. They will not work the whole day. Workers from big factories, for example, are on strike.

The workers' trade unions decided on the strike, because they do not accept the government's plans. The government intends to weaken workers' job protection, so that it is easier for small firms to dismiss employees.

The trade unions say that the government must withdraw the plan.

## Teaching at vocational schools

![Lappia Vocational Institute.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8693780)

Open image viewer

Vocational school students are proud to be "amis", that is, vocational students. Photo: Paula Hiljanen / Yle

Vocational school students are less satisfied with teaching than before.

A new survey says that about 25 percent of vocational school students are not satisfied with teaching. About 20 percent of students have considered quitting their studies.

There are many reasons why vocational school students are not satisfied. Many feel that there is a lack of discipline in lessons. Many also think that there is too little contact teaching.

However, vocational school students are proud of their own school and their own field. 90 percent of vocational school students are proud to be "amis".

## Fire in Lahti

![Fire in Lahti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10427761)

The fire broke out in Lahti during the night. Photo: Yle

There has been a big fire in Lahti. The fire destroyed part of the Mukkula shopping centre.

The fire broke out during the night between Thursday and Friday. The police say that someone may have set the fire on purpose.

The police are continuing to investigate the fire.

## Not everyone likes school lunch

![A plate of school lunch.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10418568)

Open image viewer

Some lower secondary school pupils do not always eat school lunch, even though it is free. Photo: Tapio Rissanen / Yle

In Finland school lunch is free. However, many eighth and ninth graders do not eat school lunch every day.

The differences between municipalities are big. In small municipalities almost all pupils eat the school meal.

In big cities the situation is different. For example, in Kerava 60 percent of eighth and ninth graders do not eat school lunch every day.

Some pupils say that they do not like school food. And in cities there are alternatives, for example fast-food restaurants.

The National Agency for Education reminds that the school meal is part of teaching. That is why it is a bad thing if pupils do not come to the school meal.
