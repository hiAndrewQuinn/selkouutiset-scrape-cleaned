# Friday 24.8.2018 (radio)

A serious traffic accident in Kuopio. The minister criticises the army for vegetarian food. The president turns 70. Espoo FM reports news from Finland in Arabic. Sibelius's Forest is a new nature reserve.

## A serious accident in Kuopio

![View of the accident scene.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10369964)

Open image viewer

A bus fell from a bridge onto the railway line in Kuopio. Photo: Timo Hartikainen / Lehtikuva

A serious traffic accident has happened in Kuopio. Four people have died in the accident. 17 have been injured, four of them seriously.

In the accident, a bus plunged from a bridge onto the railway line in the afternoon. A person who saw the Kuopio accident said that the bus came onto the bridge at high speed on two wheels. It hit cars that were standing at traffic lights. After the collision, the bus plunged through the bridge railing down onto the railway line.

The bus driver has told the police that the bus's brakes did not work.

## The minister criticises the army for vegetarian food

![Soldiers eating.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9167397)

Open image viewer

Soldiers eating. Photo: Katriina Laine / Yle

Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö criticises the army for vegetarian food.

The army has decided to start offering soldiers only vegetarian food twice a week. Minister Jussi Niinistö says that vegetarian food may not be enough nourishment for soldiers. The minister demands an explanation of the matter.

The company that makes food for soldiers says that vegetarian food is healthy for people and for the climate of the planet.

## First picture of the president's baby

![Sauli Niinistö, Aaro and Lennu in a Twitter picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10368600)

President Sauli Niinistö, son Aaro and dog Lennu. Photo: Twitter / Yle Uutisgrafiikka

President Sauli Niinistö has a birthday. He turns 70.

Niinistö is very popular. Most Finns say that Niinistö does his job well. In a Yle poll, almost 90 percent of people gave Niinistö a school grade of 8 or more. 80 percent of Finns want to give the president more power in domestic politics. Nowadays the president's job includes foreign policy, that is, relations with other states.

On his birthday, the president published a photo of his 6-month-old son Aaro for the first time.

## Finnish news in Arabic

An Arabic-language news channel has been set up in Finland.

The channel called Espoo FM works on the internet. It reports in Arabic on news from Finnish media about Finland. The channel is run by 17 volunteers. Almost all of them are media professionals who came to Finland as asylum seekers.

The Espoo FM channel wants to help people who have come to the country understand Finnish society and Finns.

## Sibelius's Forest is a new nature reserve

![Sunrise at Aulanko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10365705)

Open image viewer

The sun rises at Aulanko. Photo: Ville Välimäki / Yle

A new nature reserve has been established in the city of Hämeenlinna.

The protected area is at Aulanko and its name is Sibelius's Forest. The story goes that the composer Jean Sibelius composed music in the forest, for example Finlandia.

Many residents of Hämeenlinna often visit the area. They run along paths, ride mountain bikes and go horse riding. There is also a lookout tower in the forest, which tourists visit.
