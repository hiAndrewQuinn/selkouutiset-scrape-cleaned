## Drug consumption rooms

![Drug syringe on the floor of a toilet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9914604)

Open image viewer

There are no drug consumption rooms in Finland. That is why drugs are used secretly, for example in toilets. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

The debate about drug consumption rooms continues. Minister Aki Lindén said on Friday that he supports trying out drug consumption rooms.

Some experts estimate that drug consumption rooms can reduce drug deaths. In consumption rooms the use of drugs can be monitored and support can be given to drug users.

In Minister Lindén's view it can be sensible to try out consumption rooms, for example in Helsinki. That is why Lindén wants to find out what changes to the law the trial requires.

For example, drug use is illegal in Finland. The law must be changed, so that a user can take drugs in a consumption room without committing a crime.

There are many difficult questions in the matter, Lindén says. That is why the trial of drug consumption rooms cannot happen quickly.

## A warship's visit

![The US Navy amphibious assault ship USS Kearsarge has arrived for a port visit at Hernesaari in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3894,h_2180,x_0,y_405/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99084362ecde4084fdb/1659690605)

Open image viewer

USS Kearsarge at Hernesaari in Helsinki. Photo: Susanna Pesonen / Yle

The American warship USS Kearsarge is visiting Helsinki at the weekend.

The ship is longer than the passenger ships that sail between Helsinki and Stockholm. Its length is about 260 metres.

Kearsarge is in the port of Hernesaari in Helsinki. The public cannot get onto the ship.

There are about 2000 American soldiers on the ship. Next week they will train together with Finnish soldiers in the Baltic Sea.

## Strawberry pickers

![Ukrainian strawberry pickers at the Holopainen farm in Lemi in 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10862632)

Open image viewer

Ukrainian strawberry pickers in Finland in 2019. Photo: Kari Saastamoinen/ Yle

Strawberry farms do not have enough pickers.

About 2 million kilos of strawberries will be left in the fields this summer, because there are no pickers.

Usually about 70 percent of the pickers come from Ukraine. This summer fewer Ukrainian pickers are coming to Finland, because there is a war in Ukraine.

## Vanninen's world gold

![Saga Vanninen in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3886,h_2186,x_0,y_73/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99107262ed0781152a7/1659701193)

Open image viewer

Young world champion Saga Vanninen. Photo: - / MVphotos/others / MVPhotos

Finland's Saga Vanninen is the junior world champion. Vanninen won world gold in the heptathlon.

In the heptathlon competitions are held in 7 different events. The women compete, for example, in the javelin throw and the 200 metre run.

Vanninen collected more points from the different events than the other competitors. Silver and bronze went to German athletes.

The world championships for under-20 athletes are in South America, in Colombia.
