# Thursday 5.4.2018 (radio)

Finland loses billions of euros every year because Finns do not exercise enough. Finland's 100th anniversary was seen around the world. The pollen season is late. Soon it is time to change to summer tyres on cars. It is still snowing in Lapland.

## Lack of exercise is costly

![A boy in a PE lesson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10042688)

Open image viewer

Exercise is good for your health. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Finland loses a lot of money because Finns do not exercise enough. A report by the UKK Institute says that the lack of exercise costs society at least 3 billion euros every year.

For example, health care is more expensive if people are in poor shape. Poor fitness also causes illnesses that stop a person from being able to work. Then the person does not pay taxes to society, but society pays them benefits.

The director of the UKK Institute, Tommi Vasankari, wonders why people in Finland talk so little about the costs of a lack of exercise. But it is a question of billions of euros, Vasankari says.

## The 100th anniversary was noticed

![Studying in the open learning environment of Talvikangas school in Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9795531)

Finnish schools interested people abroad also in Finland's anniversary year. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

Finland's 100th anniversary was seen around the world. This is what Finland's Ministry for Foreign Affairs says.

Independent Finland turned 100 last year, and the world's media reported on Finland more than usual. The media reported on Finland most in neighbouring countries and also in Germany, Britain, the USA and Japan.

The media was most interested in Finnish education. A lot was also written about Finnish culture and, for example, hobby horses.

## The pollen season is ahead

![A birch.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8856708)

Open image viewer

There will be a lot of birch pollen this spring. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The pollen season starts later than usual this spring. Alder pollen has come to Finland from abroad with the wind, but otherwise there has not yet been pollen in the air.

Soon the trees will start to flower in Finland too. It is estimated that there will be a lot of birch pollen this year. That means problems for people with allergies.

## Summer tyres can wait

![Summer tyres in storage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9622352)

Open image viewer

You can already change to summer tyres when driving conditions get better. Photo: Antti Haavisto/Yle

Soon it is also time to change to summer tyres on cars. The period for using studded tyres officially ends next Monday.

But drivers do not have to change their tyres by Monday. Studded tyres may still be used later too, if driving conditions are bad.

There is still a lot of snow in Finland, and more snow fell at Easter. That is why tyre shops have had only a few customers who have wanted summer tyres for their cars.

Tyre shops are expecting a rush as soon as the weather gets better.

## Snow is falling in Lapland

![Park scenery, Ruskeasuo central park, Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10123753)

There is still snow in a large part of the country, and more snow is falling in Lapland. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

The wintry weather continues. On Friday more than 10 centimetres of snow may fall in Lapland. In the night between Friday and Saturday snow may also fall in the south. Driving conditions may be bad.

On Saturday it will probably be dry and sunny. The weather will also be warm on Saturday.

On Sunday there may be rain again.
