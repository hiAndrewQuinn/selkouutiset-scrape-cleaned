## Social and health care reform

![The social and health services reform, that is, the sote reform, was approved in Parliament by 105-77 votes at the plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki on 23 June 2021, where Parliament voted on approving the sote reform with a broad line-up.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_472/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82471260d31b913c19b/1625158013)

The social and health services reform was approved by 105-77 votes. Photo: Lehtikuva

Parliament has approved the social and health services reform, that is, the sote reform.

The decision means a big change in organising social and health care at the start of 2023. The organising of health care moves from municipalities to wellbeing areas, that is, to the regional level.

In Parliament's vote, 105 MPs supported the sote reform and 77 MPs opposed it. 17 MPs were absent from the vote.

## Restaurant restrictions

![Beer is poured into a glass in a restaurant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3008,h_1692,x_0,y_310/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81441460b71c4abb53c/1697528433)

Open image viewer

Restaurant restrictions remain only in Uusimaa. Photo: Yleisradio / Juho Liukkonen

Corona restrictions on restaurants are being reduced on Thursday. The government has decided that restaurants' customer numbers or opening hours will no longer be restricted in areas at the basic level of coronavirus, that is, in most of Finland.

Restrictions will remain only in Uusimaa, which is still at the acceleration stage.

In Uusimaa the opening hours of restaurants are extended, but indoors there may be fewer customers than usual. There is no restriction on terraces.

Ryanair is starting flights to several different destinations. Photo: Elli Sormunen / Yle

Airline Ryanair is opening new flight routes from Finland in the autumn. Ryanair will start flights to 8 destinations in November.

From Helsinki, Ryanair then flies, for example, to London, Paris and Milan. Ryanair also opens a route from Tampere to London.

Already in August Ryanair will start flights from Lappeenranta to Vienna and to Memmingen in Germany.

## Midsummer traffic

![Cars on Highway 4 on Midsummer Eve.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2880,h_1620,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6912365eeb45e0c6b91/1592477241)

Open image viewer

There may be queues in the Midsummer outbound traffic. Photo: Ilkka Klemola / Yle

Many Finns leave for their summer cottage on Thursday to celebrate Midsummer.

Midsummer outbound traffic is busiest on Thursday afternoon and evening, and also on Friday morning. The most congestion occurs on roads where road repair work is being done.

Midsummer return traffic is busiest on Sunday. For many Finns, the summer holiday starts at Midsummer.

## The weather

![Thursday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11996087)

Open image viewer

The weather cools down a little on Thursday. Still it is very warm.

In the southern and central parts of the country there were strong thunderstorms and rain on Wednesday. The thunderstorms have caused many power cuts. In Helsinki train traffic stopped because a tree fell on the railway line.

On Thursday the weather is partly cloudy. In Northern Ostrobothnia and Eastern Finland individual showers and thunder showers may come.

The weather cools down a little. The temperature in a large part of the country is between 20 and 28 degrees. In Lapland it is cooler in places.
