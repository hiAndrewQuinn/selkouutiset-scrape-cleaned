## Real wages

Real wages of wage earners have fallen. The fall was 2,7 per cent in January-March compared with a year earlier.

Real income means the purchasing power of wages.T

Statistics Finland says that consumer prices have risen faster than wages.

## City centres

![The city centre of Kokkola in summer](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5089261)

Open image viewer

A lively city centre gathers people. Photo: YLE / Kalle Niskala

City centres are changing. Their vitality is still weakening, but the decline may already be levelling off.

Vitality is affected, for example, by the number of shops and restaurants. Empty business premises show that vitality is falling.

In recent years the vitality figure has fallen by about 4 per cent every year. This spring the drop was just over 2 per cent.

The measurement examined the vitality of 40 cities.

## Murder verdict

![Varsinais-Suomi District Court, courtroom sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_821/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7598665ff6d9b285b0e/1610013231)

Open image viewer

Photo: Päivi Leppänen / Yle

The Varsinais-Suomi District Court has given a verdict in an old murder case.

The court ordered a life sentence for a murder that happened almost 30 years ago. The convicted man, 68-year-old Lasse Erik Lehto, once worked as an advertising agency entrepreneur and knew the victim.

The court says that Lehto murdered the lawyer Ilpo Härmäläinen on a sailing boat in August 1994 and sank the body into the sea off Airisto in Turku. The body was found on the seabed in March.

In Finland no one has ever before been convicted of a homicide such a long time after it happened.

## Monkeypox

![Monkeypox virus test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_411/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-960252628df76d4c680/1653472281)

Open image viewer

The first monkeypox case has been confirmed in Finland. Photo: Davor Puklavec / Lehtikuva

The first monkeypox case in Finland has been confirmed. It was confirmed from a sample in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district. HUS says that the patient is recovering.

HUS told about the suspicion of monkeypox on Wednesday. The infection was found in a man who has travelled in Europe.

The man was receiving home care, and he had a high fever and blisters.

Monkeypox usually gets better by itself, that is, without treatment, in a few weeks.
