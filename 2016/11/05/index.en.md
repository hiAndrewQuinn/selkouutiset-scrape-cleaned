# Saturday 5.11.2016 (radio)

The value of homes can collapse because of a poor sewer network. Germans want to remove hate speech from Facebook. A Renaissance masterpiece was saved. Lotto is changing.

## The value of homes can collapse because of a poor sewer network

![A sewer pipe is being installed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6359050)

Open image viewer

A sewer pipe is being repaired. According to Hypo bank, in many municipalities the pipes are already in very poor condition. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Ari Pauna, the CEO of Hypo bank, warns home owners of a risk worth millions of euros, which is caused by water and sewer pipes in poor condition.

According to the bank, in many municipalities the water and sewer pipes have not been repaired in time, and they are rotting under the ground until they are unusable. The repairs already cost hundreds of millions of euros. According to the bank, the repairs must be invested in as soon as possible, or else the value of homes will collapse.

## Germans want to remove hate speech from Facebook

![Mark Zuckerberg with a microphone in his hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9274663)

Open image viewer

Charges have been brought against Facebook's founder Mark Zuckerberg and other company directors in a Munich court.  Photo: EPA/PETER DASILVA

In Germany there are demands that Facebook remove racist messages from its site.

Charges have been brought against Facebook in a Munich court. According to the charges, Facebook does not remove hate speech and racist comments from its service effectively enough.

This year Facebook has set up a unit whose task is to monitor and remove racist messages.

## A Renaissance masterpiece was saved

![People stand in front of a large painting, many of them taking pictures with their phones. In the painting thirteen men have gathered around a table. On the table there are glasses, plates and a white cloth. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9274683)

Open image viewer

Giorgio Vasari's work The Last Supper has now been restored. Photo: Maurizio Degl'Innocenti / EPA

The painting The Last Supper by the 16th-century Italian artist Giorgio Vasari has now been fully restored. It was unveiled to the public in Florence on Friday. In the painting Jesus is having the last supper with his disciples.

Vasari's painting was badly damaged in the floods of the river Arno 50 years ago. At first it was thought that it could no longer be saved. With new methods the restoration took 5 years.

## Lotto is changing

![Balls spin in a lottery machine](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5008638)

Open image viewer

The probability of big wins gets smaller when Lotto is soon played with 40 balls. Photo: Veikkaus Oy

The Lotto game changes at the end of this month.

1 lotto ball is added to the game, that is, the winning row must be guessed from among 40 balls. In addition, only 1 bonus number is drawn in Lotto, not 2 as before. The price of a lotto row stays the same.

The first new Lotto will be played on 3 December.
