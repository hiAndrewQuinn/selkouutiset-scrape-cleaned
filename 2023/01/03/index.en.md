## Yle presidential poll

![Photo collage of Olli Rehn, Pekka Haavisto and Mika Aaltola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105446563af055008c07/1672746552)

None of the candidates named in the poll has yet stood as a candidate for president. Photo: Henna Tyrväinen / Yle

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto is the most popular presidential candidate in Yle's poll. The Greens' Pekka Haavisto received 25 percent support.

The governor of the Bank of Finland, Olli Rehn of the Centre Party, got 14 percent support. The third was the director of the Finnish Institute of International Affairs, Mika Aaltola. 11 percent of respondents supported him.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin of the SDP and Jussi Halla-aho of the Finns Party both received 7 percent support.

In the poll, Haavisto is supported most by women and younger age groups. Rehn is supported especially by high earners and people living in the countryside. The fastest riser was Mika Aaltola.

The first round of the presidential election is on 28 January next year. No one has yet registered for the contest.

## New factory

![Steel rolls stacked.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5759,h_3239,x_0,y_245/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-949736627125ac57a73/1672749021)

The factory is planned for the Joddböle industrial area in Inkoo. Illustrative image. Photo: EPA Photo: SSAB

A new factory may come to Uusimaa. The Norwegian steel company Blastr Green Steel is planning a steel factory and an integrated hydrogen production plant in Inkoo.

The company has signed a letter of intent with the energy company Fortum about the industrial area in Inkoo.

The value of the investment is about 4 billion euros. The factory could employ about 1,200 people. If the factory is realised, it will be one of Finland's largest industrial investments.

The major project is only at the very beginning. The actual investment decision on the steel factory will be made in 2025.

Housing sales in Finland have fallen to the levels of the 2008 financial crisis.

In Finland, new housing loans worth 1.2 billion euros were taken out in November. The figure is 750 million euros less than the November before last.

Housing sales began to fall after Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

## Moisture damage

![Mould on a wall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10143893)

Open image viewer

Mould on a wall. Photo: Andriy Popov / AOP

Moisture damage in a home in early childhood is linked to a child's permanent asthma. This is according to research by the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

However, researchers stress that there are dozens of risk factors for asthma. Moisture damage is not a particularly strong risk.

The study followed the growth and development of over 300 Finnish children for 6 years. Before the age of 1, the children lived in homes with moisture damage or visible mould.
