# Thursday 24.2.2022 (radio)

## Russia's attack on Ukraine

![Firefighters put out a fire in an apartment block that was hit by shelling.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1615,h_908,x_1,y_112/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9196616217422fdf2a2/1645790828)

Ukrainian firefighters tried to put out a fire that broke out when a Russian air strike hit a residential area in the city of Chuhuiv. Photo: Wolfgang Schwan / Getty Images

Russia has started an attack on Ukraine. The attack began on Thursday morning from many directions, including from Belarus.

Shelling and explosions have been heard, among other places, in the capital Kyiv.

Russian President Vladimir Putin spoke on Russian television in the morning. Putin claims that the operation defends the separatists whom Russia has supported in Eastern Ukraine.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has declared martial law in the country and asked for military help from abroad.

There is a lot of car traffic from Kyiv heading west and towards the neighbouring country Poland.

## Finland's leadership on the war

![Sanna Marin and Sauli Niinistö at a press briefing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5251,h_2953,x_0,y_491/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91972462174d3b9d756/1645694437)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin and President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Finland's leadership held a press briefing in the morning.

President Sauli Niinistö said that Finland strongly condemns Russia's military actions.

The President said that dialogue did not work, even though negotiations were held at many levels for a long time.

He said that many Finns now also feel fear. However, President Niinistö stressed that the threat is not directed at Finland now.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin also spoke at the briefing. Marin said that Finland fully supports Ukraine.

Finland is ready to act as part of the European Union, Marin said.

## NATO strengthens Eastern Europe

![NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89972161d8814f87311/1642172887)

Open image viewer

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg. Photo: John Thys / AFP

The NATO military alliance is strengthening its land, sea and air forces in Eastern Europe.

About 30 NATO countries have promised military equipment to Ukraine.

NATO countries will hold an emergency meeting on Friday. Finland and Sweden have also been invited to the meeting. Finland and Sweden are not NATO member countries.

The EU is also meeting already this evening. Prime Minister Sanna Marin represents Finland.

The EU, Britain and the USA are imposing sanctions on Russia.

The sanctions aim to stop Russia getting access to technologies and markets. In this way they try to weaken Russia's economy.

## The crisis's effect on Finland

![Ukrainian seasonal workers load parsley growing trays at Varpio garden.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81097460ad00a64d456/1621951222)

Open image viewer

A Ukrainian seasonal worker loads parsley growing trays at Varpio garden. Photo: Miki Wallenius / Yle

The war in Ukraine makes the economy of the whole world harder. The threat does not concern Finland, but the war may make the work of Finnish firms harder.

In Finland petrol and food may become more expensive. It may also be difficult to get Ukrainians to work in Finland.

Every year many seasonal workers have come to Finland from Ukraine.
