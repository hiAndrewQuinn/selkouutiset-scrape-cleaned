# Sunday 8.10.2017 (radio)

Upper limb pains have become more common because of heavy use of smart devices. The number of foreign students has decreased. The Air Force is starting a big military exercise. There are already thousands of wild boars in Finland.

## Smart devices can cause upper limb pain

![It is best to scroll on a phone with two hands. Good posture prevents strain on the neck and shoulder area.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9871684)

Various upper limb pains have become more common among young adults.

The Student Health Care Foundation says that, for example, pain in the hand or wrist is a common complaint among students. The pain is caused by heavy use of various smart devices, such as computers, tablets or phones.

Upper limb problems caused by smart devices have also increased among people who do office work, for example.

Chief physician Jarmo Kantonen says that heavy use of smartphones, for example, can cause various pain conditions and muscle tension. The phone is often used in a bad position, and that is why it can also cause pain and tension in the back and neck.

Physiotherapist Kati Kauppala says that a person copes better with straining working positions if they are in good condition. That is why it is worth increasing exercise. When using smart devices, it is also worth taking breaks often enough.

## Fewer students are coming from abroad

![Students reading in the main library in the Kaisa House of the University of Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9849912)

Universities started to charge tuition fees to foreign students this autumn. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Foreign students have applied for residence permits to Finland less than before.

The number of residence permit applications has decreased this autumn, because universities have started to charge tuition fees to foreign students.

The Finnish Immigration Service says that by the end of September about 4,300 foreign students had applied for a residence permit. That is 23 percent fewer than last year.

Universities charge tuition fees to students who come from outside the EU.

## The Air Force is practising

![Air Force Hornet and Sukhoi Su-24 aircraft photographed in international airspace over the Baltic Sea and the Gulf of Finland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9672954)

During the Ruska 17 exercise, Air Force aircraft start flying at 8 a.m. and stop in the evening at 11 p.m. Photo: Air Force

The Finnish Air Force begins a big military exercise on Monday. More than 5,000 people and dozens of aircraft take part in the Ruska 17 military exercise.

Aircraft of the Swedish Air Force also take part. The Swedish aircraft are practising the defence of Finland for the first time now.

The exercise can be seen and heard, because the Air Force aircraft fly from 8 different bases in different parts of the country.

The exercise ends on Friday.

## There are more wild boars than before

![Wild boar (Sus scrofa) at Ähtäri zoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9871974)

Open image viewer

There are already thousands of wild boars in Finland. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The number of wild boars has grown in Finland.

The Finnish Wildlife Agency says that at the end of last year there were perhaps about 3,400 wild boars. At the beginning of the 2010s there were only a few hundred wild boars.

The rapid growth in the number of wild boars is due, for example, to winters being milder, that is, warmer than before. The spread is also helped by the fact that wild boars eat all kinds of food and adapt to many kinds of environments.

Most wild boars live in Eastern and South-Eastern Finland.
