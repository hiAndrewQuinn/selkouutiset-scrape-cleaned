# Sunday 28.4.2019 (radio)

Spaniards have voted in the parliamentary elections. The SAS pilots' strike continues. Finland is a happy country, but not a cheerful one. Valtteri Bottas won the Baku Formula race.

## Spanish elections

![PSOE party's Pedro Sánchez voted with his wife in Madrid on Sunday morning, 28 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10758438)

Spaniards have voted in the elections on Sunday. Spanish voters are choosing a new parliament.

The result is expected to be even. No party is likely to get a majority in Parliament.

Opinion polls say that the Socialist party may get the most votes. The Socialist party is the party of the current prime minister, Pedro Sánchez.

Opinion polls also say that the new party Vox may get into Parliament. Vox wants to reduce immigration, for example.

The Spanish parties disagree about many things, for example immigration, abortion and the position of women. That is why it may be difficult for the parties to agree on Spain's new government after the elections.

## SAS strike

![A display showed cancelled flights at Arlanda airport on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10758259)

Open image viewer

Because of the strike, SAS has cancelled flights. Photo: Henrik Montgomery / EPA

The strike by SAS pilots continues. SAS pilots will not go to work on Monday and Tuesday.

SAS is a Nordic airline. SAS pilots are on strike in Sweden, Norway and Denmark.

Because of the strike SAS has also cancelled flights in Finland. On Sunday SAS cancelled one flight from Helsinki to Stockholm and 2 flights from Stockholm to Helsinki.

The reason for the strike is a pay dispute. SAS pilots want more pay.

## Finns' joy

![woman laughing](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5897163)

Open image viewer

Finns do not laugh very much. Photo: YLE / Raila Paavola

Everyone has probably heard that Finland is the happiest country in the world. Finland is a happy country because things are going well here.

But Finland is not a very cheerful country. This became clear when the Gallup firm asked people about their feelings.

People had the most positive and cheerful feelings in Latin America. The happiest people are found in Paraguay, Panama and Guatemala.

The most negative feelings were in the countries of the Sahara region in Africa. There people felt the worst in Chad and Niger.

Finns did not have a really cheerful or really bad mood. Finland is quite an ordinary country when it comes to joy.

## Bottas's win

![Valtteri Bottas in Baku](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10758566)

Open image viewer

Valtteri Bottas in Baku. Photo: Getty Images

Finnish Formula One driver Valtteri Bottas is doing well. Bottas drives a Mercedes car.

Bottas was the fastest driver in Baku, Azerbaijan, on Sunday. After the win Bottas also leads the Formula One World Championship.

Bottas has already won the Australian race this season. The Formula season continues until early December.
