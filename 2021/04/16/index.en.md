# Friday 16.4.2021 (radio)

Restaurants open on Monday. Corona vaccinations will be increased in areas with many corona infections. A new carer's leave is being planned in Finland. The weather is sunny and warm.

## Restaurants

![Restaurant customer seats out of use because of the emergency situation, but takeaway food available.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_628/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7850866051bf155fb1e/1667197213)

Restaurants will open again on Monday. However, the government has decided that restaurants will still be strictly restricted.

In the severe corona areas of Southern Finland, restaurants must stop selling alcohol at 5 pm. Bars must close at 6 pm and food restaurants at 7 pm. Dancing and karaoke are banned.

Restaurants may stay open longer in areas where the corona situation is good.

Restaurants have been closed for 6 weeks in a large part of Finland.

## Corona vaccinations

![Corona vaccines waiting on a table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3976,h_2237,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-791677606c23a6e1b9d/1617700163)

The government has also made new decisions about corona vaccinations.

Corona vaccinations will be increased in areas with many corona infections. This will be done until the end of May.

In addition, it has been decided that people will be vaccinated against corona by age order, after risk groups and older people have been vaccinated.

First, people over 60 will be vaccinated, and then people over 50. After that, vaccinations will move towards younger people, so that under-30s are vaccinated last.

2 new corona deaths have been reported in Finland. A total of 887 people have died of corona in Finland.

There are 380 new corona infections. Almost 84,000 corona infections have been found in Finland in total.

About 23 percent of Finns have been vaccinated against corona at least once.

## Carer's leave

![A comforting hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10792445)

Open image viewer

There are days when a family member needs help. Photo: Jaana Polamo / Yle

A new carer's leave is being planned in Finland.

The idea is that an employee can take time off work to help a family member. This may be necessary, for example, when the employee has to take an elderly family member to the doctor.

The idea is that an employee can take carer's leave for 5 days a year. During carer's leave, the employee does not get paid. Trade unions hope that pay will be given for carer's leave.

## The weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11889170)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

The weather is sunny and warm. The temperature is between 10 and 15 degrees across most of the country.

The warmest day of the weekend is Sunday. Then in the south it may be more than 15 degrees warm.

The sun will shine so strongly at the weekend that sensitive skin will already need protection.

Because of the dry weather there is also a lot of street dust in the air. People with allergies may get symptoms from street dust.
