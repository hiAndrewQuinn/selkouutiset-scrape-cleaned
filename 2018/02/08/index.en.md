# Thursday 8.2.2018 (radio)

A healthy lifestyle helps to prevent memory disorders even when the risk of illness runs in the family. In Kuopio, researchers are studying what kind of sauna stove pollutes the least. The SDP and the Centre Party are popular, even though the presidential election went badly. The church will choose an archbishop at the beginning of March. The EU Parliament does not want to end summer time.

## A healthy lifestyle prevents memory disorders

src="https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5934716"

Open image viewer

Some people have a higher than usual risk of getting a memory disorder in their family. Photo: YLE

Memory disorders can be prevented with a healthy lifestyle.

A healthy lifestyle also helps those people who have a lot of memory disorders in their family. A study done in Finland and Sweden showed that people's memory stayed better if they got good lifestyle advice. The advice also helped those people who have a lot of memory disorders in their family.

In the study, people were advised, for example, to eat right, to exercise enough and to do memory exercises.

## What kind of sauna stove does not pollute?

![Throwing water on the sauna stove](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10066286)

Open image viewer

A wood-burning sauna stove gives good steam, but it also releases fine particles into the air. Photo: Petri Jauhiainen / Yle

The University of Kuopio is studying what kind of sauna stove pollutes the air the least.

When wood is burned in a sauna stove, fine particles get into the air with the smoke. Fine particles are unhealthy. For example, in the Helsinki region, as many fine particles spread into the air from wood-burning sauna stoves and fireplaces as from traffic and power plants.

## Poor election result does not show in popularity

![A statistics graphic on party support.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10063486)

Open image viewer

Support for the parties in January 2018. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Two big parties, the Social Democrats and the Centre Party, did badly in the presidential election. However, the poor result does not show in Yle's new support poll. In it, the National Coalition Party is the most popular party, the Social Democrats, or SDP, are the second most popular, and the Centre Party is third.

Researchers say that Sauli Niinistö's popularity also raises the popularity of the National Coalition Party. The SDP's popularity is perhaps increased by the fact that the party opposes the new activation model for the unemployed. Many Finns think that the activation model is unfair.

In Yle's study, the Greens are fourth, then come the Finns Party, the Left Alliance, the Swedish People's Party, the Christian Democrats and Blue Reform.

## Vikström or Luoma the new archbishop

![Björn Vikström, Tapio Luoma](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10065645)

Open image viewer

Björn Vikström and Tapio Luoma. Photo: Yle

The Lutheran Church of Finland will choose a new leader, that is, an archbishop, in March.

There was a vote on the archbishop in the church today, but nobody got more than half of the votes. The most votes went to the Bishop of Espoo, Tapio Luoma, and the Bishop of Porvoo, Björn Vikström. They are in the second vote on 1 March.

The current archbishop, Kari Mäkinen, will retire in the summer.

## Summer time stays

![An alarm clock beside a bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10043722)

Open image viewer

The EU Parliament wants to keep summer time. Photo: AOP

The EU Parliament does not support ending summer time.

Finland wants the EU to stop moving the clocks to a different time for the summer. Finland's proposal lost in the Parliament's vote. However, the Parliament asked the EU Commission to find out what the benefits and the harms of summer time are.

Finnish members of the European Parliament said that moving the clock by an hour perhaps affects some people's health.
