## Travel restrictions are changing

![Pekka Haavisto and Maria Ohisalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11439866)

Open image viewer

Ministers Pekka Haavisto and Maria Ohisalo spoke about travel restrictions on Wednesday. Photo: Yle

The government is easing travel restrictions during the corona era. From next Monday, Finns can travel to many countries without quarantine.

Such countries include many European countries, for example Germany, Greece and Italy. Finns therefore no longer have to stay at home in quarantine after they return home from these countries.

In addition, work travel may start to some countries outside Europe. Work travel is allowed, for example, to Japan and China. This change also comes into force on Monday, 13 July.

Finland is easing travel restrictions only for countries where the corona situation is good. For example, in Sweden there are many more corona cases than in Finland. Therefore it is still a good idea for people to stay at home in quarantine when they come from Sweden to Finland.

## Sleeping together is good

Sleeping together improves the quality of sleep.

A couple gets more REM sleep if the couple sleeps together. This is what a new study shows.

During REM sleep a person dreams. REM sleep is important for health.

Sleeping together increases the amount of REM sleep. The reason may be that a person's stress decreases when they sleep next to their partner.

## Fish traps can be dangerous to the Saimaa ringed seal

![A Saimaa ringed seal in Savonlinna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11438210)

Open image viewer

Saimaa ringed seals live only in the Saimaa area. Photo: Juha Metso / AOP

This summer, fish traps that are dangerous to Saimaa ringed seals have been found in Lake Saimaa. The Finnish Association for Nature Conservation reports this.

A fish trap is used to catch fish. A Saimaa ringed seal may also get caught in a trap if the trap is the wrong kind. Then the Saimaa ringed seal drowns and dies. That is why some traps are banned on Lake Saimaa.

The Saimaa ringed seal is a rare animal. Saimaa ringed seals live only in Finland, in the Saimaa area.

The Finnish Association for Nature Conservation asks that people use only traps on Saimaa that are not dangerous to Saimaa ringed seals.
