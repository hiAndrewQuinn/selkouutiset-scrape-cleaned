# Friday 7.2.2020 (radio)

Finnish authorities have given instructions on travel to China. The government has given the Terrafame mine permission to sell uranium. Interest in nurse training has fallen. Smoking during pregnancy is more dangerous than was thought.

## Coronavirus

![Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11198932)

Open image viewer

Helsinki Airport is prepared for the coronavirus. Photo: Tomi Natri / AOP

The Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs has issued an instruction on travel to China. The Ministry says that Finns must now avoid travel to the whole of China. The reason for the instruction is the coronavirus, which is spreading in China.

At Helsinki Airport extra cleaning work was done on Friday. The reason was a passenger who later showed symptoms of the coronavirus in Copenhagen. It has now been reported that the passenger does not have the coronavirus.

Attempts are being made to stop the coronavirus from spreading, because the virus is dangerous. In China the coronavirus has already killed over 600 people.

## The Terrafame mine

![Terrafame open pit mine in Sotkamo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7952,h_4473,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6162245dde2dce6b1ca/1574841931)

The Terrafame mine is in Sotkamo. Photo: Taisto Lapila / Yle

The government has given the Terrafame mine permission to sell uranium. The mine is in northern Finland, in Sotkamo.

Today the Terrafame mine produces other metals, mainly nickel and zinc. Uranium is also obtained from the mine, but Terrafame has not had permission to recover it.

Terrafame will become the only uranium producer in the area of the European Union. Terrafame intends to sell uranium abroad. Energy is obtained from uranium in nuclear power plants.

## Nurse training

![A resident and a nurse in a care home.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11194890)

Open image viewer

Young people are not interested in the care sector. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Interest in nurse training has fallen. There are now fewer applicants for nurse training than before.

The number of applicants has fallen both for practical nurse training and for nurse training at universities of applied sciences. The number of applicants has dropped by thousands in a few years.

Young people do not want to work in care, because the sector's reputation is poor. Nurses' work is hard and the pay is low.

The reputation of teaching has also suffered, because contact teaching has been reduced. Students do not always get as much support from teachers as they would like.

## Smoking during pregnancy

![A cigarette burning between fingers](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10110886)

Open image viewer

Smoking during pregnancy affects the child. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There is new information about tobacco. A new study says that smoking during pregnancy is more dangerous than was thought.

Researcher Isabell Rumrich from the University of Eastern Finland says that smoking affects the child already at the start of pregnancy. For example, the child develops a smaller brain if the mother smokes during the first 3 months of pregnancy.

The problem is that a mother does not always know she is pregnant at the start of pregnancy. That is why she does not stop smoking early enough.

In 2018 about 10 per cent of Finnish mothers smoked during pregnancy. The most smokers were mothers under 20 years old giving birth.
