# Sunday 20.10.2019 (radio)

Finland's Prime Minister hopes that Britain will get more time for Brexit. 2 dead people have been found on the Tallink Silja Europa ship. Demonstrations have continued in Chile. The Nature Photo of the Year has been chosen.

## Prime Minister Rinne

![Antti Rinne ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11028575)

Finland's Prime Minister Antti Rinne. Photo: Olivier Matthys / AFP / Lehtikuva

Finland's Prime Minister Antti Rinne hopes that Britain will get more time for Brexit. Brexit means Britain's leaving of the European Union.

In the view of Finland's Prime Minister, it is important that the departure does not happen without an agreement between Britain and the EU. That is why more time is needed.

The EU and the British government already made the text for an agreement, but the British Parliament has not yet approved it.

The British Parliament was supposed to decide on the withdrawal agreement on Saturday. However, Parliament was not able to make a decision.

British Prime Minister Boris Johnson wants Britain to leave the EU already at the end of October. It is feared that the whole European economy will suffer if Britain leaves the EU without an agreement.

## The Europa ship

![Tallink Silja Europa arriving from Tallinn at the Helsinki Western Terminal on Sunday 20 October. Estonian police found two dead young adults on the ship in the Port of Tallinn on Sunday morning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11028702)

2 dead people were found on the Europa ship. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

2 dead people have been found on the Tallink Silja Europa ship. The ship was on its way from Helsinki to Tallinn.

The Estonian police say that the dead showed no signs of violence. The police also say that the cases are not connected to each other. The dead were found in different cabins.

The dead were Finnish citizens. One was a 25-year-old woman and the other was a 21-year-old man.

## Chile

![Demonstrators in Santiago.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11028515)

Open image viewer

Demonstrators in Santiago, the capital of Chile. Photo: Elvis Gonzales / EPA

In South America, in Chile, the demonstrations have continued. The demonstrations have been violent.

At least 3 people have died in the demonstrations. They died in a fire. The fire started in a shop that the demonstrators attacked.

The demonstrations began in Santiago, the capital of Chile, when the price of a metro ticket went up at the beginning of October. In the poor people's view, life in Santiago is already too expensive. In Chile, the gap between the poor and the rich is big.

The Nature Photo of the Year has been chosen. The best nature photo of 2019 is Ville Heikkinen's photo, titled "Bear and Raven". Photo: Ville Heikkinen

The Nature Photo of the Year has been chosen. The best nature photo of 2019 is Ville Heikkinen's photo, titled "Bear and Raven".

Ville Heikkinen is from Kuhmo. He is a professional nature photographer.

The judges praise Heikkinen for his technical skill. The composition of the picture is also fine. The bear and the bird can be seen as shadows against the sky.

The winner of the series for young nature photographers was Lasse Kurkela. Kurkela's photo shows a wolf cub.

The aim of the Nature Photo of the Year competition is to tell about Finnish nature and photographers' experiences in Finnish nature.
