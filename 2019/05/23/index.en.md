## Three parties stand out in Yle's EU election poll

Yle's opinion poll says that the National Coalition and the Greens may win the election in the European Parliament elections. The elections are on Sunday.

The poll says that the National Coalition may keep 3 seats. The National Coalition has been Finland's largest European party since 1999.

The Greens may get 1 to 3 more seats, on top of their current 3. The Finns Party may keep 2 seats.

The SDP may keep its 2 seats and the Left Alliance its 1. The Centre is threatened with losing a seat and a second one.

The Greens, the Finns Party and the RKP are competing for Finland's last seat. The RKP gets votes from Åland.

Predicting is difficult, because in the poll only half said which party they support. So there are many uncertain voters.

## The new social and health care reform has 18 regions

![Antti Rinne, SDP party leader and leader of the government negotiations, arrived at the government negotiations at Säätytalo in Helsinki on 23 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10797959)

Open image viewer

The government negotiations are led by SDP party leader Antti Rinne. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The government negotiations have agreed on the administrative model for social and health care. Sote means the reform of social and health services.

Antti Rinne, the SDP leader who is leading the government negotiations, said that the new sote model has 18 regions. They are a kind of self-governing areas.

The decision-makers of the regions, that is, the councillors, will be elected in direct elections.

A separate solution is coming for the capital region. It is intended to be settled by the end of the year.

The idea is that the public sector is the main provider of sote. Taxation and financing will go to parliamentary preparation. That means that opposition parties will also be able to take part in planning the financing.

## Employment has improved

![A workman at a building site](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10760308)

Open image viewer

A workman at a building site. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Employment has improved. Last month there were about 28,000 more employed people than at the same time a year ago.

Statistics Finland says that the trend in the employment rate in April was 72.4 per cent. The figure shows no seasonal variations.

There were 220,000 unemployed people. The number was 14,000 lower than a year ago.

## Role models on social media influence young people's further studies

![Three women walk on the pavement.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5113865ba49a5306dcd/1537514134)

Role models on social media influence young people's choices and plans for further studies more and more. The Young People's Future Report study says that young people get information especially from YouTube, Instagram and Snapchat.

However, the researcher says that young people need more guidance with their study choices.

An ever larger group of young people also want to take a gap year. As many as 38 per cent of upper secondary school girls intend to take a gap year. Only a few years ago the figure was 20 per cent.

During a gap year, people want time for themselves and for choosing a field of study.
