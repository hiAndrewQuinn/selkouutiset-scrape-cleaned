# Saturday 6.11.2021 (radio)

People died at a music festival in the USA. There was a serious explosion accident in Sierra Leone. The Ähtäri animal park may return the pandas to China. On Saturday it is All Saints' Day and Swedishness Day.

## People died at a music festival in Houston

At least 8 people died at a music festival in Houston in the state of Texas, USA. In total up to 300 people were injured. 17 people were taken to hospital.

Too large a crowd of people went close to the concert stage. Panic broke out. About 50 000 people were there.

The rapper Travis Scott's Astroworld festival was held for the third time now. The second day of the festival has been cancelled.

## Accident in Sierra Leone

A serious accident has happened in Sierra Leone in West Africa.

A tanker truck first collided with a lorry. After that the tanker began to leak and people came to collect fuel. Then the fuel caught fire and exploded.

About 100 people died in the accident, and there are many injured.

The accident happened in the capital of Sierra Leone, Freetown.

## Finns leave Ethiopia

![Map of the areas of TPLF and OLA that rule Ethiopia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12175716)

About 30 Finns tell the Ministry for Foreign Affairs that they will soon leave Ethiopia.

In Ethiopia in East Africa there is fighting, and the war is approaching the capital, Addis Ababa. There is a state of emergency in Ethiopia.

The Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs says that all Finns should leave Ethiopia immediately.

## Ähtäri pandas

![A giant panda eats bamboo at the Ähtäri Panda House.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4175,h_2348,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78550760531f555e8b8/1616060322)

The pandas of the Ähtäri animal park may have to be returned to China. Caring for the pandas costs about one million euros a year. The Ähtäri animal park does not have enough money.

The state earlier promised the Ähtäri animal park corona support of 1.5 million euros. The mayor of Ähtäri, Jarmo Pienimäki, says that the animal park has so far received only about 200 000 euros.

Few people have visited the Ähtäri animal park during the corona time.

The pandas Lumi and Pyry came from China to Finland in January 2018.

## All Saints' Day and Swedishness Day

![Finnish flag behind tree branches.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4791,h_2695,x_0,y_479/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7405695faab8291cf1c/1605024402)

Today, Saturday, is All Saints' Day.

On All Saints' Day close people who have died are remembered. On All Saints' Day it is customary to take candles to the graves of close and dear people.

Saturday is also Swedishness Day. This day is a common celebration day for all Finland-Swedes. The purpose of Swedishness Day is also to tell Finnish speakers about Finland-Swedes.
