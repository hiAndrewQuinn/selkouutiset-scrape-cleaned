# Thursday 8.12.2022 (radio)

State spending. Party support. Children's swimming skills. Snowfall. Weather.

## State spending

![Two men and two women study papers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12683992)

Ministry of Finance officials. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finland must cut state spending and collect more taxes in the coming years. This is what Ministry of Finance officials say.

The officials say that from next year onwards, the state must cut spending and increase taxes by a total of 6 billion euros over 4 years.

The officials also say that Finland needs more workers from abroad.

Ministry of Finance officials plan Finland's economy. However, politicians make the decisions.

## Party support

Photo: Eetu-Mikko Pietarinen / Yle

Yle has measured party support. In the poll, people were asked which party they intend to vote for.

The poll shows that support for the Centre Party has fallen. Support for the Centre Party is now 9 percent.

Support for the Centre Party is below 10 percent for the first time. The Centre Party has previously been one of Finland's big parties.

The Centre Party is now in the government. However, it has recently argued with the other government parties. The disputes may have reduced support for the Centre Party.

Support for the government party SDP is 19 percent. The SDP is the second largest party in the poll.

The largest party in the poll is the National Coalition Party. 24 percent of respondents support the National Coalition Party. The National Coalition Party is an opposition party, that is, it is not in the government.

The support figures for all parties can be found on Yle's website.

## Children's swimming skills

![Swimming school for children under 6 at the Mäkelänrinne swimming hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10213917)

Open image viewer

Children at swimming school before the corona times. Photo: Jussi Koivunoro / Yle

Children's swimming skills have got worse.

A new study says that now just over 50 percent of sixth-grade children can swim well enough. As late as 2016 the situation was better. At that time 75 percent of children could swim.

Children's swimming skills got worse during the corona times. During the corona times, swimming halls were often closed.

A lot of snow has fallen in Finland. On Thursday drivers were warned about bad driving conditions.

In southern Finland there is more snow than usual for early December. In places, there is already more than 30 centimetres of snow.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12683997)

Open image viewer

On Friday more snow falls in the north. The south and centre of the country are dry, and the sun even shines in places. Mostly, however, it is cloudy. The temperature is between -2 and -8 degrees across the whole country.

At the weekend the weather clears, that is, no more snow falls. The weather also gets colder. On Saturday the frost in Lapland may be between 10 and 20 degrees.
