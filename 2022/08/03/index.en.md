# Wednesday 3.8.2022

Young women are not satisfied with life. Many Ukrainian pupils will start school in Finland in autumn. France and Italy approved Finland's joining NATO. Many international military exercises have been held in Finland in the summer. Many got a tax refund today. Tomorrow's weather is unstable.

## Satisfaction with life

![A young woman walks on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4144,h_2331,x_0,y_291/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6117765dcd3f588b531/1574750322)

Young women are the most dissatisfied with life. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finns' satisfaction with their own life has decreased. About 20 percent of Finns are dissatisfied with life.

In Yle's survey, the most dissatisfied are young women under 30. About 30 percent of them say that they are not satisfied with life.

The most satisfied with their own life are Finns over 60.

The study also asked what things Finns dream about. Most often mentioned were things related to health, work and livelihood.

## Pupils from Ukraine

![There is an empty classroom in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5479,h_3082,x_0,y_291/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6766655ebe4d3d56216/1642772458)

Open image viewer

Comprehensive schools start next week. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Many Ukrainians will start studying in Finnish schools this autumn.

Over 3500 Ukrainians start school in Finland. Most start in basic education and in preparatory teaching, and some in upper secondary schools.

About 33,000 Ukrainians have applied for temporary protection in Finland. Of them about 1/3, that is over 10,000, are underage children and young people.

## NATO ratifications

![NATO flag, 22/30 on the NATO counter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1917,h_1076,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97276562b16bce85b79/1655795834)

Open image viewer

22 countries have now approved Finland and Sweden joining NATO. Photo: Eetu-Mikko Pietarinen / Yle

France and Italy have approved Finland and Sweden joining the military alliance NATO. The parliaments of France and Italy voted in favour of Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership.

2/3 of the NATO countries have now ratified, that is, approved, Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership.

The next ratification is expected from the United States, where the Senate votes on the matter today.

![Transport helicopters flying in Pirkkala.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4836,h_2720,x_0,y_505/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98995162ea5a1001b43/1659526535)

There have been many NATO troops in Finland this summer. NATO countries have taken part in international exercises, which have been held in different parts of the country during the summer.

For example, this week exercises are being held in the Tampere area. In the area there are special forces from the United States and Britain.

The aim of the exercises is to develop the international cooperation ability of Finnish special forces.

## Tax refunds

![A man checking tax refund matters on the Tax Administration's website.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89845461d3f50f8a7ee/1641280830)

Open image viewer

1.7 million Finns got a tax refund today. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Many Finns have received a tax refund today.

The Tax Administration says that about 1.7 million Finns have received a tax refund today. Tax refunds were now paid in total about 700 million euros.

The Tax Administration pays refunds every month from July to December.
