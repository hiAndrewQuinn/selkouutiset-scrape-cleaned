# Sunday 1.10.2017 (radio)

Catalonia has voted on independence. The police are investigating a fight in Tampere. Finns do not want to talk about money matters. On Sunday flags were flown for Miina Sillanpää.

## Catalonia voted

![Police officers and Catalan voters](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9861206)

The police tried to stop Catalans from voting. Photo: Jaume Sellart / EPA

A referendum has been held in Catalonia in Spain. In the vote, Catalans were asked whether they want to leave Spain and found their own state.

Spain thinks that the referendum is illegal, and on Sunday the police tried to close polling stations. Supporters of independence defended the polling stations. They also fought with the police.

Hundreds of people were injured during Sunday.

Catalonia is in northern Spain. Catalonia's largest city is Barcelona. Many Catalans speak the Catalan language.

## There was a fight in Tampere

![Map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9861102)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics

The Finnish police are investigating a fight that happened in Tampere. The fight was in the Särkänniemi car park during the night between Wednesday and Thursday.

2 groups were involved in the fight. Most of the fighters were under 20 years old. They used, for example, knives and batons.

The police say that the violence was senseless. After the fight the police arrested 7 people.

## Money matters are not talked about

![A man drives a convertible.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9820497)

Open image viewer

If a Finn is rich, he does not want to show it. Photo: AOP

A Finn does not want to talk about his own money matters. A Finn does not even want to say or show that he is rich. This is what a survey says that Taloustutkimus did for Yle.

Juho Rahkonen, research manager at Taloustutkimus, says that a Finn wants to be like everyone else. If someone has a lot of money, they try to keep it hidden. Rahkonen has heard, for example, about a man who had a fancy Ferrari car. The man drove the Ferrari only at night.

A rich person is most ashamed if he has inherited the money from his parents. Finns think it is better if a person has earned the money himself. That is why, for example, ice hockey players and Formula drivers can show that they have plenty of money.

## Flags were flown for Sillanpää

![Miina Sillanpää](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9164847)

Open image viewer

Miina Sillanpää in 1920. Photo: Finnish Heritage Agency

On Sunday flags have been flown in honour of Miina Sillanpää.

Miina Sillanpää was the first woman to become a minister in Finland. It happened more than 90 years ago, in 1926.

Miina Sillanpää's work as an MP began as early as 1907. That was when the Eduskunta, or Finland's parliament, was elected for the first time.

In 1907, a total of 19 women got into Parliament. They were the first women MPs in the whole world.

Miina Sillanpää was a social democrat. She wanted to improve the position of workers and women.
