# Sunday 7.8.2022

Electricity gets cheaper in the spring. Inflation frightens young people. People rent big flats. Ott Tänak won the Finland rally of the World Rally Championship. The weather is dry.

## The price of electricity

Electricity prices rise to close to 50 cents per kilowatt hour on average even at the end of the year. That quite certainly means electricity price records, because individual hours will be much more expensive. Photo: Susanna Pesonen / Yle, graphic: Samuli Huttunen / Yle

Electricity is even more expensive at the end of the year than now.

A forecast says that in the spring of next year electricity may already get much cheaper. Electricity is still much more expensive than, for example, last year. For example, the fact that gas no longer comes to Finland from Russia raises the price of electricity.

Next year Finland will be able to produce as much electricity as Finland uses. This is a big change, because Finland has for a long time bought a lot of electricity from abroad.

## Young people's worries

![People spending time in a shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98165562ced83fa4a52/1657724364)

Open image viewer

People spending time in a shopping centre. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Young people are afraid of inflation, that is, the rise in prices. In the spring Yle asked 15–29-year-old young people about their fears and worries.

The survey says that Finnish young people now fear inflation more than war or climate change. Almost 50 percent of young people say that the biggest fear is the rise in prices.

40 percent of young people fear war. Many also fear loneliness, for example.

In 2020 climate change was the biggest worry of young people in Yle's survey.

## Rental flats

![New apartment blocks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97886362c5994bee853/1721980804)

Open image viewer

Abundant flat construction has also raised the quality of rental housing. The picture shows Espoo's Finnoo, a new district. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

People now rent bigger flats than before. People also want better rental flats than before. It is hard to find a tenant for a bad flat, even if the flat is cheap.

A lot of new rental flats have been built in good locations in cities. The quality of rental flats in Finland is already better than in many other countries. This is what the landlords' association says.

## The Finland world rally

![Kalle Rovanperä drives at Harju.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2923,h_1644,x_0,y_222/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99141162ef964345743/1659868863)

Kalle Rovanperä drives at Harju. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

Kalle Rovanperä finished 2nd in the Finland race of the world rally championship in Jyväskylä. The race was won by the Estonian Ott Tänak. Rovanperä lost by 6.8 seconds. Esa-Pekka Lappi was 3rd in the race.

Kalle Rovanperä clearly leads the rally world championship series. The next race is driven in Belgium in a little under 2 weeks. There are still 6 races left in the rally world championship series.
