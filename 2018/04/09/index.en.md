# Monday 9.4. 2018 (radio)

The trial for the Turku stabbing has started. There is a strike in the electrical sector. Young people drink less alcohol. A grave memorial has been chosen for President Mauno Koivisto. Mikael Agricola Day raised the Finnish flag up the flagpole.

## The Turku stabbing

![Aberrahman Bouanane](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10150710)

The man accused of the Turku stabbings is 23-year-old Aberrahman Bouanane. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The trial for the Turku stabbing has started.

A 23-year-old Moroccan man is accused of two terrorist murders and eight attempted murders. The trial continues until May.

The Turku stabbing happened last summer. The man stabbed ten people with a knife, and two of them died.

## Electrical sector strike

The electrical sector strike lasts a week. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

A strike started in the electrical sector today.

More than 2,000 workers have stayed away from work. The strike lasts a week. The electrical workers' organisation, the Electrical Workers' Union, says that the strike slows down big construction projects.

## Young people drink less alcohol

![A man and a full beer bottle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9164516)

Open image viewer

In Finland young people have become more sober. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Young people in Finland drink less alcohol. This is reported by the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL.

Alcohol use among young Finns has gone down for many years. A survey of young people says that there are three important reasons for not drinking. Alcohol is hard to buy and parents' attitudes have become stricter. Young people also have other things to do in their free time than drinking.

The study was done in many European countries. It looked at the lives of young people aged 15 and over.

## Facebook data leak

![A man stands in front of a large Facebook sign and shows his thumb to another man, who is taking a photo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10147037)

Open image viewer

Facebook is sending messages about the data leak to its users. Photo: John G. Mabanglo / EPA

Users of the social network Facebook will get a notice about a data leak today.

The company is sending a message to those Facebook users whose personal data may have been misused. In total almost 90 million users will get the message. In Finland about 20,000 users will get the message.

The message link will appear on users' news feed page on Facebook.

## A grave memorial for President Mauno Koivisto

!["Map", visual artist and photographer Perttu Saksa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10150768)

Open image viewer

President Mauno Koivisto will get a memorial in November. Photo: Finnish Government

The grave memorial for President Mauno Koivisto has been chosen.

A competition was held for the memorial. It was won by the artist Perttu Saksa and a work called "Map".

The memorial is a black slab and it is decorated with gold. The slab is over two metres wide and about a metre high.

The memorial will be put on Koivisto's grave in November.

President Mauno Koivisto died last May. He was 93 years old. Koivisto's grave is in Helsinki, at Hietaniemi cemetery.

## Mikael Agricola Day is a flag day

![A picture from Mikael Agricola's ABC book.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5099672)

Open image viewer

A picture from Mikael Agricola's ABC book Photo: Yle

Today Mikael Agricola Day has been celebrated.

Mikael Agricola Day is also the day of the Finnish language and a flag day.

Mikael Agricola is considered the father of written Finnish.

His ABC book was the first book in Finnish. It came out in 1543.
