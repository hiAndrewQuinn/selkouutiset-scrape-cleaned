Prime Minister Sanna Marin is visiting Ukraine. Marin's visit was not announced in advance.

Marin has met President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. Zelenskyy has published pictures of the meeting on Instagram. He thanks Marin and Finland for the support that Ukraine has received.

## Corona vaccines

![Corona vaccine research at the Meilahti Vaccine Research Centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3263,h_1835,x_0,y_2039/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81596060ba165fdef42/1622808538)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Several pharmaceutical companies are developing a so-called new generation corona vaccine. It is expected on the market in the autumn.

Corona may spread again after the summer.

The new vaccine is intended to give longer protection, also against new variants.

The new vaccine must also withstand transport well.

The director of the Vaccine Research Centre, Mika Rämet, says that storability is an important matter. The vaccine must be kept at fridge temperature, because that makes distribution easier. In many countries freezer temperatures are hard to arrange.

Current vaccines protect well from a serious disease. However, protection against infection weakens quickly.

## Corona patients

![A doctor in protective clothing takes notes at the bedside of a patient on a ventilator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11610039)

Open image viewer

A corona patient in the intensive care unit of a hospital. Photo: Imago / AOP

The number of corona patients in hospitals has fallen a lot in a week.

In Finland there are now just under 490 patients in hospital related to corona. The figure has fallen by more than 100 during the week.

Fewer than 20 of the patients are in intensive care.

## Solar panels

![Solar panels on the roof of an apartment block.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2802,h_1576,x_0,y_490/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7301335f870dc357eaf/1602686608)

Open image viewer

The prices of electricity, district heating and oil are rising. That is why more and more people want to install solar panels in their homes to produce electricity. Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

Solar panels are being sold a lot now.

Lumme Energia says that the installation queues for solar panels are now long. The company's queues are more than 2 months.

Solar panels are now also more expensive than before. Transport has become more expensive, and fewer manufacturing materials are available.

Growth in demand has also pushed up prices.

## The Lumberjack's Story in Cannes

![A couple in bed. The woman is reading a book.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_201/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-957221628611d7673bd/1652953866)

Katja Küttner (left) plays Pepe's (Jarkko Lahti) wife in The Lumberjack's Story. Photo: Tero Ahonen / Aamu

A Finnish film has been awarded at the Cannes Film Festival in France. The film The Lumberjack's Story won the Critics' Week award.

The purpose of the award is to support the distribution of the film in France. The prize is 20 000 euros.

The Lumberjack's Story was directed and written by Mikko Myllylahti.

The film premieres in Finland in October.
