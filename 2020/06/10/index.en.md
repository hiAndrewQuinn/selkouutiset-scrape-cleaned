## The Olof Palme murder investigation ends

![Stig Engström in 1986.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11394148)

Open image viewer

Stig Engström, known as the "Skandia man". Photo: Weine Lexius / TT

The investigation into the murder of Swedish Prime Minister Olof Palme is ending. Palme was murdered on a street in Stockholm in February 1986.

The prosecutor says that the man suspected of the murder is Stig Engström. He was the so-called Skandia man. Engström worked in the Skandia building near the scene of the murder.

He was heard in the investigation as an eyewitness. He died already in 2000.

No charge will be brought, because Engström is dead. That is why the investigation also ends.

The murder investigation has been a great grief and trauma for Swedes. The murder was investigated for over 30 years without result.

Over the years there have been many different lines of investigation and suspects in the investigation. The investigation has been one of the biggest murder investigations in the whole world. For example, 10,000 interviews have been carried out.

The murder weapon has not been found. It was probably a revolver.

In the forensic investigation, almost 800 cartridges were test-fired. None of them could be linked to the 2 bullets that were found at the scene of the murder.

## Corona can cause late symptoms

![The audience keeps safety distances at the Uusi Alku athletics invitational in Lahti on 7 June 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11393519)

Open image viewer

Late symptoms of corona include, among others, a fluctuating fever, a long-lasting cough and exhaustion. The audience at the athletics event in the picture kept safety distances in Lahti last Sunday. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

A severe corona illness causes late symptoms. They can last for weeks or even months.

A typical symptom is a weakened sense of smell. Many people have joint pains, and for some, rheumatism has got worse. Late symptoms also include long-term exhaustion and tiredness.

The symptoms are similar to those of influenza. It is typical for the symptoms to last a long time.

Little is known yet about the long-term effects of the coronavirus. It is not yet known whether a coronavirus infection will trigger other illnesses.

## Many people have already drowned this year

![The silhouette of boaters in the midnight sun.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4992,h_2808,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-340399591bd5e63d8eb/1548426229)

The start of the boating season is already visible in drowning figures. Photo: Kaisa Siren / All Over Press Finland

More people have drowned in Finland in the early part of the year than in recent years. By the end of May, 40 people had drowned.

The number is the highest for 6 years at this point in the year.

Early in the year, people drowned through the ice, because the ice conditions were weaker than usual.

In spring, the start of the boating season and the warming of the weather have shown up in the statistics.

## Even light movement helps health

![A man stretches his calves on the street, leaning against the wall of an apartment block.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11393727)

Open image viewer

Even a small amount of exercise is better than none. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The UKK Institute has published new recommendations for adapted physical activity.

The recommendations include exercise guidance especially for people whose functional ability is limited, for example by illness or disability. There are also recommendations for those who use a rollator or a wheelchair, for example.

The new recommendations say that even so-called light movement brings health benefits. Even short bouts of movement help. Previously, at least 10 minutes of movement time has been recommended.

In a week it is good to move briskly for at least 2.5 hours.
