# Wednesday 20.11.2019 (radio)

Negotiations on the postal strike have continued. Children's Day was celebrated on Wednesday. "Doctor Google" often suggests a much worse disease than a person really has. Yle will start children's news next year.

## The situation with the postal strike

![Post boxes in front of the Main Post Office in Helsinki on 18 November](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11075102)

Because of the postal strike, letters are not moving. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Negotiations on the postal strike have continued. The negotiators are looking for a solution to the postal sector dispute. In the sector there is a dispute about pay, for example.

If the postal strike continues, the support strikes will also continue. Trade unions in many sectors support the postal workers' strike. The support strikes will stop Posti's deliveries on roads, railways, in ports and at airports.

If the postal strike continues for another week, more support strikes will come. The new support strikes will affect, for example, people's movement.

On Monday, a support strike will stop buses in the Helsinki region. Many ferries between Helsinki and Stockholm will not run during the early part of the week. On Monday there will also be support strikes at airports; for example, security check staff may stop work.

If the postal strike ends, there will be no support strikes.

The postal strike began on 11 November. Because of the strike, letters and parcels are moving very slowly.

## Children's Day

![a father shows a picture book to a child](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10998326)

Open image viewer

Every child has the right to a good life. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Children's Day was celebrated on Wednesday. International Children's Rights Day is on 20 November in many countries.

The UN, that is, the United Nations, recommends that Children's Day is celebrated in all countries. The UN Convention on the Rights of the Child was adopted exactly 30 years ago, that is, on 20 November 1989.

The Convention on the Rights of the Child says that a child has the right to a good life, protection and development.

In Finland, Children's Day is a flag day. That is why the Finnish flag has been flying on Wednesday in various parts of Finland.

## Doctor Google

![Woman at a laptop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11069209)

Open image viewer

Frightening diseases can be found on Google. Photo: AOP

Many people look for the cause online when they feel unwell. However, "Doctor Google" often suggests a much worse disease than a person really has.

An American study found that almost half of people who googled found a worse disease online than the one they had.

Hannu Halila of the Finnish Medical Association believes that the situation is the same in Finland. Many even bring doctors papers that they have printed from Google.

People often fear cancer, Halila says. The doctor's task is to remove the patient's fears.
