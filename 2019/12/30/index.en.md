# Monday 30.12.2019 (radio)

Sales of older homes grew this year. Young Finns are interested in Estonia. Many fireworks rockets are fired around New Year. In Helsinki, New Year is celebrated at Kansalaistori.

## Housing sales

![Sales contract on a table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10592201)

Open image viewer

Housing sales have been brisk. Photo: AOP

Older homes have been sold more this year than last year. Sales have grown by over 3 per cent.

Home sales have been especially high in big cities, for example in the Helsinki region and in Oulu. In big cities the prices of older block-of-flats apartments have also risen.

In small places, however, selling a home is often difficult.

## Trips to Estonia

![Young adults celebrating in the Telliskivi area of Tallinn in summer 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_99/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6265595e06206c4a278/1577460051)

Open image viewer

Young people celebrate in the Telliskivi area in Tallinn. Photo: Aurelia Minev / Estonian public broadcaster ERR

Finns are again travelling to Estonia a lot. Last year Finns' trips to Estonia decreased.

Now young Finns are interested in Estonia. Young people look for experiences in Estonia. They want to go to concerts and restaurants, for example.

In Tallinn, young Finns are interested in the new Telliskivi area. A photography museum, Fotografiska, has opened in the area, for example.

Cheap alcohol is not important to young Finns when they travel to Estonia. Earlier, Finns often travelled to Estonia to buy cheap alcohol.

## Fireworks rockets

![Rockets](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11137493)

Open image viewer

Fireworks rockets are now sold in shops. Photo: AOP

Tuesday is New Year's Eve, and then many fireworks displays are held.

Many ordinary people also buy fireworks rockets. Rockets may be fired on New Year's Eve from 6 pm.

Authorities remind people about safety glasses. The person firing a rocket must wear safety glasses.

Fireworks rockets cause eye injuries every New Year. Last year 13 people suffered eye injuries because of rockets. That is why Finland is considering whether the sale of fireworks rockets to ordinary people should be banned.

![Fireworks at Kansalaistori in 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4252,h_2392,x_0,y_286/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6267315e09d107ca393/1703949099)

Big New Year celebrations are held in many cities.

In Helsinki, people celebrate at Kansalaistori, next to the Oodi library. There is a programme for children from 5 pm, and the evening programme starts at 10 pm.

The New Year celebration at Kansalaistori can also be watched on TV1 and on Yle Areena.
