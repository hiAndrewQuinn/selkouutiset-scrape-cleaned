# Sunday 18.2.2018 (radio)

Homes are selling quickly in big cities. Finland and Sweden may take part in a peacekeeping operation in Eastern Ukraine. A disastrous plane crash in Iran. The head of Latvia's central bank has been questioned. Stricter gun laws are demanded in Florida.

## Homes are now selling quickly

![A sales contract on a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9934650)

Open image viewer

In the capital region and in the 10 largest cities, homes are now selling well. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Homes are currently selling well in big cities.

Home prices in January were about 4.5 percent higher than in January last year.

Estate agents say that people now dare to buy homes, because the economic situation has improved.

In central Helsinki there is already a shortage of medium-sized family homes.

## Sweden and Finland ready for Eastern Ukraine

![A woman stands on the edge of a wrecked house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9992967)

Open image viewer

Fighting continues in Ukraine, and peacekeepers are to be sent there. Photo: Alexander Ermochenko / EPA

Finland and Sweden may send peacekeepers to Eastern Ukraine.

Sweden and Finland said so at a security meeting in Munich, in southern Germany.

The UN is planning a peacekeeping operation in Eastern Ukraine, because there is still fighting there.

About 20 000 soldiers could take part in the peacekeeping operation.

Many other countries also took part in the Munich meeting.

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said at the meeting that Israel is ready to strike Iran.

In Netanyahu's view, Iran's conduct has become even more brazen.

Netanyahu said that Israel's relations with other Arab countries, however, have improved.

## A disastrous plane crash in Iran

![Map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10081194)

Open image viewer

The plane crash happened in mountains, which rescue workers have difficulty reaching. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

A plane crash has happened in Iran.

The aircraft crashed into the ground in mountains in southern Iran.

There were 66 passengers on the plane. Authorities believe that all the passengers died in the accident.

The cause of the accident is not yet known.

## The head of Latvia's central bank has been questioned

![The head of the Bank of Latvia, Ilmārs Rimševičs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10081338)

Open image viewer

Ilmārs Rimševičs has led the Bank of Latvia since 2001. Photo: Valda Kalnina / EPA

The head of the Latvian central bank has been detained, and the police have questioned him.

The questioning is related to a corruption investigation.

The head's home and office were searched already on Friday.

The central bank has not ordered the head's questioning.

## Stricter gun laws are demanded in the USA

![People at a demonstration in Fort Lauderdale, Florida.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10081097)

Open image viewer

Protesters are demanding stricter gun laws in the United States after the school shootings in Florida. Photo: Joe Raedle / AFP

In the United States there have been many demonstrations demanding tighter gun laws.

The protesters say that in the United States, guns are too easy to get.

Demonstrations have been organised after a young man shot 17 people at a school in Florida last Wednesday.

The protesters criticise in particular politicians who have links to the National Rifle Association, NRA, which supports guns. They criticise President Donald Trump, among others.

The federal police, the FBI, has also been criticised. The FBI had received a warning about the man who shot at the school. However, the FBI did not take the warning seriously.
