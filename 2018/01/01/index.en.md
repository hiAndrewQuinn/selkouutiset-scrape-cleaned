# Monday 1.1.2018 (radio)

The President gave a New Year's speech. 2018 was celebrated lively. The arrival of strong beer and Lonkero in grocery shops did not cause a rush.

## President: Finland's anniversary year went well

![The President of the Republic, Sauli Niinistö, enters the hall to record the New Year's speech.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9999782)

Finnish President Sauli Niinistö has given the New Year's speech. The President always gives a speech on the first day of the year in Finland. Niinistö was pleased that Finland's anniversary year 2017 went well.

In the President's view, it was wonderful to follow how the 100-year celebration of independence was celebrated in Finland, and how the festive mood took hold of people. He said that people have felt real joy in being Finnish.

The President also recalled the Finnish Civil War, which came right after independence in 1918. In his view, Finland still has scars, that is, old wounds, from the Civil War.

The President also spoke about the economy. He was happy that Finland's economy has improved and that people have got jobs. However, Niinistö wanted to warn people about the growth of debt. He was also worried about young people being excluded.

In the New Year's speech, the President also dealt with Finland's position in the world. In his view, Finland's situation is stable.

## Finland celebrated lively

![People filming fireworks](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10001372)

Open image viewer

In Helsinki, more than 70 000 people watched the fireworks. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

The turn of the year was celebrated in many different ways in Finland. The biggest public event was held in Helsinki. More than 70 000 people took part in it. Big events were also held in other cities. In Turku, for example, 20 000 people took part.

The police had a lot of work because of the celebrations. The police received many alarms, for example about fights between drunk people. But nothing serious happened.

After the turn of the year, the emergency centres received more than 3000 calls during the night. People called the emergency centres, for example, because fireworks, that is rockets, were fired at people and cars.

This year, only a few people got eye injuries from fireworks. Two years ago, almost 30 people got eye injuries.

## Strong beer did not cause a rush in shops

![Lonkero being put on a shelf](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10001461)

Open image viewer

Strong beers and Lonkero were put on the shelves of grocery shops after the turn of the year. Photo: Tero Valtanen / Yle

The new year has brought the price of strong beer down. A law has now come into force, under which grocery shops, too, may sell strong A-beer, for example. Before this, only Alko was allowed to sell it.

On the shop shelves there was strong beer right from the morning, and also Lonkero, which is popular. Lonkero costs tens of cents less in the shop than at Alko.

Finns did not rush to the shops in the morning because of the change in the law.
