# Wednesday 9.3.2022 (radio)

The situation in Ukraine. Queues at police stations. A shortage of cleaners. Weak ice. Weather.

## Ceasefire in Ukraine

![Rescue workers push an elderly woman in a shopping trolley while evacuating civilians in Irpin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4801,h_2700,x_0,y_134/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9253446227612a9a2f7/1646748038)

A ceasefire is needed so that people can escape. Photo: Sergei Supinsky / AFP

Attempts have been made to get a ceasefire in Ukraine. Ukraine and Russia have agreed on the ceasefire.

The aim is that the ceasefire is in force today, Wednesday. No weapons are used during the ceasefire. However, Ukraine has said that Russia has continued the bombing.

The ceasefire has been agreed on routes along which ordinary people flee the war. There are still many residents in Ukrainian cities who are in great danger because of the war.

Finnish military expert, Colonel Petteri Kajamaa, estimates that the war will intensify after the ceasefire. Now Russia may be preparing an attack, for example on Ukraine's capital Kyiv.

The war in Ukraine began 2 weeks ago, when Russia attacked Ukraine.

## Queues at police stations

![A queue has formed in front of the Pasila police house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5357,h_3013,x_0,y_397/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9239196222098c2a1de/1646397839)

Open image viewer

Queue in front of the Pasila police house in Helsinki. Photo: Sakari Piippo / Yle

Many police stations are congested. There are long queues at police stations to apply for passports and identity cards.

The queues are long, for example, in Helsinki, Vantaa and Turku.

The reason for the situation is the corona time. During the corona time Finns did not need a passport, because they did not travel abroad. That is why many did not even notice if their passport had expired.

Now Finns are travelling more again and need a passport. That is why many want a new passport right now.

Police stations are trying to ease the congestion. Staff are being moved from other tasks to handle passport matters.

## Shortage of cleaners

![A cleaner changes the sheets in a hotel room.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4966,h_2793,x_0,y_256/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82790160dee3c5a2bf0/1625220583)

Open image viewer

Jobs for cleaners are now available. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

The cleaning sector needs more workers. For example, in Lapland there are about 250 open jobs in the cleaning sector.

The shortage of cleaners hurts small hotels and restaurants the most. Cleaners often go to big hotel chains through staffing agencies.

In Lapland the shortage of cleaners is already holding back the growth of tourism companies. The firms compete for cleaners, for example with pay.

## Weak ice

![A snowmobile and skiers on the sea ice.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_10,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90020561dc326589adc/1641821738)

Open image viewer

Outdoor activity on the ice may already be dangerous. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The ice is already weak. This is the warning from the Finnish Swimming Instruction and Lifesaving Association.

The association says that people should no longer go onto the ice, at least in Southern Finland. The ice on lakes and the sea may be weak in spring.

In February 3 people drowned in Finland after falling through weak ice. Now the ice is weaker still.
