# Sunday 24.10.2021 (radio)

The Prime Minister on the corona pass. Young people's worries about pensions. Congestion charges. Children's sleep problems. UN Day. The weather.

## Corona pass

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin preparing for Yle Radio Suomi's Prime Minister interview hour at Kesäranta in Helsinki on 24 October 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_376/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-871008617542883d3ab/1635074771)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that using the corona pass also next year is justified. The legislation on the corona pass expires at the turn of the year.

Marin said in the Prime Minister's interview hour on Radio Suomi that the wider use of the corona pass must be considered.

Marin said that the government is not planning a vaccine obligation.

## Pensions

![An elderly woman's hand holds a strawberry-patterned coffee cup. The corner of a newspaper is also visible on the table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12037600)

Open image viewer

Young people are worried about whether they will get a decent pension in the future. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Political youth organisations are worried about pensions.

In the organisations' view, young people are losing faith in the pension system and its sustainability.

Some organisations would like to talk about cutting, that is, reducing, employment pensions. It may be part of fixing the funding problem, the organisations say.

Finland's problem is that there are more and more pensioners all the time and fewer payers.

For example, Sweden has a model in use that cuts pensions when the money is not enough.

## Congestion charges

![Traffic congestion on Porkkalankatu in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10865538)

Open image viewer

Traffic congestion on Porkkalankatu in Helsinki. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

A citizens' initiative against congestion charges is moving on to be handled in Parliament.

The initiative has collected 50 000 support statements.

The initiative demands that the preparation of laws and regulations on congestion charges be suspended.

The government programme includes a note on possible congestion charges.

## Sleep problems

![A young person lies in bed and looks at a phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11350391)

Open image viewer

More and more children use a computer or phone late in the evening. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Sleep problems among pre-school children are common in Finland. About 20 per cent of pre-school children have some degree of sleep problems.

The Institute for Health and Welfare has studied the matter. The survey says that most sleep difficulties are mild.

The health service company Terveystalo estimates that children's sleep problems have increased.

For example, using a phone and other devices before going to bed disturbs sleep.

## UN Day

![UN logo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6458320)

Open image viewer

Today, Sunday, International United Nations Day has been celebrated.

The UN is a voluntary security and cooperation organisation of independent states.

The UN Charter came into force in 1945. The goal at that time was to promote peace, security, justice and human rights.

The day has been celebrated since 1948.

## The weather

![Hoarfrost.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10579776)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The autumn frost record was measured early on Sunday morning in Sodankylä in Lapland.

At the Vuotso observation station the temperature was -21.2 degrees.

Tomorrow, Monday, heavy rain will come, especially in the eastern part of the country and in the north. Elsewhere there will be drizzle in places. During the day the weather clears from the west.

In the southern and central parts of the country the temperature is mostly between -1 and +9 degrees, in the north between -3 and -15 degrees.
