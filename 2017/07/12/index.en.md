## Long-term illness is expensive

![A doctor measures a patient's blood pressure at a health centre in Helsinki, 26 November 2013.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8503522)

In Finland there are almost two million people of working age who have been ill for a long time. They are people with long-term illnesses.

About a third of people with long-term illnesses say that it is hard for them to work because of their illness.

A long illness costs a person a lot. It costs even much more than was previously estimated.

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9715933)

Open image viewer

A long illness brings many kinds of costs that have not been taken into account before. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

A long illness also costs the employer and society. According to new research, society has to pay almost 1,600 euros a month for every person of working age who is ill for a long time.

Health care experts say that people must get treatment quickly, so that the illness does not go on for too long.

## More licences to shoot bears

![Bear on a cabin terrace in Kuusamo, July 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9711452)

Open image viewer

Bears can sometimes come too close to people's homes. Photo: Juho Liikanen

In Finland, more licences to shoot bears have been granted. Bears can now be hunted more than last year.

The Finnish Wildlife Agency gave 170 licences to hunt bears. In addition, in Northern Finland, in the reindeer herding area, 85 bears may be hunted. The hunting season starts on 20 August.

Last year, 179 bears were caught during the hunting season in Finland. Licences to shoot bears have been given especially to areas where there are many bears.

## A huge iceberg broke off from Antarctica

![Photo of the crack in the Antarctic continent.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9391328)

Open image viewer

The crack from which the iceberg broke off. Photo: NASA / John Sonntag / EPA

A big iceberg has broken off from Antarctica. The iceberg is almost 6,000 square kilometres in size, and it is about 200 metres thick. It is one of the

biggest icebergs that has been measured.

The iceberg may get in the way of ship traffic.

Scientists have been waiting for years for an iceberg to break off from the edge of Antarctica.

## Turkey rejects the EU

![Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9718331)

Open image viewer

The President of Turkey says that the country can manage without the EU. Photo: Daniel Kopatsch / EPA

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan says that Turkey does not need EU membership.

Erdogan said in an interview with the British broadcaster BBC that Turkey can manage on its own.

Turkey and the EU started negotiations as long ago as 2005, but progress has been slow.

The slowness is due, among other things, to the fact that a lot of power has been given to President Erdogan in Turkey, and citizens' rights have been violated.

## The most popular hit on YouTube has changed

![Wiz Khalifa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9718849)

Open image viewer

Wiz Khalifa Photo: AOP

The most popular music video on YouTube has changed. Before, the most popular music video was South Korean Psy's Gangnam Style.

It was the most popular music video for five years. Now the most popular music video is See You Again by American Wiz Khalifa and Charlie Puth.

The video has already been watched more than 2.9 billion times.
