# Wednesday 19.9.2018 (radio)

Danske Bank got caught up in a money laundering scandal. Menopause has a big effect on women's well-being. Finnish forests may hide many unknown mushroom species. You can now learn about the sports Finns like in plain language.

## Danske Bank

![Thomas Borgen at a press conference in Copenhagen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10412373)

Danske Bank's chief executive Thomas Borgen. Photo: Liselotte Sabroe / EPA

Finland's third-largest bank, Danske Bank, has ended up in the middle of a serious crisis. Danske Bank's chief executive Thomas Borgen is resigning from his post because of a money laundering scandal.

Danske Bank's Estonian unit is suspected of money laundering, which means illegal money transfers. The total amount of suspicious money flows is 200 billion euros.

It is suspected that the money laundering happened between 2007 and 2015.

## Menopause

![a middle-aged woman running](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9671799)

Open image viewer

During menopause it is a good idea to increase the amount of exercise. Photo: AOP

Menopause has a bigger effect on women's well-being than was known before.

A woman's menopause can begin at around 50 years of age. Menopause begins when a woman's menstrual cycle ends and the production of the hormone oestrogen decreases.

It was already known before that menopause speeds up the weakening of the bones, or osteoporosis.

Now a new study shows that during menopause women's muscles become smaller and weaker. At the same time the amount of fat in the body increases, unless countermeasures are started, that is, the amount of exercise is increased. However, many women reduce exercise when menopause begins.

The University of Jyväskylä study included about 1000 women of menopausal age.

## Mushrooms

![A lone milk-cap mushroom in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8246682)

Open image viewer

A milk-cap mushroom Photo: Marja Roiha / Yle

The forests may have many mushroom species that have not been found at all yet. There may be as many as a thousand such unknown mushroom species, says Tea von Bonsdorff, a planner at the Finnish Museum of Natural History.

So far researchers have identified about 2000 mushroom species in Finland, all of which can be seen with the naked eye. In addition, science knows thousands of Finnish fungi that are very small or even microscopically small.

New information about mushrooms is currently being collected online with the help of mushroom hobbyists. The mushroom atlas has already received over 18,000 observations of almost 2000 species.

## Finland does sport

![Players in a swamp.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7361714)

Open image viewer

In Finland people play swamp football. Photo: Kaisu Jansson / Yle

Are you interested in sport? The Sports Museum has made online videos in plain language that present different sports. The theme of the videos is Finland does sport.

The videos tell, for example, about Finnish baseball and ice hockey, wife-carrying and swamp football. The videos also tell about the Olympic Games that were held in Helsinki in 1952.

The Sports Museum's videos can be found at tarinasoitin.fi/suomiurheilee.
