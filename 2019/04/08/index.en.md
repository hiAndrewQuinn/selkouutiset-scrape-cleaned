# Monday 8.4.2019 (radio)

Civil war threatens Libya. Parliamentary elections will be held in Israel. Finland's candidates for the EU literature prize have been chosen. Counting Saimaa ringed seal nests is difficult. Summer speed limits come into force.

## Civil war threatens Libya

![Fighters gathered at the outskirts of Tripoli, the capital of Libya, at the weekend. Commander Khalifa Haftar has called on all who are loyal to him to join the attempt to seize Tripoli. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10727224)

Fighters near Tripoli, the capital of Libya. Photo: STR / EPA

A serious civil war threatens the state of Libya in Africa. The army of the government and the rebels are fighting against each other.

The rebels have taken over a large part of Libya. Now the rebels are trying to take the country's capital, Tripoli.

At least 30 people have died in the fighting. The UN says that at least 2,800 people have had to leave their homes because of the fighting.

Western countries have hoped for a ceasefire in Libya, but neither side has followed that wish.

Libya has been unsettled since 2011. Then the dictator Muammar Gaddafi lost power and was killed.

## Israel closes border crossings

![Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu spoke to journalists in Jerusalem on Thursday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10667928)

Open image viewer

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.

Parliamentary elections will be held in Israel tomorrow, Tuesday.

Israel is closing the border crossings of the Palestinian areas for election day. The army says that the border crossings of Gaza and the West Bank will be closed at midnight. The borders will stay shut for a day.

Closing the borders makes life harder for tens of thousands of Palestinians who work in Israel.

## Finland's candidates for EU literature prize have been chosen

![Heikki Kännö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10630275)

Open image viewer

Heikki Kännö is a candidate for the European Union literature prize. Photo: Helena Kulmala

Finland's candidates for the European Union literature prize have been chosen.

The candidates are Mia Franck's novel Bombträdgården (Pommipuutarha in Finnish), Heikki Kännö's Sömnö, Piia Leino's Taivas (Sky) and Eeva Rohas's Raivo (Rage).

This year a total of 14 countries get the prize. The winner will be announced at the end of May.

## Counting Saimaa ringed seal nests may be difficult

![Warm spring weather calls the seals onto the surface of the ice. A seal mother nurses a pup in spring 2003. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8809496)

Open image viewer

The Saimaa ringed seal's breeding is believed to have gone well. Photo: Tero Sipilä / Metsähallitus

Counting Saimaa ringed seal nests is difficult this year. The ice is so weak that it may not be possible to go to the seals' nests.

This spring an attempt will be made to count the number of Saimaa ringed seal pups as accurately as possible. Metsähallitus estimates that at least 80 pups will be born. Last year a record number of pups, 86, were born.

The Saimaa ringed seal needs snow for its nest. There was a lot of snow in winter, so the winter was really good for the seal's breeding.

## Summer speed limits come into force

![Man changes a speed limit sign to show a speed limit of 100 km/h.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7169845)

Open image viewer

In summer you may drive faster than in winter. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

Summer speed limits come into force on Finnish roads this week.

In summer you may drive faster than in winter. On motorways the speed limit rises to 120 km/h, and on main roads you may drive at 100.
