# Sunday 13.8.2017 (radio)

Kiira was the worst storm of the summer. The thunderstorm caused a lot of damage. Finland was left without medals at the World Athletics Championships. Fans and YouTubers met at Tubecon. Helsinki's tram lines move to new routes.

## The storm caused a lot of damage

![A man photographs trees broken by the Kiira storm on Saturday evening in Kaisaniemi Park in Helsinki on Sunday morning, 13 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9773260)

The storm broke trees in Kaisaniemi Park in Helsinki. Photo: Teemu Salonen / Lehtikuva

Strong thunderstorms and strong gusts of wind caused damage in southern Finland on Saturday evening.

The storm broke many trees and tore off roof sheets. Trees fell onto buildings, roads and railways. Tens of thousands of people were left without electricity when trees fell onto power lines. Lightning also started fires.

The Flow Festival was being held in Suvilahti, Helsinki, when the storm struck. The festival had to be stopped during the worst of the thunderstorm.

The rescue services got about 1000 alerts. However, no one was injured during the storm.

## Lightning and gusts of wind

![Lightning above Flow](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9773307)

Open image viewer

Lightning above Suvilahti in Helsinki. Photo: Jyri Äärilä

The storm was given the name Kiira. Kiira was the worst storm of this summer.

A strong thunderstorm front came to Finland from the south-west and moved fast along the south coast towards the east.

During the storm there were about 6500 lightning strikes. There was the most lightning in eastern Uusimaa and Kymenlaakso.

The strongest gust of wind, over 32 metres per second, was measured at the Helsinki lighthouse.

## The London World Championships end

![Tero Pitkämäki in the picture](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9772166)

Open image viewer

Javelin thrower Tero Pitkämäki was the best Finnish athlete at the London World Championships. Photo: EPA

Finnish athletes did not get a single medal at the World Athletics Championships in London.

The best Finn was javelin thrower Tero Pitkämäki. Pitkämäki was 5th in the men's javelin competition. He threw the javelin almost 87 metres.

The javelin competition was won by Germany's Johannes Vetter. He threw almost 90 metres.

The World Athletics Championships end on Sunday evening.

## YouTubers and fans met at Tubecon

![When Mandimai Sundberg appeared in the corridor of the Helsinki arena, she was surrounded by fans in a moment.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9772897)

Open image viewer

Fans wanted an autograph from YouTuber Mandimai Sundberg.  Photo: Tuomo Björksten/Yle

YouTubers have met their audience at the weekend at the Tubecon event in Helsinki.

Tubecon was now held for the 4th time. It is the biggest event of the YouTube community in the Nordic countries.

There are already over half a million YouTubers in Finland. YouTubers make videos for the YouTube video service. The videos can have a theme, such as fashion or games. They can also be video diaries of the YouTuber's own life.

For many young people, YouTubers are big stars.

At Tubecon, the best YouTubers of the year were given awards. The audience also chose its own favourite.

## Helsinki's trams move to new routes

![Trams 3 and 2 wait for passengers at the Eläintarha stop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7883001)

Open image viewer

There will be changes to almost all tram lines. Photo: Yle

Helsinki's tram lines change tomorrow, Monday. If you use the tram a lot, it is worth checking the new routes and timetables of the lines on the HSL website.

There will be changes to lines 1, 2, 3, 7 and 9 - that is, almost all lines.

The aim of the change is to make traffic clearer and smoother.
