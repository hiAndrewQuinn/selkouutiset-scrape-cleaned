# Wednesday 19.10.2022 (radio)

President Niinistö visits Iceland. A fence is being built on the eastern border. Minister Kiuru is speeding up corona vaccinations. Winter speeds are introduced on roads. The weather.

## Niinistö in Iceland

Icelandic President Guðni Thorlacius Jóhannesson and Finnish President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio have begun their visit to Iceland. The visit celebrates the diplomatic relations between Finland and Iceland, which were established 75 years ago.

Niinistö has talked with the Icelandic President about, for example, Finland's NATO membership and deepening Nordic cooperation. Niinistö also hopes for cooperation to prevent climate change.

Tomorrow President Niinistö and Mrs Haukio will get to know Iceland's glaciers, which have suffered badly from global warming

## A fence on the eastern border

![Illustration of the border fence.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1000,h_562,x_0,y_203/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1021732634d2b5088d74/1666099800)

Open image viewer

Illustration of the fence on the eastern border. Photo: Border Guard

A fence is being built on the border between Finland and Russia.

The parties in Parliament have discussed building the fence, and all parties support it.

The fence costs hundreds of millions of euros. Its construction will start as soon as possible, and it will take years. The fence will be built first in South-Eastern Finland and later at other border crossing points too.

The fence will be a few metres high, and barbed wire will be on top of it. The fence will be monitored technically, for example with cameras.

## Corona vaccinations

![Family and basic services minister Krista Kiuru in an interview on Aamu-tv.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1022421634f8ff764f67/1666158762)

Open image viewer

Minister Krista Kiuru visited Yle's morning programme Photo: Yle

Family and basic services minister Krista Kiuru wants to speed up corona vaccinations for older people.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, has recommended that corona vaccinations be given at the same time as flu vaccinations in November and December. Minister Kiuru thinks the vaccinations can be given more quickly, so that, for example, care homes can be protected.

THL recommends corona booster vaccines for people over 65 years old and younger people who belong to risk groups.

## Winter speeds on roads

![A speed limit sign being changed to the winter speed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8452599)

Open image viewer

Speed limits on roads will mostly drop to 80 kilometres per hour. Photo: Yle

Winter speed limits on roads will come into force from tomorrow, says the Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency.

The changes start in Lapland and the rest of Finland will follow next week. Across the country the new speed limits will be in force by next week's Friday at the latest.

On roads the speed limits are mostly being lowered to 80 kilometres per hour.. On motorways the speed limits drop to 100 kilometres per hour.
