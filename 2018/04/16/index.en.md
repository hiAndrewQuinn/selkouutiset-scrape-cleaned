# Monday 16.4.2018 (radio)

The European Union thinks that the strikes on Syria were right. Help is needed in Eastern Ukraine. Chinese-language day care teaching will start next autumn in Lauttasaari, Helsinki. Rain reduced street dust in Southern Finland. Lauri Markkanen will play basketball on the Finnish team next summer.

## The EU supports the Syria strikes

![Timo Soini](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10160160)

Open image viewer

Finland's Foreign Minister Timo Soini also took part in the meeting of EU foreign ministers. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The European Union thinks that the strikes on Syria by the USA, France and Britain were right.

The strikes were needed so that the use of chemical weapons in Syria stops. This is what the foreign ministers of the EU countries say. The foreign ministers had a meeting in Luxembourg on Monday.

The USA, France and Britain fired missiles at Syria on Saturday. They wanted to destroy places that Syria needs for making chemical weapons.

The countries decided on the strikes after people died from a dangerous gas in the Syrian town of Douma. The EU says that it was a chemical weapon and that the Syrian government was guilty.

The Syrian government and the rebels have been at war for many years already.

## The UN hopes for help for Eastern Ukraine

![Yekaterina is shocked when she remembers the shooting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10161342)

The lives of many elderly people are difficult in the crisis area of Eastern Ukraine. Photo: Antti Kuronen / Yle

Help is needed in Eastern Ukraine.

The UN, or the United Nations, says that about 3.5 million people need help. Among them are over a million elderly people.

The war in Eastern Ukraine has gone on for exactly 4 years. In the war the Ukrainian government and the rebels of Eastern Ukraine are fighting.

## Chinese-language day care teaching starts

![Day care children playing with Duplo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10136338)

Open image viewer

Soon in Helsinki you can also get day care teaching in Chinese. Photo: Emilia Malin / Yle

Chinese-language day care teaching will start next autumn in Lauttasaari, Helsinki.

The private day care centre works in English, but it plans to organise clubs in Finnish and Chinese too. Teaching in Chinese is needed because many Chinese families live in Helsinki.

The day care centre plans to teach in Mandarin Chinese. Mandarin Chinese is the biggest language in the world. There are more speakers of Mandarin Chinese than of any other language.

## Less street dust for a while

![A street sweeper washes sand off the road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9546338)

Open image viewer

The rain helped the street sweepers on Monday. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Rain has reduced street dust in Southern Finland. It rained on Monday, and on Tuesday it may also rain a little.

At the end of the week it will be drier and warmer. The temperature may rise to nearly 20 degrees. But dry and warm weather means that there will again be more street dust in the air at the end of the week.

## Markkanen plays on the Finnish team

![Lauri Markkanen signing an autograph](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10161420)

Finally, sport:

Lauri Markkanen will play basketball on the Finnish team next summer. Markkanen will take part when Finland plays World Cup qualifying games against the Czech Republic and Iceland.

Lauri Markkanen plays in the North American basketball league. Markkanen is a player for the Chicago Bulls team. 20-year-old Markkanen has done well in America.

Now Markkanen is in Finland and is taking a break from training.
