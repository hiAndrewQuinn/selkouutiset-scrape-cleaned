# Monday 1.2.2021 (radio)

Police in Russia have arrested demonstrators. The army has taken power in Myanmar. On Monday Finland reported 6 new corona deaths. Helsinki has started mass vaccinations of the elderly. There is frosty weather this week.

## Demonstrations in Russia

![Demonstrators in Saint Petersburg at a demonstration supporting the opposition leader Aleksei Navalnyi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11765340)

Police and demonstrators face to face in the city of Saint Petersburg in Russia. Photo: Anatoly Maltsev / EPA

In Russia the police have arrested thousands of people. On Sunday the police arrested more than 5,000 demonstrators. There were big demonstrations in different parts of Russia.

The demonstrators want to support the opposition leader Aleksei Navalnyi. They are angry that the police arrested Navalnyi 2 weeks ago.

Navalnyi was arrested when he returned from Germany to Russia. He had been treated in Germany because of poisoning.

Navalnyi's supporters say that an attempt was made to kill Navalnyi with poison. Navalnyi is a critic of President Vladimir Putin.

## Coup in Myanmar

![Map showing where Myanmar is located in Asia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11765873)

Open image viewer

Photo: Harri Vähäkangas / Yle

The army has taken power in the Asian country of Myanmar. The army has also arrested Myanmar's leader Aung San Suu Kyi.

The army intends to govern Myanmar's affairs for a year. The army promises to hold elections in a year. After the elections, power will pass to the winner of the elections, the army says.

Myanmar held elections last November, but in the army's view the elections were dishonest. The winner of the elections was Aung San Suu Kyi's party.

![Sonja Nykänen examines samples at Vita Laboratories.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2853,h_1605,x_0,y_464/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-765371600ada97658fc/1611324150)

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare said on Monday that there are 6 new corona deaths in Finland. A total of 677 people have died of corona in Finland.

There are 244 new infections. More than 45,000 corona infections have been found in Finland in total.

## Corona vaccinations for the elderly

![Eira-Mailis Harmanen was vaccinated against corona in Helsinki on 1 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_133/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7682596017dcdd3326d/1612176868)

Helsinki has started mass vaccinations of the elderly. The over-85s come first.

Helsinki has about 14,000 residents over 85. Many of them have already booked a vaccination appointment. An appointment can be booked online, for example.

Mass vaccinations of the elderly against corona start at different times in different municipalities. In many municipalities vaccinations have already started. In Vantaa, for example, vaccinations of over-80s began last week.

## Frost across the whole country

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11766996)

Open image viewer

On Wednesday it will snow in Eastern and Central Finland. Photo: Yle

On Tuesday in the southern part of the country it is cloudy and a little snow falls. Elsewhere the weather is dry and the cloudiness varies.

In the south on Tuesday there is light frost, and in the west the frost is about 10 degrees. In the east and north there are 12 to 25 degrees of frost.

On Wednesday it will still snow in the eastern and central parts of the country. The temperature is between 10 and 20 degrees of frost across almost the whole country.
