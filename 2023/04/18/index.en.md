# Radio Clear News Tuesday 18.4.2023

Parties' answers to Orpo. Errors at the Immigration Service. Street dust. Light phenomenon in Alaska. The weather.

## Parties' answers to Orpo

![Speaker Petteri Orpo in the Parliament café in Helsinki on 18 April 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_52/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1100754643e8ecba71e9/1681821421)

National Coalition chairman and government informer Petteri Orpo. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The parties have given their answers to the questions of the National Coalition chairman.

National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo put questions to the parties last week. They relate to matters that the National Coalition considers important.

On the basis of the answers, Orpo decides which parties he will try to put together a government with. The parties have answered questions, for example, on Finland's economy and savings.

Orpo may try to put together the government, because the National Coalition is now the largest party in Parliament. If Orpo succeeds in putting together a government, he will become the new Prime Minister.

Negotiations on a new government will take weeks.

## Immigration Service

![Immigration Service sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3264,h_1835,x_0,y_168/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-6255357dfd37717670/1474286511)

The Finnish Immigration Service, Migri, has made errors. The errors were found in the Immigration Service's own inspection.

The Immigration Service's legal services checked decisions on applications for extension of residence permits. The decisions were negative decisions from 2022.

About 10 percent of the decisions contained errors. The errors related, for example, to care and to hearing the client.

## Street dust

![Traffic raises dust in central Turku around the Paavo Nurmi statue.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1999,h_1125,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-109678664304a07c0509/1710251815)

Open image viewer

There is now a lot of street dust in the air. Photo: Vesa-Matti Ruuska / Yle

There is now a lot of street dust in the air. Streets no longer have snow, and the winter sand rises into the air.

The weather has been dry this spring. That is why air quality is now really poor.

Spring street cleaning has started in cities, so that air quality improves.

Lung doctor Maija Räsänen says that cyclists should now use quiet streets, and outdoor walkers should go to nature, away from the dust of traffic.

In addition, it is good for outdoor walkers to avoid streets at times when there is a lot of traffic.

## Light phenomenon

![Spiral-shaped light phenomenon in the dark sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_210/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1100441643e3cc7dae4a/1681800620)

Open image viewer

An odd light phenomenon was seen in Alaska. Photo: Christopher Hayden / AP

There has been an unusual light phenomenon in Alaska, in North America.

A blue spiral appeared in the Alaskan sky last Saturday. The light phenomenon appeared in the middle of the northern lights.

The spiral was formed when extra fuel was released from a rocket. At high altitude the fuel froze, and it became visible when sunlight hit it.

Fine photographs were also taken of the spiral.

On Wednesday the sun shines in southern and central Finland. In the north it is cloudy, and a little rain may also fall there.

The temperature in the southern and central parts of the country is 10–15 degrees. In the north it is about +10 degrees.

The sunny weather continues on Thursday in the south. In the north it is cloudier.
