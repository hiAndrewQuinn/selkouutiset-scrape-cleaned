## Rules for passengers

![A corona test sampling point opened at Helsinki-Vantaa airport on Monday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11478519)

Finland is tightening the corona rules for passengers. Finland can order a corona test and quarantine for a passenger who comes from a risk country.

A risk country means a country where there are more corona cases than in Finland. In a risk country, a passenger can easily catch the coronavirus.

The Balkan countries, for example, are risk countries. For example, last Saturday a flight came to Finland from North Macedonia, and many of the passengers on it had the coronavirus.

Now Finland wants to stop the coronavirus from spreading from risk countries to Finland. That is why quarantine will become compulsory for passengers who come from risk countries. They must stay at home for 2 weeks after the trip.

The Finnish government does not think it is good that Finns are now making trips to risk countries.

There are also safe countries where there are not many corona infections. These countries include, for example, Estonia, Norway, Germany and Italy. Passengers do not need to go into quarantine when they come to Finland from these countries.

## Corona infections

The coronavirus spreads quickly. There are now more than 20 million corona infections in the world. Most corona infections are in the USA, where there are more than 5 million corona infections.

In Finland there are about 7,600 corona infections. On Tuesday, 22 new corona infections were reported in Finland.

Russia has developed the corona vaccine very quickly. Experts are worried about whether the corona vaccine has been tested well enough.

Schools are starting in Finland. The autumn term already started today, on Tuesday, for example in the schools of Tampere.

For example, in Turku and Oulu the schools start on Wednesday. In the Helsinki area the schools start on Thursday.

Some schools start the autumn term only next week. School work starts next week, for example in the Swedish-speaking schools of Helsinki.
