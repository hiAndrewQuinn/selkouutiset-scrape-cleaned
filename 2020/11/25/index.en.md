## The coronavirus situation

![353 new coronavirus infections have been reported in Finland, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare (THL) said on Tuesday. The coronavirus test sampling point at the HUS hospital area in Meilahti, Helsinki, on 24 November 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11664345)

4 new coronavirus patient deaths have been reported in Finland.

363 new coronavirus infections have been reported. Most new coronavirus infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa area, where there are about 230.

The number of patients in hospital has grown. There are now 134 coronavirus patients in hospitals, which is 14 more than on Monday.

The Finnish government has held a meeting about the coronavirus situation. The government discussed whether new restrictions are needed.

## Parcel transport is growing

![Post parcel locker at the ACB station in Pitkälahti, Kuopio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3861,h_2172,x_0,y_404/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7324315f8ff09035d48/1603269640)

Open image viewer

Parcels can be sent and collected from parcel lockers. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

A very large number of parcels will soon be carried in Finland. Online shops now have a big sale week, Black Friday.

Many Finns order goods or clothes from online shops. Many also buy Christmas presents from online shops.

The Post and the transport companies say that there may be queues at the parcel lockers. New parcel lockers have been brought to many places, for example to shopping centres, because the number of parcels is growing.

## The Viking Amorella's propeller was faulty

![M/S Amorella being moved off the island of Järsö on 23 September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6108,h_3436,x_0,y_153/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7221485f6af4aa07135/1600845012)

Open image viewer

The Viking Amorella ran aground in the Åland archipelago. Photo: Kimmo Gustafsson / Yle

The Accident Investigation Board has published a report on the grounding of the Viking Amorella ship. The Viking Amorella ran aground in Åland in September. The bottom of the ship hit a rock on the sea floor.

The Accident Investigation Board says that the Viking Amorella ran aground because a fault appeared in the ship's propeller. The propeller steered the ship wrongly, and the ship went off the fairway.

## Tallink Silja is cutting staff

![The passenger ship Silja Serenade of Tallink Silja](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_328/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5907975d7a3ef175f9e/1568292698)

Open image viewer

Silja Serenade normally sails between Helsinki and Stockholm. Now there is no traffic. Photo: Peter Sjöholm/Yle

The ship company Tallink Silja is cutting staff. Tallink Silja has been in co-operation negotiations, because ship travel has almost stopped.

The jobs of about 100 people at Tallink Silja will end. In addition, almost all Tallink Silja employees are temporarily laid off.

## Polar night has begun in the north

![A view from Utsjoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11165199)

Open image viewer

Church rooms in Utsjoki on a winter evening. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

The polar night has begun in northern Finland. Polar night means that the sun stays below the horizon. In Utsjoki, for example, the sun will next rise on 16 January.

However, in northern Finland it is not completely dark during the polar night. There is a little light there, so it is dusk.
