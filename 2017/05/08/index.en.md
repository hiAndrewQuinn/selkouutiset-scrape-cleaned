# Monday 8.5.2017 (radio)

Power changes hands in France on Sunday. Emmanuel Macron's election victory is good news for Finland too. Finnish fashion interests the world. People want to strengthen the Arctic fox population in the north.

## Power changes hands in France on Sunday

![Emmanuel Macron celebrated his election victory in front of the Louvre in Paris on 7 May 2017. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9600344)

Emmanuel Macron takes the presidential oath of office on Sunday. Photo: Eric Feferberg / AFP

France is preparing for the change of power. France's new president Emmanuel Macron takes the oath of office on Sunday. The ceremony is held at the president's official residence in Paris.

Macron got about 66 percent of the votes in Sunday's presidential election. The opposing candidate, Marine Le Pen of the far right, got about 34 percent of the votes.

The turnout was about 75 percent. That is low for France. The last time turnout in a French presidential election was this bad was about 50 years ago.

## EU leaders are happy about Macron's election victory

![Supporters of Emmanuel Macron, who won the French presidential election](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9599783)

EU leaders say that Emmanuel Macron's election victory is good news for all of Europe.  Photo: Yoan Valat / EPA

Emmanuel Macron has received many congratulations from different parts of the world. EU leaders especially are happy about Macron's victory, because Macron has a positive attitude to the EU.

German Chancellor Angela Merkel said that Macron's victory gives hope not only to the French but also to Germans and other Europeans

Finland's president Sauli Niinistö and prime minister Juha Sipilä also say that Macron's victory is good news for Europe and for Finland. Sipilä says that France is an important partner for Finland in the EU. Niinistö and Sipilä have both sent Macron letters of congratulation.

US President Donald Trump congratulated Macron on Twitter.

## Finnish fashion interests the world

![clothes on hangers](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9598232)

Open image viewer

Bright colours are liked especially in Asian markets. Photo: Yle

Finnish clothes are now exported abroad more than before.  Last year 15 percent more clothes were exported than the year before.

Most clothes are exported to Sweden, Germany and Russia.

The growth in clothing exports is because the Scandinavian style is now in fashion around the world. For example, Finnish outdoor clothes are popular. The bright colours of Finnish clothes also interest people, especially in Asian markets.

## People want to save the Arctic fox population in the north

![The naali, or Arctic fox.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8912333)

Open image viewer

About 200 Arctic foxes live on the fells of Finland, Sweden and Norway. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finland, Sweden and Norway are working together to try to save the Arctic fox population in the north. The Arctic fox is a small predator that lives in the fell areas in the north.

Perhaps about 200 Arctic foxes live on the fells. Most of them are in Sweden and Norway. In Finland there are fewer than 10 Arctic foxes, so the species is critically endangered here.

People try to help the Arctic foxes, for example by feeding them. The Arctic foxes are given dog food as extra food.

The Arctic fox's worst enemy in nature is the red fox. That is why hunting red foxes also strengthens the Arctic fox population.
