# Thursday 16.3.2017 (radio)

The Netherlands has held parliamentary elections. Prime Minister Juha Sipilä has congratulated the Dutch Prime Minister on the election victory. There has been a bad traffic accident at the eastern border. Finnair is cancelling a lot of flights. The number of books at home affects children's reading skills.

## In the Netherlands the liberals won the election

![Mark Rutte on election night.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9513337)

Open image viewer

Mark Rutte, leader of the right-wing liberal VVD party, which won the Dutch election, was happy about the election result. Photo: Remko De Waal / EPA

Europe's big parties are satisfied with the result of the Dutch parliamentary election.

Dutch Prime Minister Mark Rutte's liberal party is still the biggest party. But it lost 8 seats.

The right-wing populist Party for Freedom got fewer votes than it expected. But it got 5 more seats. The party led by Geert Wilders opposes, for example, immigration and Islam.

The Dutch election has interested the rest of Europe. Many other countries will soon have elections, and right-wing populists have a lot of support. For example, Germany and France will soon have elections.

## Finnish politicians are satisfied with the Dutch election result

![Prime Minister Juha Sipilä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8721911)

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä Photo: Yle

Finnish Prime Minister Juha Sipilä has congratulated the Dutch Prime Minister on Twitter.

Sipilä said that responsibility won in the Dutch election. Sipilä said that the Netherlands is an important partner for Finland in the European Union.

The leaders of the National Coalition Party, the Greens and the Swedish People's Party have also praised the Dutch election result on Twitter.

## 3 died in a bus accident near Nuijamaa

![The crashed bus on its side on Kanavatie in Russia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9513579)

The accident site on Kanavatie near the Nuijamaa border crossing. Photo: Kalle Schönberg/Yle

3 people have died and many have been injured near Finland's eastern border. The accident happened in Russia 12 kilometres from the Nuijamaa border crossing.

In the accident a bus overturned into a ditch. The bus was coming to Finland from St Petersburg.

## Finnair has to cancel more flights

![A Finnair plane.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5299273)

Open image viewer

Finnair has to cancel flights again. Photo: Yle

The aviation labour dispute has got worse. The industrial action continues on Friday. The work stoppage starts at 15 and lasts 4 hours. Because of it Finnair is cancelling almost a hundred flights. The cancellations affect about 6,500 passengers.

More work stoppages are coming next week too.

The airline Finnair says that the aviation work stoppage will cause the company losses of millions of euros.

## Children's books are important

![Open children's books](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7761674)

Open image viewer

There is a link between the number of children's books and children's reading. Photo: Soila Ojanen / Yle

The number of books at home affects children's reading skills.

This has been studied at the University of Eastern Finland. A home's language environment and a large number of children's books help with reading. The study shows that families with a highly educated father in particular had a lot of children's books.

Many studies show that highly educated parents spend a lot of time with their children. They talk, play and read.

The University of Eastern Finland study showed no difference between the children's sexes. But girls read and wrote more than boys.
