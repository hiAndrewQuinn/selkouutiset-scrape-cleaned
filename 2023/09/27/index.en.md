## Economy

Finns believe less than before that the state's finances will develop well. 70 percent of people estimate that Finland's economy is doing worse now than a year ago. Only 5 percent of Finns estimate that Finland's economy is better than a year earlier.

People think that unemployment will increase. 60 percent of people believe that unemployment will increase during the year.

People also think that now is a bad time to buy expensive goods. 45 percent of people intend to spend less money on goods than before.

## Iraq accident

![Rescue work after a fire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1968,x_0,y_363/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11774656513b8627c73e/1695791403)

Rescue work after the fire in Iraq. Photo: AFP

A serious accident has happened in Iraq at a wedding party.

At least 100 people died when a fire broke out at a wedding venue on Tuesday evening in the city of Bakhdida in Northern Iraq. An authority suspects that the fire started from fireworks. Hundreds of guests took part in the wedding party.

At least 150 people were injured in the fire.

Iraq has declared a 3-day period of mourning in the country. The 4 owners of the venue are wanted by the police.

## Interpellation

The opposition parties Centre and Liike Nyt are making an interpellation to the government. The interpellation concerns health services and services for the elderly.

The largest opposition party, SDP, says that it is ready to negotiate about a shared interpellation of the whole opposition.

An interpellation is the opposition's means of finding out whether Parliament supports the government's policy.

## Helsinki-Vantaa airport

![Baggage belts at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_66/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11779016514217a21a70/1695818528)

Open image viewer

The renovated premises of Helsinki-Vantaa airport were taken into use in full on 27 September 2023. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

A big renovation was carried out at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. The renovation is now finished. The airport renovation cost one billion euros and the work lasted 10 years.

During the renovation the airport was changed so that now there is only 1 terminal. In addition, passengers have more space than before.

After the renovation, about 30 million passengers a year can now fly through Helsinki-Vantaa airport. This is double the number of people compared with before.
