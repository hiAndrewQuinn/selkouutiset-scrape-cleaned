# Saturday 8.2.2020 (radio)

A strike begins in the technology industry on Monday. Police Commissioner Seppo Kolehmainen is worried about hate speech. There has been a serious mass shooting in Thailand. The coronavirus is spreading faster than before.

A strike begins in the technology industry on Monday.

The employees' Pro Trade Union has rejected the settlement proposal for the labour dispute. In the Pro Trade Union's view, the settlement proposal was too poor for employees. The employers' organisation Technology Industries of Finland accepted the settlement proposal.

The technology industry strike concerns 90 technology companies and lasts 2 weeks. The technology industry includes, for example, the electronics industry and the electrical industry.

## The Police Commissioner is worried about hate speech

![Seppo Kolehmainen on Yle's Ykkösaamu on 8 February 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11199322)

Seppo Kolehmainen on Yle's Ykkösaamu on 8 February 2020. Photo: Yle

Police Commissioner Seppo Kolehmainen is worried about how hate speech can affect police officers.

About one in three police officers says that they have been targeted. Targeting means, for example, that a person is sent a lot of hate speech on social media. Many police officers may consider quitting police work because of targeting.

Hate speech can also affect prosecutors and judges. Police Commissioner Seppo Kolehmainen says that sometimes they may not dare to do their work because of hate speech.

## A soldier shot several people in Thailand

![Map of Thailand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11199495)

Open image viewer

The shooting happened in the city of Nakhon Ratchasima. Photo: Hilppa Hyrkäs / Yle

In Thailand a gunman has killed several people. At least 17 people have died and many have been injured.

The police say that the gunman is a soldier. He shot people in the city of Nakhon Ratchasima, which is also known as Korat. The soldier shot people in a temple and in a shopping centre, among other places.

The reason for the shooting is not known.

## The coronavirus is spreading faster than before

![Chinese tourists at Rovaniemi airport in February 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11199566)

Open image viewer

Chinese tourists at Rovaniemi airport in February 2020. Photo: Kaisa Siren / EPA

The coronavirus is spreading faster than before.

In one day 86 people died from the disease. This is a record. In total 722 people have died from the coronavirus. All of the dead have been Chinese.

The coronavirus has spread to 27 countries.

## A record crowd at a tennis match

![tennis South Africa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11199261)

Open image viewer

Over 50,000 spectators watched the exhibition match in Cape Town. Photo: EPA

The tennis stars Roger Federer and Rafael Nadal have played in front of a record-breaking crowd. The exhibition match was played in Cape Town in South Africa. The match had almost 52,000 spectators, which is an all-time record for tennis.

The match was played in a stadium that was built for the 2010 football World Cup. Tennis is usually played in much smaller arenas.
