# Monday 19.3. 2018 (radio)

Film director Aku Louhimies is accused of treating actors badly. Five cities are starting a language trial. Many people get free tick vaccinations. Today is Equality Day.

## A Finnish director is accused of bad treatment

![Filming of the film The Unknown Soldier at Suomenlinna. In the picture, director Aku Louhimies.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10115484)

Director Aku Louhimies Photo: Pasi Murto / AOP

Film director Aku Louhimies is accused of treating actors badly.

Some well-known actors say that Aku Louhimies has treated actors badly. The actors say that Louhimies has forced them to do things that they did not want to do. Many actresses are of this opinion.

Aku Louhimies says that he does not accept any kind of discrimination or bullying. He is sorry if actors have felt bad.

Aku Louhimies directs films. He has directed, for example, the film The Unknown Soldier. More than a million viewers have already seen the film.

## Five cities in a language trial

![A writing hand on top of a Swedish book.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5477337)

Open image viewer

Taking part in the trial is voluntary. Photo: YLE

A trial in language studies is starting in Finland.

Five cities are starting a trial in schools in which pupils do not have to study Swedish. Instead, the pupil must choose some other foreign language. About 450 pupils who are going into sixth grade are in the language trial. They are 12 years old.

The cities in the trial are Jämsä, Pieksämäki, Rovaniemi, Savonlinna and Ylöjärvi.

Swedish is the second official language in Finland, and studying it has been compulsory.

## Free tick vaccinations

![A tick walks in the crook of an elbow in June 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9723221)

Open image viewer

There are ticks in almost all of Finland. Photo: Veikka Mäki

Many people can get a free tick vaccination.

You do not have to pay for the vaccine if you live or stay in an area where there are a lot of ticks.

You can get a free tick vaccination if you stay in certain places in Lappeenranta, Kemi, Raahe and Kotka. Earlier, free vaccination has been given in Åland, Parainen or Simo.

Ticks can spread diseases, such as tick-borne encephalitis and Lyme disease.

Ticks like bushes and long grass. The tick has spread to almost all of Finland.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare announces every year the risk areas where free vaccinations can be had.

## Minna Canth and equality

![Kanttila](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7810420)

Open image viewer

Minna Canth's home is in Kuopio. Photo: Pertti Huotari / Yle

Today is the day of Minna Canth and equality.

Minna Canth lived in the 1800s. She was a Finnish writer and influencer.

Minna Canth wrote a lot about women's lives. She wanted to improve, for example, girls' education, and she was an important person in developing Finnish-language education.

Minna Canth is the first Finnish woman to have her own flag day.
