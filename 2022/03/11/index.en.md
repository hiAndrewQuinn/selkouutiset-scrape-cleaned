## Phone diplomacy

![Sauli Niinistö speaks to the media at the Presidential Palace on Thursday, 10 March 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_142/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9264046229e5e76bb0a/1647765032)

President Sauli Niinistö spoke on the phone on Friday. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

President Sauli Niinistö has talked on the phone today with the presidents of Russia and Ukraine.

First Niinistö spoke with Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. The President's Office says that the presidents discussed, for example, the military situation in Ukraine and Finland's help and support for Ukraine.

Later in the day President Niinistö talked with Russia's President Vladimir Putin. The President's Office says that the call lasted an hour.

During the call President Niinistö said that a ceasefire must be achieved in Ukraine right away, so that civilians can be evacuated safely. Niinistö also spoke, for example, about the safety of Ukraine's nuclear power plants.

## Ukraine and EU membership

![EU leaders at a summit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_91/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-926647622ac4290a261/1647004013)

Open image viewer

EU leaders are gathering in France. Photo: Ludovic Marin / AFP

Ukraine's fast-track EU membership will not happen.

Ukraine submitted its membership application last week and hoped that the EU would handle it quickly. However, no unanimous support for this was found among the EU leaders.

Ukraine's application now proceeds in the usual way to the EU Commission, which will give its opinion on it.

EU leaders also ask the EU Commission for its opinion on the membership applications of Moldova and Georgia.

## Military gear sells well

![A backpack with Molle loops, a military rucksack and a sleeping bag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4316,h_2422,x_0,y_270/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-921702621ce0e87a15e/1646060239)

Open image viewer

Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Sales of military and hiking gear have grown in Finland. Shops in the sector say that some purchases may be made for Ukraine, and some are people equipping themselves.

For example, Varusteleka says that sales have grown by tens of percent. Bulletproof vests and helmets have already sold out. First-aid supplies and hiking food have also sold well, for example.

Partioaitta has also sold a record number of camping stoves, sleeping bags and water purification equipment, for example.

Aid organisations and Yle are organising a benefit concert for Ukraine tomorrow. The proceeds of the Help for Ukraine concert will help people who are suffering from Russia's military actions in Ukraine.

Performers at the concert include, for example, Haloo Helsinki!, Katri Helena, Juha Tapio and Anna Puu.

The concert starts at 6.15 p.m. It can be watched on TV1 or on Yle Areena, or listened to on Radio Suomi. The benefit concert will also be shown in Ukraine.
