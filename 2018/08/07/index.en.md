# Tuesday 7.8.2018 (radio)

Unemployment. A big weapons seizure. A scrapping bonus for cars. The great egret had chicks. The weather gets warmer.

## Work for the long-term unemployed

![A person going to the employment office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10327125)

Open image viewer

Dealing with unemployment brings a lot of costs to the state. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

There are many people in Finland who have been unemployed for a long time.

Finance Minister Petteri Orpo says that now the different ministries must look for ways for the long-term unemployed to get work. The Ministry of Finance is making Finland's budget for next year, that is, calculating income and costs.

Dealing with unemployment costs the state a lot.

Costs must be reduced, says Finance Minister Petteri Orpo.

## The police seized a lot of weapons

![A police car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10310777)

Open image viewer

The police seized more than 100 weapons from a man's home. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The police have seized a large number of weapons in Eastern Finland.

A man living in Kouvola had more than 100 weapons at home. The man had many rifles, shotguns, pistols and revolvers.

The man did not have a permit to keep the weapons.

The police found the weapons in June.

## The state pays a scrapping bonus

![Scrapped cars in a pile at Kuusakoski's scrap metal processing plant in Vantaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9997561)

Open image viewer

Old cars end up at the car scrapyard. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

A car owner still has time to get a scrapping bonus for an old car.

The Finnish state pays a car owner a scrapping bonus if the owner takes a car more than 10 years old to the scrapyard and buys a new low-emission car instead.

The scrapping bonus is either 1,500 euros or 2,500 euros. You can still get the scrapping bonus during August.

5,800 car owners have already got the scrapping bonus.

## The great egret had chicks in Finland

![A great egret in the water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10340706)

Open image viewer

The great egret nested successfully in Finland for the first time. Photo: AOP

Rare bird chicks have been born in Finland.

Finland's first great egret chicks hatched in June in Southern Finland.

Now 4 chicks have left the nest. There have been great egrets in Finland for many summers, but their nesting has not succeeded.

This summer the great egrets nested in the same place as grey herons. Great egrets are white.
