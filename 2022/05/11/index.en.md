# Wednesday 11.5.2022 (radio)

Britain's Prime Minister. Nato application. Disputes in the municipal sector. The weather.

## Prime Minister Johnson visits

![Boris Johnson stepping out of an aircraft.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4239,h_2384,x_0,y_67/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-953433627bafeca475c/1652273184)

Britain's Prime Minister Boris Johnson is visiting Finland on Wednesday evening.

Johnson first visited Sweden. The visits are linked to security in Europe.

In Finland Johnson will meet President Sauli Niinistö. In Sweden he met Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson.

Johnson will discuss with the leaders of Finland and Sweden the war that Russia has started in Ukraine.

The discussions also talk about the military alliance NATO. Because of Russia's war policy, Finland and Sweden are considering NATO membership.

If the countries submit a membership application to NATO, Russia may take counter-measures. Russia does not want NATO to take in new member countries.

Prime Minister Johnson promises that Britain will give support and security to Finland and Sweden.

## Finland's NATO plans

![Republic's President Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4470,h_2514,x_0,y_6/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-940067625030127ee61/1649422478)

More information about Finland's NATO plans will be available in the coming days.

President Sauli Niinistö says on Thursday whether he supports Finland's NATO application.

The intention is that the opinion of the President and of Prime Minister Sanna Marin on the NATO application will be told at the same time. The information is expected on Thursday at 10 o'clock.

The biggest government party, the SDP, says on Saturday what it thinks of Finland's NATO application.

Finland may decide to apply for membership of the military alliance NATO as early as next week.

## Disputes in the municipal sector

![Chair of Tehy Millariikka Rytkönen and chair of SuPer Silja Paavola at a demonstration in Senate Square](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12436076)

Open image viewer

Nurses demand more pay. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The disputes in the municipal sector continue. Nurses do not accept the municipal sector contract proposal, which was made on Tuesday.

There has been a dispute about wages in the municipal sector. The nurses' organisations say that the proposal raises wages too little.

A proposal was made to the municipal sector workers, which includes a 5-year pay programme. The aim was that wages in the municipal sector rise a total of about 5 per cent more than in other sectors.

The nurses think that this is too little. That is why the nurses do not accept the contract proposal.

The other municipal sector workers' organisations accepted the proposal. Teachers, for example, support accepting it.

The employers in the municipal sector are also ready to accept the proposal.

Labour Minister Tuula Haatainen must now decide how the negotiations will continue.

## The weather

On Thursday it rains in Southern and Central Finland. In the north it is dry and the sun may shine.

The temperature is 10-15 degrees across the whole country. In Northern Lapland it is cooler.

On Friday the temperature rises close to 15 degrees in most of the country, but at the weekend the weather gets colder. Rain also becomes more common at the weekend.
