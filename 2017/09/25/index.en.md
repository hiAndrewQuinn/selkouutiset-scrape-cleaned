# Monday 25.9.2017 (radio)

The police have given more information about last Friday's stabbing incident. In Iraq, the Kurds have voted on independence. In Finland, people want fathers to use a larger part of family leave. The price of blueberries is rising.

## The stabbing incident

![Police officers and an ambulance in front of Parliament in Helsinki on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9846465)

The police have given more information about last Friday's stabbing incident. Then 2 young men stabbed themselves with a knife on the steps of the Parliament building in Helsinki.

The police now say that the men were asylum seekers. The reason for the act is connected to applying for asylum.

The police also say that the men are about 20 years old. However, the police do not say which country the men came to Finland from.

The men were not badly injured. They left hospital on Saturday.

The police are continuing to look into the situation together with the Finnish Immigration Service.

## The Kurds of Iraq

![Iraqi Kurdistan is voting on independence right now.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9850432)

Open image viewer

The Kurds of Iraq voted on independence.

In Iraq, the Kurds have voted on independence. Some Kurds want the Kurds to found their own state in northern Iraq.

The Kurdish leaders of Iraq plan to start negotiations with the Iraqi government if a majority of Kurds want their own state.

The Iraqi government does not accept the vote.

Kurds also live in Iraq's neighbouring countries Turkey and Iran. That is why the governments of Turkey and Iran do not want a Kurdish state to be born in Iraq.

## Family leave

![Father and child.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9828503)

Open image viewer

Fathers are wanted on family leave too. Photo: AOP

In Finland, people hope that fathers will use family leave more than now. That is why the Finnish government plans to reform family leave.

Employee and employer organisations say that the government must reserve a larger part of family leave for fathers. Women's equality in working life improves if fathers also take care of children.

However, the organisations are worried about whether there is time to make the reform carefully enough. The government wants the reform to be introduced as early as the beginning of 2019.

## Blueberries

![picking blueberries](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9754922)

Open image viewer

Healthy blueberries grow in Finland's forests. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The price of blueberries has risen and will rise more. The reason is that only a few blueberries have been picked this year, but there are many buyers.

The blueberry is a popular berry in many countries. For example, Japanese and Chinese people eat blueberries because the blueberry is very healthy.

This year, however, the blueberry harvest has been poor around the world. For example in the USA and Eastern Europe, fewer blueberries have grown than usual.

In Finland the blueberries ripened late and the harvest was not big. It is a pity, because the price of blueberries will quite certainly rise by many tens of percent.
