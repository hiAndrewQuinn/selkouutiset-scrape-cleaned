# Wednesday 27.3.2019 (radio)

Violent child pornography has been made in Finland. Finland is sending aid to the African storm area. Yle's managing director Merja Ylä-Anttila is choosing this year's Finlandia literature prize winner. Yle Oppimisen Digitreenit has put together a digital glossary.

## A serious crime

![Investigation leader, Chief Inspector Sanna Springare of the National Bureau of Investigation (KRP), at a press conference at the KRP building in Vantaa on 27 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10709530)

Violent child pornography has been made in Finland. The police suspect that Finnish men filmed child pornography and shared it online.

There are 5 men. Some shared child pornography, and some also raped children.

The victims were boys aged 6 to 15. The crimes took place between 2004 and 2018.

The Finnish men were part of an international pornography ring. Members of the ring filmed child pornography in different countries and shared it with each other. Some of the videos have been very violent.

The Finnish police began to investigate the matter in 2017, when they learned about it from the authorities of other countries.

## Help to Africa

![Photo of a school gym that has been made into an emergency shelter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10709419)

Open image viewer

Many Mozambicans have lost their homes. They have to sleep in the school hall. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Finnish Red Cross / IFRC / EPA

Finland is sending aid to the African storm area. The Finnish Red Cross is sending parts of a field hospital to Mozambique, for example.

A field hospital is a temporary hospital, but it has, for example, an operating theatre, a laboratory and X-ray equipment.

The chief nurse of the field hospital, Virpi Teinilä, says that many diseases can spread in the storm area. People in Mozambique have already fallen ill with cholera. Diseases spread because hygiene is poor in the middle of the storm damage.

The storm hit East Africa about 2 weeks ago. The storm damage affects the lives of as many as 2 million people. About 800 people are already dead.

## Choosing the Finlandia winner

![Portrait of Merja Ylä-Anttila](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4623,h_2600,x_376,y_124/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5388515c4b07dea2563/1698756769)

Managing Director Merja Ylä-Anttila is choosing this year's Finlandia winner.

Yle's managing director Merja Ylä-Anttila is choosing this year's winner of the Finlandia Prize. The Finlandia Prize is Finland's most important literary prize.

Ylä-Anttila chooses the winner from 6 candidates. A group of people in the literary field chooses the candidates.

The winner of the Finlandia Prize is announced at the end of November. At that time the best non-fiction book and the best children's book are also awarded.

## New digital glossary

![A young person uses a Pädi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10384644)

Digital skills are needed. Photo: AOP

Digital skills are important nowadays. However, many people find digital terms difficult to understand. Do you know, for example, what a browser or wifi means?

Yle Oppimisen Digitreenit has put together a new digital glossary. It explains digital terms in clear Finnish.

The glossary has more than 900 words. The glossary can be found on the Yle Oppiminen website at yle.fi/oppiminen.
