# Thursday 18.7.2019 (radio)

EU countries' interior ministers are discussing the Mediterranean refugee problem. People have died in Japan in a fire that was set on purpose. Ed Sheeran performs in Helsinki next week. The Moomins start to protect the Baltic Sea.

## Ministers' meeting in Helsinki

![Matteo Salvini](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10884662)

Open image viewer

Italy's interior minister Matteo Salvini attended the EU meeting in Helsinki on Thursday. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The EU countries' interior ministers are gathered in Helsinki. The interior ministers are discussing the Mediterranean refugee problem.

Many asylum seekers come to Europe across the Mediterranean. They usually reach the European Union area in Italy.

The interior ministers are now trying to agree that asylum seekers will also be moved to other EU countries. No agreement has been reached.

Italy wants asylum seekers to be sent away from Europe. Italy is afraid that otherwise they will stay in Italy.

So far this year 700 people have drowned in the Mediterranean while trying to reach Europe.

## Fire in Japan

![The Kyoto Animation anime studio burns in Kyoto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10883147)

Open image viewer

The Kyoto Animation studio burned in Kyoto on Thursday. Photo: AOP

There has been a serious fire in Japan. More than 30 people died in the fire. More than 30 people were also injured.

The Japanese police say that a man of about 40 set the fire on purpose. It is not yet known why the man did so.

The fire was at the Kyoto Animation studio in the city of Kyoto. The studio makes anime, that is, Japanese animation.

The police say that the arsonist did not work at the studio.

## Ed Sheeran's concerts

![Malmi Airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10883574)

Open image viewer

The concert venue for Ed Sheeran is being built at Malmi Airport. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Next week 2 big concerts will be held in Helsinki. The British singer Ed Sheeran will perform at the concerts.

The concerts will be held on Tuesday and Wednesday at Malmi Airport. On both days about 60,000 people will come.

The concert organiser says that people may only come to queue at the concert area from 9 in the morning. Elsewhere in the world people have queued for even several days.

Ed Sheeran started as a street musician. Now he is a world star.

## The Moomins and the Baltic Sea

![Sophia Jansson](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9968766)

Open image viewer

The Moomins and Sophia Jansson are starting to raise money for the Baltic Sea. Photo: Jari Kovalainen

The Moomins are starting to protect the Baltic Sea.

The Moomins' campaign for the Baltic Sea starts next year, which is the Moomins' anniversary year. Then it will be 75 years since Tove Jansson's first Moomin book was published.

The sea has always been an important theme in the Moomin books. That is why the Moomins are meant to help raise a million euros to protect the Baltic Sea, says Sophia Jansson, director of the company Moomin Characters.

The Baltic Sea is dirty, and it urgently needs help. The money raised through the Moomins will go to the John Nurminen Foundation, which develops the protection of the Baltic Sea.
