# Saturday 1.2.2020 (radio)

Britain has left the European Union. Many Scots hope for independence. The price of a taxi journey must be clearly shown. January was warmer than usual.

## Britain left the EU

![A sign that says &quot;Brexit Celebration&quot;. In front of it are British flags.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11187813)

Many Britons are celebrating the EU departure. Photo: Stephen Chung / AOP

Britain has left the European Union. Britain's EU departure took effect on Saturday night.

At the moment of departure the Britons gathered in London. Many celebrated, but many were also sad.

Britain's EU departure, Brexit, has long been a subject of dispute in Britain. However, a majority of Britons supported leaving when the matter was voted on in 2016.

Britain was a member of the European Union for almost 50 years. Britain's Prime Minister Boris Johnson says that after the departure Britain will change and succeed.

At first no big changes will happen. Britain follows the EU's rules until the end of this year.

## Scotland hopes for independence

![Protest for independence in Glasgow, Scotland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11187835)

Open image viewer

Protesters hope for independence for Scotland. Photo: Robert Perry / EPA

Britain's EU departure is a sad day for Scotland. Scotland is part of Britain.

A majority of Scots support the European Union. However, Scotland has to leave the EU along with the rest of Britain.

Many Scots now hope that Scotland will become an independent country. Many think that an independent Scotland could be a member of the EU.

Britain's Prime Minister Boris Johnson does not accept Scotland's independence. The Prime Minister reminds people that the Scots did not want independence when the matter was voted on in 2014.

Scots feel that the situation has changed because of Britain's EU departure. Now independence interests many Scots.

## The taxi fare must be visible

![Taxis at a taxi stand](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8461260)

Open image viewer

The customer must see the price of a taxi journey before the journey. Photo: Henrietta Lehtinen / Yle

In Finland a new regulation on taxi fares has been issued. The regulation says that the price must be clearly visible.

There must be a price list on the side of the taxi car, so that the customer can see the price before the journey.

The regulation applies to taxis that wait for customers, for example at a taxi stand. The regulation does not apply to taxis that are booked before the journey.

## January in southern Finland was warm

![A golfer on the golf course at Vuosaari Golf in Vuosaari](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11187139)

January was warm. In southern Finland January was almost 10 degrees warmer than usual.

Only in the north was January cold and snowy.

## It will get colder early next week

![A man standing in sleet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11020755)

Open image viewer

It will rain and sleet at the weekend. Photo: AOP

The weather this weekend is also warm. In the south it is above zero, and the rain is rain or sleet.

At the start of next week the weather will get colder. In the south too there will be a little frost.

At the end of next week, however, the weather will warm up again. Above-zero temperatures and rainy weather will return to southern Finland.
