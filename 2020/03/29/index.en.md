# Sunday 29.3.2020 (radio)

The isolation of Uusimaa continues. School certificates may be sent by post. The coronavirus is weakening the church's economy. Spain is stopping all activity. The ice is weak.

## The police have checked many cars at the Uusimaa border

![The police checking drivers at the stopping place next to Kasvihuoneilmiö in Lohja, along Vanha Turuntie, on Saturday morning, 28 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11281097)

The police stopped a car at the Uusimaa border. Checks are also carried out on trains. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The isolation of Uusimaa from the rest of Finland continues.

Saturday was the first day of the isolation. On Saturday, the police checked about 17,000 cars that were on their way out of Uusimaa or coming into Uusimaa.

The police say that people are calm and understand why the Uusimaa border has been closed. You may cross the border only if there is an essential reason.

## A movement restriction may prevent school spring celebrations

![Students put their graduation caps on their heads at the upper secondary school graduation party.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5257834)

Open image viewer

Graduation parties and other school graduation celebrations may have to be held over a remote connection, if the ban on gatherings continues. Photo: YLE

Many schools are preparing for the possibility that the spring celebrations will have to be organised in a new way. Students who graduate, for example as upper secondary students or from vocational school, may get their certificate by post.

Schools cannot hold a spring celebration if the ban on gatherings continues.

The ban on gatherings is now in force until 13 April. Schools are waiting for news on whether the ban on gatherings will be extended.

## The church's economy is weakening

![](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5008374)

Open image viewer

Church service at the Ristinkirkko church in Lahti.

The coronavirus is also weakening the economy of the church. The Evangelical Lutheran parishes of Finland calculate that they will get less money this year, because the economy is doing badly because of the coronavirus.

The parishes calculate that they will get 40–50 million euros less church tax and state money than before.

The parishes may have to reduce their activities. In some parishes, employees may have to be laid off.

## In Spain, everyone must stay at home

![Workers arrive at the exhibition centre hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11270065)

Open image viewer

Emergency hospital for coronavirus patients in Madrid, the capital of Spain. Photo: image provided by the Madrid regional government / EPA

Spain is stopping all activity for two weeks. All people must stay at home in Spain for 2 weeks, so that the coronavirus does not spread.

In Spain, only people who do work that is necessary for the functioning of society may leave home. Almost 6,000 people have died of the coronavirus in Spain.

The worst coronavirus situation (in Europe) is in Italy. In Italy, 10,000 people have died of the coronavirus.

## Don't go on thin ice

![Two people standing on the ice.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4128,h_2322,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5134605bb1c8d9a42a4/1538378099)

Open image viewer

You must not go onto the ice. The ice may break. Photo: Linus Hoffman / Yle

The police warn about weak ice.

The ice on lakes is weak, because the sun is warming it and the ice is thin. In Eastern Finland, two cars fell through the ice at the weekend. People have also fallen through the ice.

The police say that you must not go onto the ice. The ice is now weak and may break.
