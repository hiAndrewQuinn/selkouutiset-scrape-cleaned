# Tuesday 26.9.2017 (radio)

Health data leaked onto the internet. Construction is busy. A child pornography sentence. The delay of the West Metro. Bali's Agung may erupt.

## Bad data leak at the health authorities

![the entrance of THL](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9851343)

Open image viewer

Health experts work at THL.  Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

A bad data leak has happened in Finland. People's personal data has leaked onto the internet. The data leak happened at THL, where health authorities work. In the data leak, for example the laboratory test results of 6,000 people got onto the internet.

The data leak happened in August. THL says that the data was taken off the internet immediately. The data leak was an accident. An employee made a mistake in handling the data. THL has apologised for the data leak.

A data protection expert says that the leak is serious. Data Protection Ombudsman Reijo Aarnio thinks that people have trusted that their own health data does not go onto the internet. Aarnio says that people's trust has now been shaken.

## A lot of building is going on in Finland

![The Tripla construction site in Pasila.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9850370)

Many new buildings are going up now.  Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

A great many new buildings are being built in Finland this year. This year, for example, construction of 40,000 new homes will start. It is estimated that next year construction will not be as busy as this year.

A lot of old homes are also being repaired in Finland. Many homes are getting so old that they must be renovated.

## Big child pornography sentence in Tampere

![Tampere courthouse](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9192304)

Open image viewer

Tampere courthouse.  Photo: Antti Eintola / Yle

A court in Tampere has sentenced a man to more than 3 years in prison for child pornography. According to the court, more than 850,000 child pornography pictures were found on the man. In addition, the man had more than 18,000 videos with child pornography.

The man had also uploaded many pictures and videos to the internet. The crimes were committed last year and at the beginning of this year.

## The West Metro may be late again

![West Metro Aalto University metro station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9344650)

Open image viewer

People have waited a long time for the West Metro to start running.  Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

The Helsinki region's new West Metro may be delayed further. The West Metro may still not be able to open because of electrical faults.

Traffic on the new metro line is expected to start in October.  Traffic was first supposed to start as early as 2014. However, construction has been badly delayed.

## An eruption of Agung is expected

![Local residents watch the Agung volcano in Bali.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9847916)

Open image viewer

There have been hundreds of small earthquakes near Agung.  Photo: Made Nagi / EPA

On the island of Bali in Indonesia, people fear a volcanic eruption. According to experts, Bali's volcano Agung may erupt soon. Many hundreds of small earthquakes have been felt near the mountain. It may be a sign that an eruption will start soon.

More than 75,000 people have already been taken to safety on the island. Agung last erupted in 1963.
