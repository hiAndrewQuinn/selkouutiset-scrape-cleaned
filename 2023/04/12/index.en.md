# Wednesday 12.4.2023 (radio)

Helsinki wage payments. Waiting lists for specialist care. Summer speed limits. Pollen season. The weather.

## Helsinki wage payments

![Juhana Vartainen at a press briefing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690391)

Open image viewer

Helsinki Mayor Juhana Vartainen told journalists about Helsinki's wage payments. Photo: Cecilia Heikel / Yle

An investigation has been made into the problems with Helsinki's wage payments. The problems began when Helsinki started to use a new payroll system.

There have been many errors in the city employees' pay. Employees have even been left without pay.

The investigation says that there are many reasons for the problems. The change, for example, was managed badly.

Investigating the errors has cost the City of Helsinki over 4 million euros. The city says that the errors in wage payments have already decreased.

## Waiting lists for specialist care

![Orthopaedic surgeon Matti Sävelä performed the first robot-assisted knee replacement operation in the operating theatre of Satasairaala on 25.8.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_454/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8472236128ee6508e92/1630072517)

Open image viewer

For example, there are long queues for joint replacement operations. Photo: Satasairaala

Waiting lists for specialist care have grown. People have to wait longer than before for non-urgent specialist care.

The queues are long, for example, for knee replacement operations and cataract operations on the eye.

The queues are long because there are too few healthcare workers. The queues also grew during the corona time.

## Summer speed limits

![A man changes a speed limit sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6350480)

Open image viewer

Summer speed limits come into use in Finland at the end of April. Photo: Yle

Finland's roads are switching to summer speed limits.

In the south, the switch to summer speed limits began today, Wednesday.

In the north the switch to summer speed limits starts in a week.

Summer speed limits come into use when the driving conditions are good enough.

The aim is that summer speed limits are in use across the whole country by the end of April.

On motorways the summer speed limit is 120 kilometres per hour, on ordinary roads 100 kilometres per hour.

## Pollen season

![Birch pollen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4539235a4ba7ea537cc/1617542947)

Open image viewer

Many people are allergic to birch pollen, for example. Photo: Risto Salovaara / Yle

The pollen season has started.

Trees and shrubs have already started to flower, because the weather has been warm. Alder and hazel pollen is already in the air.

About 20 percent of Finns get symptoms from pollen. The symptoms include, for example, a runny nose and itchy eyes.

In spring, street dust, of which there is a lot on roads and streets, makes the situation worse. The pollen season lasts all summer.
