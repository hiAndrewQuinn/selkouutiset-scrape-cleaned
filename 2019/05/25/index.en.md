# Saturday 25.5.2019 (radio)

Finns vote in the EU elections on Sunday. There was a bomb attack in France. Indians suffer from dirty air. Finland made it to the ice hockey World Championship final. The weekend is rainy.

## EU elections on Sunday

![Matti Rönkä and Susanna Turunen rehearsing for the election broadcast.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5922,h_3331,x_0,y_573/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5657955ce7eb3cd294e/1558703231)

The EU elections are this weekend.

The EU elections are held in all EU countries. In many countries people vote on Sunday.

In Finland the polling stations open on Sunday at 9 am, and they close in the evening at 8 pm.

In the EU elections the citizens of the EU countries elect representatives to the European Parliament. The European Parliament makes EU laws and decides, for example, on EU funds.

The European Parliament has over 750 representatives. Finns elect 13 representatives to the Parliament.

On Sunday evening Yle will report who will go from Finland to the European Parliament. There are election broadcasts on radio, on television and online.

## Bomb attack in France

![Motorcycle police escorting ambulances.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10801480)

Open image viewer

Police and ambulances at the blast site. Photo: Philippe Desmazes / AFP

French police are searching for the person behind the bomb attack.

There was a bomb attack in France on Friday evening. Nobody died in the attack, but more than 10 people were injured.

The bomb attack happened in the city of Lyon. A parcel bomb exploded in the centre of Lyon.

A surveillance camera filmed a man who left a bag at the blast site. The picture shows a man of about 30 years old. The police are looking for the man in the picture.

## Air pollution in India

![Lung doctor Arup Halder examines the throat of five-year-old Arya Bose in Kolkata, India.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10800704)

A doctor examines a child who has fallen ill because of air pollution. Photo: Jenny Matikainen / Yle

Indians suffer from dirty air. There are so many pollutants in the air that many people fall ill.

More than a million Indians died because of air pollution in 2017.

Doctors and many organisations hope that a state of emergency will be declared in India because of air pollution.

## Finland to the final

![Marko Anttila](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10802040)

Open image viewer

Marko Anttila scored Finland's winning goal. Photo: Joe Klamar / AFP / Lehtikuva

Finland's ice hockey team's success at the World Championships continues.

Finland beat Russia on Saturday. That means Finland plays in the final match on Sunday.

The winner of the final match is the ice hockey world champion. The final match is played on Sunday evening.

Saturday's game was exciting. Finland and Russia fought hard.

The game was tied 0-0 for a long time. In the last period, however, Finland's Marko Anttila scored a goal, and Finland reached the World Championship final.

## A cool weekend

![Men grilling in the rain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8978831)

Open image viewer

Summer is summer, even when it rains. Photo: Mika Kanerva / Yle

The weekend is rainy. On Saturday it has rained everywhere in Finland.

Yle meteorologist Joonas Koskela says that on Sunday there will be new rain in Finland.

The weekend is also cool, so the temperature will not rise high. In the south the temperature is between 10 and 15 degrees, in the north between 5 and 10 degrees.

Early next week the weather is also quite cool.
