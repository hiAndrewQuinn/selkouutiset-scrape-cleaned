# Sunday 9.1.2022 (radio)

Protests against corona measures in Europe. Corona in the Swedish royal family. Problems at Espresso House. Donation to the Sibelius Academy. Cold on Monday.

## Protests in Europe

![Protesters gathered at Palais-Royal square in Paris.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_493/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89984861da06677610b/1641678594)

French people protest against corona measures in Paris. Photo: Christophe Archambault / AFP

People have protested against corona measures in many European countries.

About 100,000 people protested on Saturday in various parts of France. They marched in the streets against the new corona measures.

In France it is planned that only those who have had a corona vaccination will be able to get into many places. The idea is that nobody can get into restaurants, cultural events and some trains without a corona vaccination, for example.

There have also been big protests in Germany. Many are angry, because the German government is planning compulsory corona vaccinations.

About 40,000 people have marched in the streets of Vienna, the capital of Austria. The reason is that Austria will have a vaccination requirement in February, that is, everyone must get a corona vaccination.

## Corona in the Swedish royal family

![Crown Princess Victoria.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5009,h_2817,x_0,y_334/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89981761d9aad1afab0/1641655041)

Open image viewer

Crown Princess Victoria (on the right) cannot carry out all her duties now because of a corona infection. Photo: AOP

In Sweden, Finland's neighbouring country, corona has also spread to the royal family.

At the weekend it was reported that Crown Princess Victoria has got a corona infection. Victoria's husband also has a corona infection.

The crown princess's family is staying at home in quarantine. The family lives at Haga Palace.

Earlier this week it was reported that the Swedish king couple has caught corona. The Swedish king and queen are the parents of Crown Princess Victoria.

The Swedish royal family has been vaccinated against corona, so nobody has become seriously ill.

## Problems at Espresso House

![Espresso House central kitchen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3717,h_2090,x_0,y_30/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89910461d5b64c33f09/1641395856)

Open image viewer

There have been problems at the Espresso House central kitchen. Photo: Yle MOT

The coffee chain Espresso House's former employees have told of poor working conditions.

Former employees of the Espresso House central kitchen say that working conditions can even be life-threatening. They also say that hygiene in the central kitchen is poor.

Espresso House says that many of the problems have already been fixed.

Former employees of Espresso House told Yle's MOT team about the problems. You can read more about the matter on Yle's website, the address is yle.fi.

## Donation to the Sibelius Academy

![The entrance to the Sibelius Academy at the Music Centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11021289)

Open image viewer

The Sibelius Academy operates in the Music Centre in Helsinki. Photo: Joni Haavisto / AOP

The Sibelius Academy, a music education institution, has received a big donation.

The Sibelius Academy received one million dollars from the American Elsa Brule. Brule's roots are in Finland.

The donation goes to the Global Music training programme, which builds cooperation between cultures.
