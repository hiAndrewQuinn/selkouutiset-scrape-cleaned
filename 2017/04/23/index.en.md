# Sunday 23.4.2017 (radio)

The first round of the presidential election is held in France. New kinds of electricity meters are being tested in Finland. 80-year-old Antti-Jussi Tiitola is developing a new ice hockey stick. The Little Lions are in the World Championship final. The first Finnish film has been remade.

## The French vote

Candidates in France. Photo: Ian Langsdon / EPA

The French presidential election is being held today, Sunday. Now it is the first round of the election. The two candidates who get the most votes will go on to the second round.

The election is exciting, because 4 candidates are almost equally popular. They are the centrist Emmanuel Macron, the far-right Marine Le Pen, the conservative François Fillon and the far-left Jean-Luc Mélenchon.

During the day there have been about as many voters as in the last presidential election. The results of the French election will be known on Sunday evening.

## Smart meters are being tested

![Jarkko Vehmas installing new smart electricity meters in flats in an apartment block.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9577343)

Open image viewer

An installer is changing the electricity meters in an apartment block. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

New kinds of electricity meters are being tested in Finland.

The new meters tell exactly, all the time, how much electricity is used in the home. The meter sends the information to the electricity company. Exact information is needed, because a large part of electricity is now made with wind or sun, and the weather varies a lot. Electricity is cheap when there is a lot of it on offer, that is when there is strong wind or clear sunshine.

The new electricity meters are called smart meters. Finland is ahead of other countries in using smart meters.

## An 80-year-old is developing a new ice hockey stick

![The new composite stick is based on Antti-Jussi &quot;Naku&quot; Tiitola's 50 years of experience in designing and making sticks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8909045)

Open image viewer

Antti-Jussi Tiitola and the new stick. Photo: Jari Järvinen / Yle

Many of the world's best ice hockey players have used the Finnish Titan stick. For example, the famous Canadian Wayne Gretzky played with a Titan stick in the 1980s. Titan sticks have been sold 15 million times.

The Titan stick was developed by Antti-Jussi Tiitola, and they were made in Finland and in Canada. Tiitola is now 80 years old and he is developing a new stick. Tiitola wants it to become by far the best ice hockey stick. Tiitola's new sticks are made in Padasjoki.

The Little Lions play in the final of the ice hockey World Championship on Sunday evening.

The Little Lions, that is the national under-18 team, play the final against the USA. Finland reached the final when it beat Russia 2-1 on Saturday. The World Championship is played in Slovakia.

Finland was the under-18 world champion last year.

## The first film again

![A scene from the film Salaviinanpolttajat (The Moonshiners).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9572971)

Open image viewer

From the film The Moonshiners. Photo: Kuokkasen Kuvaamo

The first Finnish film in history has been remade.

Juho Kuosmanen has made a new version of the silent film The Moonshiners. The Moonshiners was a short film that was shown for the first time in Helsinki in 1907. The film reel no longer exists. It is only roughly known what it was about.

The new version of the film is also a silent film, that is, there is no speech and no sound in it. At the premiere in Tampere, a live orchestra will play the music of the film.
