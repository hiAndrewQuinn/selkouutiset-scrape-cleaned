# Friday 7.6.2019 (radio)

Matti Vanhanen is the new Speaker of Parliament. There have been no big changes in party support. The fastest supercomputer in Europe will be built in Kajaani. Friday has been the hottest day of the summer so far.

## Speaker of Parliament

![Matti Vanhanen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10820959)

Open image viewer

Parliament's new Speaker Matti Vanhanen. Photo: Yle

Parliament has elected a new Speaker. The Speaker leads the work of Parliament, that is, Finland's parliament.

The new Speaker is Matti Vanhanen. Vanhanen is from the Centre party.

Matti Vanhanen has been involved in politics for a long time. For example, he has been Prime Minister of Finland.

The job of Speaker of Parliament is important and prestigious. Officially, it is Finland's second most important job, right after the President's job.

## Party support

![PARTY SUPPORT](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10816745)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Party support has changed only a little since the election. The parliamentary elections were in April, and the new government started work yesterday, Thursday. The government is led by Antti Rinne of the SDP.

The SDP won the April election, but in Yle's new poll it is the second largest party. The SDP's support has stayed at about 17 per cent.

The largest party in Yle's poll is the Finns Party. In the election the Finns Party got less support than the SDP, but now the Finns Party's support has risen and is about 19 per cent.

The Finns Party is in opposition, that is, it is not part of the government.

The other big opposition party, the National Coalition, is in 3rd place in the poll.

Of the government parties, the Greens' support has risen and the Centre's support has fallen.

The support figures for all parties can be found at yle.fi.

## Fastest supercomputer

![ECMWF supercomputer, which calculates Europe's medium-range weather forecasts.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9501024)

Open image viewer

Photo: ECMWF

A large supercomputer is being built in Kajaani. The Europe's fastest supercomputer will be built in Kajaani in Northern Finland. The decision was made in the European Union on Friday.

The computer will be completed at the end of next year. It is 10 times more powerful than the EU's current supercomputer.

In Finland it is hoped that the supercomputer will bring opportunities for Finnish research and industry.

Similar supercomputers will also be built in Italy and Spain. The money comes from the European Union and EU countries. Building the supercomputer in Kajaani costs 200 million euros.

## Record-warm weather

![Kirjurinluoto beach in Pori on a hot day](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10819330)

Open image viewer

The beach calls on a hot day. Photo: Katja Halinen / Yle

The hot weather has continued. Friday has been the hottest day of the summer so far.

In Oulu the temperature was over 32 degrees. This high a temperature was measured in Finland for the first time this early in June.

In many other towns too, the temperature has been over 30 degrees. The hot weather also brings thunderstorms.
