# Thursday 2.8.2018 (radio)

Violence. Removal from Finland. Teachers. Horses. The weather.

## Violence has increased

![Asylum seekers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8743082)

Open image viewer

Asylum seekers. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

Violence against staff, and threats of violence, have become more common in asylum seeker reception centres.

Authorities have recorded 10-15 reports of violence every month so far this year. The violence is perhaps due to negative asylum decisions and the bad state that the decisions cause.

There are currently 11 400 asylum seekers in reception centres. 9 000 asylum seekers have received a negative asylum decision.

## Thousands have been removed from the country

![Police car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10310777)

Open image viewer

Police car. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

According to the police, about 2 600 people were removed from Finland last year. This year, about as many people will be removed from the country as last year.

The police say that some of those to be returned have received a negative asylum decision, and some of those to be returned are criminals. Last year the police were able to remove just over 500 people from the country.

Many people have been removed from Finland to Estonia, and very many also to Russia and Iraq.

## More support for children

![Boy at an interactive whiteboard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7812736)

Open image viewer

At the pre-primary class of Aittakorpi school in Kotka, letters are practised using an interactive whiteboard. Photo: Maija Tuunila / Yle

Many teachers say in the survey that children cannot be offered learning support as soon as the need for support is noticed.

The chair of the teachers' union says that the situation may be illegal. Children have the right to support immediately when they need it.

Teachers propose that group sizes be reduced and more teachers be hired.

## Horse is not eaten

![A pony and a horse in an enclosure](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10332676)

Open image viewer

A pony and a horse in an enclosure. Photo: Timi Kärki / Yle

Finns do not like to eat horse.

Eating horsemeat is decreasing all the time.

Only 31 percent of horses end up at slaughterhouses. Every year over half a million kilos of horsemeat goes to waste.

It is an old Finnish tradition that a horse is buried at the edge of a field. Many people still do so.

## Summer continues

![Maximum temperature map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10333861)

Open image viewer

Weather. Photo: Yle Sää

Nights and days are beginning to cool down.

Today a warning of heavy rain was issued for almost the whole country. Thunder showers move across the country to the east by the evening. On Friday and Saturday temperatures will still exceed the heat limit, but then the temperature falls.

On Wednesday the temperature rises again, and the heat limit may be exceeded in the south. So summer is not over yet. In Southern Finland, more heat than normal is still expected.
