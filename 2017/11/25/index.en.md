## People want to talk about sexual harassment in Parliament

![The Parliament session hall](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9947562)

Open image viewer

Parliament has not talked about harassment yet. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

People around the world have talked a lot about sexual harassment this autumn.

The opposition party SDP says that in Finland there is one important place for discussion that has not been used yet. This place is Parliament. That is why the party is making a proposal for a discussion in Parliament on the topic of sexual harassment.

The party's presidential candidate Tuula Haatainen says that MPs and Parliament cannot stay silent when the whole of Finland is talking about harassment.

## London police are looking for men who caused panic

![In the picture, six police officers in yellow uniforms stand in a row under the Christmas lights of Oxford Street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9948291)

Open image viewer

Police officers patrolled Oxford Street on Friday evening, near Oxford Circus underground station. Photo: Epa-Efe/Facundo Arrizabalaga

London police have said that they are looking for two men. The police suspect that the men have information about what caused panic and hysteria on Friday.

The men may have had a clash or an argument on the underground station platform before people fled.

Many people said that they heard gunshots, but the police have not found evidence of shots.

The British public broadcaster BBC has reported that 16 people were injured. Eyewitnesses say that people were injured when they were trampled by the crowd.

## Many moose are being shot now

![A moose in a field](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9883479)

Open image viewer

Moose hunting means that you can shoot a moose if you have a permit. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

A great many moose are being shot in this year's hunting season. So far, over 45,000 moose have already been killed. Last year, by the end of the year, a thousand fewer moose were killed.

You must have a permit to shoot a moose, and moose may be shot only at a certain time of year. In Finland, more permits were granted for shooting moose than last year. Moose may still be shot for five weeks.

## The prickly pear cactus is a very useful plant

![Egyptians collect prickly pear fruits in Cairo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9948249)

Open image viewer

The prickly pear cactus provides food for people and animals.  Photo: Moahmed Hossam / EPa

The prickly pear cactus is a useful plant that is easy to care for. It can make deserts green. The prickly pear cactus also provides food for people and animals.

An expert recommends that the prickly pear cactus should be used in deserts more than before.

People and animals can eat the fruits of the prickly pear cactus. The cactus is also nutritious food. It contains, among other things, a lot of vitamins and minerals.

Cacti collect water into themselves very efficiently. They are good water stores in dry places.
