## Coronavirus vaccinations began in Finland

![Nurse Andrea Nummi receives a coronavirus vaccine among the first, on Sunday 27.12.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11715579)

Open image viewer

Nurse Andrea Nummi received a coronavirus vaccine. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The first coronavirus vaccinations have been given in Finland.

In the afternoon, a few employees of the Helsinki University Hospital received the vaccine. They work in the intensive care unit and treat coronavirus patients there.

Other big hospitals in Finland will vaccinate staff tomorrow.

Research suggests that the vaccine protects very well against covid-19, the disease that the coronavirus can cause.

It is not yet known for certain how well the vaccine protects against coronavirus infection and against spreading the virus.

## Ski resorts in Central Europe

![The Austrian Ischgl ski resort gained a bad reputation in the spring, when thousands of tourists who visited the resort caught the coronavirus. The resort is due to open again, and Austria is against closing ski resorts.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5912,h_3326,x_0,y_305/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7466235fbf752dd743a/1606383100)

Open image viewer

The Ischgl ski resort in Austria became famous when there were many coronavirus infections there in the spring. Photo: Christian Bruna / EPA

In Central Europe, countries disagree about whether big ski resorts may be open.

Germany and France have closed all ski resorts because of the coronavirus restrictions.

Austria disagrees. In Austria the ski resorts are open, but with strict restrictions.

Austria demands that the EU must pay compensation for loss of income. Ski tourists are an important source of income for Austria.

## Sales of fireworks began today

![Safety glasses for sale in a shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5299857)

Open image viewer

Remember safety glasses too if you buy fireworks! Photo: YLE

Sales of fireworks began today. Fireworks are on sale in many places until New Year's Eve.

Rockets may only be fired on Thursday, New Year's Eve. Rockets and fireworks may then be fired from 18:00, and firing must stop at 2 in the night.

Because of the coronavirus restrictions, many cities are not holding big fireworks displays .

New Year's fireworks have been cancelled, for example, in the Helsinki area, Turku, Tampere and Joensuu. In Oulu the rockets will be set off virtually in the New Year's celebration broadcast.

## The weather warms up

![map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11715626)

Open image viewer

Last night a new winter frost record was set. At Kevojärvi in Utsjoki there was 41.1 degrees of frost.

Now the weather is warming up, that is, getting warmer across the whole country.

Tomorrow, Monday, it will snow in Lapland. In the south there may be a little sunshine.

There is a strong south wind across the whole country, and because of that the weather feels cold.
