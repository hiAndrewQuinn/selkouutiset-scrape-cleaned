# Friday 28.6.2019 (radio)

Trump and Putin met in Japan. Charges against Kittilä municipal decision-makers were dismissed. One in three got a study place at a university in the joint application. Parliament has gone on summer holiday. 2 Barbary macaque babies were born at Korkeasaari.

## Trump and Putin

![Vladimir Putin and Donald Trump met at the G20 meeting in Osaka.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10852515)

Presidents Putin and Trump met in Osaka. Photo: Brendan Smialowski / AFP

The President of the United States, Donald Trump, and the President of Russia, Vladimir Putin, have met in Japan. The presidents met at the G20 meeting of rich countries in Osaka.

The meeting lasted less than 1.5 hours.

President Trump said that it is an honour to meet Putin, with whom he has a very good relationship.

No details of the discussions have been made public. However, President Putin's spokesperson said that President Trump will get an invitation to the Victory Day celebrations in Moscow next year. Victory Day is celebrated in honour of the victory over Nazi Germany in the Second World War.

## The decision-makers of Kittilä municipality

![Prosecutors Katri Junnikkala-Heikkinen and Sari Anttonen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10425724)

Open image viewer

The prosecutors will probably appeal the district court's decision. Photo: Maija-Liisa Juntti / Yle

The Lapland District Court has dismissed all charges against the leaders of Kittilä municipality. The prosecutor demanded a suspended prison sentence for 27 municipal decision-makers, for aggravated abuse of office among other things.

The charges concern the dismissal of the former municipal manager Anna Mäkelä. The prosecutors were of the opinion that the dismissal was revenge. The accused, for their part, said that Mäkelä's dismissal was due to a lack of trust.

The district court stated that revenge was not the reason for the dismissal.

## Joint application to universities

![Reading for an exam](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9595946)

Open image viewer

About one in three applicants has got a study place at a university. Photo: Minna Rinta-Tassi / Yle

About one in three applicants has got a study place at a university in the spring joint application. There were more than 150,000 applicants in total, and over 48,000 were admitted.

About 1,000 more new students were accepted to universities this year than a year ago.

It is still possible to get a study place through the additional application round. Universities are holding additional application rounds in July and August.

## Parliament goes on summer holiday

![Prime Minister Antti Rinne speaks at Parliament's oral question time in Helsinki on 27 June 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10851837)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Antti Rinne speaks at question time in Parliament. Photo: Anni Reenpää / Lehtikuva

Parliament has gone on summer holiday. Parliament held its last plenary session before the summer break today, Friday. In the session Parliament approved the government's supplementary budget proposal.

Parliament does not go completely quiet during the summer holiday, however. For example, the Grand Committee meets in July.

Parliament's summer break ends in early September.

## Barbary macaques at Korkeasaari

![A Barbary macaque baby.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10853322)

Open image viewer

Full-grown monkeys are looking after one of the babies. Photo: Annika Sorjonen / Korkeasaari

2 Barbary macaque babies have been born at the Korkeasaari zoo. Barbary macaques have never before been born in Finland.

The other Barbary macaques were rescued from poor conditions.

Korkeasaari says that the mothers and babies are doing well. The other monkeys have also welcomed the new arrivals well.

Today Barbary macaques live in the wild in Europe only in Gibraltar. Earlier they lived as far as Central Germany and Ireland.
