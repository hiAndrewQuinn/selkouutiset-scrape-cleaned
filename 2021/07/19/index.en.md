Serious cases of violence in prisons cannot always be solved. The reason is that there is not enough evidence.

Yle's MOT team's investigation says that, for example, the picture quality of surveillance cameras is poor. In addition, there are not always enough cameras.

DNA samples do not help if the preliminary investigation is started too slowly.

In prisons there are about 300 cases of violence or threats a year.

## Digital maps

![Jyrki Puupponen hits a landmark in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6223,h_3500,x_10,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83245560f135446403a/1626421155)

A digital register map can show wrong information. The register map shows the borders of the plot.

Register maps are used in forestry work and in sales ads for houses and plots. If the map is wrong, someone may, for example, cut down trees on the neighbour's plot by mistake.

The borders must be checked in the terrain, using boundary markers. A boundary marker is a border mark that shows the correct border.

Nowadays a boundary marker is often a metal square or a cone, that is, a pipe marker. Earlier, a number was carved into rock or a big stone.

The oldest boundary markers are over 200 years old.

Nowadays the location of a boundary marker is measured to the accuracy of a centimetre. Updating the borders with modern equipment can cost the plot owner hundreds of euros.

## UV radiation

![Sunscreen is put on a man's back.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10276651)

Open image viewer

Sunscreen must be spread on the skin a lot. The protection factor must be as high as possible. Photo: AOP

The weather in Finland has been sunny for a long time. In summer there have been more days than normal when UV, that is ultraviolet radiation, is strong.

A dermatologist says that the skin needs protection, even if the skin does not burn. Doctor Ville Kiiski says that red skin has, in practice, already burned.

The skin must be protected from UV radiation all summer. Sunscreen is often used in too small amounts.

It is also worth keeping an eye on your own skin, because with skin cancer an early diagnosis is important. The year before last, a little over 1,800 new melanoma cases were diagnosed. Other skin cancers have also become more common.

## Heavy traffic

![Heavy traffic on Highway 15 from Kouvola to Kotka](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6014935da96047db62c/1571381509)

Heavy traffic is the target of police intensive monitoring this week. Photo: Tiina Karppi / Yle

This week the police are intensively monitoring heavy traffic, that is lorries and coaches. All police departments in Finland take part in the intensive monitoring.

The monitoring especially checks driving times, rest times, securing of the load and the condition of the vehicles.

The special theme of the monitoring period is the use of mobile devices and computers while driving. Attention is also paid to obstacles in the field of view. Obstacles are, for example, items on the dashboard or decorative objects on the windscreen.

The police aim to inspect about 1,800 vehicles during the week.
