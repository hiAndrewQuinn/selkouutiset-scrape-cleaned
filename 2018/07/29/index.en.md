# Sunday 29.7.2018 (radio)

Finnish firefighters have started work in Sweden. There has been an earthquake in Indonesia. Many bank cards did not work on Sunday. Estonian Ott Tänak is the winner of the Jyväskylä rally. A lot of people have drowned in July. The hot weather continues until at least Thursday.

## Finns in firefighting work in Sweden

![Finnish firefighters receiving orders.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10327585)

Open image viewer

Finnish firefighters in Sweden. Photo: Tero Lähdesmäki / Lehtikuva

Finnish firefighters have started work in Sweden. About 30 Finnish firefighters are helping Swedes put out forest fires.

The fire has been burning forests in Sweden for over 2 weeks. The most forest fires are in Central Sweden.

A group of Finnish volunteers is also taking part in the firefighting. The group has 18 Finns and 4 Estonians. They work in the forestry sector. The group has been doing firefighting work in Sweden for almost a week.

## Earthquake in Indonesia

![Earthquake damage on the island of Lombok in Indonesia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10327921)

Open image viewer

The earthquake broke houses on the island of Lombok. Photo: Eka Ramadani / EPA

There has been an earthquake in Asia. The earthquake was in Indonesia, on the island of Lombok.

The earthquake broke houses, and heavy pieces of concrete fell on people. Perhaps about 15 people died in the earthquake. There are many injured.

The island of Lombok is a popular travel destination, and many tourists visit the island. Lombok is 100 kilometres from Bali.

## Problems with bank cards

![A person paying at a payment terminal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10327669)

Open image viewer

On Sunday during the day, paying by bank card did not always work. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Many bank cards have not worked on Sunday.

For example, in shops customers were not always able to pay by bank card. Customers had to pay in cash.

There were problems with the bank cards of Nordea, OP and Danske Bank, at least. There were also problems with online banking.

The problems were caused by data network faults. The problems ended on Sunday afternoon.

## Tänak won the Jyväskylä rally

![Ott Tänak celebrates](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10327797)

Open image viewer

Estonian Ott Tänak celebrates his win in Jyväskylä. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

The Jyväskylä World Rally Championship rally has ended. Sunday was the last day of the car race.

The winner of the Jyväskylä World Rally Championship rally is Estonian driver Ott Tänak. The best Finn was Jari-Matti Latvala. He was third in the competition.

The World Rally Championship rally was first held in Jyväskylä as early as 1951.

## Many drowned in July

![Tohlopi beach](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10322802)

Open image viewer

Drowning deaths happen in hot weather, when people swim a lot. Photo: Heli Mansikka / Yle

A lot of people have drowned in July. Already 26 people have died by drowning this month.

Many people drown when the weather is hot. This also happened in 2010, when Finland had a hot summer. At that time, more than 50 people drowned in July.

## Heat and thunder ahead

![lightning in the sky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10208904)

Open image viewer

Hot and humid air brings thunder. Photo: AOP

The hot weather continues next week too. There will be heat until at least Thursday. The temperature is almost 30 degrees across the whole of Finland.

The air is also humid. Hot and humid air brings thunder. Thunder showers can be heavy. Most rain will fall in Western Finland.
