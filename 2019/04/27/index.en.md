# Saturday 27.4.2019 (radio)

The Centre has been thinking about why it lost the elections. A storm is causing damage in Mozambique. Veterans' Day was celebrated on Saturday. Singer Reijo Taipale has died. Japan's emperor is changing.

## Centre meeting

![Centre's Juha Rehula (left), deputy chairman Katri Kulmuni, chairman Juha Sipilä and Anu Vehviläinen at the Centre's party council annual meeting in Riihimäki on Saturday 27 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10757890)

Juha Sipilä (centre) will leave his post as party leader this year. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The Centre held a meeting on Saturday. The Centre considered why it lost the elections 2 weeks ago.

Party leader Juha Sipilä assessed that the parties that did well in the elections were those with sharp and hard opinions.

According to Sipilä, the Centre had good solutions, for example for climate change. The Finns Party, however, got votes from people who do not want to act against climate change. And the Greens got votes from people who think that, for example, driving must be stopped, Sipilä said.

Sipilä decided already after the elections that he will not continue as Centre leader.

## Storm in Mozambique

![People examine damage caused by Cyclone Kenneth in northern Mozambique. Photo taken by the International Red Cross.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10757704)

Open image viewer

The Kenneth storm has caused a lot of damage in Mozambique. Photo: EPA

There is a severe storm in African Mozambique. On Saturday afternoon it was reported that already 4 people have died because of the storm.

The Kenneth storm has brought heavy rain to Mozambique. Authorities have moved more than 30,000 people away from the path of the storm.

This is already the second severe storm in Mozambique in a short time. In March, the Idai storm killed people and caused great damage.

## Veterans' Day

![The festive suit of Olavi Koivisto, a war veteran from Saarijärvi, is decorated with medals.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10540495)

Open image viewer

Many war veterans have received various medals. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Veterans' Day was celebrated on Saturday. The main Veterans' Day celebration was in Kuopio. President Sauli Niinistö also took part in the celebration.

This year too many war veterans received a medal, because they have done a lot for Finland.

War veterans served in the Finnish army during the Second World War. The war ended in 1945, that is, over 70 years ago. War veterans are now already over 90 years old.

## Reijo Taipale has died

![Reijo Taipale](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10743523)

Open image viewer

Reijo Taipale. Photo: Heli Sorjonen / Yle

Finnish singer Reijo Taipale has died. Taipale was 79 years old.

Reijo Taipale sang schlagers and tangos. Taipale's first records were released in 1962. One of them was Satumaa. Many Finns say that Satumaa is Finland's national tango.

Reijo Taipale's records have sold over a million copies in total.

## A new emperor for Japan

![Crown Prince Naruhito, Emperor Akihito and Empress Michiko on Emperor Akihito's 85th birthday in Tokyo on 23 December 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10715997)

Open image viewer

Emperor Akihito (centre) gives up power. Naruhito (left) takes his place. Empress Michiko is Akihito's spouse. Photo: Kimimasa Mayama / EPA

Japan's emperor is changing. The Japanese will get a new emperor next week.

The old emperor Akihito gives up power. Akihito is 85 years old. It is very unusual for a Japanese emperor to give up power.

The new emperor is Naruhito. He is Akihito's eldest son.
