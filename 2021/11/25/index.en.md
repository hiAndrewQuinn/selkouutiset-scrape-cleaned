# Thursday 25.11.2021 (radio)

Restaurant corona restrictions. The Independence Day reception. Germany's corona situation. Sweden's prime minister. Friday's weather.

## Restaurant corona restrictions

![Krista Kiuru](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_128/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8806656194cb7aaa7d4/1637776104)

Minister Krista Kiuru told about the restaurant corona restrictions on Thursday. Photo: Seppo Samuli / Lehtikuva

The government is tightening the corona restrictions for restaurants. The new restrictions come into force in areas with a bad corona situation already at the weekend.

The restrictions say that restaurants must stop selling alcohol at 17.00.

However, the restrictions do not apply to restaurants where a corona pass is in use all the time.

The restrictions also do not apply, for example, to cafés and burger restaurants that do not sell alcohol.

The government decided to tighten the corona restrictions for restaurants, because corona infections are increasing in Finland. On Thursday there were already more than 1300 new corona infections.

There are also more corona patients than before. There are already so many corona patients in intensive care that, for example, operations have had to be cancelled.

## The Independence Day reception

![The Presidential Palace during the Independence Day reception.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5954,h_3349,x_0,y_307/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6201105debc99cd3193/1575733820)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

The Independence Day reception at the Presidential Palace has been cancelled. The reason is the bad corona situation.

The Office of the President announced on Thursday that the reception has been cancelled.

The Presidential Independence Day reception is the most important event of Finland's Independence Day. Finland's Independence Day is celebrated on 6 December.

In Germany, Christmas markets, for example, have been closed because of corona. Photo: Jared Kelly, published on Flickr under a CC licence, image size changed

The corona situation is getting worse in Germany too.

In Germany more than 100 000 people have already died of corona. Germany has 84 million inhabitants.

There are so many corona patients in hospitals that the situation is difficult in many German hospitals.

## Sweden's prime minister

![Magdalena Andersson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_250/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-883580619e7604d970f/1637852650)

Open image viewer

Magdalena Andersson may become Sweden's first woman prime minister. Photo: Pontus Lundahl / EPA

There is an unusual political situation in Sweden, Finland's neighbour.

On Wednesday, Sweden's parliament chose Magdalena Andersson as the country's new prime minister. But already on Wednesday evening, parliament rejected Andersson's budget proposal. That is why Andersson could not take on the job of prime minister.

Now the speaker of the Swedish parliament is proposing Andersson again for the job of prime minister. The Swedish parliament will perhaps vote on the prime minister on Monday.

If Magdalena Andersson becomes prime minister of Sweden, she will be the first woman in this job. Andersson is the leader of the Swedish Social Democrats.

## Friday's weather

On Friday the weather is cloudy and mostly dry. In some places there is a little light snow.

Cold air flows in from the north. On Friday it is frosty across the whole country.

In Lapland it is 10 to 20 degrees of frost, and elsewhere 2 to 10 degrees of frost.
