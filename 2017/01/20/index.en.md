## Girls move less than boys at daycare

![children go down a slide](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9413100)

Open image viewer

Exercise habits are learned already in childhood. It is important that small children move a lot and in many different ways. Photo: Lenni Kuivalainen / Yle

Girls and boys move in different ways. Movement has been studied at daycare with an activity monitor. The girls' monitors recorded fewer minutes than the boys' monitors.

During eight (8) hours, the girls' monitors showed about ten (10) minutes less brisk exercise than the boys' monitors.

The researchers say that adults may encourage girls to move in a different way than boys. Girls also often play calmer games than boys.

The differences are significant, because exercise habits are largely learned already in childhood.

## Interruptions are harmful in hospital

![A nurse puts medicines into small cups.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7180300)

Open image viewer

Handing out medicines in a hospital is precise work. Constant interruptions can harm precision and cause dangerous situations. Photo: Yle

Unnecessary interruptions in hospital harm patient safety.

A doctor may be interrupted maybe dozens of times in a working day. Interruptions are a big risk factor. They can cause mistakes and dangerous situations.

The risks can also be reduced. For example, a nurse can wear a high-visibility vest. With it, others can see that the nurse must not be disturbed. There must also be a quiet space for phone calls.

## Palmyra has been destroyed again

![The Tetrapylon of Palmyra in May 2016](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9414087)

Open image viewer

A Russian soldier at the foot of the Tetrapylon of Palmyra in spring 2016. Now the Isis group has blown up the structure. Photo: Sergei Chirikov / EPA

The terrorist group Isis has destroyed more of a historical site in Syria.

The extremist Islamic group Isis destroyed the front of an ancient amphitheatre in Palmyra. In addition, Isis destroyed the city's colonnade, the Tetrapylon. The city's main street runs through the middle of the columns.

Palmyra was an important city in ancient times. It was built in the first and second centuries AD. Palmyra is a World Heritage Site of the UN, or United Nations.

## Sweden's population is now exactly 10 million

![Stockholm Central Station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9413758)

Open image viewer

Sweden's population is already ten million. It could be seen, for example, at the railway station of the capital Stockholm before Christmas. Photo: Stina Stjernkvist/EPA

Finland's western neighbour Sweden now has ten (10) million inhabitants. Statistics Sweden's estimate of the population is accurate to the minute. The round number was reached on Friday morning at 8.49 Finnish time.

The estimate takes into account people born, people who died and people who moved to the country. That is why nobody knows whether the ten millionth person is a baby or an immigrant.

Eight (8) million was reached in 1969. The latest forecast says that 11 million will be reached in 2024.

Finland's population is growing more slowly. Statistics Finland says that at the end of November, Finland's population was 5,503,347. The population grew by more than 16,000 people last year.  The biggest reason for population growth was net migration from abroad.
