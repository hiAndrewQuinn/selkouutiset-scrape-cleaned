# Tuesday 23.4. (radio)

Parliament began today. The previous prime minister will continue as an ordinary MP. Employers do not support language studies for foreigners. The Sri Lanka bomb attacks may have been revenge. The launch of Samsung's new phone is postponed. Today it has been over 20 degrees warm.

## New Parliament

The new Parliament, that is, the Finnish parliament, has started today. The first plenary session is tomorrow. 200 new MPs were elected in the elections a little over a week ago. The three biggest parties in Parliament are the SDP, the Finns Party and the National Coalition.

## Centre chairman will continue as an ordinary MP

![Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10751244)

Open image viewer

Juha Sipilä arrived at the constituent meeting of the Centre's parliamentary group on Tuesday. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The former prime minister and Centre chairman Juha Sipilä will continue as an ordinary MP. Sipilä says that he is not interested in other posts.

Earlier Sipilä said that he would resign from the post of Centre chairman. The reason for the resignation is that the Centre lost in the parliamentary elections. The Centre will choose a new chairman in September.

## Employers do not support language studies

![Teacher in front of the class. Red Cross volunteer Eelis Lajunen makes language studies seem like play.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8748436)

Open image viewer

Red Cross volunteer Eelis Lajunen makes language studies seem like play. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

People who have moved to Finland for work do not get support from their employer to study Finnish or Swedish. The trade union SAK has made a survey. Workplace representatives were interviewed for the survey.

More than half of the workplace representatives said that there are foreign workers at their workplaces. Only 6 percent of workplaces let employees study Finnish or Swedish during working hours.

## Sri Lanka attacks may be revenge

![Sri Lankan child watches memorial candles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10751595)

Open image viewer

Sri Lankans have held memorial events for the victims of the terror attacks in various parts of the world. Photo: Rizwan Tabassum / AFP

The reason for the bomb attacks in Sri Lanka may have been revenge. Sri Lanka's deputy defence minister says that the bomb attacks may have been revenge for a terror attack carried out in New Zealand. In New Zealand an armed man attacked a mosque and killed 50 people

The minister says that, according to him, two local extremist Islamist organisations carried out the attacks. On Sunday several bomb attacks were carried out in Sri Lanka on churches and hotels. More than 300 people died in the attacks, most of them Sri Lankans.

## Samsung postpones phone launch

![DJ Koh introduced the new Galaxy model, which has a foldable screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10656299)

Open image viewer

Executive DJ Koh introduced the new Galaxy model, whose special feature is a foldable screen, in San Francisco on 20 February. Photo: Samsung

Samsung will only release a new smartphone with a foldable screen later. The new phone was supposed to come onto shops in the United States on Friday. The plan was changed, because the screens of many test phones easily broke. The phone is the first smartphone with a foldable screen to go on sale. Korean Samsung is the world's largest mobile phone maker.

## Temperature exceeded 20 degrees

![A man riding an electric scooter at speed in warming spring sunshine at Merisatama in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10751691)

Spring sunshine has pushed the temperature in Finland over 20 degrees for the first time this spring. The warmest was in Kouvola. There the warmth in the afternoon was 20.4 degrees. Temperatures over 20 degrees have also been recorded in several other places.
