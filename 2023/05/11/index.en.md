## The bridge collapsed

![Police investigating the collapsed bridge.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_252/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1112050645cbd4bca07e/1686041202)

Police investigate the bridge that collapsed. Photo: Jussi Nukari/Lehtikuva

In Tapiola, Espoo, a serious accident happened on Thursday morning in which schoolchildren were injured.

At least 24 people were injured when a pedestrian bridge collapsed. About ten were seriously injured.

Schoolchildren were walking on the bridge when it collapsed. They fell about five metres down.

The bones of many schoolchildren were broken, but nobody's life is in danger.

The police and other authorities are investigating why the accident happened.

The injured schoolchildren are in the eighth grade, that is, about 14 years old.

## Health care

![A nurse helps an elderly person into bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5777,h_3249,x_16,y_342/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99377562f660775c26b/1692788384)

Health services cost a lot of money. Photo: Sampo Saikkonen / Yle

In Finland, health care will cost a lot of money in the future.

It is calculated that in four years health care will cost 6 billion euros more than today. Health care costs more money because people are getting older.

The parties are trying to form a new government in Finland. The new government must think about how Finland's money is enough.

The new government will save money in many things. It also tries to get all people into work, because people at work pay taxes.

## Birds

![A phone in hand, with the Spring of Migratory Birds app open.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4811,h_2706,x_4,y_501/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1111709645bbc33f1f16/1683735113)

The new app knows the birds. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

Migratory birds are now returning to Finland, because summer is near.

A new app has been developed in Finland that recognises birds' songs. The app listens to a bird's song and tells which bird species it is.

For example, in schools children use the app in biology lessons.

The new Finnish app helps identify bird species. The app is really popular.

## Ice hockey

![A group of ice hockey players celebrate a goal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4582,h_2577,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99200662f213ddee7ac/1683991744)

The ice hockey World Championship starts in Finland on Friday. Finland and Latvia are organising the championship together.

The matches are played in Tampere in Finland and in Riga in Latvia.

Ice hockey teams from sixteen countries play in the championship.

Finland's first match is on Friday. Finland plays against the United States.
