# Wednesday 8.3.2017 (radio)

The authorities have fined 2 banks and 2 investment firms that have not treated elderly people well enough. Finland is founding an equality prize. 15-year-old Katariina Räikkönen will become Prime Minister of Finland for one day. A pet is good for a person's health.

## Advice to the elderly on money matters

![A bank employee advises a customer on investing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5597211)

Open image viewer

A customer's age must be taken into account when giving advice on money matters. Photo: Yle

The authorities have imposed fines on 2 banks and 2 investment firms. They must pay fines because they have not treated old people well.

In the authorities' opinion the firms must take better into account how people over 70 are advised on money matters. The firms must remember that elderly people do not understand money matters as well as professionals. It is easy for the firms to sell elderly people, for example, their own company's insurance.

The authorities fined Nordea Bank, the Helsinki Area Cooperative Bank and investment firms called Alexandria and Investium. They all say that they have already improved their practices.

## An equality prize was founded on Women's Day

![Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9497435)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä told about the new prize at a Women's Day equality celebration. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

Finland is founding an equality prize.

The state is giving 150,000 euros for the prize. The prize goes to a person who comes up with how the money can be used so that the position of women in the world improves.

The prize will be given in autumn. It is the world's first international equality prize.

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä told about the new prize on Wednesday, when Women's Day was celebrated.

## A girl in the Prime Minister's job for a day

![Katariina Räikkönen and Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9497551)

Open image viewer

Katariina Räikkönen will take over Prime Minister Juha Sipilä's duties on 11 October. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

15-year-old Katariina Räikkönen will become Prime Minister of Finland for one day. Katariina will take Prime Minister Juha Sipilä's place next autumn, on 11 October. That is the International Day of the Girl.

Katariina Räikkönen takes part in the activities of the children's rights organisation Plan. Plan came up with the idea that girls get to take decision-makers' places for one day. Last year on the International Day of the Girl, girls got to take, for example, the jobs of the President of Nepal and the Minister of Education of Egypt.

Prime Minister Sipilä hopes that girls will get to do the Prime Minister's job for one day in other countries too.

## A pet brings health to an elderly person

![a kitten in someone's lap](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9497029)

Open image viewer

It is healthy to own a cat. Photo: AOP

A pet is good for a person's health. A new Australian study says that an elderly person can live longer if they have a pet, for example a cat or a dog.

An elderly person's risk of dying from, for example, heart disease is smaller if they own a pet. During the study the researchers noticed that pet owners' risk of dying from heart disease was 26 percent smaller than for elderly people who did not have a pet. Pet owners move more, and they are also more social, meaning they meet other people more.
