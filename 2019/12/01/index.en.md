# Sunday 1.12.2019 (radio)

The new EU Commission has started work. Very few Finnish babies are put up for adoption. Many homes have a smoke alarm that does not work. Christmas parties keep the police busy. Advent has begun.

## Very few Finnish babies are adopted

![baby's toes](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11007709)

Open image viewer

A baby's toes. Photo: Martin Bennett / AOP

Finnish babies are very rarely put up for adoption. If you want to adopt a Finnish baby, you may have to wait 4 or 5 years, says the child welfare organisation Save the Children.

Save the Children handles about 70 per cent of adoption counselling in Finland. The organisation says that about 13 Finnish babies are adopted through it each year.

The organisation says that many families who hope for a child from abroad come to adoption counselling. However, the number of international adoptions has fallen since the early 2000s.

## Smoke alarm can break

![Smoke alarm](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5840230)

Open image viewer

A smoke alarm is fixed to the ceiling. Photo: Nilla Nuto / YLE

Many homes have a smoke alarm that does not work.

Many smoke alarms do not work because their batteries are flat. A smoke alarm can also be too old, warns SPEK, the central organisation of the Finnish rescue services.

A smoke alarm does not work forever. It gets dirty and can rust on the inside. Then the alarm no longer notices smoke as easily as before. The alarm sound can also become weaker than before.

SPEK says that a smoke alarm should be replaced every 5 to 10 years.

## Christmas parties keep police busy

![Police and a police car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10843965)

Open image viewer

The police have had a lot of work at the weekend. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The police have had a busy weekend. There have been many drunk drivers on the roads. In addition, there have been many fights and assaults.

In the Tampere region, for example, the police had about 150 call-outs during the night. In Central Finland there were over a hundred. The police have also been busy in the Turku region and in South-Eastern Finland.

Many people have celebrated Christmas parties over the weekend. When too much alcohol is drunk at parties, the number of fights and drunk drivers goes up.

## Advent candles were lit

Today is the first Sunday of Advent. Advent begins on the first Sunday of Advent. In Christianity, it means waiting for Christmas.

In Finnish churches today, the hymn Hoosianna has been sung and an Advent candle has been lit. Many homes also light an Advent candle.
