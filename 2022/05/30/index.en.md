# Monday 30.5.2022 (radio)

Ice hockey World Championship gold. World Championship celebrations. Labour shortage in the service sectors. The weather.

## World Championship gold

![Lions celebrate on the spot in the ice hockey World Championship final 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4210,h_2368,x_0,y_359/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9614426292909213e81/1653772490)

Finland is celebrating the ice hockey World Championship gold.

Finland became the new ice hockey world champion on Sunday. Then Finland beat Canada in the final, that is, the last game.

The final was very exciting. The 3rd period was well advanced when Finland led 3-1. Finland's win already seemed certain.

Right at the end of the 3rd period, however, Canada managed to score 2 goals, and the score was tied 3-3.

An extra period was still needed to decide the winner. In the extra period Finland scored a goal and won the final 4-3.

This was the 4th time Finland has won World Championship gold. This time the win felt very good, because it came in home competitions, that is, the World Championship was played in Finland.

The Finnish team, the Lions, won the World Championship gold in front of a home crowd in Tampere. After that the celebrations started in different parts of Finland.

On Monday evening the celebrations have continued in Tampere. It was decided that the Finnish Lions' official gold celebrations would be held in Tampere, where all the medal games of the World Championship were also played.

President Sauli Niinistö has congratulated the Finnish ice hockey team.

The President invited the world champions to visit Kultaranta. Kultaranta is the summer residence of the President of Finland. It is in Naantali, near Turku.

Ordinary Finns have celebrated the World Championship gold in their own way. Chief Superintendent Ismo Nykopp says that for some reason Finns want to climb high when they win.

This time too, people have climbed onto statues, roofs and poles. The police have helped people who have not been able to get down safely themselves. At least 1 person fell down on top of others, but was not seriously injured.

## Labour shortage in the service sectors

![A hotel customer speaks with a receptionist.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2880,h_1620,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6982155f08818eabcb5/1594394461)

Hotels, for example, are looking for workers. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There is a shortage of workers in the service sectors. About half of the firms in the service sectors say that it is difficult for them to find good workers. There are no workers, or the workers' skills are not enough.

For example, hotels and restaurants cannot find enough skilled people. There is also a shortage of good workers in administration and trade.

## The weather

A rain area arrives in the southern part of the country on Tuesday morning. In the afternoon it rains in many places in Southern and Central Finland.

In rainy areas the temperature is between 10 and 15 degrees. In Lapland the weather continues sunny and warm on Tuesday.

On Wednesday the rains spread to Lapland. In Southern and Central Finland there will only be rain showers.
