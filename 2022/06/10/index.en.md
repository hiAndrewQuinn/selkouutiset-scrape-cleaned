## LNG ship

![LNG tanker at sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5802,h_3263,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9624406295a6e3c1fe1/1653974833)

LNG tanker at sea. Photo: Marcin Bielecki / AOP

The LNG gas ship of Finland and Estonia will be placed in the port of Inkoo in Finland. Finland and Estonia have rented the ship together.

The gas tanker ship will come to Fortum's old coal port.

The ship receives liquefied natural gas, which is brought by ships. The liquid natural gas is turned back into gas and moved into Finland's gas network.

The gas ship is needed because natural gas is no longer brought from Russia.

## Drownings

![A man stands on the shore.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10310561)

Open image viewer

Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

In May, 7 people drowned in Finland, says the Finnish Swimming Instruction and Lifesaving Association.

2 drowned in water traffic, 1 while swimming, and in 4 cases it is not known how it happened.

The association reminds people about water safety during the summer.

A typical drowning person falls out of a boat or falls into the water when the boat capsizes. Many also misjudge their own swimming skills.

Important are proper preparation, life jackets, and being on the water without alcohol.

## Women entrepreneurs

![Clothing entrepreneur with Indian roots Pukhraj Ranjan works in Helsinki at the Lapinlahti hospital. Ranjan has a company called Moi Namaste.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_3,y_531/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96415762988c428059a/1654164907)

Open image viewer

Pukhraj Ranjan's clothing company Moi Namaste operates in the former Lapinlahti hospital. Photo: Vesa Marttinen / Yle

The City of Helsinki wants more immigrant-background women entrepreneurs.

Statistics already show a small growth.

In the capital region in 2020 there were almost 13,000 unemployed immigrant women. Now Helsinki encourages them to start companies and employ at least themselves.

Indian Pukhraj Ranjan started a small clothing company 2 years ago. Ranjan says that Helsinki has good help and support services for entrepreneurs.

However, Ranjan says the main focus of those services is on tech startups. A startup is a young company that wants to grow.

## Children's phones

![A child with a phone in hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7237881)

Open image viewer

Most of the phones of small children are smartphones.

In practice, every 7-year-old child has their own phone, says DNA's school children study. 87 percent of 5-6-year-olds have a phone.

Still last year, 60 percent of 5-6-year-olds had their own phone.

DNA has been researching children's phone use in Finland since 2014.

This year it was seen for the first time that 100 percent of first graders have a phone.
