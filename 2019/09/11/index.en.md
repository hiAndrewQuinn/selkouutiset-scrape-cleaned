## PAU: Posti may cut the wages of 9,000 workers

![Lorries at the Posti logistics centre in Tikkurila, Vantaa, on 2 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10955609)

Posti workers think that the wages of up to 9,000 workers could fall.

The postal and logistics union PAU says that attempts have been made to cut wages for 10 years already. During that time, Posti has tried to move workers to a cheaper collective agreement.

PAU says that this would mean about a 30 percent cut to basic wages alone. At the moment, the average monthly income of Posti workers is 2,100 euros.

The workers' organisation is ready for industrial action. This could happen if Posti's demands for pay cuts spread.

## Sentences for animal cruelty vary in different parts of the country

![Sentences for animal cruelty by region, and farms.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10958369)

Open image viewer

Photo: Asmo Raimoaho / Yle

Animal cruelty is punished in different ways in different parts of Finland. Some district courts did not give any sentences for animal cruelty last year. The most sentences are given in Pirkanmaa.

Yle went through all of last year's sentences that concerned farm animals. There were 61 sentences. The most common sentence was a fine.

Animal cruelty happens most often on small cattle farms. Often the background is a lack of money. Often the animal owners themselves are not doing well.

Researcher Tarja Koskela says that in some farms there may be half a metre of faeces, the food may be mouldy, there is a shortage of water and the animals are sick. Some animals may lie dead on the farms, because the owner has not had the energy to move them away.

## Charges have been brought for sexual crimes

![law books on a shelf](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10685716)

Open image viewer

The North Karelia District Court has given its ruling on a death caused by forcing someone into a sauna. Illustrative image. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The prosecutor has brought criminal charges for several sexual crimes. They came to light in Laajasalo in Helsinki.

The case involves 2 suspected perpetrators and 19 victims in total.

Almost all the victims were minors at the time of the crimes. The crimes took place between 2006 and 2019.

A 64-year-old man is charged with, among other things, 2 aggravated rapes. Among young people, the man was known by the name "Eno".

## Parliament deals with banning girls' genital mutilation

![Screenshot from the citizens' initiative website.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10812727)

Open image viewer

Screenshot of the citizens' initiative when the required limit of 50,000 signatures was reached. Photo: Kansalaisaloite.fi

Parliament has started to deal with an initiative that aims to ban female genital mutilation. A citizens' initiative with more than 61,000 signatures was handed to Parliament in June.

Finland's current law says that mutilation is aggravated assault. The people who handed in the citizens' initiative think the problem is that the people who carry out mutilation do not think of it as assault. For this reason, the law is not enough, the initiative says.

In the world, more than three million girls are still mutilated every year. The National Institute for Health and Welfare estimates that about 3,000 girls living in Finland are at risk of mutilation.
