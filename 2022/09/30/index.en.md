# Friday 30.9.2022 (radio)

Russia annexed areas of Ukraine. Finland closed the border to Russian tourists. Energy consumption fell. Olkiluoto 3 nuclear power plant reached full power. Weather forecast.

## Russia took Ukrainian areas

![Vladimir Putin met Russia's Security Council by video conference in the Kremlin on 29 September 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5808,h_3267,x_0,y_505/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1014743633664152e3c4/1664509348)

Russian President Vladimir Putin gave a speech on Friday afternoon. Photo: Gavriil Grigorov / Kremlin Pool / Sputnik / EPA

Russia has announced that it has annexed parts of Ukraine into Russia. Russian President Vladimir Putin gave a speech on the matter on Friday afternoon. Putin said that the Luhansk, Donetsk, Kherson and Zaporizhzhia regions are now part of Russia.

Putin said that people in the area voted in favour of this. Ukraine and other European countries do not accept Russia's announcement. The vote that Russia organised was not a real vote.

The chair of the European Commission, Ursula von der Leyen, says that the areas always belong to Ukraine.

Many countries have announced that they do not accept Russia taking areas of Ukraine. Finland also condemns Russia's announcement to annex parts of Ukraine into Russia.

## Finland closed the border to Russian tourists

![Vehicles at the Vaalimaa border crossing point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_333/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10150806336cfe032a88/1664536878)

Open image viewer

The new rules came into force right away on Friday night. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

Finland has closed the border to Russian tourists. Russians may come to Finland only for a special reason. A special reason is, for example, family, work or studies in Finland.

Russians may no longer come to Finland on a tourist trip. The new order came into force at midnight.

The Border Guard has turned away many Russians on Friday who have no right to enter Finland.

## Energy consumption fell

![Price sign at a petrol station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1909,h_1073,x_2,y_86/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10028296311fdfe0e3e8/1662123692)

Open image viewer

Petrol consumption has decreased because petrol has become more expensive. Photo: Jakob Lillas / Yle

Energy consumption has decreased in Finland. Energy consumption fell in the first half of the year, that is, January–June, by 10 percent compared with January–June last year.

The consumption of fuels, natural gas and wood fuels fell. Electricity consumption fell by about 5 percent.

Energy consumption has decreased, because the war in Ukraine has affected many things. For example, the import of natural gas from Russia has ended. Petrol has become more expensive, and therefore petrol consumption has also decreased.

## Olkiluoto 3 ran at full power

![Picture of a nuclear power plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3731,h_2099,x_2,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98273662d173ef59c96/1657894076)

Open image viewer

The new Olkiluoto 3 is the round building in the picture. Photo: Lucas Ekblad / Yle

The trial operation of the new Olkiluoto nuclear power plant has moved on to a new stage. The new Olkiluoto 3 nuclear power plant has reached full power for the first time. Raising the power to full power succeeded well.

The new Olkiluoto nuclear power plant is very important for Finland. Olkiluoto also has 2 older plants. They too operate at full power. The Olkiluoto nuclear power plants produce about 40 percent of Finland's electricity.

The new Olkiluoto 3 plant is meant to begin regular electricity production in December.
