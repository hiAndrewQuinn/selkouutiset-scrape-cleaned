# Tuesday 27.10.2020 (radio)

Finland has managed to slow the spread of coronavirus infections. The coronavirus era has affected Finns' exercise hobbies. A big demonstration against France has been held in Bangladesh. Wildfires are spreading in California.

## Spread of coronavirus infections

![Santeri Seppälä and two nurses at the Essote premises, where the movements of people with coronavirus infections are traced.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4904,h_2759,x_0,y_192/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7335755f92863b33b9b/1603438627)

Here, coronavirus infections are being traced at Mikkeli Central Hospital. Photo: Esa Huuhko / Yle

The spread of coronavirus infections has been slowed in Finland.

Coronavirus tests are used to find people who have a coronavirus infection. After that, everyone they have met is looked for.

The aim is to search for, i.e. trace, people who may have caught the coronavirus. Then care is taken that they do not spread the coronavirus further.

In this way, the spread of coronavirus infections has been slowed in different parts of Finland.

## Exercise during the coronavirus era

![Outdoor sports park Louhenpuisto in Lappeenranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2482,h_1396,x_0,y_853/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7305385f8848f2a5dcc/1602767401)

Open image viewer

Outdoor exercise has been popular during the coronavirus era. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

The coronavirus era has affected Finns' exercise hobbies.

A new study says that children's and young people's movement fell a lot in spring. At that time schools and many sports facilities were closed because of the coronavirus.

The coronavirus had less effect on exercise among the elderly in spring. Working-age people had less commuting exercise than before, but the amount of other exercise grew.

The coronavirus era has also changed the way people move. People have been moving more outdoors and less in indoor sports facilities.

## Demonstration against France

![Emmanuel Macron speaks at the school after the attack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4274,h_2404,x_0,y_160/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7314035f8aaa785125e/1602923216)

Open image viewer

French President Emmanuel Macron spoke after the teacher's murder. Photo: Abdulmonam Eassa

There are protests against France in Islamic countries.

For example, in Asia, in Bangladesh, there has been a big demonstration against France. More than 40,000 people took part in the demonstration.

Muslims are angry with the President of France, Emmanuel Macron. Macron has defended the teacher Samuel Paty, who was murdered in France.

History teacher Paty used Muhammad cartoons in his teaching. After that, a young Chechen man killed him.

Many countries have criticised Macron's speeches. For example, Turkey and Iran have been angry at France.
