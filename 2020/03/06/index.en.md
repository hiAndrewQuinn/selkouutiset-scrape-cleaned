## New coronavirus infections in Finland

![Huslab Tullinpuomi in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11243876)

Huslab Tullinpuomi in Helsinki. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Three new coronavirus infections have been found in Finland again.

An infection was found in a working-age man in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district. An infection has also been found in South Karelia.

Both infections come from Northern Italy. A third new infection was found in North Ostrobothnia.

In total 15 coronavirus infections have been found in Finland so far. The number may change all the time

## Coronavirus patients' dental care will be public

![A dentist cleans a patient's interdental space.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_69/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6477365e57c0cdec49a/1723809906)

Open image viewer

Coronavirus spreads, among other ways, by droplet infection, and the risk of infection is especially high in dental care. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

Private dentists may refuse to treat people who have the coronavirus.

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health has issued guidance on dental care. Urgent dental care for coronavirus patients will in practice be centralised in public dental care.

Dentists give guidance that appointments for non-urgent dental care must be cancelled if a person has caught the coronavirus. The risk of droplet infection is higher than usual in dental care.

## Misuse of opioids is increasing

![A Subutex seized by the police, in a bag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11244016)

Open image viewer

Subutex tablets. Photo: Police

Finland has more problem users of opioids than ever before.

Opioids are strong painkillers. They are also used as narcotics. Problem use of opioids has grown, especially among young people and women.

In 2012 there were about 15,000 opioid misusers. In 2017 it was estimated that there were almost 30,000 misusers.

Most of the growth is due to amphetamine users switching to opioids.

This is reported by the medical journal Duodecim.

## Fewer pedestrians have died in traffic

![A girl crossing the road](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10921312)

Open image viewer

Photo: Meeri Niinistö / Yle

Pedestrian deaths in traffic have decreased in Finland. Fewer pedestrians died in traffic last year than in any year in statistical history.

The Accident Data Institute says that last year 12 (twelve) pedestrians died. That is 10 fewer than in 2018.

However, 27 cyclists died. That is 8 more than in 2018.

## Tove Jansson may get a flag day

![Tove Jansson's self-portrait from 1941.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10181225)

Open image viewer

Tove Jansson's self-portrait from 1941. Photo: Paula Tiainen / Yle

The writer and visual artist Tove Jansson and Finnish art may get their own flag day. The Ministry of the Interior recommends that the flag day be 9 August.

The ministry is following how popular the flag day is. If many flags are flown in Finland on 9 August, the day may get into the calendar.

Jansson is best known for the Moomin books, but she was also a respected painter and illustrator.
