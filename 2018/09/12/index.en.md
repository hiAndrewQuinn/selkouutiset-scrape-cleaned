# Wednesday 12.9.2018 (radio)

Migri no longer returns families with children to Kabul who come from elsewhere in Afghanistan. Iraqi authorities are coming to Finland to issue passports. Pupils experience sexual harassment in many schools. New fast train connections are planned for Finland.

## Returns to Afghanistan

![Migration Service service point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10399802)

Open image viewer

Migri is again making decisions for Afghans. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The Finnish Immigration Service, that is, Migri, has given new information about the situation in Afghanistan. Migri says that the situation in Kabul, the capital of Afghanistan, is now worse than before.

That is why Migri will no longer return families with children to Kabul who come from elsewhere in Afghanistan. However, healthy unmarried men and childless married couples can be returned to Kabul, if there is no obstacle.

Migri is again starting to make decisions on Afghan asylum applications. Migri had suspended making decisions while it assessed the situation.

## Iraqis' passports

![Asylum seekers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9135167)

Open image viewer

There are Iraqis in Finland who do not have a passport. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Iraqi authorities are coming to Finland to issue Iraqi passports. Hundreds of Iraqis live in Finland who do not have a passport. Because of this, they cannot, for example, work legally.

Iraqi authorities will start issuing passports in Finland in about a week. The application point will be in the Helsinki region, and it will operate for a couple of weeks.

This was said by Finland's Minister of the Interior, Kai Mykkänen.

## Sexual harassment in schools

![A child looks at a smartphone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10385016)

Open image viewer

Sexual harassment can be abusive language. Photo: AOP

Pupils experience sexual harassment in many schools. The harassment can be, for example, nasty or abusive speech.

There are differences between schools. In some schools, pupils experience more sexual harassment than in other schools.

Associate professor Kristiina Brunila says that the matter is not about the amount of harassment. The differences between schools show that in some schools the teachers notice harassment. They also know how to intervene in the problem.

According to Brunila, all teachers need training so that they can better intervene in sexual harassment.

## Fast train connections

![Pendolino and IC2 train at the Turku rail yard depot.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9702539)

Open image viewer

Fast connections are hoped for between Helsinki, Turku and Tampere. Photo: Jouni Koutonen / Yle

New fast train connections are being planned for Finland.

The idea is that you can travel from Helsinki to Turku and Tampere by train in one hour. Nowadays the travel time is about 2 hours.

On Tuesday a report on train connections was published. According to the report, fast train connections bring benefits to the economy. Nature also does better when car use decreases.

If the new train connections are built, a lot of time and money will be needed. Years of time are needed, and billions of euros of money are needed.

No decisions have been made yet.
