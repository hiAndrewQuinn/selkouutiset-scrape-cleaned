# Wednesday 2.12.2020 (radio)

Britain starts coronavirus vaccinations. Finland has 9 new coronavirus deaths. Yle has restarted multilingual coronavirus news. The state is also sending coronavirus information by text message. The Finnish women's team beat Scotland in a European Championship qualifier.

## Coronavirus vaccinations in Britain

![A flu vaccine being given.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11650518)

Britain is also starting to vaccinate against the coronavirus. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Britain is starting coronavirus vaccinations. Britain is the first country in Europe to start coronavirus vaccinations.

Britain's vaccinations will start next week. Britain will vaccinate the residents and staff of care homes first. After that, other older people will be vaccinated.

The British government has given permission to use the coronavirus vaccine made by the drug companies Pfizer and BioNTech.

The European Union has not yet given permission for any coronavirus vaccine.

## Finland's coronavirus figures

There have been 9 new coronavirus deaths in Finland. In total, 408 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

On Wednesday it was also reported that there are 420 new coronavirus infections in Finland. Almost 26,000 coronavirus infections have been found in Finland in total.

Most coronavirus infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa area.

## Multilingual coronavirus news

![Text in Arabic saying coronavirus information in Arabic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11268613)

Open image viewer

Photo: Maiju Hakalahti / Yle

Many people who speak other languages have caught the coronavirus in Finland. People who speak other languages have a first language other than Finnish or Swedish.

However, it has been hard for people who speak other languages to get information about the coronavirus. That is why Yle has restarted multilingual coronavirus news.

The languages of Yle's coronavirus news are Arabic, Kurdish, Somali and Persian. Yle also made multilingual coronavirus news in the spring, during the bad coronavirus situation.

Coronavirus news in different languages can be found at yle.fi/coronainfo. Corona is written with the letter c in the address.

Finland's residents also receive coronavirus information by text message.

The message comes from the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health. The ministry starts sending messages today, Wednesday.

The message asks people to follow the coronavirus guidelines. Messages are sent in Finnish, Swedish, Sami and English.

This is the 3rd time during the coronavirus crisis that the state has sent text messages.

## Finland's football win

![Amanda Rantanen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11676564)

Open image viewer

Amanda Rantanen scored Finland's winning goal. Photo: AOP

Finland has won an important victory in football.

The Finnish women's team beat Scotland in a European Championship qualifier on Tuesday. Finland won 1-0 against Scotland.

The win came when Amanda Rantanen got past Scotland's defence and scored the game's only goal.

Finland also had some luck in the situation. The ball bounced off Rantanen and into Scotland's goal.

Finland now has a good chance of getting to the European Championship finals, that is, to compete for the European title.
