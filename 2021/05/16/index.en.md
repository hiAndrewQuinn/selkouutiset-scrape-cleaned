The Middle East. A poll on Marin. European corona restrictions. Rock thrush.

## Middle East

![Men dig through the rubble.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5669,h_3189,x_0,y_354/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80667260a1024d66a00/1621164682)

Israel has carried out more strikes on Gaza. At least 30 people died in the air strikes on Sunday. The Palestinian militant group Hamas has fired rockets into Israel.

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu says that Israel will continue its strikes.

The fighting between Israel and the Palestinians has lasted for a week. At least 180 Palestinians and 10 Israelis have died in the fighting in total.

UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres says that the violence must stop.

More than half of people think that Prime Minister Sanna Marin has handled the corona crisis well. 57 percent are satisfied. This is what a poll by Helsingin Sanomat shows.

However, people are not as satisfied with the prime minister as before. In March last year, 84 percent of people thought that Sanna Marin was handling the corona crisis well.

## European corona restrictions

Travel and restaurants are gradually opening up in Europe. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

Many European countries are easing a little the restrictions they have had because of corona.

For example, Italy can now be entered from all EU countries without quarantine. A corona test must be negative. Tourists can also go to Portugal from almost all EU countries.

In France, museums, theatres, cinemas and concert halls may open again. The venues have been closed for 6 weeks. At the same time, France is opening restaurant terraces.

Spain also has milder restrictions than before. For example, people are again allowed to spend time together. However, in Barcelona the police broke up a large crowd that was celebrating the end of the restrictions.

## Rock thrush

![Finland's first rock thrush.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80667460a10720a31db/1621167290)

Open image viewer

The rock thrush is a very rare sight in Europe. Photo: Riitta Mattsson

A rare bird has been seen in Finland. In Kemiönsaari in Southwest Finland a rock thrush was seen. A rock thrush has never before been seen in Finland.

The rock thrush breeds in an area that starts in Turkey and ends on the west side of the Himalayas. For the winter the bird migrates to East Africa. Rock thrushes are only rarely seen in Europe.
