## Corona situation and flights from China

![Family and Basic Services Minister Krista Kiuru.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_446/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105656063b92ac8b40dd/1673079701)

Family and Basic Services Minister Krista Kiuru was a guest on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme on Saturday. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Family and Basic Services Minister Krista Kiuru says that Finland wants to fight a possible new wave of the coronavirus. Kiuru reminds that corona patients have burdened health care during the autumn.

Kiuru says that not enough information has been obtained about the corona situation in China and about the new variant of the virus. That is why corona restrictions are needed for passengers on flights from China.

Passengers on flights from China will soon be required to show a negative corona test certificate. Kiuru says that the order will come into force as soon as possible. Family and Basic Services Minister Krista Kiuru was a guest on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme on Saturday.

## Congestion in emergency departments

![On the wall of a grey building it says Tays emergency Acuta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_489/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1047960639987b134bf8/1671006231)

Open image viewer

There has been congestion in emergency departments in many areas. That is why waiting times can be very long. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

Congestion in health care emergency departments continues. The emergency departments are burdened by, for example, a shortage of follow-up care places for elderly people. There are too few follow-up care places. That is why patients arrive at emergency departments who cannot be treated elsewhere.

For example, the Northern Ostrobothnia wellbeing services county intends to reduce service housing places and hospital places, because the wellbeing services county wants to save money.

Other wellbeing services counties are also considering ways to save money.

![Kevin McCarthy with his hands spread out.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4553,h_2561,x_0,y_268/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105641963b72c8f6f437/1672948992)

The new Speaker is Republican Kevin McCarthy. Photo: Michael Reynolds / EPA

The US House of Representatives has elected a new Speaker. The new Speaker is the Republican **Kevin McCarthy.**

Choosing the Speaker was unusually difficult and the election took 5 days. The US House of Representatives voted for the Speaker 15 times.

The House of Representatives makes laws in the USA. The Speaker of the House of Representatives is the third highest office in the US administration. Only the president and vice president are higher offices.

## Electricity subsidy

![Electricity bill from Tampere's electricity utility.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4259,h_2395,x_10,y_42/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1031122636a54c17e08f/1680684775)

Open image viewer

Electricity subsidy can be received for an electricity bill of over 400 euros. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

Kela has published a form with which one can apply for support for expensive electricity bills. Support is granted to people on low incomes.

Electricity subsidy can be received for January, February and March. Kela's electricity subsidy can be received if the electricity bill is over 400 euros a month. 60 percent of the amount over 400 euros can be received as support.

An electricity bill must be attached to the application. The electricity subsidy application form has been published on Kela's website.

## The weather

Snow will come from the west on Sunday.

The winter frost record has been broken in Muonio. At the observation station in Muonio village centre there was a frost of 34.8 degrees on Saturday morning.

On Sunday it is cloudy. In the west it begins to snow. Right in the east the sun may still shine at times. The temperature is between -5 and -15 degrees.
