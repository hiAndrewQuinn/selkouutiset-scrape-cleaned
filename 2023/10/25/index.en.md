# Radio Clear News Wednesday 25.10.2023

Conscript dies in accident. Stabbing in Kitee. Protest at the pulp mill in Kemi. Racism. E-cigarettes. The weather.

## Conscript dies in accident

![Map of the accident site between Kouvola and Lappeenranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11908376538ce29bf4b9/1698221690)

Map of the accident site between Kouvola and Lappeenranta. Photo: Mari Siltanen / Yle, ©Mapcreator.io | OSM.org

A Defence Forces vehicle was in a bad crash on Wednesday morning. 1 conscript died and 3 were injured in the accident on Highway 6. The accident site was near the border of Kouvola and Luumäki.

The Finnish Defence Forces say that the vehicle in the accident belonged to the Pori Brigade and was taking part in an exercise. The off-road vehicle skidded into a ditch and overturned.

## Stabbing in Kitee

![Kupiainen shopping centre in Kitee.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_191/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-118999765364ae0ab427/1698056984)

Open image viewer

In Kitee, at the Kupiainen shopping centre, a stabbing took place in which a minor stabbed another. Photo: Ari Haimakainen / Yle

In Kitee in eastern Finland, a stabbing took place on Sunday. The people involved are 14 years old. The stabbing happened in the shopping centre.

The police say that the boys knew each other, but they were not in the same group of friends. The victim of the stabbing was taken to hospital for treatment. The police are investigating the case as attempted murder.

## Protest at the pulp mill in Kemi

![Greenpeace protest at the gate of the Metsä Fibre bioproduct mill in Kemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4905,h_2759,x_0,y_308/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11910106538e9d1794b0/1736325028)

Open image viewer

Greenpeace protesting in front of the Metsä Fibre bioproduct mill in Kemi. Photo: Antti Ullakko / Yle

There was a protest at the pulp mill in Kemi. The protest blocked timber deliveries to the Metsä Group mill area. Several protesters got inside the mill area. There they chained themselves to the timber cranes.

The environmental organisation Greenpeace says that more than 30 people from several countries took part in the protest. Greenpeace demands that the forest industry reduce logging and the use of wood.

## Racism has increased

![Protesters in front of the Parliament House.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_114/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114489964b80b575ae25/1689784165)

Open image viewer

Anti-racism "Zero Tolerance" protest on 19 July 2023. Photo: Susanna Pesonen / Yle

Black people in Europe experience more racism than before. The survey was made last year among people of African background in 13 EU countries.

More than 30 percent of respondents said that they had experienced racism during the year. The figure rose by 10 percentage points from the previous survey, which was made 6 years ago.

In Finland, Black people experience a lot of racism. Only in Germany and Austria did people experience more racism than in Finland.

## E-cigarettes

![Two pictures side by side. One hand holds a cigarette, and in the other picture a hand holds an e-cigarette.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_323/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11835286523e75eef937/1696852187)

E-cigarettes are no healthier than traditional smoking, international research says. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle, Jaani Lampinen / Yle

Young people are smoking more e-cigarettes than before.

6 percent of boys and 5 percent of girls under 20 smoke e-cigarettes regularly. The use of e-cigarettes among girls has increased faster than among boys.

Daily smoking among all Finns fell slightly last year. Of Finns under 65, 11 percent smoke every day.
