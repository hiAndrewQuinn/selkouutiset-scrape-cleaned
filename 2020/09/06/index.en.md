## Traffic fines got stuck in the post

![Speed camera along the road from Porvoo to Kouvola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11361631)

Open image viewer

An automatic traffic camera measures speeds, and for speeding the police send the driver a traffic fine. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The police say that many thousands of traffic fines have got stuck in the post.

A driver who has been speeding may have to pay a traffic fine, that is a penalty for speeding.

Now the traffic fines may have been delayed in the post for as long as a month.

The driver may not have been able to pay the fine on time at all.

The post says that it did not get the material from the police on time, and therefore it could not send the traffic fines straight away.

A driver who has received a fine can ask for a correction.

## Opposition leader of Belarus Svetlana Tsikhanouskaya

![According to Svetlana Tsikhanouskaya, the popular movement seething on the streets of Belarus is developing into a real political change.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11529663)

Open image viewer

Belarusian opposition leader Svetlana Tsikhanouskaya is in exile in Lithuania. Photo: Jyri Pitkänen

Big demonstrations continue in Belarus.

The demonstrators demand that the country's president, Alexander Lukashenko, resigns.

The symbolic leader of the demonstrations is opposition leader Svetlana Tsikhanouskaya. She is in exile in Lithuania.

A Yle journalist met Tsikhanouskaya. Tsikhanouskaya says that the struggle of the demonstrators continues.

\- There is only one wish of our people to build a new country.

Belarusian opposition leader Svetlana Tsikhanouskaya says that Belarusians want to build a new country without President Alexander Lukashenko.

## Max Seeck is doing well

![Max Seeck, Helsinki, 28.08.2020](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5333,h_3000,x_0,y_508/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7131985f490676ca09e/1598621970)

Open image viewer

Max Seeck writes crime novels, that is, detective books where the main character is a police officer who investigates crimes. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

Now a culture news item. Max Seeck is a Finnish writer who is doing well now.

He writes crime novels, that is, detective books. The main character of the books is a police officer called Jessica Niemi.

Max Seeck has written 5 books in 4 years and they have all done well.

The biggest news is that his book may become a TV series in the United States. The writer Max Seeck is still a bit careful, however.

He says that nothing is ever certain in Hollywood.

The weather is very rainy now.

Tomorrow, Monday, there will be showers across the whole country.

Now and then the sun may peek out from behind the clouds.

The temperature in most of the country is between 18 and 14 degrees. Far in the north it is about 10 degrees warmer.
