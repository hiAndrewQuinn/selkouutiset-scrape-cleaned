# Thursday 25.1.2018 (radio)

Sauli Niinistö is still the most popular presidential candidate. The Finnish government has dismissed Prosecutor General Matti Nissinen. There has been a train accident in Italy. Yle's CEO Lauri Kivinen is resigning. Driving conditions have been slippery in Ostrobothnia.

## Support for the presidential candidates

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10038945)

Open image viewer

Sauli Niinistö is the most popular presidential candidate in Yle's survey. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

The presidential election is on Sunday.

Yle's new survey shows that Sauli Niinistö is the most popular candidate. More than 60 percent of those who answered plan to vote for Niinistö. Niinistö is the candidate of a voters' association.

The other candidates get much less support. The second most popular candidate is Pekka Haavisto of the Greens. 14 percent of those who answered plan to vote for Haavisto.

The support of the other candidates is well below 10 percent.

Based on the survey, it looks certain that Sauli Niinistö will continue as President of Finland.

## The Prosecutor General is fired

![Matti Nissinen at the hearing of his charges of misconduct in office at the Supreme Court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10041155)

Open image viewer

Matti Nissinen was fired from the job of Prosecutor General. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Finnish government has dismissed Prosecutor General Matti Nissinen. The Prosecutor General is an important person in Finland's legal system. He leads the prosecution service.

The government thought that Nissinen can no longer handle all the tasks of the Prosecutor General. The Prosecutor General must be trustworthy, but in December Nissinen was convicted of misconduct in office.

The sentence was a fine. Nissinen got a fine because he bought training for his workplace from his own brother.

The Ministry of Justice proposed dismissing Nissinen. Now the Ministry of Justice is looking for a suitable person to do the important work of Prosecutor General.

## Train accident in Italy

![A train that came off the rails.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10040022)

Some of the carriages of a local train came off the rails. Photo: Flavio Loscalzo / EPA

There has been a train accident in Italy. At least 3 people died. Many people were injured.

In the accident, some of the carriages of a local train came off the rails. The accident happened near Milan, in northern Italy.

The cause of the accident is not yet known.

![Lauri Kivinen at a Yle press conference, Lauri's info 25.01.2018](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10040871)

Yle's CEO Lauri Kivinen is resigning. Kivinen will stop working as CEO during this year.

Yle has television channels, radio channels and websites. The money for its operations comes from the Yle tax.

The CEO is Yle's most important leader. Kivinen has been Yle's CEO for 8 years.

## Slippery roads

![A bus that slid off a slippery road into a ditch is lifted in Mustasaari on 25.1.2018](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10040659)

Open image viewer

This is what it was like on the roads of Ostrobothnia on Thursday. Photo: Jarkko Heikkinen / Yle

Roads have been slippery in different parts of Finland. For example, in Ostrobothnia the roads were covered with thin ice on Thursday morning.

Many lorries drove off the slippery road. The Ostrobothnia police say that there were many lorry accidents.

Bus traffic also had difficulties. Many buses drove into a ditch. Some of the buses waited at the stop for gritting trucks. They continued their journey only when there was sand on the road.
