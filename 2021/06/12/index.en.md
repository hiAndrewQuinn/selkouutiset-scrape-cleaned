# Saturday 12.6.2021 (radio)

On Sunday it is election day for the municipal elections. About 50 percent of Finns have received a coronavirus vaccination. The European Football Championship has started. It will rain at the weekend.

On Sunday it is election day for the municipal elections. Polling places open in the morning at 9, and they close in the evening at 8 pm.

On election day a voter can vote only at their own polling place. Their own polling place is written on the voter's notification. The voter's notification has come to the voter's home by post.

In the municipal elections, people are chosen to the municipal council. The municipal council decides on the municipality's affairs. The municipal council decides, for example, what the municipality uses tax money for.

There are about 300 municipalities in Finland. Municipal elections are held every 4 years.

## Coronavirus vaccinations progress

Coronavirus vaccinations are progressing well.

About 50 percent of Finns have received the first coronavirus vaccination. That means that almost 2.8 million people have received a coronavirus vaccination in Finland.

The coronavirus vaccine is given twice. About 700,000 Finns have already received both coronavirus vaccinations.

It is good to take the coronavirus vaccine twice, because then it gives a very good protection against coronavirus.

The football European Championship has started.

The European Championship lasts a month. The European football champion will be decided in the final, which is played on 11 July.

Finland took part in the football European Championship for the first time. 24 countries are taking part.

Finland plays in the same group, that is, pool, as Belgium, Denmark and Russia. The best 2 countries in the group will certainly advance to the next round.

Next Wednesday Finland plays Russia, and a week later on Monday Belgium. Yle shows the matches on Yle Areena and on TV2.

The weather changes during the weekend.

Rain comes to Finland from the west. It will pass over Finland during the night between Saturday and Sunday. In the east it will still rain on Sunday.

After the rain the weather gets cooler. On Sunday the temperature is below 20 degrees.

Monday is a dry day, that is, it will no longer rain.
