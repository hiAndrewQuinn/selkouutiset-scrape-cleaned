# Friday 9.9.2016 (radio)

The Immigration Service wants to sort out its problems. Yle turns 90. A minister wants Fennovoima's safety matters put in order. A serious train accident in Spain. Sweden's little prince got a name.

## The Immigration Service wants to sort out its problems

![Director General of the Immigration Service Jaana Vuorio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9154890)

Open image viewer

Director General of the Immigration Service Jaana Vuorio.  Photo: Yle

The Director General of the Immigration Service says that the agency's matters will be properly investigated. Workers at the Immigration Service have said that the hard pace of work puts the fair handling of asylum decisions at risk.  Director General Jaana Vuorio says that many new workers have come to the Immigration Service, and they feel a lot of pressure in their work. On Thursday, demonstrators protested against the agency's actions in Helsinki.

The head of the Immigration Service says that the new workers have short training, but they have a lot of responsibility. At the moment, the Immigration Service makes 700 asylum decisions a week.

## Yle is now 90 years old

![Yle's logo and a balloon.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7333228)

Open image viewer

Yle is a lively 90-year-old media company. Photo: Kata Laurikainen / Yle

The Finnish Broadcasting Company Yle turns 90 today. Yle is the radio of the whole nation, that is, all Finns are owners of Yle. Yle's radio and television programmes and websites are a public service. That means that Yle's costs are paid with the Yle tax, and Yle does not show advertisements.

Finns like Yle's programmes. Last year, Yle's television channels had almost 3 million viewers every day, the radio has 2 million listeners, and online there are over 620,000 visitors.

Yle's birthday can be seen and heard on Yle's channels today and tomorrow in many ways.

## Fennovoima must put its safety matters in order

![Olli Rehn](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9106721)

Open image viewer

Minister of Economic Affairs Olli Rehn.  Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Minister of Economic Affairs Olli Rehn says that the builders of Fennovoima's nuclear power plant must improve safety. Rehn says that the project has bad shortcomings and delays. The minister reminded that safety matters must be 100 percent in order, otherwise the nuclear power plant cannot be given a permit. Things must improve already this autumn.

Rehn met the leaders of the Radiation and Nuclear Safety Authority today. The Radiation and Nuclear Safety Authority has said that there is a lot of room for improvement in Fennovoima's safety matters.

## People have died in a train accident in Spain

![A train carriage turned into a heap of metal against a tilted electricity pole. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9155938)

Open image viewer

The train driver died in the accident. Photo: Salvador Sas / EPA

There has been a serious train accident in Spain. At least 4 people died and dozens of people were injured when a passenger train came off the rails. The accident happened in north-western Spain near the town of O Porrino.

The train was on its way to Portugal. The 1st carriage of the train came off the rails and hit an electricity pole by the track. The section of track at the accident site is straight. The cause of the accident is not known.

## Sweden's little prince was christened

![A close-up of a smiling Alexander in the arms of his father, Prince Carl Philip.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9151725)

Open image viewer

The young prince in his father's arms. Photo: Kate Gabor / The Swedish Royal Court

The Swedish royals have a big celebration today. The first child of Prince Carl Philip and Princess Sofia has been christened, that is, the child has been given a name and made a member of the Christian church.

The youngest family member of the Swedish royal court is called Prince Alexander, and his title is duke.

Experts say that the royal family's new children have increased the popularity of the royals in Sweden.
