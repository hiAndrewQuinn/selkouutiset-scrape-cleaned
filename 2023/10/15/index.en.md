## Gaza refugees

![An Israeli tank and soldier in silhouette at traffic lights.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4890,h_2750,x_0,y_477/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11857466528bde8e1076/1697169934)

An estimated million people in Gaza have had to leave their homes because of the conflict between Israel and the Hamas terrorists, says UNRWA, the UN agency for Palestinian refugees. The agency believes that the number of refugees is still growing.

Israel has bombed Hamas targets in the Gaza Strip since Hamas attacked Israel a week ago. Israel is also preparing for a ground attack on the Gaza Strip.

Israel has told people to leave the area of a ground attack in the northern part of the Gaza Strip. More than a million people live in the area.

## Earthquake in Afghanistan

![People moving goods in the ruins.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5052,h_2841,x_0,y_125/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-118440965260ab39d9c0/1711628786)

Local residents cleared up the traces of the earthquake in Herat province a week ago. Photo: Mohsen Karimi / EPA

There has been another earthquake in Afghanistan.

The earthquake happened early in the morning near the city of Herat. Its strength was 6.3.

It is not yet known whether there were any deaths or damage.

This was already the third strong earthquake in Afghanistan in a month. In the earlier earthquakes more than 1000 people died.

The passenger ship Viking Grace hit the quay structures in the port of Turku yesterday evening.

The ship hit a lamp post, which fell down. No one was injured, however.

The collision was caused by strong wind. The wind blew in gusts of almost 20 metres per second.

## Children's movement

![Pupils on a climbing frame in the schoolyard during break.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1439,h_809,x_0,y_419/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11855106527e890714e6/1697114369)

Open image viewer

The movement of many children decreases when they start school. Photo: Mika Kanerva / Yle

Children and young people move less than before.

The movement of children and young people has been decreasing in Finland for a long time. In particular, those who have already moved little before are now moving even less, says the University of Jyväskylä.

Children under school age usually move enough. However, movement decreases when children start school. Only 3 percent of upper secondary school students now move enough.

According to the Finnish movement recommendation, school-age children and young people should ideally move for an hour every day.
