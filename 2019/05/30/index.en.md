# Thursday 30.5.2019 (radio)

A river boat capsized on the Danube. Prisoners are being rehabilitated to live without crime. Fewer people are applying to train as primary school teachers. The first strawberries have ripened. Ascension Day is an important day in Christianity.

## A new rehabilitation programme for prisoners

![In 2006 supervised parole was introduced into the law.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10809029)

Open image viewer

The first 15 minutes are important when a prisoner is released. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

A new rehabilitation programme for prisoners has been introduced in Finnish prisons. In the programme, prisoners are taught how to live after their time in prison without committing crimes.

Prisoners are taught, for example, to live without intoxicants, that is, without alcohol and drugs. They are taught how to manage and control anger. They discuss the values of life and relationships with them.

The authorities hope that when prisoners learn better life management, they will no longer commit new crimes.

Crime costs society about one billion euros a year.

## A river boat capsized in Hungary

![Firefighters prepare to search for victims by rubber boat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10809202)

Open image viewer

Firefighters are searching the Danube for missing people. Photo: Zsolt Szigetvary / EPA

A serious boat accident has happened in Hungary. A river boat capsized on Wednesday evening on the Danube in Budapest.

Only 7 people have been rescued from the water. Almost 30 people may have died. The authorities say that hardly anyone alive will be found in the river now.

The victims of the accident are South Korean tourists.

## Teaching does not attract people

![Classroom at Konala primary school](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10191699)

Open image viewer

Work as a class teacher no longer interests people as much as before. Photo: Elise Tykkyläinen / Yle

Interest in the profession of class teacher has fallen a lot.

Helsingin Sanomat reports that 6 years ago, in 2013, about 8,300 people applied for class teacher training. This year there were about 4,700 applicants, which is almost half as many.

Experts say in Helsingin Sanomat that interest in the teaching profession may have fallen because only bad and negative news about the school world is reported nowadays.

## The first strawberries have ripened

![Portrait](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_188/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5670455ceeb0434235c/1559146692)

Strawberry picking began on Ascension Day at Emmi Pekkala's strawberry farm. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

The first home-grown strawberries have ripened. The first strawberries of the summer have ripened in greenhouses and grow tunnels.

For example, at the Pekkala farm in Kärkölä, strawberry picking began today. The first berries are to be put on sale so that Finns can enjoy fresh strawberries on Saturday, when schools end and many homes celebrate, for example, the matriculation.

If the weather in the coming weeks is warm, open-field strawberries may be on sale in mid-June.

## Ascension Day is a holiday

![church christianity cross church candle INRI Jesus on the cross](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9112329)

Open image viewer

Christians believe that Jesus ascended into heaven on Ascension Day. Photo: Soila Ojanen / Yle

Ascension Day has been celebrated today.

Ascension Day is a public holiday in Finland. On public holidays schools and many workplaces are closed, and people have a day off.

Ascension Day is an important day in Christianity. It is the day when Jesus ascended into heaven. Ascension Day is always celebrated 40 days after Easter.
