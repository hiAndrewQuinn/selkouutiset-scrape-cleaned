# Saturday 16.3.2019 (radio)

The residents of New Zealand are mourning the victims of the terror attack. A severe storm has killed people in Africa. Father's Day is now an official flag day. Liquorice is not suitable for people with high blood pressure. The weather is rainy.

## New Zealand's grief

![Residents bring messages of remembrance and flowers for the victims of the terror attack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10692715)

People have laid flowers in memory of the victims of the terror attack. Photo: Mick Tsikas / EPA

The residents of New Zealand are mourning the people who died in Friday's terror attack. New Zealanders cannot understand how a terror attack can happen in such a small and peaceful country.

The terror attack was also a shock to New Zealand's Muslims. Muslims say that they have never had to be afraid in New Zealand. They have always been treated well.

The terror attack targeted 2 mosques during Friday prayers. The shooter killed about 50 people. He is an extreme right-wing racist.

New Zealand intends to change its gun laws. Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern says that buying guns will be made harder.

## Storm in Africa

![Cyclone Idai in a NASA satellite image over Mozambique on Friday, 15 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10692877)

Open image viewer

Satellite image of the storm in Africa. Photo: NASA

A severe storm has killed people in Africa.

About 20 people died when the storm hit Mozambique on Friday. Even before that, people had died because of heavy rain.

On Saturday the storm moved from Mozambique to Zimbabwe. More than 20 people died there. The number of dead may be higher, because many people are missing.

## Flag-raising on Father's Day

![Finnish flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9229020)

Open image viewer

On flag days the flag is raised up the flagpole. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Father's Day is now an official flag day. On Father's Day it has been customary to fly the flag, but Father's Day has not been an official flag day, the way Mother's Day has.

Interior Minister Kai Mykkänen says that Father's Day and Mother's Day are now equal flag days. It is a message that mother and father are equally important in the family nowadays.

Father's Day is celebrated in Finland on the second Sunday of November. This year Father's Day is on 10 November.

## Liquorice

![Liquorice.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9453941)

Open image viewer

Liquorice is good, but it is not suitable for people with high blood pressure. Photo: AOP

Liquorice is unhealthy for many people. Liquorice raises blood pressure. That is why it is not good for a person to eat liquorice if they have high blood pressure.

Researcher Elina Hautaniemi says that a bag of liquorice raises blood pressure as much as blood pressure medicine lowers it. Liquorice can affect blood pressure for many weeks.

So liquorice is not suitable for a person with high blood pressure. But a healthy person can eat liquorice sometimes, if their blood pressure is normal.
