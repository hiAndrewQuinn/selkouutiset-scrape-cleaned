## Mobile phones at school

![A child and a phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4160,h_2340,x_0,y_910/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6408895e414c46604d8/1581337914)

Many people want children to leave their mobile phones out of lessons. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Finns want teachers to be able to ban mobile phones in class during lessons. In a Yle survey, 85 percent say that mobile phone use in lessons should be restricted more.

Many people think that pupils do not learn if they are allowed to use a mobile phone freely in lessons.

However, the law says that pupils also have their own rights. That is why mobile phones can only be collected if the pupils agree. A teacher can also take a pupil's mobile phone away if the phone disturbs other pupils.

The government intends to change the law so that banning mobile phones in schools becomes easier.

## Storm

![Lightning strikes in the sky among the treetops.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1909,h_1077,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115285364d05efa62b9a/1691380980)

There may be a strong thunderstorm on Tuesday. Photo: Antti Haavisto / Yle

A dangerous storm may come to Finland early on Tuesday morning. The storm comes from the south, and with it there may be very heavy rain and wind. The wind may be so strong that it knocks down trees. If trees fall on power lines, they may cause power cuts.

Electricity companies say that they are prepared to repair power cuts.

In cities, water may flood the street if rain fills the drains.

When a storm comes, it is worth moving things in the yard and the car to a safe place. During a storm it is not a good idea to go to places with a lot of trees. Trees may fall on you. Do not go out on the water.

## Bank fraud

![Sign reading District Court on the wall of a building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_625/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1124910647dc8c7c9469/1685965343)

Open image viewer

Helsinki District Court. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

A young man has been sentenced in court for bank fraud. The 21-year-old man was given five years in prison.

The man defrauded S-Pankki customers. Last year he sent text messages to many hundreds of people, which looked like text messages from S-Pankki. Some S-Pankki customers told him their online banking credentials.

The man stole 2.4 million euros from S-Pankki customers.

On Tuesday morning rain and thunderstorms come from the south. In the afternoon they spread to the central part of Finland. The rain and thunderstorms may be heavy.

The temperature is between 19 and 29 degrees.

Elsewhere, except in Lapland, there are warnings of strong winds and severe thunder.
