# Saturday 1.8.2020 (radio)

Many corona restrictions ended on Saturday. Researchers are developing a corona vaccine. A Finnish mother has come back to Finland with her children from the al-Hol camp. The beer floating event was on the Vantaa river again. The weather is rainy.

TV Clear News has a summer break and it will continue on 17.8.
Radio Clear News is made every day, also in the summer.

## Corona restrictions are being removed

![Provinssi 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10852824)

This was a summer event one year ago. This year the big events have been cancelled, but now they may be held again. Photo: Mirva Ekman / Yle

Many corona restrictions ended on Saturday, that is, on 1 August.

For example, big public events can again be held. At events, there may now be more than 500 people.

The corona restrictions have worked well in Finland. Only a few new corona infections have come. That is why the corona restrictions can now be lifted.

If more corona infections start to come, the government may tighten the corona restrictions again.

The corona situation in Finland is still good. On Saturday, 11 new corona infections were reported.

## A corona vaccine is being developed

![A close-up of a nurse's gloved hand giving a vaccine injection into a man's hairy upper arm. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11136428)

A corona vaccine is being developed in many countries. Photo: Sabastien Nogier / EPA

Researchers in different countries are trying to develop a vaccine against corona. The coronavirus can be stopped only with the help of a vaccine.

A Finnish corona vaccine may be ready perhaps next autumn. Researchers will start testing the corona vaccine on animals in the coming days.

The Russian corona vaccine is almost ready. The Russian health minister says that the tests of the corona vaccine have been done and it may already be given to people this autumn.

## Finns have come back from the al-Hol camp

![Women and a child at the al-Hol camp.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11181421)

Open image viewer

Conditions at the al-Hol camp are bad. Photo: Otso Reunanen / Yle

A Finnish mother has come back to Finland with her children from the al-Hol camp. The family returned to Finland on Saturday.

The al-Hol camp is in Syria. Family members of ISIS terrorists live in the camp. They were gathered into the camp after ISIS was defeated.

There are also Finnish women and children from ISIS families in the al-Hol camp. Some of them have already returned to Finland.

The Finnish police are investigating all Finnish women who return from the former ISIS area. The police want to know whether the women have committed crimes. In addition, it is being investigated whether the women pose a danger to Finland.

On Saturday the police were also waiting for a woman who came to Finland from the al-Hol camp via Turkey.

## The beer floating event was on the Vantaa river

![The beer floating event gathered people together despite the pandemic in northern Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11476035)

Open image viewer

Because of the corona, fewer people took part in the beer floating event than in previous years. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

On Saturday there were many rubber boats on the Vantaa river in Helsinki. They took part in the beer floating event.

The beer floating event has been held every summer for more than 20 years. The idea is simple: people float on rubber boats and drink beer.

The beer floating event has no official organiser, but the event brings many people to the Vantaa river.
