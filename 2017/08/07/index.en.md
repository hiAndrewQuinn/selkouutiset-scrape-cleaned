# Monday 7.8.2017 (radio)

The S Group retail chain plans to extend shop opening hours. In Turkey there is a dispute about how women dress. In Finland, lawyers are worried about whether asylum seekers get enough legal aid. Garden plants have grown well this summer. The weather is getting warmer.

## S Group shops

![A note on a shop door says that you can go shopping around the clock](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5486203)

The S Group is getting more shops that are always open. Photo: Yle

The S Group retail chain plans to extend shop opening hours.

The S Group now has 48 shops that are always open, also at night. More of these shops are coming, the S Group says.

In addition, some S Group shops will close later in the future than now.

The S Group is extending shop opening hours because customers want it.

## Turkey's women

![Turkish women demonstrating in a park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9761460)

Open image viewer

A women's demonstration in Turkey. Photo: Emily Mahringer

In Turkey there is a dispute about how women dress. Women are angry because women have been attacked, for example, for wearing shorts. In Turkey, not all men like women wearing shorts.

Hundreds of women have demonstrated in different parts of Turkey. The women say that they have the right to dress the way they themselves want.

Turkish women's organisations are worried that the position of women in Turkey has become worse in many ways. In the women's organisations' opinion, this is the fault of President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan and the Turkish government. Erdoğan has, for example, said that a man and a woman can never be equal.

## Legal aid for asylum seekers

![Lawyer Ville Punto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9756992)

Open image viewer

Lawyer Ville Punto is also worried about legal aid for asylum seekers. Photo: Katriina Laine / Yle

Here in Finland, lawyers are worried about whether asylum seekers get enough legal aid.

In the lawyers' opinion, the situation of asylum seekers got worse last autumn. At that time the government made changes to legal aid for asylum seekers.

For example, the fee of an asylum seeker's legal counsel does not go up even if they work more hours. The fee is the same no matter how many hours. If the counsel thinks about money, it is not worth their while to do the work very carefully.

The lawyers demand that the government reverse the changes to legal aid.

## Garden plants

![The picture shows a berry bush.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7393413)

Open image viewer

There are now a lot of berries on the berry bushes. Photo: Jukka-Pekka Tyhtilä / Yle

Garden plants have grown well this summer.

The weather has not been too hot, and the plants have got enough water. For example, berry bushes are doing well, and there have been a lot of berries.

The fairly cold weather has also kept plant diseases out of gardens. The plants have been healthy and have done well.
