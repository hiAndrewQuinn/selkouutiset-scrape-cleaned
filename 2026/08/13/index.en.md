## Big changes to civilian service

Next, news about civilian service.

The government wants big changes to civilian service. Civilian service is an alternative to the military.

Civilian service is work for the benefit of society, for example at a school or hospital.

The government wants more safety training for civilian service. Safety training could include oil spill response, missing person search, and food supply. Weapons are not used in civilian service.

The government wants to allow civilian servicemen to be called up for service during exceptional circumstances. Exceptional circumstances include, for example, major accidents or epidemics of infectious diseases.

The government also wants other major changes to the civilian service.

The government will submit a bill on changes to civilian service to Parliament in the autumn.

## There was a total solar eclipse in Europe

Finally, some news about the solar eclipse.

A total solar eclipse was visible in Europe yesterday.

It's rare. A total solar eclipse was visible in Spain, Portugal, Iceland and Greenland.

The sun goes dark when the moon is in front of it. The eclipse lasted two minutes.

Many tourists traveled to Spain to watch the eclipse.

In Finland, the moon almost completely covered the sun. A partial solar eclipse was visible in Finland. It was well visible in northern and western Finland.
