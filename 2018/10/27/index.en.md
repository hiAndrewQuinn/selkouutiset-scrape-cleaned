# Saturday 27.10.2018 (radio)

There has been a big jewellery theft in Vantaa. There is peer support for people addicted to gaming. A suspect in the letter bombs has been arrested in the USA. Tonight we switch to winter time.

## A big jewellery theft happened in Vantaa

![The Laatukoru shop in the Jumbo shopping centre in Vantaa on 27 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10478987)

The broken window of the shopping centre can be seen at the top of the picture. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Police have arrested 4 people for a break-in at a jewellery shop last night. The thieves took valuables from the Laatukoru shop in the Jumbo shopping centre in Vantaa.

The thieves took at least diamond jewellery, gold chains and watches. They are worth several hundred thousand euros. A large part of the items were found in a house search.

Images of the suspects were recorded on surveillance cameras. Police say that the thieves were professional. The break-in lasted only 2 minutes. No other shops in the shopping centre were broken into.The shopping centre opened normally on Saturday morning.

This year valuables have been taken from jewellery shops at least in Helsinki, Laitila and Imatra.

## Help is available for gaming problems

![gamer](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10452076)

Open image viewer

Digital gaming becomes a problem if it harms work, school or relationships with other people. You can get help for addiction, for example from a peer group. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Computer gamers can get support for gaming problems.

Peer groups for young adults have been set up in 4 places. There are now groups in Helsinki, Tampere, Kuopio and Jyväskylä. This year there will also be a group in Oulu. The groups have been set up by the Foundation for Social Pedagogy.

Many people play digital games over 60 hours a week. That is not always a problem if the rest of life is in order. Researchers estimate that about 1 or 2 percent of digital gamers become addicted to gaming. Then it is an addiction disorder.

10 percent of gamers have problems with gaming. They have problems at school, at work or in social relationships.

Problems with digital gaming are not always recognised. In the summer, the World Health Organization WHO proposed an official disease classification for the addiction disorder.

## The sender of the letter bombs has been arrested in the USA

![The AutoZone shop in Florida where the suspect was arrested on 26 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10478903)

Open image viewer

The suspect was arrested in an AutoZone shop in Florida. Photo: Joe Raedle / AFP

In the USA police have caught a man who has been sending letter bombs. The 56-year-old man from Florida was identified with the help of fingerprints.

The authorities say that the 56-year-old man is a passionate supporter of President Donald Trump. He is suspected of sending at least 14 letter bombs.

Most of the targets are people whom Trump and the American right have criticised.Not a single letter bomb has exploded.

The man has committed crimes before. The crimes include, for example, a bomb threat, theft, fraud, a drug crime and assault. The man could get almost 50 years in prison for the letter bombs.

## Clock hands are moved back an hour tonight

![clock](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10478460)

Open image viewer

In Finland people say that clocks are always moved towards the nearest summer. At the weekend the hands are moved back an hour. Photo: Timo Jaakonaho / Lehtikuva

Tonight the hands of the clocks are moved back an hour. Then we switch to winter time. The change happens at four o'clock in the early morning.

The switch to winter time affects at least the timetables of several buses and night trains.
