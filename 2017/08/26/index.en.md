# Saturday 26.8.2017 (radio)

Flags were flying in honour of Finnish nature. The state administration interested the audience. There are more people in debt enforcement than before. Hurricane Harvey hit Texas.

## Finnish Nature Day

![Sauli Niinistö and Jenni Haukio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9799380)

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio visited the Nature Day celebration in Teijo National Park in Salo. Photo: Johanna Manu / Yle

Today, Saturday, flags are being flown in honour of Finnish nature.

Finnish Nature Day has been celebrated since 2013. This was the first time the day was also a flag day.

Hundreds of events were held in different parts of the country, celebrating Finnish nature.

For example, concerts were held in national parks, where choirs sang. Thousands of people took part in the choirs in total.

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio visited the Nature Day celebration in Teijo National Park in Salo.

Mrs Jenni Haukio is the patron of Finnish Nature Day.

## Open doors at the ministries

![Government Palace, the President's reception hall](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9797344)

Open image viewer

The public could look at the President's reception hall in the Government Palace. Photo: Vesa Marttinen / Yle

In Helsinki, several ministries opened their doors to the public today in honour of the Finland 100 anniversary year.

The public could get to know, for example, the Ministry for Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of the Interior and the Ministry of Justice, as well as the Government Palace.

In the Government Palace you could peek into, for example, the President's reception hall and the Prime Minister's office. Prime Minister Sipilä was also there and received guests.

The Säätytalo also had its doors open. There were rare documents on display there, which relate to Finland becoming independent.

The event aimed to promote openness in administration and to make the work of the state administration familiar to people.

## Debt enforcement

![Debt enforcement application](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9552531)

Open image viewer

At the turn of the year, about 500,000 Finns' debts were collected through debt enforcement. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

About one in ten Finns is in debt enforcement. At the turn of the year, the debts of almost half a million people were collected through debt enforcement.

A person ends up in debt enforcement if they have not paid their debt. In debt enforcement, the collection of the debt has been transferred to the authorities,

After the 2008 economic recession, more and more people have ended up in debt enforcement.

However, many people's debt arrears are small. For more than 40 percent, debts under 1,000 euros are collected in debt enforcement.

## Hurricane Harvey

![Hurricane Harvey batters palm trees in the city of Corpus Christi, Texas.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9799161)

Open image viewer

Hurricane Harvey batters palm trees in the city of Corpus Christi in Texas. Photo: Darren Abate / EPA

In the United States, Hurricane Harvey has hit the state of Texas.

The storm has knocked down trees, torn off roofs of houses and cut the power to at least 400,000 households.

The storm brings heavy rain. In Texas people fear that it will cause serious floods.

Harvey has been considered the strongest hurricane in the United States for 10 years. However, it has weakened since it came ashore in Texas.
