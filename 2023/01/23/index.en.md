## Security policy adviser

![The Finnish flag and the NATO flag side by side.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4660,h_2621,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9411726255440fc42e4/1649839941)

Finland's Prime Minister may get a new security policy adviser.

The Prime Minister wants to have Finland's best security policy expert to help him. Yle's information says that the search for the expert will begin as soon as possible.

Security policy becomes more important as Finland becomes a member of the NATO defence alliance. Russia's war in Ukraine has also made security policy especially important.

## Women applying for the military

![Junior Sergeant Kaisa Mäki in a local defence exercise in Kuopio, in front of the Savo Vocational College.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_224/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1004146631828d2aa28c/1677153183)

Open image viewer

In Finland women can apply for the military if they want to. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

Over 1,200 women have applied for the military. The application period ended in mid-January, about 6 weeks earlier than in previous years.

The military is voluntary for women in Finland, but compulsory for men. Service in the military lasts at least about half a year.

If a young person does not want to go into the army, they can do civilian service. Civilian service can be completed, for example, in a hospital.

## Estonia

![Map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688210)

Open image viewer

The Estonia sank off the Finnish coast near Utö island when it was on its way from Tallinn to Stockholm in 1994. Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

New information has been obtained about the sinking of the passenger ship Estonia.

Estonia sank in 1994. Now a new investigation has been made into its sinking.

The investigation says that at the time of the accident Estonia was not seaworthy. Estonia was therefore not in good enough condition for a sea voyage.

The investigation found no signs of an explosion or a collision.

The Estonia ship sank when it was on its way from Tallinn to Stockholm. 852 people died in the accident.

## The Life of Language

![Woman holding a book in her hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688118)

Open image viewer

Finnish language teacher Hanna Männikkölahti. Photo: Jukka Lehto / Yle

The history of the Finnish language can now also be read about in plain language.

Finnish teacher Hanna Männikkölahti has written a plain-language book, The Life of Language. It is a plain-language version of a book by Lari Kotilainen. Kotilainen is a researcher of the Finnish language.

![Man holding a book in his hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688119)

Open image viewer

Researcher of the Finnish language Lari Kotilainen. Photo: Jukka Lehto / Yle

The book The Life of Language tells about the birth and development of the Finnish language. The book also tells what position the Finnish language has had at different times.

In Hanna Männikkölahti's view it is important that as many people as possible can get to know the history of the Finnish language. That is why she wrote a plain-language book on the subject.

Männikkölahti's plain-language book The Life of Language can be found, for example, in the library.

## The weather

On Tuesday the sun shines in Lapland and on the west coast. Elsewhere it is cloudy. A little snow may fall in the south-east.

The temperature in the south and west is a little above zero, and elsewhere in the country it is frost on Tuesday.

On Wednesday the weather warms. A föhn wind blows from the west, which warms the air.
