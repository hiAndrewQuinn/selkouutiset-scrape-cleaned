# Saturday 7.1.2017 (radio)

US intelligence chiefs met Trump. There has been a shooting at an airport in the United States. In Finland, airport security raises questions. Bad winter weather has caused accidents. Beaver is now popular food in Russia.

## Intelligence chiefs accuse Russia of a cyber attack

![Donald J. Trump Twitter post](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9391668)

Open image viewer

Trump accused the Democrats of carelessness in information security matters. Photo: twitter

The next president of the United States, Donald Trump, has met the country's top intelligence chiefs. The heads of the intelligence services told Trump that Russia has certainly interfered in the presidential election.

Russia tried to use a cyber attack to bring down the Democratic candidate Hillary Clinton and to support the Republican Donald Trump.

Trump does not believe that Russia managed to influence the election result. He blamed the Democrats for protecting their own computer network badly. Trump is setting up a group that will focus on stopping cyber attacks in the future.

## Shooting incident at a Florida airport

![An airport in the evening. In the picture, a young woman with her arms crossed and an older woman holding a small dog in her lap. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9391724)

Open image viewer

The shooting happened in Florida. Photo: Giorgio Viera / EPA

There has been a serious shooting at an airport in the United States. A man shot at least 7 people dead at Fort Lauderdale airport in Florida. In addition, 8 people were wounded.

The shooter was a former soldier who had served in Iraq. The man was 26 years old and he had mental health problems.

The police say that the man brought the gun to the airport in his luggage. The shooter has been arrested.

## Security at Finnish airports

![Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9262736)

Open image viewer

In Finland, people can move freely at airports. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

A shooting at an airport like the one in Florida is possible in Finland too. The head of the airport company Finavia says that there are strict rules on packing weapons onto a plane.

However, a person can have a weapon elsewhere at the airport, for example in the public areas.

Passengers and their belongings are only checked at security just before they go to the departure area. Otherwise, people can move freely at airports.

## Many accidents in traffic

![Map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9391848)

Open image viewer

A big car accident happened near Hämeenlinna. Photo: Yle News graphics

Bad winter weather has caused a lot of problems in traffic. In Juuka in Eastern Finland, 7 people were injured in a crash between 2 cars. At least 1 person was seriously injured. There was a heavy snowstorm at the time of the accident.

In Janakkala in Southern Finland, there was a crash of 5 cars on the Tampere motorway. Nobody was injured in the accident.

Problems in train traffic have also continued.

## Russians like beaver

![The nutria burger at Bistro Krasnodar.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9389936)

Open image viewer

The beaver burger is a great delicacy in Russia. Photo: Erkka Mikkonen / Yle

Russians have found a new delicacy. Russians are eating beaver burgers. The swamp beaver, or nutria, is a rodent with big teeth that looks like a rat. Beaver meat tastes more like elk or bear meat than chicken or beef.

Before, beaver was mainly food for poor people. Now it has become a hit food that is sold in fancy restaurants in Moscow.

Experts say that many old food traditions have come back in Russia since economic sanctions made foreign food expensive.
