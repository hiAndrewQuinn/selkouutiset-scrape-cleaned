# Thursday 6.10.2016 (radio)

Finns buy more organic food than before. The presidents of Finland and Russia have discussed the crisis in Syria on the phone. A court has ordered that Ilja Janitskin, founder of the MV magazine, must be jailed. Paying by phone is becoming more common. The Helsinki art museum is presenting the works of the Japanese artist Yayoi Kusama.

## Organic food interests people

![Organic products on a shop shelf. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6317057)

Open image viewer

Organic food now interests shop customers. Photo: Tarja Nyyssönen / Yle

Finns buy more organic food than before. Organic food is grown without chemical substances.

Finns buy a lot of organic bananas, organic eggs and organic milk, for example. During the last year Finns have spent about 250 million euros on organic food.

People who look after their fitness and health buy organic food, for example. Families often become interested in organic food when children are born into the family. People want to give healthy food to children.

## Niinistö and Putin talked

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9203509)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Yle

The President of Finland, Sauli Niinistö, has spoken on the phone with the President of Russia, Vladimir Putin. The presidents discussed Syria.

Niinistö told Putin that he is worried about the crisis in Syria. For example, many ordinary people are dying in the city of Aleppo, Niinistö said. Niinistö also told Putin that Russia has an important part to play when trying to end the crisis in Syria.

The phone conversation between the presidents was Niinistö's idea.

## Police are looking for the founder of MV magazine

![Ilja Janitskin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9194906)

Open image viewer

Ilja Janitskin, founder of MV magazine. Photo: Yle

A court has ordered that Ilja Janitskin, founder of MV magazine, must be jailed. The police want to hear Janitskin, but Janitskin has not been found. He lives in Spain.

It is suspected that Janitskin has committed many crimes. For example, Janitskin has perhaps incited hatred against a group, that is, spoken badly about a certain group.

MV magazine is known for opposing immigration and insulting immigrants.

## Paying by phone is becoming more common

![A 58 euro money transfer view on a smartphone screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7044830)

Open image viewer

A phone is a handy means of payment. Photo: Arvo Vuorela / Yle

Soon it will be normal to pay by phone. The phone may soon replace the bank card.

Paying by phone is quick and easy. For example, at a petrol station a customer can open the fuel meter on the phone already in the car.

Paying by phone is still slowed down by the fact that different banks have different apps on their phones. Often only the bank's own customers can pay with the app.

In the European Union, however, plans are already being made so that people can choose for themselves which app they use.

## Yayoi Kusama's art in Helsinki

![Japanese artist Yayoi Kusama.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8892928)

Open image viewer

Yayoi Kusama's art often has dots and balls. Photo: Yayoi Kusama / Helsinki City Art Museum

A new exhibition opens at the Helsinki art museum on Friday. The art museum is presenting the works of the Japanese artist Yayoi Kusama.

Kusama is known all over the world. Kusama's art often has spots, dots and balls.

The 87-year-old Kusama still paints every day. She lives in a psychiatric hospital in Tokyo.
