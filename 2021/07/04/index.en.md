## Breaking religious peace

![Kaisamari Hintikka walks down the steps holding a candle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4618,h_2598,x_0,y_332/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5534945ca4af91dbe6b/1554296787)

Lutheran bishop Kaisamari Hintikka thinks the religious peace clause is outdated: God does not need to be protected by law. Religious communities need protection. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

Criminalising the breaking of religious peace may end in Finland. Now breaking religious peace is a crime.

Legal scholars say that the criminalisation restricts freedom of speech too much. The UN Human Rights Committee also said this in April.

Professor of criminal law Kimmo Nuotio thinks that religious ideas must be allowed to be criticised.

In a large part of Europe, no punishments are given for blasphemy.

However, a change to the law on religious peace is not yet being prepared at the Finnish Ministry of Justice.

## Domestic fish

![A rainbow trout farm run by Tornionlaakso Jaloste Ky operates at Naamijoki. Naamijoki, Pello, 7.7.2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9717676)

Open image viewer

Rainbow trout are raised in pools. Photo: Jarmo Honkanen / Yle

Finns eat more and more fish, but only 20 percent of it is domestic. Consumption of domestic fish has decreased by over 30 percent in the 2000s.

Instead, consumption of foreign fish has doubled in 20 years.

In Finland fish are both caught and farmed. For example, rainbow trout is farmed fish.

There is less Finnish fish on offer, and that raises the price of fish. In addition, wild fish can be had only at varying times of the year. Wild fish are, for example, pike-perch, perch and vendace.

More than half of the fish imported to Finland is fresh. Besides Norway, it is imported from Sweden, Estonia and Denmark.

## Moped cars

![Moped cars parked.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10029678)

Open image viewer

Moped cars in a queue. Photo: Hannu Harhama / Yle

The popularity of moped cars is falling.

At the peak years 2014 and 2015 there were over 8,000 moped cars on the roads in Finland. Last year there were only about 5,000 moped cars.

In 2013 the driving rights for a moped and a moped car were separated. A separate driving licence is therefore needed for a moped car.

Since then many young people have obtained only the driving right for a two-wheeled moped.

Last year about 111,000 mopeds were in traffic use.

The popularity of moped cars has also been affected by enclosed ATVs.

## Summer theatres

![The empty auditorium of a summer theatre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82166260c9de269aaa5/1623842679)

Open image viewer

Summer theatres are waiting for a better summer than last year. Photo: Antti Ruonaniemi / Yle

Many summer theatres have been able to start their performance season. Gatherings are now restricted less than before, because the corona epidemic has eased a little.

The situation is still uncertain, however. That is why there are only about 200 premieres this summer. That is half fewer than usual.

The Theatre Information Centre says that this summer is clearly better than last summer. Then there were about 20 percent of premieres compared to a normal summer.

A lot of tickets have already been sold.
