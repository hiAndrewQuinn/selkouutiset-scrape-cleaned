# Wednesday 7.11.2018 (radio)

The Democrats did well in the US elections. Women did well in the US elections. Winter road maintenance will improve. The OECD warns about superbugs.

## US elections

![Donald Trump](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10497205)

Open image viewer

President Trump's party, the Republicans, did badly in the elections. Photo: David Maxwell / EPA

There have been elections in the USA. Americans chose representatives for Congress.

The Democrats won more seats than the Republicans in the House of Representatives in Congress. That is bad news for President Donald Trump, who is a Republican.

The Democrats can now block and slow down Trump's ideas. The president's bills will not come into force if Congress does not approve them. Congress also decides on the USA's financial matters.

It is now hard for President Trump to get money, for example, for building a wall on the Mexican border. The wall has been one of Trump's favourite ideas.

## Women in the US elections

![Ilhan Omar being interviewed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10495900)

Open image viewer

Ilhan Omar is a Somali refugee and now also a member of Congress. Photo: Kerem Yucel / AFP

Women did well in the US congressional elections. Women from different minorities also got into Congress.

For the first time, Muslim women also got into Congress. There are two of them. One of them is Somali refugee Ilhan Omar. Omar wants to be an example for other immigrant women.

For the first time, Congress now also has Native American women. Native Americans are the original inhabitants of America. In addition, two women under 30 got into Congress for the first time.

## Roads in winter

![A snow plough on Ysitie during the snow chaos of 26 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10476779)

Open image viewer

Many main roads will have more snow ploughs next winter than before. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

Winter road maintenance will improve.

Last winter was hard for motorists. It snowed and it rained. The roads were often icy.

The Finnish Transport Agency wants roads to be put back in order more quickly, for example after snowfall. Main roads will be ploughed and salted more.

The Transport Agency promises a better service for 15 per cent of Finland's main roads. The agency wants better maintenance especially on roads with a lot of heavy traffic.

Next year the state will give the Transport Agency 15 million euros more for winter road maintenance.

## Superbugs

![Microscope image of a hospital bacterium that is resistant to antibiotics.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5612484)

Open image viewer

Microscope image of a superbug. Photo: BBC

The OECD organisation warns about superbugs. The OECD is an organisation of rich industrial countries.

Superbugs are dangerous, because antibiotics do not kill them. That is why superbugs are dangerous, for example, when a person has a wound. A person can die if a superbug gets into a wound.

Superbugs are also dangerous to surgery patients. The OECD says that hospitals must look after hygiene well.
