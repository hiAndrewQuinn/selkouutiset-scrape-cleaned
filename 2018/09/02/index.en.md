## Plastic bag consumption has decreased

![Plastic bags in a pile.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10311084)

Open image viewer

Plastic bags in a pile. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

Finns are buying fewer and fewer plastic bags. Shops have started charging for plastic bags, and people are also encouraged to use bags other than plastic ones.

Many people nowadays use cloth bags in the grocery store. The harms of plastic are being discussed a lot right now, because plastic waste is a really big problem.

Plastic, for example, accumulates in the sea, and in the sea plastic is dangerous to animals. Finland has a target that by the end of 2025 each Finn will consume at most 40 plastic bags a year.

## The village of the year has been chosen

![Teuro village house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10382813)

Open image viewer

The Teuro village house was built by volunteer work. Photo: Teuro-Kuuslampi village Facebook page

The village of the year has been chosen: Teuro-Kuuslampi village.

The village is located in Häme, a little over 100 kilometres from Helsinki. In the opinion of the selection board, it is a good place to live.

The village has only 250 residents, but its services are really good. In the village you can find, for example, a café, a playground, a paid village assistant, hobbies every evening and several rental premises.

The village has active residents who do many things together.

Audiobooks are popular

![The popularity of audiobooks has grown quickly.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9818971)

Open image viewer

Audiobooks can be bought from many different apps. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

The popularity of audiobooks has grown very quickly in recent years. An audiobook is a book that you can listen to.

Audiobooks can be bought from many different apps. Reading has been studied a lot. According to studies, reading improves vocabulary and memory and reduces stress.

According to a brain researcher, audiobooks also reduce stress. According to the researcher, listening to a book improves vocabulary just as effectively as reading a book.

On the other hand, grammar rules, for example, are remembered better when they are read from a book.

You may not poison rats yourself

![Two rats.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10382968)

Open image viewer

Rats can still be caught with a trap. Photo: Pat Morris / AOP

In the yard you may no longer poison rats yourself. Under the new law, the right to poison rats is now only for professionals.

Consumers may also no longer buy poisons used to control rats.

Poisons can cause poisoning symptoms in people, and poisons are really dangerous for children.

A person often gets poisoned through the skin or mouth. Symptoms of poisoning can include, for example, uncontrollable bleeding and breathing difficulties.
