## Russia's war in Ukraine

![President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, a flag and a soldier.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689334)

Open image viewer

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Kyiv on the anniversary of the war. Photo: Office of the President of Ukraine.

Russia's war in Ukraine began exactly a year ago. Russia attacked Ukraine on 24 February 2022.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy awarded medals to soldiers in the capital Kyiv on Friday.

Zelenskyy said that the war year has been a year of endurance, courage, pain and unity.

At the start of the war Russia tried to capture the Ukrainian capital Kyiv. Russia also wanted the Ukrainian president to be replaced.

However, Ukrainians did not give in. They began to defend their own country.

That is why Russia's goals have not succeeded. Kyiv is in Ukrainian hands, and Volodymyr Zelenskyy is still the president of Ukraine.

However, the war has been hard for Ukrainians. Many people have died in the war, and millions of Ukrainian women and children have fled abroad.

## Events in support of Ukraine

![Helsinki Cathedral, with blue light, and candles in front of the church.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689335)

Helsinki Senate Square and the cathedral on 24.2.2023. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Many events have been held in Finland to show support for Ukraine.

For example, an event was held in honour of Ukraine at Senate Square in Helsinki.

The event began when church bells rang for peace. The pillars of the cathedral were lit up in the colours of Ukraine, that is, blue and yellow.

## UMK final

![A group of pop music performers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689333)

Open image viewer

7 singers or groups perform in the UMK final. Photo: Nelli Kenttä / Yle

On Saturday Finland's representative for this year's Eurovision Song Contest will be chosen.

The winner of UMK, the New Music Competition, goes to Eurovision. The winner is found in the UMK final, in which 7 performers take part.

Eurovision will be held in May in England. England came 2nd in last spring's Eurovision.

The victory went to Ukraine last year, but Ukraine cannot organise Eurovision because of the war. Normally the winning country of Eurovision may host the next Eurovision.

The UMK final can be followed on Yle Areena, TV1 and Radio Suomi. The competition starts at 21:00. Viewers can take part in choosing the winner. More information is at yle.fi.

## The weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689336)

Open image viewer

On Saturday there is dry frosty weather across the whole country.

In the south and east it is cloudy. In the west and north it is sunny.

The temperature on Saturday is between -2 and -10 degrees, and the coldest is in the north.

The frosty weather continues on Sunday. The temperature is between -5 and -10 degrees.

On Monday a foehn wind blows from the west and brings warm air to the whole country.
