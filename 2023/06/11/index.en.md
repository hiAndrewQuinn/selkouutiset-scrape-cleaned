Anna-Maja Henriksson will continue as RKP's chair.

The RKP party congress elected Henriksson as chair on Sunday. There were no other candidates.

Henriksson has been RKP's chair since 2016.

Henriksson has also been Finland's justice minister for many years.

## Canada's wildfires

![Aerial image of a forest fire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5104,h_2871,x_1,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-112781364845d5e52e23/1686820309)

Open image viewer

Forest and other land are burning over large areas in Canada. Photo: AFP

The wildfires continue in Canada. Forest and land are already burning in about 400 places.

The fires already threaten the homes of many Canadians. That is why many thousands of Canadians have left their homes and fled from the wildfires.

Canadian authorities say that the wildfires are now worse than ever before. There are also wildfires over a large area in different parts of Canada.

The smoke from the wildfires has spread from Canada to the American side too. In recent days, for example, there was so much smoke in New York that people were better off staying inside.

## Heavy rain in Pakistan

![A man is carried on a stretcher to a hospital. More than ten people are around the hospital door.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3880,h_2182,x_0,y_290/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11280026484d53b0b031/1686427299)

Open image viewer

People have died and been injured in the heavy rain in Pakistan. Photo: Noman Khan / EPA

There has been heavy rain and strong winds in Pakistan. A heavy rain and a storm wind struck Pakistan on Saturday.

Pakistani authorities say that about 30 people died because of the heavy rain. There are children among the dead. Many people have been injured.

The rains have also caused a lot of damage. For example, houses have collapsed because of the heavy rain.

## Strawberry harvest

![Strawberry on its stalk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1723,h_967,x_0,y_144/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10976706435564757156/1681217423)

Open image viewer

Strawberries ripen quickly in warm weather. Photo: Erkki Suonio

The Finnish strawberry harvest will ripen soon. Strawberries are now ripening quickly, because the weather is getting warmer.

Finnish strawberries will come on sale in larger amounts in about 2 weeks, that is, around Midsummer.

It is estimated that the price of strawberries will be higher this year than last year.

## Whale

![On the map, the Turku archipelago and the Åland archipelago, Kökar, Eckerö, Mariehamn and Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11280826485b48c22a7a/1686484170)

A whale was seen at the weekend near Eckerö and Kökar in Åland. Photo: Leena Luotio / Yle

A big whale is moving in Finland's sea area. Fishers have seen the whale near Åland.

Experts estimate that the whale may be a humpback whale. A humpback whale often weighs about 30,000 kilos and is about 15 metres long.

## The weather

![Monday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11280936485df82f2dea/1686495159)

Open image viewer

The weather is getting warmer.

On Monday, the temperature is over 20 degrees in a large part of the country.

On Tuesday, the temperature in the south rises up to 25 degrees, and on Wednesday up to 27 degrees. So heat is coming to the south. It is a bit cooler on the coast, because the sea cools the air.

In the north, temperatures are lower than in the south. In Lapland the temperature on Monday is about 20 degrees, and on Tuesday the temperature in Lapland falls to between 10 and 15 degrees.
