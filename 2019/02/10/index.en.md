# Sunday 10.2.2019 (radio)

Valvira wants ghost carers taken off duty rosters. The Interior Minister proposes a Finland test for asylum seekers. President Niinistö's hip will be operated on. The top prize of Lotto went to one coupon. The bears at Korkeasaari woke from hibernation.

## Valvira wants ghost carers out of care homes

![Esperi Care Ulrika care home in Kristinestad](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10637603)

Valvira suspended the operations of Esperi Care's Ulrika care home in Kristinestad a couple of weeks ago. Photo: Petri Hakosalo / Lehtikuva

The National Supervisory Authority for Welfare and Health, Valvira, wants to remove so-called ghost carers from care homes. Valvira is considering taking to court the care homes where ghost carers have been used.

Ghost carers are mentioned when people who do not really do the work of a carer are entered on care homes' carer duty rosters. With the help of ghost carers, the duty roster is made to look as if the care home has enough carers, even though in reality there are not.

Valvira knows of many cases in which people who are actually working in the administration of a care home have been entered on the carers' duty rosters, for example.

## Interior Minister: a Finland test for asylum seekers

![Interior Minister Kai Mykkänen (National Coalition).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_359/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4637635a819f94c2ee4/1518444560)

Open image viewer

Interior Minister Kai Mykkänen. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Interior Minister Kai Mykkänen says that asylum seekers must be given a test on how well they know Finnish society and its customs

Today, every asylum seeker over the age of 16 must complete a basic course that teaches them how to act in Finnish society.

Minister Kai Mykkänen says that the course alone is not enough, and that a test must be held at the end of it. The test ensures that the asylum seeker has understood what they have been taught.

## The bears at Korkeasaari woke up

![The bears of Korkeasaari have woken from their hibernation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10097076)

Open image viewer

The hibernation of the Korkeasaari bears was shorter than usual. Photo: Olga Pukkila

In Helsinki, the bears at the Korkeasaari Zoo have woken from hibernation.

The bears have woken earlier than usual. Normally they sleep through hibernation until the turn of February and March, the zoo says.

There are 2 bears at Korkeasaari, two female bears aged 18 and 13. After hibernation, the bears were let out, where they wrestle and play in the snow.

At first the bears get only light food, such as vegetables, salad and fruit. They cannot yet stay awake for the whole day. That is why they may go back inside to doze whenever they want.
