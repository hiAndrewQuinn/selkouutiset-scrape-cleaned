## Government and the Garden project

Then politics.

The government no longer supports the Garden Helsinki project.

The decision was made jointly by the chairmen of the governing parties. The government had previously promised money for the project.

Now Prime Minister **Petteri Orpo** says that the hall project has been scaled back and delayed. That is why the government is no longer supporting the Garden Helsinki project.

The hall and the government's actions have also been discussed in Parliament today. The opposition wants to know why the government promised money for the hall project.

## Crab season

Finally, the topic is crab season.

Crab season has begun.

The Finnish Fisheries Federation says that crab catches are expected to be normal. Warm waters have helped the crabs reproduce.

Approximately 3.5 million crabs are caught in Finland every year. The majority of the catch is spotted crab.

Crab season started at noon. It will continue until the end of October.

You must have a permit to catch crabs. You must also pay a fishing fee to the state.
