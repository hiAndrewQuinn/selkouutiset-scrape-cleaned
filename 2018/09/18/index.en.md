# Tuesday 18.9.2018 (radio)

Finland may set up a fund to prepare for damage caused by terrorism. The Chancellor of Justice has reprimanded Foreign Minister Timo Soini. Train traffic stopped in Helsinki in the morning. The first tourist for a trip around the moon has been chosen. The weather will be almost summery in the coming days.

## Finland may get a fund for terrorism damage

![Sauli Niinistö lays a wreath.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9786036)

Finland may set up a fund to prepare for damage caused by terrorism.

At present victims can claim compensation from their own insurance. Depending on the situation, compensation can also be claimed from statutory insurance and from the State Treasury. For example, the State Treasury pays at most 61,000 euros for personal injury and 30,500 euros for property damage. However, there is no upper limit on compensation for loss of earnings or on maintenance compensation.

Kirsi Suopelto, chief lawyer for the finance sector, says that a model for a terrorism fund can be taken from elsewhere in Europe. The French fund gets its money from tax revenue. The chair of the Defence Committee, Ilkka Kanerva, says that the idea of a terrorism fund is important.

## Foreign Minister Soini reprimanded by the Chancellor of Justice

![Foreign Minister Timo Soini at a plenary session of Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10409844)

Open image viewer

Timo Soini in Parliament. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The Chancellor of Justice reprimands Foreign Minister Timo Soini for taking part in an anti-abortion event on an official trip in Canada.

The Chancellor of Justice considers that judgement and restraint are needed in a minister's actions. Then no misunderstandings arise about which are Finland's positions and which are the minister's own, the Chancellor of Justice considers. In the Chancellor of Justice's opinion, however, Soini's actions were not illegal.

Soini says that he will learn from the decision.

## Train traffic stopped in Helsinki

![A train leaving Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10370049)

Open image viewer

Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

Train traffic stopped in Helsinki in the morning. The reason was a fault in a safety device. The fault lasted for a couple of hours.

Trains from Helsinki to, for example, Tampere, Turku and Lappeenranta left about 40 minutes late. The delays have widely affected train traffic in Southern Finland.

## The moon tourist has been chosen

![An artist's impression of Space X's Big Falcon Rocket.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10409778)

Open image viewer

An artist's impression of Space X's Big Falcon Rocket. Photo: HO / Space X

The first tourist for a trip around the moon has been chosen.

The one going on the moon trip is a rich Japanese businessman, Yusaku Maezawa. The space travel is being planned by the company SpaceX. The trip may perhaps happen in 2023.

Businessman Maezawa owns the Zozo fashion clothing shop. Maezawa's wealth is estimated at three billion euros. He plans to take 6 or 8 acquaintances with him on the moon trip.

## The weather is almost summery

![Maximum temperature graphic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10409959)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Weather

Warm air is flowing into Finland. It brings a feeling of summer to the south and the central parts of the country.

Yle meteorologist Matti Huutonen says that in the south the 20-degree mark may be broken on Wednesday. On Thursday and Friday 20 degrees may be exceeded in the central parts of the country too. In the north the temperature will be 10 - 15 degrees in the coming days too.

Temperatures of over 20 degrees after the middle of September are rare.
