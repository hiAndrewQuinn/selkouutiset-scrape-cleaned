## Russia froze the bank accounts of Finland's embassies

![Finland's embassy in Moscow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5084,h_2859,x_0,y_140/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1115021646486d38e31a/1684310807)

Open image viewer

Finland's embassy in Moscow. Photo: Vlad Karkov / AOP

The Bank of Russia has frozen the bank accounts of Finnish missions in Russia. That is why the accounts of the missions cannot be used.

Finland has an embassy in Moscow and a consulate general in St Petersburg.

The Foreign Ministry says that the situation is serious. Finland has been in contact with the Russian authorities about it. Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says that the missions can operate for now.

## The Council of Europe set up a damage register

![Council of Europe summit in Iceland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_89/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-111490264643e26b4de7/1684319443)

Open image viewer

Council of Europe summit in Reykjavík, Iceland. Photo: Lehtikuva

The Council of Europe has set up an international damage register. The reason is Russia's invasion war in Ukraine.

Material and human damage caused by the war will be collected in the register. The aim is also to compensate for damage and to start legal proceedings.

The Council of Europe has 46 member states. Russia was expelled from the organisation last year.

40 countries set up the damage register, Finland among them. The European Union is also included. Only a few member states are left outside the register, including Turkey and Hungary.

## Quality of life is getting worse

![Two people paddling on a river.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5078,h_2856,x_0,y_262/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4456955a1adde51021e/1684332243)

Open image viewer

Enjoying waterways can be an example of good quality of life. Photo: Monkey Business Images

The quality of life of working-age people has deteriorated a lot in recent years. That is what the Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, says.

Quality of life means a person's view of their own situation. For example, health, well-being, social relationships and living environment affect quality of life.

Only every second working-age person feels that their quality of life is good. Just 4 years ago, over 60 percent of working-age people felt this way.

20 percent feel that mental strain is significant.

## More and more want to ban balcony smoking

![A person smoking on a balcony.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1704,h_959,x_136,y_186/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5928235d84c5f93da03/1684329179)

Open image viewer

Increased remote work may increase exposure to tobacco smoke.

62 percent of Finns support a complete ban on balcony smoking. That is what a survey by the health organisation Suomen ASH ry says.

Support has grown a lot in 4 years. Still in 2019, 46 percent of respondents supported a total ban on balcony smoking. Increased remote work at home increases exposure to tobacco smoke.

Suomen ASH promotes quitting tobacco and nicotine products and a life without intoxicants in Finland.
