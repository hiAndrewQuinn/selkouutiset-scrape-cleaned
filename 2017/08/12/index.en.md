# Saturday 12.8.2017 (radio)

Finns are testing a diarrhoea vaccine in Benin. Fewer foreign fighters are leaving Finland than before. The Finns Party's membership is growing. Children found a bomb in a schoolyard. Many shooting stars can now be seen in the sky.

## Vaccine research

![Benin vaccine research ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9765928)

Kirsi Makkonen dances and a Beninese music group plays along. Photo: Hans von Schantz

In Benin, West Africa, a vaccine study has started in which hundreds of Finns are taking part. The study is testing a new vaccine against diarrhoea.

A total of 800 Finnish volunteers are travelling to Benin on a 2-week holiday. Half of them get the diarrhoea vaccine. The vaccine is meant to prevent diarrhoea caused by the ETEC bacterium.

Kirsi Makkonen from Kerava is one of the test subjects. She has just come home from Benin and says that the trip was really nice. Kirsi Makkonen got a stomach bug, but only on the journey home.

Developing the new vaccine to completion takes many years. When it is ready, there will be a great need for it. In developing countries, half a million children under 5 die of diarrhoea every year.

## Foreign fighters

![An ISIS fighter and the ISIS flag. A screenshot from an ISIS video.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9771930)

About 80 people have left Finland to fight in the war zones of Syria and Iraq. About 20 of them have died and 20 have come back home, the police say.

The police say that fewer people are now leaving Finland to become foreign fighters than before. On the other hand, fewer people are also coming home than before.

## The Finns Party

![Jussi Halla-aho](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9351403)

The Finns Party's membership is growing.

About 900 new members have joined the party since the party conference in June. At the same time, about 200 people have left the party.

The Finns Party now has over 11,000 members in total.

## A wartime bomb

![A map of Sodankylä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9771266)

Open image viewer

Children dug up a bomb in a schoolyard in Sodankylä. Photo: Yle News Graphics

In Lapland, children have found an old bomb in a schoolyard.

On Friday, children were digging in the ground in the yard of a primary school in Sodankylä when they found an old bomb. The bomb weighed 2.5 kilos and dated from wartime.

The children showed the bomb to a teacher, who called the police.

The army took the bomb away from the schoolyard and destroyed it.

## Shooting stars

![A Perseid photographed in the night sky in the Neusiedlersee area of Austria on 12 August 2016. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9763243)

Open image viewer

Shooting stars can be seen well if there are no clouds in the sky. Photo: Lisi Niesner / EPA

This weekend it is worth looking at the night sky. If the weather is clear at night, that is, there are no clouds in the sky, you can see a lot of shooting stars.

The astronomical association Ursa says that the shooting stars come from the Perseid meteor shower. The Perseid meteor shower is especially active this weekend.

Shooting stars can be seen especially in the early hours of the morning south of Oulu, where the nights are already darker than in the north. It is possible to see perhaps even 70 shooting stars an hour.

Shooting stars can be seen all around the sky, but it is worth looking especially to the north-east and east.
