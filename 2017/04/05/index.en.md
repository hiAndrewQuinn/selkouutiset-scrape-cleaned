# Wednesday 5.4.2017 (radio)

The President of China in Finland. The pandas' arrival in Ähtäri is now certain. China is important for Finland's economy. The National Museum gives the Sámi people's objects back to Lapland. 80 million tulips are growing in Finland this winter.

## Visitors from China

![Chinese President Xi Jinping and Sauli Niinistö with their wives.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9548811)

The presidential couples of China and Finland. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Chinese President Xi Jinping and Mrs Peng Liyuan are in Finland.

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio received the guests in the yard of the Presidential Palace in Helsinki. All the ministers of the Finnish government were also there.

The presidents of China and Finland discussed the countries' relations and cooperation. The presidents also agreed that two panda bears will come to Finland from China this year.

President Xi leaves Finland tomorrow for the USA, where he meets President Donald Trump. A Chinese leader has visited Finland only once before, 22 years ago.

## Pandas to Finland

![Pandas](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9548167)

Open image viewer

Panda cubs resting. Photo: Lehtikuva / AFP

Ähtäri Zoo is happy that the pandas' arrival is now certain.

In Ähtäri a house is already being built for the pandas, and it will be ready in December. The zoo believes that the pandas will attract many people to visit Ähtäri. The animal park pays rent for the pandas to the Chinese state. China hopes that the pandas will have cubs in Ähtäri.

Only 1,800 pandas live free in nature. They live in the mountains in China, where the winter is a bit like in Finland.

## 150,000 Chinese tourists

![Containers in the Kwai Chung port in Hong Kong.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7790069)

Open image viewer

Containers in a Chinese port. Photo: Alex Hofford / EPA

China is important for Finland's economy. Many tourists come from China. Last year 150,000 Chinese people visited Finland.

Exports of goods to China are also growing fast now. Finland exports, for example, machines and cellulose.

But there is a danger in China's economy: China's debt to other countries is growing fast. If China's debt grows too big, it will become a problem for the whole world economy.

## The Sámi get their objects back

![National Museum curator Raila Kataja holds a Sámi horned hat in her hand. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9546069)

Open image viewer

A Sámi woman's hat. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

The National Museum of Finland gives the Sámi objects that it has back to the Sámi. The objects are given to the Sámi museum Siida, which is in Inari in Lapland.

There are  about 2,600 objects, and the oldest are more than 200 years old. The objects will be taken to the Sámi museum Siida when more space has been built there.

## The tulip is the winter favourite

![Tulips.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8817293)

Open image viewer

80 million tulips are growing in Finland's greenhouses this winter. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The tulip is Finland's most popular cut flower. 80 million Finnish tulips are bought this winter. Half of them are grown by one company in Pieksämäki.

Tulips are easy to buy from the grocery shop and take home or give to friends. They are not expensive. There are tulips in many colours.
