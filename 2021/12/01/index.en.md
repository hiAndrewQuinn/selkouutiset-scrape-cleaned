# Wednesday 1.12.2021 (radio)

Corona infections. Corona vaccines. Prime Minister Marin. Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Container transport. Weather forecast.

## Corona infections

![THL sign on Mannerheimintie in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11358354)

Open image viewer

Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

More corona infections have come.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, said on Wednesday that there are 1570 new corona infections in Finland. That is more than ever before in one day.

THL also told about new corona deaths. In Finland there are 13 new deaths where the cause is corona.

THL also told good news. The number of corona patients in hospitals has decreased. On Wednesday there were about 40 fewer corona patients in hospital care than on Tuesday.

## Corona vaccines in the EU

![Nurse Aishwarya Gauli (left) gave the first corona vaccine to Vasili Panchuev, 29, at the corona vaccination point at the Helsinki Messukeskus on 15 October 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5997,h_3373,x_0,y_169/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-86769361696faddf19a/1634629206)

Open image viewer

Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

The European Union must consider compulsory corona vaccinations. This is what the President of the European Commission, Ursula von der Leyen, says.

Von der Leyen reminds that 150 million inhabitants of EU countries still have no corona vaccine. At the same time, there are more corona infections than ever before in many countries.

Von der Leyen says, however, that the EU countries themselves decide on compulsory corona vaccinations.

The EU country Austria has already decided that the corona vaccine will be compulsory in Austria. Germany is also considering compulsory corona vaccinations.

## Helsinki-Vantaa airport

![The renovated Helsinki-Vantaa airport, photographed on 24.11.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_570/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-883431619e421053b42/1637761952)

The renovated Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

The new part of Helsinki-Vantaa airport has been opened. The new part was opened on Wednesday.

A new car park building has also been brought into use at the airport. The connections to the railway station will also get better.

The renovation of Helsinki-Vantaa airport has already lasted 8 years, and everything will be ready in 2 years. The renovation costs one billion euros.

## Container transport

![A container ship on its way to Vuosaari harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7886,h_4436,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6071725dbc1e5a31f38/1598001363)

Open image viewer

Container ship. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

Container transport has become more expensive. Ships carry goods in containers all over the world.

Container transport has become more expensive, because there is a shortage of containers around the world.

That is why container transport is now many times more expensive than just a year ago. That also raises the price of goods.

Transport in Finland's ports still works well.

## Weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12212926)

Open image viewer

December begins with wintry weather. Snowfall moves in the north, and driving conditions are poor.

In the south and middle of the country it is dry and the sun may shine a little.

The temperature is between 5 and 15 degrees of frost across the whole country, and in the night it is colder in places.
