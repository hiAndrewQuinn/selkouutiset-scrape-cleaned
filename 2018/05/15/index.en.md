# Tuesday 15.5.2018 (radio)

Teachers have more stress than others. The police are investigating how a Roma family was treated at a petrol station in Vantaa. President Putin has opened a bridge between the Crimean peninsula and Russia. Finland beat the USA at the Ice Hockey World Championships. On Tuesday, a new heat record for this spring was measured.

## Teachers' stress

![A classroom at Konala primary school](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10191699)

Open image viewer

After a school day, a teacher can be really tired. Photo: Elise Tykkyläinen / Yle

Teachers have more stress than others. The Trade Union of Education asked teachers what kind of working conditions they have.

About 60 percent of those who answered said that there is often too much work. For example, teachers at universities of applied sciences and upper secondary schools think that they have too much work.

Bullying is also a problem for teachers. Almost 50 percent of teachers say that they have been bullied or otherwise treated badly during the last year.

Violence is also familiar to teachers. About 10 percent of teachers have experienced violence.

Usually a pupil bullies or hits the teacher.

## Treatment of a Roma family

![Samira Lindeman (left) and Anneli Lindeman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10204721)

Open image viewer

The petrol station did not sell petrol to the Lindeman family. Photo: Riku Roslund

The police are investigating how a Roma family was treated at a petrol station in Vantaa. The petrol station did not sell petrol to the Roma family. There is a video of the situation, which has spread on the internet.

The police are investigating whether the petrol station discriminated against Roma people. Discrimination is a crime in Finland.

## The Crimean bridge

![Russian President Vladimir Putin behind the wheel of a lorry. Two other men are also sitting in the cab.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10206436)

Open image viewer

President Putin drove a lorry on the new bridge. Photo: Alexander Nemenov / EPA

Russian President Vladimir Putin has opened a very long bridge. The bridge is between the Crimean peninsula and Russia.

The new bridge is longer than any other bridge in Europe. It is almost 20 kilometres long.

The bridge improves transport links between Russia and Crimea. Russia took the Crimean peninsula from Ukraine in 2014.

Ukraine, and also the European Union and the USA, think that Russia did wrong when it took the Crimean peninsula for itself. That is why Ukraine thinks the new bridge is illegal.

## Finland's ice hockey game

![Sebastian Aho](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10206605)

Open image viewer

Sebastian Aho scored 3 goals in one game. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

Finland is doing well at the Ice Hockey World Championships.

On Tuesday, Finland beat the USA. Finland scored 6 goals in the game, and the USA scored 2 goals.

The hero of the Finnish team was Sebastian Aho. He scored a hat-trick, that is, 3 goals in the same game.

After Tuesday, only the 8 best teams are left. The next games are on Thursday. Finland will then play against Switzerland.

On Sunday we will find out which country is the ice hockey world champion this year.

## Spring heat record

![A sunbather in Helsinki on 15 May 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10206107)

Open image viewer

In Helsinki it was almost 30 degrees on Tuesday. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

This spring's heat record has been broken. On Tuesday, 29.6 degrees was measured at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.

Tuesday was already the 5th hot day in a row. In a heatwave, the temperature is over 25 degrees.

It is rarely as warm in May as it is now. Usually the temperature is about 15 degrees.
