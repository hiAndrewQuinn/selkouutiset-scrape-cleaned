## Patient safety act

![Sanna Marin speaking before the EU Parliament in Strasbourg on 13.9.2022](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_344/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1007185632065a06e34f/1663067604)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Photo: © European Union 2022 - Source: EP

There is a dispute in the government about the patient safety act. The government has made a bill with which nurses' right to strike can be restricted. The aim is to ensure patient safety in intensive care wards.

The Left Alliance, a government party, has said that the Left Alliance will not vote for the law. The Left Alliance does not like that the right to strike is restricted.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin spoke about the matter on Tuesday. Marin says that the government's ministers must vote for the government's proposal.

Nurses' organisations have given 5 strike warnings for different intensive care wards and for home care for elderly people.

## Disruption at S-Bank's online banking

![S-Bank logo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4128,h_2322,x_0,y_65/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4812325ae087afccd79/1697607990)

Open image viewer

The S-Bank online banking fault has been fixed. Photo: Anna Savonius / Yle

There has been a serious fault in S-Bank's online banking. Because of the fault, hundreds of S-Bank customers were able to log in to other customers' online banking.

The fault lasted over 3 months. A few people misused the fault. For example, they paid their own payments from another person's account.

S-Bank noticed the fault at the start of August. The fault has now been fixed. S-Bank has informed the customers whose accounts the problem concerned. S-Bank has made a request to the police for an investigation.

## The value of pension money

![Elderly person with a rollator](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_630/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-7891558dd401a061e8/1579152448)

Open image viewer

The fall in share prices has taken a lot of money from the pension funds of pension companies. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The value of pension money has fallen quickly by a lot. Pension money has been invested in funds. Normally a fund makes more money. This year the funds have made losses, that is, their values have fallen.

9 billion euros has disappeared from the value of the pension funds in April–June. The reason is that the values of many shares have fallen. Employment pension companies are supervised by the Financial Supervisory Authority. The Financial Supervisory Authority says that the situation is not yet alarming.

Pension companies want to increase share investments, because over the long term share investments have usually done well. In Finland it is hoped that the values of pension funds will rise again.

## Girls Girls Girls film

![Laughing girl.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4666,h_2624,x_0,y_311/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-935977624585679ac8e/1648723998)

Linnea Leino plays a figure skater in the film Girls Girls Girls. Photo: Ilkka Saastamoinen

The film *Girls Girls Girls* has been chosen as Finland's candidate for the Oscar for best international film.

The film is about three girls at the start of adulthood, and about the girls' relationships and loves. The film Girls Girls Girls was directed by **Alli Haapasalo**.

Finland's Oscar candidate was chosen by a jury of film professionals. The final Oscar nominations will be published in January. The Oscar gala is in March.
