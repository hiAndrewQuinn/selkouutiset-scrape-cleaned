## The breakup of the mercenary army Wagner

![A photo of Wagner group leader Prigozhin with a rifle in his hand, the photo surrounded by red carnations.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5363,h_3017,x_0,y_42/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116296464e968781a0fb/1693018408)

Wagner leader Yevgeny Prigozhin died in an air crash. Photo: EPA

Finland is closely following whether members of the mercenary army Wagner are trying to come to Finland. Wagner's leader **Yevgeny Prigozhin** died in an air crash in Russia on Wednesday. That is why Wagner's forces may break up.

Wagner soldiers may leave Russia. Former Wagner soldiers may also come to Finland.

Foreign Minister **Elina Valtonen** says that Finland is closely following the situation. The Finnish Security Intelligence Service and the Border Guard are monitoring travel to Finland. Authorities receive information about people who have taken part in the war in Ukraine.

## TikTok and young politicians

![TikTok company logo on a phone screen. Source code is visible in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5419,h_3048,x_0,y_159/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1100340643d4e64260c3/1681739423)

Open image viewer

Short videos are uploaded to TikTok. Photo: Jonathan Raa / AOP

TikTok has become an important social media service for young politicians. Many young politicians are building a career on social media. For example, in the spring parliamentary elections, young people who campaigned almost only on social media got into Parliament.

A study has been carried out in Finland on how politicians use the TikTok service. Short videos are published on TikTok. Young people use TikTok a lot. That is why TikTok is an easy way for many young politicians to reach voters.

TikTok users like videos in which a politician seems close and does ordinary things. Many people also like it if a politician speaks in their own dialect.

## Wine consumption

![Muddy wine bottles in wall racks in the cellar of a winery.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7952,h_4473,x_0,y_410/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-847536612b80933adba/1630484658)

Open image viewer

Wine bottles in the storage of a winery. Photo: Rinna Härkönen / Yle

Inflation, that is, the rise in prices, has reduced wine consumption in Europe. Demand for wine has fallen, but wine production has increased. That is why wine may be left unsold at wineries.

Changes in drinking habits have also reduced wine consumption in Europe. For example, the popularity of beer has grown while the popularity of wine has fallen.

Since last year, wine consumption has fallen, for example, by about 30 percent in Portugal, about 20 percent in Germany and about 15 percent in France.

## Finnish Nature Day

![A silver-washed fritillary butterfly basks in the sun by a thistle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2877,h_1620,x_0,y_270/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98501862da3b4d89a44/1658469268)

Open image viewer

The silver-washed fritillary is a colourful butterfly species. It can be seen in Finland. Photo: Antti Seppälä / Yle

Finnish Nature Day was celebrated on Saturday. The purpose of the day is to increase appreciation of nature.

Celebrating Finnish Nature Day was started 10 years ago. This year Finnish Nature Day is also a flag day for the first time.

Finnish Nature Day is always celebrated on the last Saturday of August.
