# Tuesday 17.9.2019 (radio)

The government agreed on the state budget. Tampere's new children's hospital has opened. Students of the Hive Helsinki coding school can get study grants. Storytel bought Gummerus. Bird autumn migration is now busy.

## Government agreed on money

![Minister of Finance Mika Lintilä (Centre)](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10975754)

Open image viewer

Mika Lintilä is the government's "money minister". Photo: Lehtikuva

The first state budget proposal of Antti Rinne's government is ready. The government started negotiations on the budget this Tuesday morning and finished them in the early evening.

In the morning, the party leaders of the governing parties said what kind of negotiations they expected. Many party leaders said that the most important thing is to try to increase jobs. The government is trying to increase the number of Finns who work by 60,000.

In the negotiations, the governing parties agreed how much tax the state will collect next year. The parties also agreed on what the tax money will be used for. Now the government's proposal for the state budget has been created, and it still has to be approved in Parliament.

## Tampere got a new children's hospital

![Corridor of the new children's and young people's hospital in Tampere](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10925784)

Open image viewer

A hedgehog on the wall of the new children's hospital. Photo: Heli Mansikka / Yle

Tampere's new children's hospital has opened.

The first patients moved into the children's hospital on Monday. The children's cancer ward moved first. The hospital's emergency department moved into the new building today, Tuesday. All wards will move by 9 October. After that, illnesses of the body and mind of all children and young people will be treated in the same building.

The Tampere children's hospital was built entirely with tax money.

## Study grants for coding school students

![Hive Helsinki Piscine course participants.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10970960)

Open image viewer

Young people at the entrance exams of Hive Helsinki. Photo: Hive Helsinki

Students of the new coding school Hive Helsinki can get study grants. Kela decided on the matter today.

The Hive Helsinki school is owned by the Finnish game company Supercell. The school teaches the profession of a coder, that is, making computer programs. The school's teaching is similar to the teaching of the School 42 in Paris. Studies last three years. The school has no teachers, no classes and no textbooks. Students learn by solving problems together. The teaching is free.

Supercell founded the school, because Finland needs many different kinds of people as coders.

## Gummerus gets a new owner

![A young person reads an e-book.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10379499)

Open image viewer

Storytel sells audiobooks and e-books. Photo: Pexels / Pixabay

Storytel, a company that sells audiobooks, is buying the publisher Gummerus.

Gummerus is Finland's third largest book publisher. Gummerus will continue to operate as an independent part of Storytel. Anna Baijars will continue as managing director of Gummerus. Gummerus's authors include Matti Rönkä, Minna Rytisalo, Pauliina Rauhala and Jussi Adler-Olsen.

Storytel was founded in Sweden in 2005. Its audiobook service works in 16 countries.

## Farewell to migrating birds

![Geese at Oravilahti in Rääkkylä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6308136)

Open image viewer

Geese are also heading south. Photo: Heli Tolvanen

Many migratory birds are now setting off for the south. When the air gets colder and a north wind blows, cranes, for example, set off.

Many small birds also fly with the wind towards the south. Most of the small insect-eating birds have already left, but there are still many swallows in Finland.

Bird autumn migration lasts until October.
