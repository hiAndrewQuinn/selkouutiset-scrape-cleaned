# Sunday 28.7.2019 (radio)

Kela will pay extra money to young students. Swimming ability is an important thing. The night without darkness ends. The weather cools down.

## Kela pays students extra money

![Cooking student Lauri Siivonen likes to make challenging dishes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10687969)

Kela will start paying learning materials money to students, in addition to the study grant, for students who study in a vocational school or upper secondary school after comprehensive school.

The extra money can be received by students whose family has a low income.

The learning materials money is 46 euros and 80 cents a month. Over the whole of studies the sum is about 1400 euros.

Student organisations believe that the extra money is a good thing.

The bad thing, however, is that many students have to buy a lot of books and supplies right at the start of school.

A lot of money is spent at once. Kela, however, pays the extra money a little at a time.

## Swimming ability is an important thing

![Many people in swimwear on a sandy beach and in the shallow sea water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10896506)

Open image viewer

Swimming ability is an important thing. Photo: Anniina Wallius / Yle

This year, 6 children have drowned in Finland. That is a lot, because last year no children drowned.

Swimming teachers say that many people believe they can swim well. That may not always be true.

\- People easily overestimate their own swimming skills, says swimming teacher Niko Nieminen.

A person is a good swimmer if they can swim 200 metres after falling into deep water. Of the distance, 50 metres must be swum on the back.

In total, 54 people have drowned in Finland this year. Most of them are adults.

## The night without darkness ends

![Saana fell at Kilpisjärvi bathes in the midnight sun.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10266977)

Open image viewer

It is this bright in summer on Saana fell in the light of the midnight sun. Photo: Kaisa Siren / AOP

In Northern Finland the night without darkness ends tomorrow night. That means the sun sets below the horizon again.

The sun has not set below the horizon at all for 2 months.

The northernmost municipality in Finland is Utsjoki.

There, in the northernmost village of Nuorgam, the sun sets tomorrow night at 00.42.

The sun rises at 2.08, so the dim part of the night is still very short.

## The weather is cooling in Finland

![A thirsty dog in the lake in Repovesi National Park in Kouvola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10323724)

Today in Southern Finland it was over 30 degrees warm in many places. The highest temperature of the day was 33.6 degrees.

Tomorrow, Monday, the weather will cool down.

Cold air will come to the whole country. The temperature will fall and across the whole country it will be about 15 degrees warm.
