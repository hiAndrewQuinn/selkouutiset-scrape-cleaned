## The border crisis between Poland and Belarus

![Putin and Lukashenka](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2542,h_1430,x_0,y_71/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-878602618d1092bce33/1636634856)

Belarusian President Aljaksandr Lukašenka and Russian President Vladimir Putin. Photo: Mikhail Metzel / AOP

The crisis on the border between Poland and Belarus continues. There are many people at the border who are trying to get to Poland and elsewhere in the European Union.

There are, for example, people at the border who come from the Middle East. Poland says that Belarus has sent them towards the European Union.

The European Union suspects that Russia supports Belarus's actions. Russian President Vladimir Putin says that Russia has nothing to do with the crisis.

## Finland's football win

![Huuhkajat celebrate a win in Zenica.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5294,h_2977,x_0,y_589/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-879463618fe9d8beb31/1636824878)

Open image viewer

The Finnish team celebrates an important win. Photo: Lehtikuva

More sport: Finland has won an important football match.

On Saturday Finland won the World Cup qualifying match against Bosnia and Herzegovina. Finland beat Bosnia and Herzegovina 3-1.

Finland's first goal was scored by Marcus Forss. Soon after that Finland's Jukka Raitala made a mistake and was sent off the field.

However, the Finnish team continued a fine game and scored 2 more goals. The goal scorers were Robin Lood and Daniel O'Shaughnessy.

Bosnia scored only 1 goal in the game. So Finland clearly beat Bosnia 3-1.

The win means that Finland has a chance to reach the football World Cup. The football World Cup will be held next year in Qatar.
