## The income register comes into use next year

![100 and 200 euro notes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10409088)

Open image viewer

The Tax Administration maintains the new income register and promises that data security is in order. Photo: European Central Bank

A new income register comes into use in Finland at the turn of the year.

It gathers together, for example, citizens' salary information. Pensions and benefits will also appear in the register after a year.

The register is maintained by the Tax Administration. It says that the data security of the register is in order.

Officials can see only information that belongs to their work tasks. Many authorities can access the information, at first, for example, the tax authorities, Kela and the employment pension institutions.

Ordinary citizens can see only their own information.

## The South Korean president received a letter from North Korea

![Moon Jae-in and Kim Jong-un shake hands in Pyongyang.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10411844)

Open image viewer

Relations between North Korea and South Korea are important in world politics. Photo: AFP

The leader of North Korea wants to meet the President of South Korea more regularly next year.

North Korea's leader Kim Jong-un has sent a letter to President Moon Jae-in. Kim proposes talks on nuclear disarmament on the Korean peninsula.

Kim and Moon have met 3 times this year. At the last summit, Kim promised to visit Seoul, the capital of South Korea. It is possible that the visit will happen in the early part of next year.

## NOW: Saudi Arabia has hired child soldiers

![A man stands among the ruins of a factory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10570354)

Open image viewer

The New York Times writes that Saudi Arabia has hired child soldiers for the war in Yemen. Photo: AFP

The New York Times newspaper reports that Saudi Arabia has hired child soldiers. Child soldiers have been hired from Sudan's Darfur to fight on Saudi Arabia's side in the war in Yemen.

The paper has interviewed several underage people who have returned from the front. The child soldiers revealed that Saudi Arabia pays them just under 500 euros a month.

Sudan is part of the coalition against Yemen led by the Saudis.

Saudi Arabia denies recruiting children to the front.

## Fireworks seized from a flat in Hamburg

![Fireworks](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10574733)

Open image viewer

Photo: AOP

In Germany the Hamburg police report a large seizure of fireworks.

The police seized 850 kilograms of fireworks and banger fireworks from the flat of a 23-year-old man. In Germany a private person may keep at most 1 kilogram of fireworks.

Last New Year's in Germany 2 people died because of fireworks. 5 people lost body parts, and 1 child was injured.

## The New Year's weather is bad for fireworks

![A snowstorm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9946108)

Open image viewer

In Central Finland and Kainuu up to 20 centimetres of snow may fall. Photo: Riikka Rautiainen, Yle Kemi

Finland's year changes in snowfall and strong wind. That is why New Year's Eve is not good for fireworks.

Yle's meteorologist Matti Huutonen says that a strong low-pressure system is moving across Finland at the turn of the year. The bad weather continues on New Year's Day too.

During the night towards Wednesday there may be a storm. Power cuts are also possible.
