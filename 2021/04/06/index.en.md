The European Medicines Agency continues to study the Astra Zeneca corona vaccine. There are 14 new corona deaths in Finland. Most married couples use the husband's surname. The weather is cool and rainy.

## The Astra Zeneca corona vaccine

![A nurse preparing the Astra Zeneca vaccine at the Porvoo vaccination centre. 18.3.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_169/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-785695605350e2425c4/1616073027)

The side effects of the Astra Zeneca corona vaccine are being studied. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The European Medicines Agency continues to study the Astra Zeneca corona vaccine. It has been suspected that the corona vaccine is linked to cases of blood clots.

The head of the agency's vaccines department now says that the Astra Zeneca corona vaccine may cause blood clots. However, it is not yet known what in the vaccine causes blood clots, that is, blockages in the blood circulation.

The European Medicines Agency will announce this officially later.

In Finland, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare is waiting for the medicines agency's official information. Only after that will it be decided how vaccinations with Astra Zeneca will continue in Finland.

At the moment, only people over 65 are being vaccinated with Astra Zeneca in Finland. Astra Zeneca has not caused blood clots in people of that age.

## Finland's corona situation

![A sign showing the sampling of corona tests at the Seinäjoki health centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7913946068010fba327/1617428855)

The number of new corona infections is falling. Photo: Elina Niemistö / Yle

Since Thursday, 14 people have died of corona in Finland. Corona has caused a total of 860 deaths in Finland.

On Tuesday, a little over 300 new corona infections were reported. However, the corona situation has improved a little. There are now fewer new corona infections than some time ago.

The number of corona patients has also fallen in the hospitals of the Helsinki and Uusimaa area.

One reason for the better corona situation is that corona vaccinations are progressing. Soon more than a million Finns will have received a corona vaccination.

## Married couples' surnames

![A married couple holding hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9577189)

Open image viewer

Photo: AOP

Most married couples use the husband's surname.

About 60 percent of new married couples took the husband's surname last year.

Only under 2 percent of married couples took the wife's surname. About 25 percent of married couples decided that each spouse keeps their own surname.

Married couples can freely choose a surname. However, many people think it is an old custom that the husband's name becomes the shared surname of the married couple.

But the custom is not very old. It became common in the 1800s.

Only in 1930 did a law state that a wife must take her husband's surname. The obligation ended in 1985.
