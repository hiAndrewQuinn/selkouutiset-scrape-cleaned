## Baltic herring

![Dead Baltic herring.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4920,h_2767,x_0,y_410/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117830965154524db691/1695990796)

The catch quota for Baltic herring is being reduced, because Baltic herring catches have got worse. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

An agreement has been reached in the European Union on fishing. The EU Agriculture and Fisheries Council has decided on new fishing quotas.

Baltic herring fishing will continue as normal, but the fish quotas must be reasonable.

In an earlier proposal, Baltic herring fishing was banned. That caused a lot of worry in Finland. It is reported from the Fisheries Central Union that the European Commission interpreted scientific research strictly.

So now Baltic herring fishing will continue in Finnish coastal waters, but the quota is being cut. For example, in the Gulf of Bothnia the Baltic herring quota falls by 31 percent. In the main basin of the Baltic Sea and in the Gulf of Finland the quota falls by 43 percent.

The purpose of the cut is to protect the Baltic herring, whose stock has weakened.

Finland's Minister of Agriculture, Sari Essayah, says that the new EU decision is responsible. It is also the kind of decision that Finland wanted.

## Sea accident

![On a map, the German islands of Helgoland and Langeoog in the North Sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1080,h_607,x_0,y_304/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-119031565377a53aa4a3/1698137121)

In the early evening it was still unclear how the ships collided. Photo: Harri Vähäkangas / Yle, MapCreator, OpenStreetMap

2 cargo ships collided early on Tuesday morning in the North Sea.

The early evening information said that 1 person has been found dead, 2 have been rescued and 4 are still missing. The information may still change.

The accident happened near the island of Helgoland, which belongs to Germany. In the evening it was not yet known how the collision happened.

In addition to searching for the missing, an aircraft is monitoring whether oil is leaking from the ships into the North Sea.

## Equality in Finland

![An illustration with graduation caps of different colours on a red background. In the middle are yellow tulips.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1106069644bb97bb0863/1682684500)

Open image viewer

Equality in education still needs to be improved in Finland. Photo: Venla Tirkkonen / Yle

Finland came eighth in the EU gender equality comparison. Finland ranked above the EU average in the comparison.

The comparison measured equality in the labour market, in health care and in the exercise of power, among other things. Finland's scores rose in health and work.

On the other hand, segregation in education and in leisure time lowered the scores.

Sweden ranked first in the comparison, the Netherlands second and Denmark third.

## The world's oldest dog

![A dog walks in front of a house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1190400653790edd4521/1698140513)

Bobi was photographed in February in the back yard of his home in the village of Conqueiros. Photo: Patricia De Melo Modeira / AFP

The world's oldest dog, Bobi, has died. The dog died at home in Portugal on Saturday, the British broadcaster BBC reported.

The purebred Alentejo dog was 31 years and 165 days old.

The previous record had stood for almost 100 years. In 1939 in Australia the dog Bluey died at the age of 29 years and 5 months.
