# Tuesday 11.4.2023 (radio)

Green Party chairman Maria Ohisalo is ending her work as chairman. The new Parliament's term has begun. Finns' work capacity and functional capacity are getting worse. There is a lot of street dust now. Weather forecast.

## Green Party chairman Maria Ohisalo

Green Party chairman Maria Ohisalo is ending her work as chairman. Ohisalo will not stand for a new chairmanship term at the June party congress.

The Greens had an election defeat in the parliamentary elections. The party lost 7 seats. Ohisalo says that she is responsible for the defeat.

Ohisalo says that it is good for the Greens to choose a new leadership now. A new chairman will be elected at the Greens' party congress in June.

## The new Parliament begins

![Sanna Marin hands over her credentials.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4999,h_2811,x_0,y_272/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-109769864355cbdb4969/1681219788)

The term of the new Parliament has begun. On Tuesday MPs presented their credentials to Parliament, that is, documents showing that they have been elected to the post.

The parliamentary groups of the parties held meetings, where the chairs of the parliamentary groups were decided. Many parliamentary groups chose their former chairs to continue also in this term.

Kai Mykkänen will continue as chair of the National Coalition's parliamentary group, Eeva Kalli will continue as chair of the Centre's parliamentary group, and Jussi Saramo will continue as chair of the Left Alliance's parliamentary group.

Peter Östman will continue as chair of the Christian Democrats' parliamentary group, and Antti Lindtman will continue as chair of the SDP's parliamentary group. Lulu Ranne will become chair of the Finns Party's group.

## Finns' work capacity

![Person walking in a wintry, sunny landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4866,h_2737,x_728,y_656/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107602663f5cd5263a19/1677053623)

Exercise often helps work capacity and functional capacity. Photo: Milena Hackman / Yle

Finns' work capacity and functional capacity are getting worse. The Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, and Kela have studied the well-being of Finns.

Finns' work capacity and functional capacity began to get worse about 10 years ago. Mental health problems, obesity and poor nutrition have increased. Little exercise, smoking and intoxicants also worsen work and functional capacity.

The report by THL and Kela says that the new government must strengthen Finns' work and functional capacity. One way could be a tax on foods that have a lot of sugar, salt or fat.

## Street dust

![In Nekala on Vihiojantie, the road was cleaned with a big machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3718,h_2091,x_0,y_186/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-94480962626645dfee1/1650616209)

There is now a lot of street dust in many cities. The weather is dry and sunny. No rain falls. That is why the street dust situation continues to be difficult.

Because of street dust, air quality has been poor in many cities. There is a lot of dust because there is a lot of gritting sand on the streets.

Cities have started to collect sand from the streets. There is a lot of work. That is why it may take many weeks for all the sand to be swept away.
