# Wednesday 20.2.2019 (radio)

There are fewer young people without work or education than before. Russian President Vladimir Putin promises money for families with children. The French have protested against hate speech at demonstration marches. Nine people have been executed in Egypt. Consumers' choices cause 70 percent of Finland's climate emissions.

## President Putin has spoken to Parliament

![Putin gave his annual speech on 20 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10655358)

President Vladimir Putin spoke to the Russian Congress. Photo: Alexander Nemenov / AFP

Russian President Vladimir Putin has given a speech to the country's parliament. Putin said that the lives of Russians will improve this year.

Putin promised more money especially for families with children. He said that child benefits will be raised and tax cuts will be given to families with many children.

In his speech, Putin also said that Russia's economy must grow by at least 3 percent in 2 years.

## The French protested against hate speech

![Yellow vests also took part in the Paris demonstration, distancing themselves from antisemitism. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10654347)

Yellow vest protesters also took part in the Paris demonstration. Photo: Juha Nurminen

There have been big demonstrations in France.

The French opposed hate speech directed at Jews. Tens of thousands of people took part in the demonstration marches in different parts of the country.

Recently, there have been more attacks against Jews in France than before. President Emmanuel Macron promises that France will punish the perpetrators of hate crimes.

## Egypt executed people on death row

![This is how the place where Hisham Barakat was murdered looked in June 2015. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10654953)

Open image viewer

This is how the place where Hisham Barakat was murdered looked in June 2015. Photo: Khaled Elfiqi / EPA

Egypt has executed nine people.

They were sentenced to death 2 years ago for the murder of Hisham Barakat, a senior prosecutor. He was killed in 2015. A total of 28 people were sentenced to death for the murder.

The executed people are suspected of having been members of the Egyptian Muslim Brotherhood. The human rights organisation Amnesty is calling on Egypt to stop the executions.

## There are fewer marginalised young people than before

![Ohjaamo, marginalisation, project](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10653067)

Open image viewer

There are fewer young people who are completely inactive than before. Photo: Jussi Mankkinen / Yle

Fewer young people than before have been marginalised.

A report says that there are fewer young people who have no work or education at all than before. The number has dropped by 14,000 young people in 3 years.

The marginalisation of young people differs in different parts of Finland. For example, in Eastern Finland and South-Eastern Finland it is harder to get a job or start studying than in Western Finland.

## Consumers' choices cause most of the climate emissions

![Wind turbine](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10362177)

Open image viewer

Consumers' choices affect climate change. Photo: Kari Kosonen / Yle

Consumers' choices cause 70 percent of Finland's climate emissions.

Food causes 20 percent of a Finn's emissions, and housing 30 percent. Replacing meat with plant proteins and choosing green electricity would reduce emissions a lot. Green electricity means, for example, electricity made with wind power, solar energy or hydropower.
