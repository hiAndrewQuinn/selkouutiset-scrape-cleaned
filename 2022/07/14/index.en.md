## Price rises

Price rises continue. In June prices were 7.8 percent higher than a year ago.

Prices have not risen this fast for a long time. Prices rose even faster last time, about 40 years ago.

The price of petrol and electricity has risen the most. Expensive energy is also one reason for the rise of other prices, that is, inflation.

Inflation is also visible in food prices. Fish and coffee have become the most expensive.

## Uniper

![Blue sign reading Uniper.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_525/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97992462c83dbee0309/1657372540)

Open image viewer

Uniper operates in the energy sector. Photo: Ina Fassbender / AFP

Finland and Germany are negotiating about Uniper. Uniper is a big German energy company.

Uniper has financial problems. It sold cheap Russian gas to customers before Russia attacked Ukraine.

Now less gas comes from Russia, and Uniper has to obtain more expensive gas from elsewhere. Uniper loses millions of euros every day, because its customers pay the same amount for gas as before.

Uniper's biggest owner is the Finnish energy company Fortum. Fortum belongs to the Finnish state.

Finland and Germany are now negotiating how Uniper is rescued and who pays for it.

## European forest fires

![Metsähallitus's restoration burning, that is, a controlled forest fire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4320,h_2430,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82524060d45e01013fa/1656499657)

Archive photo of a forest fire. Photo: Ari Lahtinen / Metsähallitus

There are big forest fires in Europe.

In France 4000 hectares of forest have already burned. Also in Portugal and Spain there are terrain fires.

Forest and terrain fires spread easily now, because the weather in Southern Europe is very hot. In Spain the temperature has been as high as 45 degrees.

Extreme weather phenomena increase because of climate change. Extreme weather phenomena are, for example, drought and heat.
