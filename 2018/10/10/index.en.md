# Wednesday 10.10.2018 (radio)

A meat company is laying off workers. There was heavy rain on the island of Mallorca. A serious bus accident happened in Kenya. Swans are migrating south. Today is the day of Finland's national writer.

## HKScan is laying off many people

![broiler cuts in production facilities](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9784630)

Open image viewer

Finns eat a lot of meat in summer and at Christmas. Photo: Jari Pelkonen / Yle

The meat and food company HKScan is laying off many employees. 165 employees will lose their jobs.

The company says that it also has to lay off employees who will stay at work.

At Christmas the meat company does well, because Finns buy Christmas hams. In summer Finns buy grill sausages and meat.

In spring and autumn the company does worse.

## Tourists died in floods on Mallorca

![Two police cars are parked in the middle of the road, with blue lights flashing. Two men in bright yellow overalls and a woman in a uniform stand on the road. On the right edge of the picture, there is a road sign for Sant Llorenç.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10449295)

It rained a lot in a short time on Mallorca. This caused serious floods. Photo: Atienza / EPA

On the Mediterranean island of Mallorca there have been serious floods because of heavy rain. Yesterday evening a lot of water fell in a short time.

At least 9 people have died and many people are missing.

Among the dead were tourists from Britain and the Netherlands. A lot of tourists visit Mallorca.

The island belongs to Spain.

## A serious bus accident happened in Kenya

![Remains of a bus and people around it](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10449606)

Open image viewer

The roof of the bus came off when the bus fell 20 metres. Photo: EPA

A serious bus accident has happened in Kenya. 50 people died in the accident.

The Kenyan police say that the bus drove off the road while going downhill. The bus fell 20 metres, and the roof of the bus came off.

Many accidents happen on the roads in Kenya. Thousands of people die in traffic accidents every year.

## Swans are migrating south

![Swans on a winter river.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10016739)

Open image viewer

Whooper swans on the Kajaani river in Northern Finland. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

Swans start to migrate south at this time of year. Swans leave Finland's winter for the warmth of the south.

Swans are already setting off, at least from Lapland. They fly the same routes every year.

Swans always stop to eat in the same places, when they gather strength for the journey. Then bird watchers can see thousands of swans at one time.

## Today is the day of Finland's national writer

![statue](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5212047)

Open image viewer

Aleksis Kivi has many statues in Finland. This one is in Helsinki. Photo: Pertti Nisonen

Today, Tuesday, Aleksis Kivi Day has been celebrated.

Aleksis Kivi is Finland's national writer. Kivi's most important book is Seven Brothers. It came out in 1870, almost 150 years ago.

Researcher Teemu Keskisarja says that Kivi failed his mother tongue at school. Keskisarja has written a study about Aleksis Kivi.
