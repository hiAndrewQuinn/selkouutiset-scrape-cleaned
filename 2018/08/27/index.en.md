# Monday 28.8.2018 (radio)

Many municipalities give upper secondary students benefits worth thousands of euros. The Kuopio accident bus was driving at great excess speed. Men take partial early old-age pensions more often than women. The position of Paralympic athletes in athletics is to be investigated.

## Upper secondary students' benefits differ

![Computer of a student at Alppila upper secondary school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9173524)

A laptop is the most common benefit that many upper secondary schools offer. Photo: Saija Nironen / Yle

Many municipalities give upper secondary students benefits worth thousands of euros. A Yle survey says that 42 percent of upper secondary schools give, for example, a free or almost free laptop.

Karstula upper secondary school in Central Finland gives a laptop for free, travel to school and first-year textbooks. In addition, a student who moves gets 200 euros a month in expense compensation. At the other extreme, Laanila upper secondary school in Oulu gives its students only pencils.

The approximately 100,000 upper secondary students in Finland are in unequal positions because of the different benefits.

## The accident bus was speeding

![The accident bus was lifted off the railway late on Friday evening.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10370360)

Open image viewer

No fault has been found with the accident bus's brakes. However, the bus was driving at great excess speed on the ramp. Photo: Timo Hartikainen / Lehtikuva

The Kuopio accident bus was driving at great excess speed.

The Accident Investigation Board says that the speed in the junction area was almost 100 kilometres per hour. In the junction area, the speed may be only between zero and 20 kilometres per hour. Towards the end of the journey, passengers asked the driver to slow down.

No fault has been found with the bus's brakes. Investigators are now examining, for example, the tachograph and risks in the bus's pedals.

The accident happened on Friday. Four people died, and several were injured. The bus was on its way from Sweden towards Kuopio. The bus hit five passenger cars on the ramp and plunged from the bridge down onto the railway line.

## Men retire early more than women

![A couple on a park bench.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9907891)

Open image viewer

Many want to retire even before the normal old-age pension age. Photo: Pixabay

Men take partial early old-age pensions more often than women. A survey by Suomen Tietotoimisto says that only just over 30 percent of those are women.

One reason is men's lower life expectancy. In addition, men's pensions are on average larger than women's pensions.

About 20,000 people have applied for a partial early old-age pension in the last year and a half.

## The position of Paralympic athletes in athletics is being investigated

![Leo-Pekka Tähti](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10353839)

Open image viewer

Wheelchair racer Leo-Pekka Tähti brought Finland 3 gold medals and 1 silver from Berlin. Finns do well in Paralympic athletics. Photo: AOP

The treatment of Paralympic athletes, that is, athletes with disabilities, in athletics is going to be investigated.

Last week it became public that Paralympic athletes have been charged a 900-euro deductible if they do not win a medal at major championships. The position of Paralympic athletes is one topic at a meeting that will consider Finland's weak success in athletics.

The European Championships in Paralympic athletics in Berlin ended on Sunday. Finnish Paralympic athletes won 14 medals, 8 of which were gold.
