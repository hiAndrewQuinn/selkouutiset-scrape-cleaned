# Sunday 12.8.2018 (radio)

Finland's budget. The foreign minister's opinions. A probe towards the Sun. Cheap fashion clothes. Unsettled weather.

## The prime minister talks about the budget

![Jüri Ratas and Juha Sipilä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10349063)

Open image viewer

Finland's prime minister (on the right) met Estonia's prime minister (on the left) at a cycling event on Sunday. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä says that the government may raise the very smallest daily allowances and guarantee pensions if the Finnish state has extra money left over.

There may be extra money because Finland's economy is now doing well. Prime Minister Sipilä also says that taxation must not get tighter.

The prime minister's comments are related to the Finnish state budget, which the ministries are now making for next year.

The government will decide at the end of this month what the budget will finally be like.

## The foreign minister's opinions

![Timo Soini](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10346976)

Open image viewer

Finland's foreign minister Timo Soini is against abortion. Finland's government accepts abortion. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Foreign Minister Timo Soini's opinions are again being talked about a lot.

Foreign Minister Soini has many times said publicly that he is against abortion. Many people criticise Soini for this opinion.

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä says that Soini has the right to his own opinion.

But Finland's government supports the right of women and girls to have an abortion.

## A probe travels towards the Sun

![The Delta IV Heavy launch rocket](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10349049)

Open image viewer

The probe was launched into space on board a launch rocket Photo: Bill Ingalls / NASA

The United States launched a probe into space today.

The probe travels towards the Sun. The probe carries research instruments.

The instruments study the Sun's atmosphere, the corona. The instruments collect information about, among other things, solar storms.

The probe will go closer to the Sun than any earlier device.

## Clothes cause a lot of emissions

![The shop window of the Topshop clothes store in London.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10289883)

Open image viewer

Big clothing chains sell cheap fashion clothes. Photo: Andy Rain / EPA

Cheap fashion clothes are a bad thing for the environment.

People buy a lot of cheap clothes and soon throw them in the rubbish.

A study has been done on this in Britain. The study reveals that most clothes end up in landfill.

Making clothes uses a lot of water. Making clothes also causes more emissions than air and sea traffic.

## Tomorrow will be unsettled

Tomorrow, Monday, the weather is unsettled.

In Southern and Central Finland it is cloudy and rains at times. In Northern Finland it may rain a lot.

During the day the temperature is between 15 and 20 degrees. It is cooler in the north.
