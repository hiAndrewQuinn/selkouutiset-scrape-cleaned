The Glasgow climate meeting. Environmental organisations. Father's Day. The weather.

## The climate meeting ended

![The chair of the climate meeting, Alok Sharma, gets applause at the closing ceremony of the meeting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12187556)

The UN climate meeting, that is the United Nations climate meeting, has ended. The climate meeting was in Scotland, in Glasgow.

At the Glasgow climate meeting, the countries of the world agreed on how they will try to slow down global warming.

The goal is that the climate warms by 1.5 degrees at most. That is why carbon dioxide emissions must be cut quickly.

Coal energy, that is burning hard coal, in particular increases carbon dioxide emissions and global warming. That is why it was agreed in Glasgow that the use of coal will be reduced.

In Glasgow it was also agreed that rich countries will give poor countries more support for climate action. Many poor countries suffer a lot from global warming.

Almost 200 countries are part of the Glasgow agreement.

## Environmental organisations were disappointed

![Greenpeace climate activists showed their opinion in Glasgow on Monday. Farzana Faruck is speaking, Maria Reyes is standing on the right.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2311,h_1299,x_0,y_366/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-87519061829eece440d/1635950531)

For example, the Greenpeace organisation demands more effective climate action. Photo: Jeremy Sutton-Hibbert / Greenpeace

Many environmental organisations are disappointed with the Glasgow climate meeting.

The environmental organisations were especially disappointed with what was agreed in Glasgow about coal. In Glasgow it was agreed that the use of coal will be reduced. In the opinion of the environmental organisations, the use of coal must be stopped completely.

The environmental organisations are worried that the climate may warm by more than 1.5 degrees. If that happens, nature and people will suffer. Drought and heavy rains will harm farming, and there will be a shortage of food if the climate warms too much.

## Father's Day was celebrated

![Father's Day drawings by kindergarten children](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6924268)

Open image viewer

Children often make a Father's Day card themselves. Photo: Elina Mäyrä / Yle

In Finland, Father's Day was celebrated on Sunday. On Father's Day, fathers and grandfathers are celebrated. Father's Day is a flag day.

In Finland, Father's Day is always the second Sunday of November. At that time, Father's Day is also celebrated in Finland's neighbouring countries Sweden, Norway and Estonia, and in Iceland.

This week the Father of the Year awards were also handed out. This year the award was given to 3 fathers. The award went to Marko Oikarainen from Kouvola, Sergei Silkin from Helsinki and Dan Wickholm from Sipoo.

We told you more about the award winners last Wednesday in Clear News. You can find the news about the Fathers of the Year on the Clear News website at yle.fi/selkouutiset.
