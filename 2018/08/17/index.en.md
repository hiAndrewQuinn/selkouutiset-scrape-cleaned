# Friday 17.8.2018 (radio)

It is a year since the Turku terror attack. The flood in Kerala is bad. The situation in Afghanistan is bad. A Finnair pilot was drunk. The warm weather continues

## It is a year since the Turku terror attack

![Memorial candles at Turku market square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9796051)

Open image viewer

2 people died in the terror attack. Photo: Jaakko Stenroos / AOP

Tomorrow, Saturday, it will be a year since the terror attack that happened in Turku, Finland.

Two demonstrations will be held in Turku tomorrow. The demonstrations are linked to the terror attack.

One of the demonstrations is organised by a neo-Nazi organisation. The other demonstration is against neo-Nazis.

The police are prepared for the demonstrations to be restless.

In the terror attack a year ago, a Moroccan man killed 2 people. 8 people were injured.

## A bad flood in Kerala, India

![People in a rubber boat in Kochi city in Kerala.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10357106)

Open image viewer

In Kerala you have to get around by rubber boat. Photo: Prakash Elamakkara / EPA

There is a bad flood in the southern part of the Indian state of Kerala.

Many people have died in the flood. 150 000 people have had to leave their homes because floodwater is rising into houses.

In Kerala a lot of rain falls during the monsoon season. This time the rain is really heavy.

## It is dangerous in Afghanistan

![Map of Taliban areas in Afghanistan](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10358490)

Open image viewer

The Taliban controls the orange areas. Taliban influence is felt in the yellow areas. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Afghanistan is now really dangerous, says a Finnish expert. There are battles and terror attacks all the time, says Olli Ruohomäki of the Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs.

The Taliban organisation attacks the Afghan army. The terror organisation Isis carries out attacks against Shia Muslims. Criminal groups kidnap ordinary people.

The Taliban organisation controls a large part of Afghanistan.

However, the Finnish Immigration Service is of the view that some areas of Afghanistan are safe. That is why Finland returns asylum seekers to Afghanistan.

## Finnair pilot was drunk

![Passengers sit in the cabin while the aircraft is landing at the airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10022649)

Open image viewer

Other employees noticed the pilot. Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

A pilot of the airline Finnair may have tried to fly the aircraft while drunk. Other employees noticed the pilot yesterday.

The police had the pilot blow into a breathalyser. The pilot is suspected of air traffic intoxication.

The pilot was dismissed immediately. The aircraft was still on the ground at Helsinki airport.
