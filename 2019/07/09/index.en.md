# Tuesday 9.7.2019 (radio)

Pekka Haavisto represents the EU in Sudan. A railway tunnel is wanted between Helsinki and Tallinn. The Lahti city data network was hit by a cyber attack. Households produce more waste than before. Saturn can now be seen in the night sky.

## Foreign Minister Haavisto to Sudan

![Pekka Haavisto / Foreign Minister / Ritaripuisto Helsinki 24.06.2019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4236,h_2383,x_0,y_13/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5726785d10a07f4d805/1561370811)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

Finland's Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto is discussing the situation in Sudan on behalf of the European Union.

The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says that Haavisto has permission from the EU's foreign policy chief, Federica Mogherini, to discuss the situation in Sudan on behalf of the whole EU.

Haavisto will travel to Sudan in the coming period. He will also visit several other countries in the neighbouring region.

Sudan was led for 30 years by the dictator Omar al-Bashir. In April the army took power.

There have been many demonstrations in Sudan, demanding a civilian government for the country.

## A tunnel from Helsinki to Tallinn

![Peter Vesterbacka](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10868474)

Open image viewer

Businessman Peter Vesterbacka. Photo: Lukas Rusk 7 Yle

A railway tunnel is wanted between Helsinki and Tallinn.

One tunnel plan has been made by the businessman Peter Vesterbacka.

Vesterbacka says that the plan has now taken a step forward. A large Chinese construction company, CREC, is joining the project.

If the plan is carried out, building the tunnel may take perhaps 6 years. Construction may employ up to 20,000 people a year.

The Vesterbacka tunnel project costs perhaps 15 billion euros. Vesterbacka has a preliminary agreement on financing with Chinese parties.

Finnish and Estonian authorities are also planning a tunnel between Helsinki and Tallinn.

## Cyber attack on Lahti

![Cyber attack](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9614029)

Open image viewer

Attackers spread malware in the Lahti city data network. Photo: Richie B Tongo / EPA

The police are investigating a cyber attack that was made on the data networks of the city of Lahti. The attack was made in June.

The police say that the attack was deliberate. The perpetrator or perpetrators spread malware in the Lahti city data network. Because of the malware, there are still disruptions, for example in the area's social and health services.

At least 1,000 devices were hit by the attack.

## Household waste

![Landfill](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9297513)

Open image viewer

Mixed waste at a landfill. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

Households produce more waste than before, Statistics Finland says.

In 2017 households produced about 3 million tonnes of waste. That is 100,000 tonnes more than the year before.

In total, over 100 million tonnes of waste were produced in Finland in 2017.

## Saturn shines in the night sky

![The planet Saturn.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6590877)

Open image viewer

The planet Saturn. Photo: AURA / STScI / NASA / ESA

Tonight the planet Saturn can be seen in the sky.

The astronomical association Ursa says that Saturn is now shining more brightly than usual. The planet can be seen in the night sky when you look low towards the south.

Saturn is the 6th planet. It is known for the rings that surround it.
