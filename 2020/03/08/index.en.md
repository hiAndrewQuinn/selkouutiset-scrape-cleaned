# Sunday 8.3.2020 (radio)

In Pirkanmaa a school is closed and people are put into isolation because of the coronavirus. In Northern Italy more than 16 million people are being put into isolation. The working conditions of Nepalese chefs have not improved. Aksel Kankaanranta represents Finland at the Eurovision.

## The school in Kangasala is closed because of the coronavirus

![Pitkäjärvi school](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11247246)

In Pirkanmaa people are being put into quarantine because of the coronavirus.

At Pitkäjärvi school in Kangasala, about 90 pupils and some staff have been exposed to the coronavirus. They have been in contact with a person who has been found to have the coronavirus infection. They are being put into quarantine, that is, they must stay at home for a couple of weeks. In addition, the whole school will be closed for at least a week.

Pitkäjärvi lower secondary school has about 500 pupils. It is close to the border of Tampere.

The Pirkanmaa hospital district says that students of the University of Tampere have also been exposed to the coronavirus. About 20 students have been ordered into quarantine.

In total 23 coronavirus infections have now been found in Finland.

## A wide coronavirus quarantine in Northern Italy

![Italy strictly restricts movement in areas marked in yellow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11247090)

Open image viewer

Italy is restricting movement in several provinces in the Northern Italy region. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

In Italy millions of people are going to be isolated because of the coronavirus. More than 16 million people in Northern Italy are being put into isolation

Those put into isolation include, for example, the Lombardy region around Milan and parts of the Venice, Parma and Rimini areas. The isolation lasts about 4 weeks.

No one may come into the area or leave it. The schools in the area are also closed, cultural and sporting events are not held, and cinemas and gyms, for example, are closed.

More than 1,200 new infections have been found in Italy since Friday. In total almost 6,000 people have already got the coronavirus infection in Italy.

## The working conditions of Nepalese chefs have not improved

![Food on a plate in a restaurant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10714890)

Open image viewer

Nepalese food on a plate. Photo: AOP

Helsingin Sanomat has once again examined the working conditions of Nepalese chefs in Finland. Helsingin Sanomat says that the working conditions of many chefs in Nepalese restaurants in Finland are still poor

Helsingin Sanomat reported last year that many chefs came to Finland from Nepal after they had been given false promises. In Finland, however, the chefs have to work very long days. They are also paid very little.

Helsingin Sanomat has now interviewed the chefs again. They say that working conditions are still poor.

Finland's Eurovision representative was chosen in the evening in the New Music Competition in Tampere.

The competition was won by Aksel Kankaanranta from Turku. He won with the song Looking Back.

The Eurovision Song Contest, that is, Eurovision, will be held in May in Rotterdam in the Netherlands.
