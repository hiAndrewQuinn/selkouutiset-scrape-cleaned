Corona pass. Mask recommendation for schools. Corona situation in the Baltics. Green ministers. The weather.

## Corona pass

![A traveller shows a digital corona pass to an official.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11938884)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ramon van Flymen / EPA

Plans for the corona pass are moving forward in Parliament.

The corona pass shows that a person has had corona vaccinations, or a negative corona test result, or has had corona. The information of the corona pass is in the online service Omakanta, whose address is kanta.fi.

The corona pass is an alternative to corona restrictions. For example, nightclubs in the Helsinki region could be open again if the corona pass comes into use.

The corona pass applies to adults. Its lower age limit will probably be 16 years.

An attempt is being made to get the law on the corona pass into force at the end of this week.

## Mask recommendation for schools

![The picture shows a 10-year-old boy with a respirator on his face.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7125215f478c5f2c1e2/1598526836)

In many schools the use of masks is ending. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The joint mask recommendation of schools and educational institutions is ending. Regions and schools can decide for themselves whether pupils must use a mask.

The Ministry of Education says that there have been very few corona infections in schools. That is why it is no longer necessary to use masks in all schools.

## Corona situation in the Baltics

![Nurses go in through the door to the corona patients in the intensive care ward at Tyks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11835833)

Open image viewer

There are far too few nurses, for example in Latvia. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

The corona situation in the Baltic countries is poor. One reason for this is that corona vaccinations have progressed slowly in the Baltics.

In Latvia, for example, less than 50 per cent of residents have been vaccinated against corona.

In Latvia there are so many corona patients that there is no longer room in hospitals for all patients. More places have been made for patients in tents, together with the army.

Latvia's hospitals also have big problems because there are too few health care workers.

Emma Kari is becoming a new minister in the Finnish government. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The Greens are changing ministers. The Greens have 3 ministers in the government.

The Greens decided on Monday that the new Minister of the Interior is Krista Mikkonen. Until now Mikkonen has been the Minister of the Environment.

Emma Kari becomes the Minister of the Environment.

The Greens are changing ministers because of party leader Maria Ohisalo's parental leave. Ohisalo goes on parental leave in November.

Emma Kari is the deputy of Ohisalo in the minister's post.

The Greens also have the post of Foreign Minister in the government. Pekka Haavisto continues in the post of Foreign Minister.
