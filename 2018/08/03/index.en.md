# Friday 3.8.2018 (radio)

Shooting in Sweden. Pupils' phones. The situation of integrated asylum seekers. Apple's value. Thunder showers at the weekend.

## Police actions are being investigated in Sweden

![Swedish police car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10336140)

Open image viewer

The police shot a man on a street in Stockholm, Sweden. Photo: Patrik Österberg / AOP

There was an unpleasant shooting in Sweden yesterday, Thursday.

The police shot a man on a street in Stockholm who had a weapon with him. Only after the shooting did the police notice that the weapon was a toy pistol. It also emerged that the man had an intellectual disability and was autistic. The police said that the man had behaved threateningly.

The man had run away from home, from his parents.

The police are now finding out whether the police saw that the man had a toy gun.

## Teacher guides the use of phones at school

![Pupil browsing a textbook](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10184402)

Open image viewer

In Finland, pupils may use a phone in a lesson when the teacher gives permission. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

Schools in Finland will start soon.

Many first-graders will get their first own phone when school starts. In Finnish schools, a phone may be out in view when the teacher gives permission.

France has a new law. There, pupils may not use phones at school at all. Finland does not want a ban like that. Teachers prefer to teach sensible use of the phone.

## The situation of integrated asylum seekers

![Morteza and Saara](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10326008)

Open image viewer

Morteza (left) works in entrepreneur Saara Lehtonen's company. Photo: Elise Tykkyläinen / Yle

In Finland some asylum seekers have got a job, even though they do not have a residence permit.

They work and learn Finnish. Even so, they may have to leave Finland.

Migration researcher Erna Bodström says that this is contradictory. On the one hand, Finland returns asylum seekers who work and already know some Finnish. On the other hand, there is a shortage of workers in some professions.
