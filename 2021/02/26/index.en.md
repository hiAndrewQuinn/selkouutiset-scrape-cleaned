# Friday 26.2.2021 (radio)

Coronavirus infections. Coronavirus restrictions in the Helsinki region. Upper secondary school students. Electric cars. Herola. Weather forecast.

## Finland's coronavirus figures

![Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11780252)

Open image viewer

Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The number of coronavirus infections is growing.

On Friday the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare said that there are 720 new coronavirus infections in Finland. This number is larger than ever before in one day.

More than 50 percent of the new coronavirus infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa area.

On Friday the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare also reported 2 new coronavirus deaths. In total, 742 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

## Coronavirus restrictions in the Helsinki region

![People doing push-ups in a gym.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10015730)

Open image viewer

Large private sports facilities will also be closed. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

New coronavirus restrictions will come to the Helsinki region next week.

For example, private gyms and other sports facilities will be closed. Spas and swimming halls will also be closed. The facilities will stay closed for 2 weeks.

The order does not apply to premises where there are at most 10 people.

## Coronavirus restrictions for young people

![Upper secondary school pupils sitting on a bench](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11804756)

Open image viewer

Upper secondary school pupils will have to switch to remote teaching in March. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the coronavirus restrictions for children and young people will last only a short time. Marin told Yle in an interview on Friday.

On Thursday the government decided that there will be a 3-week lockdown in Finland in March. During it, for example, upper secondary school pupils will switch to remote teaching.

However, the government's aim is that the coronavirus restrictions for children and young people end after the lockdown.

For example, upper secondary school pupils will again have in-person teaching at school when the lockdown is over at the end of March.

The number of electric cars is growing in Finland. Last year the number of electric cars doubled.

At the end of 2019 there were about 5,000 electric cars in Finland. At the end of 2020 there were already almost 10,000 electric cars in Finland.

## Silver for Herola

![Ilkka Herola](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11812740)

Open image viewer

Ilkka Herola won a world championship silver medal. Photo: Matthias Hangst/Getty Images

Finland has won a silver medal in the skiing World Championships in Germany.

Ilkka Herola was second best in the normal hill Nordic combined competition. Nordic combined has a ski jumping part and a cross-country skiing part.

Herola was in 13th place after the ski jumping part, but he rose in the cross-country skiing part to fight for medals.

So Herola won silver. The gold and bronze of the world championship went to Norway.
