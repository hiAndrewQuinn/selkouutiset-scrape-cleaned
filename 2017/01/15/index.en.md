## Social assistance is applied for from Kela

![Kela's social assistance application forms in a rack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9404633)

Open image viewer

Basic social assistance is now applied for from Kela. Photo: Yle

Applying for social assistance has changed. Basic social assistance is now applied for from Kela. Before, it was applied for from the municipal social services office.

A person may need social assistance, for example, because of unemployment. With the help of the benefit, they can, for example, buy food.

This year almost 500,000 people already have the right to get basic social assistance. The number is bigger than before.

## Floods in Thailand continue

![A street covered in water, a little boy stands on a rubber raft.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9393183)

Open image viewer

Flood water has risen onto the streets in Thailand. Photo: Fazry Ismail / EPA

The rains have continued in Thailand. Thailand's meteorological department forecasts heavy rains in the coming days too.

In January it has already rained so much in Thailand that there are floods in the country. Flood water has risen onto the streets, and it has made life difficult for almost 2 million people. 40 people have died because of the floods.

The rains and floods have also disrupted tourism. There are many tourists in Thailand in January, which is usually sunny.

Thailand's rainy season normally lasts from June to November.

## People go to the cinema a lot

![From the film Risto Räppääjä ja yöhaukka.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9404819)

Open image viewer

The film "Risto Räppääjä ja yöhaukka" got the most viewers in 2016.  Photo: Artista Filmi

Cinemas are doing well in Finland. Various online services and flat-screen TVs have not taken customers away from cinemas.

In 2016, Finns went to the cinema almost 9 million times. The year 2015 was just as good. Cinemas have not had this many customers in more than 30 years.

Finns like to watch Finnish films. Last year, 4 Finnish films were among the 5 most popular films. Risto Räppääjä got the most viewers. Finns like films that suit the whole family.

## Is a thin person happy?

![Scale.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9403935)

Open image viewer

Does the scale tell you whether you are happy? Photo: AOP

Most Finns want to lose weight. A Yle survey says that people are not happy with their own weight.

Psychologist Susanna Anglé says that reality and the ideal have moved far apart. We are fatter than before, but we admire thinner people than before. We look at really thin people in pictures that have been edited in Photoshop.

Anglé says that many people want to lose weight even though they really have other problems. People think that they will become happy if they lose weight.

Anglé thinks that a person losing weight must not be too strict with themselves. Sometimes it is also worth having treats. If you otherwise eat sensibly and regularly, your weight will also slowly go down.
