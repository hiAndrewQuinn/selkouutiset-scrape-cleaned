## Russian attacks on Ukraine

![Clearing up the damage from a missile strike in Vilnyansk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_349/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1038237637e1a671e981/1669208779)

Life is difficult for Ukrainians because of Russian attacks. Photo: Katerina Klochko / AFP

Russia has fired missiles at various parts of Ukraine. Russia is trying to make life as difficult as possible for Ukrainians.

Because of Russian attacks, the power has been out in many Ukrainian cities. For example, in the capital Kyiv, people are also without water.

Russian attacks on Ukraine have also caused power cuts in Moldova, Ukraine's neighbouring country.

The European Parliament says that Russia supports terrorism. The European Parliament reminds that Russia has attacked electricity networks, hospitals, schools and shelters.

## Finns' clothes purchases

![Clothes shop Kämp Galleria. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4694,h_2640,x_0,y_381/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8043716099119476f67/1620644269)

Open image viewer

Finns are now saving on clothes purchases. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

Finns are buying fewer clothes than before.

The change happened this autumn, when all prices rose quickly. It is easier for people to save on clothes purchases than, for example, on food purchases. That is why clothes in particular are being bought less than before.

The situation is difficult for clothing companies. There are fewer customers, but making clothes is more expensive than before. For example, fabric prices have risen.

## Shortage of school psychologists

![Schoolchildren in a school corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_343/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1026543635b900f54657/1666945239)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ari Haimakainen / Yle

There are too few school psychologists. About 350 new school psychologists are needed in Finland.

The situation is difficult in many towns. For example, in Vantaa 50 percent of school psychologist posts are vacant.

Many children and young people now have to wait a long time to see a psychologist. For example, it is difficult to get into ADHD assessments.

## Turku Cathedral's Christmas tree

![A big tree with a man standing in front of it.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12680579)

Open image viewer

Jouko Tuomola's tree became this year's Christmas tree in front of Turku Cathedral. Photo: Kimmo Gustafsson / Yle

Turku Cathedral has once again got a Christmas tree. The Christmas tree was brought in front of the cathedral on Wednesday.

The tree came from the yard of Pirkko and Jouko Tuomola. The Tuomolas wanted to donate the tree, because they were afraid that it might one day fall in a storm.

The Tuomolas' tree fits well in front of the cathedral, because it is big. The tree is 23 metres tall and weighs 3,400 kilos. The tree is 55 years old.

More than 700 lights will be put on the tree. The lights will be lit this Saturday, when the Christmas season begins in Turku.

## The weather

On Thursday it is cloudy across the whole country. In the south and southwest it may snow a little, but elsewhere in the country it is dry.

The temperature in the southern and central parts of the country is between -1 and -8 degrees. It is colder in the north.

Snowfall is expected in Finland at the weekend.
