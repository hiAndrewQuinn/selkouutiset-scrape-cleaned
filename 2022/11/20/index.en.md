# Sunday 20.11.2022 (radio)

Finnair cancels flights. The Egypt climate conference ended. US President Joe Biden turns 80. The football World Cup began. Driving conditions are very bad in Uusimaa.

## Cancelled flights

![The picture shows a Finnair aeroplane at Helsinki-Vantaa airport on 27 October 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5386,h_3030,x_0,y_353/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7350755f991f265c4af/1603872164)

Finnair is cancelling flights. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The airline Finnair is cancelling about 100 flights on Sunday and Monday. The reason is the walkout by Finnair's cabin crew. If a flight is cancelled, Finnair will inform the customer about it.

Finnair's cabin crew are organising a walkout, because Finnair said this week that it is holding change negotiations. In the negotiations, 450 employees may be dismissed.

The walkout started on Sunday at 3 p.m. The walkout ends on Monday afternoon.

## UN climate conference

![Maria Ohisalo at the Egypt climate conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-103424963736fe410574/1668780654)

The UN climate conference ended in Sharm el-Sheikh, Egypt.

Agreement was reached at the meeting on reducing climate emissions. Finland's Environment Minister Maria Ohisalo was disappointed with the results of the meeting. She says that the meeting did not make big enough decisions.

UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres also says that the decisions of the climate conference do not reduce emissions quickly enough.

US President Joe Biden turns 80 years old on Sunday.

Biden is the oldest president in US history. Biden has not said whether he is running in the next US presidential election. If Biden is elected president in the 2024 election, he will still be US president at the age of 86.

## World Cup football

![Logo of the FIFA World Cup 2022 football tournament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_640,h_360,x_109,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1032437636ce6bba6e86/1668081410)

Open image viewer

Logo of the FIFA World Cup 2022 football tournament. Photo: Fifa

The football World Cup has begun. The tournament is played in Qatar, which is a rich Persian Gulf country.

The tournament has been criticised a lot, because Qatar violates many human rights. A large number of foreign workers died when stadiums were built in the desert. Qatar also violates the human rights of women and gay people.

The World Cup is now being played in winter, because it is too hot in Qatar in the summer.

The final match of the World Cup will be played on 18 December. 32 countries are taking part in the tournament. Finland is not taking part in the tournament.

## The weather

![Cars and people in snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5115,h_2877,x_0,y_58/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10365126379e022502e3/1668931750)

The weather is dry in much of Finland. It is snowing at times in southern Finland. The temperature in southern Finland and central Finland is between -2 and -9 degrees. In northern Finland the frost is 7 to 15 degrees. In northern Lapland the frost is up to 25 degrees in places.

Driving conditions are bad in the southern part of the country. In Uusimaa, driving conditions are very bad and the pavements are slippery.
