# Monday 18.3.2019 (radio)

A terror attack has taken place in the Netherlands. Stora Enso plans to cut jobs in Oulu. Female genital mutilation is a crime in Finland. Immigrants made a programme series for Clear News.

## A terror attack took place in the Netherlands

A terror attack has taken place in the Netherlands. At least 3 people died in the attack. Many were injured.

The attack took place in the city of Utrecht, where a man shot people on a tram.

The shooter is perhaps a 37-year-old man who comes from Turkey.

Security measures have been increased in the Netherlands because of the attack.

## Stora Enso is cutting jobs

![Stora Enso's factory in Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10694880)

Open image viewer

Stora Enso's factory in Oulu. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

The forest company Stora Enso plans to cut jobs. Stora Enso is reducing the number of workers at its Oulu factory. Perhaps 400 jobs will be lost in Oulu.

Stora Enso makes paper at its Oulu factory. The factory has 2 paper machines. Stora Enso is closing one paper machine and will start making packaging board on the other. That is why jobs are decreasing.

Stora Enso is making changes at the Oulu factory, because the price of paper is falling in the world, but the price of packaging board is rising.

## Mutilation is a crime

![A separate law to ban mutilation in Finland divides opinions, but there is agreement that the keys to a solution are in information, education and open discussion.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10230189)

Open image viewer

A mutilation knife from Kenya. Photo: Juha Tuominen / Yle

Female genital mutilation is a crime in Finland. In mutilation, a girl's genitals are cut. Mutilation harms a girl's health.

Girls are mutilated, for example, in some African countries. With immigration, the problem has also come to Europe.

In Finland there are perhaps about 3,000 girls who are at risk. It is possible that they will be mutilated in their parents' former home country, for example during a summer holiday trip.

Midwife and health nurse Batulo Essak works against mutilation. Essak says that health care professionals must talk about mutilation with immigrant families.

Essak is an expert by experience. She was born in Somalia, and she was mutilated as a child. Essak hopes that more experts by experience will be brought to Finland, people who speak openly about mutilation.

More information on the matter can be found on Yle's website at yle.fi.

## Immigrants discuss

Nasib and Immigrants is the new series of Clear News.

In the series, immigrants discuss matters that are important to them. They hope that these matters will also be raised in the April parliamentary elections.

The series has 3 parts: Integration, Cultural Differences and Immigrant Women.
