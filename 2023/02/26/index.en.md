## Boat accident in Italy

![People in life jackets.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689350)

Open image viewer

Many migrants travel on the Mediterranean in poor boats. This picture is from 2022. Photo: AOP

A serious boat accident has happened in Italy. At least about 60 people have died in the accident.

The accident happened near the coast of southern Italy. The boat broke in a storm and split into two parts.

The boat was 20 metres long, and it had perhaps more than 150 passengers. The passengers were migrants, and they were citizens of Iran, Pakistan and Afghanistan.

## China and EU relations

![Ursula von der Leyen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689351)

Open image viewer

China is expecting a visit from the President of the European Commission, Ursula von der Leyen. Photo: EUROPEAN COUNCIL / POOL / HANDOUT

China is preparing visits between China and the European Union.

The intention is that EU leaders will visit China in the coming months. China is expecting a visit from the presidents of the European Commission and the European Council.

Russia's war in Ukraine has worsened relations between the European Union and China. The EU has imposed economic sanctions against Russia, but China has not taken part in them.

China, on the other hand, has cooperated with Russia.

## Finland's Eurovision representative

![A man with a winner's trophy in one hand and a microphone in the other.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689352)

Open image viewer

Käärijä won UMK and will represent Finland at Eurovision. Photo: Antti Hämäläinen / Lehtikuva

Finland's representative at Eurovision this year is Käärijä. Finland's Eurovision representative was chosen in the UMK final on Saturday.

Käärijä clearly won the final. He got the most points from TV viewers and international juries.

The UMK final had many viewers. 1.3 million viewers watched the broadcast on television.

Viewers took part in the voting eagerly. More than 200,000 viewers voted for their favourite by text message or with the Yle app.

Käärijä, that is, Jere Pöyhönen, is a 29-year-old singer. He grew up in Vantaa.

The name of Käärijä's winning song is Cha cha cha. It is believed that Käärijä has good chances of succeeding also at Eurovision.

Eurovision will be held in May in Liverpool, in Britain. The victory went to Ukraine last year, but Ukraine cannot organise Eurovision because of the war.

## Nokia logo

Nokia's new logo. Photo: Nokia

It is also reported that one of Finland's well-known logos is changing. The logo of the network equipment company Nokia is undergoing the change.

The style of the letters in the Nokia logo is changing. In addition, the blue shade of the letters becomes a little different from before.

Nokia told about the new logo at an international event on Sunday. The Nokia logo has stayed almost the same for many decades.

## The weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689354)

The weather warms up on Monday, as the foehn wind brings warm air to Finland. In a large part of the country the temperature is a little above zero.

On Tuesday the weather gets colder again. The temperature falls below zero in a large part of the country. Tuesday is also a very windy day.
