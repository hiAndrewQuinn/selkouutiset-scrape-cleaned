# Friday 17.3.2017 (radio)

Many people are seeking help from parishes. Finland wants the EU medicines agency. Finnair cancelled a lot of flights on Friday. Posti is bringing beer and soft drinks to shops. Punkaharju is the best excursion destination of the year.

## People seek help from parishes

![The diaconal reception room at the Pietarsaari parish centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9513589)

The backlog of benefits at the Social Insurance Institution, or Kela, can also be seen in the parishes' aid work.

Many people have been left without money because Kela does not have time to process social assistance applications fast enough. Kela has also rejected a lot of applications.

People seek help from parishes. People ask for advice and food aid. Some parishes can also give a little money.

## Finland wants the EU medicines agency

![The headquarters of the European Medicines Agency EMA.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9515659)

Open image viewer

The headquarters of the European Medicines Agency EMA in London. Photo: Rex / AOP

Finland hopes that the European Medicines Agency will be moved to Finland.

The medicines agency is now in London, but it must be moved elsewhere because of Britain leaving the EU.

In addition to Finland, many other countries are also interested in the medicines agency. For example, Denmark, Sweden, Spain, France and Italy are interested in it.

The medicines agency is the EU's third largest agency. About 900 people work there.

## A strike disrupted air traffic

![Finnair planes at Helsinki-Vantaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9513484)

Open image viewer

Finnair cancelled almost a hundred flights on Friday. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The aviation strike has disrupted air traffic.

Because of the strike Finnair cancelled almost a hundred flights in Finland and Europe on Friday. The cancellations  affected about 6500 passengers.

The strike started in the afternoon and lasted 4 hours. Workers who handle baggage and security checks at the airport were on strike.

The aviation union also threatens to strike next week if no agreement is found in the labour dispute before then.

## Posti now also transports beer and soft drinks

![Posti driver Robert Gustafsson ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9514256)

Open image viewer

Posti driver Robert Gustafsson takes Coca-Cola to a shop. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Posti is again starting a new service. Posti is starting to transport beer and soft drinks to shops.

Posti and Sinebrychoff have agreed that Posti will transport Sinebrychoff's drinks to shops.  Sinebrychoff makes beer, cider and soft drinks such as Coca-Cola and Fanta.

Posti's workers also put the beers and soft drinks on the shop shelves. In addition, they take away the empty bottles and cans.

Posti says that it wants to do more transport. Next it wants to transport, for example, frozen foods.

## Punkaharju is a great excursion destination

![Punkaharju aerial picture](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9516113)

Open image viewer

Punkaharju is famous for its beautiful landscape. Photo: Punkaharjun matkailu ry

Punkaharju has been chosen as the best excursion destination of the year.

Punkaharju is in South Savo in the middle of lakes. It is famous for its beautiful ridge landscapes. Punkaharju is in fact one of Finland's national landscapes.

Over 6000 people took part in the Excursion Destination of the Year vote. About 25 percent of them voted for Punkaharju as the winner.
