# Saturday 12.3.2022 (radio)

The situation in Ukraine. A demonstration in support of Ukraine. Food prices. NATO membership. Weather.

## The situation in Ukraine

![French President Emmanuel Macron and German Chancellor Olaf Scholz hold a press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8640,h_4860,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9114046200fbc09825d/1644231708)

French President Emmanuel Macron and German Chancellor Olaf Scholz. Photo: AOP

The war in Ukraine continues. Russia attacked Ukraine more than 2 weeks ago.

On Saturday the leaders of Germany and France talked on the phone with Russian President Vladimir Putin.

In the talks Germany and France demanded a ceasefire in Ukraine. No weapons are used during a ceasefire.

A ceasefire is needed so that Ukrainians can be evacuated, that is, taken away from the war zone.

For example, in the city of Mariupol there are still many residents who have no water, food or medicines.

## Demonstration in support of Ukraine

There were 4,000 people at the demonstration. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

There has been a demonstration in Helsinki in support of Ukraine. Thousands of people marched in central Helsinki and demanded peace in Ukraine.

The name of the demonstration was "Mothers for peace". The demonstration was organised by 2 mothers.

At the end of the demonstration there was an event at Senate Square in Helsinki, where support for Ukraine was shown.

## Food prices rise

![Mika Lintilä and Seija Vaaherkumpu in the TV studio of Ykkösaamu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5418,h_3047,x_0,y_223/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-927254622c61ec84315/1647076069)

Open image viewer

Minister of Economic Affairs Mika Lintilä in a TV interview on Saturday. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Food is getting more expensive because of the war in Ukraine.

Minister Mika Lintilä estimates that a shopping bag may soon cost twice as much as now.

Right now Finns spend about 10 percent of their income on food. Soon the food bill may take 20 percent of income.

Minister Lintilä also estimates that agriculture needs support. Support is important, so that enough food is produced in Finland.

## NATO membership

![The NATO flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_464/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-921567621cbcee5d7be/1649311249)

Open image viewer

The flag of the NATO military alliance. Photo: Valda Kalnina / EPA

Russia says that Finland and Sweden should not join the NATO military alliance.

Russian diplomat Sergei Beljajev says that the consequences will be serious if Finland and Sweden join NATO.

In Finland and Sweden a lot has been talked about NATO since Russia attacked Ukraine. In the view of many, Finland and Sweden would be safer if they became members of the NATO military alliance.

At present Finland and Sweden are not NATO members, but they cooperate with NATO.

Russia also accused Ukraine of wanting to join NATO. After that Russia attacked Ukraine.

People are going to libraries again, as they did before.

During the corona time many people reserved books online and only came to the library to pick them up. In addition, many started reading e-books.

Now people are again spending more time in libraries.
