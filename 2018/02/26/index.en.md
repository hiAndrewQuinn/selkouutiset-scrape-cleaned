# Monday 26.2.2018 (radio)

The homecoming celebration of Finland's Olympic team was in the afternoon. Municipalities have done well at finding places to live for underage asylum seekers. Foreigners are buying more homes in Finland. It has snowed in Rome. It is extremely cold in Finland.

## Finland's Olympic team arrived home

![Sauli Niinistö Olympic team](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10092819)

President Sauli Niinistö received a baby blanket knitted by the athletes and gave a speech to the Olympic team. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The homecoming celebration of Finland's Olympic team was in the afternoon. It was held at Helsinki-Vantaa Airport.

Among others, cross-country skiers Iivo Niskanen and Krista Pärmäkoski returned from Pyeongchang to Finland. Niskanen won gold in the 50-kilometre ski race. Pärmäkoski won 3 medals at the Games. Also at the celebration was slopestyle bronze medallist Enni Rukajärvi. President Sauli Niinistö and Sports Minister Sampo Terho also took part in the event.

Finland won a total of 6 medals at the Olympics.

## Almost all underage asylum seekers have a place to live

![Boys look at the notice board of a residence.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9452107)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

Municipalities have done well at finding places to live for underage asylum seekers who have come to Finland alone.

Just over a year ago, more than 700 children and young people who had been given permission to stay in Finland were waiting for a place in a municipality. More than half of them were in Uusimaa, North Ostrobothnia and Pirkanmaa.

The Finnish Immigration Service says that only about twenty children and young people are still without a place in a municipality.

## Foreigners are buying more homes in Finland than before

![New blocks of flats in the centre of Joensuu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10092373)

Open image viewer

Foreigners buy similar homes to Finns, but the way they do the deals is often different. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Foreigners are buying more homes in Finland. Last year, foreign private persons bought almost 5,200 properties or housing shares in Finland.

Foreigners make up about 4 per cent of all home purchases. Estate agents have, however, noticed that there are more foreigners at home viewings than before.

Last year, Estonians, Russians and Chinese bought the most homes. There are also many Indian buyers.

## Snow fell in Rome

![A couple kisses in snowy Rome.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10092283)

Open image viewer

The people of Rome have been happy about the snow, even though it has, for example, mixed up the traffic. Photo: Angelo Carcconi / EPA

In Italy's capital Rome, it has snowed for the first time since 2012. A layer of snow about 10 centimetres thick fell on the city during the night between Sunday and Monday.

The city's schools have been closed because of the rare weather event. Buses, trains and trams have been late, and many services have been cancelled.

The temperature in Rome has been above zero. During the night between Tuesday and Wednesday, it may be as cold as 8 degrees below zero.

## It is freezing cold in Finland

![wind chill table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10022439)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics

Here at home, the Finnish Meteorological Institute warns of severe frost.

This week, cold air is coming from Siberia all the way to Finland. It is coldest in the southern and central parts of the country, where it can be 15 degrees below zero even during the day.

The wind makes the frost very biting. The wind can even double how cold it feels. If it is 10 degrees below zero, it can feel like 20 degrees.

The weather forecast says that colder than usual weather will continue for the whole of March.
