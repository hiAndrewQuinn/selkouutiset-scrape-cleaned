## Budget negotiations

![Minister of Agriculture and Forestry Sari Essayah (KD), Minister of Finance Riikka Purra (PS), Prime Minister Petteri Orpo (Kok.) and Minister of Education Anna-Maja Henriksson (R) answered media questions.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4999,h_2811,x_0,y_287/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117385065095c19ab54a/1713079660)

Prime Minister Petteri Orpo's government has started budget negotiations.

In the budget negotiations the government decides on the state's economy for next year. There it is agreed what the state uses money for and where money is saved.

Finance Minister Riikka Purra said in August that the total amount of the budget proposal is about 87 billion euros. About 10 billion euros of new debt was to be taken.

Today Purra said that even more debt must be taken, because, for example, the wellbeing services counties need more money.

## Light rail

![Raide-Jokeri line.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1464,h_822,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106679763da31db36c7a/1675244164)

The light rail runs between Keilaniemi in Espoo and Itäkeskus in Helsinki. Photo: Yle

The new light rail line in the Helsinki region will start passenger traffic in a month. The first light rail tram will go into service on 21 October.

The light rail runs between Keilaniemi in Espoo and Itäkeskus in Helsinki. The track is 25 kilometres long. There are 34 stops along it.

The Raide-Jokeri light rail was completed ahead of schedule. Originally, regular traffic was to start only next summer.

## Arctic fox pups

![Four arctic fox pups sitting on a grassy hummock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6123,h_3428,x_0,y_637/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117016464fe965c15c7b/1694412238)

Four arctic fox pups in Utsjoki. Photo: Petri Piisilä / Metsähallitus

More arctic fox pups were born in Finland last summer than was thought.

Metsähallitus says that at least 25 arctic fox pups were born, when earlier it was reported that there were 16. Pups were born in 4 different nests in Lapland, in Enontekiö and Utsjoki.

The arctic fox is very rare in Finland.

## Hamina walrus

![A stuffed walrus hangs for display near the ceiling in the natural science museum. Behind the walrus a photo background of the seabed can be seen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6193,h_3483,x_0,y_447/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117348665084256bef77/1695040928)

Open image viewer

The Hamina walrus is now at the Natural Science Museum. Photo: Jari Pussinen / Yle

The Helsinki Natural Science Museum has got a new crowd-puller, a stuffed walrus.

The museum's walrus is the same walrus that unexpectedly washed ashore in Hamina last July. The walrus was starved and in poor condition. It was to be taken to the Korkeasaari wildlife hospital, but it died on the way.

The fate of the walrus interested Finns a lot, because there are usually no walruses in Finnish waters.

Now the walrus can be seen at the Natural Science Museum. It also has scientific value, because bone and DNA samples have been taken from it for researchers to use.

In the north it will rain or sleet tomorrow, and in the south it is dry.

Across the whole country it is windy, and especially in the south and east the wind may be dangerously strong.
