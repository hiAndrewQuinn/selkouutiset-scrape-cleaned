Corona infections are increasing. On Tuesday about 5,500 new corona infections were confirmed.

## Corona infection of Sweden's royal couple

![Sweden's King Kaarle XVI Kustaa and Queen Silvia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3653,h_2054,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89867161d45a270f4aa/1641306757)

Sweden's King Kaarle Kustaa and Queen Silvia. Photo: Pelle T. Nilsson / AOP

The Swedish royal couple has got a corona infection.

King Kaarle Kustaa and Queen Silvia have been vaccinated against corona, so they have only mild symptoms.

The King and Queen of Sweden are over 70 years old, so they belong to the corona risk group. They are now at home in quarantine.

## Regional elections

![The yard of the Polvijärvi health centre on an autumn day. A sign in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4603,h_2589,x_4,y_74/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-85947461531a8125879/1632837060)

Open image viewer

In the regional elections, people are chosen who decide, for example, on health centres. Photo: Petri Karvinen / Yle

The regional elections are approaching. The regional elections will be held on Sunday 23 January.

In the regional elections, regional councils are elected. They decide on health care, social services and rescue services.

So the regional councils decide, for example, on health centres and the care of the elderly.

Regional councils are elected in 21 areas. There is no election in Helsinki, because Helsinki provides the services itself.

## Yle's election compass

![The regional election compass has been opened.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89836861d2fd06dd053/1641217359)

Open image viewer

The election compass can be reached at yle.fi/vaalit. Photo: Yle

The regional elections will be held for the first time now. Before, the municipalities decided on services.

The regional elections are still a new thing for people. Many hope for more information about the elections.

Today, Tuesday, Yle's election compass was opened. It helps voters find a candidate who suits them.

The election compass can also be used in English, Russian and Arabic. It can be found at yle.fi/vaalit.

The Selkokeskus website has information about the elections in plain language. The address is selkokeskus.fi.

The Selkokeskus website also has instruction videos in plain language about how to vote in the elections.

## Finns' dogs

![A fifteen-day-old yellow Labrador retriever puppy has opened its eyes and is looking at the camera.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1536,h_864,x_0,y_605/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89023861b30372496aa/1639121952)

Open image viewer

Labrador retriever puppy. Photo: Breeder's album

Finns have acquired many dogs. Many families took a dog during the corona time.

Dog puppies are registered in Finland with the Finnish Kennel Club. Last year about 53,000 dog puppies were registered.

The most popular dog breed is the Labrador retriever. Last year about 2,500 Labrador retriever puppies were registered.

Last year the most popular dog names were Luna, Alma and Helmi.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12258992)

Open image viewer

On Wednesday the weather is cloudy across the whole country, and in a large part of the country snow falls. In places the snowfall can be heavy.

The temperature is between 0 and -10 degrees. In Lapland there is 10 to 15 degrees of frost.

By Epiphany the weather gradually dries up and gets colder.
