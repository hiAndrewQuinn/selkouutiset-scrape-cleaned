# Thursday 19.4.2018 (radio)

MP Harry Harkimo is leaving the National Coalition Party. Helsinki needs more summer substitutes in the care sector. The government plans to reform employment services. People have died of measles in Romania. Friday will be a warm day in the south.

## MP Harkimo

![Harry Hjallis Harkimo at a press conference in the State Hall of Parliament in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10168283)

Harry Harkimo. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

MP Harry Harkimo is leaving the National Coalition Party. Harkimo announced this on Thursday. Harkimo is disappointed with how the National Coalition Party is led.

Harkimo leaving is bad news for the Finnish government. The National Coalition Party is one of the government parties, and the government has only a small majority in Parliament, the Finnish parliament.

Because Harkimo is leaving, the government's majority is even smaller, and it may soon be hard for the government to win votes in Parliament.

## Summer substitutes in Helsinki

![A nurse next to the bed of a resident of a service home.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9128719)

Open image viewer

There is a shortage of care workers. Photo: Ere Ek / Yle

Helsinki needs more summer substitutes in the care sector.

In the summer Helsinki is hiring 650 substitutes in the care sector. About half of the summer substitutes are still missing. There is work for practical nurses and registered nurses.

Care sector students can also get summer jobs from the City of Helsinki. Students are needed, for example, in elderly care and home services.

It is hard for Helsinki to get summer substitutes because there is strong competition for care workers. Many workers are afraid of Helsinki's expensive rents. That is why Helsinki promises to arrange housing for summer substitutes.

## Employment services

![The door of a TE services office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10071822)

Open image viewer

Today the authorities run employment services. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

The government plans to change employment services.

Today an unemployed person gets services from the authorities at the TE Office. The authorities do, for example, the interviews of unemployed people.

But the government wants private companies to be able to provide employment services too.

The organisation of unemployed people points out that employment services are important for the unemployed. For example, the service provider deals with things that affect how much money an unemployed person gets. That is why the organisation of unemployed people is worried about the government's plan.

## Measles cases in Romania

![A nurse gives an MMR vaccination in the thigh of a one-year-old baby.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9754058)

Vaccination gives protection against measles. Photo: David Gee / AOP

One more piece of news from abroad.

In Romania many people have fallen ill with measles. About 12,000 people have had measles, and almost 50 people have died because of measles.

Most of the dead were small children. They had not been vaccinated against measles.

In Romania many parents believe writings on the internet saying that vaccinations are dangerous. That is why they do not want their children to be vaccinated against diseases.
