# Sunday 24.5.2020 (radio)

Finns are saving larger sums of money than before. Drug crime has continued to grow during the corona times. Emergency response centres answered almost 3 million calls last year. The amount of waste has increased during the coronavirus. In Oulu, the police had to step in on the evening when young people gathered.

## Now people are saving larger sums than before

![A woman checks the savings calculator in her online bank.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11366023)

The most common reason for saving or investing is preparing for a rainy day. Other reasons include, for example, surprising and expensive purchases, or holidays and travel. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Finns are saving larger sums of money than before. The Savings Banks Group says that more and more people save or invest over 400 euros a month.

Nordea says that monthly savers save on average about 300 euros. In a year, Finns save or invest on average 2,000 euros.

Nordea estimates that many people only start saving or investing after the age of 40. Saving continues until retirement age.

The most active savers are now 55 to 65 years old.

Men invest in funds and shares more often than women.

Only 15 per cent of Finns do not save at all.

## Drug crime has grown during the corona times

![Drug stash in Lahti, amphetamine in a person's hand](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11334612)

Open image viewer

A drug stash. Photo: Häme Police

Drug crime has continued to grow during the corona times. In particular, amphetamine has been used more than before.

In the spring, the police learned of twice as many drug offences as last spring.

A large part of the growth is explained by one big investigation, which concerned online drug trade.

However, the total number of crimes fell in March and April.

## Emergency centres get too many unnecessary calls

![Emergency centre employees at their workstations in the Kerava emergency centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_66/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6798255ec9bf7159512/1590280172)

Open image viewer

Many callers last year asked for the opening hours of an office or the contact details of an electricity company. That must not be done. It burdens the emergency centre for no reason. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Emergency centres answered almost 3 million calls last year.

Most of the calls concerned a general deterioration in health, falls or chest pain of heart origin. About half of all emergency reports lead to an ambulance or the police arriving.

About a third of the calls were unnecessary. They had nothing to do with urgent public authority services.

The callers asked, for example, for the opening hours of offices or contact details. About 865,000 such unnecessary calls came in.

## Waste is piling up more than before

![Full rubbish bags on the ground in front of a bin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11366041)

Open image viewer

Photo: Amanda Vikman / Yle

The amount of waste has increased slightly during the coronavirus. Cardboard waste and plastic waste have increased the most.

One reason is, for example, that a lot of goods are now bought from online shops.

However, a bigger reason is that property companies have started to collect more plastic.

During the corona times, sorting has increased.

## The police stepped in on a youth gathering

![Police car in front of the Oulu courthouse.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11366010)

Open image viewer

Archive picture of a police car in front of the Oulu courthouse. Photo: Timo Heikkala / Lehtikuva

In Oulu, the police had to step in on a youth gathering on Saturday evening. The situation threatened to become dangerous.

Hundreds of young people gathered by car, motorbike and moped at a parking area in Kaakkuri. The young people crashed their vehicles around the parking area.

Some of the young people were slightly injured.

When the police were investigating the crashes, some of the young people attacked the police cars. The young people also disturbed the work of the police and the ambulance service.

The police are investigating the acts.
