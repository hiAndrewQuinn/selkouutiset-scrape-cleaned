US strike in Afghanistan. Evacuations from Afghanistan. Coronavirus infections. The Aurora Botnia ship. Weather forecast.

## US strike in Afghanistan

![A Taliban fighter looks at the traces of the explosion near Kabul airport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4920,h_2768,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-847268612915a40d3f9/1630091520)

 A Taliban fighter looks at the traces of the explosion near Kabul airport. Photo: AFP / Lehtikuva

The US has carried out a military strike in Afghanistan. The US struck with drones. The target of the drone strike was the extremist group Isis.

The US says that the drone strike killed a person who planned the terror attacks in Kabul.

The extremist group Isis carried out a suicide attack at Kabul airport on Thursday. 170 people died in the Isis attack. 13 of them were American soldiers. Because of the Isis attacks, the US has now carried out a counterattack.

## Evacuations

Many countries have stopped evacuations from Afghanistan. For example, France, Britain, Spain and Italy stopped evacuations on Friday.

Finland has also stopped evacuations. Finnish soldiers, that is the security force, have returned from Afghanistan to Finland. In total, Finland has evacuated, that is brought to safety, more than 400 people.

The US will continue evacuations until the end of August, that is until Tuesday.

On Saturday, 619 new coronavirus infections were reported in Finland. In the last 2 weeks there have been over 8,600 new infections, which is about 1,600 fewer than in the previous 2 weeks.

At least one coronavirus vaccine dose has now been received by almost 4 million Finns, which is about 72 per cent of Finns.

Over 2.7 million Finns have received 2 coronavirus vaccine doses, which is about 49 per cent of Finns.

## Merenkurkku's new ship

![Aurora Botnia arriving at Vaasa port for the first time on 25.8.2021 after 11 o'clock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-846011612610ef0379d/1732096750)

Open image viewer

Aurora Botnia. Photo: Antti Haavisto / Yle

The new ship Aurora Botnia in the Kvarken has started traffic. The ship travels between Vaasa and Umeå. On Saturday morning the vessel left Vaasa on its first trip.

The Aurora Botnia ship is also of interest to people and shipping companies abroad. Aurora Botnia runs on natural gas. That is why its climate emissions are smaller than those of many other ships.

Aurora Botnia can also run on biogas. Then its emissions are very small. Aurora Botnia was built at the Rauma shipyard in Finland.
