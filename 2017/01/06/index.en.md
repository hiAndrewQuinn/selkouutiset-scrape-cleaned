# Friday 6.1.2017 (radio)

Finns are less afraid of being fired than a year ago. A wind turbine's engine room burned in Hamina. There are severe floods in Thailand. Ice lanterns have lit up the village of Paakki in northern Finland. In Helsinki art is made with light. The hard frost ends on Saturday.

## Fear of being fired is falling

![Shipyard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8835916)

Open image viewer

There is plenty of work for ship builders at the Turku shipyard. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

Finns are now less afraid of being fired than a year ago. A large part of Finns trust that their own job will remain.

Taloustutkimus asked over 1,000 Finns about this. 84 per cent of the respondents trusted that their job would remain. A year ago, 77 per cent of respondents felt that way.

The best situation is in Western Finland. There people are least afraid of losing their jobs.

The reason is that industry is doing well in Western Finland. Many ships are being built in Turku, and cars in Uusikaupunki. People in Western Finland trust that there is enough work.

## Hamina's unusual fire

![A wind turbine burns.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9391034)

Open image viewer

The fire started in the engine room of a wind turbine at a height of 100 metres. Photo: Mikko-Pekka Purho

There has been an unusual fire in Hamina. The fire started in the engine room of a wind turbine. The engine room is 100 metres high.

Because of the fire, parts of the wind turbine fell to the ground. Nobody was injured, however. The area has been closed.

## Floods in Thailand

![A boy plays on a raft in the Kelantan area near the Thai border.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9391107)

Open image viewer

Flood waters have also risen onto roads in Malaysia, Thailand's neighbouring country. Photo: Fazry Ismail / EPA

In Asia, Thailand has severe floods. About 10 people have died.

The water rose onto the streets because of heavy rain. Thai authorities say that at least 120,000 homes have suffered from the floods.

The flood water is also disturbing tourists. On the holiday island of Koh Samui flights have been cancelled, and some flights have been delayed.

## Ice lanterns in Paakki village

![A snowy and dark landscape with ice lanterns along the road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9389799)

Open image viewer

Paakki village is like a fairy tale, when ice lanterns light up the dark winter in the middle of the village. Photo: Marita Kaipainen

On Friday the Epiphany was celebrated. It is the last Christmas holiday feast day.

In Paakki village in northern Finland the Epiphany was celebrated in a really beautiful setting. More than 1,000 ice lanterns have brought light to the village roads.

The residents of Paakki village have made the ice lanterns themselves. They freeze water in buckets. When the ice is ready, a hole is made in the middle of it for a candle.

The tradition is more than 20 years old. The ice lanterns are lit at Christmas time, when the weather is cold enough. This year the right weather came just in time for the Epiphany.

## Helsinki's light art

![Lux Helsinki in 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9389414)

Open image viewer

Light art in central Helsinki. Photo: Kristiina Lehto / Yle

In Helsinki people enjoy a slightly different kind of light. Art is made with light.

Now there is the Lux Helsinki light festival, and there are 15 light art works in central Helsinki. Light art lights up, for example, the walls of buildings.

The light art works can be found around Senate Square. Lux Helsinki continues until Monday.
