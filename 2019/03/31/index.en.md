# Sunday 31.3.2019 (radio)

The job-seeker activation model changes on Monday. 3 people have died in a traffic accident in Kitee. In Slovakia Zuzana Čaputová has won the presidential election. Election campaigns are expensive for candidates. Gulls are in danger.

## The activation model changes

![Protest against the activation model in Senate Square, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_522/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4614275a744dcd82a0c/1517571633)

The activation model for the unemployed changes on Monday.

There has been a lot of controversy about the activation model. An unemployed person gets unemployment benefit only if they meet the conditions of the model. They must apply for work often enough or be in work enough. Activity also includes labour market training, studies or work trials.

Now unemployed people get more ways to meet the conditions of the activation model. For example, labour market training provided by a trade union, a municipality or an association will be accepted.

Until now the organiser could only be a labour authority. The training must still support getting employed.

3 people have died in a traffic accident in Kitee in North Karelia.

A car and a minibus crashed head-on on Route 6 in the early hours of the morning. 3 people were injured. One of them is in very serious condition

The police suspect that the driver of the car was driving far over the speed limit. The driver is also suspected of gross drunk driving.

## Zuzana Čaputová won the Slovak presidential election

![Zuzana Čaputová received congratulations and flowers in Bratislava, the capital of Slovakia, after her election victory was confirmed early on Sunday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10715318)

Open image viewer

The election winner Zuzana Čaputová received flowers after the presidential election. Photo: AFP/Lehtikuva

In Slovakia Zuzana Čaputová has won the presidential election.

Čaputová is the country's first female president. She received 58 percent of the votes. Čaputová has been a visible critic of the government and an anti-corruption activist.

Her opponent in the second round was Slovakia's current EU Commissioner, Vice-President of the Commission Maroš Šefčovič.

## Election campaigns are expensive

![Election posters on the street](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10709967)

Open image viewer

Parliamentary election ads came onto the streets today.  Photo: Sini Järnström / Yle

Campaigns for parliamentary elections cost candidates a lot of money.

Election budgets have grown in the 2000s. In the 2015 elections, an MP who got elected spent an average of 40,000 euros on the campaign. Other candidates' budgets were on average 8,000 euros. Those who were elected paid about a third of the campaign themselves.

The spring parliamentary elections are on Sunday, 14 April. Advance voting starts on 3 April, which is next Wednesday.

## Gulls are endangered

![Lesser black-backed gull standing on a post](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10706940)

Open image viewer

Lesser black-backed gull. Photo: Anniina Wallius / Yle

Environmental chemicals have reduced gull populations. Some species have become threatened, that is, they are in danger of disappearing.

For example, the lesser black-backed gull is already seriously endangered. Its numbers have fallen by 50 percent in 10 years.

The lesser black-backed gull eats Baltic herring and is the only Finnish gull species that migrates as far as Africa. The lesser black-backed gull is endangered because its food contains a lot of poisons.

Also at risk are the common gull, the herring gull and the black-headed gull. They have decreased by about 30 percent in 10 years.
