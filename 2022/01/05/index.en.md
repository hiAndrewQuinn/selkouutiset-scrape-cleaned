## Corona patients

The number of corona patients in hospital care has grown. About 470 corona patients are now in hospital care in Finnish hospitals.

The good news, however, is that the number of corona patients in intensive care has decreased. Intensive care needs a lot of nurses and equipment. Now 47 corona patients are getting intensive care.

The Institute for Health and Welfare has also reported Wednesday's corona figures. There are about 6,800 new corona infections, and 25 new corona deaths.

## Helsinki's corona situation

![A person wearing a face mask at a metro station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5567,h_3131,x_0,y_580/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89273861bae09c58cc6/1641187163)

Open image viewer

New corona infections come in Helsinki all the time. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The corona situation in Helsinki has deteriorated quickly.

Because of corona, Helsinki's health care is in trouble. Therefore the City of Helsinki is planning to take the Herttoniemi emergency hospital into use.

Work is already under way to get the Herttoniemi emergency hospital ready. The emergency hospital will have 50 care places.

In addition, the city is reducing health services that are not urgent. Health care workers are focusing on treating corona patients and on corona vaccinations.

Finland has a shortage of plumbers.

There are so few plumbers that it is already slowing the growth of the construction industry.

Plumbers are also expected to do a lot, because the tasks in the field have grown. For example, heat pumps now belong to the work of the plumbing field, that is, the LVI (heating, ventilation and air conditioning) field.

The LVI field is now attracting young people with environmental reasons. Good building technology reduces buildings' energy use and emissions. So a plumber gets to do important work, the LVI field says.

## Epiphany

![Christmas tree, close-up, edited.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_940,h_529,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-4068556602ead5042a/1449143982)

On Epiphany it is time to put the Christmas decorations away. Photo: YLE Picture Service

On Thursday Finland celebrates Epiphany. Epiphany is celebrated every year on 6 January.

Epiphany is the last festival of the Christmas season. Many Finns take down the Christmas tree and the Christmas decorations on Epiphany.

Epiphany is a public holiday in Finland.

Trains and buses run on Sunday timetables, and banks, for example, are closed on Epiphany.

Many shops are open on Epiphany too.

## Weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12260712)

Open image viewer

The weather on Epiphany is wintry.

Cold air begins to flow in from the north, and the weather clears in the western part of the country. The wind from the north makes the air feel cold.

Snowfall decreases. However, a little snow still falls in the east and north.

On Thursday there is frost of 5 to 20 degrees.

On Friday the frost gets stronger. Then there is frost of 10 to 25 degrees.

On Friday the weather is clear and mostly dry, that is, no snow falls.
