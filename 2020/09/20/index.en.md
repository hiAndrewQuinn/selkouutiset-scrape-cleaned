# Sunday 20.9.2020 (radio)

The passenger ship Amorella ran aground on Sunday. The coronavirus is spreading quickly in Britain. A train and a bear collided in Lieksa. On Monday it is warm in the south, and in the north it rains.

## The Amorella ship had an accident

![Amorella aground in Åland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1042,h_586,x_42,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7211215f6732b43242f/1600598811)

The Viking Line ship Amorella had an accident in the Åland archipelago. Photo: Western Finland Coast Guard

On Sunday there was a ship accident.

The passenger ship Amorella ran aground. The bottom of the ship touched rock in the Åland archipelago.

There were about 200 passengers on board. The passengers were not injured, and they were not in great danger.

After the incident, Amorella drove to the shore of the island. Help arrived, and the passengers were moved to safety in Åland.

The accident happened on Sunday afternoon. The Viking Line ship Amorella was on its way from Turku to Stockholm.

## Corona infections are increasing

![Matt Hancock](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11554293)

Open image viewer

British Health Secretary Matt Hancock. Photo: Tolga Akmen / AFP

The spread of the coronavirus in Finland and in the world continues.

In Finland it was reported on Sunday that there are 58 new corona infections. In total, about 9,000 corona infections have been found in Finland.

The coronavirus is also spreading elsewhere in Europe. It is spreading especially quickly in Britain now. In Britain, over 4,000 new corona cases a day have been found recently.

The British Health Secretary says that Britain is close to a corona lockdown. If a lockdown starts, shops, restaurants and bars in Britain can be closed.

A law will soon come into force in Britain that tries to stop the spread of the coronavirus. The law says that a person must stay away from other people if they have a corona infection. If a person breaks the law, they can get a fine of up to 10,000 pounds.

## A train and a bear collided

![Pipes arrive at the Mussalo port area by rail from Russia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9579039)

Open image viewer

A bear walked onto the railway track in Lieksa. Photo: Antti Ojala / Yle

An unusual accident has happened in Lieksa in Eastern Finland. A train and a bear collided with each other on Saturday evening.

The police say that the bear ran straight into the engine of a freight train. Because of the force of the collision, the bear rolled into a ditch. The bear was no longer found.

The authorities say that the bear was not badly injured in the accident. The bear is not a danger to people.

The week starts with warm weather in Southern Finland. The temperature on Monday is over 15 degrees. In the south it is also dry, that is, no rain falls.

In Northern Finland it will rain on Monday. The weather is also cold in the north. Between Monday and Tuesday nights, it may even snow in parts of Lapland.

Tuesday is a windy day across the whole of Finland. It is especially windy in the north.
