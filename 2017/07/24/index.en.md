# Monday 24.7.2017 (radio)

The world's largest nuclear submarine is now moving in the Gulf of Finland. This week the police are looking for drink drivers on the roads. In Finland there are more children than before who live with their grandparents. Finland did well at the World Para Athletics Championships in London.

## The large nuclear submarine

A rare vessel is now moving in the Gulf of Finland. It is the world's largest nuclear submarine.

The submarine is Russian. Its name is Dmitry Donskoy.

The submarine is 172 metres long. The submarine travels underwater.

The nuclear submarine Dmitry Donskoy is on its way to St Petersburg. There are celebrations in St Petersburg, because the Russian navy turns 100.

## Drink drivers are a danger

![Police make a driver blow into a breathalyser.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9436193)

Open image viewer

One blow is enough to show whether the driver has drunk alcohol. Photo: Kalle Schönberg / Yle

This week the police are checking traffic more than usual. The police are looking for drink drivers on the roads, that is, drivers who have drunk alcohol.

The police are checking traffic especially in places where there are many summer cottages.

The police say that there are more drink drivers in the summer than at other times. A lot of people die in traffic accidents, especially in the summer. About 30 percent of traffic deaths happen in the summer.

## Grandparents as parents

![Big and small hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5651160)

Open image viewer

A grandparent can become a child's guardian if the child's parents, for example, use drugs. Photo: YLE / Sini Salmirinne

In Finland there are more children than before who live with their grandparents.

If it is not good for a child to live with their own parents, the authorities look for a new home for them. A child needs a new home, for example, when the parents use narcotics or drink too much alcohol.

First, the authorities investigate whether a close person can take the child into their care. Nowadays many grandparents are in such good condition that they are able to bring up a grandchild.

A grandparent usually becomes a grandchild's guardian at about the age of 60.

## World Para Athletics Championships

![Amanda Kotaja.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9737583)

Open image viewer

World champion Amanda Kotaja. Photo: James Marsh / AOP

Finland did well at the World Para Athletics Championships in London. Athletes with disabilities took part in the competition.

Track athlete Amanda Kotaja became world champion on Sunday. She won a gold medal in the 100-metre race. Kotaja competes in a wheelchair.

Javelin thrower Marjaana Heikkinen won a silver medal on Sunday. She was the second-best thrower in the competition. Heikkinen throws the javelin while seated.

Finland won 6 medals in total at the World Para Athletics Championships.

Finland's best athlete in London was track athlete Leo-Pekka Tähti. He won 2 medals. One was a gold medal, which means Tähti was world champion in the 100-metre wheelchair race.
