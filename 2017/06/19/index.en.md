# Monday 19.6.2017 (radio)

A new party is going to be founded in Finland. A new big construction company is being born in Finland. President Macron's party has won the French parliamentary elections. A man drove a car into people in London. Passengers can no longer buy tickets on local trains.

## Blue Future

![New Alternative group leader Simon Elo (left), minister Sampo Terho and deputy leader Tiina Elovaara.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9678483)

The founders of Blue Future are former Finns Party members. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

A new political party is going to be founded in Finland. The plan is that the party will be called Blue Future.

The people making the plan are MPs who used to belong to the Finns Party. The Finns Party split after the Finns Party's leader changed. Some of the Finns Party MPs founded their own group in Parliament, Finland's parliament, and now they plan to found their own party.

Founding a new party requires the names of 5,000 supporters. Collecting the names starts soon.

## A new construction company

![A construction worker at work](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9624812)

Open image viewer

When the new construction company starts, it will have 10,000 employees. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

A new big construction company is being born in Finland. The new company is born when 2 construction companies merge. They are YIT and Lemminkäinen.

The new company will become Finland's biggest construction company. It will have about 10,000 employees.

The new construction company will start in November or after that. The authorities still have to approve the merger of YIT and Lemminkäinen.

## The French elections

![Macron in front of a voting booth with a ballot in his hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9677574)

Open image viewer

French President Emmanuel Macron. Photo: Christophe Archambault / EPA

France has had parliamentary elections. The winner of the elections is President Emmanuel Macron's party.

The win is important for the president. Macron needs the support of parliament for his policies.

Macron started as President of France in May. He wants to reform France.

Few French people voted in the parliamentary elections. Only 43 percent of adult French people went to vote.

## The London attack

![Police officials at the scene in the Finsbury Park area on Monday morning, 19 June. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9678193)

There has been an attack with a car in Britain.

10 people were injured when a man drove a car into people in London. 1 person also died at the scene, but the cause of death is not yet certain.

The injured are Muslims, because there are 2 mosques near the place. One of them is the large Finsbury Park mosque.

Police have arrested the driver of the car.

## Local train tickets

![Passengers at Pitäjänmäki train station in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9678575)

Open image viewer

A passenger must have a ticket when getting on a local train. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

Passengers can no longer buy tickets on local trains. Ticket sales on local trains ended on Monday.

A passenger must buy a ticket before the journey. They can also pay for the journey with a travel card. Conductors check on local trains that passengers have tickets.

Tickets can still be bought on local trains that run between Riihimäki and Lahti. The reason is that there are still stations along the route that have no ticket machines.
