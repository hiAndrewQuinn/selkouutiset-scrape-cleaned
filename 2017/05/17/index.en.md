# Wednesday 17.5.2017 (radio)

A new big trade union. The average pay of the heads of big companies is 700,000 euros a year. In many Helsinki restaurants the food is cooked by a Filipino cook. The price of petrol and diesel has risen for a year. There are only 200 Lapland cows left.

## A new trade union

![Riku Aalto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9296038)

Riku Aalto, chair of the Industrial Union. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

A new big trade union is born in Finland today, Wednesday.

The name of the new union is the Industrial Union, and it has about 230,000 members. The new union is born when the Metalworkers' Union, the Wood and Allied Workers' Union and the TEAM union merge.

People in the new union think that a big union has more power when it negotiates pay with employers.

## Pay of a big company's head 700,000

The pay of the heads of big companies has stayed almost the same in recent years.

In four years the pay of the CEOs of big companies has risen by 1.4 percent. Their average pay is 700,000 euros a year.

In big companies, CEOs are paid more bonuses, or performance pay, than before. You get bonuses if the company does well. The bosses' bonuses rose by 13 percent last year compared with the year before.

## Cooks from the Philippines

![carl and ricardo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9615723)

Open image viewer

Carl and Ricardo are studying the Finnish language. Photo: Kirsi Crowley / Yle

Many restaurants in Helsinki have a Filipino cook.

A Finnish company brings cooks from the Philippines to Finland, because there are over 1,000 open jobs for cooks in Finland. The jobs are open even though at the same time there are 5,000 unemployed cooks in Finland.

Filipino cooks get better pay in Finland than in the Philippines, but worse pay than Finnish cooks get. A Filipino cook in Helsinki gets at least 1,300 euros a month. From that he pays rent and food and sends money home to his family.

## Price of petrol and diesel has risen

![Price of petrol](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9616153)

Open image viewer

Petrol is more expensive than a year ago. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / Yle

The price of petrol and diesel has risen steadily for about a year.

Crude oil first got cheaper for many years, but now its price has started to rise slowly. The price of petrol always rises in summer, because people drive their cars more, for example on holiday trips.

The price of diesel is also rising in Finland because industry is doing better. Machines and lorries use more diesel.

## Only 200 Lapland cows

![Calves of the Lapland cow and the Eastern Finncattle peek curiously between the pasture fences.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8281767)

Open image viewer

Lapland cows eating. Photo: Siru Päivinen / Yle

In northern Finland people are trying to save the Lapland cow. The Lapland cow is an old cattle breed of the north, of which there are only about 200 cows left.

The Lapland cow is small and white. Farm mistress Helena Impiö says that the Lapland cow is sensitive. It needs tenderness and attention, and it gets angry easily. It gives less milk than other cows, but its milk is sweet and has a lot of protein.

In Lapland they are now testing what kind of ice cream, cheese and yoghurt can be made from the milk of the Lapland cow.
