# Thursday 30.3.2017 (radio)

The presidents of Russia and Finland met in Russia. Fake doctor Esa Laiho goes to prison for 5 years. New buildings often have moisture problems. More people visit bread queues than before.

## The presidents

The presidents of Finland and Russia have met in Russia. The presidents met at an international meeting in Arkhangelsk.

President Vladimir Putin said at the meeting that he is ready to meet the US President Donald Trump in Helsinki. Putin said that Finland knows how to organise important events.

President Sauli Niinistö said that Finland is ready to organise a meeting of the presidents of Russia and the USA. But the matter is not current yet, Niinistö said.

## Fake doctor

![The homicide charges against Esa Laiho, who posed as a doctor, began to be heard in the Helsinki District Court in February 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8977409)

Open image viewer

Esa Laiho. Photo: Timo Korhonen / AOP

The Court of Appeal has given its sentence to fake doctor Esa Laiho. Laiho goes to prison for 5 years.

The Court of Appeal gave Laiho a harsher punishment than the District Court. Last summer the District Court gave Laiho a sentence that was 6 months shorter. The Court of Appeal is above the District Court. That is why it can change the decisions of the District Court.

Laiho was sentenced for assault and fraud, because he made treatment errors and used a degree certificate that was not real.

Laiho must also pay back about a million euros in salaries.

Esa Laiho's court case has been talked about a lot in Finland. Fake doctor Laiho is not a real doctor. He did a doctor's work without a doctor's training. He bought false doctor's papers from abroad. Many patients suffered because Laiho did not know how to treat them correctly.

## Moisture problems

![In Jätkäsaari there is a lace-patterned building owned by Heka, where moisture problems have been found in the flats on the upper floors. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9530944)

Moisture has damaged the walls and ceiling of a new building. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

New buildings often have moisture problems.

Hurry and mistakes on the construction site are reasons why moisture gets into a building. A building can also be badly designed.

The authorities and the construction industry have together given new instructions on how to reduce moisture problems. Cities plan to start using the instructions.

## Bread queues

![People queued for food on an icy road in Myllypuro, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9491514)

Open image viewer

People queue for free food in a bread queue. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

More people visit bread queues than before. In bread queues, free bread and other food is handed out to people who do not have money to buy food.

Nowadays bread queues are also visited by people who have a job, but whose pay is not enough for food. The organisers of bread queues say that people are not as ashamed in the bread queue as before. There are many kinds of people in the queues: ordinary families with children, young people and students.

The organisers of bread queues get the food from shops that give away extra food before it goes bad.
