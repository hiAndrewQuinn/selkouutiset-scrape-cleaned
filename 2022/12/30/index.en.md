## Alcohol harms

![empty beer bottle](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6650989)

Open image viewer

Alcohol deaths are still common in Finland. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Finns still lose many years of life because of alcohol. Alcohol is the biggest cutter of life years in 15 wellbeing services counties. There are 21 wellbeing services counties in total.

So-called unnecessary deaths are also caused by suicides, drugs and many heart diseases. An unnecessary death is one that can be avoided.

The differences are large between different regions, income groups and genders.

From the beginning of the year, the wellbeing services counties will take care of social and health care and rescue services.

## Tax incentive

![Tax return](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10965642)

Open image viewer

Taxation is one way to lengthen people's working lives. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

At the start of the year, employees over 60 will get an increased earned income tax deduction, which lowers tax. The aim is to encourage people over 60 to stay at work longer before retiring.

People over sixty pay the lowest tax rate. From the same annual income they pay on average almost 1,000 euros less tax than a pensioner.

Tax does not depend only on the amount of salary. Age also affects taxes and tax-like payments.

Retiring increases the tax rate.

## Deaths of journalists

![Kashmiri journalists hold signs criticising the cutting of phone and internet services.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11182372)

Open image viewer

Kashmiri journalists protest against the cutting of internet connections. Photo: Farooq Khan / EPA

Almost 60 journalists have died in the line of duty this year.

The most dangerous countries for journalists are Iraq and Syria. In Europe the most dangerous countries for journalists are Ukraine, Russia and Turkey.

In the last 20 years almost 1,700 journalists have died in the line of duty.

![Shahban Stella Salamu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5137,h_2889,x_0,y_566/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-916177620d3bd545b66/1665453876)

Open image viewer

Shahban Stella Salamu at an exercise in the 1st Military Police Company of the Guards Jäger Regiment in Östersundom, Helsinki, on 16 February. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

More women than ever are interested in the army in Finland, says the Defence Forces.

A total of 1,040 women have been discharged from the Defence Forces and the Border Guard this year. The number exceeded a thousand for the first time.

Over 1,200 women began voluntary military service this year. That too is the highest figure in history.

## Pelé and Brazil's period of mourning

![Pelé.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_74/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1043413638b6ab3e9249/1670081428)

Open image viewer

Pelé's full name was Edson Arantes do Nascimento. Photo: Sebastiao Moreira / EPA

In Brazil, South America, a 3-day period of mourning has been declared because of the death of the famous footballer. Brazilian footballer Pelé died yesterday.

The 82-year-old football legend had long suffered from cancer.

Pelé has been named player of the century. He is the only triple men's football world champion.

Pelé was born into a poor family. Pelé's first footballs were made, for example, from a sock stuffed with newspapers.

After his sports career Pelé was, among other things, a UNESCO goodwill ambassador and Brazil's sports minister.
