# Saturday 30.10.2021 (radio)

The corona time taught preparedness. Development aid to Sudan is ended. Meeting of rich countries began. Non-fiction writer awards. Summer time ends. Weather forecast.

## Preparing for pandemics

![Mask recommendation in lower secondary schools came into force. The picture shows masks at Itäkeskus comprehensive school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5719,h_3217,x_0,y_295/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7450465fbb6d5eac6e9/1606119015)

Open image viewer

Face masks in a package. Photo: Sara Salmi / Yle

The corona pandemic has taught Finland to prepare for new health threats better than before.

University of Vaasa professor Harri Jalonen says that, for example, cooperation between authorities has improved and sped up.

Pandemics are now taken into account better in law, because the law has been reformed. This year a new plan for pandemics will also be completed.

Professor Jalonen says that in a pandemic situation it is important to see what is known about the situation and what is not known.

## Development aid to Sudan

![Protesters burn car tyres on a street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5759,h_3239,x_0,y_599/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8725526179836b9a617/1635353730)

Open image viewer

Sudanese people have demonstrated against the military coup. Photo: Mohammed Abu / EPA

The EU is ending the payment of development aid to Sudan.

The reason is that soldiers seized power in Sudan on Monday. Finnish EU Commissioner Jutta Urpilainen says that the EU cannot support a military government.

That is why the payment of development aid to Sudan is ending.

## Rich G20 countries meet

![Meeting of G20 countries.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1969,x_0,y_317/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82712860dc7a6e27690/1625062825)

G20 leaders are meeting in person for the first time in a long time. During the corona time they have only had remote meetings. Photo: Angelo Carconi / EPA

The rich countries, that is, the G20 countries, have started a meeting in the Italian capital Rome.

The leaders of the rich countries will talk about sharing corona vaccines with developing countries, economic matters and climate change.

On Sunday the UN climate conference begins in Britain. Participants in the climate conference are now especially interested in what the leaders of rich countries say about climate change.

## Non-fiction writer awards

![Lasse Kosonen of Nokia smiles at the camera.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2450,h_1378,x_0,y_545/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-873807617cfed993127/1635581989)

Open image viewer

Mushroom expert Lasse Kosonen is one of the award winners. He has written a lot about mushrooms. Photo: Heidi Mäki, Suomen tietokirjailijat

This year's non-fiction writer awards were given at the Helsinki Book Fair.

The non-fiction writer award is received by journalist, PhD Suvi Ahola, professor Anu Kantola, botanist Lasse Kosonen, professor emeritus Pentti Paavolainen and researcher Jussi Pekkarinen.

Each of them gets 8000 euros. The non-fiction writer award is given to people who have written good non-fiction books.

## Remember to change the time

![A clock whose hand is moved back to winter time.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-6142553197327452bd/1603530654)

Open image viewer

On Sunday the time is moved one hour back. Photo: Yle

Summer time ends on Sunday.

The time is moved one hour back. Summer time officially ends on Sunday morning at 4. Then the clock is 3, that is, the time is moved one hour back.

Summer time ends and begins always at the same time across the whole EU.
