## Democracy award for the Easy Language Centre

![Two women in an office. One is sitting and the other is standing next to her.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12682480)

Selkosanomat magazine layout designer Heidi Ahlström and development manager Henna Kara were happy about the award. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

The Easy Language Centre has received the Democracy Award.

Justice Minister Anna-Maja Henriksson presented the award to the Easy Language Centre on Tuesday. The justification for the award says that information belongs to everyone.

The Easy Language Centre gives information about difficult matters in easy Finnish, that is, in easy language. The Easy Language Centre has provided information about the coronavirus and Russia's attack on Ukraine, for example.

Development manager Henna Kara of the Easy Language Centre says that understandable information is a good weapon against fake news. It is important when there are crises in the world.

The Ministry of Justice awards the Democracy Award every other year. The prize is 10,000 euros.

## New reception centres

![A woman prepares a room in a reception centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-932232623b2a2782b62/1648045003)

Open image viewer

More reception centre places are needed. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

The Finnish Immigration Service, Migri, is setting up new reception centres. In addition, more places will be added to the existing reception centres.

3 new reception centres will be set up in Finland. They will be in Helsinki, Kerava and Outokumpu.

New reception centres are needed because many Ukrainians are fleeing the war. Many Ukrainians have also come to Finland.

## Doctors' remote consultations

![Eksote nutrition therapist Iida Niemi holding a remote consultation at a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2084,h_1172,x_0,y_456/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7676866013d108c1fae/1611913589)

Open image viewer

Remote consultation brings a doctor to small municipalities too. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

Remote doctor consultations are going to be increased.

It is hoped that remote services will ease the shortage of doctors. In many small municipalities there are too few doctors.

However, medical students are worried about this. They say that remote consultation work is not yet taught enough. That is why work in remote consultations is not suitable for a newly qualified doctor, the students say.

Medical professor Jarmo Jääskeläinen also says that a doctor must first be able to examine patients in the traditional way. Only after that can the doctor also hold remote consultations.

## Tampere's cat tram

![A woman and a tram with pictures of cats.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12682592)

Open image viewer

Milla Vuorela has painted black cats on the tram. Photo: Pasi Tiitola / Tampereen raitiotie

A cat tram now also runs in Tampere's traffic. So the tram has pictures of cats.

Artist Milla Vuorela has painted black cats on the sides of the cat tram. There are 151, or about 151, cats, Vuorela says. She is no longer quite sure how many cats she painted.

The cat tram is Tampere's art tram. The art tram started a year ago. The pictures on the tram change every six months. Now it is the cats' turn.
