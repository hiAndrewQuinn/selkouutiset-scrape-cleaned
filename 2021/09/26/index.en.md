## German elections

In the German elections there are three chancellor candidates this time: the Greens' Annalena Baerbock, the SPD's Olaf Scholz and the CDU's Armin Laschet. Picture from Berlin on 14 September Photo: Filip Singer / EPA

German voters voted in the parliamentary elections on Sunday. Voting ended in the evening at 19:00.

The elections will decide who leads Germany after Angela Merkel. Chancellor Angela Merkel has led Germany for 16 years. She will give up power after the election.

Opinion polls have said that the contest for the post of German chancellor is even. The candidate of Angela Merkel's party, the CDU, is Armin Laschet. The Social Democrats' candidate is Germany's Finance Minister Olaf Scholz. The Greens' candidate, Annalena Baerbock, is also trying to become chancellor.

After the election a government will be formed in Germany. The government negotiations may be difficult, because the election is even. That is why it may take many days before it is known who will be Germany's new leader.

## Green lane at Helsinki-Vantaa airport

![Passengers at Helsinki-Vantaa airport on 4 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_360/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-85852261506c1fa3d50/1634389914)

Archive photo from Helsinki-Vantaa airport from early August. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

A green lane has been opened at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.

Via the green lane you can come to Finland if you have received 2 corona vaccinations. Over 80 per cent of passengers have received 2 vaccinations.

A corona certificate is checked on the green lane only for some people. The aim is to clear queues at the airport. The green lane was described by Vantaa city manager Ritva Viljanen.

## Tattoos

![A customer shows his ready-made tribal tattoos on the back of his hand. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5597643)

Open image viewer

A customer shows his ready-made tribal tattoos on the back of his hand.  Photo: YLE / Karoliina Kantola

Many people want to remove an old tattoo. A tattoo can be removed with a laser.

Health companies say that many more tattoos are removed than 10 years ago.

Laser therapist Malin Ehnqvist of Eira hospital says that tattoos are most often removed from people under 40. Women want to remove a tattoo a little more often than men.

People often want to remove a tattoo that was done on, for example, the neck or face.

Monday's weather is sunny in many places. The temperature is between 10 and 15 degrees across the whole of Finland. In the north in particular it is warmer than usual for the end of September.

On Tuesday in Southern Finland and Central Finland it is cloudier than on Monday. In the north the weather is also sunny on Tuesday. The temperature on Tuesday is also between 10 and 15 degrees.
