# Tuesday 5.2.2019 (radio)

The parties have agreed on a dispute over a law that concerns elderly care. 2 people died in a fire in Uusikaupunki. VR is cancelling trains because of bad weather. The Finlandia House in Helsinki will soon need to be renovated. Writer Heikki Kännö has received the Runeberg Prize.

## The law on elderly care

![A home carer gives medicine to an elderly person.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9420815)

Open image viewer

An elderly person and a carer. Photo: Julia Sieppi / Yle

The debate on elderly care continues. The parties have agreed on a dispute over a new law that concerns elderly care.

The National Coalition agrees that the new law will set out how many carers there must be for the elderly. At the moment the law does not say how many carers there must be in an elderly care institution.

The National Coalition opposed the new law earlier. The other parties support the law.

The purpose of the law is to get more carers for the elderly. The government may not have time to change the law before the parliamentary elections. The elections are in April.

## The fire in Uusikaupunki

![Fire on Ylisenkatu in Uusikaupunki, 5.2.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10632230)

Firefighters extinguished the fire in Uusikaupunki. Photo: Dani Branthin / Yle

There has been a serious fire in Uusikaupunki in Western Finland. 2 people died in the fire. One person was seriously injured.

A wooden terraced house with four flats burned in the centre of Uusikaupunki. A lot of smoke spread from the fire.

It is not yet known why the fire started.

## Problems with train traffic

![Train in snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10618338)

Open image viewer

The weather has been disrupting train traffic. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

VR is cancelling trains because of the bad weather. Some train services are being replaced by buses.

Trains have frozen, for example, because there has been a lot of frost. That is why the trains need to be serviced.

Some trains are also behind schedule. Most trains are running normally.

## Renovation of the Finlandia House

![Finlandia House.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3076,x_0,y_461/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-27438056cc3450960e9/1456223369)

Open image viewer

The Finlandia House will be renovated in the coming years. Photo: Mikael Crawford/Yle

The Finlandia House in Helsinki will soon be almost 50 years old. The building will need to be renovated soon.

The Finlandia House was designed by the Finnish architect Alvar Aalto. The Finlandia House is a work of art by Alvar Aalto. That is why the renovation must be done carefully.

The building needs new technology, but in the Finlandia House the technology must not be visible. That is why the technology must be built behind the walls.

The surface of the Finlandia House is marble. In the renovation the old marble tiles will be removed, and the Finlandia House will get a new surface.

The renovation begins in 3 years and will last 2 years.

The renovation of the Finlandia House costs over 100 million euros.

## Runeberg Day

![Heikki Kännö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10630275)

Open image viewer

Heikki Kännö won the Runeberg Prize. Photo: Helena Kulmala

Runeberg Day was celebrated on Tuesday.

Johan Ludvig Runeberg is Finland's national poet. For example, the words of the Finnish national anthem come from a poem by Runeberg. Runeberg lived in the 1800s.

The Runeberg Prize was awarded on Tuesday. This year the prize went to the writer Heikki Kännö from Turku. The prize has been awarded every year for over 30 years. The prize is 10,000 euros.
