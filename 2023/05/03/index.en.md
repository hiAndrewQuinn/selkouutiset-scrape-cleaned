## Zelenskyi in Finland

![Zelenskyi was welcomed in the courtyard of the Presidential Palace. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3332,h_1874,x_0,y_220/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1107973645256819b83b/1728732560)

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyi and Finnish President Saul Niinistö on the steps of the Presidential Palace. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Ukrainian President Volodymir Zelenskyi has visited Helsinki. Zelenskyi held talks with President Sauli Niinistö. Zelenskyi said that the reason for the visit is that Finland and Ukraine are partners. President Sauli Niinistö said that Finland will continue to support Ukraine.

At the press conference, Zelenskyi said that this year is decisive for the war. This is the 4th time that President Zelenskyi has left Ukraine since Russia attacked Ukraine. The attack started last February.

Zelenskyi also met the prime ministers of the Nordic countries, who are visiting Helsinki for the Nordic and Ukrainian summit. The topic was, among other things, help for Ukraine.

The president's visit was not announced to the public in advance.

Niinistö and Zelenskyi last met in January in Ukraine.

## Press freedom

![A man with a gas mask and protective goggles has the word PRESS written in large letters on his war helmet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_356/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90773861f3b6aa56094/1643362207)

Press freedom is measured every year. Photo: Photo by engin akyurt on Unsplash

Finland is still among the top countries for press freedom. The organisation Reporters Without Borders has published a new comparison again.

Finland is no longer in the top three but fifth. In first place is now Norway, second is Ireland, third Denmark and fourth Sweden.

Just a couple of years ago, Finland was second in the comparison.

The worst press freedom is in North Korea, China, Vietnam and Iran. Russia is in 164th place.

## Nokia

![Nokia logo on the wall of a building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_213/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11015726440d2da848e7/1683116985)

Open image viewer

Nokia is starting change negotiations, which used to be called co-operation negotiations. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The telecommunications company Nokia is starting change negotiations. They may lead to over 200 jobs disappearing in Finland.

The plans concern all of Nokia's workplaces in Finland, but not, for example, the Oulu factory.

Nokia employs about 7000 people in Finland, in Espoo, Oulu and Tampere.

In March 2021 Nokia estimated that around 5,000–10,000 jobs worldwide may disappear. The exact numbers depend on how the market develops.

## Tax returns

![Tax return.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_432/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-5942157a1cce85cb17/1470221661)

Open image viewer

You do not need to fill in and return a tax return if there is nothing to add. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The deadlines for tax returns are soon. The deadline for over 5 million Finns is in May.

Before the deadline, additions can be made to the return. They concern last year's taxation.

The first deadline for employees is next Tuesday, 9 May. The next deadlines are 16 and 23 May.

The deadline for your own tax return is in the OmaVero service and in the tax return. If there is nothing to add to taxation, you do not need to do anything.
