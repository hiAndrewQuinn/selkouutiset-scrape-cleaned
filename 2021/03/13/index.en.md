# Saturday 13.3.2021 (radio)

The order of coronavirus vaccinations may change. The Prime Minister says that movement restrictions may come to the Helsinki and Turku regions. Train passengers are following the mask requirement well. Musician Pentti Lasanen has died. The weather.

## Vaccination order may change

![Customers wait for their vaccination turn at the Helsinki Messukeskus exhibition centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11825991)

The order of vaccinations may change in Finland. The national vaccine expert group says that in future coronavirus vaccines may perhaps be directed to areas and people where the risk of coronavirus infection is especially high.

The coronavirus is spreading across the whole country at the moment, but especially strongly in the Helsinki region. There are many infections, for example, among people with a foreign background.

In Helsinki, almost 40 percent of coronavirus infections are among people who speak a foreign language. In Vantaa, half of the infections, that is 50 percent, are among people who speak a foreign language.

## Prime Minister on Yle's Ykkösaamu

The government continues to prepare movement restrictions.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin said on TV1's Ykkösaamu that people's movement may perhaps be restricted in the Helsinki and Turku regions, where there are many coronavirus infections.

Marin also said that changing the vaccination order can be considered after the risk groups have been vaccinated.

About 700 new coronavirus infections have been reported in Finland today.

## Mask requirement for train passengers

![Train passengers wearing face masks on 4 September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11578727)

Open image viewer

Face masks are compulsory on VR's trains. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

VR says that people are following the mask requirement on trains well.

Face masks became compulsory on VR's trains on Thursday. A mask does not need to be worn if there is a health reason for it.

On public transport in the Helsinki region, masks must also be worn from today. In Turku and Tampere the mask requirement begins on Monday.

The mask requirement does not apply to children under 12 years old.

## Death of a jazz musician

![Pentti Lasanen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11836174)

Open image viewer

Musician Pentti Lasanen. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Jazz musician and composer Pentti Lasanen has died. He was 84 years old.

Pentti Lasanen played the saxophone, clarinet, flute and trumpet. He also sang in the band Four Cats.

Pentti Lasanen made music for many films and also won the Finnish film industry's Jussi award.

In the autumn Sävel song contest, Pentti Lasanen won in 1985 with the song Luoksesi jään.
