# Monday 10.1.2022 (radio)

Due to corona, 63 people are in intensive care. 12 Uusimaa municipalities are changing corona tracing. Fazer plans a new candy factory. The spring term of schools has started.

## Intensive care

In Finland, 63 people are in intensive care in hospitals because of corona. There are now a little more people in intensive care than before. On Friday there were 56 corona patients in intensive care.

In total, 620 people are in hospital care because of corona. This is 112 people more than on Friday.

More than 23,300 new corona infections have been found in Finland since Friday, the Institute for Health and Welfare THL says.

## Tracing

![Corona tracers at work in the Virastotalo building in Kallio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5039,h_2834,x_0,y_260/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7513525fce320367f25/1607348963)

In Uusimaa, 12 municipalities are ending tracing of some corona infections.

Among others, the municipalities of the capital region, as well as Hyvinkää, Kirkkonummi and Porvoo, will only trace corona infections in risk-group residential units, care homes and health care units.

If a person is exposed to the coronavirus, for example at work or at school, they no longer have to go into quarantine. However, a person exposed to corona must avoid meeting other people.

The reason for the change is the difficult corona situation in Uusimaa. People have to wait many days for a corona test. So there is no time to stop corona infections from spreading with quarantine.

## Vaccine against omicron

![Corona vaccines in a curved bowl.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4618,h_2593,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89954961d82fc3bcfa5/1641558091)

Corona vaccines in a curved bowl. Photo: Timo Leponiemi / Yle

A vaccine against the omicron variant of the coronavirus could be ready as early as March. This is what the directors of the pharmaceutical company Biontech say. The new vaccine will be tested from late January.

The vaccine is meant to give good protection against corona's omicron variant and also against other variants. The vaccine protects well against serious disease.

## Fazer

Fazer wants to build a new candy factory in Finland. The new factory is meant to replace Fazer's current factories in Vantaa and Lappeenranta.

Fazer will decide on the new factory during the spring. The new factory may start operating perhaps in 2025.

The spring term of schools started on Monday. In some municipalities, school began already before Epiphany or right after it.

School started with in-person teaching. If the corona situation worsens in some area, schools may quickly switch to remote teaching.
