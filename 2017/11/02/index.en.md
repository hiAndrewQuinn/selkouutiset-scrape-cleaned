# Thursday 2.11.2017 (radio)

The National Coalition Party is the most popular party. A trial against independence leaders in Catalonia. In Britain, the defence minister had to resign because of harassment suspicions. Guards of honour will remember soldiers who died in the Second World War on 6.12.

## Yle poll: National Coalition Party still the most popular party

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9911169)

Open image viewer

Two government parties increased their support in the October poll.

The National Coalition Party is still the most popular party in the Finnish Broadcasting Company's poll.

In October, Yle carried out a poll in which the National Coalition Party's support was almost 23 percent.

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä's Centre Party also gained support. 17.4 percent supported the Centre Party.

The Social Democrats, or SDP, were the second most popular party. Their support was more than 18 percent. The SDP is not in the government; it is in opposition.

## The Finns Party's popularity fell

![Timo Soini and Jussi Halla-aho at the Finns Party congress in Jyväskylä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9912052)

Open image viewer

Timo Soini handed over the leadership of the Finns Party to Jussi Halla-aho in the summer. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / AOP

The Finns Party lost support. Its support fell to 6.9 percent.

Unlike we wrongly said in our TV broadcast, the Finns Party is not a government party; it is in opposition.

The smallest government group is Blue Reform, which split from the Finns Party in the summer. Its support in Yle's poll was only about one percent.

The Greens also lost a lot of support. A month ago the party was the second most popular party, but now it fell to fourth place in the poll.

## A trial against independence leaders has begun in Catalonia

![A demonstrator held a Catalan flag as former regional leaders arrived at court in Madrid on 2 November.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9913261)

Open image viewer

Demonstrators waved the Catalan flag. Photo: Gabriel Bouys / AFP

In Spain's capital Madrid, a trial against Catalonia's independence leaders has begun.

The speaker of Catalonia's regional parliament and 14 ministers of the regional government have been summoned to court.

They pushed for Catalonia's independence, which according to Spain is illegal.

The former head of Catalonia's regional government, Carles Puigdemont, is in Belgium's capital Brussels. He did not come to court.

## The British defence minister resigned because of harassment accusations

![British Defence Minister Michael Fallon.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9818354)

Open image viewer

Michael Fallon had to leave his job because of harassment accusations. Photo: Dan Himbrechts / EPA

In Britain, the defence minister has resigned because he is suspected of sexual harassment.

Defence Minister Michael Fallon had, among other things, touched a female journalist inappropriately.

A list has been made public in which some politicians of Britain's Conservative Party are accused of sexual harassment.

The accusations threaten to weaken the position of Prime Minister Theresa May, which is already difficult because of Britain's negotiations on leaving the EU.

## War graves will get a guard of honour on Independence Day

![war graves](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5196939)

Open image viewer

In Finnish cemeteries, soldiers' war graves stand out from other graves.  Photo: Eino Nurmi

On Finland's 100th anniversary on 6 December, soldiers who died in the Second World War will be remembered in a new way.

Guards of honour will be organised in many places. At each soldier's grave, a person will stand guard who is the same age as the soldier in the grave was when he fell.

Guards of honour will be organised at least in Vantaa, Oulu, Kuopio, Tampere, Lappeenranta, Salo, Kälviä and Kaustinen.
