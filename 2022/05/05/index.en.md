## NATO's Secretary General

![Jens Stoltenberg speaking at a NATO meeting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3658,h_2057,x_0,y_57/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-939558624eff9e9b235/1649344529)

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg. Photo: Stephanie Lecocq / EPA

The discussion on the relationship between Finland and the military alliance NATO continues.

According to NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg, NATO will become stronger if Finland becomes a NATO member.

Stoltenberg also believes that NATO can process Finland's membership application quickly.

Finland may decide already during May whether it applies for NATO membership.

In Finland, NATO is being discussed because of the war in Ukraine. The security situation in Europe changed when Russia attacked Ukraine.

Many think that Finland is in a safer position if Finland is in NATO. NATO has 30 member countries, which can defend each other.

## Party support

The National Coalition Party is the most popular party in Finland.

This is what Yle's new opinion poll shows. In it, people were asked which party they support.

The support for the National Coalition Party is now 24 per cent. The figure is still high, but it has fallen a little.

The National Coalition Party has long demanded that Finland apply for membership of the military alliance NATO. This has brought more support to the party.

Now many other parties also want Finland to become a NATO member. That is why the support for the National Coalition Party has fallen.

The second largest party in the opinion poll is the SDP, whose support is now 18 per cent. The support for the SDP has also fallen a little.

After the SDP come the Finns Party and the Centre Party. Their support has risen a little.

The support figures for all parties can be found at yle.fi.

## Corona vaccinations for the elderly

![Anonymous picture of a nurse and an elderly person at the elderly person's home. The elderly person is just going to lie down in bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_208/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-922480621f32524b22c/1663312833)

Now a fourth corona vaccination is also recommended for clients of home care. Photo: Hilma Toivonen / Yle

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, has given a new recommendation on corona vaccinations.

THL recommends that the fourth (4th) corona vaccination be given to more elderly people than now.

The fourth corona vaccination will also be given to elderly people who live in home care or are cared for by family members. Until now, the fourth corona vaccination has been given to residents of care homes.

The fourth corona vaccination can also be given to all elderly people who have poor health and functional capacity. Until now it has been given only to people over 80.

Working-age people and other younger people will perhaps get the fourth corona vaccination in the autumn, because in winter the corona epidemic may get worse again.
