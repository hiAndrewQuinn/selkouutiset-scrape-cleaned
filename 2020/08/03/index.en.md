# Monday 3.8.2020 (radio)

Many workers have returned to their workplaces. The test use of the corona app starts on Tuesday. A corona information point has been opened at Helsinki airport. There are now 4 candidates for the post of Centre Party leader. July was cool.

TV Clear News has a summer break and it will continue on 17.8.
Radio Clear News is made every day, also in the summer.

## Many people's remote work has ended

![people working on computers in an open-plan office](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11401200)

Open image viewer

Workers are returning to the offices. Photo: AOP

Remote work has ended in many companies. Employees have returned to their workplaces now at the start of August.

The government recommended remote work because of the corona until the end of July. The recommendation ended at the same time as many people's summer holidays ended.

Many people are again travelling to work by train or bus. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare may decide as early as this week whether it recommends face masks for passengers.

A face mask covers the mouth and nose, and that is why it can stop the spread of the coronavirus.

## The corona app is being tested

![corona app](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11354976)

Open image viewer

The corona app is installed on a phone. Photo: AOP

The test use of the corona app starts on Tuesday. For example, a group of City of Helsinki employees will start testing the corona app.

The corona app is installed on a phone. It tells the owner of the phone if they have been close to a person who has the coronavirus. In this way people can take care not to spread the coronavirus on to others.

The corona app will be available to everyone perhaps in September. Using it is voluntary. However, the app is more helpful if it has many users.

## Airport corona info

![Corona testing point at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11477772)

A coronavirus information point has been opened at Helsinki airport. At the information point, passengers can also get a corona test.

Not all passengers are tested at Helsinki airport. For example, passengers who have corona symptoms can get a test.

## Candidates for leader of the Centre Party

![Petri Honkonen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11477319)

Open image viewer

Petri Honkonen. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

There are now 4 candidates for the post of leader of the Centre Party.

On Monday, the Centre Party's deputy chairman Petri Honkonen said that he wants the post of party leader. Entrepreneur Ilkka Tiainen also said that he is taking part in the race for party leader.

The Centre Party's current leader Katri Kulmuni wants to continue as leader. Annika Saarikko is also aiming for the leadership of the Centre Party. Saarikko returns to her work as minister this week after parental leave.

The Centre Party will choose its party leader in September.

## Cool July

![The weather forecast for Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11463465)

Open image viewer

Friday 24.7. was the coolest day of July. Photo: Seija Paasonen / Yle

July was cool. The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that July was about 2 degrees colder than usual.

In addition, a lot of rain fell in July. The amount of rain in July was about 2 times bigger than usual.
