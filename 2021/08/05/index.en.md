## The government made corona decisions

![People spent a summer day on the giant terrace at Kasarmitori on 14 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82097060c848f55ca9e/1624348674)

Corona vaccinations are being expanded in Finland.

Corona vaccinations are being extended to 12 to 15-year-old children and young people. The vaccinations start next week.

The government decided on the matter today, Thursday.

The government also decided that restaurant restrictions will be tightened because the corona situation has got worse. In areas where corona is spreading, restaurants must close at 11 pm, and the number of customers must be limited also on terraces, as well as indoors. In addition, dancing and karaoke singing are no longer allowed indoors.

The restrictions apply to restaurants in the Helsinki region, Southwest Finland, Pirkanmaa and Kymenlaakso. The restrictions come into force on Sunday.

In acceleration areas restaurants may stay open until 1 am.

Today 752 new corona infections were recorded in Finland. The number of new corona cases has grown by almost 60 percent during the week.

## Schools start with in-person teaching

![Hand sanitiser and face masks at the door of a preliminary test room.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-777519603501363981d/1614095372)

Open image viewer

In schools, corona is also fought this autumn, for example with hand sanitiser and safety distances. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The school year starts this autumn with in-person teaching.

The Ministry of Education and Culture and the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare recommend that basic schools, upper secondary schools and vocational schools start with in-person teaching. The spread of corona infections in schools is fought with familiar means, that is, good hygiene, ventilation and safety distances.

Universities decide for themselves how they organise teaching. Universities also intend to increase in-person teaching.

Finland has won a second medal at the Tokyo Olympics. Boxer Mira Potkonen won bronze.

This is Potkonen's second Olympic bronze. She also won bronze at the Rio Olympics in 2016.

Mira Potkonen intends to stop boxing after these Olympics.

## The National Coalition is the most popular

![July 2021 party support poll. The National Coalition is the largest party, with the SDP and the Finns Party following.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12046299)

Photo: Illusia Sarvas / Yle

Yle has done a new survey on party support. The result of the survey is quite similar to last month.

The National Coalition is the most popular party. About 20 percent of Finns support it.

The second most popular is the SDP, with 19 percent support.

Third is the Finns Party, which about 18 percent of Finns support.
