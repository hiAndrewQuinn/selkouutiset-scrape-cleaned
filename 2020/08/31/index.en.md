# Monday 31.8.2020 (radio)

Corona warning app to the phone. Many infections in Kuhmo. Big corona exposures. Tourism in Lapland is suffering. New study places. The weather turns sunny.

## You can download the Corona warning app to your phone

![Corona warning app on a phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11519205)

Open image viewer

Koronavilkku is an app that you can download to your phone for free. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Now everyone can download Koronavilkku to their own phone.

The Koronavilkku app is meant to stop the coronavirus from spreading.

Koronavilkku is an app that works on the phone. The app can tell the user if the user has been exposed to the coronavirus.

The app also tells you what to do if you have caught the infection.

With the app you can also tell others if you have caught the infection yourself. You can report an infection anonymously.

## Many corona infections in Kuhmo

![Restaurant Eskobar in Kuhmo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11518603)

Open image viewer

A restaurant in Kuhmo has been closed because of the corona. Photo: Elisa Kinnunen / Yle

In Eastern Finland, around the town of Kuhmo, many corona infections have suddenly been found.

There are already 18 infections. 160 people have been exposed to the corona, which means they may have caught the infection.

The people who have become ill are young adults.

There are now strict corona restrictions in the Kuhmo area.

In a case like this, it can happen that many dozens of people have to go into a 2-week quarantine.

## Tourism in Lapland suffers because of the corona

![Chinese tourists on a reindeer sleigh ride in Rovaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9515874)

Open image viewer

A reindeer safari is a common fun activity for tourists in Lapland. Photo: AOP

In Lapland, a big tourism company may have to lay off workers.

The parent company of Lapland Hotels is starting co-operation negotiations. The negotiations may affect 2,000 people's jobs.

The tourism company has been doing badly because of the corona pandemic and the corona restrictions.

Tourism employs many people in Lapland.

In Lapland there are usually many seasonal workers in winter. Now there is no work for them, because tourists do not come from abroad.

## More students to universities

![Studying radio technology at Metropolia University of Applied Sciences in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11462859)

Open image viewer

More students are being taken into universities of applied sciences and universities. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The Ministry of Education has decided that more students will be taken into higher education institutions.

In the next few years, universities of applied sciences may take almost 4,000 more students.

Universities may take over 6,000 more students.

More students will be taken, for example, into nurse, doctor and teacher training.

## The weather gets warmer

The weather gets warmer. Tomorrow, Tuesday, the sun will shine. Only in the northern part of Lapland will it be cloudy and rain may fall.

Next night there may be frost.

The temperature may therefore drop below zero at ground level.
