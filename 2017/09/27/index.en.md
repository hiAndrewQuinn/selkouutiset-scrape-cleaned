# Wednesday 27.9.2017 (radio)

The Speaker of Parliament is changing. The atmosphere in reception centres has not changed after the stabbing incident in Turku. Older people use online banking a lot. Women are finally getting permission to drive in Saudi Arabia.

## The Speaker of Parliament changes in February

![Speaker Maria Lohela at the opening session of the autumn session in Parliament in Helsinki on 5 September 2017. The renovated Parliament building was taken into use at the first session of the autumn term.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9853977)

The Speaker of Parliament is changing. The parliamentary groups have agreed that the three (3) largest parliamentary groups get a member in the Speaker's Council. These groups are the Centre Party, the National Coalition Party and the SDP. The Speaker's seat goes to the National Coalition Party. The Speaker's seat has traditionally belonged to the second largest group in Parliament.

The current Speaker is Maria Lohela of the Blue parliamentary group. She is losing the seat because the Blue group is not the second largest. The Blues split from the Finns Party in the summer. The seat of First Deputy Speaker is going to the SDP. The seat of Second Deputy Speaker is staying with the Centre Party.

The Speakers will be elected at the beginning of next year's parliamentary session in February.

## Yle asked about the atmosphere in reception centres

![An underage asylum seeker.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9851797)

Open image viewer

Crowding and long waiting times strain the lives of asylum seekers in reception centres. Photo: Yle

The mass stabbing in Turku has not affected the atmosphere in reception centres much. The situation has returned to normal after the first shock. This is what a Yle survey says. 16 reception centres answered it. There are 48 centres. The survey was for employees in leadership roles.

The residents' mood is affected most by negative asylum decisions, deportations and waiting. There are the most tensions between Muslims and Christians. Churchgoers have sometimes been threatened. Tensions have also been caused by some asylum seekers converting to Christianity. Last year there were the most tensions between Shia and Sunni Muslims.

There are only a few suspicions of radicalisation.

A Moroccan man stabbed ten (10) people in Turku in August. Two (2) of the victims died and eight (8) ended up in hospital.

## Older people in Finland use online banking a lot

![A Sampo Bank key code list on top of a computer keyboard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5002815)

Open image viewer

online banking is familiar to more and more older people. Photo: Yle

Most older people in Finland use online banking. A report by Finance Finland says that 80 percent of 65–74-year-olds use online banking.

In 2014, 66 percent of older people used online banking, and a year later 75 percent.

90 percent of Finns use online banking. That is more than elsewhere in Europe.

## Women in Saudi Arabia get permission to drive a car

![In October 2013 in Saudi Arabia, women protested against the driving ban by driving cars. 14 were arrested.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9847811)

Open image viewer

In October 2013 in Saudi Arabia, women protested against the driving ban by driving cars. Photo: STR / EPA

In Saudi Arabia, women have got the right to drive a car for the first time. The order was given by King Salman of Saudi Arabia. The reform will take effect next summer.

Saudi Arabia was the only country in the world where women have not had permission to drive.

There have been protests in the country for the right to drive since the 1990s. Women have been arrested and fired from work if they have driven a car.
