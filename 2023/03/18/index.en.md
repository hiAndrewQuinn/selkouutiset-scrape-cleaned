## Turkey and Finland's NATO membership

![Presidents Sauli Niinistö and Recep Tayyip Erdoğan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689905)

The presidents of Finland and Turkey have met in Turkey. Photo: Riikka Hietajärvi/Tasavallan presidentin kanslia

Turkey plans to approve Finland joining the NATO defence alliance.

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan said on Friday that the Turkish Parliament will start to discuss Finland's NATO membership.

Turkey's Parliament must also approve Finland's NATO membership, so that Finland can join NATO.

President Erdoğan told the news conference about Turkey's decision. Finnish President Sauli Niinistö was also there. Niinistö has been visiting Turkey.

Niinistö thanked Erdoğan. However, he said that Finland also wants Sweden to join NATO. Turkey has not yet approved Sweden's NATO membership.

Finland applied for NATO membership together with Sweden last year. The reason was Russia's war in Ukraine.

Finland and Sweden think they are safer if they are members of the NATO defence alliance.

## Ukraine and military aircraft

![Mig-29 fighter jet on the runway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5567,h_3131,x_0,y_123/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9456406265634b49a20/1650811957)

Mig fighter. Photo: Georgi Paleykov / AOP

Ukraine is getting military aircraft from Europe. On Friday Slovakia said that it will give Ukraine 13 military aircraft.

Ukraine is getting Mig-type fighters from Slovakia. A fighter is a military aircraft that is suitable for air combat.

Poland has also promised Ukraine 4 Mig fighters. The Mig fighters suit Ukraine well, because the model is familiar to the Ukrainian army.

Ukraine has especially wanted more military aircraft. There has been a war in the country for over a year already. Russia attacked Ukraine last February.

## The fitness of Finnish men

![Jogger on a snowy running path.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11057637)

Open image viewer

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), about 3.2 million people die early each year because of too little exercise. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

The fitness of Finnish men is getting worse. Men's endurance has been declining for many decades already.

Three things affect endurance. They are a man's weight, the amount of exercise, and inherited factors, that is, genes.

Men's endurance has declined so much that it already affects men's ability to function.

Soon, no men over 50 will be seen in heavy jobs. This is what the UKK Institute predicts. It researches health exercise.

On Saturday snow will fall in the central and northern parts of the country. In the south and west it will rain and sleet.

In the southern and central parts of the country it is very windy and the temperature is above zero. In the north it is freezing on Saturday, and it is a little less windy there.
