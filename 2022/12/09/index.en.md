The price of electricity will rise next week because of the frost. Electricity is already very expensive now.

In frosty weather, heating uses a lot of electricity. That is why the price of electricity rises.

The price of electricity depends on how much Finns manage to save electricity. In November, Finns used 10 percent less electricity than a year earlier.

However, there is not yet a shortage of electricity. There is still enough electricity for everyone.

## Olkiluoto 3

![Olkiluoto nuclear power plant](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4743,h_2655,x_0,y_494/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98340162d53781d6292/1664522122)

Open image viewer

The Olkiluoto 3 nuclear power plant. Photo: Lucas Ekblad / Yle

Finland will get more nuclear power, that is, atomic power, perhaps in February or later.

Then Olkiluoto's new nuclear power plant, Olkiluoto 3, will start producing electricity regularly. The plant's owner, Teollisuuden Voima, estimates this.

Teollisuuden Voima also owns the older Olkiluoto 2 nuclear power plant.

Olkiluoto 3 has already been in trial operation. In the autumn, however, faults were found in it, which are now being repaired.

The trial operation of Olkiluoto 3 continues, perhaps until the end of December. Then it may produce electricity regularly, perhaps in February.

When Olkiluoto 3 works regularly, it will produce more than 10 percent of Finland's electricity.

Olkiluoto 3 is important for Finland, because there is an energy crisis in Europe. The reason for the energy crisis is Russia's war in Ukraine.

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg. Photo: EPA-EFE

NATO, the defence alliance, Secretary General is afraid that Russia's war in Ukraine will spread elsewhere.

Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg says that the war could spread between NATO and Russia. However, Stoltenberg believes that the situation will remain under control. One reason for this is that NATO has strengthened its defence in Eastern Europe.

Norwegian radio interviewed Stoltenberg on Friday. Stoltenberg said that NATO's most important task is to prevent a big war in Europe.

Many bus services have been cancelled in the Helsinki region. On Friday some train services and tram services were also cancelled.

The reason for the situation is that there are too few drivers. For example, drivers are off sick.

Public transport has also had problems because of the snowfall.

## The weather

On Saturday it will snow in Central Finland. Elsewhere there may also be a few snow showers, but mostly it is dry.

In Lapland the frost is over 10 degrees, and in southern and central Finland about 5 degrees.

The frost tightens on Sunday. Then in Lapland there may be a frost of about 20 degrees, and in Central Finland about 10 degrees. In southern Finland the frost on Sunday is about 5 degrees.
