# Thursday 3.8.2017 (radio)

Rents for flats are rising in Finland. Travel agencies are selling a lot of trips after a cold summer. There is power in a gaze. Finnish compound words are difficult.

## Expensive rents

![A studio flat in Viitatorni.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6685383)

Many people are looking for a small rental flat. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Rents for flats are rising in Finland. At the beginning of the summer, rents were 2.5 per cent higher than a year ago.

The highest rents are found in the Helsinki region. There, the average rent is already over 18 euros per square metre of the flat.

There are too few small rental flats in Finland. That is why rents per square metre for studio flats are more expensive than rents per square metre for big flats.

## Finns' holiday trips

![Sun worship and especially getting sunburnt expose you to melanoma.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6556606)

A Finn wants to go south in winter if the summer at home was cold. Photo: Pekka Sipilä / Yle

Travel agencies are now selling a lot of trips. Finns want to travel south next autumn or winter, because the summer in Finland has been cold.

Most of the autumn trips have already been sold. During the autumn holiday, many Finns travel, for example, to the Greek islands or to Spain.

A lot of winter trips have also been sold. In winter, Finns travel, for example, to the Canary Islands and to Thailand.

Finns can now afford to buy trips, because Finland's economic situation has improved.

## A powerful gaze

![Eyes](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9753937)

Open image viewer

Does this affect you? Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

There is power in a gaze. A person is more attentive and more alert if someone looks at them. Then a person also learns and remembers better.

Psychologist Terhi Helminen from the University of Tampere has studied the power of the gaze. Helminen says that a person's hands sweat a little more than usual when someone looks at them. This can be measured in all people everywhere in the world.

Sweating hands are a sign that a person is more attentive than usual. So a person can affect another person with their gaze.

## Difficult compound words

![An illustration of the language police.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9755749)

Open image viewer

The Finnish language is sometimes difficult even for Finnish speakers. Photo: Yle News Graphics

Finnish compound words are difficult. There are many long words. For example, "työterveyslääkäriasema" (occupational health clinic) is written as one word.

Finnish speakers also make mistakes with compound words. People even argue about the mistakes, for example on Facebook.

Researcher Sari Maamies says that the number of different texts and writers has grown because of social media. That is why you see more compound word mistakes nowadays than before.

Language and its rules also change. The old rule is that, for example, "päinvastoin" (on the contrary) is written together, that is, it is a compound word. Nowadays, however, it can also be written separately, as "päin vastoin".

When you really think about compound words, even an expert can get confused, researcher Sari Maamies laughs.
