# Monday 23.11.2020 (radio)

The restrictions in Uusimaa have been tightened. The Prime Minister says that the restrictions must be increased. Viking Grace got to Turku. Tuomas Gerdt will be buried on Saturday. Many companies are not holding Christmas parties.

## Coronavirus restrictions in Uusimaa

New coronavirus restrictions have been brought in in Uusimaa today, Monday.

Events with more than 20 people are banned. For example, theatres are closed. Adult team sports are banned in the city sports halls.

In upper secondary schools in the Helsinki area, pupils are given face masks. Face masks are also recommended in workplaces.

## Marin: More coronavirus measures are needed

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin stands in front of the Pirkkala joint upper secondary school with a face mask on.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_213/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7450535fbb7041ebb78/1606119625)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Photo: Petra Ketonen / Yle

Prime Minister **Sanna Marin** says that coronavirus restrictions must be increased in Finland.

Marin says that the hospital districts and municipalities must bring in stricter restrictions, because the coronavirus is now spreading quickly.

If the municipalities and hospital districts do not add measures, the government can bring in the emergency powers act. Then the government can, for example, isolate the Uusimaa area, as the government did in the spring.

## Viking Grace got to Turku

![The Viking Line passenger ship Viking Grace, which ran aground, in the port of Mariehamn on 22 November 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11660448)

Open image viewer

The Viking Grace in the port of Mariehamn on Sunday. Photo: Niclas Nordlund / Lehtikuva

The Viking Grace ship has reached Turku. The Viking Grace ran aground on the shore in Åland on Saturday.

On Sunday the Viking Grace was able to continue its journey from Åland to Turku, and it arrived in Turku in the evening.

The ship company Viking Line says that there was no major damage to the ship. The Viking Grace will still go to the shipyard for an inspection.

## Tuomas Gerdt will be buried on Saturday

![Tuomas Gerdt.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1500,h_844,x_0,y_120/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6143505dd6926ee2a26/1574343402)

Open image viewer

Tuomas Gerdt (28 May 1922 – 1 November 2020). Photo: Touko Yrttimaa / Yle

The last Knight of the Mannerheim Cross, **Tuomas Gerdt**, will be buried on Saturday.

Gerdt's funeral will be held in Lappeenranta with military ceremonies. President **Sauli Niinistö** will also take part in the funeral.

The last Knight of the Mannerheim Cross, Tuomas Gerdt, died on 1 November. He was 98 years old.

## Few Christmas parties in companies

![Sparkling wine being served at Alko](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9983963)

Open image viewer

Christmas parties are usually celebrated in many companies. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Many companies are not holding Christmas parties this year. The EK employers' association asked companies whether they will hold Christmas parties. Most, 64 per cent, of companies say that they are not holding Christmas parties this year.

Some companies are still holding Christmas parties. About 20 per cent of companies say that they will hold Christmas parties, for example at company premises or in a restaurant.

Only 4 per cent of companies are celebrating Christmas with a remote connection.
