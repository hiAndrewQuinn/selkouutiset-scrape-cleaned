# Saturday 2.3.2019 (radio)

The youth climate meeting. The funeral of Matti Nykänen. Repeat applications from asylum seekers. The ski world championships. Weather.

## Young people made a climate programme

!["Our Climate 2030" is written on the cover of the brochure](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10670424)

Open image viewer

The young people's climate programme can be found on the website nuortenagenda.fi.

Young people held a climate meeting in Helsinki today. There were 500 young people from all over Finland at the meeting.

The young people held the meeting because they demanded more action from politicians for the climate. The young people published a programme with things that must be done.

The young people say that Finland must favour plants in food production. In addition, carbon sinks, such as forests, must be kept and increased.

## Matti Nykänen was buried

![Matti Nykänen's coffin is carried to a car before the funeral procession departs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10670322)

Ski jumper Matti Nykänen was buried today. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Famous Finnish ski jumper Matti Nykänen was buried today. He was buried in his home town, Jyväskylä.

Many Finns wanted to remember Matti Nykänen. Many people watched the funeral procession. There were also ski jumpers at the funeral who had competed with Matti.

Matti Nykänen is a Finnish sports legend.

He won 4 Olympic gold medals in ski jumping. He died suddenly a month ago.

## Repeat applications from asylum seekers

![A man behind a hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10245663)

Open image viewer

A repeat application must have good reasons for seeking asylum. Photo: Yle

The Finnish Ministry of the Interior believes that some asylum seekers submit a new asylum application without any reason. They try to delay leaving Finland by submitting a new application, even though it is pointless.

The Finnish Immigration Service dealt with 1,500 repeat applications last year. 175 people received a positive decision on a repeat application.

A new law is in force in Finland. According to it, a repeat application must have good reasons for seeking asylum.

## Norway's Therese Johaug won again

Today was the second-last day of the ski world championships. The women skied the 30-kilometre race. None of the Finnish skiers managed very well.

The race was won by Norway's Therese Johaug. She won a total of 3 competitions at the world championships.
