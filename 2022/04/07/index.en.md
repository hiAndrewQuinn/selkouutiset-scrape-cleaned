Party support. Natural gas. Telecom operators. Finlandia Hall. Weather.

## Party support

![Party support: Kok: 26.1%, SDP 19.0%, PS 13.6%](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12393625)

Open image viewer

The National Coalition Party has got a record figure in Yle's opinion poll. The support for the opposition party, the National Coalition, was 26.1 percent. In second place was the government party SDP, with 19 percent.

The support for the opposition party, the Finns Party, was 13.6 percent. The support for the government party, the Centre, was 12.3.

Of all the parties, only the support for the National Coalition and the SDP has risen.

Behind the National Coalition's rise is a change in security policy. Support for joining the defence alliance NATO has risen. The National Coalition is the most NATO-friendly party in Finland.

The support for the Greens was 8.9 and for the Left Alliance 8.4 percent. Both are in the government.

The support figures for other parties are on Yle's website.

## Liquefied natural gas

![Haminan LNG-termiinaali. Taustalla kaasun varastosäiliö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_312/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-92958762333ba1bf73f/1647524900)

Open image viewer

Haminan LNG terminal. Gas storage tank in the background. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

Finland is leasing a joint floating LNG gas terminal with Estonia. The LNG ship is intended for storing liquefied natural gas.

The ship will reduce Finland's dependence on Russian pipeline gas. The floating LNG terminal helps industry, among others, to get gas.

It is intended to be placed at Paldiski in Estonia next winter, and later in southern Finland.

## Telecom operators

![Screenshot from the mobiilimaksuinfo page.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10924803)

Open image viewer

Screenshot from the mobiilimaksuinfo.fi website. Photo: Screenshot from the mobiilimaksuinfo.fi website

Telecom operators DNA and Elisa tell Yle that they have prepared for cyber attacks.

Denial-of-service attacks have been seen, among other places, at banks after Russia attacked Ukraine. In a denial-of-service attack, for example, online banking has not worked normally.

Telecom operator Elisa says that the situation is being monitored more effectively than before.

## Finlandia Hall's lamps

![Finlandia Hall](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10376496)

Open image viewer

Photo: Pete Laakso

The 2,700 lamps of Helsinki's Finlandia Hall will be restored. The lamps are being restored by the lighting workshop Fasetti Oy in Hollola.

Finlandia Hall's major renovation began in February. Restoring the lamps will take a couple of years.

The aim is to keep the patina that has formed on them over the decades.

The lamp technology will be renewed, and LED lamps will be fitted as bulbs.

The architect Alvar Aalto designed several unique lamps for Finlandia Hall.

Finlandia Hall was completed in 1971.

Finlandia Hall is also a popular sight. Thousands of travellers from all over the world get to know the building every year.
