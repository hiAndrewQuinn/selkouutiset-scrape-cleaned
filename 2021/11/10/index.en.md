## Tax information

![The picture shows the logo of the Tax Administration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6358568)

Photo: Heini Holopainen / Yle

The Tax Administration has published last year's tax information. The tax information was told on Wednesday.

The tax information shows how much income Finns have and how much tax they pay.

Last year the largest incomes in Finland belonged to Mikko Kodisoja and Ilkka Paananen. Both had income of about 90 million euros, and both paid over 30 million euros in tax.

Kodisoja and Paananen are remembered for having founded the gaming company Supercell.

More tax information is on Yle's website. The address is yle.fi.

Yle publishes tax information for the sake of openness. Everyone has the right to know where tax money comes from and how big the incomes of important managers are.

## Wolt

![WOLT food couriers on Aleksanterinkatu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4503,h_2532,x_0,y_465/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8038766095496ec1313/1635925016)

Wolt food couriers. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

On Wednesday a big deal was also reported. The Finnish company Wolt is being sold.

Wolt is a food delivery company. Customers can order food from restaurants through Wolt.

The buyer of Wolt is the American company DoorDash. It works in the same field as Wolt.

The purchase price is about 7 billion euros. The price is paid in shares.

Wolt estimates that Finland will get over 600 million euros in taxes from the deal.

Wolt operates in 23 countries. Wolt's operations will continue as before also after the deal.

## Fathers of the year

![Three men awarded with wreaths and Family and Basic Services Minister Krista Kiuru.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3936,h_2214,x_0,y_74/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-877879618b880d311b3/1636534375)

The fathers of the year are Sergei Silkin, Dan Wickholm and Marko Oikarainen. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

The Father of the Year award was given on Wednesday to 3 fathers. They are Sergei Silkin, Dan Wickholm and Marko Oikarainen.

Helsinki native Sergei Silkin is known among Russian speakers. Silkin is a modern father. He wants to show how co-parenting works after a separation. Silkin has also led hobby clubs for multilingual children.

Dan Wickholm from Sipoo raises the children of the family and is also the carer of his wife. The wife fell ill in 2014. Wickholm says that the children bring joy to life.

In the family of Marko Oikarainen from Kouvola there are 5-year-old twins. That is why Oikarainen is active in the Monikkoperheet association. The association gives information and support to families who have twins or triplets. Oikarainen has brought a father's perspective to the association.

The fathers of the year were chosen now, because Father's Day is celebrated on Sunday.
