# Saturday 29.6.2019 (radio)

The Pride parade gathered 80,000 marchers. Jussi Halla-aho continues to lead The Finns Party. The number of applicants for practical nurse training fell sharply. The summer religious festival Suviseurat is held in Muhos. The Tuska festival may set a new visitor record.

## Pride parade

![Participants in the Pride parade.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10854786)

Open image viewer

About 80,000 people marched in the Pride parade. Photo: Teemu Silván / Yle

The colourful and noisy Pride parade has marched through central Helsinki. The organisers estimate that there were perhaps about 80,000 people in the parade.

The Pride parade marched from Senate Square to Kaivopuisto. After that, a park party was held in Kaivopuisto.

This year, for the first time, the Prime Minister of Finland also marched in the parade.

Pride is a human rights event for sexual and gender minorities. The purpose of the event is to remind people that all people have the same rights.

## The Finns Party's party congress

![The Finns Party chair Jussi Halla-aho](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10854665)

Open image viewer

The Finns Party chair Jussi Halla-aho. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

The Finns Party is holding its party congress in Tampere over the weekend. This is the first party congress since the party split after the 2017 congress.

Jussi Halla-aho will continue as chair of The Finns Party. He did not have a single rival candidate.

Halla-aho will give his policy speech tomorrow, on Sunday.

## Practical nurse training

![Practical nurse training.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9902649)

Open image viewer

A practical nurse student practises handing out medicines. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Young people are applying for practical nurse training less than before.

This spring, fewer than 8,600 people applied for practical nurse studies. That is about 4,600 fewer than 5 years ago.

The number of places to start practical nurse studies has also fallen. There are now fewer than 5,100 starting places, about 500 fewer than in 2014.

In recent years there has been a lot of talk about the problems in nursing work, and especially in elderly care. That may be one reason for the drop in applicants, say the Ministry of Education and Culture and the health sector trade union Tehy.

## The summer religious festival

![Suviseura 2019 in Muhos](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10850708)

Open image viewer

Suviseurat is one of Finland's largest summer events. Photo: Suviseurat / picture service

The summer religious festival is held in Muhos, near Oulu.

Suviseurat is a religious summer festival organised by the Laestadian revivalist movement.

Suviseurat is one of Finland's largest summer events. The busiest day was today, Saturday, when more than 80,000 people took part in the gatherings. The organisers say that there were guests from about 30 different countries.

## Tuska festival

![Tuska 2015 in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10853634)

Open image viewer

The Tuska festival is being held for the 22nd time now. This picture is from 2015. Photo: Joni Haavisto / AOP

The Tuska festival is being held in Suvilahti in Helsinki over the weekend. Tuska is a festival of heavy music.

Tuska may set a new visitor record this year, as the organisers expect more than 40,000 visitors to the festival. Today, Saturday, the day tickets for the festival were sold out.
