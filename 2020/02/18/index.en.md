## Nurses have to defend themselves

![Nurse in a corridor, New Children's Hospital, Helsinki, 8.7.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5388,h_3031,x_0,y_564/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5868615d68c06beadb7/1589787144)

Open image viewer

Nurses say that patients can be violent. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

Many nurses are learning self-defence skills. Nurses want skills that help them get along with patients.

Nurses say that they often meet patients who are violent.

Patients can hit, kick or even choke them.

In a survey by the Finnish Nurses' Union, 40 percent of nurses said that they had experienced violence at work.

For example, a memory illness can make a patient violent.

## Many factories in China are now closed

![Workers wearing protective face masks work at the port in Qingdao, China.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11214289)

Open image viewer

A lot of clothing is imported into Finland from China. Photo: AFP

In China, many factories are closed.

The workers are at home so that the coronavirus does not spread. Many factories are closed for as long as 4 weeks.

For example, clothes, smartphones and computers are not being made at all.

A lot of clothing is imported into Finland from China.

It may be that the situation of the factories will be seen in Finland. The factories may not be able to make the clothes that have been ordered.

That will become clear later, maybe only in the autumn.

## There is a big strike in Greece

![A pigeon circles above the entrance of a metro station on 18 February 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11214306)

A one-day strike against the pension reform began in Greece today.

In Athens, the strike by workers has stopped public transport, as well as trains and ferries.

Civil servants have also walked out of their workplaces.

The world's richest man is the American business leader Jeff Bezos.

He has decided to give 10 billion dollars to the fight against climate change. He says that climate change is the biggest threat to the whole planet.

Researchers and activists will get money from a fund that Bezos is setting up.

## The bears at Korkeasaari woke up

![Bears at Korkeasaari.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11214384)

Open image viewer

The female bears at the Korkeasaari zoo have already woken up from their winter sleep. Photo: Annika Sorjonen / Korkeasaari

The female bears at the Korkeasaari zoo have already woken up from their winter sleep.

The female bears slept for only 2 months. That is a really short time for bears, because usually they sleep for 4 months.

Now they woke up, because the weather is so warm.

The bears cannot yet manage to be awake all day. They can still go to a winter den for a nap, if they feel tired.

## The wind is weakening

![Vantaanjoki river in flood at Tammistonranta in Vantaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11213954)

Tomorrow, Wednesday, the weather will change, as the strong wind weakens.

In Northern Finland the sun will shine and it is frosty there.

In Southern and Central Finland the weather is cloudy. During the day the temperature is a couple of degrees above zero
