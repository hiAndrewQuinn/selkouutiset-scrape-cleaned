# Wednesday 10.2.2021 (radio)

A price cartel has been revealed in the property management sector. The opposition has submitted an interpellation about the EU recovery package. Ice hockey player Mikko Koivu is ending his career.

## Cartel

![Lobby of the Competition and Consumer Authority.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_216/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6318545e206542eb245/1612952050)

The lobby of the Competition and Consumer Authority. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

Property management firms have had a cartel. This means that the firms have agreed together on the prices of services. In this way the services cost a lot. A cartel is illegal.

6 property management firms and the Property Management Association have been part of the cartel. Authorities say that the cartel started in 2014. Firms from different parts of Finland have been part of the cartel.

Property management firms look after the administration of housing companies.

The Competition and Consumer Authority told about the cartel.

## Coronavirus figures

178,000 people in Finland have now received the coronavirus vaccine. 4,500 more people have received the vaccine than yesterday, says the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

The most vaccine has been given in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district. Almost 47,000 people have received it there.

There are 400 new coronavirus infections. About 300 of them have been found in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district.

In total, about 49,000 coronavirus infections have now been found in Finland.

## Interpellation

![Parliament House / 09.01.2019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5040,h_2835,x_0,y_244/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5353945c35d500aa8e5/1547031888)

Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

The Finns Party has submitted an interpellation, which the government must answer. The Christian Democrats and Now Movement are also part of the interpellation. The National Coalition Party is not part of the interpellation.

The interpellation is about Finland taking part in the EU recovery package. The opposition thinks that the recovery package is bad for Finland. The Finns Party, the Christian Democrats and the Now Movement say that the package is really expensive for Finland.

In July, EU countries agreed on a big recovery package. In the package, countries get money and loans from the EU.

## Mikko Koivu

![Mikko Koivu](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11781924)

Open image viewer

Koivu played as many as 15 seasons in Minnesota. Photo: Lehtikuva / Getty Images / AFP

Ice hockey player Mikko Koivu is ending his playing career.

Mikko Koivu has played in the NHL for 16 years. He played over 1,000 NHL games. Koivu played for a long time for the Minnesota Wild team.

Mikko Koivu won the ice hockey world championship with the Finnish team in 2011. He was the captain of the team. Koivu also won 2 medals at the Olympic Games.
