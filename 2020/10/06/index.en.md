# Tuesday 6.10.2020 (radio)

The corona is spreading faster than before. Finnair is cutting winter flights. President Donald Trump has left hospital. The start of October was warm.

## The coronavirus is spreading quickly

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin arrived at the Säätytalo in Helsinki on 29 September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11574662)

The coronavirus is now spreading quickly in Finland.

In Finland 227 new coronavirus infections have been found. The authorities say that the figure is the biggest of the autumn.

Most new infections are in Vaasa. In Vaasa 77 new infections have been found in a day.

In Helsinki the recommendation on using face masks has been tightened. The authorities recommend that masks are used, for example, in shops. Restaurants in the capital region may be closed if they do not follow the corona rules.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the government may decide on new restrictions because of the corona next week.

## Finnair is cutting winter flights

![A beach in Tenerife.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6746785ebbb24bb4746/1589359318)

Finnair is cutting many winter flights. Finnair flies about half as many flights in winter as a year ago. In winter Finnair flies to a total of 51 destinations. Last year Finnair flew to over 100 destinations.

In winter Finnair flies about 75 flights a day. Last year Finnair flew about 350 flights a day.

Aurinkomatkat is cancelling package holidays abroad for November. The December trips to Phuket and Krabi in Thailand by Aurinkomatkat have also been cancelled.

Aurinkomatkat is part of Finnair.

## President Trump has left hospital

![Donald Trump gives a thumbs up as he leaves the Walter Reed hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11580357)

The US President Donald Trump has left hospital.

Trump got the coronavirus and was in hospital for 3 days. Now Trump is getting treatment in the White House. President Trump's doctor says that the president is doing well and no longer has a fever.

Trump will soon continue his election campaign. Trump says that he will take part in the election debate next Thursday. The US presidential election is in November. The candidates for president are Republican Trump and Democrat Joe Biden.

## October has been record warm

![The current village of Kaamanen in Inari.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1969,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7191475f60b10161a39/1602078122)

The start of October has been really warm in Finland. Especially in Northern Lapland it has been much warmer than usual for early October. The temperature in Northern Lapland has been 6 degrees and in the whole country 4-5 degrees higher than usual.

The weather also stays warm for the rest of the week. For a while, however, the wind is strong and it rains in many places.
