# Wednesday 25.9.2019 (radio)

The new climate report is grim reading. Climate activist Greta Thunberg has won the alternative Nobel. Wärtsilä is making layoffs. Finns are eating less meat.

## New climate report published

![Icebergs in Ilulissat, Greenland](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3893,h_2190,x_0,y_255/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5933745d893a55bfb1c/1569274487)

The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change, IPCC, has published a new climate report.

The report says that human activity is speeding up the rise in the Earth's average temperature, and the consequences are serious. For example, glaciers are melting faster, the amount of snow is decreasing and the seas are warming.

The report says that, for example, 80 percent of the glaciers in the Alps, Sweden and Norway could disappear this century, if climate emissions are not cut. The glaciers of Antarctica and Greenland are also melting faster and faster.

Melting glaciers raise the sea level. The sea level could rise by as much as a metre this century. The homes of hundreds of millions of people could be under water, for example in Bangladesh, Indonesia, the Netherlands and Florida.

The sea water is also warming. It strengthens extreme weather phenomena, that is, for example, violent storms, heavy rain, heatwaves and floods.

## Greta Thunberg receives an award

![Greta Thunberg in front of the parliament building in Stockholm in November 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10988775)

Open image viewer

Greta Thunberg. Photo: Hanna Franzen / EPA

Swedish climate activist Greta Thunberg has been given an award in Sweden.

Thunberg received the Right Livelihood Award, which is also called the alternative Nobel Prize.

Thunberg was given the award because she has demanded that politicians take action to stop climate change. She has justified her demands with scientific facts.

The prize sum is one million Swedish kronor, that is, about 94,000 euros.

## Wärtsilä makes layoffs

![Wärtsilä logo on the roof of a building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10978015)

Wärtsilä plans to cut perhaps about 200 employees in Finland. Photo: Markku Ojala / AOP

The engineering company Wärtsilä plans to cut employees.

Wärtsilä plans to cut about 350 employees in total. In Finland, Wärtsilä will cut perhaps about 200 employees.

Wärtsilä makes ship engines and power plants. It operates in 70 countries.

## Meat consumption is falling

![Meat at a meat counter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10420865)

Open image viewer

The meat counter in a shop. Photo: Markku Ojala / AOP

Finns' meat consumption is turning downward this year. In the last 3 years, each Finn has eaten an average of about 81 kilograms of meat a year.

Pork consumption has been falling for several years now. Now beef consumption is falling too. In addition, poultry consumption is no longer growing, says the Pellervo Economic Research Institute.

Meat consumption is falling, for example, because meat is more expensive than before. Many people also replace meat with vegetables, for ethical or health reasons.
