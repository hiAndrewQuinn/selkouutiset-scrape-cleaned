# Saturday 16.1.2021 (radio)

Virus mutation. Corona figures. Vaccinations. Lahti. The weather.

## The mutated coronavirus

![Professor Olli Vapalahti of the University of Helsinki's zoonotic virology at Meilahti in Helsinki on 1 December 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11740715)

Professor Olli Vapalahti. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Coronavirus testing at Finland's borders must be increased. More testing is needed so that the spread of the mutated coronavirus to Finland can be prevented. This is what Professor of virology Olli Vapalahti of the University of Helsinki says.

The new, mutated coronavirus is dangerous, because it spreads easily and quickly.

Most of the mutated viruses come to Finland from Britain at the moment, but they also come from other countries.

Professor Olli Vapalahti says that in Finland it is still possible to influence the spread of the virus mutation. The mutation has not yet spread here as much as in other countries.

So far more than 60 corona infections have been found in Finland that were caused by the virus mutation.

## Finland's corona figures

![Test tubes at the Messukeskus drive-in coronavirus testing station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11615208)

A total of more than 40,000 coronavirus infections have now been found in Finland.

190 new infections have been reported, says the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

Since the spring more than 2.6 million coronavirus tests have been done in Finland.

## India started vaccinations

![Boxes of vaccines in India.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11740728)

Open image viewer

Coronavirus vaccines were brought yesterday to Dharamsala in northern India. Photo: Sanjay Baid / EPA

India has started corona vaccinations today. By early August India intends to vaccinate 300 million people.

India's vaccination programme is one of the biggest in the world, because more than a billion people live in India.

The World Health Organization, WHO, says that all countries must speed up corona vaccinations. The WHO says that all countries must start vaccinations within the next hundred days.

About 94 million coronavirus infections have now been reported in the world. More than 2 million people have died of corona in total.

## European Green Capital

![Environmental capital 2021 poster in Lahti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6364585e316cad90a19/1580303026)

Open image viewer

Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

Lahti is the new European Green Capital. Lahti's year as the European Green Capital began yesterday.

The European Green Capital must act as an example in environmental matters. Lahti intends to highlight, for example, how medium-sized cities can fight climate change.

Lahti is the first Finnish European Green Capital. Before Lahti, the Green Capital was the Portuguese capital, Lisbon.

The frost will weaken tomorrow.
