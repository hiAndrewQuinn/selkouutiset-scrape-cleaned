## Corona vaccinations for young people

![Lower secondary school pupils sitting on a bench](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11804756)

Open image viewer

Young people will soon get corona vaccinations. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

Corona vaccinations for 12 to 15-year-olds start next week.

Health sector experts say that it is good for young people to protect themselves against corona.

The experts also say that corona vaccination is safe for young people.

Young people get corona vaccines that have been carefully tested. The corona vaccines have not caused harm to young people.

12 to 15-year-olds can decide for themselves whether they take a corona vaccination. Parents or teachers may not force a young person to be vaccinated. A health care professional assesses whether the young person can decide the matter themselves.

On Saturday, Finland's new corona figures were not available. The reason is that the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare has technical problems.

Because of the problems, the institute has not been able to say exactly how many new corona infections there are.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare will tell the exact corona figures for the weekend only on Monday.

## Greek forest fires

![Vehicles burned in a ground fire north of Athens.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12047055)

Open image viewer

The Greek ground fires have caused a lot of destruction. Photo: Louisa Gouliamaki / AFP

Ground fires continue in Greece. Fire is burning forest and buildings, for example near the Greek capital, Athens.

Thousands of people have been moved to safety away from the fire area. Among them are many tourists too.

Ground fires are difficult to put out because of the wind and the hot weather. In Greece it is about 40 degrees.

In Turkey, Greece's neighbouring country, the situation is better. Ground fires are now easier to put out, because it has rained in Turkey. Most of Turkey's ground fires have already ended.

## North Korean floods

In Asia, in North Korea, there are floods. The cause of the floods is heavy rain.

North Korean television says that the flood has broken more than 1,000 homes. Thousands of people have been moved to safety.

Flood water has also covered fields. It may make the situation worse in North Korea, where there is not enough food.

It is forecast that heavy rain will continue in North Korea until the middle of next week.
