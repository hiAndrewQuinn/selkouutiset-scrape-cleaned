# Wednesday 9.6.2021 (radio)

Coronavirus times have reduced illnesses. The EU coronavirus certificate was approved. The Defence Forces will give coronavirus vaccinations to conscripts. Car scrapping has increased a lot. There are more wolves in Finland than before. Weather forecast.

## Illnesses

![Woman blowing her nose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9995822)

Open image viewer

During the coronavirus period, influenza and intestinal diseases have decreased. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The coronavirus period has reduced illnesses among Finns. For example, there has been about 50% less influenza than before. Also other respiratory and intestinal diseases have been much less common than before.

Illness has decreased because people follow good hygiene. For example, people often wash their hands and use masks.

People also meet only a few other people. That is why many diseases do not spread easily.

In Finland, the coronavirus certificate will come to the OmaKanta service. The service already has Finland's own coronavirus certificate. Photo: Ilkka Klemola / Yle

The European Parliament has approved the joint EU coronavirus certificate.

The certificate will come into use at the beginning of July. The joint EU coronavirus certificate makes travel within the EU area easier.

In Finland the EU coronavirus certificate will appear in the OmaKanta service.

## Coronavirus vaccinations in the Defence Forces

In the Defence Forces, coronavirus vaccinations will be given to those who are doing conscript service in July. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The Defence Forces will give coronavirus vaccinations to conscripts. The vaccination will be given to those male and female conscripts who start their military service in July.

Also conscripts whose service began earlier will get the coronavirus vaccination in July.

Coronavirus vaccination during military service is voluntary.

## Scrapping cars

![Scrapped cars in the storage of a scrapyard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10743358)

Open image viewer

Scrapped cars in the storage of a scrapyard. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

In Finland, far more old cars have been scrapped than before.

This year over 40,000 passenger cars have been scrapped in Finland by the end of May. That is more than ever before in the same time. If the same scrapping rate continues, perhaps over 90,000 cars will be scrapped in Finland this year.

The scrapping of cars has been increased by the scrappage bonus paid for an old car. The price of metals has also risen around the world.

## Wolves

![Wolves](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2914,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-27794056e97eef8ad48/1458143558)

Open image viewer

The wolf population, that is, the number of wolves, has been growing for a long time. Photo: All Over Press/Ismo Pekkarinen

The wolf population in Finland has grown again. The Natural Resources Institute says that there are now about 280-320 wolves in Finland.

Many wolf cubs have been born in areas where wolves have had good food. The number of wolves has now grown for many years in a row.
