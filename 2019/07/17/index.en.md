## The new president of the Commission

![Ursula von der Leyen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10880870)

Open image viewer

Ursula von der Leyen. Photo: Patrick Seeger / EPA

The German Ursula von der Leyen has been chosen as president of the European Union Commission. Von der Leyen will begin as Commission president in November, when Jean-Claude Juncker's term ends.

Ursula von der Leyen is the first woman to lead the EU Commission. She is a 60-year-old doctor with 7 children. Von der Leyen has been a minister in many German governments.

A woman has also been chosen for the first time to lead the European Central Bank: the Frenchwoman Christine Lagarde.

## Rinne told about Finland's goals

![Antti Rinne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10882036)

Open image viewer

Antti Rinne in the European Parliament. Photo: Patrick Seeger / EPA

Prime Minister Antti Rinne has told the European Parliament what Finland wants to do as the EU presiding country. Finland is the EU presiding country until the end of this year.

Finland wants to slow down climate change and increase people's safety. Finland also wants to improve companies' competitiveness and strengthen the independence of the courts.

Prime Minister Rinne said that countries must take seriously, for example, human rights, freedom and democracy, when they are members of the European Union.

## A Russian aircraft in Finnish waters without permission

![A Russian Sukhoi Su-27 in the international airspace of the Baltic Sea and the Gulf of Finland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9673079)

Open image viewer

A Russian Sukhoi fighter aircraft. Photo: Air Force

A Russian aircraft has flown on the Finnish side without permission.

The Finnish Border Guard says that the Russian aircraft flew for 2 minutes in Finnish airspace over the sea off Porvoo in the morning. The aircraft went about one kilometre into Finland's airspace.

The Border Guard is investigating the case.

## Finnair's passenger numbers grew

![Two Finnair aircraft at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. One is just taking off.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1601,h_901,x_0,y_198/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5778625d2dcc9db32e0/1563282640)

Open image viewer

Finnair aircraft at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Photo: Toni Määttä / Yle

Flying from Finland is increasing. Finnair's passenger numbers grew to a record high in April to June. There were many passengers on flights to Asia and Europe.

However, Finnair made less profit than last spring. One reason for this was the rise in the price of fuel.

Aircraft emissions are one big cause of climate warming.

## Luxury is to be sold to Chinese tourists

![Chinese tourists at the Vuoksi fishing park in Imatra](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10328398)

Open image viewer

Chinese tourists in Imatra. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

Chinese tourists spend less money in Finland than in many other countries. A Chinese tourist spends on average 1,300 euros in Finland and, for example, 3,000 euros in Britain.

The tourism industry now wants to improve the level of its services and charge more for them. The director of a tourism company, Timo Uusi-Kerttula, says that Chinese people are happy to pay for luxury, for example helicopter flights.

Paavo Virkkunen, director of the Visit Finland office, says that luxury in Finland is different from elsewhere in the world. In Finland, luxury means, for example, authenticity, nature and local food.

## A warm weekend is coming

![Festival audience at Robin Packalen's Ruisrock gig.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_672/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5758855d20b5b9ead73/1586887207)

The weather is warming up. At the weekend there will be at least 20 degrees of heat across the whole country.

Until now July in Finland has been cool. The nights in particular have been cold: there has been ground frost almost every night.
