# Thursday 13.9.2018 (radio)

Alcohol consumption has gone down. Many schoolchildren go to school in temporary premises. A powerful storm is approaching North America. There is also a strong storm in Asia. Crown Princess Mary of Denmark is visiting Finland.

## Alcohol consumption is going down

![A worker fills a drinks shelf in a shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10401167)

Open image viewer

New alcoholic drinks have come to the shops, but alcohol consumption has fallen. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

Finns drink less alcohol than before. In the first part of the year, Finns consumed almost one percent less alcohol than a year ago.

Alcohol consumption has been going down for many years already. The situation has not changed, even though new alcoholic drinks came to grocery shops at the start of this year.

Grocery shops can now sell stronger alcoholic drinks than before. However, only the sales of long drinks, or "lonkero", have grown. For example, the sales of beer and cider have gone down.

## Schools in poor condition

![Children studying in the temporary premises of Pelkosenniemi school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10400166)

Open image viewer

Schoolchildren in Pelkosenniemi now study in an old bank. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

Many schoolchildren go to school in temporary premises. Temporary premises are needed when the real school building cannot be used.

For example, in Pelkosenniemi schoolchildren go to school in an old bank. The building does not have, for example, sports facilities.

Temporary premises are usually needed because the indoor air in the school building is unhealthy. There may be, for example, mould in the air.

The state thinks that municipalities must repair school buildings faster. At the moment almost 50,000 schoolchildren are in temporary premises.

## Storm in North America

![&quot;Pray for Wilmington&quot;, reads a protected shop window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10403757)

Open image viewer

People have protected windows before the storm arrives. Photo: Nina Svahn / Yle

A powerful storm is approaching North America. Hurricane Florence is coming from the sea towards the coast. The storm will hit the east coast of the USA on Friday morning, Finnish time.

Residents of the USA have prepared for the storm. They have bought water, food and petrol to store.

Many have left to escape the storm. The authorities are urging over a million people to leave their homes.

## Storm in Asia

![A Filipino man pushes water canisters in a cart.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10402144)

Open image viewer

People buy water to store before the storm hits the Philippines. Photo: Francis R. Malasig / EPA

There is also a strong storm in Asia. A typhoon is now approaching the Philippines. The storm will perhaps hit the Philippines on Saturday.

The storm threatens an area where over a million people live. Thousands of people have been moved to safety out of the storm's path. Schools have also been closed.

## A royal guest

![Princess Mary of Denmark visiting Helsinki on 13.9.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10402325)

Open image viewer

Crown Princess Mary of Denmark in Helsinki. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Finland has received a royal guest from Denmark. Crown Princess Mary of Denmark came to Finland on Thursday. Mary is the wife of Crown Prince Frederik of Denmark.

This year it is 100 years since Denmark recognised Finland's independence. That is why the Crown Princess is visiting Finland now.

On Thursday Crown Princess Mary was in Helsinki, and on Friday she will visit Turku.
