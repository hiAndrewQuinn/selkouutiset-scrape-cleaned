# Monday 25.1.2021 (radio)

Finland's new corona restrictions. Corona infections in Finland. Mass testing point in Tornio. The corona situation in France. Weak ice at sea. The weather.

## Corona restrictions will not be lifted now

![Sanna Marin arriving at the Senate House in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11750652)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin hurried to the government's negotiations about corona restrictions. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Today the Finnish government told about new ways to fight the variants of the coronavirus.

All corona restrictions will stay in force. No restrictions will be lifted now.

The government also said that all restrictions for adults will be made first. Only after that will extra restrictions be given to children.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin also appealed to everyone that now each person should go for a test, even if their symptoms are mild.

## Corona testing at the border

![Aerial photo of the Border Guard, which carries out border checks in Tornio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5464,h_3074,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-765525600c119aaba60/1611404289)

Open image viewer

Traffic is now being closely monitored at the Tornio border crossing point because of corona. Photo: Juuso Stoor / Yle

Today a corona testing point meant for mass testing was opened in Tornio in Northern Finland.

People who cross the border between Finland and Sweden are tested there. The testing point opens in the morning at 7 o'clock and closes in the evening at 19 o'clock.

The Finnish government wants people to reduce crossing the border.

People may travel across the border only if it is really necessary.

## The corona situation is bad in France

![France is tightening restrictions to improve the country's difficult corona situation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4134,h_2325,x_0,y_213/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7362395f9b79af47318/1604025802)

Open image viewer

In the big cities of France there is a curfew in the evening and at night. Photo: EPA/YOAN VALAT

In France researchers say that this week is important for corona.

The researchers say that France must already prepare for a state of emergency. In France there have been over 20,000 new infections every day during the week.

The researchers say that the government must set new strict corona restrictions.

In France's big cities there has been, for example, a curfew in the evening and at night. Even so, corona infections are spreading fast.

## Sea ice cannot be trusted

![Ice on the Gulf of Finland in January 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-765817600e94c907ef2/1611568422)

Open image viewer

There is snow and slush on the sea ice. Under them there is only thin ice. Photo: Katri Mannonen / Yle

It is not worth going onto the sea ice now.

The Coast Guard reminds that the ice is weak across the whole Gulf of Finland area.

Water and snowfalls have weakened the freezing of the sea. Snow acts as an insulator, and the sea water cannot freeze properly.

Proper solid ice must be at least 5 centimetres thick to bear a person's weight.
