# Sunday 7.1.2018 (radio)

Exactly 100 years have passed since the Finnish Civil War. The HIV home test will come into use this year. In Stockholm, one person died in an explosion. The Golden Globe awards are handed out in Hollywood overnight. There was a warm December in the Arctic Ocean.

## The anniversary year of the Civil War

![Wounded men from the Civil War lie in the Red Cross hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10009822)

Wounded men from the Civil War in Tampere. Photo: Vapriikki picture archive

The Finnish Civil War was exactly 100 years ago.

Events to remember the war will be held this year in different parts of Finland. No big main event will be held. At the end of January, the chairs of all parties will meet at a joint event. They want to show that Finns now live in harmony, in democracy and in an intact society.

For example, party leader Anna-Maja Henriksson says that the Civil War was a time of which no one can be proud. Henriksson says that many families have terrible experiences from the Civil War. It is important to remember what happened then and why, Henriksson says.

## The HIV home test

![HIV test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10010442)

Open image viewer

Soon you will be able to do the HIV test at home. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

The HIV home test will come into use this year.

In the home test, a person takes a blood test from their own finger, and the test shows whether the person has HIV or is healthy. In Finland about 4 000 people have HIV. In addition, hundreds of Finns may have HIV, even though they do not know it.

HIV is a disease that can kill. However, with medicines the disease can be slowed down a lot.

## Explosion in Stockholm

![Police near the Vårby Gård metro station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10010408)

Open image viewer

Police near the metro station in Stockholm. Photo: Riikka Uosukainen / Yle

In Stockholm, Sweden, there was an explosion near a metro station during the day, in which one person died.

Outside the metro station, on the street, a man picked up an object from the ground, and the object exploded. The man died in hospital during the day. One woman was also injured in the explosion. The Stockholm police do not suspect that the explosion was a terrorist act.

## The Golden Globe awards are handed out

![The iconic Hollywood sign on Mount Lee hill in Los Angeles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10010184)

Open image viewer

The famous sign in Los Angeles. Photo: Juulia Klemola

In Hollywood, USA, the Golden Globe awards for films and television programmes will be handed out tomorrow night.

The award ceremony is the first since sexual harassment became a talking point in the autumn. The social media \#metoo campaign started in Hollywood and in the film industry, where there is a lot of sexual harassment and abuse.

Female guests plan to come to the Golden Globe party in black clothes. They want to show that they support the brave women who dare to speak publicly about sexual harassment.

## A warm December in the Arctic Ocean

![The area around the North Pole on a map in December 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10007638)

Open image viewer

The area around the North Pole on a map in December 2017. Photo: National Snow and Ice Data Center (NSIDC)

December was much warmer than usual in the Arctic Ocean area.

The Arctic Ocean is a large sea area around the North Pole. Its shores include, for example, Alaska, Siberia, Greenland and Iceland. In the Arctic Ocean area in December, it was as much as 6 degrees warmer than the December average.
