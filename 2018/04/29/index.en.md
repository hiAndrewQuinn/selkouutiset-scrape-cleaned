# Sunday 29.4.2018 (radio)

Finland is world champion in under-18 ice hockey. A terraced house burned down in Tampere. There was a shooting in Lempäälä. Smoking is going down, but new tobacco products are taking its place. The weather on May Day Eve is lovely, but on May Day it will rain in the south.

## World champion Finland

![The under-18 team celebrating in the World Championship final](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10184107)

Open image viewer

The under-18 team celebrates the win. Photo: Finnish Ice Hockey Association

Finland is world champion in under-18 ice hockey. Finland's Pikkuleijonat (Little Lions) won the World Championship on Sunday.

In the final, the last game, Finland played against the USA. Finland scored 3 goals and the USA scored 2 goals. Finland's goal scorers were Rasmus Kupari, Kaapo Kakko and Niklas Nordgren.

The Pikkuleijonat were the only team in the World Championship that won all their games.

The World Championship was played in Chelyabinsk in Russia.

## Fire in Tampere

![A terraced house destroyed in a fire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10183709)

Open image viewer

A terraced house burned down in Tampere. Photo: Terri Niemi / Yle

There has been a big fire in Tampere. The fire burned a terraced house of 6 families. Nobody died in the fire, but 6 people were taken to hospital.

The fire started on Saturday evening in the Pappila area of Tampere. The fire spread quickly. The firefighting went on all night.

It is not yet known what caused the fire.

## Shooting in Lempäälä

![Map of Lempäälä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10183479)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics

There was a shooting at the weekend in Lempäälä, near Tampere.

It started on Saturday when the police tried to stop a car. The car drove away. In the end the police forced the car to stop. Then the driver shot at the police. After that the police shot the driver, who died.

There were also 2 passengers in the car, but they got out of the car before the escape. The police caught the passengers later. Both were men. They were already released on Sunday. The police said that the men have not committed a crime.

## New tobacco products

![A woman smoking an e-cigarette](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9599115)

Open image viewer

The e-cigarette is one of the new tobacco products. Photo: Sebastien Nogier / EPA

Finns smoke less than before. But new tobacco products may slow down this good development.

Many people have started to use e-cigarettes, for example, and there are many snus users among young people.

The tobacco industry invents new products so that people's nicotine addiction continues. This is what an organisation that works against smoking says.

The organisation's goal is now a nicotine-free Finland, not just a smoke-free Finland.

## May Day weather

![A May Day picnic in a market square. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6613851)

Open image viewer

On May Day Finns want to be outdoors, whether the weather is warm or cold. Photo: Markku Lähdetluoma / Yle

The week starts with May Day celebrations. May Day Eve is on Monday and May Day is on Tuesday.

Yle meteorologist Kerttu Kotakorpi says that in Southern Finland it is best to celebrate on May Day Eve. Then the weather will be quite lovely and the temperature may be almost 15 degrees.

On May Day it will rain and in the south it will also be very windy. It will perhaps be dry only in Northern Finland.
