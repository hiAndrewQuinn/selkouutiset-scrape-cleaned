The final of the Eurovision song contest is today, Saturday. The final starts in the evening in Liverpool, England.

Eurovision interests Finns especially much this year, because Finland is one of the favourites in the contest. It is predicted that Finland's representative Käärijä could even win Eurovision.

Käärijä performs the song Cha cha cha at Eurovision. The Plain Language Centre has also made a plain-language version of the song's lyrics. It can be found on the Plain Language Centre's Facebook page.

The Eurovision final starts today, Saturday, at 22:00. You can follow the final on Yle Areena, TV1 and Radio Suomi.

The government negotiations are moving forward. This is how National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo, who leads the government negotiations, assesses it.

The government negotiations have continued for 2 weeks now. The National Coalition, the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats are trying to put together a new government.

Orpo says that in the government negotiations the difficult matters are the economy and immigration. For example, tax questions have not yet been solved.

Kemal Kılıçdaroğlu is trying to beat Recep Tayyip Erdoğan in the Turkish presidential elections. Photo: Yasin Akgul / AFP

Turkey has presidential elections tomorrow, Sunday.

It is possible that Turkey's current president Recep Tayyip Erdoğan will lose the election. Many Turks are tired of Erdoğan taking all the power for himself.

The opposition candidate in the Turkish presidential elections is Kemal Kılıçdaroğlu. He promises Turks more freedom.

Kılıçdaroğlu says that he can be criticised freely. During Erdoğan's time many Turks have ended up in prison, because they have insulted Erdoğan's honour.

## Mother's Day

![Medals of honour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1066,h_600,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-104024063847bcf399fc/1683989751)

Open image viewer

This year 39 mothers receive a medal of honour from the president. Photo: Mannerheims barnskyddsförbund

Mother's Day is celebrated in Finland on Sunday.

President Sauli Niinistö has also awarded medals of honour to mothers this year. In total 39 mothers receive a medal of honour from the president at a celebration held on Mother's Day.

The president rewards mothers who have done important upbringing work. This year the oldest recipient of a medal of honour is 98 years old and the youngest is 40 years old.

Mothers have been rewarded since 1946.

## The weather

![Sunday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1113182645fa559926c9/1683989883)

Mother's Day is celebrated in warm and sunny weather.

The temperature in the south is a little over 20 degrees, and in the north a little under 20 degrees.

At night, however, it is cold across the whole country. The temperature also falls to about 5 degrees at night in the south.

The weather is dry in most of the country. Rain will only come in the middle of next week.
