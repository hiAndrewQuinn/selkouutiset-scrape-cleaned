# Friday 20.11.2020 (radio)

The number of coronavirus infections is growing in Finland. New coronavirus restrictions are coming to the Helsinki area. Because of the new coronavirus restrictions, theatres are being closed. On Friday the International Day of the Rights of the Child was celebrated. In southern Finland the first snow has fallen.

The number of coronavirus infections is growing in Finland.

On Friday, 461 new coronavirus infections were reported. About 20,700 coronavirus infections have been found in Finland in total so far.

On Friday, 1 new coronavirus death was also reported. 375 people have died from coronavirus in Finland in total so far.

About 100 coronavirus patients are now in hospital care in Finland.

## Coronavirus restrictions in the Helsinki area

![Helsinki Mayor Jan Vapaavuori at the press conference of the capital region coronavirus coordination group in Helsinki on 20 November.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11657891)

Open image viewer

Helsinki's Jan Vapaavuori and the leaders of other cities spoke about the coronavirus restrictions on Friday. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

New coronavirus restrictions are coming to the Helsinki area.

Restrictions are needed, because many more coronavirus infections have come. The city leaders spoke about the restrictions on Friday.

The cities decide that events with more than 20 participants may not be held indoors.

In addition, team sports for adults where safe distances cannot be kept will not be held in the city's indoor sports facilities.

Both of these rules start in the Helsinki area on Monday and last 3 weeks.

In addition, there is a recommendation to wear masks in upper secondary schools. So upper secondary school pupils should wear a mask at school. The upper secondary school pupils get the masks from the city.

## Theatres are closing

![The Finnish National Theatre in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9646307)

Open image viewer

The Finnish National Theatre is in Helsinki. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Theatres are being closed because of the new coronavirus restrictions.

The Finnish National Theatre and the City Theatre of Helsinki will be closed for 3 weeks from Monday. The Finnish National Opera is also closed.

The new coronavirus restrictions in the Helsinki area say that there may be at most 20 people at public events. So it is not worth keeping the big theatre buildings open.

## First snow in southern Finland

![Aura River](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11657735)

Open image viewer

The first snow of Turku in 2020. Photo: Kalle Mäkelä / Yle

In southern Finland the first snow of winter has fallen.

First snow officially falls when at 8 in the morning there is at least 1 centimetre of snow.

That was the case on Friday morning, for example, in Espoo and Turku.

Saturday is a frosty day in almost all of Finland. At first it is also dry, that is, it does not rain.

On Saturday afternoon a rain area arrives from the west and moves over Finland to the east during the weekend. In the south it rains, elsewhere it snows.

Sunday is drier and warmer than Saturday.

The south wind is strong at the weekend.
