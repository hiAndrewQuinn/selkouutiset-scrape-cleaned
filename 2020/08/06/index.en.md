# Thursday 6.8.2020 (radio)

Summary: The SDP is still the most popular party. 20 new corona infections have been found in Finland. The government has added the Netherlands, Belgium and Andorra to the list of risk countries. The weekend is hot.

TV Clear News has a summer break and it will continue on 17.8.
Radio Clear News is made every day, also in the summer.

## Party support

![Party support in July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11480030)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

The SDP is still Finland's most popular party. The support for the SDP is now 23.4 per cent. A Yle survey says that support for the SDP has risen by 1.5 percentage points in a month.

Since the parliamentary election of spring 2019, support for the SDP has risen by 5.7 percentage points. The SDP is the party of Prime Minister Sanna Marin.

The Finns Party is the second largest party. The support for the Finns Party is now 18.9 per cent. Support has risen a little. The Finns Party is an opposition party.

Support for the National Coalition Party has fallen a little. The support for the opposition party, the National Coalition Party, is 17.3 per cent.

## New corona infections

![A man walks out of a tram with a face mask on.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5213,h_2932,x_0,y_497/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7051235f2971a36d209/1597287388)

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, may give a face mask recommendation next week. Photo: Arttu Timonen / Yle

The authorities may give a recommendation next week that it is a good idea to use a face mask in public places. The purpose of the face mask is that the coronavirus does not spread.

20 new corona infections have been found in Finland. More infections have been found this week than last week.

For example, travellers have brought the coronavirus to Finland from abroad. Travellers have often got the infection from either Sweden or the Balkan countries.

## Travel restrictions

![ Air passengers at Helsinki-Vantaa airport on 13 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11473399)

Open image viewer

People returning to Finland from so-called risk countries are recommended to self-quarantine for two weeks. During that time they must avoid meeting other people. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The government has decided to restrict entry into Finland from 3 countries, because there are more corona infections in them than before. The Netherlands, Belgium and Andorra have been added to the list of risk countries. The restriction starts on Monday.

People coming to Finland from risk countries are recommended to quarantine. During that time they must avoid meetings with other people. Sweden and Spain, for example, are also on the list of risk countries.

There has been more of the coronavirus than before in Germany and Denmark. However, the government has not added Germany and Denmark to the list of risk countries.

## The weather warms up

![Highest temperature weather graphic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11481573)

Open image viewer

Photo: Anniina Valtonen / Yle

At the weekend there may be a heatwave in almost the whole country. A heatwave is when the temperature is over 25 degrees.

The warmest day will probably be Saturday. On that day the temperature may be over 27 degrees in Western Finland. On Sunday the temperature may rise to 27 degrees in Eastern Finland.

Next week the weather will cool down, but there will not be much rain.
