## Vaccination requirement in social and health care

A so-called vaccination requirement has come into force in the social and health sector.

This means, for example, that nurses must have a corona vaccination if they care for risk groups. Risk groups include, for example, the elderly and the seriously ill.

Corona disease is especially dangerous for risk groups. That is why risk groups are to be protected from corona infection.

A social and health care employee will not be dismissed if they do not take a corona vaccination. However, they may be moved to other tasks.

The vaccination requirement does not apply to employees who have had corona disease within the last 6 months.

Russia has sent a letter to all OSCE member countries, including Finland. The OSCE is the Organisation for Security and Co-operation in Europe.

In practice, the question is that Russia wants to prevent the military alliance NATO from growing. Russia does not want NATO to take in new member countries.

Finland's Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says that every independent country decides for itself on its own matters, including security matters.

The new Speaker of Parliament is Matti Vanhanen. The Speaker leads the work of Parliament, that is, Finland's parliament.

Matti Vanhanen was chosen as Speaker of Parliament on Tuesday. At that time the Christmas break of Parliament ended and MPs returned to work.

Matti Vanhanen is an experienced politician. He has previously been, for example, Prime Minister of Finland. Vanhanen's party is the Centre Party.

In addition, 2 deputy Speakers were elected in Parliament.

## Students' renovation project

![Building student Atso Laitinen working on a block of flats renovation in Kotka.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_400/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90845961f7963d3b895/1643617529)

Students think that you learn best at a real construction site. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

A new kind of project is under way at the South Kymenlaakso vocational college.

Vocational college students are renovating a whole block of flats in Kotka.

The building is 50 years old. A plumbing renovation is being done, the building technology is being renewed, and all the interior surfaces are being repaired.

Dozens of students from 5 different fields are involved in the work. Teachers help all the time.

2 years have been reserved for the renovation, that is, one year more than usual for a renovation of this size.

The students are satisfied with the project. They say that you learn best at a real construction site.
