# Sunday 25.2.2018 (radio)

Finland's Krista Pärmäkoski won a silver medal at the Olympic Games. An agreement was reached in the tourism and restaurant sector. The UN demands a ceasefire in Syria. The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns of severe frost.

## Pärmäkoski won a silver medal

![Krista Pärmäkoski](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10091763)

Skier Krista Pärmäkoski won a silver medal. Photo: AOP

The Olympic Games have ended. The Games were in Pyeongchang, South Korea. The closing ceremony was held on Sunday.

The Games ended in a happy mood for Finns. Finland won a silver medal on the last day of competition.

The silver medal came in the women's 30-kilometre ski race. Finland's Krista Pärmäkoski was second in the race. The winner of the race was Norway's Marit Björgen.

Krista Pärmäkoski did well at the Olympic Games. She won a silver medal and 2 bronze medals. Pärmäkoski won half of all Finland's medals.

Finland won a total of 6 medals at the Pyeongchang Olympics. Of Finland's medals, 1 was gold, 1 silver and 4 bronze.

The gold medal winner was Iivo Niskanen. Niskanen won the men's 50-kilometre ski race on Saturday.

## An agreement was completed for the tourism and restaurant sector

![A waiter behind a restaurant counter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10010556)

In the tourism and restaurant sector, pay and other terms of employment have been agreed. The employers' and employees' organisations finished the agreement on Sunday.

The organisations still have to officially approve the new agreement. After that, they will tell more about the agreement.

## A ceasefire is being attempted in Syria

![A man carries an injured child.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10090283)

Open image viewer

The war in Syria has gone on for a long time, and people need help. Photo: Mohammed Badra / AOP

The UN, or United Nations, demands a ceasefire in Syria. In the UN's opinion, a 30-day ceasefire is needed in Syria. During a ceasefire, weapons are not used.

The UN says that a ceasefire is needed because people in the war zone must get help. If the weapons go quiet, things like medicine can be taken to people.

In Syria, the government and the rebels have fought against each other for many years. For a week now, the Syrian government has bombed an area that is near Syria's capital Damascus. Help is needed in the area.

The UN decided to demand a ceasefire only after a long dispute. The USA says that making the decision took a long time because of Russia. Russia supports the Syrian government.

## The Finnish Meteorological Institute gave a frost warning

![People outdoors in front of Merisatama in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10085169)

Open image viewer

Being outdoors in the frost can cause health problems, for example for people who have asthma. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The weather is really cold. The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns of severe frost in southern Finland and central Finland.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns that the frost can cause health problems, for example for old and sick people. The coldest day is Tuesday.

Sick people should stay indoors from Monday evening until Wednesday morning. That may be the coldest time of the winter in southern Finland. It may be more than 20 degrees below zero.
