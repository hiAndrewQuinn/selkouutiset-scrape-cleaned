# Saturday 22.10.2016 (radio)

There has been a helicopter accident in Russia. The police say that it is illegal to scare children in a clown suit. Dylan does not comment on the Nobel Prize. Part of the internet went down on Friday. Whole grain is healthy.

## Russian air accident

![A map of Russia showing Moscow and Novy Urengoy. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9246287)

Open image viewer

The accident happened near the city of Urengoy. Photo: Google / Yle News Graphics

There has been an air accident in Russia. 19 people died when a helicopter fell to the ground in Siberia. 3 people survived the accident.

The accident site is about 80 kilometres from the city of Urengoy.

The cause of the accident was perhaps bad weather or a technical problem. It is also possible that the helicopter did not follow the safety regulations. The Russian authorities are investigating the accident.

## Illegal clowns

![Clown](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9242150)

Open image viewer

Clowns must not scare people. Photo: AOP

The police say that it is illegal to scare children in a clown suit.

Police inspector Ari Järvenpää reminds people that scaring can be an illegal threat. You can get up to a 2-year prison sentence for it.

Clowns first started scaring people in the USA, and from there the habit spread to Europe and Finland.

The police believe that the people doing the scaring are often young.

## Dylan's Nobel Prize

![Bob Dylan](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9228605)

Open image viewer

Bob Dylan. Photo: Luca Castellani / EPA

Musician Bob Dylan has not yet said anything about receiving the Nobel Prize in Literature. The Swedish Academy announced already more than a week ago that it is giving the Nobel Prize to Dylan.

Dylan has not even answered the Swedish Academy's phone calls.

In Sweden nobody knows whether Dylan will come to the Nobel ceremony. The ceremony is in Stockholm on 10 December. At the ceremony the Nobel Prizes in different fields are given out.

Dylan is the first songwriter who has won the Nobel Prize in Literature. The Swedish Academy says that Dylan's songs have changed poetry.

## Internet disruption

![Stage sets of an international cyber security conference in Potsdam, Germany.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9096346)

Open image viewer

Network traffic did not work in part of the internet on Friday. Photo: Ralf Hirschberger / EPA

Part of the internet went down on Friday evening. People could not get into, for example, Spotify, Netflix and Twitter.

The reason for the situation was an attack against one network service company in the USA. The company's customers suffered from the disruption around the world.

On Saturday the internet has already worked normally.

## Healthy whole grain

![Rye breads in a basket](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8614739)

Open image viewer

Dark bread is made from whole grain. Photo: Heikki Rönty / Yle

Whole grain is healthy. A new American study says that whole grain lowers blood pressure.

In the study, people's blood pressure went down when they ate whole grain for 2 months. This means that the risk of heart disease fell by about 30 percent.

Whole grain contains all the parts of the grain. That is why it has a lot of vitamins and fibre.

Grain is not as healthy if the outer parts of the grain are removed. For example, white bread is not as healthy as dark whole grain bread.
