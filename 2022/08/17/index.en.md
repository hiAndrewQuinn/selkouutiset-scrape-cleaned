## Uniper's loss

![A huge cooling tower of a power plant photographed across the canal against a clear sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12580218)

Open image viewer

Uniper's coal power plant in Datteln. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

Uniper, the German subsidiary of the Finnish state-owned company Fortum, has made a large loss. The loss in the first part of the year is over 12 billion euros.

Uniper makes a loss of an estimated 100 million euros every day.

Uniper says that it has become a pawn in Russia's war of aggression. Gas deliveries to Europe have decreased, and prices have risen sky high.

Uniper's losses also affect Finland. Professor Timo Rothovius says that every Finn owns half of Fortum through the state. The energy company Fortum also has to pay for the losses.

## Jobs

![Two people working.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4875,h_2742,x_125,y_594/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83230560f03120d1471/1626417211)

There are more open jobs than last year. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

There are clearly more open jobs than last year, says Statistics Finland.

In the second quarter of the year, in the spring, there were over 72,000 open jobs. A year earlier there were a little over 50,000 open jobs.

Open jobs increased most in the Helsinki and Uusimaa region. Workers were sought especially much in health services and social services.

## Solar energy

![Solar panel on the roof of an apartment block.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10378060)

Open image viewer

Energy grants can be received for solar panels. In many areas more new solar power systems have been installed this year than in the whole of last year. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

The rise in the price of electricity is speeding up the purchase of solar panels.

Solar electricity can get an energy grant from ARA. ARA is the Housing Finance and Development Centre of Finland.

ARA grants subsidies to house owners and housing companies for repairs that improve energy efficiency. The condition is that the energy efficiency of the residential building improves enough.

## Electric cars

![Electric cars charging.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_513/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7174235f5a29c52774c/1624768444)

Open image viewer

Just one percent of Finland's car fleet is fully electric cars. Photo: Mira Bäck / Yle

There will soon be more electric cars than cars with combustion engines. Researchers estimate that in 3 years there will already be more new electric cars than ordinary cars.

In Finland there may be over 400,000 chargeable hybrids and over 350,000 fully electric cars in 2030.

The EU's goal is that from 2035 onwards all cars sold in Europe are fully electric cars.

## School peace

![Classroom with desks in rows.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_567/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99254962f3a74058d71/1660135345)

Open image viewer

Mannerheim League for Child Welfare's school peace has been declared at the start of the autumn term since 1990. Photo: Kirsi-Marja Lönnblad / Yle

School peace has again been declared for the current school year.

Children and young people chose friendship skills as this year's theme. The choice is a reminder that others must be taken into account and everyone must be included.

The school peace was declared in Joensuu. At the event a video greeting from the President of the Republic, Sauli Niinistö, was also heard.

School peace has been declared since 1990.
