## Shooting

Two Swedes have died in a shooting in Belgium. The shooting happened on Monday.

The dead were Swedish football fans. They were men about 60 and 70 years old. They were shot in a taxi a few kilometres from Brussels' football stadium.

The match between Sweden and Belgium was supposed to be played at the stadium. However, the match was cancelled because of the shooting.

The Belgian police caught the shooter today, Tuesday. The shooter died after the police shot him.

The police say that the shooter wanted to kill specifically Swedes. The shooter told about the act on a video after the shooting.

The shooter said that he is a supporter of the terrorist organisation Isis. In the video the shooter also said that he wanted to take revenge on behalf of Muslims.

In Sweden there have been cases where the holy book of Muslims, the Quran, has been burned. That is why many Muslims have become angry at Sweden.

Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson says that the shooting in Belgium was a terrorist act against Swedes.

## Ahtisaari's condolence book

You can write words of condolence in Ahtisaari's condolence book at the Presidential Palace until Friday. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Finns are honouring the memory of President Martti Ahtisaari. A condolence book for President Ahtisaari was opened at the Presidential Palace on Tuesday.

People can write words of condolence in it until Friday. The first to write were President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio.

Later the condolence messages will be printed in a book, which will be given to Ahtisaari's family.

President Ahtisaari died on Monday. He was 86 years old. Ahtisaari was President of Finland from 1994 to 2000.

## Polish parliamentary election

![Donald Tusk greeting his supporters.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4198,h_2361,x_0,y_59/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1187133652d666f8a540/1697474361)

In Poland the opposition has won the parliamentary election. The opposition parties got about 54 percent of the votes.

The parliamentary election in Poland was held on Sunday. The final result of the election was completed today, Tuesday.

It is estimated that the opposition's victory will strengthen democracy in Poland.

The opposition is also more positive about the European Union than the Law and Justice party, which has governed Poland since 2015.
