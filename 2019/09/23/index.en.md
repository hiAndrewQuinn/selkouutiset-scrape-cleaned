# Monday 23.9.2019 (radio)

Thomas Cook has gone bankrupt. Lapland's tourism is suffering from the collapse of Thomas Cook. The UN climate meeting has started in New York. At least 7 children have died in a school accident in Kenya. Monday is the autumn equinox.

## Thomas Cook goes bankrupt

![Passengers in departure terminal 2 at Helsinki-Vantaa airport early in the morning on 23 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10983821)

The British travel company Thomas Cook has gone bankrupt. All of the company's flights and holiday trips have been cancelled because of the bankruptcy.

About 600,000 people are currently on Thomas Cook trips in different parts of the world.

The travel agency Tjäreborg is part of the Thomas Cook group. Tjäreborg is one of Finland's biggest travel agencies. Almost 3,000 Finns are on Tjäreborg trips in different countries.

All of Tjäreborg's flights from Helsinki to holiday destinations on Monday have been cancelled. Tjäreborg hopes that the flights for the coming days can be flown.

Tjäreborg does not yet know whether it can continue its operations.

Thomas Cook, which has gone bankrupt, is the oldest travel organiser in the world.

## Thomas Cook and Lapland

![Sunset on Kätkätunturi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6980329)

Open image viewer

British tourists have been the biggest single group at Lapland's tourist destinations. Photo: Sakari Kekki

Thomas Cook's bankruptcy also affects tourism in Lapland. Thomas Cook has organised many trips for foreign visitors to Lapland.

Many tourists in Lapland come from Britain. Of the British tourists, an estimated 10 percent have come to Lapland on trips organised by Thomas Cook.

In recent years, many tourists have also come to Lapland from Asia and France, as well as from Britain.

## UN climate meeting

![A skyscraper with traffic signs in front of it in New York.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10983716)

Open image viewer

Heads of state from several dozen countries will take part in the climate meeting held at the UN headquarters in New York. Photo: Nina Svahn

The UN climate meeting has started in New York.

Leaders of states from dozens of countries are taking part in the meeting. They are expected to decide how to slow down climate change.

From Finland, President Sauli Niinistö is taking part in the meeting. US President Donald Trump is not taking part in the climate meeting.

## Accident in Kenya

![Map of Kenya](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10597349)

Open image viewer

The accident happened in the capital of Kenya, Nairobi. Photo: Yle graphics

A serious accident has happened at a school in Kenya in Africa.

At least 7 children died and several were injured when a classroom collapsed in the capital of Kenya, Nairobi. Almost 60 schoolchildren were taken to hospital.

The accident happened on Monday morning in the poor Dagoretti area.

## Autumn equinox

![Morning sun in autumn colours at Misijoki in Rovaniemi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5424786)

Open image viewer

Morning sun in Rovaniemi in autumn colours. Photo: Paula Laamanen

Today, Monday, is the autumn equinox. The autumn equinox is the moment when day and night are roughly equal in length everywhere on Earth.

Later in the autumn, the sun gradually moves from the northern hemisphere to the southern hemisphere. In about 2 months, the length of the day in Helsinki will be less than 7 hours, and in Rovaniemi less than 5 hours.
