# Wednesday 22.3.2023 (radio)

Advance voting. Number of women candidates. Multilingual election debates. The weather.

## Advance voting

![Election official and a female voter at an advance voting place.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689966)

Open image viewer

There are almost 1000 advance voting places in Finland. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Advance voting for the parliamentary election has started. Advance voting continues for a week, that is, until next Tuesday.

Advance voting places are, for example, in libraries and shopping centres. At an advance voting place, the voter must have an identity document with them.

Advance voting is popular nowadays. For example, in the last parliamentary election 1.5 million people voted in advance.

It is predicted that many people will also vote in advance this time, that is, before the actual election day. The actual election day is Sunday 2 April.

## Number of women candidates

![A row of election posters outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689965)

Open image viewer

Among the candidates there are almost as many women as men. Photo: Mira Bäck / Yle

There is a record number of women candidates in the parliamentary election now. About 43 percent of candidates are women. This is what Statistics Finland says.

Some parties have more women candidates than men candidates. The most women candidates are in the Greens. More than 60 percent of the Greens' candidates are women.

The SDP and the Left Alliance also have more women candidates than men candidates.

## Multilingual election debates

The parliamentary election is soon, and there are many election debates on radio and television.

Yle is also making election debates in foreign languages, for the first time also in Arabic and Somali.

Esraa Ismaeel hosts the Arabic-language debate, and Wali Hashi hosts the Somali-language debate. They say that multilingual election debates are important.

![Face of a woman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689962)

Esraa Ismaeel. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Esraa Ismaeel says that people must be able to talk about their own matters in their own language. Democracy means that everyone can take part.

![A man puts glasses on his head.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689961)

Wali Hashi. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Wali Hashi reminds us that in elections immigrants are always talked about. That is why it is important that immigrants themselves can also take part in the debate.

Yle also makes the election debates in Russian and English, as before.

The foreign-language election debates were recorded last week, and they will be shown on TV1 and on Yle Areena next week.

## The weather

On Thursday rain showers move across southern Finland and snow showers across central Finland.

In the afternoon it will still snow in the east. In the north it does not snow, and the sun shines there too.

The temperature in the south is above zero. In the north it is freezing on Thursday.

On Friday the weather in the south is cloudy, but only a little rain will fall. In the north the weather is still sunny.

On Friday too the temperature in the south is above zero, and in the north it is freezing.
