# Saturday 2.2.2019 (radio)

Taxis were inspected in Helsinki. The flu epidemic is not very bad this winter. A meteorite may have fallen in Cuba. The Finnish winter swimming championships are being held in Turku. It will snow on Sunday.

## Taxis

![Taxis at a stop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10588125)

Open image viewer

The taxi law changed last year. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

On Friday, more than 100 taxis were inspected in Helsinki. The inspectors say that about 70 percent of the taxis had small faults.

The most common fault was that the passenger could not see who holds the taxi licence. It was also often difficult for the passenger to see the prices and the driver's name. 3 drivers did not have a driving licence.

Transport Minister Anne Berner says that the faults must be corrected right away. In the minister's view, it is good that the taxis have handled road safety matters well.

The taxi law changed last summer. Now taxis may set their own prices, for example.

## Flu

![A man with a cold blows his nose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10395698)

Open image viewer

There have not been very many flu patients this winter. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The flu epidemic is not as bad this winter as in previous years. The National Institute for Health and Welfare reports this.

Flu is a worse illness than an ordinary cold. That is why, for example, the elderly and young children are given a vaccine against flu.

This winter the flu epidemic began later than usual. The epidemic has also spread slowly.

## Meteorite

![Pieces of meteorite on a cloth.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10627619)

Open image viewer

These pieces of meteorite were found in Cuba. Photo: Cubadebate / EPA

A meteorite may have fallen in South America, in Cuba. Smoke and light were seen in the sky over Cuba, and in addition a loud sound was heard.

The cause may be a meteorite, that is, an object that fell to the surface of the Earth from space. In the Cuban city of Viñales, people found small black stones that fell from the sky. They may be pieces of a meteorite.

A video of the incident was published on Twitter.

## Winter swimming

![Anna Savolainen and Titta Rapila splash in the Apparas in Hämeenlinna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10578583)

Open image viewer

Winter swimming, that is, swimming in an ice hole, is a hobby for many Finns. Photo: Mika Halme / Yle

A big winter swimming event is now being held in Turku this weekend. Swimmers compete to see who is the Finnish champion of winter swimming in the different events.

The competitors swim in the Aura River. A large hole, that is an ice hole, has been made in the ice of the Aura River, and the competitors swim in it. The competitors swim distances of 25 metres and 50 metres.

On Sunday afternoon there is also a public swim. It is open to everyone.

## Snowfall

![Snowy cars left behind a snow bank after the snowplough.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10620715)

Open image viewer

It is snowing more again. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

It will snow on Sunday. The snowfall starts during the night between Saturday and Sunday.

In Southern Finland and Central Finland as much as 20 centimetres of snow may fall. In the south it may also rain.

Driving conditions on the roads are poor because of the snowfall. The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns that driving conditions on Sunday may even be dangerous.
