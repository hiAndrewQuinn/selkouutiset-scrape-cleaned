# Tuesday 7.9.2021 (radio)

Government parties are negotiating the state budget. A court has given a verdict to a man who assaulted a former prime minister. Construction waste recycling is to be improved. The photo exhibition Meistä - About Us tells about people who belong to many minorities. The weather is rainy on Wednesday.

## Budget negotiations

The budget tells how the state spends money. Photo: Yle

Government parties are negotiating the state budget. The budget tells how the state collects and uses money.

The negotiations have continued all day Tuesday. It is estimated that the government parties already agree on many of the state's money matters.

However, the government parties still have to agree on how Finland will reduce climate emissions. Measures against climate change are an important part of the state's spending.

The government parties' negotiations continue on Wednesday.

## Fines for assailant

![Juha Sipilä in the district court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_381/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8506636137115bd90df/1630998956)

Open image viewer

Juha Sipilä is a former prime minister. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The court has given a verdict to a man who assaulted MP Juha Sipilä.

The man hit Sipilä, who is a former prime minister, with his fists. This happened last winter near the Parliament House in Helsinki.

In the court's view, violence against decision-makers cannot be accepted. The court ordered the man to pay a large fine.

## Recycling construction waste

![Mixed construction waste on a pallet](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6253785dfcac88958e2/1576841903)

Open image viewer

Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

Recycling construction waste is to be improved.

This autumn a new kind of construction waste recycling plant will start in Vantaa. At the plant, construction waste is not burned or crushed.

The recycling plant's robotic arms sort the construction waste so that the different materials can be recycled. The robotic arms work with the help of artificial intelligence, and they are more precise than people.

The plant is now being tested, and at the latest in November it will work at full capacity. Then the plant can handle 30,000 kilograms of construction waste an hour.

## Meistä - About Us

![Aleksandra Lemke](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11934029)

Open image viewer

An exhibition has been put together from Aleksandra Lemke's photographs. Photo: Linda Tammela / Yle

The photo exhibition *Meistä - About Us* is touring Finland. Now the exhibition is in Jyväskylä, at the multicultural centre Gloria.

The exhibition has photos taken by Aleksandra Lemke. Lemke's photos tell about people who belong to many minorities.

The people in the photos are, for example, gay people and immigrants. They have faced many kinds of discrimination, but in the photos they are strong and proud of who they are.

The photo exhibition will also be shown in Tampere and Helsinki.
