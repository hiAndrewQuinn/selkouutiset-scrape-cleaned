# Thursday 9.4.2020 (radio)

Yle's party support poll. The coronavirus situation in Finland. Intensive policing of the Uusimaa border for Easter. Entrance exams will be held remotely. Valamo Monastery has lost its income.

## Yle's party support poll

![March party support measurement.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11299665)

The popularity of the SDP has grown. Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

Yle has once again asked Finns which party they support.

The most support goes to the Finns Party. 20% of respondents support the Finns Party. The second most support goes to the SDP. Almost 20% of respondents support the SDP. The third most support goes to the National Coalition Party.

The SDP's support has grown bigger than the National Coalition's. The Finns Party and the National Coalition are opposition parties, that is, they are not in the government.

The support figures for all parties are at yle.fi.

## The coronavirus situation in Finland

![Sign of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11269515)

Open image viewer

THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare reported on Finland's coronavirus situation on Thursday. 42 people have now died in Finland from the disease caused by the coronavirus. The number of deaths has risen by two since Wednesday.

Because of the coronavirus, there are 244 patients in hospital, of whom 82 are in intensive care.

There are more than 2,600 confirmed coronavirus infections in Finland.

## The police are monitoring the Uusimaa border at Easter

![Police checking drivers at the Uusimaa border.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11300059)

Open image viewer

The police are monitoring the Uusimaa border. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The police have started intensive monitoring of the Uusimaa border because of Easter.

Normally, many people go to their summer cottages at Easter. Now there are movement restrictions in Finland, and you may cross the Uusimaa border only for an essential reason, for example to go to work. A leisure trip, for example to a summer cottage, is not an essential reason.

The police are monitoring traffic at the Uusimaa border at about 40 checkpoints. The police are also monitoring train passengers.

## Entrance exams will be held remotely

![University entrance exam.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5407845)

Open image viewer

Young people taking a university entrance exam. Photo: Yle

Entrance exams for universities and universities of applied sciences will be held as remote exams this spring.

Because of the coronavirus situation, universities and higher education institutions cannot hold traditional entrance exams.

In a remote exam, applicants do tasks, for example, at home on a computer. Universities may also select students based on their certificates, for example.

## Valamo Monastery has run into financial difficulties

![The main church of Valamo Monastery in Heinävesi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11091402)

Open image viewer

The main church of Valamo Monastery in Heinävesi. Photo: Marja-Liisa Kämppi / Yle

Valamo Monastery in Heinävesi has run into financial difficulties because of the coronavirus situation.

Normally many people visit Valamo Monastery at Easter, which is the most important feast of the Orthodox Church.

Now the restaurant, hotel, souvenir shop and college of Valamo Monastery are closed, and the monastery gets no income. The money of Valamo Monastery will be enough if it is allowed to open its doors in the summer.
