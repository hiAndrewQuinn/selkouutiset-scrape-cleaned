# Saturday 18.8.2018 (radio)

Terrorism tips are now acted on faster than before. A march was held in Turku on the anniversary of the stabbing. A citizens' initiative on female genital mutilation is moving to Parliament. Italy has a national day of mourning. Former UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan has died.

## Police and terrorism tips

![A sea of candles and flowers reminds of the victims of the Turku stabbing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9790535)

Flowers and candles at Turku market square after last August's stabbing. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

The police have changed how they handle terrorism tips after the Turku stabbing.

Today it is a year since the stabbing. A Moroccan man attacked people with a knife in the Turku market square last August. Two people died and 8 were injured.

A tip about the stabber had been given to the police even before the attack. The tip suspected that the man had been radicalised. For example, he talked a lot about the Isis terrorist organisation.

The local police sent the tip to the Security Police. The Security Police recorded the information, but then the matter was left there.

Now the way the police work has been changed. The local police also investigate tips. Tips are also acted on faster than before.

## Demonstrations in Turku

![Turku without Nazis demonstration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10359349)

Open image viewer

More than a thousand people marched in a demonstration against neo-Nazis. Photo: Pasi Peiponen / Yle

There have been demonstrations in Turku on the anniversary of the stabbing.

More than 300 people took part in the demonstration by the neo-Nazi organisation Nordic Resistance Movement.

More than a thousand people took part in the counter-demonstration "Turku without Nazis".

There were no big clashes, but the police arrested 10 people.

## Citizens' initiative on female genital mutilation

![blade](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9845673)

Open image viewer

The citizens' initiative wants female genital mutilation to be banned by its own law. Photo: AOP

50 000 people have signed the citizens' initiative, which wants to ban the female genital mutilation of girls. There are now so many signatures that the citizens' initiative is moving on to Parliament.

Female genital mutilation of girls, that is circumcision, is already against the Finnish Criminal Code.

However, the initiators of the citizens' initiative want the mutilation to be banned by its own law, which would also cover mutilations carried out abroad.

## Italy has a day of mourning

![View of the Morandi bridge after the collapse.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10354888)

Open image viewer

The Morandi bridge in Genoa the day after the collapse. Photo: Luca Zennaro / EPA

In Italy, rescue workers have found more fatalities from the Genoa bridge collapse.

Italian media report that a car with a three-person family was found in the rubble of the bridge. The authorities have not yet confirmed the information.

If the information is correct, at least 41 people died in the bridge collapse.

Italy has held a national day of mourning today and buried the dead. .

## Kofi Annan has died

![Kofi Annan](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10359498)

Open image viewer

Kofi Annan visited Helsinki in May 2017. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Former UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan has died. He was 80 years old.

Kofi Annan was from Ghana in West Africa.

He was UN Secretary-General from 1997 to 2006.

Annan was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 2001.
