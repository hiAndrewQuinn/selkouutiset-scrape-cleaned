# Tuesday 24.5.2022 (radio)

Talks with Turkey. The war in Ukraine. Fazer's confectionery factory. Food prices. Ilkka Suominen. The weather.

## Talks with Turkey

![Recep Tayyip Erdogan with his hands in the air.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2993,h_1683,x_0,y_204/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-959397628c9d8183aec/1653382576)

Turkey's President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. Photo: AOP

Finland and Sweden are sending negotiators to Turkey. The negotiators travel to Turkey on Wednesday.

The theme of the talks is the military alliance NATO. Finland and Sweden have applied for membership of NATO, but Turkey does not accept it.

Turkey thinks that Finland and Sweden protect Kurdish terrorists. Finland and Sweden must settle their disputes with Turkey, so that the handling of their NATO applications can get going.

## The war in Ukraine

![Two people at a cemetery, with many wooden crosses.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5088,h_2853,x_0,y_315/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-959240628c6dfe296c9/1671721380)

Open image viewer

Photo: Alexander Ermochenko / Reuters

The war in Ukraine has lasted 3 months. Russia attacked Ukraine on 24 February.

Ukrainians have suffered a lot from the war. Russia has bombed cities and killed ordinary Ukrainians.

More than 6,5 million Ukrainians have fled abroad because of the war. Ukrainian refugees have also come to Finland.

Already 20 000 Ukrainians have temporary protection in Finland. Temporary protection means that Ukrainians may, for example, work and study in Finland.

## Fazer's confectionery factory

![Fazer candy bags on a candy shelf in a shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_2/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-959586628cbec36e0c4/1653391115)

Photo: Petri Niemi / Yle

Fazer intends to build a confectionery factory in Lahti. If the plan comes true, the making of sweets at Fazer's factories in Vantaa and Lappeenranta will end.

The city of Lahti is pleased with Fazer's plan. Fazer's confectionery factory may bring many new jobs to Lahti.

## Food prices

![Customers and sellers at the meat counter of a grocery store.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2861,h_1610,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-56395575959d096a45/1465473657)

Open image viewer

The price of meat may rise towards the end of the year. Photo: Mika Kanerva

Food is getting more expensive this year. The Natural Resources Institute Finland forecasts that the price of food will rise by 10 per cent from last year.

For example, the prices of coffee and fish have already risen a lot. The Natural Resources Institute Finland estimates that grain, meat and dairy products will get more expensive next.

One reason for the price rises is Russia's war in Ukraine. Russia and Ukraine are important producers of grain and vegetable oils, but because of the war food transports do not work.

## Ilkka Suominen

![Ilkka Suominen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8951827)

Open image viewer

Ilkka Suominen. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Ilkka Suominen has died. Suominen was 83 years old.

Ilkka Suominen was the chair of the National Coalition Party in the 1980s. He also worked as a minister.

Suominen was also elected to the Finnish Parliament and later to the European Parliament.

Parliament held a moment of silence on Tuesday in memory of Ilkka Suominen.
