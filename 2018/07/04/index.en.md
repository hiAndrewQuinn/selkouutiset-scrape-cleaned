# Wednesday 4.7.2018 (radio)

Finland is tightening border control because of the meeting of the presidents of the USA and Russia. There is a shortage of workers and skilled people. A foreign worker has to wait a long time for a work permit. Javelin thrower Tero Pitkämäki will not take part in the European Championships.

## Border control

![Russian tourists queuing for passport control at the Nuijamaa border station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9975757)

Finland is tightening border control because of the meeting of the presidents of the USA and Russia. President Donald Trump and President Vladimir Putin will meet in Finland on 16 July.

During the meeting, Finland may also do border checks inside the Schengen area. Usually there are no border checks between Schengen countries. Of Finland's neighbours, Estonia, Sweden and Norway belong to the Schengen area.

Finland wants to make sure that people who threaten the safety of Trump and Putin cannot get into the country. That is why Finland is tightening border control.

## Shortage of workers

![A new part of the city is rising on top of Pasila's old railway yard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10191549)

The construction industry needs a lot of workers now. Photo: Vesa Marttinen / Yle

Many Finnish companies are looking for workers. 45 percent of employers say that it is hard for them to fill open jobs.

There are too few applicants, for example, for cleaning and driving jobs. There is also a shortage of workers in health care and construction.

It is hard for employers to find skilled people. For example, in information work and technology there are too few people who can do the new and difficult tasks in the field.

## Work permit

![Bangladeshi software developer Manzurul Haque is thinking about leaving Finland. He has had to wait almost a year for a work permit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10287201)

A foreign worker has to wait a long time for a work permit. A worker needs a work permit if they come to Finland from outside Europe.

Finnish law says that a worker must get a decision on a work permit within 4 months. But for example in Uusimaa in southern Finland, a foreign worker has to wait as long as a year for a work permit.

The Uusimaa TE Office says that the office has too few staff. Only a little over 10 employees handle about 2000 work permit applications. That is why the work takes a long time.

## Pitkämäki

![Tero Pitkämäki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10239814)

And finally, a sports story.

Javelin thrower Tero Pitkämäki will not go to the European Championships in August. Pitkämäki's knee is not in good enough condition.

Pitkämäki was injured in June. Pitkämäki twisted his leg when he threw the javelin. His knee will be operated on in August.

The European Athletics Championships are held in Berlin, Germany. Finns hoped that Pitkämäki would do well at the European Championships.

Pitkämäki has won many important competitions before. For example, he was the javelin world champion in 2007.
