## Finns are positive about the corona vaccine

![A corona vaccine bottle in rubber-gloved hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3543,h_1993,x_0,y_78/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7602515ff83a7d26c8c/1610715577)

A large part of Finns intend to take the corona vaccine. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

Most Finns have a positive attitude towards the corona vaccine.

In the survey Yle asked whether you intend to take the corona vaccine. Of all respondents, 86 percent said that they will certainly or probably take the vaccine.

Almost all respondents over 65 said that they will certainly take the vaccine.

Young people, that is, 18 to 24 year olds, are also positive about the vaccine. Of them, 86 percent said that they will certainly or probably take the vaccine.

9 percent of respondents said that they refuse the corona vaccine.

## Estonia has a female prime minister

![Estonia's new government in a group photo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11756201)

Open image viewer

Estonia's prime minister is now Kaja Kallas. Photo: Mattias Tammet / Office of the President of Estonia

Estonia has a new government. At the same time Estonia got its first female prime minister.

Kaja Kallas, the chair of the Reform Party, now leads the government.

Estonia's former prime minister and government resigned when it emerged that the prime minister's party had been mixed up in a corruption scandal.

## Viking Line's new ship

![Viking Glory on the water at a Chinese shipyard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_483/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-766001600ee4f7b3077/1611676785)

Open image viewer

Viking Line's new ship is the Viking Glory. Photo: Viking Line

The shipping company Viking Line's new ship has been launched at a shipyard in China.

Corona slowed the building of the Viking Glory in China, but the builders will finish the ship completely this year.

Sailing the ship from China to Finland takes 1 to 2 months.

The Viking Glory will start sailing between Turku and Stockholm at the beginning of 2022.

The ship costs about 194 million euros.

## A rare golden perch

![golden perch](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-766342600ff678d4cdd/1611658977)

Open image viewer

A golden-coloured perch is rare. Photo: Juha Kokkala / Yle

A rare fish has been found in Tampere. It is a golden-coloured perch. The golden-coloured perch was found in a fisherman's net, in the lake Pyhäjärvi.

The golden colour is apparently a change in pigment that came about through a mutation. Usually perch are black, grey or green.

The fisherman gave the perch to the Natural History Museum of Tampere.

## Snow is falling

![map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11757309)

Open image viewer

Tomorrow, Wednesday, it will be cloudy and snow will fall in almost the whole country.

In the southern and central parts of the country, and in places also in the north, a few centimetres of snow will fall.

In Southern and Central Finland the frost is 2 to 3 degrees.

In Lapland the frost is between 7 and 15 degrees.
