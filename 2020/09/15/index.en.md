# Tuesday 15.9.2020 (radio)

Many corona infections in Finland. Infections are also rising in Estonia. Opposition leader Alexei Navalny is doing better. There may be life on the planet Venus. Tomorrow it will rain and blow.

## Many corona infections in Finland

![Punatulkku service centre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11545210)

There are many corona infections in a care home called Punatulkku. It is in Mänttä-Vilppula. Photo: Antti Eintola / Yle

Today it was reported in Finland that 98 new corona infections have been found.

The number is big, but it includes infections from several days.

Most of the infections come from 2 chains of infection. One chain has been in the Lahti area and the other in Äänekoski.

Infections have also been found in a care home in Mänttä-Vilppula.

In the care home 11 infections have been found. In the care home, most of the people who caught the infection are over 80 years old.

## Corona infections are increasing in Estonia

![Estonia recommends voluntary quarantine for those arriving in the country from countries where the infection rate is over 16.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11543455)

Many people travel from Finland to Tallinn on autumn holidays. Photo: Stanislav Moshkov

In Estonia the number of corona infections has risen quickly during the autumn.

Estonia has been one of the few countries that people from Finland could travel to fairly calmly, even during the corona times.

Now the situation looks a bit bad for autumn holiday trips.

Estonian health authorities say that large crowds should be avoided if you travel to Estonia.

The Russian opposition leader Alexei Navalny says on Instagram that he is doing better.

Navalny posted a picture and wrote that he is now breathing on his own.

Navalny has been in hospital in Berlin, Germany, for 2 weeks. In Germany it was found that there was a nerve agent in his body.

No poison was found when Navalny was examined in Russia.

## Signs of life on Venus

![Venus as photographed by NASA's Mariner 10 probe in February 1974. Many probes have photographed our neighbour planet on flybys and from orbit since then, but this old image, processed by Kevin M. Gill of the Jet Propulsion Laboratory expert, is one of the most beautiful.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11540897)

Open image viewer

NASA's probe took this picture of Venus as early as 1974. Photo: NASA

Still news from space.

Researchers have found very small signs of life on the planet Venus.

An international team of researchers says that clouds cover Venus.

The clouds contain a chemical compound called phosphine.

The researchers say that the explanation for the phosphine is that there is some simple life in the clouds, probably microbes.

This discovery is so far the most convincing proof that there is life in space somewhere other than on Earth.

## An autumn storm comes on Wednesday

![A birch tree has fallen to the ground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1008,h_567,x_0,y_158/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6951165efc124a4e63a/1593578105)

In July there was a summer storm in Eastern Finland. Many trees fell then. Photo: Johanna Raatikainen / Yle

Tomorrow, Wednesday, a strong low pressure system will arrive in Finland.

It will rain and the wind will be gusty, that is, at times it will blow really hard.

The wind can knock down many trees. They may also fall onto roads and power lines.

So it may be a good idea to prepare for the possibility of power cuts.

At sea the wind will grow into a storm.
