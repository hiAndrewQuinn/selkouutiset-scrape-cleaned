# Thursday 28.11.2019 (radio)

Long-time MP Maarit Feldt-Ranta has died. Johnson's Conservative Party is the clear favourite in the British election. North Korea has fired a missile. The captain of a boat has been charged over the accident in Hungary.

## Maarit Feldt-Ranta has died

![Maarit Feldt-Ranta](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10916616)

Long-time SDP MP Maarit Feldt-Ranta has died. She was 51 years old.

Feldt-Ranta was elected to Parliament from the Uusimaa constituency in 2007. Before that, she was a special assistant to several ministers. She was chosen as the SDP party secretary in 2005.

Feldt-Ranta was diagnosed with stomach cancer in 2009. She recovered, but the cancer came back 2 times.

## Poll predicts a landslide for Johnson in the British election

Britain's Prime Minister Boris Johnson's Conservative Party is the clear favourite in the coming parliamentary election.

An opinion poll says the Conservatives could win a majority of up to 68 seats in the British parliament. The result of the poll shows that Johnson has strong support for Britain leaving the EU at the end of January.

The early parliamentary election in Britain will be held on 12 December.

## North Korea has fired a missile

![North Korean leader Kim Jong-un oversaw the testing of a new heavy rocket launcher system in an unknown location in North Korea on Saturday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10937735)

Open image viewer

North Korean leader Kim Jong-un oversaw the testing of a new heavy rocket launcher system in North Korea in August. Photo: KCNA / EPA

North Korea has fired a missile. Japan and South Korea have reported this. They did not give more information about what kind of missile it was or where it was fired.

Earlier this week, North Korea said that it had practised with artillery near the border with South Korea.

A Japanese news agency reports that North Korea has fired 2 ballistic missiles. A UN Security Council resolution says that North Korea must not fire ballistic missiles.

Japan's Prime Minister Shinzo Abe criticises North Korea. In Abe's view, North Korea is a threat to Japan and the international community. Negotiations between the USA and North Korea about North Korea's nuclear weapons have not moved forward.

## Charges against a captain over the Hungary boat disaster

![The wreck is lifted onto a barge.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10826507)

Open image viewer

The wreck of the sunken tourist boat was raised from the bottom of the river in June. Photo: Balazs Mohai / EPA-EFE

In Hungary, the captain of a river boat has been charged with many offences over the boat accident that happened in the spring.

The Mermaid boat sank in the Danube in Budapest in May, and 27 people died. The boat collided with another vessel. Almost all of the victims were South Korean tourists.

Prosecutors say that the Ukrainian captain of the Viking Sigyn was careless. He committed a breach of duty and failed to give help. In total, the captain faces 35 charges.

The captain is facing a sentence of 9 years in prison.
