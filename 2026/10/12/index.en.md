## Cancer treatment has to be waited for

First, news about cancer treatment in Finland.

Cancer is an illness whose treatment can have to be waited for too long.

Yle's MOT programme got information about a new cancer study. The study says that in other Nordic countries cancer is often treated faster than in Finland.

For example, lung cancer is treated much faster in Sweden and Denmark than in Finland.

In Finland a person waits for cancer treatment an average of 155 days. That is several months more than in Denmark, for example.

## Big differences in electricity network fees

Next, news about the price of electricity.

There are big differences in electricity network fees.

The most expensive company is Järvi-Suomen Energia. It transmits electricity in Mikkeli, Juva and Savonlinna, for example. The cheapest company is Turku energia.

In Juva the network fee for an ordinary detached house is over 2 000 euros a year. In Turku the network fee for a detached house is under 1 000 euros.

The network fee is lower if the electricity network has many users.

There are big differences in electricity network fees.

Almost 50 electricity companies raised the network fee this year. Last year almost 60 electricity companies raised the price.

The Energy Authority says that some companies raised prices too much. They will have to pay money back to customers.
