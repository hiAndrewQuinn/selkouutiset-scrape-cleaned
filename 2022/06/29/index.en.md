Finland's and Sweden's joining the military alliance NATO has taken a big step forward. Turkey now supports Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership, when earlier Turkey opposed it.

In the Tuesday evening negotiations, Finland, Sweden and Turkey signed a document in which the countries commit to supporting each other against various security threats. Finland's legislation will not change, but Finland promises, for example, to strengthen the work against terrorism.

## NATO membership moves forward

![Finland's and Sweden's application for NATO membership](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_774,h_435,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97518662ba225608d06/1656365839)

Finland and Sweden applied to become members of the military alliance NATO in May. Photo: NATO

Today the military alliance NATO has invited Finland and Sweden to become members of the military alliance.

The invitation was decided at the NATO summit in Madrid, Spain. The invitation could be presented because all NATO countries were now unanimously in favour of it.

Finland and Sweden now start accession negotiations with NATO. Finland's negotiating delegation is led by Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. It has been estimated in advance that the negotiations will take only a couple of days.

Each NATO country's parliament must still approve the results of the accession negotiations. Only after that are Finland and Sweden full members of NATO.

## Compensation sought from VR

![VR train crossing a bridge. In the foreground various tools and machines; the sky is partly cloudy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4261,h_2396,x_1,y_132/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97506862b99e68b7e4f/1656332235)

VR has received many compensation applications for train delays over the Midsummer weekend.

VR says that a few thousand people have already applied for compensation. VR cannot yet estimate how large the total sum of compensation will grow.

The Midsummer return traffic was seriously delayed, when train traffic was disrupted for a long time because of track work on the main line.

Trains were delayed by up to 15 hours. There were several thousand passengers on the trains.

## Pollinators are studied

![A butterfly and a bumblebee pollinating ninebark in the yard of a terraced house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4104,h_2309,x_0,y_539/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83346560f597a978fa5/1626709715)

Information is starting to be collected on the pollinating insects of Finland's nature. The national pollinator monitoring began in early June, says the Finnish Environment Institute SYKE.

The monitoring collects information on bumblebees, solitary bees, hoverflies and butterflies. Information is collected, for example, on how many there are.

Pollinator monitoring is carried out in over 100 places in different parts of the country. Monitoring is done, for example, in meadows, in forests and on Lapland's fells.
