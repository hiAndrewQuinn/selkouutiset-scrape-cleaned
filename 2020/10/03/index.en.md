# Saturday 3.10.2020 (radio)

US President Donald Trump has been taken to hospital. There are 147 new corona infections in Finland. A British corona vaccine may soon be ready. The release of the new Bond film has been moved again. Bird watchers are watching migrating birds this weekend. Rain is approaching Finland.

## Trump in hospital

The US President Donald Trump has been taken to hospital. Trump has a corona infection.

Trump has not had serious symptoms. However, the doctors want Trump to stay in hospital for a while. In hospital the president can quickly get intensive care if the symptoms get worse.

Trump said on Twitter that he is doing well. The President has continued working in hospital.

## New corona infections

![Mehiläinen's drive-in corona testing station opened in the car park of the Messukeskus in Helsinki on 26 August 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11573750)

New corona infections have been found in Finland. On Saturday it was reported that there are 147 new corona infections.

Most new infections are in Helsinki, and the second most new infections are in Vaasa.

About 10,400 corona infections have been found in Finland so far in total.

## British corona vaccine

![A shot being given in the arm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7045765f278b78c9a33/1596427190)

A British corona vaccine may soon be ready. Researchers estimate that the vaccine can be made available by the end of this year.

The developers of the vaccine are the University of Oxford and the pharmaceutical company AstraZeneca. The aim is for the vaccine to give good protection against the coronavirus.

Then news about James Bond. The release of the next Bond film has been moved again. The reason is the coronavirus.

The new Bond film was due to come to cinemas as early as April. Because of the corona, the premiere was moved to November. And now the premiere has been moved to next year.

Because of the corona, cinemas can only take in a few audience members. That is why it is not worth bringing new films to cinemas.

Now there is hope that Bond can finally have adventures next year.

## Bird watchers' weekend

![Greater white-fronted geese.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3579,h_2013,x_0,y_185/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7190185f607cf8ad632/1600163144)

Open image viewer

Many geese, for example, are still on their migration south. Photo: Tomas Aarvak / NOF - BirdLife Norway

Bird watchers are out this weekend in many countries, including Finland. Bird watchers are now observing migrating birds.

A large part of Finland's migratory birds have already flown south. However, many migratory birds can still be seen, for example geese and cranes. Migratory birds return to Finland in spring, when the weather warms up again.

The organiser of the bird weekend is the BirdLife organisation.

## Weather for the weekend

![The old town of Porvoo in its autumn colours.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11021624)

Open image viewer

The weather is warm, but rain is also expected. Photo: Saara Hirvonen / Yle

The warm weather continues on Sunday. At first the weather is also dry, that is, no rain falls.

On Sunday afternoon and evening, showers will come to Southern Finland. After that the rain moves towards the north.

Early in the week more rain will come to Finland. The temperature is, however, higher than usual for early October.
