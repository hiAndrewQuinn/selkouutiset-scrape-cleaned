## Electricity transmission

An electricity grid company always owns the local electricity distribution network, through which electricity is transmitted to homes and other properties. That is why the electricity grid company cannot be freely chosen. Photo: Raimo Torikka / Yle

Customers pay the most for electricity transmission in Kainuu and North Ostrobothnia, in the areas of Kajave Oy. Transmission prices are high also in North Karelia and North Savo.

Electricity is transmitted most cheaply to customers in Kerava and Turku.

The local grid company is always responsible for the electricity transmission, regardless of where the customer buys their energy.

The transmission fee is based on a local basic fee and the customer's own energy consumption.

## Bird flu virus

![Animals in cages that look like they are suffering.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2493,h_1400,x_0,y_260/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106145763c92838c2125/1674127742)

Open image viewer

The Food Authority has ordered a large number of blue foxes and minks to be put down because of bird flu. Photo: Oikeutta eläimille

Bird flu has spread to 21 fur farms in Finland, says the National Institute for Health and Welfare (THL).

THL warns that bird flu is a risk to public health. THL says that the health safety of fur farms must be improved.

Research results show that mutations, that is, changes, have been found in the viruses of fur animals. Changed viruses have been found on two fur farms.

The bird flu virus can also infect people when the virus adapts to mammals.

## Shared living

![A small studio flat, with a bed, a desk and a chair.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_890,h_501,x_0,y_286/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6726275eb51dc2a9279/1588928068)

Open image viewer

There are only enough student flats for a small number of students. Photo: Elna Äyhynmäki

The new government is encouraging students to live together.

The government is looking into housing benefit for students alongside the general housing benefit. Such a system was in use when students received a housing allowance, not the general housing benefit.

In the new model, perhaps only the applicant's own income would affect the amount of housing benefit. A student could receive housing benefit even if a partner in the same flat goes to work.

Shared living is slowed down by the fact that during the last 10 years many of the thousands of studio flats have been turned into one-room flats or family flats.

In Finland, student housing foundations own just under 69,000 student flats.

## The temperature of the seas

![A sandy beach photographed from sand level.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114712264bf9ddf619fb/1706258859)

Open image viewer

Researchers warn that the warming of the seas seriously affects people, animals and the whole planet. Photo: AOP

The temperature of the oceans has risen.

The average temperature of the sea surface is now the highest in the history of measurements. This week the average sea surface temperature was 20.96 degrees.

The previous record was in March 2016: 20.95 degrees.

Researchers are worried about the timing of the new record: high temperatures are normally in March.
