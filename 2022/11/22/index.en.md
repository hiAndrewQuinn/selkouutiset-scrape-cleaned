## Olkiluoto's new nuclear power plant

![Interior of the Olkiluoto 3 plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_166/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10150006336c05cd2854/1664532654)

Inside the new nuclear power plant. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

Olkiluoto's new nuclear power plant is delayed further. The nuclear power plant will not be ready for use yet in December.

Earlier in the autumn it was said that Olkiluoto's new nuclear power plant would start producing electricity during this year.

Later, faults were found at the nuclear power plant, which are still being repaired. That is why the nuclear power plant will only start producing electricity at the end of January or later.

Finns may have to save electricity if Olkiluoto's new nuclear power plant is not put into use soon. The new nuclear power plant is important for Finland, because there is an energy crisis in Europe.

Olkiluoto's new nuclear power plant will produce more than 10 percent of Finland's electricity when it starts to work regularly.

## Finland's population

![People walking on a bridge.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_23/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-850498613623b525556/1630939211)

Open image viewer

There are more than 5.5 million inhabitants in Finland. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finland's population is growing. Statistics Finland says that Finland's population has grown by more than 13,000 people this year.

More Finns have died than have been born, but the population is growing thanks to immigration. About 36,000 people moved to Finland from abroad, but only about 11,000 people moved from Finland abroad.

At the end of October, Finland had more than 5.5 million inhabitants.

## Power cuts in Ukraine

![People in the dark on a street in Kyiv.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7360,h_4140,x_0,y_415/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10241596355224ebb60d/1666523838)

Open image viewer

Power is often out in the Ukrainian capital Kyiv. Photo: Oleg Petrasyuk / EPA

There are many power cuts in Ukraine. The reason is Russian attacks.

Russia has destroyed Ukraine's power stations and power lines. It is difficult for Ukraine to repair the damage, because Russia keeps attacking all the time.

There are power cuts especially in the Ukrainian capital Kyiv. The Ukrainian president is asking Ukrainians to save electricity.

## Military exercise at sea

![Warship in the port of Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-94586162665efddb137/1650876278)

Open image viewer

One of Finland's warships. Photo: Pontus Nyqvist / Yle

A large military exercise has begun in Finland. The naval forces of the Finnish Defence Forces are training at sea, for example in the Gulf of Finland.

Soldiers from other countries also take part in the exercise, for example from Sweden, Germany, France and the USA. About 5,000 people and 50 ships are involved in the exercise.

Military aircraft also take part in the exercise. That is why military aircraft are flying over the skies of southern Finland.

## The weather

On Wednesday the weather is dry and wintry cold. There is a lot of cloud cover and it may snow a little in places.

The temperature on Wednesday is between -1 and -6 degrees. In Lapland it is about 15 degrees of frost.

On Thursday the frost weakens a little. On the coast the temperature is around zero, elsewhere there is a little frost. In Lapland it is about 10 degrees of frost on Thursday.
