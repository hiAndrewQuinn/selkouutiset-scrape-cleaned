## Restaurant restrictions

![A waiter starts pouring beer into a tankard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-779927603dfbc1c1116/1636604776)

In many areas the sale of alcohol must stop at midnight. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The government has set new restrictions for the restaurant sector.

The reason for the restrictions is that the corona situation has got worse. Corona infections have come, for example, from bars.

Restaurant restrictions are now coming to new areas, for example the Turku region.

The restrictions say that restaurants must close by 1 am at the latest. The sale of alcohol must stop one hour earlier.

The Helsinki region already has the same restaurant restrictions, and they continue.

## Loan interest rates

![Housing loans. Real estate office. Helsinki. 20.5.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80885660a680873758b/1623062176)

Open image viewer

Interest rates on housing loans also stay low. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

Loan interest rates stay low.

On Thursday the European Central Bank, that is the ECB, said that interest rates do not need to be raised. The ECB intends to raise interest rates only when inflation, that is the rise in prices, has been at 2 percent for a longer time.

The ECB forecasts that prices will rise by less than 2 percent for at least the next 2 years.

The European Central Bank's assessment is good news for everyone who has, for example, a housing loan. The ECB's decisions also affect the interest rates that European banks charge their own customers.

## Shortage of workers in the restaurant sector

![In the picture, a waiter wearing a face mask works at the Vltava restaurant in October 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4500,h_2531,x_0,y_295/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7307485f88853620dbb/1613669989)

Open image viewer

The restaurant sector needs more waiters, for example. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The restaurant sector needs more workers.

Restaurants lost workers during the corona crisis. As many as 20 percent of restaurant sector workers changed field when the restaurants were closed because of the corona crisis.

Now the restaurants are open again, but there are too few workers.

More workers are needed in other service sectors too. Many workers left the service sectors during the corona crisis.

Now there is work again, but workers cannot be found for barber and hairdresser shops, for example.

The National Agency for Education says that the number of students has also decreased in beauty care and hairdressing.

## Olympic Games in Japan

Welcome! This is how the Sobetsu school greets the Finnish athletes. Photo: Jani Lehtinen

The Tokyo Olympics begin in Japan. The opening ceremony is on Friday.

Finnish athletes have also travelled to Japan. For example, Finnish race walkers are preparing for the competitions in the city of Sobetsu.

Small Sobetsu has welcomed the Finns kindly. The city organised a fine welcome event for the Finnish athletes.

The people of Sobetsu have also made videos for the Finnish athletes. In the videos they wish good luck and encouragement.

The Sobetsu videos can be watched on the Yle Urheilu website.
