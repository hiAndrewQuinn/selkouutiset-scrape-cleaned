# Saturday 3.2.2018 (radio)

The presidential couple have had a baby. Finns are excited about the presidential couple's baby. Many people have fallen ill because of dirty tap water in Nousiainen. Healthy food can be found in Finnish nature. Winter weather has begun in all of Finland.

## The presidential couple had a baby

![President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10057586)

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio. Photo: Seppo Samuli / Lehtikuva

The presidential couple have had a baby. President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio had a baby boy on Friday evening.

The mother and baby are doing well. The baby was born at the Women's Hospital in Helsinki. President Niinistö was present at the birth.

This is the first time in Finland that a president has had a baby. The children of other Finnish presidents were born before the parents became the presidential couple.

Finns are excited about the presidential couple's baby. The presidential couple have received many congratulations, for example through Facebook.

## Nousiainen's water pipes are being cleaned

![A man fills a water canister from a water tank brought to the municipality of Nousiainen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10049359)

Open image viewer

In Nousiainen, people have fetched clean drinking water from tanks. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

In Nousiainen, near Turku, many people have fallen ill because of dirty tap water. About 400 people have got a stomach illness.

Dirty sewage water got into the drinking water when a pipe broke. People have not been allowed to drink tap water for a week.

Now Nousiainen's water pipes are being cleaned with chlorine.

## Health from Finnish nature

![Franco Berrino on the ice of Lake Oivanki in Kuusamo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10040759)

Open image viewer

Italian doctor Franco Berrino gets health from Finnish nature. Photo: Ensio Karjalainen / Yle

From Finnish nature you can get food that improves health. This is what Italian research doctor Franco Berrino says.

Berrino thinks that fish from a clean Finnish clean lake is medicine. He also praises the berries of Finland's forests, for example blueberries. He is sure that lake fish and forest berries improve health. Berrino thinks that Finns are foolish for not using the treasures of their own nature more.

Berrino has also fallen in love with the Finnish sauna. Going to the sauna brings peace of mind, and it improves health, Berrino says.

Now Berrino is in Kuusamo, in northern Finland. He has also brought 30 other doctors from Italy there to see Finland's clean nature.

## Freezing weather for the whole country

![A pedestrian in the frost in Muonio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10057445)

Open image viewer

There has been frost in Lapland for a long time already. Photo: Aku Häyrynen / Lehtikuva

Winter weather has begun in all of Finland. There is now frost in southern Finland too.

In the next few days it will be more than minus 10 degrees in the south. After the middle of next week the weather will get a little warmer, but the temperature will stay below zero.

There has been frost in Lapland for a long time. During this weekend the frost will get harder, says Yle meteorologist Toni Hellinen. There is a small chance that the frost in Lapland will reach as much as minus 40 degrees.
