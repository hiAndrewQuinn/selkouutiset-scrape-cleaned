# Wednesday 19.6.2019 (radio)

A court has given a ruling in a major police case. Many residents of Finland have a foreign background. Many children live in 2 homes. Midsummer brings changes to traffic.

## Police court case

![Paatero, Lardot, Aapio, Riikonen, Aarnio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10350967)

A court has given a ruling in a major police case. The case accused police bosses.

The decision of the Helsinki District Court was that the bosses did not commit a crime.

The police bosses were accused of not supervising the use of informants. An informant means a person who tells the police information, for example about criminals' plans.

The police must keep a register of informants, that is, a list. However, the former head of the Helsinki drugs police removed the informants from the register.

In the court's opinion, no crime happened in the matter.

## Residents with a foreign background

![People walk through the bustle and crowds of the city.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10838800)

Open image viewer

7.3 percent of Finland's residents have a foreign background. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Many residents of Finland have a foreign background.

There are now more than 400,000 people with a foreign background living in Finland. That is twice as many as 10 years ago. The largest groups come from Russia, Estonia, Iraq and Somalia.

Most people with a foreign background live in Uusimaa. Almost 20 percent of Vantaa's residents already have a foreign background, and in the whole capital region 25 percent of children below school age have a foreign background.

## Children with two homes

![A child gives a kiss to its mother.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10375195)

Open image viewer

After the parents' divorce, the child often lives with its mother. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

In Finland about 110,000 children live in 2 homes. The information is from Statistics Finland.

A child lives in 2 homes when it lives part of the time with its mother and part of the time with its father. This often happens after the parents divorce.

About 40,000 children live equally with their mother and father. The most common situation is that the child's official home is with its mother. In that case the child usually also lives more with its mother.

## Midsummer traffic

![A line of cars on Highway 5.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9687991)

Open image viewer

Thursday of midsummer week is a busy day on the roads. Photo: Heidi Sullström / Yle

Midsummer is celebrated at the weekend.

There will be traffic jams on the motorways on Thursday, when many people travel to celebrate midsummer. There is also a lot of traffic on Friday morning.

There are many changes to train traffic because of midsummer. On Friday, which is Midsummer Eve, the trains run according to the Saturday timetable. On Saturday, which is Midsummer Day, the trains follow the Sunday timetables.

There are also changes in public transport in the Helsinki region. On Thursday, services run according to the Friday timetables. That is why the night shifts also run during the night between Thursday and Friday.
