# Saturday 6.5.2017 (radio)

There is an election in France on Sunday. Fish are intelligent. Cows were let out to pasture in Viikki, Helsinki. The weather is getting colder.

## France is choosing a president

![Marine Le Pen and Emmanuel Macron](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9597767)

Open image viewer

Marine Le Pen and Emmanuel Macron. Photo: Eric Feferberg / EPA

There is an election in France on Sunday. The French are choosing a new president. Now it is the second round of the election, and there are still 2 candidates.

France's next president will be Emmanuel Macron or Marine Le Pen. They are very different. Le Pen, for example, does not want immigrants in France, while Macron wants to keep France an open country.

Opinion polls say that Emmanuel Macron may win the election.

## An important election for Europe

![EU flag on a pole.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8803270)

Open image viewer

The French election will decide the future of the European Union. Photo: Patrick Seeger / EPA

The French election interests the whole of Europe.

If Marine Le Pen becomes President of France, Europe will change. Le Pen hopes that France will leave the European Union. If that happens, the EU is in crisis.

If Emmanuel Macron becomes president, France's policy will not change. Macron wants France to be in the European Union and the union to be strong.

On Sunday evening it will become clear what the situation in France and in Europe looks like.

## Fish can learn

![Pike in an aquarium.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7349922)

Open image viewer

This fish is a pike. Photo: Yle

Fish are intelligent. New studies say that fish are able to learn, for example. They can learn where food can be found or where it is safe to move.

Fish are also able to use sounds. Fish do not make sounds with their mouths, but they have many other means. For example, they can let out air from their rear end.

At least 200 fish species use sounds. Some fish can even be said to be able to sing. Fish send messages to each other with sounds.

Nowadays it is known that fish also feel pain. With research it may be possible to come up with new fishing methods that do not cause pain to fish.

## The cows got to pasture

![Cows from the Viikki experimental farm being let out to pasture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9597448)

Open image viewer

The cows had fun when they got to pasture. Photo: Yle

On Saturday in Helsinki a sign of spring was seen. The cows got out to pasture in Viikki. The cows ran and jumped when they got out after the winter. They have spent the winter indoors in the cowshed.

In Viikki there is a farm of the University of Helsinki, where agriculture is researched and taught. Thousands of people came to Viikki to watch the cows' joy.

## Cold from the Arctic Ocean

![Tulip leaves in the snow](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9577808)

Open image viewer

After the warm days, sleet may fall again. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

The weather is getting colder.

The weather has been warm in recent days. On Friday 18.9 degrees was measured in Mikkeli. Saturday has also been a warm day.

Now the weather is changing. Cold air is coming to Finland from the north, from the Arctic Ocean. The weather will be cold for the next 2 weeks.
