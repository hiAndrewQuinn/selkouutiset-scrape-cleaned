# Tuesday 7.7.2020 (radio)

Many universities are getting new study places and more teachers. A working group wants a lack of consent to become a central feature of the crime of rape. VR is closing ticket sales service points in many cities. The UN warns about zoonotic diseases, that is, infectious diseases from animals.

The TV Clear News has a summer break, and will continue on 17.8. The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Universities need more teachers

![Main building of the University of Jyväskylä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11400581)

Main building of the University of Jyväskylä. Photo: Isto Janhunen / Yle

In the autumn and next year, 4,800 new study places will open at higher education institutions.

That is why more teachers are needed. For example, the universities of Helsinki, Tampere, Oulu, Jyväskylä and Turku tell Yle that they intend to hire new teachers.

The universities of applied sciences in Turku, Kajaani and Lapland are also hiring more teachers.

The teachers' union OAJ has been worried about teachers' ability to cope. OAJ stresses that hiring teachers is now really important.

## Laws on sexual offences are being reformed

![Harassment of young girls has increased.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-4820856cf07398e202/1456408625)

The legislation on sexual offences may change. The working group wants a lack of consent to be a central feature of the crime of rape.

At present the law says that rape is forcing someone into sexual intercourse with violence or by threatening violence.

Verbal harassment may also in future be sexual harassment, the working group proposes.

Verbal harassment can be, for example, words and facial expressions.

## VR closes ticket sales

![VR logo on a wall of a railway station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11436224)

Open image viewer

Fewer and fewer people buy a train ticket at the VR ticket counter. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The railway company VR is closing its ticket sales service points in Jyväskylä, Lahti, Oulu, Tikkurila and Turku. After that, tickets can be bought there in person from ticket machines or R-kiosks.

VR says that over 80 per cent of train tickets are sold in self-service. These include VR's online shop, its mobile app and ticket machines.

Last year 6 per cent of train tickets were sold through service points.

Many of VR's service points have been temporarily closed because of the corona epidemic. At the moment only the points in Helsinki, Hyvinkää, Järvenpää and Tampere are open.

## Zoonotic diseases threaten people

![Wild animal meat being cut into pieces for food in Liberia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11435988)

Open image viewer

Wild animal meat is cut into pieces for food in Liberia. In Africa the population is growing quickly, and people are in contact with different animals more than before. At the same time the risk of new diseases passing from animals to people is also growing. Photo: Ahmed Jallanzo / EPA

A report by the UN, the United Nations, warns of new infectious diseases. The UN says that new diseases will break out if they are not stopped.

Most infectious diseases in people are zoonotic, that is, they spread from animals to people.

The most recent such infectious disease is covid-19, which is caused by the coronavirus. Ebola, MERS and West Nile virus, for example, also originally come from animals.

Every year an estimated 2 million people die because of zoonotic infectious diseases. These diseases include, for example, anthrax, bovine tuberculosis and rabies. Most deaths are in countries with a low standard of living.

The demand for animal protein, environmentally harmful agriculture, the exploitation of wild animals and the climate crisis increase the spread of zoonotic infectious diseases.
