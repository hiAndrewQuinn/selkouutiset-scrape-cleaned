# Sunday 5.2.2023 (radio)

Industry wage deal. US and China ball dispute. Runeberg Day. Runeberg Prize. Weather.

## Industry wage deal

![Two men with microphones in front of them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688850)

Leaders of the workers' organisation said on Sunday what has been agreed on industry wages. Photo: Seppo Samuli / Lehtikuva

Wages in industry have been agreed on. The organisations of employees and employers approved the wage deal at the weekend.

Wages of workers in the technology industry and the chemical industry will rise by 7 percent in 2 years.

Because the wage dispute in industry is now over, the strikes in industry also end. Workers will continue working as normal.

## US and China ball dispute

![A big white ball in the sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688848)

Open image viewer

A Chinese ball in the sky above the USA. Photo: Lehtikuva

The USA and China have a dispute. The reason for the dispute is a big Chinese ball that flew in the sky above the USA.

The USA shot the ball down on Saturday. Americans want more exact information about the ball.

The USA estimates that the ball is a spy device. The ball may have been watching US military sites.

China says that the ball is not a spy device. China claims that the ball made weather observations and accidentally drifted into America.

The dispute has worsened relations between the USA and China. The US Secretary of State was supposed to travel to China on Sunday, but because of the ball dispute he stayed at home.

## Runeberg Day

![Runeberg statue in winter in Porvoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688846)

Open image viewer

Statue of Johan Ludvig Runeberg in Porvoo. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Runeberg Day was celebrated on Sunday.

Johan Ludvig Runeberg is the national poet of Finland. The national anthem of Finland, Maamme, is a poem by Runeberg.

Runeberg lived in the 1800s. He was Swedish-speaking and wrote in Swedish.

Runeberg Day is celebrated on 5 February, because it is Runeberg's birthday. Runeberg Day is a flag day.

On Runeberg Day in Finland, people eat Runeberg tarts. Runeberg ate roughly similar tarts already 170 years ago.

## Runeberg Prize

![Writer Marja Kyllönen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688847)

Open image viewer

Writer Marja Kyllönen. Photo: Outi Paappanen.

The Runeberg Prize was also awarded on Sunday. The Runeberg Prize is a prize for literature.

This year the winner of the Runeberg Prize is the writer Marja Kyllönen. The prize committee says that Kyllönen uses the Finnish language skilfully.

Kyllönen won the prize with the book Vainajaiset. The book is about life in a village in the countryside. Miscarriages and infertility are one theme of the book.

The Runeberg Prize is always awarded on Runeberg Day. The Runeberg Prize is 20,000 euros.
