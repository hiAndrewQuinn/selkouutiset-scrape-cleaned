## Russian attack on Kyiv

![A rescue worker puts out a fire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3547,h_1995,x_0,y_233/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1120684647312ff8808a/1685263352)

Russian drone attack set off fires. Photo: Pavlo Petrov / Ukrainian Ministry of Emergency Situations

Russia has carried out a large air strike on Ukraine's capital, Kyiv. Russia carried out the drone strike during the night between Saturday and Sunday. At least 1 person died in the strike.

Ukraine says that it shot down more than 40 drones over Kyiv. Ukraine assesses that the drone strike on Kyiv was the largest of the whole war. Russia started the war in Ukraine last February.

Russia carried out a large drone strike on Kyiv on the city's festival day. Kyiv turned 1541 years old on Sunday.

## Democratic elections in Somalia

A shop in Somalia's capital Mogadishu. Photo: Elyas Ahmed / EPA

Somalia promises to hold democratic elections next year.

Somalia has local elections in about a year. Then everyone may vote.

Somalia has not had such general suffrage for over 50 years. Important positions have been divided among clans.

## An award for Kaurismäki

![Jussi Vatanen, Alma Pöysti and Aki Kaurismäki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3501,h_1969,x_0,y_88/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1120566647201241b38e/1685217446)

Open image viewer

Aki Kaurismäki (right) with actor Alma Pöysti and actor Jussi Vatanen in Cannes. Photo: Loic Venance / AFP

Finnish film director Aki Kaurismäki has won an award.

Kaurismäki received the Jury Prize at the Cannes Film Festival in France. The Jury Prize is the third most important prize at the film festival.

Kaurismäki received the award for the film Fallen Leaves. The film is about the love of 2 lonely people. Fallen Leaves comes to cinemas in Finland in autumn.

Aki Kaurismäki has also received an award at Cannes once before. He received the second most important prize of the film festival in 2002.

The Cannes Film Festival is the world's most important film festival.

## Yle Language School

![Translator and journalist Jonatan Pedersen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1893,h_1064,x_6,y_5/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-111460264635a8e3a2da/1684233023)

Open image viewer

Translator Jonatan Pedersen coordinates Yle Language School.

Yle Language School offers a fun way to learn Finnish.

In the Language School you learn Finnish by watching Finnish-language TV programmes.

The programmes also have Finnish subtitles. The viewer gets a translation for difficult words.

The viewer can choose for themselves which language the translations are in. There are 14 of these support languages.

Yle Language School offers translations based on the viewer's level of Finnish.

The tasks also suit the level the viewer has chosen.

Yle Language School is free. It can be used with an app or in a browser.

More information about the Language School can be found at yle.fi/kielikoulu.
