## EU and Ukraine

![Ursula von der Leyen and Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Kyiv.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2796,h_1572,x_0,y_58/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96887462a59679dcda0/1655472932)

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen met Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Kyiv in June. Photo: Ukrainian Presidential Office

The European Union is considering its relationship with Ukraine.

The European Commission recommends that Ukraine becomes an official EU membership candidate. Commission President Ursula von der Leyen says that Ukraine has done good work towards EU membership.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy believes that the European Commission's recommendation helps Ukraine in its fight against Russia.

If Ukraine becomes an EU membership candidate, it may still take years before Ukraine becomes an EU country.

## Slow economic growth

![People in a shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_0,y_214/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9361156245a2f6dcdae/1648813483)

Open image viewer

The economy is not growing as fast now as in recent years. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finland's economic growth is slowing down. This is how the Ministry of Finance, which plans Finland's economic policy, assesses it.

In the coming years Finland's economy will grow only a little above one percent. Last year the economy still grew 3.5 percent.

Prices will also rise. This year prices may rise almost 6 percent.

## Prices

![Factory hall of Orthex Oy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5818,h_3272,x_0,y_451/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9134776205140e0c3f0/1644500026)

Open image viewer

Making goods is becoming more expensive as the price of energy rises. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

Many Finnish companies plan to raise prices. About 70 percent of companies plan to raise prices during the next 4 months.

Russia's war in Ukraine also raises prices in Finland. The war has raised the price of energy, for example, and so making goods is now more expensive than before.

The information is from a survey by the Central Chamber of Commerce, which about 2,000 Finnish companies answered.

## Rents

![Empty one-room rental flat in Töölö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5375,h_3023,x_0,y_370/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4644165a84302d1ab66/1518612974)

Open image viewer

Rents may rise when prices rise. Photo: Kristiina Lehto / Yle

The rise in prices, that is inflation, can also affect rents. Rents may rise.

Some rental agreements have been made so that the landlord can raise the rent when prices rise.

That is why renting a home may now also become more expensive.

## Traffic accident

![Ambulance](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4953,h_2786,x_0,y_279/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6374255e340525b764a/1580467679)

The cause of the accident is not yet known. Photo: Mikael Crawford

A serious traffic accident has happened in Mäntsälä. 2 people died in the accident.

Mäntsälä is less than an hour's drive from Helsinki. The accident happened late on Thursday evening.

In the accident a truck crashed into the back of a car. Both people who died were in the car. The truck driver was not injured.

On Saturday there will be rain in Finland. It rains in Southern and Central Finland. In the rain the temperature is below 15 degrees.

In Northern and Eastern Finland it is dry on Saturday, that is, no rain falls. In dry areas the temperature is almost 20 degrees.

On Sunday the rain continues in Southern and Central Finland. There is also thunder at times. In the north it is dry on Sunday too.
