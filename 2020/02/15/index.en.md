# Saturday 15.2.2020 (radio)

One person has died of the coronavirus in France. Birds have started their spring migration early. A strong winter storm has hit Europe. On Sunday it will be windy in Finland too.

## The coronavirus killed someone in France

![Airport official wearing a protective face mask at Bangkok's international airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11210673)

One person has died of the coronavirus in France. This is the first coronavirus death outside Asia.

The person who died was a Chinese tourist who was quite old. He was taken to hospital in France because of the coronavirus.

In China, the coronavirus has already killed more than 1500 people. In addition, one person has died of the coronavirus in the Philippines, Hong Kong and Japan.

The coronavirus has spread in China since December. It started spreading from the Wuhan area. Almost 70,000 Chinese people have already got the coronavirus infection.

The symptoms of the coronavirus are similar to those of flu. Symptoms include, for example, coughing and fever. The coronavirus is dangerous for people who also have another illness, for example asthma or a heart disease.

## Birds are coming back early

![Geese taking off.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11210710)

Open image viewer

A flock of geese. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The spring migration of birds has started early. Many birds are returning to Finland from the south already in February, much earlier than usual. The reason is the warm weather.

Large flocks of swans have already been seen in southern Finland. One flock can have as many as 100 swans. Usually swans return to Finland only in March or April.

This week the first bean geese have also returned to Finland. In recent years the spring migration of geese has moved earlier than before.

## The storm hit Europe

![Builders are building flood barriers in Mytholmroyd.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11210644)

Open image viewer

Flood barriers were built in England against storm Dennis. Photo: Adam Vaughan / AOP

A strong winter storm has hit Europe. The storm has been named Dennis.

The storm came from the Atlantic Ocean and first hit Iceland. The storm wind was so strong that it tore roofs off houses.

At the weekend the storm is raging especially in Ireland and Britain. In Britain, for example, several hundred flights have been cancelled because of the storm.

The storm brings not only strong wind but also rain. In Britain, as much rain can fall in one day as usually falls in a whole month.

Storm Dennis affects the weather in all of Europe at the weekend.

## It is windy in Finland

![Sunday's warning map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11210756)

Open image viewer

On Sunday there are warnings of strong wind and snow on the roads in the north. Photo: Matti Huutonen / Yle

On Sunday it will be windy in Finland too.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns of storm winds at sea. Ferry services between Vaasa in Finland and Umeå in Sweden have been cancelled.

On Sunday, rain will also come to Finland. In the south it will rain, and in Lapland it will snow.

In Lapland there is already a lot of snow, and on Sunday up to 20 more centimetres of snow may fall.
