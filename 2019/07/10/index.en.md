# Wednesday 10.7.2019 (radio)

The government intends to raise small pensions. Finland's Day of Visual Art was celebrated on Wednesday. Bear life can be followed online until Saturday. Cockroaches can come home from summer holiday trips.

## Small pensions will rise

![An elderly woman pulls a wheeled bag behind her.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10870749)

Open image viewer

Small pensions will perhaps rise at the start of next year. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The government intends to raise pensions. The pensions that will rise are those of about 1,000 euros a month.

The smallest pensions will rise the most. The guarantee pension will rise by at most 50 euros and the national pension by at most 31 euros a month.

It is estimated that the pensions of about 600,000 pensioners will rise. The aim is for pensions to rise at the start of next year.

## Schjerfbeck paintings found

![Helene Schjerfbeck's oil painting Yellow Roses (1942)](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10869616)

Open image viewer

Helene Schjerfbeck's painting Yellow Roses. The painting will be sold in the autumn. Photo: Annmaris auction house

Finland's Day of Visual Art was celebrated on Wednesday. The day is celebrated on 10 July, because it is the birthday of the artist Helene Schjerfbeck.

Schjerfbeck was born in 1862 and died in 1946. She is one of Finland's great artists.

Today's big news is that 2 Schjerfbeck paintings have been found in Tampere. The second painting had been hidden for 50 years.

The paintings will be sold at auction in the autumn. It is estimated that about 200,000 euros will be paid for each of the paintings.

## Online stream of bears' lives

![A mother bear and three cubs in the forest, with a fallen and decayed tree in the background](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10863870)

Open image viewer

A mother bear is looking after her cubs. Photo: Birgitta Tarvainen

You can now follow the life of bears on the internet. The online stream started on Wednesday evening, and it ends on Saturday morning.

A camera films what happens at the bear-watching spot. The host of the watching spot is Markku Matero.

Matero brings food to the bears shortly before seven in the evening. That is when the most happens there. The big males eat first, and the mother bears and cubs eat only after them.

There are just over 2,000 bears living in Finland.

The online stream can be found at yle.fi.

## Cockroach

![German cockroach, also known as the Russian cockroach](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10860317)

Open image viewer

A cockroach is a flat insect. Photo: Manfred Ruckszio / AOP

Cockroaches can come home from summer holiday trips. A cockroach is an insect that is hard to get rid of.

Cockroaches are a nuisance if they get into people's homes. For example, cockroaches can spoil food.

There are not many cockroaches in Finland. But on a trip abroad cockroaches can get into a suitcase. That is how they can end up in anyone's home.

American researchers have studied the effect of insecticides on cockroaches. They used 3 different poisons. The cockroach groups did not die even when they got all the poisons at the same time.

Because cockroaches are hard to get rid of, people should be careful about them. It is good to check suitcases after the trip and to keep the home tidy.
