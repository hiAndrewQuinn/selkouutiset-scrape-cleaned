The number of corona infections is growing. In Finland, almost 1 260 new corona infections have been reported on Thursday. This is the biggest number in one day so far.

During the week, that is 7 days, there have been almost 5900 new corona infections.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that the corona is spreading because not everyone has been vaccinated. Also, contacts have increased, so people meet many other people again.

## New restrictions in Uusimaa

![Standing terraces at the KooKoo ice hall in Kouvola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_344/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88119661964803c06d3/1637239998)

Open image viewer

Standing terraces at an ice hall. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

New corona restrictions will come into force in Uusimaa. At indoor standing terraces, no more than 20 people may come in. Standing terraces are, for example, at sports events or music performances.

Group singing events will also be restricted. Only half of the normal number of spectators may come to group singing events.

The restrictions do not apply to events where a corona pass is required from the audience. If a corona pass is required at an event, a normal number of spectators may come.

The new restrictions will come into force in Uusimaa next Thursday. The restrictions will continue until Christmas Eve.

## Refugee quota

![Families who fled from the north of Afghanistan to the Taliban, camping in a park in Kabul, Afghanistan, on 14 August 2021](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5275,h_2967,x_0,y_420/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-843384611cf506d9b8d/1629287769)

Open image viewer

A refugee camp in Kabul. Photo: Hedayatullah Amid / EPA

The government proposes that Finland raises the refugee quota. If Parliament approves the proposal, Finland will take in 1 500 quota refugees next year. That is 450 more quota refugees than Finland's earlier decision.

Quota refugees are people whom the UN refugee agency chooses from a destination country or a refugee camp. The reason for raising the refugee quota is the crisis in Afghanistan.

1.1 million euros will be added to Finland's budget for next year, so that the refugee quota can be raised.

## The Christmas peace declaration

![The Christmas peace was declared in Turku on 24 December 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9993189)

Open image viewer

The Christmas peace declaration on the Old Market Square is a popular event. Photo: Linus Hoffman / Yle

The Christmas peace declaration will be held as usual this year. The Christmas peace declaration is traditionally held in Turku, on the Old Market Square.

Last Christmas the area was fenced off and the public was not let onto the Market Square because of the corona situation.

Turku said on Thursday that this year people can again watch the Christmas peace declaration on the Market Square. If the corona situation gets worse, the plan may still change.
