# Thursday 26.12.2019 (radio)

Kela paid out nearly 15 billion euros in benefits in 2018. A storm killed people in the Philippines. A bus accident in Indonesia killed 35 people. Many people go to shops to exchange or return Christmas presents.

## Kela benefits

![Kela logo in a window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5258,h_2958,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4965025b3b63b8b566b/1578589144)

Kela looks after the social security of Finns. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

In 2018, Kela paid benefits of nearly 15 billion euros. Kela's task is to look after the social security of Finns.

In 2018, Kela spent the most money on sickness insurance benefits. Pension benefits took the second largest share of money.

Kela pays the most benefits per resident in Northern Finland and Eastern Finland. Less money is spent in Southern Finland and Western Finland.

Most Finns receive benefits from Kela. For example, sickness reimbursements and child benefits are paid to many people.

## Storm in the Philippines

![Residents walked on a beach in Tacloban badly damaged by typhoon Phanfone on 25 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11134702)

The storm destroyed houses in the Philippines. Photo: Bobbie Alota / Lehtikuva

There has been a severe storm in the Philippines in Asia. At least 20 people have died because of the storm.

The storm was a typhoon, that is, a tropical hurricane. Its winds blew at over 50 metres per second.

The storm caused a lot of destruction in the Philippines. It knocked down trees, destroyed houses and cut power lines.

Many Filipinos had to leave their homes to escape the storm. They have been living in temporary shelters.

## Indonesia bus accident

![Rescue staff at the accident site in Pagar Alam, South Sumatra, on 24 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11134790)

Rescuers have searched for people after the bus accident. Photo: Ahmad Tasriq / AFP

New information has come out about the bus accident in Indonesia. The accident happened in Indonesia, in Asia, on Monday.

Indonesian authorities have now said that 35 people died in the accident. Among them were at least 8 children.

In the accident the bus fell off the road into a ravine. The ravine is 150 metres deep, and there is a river at the bottom. Part of the bus was under water.

Rescuers have found at least 13 people who were not killed in the accident but survived. Rescue work has continued all the time since the accident.

## Exchanging gifts

![Christmas present.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-15761549415e24fa0f/1514465364)

After Christmas, many Finns go to shops to exchange or return Christmas presents.

Shops say that people want to exchange clothes, electronics and toys that they got at Christmas, for example. Because of online shopping, people are now used to exchanging and returning goods.

However, selling a Christmas present is difficult for Finns. In a survey by the online shop Tori.fi, 40 per cent of respondents said that selling a Christmas present is impolite. Many think it is more polite to pass a gift on than to sell it.
