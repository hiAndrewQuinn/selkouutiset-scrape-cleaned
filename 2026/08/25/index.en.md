## Physical education in schools

The next topic is physical education in schools.

Schools are getting more exercise than before. The law changed at the beginning of the month so that schools must increase physical activity for children and young people. This means, for example, that there is a program during recess and that lessons are held outdoors.

These pictures are from Haapaniemi School in Kuopio. Sixth grader **Nea Kauppinen** says that exercise helps her cope:

-Maybe then I'll be able to cope better. I'm not so exhausted that I can move around sometimes.

Many Finnish children and young people do not exercise enough. That's why the law was changed so that schools must have more physical activity than before.
