# Friday 23.4.2021 (radio)

Corona restrictions for restaurants have been eased. There is a shortage of teachers in kindergartens and pre-school. Russia is pulling troops back from the Ukrainian border. A new crew has left for the ISS space station. Weather forecast.

## Restaurant restrictions

Corona restrictions for restaurants have been eased in part of Finland.

Restaurant restrictions will be eased to the basic-level restrictions in the Central Finland and South Savo areas. In these areas, restaurants may stay open in the evening until 11 pm, that is 3 hours longer than in the worse corona areas. The sale of alcohol must stop at 10 pm.

The new level of restrictions also means that restaurant restrictions can be removed completely in the Satakunta, Kanta-Häme and Ostrobothnia regions.

## Shortage of kindergarten teachers

![Pre-school lesson, children in a circle on the floor with a teacher.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10410875)

Open image viewer

Teaching in pre-school. Photo: Elisa Kinnunen / Yle

There is a shortage of teachers in kindergartens and pre-school. The shortage of early childhood education teachers has previously been especially bad in the Helsinki area. Now there is a shortage elsewhere in Finland too.

Because there are not enough qualified teachers, people without teaching qualifications are also being hired to work in kindergartens. That is why the quality of teaching may vary.

A few years ago, places for training early childhood education teachers were added. The teacher shortage may ease a little when new students graduate from the extra places.

## Russia pulls troops back from the Ukrainian border

![Three armoured vehicles are on the seashore. On the shore there is a landing ship with its bow open. Soldiers are working on top of the armoured vehicles. Russia has started pulling troops back from the Ukrainian border. Russia says that its soldiers are now moving to train loading points and airports.

Yesterday, Thursday, Russia said that withdrawing troops from Ukraine would take about a week.

The Western military alliance NATO is following Russia's actions and the movements of its troops. The EU has estimated that there are about 100,000 Russian soldiers at the Ukrainian border.

## A new crew travels to the space station

![Docked SpaceX spacecraft](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11651000)

Open image viewer

A spacecraft docks with the ISS space station. Photo: NASA / AFP

A new crew has left Earth for the International Space Station, ISS. The rocket that carries the crew was launched from Cape Canaveral Space Centre in the USA on Friday.

The flight of the rocket to the space station takes about a day. There are 4 astronauts in the rocket. Two of them are American, one is Japanese and one is French. They will spend about half a year on the ISS space station.
