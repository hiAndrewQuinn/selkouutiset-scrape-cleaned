# Monday 17.6.2019 (radio)

A court has made a decision in a discrimination case. Finland's economic growth is slowing down. The government wants more cheap rental flats in Finland. The ice hockey World Championships will be held in Tampere in 2022. Midsummer week is warm.

## Discrimination against a dual citizen

![Conscripts collecting food from the serving line](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10642686)

Open image viewer

A dual citizen could not get a job at the army restaurant. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

The court has made a decision in a discrimination case that has been talked about a lot. The court says that a dual citizen of Finland and Russia was discriminated against when he did not get a job at the army restaurant.

In the court's opinion, the guilty party was not the man who handled the hiring of the worker. The court says the man acted according to an army order. In the court's opinion, however, the army order was illegal.

Because of the order, the job applicant was told that dual citizens cannot be hired to work at the army restaurant. The matter came to light when Yle reported on it 2 years ago.

## Slow economic growth

![People on the café terrace of the Valkea shopping centre in Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10657189)

Open image viewer

The economy is no longer growing as fast as in recent years. Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

Finland's economic growth is slowing down. The Ministry of Finance, which runs Finland's state economic policy, estimates this.

The Ministry of Finance now predicts that Finland's economy will grow at about 1 percent over the next few years. Only a year ago the Ministry of Finance predicted faster economic growth.

For example, it is hard to increase the number of jobs if economic growth slows down. The new government of Finland aims to create more jobs in Finland.

## Rental flats are needed

![Balconies of an apartment block](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10446906)

Open image viewer

It is hard to find a cheap rental flat in the Helsinki area. Photo: AOP

The government wants more cheap rental flats in Finland. The government wants to find ways to prevent overly large rent increases.

The government thinks that rents, for example in the Helsinki area, are so high that people spend too large a share of their wages on housing. It is hard for people to move to the Helsinki area for work because of high rents.

## Ice hockey World Championships to Tampere

![Tampere's Kannen arena in winter ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10836024)

Open image viewer

The new arena in Tampere will be ready shortly before the championships. Photo: Visualisation: SRV/Libeskind/Tomorrow

The ice hockey World Championships will be held in Tampere in 2022. Tampere is the main venue of the World Championships, that is, the world championship tournament.

Some World Championship games will also be played in another city. The second host city will be announced in the autumn.

The ice hockey World Championships will be held in Tampere's new arena. The arena will be ready shortly before the World Championships.

## Warm midsummer week

![Bouquet of flowers](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9688894)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mostphotos

Midsummer week has now begun, and the weather is warm. In the south the week is even hot, meaning the temperature rises above 25 degrees.

In southern Finland the ground is so dry that midsummer bonfires may not be allowed. Midsummer bonfires are usually lit on Friday, when it is Midsummer Eve.

In the north the end of the week is rainier. Rain starts on Thursday, and it may rain a lot.
