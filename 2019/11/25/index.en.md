# Monday 25.11.2019 (radio)

Strikes have disrupted traffic on Monday. The postal strike may last until Christmas. The prime ministers of Finland and Russia have met in Moscow. The polar night has begun in the far north of Finland.

## Monday's support strikes

![Buses at the Nobina depot in Roihupelto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11085526)

Strikes have disrupted traffic on Monday.

The strike has stopped the buses in the Helsinki region, for example. A large part of the buses did not run on Monday, because the drivers were on strike, that is, they did not go to work.

Strikes have also disrupted traffic at airports and in ports. Because of the strike, Finnair cancelled almost 300 flights. Many passenger ships have also stayed in port.

The strikes on Monday were support strikes. Workers from different sectors wanted to support the postal workers, who are on strike because of the labour dispute.

## Postal strike continues

![Post shop in Joensuu](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8079068)

Open image viewer

Post offices are also joining the postal strike. Photo: Esa Huuhko / Yle

The postal strike is getting longer. The postal union has decided to continue the strike until Christmas. The workers therefore plan to stay away from work for another 4 weeks.

The postal union also decided that more workers, for example from post offices, will join the strike. Postal delivery workers are already part of the strike.

The postal union decided on a bigger and longer strike, because no solution has been found to the postal dispute.

The postal strike began 2 weeks ago. Letters and many other postal items are not reaching people now.

## Prime ministers' meeting

![Dmitri Medvedev and Antti Rinne meet in Moscow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11085857)

Dmitri Medvedev and Antti Rinne. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The prime ministers of Finland and Russia have met in Moscow. Prime Ministers Antti Rinne and Dmitri Medvedev met for the first time.

The prime ministers talked about environmental protection, for example. In Moscow, Prime Minister Rinne said that he intends to invite the leaders of the Baltic Sea region to a meeting to discuss environmental protection.

Prime Minister Medvedev was pleased with how economic relations between Russia and Finland have developed.

## Polar night in the north

![Polar night, Nuorgam](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6150035dd7ed27b258b/1574432084)

The sun was seen in the sky over Nuorgam on Monday for the last time before the polar night. Photo: Inger-Elle Suoninen / Yle

The polar night has begun in the far north of Finland.

When the sun set in Nuorgam, the northernmost village in Finland, on Monday, it disappeared from the sky for more than 50 days. The sun will next rise in Nuorgam only on 18 January.

In Finland, the polar night comes in winter everywhere north of the Arctic Circle. The further north a place is, the longer the polar night lasts.

The polar night happens in Finland only in the Lapland region. Elsewhere in Finland, the sun still comes up in the sky every day, even in winter. The days are, however, so short that many people in Southern Finland also talk about the polar night.
