# Thursday 12.3.2020 (radio)

The government now recommends cancelling big public events and trips. Doctors and nurses at Meilahti hospital have been put into coronavirus quarantine. The United States has banned people from mainland Europe from entering the country. Tomorrow is the anniversary of the end of the Winter War. Car safety distances are too small.

## The government gave recommendations to prevent the coronavirus

![The government informs about the coronavirus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11253799)

Minister of Finance Katri Kulmuni, Minister of Family and Basic Services Krista Kiuru, Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Minister of Social Affairs and Health Aino-Kaisa Pekonen held the press conference. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Finnish government has announced new ways to try to prevent the spread of the coronavirus.

The government now recommends that all big public events be cancelled. The government recommends that events with more than 500 people be cancelled until the end of May.

The government also recommends that Finns postpone work trips and holiday trips.

More than 100 coronavirus infections have already been found in Finland.

THL, the National Institute for Health and Welfare, is sending an information package about the coronavirus to everyone in Finland.

## Doctors and nurses in coronavirus quarantine

![The Meilahti hospital area.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11093906)

Open image viewer

Meilahti hospital in Helsinki. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

In Helsinki, doctors and nurses at Meilahti hospital have been put into coronavirus quarantine.

In total 28 doctors and nurses have been put into quarantine, because they have been exposed to the coronavirus. They were exposed because the coronavirus was found in one of the doctors at Meilahti hospital.

The surgeon caught the coronavirus infection on a holiday trip in Austria. He managed to come back to work from the holiday before he fell ill and the infection was noticed.

A few patients and their relatives have also been exposed to the coronavirus.

## Travel ban to the USA for Europeans

![Ronald Reagan National Airport in Washington.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11253337)

Open image viewer

Ronald Reagan National Airport in Washington. Photo: Daniel Slim / AFP

The USA is banning travel from mainland Europe to the USA. The USA is trying in this way to restrict the spread of the coronavirus.

The ban comes into force on Saturday morning, Finnish time. It lasts for one month. The ban does not apply to Britain. Goods traffic can also continue as normal.

Because of the ban, Finnair has cancelled all flights to the USA for one month.

## 80 years since the end of the Winter War

Church bells will ring tomorrow morning across Finland.

The church bells will start ringing a little before 11 o'clock. They remind people that the Winter War ended 80 years ago.

The Ministry of the Interior recommends that Finnish flags be raised across the whole country tomorrow.

## Car safety distances are too small

![Cars in traffic on Kasitie.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10064757)

Open image viewer

Especially in slippery weather it is important that the distances between cars are long enough. Photo: Kalle Mäkelä / Yle

The safety distance is a familiar thing to drivers, but many do not keep to it.

In a survey by Liikenneturva, almost all drivers say that safety distances are too small. However, about 40 percent of drivers say that they themselves keep a long enough safety distance.

The safety distance means the distance left between two cars when they drive one after the other. On a main road the safety distance in metres must be at least the same as the driving speed. In built-up areas the safety distance in metres is half of the driving speed.
