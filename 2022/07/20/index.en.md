## Gas use

![Gas pipes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96325262971a9e16f0b/1654070349)

Gas pipes. Photo: Snorre Wik

The European Union wants to reduce gas use. The EU Commission proposes that member countries reduce gas use by 15 percent between August and March.

The EU Commission wants to save gas, so that there is enough energy also next winter. In winter, a lot of energy is used for heating.

The European Union is afraid that Russia will stop selling gas to Europe completely. Gas has become a subject of dispute between the EU and Russia because of the war in Ukraine.

## Thai berry pickers

![Thai pickers gather blueberries in Luumäki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10312341)

Open image viewer

Thai berry pickers are important for Finnish berry companies. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

A lot of Thai berry pickers are coming to Finland this summer.

The Thai authorities have promised that 4000 pickers may come to Finland. That is 500 pickers more than before.

Berry pickers are needed in Finland now, when blueberries and cloudberries begin to ripen.

## Dogs in the supermarket

![Dog owner Nina Maalahti and Scottish terrier Maya walking in the Iso-Kristiina shopping centre corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6155,h_3462,x_0,y_348/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98383362d67bfd3126a/1658225590)

Open image viewer

Dogs can get into many shopping centres, but not into supermarkets. Photo: Kalle Purhonen / Yle

Customers' dogs cannot get into a supermarket. Almost all supermarkets forbid dogs.

The law no longer forbids customers' dogs in supermarkets. The law changed last year.

Supermarkets still do not want dogs inside, because shops are careful about hygiene. They also remind that some customers may have an allergy.

Service dogs are still allowed in supermarkets. For example, a visually impaired person can go grocery shopping with a guide dog.

## Walrus death

![Walrus next to a boat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2409,h_1354,x_0,y_143/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98406862d7877a4e22d/1658292163)

Open image viewer

The walrus adventure in Finland ended sadly. Photo: Juha Metso / AOP

The famous walrus has died. The walrus died when it was being taken to treatment at the Korkeasaari wildlife hospital.

The walrus was in such poor condition that it could no longer survive anaesthesia and transport to treatment. Korkeasaari says that the walrus was helped as well as possible.

The walrus swam to Finland last week. It came to the Baltic Sea through the Danish straits.

Finns were very interested in the walrus, because there are no walruses living in Finland. Walruses live, for example, in the Arctic Ocean in the north.

Fires have broken out in many European countries because of the heat.

Even in Britain the temperature was over 40 degrees on Tuesday. In London, ground fires broke out, and the fire also spread to buildings.

There have been many fires also in the countries of the Mediterranean.
