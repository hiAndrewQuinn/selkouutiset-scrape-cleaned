# Saturday 4.7.2020 (radio)

There are already more than 11 million corona infections in the world. False information about the coronavirus is circulating on social media. Finns trust the government's competence. The weekend is rainy, but the sun will also show on Sunday.

The TV Clear News has a summer break and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

The coronavirus is still spreading around the world.

More than 11 million coronavirus infections have been found in the whole world. There may be many more infections, because not all infections are found.

More than 520,000 people have died because of the coronavirus. Most corona deaths are in the USA and Brazil.

The Finnish corona situation is calm.

THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, said on Saturday that there are 6 new corona infections in Finland. In total there are over 7,200 corona infections in Finland.

329 people have died of corona in Finland. THL says on Monday whether more corona patients have died over the weekend.

## False information about the coronavirus

![A technician installs a phone network antenna on a house roof in Freiburg.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10810902)

Open image viewer

Does corona spread if the data network is 5G? Photo: AOP

False information about the coronavirus is circulating on social media.

On social media, such as Facebook, it is claimed, for example, that the cause of the corona crisis is 5G technology. 5G is a new kind of powerful data network. Some believe that 5G also spreads the coronavirus. Experts say that this is not true.

In Russia even state television channels report false information about corona. For example, they have said that Americans invented the coronavirus.

Experts say that Finns usually do not believe false information. Finns can also tell which social media sites cannot be trusted.

## Trust in the government's competence

![Sanna Marin in a plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11433301)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Finns trust that Sanna Marin's government knows how to handle Finland's affairs. Sanna Marin began as Prime Minister last December.

About 60 per cent of Finns trust the government's competence. Only about 20 per cent of Finns do not trust that the government can handle the country's affairs well.

Finns trust Marin's government more than the 2 previous governments.

The information is from a study by the Municipal Sector Development Foundation.

## Rainy weekend

The weekend is rainy. On Saturday it rained in various parts of Finland.

On Sunday during the day in Southern Finland it is dry, that is, it does not rain any more. On Sunday evening, however, new rain will come to Finland.

In the south it is about 20 degrees on Sunday, when the sun shines. In the rain the temperature is about 15 degrees. In the north the temperature is under 15 degrees.
