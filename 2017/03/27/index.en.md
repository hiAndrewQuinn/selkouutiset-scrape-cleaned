## Summer time annoys people

![The picture shows the change to summer time with clock hands. At three in the early hours of the night the clocks are moved forward by an hour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7891779)

Open image viewer

Many people think that moving the clocks is not a good idea.  Photo: Anssi Leppänen / Yle

Changing the clocks to summer time annoys many people. Summer time always starts in spring, when the clocks are moved forward by an hour. The last time this was done was at the weekend.

More than 50,000 people are demanding that Parliament, that is the Finnish Parliament, change the law so that normal time continues all year round.

However, the matter is not so easy. Finland is part of the European Union, and EU countries have agreed together on summer time.

In Finland the clocks have been moved to summer time every spring for 36 years already.

## Fire in Vantaa

![This is what the fire scene looked like at night.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9531319)

Open image viewer

The fire in Vantaa produced a lot of smoke. Photo: Seppo Ahava / Yle

There has been a big fire in Vantaa. No one was injured in the fire, but a lot of smoke spread into the environment.

The fire broke out at a waste plant during the night between Sunday and Monday. The smoke was not dangerous, because the fire was burning wood, paper and cardboard. There were no dangerous substances at the waste plant.

However, the smoke disturbed traffic. The fire was near the Jumbo shopping centre, and there is a lot of traffic in the area. There is also a school in the area, which was closed on Monday because of the smoke.

## Pupils' digital strike

![Pupils in a classroom](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9528859)

Open image viewer

During the digital strike, digital devices were used only for teaching, not in free time. Photo: Samuli Holopainen / Yle

Nowadays everyone uses a phone or a tablet almost all the time.

At the international school in Turku, the pupils were on a digital strike. They did not use digital devices in their free time for 10 days. In teaching, digital devices were used as normal.

Some of the pupils did not believe that they could manage without digital devices. But everything went well, and there was also time for other things. For example, pupil Kasper Thomas says that he has read more and has also done other things that he does not usually do.

The pupils and teachers of the international school in Turku also recommend the digital strike to other schools.
