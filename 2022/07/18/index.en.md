# Monday 18.7.2022

THL recommends that municipalities start the 4th corona vaccinations. Package tours are being bought a lot. Heat torments Spain and Portugal. The weather clears up on Tuesday.

## Corona vaccinations

![Corona vaccines ready in a bowl for vaccinations.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_278/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97833262c43b0fa34b3/1658134856)

Corona vaccines ready in a bowl for vaccinations. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

An authority recommends that the 4th corona vaccination is soon given to some people. The recommendation is that vaccination starts already in July or at the beginning of August. The 4th corona vaccination is first given to people over 60 and to people in the risk group for serious corona disease.

In Oulu, Päijät-Häme and South Savo the vaccinations start already this week.

The number of corona infections has risen in various parts of Europe.

People now travel abroad a lot. Travel agencies say that people are buying many package tours abroad. The most popular travel destination is Greece.

People are already buying trips for next autumn and winter. Many now spend more money on a trip than before. Because of corona people have travelled only a little, and many have money left as savings.

## Heat in Europe

![A terrain fire spreads thick smoke in Spain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1438,h_808,x_0,y_126/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98289162d29deccd2b2/1657970348)

Open image viewer

A terrain fire spreads thick smoke in Spain. Photo: Hanne Halonen

The weather is still hot in Spain and Portugal. Over 1000 people have died because of the hot weather.

Also in Britain the weather is hot. In Britain it is even 40 degrees warm, which is a new record in Britain.

In South-western France there are fires in the terrain. Over 16,000 people have been moved away from the fires. Terrain is also burning in Spain, Croatia and Greece, at least.

Finnish women have done well at the Athletics World Championships in the USA.

Wilma Murto was 6th in the pole vault. This is the best placing of a Finnish woman at the Athletics World Championships since 2005.

Silja Kosonen was 7th in the hammer throw. Krista Tervo was 10th in the same competition.

Kristiina Mäkelä jumps in the triple jump final early on Tuesday.

On Tuesday it is at first rainy in many places. Later in the day the rain stops. During the day it is warm, 15...23 degrees.
