# Thursday 22.10.2020 (radio)

Patient data from a psychotherapy centre was stolen online. Finnish regions will get over 100 million euros in coronavirus support. A Hollywood film is being shot in Tampere. Winter speed limits begin. The rain is gradually ending.

## Data breach

![Vastaamo psychotherapy centre in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11608614)

Open image viewer

Vastaamo psychotherapy centre in Helsinki. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

An unusual data breach has happened in Finland.

A criminal stole patient data from the psychotherapy centre Vastaamo. He has already published the data of about 200 patients online.

The criminal is demanding 40 bitcoins from the psychotherapy centre so that he stops publishing the data. Bitcoin is a digital currency, and 40 bitcoins is about 450,000 euros.

The police are investigating the matter.

## Support for regions

![Coder, digitalisation](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10554373)

Open image viewer

The support is used to promote digitalisation, for example. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The regions of Finland will receive over 100 million euros in support. The aim is to help the regions strengthen after the coronavirus crisis. The support can be used to improve, for example, digitalisation and people's skills.

The Ministry of Employment and the Economy announced this on Thursday. The ministry hopes that the business sector will be renewed with the help of the support.

## Hollywood in Tampere

![rileystearns.JPG](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11596261)

Open image viewer

Director Riley Stearns is now directing in Tampere. Photo: Antti Eintola

A Hollywood film is being shot in Tampere. An American film crew came to Tampere because Finland is a safe country in the middle of the coronavirus crisis.

Only a small film crew came from America. That is why many Finns are also taking part in making the film.

The film's name is Dual. Dual is a science fiction film.

The film is directed by Riley Stearns. Stearns says that Tampere has a similar atmosphere to the American city of Seattle.

## Winter speed limits

![Changed winter speed limit sign along main road 5.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9901759)

Open image viewer

On main roads the winter speed limit is 80 kilometres an hour. Photo: Niklas Gabrielsson / Yle

Main roads are switching to winter speed limits. Changing the traffic signs began on Thursday and continues next week.

Cars must drive more slowly in winter than in summer, because road conditions in winter are often poor.

On main roads you may only drive 80 kilometres an hour in winter. On motorways the speed limit is 100 kilometres an hour. You may also drive at 100 kilometres an hour on roads that have a central median.

Winter speed limits are valid until spring.

## The weather

The weather improves at the weekend. Photo: YLE / Jorma Tuominen

The weather is becoming drier.

On Friday there will still be rain in Eastern Finland, and snowfall continues in Northern Lapland.

On Friday drier air flows into Western Finland, and at times the sun can be seen in the sky.

At the weekend it clears up in most of the country, so no more rain will fall.

The temperature in Southern Finland is close to 10 degrees. In Lapland there may be a little frost.
