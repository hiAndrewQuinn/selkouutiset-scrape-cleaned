## Wagner leader died

The private plane of Wagner leader Yevgeny Prigozhin crashed to the ground and caught fire. Photo: @grey_zone / AOP

There has been an air crash in Russia. The mercenary company Wagner says that its leader, Yevgeny Prigozhin, has died in the crash.

Prigozhin's private plane crashed to the ground yesterday on its way from Moscow to St Petersburg. There were 10 people on board in total.

It is not yet known why the plane fell. The channel linked to Wagner on the messaging service Telegram claims that the plane was shot down.

In June Yevgeny Prigozhin led Wagner's rebellion against Russia's leadership. However, he ended the rebellion soon after it began.

## Ukraine's Independence Day

![A boy waves a Ukrainian flag by the roadside, wearing a helmet, as an armoured vehicle drives past.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5731,h_3223,x_0,y_477/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1136911649fce965e369/1688195819)

Russia's attack on Ukraine has lasted 1.5 years already. Photo: Celestino Arce / AOP

Ukraine celebrated its Independence Day today. This is already the second Independence Day in the middle of the war, since 1.5 years have passed since Russia's attack on Ukraine.

In Finland Ukraine's Independence Day has been marked, for example, by flag-raising in Senate Square in Helsinki and at the Ministry for Foreign Affairs. The Ministry for Foreign Affairs building will also be lit in the colours of the Ukrainian flag in the evening.

A citizens' event in support of Ukraine was held in central Helsinki.

## Wilma Murto

![Wilma Murto won a world championship medal as the first Finnish pole vaulter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4563,h_2554,x_0,y_95/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116156064e6ead7a04dd/1692855067)

Open image viewer

Pole vaulter Wilma Murto won bronze at the World Championships. Photo: Christian Petersen/Getty Images

Pole vaulter Wilma Murto has won Finland's first medal in the athletics World Championships in Hungary. Murto won bronze, that is, she was 3rd in the evening's pole vault final.

Wilma Murto's medal is Finland's first medal at world championship level in 8 years. It is also Finland's first ever medal in pole vault at the World Championships.

The last time Finland won a medal at the World Championships was in 2015, when Tero Pitkämäki won bronze in the javelin throw.

## Children's cycling

![A small girl walks her bicycle wearing a cycle helmet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3101,h_1744,x_13,y_108/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115982264e33ec7e06e3/1692614953)

Open image viewer

Finnish children learn to cycle younger than before. Finnish children now learn to cycle on average at a little under 5 years old.

Children learn to cycle earlier because balance bikes have become more common. A balance bike has no pedals; the child pushes along the ground with their feet to gain speed.

A balance bike trains the child's body control and balance. These are skills that are needed when riding a proper bicycle. '

A child can start balance bike riding as soon as they can walk and run.
