# Tuesday 25.10.2016 (radio)

More money is needed for immigration. Finland is rethinking its arms exports. Police cadets have been killed in Pakistan. There are now more than 5.5 million people in Finland. Finland has risen on a list of recommended travel destinations.

## The government asks for more money for immigration

![The Government Palace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9091312)

Open image viewer

The Government Palace in Helsinki.  Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The government is asking parliament for more money to handle immigration matters. An extra 38 million euros is needed this year.  The immigration authorities need more money because more asylum decisions have been appealed than before. The money is needed because asylum seekers live in reception centres while the appeals are being handled. Otherwise, fewer asylum seekers have arrived in Finland this year than the authorities calculated.

## Arms exports to Saudi Arabia are being discussed

![The newest model of the SAKO TRG.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6590464)

Open image viewer

Finland has sold sniper rifles abroad. Photo: Yle

Finland is rethinking the sale of weapons to the Middle East. Defence minister Jussi Niinistö says that the civil war in Yemen affects the granting of arms export licences to Saudi Arabia. Niinistö says that if the situation in Yemen gets worse, Finland will consider the licences again. Saudi Arabia is involved in the war in Yemen.

A new research report says that Finland now exports more weapons to the Middle East than to other regions. This is a problem, because the weapons can be used against ordinary people.

Parliament will get information about the arms deals with Saudi Arabia, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan this week.

## Attack on a police academy in Pakistan

![An injured person is taken to hospital from the attack on the police academy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9249837)

Open image viewer

Islamists attacked a police academy.  Photo: Jamal Taraqai / EPA

A police academy has been attacked in Pakistan. The attackers killed almost 60 police cadets and guards. More than 100 people were wounded in the attack.

The attack happened in the city of Quetta near the Afghan border. There are many supporters of the extremist Islamic Taliban movement in the area.

The Pakistani army often fights extremist groups in the border region.

## There will soon be 6 million Finns

![a child sleeps](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9231032)

Open image viewer

Too few children are being born in Finland.  Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Finland has passed the 5.5 million mark in the number of people. Finland's population is now for the first time closer to 6 than to 5 million.

The population is growing because of immigration from abroad to Finland. A record low number of children are being born in Finland. This year only about 40,000 children have been born in Finland. Researchers say that in the future Finland's population will grow only as a result of immigration.

## Finland is an interesting travel country

![people at the edge of a pool](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9248943)

Open image viewer

A swimming pool complex in the centre of Helsinki.  Photo: Lauri Rotko / Visit Helsinki

Finland has risen for the first time to the top of a list of the most popular travel destinations. The well-known American travel guide Lonely Planet considers Finland the third (3rd) most interesting travel destination next year. Then Finland will turn 100 years old.

The new travel guide writes that in the jubilee year there are many interesting events that make it worth travelling to Finland. The guide praises Finnish cultural events, food and of course the sauna.

On the list, only Canada and Colombia are ahead of Finland.
