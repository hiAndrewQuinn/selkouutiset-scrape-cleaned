## Family reunification becomes easier

![Children of asylum seekers play at the Ruskeasuo reception centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10199568)

Open image viewer

Finland is changing the law on family reunification. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / Yle

Finland is making it easier for underage refugees and their parents to be reunited as a family.

In Finland there is now a law under which family reunification is not possible if a child turns 18 while the case is being handled. This has happened to many underage refugees and their families.

Now the law is being changed. Parents will be able to join their children if the family reunification was started while the child was a minor.

Finland's law must be changed, because the EU Court of Justice orders it.

## Is Macedonia in Greece or in the Republic of Macedonia?

![A statue of Alexander the Great dominates the central square of Skopje, the capital of Macedonia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10196004)

Open image viewer

There is a big statue of Alexander the Great in the central square of Skopje, the capital of Macedonia. Photo: Petri Raivio / Yle

Greece and Macedonia are arguing about the use of the name Macedonia.

Greece says that one of its northern regions is called Macedonia. Macedonia, which used to belong to Yugoslavia, uses the name Republic of Macedonia.

The countries also argue about whose hero Alexander the Great is.

Macedonia wants to become a member of the EU and NATO, but Greece opposes both memberships because of the name dispute. The countries have negotiated about the name dispute, and a solution is expected in the near future.

## A lot of birch pollen in the air now

![Birch blossoms in Espoo on Tuesday 29 April 2014.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7253187)

Open image viewer

The birch pollen season is now at its worst. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The birch pollen season is now at its worst across the whole country. The dry and warm weather of recent days has made life difficult for many people with birch allergies.

Allergy adviser Tiina Tarvainen advises that it is worth using a breathing mask. It is especially necessary if you jog or cycle.

At home it is also worth changing the ventilation filters. You can also add filter cloths to the ventilation windows, says the allergy adviser.

## Tornio is threatened by a severe flood

![Flood on the Torne river on 11 May 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10200359)

Open image viewer

All the rivers in Lapland are at flood level. The picture is from the Torne river in Tornio. Photo: Kimmo Hiltula / Yle

In Tornio there are fears that the Torne river will flood worse than in many decades.

The warm weather and rain may raise the water so high that it goes over the embankments that protect the centre of the town.
