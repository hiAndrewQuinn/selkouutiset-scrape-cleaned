## The proclamation of Christmas peace started the Christmas celebration

![The people listen as the Turku city protocol chief Mika Akkanen proclaims Christmas peace in Turku on Christmas Eve.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10570840)

Christmas peace was proclaimed in Turku. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

The Christmas celebration has begun. In Finland Christmas started at 12 o'clock with the proclamation of Christmas peace.

Proclaiming Christmas peace is a big event in Finland. It is held in Turku. In the proclamation, a text about 700 years old is read out about the start of Christmas.

The text says that Christmas is an important religious feast. The old text also says that people must behave quietly and calmly at Christmas.

The proclamation of Christmas peace is an important tradition for Finns.

## Finnish traditions of Christmas Eve

![ham in the oven](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9994839)

Open image viewer

Ham is still the favourite Christmas food in Finland. Photo: Soila Ojanen / Yle

In Finland there are many traditions on Christmas Eve. Traditions are followed every year.

Many people go to cemeteries. People light many candles, for example on the graves of relatives. On Christmas Eve many also go to church.

Father Christmas visits many homes to hand out presents. People eat Christmas ham or Christmas turkey. There is also a lot of fish as Christmas food.

It was also possible to shop at night before Christmas, when many big supermarkets were open 24 hours. At night people went to buy, for example, presents while the children were asleep.

## The good Christmas Spirit collection helped thousands of families

![Money and Christmas decorations on a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10570777)

Open image viewer

The Good Christmas Spirit collection helped 28,000 families' Christmas. Photo: Pixabay

Before Christmas, the Good Christmas Spirit collection was held in Finland again. Every year the Good Christmas Spirit collection raises money for thousands of families.

The money raised in the collection is used to buy gift cards for families who have no money for Christmas. A gift card can be used to pay for purchases in shops. This year the Christmas collection provided a gift card for 28,000 families.

Families have been able to use the gift cards to buy Christmas food. Thousands of firms and tens of thousands of people took part in the Good Christmas Spirit collection this year.

## Alko open for the first time on Christmas Eve

![People going to Alko on Christmas Eve.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10570831)

Open image viewer

This year you can also shop at Alko on Christmas Eve for the first time. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The alcohol shop Alko has been open on Christmas Eve for the first time this year. The Alko shops were allowed to be open this morning until 12 o'clock.

Before, the Alko shops have been closed on Christmas Eve. This year, however, a new alcohol law came into force. It changed the opening hours of Alko.

At Christmas people buy, for example, red wine and glögi from Alko. Glögi is a drink that is heated before it is drunk. It usually contains alcohol.
