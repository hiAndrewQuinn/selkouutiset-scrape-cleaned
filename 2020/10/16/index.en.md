# Friday 16.10.2020 (radio)

MP Tom Packalén has been infected with the coronavirus. Prime Minister Sanna Marin left an EU meeting because of Packalén's coronavirus infection. Disposable face masks can also be washed. The coronavirus dominates the US presidential election. It will snow in Lapland at the weekend.

## Coronavirus in Parliament

MP Tom Packalén has been infected with the coronavirus. Photo: Lehtikuva

There is 1 coronavirus infection in the Finnish Parliament. The coronavirus infection of MP Tom Packalén was reported on Friday.

Packalén is well and has only mild symptoms. Packalén is in quarantine at home.

Packalén's coronavirus infection has also affected Prime Minister Sanna Marin's work. Marin left a meeting of EU leaders in Brussels because of Packalén's coronavirus infection.

This week Marin has been in the same premises as Packalén. That is why Prime Minister Marin decided to return to Finland and go for a coronavirus test. Marin intends to stay at home in quarantine.

## Washing a face mask

![Surgical face mask](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_291/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7037185f228e9ae45cc/1596101100)

Open image viewer

Disposable surgical face mask. Photo: Jenni Joensuu / Yle

Disposable face masks can also be washed. This is what a new Finnish study says.

A disposable face mask can last up to 10 washes. A face mask can be boiled, or it can be washed in a washing machine.

In the study, disposable surgical face masks were washed. Surgical face masks can be bought in supermarkets and pharmacies. The packaging often says "surgical mouth-nose protection".

## USA elections

![Donald Trump speaking at a debate](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11598023)

Open image viewer

President Trump has been criticised for how he has handled the coronavirus crisis. Photo: AFP

The coronavirus also dominates the US presidential election. The coronavirus was again in focus at the presidential candidates' election events.

President Donald Trump said at an election event that many people have caught the coronavirus even though they wear face masks. Trump does not often wear a face mask. Trump is the Republican presidential candidate.

The Democratic candidate Joe Biden said at his own election event that, because of Trump, many people think a face mask is not important. Biden thinks Trump has handled the whole coronavirus crisis badly.

The US presidential election is on 3 November.
