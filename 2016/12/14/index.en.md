## The fighting in Aleppo

![A Syrian soldier carrying a wounded woman in Aleppo on 12 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9352514)

In Aleppo, many are waiting to get out of the city. Photo: SANA / EPA

In the city of Aleppo in Syria, people are still waiting for the fighting to end.

Russia said already yesterday that the Syrian army already controls all of Aleppo and the fighting can be stopped. Russia has supported the Syrian army in the fight against the rebels.

Now the Syrian government and the rebels blame each other for the fighting continuing.

In Aleppo there are thousands of people who are waiting to be able to leave the city.

The war in Aleppo has gone on for over 4 years.

## The cities' Christmas lights

![Rovaniemi's Christmas look seen from Lordi Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9352902)

Open image viewer

Christmas lights in Rovaniemi. Photo: Raimo Torikka / Yle

Finnish cities spend different amounts on Christmas lights.

Helsinki and Tampere spend the most money. They have set aside 150,000 euros for Christmas lights this year.

Turku spends almost 60,000 euros on Christmas lights, and Santa Claus's city Rovaniemi almost 50,000 euros.

Many cities set aside only a few thousand euros for Christmas lights. For example, the big city of Oulu spends only 1200 euros on Christmas lights.

## Men's free time

![Men casting from a rock](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9017762)

Open image viewer

Men's own pastime. Photo: Kare Lehtonen/Yle

Men often spend their free time with other men. Men do things together, for example fishing, hunting or cars.

Professor Timo Airaksinen says that a group of friends frees a man. With friends, men can talk about what they want and be as they want.

That is important, because the Finnish woman demands a lot, Airaksinen says. The woman controls how the man may talk and what kind of clothes the man may wear.

Nowadays, however, men have only a little free time. Work and family take up so much time, Airaksinen says.

## Clothes from wood fibre

![Fibre flowing into water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9325277)

Open image viewer

Durable yarn can be made from wood fibre. Photo: Yle

Clothes can also be made from wood.

New methods are being developed in Finland to make yarn from wood fibre.

With Aalto University's new technique, really durable fibre yarn can be made. It can be used for clothes and, for example, for the tyres of Formula cars.

VTT, the Technical Research Centre, is also making fibre yarn from wood. Textile factories can make fabric from it even without new kinds of machines.

In the future, fibre yarn will perhaps be made in the same place as other forest industry products.
