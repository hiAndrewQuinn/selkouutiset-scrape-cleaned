# Friday 26.6.2020 (radio)

MP Juha Mäenpää will not go to court over racist speech. There are 19 new corona infections in Finland. Few children have died of corona. Strawberries are ripening quickly. The weather will cool a little at the weekend.

The TV Clear News has a summer break and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Parliament voted on Mäenpää

![MP Juha Mäenpää (Finns Party) in a plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki on 24 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11416232)

MP Juha Mäenpää in Parliament. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

There has been an unusual vote in Parliament, the Finnish parliament. MPs voted on whether one of the MPs can be prosecuted in court.

The vote concerned MP Juha Mäenpää. Many people think that Mäenpää used racist language in Parliament when he compared asylum seekers with invasive species. An invasive species is a plant or animal that does not belong in Finland.

Parliament decided that Mäenpää will not be prosecuted in court for racist speech. An MP can be prosecuted in court only if a large part of the MPs give permission for it. For example, Finns Party MPs voted against giving permission. Juha Mäenpää himself is also a Finns Party member.

There are 19 new corona infections in Finland. THL has reported about 7,200 corona infections in total.

THL also reported 1 new corona death on Friday. In total, 328 people have now died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

## Few corona deaths among children

![Children in a school yard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11419301)

Open image viewer

Children in Rome, Italy, during the corona era. Photo: Alberto Pizzoli / AFP

Children rarely die because of the coronavirus. A new European study shows that corona deaths among children are very rare.

The study included 600 children who had a corona infection. Only 4 of the children died of a disease caused by the coronavirus.

Most of the children had only mild symptoms from the coronavirus. 10 per cent of the children needed intensive care because of the coronavirus.

## Strawberries are ripening

![Ukrainian strawberry pickers at Holopainen farm in Lemi, 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10862632)

Open image viewer

Strawberry pickers have a lot of work now. Photo: Kari Saastamoinen / Yle

Strawberries are ripening quickly now. The reason is the hot weather.

Strawberry growers are afraid that the strawberries may ripen even too quickly. Not all strawberries may be picked in time. Strawberries are good if they are picked as soon as they are ripe.

Many strawberry farms have too few pickers. Many pickers come from abroad, but because of corona restrictions fewer pickers have come this summer than usual.

## The weather cools a little

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11419917)

Open image viewer

The temperature is falling, but the weekend is still warm. Photo: Anniina Valtonen

At the weekend the weather cools a little.

In Northern Finland the temperature falls already on Saturday, but in Southern Finland it may still be up to 30 degrees on Saturday.

On Sunday the weather cools a little in the south too, and the temperature is between 20 and 25 degrees.
