# Tuesday 12.2. (radio)

The trial of Catalonia's leaders has begun in Spain. A far-right party is doing well in Estonia. Singer Olli Lindholm has died. Today, Tuesday, is Sign Language Day.

## The trial of Catalonia's leaders has begun

![This is what the courtroom in Madrid looked like when the trial of the Catalan separatists began after ten o'clock local time.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10642724)

A trial has begun in Spain in which the leaders of Catalonia are accused. Catalonia is a region that belongs to Spain.

Catalonia has its own language, culture and flag.

A year ago, the leaders of Catalonia wanted Catalonia to be independent. A referendum on Catalan independence was held. Spain's view is that the referendum in Catalonia was illegal.

The leaders of Catalonia are accused of rebellion. A conviction for rebellion can bring a 20-year prison sentence.

A demonstration is being held in the Catalan capital Barcelona on Tuesday evening. The demonstration defends the accused leaders of Catalonia.

## Support for the far right is high in Estonia

![The far-right EKRE has grown in a few years into Estonia's third-largest party.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10641956)

Open image viewer

Support for the far-right Ekre party has risen in Estonia. Photo: EKRE

Parliamentary elections will be held in Estonia in less than 3 weeks.

An opinion poll says that the far-right EKRE party will get a good result in the election. The support for the EKRE party may be as high as 17 percent.

4 years ago the EKRE party got into the Estonian parliament for the first time. The support for the far-right party in Estonia has risen to a record level and at a record speed.

## Singer Olli Lindholm has died

![Olli Lindholm](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10642652)

Open image viewer

Singer Olli Lindholm of the band Yö has died. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Singer Olli Lindholm has died. He was 54 years old. Lindholm had a seizure last night.

Olli Lindholm sang in the band Yö, which is very popular in Finland. The band Yö was formed almost 40 years ago.

Olli Lindholm also appeared on television. He was a coach on the singing competition The Voice of Finland.

Olli Lindholm was born in Pori. Flags have been flown at half-mast in Pori.

## Today, Tuesday, is Sign Language Day

![Mikaela Tillander and Thomas Sandolm in the studio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10590736)

Open image viewer

Today, Tuesday, is Sign Language Day. Sign Language Day was celebrated for the first time 10 years ago.

Sign language is the language used by deaf people. Sign language is also used, for example, by people who are hard of hearing, by relatives of sign language users, and by interpreters.

In Finland there are 2 sign languages: Finnish Sign Language and Finland-Swedish Sign Language. There are several hundred different sign languages in the world.

On Sign Language Day there are celebratory flags flying in many places around Finland.
