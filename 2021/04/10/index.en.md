# Saturday 10.4.2021 (radio)

There were demonstrations against corona restrictions in Helsinki. There is a shortage of microchips. Prince Philip is remembered in Britain. It will be dry on Sunday.

## Demonstration in Helsinki

There were demonstrations in Helsinki on Saturday. People protested against corona restrictions.

The police were notified of a demonstration by 2 people, but a few hundred people came to the centre of Helsinki.

However, the police prevented a large demonstration, because large gatherings are banned because of corona.

The police arrested many people and took people away from the scene.

However, the number of corona infections is slowly falling. Over the last 2 weeks, fewer new corona infections have been found than during the 2 weeks before.

## Shortage of microchips

![Photo collage with a car, a console game controller, a smartphone, a forest machine and a microchip.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11867949)

Open image viewer

Microchips are needed in many devices. Photo: Yle, AOP, image editing: Samuli Huttunen / Yle

There is a shortage of microchips around the world.

Microchips are needed, for example, in the manufacture of cars. For example, car makers Volvo and Ford are reducing production because they are not getting enough microchips. That is why car order times have become longer.

Microchips are also needed, for example, in making games consoles. That is why video game fans now have to wait a long time for new devices.

The cause of the situation is corona. Because of corona, the manufacture of microchips has slowed down. However, customers have bought more electronics during the corona time than usual.

## Prince Philip remembered

![Soldiers fire ceremonial salutes with old cannons in London.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11878456)

Open image viewer

Here, ceremonial salutes are being fired for Prince Philip in London. Photo: Glyn Kirk / AFP

Prince Philip has been remembered in Britain. Philip died on Friday.

The prince has been remembered with gun salutes, which were fired in various parts of Britain. In addition, flags are at half-mast, and a moment of silence has been held at sporting events.

Prince Philip was the husband of Queen Elizabeth. Philip was 99 years old.

## Sunday's weather

Photo: Yle

On Sunday, it will be dry across most of the country, which means the rain stops. The sun may also shine.

On Sunday evening, clouds will move into Southern Finland, and later rain showers too.

On Monday, rain showers will come to Western Finland and Central Finland. In Central Finland it may also snow.

The temperature is below 10 degrees at the weekend, but on Monday the weather will warm up in the south. In Southern Finland the temperature may rise above 10 degrees on Monday.
