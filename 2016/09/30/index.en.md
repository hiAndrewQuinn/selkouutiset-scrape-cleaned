# Friday 30.9.2016 (radio)

President Sauli Niinistö says that Finland kept the missile explosion test secret because the Netherlands asked it to. The Court of Appeal lengthened Jari Aarnio's sentence. The German Deutsche Bank is in difficulties. ESA destroyed the Rosetta probe.

## The President was disappointed in the Netherlands

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9203509)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Yle

President Sauli Niinistö is disappointed in the Dutch authorities in the investigation of the Malaysian plane crash. President Sauli Niinistö says that the Netherlands asked Finland to keep the BUK missile test secret. Now the Dutch have themselves talked about the test in public.

It is about the Malaysian plane that was brought down in Eastern Ukraine 2 years ago. Almost 300 people died in the crash, most of them Dutch. The investigators of the crash say that the plane was brought down by a Russian BUK missile and that the missile was fired by Ukrainian rebels who are supported by Russia.

Finland also has Russian BUK missiles. The Netherlands asked Finland to do an explosion test with a BUK missile and give the results to the Netherlands. Finland agreed, even though the purchase agreement for the missiles forbids giving information about the missile to others.

## Aarnio's sentence got longer

![Jari Aarnio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8988743)

Open image viewer

Jari Aarnio. Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

The former head of the Helsinki drug police, Jari Aarnio, got 3 years in prison at the Court of Appeal.

The Court of Appeal lengthened the sentence of the lower court. Aarnio was convicted of taking bribes, committing fraud and abusing his office. The court says that Aarnio was the buyer and the seller at the same time when the Helsinki police bought surveillance devices. Aarnio was secretly involved in the company Trevoc, which sold the devices.

Drug police officer Jari Aarnio is also accused of drug dealing. The court has not yet given a decision in the drug case. Aarnio says that he is innocent.

## A giant bank in difficulties

![The German stock exchange.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9202534)

Open image viewer

Deutsche Bank's share price has fallen 20 percent in recent weeks. Photo: Arne Dedert / EPA

The big German bank Deutsche Bank is in trouble. It has many difficulties, its share price has collapsed, and some even fear that it will collapse.

Finnish banks and pension companies do not have very many investments in Deutsche Bank. They say that Deutsche Bank collapsing is a small risk for Finland but a big risk for the international banking system.

## Rosetta's journey ended

![A drawing of Rosetta and the Philae lander that separated from it, on the way to comet Churyumov-Gerasimenko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7630236)

Open image viewer

The Rosetta probe (left) and the comet. Photo: ESA / ATG Medialab; ESA / Rosetta / Navcam

The European Space Agency ESA has destroyed its Rosetta probe on the surface of a comet in space.

The Rosetta probe orbited the comet for two years and sent a lot of information about the comet to Earth. The information helps researchers understand the history of the solar system and perhaps even the origin of life.

The European Space Agency ESA started the Rosetta project already in the 1980s, and the probe was sent into space in 2004. Now Rosetta has done its work, and contact with it was lost in the afternoon when it crashed into the surface of the comet.
