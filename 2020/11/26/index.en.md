# Thursday 26.11.2020 (radio)

New coronavirus restrictions. The number of coronavirus patients is growing. Travel advice for regions. Immigrant families. The bears of Korkeasaari.

## Coronavirus situation

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin and HUS chief medical officer Markku Mäkijärvi at the STM and THL situation briefing on the coronavirus situation at the government's press room in Helsinki on Thursday 26 November 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11667253)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin and HUS chief medical officer Markku Mäkijärvi spoke at the press briefing on Thursday. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The coronavirus situation has got worse in Finland. On Thursday almost 500 new coronavirus infections were reported in Finland.

The government is asking the municipalities to bring in more coronavirus restrictions.

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health recommends that public places, for example sports facilities, spas, indoor play parks and the shared spaces of shopping centres, should be closed.

## Coronavirus situation at the HUS hospitals

![Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11659088)

Open image viewer

The HUS area has the most coronavirus infections in Finland. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The number of coronavirus patients is growing quickly in hospitals in Helsinki and Uusimaa. The director of the hospital district says that coronavirus infections must be made to decrease. If many more coronavirus patients come, the hospitals in the Uusimaa and Helsinki areas will not be able to treat all the coronavirus patients.

Besides Uusimaa, the area of the Päijät-Häme hospital district is also in the stage of coronavirus spread, which means that infections are spreading quickly.

There are 8 areas and 6 municipalities in the stage of accelerating disease.

The Varsinais-Suomi hospital district recommends that people from Varsinais-Suomi do not travel to Uusimaa.

The Kanta-Häme hospital district also recommends that people from the area do not travel to areas where the coronavirus is spreading, or arrive from them.

The recommendations are meant to stop coronavirus infections from spreading and getting worse.

## Children with immigrant backgrounds

![Young immigrants in the Itäkeskus shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8495363)

Open image viewer

Young immigrants in a shopping centre. Photo: Milla Vahtila / Yle

In Finland, children whose parents have a foreign background have it harder than other children. For example, they do worse at school.

Children whose parents have a foreign background need child welfare services more often than other children. In many immigrant families there is little money, and poverty affects the children.

The children's situation has been studied in a wide-ranging study.

## The bears of Korkeasaari

![Two bears rest at the entrance of a den.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11667304)

Open image viewer

The bears of Korkeasaari. Photo: Korkeasaari Zoo / Mari Lehmonen

The bears at Korkeasaari Zoo will soon go into hibernation. The Korkeasaari keepers say that the bears are already tired. They move around only a little now and lie down a lot in the winter den.

The Korkeasaari bears have eaten a lot of berries, fruit and meat in the autumn. The food will help them get through the long winter.

The Korkeasaari bears will start hibernating in the next few days.
