# Wednesday 4.1.2023 (radio)

Fuel costs less than before. No tax on train tickets at the start of the year. There are many summer jobs on offer. The weather is fair.

## Fuel price

![People refuelling their cars at a petrol station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_321/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105531163b4218348a6a/1672749514)

Petrol is now cheaper in Finland than before. Experts estimate that the price of petrol will also stay low in the near future.

In December the average price of both petrol and diesel fell below 2 euros.

The price of oil has fallen in the world. The price of fuel in Finland follows fairly closely how the price of oil changes in the world.

## Train tickets

![A woman waits for a train at Tampere railway station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4034,h_2269,x_0,y_209/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-104714263980f81cfff8/1670911048)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

Train tickets are cheaper than usual until the end of April. The reason is a tax reduction. VR says that all train tickets are sold tax-free until the end of April.

Parliament approved a reduction of value added tax on passenger travel for the first part of the year. Parliament wants to ease people's lives as prices generally rise. The reduction in value added tax applies, in addition to trains, to bus, flight, ferry and taxi journeys.

![An ice cream cone and a hand holding it, wearing a rubber glove.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2014,h_1134,x_0,y_47/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98189462cfd28519cea/1657787068)

There are more summer jobs on offer for young people than before.

For example, on the job search site Duunitori there have been published over 30 percent more summer job adverts than last year.

Summer jobs are especially available in restaurants, in social and health care, in industry and in the construction sector.

## Trips to Tallinn

![A lit and decorated Christmas tree in Tallinn's Town Hall Square surrounded by people.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2268,x_0,y_94/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105541663b43f6024ada/1672757866)

Open image viewer

Photo: Rain Kooli / Yle

Finns already travel to Tallinn almost as much as before the coronavirus pandemic.

Last year Finns stayed overnight in Tallinn about 83 percent of the 2019 figure. In 2019 Estonia had a record number of tourists.

Visit Tallinn says that more Finnish families now visit Estonia than before. The decision to travel is often made quickly.

## The weather

![Snowy landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105572863b55e70c38a5/1672831290)

On Thursday the weather is fair in almost the whole of Finland. In places it is clear, that is, there are not many clouds in the sky.

In the south the frost tightens and the temperature is generally between -10 and -20 degrees. It is even colder in Lapland.
