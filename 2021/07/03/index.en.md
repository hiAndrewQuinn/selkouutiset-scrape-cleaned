## Motorhomes are bought a lot

![A motorhome parked.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2880,h_1620,x_0,y_297/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6981925f087bac1dc7e/1594392840)

Motorhomes are now very popular. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Motorhomes are being sold a lot in Finland now.

Nearly 40 percent more motorhomes have been registered than in 2019. 60 percent more caravans have been registered than in 2019, that is, before corona.

Soon there will be more motorhomes than caravans.

Finland has the most motorhomes in Europe, when the number is compared with the population.

## Shelters are too few

![A shelter room for families with children has, among other things, a cot and a changing table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6410365e41701c3a6a0/1581348684)

In the shelter room for families with children there is, for example, a cot and a changing table. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Many people have too long a distance to a shelter, says the National Institute for Health and Welfare.

Anyone who has experienced violence or the threat of it can get help at a shelter. Shelters are open all the time, every day of the year.

Because there are too few shelters, remote shelters are hoped for as help. The need is greatest in Northern Finland.

A remote shelter means that, for example, in connection with a health centre there are premises and staff who help.

Finland's only remote shelter is in Kajaani, and its central unit is the Oulu shelter.

Finland's northernmost shelter is in Rovaniemi. From Utsjoki it is about 500 kilometres to there. Utsjoki is Finland's northernmost municipality.

The Shelter Act and the Istanbul Convention say that shelters must be easy to get to. The Istanbul Convention regulates violence and domestic violence directed at women.

## There are dangers in cycling

![Heikki Mäkelä cycles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9645420)

Open image viewer

Finland's roads do not have safe cycle paths. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

Dangerous situations between cyclists and cars are increasing. Cycling is becoming more popular, and country roads are narrow.

The risk of an accident is greatest when a car overtakes a bicycle. Motorists in Finland often overtake cyclists illegally close. The Finnish Cyclists' Federation demands a clarification of the law.

The federation says that in many European countries the law sets a distance of at least 1.5 metres for overtaking. The federation wants a similar law in Finland.

## Helsinki Pride culminated in a concert

![Participants in the Pride parade.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10854786)

Open image viewer

Because of corona, a Pride parade like this could not be organised this year. Photo: Teemu Silván / Yle

Helsinki Pride, Finland's largest human rights event, has culminated in a virtual concert.

Yle Teema showed the concert live in the afternoon. It can be watched on Areena afterwards too. The performers at the concert included, for example, Sanni, Yeboyah and Benjamin.

During Pride week this year, especially the fact that homosexuality was removed from the disease classification in Finland 40 years ago is remembered. Homosexuality was removed from the Criminal Code 50 years ago.

Because of corona, Pride week events have mostly been organised virtually.
