## Stabbing in France

A serious stabbing has happened in France. A man attacked children in a playground.

The man stabbed people with a knife. Four small children were injured in the stabbing, and 2 of them got serious injuries. In addition, 1 adult was injured.

The police caught the stabber. The stabber is a Syrian refugee. The police do not know what the reason for the act was.

## Haavisto as presidential candidate

![Pekka Haavisto walks near Senate Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3984,h_2241,x_0,y_83/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11264726481920763593/1686213185)

Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Pekka Haavisto is aiming for the job of president. Presidential elections will be held in Finland at the beginning of next year.

Haavisto is 65 years old, and in recent years he was Finland's foreign minister.

Haavisto's party is the Greens. However, he will become the presidential candidate of a voters' association. Anyone who wants to can take part in the voters' association's activities.

Finland's current president, Sauli Niinistö, cannot continue in the job of president any more. He has been president for almost 12 years already. That is the maximum time in the job of Finland's president.

![Graph shows the results of the latest party support poll.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2544,h_1431,x_0,y_200/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-112569864803e8e437bc/1686132899)

Photo: Nanna Sarkka / Yle

There have been no big changes in party support since the parliamentary elections. The parliamentary elections were held about 2 months ago, in early April.

The winner of the election, the National Coalition, is also the largest party in the new poll. The National Coalition's support is now over 22 per cent.

In the poll, the second largest party is the Finns Party. It was also the second largest party in the election.

The National Coalition and the Finns Party are now negotiating about a new government. RKP and the Christian Democrats are also involved.

The government talks have already gone on for many weeks. The goal is to finish the government talks during June.

## The smoke problem in North America

![The silhouette of Manhattan in New York covered in yellowish smoke. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8256,h_4644,x_0,y_860/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1125822648061763d928/1686135442)

New York covered in smoke. Photo: Erik Pendzich / AOP

In the USA, people are being warned about dirty air. There is a lot of smoke in the air, which comes all the way from Canada. There are big forest fires in Canada.

US authorities are warning more than 100 million people about smoky air. For example, the mayor of New York says that it is good for city residents to stay inside.

On Friday, the weather is dry, that is, no rain falls. The sun also comes out. It is cloudier in the north.
