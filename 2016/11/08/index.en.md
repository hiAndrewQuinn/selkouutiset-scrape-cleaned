# Tuesday 8.11.2016 (radio)

Americans are voting for a new president. The Tampere tramway is good news for Kainuu too. Iraqi asylum seekers have been charged with manslaughter. In the snowstorm in Southern Finland it is worth driving really carefully

## The United States is choosing a new president

![Clay Smith votes in the village of Dixville Notch.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9278780)

Clay Smith was the first voter of election day in the village of Dixville Notch in New Hampshire on Tuesday. Photo: Herb Swanson / EPA

The United States is voting in the presidential election.

The first polling stations opened on the east coast of the United States on Tuesday morning at 7 o'clock Finnish time. The last polling stations to close are in Alaska, on Wednesday morning at 8 o'clock Finnish time.

A record number, 46 million Americans, have already voted in advance.

In the last opinion polls before the election, the Democratic candidate Hillary Clinton led the Republican candidate Donald Trump. However, the difference between the candidates was small.

The result of the election will be known on Wednesday morning, perhaps after about 6 o'clock.

## The Tampere tramway is good news for Kainuu too

![A tram at the Central Square - artist's impression](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9208858)

Open image viewer

Tram traffic will start in Tampere in 2021. Photo: Transtech

A tramway will be built in Tampere. The Tampere city council decided on it late on Monday evening after a long discussion and a vote.

Construction of the tramway will start next year. First the tramway will be built from the Hervanta district to the centre and to Tampere Central Hospital. Later the tramway will be built from the centre to the Lentävänniemi district.

The tramway costs  almost 300 million euros.

Tram traffic is meant to start in 2021.

The Tampere tramway is good news for Kainuu too, because the trams will perhaps be built at the Transtech factory in Kajaani.

## Iraqi men were charged with manslaughter

![Grave candles on a pedestrian and cycle path in Otanmäki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9161746)

Open image viewer

A dead man was found in Otanmäki in early September. Photo: Julia Sieppi / Yle

Two Iraqi asylum seekers are being charged with crimes that happened in Otanmäki, Kajaani, last September.

The men are accused of, for example, manslaughter and aggravated robbery.

A dead man was found in Otanmäki in early September. In addition, another man from Otanmäki was robbed.

The police say that the dead man had been hit with a fist and a knife, and he had been kicked. The man's mobile phone and bank cards had also been taken.

## Driving conditions are poor in Southern Finland

![traffic on Mannerheimintie in a snowstorm](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9278520)

Open image viewer

The roads in Southern Finland are slippery and the snow is blowing. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

In the capital region heavy snowfall has made traffic difficult in recent days.

The police say that at least 70 small crashes happened on Monday. Big lorries have also been slipping at junctions and on uphill slopes. In addition, VR has cancelled local train services because of the heavy snowfall.

Up to 15 centimetres of new snow may fall in Southern Finland on Tuesday.

It is worth being careful in traffic, because the snow is blowing and the roads are slippery. Pavements can also be really slippery.
