# Wednesday 20.5.2020 (radio)

The government has told about new corona guidelines for people over 70. Posti plans to lay off workers. The number of foreign-language residents is growing in Finland. Many Finns live alone.

## Corona guidelines for older people

![Elderly person walking in the city](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4346,h_2445,x_0,y_68/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-7891858dd40fa8cb06/1548937485)

Older people can also go outside during the corona times. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The government has told in more detail about the new corona guidelines for older people, that is, people over 70 years old.

Older people may already meet relatives and friends, if they are careful. The best place to meet is outside, and it is good to keep a 2-metre safety distance.

However, older people must not meet people who have respiratory symptoms. It is still good to avoid unnecessary meetings.

The government reminds that older people must move about and look after their health. For example, it is not worth cancelling doctor's appointments.

## Posti's layoffs

![Postman on Iso Roobertinkatu outside the Black Door pub.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4980,h_2801,x_0,y_459/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6745505ebaba773fe97/1589295946)

Open image viewer

The amount of letter post has decreased. Photo: Susanne Salin / Yle

Posti plans to lay off workers. Posti will start negotiations on layoffs with the workers.

During a layoff, workers have no work, and they do not get pay. Posti's layoffs concern workers who have administrative tasks. Posti does not intend to lay off workers in parcel and online shop jobs.

Posti says that the corona crisis has reduced the number of letters. For example, companies now send fewer invoices and adverts than before. Therefore Posti is planning not to deliver letter post on Fridays in the summer.

## The number of corona infections

![Coronavirus test sample](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3830,h_2154,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6787675ec4e50be7dfb/1589962087)

The number of coronavirus infections has risen a little.

In Finland there are now about 6,450 people who have caught the coronavirus.

304 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

The data are from the Institute for Health and Welfare.

## Finland's foreign-language speakers

![Instructions for foreign-language visitors to a swimming hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8382882)

Open image viewer

There are already many foreign-language residents in Finland. Photo: Jarmo Nuotio / Yle

The number of foreign-language residents is growing in Finland. Foreign-language speakers means people whose mother tongue is not Finnish, Swedish or Sami.

In 2040 there will be more than 2 times as many foreign-language speakers as now. That means the number of foreign-language speakers will grow by more than 500,000 people.

Finland gets more foreign-language residents especially because of immigration. The number of foreign-language residents grows most in big cities.

The data are from a report by the Association of Finnish Municipalities.

## One-person homes

![At a K-store in Seinäjoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11087177)

Open image viewer

There are many one-person households. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Many Finns live alone. Over 1.2 million Finns lived alone last year. The number is 30,000 higher than a year earlier.

Living alone has become more common especially among young and old people. Living alone has decreased only among people of about 50 years old.

The data are from Statistics Finland.
