## Antti Lindtman

![Antti Lindtman speaking at a press briefing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_265/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1100240643d2cb3d68d5/1681731283)

Antti Lindtman is seeking the post of SDP chairman. Lindtman is an MP.

The 40-year-old Lindtman is from Vantaa. He is one of the well-known politicians of the SDP, that is, the Social Democrats.

The new SDP chairman will be elected in September. At that time the SDP will hold its party congress.

The current SDP chairman is Sanna Marin. She does not want to continue in the post of chairman any more.

A new Parliament was elected in Finland in early April. Soon the country's government will also change.

In the parliamentary elections the SDP was only the 3rd largest party. That is why the SDP no longer gets the post of Prime Minister.

## Corona

![Face mask and hand sanitiser bottle on a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4020,h_2261,x_0,y_359/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9349146242e190ab1a0/1648550385)

Finns are no longer afraid of corona.

Most Finns no longer wear a face mask or keep a safety distance. This is what a new citizens' survey says.

People trust that vaccinations give protection against corona. Corona has become one disease among others.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, also says that people can treat corona in the same way as other infectious diseases. A sick person should stay at home.

## Sweden's military exercise

![A Finnish soldier aims his weapon in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_266/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96868062a47c530f50c/1654947435)

A big military exercise has started in Sweden. Finland is also taking part in the exercise.

More than 10 partner countries of Sweden take part in the exercise. They include, for example, the USA and Britain.

A total of 26,000 soldiers are training to defend Sweden. The exercise focuses on southern Sweden.

## Olkiluoto 3

![In the middle is Olkiluoto reactor 1, on the right reactor 2. In the background the third reactor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1099814643ba86f60502/1718549016)

Open image viewer

Olkiluoto nuclear power plant. Photo: Jari Pelkonen / Yle

Finland has received more nuclear power, that is, atomic power.

Olkiluoto's new nuclear reactor, Olkiluoto 3, has started producing electricity at full power.

The new nuclear reactor is important. It produces about 14 percent of Finland's electricity.

Olkiluoto 3 affects, for example, the price of electricity. It is expected that the price of electricity will no longer vary as much as before.

The nuclear reactor should have been completed 14 years ago, but the big construction project has taken a lot of time.

The plan is that Olkiluoto 3 will operate for at least 60 years.

The price of Olkiluoto 3 is 5.8 billion euros. It is perhaps the most expensive building in the world.

## The weather

![Two people sitting enjoy the sunshine by the water, photographed from behind.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5109,h_2869,x_0,y_419/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-109676164302c9558e26/1681210920)

On Tuesday the sun shines across almost the whole country. In places, however, the weather is cloudy or partly cloudy.

In the south it is more than 10 degrees warm. In the north the temperature is between 5 and 10 degrees.

On Wednesday warm and sunny weather continues across most of the country. In Lapland it may rain a little on Wednesday.
