# Tuesday 3.1.2017 (radio)

The retail company Kesko may cut jobs. There is now a lot of traffic on the Finnish-Russian border because of the Russian holiday period. In Finland cars are being tested with a black box for monitoring. Finns think that politicians must not lie in election campaigns. Many people visited art museums last year. In Inari it was almost 35 degrees of frost on Tuesday.

## Negotiations about jobs at Kesko

![Siwa at Pitkänlahdenkatu in Vaasa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9385505)

Kesko bought Siwa and Valintatalo last year. Photo: Jarkko Heikkinen / Yle

The retail company Kesko may cut jobs. Kesko has started negotiations with employees about the future of jobs. The negotiations concern almost 600 employees.

Kesko bought more shops last year. It bought Siwa and Valintatalo. Now Kesko is finding out whether all of them can continue operating. It is not yet known how many employees may be left without a job.

The trade union for employees hopes that Kesko can find new work for employees who may lose their jobs.

## Holidaymakers are coming from Russia

![Border traffic queuing at the Nuijamaa border station around New Year 2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9385116)

There is now a lot of traffic on the Finnish-Russian border. Russians are on holiday, and many are travelling to Finland.

There have been long queues at the border stations, but traffic has been moving well. The most traffic has been at the Nuijamaa border station.

## A black box has been tested in cars

![Black box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9384767)

Open image viewer

A car's black box shows, for example, where the car is moving. Photo: Risto Mattila / Yle

In Finland, cars are being monitored with the help of a black box.

The Finnish Transport Safety Agency, Trafi, has tested a black box in 1,000 cars. The device sends information, for example, about where the car is driven and how fast it goes.

Such information is needed if, in the future, drivers have to pay for using the roads. The Ministry of Transport and Communications is now considering the matter.

Black boxes are also used in aircraft.

## In an election campaign it is worth speaking the truth

![Paavo Arhinmäki of the Left Alliance, Antti Rinne of the SDP, Jyrki Katainen of the National Coalition and Carl Haglund of the RKP at Yle's party leaders' debate.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7258333)

Open image viewer

Finns expect politicians to tell the truth during the election campaign. Photo: Yle

Finns think that politicians must not lie during an election campaign. More than 60 per cent of Finns say that one must speak the truth during an election campaign.

Finns also think that it is good if a candidate says things directly. 77 per cent say that it is better to speak directly even when someone is offended by it.

The survey was made by the Local Government Development Foundation. About 1,000 Finns answered the survey.

## Art interests many people

![Ateneum Art Museum.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9348218)

Open image viewer

For example, many people have visited the Ateneum Art Museum. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Many people visited art museums last year.

Many art museums set visitor records.

For example, in Helsinki more than 700,000 people visited the Ateneum, Kiasma and Sinebrychoff museums in total.

The Helsinki City Art Museum, HAM, also set a visitor record. Audiences were especially interested in the exhibition of the Japanese artist Yayoi Kusama.
