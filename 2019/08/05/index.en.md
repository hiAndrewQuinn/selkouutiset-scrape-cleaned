# Monday 5.8.2019 (radio)

There are more and more victims of human trafficking in Finland. The number of shops is decreasing. President Trump wants US gun laws to be revised. There has been a general strike in Hong Kong. A small boy who was thrown from the viewing platform of the Tate Modern museum in London is not in danger of his life.

## Victims of human trafficking

![Door of the Finnish Immigration Service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10908912)

Open image viewer

Shelters are important in identifying victims of forced marriage. Photo: Lotta Sundström / Yle

The Finnish Immigration Service says that there are more victims of human trafficking in Finland than before. In Finland, help was given in the early part of the year to a total of 155 people who had become victims of human trafficking.

The Immigration Service says that very few victims of human trafficking linked to sexual exploitation have been identified. The service suspects that the police have too few resources.

## Problems for shops

![Boxer shorts in packaging on a shelf.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10597107)

Open image viewer

The number of clothing shops has been falling. Photo: Lucas Holm / Yle

The number of shops is falling a lot, estimates the Finnish Retail Federation (Kaupan liitto). Many shops may disappear completely and jobs will decrease. A big reason is that online shopping is becoming more and more popular all the time.

The Retail Federation says that speciality shops in particular will be fewer in the future than now. For many years there have been fewer and fewer new companies in the retail sector.

## US mass shootings

![Police patrolling the crime scene.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10908318)

Open image viewer

9 people died in a mass shooting in Ohio. Photo: Megan Jelinger / AFP

US President Donald Trump has commented on the devastating shootings at the weekend.

Trump demands that the backgrounds of gun buyers be checked more carefully. Trump also wants US gun laws to be revised.

During the weekend three serious shootings happened in the USA, in which 30 people died. Up to 60 people were injured.

People died in the shootings in El Paso in Texas and in Dayton in Ohio. In addition, shots were fired at young people in their twenties in a park in Chicago. Seven people were injured.

## Hong Kong demonstrations

![Police fired tear gas into a crowd of demonstrators in central Hong Kong, in the Admiralty area, on 5 August 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10909207)

Open image viewer

Police fired tear gas into a crowd of demonstrators in central Hong Kong on 5 August 2019. Photo: Jerome Favre / EPA

In Hong Kong a big strike has disrupted traffic and hundreds of flights have been cancelled.

In Hong Kong there have been demonstrations again. The demonstrations have continued for 2 months already. The demonstrators demand, for example, direct democracy.

Over the whole summer more than 400 people have been arrested at protests.

Hong Kong is part of China, but it has its own constitution.

## Murder attempt at Tate Modern

![Police outside the museum.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10908476)

Open image viewer

According to the police, a seriously injured boy was found on the roof of the fifth floor. Photo: Daniel Sorabji / AFP

In London a small boy was thrown from the viewing platform of the Tate Modern museum yesterday. The London police say that the 6-year-old boy is not in danger of his life. The boy is in hospital.

The police have arrested a 17-year-old boy on suspicion of attempted murder. The reason for the act is not known.

The Tate Modern, a museum of modern art, is the most popular tourist attraction in Britain.
