# Monday 24.2.2020 (radio)

It is hard for people with intellectual disabilities to get work in Finland. The authorities are investigating speeches by the deputy chair of the Finns Party youth organisation. The coronavirus is spreading in Italy.

## Jobs for people with intellectual disabilities

![A student at work at the Spesia shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11224078)

It is hard for people with intellectual disabilities to get work in Finland. They do get training, but companies do not hire them for work.

The Finnish Association for Intellectual Disabilities says that 6,000 people with intellectual disabilities are ready for work right now. Many of them have training, for example, in the restaurant or cleaning sector. These sectors need more workers, but people with intellectual disabilities are still not hired.

Often a person with an intellectual disability does not get a job, because family members and authorities are too careful. Simo Klem, an expert at the Finnish Association for Intellectual Disabilities, says that people do not believe enough in the work ability of people with intellectual disabilities.

In Finland there are 30,000 people of working age with intellectual disabilities. Of them, only about 600 do paid work.

## Finns Party youth

![Jussi Halla-aho](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11224635)

Open image viewer

Finns Party chairman Jussi Halla-aho is angry with the party's youth organisation. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The Finns Party youth organisation could lose its grant money.

The Ministry of Education and Culture is investigating the speeches of Toni Jalonen, the deputy chair of the Finns Party youth organisation. Jalonen said at the weekend that he is a fascist.

Fascists are far-right people, like the Nazis. The best-known fascist in history is the Italian dictator Benito Mussolini. He was an ally of Hitler's Germany.

Because of Jalonen's speeches, the state may cancel its support for the Finns Party youth organisation. The organisation has received about 90,000 euros in support for this year.

The Finns Party does not accept Jalonen's speeches. That is why the Finns Party is expelling Jalonen from the party.

## Coronavirus in Italy

![Milan's Cadorna station almost empty during the morning rush hour on 24 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11225321)

Open image viewer

It is quiet in the city of Milan, as people stay at home because of the risk of coronavirus. Photo: Matteo Corner / EPA

The coronavirus is spreading in Italy. More than 200 people have already got the coronavirus infection. At least 5 people have died of the coronavirus in Italy.

Most coronavirus cases are in Northern Italy. The authorities are now trying to stop the spread of the coronavirus. For example, the Venice carnival has been stopped and football events have been cancelled.

The coronavirus is spreading in Italy, but there are far fewer cases of the disease in Italy than in China. In China about 2,600 people have already died of the coronavirus.

You can get information about the coronavirus from the website of THL, the National Institute for Health and Welfare, also in plain Finnish.

## Snow to the south?

![A woman walks with an umbrella in a snowstorm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11090820)

Open image viewer

Will Southern Finland finally get snow? Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The weather is getting colder. Tuesday is a fair-weather day, that is, it does not rain.

On Wednesday and Thursday there will be rain in Southern Finland. The rain may be snow.

So this week winter may come to the south as well.
