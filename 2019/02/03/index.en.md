# Sunday 3.2.2019 (radio)

The Yhteisvastuu collection has started. Snowfall has disrupted traffic on Sunday. An unusual train accident happened on Sunday. Snow and ice may fall from roofs. It is extremely cold in Lapland, and in the south the temperature is close to zero.

## Yhteisvastuu collection

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10628295)

Open image viewer

The president opened the Yhteisvastuu collection on television. Photo: Yle

The Yhteisvastuu collection has started. President Sauli Niinistö opened the Yhteisvastuu collection on television on Sunday.

This time money is collected for children and young people. The Yhteisvastuu collection helps children and young people get to school.

In a televised speech, President Niinistö said that school changes the world. It matters a great deal that girls can also get to school.

Niinistö reminded people that in many disaster areas in the world, schools are closed. With the help of the Yhteisvastuu collection, schools can be built and teachers trained, so that children can get back to school.

The president is worried that there are also poor young people in Finland. Many need support, for example, to buy study materials. The Yhteisvastuu collection helps them too.

The Yhteisvastuu collection is held every year. The Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland organises the collection, and the president is the patron of the collection.

## Traffic in snowfall

![A train in snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10618338)

The snowfall has also disrupted train traffic. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Snowfall has disrupted traffic on Sunday.

Car crashes have happened in different parts of Finland. One person died in a car crash in Kurikka, in South Ostrobothnia.

The snowfall has also disrupted train traffic. About half of the long-distance trains were late on Sunday.

Weather is also affecting train traffic on Monday. Trains are being cancelled at least in the Helsinki region's local traffic.

On Monday morning, every other A and K train is cancelled. In addition, all D trains are cancelled on Monday.

## An unusual train accident

![VR train traffic public transport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10613117)

Open image viewer

Nothing happened to the passengers of the train when it hit an excavator. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

An unusual train accident happened on Sunday. A passenger train hit an excavator in Parkano.

The excavator was doing snow work on the neighbouring track when the passenger train was to pass by it. However, the excavator turned in front of the train.

Nobody was injured in the accident.

In Helsinki, the rescue service warns pedestrians, because snow and ice may fall from roofs.

In Helsinki it first snowed on Sunday and then rained. Wet snow is heavy, and it easily falls off the roof.

## Large temperature differences

![A January morning in Koli.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10616394)

Open image viewer

Beautiful frosty weather continues in Lapland. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The weather varies in different parts of Finland.

On Sunday in Lapland it was more than 30 degrees of frost, but in Helsinki, for example, the temperature rose slightly above zero.

On Monday too, in Lapland it is more than 20 degrees of frost, but in Southern Finland there are only a few degrees of frost.
