## Party support

![Chart of the latest party support poll: National Coalition 19.8%, Finns Party 19.5% and SDP 18.7%.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2544,h_1431,x_0,y_213/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-109197864242d23ce360/1680094274)

The differences in support for the 3 most popular parties are small. Photo: Nanna Särkkä / Yle

Yle has again asked Finns which party they support.

The most popular party is the National Coalition Party. 19.8 percent of Finns support the National Coalition Party. Support for the National Coalition has dropped slightly since the previous survey.

The second most popular party is the Finns Party. 19.5 percent of Finns support the Finns Party. Support for the Finns Party has risen slightly. The National Coalition and the Finns Party are opposition parties, that is, they are not in government.

The third most popular party is the SDP. 18.7 percent of Finns support the SDP. Support for the SDP has dropped slightly. The SDP is a government party.

There are only 3 days left until the parliamentary election. The differences in support for the 3 most popular parties are small. That is why the election is exciting.

## Spies

![Russia's embassy to the United States in Washington.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_99/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-775910602eed0da97b6/1613688226)

Open image viewer

The Russian embassy is on Tehtaankatu in Helsinki. Photo: AOP

There are fewer Russian spies, that is, intelligence officers, in Finland than before. The Security Intelligence Service says that the number of Russian intelligence officers halved last year.

The reason is that Finland expelled many intelligence officers. Visas were also cancelled for people who were intelligence officers.

Intelligence officers are often in Finland as diplomats. The task of the Security Intelligence Service is to identify people who are not real diplomats but spies.

## Property maintenance workers' labour dispute

![Cleaners at work in the corridor of a health centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5730,h_3223,x_0,y_600/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6976135f06f62507637/1594295745)

Open image viewer

The lowest wages of cleaners and property maintenance workers will be raised by 7 percent. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

The strike by cleaners and property maintenance workers has been cancelled. The cleaners and property maintenance workers were due to begin a strike on Thursday morning. The strike was due to begin because the dispute over the collective agreement continued all day on Wednesday.

However, the negotiators for employers and employees reached agreement very late in the evening. That is why the strike of cleaners and property maintenance workers was cancelled.

The new agreement is valid for 2 years. The lowest wages will be raised by 7 percent. The lowest wages will rise to about 2,000 euros.

## Pensions

![Two people sit with their backs to the camera, one of them is grey-haired and sits in a wheelchair.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_408/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99376062f6606c297c0/1669200344)

Open image viewer

Women's pensions are on average smaller than men's pensions. Photo: Sampo Saikkonen / Yle

The average monthly pension of Finns has risen to a new record.

The average monthly pension was over 1,800 euros last year for the first time. This is what the Finnish Centre for Pensions says.

Women's average pension was 20 percent smaller than men's average pension. Women's average pension was about 1,660 euros and men's average pension about 2,070 euros a month.
