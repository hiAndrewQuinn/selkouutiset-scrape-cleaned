# Tuesday 16.11.2021 (radio)

The migrant crisis at the Belarus and Poland border. Russians' views of Finland. A break in the Omavero service. Football World Cup qualifiers. The weather.

## Migrant crisis

![Migrant at the Belarus border, in Bruzgi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12190542)

Open image viewer

Migrant at the Belarus border, in Bruzgi. Photo: Yle

The migrant crisis at the Belarus and Poland border continues.

On Monday many migrants came to the border crossing, which is between Poland and Belarus. Many of the migrants say that Belarusian soldiers forced them to the border.

On Tuesday afternoon there was an incident at the border crossing. Poland says that the migrants threw stones at Polish soldiers. After that the soldiers fired tear gas at the migrants.

The migrants say that they want to get into the EU area.

## Russians' views of Finland

![St Petersburg residents about Finland](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12189435)

Open image viewer

Beauty, history and Scandinavia are things that come to Anastasija Jefremova's mind about Finland.

Russians have a positive view of Finland. This is what a new survey says. In it, Russians were asked what they think of Finland.

Almost 70 per cent of Russians have a positive view of Finland. Russians who live near Finland like Finland the most.

Only 5 per cent of Russians think negatively about Finland.

Russians remember Finland's beautiful nature. They also think that Finland is a rich country.

The most famous Finn in Russia is Ville Haapasalo. Haapasalo is a Finnish actor who has acted a lot in Russia.

## Break in the Omavero service

![The Tax Administration's OmaVero website on a computer screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_628/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-877680618a8f2f5f507/1739439608)

Open image viewer

Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

There is a break in the Omavero service this week. In the internet Omavero service, people can take care of tax matters.

The service is closed for many days because its system is being updated. The Omavero service closes on Thursday at 15.30, and the service opens again on Monday at 12.

Officials also cannot see customers' information during the break. That is why it is good for customers to take care of urgent matters before the Omavero break begins.

## Football World Cup qualifiers

![Jari Kurvinen on the pitch of the Olympic Stadium.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7935,h_4454,x_0,y_165/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-87992561928d3049deb/1636994928)

The organisers expect more than 30 000 spectators at the Olympic Stadium on Tuesday evening. Photo: Jussi Koivunoro / Yle

More football: Finland's World Cup qualifying matches continue. On Tuesday evening Finland play France at the Olympic Stadium in Helsinki.

This is Finland's last group stage World Cup qualifying match. If Finland beat France, Finland will certainly get through to the next qualifying round.

After the qualifying matches, it will be clear which countries will get into the football World Cup. The football World Cup will be held next year in Qatar.

The weather stays mild and cloudy across the whole country.

In Lapland it will snow and sleet on Wednesday. In the south and middle of the country there will only be a little rain.

The temperature is above zero across the whole country, and in the south it is even up to +7 degrees in some places.
