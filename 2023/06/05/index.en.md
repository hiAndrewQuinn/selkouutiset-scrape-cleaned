## Government negotiations

![Petteri Orpo speaks to journalists.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3896,h_2191,x_741,y_146/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1124665647d81aae8940/1686141895)

The government negotiations continue.

The government negotiations have lasted a long time. Elections were held in Finland in early April.

The government negotiations are led by National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo. The National Coalition became the largest party in Parliament in the elections.

Orpo says that the government negotiators have already agreed on many matters. However, the state's funding is still being negotiated. That is difficult, because the state must save money.

The National Coalition, the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats are taking part in the government negotiations. If they can agree on a new government, Petteri Orpo will become Finland's new prime minister.

Orpo estimates that the government negotiations may be ready perhaps by mid-June.

## Pensions and immigration

![A meeting in an office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1800,h_1013,x_0,y_143/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-858064614daffe46662/1685978173)

Open image viewer

New workers are needed in many sectors. Photo: Mostphotos

Finland needs workers from abroad so that the country's pension system works. Money for pensions is collected from wages, but the number of working-age Finns is decreasing.

Finland's pension money will still be enough, however, if about as many immigrants come to Finland in the coming years as came last year. That is how the Finnish Centre for Pensions assesses it.

Last year there were 34,000 more immigrants than people who left the country. Most immigrants got work right away.

If many new workers come from abroad, pension contributions could even be lowered, the Finnish Centre for Pensions says.

## Tallinn road works

![A large road works site with buses and trams in the background in Tallinn.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2047,h_1151,x_0,y_127/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11239286479ddd46b358/1685708714)

Tallinn is doing a lot of road works this year. Photo: Jan Erik Kooli / Yle

In Estonia's capital, Tallinn, a lot of road works are being done this summer. Many streets are now completely closed.

Because of the road works there are many traffic jams in Tallinn's centre and near the port. Traffic is often slow.

The city of Tallinn asks that Finnish tourists leave their car at home when they travel to Tallinn.

## Gulls

![Gulls at Helsinki's Market Square. 20.4.2021](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4256,h_2394,x_0,y_253/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-796618607ed6680d2b3/1618925230)

Open image viewer

Gulls know where and when food is on offer. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

Gulls are intelligent birds. Many outdoor walkers notice this in summer, when a gull steals food from them.

Researchers also confirm that gulls are intelligent. For example, British researchers observed the behaviour of gulls in a park, on a school yard and at a waste plant.

They noticed that the gulls learn which weekday and at what time of day people move about in different places. The gulls were ready when food was on offer.
