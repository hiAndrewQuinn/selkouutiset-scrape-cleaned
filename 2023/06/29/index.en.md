Finland's Prime Minister Petteri Orpo has taken part in a meeting of the leaders of EU countries in Brussels.

The leaders of EU countries have discussed, for example, how the European Union can support Ukraine. Russia's war in Ukraine continues.

The EU wants to strengthen Ukraine's security, so that Ukraine will not be attacked again in the future. Prime Minister Orpo says that the EU is considering a security guarantee for Ukraine.

## Housing prices

![A real estate agent presents an apartment in a block of flats that is for sale.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_522/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105859063c00b5acee53/1673531178)

Open image viewer

The prices of old homes have fallen. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Housing prices have fallen.

The prices of old homes have fallen the most in the Helsinki area. In May they were almost 10 per cent cheaper than a year earlier.

Housing prices have also fallen elsewhere in Finland, but less than in the Helsinki area.

Old flats in blocks of flats and terraced houses have also been sold less than before. In May, about 30 per cent fewer sales were made than before.

## Loneliness

![An anonymous figure stands with their back to the camera on a lakeshore, hood on head, birch trees in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_191/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1134455649955ad43e5d/1687770704)

Open image viewer

Many Finns are lonely. Photo: Terhi Liimu / Yle

There are more lonely people than before. This is what a University of Turku study says.

Already last year, about 20 per cent of Finns felt that they were lonely. Loneliness has increased during the corona time and also after the corona time.

Loneliness has grown most among people under 30. Students, unemployed people and single parents in particular are lonely.

## Madonna

![Madonna on a stage in 2015.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3510,h_1974,x_0,y_148/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5035045b7554415e8f1/1534415961)

Singer and pop star Madonna's world tour is being postponed. The reason is that Madonna is ill.

Madonna was in intensive care for many days, because she has a bacterial infection. Madonna is already a little better, but she is still in hospital.

Madonna intended to start the world tour in July. The world tour was to start in Canada.

On the world tour, Madonna celebrates her singing career, which has continued for over 40 years. The American Madonna is 64 years old.

## The weather

On Friday there will be rain and thunder showers in the east and in the middle of the country.

On Friday the temperature is 23–28 degrees; it is cooler in places in the rain and in the north.

On Saturday it rains in the south and the weather cools. The temperature is about 17 degrees.

In the north it is sunny on Saturday and the temperature is a little over 20 degrees.

The week changes to rainy weather, and next week the weather is colder than now.
