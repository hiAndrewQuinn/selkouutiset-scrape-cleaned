# Saturday 20.11.2021 (radio)

More corona testing. Fighting terrorism. Government parties hold meetings. The Christmas season was opened. Weather forecast.

## More corona testing

![Corona test](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11938325)

A swab stick for the traditional PCR corona test. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

Finland wants to increase corona testing. Corona testing has decreased in the autumn, because people with mild symptoms no longer need to go for a test.

Corona infections are increasing. That is why corona testing is again to be increased. Some experts say that antigen tests may perhaps be introduced for corona testing.

Antigen tests are quick. You get the result in about 15 minutes. If antigen tests start to be used in corona testing, more tests can be done.

## Fighting terrorism

![Ilkka Salmi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_355/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8819286198ae7156540/1637396242)

Open image viewer

The EU's coordinator for fighting terrorism, Ilkka Salmi. Salmi. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

On Saturday, Yle's Ykkösaamu programme talked about fighting terrorism. The guest was the EU's coordinator for fighting terrorism, **Ilkka Salmi**.

Salmi said that every year EU authorities stop even dozens of terror attacks that extremist groups plan. The work of the authorities is made harder by the fact that single people can also become radicalised and plan terror attacks.

For example, discussion on internet forums can radicalise some people. It is harder for the authorities to notice single people who have become radicalised.

## Meetings of the government parties

![Annika Saarikko speaking at the Centre Party's party council meeting in autumn 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5255,h_2955,x_10,y_155/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8819366198ba85662b8/1637399272)

Centre Party chair Annika Saarikko spoke at the Centre Party's party council meeting. Photo: Juuso Stoor / Yle

The four (4) largest government parties are holding party council meetings this weekend. The four (4) largest government parties are the SDP, the Centre Party, the Greens and the Left Alliance.

The party council discusses and decides on many of the party's matters. For example, the SDP, the party of Prime Minister Sanna Marin, published its election programme for the regional elections on Saturday.

At the Centre Party meeting, on Saturday they talked about the terms on which the Centre Party stays in the government.

On Saturday the Christmas season was opened in many cities and shopping centres. In many cities, for example, a Christmas market was opened, music was heard, and children saw Father Christmas.

The Christmas season was opened on Saturday, for example, in Espoo, Jyväskylä, Kuopio, Kokkola, Lahti, Porvoo and Rovaniemi.

Some cities will open the Christmas season only next weekend. Helsinki, Turku and Tampere, for example, do this.
