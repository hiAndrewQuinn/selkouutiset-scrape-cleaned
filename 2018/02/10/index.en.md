# Saturday 10.2.2018 (radio)

Finland got its first medal at the Winter Olympics. The Eurojackpot top prize went to Loimaa. Ghost nets are a problem in the Baltic Sea too. The government gets good ratings from farmers, pensioners and entrepreneurs.

## The Olympics

![Krista Pärmäkoski](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10069800)

Krista Pärmäkoski won Olympic bronze Photo: Lehtikuva

Finland has won its first medal at the Winter Olympics in South Korea.

Krista Pärmäkoski won a bronze medal, that is, she was 3rd in the skiing skiathlon. The race was won by Sweden's Charlotte Kalla and Norway's Marit Björgen was second.

In the skiathlon, half of the distance is skied in the classic style and half in the free style. The skiing distance was 15 kilometres in total.

## Eurojackpot

![A Eurojackpot advertisement in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9567850)

Open image viewer

The Eurojackpot jackpot and Finland's biggest gambling win of all time went to Loimaa. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The Eurojackpot top prize has come to Finland again. The top prize was 90 million euros. It is Finland's biggest gambling win of all time.

The top prize went to a group of five players. Each player gets 18 million euros. The game was played at the Prisma in Loimaa.

The Eurojackpot jackpot has now come to Finland a total of 14 times. The last time the top prize came to Finland was last October.

In this round a smaller prize also came to Finland, 3.4 million euros. It went to an online player in Oulu.

## Ghost nets

![a small turtle caught in a net](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10069510)

Open image viewer

A ghost net can be a death trap for a sea turtle. Photo: AOP

Every year a large number of fishing nets and trawls are left in the world's seas. The nets and trawls may have got away from fishermen or got stuck on rocks or wrecks on the seabed.

Such lost nets, or ghost nets, can do a lot of harm in the sea, because they often keep on fishing. Besides fish, for example dolphins, sea turtles, sharks or seals can swim into the nets and get trapped. The nets are made of durable material, so they do not break down in seawater.

There are also many ghost nets in the Baltic Sea. Germany and Poland in particular have collected them. For example, hundreds of tonnes of ghost nets have been cleaned out of Poland's territorial waters.

Finland is also considering collecting ghost nets if money can be found for the work.

## The government's popularity

![demonstration 2. 2. Senate Square, Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10056305)

Open image viewer

The activation model for unemployment benefits has angered many citizens, but it has not affected the government's popularity. Photo: Kimmo Hiltunen / Yle

The government's popularity has stayed the same.

Lännen Media reports that in a survey by Taloustutkimus, 38 percent of those who answered were satisfied with what the government has done so far. A year ago, 36 percent were satisfied.

The most satisfied are farmers, pensioners and entrepreneurs.

However, the majority, about 55 percent of those who answered, are dissatisfied with the government

The study was done at the turn of January and February. A little over a thousand people took part in it.
