# Saturday 26.2.2022 (radio)

Russia's attack on Ukraine. EU sanctions on Russia. Help for Ukraine. Demonstrations. The weather.

## Russia's attack continues

![Soldier in Kyiv.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5304,h_2983,x_7,y_41/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9207296219ee794d45f/1645866831)

A Ukrainian soldier patrols an empty street in the capital Kyiv. Photo: Daniel Leal / AFP

Russia's attack on Ukraine continues. Fighting is taking place across the country.

In the afternoon, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said that the capital Kyiv is still under the control of Ukrainian forces.

Ukraine's health minister says that so far almost 200 Ukrainian civilians have been killed in Russia's attack. In addition, more than 1,100 people have been wounded.

## EU sanctions on Russia

![A combination of two pictures: oil being pumped in the Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Okrug in Western Siberia, and the facade of the Rossiya bank.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12334742)

Open image viewer

The EU sanctions apply, for example, to Russia's oil refining and to Russian banks. Photo: Anatoly Maltsev / EPA, Alexei Andronov / AOP

The EU has imposed new sanctions on Russia. The sanctions are a counter-measure to Russia's attack on Ukraine.

Sanctions have been imposed on banks and firms that are owned by the Russian state, for example. The sanctions limit, for example, the export of technology to Russia.

Russian President Vladimir Putin and Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov are also on the sanctions list. The EU is freezing the assets that Putin and Lavrov have abroad.

Customs says that there are more than 2,000 firms in Finland that trade with Russia. Customs says that perhaps about 25 percent of these firms do the kind of trade with Russia that the sanctions restrict. Customs' job is to monitor the EU's export sanctions.

## Help for Ukraine

![Collectors of the Finnish Red Cross with collection boxes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5633012)

Open image viewer

The Finnish Red Cross (SPR) starts a box collection next week to help Ukraine. Photo: Einari Viirret / Yle Keski-Suomi

Aid organisations say that Finns have quickly started to help the victims of the war in Ukraine.

For example, in UNICEF Finland's emergency aid collection, Finns donated about 700,000 euros in just over a day.

The Finnish Red Cross disaster fund has also received a lot of support and donations. Next week the Red Cross also starts a box collection to help Ukrainians.

Thousands of Finns have demonstrated in support of Ukraine today.

![Demonstrator holding a sign that opposes Russia's attack on Ukraine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-920789621a0dd24155b/1645875029)

Demonstrations have been held in Helsinki, Tampere, Jyväskylä, Turku and Pori, for example.

In Helsinki more than 10,000 people took part in the demonstrations. They walked in a procession to the Russian embassy and shouted: "Russia out, Putin down!" In front of the embassy, the Sibelius Academy performed, as a choral song, Eino Leino's poem "Greetings, Ukraine!"
