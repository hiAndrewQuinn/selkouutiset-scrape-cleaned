## The price of petrol

![A person finishing refuelling a car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_114/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98817562e3b64ab2bed/1659092504)

Open image viewer

Refuelling has been expensive this summer. Photo: Aalto Puutio / Yle

Some Finns now drive less than before. The reason is that petrol costs a lot.

Petrol has been really expensive this summer, although in recent weeks the price of petrol has fallen a little.

Driving has clearly decreased. It is estimated that Finns have used about 10 percent less petrol this summer than a year ago.

It is hard for experts to estimate what petrol will cost in the future. Especially because of the war in Ukraine, many changes are happening in the economy.

## Ukrainian war-wound patients

![Ukrainian soldiers are treated in a front-line hospital. Soldiers sit in the corridor with drip bottles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7082,h_3983,x_7,y_750/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-954385627ddb5a9bf23/1652415662)

Open image viewer

Treating Ukrainian war wounds is difficult in the middle of the war. Photo: EPA

Finland is preparing to take patients from Ukraine who have been wounded in the war.

There are already several hundred Ukrainian patients with war wounds in European hospitals in total. About 10 patients have so far come to Finland from the war in Ukraine for hospital care.

The situation in Ukraine's hospitals is difficult. There are many patients because of the war. In addition, many hospitals have suffered damage, because Russia has also bombed hospitals in Ukraine.

Russia started the war in Ukraine a little over 5 months ago.

## Traffic accident in Savonlinna

![People are laying candles by the side of the road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_142/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98843862e523e97841b/1659184136)

Open image viewer

People brought candles to the accident site. Photo: Markku Tuhkanen / Yle

A serious traffic accident has happened in Savonlinna in Eastern Finland.

3 young people died in the accident. They were about 20 years old.

In addition, 1 person was seriously injured. They are in hospital care.

The police say that the accident happened near the centre of Savonlinna on Saturday morning. In the accident the car drove into a signpost.

The police say that the car may have been driving too fast. At the time of the accident it rained a lot.

## Floods in the USA

![Map of Kentucky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12555622)

Open image viewer

There are floods in the state of Kentucky. Photo: Yle

There are serious floods in the USA. Flood water has caused damage in the state of Kentucky. The cause of the floods is heavy rain.

Perhaps about 20 people have died in the floods. Children are also among them. More dead may still be found.

Rescue work in the flood area continues. Several hundred people have already been rescued from the flood waters.

## The weather in Finland

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12557333)

Open image viewer

In the south it is dry on Sunday, no rain falls. The temperature in the south and the central part of the country is about 20 degrees.

In the north it rains on Sunday. The weather is cool in the north, the temperature is only about 15 degrees.

At the start of next week a little warmer air comes to Finland from the south and the rain decreases.
