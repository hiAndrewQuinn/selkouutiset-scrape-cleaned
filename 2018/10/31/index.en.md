# Wednesday 31.10.2018 (radio)

10 people were sentenced to prison because they sold art forgeries. Ville Niinistö does not want to become chair of the Greens, and he no longer wants to be in Parliament. Finnish soldiers are taking part in a big NATO military exercise in Norway. With the Tax Calculator on Yle's website you can see where your tax euros go. A film about the band Queen comes to Finland on Friday.

## Long prison sentences for art fraudsters

![The picture shows art forgeries, works by Ilkka Lammi and Fernand Léger.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7760946)

Forged paintings. Photo: National Bureau of Investigation

10 people have been sentenced to prison because they sold art forgeries.

The owners of an art shop in Helsinki sold forged paintings for 5 years, for a total of over 13 million euros. One of them will go to prison for 5 years and the other for 4 years. The court also ordered the couple to pay compensation totalling 13 million euros. The other 8 people helped the couple to cheat buyers.

The forgery case may still go to the Court of Appeal.

## Ville Niinistö does not want to lead the Greens

![Ville Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10484404)

Open image viewer

Ville Niinistö. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

MP Ville Niinistö does not want to become chair of the Greens. Niinistö will also leave Parliament in the spring.

The Green party will choose a new chair on Saturday. But so far there is only one candidate: MP Outi Alanko-Kahiluoto. The new chair's first important task is the election campaign. The parliamentary elections will be held in about 5 months.

The chair of the Greens is changing because Touko Aalto fell ill and stepped down as chair last week.

## Finns are training with NATO

![NATO held a combat demonstration near Trondheim as part of the Trident Juncture military exercise.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10483943)

Open image viewer

NATO is training in Norway. Photo: Petri Raivio / YLE

The military alliance NATO is holding a big military exercise in Norway.

Finland is not a member of NATO, but Finnish soldiers are taking part in the NATO exercise. Finnish soldiers are practising defence together with other Europeans. NATO has not held such a big military exercise for many decades. About 50,000 soldiers are taking part in the exercise.

This week Russia is testing missiles in the same area where NATO is training. NATO hopes that Russia does the tests safely.

## A calculator shows where taxes are spent

Tomorrow, Thursday, is tax day.

The media report who got the most money last year and how much tax they paid.

Everyone can already look at their own taxes now. There is a Tax Calculator on Yle's website. It tells you how much money you give, for example, to schools, pensions or environmental protection.

## A film about the band Queen

![Gwilym Lee as Brian May and Rami Malek as Freddie Mercury.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10482669)

Open image viewer

Rami Malek plays Freddie Mercury in the film. Photo: Alex Bailey / Twentieth Century Fox

On Friday a film about the band Queen premieres in Finland.

Queen's singer Freddie Mercury was a famous rock star in different parts of the world. He was the first superstar to die of AIDS. The film also tells about Freddie Mercury's relationships with women and men, but the main focus is the music.
