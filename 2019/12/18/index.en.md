# Wednesday 18.12.2019 (radio)

A winter storm brought rain and wind. Pekka Haavisto won Parliament's confidence. People with a foreign background trust Finnish authorities. Young adults are the most active. The bears at Korkeasaari have gone into hibernation.

## Winter storm cut power

![Snowstorm in Sotkamo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11123155)

Open image viewer

Road conditions are poor or very poor in the southern and central parts of the country. Photo: Ville Heikkinen / AOP

The winter storm has brought strong winds and rain to Finland. In the south it has rained, and in the central part of the country it has snowed.

The winter storm has cut power in Pirkanmaa, Kanta-Häme, South Savo and South Karelia, for example. Power has been out for tens of thousands of homes. More power cuts may occur during the evening.

The rain and winds are moving east over the southern and central parts of the country. By night the storm will begin to ease.

## Haavisto won Parliament's confidence

![Pekka Haavisto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4096,h_2304,x_0,y_77/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6198885dea4bb127380/1575635918)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto has won Parliament's confidence in the interpellation vote.

The interpellation concerned Finnish family members of Isis terrorists who are in the al-Hol camp in Syria. In the interpellation, the opposition parties criticised Foreign Minister Haavisto's actions in returning the Finns in al-Hol to Finland.

## Finnish authorities are trusted

![e2 research: Same or different country?](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6240325df9fd2f01988/1576664536)

Open image viewer

Same or different country? The study examined the values and attitudes of 5 different language groups. Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

People with a foreign background have strong trust in ethnic Finns and in Finnish authorities.

A new study shows that trust is strong, for example, in the police and the judiciary, in schools and in health care. Many also trust ethnic Finns even more than people from their own home country.

The study involved over 1,500 people from different language groups. It includes people whose mother tongue is Russian, Estonian, Somali, Arabic or English.

The study was funded by the Finnish Cultural Foundation, e2 Research, the cities of Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa, and the Ministry of Justice.

## Many Finns move too little

![Graphic of the share of physically active men and women in Finland by different measures](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1428,h_803,x_0,y_29/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6237245df8dcfeb6b06/1576590700)

Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

Many Finns do too little exercise. About 30 per cent of Finnish adults do no exercise at all in their free time.

The extensive FinTerveys population study shows that young adults do the most fitness exercise.

The most popular is walking or going to the gym. Housework and garden work are also popular forms of everyday exercise.

10,000 people took part in the FinTerveys 2017 study.

## Bears at Korkeasaari went into hibernation

![Bears at Korkeasaari went into hibernation a month late](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11124723)

Open image viewer

Two bears live at Korkeasaari. Photo: Mari Lehmonen / Korkeasaari

The bears at the Korkeasaari zoo have gone into hibernation. The zoo says that hibernation began about a month late.

The animal keepers say that the two bears at Korkeasaari have been tired for the last few weeks. Until now, however, they have sat and dozed in front of their winter den. When the animal keepers closed the doors of the outdoor enclosure, the bears crawled into their own dens.

The bears at Korkeasaari often sleep until the turn of February and March.
