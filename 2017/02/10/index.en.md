# Friday 10.2.2017 (radio)

Terrafame's mine gets one new owner. People do not always stay to help at an accident site after they have called the emergency centre. There are only a few professional fishers left in Finland. Helsinki Sauna Day is approaching.

## The Terrafame mine

![Leaching heaps steaming around the raw water pond at the Terrafame Sotkamo mine in frost.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9453689)

Metals are obtained from the Terrafame mine. Photo: Kimmo Rauatmaa / Lehtikuva

A large international company is buying part of the Finnish Terrafame. Terrafame is a mining company that is owned by the Finnish state.

The buyer is a company called Trafigura. It is buying about 15 per cent of Terrafame. About 85 per cent of Terrafame will still remain with the Finnish state.

Trafigura trades in raw materials. It promises to buy almost all the metal that Terrafame produces in the next 7 years. Nickel and zinc are obtained from the Terrafame mine.

The Terrafame mine is in Sotkamo, in northern Finland. The mine has had environmental and financial problems, and the state has spent a lot of money on it.

Many residents of the area are happy about the new owner. People in the area hope that the mine's finances will get in order and that the jobs will remain.

## Emergency situations

![Employees at the emergency response centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7926220)

Open image viewer

The emergency centre tells the caller what to do at the accident site. Photo: Emergency Response Centre Administration

Emergency centres have a problem. Even though people call the emergency centre and report an accident, they may not stay at the accident site to help.

Many may be afraid that they will not know how to help, says doctor Teuvo Määttä. However, the emergency centre gives advice on the phone about what must be done.

Everyone must help people who are involved in an accident or have a sudden illness. Help is very important before the ambulance arrives at the scene.

## Professional fishers

![A catch starts to show as the seine net is hauled upwards.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8705310)

Open image viewer

Fishers at work. Photo: Riikka Pennanen / Yle

There are only a few professional fishers left in Finland. There are only about 600 full-time professional fishers. Most of them are middle-aged men. They have inherited the profession from their fathers.

Fisher Mika Liukkonen is not a fisher's son. Liukkonen says that fishing was a hobby for him, which became his profession.

Liukkonen says that fishing has its own freedom and its own job. He also likes working in the middle of nature.

## Sauna Day

![The free löylys of the rooftop saunas of Helsinki hotels were booked up in one go.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9451954)

Sauna Day, that is, the sauna day, is held again in Helsinki in March. Sauna Day was held for the first time a year ago.

The idea of Sauna Day is that people can visit saunas that they do not normally get into. For example, many company saunas are open to guests on Sauna Day. Guests can sauna for free.

On Sauna Day foreigners also have a good chance to try different Finnish saunas. Foreigners are also interested in ordinary Finns' home saunas. That is why more of them are hoped to join Sauna Day too.
