# Thursday 30.12.2021 (radio)

Strike warning. Corona infections. Junior ice hockey world championships. New Year's Eve. The weather.

## Strike warning

![Riku Aalto looks at the interviewer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3936,h_2214,x_0,y_199/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89747361cda0e546f33/1640866182)

Riku Aalto, chair of the Industrial Union, talked about strike plans on Thursday. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

A big strike threatens Finland.

The Industrial Union warns of a strike that could start in 2 weeks. If the strike starts, it could last a week.

The Industrial Union is a big trade union of workers. During the strike it plans to stop work in about 300 companies. The companies think that the whole Finnish economy will suffer if the strike starts.

The Industrial Union is in a dispute with the companies about workers' pay. A solution to the dispute will be sought next week. If a solution is found, there will be no strike.

## Corona infections

A lot more corona infections have been reported in Finland.

The National Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, says that on Thursday there are about 3,600 new corona infections.

In addition, THL reports about 6,000 corona infections from the Christmas period. THL is only reporting the Christmas-period corona infections now, because there have been problems with data transfer.

So THL reported a total of about 9,600 corona infections on Thursday.

## World championships

The junior ice hockey world championships are being stopped. The reason is corona.

Corona infections have been found in at least 3 teams. Teams from 10 countries are taking part.

The junior ice hockey world championships started in Canada on Sunday. Already on Wednesday the International Ice Hockey Federation decided that the tournament will not continue because of corona infections.

The Finnish Ice Hockey Association is considering whether the tournament could be finished, for example in the summer.

Men under 20 years old play in the junior world championships.

The Finnish team, Nuoret Leijonat, started the world championships well. Finland won its games against Germany and Austria.

Tomorrow, Friday, is the last day of the year, that is, New Year's Eve. Shops expect many customers as people prepare for parties.

The shops hope that people will remember the corona guidelines. Masks and safe distances are important when trying to prevent corona infections.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12253059)

Open image viewer

The year ends in snowy moods. Snowfall arrives in Finland during Thursday, and snow will fall again on Friday.

A few centimetres of snow will fall, but in Lapland as much as 20 centimetres of snow will come.

New Year's Eve, that is Friday, is mild. In a large part of the country there are only a few degrees of frost.

On New Year's Day, that is Saturday, the frost gets stronger. There is frost between 5 and 20 degrees.
