A Ukrainian missile accidentally hit Poland. Trump is again seeking the presidency. Finnair is cutting jobs. Many workplaces have employees with a foreign background. Children's Independence Day celebration. The weather.

## Missile in Poland

![A Russian missile flew into Polish territory on 15.11.2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10345686373e2be7aa9c/1668539207)

The missile that hit Poland was apparently an accident, says Polish President Andrezej Duda.

A missile hit Poland yesterday evening, close to the border between Poland and Ukraine. 2 people died in the explosion.

At first it was suspected that Russia may have accidentally fired the missile into Poland. However, Poland says that it is most likely a Ukrainian air defence missile, which accidentally crossed the border into Poland. .

Ukraine used air defence, because yesterday Russia fired about a hundred missiles at various parts of Ukraine.

In the United States, former President Donald Trump is seeking to become president again.

Trump said in Florida that he is again seeking to be the presidential candidate of the Republican Party.

Trump was last elected president in 2016. He was also a candidate in 2020, but lost the election to the Democrat Joe Biden.

The next presidential election is in November 2024.

## Finnair is cutting

![Aircraft were serviced in Finnair's hangar at the end of October 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2956,h_1663,x_0,y_1834/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7630276002e3d0b6556/1610802548)

Open image viewer

Finnair plans to cut cabin crew. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Finnair plans to cut perhaps as many as 450 jobs.

The jobs will be cut from cabin services. Finnair plans to outsource cabin services on the Thailand and North America routes.

Cabin services currently employ about 1,750 workers in Finland.

Finnair says that the corona pandemic and the closure of Russian airspace have weakened its profitability. Outsourcing is intended to improve the situation.

## Foreigners in workplaces

![Employees of the A-L Sorsa home bakery in November 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10521552)

Open image viewer

Employees of the A-L Sorsa home bakery a few years ago. Archive photo. Photo: Annika Martikainen / Yle

Many workplaces already have employees with a foreign background.

In a SAK survey, 70 percent of trust representatives said that they have employees with a foreign background at their workplace. In 2019 a little over half answered this way..

Most also said that Finnish and foreign-background employees mainly get along well with each other.

However, 10 percent of respondents said that people with a foreign background are discriminated against at the workplace.

## Children's Independence Day celebration

![Sanna Marin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1026930635cd3c9cabc8/1667028001)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin hosts the children's Independence Day celebration. Photo: Sakari Piippo / Yle

More than 550 children will receive an invitation to the national Independence Day celebration this year. The celebration will be held on 7 December at the House of Estates in Helsinki.

Two children from every Finnish municipality are invited to the celebration. The children are 10 years old.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin shakes hands with every child at the start of the celebration.

The children's Independence Day celebration was last held in 2019. After that the celebration was put on a break because of the corona pandemic.
