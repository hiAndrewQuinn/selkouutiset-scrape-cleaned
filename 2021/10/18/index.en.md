## Opponents of the corona pass

![Old-fashioned pink stone house, with tape marks from shops and restaurants on the ground-floor windows](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2592,h_1458,x_0,y_40/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-868226616d1e9b2f839/1634541319)

This shop in Vääksy was harassed at the weekend because of the corona pass. Archive picture. Photo: Sini Ojanperä / Yle

Opponents of the corona pass have caused disturbance in various parts of Finland.

Opponents of the corona pass have harassed firms that have introduced the corona pass. For example, they have harassed restaurant owners and employees.

Many restaurants introduced the corona pass at the weekend, as soon as it was possible. Restaurants do not have to follow corona restrictions if they require customers to have a corona pass.

The managing director of the restaurant industry organisation Mara, Timo Lappi, says that the troublemakers do not understand how important the corona pass is. With the help of the corona pass, restaurants can be open normally, and employees have work.

The idea of the corona pass is to offer a safe place for customers and employees. The corona pass shows that a person has protection against corona, for example 2 corona vaccinations.

You can get the corona pass from the internet, from the Omakanta service.

![Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_128/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-856766614a396626014/1634546491)

President Sauli Niinistö did not get a corona infection. Photo: John Minchillo / AFP

President Sauli Niinistö has no corona infection. Niinistö told about this on Twitter.

Last week President Niinistö was exposed to the corona virus, that is, he was close to a person who has a corona infection. This happened on a meeting trip to Sweden.

After the trip Niinistö has stayed at home and has not met guests. The visit of the new President of Estonia to Finland was also cancelled last week.

Now President Niinistö is back at work.

## Friend of the UN of the year

![Anne Nuorgam, who received the 2021 UN Friend of the Year honorary mention](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_256/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-868402616d4b9650403/1634553086)

Open image viewer

Anne Nuorgam received an honorary mention from the Finnish UN Association. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Sami politician Anne Nuorgam has received an honorary mention from the UN, that is, the United Nations. Nuorgam is *UN Friend of the Year.*

The Finnish UN Association says that Nuorgam has done important work for the benefit of the Sami. Nuorgam is also the chair of the UN Permanent Forum on Indigenous Issues.

Nuorgam is happy about the honorary mention. Nuorgam says that the honorary mention concerns all Sami.

The Sami are the European Union's only indigenous people.
