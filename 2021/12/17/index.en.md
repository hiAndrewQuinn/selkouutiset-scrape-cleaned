# Friday 17.12.2021 (radio)

Corona vaccines and corona pass. The Omicron variant. Mask recommendation. Income and death rates. Weather.

## Corona pass and vaccines

![A person shows the camera their phone, which displays a corona certificate.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6131,h_3450,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89067261b3611b08d3d/1639146102)

The travel and restaurant sector has turned in favour of the corona pass. Photo: Tiina Kokko / Yle

The government proposes a law to extend the use of the corona pass. The government proposes that the corona pass is possible even when there are no restrictions in force.

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health says this in a press release. The draft law goes out for comments today.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare recommends a new vaccine interval.

People who have turned 60 and risk groups can get a third dose of vaccine 3 to 4 months after the second vaccination.

Others get the third vaccine 4 to 6 months later. The new instruction is due to the spread of the Omicron variant in Finland.

## Omicron virus

![Texts reading probable, improbable, impossible to assess](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12215081)

Open image viewer

The Omicron variant spreads more easily than the delta variant. Photo: Eetu Pietarinen/Yle

The Omicron variant of the corona virus is now the main virus in the capital region. Omicron spreads many times more easily than the earlier delta variant.

In the area of the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district, more than 50 per cent of infections are of the new variant.

Experts say that avoiding contacts is currently the best way to stop Omicron from spreading.

Today HUS has raised the readiness level of the hospitals to the highest level, that is, full readiness, because of the corona epidemic.

## Mask requirement

![Holiday traffic at the railway station in Helsinki on 19 February 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11829861)

Open image viewer

The mask requirement has come back on trains. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare recommends the use of face masks across the whole country. The recommendation applies to public indoor spaces and public transport.

The recommendation applies to everyone who has turned 12 years old, including those who have been vaccinated.

The mask requirement has returned to all VR long-distance and commuter trains today, on Friday.

## Income and death rates

![Honkanummi cemetery.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4249,h_2390,x_7,y_233/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-87575461841eb65d6ec/1636051458)

Open image viewer

The risk of people on low incomes dying younger is higher than for people on higher incomes. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

Wealth affects differences in death rates. The differences in death rates between income groups are still large.

A study by the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that people on low incomes lose the most years of life.

The risk of a person on a low income dying of cardiovascular diseases, accidents and alcohol is higher than for people on high incomes.

In relative terms, the biggest factor in the causes of death was alcohol.

The study used register data from Statistics Finland.
