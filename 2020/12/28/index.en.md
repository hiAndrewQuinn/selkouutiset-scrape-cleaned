# Monday 28.12.2020 (radio)

New coronavirus variant. Coronavirus vaccinations. Mood medicines. Data breach. Finland and Russia.

## New coronavirus type in Finland

![Test tubes at the Messukeskus drive-in coronavirus testing station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11615208)

Corona samples in test tubes. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

A new type of the coronavirus has been found in Finland. A new type of the coronavirus has been found in 3 people who have come back to Finland from abroad.

They have been treated in isolation and they are doing well. The cases were confirmed in Southern Finland.

The new type of coronavirus was first found in Britain. Now the new type of coronavirus has been found in Finland for the first time.

The first vaccines were given on Sunday to workers in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district.

## Sales of mood medicines grow

![Kristian Wahlbeck](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10912887)

Open image viewer

Psychiatrist Kristian Wahlbeck says that it is good to talk about fear of corona. Photo: MIELI Suomen Mielenterveys ry.

Sales of mood medicines have grown in Finland this year. Sales of mood medicines in pharmacies have grown by about 9 percent from last year.

Doctors have prescribed more mood medicines than before, perhaps because the corona situation worries many Finns.

Psychiatrist **Kristian Wahlbeck** of the Mental Health Association is concerned about this. He says that fear of corona should not be treated with medicines. Psychiatrist Wahlbeck says that it is better to talk about fears. Then a person finds strength and can cope better.

## Data breach at Parliament

![The Parliament building dark on a November evening.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7573745fe3701439575/1608742179)

Open image viewer

The Parliament building in Helsinki. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

There has been a data breach at Parliament. The data breach was carried out at Parliament this autumn.

The intruders may have been able to read the emails of MPs and civil servants.

The police say that the intruders may have obtained information for some foreign state, or the intruders want to harm Finland with the information. The police are investigating the data breach at Parliament.

## Niinistö and Putin talked

![Presidents Vladimir Putin and Sauli Niinistö at a press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10932422)

Open image viewer

Presidents Vladimir Putin and Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Karoliina Simoinen / Yle

President **Sauli Niinistö** and the President of Russia **Vladimir Putin** have talked on the phone.

The presidents talked, for example, about matters between Finland and Russia, the corona situation and arms control.

President Niinistö and President Putin also exchanged New Year's greetings from Finland and Russia on the phone.

Diplomatic relations between Finland and Russia turn 100 years old on New Year's Eve, which is Thursday.
