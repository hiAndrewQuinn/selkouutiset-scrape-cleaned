# Wednesday 7.12.2016 (radio)

A court has remanded in custody the man who possibly shot 3 women at the weekend. The Imatra shooting worries Russians. Estonia's prime minister Jüri Ratas has visited Helsinki. There has been an earthquake in Indonesia. The Palace reception had a lot of TV viewers. Alcohol changes a young person's brain.

## The Imatra shooter

![The man charged with the triple murder with a hood on his head in the district court on 7.12.2016](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9339093)

Open image viewer

The court remanded in custody the man suspected of being the Imatra shooter. Photo: Yle

A court has remanded in custody the man who shot 3 women at the weekend. The man shot the women in front of a restaurant in the centre of Imatra. The women died immediately.

The police suspect that the shooter is a 23-year-old from Imatra. The man has not said why he shot the women. The reason may be the man's psychological problems.

## Imatra and the Russians

![A press conference in St Petersburg](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9338274)

Open image viewer

The mayor of Imatra told Russian journalists about the shooting in St Petersburg. Photo: Yle Jari Tanskanen

The Imatra shooting worries Russians.

The town of Imatra is close to the Russian border, and Imatra does a lot of cooperation with Russians. Now Russians have cancelled trips to Imatra, because they do not know whether it is safe in Imatra.

In Russia, there has been false information about the shooting on the internet. On the internet it has been said, for example, that the dead are Russians. The fact, however, is that the women were Finnish.

## The Indonesian earthquake

![Indonesia earthquake 07.12.2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9337842)

Open image viewer

The earthquake broke buildings in Indonesia. Photo: Hotli Simanjuntak / EPA

There has been an earthquake in Indonesia in Asia.

At least about 100 people have died in the quake. However, more dead may still be found.

There are many injured. The hospitals in the area are full of injured people.

The earthquake happened in the Aceh region on the island of Sumatra, and its magnitude was 6.5.

## Alcohol and the brain

![A beer bottle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9332612)

Open image viewer

Beer is a dangerous drink for a young person. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Alcohol changes a young person's brain. A new Finnish study says that alcohol affects the development of the brain.

The cortex of a young person's brain is thinner than usual if the young person uses a lot of alcohol. That means, for example, about a six-pack, that is, 6 cans of beer every weekend.

However, the brain can recover if the young person drinks less alcohol.
