# Tuesday 1.6.2021 (radio)

There are 154 new coronavirus infections. Sweden is easing corona restrictions. Finns eagerly took out mortgages in April. A Marc Chagall exhibition opens in Sastamala. Spring was rainy.

There are 154 new coronavirus infections in Finland. The most new infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district.

9 percent of Finns have received both doses of the corona vaccine.

Almost 2.5 million people have received the first vaccine dose.

## Corona in Sweden

![People sit on the steps of the Dramaten.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11957988)

Open image viewer

In Sweden, 50 people at a time are let into theatres. The picture shows the Royal Dramatic Theatre, the Dramaten. Photo: AOP

Sweden is changing its corona restrictions. The restrictions are milder than before.

Restaurants may be open later than before. Cinemas and theatres may be open, but indoors no more than 50 people may be present. Travellers from the Nordic countries may travel to Sweden without a corona test.

Sweden still has a lot of coronavirus infections.

## Loans and deposits

![A woman measures a floor with a tape measure in an empty flat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3918,h_2204,x_0,y_67/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-775685602e6f30c1bf0/1616412921)

At the end of April, household loans included consumer credit of over 16 billion euros and other loans of just over 17 billion euros. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

Finns take out a lot of mortgages.

In April Finns took out new mortgages worth 1.9 billion euros. This is almost 500 million euros more than a year ago. The average mortgage interest rate is 0.82 percent.

People have consumer credit of 16 billion euros and other loans of 17 billion euros.

People have a total of 106 billion euros in money in banks.

## Marc Chagall exhibition

![Marc Chagall's graphic work The Little Swallow from the series Daphnis & Chloé](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_720,h_405,x_0,y_48/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81399360b5ff34b59ea/1622540392)

Open image viewer

The exhibition features, among others, Marc Chagall's graphic work The Little Swallow from the series Daphnis & Chloé. Photo: Art Pappila

In Finland there is an exhibition by a famous artist.

Marc Chagall's works are on display in Sastamala in Pirkanmaa. The exhibition includes books for which Marc Chagall made the pictures. The works are the kind that have rarely been on display.

The exhibition opens on 11 June. The exhibition is open until mid-August.

Marc Chagall is a well-known and respected artist. He was born in Russia in 1887. Chagall lived in France for a long time.

Marc Chagall died in 1985. He was 97 years old.

Spring was rainy in Finland.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that in spring it rained the most in Hämeenlinna. It rained the least in Utsjoki.

In May the weather was warm. The highest temperature was 30.8 degrees. It was measured in Ilomantsi.
