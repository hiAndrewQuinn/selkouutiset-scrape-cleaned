# Radio Clear News Tuesday 27.6.2023

Hospitals. Foreign workers. Russia. The weather.

## Hospitals

![A close-up of a nurse's hands and an elderly woman's hands. The nurse checks the elderly woman's vital signs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1079791640023a047114/1687836272)

There are too few nurses in Finland. Photo: Aleksei Gorodenkov / AOP

Many hospitals and health centres close services in the summer. The reason is that there are too few workers. In many places in Finland there are not enough nurses and doctors.

For example, some wards for bed patients are closed completely in the summer, or patient places are reduced.

In many places they stay closed for as long as the workers' summer holidays continue. The problem is that hospitals and health centres cannot find enough substitutes for the workers who are on summer holiday.

It has already been difficult for a long time for hospitals and health centres to find substitutes. Still, for example in the Turku area, the situation this summer is worse than ever before.

## Foreign workers

![A tattooed man cooks food in a restaurant kitchen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4478,h_2519,x_0,y_231/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8609146155fda8a9d42/1687866720)

There were problems, for example, in the restaurant sector. Photo: Emilia Åberg / Yle

Authorities say that the position of foreign workers in Finland is not always good. There are sometimes problems, for example, in construction companies and restaurants.

The employment relationship between employer and employee is not always in order. For example, some restaurants say that a foreigner is only helping in the restaurant, even though he works all day.

Foreign workers also sometimes get too little pay.

In some construction companies, foreigners do work without a work permit.

There are also problems because foreign workers do not always know what the rights of a worker in Finland are. In addition, they do not always know what the duties of the employer are.

## Russia

![Vladimir Putin stands in front of the Russian flag indoors.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_106/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11347456499ed1595abe/1687866889)

Russia has ended the criminal investigation that concerned the Wagner soldiers' rebellion. Russia's leaders say that the Wagner soldiers will not be charged with a crime, but they must hand over their heavy weapons.

The Wagner soldiers fought in Ukraine for a long time, but at Midsummer they started a rebellion against Russia's military leaders. The rebellion ended quickly.

Wagner is a private military group that is not part of the Russian army.
