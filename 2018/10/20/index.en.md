# Saturday 20.10.2018 (radio)

In Helsinki people demand climate action from politicians. A strange case involving a Saudi journalist. A home godparent is a friend to a lonely elderly person. On Sunday it is cloudy.

## Marchers demand action from politicians

![Irma Nuuttila showed her &quot;Renewable Helsinki Now&quot; banner on the steps of the Cathedral in Senate Square before the Climate March organised by Kepa ry in Helsinki on 20 October](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10467245)

Irma Nuuttila took part in the climate march. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Today people in Finland have taken part in a climate march.

The climate march in Helsinki had thousands of people. The marchers wanted politicians to pay attention to climate change.

 The march ended at the Parliament House. There, politicians were waiting to meet the marchers.

Politicians told what their parties intend to do because of climate change.

## New information about the case of the Saudi journalist

![Saudi Arabian consulate in Istanbul.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10460542)

Open image viewer

The journalist died at the Saudi Arabian consulate in Istanbul. Photo: Sedat Suna / EPA

New information has been obtained about the strange case of the Saudi journalist.

The journalist went to the Saudi Arabian consulate in Turkey to handle some matters three weeks ago. Then he disappeared.

The journalist had written articles in which he criticised the administration of the Saudi crown prince.

Now Saudi Arabia has announced that the journalist died in a fight at the consulate.

 Finnish professor Hannu Juusola knows a lot about Middle Eastern affairs. He says that nobody believes Saudi Arabia's explanation. The professor believes that the journalist was killed because the Saudi Arabian administration ordered it.

 The journalist's case has become a big international scandal.

US President Donald Trump is the only one who says that he believes the journalist died by accident in a fight. The United States and Saudi Arabia are allies.

## A home godparent is a friend to a lonely elderly person

![Leena Nordman and Karen Martiano sit on a sofa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10467172)

Open image viewer

Leena Nordman and Karen Martiano are volunteer home godparents. Photo: Lauri Rautavuori / Yle

Many elderly people in Finland are lonely.

In the city of Lahti, volunteer home godparents have started to keep elderly people company. A home godparent visits and asks the lonely person how they are.

 In Lahti there are already 40 volunteer home godparents. The youngest of them is 17 years old.
