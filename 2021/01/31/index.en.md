# Sunday 31.1.2021 (radio)

Demonstrations in Russia. Support demonstration in Helsinki, the corona situation in Jyväskylä. Corona vaccine scammer. The weather.

## Demonstrations in Russia

![Demonstrators in Moscow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11765249)

Open image viewer

Demonstrators in Moscow. Photo: Grigory Vorobjov / Yle

Today there have been big demonstrations in different parts of Russia. People have expressed their views in Moscow and St Petersburg, and also in cities in the Russian Far East, for example.

The demonstrators have demanded the release of the opposition leader Aleksei Navalnyi. The police arrested Navalnyi two weeks ago, when he arrived in Moscow from Berlin.

The police have used harsh methods against the demonstrators. More than 3,000 people have been arrested in different parts of the country. Navalnyi's wife was also arrested.

## The corona situation in Jyväskylä

![Signpost to corona sampling at Killeri in Jyväskylä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4096,h_2304,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7510775fcdce6330c04/1607323442)

Open image viewer

Jyväskylä has moved to the spreading phase of the coronavirus. Photo: Heli Kaski / Yle

The corona situation in Jyväskylä is getting worse. The city has now moved to the spreading phase of the coronavirus.

At least 240 new infections have been found in Jyväskylä during the week. In addition, more than 1,500 people have had to go into isolation or quarantine during this week. Infections are now appearing especially among children and young people.

Jyväskylä is introducing new restrictions to try to stop the spread of corona. Comprehensive schools for grades 7 to 9 will move to distance teaching for a week from tomorrow, and children's and young people's sports hobbies will be stopped for 2 weeks. Libraries and museums will also be closed. In addition, people are asked to work from home if it is at all possible.

A total of over 45,000 coronavirus infections have already been found in Finland. In the last day 367 new infections have been found.

## Corona vaccine scammer

![A Biontech corona vaccine in an ampoule.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3071,h_1727,x_0,y_48/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7449985fbb35e7344af/1606104691)

Open image viewer

A real corona vaccine. Photo: EPA-EFE/BIONTECH SE

The police have arrested a woman in Lappeenranta who is suspected of corona vaccine fraud.

The woman went from door to door in Virolahti in Kymenlaakso. She tried to sell "coronavirus vaccines" to people. The "vaccines" cost 150 euros.

However, the woman is a fraudster and she does not have real vaccines. Real vaccines are held only by the health authorities.
