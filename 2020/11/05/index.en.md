# Thursday 5.11.2020 (radio)

Votes are being counted in the US presidential election. Steel company Outokumpu is cutting staff. VR plans to furlough staff. The Finlandia literary prize nominees have been announced. Guide dogs need foster families.

## Votes are being counted in the USA

![Vote counters in Pennsylvania.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11631335)

Open image viewer

Vote counters have had a lot of work. Photo: Spencer Platt / AFP

Votes are being counted in the US presidential election. Democratic candidate Joe Biden is in the lead.

However, the Republican candidate, President Donald Trump, doubts the honesty of the election. The Democrats are trying to prevent Trump's victory with postal votes, says Trump.

Many Americans voted by post before election day because of the coronavirus. That is why counting the votes also takes a long time.

The election result may become clear soon, but the result may be disputed for a long time.

## Outokumpu is cutting staff

![Outokumpu's Tornio plants.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10860849)

Open image viewer

The Outokumpu factory area in Tornio. Photo: Antti Heikinmatti / Yle

Steel company Outokumpu is cutting staff. Outokumpu is a Finnish company, but it operates in many countries.

Outokumpu now plans to cut staff in Finland, Sweden and Germany. In Finland, Outokumpu plans to cut 270 employees.

Outokumpu says that the coronavirus situation has made sales in the metal industry difficult. That is why Outokumpu intends to save money by cutting staff.

Outokumpu has, for example, a steel factory in Tornio, in Northern Finland. The factory's employees are sad about Outokumpu's plan to cut staff.

## VR is furloughing staff

![Pendolino on a railway track.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10366536)

Open image viewer

VR has lost passengers because of the coronavirus. Photo: Soile Laaksonen / VR Group

VR plans to furlough staff next year. During a furlough, the employee has no work duties and does not receive a salary.

VR may furlough about 4,000 employees. A furlough may last, for example, 3 months.

VR says that the reason for the furloughs is the coronavirus. Because of the coronavirus, VR now has far fewer passengers than usual.

## Finlandia nominees announced

![Anne Vuori-Kemilä, Tommi Kinnunen, Anni Kytömäki, Heikki Kännö, Ritva Hellsten, Ann-Luise Bertell](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11631858)

These are the authors whose books are Finlandia nominees: Anne Vuori-Kemilä, Tommi Kinnunen, Anni Kytömäki, Heikki Kännö, Ritva Hellsten and Ann-Luise Bertell. Photo: Karisto, Joonas Brandt, Anniina Mannila, Helena Kulmala, Aviador, Niklas Sandström

The Finlandia Prize nominees for fiction have been announced. There are 6 books among the nominees. A jury chose the nominees from 150 books.

Conductor Hannu Lintu will choose the final winner. On 25 November it will be announced which book wins the Finlandia.

The Finlandia Prize is Finland's most important literary prize. It is awarded every year. The prize is worth 30,000 euros.

## Guide dogs need foster families

![Jamppa Mikkola and guide dog Vilka](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10942536)

Open image viewer

A guide dog is an important help for a visually impaired person. Photo: Juulia Tillaeus / Yle

It should also be said that foster families are needed for guide dogs. The task of a guide dog is to help a visually impaired person.

A future guide dog spends its first year with a foster family, where it gets used to people. After that the actual training for the guide dog's task begins.

Foster families are, however, hard to find, because many people find it hard to give up the dog after a year together.
