## Klarna's data security problem

![Thiago Prudencio/SOPA](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12017473)

Klarna is a popular way to pay for online purchases. Photo: Thiago Prudencio / AOP

The payment intermediary company Klarna's system has a data security problem. This is what Yle's investigation says.

With Klarna it is possible to order goods online so that the bill goes to another person. That is why many crimes have been committed with the help of Klarna.

It is difficult for Finnish authorities to intervene in Klarna's operations. Klarna is a Swedish company, and it has customers in different parts of the world.

Klarna itself says that it has improved customers' safety.

## Finland's judgment withdrawn

![The European Court of Human Rights in Strasbourg.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6984625f0b4a5c9a4f8/1626104681)

Open image viewer

The European Court of Human Rights is in Strasbourg, France. Photo: Patric Seeger / AOP

The European Court of Human Rights has withdrawn a judgment that it gave to Finland in 2019.

The human rights court decided in 2019 that Finland had broken the European Convention on Human Rights.

The reason for the decision was the case of an Iraqi man. The man did not get asylum in Finland, and he had to return to Iraq. The man's daughter said that in Iraq the man was killed straight away.

That is why the European Court of Human Rights gave Finland a judgment and ordered that Finland must pay compensation to the daughter.

Later it turned out that the man's daughter had lied. The man has not died. That is why the judgment against Finland has now been withdrawn.

## Serious traffic accident in Eura

Eura is about 100 kilometres north of Turku. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

A serious accident has happened in Eura.

Because of the accident 6 people were taken to hospital. Among the injured are also 2 children.

Two cars were involved in the accident. They collided at a junction.

It is not yet known what caused the accident. The police are investigating the matter.

Eura is about 100 kilometres from Turku and Tampere.

## Unusually many hot days

![Rastila beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5168,h_2907,x_0,y_541/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83113760ec5e326c183/1626104380)

Open image viewer

In Finland it is not always cold. Photo: Jukka Lehto / Yle

Finland's summer is now unusually hot.

There have been more hot days than usual. On a hot day the temperature is over 25 degrees.

In June 8 hot days are normally measured. This year in June 25 hot days were measured.

This summer there has also been heat across the whole country. Yle's meteorologist Anniina Valtonen says that hot temperatures have often been measured at many weather stations.

Valtonen also says that the heat continues. It seems that there will be enough hot days until the latter part of July.

## Roller coaster turns 70

![Anna Pietilä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12019712)

Roller coaster and brake master Anna Pietilä. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

It is a day of celebration at Linnanmäki in Helsinki.

The Linnanmäki roller coaster turns exactly 70 years old today, on Tuesday.

The wooden roller coaster is still a favourite ride for many.

The Linnanmäki roller coaster opened on 13.7.1951
