# Sunday 30.8.2020 (radio)

Several corona infections in Kainuu. Air traffic will decrease for a long time. A big demonstration in Germany. Finns have gaps in their housing skills. Weather forecast.

## Coronavirus spread in Kainuu

![corona sample in tubes](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7118015f450d9d642cb/1598362316)

Tubes where corona samples are put. Photo: Ville Viitamäki / Yle

In Kainuu at least 14 people have caught the coronavirus. About 130 people have been exposed to the coronavirus, which means they have been close to people who have a corona infection.

There have not been many corona infections in Kainuu. That is why 14 corona infections there is a lot now.

In the whole of Finland, 28 new coronavirus infections were reported on Sunday.

## Air traffic will decrease for a long time

![Finnair Airbus 350 jet](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10896399)

Open image viewer

Few airplanes can be seen now. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

The corona situation has reduced air traffic a lot. Domestic air traffic within Finland has ended completely from many cities.

Domestic air traffic may decrease permanently, because people have learned to work remotely and hold meetings remotely. People's need to travel has decreased.

Later there will maybe be small airplanes that can fly within the country, even though there are few passengers.

## A big demonstration in Germany

![Demonstrators gathered in front of the Reichstag building in Berlin on Saturday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_99/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7134085f4b07f474e1b/1598752942)

Open image viewer

Demonstrators gathered in front of the Reichstag building in Berlin on Saturday. Photo: John Macdougall / AFP

Some Germans do not accept the corona restrictions. In Berlin, the capital of Germany, there was a big demonstration against the restrictions on Saturday. About 38,000 people took part in the demonstration.

The demonstrators do not accept Germany's corona restrictions, for example the mask requirement, travel restrictions or the ban on big events.

The German police arrested about 300 demonstrators who did not follow the police's instructions.

## Housing matters can be difficult

![Apartment prices](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10767422)

Open image viewer

Housing skills are lacking for some Finns, say property managers. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Housing skills are difficult for some Finns. People ask property managers more than before about ordinary housing matters.

For example, people ask why you have to pay a housing company fee for an apartment. Not everyone knows that you must make an electricity contract for an apartment so that the electricity works. Not everyone knows that it is good to take home insurance for an apartment.

Property managers look after the affairs of housing companies. They say that Finns need more advice about housing than before.
