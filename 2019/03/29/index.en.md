# Friday 29.3.2019 (radio)

The Prime Minister's EU withdrawal agreement is not acceptable to the British Parliament. The number of undocumented people has grown in Finland. Support for the Finns Party has risen. You can also get information about the elections in plain language.

## Brexit

![British Parliament rejected Prime Minister Theresa May's EU withdrawal agreement by a clear margin in its third vote on Friday, 29 March 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10714576)

British Prime Minister Theresa May. Photo: Mark Duffy / AFP

The Brexit problems in Britain continue.

The British Parliament rejected the withdrawal agreement on Friday. Prime Minister Theresa May negotiated it with the EU. Parliament has rejected the agreement for the 3rd time.

Now there is a danger that Britain will leave the European Union without an agreement. That is a bad option, for example for the economy. That is why Parliament will continue the discussion about Brexit on Monday.

The Brexit process started 3 years ago. Then the British decided in a referendum that Britain should leave the EU.

## Undocumented people

![Undocumented asylum seeker sits on a bed. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9539344)

Open image viewer

A person is undocumented if they are in the country without a permit. Photo: Carolus Manninen / Yle

The number of undocumented people has grown in Finland. A person is undocumented if they do not have a permit to be in Finland.

Among undocumented people there are people who have lived in Finland for a long time. There are also many families with children.

According to Helsinki Deaconess Institute, the number of undocumented people has grown because of Finland's strict asylum policy.

## Party poll

![Party support March 2019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10711733)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Yle has made a new party poll. Yle asked Finns which party they support.

In the new poll, support for the Finns Party has risen. 15 percent of respondents support the Finns Party. The party is the third largest in the poll.

The Finns Party's support is now bigger than the Centre Party's support. The Centre is the party of Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. The Centre's support is 14 percent.

Only the National Coalition and the biggest party in the poll, SDP, are ahead of the Finns Party. SDP's support is 20 percent, and the National Coalition's support is 16 percent.

Competition between parties is now tough, because the parliamentary elections are already in 2 weeks.

## Election debates

Next week there will be an election debate in plain language. In it, party representatives answer questions in plain language.

Yle is organising the plain-language election debate together with the Finnish Association for Intellectual Disability.

The plain-language debate will be published on Yle Areena on 4 April. The debate has subtitles.

Plain-language information about the elections can also be found in Selkosanomat. The Selkosanomat online magazine presents, for example, the parliamentary parties.

Plain-language election information has been collected on the Selkokeskus election pages. The address is selkokeskus.fi/eduskuntavaalit2019.
