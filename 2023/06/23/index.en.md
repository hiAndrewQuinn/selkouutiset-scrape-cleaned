The new Minister of Economic Affairs, Vilhelm Junnila of the Finns Party, apologises for his behaviour in the past. During the 2019 parliamentary elections, Junnila joked about his candidate number 88, which is a symbol of the far right.

Junnila apologises for this and says that he behaved badly.

Junnila also gave a speech at an event of a far-right organisation. Junnila says that he would no longer take part in the same kind of event.

The matter was discussed in Parliament on Thursday.

## Flat burglaries

![A Lithuanian gang carried out a large series of burglaries in southern Finland in summer 2012. They were especially looking for gold jewellery and money.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3422,h_1925,x_0,y_1816/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-1675354b65f3b4b42e/1627016788)

Open image viewer

The thief has messed up the place. Photo: Police

The number of flat burglaries in Finland has decreased in the 2000s. The insurance company Fennia says that people can now guard against thieves better than before. They lock and protect their homes better.

The police advise that in detached houses and terraced houses, doors and windows should be locked, even if the resident is away from home only for a short time. Expensive items are best put where nobody can see them from the window. Thieves want jewellery and electronics, for example.

If someone has broken into a home, the home should not be cleaned. It makes the police's work difficult when the police investigate a flat burglary.

Most flat burglaries are committed in June and July.

## Midsummer

![Midsummer bonfire at Kapustasalmi in Haukivuori. The weather in 2022 was warm, and dozens of boats are there. The bonfire burns on a small island.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97453462b6e8bb7e218/1656154478)

A midsummer bonfire is lit at Midsummer. Photo: Isto Matias Janhunen / Yle

Finland goes quiet on Friday, when people start to celebrate Midsummer, that is, the midsummer festival. Already on Thursday many people travel to their summer cottages or somewhere else to celebrate Midsummer.

On Thursday there is a lot of traffic on the roads and many passengers on the trains. On the big roads, the day before Midsummer Eve on Friday is usually very busy. Some roads may be congested.

The police monitor traffic closely. The police say that more than two hundred automatic cameras will monitor cars this Midsummer.

On Sunday too there is a lot of traffic, as people return home. For many, the summer holiday starts after Midsummer.
