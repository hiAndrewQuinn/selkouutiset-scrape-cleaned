# Thursday 11.6.2020 (radio)

The government is easing corona restrictions. Larger public events are also allowed. Schools are reporting the results of the joint application. Protection of the pangolin is improving.

## Corona restrictions are being eased

![Passengers at the Western Terminal in Helsinki on 11 June](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11396700)

Passengers at the Western Terminal in Helsinki on 11 June. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The government is removing some corona restrictions.

From Monday, you can travel to 6 countries without quarantine. You can travel to Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Denmark, Norway and Iceland.

You still may not go to Sweden, unless the trip is important.

The government also allows larger public events than before.

From the beginning of July, events with more than 500 spectators may be held outdoors. For example, football matches and cultural events will be easier to organise in July than they are now. Safe distances must still be kept.

Because of corona, 28 people are currently in hospital. 4 people are in intensive care.

## Good news for the pangolin

![Pangolin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11277116)

Open image viewer

Pangolin. Photo: Fabian Von Poser / AOP

Protection of pangolins is improving. China no longer accepts that pangolins are made into medicines. Pangolin scales have been widely used as medicine in China.

A pangolin is a mammal the size of a cat. It has a long tail and a covering of scales. Pangolins eat ants. Pangolins live in Southeast Asia and Africa.

There are 8 different species of pangolin. All species are endangered, because they are hunted without permission. Many pangolins are smuggled, especially to China and Vietnam.

The coronavirus pandemic is believed to have started at a Chinese market, where, among other things, pangolins and bats were sold.
