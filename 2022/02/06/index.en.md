## Debt ceiling

![overview](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_516/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5852295d5ea097698d2/1566482621)

Open image viewer

A debt ceiling could make it harder to buy a home. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

Finland will not introduce a debt ceiling for first-time home buyers. This is what the Ministry of Finance proposes.

A debt ceiling means the upper limit of the loan that one person can get. A debt ceiling can reduce people's indebtedness. A debt ceiling makes it harder to buy a home if the person does not have enough of their own money. That is why many people do not like debt ceilings.

The Ministry of Finance has made a plan that first-time home buyers will not get a debt ceiling.

## A medal for Finland at the Winter Olympics

Finland has won its first medal at the Beijing Winter Olympics.

Skier Iivo Niskanen won the bronze medal in the men's combined cross-country skiing, where Niskanen finished third.

Gold in the men's combined cross-country skiing was won by Russia's Aleksandr Bolshunov.

## Joint Responsibility fundraising

![Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5802,h_3264,x_2,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8807526194f38aaf0f5/1641030877)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö opened the Joint Responsibility fundraising. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

President Sauli Niinistö has opened the 2022 Joint Responsibility fundraising. This year the fundraising supports children and young people who are suffering from the corona pandemic.

President Niinistö says that the corona pandemic is heavy and oppressive for many children. That is why children need help. Niinistö says that children need an adult nearby who cares for and supports the child.

The Joint Responsibility fundraising helps children in Finland and in developing countries.

## Sámi National Day

![Áslat-Jon and Sivi Länsman](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1969,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6391335e3abe44dee40/1580908360)

Open image viewer

Sámi national costumes. Sámi costumes are decorative. The design of the costume varies in different areas. Photo: Vesa Toppari / Yle

Sámi National Day was celebrated on Sunday. The Sámi are an indigenous people. The Sámi have their own history, language and culture. Sámi live in Finland, Sweden, Norway and Russia.

About 10,000 Sámi live in Finland. The Sámi live in Sápmi in Lapland. Many Sámi also live, for example, in Helsinki.

The Sámi speak the Sámi language. The Sámi also have their own flag.

## Queen Elizabeth

![Queen Elizabeth II smiles as she looks at memories from past jubilee years on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2999,h_1686,x_0,y_69/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91097561fe5885f3998/1644058964)

Queen Elizabeth II looked at memories of the jubilee years on Friday at Windsor Castle. Photo: Steve Parsons/MEGA/AOP

Britain's Queen Elizabeth II has been on the throne for 70 years today, on Sunday. Elizabeth became queen in 1952, when Elizabeth's father, King George VI, died. Elizabeth was then 25 years old.

Queen Elizabeth is now 95 years old. She still does the queen's work. Queen Elizabeth has been on the throne longer than any other ruler of Britain.

The queen's 70th year as ruler is celebrated in Britain especially in June.
