# Sunday 8.12.2019 (radio)

The SDP proposes that Sanna Marin becomes prime minister. The government parties will not change. The strike in industry starts on Monday. More than 40 people died in a fire in India. On Sunday, Jean Sibelius and Finnish Music Day were celebrated.

## Sanna Marin

![Sanna Marin, chosen as the SDP's candidate for prime minister, with a bouquet of flowers after the choice at the SDP party council meeting in Helsinki on 8 December 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11108433)

Sanna Marin will become prime minister. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Finland will get a new prime minister. She is Sanna Marin.

The SDP, the Social Democratic Party, proposes that Marin becomes prime minister. The previous prime minister, Antti Rinne, resigned about a week ago.

Sanna Marin is 34 years old. She is now Minister of Transport and Communications.

Parliament will elect the prime minister perhaps as early as Tuesday. Sanna Marin's party, the SDP, is the largest party in Parliament.

## New government

![Parliament's plenary hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10873212)

Open image viewer

Five parties in Parliament are part of the government. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / AOP

The prime minister leads the government of Finland. The parties in Sanna Marin's government are the same parties that were already in Antti Rinne's government.

There are 5 government parties. They are the SDP, the Centre Party, the Greens, the Left Alliance and the Swedish People's Party (RKP). They intend to continue the same things that were important to the previous government too.

Finland's government is changing because of the dispute between the government parties. The Centre Party no longer trusted the SDP's Antti Rinne, and that is why Prime Minister Rinne had to resign.

## Industry strike

![Oras Group factory in Rauma](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6194655de8deaf6d411/1575542556)

Open image viewer

Many factories stop when workers go on strike, that is, stop doing their work. Photo: Tapio Termonen / Yle

A strike in industry starts on Monday. The strike will stop many factories in different parts of Finland. The strike lasts 3 days.

Almost 100,000 workers are taking part in the strike. They will not do any work during the first part of the week because of a labour dispute. Workers and employers in the industrial sectors are arguing about pay and working hours.

## Fire in India

![Police and firefighters near the place where the fire spread disastrously on the morning of 8 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11108038)

Open image viewer

The fire started at a factory in Delhi. Photo: Rajat Gupta / EPA

There has been a serious fire in India. More than 40 people died in the fire.

The fire was in the city of Delhi. It started early in the morning at a bag factory.

More than 100 workers were still sleeping in the factory when the fire started. Rescuers saved about 60 people from the factory.

The cause of the fire may have been an electrical fault. The factory did not have a permit to operate.

## Sibelius Day

![Jean Sibelius](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9890474)

Open image viewer

Composer Jean Sibelius. Photo: Santeri Levas / Finnish Museum of Photography

On Sunday, Jean Sibelius and Finnish Music Day were celebrated. The day is always celebrated on 8 December, because it is Sibelius's birthday.

Jean Sibelius is Finland's national composer. He was born in 1865 and died in 1957. Many people know Sibelius's music, for example Finlandia.

Jean Sibelius and Finnish Music Day is a flag day.
