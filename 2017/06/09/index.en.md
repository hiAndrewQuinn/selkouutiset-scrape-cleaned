# Friday 9.6.2017 (radio)

In Britain the Conservatives have lost their majority in the House of Commons. The Greens and the Finns Party will choose new leaders at the weekend. It is sometimes hard to get into health care. A mourning stamp has been published in memory of Mauno Koivisto.

## In Britain the Conservatives failed in the election

![Labour supporters cheer the entry of Paul Sweeney into Parliament in Glasgow, Scotland, on Thursday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9659287)

In Britain the Conservatives have lost their majority in the House of Commons.

The Conservatives are still the largest party, but they can no longer form a government on their own.

Labour gained more seats, but the gap to the Conservatives is more than 50 seats. After the election result, Labour's leader Jeremy Corbyn demanded that the Conservatives' leader, Prime Minister Theresa May, resign.

## The Greens and the Finns Party vote for a chair

![Graphic of supporters of the Greens and the Finns Party.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9659115)

There are differences among the supporters of the Greens and the Finns Party. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

At the weekend 2 Finnish parties have important elections. The Greens and the Finns Party will choose new chairs at their party congresses.

Forecasts say that the main candidates for the Finns Party are MP and Minister of European Affairs, Culture and Sport Sampo Terho, and MEP Jussi Halla-aho. Terho wants the Finns Party to become a nationalist catch-all party. Halla-aho wants to focus on immigration matters. The other candidates are MPs Leena Meri and Veera Ruoho, and also Riku Nevanpää.

The candidates for the chair of the Greens are deputy chair Maria Ohisalo, researcher Mika Flöjt, and MPs Touko Aalto, Emma Kari, Krista Mikkonen and Olli-Poika Parviainen. The challenge for the new chair is to keep the party's popularity. The Greens did well in the municipal elections and have had the best result in the party's history in opinion polls. The Greens also want to be a party that leads the government.

**Correction 11.6. at 21:00: The Greens will choose their chair on Saturday 17.6.**

## It is sometimes hard to get into health care

![Interior view of a corridor at a health centre hospital in Kokkola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7977560)

Open image viewer

Access to health care is not always easy, the survey says. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

More than half of health care patients say that getting an appointment is sometimes difficult.

However, in the survey by the National Institute for Health and Welfare, patients say that they are treated well. 75 percent of respondents said that privacy is respected. Negative experiences were most common among those who have financial difficulties or who use services a lot.

The survey was carried out by Statistics Finland.

## A mourning stamp for Mauno Koivisto was published

![Posti issued a mourning stamp in honour of President Mauno Koivisto. The stamp was designed by Timo Berry.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9659298)

The Koivisto mourning stamp can be bought from Posti in sheets of 10 stamps. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Posti has published a mourning stamp in memory of President Mauno Koivisto.

The blue-tinted stamp has a picture of Koivisto and the Grand Cross of the Order of the White Rose of Finland. The stamp also has Koivisto's signature and his years of birth and death. The stamp was designed by Timo Berry. The print run of the stamp is one million.

President Mauno Koivisto died on 12 May in Helsinki at the age of 93. Koivisto was President of the Republic of Finland for 12 years, from 1982 to 1994.

The last time a mourning stamp was published in Finland was in 1986, in memory of President Urho Kekkonen.
