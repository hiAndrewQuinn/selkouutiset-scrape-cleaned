## The situation of Ukrainians

![Rubble is being cleared next to apartment blocks destroyed in a Russian air strike in March in the town of Borobjanka in Ukraine, on 14 December 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7348,h_4125,x_0,y_767/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1048433639a09046acf9/1672247273)

Open image viewer

Russian air strikes have caused a lot of destruction in Ukraine. Photo: Oleg Petrasyuk / EPA

The UN, that is, the United Nations, is worried about the situation of Ukrainians.

The situation of Ukrainians will get worse if Russian air strikes on Ukraine continue. This is what UN High Commissioner for Human Rights Volker Türk says.

Türk says that already many millions of people are suffering in Ukraine because of Russia's attacks. Russia has fired missiles at power plants, for example. That is why many Ukrainians have no electricity.

Türk also says that because of Russia's attacks, more refugees may come to Europe from Ukraine.

The war in Ukraine has lasted almost 10 months. Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

Mikko Savola will serve as Finland's Defence Minister at the start of next year.

Savola is an MP. Savola's party is the Centre Party.

Savola will become Defence Minister Antti Kaikkonen's deputy. Kaikkonen is on paternity leave in January and February.

A child was born to Defence Minister Kaikkonen's family in the summer. Kaikkonen is the first male minister to take paternity leave.

## ECB interest rates

![The picture shows European Central Bank president Christine Lagarde. In the background are the River Main and the ECB headquarters.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12611696)

Open image viewer

ECB President Christine Lagarde. Photo: AOP / Rea Lemström / Yle

The European Central Bank, that is, the ECB, is raising interest rates. The ECB is raising its key interest rates by 0.5 percentage points.

Because of the European Central Bank's decision, ordinary banks are also raising interest rates. For example, housing loans become more expensive, because customers have to pay more interest on the loan than before.

## Pensioners working

![A pensioner with a wheeled shopping bag in front of a zebra crossing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8696056170183e47e46/1712580693)

Open image viewer

Pensioners are needed in working life. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Many pensioners are offered work. A new study says that about 30 percent of pensioners have received job offers.

Many Finns also want to work in retirement. About 40 percent of the study's respondents are interested in paid work during their retirement years.

It is believed that in the coming years pensioners will do more work than they do now. Pensioners are needed in working life, because many sectors have too few workers.

## Finnish tap water

![Water being poured into a glass.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6829865)

Open image viewer

Finnish tap water is healthy. Photo: Yle

The quality of Finnish tap water is good. This is what the report of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says.

The quality of tap water was studied in different parts of Finland. More than 99 percent, that is, about 100 percent, of the water samples met the health requirements.

It is safe for people to drink tap water across the whole of Finland.
