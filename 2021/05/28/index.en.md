# Friday 28.5.2021 (radio)

The European Medicines Agency approves Pfizer's corona vaccine for children too. Finns have a positive attitude towards corona vaccines. 8 percent of Finland's residents have a foreign background. Soilimaria Korhonen has won the Clear Speaker of the Year award. The weather warms up.

## Corona vaccine for children too

![Nurses giving the Pfizer vaccine at the Vantaa Energia Areena vaccination centre. 26.5.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3517,h_1978,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81119660adfea479224/1635310032)

The Pfizer corona vaccine is suitable for 12-year-olds already. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The European Medicines Agency has made a new decision on corona vaccines.

The Medicines Agency now also approves Pfizer's corona vaccine for children. The Medicines Agency says that Pfizer's corona vaccine is suitable for 12–15-year-olds.

The USA and Canada have already earlier approved Pfizer's corona vaccine for children over 12.

## Finns and the corona vaccine

![A nurse gives a corona vaccine to a patient at Energia Areena in Vantaa. 26.5.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4256,h_2394,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81130360ae193d3b3bf/1622031692)

Finns think that corona vaccines are a good thing.

A new survey shows that 95 percent of Finns have a positive attitude towards the corona vaccine. Almost all Finns also intend to take the corona vaccine.

## Friday's corona figures

On Friday, 2 new corona deaths were reported. In total, 948 people have died in Finland because of corona.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare also said on Friday that there are 182 new coronavirus infections in Finland.

## Residents with a foreign background

![A customer shopping in a grocery store.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11568170)

Open image viewer

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

About 450,000 people with a foreign background live in Finland. That means that 8 percent of Finland's residents have a foreign background.

The biggest groups of people with a foreign background are those with a Russian background and those with an Estonian background.

The figures are from Statistics Finland.

## Clear Speaker of the Year

![A woman with a certificate of honour and a bouquet of flowers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11953892)

Open image viewer

Clear Speaker of the Year Soilimaria Korhonen. Photo: Markku Juusola / Selkokeskus

Soilimaria Korhonen has won the Clear Speaker of the Year award. Korhonen received the award in Helsinki on Friday, when International Clear Language Day was celebrated.

The Clear Speaker of the Year award is given for work done for clear language.

Soilimaria Korhonen is a teacher at Savonia University of Applied Sciences. Korhonen has brought clear-language studies to the social and health sectors. For example, nursing students at Savonia learn clear language through practical exercises.

![Three women and one man.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11953904)

Non-Discrimination Ombudsman Kristina Stenman (on the left) and the final candidates Yle Nyheter på lätt svenska, the National Institute for Health and Welfare and Soilimaria Korhonen. Photo: Markku Juusola / Selkokeskus

The organiser of the Clear Speaker of the Year competition is the Clear Language Advisory Board. The board's panel chose 3 candidates for the final competition, and Non-Discrimination Ombudsman Kristina Stenman chose the winner from them.

## Weekend weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11954399)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

The weekend is dry, meaning no rain falls. At first the weather is cool, and especially the nights are cold.

On Saturday the sun shines in the south and west. In the east and north it is cloudy. On Saturday the temperature is between 10 and 15 degrees in a large part of the country.

On Sunday the weather warms up, and in a large part of the country the temperature is between 15 and 20 degrees.
