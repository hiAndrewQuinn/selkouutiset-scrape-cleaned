# Tuesday 5.12.2017 (radio)

Divorce is expensive. Tax refunds have been paid. Parliament has held a ceremonial session. Independence Day will be celebrated in freezing weather. The Finnish school in New York sends greetings to 100-year-old Finland.

## Divorce is expensive

![A woman takes a ring off her finger](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9506066)

Open image viewer

Divorce brings many costs. For example, more money goes on housing . Photo: AOP

Divorce is expensive. Last year, 13,500 married couples in Finland got divorced. Yle found out that, for example, the costs of a married couple with one child rise by several hundred euros a month. The rise happens if the partners move from their shared home into homes they each own. Often one spouse stays in the old home and starts paying for housing alone. There can be a big loan on the home.

Money can also go on lawyers if the couple start to argue. Legal aid offices can help if you cannot afford a lawyer.

After a divorce, life in the capital region is about 200 euros a month more expensive than elsewhere in Finland. In the south, for example, housing is expensive.

## Tax refunds have been paid into bank accounts

Tax refunds have arrived in bank accounts. About 3.5 million people get refunds. The refunds add up to 2.6 billion euros.

The average tax refund is a little over 700 euros.

## For Finland's 100th anniversary you can fly the flag all night

![A Finnish flag flies in front of pine trees](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9962710)

Open image viewer

Usually in Finland you can fly the flag all night only at Midsummer. The Finland 100 celebration is an exception. Photo: Anne Saarinen / AOP

Parliament has held a ceremonial session in honour of 100 years of Finland's independence. There were many invited guests at the ceremonial session.

At the session, Parliament decided on a share capital of 50 million euros, which the foundation of the anniversary year's children's fund will get.

In honour of tomorrow's 100th anniversary, flag flying is different from normal. Finnish flags could be raised on the flagpole already today at 6 pm, and they can stay on the pole until 10 pm on the evening of Independence Day. You can do this if the flag can be lit during the dark hours.

Usually you can fly the flag all night only at Midsummer.

It will be freezing on Independence Day

![Wednesday's weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9962913)

Open image viewer

The weather forecast promises nice weather for Independence Day. Photo: Yle

Independence Day will be celebrated in freezing weather.

During the evening, a few centimetres of snow may still fall in Southern Finland and in the central part of the country. On Independence Day the ground may be white at least in South-West Finland.

In the morning there may be a little snow on the ground even in Helsinki. On Independence Day it will be fairly sunny in the west during the day. In Eastern Finland there will be snow showers. From the central part of the country to Lapland it will be partly cloudy and dry.

The Finnish school in New York sends greetings

![The Finnish school in New York.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9962622)

A large number of Finnish children and young people live in different parts of the world and study the Finnish language and Finnish culture in Finnish schools. Now that independent Finland turns 100, the Finnish schools are sending video greetings to Finland.

The newest greetings come from the USA, from the Finnish school in New York. You can watch the greetings on the Clear News website at yle.fi/selkouutiset, in the Finland 100 section.
