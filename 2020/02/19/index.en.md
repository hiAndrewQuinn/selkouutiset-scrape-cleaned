# Wednesday 19.2.2020 (radio)

The actions of Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto are being investigated. The Finnish Immigration Service has checked old asylum decisions. Britain is planning new rules for immigrants. A winter without snow can increase winter depression.

## Foreign Minister Haavisto

![Pekka Haavisto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11217854)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Stephanie Lecocq / EPA

The actions of Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto are being investigated. On Wednesday, Parliament decided that a preliminary investigation will be made into Haavisto's actions.

The aim is to find out whether Haavisto tried to get rid of a civil servant he disagreed with. Haavisto wanted to move the civil servant to other duties when the al-Hol matter was being dealt with. Now the question is whether Haavisto acted correctly.

Al-Hol is a Syrian camp where family members of Isis terrorists also live. Among them are Finnish women and children.

In Finland there has been an argument about whether Finns in al-Hol should be helped or not. Foreign Minister Haavisto and an important civil servant also disagreed about this.

## Finland's asylum decisions

![The door of the Finnish Immigration Service office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10908912)

Open image viewer

Photo: Lotta Sundström / Yle

The Finnish Immigration Service has checked old asylum decisions. The Immigration Service checked several hundred decisions in total.

The decisions were checked because the European Court of Human Rights said in the autumn that Finland had broken a human rights agreement in one decision.

In the check, shortcomings were found in some of the decisions. Therefore, 10 asylum seekers may make a new application. These applicants did not get asylum before, but now they can apply for it again.

## Immigrants in Britain

![People walking in central London in sunny weather on 11 February 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1969,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6442005e4cc1df9c99a/1634892761)

Britain is planning new rules for immigrants.

Britain intends to give points to people who want to go to Britain to work. A person must get enough points to be allowed to move to Britain.

Points are given for education, language skills and income. An immigrant must have a job ready before moving to Britain.

The rules are the same for citizens of EU countries and citizens of other countries. Britain left the European Union at the end of January.

## The darkness of winter

![At JOJO in Seinäjoki, breakfast and bright light were offered to everyone interested during mental health week.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11082488)

A bright light lamp and healthy food help when the dark winter is distressing. Photo: Anne Elhaimer / Yle

The winter in Southern Finland has been rainy and grey. White snow is missing completely. It can increase winter depression, which means that people feel bad because it is dark.

Research professor Timo Partonen gives advice against winter depression. A bright light lamp, exercise and healthy food help a person to get through the winter. It is also important to meet friends and mates, professor Partonen says.
