## Balcony safety

First, some news about balcony safety.

The safety of balconies is being studied in Finland.

The reason is an accident that occurred in Tikkurila, Vantaa, in June. A person died after falling through a balcony railing.

An expert told Yle that the cause is the glass panel's fasteners.

The glass may have bent and come loose. However, the investigation is still ongoing.

The safety of balconies is being studied in Finland.

There are two types of balconies to explore.

Other balconies have glass railings.

Other balconies have cement fiberboard.

There may be tens of thousands of risky balconies in Finland.

For example, the rental housing company Sato has banned the use of approximately 1,600 balconies.

## Tick research

Finally, news about research on ticks.

The University of Turku collects ticks through a citizen collection.

People can send ticks to the university for research.

The University of Turku has already received approximately 15,000 ticks from people.

Researcher **Janne Sulku** says that ticks are collected from different parts of Finland.

Ticks are being studied for genetic factors and pathogens, for example.

The University of Turku collects ticks through a citizen collection.

The university hopes to see more ticks from Satakunta and Ostrobothnia.
