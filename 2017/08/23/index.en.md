# Wednesday 23.8.2017 (radio)

Two new men arrested over the Turku stabbings. The government wants to reduce the number of undocumented people. Inequality in Helsinki's schools has decreased. African women take care of their hair themselves.

## Two new men arrested

![A sea of candles and flowers commemorates the victims of the Turku stabbing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9790535)

The police arrested two new men in connection with the Turku stabbing.  Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

The police have arrested two new men in connection with the Turku stabbings. The men's nationality is not certain. The men have said in Finland that they are from Algeria, and in Sweden that they are from Morocco.

The police are investigating whether a network linked to extremist thinking has been active in the Turku area. Yle has received information that the main suspect in the Turku stabbings wanted to join the terrorist organisation ISIS. According to police information, he has had contacts abroad.

## The government wants to reduce the number of undocumented people

![A man sits at a table. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9776206)

Open image viewer

There are attempts to reduce the number of undocumented people who have received a negative asylum decision. Photo: Wasim Khuzam / Yle

There are 1400 people in Finland who have stayed in Finland after a negative asylum decision. They have refused to return to their home country. The police force them to return, but many wait a long time to be returned. The wait is because they do not have the necessary documents.

The government wants to reduce the number of these undocumented people. Minister of the Interior Paula Risikko proposed that those who help undocumented people should be punished.

## Inequality in schools has decreased

![A child does maths exercises.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9590488)

Open image viewer

Inequality in Helsinki's schools has decreased. Photo: Elisa Kinnunen / Yle

In Helsinki, inequality has decreased now that some schools have been favoured. For many years now, schools in low-income residential areas have been given more money.

This has especially benefited Finnish-speaking boys and pupils from immigrant families. Now more and more girls with an immigrant background continue studying at upper secondary school after comprehensive school. With the extra money, the schools have hired, for example, school assistants.

## African women take care of their hair themselves

![A woman braids a boy's hair](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9790813)

Open image viewer

Mother Omnia Ali braids Wakela Tia's hair. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

In Finland there are only a few hairdressers and barbers who take care of Afro hair. For example, from Kajaani in eastern Finland you have to go to Helsinki if you want a skilled hairdresser.

Omnia Ali, who came from Sudan, lives in Kajaani and takes care of hair with home-made oil. African women teach their own hair care methods to their children. The methods are different in every country.
