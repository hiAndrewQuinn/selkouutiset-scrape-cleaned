# Tuesday 21.5.2019 (radio)

Government negotiations continue. Racism has increased in Britain after the Brexit vote. Huawei phones may be left without updates. Austrian Formula One driver Niki Lauda has died.

## Finland's government negotiations

![Antti Rinne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10794716)

Antti Rinne. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The government negotiations have continued. SDP party leader Antti Rinne is leading the negotiations.

In the government negotiations the parties have discussed the economy. Rinne says that the new government does not need to cut state spending. In Rinne's view, the state can increase the use of money.

5 parties are taking part in the government negotiations. The SDP party leader is leading the negotiations, because the SDP won the election and is now the largest party in Parliament.

## Racism in Britain

![Demonstrators demanded a new referendum in London in June 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10664889)

Open image viewer

The Brexit dispute shows up in many ways in Britain. Photo: Andy Rain / EPA

Racism has increased in Britain. Racism has grown after the Brexit vote.

The Brexit vote was in the summer of 2016. At that time more than half of Britons wanted Britain to leave the European Union.

In early 2016, before the Brexit vote, a little under 60 per cent of members of ethnic minorities experienced discrimination. Now the figure is already over 70 per cent. The figures are from a British research institute.

The Guardian newspaper reports that the Brexit dispute also shows up in other ways in Britain. For example, the number of racist hate crimes has risen.

## Huawei's problems

![A podcast playing on a smartphone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10794153)

Open image viewer

A Huawei Honor phone. Photo: Massimiliano Finzi / AOP

The Chinese phone company Huawei has problems. Huawei phones may be left without updates.

The phones of Huawei will not stop working, but, for example, the security of the phones may get worse.

The reason is that the American company Google is ending Android system updates for Huawei phones.

US President Donald Trump has decided that Huawei may not buy American technology. However, the US is giving Huawei and its American partners time to change. That is why the ban will only come into force in August.

## Niki Lauda has died

![Niki Lauda at the Australian Formula One Grand Prix in 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10793079)

Open image viewer

Niki Lauda in 2018. Photo: Dave Acree / EPA

Formula One driver Niki Lauda has died. The Austrian Lauda was 70 years old.

Niki Lauda was the Formula One world champion 3 times, about 40 years ago.

In 1976 Lauda was in a bad accident. He drove his racing car off the track when the speed was over 200 kilometres an hour.

In the accident Lauda was badly burned, and he nearly died. However, Lauda came back to motor racing only a little over a month after the accident.
