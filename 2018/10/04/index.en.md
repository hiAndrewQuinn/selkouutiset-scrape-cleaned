# Thursday 4.10.2018 (radio)

The Indonesian earthquake has affected the lives of hundreds of thousands of children. The SDP is the most popular party in the September opinion poll. The harms of shift work can be reduced with good shift planning. Animal Week has begun.

## The destruction in Sulawesi

![Indonesian women mourn in the ruins of a destroyed hotel in Palu on 3 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10438189)

The Indonesian earthquake has affected the lives of at least 600,000 children, says the child rights organisation Save the Children.

The organisation says that the parents of many children have died or gone missing. The children are also in shock after the natural disaster.

On Friday there was an earthquake on the Indonesian island of Sulawesi. After that a tsunami, that is, a big wave, hit the shore.

The earthquake and tsunami caused huge destruction. More than 1,400 dead have been found. In addition, more than 2,500 people have been injured.

Help has reached the area slowly. Now Finnish aid workers are also leaving for Sulawesi.

Yle has again asked Finns which party they support.

In the September survey, the largest opposition party, the SDP, that is, the Social Democrats, gets the most support. The SDP's support has risen from last month and is now just over 22 percent.

The support of the Left Alliance and the Christian Democrats has also risen slightly.

The support of all the other parties has fallen.

## Shift work

![Two helmeted workers in front of an industrial plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10437519)

Open image viewer

Hundreds of thousands of Finns do shift work or irregular working hours. Photo: Hybrid Images / Getty Images

In Finland about 25 percent of workers do shift work or irregular working hours.

Studies show that shift work causes tiredness, sleep disorders and stomach troubles. It also increases the risk of several illnesses, such as cancer.

However, the harms of shift work can be reduced if shifts are planned well, says a new study by the Finnish Institute of Occupational Health.

For example, it is good if a shift is at most 10 hours long. There must be at least 11 hours of free time between shifts. There may be at most six consecutive shifts. It is good to have at least two free days in a row.

It is, however, especially important to listen to the employee's own wishes, the study says.

## Animal Week

![Puppy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10345753)

Open image viewer

The theme of Animal Week is the responsible acquisition of a pet. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Today International Animal Day has been celebrated. At the same time, Animal Week began.

The theme of Animal Week this year is the responsible acquisition of a pet.

The Finnish Federation of Animal Welfare Associations also awarded its Animal Welfare Act of the Year award today. The award went to the Helsinki Police Department.

The Helsinki Police Department received the award because it has set up a new investigation group that focuses on animal welfare crimes.
