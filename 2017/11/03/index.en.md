## People smuggling

![Border guard passport border passport check border station Imatra border station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7732162)

The Border Guard has investigated people smuggling. Photo: Yle

A criminal organisation has illegally brought people from Africa to Finland and other parts of Europe. People were brought to Finland, for example, via Russia.

The authorities suspect that the criminal organisation's activities went on for many years. 15 people were brought to Finland and more than 150 people to other parts of Europe.

In the summer, the Finnish authorities had a big operation against the criminal organisation. During the operation, a Russian man was arrested.

The case is now being examined by the prosecutor's office.

## The Emmaus work circle

![Goods donated to charity flea markets are in worse condition than before. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8800539)

Open image viewer

The Emmaus organisation helps people get work on Åland. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

On Swedish-speaking Åland, the Emmaus organisation helps people who need work.

Emmaus has already helped many people, for example Romanians and Bulgarians. The organisation has a work circle through which EU citizens can get work.

It is easy for people on Åland to offer work, because the Emmaus work circle takes care of the employer's obligations, for example social security payments.

Romanian Amdreea Docuta has got cleaning work through the work circle. Now Docuta is learning Swedish, because she wants to train as a kitchen assistant.

The Emmaus work circle has been running for 2 years, and it has helped almost 50 people.

## Inflecting names

![Minister of Finance Petteri Orpo in an interview with Yle on 27.06.2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9693014)

Minister "Orvon" or "Orpon" smile? Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

It is sometimes hard for Finns to know the right way to inflect a name. The goal is that names are inflected in the same way as other words. Still, some people inflect, for example, the first name Satu into the form "Sadun" and others into the form "Satun".

Expert Ulla Onkamo says that both forms are correct when it is a name.

Onkamo thinks it is polite to listen to what the owner of the name thinks. For example, Minister Petteri Orpo hopes that newspapers write "Orpon", not "Orvon".  The wish is followed and I am happy about it, Minister Orpo says.

## All Saints' Day

![A grave candle on a grave](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8417441)

Open image viewer

On All Saints' Day, candles are lit on graves. Photo: Nina Keski-Korpela / Yle

All Saints' Day is on Saturday. On All Saints' Day, Finns remember relatives and friends who have died. That is why candles are taken to graves.

All Saints' Day causes changes to traffic. For example, in the Helsinki region, local trains, buses and trams follow Sunday timetables on Saturday.

VR's long-distance trains run normally on All Saints' Day, but there are changes to long-distance bus timetables. It is a good idea to check long-distance bus departure times on the Matkahuolto website.
