## Finnair's result improved

![An aircraft at the airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10016190)

Open image viewer

Finnair's passenger numbers are growing. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The Finnish airline Finnair is doing well.

The company's result last year was over 170 million euros. That is more than three times as much as in 2016. Then the result was about 55 million euros.

Finnair carried 12 million passengers last year. Passenger numbers have grown especially between Europe and Asia.

Many of Finnair's Asian passengers want to visit Lapland. Lapland is already a more popular destination than Paris or Rome, the airline says.

## Summer cottages are getting cheaper

![A woman walks in the yard of a summer cottage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10078228)

Open image viewer

Summer cottages are getting cheaper, especially in Lapland and North Karelia. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

It is now cheaper than before to buy a Finnish summer cottage.

Summer cottage prices fell by about four percent last year compared with the year before.

However, the differences in cottage prices are big in different parts of Finland. The cheapest cottage can be found in Lapland, and the most expensive cottages are in Uusimaa.

## The Nordic Resistance Movement may continue

![A demonstration by the Nordic Resistance Movement in Helsinki on 6 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9956963)

Open image viewer

The Nordic Resistance Movement may continue its activities. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The neo-Nazi organisation the Nordic Resistance Movement may continue its activities.

The Turku Court of Appeal rejected the National Police Board's demand that the organisation be placed under a temporary ban on activities.

The Pirkanmaa District Court had earlier decided that the Nordic Resistance Movement can be banned.

## Already 50 000 undocumented people in Sweden

![Sergels torg is a meeting place. Some undocumented people spend the night on the squares and streets.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9260041)

Open image viewer

In Stockholm, the capital of Sweden, undocumented people gather at Sergels torg. Photo: Bengt Jägerskog

There are already about 50 000 undocumented people in Sweden, that is, people living in the country without permission.

Undocumented people have received a negative asylum decision in Sweden, but they have not wanted to leave the country.

The government estimates that tens of thousands more undocumented people will come to the country in the coming years.

## Finland ready to pay more to the EU

![Stars of the European Union.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10078884)

Open image viewer

Finland may pay more money to the EU in the future than it does now. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The Finnish government says that Finland can pay more money to the EU.

The EU has to ask member states for more money, because Britain is leaving the EU.

The EU budget has been about one percent of member states' gross national product. Now the share is being raised to 1.1 percent.

Finland is ready to pay more to the EU, because it wants to keep the agricultural and regional support funding.

Finland also wants the EU to direct more money to immigration and defence.

## Upper secondary schools dance the graduation dances

![Graduates dance at Jyväskylä Lyseo upper secondary school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10078713)

Open image viewer

At the graduation dances, people want to look very fine. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

Graduation day has been celebrated at upper secondary schools today.

On graduation day the oldest pupils at the upper secondary school change, when the final-year students leave school and prepare for the matriculation examination.

The school's new, oldest pupils dance at the traditional graduation dances.

At the graduation dances the pupils have fine party clothes and hairstyles.
