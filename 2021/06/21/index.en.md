## Chairperson of the Finns Party

![Jussi Halla-aho](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82383260d0844f5bb12/1624278170)

The chairperson of the Finns Party is changing.

The chairperson of the Finns Party is now Jussi Halla-aho. Halla-aho said on Monday that he does not intend to continue in the post of chairperson.

The Finns Party has its party congress in August. At that time the party's new chairperson will also be chosen.

Jussi Halla-aho has been chairperson of the Finns Party for 4 years. He started in the post of chairperson in 2017.

During Halla-aho's time, the Finns Party has, for example, opposed immigration. In the view of the Finns Party it is not a good thing that many immigrants come to Finland.

## Swedish government crisis

![Stefan Löfven at a press conference in Stockholm on Sunday 20 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_272/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82356560d008aa80492/1624250223)

Swedish Prime Minister Stefan Löfven. Photo: AFP / Lehtikuva

There is a political crisis in Sweden, Finland's neighbouring country. The Swedish government is falling, that is, it can no longer continue its work.

The Swedish parliament voted on the government on Monday. The government did not get the support of the majority of parliament.

The Swedish government's prime minister is Stefan Löfven. He is a social democrat.

Löfven must now decide whether he tries to put together a new government or orders new elections in Sweden.

## Travel to Finland

![The Helsinki West Harbour terminal in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11329952)

Open image viewer

Ports have been quiet because of corona. Now trips are starting again. Photo: Tmo Korhonen / AOP

Foreigners can now get into Finland more easily. The rules changed on Monday. The rules have been strict because of corona.

Now commuting trips from EU countries to Finland are allowed again. That is why, for example, commuting between Estonia and Finland becomes easier.

Tourists can also get to Finland more easily. Tourists may come to Finland from EU countries if they have received 2 coronavirus vaccinations or they have had corona.

## The longest day of the year

![A landscape where the sun shines near the horizon over a river bay on a summer night. The trees have leaves and the grass is green.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_169/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6955465efd6c4f93d27/1684138947)

Monday was the longest day of the year.

In southern Finland the length of the day is almost 19 hours. Right in the north the sun does not set at this time of year at all.

The longest day of the year has also been hot. The temperature has been about 30 degrees in a large part of the country.
