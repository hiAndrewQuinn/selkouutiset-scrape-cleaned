# Sunday 15.7.2018 (radio)

There are demonstrations in Helsinki. European countries and migrants. Blue-green algae spoil the bathing water. The weather stays warm.

## Demonstrations today and tomorrow

![Demonstration props.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10305514)

Open image viewer

People in Helsinki are protesting today and tomorrow, when the presidents of the USA and Russia are visiting. Photo: Liisa Takala / Yle

There have been demonstrations in Helsinki today. They were linked to the US and Russian presidents meeting in Helsinki tomorrow.

About 2 thousand people marched in a procession in Helsinki. They wanted to say that human rights, democracy, peace and love are important things.

The youth of the Finns Party organised their own demonstration. They welcomed the US President to Finland. There will be many different demonstrations tomorrow too.

## France and Malta take in migrants

![Migrants rescued from the sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10292033)

Open image viewer

In June, migrants were rescued from the Mediterranean and brought to Spain. Photo: Miguel Paquet / EPA

There are migrants waiting on two ships in the Mediterranean.

There are 450 of them in total. France and Malta have agreed that each country will take in 50 people.

Italy has decided that it will no longer accept people who arrive illegally by sea from the Mediterranean. Because of the decision, Italy has got into disputes with other EU member states. Italy demands that other countries take in more migrants.

## Blue-green algae spoil the bathing water

![A feather drifts in sea water coloured by blue-green algae on the shore of Lauttasaari in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10305166)

Open image viewer

Blue-green algae is floating on the sea shore in Helsinki. If there is blue-green algae in the water, you cannot swim in it. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

It has been very warm in Finland this summer. The warm weather makes blue-green algae bloom in the sea.

The Gulf of Finland, which is the sea area between Finland and Estonia, is almost full of blue-green algae mats.

There is also blue-green algae in lakes. If there is blue-green algae in a lake or the sea, you cannot swim in the water. Water covered by blue-green algae should not be used for anything.

## Summer heat record set today

![Lemon water on a terrace table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10305557)

Open image viewer

You must drink water in the heat! Photo: Kay Roxby / Alamy Stock Photo / AOP

Today was another warm day in Finland.

In Porvoo in Southern Finland it was 31 degrees. That is this summer's heat record.

Tomorrow, Monday, will be an even hotter day. During the day it will be over 25 degrees in almost the whole country. The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns that the heat will be oppressive tomorrow.
