First, the topic is border law.

Finland is extending the border law for 2 years. This means that Finland's borders with Russia will remain closed.

The Parliament approved the Temporary Border Act in 2024. At that time, a large number of asylum seekers quickly arrived at Finland's eastern border.

Finland suspected that the Russian state was organizing asylum seekers at the border.

## Russian ship on fire in the Gulf of Finland

![At sea, two red and white rescue boats spray water on a cargo ship producing smoke.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1081,h_610,x_114,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-17420706b02ef34a2943/1795354581)

A Russian ship (center in the picture) is being escorted towards Russia's territorial waters in the Gulf of Finland. Photo: Finnish Border Guard

Then the effects of the Russian war of aggression.

A Russian cargo ship was attacked in the Gulf of Finland on Saturday morning. The drones may have been Ukrainian.

The ship caught fire, and efforts were made to extinguish and cool it down on Sunday.

The Finnish Border Guard is leading the rescue operations because the ship drifted into Finnish territorial waters. The crew has been transferred to a Russian rescue vessel.

The towing of the cargo ship began on Sunday evening towards Russian territorial waters.

The ship is on the US sanctions list and is under the jurisdiction of the Russian Ministry of Defense.

There is currently no information about the contents of the cargo.

Then about Finland's security.

The Finnish Security Intelligence Service has detected individuals in Finland who have been preparing sabotage attacks. These attacks have been specifically targeted at defense companies.

The head of the Finnish Security Intelligence Service, Juha Martelius, reminds us that attacks like the one in Estonia are possible. In Estonia, an attempt was made to burn down a defense company.

Estonia says Russia was behind the attack.

Now the news about unemployment.

The Ministry of Employment and the Economy predicts that employment will improve by the end of the year.

However, there will still be 340,000 Finns without work next year.

In 2028, there may be slightly fewer unemployed people, 328,000.

The growth of the Finnish economy affects the number of jobs in different sectors.

More about hazard information.

On Tuesday, Finland tested how to warn of danger directly to phones.

The test message was supposed to come at 12 noon, but not everyone received it. Some received the message later. Some received the message first in the three Sámi languages and only then in Finnish and Swedish.

The test will be repeated on Tuesday, November 24. A notification sound will be heard from the FI-Alert bulletin at 12 noon, and it cannot be blocked in the phone settings.
