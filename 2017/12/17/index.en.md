# Sunday 17.12.2017 (radio)

Russian media became interested in Finland's discussion about how the army gathers information. Helsinki-Vantaa Airport has set a new passenger record. Experts are worried about Finns' muscle fitness. Researchers have got more information about the health effects of coffee.

## The army's information gathering

![Data cables and the Defence Forces logo, illustration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9981037)

Open image viewer

In Finland, people have talked at the weekend about how the Finnish army gathers information. The discussion began when Helsingin Sanomat wrote about it on Saturday.

The Finnish army made a crime report because of the article. The army thinks that someone gave secret papers to Helsingin Sanomat without permission.

Now the Russian media is also interested in the matter. The Russian media thinks that the secret papers show how Finland gathers information, especially about Russian soldiers.

## Helsinki-Vantaa's passenger record

![EFHK Helsinki-Vantaa, a Chinese tourist](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9739029)

Open image viewer

Chinese tourists at Helsinki-Vantaa Airport. Photo: Jari Tanskanen / Yle

Helsinki-Vantaa Airport has set a new passenger record. Over 18 million passengers have passed through the airport this year.

The number of air passengers is growing because people travel a lot between Europe and Asia. For example, many flights come to Finland from China.

About 50 percent of Helsinki-Vantaa's passengers are now foreigners.

## Finns' muscle fitness

![The gym has plenty of visitors when it rains.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7950064)

Open image viewer

Training your muscles is important for people of all ages. Photo: Yle

Experts are worried about Finns' muscle fitness. Today a person can live in a way that they hardly use their muscles at all.

Chief physician Kai Savonen says that people should move more. Exercise helps with many illnesses. It is an effective and cheap medicine, Savonen says.

Savonen is worried about the fitness of people under 30. Some young adults hardly move at all.

Researcher Heikki Pentikäinen also reminds us that a person's muscles start to shrink already at the age of 30. That is why it is good to train your muscles already when you are young.

Exercise classes and gym training are also suitable for older people. The principle of training is the same for a 20-year-old and an 80-year-old, Pentikäinen says. If a person keeps their muscles in good condition, they stay active for longer.

## The health effects of coffee

![ A woman taps on her mobile phone with a paper cup in front of her](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9963165)

Open image viewer

More information has been found about the health effects of coffee. Photo: AOP

One more piece of good news for coffee lovers.

Researchers have got more information about the health effects of coffee. The new information is that coffee reduces the risk of dying of bowel cancer.

If a person has bowel cancer, it is good for them to drink at least 4 cups of coffee a day. Then they have a 50 percent smaller risk of dying because of bowel cancer than a patient who does not drink coffee.

Earlier studies have already shown that coffee is probably healthy. Coffee may even prevent illnesses. But researchers do not yet know what makes coffee healthy.
