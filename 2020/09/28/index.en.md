# Monday 28.9.2020 (radio)

Illegal workers at building sites. A boulder was found in the shipping lane. Bottom trawling is bad for sea animals. The Air Force's main wartime exercise has started. Tuesday is mostly dry.

## Illegal labour at buildings

![Construction worker in safety harness at a building site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11537258)

Open image viewer

In Finland, hundreds of people have worked illegally on buildings in recent years.  Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

In Finland, a lot of people have worked illegally in the construction sector.

Many of them have come to Finland from Ukraine. They have been treated badly. For example, they have been paid only a little, or have not been paid at all for some of the work. This came out when Yle looked into the matter.

There have been hundreds of illegal workers. International criminal gangs may have brought illegal workers to Finland.

## A boulder was found in the Amorella's channel

![The Amorella being moved off the island of Järsö on 23.9.2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7232775f6ddcfa58949/1601549425)

Open image viewer

Amorella was towed to port from near the island of Järsö on 23 September. Photo: Päivi Leppänen / Yle

In Åland, the place where the Viking Amorella ship ran aground a little over a week ago has been examined at sea. A large boulder that no one knew about before has been found on the bottom of the shipping lane.

It is not yet known whether the boulder contributed to the Amorella running aground. The shipping lane is now closed to ships until further notice.

The Accident Investigation Board continues its investigations at the site.

## WWF: Bottom trawling makes the Baltic worse

![Wake of a trawler in the water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11566323)

Open image viewer

The wake of a trawler in the water. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

Bottom trawling greatly worsens the state of the Baltic Sea, says the environmental organisation WWF. Bottom trawling is a common fishing method in the Baltic Sea. In it, fishing vessels drag a fishing net along the seabed.

In bottom trawling, many other sea animals get caught in the net as well as fish. That is why it damages the seabed.

Bottom trawling is not used in Finnish waters. It is done a lot by Denmark, Germany, Poland and Sweden.

## The Air Force's main wartime exercise has started

![A Hornet fighter of the Air Force takes off from Pirkkala airfield.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11267605)

Open image viewer

A Hornet fighter of the Finnish Air Force. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

The main wartime exercise of the Finnish Air Force started today, on Monday. The name of the exercise is Ruska 20.

The exercise lasts a little over a week. More than 60 aircraft and about 4,800 people across Finland take part in it.

The Air Force's main exercise can be seen and heard in many places this week. In the sky you can see fighters, for example, and hear their loud sound.
