In Yle's poll the National Coalition Party is the most popular party. The opposition party National Coalition's support has risen and is 21.3 per cent.

Second is the prime minister's party, the SDP. Its support has fallen and is 18.7 per cent.

The support for the Finns Party has fallen to 17.6 per cent.

The support for the Centre Party is 12.2 per cent.

The Greens have risen the most of the government parties, and their support is now 11.4 per cent.

In Yle's poll the Left Alliance has fallen the most, and its support is 7.6 per cent.

## Young people's fears

![Several young people stand in a group. Various lights are visible in the foreground. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_504/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-79870460856d04e70ef/1673503675)

Open image viewer

Many young people lack a safe adult in their close circle. Photo: Tanja Heino / Yle

Almost 40 per cent of young people are afraid of discrimination or bullying at school. This is what a survey by the children's organisation UNICEF shows.

Many feel that teachers and other adults do not understand the diversity of children and young people. For example, different backgrounds are not understood well enough.

20 per cent of respondents said that they do not have a safe adult in their close circle.

## Labour shortage

![Construction workers](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11318724)

Open image viewer

The labour shortage affects especially the hotel and restaurant sector, construction and the care sector. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Almost 75 per cent of companies suffer from a labour shortage, says a member survey of the Chambers of Commerce.

A year ago about 50 per cent of companies said that they could not get enough skilled employees.

Most companies estimate that the labour shortage has limited growth and business.

![The main building of the University of Helsinki at Senate Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_383/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81032260ab9895b937b/1627046971)

The main building of the University of Helsinki. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The University of Helsinki has risen among the world's best universities.

The study covered over 1,600 universities in 100 countries. The University of Helsinki was in joint 101st place.

The world's best university is the University of Oxford in Britain.

8 of the top 10 were American universities.

## President Ahtisaari

![President Martti Ahtisaari.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4568,h_2570,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8493886130becac4a43/1630584573)

Open image viewer

President Martti Ahtisaari. He is the first Finn who has received the Nobel Peace Prize. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

President Martti Ahtisaari is giving up all public activity because of illness.

The Office of the President of the Republic says that Ahtisaari has Alzheimer's disease.

Ahtisaari, 84 years old, was President of the Republic in the years 1994–2000.

He received the Nobel Peace Prize in 2008.

## Mikis Theodorakis

Greek composer Mikis Theodorakis has died. He was 96 years old.

Theodorakis is best remembered for film scores. He composed music for, among others, the film Kerro minulle, Zorbas.

Theodorakis also composed dozens of symphonies, chamber music and cantatas.

Mikis Theodorakis was well known in Finland. He worked together with the singer Arja Saijonmaa.
