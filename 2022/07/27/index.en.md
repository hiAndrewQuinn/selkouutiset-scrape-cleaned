## The price of wheat

![Ukrainian army press officer Alexey sits on top of a downed Russian combat helicopter in a wheat field in Mykolaiv.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5997,h_3373,x_0,y_554/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9586046289fa6c1a9ac/1653210120)

Open image viewer

Wheat farming continues in Ukraine, even though there is war in the country. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

The price of wheat has fallen around the world. Wheat now costs the same as before the war in Ukraine.

During the war wheat has been expensive, because Russia has blocked Ukraine's grain shipments on the Black Sea. Russia attacked Ukraine 5 months ago.

Last week Ukraine and Russia agreed that grain shipments can start again. That is why the price of wheat is falling. Another reason is that this summer's wheat harvest will be good.

The war in Ukraine affects the price of wheat around the world, because Ukraine is a big producer of wheat. Many countries need Ukrainian wheat.

Even though the price of wheat is falling, food is getting more expensive around the world and in Finland. The price of food rises, because energy and fertilisers are getting more expensive.

## Sleepyhead Day

![Mrs Jenni Haukio holds a Sleepyhead sign, with a seascape in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_137/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98681162e0bb161deaf/1658909490)

Open image viewer

Jenni Haukio is this year's official Sleepyhead. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

On Wednesday Sleepyhead Day was celebrated. The Sleepyhead Day celebration was in Naantali.

At the celebration it was said that this year's official Sleepyhead is the president's spouse, Mrs Jenni Haukio. The presidential couple lives in Naantali in the summer.

A Sleepyhead means a person who sleeps for a long time. The tradition is that the official Sleepyhead is thrown into the sea early in the morning in the harbour of Naantali.

Mrs Haukio was not thrown into the sea, because there is a lot of blue-green algae in the sea. Blue-green algae shows that the sea is not doing well.

Mrs Haukio says that sea nature must be protected. Haukio was chosen as Sleepyhead, because environmental issues are important to her.

Sleepyhead Day is an old tradition. In Naantali, Sleepyhead Day was already celebrated in the 1800s.
