# Sunday 2.8.2020 (radio)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin has got married. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare plans to recommend the use of face masks in public places. 10 new corona infections have been found in Finland. The coronavirus is spreading fast in the world. Showers of rain will come at times during the start of the week.

TV Clear News has a summer break and it will continue on 17.8.
Radio Clear News is made every day, also in the summer.

## Prime Minister gets married

![Sanna Marin and Markus Räikkönen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11476567)

Sanna Marin and Markus Räikkönen. Photo: Minttu Saarni

Finland's Prime Minister Sanna Marin has got married. The wedding was held on Saturday.

The wedding party was at the prime minister's official residence in Kesäranta, which is in Helsinki. Only a small group of people took part in the party.

Marin's husband is Markus Räikkönen. Marin and Räikkönen have been together for 16 years. They have a 2.5-year-old daughter.

## Face masks perhaps into use

![A surgical face mask](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_291/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7037185f228e9ae45cc/1596101100)

A face mask covers the mouth and nose. Photo: Jenni Joensuu / Yle

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, plans to recommend the use of face masks in public places. THL will decide on the matter in the coming days.

A face mask covers the mouth and nose. That is why it can stop the spread of the coronavirus indoors.

In the autumn there will again be more people indoors, because the summer holidays end. The number of passengers on buses and trains will also grow.

## New corona infections

![THL sign on Mannerheimintie in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_339/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6782375ec3516ecc544/1618662483)

10 new corona infections have been found in Finland. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, reported this on Sunday.

THL reports the number of new corona infections every day. In total, almost 7,500 corona infections have been found in Finland.

329 people have died in Finland because of corona. THL will say on Monday whether there have been more deaths over the weekend.

## Corona spreads around the world

![A woman walks her dogs in the Fitzroy Gardens area in Melbourne, Australia. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2353,h_1324,x_0,y_96/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7026035f1e3b6740224/1595817024)

In Melbourne, Australia, people may go outdoors only if there is a good reason. Photo: David Crosling / Epa

In the world, the coronavirus is spreading fast.

In the USA, more than 60,000 new corona infections have been found every day for already 5 days. In the USA, more than 150,000 people have died of corona in total.

South of the USA, that is, in the countries of Latin America, corona has killed more than 200,000 people. The most deaths are in Brazil.

There is also bad news from Asia. For example, the Philippines already has more than 100,000 corona infections.

In Australia the coronavirus has started to spread again. The situation is so bad that, for example, in the city of Melbourne people must stay at home at night.

## Showers of rain are coming

![summer rain](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10265046)

Open image viewer

Photo: AOP

On Monday it will rain at times in Southern Finland. In the north it will be dry, that is, the showers of rain will stay away.

On Tuesday the rain will end in the south, and the rain will move to the north.

The temperature on Monday is between 15 and 20 degrees. On Tuesday it warms up a little, and the temperature in the south is over 20 degrees.
