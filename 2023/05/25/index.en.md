# Radio Clear News Thursday 25.5.2023

Access to care. Assistants on strike. Drought in the Horn of Africa. Crimes by minors. The weather.

## Getting to a doctor

![White outdoor wall with black lettering: Rovaniemi health centre and the city coat of arms.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6114,h_3423,x_0,y_636/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-95576962825d0de0347/1652712591)

Getting care has slowed down during the start of the year.

Only half of clients have got non-urgent care at a health centre within a week.

Still last autumn, 60 percent of doctor appointments took place within a week of the assessment of the need for care. However, about 90 percent got an appointment with a nurse within a week.

At the turn of the year, health centres were transferred to wellbeing services counties.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that changes in administration and information systems have slowed access to care.

## Assistants on strike

![An elderly person is being pushed in a wheelchair.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4740,h_2666,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7062285f2d32b2ea17b/1596802617)

Open image viewer

Photo: Janne Järvinen / Yle

Personal assistants are on strike in the capital region.

The Trade Union for the Public and Welfare Sectors, JHL, started the strike. It lasts a week. JHL says that about 1500 personal assistants are on strike.

The strike aims to advance negotiations on the collective agreement. The dispute concerns, among other things, pay and long shifts. The trade union JHL says that the employer model for personal assistants is difficult. The services are the responsibility of the wellbeing services counties, but disabled people themselves are the employers.

Next month the strike threatens to spread to the whole country.

## Drought in the Horn of Africa

![Land that has cracked because of drought.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_469/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-4900556db4f2f567e0/1457213347)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jarkko Mikkonen / Finnish Red Cross

Starvation deaths have already started to become common in the Horn of Africa, says the UN, the United Nations. In Somalia, Kenya, Ethiopia and Djibouti there is the worst drought in many decades.

The region's rainy seasons have failed 5 times in a row already.

Millions of people have had to leave their homes, because land cannot be farmed and livestock cannot be raised without water.

The UN's emergency aid office has managed to collect aid pledges from wealthier countries. Pledges worth 2.4 billion dollars have been received. The need is estimated at 7 billion dollars.

## Crimes by minors

![Police on the scene.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3535,h_1987,x_45,y_252/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-27612156d9920eea426/1457353982)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle/Linus Hoffman

The perpetrator of assault or robbery is more and more often a minor.

12 percent of those suspected of robbery are under 15 years old. There were almost 270 of them last year. Most were boys.

The share of minors grew by almost 140 percent last year compared with the previous year.

About 11 percent of those suspected of assault last year were under 15 years old. Statistics Finland reports this.
