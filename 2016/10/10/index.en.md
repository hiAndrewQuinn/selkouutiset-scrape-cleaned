# Monday 10.10.2016 (radio)

Finn Bengt Holmström has won the economics Nobel Prize. The US presidential candidates had a second election debate. The Espoo metro is delayed further. On Monday Aleksis Kivi Day was celebrated.

## The economics Nobel

![Bengt Holmström.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9221444)

Open image viewer

Bengt Holmström wins the economics Nobel Prize. Photo: Yle

The Finn Bengt Holmström has won the economics Nobel Prize. Holmström won the prize together with Oliver Hart.

Holmström was born in Helsinki and Hart was born in London. Both are now professors in the USA. They have studied contract theory, that is, for example, what a good contract looks like between the owner of a company and the director of the company.

Holmström and Hart will share prize money of more than 800,000 euros in total.

Holmström is the 5th Finn to win a Nobel Prize. Earlier, Nobel Prizes were won by the writer Frans Emil Sillanpää, the chemist A. I. Virtanen, the physiologist Ragnar Granit and the peace mediator Martti Ahtisaari. Of these, Granit was a Swedish citizen when he received the prize.

## The US election debate

![Trump stands in the foreground, Clinton further back. Trump's expression is serious, Clinton is smiling.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9221047)

Donald Trump and Hillary Clinton in the election debate. Photo: Jim Lo Scalzo / EPA

It was the second election debate between the presidential candidates in the USA. The debate was tough.

The Republican candidate Donald Trump had to explain what he has said about women. At the weekend an old video was published, in which Trump talks very insultingly about women.

Trump, for his part, accused the Democratic candidate Hillary Clinton of something that happened when Clinton was foreign minister. Clinton used private email at that time, although that is not safe in the work of a foreign minister.

The candidates have one more debate before election day, which is 8 November.

## The Espoo metro

![People at a metro station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9220762)

Open image viewer

It is not yet known when the metro will reach Espoo. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The metro between Helsinki and Espoo is delayed further.

The Espoo metro service was supposed to start as early as August, but then it was said that the service would only start in January. Now it is not known when metro service to Espoo can start.

The Espoo metro has already been built. The reason for the delay is safety tests, which have not yet succeeded well enough. The tests study how well the metro stations cope in exceptional situations, for example in a fire.

## Aleksis Kivi Day

![Aleksis Kivi cake.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9217219)

Open image viewer

The Aleksis Kivi Day cake. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Aleksis Kivi Day was celebrated on Monday.

The writer Aleksis Kivi lived in the 1800s. He wrote, for example, the book Seven Brothers.

Aleksis Kivi is called Finland's national writer. That is why Aleksis Kivi Day is also the day of Finnish literature.

Aleksis Kivi Day is always celebrated on 10 October, because it is Kivi's birthday. On Aleksis Kivi Day flags are raised.
