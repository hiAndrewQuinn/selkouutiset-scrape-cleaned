# Thursday 12.1.2023 (radio)

Young people's learning results. Ukrainians looking for work. Shipping. California storms. The weather in Finland.

## Young people's learning results

![Pupils of Norssi lower secondary school in a corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9932349)

Open image viewer

Photo: Eveliina Matikainen / Yle

The learning results of Finnish young people have got worse. Young people's skills, for example in reading and mathematics, are now clearly worse than before.

The learning results of 15-year-olds improved until the 1990s. The results began to get worse at the latest about 20 years ago.

Nowadays family background also affects children's learning results more than before. Children's learning results are better if their parents have a good education and a good salary.

The Ministry of Education and Culture told about young people's learning results on Thursday.

## Ukrainians looking for work

![TE Office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4628,h_2603,x_426,y_270/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7404185faa97100e585/1739960352)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

In Finland there are many Ukrainians who have not found a job. There are already about 7,000 Ukrainians at TE Offices who are unemployed job seekers.

Many Ukrainians fled the war to Finland only a short time ago. It is difficult for them to get a job in Finland, because they do not yet speak Finnish or Swedish.

Not all Ukrainians look for work through the TE Office. That is why there are probably more unemployed Ukrainians than the TE Office statistics say.

## Shipping

![General view of Vuosaari harbour, taken from a balcony.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-867509616942a934f17/1634288407)

Open image viewer

Shipping's carbon dioxide emissions are to be stopped. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

Shipping is moving to clean energy.

The goal is that shipping's carbon dioxide emissions end by 2050. Carbon dioxide emissions are to be stopped because they add to climate change.

Already in the coming years, ships that can also use new kinds of fuels will sail the seas.

For example, at Wärtsilä's factories in Vaasa a ship engine that also runs on ammonia is being tested. Ammonia produces no carbon dioxide emissions at all.

When shipping moves to clean energy, new kinds of ships will be needed. That is why the future of shipbuilders looks good.

## California storms

![Streets covered in water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12687735)

Open image viewer

The storms have caused floods in California. Photo: Josh Edelson / AFP

Winter storms continue in California in the USA. Rain has fallen even more than in 150 years. About 20 people have died in the storms.

On Wednesday the storm brought more heavy rain from the sea to the Californian coast. That is why more floods and landslides may come to the area.

## The weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12687737)

Open image viewer

The weather in Finland is rainy, windy and warm. The temperature is above zero almost everywhere in the country. Only in part of Lapland is there a little frost.

Rain moves over Finland. On Friday the rain area spreads from the south. The rain is water, sleet or snow.

The weekend will also be rainy. Water, sleet or snow falls in a large part of the country.
