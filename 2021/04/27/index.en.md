# Tuesday 27.4.2021 (radio)

The governing parties are negotiating about state finances. Corona affects the May Day celebrations. Veterans' Day was celebrated on Tuesday. The weather stays cooler than usual.

## The government

![Sanna Marin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-79889660867ae722142/1619451110)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin's government is having difficult negotiations. Photo: Rami Moilanen / Yle

The governing parties' negotiations have continued.

The governing parties are negotiating about state finances. The negotiations are difficult, and they have already lasted many days.

The difficult issue in the negotiations is the state debt. Because of the corona crisis, Finland has taken on more debt. The governing parties now disagree about how much the state debt may still grow.

There are 5 parties in Prime Minister Sanna Marin's government. The Centre Party wants to slow the growth of state debt more than the other governing parties.

The governing parties are also negotiating about how to create more jobs in Finland.

## May Day

![Manta's capping in 2014.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11331978)

Open image viewer

This year May Day is not being celebrated as it was in 2014. Photo: Li Jizhi / AOP

Corona affects how May Day is celebrated.

Because of corona, it is not good for people to gather for shared May Day celebrations. The police hope that, for example, not many people will come to the centre of Helsinki on May Day.

Because of the restriction, no more than 6 people may gather together in the Helsinki area. The restriction continues until the middle of May.

The City of Helsinki hopes that people will celebrate May Day remotely, through a computer.

## Veterans' Day

![The Finnish national flag in the courtyard of the Säätytalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2472,h_1391,x_0,y_189/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6686605ea6e9a9136d8/1587997295)

Open image viewer

Veterans' Day is a flag day. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Veterans' Day was celebrated in Finland on Tuesday. Because of corona, the Veterans' Day celebration was held without an audience.

The celebration was at Tampere Hall. President Sauli Niinistö gave a greeting at the celebration. The celebration can be watched on Yle Areena.

Veterans' Day is celebrated in honour of Finland's war veterans. War veterans fought in the Finnish army during the Second World War about 80 years ago.

The Second World War ended in Finland on 27 April 1945. That is why Veterans' Day is celebrated on 27 April.
