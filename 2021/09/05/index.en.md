# Sunday 5.9.2021 (radio)

Prime minister on corona restrictions. Women's protests in Kabul. There was frost in the south at night too.

## Prime minister on corona restrictions

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin poses for the camera in the yard of Kesäranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_234/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8415856116735cc1deb/1628861462)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the corona restrictions will perhaps end in Finland in October or November.

Marin says that the restrictions will end when 80 per cent of people over 12 years old have received the vaccine. This could happen as early as October.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin points out that the coronavirus can cause surprises. That is why it is not certain that the corona restrictions will end.

Parliament will decide on the corona pass this month. The purpose of the corona pass is that, for example, various events are easier to organise than now.

## Women's protests in Afghanistan

![Women have been protesting in recent days for their rights in Afghanistan. The picture was taken in Kabul on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2836,h_1595,x_0,y_97/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8501136133ee1f2dc12/1630793453)

Women have been protesting in recent days for their rights in Afghanistan. The picture was taken in Kabul on Friday. Photo: Stringer / EPA

In Afghanistan women have been organising protests. The women are opposing the Taliban, which took power in Afghanistan.

In Kabul, the capital of Afghanistan, the Taliban used tear gas when women held a protest.

The Taliban will soon form a government in Afghanistan. It is not yet known what rights the Taliban will give to women in Afghanistan.

A serious accident happened in Rauma.

3 young people were seriously injured when a car left the road and hit a rock. The cause of the accident is not known.

On Saturday a young man died when a car left the road in Utajärvi in North Ostrobothnia.

## Oulu mopeds

![A moped doing a burnout on a tyre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1048,h_590,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83237260f059d5a62b3/1626364420)

A moped doing a burnout on a tyre. Photo: Screenshot from YouTube

In Oulu the police have been checking young people's mopeds.

The police found many mopeds that had been tuned. A tuned moped can be ridden at too high a speed.

2 motorcyclists tried to escape from the police, but the police caught them later.

The young people's moped meeting went quite calmly. A week ago in Oulu, young people, among other things, threw stones at the police.

## The weather warms up

![Preliminary temperature forecasts for the start of week 36 on weather maps. The weather warms up after the weekend.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12086291)

Open image viewer

Warm autumn weather is forecast for the coming week. Photo: Kerttu Kotakorpi / Yle

In Southern Finland there was frost at night for the first time this autumn. There was frost across the whole country.

The coldest was in Lapland. In Salla the frost was 4.7 degrees. In Uusimaa, in Vihti, the frost was almost 2 degrees.

Next week the weather will warm up. Especially in Western Finland it is warmer than 15 degrees in many places.
