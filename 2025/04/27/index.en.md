## Home in Argentina

Pope Francis's real name was **Jorge Mario Bergoglio**.

He was born in 1936 in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Bergoglio's parents were immigrants. They came to Argentina from Italy.

Bergoglio became Pope in March 2013. He was Pope for 12 years.

Bergoglio chose the papal name *Francis*. **Francis of Assisi** was an Italian monk and saint who lived in the 1200s.

## The disadvantaged and the shunned

Pope Francis was the first Pope from South America.

He guided the Catholic Church in a more modern direction. For example, he spoke more positively about homosexual people than earlier popes did. He also improved the position of women in the Catholic Church to some extent.

The disadvantaged and the shunned were important to Francis.

Francis's speeches often dealt with, for example, the marginalised, the poor, the sick, migrants and prisoners. Francis also visited migrants and prisons.

Pope Francis spoke carefully about the war in Ukraine. For example, he did not condemn Russia's war crimes in Ukraine. This caution has angered many people.

## Health problems

Pope Francis had many health problems in recent years. Because of knee problems he used a wheelchair.

This year he was in hospital for a long time because of pneumonia.

The Pope's funeral was held yesterday, Saturday, in the Vatican.

After the funeral the choice of a new Pope begins. It is made at a meeting of cardinals, in which high officials and bishops of the Catholic Church from different parts of the world take part. About 120 of them are gathered.

The meeting may last for many days. The new Pope may be voted on many times.

The Pope is an important spiritual leader. About 1.4 billion people, or 18 percent of the world's population, belong to the Catholic Church.

Most Catholics live in the Americas.
