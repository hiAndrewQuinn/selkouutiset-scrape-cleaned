# Wednesday 21.12.2016 (radio)

In Germany, people have remembered those who died in the terror attack on Monday. About 30 people have died in an explosion accident in Mexico. The air is really dirty in northern China. The Christmas-time trains are almost full. Many children's gift wish is a robot pet.

## Germany's grief

![Family and friends prayed for the victims of the disaster in the Kaiser Wilhelm Memorial Church on Breitscheidplatz on 20 December 2016. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9363666)

A memorial service in a Berlin church. Photo: Maurizio Gambarini / EPA

In Germany, people have remembered those who died in the terror attack on Monday. The terror attack was carried out in Germany's capital Berlin.

There was a memorial service in a Berlin church on Tuesday. Germany's president and government took part in the service.

12 people died in the terror attack. The attacker drove a lorry into people at a Christmas market.

The terrorist organisation ISIS says that it carried out the attack. The police are investigating the matter.

## The explosion accident in Mexico

![Firefighters working at the explosion site in Tultepec, Mexico, on 20 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9365833)

Open image viewer

The explosion accident did a lot of damage in Mexico. Photo: STR / EPA

A bad explosion accident has happened in Mexico. About 30 people died in the accident. The scene of the accident is in the town of Tultepec.

The accident happened at a market where fireworks are sold. The fireworks caught fire, and the explosions went on for a long time.

Mexicans buy a lot of fireworks before Christmas.

## China's air

![A Chinese woman walks in a Beijing park with a mask on her face.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9365423)

Open image viewer

In northern China, the air is really dirty. Photo: Wu Hong / EPA

In northern China, people are suffering from dirty air. The air is so dirty that people can see only 20 metres ahead.

The air is now dangerous for health. That is why schools are closed in many cities.

The residents of northern China are trying to get away from the smog. They want to travel somewhere where the air is clean and fresh. That is why there is congestion in air traffic.

## Christmas-time trains

![People at Helsinki railway station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9353855)

Open image viewer

The trains are now full of passengers. Photo: Hanna Lumme / Yle

Many Finns travel on their Christmas holiday by train. There are only a few free seats left on the trains.

On Friday, a really long train leaves Helsinki and goes all the way to Rovaniemi. The train has 15 carriages and has room for 1500 people. Such a long train is not usually seen in Finland.

## Winter solstice

![A woman walks on a dim pedestrian street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9335688)

Open image viewer

Now is the darkest time of the year. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Wednesday was the winter solstice. That means that Wednesday was the shortest day of the year. The sun was in the sky for only a few hours.

On Wednesday, the length of the day was under 6 hours in Helsinki, about 3 and a half hours in Oulu and about 2 hours in Rovaniemi.

In the very north, the sun does not rise at all at this time of year. Elsewhere in Finland, the days start getting longer again tomorrow. At first, the day gets 5 minutes longer per week in southern Finland.

## Robot pet

![Natalia Kruglova packs Maria-Helena Tuomilaakso's purchases.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7703856)

Many children have the same gift wish this Christmas. They want to get a robot pet.

Hatchimals eggs have already sold out in the shops. In the egg there is a baby bird that can come out of the egg by itself.

Small robot dogs are also popular. Not all colours are available in every shop any more.
