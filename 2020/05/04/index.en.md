## Genes of the coronavirus are being searched for in sewage water

![Tapio Kilponen takes a sample at a sewage treatment plant in Lahti](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6693895ea949543530b/1588152723)

Open image viewer

Researchers are searching for coronavirus genes in sewage. Photo: Juha-Petri Koponen / Yle

Health authorities are studying whether coronavirus genes can be found in sewage water.

Samples are being taken from sewage water in different parts of Finland.

The researchers hope that they can find out how large a part of people have the coronavirus.

At the same time, the researchers may be able to find out whether the epidemic is growing or getting smaller.

Similar research is being done in many countries.

It takes a couple of hours for sewage to travel from the toilet bowl to the sewage treatment plant.

From sewage water, people's habits and many kinds of illnesses can be studied.

## The Norwegian state is rescuing airline Norwegian

![A Norwegian plane landing at Arlanda airport on 16 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11333975)

Open image viewer

The Norwegian airline Norwegian is in trouble. Photo: Jonathan Nackstrand / AFP

The Norwegian airline Norwegian may not go bankrupt after all.

The airline has been doing badly and no longer has any money.

The Norwegian state promises to give money to the airline Norwegian, but the airline must change how it works.

It must cut back on flights a lot.

When the Norwegian state gives money, the airline can pay its compulsory costs for a year.

Maybe in a year's time Norwegian's planes can fly again.
