# Monday 20.3.2017 (radio)

Bad Brazilian meat may also have been brought to Finland. The happiest people in the world live in the Nordic countries. The spring equinox was on Monday. Cheese fat is not unhealthy. The president's dog Lennu got its own pastry.

## Brazilian meat scandal

![Stored meat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9520463)

Open image viewer

Old meat has been sold abroad from Brazil. Photo: Miguel Schincariol / AFP

New information has been obtained about the Brazilian meat scandal. Bad Brazilian meat may also have been brought to Finland.

Meat has been brought to Finland from Brazilian plants that have quality problems. The matter was investigated by Evira. It is the agency that is responsible for food safety in Finland.

There is still no certain information on whether bad meat has also been sent from the plants to Finland.

At the weekend it was reported that Brazilian companies have sold bad and old meat abroad.

## Happy Nordic countries

![A young man is happily waving a Norwegian flag in a parade on Norway's national day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9519497)

Open image viewer

Norway is the happiest country in the world. Photo: Patrik Sterberg / IBL / AOP

Monday was celebrated as the Day of Happiness.

On the Day of Happiness, the UN, that is the United Nations, published a report on happy countries. The report says that the happiest people in the world live in the Nordic countries.

The happiest country in the world is Norway. Denmark and Iceland come right after Norway. Finland is in 5th place and Sweden in 10th place.

Happiness was measured, for example, by how healthy and free people are. Happiness is also brought by people being able to trust the government.

The least happy countries are found in Africa.

## Light wins

![Morning of the spring equinox in Hesperia park in Helsinki on 20 March 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9519517)

Open image viewer

The morning of the spring equinox in Hesperia park in Helsinki on 20 March. Photo: Teemu Salonen / Lehtikuva

On Monday it was also the spring equinox. It means that day and night are equally long. On Tuesday the day is already longer than the night.

The day starts to get longer slowly around Christmas, and in spring the light wakes up nature.

After the spring equinox, the days still get longer for 3 months in the northern half of the earth. The amount of light grows until Midsummer.

## Cheese fat

![A man's hand picks a piece of cheese from a bowl, into which half of a cheese wheel has been cut into cubes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9510664)

Open image viewer

Fatty cheese is not as unhealthy as people have thought. Photo: Clemens Bilan / EPA

Cheese fat is not unhealthy. An Irish study says that cheese does not raise the amount of cholesterol in the blood.

For example, eating butter raises the level of cholesterol in the blood. That is dangerous for health, because cholesterol sticks to the blood vessel and the blood vessel becomes narrow.

Researchers do not yet know for certain why fat is less healthy in butter than in cheese.

## Lennu pastry

![Lennu pastry](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9517381)

Open image viewer

Lennu pastry. Photo: Retu Liikanen / Yle

A cafe in Helsinki has invented a new pastry. The name of the pastry is Lennu. The pastry looks like a dog.

The real dog Lennu is President Sauli Niinistö's dog. Lennu is familiar to Finns from television.

The pastry went on sale on 7 March, because it is Lennu's birthday.
