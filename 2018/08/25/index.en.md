# Saturday 25.8.2018 (radio)

The bus in the Kuopio accident may have been driving fast. President Niinistö is pleased with the defence agreements. Driving-lesson permits are now popular. Rap singer Cheek is giving his last concerts. On Saturday, Finland's nature was celebrated.

## The cause of the bus accident is being investigated

![The accident bus from the Kuopio bus crash under technical investigation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10370631)

Those injured in the Kuopio bus accident are still in hospital. On Saturday afternoon, five of them were in intensive care.

Four people died in the accident. There were 21 people on the bus, who live in Kalix in Northern Sweden. They belong to a choir club and were on their way to a spa in Kuopio. The bus fell from the bridge onto the railway line on Friday afternoon.

The cause of the accident is not known. The investigators say that the bus may have been driving at high speed. The bus driver told the police that the bus's brakes did not work.

## President pleased with defence agreements

![Sauli Niinistö at the president's question session of Yle Radio 1 in Helsinki on Saturday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10370717)

President Sauli Niinistö is pleased with the defence agreements that Finland has made, for example with Sweden, Britain and the USA. Finland is also interested in increasing defence cooperation with France.

President Niinistö answered questions from the audience and journalists together with Yle Radio. Niinistö said that it is good if Europe improves its own defence.

## Many people are applying for driving-lesson permits

![Sakari Päivinen at the wheel of a car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10368489)

Open image viewer

16-year-old Sakari Päivinen is learning to drive a car. Photo: Heikki Haapalainen / Yle

The driving licence law changed in July. Now it is easier to get a learner's permit, with which you can teach, for example, your own child to drive a car.

The teacher no longer needs to take a test. Driving lessons can begin when the young person is 16. A second brake pedal must be fitted in the car for the teacher. So many brake pedals are wanted now that they have run out in shops. In July and August, as many as 10,000 learner's permits have already been applied for.

A young person must be 18 years old before they get a driving licence.

## Cheek's last concerts

![Cheek performing in Lahti on 24 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10370395)

Open image viewer

Rap musician Cheek. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Finland's most popular rap musician, Cheek, is giving his last concerts on Friday and Saturday in Lahti.

There were 30,000 people at Friday's concert. People of all ages were in the audience, including children and pensioners. The concert had a big stage, a colourful light show, fire and fireworks. Guest performers on the stage included Kaija Koo, Elastinen and JVG.

37-year-old Cheek is stopping performing, but he will stay in the music business. He has a record company, for example.

## Nature's celebration day

On Saturday, Finland's Nature Day was celebrated.

There have been events all over Finland, for example rivers have been restored and trips into nature have been made. At the same time, the Finnish Nature Conservation Association turns 80.

This is the second time that Finland has had a flag day in honour of nature.
