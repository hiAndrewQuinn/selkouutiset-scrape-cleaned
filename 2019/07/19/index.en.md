# Friday 19.7.2019 (radio)

Rye and oats have grown well this summer. There was a fire in the yard of the old church in Hyvinkää. A human travelled to the Moon 50 years ago. The film Frozen 2 is also being made in the Sámi language. At the start of next week there is a heatwave.

## Rye and oat harvest

![rye ears](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10314327)

Open image viewer

Rye has grown well this summer. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Rye and oats have grown well this summer. The Natural Resources Institute Finland says that the harvest is large.

The rye harvest may be the largest in 30 years this summer. The harvest is so large that Finnish rye will be enough for the whole year.

The oat harvest is the largest in 6 years.

## Fire in Hyvinkää

![The old church in Hyvinkää](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10885035)

Open image viewer

The old church in Hyvinkää was spared from the fire. Photo: Rescue service

The old church in Hyvinkää was in danger during the night between Thursday and Friday.

A fire broke out in the church yard. The fire burned a storage building. The building was next to the church.

One window frame of the church also caught fire, but the firefighters put it out. The fire did not have time to spread in the church building.

The police suspect that someone set the fire on purpose. The police are investigating the matter.

The old wooden church of Hyvinkää is more than 100 years old.

## 50 years since the Moonwalk

![Astronaut, lunar module and research equipment on the surface of the Moon](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10886425)

Open image viewer

Apollo 11 landed on the Moon 50 years ago. Photo: NASA

At the weekend a big event in human history is being celebrated: humans landed on the surface of the Moon for the first time exactly 50 years ago.

The American spacecraft Apollo 11 landed on the Moon on 20 July 1969. The astronaut Neil Armstrong was the first human to walk on the surface of the Moon.

The 50th anniversary of the Moonwalk has been celebrated for many days already. For example, Armstrong's spacesuit was unveiled at the Smithsonian space museum in Washington.

Programmes about the conquest of the Moon can be watched on Yle Areena.

## The Sámi-language Frozen 2

![Presidents of the Sámi parliaments and the Sámi Council: Tuomas Aslak Juuso (Suoma Sámediggi), Åsa Larsson-Blind (Sámeráđđi), Aili Keskitalo (Norgga Sámediggi) and Per-Olof Nutti (Ruoŧa Sámediggi) point to a major step in this regard in a joint statement.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10886181)

Open image viewer

Disney Animation has worked together with the Sámi people. Photo: Sámediggi

The film Frozen 2 is also being made in the Sámi language. Frozen 2 is an animated film made by Walt Disney Animation Studios.

Disney Animation is making the Sámi-language version of the film together with the Sámi people. Disney Animation has met Sámi people from Finland, Norway and Sweden. The Sámi are happy that Sámi children will get to see the film in their own mother tongue.

The Frozen films are fairy-tale films. The Sámi-language version of Frozen 2 will be released at the same time as the other Nordic versions. The release date is December of this year.

## Heatwave across the whole country

![A woman sits on a beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10860991)

Open image viewer

The weather is suitable for being outdoors now. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The weekend is warm. The temperature rises to heatwave levels in the south, that is, over 25 degrees.

At the start of next week the heat will spread across the whole of Finland, says Yle meteorologist Matti Huutonen. On Monday and Tuesday there may be temperatures over 25 degrees as far as Lapland.
