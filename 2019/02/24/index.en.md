## The 5G network is fast

![Huawei is the leading supplier of the components needed to build the 5G network. Picture from the Hannover technology fair in June 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10654252)

Open image viewer

The Chinese company Huawei is building a lot of 5G technology. Photo: FOCKE STRANGMANN / EPA

The mobile network will soon change into a 5G network. That means that phones and devices will work really fast.

An ordinary person notices that, for example, a call does not drop on the train. The Finnish army can also use the fast network.

A fast and efficient mobile network can also be dangerous. Many countries suspect that the Chinese may use the 5G network for spying.

The Finnish army intends to use only Finnish or European equipment.

## The Catholic Church no longer hides crimes

![Pope Francis speaking at the closing event of a meeting on the sexual abuse of children. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10660850)

Open image viewer

Pope Francis spoke in the Vatican today. Photo: Giuseppe Lami / EPA

The leader of the Catholic Church, Pope Francis, says that the Catholic Church no longer hides crimes. The Pope says that the church will punish priests who are guilty of child abuse.

The Catholic Church will soon publish guidelines. With the guidelines, the Catholic Church is trying to solve the problem.

Many cases of abuse have come to light in the Catholic Church. The perpetrators have been priests and the victims have been children.

The Pope spoke at a meeting that dealt with protecting children from sexual abuse in the Catholic Church.

## New constitution for Cuba

![Picture of a street in Havana. A bicycle taxi drives down a narrow street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10194237)

Open image viewer

Cuba is ruled by the Communist Party. Photo: Nina Svahn / Yle

A referendum on a new constitution was held in Cuba today.

The new law does not change things very much in Cuba.

Cuba is still a socialist state, in which the Communist Party holds power. But private ownership will become legal.

In addition, a prime minister will be elected in Cuba.

## A bad day for Finns at the world championships

![Niskanen Hakola team sprint](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10660853)

Open image viewer

The team sprint in skiing is a relay competition. There are only 2 skiers in a team. Photo: Lehtikuva

At the ski world championships, Finnish skiers did not have a good day today.

Men and women skied the pairs competition.

The Finnish men's team was 7th, and the women's team was also 7th. The Finnish skiers were disappointed that the competitions did not go so well.

The Finnish skiers had hoped that they would win medals today.

## The temperature varies
