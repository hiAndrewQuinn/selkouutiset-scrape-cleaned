# Wednesday 28.11.2018 (radio)

The Finlandia literature prizes were awarded. Children's mental health problems have increased. Trump's trade policy could also take jobs from Finland. Stora Enso plans to lay off staff. Berries may help fight superbugs.

## Finlandia prizes

![Winner of the Finlandia Prize for children's and young adult literature Siiri Enoranta with her book Tuhatkuolevan kirous, winner of the Finlandia Prize for fiction Olli Jalonen with Taivaanpallo and winner of the Finlandia Prize for non-fiction Seppo Aalto with Kapina Tehtailla - Kuusankoski 1918 at the Finlandia literature prize ceremony in Helsinki on 28 November.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10530215)

The Finlandia literature prizes have been awarded.

Olli Jalonen's work Taivaanpallo won the Finlandia Prize for fiction. This was already the second time Jalonen has won a Finlandia prize. He received his first prize 28 years ago.

The children's and young adult literature prize went to Siiri Enoranta. She was awarded it for the book Tuhatkuolevan kirous.

The non-fiction prize went to Seppo Aalto. He wrote the book Kapina Tehtailla - Kuusankoski 1918. The book tells of the events of the Finnish Civil War.

Each Finlandia prize is worth 30,000 euros.

## Children's mental health

![A boy sits on the ground with his face buried in his hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10528985)

Open image viewer

Help for children's mental health problems is being sought more bravely than before. Photo: Mostphotos

Help for children's mental health problems is being sought more than ever before.

In Finland about 90,000 children under 13 have some kind of mental health problem. Some of them need psychiatric treatment for serious mental disorders.

The number of patients in child psychiatry has grown throughout the 2000s. Last year about 22,000 children received psychiatric treatment.

Experts say that, for example, children's anxiety and depression have increased. However, families seek help more bravely than before.

## Trump's tariffs and Finland

![Car assembly at the Uusikaupunki car factory](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10366742)

Open image viewer

Tariffs imposed by President Trump could end jobs in Finland's car industry. Photo: Paula Collin / Yle

The trade policy of the United States threatens to end jobs in Finland too.

President Donald Trump is pursuing tariffs that could end almost 3,000 jobs in Finland, says the Research Institute of the Finnish Economy, Etla.

If tariffs come, the car industry will suffer especially, and almost 900 jobs could be lost there, Etla says.

## Stora Enso

![Stora Enso Oulu pulp mill and paper mill](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9873706)

Open image viewer

Stora Enso's pulp and paper mill in Oulu. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

Stora Enso is starting major yt negotiations at the Oulu paper mill. The negotiations concern 500 people.

Stora Enso says that demand for fine paper has fallen. Because of this, the company may lay off employees for 90 days during the early part of the year.

## Berries and superbugs

![When picking mesimarja berries you must be careful, because a ripe berry can be red or white.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9005381)

Finnish berries may help in combating bacteria.

According to a new study, berries may be useful in fighting so-called superbugs. Superbugs are bacteria that antibiotic medicines do not work against.

The Technical Research Centre of Finland, VTT, says that cloudberry, raspberry and mesimarja are especially effective. They contain compounds that restrain the growth of bacteria.
