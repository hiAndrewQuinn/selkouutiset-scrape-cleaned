# Sunday 7.3.2021 (radio)

The discussion about postponing the municipal elections has continued. The coronavirus has spread especially in Southern Finland. Making face masks in Finland is not worth it. There was a demonstration against coronavirus restrictions in Sweden. Kela has published new easy-read brochures. On Monday it is sunny.

## Postponing the municipal elections

![A person puts a ballot paper into a ballot box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4400,h_2475,x_731,y_881/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-33477258ea5356151a9/1614778108)

The discussion about postponing the municipal elections has continued.

On Saturday it was decided that the municipal elections will be postponed by 2 months, and will only be held in June. The reason is the poor coronavirus situation.

Many people have now asked whether the Ministry of Justice could not organise the elections during the coronavirus time. The Ministry of Justice is responsible for organising elections in Finland.

The Ministry of Justice does not accept criticism. Officials of the Ministry of Justice remind people that the municipal elections were postponed because of the health situation.

New coronavirus infections have also been reported in Finland on Sunday. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that there are 511 new coronavirus infections in Finland.

The coronavirus has spread especially in the south. There are many coronavirus infections in the Helsinki region, the Turku region and the Åland Islands.

## Making face masks

![A woman wearing a face mask in front of a shopping centre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_175/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-778564603797086d72c/1614255959)

Open image viewer

Finnish face masks are more expensive than foreign face masks. Photo: Markku Lähdetluoma / Yle

Making face masks in Finland is not worth it.

Iltalehti reports that Finnish face mask makers do not have enough customers. Because of this, production has to be reduced and many workers may be left without work.

The reason is the price. Finnish face masks are more expensive than, for example, Chinese face masks. A Finnish face mask costs 5 times more than a Chinese face mask.

## Demonstration in Sweden

![A sign on a metro station pillar reading "Tack för att du visar hänsyn och håller avstånd" (Thank you for showing consideration and keeping your distance).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11706433)

Open image viewer

Coronavirus measures annoy some Swedes. Photo: Amir Nabizadeh / EPA

There has been a demonstration against coronavirus restrictions in Sweden.

The demonstration was on Saturday in the Swedish capital, Stockholm. As many as 500 people took part in the demonstration.

6 police officers were injured in the demonstration. The police broke up the crowd because the demonstration was not permitted. Because of the coronavirus, large gatherings are banned in Sweden.

## Kela's easy-read brochures

Kela's easy-read brochures explain benefits briefly and clearly. Photo: Kela

Kela has published new easy-read brochures. They explain Kela's benefits and allowances in easy Finnish, that is, in plain language.

The easy-read brochures give information about all of Kela's benefits and allowances. The brochures are available at Kela's service points and, for example, at TE Offices.

The brochures can also be found on Kela's website, the address is kela.fi.
