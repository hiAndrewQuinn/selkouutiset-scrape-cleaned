# Friday 25.11.2022 (radio)

Centres where people can warm up were opened in Ukraine. Beijing's coronavirus restrictions are strict. An authority reminds that people must be helped. Alfa TV is closing down. The weather is dry.

## Ukraine

![People queuing and carrying water canisters.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8162,h_4591,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-103919263803ed370e63/1669349173)

People queuing and carrying water canisters. Illustrative image. Photo: Roman Pilipey / EPA

4,000 centres have been opened in Ukraine, where people can come when there is no electricity or water at home. In these centres people can, for example, drink, warm up and use the internet. The centres are free and always open.

Russia bombed Ukraine's power plants on Wednesday. The bombings cut off electricity and water for millions of people in different parts of Ukraine. Telephone and internet connections have been down.

## Beijing's coronavirus restrictions

![People argue with a coronavirus worker in a protective suit on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3454,h_1942,x_0,y_36/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1036925637b62d450064/1669103941)

Beijing has tightened coronavirus restrictions. Photo: Mark R. Cristino / EPA

Beijing, the capital of China, has stricter coronavirus restrictions than before.

Schools are closed. Everyone must take a coronavirus test every 2 days. A large part of the shops are closed.

In China, a record number of new coronavirus infections was recorded on Thursday and Friday.

## Helping people

![VR train](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5475,h_3080,x_0,y_436/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7388195fa3f9399db8c/1674730983)

Open image viewer

VR train. Illustrative image. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Police say that no one helped a man who fell onto the railway tracks in Espoo in October. A middle-aged man was seriously injured when a train hit him.

Police say that about 10 people were at the scene. No one even called for help.

The authority reminds that a person must always be helped if they are in distress. Otherwise, one may get a fine or even a prison sentence.

## Alfa-TV

![Microphone of Alfa-TV.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12625863)

Open image viewer

Alfa-TV is closing down. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The Finnish television channel Alfa-TV is closing down. Alfa-TV is owned by a company named Brilliance Communications. The company's money has now run out.

Brilliance Communications made a loss of more than 3 million euros last year. The company's turnover was 2 million euros.

Alfa-TV has about 30 employees.

8 new insect species have been found in Lapland. Of the new insects, 7 are flies and 1 is a wasp. The species were found when Metsähallitus studied the insects of Lapland.

New insect species will be given names in about a year's time.
