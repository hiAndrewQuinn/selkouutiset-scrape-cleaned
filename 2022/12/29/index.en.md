## Sexual offences

![Oulu courthouse](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10597600)

Open image viewer

Oulu courthouse. Photo: Timo Heikkala / Lehtikuva

The law on sexual offences is getting stricter at the turn of the year. The new law protects children and young people from sexual abuse better than now.

Sexual intercourse with a person under 16 will be child rape next year. The minimum punishment is 2 years of unconditional imprisonment.

Often the offender is another young person, and a minor can in future get a prison sentence. Still, the law does not restrict sexual relationships between young people, if they do not involve exploiting the other.

Almost half of those convicted of sexual offences against children have been young people or young adults.

## Ukraine

Russia has hit various parts of Ukraine hard with missiles.

Again, the targets of the attacks were the energy plants of many cities. For example, the city of Lviv in western Ukraine was left almost completely without electricity.

The Ukrainian army says that it managed to shoot down a large part of about 70 missiles.

Finland sent 5 truckloads of material aid to Ukraine last week. The loads contained supplies for the energy sector and hospitals, as well as school furniture.

## Housing prices

![Ellaroosa Rokka looks inside a kitchen cupboard with her back to the camera.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_625/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1047562639888496b5a8/1670940829)

Old homes' prices stayed at last year's level only in Oulu, elsewhere in the country prices fell. Photo: Sirkka Haverinen / Yle

Statistics Finland says that old flats in blocks of flats and terraced houses have become cheaper.

In November, prices of old owner-occupied flats fell by 3.4 percent from November last year. Prices fell especially in Helsinki and Tampere.

In November, estate agents sold about 40 percent fewer old homes than a year ago.

## Smuggling

![A vacuum cleaner, banknotes and other items in a cardboard box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_640,h_360,x_0,y_16/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105377463ad33cdba3ef/1672295439)

Open image viewer

Security check staff found a vacuum cleaner and a household appliance in cardboard boxes during X-ray screening. Several bundles of money had been hidden inside them. Photo: Customs

Customs has investigated a cash smuggling attempt. It was revealed in spring 2021 at an airport security check.

About 300,000 euros in cash had been hidden in the luggage, which is suspected to come from criminal activity.

Attempts were made to take the bundles of money to Iran inside household appliances.

The case is being investigated as aggravated money laundering. The suspect is a man who lives in the capital region.

## Finnair and China

![Two blue-and-white Finnair planes at Helsinki-Vantaa airport, airport equipment in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12569249)

Open image viewer

Finnair hopes that travel to China will recover. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

Airline Finnair plans to resume flights to Beijing when travel to China opens up.

China will no longer test travellers for Covid from 8 January. Finnair says that more people have already started to search for flights to China.

The company's aim is first to increase seat capacity on the Shanghai route.
