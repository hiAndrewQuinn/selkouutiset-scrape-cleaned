# Thursday 19.12.2019 (radio)

More and more women giving birth are overweight. The completion of the Olkiluoto 3 nuclear power plant is delayed again. The Christmas tree trade has started. The President has received Christmas greetings.

## Overweight is a risk for mothers giving birth

![An expectant mother's hand on her baby bump.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9908806)

Being overweight causes risks during pregnancy. Photo: Tiina-Leena Kurki / Yle

More and more women giving birth, that is, expectant mothers, are overweight. The number keeps growing.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that over 40 per cent of women giving birth are overweight. 16 per cent of women giving birth are obese.

Obesity increases many risks. They include, for example, gestational diabetes during pregnancy, caesarean sections and various infections. Overweight people also recover more slowly from operations than people of normal weight.

Last year the average age of all women giving birth was 31, and of first-time mothers just over 29.

Last year about 50,000 children were born in Finland.

## Olkiluoto is delayed again

![Olkiluoto 3 reactor building](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5885475d710a4957dcf/1632805957)

The completion of the Olkiluoto 3 nuclear power plant is delayed again.

The nuclear power plant builder Areva-Siemens has announced a new timetable. Areva says that the plant will start producing electricity regularly in March 2021.

The completion of the Olkiluoto third reactor has been delayed many times already. Originally, the nuclear power plant was to be ready in Eurajoki in Satakunta as early as 2009.

Areva says that testing of the systems has progressed slowly. In addition, there has been a shortage of spare parts.

## It's Christmas tree time again

![Nea Sipola and Christmas trees](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6231775df77cbec580e/1576500501)

About 1.5 million Christmas trees are bought. Photo: Kai Pohjanen / Yle

The Christmas tree trade has started. This Christmas, too, Finns will buy about 1.5 million trees for their homes.

Most of the trees sold are Finnish spruce.

The most environmentally friendly Christmas tree is a natural spruce that is found nearby. An artificial tree is ecological if it is used for about 20 years.

About a million Christmas trees come from Finnish tree farms. Christmas trees are also imported for sale, for example from Denmark.

Christmas trees are expected to sell in Finland this Christmas for perhaps 32 million euros.

## The President received Christmas greetings

![.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11126631)

Open image viewer

Christmas greetings for the President are already a tradition. Photo: Yle

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio have received the traditional Christmas greetings at the official residence of the president, Mäntyniemi.

Forest students gave the President a Christmas tree. The florists' association brought a Christmas flower to Mäntyniemi. Nature conservation organisations brought a basket of organic vegetables.

President Niinistö and Mrs Haukio also received a Christmas ham and a Christmas pike, among other things.

The boys' choir Cantores Minores of Helsinki Cathedral brought the President a greeting in song.

The President's Christmas greetings have a long tradition. For example, the first Christmas tree was handed over to President Urho Kekkonen in 1963.
