# Wednesday 8.5.2019 (radio)

Antti Rinne has said which parties will join the government negotiations. The right-wing parties are left out of the government negotiations. Immigration for work has grown. A Walk of Fame Finland is being built in Tampere. The weather will get warmer at the weekend.

## Government negotiations

![Anna-Maja Henriksson, Li Andersson, Antti Rinne, Juha Sipilä and Pekka Haavisto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10773629)

5 party leaders are joining the government negotiations. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

SDP party leader Antti Rinne has said which parties will join the government negotiations. Rinne is leading the government negotiations, because the SDP is the largest party in Parliament.

On Wednesday Rinne said that the Centre, the Greens, the Left Alliance and the RKP will start negotiating a government with the SDP. If the negotiations succeed, these parties will be in Finland's next government, and Antti Rinne will become Finland's prime minister.

## Right-wing parties left out

![National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10773574)

Open image viewer

National Coalition party leader Petteri Orpo. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The right-wing parties are outside the government negotiations. The Finns Party, the National Coalition and the Christian Democrats will not take part in the negotiations. If they are left out of the government, they will be Parliament's new opposition.

National Coalition party leader Petteri Orpo believes the National Coalition was not included in the government negotiations because of economic policy. The National Coalition disagrees with the SDP on many economic issues.

Orpo says that the National Coalition supports strict economic policy. This means that, in the National Coalition's view, Finland must save money.

## Immigration for work

![Workers at the A-L Sorsa home bakery in November 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10521552)

Open image viewer

Many residence permits have been applied for because of work. Photo: Annika Martikainen / Yle

Immigration for work has grown. The Finnish Ministry of the Interior reports this.

About 6,700 applicants received their first residence permit on the basis of work in 2017. In 2018 the figure was already about 7,700.

At the same time, the number of asylum applications has fallen. However, many applicants are still in the queue, because in previous years many asylum seekers came to Finland.

## Tampere's Star Street

![Pink's red high heels on a red carpet](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10639256)

Open image viewer

Singer Pink has a star on Hollywood's Walk of Fame. Photo: Nina Prommer / EPA

A new Star Street, or Walk of Fame Finland, is being built in Tampere. Stars will be added to the street for people who are skilled in the music industry.

Tampere is taking its model for Star Street from Hollywood. The world's most famous Walk of Fame is there.

The first stars will be set into Tampere's Star Street in the autumn.

## The weather changes

![Highest temperatures for the end of the week](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10772447)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Sää

The start of May has been cold. In the north, it still snowed 15 centimetres on Wednesday.

However, the weather is now changing, says meteorologist Kerttu Kotakorpi.

At the weekend it could be almost 20 degrees warm in Southern Finland. In Northern Finland it could be 15 degrees warm.

Rain is also expected at the weekend, Kotakorpi says. Rain cleans the air of pollen, and many people with allergies can feel better.
