# Monday 25.10.2021 (radio)

Mask recommendation. Corona patients and corona deaths. Corona restrictions in Estonia. NATO Secretary General. Film award. The weather.

## Use of masks

![A person wearing a face mask on a sunny autumn day in Pasila on 15 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4834,h_2719,x_0,y_506/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8540766141ee916967d/1631711295)

Train passengers can now decide for themselves whether they wear a mask. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The mask requirement on trains ends. VR told about this on Monday.

A passenger can now decide for themselves whether to use a mask on the train or not. However, VR recommends that a mask is used.

The mask requirement also ends in public transport in the Helsinki region.

On buses, commuter trains and the metro a mask is no longer compulsory. However, it is still good to use a mask.

## Corona figures

![Nurses walk in through the door to the corona patients in the intensive care unit at Tyks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11835833)

The number of corona patients in hospitals is growing. There are now 215 corona patients in Finland's hospitals.

During the weekend 11 people have died because of corona. In addition, about 1730 new corona infections were found over the weekend.

## Corona restrictions in Estonia

![Request to show a negative corona certificate at a hotel reception in Tallinn on 5 October 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_368/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8709736174df5e2fc2d/1635049348)

Open image viewer

A corona pass is now needed for everyone who visits Estonia. Photo: Teemu Salonen / Lehtikuva

New corona restrictions have come into force in Estonia, Finland's neighbouring country.

In Estonia there is now a mask requirement, for example, on buses and in shops.

Adults can only get into cinemas, theatres and concerts if they have a corona pass.

The corona restrictions in Estonia are valid until 10 January.

In Estonia it is hoped that the corona restrictions will reduce the number of corona patients in hospitals.

## NATO Secretary General

![Jens Stoltenberg and Sauli Niinistö at a press conference in the Presidential Palace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2646,h_1488,x_540,y_558/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-871233617674eb0e621/1635153194)

Open image viewer

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg and President of Finland Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg is visiting Finland. Stoltenberg talked with President Sauli Niinistö on Monday.

Stoltenberg estimates that Finland can help NATO improve relations with Russia. The relations between NATO and Russia are poor at the moment.

Finland is not a member of NATO, but Finland does a lot of cooperation with NATO.

On Friday of this week President Niinistö will visit Russia and meet Russian President Vladimir Putin.

## Film award

![Nasra (Yasmin Warsame) hugs Guled (Omar Abdi).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2933,h_1650,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82512960d4354e77309/1706789574)

Open image viewer

Grave digger Guled is played by Helsinki native Omar Abdi. Photo: Arttu Peltomaa / BUFO

A Finnish film has won an award in Africa.

The winning film is called Guled & Nasra, and it is in Somali.

The director of the film is Finnish-Somali Khadar Ayderus Ahmed.

The award was given at the Fespaco film festival. Fespaco is the most important film festival in Africa.

Guled & Nasra won the main prize of the film festival. The prize is 30 000 euros.

## Tuesday's weather

Snowfall continues in the north.

On Tuesday snow falls in Central and Northern Lapland. In Southern Lapland it rains.

Elsewhere in Finland there will be rain or drizzle, that is, very weak rain, only in places.

In the southern and central parts of the country the temperature on Tuesday is between +5 and +10 degrees, in Lapland between -5 and +1 degrees.
