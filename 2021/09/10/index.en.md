# Friday 10.9.2021 (radio)

Women on municipal boards. Biowaste from detached houses. Industrial orders. Nord Stream 2. Corona restrictions in Denmark.

## Municipal decision-makers

![Gender distribution of the chairs of municipal councils](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12092895)

Open image viewer

Photo: Asmo Raimoaho / Yle

There are more women among the chairs of municipal boards than in the last electoral term. Yle's survey says that 36 per cent of the chairs are women.

There are fewer women among the chairs of municipal councils than before.

Of all council members, women now make up a record 40 per cent.

In Finland there are 2 municipalities where the chairs are only women. These municipalities are Viitasaari and Taivassalo. In 23 municipalities the chair is only men.

## Biowaste and mixed waste

![Household biowaste in a biowaste bin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4160,h_2340,x_0,y_1950/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7132565f4924e7befd5/1671003419)

Open image viewer

Biowaste does not belong in mixed waste. Photo: Matias Väänänen/ Yle

Many residents of detached houses do not sort biowaste. They put the biowaste into ordinary mixed waste.

In the capital region as many as 45 per cent of the mixed waste from detached houses is biowaste.

The new waste act requires changes in 3 years. Then, in all municipalities with over 10,000 people, sorting will also apply to detached houses.

## Industrial orders

![Champion Door's industrial hall in Nivala, interior.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81549460b8f7bfd2ace/1622734910)

Open image viewer

Industrial hall. Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

Industry received many new orders in July. Growth was almost 47 per cent from July last year.

The most new orders were in the metal industry, over 60 per cent. Growth in the paper industry was also strong. Orders came in 30 per cent more.

During the first half of the year, industry's new orders grew 28 per cent from the previous year.

## Nord Stream 2

![Worker at the Nord Stream 2 construction site in Germany.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11854119)

Open image viewer

Construction site of the Nord Stream 2 gas pipeline in Germany in 2019. Photo: Clemens Bilan / EPA

The Nord Stream 2 gas pipeline is fully complete, says the Russian gas company Gazprom.

The pipeline runs along the bottom of the Baltic Sea from Russia to Germany. The pipeline is over 1,200 kilometres long, and it runs through Finland's exclusive economic zone in the Baltic Sea.

The gas pipeline project has been criticised a lot. A consequence may be that countries like Germany become more dependent on Russian energy. It has been suspected that Russia may pressure other countries with natural gas in political disputes.

## Corona restrictions in Denmark

![People outside a nightclub in Copenhagen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_700/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-85092161374e1134500/1631014891)

Open image viewer

Revellers outside a Copenhagen nightclub on 3 September. Nightclubs have been allowed to be open freely since the start of September. Today nightclubs also give up the vaccine pass requirement.  Photo: Olafur Steinar / AOP

Denmark has today ended all corona restrictions within the country. In Denmark, the coronavirus disease is no longer classified as a critical factor for society.

The decision is justified by the fact that the disease situation is under control in Denmark. In addition, vaccine coverage is high: four out of five Danes have received two vaccine doses.

The Institute for Health and Welfare estimates that Finland may reach this level by the end of October.

Tomorrow, Saturday, a concert for 50,000 spectators is sold out in Copenhagen. A week ago already, an outdoor festival with 15,000 visitors was held in the capital.
