## Trump's term ends

In the USA, President Donald Trump has his last day as president today.

In the USA it is customary that on the last working day the outgoing president pardons many people who have been sentenced for a crime.

Tomorrow, Wednesday, the new president, Joe Biden, will take office. Trump has announced that he will not take part in the ceremony.

## Russia's opposition leader

![Aleksei Navalnyi is expected in Moscow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11741700)

Aleksei Navalnyi returned to Moscow on Sunday. Photo: Kirill Kudryavtsev / AFP

Russia says that it does not intend to care that other countries demand the release of opposition leader Aleksei Navalnyi.

Navalnyi was sentenced yesterday to 1 month in pre-trial detention. Russia accuses Navalnyi of many crimes.

Other countries say that the accusations are made up and that they are used to stop the opposition leader from taking part in politics.

## Partial old-age pension

![An elderly person's hands holding coins.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_75/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4743715aba0300bdf66/1683557210)

Open image viewer

Many Finns want to work part-time and be partly retired. Photo: All Over Press

The partial old-age pension interests many Finns.

A partial old-age pension means that an employee shortens the working week and is retired for a few days of the week.

So he gets a wage from work and at the same time a pension.

Last year 13,300 people applied for the partial old-age pension. There were more applicants than the year before.

So far almost 56,000 people in Finland have received the corona vaccine, says the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

217 new corona infections have been found today.

In two weeks about 3,500 infections have been reported. That is as many as in the 2 previous weeks.

## Traffic decreased because of corona

![A woman wearing a face mask in a shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11695254)

Open image viewer

All traffic has decreased during the corona time. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The corona time clearly shows in traffic volumes.

Traffic has decreased on roads, railway tracks and in the air, says Fintraffic, the Finnish state traffic management company.

The number of cars decreased on main roads by about 10 percent and the number of trains decreased by 4 percent.

The number of flights decreased by almost 60 percent.

In addition, the number of passengers on ships has fallen by a record amount.

Bus and train traffic has decreased a lot, because people are moving around less now.
