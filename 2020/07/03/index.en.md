# Friday 3.7.2020 (radio)

The corona crisis is affecting shipping companies. The corona situation in Finland is good, but the coronavirus is spreading around the world. False information about the danger of radiation is spreading on social media. A tram is being tested in Tampere at the weekend.

The TV Clear News has a summer break and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Passenger ships in the corona crisis

![Birka Cruises in the port of Stockholm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11431858)

Birka Cruises in the port of Stockholm in February 2014. Photo: Timo Jaakonaho / Lehtikuva

The corona crisis is affecting shipping companies. Many passenger ships have not sailed between Finland and Sweden for many months because of corona restrictions.

Now the shipping company Birka Cruises is stopping its operations completely. The company has had a passenger ship between Mariehamn and Stockholm.

About 500 Birka employees may lose their jobs. Half of them are Finns.

The shipping company Tallink Silja is also reducing its ship employees. The company is laying off 300 employees. Most are Swedes, but about 70 of them are Finns.

## The corona situation in Finland

![Keep a safe distance! sticker on the airport floor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11331731)

The corona situation in Finland is good.

THL reported on Friday that there has been 1 new corona death and 1 new corona infection in Finland.

The coronavirus has killed 329 people in Finland in total. There are over 7,200 corona infections.

## The world's corona situation

![Medical staff moving a patient in front of a hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11431290)

Open image viewer

Hospital staff are transporting a corona patient in the USA. Photo: Go Nakamura / AFP

The coronavirus is spreading quickly around the world.

The coronavirus is now spreading especially on the American continent. The USA and Brazil are the countries with the most corona cases.

The USA has over 2.7 million corona infections, and almost 130,000 people have died because of corona. That is more than in any other country.

Brazil has the second most corona cases. Brazil has about 1.5 million corona infections, and over 60,000 Brazilians have died because of corona.

## No radiation danger

![The Radiation and Nuclear Safety Authority logo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11431766)

Open image viewer

The Radiation and Nuclear Safety Authority, STUK, monitors radiation safety in Finland. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

There is no radiation danger in Finland.

Warnings about radiation danger have been written about on social media. The Radiation and Nuclear Safety Authority says that the information is false.

On social media it has been written that a Russian nuclear power plant is causing a radiation danger in Finland. The Radiation and Nuclear Safety Authority says that there is no danger.

## Tampere tram

![Tests of the Tampere tram.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_375/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6959475efed51a2d746/1593759191)

Open image viewer

The Tampere tram is being tested at the weekend. Photo: Tampereen Raitiotie Oy

In Tampere you can see a tram at the weekend.

The new tram in Tampere is being tested on Saturday and Sunday. Test runs will continue on Monday as well.

During the test the tram's speed is increased gradually. Already on Saturday the tram will run at its maximum speed, which is 70 kilometres per hour.

Tram traffic in Tampere begins in a year, that is, in summer 2021. Then there will be 19 trams in traffic.
