# Sunday 1.8.2021

The rise in corona infections continues. There are many new corona infections in Russia. Myanmar has a serious corona crisis. People have died in forest fires in Turkey. A corona vaccination event was held in Rovaniemi. Rain weakens on Monday.

The TV plain-language news is on summer break.
The radio plain-language news is made every day, also in the summer.

## The corona situation in Finland

![A man is given a corona test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82110660c87b18bdba3/1623751620)

On Sunday over 500 new corona infections were reported in Finland. About half of the new corona infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa region.

The number of corona infections has grown quickly in recent weeks.

In the last 2 weeks about 6,700 corona infections have been reported. That is about 3,000 more than during the previous 2 weeks.

## The corona situation abroad

![Three volunteers in protective suits carry a coffin on a stretcher. Paper decorated with pale red hearts is wrapped around the coffin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12042109)

In Myanmar hundreds of people die every day because of corona. Photo: Nyunt Win / EPA

Bad corona news has also come from abroad.

In Russia on Sunday almost 23,000 new corona infections were reported. It is the biggest number in one day during the whole corona era. In addition, in Russia on Sunday almost 800 new corona deaths were reported.

In Asia, Myanmar has a serious corona crisis. In the country hundreds of people die of corona every day. There are thousands of new corona infections a day.

Soldiers seized power in Myanmar half a year ago. After that the country's healthcare has quickly got worse. Only 3 percent of Myanmar's residents have so far had a corona vaccination.

## Turkey's forest fires

![Firefighters putting out forest fires in Turkey.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_267/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8374506105d804d1025/1627773927)

Open image viewer

Firefighters are putting out a forest fire in Turkey. Photo: Erdem Sahin / EPA

There are serious forest fires in Turkey. Forest is burning in the southern parts of Turkey.

At least 6 people have died in the forest fires in total. On Saturday 2 firefighters died, who were in extinguishing work.

More than 300 people have been injured in the fires. Many people have been moved to safety away from the fire area.

The forest fires in Turkey began on Wednesday. It has been suspected that some of the fires may have been set on purpose.

The Turkish president says that the guilty will be punished severely if fires have been set on purpose.

## Vaccination event in Rovaniemi

![Lordi gets a corona vaccination.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6048,h_3402,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8375566106a0446baa5/1627824411)

It should also be said that a corona vaccination event was held in Rovaniemi.

The aim was to remind especially young people how important it is to get corona vaccinations.

Lapland celebrities were also there. Eurovision winner Lordi got a corona vaccination at the event.

Santa Claus was there too, but he has already been vaccinated earlier because of his old age.

Rain will weaken on Monday. Showers will still come in different parts of Finland. In the north it is dry on Monday, that is, no rain falls there.

Tuesday is a rainy day in the east. A lot of rain may fall and the wind may be strong. In the west it is dry on Tuesday.

During the start of the week the temperature is between 15 and 20 degrees in a large part of the country.
