# Saturday 29.10.2016 (radio)

In the USA the police are investigating Hillary Clinton's emails again. Musician Bob Dylan accepts the Nobel Prize in Literature. There was a sauna day in Helsinki. Winter weather begins next week. The clocks are changed to winter time.

## Clinton's emails

![Hillary Clinton campaigns in Winston-Salem, North Carolina.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9260499)

Hillary Clinton is the Democrats' presidential candidate. Photo: Brian Bilanco / EPA

In the USA the police are investigating Hillary Clinton's emails again. Clinton is the Democratic Party's presidential candidate.

Clinton perhaps sent secret information from her own private email when she was secretary of state. The federal police, the FBI, said in the summer that Clinton was careless, but she did not commit a crime.

Now new emails have been found that Clinton perhaps sent from her private email as secretary of state. That is why the police are investigating the matter again.

Hillary Clinton hopes that the FBI will say more about the matter as soon as possible.

The US presidential election is in a little over a week. Election day is 8 November.

## Dylan and the Nobel Prize

![The announcement event of the Nobel Prize in Literature in Stockholm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9260487)

Open image viewer

Dylan has written lyrics for songs. Photo: Pelle T Nilsson / AOP

Musician Bob Dylan says that he accepts the Nobel Prize in Literature.

The Swedish Academy announced more than 2 weeks ago that it is giving the Nobel Prize to Dylan. Until now Dylan has not said anything about it, but now he phoned the Swedish Academy. Dylan said that the Nobel Prize is a great honour.

Musician Bob Dylan got the Nobel Prize in Literature because he has written many fine lyrics for songs. The Swedish Academy thinks that they have changed the art of poetry.

## Helsinki's sauna day

![sauna benches steam stove led](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6486667)

Open image viewer

Saunas were ready for visitors in Helsinki. Photo: Yle

There has been a sauna day in Helsinki on Saturday. Helsinki Sauna Day was now held for the second time. The first time was last spring.

The idea of the sauna day is that people can go to saunas that they usually cannot get into. For example, many saunas of companies and ordinary Finns are open to visitors on the sauna day. The visitors can go to the sauna for free. On the sauna day foreigners also have a good chance to try different Finnish saunas.

## The weather is getting colder

![A young man walks in the snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9260555)

Open image viewer

It may look like this next week. Photo: Kaisa Siren / AOP

Winter is coming next week. Yle's meteorologist Matti Huutonen says that the weather is getting colder. Next week there may be frost in the whole of Finland.

Snowfall is also possible. In Southern Finland snow may fall in the middle of the week. Matti Huutonen says that now is a good time to change to winter tyres on your car.

## Remember winter time!

On Sunday morning it is good to remember that the clocks are also changed to winter time. The clocks are moved back one hour on the night between Saturday and Sunday at 4 o'clock. So this weekend Sunday gets one extra hour.
