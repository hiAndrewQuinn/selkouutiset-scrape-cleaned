# Monday 14.5.2018 (radio)

There have been violent demonstrations in the Middle East. The President of Iceland is visiting Finland. Cherry trees are blossoming in Helsinki. Light and warmth are good for health. Monday was a hot day.

## Demonstrations in the Middle East

![A Palestinian demonstrator threw stones in the Gaza Strip at Abu Safia on 14 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10204428)

Demonstrators in the Gaza Strip. Photo: Luca Piergiovanni / EPA

There have been violent demonstrations in the Middle East. Many people have died when Israeli soldiers shot demonstrators in the Gaza Strip, on the border with Israel.

The demonstrators are Palestinians. They are angry that the US embassy in Israel has moved. Previously the embassy was in Tel Aviv. Now it is in Jerusalem.

Moving the embassy means that the US considers Jerusalem to be the capital of Israel. Palestinians think that Jerusalem is the capital of the Palestinians.

## The President of Iceland

![Icelandic President Guðni Thorlacius Jóhannesson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9962600)

Open image viewer

Icelandic President Guðni Jóhannesson. Photo: Birgir Thor Hardarson / EPA

The President of Iceland, Guðni Jóhannesson, is visiting Finland. Jóhannesson will meet President Sauli Niinistö in Finland.

The presidents of Finland and Iceland will get to know Finnish companies together. They will also talk about cooperation between the Nordic countries.

Finland and Iceland are Nordic countries, together with Sweden, Denmark and Norway. The Nordic countries cooperate a lot.

## Cherry trees in Helsinki

![Cherry trees.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10203754)

Open image viewer

The cherry park in Roihuvuori. Photo: Lena Nelskylä / Yle

The cherry trees are now blossoming in the Cherry Park in Roihuvuori, Helsinki. There are 250 cherry trees in the park.

Next Saturday, a Hanami festival will be held in the park. Hanami is Japanese, and it means looking at flowers.

At the Roihuvuori Hanami festival people can also see Japanese culture. For example, butoh dance and tea art are on display at the festival. People can also buy Japanese food.

Up to 20,000 guests are expected at the Roihuvuori Hanami festival.

## The best temperature

![A view from a cottage to the lake.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10121299)

Open image viewer

Warm weather is often better than a heatwave. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finland now has summer weather. Light and warmth are good for health.

However, researcher Sirkka Rissanen says that the best temperature for Finns is +14 degrees. Then Finns have the fewest problems, Rissanen says.

For example, age, health and fitness affect how much a person can cope with hot weather. It is hard for a person to cope with heat if they have a heart disease, for example.

## Heat in May

![Beach volleyball players on the sandy beach at Hietaniemi in Helsinki on Monday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10204470)

The heat has brought people to the beach. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Monday too has been a very warm day. In Turku, for example, it was a little over 29 degrees.

There have already been 4 hot days in a row. A hot day means that the temperature is over 25 degrees. Usually in mid-May the temperature is about 15 degrees.

Yle meteorologist Matti Huutonen forecasts that the hot days will continue until Thursday or Friday.
