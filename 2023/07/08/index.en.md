## NATO and security

![NATO and Finnish flags.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4928,h_2772,x_0,y_507/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113999764a920e3658f6/1688805677)

Yle has made a survey of people about whether membership of the NATO military alliance increases Finland's security.

More than 50 per cent of people think that Finland now feels safer than before NATO membership. However, many people say that NATO does not affect life in any way. Only 3 per cent of people say in the survey that NATO membership has reduced security a lot or somewhat.

Finland has been a NATO member for about 3 months.

## Russia's attack on Ukraine

![Map of the areas Russia has captured in Ukraine on 7.7.2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1080,h_603,x_0,y_168/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113931564a79f4193e9b/1688706945)

Photo: Harri Vähäkangas / Yle, MapCreator, OpenStreetMap

On Saturday, 500 days have passed since Russia attacked Ukraine. Russian troops attacked Ukraine on 24 February 2022.

The UN says that more than 25,000 civilians have died in the war. Tens of thousands of soldiers have died on both sides.

Russia occupies large areas in southern and eastern Ukraine. At first, Russia's aim was that the Ukrainian capital, Kyiv, would be captured quickly. This did not succeed.

Millions of people have had to leave their homes in Ukraine because of Russia's invasion war.

## Unlawful stay in Finland

![Migri immigration office in Helsinki. 3.2.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_418/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-769762601aa93b34693/1684398041)

The Finnish Immigration Service, Migri, bans the entry into Finland of about 400 citizens of EU countries every year. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

In Finland there are many citizens of EU countries who do not have permission to be in Finland.

The Finnish Immigration Service, Migri, bans about 400 citizens of EU countries from entering Finland every year. The reason for the ban is often that the person has committed crimes in Finland and has therefore been deported from the country. However, many people come back to Finland soon, without permission.

It is easy to come back to Finland, because there are no border checks at the borders of Schengen countries. The most citizens removed from Finland are Romanian and Estonian citizens.

The Border Guard monitors Finland's borders more closely than usual for a week. The reason is the visit of US President Joe Biden to Finland.

Finland also monitors the borders between Schengen countries until next Saturday.

US President Joe Biden visits Helsinki on Thursday next week.
