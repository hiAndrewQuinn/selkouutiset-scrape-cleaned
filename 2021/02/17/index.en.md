# Wednesday 17.2.2021 (radio)

A court case over the murder of a young boy has begun in Helsinki. More than 500 new coronavirus infections have been found in Finland. Ferry passengers will soon not be able to enter Finland without a coronavirus test certificate. The government wants fathers to use parental leave more. Frost has cut off electricity in America. In Finland the frost weakens a little.

## Murder trial

![Candles and flowers at the Koskela murder site](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-774921602cc27766da8/1613565410)

A court case over the murder of a young boy has begun in Helsinki.

A 16-year-old boy was murdered in Koskela, Helsinki, in December. 3 boys of the same age are accused of the murder.

The case has been talked about a lot in Finland, because the act was brutal. The perpetrators beat the boy for a long time.

The case has also been talked about because the boy was a client of child welfare. Now the police are investigating whether child welfare and the school did anything wrong.

## Coronavirus infections

More than 548 new coronavirus infections have been found in Finland. There are 3 new coronavirus deaths.

Most new coronavirus infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa area. Almost all of the area's new coronavirus infections are a variant of the coronavirus, which spreads easily.

The Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district, HUS, suggests that gathering restrictions be tightened. HUS suggests that only 6 people may take part in events. Now, 10 participants are allowed.

## Ferry passengers

Ferry passengers must have a certificate of a coronavirus test from next Tuesday.

A ferry passenger must prove that they do not have the coronavirus when they come to Finland by ferry.

The ferry companies told about this on Wednesday.

## Parental leave

![Changing a baby's nappy](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11327444)

Open image viewer

Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The government plans to reform parental leave.

Parental leave will be extended by 2 months. The government proposes that both parents get 6.5 months of leave.

One parent can transfer at most 2 months of their own parental leave to the other parent.

The government especially wants fathers to stay at home to look after children more often than now.

The aim is that the reform will come into force in August 2022.

## Weather in America

![A man saws a fallen tree in the snow](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11794365)

Open image viewer

Americans have heated their homes with wood too, when the electricity has been cut off. Photo: EPA-EFE/Ralph Lauer

There is harsh winter weather in America.

In the USA and Mexico, millions of people have been without electricity, because the frost has made the delivery of gas, for example, difficult.

In the USA, dozens of people have also died because of the frost. The frost has caused traffic accidents, for example.

## Finnish weather

![Weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11796270)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

In Finland the frost weakens a little. A mild and humid air flows in from the south-west on Thursday.

Cloudiness is heavy in the western and northern parts of the country. On Thursday the sun will still shine in the east.

The frost is between 10 and 15 degrees in many places. From the west, the weather becomes milder.
