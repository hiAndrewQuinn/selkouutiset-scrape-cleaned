# Thursday 7.6.2018 (radio)

In Sweden a court has given a verdict for a terrorist crime. Finns travel abroad most to Estonia, Sweden and Spain. SDP is the biggest party in Yle's new poll. The Chinese Hebei University is starting to teach Finnish.

## Terrorist verdict in Sweden

![Rakhmat Akilov leaving the courtroom on 14 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10083559)

Rahkmat Akilov in court in Stockholm. Photo: Fredrik Sandberg / AOP

In Finland's neighbouring country, Sweden, a court has given a verdict for a terrorist crime. The court sentenced Uzbek national Rahkmat Akilov to prison.

Akilov was punished for an attack he carried out last year. He drove a lorry into people in the Swedish capital, Stockholm. 5 people died and 10 were injured.

Before the crime, Akilov applied for asylum in Sweden, but he did not get it.

## Finns' trips abroad

![Viking and Silja Line ships in the port](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9755157)

Open image viewer

A Finn often starts a trip abroad from a port. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / AOP

Finns make many trips abroad. When a Finn travels abroad, they often go to a neighbouring country.

The most popular destination is Estonia. Finns make more trips to Estonia than to any other country. However, Finns' trips to Estonia have decreased this year.

Second, Finns go most to Sweden. During the first part of the year, Finns made more trips to Sweden than a year ago.

After the neighbouring countries, the most popular destination is Spain. The number of trips to Spain has also grown.

## Yle's party poll

![Party support measurement, May-June](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10240414)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics

The Social Democrats, SDP, are the biggest party in Yle's new poll.

20 percent of the poll's respondents supported the Social Democrats. SDP is an opposition party, that is, it is not in government.

The government parties, the National Coalition Party and the Centre Party, come behind SDP. Support for the National Coalition Party is just over 19 percent, and support for the Centre Party is just under 18 percent.

The support for the 3 big parties is so close that next year's parliamentary elections may be exciting. After the election, the post of Prime Minister may go to the leader of the SDP, the National Coalition Party or the Centre Party.

There are no big changes in support for the other parties. The exact figures can be found on Yle's website.

## Finnish in China

![Taija Hämäläinen, lecturer in Beijing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10242540)

Open image viewer

Here Finnish is being studied in Beijing. Photo: Taija Hämäläinen

China is hosting the Winter Olympics in 2022.

China wants the Olympics to have guides who also speak Finnish. That is why Hebei University is starting to teach Finnish.

Hebei University intends to take about 50 or 60 Finnish students. The university believes that in 4 years' time the students will speak fluent Finnish.

Today Finnish is taught in Chinese universities in Beijing and Tianjin.
