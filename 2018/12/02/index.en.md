# Sunday 2.12.2018 (radio)

Education Minister Sanni Grahn-Laasonen hopes that all families will vaccinate their children. China and the USA have agreed that they will not bring in new tariffs against each other. People react to the dark winter in different ways. On Monday it snows.

## Vaccinating children

![A baby is being vaccinated.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10440819)

Open image viewer

The vaccine gives good protection against measles too. Photo: AOP

Education Minister Sanni Grahn-Laasonen hopes that all families will vaccinate their children. The Education Minister wants to find out whether vaccinating a child could be, for example, a condition for child benefit.

In Finland, people are now discussing the vaccination of children, because a small child got measles in Ostrobothnia, in the municipality of Luoto. Measles is a serious illness for small children. However, the vaccine gives good protection against measles.

In Luoto only 75 percent of residents have got vaccines against various diseases, because many people oppose vaccines. Elsewhere in Finland, 95 percent of residents have got vaccines.

## Trade disputes between China and the USA

![Xi Jinping and Donald Trump in a close-up.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10535783)

Open image viewer

The President of China, Xi Jinping, and the President of the USA, Donald Trump. Photo: Nicolas Asfouri / AFP

There has been good economic news from the world. China and the USA have agreed on part of their trade disputes. The presidents of the two countries negotiated at a meeting of rich industrial countries.

China and the USA agreed that they will not bring in new tariffs against each other. The USA also will not raise tariffs at the beginning of next year, as it had planned to do. The countries will try to agree on the rest of the trade disputes soon.

Tariffs have disturbed trade between China and the USA for some time. The USA has put tariffs on Chinese goods, and China has put tariffs on American goods.

## Winter darkness

![A woman walks on a dim shopping street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9335688)

Open image viewer

Alone in the dark. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

It is dark in Finland now. However, people react to the darkness in different ways, says research professor Timo Partonen.

For many, the winter darkness is hard. They eat more and move around less than in summer. They also sleep badly and meet friends less often.

Many look for help in a trip to the south. A holiday in the sun improves your mood. However, the good feeling lasts only 2 or 3 weeks.

There are also other ways to improve your mood if the winter darkness feels heavy. For example, a bright light lamp, exercise and healthy food help.

And there are also those who do well in the dark winter. Though they hope for a real winter and snow.

## Snow and rain

On Monday it snows. It snows in Southern Finland and Western Finland. Roads may be slippery.

On Tuesday the precipitation is already rain. The rain melts the snow away from the south and the west.
