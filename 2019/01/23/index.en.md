# Wednesday 23.1.2019 (radio)

Debt problems are getting worse. Young people cannot get flats. There are many unemployed shipbuilders. Pertti is a social media celebrity dog. The weather is getting colder.

## Over-indebtedness is a problem

![A big pile of bills.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_432/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-33303958dcb41c1aefa/1598850195)

Many Finns run into trouble with debts. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Many Finns have run into trouble because of debts.

The Guarantee Foundation is an organisation that helps people in debt. The Guarantee Foundation says that a record number of people needed help last year.

Ordinary people fall into debt trouble. They have a job, but the money is not enough to pay the debts.

Many people run into trouble because of payday loans. A payday loan is easy to get, but it has a really high interest rate.

## Many young homeless people in Helsinki

![Paula dances.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10610200)

Open image viewer

24-year-old Paula had been without a home of her own for 2 years. Photo: Yle

Experts are worried that young people are without a flat in Helsinki.

There are about 900 young people in Helsinki who have no home or any place to live. The number of homeless young people is growing.

Young people often have small incomes, and they cannot afford expensive rent.

There is also a severe shortage of the city's rental flats in Helsinki. More than 23,000 people are on the city's housing waiting list.

## Shipyards are short of workers, or are they?

![A welder works at a shipyard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10413936)

Open image viewer

The shipyards say that they are short of workers. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

There are many unemployed shipbuilders in Western Finland.

However, the shipyards have hired workers from abroad. The shipyards say that they are short of workers.

The shipbuilders' trade union suspects that the shipyards favour foreign temporary agency workers. The trade union suspects that temporary agency workers are paid much lower wages.

According to the trade union, a skilled worker should be paid about 15 euros an hour. Temporary agency workers may have been paid only 3 euros an hour.

There are also many animal celebrities on social media.

One of Finland's animal celebrities is Pertti the dog. Pertti is a Labrador retriever who comments on all kinds of things on Facebook. Sometimes Pertti behaves badly.

Pertti also has an important volunteer job. It searches for missing people.

## The cold weather continues

![Especially on sunny days, the pressure-ice ridges are bathed in wonderful blue shades.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10610184)

During the night there is hard frost across the whole country. Tomorrow, Thursday, will also be a cold day.

In Southern Finland the frost is about 10 degrees. In Central Finland the frost is 20 degrees.

In Northern Finland the frost may be 35 degrees.
