## The employment situation of immigrants

![From an integration school for immigrants.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7973768)

Open image viewer

Integration training helps immigrants get work. Photo: Yle

The employment situation of immigrants has improved. A new study compares immigrants who came to Finland in the 1990s and immigrants who moved to Finland in the 2000s. Immigrants of the 2000s got work clearly better during their first years.

But there are big differences between immigrant women and immigrant men. Women have found work less well than men, and women also usually get less pay. One reason for this is that it is hard for an immigrant woman to go into working life if she comes from a country where women do not go to work.

The study also says that young immigrants get work more easily than old ones. And Europeans get work more easily than immigrants who come from the Middle East or Somalia.

Helsinki is the world's 3rd best city to live in. This is what the American magazine Metropolis, whose field is architecture and design, writes. The magazine praises Helsinki's nature and good education.

Metropolis thinks the best home city in the world is Copenhagen. Berlin is 2nd best.

## Shift daycare

![Two children play with dolls.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8501536)

Open image viewer

Many families need daycare in the evening or at night too. Photo: Eeva Pentikäinen / Yle

In Finland, many children are in shift daycare. They need care at different times.

Many municipalities are thinking about how to arrange pre-school education when some children may be in daycare at night. In Finland, all children must get equally good pre-school education.

The municipalities hope for common guidelines for the whole country.

## Undocumented people

![A street musician on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6610249)

Open image viewer

There are people in Finland without a permit. Photo: Yle

The police estimate that the number of undocumented people in Finland is growing. A person is undocumented if they are in Finland without a permit.

Undocumented people live outside society. In the spring, there were perhaps about 2500 foreigners in Finland whose whereabouts the police did not know.

Chief Kari Harju from the Finnish Security Intelligence Service believes that the number will rise. Many asylum seekers found out in the summer that they cannot stay in Finland. Since July, about 3000 people have appealed to the court about it. Some of them may stay in Finland without a permit if the court does not change the negative asylum decision.

## Car key

![A car's smart key on a fridge shelf.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9164048)

Open image viewer

The smart key's signal does not go through the thick walls of the fridge. Photo: Vilma Ruokoski / Yle

The police say that it is a good idea to keep a car's smart key in the fridge. Criminals cannot steal the lock's signal when the key is in the fridge.

Another way is to wrap the key in foil.

Criminals can steal the signal of a car's smart key from as far as 100 metres away. In that way, thieves get into the car.
