## Corona vaccines

![Novavax vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5367,h_3018,x_0,y_552/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-874293617fe7a3da0f4/1639203875)

Open image viewer

Photo: AOP

New corona vaccines are expected in Europe. The new corona vaccines are made in the same way as vaccines against many other diseases.

Now, corona vaccines are in use that were made with a new kind of technology. That is why some people do not want to take them.

Doctors hope that as many people as possible dare to take a corona vaccine that was made with the old and familiar technology.

The old production technology is used, for example, by the American pharmaceutical company Novavax and the French-Austrian pharmaceutical company Valneva. Their corona vaccines are now also expected on the European market.

## Workplace reviews on social media

![A person sits on a chair with a smartphone in their hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3696,h_2078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-2602975655c9302d38b/1637751650)

Open image viewer

It is important for companies how they are reviewed on social media. Photo: Yle

Employees now review workplaces on social media.

For example, on the online service Glassdoor, millions of employees from different parts of the world tell about their experiences of workplaces.

The reviews are important for companies. It is easier for companies to get good employees if they get good reviews.

About 800 Finnish companies have also been reviewed on Glassdoor.

Finnish companies get praise, for example, for teamwork. Finnish companies get criticism, for example, for their employees progressing slowly in their careers.

## Record-warm summer

![A hot day was spent at Marjaniemi beach in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5388,h_3031,x_0,y_556/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83168560eeaad2e306a/1716791394)

Open image viewer

Picture from summer 2021. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that the average temperature of 2021 was normal.

However, summer last year was record-warm. In June there were 25 hot days, the most in 60 years.

The highest temperature last year was 34 degrees. It was measured in Heinola in Southern Finland on 15 July.

The coldest temperature last year was minus 39.7 degrees. It was measured in Utsjoki in Lapland on 20 February.

Snowfall continues. On Tuesday snow will fall in the southern and central parts of Finland, and in the north there will be snow showers.

On Wednesday the weather is cloudy and snow showers come in different parts of Finland.
