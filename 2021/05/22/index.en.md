# Saturday 22.5.2021 (radio)

Finance Minister. Ice hockey world championships. Lennu the dog. Allergy symptoms. Weather.

## Finance Minister

![Annika Saarikko and Matti Vanhanen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11907664)

Open image viewer

Annika Saarikko and Matti Vanhanen. Photo: Jussi Toivanen / Government Office

Finland's Minister of Finance, that is, the finance minister, is changing. The finance minister is now Matti Vanhanen. Vanhanen is ending his work as finance minister next week.

The post of finance minister will go to Annika Saarikko. Saarikko is the leader of the Centre Party. Vanhanen also belongs to the Centre.

Annika Saarikko is now Minister of Science and Culture. That is why the Minister of Science and Culture is also changing when Saarikko starts work as finance minister. It is not yet known who the new Minister of Science and Culture will be.

Matti Vanhanen says that now is a good time to change the finance minister. Under the finance minister, planning will soon begin on how the state will use money next year.

## Ice hockey world championships

![Finland celebrates Marko Anttila's second goal in the 2019 World Championship final.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2560,h_1440,x_0,y_100/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6731545eb678d0b9b81/1589016815)

Open image viewer

Marko Anttila was the hero of Finland's team at the world championships in 2019. Photo: CTK Photo/Vit Simanek/All Over Press

The ice hockey world championships have started.

The world championships are being held this year in Latvia. The Latvian capital Riga is the host city of the tournament.

The plan was that some world championship games would also be held in Belarus. However, Belarus may not host the world championship games, because it treats President Lukashenko's opponents badly.

The ice hockey world championships last 2 weeks. The final game is played on 6 June.

Finland won the previous world championships in 2019. Last year no world championships were held because of the corona.

## The president's dog

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö's dog Lennu has died. Lennu was a 10-year-old Boston terrier.

Lennu was familiar to Finns from newspapers and television. Lennu was often also present at the president's official events.

The Office of the President announced Lennu's death on Twitter. The president's spouse Jenni Haukio also remembered Lennu on Twitter.

Rain has eased life for allergic people. Rain cleans the air of pollen.

Pollen causes symptoms for allergic people: eyes sting and the nose runs.

This spring there has been a lot of birch pollen in the air in particular.

## Weather

On Sunday there will be rain showers in the south. Sleet showers will come in places in the north.

The rain showers continue in the south also at the start of next week. Elsewhere in the country it is dry, so no more rain will fall.

The temperature is about 15 degrees in the south and about 5 degrees in the north.
