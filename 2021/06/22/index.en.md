## Coronavirus vaccination certificate

![A coronavirus vaccination certificate on a phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4034,h_2269,x_0,y_6/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82413560d1b27c91dea/1624357404)

Open image viewer

The coronavirus vaccination certificate can be found at kanta.fi. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Finland has introduced the European Union's coronavirus vaccination certificate.

Finns find their own coronavirus vaccination certificate online, in the Omakanta service. The address is kanta.fi.

Information about the coronavirus vaccination comes to the Omakanta service at the latest 5 days after the vaccination.

People need a coronavirus vaccination certificate, for example, when travelling abroad.

## Refugees of the year

![Sara Al Husaini and Ahmed Mesaedy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4692,h_2639,x_0,y_1642/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82400360d1755e4f12a/1624340230)

Open image viewer

Sara Al Husaini and Ahmed Mesaedy were chosen as Refugees of the Year. Photo: Julius Töyrylä

The Finnish Refugee Aid has chosen the Refugees of the Year.

The refugee woman of the year is Sara Al Husaini from Espoo. Al Husaini's family is from Iraq. She was born in a refugee camp in Saudi Arabia.

Sara Al Husaini spent her childhood in Kuopio, in Eastern Finland. Now she wants to bring up the issues of children and women with refugee backgrounds.

The refugee man of the year is Ahmed Mesaedy. He fled from Iraq and came to Finland in 2015. He has worked in advising immigrants and has, for example, helped to develop the services of the city of Tampere.

## Football European Championship

![Lukas Hradecky dives in front of the ball.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3795,h_2135,x_0,y_47/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82185060ca099a6b265/1623853526)

Open image viewer

Finland's goalkeeper Lukas Hradecky has played very well in Finland's goal. On Monday, however, he also had bad luck. Photo: Anton Vaganov / EPA

Finland has lost to Belgium in the football European Championship. Belgium beat Finland 2–0 on Monday.

The game was exciting. The score was level at 0–0 for a long time. Finland needed at least a draw to get a place in the next round.

When about 15 minutes of playing time were left, Belgium scored. Finland had bad luck in the situation. The ball bounced off Finland's goalkeeper Lukas Hradecky into Finland's own goal.

A little later Romelu Lukaku scored Belgium's second goal. So Finland lost, but played well. Belgium was a difficult opponent, because it is one of the best football teams.

Finland's situation in the football European Championship is now bad. Finland may not get a place in the next round. The matter will be confirmed on Wednesday.
