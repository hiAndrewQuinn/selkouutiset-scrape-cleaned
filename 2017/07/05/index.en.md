# Wednesday 5.7.2017 (radio)

The social and health care reform is being postponed. The world is worried about North Korea's missile test. A Finnish court has for the first time dealt with the planning of an honour killing. Antibiotics are not always needed when a tick bites.

## The social and health care reform

![A health centre corridor and a sign on the wall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7741487)

Finland has been thinking for years about how best to organise health services. Photo: Minna Heikura / Yle

The social and health care reform is being postponed. "Sote" means social services and health services.

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä said on Wednesday that the government is correcting the social and health care plan. That is why the reform will not come into force in 2019, but only in 2020.

The government is correcting the social and health care plan because the plan was criticised in Parliament, Finland's parliament. Parliament's important Constitutional Law Committee said that the government's plan does not follow Finnish law.

The social and health care reform has been planned in Finland for years already. The aim is to reform health services so that there is enough money for good services in the whole country in the future too.

## North Korea's missile test

![A missile points to the sky from a motorised launch platform. A small group of soldiers and officials in dark suits stand around it. The sky is bright blue. Forest-covered hills can be seen in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9705722)

The world is worried about North Korea's missile test. North Korea announced the missile test on Tuesday.

Now it is known that North Korea carried out a test with a long-range missile. With a long-range missile, North Korea can strike far away.

US Secretary of State Rex Tillerson says that the threat against the USA and other countries has grown.

The UN, the United Nations, is also worried. The UN Security Council is holding a meeting about the matter.

## The court's decision

![A courtroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7444255)

Open image viewer

A courtroom, where important work is done. Photo: Roger Källman / Yle

A Finnish court has for the first time dealt with the planning of an honour killing.

A young man was suspected of planning to kill his own sister because the sister did not follow the customs of the Muslim family. A prison sentence was demanded for the man.

The Supreme Court has now decided that the man will not go to prison. The Supreme Court thinks that it cannot be proved that the man planned to kill his sister.

## Ticks and Lyme disease

![](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5613777)

Open image viewer

Tick pliers, or tick tweezers, can be bought from a pharmacy. Photo: YLE / Hanski Kinnunen

Many Finns are afraid of ticks in summer.

A tick is a small insect that can attach to the skin. It spreads diseases, for example Lyme disease. Lyme disease can cause bad symptoms, for example in the skin, the muscles and the heart.

Lyme disease is treated with antibiotics. Many people want a course of antibiotics from the doctor every time a tick bites.

But Professor Olli Vapalahti says that you should take antibiotics only when Lyme disease is suspected. Because of unnecessary courses of antibiotics, the power of antibiotics against bacteria gets weaker.

You can prevent Lyme disease by removing ticks from the skin, for example with tick pliers. These can be bought from a pharmacy.
