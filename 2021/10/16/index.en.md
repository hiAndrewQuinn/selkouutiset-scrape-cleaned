## Corona pass

![Sign: We check corona passes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-867938616a03d8b8ed4/1634338088)

A sign at the restaurant door says the restaurant checks customers' corona passes. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The corona pass has been introduced in Finland. The corona pass shows, for example, whether a person has had corona vaccinations.

A corona pass can be required in places where there are many people. For example, restaurants, concert venues, sports facilities and museums can require a corona pass from customers.

In these places there is no need to follow corona restrictions when the corona pass is in use. For example, nightclubs in Helsinki may be open all night again if they check customers' corona passes.

The corona pass is thus an alternative to corona restrictions. In addition to the Helsinki region, corona restrictions are also in force, for example, in the Turku region.

You get the corona pass, that is, the corona certificate, from the Omakanta service. The web address is kanta.fi.

## Cultural sector

![A big stage, from the stage to an empty auditorium](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_573/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5398645c52a509e3cae/1548920214)

Grants for the cultural sector are secured for next year. Photo: Rinna Härkönen / Yle

The government parties have agreed on the dispute over money for the cultural sector.

The government parties agreed on Friday that money for the cultural sector will not be reduced next year. The question concerns money for culture, art, science and youth work.

Culture Minister Antti Kurvinen said earlier this week that money for the cultural sector will decrease by tens of millions of euros. The reason is that the gambling revenue of Veikkaus is lower than before. The revenue from gambling is used, for example, for the benefit of the cultural sector.

However, some of the government parties demanded that the money for the cultural sector must be secured. That is why the government parties agreed that the state will give more money to the cultural sector. In this way the government compensates for the fact that gambling revenue decreases.

## Taikonauts

![China launched the crewed Shenzhou 11 spacecraft from the Jiuquan centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9246160)

Open image viewer

China has already sent taikonauts into space many times. This picture is from 2016. Photo: How Hwee Young / EPA

China's taikonauts have reached China's space station. There are 3 taikonauts, and one of them is a woman.

The taikonauts will be at the space station for half a year. During that time the taikonauts will also do spacewalks.

The goal of China's space programme is to send people to the Moon and to the planet Mars.

On Sunday it is a dry day in the south, and no rain falls. In the north it still rains on Sunday, but the rain weakens.

The temperature on Sunday is between 8 degrees in the south and 2 degrees in the north.

On Monday the rain almost completely ends, also in the north. The weather gets a bit colder, and in Lapland it may already be a little frost on Monday.
