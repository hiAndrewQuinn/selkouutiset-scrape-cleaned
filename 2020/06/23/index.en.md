# Tuesday 23.6.2020 (radio)

The government is removing corona restrictions. Researchers at Sitra want to get rid of peat faster than planned. The police are investigating Kulmuni's training courses. A herd of cows escaped in Kuopio at Midsummer, and 6 cows are still missing. The nights at the end of the week will be warm.

The TV Clear News has a summer break and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Changes to corona restrictions

![Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto and Interior Minister Maria Ohisalo at the government press conference in the Government Palace in Helsinki on 23 June 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11414470)

The Finnish government is removing corona restrictions.

From 13\. July, Finland will allow people to enter from those European countries where the disease is as rare as it is in Finland. At the moment the disease is rare enough in Germany, Italy, Austria, Greece, Switzerland, Slovakia, Slovenia, Hungary, Liechtenstein, Croatia, Cyprus and Ireland.

Finns no longer have to stay at home in quarantine after they return home from these countries.

## The same guidelines for all ages

![Social and health minister Aino-Kaisa Pekonen and family and basic services minister Krista Kiuru at the government press conference in the Government Palace in Helsinki on 23 June 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11414459)

Open image viewer

Ministers Aino-Kaisa Pekonen and Krista Kiuru. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The government is also removing the separate corona guidelines for people over 70. Until now, people over 70 have been advised to stay at home, because corona is more dangerous for them than for younger people. Now there is so little of the virus around that people over 70 do not need separate instructions.

The recommendation to work from home also ends at the beginning of August. Since the winter, 60% of Finns have worked from home so that the coronavirus does not spread at workplaces.

## Getting rid of peat

![Digging up peat on a peat bog.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11004327)

Open image viewer

Peat is being taken from the bog. Photo: Timo Aalto / Lehtikuva

Climate researchers propose that Finland stops burning peat sooner than planned.

The state research institute Sitra says that burning peat produces little energy but pollutes the air a lot. More carbon gets into Finland's air from burning peat than from all passenger cars together. In addition, taking peat from the ground damages bogs and pollutes waterways.

Sitra's experts propose that peat producers be involved in planning the end of peat use.

## Cows on the run

![These heifers from the Toivanen herd were caught during the very first night. Six more heifers are still on the run.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11413366)

Open image viewer

These cows were caught on Midsummer night. Photo: Mikko Toivanen

More than 50 cows ran away on Midsummer Eve in Kuopio in Eastern Finland. 6 cows are still missing.

The cows had been peacefully at pasture in the evening, but suddenly they started running at high speed. They may have been frightened by a bear or some other animal. Some cows ran several kilometres and swam across a lake.

Most of the cows were found the same night. 6 cows are still missing, and the owner is searching for them together with acquaintances, relatives and villagers.

## Warm nights are coming

![Lowest temperature weather graphic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11413246)

Open image viewer

It is warm even at night at the end of the week. Photo: Matti Huutonen / Yle

The nights are very warm this week. In Southern and Central Finland it is almost 20 degrees even at night.

The days are hot this week, and in the evening there is still a heatwave, that is, over 25 degrees.

At the weekend it starts to cool in the north, as cold air comes from the Arctic Ocean. In Lapland it will be about 15 degrees on Saturday and Sunday. In the south it will still be hot at the weekend, almost 30 degrees.
