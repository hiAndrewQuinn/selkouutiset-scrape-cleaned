## Alcohol deaths

![Empty drink cans and a wine bottle in a recycling box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10413296386ddc41609e/1669783068)

Alcohol law reform brought stronger alcoholic drinks to grocery stores. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finns' alcohol deaths and liver diseases have increased since the alcohol act was reformed in 2018.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare (THL) says that, for example, in 2020 about 1,700 people died from diseases and poisonings caused by alcohol. That is about 160 more deaths than before the law change in 2017. Especially men are dying more often from liver disease caused by alcohol.

The change to the alcohol act allowed the sale of stronger alcoholic drinks in grocery stores than before.

According to THL, it seems that alcohol use has increased especially among people who already drank a lot of alcohol.

## Pensioners at work

![Anonymous picture of an elderly person and a home carer. Silhouette against a window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6557,h_3688,x_16,y_429/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-922491621f325fcc7ca/1646775375)

Nurses and teachers in particular often carry on working after retirement. Photo: Hilma Toivonen / Yle

The number of pensioners working in the municipal sector has grown.

Especially in the care and teaching sectors it is common to carry on working after retirement, says a new study by the pension insurer Keva. In the care and teaching sectors, about 25 percent of pensioners under 68 work alongside their old-age pension.

The most common reason to continue working after retiring is to improve one's own financial situation.

## NATO ratifications

![Pekka Haavisto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_247/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1041651638745bb47d08/1669819750)

Open image viewer

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Robert Ghement / EPA

Hungary has promised to ratify, that is, approve, Finland's and Sweden's NATO memberships at the beginning of February, says Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto.

Haavisto and the Swedish foreign minister met the Hungarian foreign minister in Romania, where they were all at a NATO meeting. The Hungarian foreign minister told Finland and Sweden about Hungary's ratification schedule. He also said that Hungary has no preconditions for the ratification.

Hungary and Turkey are the only NATO countries that have not yet ratified Finland's and Sweden's memberships. Turkey has not said when it intends to confirm the memberships.

## French baguette

![Baguette wrapped in the French flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10021531)

Open image viewer

The baguette is part of French cultural heritage. Photo: Robert Ghement / AOP

The French baguette has been approved for UNESCO's list of intangible cultural heritage. More than 6 billion baguettes are made in France every year.

UNESCO's list includes about 600 different traditions from more than 130 countries. For example, the Finnish sauna tradition was added to the list in 2020.
