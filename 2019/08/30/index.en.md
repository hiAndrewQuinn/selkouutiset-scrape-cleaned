# Friday 30.8.2019 (radio)

The law on quick loans is changing. The risk of Finns dying early has decreased. A state of emergency has been declared in Florida. Ebola is spreading in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Jukka Jalonen will continue as head coach of the men's ice hockey national team.

## Quick loans

![Quick loans are often applied for by phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10745037)

Quick loans are often applied for by phone. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The law on quick loans, that is, high-interest loans, changes on Sunday.

The law changes so that the interest on a loan can be at most 20 per cent. In addition, the costs of a loan may be at most 150 euros a year.

The aim of the change in the law is to reduce people's indebtedness and the unreasonable interest on quick loans.

## Lifestyle

![A man shows his round stomach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9918793)

The risk of Finns dying early has fallen, says THL. However, the growth in overweight may slow down the good development. Photo: AOP

The risk of Finns dying prematurely is smaller than before. This is according to THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

The reason is that smoking, high blood pressure and high cholesterol have decreased.

However, the growth in overweight and obesity slows down the good development. An unhealthy diet, lack of exercise and too much alcohol use raise the risk of early death.

The hurricane may be of strength 4. Only strength 5 is an even worse hurricane.

## Ebola

![Protective equipment being put on a nurse in the city of Beni.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10829315)

Open image viewer

2,000 people have already died of the haemorrhagic fever Ebola in the eastern part of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Photo: Hugh Kinsella Cunningham / EPA

A total of 3,000 people have caught Ebola in the Democratic Republic of the Congo in a year. Authorities say that over 2,000 of those infected have died.

Vaccines and various treatments have been developed against Ebola, but the spread of the disease has not been prevented effectively enough.

The Ebola virus causes haemorrhagic fever, and on average half of those infected die. Bats often spread the virus.

Ebola is most common near rainforests in Africa.

## Jukka Jalonen

![Jukka Jalonen, 2019 champion](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10947267)

Open image viewer

Jukka Jalonen coached the Finnish ice hockey team to the world championship in spring 2019. Photo: AOP

Here is one more sports news item.

Jukka Jalonen will continue as head coach of the men's ice hockey national team. Jalonen's new contract runs until 2022. In 2022 both the Winter Olympics and the World Championships to be played in Finland will take place.

Finland has won 2 ice hockey world championships under Jukka Jalonen. The second championship came this year, and the first in 2011.

Jukka Jalonen's contract has an NHL clause. This means that Jalonen can move to the NHL in the middle of the contract period, if he receives a good job offer from North America.
