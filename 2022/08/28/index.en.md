EU foreign ministers intend to talk about visa restrictions for Russians next week.

The Financial Times newspaper reports that EU ministers will talk, for example, about restricting visa facilitation for Russians and cancelling Russians' travel rights at EU level,

For example, Estonia has demanded a strict visa policy for Russia, because the country is waging war in Ukraine.

For example, the Czech Republic and Poland have stopped granting tourist visas to Russians. Finland has decided that the number of tourist visas for Russians will be reduced.

## IAEA wants to go to Zaporizhzhia

![A soldier stands in front of the nuclear power plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5358,h_3013,x_0,y_558/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9976426303513278f57/1661162081)

Open image viewer

A Russian soldier guarded the Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant in May. Photo: Andrey Borodulin / AFP

The International Atomic Energy Agency IAEA is preparing an inspection visit to the Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant in Ukraine.

The New York Times newspaper reports that the IAEA has put together a 14-person expert group for the visit to Zaporizhzhia.

The Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant is in Ukraine, but it is in an area that Russia has seized. The safety of the nuclear power plant has raised international concern.

Russia has promised to let a group of independent inspectors into the power plant.

## Textbook sales

![Hanna Haukkapää, managing director of Jamera, which sells new and used learning materials, arranges shelves in a bookshop in central Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_783/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99609262fe0b3507c7d/1660817506)

Open image viewer

Jamera's managing director Hanna Haukkapää arranges books on a shelf in a bookshop in central Helsinki. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

Textbook sales at bookshops have collapsed.

Upper secondary students, that is, for example, vocational school students and upper secondary school students, no longer buy textbooks, because they get them from school for free. Schools must give pupils the books for free, because the compulsory education age now lasts until the age of 18. The reform came into force a year ago.

The Jamera bookshop sells only textbooks, new and used. Sales have dwindled so much that Jamera intends to close its doors completely, perhaps next spring.

Textbook sales at bigger bookshops have also almost halved compared with last year.

## Horse riding as a hobby

![Riders in the terrain in Kajaani.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9735200)

Open image viewer

Riders in the terrain in Kajaani. Photo: Kimmo Hiltunen / Yle

Horse riding's popularity is growing steadily.

About 160,000 Finns now ride as a hobby. The Finnish Equestrian Federation says that the number of hobbyists has doubled in just under 30 years.

The number of hobbyists did not fall even during the corona pandemic, because it was possible to ride also during the corona restrictions.

There are about a thousand riding stables in Finland in total.
