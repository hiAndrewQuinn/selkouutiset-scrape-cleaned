## Government survey

![Prime Minister Petteri Orpo and Finance Minister Riikka Purra at question time in Parliament in Helsinki on 21 September 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4476,h_2517,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1175445650c5c7153778/1695308947)

Many people doubt that the government will get Finland's affairs in order. Almost 50 percent of Finns believe that the government will break up before the 2027 parliamentary elections. This is what a survey says.

Only 9 percent of people believe that Petteri Orpo's government is handling Finland's affairs very well. Of the survey respondents, 20 percent estimate that the government succeeds fairly well.

A majority of people also do not trust the opposition's alternatives. Of the survey respondents, 31 percent believe that the opposition has good alternatives to the government's policy. 38 percent of people doubt the opposition's alternatives.

## Employee protests

Employee protests continue next week.

The Industrial Union is organising one-hour walk-outs on Wednesday at 23 workplaces in the Central Finland and Vaasa electoral districts. The trade union Pro is also organising protests at the same time, mainly on the west coast and in Central Finland.

In addition, employees in service sectors have protests on Wednesday and Thursday.

The purpose of the protests is to influence the decisions of the Finnish government concerning, for example, people's working conditions and social security.

## Student loans

![A person going into the Kela office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_10,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99386462f76f43175e9/1660383993)

Kela's Kamppi office in Helsinki on 13 August 2022. Illustrative image. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Students have difficulties paying back student loans.

Kela has this year paid back many loans to banks on behalf of students. The state guarantees a student's student loan. That is why Kela must pay the bank back the student's loan if the student does not have money to repay the loan.

By the end of July, Kela had paid over 40 million euros of student loans to banks. That is more than in the whole of last year together.

Many students have less money than before. Prices have risen quickly, and more interest must be paid on loans than before.

## Nagorno-Karabakh

![Map of Nagorno-Karabakh.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1330,h_742,x_0,y_290/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11772796512cf134f9f1/1695731507)

The UN says that over 100 000 people have fled Nagorno-Karabakh after Azerbaijan's attack. This means that almost all of the region's Armenians have now fled their homes.

Azerbaijan attacked the Nagorno-Karabakh area last week. Azerbaijan and Armenia have long had a dispute over the Nagorno-Karabakh area.
