## Support package for energy companies

![Alva power plant in Jyväskylä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6059945db98a11dd6d7/1696229894)

Energy companies are having a hard time, because the price of energy is rising quickly. Photo: Isto Janhunen

The government has agreed on a large support package for energy companies. The support package is 10 billion euros. The state can grant loans and guarantees from the support package to electricity companies that are in difficulties.

Many energy companies have ended up in a difficult situation, because they have to pay large collateral payments for future electricity deliveries. If an energy company does not have enough money, it cannot operate.

The support package is meant to make sure that there is enough electricity in Finland in all situations. The aim is also that the support package will smooth out the variation in electricity prices. Parliament must still approve the energy companies' support package.

## Germany's energy support package

![Uniper's Datteln-4 coal-fired power plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_481/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98703962e11ded915f7/1660739334)

Germany also made a support package because of high electricity prices. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

The German government has also approved a large support package because of the energy crisis.

Germany's support package is 65 billion euros. It supports households in Germany. For example, pensioners get 300 euros of support for energy costs. For students the support is 200 euros.

In addition, Germans who receive housing benefit will be paid support for heating costs.

## Companies and electricity prices

![Image shows new log stacks in a large factory hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-868399616d4b0d752b8/1634552674)

Open image viewer

For example, logs and other timber can be produced as night work. Photo: Jari Peltoperä / Yle

The rapid rise in electricity prices worries many companies. The rise in electricity increases production costs, that is, for example, making goods is more expensive than before. That is why some companies have switched to night work.

Electricity is usually cheap at night, because then use is low. For example, the Oulu-based house builder Mammuttihirsi has switched to night work. Many other companies are also considering whether it would be useful for them to switch to night work.

Electricity is cheap at night, but labour costs are higher at night. That is why moving production to the night shift is not a simple matter for companies.

## Artemis flight

![Space rocket on the launch pad.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4988,h_2805,x_11,y_410/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1000660630cb579ded70/1661777312)

The rocket for the Artemis mission waits for launch at the Kennedy Space Center in the USA. Photo: Christobal Herrera-Ulaskevich / EPA

The Artemis flight is still being postponed. The US space agency NASA says that the Artemis flight cannot set off in the next few days.

On Saturday the rocket's launch had to be cancelled because of a fuel leak. The first launch attempt last Monday also had to be cancelled. NASA says that the Artemis flight may be tried again, perhaps at the end of September.

The Artemis flight is preparing other space flights. The plan is for people to fly to the Moon in a few years, after a 50-year break.
