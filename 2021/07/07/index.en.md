Many countries are building a lot now. That is why the prices of building materials are rising. For example, the prices of timber and steel are rising.

The delivery times of building materials, for example concrete elements, have become longer. There is also a shortage of labour in the construction sector, because a lot is being built now.

Finnish construction companies say that the situation hinders the building of new homes. The building of new homes may decrease.

## The Greens' Maria Ohisalo

The Greens' chairperson and Minister of the Interior **Maria Ohisalo** wants to continue as the party's chairperson. Ohisalo says that she is also a candidate for chairperson for the next term.

The Greens will choose a chairperson at the party congress in September. The Greens may then re-elect Maria Ohisalo as chairperson.

Ohisalo also said on Wednesday that she is expecting a baby. The baby will be born in December. The Greens will decide later who will be Ohisalo's deputy as Minister of the Interior and who will be the deputy party chairperson.

## Swedish prime minister

![Stefan Löfven](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4888,h_2750,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82251460cb63c93129c/1624261621)

Open image viewer

Stefan Löfven. Photo: Olivier Hoslet / AFP

The Swedish parliament has re-elected **Stefan Löfven** as prime minister.

Löfven had to resign in June, because parliament decided by a vote that a new government must be formed in Sweden.

The Moderate Party did not succeed in forming a new government in Sweden. That is why on Wednesday the Swedish parliament decided that Löfven, of the Social Democrats, will also become prime minister of the new government.

## Blueberry harvest

![Blueberries.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1710,h_962,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5985715d9ef80b23ae6/1570699321)

Open image viewer

Drought easily affects blueberries. Photo: Julia Sieppi / Yle

Drought hinders the growth of blueberries. Blueberries may stay small this summer.

The early summer was good for blueberries. However, heat and drought have now continued for a long time. Blueberries are sensitive to drought.

Blueberries now need rain, but not much rain is coming. In cool growing places, where trees give shade, blueberries can grow better.
