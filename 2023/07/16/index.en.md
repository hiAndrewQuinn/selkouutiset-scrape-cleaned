# Radio Clear News Sunday 16.7.2023

Wildfire. Heat. Plane accident. Disruptive behaviour on scheduled flights. The weather.

## La Palma wildfire

![Wildfire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_625/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114335864b3e073b1b61/1707369628)

There is a big wildfire on the island of La Palma in the Canary Islands. Photo: Fernando Astasio / AOP

On the island of La Palma in Spain's Canary Islands there is a big wildfire. At least 4,000 people have left their homes to get out of the way of the wildfire. The wildfire broke out early on Saturday in the north of La Palma island.

The fire has destroyed more than 4,500 hectares of land. The authorities say that the wildfire is spreading to new areas.

It has been very hot on the Canary Islands this week. In the Puntagorna area, as much as 40.6 degrees was measured this week.

## Heat in the USA and Europe

The weather is also hot in the USA.

In Death Valley in California the temperature on Sunday may be as high as 54 degrees. The record in the whole history of measurements on Earth is 56.7 degrees. The temperature was measured in Death Valley in 1913.

In Europe, high temperatures are forecast early this week for Italy, Spain and Greece.

On the island of Sardinia it may be as warm as 48 degrees next week. The Italian authorities warned 16 cities about hot weather at the weekend. A heat warning was given, for example, to Rome, Bologna and Florence.

## Disruptive behaviour on scheduled flights

![Aircraft in a dark evening, with a Rovaniemi sign in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_740/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107340463ed41af0d8ac/1676493695)

Open image viewer

People behave worse on aeroplanes than before. Illustrative photo. Photo: Vesa-Pekka Hiltunen / Yle

People behave worse on aeroplanes than before.

Last year, on scheduled flights, there was 37 per cent more of various bad behaviour than the year before. According to statistics, about every 600th flight had some incident.

People did not follow the rules. In addition, passengers used verbal violence and were too drunk.

On Monday the weather is rainy in places. On Monday morning it rains in places near the eastern border. There will be showers in Lapland and North Ostrobothnia.

The night temperature is between 9 and 15 degrees. On Monday during the day the temperature is mostly between 20 and 26 degrees.

Later next week the weather is cooler than now. In many places it also rains.
