## Relations between Russia and the USA are bad because of Syria

![US Secretary of State Rex Tillerson and Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9563377)

Open image viewer

The foreign ministers of Russia and the USA met in Moscow.  Photo: Sergei Chirkov / EPA

Russia warns the USA against a new strike on Syria. Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov spoke about the matter when he met the US Secretary of State Rex Tillerson in Moscow.

Relations between the USA and Russia got worse when the USA launched missiles at Syria at the beginning of last week. The USA thinks that Russia is covering up Syria's guilt for the chemical attack in Syria.

Dozens of people died in the attack.

## There are still too few small flats

![Floor plan of a studio flat](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8350233)

Open image viewer

The biggest shortage is of studio flats.  Photo: Arkkitehtitoimisto Lukkaroinen Oy

There is still a shortage of small homes in Finland. There are not enough small flats, for example, in the Helsinki region.

There is also a shortage of small homes elsewhere in Finland, in cities that more people are moving to.

Studio flats, that is, flats with one room and a kitchen, are needed the most. There is also a shortage of flats with two rooms and a kitchen, that is, two-room flats. The housing shortage raises prices.

## The police uncovered more drug crimes

![A drug stash in a lorry.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8627633)

Open image viewer

In Vaasa, an amphetamine hiding place was found in a lorry in 2016.  Photo: Customs.

The police have solved more drug crimes than before. Last year almost 10 percent more serious drug crimes came to the attention of the police than in 2015.

According to the police, the suspect in serious drug cases is often a foreigner.

Last year more than 25,000 drug crimes in total came to the attention of the police. That is about 2,000 more than in 2015.

## Buying a train ticket is changing

![Pukinmäki station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8412944)

Open image viewer

After mid-June you can no longer buy a ticket on a commuter train.  Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Buying a train ticket is changing in the Helsinki region. After mid-June, you can no longer buy a train ticket on a commuter train. They want the ticket to be bought before you get on the train.

Commuter trains only run near Helsinki. If you travel on a long-distance train, for example an InterCity train from Helsinki to another city, you can still also buy the ticket on the train.

After the change, many more VR employees will be seen on commuter trains than now. They check tickets and take care of the passengers' safety.

## Easter traffic is busy

![Tyre pressure is being measured.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8784093)

Open image viewer

The authorities think that it is not yet worth changing to summer tyres on your car.  Photo: Yle

Tomorrow, Thursday, there will be a lot of traffic, because Easter begins. Many Finns travel, for example, to visit relatives.

The authorities say that it is not yet worth changing a car's winter tyres to summer tyres, because the road can be slippery.

The police monitor the speeds of cars, for example, with more than a hundred pole cameras. If the camera flashes, a fine can come to your home letterbox.
