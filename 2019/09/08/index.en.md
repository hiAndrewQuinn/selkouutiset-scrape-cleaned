# Sunday 8.9.2019 (radio)

The government wants to increase wage subsidies. The entrance exams for universities of applied sciences have changed. Researchers are looking into whether banned drugs can be used as mental medicine. There are troublemakers in national parks.

## Support for hiring an unemployed person

![Maria Rokka was fired from Posti ten years ago](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10949902)

The government wants to double the use of wage subsidies.

A wage subsidy means that the state pays part of an employee's wage. A company can get support from the state for 30 or 50 percent of an employee's wage. For employees of an organisation, the state even pays the whole wage. You get support if you hire an unemployed person.

The government wants to reduce unemployment with wage subsidies. If people get jobs, they do not need as much social benefits. In this way, tax money is saved.

The government hopes that the increase in wage subsidies will create 20,000 new jobs.

## New entrance exams

![Reading for an exam](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9595946)

Open image viewer

You can prepare for the entrance exam. Photo: Minna Rinta-Tassi / Yle

The entrance exams for universities of applied sciences have changed.

This autumn, part of the exam is shared by all applicants, for all fields. The shared part tests the applicant's ability to make decisions. It also tests language skills and communication skills.

In addition to the shared part, there are parts for each professional field. For example, maths skills are tested for those who apply to universities of applied sciences in business, tourism or social work.

The new entrance exams are taken on your own computer at the school premises.

## Banned drugs as mental medicine?

![Psilocybe cubensis mushroom from Colombia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10961117)

Open image viewer

Some mushrooms contain psychedelics. Photo: AOP

Researchers are looking into whether some banned drugs can be used as medicine.

Researchers are studying psychedelics, that is, substances that sometimes make a person see or hear things that are not there. Substances like this are found in some mushrooms, for example, and they can also be made in a laboratory. Researchers think that psychedelics could help, for example, in treating depression, alcoholism and anorexia, that is, an eating disorder.

The law bans the use of psychedelics as a narcotic.

## Bad behaviour in nature

More and more people are visiting national parks in Finland. For example, in Nuuksio in Espoo, the number of people has almost doubled in ten years.

Not everyone behaves well in national parks. Some leave rubbish in the park and make campfires in the wrong places. Some make noise or let their dogs run free in the park. Not everyone knows the rules of national parks, that is, what you may do there and what you may not do.

A raid was carried out at the weekend in Nuuksio National Park in Espoo. In the forest, for example, six campfires were found in places where they may not be lit.
