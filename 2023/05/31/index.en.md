## Strikes in assisted living

![Leena Pulkkinen eats food from the wellbeing services' meal service in her own kitchen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5486,h_3085,x_0,y_334/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11100646458ac7ba683a/1683555485)

In private care homes for the elderly in the Turku and Helsinki regions a strike starts on Thursday. Photo: Elias Harinen / Yle

A strike in the private social services sector starts tomorrow.

The strike concerns assisted living for elderly and disabled people in the Turku and Helsinki regions. The strike includes the nursing unions Super and Tehy and the white-collar union Erto. The strike lasts 3 days.

In many private day-care centres and child welfare units, the strike started today.

The strikes aim to speed up negotiations on the collective agreement for the private social services sector. .

## Children's and young people's hobbies

![Sofia Sistonen stands in the school yard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_149/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11216796475a949ccbe2/1685433475)

Open image viewer

Sofia Sistonen says that during the corona era she often walked in the forest with her family. Photo: Katja Halinen / Yle

The corona pandemic did not reduce Finnish children's and young people's hobbies.

The children's and young people's leisure time study says that children and young people now have just as many hobbies as before the pandemic. Almost everyone has some hobby, and a large part have many hobbies.

During the corona era many people had to give up a hobby, but new hobbies were also started. When hobby places were closed because of corona restrictions, many people started to move more in nature, for example.

There have been many international military exercises in Finland during the year. The number of exercises increased after last spring, when Finland decided to apply for NATO membership.

At the moment there are 4 big military exercises under way in Finland, in which international troops take part. Finland is hosting, for example, an air military exercise with 3000 soldiers and about 150 aircraft from the armed forces of 14 countries in total.

## Intoxicating medicines sold on the street

![From Katri Vala park in Helsinki's Kallio there are traces of intravenous drug use. In the picture is a Ksalol package.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3332,h_1874,x_0,y_1436/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1006458631ee0721ea0a/1662969017)

Open image viewer

Ksalol is circulating in street trade in the Turku region. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The police warn about intoxicating medicines that are spreading in the Turku region. For example, a medicine called Ksalol is circulating in street trade.

In the Turku region there have been several poisoning cases in a short time in which Ksalol was used. With alcohol the medicine can be life-threatening.

The police say that the youngest users of the medicines have been just 13 years old.
