## Level crossing accident

![Collision between a train and a bus in Kaskö on 5 October 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_98/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-862536615bf082e3188/1633415365)

In Ostrobothnia, in Kaskinen, an accident has happened at a level crossing on the railway.

8 people were injured when a track maintenance vehicle and a school bus collided. The injured are lower secondary school pupils, upper secondary school pupils and the driver, who were on the bus.

Nobody who was injured was in danger of their life.

There were 4 people in the track maintenance vehicle, but they were not injured in the collision.

The authorities say that the track maintenance vehicle hit the middle of the school bus. The bus was carried along with the work vehicle for tens of metres.

The Ostrobothnia police say at first that the school bus did not stop at the stop sign before the level crossing. The police are investigating the matter, and the investigation title is gross endangerment of traffic safety.

The collision happened in the centre of Kaskinen, a little before eight o'clock on Tuesday morning.

On the railway between Kaskinen and Seinäjoki, at the place of the accident, there is one track. A freight train runs on it regularly once a week.

## Small wages

![tilinauha](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5199876)

Open image viewer

Photo: YLE

About 170 000 Finns get such a small wage that they cannot manage on it without social security.

The poor wages in low-wage fields often come from old attitudes. They decide what kind of work, or whose work, is valuable.

The reason can also be that the employer does not have the money to pay a proper wage. The weakness of the trade union can also have an effect.

Often the only way to get a better wage is to change field.

More than 10 per cent of Finns work in a low-wage field. The average wage of Finns is a little over 3500 euros.

## Drug punishments

![Statistics graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12127779)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

A clear majority of MPs want drug use to stay punishable.

In Yle's survey, giving up punishability gets support only from the parliamentary groups of the Greens and the Left Alliance.

138 MPs answered Yle's survey. Of them, 122 oppose and 15 support the proposal, in which drug use would no longer be punished.

## International flights

![Passengers arriving in Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11270216)

Open image viewer

Passengers arriving in Finland at the airport. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Airlines are starting to restore flights between Europe and the USA. The flights were cancelled because of the coronavirus pandemic.

The USA is removing travel restrictions for citizens of EU countries who have a corona vaccination. The restrictions will be removed next month.

The airlines have received a record number of bookings in a week.

Finnish travel companies hope that tourists from the USA will replace the loss of Chinese travellers.
