The Finns Party wants to tighten immigration policy.

Finns Party chair Riikka Purra says that immigration has increased street gang crime. Purra says that tougher punishments must be given for youth crime and gang crime. Purra was a guest on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme on Saturday.

Purra also said that the Finns Party is preparing a programme that proposes tightening immigration.

## Support for nuclear power

![Olkiluoto 1, 2 and 3 reactors.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8174,h_4597,x_18,y_386/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10416436387441520de0/1670609806)

Open image viewer

Finland's newest nuclear power plant is at Olkiluoto. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

Support for nuclear power is growing in Finland. A majority of Finns hope that the next government will approve new permits for nuclear power. This is what a study by the Finnish Business and Policy Forum says.

55 percent of Finns think that the next government must approve all new nuclear power plant permit applications, if the applications meet the permit conditions.

Support for additional nuclear power construction has grown by a little over 25 percentage points in just over 3 years, which is quite a lot.

## Russian attacks on Ukraine

![A dark street in Odesa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1918,h_1079,x_0,y_199/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1038367637e35a2a078b/1669215842)

Open image viewer

Russian attacks on Ukraine have cut power and left cities in darkness. Photo: Vladimir Sindeyeve / AOP

There has been a big power cut in Ukraine. The city of Odesa in southern Ukraine was left without electricity at night, because Russia carried out drone attacks on the city.

Odesa region authorities say that the power was cut across a very wide area. In very important places, for example hospitals, the power stayed on.

Russian President Vladimir Putin said on Thursday that Russia will continue attacks on Ukraine's electricity network.

## Police investigation in the EU Parliament

![Greek politician Eva Kaili.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4687,h_2636,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10464016394a85fa7437/1670686865)

Open image viewer

Vice-President of the European Parliament Eva Kaili. Photo: Philippe Buissin / EU

Belgian police have arrested the Vice-President of the European Parliament, Eva Kaili. The police are investigating whether members of the European Parliament have been bribed.

Belgian police have carried out house searches and arrested 4 people.

The police are investigating whether the State of Qatar may have given gifts to members of the European Parliament, in order to influence the decisions of the Parliament.

## Nobel Prizes

![Ales Bjaljatski's spouse Natalia Pinchunk, the head of the Russian human rights organisation Memorial Jan Ratšinski and Ukrainian human rights lawyer Oleksandra Matviichuk present their Nobel Peace Prizes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8175,h_4598,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-104636663948764be4b7/1711460187)

The Nobel Prizes were awarded on Saturday at celebrations in Norway and Sweden.

This year's Nobel Peace Prize went to Belarusian human rights activist Ales Bjaljatski, and to the human rights organisations Memorial from Russia and the Center for Civil Liberties from Ukraine.

The winners of the Nobel Prizes were announced in October. The formal award ceremonies were held today, Saturday.
