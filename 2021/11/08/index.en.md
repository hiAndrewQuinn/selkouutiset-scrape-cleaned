# Monday 8.11.2021 (radio)

A boy with an intellectual disability was treated badly in a care home. The number of corona patients is growing. Working in a foreign language frightens many Finnish firms. A species called merihapsikka was found in Espoo. The weather.

## Treatment of people with intellectual disabilities

![Picture of a child tied to a chair with tape. In the background, a picture of the Onnikoti Omenapuu building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2793,h_1571,x_0,y_148/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-87627161852f46f0622/1636118966)

A minor, intellectually disabled child was illegally tied to a chair and bed in a care home in Hämeenlinna. Photo: MOT-toimitus &amp; Terhi Liimu / Yle

Serious shortcomings have come to light in the treatment of people with intellectual disabilities.

Yle's MOT went through more than 60 decisions and rulings of the authorities. In more than 50 cases, reprimands had been given.

The most serious shortcomings were revealed in a care home in Hämeenlinna owned by Mehiläinen. There, a minor, intellectually disabled boy had been treated badly and illegally for several years. For example, the boy was tied with tapes and cargo straps. He was also assaulted.

## Corona patients

![A nurse took a nasopharyngeal sample at Mehiläinen's drive-in testing station in Helsinki on 2 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5352,h_3011,x_0,y_560/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82808660df14baa4656/1638886394)

The number of corona patients in Finland's hospitals is growing.

The Institute for Health and Welfare says that there are now more than 250 corona patients in hospitals, that is 15 more than on Friday. 34 corona patients are in intensive care.

Since Friday 12 new corona deaths have been reported.

The number of corona infections is also growing. In the last 2 weeks about 9000 infections have been reported. That is about 1500 more infections than in the previous 2 weeks.

## Foreign-language employees

![Portrait of Juliana I Nengah at work at the LTP Logistic warehouse.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7935,h_4463,x_16,y_368/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-87620861851eb4506fe/1636114157)

At the workplace of Balinese I Nengah Juliana, Finnish and English are spoken. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

Many Finnish firms do not dare to hire foreign-language employees.

Although many firms have a shortage of employees, they do not dare to hire people who do not speak fluent Finnish.

In a survey by the temporary employment firm Barona, about 40 per cent of firms said that work can be done only in Finnish there.

According to the survey, the biggest obstacle to hiring foreign-language employees is the employer's language skills. Firms do not know English well enough.

## Underwater sea nature

![Bladder wrack is one of the key species of the Baltic Sea. The species was found, among other places, off Lauttasaari in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12178452)

Open image viewer

Bladder wrack was found, for example, off Lauttasaari in Helsinki. Photo: Joonas Hoikkala / Metsähallitus

Several rare species have been found in the sea nature of the capital region.

Off the capital region, for example, meriajokas, bladder wrack and näkinpartaisleviä were found. All of them are valuable species for the Baltic Sea.

In Nuottalahti in Espoo, merihapsikka was found as a new species. It is an endangered species.

In Helsinki and Kirkkonummi, the false blue mussel was also found in the sea. It is an alien species in the Baltic Sea. It has spread here from the Gulf of Mexico with ships.

Underwater sea nature has been mapped in the summer and early autumn in the capital region, the Archipelago Sea and Åland.
