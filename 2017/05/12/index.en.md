# Friday 12.5.2017 (radio)

Many foreign citizens got Finnish citizenship last year. There are now over 1000 electric cars in Finland. Friday was Johan Vilhelm Snellman Day. The Finnish schools abroad have received a prize. The Finnish school in Warsaw sends greetings to 100-year-old Finland.

## New Finnish citizens

![A woman holds Finnish and Russian passports in her hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7918034)

Open image viewer

Many new Finnish citizens also have Russian citizenship. Photo: Anna Mattila / Yle

Many foreign citizens got Finnish citizenship last year. Almost 9400 foreigners got Finnish citizenship. That is the largest number in the history of independent Finland.

Among the new Finnish citizens, there are most Russians and Somalis. Last year Finnish citizenship was given to over 2000 Russians and over 1000 Somalis.

Almost all the foreigners kept their former citizenship, even though they also got Finnish citizenship. They now have dual citizenship.

## There are only a few electric cars

![Martti Virtanen, owner of Finland's thousandth electric car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9606977)

Open image viewer

Martti Virtanen's new car is Finland's thousandth electric car. Photo: Jussi Koivunoro / Yle

There are now over 1000 electric cars in Finland. The thousandth electric car was sold in April.

An electric car does not need petrol, because it runs on electricity. Electric cars do not dirty and pollute the air like petrol cars.

There are fewer electric cars in Finland than in the neighbouring countries. For example, Sweden and Norway have many more electric cars.

Besides petrol cars and electric cars, there are hybrid cars. A hybrid car can use both petrol and electricity.

## Snellman Day

![Statue of J. V. Snellman](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9610658)

Open image viewer

The Snellman statue in Kuopio. Photo: Sakari Partanen / Yle

Friday was Johan Vilhelm Snellman Day. Snellman was an important man in the 1800s.

Snellman helped Finland get a railway and its own money, the markka. Snellman also helped make Finnish an official language. Before that, the Finnish authorities used only Swedish.

## A prize for the Finnish schools abroad

![A group from the Finnish school in Kuala Lumpur.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8906041)

Open image viewer

Pupils of the Finnish school in Kuala Lumpur in 2016. Photo: Finnish school in Kuala Lumpur

The Finn Abroad of the Year has been chosen. This year the prize is not given to one person, but to the Finnish schools abroad.

There are Finnish schools in many countries. They teach the Finnish language and Finnish culture to children and young people who are Finns abroad. Finns abroad are Finns who live in other countries.

The Finnish schools get the Finn Abroad of the Year prize because they have worked for the Finnish language and Finnish culture for about 60 years already.

The winner is chosen by Suomi-Seura, an organisation that takes care of matters for Finns abroad.
