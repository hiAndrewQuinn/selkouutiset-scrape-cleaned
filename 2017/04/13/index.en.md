## The world waits nervously for a North Korean nuclear test

![North Korean leader Kim Jong-un took part in the opening ceremony of a group of skyscrapers in Pyongyang. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9565434)

North Korean leader Kim Jong-un opened new skyscrapers in Pyongyang on Thursday.  Photo: Mika Mäkeläinen / Yle

The world is waiting nervously to see if North Korea does a nuclear test on Saturday. It is suspected that North Korea may do a nuclear test when it celebrates the birthday of the country's founder Kim Il-Sung.

The USA has sent warships close to North Korea. Relations between the countries are very tense.

Yle's correspondent is in North Korea. People have told him that they believe the USA intends to attack. But they are not worried, because North Korea has nuclear weapons.

## Investigations into the terror attack continue in Germany

![An explosion went off near the bus carrying the Borussia Dortmund team on Tuesday evening.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9562442)

Open image viewer

The football team's bus was damaged by the bombs.  Photo: AOP

The German police are still investigating the bomb attack in Dortmund. In the attack, three bombs exploded near the bus of the football team Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday.

One player of the team and one police officer were injured in the attack. The police do not yet know who placed the bombs.

3 letters were found at the site of the bombs, which write about the demands of Islamists. But it is not certain whether the attacker is, for example, the Isis organisation.

## Old blocks of flats have loan difficulties

![A block of flats waiting for a pipe renovation in Lahti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9563220)

Open image viewer

In Finland many blocks of flats may be left without a renovation loan, because the bank thinks the building is not valuable enough.  Photo: Yle

Many old blocks of flats may get into loan difficulties when a big renovation, that is, repair, has to be done on the building. The bank can say that it will not give the building a renovation loan, because the block of flats is not valuable enough.

In old buildings, for example, repairing or replacing the water pipes is very expensive. It is estimated that in Finland there are as many as 200,000 flats that may get loan difficulties with a renovation.

Blocks of flats like this are all over Finland. If a building was built, for example, in the 1970s, it may soon need a big renovation.

## Niinistö very popular in presidential poll

![President Niinistö spoke at a press conference on Thursday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9550027)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö is very popular.  Photo: Yle

President Sauli Niinistö is the clear favourite in a new presidential poll. The National Coalition Party's Verkkouutiset has done a survey on the presidential candidates.

In the survey Sauli Niinistö gets such strong support that he may get a second term already in the first round of voting in the presidential election.

Niinistö's support in the survey was 66 percent. Pekka Haavisto was second. His support was 19 percent.

## Selling tobacco is no longer always worth it

![A tobacco machine at a shop checkout.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9562160)

Open image viewer

You can no longer buy tobacco as easily as last year.  Photo: Kimmo Hiltunen / Yle

The places that sell tobacco in Finland are decreasing. The places are decreasing because a seller of tobacco must pay the municipality a fee of several hundred euros.

For example, many restaurants say that because of the fee, selling tobacco is no longer worth it. The fee went up when the new tobacco law came into force.

The goal of the new law is that smoking ends in Finland at the end of the next decade.
