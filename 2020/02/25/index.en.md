# Tuesday 25.2.2020 (radio)

Finns were put into quarantine in Tenerife, Spain. The coronavirus is spreading in Italy. Finns are postponing having children because of lack of money and lack of a partner. Big spring floods threaten Lapland.

## Finns were put into quarantine in Tenerife

![Costa Adeje Palace hotel in Tenerife.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11226346)

Guests of the Costa Adeje Palace hotel in Tenerife are in quarantine for 2 weeks. Photo: H10 Hotels

20 Finnish tourists have been put into quarantine in Tenerife because of the risk of coronavirus.

An Italian tourist who had the coronavirus was staying in the hotel where the Finns were. That is why all the guests of the hotel have been put into quarantine, that is, they have been isolated from other people. Quarantine is used to try to stop the spread of the coronavirus.

The quarantine lasts 2 weeks. During the quarantine the hotel guests may move freely within the hotel grounds, but they may not leave. There are about 1,000 guests in the hotel.

Tenerife is one of the Canary Islands. The islands belong to Spain.

## Coronavirus is spreading in Italy

![An empty street in the town of Codogno.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11223949)

The coronavirus has emptied the streets of the town of Codogno in Northern Italy. Photo: Miguel Medina / AFP

In Italy the coronavirus is still spreading.

The coronavirus has now spread in Italy to Tuscany and Sicily too. Two infections have been found in Tuscany and one in Sicily.

More than 200 people have already got the coronavirus infection in Italy. In addition, at least 7 people have died of the coronavirus in Italy.

You can get information about the coronavirus from the website of THL, the National Institute for Health and Welfare, also in plain Finnish.

## Many people postpone having children

![Two pushchairs outside](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5317055c1763e605a05/1545036909)

Open image viewer

For example, lack of money or lack of a partner can postpone having children. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finns have different reasons for postponing having children. The most common reasons are lack of money and lack of a partner.

In a survey by STTK, the trade union confederation of workers, people were asked whether they had ever postponed having children. They were also asked what the reason was.

20 percent of the respondents said that they have not had children because of poor finances. Just as many said that they have not had a partner. Other reasons included, for example, studying, insecure employment and the opinion of a partner.

Half of the respondents said that they have not postponed having children.

About 3,000 people answered the survey.

## Big spring floods may come to Lapland

![Snow in a forest in Rovaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11132459)

Open image viewer

There is a lot of snow in Lapland this winter. Photo: AOP

The Lapland ELY Centre warns that this spring Lapland may have bigger floods than usual

The floods may be big, because there is a lot of snow in Lapland.

For example, in the Kittilä and Ivalo areas there is already more snow than in April 2005, when there was a very big flood in the area.

The ELY Centre says that this spring's flood may still be an ordinary size, if the snow melts slowly.
