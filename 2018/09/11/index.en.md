# Tuesday 11.9.2018 (radio)

Food waste is to be reduced. Pupils eat only part of the school lunch. Kalle Könkkölä has died. Maria Lohela's Speaker portrait has been completed. Kimi Räikkönen is moving to the Sauber team.

## Food waste

![Vegetables on a chopping board.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9915042)

Open image viewer

Potatoes, vegetables and root vegetables are thrown away the most. Photo: Liisa Kallio/Yle

Finnish households throw away a lot of food. Every year more than 20 kilograms of food per person is thrown in the bin.

The Rural Women's Association calculates that about 6 percent of the food that households buy ends up in the bin.

Potatoes, vegetables and root vegetables are thrown away the most. A lot of dairy products, bread and home cooking are also thrown away.

This week is Food Waste Week. Food waste is to be reduced, that is, people are hoped to throw less food in the bin.

## School lunch

![A boy takes food at school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9423574)

Open image viewer

Many pupils do not take salad, bread or milk at school lunch. Photo: Timo Heikkala / Lehtikuva

Most Finnish children eat school lunch, but only a few eat everything that is served at lunch.

At school lunch, pupils are served a main dish, salad, bread, spread and milk or buttermilk.

A new study says that only less than 10 percent of pupils eat everything that is served at lunch. Most pupils do not take any salad, bread or milk at all.

However, school lunch is designed so that the pupil must eat all the parts of the lunch. Then the nutritional content of the lunch is what the pupil needs.

## Kalle Könkkölä

![Kalle Könkkölä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10397736)

Open image viewer

Kalle Könkkölä worked for the rights of disabled people. Photo: Markku Ojala / AOP

Kalle Könkkölä has died. He was a former MP and disability activist. He was 68 years old.

Kalle Könkkölä was an MP for the Green League in the 1980s. He was the party's first chair. He was also a member of the Helsinki City Council for almost 20 years.

Kalle Könkkölä worked for the rights of disabled people in Finland and abroad.

He had lost the ability to walk already as a child, and he moved around in an electric wheelchair. He also used a breathing machine.

## Portrait

Open image viewer

Maria Lohela and the portrait. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

A portrait has been completed of the former Speaker of Parliament, Maria Lohela.

The portrait was made by the photographer Elina Brotherus.

Parliament always commissions portraits of former Speakers. Maria Lohela, an MP for Blue Reform, served as Speaker of Parliament from May 2015 to February 2018.

## Kimi Räikkönen

![Kimi Räikkönen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10398007)

Open image viewer

Kimi Räikkönen is leaving the Ferrari team. Photo: Andrej Isakovic / AFP

Finnish Formula 1 driver Kimi Räikkönen will leave the Ferrari team after this season. Räikkönen has driven for Ferrari for a total of 8 seasons..

Räikkönen says that he has made a two-year contract with the Swiss Sauber team.

Räikkönen started his F1 career with Sauber in 2001.
