## A bad plane crash happened in Cuba

![The scene of the accident.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10213480)

Open image viewer

Cuba has two days of mourning because of a plane crash.

A passenger plane crashed yesterday near Havana, the capital of Cuba. There were a total of 110 people on the plane. 3 of them survived the crash, but they are in a critical condition.

The plane that crashed was almost 40 years old. The cause of the accident is still being investigated.

## Finns' swimming skills have improved

![Swimming school children in the water. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8176090)

Open image viewer

In summer, many children go to swimming school in natural waters. Photo: Jani Ilola / Yle

Finns' swimming skills have improved.

The Finnish Swimming Teaching and Lifesaving Federation estimates that about 70 percent of all Finns can swim.

Learning to swim is now easier, because you can swim in warm water in a swimming hall. Before swimming halls, you could only learn to swim in natural waters, and they could be very cold.

Being able to swim means that you can swim 200 metres without stopping.

## Climate researchers have a detective task

![Radiation levels are measured at the Glaciar Union research station in Antarctica.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8645184)

Open image viewer

In the picture, the amount of radiation is being measured in Antarctica. Photo: Felipe Trueba / EPA

Climate researchers have a strange thing to investigate. Researchers are wondering where CFC gases in the Earth's atmosphere are coming from.

CFC gases have been banned because they destroy the Earth's ozone layer. However, the gases are coming from somewhere, and researchers do not yet know where they come from.

The source of the emissions may be in East Asia. Researchers are now finding out whether someone is releasing the gas into the atmosphere on purpose, or whether it is getting out by accident.

The ozone layer is important for the Earth, because it protects people and animals from space radiation.
