# Friday 8.2.2019 (radio)

There are many people in Finland who need easy-to-read language. The basic income experiment did not increase employment, but it did increase well-being. The government survived the motion of no confidence on elderly care. A diamond was found in Lapland. The first Eurovision song by Darude has been published.

## Easy-to-read language

![Easy-to-read novels on a shelf in the Seinäjoki city library.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9771154)

There are many people in Finland who need easy-to-read language. The Easy Finnish Centre says that there are now about 750,000 people in Finland who need easy-to-read language.

Easy-to-read language helps people who have, for example, a memory illness, a reading difficulty or an intellectual disability. Easy-to-read language is also helpful for immigrants who are only just studying the Finnish language.

The need for easy-to-read language is growing, because, for example, the number of elderly people and immigrants is growing.

## Basic income

![Aila Jeskanen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10637817)

Open image viewer

Aila Jeskanen from Tampere took part in the basic income experiment. Photo: Marjut Suomi /yle

The basic income experiment did not increase the employment of the people taking part in the experiment. However, it did increase the well-being of the participants, the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health and Kela say.

The results are only preliminary.

The basic income experiment was carried out in 2017 and 2018. In the experiment, 2,000 unemployed people were paid a basic income of 560 euros a month. They received the same money every month, regardless of their other income or whether they were actively looking for work.

The people who took part in the experiment say that during the experiment they were doing better than before. For example, they had less stress than before.

## Motion of no confidence

The government won Parliament's confidence by 99 votes to 88.

Parliament has voted on the government's confidence. The government won Parliament's confidence by 99 votes to 88.

The vote on the government's confidence was held because the opposition had submitted a motion of no confidence on elderly care to the government.

In the opposition's view, the government has not taken care of elderly care well enough.

## Lapland's diamond

![Close-up of the Tankavaara diamond](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10637273)

Open image viewer

A diamond was found in Tankavaara. Photo: Courtesy of Mineralogical Museum, CeNak, University Hamburg

A diamond has been found in Lapland.

The diamond was found in Tankavaara in Sodankylä. Its diameter is about 0.7 millimetres.

The diamond was found by the German geologist Kasper von Wuthenau last August.

This is the first diamond found in Lapland. Other gemstones, such as rubies and sapphires, have been found in Lapland before.

The first competition song of Darude, Finland's Eurovision representative, has been released.

The song is called Release Me. Darude performs it together with Sebastian Rejman.

You can watch and listen to the music video on Yle Areena.

Darude is making 3 songs in total. The audience and an international jury will choose the best of them. The winning song will represent Finland at Eurovision in May.
