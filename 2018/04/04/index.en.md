# Wednesday 4.4.2018 (radio)

A strike may start in the construction industry. Three people died in a fire in Kotka. Companies have a big problem finding workers. Berries may help in the treatment of cancer. Snow will melt in Southern Finland in the coming days.

## A strike may start in the construction industry

![A ready-to-install Elpo concrete element at Rudus's Viipuri factory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9444799)

The construction industry makes concrete elements, for example. Photo: Kari Kosonen / Yle

A strike may start in the construction industry. Workers plan to stop work at 7 construction industry companies. The strike will affect work at Rudus, Betonimestarit and Parma, for example.

The Finnish Construction Trade Union says that the first part of the strike starts on 19 April, and it lasts 4 days. The second part of the strike may be at the start of May, and it also lasts 4 days.

There is a dispute in the construction industry about wages, for example.

## Fire killed people in Kotka

![Firefighters putting out a fire on Sommeenkatu in Kotka](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10143753)

Open image viewer

The fire destroyed a detached house. Photo: Sasu Mäkinen / Lehtikuva

There was a serious fire in Kotka.

A family of 3 died in the fire. The dead were the parents and an adult son.

The fire was in an old detached house. The fire started during the night between Tuesday and Wednesday.

The rescue service says that there was no smoke alarm in the house. That is why the residents did not wake up in time.

It is still not certain why the fire started.

## More workers are needed

Companies cannot find enough workers. For example, the home care sector needs more workers.

Companies are also looking for shop assistants, lorry drivers and builders. The lack of workers is a big problem for companies.

Sometimes an unemployed person does not want to take a job, because the wage reduces their support money. Companies say this in a Yle survey.

## Berries are very healthy

![Blueberries.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9035815)

Open image viewer

Blueberries contain healthy colourings. Photo: Krista Holappa / Yle

Then, information about nature.

Berries are healthy. Researchers at the University of Eastern Finland are finding out whether berries could even help in the treatment of cancer.

The secret of berries is in their colourings. The colourings of berries, that is anthocyanins, affect cancer cells.

Researchers are especially interested in the colourings of blueberries, blackcurrants and lingonberries.

## Snow melts from the south

![The highest temperatures in Finland for the end of the week.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10134975)

Open image viewer

The temperature is finally rising. Photo: Yle

Spring is moving on. On Thursday and Friday it is above zero in almost the whole country. Right in the south the temperature may rise to close to 10 degrees.

It will also rain in the coming days. In the south the rain is water. Warm weather and rain will melt the snow from Southern Finland almost completely. In the north more snow will fall.
