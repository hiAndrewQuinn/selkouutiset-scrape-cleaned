## Transports of Ukrainians

![White minibuses in a queue, two men in camouflage uniforms with guns in their hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_178/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97122162ab38b18e23a/1655388737)

Minibuses take people to a camp. For many Ukrainians the journey continues from the camp to prison. Photo: All Over Press

Russia has taken people from Ukraine to Russia. Perhaps over a million Ukrainians have been taken to Russia against their will.

Many Ukrainians have first been taken to a camp. At the camps the Russians decide which Ukrainians end up in prison.

Ukrainians have also been tortured in the camps. This is what Ukrainians who have fled from Russia say.

Russia says that it has not taken people out of Ukraine by force. Russia says that it has taken about a million Ukrainians to safety in Russia.

Read more about this on Yle's standard-language pages.

## The Jukola and Venla relays

![The start of the Venla relay in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3726,h_2086,x_0,y_388/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97195062adb7eee3190/1655889333)

Open image viewer

The start of the Venla relay. Many teams are taking part. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

This year's Jukola relay is held at the weekend. The Jukola relay is the world's largest relay orienteering competition.

This time there are 3000 teams from 16 countries. There are 17,000 competitors in total.

This year the competitors orienteer in the forests of Mynämäki in Western Finland.

The women's competition, that is the Venla relay, was run on Saturday during the day. The top 3 teams in the competition came from Sweden.

The men's competition, that is the Jukola relay, is run during the night between Saturday and Sunday. The teams come to the finish on Sunday morning.

## Mosquitoes

![Close-up of a mosquito on a hand, Jollas bog, 4.6.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4349,h_2446,x_0,y_170/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5704195d0202b56e319/1593936744)

Open image viewer

A mosquito sucks blood from a person's skin. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

It is mosquito season in Finland. Very small mosquitoes can now fly in the air in large numbers.

Mosquitoes disturb people's outdoor activities, because they try to suck blood from people's skin.

This year the mosquito season may be longer than usual, because the weather has been cool.

In Southern and Central Finland there are most mosquitoes right now, that is, from mid-June until Midsummer. Midsummer is celebrated in a week. In the north there are most mosquitoes from Midsummer until mid-July.

Mosquitoes move most in beautiful weather. In rain, wind and cool weather there are fewer mosquitoes.
