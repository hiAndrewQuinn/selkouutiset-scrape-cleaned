# Saturday 31.8.2019 (radio)

Finnish women in the ISIS camp. Police school. Finland's Nature Day. The end of the summer cottage season. The warm weather continues.

## Finnish women in the ISIS camp

![Two Finnish women in the al-Hol tent camp in Syria](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10947953)

Open image viewer

There are about 10 Finnish women in the ISIS camp in Syria. Photo: Antti Kuronen / Yle

A Yle journalist has met women in Syria who live in the camp of the terrorist organisation ISIS. Foreigners who were part of ISIS's Islamic State live in the camp.

There are about 10 Finnish women in the camp. The women have 30 children in total.

The women and children cannot live in the camp for very long. The women have been thinking about where they could move from the camp.

The Finnish government has not yet decided what will happen to the women and children. They may not be able to return to Finland at all.

## Police numbers are much discussed

![Police University College building from outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6814923)

Open image viewer

The Police College is in Tampere. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

In Finland there is much talk about whether there should be more police.

Many Finns think that Finland is safer if there are more police.

The Police College trains 400 police officers every year.

The Ministry of Finance wants to give the Police College more money so that 500 police officers can be trained every year.

But the principal of the Police College says that this will not work.

The Police College is cramped. No more students fit there. There are also too few teachers.

## Finland's Nature Day

![Blueberry hummock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10882799)

Open image viewer

Blueberries are one lovely thing in Finnish nature. Photo: Mostphotos

Today is Finland's Nature Day.

We hope that many people have been able to go outside today to enjoy Finnish nature.

Finland's Nature Day is a flag day. Therefore Finnish flags flew on the flagpoles during the day.

## The summer cottage season ends

![Lit lakeshore of a cottage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5871195)

Open image viewer

Outdoor lights look beautiful when the evening grows dark. Photo: Derrick Frilund

Today the end of the summer cottage season is also celebrated.

Many people light outdoor lights and lanterns on the shore. They look beautiful when the evening grows dark.

In some places there are fireworks. This festive evening is called venetsialaiset, or Venetian evening.

Tomorrow, Sunday, the weather is warm and good. In the south and centre of Finland the sun shines.

It may be even 25 degrees warm. In the north there are some clouds.

The day after tomorrow, Monday, the weather cools a little.
