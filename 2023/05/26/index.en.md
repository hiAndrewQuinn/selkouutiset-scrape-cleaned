## New reception centres

![A woman prepares a room in a reception centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-932232623b2a2782b62/1648045003)

Open image viewer

Asylum seekers can live in reception centres. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

The Finnish Immigration Service, Migri, is opening new reception centres. Reception centres are being opened in different parts of Finland.

There will be 17 new reception centres. In total they have 300 places for asylum seekers and refugees.

Migri says that the new reception centres will be opened as soon as possible. Recently many refugees have come to Finland from Ukraine, where there is a war.

Many military exercises have started in Finland. Soldiers are training in different parts of Finland.

Soldiers can be seen, for example, in the Helsinki region, in Kouvola and in Hamina. In addition, soldiers train at sea and on the coast.

Many thousands of soldiers take part in the military exercises. They also move by aeroplanes and helicopters.

## Ice hockey World Championship

![Mikko Lehtonen fights for the puck.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3459,h_1945,x_0,y_160/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1119862646fb08787815/1685041609)

Finland did not get into the medal games of the ice hockey World Championship.

Finland dropped out of the playoffs when it lost to Canada on Thursday evening. Canada beat Finland 4–1.

Finland's defeat is a big disappointment for Finns. Last year Finland was world champion, and many hoped for World Championship gold this year too.

The medal games of the World Championship are played in Tampere. On Sunday evening it will be known which country is this year's world champion.

The cause was thunder. A lightning strike caused a disruption to the metro's safety equipment.

Thunder passed over the Helsinki region on Friday.

## The weather

![Saturday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11204246470d6a57740e/1685116607)

On Saturday it is dry, that is, no rain falls. The temperature is about 15 degrees. In the west and north it is a little colder.

On Sunday it rains. Water falls in Western Finland and Northern Finland. Elsewhere it is dry.

The nights of the weekend are cold. In the garden it is good to protect flowers and other plants if you have already planted them.

Next week there will be no major changes in the weather. The temperature is still about 15 degrees in the south and about 10 degrees in the north.
