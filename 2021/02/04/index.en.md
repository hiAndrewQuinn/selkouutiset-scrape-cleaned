# Thursday 4.2.2021 (radio)

Restaurant corona restrictions will be continued. You can also go to a corona test by Kela taxi. The police have reported a big smuggling case. SDP is the largest party in the Yle poll. The weather is windy and cold.

## Restaurant corona restrictions

![The restaurant hall of La Famiglia restaurant in Flamingo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5280,h_2970,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6546165e7205f1be001/1585148250)

Restaurants must close early if the area has a poor corona situation. Photo: Eleni Paspatis / Yle

The corona restrictions on restaurants are going to be continued.

The government proposes to Parliament that the corona restrictions on restaurants be continued until the end of June. The intention had been that the corona restrictions would end at the end of February.

Because of the corona restrictions, restaurants must stop selling alcohol at 22:00 and close their doors at 23:00 if the area's corona situation is bad.

## A Kela taxi to a corona test

![Taxi sign on the roof of a car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2948,h_1658,x_0,y_152/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7460045fbe1a6a7ab9a/1606294792)

Open image viewer

Taxi rides under the Disability Services Act and the Social Welfare Act were late on Monday in Jyväskylä, and some were not driven at all. Photo: Vesa Toppari / Yle

You can also go to a corona test by a Kela taxi.

A customer can go to a corona test by taxi if their health is poor. A taxi can also be used if there is no bus or other public transport.

In a drive-in corona test you do not get out of the car. So only one trip is needed for the corona test, which costs the customer 25 euros. Kela pays the rest.

## Drug smuggling case

![Drugs were transported among flower deliveries](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1466,h_825,x_0,y_137/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-770125601be6f9ad717/1612441557)

Drugs were brought in among the flowers. Photo: Police

The police have reported a big smuggling case.

Large amounts of drugs were brought to Finland. The value of the drugs was as much as 20 million euros.

The drugs were brought from the Netherlands among flowers. The police suspect that 2 Dutch men organised the smuggling.

## Party support

![Party support survey 11.1.2021-2.2.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11771224)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

SDP is the largest party in Yle's new poll. This time Yle asked which party people intend to vote for in the municipal elections.

About 20 percent of respondents intend to vote for the SDP. The SDP's party leader is Prime Minister Sanna Marin.

In second place in the poll is the Finns Party. Its support is over 18 percent. The National Coalition gets almost as much support.

The Centre's support is about 13 percent. In the experts' opinion this is surprisingly little, because the Centre has usually done well in municipal elections.

Municipal elections are held in April.

The support figures for all parties can be found at yle.fi.
