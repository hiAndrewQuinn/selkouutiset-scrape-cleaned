## Students and the corona time

![Students in a lecture hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3348,h_1876,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-915916620cd8d1df1f0/1645009592)

Open image viewer

Students in a lecture hall. Photo: Anna Wikman / Yle

More young people than before have interrupted their studies during the corona time.

In the 2019–2020 academic year about 7 percent of students interrupted their studies. Most interruptions of studies were in vocational basic degree education. Almost 15 percent of students interrupted their studies in vocational basic degree education.

The corona time has affected especially students in upper secondary education. Many students' studies have slowed down, and working has decreased.

## Data centre

![The silhouette of Keilalahti is reflected in the mirror-calm sea surface.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5185,h_2917,x_0,y_130/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-877708618a8f59c9444/1647513349)

Open image viewer

The second data centre building will come here to Keilaniemi in Espoo. The second building will come to Kirkkonummi. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

The world's largest software company, Microsoft, is building a large data centre in Finland. Space for Microsoft's data centre will be in Espoo and Kirkkonummi.

The new data centre could bring up to 11,000 jobs to Finland.

Data centres produce a lot of heat. The energy company Fortum recycles the waste heat of Microsoft's new data centre to heat buildings in Espoo, Kauniainen and Kirkkonummi.

## Ski resorts

![People in a ski lift going up the slope at Messilä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_366/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90685261f135552af7e/1643197924)

Open image viewer

Messilä ski resort in Hollola. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

Winter has been good for ski resorts. There has been a lot of snow everywhere in Finland. Temperatures have also stayed below zero for a long time. The ski resort season continues well, perhaps far into spring.

Last winter the ski resorts sold lift tickets in Finland for almost 80 million euros. This winter too will be at least as good for the ski resorts.

The Finnish Ski Resort Association expects that ski resorts will do well in Finland in the coming years.

## Tax return

![The OmaVero website on a phone screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4784,h_2691,x_0,y_60/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7368115f9d3694f05ae/1666765133)

Open image viewer

The OmaVero service can be used on a mobile phone or a computer. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

This year's first tax returns arrived in the electronic OmaVero service on Thursday.

The Tax Administration says that about 800,000 Finns have ordered the tax return in electronic form. For them the tax return is sent only to the OmaVero service.

Other Finns get the tax return on paper at home in March and April.
