## Economic growth is slow

![Economic commissioner Pierre Moscovici presented the EU Commission's economic forecast in Brussels.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9457395)

Finland's economy is growing slowly.

On Monday the European Commission reported on the economic situation of the euro countries. The Commission predicts that the economy will grow more slowly in Finland this year than in other euro countries. Only in Italy is the economy growing more slowly than in Finland.

Because the economy is growing slowly, Finland has to take on more debt. Finland may soon have more debt than the euro countries' rules allow. That is why the Commission plans to discuss the matter with the Finnish government. The Commission's job is to make sure that EU countries follow the common agreements.

## Vomiting illness spreads in schools and workplaces

![A young woman is vomiting in a bathroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9457927)

Open image viewer

In vomiting illness, the patient vomits a lot. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

There is a lot of vomiting illness in Finland now. The vomiting illness is spreading at workplaces, day-care centres and schools. The illness spreads easily.

In vomiting illness, the patient's stomach hurts and the patient vomits a lot. The vomiting usually continues for 1 or 2 days.

Vomiting illness is treated by the patient resting and drinking a lot. Only rarely does the patient need a doctor's help.

The only way to prevent vomiting illness is to wash your hands with soap often enough. Hands must be washed at least when you come from the toilet or before you eat.

## Mexican demonstrations against Trump

![Several Donald Trump figures had been moulded, which are probably not to their target's liking.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9456335)

Mexicans are angry at US President Donald Trump. There have been demonstrations in Mexico against Trump.

Mexicans are angry because Trump plans to build a wall on the border between the USA and Mexico. Trump does not want people to come from Mexico to the USA without permission.

In the opinion of Mexicans, Trump has also spoken badly about Mexicans. Trump has said, for example, that criminals come to the USA from Mexico.

## Practice for emergencies at schools

![Schoolchildren run into a classroom during a shelter drill on 8 February 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9452189)

Open image viewer

Pupils at a school in Vantaa are practising what to do in an emergency. Photo: Katriina Laine / Yle

Not all primary schools practise emergencies regularly. An emergency can arise if, for example, an accident happens near the school.

It is good for pupils to know when and how they must go inside to safety. However, about 30 percent of primary school principals told Yle News that emergencies will not be practised at all this school year.

About 50 percent of principals said that one drill will be held during the school year. In the drill, children learn that they must go inside quickly if, for example, a lorry overturns near the school and some dangerous substance gets into the environment.
