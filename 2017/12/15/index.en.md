# Friday 15.12.2017 (radio)

The alcohol law. Blood donors of African background are needed. People in Raasepori help asylum seekers. Britain's exit from the EU moves forward. The weather gets colder at the weekend.

## Parliament approved the alcohol law

![A customer in front of a beer shelf in a grocery shop. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9979175)

Open image viewer

New alcoholic drinks are coming to grocery shops. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Parliament has decided that grocery shops can sell stronger alcoholic drinks than now.

This means that you can buy stronger beer in a grocery shop. You can also buy ready-made mixes of alcohol and soft drinks in a grocery shop. The mixes are called alcopops.

The decision is connected to the new alcohol law. It will come into force in March next year.

## People of African background are wanted as blood donors

![Janne Hietala donating blood at the Blood Service office in Jyväskylä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9967940)

Open image viewer

The Finnish Red Cross hopes that many people will now go and donate blood. Photo: Eveliina Matikainen / Yle

There is now a shortage of blood donors. The Finnish Red Cross hopes that many people will donate blood before the Christmas holidays.

People of African background who were born in Finland are especially wanted as blood donors. They have rare blood groups that Finns do not have.

More and more patients of African background are also being treated in hospitals. They also need blood and blood products.

The Red Cross also hopes for blood donors from other ethnic groups.

## People in Raasepori help asylum seekers

![Rimas, who will soon be 6, plays her own games at home while her two big sisters are at school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9978806)

Open image viewer

Rimas from Iraq lives in Raasepori. Photo: Paula Tiainen / Yle

In Raasepori in Western Uusimaa, the town's residents help asylum seekers a lot. Many asylum seekers live in residents' homes.

Some residents have also got flats together that families seeking asylum can use. The residents pay part of the rent for the flats.

There have been refugees and asylum seekers in the municipalities of Western Uusimaa for a long time.

## Britain's exit from the EU moves forward

![Big Ben and flags.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9817381)

Open image viewer

Brexit, that is, Britain's exit from the EU, is moving forward. Photo: EPA / Will Oliver

The leaders of the European Union countries have decided that the negotiations on Britain's exit can continue. The EU countries think that the negotiations have made good progress so far.

Next, the EU and Britain will negotiate, for example, about a transition period. That means a time when Britain still follows EU laws but no longer takes part in deciding things.

Britain will leave the European Union in March 2019.
