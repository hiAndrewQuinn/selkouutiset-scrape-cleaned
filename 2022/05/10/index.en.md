## Municipal sector conciliation proposal

![Municipal sector demonstrators on Mannerheimintie.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5301,h_2981,x_0,y_563/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9513696274fb09a01c1/1651833987)

Municipal sector demonstrators on Mannerheimintie in Helsinki last Friday. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

The conciliation board has given a conciliation proposal to the municipal sector.

The contract is for 3 years. In the proposal, the wages of low-paid workers rise more than others.

The board proposes a 2 per cent raise for the municipal sector this year.

In addition, a 5-year pay programme is proposed. It proposes a total raise of 5,1 per cent on top of the general pay rise level.

Answers from the parties are expected by Wednesday at 12 noon.

The municipal sector workers who have been on strike for a week are returning to work. The strike has closed schools and day-care centres in different parts of Finland, among other things.

## Defence Committee

Finland's membership of the military alliance NATO is the best solution for Finland's security, the Defence Committee of Parliament estimates.

The committee says that membership of NATO can raise the threshold of being the target of Russian aggression. The committee says that a small country cannot manage this alone.

The committee says that Finland nevertheless already has a strong national defence capability.

Katarina Murto, Labour Market Director of Akava, has been elected chair of the Trade Union for Education Professionals, OAJ. Murto was elected in the first round of voting at the meeting of the OAJ council.

Murto has previously been, among other things, head of working conditions at the Central Organisation of Finnish Trade Unions, SAK.

Murto will be OAJ's first female chair.

## Mental health

![A woman sits at home on her bed with a hood on her head.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11794101)

Open image viewer

Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Kela says that more and more people are taking sick leave for mental health reasons.

The number of sickness allowance days turned to grow again in 2021, Kela says.

Last year 3 per cent of working-age people received sickness allowance because of a mental health disorder. There were about 95 000 of them.

The number is about 11 000 more than in 2020. Since 2016 the number of recipients has grown by more than 60 per cent.
