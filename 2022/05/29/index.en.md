Ice hockey World Championship. Electric bicycles. Queen Elizabeth. Rains in Brazil. The weather.

## World Championship final

Finland plays in the ice hockey World Championship final on Sunday evening. In the final, that is, the last game, Finland plays against Canada.

If Finland wins, Finland is world champion. The final game is in Tampere.

If Finland becomes world champion, people will gather in the city centres to celebrate. That is why preparations for the championship celebrations have been made in Tampere and Helsinki.

In Helsinki's Market Square a protective fence has been built around the Havis Amanda statue, because celebrants often climb on top of the statue. The statue is already old and weak.

In Tampere they want to protect the fountain in the Keskustori square.

## Electric bicycles

![Manufacture of an electric bicycle at the Helkama factory. Finished Aino electric bicycles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4906,h_2760,x_0,y_161/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5538955ca6ff642a7ba/1554448319)

Electric bicycles are sold a lot. Photo: Markku Pitkänen / Yle

Electric bicycles are popular.

In 2019, 16 000 electric bicycles were sold in Finland. In 2021, more than 40 000 electric bicycles were already sold.

Riding an electric bicycle is lighter than riding an ordinary bicycle. That is why many people swap their car for an electric bicycle, for example on the commute to work.

Electric bicycles increase cycling and reduce driving by car. That is why they are good for health and for the environment.

## Queen Elizabeth

![A huge picture sheet of Queen Elizabeth on the side wall of a house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7577,h_4262,x_0,y_380/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9614906293331c8da96/1653814136)

Open image viewer

The Queen Elizabeth celebrations will start soon. Photo: Tolga Akmen / EPA

A celebration week begins in Britain. The British are celebrating Queen Elizabeth.

Elizabeth II has already been Queen of Britain for 70 years. She became queen in 1952. At that time Elizabeth's father, King George VI, died.

A big parade, concerts and many other events are now being held in London. The British especially expect the queen to greet them from the balcony of Buckingham Palace.

Queen Elizabeth is already 96 years old. She recently had corona, and she has had health problems.

Elizabeth nevertheless continues to carry out her duties, and the British respect the hardworking queen.

## Rains in Brazil

![Rescue workers move a landslide victim.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_615/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96147962931bf9564dc/1653808329)

Rescue workers search for landslide victims. Photo: Genival Paparazzi / EPA

In South America, Brazil has heavy rains.

Because of the rains there are floods and landslides in Brazil. About 30 people have died.

In parts of Brazil heavy rain has continued for many days. Water has flooded the streets and houses have collapsed.

Heavy rains are common in Brazil, but climate change gives more strength to the rains and floods.

## The weather in Finland

There are no big changes in the weather in Finland.

On Monday it is sunny in many places, but there will still be rain showers too. The temperature is 12-19 degrees.

On Tuesday rains will come to the south and the weather cools. Elsewhere in the country the temperature is about 20 degrees.
