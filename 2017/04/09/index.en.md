# Sunday 9.4.2017 (radio)

Municipal residents went to vote. The people of Stockholm gathered in Sergels torg square. Finnish Language Day. World Championship silver in synchronised skating. A difficult F1 race in China for the Finns.

## Municipal residents went to vote

![Advance votes are being counted.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9555822)

Open image viewer

Advance votes are being counted. Photo: Yle

Today Finns have chosen representatives for the councils of municipalities and cities.

In total more than 33,000 candidates ran for the councils, and about 9,000 of them got a seat.

The municipal elections were now held in spring instead of autumn for the first time. More than a quarter of those entitled to vote, that is, over a million people, voted in advance.

## The people of Stockholm gathered in Sergels torg square

![People gathered in Sergels torg square in Stockholm on 9 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9555930)

Open image viewer

Sergels torg square was filled with people from Stockholm on Sunday. Photo: Maja Suslin / EPA

On Sunday afternoon the people of Stockholm gathered in Sergels torg square to show togetherness as a protest against Friday's terror attack.

The square was filled with people.

According to the police, the evidence points more strongly than before to the suspected Uzbek man being guilty. He arrived in Sweden in 2014 and applied for a residence permit. He was not granted it, and he was supposed to be deported. The police did not manage to carry out the deportation before the attack. The Swedish police have also arrested another person in connection with the terror attack.

## Finnish Language Day

![A detail from Mikael Agricola's Abckiria (1543). ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6167149)

Open image viewer

A detail from Mikael Agricola's ABC book (1543). The primer was the first book printed in Finnish.  Photo: National Library of Finland

Today, Sunday, the day of Mikael Agricola, the father of written Finnish, and of the Finnish language is celebrated.

The day is celebrated on the day of Agricola's death, 9.4., because his exact birthday is not known. Agricola died in 1557.

Agricola created written Finnish. Among other things, he wrote the ABC book and translated the New Testament into Finnish.

## World Championship silver in synchronised skating

![Marigold IceUnity](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9334347)

Marigold IceUnity got the silver medal at the synchronised skating World Championships in Colorado.

Marigold was first in the free programme, but it was not enough for the championship. Russia's Team Paradise took World Championship gold, 0.12 points ahead of Marigold.

## A difficult F1 race in China for the Finns

![Kimi Räikkönen on the Shanghai track.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9555151)

Open image viewer

Kimi Räikkönen on the Shanghai track.  Photo: AOP

The race of the Finnish Formula drivers in Shanghai, China, was difficult.

Kimi Räikkönen dropped from fourth place to fifth. Valtteri Bottas dropped from third place to sixth after a spin.

The race was won by Bottas's teammate, Mercedes's Lewis Hamilton. Second was Räikkönen's teammate, Ferrari's Sebastian Vettel.
