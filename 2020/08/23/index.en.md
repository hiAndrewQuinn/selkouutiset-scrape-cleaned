# Sunday 23.8.2020 (radio)

Demonstrations in Belarus. Navalny in hospital. Marin becomes SDP chair. Corona infections in Finland. What does a corona test feel like? The weather cools down.

## A big demonstration in Belarus

![People on their way to a demonstration in Belarus on 23 August 2020. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4249,h_2390,x_0,y_425/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7109595f4252c684735/1598183369)

Open image viewer

Demonstrators gathered on Sunday in the capital of Belarus, Minsk. Photo: Yauhen Yerchak / EPA

There is a big opposition demonstration in Belarus today.

The opposition and the demonstrators demand that the country's president, Alexander Lukashenko, resigns.

President Lukashenko's government is trying in many ways to stop the demonstrations. Soldiers of the army are on the streets of the capital Minsk.

There have been big demonstrations in Belarus since Lukashenko was again declared the winner of the presidential election. The election is considered to have been dishonest.

## New information on Navalny may come tomorrow

![Alexei Navalny was brought to a hospital in Berlin on 22 August](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11506992)

The Russian opposition leader Alexei Navalny is still being treated in a hospital in Germany.

He is being examined because of suspicions of poisoning.

The hospital says that perhaps tomorrow, Monday, the doctors can say what the examinations have found.

## Sanna Marin becomes SDP chair

![Prime Minister and party chair-elect Sanna Marin at the 46th party congress of the Social Democratic Party of Finland in Tampere on Sunday 23 August 2020](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11507309)

Open image viewer

Sanna Marin is now Prime Minister and leader of the SDP. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / EPA

Prime Minister Sanna Marin is now the chair of the SDP. The SDP party congress chose Marin unanimously, because there were no other candidates.

Marin gave a speech as the party's new chair. She said that long-term work in the party has led her to become chair, but there were also coincidences that she could not have known about in advance.

Prime Minister and party leader Sanna Marin is 34 years old. A chair's term lasts 3 years.

## Many new corona infections in Finland

![Border guard wearing protective equipment was there to meet passengers arriving in Finland from Skopje in North Macedonia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11500879)

Open image viewer

People who return to Finland from abroad have had many corona infections. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

During the weekend, a total of 49 new corona infections have been found in Finland.

A lot of new infections have come in the last two weeks.

In total, more than 7,900 corona infections have now been found in Finland.

The most infections are in the capital region and in Western Finland, in the Turku area.

## Terrible, quick, tickly, bearable

![The drive-in sampling point of the health service company Mehiläinen's coronavirus test in Espoo on 15 May 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11502800)

Open image viewer

A corona test can be done while the customer sits in a car. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Yle asked Finns what it feels like to take the coronavirus test.

The nurse pushes the test swab into the nose and turns the swab.

One respondent said that it felt as if water had gone into the nose. Another said that it felt as if a bottle brush had been pushed into the nose.

The test was described with these words: terrible, painful, tickly, bearable, quick and easy.
