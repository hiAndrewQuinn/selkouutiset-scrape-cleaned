The last corona restrictions will end in Finland soon.

On Saturday, that is on 1 August, the restriction that bans the holding of big events will end. After that, events with more than 500 people may be held in Finland again.

From the start of August the government's recommendation on remote work will also end. Companies can again organise work so that employees return to the workplace.

The corona restrictions have worked well in Finland. Only a few new corona infections have come. That is why the corona restrictions can be lifted.

## Face masks

![A man uses a protective mask.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11335665)

Open image viewer

A face mask can prevent corona infections. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

In Finland they are already considering how the new spread of the coronavirus can be stopped. For example, it has been asked whether it is good for Finland to start using face masks.

A face mask covers the mouth and nose. It can stop the spread of the coronavirus, at least indoors.

Some experts have already suggested that Finns start using face masks, for example on trains and buses. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, will assess perhaps in the autumn whether it recommends face masks for Finns.

THL reminds that people can themselves affect the spread of corona. Washing hands and keeping safe distances still prevent corona effectively.

## Corona around the world

The coronavirus has started to spread again in Europe. There are many new corona infections, for example in Spain.

The number of corona infections is also growing in Germany. The reason is that corona restrictions have been eased and people are no longer careful enough.

The corona also affects the Muslim pilgrimage, which begins in Saudi Arabia. The pilgrimage takes place in Mecca in Saudi Arabia.

Usually more than 2 million pilgrims travel to Mecca from different parts of the world. This year only 10,000 pilgrims from Saudi Arabia may come to Mecca because of the corona.

## Walking barefoot

![Bare feet in a yard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4741,h_2667,x_0,y_319/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7027715f1ecbc0af9e7/1595856705)

Here people walk without shoes, that is, barefoot. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

In summer it is healthy to walk barefoot, that is, without shoes. This is what physiotherapists say.

Walking barefoot affects the soles of the feet the most. The muscles of the sole of the foot start to work more effectively.

The effect is felt in the whole body, however. When the muscles of the sole of the foot are activated, the whole body wakes up, physiotherapists say.
