# Thursday 14.6.2018 (radio)

A large group of foreigners received Finnish citizenship last year. The Accident Investigation Board has looked at how the authorities acted during the Turku stabbing. The results of the spring joint application have been published. The Muslim fasting month of Ramadan is ending. The football World Cup has started in Russia.

## A record number of new citizens

![A passport in a woman's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7925396)

Open image viewer

Many people received a Finnish passport last year. Photo: Tuomas Hirvonen / Yle

More than 12,200 foreigners received Finnish citizenship last year. The number is bigger than ever before.

Most of the new citizens are Russians. After them come Somalis, Iraqis and Estonians.

Almost all new citizens also keep the citizenship of their old home country. That means they are dual citizens.

## The actions of the authorities were investigated

![A sea of candles and flowers remembering the victims of the Turku stabbing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9790533)

Open image viewer

2 people died in the Turku stabbing. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

The Accident Investigation Board has investigated how the authorities acted during the Turku stabbing and before it.

The researchers say that the work of the authorities can be improved. In the researchers' view, the authorities have not used enough money on measures that prevent radical thinking.

The researchers suggest, for example, a new kind of advice service. In the researchers' view, people need advice if they have questions or worries relating to asylum seekers.

The Turku stabbing happened last summer. A man stabbed 10 people with a knife, 2 of whom died. The man was an asylum seeker.

## The results of the joint application were published

![Joint application results.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5266398)

Open image viewer

Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

The results of the spring joint application have been published. Applicants were aiming for upper secondary schools and vocational training.

More than 62,000 applicants got a place to study, and about 10,000 applicants were left without a place to study.

Upper secondary school has become slightly more popular. This year 58 percent of young people who completed comprehensive school applied to upper secondary school.

## Ramadan is ending

![Anonymous people.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10131365)

Open image viewer

Muslims live modestly during Ramadan. Photo: Jim Hollander / EPA

The Muslim fasting month of Ramadan is ending.

During Ramadan, healthy adult Muslims have eaten and drunk only after sunset.

After Ramadan a festival begins, called Eid al-Fitr. Eid al-Fitr is the most important festival for Muslims.

Finnish Muslims are also getting ready for the biggest festival of the year.

## The football World Cup has started

![An empty stand of a football stadium.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10253414)

Open image viewer

Russia is ready for the football World Cup. Photo: Donat Sorokin / AOP

The football World Cup has started in Russia.

The World Cup is a very big event. Many sports tourists are coming to Russia. For example, perhaps a million tourists will come to Moscow.

The football World Cup lasts for a month. A total of 64 matches will be played. Only on 15 July will it be known which country is the football world champion.

Finns are also following the matches eagerly, even though Finland is not taking part in the tournament.
