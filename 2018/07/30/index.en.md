# Monday 30.7.2018 (radio)

Kauhava ground fire. Macron to Finland. Shortage of Islamic teachers. Police crackdown. Blue-green algae decreased. The heat continues.

## A big ground fire is burning in Western Finland

![Rescue equipment at the Kauhava fire area.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10328328)

Open image viewer

A big ground fire is burning on a peat field in Kauhava. Photo: Mirva Ekman / Yle

A big ground fire is burning in Kauhava in Western Finland.

The ground fire started yesterday, Sunday, on a peat field. From the peat field the fire spread into the forest.

It is hard to get around in the terrain, so putting out the fire is slow. The firefighters have brought the fire under control.

The firefighters will continue their work for many more days, because the fire may start again.

## The French president is coming to Finland

French President Emmanuel Macron is coming to Finland for a visit.

They will be received by President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio. The French president will also meet Finland's Prime Minister Juha Sipilä.

## Schools need Islamic teachers

![In Turku's Islam Centre, Islam is taught to young boys.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5834132)

Open image viewer

Islam is taught at the Islam Centre. Photo: YLE

There is a severe shortage of Islamic teachers in Finland.

There are about 20 Islamic teachers. About 100 qualified Islamic teachers are needed in Finland.

Many teachers now teach Islam even though they do not have the right training for it.

## The police have a crackdown

![A police officer with a breathalyser.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10186923)

Open image viewer

In a breath test checkpoint, the police stop a driver, and the driver must blow into this device. Photo: Mikael Kokkola / Yle

This week the police are carrying out a crackdown on traffic and are looking for drunk drivers across the whole country.

The police hold breath tests especially in places where many summer cottagers live and drive.

The police say that in summer there are many accidents in which a car leaves the road.

The reason is often speeding or drink driving.

## The wind broke up blue-green algae mats

![Air photo from the Border Guard of the algae situation off Hanko in the Gulf of Finland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10305614)

Open image viewer

This is how much blue-green algae there was off Hanko in the Gulf of Finland. Photo: Border Guard

There is no longer as much blue-green algae at sea in the Gulf of Finland.

At the weekend there was strong wind, which broke up the blue-green algae mats. There has been a lot of blue-green algae in the Gulf of Finland this summer.

If there is blue-green algae in the water, you should not swim in it. Now you can swim at the seashore too, if no algae can be seen.

The heat has made blue-green algae bloom a lot. Blue-green algae blooms may come again later in the end of summer.

## The heat continues for now

![lightning in the sky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10242936)

There may be thunder in Western Finland and Lapland. Photo: AOP

The oppressive heat continues across the whole country. The heat continues for the rest of this week.

At the weekend the weather cools and then it is about 20 degrees warm.

There may be thunder in Western Finland and Lapland tomorrow, Tuesday.
