# Thursday 27.8.2020 (radio)

Flights from Skopje are suspended. Yle made a survey on the use of face masks. Private funding has been arranged for training corona dogs. Today is Baltic Sea Day.

## Flights from Skopje are suspended

![A Wizz Air passenger aircraft flying between Turku and Skopje in North Macedonia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11500878)

There have been many corona infections in Finland on the Skopje flights. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The Finnish Transport Agency Traficom is suspending flights from North Macedonia. Air traffic between Skopje and Turku will be suspended tomorrow for 2 weeks.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that there have been many people on the Skopje flights who can spread corona.

There has been 1 flight in a week. The decision does not apply to other air connections.

## Fewer than half of Finns use masks

![People hurrying from a train in face masks at Helsinki railway station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_0,y_39/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7098125f3d2fad7b293/1597848408)

Open image viewer

About half of Finns do not think a mask is necessary, among other reasons because Finland is sparsely populated. The price also holds back the use of masks. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

42 per cent of Finns say that they use a face mask as recommended or more.

However, 55 per cent say that they do not use a mask at all or use it less often than recommended.

Many think that a mask is not necessary, because Finland is sparsely populated. The price can also reduce the use of masks

The information comes from a Yle survey. Taloustutkimus made the survey this month.

Face masks are used most in the commuter municipalities of the capital region. There, 47 per cent of respondents use a face mask as recommended. 11 per cent use masks more than recommended.

In the capital region, 40 per cent use a mask as recommended and 8 per cent more often.

THL recommends the use of masks in a large part of Finland. Masks are recommended on public transport and on trips to corona tests.

In addition, masks are recommended during quarantine and when people come from risk areas.

## Training of corona dogs has received funding

![Scent dog Iines sniffs samples.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10165185)

Open image viewer

Dogs are being trained in Vantaa. The scent dog Iines is studying here. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

Corona dogs working at Helsinki-Vantaa airport has been secured. Private funding has been arranged for training the corona dogs.

Evidensia Eläinlääkäripalvelut Oy is paying for the teaching. 16 dogs will be trained at the airport to sniff out corona.

Volunteers have been running the dog school for a couple of weeks. Of the 16 dogs, 7 have already learned to smell the corona odour.

The sample is taken by wiping the skin of the passenger.

The dogs can start work in a couple of weeks at the earliest. Before the actual sniffing work, the dogs must be got used to the sounds of the airport.

New dogs are not yet being taken into training.

## Baltic Sea Day

![Border guard officer Markku Häggman of the Virpiniemi coast guard station checks fishing markings on fishing gear in the Bothnian Bay on 18 June 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10838829)

Open image viewer

Fishing is monitored in the Bothnian Bay. Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

Today is Baltic Sea Day.

Climate change affects the Gulf of Bothnia a lot, and especially the Bothnian Bay. Fishing and fish farming are the most important livelihoods of the Bothnian Bay.

Warming and eutrophication of the sea are a threat to fish farming.

Nature tourism and hobbies also suffer from the warming of the climate.
