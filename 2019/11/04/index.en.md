# Monday 4.11.2019 (radio)

Leaders in the gaming field have Finland's highest incomes. Tax information is public in Finland. Operations were cancelled at the Meilahti hospital in Helsinki. The air in Delhi, the capital of India, is very dirty. An autumn frost record was measured in Muonio.

## Finland's high earners

![Ilkka Paananen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9910643)

Open image viewer

Ilkka Paananen of Supercell has Finland's highest income. Photo: Yle

The Tax Administration has published last year's tax information. The tax information shows how much Finns earned money last year.

The leaders of the game company Supercell earned the most money. Supercell's chief executive Ilkka Paananen had Finland's highest income.

Paananen's income last year was about 110 million euros. Paananen paid over 40 million euros in taxes.

Now there is also a woman among the top high earners. She is Maria Severina, who comes from Russia. She earned over 36 million euros last year, when she sold a company called HyTest.

Among athletes, the highest earners were e-athletes, who play computer games. Topias, that is, Topson Taavitsainen, had an income of about 1.7 million euros. Last year he was the world champion of the Dota 2 strategy game.

## Public tax information

![Tax office](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11044286)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mesut Turan / Lehtikuva

Tax information is public in Finland. The Tax Administration makes a list of high earners and gives the list to the media.

This year, high earners were able to ask for the first time that they are not included in the list. This tax information goes to the media only if the media asks for it.

Many experts think it is important that the media can report on people's incomes. Society must follow where and how much the rich get money from.

## Meilahti operations

![Meilahti tower hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8542199)

Meilahti tower hospital. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Operations have been cancelled at the Meilahti hospital in Helsinki on Monday.

The reason was that operating theatres were closed because of nurses' sick leave.

The hospital says that patients' safety was not at risk.

## Delhi's air

![In New Delhi, traffic conditions were bad on Sunday 3 November because of smog.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11049364)

Open image viewer

The air in Delhi is full of pollution. Photo: Sajjad Hussain / AFP

Now abroad: in Delhi, the capital of India, the air is very dirty. It is dangerous for people to breathe Delhi's air. That is why, for example, schools are closed during the early part of the week.

Delhi's air pollution comes, for example, from traffic and factories. That is why car use has been restricted. However, it has not been enough. On Monday Delhi was the most polluted city in the world.

## Autumn frost record

![Ice crystals in a window and a sunset behind them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10608592)

Open image viewer

Photo: Risto Koskinen / Yle

The weather is cold now.

On the night between Sunday and Monday, an autumn frost record was measured in Lapland. In Muonio it was 26.4 degrees of frost.

It is also cold in the south. The temperature may be below freezing even during the day. Because of the wind, the air feels very cold.
