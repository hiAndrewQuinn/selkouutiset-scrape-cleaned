# Monday 12.2.2018 (radio)

Enni Rukajärvi won Olympic bronze. Alko's beer and long drink sales collapsed. The National Coalition Party's new ministers were appointed. The ringed seal population in Saimaa is growing stronger. Today is Sign Language Day.

## Enni Rukajärvi won bronze

![Enni Rukajärvi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10070690)

Enni Rukajärvi won Olympic bronze in the snowboard slopestyle competition Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Finland has won its second medal at the Winter Olympics in South Korea.

Enni Rukajärvi won bronze in the snowboard slopestyle competition, that is, she was 3rd. The gold medal went to the United States and the silver medal to Canada.

In the slopestyle competition, snowboarders do different tricks on a slope that has, for example, different jumps, rails and half-pipes.

Finland has now won 2 bronze medals at the Pyeongchang Olympics. Besides Rukajärvi, skier Krista Pärmäkoski won a bronze medal on Saturday in the skiathlon.

## Alko's beer and long drink sales collapsed

![Alko's drink sales in January.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10070975)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Alko's sales fell very much in January compared with January last year. For example, 27 percent less beer and 39 percent fewer long drinks were sold than a year ago in January.

January is traditionally Alko's quietest sales month.

This year Alko's sales have also been affected by a change in the alcohol law. Since the beginning of January, for example stronger A-class beer and long drinks can also be bought from grocery shops. Before, you could get them only from Alko.

## The number of Saimaa ringed seals is growing

![ringed seal](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10070762)

Open image viewer

Soon more than 400 ringed seals will live in Saimaa. Photo: Eero Korhola

The protection of the Saimaa ringed seal has gone better than expected.

The first goal of the protection was that 400 ringed seals live in Saimaa. The goal will perhaps be reached already this year. That is 7 years earlier than originally planned.

Metsähallitus says that about 380 ringed seals lived in Saimaa last winter. This spring perhaps as many as 90 pups, that is, baby seals, will be born.

WWF says that 400 seals is only an intermediate goal. The Saimaa ringed seal is still endangered and needs protection.

## Today is Sign Language Day

![Juhana Salonen in a half-length picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9921793)

Open image viewer

Writer Juhana Salonen's book got the award for the sign language achievement of the year.

Sign Language Day has been celebrated in Finland today, Monday.

In honour of the day, the Finnish Association of the Deaf has given the Sign Language Achievement of the Year award. The award goes to writer Juhana Salonen. He has written the book Viiton - olen olemassa (I Sign - I Exist). The book is a coming-of-age story of a deaf young person.

Sign language is used by people with hearing impairments. Sign language is spoken, for example, with hand movements and facial expressions.
