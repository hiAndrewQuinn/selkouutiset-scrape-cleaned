Corona medicines. Corona infections. Taxi industry. Mediterranean snowstorms. The weather.

## Corona medicines

![Molnupiravir is a corona medicine launched by the pharmaceutical company Merck. Molnupiravir pills.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5499,h_3078,x_0,y_229/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-878174618bf31d6715c/1642524929)

Corona medicines may help in treating corona patients in the future. Photo: Merck Sharp &amp; Dohme Corp

New corona medicines are being developed around the world. Now corona pills are being developed that are taken by mouth.

The corona pills are meant to help especially corona patients who belong to risk groups, that is, who have, for example, a serious illness. With the corona pills they can avoid hospital care.

Corona pills are already being tested around the world. In Finland they will be taken into use only after they have received a marketing authorisation in Europe.

## Corona infections

![A corona test sample taker takes a nasal sample from a person being tested at a drive-in testing point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90130861dff74e5a3f8/1642069269)

Only corona infections found in the official test are included in the corona figures. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

About 8,000 new corona infections have been confirmed in Finland on Wednesday.

Only those infections that were found in the official test are included in the figure. A home test is not enough for a corona infection to be counted in the official infection figure.

In Finland, 42 new corona deaths have also been confirmed. In total, 1,894 people have died in Finland because of corona.

## Taxi industry

![Taxi in traffic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4957,h_2778,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-881924619896ceefbcf/1637390872)

Open image viewer

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Corona times have been difficult for the taxi industry. It is estimated that up to 30 per cent of taxi entrepreneurs may have to stop working.

Because of corona, taxis have had fewer customers than before. The Taxi Federation estimates that incomes in the taxi industry have fallen more during corona times than in many other industries.

In addition, the conditions for the taxi licence are tightening. The law now requires that a taxi entrepreneur must pass an entrepreneur's exam.

If a taxi entrepreneur does not pass the entrepreneur's exam by the summer, they will lose their taxi licence. More than 4,000 taxi entrepreneurs have not yet passed the entrepreneur's exam.

## Mediterranean snowstorms

![In the picture a grey-haired man clears snow from car windows.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12287849)

Open image viewer

It snowed heavily in Istanbul. Photo: AOP

There has been a severe snowstorm in the Mediterranean area. A lot of snow has fallen in Greece and Turkey.

Because of the snowfall, Istanbul airport in Turkey was closed. It is one of Europe's big airports.

In Athens, the Greek capital, many motorists got stuck in the snow, and soldiers have been digging them out of their cars.

The snowfall has also disrupted food deliveries in Greece and Turkey.

On Thursday it is cloudy in the south and west. In the east snow falls. In the north the sun may shine at times.

On Thursday there is 10 degrees of frost in the north, and in the south the temperature is close to zero degrees.

On Friday the frost tightens in the north, where there is frost of 10 to 15 degrees. In the south there is only a few degrees of frost.

By the end of the week snowfall will come to Finland. Snow will fall most in the southern and central parts of the country.
