# Sunday 18.6.2017 (radio)

Municipalities do not want unaccompanied underage immigrants. The weather for Midsummer. Forest fires in Portugal. Elections in France.

## Municipalities do not want unaccompanied underage immigrants

The Ministry of the Interior wants municipalities to take in more underage children who have received asylum or a residence permit. Municipalities have taken only a few of the children who came to Finland alone.

The ministry suspects that municipalities do not take the children because it costs the municipality more than, for example, receiving a family. But children are treasures of the future, the ministry reminds.

At the moment, 100 children are waiting in reception centres for a new place to live.

## The weather for Midsummer

![Men grilling in the rain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8978831)

The Midsummer weather is cool. It may also rain on Sunday. Photo: Mika Kanerva / Yle

The midsummer festival, Juhannus, will be celebrated at the end of next week in cool weather.

Temperatures will be around 10–15 degrees in almost the whole country.

The weather will be coldest on Midsummer Eve, Friday. Then the temperature will stay below 15 degrees in the south too. In Lapland it will be only about 7 degrees.

On Sunday the temperature may be a little higher, but then it may rain in the southern part of the country.

## Forest fires in Portugal

![A forest fire reflected in a river.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9677513)

Open image viewer

A forest fire in Penela, in central Portugal Photo: Patricia de Melo Moreira / AFP

There are bad forest fires in Portugal.

During last night, dozens of forest fires started in different parts of Portugal. About 1,700 firefighters have been putting them out.

The worst situation is in central Portugal. Over 60 people have died there. Many of the dead were trapped in cars in the middle of the fire. In addition, almost 60 people have been taken to hospital.

The authorities say that this may even be Portugal's worst forest fire.

According to the prime minister, it is possible that the fires started from a lightning strike. The weather in Portugal has been hot and dry.

## Elections in France

![The front of the campaign office of the La République En Marche party.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9660229)

The French voted on Sunday in the second round of the parliamentary elections.

President Emmanuel Macron's new party is predicted to win. It may get a large majority in parliament.

The socialists are predicted to lose.

During the day, fewer French people went to vote than in earlier elections.
