# Sunday 25.9.2022 (radio)

Border traffic from Russia. Italian parliamentary elections. Demonstration for Iranian women. Deaf day. Weather forecast.

## Border traffic

![Russian passengers queue to enter Finland at the Vaalimaa border station on Sunday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-101250363302e650da0f/1664110458)

At the Vaalimaa border station on Sunday there was a queue of about half a kilometre into Finland. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Border traffic from Russia to Finland has continued busily. Yesterday, Saturday, over 8,500 Russians came to Finland.

Traffic at the border has also been busy today, Sunday. For example, at the Vaalimaa border station there has been a half-kilometre queue of cars into Finland. The number of Russians coming to Finland has increased, because Russia declared mobilisation on Wednesday, that is, men are being called to war.

Finland is preparing restrictions that would ban Russians from travelling to Finland on a tourist visa. The government will decide on the restrictions perhaps in the coming days.

## Italian parliamentary elections

![Giorgia Meloni at the closing event of the election campaign in Rome on 23 September 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2997,h_1685,x_0,y_314/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1012256632dde348263a/1663950573)

Open image viewer

Giorgia Meloni, leader of the Brothers of Italy party. Photo: IPA / BACKGRID / All Over Press

Parliamentary elections were held in Italy on Sunday. The right-wing coalition has been the favourite in the election.

If the right-wing coalition wins Italy's parliamentary elections, the country's prime minister may be the leader of the far-right *Brothers of Italy* party, **Giorgia Meloni**. She would then be Italy's first female prime minister.

If the right-wing coalition wins Italy's parliamentary elections, Italy will get a far-right government. The change would then be big, because Italy has not had a far-right government since the Second World War. The result of the Italian election may become clear on Monday.

## Women's rights in Iran

![Demonstration at Narinkka Square, with flowers, signs, flags and roses in memory of Mahsa Amini.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5193,h_2921,x_1,y_484/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10125266330451bc6d9d/1664108408)

Open image viewer

Demonstrators at Narinkka Square in Helsinki. Photo: Katriina Laine / Yle

A demonstration in support of women's rights in Iran was held in Helsinki on Sunday. About 200 people took part in the demonstration.

Demonstrators demand justice for a young woman who died in Iran because of police violence. The police treated the woman violently because her headscarf had been put on wrongly. The woman was 22 years old.

In Iran there have been demonstrations because of the police's actions. Dozens of people have died in the demonstrations and hundreds have been arrested. Now a demonstration in support of women's rights in Iran has been held in Finland.

## Deaf day

![Thomas Sandholm signs for the camera in the talk show Mikaela &amp; Thomas](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5616,h_3159,x_0,y_421/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-762749600165041bfe6/1610704182)

Open image viewer

Thomas Sandholm produces sign-language news and talk programmes for Yle. Photo: Valeria Borovskaya/ Yle

On Sunday International Day of Deaf People was celebrated. The Deaf Day is always the last Sunday of September. The Deaf Day has been celebrated since 1958.

Deaf people converse in sign language. About 3,000–4,000 deaf people in Finland use sign language. There are about 70 million deaf people in the whole world.

Yle makes news also in sign language every day. Sign language news can be seen on television and on Areena.
