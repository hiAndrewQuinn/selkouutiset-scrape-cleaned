## USA and Finland's NATO membership

![Chair of the US Senate Foreign Relations Committee Robert Menendez at a Senate session in Washington on 26 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5583,h_3140,x_0,y_581/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97388062b3d3fa09708/1695434559)

Chair of the US Senate Foreign Relations Committee Robert Menendez. Photo: Al Drago / EPA

In the USA there has been discussion about Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership. Finland and Sweden have applied for membership of the defence alliance NATO. The USA is the largest country in NATO.

American politicians discussed NATO in the Senate's Foreign Relations Committee on Wednesday. Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership received support.

The Americans said that Finland and Sweden have good armies. They also reminded that Finland and Sweden have taken part in NATO peacekeeping operations.

Finland's and Sweden's society and democracy also received praise in the Senate.

The Americans hoped that Finland and Sweden are able to settle their disputes with Turkey. NATO country Turkey does not want Finland and Sweden in NATO, because according to Turkey, Finland and Sweden protect Kurdish terrorists.

## The President's concern about the corona situation

![President Sauli Niinistö in front of the granite castle at Kultaranta on 13.6.2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_262/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97166762ac7cc339555/1655960937)

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

President Sauli Niinistö is worried about the corona situation in Finland. In an interview with Ilta-Sanomat, Niinistö says that there are many corona deaths.

In Finland there are almost 4,800 deaths linked to corona. The figure is from the statistics of the Institute for Health and Welfare THL. In the spring the figure began to grow quickly.

The president hopes that people are still careful with risk groups. For example, a corona infection is dangerous for old people.

Niinistö also hopes that younger people will also be given a 4th corona vaccination. Now the 4th corona vaccination is given to people over 80, to people in risk groups over 70, and to residents of elderly care homes.

President Niinistö has also had corona. After corona he also got pneumonia.

## Drowning risk at Midsummer

![Children in a boat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5403041)

Open image viewer

Photo: Sannimari Lehtilä / Yle

Midsummer increases drowning deaths. Last year 13 people drowned at Midsummer.

Often the person who drowns on the water is a man of retirement age. Often a heart illness or alcohol is involved in the case.

The Finnish Swimming Instruction and Life Saving Association reminds that a life jacket must be worn in a boat.

## Warning about heat

![On a hot day, people bathe at Hietaniemi beach in Helsinki on 15 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_344/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83227360f02960c42b5/1626598689)

The Institute for Health and Welfare, that is THL, warns about heat at Midsummer. In heat the temperature is over 25 degrees.

THL reminds that heat is unhealthy for some people. Especially old people and sick people suffer from heat.

During the Midsummer weekend the temperature is between 25 and 30 degrees across the whole country. Only on Midsummer Eve, that is Friday, is it a little cooler in the north.
