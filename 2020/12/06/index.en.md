# Sunday 6.12.2020 (radio)

Flag-raising. Wreath-laying. Pori Brigade. President Niinistö. Prime Minister Marin. Palace celebrations.

## Independence Day flag-raising

![Finnish flag flying on a flagpole](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4154,h_2337,x_0,y_214/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4482345a27ae2854b8e/1512550066)

Today, Sunday, Finland's Independence Day has been celebrated. The national flag-raising was held in the morning in Hämeenlinna.

The Independence Day flag-raising is usually in Helsinki, but because of the coronavirus situation the flag-raising was held in Hämeenlinna.

## Wreath-laying in Helsinki

![President Sauli Niinistö lays a wreath at Hietaniemi Cemetery.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11683772)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö laid a wreath at Hietaniemi Cemetery. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

President **Sauli Niinistö** and Mrs **Jenni Haukio** went to Helsinki Cathedral in the morning, where they lit candles.

After that, President Niinistö laid a wreath at the Hero's Monument at Hietaniemi Cemetery.

Finnish Air Force Hornet fighter jets made a flypast, that is, they flew low over Hietaniemi Cemetery. Hornet fighter jets also made flypasts in other Finnish cities.

The Independence Day military parade was not held this year because of the coronavirus situation. However, the Pori Brigade, that is, the army in Pori, and the City of Pori held a small celebration. At the celebration a wreath was laid at the Hero's Monument in Pori.

## Niinistö says that the coronavirus must be fought together

![President Sauli Niinistö and Jenni Haukio at the wreath-laying ceremony at Hietaniemi Cemetery.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11683770)

Open image viewer

Mrs Jenni Haukio and President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Jorge Gonzales / Yle

President **Sauli Niinistö** hopes that Finns will fight the coronavirus together.

In an Independence Day interview, Niinistö says that the coronavirus is a danger to the whole of Finland. Niinistö says that coronavirus measures must be taken in all areas.

President Niinistö says that Finns must stop the spread of the disease together.

## The Prime Minister's Independence Day greeting

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin lights a candle before the Independence Day ecumenical service at Helsinki Cathedral.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11683853)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin lit a candle at Helsinki Cathedral in the morning. Photo: Prime Minister's Office

Prime Minister **Sanna Marin** has sent an Independence Day greeting to Finns on Twitter.

Marin says that the coronavirus crisis will end, even though the coronavirus crisis now feels long. Marin says that we Finns will get through the coronavirus crisis, because we take other people into account.

## Palace celebrations in a new way

![The hosts of the palace celebrations, Susani Mahadura, Jussi-Pekka Rantanen, Ella Kanninen and Daniel Olin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11683398)

Open image viewer

Susani Mahadura, Jussi-Pekka Rantanen, Ella Kanninen and Daniel Olin hosted the celebration programme. Photo: Jyrki Valkama / Yle

The palace celebrations have been held in an unusual way because of the coronavirus situation. There were no big celebrations at the Presidential Palace.

However, there were performers at the Presidential Palace, and Yle broadcast the celebration programme from there. President **Sauli Niinistö** was the host of the evening.

Yle's celebration broadcast showed programmes from the Presidential Palace and from elsewhere in Finland.

You can watch the palace celebrations and the day's events on Yle Areena.
