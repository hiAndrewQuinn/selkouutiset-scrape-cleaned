## Finland's forests and climate change

![Forest landscape in Juua](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10209914)

Open image viewer

Forest is a carbon dioxide sink. Forest absorbs carbon dioxide emissions. Photo: Heikki Haapalainen / Yle

Forests are very important to Finland, because there is a lot of forest in Finland.

Forests are also important in fighting climate change. Forests are carbon dioxide sinks, because they absorb carbon dioxide emissions.

This is a conflict for Finland.

The Finnish government wants bio-fuels to be favoured in Finland. Bio-fuels are made from wood.

So the Finnish government has a different line than what climate experts consider good.

## Berlin restricts driving diesel cars

![The picture shows a traffic sign about the ban on diesel cars.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10447567)

Open image viewer

This traffic sign bans driving diesel cars in Germany. Photo: Felipe Trueba / EPA

Six cities in Germany have already decided that driving diesel cars will be restricted in them.

Starting at the turn of the year, a ban will come into force in Berlin that applies to old diesel cars. They may not be driven in central Berlin.

Old diesel cars produce too many nitrogen oxide emissions.

## An arms depot was destroyed in Ukraine

![Ukrainian soldiers guarding](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10448429)

Open image viewer

There were explosions and a fire at the arms depot. Photo: EPA-EFE

A large arms depot has been destroyed in Ukraine. Many explosions happened at the arms depot, and a fire broke out there.

The arms depot held many different kinds of ammunition.

Thousands of people were moved to safety from the nearby area. The Ukrainian Ministry of Defence suspects that someone caused the destruction on purpose.

## Playing in nature is good for children

![Children in the middle of planter boxes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10446762)

Open image viewer

Playing in nature is a good thing for children. Photo: Sini Ojanperä / Yle

Children stay healthy when they get to play in nature. Finnish researchers say this.

There are microorganisms in soil and forest floor that improve children's resistance to disease. One gram of forest soil contains about 4 billion microorganisms.

Researchers are still finding out whether contact with nature and microorganisms could prevent, for example, diabetes or rheumatism. Children are a good research subject, because their body's defence system is still developing

Researchers are of the opinion that day-care yards must have natural ground and puddles. And children must be allowed to play in them.
