# Friday 8.1.2021 (radio)

Accusations against President Donald Trump are growing. Corona tests have been added at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. In a Yle poll the Finns Party is Finland's most popular party. Spain now has a cold winter.

## Accusations against Trump are growing

![Donald Trump at the beginning of January 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11728171)

The outgoing President of the USA, Donald Trump. Photo: Mandel Ngan / AFP

Accusations against President Donald Trump are growing in the USA.

For example, the minority leader of the Senate, Democrat Chuck Schumer, has demanded that Trump be removed from the office of President or be charged.

The Speaker of the House of Representatives, Nancy Pelosi, agrees too. Pelosi and Schumer have appealed to Vice President Mike Pence on the matter.

The attack on the Capitol by Trump's supporters has been condemned in the USA across party lines. Trump himself called on supporters to go to the streets. At least 5 people have died in the riot.

President Trump has spoken in a conciliatory way after the riot.

Trump said that the protesters had stained the core of American democracy.

## Corona tests at the airport and a new face mask recommendation

![A nurse in protective clothing takes a corona test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11679424)

Open image viewer

A nurse took a corona test at the drive-in testing point at the Messukeskus in Helsinki in December. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

Corona tests have been added at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. All passengers who arrive in Finland are directed to a corona test. The corona test is free for passengers.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, has recommended that all passengers be tested. The reason is the new variant of the corona, which is spreading around the world.

THL does not want the variant to get into Finland with air passengers. Researchers estimate that because of the variant, corona spreads more easily between people.

THL has issued a new face mask recommendation. Wearing a face mask is recommended for everyone over 12 years old across Finland.

## The Finns Party leads in the Yle poll

![party support 2.12.2020 - 5.1.2021](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11726715)

Open image viewer

Photo: Leena Luotio / Yle

In Yle's new poll the Finns Party is Finland's most popular party. The support for the Finns Party is now about 22 percent.

The Finns Party is an opposition party, which means it is not in the government.

The party of Prime Minister Sanna Marin, the SDP, is the second largest party in the poll. A little over 21 percent of the poll's respondents support the SDP.

The Finns Party and the SDP get clearly more support in the poll than other parties.

The support for the opposition party, the National Coalition, is about 16 percent. The government parties, the Centre and the Greens, get over 11 percent support.

The support figures for all parties can be found on Yle's website. The address is yle.fi.

## Spain's winter is now cold

![A mother watched her two children playing in the snow in Madrid, Spain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4720,h_2655,x_0,y_494/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7600685ff778bd68153/1610053974)

A Spanish mother watched her children playing in the snow in the capital, Madrid. Storm Filomena has caused snowfall and hard frost all over Spain. Photo: Ballesteros / EPA

Spain now has a cold winter. On Thursday a new cold record was measured in Spain. The frost was 35.8 degrees.

The cold record was measured in northern Spain, but the weather is wintry in other parts of Spain too.

It is also frosty in Finland at the weekend.

The coldest areas are in the east and the north, where the frost is as much as 20 degrees. In the south the frost is under 10 degrees.
