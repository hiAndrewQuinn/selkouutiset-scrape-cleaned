## Trade union protests

![Press conference of SAK union leaders at the SAK premises in Hakaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3988,h_2243,x_4,y_215/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1175431650c55287191b/1695395413)

Trade unions talked about their plans on Thursday. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Trade unions are planning protests against the government. Large trade unions of the central organisation SAK are taking part in the protests.

The trade union protests begin next Tuesday. They will be held 3 days a week for 3 weeks.

Workers' protests can be different at different workplaces. Workers can, for example, march out of the workplace. Workers tell about the protests in advance.

Trade unions are organising protests because they are angry at the government. They do not like that the government intends to weaken workers' position. The government wants to restrict, for example, workers' opportunities to go on strike.

In addition, the government intends to weaken social security. For example, housing allowance will decrease.

According to the trade unions, the government has not listened to workers' opinions. Prime Minister Petteri Orpo hopes that the trade unions will show restraint.

## Victoria's visit

![Crown Princess Victoria greets children who are waving small Swedish flags.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2873,x_0,y_106/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1175284650c2f6bcb1d9/1695312297)

Crown Princess Victoria is popular in Finland. Photo: Lehtikuva

Sweden's Crown Princess Victoria and Prince Daniel have visited Finland.

The royals spent the morning on Thursday in Helsinki. They visited Suomenlinna, which tells about the shared history of Finland and Sweden.

In addition, the royals visited the Swedish School of Economics in Helsinki. They ate lunch together with students.

Crown Princess Victoria and Prince Daniel also visited Loviisa. There the crown princess inaugurated a square that was named Victoria Square.

From Loviisa the royals travelled on to Vantaa. They visited Fazerila in Vantaa.

Crown Princess Victoria is Sweden's future ruler, and Prince Daniel is the crown princess's spouse.

## School health survey

![A schoolchild doing maths homework.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1084501640f336c9685e/1678718563)

Open image viewer

Finnish schoolchildren are satisfied with life. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Most children and young people are doing well. They are satisfied with life and with school.

The most satisfied are pupils in grades 4 and 5 of basic education. The information is from a new school health survey.

However, young people's experiences of their own health are worrying. About 25 percent of young people feel that their state of health is average or poor.

Girls experienced their own health as worse than boys did. Girls also had more experiences of bullying.
