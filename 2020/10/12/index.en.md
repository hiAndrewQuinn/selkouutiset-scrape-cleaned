# Monday 12.10.2020 (radio)

A miscalculation raised electricity transmission prices. Fur farms are having a hard time. Many people have stopped smoking because of the coronavirus. The economics prize was awarded for developing auctions. Weather forecast.

## Electricity transmission prices are high

![Power line](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5449306)

Open image viewer

A lot of electricity runs through the power line. Photo: Yle

Yle has investigated why electricity transmission prices have risen a lot in Finland.

Transmission prices have risen a lot, because the Energy Authority may have miscalculated how much transmission prices needed to be raised. That is why transmission companies were allowed to raise their prices by as much as 40 percent. Yle calculated that the correct increase needed was 4 percent.

Transmission prices were allowed to be raised so that the transmission companies could get money to repair storm damage. However, only a little money has been spent on storm damage.

## Fur farms are having a hard time

![Arctic fox pelts hanging on a rack waiting for an auction.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5499210)

Open image viewer

Arctic fox pelts are waiting for the fur auction.  Photo: Martti Juntunen / Yle

The coronavirus situation is making it harder for Finnish fur farms to operate. Many furs and skins are sold to China. Because of the coronavirus situation, the fur trade has slowed down.

Fur farms have had to reduce their staff and fur animals. Fur farms have been doing badly for many years already.

## The coronavirus situation has reduced smoking

![Cigarette burning between fingers](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10110886)

Open image viewer

Smoking and other nicotine products are bad for human health. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Many Finns have stopped smoking because of the coronavirus situation.

The health organisation ASH Finland asked Finns whether the coronavirus situation has affected their smoking. Because of the coronavirus situation, perhaps more than 10,000 Finns have stopped smoking.

A coronavirus infection can be dangerous for a smoker, because smoking weakens the lungs.

## Auction development was awarded

![The economics Nobel prize being handed out.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11590859)

Open image viewer

The economics prize is being awarded here. The prize is worth the same as the Nobel Prizes, i.e. about 950,000 euros.  Photo: Anders Wiklund / EPA

This year the Swedish central bank's economics prize goes to the Americans **Paul Milgrom** and **Robert Wilson**.

They have developed new ways of running large auctions. Examples are auctions in which states sell radio frequencies or some other state property.

The Swedish central bank's economics prize is also called the economics Nobel Prize. It goes to Paul Milgrom and Robert Wilson.
