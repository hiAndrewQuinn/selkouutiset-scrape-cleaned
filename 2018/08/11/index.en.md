## Big compensation for a man who got cancer

![Bottles of Roundup weed killer](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10348212)

Open image viewer

Roundup is a weed killer. It contains glyphosate. Photo: Stephanie Lecocq / EPA-EFE

In the United States, a court has decided that a man with cancer gets big compensation.

The court thinks that the man got cancer because he had used a weed killer called Roundup.

Roundup is made by the company Monsanto. The company must pay the man more than 250 million euros in compensation.

Roundup is a pesticide that contains glyphosate. Glyphosate quite probably causes cancer, says the International Agency for Research on Cancer.

In Europe Roundup may be used. A Finnish expert says that it should be used only a little.

## A lot of people have died in the earthquake

![Rescuers in the ruins.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10344247)

Open image viewer

Rescue workers search collapsed houses for people. Photo: AOP

The situation in Indonesia is still bad after the earthquake.

There was a bad earthquake on the island of Lombok a week ago. Since then more than 380 dead people have been found.

More dead will certainly still be found.

On the island many people are without clean water, food and medicines.

## An unusual plane accident

![An aerial photo of the main terminal of Seattle's international airport Sea-Tac](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10348268)

Open image viewer

The man who stole the plane worked at the airport. Photo: Peter Carey / AOP

An unusual plane accident has happened in the United States.

A man took off without permission in an empty passenger plane, circled around in it for a while and finally crashed into the ground. The man died.

The local sheriff said that the man was suicidal.

## See how the budget affects you

![An older person's hand holds a bank card.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10348122)

Open image viewer

The state budget affects everyone's everyday life. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Yle's website has a budget machine.

The Ministry of Finance has made a proposal on how the Finnish state should spend money next year. The budget machine tells what the Finnish state budget may mean.

You can find the budget machine on the website yle.fi.

You can first choose which group you belong to. Then the budget machine tells how the state budget may show in your income and costs.
