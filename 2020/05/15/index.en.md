## Older people can decide for themselves

![Portrait of Sanna Marin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_464/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6765865ebe3ca81d508/1589525704)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin Photo: Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Sanna Marin said today what the government thinks about the coronavirus epidemic.

The government says that, in its view, the coronavirus must not spread in Finland at all.

This is because it is not yet known whether a person will become immune to the coronavirus if they have had it.

The Prime Minister also said that people over 70 years old can decide for themselves how they move outside and whether they meet other people.

However, the government hopes that they avoid unnecessary meetings.

## Over 5,000 people have recovered from corona

![A woman puts a face mask on her face.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11327318)

Open image viewer

A face mask prevents droplets from flying onto other people. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

In Finland there are now officially over 6,200 corona infections.

Over 5,000 people have already recovered from the coronavirus.

Most of the new infections are found in the capital region and in Southern Finland.

Elsewhere in the country very few infections are found.

## Hospitals are starting to operate again

![Surgeons operate on a patient in an operating theatre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_192/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6290135e15af7b7ba74/1578481534)

Open image viewer

Hospitals are starting to carry out non-urgent operations on patients again. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

In the capital region, hospitals are starting to carry out non-urgent operations again.

When the corona epidemic began, hospitals decided that they would reduce operations.

Now it seems that the corona epidemic situation is good. That means that doctors and nurses can be moved back to normal work.

Hospitals estimate that because of the corona, many thousands of patients have not been operated on. Therefore it may take a long time before a patient gets an operation.

## People can travel in the Baltic countries

![Railway bridge in Riga.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10693483)

Open image viewer

The capital of Latvia is Riga. The Daugava river flows through the city. Photo: Pixabay

The Baltic countries Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania have opened their borders.

Citizens of these 3 countries may travel freely in these countries.

The Baltic countries think that when people move around, they spend money, and that is good for the countries' economies.

Travellers who come to the Baltics from other countries must be in quarantine for 2 weeks.

## Tomorrow is the Eurovision show

![Aksel Kankaanranta](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11246956)

Open image viewer

Aksel Kankaanranta Photo: Miikka Varila / Yle

Tomorrow, Saturday, the Eurovision Song Contest was supposed to be held.

However, the Eurovision Song Contest had to be cancelled because of the corona pandemic.

The Eurovision countries will nevertheless put on a big TV programme, which will be shown at the same time across the whole of Europe.

Finland's representative Aksel Kankaanranta will also sing in the programme.

Yle will show the programme tomorrow at 22:00 on television and on Yle Areena.

## The weather stays cool

![Weather picture: snow and sleet in Tuusula](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11354336)

Open image viewer

On Friday morning there was sleet and slush on the ground, even in Southern Finland. Photo: Osmo Vähätiitto

The weather is cool, even though it is spring now.

Tomorrow, Saturday, it will be cloudy. Almost everywhere in the country it will rain.

In Lapland it will be dry and the sun will show.

During the day the temperature is between 5 and 10 degrees.
