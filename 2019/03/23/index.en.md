# Saturday 23.3.2019 (radio)

There have been protests against Brexit in London. Almost 700 people died in the storm in Africa about a week ago. The terrorist organisation ISIS has lost the war in Syria. A large water damage happened in Kallio, Helsinki. The Jussi awards for the film industry have been given out.

## London demonstration

![Put it to the people march in London.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10704202)

Open image viewer

Protesters do not want Britain to leave the EU. Photo: Neil Hall / EPA

There was a large demonstration in London. The subject of the demonstration was Brexit, that is, Britain's EU exit.

Protesters do not want Britain to leave the EU. They demand that Britain holds a new referendum on Brexit.

In Britain there was an EU referendum in 2016. Then more than half of Britons were of the opinion that Britain must leave the European Union.

The intention was that Britain would leave the EU in March 2019, but Britain has been given more time by the EU to arrange the exit. British politicians have not been able to decide how Brexit should be handled.

## Death toll in Africa

![Mozambicans look for clean drinking water in Sofala province, central Mozambique.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10699234)

Open image viewer

It is hard to find clean drinking water in the storm area. Photo: Josh Estey / EPA / CARE

New information says that almost 700 people died in the storm in Africa.

The storm hit Africa about a week ago. The storm killed more than 400 people in Mozambique and more than 250 people in Zimbabwe.

The number of dead may be higher, because many people went missing in the storm.

Help is needed quickly in the storm area. People may become ill, because they do not even have clean drinking water.

## ISIS's defeat

![Map of Syria](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10650819)

Open image viewer

Now ISIS is also gone from the town of Baghuz. Photo: Yle News Graphics

The terrorist organisation ISIS has lost the war in Syria. The Kurdish forces say that they beat ISIS in the town of Baghuz.

Baghuz was the last place where the terrorist organisation ISIS had power. Now ISIS no longer controls anywhere.

ISIS terrorists took power in a large area in Syria and Iraq 5 years ago. There has been a war in the area in which many people have died.

## Water damage in Helsinki

![Helsinginkatu flooded on Saturday 23 March](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10704052)

Open image viewer

Helsinginkatu in Kallio was covered in water. Photo: Anne Salomäki / Lehtikuva

A large water damage has happened in Helsinki. A large water pipe broke on Saturday in Kallio, Helsinki.

A lot of water came out of the pipe. The water even reached the cellars of apartment blocks. So much water also came onto the street that it disturbed traffic.

## Film industry awards

![Aleksi Salmenperä, director of Tyhjiö, was awarded the Jussi for Best Direction at the Jussi gala in Helsinki on 22 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10703840)

Open image viewer

Aleksi Salmenperä, director of the film Tyhjiö. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

The Jussi awards for the film industry have been given out. The Jussis were handed out on Friday evening in Helsinki.

In the jury's opinion, last year's best Finnish film was Tyhjiö. It is about art, work and relationships. The film's director, Aleksi Salmenperä, also received the award for best director.

The audience also voted for its favourite film. The audience liked the film Veljeni vartija most. It tells about the life of the rapper Cheek.
