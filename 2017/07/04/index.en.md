# Tuesday 4.7.2017 (radio)

North Korea carried out a missile test. Putin is visiting the Savonlinna Opera Festival. There was a demonstration at the airport against forced returns. Biowaste does not belong with mixed waste.

## North Korea tested a missile

![South Koreans watched a news broadcast about North Korea's missile test in Seoul on 4 July. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9704033)

North Korean television reported on the missile test. Photo: Jeon Heon-Kyun / EPA

North Korea has once again carried out a missile test. North Korea says that it tested a long-range missile and that the test went well.

North Korea says that it can now carry out a missile attack anywhere in the world.

Japan says that the North Korean missile flew for about 40 minutes and then fell into the Sea of Japan.

US military experts do not believe that the North Korean missile was a long-range missile. They say that it was perhaps a medium-range missile.

Experts say that North Korea carried out the missile test right now because today, Tuesday, is the national day of the United States.

## Putin is coming to Savonlinna

![Vladimir Putin ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9704584)

Russian President Vladimir Putin. Photo: Michael Klimentjev / EPA

Russian President Vladimir Putin will visit Finland at the end of July. Putin will visit the Savonlinna Opera Festival on 27 July.

The Russian Bolshoi Theatre will be performing in Savonlinna then. The theatre will perform Pyotr Tchaikovsky's opera Iolanta.

According to Yle's information, Putin is in this way taking part in the celebration of Finland's 100 years of independence.

Putin last visited Finland in July last year.

## Forced returns were opposed at the airport

![The "Illegal deportations must be stopped" demonstration in Kansalaistori square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9548941)

The demonstrators think that asylum seekers should not be returned, for example, to Iraq or Afghanistan.  Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

There has been a demonstration at Helsinki-Vantaa Airport against the forced returns of asylum seekers.

The demonstrators say that asylum seekers must not be returned by force, for example, to Afghanistan or Iraq.

The demonstration was held during the night between Monday and Tuesday. The demonstrators say that a flight to Afghanistan left Finland during the night.

Police arrested two demonstrators because they did not obey police orders.

## Household waste should be sorted well

![Biowaste mixed in with mixed waste.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9701974)

Open image viewer

Biowaste must not be put in with mixed waste. Photo: Siiri Niiranen

Finns still have things to learn about sorting waste. Many people put a lot of waste in the mixed waste that does not belong there.

Household rubbish bags have been studied at waste plants in Jyväskylä. The study found that about 30 percent of the contents of mixed waste bags is biowaste.

Biowaste is the raw material for compost soil and biofuel.

If biowaste is in a mixed waste bag, it ends up being burned. But damp biowaste has a poor heating value.

All household waste can be recycled or used for energy production if the waste is sorted well.
