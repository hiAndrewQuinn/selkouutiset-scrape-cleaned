# Thursday 18.6.2020 (radio)

Hardship for families with children. Corona restrictions. Electric bicycles. Bird breeding peace. Midsummer weather.

## Many families with children have had a hard time

![Food aid in boxes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11396043)

Open image viewer

Food distribution by aid organisations has been important for many people this spring. Photo: Kristiina Lehto / Yle

Aid organisations have been important during the corona epidemic.

For example, many families with children have suffered from a lack of money.

The Central Union for Child Welfare says that about 55,000 new families with children have been in a bad situation during corona.

The situation will remain difficult until the end of the year. Many have to turn to aid organisations and join bread queues.

## Corona restrictions are being eased

![Customers on a restaurant terrace at lunchtime in Helsinki on 2 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11407952)

Open image viewer

Restaurant corona restrictions will be eased after Midsummer. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Finnish government decided yesterday to ease some corona restrictions.

Next week restaurants can serve food from a buffet. Restaurants can also stay open longer, and they can sell alcohol until late at night.

Restaurants can also let in more customers.

Gathering restrictions will be lifted from the beginning of August. Then events with more than 500 people may be held.

The government still recommends limiting visits to care homes, if they are necessary.

Visits must be arranged safely. The head of the care home decides whether visits can be arranged safely.

## You can ride too fast on an electric bike

![A cyclist has stopped on an electric bike, waiting to cross the street. The bike and the rider's legs are visible in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10726874)

Open image viewer

New electric bikes can go so fast that it is forbidden by law. Photo: Markku Pitkänen / Yle

Many people now move easily and quickly by electric bike.

New electric bikes can be ridden at high speeds. By law, an electric bike may be ridden at a maximum speed of 25 kilometres per hour.

If an electric bike can go faster than this, it must not be ridden on the carriageway or the cycle path.

The police can give a fine to an electric bicycle rider who rides too fast.

The problem is that Finnish law does not keep up as electric bikes and other electric devices develop.

## Birds must not be disturbed now

![Green birds' eggs in a nest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6791035ec55bedad4b0/1589992528)

Open image viewer

Birds are nesting now. They must be given breeding peace. Photo: Ari Welling / Yle

The bird association BirdLife Finland reminds Midsummer celebrants that everyone must respect birds' breeding peace.

Landing on bird breeding islands and skerries is not allowed.

If a Midsummer bonfire may be lit, its place must be chosen so that it does not disturb birds.

Bird nests and abandoned baby birds must also be left alone.

## Midsummer is warm

![Water temperatures in lakes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11407401)

Open image viewer

The lakes have warm water now. The map shows how warm the water is at the surface. Photo: Yle Weather / Anne Borgström

People can enjoy Midsummer in good weather.

The thunderstorms will pass and the weather will become dry.

Tomorrow, Friday, the weather will be sunny and, in a large part of the country, warm. In the south the temperature will rise to heatwave levels.

The whole Midsummer weekend will be dry and sunny.
