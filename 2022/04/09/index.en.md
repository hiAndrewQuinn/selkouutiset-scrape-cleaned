## Marin's interview

In Finland there is discussion about the country's security.

The government's report on security matters will be published on Wednesday. This is what Prime Minister Sanna Marin said in a TV interview on Saturday. The government will give the report to Parliament, which will start a discussion about it.

The report has been made because of the war in Ukraine. Europe's security situation changed when Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

In Prime Minister Marin's view, Finland must decide during the spring whether Finland will apply for membership of the defence alliance NATO. Finland is already cooperating with NATO, but is not a NATO member.

## The Centre and NATO

![Annika Saarikko at a party council meeting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4765,h_2668,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-94019062513aad42ea3/1649490713)

The Centre has a meeting at the weekend in Vaasa. In front is party leader and minister Annika Saarikko. Photo: Timo Aalto / Lehtikuva

The Centre believes that Finland can join the defence alliance NATO. On Saturday the Centre decided that it does not oppose Finland's membership of NATO.

The Centre is one of Finland's government parties. That is why the Centre's ministers are involved in deciding whether Finland applies for NATO membership or not.

A lot is now being said about Finland's relationship with NATO because of the war in Ukraine. Ukraine and Finland are both neighbours of Russia.

In Finland, many people think that Finland is safer if it joins the defence alliance NATO.

## Russian lorries

![A lorry next to the customs building at Nuijamaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5966,h_3358,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-94024862517e828f18c/1649508245)

Open image viewer

It is quiet at the Nuijamaa border station, because Russian lorries can no longer get into Finland. Photo: Kalle Purhonen / Yle

Russian and Belarusian lorries can no longer get into Finland. The reason is the sanctions that the European Union has imposed on Russia because of the war in Ukraine.

All commercial traffic of Russian and Belarusian lorries will be stopped. Lorries must turn back at the border. The same also applies to lorries and vans.

## Mikael Agricola Day

![Detail from Mikael Agricola's ABC book (1543).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6167149)

Open image viewer

The ABC book (Abckiria) is the first book in Finnish. Photo: National Library

Saturday was Mikael Agricola Day. The day is also the Finnish language day.

Mikael Agricola lived about 500 years ago. He is said to have been the father of the written Finnish language.

Agricola's ABC book was published in 1543. It was the first book in Finnish.

The ABC book teaches, for example, the alphabet and number words. In addition, the book teaches the basics of Christianity.

Today, Mikael Agricola Day is a flag day.

The rain weakens on Sunday. However, in different parts of Finland a little snow or sleet may fall. On Sunday the temperature is between 2 and 4 degrees.

In the night between Sunday and Monday, snowfalls will move across central and northern Finland.

Monday is dry in the south, that is, no rain will come. The sun may also shine. In the east and north a little snow will still fall on Monday.
