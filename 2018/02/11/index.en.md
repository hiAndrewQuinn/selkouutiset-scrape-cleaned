# Sunday 11.2.2018 (radio)

A passenger plane crashed in Moscow. Call congestion slows down emergency calls. Saturday did not bring Olympic medals for Finns. The matriculation exams begin. The I Read to You campaign has ended.

## A plane crashed in Moscow

![A Saratov Airlines plane at an airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10070338)

The plane was on its way from Moscow to the city of Orsk, which is near the border with Kazakhstan.. Photo: Yuri Smityuk / AOP

There has been a serious plane accident in Russia.

Russian news agencies report that a passenger plane crashed south-east of Moscow. There were 71 passengers and crew members on the plane. The plane left Moscow in the afternoon towards the city of Orsk.

There is no information yet about the cause of the crash. The plane was completely destroyed in the crash.

## The emergency call system is late

![A map of the regions of the emergency response centres.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10065451)

Open image viewer

Finland has 6 regional emergency response centres. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Emergency calls are still answered too slowly. The reason is call congestion.

Answering was supposed to be made faster, but the new Erica information system is late. The system was supposed to be in use already in 2015. Now the Emergency Response Centre Agency believes that the system will work in the whole of Finland next spring.

The Erica system connects all of Finland's 6 regional emergency response centres. With the system, an emergency call does not stay in the call queue of its own region but is quickly connected to some other emergency response centre.

Last year more than 2.6 million emergency calls came to the emergency number 112.

## Saturday did not bring Finland a medal at the Olympics

![Olli Hiidensalo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10070324)

Open image viewer

Biathlete Olli Hiidensalo completed 10 kilometres without misses and was 47.5 seconds behind the leader. Photo: Lehtikuva

Finland's men skiers were far behind the leaders in the skiathlon at the Winter Olympics in South Korea. The best Finn was Iivo Niskanen, who was 19th. The Norwegians took the top three places.

In mixed doubles curling, the Finns finished last in their group but ended their competition with a win over the USA. In women's ice hockey, the USA beat Finland 3-1.

All 3 Finnish biathletes got into Monday's pursuit race when they did well in the biathlon sprint.

## The matriculation exams begin

![The matriculation exams are starting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6579883)

Open image viewer

The matriculation exams begin on Monday. After Thursday's last school day celebrations, the final-year upper secondary students begin a month of study leave. Photo: Yle

The matriculation exams begin in upper secondary schools on Monday. The first exam is in mother tongue text skills.

The final-year upper secondary students celebrate their last school day on Thursday, that is, they are at school for the last day. On Friday, the second-year students celebrate the Day of the Oldest Students.

The written exams of the matriculation exams begin in a month.

## The reading campaign ended

The I Read to You campaign has ended.

More than 600 immigrant students took part during the week. They held reading sessions for elderly people and people with intellectual disabilities. In the campaign, volunteers read plain language books and the Selkosanomat newspaper aloud in about 30 places.

On our website you can watch how the campaign went at a Validia home in Helsinki. You can watch the story at yle.fi/selkouutiset. The I Read to You campaign was organised by the Plain Language Centre and the Association for Elderly and Neighbourly Help Valli.
