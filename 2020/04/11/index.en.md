# Saturday 11.4.2020 (radio)

There are 136 new coronavirus infections in Finland. The Chancellor of Justice will investigate how the government monitors movement. Health companies are offering jobs to Finnair employees. Spring is advancing quickly.

## Almost 140 new infections have been found

![An ambulance in the area of HUS's Meilahti hospital in front of the Haartman hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11302943)

More than 2,900 coronavirus infections have been found in Finland. The authorities say that there were 136 new cases in a day.

49 people have died of the disease in Finland. The average age of those who died is 81. There are 235 people in hospital care, of whom 80 are in intensive care.

Most infections are still in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district. In Helsinki there are more than 1,000 infections.

Worldwide, more than 100,000 people have already died of the coronavirus. The most deaths are in Italy, the United States and Spain.

## The Chancellor of Justice will investigate the government's corona measures

![Police checking access permits at a checkpoint on the Uusimaa border on main road 6 in Lapinjärvi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11303003)

The Chancellor of Justice has received many complaints about the closing of the Uusimaa region.

The Chancellor of Justice also wants to investigate how the government monitors people's movement. The question is how the government uses technology and location data when it fights the coronavirus.

Telia said a week ago that it would give the government people's phone location data. Location data shows where people move.

## Health companies are offering jobs to Finnair employees

![Passengers at check-in for a Finnair flight to Los Angeles at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11303021)

Open image viewer

In Finland, private health giants are interested in hiring Finnair staff for temporary jobs. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

Many employees of the airline Finnair have been laid off because of the coronavirus. Now health companies are offering them temporary work. For example, the health company Mehiläinen wants Finnair staff to work for it.

Finnair says that many employees have health care training. 67 Finnair employees have said that they are interested in Mehiläinen's offer. A few Finnair employees have already started work at Mehiläinen.

At Mehiläinen, Finnair employees do work, for example, as practical nurses. After the layoff, the employees go back to work for Finnair.

## Spring is advancing quickly

Many migratory birds have finished their journey early this year. These cranes were photographed on 8 April in Vesilahti in Pirkanmaa. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

Spring is coming very quickly now.

In Northern Finland there is still snow on the ground, but in the south there are already many signs of spring. For example, in Pirkanmaa there have been coltsfoot and hepatica already in March. In Southern Finland there are, for example, wood anemones, bumblebees and many butterflies.

Migratory birds have come to Finland earlier than usual, because the winter was warm.
