## Foreign Minister Haavisto is being examined

![Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto adjusts his glasses](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_18/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6238605df9a9d9976f4/1576643116)

Open image viewer

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Lehtikuva

The Constitutional Law Committee of Parliament is examining whether Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto has acted according to the law.

10 MPs have demanded that the legality of Haavisto's actions in bringing the Finns in the al-Hol camp back home must be examined. The MPs want to find out, for example, whether Haavisto pressured Foreign Ministry officials to make unlawful decisions.

The Constitutional Law Committee will hear Haavisto on 14 January.

## Minister Matti Ahde has died

![SDP's long-serving MP, minister Matti Ahde.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11129006)

Open image viewer

Minister Matti Ahde. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

SDP's former MP, minister Matti Ahde, has died. Ahde was 73 years old.

Matti Ahde came from Oulu. He was 24 years old when he was elected to Parliament. He was an MP for almost 30 years in total.

Ahde also served as a minister and as Speaker of Parliament in the 1980s. He was also for a long time chief executive of Veikkaus.

Matti Ahde received the title of minister in 2017.

A moment of silence was held in Parliament today in memory of Matti Ahde.

## Parliament went on its Christmas break

![At the last plenary session of the year, Parliament held a moment of silence in memory of the late Speaker Matti Ahde.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11131210)

Today was Parliament's last working day before the Christmas break. The day traditionally began with a Christmas coffee, which was provided by the Speaker of Parliament. The Sibelius Upper Secondary School chamber choir sang.

MPs will be on holiday until early February.

## The Smiling Man is the critics' favourite

![Film director Juho Kuosmanen in the lobby of the Bio Rex cinema.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5359,h_3014,x_0,y_419/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6243745dfa53fa8bebc/1576686886)

Open image viewer

Film director Juho Kuosmanen. Photo: Katriina Laine / Yle

Film critics have chosen the best Finnish film of the decade.

34 critics answered Yle's poll. 22 critics said that director Juho Kuosmanen's film The Smiling Man is the best Finnish film of the decade.

The Smiling Man tells the story of boxer Olli Mäki. The film won 8 Jussi Awards for Finnish films. It was also shown at the Cannes Film Festival.

The Clear News TV broadcast went on its Christmas break today.

On the radio, Clear News will come almost as usual during the Christmas holidays too. News in clear Finnish can also be read and listened to on our website.
