# Saturday 7.12.2019 (radio)

Finns think the prime minister caused the government crisis himself. Independence Day passed calmly. The Presidential Palace reception had 2.3 million viewers. The Eurojackpot top prize has come to Finland again.

## Cause of the government crisis

![Antti Rinne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11107595)

Open image viewer

Antti Rinne resigned as prime minister on Tuesday. Photo: Pekka Sipola / AOP

There has been a government crisis in Finland this week. Because of the crisis, Prime Minister Antti Rinne resigned as prime minister on Tuesday.

Finns think the prime minister himself caused the government crisis. This is what Yle's poll shows. 56 per cent of those who answered the poll said that the prime minister was the cause of the crisis.

The crisis arose when the government party, the Centre Party, no longer trusted the prime minister. That is why the Centre Party wanted Rinne to resign. Rinne is from the SDP, which is the largest party in the government.

## Independence Day demonstrations

![Torch procession.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11106935)

Open image viewer

Demonstrations were held on Independence Day. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Independence Day passed calmly. Independence Day was on Friday, and there were many demonstrations in Helsinki that day.

On Independence Day, the far right and their opponents held demonstrations. The police did not let the different demonstrations get close to each other, and everything went calmly.

However, the police arrested 13 people in total, but they have already been released.

## Haukio's dress

![Jenni Haukio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11106881)

Open image viewer

Mrs Jenni Haukio on Independence Day. Photo: Yle

There was a big celebration at the Presidential Palace on Independence Day. The presidential couple invited 1,700 guests to the palace.

Finns watch the Palace party on television every year. This year there were 2.3 million viewers. This year, too, the party dresses interested Finns.

Yle held a vote for viewers on whose dress was the most beautiful. The winner was Jenni Haukio, the spouse of President Sauli Niinistö.

Haukio's fuchsia-red party dress got the most votes. Many people thought it was good that Haukio wore the dress for a second time. Haukio had the same dress at the palace party in 2015. Haukio showed that a beautiful dress can be worn more than once.

According to the audience, the second most beautiful dress was Jitka Nováčková's. She is the partner of Finnish football team captain Tim Sparv.

The third in the dress vote was Sari Salo. Salo is a teacher, and she has been chosen as Class Teacher of the Year.

## Eurojackpot top prize

![Eurojackpot coupons at Prisma in Loimaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10192425)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mari Kahila / Yle

The top prize of Eurojackpot has come to Finland again. Eurojackpot is a European lottery.

The top prize this week was 23 million euros. The winning coupon was played at the Linnatuuli service station in Janakkala.

Finns have been lucky in the Eurojackpot lottery before. The top prize has come to Finland 17 times already. Eurojackpot began almost 8 years ago.
