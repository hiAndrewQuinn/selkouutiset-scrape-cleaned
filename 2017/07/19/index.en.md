# Wednesday 19.7.2017 (radio)

One person died in a plane crash. Russian President Vladimir Putin is coming to Finland next week. In Vuosaari in Helsinki, nurses have time for the elderly. The police have found false banknotes in Finland. A Finn trusts a firefighter. Aleksi Ojala walks faster than others run.

## Good care

![Practical nurse Leila Kantanen watches as Sirkka Arola eats at home.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9727307)

Practical nurse Leila Kantanen has time for Sirkka Arola at her home in Vuosaari. Photo: Kari Ahotupa / Yle

Carers for the elderly are often in a hurry.

In Vuosaari in Helsinki the situation is better. The nurses have time for the elderly, because they work 9 hours a day. A longer working day reduces rush, and there are more days off.

Nurses and the elderly are satisfied with the trial. That is why a longer working day for elderly care nurses is also being considered elsewhere in Helsinki.

## False banknotes

![50-euro specimen banknote with a mark showing it is not valid as a means of payment.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9730146)

Open image viewer

The text "invalid" tells that the banknote is not genuine. Photo: East Finland Police Department

The police have found false banknotes in Finland.

False 50-euro banknotes have been in circulation in Eastern Finland. The banknotes have been used, for example, in restaurants.

The police arrested 3 young men in Kuopio. The men had false banknotes.

The police say that the banknotes look genuine. However, the banknotes were ordered from abroad over the internet.

The banknotes have the word "invalid". It tells that the banknote is not genuine.

The police have also found false banknotes in Turku. False 100-euro banknotes have been in circulation there.

## A trustworthy firefighter

![Firefighter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9728675)

Open image viewer

A firefighter is trustworthy in the opinion of a Finn. Photo: Fredrika Sundén / Yle

A Finn trusts a firefighter.

The magazine Valitut Palat asked readers which professions' employees they trust. The result is that Finns trust firefighters and pharmacy employees the most.

Finns trust telephone salespeople the least. Politicians are also trusted only a little.

## Fast walker

![Aleksi Ojala walks past a runner.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9726115)

Open image viewer

Aleksi Ojala walks past a runner.

A walker is slower than a runner. That is usually so, but not always.

Aleksi Ojala is a man who walks faster than an ordinary person runs. Ojala is a race walker.

When Ojala is out jogging, runners are amazed. They cannot understand how someone can walk past them.

Many cannot even cycle as fast as Ojala walks. Ojala's speed can be as much as over 20 kilometres per hour.
