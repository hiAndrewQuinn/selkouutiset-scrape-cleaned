# Saturday 10.9.2022 (radio)

Charles was proclaimed king. Denmark celebrates Queen Margrethe II. Sweden holds parliamentary elections on Sunday. Hunting of forest game birds began. The weather is dry.

## King Charles III

![King Charles III.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2833,h_1593,x_3,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1005868631b710d107cf/1662743759)

In Britain Charles was officially proclaimed king on Saturday afternoon. He is now King Charles III. The ceremony was at St James's Palace in London.

Later there will be another occasion where Charles will be crowned king. The date of the occasion is not known.

Queen Elizabeth died on Thursday. Charles is Elizabeth's son. Charles inherited the position of ruler of the United Kingdom from Elizabeth.

Denmark's Queen Margrethe II has been on the throne for 50 years. Denmark's queen is being celebrated this weekend in Copenhagen.

President Sauli Niinistö and Jenni Haukio are taking part in the celebrations. The celebrations will end on Sunday with a fine dinner at Christiansborg Palace.

In Sweden people vote in parliamentary elections on Sunday.

The party leaders debated on television in the evening. In Friday's debate they talked about energy, health care and gang crime.

In the Swedish election the post of prime minister is being fought for evenly by the Social Democrats' Magdalena Andersson and the Moderate Party's Ulf Kristersson.

## Forest game birds

![A Finnish Spitz lies on moss, and in front of it is a dead capercaillie. The dog has a tracking collar around its neck.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1454,h_817,x_2,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1001522630f3c6308d63/1661943066)

Open image viewer

A Finnish Spitz lies on moss, and in front of it is a dead capercaillie. Photo: Jussi Leipälä's home album

Hunting of many forest game birds began today, Saturday. The exact hunting season varies.

Capercaillie, black grouse and hazel grouse are birds that may be hunted in most of Finland this year for 2 or 3 months. Male capercaillies and black grouse may usually only be hunted in January in Northern Finland. There are some exceptions to the rule.

In the US state of California there is a large wildfire. The fire broke out on Monday near the city of Los Angeles.

The fire is spreading quickly, because the area is dry and the wind is strong. More fires may start. Many people have been moved away from the path of the fires.
