# Sunday 1.11.2020 (radio)

Public packaging waste collection points are decreasing. 2 people have died in an attack in Québec, Canada. Older Finns are in better condition than people of the same age 30 years ago. The winter tyre season has begun.

## Eco points decrease

![Eco point waste in Ivalo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11555058)

Open image viewer

The responsibility for waste sorting in properties is growing. That is why public waste points are decreasing. Photo: Vesa Toppari / Yle

Public packaging waste collection points are decreasing.

For example, near supermarkets there are collection boxes of the Rinki eco points. Cardboard, glass, metal and plastic can be brought to them.

There are now about 2,000 eco points.

The number may fall by almost half from the current number. The changes are due to the reform of the Waste Act. For example, housing companies must take more care of waste collection.

More waste bins for collection and sorting are needed in the yards of buildings. That is why residents' waste bills may grow.

## 2 people have died in an attack in Canada

![Old town of Québec.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11625246)

The old town of Québec last May. Photo: AOP

In Québec, Canada, 2 people have died in an attack.

A man of about 20 years old is suspected of the act. He was dressed in medieval clothes, and he had a sword.

The man attacked people late yesterday in several different places.

The motive for the attack is not known.

Earlier on Thursday there was a stabbing in France. 3 people died in the stabbing.

The stabbing happened in a church in the city of Nice. The Nice stabbing is being investigated as a terror act.

## Today's old people are often in good condition

![Saimi Tikkanen at her study desk with a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9090711)

Open image viewer

Today's old people are in many ways in better condition than people of the same age 30 years ago. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

75-year-old and 80-year-old Finns are in better condition than people of the same age 30 years ago.

There are good changes in physical, i.e. bodily, condition and in intellectual abilities. For example, muscle strength, walking speed, reaction speed and reasoning ability have improved.

Verbal fluency, i.e. the ability to remember and produce words, has also improved.

Today's elderly also have less depression and fewer experiences of loneliness.

The information is from a study by the University of Jyväskylä. It compared 75-year-old and 80-year-old Finns in recent years and 30 years ago.

The researcher says that in the comparison, the whole life course of people must be remembered. For example, most of the older age group only went to a short school, had already done heavy work when young, and lived in a different society.

## Now winter tyres may be used

![Matti Alppinen changes winter tyres on his car in Fallkulla, Helsinki, on 20 October 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11605068)

Open image viewer

Summer tyres must not be used if the road conditions require winter tyres. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The winter tyre season began on Sunday.

The new Road Traffic Act says that winter tyres must be used until the end of March, if the road conditions require it.

If you drive on summer tyres in winter conditions, you may get a fine. Summer tyres may be used if the conditions are good.

The law also sets rules on the condition of tyres. The depth of the main grooves must be at least 3 millimetres.
