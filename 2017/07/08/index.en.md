# Saturday 8.7.2017 (radio)

The meeting of the big economies continued in Hamburg. There were also violent demonstrations in Hamburg. The Ruisrock festival is in Turku this weekend. The Eurojackpot top prize came to Finland again. Swimming water is cold.

## The Hamburg meeting

![The G20 meeting group photo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9711787)

Leaders of the big economies in Hamburg, Germany. Photo: Lukas Barth / EPA

The leaders of the big economies have met in Germany. The meeting of the countries' leaders was on Friday and Saturday in Hamburg.

During the meeting, US President Donald Trump met Russian President Vladimir Putin for the first time. President Trump was satisfied with the meeting.

The leaders of Germany, Russia and France discussed the situation in Ukraine in Hamburg. The leaders agreed that there must be peace in Eastern Ukraine.

## The Hamburg demonstrations

![Protesters ran riot, breaking into, among other places, a shop of the Rewe chain in Hamburg on 7 July. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9713052)

Open image viewer

The demonstrators also looted shops. Photo: Odd Andersen / AFP

There have been big demonstrations in Hamburg during the meeting of the economies. Some of the demonstrations were peaceful, but some were violent.

The demonstrators burned police cars and looted shops. The night between Friday and Saturday was violent. Many police officers and demonstrators were injured.

A total of about 100,000 people took part in the demonstrations.

## Ruisrock in Turku

![Maisa Saloniemi and Tuire Tuominen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9712994)

People of different ages go to Ruisrock. Photo: Paula Koskinen / Yle

Summer events continue in Finland.

Ruisrock is being held in Turku this weekend. It is a big rock music festival. The audience is about 100,000.

Ruisrock is also an old festival. It was first held already in 1970.

More information about Ruisrock is available on the festival's website. The website also has a section in plain language. The address is ruisrock.fi.

## Eurojackpot millions to Finland

![Eurojackpot balls.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7469752)

Open image viewer

The winning Eurojackpot numbers are always drawn on Friday. Photo: Veikkaus

Finns' good luck in the Eurojackpot game continues. Eurojackpot is a European lottery that is played in many countries.

This week's top prize came to Finland. The winner gets over 46 million euros. The top prize went to Tampere.

The top prize last came to Finland in April, to Tampere then too. Three people from Tampere together won about 87 million euros.

Eurojackpot has been played for 5 years. The top prize has already come to Finland 12 times.

## Cold swimming water

![People spending a summer day on a beach. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9712909)

Open image viewer

The sun calls people to the beach, but the water is cold. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

The weekend weather is warm. Sunday is a sunny day. The temperature is over 20 degrees.

But swimming waters are now cold, because the weather has been cold for a long time. The coldest water was found on Saturday morning at Kilpisjärvi in Lapland. There the surface water temperature was only 5.3 degrees.

Quite warm lake water was found in Saimaa and Päijänne. There the water temperature at some beaches was 17.5 degrees.

At this time of year the temperature of swimming water is often over 20 degrees.
