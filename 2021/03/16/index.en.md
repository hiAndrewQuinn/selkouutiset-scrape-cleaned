## People have cancelled vaccination appointments

![A nurse holds a coronavirus vaccine made by Astra Zeneca.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5076,h_2855,x_0,y_127/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78466160506d23d252e/1615883659)

There have been no problems in Finland with the Astra Zeneca vaccine. Photo: AFP / Lehtikuva

Some Finns have cancelled some coronavirus vaccination appointments. The reason is the debate about the safety of the Astra Zeneca coronavirus vaccine.

For example, in Espoo some dozens of vaccination appointments have been cancelled. In Kuopio, Turku and Tampere a few appointments have been cancelled. People have also been asking more questions about the vaccine than before.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that no problems have been observed in Finland with the Astra Zeneca vaccine.

## A year of the coronavirus time

![A woman reads a book on the floor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11289499)

Open image viewer

Finns are satisfied with life. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

It is now exactly a year since the coronavirus time and the emergency conditions began in Finland. The coronavirus time has changed people's lives a lot.

Even so, Finns are satisfied with life. In a Yle survey, more than 80 percent of Finns say that they are satisfied with life.

The coronavirus time is especially hard for young people. The satisfaction with life of young people aged 15 to 29 has decreased. However, young people trust the future more than older people do. Young people also believe in their own ability to make a difference.

## Not everyone is allowed to work remotely

![A remote worker stretches their shoulders.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7441715fb694b649c99/1605846107)

Open image viewer

About a million Finns are working remotely. Photo: Petteri Juuti / Yle

Only some Finns are allowed to work remotely. Yle has asked Finns whether they are allowed to work remotely. More than half of the respondents say that their employer does not allow them to work remotely, even though the job suits remote work.

The respondents say that the employer does not trust employees. For example, the employer may think that the employee does not do enough work at home.

In Finland there is a recommendation to work remotely. More than a million Finns have moved to remote work because of the coronavirus situation.

## Nokia is cutting staff

![Nokia's head office in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11839320)

Open image viewer

Nokia's head office in Espoo. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The Finnish network company Nokia is cutting a lot of staff.

Nokia plans to cut as many as 10,000 jobs in different countries over 2 years. Nokia now has 90,000 people working around the world. Nokia's goal is to save about 600 million euros.

Nokia says that more workers are needed in Finland, however. Nokia needs more workers to design and develop fast 5G networks.
