# Wednesday 16.5.2018 (radio)

The Helsinki police chief may not continue in his job. Facebook has closed fake accounts. Many upper secondary school students plan to take a break from studying. Finns use a lot of snus. In Finland, 3000 kilometres of main roads are being repaired.

## Helsinki police

![Lasse Aapio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10187318)

Open image viewer

Head of the Helsinki Police Department Lasse Aapio Photo: Markku Pitkänen / Yle

The Ministry of the Interior has decided that the head of the Helsinki Police Department, Lasse Aapio, may not work as police chief.

Aapio is accused of an offence in office. He may not do his job as long as the charge is being dealt with in court.

The Ministry of the Interior says that if Aapio worked as police chief, it would harm the reputation of the police and people would trust the police less than before.

## Facebook's fake accounts

![The Facebook app open on a phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10148528)

Open image viewer

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Facebook says that it has closed a lot of fake accounts.

Facebook closed over 580 million fake accounts in January, February and March .

The company closes fake accounts because it is trying to reduce inappropriate pictures, hate speech and terrorist propaganda.

In addition, Facebook has removed posts and given warnings.

## Upper secondary and vocational school students

![Upper secondary school students in a lesson](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10139247)

Open image viewer

Photo: Paula Koskinen / Yle

Many young people studying at upper secondary school plan to take a break after upper secondary school.

Upper secondary school students were asked about this in a study. In the study, 30 percent of the students said that they plan to take a one-year break after upper secondary school. 25 percent could not say where they plan to continue studying.

The researchers think that a break after upper secondary school may cause young people to end up outside society.

Young people studying at vocational schools do not want to take a break as much as upper secondary school students. Almost half of vocational school students want to continue studying right after they have graduated from vocational school.

## Use of snus

![A tin of snus](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9438929)

Open image viewer

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finnish Customs seizes a lot of snus.

Finnish Customs says that since 2010 it has seized a hundred times more snus than before that.

Snus is a substance that is made from tobacco. It is usually put in the mouth under the lip. Snus is harmful to health. In Finland, selling snus is illegal.

Most snus is smuggled into Finland from Sweden. A lot of it is sold on social media.

Finns spend 50 million euros a year on buying snus.

## Roads are being repaired

![A future cycle path and a temporary detour are being built in Viiskulma.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8892378)

Open image viewer

A future cycle path and a temporary detour are being built in Viiskulma. Photo: Finnish Transport Agency

In Finland, a total of about 3000 kilometres of main roads are being repaired this year.

The Finnish Transport Agency says that the biggest works are being done in Uusimaa and around the city of Lahti.

About 139 million euros are being spent on repairing roads. Most of the money is spent on new asphalt.
