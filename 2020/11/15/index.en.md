## The Jyväskylä accident

The police are investigating the Jyväskylä motorcycle accident. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

A serious motorcycle accident has happened in Jyväskylä. 2 boys died in the accident. The boys were minors, i.e. under 18 years old.

The boys' motorcycle collided with a car. There was a man in the car, who was not injured.

The police say that the boys' motorcycle had no lights. The police are investigating the accident.

## Drownings in the Mediterranean

![A boat full of migrants in the Mediterranean.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11371408)

Open image viewer

People often try to cross the Mediterranean in poor boats. Photo: Hannah Wallace Bowman / EPA

Many people have drowned in the Mediterranean while trying to reach Europe from Africa. This week dozens of people have drowned.

The organisation Doctors Without Borders says that this year about 700 people have already died in the Mediterranean during their escape journey.

Many Africans flee war and poverty. Many drown in the Mediterranean because the escape boats are poor.

Doctors Without Borders says that the deaths are the fault of the European Union, i.e. the EU. The EU has banned rescue ships from operating in the Mediterranean.

The start of November has been warm.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that November so far has been about 5 degrees warmer than usual.

Usually in Finland there is already frost at this time of year.

November also continues warm. In addition, it is forecast that December and January will also be warmer than usual.

## Teach smart devices!

![Donate speech, ask your favourite.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11648735)

Open image viewer

It is important that smart devices learn to understand all of us. Photo: Andrea Piacquadio / Pexels

Finally, we look to the future.

In the future, devices will be easy to use. We only need to talk to the devices. And they will do what we ask. That is, if they understand us.

Now the Donate speech campaign wants to teach devices to understand Finnish. That is why the campaign is collecting people's speech.

Anyone can record their own speech on the campaign's website or mobile app. It is easy and quick.

Only researchers and device developers may listen to the speech, nobody else.

Devices must also learn that people speak Finnish in many different ways.

That is why the Donate speech campaign hopes for many kinds of Finnish speech. Finnish does not need to be the speaker's first language.

Participation instructions can be found on the website lahjoitapuhetta.fi.
