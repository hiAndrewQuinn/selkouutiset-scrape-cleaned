# Thursday 2.1.2020 (radio)

Finns think nurses' pay should be raised. In technology, men earn more than women. The world's youngest prime minister will soon be found in Austria. Last year was warmer than usual in Finland. Norway broke its January heat record.

## Nurses' pay rises

![Statistics graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11122523)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mikko Airikka / Yle

Most Finns support bigger pay rises for nurses than for other workers.

In Yle's poll, about 80 per cent of respondents think that health care workers can get bigger pay rises than others in the spring negotiations.

The large support is due to the high regard for nursing work.

However, the employers, that is, the municipalities, say that they do not have the money to raise nurses' pay.

## Pay gaps in technology

![Female engineer at work](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11141448)

Open image viewer

An engineer at work. Photo: AOP

In the technology sector there is a 5 per cent pay gap between men and women.

A report by the organisation Tekniikan Akateemiset says that right after graduation, men's and women's salaries are at the same level. However, the pay gap starts to grow at about age 35, when women's salaries fall behind men's.

The pay gap may be due to men advancing in their careers more often and faster.

## The youngest prime minister

![Sebastian Kurz waves. In the background is a large word, Danke, meaning Thank you.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10996392)

Open image viewer

Austria's Sebastian Kurz is the world's youngest prime minister. Photo: Christian Bruna / EPA

Finland's Prime Minister Sanna Marin is no longer the world's youngest prime minister. Marin has lost the title to the leader of Austria's conservatives, Sebastian Kurz.

In Austria the conservatives and the Greens form a joint government. Its prime minister will be 33-year-old Sebastian Kurz.

Kurz is a year younger than Sanna Marin, so he is the youngest prime minister in the world at present.

## Last year was warm

![Outdoor enthusiasts in snowless Helsinki on New Year's Day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11141550)

Open image viewer

Outdoor enthusiasts in Helsinki on New Year's Day. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The year 2019 was warmer than usual in Finland, says the Finnish Meteorological Institute. The average temperature across the whole country was about one degree warmer than usual.

The lowest temperature of the year was -39.1 degrees. It was measured at Kevojärvi in Utsjoki on 5 February. The highest temperature of the year was 33.7 degrees. It was measured at Emäsalo in Porvoo on 28 July.

The average temperature in December was 3 to 6 degrees higher than usual.

There was less snow across the country in December than usual at that time. In Lapland, however, there was a record amount of snow.

## Norway's heat record

![Sunndalsøra fjord](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11141656)

Open image viewer

A föhn wind raised the temperature in Sunndalsøra in Norway . Photo: AOP

Central Norway has recorded a temperature of 19 degrees today. Norway has never before measured such a high temperature in January.

A Norwegian meteorologist says that the high temperature is due to a warm föhn wind.
