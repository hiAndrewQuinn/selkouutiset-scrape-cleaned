# Tuesday 6.2.2018 (radio)

Minister of the Interior. The parliamentary session. Security in Iraq. Stock prices. The Sámi National Day. Slippery roads.

## Mykkänen and Virolainen are new ministers

![Anne-Mari Virolainen becomes a new minister, and Minister for Foreign Trade and Development Kai Mykkänen is chosen as Minister of the Interior.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10061880)

Open image viewer

The National Coalition Party chose Kai Mykkänen as Minister of the Interior and Anne-Mari Virolainen as Minister for Foreign Trade and Development. Photo: Yle

Finland's new Minister of the Interior is Kai Mykkänen.

The previous Minister of the Interior, National Coalition Party MP Paula Risikko, moved to become Speaker of Parliament.

Kai Mykkänen was previously Minister for Foreign Trade and Development.

Anne-Mari Virolainen will replace Mykkänen as Minister for Foreign Trade and Development.

Both ministers are National Coalition Party MPs.

## The President of the Republic opened the parliamentary session

![President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö and Speaker of Parliament Paula Risikko shake hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10061338)

The Speaker of Parliament and the President of the Republic shook hands at the opening of the parliamentary session. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtíkuva

Parliament gathered today for the opening of the parliamentary session.

The parliamentary session is Parliament's working year.

The session was opened by the President of the Republic, Sauli Niinistö. In his opening speech, Niinistö said that Finland must save money, even though the economy is improving.

## Migri: The security situation in Iraq has improved

![Local residents at the scene of a suicide attack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10060939)

Open image viewer

Local residents at Tayaran Square in Baghdad, where a suicide attack had been carried out. Photo: Ahmed Jalil / EPA

Security in Iraq has improved, says the Finnish Immigration Service Migri.

Security has improved because the extremist group Isis has lost power.

However, the situation is different in different parts of Iraq, says Migri.

For example, in Iraq's capital Baghdad, criminals kidnap people and demand ransoms for them.

In addition, there are still thousands of Isis fighters in Iraq, who are now in hiding.

## Stock prices fell sharply

![The New York Stock Exchange](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10061842)

Open image viewer

The fall in stock prices started in the United States and also spread to the rest of the world. Photo: Justin Lane / EPA

Stock prices fell a lot today in different parts of the world.

Stock prices already fell yesterday evening in the United States, and after that they also fell elsewhere in the world.

Share prices also fell on the Helsinki Stock Exchange in the morning.

Experts at Finnish banks say that the fall in stock prices was not a surprise.

Now stock prices have come up a little.

## The Sámi celebrate their National Day

![sámi national day in inari 2018](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10061722)

Open image viewer

The Sámi have their own flag, whose colours come from their national costumes. Photo: Ville-Riiko Fofonoff / Yle

Today is the Sámi National Day.

The Sámi are an indigenous people who live in the northern parts of Finland, Sweden, Norway and Russia.

There are about 60,000 Sámi. They have their own language and culture.

The Sámi also have their own flag and their own parliament, the Sámi Parliament, which defends the Sámi way of life.

The Sámi way of life includes, for example, reindeer herding and fishing.

You can also get to know the Sámi on Yle Areena: https://areena.yle.fi/1-4353700

Footpaths are now very slippery

![A slippery, icy pavement.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10060223)

Open image viewer

Pavements are now very slippery Photo: Bernd Schmidt / AOP

On Finland's south coast and in south-eastern Finland, the roads are now very slippery.

In the evening it will snow, and the roads will become even more slippery.

Töölö Hospital in Helsinki has had many patients in recent days who have slipped on the road.

The upper arm bone, the ankle bone and the shin bone break easily if you fall on a slippery road, says a doctor at Töölö Hospital.
