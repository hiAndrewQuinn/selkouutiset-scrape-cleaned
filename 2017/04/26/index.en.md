# Wednesday 26.4.2017 (radio)

Day-care fees are falling. The Midwives' College hospital will be closed in Helsinki. Because of the cold weather, the birch pollen season has not yet started. The price of fish has risen. The Helsinki police are testing body cameras.

## Day care is getting cheaper

![Children playing with a train under the guidance of a day-care aunt](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9164452)

Open image viewer

In day-care centres children get early childhood education. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Day-care fees are falling. The fees fall for many families, and for almost 7,000 families the day-care fee ends completely.

The government decided on the matter on Tuesday. The government believes that people should go to work when day care is cheap or free. That is why new jobs will come to Finland, the government estimates.

The government also supports day care by training more workers for day-care centres.

## A big hospital is closed

![Midwives' College hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8800029)

Open image viewer

The Midwives' College is Finland's biggest maternity hospital. Photo: Kai Gustafsson / AOP

A large hospital is being closed in Helsinki. The name of the hospital is the Midwives' College Hospital. It is Finland's biggest maternity hospital. About 5,500 children are born there every year.

The Midwives' College Hospital is being closed because its indoor air is bad. There is mould and microbes in the hospital. The situation has not improved, even though many repairs have been made in the hospital.

Many midwives say that they have become ill because of the hospital's poor indoor air. For mothers and babies the poor indoor air has not been harmful, because they have only been in the hospital for a short time.

## No birch pollen yet

![](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5330599)

Open image viewer

Many people are allergic to birch pollen. Photo: Yle / Pekka Sipilä

Cold weather makes life easier for people who are allergic to birch. Because of the cold weather, there is no birch pollen yet. It will be at least 2 weeks before the birch pollen season begins.

In southern Finland, people allergic to birch may get symptoms, because birch pollen travels with the wind from abroad to Finland.

## Fish is expensive

![A woman buying cold-smoked salmon at the market](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8048509)

Open image viewer

Many people buy fish at the market. Photo: Miki Wallenius / Yle

Finns like to eat fish. A lot of fish is bought at the market.

However, fish is expensive. The price has risen a lot. For example, salmon now costs much more than a year ago.

People may now be buying fish also because Finnish authorities have found dangerous bacteria in chicken meat. Many people think that fish is healthy and safe food.

## Police body cameras

![Body camera](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9579521)

Open image viewer

Body cameras are useful in police work. Photo: Berislav Jurišić / Yle

The Helsinki police are testing body cameras. The police overalls have a video camera that films what is happening around them.

The police think the camera is useful. Video footage helps to solve crimes. It also reduces violence against the police, because people behave better when they are in the view of a video camera.

In addition, the body camera footage helps to find out whether the police have acted correctly in difficult situations.

Body cameras are so useful that the police may start using the cameras permanently.
