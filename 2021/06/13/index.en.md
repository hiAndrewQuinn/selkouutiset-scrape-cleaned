## National Coalition Party did well in municipal elections

![orpo marin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11980568)

Open image viewer

Petteri Orpo's National Coalition Party was the largest party in the municipal elections. Sanna Marin's SDP was the second largest. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Municipal elections have been held in Finland. The election day for the municipal elections was on Sunday.

Finns voted lazily in the municipal elections. Only 55 percent of people who have the right to vote went to vote.

In the election the most votes went to the National Coalition Party. The National Coalition Party is Finland's largest municipal party.

After the National Coalition Party come the SDP and the Centre. However, their support has fallen when looking at the support figures for all of Finland.

The 4th largest party in the municipal elections is the Finns Party. The Finns Party's support is now greater than in the municipal elections 4 years ago.

The support figures for all of Finland do not tell how the parties did in different municipalities. Each municipality has its own municipal council.

In the municipal elections, voters decide who gets into the municipal council. In the municipal council, decisions are made, for example, about what the municipality uses tax money for.

There are about 300 municipalities in Finland.

In some cities the municipal elections are also mayoral elections. The mayor's post goes to the party that gets the most votes in the municipal elections.

For example, in Helsinki, Turku and Tampere the city is led by a mayor.

In Helsinki the mayor's post goes to the National Coalition Party. The National Coalition Party got the most votes in Helsinki.

## Finland beat Denmark

![Joel Pohjanpalo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2753,h_1549,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81975960c5281892233/1623533639)

Open image viewer

Joel Pohjanpalo scored Finland's first European Championship goal. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

Finns have celebrated success in football.

On Saturday, Finland beat Denmark in the football European Championship. The European Championship started this weekend.

Finland beat Denmark 1–0. Finland's goal was scored by Joel Pohjanpalo.

Finland took part in the football European Championship for the first time. The match against Denmark was Finland's first European Championship match in football.

The match was also sad. Denmark's star player Christian Eriksen had a cardiac arrest during the match. Eriksen was taken to hospital, and the match continued later.

Finland and Denmark played in Copenhagen, Denmark. Finland's next match is on Wednesday. Then Finland plays Russia in St. Petersburg, Russia.
