# Radio Clear News Monday 21.8.2023

Bankruptcies. Expensive electricity. More lessons. Weather.

## Bankruptcies

![A man paints the facade of an apartment block from a crane.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5274,h_2952,x_0,y_439/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115648564da2c8ce4e1c/1693921340)

In July there were many company bankruptcies. In no July in the 2000s have there been as many company bankruptcies.

There were many bankruptcies in the construction sector. Things are going badly in the construction sector, because people are not buying homes. The main reason is the rise in interest rates.

According to the Entrepreneurs of Finland organisation, the large number of bankruptcies shows that many companies have been doing badly for a long time.

The organisation estimates that more than 3,000 bankruptcies will happen this year as a whole.

## Expensive electricity

![A man and a woman on the benches of a sauna. The man throws water on the stove.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_211/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4520395a3a8b012d897/1513786435)

An electric sauna heater uses a lot of electricity. Photo: © 2012 Petri Jauhiainen

Electricity was very expensive on Monday. When the price was at its most expensive, one kilowatt-hour cost 70 cents. Usually this summer one kilowatt-hour has cost less than 10 cents.

The electricity price rose because Finland's nuclear power plants are not producing electricity normally. Maintenance and repair work is being done at the nuclear power plants.

In addition, less electricity than usual is coming to Finland from Sweden because of maintenance work.

Because electricity was so expensive, Finns used less electricity than usual on Monday. Electricity and money can be saved when you do not use the oven, for example, do not charge an electric car and do not heat the sauna.

Both ordinary people and companies learned to save electricity last winter, when electricity was extremely expensive.

## More lessons

![A schoolchild doing maths homework.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_625/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1084504640f336e7e741/1691654859)

Schoolchildren will get more lessons. The government programme promises more money for maths and Finnish language teaching in primary schools.

The reason is that schoolchildren are not learning as well as before. Learning results have worsened.

The government wants to stop the bad trend. If the government carries out the plan, there will be about two or three more hours a week of Finnish language and maths.

Teachers will have more time to teach the same things as before.

Finland's comprehensive school was for a long time perhaps the best in the world. Now many countries have overtaken Finland. Especially boys learn worse in Finland than before
