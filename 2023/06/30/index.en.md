## Minister's resignation

![Minister of Economic Affairs Vilhelm Junnila.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4896,h_2754,x_0,y_31/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1136639649ea55a9de24/1688553837)

Vilhelm Junnila. Photo: Eeva-Maria Brotherus / Lehtikuva

Minister Vilhelm Junnila is resigning. Junnila belongs to the Finns Party.

Junnila has been a minister for only 10 days. He is the Minister of Economic Affairs in Finland's new government.

The reason for the resignation is Junnila's speeches about the far right and Nazis. Junnila has spoken positively about Nazis.

Many newspapers around the world have also written about Junnila's speeches. In Finland, there has been concern that Junnila's speeches damage Finland's reputation also around the world.

The other government parties are pleased that the Finns Party's Vilhelm Junnila is leaving the government.

Prime Minister and chair of the National Coalition Petteri Orpo says that Junnila's resignation is the only right solution.

The chairs of RKP and the Christian Democrats have also thanked Junnila for resigning.

## French protests

![A large crowd of people walks down the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5760,h_3240,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1136387649dc5242d2a5/1688139123)

Open image viewer

Photo: STELLA Pictures/ddp/abaca press

There are big protests in France. Many French people are angry because of police violence.

A 17-year-old boy died on Tuesday when a police officer shot him. The police stopped the boy because he had broken traffic rules.

The boy had an Algerian background. The boy's mother says that the reason for the shooting was racism.

The authorities have arrested the police officer who shot the boy. The authorities suspect that he is guilty of manslaughter.

After the shooting, the protesters have burned houses and cars and robbed shops. Many hundreds of protesters have been arrested.

The French government has held crisis meetings because of the protests.

## Picking robots

![Ukrainian berry pickers (women) in a strawberry field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_116/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1135160649ad50c5ba8c/1687869719)

Open image viewer

There are still people working in strawberry fields. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

Strawberry picking will change in the future. Soon robots will pick strawberries.

The robot manufacturers estimate that picking robots will be common also on Finnish strawberry farms in about 10 years. Picking robots are already used around the world.

Picking robots are still being developed. For now, a person picks strawberries more carefully than a robot.

The picking of this summer's strawberry crop is just starting. Strawberries are already ripening in Southern Finland, and next week the strawberry crop will start to ripen in Central Finland too.
