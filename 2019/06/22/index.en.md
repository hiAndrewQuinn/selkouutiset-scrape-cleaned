# Saturday 22.6.2019 (radio)

There were accidents during midsummer. A serious fire has broken out in Indonesia. Next week will be really hot in Europe. Kaapo Kakko was drafted by the NHL with number 2.

## Midsummer accidents

![A lifebuoy at the Munkkiniemi boat harbour in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10843403)

Midsummer has passed fairly calmly, but accidents have happened. People have died in the accidents.

At least 3 people have drowned. In southern Finland 1 man drowned, and in central Finland 2 women drowned.

In northern Finland a young man died in a car accident. The car drove off the road.

The rescue services have been busy during midsummer, because they have been putting out fires and bonfires. Fires broke out in many saunas.

In addition, people lit bonfires in areas where there is a forest fire warning. During a forest fire warning it is forbidden to light fires outdoors.

The police have had work to do, because people have fought during midsummer.

## The fire in Indonesia

![A factory making detonators in North Sumatra burned on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10843663)

Open image viewer

The fire destroyed a detonator factory. Photo: Hermansyah / EPA

In Asia, in Indonesia, a serious fire has broken out. 30 people died in the fire. Among them were 5 children.

The fire started in a factory where detonators were made. The fire began when one detonator exploded.

People could not get out of the factory, because its outside door was locked. The dead were factory workers and the children of the workers.

The police have arrested 2 people because of the fire. It is said that they are the factory owner and the factory manager. The police say that they have not looked after the factory's safety properly.

## Heat in Europe

![A man lies in the sun on a scorched lawn in shorts.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10327501)

Next week will be really hot in Europe. The weather forecasts say that the temperature may reach as high as 40 degrees next week in many countries.

In France people are afraid that the hot weather will last the whole week. It was just as hot in France in 2003. Back then 15,000 people died because of the hot weather.

In Spain, too, meteorologists are warning that the weather next week will be unusually hot. They are afraid that the whole summer will be hotter than normal.

## Kaapo Kakko's success

![Kaapo Kakko, Jack Hughes and Kirby Dach](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10843548)

More sport:

Finnish ice hockey player Kaapo Kakko has been drafted into the North American ice hockey league, the NHL.

Kakko was drafted with number 2. Only the American Jack Hughes was drafted before Kakko.

Kakko was drafted by the New York Rangers team.

Kakko played really well at the ice hockey World Championships in May, when Finland became world champion.
