## Work-based immigration

![Practical nurse Bern Amar working, helping an elderly person, Attendo, Vantaa, 12.11.2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4330,h_2435,x_347,y_1083/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5226515be984d9ae497/1655812843)

Open image viewer

Many workers are needed, for example, in the care sector. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

Finland and other countries compete for workers. The Confederation of Finnish Industries, EK, demands that work-based immigration be made easier.

EK says that the number of immigrants must be doubled to 40,000 a year. EK demands a change in the next Government term.

The number of international students must be tripled to 15,000.

EK calculates that in this way more than 50,000 new workers a year can be obtained for Finland.

## Hungary and Nato

![Speaker of Parliament Matti Vanhanen being interviewed by the media in Parliament House on Wednesday 8 March 2023. Deputy Speaker of the Hungarian Parliament Csaba Hende and Speaker of the Finnish Parliament Matti Vanhanen. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

A delegation from the Hungarian Parliament has visited Finland. The delegation expressed support for Finland's Nato membership.

The delegation met Speaker Matti Vanhanen in Parliament. The Hungarians told Vanhanen that Hungary does not intend to tie Finland's membership to disputes. The dispute has been about how Hungary follows the European Union's principle of the rule of law.

Hungary and Turkey are the only Nato countries that have not yet ratified, that is, approved, Finland's and Sweden's membership in the defence alliance.

## ADHD diagnoses

![Sign with ADHD.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2952,h_1660,x_0,y_950/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-43129059ca55270f031/1506432361)

Open image viewer

An ADHD diagnosis cannot be made during one or two doctor's appointments. Careful examination takes time. Photo: Most Photos/DOC_PhotoStock

In Finland ADHD diagnoses may have been given too lightly. Some of the diagnoses may be wrong. At least the risk of errors has grown, say doctors whom Yle interviewed. ADHD means a disorder of activity and attention.

The number of users of ADHD medicines has increased 8-fold in Finland since 2010.

Psychiatry specialist Suvi Olkkola says that a careful ADHD examination takes time.

## International Women's Day

![A traditional flower gift delights the mind of many teachers and nurses. However, vases may run out if every child brings a flower package.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7851230)

Women often receive flowers on Women's Day. Photo: Alex Turunen / Yle

On Wednesday the United Nations' International Women's Day was celebrated. This year's theme is to use innovations and technology for gender equality.

The UN's International Women's Day was celebrated for the first time in 1975. The history of Women's Day, however, goes back to the early 1900s.

## Correction to the news on Tuesday, which concerned mental health services

There was an error in the Mental Health Association's press release on Tuesday: Finns' trust in mental health services has not improved, as the press release said, but has weakened.

In January 38 percent of Finns considered mental health services to be of good quality. Two years ago the figure was 52 percent.
