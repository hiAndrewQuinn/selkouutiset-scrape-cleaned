# Saturday 25.7.2020 (radio)

The Finnish members of the European Parliament consider the corona crisis aid package necessary. Thailand has decided to give berry pickers permission to travel to Finland. The corona situation is getting worse in the world. In Helsinki, a big bungee jumping tower is being lifted out of the sea.

TV Clear News has a summer break and it will continue on 17.8.
Radio Clear News is made every day, also in the summer.

## Assessments of the EU aid package

Eero Heinäluoma, Ville Niinistö and Sirpa Pietikäinen are among the Finnish members of the European Parliament.

Most of the Finnish members of the European Parliament think that the corona crisis aid package is necessary.

EU leaders agreed at the start of the week on an aid package that will help the economies of EU countries after the corona crisis. The size of the aid package is 750 billion euros.

In the opinion of the Finnish members of the European Parliament, it is important that EU leaders were able to make important decisions together.

Only 2 members of the Finns Party and 1 member of the Centre Party thought that the decision on the aid package was not good.

Finland has 14 members in the European Parliament. The European Parliament has members from all EU countries. There are 705 members in total.

## Thai berry pickers

![Thai pickers gather blueberries in Luumäki in 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6977635f07273dccf94/1676041259)

Finland needs Thai berry pickers. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

Finland will get berry pickers from Thailand this summer too.

Thailand has decided to give berry pickers permission to travel to Finland. Earlier, Thailand banned travel because of the corona crisis.

Thailand will let the berry pickers into Finland if Finnish companies take care of the berry pickers' safety. The companies must do everything possible so that the berry pickers do not get the coronavirus in Finland.

Thai berry pickers are important to Finnish companies. Thai pickers gather forest berries in Finland, for example blueberries and lingonberries.

## Corona situation

![A nurse takes a corona test from a patient.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11465494)

Open image viewer

For example, many corona cases have been found in India. Photo: Dibyangshu Sarkar / AFP

The corona situation in Finland is still good.

Only 8 new corona infections have been found in Finland in one day. In Finland there are in total a little under 7,400 corona infections and 329 corona deaths.

In the world, however, the corona situation is getting worse.

The World Health Organization WHO says that on Friday more than 280,000 new corona infections were found in the whole world. That is more than ever before in one day.

About 9,800 people died of corona on Friday. That is a sad record for this summer.

## Bungee jumping tower

![Broken bungee tower](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4943,h_2780,x_0,y_541/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7025275f1c0b7e3ad3d/1595673614)

The bungee jumping tower fell into the sea, but nobody was hurt. Photo: Thomas Hagström / Yle

In Helsinki, a big bungee jumping tower is being lifted out of the sea. The tower fell into the sea on Friday morning at the Kaivopuisto beach.

It is a crane that is 150 metres high. It is used in summer for bungee jumps, that is, people can jump from the tower with safety ropes on their feet.

There was no one in the bungee jumping tower when it fell.
