# Tuesday 3.12.2019 (radio)

Prime Minister Antti Rinne has resigned. Finland may get a new prime minister next week. Finnish children's reading skills are good. All snowflakes are different.

## Prime minister resigns

![Prime Minister Antti Rinne asked President Sauli Niinistö to resign](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11100531)

Finland's Prime Minister Antti Rinne has resigned. Rinne asked President Sauli Niinistö to accept his resignation on Tuesday.

Rinne could not continue as prime minister because of a dispute between the government parties. The dispute was between the Centre Party and Rinne's own party, the SDP.

On Monday, the Centre Party said that it no longer trusts the prime minister. In the Centre Party's view, the prime minister lied during the postal strike, that is, things did not happen the way the prime minister said. That is why the Centre Party wanted the prime minister to resign.

However, it is likely that co-operation between the government parties will continue after Rinne's resignation.

## Choosing the prime minister

![Sanna Marin and Antti Lindtman](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11100114)

Open image viewer

Sanna Marin (left) or Antti Lindtman (right) could be the next prime minister. Photo: Yle and Parliament

So Finland will get a new prime minister. The post of prime minister goes to the SDP, because it is the largest party in the government and in Parliament.

It is estimated that the new prime minister will be Sanna Marin or Antti Lindtman. Sanna Marin is now Minister of Transport and Communications. Antti Lindtman leads the SDP group in Parliament.

The SDP may decide next week who will become the new prime minister.

## Children's reading skills

![book](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10509955)

Open image viewer

Reading does not interest boys. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

Finnish children's reading skills are good. This is what the new PISA study shows.

Almost 80 countries took part in the study. Only in China, Singapore and Estonia were children's reading skills better than in Finland.

However, children's reading skills have got worse in Finland. Finland's results used to be even better.

In Finland, girls are much better at reading than boys. In no other country is the gap between girls and boys as big as in Finland.

Finnish boys no longer like reading. In the PISA study, over 60 per cent of Finnish boys say that they read only if they have to.

The PISA study tests 15-year-old pupils.

## Shape of snowflakes

![Snowflakes on a rope](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10086491)

Open image viewer

Snowflakes are different. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

It is said that all snowflakes are different. A snowflake's journey from the cloud to the ground is long. During that long journey, the snowflake takes its own unique shape.

However, American researcher Kenneth Libbrecht has succeeded in growing snowflakes in a laboratory that look exactly the same.

A snowflake contains so many water molecules that they can join together in an endless number of ways. That is why every snowflake on Earth is unique. No two alike exist in nature, Libbrecht says.
