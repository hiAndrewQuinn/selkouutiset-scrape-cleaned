# Sunday 10.5.2020 (radio)

Mother's Day is different in the corona times. Will opening schools increase corona infections? Corona infections in Finland. The coronavirus is spreading in Russia. Corona restrictions are being eased in France. The weather is getting cooler.

## Mother's Day is different in the corona times

![Finnish White Rose first class medals to be given on Mother's Day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1346,h_757,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6729155eb55579ebfe8/1588942289)

The mothers' merit badges will be handed out later, because there are corona restrictions now. Photo: Samuli Miettinen / MLL

Today Mother's Day has been celebrated.

On Mother's Day, the President of the Republic honours mothers who have excelled as raisers of children.

36 mothers will receive the honour, which is called the first class medal of the Order of the White Rose of Finland with gold rays.

They will receive the honours later because of the corona epidemic.

The President of the Republic, Sauli Niinistö, gave a speech to all mothers. He said that Mother's Day is a good time to remember that family and close communities are important.

The President reminded that many mothers live alone. In this exceptional time, new ways must be found to keep in touch.

## Will corona infections increase?

![Empty classroom at Vesilahti comprehensive school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11331639)

Open image viewer

Comprehensive school pupils will return to school next Thursday. Photo: Miikka Varila / Yle

Comprehensive school pupils will return to schools for face-to-face teaching next Thursday.

Now there is a lot of talk about whether corona infections will increase when schools open.

Virus expert, Professor Kalle Saksela does not believe that continuing school teaching will cause many corona infections.

According to statistics, it seems that only a few infections have spread through children.

Virus expert Saksela says that a child can spread the same kinds of viruses as an adult.

In Denmark, it happened that the virus started to spread more when schools were opened.

## The coronavirus is spreading quickly in Russia

![Streets were disinfected in Moscow on 24 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11324150)

Open image viewer

Streets are being disinfected in Moscow, Russia. Photo: Sergei Chirikov / EPA

The coronavirus is spreading quickly in Russia. There, the authorities announced today that over 11,000 new infections had been found.

In total, Russia has over 200,000 infections.

Russia has now recorded the fifth most infections in the world.

The most infections are in the USA. There are 1.3 million infections there.

In the whole of Europe there are 1.7 million infections in total.

## French people can go out

![mother, father and child walking in front of a metro station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5708,h_3211,x_0,y_348/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6731845eb6a69e99297/1589028659)

Open image viewer

Corona restrictions are being eased in France. Photo: Juha Nurminen

In France, people will return to normal working life tomorrow.

The ban on going outside, which lasted almost 2 months, is ending.

Many people are worried when restrictions are reduced. People are afraid that the coronavirus will spread, for example on the metro and buses, and in schools.

The weather is getting cooler and turning rainy.

During the night, a lot of rain may fall in the eastern part of the country, first water and then snow.

Tomorrow, Monday, it will be about 5 degrees warmer in Southern Finland and it will rain.

In Northern Finland it may snow.
