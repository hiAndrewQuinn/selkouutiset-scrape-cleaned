# Saturday 21.4.2018 (radio)

MP Harry Harkimo has founded a new political movement. Swedes' attitude to refugees has become stricter. The Saudi Arabian authorities have closed a women's gym in Riyadh. About 80,000 summer cottages in Finland get their electricity from solar panels.

## Harkimo founded the Liike Nyt group

![Harry Harkimo as a guest on TV1's Ykkösaamu on 21 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10171401)

MP Harry "Hjallis" Harkimo has founded a new political movement. Harkimo has left the National Coalition Party.

Harkimo said on Yle's Ykkösaamu that the name of the new movement is Liike Nyt (Movement Now). Also, for example, former SDP MP and party secretary Mikael Jungner, journalist Tuomas Enbuske and entrepreneur Alex Nieminen are involved in the movement.

Harkimo says that the movement will not necessarily put up candidates in elections, but it wants to reform the ways politics works.

## Swedes have a stricter attitude to refugees than before

![Eritrean asylum seekers in front of a plane at Luleå airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8446130)

Open image viewer

Eritrean asylum seekers in Sweden. Photo: Robert Nyholm / TT

Swedes' attitude to refugees has become stricter.

The newspaper Dagens Nyheter asked about attitudes to refugees. 41 per cent wanted Sweden to take in far fewer refugees than now. The number of opponents has doubled since 2015. Only 12 per cent of Swedes said that more refugees could be taken in than now.

44 per cent of those who answered say that immigration is mainly a good thing for Sweden. Just 3 years ago 63 per cent of those who answered thought so.

## A women's gym was closed in Riyadh

![A woman exercising on a beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9529216)

Open image viewer

A Saudi woman jogging on a beach. Photo: Antonio Guillem Fernández / AOP

The Saudi Arabian authorities have closed a women's gym in the capital Riyadh. The authorities say that the gym's advert has an indecent video.

In the video a woman trains in workout clothes with her hair down. The sports authorities say that the scenes of the video may "corrupt public morals".

Women's gyms started getting permits last year. Girls' physical education has also been increased in schools and women can get into some sports events. Saudi Arabia's 32-year-old crown prince Mohammed bin Salman has made reforms in the country which also concern the position of women.

## 80,000 cottages get electricity from the sun

![Installing solar panels.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9280688)

Open image viewer

A solar panel is being installed on a roof. Photo: Mike Nelson / EPA

About 80,000 summer cottages in Finland get their electricity from solar panels.

Non-fiction writer Janne Käpylehto says that most panels are on cottages that are not connected to the power grid. In cottages that do have electricity, the electricity bill also gets smaller if solar power helps. Rural businesses are also interested in solar power.

Solar power is used most in Southern Finland, but the sun also gives warmth in the north and in winter. Käpylehto says that a cold solar panel produces more electricity than a warm panel.
