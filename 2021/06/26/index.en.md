## Savonlinna fire

![Apartment block in flames in Savonlinna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11998467)

Open image viewer

The fire may have started from a lightning strike. Photo: Nikke Kaartinen

There has been a big fire in Savonlinna, in Eastern Finland. The fire broke out in an apartment block on Midsummer night.

The cause of the fire may have been a lightning strike. There was a strong thunderstorm in Savonlinna on Midsummer night.

No one died in the fire. Nobody was seriously injured either. However, 5 people were taken to hospital care.

All the residents of the building were moved to safety. There were not many people in the building at the time of the fire, because many were elsewhere celebrating Midsummer.

The building has 6 floors. The fire completely destroyed the upper floors. Damage was also caused to the lower floors.

The Savonlinna case is rare. It is not usual for lightning to strike an apartment block.

![Car queue at the Vaalimaa border station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_245/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82464960d310ed360fd/1624536182)

Many Finnish football fans have returned from Russia this week. Photo: Niklas Joki / Yle

Many Finns have caught coronavirus in St. Petersburg, Russia. More than 120 coronavirus infections have already been found in Finland among tourists who have been to St. Petersburg.

Many Finns went to St. Petersburg to watch football. Finland had football European Championship matches in St. Petersburg.

The National Institute for Health and Welfare hopes that everyone who has been to St. Petersburg goes for a corona test. They should go for a corona test already during this weekend.

Corona is spreading fast in Russia.

In Russia there were almost 22,000 new coronavirus infections in one day. More than 600 people died because of corona during one day.

Russia was the first country in the world to approve a coronavirus vaccine for use. However, most Russians have not taken the coronavirus vaccine.
