# Sunday 17.7.2022

Russia steps up its attack. A plane crashed in Greece. There are demonstrations in Hungary. Kalle Rovanperä won the World Rally Championship event in Estonia. The weather is dry on Monday.

## Plane crash in Greece

A plane has crashed in Greece. 8 people died in the accident. The plane left from Serbia and was carrying weapons to Bangladesh.

The plane fell in Northern Greece on Saturday evening. The plane was apparently on fire even before it fell near the town of Kavala.

In Hungary people are demonstrating against the government.

Thousands of people demonstrated against the government of Prime Minister Viktor Orbán on Saturday in Budapest. There have been demonstrations for 5 days in a row.

The people oppose the tax law. The demonstrators say that the law is bad for small entrepreneurs.

## Walrus near Kotka

![A walrus lies between rowing boats.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2400,h_1350,x_0,y_514/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98287762d28d5fc30fd/1657966128)

Open image viewer

A walrus lies between rowing boats. Photo: Police

A walrus got caught in a fisherman's gear near Kotka. This is reported by the Coast Guard.

The Coast Guard got the walrus free from the gear. The walrus at the same time capsized the fisherman's boat. The Coast Guard pulled the fisherman out of the water.

The walrus is probably the same animal that people saw earlier in Hamina. On Saturday the walrus swam back to the sea from Hamina.

Walruses are not normally found in Finland. Walruses live, for example, in the Arctic Ocean.
