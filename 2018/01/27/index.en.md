# Saturday 27.1.2018 (radio)

On Sunday people vote in the presidential election. About 100 people have died in a bomb attack in Afghanistan. The Finnish Civil War began exactly 100 years ago. This weekend people are watching birds.

## Sunday is election day

![A ballot paper for the presidential election.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10026304)

Open image viewer

Finns vote for president on Sunday. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The presidential election will be held on Sunday. In the election, a president is chosen for Finland for the next 6 years.

There are 8 candidates in the election. If one of the candidates gets more than 50 percent of the votes on Sunday, he or she will become president.

If none of the candidates gets more than 50 percent of the votes, a second round of the election will be held. The 2 candidates who get the most votes now on Sunday will go on to the second round. The election day of the second round is in 2 weeks.

Experts estimate that a second round will not be needed in this election. The current president, Sauli Niinistö, has done really well in surveys. For example, in Yle's survey more than 60 percent of those who answered said that they plan to vote for Niinistö.

## A bomb exploded in Kabul

There has been a bomb attack in Afghanistan. Perhaps about 100 people died in the attack.

The bomb exploded in the capital, Kabul. The Afghan authorities say that it was a car bomb. The bomb was perhaps hidden in an ambulance.

The extremist group Taliban has announced that it carried out the attack.

## The civil war began in January 1918

![Soldiers in a row.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10036497)

Open image viewer

Soldiers from the time of the civil war. Photo: Yle

The Finnish Civil War began exactly 100 years ago. The war began on 27 January 1918. The war ended in May of the same year.

In the civil war, Finns fought against Finns. The Reds and the Whites were on opposite sides.

Many Reds were poor. The Reds' goal was a socialist Finland.

The Whites supported Finland's bourgeois government. The Whites won the war.

Almost 40,000 Finns died in the civil war. Most of those who died were Reds. Many Reds died in prison camps after the war.

## Now it's time to spot birds

![A great tit and a willow tit eating](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5004108)

The great tit and the willow tit are happy in Finland in winter too. Photo: Yle

Many people are watching birds this weekend. The bird organisation BirdLife is organising a garden bird count.

In the garden bird count, people watch birds in their yard for an hour. After that they tell BirdLife which birds they have seen. The report can be made online.

With the event, BirdLife wants to inspire people to watch winter birds.
