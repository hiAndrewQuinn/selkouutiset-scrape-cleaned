# Tuesday 18.7.2017 (radio)

Customs has found a dangerous medicine, carfentanil, in Finland. Finnish researchers are developing a diabetes vaccine. There are big forest fires in Southern Europe. A dog helps with fear of the dentist.

## Dangerous substance found

![Customs illustration of a lethal amount of carfentanil.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9727780)

Even a small amount of carfentanil kills a person. Photo: Customs

Customs has found a dangerous medicine in Finland. The name of the medicine is carfentanil.

Carfentanil is sold on the world market as a drug, even though it is very dangerous. It is thousands of times stronger than morphine. Only about 0.03 milligrams of carfentanil kills a person.

Customs suspects that carfentanil is also sold as a drug in Finland.

Carfentanil is legally used as a veterinary medicine. It is used to stun large animals.

## A vaccine is being developed for diabetes

![Blood sugar is measured at a clinic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9706025)

Open image viewer

Diabetes raises blood sugar levels. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

Finnish researchers have developed a vaccine against diabetes, that is, sugar disease. The vaccine has been developed against type 1 diabetes. Children are the ones who suffer from it the most.

Researchers will start testing the new vaccine on people next year. The tests start at the end of 2018. If the vaccine works in people, it could come into use in about 8 years.

The new vaccine could save a lot of money. If a person gets diabetes as a child, treatment costs about one million euros in total over their whole life.

## Forest is burning in Southern Europe

![A forest burns fiercely in the village of Podstrana near the city of Split in Croatia, 18 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9728359)

Open image viewer

The forest burned near the city of Split in Croatia. Photo: AFP

There are big forest fires in Southern Europe. Forests are burning in many countries. There are big fires at least in Croatia, Montenegro, Italy and Portugal.

In Croatia the fire came very close to the big city of Split. Outside the centre, the fire was already burning houses and cars. In the end the firefighters got the fires under control. The fires were put out with the help of aeroplanes, for example.

Also in Montenegro, which neighbours Croatia, the fires are big. Montenegro asked other countries for help in putting out the fires.

## A dog helps with fear of the dentist

![Dog Mimmi helps people who are afraid of the dentist.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9704447)

Open image viewer

Mimmi the dog helps people who are afraid of the dentist. Photo: Juha Hintsala / Yle

Are you afraid of the dentist? You are not the only one. 10 percent of patients are very afraid of going to the dentist. It can even prevent teeth from being treated and cause health problems.

In Oulu a new help for fear of the dentist has been developed. It is a dog. Dogs that have been tested and trained help the dentist's patients.

A dentist's patient calms down when a dog is a companion. A person's heart beats more slowly when they stroke a dog. Stress decreases. In this way, going to the dentist works, even if the patient is afraid of the dentist's chair.

Oulu may be the first place in the world where it is being studied how a dog helps with fear of the dentist.
