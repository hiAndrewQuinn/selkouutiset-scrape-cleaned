## Third corona vaccination

![Pfizer corona vaccine vials](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8373466104e6574674d/1627711184)

The Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, has issued guidance on a 3rd corona vaccination.

THL's guidance to municipalities is that a 3rd corona vaccination is offered to immunocompromised people. Immunocompromised people include, for example, many cancer patients. They do not get enough protection against corona from 2 corona vaccines like others do.

Municipalities can start the 3rd vaccination round for immunocompromised people straight away, if they have enough corona vaccines.

The Helsinki region and the Turku region have already announced that they will start the 3rd vaccination round for immunocompromised people.

## Vaccinations for nurses

![Nurse in a corridor, New Children's Hospital, Helsinki, 8.7.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4974,h_2798,x_468,y_417/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5868905d68c5ca6c94f/1613564915)

In Finland people are talking about vaccinations for nurses.

The director general of the Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, Markku Tervahauta, says that corona vaccination must be made compulsory for nurses who care for risk groups.

Nurses already have to have some vaccinations today if they care for elderly people, for example. Now THL is investigating whether corona vaccination should also be added to the list.

In the view of THL's director general, compulsory corona vaccination for nurses is necessary. However, the nurses' trade unions disagree.

Sari Viinikainen, chair of the nurses' union Tehy, says that nurses understand the importance of vaccination even without compulsion.

Police investigations into the violent incident in Turku have progressed. The violent incident happened at an elderly care unit in Turku in early September.

Police still suspect that the perpetrator was a male resident of the elderly care unit. The man was moved elsewhere after the act.

1 person died in the case and 1 person received hospital treatment. Both were female residents of the elderly care unit.

The police will give more information about the case later.
