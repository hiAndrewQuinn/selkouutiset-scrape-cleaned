## Climate change and Paulig coffee

Then from the coffee market.

The effects of climate change are becoming more widespread and are affecting coffee yields. This has forced coffee giant Paulig to change its operations.

About 5 years ago, Paulig decided to make a turn in the food industry.

The company has acquired several companies from other parts of Europe. It is now the European market leader in Tex-Mex food products. The majority of its turnover is now food

Paulig coffee roastery was founded in 1876 in Helsinki.

Paulig still produces up to one hundred million coffee packets a year at its Vuosaari roastery. However, the coffee business has been unpredictable because climate change has affected the coffee harvest.

## Coast Guards in First Aid

Still from nursing.

Coast Guards have been working as paramedics in the Archipelago Sea this summer. Coast Guards have been assisting the welfare area with first aid tasks.

There have been about 30 missions. Coast Guards have been the first to arrive at summer cottages in 10 cases.

The goal of the collaboration is to get help to the destination as quickly as possible.

The Coast Guard has previously provided similar assistance in the Gulf of Finland.

Coastguards have been working as paramedics this summer. They have even been trained to deliver babies.
