# Tuesday 13.11.2018 (radio)

Diesel tax. Olympic Games. A warship. Veijo Vanamo.

## Support for abolishing the diesel tax

![Diesel car](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10506339)

Open image viewer

Diesel cars have many supporters. Photo: Christian Ohde / AOP

In Finland it is proposed that the diesel tax be abolished. A citizens' initiative to abolish the diesel tax was submitted to Parliament yesterday. Today, by the afternoon, the initiative already had over 90,000 signatures. That means Parliament will take the citizens' initiative up for consideration.

Behind the initiative is the fact that a litre of diesel can now cost even more than ordinary petrol at a petrol station. Ordinary 95 petrol used to be clearly more expensive than diesel. That is why many people have wanted to own a diesel car in Finland. Now people are wondering why the prices are almost the same or have even turned the other way round.

An expert from the Finnish oil company Neste says that the high price of diesel is linked to diesel stocks around the world. There is little diesel in stock now. The price is rising because a lot of diesel is bought when the economy is doing well. Diesel is used by lorries and vans, for example.

## Finland is looking into the Olympics

![1952. Olympic flame in the tower of the Stadium, with the Exhibition Hall in the foreground at the corner of Mannerheimintie and Toivonkatu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9690634)

Open image viewer

In 1952 the Olympic flame burned in the Stadium tower in Helsinki. Photo: Dilén Börje / Helsinki City Museum

In Finland it is being examined whether the Olympic Games and the Paralympic Games could be held here. The Finnish Olympic Committee is studying what bidding for the Olympics means nowadays.

The matter was decided to be investigated because the international requirements for organising the Olympics have changed a lot. In future, for example, the aim is that the Olympics are environmentally friendly events.

The study is supposed to say whether bidding for the Olympics would be sensible for Finland.

## A warship sank in Norway

![KNM Helge Ingstad sinking near Bergen in Norway on 13 November.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10505734)

Open image viewer

The sunken warship is over 130 metres long. Photo: Marit Hommedal / Scanpix / AFP

A warship that collided with an oil tanker has sunk in Norway. The warship collided with the tanker north of Bergen last week. There were 137 crew members on the warship when the collision happened. They were rescued from the ship.

The warship was run aground in shallow water. However, the vessel has slid quite deep. Only the mast and a small part of the ship's deck are visible. The warship is fairly new. The Norwegian navy says that the vessel is still to be raised.

## Veijo Vanamo has died

![Veijo Vanamo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10505221)

Open image viewer

Veijo Vanamo was one of Finland's first TV chefs. Photo: Yle

The famous Finnish TV chef Veijo Vanamo has died. Veijo Vanamo became famous from the TV2 programme Patakakkonen. In it, various Finnish dishes were cooked and the healthiness of food was discussed.

Patakakkonen was very popular. At one time it had a mass audience of millions.

Veijo Vanamo lived to be 91 years old.
