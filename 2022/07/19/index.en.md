## Uniper

![Minister for European Affairs and Ownership Steering Tytti Tuppurainen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5471,h_3077,x_0,y_198/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98381362d679087ed13/1658223037)

Minister Tytti Tuppurainen does not promise more money to Uniper. Photo: Sakari Piippo / Yle

Uniper's financial problems have been discussed in Parliament.

Uniper is a German energy company, but its biggest owner is the Finnish Fortum. Fortum belongs to the Finnish state.

Minister Tytti Tuppurainen told Parliament's Finance Committee about Uniper's situation on Tuesday. Tuppurainen said that Finland will no longer give money to Uniper. Fortum has already helped Uniper with 8 billion euros.

Uniper's problems are due to the price of gas. Uniper previously passed on cheap gas from Russia to Germany. Because of the war in Ukraine, Uniper now has to obtain more expensive gas from elsewhere.

However, Uniper's customers pay the same amount for gas as before. That is why Uniper loses tens of millions of euros every day.

## Walrus

![A walrus lies on the grass.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3489,x_0,y_385/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98381062d67546cdc29/1670403800)

Open image viewer

On Tuesday the walrus rested in a yard in Kotka. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

The walrus's adventure in Finland continues. On Tuesday it was found again in Kotka.

The walrus rested and gathered strength in a house's yard. Helpers are considering whether the walrus will be moved to the Korkeasaari wildlife hospital.

The walrus came to Finland last Friday. It perhaps got lost and swam many hundreds of kilometres through the Danish straits to Finland.

The walrus interests Finns, because walruses do not live in Finland. The visitor has swum in the Gulf of Finland in the Hamina and Kotka area.

The walrus's adventure has also been followed in the news and in newspapers.

Intense heat continues in Europe.

In Britain a temperature over 40 degrees has now been measured for the first time. At Heathrow airport the temperature on Tuesday was 40.2 degrees.

One reason for the intense heat is climate change. Because of climate change heat waves are stronger than before and they last longer.

## The weather in Finland

The weather in Finland is pleasantly warm.

The temperature on Wednesday is between 20 and 25 degrees in a large part of the country. In Lapland it is a little cooler.

On Wednesday the weather also clears up. It no longer rains.

From Thursday it will be a sunny day. The temperature rises above the heat limit, that is, over 25 degrees, in almost the whole of Finland.
