# Wednesday 24.7.2019 (radio)

There has been a shooting in Helsinki. The politician and writer Claes Andersson has died. The Finnish forest company UPM is building a large pulp mill in Uruguay, South America. It is very hot in different parts of Europe.

## The Helsinki shooting

![Police have closed the junction where a red car stands, with a dented side and broken side windows.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10890790)

There has been a shooting in Helsinki.

The incident was on Tuesday. The police have arrested a 32-year-old man because of the incident.

The man shot at 3 people. 2 of them were injured and had to go to hospital.

The police say that it was a dispute. The shooter knew the people he shot at.

The shooting happened in central Helsinki. There were many people on the street, because it was about 4 pm and it was rush hour. That is why many people were in danger, the police say.

## Claes Andersson has died

![Claes Andersson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10891224)

Open image viewer

Claes Andersson 1937- 2019 Photo: Markku Ojala / AOP

The politician and writer Claes Andersson has died. Andersson was 82 years old. He had been ill for a long time.

Andersson was the leader of the Left Alliance in the 1990s. He was also Finland's Minister of Culture.

Claes Andersson was also known in the arts. He wrote novels and poems. He also played jazz music.

Claes Andersson was trained as a doctor and psychiatrist. He spoke up for people with mental health problems, alcoholics and prisoners.

## UPM to build a new factory in Uruguay

![Artist's impression of the new factory in Uruguay.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10890816)

Open image viewer

The Finnish company UPM is building a factory like this in Uruguay. Photo: UPM

The Finnish forest company UPM is building a large pulp mill in Uruguay, South America. UPM already has one pulp mill in Uruguay.

The new factory costs 2.4 billion euros. It is much bigger than Finland's pulp mills.

Pulp mills make pulp from wood, which is needed, for example, for making cardboard and paper. Products that replace plastic are also made from pulp.

Pulp is bought in large amounts around the world. UPM believes that the price of pulp will be high in the future.

UPM also says that a lot of forest has been planted in Uruguay. That is why there is enough wood for the pulp mills.

## A very hot summer

![Young people jump into the sea in the city of Saint-Malo, in north-western France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10891821)

The summer is now very hot in different parts of Europe. For example, in France and Germany it has been about 40 degrees.

The heat has affected Europeans' lives in many ways. For example, in the Netherlands some day-care centres have been closed because of the heat.

It is also hot here in Finland, that is, the temperature is over 25 degrees. Yle meteorologist Joonas Koskela says that Saturday may be the hottest day of the week. Then it will be over 30 degrees warm in different parts of Finland.
