The police have had a lot of tasks in different parts of Finland. The warm summer weekend has increased public order disturbances.

Corona restrictions have been reduced and people have started to move around more again. Public order disturbances have increased in public places, for example in parks and at beaches.

Last year public order disturbances increased in homes, but now they have moved outside again.

## Event guarantees have been granted to many events

![Audience at a Years & Years concert at Ruisrock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5593,h_3146,x_0,y_542/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4971175b4093523cef7/1530958735)

Open image viewer

An event organiser can get compensation from the event guarantee if the event is cancelled because of corona. Photo: Juuli Aschan, YleX

Finland has now granted event guarantees to event organisers worth about 13 million euros. The application period began in early June.

The most guarantee applications have been made for music festivals, concerts and exhibitions. 30 guarantee applications have come in. A guarantee has already been granted to 20 applicants.

An event guarantee means that the state pays back event costs if the event has to be cancelled because of corona restrictions.

## Corona times have worsened swimming skills

![Swimming school in the children's pool of a swimming hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5371,h_3021,x_0,y_277/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6869795ede2ee10b1b7/1591619698)

Open image viewer

Children at swimming school. Children like to swim. Photo: Paula Collin / Yle

Corona times have worsened children's swimming skills. For example, swimming lessons at school have been cancelled during the corona times in many schools. That is why many children have not had swimming lessons for 2 years.

In many municipalities, swimming schools have also been cut because of corona or because of savings.

The Finnish Swimming Teaching and Life Saving Association says that an important age for learning to swim is about 7 years. At about 7 years old, children learn to swim well if they get to practise swimming.

About 25 percent of 12-year-old children cannot swim well.

## Remember to drink in the heat

![Water is poured from a tap into a glass.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4908,h_2761,x_0,y_1012/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5937225d8b1b5baade8/1711011495)

Open image viewer

Water is the best drink in the heat. Mineral water is also a good drink. Photo: Mostphotos

In many areas of Finland there is now heat. On Sunday 31 degrees was measured in Nurmijärvi in Uusimaa.

The National Institute for Health and Welfare reminds that it is important to drink water in the heat. In hot weather a lot of fluid evaporates from the human body.

On a hot day it is good to drink about 2 litres of water. You can also drink mineral water, if you like bubbles in the water.
