# Sunday 4.9.2016 (radio)

The Prime Minister trusts the courts on asylum decisions. Norway puts a fence at a border station. Mother Teresa becomes a saint. Heavy rain in Espoo.

## Prime Minister: the court will correct a wrong asylum decision

![Juha Sipilä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9114054)

Open image viewer

According to the Prime Minister, a wrong asylum decision can be corrected in court.  Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

In Finland, people are now talking a lot about asylum decisions for immigrants.

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä says that the government has not told the Immigration Service to make asylum decisions too quickly.

Helsingin Sanomat reported today that the workers at the Immigration Service are under heavy work pressure. The workers tell the paper that they probably make mistakes in asylum decisions because they are in such a hurry.

Sipilä admitted on the radio today that the Immigration Service now has really a lot of work as it makes asylum decisions for immigrants. But according to Sipilä, the government does not want decisions that have been made badly because of the hurry. Sipilä trusts that a wrong decision will be corrected when it is appealed to the court.

The workers at the agency also believe that they are expected to make negative asylum decisions. Prime Minister Juha Sipilä wonders where such an idea has come from.

## Norway builds a fence at a border station with Russia

![Fence builders](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9143048)

Open image viewer

Norway's border fence is 200 metres long.  Photo: NRK Finnmakr

Norway is building a fence on the Russian border. The steel fence is being built at the Storskog border station in Northern Norway. It is the only place where you can cross the border between Norway and Russia.

The Norwegian authorities say that the fence is being built because last autumn over 5000 asylum seekers came to Norway at Storskog. Many of them had come from crisis countries in the Middle East.

Norway now wants to improve security at the Storskog border crossing. The fence is 200 metres long and 3.5 metres high.

## Mother Teresa is now a saint

![A nun decorates a picture of Mother Teresa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9126286)

Open image viewer

Mother Teresa was a nun who helped the poor in Calcutta.  Photo: Imago / AOP

The leader of the Catholic Church, Pope Francis, has declared Mother Teresa a holy person, that is, a saint.

Mother Teresa was a nun who helped poor people in Calcutta, India, who had no home. She was famous all over the world. She received the Nobel Peace Prize in 1979.

Mother Teresa was declared a saint in the Vatican, in St. Peter's Square. An event was held there, with tens of thousands of people.

## Heavy rain fell in the south

![SINGLE USE RIGHTS Heavy rain in Espoo on 3 September 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9143027)

Open image viewer

Water flooded footpaths in Espoo.  Photo: Saara Torikka

Last night almost a month's worth of rain fell in Espoo. According to the Finnish Meteorological Institute, over 60 millimetres of rain fell in Espoo in less than a day.

There was also heavy rain elsewhere in Uusimaa and in Kymenlaakso.  In Porvoo and Virolahti, over 50 millimetres of rain fell.
