## Many people work and get unemployment benefit

![North Karelia Central Hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10108475)

Open image viewer

Gig work is common in the care sector, where most workers are women. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

More and more unemployed people do gig work and part-time work. Working while on unemployment benefit has become more common in the 2010s.

Working while unemployed is especially common in certain fields where most workers are women, such as care work, schools and services. In 2014 a protected amount was introduced. An unemployed person can earn 300 euros a month without it affecting their benefits.

A study says that working while unemployed has increased regardless of the protected amount.

## Finns trust their co-workers

![A man at a computer and a robot hand in the foreground](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10166258)

Open image viewer

About 30 percent of Finns would be happy to work with a robot. Photo: BSIP SA / AOP / Yle

79 percent of Finns trust their own workplace and co-workers. A survey says that people trust their co-workers more than their own boss. Almost 90 percent of those who answered trust their co-workers. 74 percent trust their boss.

56 percent believe that their employer talks about things openly. 36 percent believe that their boss holds back information. About 30 percent of Finns are ready to work with a robot.

The study was done by the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment.

## Problems with Kela taxis may lead to sanctions

![The Kela logo in a window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10286680)

Open image viewer

Kela is giving the weekend to get Kela rides working. After that, service centres may be given sanctions if the rides do not go smoothly. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Kela plans to impose sanctions on service centres that cannot arrange Kela rides. Kela says that the rides must be working properly during this weekend. The sanctions may be thousands of euros.

The problem is connected to the tendering of taxi rides and the opening up of taxi competition. The taxi has not always arrived, or people have had to wait many hours for a ride. Sometimes it has not been possible to get through to the booking centre at all.

The reforms came into force at the start of July.

## The Pori Housing Fair has opened

![Housing fair houses on a riverbank.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10290699)

Open image viewer

Karjaranta, the Pori Housing Fair area, is right on the bank of the Kokemäenjoki river. Photo: Liisa Kallio / Yle

The Housing Fair has opened in Karjaranta in Pori, Satakunta. The residential area has been built on an old industrial area. It shows modern urban living in small houses.

The themes, which change every week, are life in Pori, neighbourliness, the future and a good everyday life. This year's special feature is a service home for older people.

## A baby's gaze can tell about sociability

![A baby in a parent's arms](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10203122)

Open image viewer

A baby's gaze can tell about social development. Photo: AOP

A baby who is interested in human faces may be socially gifted.

A study at the University of Tampere included over 200 babies. The study says that children who liked to watch other people's faces at 7 months were usually sociable at 2 years. Development was assessed, for example, by measuring eye movements.

This information may be important when trying to help a child who has difficulties in social development.
