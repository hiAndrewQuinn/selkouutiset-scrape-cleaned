## Blinken's visit

![Antony Blinken.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5110,h_2874,x_10,y_88/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11235636479956996ccd/1685689889)

US Foreign Minister Antony Blinken in Helsinki. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

US Foreign Minister Antony Blinken has visited Finland.

Blinken spoke in Helsinki. Blinken said that Finland's NATO membership is an important change.

Finland decided to join the defence alliance NATO after Russia attacked Ukraine.

Blinken said that Finns understood that Russia may also attack Finland.

In his speech Blinken used the Finnish idea of grit. Grit means will and determination, Blinken said.

With grit, Finns got through when Russia attacked Finland about 80 years ago. Now Finns see the same grit in the fight of Ukrainians, Blinken said.

Foreign Minister Blinken discussed in Helsinki with Finland's Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Blinken and Haavisto said that the countries' support for Ukraine will continue.

## Documentary series about Marin's government

![Anna-Maja Henriksson, Annika Saarikko, Sanna Marin, Maria Ohisalo and Li Andersson stand side by side and look at the camera.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5919,h_3329,x_0,y_518/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11017176440ebaf7ec0b/1682009643)

HBO Max made a documentary series about the party leaders of Marin's government. Photo: Pressbild / HBO Max

A documentary series has been made about Prime Minister Sanna Marin's government. It tells about the leaders of the government parties.

In Marin's government there are 5 parties, and all of them have women leaders. The documentary series is called Five Chosen.

The documentary's director Mia Halme has got close to politicians. The series shows, for example, how Prime Minister Marin cries because of the suffering of Ukrainians.

The documentary series about Finnish women ministers can be seen on HBO Max in Finland and abroad.

## Record number of seal pups

![Saimaa ringed seal, a pup on the ice.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3233,h_1819,x_0,y_384/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5607885cd01836ed594/1681561693)

Open image viewer

A pup is the young of the Saimaa ringed seal. Photo: EPA

A record number of Saimaa ringed seal pups have been born this year in the Lake Saimaa area.

Metsähallitus has counted that there are now 100 pups in Saimaa. That is more than has ever been counted before.

Last winter there was enough snow for the Saimaa ringed seals to build a den. The Saimaa ringed seal gives birth to its pups in a den that it builds in a snowdrift on the lake's ice.

The Saimaa ringed seal is a rare animal. Saimaa ringed seals live only in the Lake Saimaa area in Eastern Finland.

It has snowed in Lappeenranta. Usually Lappeenranta is one of Finland's sunny and warm places.

However, today, Friday, several centimetres of snow fell in Lappeenranta. The snowfall lasted 3 hours.
