# Saturday 5.3.2022 (radio)

Finland is strengthening cooperation with the USA. Finland is also increasing cooperation with Sweden. China censors the Paralympics opening speech.

## Niinistö and Biden

![Sauli Niinistö and Joe Biden at their meeting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3749,h_2108,x_883,y_1304/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9241616223039e48840/1646463131)

Sauli Niinistö and Joe Biden at their meeting. Photo: Sarah Silbiger / Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö has visited the USA. Late on Friday Niinistö met US President Joe Biden in Washington. The presidents talked about Russia's attack on Ukraine.

After the meeting Niinistö said that Finland and the USA will cooperate more than before. The countries will increase defence cooperation.

Sweden, too, is increasing cooperation with the USA.

## Finland and Sweden

![Sanna Marin and Magdalena Andersson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3455,h_1943,x_613,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-92421662234e1ba85a0/1646481011)

Finland is also doing more cooperation with Sweden. Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that Finland and Sweden will also cooperate more than before with NATO.

Sweden's prime minister and defence minister visited Finland. Sweden's Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson and Defence Minister Peter Hultqvist met Prime Minister Sanna Marin and President Sauli Niinistö.

At the meetings they talked about Russia's attack on Ukraine. Finland and Sweden condemn Russia's attack.

## Paralympics

![Andrew Parsons during the opening speech of the Beijing Winter Paralympics.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8634,h_4856,x_1,y_601/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-924181622321be69385/1646469772)

Open image viewer

Andrew Parsons during the opening speech of the Beijing Winter Paralympics. Photo: Bob Martin / AOP

The Beijing Paralympics opening ceremony was on Friday evening.

China censored the opening speech of the Games on television. People in China did not hear it when the chair of the International Paralympic Committee, Andrew Parsons, condemned the war in Ukraine.

In the live television broadcast Parsons' words were not translated into Chinese. The voice of the speech also came on television more quietly than the rest of the broadcast.

On Chinese television the live broadcast is not quite live, so that censorship has time to act. The authority removes from programmes the parts that it does not want people to see.

Para-athletes compete in the Paralympics. Finland's team at the Beijing Paralympics has 6 athletes and 1 guide skier. The athletes take part in snowboarding, slalom and cross-country skiing.
