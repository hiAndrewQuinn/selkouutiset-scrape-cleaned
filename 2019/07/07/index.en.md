# Sunday 7.7.2019 (radio)

A new parliament is being elected in Greece. Many pensioners are working. Less hormone treatment is used for menopause than before. On Sunday the Open Gardens Day was celebrated.

## Greek election

![A woman leaves a voting booth in Athens on Sunday 7 July 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10866034)

Greeks are choosing a new parliament. Photo: Orestis Panagiotou / EPA

Greeks voted in the election on Sunday. A new parliament is being elected in Greece.

Opinion polls predict that the Greek government will change after the election. The current Greek government is led by the left-wing Syriza party.

Syriza has had less support in opinion polls than the right-wing New Democracy party. It is possible that the right will win the election.

The right-wing New Democracy has promised to cut Greeks' taxes. Many Greeks are tired of having less money than before because of taxes and government savings.

Greece's economic situation has been bad for a long time. Greece's problem is that the state has a huge amount of debt.

## Pensioners at work

![A nurse and a patient in a hospital corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9279254)

Open image viewer

There is also plenty of work for pensioners in the care sector. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Many Finns do not retire as soon as they are able to. For example, many teachers, doctors and managers continue working for longer than they have to.

Some also work in retirement, that is, they receive a pension and a salary. Pensioners work, for example, in the transport sector and the care sector.

Pensioners often do not work for the money. They want to have something sensible to do in life.

## Menopause treatment

![A woman puts a hormone patch on her skin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9838940)

Open image viewer

Hormones can also be given with a hormone patch. Photo: AOP

Treatment of women's menopause has changed. Less hormone is used in treatment today than before.

Helsinki Women's Clinic says that hormone doses have become smaller. Small doses also help many women.

The amount of hormones is to be reduced, because there are risks in hormone treatment. For example, the risk of breast cancer may increase with some hormone treatments.

Menopause usually begins at about the age of 50. In Finland more hormones are used for menopause treatment than in other Nordic countries.

## Open gardens

![Pollinators on a dahlia flower, Eija's Garden](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10330227)

Open image viewer

Insects are also part of a sustainable garden. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

On Sunday the Open Gardens Day was celebrated. The idea of the event is that people can see different gardens. This year more than 400 gardens took part in the event.

The theme of the event this year was the sustainable garden. A sustainable garden grows many different plants, including wild plants. That is why insects, for example, also thrive in a sustainable garden.
