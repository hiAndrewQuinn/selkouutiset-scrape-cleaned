Changes in the bird flu virus have been found on 2 new fur farms. Changed virus has now been found on 5 fur farms in total. The Food Authority says that

the changes help the virus to multiply in mammals. However, the changes do not help the virus to spread to people.

The Food Authority has ordered that all minks must be put down on farms where there is bird flu.

Bird flu has been found on 24 fur farms in total.

## Early childhood education for immigrant children

![Children playing in the yard of Puropuisto day-care centre in Nokia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4494,h_2527,x_0,y_421/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10295396364f3562c474/1667560470)

Open image viewer

Early childhood education can improve the integration of the whole immigrant family, the government believes. Photo: Antti Eintola / Yle

The government wants immigrant children to go to day-care centres at a younger age than now. The government thinks that it supports the integration of the whole family.

In multicultural families it is common for a child to be looked after at home until about the age of 3. The reason may be cultural differences or a parent's poor work situation.

Many immigrant families want the child to learn the family's own language first and only then go to a day-care centre.

In many cultures it is also thought that relatives look after children.

In Helsinki, 78 percent of under-6-year-olds who speak a foreign language were in early childhood education. Among those who speak a home language, 84 percent were in day-care centres.

## Online shopping fell

![Lockers at a post office or online shop pick-up point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90615261ef29a5cbc80/1692087785)

Open image viewer

Online shopping has fallen a lot this year. Photo: Hanna Othman / Yle

Domestic online shopping has collapsed in spring and summer. There has been a drop of 24 percent since 2021.

Housing and food have become more expensive, and less money is left for online shopping than before.

The Finnish Commerce Federation says that domestic online shopping grew for more than 10 years. Sales already fell 12 percent last year.

## New invasive species

![Giant goldenrod in flower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3872,h_2178,x_0,y_206/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5550845cade6c0333a4/1692082448)

Open image viewer

Giant goldenrod is one species that is now on Finland's list of invasive alien species. Photo: Mostphotos/LianeM

7 new invasive alien species have been added to the list of harmful invasive alien species.

Three goldenrods were added to the list of plant species: Canada goldenrod, tall goldenrod and giant goldenrod. Also added to the list were sycamore maple, false spiraea and white meadowsweet.

There is also a new animal in the invasive species, the black-headed slug.

Harmful invasive species threaten the biodiversity of nature.
