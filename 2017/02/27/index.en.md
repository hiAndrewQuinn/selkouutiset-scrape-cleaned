# Monday 27.2.2017 (radio)

Farmers are weighed down by exhaustion and money worries. Parties are finding it difficult to find candidates for the municipal elections. Nokia-brand phones are returning to shops. The Oscars for the best films were handed out in the USA.

## Farmers have money worries and exhaustion

![Tractor march](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9481828)

Open image viewer

Farmers made a tractor march to Helsinki last spring. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Many farmers have difficulties with money and with coping.

The farmers' money worries and exhaustion have been revealed in a project that the Farmers' Social Insurance Institution Mela started at the beginning of this year. The project has already helped over 100 farmers get therapy and financial advice.

The helpers say that the farmers' difficulties may be even bigger than in the 1990s, when Finland had a big recession. Statistics also show that the profitability of farms has weakened for 5 years in a row.

## Council work does not attract people

![Municipal election ads at the edge of the market square in Rovaniemi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6335316)

Open image viewer

In 2012 it was easier to find candidates for the municipal elections than now. Photo: Hillevi Antikainen / Yle

The municipal elections are threatened by a shortage of candidates. Clearly fewer candidates are now running for municipal councils than in the previous elections in 2012. The big parties especially have a shortage of candidates, Kuntalehti reports.

There are many reasons for the shortage of candidates. Many people say that they do not have time for council work. The social and health care reform and the regional reform also cause uncertainty, says Marianne Pekola-Sjöblom, research manager at the Association of Finnish Municipalities.

The nomination of candidates for the municipal elections ends on Tuesday. The municipal elections are held on 9 April. In the municipal elections, council members are chosen for the next 4 years.

## Nokia phones return to shops

![The Nokia 3310 retro model.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9482599)

Open image viewer

Nokia's old favourite, the 3310 phone, now looks like this. Photo: Quique Garcia / EPA

The Finnish phone maker HMD Global has announced new Nokia-brand phones.

The company has presented 3 Nokia-brand smartphones and also the old familiar 3310 model, which was really popular at the start of the 2000s.

The new phones will go on sale in over 100 countries, including Finland.

Although the phones are Nokia-brand, Nokia does not make them. Nokia gave up the mobile phone business in 2014.

The company HMD Global has the right to use the Nokia trademark for 10 years. The phones are made by the Taiwanese company Foxconn.

## La La Land grabbed Oscars

![Damien Chazelle won the Oscar for best directing for the film La La Land.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9481851)

Open image viewer

Damien Chazelle won the best director Oscar for the film La La Land. Photo: AOP

The film industry's Oscars have been handed out in the USA.

The musical film La La Land won the most awards, 6 Oscars in total.

A rare mistake happened at the gala when the best film of the year was awarded. First it was announced that the winner is La La Land. Then it turned out that the award envelopes had been mixed up and the real winner was the film Moonlight. Moonlight won 3 Oscars in total.

The award for best non-English-language film went to The Salesman, directed by Iranian Asghar Farhadi.
