# Saturday 6.8.2022

Minister of Finance Annika Saarikko defends the extra child benefit. Israel strikes Gaza. There was a serious bus accident in Croatia. Assembly turns 30. The storm has caused damage in Eastern Finland and Kainuu.

TV's Clear News has a summer break 6.6.–12.8.

## An extra child benefit

![Annika Saarikko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_364/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99095062ece85b1e436/1664448175)

Minister of Finance Annika Saarikko. Photo: Sakari Piippo / Yle

Money minister Annika Saarikko still says that it is worth paying an extra child benefit to all families. Saarikko already proposed earlier that families get 2 child benefits in December.

Saarikko's proposal has been criticised. In some people's view the extra child benefit does not need to be paid to families who have good incomes.

Annika Saarikko now says that child benefit has been paid for decades to all families. In Saarikko's view the system cannot be changed quickly.

## Israel

People hold up Israeli flags. Illustrative image from May. Photo: Atef Safadi / EPA

Israel has carried out strikes in the Gaza area. At least 11 people have died and 80 people have been wounded in Israel's strikes. Israel began the strikes on Friday.

Israel is striking against Islamic Jihad. Israel says that Islamic Jihad is a terrorist organisation that is supported by Iran.

Israel also says that Islamic Jihad is constantly firing rockets from Gaza into Israel.

## Bus accident in Croatia

There has been a serious bus accident in Croatia. At least 12 people died and over 30 were injured, when a bus drove off the main road.

The accident happened about 50 kilometres north of the Croatian capital Zagreb. The bus was Polish.

Croatian television reports that the bus driver apparently fell asleep.

## Mushrooms

![Maart Piitulainen, chair of the Turku Mushroom Society, with a mushroom basket in hand in Ruissalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_76/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99108462ed0bf355e2e/1659702349)

Open image viewer

Maart Piitulainen, chair of the Turku Mushroom Society, with a mushroom basket in hand in Ruissalo. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

There are now a lot of mushrooms in the forest.

Especially chanterelles are more numerous than usual in the forests of Southern and Central Finland in early August. Instead, there are fewer boletes in the forest than usual.

The weather has been good for mushrooms. It is good for mushrooms when it is warm at times and rains at other times.

Many mushrooms are in the forest most in September.

## The storm

![An aspen felled by the storm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_52/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82127660c896d79c576/1623758761)

There has been a storm in Eastern Finland and Kainuu. At least in North Karelia and Kainuu trees have fallen. Electricity has been cut off in many homes.

On Sunday in the daytime the weather is dry and in places clear. In some places, however, a little rain falls on Sunday.
