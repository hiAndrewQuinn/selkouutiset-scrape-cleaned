# Friday 12.4.2019 (radio)

Journalist Johanna Vehkoo has been fined for offending Junes Lokka's honour. 3 people died in a fire in Lapland. Purchases of 50 euros can now be paid by contactless payment. Finns drank 10 kilos of coffee per person last year. The weather is dry and fine at the weekend.

## Fines for defamation

![Junes Lokka, Martina Kronström and Johanna Vehkoo at the Oulu District Court](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10698141)

Johanna Vehkoo (right) and Junes Lokka (left) in the courtroom. Photo: Timo Heikkala / Lehtikuva

The court has ordered a punishment for journalist Johanna Vehkoo. Vehkoo has to pay a fine, because she has offended Junes Lokka's honour.

Junes Lokka is a city councillor in Oulu. Lokka opposes immigration. Vehkoo wrote on Facebook that Lokka is a racist and a Nazi. Lokka got angry and took the matter to court.

Vehkoo thinks it is wrong that the court ordered her to pay a fine. Vehkoo intends to appeal the punishment. That means the handling of the case continues.

At the same time Junes Lokka has himself been charged with a crime. Lokka may have committed a crime when he uploaded a video to YouTube and Facebook. The matter will be dealt with in court, because the video may insult immigrants and Muslims.

## Fire in Kittilä

![A cabin fire is being put out in Levi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10736140)

A fire is being put out in Levi in Kittilä. Photo: Lapland Police

There has been a serious fire in Lapland. The fire was in Levi in Kittilä, where there is also a winter sports centre.

3 people died in the fire. They were Finnish tourists.

The fire broke out on Friday morning after 3 o'clock. The fire burned a log cabin with 2 floors.

## Contactless payment limit

![Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10735159)

Open image viewer

No PIN code is needed for contactless payment. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Purchases of up to 50 euros can now be paid with a bank card by contactless payment. Until now a customer has not been able to use contactless payment if the purchase cost more than 25 euros.

Contactless payment means that the customer does not need to type in a PIN code. They just show the bank card to the payment terminal.

However, shops must update the payment terminal, so that the payment terminal accepts the new 50 euro limit for contactless payment. Some shops have not yet updated their payment terminals.

## Finns' favourite drink

![Coffee cup in hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9797116)

Open image viewer

A coffee cup belongs in a Finnish hand. Photo: Pexels / Pixabay

Finns drink a lot of coffee. In 2018 each Finn consumed 10 kilos of coffee. That is 3 percent more than in 2017.

Finns drink the most filter coffee. There are many different coffees in shops nowadays, but light-roast filter coffee is the Finns' favourite drink.

## Spring weather

The weather at the weekend is dry, that is, no rain falls.

On Saturday the temperature rises above 5 degrees in a large part of the country. On Sunday it can be almost 10 degrees warmer in the west.

Also next week the weather is warm. In the south the temperature can be 15 degrees.
