# Wednesday 15.2.2023 (radio)

The transport sector strike has begun. The transport sector strike affects many matters. Afghan women are granted refugee status in Finland. Nato countries are sending more weapons to Ukraine. Weather forecast.

## Transport sector strike

![Sunny Vuosaari harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_66/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107314863ecc16f7ad38/1676460582)

The harbour strike stops exports and imports. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

A strike has begun in the car and transport sector. The strike began on Wednesday morning, because no agreement was reached in the pay negotiations on Tuesday.

The harbour strike almost completely stops Finland's exports and imports. The value of exports and imports is about 400 million euros a day. That is why the strike affects Finland's economy.

The industry organisation Teknologiateollisuus is angry about the car and transport sector strike. An agreement has already been made for the technology industry sectors, but the transport sector strike hurts the work of the technology industry a lot.

## Effects of the strike

![A rubbish bin is full of used grave candles in Asikkala. In the background a winter cemetery.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4027,h_2266,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105479263b298c7d13dd/1672649015)

The car and transport sector strike affects many things. For example, in the harbours ships are not loaded or unloaded. That is why the strike is also visible in shops.

For example, oranges or bananas may run out of shops, because they are imported from abroad.

The movement of post and parcels may slow down. In the Helsinki region the transport of waste does not work normally. That is why the bins at houses may fill up.

## Afghan refugees

![Afghan women students protest against the Taliban university ban in Kabul, Afghanistan, on 21.12.2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_567/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105589563b5a93233cfc/1672850987)

Open image viewer

The position of women has worsened in Afghanistan. Photo: STELLA Pictures/ddp/abaca press

The Finnish Immigration Service has a new line for Afghan refugees. All Afghan women and girls will be granted refugee status in Finland.

The Immigration Service says that the position of women has worsened a lot in Afghanistan because of the Taliban's takeover of power.

The Immigration Service says that all women face persecution in Afghanistan. That is why Afghan girls and women have the right to asylum in Finland.

## Finland, Sweden and Nato

![Jens Stoltenberg.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3897,h_2185,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107323963ecd62bed5f6/1676465735)

Nato Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg urges Turkey to ratify the Nato membership of Finland and Sweden at the same time.

Stoltenberg says that he has told Turkey that Turkey must approve the Nato applications of Finland and Sweden. Stoltenberg says that Turkey decides for itself whether it approves the applications at the same time or at different times. Stoltenberg visits Turkey on Thursday.

Nato Secretary General Jens Stotenberg spoke to the media after the Nato summit in Brussels. Stotenberg said that Nato countries are sending more heavy weapons to Ukraine. Nato countries are also increasing military training for Ukrainian soldiers.
