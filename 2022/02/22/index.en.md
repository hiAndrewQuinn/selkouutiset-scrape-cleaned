# Tuesday 22.2.2022 (radio)

Russia has sent soldiers to Eastern Ukraine. Western countries condemn Russia's actions. Restaurant restrictions are being dropped. The number of clients of the Crime Victim Helpline is growing. The joint application to upper secondary education has started. The weather.

## Ukraine

![Map showing the Luhansk and Donetsk regions and the Russian-backed separatist areas in Eastern Ukraine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12328751)

The crisis in Ukraine has got worse.

Russian President Vladimir Putin has ordered Russian army troops into Eastern Ukraine. Putin says that Russian soldiers are keeping the peace in the rebel area in Eastern Ukraine. Putin claims that the troops of the Ukrainian government are shelling the rebel areas and planning an attack there.

Yesterday Putin decided to recognise the independence of the Donetsk and Luhansk rebel areas. In Putin's view, this decision means that the areas no longer belong to Ukraine.

The Donetsk and Luhansk areas have been under the control of pro-Russian rebels since 2014. However, under international law the rebel areas are part of Ukraine.

## Sanctions on Russia

![Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7701,h_4331,x_491,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9186596214b96c6b726/1645531546)

Finland and other Western countries condemn Russia's actions. Western countries are also imposing new sanctions on Russia.

For example, Germany says that the Nord Stream 2 gas pipeline project will be suspended. The pipeline was to bring natural gas from Russia to Germany.

Britain, for its part, is imposing sanctions on Russian banks and on private people who are close to President Putin.

Restaurant corona restrictions may end at the beginning of March.

The government's corona ministerial working group proposes that restaurant restrictions be dropped. Only hygiene obligations will remain, and they are in force until the end of March in all areas.

However, restrictions will be considered again if the number of intensive care patients grows a lot.

## Crime Victim Helpline

![Crime Victim Helpline material.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7810543)

Open image viewer

The number of clients of the Crime Victim Helpline is growing. Photo: Risto Koskinen / Yle

The Crime Victim Helpline says that it has more clients than before. The helpline says that its number of clients grew last year by almost 20 percent compared with the year before.

Almost half of the clients were victims of domestic violence, sexual crimes or other violence.

Many people contact the Crime Victim Helpline when the police have suggested it to them.

## Joint application to upper secondary education

![Chalkboard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5839979)

Open image viewer

Student selections for the joint application will be announced in June. Photo: Yle / Martti Juntunen

The joint application to upper secondary education began today. The application continues until 22 March.

In upper secondary schools and vocational education there are about 40,000 places in total.

The compulsory education obligation was widened last autumn. Young people must now apply for upper secondary education after basic education.
