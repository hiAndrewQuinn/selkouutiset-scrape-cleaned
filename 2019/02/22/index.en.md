# Friday 22.2.2019 (radio)

The social and health care reform has moved forward a little. The population is growing in the Helsinki, Turku and Tampere areas, and shrinking elsewhere. The situation in Venezuela is getting worse. At the weekend the frost turns into above-zero temperatures.

## Sote reform moves forward

![Ville Niinistö, Annika Lapintie, Markus Lohi and Mia Laiho.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10659731)

Members of the Constitutional Law Committee of Parliament. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The sote reform has moved forward a little. Sote means social and health services.

On Friday, in Parliament, it was heard what the Constitutional Law Committee says about the reform. The committee wants some changes to the government's proposals.

It is uncertain whether Parliament will have time to deal with the changes and approve the sote reform before the elections. The elections are already in April.

If the sote reform is not ready before the elections, the future of the whole reform is uncertain.

## The capital region is growing

![café on Aleksanterinkatu in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10000906)

Open image viewer

The population of Helsinki is growing. Photo: AOP

The differences between regions in Finland are growing, when the number of residents is compared.

The population grows in the three largest city areas, that is, the Helsinki, Turku and Tampere areas. For example, the Helsinki area will have about 300,000 more residents in 2040 than now. More than 60 percent of the new residents of the Helsinki area are immigrants.

In the rest of Finland, the number of residents decreases. Many people move to the Helsinki area. At the same time, the birth rate is falling, that is, Finns have fewer children than before.

The figures are from a report by the consultancy MDI.

## Quarrel in Venezuela

![Food and other aid waiting in cardboard boxes on the Colombian side to go to Venezuela.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10659679)

Open image viewer

In Venezuela there is a quarrel about whether aid packages may be brought across the border. Photo: Ernesto Guzman jr / EPA

The situation in Venezuela, in South America, is getting worse. One person died and many were injured when soldiers fired at people near the Brazilian border.

The people wanted the border to stay open, so that aid goods can be brought from Brazil to Venezuela. Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro has ordered that the border must be closed.

The quarrel is part of Venezuela's political struggle. Juan Guaidó, the opponent of President Maduro, is working so that Venezuela's borders stay open and aid is brought into the country.

## The frost ends

![Three weather maps showing the change in temperatures. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10658518)

Open image viewer

The weather on Saturday morning is completely different from the weather on Friday morning. Photo: Weather desk / Yle

At the weekend the frost turns into above-zero temperatures across the whole country.

In Muonio, Lapland, it was 35 degrees of frost on Friday morning. On Saturday morning in Muonio it may be 5 degrees above zero. So the temperature rises by 40 degrees in one day.

When the weather warms up, the snow melts. From Western Finland and Southern Finland the snow may melt completely in less than 2 weeks.
