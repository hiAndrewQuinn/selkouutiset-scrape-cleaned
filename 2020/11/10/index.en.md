# Tuesday 10.11.2020 (radio)

A coronavirus vaccine is being reserved for everyone in Finland. A Chinese vaccine trial has been suspended. Meyer Turku is laying off 84 people. The white-backed woodpecker population has recovered.

## A coronavirus vaccine is promised to everyone in Finland

![An ampoule of Russian coronavirus vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5881,h_3308,x_0,y_331/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7297325f86880538033/1602652371)

The whole world hopes that a vaccine against the coronavirus will be found soon. Photo: Alamy / All Over Press

When the coronavirus vaccine is available, there will be enough for everyone in Finland. This is what the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health promises. Chief physician Sari Ekholm says that Finland has prepared to buy vaccines for everyone who wants one.

Finance Minister Matti Vanhanen says that getting the coronavirus vaccine to Finland will not depend on money. More than 200 million euros have already been reserved for vaccine purchases in supplementary budgets.

In Finland only vaccines that have received a marketing authorisation from the European Medicines Agency are used. In Europe it has been decided that the vaccine will be brought into use for all countries at the same time.

The coronavirus vaccine is not yet on the market. More than 200 different vaccines are in development against the coronavirus. Two vaccines are being developed in Finland.

Yesterday it was reported that the Pfizer and BioNTech vaccine has progressed. In trials the vaccine has prevented the coronavirus disease by 90 percent.

## Chinese vaccine faces headwinds

![Vaccine development work in the laboratory of the Chinese company Sinovac Biotech in Beijing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11638835)

Vaccine development work. Photo: Wu Hong / EPA

Testing of the Chinese coronavirus vaccine is being suspended. The coronavirus vaccine of the company Sinovac has been tested in Brazil.

Brazilian health authorities say that a volunteer vaccine tester has had serious side effects. The symptoms were not described in more detail.

The authorities say that suspending a trial is normal practice in such cases.

Sinovac says that the tester's case is not related to the vaccine.

## Meyer Turku lays off 84

![Welder at the Meyer Turku shipyard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_131/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-422461599ad90d81f6a/1503320380)

A welder at the Meyer Turku shipyard. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The Turku shipyard of the shipbuilding company Meyer lays off 84 employees. The group includes 9 workers and 75 white-collar employees in outfitting and design functions.

Earlier in the autumn, 166 employees had already lost their jobs. The original estimate of the number of redundancies was 450 in April.

Meyer Turku says that the reason for the end of work is the coronavirus crisis. It has changed the cruise market and the shipbuilding industry dramatically.

## Breeding of the white-backed woodpecker has been successful

![White-backed woodpecker in a tree.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3754,h_2112,x_0,y_551/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7398955fa939364f348/1604925815)

Open image viewer

The white-backed woodpecker is a large woodpecker with white horizontal stripes on its back. The sexes look almost the same: the male can be told by its red crown, the female by its black one. Photo: Ilkka Markkanen

Record numbers of white-backed woodpeckers have bred in Finland this year. The white-backed woodpecker is a forest bird that is endangered, i.e. at risk of disappearing.

Still in the 1970s only a few pairs of white-backed woodpeckers bred in Finland. This summer more than 160 nests have been found.

Metsähallitus (Forest and Park Service) monitored the white-backed woodpecker in 2003–2020. Last year and this year the monitoring has involved only bird enthusiasts.
