# Thursday 4.6.2020 (radio)

The government wants schools to be able to provide distance teaching in the autumn too. SDP's support has grown during the corona crisis. No new corona infections were reported on Thursday. The corona crisis has caused damage to many immigrant companies.

## Distance teaching in schools

![Kosonen Andersson](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11384993)

Open image viewer

Ministers Hanna Kosonen and Li Andersson told about the government's plans on Thursday.

The government is preparing for the autumn term at schools. Pupils will go to school normally in the autumn.

However, the government wants schools to be able to provide distance teaching in the autumn too. Distance teaching may be needed if the corona situation gets worse. Teaching can be arranged, for example, so that pupils are at school at times and in distance teaching at other times.

The government proposes that the law be changed so that distance teaching is possible during next autumn. Parliament decides on the law change.

## Party support

![Party support 11.5.–2.6.2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11382729)

Open image viewer

Party support figures now look like this. Photo: Otso Ritonummi / Yle

SDP's support has grown during the corona crisis. SDP is the party of Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Finns are satisfied with how Marin has handled the corona crisis.

SDP was the largest party when Yle asked Finns which party they support. About 23 per cent of respondents support SDP.

However, the Centre Party, the other big government party, has lost support. The Centre Party's support has fallen to about 10 per cent.

The support for the other parties has not changed much. The party support figures can be found on Yle's website. The address is yle.fi.

## Finland's corona situation

![No entry sign warning of infection risk in a laboratory where coronavirus samples are analysed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11355463)

Open image viewer

Corona infections have already decreased. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finland's corona situation is good.

No new corona infections were reported on Thursday. In total, about 6,900 people in Finland have caught the coronavirus infection.

One more death has occurred. The coronavirus has now killed 322 people in Finland.

The coronavirus is a particular problem for Helsinki. Most of the new corona cases are in Helsinki and around Helsinki in Uusimaa.

Many other areas have not found a single new corona case for a week.

## Immigrant companies

![An employee disinfects a shop door handle in a shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11331648)

Open image viewer

The corona crisis has brought problems, for example, to the retail sector. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The corona crisis has caused damage to many immigrant companies. Immigrant entrepreneurs have suffered from the corona crisis even more than other entrepreneurs.

About 60 per cent of immigrant entrepreneurs say that sales have fallen. About 20 per cent say that sales stopped completely because of the corona crisis.

The corona crisis has been very difficult for immigrant entrepreneurs, because they often work in service sectors. The corona time has been difficult especially in service sectors.

The information was collected by the organisation Suomen Yrittäjät (Entrepreneurs of Finland).
