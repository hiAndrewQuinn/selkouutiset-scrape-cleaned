# Tuesday 20.2.2018 (radio)

The Finnish Immigration Service considers it safe for asylum seekers to return to Baghdad. The number of unemployed people is falling. The spring joint application to vocational schools and upper secondary schools has started. There was a royal funeral in Denmark. The frosty weather continues.

## Iraq's security situation

![A soldier guards a market in Baghdad, the capital of Iraq.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10082392)

Open image viewer

The security situation in the Iraqi capital Baghdad has improved. Photo: Ali Abbas / EPA

The Finnish Immigration Service, Migri, has assessed security in Iraq.

Researchers from the Immigration Service visited the Iraqi capital, Baghdad. They investigated whether it is safe for asylum seekers to return to Baghdad.

The Immigration Service's assessment did not change because of the trip. Migri still holds the view that Baghdad is a fairly safe city. That is why Finland continues to return Iraqis, that is, asylum seekers are sent back to Iraq.

However, the Immigration Service also says that Iraq has dangerous areas as well. No one is sent to dangerous areas.

## Fewer unemployed people

![The door of a TE Office service office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10071822)

Open image viewer

The decrease in the number of unemployed people has also been noticed at the TE Offices. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

The number of unemployed people is falling. In January this year there were 4000 fewer unemployed people than in January last year.

There are now about 230 000 unemployed people. These figures are from Statistics Finland.

The TE Offices also say that the number of unemployed people is falling. Young unemployed people are also now fewer among the TE Offices' clients than a year ago.

## The joint application has started

![A boy studying construction at a vocational school measures a board.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6557030)

Open image viewer

Now is the time to apply, for example, to a vocational school. Photo: Yle

The spring joint application has started. Young people can now apply to vocational school and upper secondary school. The joint application ends on 13 March.

Young people will find out in June whether they got a place to study. Studies start in the autumn.

## Royal funeral in Denmark

![The funeral of Prince Henrik of Denmark at Christiansborg Palace Chapel in Copenhagen, 20 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10083709)

Open image viewer

Prince Henrik's coffin in the Christiansborg Palace chapel in Copenhagen. Photo: Liselotte Sabroe / AFP

There was a royal funeral in Denmark on Tuesday. Prince Henrik of Denmark was buried in Copenhagen, the capital of Denmark.

Prince Henrik was the husband of Queen Margrethe. Margrethe has been Queen of Denmark for 46 years.

Denmark and Finland belong to the Nordic countries. The Nordic countries have good and close relations. The Danish royal family has also often visited Finland.

Danish Prince Henrik died a week ago.

## The frost continues

![A man goes swimming in the Oulu river in winter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10059453)

Open image viewer

Now is the time to enjoy the winter. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

The frosty weather continues in Finland. This week is cold, and at the start of next week the frost will be even harder.

Yle's meteorologist Joonas Koskela says that very cold air will then come to Finland from Siberia in Russia.

Next week the frost may reach as much as 30 degrees at night. During the day the sun will already warm a little and the frost will drop to between 10 and 20 degrees. Because of the wind, however, the frost will feel harder.

It is not usual for Southern Finland to have such hard frost still at the end of February.
