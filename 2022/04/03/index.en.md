## War crimes

![A demonstrator demands on his sign that the perpetrators of war crimes be held to account.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1916,h_1075,x_0,y_200/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9361096245a1b88411e/1648730689)

A demonstrator demands on his sign that the perpetrators of war crimes be held accountable. Photo: Charles M Vella / AOP

Ukraine's Ministry of Defence accuses Russian troops of arbitrary executions of civilians in the city of Bucha near Kyiv.

Journalists of the news agency AFP got to Bucha on Saturday. They say that the bodies of 20 men in civilian clothes were found on a street.

The mayor of the city said that at least 280 bodies have been covered in a mass grave in Bucha.

## Human rights organisation

![Human Rights Watch logo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_369/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-794708607701a166df5/1618412003)

Open image viewer

The human rights organisation has obtained its information from documents, victims and local organisations, among other sources. Photo: Milan Adzic / AOP

The human rights organisation Human Rights Watch demands an investigation into Russia's war crimes in Ukraine. The organisation has documented acts by Russian military forces that are suspected of being war crimes.

The organisation has documented several cases in Chernihiv, Kharkiv and near Kyiv.

The European Union is helping Ukraine and civil society organisations to collect evidence for international courts.

President Sauli Niinistö and Prime Minister Sanna Marin have condemned Russia's actions. They demand that war crimes be investigated and the guilty be held accountable.

## Finnish and Swedish conservative parties

![Ulf Kristersson](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11860096)

Open image viewer

Ulf Kristersson, leader of the Moderate Party. Photo: F. Wennerlund / AOP

The Finnish National Coalition Party and the Swedish Moderate Party have set up a joint security policy working group. The group will deal with questions concerning possible membership in the defence alliance NATO.

The aim of the parties is for Finland and Sweden to submit membership applications. The parties want the applications to be submitted before NATO's June summit meeting.

The Finnish National Coalition Party and the Swedish Moderate Party are opposition parties.

## Gas

![A demonstrator holds a piece of cardboard in the air, close-up of a hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12382995)

Open image viewer

The use of Russian gas will be reduced everywhere in Europe. "Rather cold than Putin's gas", read a demonstrator's piece of cardboard in Cologne, Germany. Photo: AOP

All the Baltic countries have stopped importing Russian natural gas, says the director of a Latvian natural gas company.

In an interview with Latvian Radio, the director said that Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania are now using gas that has been stored in Latvia.

Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda had already confirmed the information about Lithuania's gas deliveries earlier.

## Partial unemployment benefit

![A café worker pours milk into coffee.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8750690)

Open image viewer

Partial unemployment benefit is most often received in healthcare and service sectors. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

More and more Finns combine part-time work or gig work with unemployment benefit. The number of people receiving partial unemployment benefit doubled in the 2010s.

Partial unemployment benefit is most often received in healthcare and service sectors.

It is rare in industry.

## The weather

On Monday, heavy snow or sleet will spread to the southern and central parts of the country. In the north there will mostly be snow showers.

In the south the temperature is above zero, and in the north there is a little frost.

On Tuesday there will be snowfall in Lapland. In the eastern part of the country, 10–20 centimetres of snow may fall.
