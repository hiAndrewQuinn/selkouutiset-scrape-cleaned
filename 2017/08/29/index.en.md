# Tuesday 29.8.2017 (radio)

Niinistö is pleased with the Washington talks. The North Korean missile test was condemned around the world. Only 2 people remain in custody in the Turku stabbing investigation. Manasse, the male polar bear from Ranua, is gone.

## Niinistö pleased with Trump's meeting

![President Sauli Niinistö at a press event at the Finnish embassy in Washington.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9804030)

President Niinistö told journalists about Trump's meeting at the Finnish embassy in Washington. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö has told journalists about the talks with President Donald Trump. Niinistö said that he is very pleased with the visit. In Niinistö's opinion, the discussion about the situation in the northern regions of the Earth, that is, the Arctic regions, was important.

Niinistö talked with Trump about, for example, how dangerous black carbon, that is, soot, is to the Arctic regions. According to Niinistö, Trump knows that soot is very dangerous to the Arctic regions. According to experts, soot warms the climate strongly.

Niinistö also assessed that Trump's talk about fighter jets was the talk of a trader, that is, a businessman. In his opinion, Trump knows that Finland has not yet decided on buying new fighter jets. In Finland people have been puzzled by Trump's statement on fighter jets. Trump said at the press conference that Finland is buying American fighter jets.

## North Korea launched a big missile

![People in Seoul watch a news broadcast about North Korea's missile test, 29 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9802756)

Open image viewer

The missile was launched from near Pyongyang and flew over Japan. Photo: Jeon Heon-Kyun / EPA

World leaders have condemned North Korea's missile test. North Korea launched a missile over Japan last night. It flew over Japan's Hokkaido island and fell into the sea about a thousand kilometres from the Japanese coast.

The Japanese authorities warned people and said that citizens must go to shelter. However, the missile was not shot down.

North Korea has recently launched many missiles. Its activity worries people in different parts of the world.

## Police released two suspects in the Turku stabbing

![Memorial candles in the Turku market square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9799571)

Open image viewer

Only two people are now in custody in the Turku stabbing case. Photo: Päivi Happonen / Yle

The police have released two Moroccan men in the Turku stabbing investigation. The men had been in custody for a week, because they were suspected of a crime in the Turku attack. Now they are no longer suspected of a crime. However, the police intend to question them as witnesses.

Only the main suspect and one other man are now in custody. Yesterday, Monday, it became clear that the main suspect in the stabbings is Abderrahman Bouanane and not Abderrahman Mechkah.

## Ranua's male polar bear had to be put down

![Manasse, the male polar bear, in icy water at Ranua zoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9803907)

Open image viewer

Manasse's favourite pastime was swimming. Photo: Ranua zoo

The Ranua zoo has had to put down the park's old male polar bear. The zoo says that the bear was put down because it suffered from the ailments of old age, and it began to fall ill in the summer.

The male, named Manasse, was 27 years old. Male polar bears usually live in zoos to about 30 years old. Manasse has 2 polar bear cubs, which were born in Ranua.
