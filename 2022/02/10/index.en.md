## Silver and bronze

![Kerttu Niskanen and Krista Pärmäkoski posed with their Olympic medals.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9133846205078889df1/1644587950)

Kerttu Niskanen and Krista Pärmäkoski. Photo: Lehtikuva

Finland has won 2 new medals at the Beijing Olympic Games. The medals came in the women's 10 kilometre classic cross-country skiing.

Finland's Kerttu Niskanen skied second, so she won silver. Krista Pärmäkoski skied third, so she got bronze. The race was won by Therese Johaug of Norway.

Finland now has a total of 3 medals from the Beijing Winter Olympics. Iivo Niskanen won bronze in the men's Nordic combined last Sunday.

## Finland's corona situation

![Positive and negative result on a coronavirus rapid test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4494,h_2516,x_0,y_374/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90910461f91ab81a035/1643715855)

Open image viewer

Negative and positive coronavirus rapid test. Photo: Antti Eintola / Yle

Finland's corona situation is a little better.

The number of coronavirus infections has gone down for the fourth week in a row. On the other hand, fewer coronavirus tests are being done now, so the real number of infections is no longer known.

The need for hospital care has stayed high for the last 2 weeks. However, there are fewer coronavirus patients in intensive care than, for example, at the start of January.

The weekly corona briefing also reported that the omicron variant of the coronavirus has replaced the delta variant in almost the whole country. In the Helsinki region it looks like the peak of the omicron wave may already be over.

## Border control

![Foreign aircraft at Rovaniemi airport, people boarding the plane.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_192/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89421661c03bcf054f6/1639988223)

Aircraft at Rovaniemi airport in December. Photo: Mia Sivula / Yle

Travel into Finland by people from outside the EU will be made easier.

From next Tuesday, at the border a negative coronavirus test result is no longer required from a traveller who comes to Finland from outside the EU and Schengen area.

Passengers must still show proof that they have had coronavirus disease or have received a full course of coronavirus vaccinations.

## Police security

![Police House 1 in Pasila, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_92/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9133066204fa477b762/1644493398)

Open image viewer

Police car in front of a police station in Länsi-Pasila, Helsinki. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

The police have tightened security measures at police stations. The police say that there is a security threat to the police. The police do not say what kind of security threat it is.

According to Yle's information, all police officers in Finland have been told to be on guard. In Helsinki the Pasila police house is protected by an armoured vehicle, among other things.

The threat to the police is rare in Finland.

## Leaving-school parties

Some final-year upper secondary students have celebrated leaving-school parties today. Leaving-school parties are when students celebrate the end of their school work.

Last year the leaving-school parties were cancelled because of corona restrictions. Now too, in many municipalities, the parties have been moved later because of the corona.
