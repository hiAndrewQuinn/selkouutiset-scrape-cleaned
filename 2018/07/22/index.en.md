# Sunday 22.7.2018 (radio)

Forest fires in Finland and Sweden. Summer time in hospitals. Specialty coffees. The weather.

## Forest fires in Finland are being put out

![Ground fire being put out in Raja-Joosep.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10316995)

Open image viewer

The rescue service has brought the Lapland forest fires under control. Photo: Lapland Border Guard

The Lapland rescue service says that the forest fires in Northern Finland are no longer serious.

In Inari the forest fire has already been put out. In Salla there is no longer any need to fear that a forest fire will come from Russia to the Finnish side.

Rainy weather is expected in Lapland. The rescue service hopes that heavy rain will completely put out all the remaining fire spots.

## It is very hot in Sweden

![Forest fire in Ljusdal municipality](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10316963)

Open image viewer

Serious forest fires have been raging in Sweden all this week. Photo: Maja Suslin / EPA-EFE

In Sweden there are fears that the forest fires raging there will get worse. Weather data show that in Sweden it may be as much as 35 degrees next week.

There are more than 70 forest fires across the country in Sweden. Many European countries are sending rescue workers to put out the fires.

Many ordinary Swedes have announced that they want to help with the firefighting.

## In summer, hospitals have few staff

![Nurse and patient in hospital](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10307076)

Open image viewer

In summer, hospitals are short of good holiday cover. Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

Summer is a difficult time for hospitals. Many hospitals cut back their work in summer, when staff are on holiday.

Hospitals find it hard to find good summer holiday cover for doctors and nurses, because a cover does not learn the work very quickly. Because of the shortage of cover, hospitals only carry out urgent operations in summer.

Hospitals arrange their staff's holidays so that wards do not have to be closed completely for the summer.

## Do you take butter coffee?

![A cup of coffee and coffee beans.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10094175)

Open image viewer

For example, butter can be mixed into coffee. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finns drink a lot of coffee. A Finn consumes 10 kilos of coffee a year.

Now many people drink specialty coffee. Butter coffee is made when butter or coconut oil, for example, is mixed into hot coffee.

Among the coffee beans there may also be roasted grains or dandelion roots. During the war, Finns boiled coffee from a powder made from the root of the chicory plant.
