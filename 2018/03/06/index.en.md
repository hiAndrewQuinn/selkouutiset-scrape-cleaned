# Tuesday 6.3.2018 (radio)

The universities will not go on strike. Doctors are talking about behaviour in the workplace. Few children were born last year. 600 new words in the dictionary. The Air Force turned 100.

## The universities will not go on strike

![A university demonstration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10095542)

Open image viewer

A solution was found to the universities' labour dispute. Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

No strike will begin at Finnish universities on Wednesday; work will go on. The strike was called off when a solution was found to the labour dispute in the university sector.

A settlement proposal was made in the labour dispute, and it was accepted. Pay will rise by 3.45 per cent over two years.

## Doctors are talking about behaviour in the workplace

![Young doctors](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10099888)

Open image viewer

Young doctors feel that some older doctors bully them. Photo: Yle News Graphics

Many young doctors say that old doctors treat young doctors badly. Yle interviewed many young doctors. They said that an older doctor may have insulted a young doctor on purpose. The young doctors say that the problems have been known about for a long time.

The association of young doctors says that the treatment is usually good. But sometimes the treatment can also be bad. The Finnish Medical Association says that bullying must not be accepted. Bullying can lead to bad care.

## Few children were born last year

![Babies in a maternity ward.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10104533)

Open image viewer

The birth rate has been falling in Finland for many years now. Photo: Leif Öster / Yle

Few children were born in Finland last year. The authorities say that more than 2,500 fewer children were born than in 2016. In total, more than 50,000 children were born, as many as 150 years ago.

Even though really few children were born last year, Finland's population grew. The growth in the population is due to immigration. About 17,000 more people moved to Finland than moved away.

Finland's population is now over 5.5 million.

## 600 new words in the dictionary

![A drone flying.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10087474)

Open image viewer

In Finnish, this is a "drooni". Photo: AOP

The Finnish language officially gets 600 new words this year. The Finnish language is looked after by the Language Office, which has the dictionary of the Finnish language. This year, 600 new words are added to the dictionary.

New words in the Finnish dictionary include, for example, "drooni", "tubettaa" and "jonne". A drooni is a small remote-controlled aircraft, a drone. "Tubettaminen" means publishing something on the YouTube video service on the internet. "Jonne" is a name that means a young person or a person who behaves childishly.

Other new words include, for example, alien, vegan, energy certificate, G-spot, city bike and experience expert.

## The Air Force turned 100

![A flyover of Hawk jet trainers from Fighter Squadron 41 celebrated the Air Force's 100th anniversary in Vaasa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10103949)

Open image viewer

Army aircraft flew in the sky over Vaasa in honour of the Air Force's birthday. Photo: Jarkko Heikkinen/Yle

The Finnish Army's Air Force turned 100 today. The Air Force's birthday was celebrated in the sky over the Helsinki region. During the day, 4 Hawk aircraft flew in the sky.

There have also been celebrations of the Air Force's anniversary elsewhere in Finland.
