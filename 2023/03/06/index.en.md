## Renovation of Mannerheimintie

![Traffic on Mannerheimintie in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_756/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10816416405d90d1142b/1678105660)

A big renovation that will last more than 2 years is starting on Mannerheimintie in Helsinki. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

The biggest street renovation in Helsinki's history has begun. The thorough renovation of Mannerheimintie began today, and the renovation will last more than 2 years.

The thorough renovation will replace, for example, the water pipes and electricity cables that run under Mannerheimintie.

The renovation is done in two stages. First the road section between Postitalo and the Opera is repaired. After that the part between the Opera, that is, Runeberginkatu, and Reijolankatu is repaired.

Because of the repair work, there will be changes to the routes of buses and trams.

## Phishing for bank codes

![A hand holds a mobile phone, on whose screen there is a warning about phishing of online banking codes on the Osuuspankki login page.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5183,h_2915,x_0,y_290/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-108088664023b897d7fd/1677868093)

Open image viewer

The bank warns about phishing of bank codes on the online bank login page. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

Finns lost 10 million euros last year to criminals who phished for bank codes.

Phishing messages came, for example, in the names of banks and authorities. Citizens of all ages became victims of the fraud.

The National Cyber Security Centre says that phishing for bank codes has increased in recent years. For example, last year reports of phishing came as many as 200 a month.

## Youth elections

![Inna Mattila receives a ballot in the Youth Elections.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_307/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10816316405d368136e2/1678103461)

Open image viewer

Inna Mattila votes in the Youth Elections in Rauma. Photo: Karri Laihonen / Yle

Youth elections have started across Finland. In the Youth Elections everyone under 18 has the right to vote.

Polling places have been set up, for example, in comprehensive schools, upper secondary schools, workshops and vocational institutions. In the elections, the same candidates are voted for as in the parliamentary elections in April.

The elections are organised by the umbrella organisation of the youth sector, Allianssi. Allianssi says that the Youth Elections are above all a learning and teaching event for young people. In this way young people get to know elections, parties and candidates, and get the experience of voting.

The results of the Youth Elections will be published in 2 weeks, on Tuesday 21 March.

## Winter frost record

![Winter frosty landscape with frost on the window; the sun shines from behind a hill.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_142/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10815746405be67f2912/1678098181)

Open image viewer

The frost record was broken in Utsjoki, Lapland. Photo: Kaisa Aikio / Yle

A frost record for this winter has been measured in Lapland.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that in Lapland, at Kevojärvi in Utsjoki, there was frost of 35 degrees this morning.

The earlier frost record of this winter was from Muonio in Lapland. There, just under 35 degrees below zero was measured at the start of January.
