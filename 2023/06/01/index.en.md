## Sweden's NATO membership

![NATO meeting in Oslo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5037,h_2834,x_0,y_235/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11233196478a0be9dac0/1706527109)

Pekka Haavisto at the NATO meeting in Norway. Photo: Lehtikuva

Finland's Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says that Sweden's NATO membership may perhaps come true soon.

Sweden's NATO membership still needs the approval of Turkey and Hungary. Haavisto believes that Turkey may perhaps approve Sweden's NATO membership soon.

Haavisto took part in the meeting of NATO countries' foreign ministers in Norway. Haavisto says that all the other countries told Turkey and Hungary that they want Sweden in NATO quickly.

## Rising prices

![A customer carries a shopping basket in a food shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-774563602b9bbe33527/1613471412)

Price rises have slowed down. That is what Statistics Finland says.

In May prices were about 5 percent higher than a year ago. Still in April the rise in prices, that is inflation, was about 6 percent.

Food prices have risen the most. Food is now about 11 percent more expensive than a year ago.

## Strike in the social sector

![A nurse sits and talks with a resident of a service housing unit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_96/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-112259464774eb14e0e8/1685540714)

Open image viewer

Residents of service housing are looked after also during the strike. Photo: Johanna Manu / Yle

Some of the staff of service housing are on strike. They started the strike today, Thursday, and will be off work for 3 days.

The strike concerns service housing for elderly and disabled people in the Helsinki and Turku regions. The strike concerns only private service housing.

The strike includes, for example, workers of Esperi Care and Mehiläinen. They want more pay.

Care sector pay is nowadays lower in private companies than on the public side, for example in municipalities.

The staff of private service housing are therefore on strike in the Helsinki and Turku regions.

However, residents of service housing still get care during the strike. For example, bosses now also do nursing work.

## Viper

![A grey-black viper lies on an old mat at the bottom of a padded bucket.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_200/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11103446458e92a593f0/1687437398)

Open image viewer

The viper is Finland's only venomous snake. Photo: Juha Kokkala / Yle

The viper is now protected in Finland too. A new law says that vipers may no longer be killed.

However, anyone may catch a viper if it comes into their own yard. The viper may be moved away from the yard.

The viper is Finland's only venomous snake.

Finland was the last Western European country to protect the viper.

## June cold record

![In the foreground mountain rock rubble and in the background wide fells.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8174,h_4588,x_0,y_853/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1122892647857a3052b0/1685608514)

A June cold record has been measured in Lapland. There was 7.7 degrees of frost on Saana fell.

The previous record was measured over 60 years ago. In June 1961 in Lapland there were 7 degrees of frost.

Also on Friday the weather is cold. The temperature in the south is about 10 degrees. In the north the temperature is under 10 degrees.

The weather is also cloudy. On Friday there will also be rain showers in various parts of Finland.

At the weekend the weather warms slightly, and the temperature rises above 10 degrees in the south. The weather also gets drier, and there will be only a few rain showers.
