## Corona pass

![A mobile phone with a corona pass app on its screen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12038566)

Open image viewer

France's corona pass looks like this. Photo: AOP

Restaurant and event businesses want a corona pass to be introduced in Finland as soon as possible.

A corona pass is an official proof that a person has been vaccinated, has had corona disease, or has just received a negative result in a corona test. With the pass a person can get into restaurants, theatres, concerts or other public events, for example.

Restaurant and event businesses consider the pass good, because then restaurants no longer need to be closed and events do not need to be cancelled.

In many European countries the corona pass is already widely used. Minister of Economic Affairs Mika Lintilä says that he will start promoting a corona pass in Finland too.

## Today's corona figures

![Mika Salminen at the THL information event](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3936,h_2214,x_0,y_172/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7243275f7320216b7fc/1604921324)

Mika Salminen, director of the health security department of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

Finland's corona figures are high again today.

There are over 600 new infections and over 50 corona patients in hospital care.

Mika Salminen of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that there are now more infections among people who have already received one corona vaccine.

In Finland 65 percent of the population has received the first vaccine dose. About 32 percent have received the second dose.

In total, 982 people have now died of corona in Finland. During the week, 2 new corona deaths have been reported.

Petrol has become more expensive during the summer. Photo: Hilma Toivonen / Yle

The price of petrol has risen during the summer.

The price of 95E10 petrol has risen by about 10 cents a litre. Now it costs on average 1.70 a litre.

The most expensive petrol is now sold in Lapland, in Kaamanen in Inari. There a litre of petrol already costs almost 2 euros.

## Tourism in Eastern Finland

![Women in the yard of a hotel in Koli](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_750/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83566360ff9024daa99/1627361369)

Helsinki residents Reetta Huomo, Satu Turula and Sini-Maria Saarnio are holidaying in Koli in North Karelia. Photo: Mari Kahila / Yle

There have been a lot of tourists in Eastern Finland this summer. The tourists are Finns who are holidaying in their own country because of corona.

For example, in North Karelia and North Savo there have been so many tourists that hotels have at times been completely full.

Hotels say that because of corona, last summer was already busy, but this summer is even busier.
