# Tuesday 17.11.2020 (radio)

A second coronavirus vaccine is coming. Finland's coronavirus situation. 2 new national parks. The President's Independence Day reception. A different Christmas Peace proclamation. Rainy and warm weather continues.

## A second coronavirus vaccine is coming

![A volunteer test subject was given Moderna's vaccine in Detroit on 5 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11651304)

Open image viewer

A volunteer receives Moderna's coronavirus vaccine. Photo: AFP

The American company Moderna says that it has got good results from its coronavirus vaccine.

The vaccine that the company has developed protects well against the disease caused by the coronavirus.

The vaccine's efficacy is 94.5 percent.

Now research results have been reported for 2 coronavirus vaccines. Another company reported last week that its vaccine's efficacy is 90 percent.

The vaccines do not protect against the coronavirus itself.

They prevent the symptoms that belong to the COVID-19 disease. If you take the vaccine, you may not get the dangerous disease.

## 2 new national parks

![Sallatunturi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10663735)

Open image viewer

There is a ski slope on Sallatunturi. Photo: Kristof Göttling

Environment Minister Krista Mikkonen proposes that 2 new national parks be established in Finland.

The Salla National Park will be in Eastern Lapland, and the Evo National Park will be in Southern Finland. Salla is a fell area and Evo is a large forest area.

There are more than 40 national parks in Finland.

National parks are nature conservation areas, but you can also go hiking in them.

During the coronavirus era, national parks have been popular places for hiking trips.

## The Independence Day celebration is different

![Sauli Niinistö and Jenni Haukio dance while others watch them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8508569)

Open image viewer

Guests usually dance at the President's Independence Day reception. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

This year Independence Day, 6 December, is celebrated differently than usual.

The President of the Republic's Independence Day reception is now a celebration with a lot of programme.

At the Presidential Palace there are no guests because of the coronavirus, as there usually are.

There is a lot of music and, for example, ballet dancing at the celebration.

The celebration can be followed live on Yle's channels.

## A different Christmas Peace proclamation

![Christmas Peace proclamation in Turku's Old Market Square in 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4128,h_2322,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-3245765894559943731/1545644501)

Open image viewer

Many people usually gather in the square when the Christmas Peace is proclaimed in Turku. Photo: Yle/Linus Hoffman

The traditional Christmas Peace proclamation is also different this year.

The Christmas Peace is always proclaimed at 12 noon on Christmas Eve in Turku.

People have gathered in the square to listen to the proclamation. This year people will not be let into the square, even though the event is held in the traditional way.

Because of the coronavirus, large gatherings must be avoided.

The Christmas Peace proclamation can be watched on television.
