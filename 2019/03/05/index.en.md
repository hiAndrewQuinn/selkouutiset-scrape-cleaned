# Tuesday 5.3.2019 (radio)

Timo Soini will not stand in the parliamentary elections. Young people believe that they can make a difference by voting in elections. The most polluted cities are in India. HIV has disappeared from a man. The Nobel Prize in Literature from last year will be awarded this year.

## Timo Soini will not stand for parliament

![Timo Soini (left) held a press conference in Parliament on 5 March 2019. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10674244)

Timo Soini will not stand as a candidate in the spring parliamentary elections. Photo: Yle

Timo Soini will no longer stand as a candidate in the parliamentary elections.

Timo Soini is the founder of the Finns Party and the current foreign minister from the Blue party. He has been an MP for four terms, that is, 16 years. Four years ago, Soini received almost 30,000 votes in the parliamentary elections.

Soini does not yet say anything about being a candidate in the European elections.

The nomination of candidates for the April parliamentary elections ended today, Tuesday, at 4 p.m.

## Young people believe in voting

![Presidential election advertisements at Töölö Square in Helsinki in January 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10673290)

Young people believe that they can make a difference by voting. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Young people believe more than others that by voting in elections they can influence matters.

In a Yle survey, 75 percent of under-30s believe that they can influence society by voting. However, young people vote the least in elections.

## The world's most polluted cities are in India

![Cars and mopeds on a road under an underpass. Everything is covered in brownish-yellow smog. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9274863)

Delhi is one of the world's most polluted cities. Photo: Rajat Gupta / EPA

22 of the world's 30 most polluted cities are in India.

A new report says that the most polluted city in the world is Gurgaon in India. Gurgaon is near the Delhi metropolitan area.

Among the 30 most polluted cities there are, in addition to India, cities from Pakistan and China, and one city from Bangladesh.

## HIV disappeared from an infected man

![a person taking an HIV test](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10371687)

Open image viewer

For the second time in history, HIV has been successfully eradicated from an adult patient.

In London, HIV has been eradicated from an infected man.

The man was given bone marrow transplants from people who are immune to the virus. After the treatment, the man has had no HIV for 19 months.

This is the second time in history that the virus has been eradicated from an adult patient. In 2007, an American patient received the same treatment in Berlin, and no HIV has been found in him since then.

HIV infection is treated with medicine that stops the infection from developing into AIDS. However, the medicines do not cure the patient.

According to estimates, more than 35 million people have died of AIDS in the world.

## Two Nobel Prizes in Literature will be awarded this year

![Announcement of the Nobel Prizes at the Swedish Academy](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10159854)

Open image viewer

The Nobel Prize in Literature, which was not awarded last year, will be awarded this year. Photo: Pelle T Nilsson / AOP

The Swedish Academy will award 2 Nobel Prizes in Literature this year.

Swedish media report that the Swedish Academy will also award this year the prize that was not awarded last year. The prize was not given last year, because the Swedish Academy was disturbed by a scandal related to sexual harassment.

The Nobel Prize in Literature was first awarded in 1901. The Nobel Prize in Literature is worth about one million euros.
