## Electricity price

![Power lines. Illustrative image.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_570/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1034319637381dca9a1a/1668541425)

The energy crisis has become expensive for Finnish electricity consumers.

Researchers calculate that in the second half of last year Finns paid perhaps about 5 billion euros more for electricity than usual.

The researchers calculated the cost of the energy crisis for Finns when Yle's MOT team asked them to.

Professor Peter Lund of Aalto University says that electricity companies benefited from the energy crisis. Although the selling price of electricity rose, producing it cost the same as before. So electricity companies got big profits from the energy crisis.

The Energy Industries says that the experts' calculations of windfall profits are not true. The Energy Industries says that the electricity companies' profits are not 5 billion, but perhaps about one billion euros.

About half of Finns are satisfied with the current alcohol policy.

In the survey of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, 53 percent say that the current restrictions are appropriate.

Nearly half of respondents want wine to be sold in grocery shops as well. However, the vast majority of respondents do not want strong alcoholic drinks in grocery shops.

About a thousand Finns over 18 answered THL's survey in January.

## Finland's biggest fish

![Dead cod.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2554,h_1436,x_0,y_607/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1084159640ed7618a786/1678694329)

Open image viewer

Finland's biggest fish weighed nearly 30 kilos after gutting. Photo: Patrik Lundin / Lehtikuva

A record-sized fish was caught in Åland.

Last May a very big cod swam into a net in Åland. The cod was 141 centimetres long and weighed almost 30 kilos. The cod was weighed gutted, so its real weight was even bigger.

The Central Association of Fisheries says that the cod was recorded in the record fish register as Finland's biggest fish.

Film industry Oscar awards have been handed out in the USA.

The film that won the most Oscars was called Everything Everywhere All at Once. It won 7 Oscars in total.

The film was awarded, for example, for best direction, best actress and best supporting roles. It was also awarded as the best film of the year.

The weather warms and turns rainy.

On Tuesday in the south it rains or sleets. Elsewhere in the country it snows.
