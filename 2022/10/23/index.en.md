# Sunday 23.10.2022 (radio)

The government plans support for companies' electricity bills. Rishi Sunak seeks to become British Prime Minister. Xi Jinping continues as China's leader. The weather is dry.

## Prime Minister Sanna Marin

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5127,h_2878,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-102416563552722393ec/1666525240)

Sanna Marin at the Prime Minister's interview hour at Kesäranta. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

The government intends to support companies in the energy crisis. Prime Minister Sanna Marin says this in an interview with Yle.

Many European countries have already given companies support packages. Prime Minister Marin says that Finland is also planning support packages.

Companies' electricity bills are much bigger than before, because the price of energy has risen. Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the price of energy must be made lower than it is now.

## Rishi Sunak

![British politician Rishi Sunak.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4123,h_2319,x_0,y_44/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10033646315ba40b2991/1666586167)

Open image viewer

British politician, former Chancellor of the Exchequer Rishi Sunak. Photo: Neil Hall / AOP

In Britain Rishi Sunak says that he wants to become Prime Minister. Rishi Sunak was previously Britain's Chancellor of the Exchequer. Sunak is the favourite to be Britain's next Prime Minister.

Penny Mordaunt, leader of the House of Commons, also wants to become Prime Minister. Former Prime Minister Boris Johnson has not yet officially said whether he will take part in the race for Prime Minister.

Britain's Prime Minister is changing, because Liz Truss said on Thursday that she will not continue as Prime Minister. Truss was Britain's Prime Minister for only about 6 weeks, before she decided to resign from the post.

In China, Xi Jinping continues as the country's leader. The Chinese Communist Party gave Xi a further term as the party's leader. This means that Xi continues as China's President.

Xi Jinping has a lot of power in China. For example, he leads China's army. Xi has been China's leader for about 10 years.

## VR

![Double-decker InterCity train at Tampere railway station. Empty tracks in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_424/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98142562ce956521a0c/1657706182)

Open image viewer

A train at Tampere railway station. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

The state railway company VR is saving electricity. VR says that many freight trains will in future run at night. The drivers of all trains also try to drive so that as little electricity as possible is used.

Electricity's share of the train ticket price is about 10 percent. VR says that in winter electricity's share of VR's costs will grow a lot. However, VR is not raising train ticket prices.

The weather is dry across almost the whole country. The sun shines in South Finland and Central Finland. In the north there are clouds in the sky.

On Monday during the day the temperature is between 2 and 6 degrees, in Lapland the temperature is close to zero.
