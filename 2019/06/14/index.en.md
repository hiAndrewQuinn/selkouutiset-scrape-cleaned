## Online banking codes

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8262868)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

It is good to keep your online banking codes safe.

If online banking codes are stolen, big losses can happen. With the codes, a thief can go online to a quick loan service.

The owner of the account may be responsible for the quick loan, that is, the instant loan that the thief takes out using the codes. The account owner may have to pay back the whole loan.

If a thief gets the online banking codes because the owner of the codes was careless, the owner of the codes must pay back the loan.

## The situation in the Persian Gulf

![The Norwegian shipping company's tanker Front Altair burning in the Gulf of Oman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10830433)

On Thursday 2 oil ships were attacked, and one of the ships caught fire. Photo: IRIB / EPA

US President Donald Trump says that Iran is guilty of the attack on oil ships. The attack happened on Thursday near the Persian Gulf.

The USA says it has a video that proves Iran's action. Iran says that it did not carry out the attack.

The attack was made on 2 oil ships. The crews of the ships were rescued.

Relations between the USA and Iran are bad. The situation in the Persian Gulf is tense.

## Train travel

![Pendolino on the railway track.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10366536)

Open image viewer

Photo: Soile Laaksonen / VR Group

Finns travel by train more than before. In May there were 1.2 million trips on long-distance trains. That is 6 per cent more than a year ago.

Train traffic grew the most between Helsinki and Rovaniemi. On this route there was 25 per cent more train traffic in May than a year ago.

Many people travel by train for the sake of the environment. A train does not pollute nature, whereas aircraft emissions warm the climate.

## Fare dodging

![Metro train standing at Kamppi metro station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10085865)

Open image viewer

Many people travel on the metro without a ticket. Photo: Kari Ahotupa / Yle

A lot of passengers travel without a ticket on public transport in the Helsinki region.

About 100,000 people were caught travelling without a ticket last year, that is, fare dodging. Because of these passengers, public transport lost 10 million euros in ticket revenue.

Many people fare dodge on the metro, on trams and on commuter trains. Bus passengers must always show their ticket to the driver.
