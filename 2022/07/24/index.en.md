## Helping Ukrainians

![Canned food in cardboard boxes, close up. Next to them a note that says Ukraina. The picture is from the aid centre for Ukrainian refugees in Lahti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4027,h_2266,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98147162ce9d0c7cb6b/1657707900)

Open image viewer

Photo: Petri Niemi / Yle

The Finns' wish to help Ukrainians has decreased. Still in the spring the Finns helped Ukrainians more than now.

The Finns began to help Ukrainians actively when Russia attacked Ukraine in February. The Finns gave money and goods, and many did volunteer work for the refugees.

Now less help is coming. This is what the Ukrainian association in Finland says, for example.

It is estimated that the Finns have become used to war news. The distress of Ukrainians is no longer a new thing, because the war has already lasted 5 months.

Still there are many Ukrainian refugees. The UN, that is, the United Nations, estimates that there are about 6 million Ukrainian refugees in Europe.

## Volcanic eruption in Japan

![Sakurajima volcano erupting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6782693)

Open image viewer

The Sakurajima volcano often has small eruptions. This picture is from 2013. Big and violent eruptions are rare. Photo: Reuters-TV

A volcano is erupting in Japan. The Sakurajima volcano began to erupt on Sunday.

Sakurajima is on the island of Kyūshū. Japanese authorities have said that residents of the area must move to safety.

The Sakurajima volcano last erupted violently in 1914.

## Ground fires in Greece

![A local resident tries to save his property at the seaside resort Vatera on the island of Lesbos on 23 July 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5110,h_2874,x_0,y_212/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98578362dc3c8b9766d/1658600612)

Open image viewer

A man tries to save a house from fire on the island of Lesbos in Greece. Photo: Lehtikuva

Ground fires continue in the Mediterranean area. The fire is burning forests and grasslands.

The ground fires have spread quickly, because the weather is dry and hot. The temperature has often been over 40 degrees.

Now the firefighters are fighting the fire in different parts of Greece. At the weekend residents and tourists were moved to safety from the island of Lesbos, when a ground fire broke out on the island and the fire also spread to buildings.

## World Athletics Championships

![Lassi Etelätalo throws the javelin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4378,h_2462,x_0,y_163/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-94087662544e70693b3/1659858984)

Open image viewer

Javelin thrower Lassi Etelätalo. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

The athletics world championships, that is, the world championship competitions, end today on Sunday. The athletes have competed in Oregon in the USA.

Finland has not won any medals in Oregon. At the weekend the Finns were waiting for a medal in the men's javelin throw, but no medal came.

Still, the Finnish javelin throwers did quite well. Lassi Etelätalo came 6th, and Oliver Helander came 8th.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12549017)

Open image viewer

The weather gets warmer on Monday. In a large part of the country the temperature is 22-26 degrees. In the north it is a little cooler.

On Monday it is also dry, that is, no rain falls. Dry weather continues in Eastern Finland also on Tuesday.

Rain comes to Western Finland on Tuesday. In the rain the temperature drops below 20 degrees.
