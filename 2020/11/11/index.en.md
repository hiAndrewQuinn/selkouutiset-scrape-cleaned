## Finland starts planning vaccinations

![One of the first South African vaccine trial participants is injected during a clinical study with a potential vaccine against the coronavirus at Baragwanath Hospital in Soweto, South Africa, on 24 June 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7068985f3176d71854d/1597079036)

The coronavirus vaccine is being tested in South Africa. Photo: Siphiwe Sibeko / EPA

Finland starts next week to plan how and when vaccinations against the coronavirus will be given to people.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that the first vaccines may perhaps be given at the beginning of next year.

Finland's preliminary plan is to obtain 3 different vaccines. The vaccines must be different so that a suitable one is found for all population groups.

Finland has not yet decided which vaccines will be used here.

## The coronavirus vaccine must be stored at very cold temperatures

![A person working in a laboratory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11588209)

Open image viewer

Vaccine laboratory of the French pharmaceutical factory Sanofi. Photo: Joel Saget / AFP

The development of coronavirus vaccines is progressing. However, special problems lie ahead.

On Monday German pharmaceutical companies told about their own vaccine.

It has worked well in trials.

What is special is that the vaccine must be stored at minus 70 degrees.

Vaccine experts must now solve how the vaccine can be transported and stored.

Most of the infections have been found among young adults.

## Sleep apnea can make coronavirus illness worse

![Hanna Tuovila with a CPAP device.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11030898)

Open image viewer

If a person suffers from sleep apnea, they must sleep with a positive airway pressure device. Photo: Jari Pussinen / YLE

Sleep apnea may increase the risk that a person who has caught a coronavirus infection ends up needing hospital care.

This emerged when the University of Turku and Turku University Hospital studied patients from the first wave of the coronavirus.

29 percent of the patients who had to go to hospital because of the coronavirus also suffered from sleep apnea.

Sleep apnea means that a person's breathing stops during sleep.

## The operation of pharmacies has been investigated

![Pharmacy checkout.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11640862)

Open image viewer

A pharmacist owns a pharmacy. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The authorities have investigated the operation of pharmacies in Finland.

The investigation says that it is possible to lower the price of medicines.

Pharmacists, i.e. the owners of pharmacies, earn a lot. They therefore take a fairly large share of the price that the customer pays for a medicine.

The consumer authority also says that the price of self-care medicines would fall if they could be sold by, for example, supermarkets.

A self-care medicine is a medicine that can be bought without a prescription.
