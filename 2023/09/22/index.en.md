## Grain harvest

![Wheat field that has ripened earlier than usual because of the dry summer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_101/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8365246102467999a79/1679932018)

Weather conditions have weakened the grain harvest. Photo: Jarkko Kumpulainen / Yle

The grain harvest is becoming the third weakest of the 2000s.

The Natural Resources Institute Finland, Luke, says that this autumn an estimated 2.9 billion kilos of grain will be harvested.

That is about 20 percent less than last year.

The early summer was sunny and dry, and recently a lot of rain has fallen. That has weakened the harvest.

The police are considering intervening more strictly in speeding.

The police have usually intervened only for speeding that is 10 kilometres per hour over the limit. In practice, for example in a 30 km/h limit area, a traffic fine, that is, a penalty, is given only when the speed exceeds 40 km/h.

Statistics say that more than half of drivers speed on Finnish roads.

## Taxation of plots

![Apartment blocks in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5221,h_2936,x_0,y_275/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1135697649c305ac2c52/1687957859)

Open image viewer

If the taxation of plots becomes tighter, it will also show in apartment blocks on owned plots. Then pressure may arise to raise housing company charges. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

The property tax on plots is rising next year in most of the country. If the bill goes through, the change will concern everyone who owns a plot.

The change also concerns those who live in a housing company that owns a plot. The rise in tax may also raise housing company charges.

For example, the tax on a plot worth 100 000 euros may rise by 370 euros a year in Helsinki. In the calculation, the current tax is 930 euros and next year it is 1 300 euros.

In Oulu the corresponding tax rise is 150 euros a year.

## Unemployment funds

![Different plates stacked on a restaurant shelf.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5458,h_3070,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7313765f8a811cc40a5/1602913199)

Open image viewer

Layoffs show in unemployment funds' payments. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Unemployment funds have paid more earnings-related daily allowances than before.

The Joint Organisation of Unemployment Funds says that in August the increase was 9 percent compared with last year's August.

Costs grew at every second unemployment fund.

The growth is partly due to there having been many layoffs. The organisation says that laid-off people were 45 percent more in August than in last year's August.

## Soft tissue rheumatism

![A woman holds her back.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2000,h_1125,x_0,y_266/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6079455dc011a84ca4c/1572868591)

Open image viewer

About 4 percent of the population suffers from the syndrome. Most patients are women. Photo: Jaana Polamo / Yle

Bullying at school or another negative childhood event can trigger a rheumatic illness in adults.

A doctoral dissertation at the University of Tampere says that negative events in childhood are linked to soft tissue rheumatism.

Soft tissue rheumatism, that is fibromyalgia, causes long-lasting and widespread pain in the musculoskeletal system. It includes bones, joints, tendons and muscles.

Symptoms also include exhaustion, sleep that does not refresh, and disturbances of information processing and mood.
