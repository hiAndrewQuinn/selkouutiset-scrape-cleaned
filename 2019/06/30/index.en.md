# Sunday 30.6.2019 (radio)

The leaders of the USA and North Korea met at the border between the Koreas. The European heatwave is starting to ease. Many Finns suffer from migraines. A nest camera revealed that the golden eagle is a keen cleaner.

## Meeting at the border between the Koreas

The leaders of the USA and North Korea have met.

US President Donald Trump and North Korean leader Kim Jong-un met today, Sunday, at the border between North and South Korea.

Trump and Kim greeted each other and then walked together across the border line to the North Korean side. After that they walked together to the South Korean side.

Trump said that the meeting at the border was a sign of friendship. Trump and Kim also talked for just under an hour.

## The heat in Europe

![People cooling down](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10853967)

Open image viewer

People cool off at a fountain in Paris. Photo: AOP

The European heatwave is gradually starting to ease.

In France and Spain the temperature has already dropped a little today, Sunday. In Germany the weather will start to cool down tomorrow.

The heatwave has scorched Southern and Central Europe for about a week. At least 8 people have died in the heat.

In the hot weather several land fires have broken out, for example in Spain and France. In Spain more than 2,000 hectares of land have burned. In France about 40 fires have been reported.

## Migraine

![A woman sits on a sofa holding her head.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10276861)

Open image viewer

Migraine is more common in women than in men. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Many Finns suffer from migraines.

The Finnish Migraine Association says that at least 700,000 Finns suffer from migraines. Migraines are more common in women than in men.

Migraine is an illness in which a person gets severe headache attacks. Many different things can trigger an attack. For example, flickering lights, changes in the weather, stress or alcohol can start a migraine attack.

Some migraine patients are completely unable to function during a migraine attack, that is, they cannot do anything at all. Some patients get help from medicines.

New migraine preventive medicines are coming in the autumn.

## Golden eagles

![Two young golden eagle chicks wait in the nest for food.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8711216)

Open image viewer

Two young golden eagle chicks are waiting for food in the nest. Photo: Taito Siira. From the Yle programme Kotkaperhe (Eagle Family).

New information has been gained about the life of the golden eagle. The new information was gained when the golden eagle's nest was monitored with a camera.

The nest camera of the Finnish Nature Conservation Union has revealed, for example, that the golden eagle's most common prey is the young of the crane. Young swans are also on the golden eagle's menu.

The mother eagle is also keen to clean. The mother diligently removes bones and other prey waste from the nest.

Another new finding is that an eagle chick less than a month old can already be left alone in the nest for up to a day.
