# Monday 19.12.2016 (radio)

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä promises a review of how the government prepares new laws. Singer Saara Aalto got the Finland Prize. Gold mines do not make very much money, because the price of gold has fallen. Finns are buying wristwatches again. Finns are interested in insect food.

## Preparing laws

![Prime Minister Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9200140)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä promises a review of how the government prepares new laws.

At the weekend, Chancellor of Justice Jaakko Jonkka said that the government prepares laws badly. Laws are prepared, for example, in too much of a hurry. The Chancellor of Justice has also felt that the government has not listened to him during the preparation work.

In the Prime Minister's opinion, the criticism is serious, and he intends to discuss the matter with the Chancellor of Justice. In Finland, the Chancellor of Justice makes sure that the authorities do their work as the law requires.

## The Finland Prizes

![Fans surround Saara Aalto at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9335569)

Singer Saara Aalto. Photo: Timo Korhonen/ AOP

Singer Saara Aalto has received the Finland Prize. Aalto got the Finland Prize because she has shown Finnish skill to millions of TV viewers. A while ago, Aalto came second in Britain's X Factor music competition.

This year 8 Finland Prizes were given. The prizes were over 20,000 euros. The prizes were given by minister Sanni Grahn-Laasonen.

## Gold mines

![Dragon Mining's gold mine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9350880)

Open image viewer

A gold mine in Huittinen. Photo: Janne Toivonen / Yle

The price of gold has fallen. Because of that, gold mines do not make very much money.

In Finland, 2 gold mines have been closed, and 4 gold mines are left. The biggest of them is the Kittilä gold mine. The others are small mines.

The owners of Finland's gold mines are foreign firms. For example, the Huittinen gold mine in southern Finland is owned by an Australian firm.

## Wristwatches

![A wristwatch](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9359683)

Open image viewer

Expensive wristwatches. Photo: Yle

Finns are buying wristwatches again. Many thought that watches would disappear, because you can also see the time on your phone.

Mia Rautakoura, a seller in a watch and jewellery shop, says that many customers want to buy a mechanical watch. People think it is great that someone has been able to build a watch from many hundreds of parts that keeps the right time.

Young people buy slightly cheaper fashion watches, but now expensive watches are also being sold. One watch can cost many thousands of euros.

## Insect food

![Chocolate pastries coated with larvae.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5307894)

Open image viewer

Chocolate and larvae. Photo: YLE

Finns are interested in insect food. This is what a new survey study says.

70 percent of respondents said that they are interested in insect food. About 30 percent of respondents have already eaten insects, for example larvae.

Finns do not like eating whole insects. The respondents said that they want insects as a powder.

Insects are healthy food; they have, for example, a lot of protein.
