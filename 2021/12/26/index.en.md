## Desmond Tutu

![Desmond Tutu greets children.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2860,h_1605,x_0,y_58/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89635261c81b4693ba1/1640504391)

Open image viewer

Everyone was important to Desmond Tutu. Photo: Nic Bothma / EPA

South African Desmond Tutu has died. Tutu was 90 years old.

Desmond Tutu worked for human rights. He received the Nobel Peace Prize in 1984.

Tutu opposed apartheid in South Africa. At that time, power was in the hands of white people. Tutu wanted all South Africans to be equal.

Desmond Tutu fought against apartheid without violence. He was also a priest and an archbishop.

Desmond Tutu was an important person also after apartheid ended in South Africa in the 1990s. Tutu led the truth commission. The truth commission investigated crimes from the apartheid era.

## Reindeer

![Reindeer from Kuusamo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4310,h_2424,x_0,y_88/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-774740602bc3aa4d076/1668177026)

Open image viewer

In winter, reindeer look for lichen under the snow.

Reindeer herders have problems in Lapland. Thousands of reindeer have gone missing, and the herders are searching for them even with helicopters.

The reindeer have set out to look for food. Normally, reindeer eat lichen from under the snow. Now, however, there is hard ice on top of the lichen.

Ice can form on lichen if, for example, the first layer of snow of the winter melts before the hard frosts. When the frost starts, the water and wet snow freeze.

The British BBC has also reported on the problems of the reindeer herders. The BBC has met reindeer herders in Finnish and Swedish Lapland.

Monday is also dry, with no snow falling.

On Tuesday the weather starts to get milder, that is, the frost weakens.
