## A coronavirus infection has been found in Finland

![Lapland Central Hospital in Rovaniemi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11183220)

Open image viewer

A patient with a coronavirus infection is being treated in the hospital in Rovaniemi. Photo: Laura Haapamäki / Lehtikuva

The first coronavirus infection in Finland has been confirmed.

A Chinese woman is in hospital in Rovaniemi in northern Finland.

The woman comes from the Wuhan area in China. She came to Finland on a holiday trip and ended up in hospital here because of symptoms.

It was confirmed today that the woman has the coronavirus.

The coronavirus spreads from person to person, but only if people are very close to each other.

The Chinese woman is in isolation in the hospital.

## It is not worth travelling to China

The authorities of many countries now say that it is not worth travelling to China, unless it is absolutely necessary.

The coronavirus is spreading quickly in China.

The coronavirus can be dangerous for people who have a long-term illness.

## Immigrant mothers are easing the baby shortage

![A baby's hand](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_284/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6296125e182030b7f00/1578639528)

Open image viewer

There is a baby shortage in Finland, because very few babies are being born. Photo: Juha-Petri Koponen / Yle

In Finland people are talking about a baby shortage, because so few babies are being born.

Immigrant mothers give birth to a lot of babies. That eases Finland's baby shortage.

In Finland, every 7th child is born to a mother who has moved to Finland from abroad.

The most immigrant mothers have come from Russia. There are also many from Estonia, Somalia and Iraq.

## The police spend a lot of time investigating crimes

![A large police operation was under way in Porvoo early on Sunday 25 August. The investigation was carried out in the Ölsten industrial area.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10937487)

Open image viewer

Sometimes crimes are such that they are hard to investigate. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The police now spend more time investigating crimes than before.

There are many reasons for this.

There are now fewer police officers investigating crimes than 10 years ago.

Young police officers do not want to become investigators. They want to work on the streets and in traffic.

## Higher earners also take payday loans

![A payday loan is applied for on a phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10745037)

Open image viewer

A payday loan can be taken out online. Payday loans have a really high interest rate. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Payday loans cause a lot of problems.

A payday loan means that a company operating online lends money to a person. A payday loan is easy and quick to get.

Payday loans have a really high interest rate.

People can get caught in a debt spiral because of a payday loan. A person has to take out a new payday loan in order to pay off the old payday loan.

A new problem is that people with high incomes also take out payday loans.

They may be using the money to play games of chance.

## Thursday will be cloudy

![Weather picture: a blue tit in a winter landscape](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11181898)

The blue tit is a bird that is often seen at the bird table in winter. Photo: Aino Piirainen

Tomorrow, Thursday, it will be cloudy across the whole country.

In southern and western Finland it will snow or sleet. The temperature is a little above zero.

In eastern and northern Finland it will only snow in some places. In eastern and northern Finland there is frost.

In the north the frost may be 15 degrees.
