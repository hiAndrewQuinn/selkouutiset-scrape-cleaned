# Thursday 20.6.2019 (radio)

Food is expensive in Finland. Sweden is introducing a plastic bag tax. A fake police officer was sentenced to prison for cheating elderly people. A new mosquito species was found in Rovaniemi. Midsummer bonfires must not be lit in southern and central Finland.

## The price of food

![The price of food in Finland compared with European price levels, graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10841022)

Open image viewer

Food is more expensive in Finland than in many other EU countries. A Finn pays about 20 percent more for food than other EU citizens on average, a new comparison says.

For example, fruit, vegetables and potatoes cost almost 30 percent more in Finland than in other EU countries on average. Only fats and oils are cheaper in Finland than elsewhere.

In the comparison of EU countries, Finland was last year the country with the fifth most expensive food. The cheapest food in the EU was eaten in Poland and Romania,

However, wage levels in Finland are 60 percent higher than the EU average.

## Sweden's plastic bag tax

![A woman carries shopping in plastic bags.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10841605)

Open image viewer

Every Swede buys about 83 plastic bags a year. Photo: Francis Dean / AOP

Sweden is introducing a plastic bag tax. The tax will be introduced during next year.

After the tax reform, a standard-size plastic bag in Sweden will cost 7 kronor, which is about 65 cents.

The plastic bag tax is intended to bring about 300 million euros into the state's coffers.

The aim of the tax is to get Swedes to buy fewer plastic bags. Today every Swede buys an average of 83 plastic bags a year.

## A fake police officer cheated elderly people

![A police warning sticker.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10034545)

Open image viewer

A real police officer never asks anyone for online banking codes. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

The Päijät-Häme District Court has sentenced to prison a man who had posed as a police officer. The man got a little over a year in prison for 17 crimes.

The man pretended to be a police officer and cheated money from elderly people. He managed to cheat elderly people out of online banking codes and cash. The man got tens of thousands of euros through the scams.

The victims of the fraud lived in different parts of Finland.

## A new mosquito species

![A swarm of mosquitoes](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10202248)

Open image viewer

About 40 mosquito species have now been identified in Finland. Photo: AOP

A new mosquito species has been found in Finland. The new species was found in Rovaniemi.

The name of the mosquito species is Aedes rossicus. The species may have lived in Finland for hundreds of years, but it was only now noticed. Mosquitoes of this species have previously been seen in the lower part of the Tornio River, on the Swedish side.

Aedes rossicus likes shady places and flood areas. It bites people during the daytime.

There are about 40 mosquito species in Finland in total.

## Midsummer bonfires

![Midsummer celebrations at Seurasaari in 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10841438)

Open image viewer

Midsummer bonfires may not be lit even on water when a forest fire warning is in force. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Many midsummer bonfires will not be lit this year.

In southern and central Finland a forest fire warning and a grass fire warning are in force. This means that midsummer bonfires may not be lit in these areas. A bonfire may not be lit even if it is built on water.

Rain and thunderstorm showers have been forecast for midsummer. According to the forecast, however, the forest fire warning area will stay the same for the whole weekend.
