# Sunday 27.10.2019 (radio)

The leader of the terrorist organisation ISIS has died. Finland's most common road or street name is Rantatie. The ringed seal Sanelma has again received help in Saimaa. The weather is getting colder and the roads are slippery.

First, news from abroad.

The leader of the terrorist organisation ISIS has died. ISIS leader Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi died in an attack that the USA carried out in Syria.

President Donald Trump told about the American operation on Sunday. Trump said that al-Baghdadi blew himself up when the American soldiers arrived at the place.

In Trump's view, al-Baghdadi was the leader of the worst terrorist organisation in the world. That is why Trump wanted al-Baghdadi to be caught.

## Rantatie is the most common

![Rantatie road sign in Joutsa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3307,h_1860,x_0,y_718/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6044025db2de01c1647/1572004040)

This road name can be found in many places. Photo: Kalle Purhonen / Yle

The most common road or street name in Finland is Rantatie. There are 226 Rantatie roads in Finland.

Rantatie is a common road name, because Finland has many lakes. This is what Tiina Manni-Lindqvist from the Institute for the Languages of Finland says.

The second most common road or street name is Kirkkotie. In third place is Koulutie.

In fourth place is Teollisuustie. Its popularity has grown, because many new industrial areas have been built in Finland.

New road and street names keep coming, but some names are old. For example, in Turku Hämeenkatu has existed for almost 600 years.

## Sanelma the ringed seal got help

![Farewell moment of Sanelma and Pekka Sarkanen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10924098)

Sanelma the ringed seal needed help already in the summer. Photo: Petri Vironen / Yle

Still news from Lake Saimaa.

The rescue service received a report on Saturday evening about a Saimaa ringed seal that was on the shore of the lake. The seal was not well.

A veterinarian went to check the seal's condition. He noticed that Sanelma, the ringed seal, was on the shore. Sanelma had received help from people already in the summer, when it was in poor condition.

The veterinarian prescribed food for Sanelma, that is, one kilo of vendace, and medicine. Sanelma got food from cottage owners. Its condition improved so much that on Sunday it swam back into the lake.

However, the veterinarian intends to follow Sanelma's situation. In the veterinarian's view, Sanelma needs more care so that it gets fat enough. A Saimaa ringed seal must be fat enough to survive the winter.

## The road may be slippery

![Snowy road in Lapinlahti](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11029621)

Open image viewer

Drivers must now be careful, because the roads are slippery. Photo: Anne-Pauliina Rytkönen / Yle

The weather is getting colder. In Lapland, in Utsjoki, 23.4 degrees of frost was measured on Sunday morning.

During the early part of the week, the weather will get colder across Finland. At night there will be frost across the whole country.

Snow or sleet may fall a little even in Southern Finland.

Because of night frost and sleet, the roads are slippery. It is good to have winter tyres on your car across the whole country.
