# Friday 12.7.2019 (radio)

All the verdicts have now been given in the Oulu sexual crime case. Prime Minister Antti Rinne has met Finns whose relatives are in the Isis camp in Syria. The first new kind of surveillance camera has been put into use on the roads. There are large forest fires in Alaska. The weekend in Finland is cool.

## Verdicts in the sexual crime case

![Oulu courthouse.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10698386)

Open image viewer

The court has given verdicts in the Oulu sexual crime case. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

All the verdicts have now been given in the Oulu sexual crime case. The court gave the first verdicts in May and the last verdicts today, Friday.

On Friday the court sentenced 2 men to prison. The men will go to prison for 4 years.

The men received prison sentences because they raped and sexually abused a 13-year-old girl.

In total 8 men have been convicted in the Oulu sexual crime case. All of them have received prison sentences.

All the men sexually abused the same girl. The men have a foreign background.

## The Finns in the Isis camp

![Antti Rinne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10870567)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Antti Rinne wanted to meet relatives of the Finnish women in the Isis camp. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Antti Rinne has met Finns whose relatives are in the Isis camp in Syria. Rinne wanted to discuss the situation in the camp.

The camp holds many family members of Isis terrorists from different countries. Among them are also about 40 Finns. They are the wives and children of Isis terrorists.

The Finnish women in the Isis camp have said that they want to come back to Finland. The Finnish government has not yet decided whether it will help the women.

Finland hopes that Finnish children can get out of the Isis camp. However, the government has no plan for how the children will be brought out of the camp.

## A new kind of surveillance camera

![Speed surveillance camera](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10871613)

Open image viewer

A new kind of camera monitors the speed of cars. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

The first new kind of surveillance camera has been put into use on the roads. The camera is on Route 6 in Kouvola.

The new camera can monitor several cars and lanes at the same time. More new kinds of cameras will be put into use once the first camera works well.

The cameras monitor the speed of cars. However, the new cameras take such precise pictures that they can also be used to monitor, for example, the use of seat belts.

## Forest fires in Alaska

![Forest fire in Alaska.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10866721)

Open image viewer

Forest has burned in Alaska. Photo: Alaska State Troopers / EPA

There are large forest fires in Alaska. Alaska is the northernmost state of the USA.

The forest has caught fire and is burning in intense heat. In Alaska it has been over 30 degrees warm. Normally in Alaska it is 20 degrees warm in July.

Weather forecasts now say that the heat in Alaska is ending.

Here in Finland the weekend is not hot. The temperature varies between 10 degrees in the north and 20 degrees in the south.

The sun may shine on Saturday in Western Finland. Otherwise the weekend is cloudy.

Next week the weather will warm up a little.
