# Radio Clear News Tuesday 8.8.2023

Storm in Finland. Storm in the Nordic countries. Bird flu. Weather.

## Storm in Finland

A strong storm came to Finland early on Tuesday morning. The storm was named Sylvia.

The storm was not quite as bad as was predicted on Monday. The wind knocked down trees onto roads and power lines in different parts of Finland. During the day almost 50,000 people were without electricity.

Some of the power cuts were repaired quickly.

The rescue services received a huge number of alarms when trees fell.

In Oulu, three children were swimming on an air mattress when a strong wind carried them 400 metres away from the shore. The children were rescued to the shore.

If the weather has caused damage to property, insurance may cover it. The insurance company Lähi-Tapiola reminds people that it is good to photograph the damage for the insurance company.

## Storm in the Nordic countries

![A river floods in Norway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_161/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115379564d2439f0bfba/1691502255)

The weather in Sweden and Norway has been much worse than in Finland. In Sweden the storm caused a big flood in the village of Åre in Jämtland.

The Swedish authorities warned that floods may still come in the southern part of Sweden until Thursday.

In the central part of Norway there are mountains, and there have been floods and landslides there. In the central part of Norway at least two houses were buried under a landslide.

In the village of Leirsund near Oslo, several dozen people were evacuated because of a flood.

## Bird flu

![Two minks have risen against the mesh of a mink fur farm run by Markus Sjöholm from Uusikaarlepyy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6832,h_3843,x_0,y_900/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106577163d79121d8bca/1686551979)

Two minks in a cage on a fur farm. Photo: Janne Järvinen / Yle

The authorities say that bird flu has eased somewhat in Finland. The spread of the disease has slowed in birds and fur animals.

Bird flu spread in the summer from birds to fur animals that live on fur farms.

Bird flu has spread to more than 20 fur farms. According to the authorities, the spread of the disease to new fur farms has decreased.

Bird flu is dangerous for fur animals. There is a risk that the virus in fur animals changes and starts to spread to people.

80,000 fur animals have been put down because of the danger of bird flu.
