Government pulled back many bills. The Sámi Parliament Act goes to Parliament. Finland's economic growth may stop. Pig meat consumption is growing. Weather forecast.

The government has reached agreement in the dispute over bills. There has been a lot of dispute in the government over many bills. Because the government did not agree on all bills, it will not take all bills to Parliament.

Left out are, for example, the bills on capital gains tax, the euro conversion of unemployment benefits, regional study loan compensation, or congestion charges.

The government had to submit all remaining bills by Thursday at the latest, so that Parliament has time to deal with them before the end of the electoral term.

## Sámi Parliament Act

![People watching question time.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1035567637650673cdcd/1668698464)

The Sámi Parliament Act may be discussed a lot in Parliament. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The government is taking a bill on the Sámi Parliament Act to Parliament. There has been a big dispute in the government over the Sámi Parliament Act.

However, a majority of the government decided that the bill will be given to Parliament. In the government vote, 11 ministers supported taking the law to Parliament. 3 Centre Party ministers voted against it.

The purpose of the Sámi Parliament Act is to increase the self-determination of the Sámi. It is not yet known whether the law will get enough support in Parliament.

## Finnish economy

![grocery store shelves](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_114/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97323862b1cb69cc9f3/1655819774)

Open image viewer

Rising prices add to distrust of the economy. Photo: Susanna Pesonen / Yle

The International Monetary Fund IMF is assessing Finland's economic situation. The IMF says that Finland's economic growth may stop next year. The reason is the war in Ukraine.

The war adds many risks. People and companies are cautious. That is why economic growth is slowing down.

The IMF says that Finland's government has handled the coronavirus situation well. However, rapid price rises, that is inflation, reduce people's purchasing power. This reduces trust in the economy, because people buy fewer services and goods.

## Pig meat consumption

![Raw meatballs in a frying pan. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_258/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4748275abbbe2d76f31/1522253590)

Open image viewer

Minced meat consumption is also increasing, because minced meat is cheap. Photo: Mostphotos

Pork consumption has grown in Finland. Pork consumption started to grow in the summer. The reason is that food prices have risen. Pork is cheaper than beef. That is why people have started to buy more pork and less beef.

Natural Resources Institute Finland (Luke) says that total meat consumption has not grown much, but people have started to buy cheaper meat. That is also why minced meat consumption has grown.

Farming is difficult, because prices are rising. Pig farms are also having a hard time. The association Suomen Sikayrittäjät estimates that about 10 percent of pig farms will stop operating this year.
