The European Union's disease agency predicts that the corona pandemic will continue. The agency says that a new omicron variant will spread to Europe during November.

Based on tests, the variant evades the immune system more effectively than before, but it does not cause more serious disease.

## Grain ships

![A grain ship from Ukraine in the port of Istanbul.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5110,h_2874,x_0,y_425/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1026978635d3fff55d6e/1667067221)

Open image viewer

A grain ship from Ukraine in the port of Istanbul. Photo: Yasin Akgul / AFP

Finland supports the World Food Programme's grain ship's journey from Ukraine to Somalia.

The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says that at the same time Finland is increasing its humanitarian aid to the Horn of Africa, that is, north-eastern Africa. There more than 20 million people are in need of food aid.

The region is especially dependent on grain, which is brought from Russia and Ukraine. That is why the effects of Russia's war of aggression hit the region hard.

Finland is now giving the Horn of Africa almost 16 million euros in total. Of that, almost 7 million goes to supporting grain transport through the food programme.

## Hunger Day collection

![Sixth-graders Sanni Lahtinen and Melina Jalonen as Hunger Day collectors for the Red Cross.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4857,h_2732,x_0,y_250/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7226475f6c5819e0e82/1600936047)

Open image viewer

Sixth-graders Sanni Lahtinen and Melina Jalonen as Hunger Day collectors for the Red Cross. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

A total of 2.2 million euros was donated to this year's Hunger Day collection, says the Finnish Red Cross. The collection was on the streets and online in September and October.

The proceeds of the collection go to the Red Cross disaster fund. The disaster fund helps people living in the middle of disasters and conflicts.

In Finland the disaster fund is used to prepare for help in emergencies and accidents.

Last year the result of the Hunger Day campaign was 2.8 million euros.

## Lumo Energia

![Screenshot from Lumo Energia's website, with the text: &quot;Attention. Open image viewer

The electricity company Lumo Energia has filed for bankruptcy. The bankruptcy application was submitted to the Helsinki District Court.

Yle reported on Lumo Energia's bankruptcy plans at the end of October. At that time negotiations on customer compensation ended without a result. The negotiations were held with the Finnish Competition and Consumer Authority.

Lumo Energia stopped supplying electricity at the start of September. It terminated about 8,000 customers' fixed-term electricity contracts early.

## Parish elections

![Voters arrive to vote in the parish elections.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1023,h_576,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-100953763280da23effc/1663570275)

Parish elections are held every four years. In November almost 8,000 new trust members are elected to 354 parishes in total. Photo: Tuija Veirto/YLE

Advance voting in the parish elections of the Evangelical Lutheran Church has begun. The actual election day is 20 November.

In the parish elections the members of the church council and the parish council are chosen. They decide on the parish's administration and finance matters.
