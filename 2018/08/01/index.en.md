# Wednesday 1.8.2018 (radio)

Prisons are becoming smoke-free. The forest fires in Sweden are calming down. Crown jewels were stolen. Earth Overshoot Day is today. There were hot days in July. Thunder showers are coming.

## Smoking is not allowed in prison

![A cell in Helsinki prison.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10284228)

Open image viewer

Prisoners' smoking is being restricted in prisons. Photo: Criminal Sanctions Agency

In Finnish prisons, prisoners' smoking is being restricted.

Smoking will be banned inside all prisons. Prisoners may smoke 5 cigarettes a day outside.

Smoking is being restricted because the staff of the prisons want to be protected.

## Forest fires in Sweden are calming down

![Burnt forest in Kårbölen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10332606)

A lot of forest has burned in Sweden. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

The bad forest fires in Sweden are dying down.

The firefighters have brought the forest fires under control. There is still a lot of firefighting to do, however.

The Finnish firefighting team will still be part of the firefighting work for another week.

## Thieves stole crown jewels

![The stolen items included both crowns and the gilded orb of state in the middle of the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10332342)

Open image viewer

The stolen items are from the 1600s. Photo: The Royal Armoury / Public Domain CC 3.0 BY-SA

In Sweden, thieves have stolen real crown jewels from a church.

The thieves stole 2 crowns from the church, for example. They have crystal and pearls in them.

The items are so expensive and well known that the thieves cannot sell them anywhere. The items date from the 1600s.

The thieves may have escaped by boat. The church is on a lakeshore near Stockholm.

## People use too many natural resources

![Bamboo dishcloth and a traditional scrubbing brush under a water tap](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6944025)

Open image viewer

Consumption can be reduced by getting a washable bamboo dishcloth instead of a disposable one. Photo: Tuomas Hirvonen / Yle

Today is the world's Earth Overshoot Day.

That means that today the natural resources of the planet that should last the whole year have been used up. Using natural resources is not sustainable, says the World Wide Fund for Nature, WWF.

WWF calculates that in Finland the overshoot day was already in April.

## July was really hot

![People enjoy a hot summer day at Eiranranta in Helsinki on 30 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10331567)

Open image viewer

July had 27 hot days. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

July was the warmest on record, says the Finnish Meteorological Institute.

In total, July had 27 hot days in different parts of the country. A hot day is one where the temperature is 25 degrees or more.

The highest temperature was 33.7 degrees. It was measured in Vaasa.

It is now really warm in Southern Europe. In Portugal and Spain it may be as much as 50 degrees at the weekend.

## Thunder showers may come tomorrow

Tomorrow, Thursday, is also a fairly hot day. In Southern and Central Finland the temperature may be 30 degrees.

Lapland, on the other hand, is cooler.

Thunder showers may come across almost the whole country.
