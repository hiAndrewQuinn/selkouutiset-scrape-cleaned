# Friday 6.5.2022 (radio)

Demonstration of municipal workers. Reception centres. Swedish course for immigrants. Ice hockey World Championship. The weather.

## Demonstration of municipal workers

![Municipal sector demonstrators in Töölö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5280,h_2970,x_0,y_239/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9513666274fb0020127/1675702591)

Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

There has been a big demonstration in Helsinki. As many as 4000 municipal sector workers took part in the demonstration.

The demonstrators demand better wages. That is why the name of the demonstration was Pay Day.

The demonstration was linked to the municipal sector strike. About 80 000 municipal sector workers have stopped work for a week because of a dispute over wages.

There are teachers, for example, on strike. That is why many schools are now closed. The strikers will return to work on Tuesday.

A solution has been sought to the municipal sector disputes. Next Tuesday, a proposal for a solution will be given to the employer and the employees. If they accept the proposal, the municipal sector disputes will end.

## New reception centres

![A hall with sofas, a billiard table and an outside door.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4995,h_2809,x_4,y_489/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-92492262262699f1158/1646668318)

A reception centre in Mikkeli. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

The Finnish Immigration Service, or Migri, intends to set up new reception centres. Migri will also add places in reception centres that already operate.

Many asylum seekers will come to Finland this year. The reason is the war in Ukraine.

About 22 000 citizens of Ukraine have already come to Finland, and the number is growing. As many as 80 000 Ukrainian refugees may come to Finland this year.

## Swedish course for immigrants

![People in a classroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12435084)

Open image viewer

The Swedish course for Russian-speaking immigrants has continued all winter. Photo: Yle

Swedish language teaching is being organised in Finland for Russian-speaking immigrants.

One of the organisers is Folkhälsan. Folkhälsan is a health sector organisation that works in Swedish.

At the end of the course there is a language test. In it, the course participants can show that they can study or work in the Swedish language.

The aim of the course is for the course participants to get jobs in the health care sector. Workers who know Swedish are needed in the health care sector.

The course participants come from many different countries, but studying together goes well.

The ice hockey World Championship starts in Finland in a week, on Friday 13 May. The venues are Tampere and Helsinki.

In Tampere the World Championship games will be played in the new Tampere Kansi Arena. In ice hockey games, about 13 000 spectators fit into the arena.

The medal games will be played in Tampere at the end of May. On 29 May it will be known which country is this year's ice hockey world champion.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12435340)

Open image viewer

Friday has been the warmest day of the spring so far. In Kankaanpää the temperature was over 18 degrees.

On Saturday a rain area will arrive from the west, moving east across the country during the day.

After the rains cooler air will flow into Finland. In the east the temperature during the day is still about 15 degrees.

On Sunday the weather is cool, but widely sunny.
