## Corona vaccinations for lower secondary school pupils

![A nurse gives a corona vaccination at the Messukeskus in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81042360abc0b3148af/1629289995)

In Finland, corona vaccinations for lower secondary school pupils are being planned. Lower secondary school pupils means 12 to 15-year-olds.

Corona vaccinations for lower secondary school pupils may start before the school summer holidays end. However, an exact time cannot yet be given.

Chief physician Hanna Nohynek of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that 12 to 15-year-olds can catch corona. They can also pass it on to others. That is why it is good to give lower secondary school pupils a corona vaccination.

Corona vaccinations are already given to lower secondary school pupils who belong to risk groups. Risk groups include people who have a serious illness.

Hanna Nohynek estimates that all 12 to 15-year-olds will get 2 corona vaccinations, perhaps by October or November.

## Corona figures in Finland

![Two nurses and a patient on a corona ward in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11819309)

Open image viewer

There are 56 corona patients in hospital care. Photo: Tuomas Aro / Yle Kuvapalvelu

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare said on Wednesday that there are 453 new corona infections in Finland.

The number of new corona infections has grown, but there are not many more patients in hospital care than before. The reason is that many Finns already have a corona vaccination. It gives good protection against serious corona disease.

Now there are 56 corona patients in hospital care.

## Floods in China

![People wade through flood water on a street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3937,h_2215,x_0,y_271/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83386660f7bfb2d817e/1626849272)

Open image viewer

It has rained a record amount in Henan in China. Photo: AFP

There are serious floods in China. Large areas of Henan province are under water.

For example, in the capital of the province 12 people died when the water flooded the metro passages.

The cause of the floods is heavy rain. Meteorologists say that rain this heavy comes only once in 1,000 years.

## The crayfish season has started

![Crayfish in a bowl.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9048900)

Open image viewer

A few million crayfish are caught a year. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

The crayfish season has started.

Crayfish are caught in Finnish waters until the end of October. A few million crayfish are caught in Finland each year.

Crayfish may be caught only with the permission of the owner of the water area. In addition, a fishing management fee must be paid.

Especially Swedish-speaking Finns organise crayfish parties. The crayfish are prepared by boiling.
