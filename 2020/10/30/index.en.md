# Friday 30.10.2020 (radio)

On Friday 344 new coronavirus infections were reported. The coronavirus is spreading fast around the world. A strong earthquake has happened in Turkey and Greece. Yle's Swedish-language Clear News has won an award.

## Coronavirus infections in Finland

![Mika Salminen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11568262)

Open image viewer

THL director Mika Salminen. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

On Friday a record number of new coronavirus infections was reported. THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, said that there are 344 new coronavirus infections in Finland.

THL says that Friday's figure includes coronavirus infections from several days. THL director Mika Salminen says that the autumn holidays may also have delayed information about coronavirus infections.

So far, almost 16,000 coronavirus infections have been reported in Finland.

THL also said on Friday that 4 new coronavirus deaths have been reported in Finland.

The coronavirus is spreading fast around the world. The new infection figures are large.

In the USA the daily number of infections was over 90,000. In Russia, 18,000 new coronavirus infections were reported.

There are also many new coronavirus infections in Europe. For example, in Germany about 19,000 new coronavirus infections were reported per day.

## Earthquake in Turkey and Greece

![People amid the ruins of a collapsed building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11623674)

Open image viewer

The earthquake broke houses in Turkey and Greece. Photo: Mehmet Emin Menguarslan / EPA

A strong earthquake has happened in Turkey and Greece. The earthquake happened at sea, between Turkey and Greece.

People have died in the earthquake, and many have been injured. The earthquake also broke houses.

The strength of the earthquake was as high as 7.0, so it was unusually strong.

## Award for Yle's Swedish-language Clear News

![Woman at a desk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11195128)

Open image viewer

Yle has given an award to the Swedish-language Clear News as the best Swedish-language audio content of the year. Journalist Ann-Lis Fredriksson is one of the programme's news writers. Photo: Yle

Last, here is an award story. Yle's Swedish-language Clear News has won an award.

Swedish-language Clear News, i.e. Yle Nyheter på lätt svenska, is Yle's best Swedish-language audio content this year.

Swedish-language Clear News is written in easy Swedish, and it is read slowly. That is why listeners who find ordinary Swedish-language news too difficult also understand it.

You can listen to Yle's Swedish-language Clear News on the radio and on Yle Areena. On Yle Areena you can also read the texts of the news.

Yle makes programmes in both of Finland's languages, i.e. in Finnish and in Swedish.

Listen to and read the Swedish-language Clear News on Yle Areena
