## Patient safety act

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin at question time in Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_215/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10085386323386fa06ff/1663312882)

The government has reached agreement on the patient safety act. The patient safety act restricts nurses' right to strike.

There was a dispute in the government about the law, because the Left Alliance does not like that the right to strike is restricted. The government has now agreed on the content of the law, so that all government parties will approve the law.

The aim is that the regional state administrative agency can, for example, postpone a nurses' strike or ban a strike for a fixed period, if the strike endangers patient safety. '

The plan is that Parliament will approve the law on Monday.

## Nurses' demonstration

![Tehy and Super organised a demonstration on a rainy Friday in Helsinki in September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4999,h_2811,x_0,y_17/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1009044632466ddb4c01/1671646244)

Open image viewer

About 600 nurses gathered in Helsinki for the demonstration. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Nurses organised a demonstration in Helsinki on Friday. About 600 nurses from different parts of Finland took part in the demonstration.

Nurses oppose the government's law proposal, which restricts the right to strike. Nurses say that the patient safety act violates nurses' basic rights.

Nurses say that the law takes away nurses' right to industrial action, and therefore the law also makes the nurse shortage worse.

## Storm in Italy

![Damage caused by the flood is being cleaned up on a street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3072,h_1728,x_0,y_82/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-100911963247afb6405f/1663335312)

Open image viewer

People cleaned up the damage of the flood in Senigallia, Italy, on Friday. Photo: Carlo Leone / EPA

There has been a severe storm in central Italy. On Friday it was reported that at least 10 people have died in the storm in Italy. The wind was strong and a lot of rain fell.

The worst damage was caused by the storm in the Marche region in central Italy. A lot of water flooded the city streets. The water swept cars away. Electricity and phone connections were cut in many places because of the storm.

In Italy it has been very dry in the summer. Now a lot of rain has fallen. The strong variation in weather may be a result of climate change.

On Friday evening a service was held in Helsinki in memory of Queen Elizabeth.

The service was organised by the Finnish Anglican Church and the British Embassy. The memorial service was open to all people.

Queen Elizabeth will be buried on Monday. Yle will show the funeral on TV1 on Monday from 12.30.
