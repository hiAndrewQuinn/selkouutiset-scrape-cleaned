# Wednesday 24.8.2022 (radio)

Prime Minister Marin's party pictures. The SDP's parliamentary group. The cost of housing. Ukraine's national day. Weather forecast.

## The prime minister's partying

![Sanna Marin, Antti Lindtman and Kristiina Salonen take part in the summer meeting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4665,h_2624,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99887863062e1963ab7/1661349583)

Prime Minister Marin and the SDP's parliamentary group have been at a meeting in Lahti. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Sanna Marin has commented on party pictures that have become public. Last week a video came out in which Marin parties heartily with friends. This week a picture taken by other people at a party at the prime minister's official residence in Kesäranta came out.

The pictures have sparked a lot of debate in Finland. Some Finns think that the prime minister may party hard in their free time. Some Finns think that the prime minister should not party so hard that pictures of it spread to the public.

Marin spoke in Lahti today. Marin said that she will learn from what happened.

## The SDP's parliamentary group and Marin's partying

![Antti Lindtman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99627062fe34ca0b8d6/1660826912)

Open image viewer

Chair of the SDP's parliamentary group Antti Lindtman. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto/Lehtikuva

The SDP's parliamentary group has discussed the matter of Prime Minister Sanna Marin's partying and how the matter affects the party. Sanna Marin is the chairperson of the SDP.

The chair of the SDP's parliamentary group, Antti Lindtman, says that the parliamentary group has had an open discussion about the matter. Lindtman says that the SDP's parliamentary group appreciates that Marin promises to learn from the events.

Lindtman says that Marin has the support of the SDP's parliamentary group.

## The cost of housing

![A detached house viewed from above. There is forest in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3648,h_2052,x_0,y_957/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99877063060b1116d6e/1661341677)

Open image viewer

Living can become very much more expensive in detached houses with electric heating. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

The cost of housing is rising. A report by the Property Federation and the Finnish Detached House Association says that the share of housing expenses grows a lot this year.

People are left with less money for other things, because a bigger share of people's money goes to housing than before.

The biggest reason is the rise in the price of energy. Housing costs grow especially in detached houses with electric heating.

## Ukraine's national day

![A carpet painted in the colours of the Ukrainian flag in Imatra on 24.08.2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9987026305fe422a3c2/1661337977)

Open image viewer

Ukraine's national day is 24 August. Photo: Kare Lehtonen / Yle

Today it is half a year since Russia started the attack on Ukraine.

At first Russia took over large areas of southern Ukraine. Russia failed in northern Ukraine, for example in capturing the capital Kyiv.

Russia has retreated in the north. After the retreat, several Russian war crimes came to light in the north. The front lines of Russia and Ukraine have come to a standstill.

Today, Wednesday, is also Ukraine's national day. It has also been commemorated in other countries. In Finland, for example, many towns flew Ukrainian flags.
