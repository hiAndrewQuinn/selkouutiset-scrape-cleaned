## Own hospital district is praised for fighting corona

![Vaccinated customers wait to leave after the vaccination at a corona vaccination centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11832951)

Finns think their own hospital district has done best in fighting corona. The Municipal Development Foundation looked into this.

65 percent of respondents give a good grade to their own hospital district.

Their own home municipality has done the second best in fighting it. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare is in third place.

More than half of respondents say that the government has done very or fairly well. However, more than a quarter say that the government has done poorly in stopping corona.

## Criminals have been selling fake vaccines

![The shape of a person is reflected in the glass door of a corona vaccination site. The window has text that says corona vaccination.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5493,h_3090,x_0,y_575/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7862646054bea2eb703/1616167157)

Open image viewer

There is definitely real vaccine in the EU area. No fraud attempt by criminals has succeeded. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

Criminals have tried to sell fake corona vaccinations. Attempts have been made to sell them to EU member states for a total of 14 billion euros.

The European Anti-Fraud Office, OLAF, reports this.

The head of OLAF, Ville Itälä, tells Yle that fraud attempts have also been reported in Finland.

OLAF is not aware of a single fraud attempt that has succeeded.

## Turkey has left an agreement that protected women

![Recep Tayyip Erdoğan](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11593318)

Open image viewer

President of Turkey Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. Photo: Adem Altan / AFP

Turkey has left an international agreement that fights violence against women.

The Istanbul Convention was signed in 2011. Laws based on it have made it possible to bring charges for things like domestic violence and rape within marriage.

Domestic violence and killings of women are a serious problem in Turkey. A local civil society organisation says that 300 women were murdered in Turkey last year.

## Finns are among the world's most efficient at recycling bottles

![Bottle return machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10815010)

Open image viewer

Bottle return machines. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

Finland recycles drink packages more efficiently than any other country.

The company Suomen Palautuspakkaus says that last year more than 90 percent of bottles and cans with a deposit were returned for recycling. A deposit is paid on a drink package, and you get money back when you return it.

Last year, Finland passed the limit of 2 billion returns. Aluminium cans were returned the most.

## The Saimaa ringed seal's winter has been good

![Saimaa ringed seal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_113/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-77190860226cd4c7cef/1612868884)

Open image viewer

Saimaa ringed seal on a rock. Photo: Jouni Koskela

Saimaa ringed seals have bred very well this winter, says Metsähallitus.

Breeding has been helped by there being enough snow on the shores, which has been piled into drifts.

Saimaa ringed seals live only in Lake Saimaa in Finland. The Saimaa ringed seal is still critically endangered, which means it is at risk of disappearing. The seal will need protection for a long time yet.

## The spring equinox brings us towards spring

![Morning of the spring equinox in Hesperia park in Helsinki, 20 March 2017. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9519517)

Today, Saturday, is the spring equinox. Day and night are roughly equal in length everywhere on Earth.

As spring goes on, the days get longer and the nights get shorter in Finland and elsewhere in the northern hemisphere.
