# Sunday 16.12.2018 (radio)

The climate meeting in Poland has ended. The state's economic situation is good this year. The Kennel Club rewarded 18 hero dogs. On Monday it snows.

## The climate meeting in Poland has ended

![Michał Kurtyka](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10556913)

People at the climate meeting were happy when the negotiations succeeded. Photo: Janek Skarzynski / AFP

The climate meeting in Poland has ended. The countries of the world agreed in Poland on the rules of the climate agreement. The climate agreement was made in Paris in 2015, and it comes into force in 2020.

The Paris climate agreement requires that countries reduce emissions that warm the earth's climate. Emissions come, for example, from industry and traffic. When a person burns coal or oil, for example, carbon dioxide gets into the air. It warms the climate.

In Poland it was agreed how the amount of emissions will be monitored. The climate meeting decided that all countries will report on emissions using the same rules. That is how monitoring emissions is reliable. However, poor countries get extra time so that they can develop the measuring of emissions.

The aim of the climate agreement is that the earth's climate warms by less than 2 degrees. If the climate warms too much, people also suffer. For example, food may run out for many people, because the warming of the climate brings storms and drought.

## The state's economic situation is good

![Twenty-euro notes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10032641)

Open image viewer

Finland's state income has been surprisingly good this year. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finland's state income this year is bigger than its spending. That is why Finland can reduce state debt. That has not happened in 10 years.

Because of the state's good economic situation, Finland does not need to take out more loans at the end of this year.

Helsingin Sanomat wrote about the matter.

## Hero dogs received an award

![Turre, a dog.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10512700)

Open image viewer

Turre is a hero dog. It found an old woman in the forest. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

Finland has received new hero dogs. 18 dogs received the hero dog award on Sunday.

The dogs received the award from the Kennel Club. The Kennel Club is an organisation for dog enthusiasts.

The Kennel Club gives the hero dog award to a dog that has saved a person. A dog can, for example, warn of a fire or fetch help for a person who has a medical attack.

## Snow all over the country

![A woman walks with poles in a snowstorm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9268305)

Open image viewer

The weather forecast promises snowfall. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

On Monday it snows. Snowfall starts in the south already on Sunday evening, and on Monday morning there may already be several centimetres of snow.

Yle's meteorologist Toni Hellinen warns drivers. Drivers must allow more time for traffic than usual.

During Monday the snowfall moves from south to north. It snows over the whole of Finland.

It may snow so much that Christmas is white in the whole country. A more precise weather forecast for Christmas will come during the early part of the week.
