## Christmas peace

![Registry office secretary, licentiate in law Eero Soikkanen reads the Christmas peace proclamation on the balcony of the Brinkkala house in 1962.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4518925a3a56f010280/1513773012)

Here registry office secretary, licentiate in law Eero Soikkanen reads the Christmas peace proclamation on the balcony of the Brinkkala house in 1962. Photo: Yle kuvanauha / collage: Elina Yli-Ojanperä

The Christmas peace has once again been proclaimed in Turku at the Old Great Square. The proclamation was read from the balcony of the Brinkkala house by protocol chief Mika Akkanen. For him, this was already the tenth time he has read the proclamation.

The atmosphere was familiar again, because there was an audience present after two corona years.

The Christmas peace proclamation has been read in Turku for 700 years, ever since the 1300s. The Christmas peace could not be proclaimed in full because of fear of air raids at Christmas 1939, and during the militia strike in 1917.

## Lapland tourism

![Skiers in the lift queue at Levi at the 2022 season opening.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_529/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1017914633fd1bec722c/1665126933)

Skiers in the lift queue at Levi at the 2022 season opening. Photo: Pekka Viinikka / Yle

International tourists have returned to Finnish Lapland after the coronavirus restrictions.

Most of the tourists in Rovaniemi and Saariselkä come from abroad. More domestic travellers come to the Levi fell resort.

In Lapland's big tourist centres, most accommodation is booked until the end of the year. Visit Rovaniemi and Visit Levi say that there are already almost as many travellers as before the coronavirus pandemic.

## Good Christmas Spirit collection

![Logo of the Good Christmas Spirit collection.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9993588)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

The Good Christmas Spirit collection has brought a good result. The collection is organised by the Finnish Red Cross and the Mannerheim League for Child Welfare.

Food gift cards from the collection have been distributed to low-income families with children across Finland. The value of a gift card is 70 euros. This year 21,000 families received a gift card.

More than 10 percent of children live in a low-income family. Rising prices have increased the difficulties of families with children.

The Veikko and Lahja Hurst Charity Work organisation again organised a Christmas celebration for lonely and low-income people. The celebration was at the Helsinki Messukeskus. The Christmas celebration for lonely people has been held for many decades; only in the corona years was there a break.

## Winter storm

![Man tries to move a car stuck in snow in Delaware.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10009924)

Open image viewer

A winter storm in the USA is disrupting traffic and flights. Photo: Mark Wilson / AFP

An unusually strong and wide winter storm in North America has caused chaos in traffic. Because of large multi-car pile-up crashes, many motorways were closed in the USA and Canada.

Thousands of flights have been cancelled.

More than 200 million people in the USA and Canada have been warned about the storm and severe frost.

## Weather

![Finland's weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12686814)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

On Christmas Day, Sunday, the weather is quite sunny. Snow showers are moving mainly in the north.

The temperature in the south and west of the country is between -2 and -8 degrees. In the east and north there is frost of 9 to 20 degrees.

On St Stephen's Day, Monday, cloud cover increases slightly, the weather warms up, and snow showers spread to Lapland. In the west light snow may fall.
