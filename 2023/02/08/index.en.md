# Wednesday 8.2.2023 (radio)

More than 11,000 people have already died in the earthquakes in Turkey and Syria. A strike begins in shops. The Government answered the opposition's interpellation. The weather is windy.

## Earthquakes in Turkey and Syria

![A building destroyed in the earthquake.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3658,h_2057,x_0,y_4/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106998963e32e7fbde25/1675833041)

The number of people who have died in the earthquakes in Turkey and Syria is rising. More than 11,000 have already died in the quakes. Most likely even more people died in the earthquakes, because many people are missing.

Tens of thousands of people were injured in the earthquakes. The earthquake destroyed the homes of millions of people in Turkey and Syria.

In Turkey many people are angry that the rescue work is going too slowly.

## Strike in shops

![People shopping at Prisma.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107023063e39a1957175/1683522331)

The shop strike begins tomorrow, on Thursday.

Kaupan Liitto says that workers will go on strike in 185 Kesko and S-Group shops in different parts of Finland. The strike ends on Saturday morning. About 20,000 workers will go on strike.

Workers and employers have not reached an agreement on pay rises. Shop workers demand 200 euros more in monthly pay. In the employer's view this is too much.

In a week there will be a bigger strike in shops, if the labour dispute is not resolved before then.

## Interpellation

![Annika Saarikko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5669,h_3188,x_0,y_220/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107025363e39ee7b76d0/1675867220)

In Parliament the Government has answered the opposition's interpellation. The opposition accuses the Government of having taken on too much debt.

The Government replied that it had to take on debt. Finance Minister Annika Saarikko said that money was needed, for example, to deal with the corona epidemic. Russia's attack on Ukraine also caused costs for Finland.

## Repayment of study grants

![Lecture hall for medical studies at the University of Tampere.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_625/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106925663e10da81a918/1675693799)

Open image viewer

Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

Many students have to pay back study grants. Kela says that about 47,000 students have to pay back study grants. This is 6,000 more students than a year earlier.

A student has to pay back study grants of 976 euros on average.

A student has to pay back study grants if they have earned wages from work above the income limit. This year the income limit is 18,700 euros.
