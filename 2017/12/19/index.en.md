## Stronger beer will soon come to the shops

![Beer cans.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9954263)

Open image viewer

Beer cans in a row. Photo: Eveliina Matikainen / Yle

Parliament has approved the reform of the alcohol law and a rise in the alcohol tax.

The decision was made with 124 votes for and 65 against. The reform means that shops, kiosks and petrol stations can sell alcoholic drinks of 5.5 percent.

Shops can sell stronger beer, strong cider and mixes made from spirits.

The new alcohol law will come into force next year at the beginning of March.

## A crime report about MP Hakkarainen

![Teuvo Hakkarainen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9983675)

Open image viewer

Teuvo Hakkarainen is trying to give up alcohol. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

National Coalition Party MP Veera Ruoho is making a crime report about MP Teuvo Hakkarainen. Last Thursday, the Finns Party's Christmas party caused disturbances in Parliament.

Ruoho said that MP Hakkarainen tried to kiss her by force. Ruoho was in the café during a break in the session when Hakkarainen grabbed her by the neck and tried to kiss her.

Hakkarainen has received a serious warning from his parliamentary group. He has expressed regret for what happened on Facebook and apologised to Veera Ruoho.

Hakkarainen says that he intends to give up alcohol.

President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio have received the traditional song greeting at Mäntyniemi. The Christmas greeting was performed by Finland's Lucia maiden.

The presidential couple also received the traditional Christmas greetings from organisations. They include organic vegetables, a Christmas tree, a ham and a pike.

## The Christmas collections were a success

![A woman and a big pile of presents](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7690148)

Open image viewer

Marjo Leino wraps presents donated to the Christmas Tree collection.  Photo: Dmitri Volgin / Yle

Christmas-time collections for the poor have grown year by year. This year the collections are raising a great deal of money. The amount is bigger than before.

For example, a collection called Christmas Tree has reached a new record in Helsinki this year. Over 20,000 present packages have been received. They will be given to child welfare clients.

## The flu is going around

![A person blows their nose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5097473)

Open image viewer

You must not sneeze on other people. Photo: Arja Lento / Yle

A lot of flu is going around now. It spreads easily in workplaces, because it can pass from one colleague to another through a sneeze.

According to a researcher, you must not sneeze on other people. It is good to cough into a tissue or your sleeve, and it is good to wash your hands a lot. It is best to stay at home if you are really ill. Viruses spread easily when there are many people in one place.

The actual influenza season has not started yet.
