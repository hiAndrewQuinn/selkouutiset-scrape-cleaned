# Sunday 18.12.2022 (radio)

Emissions trading. Accident in Afghanistan. Ukraine at Eurovision. Lack of Christmas presents. Weather.

## Emissions trading

![Coal power plant towers and chimneys, with steam rising into the sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12256644)

Open image viewer

Emissions from industry and energy plants must be reduced so that global warming slows down. Photo: Sasha Steinbach / EPA

The European Union is reforming emissions trading. The idea of emissions trading is that pollution must be paid for.

However, industry has had free emission allowances. Now it has been decided that free emission allowances will no longer be handed out from 2026 onwards.

In Finland, free emission allowances have benefited steel mills and the forest industry, for example.

The EU is reforming emissions trading, so that global warming can be slowed down.

## Accident in Afghanistan

A serious accident has happened in Afghanistan. A tanker lorry exploded in a tunnel on Saturday evening.

At least 14 people died and more than 20 were injured. Among the dead are children and women.

The accident happened in the Salang Tunnel. The tunnel connects the Afghan capital Kabul and northern Afghanistan.

## Eurovision

![2 men whom journalists are filming.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12685546)

The pop group Tvorchi (on the right) is Ukraine's representative at Eurovision. Photo: Gennady Minchenko / EPA

Ukraine has chosen a representative for the Eurovision song contest. Ukraine's Eurovision representative is a pop group of 2 men.

Ukraine's representative was chosen in a broadcast made from a Kyiv metro station. The metro station has also been a bomb shelter during the war.

The Eurovision song contest will be held in May in England. England came 2nd in last spring's Eurovision. The win went to Ukraine, but Ukraine cannot host Eurovision because of the war.

Russia's war in Ukraine has lasted almost 10 months already. Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

## Lack of Christmas presents

![Christmas gift parcels](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7570905fe2df16b0daf/1608722432)

Open image viewer

Not everyone can afford to buy Christmas presents. Photo: Kari Saastamoinen / Yle

Rising prices affect many families' Christmas. Many now have to save on Christmas presents too.

Professor of educational psychology Niina Junttila says that it is good for parents to talk honestly about this with children. It is good to tell a child if there is no money for Christmas presents.

Junttila says that Christmas can be nice even without presents. Parents can do all kinds of nice things together with the children. Then Christmas leaves a nice memory for the child, Professor Junttila says.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12685547)

Open image viewer

On Sunday there were big differences in temperature in different parts of the country. In southern Finland the temperature rose above zero, but in Lapland there was frost of almost 30 degrees in places.

On Monday the temperature in the south is around zero, and in the north there is still frost. Much of the country is dry, that is, no precipitation will fall.

On Tuesday the weather warms up, and the temperature rises above zero in the south. New precipitation will come to Finland on Tuesday, and in the south it may be rain.
