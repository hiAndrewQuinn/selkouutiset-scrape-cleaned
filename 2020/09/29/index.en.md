# Tuesday 29.9.2020 (radio)

The coronavirus pandemic is accelerating. Schoolchildren need more support. The price of food has not risen much. The Linna Party becomes a television programme. Weather.

## The coronavirus pandemic is accelerating

![A mask in a person's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7157325f538865b4b99/1599329098)

Open image viewer

It is good to wear a face mask always in places where many people are close together, for example on a bus, in a theatre, in an upper secondary school and in a vocational school. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The coronavirus pandemic is accelerating in the Helsinki area. Infections have increased. In particular, infections among 20 to 30-year-olds have increased.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that the municipalities in the Helsinki area must increase precautions.

Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa have given a recommendation on face masks. The cities recommend that people over 15 years old wear a face mask in upper secondary schools, vocational schools and other places where many people are present.

## Corona has increased the need for support in schools

![Many schools are going back to in-person teaching next week. In the picture, pupils of Otalampi school in a history and social studies lesson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11477982)

Open image viewer

Most comprehensive school pupils were in remote teaching in the spring. Now the schools are open normally again. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

Many comprehensive school pupils need more support for schooling than before. Pupils were in remote teaching a lot in the spring because of the corona situation.

Yle asked different municipalities how the exceptional conditions of the spring have affected pupils. As many as 30 percent of pupils need more support for schooling than before.

Municipalities have hired more teachers and school assistants, for example, to help pupils.

## Corona did not raise the price of food much

![Close-up of a S-market shopping basket with purchases.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11338379)

Open image viewer

Berries, fruit, meat and vegetables are getting most expensive. Photo: Eleni Paspatis / Yle

The corona situation has not raised the price of food much in Finland. Food products have been brought to Finland from abroad well.

Researchers estimate that the price of food may rise by 1.5 percent in Finland this year. Next year the price of food may rise by 0.5 percent.

Berries and fruit are getting the most expensive. Meat and vegetables are also getting more expensive.

## The Linna Party has been cancelled

![The president dancing at the Linna Party.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11107088)

Open image viewer

There is no celebration at the Presidential Palace this year, but Yle is broadcasting a lot of programmes about the Independence Day events. Photo: AOP

The traditional Independence Day reception, the Linna Party, has been cancelled for this year. Usually about 2,000 people gather for the Linna Party. Because of the corona situation, no party will be held this year.

President **Sauli Niinistö** says that Independence Day will now be celebrated with all Finns. Yle is showing Independence Day events on television and online. Yle is also showing compilations of old Linna Parties.

Finland's Independence Day is 6 December.
