The terrorist organisation Isis is still a danger to Europe. This is how Finland's Security Intelligence Service estimates it.

The head of the Security Intelligence Service, Antti Pelttari, said in an interview with Yle that there are also Isis supporters in Finland.

In Denmark, police arrested more than 10 people on Friday. The police suspect that they were planning a terror attack.

The Danish police do not yet know whether Isis is behind the plan. It may also be that the arrested people got the idea from the internet.

## Corona anxiety

![An elderly person walks up stairs in Shibuya, Tokyo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11788809)

Open image viewer

The coronavirus time has increased the loneliness of Japanese people. Photo: Yoshiyoshi Hirokawa / Getty Images

People around the world are talking about corona anxiety. People feel bad, because they are often alone because of the coronavirus crisis.

For example, in Japan many suicides have been committed. Last year, almost 21,000 Japanese people took their own lives. That number is clearly higher than before the coronavirus.

Now there is a new minister in the Japanese government, whose job is to improve the situation. The minister must reduce the problems whose cause is loneliness.

## Melting water pipes

![Water pipes being blown with a hairdryer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7733919)

Open image viewer

If water pipes freeze, it is not worth melting them yourself. Photo: Tarja Nyyssönen / Yle

Hard winter frosts may have frozen water pipes, for example in detached houses.

The central organisation of the rescue sector reminds people that melting water pipes is work for professionals.

Water pipes can break if residents melt them themselves, for example with a hot-air blower. Then there is also a big risk of fire.

## World Radio Day

![Journalists Samuli Aaltonen and Elina Rantalainen in the radio studio in Lahti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6120,h_3443,x_0,y_57/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7736116026828792c0f/1613136691)

Open image viewer

Photo: Juha-Petri Koponen / Yle

On Saturday World Radio Day was celebrated. World Radio Day is celebrated on 13 February.

World Radio Day is an idea of the UNESCO organisation. UNESCO is part of the UN, that is, the United Nations.

UNESCO wants people to notice how important radio is. Radio can pass on information freely, even across national borders.

Radio is an old medium. Radio is more than 110 years old.
