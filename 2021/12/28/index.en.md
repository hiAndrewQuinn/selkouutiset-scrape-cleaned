## Corona restrictions

![Equipment and weights in a large gym.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6073,h_3416,x_1,y_632/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-877958618ba74f71a16/1636545259)

Corona restrictions have been tightened. New corona restrictions came into force today, on Tuesday. The corona restrictions are being tightened because corona is now spreading quickly.

The new corona restrictions apply to restaurants, for example. In most of Finland, restaurants must stop selling alcohol already at 5 pm and close their doors at 6 pm.

In addition, there are separate corona restrictions in different areas. For example, in the Helsinki and Uusimaa region, events with an audience may not be organised. Therefore, theatre performances, for example, have been cancelled until 17 January.

Hobby facilities for adults in the Helsinki and Uusimaa region have also been closed. For example, sports facilities and gyms will stay closed until 10 January. Hobby activities for children may be organised.

## Border control

![Suitcases in the arrivals hall of Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5027,h_2828,x_0,y_330/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83538760fe717b29b2a/1627288001)

Because of the bad corona situation, border control in Finland is also being tightened.

Now passengers arriving in Finland from the European Union area are also checked. They too must have a certificate of a negative corona test result.

Every passenger must also have a certificate of corona vaccinations, or proof that they have had corona disease.

## Party support

![Poll of party support before the regional elections.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12245491)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

Yle has published a survey of party support before the regional elections.

In the survey, the National Coalition Party gets the most support. Its support is almost 22 per cent. In the survey, the SDP is second and the Finns Party is third.

The first regional elections will be held on 23 January. In the elections, the regional councils of the different areas are elected. The regional councils decide on social and health care, that is, for example, care for the elderly and health centres.

Finland is playing well at the junior ice hockey world championships. Players at the junior world championships are men under 20 years old.

On Monday evening Finland beat Austria. The Finnish team, Nuoret Leijonat, was clearly better than the Austrian team. Finland won the game 7-1.

The junior ice hockey world championships are played in Canada. Finland's next game is on Wednesday. Then Finland plays the Czech Republic.
