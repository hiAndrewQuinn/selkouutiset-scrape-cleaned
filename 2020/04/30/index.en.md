# Thursday 30.4.2020 (radio)

Corona infections in Finland. Primary schools will open. A company starts making masks. There is a shortage of strawberry pickers. The weather stays cool.

## The corona is spreading too slowly in Finland

![A restaurant closed because of the coronavirus in the Iso Omena shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11328196)

Open image viewer

Restaurants have been closed for many weeks. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Health authorities say that the coronavirus is spreading too slowly in Finland.

If the virus spreads this slowly, it may happen that in the autumn there will suddenly be a very large number of infections.

That is not a good thing, the health authorities say.

In Finland there are now officially almost 5,000 coronavirus infections. 211 people have died of the coronavirus in Finland.

## Primary schools will open

![Empty classroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11328822)

Open image viewer

Primary school pupils return to school on 14 May. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Primary schools will open on 14 May.

Distance teaching ends, and schoolchildren go back to school.

Schools must make sure that pupils and teachers are safe.

Schools must divide pupils into small groups. For example, small children may be at school in the morning and older children in the afternoon.

Spring celebrations will not be held.

Upper secondary schools, vocational educational institutions and universities will continue distance teaching.

## A company starts making masks

![Respirator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6518545e679fb629c76/1583849524)

Open image viewer

A company from Oulu is starting to make respirators like these. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

A company from Oulu is starting to make respirators and masks.

There is a serious shortage of them in Finland. Hospitals need a lot of protective equipment because of corona patients.

The company is making about 6 million protective items and masks in total for the big hospitals.

## Strawberry pickers are needed

![Picking strawberries on a farm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11305920)

Open image viewer

A fast picker picks 10 kilos of strawberries an hour. Photo: Kimmo Mäntylä / Lehtikuva

Strawberry growers are worried about who will pick the strawberries in the summer.

Many berry farms hire workers from abroad, for example from Ukraine. Ukrainians cannot now come to Finland to work because of the corona.

Strawberry growers do not willingly hire Finns.

Strawberry growers say that Finns are slower pickers than foreigners.

The growers say that more Finns should be hired.

Several thousand workers are needed on berry farms during the summer.

## May Day is being celebrated virtually

![JVG, virtual May Day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11330469)

Open image viewer

In the virtual gig it looks as if JVG is performing at Senate Square in Helsinki. Photo: Jussi Mankkinen / Yle

Today the eve of May Day is being celebrated.

Because of the corona restrictions, people cannot gather together.

Various May Day celebrations are being organised virtually, that is, online.

Favourite band JVG is performing virtually.

It looks as if the band is at Senate Square in Helsinki, where May Day is usually celebrated.

You can watch the gig on the website helsinkikanava.fi.

## It is cool on May Day

![May Day food on a picnic table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10773184)

Open image viewer

Tomorrow the May Day picnic may have to be eaten indoors. The weather outside is cool. Photo: Mari Kahila / Yle

Tomorrow, on May Day, it will rain in Western Finland and there will be snow showers in places in Lapland.

Elsewhere it will be fine.

In Southern Finland it is about 10 degrees warm, and in Northern Finland about 5 degrees.
