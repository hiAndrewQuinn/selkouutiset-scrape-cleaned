# Friday 19.1. 2018 (radio)

Political researchers assess President Niinistö's first term. A court dealt with Anneli Auer's compensation. Finland wants mothers to breastfeed babies more and for longer. Finland's most active municipality is Asikkala. The weekend will be wintry.

Researchers assess Niinistö

Sauli Niinistö's first term as president will end soon.

Yle asked political researchers to assess Niinistö's work.

The researchers praise Niinistö for meeting world leaders. Niinistö has met the leaders of the United States, China and Russia. The researchers think that this is a good thing, because Finland is a small country.

Niinistö is criticised for speaking unclearly about some things. The researchers think that the president's speeches have sometimes been understood in many different ways.

Niinistö started as president in 2012. He is seeking to become president again.

The court decided on Auer's compensation

Anneli Auer Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

A court has dealt with Anneli Auer's compensation.

Anneli Auer's court case has been talked about a lot in Finland.

Now the court has decided that the state will pay Auer about 23,000 euros more in compensation.

Auer was in prison for the murder of her own husband. Later the court found that Auer was innocent. So Auer has already received about 500,000 euros in compensation.

Anneli Auer wanted much more compensation, 2.5 million euros in total.

Finland encourages mothers to breastfeed

![a baby in someone's arms](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10025963)

Open image viewer

Mothers are encouraged to breastfeed. Photo: Elise Tykkyläinen / Yle

Finland wants mothers to breastfeed babies more and for longer.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare wants mothers to get more support for breastfeeding their baby. Support and guidance are given, for example, in maternity hospitals and child health clinics.

The aim is that babies get only breast milk for the first 6 months.

Many studies show that breast milk is important for a baby's health.

Finland's most active municipality was chosen

![Representatives of Asikkala municipality celebrate the Finland's most active municipality award together with the Athlete of the Year, Iivo Niskanen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10030627)

Finland's most active municipality is Asikkala.

The Association of Finnish Municipalities held the Finland's most active municipality competition for the first time.

Asikkala has hired sports instructors and gives information about exercise. People can exercise for free in the municipality's sports facilities.

Asikkala municipality is in southern Finland, near Lahti.

100 municipalities took part in the competition.
