## Interest rates

![Man in a suit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12685496)

Open image viewer

Bank of Finland Governor Olli Rehn. Photo: Yle

Bank of Finland Governor Olli Rehn estimates that interest rates will rise even further. Rehn estimates that the European Central Bank will raise rates at least until the early part of next year.

The European Central Bank's interest rates also affect the interest rates of ordinary banks. That is why many bank customers notice that, for example, the interest rate on a housing loan rises and repaying the loan becomes more expensive.

The European Central Bank has raised rates because of inflation, that is, the rise in prices. When loan interest rates rise, people consider buying more carefully, and the rise in prices slows down.

## Floods in the Democratic Republic of the Congo

![Map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12685498)

Open image viewer

The Democratic Republic of the Congo is in Africa. Photo: Mapcreator

There have been serious floods in the African Democratic Republic of the Congo.

In the capital Kinshasa, more than 160 people have died because of the floods. So much rain fell in the city that water covered the streets in the city centre.

The city of Kinshasa is on the banks of the Congo River. The city has 15 million inhabitants.

The Automobile Association's roadside service has started its Christmas campaign again.

The name of the campaign is Operation Snowflake. The Automobile Association's volunteers help motorists who get into difficulties on the road.

At Christmas time people drive a lot by car. Many visit relatives precisely at Christmas.

Operation Snowflake continues until 1 January.

## Health effects of indoor plants

![Plants and a wall made of plants.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12685501)

Open image viewer

Green plants can be assembled into a living wall. Photo: Yle

Indoor plants can do good for people's health. This is what researcher Laura Soininen says.

In Soininen's study, living walls with plants were brought into an office. After 2 weeks, a greater amount of different microbes was found on the employees' skin.

Microbes can help to prevent allergies, for example.

## Amur tigers at Korkeasaari

![Amur tiger cubs in the snow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12685503)

Open image viewer

Amur tiger cubs like snow. Photo: Mari Lehmonen / Korkeasaari Zoo

The Amur tiger cubs at Korkeasaari Zoo in Helsinki have been allowed outside. They were in snow and frosty weather for the first time.

The frost did not bother the cubs. The cubs already have thick fur. They were born in August.

There are 3 cubs. They stay at Korkeasaari in the care of their mother for about 2 years. Then they get new homes in other zoos in Europe.

Zoos cooperate when they try to save rare animal species.
