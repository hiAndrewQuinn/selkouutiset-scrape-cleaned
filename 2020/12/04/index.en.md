# Friday 4.12.2020 (radio)

Finns are satisfied with the government's work in the coronavirus crisis. In Finland 7 new coronavirus deaths were reported on Friday. In Lapland, coronavirus guidelines have been given to travellers. The scrappage bonus is back in use. The weekend is cloudy.

## Finns are satisfied with the coronavirus measures

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin (SDP) and Minister for Basic Services Krista Kiuru (SDP) arrived at the government press briefing on 26 November.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11666701)

Most Finns are satisfied with the government's work in the coronavirus crisis. This is what a new survey for Yle, carried out by Taloustutkimus, shows.

About 80 per cent of the survey respondents thought that the government has succeeded well in handling the coronavirus crisis. Respondents also thought that the president and the health authorities have done good work.

However, Finns are not satisfied with the opposition. Less than 50 per cent of the survey respondents said that the opposition has done good work in handling the coronavirus crisis. The opposition means the parties that are not in the government.

## New coronavirus figures

![The new intensive care unit at Seinäjoki Central Hospital](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10145861)

Open image viewer

Intensive care is given, for example, at Seinäjoki Central Hospital. Photo: Tarmo Niemi / Yle

On Friday, 7 new coronavirus deaths were reported in Finland. In total, 415 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

336 new coronavirus infections were also reported in Finland. The number is a little smaller than on the previous days.

Doctors hope that the strict coronavirus restrictions will start to have an effect and that the growth in coronavirus infections will slow down.

However, hospitals are worried about whether there will be enough intensive care places. In recent weeks, coronavirus infections have increased among people over 60. Older people often need intensive care if they fall ill with the coronavirus.

## Coronavirus guidelines for travellers to Lapland

![Santa Claus Village in Rovaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10589803)

Open image viewer

Lapland's Christmas is now waiting for Finnish travellers. No foreigners are coming because of the coronavirus. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Coronavirus guidelines have been given to travellers in Lapland.

The guidelines say that travellers must remember masks and safe distances. It is important that people only set off on a trip if they are healthy.

The guidelines were given because about 35,000 Finns are travelling from southern Finland to Lapland at Christmas.

## Scrappage bonus back in use

![Cars at a scrapyard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10339769)

Open image viewer

Now is a good time to scrap an old car. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The scrappage bonus is back in use.

The state pays the scrappage bonus to a motorist who scraps an old car. The scrappage bonus is 1,000 to 2,000 euros.

The idea of the scrappage bonus is that the motorist swaps the old car for a new, environmentally friendly car.

The new car may be, for example, an electric car. An electric car does not pollute the air like an old petrol car does.

The scrappage bonus can also be used for an electric bike or a public transport ticket.

There is a lot of cloud on Saturday. Snow falls in the middle of the country. In the south of the country there is light drizzle.

Warm air is flowing from the south. The temperature rises to between +1 and +5 degrees on Saturday. In Lapland there is a little frost.

The cloudy weather continues on Sunday. The temperature is above zero. Only in Lapland is there frost.
