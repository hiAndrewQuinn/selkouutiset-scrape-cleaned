# Tuesday 11.4.2017 (radio)

The police acted correctly in the deportations to Afghanistan. The Stockholm attacker confessed to the terrorist act. Too few babies are born in Finland. Singer Saara Aalto is conquering Finland.

## The police acted correctly in the deportations to Afghanistan

![People in front of the Pasila police building. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9545534)

Open image viewer

Demonstrators opposed the deportations in front of the Pasila police building in Helsinki.  Photo: Yle

The deportation of asylum seekers to Afghanistan went properly. This is what the Non-Discrimination Ombudsman says, who monitors deportation flights and the actions of the police. The Ombudsman says that the police had not arranged an interpreter, but otherwise there is nothing to remark on in the actions of the police. The monitors think that the police treated people well both at the police building and on the flight to Afghanistan.

A week ago Finland sent 10 people back to Afghanistan by plane. A group of people opposed the deportations in front of the Helsinki police building and at the airport. The police were accused, for example, of sending children back to Afghanistan. But all the asylum seekers who were sent back were adults.

## The Stockholm attacker admitted the terrorist act

![Rakhmat Akilov, suspected of the Stockholm attack, photographed in a police car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9560112)

Open image viewer

In the car sits Rakhmat Akilov, who has confessed to the terrorist act.  Photo: AOP

In Sweden a court has remanded in custody the man who is suspected of the Stockholm terror attack. The 39-year-old man admitted in court that he is guilty of the terrorist act. The man comes from Uzbekistan.

The court did not give permission to detain a second suspect. But this man will not be set free, because there is a deportation order in force against him.

The terror attacker drove a lorry into a crowd in central Stockholm last Friday.  4 people died in the attack and more than 10 people were injured.

## Finns have too few children

![A baby.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9527004)

Open image viewer

Too few babies are born.  Photo: Minna Heikura / Yle

Too few babies are born in Finland. Last year about 53,000 children were born in Finland, which is almost 3,000 fewer than in 2015. The number of new babies has fallen for 6 years in a row now. Now more people die in Finland than babies are born. This has happened only 2 times before, when Finland was at war.

Experts say that the Finnish economy will not do well if too few people are born. Experts think that more homes must be built in Finland and more must be invested in daycare, so that young people dare to have children.

## Saara Aalto gives a big concert in Helsinki

![Saara Aalto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9546337)

Open image viewer

Saara Aalto practises 15 hours every day.  Photo: Sini Liimatainen / Yle

Singer Saara Aalto is conquering Finland. Aalto is popular because she did well in Britain's X Factor singing competition. She came second in the competition.

Aalto has toured big venues in Britain, but now the singer is performing on a big stage in Finland for the first time. This weekend in Finland, Aalto goes on stage at Hartwall Arena in Helsinki in front of 13,000 spectators. Aalto practises 15 hours every day, so that the show goes as well as possible.

A book has also been made about Saara Aalto, and it will be published at the weekend.
