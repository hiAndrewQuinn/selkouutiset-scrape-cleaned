# Friday 7.9.2018 (radio)

Finland's exports of goods abroad have grown. Diver Mikko Paasi has received an honour in Thailand. In Finland a new technology is being developed, with which charging an electric car is easy. A unisex changing room is being opened at the Itäkeskus swimming hall in Helsinki. The weekend is warm.

## Finland's exports are growing

![Stevedores on containers in the port.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10215510)

Finland's exports of goods abroad have grown.

In July this year, goods worth almost 5 billion euros were exported from Finland. That is 5 percent more than in July last year.

The figures are from Customs statistics.

## A Finnish diver was honoured

![Mikko Paasi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10393220)

Open image viewer

Mikko Paasi received an honour from the King of Thailand. Photo: Lillian Suwanrumpha / EPA

Finnish Mikko Paasi has received an honour in Thailand. Paasi received the honour from the King of Thailand.

Mikko Paasi is a diver. In the summer he helped rescue 12 boys in Thailand. The boys could not get out of the cave without help, because part of the cave was under water.

Paasi received the honour at a ceremony held in honour of the rescue operation. At the ceremony Paasi also met the boys he had helped to rescue. Paasi said that the boys are doing well.

## Charging electric cars becomes easier

![Electric car charging in central Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10239987)

Open image viewer

Soon an electric car will be able to find a charging point by itself. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Charging electric cars will be easier in the coming years than it is now.

Nowadays the driver of an electric car must plan the route so that there are enough charging points along the way.

In Finland a new technology is being developed, with the help of which the driver does not need to think about charging. The electric car finds a free charging point by itself and guides the driver to the destination.

The idea of the new technology is that electric cars and charging points talk to each other online.

New technology is needed so that the number of electric cars can grow. The goal is that there are 250,000 electric cars in Finland by 2030.

Nowadays there are only 2,000 electric cars in Finland and about 10,000 cars that can run on both petrol and electricity.

## A unisex changing room is coming to Itäkeskus

![Itäkeskus swimming hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9638659)

Open image viewer

Everyone is welcome to the Itäkeskus swimming hall. Photo: Merja Huovinen / City of Helsinki

A new kind of changing room is being opened at the Itäkeskus swimming hall in Helsinki.

The new changing room is a unisex changing room. It is not a women's or men's changing room, but is open to everyone.

The new unisex changing room is important, for example, for gender minorities. Many gender minority people have experienced bullying in sports venues. Because of this, they have even stopped doing sport as a hobby.
