# Sunday 16.4.2017 (radio)

North Korea did a missile test. Turkey voted on the president's powers. The Pope demanded an end to the war in Syria. Ylivieska remembered last Easter's church fire. The world's oldest person died.

## North Korea launched a missile

![Celebration parade in Pyongyang.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9567987)

Open image viewer

North Korea showed missiles in a celebration parade on Saturday. Photo: How Hwee Young / EPA

North Korea has done a missile test. The test apparently failed.

The South Korean Ministry of Defence says that North Korea launched a missile from the east coast on Sunday morning. But the missile exploded only a few seconds after the launch.

The missile test was done only a day after North Korea showed weapons and missiles in a big military parade in the capital Pyongyang.

## Turks voted in large numbers

![The polling station in Beşiktaş from outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9568101)

Open image viewer

In Istanbul many polling stations were crowded right in the morning. Photo: Evelin Kask / Yle

Turkey has voted on the president's powers. The result of the referendum will perhaps be decided by Monday morning.

The vote asked the people whether the constitution will be changed so that the president gets greater powers. If the new law is accepted, the president can, for example, appoint ministers and judges, make laws and dissolve parliament.

The law reform also means that Turkey's current president Recep Tayyip Erdogan can continue as president for 2 more terms, even until 2029.

In opinion polls, the yes and no votes have been level.

## The Pope demanded an end to the war in Syria

![The Pope photographed from behind, with a crowd of listeners in front in St. Peter's Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9568128)

Open image viewer

At the end of the Easter Day mass, Pope Francis sent a blessing to the whole world. Photo: L'Osservatore Romano / EPA

Easter has been celebrated in the Vatican in the traditional way.

The head of the Catholic Church, Pope Francis, led the Easter Vigil mass in St. Peter's Basilica. In the mass the Pope spoke for the poor, the elderly and migrants.

The Easter Day mass was held in St. Peter's Square. Thousands of people came there.

The Pope said in the mass that the horrors and death of the war in Syria must be brought to an end. At the end of the mass the Pope sent a blessing to the whole world.

## Ylivieska is waiting for a new church

![Easter mass on the ruins of the church in Ylivieska.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9568149)

Open image viewer

In Ylivieska the Easter Vigil mass was held on the ruins of the old church. Photo: Yle

In Ylivieska, people have remembered the church fire that happened a year ago. This year the Easter Vigil mass was held on the ruins of the old church.

The church of Ylivieska was more than 200 years old. An arsonist set the church on fire last Easter.

A new church is going to be built in the place of the burned church.

## The world's oldest person died

![Emma Morano sits on the bed and looks at a picture of herself as a young woman in Verbania, Northern Italy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9567884)

Open image viewer

Italian Emma Morano died at the age of 117. Photo: Antonino di Marco / EPA

The world's oldest person, Italian Emma Morano, has died. She died at the age of 117.

Emma Morano was perhaps also the last person who had been born in the 1800s. She had been born in 1899.

Emma Morano's rules for life included, for example, drinking a glass of cognac a day.
