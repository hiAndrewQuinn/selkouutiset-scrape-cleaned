# Wednesday 1.5.2019 (radio)

May Day was celebrated on Wednesday. May Day has passed calmly. Parties have held May Day celebrations. The restaurant sector lacks students.

## Traditional May Day

![May Day celebrations at Kaivopuisto in Ullanlinnanmäki, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10762640)

May Day celebrations at Kaivopuisto in Helsinki. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Today May Day has been celebrated. May Day is the festival of students, workers and spring. May Day celebrations have been held all over Finland.

In Helsinki many people spent May Day in Kaivopuisto. People ate May Day food outdoors and listened to choir singing. The choir singing was performed by the Student Union's singers.

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio received May Day greetings in the yard of the Presidential Palace. The presidential couple received a May Day flower, and music was played for the presidential couple.

## Calm May Day

![Students celebrate at the Havis Amanda statue on May Eve.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10762589)

Young people know how to have fun without alcohol too.

Finns have celebrated May Day fairly calmly. Drunk and violent people have caused some disruption.

The police, however, say that young people do not drink as much alcohol nowadays as they used to. That is why May Day has passed calmly.

## May Day speeches

![Antti Rinne takes part in the Helsinki Workers' May Day march.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10762676)

Antti Rinne took part in the May Day celebration in Helsinki. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Political parties have also held May Day celebrations. Party leaders have given speeches at the celebrations. One theme of the speeches was the parliamentary elections.

SDP chairman Antti Rinne said that voters got tired of the right-wing government. The SDP is a left-wing party, and it won the parliamentary elections a couple of weeks ago. Rinne promised to improve the position of workers and poor pensioners.

Climate change was also on the agenda in the party leaders' speeches. According to Finns Party chairman Jussi Halla-aho, too strict climate policy worsens the competitiveness of Finnish companies. Greens chairman Pekka Haavisto said that fighting against climate change is very important for the Greens.

## Restaurant sector

![Pia Takaoja from Savonlinna, who has qualified as a cook.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10761009)

Open image viewer

The restaurant sector needs workers. Photo: Kalle Purhonen / Yle

The restaurant sector is short of students. There are more study places than applicants.

The restaurant sector does not interest young people, even though there is a lot of work in the sector.

There are many reasons why the restaurant sector does not interest young people. Pay in the sector is low, and the work is often shift work.

Many restaurants also cannot offer full-time work. They need workers most at weekends and in summer.

Restaurants hope that taxes on restaurant services will be reduced. Then restaurants may be able to afford to raise workers' pay.
