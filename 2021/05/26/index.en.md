# Wednesday 26.5.2021 (radio)

The new Minister of Science and Culture is Antti Kurvinen. Advance voting for the municipal elections began. There are 220 new coronavirus infections. Restaurants and hotels have suffered a lot from corona.

The Centre Party is changing ministers.

The new Minister of Science and Culture is MP Antti Kurvinen. Kurvinen is the chair of the Centre Party's parliamentary group.

Until now the Minister of Science and Culture has been Centre Party leader Annika Saarikko. She is now moving to become finance minister.

Until now the finance minister has been Matti Vanhanen. He will no longer continue as a minister.

## Advance voting began

![Advance voting place](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81140660ae387cb8be4/1622030517)

Open image viewer

Advance voting place. Photo: Jarkko Heikkinen/Yle

Advance voting for the municipal elections has begun.

Advance voting lasts 2 weeks and ends on 8 June. The municipal election day is 13 June.

In many municipalities you can vote outdoors because of corona. In some municipalities you can also vote in your own car.

The Ministry of Justice has made a video in clear language about how advance voting works. The video is on the Ministry of Justice's YouTube channel.

## Corona figures

![Testing staff in the yard of the corona test point at the Tornio border crossing point](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80740860a3a0c127722/1621336714)

There are 220 new coronavirus infections in Finland. There are a little more coronavirus infections than on previous days. For example, on Tuesday there were 125 coronavirus infections.

There are 4 new corona deaths in Finland. In total, 946 people have now died in Finland because of corona.

There are 101 people in hospital because of corona. 18 people are receiving intensive care.

More than 42 percent of the population has received the first dose of the corona vaccine. About 7 percent of people have received both doses. Of those over 80 years old, already 91 percent have been vaccinated.

## Restaurants and hotels suffer from corona

![A hand sanitiser bottle at the door of the Amarillo restaurant in Pori.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5728,h_3222,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6874875edf6ef8dad88/1591702261)

Open image viewer

A hand sanitiser bottle at the door of the Amarillo restaurant in Pori. Photo: Jari Pelkonen / Yle

Restaurants and hotels have suffered a lot from corona. This is what the Research Institute of the Finnish Economy, Etla, says.

Etla estimates that the situation of hotels and restaurants will improve already during the summer. However, recovery is slow.

Tourism will recover from corona towards the end of the year. Recovery will take many years.

## Marijuana to Finland from Latvia

![Customs officer inspecting a lorry at the border between Tornio and Haparanda](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_530/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7896846061dae1a4887/1617026112)

According to the preliminary investigation, behind the criminal case is a new type of organised criminal group in Finland. The picture shows Customs carrying out an inspection of a lorry at the border between Tornio and Haparanda in March. Photo: Antti Ullakko / Yle

A lot of marijuana has been brought to Finland from Latvia. The authorities have found more than 110 kilograms of marijuana.

15 people are suspected of the crimes. 10 people are still in custody.
