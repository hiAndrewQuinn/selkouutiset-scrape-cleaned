# Tuesday 25.8.2020 (radio)

Finnair is cutting staff. Finns are complaining about how the corona crisis has been handled. Light cars will not come onto Finland's roads. Growing oil crops is decreasing in Finland.

## Finnair is making redundancies

![Finnair aircraft at Helsinki-Vantaa airport in May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11491091)

The airline Finnair plans to reduce jobs.

Finnair will cut about 1,000 jobs. Finnair also intends to increase the number of layoffs of employees.

Finnair's chief executive Topi Manner says that the corona pandemic is the worst crisis in the history of air traffic. Finnair has to reduce its costs, because it looks like the corona pandemic will not ease quickly.

Finnair has more than 6,000 employees in Finland. Almost all employees have been laid off at some stage in the spring or summer.

## The corona crisis is producing complaints

![Serving school food at Sammonlahti school](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6670755ea04195a8d87/1587561134)

Municipalities were supposed to arrange school meals for pupils also during distance teaching. Many complainants felt that the meals had been arranged badly. Photo: Kare Lehtonen/Yle

Finns have made hundreds of complaints about the handling of the corona crisis. The Parliamentary Ombudsman has already received more than 700 complaints, and more keep coming all the time.

The most complaints have been about university entrance examinations and student selection. About 150 complaints have been made about these.

Complaints have also been made, for example, about school meals during distance teaching and about visiting bans in care institutions.

## The EU has banned light cars

![Light cars at a car dealership yard in Vantaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3072,h_1728,x_0,y_672/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5887905d7208c20cd2d/1567755104)

A light car is an ordinary passenger car whose speed is limited. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

Finland may not introduce so-called light cars.

The European Commission has not given Finland an exemption to introduce light cars. The Commission says that light cars are against EU legislation.

Light cars are ordinary passenger cars whose speed is limited. They may drive at most 60 kilometres per hour. The cars were aimed at 15 to 17-year-old young people who do not yet have a driving licence for a passenger car. A light car was thought to be a safer vehicle than a moped car.

The Finnish Parliament has already managed to approve laws on light cars, so now the laws must be repealed. The laws were due to come into force at the beginning of November.

## Farmers are giving up oil crops

![A rapeseed field in flower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9714889)

Open image viewer

A rapeseed field is in flower. Photo: Pauliina Jaakkola/Yle

Growing oil crops has decreased in Finland.

The combined production of oil crops, that is, turnip rape and rapeseed, is now about 80 per cent smaller than in the peak year 2010.

More rapeseed is grown than turnip rape. Together their growing area is about 30,000 hectares.

Many farmers have given up growing oil crops, because they need a lot of work and the risk of the harvest failing is high. For example, pests, plant diseases and weeds have to be poisoned many times.
