# Saturday 4.3.2023 (radio)

End of the bus strike. Interest rate rises. Finland and Turkey. Fighting in Bakhmut. Weather forecast.

## End of the bus strike

![Nobina buses in Viikki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3847,h_2163,x_100,y_1/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107854563fcb0e057032/1739166730)

The bus strike ended on Saturday. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

The bus strike has ended. Employers and employees reached an agreement on a new collective agreement late on Friday evening.

The agreement applies to bus drivers and maintenance workshops. The new agreement is valid for 2 years. Workers will get pay rises of about 6 percent. Pay rises in the bus sector are at the same level as in the other new agreements in the Automobile and Transport sector.

Because an agreement was reached in the bus labour dispute, the bus strike ended immediately on Saturday. Some bus trips may still not run over the weekend.

## Rise in interest rates

![The graphic shows how the 12-month Euribor rate, which was still negative at the start of 2022, had risen to 3.826 percent on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3181,h_1789,x_1789,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10807706402056b7d722/1677854115)

The 12-month Euribor has already risen above 3.8 percent. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle, graphics: Samuli Huttunen / Yle

The rise in interest rates reduces house sales. The most common rate on housing loans in Finland is the 12-month Euribor. The Euribor rate has already risen to 3.8 percent this week.

Because interest rates rise, the costs of housing loans also rise. A person may have to pay hundreds of euros more per month on the loan than, for example, a year ago. That is why house sales have also decreased. Many Finns do not want to take out a new loan, because interest rates are rising.

The housing loan bank Hypo says that house sales have decreased by as much as 40 percent. The prices of many homes are falling, because buyers have decreased.

## Finland and Turkey

![Speaker of Parliament Matti Vanhanen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3999,h_2249,x_0,y_137/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10809196402fd273ff33/1677917643)

Speaker Matti Vanhanen was a guest on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

The Speaker of Parliament, Matti Vanhanen, says that in Nato Finland must also be interested in Turkey's security concerns. Vanhanen was a guest on the Ykkösaamu programme on Yle on Saturday.

Finland and Sweden will continue discussions with Turkey next week about Finland's and Sweden's Nato membership.

Turkey has not yet approved Finland's and Sweden's Nato membership. Speaker Vanhanen says that in Finland and Sweden Turkey's concern about terrorist threats is taken seriously.

## Fighting in Ukraine

![A residential building burns in Bakhmut, Ukraine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3780,h_2126,x_0,y_328/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107409563eef93ba3661/1676605884)

Fighting is intense in and around the city of Bakhmut in Ukraine. This is what British intelligence says. Bakhmut is located in eastern Ukraine.

Russian forces have advanced on the northern edge of Bakhmut. It is an important area, which is still under Ukrainian control. British intelligence says that Russian forces are attacking Bakhmut from three directions.

Russia has destroyed 2 bridges leading to Bakhmut. That is why Ukraine has only a few routes left through which Ukrainian forces get supplies to the city.
