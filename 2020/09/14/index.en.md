# Monday 14.9.2020 (radio)

Neste is laying off workers. The Finnish state budget. Corona infections in Finland. Theatres are doing badly. Weather information.

## Neste will close an oil refinery

![Neste's Naantali oil refinery](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11543126)

Open image viewer

Neste is closing the oil refinery in Naantali. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The oil refining company Neste is starting big co-operation negotiations.

The company may lay off as many as 470 people.

The company says that the demand for traditional oil products has decreased. Nowadays people want to use less oil, which is a non-renewable energy source.

Neste intends to close the oil refinery in Naantali in Western Finland completely.

Neste intends to focus more on renewable energy sources.

The Finnish state owns more than 40 percent of Neste.

## The government started budget negotiations

![Two women on the steps](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11542618)

Open image viewer

On the left, Centre Party chairperson Annika Saarikko, and on the right, SDP chairperson, Prime Minister Sanna Marin. They are going to the budget negotiations.

The Finnish government has started negotiations about next year's budget, that is, the income and expenditure estimate.

The 5 governing parties have to agree on how the state will use money next year.

It is expected in advance that the negotiations will be difficult, because the corona epidemic has affected the Finnish economy a lot.

For example, many municipalities need more help from the state. Municipalities have spent a lot of money, for example, on organising corona tests.

The government may tell tomorrow what next year's budget could look like.

## Many corona infections in Mikkeli

![Corona sample tent of the South Savo Rescue Department in Mikkeli on 10 September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11543070)

Open image viewer

Corona tests are being done in a big tent in Mikkeli. Photo: Seppo Nykänen / Lehtikuva

47 new corona infections have been found in Finland.

During the last two weeks, many more infections have been found than before.

In Mikkeli in Eastern Finland there have been many corona infections among children and young people.

In Mikkeli many sports venues have been closed, because infections have spread in children's and young people's sports hobbies.

## Theatres are doing badly

![Rehearsal of Maija Poppanen, Svenska Teatern](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4537,h_2552,x_0,y_234/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7169325f58b60c2a123/1599649819)

Open image viewer

A musical called Maija Poppanen is being rehearsed at the Swedish-language Svenska Teatern. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

Theatres face a gloomy autumn.

Many big theatres had already decided earlier that this autumn they would perform big musicals.

The theatres could not know that the coronavirus would come and mess up everything.

When a theatre makes a big musical, it has to get many audience members so that the costs can be paid.

Now every second seat in the auditorium is empty, because there has to be a safety distance.

Theatres do not make a profit from ticket sales.

## Still dry weather on Tuesday

![Boats at the jetty in a storm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6938368)

Open image viewer

On Wednesday and Thursday it will rain and blow hard. So the first storm of autumn is coming. Photo: Ari-Pekka Sirviö / Yle

Tomorrow, Tuesday, the weather will be mostly dry across the country.

The temperature is about 16 degrees in the south and about 10 degrees in the north.

The day after tomorrow, on Wednesday, the weather will turn rainy and the wind will start to blow hard.

A strong storm is coming.
