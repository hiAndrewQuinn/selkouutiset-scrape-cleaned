# Sunday 3.6.2018 (radio)

Young people have celebrated the end of school. Large passenger ships are being built in Turku. Young men use a lot of snus. Finns are more afraid of violence than before. The dry weather has lasted for a month already.

## Young people celebrate

![Young people drinking beer in a park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10229707)

Open image viewer

The beautiful weather got young people celebrating outdoors. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Young people have been celebrating in different parts of Finland. Young people are celebrating because school summer holidays have started. Young people are celebrating in beautiful weather in parks, on beaches and in marketplaces.

The police say that there have been no big problems. However, the police calmed young people's fights in Turku and Vantaa, for example.

The police also took alcohol away from minors. In Helsinki, for example, the police collected 150 litres of mild alcoholic drinks and 10 litres of strong alcoholic drinks from young people.

## Shipbuilding

![Mein Schiff 2 at the Turku shipyard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10234251)

Open image viewer

A new ship was launched in Turku on Friday. Photo: Petra Ristola / Yle

Large ships are being built in Turku.

On Friday there was a celebration at Meyer's Turku shipyard, when a new ship was launched. The ship will be fully finished next year. It is 315 metres long, and about 3,100 passengers can fit on it.

A total of 8 large ships have been ordered from the Turku shipyard. All of them are big passenger ships.

Because the ships are growing in size, the shipyard is bringing a new large crane into use. The crane is called Goliath, and it will come into use in about 2 weeks.

## Snus

![Suvi Turunen / Yle](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9548200)

Open image viewer

Snus is a powdered tobacco product.

Young men use a lot of snus. The National Institute for Health and Welfare says that about 25 percent of 18-year-old men use snus every day.

Snus is a powdered tobacco product. It is sniffed up the nose or placed under the lip.

The sale of snus is banned in the European Union. In Sweden, however, snus may be sold, and many Finns also buy snus in Sweden.

## Fear of violence

![Shadows of a man's legs and hand and another man's trainer-clad feet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10217941)

Open image viewer

There are not more violent incidents than before, but people fear violence more. Photo: Rami Koivula / Yle

Finns are more afraid of violence than before. A new study says that now about 30 percent of Finns fear street violence.

People are most afraid in big cities that someone will hit them on the street. Women fear street violence more than men.

Fear of street violence has grown for a few years now. However, researchers say that there are not more violent incidents nowadays than before.

The newspaper website Sunnuntaisuomalainen reported on this.

## Drought

![A farmer hoes dry potato ground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10228731)

Open image viewer

Farmers are worried because of the drought. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

It is dry in Finland now. In Helsinki and Tampere, for example, it last rained on 3 May, that is, a month ago.

Yle meteorologist Matti Huutonen says that it is this dry only a few times in 100 years.
