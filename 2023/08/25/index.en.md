## Budget proposal

![A beer tap at Triplan Prisma in Helsinki in July 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_331/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6953725efc83b0e9b6c/1593609745)

Taxation of beer will be reduced in the budget proposal. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The Ministry of Finance has announced the budget proposal for next year. In the proposal the budget is in a deficit of 10 billion euros, that is, the state has to borrow another 10 billion euros.

Taxes on work, fuel and beer will be lightened if the proposal is approved. Taxes on wines and strong alcoholic drinks will be tightened. A tax will be introduced on nicotine pouches.

Minister of Finance **Riikka Purra** says that various savings will grow every year during the term of office. The government will discuss the budget proposal at the end of next month. Parliament makes the final decision on the budget.

## Death of Yevgeny Prigozhin

![Prigozhin stands in his military uniform.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_157/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116170764e719e82c6f6/1719115764)

Yevgeny Prigozhin. Photo: UPI / AOP

The Russian air crash is causing a lot of discussion in Western countries. The plane of the leaders of the mercenary company Wagner fell in Russia on Wednesday. Wagner's leader **Yevgeny Prigozhin** died in the crash.

Many experts and Western leaders believe that the crash may have been an assassination. Russian President **Vladimir Putin** may have wanted to kill Wagner's leader Yevgeny Prigozhin. Russia denies the claim. Russia says that talk of an assassination is Western lies.

It is not yet known why Prigozhin's plane fell. There may have been a bomb on the plane, or the plane may have been shot down.

Prime Minister **Petteri Orpo** wants to increase the EU's support for Ukraine. Orpo says that European countries must support Ukraine more than now.

Orpo says that the war will end more quickly only if Ukraine gets enough support. Prime Minister Petteri Orpo spoke at the National Coalition Party's summer days.

Finland decided this week on a new support package for Ukraine. The package is worth 100 million euros. Finland's total support for Ukraine is now 1.8 billion euros.

## Residence permits

![Finnish Immigration Service in Helsinki. 3.2.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_296/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-769765601aa93c154d6/1643195926)

More and more residence permits for students are being applied for from outside the EU. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

More students than before want to come to Finland from outside the EU. Applications for student residence permits from outside the EU have increased a lot. This is what the Finnish Immigration Service says.

By the end of July, almost 8,800 people from outside the EU had applied for a student residence permit in Finland. The number is almost 50 percent more than last year.

The most applications have been submitted by citizens of Bangladesh, China, Sri Lanka, India and Russia.
