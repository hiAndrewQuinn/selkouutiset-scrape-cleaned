Corona vaccinations. Restaurants. Willow-twig visiting. Sahara sand. The weather.

The government intends to increase corona vaccinations in the Helsinki and Turku areas. Prime Minister Sanna Marin said this on Sunday.

There are a lot of corona infections in the Helsinki and Turku areas. That is why they need more corona vaccinations than other areas.

It is not yet known how many corona vaccines will be reserved for the Helsinki and Turku areas.

However, first all risk groups must be vaccinated, the Prime Minister reminds. Risk groups include people who have an illness.

## Restaurants still closed

![People collecting takeaway lunch at the Kaari shopping centre in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11859915)

Open image viewer

Restaurants may only sell takeaway food. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The restaurant closure continues.

Restaurants will be closed for another 3 weeks. Parliament decided on this on Sunday.

Restaurants have been closed for almost 3 weeks already across almost all of Finland. Parliament decided to continue the restaurant closure, because the corona situation is still bad.

Customers may not eat in restaurants, but they can buy takeaway food, that is, takeaway portions.

Restaurants will stay closed until 18 April.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare said on Sunday that there are 458 new corona infections in Finland.

Once again, the most new corona infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa area.

The good news is that fewer new corona infections have been found this week than a week ago.

The total number of corona infections in Finland is more than 76,000.

## Palm Sunday willow-twig visitors

![Lilja Nurmio visiting her grandfather Tarmo Nurmio with willow twigs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_619/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78928260606553acdb2/1616930326)

Open image viewer

Lilja Nurmio visited her own grandfather Tarmo Nurmio with willow twigs. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

Palm Sunday was celebrated on Sunday.

Palm Sunday includes willow-twig visiting. Children decorate willow twigs, which they use to touch, for example, their neighbours. In this way, the children wish good health and get chocolate eggs as a reward, for example.

This year, children have visited only close family and preferably outdoors. Because of corona, children have not gone into people's homes.

## Sahara sand in Finland

![The Sahara desert near Mauritania, photographed from high up.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1980,h_1114,x_0,y_102/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7768596032c45822c73/1613940032)

Open image viewer

Sand from the Sahara flew as far as Finland. Photo: STELLA Pictures / ddp

The Finnish Meteorological Institute has received many sand samples from Finns. The sand samples contain Sahara sand, which flew from Africa as far as Finland this winter.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute asked people for sand samples in February. Many thousands of samples have come in.
