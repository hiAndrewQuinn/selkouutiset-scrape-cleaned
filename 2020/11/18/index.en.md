# Wednesday 18.11.2020 (radio)

Finland is thinking about how coronavirus vaccinations will be carried out. There are 3 new coronavirus deaths in Finland. The film Tove is Finland's Oscar candidate. On Thursday it will rain a lot.

## Coronavirus vaccinations in Finland

![A volunteer test person was given the Moderna vaccine in Detroit on 5 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11651304)

Finland is preparing for coronavirus vaccinations. Finland will perhaps vaccinate the nurses who treat coronavirus patients and the risk groups first.

A group of experts announced on Wednesday a proposal for the order in which Finns may perhaps be vaccinated against coronavirus.

The group's proposal is that the nurses who treat coronavirus patients and the workers in elderly care homes are vaccinated first.

Among the first people, the elderly and people who have some serious illness will also be vaccinated. A person can get a severe coronavirus disease if they have some serious illness, for example asthma.

Next, various authorities will assess the order in which the vaccinations are given. The government makes the final decisions.

Coronavirus vaccinations may start next year, when drug companies have finished the coronavirus vaccine.

There are 3 new coronavirus deaths in Finland. So far, 374 people have died from coronavirus in Finland in total.

There were also 288 new coronavirus infections found in Finland. So far, almost 20,000 coronavirus infections have been found in Finland in total.

The number of coronavirus infections is growing especially in Helsinki and the Uusimaa region. Kirsi Varhila, permanent secretary of the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health, says that the authorities will probably have to tighten the coronavirus restrictions in Helsinki and Uusimaa.

## Finland's Oscar candidate

![Tove (Alma Pöysti) sits at the edge of the stage and looks thoughtful.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7203615f635bfe7bae2/1600347251)

Alma Pöysti plays Tove Jansson in the film, which is Finland's Oscar candidate. Photo: Sami Kuokkanen / Helsinki-filmi

The Finnish Oscar candidate has been chosen. It is the film Tove.

The film Tove is an Oscar candidate in the best international film category.

The film is about the life of Tove Jansson. Jansson was an artist and writer who created the Moomins.

The film is about young Tove. Actress Alma Pöysti plays the role of Tove.

Tove Jansson spoke Swedish. The film was also made in Swedish.

The film Tove came to cinemas in Finland at the beginning of October. It has got many viewers.

The Oscar awards will be handed out next year only in April because of the coronavirus. Usually the Oscar awards are handed out already in February.

On Thursday there will be heavy rain all over Finland.

In the north the rain will be snow. In the south it will rain during the day, but in the evening the rain will be snow in the south too.

The weather is very windy. At sea there may even be a storm.

The weather is warmer than usual for November. On Thursday in southern Finland it is about 10 degrees warm.

On Friday the weather gets colder.
