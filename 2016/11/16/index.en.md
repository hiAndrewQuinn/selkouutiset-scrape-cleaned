# Wednesday 16.11.2016 (radio)

The car factory's success. Winter tourism. Children's well-being. Moose collisions. The snow melts.

## The Uusikaupunki car factory is growing

![car on an assembly line in a car factory](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9296371)

Open image viewer

More and more Mercedes-Benz passenger cars, that is, Mercedes, are made in Finland. Photo: Yle

Finland's car industry is doing very well. Finland's only passenger car factory says that it is taking on a thousand new employees.

The factory is owned by Valmet Automotive. It is on Finland's coast, in Uusikaupunki. The Uusikaupunki car factory makes Mercedes-Benz passenger cars.

Next year the factory will also start making Mercedes crossover SUVs. That is why new employees are needed. More than 1,500 people now work at the car factory.

## Finland is popular with tourists

![Northern lights and snow-covered trees](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8681697)

Open image viewer

Finland's winter attracts many tourists to Lapland. Photo: Jukka Brusila

Finland is now a popular travel destination. The travel expert Visit Finland says that Finland is now the most popular winter tourism country in the Nordic region.

It is estimated that the number of tourists in Finland will grow a lot this winter. For example, many winter tourists will come to Lapland from Britain. Many French tourists will also be seen in Lapland.

Experts estimate that the number of Chinese people will also grow a lot. Chinese people are interested in Lapland, Helsinki and Finland's lake areas. However, the biggest group of tourists is Russians.

## Almost all children have friends

![Children run on the school yard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9296230)

Open image viewer

Children in Finland have a lot of joy. Bullying causes sorrow. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Most Finnish children are doing well. For the first time in Finland, a study was made in which children were interviewed about their lives. More than 400 six-year-old children took part in it.

According to the study, almost all children have friends. The fact that they can talk about happy things also shows that children are doing well.

However, the children also told sad things. The things that cause great sorrow are bullying and being hurt.

## Moose collisions have decreased a lot

![A car's windscreen broken in a moose collision.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5464863)

Open image viewer

A moose is a big animal, and a moose collision can be a serious accident. Photo: Police

Moose collisions have decreased a lot in Finland. In 2001, cars collided with a moose in Finland more than 3,000 times.

Last year there were about 1,800 moose collisions. Moose collisions often happen in September, in the dark.

## The snow is disappearing in southern and central Finland

![Snow splashing towards a sledder whose feet can be seen in the picture](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8739434)

Open image viewer

The snow games are soon over in southern Finland. Photo: Niklas Joki / Yle

The snow cover in southern and central Finland will shrink a lot by the end of the week. The weather is now so warm that the snow melts and turns into slush.

Yle's meteorologist Matti Huutonen says that the weather at the end of the week will be rainy, damp, mild, windy and grey. So the sun will not shine.

In the south it will rain, and in central Finland there will be sleet and snow. Rain will also turn into water later in central Finland.

At the end of the week it will also be windy on all of Finland's sea areas.
