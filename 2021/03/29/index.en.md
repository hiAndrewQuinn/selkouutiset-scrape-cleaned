# Monday 29.3.2021 (radio)

Parliament's committees are handling the movement restrictions law. Gyms may be closed in Uusimaa. Language study is decreasing. The number of drivers under the influence of drugs is growing. There is a shortage of community service places.

## Movement restrictions under review

![Mikko Savola of the Centre Party spoke at the plenary session of Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_136/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78927960605c251810b/1616928329)

Parliament's committees have started handling the movement restrictions law. The Constitutional Law Committee, for example, is handling the law.

Members of the Constitutional Law Committee are considering, for example, whether the movement restrictions are necessary and when they should start. The Constitutional Law Committee is also asking for the opinions of experts.

The whole of Parliament will handle the movement restrictions law next Tuesday at the earliest. That is why the movement restrictions will not come into force before Easter, at least.

## Gyms may be closed

![General view](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_34/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5076485b8facf696abc/1536142691)

Open image viewer

Gyms may be closed in Uusimaa and Helsinki. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

Private gyms and sports facilities may close on Thursday in the Helsinki and Uusimaa areas. The Regional State Administrative Agency of Southern Finland is preparing a decision that gyms can be closed because of the corona situation.

On Sunday, Parliament decided on a change to the law that makes it possible to close private gyms as well because of the corona situation.

## Language study decreases

![Close-up of an Italian textbook with food vocabulary.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1890,h_1063,x_0,y_177/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6030555daf204096760/1660819060)

Open image viewer

The popularity of languages other than English has decreased. Photo: Yle/Lone Videstam

Finns are studying fewer languages than before. Finns study a lot of English, but the study of other languages has decreased.

Companies, for example, have a shortage of Finns who speak German. Germany is an important export country for Finland.

Experts say that Finnish companies may miss out on orders if the company does not have anyone who speaks German.

## People drive more under the influence of drugs

![A police officer monitors traffic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10909003)

Open image viewer

A police officer monitors traffic. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

More and more drivers in Finland are driving cars under the influence of drugs. Last year, police checks caught about 12,000 drivers who were under the influence of drugs.

As many drivers drove under the influence of drugs as under the influence of alcohol.

There are more drivers who drive under the influence of drugs than before, and fewer drivers who drive under the influence of alcohol than before.

## Shortage of community service places

![Law books in the library of the Päijät-Häme District Court in the courtroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10087322)

Open image viewer

A law book in a courtroom. Photo: Emilia Malin / Yle

There is a shortage of community service places in Finland. More people have been sentenced to community service than before during the corona time. Last year, about 1,300 people were sentenced to community service.

Community service is a punishment for a crime. In community service, a person does work, for example for the state, a municipality or a parish.

Because of the corona situation, many community service places have been closed. The Criminal Sanctions Agency is looking for new places for community service. There is a shortage of places.
