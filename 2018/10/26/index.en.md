# Friday 26.10.2018 (radio)

Dispute over dismissals. An international military exercise. Bombs in the United States. The weather.

## Agreement in the dismissal dispute

![Minister of Employment Jari Lindström, Prime Minister Juha Sipilä and Minister of Finance Petteri Orpo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10476082)

Ministers Jari Lindström, Juha Sipilä and Petteri Orpo told journalists on Thursday about the meeting with the leaders of the trade unions. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The trade unions have accepted the government's proposed solution in the dispute about the dismissal law.

The dispute between the trade unions and the government began when the government proposed a new dismissal law. The government's idea was that small firms could dismiss employees more easily.

The trade unions got angry about it. They have even organised strikes because of the dispute.

The government has now given up the idea that small firms could dismiss people more easily. The trade unions are ready to stop the industrial action.

But the trade unions want the disputed law to be prepared so that employers, employees and the government are all involved.

## A military exercise begins

![The Northern Coasts 18 exercise can be seen on the Aura river in Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10477425)

Open image viewer

The military exercise can be seen and heard on the Finnish coast for two weeks. Photo: Petra Ristola / Yle

An international military exercise can be seen and heard on the Finnish coast for two weeks.

Finland is leading the exercise for the third time. About 3,600 soldiers are taking part in the exercise. The soldiers come from 13 different countries.

The exercise is being held in Turku, in the coastal area of Finland. The aim is to practise international operations at sea.

## Bomb threats in the United States

![Newspapers](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10476400)

Open image viewer

American newspapers report on the bomb threats. Photo: AOP

In the United States many people have been threatened with bombs.

Police are investigating the threats in several states. Police suspect that the packages containing explosives were posted from Florida.

The bombs have not exploded. According to police, perhaps all ten bombs were sent from a mail centre in Miami. Miami is a big city in Florida.

The bombs have been sent to people who are critical of President Donald Trump. For example, a bomb was sent to the very famous actor Robert De Niro.

## The weather gets colder

![A snowplough on Highway 9 in the snow chaos of 26 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10476779)

Open image viewer

A snowplough in the snow chaos on Friday. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

The weather will get colder at the weekend, when cold air arrives in Finland from the north.

On Saturday afternoon there may be a lot of snow or sleet in Southern Finland. In the far south there may perhaps be so much snow that it disrupts traffic.

This morning there was a lot of snow in Central Finland and traffic was thrown into chaos. As much as 20-30 centimetres of snow fell.

On Monday the weather will be quite cold, with frost expected across the whole country. Next Tuesday or Wednesday mild air will flow into Finland from the south and the weather will get warmer.
