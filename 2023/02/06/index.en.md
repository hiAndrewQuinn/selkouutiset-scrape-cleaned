# Monday 6.2.2023 (radio)

Earthquake causes destruction in Turkey and Syria. Practical nurses move to temporary work. Today is the national day of the Sami people. Museums have plenty of visitors. The weather.

## Turkish earthquake

![A large crowd of people clearing a collapsed apartment block.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_443/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106914363e0f77c5e277/1675687855)

Many people have died in the earthquakes in Turkey and Syria. More than 2300 dead have already been found. There are also thousands of injured people.

The first earthquake happened in the early morning in south-eastern Turkey, near the border with Syria. In the afternoon a strong aftershock caused more destruction.

Many houses collapsed in the quakes, so the death toll may rise as the rescue work goes on.

The EU is sending rescue teams to Turkey to help the victims of the earthquake. France, Germany and Finland, for example, have also promised to give help to Turkey.

## Practical nurses on temporary work

![Essi Vartiainen puts on a mask on her face before a client visit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5328,h_2997,x_0,y_585/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106687563da4189f0e6b/1675250407)

Practical nurse Essi Vartiainen is now on a 6-week temporary job in Asikkala. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

Many practical nurses have moved to temporary work. An estimated tens of thousands of practical nurses already do temporary work through rental firms.

There is a serious shortage of practical nurses, and thousands of jobs are open to them in different parts of the country. When permanent nurses cannot be found, employers have to hire them from staffing agencies.

Many nurses have even left a permanent job and moved to work through rental firms. In temporary work, nurses can choose when and where they work. At a rental firm, nurses' pay is also better than in the public sector.

For example, practical nurse Essi Vartiainen says that in temporary work she earns about 1000 euros more per month than in a permanent job in the public sector.

## National day of the Sami people

![Sami people celebrate their national day at Senate Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2267,x_0,y_426/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106927063e114a84585a/1675695378)

Open image viewer

The Sami flag at Kansalaistori in Helsinki. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Today the national day of the Sami people was celebrated.

The day began with a flag raising at the Sami museum Siida in Inari. In Helsinki, too, the Sami flag was raised on the flagpole at Kansalaistori in the morning.

The Sami are an indigenous people. The Sami have their own history, language and culture.

The Sami live in Finland, Sweden, Norway and Russia. There are perhaps as many as one hundred thousand of them in total. Most Sami live in Norway.

People are interested in museums.

In Finnish museums there were about 6.9 million visits last year. There were 2.5 million more museum visits than the year before, says the National Board of Antiquities.

The most popular places were the Helsinki City Museum, Amos Rex and Kiasma.
