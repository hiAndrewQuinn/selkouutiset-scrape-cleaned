## Health care

![A dentist's fingers and a tool in a patient's mouth, and a patient in a gynaecologist's chair.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1237,h_695,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-112854664871e6575970/1686576859)

The human rights organisation Amnesty International criticises Finland's public health care.

The organisation says that the right to health is not equal. Amnesty says that Finland is breaking its human rights obligations.

Amnesty's report says that Finland has too few sexual health services, for example. Gynaecologist appointments, for example, are often in the private sector.

However, Finland has committed to removing discrimination based on gender.

There are also problems in dental care and mental health care.

Amnesty says that Finland's public health care has been weakening for a long time.

## Scams in the name of the Tax Administration

![A person looks at the vero.fi website on a computer screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2991,h_1682,x_112,y_15/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7368125f9d36951db80/1666765174)

Open image viewer

Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The Tax Administration warns that scam messages have been sent in its name. The messages promise tax refunds.

The messages are not from the Tax Administration.

There are two kinds of scams going around. Scam text messages or emails have been sent to customers. In addition, there is a scam website online that looks like the Omavero service.

The messages ask you to click a link and ask for bank details or personal details.

The Tax Administration advises that the messages should be destroyed immediately. You should not open the links in the message.

Scams are going around now, because tax refunds will be paid soon.

The Car Industry Information Centre predicts that in Finland there will be over 1.5 million electric cars in 2040.

Already by 2025, 42 per cent of new cars in Finland will be electric cars.

Last year, the average price of an electric car was about 50,000 euros.

## Food

![A man with "tulli" written on his back stands with his back to the camera. He looks at a car that has stopped in front of him.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_697/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9981366304aca4b0675/1661250849)

Customs suspects that food is being brought into Finland illegally.

Private individuals have brought in food privately, but sold the food on. The food has been brought in from outside the European Union.

Customs suspects that the food has been used, for example, in restaurants.

Customs says that this kind of activity tries to avoid taxes. In addition, the safety of the food is not monitored.

Among other things, banned food additives have been found in food.
