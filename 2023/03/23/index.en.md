# Thursday 23.3.2023 (radio)

Train strike. NATO laws. Aid package for Ukraine. Cleaning industry strike. The weather.

## The train strike ends

![Train running through a winter landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689976)

Open image viewer

The strike is ending, and trains will start running again. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

The train strike is ending. Trains will start running again early on Friday morning. Train timetables may still have exceptions on Friday.

The train drivers are returning to work, because the labour dispute in the sector has been settled. The train drivers agreed with their employer on, for example, pay and rest periods.

The train strike began on Monday, and it stopped all train traffic in Finland. Many people's lives will get easier now that the strike is ending and trains are running again.

## NATO laws are ready

![President Sauli Niinistö writes at a desk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689987)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö confirmed the NATO laws. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finland's NATO laws are ready. President Sauli Niinistö confirmed the laws on Finland's NATO membership on Thursday.

The President wants Finland to be ready for NATO right away, as soon as all NATO countries have approved Finland's membership.

Finland is still waiting for approval from Turkey and Hungary. They have intended to approve Finland's NATO membership soon.

Finland decided to apply for membership of the NATO defence alliance last year, in May. Finland estimates that NATO membership will improve the country's security.

The situation in Europe changed when Russia attacked Ukraine last February.

## Weapons aid for Ukraine

![Armoured vehicle in a winter forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689988)

Open image viewer

Armoured vehicle. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Finland is giving more weapons aid to Ukraine.

This time Finland is giving Ukraine 3 armoured vehicles. Finland also promises to give training for the use and maintenance of the armoured vehicles.

In addition, the aid package includes other weapons and ammunition. The value of the aid package is about 160 million euros.

## Cleaning industry strike

![Cleaner's tools: a bucket, cloths, a floor squeegee and cleaning products.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3121,h_1755,x_0,y_303/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-935326624414ddd47f0/1727426130)

Open image viewer

Photo: Elina Rantalainen / Yle

There is a strike in the cleaning industry.

About 25,000 cleaners and property maintenance workers stopped work on Friday. They are on strike for 2 days.

The strike affects, for example, the cleaning of shops, offices and hotels.

The cleaners are on strike because they demand more pay. A solution to the pay dispute will be sought next time on Monday.

If no solution is found to the dispute, the next strike in the cleaning industry will begin at the end of March.

## The weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689989)

Open image viewer

On Thursday more snow fell. In the central parts of the country up to 20 centimetres of new snow fell.

On Friday the weather is cloudy across wide areas. In the south a little rain falls, and in the central part of the country snow. In Lapland the sun shines in places.

In the south the temperature on Friday is between +1 and +5 degrees, in the north between -1 and -10 degrees.

During Saturday the rain stops, but on Sunday snow will fall in southern Finland. At the same time the weather gets colder.
