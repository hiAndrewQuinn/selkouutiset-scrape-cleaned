# Friday 26.11.2021 (radio)

Many patients in intensive care. A record number of corona infections. Restaurant restrictions come into force on Sunday. A new virus variant was found in Africa. Weather information.

## Intensive care

![Nurse Jenna Muurama works in the intensive care ward](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4988,h_2806,x_0,y_522/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8669216167fe3c50ecd/1634893574)

The number of corona patients in intensive care in hospitals has risen worryingly. Across the country today there are 44 corona patients in intensive care.

The limit of 50 intensive care patients is the point after which, for example, operations must be cancelled. Non-urgent operations must be cancelled, so that there are enough staff for intensive care.

Professor Matti Reinikainen of intensive care says that the situation is easier if intensive care patients are spread evenly across the country. Now a large part of them are in hospitals in southern Finland.

There are now many intensive care patients, for example, in the Helsinki, Lahti and Turku regions.

Today a record number of new coronavirus infections in one day has been found in Finland. More than 1400 infections have been found.

Most infections were found in the Helsinki and Oulu regions.

There are 336 corona patients in hospitals, that is 30 more than yesterday.

7 new deaths have been reported.

## Corona restrictions for restaurants

![A waiter checks a customer's corona pass at the bar of One Pint Pub in Ruoholahti, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4092,h_2302,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-878355618cd4820776b/1636619458)

Open image viewer

Restaurants can be freed from the restrictions if they introduce a corona pass. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The government has confirmed new restaurant restrictions for the worst corona areas.

The new restrictions apply to restaurants that sell alcohol. The sale of alcohol must stop at 17.00 in the afternoon.

Restaurants can be freed from the restrictions if they require a corona pass from all customers.

The restrictions come into force on Sunday.

## A new virus variant

![Biontech corona vaccine bottles on a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_57/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-77186560226447ed535/1612867167)

Open image viewer

It is not yet known how well the current corona vaccines protect against the new virus variant. Photo: Juha-Petri Koponen / Yle

A new kind of corona virus variant has been found in southern Africa.

Researchers say that it is not yet known how dangerous the new virus variant is. However, many mutations, that is changes, have been found in it, and because of this the researchers fear that the virus may be very easy to catch.

The new virus variant has been found, in addition to African countries, in at least Hong Kong and Israel. A new virus variant has also been found in a patient in Belgium.

The medicine company Biontech says that it is urgently studying how well its vaccine protects against the new virus variant. Biontech says that research results will be available in a couple of weeks.
