## Koskela murder verdicts

![Roses and candles at the Koskela murder site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_94/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7528375fd1fe929dded/1613658090)

Candles at the Koskela murder site. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

The Helsinki District Court has given verdicts for murders in Koskela in Helsinki.

The court sentenced 3 boys to prison for the murders. At the time of the crime they were 16 years old. The victim was also a 16-year-old boy.

The boys received prison sentences of 8 to 10 years.

In Finland a minor cannot get a life sentence for murder. Juveniles who are first-time offenders serve a third of their sentences.

The murder happened near the Koskela hospital on 4 December. 2 of the accused had known the victim since kindergarten. 1 got to know the victim only last autumn.

The court said that the boys used long-term, varied and strong violence against the victim.

## Air traffic

![Ryanair aircraft at a London airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11741106)

Open image viewer

Air traffic is starting to recover from the corona pandemic. Photo: Andy Rain / EPA

Air traffic is starting to return towards normal. The corona pandemic stopped a large part of flights.

In Europe air traffic may increase quickly towards the end of the year. For example, the Irish low-cost airline Ryanair believes that next summer there will already be more passengers than before the pandemic.

Finnair also believes that traffic within Europe will increase faster than was thought.

In Finland traffic is recovering more slowly, although bookings have started to come in.

There has been a lot of activity in Finland's national parks.

In the early part of the year more visits were made to the parks than at the same time last year. Metsähallitus says that the increase compared with 2019 is 25 per cent. This year there have been about 2.5 million visits.

Last year too, national parks were visited a record number of times.

Visitor numbers at Pallas-Ylläs National Park rose by 36 per cent.

## Cabin No. 6

![Actress Seidi Haarla in the film Hytti nro 6](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4160,h_2340,x_0,y_169/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82825260e02becc9f95/1625304565)

Actress Seidi Haarla in the film Hytti numero 6. Photo: Festival de Cannes

Juho Kuosmanen's film Hytti nro 6 has won an award again. The film won the main prize at the Jerusalem International Film Festival.

Hytti nro 6 was also awarded in July at the Cannes Film Festival. It received the festival's second most prestigious prize, the Grand Prix.

Hytti nro 6 is based on Rosa Liksom's novel of the same name, which won the Finlandia Prize in 2011.

The film will have its premiere in Finland at the end of October.

One more news item from the Tokyo Paralympics.

Track cyclist Toni Piispanen won silver in the 100 metre cycling in the afternoon. Piispanen had already won a gold medal in the 200 metre cycling earlier in the Games.

Finland now has 5 medals from the Games.
