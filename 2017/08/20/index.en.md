# Sunday 20.8.2017 (radio)

The police continue to investigate the knife attack in Turku. The promise to interview unemployed people is not being kept. Many people are taking a repayment holiday on their mortgage. Bear and duck hunting began.

## The knife attack in Turku

![Turku stabbing](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9784991)

Open image viewer

The police have questioned the man suspected of Friday's knife attack in Turku. Photo: Tapani Teittinen

The police continue to investigate the knife attack in Turku.

The police have questioned the main suspect, an 18-year-old Moroccan man. The police will ask for him to be remanded in custody at the district court on Monday.

The police have also questioned 4 other suspects. However, it is still unclear whether they were in some way involved in the stabbings.

In addition, the police have questioned eyewitnesses and carried out house searches in Turku.

In Friday's stabbings in Turku, 2 people died and 8 were wounded.

## Interviews of unemployed people

![Queuing at a TE Office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9604338)

Open image viewer

The government has promised that all unemployed people will be interviewed every 3 months. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Only a little over 50 per cent of unemployed people are interviewed as often as they should be.

The government has promised that the employment and economic development offices, that is, the TE Offices, will interview all unemployed people every 3 months. However, only about 57 per cent of unemployed people had been interviewed at the beginning of June, the Ministry of Employment says.

The trade union confederation SAK says that the interviews have not been done because there may not be enough staff in the TE Offices.

## A mortgage repayment holiday

![A mortgage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9639448)

Open image viewer

During a repayment holiday, only interest is paid on the mortgage. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Many people are taking a repayment holiday on their mortgage.

About 40 per cent of the people who have a mortgage have taken a repayment holiday. Families with children in particular have taken a repayment holiday.

A repayment holiday means that you agree with the bank on a break in paying back the mortgage. During the repayment holiday, only the interest on the loan is paid to the bank. Then the time to pay back the loan also gets longer.

For the bank, a repayment holiday is profitable, because it then gets more income from interest.

## Bear and duck hunting

![A bear builds up a layer of fat before going into hibernation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9395200)

Open image viewer

The number of bears has grown especially in eastern Finland.  Photo: Tuomo Björksten / Yle

Today, Sunday, the bear hunting season has begun.

This year, permission has been given to kill, that is, hunt, a total of 255 bears. That is about 70 more than last year.

The Natural Resources Institute says that there are more bears in Finland's forests than before. There are perhaps about 1700 adult bears in total. The number of bears has grown especially in eastern Finland.

The bear hunting season lasts until the end of October.

The duck hunting season also began today. It lasts until the end of December.

There are about 300,000 people in Finland who hunt as a hobby.
