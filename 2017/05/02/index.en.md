# Tuesday 2.5.2017 (radio)

The technology industry is doing well. A new online service makes it easier to sell timber. The name law is being reformed. There are fewer Saimaa ringed seal pups this year than a year ago.

## The technology industry

New jobs have arisen in the fields of the technology industry. Photo: AOP

Good news has been received about the economy.

The technology industry is now doing well. The technology industry includes, for example, the electronics industry, the metal industry and information technology.

Products of the technology industry are now sold abroad in large amounts. New jobs also arise in the field.

The companies that do best are those that know how to use new technology.

## Timber trade

![Forest felling.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9102193)

Open image viewer

A lot of timber is sold and bought in Finland. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Selling timber is becoming easier.

A new online service opens this month. In the service, a forest owner can offer timber for sale. Buyers can say how much they are ready to pay for the timber.

Many Finns own a small amount of forest, which is old family property. Often the forest owner lives and works in a city. That is why the new online service is handy for many forest owners.

A lot of timber is sold and bought in Finland, because Finland has a lot of forest industry, for example paper mills.

## Name law

![In the name reform a child can get four first names and a double-barrelled surname.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9590796)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka/Leena Luotio

The name law is being reformed. A working group of the Ministry of Justice proposes that people's freedom in name matters will grow.

The working group proposes that a person can have 4 first names. Today a person may have only 3 first names. The working group also thinks that first names do not have to be Finnish.

The working group wants more freedom also in surnames. The working group proposes that surnames can be combined. If the proposal is accepted, for example, a child can get a surname from both parents if the parents have different surnames.

## Saimaa ringed seal

![Saimaa ringed seal on a rock](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9377420)

Open image viewer

An adult Saimaa ringed seal is big. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

There are fewer Saimaa ringed seal pups, that is pups, this year than a year ago. More Saimaa ringed seal pups have died than usual.

Saimaa ringed seal pups are counted every spring. Finns follow closely how the Saimaa ringed seal is doing, because Saimaa ringed seals live only in Finland.

The Saimaa ringed seal is a big animal. An adult Saimaa ringed seal is about 140 centimetres long, and weighs about 70 kilograms.

The Saimaa ringed seal lives in Lake Saimaa. The Saimaa ringed seal moves a lot under the water, and it eats fish.
