## Corona deaths in Finland

![An elderly person holding another person's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11192424)

Open image viewer

There have been many coronavirus infections in care homes. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Health authorities say that it is somewhat unclear how many people in Finland have died because of the coronavirus.

Health authorities say that the official figure is 90.

It has now emerged that many deaths are missing from this figure.

The health authorities have not been told about deaths that have happened in elderly care homes.

The problem is that information about the deaths has not reached the health authorities.

The health authorities' figure shows how many people have died in hospital because of the corona.

There are now 70 coronavirus patients in intensive care in hospitals.

## The coronavirus spreads in crowds

![A huge crowd in front of a state parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5169,h_2908,x_0,y_178/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6331955e25e406b85ae/1579541628)

Open image viewer

The coronavirus spreads quickly in a large crowd like this. Photo: Shawn Thew / EPA

Health authorities say that in Finland the coronavirus has spread at large events and parties.

Examples are concerts, family parties and events where there were many other people.

Experts say that the greatest risk of getting a coronavirus infection is in a place where there are many other people.

## Slot machines are closed

![Closed slot machines in the supermarket floor of Iso Omena in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11312346)

Open image viewer

Gambling machines are now closed. That may make life easier. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Gambling machines have been closed because of the coronavirus. Slot machines, which are for example in supermarkets, have been closed so that the coronavirus does not spread.

This has been a good thing for people for whom slot machines are a problem.

The Peluuri service, a gambling help service, helps people who have a gambling problem.

Peluuri reports that many people have called and said that life is now easier, now that the slot machines are closed.

## A big charity event online

![The event logo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11312579)

Open image viewer

You can follow the event on Yle Areena or on YouTube. Photo: Minna Uusi-Eskola / Yle

Tomorrow night, a big international charity event will be held because of the coronavirus pandemic.

It will be held online, and many performers from different countries will take part.

The name of the event is The World Together at Home. The idea was thought up by the American musician Lady Gaga.

Yle will show the event on Areena. The programme starts on Areena at 9 pm.

First there is the pre-show, that is, the opening celebrations. The actual concert starts at 3 am.
