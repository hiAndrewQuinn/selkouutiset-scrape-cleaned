# Friday 22.5.2020 (radio)

The housing market has gone quiet. Many bicycles are being bought. Children's teeth decay easily. On Friday there were no corona deaths. On Saturday it is warm.

## The housing market has gone quiet

![Apartment viewing in Katajanokka, Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11335186)

Open image viewer

Apartment viewing. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The corona situation has made the housing market quiet. Many people do not want to buy an apartment now. Many people are careful, because the economy is doing badly.

Hypoteekkiyhdistys is a bank that specialises in housing loans. Hypoteekkiyhdistys says that apartment prices may fall a little, because people are now buying fewer apartments.

The situation may be helpful for buyers of a first home. It may be easier to buy small apartments now.

## Bicycle trade has picked up

![Bicycle trade is starting to pick up.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5244236)

Open image viewer

Bicycles in a shop. Photo: YLE / Kirsi Brück

Many bicycles are being sold now. Bicycle shops say that bicycle sales have grown a lot. People may be buying bicycles because many hobbies have ended because of the corona situation. Many people now have had time to cycle and move about in nature.

For example, bicycle manufacturer Helkama says that bicycle sales have grown by 70% this spring.

In addition to bicycles, people have bought more electric scooters than before.

## Children's teeth decay

![Child at a dental clinic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8207526)

Open image viewer

A child at a dental clinic. Photo: Keijo Salokangas / Yle

The teeth of more children than before are in poor condition. Dentists are treating children's teeth under anaesthesia more than before. A patient can be put under anaesthesia when a dentist has to fill many teeth.

Specialist dentist **Päivi Rajavaara** said in a Yle interview that tooth decay in children is a common problem in Finland.

During exceptional times, many people may be eating more snacks and treats, because they are at home a lot. Brushing teeth may also be easier to forget. Teeth should be brushed in the morning and in the evening.

## No corona deaths on Friday

In Finland, no new corona deaths have been reported today, on Friday.

Yesterday, Thursday, 2 corona deaths were reported. Today, on Friday, the number is 0.

The number of coronavirus infections has also fallen. Today, on Friday, 44 new infections have been reported, that is 6 fewer than on Thursday.
