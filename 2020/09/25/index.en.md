# Friday 25.9.2020 (radio)

There are 105 new corona infections in Finland. The coronavirus is spreading quickly in the Helsinki area. Face masks are recommended for upper secondary school students in different parts of Finland. Joalin Loukamaa is the most popular Finn on Instagram. The weekend is warm in the south and cool in the north.

First, information about corona infections.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, said on Friday that there are 105 new corona infections in Finland.

About 9,500 corona infections have been found in Finland this year.

There are no new corona deaths in Finland. The coronavirus has killed 343 people in Finland so far.

The coronavirus is spreading quickly in the Helsinki and Uusimaa area.

The chief medical officer of the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district, Markku Mäkijärvi, urges people to use face masks. A face mask is good whenever safety distances cannot be kept, for example on buses and in shops.

Mäkijärvi also hopes that people will not organise big private events. It is good if there are fewer than 20 guests.

## Face masks for students

![Emmi Tenkula wore a face mask on the bus on the first recommendation day, 14 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_56/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7081945f36380c69b8e/1597389126)

Open image viewer

A face mask is now necessary. Photo: Miikka Varila / Yle

Face masks are recommended for many upper secondary school students.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare recommends face masks for students in areas where corona infections are growing quickly.

For example, Häme University of Applied Sciences decided on Friday that masks are used at school. At school only, for example, small group work is done, and other teaching is remote teaching. Häme University of Applied Sciences has campuses in Hämeenlinna and Riihimäki.

On Yle's website there is an information package for students about how to use a face mask at school. The information package can be found at yle.fi.

The weekend weather is warm. In Southern Finland the temperature is about 20 degrees.

At the level of Oulu it rains at the weekend and the temperature is about 15 degrees.

Further north in Lapland it is dry, that is, no rain falls. The temperature in Lapland is under 10 degrees.
