# Saturday 26.5.2018 (radio)

Ireland is making it easier to get an abortion. The regional elections cannot be held in October. Blue-green algae may come to waterways within the coming weeks. The news leak show is ending after more than 20 years.

## Ireland allows abortion

![Two women hug and cheer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10225059)

Open image viewer

Voters Andrea Horan and Cara Sanquest rejoice at the fall of the abortion ban. Photo: Paul Faith / EPA

The Irish have voted to change the abortion law. It seems that a clear majority of Irish people want to make it easier to get an abortion.

The Irish government now intends to reform the abortion law so that it is similar to that of many other European countries. Abortion will be allowed up to 12 weeks of pregnancy. Abortion can also be obtained later, if the mother's life or health is in danger or if the foetus has a developmental disorder that will cause it to die.

Until now, Ireland's abortion law has been the strictest in the world. A legal abortion could only be obtained if the mother's life was in danger.

An illegal abortion could bring a prison sentence of up to 14 years.

## Regional elections are postponed

![Petteri Orpo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10218160)

Open image viewer

Chairman of the National Coalition Party Petteri Orpo. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The regional elections may not be held in October yet, says National Coalition Party chairman Petteri Orpo.

Orpo said in an interview on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme that Parliament must first approve the legislation for the health and social care reform and the regional reform. Only after that can it be decided when the regional elections will be held.

The regional elections were supposed to be held in October. However, the handling of the law reform in Parliament has been delayed.

## Blue-green algae will come to waterways within the coming weeks

![Blue-green algae on a rocky beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7502747)

Open image viewer

If there is blue-green algae in the water, you must not swim in it. Photo: Hanne Kinnunen / Yle

Blue-green algae may come to waterways this summer earlier than usual.

The Finnish Environment Institute says that the first sightings of blue-green algae in lakes may be made as early as the first half of June. Blue-green algae may also be seen in shallow sea bays within the coming weeks. That is two or three weeks earlier than usual.

The May weather has been so warm that the surface waters have already warmed up. Blue-green algae also thrive in warm water.

Blue-green algae is a poisonous alga. You must not drink water that contains blue-green algae, and you must not swim in it.

## The news leak says goodbye

![News leak](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10224583)

Open image viewer

Performers from over the years of the News Leak show: Stan Saanila, Katja Ståhl, Jari Tervo, Baba Lybeck, Peter Nyman, Anna Perho, Jani Halme, Petteri Ahomaa. Photo: Jussi Mankkinen / Yle

The television favourite show News Leak (Uutisvuoto) is ending. The last episode of News Leak will be shown today, Saturday.

News Leak was made for more than 20 years. The first episode was shown on Yle's TV1 in February 1998. In total, 700 episodes of the programme were made.

News Leak was a satirical quiz in which two teams talked about the week's news in a funny and witty way.

News Leak became a favourite with audiences right from the start, and the programme won several Venla television awards. Critics did not like the programme.
