# Sunday 26.03.2017 (radio)

Russian police have arrested opposition leader Aleksei Navalny. Sunday has been the warmest day of spring so far. Formula car races have started. More than 700,000 people with a Finnish background live in Sweden.

## Russian opposition leader

In Russia the police have arrested an important opposition leader. The police arrested Aleksei Navalny at a demonstration in Moscow.

Navalny is aiming to become a presidential candidate. He has criticised Russia's leaders, President Vladimir Putin and Prime Minister Dmitry Medvedev.

## Warm weather

![A crocus pushing up out of the ground after the winter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9529856)

Open image viewer

Spring is coming! Photo: Yle / Sini Liimatainen

Summer time has started here in Finland. The clocks were moved forward by an hour during the night between Saturday and Sunday.

Summer time began in warm weather. Sunday has been the warmest day of spring so far.

In Western Finland the temperature has been over 10 degrees. In Åland the temperature rose to 15 degrees.

## Formula races

![Valtteri Bottas and Kimi Räikkönen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9530923)

Formula 1 drivers Valtteri Bottas and Kimi Räikkönen. Photo: AOP

The Formula 1 season has started. The racing season for Formula cars began in Melbourne, Australia.

The winner of the Australian race was Ferrari driver Sebastian Vettel. Second (2nd) was Mercedes driver Lewis Hamilton.

Finnish drivers did well in Australia. Valtteri Bottas was third (3rd) in the race and Kimi Räikkönen fourth (4th). Bottas drives a Mercedes and Räikkönen drives a Ferrari.

The Formula 1 season may become exciting, because tough competition between the Ferrari drivers and the Mercedes drivers started already in the first race. The next race is in 2 weeks' time in Shanghai, China.

## Swedish Finns

![Amanda Lähdeaho](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9528196)

Open image viewer

Amanda Lähdeaho's grandparents moved from Finland to Sweden. Photo: Mike Toivonen / Yle

More than 700,000 people with a Finnish background live in Finland's neighbouring country, Sweden. They are called Swedish Finns.

One of them is 18-year-old Amanda Lähdeaho. Amanda's grandparents moved from Finland to Sweden when Amanda's mother was young. Amanda was born in Sweden.

Amanda is proud that she is Finnish. Amanda's school friends say that Amanda is clearly Finnish, because she is hardworking and does everything so well.

Swedish Finns have not always been proud of their Finnish background. About 50 years ago, many Finns moved to Sweden to work. The migrants were quite poor. At that time the children of Finnish families often felt ashamed that they were different from Swedes.

Amanda is like Swedish young people, but she also has an interesting Finnish background. And what things are important to Amanda in Finland? The Finnish language, food, nature, dance and music, she says. Amanda's favourites are Cheek, Antti Tuisku and Chisu.
