# Friday 4.11.2022 (radio)

Problems with the prison information system endanger safety. President Sauli Niinistö visits Lithuania. A conscript died in an accident. Fewer people are invited to the Presidential Palace reception than before. The weather is cloudy.

## Prison information system

![Sign above the main entrance of Helsinki prison.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_398/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1027252635f993b3d5cc/1667209679)

Many people working in prisons say that the new information system has messed up everyday life in prisons. Photo: Jari Kärkkäinen / Yle

There are big problems with the new information system in prisons.

Yle has interviewed several prison guards. They say that the new information system Roti is bad in many ways. The safety of prisons is in danger.

For example, it is difficult to find information about prisoners' sentences in the system. There are also many other faults in the system.

The Roti information system came to prisons half a year ago. The information system cost over 40 million euros.

## President Niinistö in Lithuania

![Sauli Niinistö, Gitanas Nauseda.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4561,h_2558,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10295376364f2cf077f2/1667560177)

Open image viewer

Presidents Sauli Niinistö and Gitanas Nausėda. Photo: Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö is visiting Lithuania.

Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda said that Russian tourists travelling abroad is a moral problem. He said that the visa ban for Russian tourists is one way to put pressure on Russia.

President Sauli Niinistö said that Finland has not set any time limit on the visa ban for Russian tourists. The visa ban is therefore in force for as long as it is needed. Russian tourists do not get a visa to Finland, because Russia is waging a war of aggression in Ukraine.

## A conscript's death in an accident

![The Finnish flag flies in the yard of the Kainuu Brigade.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_107/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8699206171497ba4e17/1634815541)

Open image viewer

Kainuu Brigade, archive photo. Photo: Mimmi Nietula / Yle

A serious accident happened in the army on Thursday.

A conscript died in the accident at the Kainuu Brigade. Police say that the conscript's own weapon went off before a shooting exercise. The conscript suffered serious head injuries. He later died at Kuopio University Hospital.

No one is suspected of a crime. The police are investigating the accident.

## Presidential Palace reception

![Guests at the Presidential Palace in 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_141/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7499985fc8f6eb4feb6/1607006001)

Guests at the Presidential Palace's festive reception in December 2019. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

The Independence Day celebrations will be held at the Presidential Palace this year.

About 30 percent fewer people are invited to the reception than in previous years. About 1,300 people from all over Finland will receive an invitation to the Presidential Palace reception. The invitations will be sent by post soon.

Last year the Presidential Palace reception was not held at all because of corona. In 2020, too, Independence Day was celebrated in Finland under exceptional circumstances. There were no guests at the Presidential Palace reception at that time.
