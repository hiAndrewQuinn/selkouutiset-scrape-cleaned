# Tuesday 18.5.2021 (radio)

The EU recovery package has been approved. The export industry is pleased with the approval of the recovery package. Fuel taxes will not rise now. Finns want a corona vaccine.

## Finland approved the EU recovery package

![Parliament approved the EU recovery package.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11936615)

Open image viewer

Parliament voted on the EU recovery package. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

Parliament has approved the EU recovery package. Parliament voted on the matter this afternoon, on Tuesday.

Parliament was supposed to vote on the matter already last week. However, the vote was moved to this week, because the Finns Party MPs made very many speeches on the matter.

The EU recovery package is 750 billion euros. It is used to help the EU's economy after the corona crisis.

## The export industry is delighted with the recovery package

![A port crane made by Cargotec.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6882138)

Open image viewer

A port crane made by the Finnish company Cargotec. Photo: Cargotec

Finland's export industry is pleased that the EU recovery package was approved in Parliament.

Companies' customers get EU recovery money, which they can use for purchases, for example from Finnish companies.

For example, ports can buy cranes and other port machines from a Finnish company.

## Fuel tax will not rise now

![Road 15 photographed from the overpass at Anjalankoski.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7397335fa909813c074/1606294526)

Open image viewer

Traffic emissions are to be reduced through taxes. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

An increase in the fuel tax has been proposed. A working group proposes that car tax is reduced and fuel taxes are increased.

The aim is to shift taxation more towards consumption. The aim is that people drive less and traffic emissions fall.

However, the government is not changing the fuel tax now. Finance Minister **Matti Vanhanen** says that there will be no increases in the fuel tax during this government's term. Instead, the ministry will start to investigate whether car use could be taxed according to the distance driven.

## Finns want a corona vaccine

![A corona vaccine being put into an arm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3954,h_2224,x_0,y_37/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-791676606c23a687127/1619686949)

Open image viewer

Most Finns are willing to take the corona vaccine. Only 3 percent say they do not want the vaccine. Photo: Pasi Takkunen / Yle

Most Finns want to take the corona vaccine. The Confederation of Finnish Industries has commissioned a survey on the matter.

55 percent of Finns say that they will certainly take the corona vaccine. 23 percent of Finns say that they will probably take the corona vaccine. Only 3 percent of respondents do not want the corona vaccine.

## New school leavers announced

![A white student cap in an apple tree.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4928,h_2772,x_0,y_246/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6828055ed0f17b0d6d9/1590751810)

Open image viewer

School leavers get a white cap on their heads. Photo: Anniina Nirhamo/Yle

The names of the new school leavers were published today, Tuesday.

This spring about 24,700 new school leavers will graduate.
The upper secondary school exams were held in about 400 upper secondary schools.

School leavers will graduate on Saturday 5 June. Because of the corona situation, the graduation celebrations will be limited. For example, in many upper secondary schools the school leavers' parents cannot join the school celebrations. Guests can also only be invited to homes in a limited way.
