# Friday 2.2.2018 (tv)

There was a big demonstration in Helsinki against the activation model for unemployment benefits. Because of the activation model, there were also strikes on Friday. Artist Kari Vehosalo has received the Ars Fennica award. 102 athletes are going from Finland to the Olympics in South Korea. The weather.
