## A strike on Syria

![The UN Security Council.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10160592)

Open image viewer

The UN Security Council met on Saturday after the air strikes on Syria. Photo: Drew Angerer / AFP

The UN Security Council has held an emergency meeting and rejected Russia's demands. The Security Council met on Saturday after the air strikes on Syria.

Russia demanded that the strike on Syria by the United States, Britain and France must be condemned. Russia did not get enough supporters for its demand.

## Radio adverts

![Old radio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10144793)

Open image viewer

An old radio. Photo: Juha Kivioja / Yle

Commercial radio stations will soon be able to broadcast adverts freely. Until now adverts have been allowed to take up at most 20 per cent of broadcasting time. Now the limit will be removed completely. But adverts must be clearly separated from programmes.

The aim is, for example, that radio stations earn more money.

## A spring smile

![spring sun shining](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10158566)

Open image viewer

The spring sun is shining. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen /Yle

Finns start to smile in spring. The spring sun has positive effects.

85 per cent of Finns feel that the arrival of spring affects them. According to a researcher, most Finns feel the effect is positive.

People become interested in other people, in being together and in what other people say and feel. Physical energy also increases and appetite goes down.

The researcher says that in summer a person sleeps well if they avoid light late in the evening. The quality of sleep is good and sleep refreshes. So a person has more energy left for the day, when they are awake.

## Climate anxiety

![rainforest in mist](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10142792)

Open image viewer

Climate anxiety causes anger, sadness and guilt. Photo: AOP

Climate anxiety is common, especially for many young people and young adults. Climate anxiety causes anger, sadness and guilt. The anxiety comes from the feelings caused by climate change.

Climate change means that people, through what they do, cause the climate to warm, and this leads to very big problems. Aalto University offers students a discussion group. There, feelings and anxiety are dealt with.

## Relationships between plants

![Parsley and tomato.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10159617)

Open image viewer

Parsley and tomato are dream partners. Photo: Yle

In the plant world relationships can be easy or difficult – just like with people. Plants and growers benefit from species that go well together.

An expert says that, for example, basil and cucumber are a perfect pair, because basil makes sure that the cucumber does not get ill. Often one plant also improves the taste or growth of another plant, or keeps away harmful insects.
