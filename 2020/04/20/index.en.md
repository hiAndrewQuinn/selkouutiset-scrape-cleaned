# Monday 20.4.2020 (radio)

Only a few young people think that any work is acceptable, as long as it is paid well enough. The return of Finnish children from Al-Hol has not made progress. Millions of respiratory masks have been rejected in Denmark. The first adoptions into families of same-sex couples have been confirmed.

## Young people know what they want from work

Young people are committed to work, says the Youth Barometer. For young people, work has meaning especially when the young person's occupation is being an entrepreneur.

Young people think that work must give something to a person. Only a few young people think that any work is acceptable, as long as it is paid well enough.

Almost half of students say that choosing an occupation stresses them. A third say that choosing an occupation frightens them.

The Youth Barometer measures the values and attitudes of young people every year.

## The return from Al-Hol has been delayed

![Women and a child at the Al-Hol camp.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11181421)

Open image viewer

Women and a child at the Al-Hol camp. Photo: Otso Reunanen / Yle

The bringing of Finnish children from Al-Hol to Finland has not progressed because of the coronavirus. The Al-Hol camp is in north-eastern Syria. The camp holds people from the caliphate of the terrorist organisation ISIS.

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says that the return has been delayed also because of the Kurdish administration. The Kurdish administration plans that the women in the camp may be sentenced for crimes on the spot.

There are about 30 Finnish children and about 10 Finnish women at the Al-Hol camp. Two orphaned children were evacuated from the camp to Finland in December 2019.

The Finnish government wants the children of the camp to come to Finland as soon as possible.

## Doctors talk about protective equipment

![A nurse with a respirator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11307148)

Open image viewer

Staff respirators at the Central Finland Central Hospital in Jyväskylä in early April. Stock image. Photo: Tommi Anttonen / Lehtikuva

Only 64 percent of doctors in corona wards say that there is enough protective equipment in their unit.

In a survey by the Finnish Medical Association, 30 percent of respondents said that there is sometimes a shortage of protective equipment. 6 percent said that there is a constant shortage of equipment.

Protective equipment is best available in hospitals.

## Millions of masks have been rejected in Denmark

![A Chinese worker makes respirators at a factory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11311907)

Open image viewer

Masks are being made. The factory in the picture is not related to the story. Photo: AFP

In Denmark, a large share of the respirators have been rejected for use.

In Denmark, 20 million protective items that were intended for use by doctors and nurses were examined. 5 million protective items were rejected. At least some of the protective items were from China.

Respirators are filtering half masks. They must be made according to international and European standards.

## Adoptions in rainbow families

![A man holding a rainbow flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10141058)

Open image viewer

The rainbow flag has been used to represent diversity. Today the best-known rainbow flags are those of sexual and gender minorities, the peace movement and the co-operative movement. Photo: Lucas Coch / EPA

The first adoptions into families of same-sex couples have been confirmed in Finland. The adoptions were confirmed in a court of law. This is according to the Rainbow Families association.

So far at least 2 adoptions have been confirmed. 1 adoption was confirmed into a female couple's family and 1 into a male couple's family. In both families a baby-aged child was adopted.

3 years ago the reform of the Marriage Act came into force. That is why external adoption is now possible for couples of the same sex.
