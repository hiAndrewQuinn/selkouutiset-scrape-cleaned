# Wednesday 27.2.2019 (radio)

Trump and Kim are talking in Vietnam. The winner of Nigeria's presidential election is the country's current president, Muhammadu Buhari. Sales of strong long drink have grown a lot. Iivo Niskanen won a bronze medal at the world championships. The weather turns colder on Thursday.

## The meeting of Trump and Kim

The meeting between Kim Jong-un and Donald Trump has begun. Photo: Ye Aung Thu / Lehtikuva

The meeting between the leaders of the USA and North Korea has begun in Vietnam. President Donald Trump and North Korean leader Kim Jong-un are meeting in the capital of Vietnam, Hanoi.

On Wednesday the leaders met briefly, but nothing was said about the content of the talks. The official talks will be on Thursday.

Then the presidents will discuss nuclear weapons. The USA wants North Korea to give up its nuclear weapons.

## Nigerian presidential election

![Muhammadu Buhari spoke to the media at a polling station in Katsina, Nigeria, on 23 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10664330)

Muhammadu Buhari will continue as president of Nigeria. Photo: George Esiri / EPA

The result of the Nigerian presidential election has been announced. Nigeria's current president, Muhammadu Buhari, won the election. Buhari has been president of Nigeria since 2015.

Nigerians voted in the presidential election on Saturday. There were problems in the election. It is reported that voters were intimidated. More than 50 people died at the weekend because of violence.

Nigeria has almost 200 million residents. That is more than in any other African country.

## Sales of long drinks

![Long drink for sale in a shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10169653)

Open image viewer

Long drink, or lonkero, has become a hit product in grocery shops. Photo: Pekka Pantsu / Yle

There has been a change in the sales of alcoholic drinks.

Strong long drink, or lonkero, has grown in popularity. Last year, 3 times more strong lonkero was sold than a year earlier.

The new alcohol law is visible in the figures. The law gave grocery shops permission to sell stronger alcoholic drinks than before. Today, grocery shops may sell drinks with 5.5 percent alcohol. Strong long drink also contains that much alcohol.

At the same time as sales of strong long drink grow, sales of other alcoholic drinks have decreased.

## Bronze for Niskanen

![Iivo Niskanen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10665681)

Open image viewer

Iivo Niskanen congratulates the world champion, Norway's Martin Johnsrud Sundby. Photo: Lehtikuva

Finland has won its first medal at the ski world championships in Seefeld, Austria.

Iivo Niskanen was third in the men's 15-kilometre race. However, the bronze medal was a disappointment for Niskanen.

At the previous world championships Niskanen was the 15-kilometre world champion. Niskanen's goal this time was also to win the world championship gold, but that did not succeed.

However, Finns are happy about Niskanen's bronze medal.

## Weather

![A skier on the track.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10599400)

Open image viewer

At least in Northern Finland you can still ski. Photo: Henrik Isaksson / AOP

The weather turns colder on Thursday. The temperature in Southern Finland is above zero during the day, but elsewhere there is frost.

In Southern Finland and Central Finland it may snow or sleet. In Northern Finland it is dry, so it does not rain there.
