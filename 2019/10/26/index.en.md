# Saturday 26.10.2019 (radio)

An autumn storm cut the electricity to thousands of homes. More employees are needed in the technology industry. You can change your sleep rhythm. Normal time gives one more hour on Sunday.

## Autumn storm

![storm in Pori](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11038690)

Open image viewer

There was a storm in Pori during the night between Friday and Saturday. Photo: Yle

There has been an autumn storm in Finland. The storm cut the electricity to thousands of homes.

During the night between Friday and Saturday, more than 40,000 homes were without electricity. In Western Finland, for example in Pori, the storm cut the electricity for many city residents.

The repair work has gone quickly on Saturday. Almost all homes have already had their electricity back.

## Technology industry

![Helsinki ABB.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9526867)

Open image viewer

The technology industry needs more workers. Photo: AOP

More employees are needed in the technology industry. The technology industry makes, for example, IT and machines. The sector is important for the whole Finnish economy.

The technology industry estimates that it needs thousands of new skilled people every year. Especially, people with good education are needed. For example, more diploma engineers are needed.

Universities of technology are worried, because they have not received more money from the state.

The technology industry's organisation suggests more cooperation between universities and companies. Companies may be able to offer internship places and take part in organising teaching, the organisation suggests.

## Sleep rhythm

![a sleeping woman under a blanket](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11037345)

Open image viewer

Light, exercise and food help if mornings are hard. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Your sleep rhythm affects your health.

Many people find it hard to wake up early in the morning and go to bed on time in the evening. These night owls get some illnesses more often than others, says research professor Timo Partonen.

Partonen also says that you can change your sleep rhythm. Light, exercise and food affect the sleep rhythm.

It is easier to wake up in the morning if a person gets light in the morning between 5 and 9 o'clock. That is why many people use a bright light lamp during the dark winter.

Exercise can also make mornings easier. If a person exercises for an hour in the morning at around 7 o'clock or in the afternoon between 13 and 16, it becomes gradually easier for them to wake up in the morning.

Food also affects the sleep rhythm. A good breakfast and a light evening meal can help if mornings feel heavy.

## Winter time

![Clocks switch to winter time tonight at 4.00.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5444412)

Open image viewer

On Sunday morning at 4 o'clock the clocks are moved one hour back. Winter time begins. Photo: Yle News Graphics

On Sunday morning you can sleep longer, because summer time ends. Normal time, that is, winter time, begins at 4 in the morning. Then the clock will actually be only 3.

All EU countries switch to winter time together. Finland has proposed that the EU end the changing between summer time and winter time. Some EU countries do not like the idea.
