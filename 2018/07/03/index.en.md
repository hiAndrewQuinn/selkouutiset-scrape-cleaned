# Tuesday 3.7.2018 (radio)

In Thailand, people are working out how to rescue a youth football team from a cave. There have been problems ordering Kela taxis. A small plane has had an accident in Rovaniemi. Yle looked into market prices for strawberries.

## In Thailand, people are looking for a way to rescue the boys from the cave

![In new pictures taken deep in the cave, the boys look exhausted and thin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10285602)

In Thailand, people are working out how to rescue a youth football team from a cave.

On Monday, divers found all 12 boys and the coach alive.

The plan is to take them food for several months. It is not yet known how the team can be brought to safety. One option is that the boys are taught to dive out of the cave. 

The team got trapped in the Tham Luang cave network on Midsummer Saturday, when the boys went to shelter from heavy rain. The team is 2.3 kilometres deep in the cave.

Divers have taken food and medicine to the boys.

The temperature in the caves is about 26 degrees, and drinkable water runs down the walls. Because of this, the boys have probably avoided dehydration for 9 days. 

## Ordering Kela taxis has been difficult

![Taxis waiting for a fare](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5236543)

Open image viewer

Kela taxis were hard to get on Monday and Tuesday. Photo: Yle

There have been problems ordering Kela taxis.

People say that the taxi has not always arrived. Also, people have had to wait many hours for taxis. Sometimes it has not been possible to get through to the booking centre at all. 

This year, Kela taxi rides were put out to tender for the first time. In each of the 17 regions, a service provider was chosen to handle bookings. The change came into force on Sunday. 

Kela says that there have been problems especially in North Savo, Satakunta and Lapland. Ordering went more easily on Tuesday than on Monday.

Kela says that there have not been enough Kela taxis in all areas. The reason is that drivers made agreements with the service provider at the last minute. 

## A small plane was destroyed on a test flight

![A helicopter flies near the place where the small plane fell.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10286023)

Open image viewer

A helicopter flies near the place where the small plane fell Photo: Uula Kuvaja / Yle

A small plane has had an accident in Rovaniemi.

The plane made an emergency landing in the morning in Rovaniemi near the airport and caught fire. 

The small plane was on a test flight.

It was a prototype plane of Atol Avion, an aircraft maker from Rovaniemi. The plane's canopy opened during the flight. 

There were 2 people on the test flight. They got out of the plane by themselves but got burns.

The plane was completely destroyed in the crash.  

## The price of strawberries varies

![Boxes of strawberries at the Kuopio market](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10285252)

Open image viewer

Boxes of strawberries at the Kuopio market. Photo: Sakari Partanen / Yle

Yle looked into market prices for strawberries in about 20 towns. There were big differences in the prices,

The cheapest price per litre, 4 euros, was in Kuopio and Pori on Monday. The most expensive price per litre, 7 euros, was in Kokkola and Rovaniemi. 

A 5-kilo box cost the least, 30 euros, in Kuopio, Kouvola and Tampere. The most expensive box was at the Helsinki Market Square, 48 euros. A box also cost 40 euros or more in Kotka, Kokkola, Seinäjoki and Kajaani.
