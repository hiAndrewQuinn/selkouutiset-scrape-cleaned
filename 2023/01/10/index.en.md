## Corona

The World Health Organization, WHO, says that China's poor corona situation does not affect Europe.

There are now very many corona infections in China, because China only recently ended strict corona restrictions. However, WHO does not believe that the number of corona infections will grow in Europe because of China.

In Europe there is fear that a new corona wave will come to Europe from China. That is why many countries intend to require that passengers have a negative corona test result when they come from China.

Finland too intends to require a corona test from passengers who come from China.

## Security sector

Many employees in the security sector have lost their permits to work in the sector. Last year the police revoked the permits of about 750 people to work in the security sector.

The National Police Board investigated how the police monitor the activities of security sector firms and employees. It is not yet known whether there will be changes in the security sector.

In Finland a lot is now being said about the activities of guards. People have been shaken by the death at the Iso Omena shopping centre in Espoo.

A woman died at the shopping centre last Saturday. The woman died in a situation where guards had apprehended her.

In addition, news this winter has reported that a group of public order officers may have assaulted people.

## Electricity subsidy

![Control panel of a dishwasher.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5422005)

Open image viewer

Photo: YLE / Anssi Leppänen

The government parties have agreed on an electricity subsidy.

With the electricity subsidy the government wants to help households, because electricity is now expensive. The government intends to support all households that pay more than 10 cents per kilowatt hour for electricity.

The state intends to pay 50 percent of electricity bills for 4 months. The months are November, December, January and February. The support may be paid together with the March electricity bill.

The support can be at most 700 euros a month, and everyone must pay at least 90 euros a month themselves.

The electricity subsidy does not need to be applied for, as it is paid automatically.

The details of the electricity subsidy may still change.
