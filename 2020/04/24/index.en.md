## People are avoiding unnecessary medical examinations

![Operating theatre sign at Tays.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_313/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6673245ea150ee578ad/1587631385)

Non-urgent treatment of illness may get congested after the corona epidemic. People are now cancelling or postponing doctor's appointments themselves.

Some do not come for treatment or to a doctor, because they do not want to burden the health care system. A drop in patients has already been noticed in emergency departments across the country.

Experts say that the situation is serious. If long-term illnesses are not monitored and treated, the patient's condition may get worse. The condition may be life-threatening, or the risk of related illnesses increases.

The doctors' strong message is this: when an expert assesses that you are called in for treatment, you can and should come.

## Those with heart symptoms should seek treatment right away

![Heart examination](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5101165)

Patient in a heart examination. Photo: Yle

Many people get heart symptoms but avoid seeking treatment because of fear of the coronavirus.

The number of heart patients in treatment has fallen to about half of normal.

Unfortunately, some patients die at home or in hospital from a heart attack, because they do not go for treatment or go too late.

## Many people are applying for a repayment holiday on mortgages

![A pen and mortgage papers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10506212)

Open image viewer

Because of the corona, many people have been laid off or have lost their jobs. That is why they are applying for a holiday from repayments on their mortgage. Photo: Kimmo Hiltunen / Yle

A large number of Finns have applied to their bank for a repayment holiday on their mortgage. In total about 165,000 applications have come in.

Most came from private customers. About 20,000 applications have come from companies.

Applications have come many dozens of times more often than before the corona era.

Finance Finland (Finanssiala ry) says that a large part of the applications have already been processed. Almost all holiday applications have been approved.

## Police are investigating forgery in an asylum application

Yle has received information about a possible forgery in an asylum application. The police suspect a woman whose father applied for asylum in Finland of forging a document.

An Iraqi man applied for asylum, but he was sent back to Iraq. Later it was claimed that the man was murdered shortly after returning

Finland received an adverse decision in the case from the European Court of Human Rights. The National Bureau of Investigation is investigating the case, because the man may be alive.

The district court today put the Iraqi man's daughter in custody. She is suspected of aggravated fraud and aggravated forgery.

## Waiting for early potatoes is a sign of spring

![Aerial image of Tapio Pietilä driving a tractor on a potato field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6668875ea019c345a54/1587551162)

The warm weather got tractors going on fields in Häme too. In Räyskälä, Jussi potatoes that will be ready by Midsummer were planted. Photo: Ville Välimäki / Yle

Early potatoes are an important summer delicacy for Finns. For example, in Häme the first early potatoes can be had at Midsummer.

Potatoes are now at a normal spring pace. First it was warm, and then a late winter spell came.

Not all farmers plant early potato varieties. In that case, the potatoes are usually lifted only in the first half of July.
