## Maths in upper secondary school

Many young people have started studying long maths in upper secondary school. Young people choose maths because they want to get into university.

University admissions are being reformed next year, and the maths grade will become very important.

Headteachers of upper secondary schools fear that because of the reform, the study of other subjects in upper secondary schools will decrease.

## Microplastics

![Finger with exfoliating cream on it](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9592449)

Open image viewer

Microplastics are found in cosmetics, for example. Photo: AOP

Microplastics are now everywhere. Microplastics are very tiny plastic particles. Microplastics also get into the human body.

A report from the nature organisation WWF says that 5 grams of microplastic get into the human body every week.

Microplastics get into nature from cosmetics, clothes and traffic, for example.

Microplastic particles are often so small that microplastics also travel into drinking water.

## Biogas

![A gas refuelling hose attached to a car's gas tank.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10494725)

Open image viewer

Biogas is an environmentally friendly fuel. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

In the future many cars may perhaps run on cow manure. Biogas can be made from cow manure for gas cars.

Biogas reduces traffic emissions, which warm the climate. That is why Finland's goal is that 10 per cent of cars will run on biogas by 2045.

Making biogas from manure works well. Biogas is also a good option for the motorist's purse, because it is not expensive.

## The Moon

Long ago an asteroid hit the Moon. Photo: Nasa

There is a large piece of metal inside the Moon. American researchers say so.

The piece may be part of an asteroid. The asteroid hit the Moon perhaps about 4 billion years ago.

Asteroids are rocky small planets. Researchers believe that an asteroid made of iron and nickel hit the Moon.

## Footballers' earnings

![Lionel Messi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10828247)

Open image viewer

Lionel Messi earns a lot of money. Photo: Getty Images

Footballers earned the most of all athletes last year.

The earnings of the Argentine Lionel Messi were the highest of all athletes. He earned 112 million euros. Messi is a superstar of the football team Barcelona.

The Portuguese Cristiano Ronaldo earned the second most last year, and the Brazilian Neymar the third most. The earnings of Ronaldo and Neymar were over 90 million.

The figures are from the business magazine Forbes.
