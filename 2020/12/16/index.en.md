All EU countries will start coronavirus vaccinations on the same day. 6 new coronavirus deaths have been reported in Finland. Many Finns are swapping their car for an electric bike with the help of the scrappage bonus. The new buzzword among young people is legit. It is frosty on Thursday, and on Friday the weather warms up again.

## EU coronavirus vaccinations

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen spoke about the EU's vaccination plans. Photo: John Thys / AFP

The European Union has spoken about coronavirus vaccinations.

The EU said that all EU countries will start coronavirus vaccinations on the same day. In this way the EU wants to stress that the member states are fighting the coronavirus together.

The European Medicines Agency will assess as early as next Monday whether it approves the Pfizer and Biontech coronavirus vaccine for use in EU countries.

The European Union wants to start vaccinations soon, perhaps already this year.

The EU wants to hurry the coronavirus vaccinations, because vaccinations have already started in North America, Britain and Russia.

## Scrappage bonus

![A car at a scrapyard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10380612)

Open image viewer

You can now get a scrappage bonus for scrapping an old car. Photo: AOP

Many Finns are now swapping their car for an electric bike.

The reason is the scrappage bonus. The scrappage bonus campaign started at the beginning of December.

The state pays the scrappage bonus to a motorist who gives up an old car and scraps it. In this way the state wants to reduce old cars that pollute the air.

The scrappage bonus can be used to buy a new car, an electric bike or a public transport ticket. Finns are most interested in the electric bike.

The scrappage bonus for buying an electric bike can be as much as 1,000 euros. That is why many people intend to swap an old car for an electric bike during the scrappage bonus campaign.

The scrappage bonus campaign continues through the whole of next year.

## The buzzword "legit"

![Young people in town.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_543/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7264315f7b06ccd7960/1601905979)

Young people's language is changing all the time. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Here is young people's new buzzword. It is *legit*.

*Legit* means "really" or "are you really of that opinion". It can also be used to stress something.

Young people's buzzwords usually come from English. They are only in use for a while. Fashions change quickly.
