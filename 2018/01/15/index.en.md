# Monday 15.1.2018 (radio)

SAK is organising a big demonstration. Money has disappeared from the accounts of Osuuspankki customers. The number of church members is falling. The two Koreas continue talks about the Olympics. The Norwegian government wants to end fur farming. Ideas were made for easy social media services.

## SAK organises a demonstration against the activation model

![SAK's premises.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9144638)

Open image viewer

SAK is Finland's largest wage earners' organisation. Photo: Petteri Paalasmaa / AOP

The workers' organisation SAK plans to organise a big demonstration against the government's activation model.

The activation model weakens the security of unemployed people. Unemployed people get less money if they do not work now and then or get training. SAK says that it is humiliating for the unemployed.

It is also harder to get work in the countryside than in the city, SAK reminds.

The demonstration will be held on 2 February in Helsinki.

## Money disappeared from Osuuspankki accounts

![OP bank](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9820401)

Open image viewer

A technical fault at Osuuspankki has taken money from customers' accounts. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Money has disappeared from the bank accounts of Osuuspankki customers.

A fault has appeared in the bank's system, and so money has disappeared from customers' accounts, Osuuspankki says.

Many customers say that money had disappeared from their account last night. The fault is being fixed and the money will be returned to customers' accounts.

## More than 50,000 people left the church

![Cathedral](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9975076)

Open image viewer

The number of church members is falling in Finland. In the picture, Turku Cathedral. Photo: Viivi Sihvonen / Yle

More than 52,000 people left the Evangelical Lutheran Church last year.

That is more than in 2016.

About 16,000 people joined the church last year.

Almost 4 million people belong to the church, or about 70 percent of Finns.

## The Koreas continue talks about the Olympics

![The Olympic village in South Korea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9974576)

Open image viewer

The Winter Olympics will be held in South Korea. Photo: Jenny Matikainen / Yle

North Korea and South Korea are holding more talks about the Pyeongchang Olympics.

The countries agreed that North Korea can send a 140-person orchestra to the Olympics next month.

The Winter Olympics have got the Koreas to hold talks for the first time in two years.

## Norway may end fur farming

![A mink in a cage at a fur farm in Ypäjä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10022956)

Open image viewer

On fur farms, the animals are usually in small cages. Photo: Kimmo Mäntylä / Lehtikuva

Norway plans to ban fur farming.

Norway has 340 fur farms, where foxes and minks are raised in cages. Finland has 914 fur farms. That is the second most in all of Europe.

The Norwegian government proposes that fur farming should end by 2025.

## Ideas for easy social media services in Helsinki

![The Eficode team's virtual pet Markotti was the best idea for an easy social media service at the Kohdataan hack event.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10023882)

Open image viewer

The Eficode team came up with a fun and smart virtual pet. Photo: Kaisa Kaatra / Kehitysvammaliitto

You can find new friends on social media. However, using social media is difficult for many people.

At the weekend, an event was held in Helsinki where the participants came up with ideas for an easy social media service. The event was part of a project by the Finnish Association on Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities and the Finnish Association for Mental Health.

The goal of the project is a service where it is easy to get to know new people.

The best idea of the event was given an award. It was Markotti, a virtual pet. With Markotti, it is easier for people to approach each other.
