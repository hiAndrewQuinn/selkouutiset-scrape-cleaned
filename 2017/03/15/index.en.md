# Wednesday 15.3.2017 (radio)

There are a lot of young immigrants in Finland. In Kouvola immigrants are learning Finnish in a new way. The Great Journalism Awards have been handed out. The polar bears at Ranua Zoo came out of their winter den. Tourists can buy Finnish air in a bottle.

## Young immigrants

![Anonymous people in a shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9090961)

Open image viewer

There are a lot of immigrants among people under 30. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There are a lot of young immigrants in Finland. 8 percent of young people aged between 15 and 29 are immigrants. In other age groups there are fewer of them.

Most immigrant young people are in Uusimaa. Helsinki is the centre of Uusimaa.

## A Finnish course for stay-at-home mothers

![Children's feet under a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8063439)

Open image viewer

The children have their own programme while the mothers learn Finnish. Photo: Titta Puurunen / Yle

In Kouvola immigrants are learning Finnish in a new way. The idea is that things are done together. If, for example, they are learning what "driving a car" means, they turn a steering wheel in play.

On the Kouvola courses there is also a lot of discussion. It is important that the course participants dare to speak.

Stay-at-home mothers attend the Kouvola Finnish language courses. That is why there is also childcare at the course venue.

Immigrant mothers like the Kouvola courses. That is why similar courses are planned in other cities too.

## An award for Jani Halme

![Jani Halme.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9512744)

Open image viewer

Jani Halme celebrated the award together with his wife. Photo: Yle

The Great Journalism Awards have been handed out. The media company Bonnier gave awards to journalists on Tuesday evening.

Awards were given in 4 different categories. 3 competitors were chosen for each category.

One category was Journalistic Act of the Year. Jani Halme was chosen as the winner of the category. He collects news from newspapers and tells everyone about it on Twitter. Halme reads news from about 160 newspapers from different parts of Finland.

Clear News was also included in the Journalistic Act of the Year category. The reason was that Clear News helps immigrants integrate and learn Finnish.

Clear News congratulates the category winner Jani Halme!

## Spring for the polar bears

![Polar bears outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9511366)

Open image viewer

The polar bear mother and cub came out of the winter den. Photo: Ranua Zoo

Spring has come for the polar bears at Ranua Zoo. The hatch of the winter den was opened on Wednesday, and polar bear mother Venus went outside together with her little cub. The cub was born in November, and it left the den for the first time.

Thousands of people followed on the internet how the polar bears' day went.

## Air for tourists

![CEO Jouni Aura enjoys bottled South Ostrobothnian air.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9511780)

Open image viewer

Jouni Aura is head of a company that sells bottles of air. Photo: Olli Koski/Yle

Finland has clean air. Now air is being sold to tourists.

Last year a group of young people founded a company that puts Finnish air into bottles. The company's idea was born when the young people thought about what is good in Finland in the opinion of an Indian or a Chinese person. The answer was clean air.

Tourists can buy a bottle of air as a souvenir, which costs about 17 euros. Then the bottle has air from Ostrobothnia. If the air is from Lapland, the bottle is a little more expensive.
