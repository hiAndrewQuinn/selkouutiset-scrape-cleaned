# Thursday 25.5.2017 (radio)

President Mauno Koivisto was buried. Britain criticises the United States over a leak of information. NATO joins the coalition against ISIS. Gifts for teachers.

## President Koivisto was buried

![ President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö speaks at President Mauno Koivisto's funeral.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9632347)

President Mauno Koivisto was buried today.

Koivisto's funeral service was held in Helsinki Cathedral.

President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö spoke in the church. He said that a great Finn has left us:

\- He left, but he is still close to us with his thoughts, his ways, his values and his principles. He is present.

The funeral procession carried Koivisto's coffin from the Cathedral to Hietaniemi Cemetery. Helsinki's churches rang their bells during the funeral procession:

The funeral procession stopped in front of the Bank of Finland, the Government Palace and the Presidential Palace. There were as many as 30,000 people on the city's streets who followed the funeral procession.

According to the police, the event went calmly.

President Mauno Koivosto died at the age of 93. He was president of Finland from 1982 to 1994.

## Britain criticises the United States over a leak of information

![Two heavily armed police officers stand on a street. The men have police caps, beards and assault rifles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9632247)

Open image viewer

Police patrol with weapons in Manchester after the bomb attack. Photo: Andy Rain / EPA

Britain says that the United States has revealed secret information about the Manchester bomb attack.

Britain gave the United States secret information, such as pictures of the bomb. According to Britain, the United States revealed the information to the media, and the information was published in the media without Britain's permission.

Britain will no longer give the United States secret information about the bomb attack, says the British public broadcaster BBC.

British authorities say that preventing terrorism is difficult if secret information is revealed too early.

## NATO part of the coalition against ISIS

![NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9632170)

Open image viewer

NATO Secretary General Stoltenberg said that NATO is part of the coalition against ISIS. Photo: Armando Babai / EPA

The military alliance NATO is taking part in the coalition that is fighting the terrorist organisation ISIS in Iraq and Syria.

But NATO is not taking part in the fighting. NATO plans to increase intelligence work and training in the area.

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg said this before the NATO summit began today in Brussels.

## Teachers get gifts - a thank you is enough too

![A man at a lectern.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8024450)

Open image viewer

Teachers often get flowers and other gifts when school ends.  Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Teachers get gifts from pupils when school ends. People often buy flowers for the teacher.

Many families wonder whether they have to take the teacher a gift. We asked an etiquette trainer, who teaches good manners.

The etiquette trainer says that you do not have to take the teacher a gift. The best gift is to say thank you and have a good summer, the etiquette trainer says.

A good gift for a teacher is, for example, a card that the pupil has made themselves.

The teachers Yle interviewed agree.
