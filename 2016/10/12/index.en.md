# Wednesday 12.10.2016 (radio)

The Kela reimbursements for medicines are changing. The airspace violations have been investigated. Foreign Minister Soini considers the situation in Syria terrible. A serious terror threat was stopped in Germany.

## A change is coming to medicine reimbursements

![Old medicines to be thrown away in a collection container.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9219079)

Open image viewer

Medicines worth as much as 125 million euros a year are wasted in Finland. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

The way medicines are reimbursed will change next year. For an expensive prescription medicine, Kela will only reimburse a 1-month pack at a time. A medicine is expensive if one pack costs more than a thousand euros.

Also a new, long-term prescription medicine is at first only given from the pharmacy as a 1-month pack. This means, for example, that many old people will have to visit the pharmacy more often than now.

If the medicine suits you, you can then buy a larger amount.

The aim is to reduce the amount of medicine that is wasted.

From the beginning of next year, prescriptions are usually valid for 2 years. Now they are valid for 1 year.

## Foreign Minister Timo Soini considers the situation in Syria terrible

![Timo Soini.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9127897)

Open image viewer

Foreign Minister Timo Soini says that the killing must be stopped in Syria and that humanitarian aid must get moving to Aleppo. Photo: Petteri Paalasmaa / AOP

Foreign Minister Timo Soini says that the international community's powerlessness in Aleppo in Syria is morally terrible.

For example, the United Nations, that is, the UN, and the European Union, that is, the EU, have not found a solution to the crisis.

Soini spoke to Parliament's Foreign Affairs Committee on Wednesday. The EU is considering the situation in Syria on Monday.

## The airspace violations have been investigated

![Russian SU-27 fighter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9215834)

Open image viewer

The Finnish Air Force identified a Russian SU-27 fighter and photographed the aircraft. Photo: Defence Forces

The Border Guard has completed the preliminary investigation into the airspace violations by Russian fighter aircraft.

The investigation found that two Russian SU-27 fighters crossed Finland's territorial waters border last Thursday. The aircraft flew in Finnish airspace off Porvoo for about one minute.

Both aircraft flew about 13 kilometres in Finnish airspace.

According to the Border Guard, the weather does not explain the airspace violations, for example.

## A serious terror threat was stopped in Germany

![A police officer escorts a woman and a child from the courtyard of an apartment block.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9221241)

Open image viewer

Police evacuate residents of a building in Leipzig where there was a suspected terrorism case at the weekend. Photo: Hendrik Schmidt / EPA

The German security police have stopped a serious terror threat.

A Syrian man was planning a terror attack on one of Berlin's airports. The man was caught on Monday. The 22-year-old Jaber Albakr has asylum in Germany. The man was followed in the city of Chemnitz, but he managed to escape. 700 police officers were involved in the search.

Albakr fled to Leipzig, where he spent the weekend with two Syrians. On Sunday one of the Syrians saw a photo of Albakr on Facebook. The Syrians caught the terrorist and contacted the police.

One of the men says that they are grateful that they get to be in Germany. They could not accept that Albakr intended to do harm to Germans.
