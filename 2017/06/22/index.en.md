# Thursday 22.6.2017 (radio)

Support for the New Alternative group has been measured for the first time. Kike Elomaa switched back to the Finns Party. More people on low incomes will be freed from the Yle tax. Yle Areena is being renewed. The arctic bramble is being tamed.

## Yle has also measured support for New Alternative

![A graphic of party support](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9684965)

Open image viewer

Yle has made the first party support poll since the Finns Party split into 2 groups.

Support for the Finns Party is now 9.7 percent. The New Alternative group broke away from the party, and its support is 2.3 percent. It was now measured for the first time.

24 percent of respondents say that they might vote for the Finns Party or New Alternative.

In the poll, the most popular party is still the National Coalition Party, with support of over 21 percent. The SDP and the Centre Party are almost even; their support is about 17 percent.

## Ritva "Kike" Elomaa returned to the Finns Party

![Kike Elomaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9667807)

Open image viewer

Finns Party MP Ritva Elomaa switched to the New Alternative group after the party conference but regretted the decision. Now she has returned to the Finns Party. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

MP Ritva "Kike" Elomaa is switching back to the Finns Party parliamentary group. Elomaa told about it at a press conference in Parliament.

Elomaa switched from the Finns Party parliamentary group to the New Alternative group together with 19 other MPs. This happened only 2 days after the party conference. At the conference, Jussi Halla-aho was elected as the new leader of the Finns Party.

Elomaa's switch means that the Finns Party parliamentary group grows to 16 members. After the switch, the New Alternative parliamentary group has 19 MPs.

## More people on low incomes will be freed from the Yle tax

![The Yle Link Tower and a flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9526714)

Open image viewer

The Yle tax will be dropped for those whose yearly income is under 14,800 euros Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

More people with small incomes will be freed from the Yle tax. The lower limit of the Yle tax is now 11,100 euros. The new limit is 14,800 euros. In Finland there are perhaps 400,000 people whose yearly earnings are less than 14,800 euros.

The Yle tax for people with high incomes will rise by about 20 euros a year.

The change caused a dispute in Parliament, but an agreement was found on Thursday.

## Yle Areena is being renewed

Yle Areena is being renewed, and listening to the radio on mobile will become easier after Midsummer. From Monday, all of Yle's radio channels and other audio content will be in Areena under one service.

For example, you can choose a regional radio station from the live broadcasts menu.

You get the new version when you update the Yle Areena app.

## The wild arctic bramble is being tamed

![Arctic brambles in pots. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9684470)

Open image viewer

Growing arctic bramble has succeeded even in a pot - at least on the university roof. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

The arctic bramble has now been tamed to grow even in a pot.

The University of Eastern Finland has developed the wild berry so that it can be grown in tower pots. For now, the arctic bramble is grown on the university roof. The researchers hope that the arctic bramble can also be bred into a cultivated berry. The arctic bramble has already been grown in fields, but the results have been poor.

There is almost unlimited demand for arctic bramble in the food industry, in restaurants and in making liqueur.
