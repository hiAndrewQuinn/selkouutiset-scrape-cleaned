# Friday 22.4.2022 (radio)

Ukraine needs help. The UPM strike ended. Food is getting more expensive. Electric cars are popular. Spring progresses. The weather cools.

## Ukraine needs help

![Three people walk in the yard of an apartment block destroyed by bombing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_78/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-943602625f7c0749bff/1650424899)

Photo: Alexander Ermochenko / Reuters

The need for help is growing in Ukraine.

About 16 million Ukrainians need help in the middle of the war, estimates the United Nations, the UN.

Because of the war, Ukrainians do not have enough clean water, for example. However, aid is difficult to deliver.

The war in Ukraine has already lasted 2 months. Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

Because of the war, about 25 percent of Ukrainians have had to leave their own homes.

## The UPM strike ends

![Strike guards at the gate of a UPM factory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-944672626216b13b8a8/1650633210)

Open image viewer

The Paper Union had a long strike at UPM's mills. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The strike at the forestry company UPM's mills is ending.

The employer and employees reached an agreement on Friday. The agreement concerns, for example, paper production.

UPM and employees have had a dispute about, for example, working hours.

The strike at UPM's mills has lasted a long time. The employees stopped working at the start of the year, when the strike began. Now they return to work straight away.

## Food is getting more expensive

![A customer next to a fridge cabinet in a grocery shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5192,h_2921,x_0,y_178/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-771104601ff43689830/1612711630)

Food is getting more expensive in Finland.

The Pellervo Economic Research Institute estimates that prices will rise fast towards autumn. For example, meat, cereal products and dairy products may become more expensive.

The Pellervo Economic Research Institute estimates that food will become about 11 percent more expensive this year.

Prices are rising because energy is now expensive. Russia's war in Ukraine also affects the economy.

## Electric cars are being bought

![Tesla electric cars charging in Norway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4792,h_2696,x_0,y_217/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-862659615c0f93e8442/1730535785)

Photo: Tore Meek / EPA

Electric cars are now popular.

Many want to buy an electric car because petrol is expensive. There are so many buyers that a new electric car may have to be waited for as long as a year.

That is why many buy a used electric car. A used electric car can be obtained faster than a new one.

Used electric cars are, however, expensive, because there are many buyers. An electric car less than a year old may even cost more than a new one.

Used electric cars are brought to Finland from Germany and Sweden. They sell quickly. Many customers buy an electric car they have not even seen.

In Helsinki snow is now only in shaded places, but in Lapland snow is in places even more than half a metre deep.

## The weather cools

![Map of the day's highest temperatures, 23.4.–25.4.2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-944997626291a6065ec/1650627052)

On Saturday the weather cools slightly. Cloudiness varies, and in places there will be showers.

The temperature across a large part of the country is between 5 and 10 degrees; in the south it is warmer in places.

The temperature remains cool on Sunday too, but in places the sun shines.
