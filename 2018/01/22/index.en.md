# Monday 22.1.2018 (radio)

Advance voting in the presidential election continues to be busy. The Clear News website tells more about the candidates and the election. In Heinola, people want to move a wartime mass grave. New Year's rockets caused only a few eye injuries. In the south, the frost will change to above-zero temperatures at the end of the week.

## A wartime mass grave

![A memorial for the mass grave of the 1918 civil war in Vierumäki, Heinola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10031356)

Open image viewer

The hundred-year-old mass grave and its memorial are in the middle of a busy industrial area in Vierumäki. Photo: Juha-Petri Koponen / Yle

In Heinola, people want to move an old mass grave to a new place.

The grave is in Vierumäki, in the middle of a busy industrial area. People want to move it to the church cemetery.

The mass grave is from the time of the Finnish Civil War, so it is a hundred years old. The grave perhaps has the remains of about 300 deceased, that is, dead people. They fought in the civil war on the side of the Reds.

Moving the grave needs a permit from the Regional State Administrative Agency for Eastern Finland.

## New Year's eye injuries

![Safety glasses](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7007838)

Open image viewer

If you set off fireworks, you must wear safety glasses. Photo: Pekka Loukkola / Yle

The Finnish Safety and Chemicals Agency Tukes has happy news about the New Year celebrations. This year small children did not get a single eye injury from New Year's rockets.

Tukes says that this was the first time in the whole 2000s that children under 12 did not get eye injuries from New Year's rockets.

At the turn of the year, fireworks caused 11 eye injuries that had to be treated in hospital. Of the injured, 3 were under 18.

Of all the patients, only one had worn safety glasses.

## The weather gets milder

![Weather forecasts for the coming Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday on a map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10033969)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

There will be a big change in the weather this week. The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that between Wednesday and Thursday the temperature may change by more than 20 degrees.

Niko Tollman, a meteorologist at the Finnish Meteorological Institute, says that on Tuesday there will still be frost in the whole country. Also in the southern and central parts of the country, it may be minus 20 degrees.

On Wednesday it starts to get milder, that is, the weather gets warmer. On Thursday it may already be four or five degrees above zero in the south, meteorologist Niko Tollman says.

In the south the weather will be mild for the rest of the week, but in the north the wintry frosty weather continues.
