## Summer jobs

Municipalities are offering park worker tasks as summer jobs. Right now the most summer jobs are on offer in the social and health sector. Photo: Malin Valtonen / Yle

There are now plenty of summer jobs in Finland. Over the last few weeks more than 100 new summer jobs have come on offer every day.

For example, on the Oikotie online service, job advertisements have been read about 5 percent more than on average in recent years.

Summer jobs have come fastest to the social and health sector. Its share has already risen to more than 10 percent.

## Taxi driver shortage

![A sign on the roof of a taxi in snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5368,h_3019,x_0,y_126/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105264463a58b2d38136/1671794216)

Open image viewer

There is a shortage of drivers in the taxi sector. Photo: Ville Välimäki / Yle

In Finland there was a record number of taxi driver licences last year. Even so, there is a shortage of drivers in the taxi sector.

The Finnish Transport and Communications Agency, Traficom, says that, based on population, drivers are most needed in Uusimaa, Southwest Finland and North Ostrobothnia.

Last year, taxi driver jobs were on offer on average 450 each month.

## Mental health

![Drawing by Suvi Kirvesmäki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1224,h_683,x_0,y_382/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1038351637e2fffaea0c/1669214278)

Open image viewer

Finns' trust in the quality of mental health services has grown. Photo: Suvi Kirvesmäki

Finns trust the quality of mental health services more than they did 2 years ago. This is what the Mental Health Association says.

In January a little over half of Finns trusted the quality of mental health services. Two years earlier the figure was under 40 percent.

Nearly 40 percent of respondents, however, were of the opinion that mental health services are getting worse than before.

**Correction to this mental health news story.**

In yesterday's press release from the Mental Health Association there was an error: Finns' trust in mental health services has not improved but has weakened. In January 2023, 38 percent of Finns considered mental health services to be of good quality, when two years earlier the figure was 52 percent.

Corrected 8.3.2023 at 13.34.

## Energy drinks

![Energy drinks in cold cabinets.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5410,h_3043,x_1,y_256/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90675861f11b31d5afc/1678194777)

Open image viewer

There is a large selection of energy drinks. There is no age limit for energy drinks in law, but their use is not recommended for under-15s. Many shops restrict sales. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

Young people who drink energy drinks use intoxicants more often than other young people. They also sleep less than other young people.

This is revealed by a questionnaire study by the University of Jyväskylä.

A link was also found with young people who drank energy drinks less often than every week.

The study does not say whether the use of energy drinks causes sleep disorders. It may be that energy drinks are drunk because of sleep disorders.

About 2,400 young people aged 13–15 answered the University of Jyväskylä study.

## Thin ice

![A man breaks the edge of the ice with ice picks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11839742)

Open image viewer

The load-bearing capacity of ice is hard to estimate. If you fall into the ice, it is hard to get out of the water. In this picture the man has ice picks to help him. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

The Finnish Swimming Instruction and Life-Saving Federation reminds that spring ice is treacherous. The ice cover of the sea and lakes may deceive.

In spring it is hard to estimate how much weight the ice can bear. Even thick ice is not necessarily safe any more. There may be weak spots in the ice cover which cannot be seen.

The federation says that ice picks, that is, ice spikes, are an important safety equipment.

Preliminary information says that one person drowned in February.
