## Government negotiations

![Government formateur, National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo (second from left), held a press conference together with RKP chair Anna-Maja Henriksson (left), Finns Party chair Riikka Purra and KD chair Sari Essayah at the House of Estates in Helsinki on 9 May 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1111030645a5f3d2233b/1683644256)

The government negotiations have continued. The parties have discussed, for example, the economy and taxes.

Finns Party chair Riikka Purra says that taxes cannot be cut now. In Purra's view, Finland's economic situation is so bad that cutting taxes is not possible.

The Finns Party disagrees with the National Coalition, which is leading the government negotiations. The National Coalition's goal is that taxes are cut for everyone.

There are 4 parties in the government negotiations. Besides the National Coalition and the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats are included. The goal of the parties is to put together a new government for Finland.

Finland has got into the Eurovision final, the final competition.

The Eurovision song contest is this week in Liverpool, England.

Finland's representative Käärijä performed in the Eurovision semi-final on Tuesday.

Käärijä's performance had pace, energy and joy. Käärijä got through, and he performs in the final on Saturday.

Käärijä sings in Eurovision in Finnish. He wants to show that a performance in Finnish can also win.

## Lesser white-fronted geese

![A lesser white-fronted goose stands in a field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2518,h_1415,x_0,y_212/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1111620645b96530ecd8/1683723972)

Open image viewer

lesser white-fronted goose. Photo: Seppo Haavisto / AOP

Lesser white-fronted geese have returned to Finland. The nature foundation WWF reports this.

The lesser white-fronted goose is a really rare bird. However, in the course of a week about 60 lesser white-fronted geese have been seen in Finland.

The lesser white-fronted geese stop in North Ostrobothnia when they are on the way to Norway. Nesting by lesser white-fronted geese has failed in Finland for many years.
