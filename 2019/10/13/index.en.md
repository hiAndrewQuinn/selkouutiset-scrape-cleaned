# Sunday 13.10.2019 (radio)

Family members of Isis fighters have escaped from the Ain Issa camp. The typhoon caused deaths in Japan. Poland holds parliamentary elections. California bans the production and sale of fur.

## Kurds flee Turkey's attack

![A Kurdish family flees the Turkish attack in northern Syria.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11015309)

Kurdish families flee from the town of Ras al-Ain on Thursday. Photo: EPA

Turkey's attack on north-eastern Syria has continued.

The UN says that 130,000 people have had to leave their homes. The UN estimates that as many as 400,000 people may have to leave their homes in total.

Kurdish authorities say that hundreds of family members of Isis fighters have escaped from the Ain Issa camp in northern Syria. There are currently no guards at the camp. The camp is located near the area where Turkey is fighting Kurdish forces.

In the prisons guarded by the Kurds in Syria there are perhaps 12,000 Isis fighters in total. In the refugee camps there are just as many foreign women and children.

## Deaths in Japan's typhoon

![Floods in Nagano, Japan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5261,h_2959,x_0,y_271/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5996095da2a9724f586/1570941367)

Open image viewer

Floods in Nagano, Japan.

In Japan, at least 26 people have died and 8 are missing because of Typhoon Hagibis. News agencies say that at least 150 people have been injured.

Hagibis made landfall on the coast of Honshu, Japan's main island, on Saturday evening. Hours of pouring rain caused many floods and landslides.

Traffic in Tokyo was thrown into chaos because of the storm. All flights in the Tokyo area were cancelled, and most train services as well.

In Nagano, a dam breach sent water flooding into a residential area. The water rose in houses up to the second floor.

Now the storm is already weaker.

## Polish parliamentary election

![Jaroslaw Kaczynski.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8961014)

Open image viewer

Jarosław Kaczyński, leader of the Law and Justice party in Poland. Photo: Radek Pietruszka / EPA

Poland holds parliamentary elections on Sunday. The favourite to win the election is the Law and Justice party.

Opinion polls have shown that the nationalist Law and Justice party will get more than 40 percent of the votes. The party already has more than half of the seats in the lower house of parliament.

The opposition has criticised the party for weakening Poland's democracy and legal system. The European Union has also criticised Poland.

## California bans fur

![A woman's face is visible in the middle of fox and mink fur.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1969,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5996245da2e08052a99/1570955564)

Open image viewer

Fur sales have already been banned in many cities in California, including Los Angeles and San Francisco. In this archive picture, a model shows fox and mink fur at a trade fair in Frankfurt, Germany. Photo: Frank May / EPA

In the USA, the state of California is banning the production and sale of fur. The ban will come into force in 2023. California is the 1st US state to ban the fur business across the whole state.

Second-hand fur may still be sold in future. The law does not cover leather or stuffed animals either.

If you are caught breaking the law, you must pay a fine..
