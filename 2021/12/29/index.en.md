Corona situation. Corona hospitals. Corona pass. Labour shortage. Weather forecast.

## Corona situation

![The main building of Vaasa Central Hospital in sunshine, with snow-covered treetops in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4952,h_2786,x_0,y_170/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-775715602e76ff69c72/1637850058)

In Vaasa, the difficult corona situation is limiting visits to the hospital. Photo: Yle/Roy Fogde

Almost 2,600 new corona infections have been confirmed in Finland. The number of corona patients in hospitals is growing. There are 340 corona patients in hospitals, of whom 56 are in intensive care. There are 22 new corona deaths.

The hospitals have coped well with the corona situation over Christmas. The load on the hospitals has been spread more evenly across different areas.

However, the load on the hospitals may grow soon, because the omicron variant is spreading quickly.

## Proposal for corona hospitals

![Chief physician of the HUS readiness unit Eero Hirvensalo grabs the door handle of an intensive care unit in a hospital corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_14/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89692161cb28f7ce809/1640749988)

Open image viewer

Chief physician of the HUS readiness unit Eero Hirvensalo. Photo: Tero Valtanen / Yle

Chief physician **Eero Hirvensalo** of the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district says that Finland needs corona hospitals, where only corona patients are treated.

Hirvensalo says that if corona hospitals are set up in Finland, other hospitals will have more time to treat other patients.

Now, treating corona patients gets in the way of treating other patients, because there are many corona patients.

## Corona pass restrictions

![A person shows a phone screen with a corona certificate directly to the camera.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6131,h_3450,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89067261b3611b08d3d/1639146102)

Open image viewer

Corona pass on a phone screen. Photo: Tiina Kokko / Yle

There will be restrictions on the use of the corona pass. From Thursday, the corona pass can no longer be used in areas where the coronavirus is spreading.

For example, at sports events, theatres and museums, gathering restrictions must be followed. People cannot be let in normally with a corona pass.

The change has come as a surprise to many events and authorities.

## Labour shortage

![Sheet metal welder Nikolas Goryainov with an orbital welding machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4606,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-877787618b6eef042ec/1636531348)

Open image viewer

Sheet metal welder at a metal company. For example, metal industry companies like this have a shortage of workers. Photo: Kari Saastamoinen / Yle

The labour shortage makes it hard for many companies to operate. The labour shortage has grown during the corona situation. More companies than before now have a labour shortage. This is shown by a survey by EK, the joint organisation of companies.

EK says that it is also hard to get workers from abroad to Finland. Many European job seekers are not interested in Finland.

It is hard to come to work in Finland from outside Europe, because permits are hard to get and permit applications take a long time to process.

The weather on Thursday is cloudy. Snow falls lightly in many places. On the south coast and in Lapland, heavy snow falls at times. In the west and in southern Lapland, freezing drizzle, that is, rain that freezes, may also fall.
