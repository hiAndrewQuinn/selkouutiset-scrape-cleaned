# Sunday 15.12.2019 (radio)

The Madrid climate meeting did not reach results. At least 5 of the Finns in the al-Hol camp want to return to Finland. VR has renewed train connections and timetables. Finns may also invite Yle for coffee next year.

## Madrid climate meeting

![Climate meeting in Madrid, 12 December 2019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11115461)

The UN climate meeting was held in Madrid this time. Photo: Carlos Hidalgo / EPA

The UN climate meeting in Madrid has ended without major results.

The meeting agreed that new emission cuts will be discussed again at the next meeting next year. Plans for emissions trading also did not move forward.

Environmental organisations say that the Madrid meeting was a disaster.

## Finns at al-Hol

![Finnish women at the al-Hol camp in Syria.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11113296)

Open image viewer

Finnish women at the al-Hol camp in Syria. Photo: Antti Kuronen / Yle

At least 5 of the Finnish women in the al-Hol camp in Syria want to come back to Finland.

There are 11 Finnish women in the al-Hol camp in total, and about 30 children. The women and children are family members of Isis terrorists.

Yle has received information about 6 of the women. Five of them want to come back to Finland with their children. However, one wants to stay in Syria, because she hopes that Isis will return to power.

In Finland there is debate about whether the women and children in the camp should be helped back to Finland. Many people think the women could be a threat to Finland's security.

## New train timetables

![A train at the Joensuu station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11118479)

Open image viewer

A train at Joensuu station. Photo: AOP

The timetables of many long-distance trains have changed today.

VR started a new timetable period today. At the same time, VR brought in new train services, and some train connections became faster.

The changes were made because the popular long-distance services on the main line were often sold out.

The biggest changes are between Oulu and Helsinki. For example, the travel time between Oulu and Helsinki gets shorter, because there is no longer a need to change trains. New commuter train connections are also introduced in the Tampere region and in Kymenlaakso. However, cross-country traffic, that is, train connections west from Kuopio, will decrease.

Yle people had coffee at the Helsinki Deaf Association.

Finns can also invite Yle for coffee next year.

The joint coffee moments between Finns and Yle were started this year, and they have been very popular.

Coffee moments have been held in different parts of the country about 150 times this year. About 1,500 Finns and more than 200 Yle employees have taken part. Coffee invitations have come from families, work and hobby groups, and various associations, for example.

During the coffee moments, people can give Yle "risuja ja ruusuja", that is, thorns and roses: they can say what is bad or good at Yle and in its programmes. At the same time, they can ask questions and make suggestions on how Yle can improve its service.
