# Saturday 7.5.2022 (radio)

Fire in Jyväskylä. Finland's NATO decision. Shortage of interpreters. Afghan women. Mother's Day. The weather.

## Fire in Jyväskylä

![Fire at the Rautpohja paper mill in Jyväskylä. The picture was taken from Vesanganti road, from which the smoke from the burning factory could be seen farther away.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12435721)

A lot of smoke from the fire rose into the sky over Jyväskylä. Photo: Toni Peltonen / Lehtikuva

A big fire was put out in Jyväskylä on Saturday. The fire started at a factory in Rautpohja, Jyväskylä.

The rescue service took the workers away from the factory area, and only 1 person was injured. The person was not seriously hurt.

Smoke spread from the fire into the surroundings. That is why the authorities asked the residents of the nearby area to stay indoors.

The fire perhaps started from testing a new paper machine. The machine is being built right now at the Rautpohja factory.

## Finland's NATO decision

![President Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_169/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-93561962446d4511481/1648655835)

Open image viewer

President Niinistö will give his own opinion on NATO perhaps next Thursday. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

It will become clear in the near future whether Finland applies for membership of the military alliance NATO.

President Sauli Niinistö will probably say on Thursday whether he supports NATO membership.

The SDP, the party of Prime Minister Sanna Marin, will give its opinion next Saturday. After that, it is still awaited what Parliament decides on the matter.

Finland is considering NATO membership because Russia is waging war in Ukraine. In many people's opinion, Finland's own security will improve if Finland becomes a NATO member.

## Shortage of interpreters

![A pinboard with different posters. One has a chart of applying for asylum in Finland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_423/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-924924622626a4c4ca0/1646668328)

Open image viewer

An asylum seeker needs a good interpreter. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

In Finland there are too few skilled interpreters. For example, it is important for asylum seekers that they get help from good interpreters.

Tendering of interpreters also brings problems. When interpreters compete for work, the work often goes to the cheapest interpreter. The organisation of interpreters says that the cheapest interpreter is not always the best.

In Finland, Ukrainian language interpreters are especially needed now, because many Ukrainian refugees are coming to Finland.

## Afghan women

![Three women dressed in burqas at a refugee camp in Islamabad.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_98/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-843920611e41614d087/1629374976)

Open image viewer

Afghan women now have to wear a burqa. Photo: Sohail Shahzad / AOP

The position of women is getting worse in Afghanistan.

The leader of the Taliban movement has ordered that women must wear a burqa. The burqa also covers the face.

Nowadays it is also hard for women and girls to get to school and study in Afghanistan. The Taliban movement wants women to stay at home.

The Taliban movement has governed Afghanistan since last August. That was when the international troops left Afghanistan.

## Mother's Day

![First class medals of the Finnish White Rose to be given on Mother's Day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1346,h_757,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6729155eb55579ebfe8/1588942289)

Open image viewer

This year 30 mothers will receive a medal. Photo: Samuli Miettinen / MLL

Mother's Day is celebrated in Finland on Sunday. Mother's Day is always on the second Sunday of May.

On Mother's Day, mothers who are good raisers of children are honoured. This year 30 mothers will receive a medal.

President Sauli Niinistö will give the medals to the mothers at the Mother's Day celebration in Helsinki.
