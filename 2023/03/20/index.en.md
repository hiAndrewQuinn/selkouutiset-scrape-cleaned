# Monday 20.3.2023 (radio)

Train strike. Happy Finland. English-language teaching. The spring equinox. The weather.

## Train strike

![Empty railway station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689937)

Open image viewer

It has been quiet at the train stations because of the strike. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

The train drivers' strike has begun. The train drivers stopped work on Monday.

Because of the strike, train traffic has stopped. Long-distance trains and commuter trains are not running. VR is cancelling trains one day at a time, as long as the strike continues.

The train drivers stopped work because they have a dispute with their employer, for example about one week's free days. The train drivers say that there is often less rest time between shifts than in office work.

The train drivers will return to work only when the disputes are settled.

The train strike also affects other traffic. There are many passengers on buses, and some long-distance buses have been full. In addition, Finnair has flown some domestic flights with bigger aircraft than usual.

## Happy Finland

![Two people sit outside on a bench, next to them two adults and one child playing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689938)

Open image viewer

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finland is once again the happiest country in the world. This is the 6th time in a row that Finland is number one in the happiness ranking.

World Happiness Report 2023 was published on Monday. In it, all the Nordic countries are among the 10 happiest countries in the world.

In recent years there have been many crises in the world. After the corona pandemic, the war in Ukraine began. Then inflation began, that is, prices rose.

However, the crises have not reduced people's positive feelings in the world. On the contrary, people have started to help others.

## English-language teaching

![People in a teaching room.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689939)

Open image viewer

Universities often teach in English. Photo: Katja Halinen / Yle

Many Finnish students cannot use the terms of their own field in Finnish. The reason is that in many fields English-language teaching has increased.

This is what linguist Janne Saarikivi says. He has studied the position of Finnish and Swedish at universities.

Saarikivi says that English-language teaching has increased a lot in the last 10 years. In addition, many students now write, for example, their master's thesis in English.

## The spring equinox

![Spring winter day on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689940)

Open image viewer

The daylight time is increasing every day now. Photo: Jouni Tanninen / Yle

Today, Monday, is the spring equinox. That means the day and the night are equally long today.

Earlier in the winter the night was longer than the day. Now the daylight time is getting longer quickly, about 5–10 minutes per day. Daylight time keeps getting longer until the end of June.
