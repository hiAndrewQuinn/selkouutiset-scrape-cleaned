## Missile strike in Ukraine

![The missile has destroyed an apartment block.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12520359)

Open image viewer

An air strike that hit this apartment block killed 16 people in the town of Serhiivka, about 80 kilometres from Odesa.  Photo: Maksym Voitenko / AOP

Russia has fired more missiles at civilian targets in Ukraine. Civilian targets have ordinary people who are not soldiers.

On Friday Russia fired missiles at the Odesa region. The missiles hit an apartment block and a leisure centre. About 20 people died in the strike. There are many injured.

Earlier this week Russia fired missiles at the city of Kremenchuk. The missiles hit a shopping centre, where about 20 people died.

Russia started the war in Ukraine in February, so the war has continued for over 4 months.

Rising prices continue.

Prices rose in the euro area by 8.6 percent from last year in June. The countries belonging to the euro area are countries whose money is the euro.

Prices are rising because of the war in Ukraine. Especially the price of energy has risen, after Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

## Tampere's drug problem

![Drug syringes on the ground on a granite portal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4364,h_2454,x_0,y_297/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97667262bda5f8cb0de/1661945561)

Open image viewer

Drug syringes can be seen on Tampere's streets. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

Tampere has a serious drug problem. Drugs are used so much that the police do not have time to monitor drug trade.

The police say that many more drug dealers have come. Nowadays anyone can order drugs from the internet and then sell them on the street.

Many drug users seek help from substance abuse services. Several hundred new clients with problems with drugs have come to Tampere's substance abuse services.

Many events are held in Finland at the weekend. Finns go a lot to summer festivals.

## Summer gatherings

![thousands of motorhomes](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9702117)

Open image viewer

There are always many people at summer gatherings. The picture is from the 2017 summer gatherings. Photo: Tapio Termonen / Yle

Religious summer celebrations are also part of the Finnish summer.

This weekend, for example, the Summer gatherings are held. It is a summer celebration of the Laestadians. The Laestadians are one of the revival movements of the Finnish church.

The Laestadian revival movement is strong especially in Northern Finland. This summer the Laestadians gather in Lopi.

The Summer gatherings are a big event. This year about 76,000 people will come there.

Saturday is a hot day. The temperature is over 30 degrees in a large part of the country. Thunder showers come on the west coast and in Lapland.
