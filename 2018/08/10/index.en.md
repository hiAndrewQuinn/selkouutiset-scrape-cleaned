# Friday 10.8.2018 (radio)

The Finnish Red Cross is sending help to the Lombok earthquake area. Ticks have spread fewer diseases this summer than last summer. The SDP is the biggest party in Yle's support poll. The weather gets cooler.

## The Finnish Red Cross helps in Indonesia

![Aid workers in the ruins of a collapsed mosque on Lombok.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10344312)

The earthquake destroyed buildings on the island of Lombok in Indonesia. Photo: Adi Weda / EPA

Help is being sent from Finland to Indonesia. The Finnish Red Cross, SPR, is sending help to the Lombok earthquake area.

There was an earthquake on the island of Lombok last Sunday. The earthquake destroyed houses, and many residents of Lombok are without a home. They need help soon, because they have no food or place to sleep.

More than 300 people died in the earthquake in Indonesia.

## Ticks have spread few diseases

![A tick walks on a hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10331736)

Open image viewer

A tick is so small that it is hard for a person to see it. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

Here in Finland ticks have spread fewer diseases this summer than last summer. There has been little Lyme disease and tick-borne encephalitis this summer.

Experts do not believe that there are fewer ticks. The ticks just have not been active, because the summer has been dry and hot.

A tick is a small insect. It is a common animal in Finland.

A tick is really small, only 2 or 3 millimetres long. That is why it is hard for a person to see a tick.

A tick lives in hay and grass. It does not fly, but it can climb onto a person's skin. On the skin the tick sucks blood, and that is why it can spread diseases.

## The SDP is the biggest

![Party support graph 8/2018](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10344650)

Open image viewer

The SDP, the National Coalition Party and the Centre Party get the most support in the new poll. Photo: Yle News Graphics

Yle has again asked Finns which party they support.

The SDP, the Social Democrats, gets the most support. A little over 21 percent of the people who answered the poll supported the Social Democrats.

The SDP is an opposition party, which means it is not in the government. The big government parties, the National Coalition Party and the Centre Party, get less support than the SDP, although their support has grown.

Support for the National Coalition Party is now almost 20 percent, and support for the Centre Party is almost 18 percent.

Finland has elections next year. Then the SDP, the National Coalition Party and the Centre Party will compete over which party is the biggest and gets the post of prime minister.

At the same time as support for the SDP has risen, support for the other opposition parties has fallen. Support for the Greens, the Left Alliance and the Finns Party is now smaller than in the previous poll.

The exact figures of the poll can be found on Yle's website.

## The temperature falls

![A sunbather on Solvik beach in Hämeenlinna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10338283)

The weather gets cooler. Friday was perhaps the last day of the summer when the temperature was about 30 degrees.

At the weekend there are thunderstorms, and the wind is strong. The temperature falls to about 20 degrees.
