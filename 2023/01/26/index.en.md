## Mortality and birth rate

![Dozens of memorial candles lit on the steps of the Muualle graves at Hietaniemi cemetery.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2541,h_1429,x_0,y_115/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8766106186a89511f8a/1636216540)

In Finland mortality has risen and the birth rate has fallen. Photo: Ilkka Klemola / Yle

The number of deaths has risen in Finland. Statistics Finland says that almost 63,000 people died in Finland last year. That is over 5,000 more than the year before last.

The number of deaths was the highest since the war years.

The corona pandemic is behind this, and it also lowered life expectancy. Life expectancy describes the age at which people on average die.

Fewer children were born in Finland last year than in 150 years. Just under 45,000 children were born. That is over 4,600 fewer than the year before last.

## School health

![A schoolchild is given an eye test as part of the school health check.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2268,x_0,y_94/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-94071462540feb9cd6b/1708511862)

Open image viewer

A schoolchild is given an eye test at the health check. Photo: Mari Kahila / Yle

School health checks are often missed because of a shortage of doctors. Thousands of children and young people do not get to a health check.

For example, in Tampere many doctors were moved to other duties during the corona era. The doctors traced corona cases or gave corona vaccinations.

Only about 50 percent of the city's schoolchildren get the health check for their age group.

There are also too few doctors in health centres. About 300 more doctor jobs are needed.

## Joint application

![Main entrance of the Academy of Fine Arts at the University of the Arts.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8083,h_4537,x_0,y_168/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90377761e8043cb6759/1642595483)

Main entrance of the Academy of Fine Arts at the University of the Arts. Photo: Tuomas Uusheimo

There have been many applicants in the first joint university application of the spring. The number has almost doubled from last year.

About 62,000 applicants are applying for foreign-language degree programmes at universities and for the University of the Arts.

The National Agency for Education says that the record number of applicants is explained by the easing of the international application process.

The most popular programmes can be viewed at yle.fi.

## Kone cuts jobs

![Logo of the lift and escalator company Kone Oyj on a wall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3721,h_2093,x_0,y_266/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98424462d7d7cd14db3/1658312751)

Open image viewer

Kone is a successful and famous Finnish lift manufacturer. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The Finnish lift company Kone is planning to cut about 1,000 jobs.

150 jobs may leave Finland.

Kone says that the cuts are part of renewing its operating model. The company wants savings of about 100 million euros a year.

Kone makes lifts, escalators and automatic doors.

## Trump and social media

![Donald Trump's Twitter account in the foreground, with several Twitter logos in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2790,h_1569,x_149,y_760/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8616366157e365128eb/1633149927)

The Twitter account of the former President of the USA, Donald Trump, was suspended in January 2021. Twitter feared that Trump would use the account to incite violence. Now Trump is back on Twitter and will soon also get onto Facebook and Instagram. Photo: Rafael Henrique/SOPA Images/Shutterstock/All Over Press

The former US President Donald Trump is getting back onto social media.

Meta, the parent company of the services, has announced that it will restore Trump's accounts on Facebook and Instagram.

Already last November Trump's Twitter account was restored.

Trump was removed from many social media services 2 years ago. At that time Trump's supporters attacked the US Capitol building.

Trump has announced that he is seeking to become president in the 2024 elections.
