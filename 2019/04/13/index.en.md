# Saturday 13.4.2019 (radio)

On Sunday it is election day for the parliamentary elections. There are also many voters with an immigrant background in the parliamentary elections. A Finnair plane made an emergency landing in Stockholm. The lake ice is already weak.

On Sunday it is time to choose whom to vote for. Photo: Tero Kyllönen / Yle

On Sunday it is the election day of the parliamentary elections. There are about 2,500 candidates in the elections in total. Of them, 200 get into Parliament.

Voting places are open on Sunday from 9 to 20. On election day everyone must vote at the place that is written on the voting notice. The notice came to your home by post.

Voters must have an identity document with them, for example a passport, an identity card or a driving licence.

Yle reports on Sunday evening how the elections went. You can follow the elections on television, radio and online.

Before that you can still watch Yle's election debates at yle.fi/vaalit. For example, the plain-language election debate is helpful for everyone who is still thinking about which party to vote for.

## Voters with an immigrant background

![Young immigrants at the Itäkeskus shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10694132)

Open image viewer

Many Finns with an immigrant background have the right to vote. Photo: Milla Vahtila / Yle

There are many voters with an immigrant background in the parliamentary elections.

About 100,000 people with an immigrant background can vote in these elections. About 7,000 Finns with an immigrant background may vote for the first time now.

Only Finnish citizens may vote in parliamentary elections.

## Emergency landing in Stockholm

![Finnair Airbus A321 taking off from Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8769797)

Photo: Markku Ojala / AOP

A Finnair plane has made an emergency landing. The plane made an emergency landing in Stockholm on Saturday morning.

A fault was noticed on the plane when it was on its way from Helsinki to Manchester in England. The fault was in the pressurisation. That is why the plane landed in Stockholm already.

The plane landed normally. Passengers were not in danger, and nobody was injured.

Finnair sent another plane from Helsinki to Stockholm, and the passengers were able to continue their journey.

## Weak ice

![Melting ice surface at Vierumäki in Heinola on 29 March. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10737639)

Open image viewer

Lake ice is already weak. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Many lakes are still frozen, but the ice is already weak.

In Kuopio an unusual accident happened on Saturday. A dog fell through the ice into the lake at the shore of its own home. The woman owner of the dog set off to save the dog and fell into the ice herself.

Next the woman's husband set off to save his wife. He also fell into the ice.

The man, however, got the dog, his wife and finally himself up out of the lake. All's well that ends well.
