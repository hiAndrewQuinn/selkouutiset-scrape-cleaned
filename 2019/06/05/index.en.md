# Wednesday 5.6.2019 (radio)

The world's schools made a climate agreement. Drink-driving enforcement begins. There has been a new sentence in the Oulu sex crimes. Jussi Halla-aho wants to stay on as leader of the Finns Party. About 200 wolves live in Finland. Helsinki's beaches are to be cleaned by volunteers.

## Schoolchildren fight climate change

![Schoolchildren at the world climate meeting listened at the end of May in North Karelia to instructions on how to plant tree seedlings.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10815621)

Schoolchildren from around the world listened to instructions on how to plant tree seedlings. Photo: Korpipaja/ ENO Schoolnet

The world's schoolchildren's climate meeting has ended.

At the end of the meeting, the world's schools' climate agreement was published. In the agreement, the schools promise to plant 100 million tree seedlings in different parts of the world. This is how climate change will be fought.

The climate meeting was held in Helsinki and in Liperi in North Karelia.

135 pupils and 100 teachers took part in the meeting. They came from 70 different countries.

## Police monitor drink drivers

![A police officer with a breathalyser in hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10186923)

Open image viewer

The police are looking for drink drivers with breath tests. Photo: Mikael Kokkola / Yle

Drink drivers are the target of police enforcement for the rest of the week.

The enforcement will begin tomorrow, on Road Sobriety Day. The police will then carry out many breath tests on motorists. The breath tests will continue for the rest of the week.

With breath tests, the police find out whether a person has drunk alcohol. At the same time, the police keep an eye out for drug drivers on the roads.

Because of drink drivers, dozens of people die in Finland every year and hundreds are injured.

## New sentence in the Oulu sex crimes

![The Oulu courthouse.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10698386)

Open image viewer

The investigation of the sex crimes continues at the Oulu District Court. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

A third sentence has been handed down in the Oulu sex crime cases.

The District Court sentenced a 23-year-old man to 3 years in prison. The man was sentenced for aggravated sexual abuse of a 13-year-old girl.

Two men have already been sentenced for abusing and raping the same girl. One of them received a four-year prison sentence and the other a two-year prison sentence.

All the convicted men have foreign backgrounds.

Halla-aho has led the Finns Party for 2 years.

## About 200 wolves live in Finland

![Infographic / map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10815564)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mikko Airikka / Yle

The number of wolves in Finland has grown.

About 200 wolves may now live in Finland. That is 10 per cent more than a year ago.

About 11 wolf packs may now live in Eastern Finland, when there were 8 packs before. In Western Finland there used to be 16 wolf packs, but now there are 13.

## Helsinki's beaches cleaned by volunteers

![Plastic jar on the beach](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9598956)

Open image viewer

You can pick up rubbish on the beach and take it to a bin. Photo: Petra Ristola / Yle

Today the World Environment Day has been celebrated. In Helsinki the day is being marked by starting volunteer clean-up work. The aim is to clear the rubbish from Helsinki's beaches.

Helsinki has 130 kilometres of coastline. The hope is that Helsinki residents will be inspired to move around the beaches and at the same time pick up rubbish. City dwellers can borrow rubbish tongs from the libraries for the clean-up.
