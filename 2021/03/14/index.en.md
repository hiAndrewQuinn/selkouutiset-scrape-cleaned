A record number of new coronavirus infections has been reported in Finland.

Today 863 infections have been reported. This is the largest number of infections reported in Finland in a single day so far.

More than half of the new infections are in the Helsinki region.

## Helsinki city centre has gone quiet

![Nursing student Johanna Lind was photographed at Narinkka Square in March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-784062604cba04d997d/1615641985)

Open image viewer

Nursing student Johanna Lind on the deserted Narinkka Square. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The coronavirus pandemic has made the inner city of Helsinki quiet.

In the inner city, that is, around the railway station, Kamppi and Esplanade, far fewer people are moving around now than a year ago.

According to statistics compiled from Telia's mobile phone network, in the first week of March fewer than 300,000 people spent time in the inner city. Last year, about 800,000 people spent time in the area at the same time.

## Kaija Saariaho to be awarded

![Composer Kaija Saariaho, Finnish National Opera, 07.04.2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3076,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-7956958e7eec297533/1491595063)

Open image viewer

Composer Kaija Saariaho. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

Finnish composer Kaija Saariaho is to receive a significant international prize. The Venice Biennale is awarding Saariaho's life's work with the Golden Lion prize.

The prize jury says that Kaija Saariaho is one of the most important living composers.

The prize will be handed over in September, when a contemporary music festival is held in Venice. The festival programme also includes Saariaho's work Oltra Mar, which the prize jury considers a masterpiece.

## Oil spill at Kilpilahti

![Oil spill at Kilpilahti 13032021](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11836690)

The oil spill at Kilpilahti was noticed on Saturday. Photo: East Uusimaa Rescue Department

In Porvoo, at the port of Kilpilahti, oil is being collected from the sea.

The oil spill was noticed yesterday, when the cargo of an oil tanker was being unloaded at the port.

The source of the oil spill is still being sought. It is not yet known how much oil has got into the sea.

## Winter feeding of birds

![A house sparrow and two tree sparrows eat nuts and sunflower seeds from mesh bags hanging on a wall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11835256)

Open image viewer

For example, nuts and seeds can be offered to birds in winter. Photo: Anniina Wallius / Yle

Winter feeding of birds has decreased.

Winter bird monitoring has counted birds and their feeding places for several decades now. The monitoring has found that there are now perhaps about 30 percent fewer feeding places than in the 1980s.

Finland has a long tradition of feeding birds in the winter months. Researchers wonder why this tradition is now fading.

The Finnish Museum of Natural History, Luomus, is investigating the reasons for the decrease in winter feeding with a questionnaire survey. You can answer the survey on the Luomus website.
