## Party support

![Party support in July 2022](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1622,h_912,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97881562c58e69233c1/1657124894)

The National Coalition Party is the most popular party. Photo: Yle

Yle has again asked Finns which party they support.

The most popular party is the National Coalition Party. Almost 24 percent of the survey respondents support the National Coalition Party. The National Coalition Party is an opposition party, that is, it is not in government. The support for the National Coalition Party has fallen slightly since June.

The second most popular party is the SDP. Almost 20 percent of the survey respondents support the SDP. The SDP is the prime minister's party, that is, it is in government and the prime minister is from the SDP. The support for the SDP has risen slightly since June.

The third most popular party is the Finns Party, whose support is 15 percent.

The support for the Centre Party is a little under 12 percent, for the Greens a little under 10 percent and for the Left Alliance a little under 8 percent. The Centre, the Greens and the Left Alliance are government parties.

## Border Guard Act

![Border marker on Finland's national border.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4011,h_2256,x_0,y_242/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-954406627defb69d4ef/1652428016)

Open image viewer

Border marker on Finland's border. Photo: Heikki Haapalainen / Yle

Parliament has approved the new Border Guard Act.

The Border Guard Act will give broader possibilities to restrict border traffic in exceptional situations than before. If, for example, Russia sends many asylum seekers to Finland's border, Finland can close the border.

There were many different opinions in Parliament about the new Border Guard Act. In the end, however, Parliament approved the new law unanimously, that is, without a vote.

## British government crisis

![Boris Johnson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1679,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97908962c69adc4ca99/1657182978)

Open image viewer

British Prime Minister Boris Johnson resigns. Photo: Tejas Sandhu / AOP

There is a crisis in the British government. Prime Minister Boris Johnson announced on Thursday afternoon that he will resign as leader of the Conservative Party and from the post of prime minister. The reason is that many ministers and other people in the British administration are dissatisfied with Johnson.

Johnson says, however, that he wants to remain prime minister until a successor has been chosen in the party.

The new British prime minister will be appointed in the autumn, by October at the latest. It is not yet known whether Johnson may remain prime minister until then.

## Corona situation

![Statistical graphics of corona patients who were in hospital care and intensive care.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97915262c6aa456f24c/1657189862)

Open image viewer

The number of corona patients has started to grow again. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Finland's corona situation has turned slightly worse again. The Institute for Health and Welfare THL says that the number of corona patients in hospitals has started to grow again.

On Thursday during the day there were 475 corona patients in hospital care. There were 8 corona patients in intensive care.

During the spring the number of corona patients fell. Now the burden on hospital care has turned for the worse.
