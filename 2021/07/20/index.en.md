Many homes are being built in Finland now.

## The condition of inland waters

![A lake floods the shores.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2948,h_1658,x_0,y_129/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81174160af43d70fcc8/1622099048)

Open image viewer

Climate warming also affects lakes and rivers. Photo: Virpi Kukkonen

Finland's inland waters, that is, the lakes and rivers, are in good condition, says a comparison of EU countries.

The Finnish Environment Institute says that climate change nevertheless creates risks.

The ecosystem of the waters is sensitive to rising temperatures. Warming can cause invasive species to spread into Finnish waters.

The stocks of cold-water fish species can also suffer. These species include, for example, salmon and trout, and vendace.

## Snus

![Snus tins](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7219865f6a02afac792/1600783037)

Open image viewer

Snus tins. Photo: Juuso Stoor / Yle

Customs has uncovered a large spread of snus in the Turku area.

The suspect in the sale of snus was caught in May in a home search. The suspect got the snus mainly from other people in Finland.

According to Customs, more than 300 kilos of snus have been spread during the year. There have been at least 150 buyers.

The investigation title of the case is aggravated tax fraud.

Buying snus in Finland is banned by law.

## Biogas in heavy traffic

![A green tank contains biogas.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5771,h_3246,x_0,y_298/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7478935fc4c401d2551/1606730999)

Open image viewer

Biogas is produced in this tank. Photo: Pasi Takkunen / Yle

Biogas is used mainly in heavy traffic. Biogas is used more and more in lorries and buses.

The food company Valio and the energy company St1 are starting to produce biogas together. It is made into a liquid from the manure of animals on dairy farms.

With manure-based biogas, the aim is to reduce emissions from traffic and milk production, that is, environmental harms.

Valio and St1 are founding a joint company. It could produce perhaps a third of the traffic biogas at the turn of the decade.

The aim is that Valio's vehicles use biogas and that dairy farms get extra income.

A lorry can drive on biogas for about 1,000 kilometres.

Biogas is not directly suitable for passenger cars that run on gas.

## Sweden's immigration laws

![Stockholm's Sergel square, with few people, on a summer evening](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_155/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83325060f5346f7e7f9/1626682543)

Open image viewer

The new legislation that comes into force today, Tuesday, affects all permit matters handled by the Swedish Migration Agency. Photo: Daniel Ivarsson

Sweden's immigration laws became stricter today. Sweden will mainly start granting temporary residence permits.

Only quota refugees are directly granted a permanent residence permit, and even their conditions are being tightened.

You can apply for a permanent residence permit after 3 years. Later, a requirement to know the Swedish language and society will be added to the conditions.

The right-wing parties voted against. They criticised the easing of the law. It is possible to stay in Sweden if there is a particularly weighty humanitarian reason.
