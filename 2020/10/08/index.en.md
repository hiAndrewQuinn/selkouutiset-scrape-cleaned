# Thursday 8.10.2020 (radio)

There are many new corona infections in Finland. There have been no big changes in party support. The American Louise Glück receives the Nobel Prize in Literature. The weather is fairly warm.

## Corona infections

There are many new corona infections in Finland. On Thursday almost 300 new corona infections were reported.

The total number of corona infections in Finland is already over 11,000.

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health estimates that Finland is now in the second phase of the corona epidemic. The first phase of the corona epidemic started in the spring.

The coronavirus is now spreading, for example, in educational institutions and day-care centres. The coronavirus has also spread in restaurants, and that is why restaurants may no longer be open at night.

## Party support

![Party support 7.9.–6.10.2020](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11582651)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

There have been no big changes in the support for political parties.

Yle's new survey shows that the SDP is still the biggest party in Finland. About 22 percent of the survey respondents support the SDP. The SDP is the party of Prime Minister Sanna Marin.

The second biggest governing party is now the Centre. The support for the Centre is a little under 12 percent. The support for the Centre has not risen, even though the party got a new chairperson in September, Annika Saarikko.

The support for the opposition parties has not changed much either. The opposition parties are not part of the government.

The biggest opposition party is the Finns Party. The support for the Finns Party is 19 percent. The support for the National Coalition, another opposition party, is a little under 18 percent.

The support figures for all parties can be found on Yle's website. The address is yle.fi.

## Literature Nobel

![Louise Glück and Barack Obama in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11585257)

Open image viewer

Nobel laureate Louise Glück. Photo: AOP

The winner of the Nobel Prize in Literature has been chosen. The Nobel Prize winner is the American Louise Glück.

Glück is a 77-year-old writer. She was born in New York in 1943

Glück writes poems. Glück's poems tell about very different things, for example traumas and nature.

The Swedish Academy announced the winner of the Nobel Prize in Literature on Thursday in Stockholm. The value of the prize is almost one million euros.

The warm weather continues in Finland. The temperature is higher than usual for October.

In Southern and Central Finland the temperature is between 10 and 15 degrees, in Northern Finland it is colder.

The weather is also humid. It rains in different parts of Finland.
