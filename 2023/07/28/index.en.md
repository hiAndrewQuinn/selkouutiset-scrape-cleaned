## Minister Rydman

![Finns Party members Vilhelm Junnila, Riikka Purra and Wille Rydman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1599,h_899,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114897064c3c108c777b/1690558149)

Finns Party politicians Vilhelm Junnila, Riikka Purra and Wille Rydman have drawn attention with old racist writings. Photo: Lehtikuva, Yle

The discussion about the racist writings of Finns Party ministers continues. Now in Finland people are talking about Minister Wille Rydman.

Helsingin Sanomat has published Rydman's old messages to a former girlfriend. Rydman's messages are racist. The messages insult Somalis and Arabs, for example.

The chair of the National Coalition Party and the Prime Minister of the government, Petteri Orpo, says that Rydman's messages are not appropriate. However, Orpo is pleased that Rydman promises to take part in the government's work against racism.

Helsingin Sanomat published Rydman's private messages because Minister Rydman has power and an important position in Finland. That is why people must be able to find out what Rydman thinks.

Rydman became a minister after Vilhelm Junnila of the Finns Party. Junnila had to resign, because Junnila's old Nazi speeches shocked many people.

Most of the public discussion has been about the old racist writings of the Finns Party chair, Minister Riikka Purra.

## Children's swimming skills

![Swimming pupils in the pool of a swimming stadium, with teacher Tanja Kautiainen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_12/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96830862a323cd21a1c/1654860315)

Open image viewer

Swimming lessons are important for children. Photo: Mårten Lampén

Finnish children's swimming skills are now worse than before. This is what the Finnish Swimming Instruction and Lifesaving Association says.

Children's swimming skills got worse during the corona time. At that time many swimming schools had a break.

Still in 2016, about 75 percent of sixth-graders could swim. In 2022, only 55 percent of sixth-graders could swim.

Swimming teachers remind us that not all families go to the swimming hall. That is why swimming lessons at school are important.

## The midnight sun

![A landscape where the sun shines close to the horizon over a river bay on a summer night. The trees have leaves and the grass is green.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_169/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6955465efd6c4f93d27/1684138947)

Open image viewer

The sun shines in Lapland even on summer nights. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

The midnight sun has ended in Utsjoki. Utsjoki is the northernmost municipality in Finland.

During the midnight sun, the sun does not set at all. The sun stays in the sky the whole time.

On Friday the sun set in Utsjoki for the first time after the midnight sun. The midnight sun began in Utsjoki in May, so it lasted more than 2 months.

## Kaleva Games

![Kaleva Games.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_351/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114907564c3df8655e4a/1690558369)

There is a large audience at the Kaleva Games. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

The Kaleva Games are held this weekend. The Kaleva Games are the Finnish championship competitions in athletics.

The first Kaleva Games were held in 1907. This year the athletes compete in Lahti.

The Kaleva Games can be watched on Yle Areena and TV2.
