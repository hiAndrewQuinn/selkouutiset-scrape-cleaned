# Sunday 19.1.2020 (radio)

There are many disputes about flight delays. Taxi fares have risen. Nightwish is releasing a new album. A cold record was set in Utsjoki. The weather.

## Flight delays and parking

Many customers make a complaint to the Consumer Disputes Board.

A customer can make a complaint if they think something went wrong with a purchase.

The seller or company may, however, disagree about the matter. The Consumer Disputes Board then decides the matter.

Customers complain most about wanting compensation if their plane has been delayed.

Many disputes also arise about car parking charges.

The Consumer Disputes Board receives more and more complaints every year.

## Taxi fares have gone up

![taxi, taxis, taxis](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_51/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5733905d1357421c301/1575478025)

Open image viewer

Taxi fares have gone up. Photo: Antti Lähteenmäki / YLE

A new taxi law has been in force in Finland for some time. That means that almost anyone can become a taxi driver.

Now it has come out that there are many new taxi drivers in the big cities.

Taxi fares have gone up. If a taxi journey cost 30 euros before the reform, now it costs almost 34 euros.

Originally the plan was that taxi fares would fall when the new law came into force.

Now the Finnish government is investigating the taxi law. The law may perhaps be changed, because it has some problems.

## A cold record was measured in Utsjoki

![View from Utsjoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11165199)

Open image viewer

In Utsjoki it was minus 37.9 degrees last night. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

It was really cold last night in northern Finland.

In Utsjoki, Finland's northernmost municipality, it was minus 37.9 degrees last night. That is this winter's cold record.

The temperature difference between northern and southern Finland is large. In southern Finland it was 5 degrees above zero at night.

The weather will continue in the same way.

Tomorrow, Monday, is a rainy and windy day.

After Monday, on Tuesday, it will be sunny.
