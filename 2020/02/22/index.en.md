# Saturday 22.2.2020 (radio)

Finland will take in asylum seekers from the Mediterranean area. More and more young people live alone. A partial ceasefire has started in Afghanistan. The mild weather makes trawling possible. Avalanches may happen in Lapland.

## Finland takes in asylum seekers from the Mediterranean

![Afghan mother and child at a refugee camp on Lesbos in Greece.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11222943)

Open image viewer

Afghan mother and child at a refugee camp on Lesbos in Greece. Photo: Dimitris Tosidis / EPA

Finland will take in asylum seekers from the Mediterranean area. The government has agreed that Finland will take in no more than 175 asylum seekers.

Finland will take in children and single-parent families who have left Syria, Afghanistan or other crisis countries.

There are many asylum seekers in camps in Greece, Cyprus, Malta and Italy.

## More and more young people live alone

![K-Supermarket in Seinäjoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11087177)

Open image viewer

More and more Finns live alone. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Many Finns do not have a partner. The number of people without a partner has grown a lot in 10 years. Yle found out that there are now 20 percent more 30-year-old Finns living alone than 10 years ago.

Researchers say that the situation also affects the fact that the birth rate has fallen in Finland. Fewer children are born when young people live alone.

In Finland there is worry that fewer children are being born than before.

## A partial ceasefire started in Afghanistan

![Rebels handing over weapons.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11222185)

Open image viewer

Taliban soldiers hand over weapons in October 2018. Photo: Saifurahman Safi / AOP

A partial ceasefire has started in Afghanistan. The USA and the extremist group Taliban announced on Friday that they will start reducing violence from Saturday.

The USA and the Taliban say that in a week, next Saturday, they will sign an agreement on peace talks.

The USA has been negotiating with the Taliban for over a year. The Taliban want the USA to withdraw its soldiers from Afghanistan.

## Fishing on Lake Päijänne like in summer

![Trawling for vendace on Lake Päijänne in February 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6453865e4f93822ef8a/1582273526)

Open image viewer

Trawling for vendace on Lake Päijänne. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

The mild, that is, warm, winter is changing fishing. On Lake Päijänne, fishermen are catching vendace with a trawl. Usually in February the lakes are frozen and you cannot fish with a trawl. Trawling means that fish are gathered in a big net, which a fishing boat pulls.

Fisherman Jorma Kääpä says that he has never before fished with a trawl so late in the winter.

## There is a risk of avalanches in Lapland

![Three skiers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11223081)

Open image viewer

There is a lot of snow in Lapland. Photo: AOP

There is now a lot of snow in Lapland, and avalanches can easily happen there. Weather researchers, that is, meteorologists, say that people at ski resorts must be careful now.

There is a lot of snow, and it can start moving on steep slopes. Avalanches can easily happen if a skier goes off piste into the forest, away from the normal slope.

At ski resorts, people must follow the forecasts for weather and avalanches every day now.
