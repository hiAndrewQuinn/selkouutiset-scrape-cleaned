## Harassment of minorities

![At the Sápmi Pride parade in Utsjoki rainbow flags wave and a sign with a Sami-language text that means &quot;love builds a family&quot;.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_498/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-84041661127adced312/1628601461)

Minorities in Finland often encounter hate speech and harassment.

More than one third (1/3) of people in minority groups have experienced hate speech or harassment more than once a month. This is what a report by the Ministry of Justice says.

The most common harassment is that someone says something offensive to a person belonging to a minority group.

Less than one third (1/3) of people in minority groups report the harassment onward.

More than two thirds (2/3) of people in minority groups felt that they did not get enough support in the matter.

## Terrafame mine

![Terrafame's quarry in Kainuu, on the border area of Sotkamo and Kajaani.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5460,h_3071,x_0,y_568/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-874170617fc3abb2e88/1635767188)

Open image viewer

Terrafame's mining area in Sotkamo. Photo: Timo Sihvonen

The mining company Terrafame has received a new environmental permit for the mine's operation in Sotkamo. The new environmental permit is valid until the end of March 2030.

Terrafame's predecessor mines had major shortcomings in the leaching method, which became environmental problems.

That is why handling the new environmental permit took almost five years.

## Midsummer traffic

![Traffic jam on Route 9 in Kuopio at the airport junction, near the Jänne river bridge, caused by a traffic accident.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82539960d48686576b6/1624541105)

Open image viewer

Midsummer's outbound traffic is usually very busy on Thursday. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

At the end of the week Midsummer is celebrated. Then many Finns start their holiday and go to their summer cottages. Midsummer Eve is on Friday.

The traffic management company Fintraffic predicts that Midsummer's outbound traffic will be busiest on Thursday. The traffic is very busy on Thursday from 12 to 19 in Southern Finland. In Northern Finland traffic is still busy on Thursday evening.

On Midsummer Eve on Friday too there is a lot of traffic, but less than on Thursday.

## World Refugee Day

![Lama Kourdi and Aladdin Delli are the refugees of the year.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7467,h_4180,x_0,y_311/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97242762b03db18c0c5/1655724907)

Open image viewer

Refugee woman of the year Lama Kourdi and refugee man of the year Aladdin Delli. Photo: Julius Töyrylä

World Refugee Day was celebrated on Monday. The Finnish Refugee Aid association (Suomen Pakolaisapu ry) has chosen the refugee woman of the year and the refugee man of the year.

The refugee woman of the year is Lama Kourdi from Tampere. The refugee man of the year is Aladdin Delli from Tampere. Kourdi and Delli come from Syria. Syrians are one of the world's largest refugee groups.

Russia's attack on Ukraine has also caused a big refugee crisis. It is the largest and fastest refugee crisis since the Second World War. More than 6.5 million people have fled from Ukraine.
