## Finland's taxis will start to compete

![Taxi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8702563)

Open image viewer

Finland's taxis will start to compete in 2018. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

The taxi business in Finland is changing, and competition is coming to it. A person can start to drive a taxi if the authorities think they are suitable as a taxi entrepreneur and they are given a licence.

In the future, the authorities will no longer decide how many taxis there may be in a municipality. That means that more taxis than now may start to be seen.

In the future, taxis may also decide for themselves how much must be paid for a taxi journey. But if the authorities think the price rises too much, they can give an order on the maximum price.

The new taxi rules try to increase competition between taxis. The new rules come into force at the beginning of July 2018.

## Cuban volleyball players were convicted of gang rape

![A trial in Tampere.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9179546)

Open image viewer

The Cuban volleyball players will be put in a Finnish prison.  Photo: Yle

A rare court sentence for gang rape has been given in Finland. The court says that five Cuban volleyball players were guilty of aggravated rape in the summer. The rape was committed in a hotel in Tampere at the beginning of July.

The convicted men were players in Cuba's national volleyball team. Four men were convicted of aggravated rape and got five years in prison. One man's sentence was shorter, and one of the men was acquitted completely.

## The suspect in the Helsinki assault will be remanded tomorrow

![Memorial candles at Asema-aukio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9179974)

Open image viewer

The place of the assault is full of flowers.  Photo: Heidi Kononen / Yle

The investigation into the serious assault at Asema-aukio in Helsinki is moving forward. The police think that the court must now remand in custody the man who is suspected of assaulting a 28-year-old Finnish man.

The man got a bad skull injury in the assault, and he died last Friday. The police have arrested the suspect, who was born in 1990.

The police say that the suspect is a member of the Nazi-minded Nordic Resistance Movement. The court will decide tomorrow whether he will be remanded in custody.

## Autumn colours paint nature

![A picture of autumn colours in Heinola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8491825)

Open image viewer

This is what the autumn colours looked like in Heinola last year.  Photo: Seppo Manninen

In Finnish nature, you can now admire the autumn colours.  Ruska means the time when the leaves of trees and plants change colour. The colour of green leaves changes, for example, to red, orange, yellow and brown.

During the autumn colour season, colourful trees include, for example, the aspen, the maple and the rowan. The bilberry, the alpine bearberry, the dwarf birch and the bog bilberry are also in bright colours.

The autumn colours are often most colourful and most beautiful in Lapland. In Lapland, there are colourful autumn colours already in September. In Southern Finland, the autumn colours peak only in October.
