## It is worth making a travel notification

![Starfish Island is a tourist island off the Palawan holiday island in the Philippines. The island is in Honda Bay near Puerto Princesa, the largest centre on Palawan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10385676)

You can make a travel notification on the Ministry for Foreign Affairs' website. Photo: Kai Jaskari / Yle

A travel notification is worth making whenever you travel abroad, says Finland's Ministry for Foreign Affairs.

Finns have made few travel notifications, even though Finns make many millions of trips abroad every year.

In a travel notification, the traveller gives the Ministry for Foreign Affairs their contact details and information about the trip. The Ministry can then contact the traveller if, for example, an accident happens at the destination.

A travel notification is worth making even if the destination is a neighbouring country or an ordinary tourist destination.

## There are thousands of people missing in Indonesia

![Collapsed hotel.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10443032)

Open image viewer

The earthquake and tsunami happened on the Indonesian island of Sulawesi. Photo: Kirsi Crowley

In Indonesia, the authorities fear that the natural disasters of last week may still claim many thousands of lives.

The earthquake and tsunami happened on the Indonesian island of Sulawesi. It is already known that more than 1,700 people have died.

The authorities estimate that more than 5,000 people are missing.

The authorities have been searching for people for 10 days already. The searches will continue for a few more days.

## The language requirement for the police college has been eased

![Police college students.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7069735)

Open image viewer

The language requirement for the police college has been eased. Photo: Marko Melto / Yle

It is now easier for people with an immigrant background to get into the police college. Previously it was difficult, because the student also had to know Swedish.

Now the language requirement has been eased. For example, in the capital region police are needed who know various languages.

A Finnish citizen may take part in the police college entrance exam.

## Cars were burned in Espoo

![Burned cars in Matinkylä, Espoo, on 7 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10444002)

Open image viewer

5 cars were set alight during the night in Matinkylä, Espoo. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

In Southern Finland, in Espoo, cars were set alight during the night.

In the Matinkylä area, a total of 5 cars had been set on fire. The fire sites were close to each other.

The police are investigating the matter. The police say that someone may have planned the fires.
