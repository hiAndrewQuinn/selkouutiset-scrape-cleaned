## An uproar in the USA about Trump's ties with Russia

![Donald Trump, who is becoming President of the United States](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9398462)

Donald Trump denies pressure from Russia. Photo: ALBIN LOHR-JONES / EPA

There has been an uproar in the United States about an intelligence report.

The report claims that the next President of the United States, Donald Trump, has suspicious ties with Russia.

The report also claims that Russia has information that can harm Trump.

Trump says that the claims in the report are lies. Because of them, he has sent several angry messages on Twitter.

US intelligence services are examining the report. The things in it have not been confirmed as true.

## Doctors worried about super snus

![Snus in a tin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8618178)

Open image viewer

Snus is used especially by young men. Photo: Mika Viljanen / Yle

Young people's use of snus worries doctors.

Super-strong snus with a lot of nicotine has now come on sale.

Snus is put in the mouth, where the nicotine is absorbed into the bloodstream.

Strong snus can cause young people symptoms similar to poisoning.

Snus is made strong because people are now allowed to bring less of it into Finland than before.

Snus is brought from cruise ships that go to Sweden. Sweden is the only EU country where snus can be sold.

A test by cancer organisations revealed in the autumn that the sale of snus is not monitored enough on the ships.

The shipping companies have promised to improve monitoring.

## The number of refugees in Germany fell

![Refugees walk from Austria to Germany.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8520598)

Open image viewer

Many asylum seekers have walked a long way to the Balkans, Austria and Germany.  Photo: Peter Kneffel / EPA

The number of asylum seekers has fallen in Germany.

Last year about 280,000 asylum seekers came to Germany. A year earlier, as many as 890,000 asylum seekers came to Germany.

The number fell because the Balkan countries put up obstacles to travel. In addition, Turkey and the EU made a refugee agreement.

Germany accepted about 60 percent of the asylum applications that were processed in 2015.

## Pikku Kakkonen turned 40

![Circus clown Hermanni with his Äitiliini ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9098242)

Open image viewer

Hermanni the clown won a Yle News vote that asked for the favourite Pikku Kakkonen character. Photo: Arja Lento / Yle

Finland has celebrated Yle's popular children's programme Pikku Kakkonen.

Pikku Kakkonen turns 40 years old today, Wednesday.

Pikku Kakkonen has had funny and even slightly exciting characters who are dear to many Finns.

Popular characters have included, for example, Ransu the dog and Hermanni the clown. New favourite characters are the funny traffic police officers Maltti and Valtti.
