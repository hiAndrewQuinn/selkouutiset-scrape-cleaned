# Tuesday 8.6.2021 (radio)

The police are fighting organised crime. More wind power is planned to be built in Northern Finland. Advance voting ends. Prison sentence for a man who threatened Timo Soini. Blueberries are flowering.

## International police operation

![police in protective gear](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2000,h_1125,x_0,y_66/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81748560bf33839be70/1623143333)

An international police operation struck against organised crime. Photo: Police

The police have carried out a large international operation against organised crime. Finland also took part.

About 800 people were arrested in the operation. Arrests were made in 16 countries around the world. The arrests were related, for example, to international drug trade. The police also seized drugs and money.

In Finland, the police arrested about 100 people. In addition, in Finland over 500 kilos of narcotics, dozens of weapons and hundreds of thousands of euros in cash were seized.

## More wind power

![wind turbine spinning in a gif](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11794762)

Open image viewer

Wind turbine. Photo: Yle

Lots more wind power is planned to be built in Finland in the coming years. There are now more than 800 wind turbines. Their number will at least triple during this decade.

Wind power is planned to be built especially in Northern Finland, in the Bothnian Bay area. Most of the electricity, however, is used in Southern Finland. That is why more power transmission connections from the north to the south must also be built. The grid company Fingrid says that the number of power transmission connections must at least double from the current level.

Residents of the north are worried about the building of wind power. They are worried about the large size of the wind turbines and how they will change the landscape.

![Advance voting place in Mikkeli market square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_155/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81114760adf132ddce6/1622012299)

Advance voting in the municipal elections ends today.

About 30 percent of those entitled to vote voted in advance. That is more than in the last municipal elections 4 years ago. Advance voting also lasted a week longer this time than in the last elections.

The actual election day for the municipal elections is on Sunday, 13 June.

## Sentence for the man who threatened Timo Soini

![Timo Soini](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_47/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5674625cf2917c00104/1559400890)

Open image viewer

Former foreign minister Timo Soini. Photo: Antti Lähteenmäki/YLE

The Itä-Uusimaa District Court has sentenced a man who attacked Timo Soini a little over 2 years ago. The middle-aged man was sentenced to 7 months in prison, suspended.

The incident happened in Vantaa at the Korso country market in March 2019. Timo Soini was foreign minister at that time. Soini was at an election tent meeting voters when the accused tried to hit Soini with a plastic bottle. The blow hit Soini's bodyguard.

## Blueberries are flowering

![Blueberries are flowering.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8873601)

Open image viewer

Blueberry flowers. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Blueberries are now flowering across the whole country. The Natural Resources Institute says that there are clearly more blueberry flowers than usual.

The blueberry harvest usually ripens at the beginning of July, starting in Southern Finland.
