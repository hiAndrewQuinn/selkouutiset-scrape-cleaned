## Vesa-Matti Loiri has died

![Vesa-Matti Loiri](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_940,h_529,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-611253ac0654ea92b/1403782796)

Open image viewer

Vesa-Matti Loiri. Photo: Antero Tenhunen

The artist Vesa-Matti Loiri has died. He died of cancer at the age of 77.

Vesa-Matti Loiri was an actor, singer and athlete. He was already acting at the age of 17 in Mikko Niskanen's film Pojat in 1962. On the theatre stage Loiri acted, for example, in The Lapua Opera and in Seven Brothers.

Loiri also did a lot of entertainment. He played, among other things, Uuno Turhapuro in many films.

As a musician, Vesa-Matti Loiri is known especially as a singer of Eino Leino's poems.

As an athlete Loiri played, for example, football, water polo and handball.

## Biden signed Finland's NATO document

![Joe Biden signs the ratification document for Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3493,h_1965,x_0,y_72/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99230362f337516f540/1660106827)

Joe Biden signs the ratification document for Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership. Photo: BACKGRID/All Over Press

US President Joe Biden has confirmed that the USA accepts Finland's and Sweden's joining NATO. Biden signed the papers on Tuesday evening in Washington. Biden said that the signing of the papers was an important moment for NATO.

NATO has 30 countries, all of which must approve Finland's and Sweden's membership. 23 countries have already approved Finland and Sweden joining NATO.

## Alko's sales decreased

![A customer at Alko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4993,h_2809,x_120,y_631/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-77706760339bcd38d67/1613995539)

Open image viewer

Alcoholic drinks at Alko. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Less alcohol has been sold at Alko than before.

In July, 14 percent less alcohol was sold at Alko than in the same time last year.

Alko estimates that less alcohol was sold than last year, because there are no longer any corona restrictions. More alcohol was sold in restaurants than before and people also brought alcohol from abroad.

## Schools start

![Classroom with desks in rows.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_567/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99254962f3a74058d71/1660135345)

Schools start in almost the whole of Finland by Thursday at the latest.

On Wednesday school started in Tampere, for example. On Thursday pupils start school in Helsinki, Lappeenranta and Joensuu.

In some municipalities school already started on Tuesday. In Vaasa school starts only next week.

## The weather is warm

The weather is dry in almost the whole country. Only in Northern Finland may a little rain fall.

On Thursday in the daytime it is warm, 22...26, in the north 15...21 degrees.

The weather is warm for the whole of this week. In Southern and Western Finland it may even be up to 30 degrees warm in places.
