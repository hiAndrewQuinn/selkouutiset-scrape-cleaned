# Tuesday 4.6.2019 (radio)

Unemployment has fallen in the euro area. Finland is short of police officers and restaurant workers. Muslims are celebrating Eid now. The weather is very warm at the end of the week.

## Unemployment in the euro area

![Employment office in Bordeaux.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10815524)

Open image viewer

There are more unemployed people in Southern Europe than in Northern Europe. Photo: EPA

Unemployment has fallen in the euro area. The euro area is the countries whose currency is the euro.

In April the unemployment rate in the euro area was 7.6 per cent. The unemployment rate shows how large a share of people of working age are without work.

Unemployment in the euro area has not been this low for over 10 years.

## Shortage of police officers

![Police.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10556424)

Open image viewer

The police want tips. Illustrative image. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Finland is short of police officers. There are not enough police officers for all tasks. That is why some crimes are not solved.

Too few new police officers are graduating. Study places have increased, but that does not yet show in the number of new police officers.

Finland's new government intends to increase the number of police officers. 300 new police officers will be hired in Finland.

## Shortage of restaurant workers

![Chef students serving porridge into bowls.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10702410)

Open image viewer

Finland needs more chefs. Photo: Laura Valta / Yle

There are also too few workers in the restaurant sector. More restaurant workers are needed across the whole country.

Young people are not interested in the restaurant sector. This year about 1,500 people applied to study in the restaurant sector. Five years ago there were 3,500 applicants.

Restaurant workers say that the sector has a bad reputation. The work is heavy and the pay is low.

Temp agencies also damage the sector's reputation, says chef and shop steward Sasu Haapanen. Nowadays there are restaurants that have no employees of their own at all. In Haapanen's view, restaurants get workers if they give workers proper employment contracts.

## Eid celebration

![In Taipei, Taiwan, a girl carried a candy basket at the Eid al-Fitr celebrations at a mosque.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10257173)

The Muslim fasting month of Ramadan has ended. Now it is time for the Eid celebration.

Eid is a joyful festival for Muslims. People meet relatives and friends. Muslims celebrate together over good food.

For children, Eid is fun time. In Muslim families, children receive presents and new clothes.

## Warm end of the week

![People enjoying the sunny weather on Ascension Day in Esplanade park in Helsinki on 30 May 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10812426)

The weather warms up at the end of the week. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The weather is very warm at the end of the week.

Yle meteorologist Joonas Koskela says that the temperature will already exceed the heat limit in Southern Finland on Wednesday. That means the temperature will be over 25 degrees.

On Thursday and Friday the temperature will rise even higher. In Southern and Western Finland it may be about 30 degrees.

At the weekend there will be thunderstorms. Joonas Koskela says the thunderstorms will be strong. A lot of rain may fall.
