# Friday 20.7.2018 (radio)

A big fire. A serious car crash. A TV channel. Grain harvest. Weather.

## Fire under control

![Firefighting in Pyhäranta on 19 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10314231)

Open image viewer

The Pyhäranta forest fire is under control. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

In Pyhäranta in Western Finland, the big fire was brought under control during the day.

The forest has burned over a large area. There are many firefighters at the firefighting work. It rained during the day, and that helped the firefighters a lot.

Excavators arrived on site to dig into the ground. This way the firefighters can put out fires that are deep down.

The fire has not burned any houses, but several people have been moved away from the fire area. It is not yet safe to return to the area.

## The serious crash in Mäntyharju

![Site of the head-on collision in Mäntyharju.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10313833)

Open image viewer

Site of the crash in Mäntyharju. Photo: Esa Huuhko / Yle

The investigation into the serious car crash in Mäntyharju in Eastern Finland has moved forward. 3 people died yesterday when a drunk driver crashed into another car.

The drunk driver fled from the police and drove very fast. According to the police, the car may have been driving at about 200 kilometres per hour. A middle-aged couple died in the crash, and the driver who was drunk and crashed into the couple.

In addition, two people were injured. The drunk driver did not have the right to drive a car.

## MTV has been sold

![Logos of Telia and MTV](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10314392)

Open image viewer

Logos of Telia and MTV. Photo: Lehtikuva and Yle

The owner of the media company MTV is changing. MTV is moving to Telia's ownership. Telia is a telecoms operator. Telia is buying Bonnier Broadcasting, a company that owns, among other things, the Finnish MTV. The deal still needs approval from the competition authorities.

Telia says that MTV's news will continue and will remain independent.

The purchase price is almost 900 million euros. At the end of May, Telia already confirmed that it was negotiating the purchase.

## The grain harvest is poor

![Rye ears of grain](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10314327)

Open image viewer

The rye harvest will be half of last year's. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

This year's grain harvest looks very poor. There have now been three bad harvest years in a row.

The rye harvest will be 50 percent of last year's, and the wheat harvest will fall by about 30 percent from last year. The barley harvest is forecast to be the weakest in 30 years.

The oat harvest will fall only slightly. The grain harvest is affected by the heat and drought.

The 2018 grain harvest resembles the crop failure year that was 20 years ago.

## Thunderstorms in store

![weather maps](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10314950)

Open image viewer

Thunder showers will become more common on Saturday. Photo: Yle Sää

The hot temperatures will ease at the weekend. Thunder showers will become more common on Saturday. Between the showers, the temperature rises in sunny areas. On Sunday, rain and thunder will be most widespread in the east and north.

The weather will nevertheless stay very warm.
