# Sunday 4.2.2018 (radio)

The Yhteisvastuu (Common Responsibility) collection has begun. There was a train accident in the USA. The Finnish music industry's Emma awards have been handed out. Finland got bronze at the bandy World Championships. This winter's new cold record is -37.1 degrees.

## The Common Responsibility collection

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10048582)

Open image viewer

President Niinistö opened the Common Responsibility collection. Photo: Pekka Sipilä / Yle

The Common Responsibility collection has begun. President Sauli Niinistö opened the Common Responsibility collection on television on Sunday.

This time the Common Responsibility collection helps people who live in the middle of hunger and poverty.

President Niinistö said that hunger has not disappeared from the world. Almost 800 million people go to bed hungry every night, the president said. So help is needed.

The Common Responsibility collection helps people in Finland and abroad. The Common Responsibility collection takes place every year.

## Train accident in the USA

![Map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10058125)

Open image viewer

The accident happened in South Carolina on a train route that runs between New York and Miami. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

There has been a train accident in the USA. 2 people died and about 70 people were injured. The accident happened on a train route that runs between New York and Miami.

In the accident, a passenger train crashed into a freight train. Some of the train's carriages came off the rails. There were about 150 people on the train.

After the accident, the authorities got all the passengers out of the train, and the injured were taken to hospital.

## Emma awards

![Cheek](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9941177)

Open image viewer

Rap singer Cheek won both public votes at the Emma Gala. Photo: Yle

The Finnish music industry's Emma awards have been handed out. The awards were handed out at the Emma Gala on Saturday. The Emma Gala is the most important celebration of Finnish pop music during the year.

A band called Haloo Helsinki won the most awards this year. It got 4 Emma awards. Haloo Helsinki was chosen, for example, as the band of the year.

The public chose the winner in 2 categories, which were Artist of the Year and Music Video of the Year. The winner in both categories was Cheek.

## Bandy World Championships

![Russia and Finland in a bandy battle](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10057475)

Open image viewer

Bandy is a fast game. Photo: Lehtikuva

Sports:

Finland has won a bronze medal at the bandy World Championships. The Finnish team came third when it beat Kazakhstan in the bronze match. Finland scored 8 goals, and Kazakhstan scored 4 goals.

Finland's best goal scorers were Markus Kumpuoja and Mikko Lukkarila. Both scored 2 goals.

The bandy world champion is Russia. It beat Sweden in the final.

The bandy World Championships were held in Russia, in the city of Khabarovsk.

## Cold record

![Utsjoki's church cottages on a cold winter evening.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9946419)

Open image viewer

This winter's cold record is now -37.1 degrees. The record was measured in Utsjoki. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

On Sunday morning a new cold record for this winter was measured. The record was measured in the north, in Utsjoki in Lapland. It was minus 37.1 degrees there.

The winter's cold record is measured in the north almost every year. Since 1961, the winter's cold record has been measured somewhere other than the far north only 3 times.
