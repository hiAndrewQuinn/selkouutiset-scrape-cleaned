# Tuesday 11.2.2020 (radio)

The owner of many Finnish newspapers is changing. The Finnish game company Supercell is doing well. Many foreigners experience discrimination in Finland. The I Read to You campaign is held this week.

## Media company deal

![Printed editions of Aamulehti and Helsingin Sanomat](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_628/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6412305e42696208387/1581412750)

Helsingin Sanomat and Aamulehti will soon belong to the same company. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

The owner of many Finnish newspapers is changing. The reason is that the media company Sanoma is buying another media company, Alma Media.

Sanoma already owns Helsingin Sanomat, and after the deal it will also own Aamulehti, a big newspaper from Tampere.

In the deal Sanoma also gets many small newspapers.

The authorities still have to approve the deal.

## Supercell's success

![Supercell's Ilkka Paananen at an information event.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11202695)

Open image viewer

Supercell's chief executive Ilkka Paananen. Photo: Toni Määttä / Yle

The Finnish game company Supercell is doing well. The company's result last year was good.

Supercell's chief executive Ilkka Paananen says that Finland is a good place for a game company. The problem, however, is that top experts have to wait a long time for a residence permit. Paananen thinks that permit matters must go much faster than they do now.

Supercell has about 300 employees from over 30 different countries.

## Foreigners' experiences of discrimination

![Immigrant young people at the Itäkeskus shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10694132)

Open image viewer

Photo: Milla Vahtila / Yle

Many foreigners experience discrimination in Finland. The information is from a new study by the National Institute for Health and Welfare.

About 40 per cent of people with a foreign background have experienced discrimination during the last year. Discrimination means that a person is treated worse than others, for example because of their skin colour.

People with a foreign background are also often lonely. People with a foreign background experience more loneliness than other Finns.

Foreigners who have come to Finland from the Middle East and North Africa have the most health problems. For example, they are often depressed. The reason may be that they have had a hard life. They have come to Finland, for example, to escape war.

## The I Read to You campaign

![People in the premises of a service centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11203662)

Open image viewer

Students from the Stadin Osaamiskeskus visited the Kontula service centre, where they read easy-language books to older people. Photo: Yle

This week the I Read to You campaign week is being held. During the week, immigrants visit older people, for example, and read easy-language books aloud to them.

Students of the Stadin Osaamiskeskus in Helsinki also take part in the campaign. They visited the Kontula service centre on Monday.

![Two women at a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11203633)

Open image viewer

Sahra Shikahmed reads an easy-language book to Eija Kinnunen. Photo: Yle

The students read an easy-language book to the older people. The book tells of the 1950s. The book brought the older people memories of old times, and the students got to use the Finnish language.

The I Read to You campaign speaks up for a multicultural Finland and for easy language. The organiser of the campaign is the Easy Language Centre, Selkokeskus.
