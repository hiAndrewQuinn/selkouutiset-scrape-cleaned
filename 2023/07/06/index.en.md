## Finland's relations with Russia

![Finland's consulate coat of arms on the wall of a building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3552,h_1998,x_0,y_148/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113892364a6a29d368f1/1688642320)

Finland has a consulate general in St Petersburg. Photo: AOP

Finland's and Russia's relations are bad.

Russia said today, Thursday, that it is expelling 9 Finnish diplomats from Russia.

In addition, Russia is closing Finland's consulate general in St Petersburg. The office's job is, for example, to give help to Finns who are in Russia.

Russia says that this is a countermeasure against Finland. Finland expelled 9 Russian diplomats a month ago, because they were secretly gathering information about Finland.

President Sauli Niinistö says that Russia's countermeasure is harsh. That is why Finland intends to close Russia's consulate general in Turku.

## Party support

![Graphic shows party support in July 2023. National Coalition 22.1, SDP 20.6 and Finns Party 20.2 per cent.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2540,h_1428,x_0,y_33/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113834064a53fafc434c/1688551805)

The support of the government parties has fallen. This is what Yle's new poll says.

The largest party in the poll is the National Coalition. It is also the largest government party.

However, the National Coalition has lost a little support. Its support is now about 22 per cent.

The second largest party in the poll is the SDP. Its support has risen. The SDP is an opposition party, that is, it is not in the government now.

In third place in the poll is the government party the Finns Party. The Finns Party's support has fallen, and the party has come in behind the SDP in the poll.

It is estimated that the Finns Party's support has fallen because of the resignation of Minister of Economic Affairs Vilhelm Junnila. The reason for Junnila's resignation was his positive speeches about Nazis.

Wille Rydman has started work as Minister of Economic Affairs. The Finns Party's Rydman started in the post of Minister of Economic Affairs today, Thursday.

The government parties have agreed that the post of Minister of Economic Affairs currently belongs to the Finns Party.

After Vilhelm Junnila's resignation, the Finns Party decided that Rydman would become Minister of Economic Affairs.

## Prize for Niillas Holmberg

![Niillas Holmberg leans on a railing at home in Utsjoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5217,h_2934,x_0,y_172/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1135911649d1e22a0fd3/1688018613)

Open image viewer

Writer Niillas Holmberg. Photo: Sara Kelemeny / Yle

Writer Niillas Holmberg has received the Eino Leino Prize. Holmberg is a Sámi writer from Lapland.

Holmberg writes in Sámi and in Finnish. He wants to tell Finns what the life of the Sámi is like in Finland today.

Holmberg received the Eino Leino Prize, because he is a skilful multilingual writer.

The Eino Leino Prize is awarded on 6 July. On that day, the poet Eino Leino's day is celebrated.
