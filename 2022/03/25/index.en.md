## Finland's debt

![Annika Saarikko speaking.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3919,h_2204,x_839,y_584/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-93126962398861dc7b9/1647944204)

Because of the war in Ukraine, the government is taking on more debt. At least hundreds of millions of euros will be borrowed. This is the estimate of Minister of Finance and Centre Party chair Annika Saarikko.

The government is adding spending on defence, agriculture and energy.

The Centre Party is now also in favour of debt. In November, it still demanded fiscal discipline from its government partners.

## Energy in Europe

![Ursula von der Leyen and Joe Biden.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2598,h_1461,x_203,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-933144623d70af797d8/1648193800)

US President Joe Biden has ended his visit to Brussels.

Biden held a press conference with European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen. Both stressed the importance of cooperation on sanctions against Russia.

The US is trying to make sure that more liquid natural gas is delivered to the European Union. In this way, the EU's dependence on Russian gas can be reduced.

The war in Ukraine has lasted more than a month.

Germany plans to halve its oil imports from Russia by the summer.

German Economy Minister Robert Habeck says that coal imports from Russia to Germany will end completely by autumn.

In addition to oil and coal, Germany has been especially dependent on Russian natural gas.

## Organic farming

![Fertiliser that has just come out of the Palopuro biogas plant is ready in the fields of Knehtilä organic farm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2676,h_1503,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9284536230950284250/1647351673)

Open image viewer

Nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium from the fields are recycled from the plant back to the fields. Before that, biogas has been recovered at the plant. Photo: Kristiina Lehto / Yle

The crisis may promote organic farming in Finland. The experiences of organic farms are moving to other agriculture.

For example, fertilisers are now harder to get, and they are expensive. About a third of Finland's fertilisers have come from Russia, which is now at war in Ukraine.

Sanctions, that is, restrictions, prevent the import of fertilisers.

In organic production, the soil gets its nitrogen fertiliser mainly from plants. Nitrogen-fixing plants of this kind include clover and legumes, for example.

Animal manure and recycled fertilisers are also used as fertiliser. More recycled fertilisers are now being produced than before.

The train company VR is suspending Allegro train services for now from Monday. The reason is the sanctions against Russia.

Allegro runs between Helsinki and St Petersburg.

The last Allegro service will run on Sunday morning.

## The weather

![Map of Finland's weather](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12377464)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

A winter storm will hit the southern and central parts of the country on Saturday. The wind speed may rise a little above the storm limit, which is 21 metres per second.

Driving conditions may be dangerous from North Ostrobothnia and Koillismaa southwards.

Only in the south is the temperature above zero during the day. On Sunday there is frost even in the south.
