# Thursday 13.2.2020 (radio)

Finnair is starting change negotiations. The risk for a traveller to catch the coronavirus abroad is very small. The candidates for the big journalism awards have been announced. Yle Radio Suomi is the most listened-to radio channel. The ringed seal pups in Saimaa are in danger, because there is too little snow.

## Finnair starts change negotiations

![Finnair planes at Helsinki-Vantaa airport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11197103)

Open image viewer

Finnair stopped its China flights at the beginning of February. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The airline Finnair is starting change negotiations.

In the negotiations, they will assess whether there is enough work for all pilots. However, the pilots will not be laid off.

The change negotiations start next week. They concern all long-haul pilots of Finnair.

The reason for the change negotiations is the coronavirus. Finnair has cancelled all flights to China until the end of March. In total, about 200 flights have been cancelled.

The news was first reported by Iltalehti.

## The risk for a holidaymaker to catch the coronavirus is small

![Man and woman with luggage at an airport, both wearing breathing masks. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11195513)

Open image viewer

The risk for holidaymakers of catching the coronavirus is small, unless they travel to China. Photo: AKM Images / GSI Media / AOP

The risk for a traveller to catch the coronavirus abroad is very small. The risk is high only in China. This is what the National Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, estimates.

Most of the people who are ill live in China. About 1400 people have died from the coronavirus. Only 1 person has died outside China.

## The Great Journalism Award

![Antti Kuronen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11206528)

Open image viewer

Antti Kuronen. Photo: Yle

The candidates for the big journalism awards have been announced.

Yle journalist Antti Kuronen is nominated for Journalist of the Year. The other candidates are Sonja Saarikoski from Image and Paavo Teittinen from Helsingin Sanomat.

Nominated for Story of the Year are Yle's series of articles on problems in care homes for the elderly, Helsingin Sanomat's article about Veijo Baltzar's cult, and the Iltasanomat and Taloussanomat article about a training event by Instru Optiikka.

Awards will also be given for the Journalistic Deed of the Year and the Journalistic Boss of the Year.

The Great Journalism Award is the most important journalism prize in Finland.

Radio listening has been studied.

In Finland, Yle Radio Suomi is listened to the most. The next most popular channel is Radio Nova.

Last year, people listened to the radio for an average of 2 hours and 40 minutes a day. 69 percent of Finns listen to the radio every day. Older people listen to the radio the most.

## The ringed seal pups in Saimaa are in danger

![Ringed seal and a gull](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11163852)

Open image viewer

Saimaa ringed seal. Photo: Juha Metso / AOP

The pups of the Saimaa ringed seal are in danger.

The pups are born at the turn of February and March. A pup needs snow and ice. Now there is very little snow and ice in southern Lake Saimaa.

The Saimaa ringed seal gives birth in a nest that it has dug in the snow.

The nest gives the pups shelter. Up to half of the pups can die if there are no nests.
