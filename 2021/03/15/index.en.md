## Neste's new refinery

![Neste's office building in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11837463)

Open image viewer

Neste's head office is in Espoo. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Oil company Neste has chosen a building site for a new refinery. The refinery will be built in Rotterdam, in the Netherlands.

Porvoo in Finland was also a candidate as the refinery's building site. Neste chose Rotterdam, because it is cheaper to build there and the transport connections are better.

Neste says that the availability of raw materials is also better in Rotterdam than in Finland.

## The Astra Zeneca coronavirus vaccine

![Astra Zeneca coronavirus vaccine vials.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_173/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-779980603e1b48c6be6/1614683116)

Astra Zeneca vaccine. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

The pharmaceutical company Astra Zeneca says that its coronavirus vaccine is safe for people. Many European countries have suspended vaccinations with the Astra Zeneca vaccine.

Astra Zeneca has checked the data of more than 17 million vaccinated people in Britain and EU countries.

Astra Zeneca says that no evidence has been found in the data that the vaccine causes blood clots.

The European Medicines Agency also says that the Astra Zeneca vaccine may be used.

## Finland wants work-based immigration

![Slush visitors in a dim corridor with coloured lights.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11366887)

Open image viewer

There is a shortage of skilled people, for example in the IT sector and in health care. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Finland wants to speed up work-based immigration. The Ministry of Employment is preparing a fast track for work-based immigration.

The fast track means that foreign experts and their families can get a residence permit decision within 14 days.

There is a shortage of workers in Finland, for example in the IT sector and in health care. The Ministry of Employment is also preparing trials to attract skilled people from the USA and India to Finland.

Finland also intends to shorten the processing time for work-based and education-based residence permits.

## Matriculation examinations begin

![Matriculation examinations about to begin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6579883)

Open image viewer

The matriculation examinations begin on Tuesday. Photo: Yle

The spring matriculation examinations begin tomorrow, Tuesday, with the mother tongue reading test.

Matriculation exams will be held over the next 3 weeks. Upper secondary schools follow coronavirus guidelines.

Many upper secondary school students have been in voluntary quarantine, so that they can be sure to stay healthy before the matriculation examinations.

Universities and universities of applied sciences are preparing for the spring entrance exams.

Universities and universities of applied sciences are holding the spring entrance exams as normal on-site exams, even though Finland has a coronavirus situation.

There are strict coronavirus arrangements for the entrance exams, for example distancing.

More entrance exam days have been added at universities of applied sciences, so that applicants are spread over more days.
