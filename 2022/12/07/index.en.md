## Dispute between the government parties

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin at a press briefing in Parliament in Helsinki before the plenary session on 7 December 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-104484263908a2a51a76/1670420910)

The government parties have settled the dispute.

A dispute arose among the government parties when one of the government parties, the Centre Party, voted in Parliament against the government's own bill.

The Centre Party voted with the opposition on the Nature Conservation Act. The opposition consists of the parties that are not part of the government.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that after this, all the government parties must defend the government's proposals in Parliament.

## Viewers of the Presidential Palace reception

![Reception at the Presidential Palace reception.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1984,h_1116,x_0,y_562/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1044407638f7bcbcd891/1701606648)

A man's outfit can also be surprising. Here is ballet dancing by Atte Kilpinen at the Presidential Palace reception. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

Many Finns watched the Presidential Palace reception on television on Independence Day. The Presidential Palace reception had almost 3 million viewers.

The reception interested viewers, because the presidential couple invited guests to the Presidential Palace again. In 2020 and 2021 the presidential couple could not invite guests because of corona.

Guests at this year's Presidential Palace reception included, for example, nurses and doctors. There were about 1,300 guests in total. That is fewer than before corona.

President Sauli Niinistö and his wife Jenni Haukio shook hands with guests this year too, as they did before the corona times. However, they cleaned their hands with hand sanitiser many times.

## Petrol prices

![A person refuels a car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_456/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98817362e3b649c164f/1659092483)

Photo: Aalto Puutio / Yle

The price of petrol has fallen a little.

Last week the price of a litre of petrol was for once a little under 2 euros. A litre of petrol cost on average 1 euro and 98 cents.

The last time a litre of petrol cost less than 2 euros was last February. Russia attacked Ukraine at the end of February. Because of the war, petrol has been very expensive this year.

Diesel now costs more than petrol. Last week the price of a litre of diesel was on average 2 euros and 14 cents.

## Google search terms

![iPhone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5052,h_2842,x_0,y_529/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5107665ba211ffc61a9/1723036768)

Google has said which search terms Finns have used on the internet this year.

Finns have used Google to find information, for example, about Russia's war in Ukraine. Popular search terms have been Ukraine, Russia, Nato and Putin.

Because of the war, the price of energy has also risen. That is why Finns have searched for information with the search terms spot electricity price and petrol price.
