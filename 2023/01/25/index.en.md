## Leopard tanks

![A Leopard tank drives through a snowy landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3641,h_2047,x_0,y_226/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106340163d01a10a99b1/1674620806)

A decision on sending Leopard tanks to Ukraine has long been awaited from Germany. Photo: Kimmo Mäntylä / Lehtikuva

Germany has given permission for the delivery of Leopard tanks to Ukraine.

Now all countries that have Leopard tanks can send them to Ukraine. Many countries have been waiting for permission from Germany, which is the maker of the Leopards.

At least Poland, Spain, Portugal, Norway and the Netherlands have already announced that they intend to send Leopards to Ukraine.

Germany itself is sending 14 tanks to Ukraine. The training of Ukrainian soldiers in Germany will begin soon.

The tanks are intended to help Ukraine in Russia's war of aggression.

## Finland's aid to Ukraine

![Mikko Savola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1048963639b28d59ee5d/1671113181)

Open image viewer

Defence Minister Mikko Savola (Centre). Photo: Sakari Piippo / Yle

Defence Minister Mikko Savola says that Finland also takes part in the Leopard project.

It is not yet certain whether Finland will send tanks or whether Finland will be involved in some other way. Savola says that the state leadership is preparing the matter together with the Defence Forces.

Finland cannot send a large amount of military supplies, because Finland must look after the defence of its own country.

## Free media

![Newspapers (Länsiväylä) on the line at the Tuusula printing house, 17.9.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_384/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5926655d8496b412f98/1569850883)

Newspapers on the line at the Tuusula printing house. Photo: Katriina Laine / Yle

Almost all Finns think that free media is important for the functioning of democracy. A public opinion poll by the Finnish Media Federation says that 93 percent of respondents think so.

Almost as many say that reliable information is important or very important for mental preparedness. Preparedness means preparing for possible crises and disruptions.

## Post

![Postal workers on a zebra crossing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2877,h_1619,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9454736264d7b5430db/1650776051)

Open image viewer

Alternate-day delivery means that post would be delivered on Monday, Wednesday and Friday in one week, and on Tuesday and Thursday the following week. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Delivery of letter post is changing to three days a week across the whole country. Parliament has approved the amendment to the Postal Act.

The reason for the change is that less and less traditional paper post is being sent.

After the change in the law, post will be delivered on alternate days across the whole country. The change is coming into force from the beginning of October. Posti has already tested alternate-day delivery in several areas.

Newspaper delivery will receive temporary state aid.

## Child protection

![A child swinging.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11654231)

Open image viewer

In child protection, social workers may have too many clients. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Many child protection social workers have too many clients.

14 percent have more clients than the law allows. The law says that one social worker may have at most 35 clients.

More than 1,200 social workers work full-time in child protection social work.

The figures are from THL's, the National Institute for Health and Welfare's, municipal survey.
