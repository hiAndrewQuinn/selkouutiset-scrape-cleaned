## Discussion on NATO

![Sanna Marin and Pekka Haavisto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4999,h_2811,x_0,y_359/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9439976260154b6c359/1650464176)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto in Parliament. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Security is being discussed in Finland's Parliament, the Eduskunta. Parliament has received a report on security matters from the government.

The report has been made because of the war in Ukraine. Europe's security situation changed when Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

Now Parliament is considering whether Finland should apply for membership of the defence alliance NATO. Finland is already cooperating with NATO, but is not a NATO member.

In Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto's view, cooperation between Finland and Sweden on the matter is important. It is good if Finland and Sweden decide on NATO at the same time, Haavisto said in Parliament.

In Sweden too, NATO membership is being considered. Sweden's situation is the same as Finland's, that is, Sweden is not a NATO member.

## NATO support in Sweden

![Flags of Finland, Sweden and NATO.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7438019)

Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Support for the defence alliance NATO has grown in Sweden.

In a survey by a Swedish newspaper, support for NATO is almost 60 percent. About 20 percent of respondents did not want Sweden to join NATO.

## Summer cottages

![A summer cottage by the water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5243,h_2949,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7768966033378376cac/1613969450)

Open image viewer

Photo: Alamy / All Over Press

Summer cottages are still popular.

During the corona time, many summer cottages were bought, because holidays were spent in Finland. A summer cottage was also a good place to avoid corona infection.

Now summer cottage sales have decreased, but many cottages are still being sold, especially near large cities. City dwellers want to get to a cottage quickly.

Real estate agent Tiina Björkman says that simple cottages are suitable for young people. For young people the most important thing is nature.

Older buyers hope for comforts. A cottage must have, for example, an indoor toilet and an electric sauna, Björkman says.

## The weather

![Weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12411392)

Open image viewer

Sunny spring weather continues across almost the whole country.

At night there is frost, but during the day the temperature rises above +15 degrees in the south and above +10 degrees in the north too.

Towards the end of the week the weather cools slightly across the whole country.
