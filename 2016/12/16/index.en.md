# Friday 16.12.2016 (radio)

Undocumented people have a right to support. The evacuation of East Aleppo in Syria has been suspended. The USA threatens Russia with countermeasures because of data breaches. Iraqi men were convicted of manslaughter. In Finland, people spend moderately at Christmas.

## Undocumented people have a right to support

![A street musician on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6610249)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

In Finland, a foreign person has a right to help, even if he or she is in the country without a permit.

The ministerial working group on immigration decided on the matter today.

The working group gave municipalities instructions, according to which a foreigner has a right to emergency accommodation, food and important medicines, even if he or she is in the country without a permit.

The working group's instructions also say that a person must be directed into the return system if he or she has been left without asylum.

## The evacuation of East Aleppo suspended

![A green bus with people gathered around it.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9356676)

Open image viewer

The evacuations of East Aleppo started yesterday, Thursday. Photo: SANA / EPA

In Syria, the evacuation of East Aleppo's residents and rebels has been suspended.

The authorities have told aid organisations to withdraw from the area, says the World Health Organization WHO.

According to the Syrian army, the rebels have tried to transport prisoners out of the besieged area. They also say that the rebels have broken the ceasefire.

The rebels say that Shia police have fired at buses that have been evacuating people from East Aleppo.

## The USA threatens to retaliate for Russia's data breaches

![A man's hands on a computer keyboard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5540174)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

The president of the United States Barack Obama says that the United States will take action against Russia because of data breaches.

According to the US administration, Russia broke into US information systems because it wanted to influence the result of the US presidential election.

According to the United States, Russia's president Vladimir Putin gave permission for the data breaches.

President Obama said in a radio interview that the United States must respond if a foreign state tries to influence elections.

The intelligence service CIA is still investigating the matter. Russia denies the data breach.

## Iraqi men convicted of manslaughter in Kajaani

![The men charged with the Otanmäki manslaughter covered their faces in court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9309642)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jonna Karjalainen / Yle

In Finland, two Iraqi asylum seekers have been sentenced to prison for manslaughter and aggravated negligent homicide.

The men killed the victim in Otanmäki, Kajaani, in September.

The men's sentences were over 10 years in prison. They were also convicted of aggravated robbery and payment instrument fraud.

## Finns spend moderately on Christmas

![](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6040450)

Open image viewer

Photo: YLE Central Finland / Jukka Virenius

Finns spend the second least money on Christmas in Europe, a survey by the payday loan company Ferratum shows.

Finns spend about 15 percent of their monthly salary on Christmas shopping, that is, on average less than 500 euros.

The Dutch spend the least money on Christmas.

The Germans spend the most money on Christmas, on average 30 percent of their monthly salary.

Ferratum did the survey in 20 countries.
