# Friday 19.3.2021 (radio)

Vaccinations with the Astra Zeneca vaccine are being stopped. President Sauli Niinistö has received the coronavirus vaccine. There are more women on the boards of stock exchange companies than before. Finns are once again the happiest people in the world. Minna Canth and Equality Day.

## Finland stops giving the Astra Zeneca vaccine

![Astra Zeneca vaccine bottle.18.3.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_244/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78593460545ffacd1b3/1616142425)

Finland wants to study the Astra Zeneca corona vaccine to be on the safe side. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare is stopping coronavirus vaccinations with the Astra Zeneca vaccine. The stop will last at least next week.

In Finland, 2 cases of blood clots in the brain have been reported after people received the vaccine. It is not yet known whether the clots are linked to the vaccine.

Stopping the Astra Zeneca vaccinations is a precaution. THL wants more information about the cases.

Vaccinations with other coronavirus vaccines continue as normal.

## Women already make up a third of stock exchange company boards

![Mervi Airaksinen, IBM CEO.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7855146053214930cb8/1616060777)

Open image viewer

Mervi Airaksinen, CEO of IBM Finland, thinks that women's career progress is held back by being too hard on themselves. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

There are more women on the boards of stock exchange companies than before. Their number has risen every year and is now 30 percent.

Women also make up almost a third of the management teams of companies.

The Central Chamber of Commerce looked into this.

In many countries, a quota has been set for women's seats on boards. For example, in France and Norway there is a 40 percent quota for women.

Finland and Sweden do not have a quota.

However, women still have a long way to go to reach the top of business life. Only 10 of the CEOs of 124 stock exchange companies are women.

![A young person watches the sunset.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_628/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-779516603ca2ca00194/1653638979)

The happiness report says that Finns are still the happiest people in the world.

Finland has held first place for the fourth year in a row.

Iceland, Denmark, Switzerland and the Netherlands are also among the 5 happiest countries.

The 20 last countries in the report are mostly in Africa.

## Minna Canth and Equality Day

![A wreath is laid at the statue of Minna Canth. The president of Lions Club Canth Kuopio, Ulla Paksula, lays a wreath at the Minna Canth statue in Kuopio in 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11007982)

Open image viewer

The Minna Canth statue in Kuopio. Photo: Pekka Niiranen / Yle

Friday was Minna Canth Day. Minna Canth was born in 1844.

She was a Finnish writer and a social activist.

Canth is the first Finnish woman to have her own flag day. Minna Canth Day is also Equality Day.

In 2023, two new official flag days will be added to the Finnish calendar: Finland's Nature Day and the Day of Miina Sillanpää and Civic Participation.

Miina Sillanpää was a key figure in the social democratic labour movement. Sillanpää was an MP for 38 years in total. She became Finland's first female minister in 1926.
