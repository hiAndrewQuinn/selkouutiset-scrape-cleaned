# Monday 29.6.2020 (radio)

An explosion went off at a theatre building in Helsinki. The pace of temporary layoffs is slowing. The corona situation in Finland is improving. People are taking too many painkillers. Water scooters are selling a lot now.

The TV Clear News has a summer break and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Explosion accident in Helsinki

![Authorities and rescue staff at the Helsinki City Theatre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11423917)

An explosion happened at a theatre building in Helsinki on Monday. One person was injured in the explosion.

The explosion happened at the Helsinki City Theatre while maintenance work was being done there. The police say that the explosion was an accident. The person who was injured in the explosion was taken to hospital.

The police are investigating why the explosion accident happened at the Helsinki City Theatre.

## Fewer temporary layoffs

![Closed restaurant area in Tapiola, Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2670,h_1502,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6651995e987267be7b4/1587050695)

Open image viewer

For example, many restaurants closed and laid off workers because of the corona situation. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The number of temporary layoffs in Finland is getting smaller. When the corona crisis began, companies began to lay off many people. A temporary layoff means that work stops for a while, for at most three (3) months.

Now companies are laying off fewer people than in the spring. Businesses are doing better and the corona crisis is easing.

In Finland there are now about 130,000 people on temporary layoff. About 300,000 people are unemployed.

## The corona situation is improving in Finland

![Sign of HUS, the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district, at the Meilahti hospital area in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11423530)

Open image viewer

Monday was the first day when there were no corona patients in intensive care at HUS hospitals. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The corona situation in Finland is improving. On Monday, for the first time, there are no corona patients in intensive care in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district, HUS.

On Monday morning there were 17 corona patients on the ordinary wards of HUS hospitals. There were no patients in intensive care.

In Finland, 11 new corona infections have been reported on Monday.

## Too many painkillers

![A glass of water and aspirin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10906506)

Open image viewer

Painkillers must be used according to the instructions. Not too much and not for too long. Photo: AOP

Some Finns take too many painkillers. The Finnish Medicines Agency Fimea says that some Finns take painkillers too much, or use them for too long.

If, for example, anti-inflammatory painkillers are used too much, they can be bad for the stomach and intestines.

Another painkiller, paracetamol, can damage a person's liver if paracetamol is used too much.

## Water scooters are popular

![Water scooter on the waters of Hanko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4368,h_2457,x_1749,y_746/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6036265db07b4c99441/1572358970)

Open image viewer

You can go fast on a water scooter. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

Finns have bought many water scooters this summer.

Last year over 1,000 water scooters were sold in Finland. Water scooter shops say that this year far more water scooters have been bought than before.

Water scooters have run out in many shops. Because of the corona situation, some factories abroad have been closed, and shops cannot get more water scooters.
