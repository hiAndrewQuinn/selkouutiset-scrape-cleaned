# Monday 30.4.2018 (radio)

The May Day celebrations have started. A serious traffic accident happened in Central Finland. The governments of Finland and Estonia are holding a joint celebratory meeting. The under-18 ice hockey team, the Pikkuleijonat, came home. May Day will be rainy and windy in the south.

## A happy May Day Eve

![students put a cap on a statue](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10185728)

Open image viewer

Manta got her cap this year too. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

May Day has begun. May Day Eve was celebrated on Monday.

Manta got her cap at the Market Square in Helsinki. Students from the University of Helsinki put a student cap on the head of the Havis Amanda statue at exactly 6 pm. Manta always gets a student cap on May Day Eve.

Students have organised big events in many other cities too. On May Day students go around wearing their student caps. Many also have overalls, the uniform of their student organisation. From the overalls you can see what field the student is studying.

Many older Finns also wear their own old student cap on May Day.

## A bad traffic accident

![Map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10185146)

Open image viewer

The accident happened in Konnevesi, north of Jyväskylä. Photo: Yle News Graphics

On May Day Eve a serious traffic accident happened in Central Finland. The accident site is on the road between Konnevesi and Suolahti. There, 3 cars crashed into each other.

In total 3 people died in the accident: 1 young girl and 2 middle-aged men. In addition, 2 people were injured in the accident, one of whom is a child.

The police do not yet know what caused the accident.

## The neighbours' celebratory meeting

![A postcard showing old Tallinn in a tourist's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10075988)

Open image viewer

Estonia turns 100 this year. Photo: Anniina Wallius / Yle

The governments of Finland and Estonia are having a joint celebratory meeting. The meeting will be held in the Estonian capital Tallinn next Monday.

Finland and Estonia are holding the celebratory meeting in honour of their 100th anniversaries. Last year independent Finland turned 100, and this year is Estonia's 100th anniversary.

Finland and Estonia have never before held a joint meeting of their governments.

## The return of the Pikkuleijonat

The Pikkuleijonat ice hockey team has come home. The under-18 players won the World Championship in Russia at the weekend.

The world champions got a warm welcome at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. A big crowd of fans was there to meet the Pikkuleijonat.

## May Day

![May Day at Ullanlinnanmäki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8858062)

Open image viewer

A May Day picnic in lovely weather a couple of years ago. Photo: Leila Oksa / Yle

The May Day celebrations continue on Tuesday, when May Day is celebrated. In Southern Finland May Day Eve has been lovely and warm, but May Day will be rainy and windy in the south.

But many people are sticking to an important May Day tradition, the May Day picnic. Then people eat together with friends outdoors, for example in a park.

May Day balloons are also sold outdoors. Many children think a big, colourful balloon is the best thing about May Day.

**Clear News wishes everyone a happy May Day!**
