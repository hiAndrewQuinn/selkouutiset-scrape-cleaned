# Sunday 1.1.2023 (radio)

President Niinistö's speech. Eye injuries. Sweden holds the EU presidency. Euro for Croatia. Weather.

## President Niinistö's speech

![Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4947,h_2769,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105468363b156f6f2387/1672566559)

Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The President of the Republic, Sauli Niinistö, has given a New Year's speech.

Niinistö spoke about Russia's attack on Ukraine. He compared Russia's attack to Finland's Winter War. The President said that both the Soviet Union and Russia misjudged their opponent.

President Sauli Niinistö also said that the rise in the prices of electricity and other things has made Finns' lives difficult. Niinistö assured that people will get through the difficulties. Finns have previously survived wars, a big energy crisis and times of recession.

President Niinistö stresses that people in difficulties must be helped.

## New Year celebrations

![New Year's fireworks in Joensuu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4092,h_2301,x_3,y_276/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105456663b066aec50a7/1672514869)

Open image viewer

Photo: Emilia Saukkonen / Yle

New Year celebrations caused eye injuries and the police had a lot of tasks.

At least 8 people got eye injuries from New Year's fireworks. Of the patients, 6 have been treated in Helsinki, 1 in Oulu and 1 in Tampere. In Tampere a patient's eye had to be operated on because of the injury.

The police say that, for example, in south-west Finland rockets were fired in the middle of people and towards people.

The Helsinki city rescue service had a lot of tasks over the weekend, which involved alcohol, violence and falls.

## Sweden holds the EU presidency

![Ulf Kristersson speaks at a press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6192,h_3483,x_0,y_420/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1034661637496ad43ba1/1668585162)

Open image viewer

Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

Sweden now holds the EU presidency.

Sweden holds the EU presidency for six months. Sweden's Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson says that important matters of Sweden's presidency include security and democracy, among others.

Sweden now holds the EU presidency for the 3rd time. Before Sweden, the EU presidency was held by the Czech Republic. At the beginning of July it is Spain's turn to hold the presidency of the European Union.

## Euro for Croatia

![The headquarters of the European Central Bank in Frankfurt. In the foreground four EU flags. In the background the sky is dark and ominous.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5600,h_3150,x_0,y_464/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6963835f01c6006a9bc/1593951869)

Open image viewer

Photo: ullstein bild - Sven Simon/ All Over Press

Croatia has adopted the euro. There are now 20 euro countries in total.

Croatia also joined the Schengen area. The Schengen area now includes 27 European countries. There are no travel restrictions or border checks between Schengen countries.

Croatia has been a member state of the European Union since 2013.

## The weather

![Sunshine](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4566,h_2561,x_0,y_90/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105471463b18f1bca950/1672580921)

The weather is fair. There are not very many clouds in the sky.

The weather is getting colder. The temperature falls to between -3 and -10 degrees, and in the northern part of the country to between -5 and -15 degrees.

At first the wind is strong in the Gulf of Bothnia, and on the roads driving conditions are poor in North Ostrobothnia and Kainuu.
