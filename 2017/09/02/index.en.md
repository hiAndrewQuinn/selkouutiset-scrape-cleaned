# Saturday 2.9.2017 (radio)

SDP has chosen a presidential candidate. The Houston floods are easing. In Britain, women will be allowed into combat roles. Basketball is now popular in Finland. A big space rock is passing the Earth.

## SDP has a presidential candidate

![Tuula Haatainen poses after being chosen as the Social Democrats' presidential candidate at the SDP's extraordinary party council meeting in Jyväskylä on Saturday 2 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9812760)

Tuula Haatainen is the SDP's presidential candidate. Photo: Tommi Anttonen / Lehtikuva

The SDP, that is, the Social Democratic Party, has chosen its own presidential candidate. It is Tuula Haatainen, who is currently an MP for the SDP. Haatainen is 57 years old. She has previously been, for example, Minister of Social Affairs and Health. Haatainen wants to raise the well-being of Finns as a key issue in the election.

There are many candidates in the presidential election. The current president of the republic, Sauli Niinistö, also wants to stay in the post.

The presidential election is at the start of next year.

## Houston floods are easing

![A man looks at his kitchen after the flood in the Houston area, before evacuation, Texas, USA.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9810134)

Open image viewer

People's homes have suffered badly in the floods. Photo: Tannen Maury / EPA-EFE

People have been able to return to their homes in the Houston flood area in the USA. However, the floods continue on the west and east sides of Houston as well. More than 40,000 people have been in emergency shelter because of the floods.

The governor of the state of Texas says that repairing the damage will take many years, and at least 125 billion dollars will be needed. President Donald Trump will visit the disaster area again on Saturday.

## Women in Britain can take combat roles

![Female soldier in line with men](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9812520)

Open image viewer

Women will be allowed into combat roles in Britain. Photo: Andy Rain / EPA

In Britain, women will be allowed into combat roles in the armed forces next year. Next year women will be taken into at least the infantry units of the air force and the navy. The government decided earlier that women can be hired for all military roles.

The change has also been opposed. A former military commander says that women are weak and not suited to combat roles. For example, a combat pack weighs 45 kilos. Studies have found that women also get injured more easily than men. Women must therefore pass strict physical tests before they can take combat roles.

## Basketball interests Finns

![Lauri Markkanen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9806983)

Open image viewer

Lauri Markkanen is Finland's basketball star. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

Basketball is now a very popular sport in Finland. 19,000 enthusiasts have already got a player licence, that is, a competition licence. The number has grown by over 3,000 in a few years.

The popularity of basketball has grown because the Finnish national team has done well. In addition, Lauri Markkanen got to play in the professional league NBA, and this has increased the popularity of the sport.

The basketball European Championships are being held in Finland right now. Finland won its first game in overtime against France.

## A big asteroid passes the Earth

A big asteroid, that is, a space rock, is moving past the Earth. The asteroid is passing so close to the Earth that it can be seen with a telescope.

The asteroid is over 4 kilometres long.
