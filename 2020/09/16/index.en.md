The Finnish state budget. Corona is dangerous for the elderly. Koronavilkku. Residence permits. Weather forecast on the storm.

## The Finnish state is borrowing a lot of money

![Minister of Education Li Andersson, Minister of the Interior Maria Ohisalo, Prime Minister Sanna Marin, Minister of Science and Culture Annika Saarikko and Minister of Justice Anna-Maja Henriksson at the government's budget negotiations press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11548038)

The government told about the budget proposal in the afternoon. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Finnish government has finished a proposal for how the state will use money next year.

The state's budget for next year, that is, the economic plan, has a deficit of 10.7 billion. That means expenses are much bigger than income.

That is why the Finnish state has to borrow a lot of money.

Handling the corona epidemic takes a lot of state money. For corona tests, 1.4 billion euros has been set aside for next year.

With that sum, 30,000 tests can be done every day.

## Corona is more dangerous than flu

![A person blowing their nose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5097473)

Open image viewer

The coronavirus is more dangerous for the elderly than ordinary flu, health authorities remind. Photo: Arja Lento / Yle

Finnish health authorities remind people that the coronavirus is more dangerous for the elderly than ordinary flu.

Because of the coronavirus, fewer people have fallen ill this autumn than in the spring. However, the coronavirus has not become weaker, the health authorities say.

In Finland, 25 new corona infections have been found today.

## Koronavilkku is popular

![A phone in a person's hand with the Koronavilkku app.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5178,h_2913,x_0,y_421/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7141595f4e35d8dc7ef/1654083412)

Open image viewer

Koronavilkku is a free app for the phone. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

2 million Finns have downloaded Koronavilkku.

Koronavilkku is a phone app that tells the user of the phone if they may have been exposed to the coronavirus.

With the help of Koronavilkku, the chains of coronavirus infection can be traced.

## Getting a residence permit takes a long time

![Workers at a building site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4705,h_2647,x_855,y_365/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5752935d1dae864c8db/1562226703)

Open image viewer

Construction needs workers from abroad. Photo: Karoliina Simoinen / Yle

Finland needs workers, and the Finnish government wants people from abroad to move to Finland to work too.

However, moving to Finland is difficult, because getting a residence permit takes a long time.

The goal of the Finnish authorities is that a person gets a residence permit within a month if they come to Finland to work.

The processing time is, however, 3 months on average.

For example, construction companies want workers from abroad.

But it is difficult, when getting a residence permit takes so long. The Finnish Immigration Service Migri hopes that it gets more staff to make residence permit decisions.

## Autumn storm tests Finland

![The wind battering Eiranranta in Helsinki on 26 September 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11547315)

Still weather information.

An autumn storm will test Finland during the night and tomorrow, Thursday.

Especially in Central and Southern Finland the wind will be strong and gusty.

Tomorrow, Thursday, it will rain a lot in the eastern part of the country.

The storm wind can knock down many trees, because the ground is still soft.

Usually the strong storms come to Finland only in November and December.
