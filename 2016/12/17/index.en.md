## Janitskin to court in Finland

![Ilja Janitskin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9194906)

Open image viewer

Ilja Janitskin. Photo: Yle

Ilja Janitskin, the founder of the online magazine MV, will probably be brought to Finland for trial. A Spanish court decided that Janitskin will be handed over to Finland. Janitskin has lived in Spain in recent years.

Janitskin is suspected of having committed many crimes. For example, Janitskin may have incited hatred against a group of people, that is, spoken badly about some group.

The MV magazine is known for opposing immigration and insulting immigrants.

## The bomb attack in Turkey

![A bus destroyed in a bomb attack in Kayseri, Turkey, on 17 December 2016. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9359139)

Open image viewer

A bus carrying soldiers was destroyed in the bomb attack. Photo: EPA

A bomb attack has been carried out in Turkey. The attack was carried out against a bus that was carrying soldiers. 13 people died, and about 50 people were injured.

The bomb attack was carried out in central Turkey, in Kayseri. On the bus there were soldiers who had a day off. They were on their way to the market square.

The previous bomb attack in Turkey happened only a week ago.

## Beijing's air

![Beijing in smog.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9298536)

Open image viewer

Beijing's air is often dirty. Photo: Mika Mäkeläinen / Yle

In China's capital Beijing, the air is really dirty. There is so much pollution in the air that breathing it is unhealthy.

The Beijing authorities have given an alert because of air pollution. That means that factories must stop work for a while or at least pollute less. Construction work and traffic are also being reduced. The orders are in force until Wednesday.

Dirty air often bothers the residents of Beijing.

## Operation Snowflake

![Operation Snowflake in Tampere](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9359449)

Open image viewer

Operation Snowflake in Tampere. Photo: Kai Pohjanen / Yle

Operation Snowflake has started on Finland's roads. It helps drivers who have problems in Christmas traffic. At Christmas time there is much more traffic than usual.

Drivers need help when, for example, they run out of petrol or a tyre breaks. If it is cold at Christmas time, cars can also freeze.

The helpers of Operation Snowflake are volunteers. The organiser of the operation is the Automobile and Touring Club of Finland.

## Christmas atmosphere

![A Christmas candle in the courtyard of the crafts quarter in Joensuu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5689222)

Open image viewer

What brings the Christmas atmosphere? Photo: YLE / Janne Ahjopalo

Christmas atmosphere is an important thing for Finns. But what actually is Christmas atmosphere?

Psychologist Kirsi Salonen says that many people build a Christmas atmosphere, for example, with candles, colours and smells. However, it is not worth looking for Christmas atmosphere only on a shop shelf, Salonen says.

Making a Christmas atmosphere can cause stress and arguments. It is worth taking it easy sometimes and going out into nature, for example, psychologist Salonen says. An important part of Christmas atmosphere is calming down.
