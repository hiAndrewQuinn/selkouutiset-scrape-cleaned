# Thursday 15.8.2019 (radio)

Female-dominated sectors demand bigger pay rises than export sectors. Many Finns have already bought a trip for next winter. White is the new favourite colour in Finnish cars. Foreign reindeer herders were hired in Kuusamo. The Night of the Arts is held on Thursday.

## Pay negotiations

![Nurses in a hospital corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10905392)

Open image viewer

Many women with low pay work in the social and health sector. Photo: Karoliina Simoinen / Yle

The next pay negotiations will start soon. The export sectors, that is, the sectors that sell products abroad, will negotiate first.

Later, negotiations will take place in the public sectors where many women work. For example, many women work in the social and health sector.

Female-dominated sectors are already demanding bigger pay rises than the export sectors. The reason is equality. In female-dominated sectors pay is lower than in export sectors.

It may be that women's pay becomes a contested issue in the pay negotiations.

## Winter trips

![Playa de las Américas, Tenerife.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10062562)

Open image viewer

The Canary Islands are a favourite destination for Finns. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Many Finns have already bought a trip for next winter. Travel agencies have sold a lot of trips to the south.

About 400,000 Finns will travel south next winter. The favourite destinations are the Canary Islands and Thailand.

A new favourite destination for Finns is Cape Verde. It is in the Atlantic Ocean, about 500 kilometres from Africa.

Flights from Finland to the southern destinations are long. Flights warm the climate. However, that has not reduced Finns' desire to fly.

## Car colours

![Car](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10921547)

Open image viewer

Buyers now like white cars. Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

White is the new favourite colour in Finnish cars. Almost 30 per cent of new cars are white. Grey and black cars are also sold a lot.

Red cars are still popular, but Finns no longer buy them as much as before.

The information is from the Finnish Transport and Communications Agency.

## Reindeer herders

![A woman leads two reindeer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10923893)

Open image viewer

Brazilian Suzana Paquete has worked with reindeer in Finland. Photo: Yle

The Kuusamo tourism association has hired 2 foreign reindeer herders this summer.

The herders have looked after the reindeer and taken them around to tourist destinations. At the same time they have written blogs, which make Finland known around the world.

One reindeer herder is Jamaican-German Amelie Le Brun, and the other is Brazilian Suzana Paquete. They have enjoyed being with the reindeer very much.

## The Night of the Arts

![Artist Veikko Törmänen in his workshop during the Night of the Arts 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10923984)

Artist Veikko Törmänen takes part in the Night of the Arts in Oulu. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

Today, Thursday, the Night of the Arts is held. The event is in Helsinki, Turku and Oulu.

There are many different cultural events at the Night of the Arts. People can take part in hundreds of events.

The Night of the Arts is already an old event. In Helsinki and Turku the Night of the Arts is now 30 years old.
