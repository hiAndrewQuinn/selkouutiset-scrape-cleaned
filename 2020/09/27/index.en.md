## Work bicycles interest Finns

![Development manager Veera Luostarinen has been commuting on a leased bicycle provided by her employer Sponda for a year and a half.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11565492)

Work bicycles interest Finns.

From next year, a work bicycle is tax-free up to 1,200 euros.

In practice, the employer acquires a bicycle for the employee to use. The employee does not have to pay tax on the bicycle if its use value is at most 100 euros a month.

Tax revenue will decrease by 4 million euros if 10,000 employees take up the full tax benefit.

## Many egg cells and sperm are donated again

![Sperm bank in Denmark.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10092484)

Open image viewer

Sperm bank in Denmark. Photo: Riikka Uosukainen / Yle

This year, a record number of egg and sperm donors have come to public health care.

In the public sector, donation treatments were stopped in 2016. The treatments were started again at the end of last year.

A healthy woman under 36 years old is suitable as an egg donor. A healthy man under 46 years old is suitable as a sperm donor.

## Denmark's corona app has been a disappointment

![A cyclist on the metro in Copenhagen, the capital of Denmark.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11553308)

Open image viewer

A cyclist on the metro in Copenhagen, the capital of Denmark. Photo: Olafur Steinar Rye Gestsson / EPA

Denmark's corona app works badly on mobile devices. In 2 months, only 8 new infections have been found with the help of the app.

The app is meant to send a warning to everyone who has been in contact with a person who has caught the infection.

Almost 1.4 million Danes have downloaded the app in total. However, only about 600 warnings have been sent in July to September.

The authorities promise to fix the app.

## Mushroom spores can cause an allergic reaction

![A young Suillus luteus mushroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2100,h_1181,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-3292755eebd6c79a20/1599906125)

Open image viewer

Suillus luteus are gilled mushrooms whose spores ripen in the autumn. Photo: Yle, Risto Salovaara

Spores of forest mushrooms can cause symptoms similar to hay fever.

A spore is a cell that is specialised in reproduction or spreading. Many organisms and plants have spores.

Bolete, Russula and Lactarius mushrooms develop spores that can cause symptoms in sensitive people.

People who are allergic to mushroom spores usually already suffer from several different allergies. However, they get symptoms from spores fairly rarely.

## This week has been Deaf Week

![A woman signing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11531077)

Open image viewer

Sign language is the mother tongue of Deaf people. Photo: Valeria Borovskaya / Yle

On Sunday the International Day of Deaf People was celebrated. It has been celebrated since 1958.

Later the day was extended to its present form, and that is why Deaf Week has been celebrated this week. In Finland the festive broadcast was organised online because of the corona epidemic.

## Northern lights can now be seen in the south

![Northern lights photographed at Syrjävaara in Kaava.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_150/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7153985f522c080396e/1599221515)

Now it is possible to see northern lights in the sky also further south than Lapland. This is due to good space weather.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that beautiful northern lights have been seen at the weekend as far as Tampere at least.

From the night between Sunday and Monday there will be quite a clear sky in Southern and Central Finland. Some light fog may form, however.
