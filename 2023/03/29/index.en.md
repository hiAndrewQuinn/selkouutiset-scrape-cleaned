## Cleaners' strike

The strike by cleaners and property maintenance workers begins tomorrow.

Yesterday the National Conciliator made a settlement proposal in the labour dispute, but the employees' organisation, the Service Sector Union PAM, and the employers' organisation, Property Employers, rejected it.

27,000 cleaners and property maintenance workers in 18 different companies will go on strike at midnight. The strike lasts 3 days, that is, until Saturday.

The property service sector has already had 2 strikes before this.

## End of the parliamentary term

![President Sauli Niinistö commenting to the media in front of Parliament after the end of the parliamentary term on 29 March 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3984,h_2241,x_0,y_270/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-109196664242a196bac2/1680091736)

President Sauli Niinistö declared the parliamentary term ended. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The current Parliament has ended its work. The end-of-term celebrations of the parliamentary term were held today with ceremony.

At noon there was a church service for Parliament in Helsinki Cathedral. After that, in the great hall of Parliament, President Sauli Niinistö declared the parliamentary term ended. Finally, Speaker Matti Vanhanen offered end-of-term coffee to the MPs in the State Room.

In the speeches, people remembered how unusual this parliamentary term has been. The term included, for example, the corona pandemic, Russia's attack on Ukraine and Finland's application to join the NATO military alliance.

A new Parliament will be elected in the elections next Sunday. It will begin its work two weeks later.

## Finland's NATO membership

![Erdogan and Sauli Niinistö in front of a NATO flag in a photo manipulation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-108662064144da33880d/1679890092)

Finland's NATO membership will be confirmed perhaps tomorrow. Then the Turkish Parliament may ratify, that is, approve, Finland's membership.

Hungary approved Finland's membership on Monday, so Turkey is now the last country whose ratification of Finland is missing.

When the Turkish Parliament has approved Finland's NATO membership, the Turkish President must still confirm the decision.

![Frozen Lake Näsijärvi seen from the shore in the early morning dusk. Further away the moonlight shines, next to which stands Näsinneula.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1024,h_576,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-104466963903dec725f3/1670397559)

Tampere is Finland's most attractive city. Tampere was the most attractive city for the third time in a row.

In the T-Media study, people assessed Finland's 10 largest cities. 1,400 Finns answered the survey.

In the survey Tampere received excellent grades, for example for its location and economic vitality.

After Tampere the most attractive cities were Kuopio, Oulu, Jyväskylä and Turku.
