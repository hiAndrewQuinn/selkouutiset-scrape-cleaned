# Tuesday 1.1.2019 (radio)

The President gave the traditional New Year's speech. The police and rescue services had a lot of work on New Year's Eve. Finland's name law has changed. Tonight there will be a storm at sea.

## The President's New Year's speech

![President Sauli Niinistö recording the New Year's speech.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10577754)

President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö has given the traditional New Year's speech.

In the speech Niinistö dealt with many things. For example, he defended European values, democracy and popular rule. At the same time he warned against extremist movements such as anarchists and Nazis.

Niinistö said that Europe is now living through a period of upheaval. It requires the ability to listen and to try to understand the opinions of others. It requires the ability to reconcile different views.

The President also took a position on immigration and asylum seekers. He said that people who are suffering distress must be helped. However, people who arrive in the country must have the will to adapt to Finnish society and behave in line with Finland's laws and values.

The President also spoke about climate change. He said that fighting climate change requires giving things up. However, people are adaptable and resourceful. A new good thing can thus be even better than before, the President said.

## New Year's celebrations

![fireworks in the sky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10573793)

Open image viewer

About 5 people got an eye injury from fireworks this year. Photo: AOP

The New Year's celebrations have caused a lot of work for the police and rescue services. Hundreds of reports came to emergency response centres across the country during the night.

Fights and assaults, drunk drivers and home visits gave the police work.

The South-Western Finland police say that the misuse of fireworks also caused a lot of work. People fired rockets at other people, cars and houses.

At least 1 person has got a serious eye injury from fireworks. In addition, a few moderate eye injuries have been treated in hospitals.

## Name law changed

![Choosing a name with a dart from a calendar.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10352492)

Open image viewer

A child can now be given 4 first names. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Finland's name law has changed.

From today, a person may have 4 first names. Before, a person could have at most 3 first names.

The law change also brings more choice for surnames. Now both spouses can take a combined name, that is, a name in which their own surname and their spouse's surname are combined. Both may also keep their own names, but a child can still be given a surname that is a combination of the parents' surnames.

## Storm at sea

![Strong wind at sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8552699)

Open image viewer

There will be a storm at sea tonight. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

An unusually strong storm is expected at sea from the evening onwards.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute has issued a warning at sea of a storm and high waves. Around midnight the wind at sea may blow as strong as 27 metres per second.

Meteorologist Eveliina Tuovinen says that a storm this strong is experienced at most once a year.
