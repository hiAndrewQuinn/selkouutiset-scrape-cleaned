# Saturday 29.2.2020 (radio)

There are refugees at the border between Turkey and Greece who want to get into Europe. The Finns Party and its youth organisation are arguing. It was Leap Day on Saturday. Yle will show films directed by women in March.

## The border between Turkey and the EU

![Clash at the Greece-Turkey border on 29 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11234820)

Greek police are not letting refugees across the border from Turkey. Photo: Ozan Kose / AFP

The situation at the border between Turkey and Greece is difficult. There are refugees at the border who want to get into Europe.

Turkey has decided to open the border for refugees, but Greece is not letting refugees across the border. There is unrest at the border.

In Turkey there are, for example, 4 million Syrians who have fled the war. The European Union has had an agreement with Turkey that Turkey will not let refugees into the EU area.

However, the Turkish president said on Saturday that there are already so many refugees that Turkey can no longer look after them.

## Finns Party youth

![Chairman of the Finns Party youth, Asseri Kinnunen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11234723)

Chairman of the Finns Party youth, Asseri Kinnunen. Photo: Mesut Turan / Lehtikuva

The Finns Party is in a dispute. The Finns Party and the Finns Party youth organisation disagree about matters.

The Finns Party does not like that there are far-right and racist opinions in the youth organisation. For example, a week ago the deputy chair of the youth organisation said that he is a fascist.

The Finns Party now wants to supervise the young people's activities. That is why the party wants more power over the youth organisation's affairs. However, the youth organisation has not agreed to this.

The dispute was on the agenda at the Finns Party youth organisation's meeting on Saturday. No solution has been found yet.

## Leap Day

![An old sunflower fabric.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_720,h_405,x_0,y_438/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6114905dcc09ded701b/1573654136)

Open image viewer

On Leap Day a woman can get a husband or a dress fabric. Photo: Ville Välimäki / Yle

Today, Saturday, is 29 February, that is, Leap Day. Leap Day comes every 4th year. Usually February has 28 days.

Leap Day is needed so that the seasons stay in the right place in the calendar. The Earth goes around the sun in 1 year and 6 hours. That is why an extra day builds up in 4 years.

There is an old tradition linked to Leap Day. On Leap Day a woman could also propose, that is, ask a man to marry her. Usually the man proposed to the woman.

The Leap Day tradition went on like this: if a man did not want to get married, he had to buy the woman a dress fabric.
