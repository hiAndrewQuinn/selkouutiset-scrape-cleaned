# Monday 17.12.2018 (radio)

Very few redundancies this year. Less money is being spent on Christmas. A poor grain harvest may affect the availability of hams. Firms do not pay bills on time. More drones are used in the war in the Middle East. Snow makes the roads slippery.

Few redundancies

![A woman at an employment office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10146011)

Open image viewer

There is already a shortage of skilled labour, and redundancies remain at 3,500 this year. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The good economic situation in Finland has reduced redundancies.
There are about 3,500 redundancies this year. The number is small. Ten years ago firms made 15,000 people redundant a year. Now exports are pulling, and there is a shortage of workers.

## Saving on Christmas

![Christmas presents in bags.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9980073)

Open image viewer

Christmas costs money. Food and Christmas presents cost money. Photo: Dalbir Ram / Yle

Less money is being spent on Christmas than last year.
30% of Finns say that they are spending less money on Christmas now. Only 6% of Finns say that they are spending more money on Christmas than last year.

On average, 580 euros per adult is spent on Christmas.

## A poor grain harvest may show in the availability of hams

![Organic piglets in a pen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10556768)

Open image viewer

This year there are enough hams, but next year Finnish hams may be available in shops in smaller numbers. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

A poor grain harvest may affect the amount of domestic Christmas hams next year. Piglets eat grain. The harvest was small.

This Christmas there are enough Finnish hams. About 7 million kilograms of ham are eaten in Finland at Christmas.

## Bills are not paid on time

![A pile of bills.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9481263)

Open image viewer

Firms may announce that they will only pay after 60 to 120 days. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Many big firms are slow to pay bills. It is a problem for small firms and entrepreneurs that big firms do not pay bills on time.

Entrepreneurs say that many big firms pay bills only after many months. Small firms have to wait a long time for their money.

By law, a bill must be paid within one month, unless something else is agreed.

## Drones are common in the war in the Middle East

![MQ-1 military drone](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8828360)

Open image viewer

The picture shows the USA's Predator military drone. Photo: Valda Kalina / EPA

The war in the Middle East has changed. A lot of drones are used in the war in the Middle East. China makes military drones for many countries.

Jordan, Iraq and Saudi Arabia have acquired drones. In addition, Israel, Turkey and Iran make their own military drones.

## Snow and frost are coming

![Ice skates.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10482905)

Open image viewer

It is snowing this week, and there is frost across the whole country. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

And a little more about the weather. Snowfall disturbed traffic on Monday in the southern and western parts of the country. Streets and roads are slippery. More snow will fall this week. At times it may rain, and the rain freezes. There is frost across the whole country.
