## Corona situation in Finland

![Corona test sign in Tampere in December 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_164/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7505775fca3f36da10f/1607090828)

The number of corona infections is rising again in Finland. On Wednesday 1 150 new corona infections were reported. That is over 300 more than on Tuesday. There are 6 new corona deaths.

There are about 238 corona patients in hospitals, that is 12 patients more than on Monday.

Eeva Ruotsalainen, deputy chief physician of HUS, the Hospital District of Helsinki and Uusimaa, says that Finns should still use face masks in places where there are other people.

## Corona situation in Germany

![Sign informing that it is mandatory to wear a face mask outside the town hall in Augsburg.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_244/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7893296060ce58c9fac/1616957116)

Open image viewer

Mask recommendation at a bus stop in Germany. Photo: Johnny Sjöblom / Yle

The corona situation has also worsened in Germany. Germany's health minister Jens Pahn says that a lot of people who have no corona vaccination are getting corona disease in Germany.

In Germany only about 66 per cent of Germans have received a corona vaccination. Germany's health minister says that intensive care places may run out in German hospitals, because there are many corona patients.

The health minister says that restrictions may need to be introduced in Germany for events and public premises.

## Security of supply

![Emergency stock of the Security of Supply Centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2560,h_1440,x_0,y_240/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7815056042175085bf7/1636026263)

Open image viewer

Finland's Security of Supply Centre's stores contain, for example, hospital supplies and many other important supplies. Photo: Huoltovarmuuskeskus / Försörjningsberedskapscentralen

The Nordic countries are strengthening cooperation on security of supply. The Nordic prime ministers have agreed on the matter.

Security of supply means preparing for different exceptional situations. Also in exceptional situations people must get water, food, medicines and energy. Transport must work and digital networks must work.

The Nordic countries, that is, Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Iceland, want to develop security of supply together.

## Raahe gold mine

![Excavators and transport trucks dig gold ore at the Raahe mine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-87493361825122bf144/1635930617)

Open image viewer

Raahe gold mine. Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

The opening of a gold mine has been celebrated in Raahe in North-western Finland. The Canadian mining company Otso Gold has started mining operations in Raahe.

The previous mining company stopped in Raahe two years ago. The new mining company says that it knows what problems the previous mining company had. That is why the ways of operating have been changed.

About 260 people work at the Raahe gold mine. They come from about 20 different countries. The Raahe gold mine intends to mine more than 2500 kilograms of gold a year.
