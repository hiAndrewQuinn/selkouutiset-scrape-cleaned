## Foreign workers

Finland needs workers from abroad. That is what the Confederation of Finnish Industries, known as EK, says. EK is an employers' organisation.

Yle interviewed the chief executive of EK, Jyri Häkämies, on Saturday. Häkämies said that Finnish companies also need workers from outside Europe.

Häkämies hopes that the labour shortage will also be remembered in the government negotiations. Immigration is one of the disputed topics as the parties negotiate a new government.

For example, the Finns Party has proposed that foreign workers must have such a good salary in Finland that they do not need benefits. According to the Finns Party, a good enough salary is 3000 euros a month or more.

EK chief executive Jyri Häkämies says that the demand is too high. For example, workers are needed from abroad for the care sector, but pay in the sector is under 3000 euros.

US President Joe Biden may visit Finland. That is what the American NBC channel says.

The channel says that Biden may intend to visit Finland in the summer. Biden will attend the NATO defence alliance meeting in Lithuania in July.

If Biden visits Finland in the summer, he may also meet leaders of other Nordic countries here.

## May Day begins

![Veikko Jokelainen sells May Day balloons at Ullanlinnanmäki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5114,h_2876,x_0,y_465/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-948685626e3e0733f9c/1651392078)

May Day is celebrated on Sunday and Monday. May Day is a celebration of students and workers.

On Sunday, which is May Eve, students organise May Day events in various parts of Finland.

For example, in Helsinki the statue of Havis Amanda, also called Manta, gets a student cap. The event starts at Market Square at 18:00.

On Monday, which is May Day itself, students and other May Day revellers gather in parks for picnics. Political parties also organise events on May Day.

Many Finns celebrate May Day with a student cap on their head. The custom is old, but today white student caps are also part of May Day. A student cap shows that its owner has passed the matriculation examination.

## The weather

![Sunday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1106443644d2d9974d60/1682779582)

The weather on Sunday, which is May Eve, is cool and cloudy.

It will rain on May Eve in the southern and central parts of the country. The temperature is below 10 degrees across the whole country.

On Monday, which is May Day, the rain will ease. In most of the country the weather is dry.

On May Day the weather also warms slightly and the sun may shine. The temperature rises to about 10 degrees in the south.
