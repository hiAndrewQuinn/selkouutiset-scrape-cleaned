# Tuesday 5.6.2018 (radio)

A cigarette butt is dangerous rubbish. Plastic waste is good to recycle. There are many applicants to the police college. There was a serious volcanic eruption in Guatemala. Alien species grow in Finnish nature. Today was a cool day.

## The cigarette butt is a common litter

![Cigarette butts on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6566125)

Open image viewer

Cigarette butts contain plastic and many poisons. Photo: Anu Rummukainen / Yle

A cigarette butt is a very common piece of rubbish. A cigarette butt is dangerous for nature. Cigarette butts contain plastic and many poisons.

Animals eat plastic and poisons while they eat in nature. Plastic and poisons also travel with the water into the sea.

## Plastic waste into collection containers

![Plastic waste in a waste container.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9597762)

Open image viewer

There is a lot of plastic waste at home. Many people take plastic waste from home to collection containers. Photo: Mari Kahila / Yle

There is a very large amount of plastic waste at home. Many people take plastic waste from home to collection containers.

Plastic is collected because plastic can be used again.

When old plastic is recycled, factories do not need to make as much new plastic.

Plastic factories produce a lot of emissions, which are dangerous for the whole planet.

## Many applicants to the police college

![Students at the Police University College.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5074254)

Open image viewer

You can study to become a police officer at the police college. There are many applicants to the police college. Photo: YLE

You can study to become a police officer at the police college. There have been many applicants to the police college.

1,500 people applied to the police college. 400 people get to study.

Men and women can apply to the police college. In Finland you can also study to become a police officer in Swedish.

## Volcanic eruption in Guatemala

![The Fuego volcano erupting in Guatemala on 3 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10236563)

Open image viewer

The volcano produced an ash cloud many kilometres high. Photo: Conred / AFP

There has been a serious volcanic eruption in Guatemala in Central America.

Lava erupted from the volcano. The volcano also produced an ash cloud that was many kilometres high. The ash covered people and the whole of nature. Rescuers are trying to find people who were buried under the ash.

Many people have died.

## Alien species in Finnish nature

![Giant balsam](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9714770)

Open image viewer

Giant balsam does not belong in Finnish nature. Photo: Panu Kunttu / WWF

Plants grow in Finnish nature that do not really belong in Finnish nature. They are called alien species.

Alien species spread quickly and take space from other plants.

Alien species are now growing well, because the weather is warm.

One alien species is giant balsam. Giant balsam came to Finland from the Himalayas.

## Today was a cool day

A fleece can be placed over plants. Under the fleece the plant can withstand cold weather. Photo: AOP

Today was a cool day. During the day there was also a strong wind.

At night the wind calms down. At night there may be frost.

Tomorrow, Wednesday, the weather will warm up again.
