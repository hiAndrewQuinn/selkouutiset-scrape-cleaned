## Municipal sector wages

![Municipal sector demonstrators in Töölö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4006,h_2253,x_0,y_538/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9513716274fb1170dc5/1651833962)

Open image viewer

There were demonstrations in the municipal sector in the spring. Now the disputes are over. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

Wages in the municipal sector have been agreed. The municipal sector gets the same pay rises as most other sectors.

In addition, a special pay programme is coming to the municipal sector. The pay programme lasts 5 years, and during it wages in the municipal sector rise by about 5 per cent more in total than in other sectors.

A large group of municipal sector workers is included in the agreement, for example teachers and bus drivers.

The agreement does not, however, cover the health care sector. The negotiations of nurses continue later.

## Car drives into a crowd

![A car crashed through a shop window into the store.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7357,h_4119,x_0,y_766/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96690162a07a44e2777/1654684419)

Open image viewer

The car finally crashed into the shop window. Photo: Filip Singer / EPA

In Germany a car has driven into a crowd of people. At least 1 person died and 12 people were injured.

The incident happened in the German capital, Berlin. A car drove over people on a shopping street. After that the car crashed into a shop window.

The police have arrested the driver of the car. He is a 29-year-old German-Armenian man.

The police are now investigating whether the driver drove into people on purpose.

## Early potatoes

![Early potato harvest in hands](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_402/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6813095ecd40be26bf2/1590509877)

Open image viewer

Early potatoes ripen early. Photo: Kalle Mäkelä / Yle

Early potatoes are growing well now, and early potatoes are getting into shops. Still in spring, cold weather slowed the growth of early potatoes.

This year, however, is difficult for early potato farmers. For example, fertilisers are now 20 per cent more expensive than last year.

Early potatoes ripen earlier than other potatoes, already during spring and early summer. Early potatoes are a popular festive food. Finns eat early potatoes especially at Midsummer celebrations.
