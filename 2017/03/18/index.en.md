# Saturday 18.3.2017 (radio)

Merja Kyllönen is the Left Alliance presidential candidate. There was a shooting at a French airport on Saturday. More tourists are coming to Finland than before. It is not yet worth changing to summer tyres.

## Left Alliance presidential candidate

![Merja Kyllönen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9435945)

Open image viewer

Merja Kyllönen is currently an MEP in the European Parliament. Photo: Nick Tulinen

The Left Alliance has chosen its presidential candidate. The Left Alliance's presidential candidate is Merja Kyllönen.

Merja Kyllönen is 40 years old, but she has been involved in politics for a long time. Kyllönen has been, for example, Finland's Minister of Transport and an MP in the Finnish Parliament. Today she is an MEP in the European Parliament.

The Left Alliance is the third party that has chosen its own candidate for the presidential election. The Centre and the Greens have already said earlier who their candidate is. The other parties have not yet chosen a candidate.

The presidential election will be held at the beginning of next year, that is in January 2018. The current president, Sauli Niinistö, has not yet said whether he will try to become president a second time. In Finland the president's term lasts 6 years, and one person can be elected president 2 times.

## French shooting

![People and emergency vehicles outside the terminals of Orly airport on 18 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9518126)

Open image viewer

Orly airport was closed because of the shooting. Photo: Christophe Petit Tesson / EPA

There was a shooting at a French airport on Saturday. A man was shot at the airport because he tried to take a gun from a soldier. The man died. No one else died or was injured.

This happened at Orly airport near Paris. The airport was closed for some time, and about 3,000 people were taken to safety from the airport. The police checked that the man had not left a bomb at the airport.

The same man shot at a police officer early on Saturday morning in Paris and then fled the scene. The police officer was not badly injured.

The man was known to the police. He had also committed crimes before.

## Number of tourists

![A Chinese traveller in the Arctic Circle](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8573383)

Open image viewer

Lapland interests Chinese tourists. Photo: Jarmo Honkanen / Yle

More tourists are coming to Finland than before. In January, almost 20 percent more foreign tourists stayed overnight in Finland than a year earlier.

The number of Chinese tourists has grown the most. However, the largest group is Russians. Chinese and Russian tourists visit Lapland a lot.

## Not yet summer tyres

![Changing tyres.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8827429)

Open image viewer

It is not yet time to change to summer tyres. Photo: Antti Mikkola / Yle

The snow has almost completely melted from southern Finland.

However, meteorologist Seija Paasonen says that you should not yet change to summer tyres on your car. It is still winter weather in March. Although the roads are dry in the daytime, frost can make the roads slippery at night.

In northern Finland there is still a lot of snow. In Lapland, for example, at Kilpisjärvi there is still 125 centimetres of snow.
