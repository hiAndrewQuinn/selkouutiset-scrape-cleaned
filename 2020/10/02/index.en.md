# Friday 2.10.2020 (radio)

US President Donald Trump has a corona infection. The number of corona infections is growing in Finland. The government has won Parliament's confidence. The ski season has started in Northern Finland. The weekend weather is warm.

## Trump caught corona

![US President Donald Trump arrived at the White House on Thursday 1 October 2020, just before he said he had caught the coronavirus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1969,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7260325f7740de3b6b9/1601651765)

US President Donald Trump. Photo: EPA-EFE/Yuri Gripas / POOL

The President of the USA, Donald Trump, has caught the coronavirus. The President told about it himself on Twitter.

Trump said that Mrs Melania Trump also has a corona infection. The President and Mrs Trump started quarantine straight away, that is, they do not meet other people.

Trump's doctor says that the presidential couple is doing well. The President is working from home.

Now people are wondering how the US presidential election will go. The election is due to be held at the beginning of November.

## There have been more corona infections

The number of corona infections is growing in Finland.

On Friday it was reported that there are 141 new corona infections. In total, over 10,200 corona infections have been found in Finland.

On Friday 1 new corona death was also reported. In total, 345 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

## Parliament has confidence in the government

![MPs getting ready to vote in the plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki on 2 October 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11576020)

Open image viewer

More than half of the MPs voted for the government in Parliament on Friday. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The government has won Parliament's confidence. On Friday Parliament voted on whether it still has confidence in the government.

The subject of the vote was the government's economic policy. The opposition parties think that the government has handled economic policy badly. That is why they voted against the government.

However, the government parties have more MPs in Parliament, and the government won the vote. That is why the government can stay in power.

## The ski season started in the north

![The first skiers on the Levi slopes on opening day](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7258805f77010b7e01b/1601634653)

The first skiers of the winter on the Levi slopes. Photo: Sauli Antikainen / Yle

The ski season has started in Northern Finland. The ski slopes of Levi and Ruka were opened on Friday.

The slopes have snow from last winter, which has been kept over the summer.

The tourism sector in Northern Finland has also received other good news. The German airline Lufthansa and the Swiss airline Swiss have decided to fly from Central Europe to Northern Finland. They have many direct flights to Northern Finland this winter.
