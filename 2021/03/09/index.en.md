# Tuesday 9.3.2021 (radio)

Many crashes have happened in the snowstorm in Southern Finland. Valmet Automotive is recruiting a thousand new workers. Housing companies are building charging points for electric cars. Nearly half a million Finns have received the coronavirus vaccine. Weather report.

## Snowstorm brings a wave of crashes

![Several cars in a chain-reaction crash in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11828318)

Open image viewer

A chain-reaction crash involving dozens of cars happened on Turunväylä in Espoo. Photo: Eyewitness image

Many crashes have happened today in Southern Finland.

In Hollola, a crash involving 6 cars happened in the morning. 7 people were injured.

In Vantaa a lorry fell off a bridge on the Hämeenlinna motorway. 2 cars were also involved in the accident. The lorry driver was seriously injured.

On the Turku motorway in Espoo there was a chain-reaction crash involving several dozen cars. At least ten people were injured.

In addition, dozens of smaller crashes, rear-end collisions and run-off-road accidents have happened.

Driving conditions in Southern Finland have been dangerously poor. The roads are icy and snow has been blowing all day.

## Valmet Automotive is recruiting

![Valmet Automotive's battery factory in Salo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5889,h_3313,x_71,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6141965dd65455cb0eb/1615280396)

Valmet Automotive's battery factory in Salo. Photo: Valmet Automotive

The car manufacturer Valmet Automotive is recruiting many new workers. The company is hiring about a thousand new workers and white-collar staff.

New workers are needed for car and battery manufacturing. The jobs are at Valmet Automotive's factories in Salo and Uusikaupunki.

The company says that more workers are needed because the order volumes for cars and batteries have grown quickly.

## Charging points for electric cars

![An electric car being put on charge.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_432/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6953955efc859150fa9/1593607901)

Open image viewer

An electric car being put on charge. Photo: Arttu Timonen / Yle

Housing companies are eagerly building charging points for electric cars.

The Real Estate Federation says that about 30 percent of housing companies are now building charging points. In addition, the most important project for many housing companies in the coming years is to change parking spaces so that they suit electric cars.

The Housing Finance and Development Centre of Finland, ARA, gives grants for building charging points. So many applications come in that last year the grant money ran out before the year was over. This may happen again this year.

## Coronavirus vaccinations are progressing

![The figure of a person wearing a mask is reflected in the glass door of a coronavirus vaccination point, which says "coronavirus vaccination".](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4293,h_2415,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-778387603767e07410f/1614244044)

Vaccination against the coronavirus is progressing.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that about 65 percent of Finns over 80 years old have already been vaccinated. Of all Finns, about 9 percent have received at least one vaccine dose.

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health and THL say that all adults may receive at least the first vaccine dose already during the summer.

Today about 600 new coronavirus infections and 2 new coronavirus deaths have been found.
