## Coronavirus restrictions in the Helsinki area and Uusimaa

![Helsinki Mayor Jan Vapaavuori at the press briefing on the coronavirus epidemic situation of the City of Helsinki and HUS at Helsinki City Hall on Tuesday 24 November.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11662861)

Open image viewer

Helsinki Mayor Jan Vapaavuori spoke about the coronavirus restrictions. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

New coronavirus restrictions are coming to the Helsinki area. The restrictions apply to Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa and Kauniainen.

It is important that all people now avoid unnecessary meetings with other people.

All events with more than 10 people are banned.

All hobbies for adults are stopped, and indoor hobbies for children are stopped.

All public places of the municipalities will be closed. Libraries will provide some services.

Upper secondary schools and vocational schools will move to distance teaching.

In addition, health authorities hope that people will only meet members of their own family. Family celebrations must not be held.

The restrictions start on Monday. They are in force until 20 December.

Many municipalities in Uusimaa are also following the restrictions.

Today, 618 new coronavirus infections have been reported in Finland.

That is the largest number for one day in Finland during the whole coronavirus epidemic.

More than half of the infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa area.

## Maradona was buried

![Maradona was buried in the Bella Vista cemetery on the outskirts of Buenos Aires.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1980,h_1114,x_0,y_102/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7470465fc044aa6f0ed/1606436238)

Open image viewer

Football player Diego Maradona was buried. Photo: AFP / Lehtikuva

The Argentine football player Diego Maradona was buried on Thursday. The funeral was small. Close relatives of Maradona took part.

Maradona died of a heart attack. He was 60 years old.

Tens of thousands of people went to see Maradona's coffin when it was at the presidential palace in Argentina.
