# Sunday 21.1.2018 (radio)

Most Finns think that the activation model for unemployment benefits is bad. People have died in a hotel attack in Afghanistan. Turkey has attacked northern Syria. Krista Pärmäkoski won the 10-kilometre race in the skiing World Cup.

## The activation model gets no support

![A man queues at the employment office in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10018883)

Open image viewer

In the activation model, an unemployed person must look for work actively. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Most Finns think that the new activation model for unemployment benefits is bad.

The activation model means that an unemployed person gets smaller benefits if he or she does not actively look for work. In the activation model, an unemployed person must, within 3 months, work for 18 hours or, for example, be in training for 5 days. Otherwise the benefit gets smaller.

Taloustutkimus did a survey about the activation model for Yle. Only 37 percent of those who answered thought that the activation model is good. 56 percent of those who answered do not want the activation model to be introduced.

The activation model came into force at the beginning of this year.

## Attack on a hotel in Afghanistan

![soldiers and a smoking hotel](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10033630)

Open image viewer

Then news from abroad.

People have died in a hotel attack in Afghanistan. The attack happened in Afghanistan's capital, Kabul.

A group of men attacked the Hotel Intercontinental on Saturday evening. The men shot at the hotel's guests and staff. Some people managed to escape, but some were taken prisoner by the attackers.

Afghan security forces fought with the attackers. The security forces got the situation under control on Sunday morning.

Many people died in the attack. The extremist group Taliban has announced that it carried out the attack.

## Turkey's attack on Syria

![Map of Syria](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10033581)

Open image viewer

Turkey attacked northern Afghanistan, the Afrin area. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Turkey has attacked northern Syria. Turkey says that it is striking against Kurdish forces and the terrorist group Isis.

People have died in Turkey's attack. Turkey says that all those who died are soldiers of the Kurdish forces. The Kurdish forces say that ordinary people have also died because of Turkey's attack.

Turkey attacked northern Syria at the weekend. Turkey is attacking an area that is held by Kurdish forces.

## World Cup win for Pärmäkoski

![Krista Pärmäkoski](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10033552)

Open image viewer

Skier Krista Pärmäkoski is now in good shape. Photo: Lehtikuva

Finally, a happy sports story.

Finland's Krista Pärmäkoski has won a race in the skiing World Cup. Pärmäkoski won the women's 10-kilometre race. The competitors skied the distance in the classic style.

The race was in Planica, in Slovenia. The second best skier in the race was Sweden's Charlotte Kalla. Third was Norway's Heidi Weng.

Kalla and Weng are skiing superstars, and so Pärmäkoski's win was a good achievement. The win was also Pärmäkoski's first win in the World Cup.
