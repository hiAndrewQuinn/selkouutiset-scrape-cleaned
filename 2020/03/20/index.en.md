# Friday 20.3.2020 (radio)

The government is putting together an aid package for companies. The President asks everyone to follow the instructions. Ski resorts are being closed. Grades 1-3 may go to school. Ski resorts are being closed. The days are getting longer.

## A big support package for the economy

![Krista Kiuru, Sanna Marin and Aino-Kaisa Pekonen at the government's press conference in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11267185)

The government spoke about support measures on Friday. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The government is putting together a big support package for Finland's economy. The support package is 15 billion euros.

The support package will help companies so that their operations do not end because of the coronavirus situation. When companies are helped, many jobs are kept.

Laying off workers is being made easier. Paying unemployment benefits will be speeded up and extended to freelancers and small business owners.

Finland is taking on more loans, so that Finland can help companies and people who are laid off or become unemployed.

## The President appeals to everyone in Finland

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11267525)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö spoke in an interview with Yle. Photo: Juha Kivioja / Yle

The President of the Republic **Sauli Niinistö** asks that all people take part in protecting themselves from the coronavirus.

In an interview with Yle, Niinistö said that everyone must now follow the rules and instructions that the government has given. The instructions say that contact with other people must be avoided.

Not everyone has wanted to follow the rules. Some say that it does not bother them if they fall ill. President Niinistö says that it is dangerous to think like that.

Niinistö says that the rules protect everyone in Finland.

Big ski resorts are being closed because of the coronavirus situation.

There have been many people at the ski resorts. The coronavirus has infected some people at the ski resorts and spread with them to other places.

Many big ski resorts will close next Friday.

## Small pupils may go to school

![A lesson in progress.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10990932)

Open image viewer

Grades 1-3 may go to school. However, it is better for children to be at home, if they can be at home. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

The instruction on closing schools is changing. All pupils in the first, second and third grades may go to face-to-face teaching at school from Monday onwards. A pupil may go to school if, for example, they cannot be alone at home.

The government recommends that children stay at home if that is possible.

This week only the children whose parents work in critical sectors were allowed to go to school. Children who need special support have also been able to be at school this week.

## It is the spring equinox on Friday

![Dandelion](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10696888)

Open image viewer

The dandelion blooms in spring. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Today, Friday, is the spring equinox. The spring equinox means that day and night are equally long everywhere on Earth.

As spring goes on, the days get longer and the nights get shorter. The days get longer and the nights get shorter in Finland and elsewhere in the northern hemisphere.
