# Friday 24.1.2020 (radio)

No coronavirus has been found in Finland. There has been a shooting in Germany. The Finnish government may raise child benefits. Southern Finland is snowless.

## No coronavirus in Finland

![Ivalo health centre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11174778)

Chinese tourists went to the Ivalo health centre because of flu symptoms. Photo: Vesa Toppari / Yle

No coronavirus has been found in Finland. This is what the National Institute for Health and Welfare says.

On Friday morning it was reported that 2 Chinese tourists may have the coronavirus. The tourists were tested, but they did not have the virus.

The tourists are in Ivalo in Finnish Lapland. They come from Wuhan, where the new virus disease may have started. The tourists went to a doctor in Lapland because they had flu symptoms.

The coronavirus causes symptoms similar to flu or pneumonia. In China almost 30 people have died from the coronavirus.

The authorities say that Finns do not need to be afraid. Finland's health care system is ready to act if the coronavirus is found in Finland.

## Shooting in Germany

![Police investigate the crime scene in Rot am See, Germany.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11175145)

There has been a shooting in Germany. The shooter killed several people.

The shooting happened in Root am See. It is a small town in southern Germany.

There was only one shooter. He is a German citizen. The police arrested the shooter.

The reason for the shooting was probably personal. The matter may have been about the shooter's personal relationships.

## Child benefits may be raised

![A 6-day-old girl breastfeeds on a nursing pillow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4878,h_2744,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6318475e2063c829f45/1579181330)

The Finnish government is worried, because very few babies are being born. Photo: Kristiina Lehto / Yle

The Finnish government may raise child benefits. Iltalehti reported on the matter on Friday.

Child benefits are to be raised, because very few children are now being born in Finland.

Statistics Finland has said that last year about 45,000 children were born in Finland. The last time so few children were born was about 150 years ago. Last year more people died than were born.

The government wants more children to be born. That is why it may intend to raise child benefits. Families may have more children if child benefits are bigger.

## Snowless southern Finland

![Snow pictures from Tampere's winter of 2011](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1778,h_1000,x_134,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6341855e29465a7108b/1579763407)

Tampere had this much snow exactly 9 years ago. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

The winter in southern Finland is now snowless, that is, there is no snow at all. A snowless winter is not usual in Finland.

For example, 9 years ago, that is in 2011, there was a lot of snow in January. In Helsinki, Turku and Tampere, for example, there was almost half a metre of snow. There was so much snow that it was hard to keep the city streets open.

The snowless winter now continues in southern Finland. On Sunday it will rain, but in the south the rain will be sleet or rain.
