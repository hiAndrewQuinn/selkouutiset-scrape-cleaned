## Government talks

![Politicians in a large meeting room.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690447)

Open image viewer

The parties started talks on the government on Friday. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The parties' talks on a new government have started.

The talks are led by National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo, because the National Coalition is now the largest party in Parliament.

On Friday Orpo gave the other parties questions about matters that, in the National Coalition's view, are important for the new government. Orpo wants the answers by Tuesday at the latest.

On the basis of the answers, Orpo decides which parties he will try to put together a government with.

Political researcher Markku Jokisipilä estimates that the Finns Party will come into government with the National Coalition. The National Coalition and the Finns Party think quite alike, for example, about state savings.

According to Jokisipilä, a government of the National Coalition and the SDP is not as likely an option.

## Prices at health companies

![A doctor with a stethoscope in hand, and a patient beside him.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690446)

Open image viewer

Private medical clinics must clearly say what their services cost. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Private medical clinics must state prices clearly. This is what the Consumer Ombudsman says. The Consumer Ombudsman's task is to protect the rights of consumers, that is, buyers.

According to the Consumer Ombudsman, health sector companies do not nowadays clearly state how much their services cost. The Consumer Ombudsman has examined the activities of Mehiläinen, Terveystalo and Pihlajalinna.

The Consumer Ombudsman demands that they state prices clearly also in their online services. The customer must see the full price, so that, for example, the evening supplement and the service fee are included.

The price must also be shown on the same page where the customer books an appointment. A link from the appointment page to the price page is not enough, the Consumer Ombudsman says.

Health sector companies have promised to correct the shortcomings.

## Food prices

![Packets of flour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690449)

Open image viewer

For example, the price of flour has risen a lot. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Food has become more expensive. Food was about 16 percent more expensive in March than a year earlier.

The most expensive increases are for sugar, flour and eggs. Their price rose by over 30 percent in a year.

Prices are now rising at a quick pace. The last time food prices rose this fast was about 50 years ago.

Australia has had record-strong wind.

The hurricane brought wind to Australia that blew at up to 289 kilometres per hour.

Australian authorities say that storm winds will not hit big cities with full force.
