# Friday 24.12.2021 (radio)

Christmas peace was declared in Turku. Corona restrictions affect Christmas celebrations across Europe. The UN is helping the Philippines.

Finland has quieted down for Christmas celebrations.

Christmas peace was declared in Turku at noon. Turku city protocol chief Mika Akkanen declared it from the balcony of the Brinkkala house.

Christmas peace was declared in the Old Market Square in Turku without an audience. The reason is the corona restrictions. Christmas peace was also declared without an audience last year.

The declaration of Christmas peace is an old tradition in Finland. Christmas peace was declared in Turku for the first time already in the 1300s. The declaration was broadcast live on the radio for the first time in 1935.

## Corona restrictions in Europe

![People queuing at a pharmacy in Milan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3072,h_1728,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89620261c5967288ccf/1640339187)

In many European countries, corona restrictions affect how Christmas and New Year are celebrated.

In Italy and Greece, face masks must be worn again. In Italy, masks are mandatory in public places. In Greece, masks must also be worn at indoor parties.

In Lithuania, a corona pass is required at petrol stations if a stop lasts more than 15 minutes. In addition, members of the Lithuanian Parliament must get a corona vaccine.

In Italy this week, parties have been cancelled and nightclubs closed until January. In Scotland, nightclubs will close next week and stay closed at least until New Year's Eve. In Sweden, new corona restrictions were introduced on Thursday.

France and Britain reported a record number of new corona infections. In Finland too, there is a record number of new corona infections again.

## Storm damage in the Philippines

![A man in a short-sleeved T-shirt and shorts looks at storm damage on the beach. The beach is full of wooden debris.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89417461bff56dd7600/1639970355)

The UN is collecting money for the Philippines. The Philippines is suffering from the damage caused by the storm.

The UN aims to collect 100 million euros. The money is needed for clean drinking water and healthcare, for example.

A strong storm caused damage in the Philippines last week. At least 375 people died. The homes of thousands of people were destroyed. More than 500,000 people were affected by the storm.
