# Monday 12.8.2019 (radio)

Etla estimates that the competitiveness agreement will bring more jobs to Finland. A software error was the reason why the Tax Administration's letters had wrong information. 70 years ago the Geneva Convention was signed, which set rules for war. There was a fire at Helsinki's Women's Clinic. Raspberries have ripened slowly.

## The KiKy agreement

![A person signing an employment contract.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10389659)

Open image viewer

The competitiveness agreement, or KiKy agreement, lengthened working hours. Photo: Pixabay

The competitiveness agreement has brought more jobs to Finland. That is what the Research Institute of the Finnish Economy, Etla, estimates.

The competitiveness agreement, or KiKy agreement, came into force in 2016. The agreement, for example, lengthened working time by 24 hours a year. Etla says that it brings thousands of jobs to Finland.

The competitiveness agreement may cause a dispute in the autumn pay negotiations. Employers want to continue lengthening working time. Employees want the lengthening to end.

## The Tax Administration's error

![Vero logo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10915970)

Open image viewer

A software error caused confusion in the Tax Administration's letters last week. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The Tax Administration has said why there were errors in its letters. The cause was a printing program.

Last week the Tax Administration sent customers letters with wrong information. A customer received, for example, another person's tax information.

However, fewer errors happened than was feared. There was wrong information in fewer than 3,000 letters.

The Tax Administration has said that a customer can destroy a letter with wrong information.

## The Geneva Convention

![Kalle Löövi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7780539)

Open image viewer

Kalle Löövi says that modern war often breaks the Geneva Convention. Photo: Eljas Niskanen / Yle

It is now 70 years since the Geneva Convention was signed. The convention sets rules for war.

The convention says, for example, how soldiers must protect and help people who do not take part in the fighting.

Kalle Löövi of the Finnish Red Cross says that modern war constantly breaks the Geneva rules.

The problem is, for example, that battles are often fought in cities among people. Aid workers also end up in the middle of battles.

## Fire at the Women's Clinic

![Firefighters and a fire engine in front of the Women's Clinic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10918117)

Open image viewer

There was a fire at the Women's Clinic on Sunday. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

There has been a fire at Helsinki's Women's Clinic. The fire started on Sunday in a rubbish chute. Smoke also got inside the building.

Because of the fire, about 70 families were moved away from the Women's Clinic.

The fire has also affected the Women's Clinic's work on Monday. Women giving birth have been directed to other hospitals.

## Raspberries are ripening

![Garden raspberries in Sotkamo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10902109)

Open image viewer

The raspberry is a popular berry. Photo: Timo Sihvonen / Yle

Raspberries have ripened slowly, because the summer has been quite cold.

Raspberries are also grown in polytunnels. In polytunnels too the berries have ripened slowly because of cloudy weather and cold nights. Raspberries outdoors in the open field have ripened even faster than in polytunnels.

Raspberries will still ripen for a long time. Berries are still picked in September too.
