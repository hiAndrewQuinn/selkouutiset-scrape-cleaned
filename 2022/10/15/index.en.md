## NATO talks

![Pekka Haavisto smiles at the camera.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1021371634a81cf51fac/1665827392)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Rinna Härkönen / Yle

Finland's and Sweden's NATO talks with Turkey have slowed down. The reason is that Sweden's government is changing. This is what Finland's Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says.

Finland and Turkey have been waiting for Sweden's new government to start work. There were elections in Sweden in September, and the Swedish parliament will vote on the new government next week.

Finland and Sweden are negotiating with Turkey so that Turkey approves them joining the NATO defence alliance. Turkey says that Sweden in particular must be tougher against terrorists.

Finland and Sweden will become NATO members when all NATO countries have approved them as members of the defence alliance. Approval is still expected from Hungary and Turkey.

## Diesel price

![refuelling at a petrol station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6002815da58e56bfc20/1571131637)

Open image viewer

The price of diesel may rise in winter. Photo: Markku Pitkänen / Yle

The price of diesel may rise a lot in winter. The reason is Russia's war in Ukraine.

Because of the war, sanctions have been imposed on Russia. For example, Russian diesel will no longer be brought into Europe from February onwards.

About 40 percent of Europe's diesel now comes from Russia. When such a large amount of diesel is removed from the market, the price of diesel will probably rise.

## Accident in Turkey

![Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan spoke at a press conference in Belgrade with the President of Serbia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3382,h_1902,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1004445631896c855412/1663664766)

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan says that the cause of the accident will be found out. Photo: Andrej Cukic / AOP

A serious accident has happened in Turkey. About 40 people died when a mine exploded.

The accident happened at the mine on Friday evening. More than 100 people were deep underground in the mine at the time of the explosion.

The accident happened in a coal mine. The mine is in the town of Amasra on the Black Sea coast.

Turkish authorities are investigating the accident.

## Biden on the protests in Iran

![Screenshot from a video showing Iranian security forces beating protesters.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_996,h_560,x_0,y_256/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-102114863496725d5566/1665754967)

Open image viewer

Iranian police have been violent towards protesters. Photo: AFP

US President Joe Biden says that Iran must stop the violence against protesters.

In Iran there have been big protests for many weeks. The protesters are protesting against Iran's police.

The protests began when Iran's police beat a young woman so badly that she died. The police beat the woman because her hijab, that is, her headscarf, was on her head in a way the police thought was wrong.
