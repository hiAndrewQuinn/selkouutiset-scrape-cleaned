# Wednesday 13.5.2020 (radio)

The return of comprehensive school pupils to face-to-face teaching divides opinion. Customs and health authorities are worried about strong nicotine pouches. New information is being collected on women's health. Universities and polytechnics may take in extra students.

## Yle asked about opening schools

![Schools return to face-to-face teaching on Thursday 14.5. What do you think of the decision?](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11347631)

Photo: Otso Ritonummi / Yle

The return of comprehensive school pupils to face-to-face teaching divides opinion. Yle asked about the matter.

44 per cent of Finns support the government's decision to open schools. 43 per cent oppose it.

More women support opening schools than men. Of women, 46 per cent support opening schools.

Of men, 47 per cent said that the decision to open schools was wrong. 43 per cent supported the decision.

The survey was made by the research company Taloustutkimus. Face-to-face teaching starts on Thursday.

## Nicotine pouches cause concern

![snus tin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_128/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6538045e6f99ca475e8/1584372226)

Nicotine pouches contain up to 2 times more nicotine than snus. Photo: Juha Heikanen / Yle

Customs and health authorities are worried about strong nicotine pouches. Their use is becoming more common in Finland.

Nicotine pouches are used like snus (a moist tobacco product placed under the lip), but they do not contain tobacco. The pouches can be up to 2 times stronger than tobacco snus.

Strong nicotine pouches are brought into Finland from Estonia, among other places. In Estonia they may be sold freely. In Finland, the sale of strong nicotine pouches is banned.

Nicotine snus is the new hit product of the tobacco giants. Yle's information shows that nicotine snus is already sold in 14 EU countries. In Tallinn alone there are about 200 points of sale.

Nicotine comes into Finland through tourists and mail order.

## Research into women's health is starting

![Samples in test tubes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9533898)

Open image viewer

There is too little research information on women's health. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

New information is being collected on women's health. A large health study is being carried out in Oulu and Helsinki. 4,500 Finnish women born in 1986 are being invited to it.

The aim is to get information on, for example, the effects of gynaecological factors, that is, women's diseases, on health.

Women have complaints that need more research information. These include, for example, pain in the external genitals and a separation of the abdominal muscles.

Earlier, lack of funding and embarrassment have slowed down research on women's health.

## Universities may get extra places

![Library of the University of Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-7838358d4e59162495/1699530488)

Universities and polytechnics may take in extra students. The aim is to reduce youth unemployment after the corona epidemic.

Places to start studying were supposed to be added in next year's student admissions. Because of the corona situation, some of next year's extra places may move to this year already.

Universities make their decisions independently. Therefore, not all universities will add the same number of places to start studying.

Yesterday, the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment, Jari Gustafsson, appealed to universities on A-studio. Gustafsson hoped that universities would take part in the corona community effort by adding places to start studying.
