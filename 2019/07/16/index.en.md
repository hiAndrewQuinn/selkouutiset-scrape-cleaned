# Tuesday 16.7.2019 (radio)

The Finnish government is planning for all children to get full-day care again. Iraq says it has sent away children from Iraq who have links to the Isis terrorist organisation. A lot of used cars are imported into Finland from abroad. A lunar eclipse can be seen in the sky during the night between Tuesday and Wednesday.

## Early childhood education for all

![Children and day-care workers playing a circle game in a park](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10334580)

The Finnish government is planning for all children to get full-day care again. The government believes that all children should have the same right to early childhood education.

Finland's previous government restricted early childhood education in 2016. Since then a child could get early childhood education only 20 hours a week if the child's parents were not working or studying all day.

Now Finland's new government intends to restore the right to full-day care for all children. The law may change in about a year.

## Iraq is sending children away

![A veiled woman leads a small boy by the hand beside the tents of a refugee camp.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10875803)

The wife and child of an Isis terrorist in a Syrian camp. Photo: Ahmed Mardnli / IEPA

Iraq says that it has sent away from Iraq children who have links to the Isis terrorist organisation.

Iraq says that it has sent almost 500 children out of the country. The children are citizens of different countries. Iraq says that there are also Finns among them.

Finland's Ministry for Foreign Affairs does not know more about the matter, but it is looking into it.

Prime Minister Antti Rinne reminds that the matter concerns Iraq, not Syria. There are Finnish women and children in the Isis camp in Syria, which has been much talked about in Finland.

## Used cars from abroad

![A car lorry unloads used cars from a Finnlines ship at Vuosaari harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4875,h_2742,x_0,y_198/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5771165d283730dc604/1723629512)

Most used cars are imported from Sweden and Germany. Photo: Pekka Pantsu / Yle

A lot of used cars are imported into Finland from abroad. This year a new record will be set, almost 50,000 cars.

Most used cars are imported from Sweden and Germany. Finns buy the most Mercedes, Volvos and BMWs.

At the same time fewer new cars are being sold than before. The reason is taxes. Because of taxes, it pays the customer to buy a used car from abroad.

That is why the car industry thinks that taxes on new cars should be lowered. The car industry reminds that new cars pollute nature less. That is why it is good if the tax on new cars is reduced.

## Lunar eclipse

![A partly darkened reddish moon above a lake.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10877873)

Open image viewer

This is what a partial lunar eclipse looked like 2 years ago. Photo: Markku Ruonala

A lunar eclipse can be seen in the sky during the night between Tuesday and Wednesday.

The eclipse is partial, that is, only part of the Moon's surface will be in the Earth's shadow. When the eclipse is at its largest, more than half of the Moon's surface will be in the Earth's shadow.

During the eclipse the Moon is low and its colour is quite red.

It is worth watching the eclipse between 23:00 and 03:00. Clouds may cover the eclipse.
