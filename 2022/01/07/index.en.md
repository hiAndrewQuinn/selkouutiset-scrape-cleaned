## New study on corona

![A nurse watches a corona patient through glass.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4354,h_2449,x_0,y_174/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-884329619ff71bcc671/1637874541)

In Finland the first study on long-term corona has been published.

The study says that long-term corona, that is, long covid, is more common in people who have needed hospital care because of corona.

About 10 to 20 per cent of adult corona patients have had symptoms that lasted more than 3 months and that have significantly affected daily activities. Up to 50 per cent of adult corona patients have had milder symptoms.

Symptoms of long-term corona disease include, for example, exhaustion, weakness and shortness of breath. Many also have cognitive disorders, that is, it is hard for them to remember and process information.

## Today's corona figures

Many new corona infections have again been confirmed in Finland.

The Institute for Health and Welfare says that almost 10,000 new infections have been confirmed today. In Finland, over 300,000 corona infections have been confirmed in total during the whole pandemic.

There are now about 500 corona patients in hospital. Of them, 55 are in intensive care.

There have been 6 new deaths since yesterday.

## Advance voting for the regional elections

![A car and an official at a drive-in polling place.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2500,h_1406,x_0,y_160/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81140060ae35b321a75/1622029960)

Open image viewer

Drive-in voting was also organised in the municipal elections in June. Photo: Janne Nykänen / Yle

In the regional elections you can also vote in advance outdoors. The Ministry of Justice says that advance voting outdoors can be done in about 30 municipalities.

Municipalities are organising outdoor voting, for example in tents, containers and drive-in points.

On election day too, one can vote in the yard of the polling place if a doctor has ordered the voter into quarantine or isolation.

Advance voting for the regional elections starts next week on Wednesday. The actual election day is Sunday 23 January.

## Police search for a reindeer

![A runaway reindeer in Helsinki on 7 January 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1599,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89963261d846b364b1b/1641563893)

A reindeer has been roaming loose in Helsinki today. The reindeer was seen in daytime, for example along Hämeenlinnanväylä and in North Haaga.

The police tried to catch the reindeer, but at least early in the evening it was still on the run.

The police also want to find out who owns the reindeer, so that it can be returned home.
