# Friday 10.5.2019 (radio)

The Finns Party is the most popular party. Government negotiations continued. At least 60 drowned in the Mediterranean. Calls and text messages are getting cheaper in the EU. An animal smuggler was sentenced to prison. There is more rabies in Finland than before.

## Party support

![Party support figures](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10775924)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

The Finns Party is now the most popular party in Finland. About 19% of Finns support the Finns Party.

Support for the SDP is about 18%. The SDP is now the second most popular party.

The third most popular party is the National Coalition. About 17% of Finns support the National Coalition.

The Centre and the Greens are equally popular. Each party is supported by 13.3% of Finns.

## Ship disaster in the Mediterranean

Tunisia Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

At least 60 people have died in a ship disaster in the Mediterranean.

The ship sank off the coast of Tunisia. The ship was on its way from Libya to Europe.

Local fishermen managed to rescue 16 people.

Some of the boat's passengers were from Bangladesh and Morocco.

## Calls and text messages get cheaper in the EU

![A mobile phone screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10287498)

Open image viewer

Photo: Perttu Ruokangas / Yle

The prices of calls and text messages made within the EU area are going down.

From next Wednesday there will be a maximum price for calls and text messages. A call may cost no more than 19 cents a minute. Text messages may cost no more than 6 cents each.

VAT is added to the prices, and the price that the call or text message costs in Finland.

## Dog smuggler to prison

![Labrador retriever puppies.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5433793)

Open image viewer

Photo: Julian Smith / EPA

A person who smuggled dogs into Finland has been sentenced to prison. They will have to be in prison for a year.

The sentenced person had illegally brought many hundreds of dogs into Finland and sold the dogs.

The new owners noticed that the dogs were seriously ill. The dogs had to be put down.

Two other people were also sentenced, because they had smuggled dogs. They will not go to prison, but they will have to pay fines.

## Rabies in Finland

![A man waits with his dogs for his turn for a vaccination at the vet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6524703)

Open image viewer

A man waited with his dogs for his turn for a vaccination at the vet in Denpasar, Bali, Indonesia, in November 2010. The authorities in Bali then started a dog vaccination campaign, to bring under control the rabies epidemic that had raged on the island since 2008 and killed more than 100 people. Photo: Made Nagi / EPA

Rabies, or hydrophobia, may be increasing in Finland.

In Finland, last year, the preventive treatment for the disease was given to almost 100 people. Many people catch it abroad.

Rabies is spread to people when an animal bites or licks them. The disease causes death if it is not treated.
