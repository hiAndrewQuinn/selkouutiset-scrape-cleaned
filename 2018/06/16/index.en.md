# Saturday 16.6.2018 (radio)

The Finnish Air Force is celebrating its 100th anniversary. A fatal crash happened at the Rally Finland. Half of Finns support a tunnel between Helsinki and Tallinn. Many street dogs are brought to Finland from abroad. Tonight the Jukola relay race is run.

## The Finnish Air Force celebrates

![Midnight Hawks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10259134)

Open image viewer

The celebration shows of the air show also feature the aerobatic team Midnight Hawks. Photo: Pyry Koivunen

The Finnish Air Force is celebrating its 100th anniversary this weekend.

To mark the celebration, the Air Force is holding a big flying display in Tikkakoski in Central Finland on Saturday and Sunday.

The shows will present the Air Force's old and new equipment. There will also be 5 different fighter jets to see, which Finland is still considering buying. About 30 000 people are expected at the air shows over the weekend

## Accident at a rally

![map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10258648)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

There has been an accident at a rally competition in Seinäjoki. The accident happened on Friday evening at a Finnish Rally Championship event.

A rally car left the road and hit a woman who was watching the competition. The woman died immediately.

The event organisers say that the woman was watching the competition in an area that is classed as dangerous. However, the area was not forbidden or closed to the public.

The Accident Investigation Board is investigating the accident.

## Tunnel to Tallinn

![Comparison of travel times between Helsinki and Tallinn](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10095072)

Open image viewer

The tunnel would shorten the travel time between Helsinki and Tallinn. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Almost half of Finns support building a rail tunnel between Helsinki and Tallinn.

Uutissuomalainen asked a thousand Finns what they think of the tunnel. Almost 50 percent supported it and a little over 30 percent opposed it.

Support for the tunnel was highest among National Coalition voters. Opposition to the tunnel was highest among Finns Party voters.

## Homeless dogs

![A homeless dog on the pavement.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10249733)

Open image viewer

A homeless dog on the pavement. Photo: Narong Sangnak / EPA

Many so-called rescue dogs are brought to Finland from abroad.

A rescue dog is a street dog, which has lived on the street, so it has no home.

For example, last year perhaps about 2000 rescue dogs were brought to Finland. Many of the dogs came to Finland from Spain, Greece or Russia.

The Helsinki animal protection association says that Finns often do not notice that there are also homeless dogs in Finland. For example, the Helsinki animal protection association has many dogs that are looking for a new home.

## Jukola relay

![Start of the Venlojen relay 2018, Krista Pärmäkoski and Aino-Kaisa Saarinen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10259112)

Before the Jukola relay there was the Venlojen relay, the women's orienteering race. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

This weekend the biggest orienteering event in Finland, the Jukola relay, is being held. This time the orienteering relay will be run in the forests of Lahti and Hollola.

About 20 000 orienteers take part in the Jukola relay. There are top orienteers from around the world, but also ordinary keep-fit people.

The Jukola relay starts in the evening, at about 23:00. It lasts through the night, and part of the route is run by headlamp light.

In orienteering, you look for controls in the forest, which are marked places. The help you have is a map and a compass.
