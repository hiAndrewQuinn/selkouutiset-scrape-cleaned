# Monday 21.1.2019 (radio)

Teachers are dissatisfied with the new curriculum. MP Maria Lohela is switching to Harkimo's Now Movement. A Finnish prescription can now also be used to buy medicines in Estonia. The number of tourists grew last year.

## The new curriculum for comprehensive school

![Raised hand, primary school, Eteläiset school](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5060,h_2846,x_0,y_261/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5371025c4083bfb8c15/1547732733)

The new curriculum for comprehensive school divides opinions.

Yle asked more than 600 teachers and head teachers what they think of the new curriculum. Over 60 percent of head teachers said that the new curriculum helps learning. Only 25 percent of teachers agreed.

Most teachers said that the new curriculum has not steered learning in the right direction. Teachers say that very young pupils are now given too much responsibility for their own learning. Teachers are also very worried that the differences in level between pupils will grow.

The new curriculum emphasises, for example, digitalisation, the pupil's own activity and projects that combine different school subjects. The tasks are often done electronically online. Textbooks are rarely used.

## Maria Lohela leaves the Blues

![Maria Lohela and Hjallis Harkimo at an information event.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10606633)

Open image viewer

Maria Lohela and Harry Harkimo. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

MP Maria Lohela is leaving the Blue Future party. Lohela is moving to the Now Movement parliamentary group.

The founder of the Now Movement group is MP Harry Harkimo. Harkimo founded the group when he left the National Coalition last spring.

Maria Lohela says that she is leaving the Blues because she cannot support the government's social and health care reform. Lohela says that she intends to vote against the social and health care reform.

Maria Lohela intends to leave Parliament after this spring. She is not standing in the parliamentary elections or the European Parliament elections.

## A Finnish prescription is also valid in Estonia

![Electronic prescription.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5436772)

Open image viewer

The aim is that by the end of the year, Estonian prescriptions will also be valid in Finnish pharmacies. Photo: Heikki Ahonen / Yle

A Finnish medicine prescription can now be used to buy medicines in Estonia too.

The Institute for Health and Welfare says that an electronic prescription issued in Finland is now also valid in Estonian pharmacies. The aim is that by the end of this year, Estonian medicine prescriptions will also be valid in Finland.

This is the first health care service that crosses national borders in Europe. The aim is for the service to expand to the rest of Europe too.

## Travel grew around the world

![People sunbathing in San Cristóbal, Spain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5347997)

Open image viewer

The number of tourists grew last year.

The World Tourism Organization says that 1.4 billion tourists travelled around the world last year. The number of tourists rose by 6 percent from the previous year.

Trips to Southern Europe, the Middle East and Africa increased the most.

The World Tourism Organization says that the growth in tourism is due to good economic growth and the development of technology.
