# Saturday 22.9.2018 (radio)

The police have a big operation in the Turku region this weekend. Trade unions have gained many members with an immigrant background. Pope Francis is visiting the Baltic states. The mother's diet during pregnancy and breastfeeding affects the baby. There was an autumn storm in Finland on Saturday.

## Big police operation

The police have a big operation in the Turku region this weekend. The police are carrying out searches in many places.

The police are investigating a firm that has perhaps laundered money worth many millions of euros. The purpose of money laundering is to hide the fact that the money was obtained through crime.

The operation will continue at least until Sunday evening.

## Immigrants in trade unions

![YIT is building a block of 12 apartment buildings in Jouppi, Seinäjoki. Photo from January 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9423186)

Open image viewer

A trade union defends the interests of the workers in its field. Photo: Pasi Takkunen / Yle

Trade unions have gained many members with an immigrant background. Uutissuomalainen reports on the matter.

A trade union is made up of workers in the same field, whose interests the trade union defends.

For example, the Service Union United, PAM, has over 11,000 members with an immigrant background. The Trade Union for the Public and Welfare Sectors, JHL, has about 6,000 immigrant members.

PAM says that immigrant members think the trade union is important. However, not everyone knows exactly what the trade union does.

## The Pope in the Baltic states

![Pope Francis](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10416823)

Open image viewer

Pope Francis. Photo: Maurizio Brambatti / EPA

Pope Francis is visiting the Baltic states. The Pope's visit began on Saturday in Lithuania. On Monday the Pope travels to Latvia and on Tuesday to Estonia.

The Pope is the leader of the Roman Catholic Church. There are also many Catholics in the Baltic states. 75 percent of Lithuanians and over 20 percent of Latvians are Catholics. Only in Estonia are there few Catholics.

On Tuesday the Pope will hold a mass in Freedom Square in Tallinn. It is expected that as many as 10,000 people will come to the mass. Several hundred Finnish Catholics are also travelling to Tallinn.

## Diet during pregnancy

![A fish dish](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5919866)

Open image viewer

Eat fish if you are pregnant. Photo: YLE

The mother's diet affects the baby. It is good for the mother to eat fish during pregnancy and breastfeeding.

Fish has healthy fat. When the mother eats fish regularly during pregnancy, the child's brain develops better.

The child's eyesight also develops better if the mother eats fish during pregnancy. A 2-year-old child usually does well in a sight test if the mother ate fish at least 3 times a week during the last months of pregnancy.

The information is from a study by the University of Turku and Turku University Hospital.
