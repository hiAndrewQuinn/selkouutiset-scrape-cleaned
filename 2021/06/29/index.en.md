The number of coronavirus infections in Finland has turned to growth during the last week. Over 200 new infections have been reported today.

## Plant proteins

![Vegan products.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_470/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7542535fd893a876d33/1615979683)

Open image viewer

Sales of plant proteins are now growing quickly. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

Finns eat more plant proteins than before.

Last year Finns bought, for example, Nyhtökaura (pulled oat) and other meat substitutes for over 50 million euros. Consumption of plant proteins grew by over 30 percent from the previous year.

The Natural Resources Institute says that at the same time meat consumption has decreased a little. Last year Finns ate about 79 kilos of meat per person.

## Plastic recycling

![A plastic recycling point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82465960d3131c7130b/1624445922)

Open image viewer

A record amount of plastic was brought to recycling points last year. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Finns recycle plastic diligently.

Last year Finns took about 29,000 tonnes of plastic to recycling boxes. That is a record amount and 40 percent more than a year earlier.

The recycled plastic is taken to a plastics processing plant in Riihimäki. There the plastics are sorted, washed and shredded.

Recycled plastic is made into, for example, flower pots and toilet brushes.

## Astronaut application

![Astronaut Tim Kopra on a spacewalk on 22 December 2015.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8597683)

Open image viewer

American astronaut Tim Kopra on a spacewalk in 2015. Photo: Nasa / EPA

Thousands of people have applied to become astronauts, that is, space pilots. The European Space Agency ESA received a total of 22,000 applications.

About 300 Finns also applied to become astronauts. About 60 of them were women.

The European Space Agency chooses at most 6 career astronauts. Career astronauts are ESA staff, and they get to go on long space flights.

In addition, ESA chooses about 20 project astronauts. They can be hired, if needed, for a particular flight.
