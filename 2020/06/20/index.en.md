# Saturday 20.6.2020 (radio)

Midsummer has been a busy time for the police. 9 new corona infections have been found in Finland. The coronavirus is spreading quickly around the world. Nobel laureate Malala Yousafzai has graduated from university. The warm weather continues.

(The TV Clear News has a summer break. The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.)

## Midsummer celebrations

![Police officers at Hietaniemi beach in Helsinki on Midsummer night, 20 June 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11410854)

Police at Hietaniemi beach. Photo: Leevi Soikkeli / Lehtikuva

Finns have spent Midsummer this weekend. The night between Friday and Saturday, Midsummer night, was busy for the police.

For example, in Helsinki at Hietaniemi beach there was a mass fight between young people. The young people also threw stones, bottles and fireworks at the police when the police arrived.

The police arrested 5 young people. All of them are men under 18 years old. There were several hundred young people at Hietaniemi beach in total.

Several drownings have also happened during Midsummer. At least 4 people have drowned during Midsummer.

## Corona infections in Finland

![Sign of THL on Mannerheimintie in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5172,h_2909,x_0,y_259/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6782355ec3516e816dd/1616069887)

9 new corona infections have been found in Finland. In total, just over 7,100 people in Finland have caught the coronavirus.

So far 326 people have died in Finland because of corona.

## Corona infections around the world

![Corona victims' cemetery in Manaus, Brazil, photographed on 2 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11383959)

Open image viewer

Many people who died of corona have been buried in Brazil. Photo: Michael Dantas / AFP

In Finland the corona situation is good, but around the world the coronavirus is still spreading.

The World Health Organization, WHO, warns that the situation is dangerous. The coronavirus is spreading quickly, and people no longer have the energy to stay at home.

More new corona infections have now been found in one day than ever before. The new record is more than 150,000 corona infections in one day.

The most new corona infections are in America. The USA and Brazil are the countries where corona is most common.

The coronavirus has already killed more than 450,000 people around the world.

## Malala Yousafzai

![Malala Yousafzai](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10141080)

Open image viewer

Malala Yousafzai thinks that children must be able to go to school. Photo: Sashenka Gutierrez / EPA

Nobel laureate Malala Yousafzai has graduated from university. She completed her degree at the University of Oxford in Britain.

The Pakistani Yousafzai is the youngest Nobel Peace Prize winner. Yousafzai won the Peace Prize in 2014. She was 17 years old then.

Yousafzai is known for defending girls' right to education. The extremist group the Taliban shot Malala Yousafzai in 2012, but she survived.

## Weather for the coming days

![Hietaniemi beach in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11410477)

Open image viewer

Midsummer in Helsinki. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Midsummer Eve, that is Friday, was warm. The warmest place was Salo, where 28.7 degrees were measured.

The warm weather continues in Finland too. The sun shines across the whole country.

In Southern Finland there is a heatwave on many days, that is, the temperature is 25 degrees or more. At the beginning of next week the temperature will be almost 30 degrees.

The weather will also warm up in Lapland. In Lapland the temperature may be close to 25 degrees on Sunday.
