# Saturday 20.5.2017 (radio)

President Niinistö has taken a stand on asylum seekers. Donald Trump has started a tour abroad in Saudi Arabia. Finnish Fiskars scissors turn 50. The Arab country Lebanon has held its first Pride week this week.

## The president spoke about asylum seekers and the law

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9599672)

Open image viewer

Peresident Niinistö has taken a stand on asylum seekers who have received a negative decision. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

President Sauli Niinistö says that the treatment of people who have received a negative asylum decision and of undocumented people must be based on the law. Niinistö says that the decisions of the justice system must be respected.

The president says that he understands the wish of the church and organisations to help people who are in need. But Niinistö says that Finland cannot get a reputation as a country where help is permanent and where it is good for undocumented people to stay.

On Saturday Niinistö took part in a discussion event at the Church Days in Turku.

## Fiskars scissors have a birthday

![Fiskars scissors. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9624439)

Open image viewer

Finnish Fiskars scissors are perhaps the most famous scissors in the world. They are having their 50th birthday right now.  Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finnish Fiskars scissors turn 50. Fiskars is the market leader in selling scissors in the whole world. The success began in 1967, when Fiskars developed orange scissors with plastic handles. They quickly became a classic of everyday design.

The scissors were supposed to be black, red or green. But the machine operator decided to use up the orange colour in the plastics machine. In the end there was a vote between orange and black. Orange beat black by 9 votes to 7.

Around the world you can also see scissors that are not real Fiskars scissors. The orange scissors have been copied and faked a lot.

The scissors are still also made in Finland at the Billnäs factory in Raasepori. The factory still has almost 300 employees.

## Beirut Pride is under way in Lebanon

![Beirut Pride ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9620847)

Open image viewer

Beirut Pride tries to break the harsh attitudes in Arab countries towards sexual and gender minorities. Photo: Beirut Pride

The Arab country Lebanon has held its first Pride week this week. Pride highlights the rights of sexual and gender minorities.

The event is exceptional, because in Arab countries attitudes towards sexual and gender minorities are very negative. In many countries minorities face fines, prison sentences and in some countries even the death penalty.

The organisers of Beirut Pride say that Pride is a sign that attitudes have changed, at least in Lebanon. The organisers say that the young generation no longer wants to stay hidden or pretend to be straight.
