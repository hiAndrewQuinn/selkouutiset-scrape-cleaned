## Proposal for Finland's budget for next year

We start with news about the Finnish state budget.

The Ministry of Finance began negotiations today on Finland's budget for 2027.

The Ministry of Finance will make a proposal to the government on how the Finnish state will spend money next year.

Finance Minister **Riikka Purra** says that Finland will have to take on 13 billion euros in debt.

Finland is forced to take on a lot of debt because the state does not have money for all important expenses.

Negotiations on next year's Finnish state budget began today.

Purra says that the government will not propose new savings for next year. The government announced the savings already in the spring.
