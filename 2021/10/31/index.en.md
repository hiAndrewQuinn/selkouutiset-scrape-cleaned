## Loneliness in the corona time

![An elderly person walking in the city](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4346,h_2445,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-7891958dd4108b5306/1712639702)

Open image viewer

Pensioners are mostly satisfied with life also during the corona time. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The corona situation has increased the loneliness of many pensioners. The Institute for Health and Welfare has studied the matter.

Pensioners' loneliness increased because people met less. Visits were restricted in care homes and hospitals. However, many pensioners are satisfied with life also during the corona time.

Researchers say that long restrictions or isolation are not a good thing. Researchers say that long restrictions and isolation should be avoided also in exceptional times.

## Corona restrictions and the economy

![Diners at the Huipulla restaurant in Joupi, Seinäjoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8670746168257fe5f4b/1634215940)

Open image viewer

During the corona time people's consumption has decreased. Photo: Mirva Ekman / Yle

Corona restrictions have not worsened the economy in Finland as much as people have thought.

Economic experts say that firms' difficulties are due more to people's caution than to corona restrictions. People have not wanted to go to shops or restaurants much, for example, because there are many people in them.

Economic experts say that without corona restrictions, people would get sick and die much more. Then firms would also be doing much worse.

## Pay rises

![Euro banknotes on a table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/17-47653392b6db2451/1396859410)

Open image viewer

Many Finns say that a pay rise must be asked for. Photo: Yle

Men get pay rises more often than women. Men also ask for pay rises more often than women. This is what aTalent's research says.

Of men, 17 per cent say they have never received a pay rise. Of women, 24 per cent say they have never received a pay rise.

About half of Finns say that a pay rise can be received only if the employee asks for one.

The UN climate conference has started in Britain. The meeting discusses, for example, how the Paris climate agreement is being implemented.

The common concern of the countries of the world is the warming of the climate. However, it is difficult for different countries to agree on common means to reduce climate emissions.

The UN climate conference lasts 2 weeks.
