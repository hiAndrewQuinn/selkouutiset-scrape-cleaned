## The situation in healthcare

![Tehy chair Millariikka Rytkönen (left) and SuPer chair Silja Paavola shout slogans at a demonstration](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4478,h_2518,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9407706254217adbcc8/1649680929)

Nurses are angry because of poor pay. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The nurses' strike is cancelled.

The plan was that 35,000 nurses would go on strike, that is, stop work, on Wednesday. Now the nurses' organisations say that the strike will not start.

The nurses are planning even tougher measures than a strike. The nurses' organisations are planning for a large group of nurses to stop work completely, that is, to resign.

The reason is the patient safety law. If the new patient safety law comes into force, nurses can be forced to work during a strike more easily than now.

Many nurses were already on strike before Easter.

The reason for the disputes in healthcare is money. Nurses demand more pay. They also say that more employees are needed in healthcare.

## Russia's attack on eastern Ukraine

![Ukrainian soldiers on top of an armoured personnel carrier. A cannon in front of a tank.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5771,h_3246,x_0,y_287/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-943545625ec459532f1/1650377873)

Open image viewer

Ukrainians have decided to defend their own country. Photo: EPA

A new phase has begun in the war in Ukraine. According to Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov, the new phase is important in the war.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy estimates that Russia has begun a major attack on eastern Ukraine.

Russia already holds part of eastern Ukraine. Now Russia wants to take the whole Donbas region of eastern Ukraine, President Zelenskyy says.

Zelenskyy also says that Ukraine is defending itself against the attack.

The major Russian offensive has been expected for some time. Military experts are not yet sure, however, how large the attack was when it began on Monday.

## Shortage of car tyres

![Niko Jokinen, a tyre fitter at Euromaster in Iisalmi, changes a winter tyre on a van.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10763771)

Open image viewer

Next winter it may be difficult to get new winter tyres. Photo: Antti Karhunen / Yle

There may be a shortage of car winter tyres next autumn. At that time, motorists may find it difficult to get new winter tyres.

Fewer winter tyres are arriving at Finland's tyre shops than usual, because tyres are no longer imported from Russia.

Car tyres are no longer bought from Russia because of the war in Ukraine. Previously, more than half of car tyres came from Russia.

Next autumn, good brand tyres in particular will be difficult to get. The situation is also visible in prices. Car tyres are becoming more expensive.
