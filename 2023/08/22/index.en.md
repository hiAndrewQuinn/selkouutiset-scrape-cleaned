## Housing gets more expensive

![Asemantorni II apartment block on Rautatienkatu in Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115634764da06775ffe3/1692010407)

Housing is getting more expensive this year. For some Finns housing is getting more expensive, and for others less.

Housing gets most expensive if you have a mortgage.

Researchers calculate that in a flat in an apartment block that has a mortgage, housing costs will rise by 15 percent.

The situation is better if you rent. Then housing costs rise only a little.

Mortgages are getting more expensive because interest rates have risen.

Rents are not rising as much. There are many empty rental flats in the cities. That is why rents cannot be raised very much.

At the same time the number of open job vacancies is decreasing. That may mean that unemployment will grow later this year.

## Government and trade unions

![Tehy and Super held a demonstration in rainy Helsinki in September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4987,h_2805,x_0,y_528/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1009037632466dae428c/1663330669)

Relations between the government and the trade unions are bad. The trade unions oppose the government's plans, which weaken the position of employees.

The government intends, for example, to weaken unemployment security and employees' right to strike.

The government also wants to make it easier to dismiss employees and to make the conditions for sick leave stricter.

The government intends to negotiate the plans with the trade unions. The trade unions do not believe that the negotiations will help.

The trade unions say that they are trying to stop the government's plans. They are preparing for strikes and other countermeasures in the autumn.

## Russia and Finland

![Sauli Niinistö speaks at the ambassadors' conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_420/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116020464e465bc745a1/1692690956)

President Sauli Niinistö spoke to ambassadors, that is, diplomats. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Russia is creating an image of Finland as an enemy, says President Sauli Niinistö. That means Russian politicians are spreading false information about Finland to Russians.

President Niinistö spoke about the matter to foreign diplomats on Tuesday.

According to Niinistö, Russian politicians claim to Russians that Finland wishes Russia harm.

According to Niinistö, Russia can also cause Finland all kinds of trouble.

According to Niinistö, the world is more and more divided. That is why it is important that Finland looks after its own security.
