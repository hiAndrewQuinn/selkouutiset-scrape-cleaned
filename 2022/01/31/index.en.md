# Monday 31.1.2022 (radio)

Corona restrictions. Corona vaccination certificate. Watching television. The cold weather continues.

## Corona restrictions

![Sanna Marin at the regional election night event at Kuntatalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5194,h_2921,x_0,y_195/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90606061eeba73acc1d/1643774755)

Corona restrictions will soon end in Finland. Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that all corona restrictions will end during February.

It was already decided earlier that some corona restrictions will ease on Tuesday, that is, on 1 February. For example, food restaurants may stay open longer, and in many places sports facilities are being opened.

In the Helsinki and Uusimaa region, for example, gyms and swimming halls will open again on Tuesday.

Doctor Mika Valtonen says that corona restrictions can be removed gradually. Valtonen is the chief physician of the intensive care unit at Turku University Hospital.

However, Valtonen says that it is not good to remove all corona restrictions at the same time. Valtonen reminds that almost 20 per cent of intensive care places are still used by corona patients.

On Monday, new deaths were also reported. There are 48 new corona deaths. In total, 1,990 people have died in Finland because of corona.

## Corona vaccination certificate

![Corona vaccination certificate on a phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4034,h_2269,x_0,y_6/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82413560d1b27c91dea/1624357404)

Open image viewer

Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

There will be changes to the use of corona vaccination certificates for travel. The changes will come into force at the beginning of February.

After that, Finns' corona vaccination certificates will be valid in Europe for 9 months after the second corona vaccination.

If more than 270 days have passed since the second corona vaccination, the certificate can no longer be used for travel in Europe.

If a traveller has 3 corona vaccinations, the corona vaccination certificate does not expire. It is valid until further notice.

## Watching television

![Netflix being watched on a computer, photographed over the shoulder.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4992,h_2808,x_0,y_257/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-86548861644883f2c79/1633964356)

Open image viewer

Photo: Katriina Laine / Yle

Watching a lot of television is unhealthy. The reason is that the TV viewer sits for a long time.

A new international study says that sitting causes circulation problems. If a person sits in front of the television for a long time, the risk of a blood clot in a vein increases.

Researchers say that it is good for TV viewers to get up now and then and walk a little.

Exercise does not reduce the risk of a blood clot in a vein if, after exercise, one sits or lies on the sofa for a long time.
