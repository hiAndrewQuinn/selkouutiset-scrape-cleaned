# Thursday 19.7.2018 (radio)

3 people died in a car chase in Mäntyharju. There is a big forest fire in Pyhäranta. Turku's hero Hassan Zubier will receive a medal from the British queen. Thunderstorms are coming at the weekend.

## Crash in Mäntyharju

![Site of the head-on collision in Mäntyharju.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10313833)

3 people died in the crash in Mäntyharju. Photo: Esa Huuhko / Yle

A serious crash has happened in Mäntyharju in Eastern Finland. 3 people died in the crash.

The situation began when the police were told about a car whose driver may have been drunk. The police found the driver, who fled in the car. The police followed the car.

In the end the getaway car crashed into 2 other cars. The driver of the getaway car and 2 other people died. The getaway car caught fire in the collision.

The situation was rare. In Finland it is not usual for outsiders to die when the police try to stop a car.

## Forest fire in Pyhäranta

![A helicopter puts out a forest fire in Pyhäranta in July 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10313267)

A helicopter is putting out the fire. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

In Pyhäranta in Western Finland there is a big fire. The forest is burning over a large area.

Firefighters, who get help from the army, are working on the fire. About 200 people are on site.

Helicopters are also taking part in the firefighting. The firefighting will still take a long time.

The fire has not burned any houses, but more than 50 people have been moved away from the fire area.

## Medal for Zubier

![Hassan Zubier](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10273299)

Open image viewer

Hassan Zubier already has a medal from Finland, and now a medal is coming from Britain too. Photo: Helena Andersson

Swedish citizen Hassan Zubier will receive a medal from the British queen. Zubier will receive the medal for a brave deed.

Zubier was in Finland last August, when a stabbing happened in Turku. Zubier helped a woman who was stabbed by the attacker.

The attacker also attacked Zubier. That is why Zubier is now in a wheelchair.

Queen Elizabeth will also award medals to people who helped others in the London terror attack. The queen will hand out the medals in the autumn at Buckingham Palace in London.

Zubier now lives in Sweden, but he was born in Britain.

Zubier has already received a medal from Finland before. The medal was awarded by President Sauli Niinistö.

## Thunder is coming

![lightning in the sky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10208904)

Open image viewer

At the weekend, lightning may be seen in the sky. Photo: AOP

The weather changes at the weekend. Thunderstorms will come to Finland. The thunderstorms will start on Friday and become more widespread on Saturday.

The thunder also brings lightning to the sky. Yle meteorologist Matti Huutonen says that the lightning may be spectacular.

Thunder rain may be strong in Southern Finland and Western Finland already on Friday. Matti Huutonen says that it may rain several centimetres per hour. However, the differences between the areas are large.

On Saturday the rain will continue. The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns people of heavy rain on Saturday across the whole country.
