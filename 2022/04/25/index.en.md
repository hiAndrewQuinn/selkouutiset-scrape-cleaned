## Macron won the election

![Emmanuel Macron at the victory celebration at the foot of the Eiffel Tower on the Champ de Mars square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4993,h_2809,x_0,y_325/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9457186265b08aa7728/1650831717)

Emmanuel Macron has won the French presidential election. Macron will continue as President of France for the next 5 years.

The French voted in the presidential election on Sunday. The current president, Macron, beat Marine Le Pen in the second round of the election.

Macron got about 58 percent of the votes. The far-right candidate, Marine Le Pen, got about 41 percent of the votes.

The French election interested the whole of Europe. Macron supports a strong European Union, but Marine Le Pen is critical of the European Union.

Finland's Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto believes that good cooperation between Finland and France will also continue after Macron's win.

Haavisto estimates that Macron's France will, for example, support Finland if Finland decides to apply for membership of the defence alliance NATO.

US Defence Secretary Lloyd Austin, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy and US Secretary of State Antony Blinken in Kyiv. Photo: Office of the President of Ukraine / Reuters

US ministers have visited the Ukrainian capital, Kyiv.

US Secretary of State Anthony Blinken and Defence Secretary Lloyd Austin met Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Kyiv.

The ministers promised more support for Ukraine. The USA will give Ukraine military support worth 700 million dollars.

The war in Ukraine has already lasted 2 months. Russia attacked Ukraine at the end of February.

## The world's oldest person has died

The world's oldest person at the moment has died. She was Japanese, Kane Tanaka. Tanaka was 119 years old.

Tanaka was fairly healthy right up to her death. She lived in a care home.

Tanaka had many hobbies. She played board games and practised calligraphy, that is, the art of writing. Kane Tanaka's favourite treat was chocolate.

## Exercise at the airport

![Helsinki-Vantaa airport, new terminal, passengers at Finnair check-in.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3773,h_2122,x_0,y_362/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9314686239bc17787f3/1647951149)

At Helsinki-Vantaa airport, operations in an accident situation are being practised. The exercise is held on Wednesday.

The exercise is large. The rescue service says that about 450 people are taking part, as well as many emergency vehicles, such as ambulances and fire engines.

During the exercise there will be smoke around Helsinki-Vantaa airport. The smoke is not dangerous.

On Tuesday it is windy across the whole country and the weather feels cold. In Lapland and Kainuu a little snow may fall; elsewhere the sun mostly shines.
