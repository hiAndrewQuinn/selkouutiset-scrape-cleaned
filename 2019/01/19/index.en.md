# Saturday 19.1.2019 (radio)

A political dispute may arise over fighter jet purchases. For many immigrant women, sexual violence is part of family life. Travelling overland is of interest. Climate change is visible in European river traffic.

## Yle asked about fighter jet purchases

Finland now has 64 Hornet fighter jets, which are starting to get old. Photo: AOP

The parties disagree about how many fighter jets Finland needs. Yle asked the parties about the matter.

![Survey graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10591733)

Open image viewer

The SDP, the Left Alliance, the Greens and the Finns Party say that the number of new fighter jets may be smaller than planned. The other parties want the number of fighter jets not to be changed.

Finland has made requests for tenders for 64 fighter jets. That is the same number as Finland now has Hornet fighter jets.

Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö of the Blue party says that Finland needs as many as 100 fighter jets.

## Immigrant women experience sexual violence

![Ljudmila Kettunen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10603397)

Open image viewer

Ljudmila Kettunen. Photo: Monika-Naiset liitto ry

For many immigrant women, sexual violence is part of family life. This is what the director of a women's shelter, Ljudmila Kettunen, says.

Most of the women do not think that they are victims. They come to the shelter only when something really serious has happened. Some come only after an outsider has intervened.

Ljudmila Kettunen runs the shelter of the Monika Women's Union. It is the only shelter in Finland that is intended especially for immigrant women and the women's children. The union's crisis centre and shelter have about 700 clients every year.

Immigrant women experience violence 2 to 3 times more often than women in the majority population. Of the perpetrators, 75 percent were men with an immigrant background.

## Travelling overland is a new trend

![Passengers arrive in Helsinki on the Allegro train from St Petersburg on 2 January.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10592145)

Open image viewer

Passengers arrive in Helsinki on the Allegro train from St Petersburg on 2 January. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Many tourists are worried about climate change. Many want to choose the train.

Travelling overland is becoming a new trend. However, information about routes and options is hard to find. Train journeys cannot be compared as easily as flights.

A journey to the world overland takes more time than flying. Journeys are usually also more expensive than flying.

On Saturday, a shadow event about travelling overland was held in Helsinki. It was held in connection with the travel fair.

## Drought is troubling the Rhine

![Photo of industrial buildings in Mannheim. In the foreground is the Rhine, whose water level is lower than normal. The ferry has been temporarily closed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10604521)

Open image viewer

Drought on the Rhine is becoming expensive when goods have to be transported overland. Photo: Ronald Witterk / EPA

Climate change is visible in European river traffic.

Large German industrial companies are worried that the amount of water in the Rhine is decreasing. The Rhine is Europe's most important transport route for industry. At the end of last year, ship traffic on the Rhine had to be stopped for almost 2 months.

The weather was dry for a long time, and the river became too shallow for ships. The water of the Rhine comes from rain and from meltwater from the glaciers of the Alps.

The glaciers are shrinking because of global warming.
