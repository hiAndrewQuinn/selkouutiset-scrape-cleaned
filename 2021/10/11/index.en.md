# Monday 11.10.2021 (radio)

President of Estonia. Corona patients. Child marriages. Remote work. The weather.

## President of Estonia

![Estonia's outgoing president Kersti Kaljulaid and elected president Alar Karis meeting at the end of August 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3072,h_1728,x_0,y_158/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-864066615f022f6a55d/1633616519)

Estonia's former president Kersti Kaljulaid and new president Alar Karis. Photo: Ken Mürk / ERR

Finland's neighbouring country Estonia has got a new president. He is Alar Karis. Karis started in the post of president on Monday.

Alar Karis is 63 years old. Karis was previously the director of the Estonian National Museum. He has also been the rector of the University of Tartu.

Karis was chosen as president of Estonia at the end of August. In Estonia, parliament chooses the president. In Estonia the president has less power than in Finland.

## More corona patients

![Patient in intensive care, equipment.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5140,h_2891,x_0,y_16/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6679305ea2c590d6c75/1587998801)

Some corona patients need intensive care. Photo: Tiina Mäki / Oulu University Hospital

The number of corona patients is growing in Finland's hospitals.

The Institute for Health and Welfare says that 180 people are now getting hospital care because of corona. That is 9 more than on Friday.

The number of corona deaths has also grown. Since Friday, 4 new deaths have come. During the whole corona time, 1100 people have died in Finland because of corona.

## International Day of the Girl

![Afghan bride](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9222089)

Open image viewer

Many girls are married too early. Photo: Farahanaz Karimy/EPA

On Monday International Day of the Girl has been celebrated. The child rights organisation Save the Children reminds us of the situation of girls during the corona time.

Corona has increased child marriages in many countries, because there are more poor families. Schools have also been closed because of corona.

The child rights organisation Save the Children says that 60 girls die every day because of pregnancy or childbirth. Deaths are especially in Africa.

## Remote work recommendation ends

![An office worker works.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_630/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-762807600184f3a3ff5/1702640080)

Open image viewer

Many have worked remotely at home during the corona time. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The recommendation for remote work from the corona time will end soon. The remote work recommendation is valid only for this week.

Workplaces are now looking for new ways to organise work. A Yle investigation says that many firms intend to combine remote work and on-site work.

Firms say that remote work has worked well in many jobs. However, workplaces consider it important that employees also meet each other and come up with new ideas together.

Returning to on-site work is possible, but firms must also take care of employees' safety. That may be difficult, because an employee does not have to say whether they have had corona vaccinations or not.

## Tuesday's weather

On Tuesday the weather continues rainy in a large part of the country. It rains most in the south and east of the country, and in the north.

During the day the temperature is still close to 10 degrees in a large part of the country, but after the rain the weather gets colder across the whole country.
