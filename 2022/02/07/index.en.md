# Monday 7.2.2022 (radio)

Corona situation. Harassment of journalists. Protest in Canada. Australia's borders open. New ship for Viking Line. Weather forecast.

## Corona situation

Almost 20,000 new corona infections were confirmed in Finland over the weekend. About 25 per cent of the new corona infections were confirmed in the Helsinki region.

A new omicron subvariant of the corona virus is now spreading in the Helsinki and Uusimaa region. It spreads very easily and quickly.

The new omicron subvariant does not usually cause serious disease in people who have had corona vaccinations.

## Harassment of journalists

![Convoy Finland, a movement, organised a protest in central Helsinki on Friday 4 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4999,h_2811,x_0,y_288/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91089261fda82e034af/1644014241)

Open image viewer

The work of journalists was harassed during the weekend demonstration. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The discussion about the big demonstration over the weekend continues. The demonstrators opposed corona restrictions and the high price of petrol in Helsinki.

The editors-in-chief of news media say that harassing journalists at the demonstration was wrong. The demonstrators threatened journalists and took work tools away from journalists.

The editors-in-chief hope that the demonstrators will let journalists do their work in peace.

There is also a protest against corona restrictions in Canada.

The protest is in the Canadian capital, Ottawa, and it has already lasted 10 days. Because of the protest, the city centre is blocked.

The mayor of Ottawa says that a state of emergency now applies in the city.

## Australia's borders open

![Scott Morrison](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5170,h_2908,x_0,y_74/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-87155161779882e7c2c/1635227854)

Australia has decided to open its borders. Australian Prime Minister Scott Morrison said so.

In 2 weeks, tourists with corona vaccinations will be able to enter the country again. Australia closed its borders almost 2 years ago because of corona.

## New ship for Viking Line

![Audience at Ruissalo watching M/S Viking Glory arrive in Turku, a red ship on icy sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5304,h_2983,x_0,y_290/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-911536620121a624434/1644241532)

Open image viewer

Viking Glory arrived in Turku on Sunday. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

The ferry company Viking Line has received a new ship. The ship's name is Viking Glory.

The Viking Glory ship arrived in Turku on Sunday afternoon. The ship was built in China. The journey to Finland took 5 weeks.

The Viking Glory ship will start sailing between Turku and Stockholm in March. Viking Glory is a passenger car ferry, that is, it carries passengers and cars.

Viking Line hopes that the new ship will attract many passengers. Because of the corona situation, ferry travel has decreased a lot.
