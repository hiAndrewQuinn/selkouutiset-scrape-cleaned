## Patient safety act

![Picture of the Riksdag voting board, with 109 yes votes, 38 no votes, 1 blank vote and 51 absent.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4362,h_2453,x_0,y_310/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-101002163287ba4f0fc4/1663597541)

Parliament's clear majority supported the patient safety act. Photo: Lehtikuva

Parliament has approved the new patient safety act. A broad majority supported the law. 109 MPs supported the law. Only 38 MPs opposed the law.

The patient safety act means that nurses can be ordered to work during a strike, that is, during a labour dispute. The aim is to protect patients' lives in hospitals. Regional state administrative agencies can also postpone or interrupt strikes.

There is a big dispute in Finland about nurses' working conditions. The court has banned nurses' strikes in intensive care wards in three hospital districts. On Monday the court also banned the nurses' strike in Helsinki's city home care.

## Queen Elizabeth's funeral

![The queen's coffin is carried towards the altar.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_300/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1009785632850634f3f3/1663586530)

The blessing ceremony was held at Westminster Abbey. Photo: Ben Stansall / AFP

Queen Elizabeth has been buried in Britain. The blessing ceremony was held at Westminster Abbey. The royal family and many leaders of different countries were present. Finland's President Sauli Niinistö and Niinistö's spouse, Mrs Jenni Haukio, also took part in Queen Elizabeth's blessing ceremony.

After the blessing ceremony the queen's coffin was taken to Windsor Castle, where the queen was buried next to Prince Philip. Prince Philip was Queen Elizabeth's spouse.

Queen Elizabeth died on 8 September. She had been queen for over 70 years.

## Finland's economy worsens

![Blue-green petrol station sign with red light letters showing petrol and diesel prices. The lowest price for diesel is 1 euro and 989 cents.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_289/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99458962fa3b78663e5/1660566821)

Open image viewer

Rising fuel costs, for example, have increased inflation. Photo: Juha-Petri Koponen / Yle

Finland's economy is turning for the worse. This is what the Ministry of Finance and the Research Institute of the Finnish Economy (ETLA) forecast. Inflation, that is, the rise in prices, slows economic growth, for example. The Ministry of Finance estimates that inflation will rise to 6.5 percent this year.

Domestic production, that is, gross domestic product, grows by 1.7 percent this year, but only by 0.5 percent next year. If the forecast comes true, gross domestic product will grow less next year than this year.

The worsening of the economy makes public finances more difficult.

## Food company Atria

![Atria's factory in Sahalahti, Kangasala, photographed from outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-100985163285c244eaa3/1663589515)

Open image viewer

Atria's factory in Sahalahti, Kangasala. Photo: Juha Kokkala / Yle

Food company Atria may close its factory in Kangasala in Pirkanmaa. The factory makes food products, for example from broiler chicken.

Atria will start change negotiations on the jobs of all 130 people at the Sahalahti factory. Atria may close the factory, because Atria wants to save about 5 million euros a year.

Later Atria intends to start change negotiations also at another poultry factory, in Seinäjoki.
