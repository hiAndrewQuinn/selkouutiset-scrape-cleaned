# Saturday 18.5.2019 (radio)

Healthcare has become more expensive than prices in general. Robots have been introduced in Finnish schools. Sri Lanka marks the anniversary of the end of its civil war. The Eurovision final is held in the evening.

## Healthcare has become more expensive

![A doctor listens to a patient's lungs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8103185)

Open image viewer

Healthcare is now as much as 30 per cent more expensive than in 2015. Photo: Milla Takala / Lehtikuva

Healthcare has become more expensive than prices in general.

Healthcare is now 17-30 per cent more expensive than in 2015. Consumer prices in general have risen by about 3 per cent since 2015.

The biggest price rise is in health services, which the state and municipalities can influence. In 2016 Kela reimbursements were cut and a 50 euro co-payment was introduced. Last year hospital charges went up.

Statistics show that prescription medicine prices have fallen by almost 20 per cent since 2015. However, the fall is not visible to the customer. The reason is that, at the same time, Kela reimbursements fell.

## A robot helps a sick child at school

![Tablet computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7857273)

Open image viewer

A pupil can, for example in hospital or at home, go to school with the help of a robot and a tablet. Photo: Mikko Kuusisalo / Yle

School robots have been introduced in Finland.

Children with long-term illnesses can take part in their own class's lessons with the help of a robot. The robot is in the classroom and sends video to the child, who is at home or in hospital. The picture is shown on the pupil's tablet computer.

The pupil can raise a hand and speak through the robot, using the tablet. They can also turn the robot's head, which has a camera.

Through the robot you can also talk with classmates and the teacher. It is also possible to do group work with the robot.

Robots are in use in about 10 municipalities.

## Sri Lanka has marked a day of remembrance

![Two Sri Lankans read a newspaper on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10750374)

Open image viewer

The day of remembrance for the end of the civil war in Sri Lanka is overshadowed by the threat of terrorism. Photo: M.A. Pushpa Kumara / AOP

Sri Lanka marks the anniversary of the end of its civil war. The civil war ended 10 years ago.

At the weekend the war's victims have been remembered. Security measures are tight, because the country has been unsettled since the Easter terror attacks.

The war between the Sri Lankan government and the Tamil rebels lasted almost 40 years. At least 100,000 people died in the war, and more than 20,000 people disappeared.

After the war, Sri Lanka's economy grew. The country became a popular tourist destination. However, the Easter terror attacks broke the peaceful period. Almost 260 people died when extremist Islamists attacked churches and hotels.

The Eurovision Song Contest final is today. 26 countries are in the final in Tel Aviv, Israel.

Finland's Eurovision representative Darude, that is Ville Virtanen, was knocked out of the contest already in the first semi-final.
