## The danger of terrorism

![Head of the Security Intelligence Service Antti Pelttari and deputy head Seppo Ruotsalainen at the Supo press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10125916)

The danger of terrorism has not ended in Finland. This is what the Security Intelligence Service, Supo, says.

Last summer, the Security Intelligence Service assessed that the danger of terrorism had grown. At that time the Security Intelligence Service raised the terrorism threat level from level 1 to level 2. There are 4 levels.

Now the Security Intelligence Service says that the threat has remained just as high. One reason is Islamist propaganda.

The Security Intelligence Service works against terrorism. For example, it monitors about 370 people who have, for example, terrorist training.

## Product packaging

![A dairy product shelf in a grocery shop](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10125674)

Open image viewer

The customer must notice the packaging in the grocery shop. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

New products are constantly arriving on grocery shop shelves. Often they disappear quickly, because customers do not buy them.

Packaging is an important part of a new product. The packaging must look interesting so that customers notice it. If a customer does not notice a product within 4 seconds, they will not buy it.

The packaging must also work well, so that the customer buys it again. Bad packaging can kill a product.

## The Arctic immigrants of the year

The Arctic immigrants of the year 2018 have been chosen. They are Firuza Miller-Tissari from Rovaniemi, who is Uzbek, and Omar Shariif Hassan from Kemi, who is Somali.

The choice was made by the Arctic Immigrants association, which works in Lapland.

## Gender-neutral bus card

![Fölin travel cards.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10126212)

Open image viewer

Turku is considering whether a bus card must show whether the card holder is a woman or a man. Photo: Sannimari Lehtilä / Yle

Turku intends to introduce a gender-neutral bus card. The new bus cards may come into use as early as the summer.

A gender-neutral bus card does not show whether the card holder is a woman or a man.

Today, the gender of the card holder is shown on Turku's bus card. In that case, it is harder for people to misuse discount cards. Discount cards are personal.

People in Turku have talked a lot about the matter, after a ticket inspector asked about a child's gender. The child had a boy's bus card and pink clothes.

"We have learned that gender is not something that has to show on a card," says Turku's head of public transport.

## A tall sandcastle is being planned

![A sandcastle rises to great heights](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9812244)

Open image viewer

A sandcastle this tall was built in Germany last summer. Photo: Fried Riedemann Vogel / EPA-EFE

People in Lappeenranta intend to build the world's tallest sandcastle next summer. The castle will be 18 metres high, which is as high as a 6-storey building.

The tallest sandcastle in the world so far was built in Germany last summer. It was a little under 17 metres high.

Building the sandcastle in Lappeenranta starts in May. The construction takes 2 months.
