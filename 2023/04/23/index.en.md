## Finns in Sudan

![Sudanese army soldiers stand on a street next to a military vehicle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4288,h_2412,x_0,y_339/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1102195644203cdcc35d/1682048205)

There is a civil war in Sudan. Photo: AFP

The Finnish government is following the situation of Finns in Sudan. This is what Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says.

In Sudan, in North Africa, a civil war has started. That is why, for example, many European countries intend to bring their own citizens out of Sudan.

Foreign Minister Haavisto says that Finland will not send soldiers to fetch Finns from Sudan. Finland intends to cooperate with other countries, so that Finns can be brought to safety.

There are about 10 Finns in Sudan.

## Shortage of nurses

![A doctor in protective equipment checks an ECG on a screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5615,h_3158,x_1,y_585/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9292266232bad70338c/1667384282)

More nurses are needed in Finland. Photo: Wavebreak Media ltd / Alamy/All Over Press

The discussion about the shortage of nurses in Finland continues. There are too few social and health care workers.

Finland also needs nurses from abroad. For example, the Finns Party wants nurses from Southern Europe to come to Finland.

However, experts estimate that nurses and other social and health care workers cannot be found in Europe.

Finland's Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment also estimates that there are no spare nurses in Europe.

The nurses' union Tehy reminds that many countries compete for nurses. That is why it is difficult to get European nurses to work in Finland in particular.

## Problems with solar panels

![Solar panels being installed on the roof of a detached house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1613,h_907,x_1,y_47/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10985416437cb25a064a/1681378234)

Open image viewer

Solar panels are now being installed a lot. Photo: Antti Seppälä / Yle

More complaints are made about solar panels than before.

Last year buyers of solar panels reported problems to the authorities more than 200 times. That is 2 times as many as a year earlier.

The problems included, for example, installation errors, too high prices and long delivery times.

The cause of the problems is that solar power is growing in popularity quickly. That is why many new companies have come to the field, and there are not enough good installers.

## Ulf Sundqvist

![Ulf Sundqvist.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1724,h_969,x_0,y_37/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-110300164452a5ff4152/1682254493)

Ulf Sundqvist. Photo: Pekka Sipilä / Yle

Finnish politician Ulf Sundqvist has died. He was 78 years old.

Sundqvist held many important posts. He was SDP chairman, an MP and a minister.

Sundqvist died on Saturday in Helsinki after a short illness.

## The weather

![Monday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1103079644551d06d2a7/1682264595)

The new week is rainy. In the north it snows on Monday morning and the driving conditions on the roads are poor.

Rain will come to the south on Tuesday, and after that rain showers will come almost every day. The good news is that the rain cleans the air of street dust and pollen.

On Monday the temperature in the south is between 10 and 15 degrees, in the north between 1 and 5 degrees.

On Tuesday the weather warms in the south, and the temperature may be over 15 degrees. In the north it is only a few degrees above zero.
