## Social and health care reform

![TAYS hospital building in Tampere in December 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_127/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7505665fca3f3523e8a/1607091154)

Tampere University Hospital TAYS. Photo: Miikka Varila / Yle

The reform of social and health services, the sote reform, has progressed a lot. The Social and Health Committee of Parliament has dealt with the proposal for the sote reform. The committee supports the proposal.

Parliament will decide on the sote reform perhaps as early as next week.
In the sote reform, social and health services and fire and rescue services are moved from the municipalities to 21 wellbeing areas.

The purpose of the sote reform is that Finns get good social and health services everywhere in Finland.

## Seasonal workers

![Ukrainian seasonal workers load parsley growing trays at Varpio garden.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81097460ad00a64d456/1621951222)

Open image viewer

Foreign seasonal workers at Varpio garden. Photo: Miki Wallenius / Yle

Many seasonal workers from abroad will come to Finnish berry farms this summer too.

More than 10,000 seasonal workers will come to the berry farms from abroad. Many of them come from Ukraine. More than 50 charter flights will bring berry pickers to Finland.

Berry farms must make a corona plan. The plan must say how the berry farms protect themselves against coronavirus infections, or what is done if a worker falls ill with coronavirus.

## Corona restrictions

![Open air event at the harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10905100)

Open image viewer

In many areas, gatherings can again be organised without restrictions. Photo: Risto Koskinen / Yle.

The corona situation has improved and corona restrictions have been reduced. In the Helsinki region, Kerava and Central Uusimaa, gatherings may now be organised, but at gatherings there must be safety distances.

In East Uusimaa and almost all of West Uusimaa the gathering restrictions have ended, and in those areas gatherings may be organised without safety distances.

On Tuesday 73 new coronavirus infections have been reported.

## Storm

![Birch tree fallen on a terraced house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82101360c860b864ae0/1623744775)

The storm also knocked down trees onto houses, for example. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

There has been a storm in Finland. The storm cut power to many homes during the night and on Tuesday during the day.

On Tuesday during the day more than 60,000 homes were without power. Power faults have been repaired all day.

The storm also tore boat piers off the shores and roof sheets off houses. The storm also knocked down many trees.
