## Stora Enso

![A Stora Enso factory. Smoke rises from its chimney.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5128,h_2884,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1130008648abd0ec1383/1686817372)

Stora Enso is closing the Sunila pulp mill. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The forestry company Stora Enso plans to reduce the number of workers.

For example, Stora Enso plans to close the Sunila pulp mill. The mill is in the city of Kotka.

Stora Enso plans that the operations of the Sunila pulp mill will end still during this year. The plan concerns 250 workers in Kotka.

Stora Enso is also closing operations abroad, that is, in the Netherlands, Poland and Estonia. In addition, Stora Enso is reducing office workers.

Stora Enso's plans may mean that more than 1,000 workers in total will be left without a job.

## Interest rates

The ECB's main building is in Frankfurt in Germany. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

The European Central Bank, that is, the ECB, is raising interest rates. The ECB said on Thursday that it will raise its key interest rates by 0.25 percentage points.

With interest rates, the ECB is fighting inflation, that is, the rise in prices. The ECB has already been raising interest rates since last summer.

The ECB's interest rate decisions have also affected the interest rates on many housing loans. Many households' costs have grown, because when the interest rate on a housing loan has risen, paying off the loan is more expensive than before.

## The Finnish economy

![People in a shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_0,y_214/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9361156245a2f6dcdae/1648813483)

Open image viewer

The economy grows if people spend money. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The Finnish economy will not grow this year. This is the estimate of the Ministry of Finance, which prepares Finland's economic policy.

The economy will not grow this year, because prices and interest rates have risen.

Next year, however, the rise in prices will slow down, and people will again be able to afford to spend more money. That is why the economy will start to grow next year.

In 2025, the economy will grow even faster, the Ministry of Finance estimates. One reason for this is the spread of environmentally friendly technology.

Finland has an important football match on Friday. Finland plays a European Championship qualifying match against Slovenia.

In the European Championship qualifiers, it is decided which countries will get into the final tournament of the European Championship.

So far in the qualifiers, Finland has beaten Northern Ireland and lost to Denmark.

The football match between Finland and Slovenia will be played on Friday at the Helsinki Olympic Stadium.

The European Championship in football is held every 4 years. The next final tournament is in a year's time, in Germany.
