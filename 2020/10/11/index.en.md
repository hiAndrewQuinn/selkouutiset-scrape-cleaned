## Managers often have longer commutes

![IMG_8061 (1).jpg](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10525731)

Open image viewer

In Helsinki you can go to work by metro.  Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

The commutes of Finns differ a lot. In addition to where they live, the commute is affected by the job and the employee's position at work. For example, the commutes of managers and senior white-collar employees are often longer than those of junior white-collar employees and workers.

On average, Finns spend 23 minutes on their commute one way.

About 30 percent of Finns have a commute of only 10 minutes or less. The longest commutes are in the Helsinki area

## Crimes committed under the influence of drugs

![Police put handcuffs on a detainee's hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5493,h_3090,x_0,y_532/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4988675b4f138653e79/1583757350)

In many big cities, more crimes are already committed under the influence of drugs than while drunk. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

In Finland, more crimes are committed under the influence of drugs than before. Fewer crimes are committed under the influence of alcohol, i.e. while drunk, than before.

Yle has compared different crimes.

In many big cities, more crimes are already committed under the influence of drugs than while drunk. The reason is that drug use has increased in Finland.

## Restaurant restrictions tightened

![People in a restaurant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4087,h_2299,x_0,y_421/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7108635f40ed217fbff/1598091056)

Open image viewer

Restaurant restrictions are trying to stop the spread of the coronavirus. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Restaurant opening restrictions have been tightened today, Sunday, in 6 regions. Restaurants must stop selling alcohol at 22:00 and close their doors at 23:00. Only half of the usual number of customers may be in restaurants, so that people can keep safe distances.

The areas where the new restrictions came into force are Uusimaa, Southwest Finland, Pirkanmaa, Kanta-Häme, Ostrobothnia and South Ostrobothnia.

On Sunday, 149 new coronavirus infections were reported in Finland. Most new infections are in Helsinki, where there are 64.

## Sunday is girls' day

![Girls with their backs to the camera.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10946908)

Open image viewer

Girls' Day is a celebration.  Photo: Tiina Karppi / Yle

Today, Sunday, International Girls' Day is celebrated.
Girls' Day reminds us that girls are often discriminated against because of their age and gender everywhere in the world.

This year, harassment on the internet is especially reminded of. The children's rights organisation Plan International says that girls are harassed on the internet about 30 times more than boys.

Girls' Day is also a celebration. It reminds us that girls have strength and opportunities.
