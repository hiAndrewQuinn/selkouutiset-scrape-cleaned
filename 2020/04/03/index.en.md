## The interest cap on quick loans will be halved

![A woman takes out a loan on her phone](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9331210)

Open image viewer

Quick loans are applied for on the phone, for example. Photo: Yle

Finland is capping the interest on quick loans.

The highest interest that can be charged on quick loans will be cut to half of the current level. In future, the interest on a quick loan may be at most 10%.

A quick loan is a loan that is easy to get, but the interest on a quick loan is often high. The coronavirus situation is making many people's financial situation worse, and the demand for quick loans may increase.

## Support applications will cause a big queue

![A Kela support application form.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7542275)

Open image viewer

Perhaps 100,000 support applications will come to Kela and the unemployment funds in two weeks. Photo: Yle

The coronavirus situation is causing a big queue in handling support applications at Kela and at the unemployment funds.

Many people have been laid off or are becoming unemployed, because many companies are now doing very badly. Kela and the unemployment funds are receiving a very large number of support applications. Handling the applications may take several months. Kela is hiring more employees to handle the applications.

Kela asks that support applications be made online, because it is not a good idea to go to Kela's offices because of the coronavirus situation.

## The coronavirus situation is increasing alcohol sales

![Alko](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8753088)

Open image viewer

A selection of beer in a shop. Photo: Yle

Alcohol sales in shops and in Alko have increased because of the coronavirus situation.

Sales of beers and long drinks in shops have increased by about 10%. At Alko shops, many customers are buying more alcohol at once than before. Sales may grow even more, because restaurants are closing.

Buying food to stock up at shops has decreased. When the coronavirus situation began, people bought a lot of food and goods from shops. Now the amounts bought are back to normal.

## Many parcels are now moving through the post

![Postal parcels on a conveyor belt](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10549198)

Open image viewer

Parcels being sorted at the post office. Photo: Anne Elhaimer / Yle

The number of postal parcels has increased a lot because of the coronavirus situation. People are now ordering goods from online shops, because it is not a good idea to go to an ordinary shop.

Posti delivers many times more parcels to people's homes than usual. Parcel delivery may take a little longer than usual.

Because of the coronavirus situation, Posti does not hand the parcel to the recipient in person. Instead, Posti puts the parcel on the ground or on the floor.

## The price of petrol fell

![A woman filling her car with fuel.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11020573)

Open image viewer

The price of petrol has fallen because consumption has decreased. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The price of petrol has fallen clearly in recent weeks.

The price of petrol has fallen because people's movement has decreased a lot because of the coronavirus situation. That is why the consumption of oil has also decreased.

For example, far fewer kilometres are being driven by car than usual.
