## Restaurants under close watch

![People on the Laituri terrace of the Lasipalatsi in Helsinki on 1 June](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11379849)

Restaurants may be open until 23:00. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The police are monitoring restaurants more closely this weekend. Restaurants opened on Monday. This is the first weekend when restaurants have been open after more than 2 months.

The Regional State Administrative Agency has given restaurants many instructions that restaurants must follow so that the coronavirus does not spread. For example, all customers must have a seat in the restaurant. There must be space between people, that is, safety distances. Inside the restaurant, there may only be 50% of the usual number of people.

If a restaurant does not follow the instructions, the police may close the restaurant.

## The corona situation may change children's summer

![](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5277014)

Open image viewer

It is nice to spend time with grandparents in the summer. Photo: YLE / Seppo Luotohaara

The corona situation is changing children's summer in many families. In many families, grandparents look after children in the summer, or children go to their grandparents. In some families, children may stay with their grandparents for example for a week, if the parents are at work.

This summer grandparents may not be able to help with childcare. In Finland there is a guideline that because of the corona situation, it is not a good idea to visit people over 70 years old.

It is good to meet grandparents outside and keep safety distances.

## There is still time to report spring observations

![Peacock butterfly (Aglais io)](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10640544)

Open image viewer

Peacock butterflies start to appear in spring in April.

The Nature League's spring monitoring ends this weekend. Spring monitoring means that people from different parts of Finland have reported to the Nature League observations of spring, for example which plants or animals they have seen.

The observations have been used to get information on how spring is progressing in different parts of Finland.

Now the spring monitoring ends for this year. For example, almost all migratory birds have already come to Finland, and summer has begun.
