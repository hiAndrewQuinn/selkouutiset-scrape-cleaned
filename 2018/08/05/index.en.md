# Sunday 5.8.2018 (radio)

Fewer children are injured in traffic. Marriages of Iraqis and Finns under watch. Venezuela's president alleges a murder attempt. A museum plane fell in Switzerland. Action star Steven Seagal becomes Russia's special envoy. Few mosquitoes this summer.

## Fewer children are injured in traffic than before

![Small children playing in a traffic area built for children.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10331449)

Open image viewer

The "Children in traffic" sign warns drivers, for example, about small schoolchildren. Photo: Esa Huuhko / Yle

Far fewer children are injured in traffic than 10 years ago.

The police say that injuries have gone down by 40% in 10 years.

Next week 60,000 small children start school, so drivers must be really careful.

In total, about half a million schoolchildren travel on roads and streets.

## Marriages of Iraqis and Finns are suspected

![a man and a woman kiss in the sunset](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10245385)

Open image viewer

The Finnish Immigration Service suspects that several marriages of Finns and Iraqis are sham marriages. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

This year 130 Iraqis have applied for a residence permit because they are married to a Finn. The Finnish Immigration Service says that such applications have increased.

But the Finnish Immigration Service suspects that many marriages between Finns and Iraqis are not real. The agency thinks they are sham marriages. A sham marriage is made so that a person gets asylum in Finland.

This year the Finnish Immigration Service has rejected 40% of the residence permit applications in which the reason for applying is marriage to a Finn.

The newspaper Sunnuntaisuomalainen reported on the matter.

## 20 died in a museum plane crash in Switzerland

![The rescue operation at the accident is under way.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10338619)

Open image viewer

The cause of the accident is being investigated from the remains of the crashed plane. Photo: CHSRF

In Switzerland, an almost 80-year-old plane fell to the ground yesterday. There were 20 people on the plane. They died in the accident, say the police.

The cause of the accident is hard to find out, because the plane was an old museum plane. The plane did not have, for example, devices that record speech.

## Venezuela's president alleges a murder attempt

![President Maduro together with soldiers on a speaker's platform](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10338481)

Open image viewer

Venezuela's president Nicolas Maduro was giving a speech at a military parade. He was evacuated because of an explosion. Photo: Miguel Gutiérrez/EPA

Venezuela's president Nicolas Maduro says that someone has tried to murder him.

The president was speaking on TV when a remote-controlled aircraft, a drone, exploded near him. The president was not injured in the explosion.

The president blames Venezuela's far-right groups, Colombia and the USA for the explosion.

A Venezuelan rebel group says that it carried out the attack.

## American action hero becomes Russia's envoy

![Steven Seagal](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10338624)

Russia has appointed the American actor Steven Seagal as a special envoy.

Seagal will promote cooperation between Russia and the USA, for example in culture.

Seagal got Russian citizenship two years ago.

## Few mosquitoes in Finland

![Mosquitoes on human skin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10338360)

Open image viewer

No more mosquitoes are coming for the rest of the year, a researcher estimates. Photo: AOP

There are few mosquitoes in Finland now, because the summer has been warm and dry.

Mosquitoes breed for example in pools of water, but the pools have dried up in the warm weather.

Billions of mosquitoes have not been able to hatch this summer, a researcher estimates.
