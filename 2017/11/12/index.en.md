# Sunday 12.11.2017 (radio)

The Finnish forest company UPM plans to build a big mill in Uruguay in South America. Teemu Laajalo started as a bishop of the Lutheran church in Helsinki. Father's Day was celebrated on Sunday. Piritta Näkkäläjärvi is Sámi of the Year 2017.

## A pulp mill in Uruguay for UPM

![The former Botnia pulp mill, now UPM](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6822468)

The company UPM already has a pulp mill in Uruguay, but the new mill will be bigger. Photo: Leo La Valle / EPA

The Finnish forest company UPM plans to build a big mill in Uruguay in South America. The mill will be the world's biggest pulp mill.

In a pulp mill, pulp is made from wood. Pulp is needed to make paper. At the moment, the world's biggest pulp mill is in Finland.

If UPM's plan comes true, the new mill will produce as much as 2 million tonnes of pulp a year.

Uruguay is a good place for a pulp mill, because it is a good place to grow eucalyptus trees. Eucalyptus grows much faster than, for example, the Finnish pine.

Experts say that making pulp and paper is profitable. For example, in Asia millions of people are getting out of poverty, and they are starting to buy things like toilet paper and kitchen paper. They also order goods from online shops that are packed in paper.

## Helsinki's new bishop

![Helsinki's new bishop Teemu Laajasalo preached at his consecration mass in Helsinki Cathedral on 12 November 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9927743)

Helsinki's new bishop Teemu Laajasalo in Helsinki Cathedral on Sunday. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The Finnish Lutheran church has got a new bishop. Teemu Laajasalo is the new Bishop of Helsinki. Laajasalo was consecrated as bishop on Sunday in Helsinki Cathedral.

Laajasalo now leads the church's work in the Helsinki area.

There are 9 bishops in the Finnish Lutheran church. Each bishop leads the church's work in their own area. There is also an archbishop, who is the leader of the church in the whole of Finland.

## Father's Day

![Father's Day drawings made by a child.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9913631)

Open image viewer

Many children make a card for their father on Father's Day. Photo: Elina Mäyrä / Yle

Father's Day was celebrated on Sunday. In Finland, Father's Day is always on the second Sunday of November. Father's Day came to Finland from the USA in the 1970s.

Statistics Finland's figures show that there are about 1.2 million fathers in Finland. Sunday was a day of celebration for all of them.

## Sámi of the Year

![Yle Sápmi, Yle Ođđasat, Pirita Näkkäläjärvi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9925804)

Piritta Näkkäläjärvi is Sámi of the Year 2017. Näkkäläjärvi was chosen as Sámi of the Year because she has worked for Sámi-language media.

Näkkäläjärvi was head of Yle Sámi from 2012 to 2016. At that time, Yle Sámi started to make many new Sámi-language programmes for television, radio and the internet.

The Sámi association of the Helsinki region chooses the Sámi of the Year. The idea is to keep the Sámi language and Sámi culture visible.

The old Sámi culture is part of Lapland. Today, many Sámi have moved south, especially to the Helsinki region.

About 10,000 Sámi live in Finland. Sámi also live in Norway, Sweden and Russia.
