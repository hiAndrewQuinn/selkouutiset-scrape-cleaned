# Monday 20.5.2019 (radio)

The beer rally from Estonia has started again. Summer cottages are being rented out busily. The Kuopio city library is stopping covering books in plastic. A new bird species has been spotted in Finland.

## Finns are again buying beer in Estonia

![A man pulls beer trolleys behind him.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9853125)

Many Finns bring alcohol from Estonia to Finland. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / AOP

Finns have started the beer rally from Estonia again, the Brewers' Association says.

A beer rally means that Finns go to Estonia to buy beer, which they then bring to Finland.

Last year, beer imports from Estonia fell, because Estonia had raised its alcohol tax. Now imports have started to grow again, because Finland raised its beer tax at the start of the year.

Between February and April, 40 per cent more beer was imported from Estonia than a year earlier. Imports of cider and long drinks have also grown.

## Summer cottages should have quality and comfort

![A cottage on the shore of a lake.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9630822)

Open image viewer

There are over half a million summer cottages in Finland. Photo: Arja Lento / Yle

Summer cottages are being rented out busily now.

The most popular holiday weeks and waterside cottages have already been booked. However, you can still find a free cottage if you can move your holiday a little, or if it does not matter where the cottage is.

Esko Valkonen, a cottage entrepreneur from Mikkeli, says that cottages are booked up to a year in advance. More and more renters come from abroad.

People demand quality and comforts from a cottage. The equipment must be the same as in a detached house.

There are about half a million summer cottages in Finland. Perhaps about 15,000 of them are used for rental.

## Kuopio library gives up plastic covers

![Library books without plastic covers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10789605)

Open image viewer

In Kuopio, library books are no longer covered in plastic. Photo: Sakari Partanen / Yle

The Kuopio City Library intends to stop covering books in plastic.

Normally the covers of books are covered in plastic, so that the books stay neat and last longer.

The Kuopio City Library says that it is giving up plastic covers because it wants to promote environmental protection.

Giving up plastic covers also saves time and money. New books reach the shelves faster, when they do not need to be covered. Money is saved too, because there is no need to buy plastic. The savings can be used to buy more books, for example.

However, the plastic covers will be brought back into use if it starts to look as if the books last worse than before.

## A little bunting came to visit

![Little bunting](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10791431)

Open image viewer

The little bunting is a rare visitor to Europe. Photo: Pekka Ikonen

A new bird species has been spotted in Finland. A little bunting has been spotted in Kirkkonummi.

The little bunting is rare. It breeds and winters mainly in Asia. In Europe, outside Russia, it has been seen fewer than 10 times.

The little bunting has been seen in the Nordic countries only once before. It was seen in Sweden in October 2016.
