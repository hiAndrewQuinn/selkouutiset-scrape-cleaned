# Thursday 12.12.2019 (radio)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin has attended an EU summit in Brussels. Parliamentary elections have been held in Britain. Faisa Egge is Immigrant Entrepreneur of the Year.

## Marin in Brussels

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin at the European Union summit in Brussels.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11115304)

Finland's Prime Minister Sanna Marin has attended an EU summit in Brussels. This is the first time Marin has represented Finland at an EU summit. Marin has been prime minister for only a couple of days.

All the EU leaders take part in the summit. The Finnish prime minister told them what kind of budget Finland proposes for the European Union. Finland has made a budget proposal because Finland holds the EU presidency this autumn.

Finland's budget proposal has been criticised. Many say that Finland wants to give too much money to agriculture. In many other matters, Finland wants to save EU money.

## Britain's election

![Boris Johnson hammered a Brexit sign into his supporter's garden in Benfleet on 11 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11114128)

Conservative leader Boris Johnson promises a quick Brexit. Photo: Ben Stansall / AFP

The British voted in parliamentary elections on Thursday. The election had to be held, because the British Parliament could not decide on Brexit, that is, Britain's exit from the EU.

The Conservative Party promises voters that Britain will leave the European Union quickly. Conservative leader Boris Johnson is Britain's prime minister, and he negotiated the withdrawal agreement with the EU in the autumn. Everything is ready for a quick exit, says Johnson.

The Labour Party thinks Johnson's withdrawal agreement is bad. The Labour Party promises to negotiate a new agreement if it wins the election. After that, the British would still get to vote on the agreement and on leaving the EU.

So parliamentary elections have been held in Britain. The main theme has again been Britain's EU exit, even though the British already supported leaving the EU in the 2016 referendum.

## Immigrant Entrepreneur of the Year

![Faisa Egge receives the entrepreneur award](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_334/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6217355df1f2163450d/1576137530)

Faisa Egge (right) is Immigrant Entrepreneur of the Year. Photo: Elina Hakola / Yrittäjät

The Immigrant Entrepreneur of the Year has been chosen. It is Faisa Egge.

The choice was made by an organisation called Suomen Yrittäjät, the Federation of Finnish Enterprises. An entrepreneur is a person who has their own company.

Faisa Egge has a home care company. The company provides home care for elderly people and people with disabilities. It operates in the small city of Kokkola in Ostrobothnia, in Western Finland.

The award givers say that Egge has brought Kenyan brightness and positivity to the care sector. Egge is from Kenya.

Faisa Egge moved to Finland in 2008. She worked, studied to become a nurse, and eventually founded her own company. Now Egge's company already has almost 10 employees.
