## Coronavirus vaccinations in the EU

![People queuing for coronavirus vaccination at the vaccination point at the Tampere Messukeskus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_567/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82212060cb0876eab0e/1623918903)

People are queuing for coronavirus vaccination in Tampere. Photo: Miikka Varila / Yle

Coronavirus vaccinations are progressing well in the European Union.

The EU's goal is that 70 percent of adults have received a coronavirus vaccination by the end of July. The EU now estimates that the goal will be achieved.

However, coronavirus vaccinations are progressing at different speeds in different EU countries.

In Finland coronavirus vaccinations are progressing quite quickly. About 3.3 million Finns have already received a coronavirus vaccination.

## Handling the corona crisis in Finland

![Sign that guides to coronavirus testing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81778960bf75ef7fc76/1623661984)

Finland has handled the corona crisis well. This is what the German newspaper Der Spiegel writes.

The paper compared the corona situation of different countries. It compared, for example, the number of corona deaths, corona restrictions and the effects of corona on the economy.

Finland got the best points. Right after Finland came Luxembourg and Norway.

In Turku there were 2 fights during the night between Friday and Saturday.

One fight was at Restaurant Studio. Dozens of people took part in the fight. The police say that different groups took part in the fight. However, nobody was seriously injured.

There was also fighting on the river bank in Turku. 2 people were taken to hospital because of the fight.

## Finnish film at Cannes

![Juho Kuosmanen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_445/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83047860e8385c4a6bf/1625831629)

Open image viewer

Director Juho Kuosmanen. Photo: Juha Nurminen / Yle

A Finnish film was shown at the Cannes Film Festival on Saturday. The film's name is "Compartment No. 6" (Hytti numero 6).

The film is part of the Cannes competition. It is the most important film competition in the world.

"Compartment No. 6" is about a train journey in Russia in the 1990s. On the train a Finnish student girl and a Russian man meet. The story is from a book written by Rosa Liksom.

The film's director is Juho Kuosmanen. In a week's time it will be clear how well Kuosmanen's film does at Cannes.

Wildfires are common in California. This picture is from last year. Photo: David McNew / Getty Images

Hot and dry weather continues in California, in the USA. On Friday temperatures of over 50 degrees were even measured in California.

The terrain in California is now dry, and there are many wildfires in the area. Authorities have moved people away from the paths of the fires.
