# Tuesday 8.9.2020 (radio)

The birth rate is falling in Finland. Finland is making travel easier. Event companies are having a hard time. Vexi Salmi has died. Weather forecast.

## The birth rate is falling

![Babies at a baby meet-up in Laitila](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4959,h_2789,x_0,y_256/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6419115e440252505eb/1581515428)

Children are born in Finland at a rate of 1.35 children per woman. Photo: Paula Collin / Yle

The birth rate is falling in Finland. Fewer children are born to Finns than to people in many other countries. If the birth rate does not grow, the number of Finns will decrease. The decrease starts in the 2030s.

The Finnish economy will get worse if the number of Finns decreases. If the number of Finns decreases, it will make the pension system harder in the 2040s.

Children are born in Finland at a rate of 1.35 children per woman. It is a bad situation for Finland that the birth rate has fallen.

## Finland makes tourism easier

![Santa Claus Village in Rovaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10589803)

Open image viewer

Santa Claus Village in Rovaniemi. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Finland may make travel to Finland easier. The Finnish government is making a new law that people can come to Finland from abroad if they first take a corona test and get a negative result.

If Parliament approves the new law, people may also come to Finland from a country with many corona infections.

Now corona restrictions prevent many people from travelling to Finland. Travel restrictions are harming Finnish tourism companies, especially in Lapland.

## Event companies want support

For example, fairs are not being held now. Many people often work at fairs. Now there is no fair work. Photo: Elina Niemistö / Yle

Companies that organise events are having a hard time because of the corona situation.

Many concerts, theatre performances and fairs have been cancelled. People do not buy tickets for new events, because events can be cancelled.

Event companies say that they need support from the state. Every year many Finns work at events. Many of them may need a new job.

## Lyricist Vexi Salmi has died

![Vexi Salmi in 2012.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11533146)

Open image viewer

Vexi Salmi in 2012. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Record producer and lyricist Vexi Salmi has died. Vexi Salmi was 77 years old. Vexi Salmi wrote the lyrics for thousands of songs.

He was also a music producer and manager. For example, he was the manager of the musician Irwin Goodman, whose real name was Antti Hammarberg.

Vexi Salmi's real name was (Veikko) Olavi Salmi, but he is known by the nickname Vexi Salmi.
