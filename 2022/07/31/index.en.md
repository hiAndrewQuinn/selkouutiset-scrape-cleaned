## Electricity shortage

![A salesperson fills the shop's refrigerator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5130,h_2886,x_0,y_256/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6963465f015db83cb9d/1593925100)

Open image viewer

Chilled counters and fridges last a few hours without electricity. Photo: Tiina Jutila \ Yle

Shops are preparing for power cuts. Power cuts are possible next winter.

The reason is that there may be a shortage of electricity. That is why electricity must be saved. Power cuts can be one saving measure.

Freezer counters in grocery shops last a few hours without electricity. A bigger problem are the cash registers, which need electricity all the time.

If there is a serious shortage of electricity, some shops may be closed.

The electricity supply of the whole of Europe is in difficulties because of the war in Ukraine.

Finland's situation is easier if the new Olkiluoto nuclear power plant starts producing electricity next winter.

## Social benefits

![The Kela flag flutters in the wind.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6781075ec2889cca19f/1589807550)

Open image viewer

Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

Many social benefits go up on Monday. Benefits rise by 3.5 percent from the start of August.

For example, the labour market subsidy rises by about 26 euros a month. The full national pension rises by about 24 euros a month.

Social benefits are increased because of inflation. Inflation, that is, the rise in prices, is now fast, and it is harder to get by on social benefits.

## Problems with remote work

![A woman stretches at a remote work station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11564542)

Open image viewer

Remote work is lonely. Photo: Tommaso Altamura / AOP

Remote work is popular. Many began to work from home during the corona time.

The Finnish Institute of Occupational Health says that remote work also has problems. As many as 60 percent of remote workers miss meetings with work colleagues.

Remote work also causes problems for bosses. They do not see the employee's feelings and reactions, and that is why things can be misunderstood.

The Finnish Institute of Occupational Health studied the situation of 6 Finnish companies during the corona time.

In Savonlinna young people who died in the traffic accident on Saturday have been remembered.
