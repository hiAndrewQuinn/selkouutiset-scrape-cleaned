# Tuesday 22.8.2017 (radio)

Suspects in the stabbings were remanded in custody. The law on staying in the country illegally is going to be changed. A demonstration in Tornio. Many people looked after their health badly because they do not have enough money. Mosquitoes had a dismal summer.

## Suspects were remanded in custody

![The remand hearing of the Turku stabber.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9790606)

Open image viewer

The main suspect was heard by video link because he is still in hospital. Photo: Eino Kossila / Yle

The man who is the main suspect in the Turku stabbings has been remanded in custody. He is suspected of two murders committed with terrorist intent.

In addition, he is suspected of eight attempted murders. The main suspect admits that he carried out the acts that led to deaths, but not that they are murders. The police are still investigating whether the main suspect has given his real name and age.

Besides the main suspect, three other men have been remanded in custody. One man was released and he is no longer suspected of being involved in the stabbings.

## The law is going to be changed

![Men wearing flip-flops](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8665568)

Open image viewer

Some asylum seekers stay in Finland illegally after they have received a negative asylum decision. Photo: Toni Määttä / Yle

The government is planning changes to the law that will make it harder to stay in Finland illegally. Punishments are going to be made harsher.

At the moment, staying in the country illegally is punished with a fine. If the law changes, a person who is in Finland illegally may get as much as 6 months in prison. The law may also change so that the police can locate a person by their mobile phone.

## Not enough money for health care

![A doctor listens to a man's heart.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9403436)

Open image viewer

People cut back on health care if they do not have enough money. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Many Finns look after their own health badly because they do not have enough money. One in five says that they have not taken medicines or have not gone to a doctor or for treatment. People on low incomes in particular cut back on health care.

Kela pays compensation for medicine costs, but the customer must first pay a deductible themselves. It is 50 euros.

## The cool summer was bad for mosquitoes

![A mosquito bites](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9714790)

Open image viewer

The cool summer was dismal for mosquitoes. Photo: Tarja Porkkala

The cool summer has been bad for mosquitoes and small birds. There have been fewer mosquitoes, because in dismal weather mosquito swarms do not grow. And small birds' nesting went wrong because the early summer was so cold.
