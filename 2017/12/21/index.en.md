# Wednesday 20.12.2017 (radio)

A large study of Finns' genes and health has begun. Prosecutor General Matti Nissinen was fined for breaching his official duty. Fat from roasting ham is being collected. The elf door is this Christmas's favourite decoration. Most of the country will get snow for Christmas.

## FinnGen studies Finns' health and genes

![A doctor measures blood pressure](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9962834)

The FinnGen study collects information about the health and genes of half a million Finns. Photo: Vilja Vehkaoja / Lehtikuva

A big study of Finns' health has begun in Finland. The FinnGen study looks into Finns' genes and health, and the effect of genes on health.

Genes mean the features that a person has inherited from their parents and ancestors. Genes can, for example, be the reason why a person gets some disease more easily than someone else.

The study collects information about the genes of half a million Finns. After that, the information is combined with each participant's health information.

With the help of the study, people are trying to understand illnesses and their causes better.

The study may also lead to new medicines being developed. That is why drug companies are also interested in the study.

## A fine for the Prosecutor General

![Matti Nissinen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9986050)

Open image viewer

Prosecutor General Matti Nissinen Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Supreme Court has sentenced Prosecutor General Matti Nissinen to a fine. The Supreme Court says that Nissinen has breached his official duty.

Nissinen ordered training for his own workplace. The training was bought from a firm that was owned by Nissinen's brother.

The Ministry of Justice will decide later whether Nissinen can continue in the job of Prosecutor General.

The Prosecutor General is Finland's highest prosecutor.

## Fuel from ham fat

![Ham fat in a milk carton](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9985697)

Open image viewer

Diesel is made from ham fat Photo: Juha Kemppainen / Yle

The fat from roasting Christmas hams is again being collected in different parts of the country. There are 150 collection points for ham fat in total.

Diesel fuel is made from ham fat at Neste's refinery in Porvoo.

With the fat from one Christmas ham you can drive a car about 3 kilometres.

## The elf now goes through its own door

![An elf door on a sales table at a Christmas fair](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9981221)

Open image viewer

An elf door brings Christmas atmosphere to the home. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

This Christmas's favourite decoration is the elf door. An elf door is a small door that is attached to the wall of a home, right at floor level.

You can make an elf door yourself, but they are also sold in shops.

The elf door is the elf's own door. Through it the elf can easily peek to see whether there are good children and adults in the house.

## A white Christmas in almost the whole country

![The snow situation](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9986718)

Open image viewer

There is already snow in almost the whole country. Photo: Yle

Almost the whole country will get a white Christmas, that is, there will be snow on the ground at Christmas, says Yle meteorologist Joonas Koskela.

In Northern and Eastern Finland there is already a lot of snow now. More snow will also fall there before Christmas.

Joonas Koskela says that on Saturday, the day before Christmas Eve, there may also be snowstorms in Southern Finland.

But in South-West Finland and on the west coast Christmas may be without snow. There the temperature is close to zero and the rain falls as water.
