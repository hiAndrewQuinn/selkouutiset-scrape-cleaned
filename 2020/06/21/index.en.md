# Sunday 21.6.2020 (radio)

A patrol boat sank in the Gulf of Finland. People have died on the water and on the roads during Midsummer. There are few new corona infections. Restaurant corona restrictions are being eased. Sunday is the longest day of the year. The warm weather continues.

(The TV Clear News has a summer break. The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.)

## Patrol boat sinks at sea

![Sunken patrol boat off Loviisa at sea on Sunday 21 June 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11411192)

The patrol boat sank almost completely into the sea. The boat is 10 metres long. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

An accident has happened in the Gulf of Finland.

A Coast Guard patrol boat sank into the sea on Saturday. One person died in the accident.

The patrol boat may have hit a rock and a hole was made in it. Therefore the boat took on water and sank into the sea.

There were 3 coast guards on the boat. 2 of them got off the boat, but 1 coast guard died.

The authorities are investigating the accident. It is not yet known what caused it.

## Good corona situation continues

![THL sign on Mannerheimintie in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_339/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6782375ec3516ecc544/1618662483)

Open image viewer

THL says on Monday whether there have been new corona deaths in Finland. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Only a few new corona infections have been found around Midsummer.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, reported only 1 new corona infection on Sunday. On Saturday, 9 new infections were reported.

In total, about 7,100 Finns have caught the corona infection. 326 people have died because of the coronavirus. THL says on Monday whether more deaths have occurred over the weekend.

## Restaurant corona restrictions

![Customers on the terrace of Ravintola Sohvi in Jyväskylä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_294/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6880235ee0b388c080d/1591784819)

Open image viewer

Restaurants will get more freedoms from Monday because of the good corona situation. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

Restaurant corona restrictions are changing. From Monday, the restrictions are no longer as strict as before.

Restaurants may stay open until 2 am. Alcohol sales may continue until 1 am.

Restaurants may also let in more customers than before. In addition, buffets, where customers serve themselves, are allowed.

Corona restrictions can be eased, because there are now few new corona infections in Finland. Good hygiene and safe distances are still important in restaurants, however.

## The longest day of the year

![Rowing boat on a lake on a summer night.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10268970)

Open image viewer

Now the day is long and the night is light too. Photo: Kaisa Siren / AOP

Today, Sunday, is the summer solstice. Up here in the north, it is the longest day of the year.

In Southern Finland the day is now about 19 hours long. In Lapland the sun does not set at all at midsummer.

After the summer solstice the days begin to get shorter. The day will still be longer than the night for another 3 months.

## Warm weather continues

The weather is still warm. In various parts of Finland there is a heatwave, that is, the temperature is at least 25 degrees.
