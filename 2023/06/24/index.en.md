# Radio Clear News Saturday 24.6.2023

The situation in Russia. Finland's comments on the situation in Russia. Midsummer accidents. The weather.

## The situation in Russia

![Wagner mercenaries in Rostov-on-Don.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5164,h_2904,x_0,y_326/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11342346496ebbabe450/1687624402)

Wagner troops in the city of Rostov-on-Don in Russia. Photo: Sergey Pivovarov / AOP

The situation in Russia is dangerous. A group of mercenaries is on its way towards the Russian capital, Moscow.

The mercenaries belong to the Wagner troops. They have taken part in Russia's war against Ukraine.

Now the leader of the Wagner troops, Jevgeni Prigožin, wants to change the leaders of the Russian army. In Prigožin's view, they have led the war badly.

Russian President Vladimir Putin says that Wagner has betrayed Russia. That is why he has ordered that the Russian army must stop the Wagner troops.

The Russian army has started fighting against the Wagner troops. In Moscow, many workplaces are kept closed, so that people do not need to move around the city.

## Finland's comments

![Sauli Niinistö speaks to guests at the Presidential Palace, with green plants in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2286,h_1285,x_806,y_434/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1131753648f2d5e5045d/1687104959)

President Sauli Niinistö is following the situation in Russia. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

In Finland, people are following how the situation in Russia develops. President Sauli Niinistö says that the situation in Russia is being followed closely.

Prime Minister Petteri Orpo has discussed the situation in Russia with the prime ministers of Estonia and Latvia. The countries intend to cooperate.

Authorities say that Finns should not travel to Russia. It is estimated that there are now about 200 Finns in Russia.

## Midsummer accidents

![On Midsummer Eve, the men's sauna session is traditionally held at the Tali allotment garden.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1918,h_1078,x_0,y_120/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11341406495adc414381/1687531004)

Midsummer went calmly. Photo: Jussi Mankkinen / Yle

Midsummer has been celebrated fairly calmly in Finland.

However, the police had a lot to do on Midsummer night. Midsummer revellers fought and also caused other disturbances in different parts of Finland.

On Midsummer Eve, a group of Finns had an accident at sea near the Estonian capital, Tallinn. The Finns' boat ran aground, that is, it hit a stone or a rock.

Estonian sea rescuers took the Finns to safety, and nobody was injured. The Estonian police say that the driver of the boat had not been drinking alcohol.

There have also been car accidents at Midsummer.

In Tornio in northern Finland, a car accident happened on Midsummer Eve. An about 80-year-old woman died when the car drove off the road on a bend. The car's driver, who is also about 80 years old, was taken to hospital.

In southern Finland, in Akaa, a car accident happened on Midsummer night. One person died there when a car drove off the road.

On Sunday there will be showers in southern Finland. Elsewhere it is sunny.

In the rain the temperature is below 20 degrees, and in the sun there is even a heat wave, that is, the temperature is at least 25 degrees.

The warm weather will continue next week.
