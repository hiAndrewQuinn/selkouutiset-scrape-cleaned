# Tuesday 10.3.2020 (radio)

There are 10 new coronavirus infections in Finland. Italy has expanded its coronavirus measures. Combining work and family usually works well. The matriculation examinations have started. Turkey wants a new refugee agreement.

## The number of coronavirus infections is growing

In total 40 coronavirus infections have now been found in Finland. THL, the National Institute for Health and Welfare, said on Tuesday afternoon that there are 10 new coronavirus cases in Finland.

All the new patients were travelling in Italy, or they have been in contact with people who came from Italy.

## Italy has expanded the coronavirus measures to the whole country

![A person in protective clothing points a camera at a passenger with a thermometer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1969,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6514105e665e7ae8971/1585748648)

Italy has expanded to the whole country the measures that are meant to stop the spread of the coronavirus.

Public gatherings are banned and sports events are cancelled. Weddings and funerals may not be held. Universities and schools are closed. In Italy there are about 9,000 coronavirus cases, and about 460 people have died of the disease.

Movement is restricted across the whole of Italy, so that the coronavirus does not spread.

The police monitor the movement restrictions at airports, railway stations and motorways. In Italy you may now only go out if you are going, for example, to a doctor or to work.

## Combining work and family usually works well

![A child and a mother climbing the steps of a school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1916,h_1078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6501315e60cc4a724ce/1583402141)

Parents of small children are happy with work and family.

Most parents think that they can manage well both working and being with the family. Family life and work go well if the working hours are flexible, that is, if you can choose yourself when you work. Family life and work are also helped if you can work remotely, that is, work from home.

The Population Association has asked parents how family and work fit together. Combining family and work is difficult if the work is demanding or the working days are long.

## The matriculation examinations have started

![A school hall ready for the matriculation examination, Lahti Lyseo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8794570)

The spring matriculation examinations started today, on Tuesday. The first subject is the Finnish reading test. The Finnish writing test is on Thursday.

About 43,000 candidates take part in the matriculation examinations this spring. That is about 3,000 candidates more than a year ago.

## Turkey wants a new refugee agreement

![Greek riot police watch migrants at the border between Turkey and Greece.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11241572)

Open image viewer

Greek riot police watch migrants at the border between Turkey and Greece. Photo: Sakis Mitrolidis / AFP

Turkey wants to make a new refugee agreement with the EU. Turkey wants the EU to make it easier to grant visas to Turkish citizens. Turkey says that the situation in Syria must also be taken into account in the new agreement.

There is a dispute between Turkey and the EU about the refugee situation. The EU says that Turkey must keep to the refugee agreement that was made in 2016. Turkey wants a new agreement.
