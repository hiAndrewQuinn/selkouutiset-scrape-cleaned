# Sunday 18.8.2019 (radio)

Child poverty in families is growing. Berry companies need more berry pickers. Sales of new cars have slowed down. In Turku there was a march on the anniversary of the terror attack. Football player Teemu Pukki scored a hat-trick.

## Child poverty in families

![A young boy sits on the floor with his hands on his face.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10426567)

Child poverty in families is growing in Finland.

Statistics show that child poverty in families has grown steadily since 2012. About 150,000 children now live in low-income families.

On Friday the Ministry of Finance published the budget proposal, in which the position of families with children is going to be improved. For example, basic security and the child benefit for single parents are going to be raised a little.

## Shortage of berry pickers

![Thai berry picker, Polarica berry picking, Samai Leka](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10895722)

Berry companies say that they need more berry pickers. Pickers are needed to pick wild berries in Finland's forests and bogs.

About 2,400 berry pickers came to Finland from Thailand this year. The number is the same as a year ago.

Berry companies say that pickers must also be brought in from countries other than Thailand. For example, Kiantama Oy of Suomussalmi is considering hiring pickers from Vietnam.

## Car trade is struggling

![Car](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10921533)

Open image viewer

Car dealers say that about 10 per cent fewer new cars will be sold this year than last year. Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

Sales of new cars have fallen.

Car dealers say that this year perhaps a little over 100,000 new cars will be sold. That is about 10 per cent fewer than last year.

Sales of new cars have fallen, for example, because a record number of used cars have been imported to Finland from Sweden. Many people therefore buy a used car rather than a new one.

The discussion about traffic emissions has also made buyers hesitate about whether it is still worth buying a new petrol or diesel car.

## Anniversary of the Turku knife attack

![Kukkavirta 188 procession](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10927339)

Open image viewer

The Kukkavirta procession laid flowers in the Aura river. Photo: Andy Ödman / Yle

In Turku there was a march on the anniversary of the Turku terror attack.

The nationalist Kukkavirta procession marched to the bank of the Aura river. In addition, the Turku Without Nazis counter-demonstration marched on the city centre streets.

It is 2 years today since the Turku knife attack. In August 2017 the Moroccan Abderrahman Bouanane stabbed 2 people to death. In addition, 8 people were injured.

## Teemu Pukki scored a hat-trick

![Teemu Pukki in the picture](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10927009)

Open image viewer

Teemu Pukki is playing his first season in the English Premier League. Photo: AOP

Finnish footballer Teemu Pukki is becoming a superstar of the English Premier League. Pukki plays football for Norwich in the Premier League.

On Saturday evening Norwich had a match against Newcastle. Norwich won the match 3-1. Teemu Pukki scored all of Norwich's goals, that is, he scored a hat-trick. At the same time Pukki rose to number one in the Premier League goalscorers' table.

Last season Norwich still played in the Championship. Teemu Pukki was the Championship's top scorer with 29 goals.
