# Wednesday 24.3.2021 (radio)

Many vocational school students have big gaps in basic skills. The number of corona infections rose again. Remote teaching in schools in Uusimaa and Southwest Finland continues. The use of masks has become more common on public transport. The weather on Thursday.

## Problems for vocational school students

![Vocational school students at work.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11849404)

Open image viewer

You can study many different trades at a vocational school. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

It is hard for many young people to get through vocational studies.

More and more young people who need special support for their studies are coming to vocational school. At the moment about 15 percent of students in basic vocational qualifications need special support.

Yle interviewed more than 30 vocational school teachers and counsellors from all over the country. They say that many students have big gaps in basic skills. Reading, writing and basic arithmetic cause problems, for example. Students may also have difficulties managing their lives, which means they cannot, for example, get to school on time.

Every year, more than 10,000 young people drop out of vocational school.

About 800 new coronavirus infections have been confirmed in Finland. More than half of the infections were confirmed in Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa.

2 new corona deaths have been confirmed.

Almost 300 corona patients are in hospital care. More than 60 of them are getting intensive care.

## Remote teaching continues

![A child goes to remote school using a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4908,h_2761,x_0,y_253/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6825505ed0adc5b0f02/1617966353)

Open image viewer

Remote school continues over Easter. Photo: Petteri Juuti / Yle

Remote teaching in lower secondary schools and in second-level education institutions continues in the Helsinki area and elsewhere in Uusimaa. Remote teaching also continues, for example, in the Turku area.

Remote teaching will continue in lower secondary schools, vocational schools and upper secondary schools at least over Easter, until 5 April.

The government's recommendation on remote teaching ends this week. However, Education Minister Jussi Saramo says on Twitter that continuing remote teaching may be needed in the worst epidemic areas.

Regional authorities, that is the municipalities and the Regional State Administrative Agency, decide on remote teaching.

## Masks on buses

![A young person wearing a face mask at a bus stop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11628225)

Open image viewer

Masks are compulsory on HSL buses. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The use of face masks has become more common on public transport in the Helsinki region. HSL says that on average 90 percent of passengers now wear masks.

HSL introduced a mask requirement just under 2 weeks ago. Before that, there was a recommendation to wear masks.
