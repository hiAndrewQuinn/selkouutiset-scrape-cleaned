## The new government's plans

![A child sits with their back to the camera by the window, looking out.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_493/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10797966400308806a2e/1678292000)

Researchers say that the plans of Finland's new government may increase the money problems of many families with children.

The government plans to pay, for example, less housing benefit and unemployment benefit than before.

Housing benefit is paid to people whose money is not enough for rent. Unemployment benefit is paid to people who do not have work.

The new government also saves in many other things. The government wants to save money, because Finland has a lot of debt. The government told about its plans on Friday.

## Social media

![A finger on top of the Facebook app.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7339,h_4128,x_0,y_417/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107930563ff0fbeb1aee/1677660143)

Thieves want to get into people's Facebook pages. Photo: Yau Ming Low / AOP

Many accounts on Finns' social media are stolen. For example, many Facebook accounts were stolen in May and June.

In May, four times as many accounts were stolen as at the start of the year.

Thieves send a message to the phone that is a scam. Some people do not understand that the message is a scam. That is why they give information to the thieves, which lets the thieves get into their Facebook pages, for example.

People must think carefully about who they give information to on the phone and online.

## Blue-green algae

![Blue-green algae in shallow water on a sea shore.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_58/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99346062f60644adee1/1660290731)

Open image viewer

Blue-green algae looks like this. Photo: Paula Collin / Yle

Researchers say that this summer, many lakes will have a lot of blue-green algae. Blue-green algae is a plant that can cause health problems.

Health problems can come if a person swims in a lake that has a lot of blue-green algae.

Blue-green algae is coming, because the weather is warm. Fertilisers used on fields have also got into many lakes. The fertilisers contain food for blue-green algae.

Usually there is a warning on the beach if there is a lot of blue-green algae in the water. Then you cannot swim in the lake.
