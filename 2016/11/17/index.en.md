# Thursday 17.11.2016 (radio)

The Chinese plan to build a bio-factory in northern Finland. The Finnish police have turned 200 years old. The police have started to investigate politician Saido Mohamed's actions. Barack Obama is touring Europe for the last time as US President. Construction work at Pasila station in Helsinki continues.

## The Chinese factory plan

![Boreal bioref agreement signing](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9298689)

Open image viewer

The Chinese intend to build a bio-factory in Kemijärvi. Photo: CAMC Engineering

The Chinese intend to build a factory in northern Finland, in Kemijärvi. The final decision on building the factory will perhaps be made next year.

If the factory is built, a large bio-factory will come to Kemijärvi. There they intend to make many kinds of products from wood. The factory intends to make pulp, which is a raw material for paper, as well as tall oil, electricity and heat.

The plan is that the factory will be ready in 2020. If the factory is built, it will bring up to 1,000 jobs to Kemijärvi.

## The police's birthday

![Police logo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9283612)

Open image viewer

The Finnish police are already 200 years old. Photo: Terhi Marjakangas / Yle

The Finnish police have turned 200 years old. The birthday was celebrated on Thursday in Turku, because Finland's first police station was set up in Turku in 1816. Helsinki got a police station 10 years later.

On the feast day there have been more police in Turku than usual. At the Hansa shopping centre, for example, there was a temporary police station for 2 hours. People were able to get to know police work there.

## The case of Saido Mohamed

![In the picture, Saido Mohamed, chair of the Finnish Somali network, sits behind a table. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9247641)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

The police have started to investigate the actions of politician Saido Mohamed.

Saido Mohamed's sister was sentenced because she misused child benefits. The sister received child benefits from Finland for 10 years, even though she lived in Britain. The sister did not tell Kela that she had moved away from Finland. In court the sister said that Saido Mohamed used the money. The sum is more than 44,000 euros.

Because of this matter, Saido Mohamed will not stand as a candidate in next spring's municipal elections. He is a member of the Helsinki Greens.

## Barack Obama's trip

![Barack Obama and Angela Merkel.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9300659)

Barack Obama and Angela Merkel. Photo: Kay Nietfeld / EPA

Barack Obama is travelling in Europe for the last time as US President. Obama's time as president ends in January.

Obama began the trip in Greece. Obama thanked Greece, because Greece has helped many asylum seekers.

From Greece Obama travelled to Germany. There he has met German Chancellor Angela Merkel. On Friday Obama will also meet other leaders of European countries.

## Pasila station

![Triple construction site in Pasila](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9296583)

Open image viewer

Pasila station has been a big construction site for many years. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Construction work at Pasila station in Helsinki continues.

Trains run normally during the construction work. 1,000 trains run through Pasila every day.

150 workers are working under the station, day and night. They are building foundations for the new station, which will be finished in a few years. The old station building will be demolished next summer.

A big shopping centre will also be built in Pasila.
