# Friday 17.2.2023 (radio)

The current Parliament votes on Finland's Nato membership. More than 50,000 Ukrainian war refugees have sought protection in Finland. Finnair has cancelled flights to Germany. It snows heavily in places.

## Nato

![The Foreign Affairs Committee of Parliament on Friday, 17 February 2023, gave its report on the government's proposal on Finland's Nato membership.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_188/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107429063ef58e051c74/1676630314)

Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Finland's MPs will vote in the near future on whether Parliament approves Finland joining Nato. The exact date of the vote is not yet known.

All Nato member countries must approve Finland's membership of Nato. Turkey and Hungary have not yet approved Finland joining Nato. So Parliament may approve Finland's Nato membership, even though Finland cannot yet join Nato.

Finland's aim is still that Finland and Sweden join Nato at the same time.

## People who fled Ukraine who are in Finland

More than 50,000 people who have fled the war in Ukraine have sought protection in Finland. More than 49,000 of them are citizens of Ukraine and about 1,000 are citizens of other countries.

Children and children's mothers in particular seek protection in Finland. Children without a mother have also come to Finland.

## Cancelled flights to Germany

![An aeroplane, with a Rovaniemi sign in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_3,y_758/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107340563ed41b0d96a4/1676493708)

Open image viewer

Photo: Vesa-Pekka Hiltunen / Yle

Flights from Finland to Germany have been cancelled.

Finnair has cancelled a total of 12 flights to Germany. Finnair has cancelled outbound and return flights to the airports of Frankfurt, Munich and Hamburg. Most of Finnair's customers have got a new flight for Saturday.

There have been strikes at German airports on Friday. All flights at the Frankfurt and Munich airports were cancelled because of a strike. Many other flights were also cancelled at German airports.

## The weather

Tomorrow, Saturday, it will snow in many places. The most snow falls in southern Finland and western Finland.

The temperature in the southern and western parts of the country is about 0 degrees. In the eastern and northern parts of the country it is between -1 and -7 degrees.

On roads, driving conditions will be very bad in some places tomorrow, on Saturday.
