## Restaurant subsidies

Many restaurants must pay back corona subsidies. Over 1,000 restaurants have received too much corona subsidy. The subsidy was meant to help restaurants that were doing very badly.

Some restaurants were not doing very badly. They received too much subsidy, which must be paid back. The subsidy had to be paid back already last year. About 500 restaurants have still not paid back the too much corona subsidy.

The restaurant organisation MaRa says that many restaurants have financial difficulties. It may be hard for them to pay back the corona subsidy.

## Trade fairs and corona

![Empty exhibition centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5997,h_3373,x_0,y_543/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-84725561290219c9097/1630148283)

Open image viewer

Empty exhibition centre. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

It is hard to organise trade fairs because of the corona restrictions. Trade fair companies have cancelled many fairs, because the corona restrictions have lasted a long time.

The corona restrictions have been extended many times. Trade fair companies do not know when people can come to fairs. For example, the Helsinki Exhibition Centre has now also cancelled the September fairs.

Trade fair companies hope that a corona pass will soon be introduced in Finland, so that fairs can be organised. At the Turku Exhibition Centre the first fairs in a long time were held this weekend.

## Presidential election in Estonia

![Estonian parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11178284)

Open image viewer

The Estonian flag is on the roof of the Estonian parliament building. Photo: Stanislav Moshkov / Yle

In Estonia, they will vote on a new president on Monday. The president is chosen by the Estonian Parliament. In Estonia it has been hard to find presidential candidates who are acceptable to the different parties.

The candidate for president is the director of the National Museum, **Alar Karis**. The Estonian Parliament will vote on the president on Monday. It is possible that Karis will not get enough support in the vote. If Karis does not get enough support, a new vote will be held on Tuesday.

The term of the current president of Estonia, **Kersti Kaljulaid**, ends at the beginning of October.

## Bronze in javelin for Finland

![Marjaana Heikkinen throws a javelin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3039,h_1709,x_0,y_157/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-805712609bcc5316c30/1620823158)

One more sports news item.
Javelin thrower **Marjaana Heikkinen** has won a bronze medal at the Tokyo Paralympics.

Heikkinen threw for bronze in the F34 class javelin throw. The result was 17.47 metres. Marjaana Heikkinen's bronze medal is Finland's first medal this year at the Paralympics.

The gold medal in the javelin event went to China and the silver medal to Germany.
