# Wednesday 15.8.2018 (radio)

This year there have been many forest fires. The Swedish police are looking for arsonists. In Italy the collapsed bridge is still being cleared. Summer was good for butterflies. The first night frosts have been measured in the north.

## There have been many forest fires

![A tree burning from the root.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10313857)

Open image viewer

Forest burned in Pyhäranta in Southwest Finland in July. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

In Finland there have been more forest fires this year than usual.

There have already been about 3300 forest fires this year. Usually there are just over 2000 forest fires in the whole year, says rescue chief inspector Rami Ruuska of the Ministry of the Interior.

In Sweden this year there have been many large fires that have been hard to put out. In Sweden about 20 000 hectares of forest have burned during the summer.

Forest fires in Finland have also been bigger than average this year. However, they have not been as big and difficult as in Sweden, says Rami Ruuska.

## The Swedish police are looking for arsonists

![Burnt cars in a car park in Gothenburg.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10353811)

Open image viewer

Almost a hundred cars were burned in Sweden on Monday evening. Photo: Adam Ihse / EPA

The Swedish police have arrested two people who are suspected of car arson. The police also say that the Turkish border police have arrested one person whom the Swedish police have put out a wanted notice for over arson.

In Sweden almost a hundred cars were burned the night before last. Last night four cars were set on fire.

At one fire site, a video was filmed showing young people with masks and dark clothes who were setting fires.

## Collapsed bridge is being cleared in Italy

![Collapsed motorway bridge in Genoa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10353320)

Open image viewer

Collapsed motorway bridge in Genoa. Photo: Valery Hache / AFP

In Genoa, Italy, clearing work continues after yesterday's bridge collapse.

39 dead have already been found, says Italy's interior ministry.

Yesterday a section of about a hundred metres of the motorway bridge collapsed. More than 30 cars fell from a height of almost 50 metres.

## Butterfly populations strengthened

src="https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5198070"

Open image viewer

Common blue. Photo: Erkki Suonio / YLE

This summer has been good for butterflies. The Finnish Environment Institute says that there were more day-flying butterflies than in previous years.

This summer there were many common blues and silver-studded blues, for example. There have also been more small tortoiseshells, painted ladies and red admirals than last summer.

Because of the long heatwave, day-flying butterflies emerged up to 3 weeks early.

## There was already a night frost in the north

![Weather graphics for Friday, Saturday and Sunday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10353711)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Sää

Summer still continues. It seems that summer is gradually turning into autumn, because the first night frosts have been measured.

Last night frost was measured at 6 different measuring stations in Northern Finland in total. Minus temperatures were measured, for example, in Taivalkoski, Ylitornio and Vaala.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that the first night frosts came a little earlier than usual.

Frost may also occur again tomorrow night in North Ostrobothnia and Kainuu.

Meteorologist Marjo Hoikkanen, however, says that summer continues. And the weather warms up again towards the end of the week.
