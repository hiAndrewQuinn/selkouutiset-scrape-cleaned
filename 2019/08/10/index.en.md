# Saturday 10.8.2019 (radio)

The Centre Party will soon choose a new party leader. A serious accident has happened in Tanzania. A storm has hit China. A tornado was seen in Luxembourg. The German band Rammstein has performed in Tampere.

## The Centre Party chooses a party leader

![Centre Party MPs Jouni Ovaska and party leader candidates Katri Kulmuni and Antti Kaikkonen (right) at a discussion event at the Centre Party's party day at Suomi-Areena.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10916421)

The Centre Party will soon choose a new leader. There are 2 candidates. They are Minister Katri Kulmuni and Minister Antti Kaikkonen.

Now Kulmuni and Kaikkonen are travelling around different parts of Finland. They discuss politics in front of an audience.

The first debate was on Saturday in Rovaniemi. The candidates will visit 18 places in total.

The Centre will choose a new party leader in September. The previous party leader, Juha Sipilä, decided to stop, because the Centre lost the elections in the spring.

## A diver died in an accident

![Rescue department men on the shore of a lake.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10917387)

Open image viewer

Many rescuers came to search for a diver on Iso-Melkutin lake. Photo: Timo Leponiemi / Yle

A diving accident has happened in Southern Finland. One person died in the accident.

The accident happened on Saturday at Iso-Melkutin lake. A group of divers were on the lake, and one of them disappeared.

The rescue department came to search for the diver. When the diver was found, he was already dead.

Iso-Melkutin is popular with amateur divers. The lake is about 20 metres deep.

## A tanker exploded in Tanzania

![Traffic on the main road between Tanzania and Zambia in March 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10917189)

Open image viewer

There is a lot of traffic on the roads in Tanzania. Photo: Friedrich Stark / Alamy / AOP

A serious accident has happened in Tanzania, in Africa. Over 60 people died when a tanker exploded. Many people were injured.

The tanker first had a collision and overturned. People came to the scene and tried to take fuel from the tanker. Then the tanker exploded.

The cause of the explosion may have been a cigarette. The fire may have caught from a cigarette to the fuel, which then exploded.

## A typhoon hit China

![A worker searches for his belongings at a damaged construction site in Wenling, China, on 10 August 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10917159)

Open image viewer

The typhoon caused a lot of destruction in China. Photo: Jin Yunguo / EPA

A storm has hit China. People have died in the storm.

The storm is a tropical hurricane, that is, a typhoon. The typhoon has been named Lekima.

The rain and wind of Lekima have cut off electricity and felled trees. Houses have also collapsed.

Chinese officials moved more than a million people to safety when Lekima approached China.

## Rammstein performed in Tampere

![Rammstein, Ratina](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10917284)

Open image viewer

Rammstein is building a big show on the stage. Photo: Jussi Mankkinen / Yle

The famous German band Rammstein is in Tampere at the weekend. Rammstein has 2 concerts in Tampere.

The first concert was on Friday, and the second is today, Saturday. About 30,000 people will attend each of them.

Rammstein plays metal music. A Rammstein concert is always a big show, in which fire plays an important part.

Rammstein was founded 25 years ago.
