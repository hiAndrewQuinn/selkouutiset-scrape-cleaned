# Thursday 21.3.2019 (radio)

In Mozambique, efforts are being made to rescue people cut off by floods. The Dutch shooter faces terrorism charges. The number of killings committed while drunk has grown. Finnish children are also becoming victims of human trafficking.

## Efforts are being made to rescue people cut off by floods in Mozambique.

![Aerial image of the village of Praia Nova after the cyclone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10697949)

The flood has caused great damage in Mozambique. Photo: John Estey / CARE

There has been a serious natural disaster in Mozambique in Africa. A severe storm hit Mozambique, causing heavy rain and floods. At least 500 people have died. There may be many more dead.

There are areas in the city of Beira that have been completely destroyed. There have also been severe floods in the neighbouring countries of Mozambique, Zimbabwe and Malawi.

The storm has also destroyed roads.

Rescue authorities have brought tens of thousands of people to safety from the middle of the floods. International aid organisations are present, but bad weather makes it difficult to get aid through.

## Utrecht shooter faces terrorism charges

![Police special units inspect a tram.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10696169)

Open image viewer

The suspect in the Utrecht tram shooting faces charges of murders committed with terrorist intent. Photo: Ricardo Smit / EPA

The suspect in the Utrecht tram shooting in the Netherlands faces charges of murders committed with terrorist intent. The 37-year-old suspect will go to court tomorrow, Friday.

It is still being investigated whether the motive was entirely terrorist, or whether there were other reasons for the act.

Three people died and seven were injured in the shooting on the tram on Monday.

A march in memory of the shooting victims will be held in Utrecht tomorrow, Friday. The march will end at the place where the attack happened.

## Alcohol is often behind killings

![Police operation related to the investigation of a violent crime in Kallio, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10696938)

Alcohol is often the reason behind killings committed by Finnish men.

The number of deaths caused by people who were drunk grew by as much as 30 percent last year. The death rate in killings among women fell a little, but the death rate among men almost doubled.

Last year, 91 people died as victims of killings, and 2 years ago 68 people died.

A study shows that the perpetrator and the victim were both usually about 2 per mille drunk.

## Children are becoming victims of human trafficking in Finland

![A young person browsing a phone, silhouette.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10105906)

Open image viewer

Finnish victims of human trafficking are poorly identified. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Children and young people become victims of human trafficking in Finland. The Institute of Criminology says that probably only a small part of human trafficking is noticed.

Finnish victims in particular apparently go without help. Children taken into care, or young people who run away from their placement, are at risk, for example.

Victims of human trafficking are better identified among asylum seekers.

Children and young people are forced into the sex trade and to commit crimes. They are also forced into marriage.
