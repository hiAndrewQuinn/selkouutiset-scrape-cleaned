## The corona situation and health care

![Nurses beside a patient.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_247/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-860987615687b02536c/1633066224)

Open image viewer

The number of corona patients is growing. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

The corona situation is putting a load on health care in Finland. The corona situation may make it harder to see a doctor in different parts of Finland.

The reason is that the number of corona patients is growing. That is why other illnesses cannot be examined or treated as much. There is also a queue in health care, because the treatment of many patients has already been moved to later.

In Helsinki, patients have now also started to be treated at weekends, because there are many patients in the queue.

## The corona situation in Europe

![A festively decorated sweets stall at the Munich Christmas market, with people in front of the stall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_720,h_405,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-6931158380f527580e/1480069131)

Christmas markets are being closed in Germany and Austria because of the corona situation. Usually a very large number of people visit these Christmas markets. Photo: Jared Kelly, published on Flickr under a CC licence, image size changed

The corona situation is getting worse quickly in Central Europe. For example, in France, Germany and Austria, corona infections have increased a lot.

In Austria a lockdown starts on Monday. In Austria, people may only leave home, for example, to go to the grocery shop, to school or to a doctor.

Also in Germany, the Bavaria region is closing all Christmas markets because of the corona situation.

In many European countries there were demonstrations against corona restrictions on Saturday. Some people do not accept the corona restrictions.

In the Netherlands some of the demonstrations became violent. In Austria and Croatia there were also big demonstrations against corona restrictions yesterday.

In Helsinki too, about 1 500 people gathered at Rautatientori on Saturday for a demonstration against corona restrictions.

## Problems for Christmas shopping

![Toy shelf in Prisma](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6185165de619bd4c47b/1575362540)

Open image viewer

Some Christmas presents may run short, because goods take a long time to travel to Finland. Photo: Kare Lehtonen/Yle

A shortage of microchips makes Christmas shopping harder. Shops say that, for example, there is a shortage of the new PlayStation 5 game console. There is also a shortage of the new model of the iPhone mobile phone.

Problems with sea transport also make Christmas shopping harder. A large part of the goods are made in China.

Goods can take a long time to reach Finland, because there are problems with sea transport. That is why popular gifts can sometimes run out of shops.

## Birds wintering

![Willow tit on a branch](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4535955a47bcc6623e5/1514651078)

Open image viewer

The willow tit is a bird that many Finns know, which spends the winter in Finland. Photo: Yle/ Risto Salovaara

The number of birds that spend the winter in Finland has grown a lot in the 2000s. The reason is that winters are getting warmer.

The bird organisation BirdLife says that the warming of winters is very useful for birds. Birds have enough food, and birds use less energy to keep warm.

About 100 bird species regularly spend the winter in Finland.
