# Radio | News Week in Clear Finnish | Sunday 28.1.2024

The first round of the presidential election.

The topic of this News Week is the first round of the presidential election.

In the first round of the presidential election, Alexander Stubb and Pekka Haavisto received the most votes. They will go on to the second round of the presidential election. Now we tell about the results and the winners of the first round.

Finns voted in the presidential election on Sunday. The election interested Finns. 75 percent of the people who have the right to vote took part in voting.

There were 9 presidential candidates. None of them got more than 50 percent of the votes. That is why there will be a second round of the election 2 weeks later.

Alexander Stubb and Pekka Haavisto will go to the second round of the presidential election. They received the most votes in the first round. Stubb got 27 percent of the votes and Haavisto about 26 percent.

The other candidates received fewer votes. Jussi Halla-aho got 19 percent of the votes and Olli Rehn about 15 percent. The other candidates got about 5 percent of the votes or even less.

In the first round of the presidential election, Alexander Stubb received the most votes. Stubb's party is the National Coalition Party.

Stubb is 55 years old. He is a former Prime Minister and Foreign Minister of Finland.

Stubb strongly supports the European Union. In recent years Stubb has been a professor at the EU university in Italy.

Stubb is married to Suzanne Innes-Stubb. Innes-Stubb is from England.

In the first round of the presidential election, Pekka Haavisto received the second most votes. Haavisto's party is the Green League.

Haavisto is 65 years old. He is a former Foreign Minister of Finland.

Haavisto has also had international tasks. The tasks have been related to many crises in the world.

Haavisto's partner is Antonio Flores. Flores is from Ecuador.
