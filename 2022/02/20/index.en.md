## Finland's Olympic ice hockey gold

![Team photo of the Lions' gold-winning team.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_32/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9178246211e7ac6deba/1645340631)

The Olympic gold in ice hockey was Finland's first. At the World Championships Finland has won gold 3 times. Photo: Getty Images

Finland's ice hockey team has won gold at the Beijing Winter Olympics. The Russian Olympic Committee team lost the final game 2-1.

On Yle Puhe radio, commentator Simo Leinonen cheered. The gold medal was Finland's first Olympic gold in ice hockey. The goals were scored by Ville Pokka and Hannes Björninen.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Culture Minister Antti Kurvinen have congratulated Finland's team, the Lions.

President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö also wrote congratulations on Twitter: "The lion tamed the bear. Congratulations, steady and strong team!" Niinistö said.

Finland also won a second medal today. Kerttu Niskanen skied for bronze in the 30 kilometre race in the morning.

In total Finland won 2 golds, 2 silvers and 4 bronzes at the Games.

## Worries of young women

![Woman places a credit card in a payment terminal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10748534)

Open image viewer

Women who were most confident about the future had a job contract, a good financial situation and higher education. Photo: AOP

One in five young women is worried about future career prospects. A University of Jyväskylä study says that the worry also affects decisions about having children.

Women who were most confident about the future had a job contract, a good financial situation and higher education. Living in the capital region also had an effect.

Others were more worried, and many were ready to put off family plans because of work.

One change could be the family leave reform. In it, parental leaves can be shared more evenly between spouses.

It is hard to get a job if you have little previous work experience. Poor education also hinders getting a job. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Finnish companies have difficulty hiring new employees. The most common reason is that applicants have too little work experience.

This is what the Confederation of Finnish Industries' survey says.

Hiring is also made harder by poor specialist skills and little education.

## The winter of the Saimaa ringed seal

![Saimaa ringed seal pup in April 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10744965)

Open image viewer

Winters without snow are dangerous for the Saimaa ringed seal. Photo: Ismo Marttinen

The conditions for the Saimaa ringed seal's breeding are good this year. Much snow has fallen in the winter, and the winds have made good snowdrifts.

The Saimaa ringed seal gives birth to a pup in a den that it has dug in the snowdrift. The snow den protects the pup from predators and from the cold.

Saimaa ringed seals live only in Lake Saimaa in Finland.

## Weather

On Monday a snowstorm arrives in the south. The snowfall is heavy in the evening and during Tuesday night. Snow may fall in places, up to 30 cm. In the south there is a little frost, and in the north it is 10 to 13 degrees below zero.

In the north it gets colder early in the week, and the weather is fairly sunny.
