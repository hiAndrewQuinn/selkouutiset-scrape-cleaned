# Wednesday 1.9.2021 (radio)

EU interior ministers: Afghan refugees must be helped first in Afghanistan's neighbouring countries. Only 1 per cent of children eat enough vegetables, fruit and berries. The future memorial to President Mauno Koivisto is the work Välittäjä. The Women's Lions won bronze at the World Championships.

## EU and Afghanistan

EU interior ministers have discussed the Afghanistan crisis in Brussels.

At the meeting it was stressed that refugees must be helped first in Afghanistan's neighbouring countries.

The ministers said that preventing a humanitarian crisis in Afghanistan is the best way to prevent large-scale migration. It also prevents flows of refugees to Europe.

## Children and food

![After a course of antibiotics, the gut needs healthy and varied food. Broccoli in particular has a lot of substances that protect the gut.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7868385)

There is room for improvement in children's diets, says a nationwide study by the University of Turku.

Children who are approaching school age in particular need a lot of encouragement to eat vegetables, greens and berries.

Only 14 per cent of children had a good nutritional quality in their diet.

Only 1 per cent of children ate enough vegetables, fruit and berries. The recommendation is 5 portions a day.

The quality of the diet was compared with children's overweight and obesity.

The study also looked at how parents' education, income level and lifestyle affected the child's diet.

## Koivisto's memorial

![Mauno Koivisto memorial](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12080653)

Open image viewer

Photo: Government

The design competition for the memorial to President Mauno Koivisto has been decided.

The competition was won by the work Välittäjä, designed by Kirsi Kaulanen. Kaulanen says that the work's 2 parts depict the president between East and West.

The aim is that the memorial will be ready in autumn 2023. By then 100 years will have passed since Koivisto's birth. The work will go to Pikkuparlamentti park in Helsinki.

Koivisto was a Finnish social democratic politician, bank manager and sociologist.

He was the 9th President of the Republic of Finland in the years 1982 - 1994.

## Women's ice hockey bronze

![The Women's Lions.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5791,h_3257,x_0,y_299/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-848583612ebf94963b6/1630454765)

The Finnish women's ice hockey national team has won bronze at the World Championships in Calgary. In the medal match the Women's Lions beat Switzerland, the surprise country of the tournament, 3–1 in goals.

Canada became world champions when it beat the USA.

Finland has also won medals at the Tokyo Paralympics.

Leo-Pekka Tähti won silver in the track cycling 100 metre final in the T54 class. Track cyclist Amanda Kotaja rode to silver in the women's T54 100 metres.

Toni Piispanen won Paralympic gold in the 200 metre track cycling in the T51 class. Javelin thrower Marjaana Heikkinen won bronze in the F34 class.
