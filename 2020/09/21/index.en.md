# Monday 21.9.2020 (radio)

The move of the passenger ship Amorella to a dry dock is being examined. Cash withdrawals started to rise to almost normal pace in the summer. Households throw a lot of bio-waste into mixed waste. Morian camp residents in Greece have been moved to a new camp.

## Amorella is still stuck

![Viking Line's Amorella on the shore of the island of Järsö in Åland on the evening of Sunday 20 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11555110)

Viking Line's Amorella on the shore of the island of Järsö in Åland on the evening of Sunday 20 September. Photo: Coast Guard

The passenger ship Amorella will be moved to a dry dock in Turku as soon as possible. However, it may take days.

Amorella ran aground on Sunday on its way from Turku to Stockholm. The passengers were moved to safety in Åland.

Viking Line's experts are planning how to free Amorella.

Amorella will be replaced by the ship Gabriella.

The Accident Investigation Board of Finland will start investigating the accident. First, the course of events will be examined. This may take 1-2 months.

## More cash is being withdrawn than in spring

![Cash machine](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4476,h_2518,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6676335ea27a74c24e2/1587706703)

Cash withdrawals started to rise in the summer to almost normal pace. At the start of the corona epidemic, cash withdrawals fell by about 50 percent.

The Bank of Finland says that more than 10 billion euros were withdrawn from cash machines last year.

This year, the euro amount of withdrawals may be less than 10 billion.

The number of machines is not going to be reduced because of the corona.

## Bio-waste is going to waste

![bio-waste](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10755980)

Open image viewer

Food scraps belong in bio-waste, not in mixed waste. Photo: AOP

Finnish households throw a lot of bio-waste into mixed waste.

Over 300 million kilos of bio-waste go to landfill with mixed waste every year.

Research by waste companies says that about a third of mixed waste is bio-waste. The share is also large in houses where there is a separate collection container for bio-waste.

The reform of the Waste Act increases the obligation of housing companies to sort waste.

Bio-waste gives more benefit in a biogas plant than when it is burned with mixed waste.

The biggest obstacle to sorting bio-waste is people's attitudes. Many cannot be bothered or do not have the energy to sort.

In addition, not all kitchens have enough good space for sorting.

## Morian residents have got into a new camp

![people in a basic camp](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11555218)

Open image viewer

More than 9,000 asylum seekers and migrants have moved from the burned Moria camp to the Kara Tepe camp on the island of Lesbos.

In Greece, residents have been moved from the Moria reception centre to a new camp.

The Moria camp on the island of Lesbos was destroyed in a fire. The new camp is the Kara Tepe camp.

All residents of the new camp have been tested for the coronavirus. Over 200 people have a corona infection.

They are now in quarantine in a part of the camp that is isolated from the rest of the camp.

Greek authorities say that more than 9,000 people have arrived at the Kara Tepe camp. The Moria reception centre had almost 13,000 migrants and refugees.

Some of the minors have been moved away from Lesbos. They are meant to be moved to different EU countries.

Finland has promised to receive 11 people.
