New coronavirus infections have been found in Finland.

The authorities say that there are 5 new infections in total. Three of the cases have been found in the capital region, 1 in Pirkanmaa and 1 in Hämeenlinna. All the patients are in good condition and at home.

In Finland there have now been 12 coronavirus infections in total.

## Yle's opinion poll

![Party support estimates for February 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11239986)

Open image viewer

Party support estimates for February 2020. Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

Yle has published a new measurement of party support.

The support of the government parties, the Social Democrats (SDP) and the Centre, has improved. The support of the SDP is 16.6 and of the Centre 12.4 percent. The support of the Centre has risen the most.

The opposition party, the Finns Party, is still clearly the biggest party. However, the support of the Finns Party has decreased.

The National Coalition Party is the second biggest party. The support of the National Coalition has risen a little. The National Coalition is in opposition.

The support of the Greens and the Left Alliance has decreased. Both are government parties.

## A new youth organisation for the Finns Party

![Finns Party chairman Jussi Halla-aho.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11242301)

Open image viewer

Finns Party chairman Jussi Halla-Aho.

The Finns Party is setting up a new youth organisation. The name of the new association is Perussuomalainen nuoriso ry.

The Finns Party is ending co-operation with the current youth organisation, PS-nuoret. Finns Party chairman Jussi Halla-Aho says that the party and PS-nuoret have too serious disagreements.

The dispute arose, for example, when the former deputy chair of PS-nuoret, Toni Jalonen, said that he is a fascist.

## The border dispute between Greece and Turkey

![Asylum seekers outside, with a mosque visible in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_108/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6494215e5e6491f151a/1583244560)

Open image viewer

Asylum seekers near the city of Edirne, in the border area between Turkey and Greece. Photo: EPA / AOP

The dispute between Greece and Turkey has continued badly.

Turkey does not agree to take back migrants who try to go from Turkey to Greece. There may be more than 10,000 people at the Greece-Turkey border who want to come to the EU area.

Turkey said on Thursday that it will send special forces to the border. The purpose of the forces is to make sure that migrants can no longer get back into Turkey.

Turkey said last week that it would let the migrants in the country go to Greece. Thousands of migrants set off for the border. However, Greece has closed the border.

The Greek government says that it has stopped almost 35,000 people from entering the country since last Friday.
