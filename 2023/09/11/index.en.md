## Terrorism trial

![Confiscated submachine guns.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1308,h_735,x_0,y_21/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114506364b8dfe347773/1689841800)

A trial has begun in court concerning terrorism linked to the far right. 3 Finnish men are charged with terrorism. The prosecutor says they trained to commit a terrorist crime.

2 of the accused discussed an attack on a Social Democrat event in Lahti.

In the district court the prosecutor demands at least almost 3 years of unconditional prison for the main accused. The prosecutor says that the man has adopted a far-right ideology.

The prosecutor also says that the man has made firearms for terrorist purposes.

The prosecutor says that the goals of 2 men were violence, the start of a race war and the collapse of society.

One man is charged with aiding terrorist crimes.

## Vandalism increases

![Targets of vandalism at the Varkaus school centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116946664fb1b93ba323/1694178361)

Pictures of vandalism in the city of Varkaus. Photo: Ari Haimakainen / Yle

Vandalism, that is, damage caused in public places, has increased in Finland. Yle made a survey of big cities about vandalism.

For example, Kuopio, Jyväskylä, Oulu and Tampere say that vandalism has become more common since the corona epidemic began.

In North Savo, the Varkaus school has suffered from vandalism exceptionally much. Places have been graffitied, and soft play equipment, garden furniture and windows have been broken a lot.

In total, the city of Varkaus has already had to repair the traces of vandalism at a cost of 100 000 euros.

## Citizens' initiative on fur farming

![Minks in cages at a fur farm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7408,h_4167,x_7,y_527/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115665864db31c3becb6/1692094791)

Open image viewer

The citizens' initiative "Fur farming to history" has collected signatures in a week. Photo: Janne Järvinen / Yle

The citizens' initiative against fur farming has already collected over 97 000 signatures.

The initiative proposes a plan for Finland that would end fur production. At the same time it proposes that fur farms get a reasonable transition period and a support system.

The initiative collected 50 000 names already on its opening day last Monday. That is the limit that is required for a citizens' initiative to proceed to handling in Parliament.

## Arctic fox nests

![Four arctic fox pups sitting on a grassy hummock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6123,h_3428,x_0,y_637/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117016464fe965c15c7b/1694412238)

Open image viewer

Photo: Petri Piisilä / Metsähallitus

In Finland, 2 arctic fox nests were observed in the summer. The arctic fox is an animal that lives on the tundra of the northern hemisphere.

The arctic fox nesting succeeded in the fell areas of Utsjoki and Enontekiö in Northern Finland, in Lapland.

Metsähallitus says that at least 16 pups were born.

Arctic fox nesting also succeeded last year in Enontekiö. It was the first time in over 25 years in Finland.
