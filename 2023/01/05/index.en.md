# Thursday 5.1.2023 (radio)

The Finns Party overtook the SDP in Yle's support poll. Exhaust fumes were the reason for the death of 4 people in Kirkkonummi. Sweden and Germany demand a corona test from people who come from China. The weather is fair and cold.

## Party support

Yle has published a new poll of party support.

The National Coalition Party is still Finland's most popular party. The National Coalition's support in Yle's poll is 23 percent. The National Coalition's support has fallen by 1 percentage point. The National Coalition is in opposition.

The Finns Party has overtaken the SDP. The opposition party, the Finns Party, is now in second place in the poll. The Finns Party's support is 19.3 percent. The Finns Party's support has risen by 1.9 percentage points.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin's party, the SDP, has dropped to third place. Less than 19 percent of the poll's respondents support the SDP. The SDP's support has fallen a little.

The Centre Party's support is now about 11 percent. The Centre Party's support has risen by almost 2 percentage points.

There are about 3 months until the parliamentary elections.

## Kirkkonummi accident

![Police tape around a detached house in Kirkkonummi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_360/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105093563a17c4ac84e5/1671530302)

The death of 4 people in Kirkkonummi in December was caused by exhaust fumes. An elderly couple and 2 people aged about 30 were found dead in a detached house last month.

The Safety Investigation Authority says that the car engine was running in the garage. From there the exhaust fumes spread into the house. The police do not suspect a crime.

On average 1 or 2 people die from engine exhaust fumes a year.

## Corona tests for people coming from China

![Airport staff hand out corona tests to arrivals from China. A passenger pulls a suitcase behind them and wears a protective overall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5500,h_3093,x_0,y_349/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105627663b6c949890b8/1672923606)

Sweden requires a negative corona test from people who come to Sweden from China. The requirement does not apply to Swedish people. A negative corona test must be from Saturday onwards if one comes to Sweden from China.

Germany too will in future require at least a negative corona test from people who come to Germany from China.

On Wednesday the EU recommended that EU countries require a negative corona test from passengers arriving from China. In Finland the ministers will discuss on Thursday evening whether Finland will in future require a corona test from passengers arriving from China.

The coronavirus is spreading quickly in China. China had strict corona restrictions for a long time, but now China has removed the restrictions.
