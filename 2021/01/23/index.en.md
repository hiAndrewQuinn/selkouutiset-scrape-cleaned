# Saturday 23.1.2021 (radio)

Finland tightens passenger restrictions. Restrictions prevent construction workers from crossing the border. Estonia asks Finland to ease the new restrictions. Corona vaccinations are delayed because of problems at the drug company AstraZeneca. There have been big demonstrations in Russia. The weekend continues rainy.

## Restrictions are coming for passengers

![Maria Ohisalo and Krista Kiuru at the government's press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11752056)

Finland is tightening restrictions on passengers.

Finland wants traffic at Finland's borders to decrease. A corona test is to be given to all passengers who come to Finland.

Finland is trying to stop the mutated coronavirus from spreading in Finland. The mutated coronavirus is dangerous, because it spreads easily.

Only a little of the mutated coronavirus has so far been found in Finland, but abroad there is a lot of it. That is why Finland is setting new restrictions.

The Finnish government has decided, for example, to stop commuter traffic to Finland almost completely. Only those workers whose work is essential may travel to Finland. They include, for example, health care workers.

The new restrictions start on Wednesday. The restrictions will continue for a month, that is, until 25 February.

## Estonia opposes the restrictions

![View from the Western Harbour terminal on 5 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11244969)

Estonia asks Finland to ease the new restrictions. Estonia thinks the restrictions are too strict.

Because of the restrictions, for example, construction workers cannot cross the border between Finland and Estonia. There are many Estonian builders working in Finland.

## The schedule for corona vaccinations is uncertain

![A person holds a dose of the AstraZeneca corona vaccine. More doses can be seen in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_426/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7582385fec2ca433927/1609313502)

Corona vaccinations are being delayed. The vaccinations of all residents cannot be started in Finland as quickly as was hoped.

The reason is the problems of the drug company AstraZeneca. AstraZeneca cannot send corona vaccines to Europe as many as it estimated earlier.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare cannot say when corona vaccinations for healthy adults in Finland will start.

## There are demonstrations in Russia

![Police in riot gear arrest a protester in a snowy city.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5799,h_3262,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-765489600bc9745dcf1/1611385293)

There have been big demonstrations in Russia. A demonstration was held in the capital, Moscow, for example.

The demonstrators want to support the opposition leader Aleksei Navalnyi. They are angry that the police arrested Navalnyi last Sunday.

Navalnyi was arrested when he returned to Russia from Germany. He had been treated in Germany because of poisoning.

Navalnyi's supporters say that Russia's security service tried to kill Navalnyi with poison. Navalnyi is a critic of President Vladimir Putin.
