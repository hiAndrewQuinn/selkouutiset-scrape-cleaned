# Saturday 30.9.2017 (radio)

There has been a big demonstration in Finland's neighbouring country Sweden. In Catalonia in Spain, people are trying to hold a referendum on independence. The new editor-in-chief of Yle News supports gender-neutral job titles. In Finland there has been a competition for who has grown the biggest vegetables.

## Demonstrations in Sweden

There has been a big demonstration in Finland's neighbouring country Sweden. The demonstration was in Gothenburg, in southern Sweden.

Hundreds of people took part in the neo-Nazi demonstration. The neo-Nazis do not want, for example, immigrants in Sweden.

In Gothenburg there was also a big demonstration by left-wing people. They demonstrated against the neo-Nazis.

There were many police officers in Gothenburg. They tried to prevent fights between the neo-Nazis and the left-wing people.

## The Catalonia referendum

![Map of Catalonia](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9850283)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics

In Catalonia in Spain, a referendum is planned for Sunday. In the referendum, Catalans are asked whether they want to leave Spain and found their own state.

The Spanish government does not want the referendum to be held. The government says that the referendum is illegal. That is why the government plans to prevent the vote.

The Spanish government has sent many police officers to Catalonia. The police are trying, for example, to close school buildings that are polling stations.

Catalonia is in northern Spain. Catalonia's largest city is Barcelona. In Catalonia people speak Catalan, Spanish and to some extent also Occitan.

## Gender-neutral job titles

![Jouko Jokinen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9860094)

Open image viewer

The new editor-in-chief of Yle News, Jouko Jokinen, wants the language of the news to treat women as well as men. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

In Finland there is talk about whether we can already forget the old job titles that end in the word "mies" (man).

For example, a "lakimies" (lawyer, literally "law man") is nowadays often a woman. That is why many think that it is better to say "juristi" (jurist).

The new editor-in-chief of Yle News, Jouko Jokinen, also thinks so. He wants the news to talk about jobs in a gender-neutral way. Gender-neutral job titles suit men and women.

In Jokinen's opinion, women's equality improves if equality is also visible in language. Words are important, because language is power, Jokinen says.

Jouko Jokinen will start as editor-in-chief of Yle News later in the autumn. Now he is the editor-in-chief of Aamulehti. Aamulehti started using gender-neutral job titles this autumn.

## Big vegetables

![A big courgette.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9860678)

Open image viewer

Mika Tiilikainen's courgette weighed over 47 kilos. Photo: Tanja Heino / Yle

In Finland there has been a competition for who has grown the biggest vegetables. The competition was held on Saturday in Mäntsälä.

Mika Tiilikainen did well in the competition. He was the best in many categories.

For example, Tiilikainen's courgette weighed 47.2 kilos. Such a big courgette has never been grown in Finland before.
