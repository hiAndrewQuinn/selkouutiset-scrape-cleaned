# Tuesday 3.10.2017 (radio)

Police have given more information about the Las Vegas shooter. A lot of phones have been stolen from a Post warehouse. There are queues at the Immigration Service's customer service in Vaasa. Children donated money to nature conservation.

## The Las Vegas shooter had many guns

![Relatives bring candles to the memorial site](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9864045)

In the USA the police have given more information about the Las Vegas shooting.

About 60 people died in the city of Las Vegas, when a man shot at people from a hotel room window. The people were outside at a concert.

The police have now said that the man had many guns. About 20 guns were in the man's hotel room. The police also found guns at the man's home.

Las Vegas hospitals have had a lot of work after the shooting. More than 500 people have received hospital care after the shooting.

The police do not yet know why the man shot people.

## Phones were stolen from the Post

![Post logo on a jacket](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8454152)

Open image viewer

The thieves found their way to a Post warehouse at the weekend. Photo: Heikki Haapalainen / Yle

A lot of phones have been stolen from the Post warehouse. The thieves went to the Post warehouse in Toijala at the weekend. The thieves went to the warehouse on 2 nights.

The thieves took more than 2,000 new phones from the warehouse. They were Apple iPhones and Samsung Galaxy phones. The phones are worth more than one million euros in total.

## Queues at the Immigration Service in Vaasa

![An immigrant learns to read and write](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8710563)

Open image viewer

Immigration Service customers have to queue in Vaasa. Photo: Hannu Karjalainen / Yle

There are queues at the Immigration Service's customer service in Vaasa. The customer has to queue for a long time for an appointment. The first free times in Vaasa are only available in 2 months' time.

Because of the queues, the Immigration Service intends to hire more staff in Vaasa.

The Immigration Service now has more tasks than before. Nowadays the customer must take, for example, a residence permit application and a citizenship application to the Immigration Service. Earlier, the customer could leave them with the police.

The Immigration Service serves customers in Finland in 9 different places. Only Vaasa has a serious queue problem. In Helsinki, for example, a customer can get an appointment quickly.

## Schoolchildren donated to nature conservation

![Ten-year-olds donated most of their quiz prize to marine protection.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9864600)

Lia Sinijärvi (on the left) and Juhi Juntura (second from the right) talked about nature conservation on the morning TV programme. Photo: Yle

One more news item about two 10-year-old schoolchildren. Juhi Juntura and Lia Sinijärvi took part in a television quiz, and they won 15,000 euros.

The children gave almost the whole prize, that is, 12,000 euros, to nature conservation. Juhi and Lia decided together with their classmates that they wanted to help protect the Baltic Sea.

The children's school is in Helsinki, near the sea. They have seen that there is a lot of litter in the sea. That is why they wanted to use the money for the good of the Baltic Sea.
