# Friday 15.9.2017 (radio)

The Cassini space probe's work is done. A school bus crashed in Suonenjoki. A bomb exploded on the London Underground. Income differences are growing. School pupils live healthily. There were very few electric cars at the Frankfurt motor show.

## Cassini's mission is complete

The space probe Cassini's work is done, and it has burned up in Saturn's atmosphere.

The Cassini probe travelled 8 billion kilometres in space and orbited the planet Saturn for 13 years. It sent photographs and other information to Earth. Thanks to Cassini, humans know a lot about planets that are far from Earth.

Saturn is the sixth (6th) planet from the Sun; Earth is the third (3rd).

## Bomb at a metro station in London

A bomb has exploded at a metro station in London, Britain.

22 people were injured in the explosion. The police say that a home-made bomb that had been left at the station exploded there in the morning. The police do not know who set off the bomb.

## Income differences are growing

![People get food in a bread queue.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9538533)

Open image viewer

People fetch food from a bread queue. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Income differences between Finns will grow next year. The rich will get more money than this year and the poor less.

The poor will get less money because the state is reducing social benefits. The rich will get more money, for example, because the prices of company shares are rising. When companies do well, they give more money to their owners and better wages to their workers. The incomes of the rich are also rising because the state is lowering taxes.

The income difference between the rich and the poor shrank until the 1970s. In the 90s it grew, and in the 2000s it has stayed fairly the same.

## School pupils live healthily

![A young person.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9826511)

Open image viewer

10 percent of young people are lonely. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Finnish school pupils live quite healthily.

School pupils drink alcohol and smoke less than before. Young people's weight has risen a little, and they are often tired. Girls are lonely more often than boys.

Other young people have bullied 25 percent of lower secondary school pupils. Young people bully most those who look different or are different in some other way.

## Few electric cars in Frankfurt

![A Volkswagen electric car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9832701)

Open image viewer

A Volkswagen electric car. Photo: Markku Pitkänen / Yle

People talk a lot about electric cars, but at the motor show in Germany there are few of them. 1,000 companies are showing cars in Frankfurt, and almost all of them have a petrol or diesel engine.

Many German car companies say that they will sell more electric cars in about 5 years. However, Volkswagen says that even in 10 years, 85 percent of its cars will still run on petrol or diesel.

Mercedes says that in the future the best may be a hydrogen car. Only water comes out of a hydrogen car's exhaust pipe.
