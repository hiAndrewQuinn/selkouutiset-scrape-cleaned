# Wednesday 14.6.2017 (radio)

The Security Police say that the danger of a terror attack has grown in Finland. A big apartment block burned in London. The number of children and young people who are overweight has grown in Finland. Finns living abroad celebrated 100 years of Finland in Helsinki.

## The danger of a terror attack in Finland

![Antti Pelttari.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9669035)

Open image viewer

Head of the Security Police Antti Pelttari. Photo: Linda Manner / Lehtikuva

The Security Police have assessed the danger of a terror attack in Finland. The Security Police say that the danger of a terror attack is now a little bigger than before.

The danger of a terror attack has grown, because there are also Finns in the Isis terror organisation. In addition, the police have information about the terrorists' plans in Finland.

The Security Police use a scale that shows how big the danger of a terror attack is. The scale has 4 steps. Now the danger of a terror attack is on the second-lowest step of the scale.

## Fire in London

![Grenfell Tower burns in London.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9670131)

Open image viewer

Grenfell Tower burned in London on Wednesday. Photo: Daniel Leal-Olivas / AFP

There was a big fire in London. The fire started in an apartment block that had 27 floors.

It is not yet known exactly how many people have died or been injured. At least 200 people lived in the building. Not everyone has been found yet.

The authorities are investigating what caused the fire.

## Children who are overweight

![Child eats bread](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8590762)

Open image viewer

Healthy food prevents being overweight. Photo: Anna Sirén / Yle

The number of children and young people who are overweight has grown in Finland.

Many things have been done in Seinäjoki so that the weight of children and young people does not go up. Day-care centres and schools offer healthy food, which does not have too much salt, sugar or fat.

In addition, you can play and move a lot in the yards of day-care centres and schools.

Other municipalities also want to do the same things.

In the countryside, children travel a lot by car, and then their weight can go up.

## Summer tourists

![Chinese tourist taking photos at Senate Square](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9646796)

Open image viewer

Chinese people make many trips to Finland. Photo: Riku Tonttila / Yle

A lot of tourists are expected in Finland this summer.

Visit Finland estimates that the number of foreign tourists will grow by as much as 10 percent. For example, Chinese tourists now prefer to come to Finland rather than elsewhere in Europe.

Visit Finland is an organisation that promotes tourism in Finland.

## Finns living abroad celebrate in Helsinki

![Finlandia Hall](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7960719)

Open image viewer

There is a concert by Finns living abroad who are artists at Finlandia Hall on Thursday evening. Photo: Klaus Ashorn / Yle

Finns living abroad have come to Helsinki to celebrate 100 years of Finland. Finns living abroad are Finns who live in other countries.

On Thursday evening there is a concert at Finlandia Hall, where artists who are Finns living abroad from different countries perform. For example, they perform tango music and classical music.

The Parliament of Finns Abroad meets in Helsinki at the weekend. The Parliament of Finns Abroad moves forward matters that are important to Finns living abroad. This time they talk about, for example, the matters of young and old Finns living abroad. More than 20 countries have participants at the meeting.
