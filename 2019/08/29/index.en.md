# Thursday 29.8.2019 (radio)

The district court has detained the men suspected of the Porvoo shooting. Some Posti employees go on strike at the beginning of next week. Protection of white-tailed eagles has succeeded well. Valtteri Bottas has made a new contract with the Mercedes team.

## Swedish men were detained

The district court has detained the men suspected of the Porvoo shooting.

The district court detained the Swedish men, who are 25 and 30 years old. The men are brothers. The men are suspected of several attempted murders. The men tried to murder police officers.

2 police officers were injured when they were shot at on Sunday night in Porvoo. After that the shooters tried to escape from the police. They also fired at the police during their escape.

The police caught the shooters on Sunday evening near Tampere.

## Posti strike

![Posti logo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10925929)

Photo: Anni Reenpää / Lehtikuva

Some of Posti's employees will go on strike at the beginning of next week. During the strike the post will not be delivered.

The strike concerns employees who sort the post. They will not go to work on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday.

The reason for the strike is that Posti intends to change the contract of the parcel sorting employees. That means, for example, lower pay for the employees.

Posti says that it must save money.

## Protecting white-tailed eagles

![White-tailed eagle in a tree.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10742479)

Open image viewer

The white-tailed eagle is a bird of prey. Photo: Yle / Jari Salonen

Protection of white-tailed eagles has succeeded well.

The white-tailed eagle is Finland's largest bird of prey. It usually lives near the sea.

About 40 years ago there were only a few white-tailed eagles left in Finland. The reason was environmental poisons.

Now environmental poisons are used less, and the eagles' nesting places are protected.

The protection has succeeded so well that more than 550 white-tailed eagle chicks have been born this year.

The white-tailed eagle is a success story of Finnish nature conservation.

## Bottas's contract

![Valtteri Bottas at Silverstone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10876737)

Formula 1 driver Valtteri Bottas. Photo: AOP

Here is one more sports news item.

Finnish Formula 1 driver Valtteri Bottas has made a new contract with the Mercedes team. The contract lasts one year.

Valtteri Bottas started as a Mercedes driver in the 2017 season. At that time he was 3rd in the world championship.

Bottas is Mercedes' second driver. Mercedes' first driver is Lewis Hamilton, who is the Formula 1 drivers' world champion.

This summer a lot has been said about whether Mercedes will continue Bottas's contract. The new contract means that Valtteri Bottas's fight in Formula 1 continues. Bottas's aim is to be world champion one day.
