## Finns Party

![Riikka Purra gives a speech.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_32/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115555364d7322667f7d/1691824979)

The Finns Party holds its party congress in Tampere on Saturday and Sunday. On Saturday the party congress decided that the current chair, **Riikka Purra**, will continue in the post. Purra was the only candidate.

Purra gave a speech at the party congress. In her speech she criticised the media. Over the summer the media has covered, for example, Purra's old racist writings.

Purra said that it was a witch hunt.

According to Purra, the media's discussion about racism and the Finns Party has been dishonest and insolent. Purra said that the media is trying to destroy the reputation of the Finns Party.

Other Finns Party members also criticised the media at the party congress. Party office employee **Matti Putkonen** said that the racism debate has been empty talk.

## Electricity price

![Power lines and tall electricity pylons against the sky. Stock image.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5005,h_2815,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1034317637381dbc12fa/1731993782)

Expensive electricity caused financial problems for many last winter. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

Electricity will probably be much cheaper next winter than last winter.

Last winter electricity was very expensive, but the authorities say that the situation is now better.

There is a new nuclear reactor at Olkiluoto that produces a lot of electricity. New wind turbines also produce a lot of electricity.

When more electricity is produced, the electricity price falls.

Many customers of electricity companies still want a fixed-term electricity contract, in which the electricity price does not change. They are afraid that the electricity price will rise again when winter comes.

## Hawaii wildfire

![An area of housing near the beach has been badly burned. The photo was taken from the air.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115525464d611b91121e/1691753639)

Houses were destroyed in fires in the city of Lahaina, Hawaii. Photo: Patrick T. Fallon / AFP

In the USA, Hawaii has had a serious wildfire. It has caused a lot of destruction on Maui island. At least 80 people have died in the wildfires. The governor of Hawaii says that the number of dead may be higher.

Several thousand houses have been destroyed or damaged.

The fires started on Tuesday. They spread very quickly because of the dry land and strong wind.

Residents of the island may not have been warned enough. Maui island has 80 warning sirens for emergencies, but the warning sirens stayed silent.

The worst damage is in the city of Lahaina.
