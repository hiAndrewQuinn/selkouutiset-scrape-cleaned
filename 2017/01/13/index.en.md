# Friday 13.1.2017 (radio)

There has been a strong storm in Central Europe. The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns of avalanches in Lapland. Last year companies fired fewer workers than in many years. Snacks can contain a lot of unhealthy sugar.

## Europe's storm

![High waves on the seafront in New Brighton on 11 January.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9401857)

Open image viewer

Storm winds reached as far as England. Photo: Paul Ellis / AFP

There was a winter storm in Central Europe on Friday. The wind has blown really hard.

The storm has disrupted traffic in many countries. Roads were closed and trains were stopped in Germany, France and Belgium.

More than 100 flights were cancelled at Frankfurt airport because of the storm. Frankfurt is Germany's biggest airport.

The storm also disrupted many homes. In Germany and France, many homes were without electricity. On the coast of England, people were moved out of their homes because the sea water rose high because of the storm.

## Danger of avalanches

![The Ylläs ski resort.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9392559)

Open image viewer

There is now thick snow on the fells of Lapland. Photo: Kimmo Brandt / AOP

Here in Finland, the Finnish Meteorological Institute warns of avalanches. The danger of avalanches has grown in the north.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that snow can slide down from the fells of Lapland. Fells are the mountains of Lapland, and in winter they are covered in thick snow.

Many skiers and downhill skiers visit the fells. They must now watch out for avalanches. For example, there is a danger of avalanches at Levi, Luosto and Pyhätunturi. They are all also winter sports resorts.

## The number of dismissals

![Illustration. A person is dismissed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9211157)

Open image viewer

The number of dismissals has fallen. Photo: Jonna Karjalainen / Yle

Last year companies fired fewer workers than in many years.

For example, in industry jobs have been kept better than before. Tourism and the restaurant sector are also doing well. For example, even more waiters are needed.

The number of dismissals has grown only in the retail sector.

Microsoft Mobile, Nokia and Anttila were among the companies that dismissed many workers last year.

The information was collected by the trade union confederation SAK.

## Sugar in snacks

![Professor of nutrition science Mikael Fogelholm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9398666)

Open image viewer

Professor Mikael Fogelholm. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

Snacks can contain a lot of unhealthy sugar.

Rye bread is a healthy snack, but sometimes sugar has been added to it. Some rye breads have as much sugar as soft drinks.

Children like breakfast cereal. But cereal can contain up to 3 times more sugar than soft drinks.

Professor Mikael Fogelholm says that parents should read the product label before they buy snacks.
