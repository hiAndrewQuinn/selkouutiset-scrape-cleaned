# Wednesday 29.5.2019 (radio)

Economic growth slowed. The government may increase spending. More air strikes in Syria. Hospital bacteria found in pigs. Party subsidies were paid out. The world's schoolchildren's climate meeting began in Finland.

## Economic growth slowed

![New cars in a car showroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10613343)

A car is a big purchase. Many people now think more carefully about whether to buy a new car. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

Finland's economic growth slowed in January to March. Slower economic growth is caused by a fall in private spending. Many people think carefully about what to buy. For example, car sales have fallen.

## Rinne: spending can be increased

![Antti Rinne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10805153)

Open image viewer

Taking on debt may perhaps be stopped, says Antti Rinne. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

The government negotiations have continued at Säätytalo. The leader of the government negotiations, the SDP's **Antti Rinne**, says that the government can increase state spending, even though the economy is getting worse. Rinne says it would be good if taking on extra debt could be stopped during this government's term.

## More air strikes in Syria

![Map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10808078)

Open image viewer

The Idlib area is held by rebels. Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle, Data: Live Universal Awareness Map

Syria and Russia are carrying out more air strikes on rebel areas in Syria. Syria and Russia have carried out air strikes in Idlib province. About 30 people have died in the air strikes over two days. More than 200,000 people have fled their homes to escape the air strikes.

Russia and Syria say that they are fighting Islamic extremist groups in Idlib.

## MRSA bacteria in pigs

![Piglet](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10399743)

Open image viewer

MRSA bacteria have become more common in pigs. The bacteria spread easily. Pork must be cooked thoroughly, and hands and utensils must be washed well. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

Bacteria that can be passed on to people have been found in Finnish pigs. Pigs have MRSA bacteria, which is also called hospital bacteria.

Eating pork is safe when the meat is cooked. Hands and tools must be washed well if you have handled raw pork. The Finnish Institute of Occupational Health advises that workers must protect themselves on pig farms and in slaughterhouses.

## Party subsidies were paid out

![The plenary chamber of Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5456,h_3069,x_0,y_264/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-42596059ae50378484a/1504596150)

Open image viewer

Party subsidies are paid according to the number of MPs. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

The government has granted party subsidies for the rest of the year, according to the seat distribution of the new Parliament.

The SDP gets the most support, almost 4 million euros. The party gets about 100,000 euros of party support for each MP. About 20 million euros of party support is paid out.

## The world's schoolchildren at a climate meeting in Finland

![Climate protest signs in a country](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10798358)

Open image viewer

Young people have recently also taken part in climate strikes for a cleaner climate. Photo: Anna Sirén / Yle

Many schoolchildren and teachers from around the world have come to Finland for a climate meeting. The world's schoolchildren's climate meeting is held in Joensuu, Liperi and Helsinki. The aim is to get schools around the world to act to reduce climate change.

The patron of the world's schoolchildren's climate meeting is President Sauli Niinistö.
