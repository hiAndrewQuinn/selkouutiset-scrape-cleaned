# Tuesday 27.8.2019 (radio)

A police patrol did not have time to use its gun in the Porvoo shooting, Yle's information says. One in six taxi entrepreneurs has not paid taxes or enforcement fees. Helsinki's central library Oodi has received the world's best public library award. Finland's lakes are in good condition.

## Police have heard the Porvoo suspects

![In Porvoo there was a large police operation early on Sunday 25 August. The investigation was carried out in the Ölsten industrial area.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10937487)

Yle has received information that a police patrol did not have time to use its gun in the Porvoo shooting. 2 police officers were injured when they were shot at while on duty during the night between Saturday and Sunday in Porvoo.

The shootings are suspected to have been carried out by Swedish brothers. They are 25 and 30 years old. They were caught after a car chase on Sunday evening in Ikaalinen, near Tampere.

The Criminal Investigation Department heard the suspects for the first time on Tuesday. The police suspect the men of several attempted murders.

The brothers are Swedish citizens. One of them also has Finnish citizenship.

The police intend to demand on Wednesday that the men be held in custody.

## Taxi entrepreneurs have arrears

![Taxis at a taxi rank](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10856197)

Open image viewer

Many taxi companies have 3 months to clear their arrears. Photo: Antti Lähteenmäki / Yle

Hundreds of taxi companies are in financial difficulties. Over 300 taxi entrepreneurs, that is, one in six taxi entrepreneurs, has not paid taxes or enforcement fees. The tax debt is on average 16,500 euros.

Traficom will send a reminder letter to taxi entrepreneurs in the coming days. Traficom is the agency that grants taxi licences. The letter asks that payments be sorted out within 3 months. If that is not possible, the taxi licence will be withdrawn.

There have been over twice as many reminders as during the old taxi act. The new taxi act came into force last July. 4,000 new taxi licences have been granted.

## Oodi is the world's best public library

![Central library Oodi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10940186)

Open image viewer

The Oodi central library has received another new recognition. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Helsinki's new central library Oodi has received the world's best public library award. Behind the award are the International Federation of Library Associations, IFLA, and the IT company Systematic.

16 libraries from around the world applied for the award. The finalists were chosen by an international jury made up of library experts.

The value of the award is 5,000 US dollars.

Oodi opened last December, on the eve of Independence Day. Oodi has already had over 2.1 million visits this year.

## Finland's lakes are in good condition

![Pielinen in July 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10882223)

Open image viewer

Photo: Pauliina Tolvanen / Yle

The Finnish Environment Institute says that Finland's lakes are doing well.

87 per cent of the lakes' area is in good or excellent condition. Small lakes are in worse condition than large ones.

The condition of inland waters has not changed significantly since 2013.

The coastal waters of the seas, however, are not in as good a condition. The Gulf of Bothnia is in better condition than the Gulf of Finland.
