## Ukraine's counter-offensive

![Armed soldiers on the back of a truck.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4500,h_2531,x_0,y_140/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1127939648476ca4cde6/1686402820)

New information has been reported about the situation of the war in Ukraine.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on Saturday that Ukraine's counter-offensive has started. Experts had already been waiting for news about the counter-offensive.

Ukraine started the counter-offensive against the Russian troops that have invaded Ukraine. Russia started the war in Ukraine last year in February.

## New chair of the Greens

![Sofia Virta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1127778648448c513d01/1686391041)

Open image viewer

Sofia Virta is the new chair of the Greens. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The Greens have a new chair. The new chair is Sofia Virta. Virta is a 32-year-old MP.

The Greens' previous chair, Maria Ohisalo, decided in the spring to stop being chair. In the spring parliamentary elections, the Greens lost many seats in Parliament.

The Greens chose the new chair at the party congress, which is this weekend.

## RKP party congress

![Anna-Maja Henriksson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4252,h_2391,x_0,y_443/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1127967648489b5dcba5/1686474906)

RKP also has a party congress this weekend.

RKP's chair Anna-Maja Henriksson spoke at the party congress on Saturday. Henriksson said that the government talks are difficult.

According to Henriksson, the reason is that RKP and the Finns Party disagree about many issues. The parties disagree, for example, about immigration.

The National Coalition, the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats are involved in the government talks. The government talks have already gone on for many weeks.

## An unusual crime

![The image shows the FaceTime logo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11278016484532e19095/1686393705)

Open image viewer

The crime was committed using a video service. Photo: Osmo Päivinen / Yle

The court has given a punishment for an unusual crime.

The court sentenced a 15-year-old boy for a robbery where the boy was not there himself. The boy committed the crime using a FaceTime video call.

The boy used a video call to direct another boy, who carried out the robbery. The perpetrator did not get a punishment himself, because he was only 14 years old.

## Sale of summer cottages

![Palokka's Viitalampi. On the far shore, a summer cottage can be seen in the shelter of the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5298,h_2980,x_0,y_422/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98129262ce707fc5f1e/1657702777)

Open image viewer

Having your own shore raises the price of a cottage. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Fewer summer cottages have been sold this summer than a year ago.

A summer cottage usually costs about 120,000 euros. Prices are now a little cheaper than in the corona years.

However, there are big differences in summer cottage prices between different places. The most expensive summer cottages are found in Lapland and on the shores of lakes and the sea.

## The weather

![Sunday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-112796964849354d801a/1686410105)

The weather in Finland is now warm and dry.

Warm air comes to Finland from the west on Sunday. The temperature rises above 20 degrees all over the country.

The whole of next week will be warm. In places, the temperature may rise to almost 30 degrees.
