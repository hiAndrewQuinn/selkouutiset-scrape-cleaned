# Sunday 7.6.2020 (radio)

The restaurants' weekend went well. Hospitals are preparing for summer. A storm caused power cuts. Bathing waters are warming quickly. Weather information.

## The restaurants' weekend went well

![Linda, Veera, Nina and Tiina enjoy sparkling wine on the Mattolaituri terrace in the evening in Helsinki on 5 June 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11389504)

Restaurant terrace in Helsinki. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The first weekend after the corona break has gone well for restaurants. The Regional State Administrative Agency says that restaurants have followed the safety instructions that were given to restaurants because of the corona situation.

For example, all customers must have a seat, and there must be fewer people inside the restaurant than before. The sale of alcohol must end at 22:00. If a restaurant does not follow the instructions, the restaurant may be closed.

The Regional State Administrative Agency says that the weekend has gone well in restaurants. No restaurant has had to be closed.

## Hospitals' work may increase in summer

![OYS joint emergency unit, exterior view](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6467455e5519f55c379/1582635638)

Open image viewer

You can go to the hospital emergency department if you have had an accident, for example. Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

Hospitals are preparing for summer. In hospitals and health centres it has been quiet in the spring, because people have been at home a lot.

Now the corona restrictions have been eased and people have started to move about more and go to hobbies again. When people move about and take part in hobbies, accidents may also happen more often.

Hospitals are preparing for patient numbers to grow in the summer.

## The wind cut off power

![Trees fallen onto a power line.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10426365)

Open image viewer

For example, wind may knock trees onto power lines. Then the power is cut off for many homes. Photo: Jari Tanskanen/Yle

There has been a strong wind in Finland on Saturday and Sunday.

For example, the wind has knocked trees onto power lines and caused power cuts in different parts of Finland.

On Saturday evening the wind knocked a tree onto the railway line between Hämeenlinna and Tampere. Train traffic could not run, and the rail company VR transported people by bus.

## Lakes are warming quickly

![Frog pond in Puijonlaakso, Kuopio, autumn 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10990304)

Open image viewer

The water in many lakes is now warmer than usual at the beginning of June. Photo: Helmi Nykänen / Yle

Bathing waters are now warming quickly in Finland. For example, in Tampere, in Lake Näsijärvi, the water temperature was over 18 degrees on Saturday.

In many lakes the water temperature is about 15 degrees. Bathing waters have warmed quickly, because the winter was warm. The ice melted early and the waters were able to warm early.

On Sunday the wind has cooled the bathing waters a little, but they will warm up quickly again.
