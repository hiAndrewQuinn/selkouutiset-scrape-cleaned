# Tuesday 11.7.2017 (radio)

Finns have a relaxed attitude to drunk cycling. In Russian Karelia, Finnish soldiers who died in the war are being searched for. The Porin SuomiAreena interests the audience. The honey harvest now needs good weather.

## Drunk cycling

![Silhouettes of cyclists](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9716533)

Open image viewer

By law, you must not ride a bicycle while drunk. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Finns have a relaxed attitude to drunk cycling, that is, cycling while drunk.

In a survey by Liikenneturva, more than 25 percent of the people who answered said that they had ridden a bicycle while drunk in the last 5 years. 14 percent said that they often cycle while drunk.

More than 40 percent of Finns accept cycling after having a little to drink. Instead, 50 percent do not accept drunk cycling at all.

By law, you must not ride a bicycle while drunk. You can be fined or sent to prison for up to 3 months for it.

## Soldiers who died in the war

![Nina Stolepova, Oleg Stolepov and Ismo Mikkonen search for the remains of soldiers on the Maaselkä Isthmus](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9696636)

Nina and Oleg Stolepov and Ismo Mikkonen are searching together for the remains of soldiers who died in the war. Photo: Heikki Haapalainen / Yle

In Russian Karelia, the remains of Finnish soldiers who died in the war are being searched for during the summer.

More than 90,000 Finnish soldiers died in the battles of the Winter War and the Continuation War. About 12,000 bodies were left on the battlefield. About 1,200 of them have been found so far.

Ismo Mikkonen from Savonlinna is searching the Krivi forest on the Maaselkä Isthmus. Hard battles were fought in the area in autumn 1942.

Mikkonen uses a metal detector to look for things such as a identity disc, a metal button or a belt buckle. They can be a sign that the bones of a dead person are also in the ground.

This is already the 24th summer that Ismo Mikkonen has been doing search work. Over the years he has managed to find the remains of more than 100 Finnish soldiers.

## SuomiAreena

![Audience at a SuomiAreena discussion](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9714607)

Open image viewer

Discussion events at SuomiAreena interest the audience. Photo: Päivi Meritähti / Yle

Social discussion interests Finns, even in the middle of the summer holidays. The traditional SuomiAreena started in Pori on Monday. On its very first day it drew a record audience, more than 10,000 visitors.

More than 170 discussions are held at SuomiAreena over 5 days. Almost 1,000 speakers take part in them. There are various influential people in society, such as the President and the Archbishop, politicians and business leaders.

Last year SuomiAreena drew about 63,000 visitors to Pori.

## Honey harvest

![Farmed bees at the hive entrance](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8939426)

Open image viewer

Bees make honey. Photo: Heikki Rönty / Yle

The weather in the next few weeks will decide what this summer's honey harvest is like.

The honey harvest of the last 2 summers has been poor. This year too, we will not get a top harvest, says Markku Puumalainen, the chairman of the Savo beekeepers.

Puumalainen says that it is still possible that the honey harvest will be average. But for that, a couple of weeks of warm and dry weather are needed now.
