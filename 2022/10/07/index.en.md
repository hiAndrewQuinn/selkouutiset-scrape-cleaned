Government's confidence. Nobel Peace Prize. Human rights in Russia. Non-urgent specialist care. Ski resorts. Weather forecast.

## Government's confidence

![Sanna Marin speaks in the Parliament's interpellation session on 5.10.2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3408,h_1917,x_0,y_250/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1017545633eb9f85dbbc/1666064113)

Sanna Marin's government won the majority of Parliament's confidence in the interpellation vote on Friday. Photo: Jorma Vihtonen / Yle

Parliament has voted on the government's confidence. The government received Parliament's confidence by 91 to 72 votes.

Parliament voted on the government's confidence, because the opposition tabled an interpellation on whether the government has handled the energy crisis well.

The interpellation was tabled by the Finns Party, the Christian Democrats and Liike Nyt. They are opposition parties, that is, they are not in the government. Because the government received confidence, the government may continue its work.

## Nobel Peace Prize

![Ales Bjaljatski](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3168,h_1781,x_0,y_210/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1018053633fee5543658/1665134198)

The Nobel Peace Prize has been awarded to 1 person and 2 human rights organisations.

The Nobel Peace Prize goes to the Belarusian activist **Ales Bjaljatski**, the Russian human rights organisation **Memorial** and the Ukrainian human rights organisation **Center for Civil Liberties**.

The Belarusian Ales Bjaljatski and the organisations Memorial and Center for Civil Liberties have done a lot of work for human rights.

## UN Human Rights Council

![UN logo or emblem.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-83097592581daa5fd4/1495630457)

Open image viewer

The UN Human Rights Council has not previously monitored human rights in Russia´. Photo: Wikimedia Commons.

The UN Human Rights Council will begin to monitor human rights in Russia.

The UN decision is historic. Never before has the UN intervened in Russia's human rights situation. Almost all EU countries approved the decision in the UN Human Rights Council. Only Hungary voted against.

A total of 17 UN member states supported the decision that Russia's human rights must be monitored. 6 countries opposed the decision.

## Non-urgent specialist care

![sss](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5271,h_2964,x_0,y_96/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10082576322e4826f714/1663244824)

Open image viewer

Waiting for specialist care is longest in North Savo. The shortest waiting times are in South Karelia. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

Queues for non-urgent specialist care have grown longer.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that at the end of August over 150,000 people were waiting to get non-urgent specialist care. The number is over 12,000 patients more than a year ago.

The waiting time can be long. Almost 14 percent of people have waited over half a year to get non-urgent specialist care.

The first ski resorts have opened today, Friday.

The season began, for example, at the Levi ski resort in Kittilä and at the Ruka ski resort in Kuusamo.

The ski resorts have stored snow from last winter. Now the stored snow has been spread on the slopes. In this way snow was already put onto the ski slopes.
