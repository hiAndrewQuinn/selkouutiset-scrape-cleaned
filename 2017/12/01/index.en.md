# Friday 1.12.2017 (radio)

The National Coalition Party is popular. Cheating in car taxation. People are buying blue and white decorations. The Unknown Soldier is a much-watched film. Independence Day is coming soon.

## The National Coalition Party is the most popular party

![A graphic of party support.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9956369)

Open image viewer

Yle has measured support for the parties. In Yle's poll, the National Coalition Party is the most popular party in Finland. Support for the National Coalition Party is now 22.3 percent.

Support for the SDP was under 16 percent at the end of the summer, but now it has risen to almost 20 percent. At the same time, the gap to the National Coalition Party, which remains the most popular, has become smaller.

The Centre Party now stayed at the same figures as last month. Support for the Centre Party is now 17.4 percent

## Tax is not always paid correctly on cars

![A car's steering wheel and a pedestrian crossing with pedestrians](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9940493)

Open image viewer

Used cars are brought in from abroad. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Used cars are brought to Finland from abroad. According to a new study, taxes are not always paid honestly on the cars .

The tax cheating happens like this: the car's owner says that the car has already been driven a lot. In reality, the car has been driven less than the owner says.

That is why the amount of car tax in the taxation is lower than the correct value.

According to the study, the state has lost millions of euros in tax income because of the lying.

## People are buying blue and white decorations

![Finnish flags on a string in a green plastic tree.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9957935)

Open image viewer

The flag strings ran out in the shop. Photo: Katja Halinen / Yle

People are now buying a lot of decorations that are in the colours of the Finnish flag. For example, a string with flags on it is very popular. The flag strings have sold out, and you can no longer get them in the shops.

People are also interested in other blue and white decorations. On the shop shelves, blue baubles are beating red baubles. Christmas seems to be turning blue and white.

The film The Unknown Soldier will soon also be shown in Finland's neighbouring country Sweden. An international version of the film will be shown there.

## There will soon be a celebration at the Presidential Palace

![The Presidential Palace](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8507730)

Open image viewer

On Independence Day there is a celebration at the palace. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Finland's Independence Day is next week, and Finland will then turn 100. Independence Day is celebrated at the Presidential Palace.

About 1,900 guests have been invited to the Presidential Palace this year. People who were in the war are once again guests of honour this year.

This year the Independence Day reception will last about an hour longer than usual. The presidential couple Sauli Niinistö and Jenni Haukio will start greeting the invited guests at 7 pm, and the music will stop at 11 pm.

The guests will be entertained by, among others, the popular singers Katri Helena and Diandra.
