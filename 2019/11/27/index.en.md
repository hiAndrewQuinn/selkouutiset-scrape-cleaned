# Wednesday 27.11.2019 (radio)

The postal strike has ended. Christmas post will arrive in time. Pajtim Statovci has won the Finlandia Prize for literature. The weather is rainy.

## Postal strike ends

![Postal strike](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2048,h_1152,x_0,y_192/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6162355dde342555d0c/1574843835)

Postal workers are happy, because an agreement has been reached. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

The postal strike has ended. The employers' and employees' organisations approved a new agreement on Wednesday. The agreement sets the wages and working hours in the sector.

The postal strike ends straight away, and the postal workers will go back to work as soon as possible. Postal delivery workers, for example, have been on strike.

The postal strike lasted more than 2 weeks. During the strike, postal workers received support from workers in other sectors. The support strikes, for example, stopped part of the traffic on Monday. Now the support strikes are also being called off and ending.

## Christmas post arrives in time

![Christmas letters in the Posti sorting centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9341936)

Open image viewer

Finns send a lot of post at Christmas. Photo: Anna Nevalainen / Yle

The end of the postal strike means that Christmas post will arrive in time.

Posti is trying to return to its normal schedule as quickly as possible. Posti's workers are starting straight away to clear the backlog that built up during the strike.

However, it will take some time for the post from the strike period to arrive. A small part of the post may only arrive in early January.

## Finlandia prizes

![Pajtim Statovci and the book Bolla.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11089309)

Open image viewer

Pajtim Statovci received the Finlandia Prize for the book Bolla. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

This year's Finlandia Prizes have been awarded. The Finlandia Prize is Finland's most important literary prize.

The winner of the Finlandia Prize for fiction is Pajtim Statovci. The prize was for Statovci's book Bolla.

Bolla tells about love and dreams. The story is sad, because the background is the war in Kosovo.

Pajtim Statovci is 29 years old. He is the youngest writer ever to win the Finlandia Prize.

Statovci comes from Kosovo. He was 2 years old when he moved to Finland with his family. Statovci says that through writing he tries to find out what kind of place he comes from.

The best non-fiction book and the best children's book also received prizes.

The non-fiction Finlandia Prize went to the book Metsä meidän jälkeemme. It tells about the forest and the relationship Finns have with the forest.

The children's and young adult Finlandia Prize went to the book Auringon pimeä puoli. In the book, for example, people travel through time.

## Rainy Thursday

![Snowy autumn landscape in Heinävaara, Joensuu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11026161)

Open image viewer

On Thursday, it will snow, sleet and rain in Finland. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The weather is rainy. On Thursday, it will rain almost everywhere in the country.

In the north, it will snow, and there may be more than 10 centimetres of snow. In the south and west of the country, the precipitation will be rain or sleet, because the temperature is above zero.
