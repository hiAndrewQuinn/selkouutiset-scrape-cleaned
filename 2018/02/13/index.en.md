# Tuesday 13.2.2018 (radio)

The car factory in Uusikaupunki is hiring 1000 new workers. The UN refugee agency UNHCR criticises Finland's asylum decisions. Many cancers are becoming more common because of obesity and diabetes. Kela is again reclaiming student financial aid. Finland was left without medals on Tuesday at the Winter Olympics.

## More jobs are coming to Uusikaupunki

Last year more than 2000 car builders were hired at the Uusikaupunki factory. Photo: Valmet Automotive

About 1000 new workers will be hired this year at Valmet Automotive's car factory in Uusikaupunki.  New workers are needed because production of the new Mercedes-Benz A-Class is starting in Uusikaupunki.

 At the end of the year, about 4,500 workers will be working at the Uusikaupunki factory.

## The refugee agency criticises Finland's asylum decisions

![Baghdad street view](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9919072)

Open image viewer

A man sells fruit in Baghdad Photo: Mohammed Messari / EPA

The UN, or United Nations, refugee agency UNHCR criticises Finland's asylum decisions.

UNHCR says that negative decisions rely too much on the possibility of so-called internal flight. It means that a person can return to their home country to some other place than the one they left from.

Jorma Vuorio, the director general of the Migration Department at the Ministry of the Interior, does not see a problem in this. Vuorio says that UNHCR is one organisation among others and its view does not decide what Finland does.

In June 2016, 77 percent of Iraqi asylum seekers got a negative decision. In 2015, only 13 percent got a negative decision.

## Cancers are becoming more common because of obesity

![A man's waist is measured with a tape measure.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9569410)

Open image viewer

The current recommendation is that men's waist measurement is at most 94 centimetres and women's 80. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Many cancers are becoming more common because of obesity and diabetes.

About 6 percent of all cancers are caused by obesity and diabetes. Of liver cancers and oesophageal cancers, 25 percent were caused by obesity and diabetes. Of cancers of the lining of the womb, 40 percent were caused by them.

Today about a third of the people on Earth are overweight or obese. About 415 million adults have diabetes. In Finland, about 500,000 people have diabetes. One in five people of working age is overweight. 175 countries were included in the study.

## Kela reclaims student financial aid

![A student financial aid application.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9720294)

Open image viewer

A student financial aid application. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Kela is reclaiming student financial aid for the year 2016.

Almost 35,000 students have to pay money back to Kela. The number is a little bigger than a year earlier. The sum is on average 1,300 euros per student. In total, more than 45 million euros of student financial aid is being reclaimed.

A little over 320,000 students got student financial aid in 2016.

## Finland was left without medals at the Olympics on Tuesday

No Finn reached the medals at the Winter Olympics on Tuesday. Finland has 2 medals so far.

The first doping case has been revealed at the Olympics. Japanese short track speed skater Kei Saito was caught doping.
