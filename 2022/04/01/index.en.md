# Friday 1.4.2022 (radio)

Nurses' strike. Patient safety. Kela. Ramadan. Weather forecast.

## Nurses' strike

![Nurses at a demonstration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7384,h_4153,x_523,y_1307/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9363716246838c2147b/1648788606)

Nurses held demonstrations in various parts of Finland on Friday. This picture is from Helsinki. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

The nurses' strike began on Friday morning. The strike started in the 6 largest hospital districts. About 25,000 nurses are on strike.

The nurses' unions Tehy and Super rejected the National Conciliator's compromise proposal on Wednesday. The nurses say that the pay rises in the compromise proposal were too small.

Nurses want more pay so that more workforce can also be recruited to the sector. The nurses' strike will spread in 2 weeks if no agreement is reached on pay and new working conditions.

## Patient safety

![A woman lies in a bed while receiving chemotherapy through her arm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_297/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8652626163fb477465e/1633942456)

For example, cancer treatments, births and other urgent matters are also being handled during the strike. Photo: Alamy / All Over Press

The nurses' strike worries the authorities. Authorities are worried whether patient safety is at risk because many nurses are now away from work. Because of the nurses' strike, a lot of non-urgent care is being cancelled in hospitals.

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health is preparing a law by which nurses can be ordered to work. Under the new law, nurses who are on strike can be ordered to work so that patient safety is not endangered.

The nursing organisations Tehy and Super say that patient safety is not endangered because of the nurses' strike. The nursing organisations do not like that Finland is preparing a law by which nurses can be ordered to work.

## Kela

![Kela forms on a shelf.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3468,h_1951,x_0,y_102/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81249060b0c3aa21b9d/1643701341)

Open image viewer

Some of the attachments to Kela's letters are blank because of a printing problem. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

The social security institution Kela has sent tens of thousands of Finns incomplete letters. In Kela's letters and decisions, some of the pages of the attachments are blank. The reason is that there was a fault in printing the letters. In a letter, for example, the application form may be a blank page.

Kela says that if customers have sent Kela forms that are missing information, Kela will contact the customer and ask for the information.

The printing problem concerns the attachments to Kela's letters. Kela's printing problem does not concern the letters and decisions on social assistance.

## Ramadan

![People on a beach and in the sea. Sunset in the capital of Oman, Muscat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2896,h_1629,x_0,y_250/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-92534062275edd7e16b/1682676553)

Open image viewer

During Ramadan, people eat only after the sun has set. Photo: Magdy Shazly / EPA

At the weekend the Muslim fasting month, Ramadan, begins. In Finland, Ramadan begins on Saturday.

Ramadan is a holy month for Muslims. Ramadan includes fasting. During the fast, one may not eat or drink while the sun is in the sky.

After Ramadan, the id al-fitr festival is celebrated. In Finland, id al-fitr is this year on 2 May.
