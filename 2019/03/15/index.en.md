# Friday 15.3.2019 (radio)

A terror attack has been carried out against mosques in New Zealand. Schoolchildren have protested against climate change. Under-18s may no longer get married, not even with an exemption permit. The weather is humid in the south, and sunny in Northern Lapland.

## Terror attack in New Zealand

![Map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10690037)

Graphic: Yle News Graphics

A terror attack has been carried out in New Zealand. 49 people died in the attack. They were shot.

The attack targeted 2 mosques. The mosques are in the city of Christchurch.

The main perpetrator of the attack is a 28-year-old Australian man. Australia is New Zealand's neighbouring country.

Before the attack, the man wrote about the matter on an internet discussion board. He wrote that he is white and a racist. He wanted to avenge the terror attacks that Islamists have carried out in Europe.

New Zealand's prime minister says that Friday was a bad day in New Zealand's history.

## Schoolchildren protest against climate change

![Protest in Tampere](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10690514)

Open image viewer

Schoolchildren also demonstrated in Tampere. Photo: Marko Melto / Yle

Schoolchildren have protested against climate change.

The schoolchildren were absent from school and marched against climate change in different parts of Finland. For example, more than 3,000 schoolchildren took part in the demonstration in Helsinki.

Schoolchildren's demonstrations have taken place in many countries. Young people think that adults are ruining young people's future. Adults do nothing, even though the climate is warming so much that people's lives will become difficult.

The schoolchildren's hero is 16-year-old Swede Greta Thunberg. She started demonstrations in front of the Swedish parliament building last year. Now Thunberg is being proposed for the Nobel Peace Prize.

## Marriage law

![wedding couple](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10507593)

Open image viewer

Marriages of under-18s will be banned. Photo: AOP

The marriage law is changing.

Marriages of under-18s will be banned completely. Under-18s may no longer marry, not even with an exemption permit.

Until now, an under-18 could get an exemption permit for marriage. The exemption permit has been granted by the Ministry of Justice.

In recent years, few exemption permits have been granted. For example, in 2017 the Ministry of Justice granted permission for 7 marriages of minors. Usually the applicants have been 17-year-old girls.

The amendment to the Marriage Act comes into force at the beginning of June.
