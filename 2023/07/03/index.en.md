## Joe Biden in Helsinki

![Sauli Niinistö talks with Joe Biden.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2047,h_1151,x_0,y_42/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113730464a1901d84da0/1688310025)

Helsinki is preparing for the visit of US President Joe Biden. The aim is for Biden's visit to be safe.

When Biden is in Helsinki, many areas in the centre of Helsinki will be closed.

Many police officers will come to Helsinki from other parts of Finland. Many American security guards will also come with Biden.

In addition to the presidents of the USA and Finland, the leaders of the other Nordic countries will also meet in Helsinki. They will discuss, for example, the military alliance NATO. Finland joined NATO in the spring.

Biden is coming to Finland on 13 July.

## Housing sales

![A person looks at an apartment in a block of flats that is for sale.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105860063c00b8b1386f/1682666389)

A small flat can be cheap now. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Housing prices have fallen a lot. Flats are now 7.4 per cent cheaper across the whole country than a year ago.

Housing prices have fallen the most in Helsinki and Vantaa. In Helsinki and Vantaa, flats are almost 10 per cent cheaper than a year ago.

The prices of small flats, that is, one-room flats, have fallen the most. In big cities there are now many new small flats that nobody buys.

Housing prices have fallen because interest rates have risen. Interest has to be paid when you take out a housing loan.

## Breast cancer

![Mammography examination in progress.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4288,h_2412,x_0,y_221/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-32959258bfc2bda107e/1688387425)

Open image viewer

Mammography examines the breasts. Photo: All Over Press

More people get breast cancer than before. About 5,000 people a year get breast cancer in Finland.

One in eight women gets breast cancer at some point in their life.

One reason is that people live so long nowadays. Old people get cancer more easily than young people.

There may also be other reasons. Researchers do not know what they are.

85 per cent recover from breast cancer. Still, about 900 people die of breast cancer in Finland each year.

The beginning of July is colder than the last few weeks. In addition, it may rain and be very windy. This whole week is rather cool.

The end of June was warm. There were 19 heat days in June in Finland. Heat means that the temperature is over 25 degrees.
