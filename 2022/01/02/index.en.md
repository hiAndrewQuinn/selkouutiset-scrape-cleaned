## Event guarantee

![Kymi Sinfonietta's dress rehearsal at the Kotka concert hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_650/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8664166166bf0043902/1634124185)

Corona and cancelled events have become expensive for the event industry. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

Almost 470 event industry entrepreneurs have received help from the corona-time event guarantee. About 600 compensation applications came to the State Treasury.

By the end of the year, about 14 million euros in compensation had been paid. The compensation was paid for costs caused by cancelling or scaling down an event.

The most compensation went to music festivals, concerts and exhibitions, and to art and cultural events.

## Household tax deduction

![A rug being vacuumed in the living room.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5288,h_2975,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-780747603fb73ea13ed/1614788967)

Open image viewer

You get the household tax deduction when the work is done by a company. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The household tax deduction changed at the start of this year.

The deductions for household work and for care and nursing work are going up. The deduction percentage of the labour share is now 60. Giving up oil heating is also supported with corresponding increases.

Instead, the deductions for other renovations stay the same. Their reimbursement percentage is still 40.

The own share stayed at 100 euros. It must be paid, whether one has used the household tax deduction a little or a lot.

The deductible amount also rises. It is now 3,500 euros. Last year the amount was 2,200 euros.

A 2-year trial begins for household work and for care and nursing work. It will examine the effect of the deduction on employment.

## Biowaste

![Typical biowaste: a coffee filter and a banana peel.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2048,h_1152,x_0,y_192/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81438060b70660f33d6/1622607529)

Open image viewer

Enormous amounts of biowaste go to waste. Recycling differs between municipalities. It is worth checking your own municipality's waste rules. Photo: Jouni Tanninen / Yle

Too little biowaste is collected in Finland compared to the target. Of all biowaste, less than half is now estimated to be recycled, the Finnish Environment Institute says.

The target is that by next year, 60 per cent of biowaste is put to use.

Biowaste is food and kitchen waste that rots and can be composted.

About half of the recyclable biowaste goes to composting and half to digestion plants. In the plants it is processed into biogas and fertilisers.

## Emergency calls

![An emergency centre operator at work, taking calls at their workstation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6573,h_3697,x_14,y_169/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89246661b9e469b4765/1639573089)

Open image viewer

Emergency centre operator at work. Photo: Patrik Molander / Yle

On New Year's, the emergency centre answered almost 15,000 emergency calls in Finland. About half of the calls led to an authority's emergency task.

The most common emergency tasks concerned vandalism, disturbing behaviour, falls and illness.

90 per cent of emergency tasks were passed on either to emergency medical services or to the police.

## The weather

![Weather map of Finland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12255709)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

On Monday snowfall moves east and weakens. There is a lot of cloud.

Snow or snow showers will come in the south and in places in the north. Driving conditions will become poor in many places.

The driest weather is in the central part of the country.

The temperature in the southern and western parts of the country is between -1 and -10 degrees. In the east and north it is between -10 and -25 degrees.
