## Nord Stream

![Bubbling water in a picture taken from an aircraft.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1146,h_644,x_0,y_47/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10133716332f057864e6/1664286004)

Open image viewer

Gas bubbles at the blast site of the Nord Stream gas pipeline. Photo: Forsvaret, Danmark

There is new information about the Nord Stream gas pipeline explosions in the Baltic Sea last autumn. The Danish Defence Forces have confirmed that a Russian special vessel moved near the gas pipelines. The ship was in the area 4 days before the explosions that broke the pipes.

Experts say that this Russian vessel has, among other things, a mini submarine.

A Danish patrol boat took 26 pictures of the Russian vessel. The pictures were taken east of the island of Bornholm on 22 September. The gas pipelines were blown up on 26 September.

The pipes run from Russia to Germany.

## UN and the Taliban

![Women in burqas at a market in Kabul.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3478,h_1956,x_3,y_154/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98724862e142049abc6/1660202682)

Open image viewer

Women in Kabul, Afghanistan. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

The UN, the United Nations, has taken a position on the situation in Afghanistan. The UN Security Council demands that the Taliban government cancel all measures that restrict women's rights.

The Security Council's decision was unanimous. The decision also condemns the Taliban's order that Afghan women may not work under the UN.

The decision stresses that women must be able to study, work and move freely.

The UN said last month that women are oppressed more in Afghanistan than in any other country in the world.

OP Group still predicts a bad time for the housing market. OP estimates that prices will fall by 4–6 percent on average this year.

In the capital region, the fall could be as much as 8 percent.

The rise in mortgage interest rates has been strong.

The VAT reduction on electricity ends at the beginning of May. The reduction was introduced at the beginning of December, when the electricity price rose a lot. VAT temporarily fell from 24 percent to 10 percent.

The rise in VAT shows directly on the electricity bill.

## Saimaa Canal

![A ship, M/S Carelia, in the Saimaa Canal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1096500642eb08aaf0e8/1680781526)

Open image viewer

The canal connects the Saimaa lake system from Lappeenranta to the Gulf of Finland via Vyborg. The canal is in both Finnish and Russian territory. Photo: Pasi Tapanainen / Yle

The Saimaa Canal will open to traffic again next Tuesday.

The canal is open to ship traffic around the clock during the sailing season.

The Saimaa Canal is 43 kilometres long. Almost half of the canal is in an area that is leased from Russia. The canal lease is an international agreement that Parliament has approved.

EU sanctions against Russia do not stop traffic on the Saimaa Canal
