# Tuesday 20.10.2020 (radio)

Sexual harassment has increased in service sectors. Unemployment is rising. Finnair is laying off hundreds of people. Coronavirus restrictions on restaurants are being changed. Parliament gets a mask recommendation.

## Sexual harassment in service sectors

![A waiter filling a fridge with drinks in a restaurant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7255445f75f631c1d1d/1613977314)

Many waiters face sexual harassment. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Many workers in service sectors experience sexual harassment. For example, waiters and shop assistants experience harassment. They meet a lot of people.

For example, about 70 percent of waiters say that they face sexual harassment at work.

More and more workers in service sectors are now experiencing sexual harassment. The service sector trade union asked service sector workers about this.

## Unemployment is rising

![A helmet on a rack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2500,h_1406,x_0,y_133/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6448725e4e5c2452cb4/1582194195)

Unemployment is rising. The coronavirus situation has worsened many companies' situation. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Statistics Finland has published a new statistic on unemployment in Finland. Unemployment is rising.

In September, 208,000 people in Finland were unemployed. There were 47,000 more unemployed people than in September last year.

There were 35,000 fewer employed people, i.e. people who are at work, than in September last year.

## Finnair is laying off

![A Finnair aircraft being moved at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3706,h_2085,x_0,y_249/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7265905f7b2a9ba2996/1601907408)

Most aircraft stay on the ground. It may take a long time before travel increases again. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

The airline Finnair is cutting many jobs. Finnair has held co-operation negotiations with staff, because Finnair is doing very badly.

Finnair is cutting 700 jobs. About 600 people will lose their jobs in Finland and about 100 people abroad. In addition, Finnair is continuing to furlough staff.

The airline is doing badly because travel has fallen a great deal.

## Restaurant restrictions

![Restaurant Yuca in Rovaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3603,h_2027,x_0,y_186/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7322545f8eeda7822ce/1603202564)

The restrictions are aimed at nightclubs and bars, i.e. restaurants where the coronavirus spreads more easily. Photo: Pekka Viinikka / Yle

Coronavirus restrictions on restaurants are being changed. From November, food restaurants and cafés may take in more customers than nightclubs and bars.

Nightclubs and bars may take in customers at 50 percent of their seating capacity. Food restaurants and cafés may take in customers at 75 percent of their seating capacity.

The restrictions are aimed at where the coronavirus spreads more easily.

## Mask recommendation for Parliament

![Ministers in face masks at Parliament's question time in Helsinki: Anna-Maja Henriksson, Sanna Marin, Matti Vanhanen, Annika Saarikko, Krista Kiuru, Maria Ohisalo and Li Andersson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11562170)

A mask recommendation is coming into Parliament. MPs and employees must wear face masks in shared spaces.

In offices or rooms where people are on their own, a mask does not need to be worn.

A lot of work is also done remotely in Parliament. For example, on Wednesday committees will hear Prime Minister Sanna Marin over a remote connection.
