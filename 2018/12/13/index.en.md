# Thursday 13.12.2018 (radio)

The director general of the Finnish Transport Safety Agency, Trafi, is resigning. There has been a serious train accident in Turkey. Google has said what things people have searched for information about this year. On Thursday St Lucia's Day was celebrated.

## Director General of Trafi

![Mia Nykopp](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10552745)

Mia Nykopp. Photo: Handout / Lehtikuva

The director general of Trafi is resigning. Director General Mia Nykopp announced her resignation on Thursday.

Trafi is the Finnish Transport Safety Agency. For example, it develops traffic safety.

The director general is resigning because people's driving licence information was not safe in Trafi's electronic service.

The service was closed, and it will be opened again gradually once the information is secured.

## Train accident in Turkey

![The damage from the accident is visible in daylight in Ankara, Turkey, on 13 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10552304)

The train accident happened near Marsandiz railway station in Ankara. Photo: Adem Altan / AFP

There has been a serious train accident in Turkey. At least 9 people died and almost 50 people were injured.

The accident happened in Ankara, the capital of Turkey. The train first crashed into a maintenance locomotive and after that into a footbridge. The bridge collapsed onto the train.

Turkish police have arrested 3 railway workers. It is suspected that they did not do their work well enough.

## Google search

![A cat looks at the Google search page.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10549736)

Photo: John G. Mabanglo / EPA

Google has said what things people have searched for information about this year. Google is the biggest search company in the world.

Finns searched on Google this year for information about, for example, the presidential elections. There were presidential elections in Finland at the beginning of this year.

Finns were also interested in Helsinki's new library, Oodi. Oodi was opened in December.

In the summer Finns were interested in the hotel and restaurant Kalastajatorppa. US President Donald Trump stayed at Kalastajatorppa when he met the Russian president in Helsinki. The meeting was in July.

The summer was also really hot. That is why many Finns searched on Google for, for example, how to cool a room and how to treat heat stroke.

## Lucia

![President Sauli Niinistö and his wife Mrs Jenni Haukio receive traditional Christmas greetings at Mäntyniemi on 19 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9983954)

On Thursday St Lucia's Day was celebrated.

Finland's Lucia was crowned in Helsinki Cathedral. A candle crown was put on Lucia's head. After that Lucia went through the centre of Helsinki in a big procession.

Finland's Lucia this year is Elin Qvist. She is 19 years old and from Kauniainen.

Lucia is important for Swedish-speaking Finns, that is, Finland-Swedes. For example, St Lucia's celebration is held in many Swedish-speaking schools.

During the winter Lucia visits, for example, care homes and brings joy to the elderly. It is said that Lucia brings light to the dark winter.
