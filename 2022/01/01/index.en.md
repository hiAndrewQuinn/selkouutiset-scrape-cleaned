## New Year

There are usually many people in Senate Square in Helsinki when the year changes. This year it was different. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

The year has changed across the whole country more quietly than usual. Corona restrictions, among other things, held back the celebrations.

The police had work to do because of, for example, home alarms and fireworks. At least 9 people have got eye injuries from fireworks.

Only one of them was wearing protective glasses.

## The president's speech

![President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö giving his traditional New Year's speech for 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4813,h_2707,x_1,y_164/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89799961d02780bfbcc/1641034234)

Open image viewer

In his New Year's speech, President Sauli Niinistö stressed that the spheres of influence of great powers do not belong in the 2020s. Photo: LEHTIKUVA / RONI REKOMAA

President Sauli Niinistö says that discord is a bigger threat than the pandemic. In his New Year's speech, Niinistö hoped that protecting health would not become a source of dispute.

Niinistö stressed that the third year of the pandemic is starting and requires perseverance. Different opinions belong in democracy, but discord and ill will do not, the president said.

President Niinistö also spoke about security policy. Niinistö said that Finland's room for manoeuvre also includes the option of joining a military alliance. Finland can apply for membership of the military alliance NATO, if Finland itself decides so, Niinistö said.

## Unemployment

![TE Office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_183/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7404295faa9713df2b7/1605093561)

Open image viewer

Unemployment no longer worries Finns as much as before. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

Finns' worry about unemployment has decreased a lot since a year ago, according to a Taloustutkimus survey.

A year ago, 39 per cent answered that unemployment was the second most important concern. Now only 17 per cent of respondents are worried about unemployment.

Instead, 41 per cent of respondents are worried about inequality and the growth of income differences.

## Strike

![Two men in high-visibility vests stand in front of the UPM factory in Lappeenranta early on the morning of 1 January 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3543,h_1992,x_0,y_369/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89799361d00a19959ae/1641024175)

Open image viewer

Strikes began early this morning, including in front of UPM's Kaukas factory in Lappeenranta. The strikes are due to continue until 22 January. Photo: Lehtikuva

The Paper Union, the Electricians' Union and the Trade Union Pro have started strikes at the factories of the forest company UPM. The strike locations are Jämsänkoski, Kouvola, Lappeenranta, Pietarsaari, Rauma, Tampere and Valkeakoski.

The strike will last 3 weeks if the parties cannot agree on new working conditions.

The Paper Union demands one agreement that applies to the whole group. The forest company UPM wants 5 collective agreements, one for each sector separately.

## EU presidency

![French President Emmanuel Macron.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3114,h_1751,x_4,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89718961cc46409a34a/1640777508)

Open image viewer

The country's presidential election also falls during France's EU presidency. The picture shows President Emmanuel Macron. Photo: Johanna Geron / AFP

France is now the presiding country of the European Union. The term lasts half a year.

France wants to focus on migration, the Schengen rules and border security, for example. During France's term, defence and the economy are also in focus.

The presidency rotates from one member country to another.

## The weather

On Sunday snowfall reaches the western part of the country and Western Lapland during the afternoon and evening. In the east it is still mostly dry.
