## The situation in Russia

![People at the fence around the Kremlin in Moscow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_152/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11342256496d0b9baeaa/1687622277)

Security measures in Moscow have been strict at the weekend. Photo: Boris Alekseev / AOP

In Russia, the Wagner troops' rebellion is over. The Wagner troops have stopped their actions against the Russian military leadership.

Russia says that the leader of the Wagner troops, Jevgeni Prigožin, is leaving Russia. In addition, it has been agreed that the Wagner soldiers will not be punished because of the rebellion.

On Saturday the Wagner troops were on their way towards the Russian capital, Moscow. The Wagner leader Prigožin wanted to change the leaders of the Russian army.

The Wagner troops have taken part in Russia's war against Ukraine. In Prigožin's view, the leaders of the Russian army have led the war badly.

On Saturday evening, Prigožin announced that the journey of the Wagner soldiers towards Moscow would end. However, security measures in Moscow have been strict on Sunday too.

## Amusement park accident

![Roller coaster in a summer amusement park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113429764982979b4aaf/1687705175)

Open image viewer

Gröna Lund amusement park in Stockholm. Photo: Yle / Jutta Mattsson

A serious accident has happened in Sweden. The accident happened in Stockholm, at the Gröna Lund amusement park.

1 person died when a roller coaster car came off the track. In addition, 9 people were injured.

The top speed of the Jetline roller coaster is 90 kilometres per hour. The roller coaster runs at a height of up to 30 metres.

After the accident, the Gröna Lund amusement park was emptied and closed.

The cause of the accident is not yet known.

## Meeting of reserve soldiers

![Blue and white NATO flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1918,h_1078,x_0,y_212/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9627436295f4b920940/1653994733)

Open image viewer

Reserve soldiers from many NATO countries are coming to the reserve organisation's summer meeting. Photo: Pertti Aherva/Yle

A big summer meeting of a reserve soldiers' organisation begins in Helsinki. During it, many military uniforms will be seen on the streets of Helsinki.

About 500 reserve soldiers from different countries are coming to the summer meeting. Reserve soldiers are no longer in the army, but they are called into service in a crisis situation.

The reserve organisation's summer meeting continues until Friday.

## Special Olympics

![Veera Luhtanen, gymnast.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_125/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11331306492c266b2f99/1687340260)

Veera Luhtanen won 5 gold medals in Berlin. Photo: Lauri Jaakkola / Finnish Paralympic Committee

Finland has done well in the Special Olympics world games for people with intellectual disabilities.

The Finnish team Sisujengi won 54 medals. Most medals came from rhythmic gymnastics, in which Finland won 17 medals.

The star of rhythmic gymnastics was Veera Luhtanen. Luhtanen won 5 gold medals.

The Special Olympics world games were held in Berlin, Germany.
