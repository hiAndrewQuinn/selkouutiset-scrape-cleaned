# Tuesday 21.3.2023 (radio)

Electricity production is working well in Finland now. Housing company maintenance charges rise in spring. Advance voting for the parliamentary election starts on Wednesday. There have been demonstrations in France because of a pension law. Weather forecast.

## Electricity production

![Wind turbines behind a lake and forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4800,h_2700,x_0,y_379/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-108249064087c981ed30/1679407149)

Many wind turbines have already been built in Finland. Photo: Jesper Alm / Yle

Electricity production is working well in Finland now. On Saturday Finland's power plants produced much more electricity than was used in Finland. Therefore electricity was sold abroad at full capacity, that is, as much electricity as could be transmitted from Finland along the power lines. The situation was historic: Finland has never before been able to produce so much electricity beyond its own needs.

Today, on Tuesday too, Finland's power plants have produced more electricity than is used in Finland. So electricity has been enough to sell from Finland today as well.

A lot of electricity is produced in Finland now, because the new Olkiluoto 3 nuclear power plant is in operation, and because many wind turbines have been built in Finland.

## Housing company maintenance charges

![Apartment blocks in an urban setting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2267,x_0,y_405/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-108623264131825dffec/1678973269)

Open image viewer

Houses' costs have risen. Therefore the charges must be raised. Photo: Anna Polo / Yle

The housing company maintenance charges of many housing companies rise in spring. Housing companies include, for example, apartment blocks and terraced houses.

Houses' costs have risen, because the price of energy has risen and the interest rates on loans have risen. Many housing companies have loans, for example for repairs.

New houses may have a lot of loans from building the house. Therefore the housing company must raise the maintenance charge, or the financing charge that residents pay.

The Finnish Housing Management Association estimates that in many buildings the charges will rise by 10–20 percent.

Advance voting for the parliamentary election starts tomorrow, on Wednesday. Advance voting lasts 7 days, that is, until next week's Tuesday.

There are about 950 advance voting places in Finland. Advance voting places are, for example, in libraries, shopping centres and municipal halls. Abroad there are advance voting places in 86 different countries.

The actual election day for the parliamentary election is Sunday 2 April.

There have been big demonstrations in France because of the new pension law. There have been demonstrations in the capital, Paris, and in other French cities.

Protesters are angry about the pension reform that President Emmanuel Macron's government has made. The government did not take the law to the lower house of Parliament for a decision. Instead, the government decided on the law itself.

That is why many French people got angry. The new law raises the retirement age in France. Also, the working career must be longer than before in order to get a pension.

The weather varies in different parts of Finland.

On Wednesday it will rain in southern Finland and snow in central Finland. In the north it does not rain, and the sun shines there too. The temperature in the south is +3 degrees, and in the central part of the country zero degrees. In the north there is a little frost.
