## Finland and NATO

![Flags of NATO and Finland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2591,h_1457,x_0,y_501/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-954582627e1d85156f2/1652618008)

Open image viewer

Flags of the military alliance NATO and Finland. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Finland's state leadership has made the official decision to apply for membership of the military alliance NATO. The decision was made at a meeting of the President of the Republic and the government's Foreign and Security Policy Committee.

President Sauli Niinistö and Prime Minister Sanna Marin told about the matter at a press conference. The President said that when Finland's security grows, it takes nothing from anyone.

The President said that ordinary Finns have been the most active in supporting NATO.

The report will still go to the government for consideration on Sunday. Parliament will debate the NATO application on Monday. After that Finland will submit the application.

## Ethical strain

![Anonymous picture of an elderly person and a home care nurse. Silhouette against a window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6580,h_3701,x_1,y_332/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-922489621f325e145f7/1646775420)

Open image viewer

Home care also brings surprises: for example a client falling, a blocked drain or ordering heating oil. They take time away from the actual care. Photo: Hilma Toivonen / Yle

Ethical strain burdens the workers of elderly care services.

Ethical strain means that a nurse has to do work that goes against norms or their own values. Ethical strain weakens the ability to work.

In elderly care services, ethical strain is almost double compared with the rest of the social and health sector.

A quarter of home care has too few nurses every week.

## Eurovision

![Kalush Orchestra performing in coloured lights.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2000,h_1125,x_0,y_39/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-952657627a131e0682e/1652169638)

Ukraine's Kalush Orchestra won the Eurovision. Photo: Corinne Cumming / EBU

Ukraine has won the Eurovision in Turin, Italy. Ukraine was represented by Kalush Orchestra with the song Stefania.

The favourite got about 630 points in total, most of them from the audience.

Kalush Orchestra received a special permit to leave Ukraine for the Eurovision. The permit ends on Monday. Because of the war, the men are not allowed to leave Ukraine.

Finland's representative The Rasmus finished 21st. Finland got 38 points in total, of which 26 came from the audience.

## Ticks

![Tick on a palm](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1080,h_608,x_0,y_658/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6718465eb39c373cdb3/1651505663)

Open image viewer

A tick must be removed from the skin soon, before it has time to pass a bacterium to a person or an animal. Photo: Yle

Ticks are once again a nuisance for people and animals.

Many tick sightings have been made in Finland during 30 days. The Punkkilive website says that more than 6000 sightings have been recorded. In the earlier period there were about 200 sightings.

Ticks spread dangerous diseases. Bacterial Lyme borreliosis is treated with antibiotics. Only the viral tick-borne encephalitis, TBE, has a vaccine.

## Memorial Day for the Fallen

![Hero graves at the Hietaniemi cemetery in Helsinki on Memorial Day for the Fallen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10214466)

Open image viewer

Hero graves at the Hietaniemi cemetery in Helsinki.

On Sunday Memorial Day for the Fallen was celebrated. The day is in honour of those who have died in Finland's wars or in peacekeeping duties.

A traditional part of the day is visiting the hero graves. Memorial Day for the Fallen has also become an established flag day.
