## Crisis resilience of Finns

![Psychologist Ville Ojanen of the Kettu working group.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3997,h_2248,x_2,y_72/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-965912629df3e5b63dd/1654519132)

Open image viewer

Psychologist Ville Ojanen works at the Prime Minister's Office. He is a member of the expert group that produces behavioural science information for decision-makers. Information is sought, for example, on vaccinations and restrictions. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

The government is starting to investigate the mental resilience of Finns. The matter is studied with the methods of behavioural science.

The corona pandemic, its restrictions, Russia's attack on Ukraine and the climate crisis cause fear. They also cause insecurity and mental health problems, especially for young people.

Psychologist Ville Ojanen gathers information for political decision-makers. Ojanen says that a behavioural science perspective is needed. It can help administration and decision-making.

Crises and restrictions have caused damage that will still be repaired for many years.

## Turkey and Finland's NATO membership

![Recep Tayyip Erdogan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4509,h_2536,x_0,y_508/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96731162a16ca075210/1654746313)

Open image viewer

NATO country Turkey's President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan has set many conditions for Finland and Sweden to become NATO members. Photo: AOP

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan demands that Finland and Sweden interfere in the activities of the state televisions.

Erdoğan has set new conditions for Turkey to accept the countries' NATO membership.

Erdoğan says that the state televisions of Finland and Sweden show interviews with terrorist leaders. By this the president means Kurds.

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says that Finland does not intend to interfere with press freedom.

Yle's news editor-in-chief Jouko Jokinen says that Turkey's demand shows the weak state of freedom of speech.

## Toilet wastewater

![Cargo ship is tied up at the harbour quay.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2738,h_1539,x_0,y_120/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-321800587b3af434d53/1484471465)

Open image viewer

Cargo is loaded onto a cargo ship. Photo: WENN.com

About 25 000 seafarers' toilet wastewater is discharged into the Baltic Sea every day. Every day 2 000 cargo ships sail on the Baltic Sea, and they release wastewater into the sea.

The sea is polluted, even though toilet wastewater can be emptied in the port free of charge.

For example, about 2500 ships visit the ports of Kotka and Hamina every year. Of them, only 14 left wastewater in the port last year.

## Åland's self-government

![Map of the demilitarised zone under the Åland agreement.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9299186234510b89ed7/1647596039)

Open image viewer

The capital of Åland is Mariehamn. About 40 per cent of the whole population of Åland lives in Mariehamn. The city is monolingually Swedish-speaking, and more than 83 per cent of its residents speak Swedish as their mother tongue. Photo: Ministry for Foreign Affairs / Derrick Frilund / Yle

Åland is celebrating the 100th anniversary of its self-government.

Åland is in the Baltic Sea between Finland and Sweden. Åland is Finland's only region that has its own self-government. It has its own parliament, called the Provincial Parliament.

The province has more than 6 700 islands. It has more than 30 000 residents.

Åland is an autonomous, demilitarised and Swedish-speaking part of Finland.

Russia's attack on Ukraine in February has caused discussion in Åland. The defence of Åland belongs to Finland.
