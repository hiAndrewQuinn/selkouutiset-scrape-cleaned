# Friday 10.3.2023 (radio)

Prime Minister Marin and Environment Minister Ohisalo visited Kyiv. More help for Ukraine from Finland. Threat of a rail strike. There is a severe shortage of pharmacists. A poor vole year is driving owls into cities. The weather.

## Marin and Ohisalo in Ukraine

Prime Minister Sanna Marin has visited Ukraine today.

Marin has met the Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Kyiv. Marin and Zelenskyy took part in a memorial event for a Ukrainian soldier. The soldier had been killed in the fighting at Bakhmut in eastern Ukraine.

Marin also visited a military hospital and a school.

The Government's Office says that the visit also includes Environment Minister Maria Ohisalo.

## More aid for Ukraine

![Volunteers distribute humanitarian aid to residents on the outskirts of Kharkiv in Ukraine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4493,h_2525,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1049728639d608f4a8c3/1671260829)

Finland is sending more aid to Ukraine.

Finland is giving Ukraine humanitarian aid and development cooperation support worth 29 million euros in total. Support will be directed, for example, to food security, health care and education.

In addition, 37 million euros has been reserved this year for supporting Ukraine through development cooperation. Ukraine is becoming Finland's largest development cooperation partner country.

## Threat of a rail strike

![R-train to Helsinki from Tampere.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10471626398189fb4392/1670912583)

Open image viewer

Locomotive drivers are threatening a strike. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

A strike threatens rail traffic in 10 days.

The strike begins on 20 March, if no solution is found to the locomotive drivers' labour dispute before then.

A compromise proposal was given for the labour dispute on Tuesday. However, the employees' union, Rautatiealan Unioni, rejected the proposal.

The dispute concerns pay rises and family leave.

## Electricity consumption

![Power line on a snowy rock, snow on tree branches, purple sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_121/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107779363f8f2c99283a/1700654949)

Open image viewer

Electricity consumption reached its winter record on Thursday morning. Photo: Terhi Liimu / Yle

Electricity consumption in Finland rose yesterday to a winter record level.

Electricity consumption was at winter record levels on Thursday morning, between 8 and 9 o'clock. At that time more than 12,000 megawatts of electricity were used, says the main grid company Fingrid.

Although consumption was at peak levels, the adequacy of electricity was not in danger.

Fingrid says that it is very exceptional for the winter peak in electricity consumption to be reached only in March.

## Owls

![A Lapland owl sits on a branch.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6533934)

Open image viewer

This is what a Lapland owl looks like. Photo: Raine Martikainen

Many owls have appeared in the towns of South-Eastern Finland.

Owls have been observed, for example, in Kouvola, Hamina, Luumäki, Porvoo and Kotka. In Kotka, for example, 3 owls have been observed: 2 Lapland owls and one Ural owl.

The owls have come to the towns to look for food. The vole year has been poor, so the owls have had to leave the areas where they normally live.
