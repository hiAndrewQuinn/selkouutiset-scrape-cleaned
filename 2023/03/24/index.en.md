## Electricity subsidy

![Light fixture hanging from a ceiling.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_10,y_528/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98160762cec5f5b6fd7/1662558153)

The electricity subsidy will come as a discount on the bill, if the household's electricity bill is over 90 euros a month. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The EU has approved the electricity subsidy, that is, Finland may give support for paying electricity bills. Electricity companies will start paying the electricity subsidy next month, that is, in April.

The electricity company gives the subsidy as a discount on the electricity bill. The electricity subsidy does not need to be applied for. Households whose electricity bill was over 90 euros a month get the electricity subsidy.

The electricity subsidy is paid afterwards for 4 months, starting from November.

## The end of the train strike

![Commuter train.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_308/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1089796641d423fab306/1679639290)

Open image viewer

Trains are running again. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

The train strike is over. The train drivers and the employer reached agreement in the labour dispute on Thursday.

Train traffic started running again this morning, on Friday. However, it takes about a day for train traffic to return to normal.

The train drivers' strike began on Monday and lasted 4 days. The strike stopped all train traffic in Finland. Now the strike is over, and the trains are running again.

## French demonstrations

![Firefighters put out smouldering rubbish that protesters have set on fire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1089767641d1b8fab6c4/1679671792)

Protesters set many fires. Photo: Anna Kurth / AFP

There have been big demonstrations in France again. The French are angry about the pension reform, which raises the retirement age.

Demonstrations have been held in many cities in France. The French police say that more than one million people took part in the demonstrations yesterday. In Paris, for example, protesters set more than 900 fires. The police arrested almost 500 protesters.

The demonstrations over the pension reform started in France in January. Yesterday's demonstration was the biggest so far.

## Scam messages

![Scam text message with a link to a website.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_972,h_546,x_49,y_27/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98434762d7f6d7cdf51/1691845812)

Open image viewer

Such messages have also come to many Finns before. Strange messages asking you to log in to some service are usually scam messages. Photo: Antti Seppälä / Yle

The Finnish Communications Regulatory Authority warns about scam messages that have the name of Posti (Finland Post) in them. Text messages have been sent to Finns asking for people's bank codes.

The message is made so that it looks as if it has come from Posti. The message says that a parcel has arrived for the person. The person is asked to log in to the service with their bank codes.

In reality the message has not come from Posti, but it is a scam message. The Finnish Communications Regulatory Authority says that if the person is not expecting a parcel, the message is a scam.

## Police traffic control

![The police officer is holding a breathalyser. The breathalyser has a tube.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_357/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1019053634404f517c93/1668413869)

The police make car drivers blow into breathalysers and also do quick drug tests. Photo: Arash Matin / Yle

The police are monitoring car drivers' fitness to drive intensively over the weekend across the whole country.

The police carry out many breath-test raids, in which they check whether the driver has drunk too much alcohol. In the raids the police also do quick drug tests.

The police's intensive monitoring starts on Friday evening and ends on Sunday morning.
