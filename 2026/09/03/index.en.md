## Education level

Finally, news about youth education.

The educational level of Finnish young adults is no longer increasing.

For example, Estonia and Sweden have overtaken Finland in higher education.

The Finnish Institute for Economic Research Etla warns that this could harm the Finnish economy.

Etla researcher **Aino Kalmbach** says that there have been too few study places at universities. There are many more applicants.

Many young people apply for a study place multiple times and have to wait in line.

The educational level of Finnish young adults is no longer increasing because there are too few study places.
