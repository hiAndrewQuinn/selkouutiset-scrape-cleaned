## Changing jobs and the labour shortage

![walker parked at the door of a care home](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6117,h_3441,x_0,y_315/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-839001610bb000760e4/1628157126)

There are now many open jobs in the health and social care sector. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

People in Finland are now changing their job and their field of work in an unusually large number.

At the start of the corona time, workers left mainly the restaurant field and the nursing field for other jobs. Now there is a shortage of workers in these fields.

People are now changing field, for example, from health care, early childhood education and schools, and from the transport field.

The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment says that there are now 600 000 job seekers. Of them, 200 000 have a job but want to change field.

For example, in the social and health care sector there are 50 000 open jobs.

## Corona patients in hospitals

![Corona testing at a health centre in Kuopio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_244/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7413415fad2327d6d17/1607588525)

Because of the coronavirus, 293 people are in hospital care. Of them, 36 are in intensive care, says the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

The number in hospital care has grown by 33 and in intensive care by 5 since Friday.

In Finland, 14 new deaths linked to the coronavirus have been reported. There are now 1 230 corona deaths.

## Citizens' initiative

![French corona pass on a smartphone](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5101,h_2869,x_0,y_287/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-844279611f4ea86daa2/1634203108)

Open image viewer

A corona pass is not required at workplaces. The citizens' initiative demands that a corona pass will not be required at workplaces in the future either. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

A citizens' initiative against the corona pass at workplaces is moving on to Parliament for discussion.

The initiative demands a law in Finland that would stop the corona pass from being used at workplaces.

The initiative says that a workplace must not put workers in an unequal position based on vaccination.

## Food supplements

![Food supplement shelf in a shop](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9987423)

Open image viewer

Many different food supplements are for sale. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

Food supplements are sold with adverts that make claims about the health effects of the products that are against the law. The claims are not based on scientific evidence.

Municipalities must supervise companies that sell food supplements. Municipalities have intervened in the activities of several companies.

Yle's MOT team looked into the matter. In the investigation there were 10 companies, and 7 of them received criticism from the authorities for illegal marketing.

## Obeying the stop sign

More than 25 per cent of drivers do not stop at a stop sign the way the law requires.

Liikenneturva observed drivers' behaviour at stop signs last summer. Observations were made in more than 4 000 places all over Finland.

However, a survey by Liikenneturva says that 90 per cent of drivers know the traffic rule that obliges them to stop at a stop sign.
