The SDP is the most popular opposition party, Yle's new opinion poll says. Social exclusion is the biggest challenge to Finland's internal security. The British writer Kazuo Ishiguro has won the Nobel Prize in Literature. The latest greetings to 100-year-old Finland come from the Finnish school in Silicon Valley, that is, Piilaakso, in the USA.

## The SDP became the largest opposition party

![Party support estimates](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9865957)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

The SDP is the most popular opposition party, according to Yle's new opinion poll. It was carried out by Taloustutkimus. The SDP overtook the Greens, the opposition party, at the same time. In the survey, more than 17 percent of respondents supported the SDP. The increase is almost 2 percentage points. More than 16 percent supported the Greens.

Support for the Centre has fallen below 16 percent. Support for the National Coalition is almost 22 percent.

The National Coalition and the Centre are government parties. Taloustutkimus's managing director Jari Pajunen says that a big gap in support can hamper the work of the government.

Almost 2,000 people answered the survey.

## Social exclusion causes crime

![District prosecutor Eija Velitski](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9317282)

Open image viewer

District prosecutor Eija Velitski sees in her own work how social exclusion causes crime. Photo: Juha Kivioja / Yle

Social exclusion is the biggest challenge to Finland's internal security. This is how the government's recent security strategy, that is, plan, assesses it. Crime statistics contain many low-income, unemployed and indebted young adults. Less than five (5) percent of the population commits more than half of all crimes.

The plan says that work best prevents social exclusion. However, well-being is distributed unevenly, and many people become excluded in many different ways.

The chairman of the Crime Prevention Council, Eija Velitski, says that crime is prevented poorly in Finland. Velitski proposes that one authority starts to prevent crime and social exclusion. It can be an agency or an institution. The task of the other authorities is to look after their own area and cooperate with others, Velitski says.

More than a hundred (100) experts made the internal security plan.

## Kazuo Ishiguro wins the Nobel Prize in Literature

![Kazuo Ishiguro](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9867548)

Open image viewer

Ishiguro was not the favourite for the Nobel Prize. Photo: WENN / AOP

The British writer Kazuo Ishiguro has won the Nobel Prize in Literature.

Ishiguro was born in Japan in 1954, but he grew up in England. Seven (7) of Ishiguro's books have been translated into Finnish.

He is best known for a book that has been translated into Finnish under the title Pitkän päivän ilta. A film of the same name has been made from it.

Around the world there is a large group of Finnish children and young people who study the Finnish language and Finnish culture at Finnish schools.

Now that independent Finland is turning 100 years old, the Finnish schools are sending greetings to Finland on video.

The latest greetings come from the Finnish school in Silicon Valley, in the USA.
