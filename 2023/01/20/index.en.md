# Friday 20.1.2023 (radio)

Weapons aid for Ukraine. Finnish corona vaccine. Summer jobs. Weather.

## Weapons aid for Ukraine

![People in a meeting hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688124)

Open image viewer

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy took part in a meeting in Germany by video link. Photo: EPA-EFE

Finland is giving more weapons aid to Ukraine. Finland is sending, for example, ammunition to Ukraine.

The value of the aid package is over 400 million euros. It is the largest aid package that Finland has sent to Ukraine so far.

Finland is cooperating with Sweden. Thanks to the cooperation Finland's own defence is still good, even though Finland is sending weapons to Ukraine.

Many other countries have also decided to give more aid to Ukraine. For example, Britain and Estonia have announced additional aid to Ukraine.

A meeting was held in Germany on Friday where various countries discussed helping Ukraine. Finland's Defence Minister Mikko Savola also took part in the meeting.

In Ukraine there is fear of a new big Russian attack. Russia began the war in Ukraine last February.

## Finnish corona vaccine

![A woman shows a test tube to two men in a laboratory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688127)

Open image viewer

Finnish researchers are developing a new kind of corona vaccine. Photo: Lehtikuva

The development of a Finnish corona vaccine is progressing. Volunteer test subjects will receive the new corona vaccine still during this year.

The Finnish corona vaccine is easy to take. The vaccine does not need to be given with a needle. It is sprayed into the nose.

Tests of the new corona vaccine have been postponed because of new corona variants. The aim is to make the vaccine work also against new corona variants.

The aim is that the Finnish corona vaccine prevents corona disease. In addition, it is hoped that the new corona vaccine will also prevent the spread of the coronavirus.

## Summer jobs

![A large building under construction, with wind-protection boards and battens on the wall. Building materials in the yard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_159/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99183762f1159498927/1659967382)

For example, summer workers are needed in the construction sector. Photo: Soila Ojanen / Yle

There are many summer jobs for young people next summer. That is how the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment assesses it.

In many sectors there are too few employees. That is why it may be difficult for companies when employees go on summer holiday. Companies cannot cope without summer workers.

For example, there is a shortage of employees in service sectors. Summer workers are also needed in health care, in industry and in the construction sector.

## The weather

The weekend is mostly fair across the whole country, that is, no rain falls.

The sky is cloudy, but in the west and north the sun may shine.

The temperatures are below freezing across the whole country. In Lapland on Saturday there is over 10 degrees of frost.
