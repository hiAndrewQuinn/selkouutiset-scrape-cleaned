## The upper secondary school autumn term

![Woman studying in a classroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11356609)

Open image viewer

Upper secondary schools aim to start the autumn term with face-to-face teaching. Photo: Kalle Purhonen / Yle

The new academic year for upper secondary schools starts in August. According to current information, the new academic year will start in the usual way, with face-to-face teaching.

The National Agency for Education, however, reminds people that teaching may change if the coronavirus situation requires it. Changes can come quickly, and they may concern only individual schools. For this reason the National Agency for Education recommends that students and parents follow their own school's website and other communication channels closely.

At the start of the academic year, upper secondary schools will look at what the students' situation is after last spring's distance teaching. Students will be offered, for example, review courses and extra tuition as needed.

Vocational institutions will also begin the new academic year with face-to-face teaching in schools and workplaces.

## Coronavirus in the world

![A corona test being done.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11454946)

Open image viewer

In Los Angeles, USA, free coronavirus tests were done last week. Photo: Mario Tama / AFP

The coronavirus is still spreading quickly around the world.

The World Health Organization, WHO, says that a record number of new corona infections were recorded yesterday. There were about 260,000 infections.

In total, over 14 million coronavirus infections have been found in the whole world.

The Johns Hopkins University corona tracker says that over 600,000 people have already died from the coronavirus. The most corona deaths have been in the USA, Brazil and Britain.

## Buy-to-let flats

![Kitchen of a flat in an apartment block.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10354066)

Open image viewer

Kitchen of an apartment block flat. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

Buy-to-let flats interest Finns.

Nordea and the OP banks say that in the first part of the year they have granted more loans for buy-to-let flats than in the same period last year.

However, flat prices in university cities have risen quickly. That is why a buy-to-let flat is no longer a sure route to good returns, the banks say.

## Fire in Varkaus

![The fire that broke out on Saturday in the wood chip piles at Stora Enso's factory in Varkaus was still smoking on Sunday morning, 19 July 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11455596)

Open image viewer

A lot of smoke has spread from the Stora Enso fire in Varkaus. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

A big fire has been put out in Varkaus in North Savo over the weekend.

The fire broke out on Saturday morning at the factory of the forestry company Stora Enso. The wood chip piles in the factory area caught fire, and a lot of smoke has spread from them. Nobody was injured in the fire.

Extinguishing the fire may last until tomorrow.
