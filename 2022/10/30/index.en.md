The Helsinki District Court has remanded a senior official in custody. The District Court has remanded the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment's government counsellor **Olli Sorainen** in custody. Police suspect that Sorainen may have taken bribes and misused his position as an official in the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment.

Police suspect that Thai berry pickers have become victims of human trafficking. Government counsellor Sorainen was remanded in custody because police suspect that Sorainen has committed a crime.

Police say that with the help of bribes the berry-picking company has gained a lot of benefit in matters with the authorities.

## Household debt

![Banknotes](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5052,h_2841,x_0,y_55/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-6322657ebb3b8731d5/1475064799)

Open image viewer

Interest rates and costs are rising. Large debt can be a risk.

The debt of Finnish households has grown. Families with children have the most debt. They have bought a flat in a growth centre, for example in a big city.

Households have more debt than ever before. Economic expert **Hanna Putkuri** says that large debt is a risk, because interest rates and other costs are rising. Hanna Putkuri is an adviser at the Bank of Finland. Putkuri says that households must prepare for the growth of interest rates and other expenses.

Many banks are preparing for requests for repayment holidays to increase. A repayment holiday means that you can get a postponement of the payment of a loan instalment.

## Accident in a crush in South Korea

![Ambulances and rescue workers are visible on the street scene.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5107,h_2867,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1027026635e137731fe5/1667109974)

Open image viewer

More than 150 people died in a crush in Seoul, the capital of South Korea. Photo: Jung Yeon-Je / AFP

A serious accident has happened in South Korea. More than 150 people died in a crush at an outdoor party in the capital, Seoul, yesterday. About 100,000 people gathered on the streets of a popular bar area to celebrate Halloween.

The accident happened when people fell on top of other people on a narrow street, which is on a slope. Many people on the street began to fall on top of other people downhill. People ended up under other people. Many of the dead were teenagers or young adults.

South Korea's president has ordered that other Halloween party venues must be checked so that new accidents do not happen.
