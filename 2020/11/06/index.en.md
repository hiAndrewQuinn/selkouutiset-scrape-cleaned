# Friday 6.11.2020 (radio)

The US is waiting anxiously for the election result. Face masks are blocking drains. Coronavirus infections in Finland. Svenska dagen. The weather is unusually warm.

Vote counting in the US presidential election has continued. The mood in the USA has been tense, because the situation in the election has been very even.

President **Donald Trump** has again called the election fraudulent. Trump said that **Joe Biden**'s supporters are trying to steal the election victory.

Trump's claims about election fraud are seen as lies. No election fraud has come to light in the USA.

## Face masks block drains

![A toilet bowl.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8954546)

Open image viewer

Face masks or cleaning wipes must not be flushed down the toilet. Photo: Julia Sieppi / Yle

The use of face masks is causing environmental problems. People have thrown face masks into toilets, for example, and drains have become blocked.

Blockages in drains have also been caused by disinfecting cleaning wipes, because they have been thrown into toilets. For example, in Kouvola a maintenance man has had to open the drainage pipes of a health station 3 times already, because face masks have blocked the pipes.

Face masks or cleaning wipes must not be thrown into drains.

## Svenska dagen (Swedish Day)

![Pippi Longstocking soft toy in the Lahti city library.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10114413)

Open image viewer

At the library, Swedishness Day was marked, for example, with books about Pippi Longstocking. Photo: Emilia Malin / Yle

Swedish Day was celebrated on Friday. Because of the coronavirus situation, traditional events were not held this year.

Svenska dagen, i.e. Swedishness Day, celebrates the fact that Finland's Swedish speakers may use Swedish in Finland, which is their mother tongue.

## Weather

![The weekend is sunny across almost the whole country.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11635458)

Open image viewer

The weather is quite warm at the weekend and in many areas sunny. Photo: Yle

In Finland it has been very warm on Friday. In Åland the temperature was almost 17 degrees. In South-West Finland the temperature was about 13 degrees. Normally in November it is much colder.

On Saturday it is mostly dry, i.e. no rain falls, and the sun also shines.

The temperature is about 10 degrees. In Lapland it is cooler, and snow and sleet fall.
