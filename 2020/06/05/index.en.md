# Friday 5.6.2020 (radio)

Finance Minister Katri Kulmuni is resigning from the post. No new corona deaths have been reported in Finland. The number of corona deaths is growing in Brazil. Female genital mutilation may have decreased in Finland. HSL is adding traffic to Suomenlinna in the summer.

## Minister Kulmuni resigns

![Katri Kulmuni at a press briefing on 5 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11387857)

The Finnish finance minister, that is, the money minister, is changing. The current finance minister, Katri Kulmuni, is resigning from the post.

Kulmuni is resigning because of a row. Many people have criticised Kulmuni, because she bought public speaking training for herself at the state's expense.

In the training, Kulmuni practised giving speeches, for example. The training has also been useful to Kulmuni in party politics.

The training cost 50,000 euros. Kulmuni said earlier that she will pay the training money back to the state.

## No new corona deaths

No new corona deaths have been reported in Finland. The Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, said on Friday that the number of corona deaths has not grown.

So far, the coronavirus has killed 322 people in Finland.

30 new coronavirus infections have been found. In total, just under 7,000 Finns have caught the coronavirus infection.

## The corona situation is bad in Brazil

![Cemetery for corona victims in Manaus, Brazil, photographed on 2 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11383959)

In South America, in Brazil, the number of corona deaths is growing quickly. Already over 34,000 Brazilians have died because of the coronavirus.

Only in the USA and Britain have more people died of corona than in Brazil.

## Female genital mutilation is decreasing

![Graphic of types of female genital cutting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10521528)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics, FGM

Female genital mutilation, that is, circumcision, may have decreased in Finland. This is what the Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, says.

THL carried out a survey of girls in upper secondary schools and vocational education institutions. 0.2 per cent of girls said that they had been circumcised. Girls with a Somali background talked about circumcision more often than others.

In circumcision, a girl's genitals are damaged or removed. Genital mutilation is a crime in Finland. Finland has also banned taking girls abroad for mutilation.

## Suomenlinna transport links improve

![Suomenlinna ferry at the Kauppatori waterfront in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10298823)

Open image viewer

You can get to Suomenlinna by ferry from the Kauppatori market square in Helsinki. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The Helsinki Regional Transport authority, HSL, is adding traffic to Suomenlinna. HSL's ferries to Suomenlinna have shorter intervals in the summer than usual.

The ferries take about 150 passengers. That is only 30 per cent of the usual number of passengers. HSL wants in this way to make sure that passengers can keep safety distances.
