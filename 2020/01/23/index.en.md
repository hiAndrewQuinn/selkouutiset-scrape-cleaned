# Thursday 23.1.2020 (radio)

Finland has prepared for infectious diseases. A city of millions in China has been isolated because of the coronavirus. The Prosecutor General asks Parliament for permission to charge MP Juha Mäenpää. The forest helps people's minds.

## Finland is prepared for viruses and diseases

![A Malaysian health official checks passengers with a thermal scanner.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11168980)

A Malaysian health official checked passengers with a thermal scanner at Kuala Lumpur International Airport on Tuesday. Photo: Ahmad Yusni / EPA

Finland has been prepared for a long time for diseases that are infectious, that is, that spread to people. The matter is in the news now, because a new disease is spreading in China. The cause of the disease is a new kind of coronavirus. It causes symptoms similar to pneumonia.

In China about 600 people have already been infected. About 20 people have died. The situation, however, is changing all the time.

HUS, that is, the Helsinki and Uusimaa Hospital District, has had guidelines on infectious diseases for over 10 years. The guidelines also cover virus threats that are not very likely. The coronavirus is such a virus. It may never come to Finland, but it must be prepared for.

## Wuhan city has been isolated because of the coronavirus

![Authorities disinfect Hanko railway station in Wuhan, China, on 22 January.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11171344)

Open image viewer

Authorities disinfect Hanko railway station in Wuhan, China, on 22 January. Photo: AFP

In China the million-strong city of Wuhan has been almost completely isolated. Wuhan is possibly the starting point of the new coronavirus. Air traffic, train traffic, the metro and bus traffic have been stopped.

The authorities have banned the city's residents from leaving without an important reason. Residents are also obliged to use face masks in public places.

11 million people live in Wuhan. 2 other million-strong cities have also been isolated.

The coronavirus has also been found in some other Asian countries and in the USA. In addition, 2 cases that may be the coronavirus have been confirmed in St Petersburg in Russia.

## MP Mäenpää may face charges

![Juha Mäenpää](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11172123)

Open image viewer

Juha Mäenpää, MP for the Finns Party. Photo: Parliament

The Prosecutor General is asking Parliament for permission to charge MP Juha Mäenpää. Charging means that punishment for Mäenpää can be demanded.

Mäenpää is an MP for the Finns Party. The Prosecutor General needs Parliament's permission for the charge.

The police suspect Mäenpää of incitement against a population group. Mäenpää compared asylum seekers and invasive species in Parliament. An invasive species is a plant or animal that is not a native species in a certain area.

## The forest does the mind good

![Researcher Tytti Pasanen drinks tea from a cup in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6338945e283aeb90e26/1579695022)

Tytti Pasanen's doctoral research supports earlier research results that moving about in nature helps mental wellbeing. Photo: Anne Savin / Yle

The forest is good for people's minds. Regular movement in nature helps, says researcher Tytti Pasanen.

It was already known earlier that spending time outdoors in nature lowers the heart rate and blood pressure, and eases stress and improves mood.

Researcher Pasanen says that nature also gives people wellbeing that lasts a long time. Nature is a big help when a person is very stressed.
