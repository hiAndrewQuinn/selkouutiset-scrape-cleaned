# Monday 22.2.2021 (radio)

A new communicable diseases act came into force. Coronavirus infections are increasing. Some people are ill for a long time. Drivers' rest times are being broken. The second-level application period begins.

## Communicable diseases act

![People in a gym](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-776915603356d030ae8/1613977382)

For example, gyms can be closed if the coronavirus situation is bad. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

A new communicable diseases act has come into force in Finland. Authorities in different areas can now close, for example, private sports facilities, gyms and spas if the coronavirus situation is bad.

Authorities can also close shopping centre lounge areas and limit the number of passengers on trains and buses.

Stricter gathering restrictions came into force in Helsinki and Uusimaa today, on Monday. Public events and public meetings with more than 6 people are now banned in Helsinki and Uusimaa for 3 weeks.

## Coronavirus infections

On Monday, 390 new coronavirus infections have been reported in Finland. There are 7 new coronavirus deaths.

In 2 weeks, over 6,000 coronavirus infections have been found. That is about 1,000 more infections than in the 2 weeks before that.

170 coronavirus patients are in hospital.

## Long coronavirus symptoms

![A woman lying under a blanket on the sofa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11391258)

Open image viewer

For some people, coronavirus illness can last a long time after the actual illness. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

The coronavirus can cause a long-lasting illness. Doctors have found that a coronavirus patient can be ill for even several months.

There are perhaps thousands of Finns with long-term coronavirus symptoms. The problem is that the symptoms of long coronavirus illness are not always recognised.

It is not yet known why the coronavirus illness can last a long time after the actual illness.

## Drivers' rest-time violations

![Lorries on a snowy road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7507175fcb670d0d0c0/1607165838)

Open image viewer

Transport has increased. For example, the growth of online shopping increases transport. Photo: AOP

Violations of driving and rest times in heavy traffic have increased. For example, transport for online shopping has increased a lot.

Because of the coronavirus situation, drivers have to stay off work whenever they have flu symptoms. In that case, for example, a driver who may not have rested enough after the previous drive may end up driving a lorry.

The police monitor drivers' rest times. There have been more rest-time violations than before.

## Compulsory joint application

![Students in a lecture hall at a vocational school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7293501)

Open image viewer

Students in a lecture hall at a vocational school. Photo: Yle

The joint application for second-level schools starts tomorrow, on Tuesday. Pupils whose basic education ends this spring must apply for second-level education, for example to upper secondary school.

The obligation to apply is a new thing. It came into force this year.

The application period for second-level education lasts until 23 March.
