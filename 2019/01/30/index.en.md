# Wednesday 30.1.2019 (radio)

The opposition parties are tabling a motion of no confidence on care services. Wärtsilä is cutting employees. The EU does not want to change the Brexit agreement. Fewer refugees and migrants came across the Mediterranean than before. Trains have been running late. More snow has fallen.

**The opposition parties are tabling a motion of no confidence on care companies**

![Parliament House](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9881327)

Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

All the opposition parties are together tabling a motion of no confidence that concerns the care of the elderly. The opposition parties demand that the supervision of elderly care be made more effective. The parties also demand an addition to the law that sets a minimum level for the number of carers. The opposition parties are submitting the motion of no confidence because there are many problems in elderly care places. The motion of no confidence will be put forward in Parliament on Friday. The government has two weeks to respond to the motion of no confidence.

**Wärtsilä is firing 150 employees in Finland**

![Wärtsilä logo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9882350)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ilmari Herranen / AOP

The large Finnish company Wärtsilä plans to cut 150 employees in Finland. Wärtsilä makes, for example, engines for ships. The company is closing a total of 1,200 jobs in different countries. Wärtsilä has 19,300 employees in 80 different countries. In Finland, the most employees are being cut in Vaasa. Wärtsilä says that it wants to save 100 million euros every year.

**The EU no longer wants to change the Brexit agreement**

![British Prime Minister Theresa May at Prime Minister's Questions on Wednesday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10621969)

Open image viewer

British Prime Minister Theresa May at Prime Minister's Questions on Wednesday.  Photo: British Parliament

Britain wants to negotiate the Brexit agreement with the EU once more. The British Parliament yesterday approved a proposal that British Prime Minister Theresa May must negotiate with the EU about changing the Brexit agreement. The EU no longer wants to negotiate the Brexit agreement with Britain. The EU countries consider it possible that Britain's exit date will be moved later. Right now, Britain's exit date is 29 March.

**Fewer refugees and migrants came across the Mediterranean**

![Migrants in a rubber boat at sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10488161)

Open image viewer

A rubber boat packed to the edge with migrants heading from Africa to Europe was rescued in the Mediterranean in January 2018. Photo: AOP

Fewer refugees and migrants came to Europe across the Mediterranean last year than before. A report by the UN refugee agency UNHCR says that more than 139,000 migrants or refugees came to Europe across the Mediterranean in 2018. In 2017 there were more than 172,000 of them. In 2018, almost 2,300 people died or disappeared in the Mediterranean.

**Trains have been running late**

![Train in snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10618338)

Trains have been very late because of the weather. Several long-distance trains are up to one hour behind schedule. Short-distance trains are also much delayed. The train company VR says that the reasons for the delays are the weather and technical faults.

**More snow has fallen**

A lot of snow has fallen today too. Between Wednesday and Thursday night the snowfall moves west and away from Finland. Because of the snow, driving conditions are poor across the whole country.
