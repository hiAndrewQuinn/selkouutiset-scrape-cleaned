# Tuesday 18.12.2018 (radio)

The dismissal law has been approved. Economic growth is slowing. Nature is doing badly. New armoured vehicles for the police. Agreement reached on car emission cuts. The weather stays wintry.

## The dismissal law has been approved

![Parliament building](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10556058)

Open image viewer

Many trade unions opposed the dismissal law. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Parliament has approved the new dismissal law.

Small firms can dismiss an employee more easily than before. The law does not mention the size of firms. Many trade unions opposed the law, which makes dismissals easier.

The government says that the law helps jobs to be created in small firms.

## Economic growth is slowing

![Ships being loaded at the Vuosaari harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10548216)

Open image viewer

Finland's exports are pulling. If exports slow down, it affects Finland's economy. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The Bank of Finland forecasts that economic growth will slow a little next year. The Bank of Finland forecasts that economic growth will also slow the year after that.

Finland's exports abroad have grown. Exports may slow down. The Bank of Finland estimates that about 60,000 new jobs will be created in Finland this year.

## The state of nature is getting worse

![Heath carpet moth (Chlorissa viridata)](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8933374)

Open image viewer

There are about 400 habitat types in Finland. Photo: Timo Leponiemi / Yle

Finland's nature is doing badly. Habitat types are threatened by the renewal of forests, forest management, the building of fields and the eutrophication of waters. Habitat types are, for example, forest or meadow. The state of habitat types has been getting worse for 10 years already. There are about 400 habitat types in Finland.

## New armoured vehicles for the police

![Armoured vehicles acquired for police use were presented at the premises of the Helsinki Police Department on 18 December 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10560442)

Open image viewer

The police will acquire 15 bulletproof Mercedes-Benz cars during this year and next year. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

New armoured vehicles are coming to the police. The police are buying 15 bulletproof armoured vehicles. The armoured vehicles will come into use at all police departments. There are 11 police departments in Finland.

The police can use the armoured vehicles, for example, at public events and in emergency tasks.

## Agreement reached on car emission cuts

![Cars at a crossroads in winter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5497497)

Open image viewer

Traffic produces a lot of emissions. Photo: YLE / Tiina Kokko

An agreement has been reached in the EU on car emissions. The emissions of new cars must fall a lot by 2030.

Car factories must build cars that produce less pollution. Car emission targets have been negotiated for a long time.
