# Monday 27.2.2023 (radio)

A strike may begin in bus traffic. The strike at ports continues. Free support for mental health is offered in North Savo. Passports and identity cards are being renewed. Northern lights can be seen in the night sky. The weather.

## Bus strike

![People waiting for a bus in snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_101/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105244963a556c587943/1671780355)

A strike threatens bus traffic.

The strike may begin the day after tomorrow, on Wednesday, and it may last until next week's Friday.

If the strike begins, it will be seen especially in city local transport. The strike will stop at least some local buses, for example in Helsinki, Tampere and Jyväskylä.

The labour dispute in bus traffic has been mediated today. Mediation will continue tomorrow.

## Dock workers' strike

![Sunny Vuosaari harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_66/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107314863ecc16f7ad38/1676460582)

Open image viewer

Vuosaari harbour in Helsinki. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The dock workers' strike in Finland's ports continues.

The labour dispute was mediated yesterday for 9 hours, but no new compromise proposal has yet been given. National Conciliator Anu Sajavaara says that, however, progress has been made in the negotiations.

The dock workers have now been on strike for almost 2 weeks. Because of the strike, most of Finland's foreign trade has stopped.

## Support for mental health

![A mobile phone in hand. On the screen, the app of the Mieliteko programme.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_289/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107710163f848dd1d91c/1677216025)

Open image viewer

In North Savo a trial is being tested to support mental health with a mobile app and an online survey. Photo: Antti-Petteri Karhunen / Yle

A trial is starting in North Savo, which aims to support people's mental health.

All adults can get free help for improving their mental health for a month. The service includes a mobile app and an online survey.

The trial is offered by the Mieliteko development programme, whose aim is to promote people's mental health and reduce the use of intoxicants.

## Passports are being renewed

![Passport and identity card.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3990,h_2244,x_8,y_206/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107834463fc6a263cb2b/1677490453)

Open image viewer

A new kind of passport and a new kind of identity card. Photo: Police

The appearance of Finland's passports and identity cards is being renewed.

The illustrations of the new passports and identity cards show nature of the Archipelago Sea. The cover of the passport is renewed a little, and on the new identity card the face photo is bigger than before.

New kinds of passports and identity cards will begin to be issued in two weeks, that is, from 13 March.

Old passports and identity cards are valid as usual also after the renewal.

## Northern lights in the night sky

![The Moon and the northern lights shine over the sky of Prästkulla in Raasepori on Sunday evening.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2949,h_1656,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107833463fc650a6da76/1677586819)

Open image viewer

The Moon and the northern lights shone over Raasepori's sky on Sunday evening. Photo: Viveka Rabehl / Raasepori

A lot of northern lights have been seen in the sky during the last night.

The astronomical association Ursa says that it received 30 observations of northern lights. The observations were made during the evening and night south of Kajaani. Northern lights were seen last night, for example, in Helsinki, says the Finnish Meteorological Institute.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that also next night, that is, the night between Monday and Tuesday, there is a good chance to see northern lights.

Northern lights are created when solar wind hits the Earth's atmosphere.
