# Thursday 10.10.2019 (radio)

Turkey attacked Syria. A man shot people in Germany. A court sentenced an MP over a Facebook post. Posti is adding more parcel lockers. More vegetarian food is wanted in upper secondary schools and vocational schools.

## Turkey attacked Syria

![Civilians flee the Turkish offensive in northern Syria.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_264/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5984075d9ec992883a3/1570696614)

Turkey has attacked northern Syria. The attack began yesterday. Turkey wants there to be an area in northern Syria where there are no Kurdish soldiers. Turkey thinks that the Kurds are terrorists. Many tens of thousands of people have fled from the area. Kurds living in Finland held a demonstration in Helsinki today against the attack.

## A man shot people in Germany

![Police stand next to a wall. Some are climbing a ladder over the wall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_69/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5984575d9eddb7bfcc2/1570692625)

In Germany, two people died and many were injured when a man attacked a Jewish synagogue. The shooting happened in the city of Halle. The police arrested the man. The man posted a video on the internet that he had filmed while he was attacking. Earlier, a text opposing Jews had been posted on the internet. The man is suspected of having written it.

## A court sentenced an MP over a Facebook post

![MP Sebastian Tynkkynen, Finns Party, in the plenary session](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5613,h_3157,x_0,y_289/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5906485d7a1ca983a0e/1570709443)

Open image viewer

Photo: Pekka Tynell / YLE

A court has convicted an MP of the Finns Party of inciting hatred against a group of people. The court ordered MP Sebastian Tynkkynen to pay a fine of about 4,000 euros. In 2016, Tynkkynen published a post and a picture on Facebook. The court says that in the picture and the text, Tynkkynen claimed that all Muslims are terrorists. The court also sentenced Tynkkynen for the same reason in 2017.

## Posti is adding more parcel lockers

![General view](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_419/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4412995a01a3f6285d2/1510057056)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mikko Koski/Yle

Posti says that many more parcel lockers are coming. There are now 1,600 parcel lockers. In three years' time there will already be 4,000. Posti wants that in cities, every home should be at most 3 kilometres from the nearest parcel locker. Elsewhere in Finland, every home should be at most 5 kilometres from the nearest parcel locker. With a parcel locker, a person can send and collect parcels by themselves.

## More vegetarian food is wanted in upper secondary and vocational schools

![Vegetarian and meat dishes. Both have an equally good amount of protein.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-7127958529b86454e0/1481808878)

Open image viewer

Photo: Helena Lappeteläinen

Upper secondary schools and vocational schools have been given a new food recommendation. The recommendation has been published by the National Agency for Education and the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare. They want pupils to be offered vegetarian food every day at school, in addition to ordinary food. The food must also be healthy. The effect of the food on the climate and the environment must be as small as possible.
