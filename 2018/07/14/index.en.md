# Saturday 14.7.2018 (radio)

Trump is coming on Sunday. The police caught the stabbers. The people rescued in Thailand will soon go home. Football is played in a bog. The warm weather continues.

## The Trumps are coming to Finland on Sunday

US President Donald Trump and his wife Melania are coming to Finland tomorrow, Sunday.

They will come to Finland in the evening.

The day after tomorrow, on Monday, the US President and Russian President Vladimir Putin will meet in Helsinki.

Finnish President Sauli Niinistö will also meet them.

## The police caught the knife men

![Police car in front of a restaurant in central Joensuu on 13.7.2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10302541)

Open image viewer

2 men stabbed other people in a restaurant in Joensuu. Photo: Jouki Väinämö / Yle

In Joensuu in Eastern Finland, the police have caught 2 men who stabbed people in a restaurant.

The police searched for the men for the whole of yesterday. The police found the men because the men had stolen a car. The police knew where to look for the car.

The men stabbed 3 people in the restaurant. Everyone was seriously injured.

## The boys and their coach will soon go home

![Rescued boys in hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10299959)

Open image viewer

The boys were rescued from the cave. They have been in hospital since then. Photo: Thai government communications unit

The boys rescued in Thailand will soon be able to leave hospital.

Thailand's health minister says that the boys and their coach will be able to go home next Thursday. The boys have been in hospital since they were brought out of the cave.

The boys were in the cave with their coach for 2 weeks. They are doing quite well.

The Thai authorities are trying to protect the boys from publicity.

## Football is played on a bog

![Bog football World Championships in Hyrynsalmi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10304673)

Open image viewer

A bog is a damp area in the forest. A whole person can sink into a bog. Photo: Kimmo Brandt / EPA

There are unusual events in Finland in the summer.

Today there is an event where football is played on a bog. A bog is a very damp area in the forest. A whole person can sink into a bog.

165 teams from many European countries are playing football on the bog.

## The weather stays warm

![An old barn in a summery field landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10304217)

Open image viewer

Finland is now very warm. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Tomorrow, Sunday, it will be 30 degrees in many places. The day after tomorrow, on Monday, it will be even warmer. Then the weather could be tropical in Finland too.

The weather will stay warm all next week.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns of oppressive heat. The heat can be oppressive for the elderly, children and people with heart disease.
