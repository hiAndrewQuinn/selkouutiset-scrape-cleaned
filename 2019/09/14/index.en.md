# Saturday 14.9.2019 (radio)

A drone attack hit an oil refinery in Saudi Arabia. A new storm is expected in the Bahamas. In Spain, 5 people have died in floods. Just over 9,000 foreigners got Finnish citizenship last year. Bird autumn migration is now busy. Teemu Pukki was chosen as the Premier League player of the month.

## Drone attack

![An oil field in the dusk. A large flame rises from a drilling tower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10782536)

The Aramco oil field in the east of Saudi Arabia. Photo: Ali Haider / EPA

Two oil refineries in Saudi Arabia have been hit in attacks. The attacks were carried out with drones, that is, unmanned aircraft.

The Houthi army of Yemen says that they are behind the attacks. The Houthis say that they attacked Saudi targets with 10 drones in total.

The attacks were carried out on the production plants of Aramco, the Saudi state oil company. The attacks also started fires at the plants.

## Storms in the Bahamas

![In the city of Freeport in the Bahamas, damage caused by Hurricane Dorian was being cleared up on 8 September 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10971146)

In the Bahamas, damage caused by Hurricane Dorian is still being cleared up. Photo: Cristobal Herrera / Epa

A new tropical storm is approaching the Bahamas.

Storm Humberto is approaching the Bahamas, and it could still strengthen into a hurricane, says the US National Hurricane Center.

The last time a hurricane hit the Bahamas was almost 2 weeks ago. At least about 50 people died in Storm Dorian. The number of dead may rise, because about 1,300 people are still missing.

## Floods in Spain

![In Alicante, eastern Spain, cars were under floodwater because of heavy rain and the strong floods it caused, on 12 September 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10969655)

Open image viewer

In Alicante in Spain, cars were under floodwater. Photo: Epa / Morell

In Spain, at least 5 people have died in floods. The floods are caused by heavy rain in the east and south of Spain in the last few days.

About 3,500 people have been evacuated from their homes. Because of the floods, roads and bridges have collapsed. The floodwater has also swept away cars, for example.

## Finnish citizenship

![A Finnish and European Union passport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9442254)

Open image viewer

A Finnish citizen has a Finnish passport. Photo: Timo Jaakonaho / Lehtikuva

Even fewer people got Finnish citizenship last year.

About 9,200 foreigners got Finnish citizenship last year. That is about 25 percent fewer than the year before, according to Statistics Finland.

The year 2017 was a record year. Then about 12,200 foreigners got Finnish citizenship.

Last year, most Finnish citizenships were granted to Russians, Somalis, Iraqis and Estonians.

## Heading south

![Common cranes are impressive when they fly in a large flock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9158182)

Open image viewer

A flock of cranes. Photo: Antti Haavisto/Yle

Bird autumn migration is now busy.

The bird organisation BirdLife Finland says that the migration of cranes, for example, will soon start.

Cranes have already gathered in large flocks. In Muhos, near Oulu, for example, more than 10,000 cranes are waiting for good migration weather.

Many small birds are also setting off. In the mornings you can see many chaffinches and redwings, for example, starting their journey.

## Player of the month

![Teemu Pukki in the picture](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10927009)

Open image viewer

Teemu Pukki is a forward for Norwich. Photo: AOP

The Finnish footballer Teemu Pukki has been chosen as the Premier League player of the month in England.

Teemu Pukki was chosen as the best player in the English Premier League for August. Pukki scored 5 goals in Premier League matches in August.

Teemu Pukki is playing his first season in the Premier League. He plays as a forward for the Norwich team.
