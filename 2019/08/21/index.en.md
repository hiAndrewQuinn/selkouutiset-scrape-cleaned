# Wednesday 21.8.2019 (radio)

Russian President Vladimir Putin is visiting Finland. Putin's visit has been seen in central Helsinki. Selling train tickets for cash will end on long-distance trains. President Trump is not visiting Denmark, because Denmark does not want to sell Greenland to the USA.

## Putin in Finland

![President Vladimir Putin writes in the guest book of the Presidential Palace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10932433)

Presidents Putin and Niinistö in Helsinki. Photo: Alexei Druzhinin / EPA

Russian President Vladimir Putin is visiting Finland. Putin arrived in Finland on Wednesday afternoon.

President Sauli Niinistö received Putin in Helsinki. The presidents have been talking at the Presidential Palace.

The presidents' evening continues at Suomenlinna, where they will have dinner.

## Visit in the street scene

![People waiting for Putin's arrival.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10932064)

Open image viewer

Putin's visit interested people in Helsinki. Photo: Laura Hyyti / Yle

Russian President Putin's visit has been seen in central Helsinki. For example, Helsinki's Market Square has been closed all day. The Market Square is next to the Presidential Palace.

A large group of police officers has made sure that Putin's visit goes smoothly. Helsinki police have also received help from elsewhere in Finland. During Putin's visit there are as many police from elsewhere in Finland in Helsinki as there are from Helsinki itself.

There have also been demonstrations against Putin in Helsinki. The demonstrations have been small, with only a few people taking part. The theme of the demonstrations was Russia's poor human rights.

## Train ticket sales

![Conductor getting onto a train carriage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10644179)

Open image viewer

You will still be able to buy a ticket from the conductor in future, but only with a card. Photo: Roger Källman / Yle

Selling long-distance train tickets for cash will end. From the start of September, passengers can buy tickets from the conductor only with a payment card.

Most passengers buy their ticket before the journey. Passengers can buy train tickets, for example, online, from machines and at R-kiosks.

VR says that 99 per cent of passengers buy a train ticket before the journey or pay for it by card on the train.

## Trump and Denmark

![President Donald Trump.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10932229)

Open image viewer

President Trump wanted to buy Greenland. Photo: Andrew Harrer / EPA

US President Donald Trump has cancelled his visit to Denmark. Trump intended to visit Denmark at the beginning of September.

President Trump is not travelling to Denmark, because Denmark does not want to sell Greenland to the USA. Trump said a little while ago that the USA wants to buy Greenland.

Greenland is the largest island in the world. Greenland belongs to Denmark, but Greenland has self-government, that is, it decides many matters itself.

Most of Greenland is covered by ice. Climate change, however, is melting Greenland's ice cover. Under the ice, land is revealed, where there are, for example, expensive minerals.
