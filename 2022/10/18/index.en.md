The Estonian parliament, the Riigikogu, condemns Russia's illegal annexations of territory and says that Russia is a terrorist state.

## Work and the psyche

![Construction worker at a building site](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3993,h_2243,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10104956329b6e79cb80/1663678645)

Open image viewer

For a person with a mental health problem, work is not always a burden but often a resource. Photo: Kare Lehtonen / Yle

Working can help with psychiatric illnesses. A long sick leave does not always improve well-being of the mind.

The Finnish Mental Health association says that the role of work in mental health is not understood well enough. Work may not necessarily be a burden but a resource for many people.

Work can help even in the middle of serious mental health problems. It is good if working conditions can be changed, for example by shortening the working week.

Psychologist Sari Nuikki says that work meets three basic psychological needs of a person. These are competence, community and autonomy, that is, the chance to decide on your own matters.

![Haso flats being built on Vinsentinkatu in Sompasaari.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5177,h_2913,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1008077632200de79ae1/1663173195)

Most young people still dream of owning a home, a Danske Bank survey says.

36 percent of 18 to 40-year-olds are considering buying a first home. Of the rest, 85 percent would like to own a home one day.

40 percent say that a flat is both a home and an investment.

## Kari Tikka

![Artistic director of the Urkuyö ja aaria festival, conductor Kari Tikka.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5835189)

Open image viewer

Artistic director of the Urkuyö ja aaria festival, conductor Kari Tikka. Photo: Urkuyö &amp; aaria

Finnish composer, conductor and oboist Kari Tikka has died. Tikka died in August of a long-term illness. Tikka was 76 years old.

Tikka composed 5 operas in total. He also composed choral and solo songs, as well as chamber and orchestral music.

Tikka's works are often based on religious texts. His best-known work is Armolaulu.

As a conductor, Tikka worked, among other places, at the Finnish National Opera, the Radio Symphony Orchestra and the Royal Swedish Opera.

## Solar eclipse

![Solar eclipse in progress.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2104,h_1184,x_0,y_122/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81836660c0c5920bc93/1623246307)

Open image viewer

A partial solar eclipse photographed in São Paulo, Brazil, in December 2020. Photo: Sebastiao Moreira / EPA

The sun will be partly eclipsed across the whole country next Tuesday, 25 October.

The eclipse will be visible in Finland most in the east and north, and least in the west and south.

This year's eclipse will not be total anywhere. At its largest the eclipse is in Siberia, where 86 percent of the Sun will be covered by the Moon.

You can follow the course of the eclipse anywhere, as long as the sky is clear enough.
