## A Finnish name brings work

![Akhlaq Ahmad ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_574/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5991985da07418eaa56/1720016175)

Researcher Akhlaq Ahmad was surprised by how much a name affects job hunting. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

In Finland, it is easier to find a job if the applicant has a Finnish name. This is what a new Finnish study says.

The study was done by University of Helsinki researcher Akhlaq Ahmad. He sent 5000 job applications with names from different languages. The applicants' names were made up, they were not real.

The applications were written so that the applicants were equally good. All of them also spoke Finnish well.

The differences were big, Ahmad says. For example, it is much harder to get a job with the name Abdirashid Mohamed than with the name Aino Hämäläinen.

In the study, companies invited the most applicants to a job interview who had a Finnish name. Out of 1000 applicants, almost 400 applicants got an invitation.

Applicants got fewer invitations to a job interview if they had an Iraqi or Somali name. Out of 1000 applicants, 134 applicants got an invitation if the names were Iraqi.

The fewest invitations went to Somali applicants. Out of 1000 applicants, only 99 applicants got an invitation if the names were Somali.

Researcher Akhlaq Ahmad is surprised that the differences between the groups were so big.

## Stora Enso's jobs

![](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5239386)

Open image viewer

Stora Enso's mill in Oulu. Photo: YLE

The forestry company Stora Enso plans to cut employees.

Stora Enso will perhaps cut 135 employees in Oulu. Employees were also cut at the Oulu mill last spring.

Stora Enso makes paper at the Oulu mill. Fewer workers are needed because the mill's operations are changing.

The mill has 2 paper machines. Stora Enso will start making packaging paper on the second paper machine and will close the other paper machine. That is why jobs will decrease.

Stora Enso is making changes at the Oulu mill because the price of packaging paper in the world has risen, but the price of other paper has fallen.

## The deaths on the Silja Europa

![Tallink Silja Europa arriving from Tallinn at the Helsinki Western Terminal on Sunday 20 October. Estonian police found two dead young adults on the ship in the Port of Tallinn on Sunday morning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11028702)

Open image viewer

The deaths on the Tallink Silja Europa were not crimes. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

The Estonian police will not start investigating the deaths on the Silja Europa ship. The deaths happened at the weekend.

The Estonian police do not suspect a crime. There were no signs of violence on the dead.

The dead were a 25-year-old woman and a 21-year-old man. An autopsy will still be carried out on them, so that the cause of death will be found out.
