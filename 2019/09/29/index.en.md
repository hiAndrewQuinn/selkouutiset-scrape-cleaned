# Sunday 29.9.2019 (radio)

EU countries are defending the principle of the rule of law. There are shortcomings in the treatment of osteoporosis. Illegal taxis were caught in Oulu. Jacques Chirac was remembered in Paris. The first snow fell in northern Finland.

## Rinne: EU countries defend the rule of law

![Prime Minister Antti Rinne prepared for the live broadcast of Yle Radio Suomi's Prime Minister interview hour at Kesäranta in Helsinki on 29 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10996122)

Prime Minister Antti Rinne spoke about this on Yle's Prime Minister interview programme on Sunday. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

EU countries are defending the rule of law together. Prime Minister **Antti Rinne** says that the EU countries have agreed that EU funding will be linked to the rule of law. A country must follow the EU's rule of law principle so that it can receive EU funding.

The rule of law principle means that the law must be followed in a country and people must be treated well. The state must not interfere with the work of the media, that is, radio, television or newspapers.

Many EU countries say that Poland and Hungary may not be following the EU's rule of law principle.

## There are shortcomings in treating osteoporosis

![Measuring osteoporosis, that is, bone loss.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5861041)

Open image viewer

Bone loss, that is, osteoporosis, is measured here. Photo: Yle

There are shortcomings in the treatment of osteoporosis, that is, bone loss. Many people who have osteoporosis have not received enough guidance on treating the illness. Patients lack information about suitable food and exercise. Not all osteoporosis patients have been given instructions on how to guard against falls. A care plan has not been made for all patients.

Osteoporosis is a disease that makes bones weak. The bones of an osteoporosis patient can break easily. About 400,000 Finns have osteoporosis.

## Illegal taxis were caught in Oulu

![Raindrops on a windscreen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10670890)

Open image viewer

The police checked taxis in Oulu at the weekend. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

The police have found many illegal taxis in Oulu. The police checked taxis in Oulu at the weekend. The police say that not all drivers had a traffic permit for the car or a taxi driver's licence. Some taxis had not registered the car for taxi use.

The police say that dozens of illegal taxis drive every week in the Oulu area. Illegal taxis do not pay taxes and other charges.

A memorial service has been held in France for former president **Jacques Chirac**. Chirac died on Thursday. He was 86 years old. A large memorial service was held in Paris on Sunday afternoon.

Jacques Chirac will be buried on Monday at the Montparnasse Cemetery in Paris. Jacques Chirac was President of France from 1995 to 2007.

## First snow fell in northern Lapland

![First snow at Nuorgam.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10995979)

Open image viewer

First snow at Nuorgam in a road weather camera picture. Photo: Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency

First snow has fallen in northern Lapland. In Utsjoki there was 2 centimetres (cm) of snow on the ground in the morning. Meteorologists say that the end of September is a normal time for the first snow to fall in northern Lapland. In southern Finland the first snow falls much later, often only in November.
