President Sauli Niinistö wants to reduce visas for Russians. Russians need a visa if they travel to Finland.

In Niinistö's opinion, for example, buying a flat or a summer cottage in Finland is not a good reason for a visa. Nowadays Russians get a visa more easily if they own a flat in Finland.

![Memorial picture of Queen Elizabeth in central London.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5858,h_3288,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1005257631a34eca761c/1662663429)

In London there is a long queue for Queen Elizabeth's coffin. Many people want to pay their respects to the queen, who died last week.

On Thursday during the day the queue for the queen's coffin was 6 kilometres long. People are being let in to the coffin until Monday morning.

Queen Elizabeth's funeral is on Monday afternoon.

## Sweden's prime minister

![Magdalena Andersson at her press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8191,h_4608,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-100809363221a29e6347/1663179350)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson resigns. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

Sweden's prime minister Magdalena Andersson has resigned. However, Andersson will carry out the duties of prime minister until Sweden gets a new prime minister and government.

Magdalena Andersson is a Social Democrat. She leads the Swedish left, which lost the election last weekend.

The winner of the election is the right, which is led by the Moderate Party's Ulf Kristersson. Kristersson is now trying to put together a new government in Sweden.

If Kristersson succeeds in the task, he will become Sweden's new prime minister.

## Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership

![NATO flag](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3355,h_1882,x_0,y_64/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-954676627e360ccfded/1652438601)

Open image viewer

The flag of the NATO defence alliance. Photo: Eetu-Mikko Pietarinen / Yle

Greece has approved Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership. Greece's parliament approved on Thursday that Finland and Sweden will join the NATO defence alliance.

Now 25 NATO countries have approved Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership. There are 30 NATO countries.

Finland and Sweden will become NATO members when all the old NATO countries have approved them.

Finland and Sweden decided to apply for NATO membership when Russia attacked Ukraine.

Finland and Sweden estimate that their security will improve in NATO. NATO countries help each other if they are attacked.

## Finnair flight

![Finnair aircraft. Helsinki-Vantaa airport. 26.3.2021](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3041,h_1711,x_269,y_326/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-788978605de8d2622a5/1625800481)

Finnair aircraft. Illustrative image. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The Finnair flight from Helsinki to Málaga in Spain did not go according to plan on Thursday morning.

The plane was supposed to land in Paris on the way. The reason was a passenger who behaved badly.

In Paris the passenger was removed from the plane. After that the journey continued towards Málaga.

Iltalehti reported on the matter.
