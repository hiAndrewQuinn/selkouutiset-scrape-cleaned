# Friday 27.9.2019 (radio)

Young people have protested today because of climate change. Europe's native tree species are in danger. The athletics world championships have started in Doha, Qatar. The car ferry Estonia sank 25 years ago.

## The climate has been defended around the world

![A huge crowd of people.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10993329)

Climate strikers at a demonstration. Photo: Massimo Percossi / EPA-EFE

Young people have protested today because of climate change. The international climate week has ended with a climate strike.

There have been climate strikes in about 20 places in Finland. In Helsinki, for example, schoolchildren were given permission to strike and be away from school.

Young people demand more ambitious climate policy from the government. The Finnish government's goal is to be carbon neutral by 2035. Carbon neutral means that only as much carbon emissions are released into the atmosphere as can be bound from the atmosphere. Forests, for example, bind carbon.

However, the government does not yet know how the cuts will be carried out.

Elsewhere in the world, millions of young people took part in the climate strike and demonstrations. They have sent pictures of protests and slogans to social media.

The climate strikers have demanded action from decision-makers. Young people have reminded them that there is no time to waste.

## Many European trees are in danger

![In Kurikka, hidden in the soil layers, lies a unique groundwater deposit. It has been studied for ten years already, and perhaps just as much research still lies ahead.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10957170)

Trees are threatened by logging and urbanisation, for example. Photo: Pasi Takkunen / Yle

Europe's native tree species are in danger. More than half of the species are at risk of becoming extinct. Extinction means that a species disappears for good.

The International Union for Conservation of Nature warns of this.

Pests, large-scale logging of forests and urbanisation are the causes of the loss.

66 tree species in Europe are critically endangered, that is, very close to extinction. For example, the horse chestnut is on the list of endangered trees.

## Athletics world championships began in Doha

The athletics world championships have started in Doha, Qatar.

Today, Friday, there have been heats and qualifiers in many sports. 8 Finnish athletes are taking part on the opening day.

The world championships start with the women's marathon at midnight, Finnish time. Finland's Anne-Mari Hyryläinen and Alisa Vainio will run in the marathon.

The championships end on 6 October.

## 25 years since the Estonia sank

![Wreck of the Estonia](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10987100)

Open image viewer

The car ferry Estonia sank in a violent autumn storm 25 years ago. Photo: Yle

Next night it will be 25 years since the passenger ship Estonia sank.

The Estonia was on its way from Tallinn to Stockholm in the autumn of 1994. There was a violent storm at sea then. 852 people died in the sinking. Only 137 people survived. Most of the dead were Swedish and Estonian.

The accident was caused by the bow visor, that is, the bow door, coming loose in the storm.

The Estonia was very significant for the Republic of Estonia, which had only just become independent from the Soviet Union in the autumn of 1991.

Juha Korsi, director of maritime affairs at the Finnish Transport Agency Traficom, says that the safety of car ferries was greatly improved after the Estonia accident.
