# Tuesday 10.7.2018 (radio)

In Thailand, rescuers have brought all 12 boys out of the cave. Iraq does not want Finland to send asylum seekers back by force. The berry crop in the forests will be better in the south than in the north this year.

## The boys were rescued in Thailand

![Rescue unit leader Narongsak Osottanakorn presents the rescue plan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10296406)

The rescue work in Thailand has had a happy ending. The rescuers have now brought all 12 boys out of the cave.

The last 4 boys got out of the cave on Tuesday. The boys' coach was also rescued on Tuesday. The boys and the coach belong to the same football team.

It all started on 23 June. At that time the boys and the coach went into the Tham Luang cave in Northern Thailand. They went into the cave because it was raining outside.

However, rainwater filled part of the cave so quickly that the boys could no longer get out of the cave. The rescuers found the boys on 2 July.

After that, food and medicine could be taken to the boys. However, the boys could not get out of the cave, because moving in the cave was difficult and dangerous.

The rescuers had to dive long distances, because part of the cave was full of water. Diving was difficult, and one diver even died.

In the end the boys were brought out of the cave so that each boy was accompanied by 2 divers. The distance in the cave was more than 3 kilometres long.

Rescuers from abroad also took part in the rescue operation. A Finnish diver was also there.

The whole world followed the rescue of the boys.

## Iraq does not accept forced returns

![An undocumented asylum seeker sits on a bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9539344)

Open image viewer

Finland has returned asylum seekers to Iraq. Photo: Carolus Manninen / Yle

Iraq does not want Finland to return asylum seekers by force. If Finland tries to take asylum seekers back to Iraq against their will, Iraq will not accept them.

Iraq only lets in those asylum seekers who are coming back voluntarily.

Finland's view is that Iraqis must return to Iraq if they do not get asylum in Finland. However, forced returns of Iraqis cannot be carried out now, because Iraq does not accept forced returns.

## Normal berry crop in the south

![Close-up of a bilberry.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7398851)

Open image viewer

Bilberries will soon ripen. Photo: Jarkko Remahl / Yle

The berry crop in the forests will be better in the south than in the north this year. This is the estimate of the research institute called the Natural Resources Institute.

The Natural Resources Institute says that quite a normal amount of bilberries and lingonberries will be picked. In the south, bilberry and lingonberry are flowering well, and the berries will soon ripen.

Cloudberry also flowered well. However, not many cloudberries will be picked, because there were frosty nights in June. Then there was frost near the ground, and that reduces the cloudberry crop.
