## Most popular presidential candidates

Pekka Haavisto is the most popular person for president in Yle's poll. Photo: Jarmo Kuusinen / AOP, Silja Viitala / Yle, graphics: Nanna Särkkä / Yle

Yle has asked Finns who they will support as the next president. The most popular person for president is the Greens' Pekka Haavisto. 28 percent of Finns support Haavisto.

The second most popular person for president is Bank of Finland governor Olli Rehn. 15 percent of Finns support Rehn.

The third most popular person for president is the director of the Finnish Institute of International Affairs, Mika Aaltola. 13 percent of Finns support Aaltola.

The support for all three, that is Haavisto, Rehn and Aaltola, has grown since Yle's previous poll.

## Air Force plane crash

![The rusted body of a Hawk in a forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_625/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1114649646362e0031e5/1684501555)

Open image viewer

Hawk training aircraft was destroyed when it fell to the ground. Photo: Elias Harinen / Yle

Finland's army had an air accident on Monday. An Air Force Hawk training aircraft fell in Keuruu. There was a student pilot and a flight instructor on board. They were saved by ejection seats. The Hawk aircraft was destroyed when it fell.

Investigators have started investigating the accident. The investigators said on Tuesday that the engine of the plane stopped before the plane fell. The engine will be taken for examination.

The plane's flight recorder has also been found. It will help to clarify why the Air Force's Hawk fighter fell in Keuruu.

## Ukraine

![Storm Shadow missile.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2982,h_1677,x_0,y_312/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5052425b7ff6f2cecad/1535113006)

Britain is giving Ukraine Storm Shadow missiles. Photo: AOP

Russia has fired many missiles at Ukraine's capital, Kyiv. Ukraine says that Russia fired 18 missiles at Kyiv last night. Russia fired missiles from many directions.

Ukraine says that the Ukrainian army shot down all the missiles. That is why apparently nobody was injured or killed. Ukraine says that the Ukrainian army also shot down 6 drones that Russia sent to Kyiv.
Also on Tuesday during the day, Ukraine has shot down Russian missiles.

Russia does not like that Britain is giving Ukraine long-range cruise missiles. Russia threatens to carry out more strikes if Britain gives missiles to Ukraine.

## Finnair's baggage rules

![Suitcases being packed into an aircraft.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_497/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9630326296333dc75d8/1654010793)

Open image viewer

Finnair wants bags to be put in the hold. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

The airline Finnair is tightening its baggage rules. Finnair wants to reduce hand luggage in the aircraft cabin.

In future, the cheapest flight ticket includes only a small bag that fits under the seat. Shop purchases at the airport shops must also fit under the seat.

The changes apply to flight tickets bought from the start of June.
