# Radio Clear News Wednesday 16.8.2023

Alexander Stubb to become the National Coalition Party's presidential candidate. SDP asked Jutta Urpilainen to be a candidate. Commuter train tickets get more expensive. Lost property offices ordered to stop operating. Weather forecast.

## Stubb to become the National Coalition Party's presidential candidate

![Alexander Stubb speaks into a microphone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3984,h_2241,x_0,y_415/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115718864dc76a854042/1692169979)

Alexander Stubb is going to be the National Coalition Party's presidential candidate.

Stubb has been chair of the National Coalition Party, and for example prime minister and foreign minister. At the moment he is a professor at the European University Institute in Florence.

At a press conference Stubb said that Russia's attack on Ukraine had a significant effect on his decision to stand as a candidate.

Stubb is worried about the internal situation in Finland. He stressed the importance of freedom of the press and a respectful culture of debate.

If he becomes president, Stubb intends to work in close cooperation with the government and Parliament.

The National Coalition Party's party congress will confirm the party's choice of presidential candidate later.

SDP chair Sanna Marin has asked EU Commissioner Jutta Urpilainen to be a presidential candidate. The Social Democrats were gathered at the summer meeting of the parliamentary group.

Urpilainen says that she is considering the candidacy. Urpilainen will decide on the matter by November.

The SDP's presidential candidate will be chosen by the party council during the rest of the year.

## Commuter train ticket prices

![A commuter train carriage and seats.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_498/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89868161d45ce774bc4/1641308312)

Open image viewer

Commuter train tickets will rise by about 5 percent. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

The railway company VR is raising the prices of single and season tickets for commuter traffic from 28 August.

The prices of single and season tickets for commuter traffic will rise by just under 5 percent on average, that is, by 50 cents.

However, the prices from Helsinki to Hyvinkää, Järvenpää and Mäntsälä will stay the same.

VR says that the reasons for the price increase are inflation, that is, the general rise in prices, and the rise in the price of energy.

## Lost property offices

![Finnish Lost Property Service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_128/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6097735dc549714224b/1573491985)

Open image viewer

Lost property offices will be closed for a month from 12 September. Photo: Rehnman Kaj / Yle

The operation of the Finnish Lost Property Service has been banned for a month.

The Regional State Administrative Agency for Western and Inland Finland says that the lost property office has repeatedly and seriously broken the Lost Property Act.

For example, the office has added value added tax to the storage of lost property. In addition, the office has charged a special fee for handling lost property, against the law.

The lost property office's ban on operating begins on 12 September and lasts for a month. All its branches are closed during this time.
