# Sunday 5.1.2020 (radio)

6 people died in a car accident in Italy. Finland was 4th at the Ice Hockey World Junior Championships. Popular first names change with the times. Epiphany is celebrated on Monday.

## The accident in Italy

![Map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11145175)

Open image viewer

The accident happened in Lutago in northern Italy. Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

There has been a serious car accident in Italy. 6 people died in the accident. 11 people were injured.

The accident happened in northern Italy, near ski resorts. The people who died were young German tourists.

The car drove into the young people in the night, when they came out of a bus. The bus had brought the young people from a restaurant to a hotel.

The driver of the car was drunk. He is 28 years old. The police have arrested the driver.

## Ice hockey World Juniors

![Joonas Oden](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11142685)

Open image viewer

Finland's young ice hockey players played well in the Czech Republic, but they went without a medal. Photo: SJL

Finland has finished in 4th place at the Ice Hockey World Junior Championships. In the junior world championships, the players are men under 20 years old.

Finland lost the bronze medal game to Sweden. Finland scored 2 goals in the game, but Sweden scored 3 goals. So the bronze medal went to Sweden, and Finland was fourth in the championship.

Finland has usually done well in junior ice hockey. In the previous Ice Hockey World Junior Championships, Finland was the best country, that is, the world champion.

The Ice Hockey World Junior Championships are held every year. This time the championships were in the Czech Republic.

## Popular first names

![Minna Saarelma-Paukkala](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9332621)

Open image viewer

Minna Saarelma-Paukkala is the director of the University's Almanac Office. Photo: Ilkka Klemola / Yle

Popular first names change with the times. In the 2010s, many boys in Finland were given the name Elias, and many girls were given the name Sofia.

Minna Saarelma-Paukkala from the University of Helsinki says that many names are now popular in many European countries at the same time. For example, Sofia is now a common name all over Europe.

Often old names come back into fashion about every 80 or 100 years, Saarelma-Paukkala says. That is why she believes the names that were popular in the 1940s will soon come back into fashion. Such names are, for example, Reino and Marja.

Many names about nature are also in fashion now, Saarelma-Paukkala says. Such names are, for example, the girl's name Lumi and the boy's name Otso, which means bear.

## Epiphany

![Christmas will also end in time. Pine needles and a Christmas bauble on the floor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10908653)

Open image viewer

On Epiphany it is time to take the Christmas tree away. Photo: Yle / Jouni Tanninen

Epiphany is celebrated on Monday. Epiphany is the last of the Christmas holidays. Epiphany is always celebrated on 6 January.

On Epiphany, many Finns take the Christmas tree away and put the Christmas decorations back in the cupboard to wait for next Christmas.

Health centres, libraries and offices are usually closed on Epiphany. Buses run on Epiphany according to the Sunday timetables.
