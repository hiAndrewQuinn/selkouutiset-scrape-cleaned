## Rising prices

Central banks have raised interest rates so that inflation slows down. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

The rise in prices, that is, inflation, has sped up further. In January inflation was 8.4 percent. In February inflation was already 8.8 percent, so inflation grew by 0.4 percentage points.

The biggest rises have been in the price of electricity and in the interest on housing loans and other loans.

Central banks have raised interest rates. The aim is to slow inflation. Now the raising of interest rates has increased inflation, but the aim is that inflation slows down.

## Finland's Nato membership

![Ulf Kristersson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6894,h_3859,x_0,y_143/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106339963d017ef9a960/1674582044)

Open image viewer

Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson. Photo: Pelle T. Nilsson / AOP

Swedish Prime Minister **Ulf Kristersson** says that Finland may join Nato before Sweden. Kristersson says that Turkey has made it clear that Turkey is ready to approve Finland's Nato application before Sweden's Nato application.

Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson spoke about the matter to the media on Tuesday in Stockholm.

Kristersson also said that it is not certain, however, that Finland and Sweden will join Nato at different times.

## Street gangs

![Two police cars in a courtyard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7403345faa787050ffe/1613413115)

Open image viewer

Street gangs are in Helsinki, Turku, Tampere and Oulu. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

The Helsinki police are increasing monitoring of street gangs. Two new special groups are starting in the Helsinki police, whose task is to combat street gang crime.

One group combats the crime of street gangs. The other group combats the formation of new street gangs. The group dismantles street gangs and helps young people to get out of street gang activity.

The police know of about 10 street gangs. There are about 200 members in street gangs. In addition to the Helsinki region, there are street gangs in Turku, Tampere and Oulu.

## Abortions

![A person holds a pregnancy test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6476335e57a5ccc6d69/1664439737)

Open image viewer

Pregnancy test. Photo: Aleksandr Davydov / Mostphotos

Fewer abortions, that is, terminations of pregnancy, are carried out in Finland than in other Nordic countries. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare has published statistics for 2022.

In Finland fewer than 7 people per 1,000 inhabitants had an abortion. In Sweden about 15 people per 1,000 inhabitants had an abortion. Abortions have decreased in recent years in all the Nordic countries.

The year before last, a total of 53,000 abortions were carried out in Finland, Sweden, Norway and Iceland.
