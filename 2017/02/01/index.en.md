# Wednesday 1.2.2017 (radio)

Ombudsman: Sipilä acted correctly in the Terrafame matter. Finns sleep less than before. Organic milk for schools. Using a mobile phone in EU countries is getting cheaper. Yle's voting advice application is open to candidates, later in the spring to voters.

## Ombudsman: Sipilä acted correctly

![Juha Sipilä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9419788)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä did nothing wrong in the Terrafame matter. This is what the Parliamentary Ombudsman says.

In November the government gave 100 million euros to the Terrafame mine so that the mine can continue operating. Prime Minister Sipilä's relatives have business with Terrafame, and some Finns suspected that the Prime Minister wanted to help his relatives' company. They asked the Ombudsman to investigate the matter.

The Ombudsman says that the company of Sipilä's relatives got no benefit from Terrafame's money.

## Many people sleep too little

![a woman looks at a phone in bed](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9433164)

Open image viewer

Sometimes sleep just does not come. Photo: Lenni Kuivalainen / Yle

Finns sleep 7 hours a night. That is an hour less than before.

Researchers say that many people are tired in the morning because they sleep too little. Women are more tired than men, and city people are more tired than country people.

Too little sleep is unhealthy. It can lead, for example, to diabetes, or sugar disease, or dementia, a memory disease.

## Organic milk for schoolchildren

![Cows outside](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9379509)

Open image viewer

An organic cow gets to go outside even in winter.  Photo: Anu Hämäläinen

Politicians want schools to drink more organic milk.

The government will start paying a subsidy for organic milk, so that organic milk is no longer more expensive for schools than ordinary milk.

In general too, the state wants Finns to eat more organic food of all kinds. Food is organic when it has been grown naturally. It means, for example, that cows have enough space inside the cowshed and they can go outside in winter too.

## Calls from one EU country to another are getting cheaper

![Young people looking at their mobile phones.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9305940)

Open image viewer

In the summer, using a mobile phone between EU countries will get cheaper. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Using a mobile phone in another EU country will get cheaper next summer.

Today, calls and internet use are more expensive abroad than in your home country. EU countries have now agreed that there is no need to pay an extra charge within the union.

Phone companies say that the change may raise domestic prices in Finland.

## Yle's voting advice application is open to candidates

![The election gallery app that candidates fill in, on a phone screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9434916)

Open image viewer

First the candidates answer the questions of Yle's voting advice application.  Photo: Yle

Yle is again helping Finns choose who to vote for in the elections. Help is given online by a "voting advice application".

In April there will be elections in which Finns choose the decision-makers of municipalities and cities. There are about 30,000 candidates. All candidates can answer questions made by Yle online and make a short video of themselves for Yle's website.

In about a month, voters can answer the same questions and see which candidate agrees with them.

Yle is making the voting advice application for the first time together with a few newspapers.
