## Russian elections

![Vladimir Putin in a video meeting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5010,h_2818,x_0,y_407/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-856256614898780f979/1632147685)

In Russia, the party of President **Vladimir Putin**, United Russia, has declared itself the winner of the elections. The party says that it has won a two-thirds majority in the parliamentary elections.

The United Russia party received about 50 per cent of the votes. The Communist Party received the second most votes.

Election observers say that there was a lot of electoral fraud in Russia's elections. For example, it was difficult for the opposition to get candidates into the election.

## Economic growth

![Lorries and containers at the port in Vuosaari, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_536/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82099160c857698e1bf/1623742367)

Lorries and containers at the port. Finland's exports are growing. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The Research Institute of the Finnish Economy, Etla, has made a forecast of Finland's economic growth. Etla forecasts that Finland's total output will grow by 3.5 per cent this year. Next year total output may grow by 3 per cent.

The economy is now growing fast after the corona crisis. The reason is that private consumption is growing.

Etla forecasts that Finland's exports will grow by 4 per cent this year and by 9 per cent next year. Exports are being boosted by the fact that exports of services are growing by as much as over 20 per cent.

## Corona vaccine for children

![A Moderna vaccine is drawn from a vial into a syringe.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-84061461138f31b243d/1642751923)

Open image viewer

The corona vaccine was safe in the study for 5–11-year-old children. Photo: Kristiina Lehto / Yle

Research on the corona vaccine for children has progressed well. The drug companies Pfizer and BioNTech say that their corona vaccine has now been tested on children.

The study says that the Pfizer and BioNTech vaccine is safe for 5–11-year-old children. In the study the vaccine gave the children strong protection against the coronavirus.

The drug companies Pfizer and BioNTech are now applying for permission for the vaccine to be given to children in Europe and the USA.

## Deaf Week

![Marko Vuoriheimo, who is also known by the stage name Signmark.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_313/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5728425d10d8d61280d/1561385310)

Open image viewer

Signmark, that is Marko Vuoriheimo, raps in sign language. Photo: Jarl Hanhikoski / Yle

This week International Deaf Week is being celebrated. On Thursday the UN's International Day of Sign Languages is celebrated.

In Finland the feast day of Deaf Week is Friday. Then the Deaf Week celebrations are held on the website of the Finnish Association of the Deaf.

The purpose of Deaf Week is to strengthen the sense of community among deaf people. The aim is also to support work that improves the position of deaf people everywhere in the world.
