# Friday 7.10.2016 (radio)

Russian military aircraft may have been in Finnish airspace. Colombian President Juan Manuel Santos wins the Nobel Peace Prize. The Centre Party and the SDP now have equal support. A storm has caused great damage in Haiti in the Caribbean Sea. The year-in-integration-training video blog continues.

## Foreign aircraft

![The Air Force posted on Twitter a picture of an SU-27 fighter suspected of violating airspace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9215705)

This aircraft may have been on the Finnish side. Photo: Defence Forces

Russian military aircraft may have been on the Finnish side. Finland suspects that this happened twice on Thursday. On both occasions the Finnish army took a photograph of the aircraft.

Russia says that Russian military aircraft have not flown in Finnish territory.

Finland is asking Russia for an explanation of the matter. Finland intends to investigate the matter carefully. Foreign Minister Timo Soini says that Finland does not accept military aircraft of another country being in Finnish territory.

## The Nobel Peace Prize

![Colombian President Juan Manuel Santos thanked everyone who took part in the peace mediation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9217391)

Open image viewer

Colombian President Juan Manuel Santos and the president's spouse. Photo: LEONARDO MUÑOZ / EPA

The winner of the Nobel Peace Prize has been chosen. The prize goes to Colombian President Juan Manuel Santos.

President Santos has done a lot of work so that the war in Colombia will end.

In South American Colombia there was a war for over 50 years. The Colombian government and the rebels fought against each other. They ended the war last summer.

The Nobel Committee decided to give the peace prize to President Santos, because it wants to support peace in Colombia. The committee says that the prize belongs to all Colombians.

## Party support

![Party support estimates](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9213491)

Open image viewer

The Centre Party and the SDP are equal in the new measurement. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

The Centre Party and the SDP, that is, the Social Democrats, now have equal support. In Yle's new measurement, the support of both parties is 20.5 per cent.

The Centre Party is in the government, and Prime Minister Juha Sipilä is the leader of the Centre Party. The SDP is not in the government; it is the biggest party in opposition.

The contest between the government and the opposition for support is tough. The Centre Party's support has fallen a little, and the SDP's support has risen a little.

The third biggest party in the poll is the National Coalition Party. Its support is about 17 per cent. After it come the Greens and the Finns Party.

## Storm in Haiti

![Hurricane Matthew caused great damage in Haiti. Picture from Les Cayes on 6.10. 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9215417)

Open image viewer

The Matthew storm caused a lot of damage in Haiti. Photo: EPA/Orlando Barria

The hurricane Matthew has caused great damage in Haiti in the Caribbean Sea. Hundreds of people died in the storm.

The storm also destroyed houses. For example, the city of Jeremie lost 80 per cent of its houses.

The storm is now moving towards the USA, where more than 2.5 million people have fled from the storm.
