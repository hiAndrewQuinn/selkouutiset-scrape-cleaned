# Thursday 17.10.2019 (radio)

The Brexit agreement is ready. Pasila's new station has opened. On the night between Thursday and Friday, the Night of the Homeless is held. Even light exercise is good for the heart, also for young people.

## Brexit

![Today is Brexit's day of fate. A close vote is expected in Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11025684)

The Brexit agreement is ready. The European Union and Britain finished the agreement on Thursday.

It is not yet certain whether the agreement will be approved. The agreement will enter into force if all EU countries approve it. It must also be approved in the European Parliament and in the British Parliament.

The British Parliament may not approve the Brexit agreement. Parliament will vote on the agreement on Saturday.

The hardest issue in the Brexit agreement has been the border between Ireland and Northern Ireland. After Brexit, the border will also be the border between the EU and Britain.

Brexit means Britain's leaving of the European Union. Brexit has been a long and difficult process. The British voted for Brexit already 3 years ago, but Britain is still part of the European Union. Prime Minister Johnson wants the departure to happen at the end of October, that is, in about 2 weeks.

## Tripla

![Opening of Tripla.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11024192)

Open image viewer

People queued for the new Tripla on Thursday morning. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Pasila's new station has opened. The opening party was held on Thursday.

More than 1000 trains stop at Pasila station every day, and 50,000 train passengers pass through the station every day.

The new shopping centre Tripla is also now open. Tripla is next to Pasila station.

Tripla is a big shopping centre. There are 250 shops in Tripla.

The construction of the whole Tripla area cost about 1.2 billion euros.

## Night of the homeless

![Markku Korhonen says that before becoming homeless, he worked as an entrepreneur.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10410847)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mari Jäntti / Yle

On the night between Thursday and Friday, the Night of the Homeless is held. A person is homeless if they do not have a home of their own.

There are fewer homeless people in Finland than before. Finland is the only EU country where the number of homeless people is falling.

Among the homeless are also young people and women. About 1000 people under the age of 25 are without a home. There are about 1200 homeless women.

There are Night of the Homeless events in various parts of Finland.

## Light exercise

![A skier gliding on one ski.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10696646)

Open image viewer

Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

Even light exercise is good for the heart. Light exercise is beneficial even for young people.

The University of Oulu studied more than 3000 young men. Researcher Jaakko Tornberg says that light regular exercise increased heart rate variability. That is a sign of good heart health.

Tornberg hopes that the study will inspire young people to start exercising as a hobby.
