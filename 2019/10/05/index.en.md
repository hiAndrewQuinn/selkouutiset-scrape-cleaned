# Saturday 5.10.2019 (radio)

Groundwater from Lahti is to be exported to China. The processing of applications at the Finnish Immigration Service has slowed down. The prime ministers of Finland and Sweden discussed Brexit. Finns trust Yle's news. A star walk for the music world has been opened in Tampere.

## Water from Lahti is exported to China

![Water runs into a glass.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10072855)

Open image viewer

Water from Lahti will soon be exported to the Chinese market. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Groundwater in Lahti is about to be exported to China. The export is due to start next summer.

A production plant where the water will be bottled is to be built in Lahti during the winter. The plant aims to bottle one million litres of water a day.

For now, water will be exported only to China. There is a large market there and a severe shortage of clean water.

The project costs 20 million euros. The financier was found in Singapore.

## Migri's processing of permits has slowed down

![A door of the Finnish Immigration Service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10908912)

Open image viewer

The processing of residence permits has slowed down. Photo: Lotta Sundström / Yle

Foreign experts' access to Finland has slowed down.

Statistics from the Finnish Immigration Service show that processing a first residence permit application takes 52 days on average. Last year, permit processing took about 25 days on average.

Permit processing has slowed down, for example, because of a shortage of staff.

However, almost all foreign special experts still get a residence permit for Finland.

## Finns trust Yle

![Graphic: How reliable do you find the news reporting of different media?](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11006342)

Open image viewer

Photo: STT, source: Kunnallisalan kehittämissäätiö 2019

Finns trust Yle's news the most.

The Municipal Sector Development Foundation asked Finns which media's news they trust the most.

More than 80 percent of Finns trust Yle's TV and radio news the most. Almost as many Finns trust the news of the Finnish News Agency STT.

A majority of Finns also trust the news of MTV3, Helsingin Sanomat and their own region's biggest newspaper.

By contrast, only under 30 percent of respondents trust the news of the tabloid newspapers.

## A star walk has been opened in Tampere

![Star tiles for Jean Sibelius and Katri Helena in Tampere.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11006073)

Open image viewer

Composer Jean Sibelius and Katri Helena received their own tiles on the star walk in Tampere. Photo: Päivi Solja / Yle

A star walk for the most prominent figures in Finland's music world has been opened in Tampere.

The star walk is on Åkerlundinkatu, near Tampere Hall. Prominent figures of the music world can get their own name and star on the pavement of the street.

For a start, 11 star tiles will be set into the street. Among those who get their own tile are composer Jean Sibelius, singer Katri Helena, rapper Cheek and the Tampere band Popeda.
