# Thursday 27.6.2019 (radio)

The Court of Appeal did not change Jari Aarnio's sentence. Finland will not bring Finnish women back from the Isis camp in Syria. Prime Minister Antti Rinne is taking part in the Helsinki Pride parade. There will be a good strawberry harvest this year. The heatwave in Europe continues.

## Aarnio's sentence

![Jari Aarnio at the hearing of the police leadership's official misconduct case at the Helsinki District Court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10850290)

Jari Aarnio is a former drugs police officer who committed drug crimes. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The Court of Appeal has given a ruling in Jari Aarnio's case. Aarnio is the former head of the Helsinki drugs police.

The Court of Appeal did not change the sentence that the lower district court earlier gave Aarnio. The sentence is 10 years in prison.

Aarnio received the sentence because he himself committed drug crimes. In the Court of Appeal's opinion the crimes were very serious, because Aarnio was a police officer and worked in a leadership position.

## The women of the Isis camp

![Finnish women from the ISIS camp in Syria.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10799396)

Open image viewer

There are also Finnish women at the Isis camp. Photo: Yle

Finland will not bring Finnish women back from the Isis camp in Syria. Prime Minister Antti Rinne told Parliament about this on Thursday.

About 10 Finnish women are at the al-Hol camp in Syria. The women have said that they want to return to Finland.

The al-Hol camp has many family members of Isis terrorists. The families were taken to the camp when the Isis terrorists lost their battles.

## Prime Minister and Pride

![Antti Rinne, chair of the SDP, elected as Prime Minister by Parliament, holds a press conference in the Parliament's state room in Helsinki after the prime ministerial election on 6 June 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10818649)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Antti Rinne. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Antti Rinne is taking part in the Helsinki Pride parade. The parade will be held on Saturday.

Rinne is the first Finnish prime minister to take part in the Pride parade.

The parade is part of Helsinki Pride week. Pride is an event for gay people and other sexual minorities.

Last year 100,000 people took part in the Pride parade. In Rinne's opinion, this shows that Finns want to defend human rights and equality.

In the Prime Minister's opinion, it is great that sexual minorities do not have to walk alone.

## Strawberry harvest

![A box of strawberries.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10849035)

Open image viewer

A 5-kilo box of strawberries costs about 35 euros. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

There will be a good strawberry harvest this year. Strawberry farmers say that a lot of strawberries are growing and the quality is good.

The farmers estimate that the price of a box of strawberries will also be about 35 euros this summer. A box holds 5 kilos of strawberries.

A large part of Finland's strawberries grow in North Savo. There the main strawberry harvest starts to ripen in about 2 weeks.

## The heat in Europe

![A man lies on a park bench.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10852098)

Open image viewer

It is 40 degrees in Spain. Photo: Juan Carlos Hidalgo / EPA

The heatwave in Europe continues. In France it was almost 40 degrees. For example, schools were closed because of the heat.

It is even hotter in Spain than in France. In Catalonia in Spain it may be as hot as 44 degrees on Friday. That is very bad news, because there is a large forest fire in Catalonia. Because of the heat, fire can spread further in the forest.
