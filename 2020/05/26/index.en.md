# Tuesday 26.5.2020 (radio)

The EU plans to support the economies of EU countries. In summer there are more day-care centres open than usual. Finland's corona situation has moved in a good direction. An explosion happened in an Espoo flat. Tuesday was a warm day.

## The European Union

![Flags of EU member states flew in front of the parliament in Strasbourg, France, on 2 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10955652)

The European Union plans to support the economy of the EU area. The economies of EU countries have suffered because of the corona crisis.

The support package will be announced tomorrow. It is estimated that the support package is hundreds of billions of euros.

A dispute may arise between EU countries over whether the support is a loan or a gift. Some EU countries demand that all countries must pay the support money back. Some EU countries already have so much debt that it is difficult for them to take on even more debt.

## Day-care centres in summer

![Day-care children play in a school classroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6311775e1eceea20e75/1579077408)

Open image viewer

Many day-care centres are open this summer. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

Here in Finland, more day-care centres are open this summer than usual. The reason is the corona situation.

Many parents' holidays have been moved because of the corona crisis. Grandparents also cannot look after the children, because the elderly must be careful about the coronavirus.

For example, in Oulu half of the day-care centres are open in the summer. That is more than in summer in general.

In Oulu, it is estimated that 40 per cent of children need day care in the summer. In a normal summer, only 20 or 30 per cent of children need care.

It is also estimated that in other big cities, more day care is needed in the summer than usual.

## Finland's corona situation

![Taneli Puumalainen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11173952)

Open image viewer

Chief physician Taneli Puumalainen, THL. Photo: Rami Moilanen / Yle

Finland's corona situation has moved in a good direction. There are not many new corona infections, because the restrictions have worked well.

In total, about 6,600 people in Finland have caught the coronavirus, and 312 people have died because of corona.

The chief physician of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, Taneli Puumalainen, says that in many hospital districts no new infection has been found during the past week.

## The explosion in Espoo

![Police and a police car in Mäkkylä, Espoo, on Monday evening shortly before midnight on 25 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11368145)

Open image viewer

Police are investigating an explosion in Mäkkylä, Espoo. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

An explosion happened in Espoo on Monday evening. The explosion happened in a flat, which the police went to check.

The explosive went off when the police opened the door of the flat. 2 police officers were injured.

The police found a man in the flat who had died. The man may have installed the explosive before he died.

The police say that the man had financial difficulties. The police do not believe that the matter is terrorism.

## Summer weather

Tuesday has been a warm day. The temperature has been over 20 degrees in different parts of Finland.

Wednesday is a cooler day than Tuesday. In the south and east the temperature will still rise to 20 degrees. However, a rain area is coming from the west, and in the rain the temperature is only 10–15 degrees.

Thursday is also a cool day, but on Friday the weather will start to warm up again.
