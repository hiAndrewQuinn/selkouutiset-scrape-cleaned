Asylum seekers and undocumented people are exploited in the labour market.

Yle's Swedish-language newsroom reports on four asylum seekers. The men worked for a Finnish employer for nine months, but did not get any pay at all. The men had signed an employment contract.

The asylum seekers have now returned to the reception centre. A criminal complaint has been made about the employer.

## A storm killed people in Moscow

In Moscow a powerful storm killed at least 14 people yesterday. Over 130 people were injured.

Several people were trapped under trees when they fell because of the strong wind. The wind also tore off the roofs of houses, cut power lines and disrupted traffic.

## The prosecutor is appealing the verdict on the Iraqi brothers

The prosecutor plans to appeal the verdict that freed the Iraqi brothers. They were accused of taking part in a mass murder.

The Pirkanmaa District Court decided last week that the brothers did not take part in the mass murder.

The prosecutor is appealing to the Court of Appeal. The prosecutor says that the brothers took part in the mass murder and demands that they be sentenced to life imprisonment.

The mass murder was committed at the Camp Speicher military base in Iraq in 2014.

Three men have come from Russia to Finland without permission.

The men crossed the border between Russia and Finland last night. They have applied for asylum in Finland.

The men are about 30 years old. It is not yet known which country they are citizens of.

## Many reports about fake police officers

![A person surfing the internet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8416145)

According to the police, a typical person who is cheated is willing to help and trusting. The victims are both women and men. Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

Fake police officers have already been reported to the police almost 200 times this year.

Fraudsters who pretend to be police officers phone elderly people and ask for their online banking codes.

If the fraudsters get the banking codes, they go into people's bank accounts and steal money from them.

The police remind people that they never ask for online banking codes by phone.

## Researchers want children to believe in themselves

Children believe that they learn new things easily only for about the first two years at school.

Pupils' trust in themselves weakens when they are in the third grade.

Finnish researchers are now developing a programme that helps children learn to trust themselves.

If the programme works, it may also be used in other schools and in teacher training.

The programme may also be taken abroad.
