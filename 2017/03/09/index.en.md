# Thursday 9.3.2017 (radio)

Rents have risen. Finnair is cancelling flights on Friday. The owner of a new phone number may get messages that were meant for the number's previous owner. New hotels are being built in Finland. The fashion magazine Vogue writes about Finnish pantsdrunk.

## Rents in Finland

![The inner courtyard of an apartment building](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9479941)

Open image viewer

The highest rents are in the Helsinki region. Photo: Terri Niemi / Yle

Rents have risen. In 2016 rents rose by over 2 percent.

Last year a tenant usually had to pay about 14 euros per square metre in rent.

In the Helsinki region rents are higher than elsewhere. Last year a tenant usually paid a little over 18 euros per square metre in rent if they lived in the Helsinki region.

## Flights are being cancelled

![An airline Finnair plane at Helsinki-Vantaa Airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9500187)

Open image viewer

Finnair is cancelling flights because of a strike. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Finnair is cancelling flights on Friday. Finnair is cancelling over 100 flights in Finland and Europe.

Finnair is cancelling flights because of a strike. On Friday the workers who handle baggage and security checks will stop work.

Because of the strike there will probably be congestion at Helsinki Airport on Friday.

## Wrong messages

![Text message](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8941443)

Open image viewer

A wrong message can be frightening. Photo: Yle

The owner of a new phone number sometimes has an unpleasant problem: They get wrong messages. The messages are meant for the number's previous owner.

Phone companies say that a phone number is out of use for at least 6 months before it is given to a new owner. But that is not always enough.

If a lot of wrong messages come, the customer can change the number for free.

## The new hotels

![Clarion hotel. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9498422)

Open image viewer

Clarion is one of Helsinki's new hotels. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

New hotels are being built in Finland.

Hotels are needed because tourism is growing in Finland. Tourists come, for example, from Russia and Asia.

In Finland a billion euros is being spent on building hotels and spas in the years 2015-2020.

Norwegians have also joined the Finnish hotel business. For example, Petter Stordalen is building hotels in Finland. He owns the Nordic Choice hotels, which are in different Nordic countries. He wants customers of Nordic Choice hotels to discover Finland too.

## Finnish pantsdrunk

![Emojis](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9499863)

Open image viewer

An emoji has also been made of pantsdrunk. Photo: Ministry for Foreign Affairs

The international fashion magazine Vogue is writing about Finland. The magazine has found an interesting trend in Finland. It is kalsarikännit, or pantsdrunk.

Pantsdrunk means that a person drinks alcohol alone at home wearing only their underwear. In Vogue's opinion pantsdrunk describes well what a weekend is like when there is nothing to do.

Vogue also writes that its pages had pictures of pantsdrunk already 40 years ago. In Vogue's old pictures beautiful models drink champagne at home in their underwear. Finns think that Vogue does not give quite the right picture of Finnish pantsdrunk.
