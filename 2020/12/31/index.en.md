# Thursday 31.12.2020 (radio)

New year. Brexit. Landslide in Norway. Road deaths.

## The year changes

![Sydney Harbour Bridge. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11721326)

Open image viewer

Midnight fireworks in the harbour of Sydney, Australia. Photo: EPA-EFE/DEAN LEWINS

The new year is being welcomed in different parts of the world.

The year 2021 began first in the island states of Samoa and Kiribati in the Pacific Ocean. The year will change last on the uninhabited islands of Howland and Baker, which belong to the USA.

This year the new year is being celebrated on home sofas, through television and computers. Because of corona and corona restrictions, big concerts and public events have been cancelled or they will be held without an audience.

In Finland too, cities have cancelled New Year's parties and fireworks.

However, in Helsinki a light art work will be lit up on the tower of the Olympic Stadium on New Year's Eve. The green laser beams of the artwork will flash the years 2020 and 2021 across the sky until morning.

## Brexit

![Union Jack umbrella and Big Ben in London in 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11719439)

Open image viewer

Britain's exit from the EU comes into force when the year changes. Photo: Justin Tallis / AFP

Today is the last day that Britain is a member of the EU. Britain's exit from the EU, Brexit, comes into force at midnight, when the year changes.

Britain actually left the EU already last February, but the transition period has lasted 11 months, until the turn of the year.

New opinion polls say that 56 percent of Britons think that leaving the EU was a mistake. On the other hand, 52 percent think that Britain should not join the EU again.

## Landslide in Norway

![Roofs of the Gjerdrum residential area, with the landslide area in the middle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11721058)

Open image viewer

In Norway there is fear that new landslides will come in Gjerdrum. Photo: Fredrik Hagen / EPA

In Norway rescue work continues after yesterday's landslide. The work is difficult, because the clay ground is unstable and new landslides are possible.

Sweden and Denmark have offered Norway help with the rescue work. Interior Minister Maria Ohisalo says that Finland is also ready to help Norway.

The landslide in the municipality of Gjerdrum near Oslo has swept away at least 30 homes. At least 10 people have been injured and hundreds of people have been evacuated. Several people are also missing.

## Road deaths

![A car that has crashed and is on its roof](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7440709)

Open image viewer

About 200 people died in traffic this year. Photo: Police

The same number of people have died on Finnish roads this year as last year. About 200 people died in road accidents.

However, this year about 10 percent fewer people have been injured than last year. Statistics Finland says that by the end of November about 500 people had been injured in traffic.

Liikenneturva says that during the corona year traffic on main roads has decreased by maybe about 10 percent from the previous year.
