# Thursday 6.4.2023 (radio)

Marin's government asked to resign. The Left Alliance is not interested in a government post. The election defeat cuts the party's income. Easter is celebrated at the weekend. Weather forecast.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin has submitted the government's request for resignation to President Sauli Niinistö. Niinistö accepted the government's resignation. Niinistö thanked Marin's government for cooperation in difficult years.

Marin's government asked to resign, because there were parliamentary elections in Finland on Sunday. The old government's work ends and a new government will be formed in Finland.

Sanna Marin's old government is now a caretaker government until a new government has been formed. The new government is being formed by National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo, because the National Coalition won the election.

## The Left Alliance's thoughts on a government post

![Li Andersson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_290/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10942876429ef3b42a15/1680470669)

Open image viewer

Left Alliance chairman Li Anderson. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The Left Alliance is not very interested in a government post. Left Alliance chairman Li Anderson says that the Left Alliance may not take part in the government negotiations.

The reason is that the Left Alliance disagrees with the National Coalition, which is forming the government, on many economic matters.

The National Coalition wants to save money from the state's economy, that is, cut spending. The Left Alliance does not like cuts.

## Party subsidy

![A deflated green balloon that says Centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2169,h_1220,x_0,y_723/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-109372464299e1cb14c2/1680449280)

Open image viewer

The Centre lost the most seats. That is why its party subsidy will fall the most. Photo: Marianne Mattila / Yle

The election defeat reduces parties' income a lot. Parties are paid a party subsidy for each MP. The party's income falls if the number of its MPs falls.

The most MPs were lost in the parliamentary elections by the Centre, the Greens and the Left Alliance. The Centre lost the most seats, 8. The Centre's party subsidy falls by about 1.4 million euros a year.

The fall in income may affect parties' activities. The Greens and the Left Alliance may start change negotiations. In change negotiations, decisions can be made, for example, on dismissals or on a reduction of work.

## Easter

The weekend is Easter. Friday is Good Friday. It is a public holiday. Also Easter Monday, the 2nd day of Easter, is a public holiday. That is why banks, offices and schools are closed for 4 days.

Also, for example, Alko's shops and many libraries are closed over the Easter weekend. Many grocery shops are also open at Easter.

Many Finns have 4 days off work. That is why many families travel, for example to ski centres, to spend the Easter holiday.
