# Friday 5.4.2019 (radio)

Britain's exit from the EU may be postponed. There are more women candidates in the parliamentary elections than before. Information about the elections is also available in plain language. Actor Lasse Pöysti has died.

## Brexit timetable

![Opponents of Brexit in London on 27 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10724544)

Open image viewer

This Briton opposes Brexit. Photo: Leal olivas / AFP

Britain's exit from the EU, that is, Brexit, may be postponed. British Prime Minister Theresa May asks the European Union for Brexit to be postponed until the end of June.

Britain intends to leave the European Union earlier if the British Parliament approves the withdrawal agreement. Prime Minister May hopes that Britain does not need to take part in the EU Parliament elections. The elections are held in all EU countries in May.

The other EU countries must accept that Britain is given more time to organise its exit from the EU. If Britain does not get more time, it must leave the EU as early as next week without an agreement. The economy of Britain and the whole of Europe will suffer if that happens.

## Women candidates in the elections

![election posters](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10710662)

Open image viewer

There are more men than women among the candidates. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

The Finnish parliamentary elections are approaching.

There are more women candidates in the elections than before. Now 42 percent of the candidates are women.

In the previous parliamentary elections 4 years ago about 39 percent of candidates were women. Then 83 women got into Parliament.

Parliament, that is, the Finnish parliament, has 200 members in total. In a week it will become clear how many women Parliament gets this time.

Election day is Sunday, 14 April.

The Clear News series "Nasib and immigrants" tells about election themes that interest immigrants. The series can be found on Yle Areena and on the Clear News website.

## Lasse Pöysti has died

![Lasse Pöysti as Olli in Suomisen Olli in 1941. Olli's sister Pipsa was played by Maire Suvanto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10724514)

Open image viewer

Lasse Pöysti (left) in 1941. Photo: Finnish Film Archive

Actor Lasse Pöysti has died. Pöysti was 92 years old.

Lasse Pöysti started acting almost 80 years ago. Pöysti was only a child when he acted in the Suominen family films.

Pöysti acted in many films, on television and in theatre. Pöysti last appeared in 2012. Then he acted at the Helsinki City Theatre.
