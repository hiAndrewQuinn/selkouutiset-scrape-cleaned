## Coronavirus vaccines

![Coronavirus vaccine by Pfizer-BioNTech.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4714,h_2652,x_0,y_390/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7637616006bc8cf1247/1611054443)

About 800,000 doses of coronavirus vaccine will come to Finland in June. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

A large batch of coronavirus vaccine is coming to Finland now in June. Vaccinations are progressing well. The National Institute for Health and Welfare says that after June about 80 percent of Finns aged at least 16 will have received the first vaccine dose.

All Finns aged at least 16 will get the first coronavirus vaccine by August at the latest.

Many Finns will already get the second vaccine dose, that is, the booster vaccine, from the June batch.

## Free education

![Students from the Swedish-speaking upper secondary school in Mustasaari visiting the upper secondary school in Suomussalmi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10496220)

Open image viewer

Upper secondary schools and vocational schools are second-level educational institutions. Study there is free from the autumn onwards. Photo: Tiia Korhonen / Yle

The new free education in upper secondary schools and vocational schools may increase the separation of schools. The Association of Finnish Local and Regional Authorities estimates that many young people want to go to the best schools. Schools for good students may perhaps form in big cities.

If school grades rise in popular cities, not all young people will get into an upper secondary school in their own city. Schools for weaker students may form outside the popular cities.

Studying in upper secondary schools and vocational schools becomes free in the autumn.

## Boating accident

![Motorboat that hit a rocky shore at Vessölandet in Porvoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2100,h_1181,x_0,y_394/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82317360cdb3558cc66/1624096166)

Open image viewer

The boat was going so fast that it slid into the forest. Photo: Border Guard

A serious boat accident happened in East Uusimaa in Porvoo on Saturday morning. A 6-metre-long motorboat hit the shore at Vessölandet in Porvoo.

The boat was going very fast. The boat slid from the shore into the forest. There were 3 men in the boat. They were seriously injured when the boat hit the shore.

The police say that the boat driver may have been drunk. The police are investigating the Porvoo boat accident as drunken boat steering and a water traffic offence.
