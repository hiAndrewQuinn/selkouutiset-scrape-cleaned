# Friday 28.10.2016 (radio)

People in Finland have been talking about the working methods of parliament. Efforts are being made to prevent school bullying. A new way to treat alcoholism has been developed in Finland. Winter time begins at the weekend.

## Speaking turns in parliament

![Antti Lindtman](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9257731)

Open image viewer

Antti Lindtman in parliament. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

People in Finland have been talking about the working methods of the Eduskunta, that is, the Finnish parliament. The news reported on the situation of the Social Democratic group in parliament. The group's chairman Antti Lindtman has tried to influence which of the group's MPs get speaking turns in parliament.

Lindtman has given the speakers of parliament lists of the Social Democratic MPs who in his opinion are suitable speakers. The speakers decide on speaking turns, because they lead the work of parliament.

In parliament it was said that Lindtman's lists do not fit parliament's working methods. Lindtman apologised, and the MPs of the Social Democratic group agreed that lists will no longer be made. The group also decided that Lindtman may continue as chairman of the group.

## A project against school bullying

![Children standing outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9107253)

Open image viewer

School bullying is always wrong. Photo: Antti Ojala / Yle

Efforts are being made to prevent school bullying.

In Southern Finland a group of schools has started a new project against school bullying.

The project includes, for example, the Swedish-speaking Billnäs school. Teacher Krista Halonen says that pupils must learn to notice how their own actions affect other people.

Pupil Lisa Rosenblad says that the pupils choose peer mediators. When pupils argue, a peer mediator helps to end the argument.

In the Billnäs school yard there is a buddy bench. Pupils who are lonely are fetched from the bench to join in the games.

## Treatment of alcoholism

![A man sits at a table with a beer bottle in front of him.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9164537)

Open image viewer

Alcoholism is a disease that must be treated. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

A new way to treat alcoholism has been developed in Finland.

The new treatment method combines medication and therapy. In addition, the patient can decide for himself whether he aims for total abstinence or moderate use of alcohol.

Usually alcoholism has been treated so that the person must aim for total abstinence, that is, he may no longer drink any alcohol at all.

In Finland thousands of patients have already been treated with the new Contral method. Now the method is also being taken to the USA and Canada.

## Winter time begins

![Clocks change to winter time at 4.00.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5444600)

Open image viewer

On Sunday morning at 4 o'clock the clocks are moved back one hour. Photo: Yle News Graphics

Winter time begins at the weekend. Winter time, that is, standard time, begins on the night between Saturday and Sunday. Then the clocks are moved back one hour.

Winter time begins in all European Union countries on the last Sunday of October. Summer time starts again on the last Sunday of March.

Winter time and summer time have been in use in Finland for 35 years already.
