# Thursday 26.8.2021 (radio)

An explosion has happened in Kabul. There are fewer new coronavirus infections than before. Finns travelled a lot within Finland in the summer. The weather is sunny tomorrow.

## Explosion in Kabul

![Aerial photo of Kabul.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8467946127a2a02593c/1629987529)

An explosion has happened near Kabul airport in Afghanistan. The explosion was big. The explosion was probably a terror attack.

Many people died in the explosion. The Ministry of Defence says that no Finns died or were injured.

Several countries warned earlier that terror attacks were being planned on Kabul airport. There are many people near the airport who are trying to get out of Afghanistan.

## Evacuations from Afghanistan

![People were being taken onto an evacuation flight at Kabul airport on Tuesday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12071662)

Open image viewer

People were being taken onto an evacuation flight at Kabul airport on Tuesday. Photo: AOP

On Wednesday, 51 people were brought to Finland from Kabul. In total, almost 340 people have now been evacuated from Afghanistan to Finland, says the Ministry for Foreign Affairs.

Yle's information says that Finland will stop evacuations from Afghanistan on Friday. Many countries have already stopped evacuating people.

## Coronavirus infections

![Corona testing from a car window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12070500)

Open image viewer

Corona testing from a car window. Photo: Antti Eintola / Yle

There are fewer new coronavirus infections in Finland than before. An authority says that new infections have decreased this week among people of almost all ages.

Last week there were about 800 fewer new coronavirus infections than a week earlier. On Thursday there were 650 new coronavirus infections in Finland.

About 71 per cent of people in Finland have now received the first dose of the coronavirus vaccine.

## Travelling within Finland

![People on a ferry in Nauvo on their way to the island of Seili.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83166660eea51fbbf62/1626713997)

Open image viewer

Visitors going to the island of Seili in June 2021. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finns travelled within Finland more in July than ever before. People spent the night in hotels and cottages in Finland more than 3.3 million times in July. This is 2 per cent more than 2 years ago.

The number of foreign tourists in Finland fell a lot because of corona. There were 77 per cent fewer foreign tourists in Finland in July than 2 years earlier.

## Gunilla Bergström

![Gunilla Bergström, the author of the Mikko Mallikas books.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4368,h_2457,x_0,y_123/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-84647961274bad4b1af/1629965315)

The Swedish author Gunilla Bergström has died. She was 79 years old.

Bergström wrote books for children. Many of Bergström's books feature Mikko Mallikas. Mikko Mallikas is also the main character of many TV series and plays.

Gunilla Bergström also wrote other picture books.

The weather is sunny on Friday in almost the whole country. Only in the south is the weather cloudy. In the south it may rain a little at times.

The weather also gets warmer. During the day it is at least 14 degrees. In places the temperature rises close to 20 degrees.
