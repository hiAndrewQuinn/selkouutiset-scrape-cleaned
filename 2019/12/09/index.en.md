# Monday 9.12.2019 (radio)

Ministerial posts are being changed in the government. Strikes in export industries have started. Cuts to reimbursements have increased queues at health centre dentists. Police may take a long time to arrive in some places.

## New government being formed

![Sanna Marin and Antti Rinne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11108958)

New Prime Minister Sanna Marin (SDP) and former Prime Minister Antti Rinne (SDP). Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The government parties have agreed on new ministerial posts. The parties that form the government are swapping ministerial duties, that is, their portfolios. SDP chair Sanna Marin is the new prime minister. The previous prime minister, Antti Rinne, is no longer in the government.

Centre Party chair Katri Kulmuni will become Minister of Finance, and Mika Lintilä will become Minister of Economic Affairs. Sirpa Paatero of the SDP, who resigned from the government because of the Posti dispute, is returning to the government. Paatero will become Minister of Local Government.

Other ministers are also moving from one ministerial post to another. The new government will be appointed perhaps on Tuesday.

## Export industry strikes have started

![Strike guards at the gate of the Meyer Turku Oy shipyard in Turku](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11108667)

Strike guards at the gate of the Meyer Turku Oy shipyard in Turku. Photo: Kalle Mäkelä / Yle

Strikes in Finland's export industries have started. There are 9 collective agreement areas of the Industrial Union on strike, and over 180 workplaces. About 100,000 workers are on strike.

People are on strike, for example, at oil refineries, mines, medicine factories and sawmills. Workers and employers are in dispute over how much wages should be raised.

## Queues for dental care at health centres

![Dentist and dental nurse at work.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10935130)

Open image viewer

Kela reimbursements for dental care have fallen a lot. Photo: Juha-Pekka Inkinen / Yle

Cuts to Kela reimbursements have caused queues for dental care at health centres. Many people no longer go to a private dentist, because the Kela reimbursement has fallen.

The Kela reimbursement used to be 30 per cent of the price of a private dentist. Now the Kela reimbursement is only 15 per cent.

Many more patients have come to municipal health centre dentists. You may have to wait a long time for treatment.

## Police may take a long time to arrive in many places

![Police car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10400205)

Open image viewer

The police may take a long time to arrive in sparsely populated areas. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

In many places in Finland, you may have to wait a long time for the police. The police have looked into how quickly police patrols can reach help in different parts of Finland.

In almost 60 places, a police patrol takes more than 40 minutes to arrive after the alarm.

In areas where few people live, the distances are long. For example, in Savukoski in Eastern Lapland, it takes the police 1 hour and 10 minutes to arrive after an alarm.

In cities, the police arrive quickly. In Helsinki, for example, the police usually arrive within five minutes.
