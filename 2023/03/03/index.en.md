# Friday 3.3.2023 (radio)

Yle's election tool has opened. Court cases take too long. Writer Maikki Harjanne has died. Finland won a medal at the cross-country skiing World Championships. The weather is dry.

## Yle's election tool

![A person holds a mobile phone whose screen shows a picture of Yle's 2023 parliamentary election tool.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3496,h_1966,x_1,y_758/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10802366400ce8824aec/1677774724)

Yle's election tool is now open on the internet. The election tool helps voters find a good candidate in the parliamentary elections, which are in a month. From the election tool you can see which candidate agrees with you on issues.

About 90 percent of the candidates in the parliamentary elections have answered the questions in Yle's election tool. The tool also has the parties' answers to the questions.

The election tool is in Finnish, Swedish and North Sami, and also in English, Russian and Arabic. The election tool is at yle.fi.

## Court cases

![Name of the courthouse.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_456/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107953063ff4b5795c1f/1677676531)

District courts and courts of appeal take too long to handle court cases. An authority says that the courts have too few employees. There are too few employees because there is not enough funding.

Last year there were about 512,000 cases in the district courts in total. There were about 8,000 cases in the courts of appeal.

## Maikki Harjanne has died

![Maikki Harjanne next to a fence made of big pencils.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4171,h_2340,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10806886401f34f27594/1677849449)

Open image viewer

Children's writer Maikki Harjanne has died. She was 78 years old. Maikki Harjanne is known especially for the Minttu books, which she wrote from the 1970s onwards.

Maikki Harjanne wrote and illustrated more than 100 works. She received several awards, such as the State Prize for Children's Culture and the Pro Finlandia Medal.

## Skiing medal

![Niko Anttola on the shoulders of Iivo Niskanen, Perttu Hyvärinen and Ristomatti Hakola. A relay silver for Finland at the World Championships.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5297,h_2979,x_0,y_354/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1080776640209a722e8f/1677855196)

Open image viewer

The Finnish team won silver in the men's relay at the cross-country skiing World Championships. In the Finnish team that finished 2nd, Ristomatti Hakola, Iivo Niskanen, Perttu Hyvärinen and Niko Anttola skied. The race was won by the Norwegian team, and 3rd was Germany.

The last time Finland won a medal in the men's relay at the World Championships was in 2009.

This year's medal at the cross-country skiing World Championships is Finland's 1st. The skiing World Championships are under way in Planica, Slovenia. The championships end on Sunday.

A few more weather reports.

On Saturday, during the day, it is cloudy and mostly dry. In some places snow may fall. The temperature varies during the day between 0 and -5 degrees.

In many places it is windy. On roads, driving conditions are poor in Southern Finland and Western Finland.
