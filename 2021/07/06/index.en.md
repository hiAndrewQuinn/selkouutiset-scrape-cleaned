## Travel restrictions

![Mask-wearing air passenger arriving in Finland at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5005,h_2815,x_0,y_375/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82792060dee7cf265cd/1625221160)

The government parties have agreed more precisely on travel restrictions.

Last week it was decided in Finland that people may come to Finland freely from countries where the corona situation is good. Travellers from these countries are not required to have coronavirus vaccinations or a corona test when they come to Finland.

The government parties have, however, disagreed about when the corona situation in a country is good. On Tuesday the government parties agreed that the limit is 10 corona cases.

That means that a country may have at most 10 coronavirus cases per 100,000 inhabitants over 2 weeks. Then people may come freely to Finland from that country. There are only a few countries where there are so few coronavirus cases.

From other countries a traveller can get into Finland without a corona test only if they have received both coronavirus vaccinations or have had coronavirus disease.

Coronavirus infections are growing fast in Russia. On Tuesday it was reported that there are over 23,000 new coronavirus infections in the country.

Coronavirus deaths in Russia are also growing. Almost 140,000 people have died in total in Russia because of corona.

## Helsinki rents

![Cityscape of Jätkäsaari in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11017927)

Open image viewer

Living is expensive in Helsinki. Photo: Carolina Husu / Yle

Rents in Helsinki are expensive. The rent of a flat can be as much as 3 times higher in Helsinki than elsewhere.

Helsinki's most expensive rents are in the centre, where the rent for a studio flat is often almost 30 euros per square metre.

A cheap rent in Helsinki is equal to an expensive rent in Tampere, Turku and Oulu.

## Eino Leino's day

![Summer night at the foot of a fell, a small barn and in front of it meadow buttercups.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_108/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5730145d11f95207dd4/1561459149)

Open image viewer

Eino Leino's day is a celebration of poetry and the Finnish summer. Photo: Yle / Anna-Kaisa Brenner

On Tuesday Eino Leino's day was celebrated. The writer Eino Leino was born on 6 July 1878.

Eino Leino wrote many poems. That is why Eino Leino's day is also the day of poetry and summer. The day of poetry and summer is a flag day.

On Tuesday the Eino Leino prize was also awarded. This year the Eino Leino Society gave the prize to the poet Miia Toivio.
