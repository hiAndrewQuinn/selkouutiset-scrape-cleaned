# Tuesday 20.8.2019 (radio)

Many young people are lonely. Employment is not growing. Alcohol imports from Estonia have grown. Putin's visit will disrupt traffic on Wednesday. Grain is now being threshed.

## Young people's loneliness

![A woman walks along the bank of the Aura river in Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10252045)

Open image viewer

Loneliness can increase difficulty falling asleep and nervousness. Photo: Kalle Mäkelä / Yle

Many young people are lonely. A study by the University of Jyväskylä found that more pupils in the 9th grade of comprehensive school feel lonely than in the 7th grade.

20 per cent of 9th grade pupils say that they are lonely.

14 per cent of 7th grade pupils feel lonely.

Girls feel lonely more than boys.

Lonely pupils are also nervous, they experience pain symptoms, and they have more difficulty falling asleep than other pupils.

## Employment

![Hands on a keyboard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10918303)

Open image viewer

The growth in employment has stopped. Photo: Ilmari Fabritius / Yle

Employment has not improved as well as the government wants.

In July only a little more people were at work than in July last year. An expert says that the improvement in employment has stopped in the early part of the year.

The employment rate trend in July was 72.4 per cent. The government wants the employment rate to rise to 75 per cent.

## Alcohol imports from Estonia

![Drinks trolley in the harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7916512)

Open image viewer

Estonia reduced its alcohol tax at the beginning of July. Photo: Yle

Finns are bringing more alcohol from Estonia to Finland than before.

In July Finns brought a total of about 4.7 million litres of alcohol from Estonia to Finland. That is 1.4 million litres more than in July last year.

Alcohol imports have grown because alcohol is now cheaper in Estonia than before. Estonia reduced the alcohol tax by 25 per cent at the beginning of July.

## Putin's visit

![Russian President Vladimir Putin comments on Ukraine in Beijing on the closing day of the New Silk Road meeting on Saturday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10757988)

Police say that tomorrow, Wednesday, it is worth using public transport if you want to go to central Helsinki. It is also worth allowing extra time for travelling.

Russian President **Vladimir Putin** visits Helsinki on Wednesday. Other traffic may have to wait at times while Putin's car moves in the centre.

Because of Putin's visit, the Market Square is also closed all day on Wednesday.

## Threshing of grain

![Barley field in autumn](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10403416)

Open image viewer

Barley is now being threshed too. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

Rain and at times cold weather have slowed the harvesting of grain, that is, threshing. In the southern parts of the country the rye will soon be all harvested, but in Central Finland threshing of rye begins this week. Wheat is also being threshed now.

Rye and wheat harvests are quite good this year. In some areas it has been dry and the grain has grown poorly.
