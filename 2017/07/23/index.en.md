## Diabetes is increasing alarmingly

![Blood sugar is measured at a clinic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9706025)

Open image viewer

Blood sugar is measured with a blood test from a fingertip. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

Adult-onset diabetes is increasing quickly.

The International Diabetes Federation estimates that one in ten (10) adults will get diabetes by 2035.

Adult-onset diabetes is a metabolic illness in which the level of sugar in the blood is raised. It rises because the pancreas does not produce enough of the hormone insulin.

The World Health Organization says that five (5) years ago, 1.5 million people died of the illness. Diabetes is becoming the seventh (7) most common cause of death in the world.

In China alone, a hundred (100) million people have diabetes.

## Chinese online shopping is busy

![Products from one end to the other in Chinese shops.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9727754)

Open image viewer

Chinese online shops sell all kinds of goods and clothing. Photo: Wish.com

The popularity of Chinese online shops is growing wildly. For example, on Facebook, products from Chinese online shops are presented and reviewed in many different groups.

Last year, 15 million parcels came from China through the post. Over the last couple of years growth has been about 50 percent a year.

Parcels often cause difficulties for Posti, because they have irregular shapes. Parcels cannot be sorted automatically, so they need to be sorted by hand. Hand work, on the other hand, is expensive.

## Tall Ships Races ended in Turku

![Sailing ships in Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9736888)

Open image viewer

The Tall Ships Races could be seen on the banks of Turku. Photo: Jaakko Stenroos / AOP

The event for big sailing ships has ended in Turku. In Turku the parade of sails was watched when the Tall Ships Races event ended. The first ships raised their anchors from the Aura river and set off towards the Airisto in the afternoon.

The international sailing ship race is held every year. The race itself is held every fourth (4) year in the Baltic Sea. In Finland the ships sailed this summer first to Kotka, and from there to Turku.

From Turku the ships continue their journey to Klaipėda in Lithuania.

## Snakes can come inside buildings

![Kyy](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8895929)

Open image viewer

Finland's only venomous snake is the adder. Photo: Yle

A cool summer has made snakes behave strangely. Snakes can come inside buildings, because they want to warm up or are just curious.

Snake expert Toni Beckman says that grass snakes get into every place. Harmless grass snakes are used to sharing their surroundings with people. Finland's only venomous snake, the adder, on the other hand, is afraid of people.

Removing a snake from a building often needs outside help. Many people call the rescue service to remove the snake from indoors. A snake does not normally attack a person. It attacks only if it feels that it is threatened.

Beckman says that snakes inside buildings, however, are a rare sight.
