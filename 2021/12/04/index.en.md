# Saturday 4.12.2021 (radio)

The corona virus Omicron variant. Few side effects from the corona vaccine. Suspected terrorism. The price of food. Frost.

## Finland's Omicron cases

![Test swab in a nurse's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_426/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88813761ab16106d6df/1638700927)

Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

In Finland there are now 7 corona infections where the cause is the Omicron variant of the corona virus.

The infections have been found in different parts of Finland, but they belong to the same chain of infection.

For now, Finland's Omicron cases are not serious. People have only had small symptoms. They have not had to go to hospital.

The authorities are following the Omicron cases closely, because the Omicron variant was found only a short time ago.

That is why there is still little information about the Omicron variant. For example, it is not yet known how well the corona vaccine protects against the Omicron variant.

## Few side effects from the corona vaccine

![Niko Torikka gets his second corona vaccine, given by nurse Maroufian Pouye.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-87034561726c5b081ba/1639583307)

Open image viewer

Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

The corona vaccine causes only a few side effects.

About 1000 people have applied for compensation for side effects of the corona vaccine in Finland. They have had, for example, a fever after the corona vaccine, or they have had a sore arm after the vaccination.

Serious side effects are very rare. For example, 1 person in 100 000 has had serious allergic symptoms from the corona vaccine.

## Suspected terrorism

![Weapons and explosives in a police photo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1192,h_670,x_59,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88817161ab5e7125e93/1638636810)

Open image viewer

Photo: Police

The police have told about an unusual crime suspicion. On Friday the police said that a group of men may have planned terrorist crimes.

The court has put the men in custody. There are 5 men, and they were put in custody in Satakunta, in Western Finland.

The police found weapons and dynamite from the men. However, the police do not say what kind of terrorist act the men were planning.

The police say that the men belong to a far-right group. They are about 25-year-old Finns.

This is the first time that the police suspect far-right terrorism in Finland.

## The price of food

![A customer shopping in a grocery shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11568170)

Open image viewer

Food has become more expensive slowly in Finland. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Food is getting more expensive in Finland more slowly than in the rest of Europe.

In October, food became 1 per cent more expensive in Finland, but in other EU countries prices rose by more than 2 per cent.

For example, the prices of cooking oils and cooking fats have risen in Finland much less than in other EU countries.

Even though food prices have risen more slowly in Finland than in other EU countries, food in Finland is still clearly more expensive than elsewhere in the EU.

## Frost

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12217748)

The weather is cold now. On Sunday night it may be 40 degrees of frost in Lapland.

On Sunday during the day there is frost across the whole country, between 10 and 30 degrees. In Lapland the frost may be over 30 degrees in some places.

The wind makes the cold feel even colder.
