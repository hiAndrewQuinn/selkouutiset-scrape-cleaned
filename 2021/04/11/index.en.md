## Finns' savings

![Banknotes in a wallet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2688,h_1512,x_0,y_67/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7367855f9d0e8b455d0/1715416218)

Open image viewer

It has been hard to spend money during the corona time. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Finns' savings have grown during the corona time.

There are now billions of euros more in Finns' bank accounts than before corona. The reason is that during the corona time, people have not had as many chances to spend money as before.

Experts estimate that after corona, money will be spent again more. Corona restrictions are likely to be lifted during the rest of the year. Then people will be able to eat at restaurants and travel abroad again.

## Sunday's corona figures

20 percent of Finns have received a corona vaccination. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that there are 346 new corona infections in Finland.

On Sunday too, the most new corona infections were in the Helsinki and Uusimaa area. There were almost 200 new corona infections there on Sunday.

Corona vaccinations are progressing. Already about 20 percent of Finns have been vaccinated against corona at least once.

Cycling is a good way to combine tourism with Finland's beautiful nature. Thanks to electric bikes, older tourists can also enjoy cycling.

## A cold snap in France

![In the Loire valley, torches and charcoal braziers were lit between the vines at a vineyard in Saint Nicolas de Bourgueil.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4250,h_2391,x_0,y_219/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7932386071d3d982dc1/1618072841)

Open image viewer

Wine producers have tried to keep frost away from the vineyards using fires. Photo: AOP

There has been a cold snap in France this week. The temperature fell below zero. There was frost of as much as 6 degrees.

The cold snap is causing damage to French wine producers. Because of the cold snap, this year's harvest will be small. The French government intends to give emergency aid to wine producers.

The frosty weather came at a bad time in France. Before the frost, France had summer-like warmth. That is why plants had already grown and flowered when winter came back.

## Monday's weather

Here in Finland, Monday is a warm day in the south. In Southern Finland the temperature may be almost 15 degrees.

On Monday it will rain in Western Finland and Central Finland. It is colder in the rain area than in the south. In Central Finland it may even snow heavily.

On Tuesday the weather will cool down in the south. The temperature in Southern Finland on Tuesday is below 10 degrees.

On Tuesday the rain will move north, and in Lapland it may snow.
