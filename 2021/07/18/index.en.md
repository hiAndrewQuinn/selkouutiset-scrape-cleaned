## Storm Riikka

![Gusts of wind](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83295960f2caf9e6613/1626524677)

Riikka's strong winds moved across Finland during Sunday.

In Finland there have been heavy thunderstorms on Sunday. The thunderstorms were brought by storm Riikka.

Riikka has moved across Finland towards the east. During the evening the thunderstorms move to the Russian side.

Riikka's thunderstorms have been heavy. For example, in Kuopio so much rain fell that rainwater got into house cellars. Riikka also had a lot of lightning.

Because of storm Riikka, thousands of homes have been without electricity.

## Victims of the floods

![A man wades in brown water up to his armpits. All around are completely destroyed cars and other debris.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12025376)

Rescue worker in Belgium. Photo: Julian Warnard / EPA

The number of victims is growing in the European flood area. On Sunday it was reported that almost 200 people have died in the floods.

There may be even more dead, because hundreds of people are missing. It is not yet known whether they are alive or dead.

Rescue workers are searching for dead people, for example in cars that have been under water. The rescue work will continue for a long time.

The floods have caused a lot of damage in western Germany and Belgium. Now the water level of the rivers has risen also in other parts of Germany and in Austria.

## Cause of the floods

![Cars float in the flooded Meuse river](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12025221)

Open image viewer

In Europe people are asking whether this is climate change. Photo: Philippe Bourguet / AOP

In Europe people are now asking what the cause of the floods is. There are floods in Europe every summer, but now the floods are much worse than usual.

The water of the rivers has risen to a record high because of heavy rains. In some areas it has rained in 2 days as much as usually rains in 2 months.

For example, in Germany many researchers and politicians say that the cause of the heavy rain and the floods is climate change. Because of climate change, extreme weather events are increasing.

That means there are more really hot and dry spells, and also more really rainy spells than before. They also last longer than before.

## Award for a Finnish film

![Actors Seidi Haarla and Juri Borisov in the film Compartment No. 6](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12025532)

"Compartment No. 6" is about a Finnish girl and a Russian man who meet on a train. Photo: Festival de Cannes

A Finnish film has won an award at the Cannes Film Festival.

The name of the film is "Compartment No. 6". It won the second best prize of the festival, the Grand Prix.

The director of the film is Juho Kuosmanen. Kuosmanen hopes that the award will bring out Finnish skills in the film industry.

"Compartment No. 6" is about a train journey in Russia. During the journey, a Finnish student girl and a Russian man get to know each other. The story is from a book by the Finnish writer Rosa Liksom.
