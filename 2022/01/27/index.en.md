# Thursday 27.1.2022 (radio)

The Finnish forest company UPM plans to build a biorefinery in the Netherlands. The Ukraine crisis is already affecting trade between Finland and Russia. Corona restrictions have improved Finland's corona situation. Finland's population grew last year thanks to immigration. On Friday there will be sunshine and snow.

## UPM's biorefinery in the Netherlands

![A map shows the location of Rotterdam.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12289636)

Photo: Samuli Huttunen / Yle

The large Finnish forest company UPM plans to build a factory in the Netherlands. The new factory will come to the city of Rotterdam.

The factory will be a biorefinery, which makes biofuels.

In Finland people are disappointed that the forest company UPM is building the factory in Rotterdam. The other option was the city of Kotka in Finland.

In Finland it was hoped that the factory's jobs would be brought to Finland.

## Trade between Finland and Russia in trouble

![Oras water taps in an exhibition space.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6820785ecf7b10e159c/1590655804)

Open image viewer

Finland exports many kinds of products to Russia, for example Oras water taps. Photo: Rami Koivula / Yle

The Ukraine crisis is already affecting trade between Finland and Russia.

Around the world there is fear that Russia will attack Ukraine and that a war will break out. Therefore the value of the rouble has weakened.

Because of the weak rouble, Finnish products are more expensive for Russians than before. Therefore it is harder for Finnish companies to sell products to Russia.

Russia is an important country for Finnish companies. Russia is one of Finland's big trading partners.

## Corona situation improving

![A sample taker talks with a person being tested at a drive-in corona testing point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_10,y_126/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90616761ef8410ec9a1/1643087638)

Many corona cases are still being found. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Corona restrictions have improved Finland's corona situation.

There are still many new corona infections, but the number of corona infections is no longer growing as fast as it was a short while ago. On Thursday about 6,300 new corona infections were reported.

Experts estimate that corona restrictions may perhaps be gradually eased. For example, gyms may perhaps soon be open again. Some relief may also come to the opening hours of restaurants.

Experts say it is important that everyone behaves in a corona-safe way. They also say that corona vaccinations are the best way to prevent corona.

## Finland's population

![Baby on mother's lap.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5153,h_2899,x_0,y_242/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82973260e69a843e89d/1716539171)

Almost 50,000 children were born in Finland last year. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Finland's population grew in 2021. Statistics Finland says that Finland's population grew by about 16,000 people last year. Finland now has about 5.5 million inhabitants.

Almost 50,000 children were born in Finland last year. However, this did not lead to population growth, because even more people died. Last year there were almost 8,000 more deaths than births.

Finland's population grew last year because of immigration. There were over 22,000 more people moving to Finland than those who moved away from Finland.
