## The number of insects is decreasing

![Wheat being threshed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10640615)

Open image viewer

Poisons reduce the number of insects. Photo: Jim Pickerell / AOP

Insects are disappearing around the world. An international study says that in 10 years' time, 25 percent of insects will be gone.

The study shows that in 100 years' time there will be no insects at all. If there are no insects, nature will not survive.

The decrease in insects is caused by insecticides. Urban sprawl and global warming also reduce the number of insects.

## The EU needs new nuclear power plants, says the Commission

![A nuclear power plant in Cattenom, France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10636429)

Open image viewer

More nuclear power plants are needed, says the European Commission. Photo: Julien Warnand / EPA

The European Commission says that the EU needs many dozens of new nuclear power plants.

In the European Commission's view, more nuclear power plants must be built so that there are fewer climate emissions. The European Union wants to stop using coal completely in 30 years' time. Coal causes a lot of emissions.

In the future, the EU's electricity will come only from nuclear power and renewable energy. For example, wind power is renewable energy. Climate change raises the temperature of the Earth. Emissions speed up climate change.

## Poisonous liquor kills people in India

![Homemade country liquor being drunk on a street in Calcutta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10640840)

Open image viewer

Homemade liquor is drunk on a street in India. Photo: Piyal Adhikary / EPA

In India more than 100 people have died after drinking illegal alcohol. In addition, many people have been taken to hospital because of poisoning.

The victims of the poisoning suffer from stomach pains and breathing difficulties before they die.

In India, poor people cannot always afford to buy alcohol from a shop. Poisonous alcohol often kills people in India. However, the case that has now happened is unusually serious.

## A record number of tourists visited Lapland

![Christmas Santa Claus Village in Rovaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10589803)

Open image viewer

Tourists at Santa Claus Village in Rovaniemi, Lapland. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

More tourists visited Lapland last year than ever before.

The most foreign tourists came to Lapland from Great Britain and France. Foreign visitors stayed overnight in Lapland almost 6 percent more than a year earlier. The number of travellers in Lapland has grown much faster than elsewhere in Finland.

Most tourists go to Lapland in December.

## A new law regulates names

![Surname board](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10581556)

Open image viewer

Finland has a new name law. Photo: Timo Sihvonen / Yle

Finland has a new name law. The law came into force at the beginning of this year. The name law regulates what kinds of first names and surnames are allowed in Finland.

On the Clear News website there are now 2 videos that tell about the new name law. One video is about first names, and the other video is about surnames.
