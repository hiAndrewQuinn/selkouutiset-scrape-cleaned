# Saturday 30.6.2018 (radio)

About 100,000 people took part in the Pride parade in Helsinki. The taxi law is changing. Finns eat as much meat as before, but they eat a little more grain. There are more drivers over 80 than before.

## The Pride parade

There was a colourful Pride parade in Helsinki on Saturday. About 100,000 people walked through the centre of Helsinki. They demanded equality for homosexuals and other sexual minorities.

Many political parties also took part in the Pride parade. For example, the National Coalition Party, the Social Democrats and the Greens were in the parade. Helsinki mayor Jan Vapaavuori walked near the front of the parade.

## The new taxi law

![Ordering a taxi with a smartphone app](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10276848)

Open image viewer

You can get a taxi by phoning or easily with a phone app. Photo: Berislav Jurišić / Yle

The taxi law is changing. The new taxi law comes into force on 1 July, that is, on Sunday.

The new taxi law frees up the taxi business. The authorities will no longer decide how many taxis or taxi drivers there can be. So new companies will come into the taxi business. Taxi companies decide their prices themselves.

The new law brings more competition to the taxi business. Taxi companies can compete for customers with prices and service.

Customers can order a taxi, for example, with a phone app. Many apps also say how much the taxi ride costs. If the app does not say the price, it is a good idea for the customer to ask the driver the price before the trip.

## What Finns eat

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10281814)

Open image viewer

Pork is still number one, but people eat less of it than before. Photo: Yle News Graphics

The Natural Resources Institute has given new information about what Finns eat.

Finns eat as much meat as before. Last year one Finn ate 81 kilos of meat. Finns eat the most pork, but pork consumption has gone down. People eat more chicken than before.

Finns eat a little more grain, because they eat more oats and rice than before. Last year one Finn ate about 80 kilos of grain.

## 80-year-old drivers

![Eero Seppänen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10266433)

Open image viewer

83-year-old Eero Seppänen still drives a lot. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

The number of old people in Finland is growing. So there are also more old drivers than before. There are now almost 2 times as many drivers over 80 as 10 years ago.

The driving licence law changes at the start of July. Now a person needs a doctor's certificate at about 75 when they want a new driving licence. Before, a person needed a doctor's certificate already at 70.

Experts say that an old and healthy driver is not a danger in traffic. It is important that drivers notice their own weaknesses. For example, a driver can avoid driving in the dark if they do not see well in the dark.
