# Wednesday 30.11.2016 (radio)

The Prime Minister says that he did not try to influence Yle's news reporting. No decision on support for undocumented people. In Syria, people are fleeing East Aleppo. The police suspect that three girls planned a school attack. Sitting weakens success at school.

##

## Sipilä denies trying to influence Yle

![Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9325431)

Open image viewer

Sipilä held a press conference today, where he commented on his contacts with Yle. Photo: Yle

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä says that he has not tried to influence Yle's news.

Yle reported on Friday that a firm owned by the Prime Minister's relatives has received a big order from the Terrafame mine, which the Finnish state owns. Sipilä criticised Yle's news story, because he did not have enough time to comment on it before it was published.

Suomen Kuvalehti wrote today that Prime Minister Sipilä has put pressure on Yle, and that is why Yle has no longer made news about the matter.

Atte Jääskeläinen, the editor-in-chief of Yle News, also says that Sipilä has not influenced Yle's news.

## No decision on support for undocumented people

![An anonymous man walks into a dark gateway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6592588)

Open image viewer

Undocumented people do not always get social assistance. Photo: Yle

No decision has been made on social assistance for people who have received a negative asylum decision, that is, undocumented people.

A government working group was supposed to say today what kind of support undocumented people will get. The working group only decided that they must be offered food and important medicines for a few days.

At the moment, some municipalities pay social assistance to undocumented people and other municipalities do not.

Undocumented people have stayed in Finland without a permit.

## In Syria, people are fleeing East Aleppo

![Sana / EPA](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9325303)

Open image viewer

A lot of people are now fleeing East Aleppo.

In Syria, tens of thousands of people have fled East Aleppo.

In four days, over 50,000 people have fled the eastern parts of the city, says an organisation that monitors the Syrian civil war.

Government forces have advanced quickly, and they have already taken a third of East Aleppo from the rebels.

## Police: Girls planned a school attack

![A police sign on the wall of a building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9324980)

Open image viewer

The police told about their suspicions today. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The police suspect that three schoolgirls have planned a dangerous attack on a school in Riihimäki.

The police say that the girls threatened people's lives and health. They had sent a threat to other pupils on the WhatsApp instant messaging service.

Two girls have been remanded in custody because of the threats, and one girl is in the care of the authorities.

The police have searched homes, but have not found any weapons or explosives.

## Schoolchildren should not sit

![A school bag and an empty desk in a classroom. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9082085)

Open image viewer

At school, people sit for many hours. Photo: Matleena Ylikoski / Yle

A pupil may not do well at school if he or she sits too much.

A study by the University of Eastern Finland says that especially boys' schoolwork can suffer if they do not move enough.

The study found that sitting was linked to poor reading skills in 6-8-year-old boys.

Sitting did not affect girls' success at school in the same way as boys'.
