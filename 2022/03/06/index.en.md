# Sunday 6.3.2022 (radio)

Russia continues its attack in Ukraine. More than 1.5 million people have fled Ukraine. Protesters have been arrested in Russia. Iivo Niskanen skied to 4th place at Holmenkollen.

## Russia's attack on Ukraine

![A Ukrainian armoured vehicle in Odesa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3996,h_2247,x_3,y_529/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9204316218b9474d45f/1645795906)

Russia's attack on Ukraine continues.

Ukraine says that Russia has already lost more than 11,000 soldiers in Ukraine. The Russian army has not commented on the figure.

Russia has shelled areas that are near Ukraine's capital Kyiv. Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelenskyi says that Russia plans to bomb the city of Odesa. Zelenskiy says that bombing Odesa is a war crime.

The city of Kherson is under Russian control.

Russia is besieging the city of Mariupol. Civilians could not be taken out of the city on Sunday. About 400,000 people live in Mariupol. The city has a severe shortage of water, electricity and food.

Turkey's President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan asks Russia to make peace.

## Refugees

More than 1.5 million people have fled the war from Ukraine. This is the estimate of the UN.

People have fled Ukraine to neighbouring countries, among them Poland, Moldova, Romania, Hungary and Slovakia.

Russia attacked Ukraine on Thursday, 24 February.

## Protests in Russia

![Police arrested a protester at an anti-war demonstration in central St Petersburg on 27.2.2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5231,h_2942,x_0,y_33/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-923149622095fbe45f4/1646302904)

Open image viewer

Police arrested a protester at an anti-war demonstration in central St Petersburg on 27.2.2022. Photo: Sergei Mikhailichenko / AFP

A large number of protesters have been arrested in Russia. The people taking part in the demonstrations oppose the war in Ukraine.

The civic organisation OVD-Info says that more than 2,000 protesters were arrested on Sunday in various parts of Russia. The most people were arrested in Novosibirsk and Yekaterinburg. Demonstrations have been held in dozens of cities.

Russian police said on Friday that illegal demonstrations will be prevented.

The race was won by the Norwegian Martin Löwström Nyenget. Norwegian skiers also took places 2 and 3.
