# Monday 17.4.2017 (radio)

The Turkish opposition does not accept the result of the referendum. In France people demonstrated against Marine Le Pen. Easter does not show on everyone's dinner table. The pollen season has already begun.

## The yes votes won the Turkish referendum

![A supporter of the Turkish government](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9568555)

Open image viewer

A supporter of the Turkish government celebrated the result of the referendum. Photo: Tumay Berkin / EPA

In Turkey the president gets more power than before.

The Turks accepted the change to the constitution in Sunday's referendum. About 51 percent of voters supported the change.

The change to the constitution gives the president the right, for example, to appoint ministers and judges, make laws and dissolve parliament. The office of prime minister is abolished completely.

Opponents of the law say that the constitutional reform means the end of Turkey's democracy.

The opposition accuses the government of election fraud. The opposition says that perhaps as many as 1.5 million votes that had not been stamped were accepted in the vote.

The opposition says that because of the fraud the result of the referendum cannot be accepted, that is, the result must be cancelled.

## The US Vice President visited the border between the Koreas

![US Vice President Mike Pence arrived on a state visit to South Korea on Sunday 16 April. Next to him are his wife Karen and South Korean leaders.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9568033)

Open image viewer

The US Vice President is currently visiting South Korea. Photo: Chung Sung-Jun / EPA

US Vice President Mike Pence has visited the border zone between the Koreas.

Pence visited the border only a day after North Korea's missile test. North Korea did a missile test on Sunday, but it failed.

Pence promised the United States' support to South Korea. Pence also said that the United States and South Korea will no longer tolerate new missile tests from North Korea.

Pence  also said that the visit to the border between the Koreas was important to him, because Pence's father fought in the Korean War in the US army.

## Chocolate eggs are the most popular Easter treat

On many Finns' dinner tables, Easter does not show in any way.

The association Ruokatieto asked 1,000 Finns what they eat at Easter. 40 percent answered that they eat exactly the same kind of food at Easter as on an ordinary weekend.

60 percent said that during Easter they eat one meal that is more festive than usual.

But many people buy chocolate eggs for Easter, even if they do not otherwise eat Easter food.

## Alder and hazel are flowering

![An allergic person blows their nose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9566186)

Open image viewer

Symptoms of pollen allergy are a runny nose, cough, stinging eyes and an itchy throat. Photo: AOP

The pollen season has begun. In the southern and central parts of Finland, alder and hazel are already flowering.

20 percent of Finns get allergic symptoms from pollen.

Birch causes the most allergy symptoms. 15 percent of Finns get symptoms from its pollen. Birch flowers in spring and early summer.

Symptoms of pollen allergy are, for example, a runny nose, cough, stinging eyes and an itchy throat.

Pollen allergy can start at any age.
