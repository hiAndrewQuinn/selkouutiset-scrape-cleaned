# Thursday 14.1.2021 (radio)

The Finnish government wants to stop the mutated coronavirus from spreading in Finland. 6 new corona deaths have been reported in Finland. Alko's sales have grown because of corona. Writer Sinikka Nopola has died. Yle journalist Liselott Lindström will receive the Topelius Prize. Sharp frost continues on Friday in Southern Finland.

## The government's corona line

![Family and basic services minister Krista Kiuru.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11736574)

Open image viewer

Minister Krista Kiuru told about the government's plans on Thursday. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The Finnish government has been considering the corona situation. The government wants to stop the mutated coronavirus from starting to spread in Finland.

The mutated coronavirus is dangerous, because it spreads easily and quickly. The mutated coronavirus is already in many countries.

That is why Finland intends to direct all passengers who come to Finland to a corona test. In addition, it is wanted to reduce travelling abroad.

In this way Finland wants to stop the mutated coronavirus from starting to spread here too.

## Alko's sales

![A lot of alcohol bottles](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4649755a85aecebfd4f/1518718430)

Open image viewer

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Alko's sales have grown. Alko's sales grew last year because of corona.

Wine sales grew by 15 percent, and sales of strong alcoholic drinks grew by 10 percent.

Alko estimates that sales grew precisely because of corona. During the corona time Finns have not made trips abroad. That is why buying alcohol abroad has also decreased.

Because of corona, restaurants were also closed for a long time last year. That is why drinking alcohol moved from restaurants to homes.

## Sinikka Nopola has died

![Sinikka Nopola in snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_231/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5397385c519de3c3090/1610628557)

Open image viewer

Sinikka Nopola. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

Writer Sinikka Nopola has died.

Nopola wrote about 80 books. Many remember Nopola especially for children's books.

The sisters Sinikka and Tiina Nopola wrote together the books about Heinähattu and Vilttitossu, and the books about Risto Räppääjä. Films have also been made about Risto Räppääjä.

Sinikka Nopola was 67 years old. She died in Helsinki because of a serious illness.

## Liselott Lindström was awarded

![Liselott Lindström.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1280,h_720,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5614355cd2b18ec1708/1557311939)

Open image viewer

Liselott Lindström. Photo: Hanna Nordenswan

Yle journalist Liselott Lindström will receive the Topelius Prize. The Topelius Prize is given in Finland every year to a successful Swedish-speaking journalist.

Liselott Lindström is Yle's Africa journalist. She has told Finns about events in Africa in Swedish and in Finnish.

Lindström has given Finns information about African countries that European journalists rarely visit.

Sharp frost continues on Friday in Southern Finland. The frost is as much as 25 degrees.

In the central and northern parts of the country the frost weakens. In Lapland the frost on Friday is under 10 degrees.

At the weekend the frost weakens in the south too.
