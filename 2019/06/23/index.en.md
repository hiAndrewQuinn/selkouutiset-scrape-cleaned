# Sunday 23.6.2019 (radio)

People have died in midsummer accidents. The USA has made a new plan for the Middle East. Finnish teachers are satisfied with their work. Finland's Emil Ruusuvuori has won the tennis tournament in Uzbekistan.

## Midsummer return traffic

![Midsummer return traffic on Highway 9 between Tampere and Orivesi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10844315)

Midsummer return traffic went smoothly, without big traffic jams. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Traffic has been busy on Sunday, as people have come home from celebrating midsummer. There were no big traffic jams, because many people stay at their summer cottages after midsummer to enjoy their summer holidays.

People have also died in midsummer accidents this year.

At least 2 people died in traffic accidents. In addition, at least 5 people drowned.

## The USA's plan

![Jared Kushner in a dark blue suit and tie.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10463156)

Open image viewer

Jared Kushner, the son-in-law of President Donald Trump, presents the USA's plan on Tuesday. Photo: Kevin Dietsch / Pool / EPA

The USA has made a new plan for the Middle East. The aim of the plan is peace between Palestinians and Israelis.

The first part of the plan concerns the economy. The USA promises to improve the economy of the Palestinian areas. It intends to collect billions of dollars for economic aid.

Economic aid will be negotiated on Tuesday. The Palestinians, however, do not want to take part in the meeting. They say that disputes between Palestinians and Israelis cannot be solved with money. In the Palestinians' opinion, only a political solution can bring peace.

The disputes between Palestinians and Israelis have continued for decades already.

## Teachers' satisfaction

![Ninth-grade pupils in an English lesson, taught by senior lecturer Katariina Pekuri, Riihikallio school, Tuusula, 15.1.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_368/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5366975c3ef330d1911/1547629430)

Open image viewer

Teachers in Finland feel that they are valued. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

Finnish teachers are satisfied with their own work. This is what a study by the economic organisation OECD says.

48 countries took part in the study. Finnish teachers were more satisfied than the others. They say that they are valued.

The problem for Finnish teachers is classroom peace. Finland is one of the countries where pupils insult and bully each other the most.

In Finland, teachers hope for smaller teaching groups.

## Ruusuvuori's win

![Emil Ruusuvuori](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10843291)

Open image viewer

Emil Ruusuvuori is a young Finnish tennis player. Photo: AOP

Finland's Emil Ruusuvuori has won the tennis tournament in Uzbekistan.

The win was important, because it was Ruusuvuori's first win in a Challenger-level singles match. In the final match the Finn beat Roberto Cid Suberv of the Dominican Republic.

Emil Ruusuvuori is a 20-year-old tennis player. Many people expect that Ruusuvuori will become the next Finnish tennis star.

## The week's weather

![A view from the shore across a lake.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10339049)

In southern Finland it is a little over 20 degrees warm during the early part of the week. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

In Central Europe people are preparing for intense heat. The temperature may rise to around 40 degrees in many countries.

Here in Finland the weather is pleasantly warm. In the south the temperature is a little over 20 degrees, and in the north it is under 20 degrees.
