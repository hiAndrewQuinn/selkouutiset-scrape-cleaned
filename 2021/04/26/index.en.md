# Monday 26.4.2021 (radio)

Most minors encounter sexual grooming online. Finns plan to retire later than before. Closing the Veitsiluoto mill affects jobs in the town. The tobacco act will be tightened. This summer's Tango Market is cancelled.

## Sexual harassment of young people is common online

![A young girl uses a smartphone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10826923)

Almost 90 percent of respondents have at some point received sexually tinged messages from an adult.

About 9 percent of the messaging leads to a video connection, and 6 percent to a meeting outside the internet. More than half of those who answered the survey have told someone about sexual grooming.

Most of the respondents in the survey were girls. The police suspect that boys may not recognise harassment as well as girls do.

## Finns plan to retire later than before

![A pensioner on a bus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-7837058d4ddcaa3f02/1490345671)

Finns plan to retire at a later age than before.

Employees' plans to retire have been postponed by 10 years. The Finnish Centre for Pensions says that retirement plans have been postponed by almost 2 years.

In 2018, people planned to retire at an average age of 64 years and 7 months. In 2008, the planned retirement age was on average 62 years and eight months.

Men and highly educated people in particular plan to retire later than others.

The Finnish Centre for Pensions says that the change is significant. The planned retirement age rises at the same pace as the lowest old-age pension age.

## Closing Veitsiluoto affects the town

![Stora Enso's Veitsiluoto paper mills in Kemi, 21 September 2011.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11892394)

Open image viewer

Veitsiluoto mill in Kemi. Photo: Kimmo Mäntylä / Lehtikuva

Closing the mill of the forest giant Stora Enso may cause 1,500 jobs to disappear in the area. Last week Stora Enso said that it will close the Veitsiluoto paper mill in Kemi.

Almost 700 jobs will disappear from the mill after the closure. In addition, there will be losses at subcontracting companies.

Purchasing power will also weaken in the town. This may put jobs at risk, for example in retail.

## The tobacco act will be tightened

![A man smoking a cigarette.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11762843)

Open image viewer

The ban on scents and flavours would extend to cover all tobacco products. Photo: Laura Ukkonen / Lehtikuva

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health proposes tightening the tobacco act.

Brand identifiers and logos, that is, the graphic design of the product, may be removed from cigarette packs.

The goal is to prevent packaging from being used in marketing.

The ministry wants to ban smoking in playgrounds, public beaches and under bus stop shelters.

The change may also tighten smoking on restaurant terraces from the start of 2024.

## Tango Market is cancelled

![Participants in the tango singing competition's general heats in Seinäjoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10874812)

Open image viewer

Participants in the tango singing competition's general heats in Seinäjoki in 2019. Photo: Timo Aalto / Lehtikuva

The Seinäjoki Tango Market has been cancelled for next summer. The tango singing competition is also cancelled because of the corona situation.

Tickets for last year's and this year's events will be moved to next year. Tickets that have been bought can also be returned by the end of July.

However, the tango composition and lyrics competition will still be held this year.
