# Tuesday 30.8.2022 (radio)

Former executive of the Youth Foundation Perttu Nousiainen has been sentenced to prison. Food is more expensive than before. More nurses are needed in care homes for the elderly. The weather is getting colder.

## Perttu Nousiainen

![Former chair of the Youth Foundation's board Perttu Nousiainen confessed to his crimes at Helsinki District Court on 23 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5344,h_3006,x_0,y_556/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9981716304b3bb870da/1661252784)

Former executive of the Youth Foundation Perttu Nousiainen has been sentenced to prison. The sentence is 4 years and 4 months. Nousiainen confessed to the crimes last week.

Nousiainen must also pay over 20 million euros in compensation. Among other things, Nousiainen took bribes from companies, that is money, while he worked at the Youth Foundation. Nousiainen made agreements that were harmful to the Youth Foundation.

Perttu Nousiainen held management positions at the Youth Foundation until 2018. Among other things, the Youth Foundation rents cheap flats to young people.

## Food prices

![Person bends down to look at cheeses from a cold counter in the Stockmann Herkku grocery store.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_100/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97265962b08edf86a6e/1655739718)

Food prices rose clearly more than general price levels. Photo: Aalto Puutio / Yle

Food costs more than before. In July, food cost over 12 percent more than in July 2021.

Food prices rose more than the prices of other products and goods. Inflation, that is the general rise in prices, was 7.8 percent in July. For example, tobacco and alcohol cost 4 percent more than a year earlier.

## Support for electricity bills

![Power lines in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4928,h_2772,x_0,y_318/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1000314630b5dafc8612/1661689303)

Open image viewer

Power lines in Espoo. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

The government is planning new support for people. Helsingin Sanomat reports that the support is based on, among other things, electricity bills and income.

A person can get support if their electricity bill is much bigger than before. The new support is meant to help the people whom the rise in electricity prices hurts the most.

The government is only planning the support so far, and there is not yet very precise information about it.

## Nurses

![Person in a wheelchair looks out from a balcony.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_215/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99376962f66073e735e/1661834036)

More nurses are needed in care homes for the elderly.

Finland needs 3,400 more nurses by spring so that the law can be implemented. The law says that in April there must be more elderly care nurses in the institutions than now. Every elderly person must have at least 0.7 nurses on average.

The government is now considering whether the law should be brought into use later than April.
