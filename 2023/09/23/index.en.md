## Balancing the economy

![Riikka Purra in Ykkösaamu on 23.09.2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5054,h_2842,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1176061650e96a73b46f/1695455584)

Finance Minister Riikka Purra was a guest on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

There may need to be changes to the budget in Finland. This is what Finance Minister **Riikka Purra** says. Purra was a guest on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme on Saturday.

Purra says that balancing the economy may need more measures. A big reason is, for example, the rise in interest rates. State expenditure grows, because interest rates rise. Also, the state will get less money from taxes than was estimated earlier.

Finance Minister Purra says that new measures are going to be considered at the Ministry of Finance. The government will decide later on possible new savings or other measures.

The Centre Party has named a presidential candidate for the party. The Centre's presidential candidate is the governor of the Bank of Finland, **Olli Rehn**. The Centre's extraordinary party congress decided on the matter on Saturday.

Olli Rehn is also the presidential candidate of an electoral association, that is, he had enough supporters to stand as a presidential candidate even without a party.

The first round of the presidential election is at the end of January.

## Trade unions' industrial action

![The head office of the Industrial Union in Hakaniemi, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114288064b13de96ded0/1689340269)

Open image viewer

The Industrial Union's building in Hakaniemi, Helsinki. Photo: Joel Peltonen / Yle

Trade unions are organising industrial action next week. The trade unions oppose the government's labour market policy.

The Industrial Union, the trade union Pro and the Electricians' Union are organising work stoppages, walk-outs and demonstrations on Wednesday at several workplaces in Satakunta and Varsinais-Suomi. For example, employees can stop work for a while during the day or leave work earlier than normal.

Some trade unions already said on Friday that they intend to organise demonstrations next week. The trade unions do not want the government to weaken the position of the trade unions or of workers.

## Ground collapse in Sweden

![Aerial image of a road collapse on the E6 highway in Stenungsund, Sweden.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1999,h_1124,x_0,y_100/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1176070650ea1f90c92b/1695458356)

Open image viewer

The road broke in many places because of the ground collapse. Photo: Mikael Berglund

A big ground collapse has happened in Sweden. The ground collapse happened at night on a motorway in western Sweden. The place of the collapse is about 50 kilometres north of the city of Gothenburg.

Ground about 500 metres long fell away from under the motorway. A few cars and 1 bus drove into the collapse. 3 people were taken to hospital.

The Swedish police are investigating the case. Blasting work has been done near the collapse site. The Swedish police are investigating, for example, whether the blasts may have caused the collapse.
