# Friday 29.11.2019 (radio)

Paatero has resigned as a minister. The theme of the Presidential Palace reception is knowledge. State prizes were awarded. A camera follows everyday life in the Mustola cowshed.

## Minister's resignation

![Sirpa Paatero](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11094407)

Open image viewer

Sirpa Paatero. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Minister Sirpa Paatero has left the government.

Prime Minister Antti Rinne said today that Paatero has asked to resign as minister. Paatero has also taken 2 weeks of sick leave.

Paatero is resigning because of the dispute that started over Posti's collective agreements. Prime Minister Rinne says that Paatero did not say clearly enough to Posti's management that Posti's workers must not be moved to a cheaper collective agreement.

The state owns Posti. Paatero's job was to supervise state companies.

## Presidential Palace reception

![Guests at the Palace reception.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4176,h_2347,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-317340584715aa478f6/1481053677)

Guests at the President's Independence Day reception. Photo: Yle / Kalevi Rytkölä

The theme of this year's President's Independence Day celebrations is knowledge and social debate.

President Niinistö explained the theme. He said that today people can get more information than ever before. However, more half-truths and lies have come into social debate.

The food served at the Palace reception reflects the environment. The menu includes pike and roach, chanterelles and broad beans, redcurrant and lingonberry. The drinks include vegan wine and organic tea, for example.

This year, 1,700 guests have been invited to the Presidential Palace Independence Day reception.

## State prizes for art

![Ailu Valle](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6174615de0eda4c3838/1575023094)

Rapper Ailu Valle. Photo: Linda Tammela / Yle

State prizes for art have been awarded. This year, 14 artists from different fields received a state prize.

For example, writer Eeva Kilpi received the state prize for literature, and musician Paula Vesala received the state prize for music. Rapper Ailu Valle received the state prize for children's culture, and critic Kalle Kinnunen received the state prize for film art.

The state prize for children's culture is 30,000 euros. The other state prizes for art are 14,000 euros.

## Mustola cowshed

![A cow looking straight at the camera.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_228/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6168625ddf7cf962526/1574927791)

70 cows live in the Mustola cowshed. Photo: Elisa Kinnunen / Yle

On Yle's website you can watch a live broadcast from the cowshed at Mustola farm in Puolanka. 70 milking cows live in the Mustola cowshed.

The live broadcast shows how the cows are fed, looked after and milked. The cowshed also has various devices, such as a milking robot and a machine that scratches the cows.

Last night a new calf was born in the cowshed. A second calf could also be born at any time.

The cowshed has 2 cameras, which the viewer can turn to choose the direction they look in. One camera films the cows and the other films the calves.

You can watch what happens in the cowshed until Tuesday.
