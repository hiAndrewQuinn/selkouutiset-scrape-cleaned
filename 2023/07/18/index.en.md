Parliament will get a report in the autumn on how the government will improve equality and reduce discrimination. A new working group will start preparing the report.

The report will be given to Parliament at the same time as Parliament votes on whether it trusts the government.

People have talked a lot lately about what the government does. The reason is the old racist speeches and writings of the Finns Party ministers.

## Electricity price

![Mika Potinkara, managing director of Hepokullan Lämpö, looks at electricity meters for individual flats. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_305/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114229764b0009865a7b/1689256179)

Electricity is not expected to rise sharply next winter.

Last winter electricity was so expensive that many homes had trouble paying their electricity bills. Some homes got support from the state to pay their electricity bills.

Now it is estimated that next autumn and winter the electricity price will be clearly lower than a year earlier.

## Café prices

![Berry pies at the Naapantuura café in Kouvola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5789,h_3256,x_0,y_441/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114165064aeb8090e6d4/1689172488)

Open image viewer

Prices in cafés are more expensive than before. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

Café prices have risen. Prices may have risen by as much as 20 percent in a year.

Yle asked cafés in different parts of Finland what their products cost. For example, the most expensive piece of cake costs almost 9 euros.

Prices have risen because cafés' costs have grown. Raw materials are more expensive than before. In addition, the electricity price and workers' wages have risen.

The situation is hard for cafés. Prices cannot rise as much as costs, because then there would be fewer customers.

## Para athletics World Championships

![Amanda Kotaja celebrates her world champion title in Paris. Next to her is Tatyana McFadden of the United States.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4154,h_2336,x_0,y_116/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114313764b2957510314/1689425338)

Open image viewer

Amanda Kotaja (on the left) is the world champion. Photo: Getty Images

Finland did well at the para athletics World Championships, that is, the world championship competitions. Finland's disabled athletes won 6 medals in total.

Finland's track athletes did the best. Track athletes compete to see who is the fastest in a wheelchair over different distances.

Track athletes won Finland 4 medals. One of them was a gold medal, which was won by Amanda Kotaja.

Finland also won medals in the women's javelin throw and the men's shot put.

The para athletics World Championships were held in Paris.

## Heat

People in the heat in Rome. Photo: AOP

The World Meteorological Organization warns about heat in different parts of the world.

Intense heat increases heart attacks and deaths. It is especially dangerous for health that the temperature does not drop much even at night.

This week, temperatures above 40 degrees can be measured in different parts of the world.
