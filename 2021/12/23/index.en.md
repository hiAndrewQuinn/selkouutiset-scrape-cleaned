# Thursday 23.12.2021 (radio)

You can make a Christmas visit to older people. A new record in corona infections again. Strict corona restrictions in the regions. Candidate lists for the regional elections published. Travelling for Christmas in poor driving conditions. Weather forecast for Christmas Eve.

## Christmas visit to a care home

![A care home resident picks a Christmas card from a basket.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10561196)

Open image viewer

The corona time has increased loneliness among older people. Photo: Juulia Tillaeus / Yle

Many older people's care homes allow relatives to visit older people this Christmas.

The care homes remind that only healthy people may come to visit. In addition, the visitor should do a quick corona test and wear a face mask.

The visits of relatives are seen as important for the mental wellbeing of older people, because during the corona time the loneliness of many older people has grown.

## A record number of infections

![People queuing for a quick corona test at Kamppi square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_555/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88810361aa687e5d768/1640272609)

In Finland there has once again been a new record in the number of corona infections.

Today 3000 new infections were reported. That is the largest number of infections in one day during the whole epidemic.

In two weeks about 26 000 infections have been found. That is just under 9000 more than in the two previous weeks.

## Regions tighten restrictions

![People walking at Itäkeskus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4512,h_2538,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-877134618927eaeb54b/1636385795)

Open image viewer

Banning public events closes cinemas. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

In many areas new, strict restrictions have been ordered because of corona. The restrictions cannot be got around with a corona pass.

For example, in Pirkanmaa, Uusimaa and Satakunta all public events are banned.

In addition, for example sports facilities, hobby facilities and public areas of shopping centres are closed. However, the facilities may be used for supervised hobby activities for children and young people.

## Candidate lists for the regional elections published

![Sign on the roof of the main health station in Kuopio that reads Social and Health Centre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_321/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88752861a9c696944c4/1638517863)

Open image viewer

The organising of health care, social care and rescue services is moving from the municipalities to the regions. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

The candidate lists for the regional elections are published today. The candidates get their voting numbers at the same time.

Finland's first regional elections will be held on 23 January. Then representatives will be chosen to the regional councils.

The regional councils are responsible for organising health care, social care and rescue services. From the beginning of 2023 the task moves from the municipalities to the regions.

In Helsinki the city will also organise social and health services in the future. That is why Helsinki residents do not vote in the regional elections.

## Poor driving conditions in Christmas traffic

![traffic in winter weather](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10590139)

Open image viewer

Driving conditions were poor across the whole country today. Photo: AOP

Today has been the busiest day of Christmas traffic. On the main roads traffic got busier after midday.

Across the whole country people drove in poor driving conditions. Because the weather has warmed, the roads have been slippery. In addition, there is frost snow in the air that swirls, so visibility may be poor.

There have also been many passengers on the trains. VR says that the train carriages have had more intensive cleaning, and there is also a mask requirement on the trains.
