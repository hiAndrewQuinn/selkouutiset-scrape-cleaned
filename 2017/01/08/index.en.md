# Sunday 8.1.2017 (radio)

Sweden is worried about security. Many soldiers have died in Israel. The Nokia name returns to the mobile phone market. Top ice hockey player Patrik Laine was injured. Next week it will be warm again.

## Prime Minister worried about Sweden's security

![Half-length picture of Löfven in a dark suit. The Swedish flag in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9175476)

Swedish Prime Minister Stefan Löfven Photo: Maurizio Gambarini / EPA

The Swedish Prime Minister is worried about the country's security. Prime Minister Stefan Löfven says that the biggest threats are Russia, terrorism and climate change. The Prime Minister mentions, for example, strange submarines in Swedish waters and the growth of military activity in the Baltic Sea.

The Prime Minister also sees criminal gangs as threats.

The Swedish government will present a new security strategy on Monday.

## Soldiers died in a terror attack in Israel

Photo: Reuters

A truck has driven into soldiers in the old city of Jerusalem in Israel. 4 soldiers died and 15 people were injured in the attack. The soldiers were getting off a bus when the attack happened. The driver of the truck was shot. The police consider the act terrorism.

For more than a year, Palestinians have carried out attacks against Israelis with vehicles and knives. 40 Israelis have died in the attacks. During the same time, more than 200 Palestinians have been shot in incidents.

## Nokia phone made public

![Nokia 6 smartphone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9392276)

Open image viewer

The Nokia 6 phone.  Photo: HMD Global

The Nokia name is returning to the mobile phone market.  The Finnish company HMD Global has announced its first Nokia-brand phone. The Nokia 6 is a smartphone and at first it will go on sale only in China.

In China there will soon be almost 600 million people who use smartphones.  The company says that it plans to bring even more new phones onto the market during this year.

The Nokia company no longer makes mobile phones. The company focuses on building telephone networks.

## Finnish top ice hockey player injured

See the tackle yourself by clicking the picture. Photo: Viasat

Finland's hottest ice hockey player Patrik Laine has been injured in an NHL ice hockey league game. Patrik Laine, a player for the Winnipeg Jets ice hockey team, was tackled hard in the middle of the rink and he had to leave the game. Laine and another player apparently hit their heads together. Doctors are now examining whether Laine got a concussion in the tackle.

18-year-old Laine is playing his 1st season in the NHL professional league and is Winnipeg's best forward.

## Warm weather returns to Finland

![frozen flower](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9384790)

Open image viewer

Freezing cold is changing to mild weather.  Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The weather is changing sharply in Finland. This week there were 20-40 degrees of frost all over Finland, but now the weather is getting clearly milder. In the next few days it will be warm again and the temperature will be above zero in almost the whole country. In the south there may be sleet or rain on Tuesday and Wednesday. At the same time, the snow will disappear from the ground. Such big changes in temperature are unusual.
