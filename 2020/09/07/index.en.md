# Monday 7.9.2020 (radio)

Exports decrease. Hand sanitiser is asked about more than before. Tyre safety week has started. 1 new corona death in Finland. Weather forecast.

## Exports decrease

![Paper rolls at a paper mill.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10494290)

Open image viewer

Paper rolls at a paper mill. Photo: Helena Rosenblad / Yle

Finland's exports have decreased because of the corona situation.

Goods exports fell by about 14 percent in July compared with last year's July. The trade in oil products, paper and transport vehicles from Finland to other countries has decreased.

Many export companies have cut staff, because exports are falling and companies are doing worse than before. This year the technology industry may lose about 10,000 jobs.

## Hand sanitiser is asked about more than before

![Hand sanitiser at a Prisma store in Kaleva.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5149,h_2896,x_0,y_265/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7034435f2158e306135/1596021455)

Open image viewer

Hand sanitiser is now everywhere, for example at the shop door. Photo: Antti Eintola / Yle

More and more calls about hand sanitiser are coming to the Poison Information Centre. Callers often ask about children under 6 years old. For example, a child has put a little hand sanitiser in its mouth. This is usually not dangerous. Some adults have drunk hand sanitiser on purpose. That is dangerous.

Finns use much more hand sanitiser than before. That is why more questions are coming to the Poison Information Centre than before.

There have been more than twice as many questions as last year.

## It is tyre safety week

![Measuring the depth of tyre tread grooves.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6280321)

Open image viewer

A tyre must have a good surface. You must not drive on worn tyres. Photo: Paula Hiljanen / Yle

This week is tyre safety week. During tyre safety week, drivers are reminded that cars must have good tyres.

Worn tyres are slippery and therefore dangerous. Worn car tyres can cause accidents and deaths.

Because of the corona situation, there will be no traditional tyre checks this year, where drivers are advised. The police will still monitor traffic and car tyres, however.
