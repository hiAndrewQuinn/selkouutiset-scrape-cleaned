## Taxis and taxes

![Taxis at a Triplan taxi stand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3651,h_2053,x_0,y_380/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9582906288c0e5c3b1d/1659440370)

Open image viewer

Many taxis have been caught, among other things, for tax fraud. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

The Tax Administration has uncovered tax fraud in the taxi industry worth millions of euros. Tax fraud means, for example, that a cash payment is not recorded as a sale, or the taximeter is used incorrectly.

In intensive inspections almost 9 million euros of income outside of the accounts was found. In addition, other tax errors worth more than 4 million euros were found.

Undeclared wages worth almost 3 million euros came to light. Undeclared wages means that no taxes or employer contributions are paid on them.

## Abortion

![The government presented its budget proposal for next year to Parliament on 20 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_197/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10104076329a1df8062d/1664361290)

Open image viewer

Parliament has approved a new Abortion Act, which makes it easier to get an abortion. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Parliament has approved the new Abortion Act by 125 votes to 41. 1 voted blank and 32 MPs were absent.

The approved proposal was based on the citizens' initiative Oma tahto 2020.

Two doctors' statements are no longer needed for a termination of pregnancy. In addition, up to week 12 of pregnancy, the pregnant woman's own request is enough for an abortion.

## Rental housing

![Housing sale, empty flat, rental flat](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11046609)

Open image viewer

More and more people live in rented housing, although living in owner-occupied housing is still more common. Photo: Karoliina Marttala / Yle

The number of tenants has exceeded the 1.5 million mark for the first time.

Statistics Finland says that at the end of last year 28 percent of the housing population lived in rental flats. The share is 4 percentage points higher than in 2010.

Since 2018 the number of tenants has grown by more than 100,000.

However, owner-occupation is still the most common form of housing in Finland.

## Health fees

![YTHS](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5395038)

Open image viewer

YTHS looks after the health of university students. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

Many students have forgotten to pay the health care fee. Kela has sent a payment reminder to about 46,000 students.

The reminder is sent to those who registered as present at the latest by the end of September. The reminder is sent to those whose payment has been forgotten.

The health care fee for university students covers all the services of the Student Health Service Foundation for the student.

## Ateneum

![Ateneum Art Museum.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_245/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-933730623ee53e6ab28/1648289396)

Open image viewer

Ateneum has received a one million euro donation through a will. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

The Ateneum Art Museum in Helsinki has received a rare donation.

A private Finnish person has bequeathed over one million euros to the museum. The donation will be used for exhibitions and museum activities, in line with the donor's wish.

Museum director Marja Sakari says that the donation is unique in Ateneum's history.

Ateneum has been closed for renovation since March. The museum will reopen early next year.
