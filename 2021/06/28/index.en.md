## Finland's corona situation is getting worse

![Finnish supporters in the stands at the European Championship match against Russia on 16.6.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82601160d9a9b92af5a/1624877568)

Open image viewer

Finnish football tourists in St. Petersburg. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

Experts are worried about Finland's corona situation. A lot of coronavirus infections have come to Finland from St. Petersburg, Russia.

The coronavirus infections came with Finnish football tourists. They were in St. Petersburg to watch the football European Championship matches.

Some of the football tourists' coronavirus infections are of the delta variant of the coronavirus. The delta variant spreads very easily.

The delta variant of the coronavirus can also spread to a person who has received a coronavirus vaccination. However, the coronavirus vaccination gives protection against serious illness.

Authorities say that everyone should go for a corona test after the trip to St. Petersburg. After the trip it is also good to stay at home in quarantine for 2 weeks.

## Swedish government resigns

![Swedish Prime Minister Stefan Löfven](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11999952)

Open image viewer

Swedish Prime Minister Stefan Löfven. Photo: Stina Stjernkvist / AFP

There is a difficult political situation in Sweden, Finland's neighbouring country.

The Swedish government resigned on Monday. The government no longer had the support of the majority of the Swedish parliament.

Now the Swedish parties are negotiating about a new government. The negotiations will show whether Prime Minister Stefan Löfven can put together a new government or whether the Swedish prime minister will change.

Löfven is the leader of the Swedish Social Democrats. He has been Prime Minister of Sweden since 2014.

## Said Ahmed gets into Parliament

![City councillor Suldaan Said Ahmed, Helsinki, Left Alliance.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_171/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-807915900bec679827/1553521725)

Open image viewer

Suldaan Said Ahmed. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Finland gets its first MP with Somali background. This is the Left Alliance's Suldaan Said Ahmed.

Suldaan Said Ahmed will come to Parliament in place of Paavo Arhinmäki. Arhinmäki moves to work as deputy mayor of Helsinki.

Suldaan Said Ahmed wants to work, for example, against social exclusion.

## Helsinki Pride begins

![Pride march participants gathering at Senate Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10854729)

Open image viewer

Helsinki Pride 2 years ago. Now the event is online because of corona. Photo: Minna Rinta-Tassi / Yle

The Helsinki Pride event has begun.

Helsinki Pride is Finland's largest human rights event. Its theme is, for example, the rights of homosexual people.

Pride is celebrating an anniversary this year. Homosexual acts have not been a crime in Finland for 50 years.

Helsinki Pride lasts a week. The event is mainly online because of corona.

The patron of Helsinki Pride is Prime Minister Sanna Marin.
