## The government discussed

The government parties discussed the government's problems and rules of the game on Tuesday. In the government, especially RKP and the Finns Party have poor relations. RKP and the Finns Party do not trust each other.

Prime Minister Petteri Orpo says that the government parties had good and honest discussions on Tuesday. According to Orpo, the government now wants to focus on policy.

Last week the Finns Party minister Vilhelm Junnila resigned from his post as minister. It led to a dispute in the government.

## NATO Secretary General

![NATO chief Jens Stoltenberg at a press conference during a military exercise in Lithuania.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_317/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113780464a3d1cae924f/1688457971)

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg. Photo: EPA-EFE

Jens Stoltenberg will continue in the job of Secretary General of the NATO military alliance. The Secretary General is the leader of NATO.

Stoltenberg will continue as Secretary General at least until October 2024. Stoltenberg has been NATO Secretary General since 2014.

During Stoltenberg's time, NATO has changed because of the threat from Russia. NATO's most important task is to defend its member countries.

The 64-year-old Stoltenberg is Norwegian. Finland joined NATO last April.

## Finland's success

![A sign on the floor that says "kassa" (checkout).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_321/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107439063ef6b93de006/1676635280)

Finland's companies are not doing as well as a year ago. Photo: Sakari Piippo / Yle

Finland is doing worse than before in competition with other countries. This is what a new study says.

More than 60 countries took part in the study. Finland was eleventh. A year ago Finland was eighth.

The comparison looked at, for example, a country's economy, companies, administration and many other things. Finnish companies in particular did worse than a year ago.

The best country in the study was Denmark. The second best was Iceland.

## Cold lakes

![People on the landing stage of a beach drying themselves.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3824,h_2152,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113725864a143ce699c9/1688291125)

Open image viewer

Lake water is now quite cold. Photo: Miki Wallenius / Yle

Strong wind and cool weather have made the lakes colder.

At the weekend, the lake temperature was over 22 degrees in many places. Now cool weather, strong wind and rain have lowered the temperature of the lakes by many degrees.

Strong wind mixes the lake water, and cold water rises from the bottom to the surface.
