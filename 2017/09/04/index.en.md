# Monday 4.9.2017 (radio)

The IT security of many Finnish companies is weak. A suspicion of crime has been raised against the state prosecutor. Sauna heaters must be made safer. The Harvey storm was expensive. The Ranua polar bear has got a name.

## Companies' IT security

![Computer keys](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9724428)

Open image viewer

Criminals fish for business secrets, for example through phone, email and social media. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Many Finnish companies do not know how to protect themselves against corporate espionage.

The IT security company Silverskin tested how Finnish companies protect themselves. The test included, for example, listed companies, small companies and public administration.

Just over 30 percent of the companies got a poor grade in the test. About the same number had IT security at a good level.

In the test, attempts were made to find out business secrets through phone, email and social media. In addition, the premises of some companies were broken into.

The methods were the same as those that criminals use too.

## Suspicion of crime against the state prosecutor

![State Prosecutor Matti Nissinen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9235472)

Open image viewer

State Prosecutor Matti Nissinen. Photo: Juha Kivioja / Yle

State Prosecutor Matti Nissinen is suspected of a crime. He has been charged with breaching his official duties.

Nissinen ordered training for prosecutors from a company led by Nissinen's brother. The training cost over 70,000 euros.

Nissinen says that he has been careless. However, he says that he has not committed a crime.

## Sauna heater safety

![Electric sauna heater](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9801791)

Open image viewer

An electric sauna heater heats a sauna in an apartment block. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The Accident Investigation Board says that the safety of sauna heaters must be improved.

The Accident Investigation Board says that heater manufacturers must add more safety features to heaters. The instructions for using the heaters must also be made clearer.

The Accident Investigation Board's recommendations are due to a fire that broke out in Vuosaari, Helsinki, last year. The fire started from an electric sauna heater.

4 people died in the fire.

## Damage from Storm Harvey

![Buildings destroyed by Harvey.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9807503)

Open image viewer

More than 200,000 homes were damaged in Storm Harvey. Photo: Malcolm McClendon / EPA-EFE

Storm Harvey caused big economic damage in the USA. The storm caused damage worth at least 150 billion euros.

The authorities say that more than 200,000 homes have been damaged.

Almost 40,000 people are still in emergency shelter in the states of Texas and Louisiana.

## Ranua polar bear cub

![Ranua polar bear cub Sisu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9814235)

Open image viewer

The Ranua polar bear has got the name Sisu. Photo: Ranua zoo

The polar bear cub of the Ranua zoo in Southern Lapland has been given a name. The cub's name is Sisu.

The cub's name was decided by a public competition. More than 18,000 suggestions came into the competition.

Almost a hundred people suggested the name Sisu. Popular names were also Lumi, Sulo and Pyry.
