# Saturday 14.3.2020 (radio)

The number of coronavirus cases is growing. The government is considering more measures. Countries are closing borders. Finnair is cancelling flights. A state of emergency has begun in the USA.

## The number of coronavirus cases is growing

The number of coronavirus cases is growing.

There are now more than 220 people in Finland who have been found to have the coronavirus infection. Most coronavirus cases are in the Helsinki area.

In Tampere the upper secondary schools will close on Monday. Remote teaching will be arranged for upper secondary pupils. In Tampere, remote teaching is offered to lower secondary pupils if they do not want to go to school. Primary schools in Tampere will carry on as normal.

In Finland only a few schools have been closed because of the coronavirus situation.

## The government's five-person group discusses more measures

![The government's five-person group](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11127510)

Open image viewer

The government's five-person group: Minister of Education Li Andersson, Minister of Finance Katri Kulmuni, Prime Minister Sanna Marin, Minister of Justice Anna-Maja Henriksson and Minister of the Interior Maria Ohisalo. Photo: Government

The leading five-person group of the Finnish government, that is, five government members from different government parties, will hold a meeting on Sunday about the coronavirus situation. Prime Minister **Sanna Marin** says that they will discuss which new measures should be introduced in Finland because of the coronavirus.

The whole government will meet on Monday to discuss what measures can be used to prevent the coronavirus from spreading.

## Borders are being closed because of the coronavirus

![On the Juutinrauma bridge between Denmark and Sweden there is no longer any traffic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11257375)

Open image viewer

It is quiet on the Juutinrauma bridge between Denmark and Sweden. Photo: Kirsi Heikel / Yle

Denmark has closed the country's borders to travellers because of the coronavirus. Denmark's own citizens who return to Denmark may enter. Foreigners who work in Denmark can also get into Denmark.

Norway has also partly closed its border. Only those travellers who have a place where they can be in quarantine for 2 weeks may enter Norway.

Other countries are also restricting the entry of foreigners into the country. Poland, Ukraine, Cyprus and Russia are restricting which foreigners may enter the country.

## Finnair cancels more flights

![A timetable display showing arriving and departing aircraft at Helsinki-Vantaa airport on 4 March 2020](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11249496)

Open image viewer

The cancellations of flights are shown on a display. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The airline Finnair is cancelling more flights.

Finnair is suspending flights to Denmark, Norway, Russia, Prague and Poland until the end of March. Finnair is cancelling flights because there are no passengers on the flights and because many countries have closed their borders.

Because of the coronavirus, travelling has decreased a lot.

## The USA has declared a state of emergency

![Donald Trump holds a press conference at the White House](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5420,h_3049,x_0,y_279/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6524465e69a0a14cbe4/1583980792)

Open image viewer

Donald Trump. Photo: Doug Mills / EPA

US President \*\*Donald Trump \*\*has declared a state of emergency in the USA because of the coronavirus. With the state of emergency, the USA can quickly give money for the handling of the coronavirus situation, for example to hospitals.

Americans are offered, for example, food aid, free coronavirus tests and pay if they have to take sick leave.
