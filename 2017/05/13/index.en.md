# Saturday 13.5.2017 (radio)

Finland's former president Mauno Koivisto has died. Koivisto was president of Finland for 12 years. As president, Koivisto decided on the return migration of the Ingrian Finns. Mauno Koivisto has been remembered in many ways.

Finland's former president Mauno Koivisto has died. Koivisto was 93 years old. He died on Friday evening in Helsinki.

Mauno Koivisto was president of Finland for 12 years. Koivisto's time as president began in 1982 and ended in 1994.

Mauno Koivisto was Finland's 9th president. Koivisto's party was the SDP, the Social Democratic Party.

## Koivisto got ahead by studying

![Mauno Koivisto playing volleyball](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9611538)

Mauno Koivisto played volleyball as a hobby. Photo: Kimmo Mäntylä / Lehtikuva

Mauno Koivisto was born in Turku in 1923. The family was poor, and Mauno Koivisto's mother died young.

The young Mauno Koivisto worked in the port of Turku. After the working day he went to evening school. Koivisto passed the matriculation exam, and he became a teacher.

Later Mauno Koivisto moved from the Turku area to Helsinki. His wife Tellervo Koivisto and daughter Assi came too.

In Helsinki Mauno Koivisto became a banker and a politician. Before Koivisto became president, he was, for example, governor of the Bank of Finland and prime minister of Finland.

Many Finns remember Mauno Koivisto as a president who was an ordinary Finnish man. Koivisto's fringe was not always neatly combed, and Koivisto liked to play volleyball.

## Koivisto decided on the return migration of the Ingrian Finns

![People in a classroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5626120)

Ingrian Finns studying Finnish in 2010. Photo: Yle

Mauno Koivisto is also remembered for the return migration of the Ingrian Finns. In the early 1990s the president decided that the Ingrian Finns may return to Finland.

The Ingrian Finns are people who live in Russia. They have Finnish family roots. The families of the Ingrian Finns moved to Russia about 400 years ago.

The return migration of the Ingrian Finns continued until 2016. Over 30,000 Ingrian Finns moved from Russia to Finland as returning migrants.

## Koivisto has been remembered in many ways

![Flags at half mast on Luotsikatu in Helsinki on Saturday morning in memory of former President of the Republic Mauno Koivisto, who died on Friday evening](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9611507)

Finns are sad because of President Koivisto's death. On Saturday flags flew at half mast all over the country, that is, the flags were raised only halfway up the flagpole.

Finland's current president Sauli Niinistö spoke on Saturday morning about President Koivisto's importance. Niinistö said that all Finns thank Koivisto for his work for Finland.

Mauno Koivisto has been remembered in many ways. For example, at the Ice Hockey World Championships on Saturday, Finland's players had black mourning bands on their sleeves.
