# Wednesday 26.2.2020 (radio)

A coronavirus case has been found in Helsinki. Two other suspected coronavirus cases are also being examined in Finland. The police feel that their work safety has got worse. Thursday and Friday are sunny frosty days.

## Coronavirus case in Helsinki

![Huslab Tullinpuomi sign in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11182299)

A coronavirus infection was confirmed at HUS, the Hospital District of Helsinki and Uusimaa. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The coronavirus continues to spread.

Now a coronavirus case has also been found in Finland. The case is in Helsinki.

The patient is a Finnish woman of working age. The patient is in good condition, but has a fever.

The Finnish woman caught the coronavirus infection in Milan, in Northern Italy. Coronavirus cases have increased quickly in Italy.

Until now there has been only one coronavirus patient in Finland. He was a Chinese tourist who was travelling in Lapland. The patient already got better at the beginning of February.

## Suspected coronavirus cases elsewhere in Finland

![Passengers at Helsinki railway station in February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11228294)

A passenger on the Jyväskylä train. Photo: Hanna Matikainen / Lehtikuva

Two other cases have also been examined in Finland, where a coronavirus infection was suspected.

In Jyväskylä, a train passenger was suspected of having a coronavirus infection. On Tuesday he travelled by train from Helsinki airport to Jyväskylä. VR took the train out of service for cleaning. On Wednesday evening it was reported that the train passenger did not have the coronavirus.

A coronavirus infection is also suspected in Eastern Finland. The South Savo hospital district says that the patient has flu symptoms. There is a small chance that it is the coronavirus. The matter will be clear on Thursday.

## Symptoms of the coronavirus

![A woman coughing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11212209)

Open image viewer

Coughing and fever are symptoms of the coronavirus. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

The coronavirus does not spread very easily.

The coronavirus can pass to another person when a coronavirus patient coughs or sneezes so that the coronavirus gets into the air. Many days can pass after the infection before the patient gets symptoms.

The coronavirus causes symptoms similar to influenza. Symptoms include, for example, coughing and fever.

Information about the coronavirus in plain Finnish is available on the website of THL, the National Institute for Health and Welfare.

## Police work safety

![A police officer from Lahti in a stock photo at the yard of a police station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10082284)

Open image viewer

Police work is not always safe. Photo: Emilia Malin / Yle

The police say that the work safety of police officers has got worse, especially in Helsinki. The Police Unions asked the police about this.

In Helsinki more than 40 percent of police officers said that their work safety is worse than before. Violence against police has increased.

The differences between regions are big. In Northern Finland only 20 percent of police officers said that work safety has got worse and violence against police has increased.
