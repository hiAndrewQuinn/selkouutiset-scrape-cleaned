# Sunday 24.6.2018 (radio)

EU leaders have met in Brussels. Turks have voted in elections. Women can drive a car in Saudi Arabia. Summer came early to nature in Finland this year.

## EU leaders met

![Migrants in a boat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8271322)

Open image viewer

EU leaders are trying to agree what to do about people arriving in Europe across the Mediterranean. Photo: Alessandro di Meo / EPA

Finland's Prime Minister Juha Sipilä has taken part in a meeting of EU leaders in Brussels.

Leaders of 16 EU countries took part in the meeting. They discussed immigration and asylum matters.

Many people try to come to Europe across the Mediterranean. EU countries disagree about what should be done with these people.

The official meeting of all EU countries on asylum matters will be held on Thursday.

## Turks voted

![Street view.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10270342)

Open image viewer

Election advertisement of Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. Photo: Thomas Lecomte

Turks have voted in elections. Presidential and parliamentary elections were held in Turkey on Sunday.

Before the election, President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan and his party, the AKP, did well in opinion polls. The polls predict that Erdoğan may win the election.

After the election, a new law will come into force in Turkey. The law gives the president more power. Erdoğan's opponents say that Erdoğan wants to take all the power for himself.

Turkey's political situation is difficult. In 2016 a group of Turkish army soldiers tried to take power away from President Erdoğan. The attempt did not succeed, and many of Erdoğan's opponents have ended up in prison.

## Saudi women drive cars

![Female car passenger](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10241639)

Open image viewer

A new era has begun for Saudi Arabian women. Photo: Pekka Koli / Yle

Women can now drive cars in Saudi Arabia. The ban on women driving ended on Sunday.

On the streets of Saudi Arabia on Sunday there were many police officers watching the traffic. Some police officers gave roses to women drivers.

Women were allowed to drive because Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman is reforming Saudi Arabia. Saudi Arabia was the last country in the world where women were not allowed to drive a car.

## Summer came early

![Female Small Tortoiseshell](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9375473)

Open image viewer

It is not usual for the Small Tortoiseshell butterfly to fly in Finnish nature as early as June. Photo: Juha Jantunen

Summer came early to nature in Finland this year.

It became hot in May, and the hot weather lasted a long time. That is why you can now see butterflies and flowers that belong to late summer.

Researcher Kimmo Saarinen says that, for example, a lot of Small Tortoiseshell butterflies are flying now. The Small Tortoiseshell is Finland's most common butterfly. It is unusual to see Small Tortoiseshells as early as June.

![rosebay willowherb](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10265566)

Open image viewer

Rosebay willowherb is flowering early this year. Photo: Jouko Tykkyläinen

Of the plants, rosebay willowherb is already flowering. Saarinen says that the flowers of rosebay willowherb are usually a sign that summer is turning towards its end.

However, summer is not over yet. Researcher Saarinen says that, for example, the very biggest butterflies are only just coming.
