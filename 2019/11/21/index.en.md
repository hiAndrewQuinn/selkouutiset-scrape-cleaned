# Thursday 21.11.2019 (radio)

A working group will start looking for a solution to the postal dispute. Growth in the world economy is slowing down. Skier Juha Mieto has turned 70. A crow feels unwell if it eats junk food.

## A working group is looking into the postal strike

Minister Sirpa Paatero set up a working group because of the postal strike. Photo: Pekka Tynell / YLE

An attempt is being made to end the postal strike. Minister Sirpa Paatero set up a working group on Thursday, with 4 people in it. They will start looking for a solution to the postal dispute.

All the members of the working group have a lot of experience of negotiations. They will investigate the postal dispute for at least the weekend.

The postal sector trade union and Posti's managers asked Minister Paatero for help in resolving the dispute. The state can intervene in Posti's affairs, because Posti is a state company.

The postal strike has lasted almost 2 weeks. Many Posti employees stopped work on Monday 11 November. Right now Posti is not able to deliver letters, for example.

## Economic growth slows in the world

![container port](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10612034)

Open image viewer

The pace of economic growth is slowing down. Photo: AOP

Growth in the world economy is slowing down. This is what the organisation of industrial countries, the OECD, says.

The OECD estimates that the world economy will grow by less than 3 percent next year.

Economic growth is slowing because of trade disputes. For example, the USA and China have imposed tariffs on each other's goods.

## Juha Mieto turned 70

![Juha Mieto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10670335)

Open image viewer

Juha Mieto. Photo: Yle / Jonna Karjalainen

Skier Juha Mieto has turned 70.

Mieto has won medals at the Olympics and at the world championships. Mieto's best competition time was about 40 years ago.

Finns remember the 1980 Olympics well. At that time, Juha Mieto lost the gold medal in the 50 kilometre cross-country race by one hundredth of a second.

Misfortune is also part of life, Mieto says. He is satisfied with his life and his great career.

## Junk food harms a crow

![a crow whose feathers have suffered from eating junk food.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4409,h_2480,x_0,y_193/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6142065dd65d402bc5d/1574415211)

The colour has gone from the crow's wing feathers because of junk food. Photo: Mika Rokka / Banja Media Oy

Junk food or fast food is unhealthy. Even a crow can feel unwell if it eats junk food.

The crow is a bird that often lives in the city. Crows can be seen, for example, near grill kiosks. There they eat food that people throw away.

And what happens to a crow when it eats junk food? The feathers on its wings go into poor condition. The feathers have colourless spots, and some feathers are missing completely. That is why the crow flies badly.

The crow's poor condition is due to junk food giving it too few vitamins. Healthy food is especially important for crow chicks. They do not develop properly if they only eat at grill kiosks.

A crow starts to feel better if it starts eating healthy food. A city crow's wing feathers recover when it moves to the countryside.
