More than 600 new coronavirus infections have been found in Finland. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that 632 coronavirus infections were found on Saturday.

In 2 weeks, there have been about 1,100 more new coronavirus infections than in the 2 weeks before that.

The rate of coronavirus infections in the Helsinki and Uusimaa area is double the average for the whole of Finland.

## Children's memory problems

![A child attends remote school using a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4908,h_2761,x_0,y_253/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6825505ed0adc5b0f02/1617966353)

Open image viewer

It can be harder to remember things in remote school than in a classroom. Photo: Petteri Juuti / Yle

The coronavirus time can strain children's memory. Children can also have memory problems, as adults do.

Because of the coronavirus, you sometimes have to remember a lot of things. For example, you have to remember to keep your distance and wash your hands. At school you also have to remember a lot of things that the teacher teaches.

Many children are in remote school. In remote school it can be hard for a child to remember things, because the teaching happens on a computer screen. Concentration can be difficult.

## Crisis preparedness

![Anita Korva doing her military service in the Kainuu Brigade. The picture shows her from behind, standing in the forest in full combat gear.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5037,h_2833,x_0,y_425/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5937545d8b27aead838/1569401372)

Crises are increasing Finns' wish to join the Reserve Officers' Association. Photo: Mikael Oivo / Yle

The coronavirus situation has increased Finns' wish to prepare for crises.

Many more Finns have joined the Reserve Officers' Association during the coronavirus time. Especially young people and women have joined the Reserve Officers' Association.

Cyber security threats have also increased Finns' wish to prepare for crises. A lot of new members also joined the Reserve Officers' Association during the Ukraine crisis.

## Sled dog teams

![Husky dogs at Huruksela in Kotka.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4216,h_2372,x_0,y_270/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-776218602fa6c7eba73/1613736032)

Open image viewer

Husky dogs at Huruksela in Kotka. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

New things are being done in the tourism sector because of the coronavirus situation. For example, sled dog teams have been brought from Rovaniemi to Kotka in Southern Finland. The dogs have no work in Lapland this winter, because there are no foreign tourists in Finland.

In Kotka, 2 routes have been built in the forest for the sled dog teams, where they will take people on rides during the winter holidays. 40 Husky dogs and their handlers have come from Rovaniemi to Kotka.

Sled dog rides interest people a lot. The handlers say that almost all the sled dog rides have been booked up.
