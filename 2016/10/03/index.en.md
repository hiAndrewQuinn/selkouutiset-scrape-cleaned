## Police: gun use prevented a serious act of violence

![Map showing the shooting place.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9205624)

Open image viewer

A man was beating a woman when police shot him on Sunday afternoon on Highway 4 in Orimattila. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

The police have given more details about the shooting at the weekend.

The police shot the man on Highway 4 in Orimattila, because the police wanted to save a human life.

The man had a woman in the car with him, and she was seriously injured in the situation. The man is suspected of causing the woman's injuries.

The woman is still in hospital. The prosecutor is investigating the police's use of firearms.

## Estonia finally has a president

![Kersti Kaljulaid.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9207146)

Open image viewer

Kersti Kaljulaid was elected president. Photo: Valda Kalnina / EPA

Estonia, Finland's southern neighbour, has finally got a new president.

Parliament voted 46-year-old Kersti Kaljulaid in as president. She was the only candidate.

The presidential elections failed twice before. None of the candidates got enough votes in the earlier votes.

## Colombians rejected the peace agreement

![People sit on white plastic chairs in a large room, where cardboard voting booths can be seen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9205600)

Open image viewer

The Colombian referendum rejected the peace agreement by a very narrow margin. Photo: Leonardo Munoz / EPA

Colombia did not get a peace agreement. The government and the Farc guerrilla group negotiated the peace agreement, but the Colombian people did not accept it.

Colombia had a referendum yesterday, and 50.2 per cent voted against the peace agreement.

In their view, the peace agreement is bad, because the fighters of the Farc guerrilla group will not be punished for their cruelties.

The Colombian government and the Farc guerrilla group have fought for over 50 years.

## Parents of children have many fears

![A child and an adult look at a river.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9205794)

Open image viewer

Many parents are afraid for their child. Photo: Tiia Hemminki / Yle

Here at home, raising children involves many fears.

Yle asked parents of children what they are afraid of.

Parents are most afraid that someone will do something bad to the child.

Parents are also afraid that the child will be bullied or will become seriously ill.

## Fishermen are decreasing – also at the herring markets

![Herring.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6682868)

Open image viewer

Herring is fished from the sea. Photo: Antti Haavisto / Yle

There are fewer fishermen in Finland than before.

This can also be seen at the herring market in Helsinki's Market Square, where fishermen sell herring seasoned and prepared in different ways.

This year there are fewer than 20 fishing boats at the herring market, while five years ago there were about 40 fishing boats.

Fisherman Hannu Hellsten from Pyhtää says that the number of fishing boats has fallen, because fishing does not bring in enough money.

## The Nobel Prizes have started

![Yoshinori Ohsumi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9206819)

The Nobel Prize in Medicine has been won by the Japanese scientist Yoshinori Ohsumi.

The Nobel Prize is one million Swedish kronor, or about 830,000 euros.

On Friday it will be revealed who wins the Nobel Peace Prize.
