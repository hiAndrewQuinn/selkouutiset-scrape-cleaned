# Tuesday 1.11.2016 (radio)

MP Teuvo Hakkarainen is going to court. The tax data has been published. The Pope is visiting Sweden. Money is being spent on pre-Christmas parties again. Snow will disrupt traffic on Wednesday.

## An MP has been charged

![The Finns Party's Teuvo Hakkarainen at a plenary session of parliament on 18 October 2013.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7175623)

Finns Party MP Teuvo Hakkarainen is going to court. Hakkarainen is being charged with incitement against a population group. Deputy Prosecutor General Raija Toiviainen has decided to bring charges over Hakkarainen's Facebook posts. The prosecutor thinks that the MP committed a crime with his post, which threatens and insults Muslims. The post was related to the Nice terror attack.

The Deputy Prosecutor General thinks that freedom of speech does not include the right to hate speech. MP Teuvo Hakkarainen says that he has not committed any crime.

## You earn well in the games industry

![Ilkka Paananen in 2014.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9264285)

Open image viewer

Ilkka Paananen. Photo: AOP

Finns' tax data has been published.

The tax data shows that you can earn good money in the games industry in Finland. In 2015 the directors of the games company Supercell got the highest pay, that is, earned income. For example, Supercell's CEO Ilkka Paananen earned more than 12 million euros.

Supercell also paid the most taxes of all companies. The company paid almost 176 million euros in taxes to the state.

Taxes are compulsory payments that are paid to the state and the municipalities. The money is used, for example, for education and health care.

## The Pope is in Sweden

![The Pope with his entourage in Lund, Sweden, on 31 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9263295)

Open image viewer

Pope Francis Photo: Stefan Lindblom / AOP

Pope Francis is visiting Sweden. A visit by the Pope, the highest leader of the Catholic Church, is rare, because the last time a pope visited the Nordic countries was about 30 years ago.

In Sweden the Pope is taking part in the 500th anniversary celebration of the Reformation.

The Reformation is an important thing in the Christian world. The German priest Martin Luther started the Reformation in the 16th century. As a result, the Catholic Church split and, for example, the Lutheran Church was born.

The Pope's visit is historic, because never before have Lutherans and Catholics celebrated the Reformation together.

## Money is being spent on pre-Christmas parties again

![pre-Christmas party old fire station ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5458188)

Open image viewer

At pre-Christmas parties people eat and drink at the employer's expense. Photo: YLE

Companies' own pre-Christmas parties are an important party tradition in Finland. Pre-Christmas parties are held at the beginning of December. Pre-Christmas parties are the most important sales period of the year for restaurants, because companies usually pay for the employees' food and drinks.

Companies have often saved money on pre-Christmas parties because of their poor financial situation. Now restaurants say that companies are again spending more money on pre-Christmas parties.
