# Thursday 13.10.2016 (radio)

The King of Thailand, Bhumibol, has died. American songwriter Bob Dylan wins the Nobel Prize in Literature. The terrorist group Boko Haram has released girls in Nigeria. The lingonberry is the most common berry in Finnish forests. Many Finnish churches are ringing bells for the victims of Aleppo in Syria.

## The King of Thailand has died

![Bhumibol Adulyadej](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9228521)

Open image viewer

The Thai royal family. King Bhumibol in the middle. Photo: Pongmanat Tasiri / EPA

The King of Thailand, Bhumibol, has died. Bhumibol was 88 years old. He was King of Thailand for about 70 years.

The Thai people are sad because of the king's death. People cried in the streets when the news of the king's death came. Thai officials began a year of mourning.

The new King of Thailand is Bhumibol's son, 63-year-old Vajiralongkorn.

## Bob Dylan wins the Nobel

![Bob Dylan](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9228099)

Open image viewer

Bob Dylan. Photo: Vi Khoa / EPA

American songwriter Bob Dylan wins the Nobel Prize in Literature. Dylan has written songs that have renewed poetry. That is what the Swedish Academy says, which decided on the prize.

Bob Dylan has been writing and performing songs for more than 50 years. Dylan is 75 years old.

This is the first time the Nobel Prize in Literature has gone to an artist who does not write books or plays.

The Nobel Prize in Literature is about 860,000 euros.

## Terrorists released girls

![Nigerians protested on behalf of the kidnapped schoolgirls on the anniversary.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8562571)

Open image viewer

Nigerians hope that all the girls will get away from the terrorists. The picture shows a protest for the girls. Photo: EPA

The terrorist group Boko Haram has released 21 girls in Nigeria, in Africa. The girls had been prisoners of Boko Haram for 2 years.

More than 200 girls are still missing. The Nigerian authorities do not know where the girls are.

Boko Haram terrorists took 270 schoolchildren prisoner in 2014.

Boko Haram is an Islamist terrorist group that fights against the Nigerian government.

## Lingonberries bring health

![Red ripe lingonberries in boxes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7458305)

Open image viewer

Only a small part of the lingonberry harvest is picked. Photo: Anne Rahjola / Yle

The lingonberry is the most common berry in Finland's forests.

The organisation Arctic Aromas says that the lingonberry is a really healthy berry. For example, it has a lot of fibre and little energy. The lingonberry protects against heart disease, cancer and obesity, for example.

The organisation Arctic Aromas says that the lingonberry is still not known around the world. However, the organisation believes that the lingonberry fits well on Asian dinner tables, for example.

Every year about 250 million kilograms of lingonberries grow in Finland's forests. Only a small part of the harvest is picked in the autumn.

## Bells ring for Aleppo

![A church bell in the belfry of Pulkkila church.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8491005)

Open image viewer

Church bells ring in memory of the victims of Aleppo. Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

Many Finnish churches are ringing bells for the victims of Aleppo in Syria. The idea came from Kallio Church in Helsinki.

The bells are rung every day at 5 pm. This is done until the UN day, that is, the United Nations day, which is 24 October.

Many people have died in the city of Aleppo. The city has been bombed a lot during the Syrian war.
