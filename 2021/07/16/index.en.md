## European flood disaster

There is a flood disaster in Europe. People have died and houses have been destroyed as flood water rushes down the streets of cities.

The water of many rivers has risen to record levels because of heavy rain. In some areas it has rained in 2 days as much as usually in 2 months.

There are serious flood areas in many European countries, for example in Germany and Belgium. In the flood areas rescue workers are moving people to safety.

## Finland's corona situation

![People on the Kasarmintori terrace in hot weather in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_152/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82504260d41a0ae8c1b/1626416263)

Restaurant restrictions may come to new areas. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Finland's corona situation is getting worse. New coronavirus infections are now being found 3 times more than a month ago.

Now there are over 1,600 new coronavirus infections a week. There are over 40 corona patients in hospital care.

New coronavirus infections are especially among young people. Young people have caught coronavirus, for example, in bars.

Minister Krista Kiuru says that restaurant restrictions may come to new areas next week. Restrictions may come, for example, to the Turku region.

The restrictions require that restaurants close by 1 am at the latest. Alcohol sales end an hour earlier.

In the Helsinki region there are already the same restaurant restrictions, and they continue.

## Talk of brain waves

![The image shows a patient in intensive care in France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11558692)

Open image viewer

A person can lose the ability to speak, for example because of an illness or an accident. Photo: Caroline Blumberg / EPA

American researchers have built a device that turns a person's brain waves into speech.

The device was tested on a man who was paralysed. He was no longer able to speak himself.

The device measured the man's brain and turned the brain waves into speech. The device gave the man his voice.

The research gives hope to many people. Every year thousands of people lose the ability to speak. The cause can be a stroke, an illness or an accident.

## Finnish film to the USA

![Actors Seidi Haarla and Juri Borisov in the film Compartment No. 6.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82825060e02be9bc5ee/1625304508)

"Compartment No. 6" is about a Finnish girl and a Russian man who meet on a train. Photo: Festival de Cannes

A Finnish film is coming to cinemas in the USA. The name of the film is "Compartment No. 6".

The same film is also part of the Cannes film competition. The competition's prizes are awarded on Saturday.

"Compartment No. 6" is about a train journey in Russia. During the journey a Finnish student girl and a Russian man get to know each other.

The film's story is from Rosa Liksom's book. The film's director is Juho Kuosmanen.

It is rare for a Finnish film to come to American cinemas.
