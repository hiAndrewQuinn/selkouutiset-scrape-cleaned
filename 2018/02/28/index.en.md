# Wednesday 28.2.2018 (radio)

MPs talked about equality in education in Parliament. Finland's economy is finally doing well. People may be able to see the ruins that were found under the floor of a school in Turku. A woman tried to avoid plastic for a month. The frost is easing.

## Parliament talked about education

![Touko Aalto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10096175)

Open image viewer

MP Touko Aalto criticised the government's education cuts in Parliament. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Parliament has talked about equality in education.

The opposition parties said that not all children have equally good chances to get good teaching. The reason is that the government is saving money. For example, vocational schools and higher education institutions have fewer lessons than before. Not all children get early childhood education in a day care centre, the opposition said.

The government said that education in Finland is equal. The government said, for example, that there are no big differences in quality between schools.

## The economy is growing fast

![Aerial photo of Vuosaari harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10095570)

Open image viewer

Vuosaari harbour in Helsinki. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Finland's economy is doing well.

More goods are being exported abroad than in years. Companies are investing, which means expanding their activities, building new things and buying technology. Finally, more money is being spent on research and product development. People have waited 7 years for that. Jobs are being created.

Last year all sectors grew: industry, construction and services. Finland now exports more goods and services than it imports, so more money comes into Finland than goes out of here as payments abroad.

Finland's economy had a hard time for a long time before last year, when growth began.

## People may be able to see the ruins in Turku

![Turku from the 1300s is revealed under the floor of the Katedralskolan gym.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10079830)

Open image viewer

Under the floor of the gym of a school in Turku there is a piece of a 14th-century town. Photo: Jouni Koutonen / Yle

In Turku, people are thinking about what to do with the ruins that were found under the floor of a school.

During the renovation of a school in Turku, ruins from the 1300s were found under the floor of the gym. Parts of two stone houses and a bit of street were found there.

In spring, people may be able to see the ruins. It has not yet been decided whether the ruins will be covered again under the gym floor or whether they will be made into a museum.

## Can you live without plastic?

![The plastic waste produced by Satu Lapinlampi during one month.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10092597)

Open image viewer

This was Satu Lapinlampi's plastic waste in February. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

Satu Lapinlampi from Oulu tried to live for a month so that she did not buy any plastic at all.

She did quite well. She had to buy only one small plastic bag and one big one. The big bag had toilet paper in it. Satu Lapinlampi did, however, have to change her shopping a lot. She could not buy, for example, lettuce, cucumbers and many fruits, because they are packed in plastic.

Milder days

The frost eases during the last days of the week.

On Thursday, Friday and Saturday during the day, it is less than 10 degrees below zero all over Finland. At night the hard frost still continues. Even though the hardest frosts are over tomorrow, the wintry weather continues.
