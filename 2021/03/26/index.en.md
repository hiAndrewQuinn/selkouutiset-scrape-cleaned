## Parliament discusses movement restrictions

Parliament has started discussing movement restrictions.

The government gave Parliament a proposal on movement restrictions yesterday. If the law is approved, people's movement will be restricted in the Helsinki area and in Turku.

Handling the law will take several days. Movement restrictions may perhaps be introduced after Easter.

## Stockmann prepares for movement restrictions

![The picture shows a Stockmann store at the corner of Aleksanterinkatu and Mannerheimintie in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_200/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6547785e722a5d4136f/1595572661)

Open image viewer

The Stockmann department store in Helsinki. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The department store company Stockmann is starting co-operation negotiations. They concern 1,200 employees.
Stockmann is preparing for movement restrictions.

If movement restrictions begin, there will be no customers in the department stores. In that case, Stockmann may lay off staff.

Stockmann may also close department stores if movement restrictions begin.

## Remote work reduces emissions

![The picture shows an almost empty motorway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5502,h_3095,x_0,y_576/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6613085e870674a2000/1587998909)

Open image viewer

Traffic has decreased because people are working remotely a lot. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Remote work has increased a lot because of the corona situation. It may be a good thing for climate emissions. People may do a lot of remote work even after the corona situation.

A study on this has been done at the University of Turku. During the corona time, commuting traffic has decreased by 13 percent. All car traffic has decreased by 6 to 8 percent.

Climate expert, Professor **Markku Ollikainen**, says that the reduction is big. When car use falls this much, it reduces climate emissions a lot.

## Children's sleep problems have increased

![A little boy playing Pokémon Go.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9239157)

Open image viewer

Long screen time, for example gaming, can make sleep worse. Photo: Heli Kaski / Yle

Sleep problems among children and young people have increased. Many children and young people sleep too little.
In school, pupils are tired and cannot manage to study or concentrate on lessons.

Among primary school-aged children, sleep is disturbed by screen time, for example playing on a phone or watching programmes. Among lower secondary pupils, stress also disturbs sleep.

Experts say that children should have a regular rhythm and a set bedtime. Screen time should be reduced and exercise increased.

## Migratory birds arrive

![Northern lapwing in the snow](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8786373)

Open image viewer

Northern lapwing. Photo: Mikko Rötkö

The spring migration of birds has started. The bird organisation BirdLife says that birds have started their spring migration earlier than usual this year.

Already at the turn of February and March, many birds such as northern lapwings and skylarks came to Finland.

Not many migratory birds have yet arrived inland, because there is still snow there.
