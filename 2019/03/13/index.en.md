# Wednesday 13.3.2019 (radio)

Airlines may no longer fly Boeing 737 MAX 8 aircraft. Many lower secondary pupils are absent from school a lot. Public transport prices in the Helsinki region change at the end of April. More used cars are being brought into Finland than before. In future, you may have to pay a large sum of money if you catch an endangered fish.

## Boeing planes banned from flying

![Norwegian's Boeing 737-8 MAX aircraft.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10682269)

Open image viewer

Boeing 737 MAX 8 aircraft. Photo: AOP

Airlines may no longer fly Boeing 737 MAX 8 aircraft. The European Aviation Safety Agency ordered the planes banned from flying on Tuesday.

2 planes of the same kind have crashed to the ground within half a year. Because of that, aviation authorities suspect that the plane is not safe.

The Norwegian airline Norwegian has 18 Boeing aircraft that have been ordered to stay grounded. Norwegian has had to cancel many flights, for example from Finland.

Norwegian intends to demand compensation from the manufacturer of the Boeing aircraft.

## Lower secondary pupils skip school a lot

![Boy with a cap on his head.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10678513)

Open image viewer

Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

In many lower secondary schools there is a pupil, or pupils, who are absent from school a lot. When a pupil is absent from school without permission, they are skipping school.

There can be many reasons for skipping school. The pupil may have problems of their own, or perhaps there are problems at home. Sometimes there is no particular reason. The school just isn't fun.

If a pupil is absent from school without permission for more than 100 hours, the school must make a child welfare notification about the pupil.

## HSL fares are changing

![Simplified map of HSL zones.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10685897)

Open image viewer

HSL's new fare system is based on zones. There are 4 of them, A, B, C and D. Photo: Yle News Graphics / HSL

Public transport prices in the Helsinki region change at the end of April.

On 27 April, the Helsinki Region Transport (HSL) will introduce new fare zones.

HSL has divided the Helsinki region into 4 zones. The price of a trip depends on how far it is to Helsinki city centre.

Some tickets get more expensive. Some tickets get cheaper.

## Used cars sell a lot

![Used cars on display at Veho.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10569025)

Open image viewer

Many people buy a car that is 3–4 years old. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

More used cars are being brought into Finland than before. Last year a record number of used cars were imported into Finland.

About 40,000 used passenger cars were brought into Finland last year. Most of the cars imported were 3 or 4 years old.

Finns went to buy used cars especially in Sweden.

## Endangered fish must not be fished

![The lake salmon of the Vuoksi water system is extremely endangered. In the picture is the lake salmon of Saimaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10686666)

Open image viewer

For example, the Saimaa landlocked salmon is an endangered fish. Photo: Tapio Gustafsson / Finnish Fisheries Central Association

In future, you may have to pay a large sum of money if you catch an endangered fish. A fish is endangered if only very few fish of that species are left.

Parliament has approved an amendment to the Fishing Act. The law reform requires that the value of the fish must be paid to the state if an endangered fish is caught.

If, for example, you catch a landlocked salmon parr, you may have to pay the state perhaps as much as 7,000 euros.

The new law comes into force at the beginning of May.
