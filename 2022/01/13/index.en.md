## Corona quarantine

![A woman in quarantine works remotely from home in Porvoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6549055e723cd19f036/1584545299)

In corona quarantine you must stay at home, for example you may not go to work. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

Corona quarantine is getting shorter.

During corona quarantine, a corona patient may not meet anyone from outside their own family, so that corona does not spread.

Corona quarantine has been 10 days. Now it gets shorter, to between 5 and 10 days.

Corona quarantine is getting shorter partly because of the omicron variant of the coronavirus. The omicron variant has sped up the corona disease.

## Advance voting

Advance voting for the regional elections started on Wednesday.

On the first day of advance voting, about 146,000 people voted.

Advance voting places are, for example, in libraries. In small places, advance voting buses also travel around.

In the regional elections, people are chosen who decide on health services and social services.

The Yle election compass helps voters find a good candidate. The election compass also works in English, Russian and Arabic. The election compass is at yle.fi/vaalit.

At selkokeskus.fi there is information about who may vote in the regional elections and how voting is done in Finland.

## Dogs' language skills

![A dog wearing headphones lies in an MRI tube.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12267082)

Open image viewer

A dog listens to a book being read during an MRI scan. Photo: Enikő Kubinyi

A dog can recognise languages. A dog can tell its owner's mother tongue apart from other languages.

The subject has been studied by the Mexican researcher Laura Cuaya. It all began when Cuaya moved to Hungary. She wanted to know whether her dog understood that Hungarians speak a different language from Cuaya.

Cuaya chose a group of dogs that learned to lie in an MRI machine. Then a book was read to them in Hungarian and in Spanish. Each dog's owner speaks only one of these languages as a mother tongue.

MRI images of the dogs' brains showed that the owner's mother tongue activated a different area of the dog's brain than a foreign language. So the dogs did tell the languages apart.

## The weather

The weather is getting colder.

On Friday it is dry across a large part of the country. Cloud cover varies from clear to partly cloudy.

The temperature is between 0 and +3 degrees, and in the east and north between -1 and -6 degrees. A gusty north-westerly wind adds to the feeling of cold.
