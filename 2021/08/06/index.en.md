## Filipino nurses

![Marichu Dalaguna pushes an elderly person sitting in a wheelchair and looks at the camera.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5811,h_3269,x_0,y_281/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-839002610bb000ea21c/1628219801)

Filipino Marichu Ebunalo arrived in Finland to work in the care sector. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

Dozens of new care sector workers from the Philippines will come to Finland this year.

For example, Mehiläinen intends to hire 50 Filipino nurses, and Esperi Care more than 20. Attendo, in turn, has said that it intends to hire 1,000 Filipino nurses in the coming years.

Nurses are being brought from abroad, because there is a severe shortage of nurses in Finland, and in the future the shortage will only get worse. It has been estimated that already now over 8,000 new nurses are needed in Finland.

## The school year starts

![A small schoolchild on a pedestrian crossing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6766476)

Open image viewer

From next week first-graders are back on the roads. Photo: Riikka Rautiainen / Yle

The autumn term of basic schools starts next week.

This autumn 60,000 new first-graders start school.

In second-level institutions, that is, upper secondary schools and vocational schools, about 79,000 new students start in total.

Young people under 18 had to apply for a place in second-level education after basic school. The National Agency for Education says that about 3 percent still do not have a place in further education. However, the National Agency for Education believes that everyone will find a place to study during the autumn.

## Corona patients

![Corona sampling at the Tampere fair and sports centre on 21.7.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_581/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83414960f81b5628a42/1627894015)

Open image viewer

A corona sample is being taken in Tampere. Photo: Antti Eintola / Yle

The number of corona patients in hospitals is growing slowly.

In hospitals there are now 100 corona patients in total. Of them, 18 are in intensive care.

However, there are now fewer corona patients in hospitals than in the spring.

Today 738 new corona infections have been reported.

Many kinds of aircraft have roared in the sky over Helsinki today.

The Kaivari 21 air show has brought to the sky, for example, old and historic aircraft as well as brand-new military aircraft. Aerobatic teams from France and Finland are also taking part.

The Kaivari 21 event includes a ground exhibition in Kaivopuisto. It is open tomorrow too.

## Icy art work

![A passer-by looks at an art work made of ice blocks at Kansalaistori in Helsinki on 6.8.2021](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_510/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-839332610cd645e51d0/1628233716)

Open image viewer

The art work has 9 ice cubes, which are melting slowly. Photo: Petri Niemi / Yle

The European Green Capital, Lahti, has brought an art work to Helsinki, which warns about climate change.

Large ice cubes have been brought to Kansalaistori, and they are melting slowly. The work reminds us that climate change raises sea levels and so threatens many coastal cities.
