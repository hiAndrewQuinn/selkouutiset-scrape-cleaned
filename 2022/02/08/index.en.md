## Ukraine crisis

French President Emmanuel Macron (on the left) and Russian President Vladimir Putin. Photo: EPA/THIBAULT CAMUS / POOL MAXPPP OUT

French President Emmanuel Macron is looking for a solution to the Ukraine crisis.

Macron met Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky on Tuesday. On Monday he had already met Russian President Vladimir Putin.

The situation in Ukraine is difficult. Russia has moved soldiers close to the border with Ukraine. There are fears that Russia will attack Ukraine.

Russia does not want Ukraine to join the NATO military alliance. Russia has demanded that the NATO military alliance not take in new member countries.

Macron says that NATO's doors will remain open. In Macron's view, this is important also for Sweden and Finland, if they want to join NATO.

The police have completed the preliminary investigation related to street gangs in the Helsinki region.

The police suspect members of street gangs from Helsinki and Espoo of many crimes. It is suspected that members of street gangs have committed many violent crimes.

Gangsta rap music has influenced the birth of street gangs. In gangsta rap music, a criminal way of life and the fight of street gangs are admired.

Members of street gangs are often young men with an immigrant background.

## Memory diseases

![Activity instructors push elderly people in wheelchairs at the Myyrmäki elderly care centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5539,h_3116,x_0,y_363/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6490285e5d269b2de84/1583163455)

Open image viewer

Memory diseases increase as the number of elderly people grows. Photo: Eleni Paspatis / Yle

New information has been obtained about memory diseases.

Researcher Elisa Neuvonen says that personality traits can affect the onset of memory disease. An elderly person has a 2 or 3 times greater risk of getting a memory disease if they are cynical and angry.

Depressive symptoms can also be linked to memory diseases. That is why it is important that depressive symptoms are noticed and treated, researcher Neuvonen says.

## Pink ribbon

Pink ribbon from 2018. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

The Pink Ribbon campaign has been very successful. The Pink Ribbon campaign collects money for cancer research.

During last autumn, the Pink Ribbon campaign collected almost 5 million euros for cancer research. That is over half a million euros more than last time.

Many people know the Pink Ribbon campaign from its ribbons. Money for the campaign is collected by selling the ribbon pins.

## Weather

On Wednesday the weather is dry across the whole country.

In the southern part of the country the sun shines. Elsewhere there is a lot of cloud.

There is 1-10 degrees of frost. Only on the southern and western coasts is the temperature a degree above zero.

At the end of the week the weather is dry and fairly sunny.

In the south the temperature is still slightly above zero on Thursday, but after that it is frost in the south too.
