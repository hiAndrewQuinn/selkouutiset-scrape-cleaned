# Thursday 6.1.2022 (radio)

Remote school. Hospitals. Storming of the US Congress. Epiphany. Weather forecast.

## Remote school is being discussed

![The picture shows an empty classroom in Hyvinkää in August 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_275/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7070515f325e8a263bf/1613318282)

Open image viewer

Empty classroom. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

In Finland, remote teaching in schools is being discussed. The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health intends to recommend remote teaching because of the corona situation. The teachers' union OAJ supports remote teaching. The teachers' union hopes that schools will soon switch to remote teaching.

In many schools the Christmas holiday ends on Sunday. The government may discuss remote school on Friday. Not all government ministers support remote school, because it can be difficult for many families.

If the government gives a recommendation on remote school on Friday, remote school may start next week.

## Hospitals are crowded

![A nurse moves a patient to the surgical outpatient clinic at the Mehiläinen Länsi-Pohja central hospital in Kemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_294/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88463461a0b76ce6710/1648283792)

Open image viewer

The patient is moved in the hospital. Photo: Antti Ullakko / Yle

The worsened corona situation is crowding hospital emergency departments and wards. The concern is especially whether there are enough staff to treat patients. Some nurses and doctors are either sick or in quarantine.

The crowding is especially in the big hospital districts of Southern Finland. Elsewhere in Finland the situation is not as difficult, but it may also get worse elsewhere in Finland.

In Finland, almost 6,000 new corona infections have been reported on Thursday.

## One year since the storming of the US Congress building

![Joe Biden speaks at the Capitol.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8192,h_4608,x_0,y_856/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89925761d700236ce37/1641480355)

Open image viewer

President Joe Biden gave a speech. Biden said that the storming of the Congress building must not happen again. Photo: Jim Lo Scalzo / EPA

One year has passed since the storming of the US Congress building. Supporters of President Donald Trump forced their way into the US Congress building in Washington on 6 January last year.

![Protesters on the steps of the US Congress.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_157/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89873761d5478ee9fc1/1641368884)

Open image viewer

This is how it looked at the US Congress building on 6 January 2021. Photo: Michael Reynolds / EPA

Trump's supporters did not want Congress to confirm Joe Biden's election victory. Five people died in the storming of the Congress building.

In the USA the storming of the Congress building has been remembered. President Joe Biden gave a speech to Americans. Biden said that the same must never happen again.

## Epiphany

![The three wise men and the Star of Bethlehem.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1743,h_980,x_90,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-319349585a61ca62f44/1641154488)

Open image viewer

On Epiphany, for example, the three wise men are remembered. Photo: Pixabay - Public Domain

For many people, Epiphany ends the Christmas season. Epiphany is a public holiday in Finland, that is, offices and banks were closed on Thursday.

Epiphany is one of the oldest festivals of Christians. Epiphany has been celebrated for even longer than Christmas.

In many Christian churches, on Epiphany the wise men are remembered, the ones who came to see the Child Jesus.
