# Wednesday 23.11.2016 (radio)

Heads of government agencies criticised for their spending. There is a lot of loneliness in Finland. Foreign Minister Timo Soini goes on sick leave. Viking Line orders a ship from China. Helsinki is testing a new transport service package. Mira Potkonen won silver at the European boxing championships.

## Agency heads criticised for their spending

![A calculator and euros.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9139160)

Open image viewer

State agencies must not waste money. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / Yle

The heads of state agencies have received remarks about their spending

The heads of the agencies have used too much state money, for example on taxi rides and restaurants.

In addition, the heads of the agencies have not always followed the instructions when they have made decisions.

Inspectors have given a remark to the heads of more than 40 agencies. For example, Aulis Pitkälä, the director general of the Finnish National Board of Education, received a remark.

Agencies and their employees are closely monitored, because agencies are institutions owned by the state. They take care of important state tasks with shared tax money.

## Many Finns are lonely

![A woman looks out of the window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9294317)

Open image viewer

Many Finns experience loneliness. Photo: Arja Lehto / Yle

There is a lot of loneliness in Finland.

In a Yle survey, one in five, or 20% of Finns, said that they are sometimes lonely.

In the survey, people said that they get the most help with loneliness from friends, family and relatives. Many also got help from hobbies where you can meet other people.

Various organisations also help lonely people. For example, the Finnish Red Cross, HelsinkiMissio and the Mannerheim League for Child Welfare arrange friends for lonely people and families.

Foreign Minister Timo Soini goes on sick leave.

Soini's sick leave lasts until the beginning of December.

## Viking Line orders a passenger ship from China

![Viking Line's ship Mariella off Helsinki on 1 September 2015](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8372118)

Open image viewer

Many ship trips are made from Finland to Stockholm and Tallinn. Photo: Aku Häyrynen / Lehtikuva

The Åland shipping company Viking Line is ordering a new passenger ship.

The ship is being ordered from China, and it costs about 190 million euros.

According to Viking Line's CEO, the ship is being ordered from China because it was the cheapest option.

Still, Finnish firms are also taking part in building the ship.

The ship will come to Finland in 2020, and it will start sailing from Turku to Stockholm.

The ship has room for about 2,800 passengers.

## Helsinki is testing a new travel package

![Passengers walking on a railway station platform.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8033462)

Open image viewer

A ticket for travel within Helsinki now costs 52 euros a month at the cheapest, but it can only be used on public transport. Photo: Teemu Salonen / Lehtikuva

A new mobility service package is being tested in Helsinki.

For 250 euros a month you can travel freely on Helsinki's buses, metro, trains and trams. In addition, the package includes some taxi rides and car rental time.

A limited test group is taking part in the trial.

## Silver for Mira Potkonen at the European Championships

![Mira Potkonen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9121581)

Mira Potkonen has boxed her way to a European Championship silver.

The European gold went to Russia's Daria Abramova.

This season, Potkonen has won a World Championship bronze and an Olympic bronze.
