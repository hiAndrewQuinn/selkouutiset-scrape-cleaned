# Wednesday 24.10.2018 (radio)

Touko Aalto will not continue as chair of the Greens. Finland is taking part in a NATO military exercise in Norway. Security guards have started a strike. The European Union is banning plastic items. Winter speed limits are being brought into use on the roads this week.

## Aalto is stepping down

![Touko Aalto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10472681)

Open image viewer

Touko Aalto is stepping down as chair of the Greens. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

Touko Aalto will not continue as chair of the Greens. The Green party announced it on Wednesday.

Touko Aalto went on sick leave in mid-September. Aalto's sick leave will continue at least until the end of the year.

The reason for the sick leave is exhaustion. That is why Aalto needs rest. Aalto says that he also has depression.

The Green party will choose a new chair on 3 November. The new chair's most important task is to lead the election work.

Finland has elections next spring. Support for the Greens has fallen recently.

## NATO is training

![Men raise a NATO flag up a pole.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10300260)

Open image viewer

Finland does not belong to NATO, but it is taking part in its exercise. Photo: Armando Babani / EPA

Finland is taking part in a NATO military exercise in Norway. The exercise of the military alliance NATO starts on Thursday.

Finland and Sweden do not belong to NATO, but they are taking part in the exercise. A total of about 50,000 soldiers are taking part in the exercise.

At the same time as the NATO exercise, a naval exercise is also being held in the Baltic Sea. The naval exercise is international, and Finland is leading it.

## Security guards are on strike

![A security guard at Helsinki-Vantaa Airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10470893)

Open image viewer

Security guards are on strike for 3 days. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Security guards started a strike on Wednesday. They will not work for 3 days. The guards will return to work on Saturday.

During the strike there may be congestion at Helsinki-Vantaa Airport. Passengers may have to queue for the security check longer than usual.

Because of the strike, some railway station buildings are also closed. But passengers can get on trains normally.

The guards are on strike because there is a dispute about pay in the security industry.

## Plastic items are being banned

![Plastic rubbish near a collection point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10167674)

Open image viewer

People use many plastic items only once. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

The European Union plans to ban plastic items that are used only once. The EU Parliament voted on the ban on Wednesday.

The EU wants people to stop using, for example, plastic cutlery. The aim is to ban plastic items because they are harmful to the environment.

The same items can also be made from other materials, for example cardboard. That saves nature.

## Winter speed limits begin

![80 km/h speed limit sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5337842)

Open image viewer

On main roads, the winter speed limit is 80 kilometres per hour. Photo: Yle

Winter speed limits are being brought into use on the roads this week.

In winter you may drive at 80 kilometres per hour on main roads. On motorways the speed may be 100 kilometres per hour.

The traffic signs will be changed by Friday at the latest.
