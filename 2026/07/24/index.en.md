## Museum card

Now about the Museum Card.

The popular Museum Card has almost doubled the number of visitors to museums. At the same time, the prices of single tickets have doubled.

The museum card was introduced in 2015. The museum card is a common admission ticket for Finnish museums. The annual price is now 86 euros.

The museum receives about half of the ticket price from cardholders. The higher the ticket price, the more money it has received. This has increased the price of tickets.

Prices have also been driven up by the rise in the general price level.

The museum card has doubled the number of visitors to museums and the prices of single tickets.

A price cap of just under 24 euros will come into effect next year. Museums will not receive Museum Card compensation for prices higher than that.

## Bumblebee research

The buzzing of bumblebees has been monitored using new technology.

It was noted that pollination is most active between 10 am and 3 pm.

Bumblebees have been monitored using new technology.

Based on the observations, bumblebee nests were built in better locations on the farm, in the middle of the tunnels.
