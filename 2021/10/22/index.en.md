## Corona vaccinations

![The picture shows Kirsi Varhila in July 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_219/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7014415f17f5ff4e493/1595410827)

Permanent secretary of the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health Kirsi Varhila. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The authorities are considering new ways to get young people to take corona vaccinations.

Permanent secretary Kirsi Varhila says that one way is a vaccination bus. The plan is that the vaccination bus travels around areas where the trip to a vaccination point could otherwise be long.

Varhila says that young people can also be reached, for example, at ice hockey and football matches.

Varhila says that the authorities have already, for example, made vaccination advertisements on Tinder.

In Finland about 77 per cent of over 12-year-olds have received 2 corona vaccinations.

However, corona vaccinations among young people have slowed. Especially young adults, that is 20–40-year-olds, have been slow to get corona vaccinations.

## TE Office online broadcasts

![Service director Mari Tuomikoski of the TE Office of North Ostrobothnia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2850,h_1603,x_0,y_116/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-86975161711533752f9/1634801440)

Open image viewer

Service director Mari Tuomikoski of the TE Office in North Ostrobothnia. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

Firms are looking for employees in a new way. Firms present open jobs in TE Office online broadcasts.

Online broadcasts have been tried, for example, at the TE Office of North Ostrobothnia. The office's service director, Mari Tuomikoski, says that the TE Office must keep up as communication develops.

With the help of the TE Office online broadcast, information about open jobs spreads well. That is important for firms, because in many fields there is a shortage of employees.

## Death during a film shoot

![Actor and Hollywood star Alec Baldwin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5170,h_2908,x_0,y_590/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-87024161722a4d658e0/1634880692)

Open image viewer

Actor and Hollywood star Alec Baldwin. Photo: Angela Weiss / AFP

An unusual death has happened in the USA. A 42-year-old woman died during the filming of a western film.

The woman was shot with a gun that is used in films. The director of the film was also injured.

The gun was used by actor Alec Baldwin. He is a famous Hollywood star.

Filming of the film has been interrupted, and the police are investigating the case.

## Friday's autumn storm

![At Arabianranta, water has risen into the park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1974,h_1110,x_3,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8704426172848c81ddb/1634895059)

Open image viewer

The storm covered the coastal parks with water. Photo: Juha Blomberg / Yle

There was an autumn storm in Finland on Friday.

In Helsinki the water rose to the shores and covered the coastal parks. In Porvoo a river flooded the streets, and streets had to be closed.

The strong wind also hindered work in ports. The wind, for example, knocked over containers in the port of Kotka, and port cranes could not be used in the strong wind.

The autumn storm also cut off electricity in various parts of Finland. Thousands of homes were without electricity on Friday.
