# Friday 7.7.2017 (radio)

A meeting of the big economies has begun in Germany. Flood waters have killed people in Japan. Finland's first support centre for victims of sexual crimes has opened in Helsinki. Finland's women's relay team won a bronze medal at the World Orienteering Championships. Aino Niemi is the new tango queen.

## A meeting of the big economies

There is a meeting of the big economies in Germany. The presidents of the USA and Russia, for example, are taking part. Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin have now met for the first time.

The meeting is in Hamburg, and there are many demonstrators in the city.

The demonstrators have, for example, burned cars. They have also fought with police. Many police officers have been injured.

There were 20,000 police officers in Hamburg on Friday, and even more police are coming to the city.

## The floods in Japan

Flood waters have caused damage in Japan. People have died in the floods. The flood water has also carried away buildings and roads.

The reason for the floods is that it has rained really a lot in Japan. Weather forecasts say that the heavy rains in Japan will continue.

## A support centre for rape victims

![Leena Malmi, midwife, HUS Seri support centre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9706986)

Open image viewer

The support centre's midwife Leena Malmi. Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

Finland's first support centre for victims of sexual crimes has opened at the Women's Hospital in Helsinki.

Midwife Leena Malmi says that a client does not need a doctor's referral to the support centre. They can walk in through the door when they want.

If a person has been raped, they get help from a midwife and a psychologist at the support centre. All the help can be found in the same place.

Similar support centres are planned for other parts of Finland too.

## A World Championship bronze medal for Finland's women

![Venla Harju](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9699904)

Open image viewer

Venla Harju was part of the women's relay team that won bronze. It was Harju's second bronze medal at the World Championships in Estonia. Photo: Markku Ulander/Lehtikuva

Finland has won a second medal at the World Orienteering Championships. Finland's women's team was third in the relay and won a bronze medal.

Sweden won the women's relay and became world champion. Russia was second in the race.

The relay team had 3 orienteers, who ran in turns. They looked for checkpoints in the forest, that is, places marked on the map.

Finland's Venla Harju won a bronze medal in the women's orienteering race on Thursday.

The World Orienteering Championships were held in Estonia.

## Tango queen Aino Niemi

![Aino Niemi is the new tango queen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9710510)

Open image viewer

Aino Niemi is this year's Tango Queen. Photo: Timo Aalto / Lehtikuva

The big music event Tangomarkkinat (the Tango Festival) is now on in Seinäjoki. On Thursday there was a women's tango singing competition at the festival. The competition was won by Aino Niemi, who is the new tango queen.

The new tango queen comes from Kouvola. Aino Niemi is 18 years old. The competition judges said that Niemi is a charming, sensitive and poetic singer.

The Tango Festival continues at the weekend. The men's tango singing competition is on Saturday. The winner of the men's competition will become the new tango king.

The Tango Festival is held in Seinäjoki every summer.
