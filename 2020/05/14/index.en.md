# Thursday 14.5.2020 (radio)

Companies can apply to the state for business support. Finns do not think a face mask requirement is necessary. Commuting traffic between Finland and other Schengen countries has started. People smuggling was revealed at Helsinki-Vantaa.

## A decision has been made on business support

![Sanna Marin, Mika Lintilä, Katri Kulmuni and Tuula Haatainen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11351403)

Companies can apply to the state for business support. The application may start at the turn of the month.

On Wednesday evening the government reached agreement on the support. On Thursday morning, Minister of Economic Affairs Mika Lintilä said that the preparation of the law is already in the final stretch.

The companies that get support are those that have suffered from the corona situation and whose turnover has fallen a lot.

The support is between 2,000 and 200,000 euros. The support is meant to prevent bankruptcies.

## Compulsory face masks do not get support in Finland

![Results of the mask survey. Citizens most support using masks at their own discretion.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11347241)

Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Most Finns think that a face mask requirement is not necessary.

Yle made a survey about masks. Only 11 per cent are in favour of a face mask requirement. In Finland, for the time being there is no recommendation or order to use face masks.

The use of face masks is compulsory in public places in many European countries. For example, in Germany, Austria and Poland there is a face mask requirement because of the coronavirus.

Some Finns hope that the authorities will give clear instructions.

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health is studying research information on face masks. The study is meant to be finished during this month.

## Commuting to Sweden and Estonia has opened

![Viking Grace and Silja Galaxy meet in the Åland archipelago near Mariehamn.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11084576)

Commuting traffic between Finland and other Schengen countries has started. Traffic started in the morning after a break of a month.

Car travellers, for example, can also return to Finland by ferry from Sweden and Estonia.

Visiting a close relative or studying are also allowed traffic.

Restrictions are still in force. Cruises or travel for leisure are not yet allowed traffic.

## Many cars are being rented now

![A man rents a car at an Avis office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9733564)

Open image viewer

A person at the counter of a car rental office. Illustrative picture. Photo: Juha Kivioja / Yle

Many cars are being rented now. Long-term rental of passenger cars has increased by at least 50 per cent during the corona epidemic.

The car rental companies Hertz and Europcar say that rental periods of more than a month are common. This became common as soon as the lockdown of Uusimaa ended.

People want to travel as safely as possible and avoid contacts. In addition, public transport services have been reduced.

## People smuggling was revealed at Helsinki-Vantaa

![Helsinki-Vantaa airport terminal 1 seen from outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3383,h_1903,x_0,y_446/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6539825e70911d912f5/1585149334)

The Border Guard has revealed a ring that smuggled people at Helsinki-Vantaa.

People have been taken from mainland Europe to Great Britain for about 8,000 euros.

The police suspect that a professional smuggling ring is behind it. The ring is active at least in Sweden and in Great Britain.

The criminal group was revealed in January, when two suspects were caught at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. One was a Swedish citizen. The other was a Kuwaiti who used another person's Swedish passport.

The men were travelling from Paris via Helsinki to London.
