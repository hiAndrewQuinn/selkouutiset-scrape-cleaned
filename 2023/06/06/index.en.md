## Nova Kakhovka dam

![Blown-up dam and river.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2966,h_1669,x_104,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1125214647eed9bcf1bb/1686046325)

The Nova Kakhovka dam in Kherson. Photo: AFP

Russia has blown up the Nova Kakhovka dam in Kherson, Ukraine. That is why the Dnipro river is flooding the Kherson area. Authorities have evacuated people from the area, because the water is rising there.

The dam was 30 metres high and 3 kilometres long. The Kakhovka dam is an important structure.

Experts have warned for a long time that the dam is a possible target for a military attack. That is why it is no big surprise that the dam was blown up.

## Espoo bridge accident

![A construction site where a bridge is being built and a sign for pedestrians.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_62/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1112030645cb9b933cad/1683798491)

Open image viewer

A temporary pedestrian bridge collapsed in May in Tapiola, Espoo. Photo: Jussi Nukari/Lehtikuva

The Accident Investigation Board has reported new information about the Espoo bridge accident. In Tapiola, Espoo, a construction site's pedestrian bridge collapsed at the end of May. 23 schoolchildren and 1 teacher were injured in the accident.

The Accident Investigation Board says that there were errors in building the bridge. Nobody made structural calculations when the bridge was built. That is why nobody knew how many people the bridge could carry.

The Accident Investigation Board says that other similar bridges in Finland must also be checked. Bridge building processes must also be improved in future.

## Women in the military

![Minttu Sainio and Hilma Kemppinen are currently doing military service in the Kainuu Brigade.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_455/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11218846475c8e7b5c19/1685440936)

Open image viewer

Women feel that the military is useful in working life. Photo: Timo Sihvonen / Yle

The military can help women advance in their careers. That is how many women who have done military service feel. Yle has asked women whether the military has been useful in working life.

Many women say that they have gained confidence and courage from the military. Then you also manage in workplaces with many men. Also in workplaces dominated by women, the military can be useful. For example, a woman is listened to more at work if she has done military service.

Military service is voluntary for women in Finland. Women's interest in the military has grown. Last year there were a record number of women in the military.
