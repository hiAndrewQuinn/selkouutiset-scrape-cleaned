## Eurovision

![Käärijä being interviewed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5042,h_2835,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11134726460644619ce1/1684038765)

Finland has come second in the Eurovision song contest. The winner of the contest is Sweden.

The contest between Finland and Sweden for victory was tight. Both countries received over 500 points, but Sweden about 50 points more than Finland.

In the Eurovision final, the competitors received points from the professional juries of different countries and from the audience. Sweden's representative Loreen received the most votes from the professional juries, but Finland's representative Käärijä received the most votes from the audience.

18 countries gave Finland the best audience points, that is, 12 points. Sweden did not get 12 audience points from any country.

However, Sweden won the contest when the points from the professional juries and the audience were added together. That is why Finland's Käärijä was disappointed after the final.

## Congratulations to Käärijä

![People holding up a Käärijä banner.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_674/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11135506460f94b10616/1684076973)

Well-wishers waited for Käärijä to come home from Eurovision on Sunday. Photo: Nina Svahn / Yle

Käärijä's second place in the Eurovision song contest is a good achievement. Finland has succeeded in Eurovision better only once, when Lordi won Eurovision in 2006.

That is why Käärijä has received many congratulations from Finland's political leaders too. President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio have also congratulated Käärijä.

Käärijä's home city Vantaa is congratulating Käärijä with a wall painting. A large wall painting, that is, a mural, will be made of Käärijä in Vantaa. Käärijä is already part of Eurovision history.

## Mother's Day

![Sauli Niinistö in a group photo with mothers at the Mother's Day celebration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_422/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11135286460dadc7f819/1684069426)

The president rewarded mothers at the Mother's Day celebration in Helsinki. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Mother's Day was celebrated on Sunday. Mother's Day has been celebrated in families and at official celebrations.

The national Mother's Day celebration was held in Helsinki. President Sauli Niinistö presented medals of honour to 39 mothers at the celebration.

The president rewarded different kinds of mothers. Among them are, for example, single parents and adoptive mothers, as well as mothers of children with special needs from different parts of Finland.

The president gave a medal of honour to, for example, 81-year-old Kyllikki Tanhua.

![Kyllikki Tanhua receives the White Rose 1st class medal on Mother's Day 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_132/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1110872645a3a9235956/1683634926)

Kyllikki Tanhua. Photo: Linda Tammela / Yle

Tanhua is a mother and a grandmother. She has also been a carer for her mother-in-law and for a sick husband.

At work Tanhua was a primary school cleaner and a safe adult for many pupils at the school. Today Tanhua takes part in the elderly council of her home municipality, Ivalo.

You can read Kyllikki Tanhua's interview at yle.fi.
