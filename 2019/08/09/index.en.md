# Friday 9.8.2019 (radio)

The Tax Administration has sent customers letters with wrong information. Finland's Minister of Science and Culture has changed. Finnish forest owners protect forests voluntarily. A strong storm threatens China. The weather in Finland at the weekend is changeable.

## The Tax Administration sent wrong information

![Tax decision](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9800080)

Open image viewer

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The Tax Administration has sent customers letters with wrong information. There has been wrong information in 27,000 letters.

A letter may, for example, have a wrong name for the spouse. Customers have also received other people's information.

The mistake is serious. Data protection is an important matter in Finland. People's information must not get into the wrong hands.

The Tax Administration says that a customer can destroy a letter that has wrong information.

## A minister has changed

![Hanna Kosonen as Minister of Science and Culture on 9 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10915911)

Open image viewer

Hanna Kosonen (right) started work as minister on Friday. Photo: Jussi Toivanen / Government Communications Department

The Finnish Minister of Science and Culture has changed. Minister Annika Saarikko took a year off work on Friday. Saarikko is soon to have a baby.

Hanna Kosonen will do the work of the Minister of Science and Culture for a year. President Sauli Niinistö appointed Kosonen as minister on Friday.

Saarikko and Kosonen belong to the Centre Party.

## Forests are protected

![Perttu Laakso](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10914292)

Open image viewer

Forest owner Perttu Laakso set up a protected area in his own forest. Photo: Timo Leponiemi / Yle

Finnish forest owners protect forests voluntarily.

Many forest owners have set up protected areas. No trees are cut down in a protected area. Nature can be left in peace.

A forest owner receives compensation, because they cannot sell the timber from a protected area. In Finland, 25 million euros have been reserved this year for compensation.

Many forest owners protect forests because of climate change. Forests slow down the warming of the climate.

## A storm threatens China

![Weather satellite image](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10915289)

Open image viewer

Typhoon Lekima is approaching China. Photo: NASA / EPA

A strong storm threatens China. The storm will hit China on Saturday.

The storm is a typhoon, that is, a tropical hurricane. The typhoon has been named Lekima.

It is feared that the storm will bring very heavy rain and wind. Therefore flights and train services on Saturday have been cancelled.

Thousands of people are being moved to safety from the Shanghai area, out of the storm's path. Officials say that the area has not seen such a strong storm in 5 years.

## The weather is changeable

![Sun shining from a clear sky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10317338)

Open image viewer

On Saturday the sun shines in the south. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

The weather in Finland this weekend is quite ordinary.

On Saturday it is dry, that is, no rain will fall. In the southern and central parts there is sunshine and the temperature is over 20 degrees. In the north it is cloudier and cooler.

On Sunday there will be rain in the southern and central parts of the country; in the north it will warm up.
