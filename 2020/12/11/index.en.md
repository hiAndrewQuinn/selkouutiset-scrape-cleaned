# Friday 11.12.2020 (radio)

The disturbance at the Olkiluoto nuclear power plant is over. On Friday 501 new coronavirus infections were reported. Most comprehensive school pupils in Turku are moving to distance learning. Germany has a serious coronavirus situation. Birds are safe on the lake's ice. The weather warms up on Saturday.

## The nuclear power plant disturbance is over

![Olkiluoto 1 and Olkiluoto 2 nuclear power plant area in Eurajoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10197128)

Open image viewer

The Olkiluoto nuclear power plant area is in Eurajoki. Photo: AOP

The disturbance at the Olkiluoto nuclear power plant is over. The disturbance started on Thursday.

The disturbance was not a danger to people. No radiation got out of the nuclear power plant into the environment.

The cause of the disturbance was a fault in the reactor water cleaning system. Because of the fault, the reactor shut down.

The fault has been repaired, and the reactor will produce electricity again, perhaps as early as Sunday.

## Turku pupils move to distance learning

![A pupil at a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4718,h_2654,x_0,y_442/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6621315e8b3b736b01b/1586183086)

Open image viewer

Turku pupils are moving to distance school. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

Most comprehensive school pupils are moving to distance learning in Turku. Because of the coronavirus, pupils from Year 4 upwards will be taught remotely.

Distance learning starts next week, and it lasts until the Christmas holiday. However, pupils in preparatory teaching and pupils with special support will get classroom teaching at school.

In the Helsinki area, comprehensive schools stay open, and classroom teaching continues in upper secondary schools too. The decision applies to the schools of Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa and Kauniainen.

The coronavirus situation is getting worse in Germany. On Friday Germany reported about 30,000 new coronavirus infections and about 600 new coronavirus deaths.

Germany has more than 80 million residents, and there are well over a million coronavirus infections.

Germany has strict coronavirus restrictions. For example, restaurants and bars are closed.

The coronavirus situation is so bad, however, that Germany is planning new coronavirus restrictions.

## Birds on the lake ice

![A swan and ducks on the shore.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1440,h_810,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4599675a6c807d9ae39/1517060272)

Open image viewer

A swan manages well in the cold. Photo: Juha Hintsala / Yle

In many Finnish lakes there is already thin ice. Birds can also be seen on the ice, for example swans.

However, many people are worried about the birds. They think that the birds will freeze to the ice. That is why they call the rescue service.

However, the rescue service says that the birds do not freeze to the ice. They can even sleep on the ice.

The lake ice is now safe for the birds, because thin ice cannot yet bear the weight of predators.
