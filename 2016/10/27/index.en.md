# Thursday 27.10.2016 (radio)

90 percent are satisfied with municipal services. Mika Lintilä is the new minister. President Niinistö visited Iran. A man gets 8 years in prison for incest. The Helsinki Book Fair began.

## Good services in the municipalities

![Statistics graphic about municipal services.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9255360)

Open image viewer

Finns are satisfied with municipal services.

Almost 90 percent of Finns are satisfied with municipal services.

Yle asked Finns what they think. Municipal services include, for example, health centres, day care centres, schools and old people's homes. Only 12 percent were not satisfied.

Yle's survey also revealed that more than half of Finns do not know who decides on matters in the municipalities. Of young people and those who have only been to comprehensive school, fewer than 20 percent knew that the council decides.

## The minister changes

![Mika Lintilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9255370)

Open image viewer

Mika Lintilä.  Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

A new minister is coming into the Finnish government.

The new Minister of Economic Affairs is MP Mika Lintilä. Mika Lintilä has been an MP for the Centre Party of Finland for 18 years. Lintilä will start in the government at the beginning of next year, when Olli Rehn moves to become director of the Bank of Finland.

Pekka Puska will become an MP in Olli Rehn's place. Puska is a retired doctor and civil servant.

## The president in Iran

![Presidents Niinistö and Rouhani.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9254777)

Open image viewer

The presidents of Finland and Iran in Tehran. Photo: Jussi Koivunoro / Yle

President Sauli Niinistö visited Iran. The visit was rare: a Finnish president has not been to Iran for more than 40 years.

President Niinistö met all of Iran's leaders. Niinistö and Iran's president Hassan Rouhani said that a new era is beginning in relations between Finland and Iran, as Western countries start trading with Iran again.  Finland and other Western countries did not trade with Iran for many years, because the Western countries believed that Iran was building a nuclear weapon.

## 8 years in prison for incest

![The main hearing of the incest case at Oulu District Court on 26.10.2016. A lawyer, the accused covering his face, and an interpreter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9252462)

Open image viewer

The man covered his face in court. Photo: Tiina-Leena Kurki / Yle

In Oulu a man has been sentenced to 8 years in prison for the sexual abuse of his own daughter, that is, incest.

The man abused his daughter for 9 years, from when the daughter was 9 years old. The girl had her first abortion at the age of 11 and she gave birth to her father's child at 16. The daughter is now an adult and she wanted the trial to be public. She wants people to no longer stay silent about incest. If incest is talked about more, children may get help more easily.

In Oulu there is an investigation into why the authorities did not help the girl.

## 1,000 writers at the fair

![The Helsinki Book Fair.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9255905)

Open image viewer

The book fair continues until Sunday evening. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

The book fair began in Helsinki on Thursday.

At the fair you can, for example, buy new and old books. You can also listen to writers there - more than 1,000 writers, scientists and artists are appearing at the fair. The fair continues until Sunday evening, and 80,000 visitors are expected there.
