Ukraine needs more ammunition. Ukraine's Foreign Minister **Dmytro Kuleba** asks that Germany speed up deliveries of ammunition to Ukraine. Kuleba spoke about the matter in an interview with a German newspaper.

Kuleba says that German arms firms are waiting for permission from the German Government to deliver ammunition.

## US bomber over the Gulf of Finland

![A B-52 bomber of the US Air Force in the air.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4830,h_2716,x_7,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1083981640d8a7108fae/1678609582)

A B-52 bomber of the US Air Force. Photo: Tech. Sgt. Richard P. Ebensberger / Pacific Air Forces Public Affair

On Saturday a US bomber came to the Gulf of Finland. The US B-52 bomber came to the Gulf of Finland from the direction of Poland.

The US bomber approached Russia's Suursaari island, but turned to Estonia before Suursaari. Russia has military activity on Suursaari.

It is unusual for a large B-52 bomber to fly over the Gulf of Finland. Military experts say that the US bomber went over the Gulf of Finland, because the USA wants to show Russia that the USA defends the West.

## Production of meat, milk and eggs

![Slaughtered pigs at Snellman Ab in Jakobstad.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2016,h_1134,x_0,y_189/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5668295cee4064d2782/1559118029)

Open image viewer

The production of pork decreased the most. Photo: Yle/Kjell Vikman

The production of meat, milk and eggs decreased in Finland last year. The Natural Resources Institute says that the production of pork decreased the most.

Pork production decreased by about 3 percent from the previous year. The production of beef, milk and eggs also decreased a little last year.

The reason is the energy crisis and inflation, that is, the rise in prices. The costs of many farms have risen a lot. The prices of energy and animal food, that is, feed, have risen a lot. Some farms stopped operating last year, because costs rose.

## Digital matriculation certificate

![Matriculation cap.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_52/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4899995b114e4eef41c/1527860864)

Open image viewer

Matriculants put on the matriculation cap. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Matriculants will receive the first digital matriculation certificates this spring.

The digital matriculation certificate makes applying, especially to foreign educational institutions, easier. The digital matriculation certificate is also harder to forge than a paper matriculation certificate. Matriculants also receive a paper matriculation certificate.

The matriculants of this spring are the first matriculants who will receive a digital matriculation certificate. The digital matriculation certificate is also given to earlier graduates who started their matriculation exam in spring 2008 or later.
