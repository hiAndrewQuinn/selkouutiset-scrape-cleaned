# Monday 15.2.2021 (radio)

Foreign Minister Haavisto visited Russia. There is a rush to process social assistance. Finland gets EU recovery money. Niinistö had a medical check-up. The opera gets a valuable violin.

## Haavisto in Russia

![Pekka Haavisto and Sergei Lavrov talking at a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11791556)

Open image viewer

Foreign Ministers Pekka Haavisto and Sergei Lavrov talked on Monday in St Petersburg. Photo: AFP

Finland's Foreign Minister **Pekka Haavisto** has visited Russia. Haavisto met Russia's Foreign Minister **Sergei Lavrov**.

Haavisto and Lavrov talked about relations between the EU and Russia. Haavisto told Lavrov that the EU does not accept that Russia has jailed the opposition leader **Aleksei Navalny**.

Haavisto and Lavrov also talked about the human rights situation in Russia and the occupation of Crimea. Haavisto says that relations between the EU and Russia are in a difficult situation.

## Social assistance applications

![A person stands in front of a notice at a Kela window listing different service numbers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5267,h_2963,x_0,y_263/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7675056012d5fd2ab39/1611847671)

Open image viewer

A Kela office. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Kela is in a great rush to process social assistance. In an interview with Yle, Kela employees say that they have to make as many as 15 decisions a day.

The employees say that because of the rush, mistakes happen.

Because of mistakes, an applicant may, for example, be left without social assistance or get too little money.

## Recovery money for Finland

![Minister of Finance Matti Vanhanen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4252,h_2392,x_0,y_226/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7269655f7c75862bf39/1601992110)

Open image viewer

Minister of Finance Matti Vanhanen. Photo: Kimmo Brandt / EPA

Minister of Finance **Matti Vanhanen** has talked about plans for how Finland will use the EU recovery funds. Finland gets about 2.7 billion euros from the EU.

Finland must first tell the EU how Finland will use the recovery money. Minister Vanhanen says that Finland wants to use the recovery money to fight climate change and to develop digitalisation, health care and employment.

Finland will present the plan to the EU in March.

## Sauli Niinistö is well

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11748801)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Riikka Hietajärvi / Office of the President of the Republic

President **Sauli Niinistö** has had a medical check-up.

Niinistö has given a medical statement to Yle. The medical statement says that President Niinistö has no illnesses or symptoms that would hinder the president's work.

In Finland there is a tradition that presidents make their health information public. Then Finns know that the president can do the presidential job well.

## The opera gets a fine violin

![Montagnana valuable violin from 1740.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4211,h_2369,x_0,y_217/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-774227602a6ad1f0fd5/1613393250)

Open image viewer

The Montagnana violin is perhaps from 1740. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

The orchestra of the Finnish National Opera gets to use a rare valuable violin.

The orchestra of the Opera and Ballet gets to borrow a rare Montagnana violin. It was built in Italy in 1740.

The support foundation of the Opera and Ballet has received a big donation, with which it is buying the Montagnana violin for the opera's orchestra.
