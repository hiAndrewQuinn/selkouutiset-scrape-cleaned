## Jyväskylä trial

![The accused sits with his lawyer in the courtroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_60/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7773056034c64422e9c/1614071414)

The trial began on Tuesday in Jyväskylä. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

An unusual trial has begun in Jyväskylä. The court is dealing with an attempted murder that is linked to politics.

2 men beat Pekka Kataja, the Finns Party's Central Finland election campaign manager, last summer.

Now the prosecutor is demanding punishment for a man who may be the other of the two attackers. The prosecutor says that the man tried to murder Kataja.

The prosecutor also says that the reason for the act was political. Behind it was a dispute within the Finns Party about who gets to be the Finns Party's candidate in the parliamentary elections.

The accused man says that he has not committed a crime.

## Coronavirus cases in Rauma

![Rauma shipyard, Rauma Marine Constructions.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11800665)

Open image viewer

Work has stopped at the Rauma shipyard because of the coronavirus. Photo: Kasper Heimolehto / Yle

There are many coronavirus infections at the Rauma shipyard. The city of Rauma is worried about the situation.

Ships are built at the shipyard, but now work has stopped because of coronavirus infections.

About 240 Rauma shipyard workers have caught the coronavirus, and about 800 workers are in quarantine.

One reason for the spread of the coronavirus is that the shipyard workers live close together in shared accommodation. Many of the shipyard workers are from abroad.

## The joint application has started

![Hairdresser training, a haircut in progress](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1687,h_949,x_0,y_158/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-762706600156b2a747f/1610700568)

Open image viewer

There are many different fields of study at vocational schools. Photo: Katja Halinen / Yle

The joint application has started. Young people can apply for a place to study at upper secondary school or vocational school.

This year, young people are required to continue studying after basic education. A new law says that young people have a duty of education until they are 18 years old. The law already affects young people who finish basic education this year.

Experts say that in the future there will be work in almost all fields. About 50 percent of current workers will be retired in 15 years.

## Teachers from abroad

­

![Oksana Kuzmina / AOP](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11171160)

Open image viewer

More teachers are needed for early childhood education. Photo: Oksana Kuzmina / AOP

Finland is hiring early childhood education teachers from abroad. Finland is hiring 45 early childhood education teachers from Spain and Greece.

There are too few early childhood education teachers, and many of them will soon retire. In 10 years' time, Finland may be missing 5,000 early childhood education teachers.
