# Thursday 3.1.2019 (radio)

The traces of the storm are still being repaired. Bad weather is slowing the searches in Norway. The Oulu suspect was caught again in Germany. Posti: fraud messages are being sent to people.

## The traces of the storm are still being repaired

![Fallen trees.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10579982)

Open image viewer

This picture is from Åland. There the storm was extremely violent. Photo: Jonas Edsvik / Nya Åland / Lehtikuva

In Finland, power cuts from the severe storm are still being repaired. Thousands of people are also without electricity today. Many tens of municipalities are suffering from power cuts. Houses are being heated by burning wood.

One person has died because of the winter storm. A man from Åland died when he was clearing trees that had fallen in the storm. Wednesday's winter storm was very severe in Finland. As many as 120,000 homes were without electricity at the same time.

After the storm there was also severe frost. Police have had to evacuate people who have no electricity. Many municipalities and companies offered emergency help to people last night.

## Bad weather slows the searches in Norway

![Map of Northern Norway](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10579487)

Open image viewer

The group disappeared in Troms county. Photo: Yle News Graphics

In Norway an attempt is being made to start searching for three Finnish men and one Swedish woman who went missing in the mountains. They were skiing in the north, in the Tromsø area.

The group disappeared in an area where there has been an avalanche. It is feared that they have been buried under the avalanche.

However, the search is difficult because of the bad weather. The Norwegian police say that, for example, a helicopter cannot be used.

According to the authorities, the helicopters may be able to fly to the avalanche area only tomorrow, Friday. The authorities say that sending searchers over land too is still too dangerous.

## The Oulu suspect was caught again in Germany

![Police station sign in Frankfurt.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10560058)

Open image viewer

German police caught the Oulu suspect for a second time. Photo: Ronald Wittek / EPA

German authorities have caught a man who is suspected of sex crimes in Oulu. The man was arrested on Wednesday evening in the city of Saarbrücken.

German police had caught the man once before, but he was accidentally released.

In Oulu, one or more people with a foreign background are suspected of a sex crime. The victim has been an underage girl.

## Posti: fraud messages are being sent to people

![A young woman uses a mobile phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10244996)

Open image viewer

Posti says that fraud attempts are made, for example, by text message to mobile phones. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Posti warns about fraud messages. According to Posti, someone is sending people text messages and emails that claim that the sender is Posti. The messages are, however, trying to defraud people.

The messages claim that the person has received a delivery, has won a prize, or that a shipping fee is unpaid. The message asks the person to pay by clicking on a link.

Posti says that the message should be deleted. You should not open the link. Posti says that its real arrival notices do not ask people to give information or open links.
