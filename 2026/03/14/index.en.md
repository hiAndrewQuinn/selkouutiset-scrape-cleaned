# News of the Week in Clear Finnish | Saturday 14.3.2026

Changes in immigration.

This week's topic is changes in immigration. Above all, people are moving to Finland from Asia.

Now we tell more about the topic.

## The number of foreigners in Finland is growing

More and more residents of Finland have a foreign background. That means a person whose parents were born abroad.

About 5.6 million people live in Finland now. About 11 percent have a foreign background.

In 2014 there were 323 000 people with a foreign background in Finland. By the year before last there were already 624 000.

There are differences between areas.

In Liipola in Lahti, 30 percent of people have a foreign background. Next to it, in the Launee–Nikkilä area, there are only 6 percent.

## The background of immigrants is changing

Most people with a foreign background have so far come from Estonia, Russia and the former Soviet Union.

At the end of the year before last there were about 180 000 of them.

Russia's war of aggression against Ukraine 4 years ago brought a change. Ukrainians have risen to the top of the migration statistics.

The real big change is that people are now moving to Finland above all from Asia.

Many people move to Finland from China or India.

The year before last there were about 40 000 of them in total. They often come to study or work in the technology sector.

In Otaniemi in Espoo, more than half of residents have a foreign background.

## Asia, the Middle East and Africa

Many people come to Finland from Asia specifically for work. These countries include the Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Pakistan, Nepal, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka.

The year before last about 90 000 people from these countries lived in Finland.

The number of Filipinos has grown the most. They have been wanted in Finland especially for care work.

People also move to Finland from the Middle East and Africa.

Countries include Iraq, Iran, Syria, Turkey and Somalia. The year before last about 98 000 of them lived in Finland.

About half of all people with a foreign background live in the Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa area.

## Summary

Let us go over it once more.

The number of people with a foreign background is growing in Finland.

About 11 percent of Finland's population have a foreign background.

The differences between areas are large.
