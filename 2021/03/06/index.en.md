# Saturday 6.3.2021 (radio)

Municipal elections are being postponed because of the coronavirus. The number of coronavirus infections continues to grow. Construction companies have tightened coronavirus measures. Long jumper Kristian Pulli brought Finland European championship bronze. The weather is getting colder.

## Municipal elections are postponed

![A voter's envelope being stamped while voting in the municipal elections at Iivisniemi school in Espoo on election day, Sunday 9 April 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11824162)

Municipal elections are being postponed because of the coronavirus.

The municipal elections were to be held on 18 April. However, the municipal elections will be postponed by 2 months. The new election day is 13 June.

The municipal elections are being postponed because Finland's coronavirus situation is poor. There are many new coronavirus infections now, and in April there may be even more coronavirus infections if the spread of the coronavirus continues.

Experts estimate that the coronavirus situation will be better in the summer. By the summer, many people will also have had time to get a coronavirus vaccination.

The parties agreed on the postponement of the municipal elections. Only the Finns Party wanted the municipal elections to be held in April. All the other parties thought that June is a better time for the elections.

## Coronavirus measures at building sites

![Building site, safety vests and helmets on a hook.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_47/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7592955ff44d5993914/1611419965)

There are now more social facilities at building sites than before. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Construction companies have tightened coronavirus measures. For example, YIT and SRV have introduced mask requirements.

At building sites there are now also more social facilities for workers. Social facilities are also cleaned more often than before.

In addition, workers' breaks are arranged so that only a small group is on break at the same time. Then there is no congestion in the social facilities.

## Bronze at the European championships

![Long jumper Kristian Pulli at the European Athletics Indoor Championships in Toruń in March 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5368,h_3020,x_0,y_243/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78178860428ba26baf1/1614974081)

Open image viewer

Kristian Pulli jumped 8.24 metres at the European indoor championships. Photo: imago images/Chai v.d. Laage/ All Over Press

Finland has won a bronze medal at the European indoor athletics championships.

Kristian Pulli brought Finland bronze in the men's long jump. Pulli came 3rd in the Friday evening final. Pulli jumped 8.24 metres.

The European champion, that is, the European gold, went to Greece, and the European silver went to Sweden.

Pulli's bronze medal is Finland's first medal in indoor athletics since 2000.

The European indoor championships were held in Poland.

On Saturday it has snowed across a large part of the country.

On Sunday the snowfall decreases and the weather gets colder. The frost is between -1 and -9 degrees.

On Monday it is dry and no snow falls. The week begins with sunny and cold weather.
