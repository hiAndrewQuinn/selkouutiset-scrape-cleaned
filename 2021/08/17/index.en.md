# Tuesday 17.8.2021

Finland suspends development cooperation in Afghanistan. Over 1,400 dead in the Haiti earthquake. The Finnish economy has recovered well from corona. 5 candidates are chasing the Ars Fennica award.

The TV plain-language news is on summer break. The plain-language news will be back on TV on Monday 23.8. The radio plain-language news is made every day, also in the summer.

## Taliban in Afghanistan

Afghans fled Taliban attacks in Ghor province in July. The Taliban have controlled the capital, Kabul, and government buildings since Monday. Photo: Jalil Rezayee / EPA

The extremist movement Taliban's grip on Afghanistan is strengthening.

Taliban people have gone into the premises of the Interior Ministry in the Afghan capital, Kabul. Video footage shows Taliban fighters moving in front of the Interior Ministry building. The fighters have weapons.

The Taliban have announced an amnesty for employees of the Afghan administration. The Taliban say that administration employees should go back to work.

Foreign countries have been bringing their citizens out of Afghanistan.

Finland suspends development cooperation in Afghanistan. Afghanistan has been Finland's biggest development cooperation partner. Finland has given Afghanistan about 30 million euros a year.

## Haiti earthquake

![Map of the Haiti earthquake.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12059220)

Open image viewer

Photo: Laura Merikalla / Yle

There was a serious earthquake in Haiti at the weekend. At least 1,400 people have died and several thousand have been injured. A state of emergency is in force in Haiti.

The earthquake destroyed thousands of buildings. The most destruction is in the city of Les Cayes. The city's hospital is completely full of people. There is a shortage of medicines.

At the moment the situation in Haiti is being made worse by heavy rain. In Haiti it rains as much as 38 centimetres of water a day.

## Ars Fennica

![Eija-Liisa Ahtila](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12060982)

Open image viewer

Eija-Liisa Ahtila. Photo: Ars Fennica

5 visual artists are chasing the Ars Fennica award. The candidates are Eija-Liisa Ahtila, Anne-Karin Furunes, Jesper Just, Viggo Wallensköld and Magnus Wallin.

Ars Fennica is Finland's biggest visual arts prize. The award turns 30 years old. The prize is 50,000 euros this year.

The winner will be announced in February next year.

The previous Ars Fennica winner was the Icelander Ragnar Kjartansson.
