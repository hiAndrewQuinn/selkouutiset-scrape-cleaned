# Saturday 10.11.2018 (radio)

The government wants to change the law on demonstrations. 17 per cent of babies are born by caesarean section. There are large fires in California. 10 per cent of Germans dislike Jews. The TV series Babylon Berlin tells of 1920s Berlin.

## The law on demonstrations may change

![People demonstrated at Senate Square in Helsinki during the meeting of Trump and Putin on 16 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10307362)

Demonstration in Helsinki in the summer. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

The government wants to change the law on demonstrations.

At present a demonstration must be reported to the police 6 hours before. The government wants demonstrations to be reported 3 days before. Then the police have time to call in more police officers to work. The government says that demonstrations are safer for everyone if there are enough police present.

Experts say that demonstrations in Finland are almost always peaceful. That is why the law may not need to be changed, the experts say.

## Many babies are born by surgery

![Ursula Änäkkälä's daughter was born on 5.11.2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10496289)

More babies are being born by caesarean section than before.

Last year 17 per cent of babies were born by a surgical procedure called a caesarean section. A caesarean section is usually done when a normal birth is dangerous for the baby or the mother. Nowadays a caesarean section is also often done because the mother is afraid of giving birth.

## Large fires in California

![A sea of flames in the background. In the foreground a firefighter sprays water on the flames.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10502254)

In California in the USA there are large forest fires.

In Paradise, a town in Northern California, 9 people have died when their home or car was caught in the middle of the fire. All the residents of Paradise fled the fire. The fire spread quickly, because there was strong wind and the ground was dry. In the town of Paradise 7,000 houses have burned.

In Southern California the fire is spreading towards the town of Malibu, where many Hollywood stars live.

## Antisemitism in Germany

![Margot Friedlander](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10496406)

Open image viewer

Margot Friedländer. Photo: Uwe Tautenhahn / Yle

In Germany 10 per cent of people do not like Jews. This is what a new survey says. In Germany there is also a lot of hate speech and crime against Jews nowadays.

97-year-old Margot Friedländer says that the genocide of the Jews began in the same way in Germany in the 1930s. It began with hate speech. Friedländer says that politicians must stop antisemitism.

Friedländer is Jewish. She survived when millions of Jews were murdered in Germany in the 1930s and 1940s.

## TV series about 1920s Berlin

![Two men](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10501673)

Open image viewer

Babylon Berlin is a German TV series. Photo: Beta Film GmbH

A big and expensive German TV series starts on television today, Saturday.

The series is called Babylon Berlin. It tells of life in the city of Berlin in the 1920s. The First World War had ended, and the Nazis were not yet in power.

Babylon Berlin starts on TV1 on Saturday evening at 22:00. It can also be watched online on Yle Areena.
