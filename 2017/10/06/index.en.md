# Friday 6.10.2017 (radio)

The anti-nuclear weapons campaign ICAN has won the Nobel Peace Prize. ICAN's Finnish coordinator Kati Juva is pleased with the decision of the Nobel Committee. The winter ski season has been opened at Ruka. A mountain is about to collapse in Norway. Luksia students tell what is new.

## ICAN wins the Nobel Peace Prize

![A group of activists stands at the gate of a base holding a red sheet that says in English: Ban nuclear weapons.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9869652)

ICAN activists protested in front of a US military base in Alice Springs, Australia. Photo: Tim Wright / ICAN / EPA-EFE

The anti-nuclear weapons campaign ICAN, that is, Ican, has won the Nobel Peace Prize. The winner was announced in Oslo, Norway.

ICAN is a coalition of civil society organisations. Its goal is for the states of the world to give up nuclear weapons. ICAN was involved in pushing for the nuclear weapons ban treaty. It was adopted at the UN, the United Nations, in the summer.

122 countries have accepted the treaty. However, no nuclear-armed state is part of the treaty. Many other countries, for example Finland and Norway, also do not intend to join the treaty.

More than 300 people or organisations were nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize.

## Joy in Finland over the prize

![Kati Juva](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7759543)

Open image viewer

Kati Juva is the coordinator of the Finnish branch of the ICAN network. Photo: Yle

Kati Juva, coordinator of the Finnish network of the ICAN campaign, is very pleased with the decision of the Nobel Committee.

Juva says that the news was expected a little, because the treaty banning nuclear weapons has been under consideration at the UN.

However, Kati Juva is disappointed that Finland does not intend to sign the treaty.

## Ruka opened the ski season

![Ruka ski season opened on 6.10.2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9869910)

Open image viewer

The season at Ruka has been opened, and it lasts until next year's Mother's Day. Photo: Ensio Karjalainen / Yle

The winter ski season has started in Finland. Ruka in Kuusamo opened its slope for the season first.

The slope could be opened, because Ruka had snow stored from last winter. Over 50,000 cubic metres of snow were spread on the Saarua slope.

Levi in Kittilä has also stored snow during the summer. It is needed for the Alpine World Cup in November.

Other ski resorts have not stored snow for skiing.

## A mountain is about to collapse in Norway

![Photo of Mannen mountain in October 2014.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8317249)

Open image viewer

Mannen mountain in 2014. Photo: Terje Pedersen / EPA

On the west coast of Norway a mountainside is about to collapse into the Romsdal valley. A boulder more than a thousand metres high is breaking away from Mannen mountain.

Mannen mountain has been monitored since 2009 because of the risk of collapse. The authorities are trying to speed up the collapse and have pumped water into the cracks in the mountain. During the night the cracks widened.

People living in the danger area were evacuated on Thursday evening. The nearby railway has also been closed because of the risk of collapse.
