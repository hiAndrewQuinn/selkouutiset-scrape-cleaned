# Friday 2.3.2018 (radio)

The SDP is the most popular party. The USA plans to put import duties on steel. More permanent work is on offer than before. Elderly care is hard work in Finland. Lahti is hosting the winter surfing world championships.

## The SDP is the most popular

![Statistics graphic on party support.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10097041)

Open image viewer

The most popular parties are the SDP, the National Coalition Party and the Centre Party. Photo: Yle News Graphics

The most popular party has changed. The SDP is now the most popular in Yle's poll.

One reason for the SDP's popularity is the demonstration and strikes that were held in January against the activation model for unemployment benefits. The activation model means that unemployment benefit gets smaller if the unemployed person does not do a little work or study now and then.

The popularity of the Left Alliance also grew a little. The National Coalition Party was the most popular party for almost a year. Now it is second and the Centre Party third.

44 per cent of Finns now support the opposition parties. About 39 per cent support the government parties.

## Customs duties on steel from the USA

![Coil storage Hämeenlinna](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7045033)

Open image viewer

Steel in a factory. Photo: Jukka Male / Rautaruukki

The USA plans to put import duties on steel and aluminium.

It means that the price of foreign steel goes up in the USA. President Donald Trump hopes that after that, US factories will use more domestic steel. Customs duties reduce trade between countries. If trade falls a lot, there may be a new recession and many people may become unemployed.

## Permanent work on offer

![Construction workers working in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10098151)

Open image viewer

There is also a lot of permanent work for construction workers. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Finnish firms are now taking on more permanent employees than in a long time.

Last year, firms started doing better and they took on more employees. Most of the employees got a permanent job. Before, most were hired only for a fixed term. Now there is even a shortage of good employees, and that is why firms want to hire them permanently.

Men get more permanent jobs than women. 80 per cent of men are in permanent full-time work, but only 66 per cent of women.

## Elderly care is harder than in the other Nordic countries

![Practical nurses at work in an assisted living ward for the elderly.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10098499)

Open image viewer

Elderly carers at work. Photo: Markku Sandell / Yle

The work of carers for the elderly is hard in Finland.

A new study compared the work of carers in Finland, Sweden, Norway and Denmark. In Finland, carers for the elderly have more clients than in the other Nordic countries. In institutions, there are 4 clients for each Finnish carer - in the other Nordic countries 2 or 3.

A similar study was done about 10 years ago. At that time, things were better in Finnish elderly care than now.

## Winter surfers at full speed in Lahti

![Winter surfing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10097794)

Open image viewer

A kite pulls winter surfers on the ice. Photo: Heikki Kiseleff / Yle

The winter surfing world championships are in Lahti. More than 100 people from 10 countries are competing in them.

In winter surfing, people move on the ice with the help of skis, boards, sails and kites. People go fast on the ice: the speed can be as much as 80 kilometres an hour. However, no motors are needed; people move with the power of the wind.
