## Climate measures

The government's climate measures are now being watched by environmental organisations. Photo: Yle

Finland's environmental organisations can take the government to court if the climate measures are not sufficient. The environmental organisations are monitoring how the goal of carbon neutrality will be reached.

In June the Supreme Administrative Court made a new decision. In future the government must be able to show that the climate measures are effective enough. The measures must meet the requirements of the Climate Act.

## Defence agreement with the USA

![Armoured vehicles with US flags fluttering on their roofs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6423,h_3612,x_0,y_669/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115464064d4b898d1e7a/1691941042)

A Stryker armoured vehicle in a military exercise at Niinisalo in Kankaanpää in May 2022. Photo: Operation 2022 / Alamy/All Over Press

Finland may make a new defence agreement with the USA.

American soldiers may be given areas in Finland for training. In these areas weapons may also be stored and equipment maintained.

No actual bases are coming.

The soldiers may be allowed to use at least one Finnish airport, port and army training area.

The agreement is the biggest change in foreign policy since Finland became a member of NATO.

In the past, Finland has only had troops from other countries during exercises.

## Children's cancer prognoses

![A toddler at the Vuori ward of the New Children's Hospital on a rainy day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3024,h_1701,x_0,y_1113/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-102324563513eb585bfb/1666269286)

Open image viewer

Most cases of children's cancer can be treated. Photo: Hanna Käyhkö

More and more children in Finland are recovering completely from cancer.

The prognoses have improved a lot since the 1970s and considerably more in the 2000s. This is what the Finnish Cancer Registry says.

Former treatments for children's cancers have developed, and new treatments have been introduced.

Over 90 percent of children are alive 5 years after leukaemia or lymphoma, that is, lymphatic cancer, has been diagnosed.

Leukaemia is the most common cancer in children.

## Emergency calls

![An emergency centre operator receiving calls at their workstation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6573,h_3697,x_14,y_169/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89246661b9e469b4765/1639573089)

Open image viewer

The emergency centres receive a huge number of contacts. Unfortunately, a large part of them are misdialled calls. Photo: Patrik Molander / Yle

A lot of emergency reports have been made at the start of the year. Emergency centres received more than 1.5 million emergency reports in total in January to June.

The number of reports grew by 10 percent compared with the start of last year.

The calls that increased the most were those that do not belong to the emergency centres' service. For example, there were 450,000 misdialled calls.

This year the Emergency Response Centre Agency has passed more than 730,000 tasks on to other authorities. The rest of the tasks were handled with the help of the emergency centre's advice and guidance.
