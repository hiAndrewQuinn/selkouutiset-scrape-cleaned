## Bike thieves are on the move again

![An electric bike with a frame lock at the edge of the Kuopio market square](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8277212)

Open image viewer

Bicycles are stolen especially in spring and summer. Photo: Vilma Ruokoski / Yle

The police warn about bicycle thieves.

Spring has got bike thieves moving again. For example, the Police of Central Finland say that the number of bike thefts has grown since the winter months.

More bicycles are stolen in spring and summer than in other seasons. A large part of the thefts are never solved.

Finance Finland says that last year more than 20,000 reports of bicycle thefts were made to the police and almost 18,000 to insurance companies.

## The United States dropped a bomb on Afghanistan

![GBU-43 bomb.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9566724)

Open image viewer

The GBU-43 bomb was dropped in Afghanistan on an area that is held by Isis terrorists. Photo: Department of Defense USA / EPA

The United States has dropped a very big bomb on Afghanistan. The bomb was dropped on an area that the terrorist organisation Isis controls.

The Afghan authorities say that the bomb killed 36 Isis fighters. The bomb also destroyed tunnels and caves that the Isis fighters used.

The US Department of Defense says that such a big bomb was now used in battle for the first time.

The bomb weighs more than 10 tonnes. In destructive power, it is the biggest bomb of the United States after the nuclear weapon.

## Russia is staying out of the Eurovision Song Contest

![Julia Samoilova](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9507608)

Open image viewer

Ukraine does not give permission to enter the country to Russia's Eurovision representative Julia Samoilova.  Photo: Eurovision

Russia plans to stay out of the Eurovision Song Contest this year. Russia is staying out because the contest is held in Ukraine and Ukraine does not want to let Russia's representative into the country.

Ukraine's ban is because Russia's representative Julia Samoilova performed in Crimea in summer 2015. Crimea used to belong to Ukraine, but Russia took it for itself 3 years ago.

The European Broadcasting Union EBU has suggested to Russia that Julia Samoilova can take part in the contest via satellite. But Russia did not agree to the suggestion. Russia also does not agree to choose a new representative for the contest.

## Kevätpörriäinen is a sure sign of spring

![Kevätpörriäinen, 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9560586)

Open image viewer

Kevätpörriäinen ("The Spring Buzzer"), the Helsinki schoolchildren's own magazine, has come out every spring for almost 70 years.  Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

Kevätpörriäinen, the very own magazine of Helsinki schoolchildren, comes out again after Easter. Kevätpörriäinen has come out every spring for almost 70 years already.

Children have drawn all the pictures in the magazine and written all the stories and jokes. The schoolchildren also take care of selling the magazine themselves.

Kevätpörriäinen's editor-in-chief Liisa Vasamies says that current affairs always show in the magazine's content. For example, this year there have been many drawings and jokes about the US President Donald Trump.
