# Sunday 11.11.2018 (radio)

The First World War ended 100 years ago. Prime Minister Sipilä: "Russia may have interfered with GPS devices." In Vantaa a hospital has started using a new computer programme called Apotti. Father's Day becomes an official flag day.

## It is 100 years since the First World War

![State leaders gathered in Paris to remember the end of the First World War.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10502765)

70 heads of state took part in the Paris celebration. Photo: Ludovic Marin / EPA

Today it is 100 years since the First World War ended.

The main European celebration was in Paris. 70 heads of state from different parts of the world took part in the celebration. President Sauli Niinistö was also there. At the Paris celebration, French President Emmanuel Macron spoke. He said that disputes must be settled with words, not with weapons. Macron also said that peace is fragile, that is, it breaks easily. He wanted people to have hope and not fear.

Tens of millions of people died in the First World War.

## "Someone deliberately interfered with aircraft"

![Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10502845)

Open image viewer

Juha Sipilä. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä says that someone deliberately interfered with the GPS devices of aircraft in Lapland this week.

It is not yet known for certain who interfered with the GPS devices, that is, the satellite navigation. However, Prime Minister Sipilä said on the radio that Russia, for example, is able to interfere with GPS devices. Sipilä said that interfering with GPS devices is as serious a matter as flying over a state without permission. The GPS devices of aircraft were interfered with in Lapland at the same time as the military alliance NATO was holding a war exercise.

Norwegian authorities said that similar interference has come from Russia before.

## Patient information in one place

![Nurses using a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10099797)

Open image viewer

Apotti is a hospital computer programme. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

In Vantaa, Peijas Hospital has started using a new computer programme called Apotti.

Apotti is a very big and complicated computer programme. It has been designed and built for many years, and it has become expensive. Apotti will be introduced in many cities in the Uusimaa region. It collects all the information of 1.6 million patients in one place.

At Peijas Hospital the introduction of Apotti went fairly well. There were no big problems.

## Father's Day becomes an official flag day

![Graphic of a loving father with a baby in his arms.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10500519)

Open image viewer

Father and baby. Photo: Asmo Raimoaho, Yle.

Father's Day is becoming an official flag day in Finland. This means that on Father's Day the Finnish flag is raised on the flagpole, as on Mother's Day too.

Finland has only 6 official flag days. Father's Day will be the seventh. The flag may also be raised on other days.

Today, Sunday, is Father's Day. In many homes children made breakfast for their dads and gave them a card or a gift.

There are about 1.3 million fathers in Finland. The most common jobs among fathers are lorry driver, sales representative and construction worker.
