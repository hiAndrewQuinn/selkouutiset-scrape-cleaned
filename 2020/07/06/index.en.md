# Monday 6.7.2020 (radio)

The transfer of unaccompanied minor asylum seekers to Finland from refugee camps begins. The Pharmacists' Association has opened an online drug search service. About 12,000 new recruits have started national service. Italian composer Ennio Morricone has died. Eino Leino Day was celebrated on Monday.

The TV Clear News has a summer break, and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Children are brought to Finland from refugee camps

![A child carries a water barrel at the Moria refugee camp in Greece.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11355128)

The Moria refugee camp on the Greek island of Lesbos. Photo: Orestis Panagiotou / EPA

The transfer of unaccompanied minor asylum seekers from camps to Finland begins. The transfer from refugee camps in the Mediterranean to Finland begins this week.

25 children and young people who are without a guardian will come from Greece. In February, Finland decided to receive 175 unaccompanied minor asylum seekers. The first group is now coming to Finland.

The Finnish Immigration Service, Migri, does not reveal the exact arrival date.

Because of the corona epidemic, the asylum seekers will first be in 2-week quarantine. After that they will be placed in centres that specialise in receiving minors.

## Medicines are not always available at the pharmacy right away

![Infographic of shortage notices reported to Fimea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11431751)

Open image viewer

The Pharmacists' Association has opened an online drug search service. Anyone can search by drug name for information about where the drug is available.

The drug search was developed because not all medicines have always been available at every pharmacy. By the end of June, over 1,200 shortage notices had been made. That is as many as in the whole of the year before last.

Most medicines can be replaced with a parallel medicine, or the medicine can be obtained from a wholesaler in a few days.

## 12,000 young people started the army

![Black combat boots of conscripts.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_148/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5933955d89b7abad0b3/1589793420)

About 12,000 recruits have started national service, that is, the army, in Finland today. Most begin in the Army. The rest serve in the Navy, Air Force and Border Guard units.

Over 700 of the recruits are women.

Because of the corona epidemic, the recruits and instructors are divided into 3 units. The groups take turns between service and leave.

## Composer Ennio Morricone has died

![Ennio Morricone conducting an orchestra in Amsterdam on 21 February 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8742635)

Open image viewer

Ennio Morricone conducting an orchestra in Amsterdam on 21 February 2016. Photo: Paul Bergen / EPA

Italian composer Ennio Morricone has died. He died at the age of 91.

Morricone composed music for 500 films over 6 decades. He is remembered especially for his compositions for Sergio Leone's western films. The best known is the theme song from The Good, the Bad and the Ugly.

Morricone was nominated for an Oscar 6 times and won the award in 2015. In addition, in 2007 he received an honorary Oscar for his life's work.

## Eino Leino Day was celebrated on Monday

![Statue of Eino Leino in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9572464)

Open image viewer

Statue of Eino Leino in Helsinki. Photo: Esa Hiltula / AOP

On Monday Eino Leino Day was celebrated in Finland. It is celebrated on the birthday of the poet Eino Leino, 6 July. It has been a flag day since 1992.

Eino Leino was born in 1878 and died in 1926. He was a poet, journalist and critic.

6\. July is also the Day of Poetry and Summer.
