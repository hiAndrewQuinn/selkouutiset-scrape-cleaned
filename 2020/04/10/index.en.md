# Friday 10.4.2020 (radio)

The managing director of the National Emergency Supply Agency is resigning. Euro countries have agreed on 500 billion euros of economic aid. A lot of baking supplies are being bought now. Very low ozone levels have been observed in Finland. Pope Francis will lead the Good Friday events without an audience.

## The head of the National Emergency Supply Agency resigns

![Tomi Lounema](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11302747)

Open image viewer

Tomi Lounema. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The managing director of the National Emergency Supply Agency is resigning from the post. Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that director Tomi Lounema does not enjoy trust.

The National Emergency Supply Agency has paid millions of euros for respiratory masks that did not meet quality requirements.

Marin says that the agency acted wrongly. The agency did not check the backgrounds of the parties it made the deal with well enough. There are, for example, financial crimes behind it.

## Euro countries agree on financial aid

![Wopke Hoekstra ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11302443)

Open image viewer

Dutch Finance Minister Wopke Hoekstra attended a meeting of euro ministers in The Hague. Photo: Bart Maat / AFP

The euro countries have agreed on 500 billion euros of financial aid. It will be used to help the economies of the euro countries during the corona epidemic.

There are 27 member states in the EU, of which 19 use the euro currency.

After the euro crisis of 2019, a crisis fund was created, that is, the EVM. EVM stands for the European Stability Mechanism. Its money can now be used for health care costs.

Shared debt of the euro countries, that is, coronabonds, will not come.

## Now a lot of baking is being done

![A baker arranging ladyfingers on a baking tray.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10230380)

Open image viewer

Baking is now popular, when people are at home a lot. Photo: Antti Karhunen / Yle

People have to be at home as much as possible now, so that the coronavirus does not spread. This shows in the sales of the shop chains.

A lot of baking supplies are being bought now. For example, the S Group says that flour and yeast are being bought 2 times more than before the epidemic.

Lidl and K Group also report the same. Yeast has at times even run out.

Otherwise too, the lockdown has increased shops' turnover this spring. For a while, sales have grown by many tens of percent.

## The ozone layer over Finland is thin

![Sunglasses on a table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8790883)

Open image viewer

It is worth protecting yourself from UV radiation. Photo: Yle

Very low ozone levels have been observed in Finland. There has been almost half less ozone over Finland than normal in spring.

Ozone is located in the uppermost layer of the atmosphere, that is, in the stratosphere.

The cause of the ozone loss is a strong polar vortex. It may stay near Finland for perhaps another 10 days.

The ozone layer protects the Earth from ultraviolet radiation. There is a lot of UV radiation now, because the ozone layer is so thin. It is worth protecting your skin outside, even if it is cloudy. Sunglasses are important now.

Pope Francis will lead the Good Friday events in the Vatican in the evening without an audience.

The Good Friday mass and the Way of the Cross prayer event will be streamed live.

The Easter Sunday Urbi et Orbi (to the city and the world) address will also be held as a live stream this year because of the coronavirus situation.

Normally St Peter's Square in the Vatican is full of people at Easter.
