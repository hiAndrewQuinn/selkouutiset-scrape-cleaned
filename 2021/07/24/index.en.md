The growth in corona infections continues.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare said on Saturday that there are 570 new corona infections in Finland. Most new infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa region.

In addition, it has been reported that there is 1 new corona death in Finland. In total, 980 people have died in Finland because of corona.

There are now 54 corona patients in hospital care.

Bad corona news has come from around the world. The corona situation is bad in Indonesia, in Asia, for example.

India is suffering from heavy rains. At least 125 people have died, because the rain has caused landslides and accidents.

The number of dead may rise, because many people are missing. Houses were buried under the landslides.

Rescue workers have moved people to safety.

In the Indian state of Maharashtra there has not been rain this heavy for 40 years.

## Collisions with large game animals

![Close-up of a white-tailed deer in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10302024)

Open image viewer

There are more deer in Southern Finland than before. Photo: Heli Mansikka / Yle

There are more collisions with large game animals in Finland than before.

Ten years ago there were about 4,000 collisions with large game animals a year, but now already more than 13,000 a year.

In a collision with a large game animal, a car hits an animal from the deer family: a moose, a deer or a roe deer. A collision with a moose is the most dangerous, because the moose is the biggest of the deer family.

Most collisions, however, happen with deer. The number of deer has grown quickly in Southern Finland. That is why deer also move on the roads more than before.
