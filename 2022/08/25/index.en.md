# Thursday 25.8.2022 (radio)

Fortum made a loss. As many people travelled in Finland as before the corona epidemic. The summer was warm. There is quite a lot of blue-green algae in the sea.

## Fortum

The energy company Fortum did badly in the spring. Between April and June Fortum made a loss of over 9 billion euros. A year earlier Fortum's loss was 840 million euros.

Fortum's result was bad, because the German Uniper made large losses. Fortum owns part of Uniper. In July Fortum and the German state agreed that Fortum will in future own a smaller part of Uniper than before.

The Finnish state owns over 50 percent of Fortum.

## Tourism

![A thrill ride at Linnanmäki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12424206)

Open image viewer

Linnanmäki is a popular tourist destination in Helsinki. Photo: Ulla Malminen / Yle

In July there were the same number of tourists in Finland as 3 years ago. So now as many people travel in Finland as before corona.

In July there were 9 percent more Finnish people in Finnish hotels than in July 2019. In July there were 29 percent fewer foreign tourists than in 2019. The most foreign tourists were from Sweden, Germany and the USA.

A hotel room cost on average 118 euros in July. A year earlier the average price of a hotel room in Finland was 106 euros.

## A warm summer

![Highest temperatures 1.6.–21.8.2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12591850)

Open image viewer

Highest temperatures 1.6.–21.8.2022. Photo: Matti Kurkela / Yle

The summer has been warmer in Finland than usual.

The highest temperature of the summer was 32.9 degrees. The temperature was measured in Pori at the end of June. In Utsjoki in Northern Lapland the highest temperature was 32.5 degrees.

The highest average temperature was in the Kuopio area. The summer was also really warm in the Kouvola area.

The most rain fell in the summer in South Ostrobothnia. In Ylistaro almost 90 millimetres of rain fell in one day on 5 August.

There is still quite a lot of blue-green algae in the sea. There is more blue-green algae than usual at this time of year. There may still be blue-green algae in the sea late into the autumn.

There is a lot of blue-green algae in the sea this summer, because the weather has been warm.

In the lakes there is a normal amount of blue-green algae.

## The weather

The weather is dry across the whole country, that is, no rain falls. The temperature on Friday in the daytime is between 18 and 24 degrees.

Warnings of forest fires have been issued in the regions of Kymenlaakso and South Karelia.
