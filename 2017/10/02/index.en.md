# Monday 2.10.2017 (radio)

More than 50 people have died in a mass shooting in Las Vegas. In the referendum in Catalonia, those who want independence won. Ambulance staff and firefighters will get bulletproof helmets and protective vests. At the Satakunta central hospital in Pori, a patient has died of tuberculosis.

## Dozens died in Las Vegas

More than 50 people have died in a mass shooting in Las Vegas in the United States. More than 200 people were injured.

The shooting happened near a casino, outside of which a country music festival was being held. The suspect fired at the crowd from the upper floors of a building. The police have identified the shooter, who was a 64-year-old local man. The shooter apparently took his own life.

The reason for the mass killing is not yet known. On Monday the police assessed that the man did not belong to any armed group.

## Catalans voted for independence

![Catalans celebrate the voting result in the evening in central Barcelona.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9861632)

Catalans celebrated the voting result on Sunday evening in Barcelona. Photo: EPA / Santi Donaire

Supporters of independence have won the Catalan referendum.

The regional government of Catalonia says that 90 percent voted in favour of Catalonia becoming independent from Spain.

Hundreds of people were injured on Sunday, when the Spanish police tried to stop the voting. Authorities seized ballot papers and ballot boxes. Spain considers that the referendum was illegal.

## Ambulances get protective equipment

![Ambulance worker puts on a protective vest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9856928)

Open image viewer

Ambulance staff get protective vests and bulletproof helmets as protective equipment. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Ambulance staff and firefighters will get bulletproof helmets and protective vests as protective equipment. The equipment will be obtained this year and next year. The reason for preparing is violence and the threat of violence. Behind it is a risk assessment that was made with the police.

The Interior Ministry has instructed the rescue services to acquire protective equipment. Staff have also been instructed to avoid wearing uniforms in their free time. In Europe there have been several situations where staff in uniform have been attacked.

For example, in Helsinki violent situations happen about once a week. That is why staff already have protective vests that protect against stab wounds.

## A patient has died of tuberculosis in Pori

![Man coughing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9397525)

Open image viewer

Fortunately, tuberculosis is rare nowadays, and it does not spread easily. Photo: Laura Tolonen / Yle

At the Satakunta central hospital in Pori, one patient has died of tuberculosis.

The man, who had a foreign background, died last Wednesday. He had come to the hospital the Saturday before. The Satakunta central hospital says that the patient could no longer be treated.

The man had sought treatment for symptoms already in early spring, but cancer was suspected in health care. Tuberculosis is very rare, and it was not thought of as a possibility. Therefore, a chest X-ray was not taken of the man early enough.

The City of Pori and the Satakunta hospital district are now looking for other people who may have been exposed to tuberculosis. Everyone who was exposed will be told about it personally, and they will be called for examinations.

Doctors say that tuberculosis does not spread easily.
