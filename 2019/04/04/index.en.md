# Thursday 4.4.2019 (radio)

Jihadist activity has increased. More and more people are going on unemployment pensions. Payment defaults have increased. No fault was found in the Ethiopian crash plane. Summer speed limits come into force. Yle held a plain-language election debate.

## Jihadist activity has increased

![Map of Syria](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10650819)

Open image viewer

About 80 jihadists have left Finland for Syria and Iraq. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Jihadist activity has increased in Finland. For example, about 80 jihadists have left Finland for Syria and Iraq. 20 of them are women. Most of the women who went to Syria are Somalis or Finns who have converted to Islam. Jihadism means an Islamist movement that opposes the social order and carries out terror attacks. According to the Ministry of the Interior, there is very little jihadism in Finland.

## More and more people have gone on disability pensions

![lonely man on a bench, skaters in the background](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10627627)

Open image viewer

The growth in mental health problems can also be seen in people going on unemployment pensions. Photo: AOP

Going on disability pensions is increasing. Last year 7 percent more people went on disability pensions than the year before. More people went on unemployment pensions because of mental health problems than before. Sick leave has also increased because of mental health problems.

## Payment defaults increased

![credit card is cut](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10011120)

Open image viewer

A payment default record can prevent you from getting a credit card or a loan. Photo: AOP

The number of payment defaults is growing. A payment default affects more than 8 percent of Finns. The number has grown since last year. In Finland there are over 380,000 people with a payment default record. A payment default record comes if you do not pay your bills.

## Pilots of crashed plane followed instructions

![Rescue workers at the site where the Ethiopia Airlines plane crashed into the ground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10712646)

Open image viewer

A Boeing 737 MAX aircraft crashed in Ethiopia in March. Photo: EPA

The investigation into the Ethiopian air crash continues. Ethiopian authorities say that the pilots of the crashed plane followed the instructions, but they could not bring the plane under control.

The authorities say that no technical faults have been found in the plane. 157 people died when a Boeing 737 MAX aircraft crashed in Ethiopia in March.

## Summer speed limits for roads

![Road Administration worker changed the winter speed limit to a summer speed limit on Lahdentie. Archive photo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9549607)

Open image viewer

On many motorways you may drive 120 km/h in summer. On country roads the highest speed limit in summer is 100 km/h. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Summer speed limits come into use on country roads and motorways next week. In summer you may drive faster on many roads than in winter. Some roads may have potholes, or the asphalt may be broken after winter. If the road is in poor condition, the summer speed limit is not changed on that road.

## Election debate in plain language

![Party representatives in the election debate.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10723117)

Open image viewer

The studio audience shows if the debate is hard to understand. Photo: Kaisa-Maaria Hilden / Yle

Today Yle's first plain-language election debate has been published. In it, politicians try to speak so that everyone understands. Politicians from 9 parties are taking part.

The debate talks about things that interest users of plain language. The studio audience shows politicians whether the speech is easy or hard. The plain-language election debate can be watched on Yle Areena.
