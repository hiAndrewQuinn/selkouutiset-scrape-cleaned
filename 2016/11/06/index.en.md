## The air in Delhi is dangerously polluted

![Cars and mopeds on a road under an underpass. Everything is covered by a brownish-yellow smog. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9274863)

Open image viewer

Traffic in the Delhi smog last Wednesday. Photo: Rajat Gupta / EPA

There is now bad smog in the capital of India. The air in Delhi is so bad that you can see only maybe 50 metres ahead.

Delhi's air quality is now the worst in 17 years. The recommendation of the World Health Organization, WHO, has been exceeded 70 times over. The government has ordered schools to be closed for three (3) days because of the pollution.

In Delhi about 30,000 people die every year because of bad air. The pollution usually comes from diesel, coal power plants and factories. In addition, farmers burn straw after harvesting the rice crop.

Air quality is also very poor in other big cities of Northern India.

## Lack of sleep makes you eat more and fattier food

![A silhouette in a window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8811180)

Open image viewer

After a short night's sleep you feel like eating more than normal, and fatty food too. Photo: Antti Seppälä / Yle

A person eats more than usual if they have slept too little. A study says that long-lasting lack of sleep can cause overweight.

After a short night's sleep a person eats about 400 kilocalories more than after enough sleep. A tired person also often eats fatty food.

Studies have already found earlier that there is a link between lack of sleep and children and young people putting on weight.

## Sunday has been Swedish Day

![Blue and white-yellow flowers in a bouquet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9275516)

Open image viewer

Swedish Day is a national celebration and flag day in Finland. Photo: Charlotte Vainio / Yle

Swedish Day has been celebrated in Finland on Sunday. In Swedish the name of the day is svenska dagen. Swedish Day is also a flag day.

Swedish Day is always on 6 November. Then the Swedish language is celebrated, which is Finland's second official language.

About 300,000 Finns speak Swedish as their mother tongue. Swedish-speaking Finns, or Finland Swedes, live mostly in coastal areas. However, Finland-Swedish culture is important for the whole of Finland.

## November is now white in the south

![A woman walks in the snowfall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8589472)

Open image viewer

In the south the ground is white already in November, for the first time in a long while. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

November in Southern Finland is turning white. Snow will fall heavily early in the week, at least on the south coast. The weather forecast says that 10-20 centimetres of snow may fall.

The situation is rare, because in the coming week Southern Finland has more snow than anywhere else in Finland. Many people have been waiting to be able to go skiing or sledging.

In the coming week temperatures will be below zero, that is, freezing. That is why the snow will stay and not melt. The wind is strong, and you must be careful in traffic.
