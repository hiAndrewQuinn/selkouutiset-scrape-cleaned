# Tuesday 12.5.2020 (radio)

Schools are ready for the children. The best matriculation exam result was written in Tampere. Face mask requirement on Finnair flights. Spring sowing has started.

## Schools ready for Thursday

![Classroom at Leppälä school.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_239/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6721605eb3e1096d555/1588847045)

This morning the government said how things will be done in schools when schools open on Thursday.

Different teaching groups will be kept apart. Pupils are not close to each other at school. Each pupil uses their own tools at school, for example pencils and erasers. Hands are washed many times a day at school.

The Basic Education Act will be changed so that in the autumn, schools can sometimes have distance teaching and sometimes teaching at school, if the corona situation is difficult in the autumn.

## Face mask requirement on Finnair flights

![Finnair Airbus A320 aircraft at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_47/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-31483758307400cb0ee/1479570515)

Open image viewer

Finnair Airbus A320 aircraft. Photo: Markku Ojala / AOP

The airline Finnair is introducing a requirement to wear face masks on its flights.

Passengers and staff must wear face masks on the aircraft. The mask requirement comes to Finnair flights next Monday. You can also make a face mask yourself.

The staff at Finnish airports must also wear a mask. Airport staff must wear a face mask if they serve customers and if there is no plastic sheet, that is, a protective Plexiglas screen, in between. The airport company Finavia recommends that passengers too should wear a mask at airports.

## The best matriculation exam result in Tampere

![Matriculation cap in the middle of roses](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7998929)

Open image viewer

A matriculation cap. Photo: Raila Paavola / Yle

The best matriculation exam result of the spring was written in Tampere. At Tampere Lyceum, one student wrote 10 top grades (laudatur) and one second-best grade (eximia) in the matriculation exam.

About 25,000 Finns will graduate from upper secondary school this spring. The students graduate on 30 May. The students put on a white cap. Traditional graduation parties cannot be held because of the gathering restriction.

On the Yle website there is a matriculation calculator, where you can see who will pass the matriculation exam this year.

## Spring sowing is under way

![Tractor sowing in spring](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10215849)

Open image viewer

A tractor sowing in spring. Photo: Kari Kosonen / Yle

Cereal sowing in Southern Finland is about a week ahead of normal. In Southern Finland, about 70% of spring cereals have already been sown. In Central Finland and in Central and Northern Ostrobothnia, sowing has only just started.

On strawberry and currant plants that are protected with gauze, flower buds can already be seen in Southern Finland. On strawberry fields that were not protected with gauze for the winter, damage can be seen, because there was no snow to protect them.

Raspberry growth has also started in Southern and Central Finland.
