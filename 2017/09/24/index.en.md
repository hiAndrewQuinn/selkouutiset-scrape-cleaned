# Sunday 24.9.2017 (radio)

Germany has had a parliamentary election. Some MPs want to ban buying sex completely. Football fans fought in Helsinki. A Taiwanese cyclist is travelling in Finland.

## The German election

![A person votes in the German federal election.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9849030)

Germans voted in the parliamentary election on Sunday. Photo: Philipp Guelland / EPA

Germany has had a parliamentary election. The Christian Democrats got the most votes in the election.

The leader of the Christian Democrats is Angela Merkel. Merkel is also the Chancellor of Germany, which means she leads the German government.

The Christian Democrats' success in the election means that Merkel can continue as Chancellor.

The AfD party also did well in the election. It is a fairly new party. It is now getting members into the German parliament for the first time.

AfD, or Alternative for Germany, is a far-right party. For example, it does not want immigrants in Germany.

AfD will not get into the government even though it did well in the election. The other German parties do not want to cooperate with it.

## Buying sex

![a woman in fishnet tights leans against a fence](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9848872)

Open image viewer

Politicians are discussing whether buying sex should be banned completely. Photo: AOP

In Finland's parliament, the Eduskunta, the sex business is being discussed.

Some MPs of the Centre Party want buying sex to be banned completely. They say that a ban is needed because people are exploited in the sex business.

Now Finnish law says that buying sex is wrong if the seller of sex is under 18 years old. It is also not allowed to buy sex from a victim of human trafficking who has been forced to sell sex.

## A fight between football fans

![Football training](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9503652)

Open image viewer

Football is sometimes also a cause of quarrels. Photo: Ari Lahti / AOP

There was a big fight in the centre of Helsinki during the night between Saturday and Sunday. A little over 10 people were involved in the fight.

The police say that the fight started from a quarrel between supporters of two football teams. Fans of HJK and Ilves got angry with each other.

Many police officers were needed at the scene before the situation calmed down.

## A Taiwanese cyclist

![ Taiwanese world traveller Reo Jui-Ti Wang is grateful.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9848177)

Open image viewer

The Taiwanese cyclist Ryo Jui-Ti Wang got new equipment from Finns when his old things were stolen. Photo: Jaakko Matikainen.

Finally, a news story about a Taiwanese cyclist who is travelling in Finland.

30-year-old Ryo Jui-Ti Wang cycled from Taiwan to Finland through China and Russia. The journey took a year.

In Finland, Wang wanted to see Santa Claus. The journey to Santa Claus in Rovaniemi stopped in Kemi, because someone stole Wang's bike and things. The police found the bike, but not Wang's things.

When Finns heard Wang's story, many wanted to help him. Wang has received clothes and equipment from Finns. Now he can cycle to Santa Claus.

After meeting Santa Claus, Wang's bike journey will continue. He plans to travel around the world for 10 years.
