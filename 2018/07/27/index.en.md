# Friday 27.7.2018 (radio)

Finland is sending help for the firefighting in Sweden. Hotels and shops are doing more night work than before. Greek authorities suspect that someone set forest fires on purpose. The sea water is now warm. 2018 may become the warmest year on record. On Friday evening there is a total lunar eclipse.

## Finland helps Sweden

![Group of firefighters about to leave for Sweden to put out forest fires, posing in front of a fire engine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10325684)

Finnish firefighters about to leave. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Finland is sending help for the firefighting in Sweden. In Sweden, big forest fires are being put out in different parts of the country.

About 30 Finns are leaving to put out the forest fires in Sweden. Finland is also sending firefighting equipment to Sweden, for example fire engines and hoses.

The Finnish firefighters will be in Sweden by Saturday morning at the latest. They will help the Swedes with the firefighting for at least a week.

Sweden has also received help from many other countries.

## Night work is unhealthy

![Prisma Kaari 24h shop](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10017817)

Open image viewer

Night work is done in many shops. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Hotels and shops are doing more night work than before. A lot of women work in these sectors.

The risk of women getting breast cancer and heart disease grows if they work many nights in a row.

That is why the government intends to change the law so that night shifts may be worked no more than 5 in a row.

## Greek forest fires are being investigated

![Greek rescue workers carry a body bag in the town of Mati.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10323220)

Open image viewer

Over 80 people died in the forest fires in Greece. Photo: Angelos Tzortzinis / AFP

Greece has released more information about the country's forest fires. Greek authorities suspect that someone set forest fires on purpose.

The fire burned a lot of forest in Greece this week, and over 80 people died in the fires.

The worst fires began on Mount Penteli. Now the authorities are investigating why the fire on Mount Penteli started. The authorities suspect a crime.

## The sea has warmed

![A pilot boat takes a pilot to a cargo ship.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9702679)

Open image viewer

The sea water is now warm. Near Finland in the Baltic Sea, the water temperature is already 27 degrees.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute has measured sea water temperatures for over 20 years. The sea has never been this warm before.

## The heat continues in Europe

![A man walks and a woman sits in front of the Eiffel Tower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10311321)

The hot summer in Finland continues. The weekend will also be hot and warm. The temperature during the day is 25-30 degrees.

The summer is hot across the whole of Europe. The temperature has been over 30 degrees in Britain, France and Germany, for example.

Researchers say that this whole year has been warm. It is possible that 2018 will become the warmest year on record.

## The lunar eclipse is visible on Friday

![red moon](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10325060)

Open image viewer

The eclipse turns the Moon red. Photo: Rolex Dela Pena / EPA

On Friday evening there is a total lunar eclipse. The Moon will be completely in the Earth's shadow for almost 2 hours.

During the lunar eclipse the Moon looks dark red. The lunar eclipse can be watched without protective glasses.

The total lunar eclipse begins on Friday evening at 22:30, and it ends at 00:13.
