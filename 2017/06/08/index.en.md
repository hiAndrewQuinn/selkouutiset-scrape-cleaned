# Thursday 8.6.2017 (radio)

The National Coalition is still Finland's most popular party. The Britons have voted in the parliamentary election. Plants are flowering later than usual this summer. In Lapland you can now ski in summer weather.

## Party support

![Ville Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9558631)

Open image viewer

Greens chairman Ville Niinistö. Support for the Greens has risen during Niinistö's time. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

The National Coalition is still Finland's most popular party, Yle's party survey says. Of the survey's respondents, a little over 20 percent supported the National Coalition.

The Centre got the second most support, a little over 18 percent. The Centre and the National Coalition are in the government.

The Social Democrats, that is the SDP, have dropped to third place. Their support was below 18 percent. The SDP is not in the government; it is the largest opposition party.

The support of the Greens, the other opposition party, has risen. The Greens were the fourth largest party in the survey, with support of about 15 percent.

Behind the Greens came the Finns Party, which is in the government together with the National Coalition and the Centre. The Finns Party's support in the survey was 9 percent.

## Elections in Britain

![Jeremy Corbyn and Theresa May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9656252)

Open image viewer

Labour leader Jeremy Corbyn and Conservative leader Theresa May. Photo: Dan Kitwood, Lindsey Parnaby / AFP

Elections were held in Britain on Thursday. The Britons have voted in the parliamentary election.

Britain's largest parties are the Conservatives and Labour. Prime Minister Theresa May is the leader of the Conservatives, and she will be able to continue as prime minister if the Conservative party wins the election.

May's rival is the Labour leader Jeremy Corbyn. On Friday it will become clear which of the party leaders will be Britain's prime minister after the election. In opinion polls the Conservatives have had more support than Labour.

## Flowers late

![Bilberry in flower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9656703)

Open image viewer

Bilberry flowers can only be seen in Southern Finland. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Plants are flowering later than usual this summer. The spring was cold, and that is why the flowers are about 2 weeks late.

For example, bilberry is only flowering in Southern Finland. The June weather will decide how many flowers the bilberry gets, and later how many berries.

Bird cherry flowers have also only been seen in Southern Finland and in part of Central Finland. The bird cherry is a tree whose flowers' scent is, for many Finns, the best scent of summer.

## Skiing in summer weather

![Saariselkä's Iisakkipää summit on 7 June 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9654704)

Open image viewer

There is still snow on the ski slopes of Saariselkä. Photo: Eki Saniola / Ski Saariselkä

In Lapland it is still possible to ski, even though the weather has warmed up. The spring was so cold that snow is still left.

Downhill skiing is also possible. The Saariselkä ski centre opened its ski slopes on Thursday. You can go downhill skiing until Saturday.

You can now go downhill skiing in summer weather. Up to 20 degrees of warmth has been promised in Lapland until Saturday.
