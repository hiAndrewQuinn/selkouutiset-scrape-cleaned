# Thursday 16.5.2019 (radio)

Finns must change their habits because of climate change. There has been a bad fire in Pori. Many schools will soon get an easy-to-read book about Kimi Räikkönen. The northern lights may be visible in the sky on Friday night. The weekend will be warm.

## We must change our habits

![Traffic jam in Ruoholahti](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10228439)

Open image viewer

Traffic emissions must be reduced. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Finns must change their habits because of climate change. Finns must cut the emissions that cause climate change. This is what a report from the Sitra organisation says.

Sitra researchers say that Finns must stop, for example, driving cars in their free time. Traffic emissions will fall if people use trains and buses more and cycle.

The researchers say that Finns must also change their eating habits. For example, Finns must stop eating red meat, because raising livestock increases emissions.

Sitra thinks that Finns must cut emissions a lot and quickly, if climate change is to be slowed down.

## Apartment block burned in Pori

![Fire at Keskuskartano](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10785417)

The fire burned the attic of an apartment block. Photo: Jenni Joensuu / Yle

There has been a bad fire in Pori, in western Finland. However, nobody died or was injured.

The fire started in the attic of a big apartment block. The fire burned the attic and the top floor of the flats.

The police are investigating the cause of the fire.

## Schools will get an easy-to-read book about Räikkönen

![Kimi Räikkönen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10757631)

Open image viewer

Formula One driver Kimi Räikkönen. Photo: Getty Images/Dan Istitene

Many schools will soon get an easy-to-read book about Kimi Räikkönen. The book tells the story of the life of Formula One driver Kimi Räikkönen.

The author of the easy-to-read book is Hanna Männikkölahti. She has made an easy-to-read version of a book called "The Unknown Kimi Räikkönen".

Many lower secondary schools will get the book in May or June. The book will only go on sale in August.

"The Unknown Kimi Räikkönen" is one of the books that help encourage pupils to read. It is easier for pupils to start reading as a hobby with an easy-to-read book.

## Northern lights in the sky

![Northern lights.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10444627)

Open image viewer

Northern lights in the sky. Photo: Jussi Lindroos / Yle

On the night between Thursday and Friday, the northern lights may be visible in the sky. The northern lights are made of particles that travel from the Sun towards the Earth.

This may be the last chance to see the northern lights this spring. In summer the northern lights are hard to see, because summer nights are light in Finland.

## Summer is near

The weekend will be warm.

Yle meteorologist Joonas Koskela says that in the south the temperature may already be almost 25 degrees on Sunday. In the north it is colder.

At the weekend it is also sunny. The sun shines so warmly that it is good to protect your skin, meteorologist Joonas Koskela says.
