# Friday 25.8.2017 (radio)

A security expert wants to improve Finns' first aid skills. Asylum appeals have congested the administrative courts. Stockholm is celebrating 100 years of Finnish independence. Finns have made many nature donations. The weather feels like autumn.

## Many people lack first aid skills

![The rescue service at the scene of the stabbing at the Turku market square on Friday 18 August 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9785616)

A security expert proposes compulsory first aid and safety training in schools.

At the moment, most people do not know how to act correctly in accidents and emergencies, says security expert Jari Stolt.

For example, in the Turku knife attack many people concentrated only on filming what was happening in the square.

Self-defence courses have filled up in Turku after last Friday's knife attack. Women in particular have signed up for the courses. Most of the victims of the Turku stabbing were women.

## Congestion in the administrative courts

![The sign of the Helsinki Administrative Court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8962178)

Open image viewer

The administrative court decides an asylum appeal in about 7-8 months. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Finland's administrative courts are badly congested because of asylum appeals.

Almost everyone who has received a negative asylum decision makes an appeal.

More people have been hired to handle the appeals. Even so, you must wait about 7-8 months for the administrative court's decision.

If the administrative court's decision is appealed to the Supreme Administrative Court, you must wait about 3 more months for the decision.

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä has said that the handling of appeals must be speeded up and the waiting time made shorter.

## The 100-year-old celebrates next door

![ The audience in Kungsträdgården park on Thursday evening.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9797050)

Open image viewer

A big audience came to Kungsträdgården. Many also spoke Finnish. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Stockholm is celebrating Finland's 100th birthday.

In Kungsträdgården park there is a 3-day city festival where you can get to know Finland and Finnishness.

The programme includes, for example, the Moomins and going to the sauna, a mobile phone throwing competition and wife carrying. Music is performed by, for example, the young singers Alma and Isaac Elliot.

About 100,000 visitors are expected at the event.

## Nature donations

![Cotton grass on the Hankasuo bog.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6682079)

Open image viewer

A protected area can be, for example, a forest or a bog whose nature is diverse or especially beautiful. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

As much as a thousand hectares of forest is to be protected in honour of Finland's 100th anniversary year.

So far, over 600 hectares of private forest have been protected. In addition, protection decisions are pending for about 400 hectares. ¨¨

A campaign has encouraged Finns to give forest as a protected area.  A protected area can be, for example, a forest or a bog whose nature is diverse or especially beautiful.

## Autumn in the air

![Maple leaves on the ground in autumn.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9456792)

Open image viewer

Autumn turns the maple leaves yellow. Photo: Hanne Leiwo/Yle

The weekend weather looks as if autumn has begun.

The sun may shine in parts of the country, but mostly it will be cloudy, rainy and cool.

Yle meteorologist Toni Hellinen says that sleet or snow may already fall on the fells of Lapland. The temperature in Lapland is about 5 degrees.

In the south the temperature is perhaps 15 degrees.

Toni Hellinen says that it is now colder than usual for this time of year.

Asylum appeals are congesting the administrative courts. First aid training is wanted in schools. Finland, at 100, celebrates in Stockholm.
