# Tuesday 14.2.2017 (radio)

An important adviser to the US president has resigned. Finns want to toughen punishments. Finns' relationships are doing well. A dam threatens to break in the USA.

## An important adviser to the US president resigns

![Michael Flynn](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9458832)

Open image viewer

The president's security adviser Michael Flynn. Photo: Andrew Harrer / EPA

The perhaps most important adviser to the President of the United States is resigning from his post. The president's adviser on security matters, the national security adviser Michael Flynn, is resigning because he was caught lying. He had lied to the vice president, which is a serious matter.

The scandal is about the adviser having talked with a Russian diplomat without permission and having lied about it. The adviser had been in the job for less than a month.

## Citizens want harsher sentences

![Isolation cell in a prison](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9423659)

Open image viewer

Harsher punishments are wanted for violent and sexual crimes.  Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

Finns want harsher punishments for criminals. In a survey, citizens and judges said that sentences must be harsher, especially for serious crimes. Harsher punishments are wanted for violent crimes and sexual crimes. Punishments for drug crimes and drink-driving, that is driving while drunk, are also wanted to be increased.

Justice Minister Jari Lindström says that the opinions of Finns will be listened to when the government considers reforming the laws.

## Initiative on mercy killing goes to Parliament

![Speaker of Parliament Maria Lohela receives a citizens' initiative supporting the legalisation of euthanasia. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9459174)

Open image viewer

Speaker of Parliament Maria Lohela received the initiative in Parliament.  Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

An initiative on euthanasia, that is mercy killing, has been handed to Parliament. The initiative asks Parliament to make a law that would let a doctor help a patient to die. At the moment, helping a person to die is a crime.

The initiative collected more than 60,000 Finnish signatures.

## Relationships are doing well

![A young man is spray-painting heart decorations.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9457176)

Open image viewer

Finns are happy with their relationships.  Photo: Safin Hamed / AFP

Finns' relationships are doing well. Almost 90 percent of people say that they love their wife or husband. Marriages are also quite happy, because 75 percent of people think their own marriage is happy.

Not everyone finds their love right away, though. About 40 percent of marriages end in divorce. A new love may then be the right one.

Today is Valentine's Day, which is the day for lovers and good friends.

## A dam threatens to break in the USA

![Aerial photo of the Oroville dam](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9456758)

Open image viewer

Oroville dam Photo: EPA / California department of water resources

Almost 200,000 people have had to leave their homes in the state of California in the USA, because a large water dam threatens to break. The Oroville dam is the highest dam in the USA, and a large hole 9 metres high has appeared in it. If the dam breaks, the water threatens to destroy homes and other buildings in the area.

Officials are repairing the dam with excavators and helicopters.  The worst danger is over for now. However, new storms have been promised for the area, and the dam could fill up with water again almost completely.
