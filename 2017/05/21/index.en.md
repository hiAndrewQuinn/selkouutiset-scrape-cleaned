# Sunday 21.5.2017 (radio)

A Finnish woman has been kidnapped in Afghanistan. A war heroes' funeral has been held in Lappeenranta. Finnish volunteers are invited to Africa to test a diarrhoea vaccine. The Baltic ringed seal is in danger. The Doomsday Vault is being improved.

## A Finnish woman was kidnapped in Kabul

![The international guest house in Kabul that was attacked on 21 May. In the attack a Finnish woman was kidnapped and a German woman and a local guard were killed. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9625268)

The guest house in Kabul that was attacked on 21 May. Photo: Shah Marai / AFP

A Finnish woman has been kidnapped in Kabul, the capital of Afghanistan.

The Finn was kidnapped in an attack on the accommodation of an aid organisation. The Finnish woman was doing aid work through a Swedish organisation. A local guard and a German employee of the aid organisation died in the attack.

The Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs demands that the kidnapped woman be released immediately.

## A funeral was held for fallen soldiers in Lappeenranta

![The remains of 38 unidentified Finnish soldiers were laid in a common grave in Lappeenranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9625290)

Open image viewer

Cadets moved the coffins from the lawn to the common grave. Photo: Ulla Ylönen / Yle

A war heroes' funeral for the Day of Remembrance of those who fell in the wars has been held in Lappeenranta.

At the ceremony almost 40 fallen soldiers who have not been identified were blessed and buried. Of them, 10 fell in the Winter War and about 30 in the Continuation War.

The fallen have been found at the battle sites of the Karelian Isthmus and Ladoga Karelia.

The ceremony is part of the Finland 100 anniversary year.

## Finns test a diarrhoea vaccine in Africa

![A small child lies on a drip. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9625238)

Open image viewer

Diarrhoea is one of the biggest causes of death of children under 5 in developing countries. Photo: Abir Abdullah / EPA

Finnish volunteers are invited to Africa to test a diarrhoea vaccine.

The University of Helsinki and its research partners make the invitation on a website. The test trip lasts 2 weeks and is half free. During the year 800 people will be taken into the tests. Nothing else is required of them except holidaying in Benin in West Africa. Half of the travellers get the ETEC vaccine, half a placebo vaccine.

Every year tens of thousands of small children in developing countries die of diarrhoea.

## The Baltic ringed seal is in danger

![Baltic ringed seal](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5538583)

Open image viewer

The Baltic ringed seal is the smallest seal species in the world. Photo: Ekokuva Oy / Mauri Rautkari

The Baltic ringed seal is in danger of disappearing from its southern habitats. The nature conservation organisation WWF says that the biggest threat to the Baltic ringed seal now is climate change. The ringed seal needs ice and snow for its den.

There are about 20,000 Baltic ringed seals, which is a tenth of the number 100 years ago. As late as the early 1900s there were a few hundred thousand ringed seals in the Baltic Sea. The populations collapsed because of hunting and environmental poisons.

Most Baltic ringed seals live in the Bothnian Bay. The Baltic ringed seal is the smallest seal species in the world.

## The Doomsday Vault is being maintained

![The Doomsday Vault in Svalbard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9018368)

Open image viewer

The Doomsday Vault is in Svalbard, Norway, where there is permafrost. The vault needs strengthening. Photo: Timo Sipola / Yle

The seed store in Svalbard, Norway, is being strengthened. The seed vault is in the ice inside the permafrost. A waterproof wall is being built in the seed vault, because last year's heat caused water damage.

The vault is also called the Doomsday Vault, because it keeps the genes of the world's plants in case of disasters.

The vault stores, for example, all the landrace strains of Finnish grain crops. A landrace strain is a variety that was already being grown before scientific plant breeding.
