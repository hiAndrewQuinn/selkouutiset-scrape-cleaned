## SDP and NATO

![SDP chair, Prime Minister Sanna Marin at the SDP party council meeting in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_113/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9370766248274d6ce04/1648895859)

SDP chair, Prime Minister Sanna Marin at the SDP party council meeting in Helsinki. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Sanna Marin believes that Finland's possible membership application to the defence alliance NATO could be quick.

Marin said at the SDP party council that Finland must seriously consider military alliance again.

Marin said that she will tell about her own NATO position during the spring. Marin is also the chair of the SDP.

Centre Party chair Annika Saarikko said on Yle's Ykkösaamu that the Centre Party's party council will deal with NATO membership in a week's time.

## Ukraine

![A woman stands next to a badly damaged apartment block.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-935409624431fc8cc17/1648636829)

On Friday more than 3,000 people were evacuated from the city of Mariupol in Ukraine. This was reported by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

An estimated 160,000 Ukrainians may still be stuck in Mariupol, which Russian troops are besieging.

British military intelligence says that Ukrainian troops have advanced on many fronts.

Intelligence says that Russian troops have pulled back near the capital Kyiv, and from Antonov airport in Hostomel.

Ukrainian troops have also regained parts of eastern Kharkiv. There has been heavy fighting in the area.

## Bicycle mechanics

![Bicycle repairer at work at Järvinen's bicycle shop in Lahti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-840136611124de0a77c/1628513638)

Open image viewer

Bicycle repairer at Järvinen's bicycle and sports shop in Lahti. Photo: Mikko Tuomikoski/Yle

There are too few bicycle repairers in Finland. Cycling is becoming more and more popular, and there are many new bicycle models. That is why repairers need new skills.

However, only 1 school in Finland trains bicycle mechanics. A survey by the Finnish Cyclists' Federation in 2019 says that most bicycle repairers have learned the trade themselves.

In Hämeenlinna, Tavastia Vocational College began training bicycle mechanics in 2019. The training was the first in Finland. About 30 students have completed the training.

The studies are part of the basic vocational qualification in the car trade. In the autumn, bicycle mechanic training will also start elsewhere in Finland.
