# Radio Clear News Thursday 18.5.2023

Government negotiations and taxes. Drug drivers. Group lotto taxation. Italian floods. Spanish drought. The weather.

## Government negotiations

![A woman's hand holds a cigarette on a table, where there is also an ashtray and a cigarette packet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_84/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81402660b60e5ce8405/1730451612)

The new government may decide to raise the tobacco tax. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

The government negotiations continue. Now tax is being discussed in the government negotiations.

4 parties are in the government negotiations. They are the National Coalition, the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats.

The National Coalition wants to lower the payroll tax. According to the National Coalition, the state can raise more money by raising harm taxes, for example the tobacco tax.

The Finns Party does not support lowering the payroll tax. In the party's view, it is not sensible to cut taxes at the same time as the state has a lot of debt.

The government negotiations are led by National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo. Orpo's goal is that the parties agree on a new government during June.

## Drug drivers

![A police officer stands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_400/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7774666034f89f854c0/1614084478)

Open image viewer

Police monitor traffic. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

The number of drug drivers has decreased. A drug driver drives a car even though they have used drugs.

In 2022 the police found about 3000 fewer drug drivers in traffic than in 2020.

There are fewer drug drivers because the police have found many criminals' drug hiding places. That is how the police assess it.

Police inspector Heikki Ihalainen says that safety in traffic has improved. When the number of drug drivers decreases, traffic accidents also decrease.

## Group lotto

![A pen and a lottery coupon.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_547/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6340985e2860c677eae/1579705558)

Open image viewer

Many people play lotto in a group. Photo: All Over Press

The rules for group lotto are changing. In group lotto, a group of people plays lotto together.

The new rules say that all players in the group must register, for example on Veikkaus's website.

If some players in the group lotto are not registered with Veikkaus, they must pay tax on a lotto win. Normally a lotto win is tax-free.

## Italian floods

![Flood water has risen to the fields.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3375,h_1889,x_0,y_70/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-111579664660ba20d7c9/1684425915)

There are severe floods in Italy. The cause of the floods is heavy rain. The rain has continued in Northern Italy for many days.

Flood water and landslides have caused a lot of damage. Thousands of people have had to flee the floods. More than 10 people have died in the floods.

## Spanish drought

![A dried-up channel between fields.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11154986464d748829fd/1684333810)

Open image viewer

The weather causes problems in Spain too. Spain is suffering from drought, because there has been no rain in the country for a long time.

The drought harms a large part of Spain's countryside. Spanish farmers say that, for example, over 50 percent less wheat and olive oil is obtained than usual.
