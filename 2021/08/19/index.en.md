## Evacuation from Afghanistan

A total of 34 Finns have now been brought to Finland from Afghanistan, says Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto.

In addition, some Afghans who have worked for Finland have been brought to Finland from Afghanistan. There are still a few dozen Finns in Afghanistan.

People are fleeing Afghanistan, because the strictly Islamist Taliban has taken power.

## Quota refugees

![Interior Minister Maria Ohisalo visited Yle's Aamu on 19 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_227/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-843608611ddcb9d1acd/1630497864)

Interior Minister Maria Ohisalo visited Yle's Aamu on 19 August. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Interior Minister Maria Ohisalo of the Greens is of the view that Finland must take in more refugees. Finland is preparing for people to flee from Afghanistan.

Finland has previously promised to take in 1,050 so-called quota refugees from various countries. The Interior Ministry's new proposal is that Finland takes in 2,000 refugees.

The governing party, the Left Alliance, supports the Interior Ministry's proposal. The opposition parties, the National Coalition and the Finns Party, oppose raising the refugee quota. In the view of the Finns Party, Afghans must be helped close to Afghanistan.

## Cultural events

In theatres and concerts they will give up the 2-metre safety distances. This is what Culture Minister Antti Kurvinen says.

At the moment there must be a 2-metre safety distance in the theatre and concert audience. The aim is that corona does not spread. Organising performances is difficult, among other things because the audiences must have safety distances.

## Commuter municipalities

![Aerial photo of Helsinki's Kalasatama in May 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81317360b3385405bc0/1671099260)

In Helsinki's Kalasatama both services and public transport are close. Photo from last May. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

During the corona era many people have moved to a commuter municipality. A commuter municipality is a municipality next to a big city.

Among others, people are moving from Helsinki, Tampere and Oulu to a smaller municipality.

Commuter municipalities have now grown much faster than before corona.

People are moving most to Tuusula, Järvenpää and Sipoo. All of these are near Helsinki. The number of residents is also growing quickly in Kaarina. Kaarina is near Turku.

People move to commuter municipalities, for example, because flats are cheaper than in big cities.
