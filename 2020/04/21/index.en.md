# Tuesday 21.4.2020 (radio)

The price of oil. Remote entrance exams. Summer events. Resident birds. The weather.

## The price of crude oil is really low

![Oil pumps](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9598263)

Open image viewer

There is too much crude oil in the United States. Photo: Larry W. Smith / EPA

Strange things are happening in the world economy now.

In the United States the price of crude oil is really low. Yesterday it happened that for a moment the price of crude oil was negative.

That means that the seller of oil paid the buyer for buying the oil.

No buyer wanted to buy crude oil, because buyers already have all their storage full of oil.

There is too much oil now, because of the coronavirus pandemic it is not being used as much as usual.

It may be that the fall in the price of crude oil will later also show at petrol stations.

Perhaps the price of petrol will fall.

In Finland, the petrol that is filled into cars is expensive. The Finnish state puts a lot of tax on the price of petrol.

## University remote entrance exams

![Students at a lecture at the Helsinki School of Economics.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10976088)

Open image viewer

No big joint entrance exams will be held this spring. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Universities and universities of applied sciences will say next week how they will hold entrance exams this spring.

Universities and universities of applied sciences are not holding normal entrance exams because of the coronavirus epidemic.

Entrance exams will be held remotely, that is, they will be done online.

Holding entrance exams remotely is complicated.

The organisers must consider, for example, how applicants will prove their identity.

## Ruisrock is not happening this summer

![A beautiful evening and sunset at the festival day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10865564)

Open image viewer

In 2019 there was an evening this beautiful at Ruisrock. Photo: Rosa Huuska / Yle

One big summer event has been cancelled because of the coronavirus.

Ruisrock in Turku announced today that the event will not be held this summer.

Ruisrock is always in July in Turku, and it has already been held 50 times.

The organisers say that they do not want to hold the festival if it cannot be done safely

## Birds are disappearing from the forests

![A great spotted woodpecker on a tree.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4463975a1e7adf83761/1511947431)

Open image viewer

The great spotted woodpecker is the most common woodpecker in Finland. Photo: Raine Martikainen

Birds have decreased in Finland's forests.

Many resident birds live in the forests. Resident birds live in Finland all year round.

Resident birds are decreasing because a lot of trees are cut down in Finnish forests.

Especially in the forests of Southern Finland, birds have disappeared.

For example, the willow tit and the great spotted woodpecker have decreased very much.

There is very little protected forest in Southern Finland.

Protected forest must not be cut down.
