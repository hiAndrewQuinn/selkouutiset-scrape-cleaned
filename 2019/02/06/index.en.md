## Yle investigated deaths in care homes

![A man who needs crutches.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10529716)

Yle has investigated the deaths of elderly people in care homes.

There may have been 15 deaths in care homes that were caused by poor care. A death may have been caused, for example, by a medical error. The authorities are investigating deaths from the last 2 years.

In addition, Yle knows of many cases in which a relative accuses a care home of causing an elderly person's death.

## Vaccination coverage in Finland is good

![A baby being vaccinated.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10440819)

Open image viewer

Children in Finland are vaccinated. Photo: AOP

Almost all children in Finland are given a vaccination. Vaccination prevents diseases from spreading.

For example, measles is a contagious disease. Measles spreads easily if vaccination is missing.

Some parents think that vaccinations can be harmful. In Ostrobothnia, for example, too few children are vaccinated.

## People have to wait for an identity card

![Picture of the design of the new passports and identity cards introduced at the start of 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9873107)

Open image viewer

People have to wait a long time for an identity card. Photo: Police

Police stations still have serious queues. People may have to wait up to 2 months for an identity card.

A driving licence can no longer be used to prove identity when applying for a passport. That is why many people are applying for a new identity card.

A driving licence can still be used to prove identity, for example, in a shop or at the post office. An identity card is only needed for online banking codes or a passport. A valid passport can always be used to prove identity.

## The Sámi have celebrated their national day

![Sámi National Day 2019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10633181)

Open image viewer

Residents of Inari, Riitta Lehtola and Anne Kirste Aikio, are celebrating the Sámi National Day. Photo: Vesa Toppari / Yle

The Sámi National Day was celebrated on Wednesday. The Sámi are an indigenous people. The Sámi have their own history, language and culture.

Sámi live in Finland, Sweden, Norway and Russia. There are about 10,000 Sámi in Finland. The Sámi live in Sápmi in Lapland. A lot of Sámi also live in Helsinki, for example.

The Sámi speak the Sámi language. There are 3 Sámi languages in Finland.

The Sámi also have their own flag.
