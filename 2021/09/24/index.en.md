# Friday 24.9.2021 (radio)

Remote work can soon be ended. Helsinki shares information about corona vaccinations in different languages. The environmental movement Elokapina plans a 10-day demonstration. Walking and cycling are to be increased in Finland. Rain ends at the weekend.

## Remote work ends

![Lecturer Pentti Heikkinen giving a remote lecture at home.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2472,h_1391,x_0,y_174/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6662255e9dc2d6193bb/1587397528)

Many people have been working from home because of corona. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Many employees may soon be able to stop remote work. The ministries propose that the remote work recommendation ends in 3 weeks.

For example, many office workers have been working from home for a long time because of corona. Now the ministries propose that employees can return to their workplaces on 15 October.

Remote work can be ended, or at least reduced, in mid-October because of corona vaccinations. It is estimated that about 80 per cent of people over 12 in Finland will have received corona vaccinations by then.

In many fields remote work is not possible. For example, retail and construction workers have continued to work at workplaces throughout the corona period.

## Demonstration next week

![Elokapina demonstration on Mannerheimintie](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4158,h_2339,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82353760cf91ade61a8/1624217297)

Elokapina has also organised demonstrations before. Photo: Berislav Jurišić / Yle

The environmental movement Elokapina is planning a new demonstration. Elokapina intends to close traffic in central Helsinki in the middle of next week.

The idea is that traffic is blocked for 10 days. This is how the Elokapina movement intends to protest against Finland's environmental policy.

Elokapina demands that the government takes climate change seriously. In the Elokapina movement's view, a climate emergency must be declared in Finland.

The Elokapina movement held a similar protest in the summer. The movement's supporters sat in the street in Helsinki and stopped traffic.

## More walking and cycling

![A cyclist rides in a bike lane on Hallituskatu in Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8469656)

Open image viewer

Photo: Antti Pylväs / Yle

Walking and cycling are to be increased in Finland.

The Ministry of Transport's report hopes that walking and cycling trips will grow by 30 per cent in about 10 years.

Walking and cycling improve people's health and well-being. It is also good for the environment that people drive less.

One way to increase cycling is to support the purchase of electric bikes, the Ministry of Transport's report says.
