# Friday 13.4.2018 (radio)

Finland's economy is growing. Experts are investigating whether a gas weapon has been used in Syria. Girls have a more positive attitude to multiculturalism than boys. Finnish adults are fatter than before. More men than women die because of accidents. Spring is moving on.

## Finland's economy

![Containers being unloaded from a ship at the port of Mussalo in Kotka.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10154800)

Open image viewer

Trade is going well and the economy is growing. Photo: Sakari Saksa / Yle

Finland's economy is doing well. Finland's economy is now growing fast.

The Ministry of Finance estimates that the economy will grow by 2.6 per cent this year. In the coming years too, the economy will grow by more than 2 per cent a year.

The number of unemployed people will also go down in the coming years, the Ministry of Finance estimates.

The task of the Ministry of Finance is to plan Finland's economic policy.

## The war in Syria

![Columns of smoke after bombing in Douma.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10150086)

Open image viewer

Douma 7.4.2018. Photo: Ammarx Safarjalani / AOP

A group of international experts is travelling to Syria. They will investigate whether a gas weapon has been used in the war in Syria.

The Syrian opposition says that the Syrian army dropped dangerous gas on the town of Douma a week ago. The gas killed many people.

The experts will start their investigations at the weekend. Syria has promised that it will not disturb the experts' work.

The Syrian opposition has fought against the Syrian government and its army for many years already.

## Young people and multiculturalism

![A project coordinator from the Walter association talks with four pupils from Donner school in a gym.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6393247)

Open image viewer

Girls have a more positive attitude to multiculturalism than boys. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

Young Finns and young adults think that multiculturalism has good sides.

Girls have a more positive attitude to multiculturalism than boys. Multiculturalism scares or annoys many boys.

The information comes from a report by the Finnish National Agency for Education.

## The health of Finns

![A man shows off his round belly.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9918793)

Open image viewer

Finnish adults are getting fatter. Photo: AOP

There is bad news about the health of Finns.

Finnish adults are fatter than before. Already almost 30 per cent of adults are obese.

Young people are also in poor shape. About 10 per cent of 30 to 40-year-olds have difficulty running 100 metres.

The information comes from a study by the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

## Accident Day

![A leg in a plaster cast.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10158092)

Open image viewer

Men hurt themselves more often than women. Photo: Alistair Scott / AOP

Accident Day was held on Friday.

This time the theme of the day is that too many accidents happen to men. More men than women die because of accidents.

Men often hurt themselves at home or in their free time. Men may forget about safety, for example, when they are doing renovation work.

## Spring weather

![spring sun shining](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10158566)

Open image viewer

The sun will shine on Saturday. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen /Yle

Spring is moving on. Yle meteorologist Marjo Hoikkanen says that Saturday may be the warmest day of the week. In the south it may be 15 degrees. In the north the temperature will be 5 to 10 degrees.

On Sunday it may rain in the south. But that is a good thing, because rain cleans the air. There is now a lot of street dust in the air.
