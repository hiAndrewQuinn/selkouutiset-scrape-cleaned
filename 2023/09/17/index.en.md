## Construction

Very few new homes are now being built in Finland.

Few homes are also being built in the big cities of Southern Finland. Usually the most homes are built there.

For example, in Vantaa construction companies have started building fewer than 450 homes this year. That is 80 percent less than the normal amount.

In Helsinki, construction companies have started building about 3 000 homes. That is also much less than the normal amount.

Few new homes are being built because people are not buying them. They are afraid of economic problems.

Construction companies are in a bad spot. Therefore the construction companies hope for state support.

## Libya flood

![Rescue workers in protective overalls move flood victims wrapped in plastic bags.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3192,h_1795,x_0,y_119/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117301065067e1418b3d/1694924479)

Rescuers are finding more dead people in Libya. Photo: EPA

In Libya, in North Africa, over 11 000 people have died in the flood. This is what the UN, that is the United Nations, says.

Many tens of thousands of people are without a home.

The flood happened a week ago, when two dams broke near the city of Derna. The cause was a storm.

After that, the water surged into the city.

Authorities and rescuers keep finding more dead people.

Authorities say that many more dead people will still be found.

## Albert Edelfelt

![Statue of Albert Edelfelt on the left, and on the right the artist's paintings hung on a green wall at the Ateneum exhibition.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113376964941b8a29c41/1687428233)

Open image viewer

Image from the Ateneum Albert Edelfelt exhibition. Photo: Tiina Kokko / Yle

The Albert Edelfelt exhibition at the Ateneum Art Museum was the most popular in the museum's history. The exhibition opened in May and ended on Sunday. Over 340 000 people visited the exhibition.

Many of the exhibition's paintings were on display in Finland for the first time.

In the history of the Ateneum, the second most people have visited the Pablo Picasso exhibition, which about 315 000 people visited.

Albert Edelfelt was a famous Finnish artist. He was born in 1854 and died in 1905.

The Ateneum is Finland's best-known art museum. It was opened in Helsinki in 1888.
