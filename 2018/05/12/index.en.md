# Saturday 12.5.2018 (radio)

Adoptive families are about to get the same parental leave rights as other families. The worst flood in many years is coming to Saimaa. Muslim scholars say that terrorism goes against the principles of Islam. Saara Aalto competes in the Eurovision final in Portugal this evening.

## The rights of adoptive families are improving

![a child holding its parents' hands](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10201832)

Open image viewer

Adoptive families have been in a different position from other families. Photo: AOP

Adoptive families are about to get the same rights to parental leave as other families have.

At the moment, adoptive parents get about a month less parental leave than biological parents. Adoptive families of children over 7 years old have not received any parental leave at all. An adoptive family also receives less home care allowance than other families.

The changes will come into force perhaps at the start of next year.

## The worst flood is coming to Saimaa

![A half-sunken boat in the port of Lappeenranta](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10200269)

Open image viewer

The flood threatens boats and cottages on the shore of Saimaa. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

The worst flood in many years is coming to Saimaa in Eastern Finland. Saimaa is the biggest lake in Finland and the fourth biggest lake in Europe.

Last autumn a lot of rain fell, and in the winter a lot of snow came. Because of this, the water level of Saimaa is rising quickly. At the moment the water level is 15 centimetres higher than normal. The water level may still rise by perhaps 30 centimetres during the summer.

In Tornio in Lapland, building of flood protection has started in the centre of the town. In Tornio the flood may be the worst in 50 years, and several houses are threatened with getting wet. There is also a risk of flooding in Kittilä. A flood is predicted for Rovaniemi that will be one of the biggest in the measurement history.

## Muslim scholars condemn terrorism

![Indonesian President Joko Widodo greets Muslim scholars who have arrived in Indonesia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10202026)

Open image viewer

Indonesian President Joko Widodo greets Muslim scholars. Photo: Bagus Indahono / EPA

Muslim scholars say that terrorism goes against the principles of Islam.

Muslim leaders from Afghanistan, Pakistan and Indonesia have gathered in Indonesia. There are 70 respected Muslim leaders at the meeting. They are trying to bring an end to the conflict in Afghanistan.

At the same time, the meeting gave Muslims around the world a religious instruction, a fatwa. The fatwa says that violent extremism and terrorism go against the principles of Islam.

## A whale got stuck in a net in Merenkurkku

![A whale in Merenkurkku](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10202211)

Open image viewer

Sea rescuers filmed the whale that they had released. Photo: Sea Rescue Centre

A whale got stuck in a fish trap in Merenkurkku. The Sea Rescue Centre received a report about the whale in the afternoon, shortly before two o'clock. The whale has been freed from the net.

Whales are very rare in Finnish waters.

## Mr. Lordi came to cheer on Saara Aalto at the Eurovision

![Mr. Lordi and Saara Aalto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10201880)

Open image viewer

Mr. Lordi came to cheer on Saara Aalto in Lisbon. Photo: Berislav Jurišić / YLE

Finland's Saara Aalto competes this evening in the Eurovision final in Portugal.

Aalto's performance is number 17. That is the same number as Lordi, who won the Eurovision in 2006. That was Finland's first win. Mr. Lordi met Saara Aalto at the Lisbon Eurovision arena on Friday.

The broadcast of the Eurovision starts at 22:00 on Radio Suomi, on TV2 and on Yle Areena.
