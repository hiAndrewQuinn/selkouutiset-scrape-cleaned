# Tuesday 15.2.2022 (radio)

Talks about Ukraine. Summer jobs for young people. False information about social services in Sweden. An easy-language commentary on the UMK final. Weather.

## Talks about Ukraine

![President Vladimir Putin photographed at a press conference in Moscow on 7 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12318740)

Russian President Vladimir Putin. Photo: Thibault Camus / EPA

A solution is being sought to the Ukraine crisis.

Germany's Chancellor Olaf Scholz has met Russian President Vladimir Putin. Scholz and Putin agree that talks about the Ukraine crisis can continue.

In the view of Finland's Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto, it is important that world leaders meet each other. Haavisto believes that a solution to the Ukraine crisis can be found through talks.

The talks aim to prevent Russia from attacking Ukraine. Russia has moved soldiers close to Ukraine's border. Russia wants to prevent Ukraine from joining the NATO military alliance.

This year there are many summer jobs on offer for young people.

For example, there are summer jobs in hotels and restaurants. It is estimated that at least 18,000 summer workers will be hired for the hotel and restaurant sector.

There are even more summer jobs this year than before the corona time. However, the good situation requires that no new corona restrictions come.

## False information in Sweden

![A child looks out of a window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4567,h_2569,x_0,y_471/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6615835e8745804ff59/1604930862)

Open image viewer

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

False information about social workers is spreading in Sweden.

In Arabic-language social media groups it is said that Swedish social workers are kidnapping children from Muslim families.

Many Muslims are angry because of the false information. For example, in Gothenburg there was a demonstration against social services.

The demonstrators say that social workers take children away from Muslim families more often than from Swedish families.

Social workers say that the aim is to help children. In immigrant families there may be serious problems, because immigrant families do not always get help in time.

Soon the representative of Finland for this year's Eurovision Song Contest will be chosen.

The winner of the New Music Competition, UMK, gets into the Eurovision Song Contest. The winner will be decided in the UMK final on Saturday 26 February.

This year the UMK final will also be commented on in easy language. The UMK final can be followed in easy language on Yle Areena.

On Wednesday it snows in Central and Northern Finland. In the south-west there is sleet and rain.

In Lapland the sun may shine a little, and it is 10 degrees below zero. In the south the temperature on Wednesday is +2 degrees.

On Thursday new snowfall will arrive in Finland from the south. It will snow on Friday too.

Because of the snowfall, driving conditions are poor on Thursday and Friday.
