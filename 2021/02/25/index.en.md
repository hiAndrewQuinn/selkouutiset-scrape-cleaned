## Finland will be shut down

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin, Interior Minister Maria Ohisalo and Science and Culture Minister Annika Saarikko at the government press conference on 25 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_184/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7784476037795c59085/1614435567)

The Finnish government is tightening measures against the coronavirus.

On Thursday the government said that a lockdown will come to Finland in March.

The lockdown applies to most of Finland, because the coronavirus situation has worsened in different parts of the country.

During the lockdown, the upper grades of comprehensive school, upper secondary schools and vocational schools will be on remote teaching. Hobby activities for people over 12 years old will also be stopped.

In addition, the government plans to close restaurants. Bars and cafés are also planned to be closed.

The lockdown starts on 8 March, and it lasts 3 weeks.

More coronavirus restrictions will come to the Helsinki region as early as next week.

Next week, for example, private gyms and sports facilities will be closed in the Helsinki region. Spas will also close.

In addition, indoor play areas and the lounge areas of shopping centres will be closed.

The reason for the orders is that the coronavirus is spreading quickly in the Helsinki region.

The number of coronavirus infections continues to grow. On Thursday it was reported that there are 565 new coronavirus infections.

There are 3 new coronavirus deaths. In total, 740 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

About 320,000 Finns have received the coronavirus vaccination.

## Icebreakers at work

![Icebreaker Fennica.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1200,h_675,x_0,y_17/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-778281603748430bc10/1614235786)

The icebreaker Fennica is back at work. Photo: Arctia Oy

Already this winter, 7 icebreakers have started work in Finland. There is now a lot of ice at sea because of the hard winter frosts.

Finland has 8 icebreakers in total, that is, ships that break ice at sea so that other ships can get through.

On Thursday the icebreaker Fennica went out to sea. It last worked 3 years ago. For the last two years there was so little ice that Fennica was not needed.

## Bears at Korkeasaari

![Both bears have been playing and wrestling in the snow today, one leaning against a tree, the other digging in the snow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11809777)

Open image viewer

The bears' hibernation at Korkeasaari has ended. Photo: Mari Lehmonen / Korkeasaari

The bears at Korkeasaari have woken from hibernation. They came out of their winter den on Thursday.

The bears of Korkeasaari zoo slept for 3 months this winter. In nature, bears can sleep for longer. In Northern Finland, for example, bears can sleep as long as half a year.
