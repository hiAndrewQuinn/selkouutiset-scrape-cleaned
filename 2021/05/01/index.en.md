# Saturday 1.5.2021 (radio)

May Day has been spent calmly. Politicians have given May Day speeches. India's bad corona situation continues. Taxi roof lights are now mandatory in taxis. The weather is cold, but dry.

May Day has been spent calmly. Because of the corona, there have been no big gatherings.

People have spent May Day at home or outdoors in small groups.

In the parks of big cities there were many people on the evening of May Eve. In Helsinki, for example, the police cleared Kaivopuisto park. There were too many people in the park, in this corona time.

![Petteri Orpo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4502,h_2532,x_0,y_373/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-770137601beb5905438/1619533863)

Petteri Orpo, leader of the opposition National Coalition Party, criticised the government in his May Day speech. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

Party leaders have also given speeches this May Day.

The speeches were virtual, because the political parties did not have big May Day celebrations because of the corona.

SDP party leader and Prime Minister Sanna Marin spoke about the time after corona. Marin said that money for education, social benefits and health care will not be cut.

The opposition parties, the National Coalition Party and the Finns Party, criticised the government's economic policy. For example, the leader of the National Coalition Party, Petteri Orpo, said that the government is borrowing too much.

India's bad corona situation continues.

In India, more than 400,000 new coronavirus infections were found in one day. In addition, more than 3,500 Indians died because of the corona in one day.

India is trying to vaccinate all adults as quickly as possible against corona. However, in many Indian states, corona vaccines have run out.

## Taxis

![A taxi in Helsinki in July 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6977265f071acaa7d6d/1594301611)

Open image viewer

The taxi roof light is now mandatory. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The rules for taxis have changed. The taxi roof light is now mandatory in taxis.

The taxi roof light must be at least 20 centimetres wide and 10 centimetres high. The taxi roof light must be yellow and its text must be black.

The taxi roof light helps customers recognise a taxi. In addition, the taxi roof light makes it easier to supervise taxis.

## Weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11911068)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

The weather at the May Day weekend is quite cold. Cold air is coming to Finland from the north.

During the day in Southern Finland it is about 10 degrees warm. Elsewhere it is colder. At night there is frost across the whole country.

However, the weather is dry, meaning no rain will fall. In Central Finland a little snow or sleet may fall on Sunday.

At the start of next week the weather will continue to be similar. So the weather is quite cold, but dry.
