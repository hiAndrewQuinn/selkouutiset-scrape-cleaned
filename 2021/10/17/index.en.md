## Problems with the corona pass

![Corona certificate with vaccination information, printed from the Omakanta service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_219/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83156060ee7cf4d5328/1626242527)

The corona pass, that is, the corona certificate, can also be printed from the Omakanta service. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Problems have been noticed with the corona pass.

There are problems with people who have had corona. They do not get a corona pass if more than 6 months have passed since the corona illness.

A person also does not get a second corona vaccination if they have had corona. However, 2 corona vaccinations are needed for the corona pass.

Because of these reasons, a person may be left without a corona pass after the corona illness.

The corona pass was introduced on Saturday. It shows that a person has protection against corona. For example, many restaurants require customers to have a corona pass.

You get the corona pass, that is, the corona certificate, from the Omakanta service on the internet.

## Night of the homeless

![A woman at the Night of the Homeless event in Sörnäinen, Helsinki, in 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4928,h_2772,x_0,y_154/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-868059616bb0b5ab6e2/1634447770)

Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The Night of the Homeless event was held on Sunday.

At the end of last year there were more than 4 500 people in Finland who have no home.

There are many reasons for homelessness. For example, unemployment and alcohol and drug problems can be reasons why a person is left without a home.

Earlier there were more homeless people. For example, in the 1980s there were about 20 000 people in Finland who had no home.

The number of homeless people has been reduced by the Housing First programme. In it, a home is first found for the homeless person, and other problems are solved only after that. It is easier for a homeless person to get free of alcohol or drugs, for example, if they get a home.

## Corona restrictions in Mecca

![In the picture there are men in white robes bowing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11877762)

Open image viewer

This is how corona restrictions have been followed in Mecca. Now the distances disappear, but everyone must have corona vaccinations. Photo: AOP

Saudi Arabia has removed corona restrictions. Saudi Arabia has stopped almost all corona restrictions at the Grand Mosque of Mecca.

Pilgrims, for example, can again pray close to each other. During the corona time, people had to be far enough from each other in the area of the Grand Mosque.

However, Saudi Arabia requires that visitors to the Grand Mosque have corona vaccinations. Visitors must also wear face masks.

## The weather in the autumn holiday week

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12148376)

Open image viewer

The most beautiful day of the week is perhaps Tuesday.

In Lapland it has snowed at the weekend. Snow fell generally about 20 centimetres.

Autumn holidays have started in many Finnish schools. The best time for outdoor activities is right at the start of the holiday, says Yle meteorologist Joonas Koskela.

The most beautiful day of the week is Tuesday, Koskela says. Then the sun shines in a large part of the country.

Later in the week rain will come to Finland. Snow and sleet fall in many places and the wind strengthens.
