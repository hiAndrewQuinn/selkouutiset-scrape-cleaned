# Monday 21.2.2022 (radio)

Finland's Olympic team has returned home. A mass audience watched the Olympic Games on Yle's channels. The winter holidays began in Southern Finland. Tomorrow is a popular wedding day. A snowstorm in Southern Finland, and the weather.

Finland's Olympic athletes have returned home.

In the afternoon, for example, the skiing medallists Kerttu Niskanen and Krista Pärmäkoski arrived in Finland from Beijing, and so did the men's ice hockey national team, which won Olympic gold yesterday.

Finland's ice hockey success is being celebrated in the evening at a people's festival at Helsinki's Olympic Stadium. The men won Olympic gold in ice hockey and the women won bronze.

About 20,000 people take part in the celebration.

## The Olympics were of interest

Yle brought the Beijing Olympics to a mass audience.

Competitions and news were reported in Finnish and Swedish on television, on radio, on Yle Areena and on Yle's websites.

Yle's TV broadcasts reached 3.9 million people. The men's cross-country relay a week ago Sunday gathered the largest average audience, nearly 1.6 million viewers.

## Winter holidays

![The burning Kuukkeli shopping centre in Saariselkä. A fire engine and firefighters are putting out the fire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3824,h_2142,x_0,y_358/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91805262133c238888b/1645428627)

In Saariselkä the winter holiday week began with a shopping centre fire. Photo: Anneli Lappalainen / Yle

The winter holiday has begun in schools in Southern Finland.

Many families have travelled to Lapland to ski and go downhill skiing.

The early winter had little snow in Lapland, but now in many places there is more snow than usual.

For example at Levi in Kittilä, in Saariselkä in Inari and in Käsivarsi there is now about one metre of snow.

In Saariselkä the holiday week began with a big fire. The Kuukkeli shopping centre in the centre of Saariselkä was completely destroyed in the fire. The building contained, for example, the only grocery shop of the tourist centre.

Tomorrow is a popular wedding day. It may even be the most popular wedding day of the year for civil weddings, says the Digital and Population Data Services Agency.

Many people want to get married tomorrow because of the date. Written in numbers, tomorrow's date is 22022022.

All of the Digital and Population Data Services Agency's wedding times for tomorrow are booked, from Helsinki to Kittilä. The agency will marry almost 400 couples tomorrow.

## Tuesday's weather

![Finland's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12328016)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

A snowstorm has arrived in Southern Finland. The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that by tomorrow up to 25 centimetres of snow may fall in places.

Driving conditions along the whole south coast are very poor.

Tomorrow, Tuesday, snow will still fall in the eastern part of the country. During the day, however, the weather is dry across the whole country.

In the south there is a light frost, and in the north it is about 10 degrees below zero.
