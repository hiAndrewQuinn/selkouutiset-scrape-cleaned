# Monday 3.9.2018 (radio)

Wolf. Deaths of babies. Removal companies. Sentencing of journalists. Finland's summer.

## Wolves are being monitored

![Wolf photographed at the Ähtäri Zoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10246272)

Open image viewer

Wolf at the Ähtäri Zoo. Photo: AOP

There are many wolves in Western Finland. Wolves are now going to be monitored and controlled in many different ways.

The state is giving 300,000 euros more for wolves. The money will be used to fit wolves with collars and to hire a person to monitor the wolf population.

Fitting wolves with collars gives information about the movements of packs. In addition, shooting troublesome wolves will be made easier.

## Pollution affects babies

![A baby's small toes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10210607)

Open image viewer

Sudden infant death means the unexplained death of a healthy baby during sleep. Photo: Anu-Maija Kärjä / Yle

According to a study, air pollution can affect sudden infant deaths.

On a polluted day, the risk of sudden infant death rises. Sudden infant death means the unexplained death of a healthy baby during sleep.

The universities of Oulu and Birmingham have studied 200 sudden infant deaths that happened in Britain. In cities, more sudden infant deaths have been recorded in proportion than in the countryside.

In the coming years, the study is also planned to be done with Finnish material.

## The move may lead to disputes

![Moving boxes and rubbish bags.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10360735)

Open image viewer

Moving boxes and rubbish bags. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

People often use removal companies when they change homes.

Moving does not always go well, and then a dispute with the firm can arise. The use of removal companies is causing more and more disputes.

Typical reasons for disputes are goods or the surfaces of flats that have been damaged. Another fairly common cause of dispute is a surprisingly large bill. The consumer adviser helps people in dispute situations.

## Two journalists to prison

![Myanmar sentenced two Reuters news agency journalists to seven years in prison.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10383619)

Open image viewer

Myanmar sentenced two Reuters news agency journalists to seven years in prison. Photo: Lynn Bo Bo / Epa

Myanmar has sentenced two journalists from the Reuters news agency to prison. The journalists were given a seven-year sentence for revealing state secrets.

Prison sentences for journalists have been widely handed down around the world. According to the journalists, they were framed as guilty.

The journalists had previously reported on the persecution of the Rohingya people in Myanmar.

## The summer was warm

![Sunbathers on the beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10382671)

Open image viewer

Sunbathers at the Hietaniemi beach in Helsinki. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Finland's summer was really warm. The average temperature across the whole country in the summer was as much as 0.3 degrees higher than in the previous very warm summer of 1937.

The highest temperature of the summer was 33.7 degrees. In Finland this summer, heat was measured on 63 days. Heat means a temperature above 25 degrees.

According to the Finnish Meteorological Institute, the summer, that is June to August, was about two degrees warmer than normal this year. Such a warm summer recurs in Finland on average every 20 to 30 years.
