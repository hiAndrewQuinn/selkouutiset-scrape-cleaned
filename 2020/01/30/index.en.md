# Thursday 30.1.2020 (radio)

The coronavirus is spreading around the world. The coronavirus does not spread with parcels. Finnair has cancelled flights to China. Jörn Donner has died.

## The coronavirus is spreading

![Washing hands with a bar of soap](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10996585)

Open image viewer

Washing hands is a good way to prevent the coronavirus. Photo: Danil Roudenko / AOP

The coronavirus is spreading around the world. The virus is already in many countries.

A patient with the coronavirus was also found in Finland on Wednesday. The patient is a Chinese tourist.

The tourist is in hospital in Rovaniemi, in Lapland. On Thursday the hospital has been looking for people whom the virus may have infected.

The coronavirus causes symptoms similar to flu. Symptoms include, for example, fever and cough.

The coronavirus can be dangerous, for example, for elderly people. The virus is also dangerous for people who have some illness.

The National Institute for Health and Welfare says, however, that Finns do not need to worry. Finland is prepared for cases of illness.

The institute's chief physician Taneli Puumalainen says that people can prevent the coronavirus in the same way as they prevent flu. For example, people must wash their hands often enough.

The coronavirus started in December in the city of Wuhan in China. Now there are already thousands of people in China who have caught the coronavirus. Almost 200 Chinese people have died from the coronavirus.

## Chinese parcels are safe

![A postal worker at a parcel machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10321367)

Open image viewer

The coronavirus does not travel with parcels. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

People can still order goods from China. This is what the National Institute for Health and Welfare says.

The coronavirus does not survive long on the surface of goods. The journey from China to Finland is long, and the virus will die during the journey.

Chinese food can also be accepted, the National Institute for Health and Welfare says. However, food must be heated thoroughly if it is made from an animal.

## Finnair cancels flights

![Finnair aeroplanes at Helsinki-Vantaa airport in Vantaa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11155498)

Open image viewer

Finnair is cancelling flights to China because of the coronavirus. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The coronavirus is also affecting travel. For example, many airlines are reducing flights to China.

The Finnish airline Finnair has cancelled all flights to Nanjing and to Beijing's Daxing airport in February and March.

Finnair's other flights to China continue. However, customers have not been able to book flights to China for February or March.

The Nordic airline SAS is also cancelling flights because of the coronavirus. SAS is cancelling all its China flights until 9 February.

## Jörn Donner has died

![Jörn Donner](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11184874)

Open image viewer

Jörn Donner was a cultural figure. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Jörn Donner has died. Donner was 86 years old. He had a lung disease.

Donner was a writer, film director and politician. He was a Finnish cultural figure. It can be said that all Finns know who Jörn Donner is.
