# Thursday 3.10.2019 (radio)

The Finns Party is the most popular party. A day of flags at half-mast was held in Finland for the events in Kuopio. Economic growth is slow. A new natural gas company is coming to Finland. The weather is cool.

## Yle's support poll

Support for the governing party, the SDP, has fallen. In Yle's poll, under 16 percent of respondents support the SDP. The most popular party is the Finns Party. About 21 percent of respondents support the Finns Party. The second most popular party is the National Coalition.

The National Coalition and the Finns Party are opposition parties, so they are not in the government. Among the governing parties, support for the Centre has risen a little.

## Flags at half-mast after the school attack

![Kuopio school murder, city hall flags at half-mast](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11002162)

Open image viewer

Flags at half-mast. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

A day of flags at half-mast was held in Finland today. The day was held for the victims of the school attack in Kuopio. On Thursday, flags were at half-mast on many buildings.

A young man stabbed people at the Savo Vocational College in Kuopio on Tuesday. 10 people were injured and one person died. The police caught the attacker.

Prime Minister **Antti Rinne** will visit Kuopio tomorrow because of the school attack. In Kuopio, Rinne will meet teachers and students of the Savo Vocational College, and people who helped the victims.

## The euro area economy is growing slowly

![Euro area economy](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5214254)

Open image viewer

Economic growth in the euro area has slowed down. Photo: YLE24

The Bank of Finland has made a forecast of how economic growth in the euro area will continue. The Bank of Finland estimated that economic growth will not speed up much in the coming years. Economic growth in the euro area has slowed down a little.

The trade war between the USA and China and Britain's exit from the EU could slow economic growth even more. Economic growth has weakened the most in industrial countries such as Germany.

## Natural gas gets competition

![Gasum gas refuelling station near the port of Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9278590)

Open image viewer

Natural gas is used, for example, as fuel for cars. Photo: Jussi Kallioinen / Yle

A new natural gas company is coming to Finland. The Estonian natural gas company Elenger will start in Finland at the beginning of next year. Then natural gas pipelines in Finland will open to competition, and new gas companies will be able to come to Finland.

The arrival of a new natural gas company is a big thing, because Finland has had only one natural gas company, Gasum. When a second natural gas company comes to Finland, competition arises between the companies, and prices may fall.

Natural gas is used as fuel, for example, in factories, thermal power plants, cars and ships.
