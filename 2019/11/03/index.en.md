# Sunday 3.11.2019 (radio)

In Finland, taxing the rich is cheap. Price scanners have come to shops. The USA is accepting fewer refugees. The weather is getting colder.

## Taxing the rich is cheap

![Banknotes fanned out in the picture](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2212,h_1244,x_0,y_114/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5932895d88b930b543d/1573115776)

An owner gets money from the company, which is taxed less than a salary. Photo: Pxhere CC0

Taxation in Finland is good for rich people. Yle asked a researcher how taxation affects the rich. The researcher says that in Finland there is a group of very rich people, whom Finland's taxation favours.

The very rich own companies. They get money from the companies, which is taxed less than an ordinary salary.

The researcher says that the very rich may not buy new machines for the company, because it does not give them much benefit in taxation. The money is used for investments, because they are taxed less.

## Price scanners have come to shops

![Hand scanners at the Prisma in Riihimäki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11045928)

Open image viewer

Price scanners in a shop. Photo: Timo Leponiemi / Yle

Shops are trying out new technology that calculates the price of purchases. In some shops there are scanners, with which the customer can read the price of a product when they take it from the shelf and put it in the basket.

The scanner shows how much the purchases cost in total. In some shops you can scan the purchases with a smartphone.

The purchases do not need to be lifted onto the conveyor belt at the checkout. The checkout gets the price of the purchases directly from the scanner or the smartphone. The new technology is coming to shops gradually.

## The USA accepts fewer refugees

![Migrants from Central America and from Cuba on the International Bridge of Santa Fe in Mexico in October 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10990416)

Migrants on the International Bridge of Santa Fe in Mexico. Photo: Favia Lineli Lucero / EPA

The USA is reducing the number of refugees. The USA is letting in fewer refugees than before. US President Donald Trump has decided that 18,000 refugees may come to the USA next year. The number is smaller than ever before.

During the term of the previous president, Barack Obama, 85,000 refugees per year could come to the USA under the resettlement programme. The resettlement programme means that refugees ask for permission to come to the USA while they are abroad.

## The weather is cold

![Weather forecast of the highest temperatures from Monday to Wednesday of next week.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11049350)

Open image viewer

Cold air is coming to Finland. The weather is colder than usual for early November in the early part of the week. On Monday it is fair, that is, it does not rain. The sun may also shine. The wind may be strong and feel cold.

In Southern Finland there is a little frost or a few degrees of warmth. In the north there is frost of 5-15 degrees.

After Wednesday the frost will weaken, and rain may come later in the week.
