# Friday 7.4.2023 (radio)

Centre. Good Friday. Migration of birds in spring. Ice storm in Canada. The weather in Finland.

## Centre

![People at a table, behind the table is a Centre poster.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690333)

Open image viewer

The Centre did badly in the parliamentary elections. Photo: Jaana Polamo / Yle

The Centre does not want to take part in the next government.

The Centre's district leaders say that the party is better off going into opposition. The district leaders lead the Centre's work in different parts of Finland.

The Centre has been part of Sanna Marin's government. However, the Centre lost in the parliamentary elections last Sunday. The party lost many MPs' seats.

Many Centre decision-makers think that responsibility for a new government belongs to the parties that won the election.

Negotiations on a new government begin right after Easter. The negotiations are led by the National Coalition, which won the election. The National Coalition is the largest party in the new Parliament.

Good Friday has been observed in Finland. On Good Friday Christians remember the death of Jesus. Good Friday is one of the holy days of the Easter period.

Easter Sunday is on Sunday. Then Christians around the world remember the resurrection of Jesus, that is, that Jesus came back to life after death.

Easter is the most important festival for Christians.

## Migration of birds in spring

![Geese on spring migration at Tyrnävä on 18.4.2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9569660)

Open image viewer

A flock of geese returning from the south to Finland. Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

The spring migration of birds from the south to Finland has gone slowly this spring. This is what the bird organisation BirdLife Finland says.

The spring migration of birds has slowed because of cold weather. The nights in particular have been cold.

The spring migration of birds has also been hindered by a headwind, which has blown from the north towards the south.

BirdLife Finland says that, however, many geese and skylarks, for example, have already come to Finland.

The spring migration of birds will probably pick up soon.

## Ice storm in Canada

![Trees have fallen on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690336)

Open image viewer

The storm has disrupted traffic in Canada, for example. Photo: Andrej Ivanov / AFP

Canada has harsh winter weather. An ice storm has raged across the country. At least 2 people have died because of the ice storm.

The storm also cut the power for a million Canadians. The storm has also disrupted traffic, because it has knocked trees onto car roads.

There are still many trees in the storm area that are covered in ice. That is why the Canadian authorities warn people about trees that may fall because of the heavy ice.

## The weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690338)

Open image viewer

Over Easter the weather in Finland is dry, no rain falls. It is also sunny over Easter.

Much of the country still has snow. In the north there is snow as deep as over a metre.

The temperature is between 5 and 10 degrees across almost the whole country. In the south the temperature may rise above 10 degrees. In Kaarina on Friday 12.8 degrees was measured.

The nights, however, are still cold.
