# Monday 17.10.2016 (radio)

The Iraqi army has started an attack on the city of Mosul. Russians are shopping in Finland again. The metal industry needs new workers. Many people are replacing meat with new vegetarian products. The official first snow fell in Northern Finland on Monday.

## The battle for Mosul began

![Armoured vehicles early in the morning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9234091)

Open image viewer

Iraqi Kurdish soldiers. Photo: Andrea Dicenzo / EPA

The Iraqi army has started an attack on the city of Mosul. Iraq wants to free the city from the rule of the terrorist organisation Isis.

The Iraqi army is attacking Mosul together with Kurdish soldiers. US military aircraft are supporting the Iraqi army and the Kurdish soldiers.

It is estimated that the battle against Isis in Mosul will last a long time. The situation of Mosul's residents in the middle of the war is difficult.

Isis terrorists took power in Mosul 2 years ago.

## Russian customers are returning

![A tax-free sticker on a shop door](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9231567)

Open image viewer

Russian customers have been awaited in Eastern Finland. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

Russians are shopping in Finland again. For example, in Imatra and Lappeenranta the amount of Russian shopping grew in September.

In recent years Russians have done only a little shopping in Finland, because the value of the rouble has fallen. That is why euro prices have been expensive for Russians.

Now the value of the rouble has even risen a little, and it is worth it for Russians to come shopping in Finland again.

## The metal industry is worth studying

![The gate of the Turku shipyard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9147155)

Open image viewer

There is now work at the Turku shipyard. Photo: Ari Welling / Yle

The metal industry needs new workers.

Work is available, for example, at the Turku shipyard, where ships are built. The shipyard has work for hundreds of new workers, because more big passenger ships have been ordered from it.

People hope that young people will start studying the metal industry more again. In recent years the field has not interested young people, because many metal companies have laid off workers.

Now the companies are ready to take part in training young people. The metal industry may interest young people more if part of the training is practical work in the companies.

## Vegetarian products replace meat

![Pulled oats salad](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8919855)

Open image viewer

You can replace meat with pulled oats. Photo: Juha Mäntykenttä / Yle

Vegetarian food is popular nowadays. Meat is replaced with products made, for example, from oats or beans. They contain a lot of protein.

For example, pulled oats and broad bean products have already come into home kitchens. Many ordinary eating places also offer new vegetarian products. For example, the ABC restaurants at service stations always have veggie burgers too.

Fine restaurants have not become excited about the new products. One reason for this is that they want to make everything themselves and from Finnish ingredients. For example, bean products often come from abroad.

## The official first snow fell

![A road camera image from Pello (road 21 northwards) on 17 October. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9234314)

Open image viewer

The official first snow. Photo: Kelikamerat.info

The first snow fell in Northern Finland on Monday.

A little snow fell already on Sunday, but only on Monday did 1 centimetre of snow fall. Then you can already talk about the official first snow.

Last year, too, the first snow fell in the north in October.
