# Friday 4.8.2017 (radio)

Finns do not need to worry about the Dutch poison eggs. Laura Huhtasaari will be the Finns Party's presidential candidate. A bear attacked a man in Lapland. A lot of caraway is grown in Finland. The World Athletics Championships began in London.

## Poison eggs

![Eggs being destroyed in Onstwedde, the Netherlands, on 3 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9757119)

Open image viewer

In the Netherlands, eggs have been destroyed because they contained poison. Photo: Patrick Huisman / EPA

The authorities say that Finns do not need to worry about the Dutch poison eggs. No eggs have been brought to Finland from the Netherlands.

People have been worried because dangerous insecticide has been found in Dutch eggs.

Millions of Dutch eggs have been taken out of shops in the Netherlands and in the Netherlands' neighbouring countries Belgium and Germany. However, there are no Dutch eggs in Finnish shops.

## Huhtasaari

![Laura Huhtasaari](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9758277)

Open image viewer

The Finns Party's presidential candidate Laura Huhtasaari. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Laura Huhtasaari will be the Finns Party's presidential candidate. The party announced this on Friday.

Huhtasaari is the deputy chair of the Finns Party. She opposes immigration and Islam.

The presidential election is at the beginning of next year. The current president Sauli Niinistö also wants to continue in the job of president. Many think it is fairly certain that Finns will choose Niinistö as president again.

## Bear

![A bear photographed in Viiksimo, Kuhmo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9758423)

Open image viewer

Bears do not usually attack people. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

A rare incident happened in Lapland on Thursday. A bear attacked a man.

The man was on a bog looking to see whether the cloudberries were ripe. Then a bear came from behind and struck the man on the arm.

Marks from the bear's claws were left on the man's arm. The man went to a doctor, who checked the man's arm.

It is really rare for a bear to attack a person.

## Caraway

![Caraway](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9749992)

Open image viewer

Caraway is a spice plant. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

A lot of caraway is grown in Finland. Caraway is a spice plant.

Farmer Ilari Sundberg says that the quality of Finnish caraway is good. The aroma of caraway is strong when the caraway has grown in the bright and cool Finnish summer night.

Because of its good aroma, a lot of Finnish caraway is exported, for example to Central Europe and Asia. The price of caraway has stayed fairly high.

About 25 per cent of all the world's caraway comes from Finland.

## World Championships

![The women's relay at the 2013 World Championships in Moscow](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8231517)

Open image viewer

There are athletes from over 200 countries in London. Photo: Bernd Thissen / EPA

The World Athletics Championships begin in London. Athletes compete in different events to see who is world champion. There are athletes from over 200 countries.

The championships open on Friday evening, and they continue all next week. The last day of competition is Sunday, 13 August.

Finland has 12 athletes at the World Championships. Finns hope that at least javelin thrower Tero Pitkämäki does well. The javelin final is in a week's time, on Saturday.
