# Saturday 25.2.2017 (radio)

The protection from the flu vaccination may have weakened. There are shortcomings in animal protection in Finland. Finland won its first medal at the World Championships in Lahti. A polar bear interests tourists in Lapland. Shrovetide is celebrated on Sunday.

## The flu vaccine's protection has dropped

![This is what the influenza A virus looks like. The picture is magnified 60,000 times.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9479529)

This is what the influenza A virus looks like. The round balls in the picture are the flu. The picture is magnified 60,000 times. Photo: Department of Virology, Haartman Institute, University of Helsinki / Lehtikuva

The protection from the flu vaccination may have weakened. This has been studied in Finland and Sweden.

At the start of the flu season, in December, the vaccine's protective effect was about 50 percent. Now it is maybe 32 percent. The reason is that the influenza A virus has changed. The good news is that the peak of the influenza A season is already over.

About 20 cases of influenza B have been reported per week.

## There are shortcomings in animal protection

![A dog being examined at the vet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6994845)

Open image viewer

A dog being examined at the vet. Photo: Yle

Researchers say that the authorities must deal more strictly with animal welfare crimes.

Animal welfare authorities supervise, for example, the transport of animals and the places where animals are kept. A researcher says that the authorities may do inspections many times, but the animals' conditions do not improve.

There is a known case where an inspector visited the place 14 times before telling the police about the animals' bad conditions.

## Finland got its first World Championship medal in Lahti

![Krista Pärmäkoski](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9480076)

Open image viewer

Skier Krista Pärmäkoski brought Finland its first medal when she skied to silver at the Lahti World Championships. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

Finland has won its first medal at the Nordic Ski World Championships in Lahti.

Krista Pärmäkoski won silver in the women's skiathlon. The race was 15 kilometres.  The first part was skied in classic style and the last part in free style. The race was won by Norway's Marit Bjørgen.

In the men's skiathlon the best Finn was Lari Lehtonen. He was 9th in the race.

The races were skied in front of full stands - 35,000 tickets were sold. The Lahti World Championships started on Wednesday.

## A polar bear charms tourists in Lapland

![](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5074994)

Open image viewer

The little polar bear does not have a name yet. The mother's name is Venus. Venus's first cub was born five years ago. Its name is Ranzo. Photo: Ranua Zoo

The little polar bear is important for tourism in Lapland. The polar bear was born at Ranua Zoo in November.

The zoo believes that this year will be a super year for the zoo. The zoo believes that the cub will attract a lot of tourists next year too.

The polar bear cub is already the second cub for its mother Venus. It does not have a name yet. The cub is still in the den with its mother but is already sniffing the outside air. The cub now weighs at least (7) seven kilos.

## Sunday is Shrove Sunday

![Shrove buns on a plate.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7801492)

Open image viewer

Inside a Shrove bun there is whipped cream and either almond paste or jam. Photo: Hanna Lumme / yle

Shrovetide is celebrated on Sunday.

The special days of Shrovetide are Shrove Sunday and Shrove Tuesday. In Finland Shrovetide includes, for example, sliding down a hill on a sled or a toboggan.

At Shrovetide people also eat Shrove buns. Many Finns argue about whether a Shrove bun should have almond paste or jam inside in addition to whipped cream.
