## Climate change is intensifying

![Chimneys](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5816374)

Open image viewer

Carbon dioxide emissions cause climate change. Photo: YLE

The international climate panel IPCC has published a report on the state of climate change. Climate change is intensifying.

The report says that people must reduce carbon dioxide emissions quickly. If emissions are not reduced, the temperature on Earth will continue to rise.

The climate panel's report also says that carbon dioxide must be collected out of the air so that climate change can be prevented.

## Students are tired of distance teaching

![A lecture hall at the University of Lapland where the introductory course in administrative science is taught.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6204875dee3ca9a956a/1575894324)

Open image viewer

Usually there are many students at a lecture. Distance studying lacks the support and help of friends. Photo: Annu Passoja

Many students have grown tired of distance studies at universities of applied sciences and universities. Distance teaching began because of corona in the spring of last year.

Many students say that they can no longer cope with distance studies. Distance studying is lonely. It lacks the support of friends.

Some students say that they may quit their studies if distance studying continues. Some new students also say that they may not start studying if distance teaching continues.

## Corona vaccinations for young people

![Corona vaccines in a cardboard container.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5515,h_3102,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7585735fed93e381752/1609405470)

Open image viewer

Corona vaccinations for 12–15-year-olds started on Monday. Photo: Esa Huuhko / Yle

Corona vaccinations for 12–15-year-old children have started in Finland. Many municipalities started the vaccinations straight away on Monday.

Vaccinations for 12–15-year-olds can be given by appointment or without an appointment at vaccination points. School health nurses can also give vaccinations at schools.

On Monday 449 new coronavirus infections were reported in Finland. That is fewer than on many days last week.

## A tram now runs in Tampere

![Tampere tram.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_375/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-839839610fbb6ec980a/1628421392)

Open image viewer

Tampere's new tram. Photo: Clarissa Jäärni / Yle

Tram traffic has started in Tampere. The first tram service on Monday went from the Hervanta district to the centre of Tampere.

The tram changes Tampere's public transport a lot. The routes and numbers of some buses have changed. Some stops have also changed.

Tampere has 2 tram lines at first. However, more lines will be built. The extension of the tram lines will be completed in Tampere in 2024.
