# Sunday 7.5.2017 (radio)

Emmanuel Macron has won the French presidential election. The French chose a president who supports open borders and European cooperation. Finland lost to France at the Ice Hockey World Championships. The weather is cold.

## Macron won the election

![Emmanuel Macron with his thumb up.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9599551)

Open image viewer

Emmanuel Macron will become the new president of France. Photo: Yoan Valat / EPA

The French have voted in an election. France had a presidential election on Sunday. The winner of the election is Emmanuel Macron. Macron will become the next president of France.

Macron's rival in the election was Marine Le Pen. Macron got about 65 percent of the votes, and Marine Le Pen got about 35 percent of the votes.

Le Pen and Macron are very different politicians. You could say that in this election the French voted on what kind of country France will become.

Le Pen and Macron disagree, for example, about immigration. Le Pen does not want immigrants in France. Macron wants to keep France's borders open. Because Macron won the election, France will take in immigrants in the future too.

## A victory for the EU too

![Macron at a campaign event.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9599422)

Open image viewer

Flags of France and the European Union were seen at Macron's election events. Photo: Ian Langsdon / EPA

The French election has interested all of Europe. The election was important for Europe, because Macron and Le Pen also disagree about the European Union.

Le Pen thinks France should leave the European Union. Macron thinks the European Union is a good and important thing for France too.

Many leaders of the European Union are happy that Macron is the next president of France. It means that France will stay in the European Union.

## Macron is an economics man

![Hollande and Macron](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9579709)

Open image viewer

Emmanuelle Macron was economy minister under President François Hollande. Photo: Etienne Laurent / EPA

Many people are interested in what kind of man the next president of France is.

Emmanuel Macron was not the candidate of any party in the presidential election. He founded his own political organisation for the election. Open borders and European cooperation are important in Macron's thinking.

Macron knows economic matters. He has been France's economy minister, and he has also worked at a London bank.

The French are not usually interested in the president's private life. But the French are interested in Macron's wife. She is Macron's former teacher, and Macron fell in love with her already at school.
