# Sunday 16.6.2019 (radio)

Pork exports to China are growing. Kultaranta discussions consider the future of Europe and Finland. According to many parents, the baby sleeps badly. Today wild flowers are admired in all the Nordic countries.

## Finland can benefit from China's meat shortage

![A meat counter with a seller standing behind it.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10141847)

Open image viewer

China is the world's biggest producer and consumer of pork. Photo: How Hwee Young / EPA

Finnish meat companies believe that pork exports to China will grow.

The Chinese pork market is very big. China is the largest consumer of pork in the world. China is also the largest producer of pork in the world. Almost half of the world's pork is produced in China.

However, African swine fever has also spread to China. African swine fever is an animal disease that spreads easily. In China about 200 million pigs die or have to be slaughtered, that is, killed, to stop the spread of swine fever. Because of this, there may be a shortage of pork in China.

The Finnish meat companies Atria and HKScan believe that pork exports to China will grow. The companies also say that pork will get an even better price.

## Kultaranta discussions consider Europe's future

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4173,h_2347,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-2931645768078a648ac/1528717463)

President Sauli Niinistö hosts the Kultaranta discussions. Photo: All Over Press / Petteri Paalasmaa

The Kultaranta discussions have started.

This year the discussions are about the future and security of Europe, the change in politics and democracy.

About 100 experts and influential people from different areas of society have been invited to the discussions. The main guest of the discussions is the German Federal President Frank-Walter Steinmeier.

The Kultaranta discussions are held in Naantali at President Sauli Niinistö's summer home. The discussions started today, Sunday, and will continue tomorrow.

## Many babies sleep badly

![A child sleeps.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9231032)

Open image viewer

A child sleeps. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

According to many Finnish parents, the baby in the family sleeps badly.

The parents say that the baby sleeps badly especially at 8 months of age. Parents' experience is that at 8 months as many as 40 per cent of babies suffer from sleep disorders.

At 3 months about 25 per cent of babies have sleep disorders. The number of sleep disorders is the same also for children of one and a half years.

The National Institute for Health and Welfare has asked parents about their experiences of children's sleep. The study shows that many parents also find babies' normal night waking to be a problem.

## Wild flowers to get to know

![Daisies, meadow knapweed and other wild flowers thrive on the slope of the University of Tampere.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10829615)

Open image viewer

Daisies and meadow knapweeds are in bloom. Photo: Jouni Tanninen / Yle

Today Wild Flower Day has been celebrated. This year the theme plant of the day is water lilies.

The aim of Wild Flower Day is for people to learn to know nature and plants better. That is why dozens of plant excursions have been organised across the country today.

Wild Flower Day is celebrated in all the Nordic countries. Wild Flower Day is always on a Sunday about a week before Midsummer.
