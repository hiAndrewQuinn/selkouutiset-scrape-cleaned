# Monday 2.3.2020 (radio)

The CEO of Nokia is changing. 130 pupils had to go into quarantine because of the coronavirus. A helpline was opened in Helsinki because of the coronavirus. The race for the Democratic presidential candidacy in the USA is narrowing. The situation at the Greece-Turkey border is still tense.

## Nokia's CEO

![Nokia's new CEO Pekka Lundmark and outgoing CEO Rajeev Suri.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11236410)

The CEO of the technology company Nokia is changing.

The new CEO will be Pekka Lundmark. He is coming to Nokia from the post of CEO of the energy company Fortum.

Nokia's current CEO Rajeev Suri has been the CEO of Nokia and Nokia Siemens Networks for 10 years. Rajeev Suri says that he now wants to do something new.

## Schoolchildren into home quarantine

![An empty classroom at the Viikki Normal School.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5457,h_3070,x_0,y_573/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6489335e5d0ee4dac6b/1584556815)

An empty classroom at the Viikki Normal School. Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

Today a large group of schoolchildren in Helsinki stayed at home because of the coronavirus.

About 130 pupils stayed at home. They were ordered into quarantine, that is, isolation, because a pupil had tested positive for the coronavirus at the weekend. Four classes from the Viikki Normal School are in quarantine, as well as the HJK junior football team.

The quarantine of the schoolchildren is a precaution. Chief physician of infectious diseases Asko Järvinen says that the risk of infection is very small.

The schoolchildren will study remotely for 2 weeks. Other family members may move around freely.

## Helpline about the coronavirus

![Hand washing with a bar of soap.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10996585)

Open image viewer

The best way to fight the coronavirus is to keep your hands clean. Photo: Danil Roudenko / AOP

In Helsinki a helpline was opened in the morning because of the coronavirus. You can call the phone if you suspect that you have caught the coronavirus.

Already during the morning almost 200 calls came to the helpline.

Coronavirus tests have already been done on dozens of people in Finland. So far 6 people have been found to have the infection.

## The Democrats' presidential race

![Pete Buttigieg](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11203951)

Open image viewer

Pete Buttigieg. Photo: Katherine Taylor / EPA

In the USA the Democratic Party's presidential race is narrowing.

Former mayor Pete Buttigieg says that he is dropping out of the race and will no longer try to become the Democrats' presidential candidate. Buttigieg is the first leading candidate to drop out of the race.

8 candidates are still aiming to become the Democratic Party's presidential candidate. At the top are Senator Bernie Sanders and former Vice President Joe Biden.

One of the important days of the election campaign is tomorrow, Tuesday. Primaries will be held then in 14 states.

## Refugees at the EU border

![Clash at the Greece-Turkey border on 29 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11234820)

Open image viewer

The Greece-Turkey border. Photo: Ozan Kose / AFP

The situation at the Greece-Turkey border is still tense.

Greece says that it has already turned back 10,000 refugees who have tried to cross the land border from Turkey into Greece. More than 1,000 asylum seekers have arrived on the Greek islands in just over a day.

Last week Turkey said that it would open its gates to Europe. There are many Syrian refugees in Turkey, and in Turkey's view it is now the EU's turn to help them.
