## A stir about Marin's restaurant evening

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin met the media outdoors at Puhemies square behind the Parliament building in Helsinki on 8 December](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_119/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88949761b0a876bec12/1638967479)

A stir has arisen in Finland around Prime Minister Sanna Marin.

Marin spent the evening in a nightclub last Saturday, even though she knew that she had been exposed to the coronavirus. Marin was exposed to corona because she was at the same meetings as Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. A corona infection was found in Haavisto on Saturday.

Marin did not follow the government's corona guidance. The guidance says that ministers should avoid contacts straight away when they have been told of a corona exposure. The guidance had been updated almost 2 weeks earlier.

Prime Minister Marin says that she had not received new guidance.

On Saturday evening a message was also sent to Marin's official phone saying that, because of the exposure, she must avoid contacts. However, Marin did not get the message, because she did not have her official phone with her.

The corona guidance for ministers is stricter than for ordinary citizens.

## Corona patients fill intensive care

![intensive care monitor](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10112532)

Open image viewer

The patient's vital functions are shown on the monitor. Photo: Yle

The number of corona patients in intensive care is growing.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that there are now 60 corona patients in intensive care. 50 intensive care patients is the limit, after which, for example, non-urgent operations must be cancelled.

There are currently about 300 corona patients in hospital. That is about thirty more than yesterday.

About 1300 new infections have been recorded today. There are 11 new deaths.

## A vaccine requirement is wanted for nurses

![A nurse helps an older person eating at a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11724018)

Open image viewer

The government proposes that the corona vaccine becomes compulsory for nurses who work with risk groups. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

Health care workers may perhaps be required to have the corona vaccine.

The government proposes that the corona vaccine becomes compulsory for social and health care workers who work with risk groups.

A person may refuse the vaccine only for health reasons. If the vaccine cannot be taken, a negative corona test result must be shown.

## Power changes hands in Germany

![Olaf Scholz and Frank-Walter Steinmeier walking with masks on their faces](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88948661b0a63acf901/1638966983)

In Germany, Angela Merkel's time in power is over. Merkel was chancellor of Germany for 16 years.

Olaf Scholz has been appointed as the new chancellor. He is from the German Social Democratic Party, the SPD.

Scholz leads a government that includes the Social Democrats, the Greens and the liberal party FDP.
