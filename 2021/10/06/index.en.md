## Corona patients in hospitals

![Two nurses and a patient on a corona ward in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11819309)

Open image viewer

Caring for corona patients is hard work. Photo: Tuomas Aro / Yle Kuvapalvelu

The number of corona patients is growing in Finland's hospitals. There are now 145 corona patients in hospital care.

If the number of corona patients grows more, the care of other patients will suffer. Soon some hospitals will not be able to do all operations, for example.

People who now catch corona are people who have not taken corona vaccinations. Finland will use up to a billion euros in the coming years, when it gives hospital care to corona patients who have not been vaccinated.

## Award for Inka Häkkinen

![In the picture Queen Silvia and Inka Häkkinen. Häkkinen received a diploma for the development of innovative nursing work.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2048,h_1152,x_0,y_128/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-863139615d59c9aae3e/1633507929)

Open image viewer

Queen Silvia of Sweden presented the award to Inka Häkkinen. Photo: Queen Silvia Nursing Award / Yanan Li

Finnish nurse Inka Häkkinen has received an important award.

The name of the award is Queen Silvia Nursing Award. It is an award in the field of nursing, which is named after Queen Silvia of Sweden.

Queen Silvia herself presented the award to Häkkinen at the Royal Palace in Stockholm.

Inka Häkkinen has developed the Finnhelp service. The service offers talk support to health care workers. The goal is to improve the coping of workers.

## Thai berry pickers

![A lingonberry bucket and two pickers in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2133,h_1200,x_0,y_93/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7241705f72ef6dd65b4/1601367986)

Open image viewer

The berry pickers earned well this year, because the lingonberry harvest was good. Photo: Petri Vironen / Yle

Thai berry pickers have earned well in Finland this summer.

The berry company Polarica says that the pickers earned on average 4000 euros. Some of the pickers earned as much as 12 000 euros during the summer, which is as much as a Thai rice field earns in 15 years.

The blueberry harvest in Finland was poor this year, but the lingonberry harvest was good.

## Bicycle thefts

![Elielinaukio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8724179)

Open image viewer

Bicycles interest thieves. Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

A lot of bicycles are stolen in Finland. Last year more than 23 000 bicycles were stolen in Finland.

Thieves often sell bicycles on the internet. If the price of a bicycle on the internet is really cheap, the bicycle may be stolen.

Often bicycle thieves need money for drugs.

Yle's Swedish-language programme Spotlight investigated bicycle thefts.

The programme's journalists left bicycles in different parts of Helsinki. The bicycles were locked, but everything was stolen.

You can watch the programme on Yle Areena. The programme has Finnish subtitles.

## Thursday's weather

The weather is rainy.

On Thursday it rains in a large part of the country. In Northern Lapland the rain is sleet in places, elsewhere it rains.

Right in the east it is still dry during the day, and the cloud cover may break in places.

It is warm in a large part of the country, between 9 and 13 degrees. In Lapland the weather gradually cools, and it is between 2 and 6 degrees.
