# Friday 17.2.2017 (radio)

A really big trade union may be founded in Finland. The bears at Korkeasaari Zoo are starting to wake up from their winter sleep. Upper secondary schools held the old dances. The winter holiday has started in schools in Southern Finland. Humour keeps a relationship in good shape.

## Industrial trade union

![Shaping a leaf spring with a grinding machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8649085)

Open image viewer

The unions of different industries plan to merge. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

A really big trade union may be founded in Finland. The new trade union will be born when the unions of the metal industry, the forest industry and other industries merge.

A trade union represents the workers of some field. It talks with employers about, for example, wages.

Industrial workers get more power in negotiations when they found a big trade union. About 230,000 industrial workers are part of the new trade union.

The final decision on founding the new trade union will be made in May, and it is planned to start operating next year.

## The bears of Korkeasaari

![Bear](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9466908)

Open image viewer

The bears woke up at Korkeasaari. Photo: Mari Lehmonen / Korkeasaari

At Korkeasaari Zoo in Helsinki, the bears are starting to wake up from their winter sleep.

On Friday, 2 bears came out of their winter den. After their winter sleep they were very hungry.

At first the bears got apples, nuts and raisins. At the weekend they will get meat.

This winter the Korkeasaari bears slept for 3 months. That is quite little. In Finland bears can sleep for as long as 6 months.

In nature, bears usually wake up in March or April.

## The old dances

![Young people at the old dances. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9466215)

Open image viewer

At the old dances, young people wear formal clothes. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

The old dances were held in upper secondary schools on Friday.

Second-year students of upper secondary school celebrate that they are now the oldest students in the school. The abiturients, or third-year students, no longer come to school, because they are studying at home for the matriculation exams.

At the old dances the students dress in fine party clothes. They perform dances that were in fashion about 100 years ago.

## Winter holiday in Southern Finland

![A skier at Tahko during the southern ski holiday week.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8705018)

Open image viewer

Many people travel north during the ski holiday. Photo: Helmi Nykänen / Yle

The winter holiday has started in schools in Southern Finland. The winter holiday lasts a week.

The winter holiday is also called the ski holiday. But it is now quite warm in Southern Finland, and the temperature is above zero.

Yle meteorologist Matti Huutonen says that the weather will change next week. The weather will get colder, and winter will come back to Southern Finland too.

## Humour in a relationship

![A man and a woman laughing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8401091)

Open image viewer

Humour keeps a relationship in good shape. Photo: Arne Dedert / EPA

Finally, an important question: What keeps a relationship in good shape? Humour, answers American professor Jeffrey Hall.

Professor Hall says that humour keeps people together. People are happy in a relationship where the partners laugh at the same things.

Only nasty humour is a bad thing in a relationship. If partners hurt each other with nasty humour, the relationship will probably not last long.
