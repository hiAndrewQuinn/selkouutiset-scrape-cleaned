# Wednesday 12.10.2022 (radio)

UN criticises Finland over children from the al-Hol camp. About 1000 quota refugees will come to Finland next year. In Britain, King Charles will be crowned in May. The weather is cloudy.

## Children's rights

The UN says that Finland has violated children's rights. The UN Committee on the Rights of the Child criticises Finland for not bringing children out of the al-Hol camp in Syria.

The children were supposed to be in the camp for many years. The camp was dangerous. This violated children's rights.

The UN committee's decision concerns six children. Three of the children have now come to Finland, and three children are still in the camp in Syria.

The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says that Finland has helped a total of 35 people out of the al-Hol camp to Finland. 26 of them are children. About 10 Finnish people are currently in the al-Hol camp in total.

Just over 1000 quota refugees will come to Finland next year. Most of the refugees coming to Finland will be Syrians. About 400 of them will come to Finland from Lebanon and Turkey.

Finland will take in refugees next year mostly from the same countries as in previous years.

## Arrests in Russia

The bridge over the Kertš Strait between the Crimean Peninsula and Russia.

Russia has arrested people who are suspected of taking part in the blowing up of the Kertš Strait bridge. Russia says that it arrested 8 people.

The Kertš Strait bridge between Crimea and Russia was blown up on Saturday. Russia says that the Ukrainian army's intelligence service organised the explosion. Russia took revenge for the bridge explosion with air strikes on Ukraine.

King Charles's coronation in Britain will be in May next year. Charles will be crowned king on 6 May in London at Westminster Abbey.

Charles became king right after his mother, Queen Elizabeth, died on 8 September.

Britain's royals have been crowned at Westminster Abbey for over 900 years. The previous occasion was in 1953, when Charles's mother Elizabeth received the crown.
