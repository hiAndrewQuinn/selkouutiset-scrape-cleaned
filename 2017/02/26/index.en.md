# Sunday 26.2.2017 (radio)

Terhi Kiemunki, chair of the Finns Party in Tampere, has been expelled from the party. Most Finns do not trust decision-makers. Finland won bronze in the men's team sprint in Lahti. US President Donald Trump is skipping the press dinner. The Oscars are being handed out.

## Terhi Kiemunki has been expelled from the Finns Party

![Terhi Kiemunki ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9480751)

On Friday the Finns Party's party board expelled Terhi Kiemunki, chair of the Finns Party in Tampere, from the party. STT was the first to report it. Photo: Kalle Parkkinen / Lehtikuva

Terhi Kiemunki, chair of the Finns Party in Tampere, has been expelled from the party.

Finns Party party secretary Riikka Slunga-Poutsalo tells Yle that the reason for the expulsion is unclear travel expense claims. Slunga-Poutsalo says that the travel claims are a single incident, but the expulsion was "the sum of many things".

Kiemunki is an assistant to Finns Party MP Lea Mäkipää. Kiemunki has been in the public eye, among other things, because of racist comments. She has been fined for incitement against an ethnic group.

## In Finland people trust decision-makers only a little

![A mini plenary session in the European Parliament on 1 February in Brussels.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9480026)

Open image viewer

18 percent of Finns trust the decision-makers of the European Union, or the EU. Photo: Stephanie Lecocq / EPA

Most adult residents of Finland do not trust decision-makers such as politicians. This was studied by the Foundation for Municipal Development.

60 percent of respondents do not trust municipal decision-makers very much or at all. Only 26 percent trust national and regional decision-makers. 18 percent trust the decision-makers of the European Union.

Trust in municipal decision-makers was at about the same level four (4) years ago.

## Finland won bronze at the Lahti World Championships

![Sami Jauhojärvi and Iivo Niskanen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9480884)

Open image viewer

Sami Jauhojärvi and Iivo Niskanen won bronze in the men's team sprint. The race had a dramatic end when Niskanen fell in the fight for gold in the finish area. Photo: Lehtikuva/Martti Kainulainen

Finland has won a second medal at the Nordic Ski World Championships in Lahti.  Sami Jauhojärvi and Iivo Niskanen won bronze in the men's team sprint.

The race had an exciting end when Niskanen fell in a collision with a Norwegian skier. The race was won by Russia.

In the women's team sprint Aino-Kaisa Saarinen and Kerttu Niskanen came fifth. Finland was also fifth in the men's Nordic combined team competition.

## Trump will not come to the press dinner

![Donald Trump](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9281170)

Open image viewer

Donald Trump Photo: Mikhael Reynolds / EPA

US President Donald Trump will not come to the press dinner that is held at the White House.

 Every spring there is a meeting of politicians, celebrities and journalists at the White House. The White House Correspondents' Association has organised the dinner since the 1920s.

Trump announced on Twitter that he will not come to the dinner. Trump did not say why he is staying away. Relations between President Trump and the media have been difficult. Trump has accused many media outlets of fake news.

## The Oscars are handed out again

![An Oscar statue in front of the Kodak Theatre in Hollywood.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5003318)

Open image viewer

You can follow the Oscar gala on the night between Sunday and Monday. Photo: Mike Nelson / EPA

The film industry's Oscars are handed out in the USA during the night, Finnish time.

The award gala from Hollywood is shown live on Yle Teema from half past three. Before that you can follow the mood of the stars on the red carpet.

Early favourites are, for example, the musical La La Land and the coming-of-age drama Moonlight.
