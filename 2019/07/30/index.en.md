# Tuesday 30.7.2019 (radio)

The Turku shipyard has started building a new cruise ship. American rapper Asap Rocky is waiting for a court verdict in Sweden. Logging in to internet banking is changing. Ethiopians planted 350 million trees in a single day.

## The Turku shipyard has plenty of work

![Meyer Turku Oy shipyard area.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10858686)

Meyer's Turku shipyard area. Photo: Meyer Turku Oy

Building of a new ship has started in Turku. The construction work began on Tuesday.

Meyer's Turku shipyard is building a large cruise ship. The ship will have over 2,600 cabins and it will fit 6,600 people.

The new ship will be named Costa Toscana. It will be ready in 2 years' time.

The customer for the ship is a company called Costa Cruises. It has ordered 2 ships from Turku.

The first ship, Costa Smeralda, will be completed this autumn.

## Rap star ends up in court

![Asap Rocky ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10898925)

Open image viewer

Rap star Asap Rocky Photo: AOP

Court proceedings have started in Sweden, Finland's neighbouring country, and they interest people in many countries.

The court case is about American rapper Asap Rocky. He is on a performance tour in Europe.

The Swedish police arrested Asap Rocky over a fight in Stockholm at the beginning of July. He has been waiting for his trial in prison.

The court case of the American star in Sweden has attracted a lot of attention. For example, US President Donald Trump called the Swedish Prime Minister, Stefan Löfven.

Trump told Löfven that Asap Rocky must be freed. Löfven told Trump that politicians may not order how the court operates.

The court will give Asap Rocky its verdict, perhaps on Friday.

## Internet banking security is improving

![A password for internet banking being typed on a keyboard from a list of codes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10898336)

Open image viewer

The list of codes will soon no longer be enough for internet banking. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

Logging in to internet banking is changing.

Many people go to internet banking using codes. However, a paper list of codes will soon no longer be enough.

The European Union wants the security of internet banks to grow. Therefore, strong identification is needed.

A customer can get instructions from the bank on how strong identification is done.

Some banks require that the customer has an identification app on their phone or a code device.

Many banks also continue to use paper code lists. Then the code is confirmed by text message.

The change will come into force in the autumn. It also affects, for example, the Kela online services that a customer enters with bank IDs.

## Ethiopians are planting trees

![Street view in Mekele, Ethiopia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10898867)

Open image viewer

Ethiopians want more trees in the country. Photo: Michael Tewelde / AFP

In Africa, in Ethiopia, many trees have been planted. Ethiopians planted 350 million trees in a single day on Monday.

Ethiopia intends to plant 4 billion trees in total in half a year. Ethiopia wants to prevent forests from disappearing and a desert, where nothing grows, from taking their place.
