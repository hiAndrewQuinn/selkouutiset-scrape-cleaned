People still die in Finland because of corona. For example, in April there were about 500 deaths in Finland that were linked to corona.

Chief medical officer Markku Mäkijärvi hopes that everyone takes corona vaccinations. Corona vaccinations are the best way to prevent corona deaths, Mäkijärvi says.

## Ukraine's grain transports

![General view of an agricultural machine in a field. Branches in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5714,h_3214,x_0,y_263/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-966362629f382b2f9bf/1654769600)

Open image viewer

Ukraine is an important grain producer. Photo: Dmytro Smoliyenko / Getty

Ukraine plans to transport grain abroad along new routes. Ukraine says that it is able to transport grain abroad through Poland and Romania.

Ukraine is trying to transport 30 million tonnes of grain abroad along new routes. Usually the route goes through the Black Sea, but because of Russia's military actions this is not possible now.

Without Ukraine's grain, many countries may face a food shortage.

## Air traffic

![Passengers queue at the airport for a security check.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5388,h_3030,x_0,y_67/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96835962a33010f210d/1654862062)

Open image viewer

Queue at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Air traffic is increasing in Europe. After the corona time, people want to travel abroad again.

However, there is a shortage of workers in air traffic. During the corona time, many air traffic workers changed fields, because there was little work in air traffic.

There have also been crowds at Helsinki-Vantaa airport now that the summer holidays have started. For example, on Friday passengers had to queue for up to an hour for a security check, because there are too few workers in the security check.

## Kultaranta discussions

![Sauli Niinistö walks with Jens Stoltenberg.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4498,h_2530,x_0,y_340/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96897562a5ce7e26395/1655462184)

Open image viewer

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg and President Sauli Niinistö at Kultaranta. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

The Kultaranta discussions have started. Kultaranta is the president's summer residence in Naantali.

The host of the Kultaranta discussions is President Sauli Niinistö. The discussions are about foreign policy.

This summer the theme of the Kultaranta discussions is the security of the Nordic region. The main guests are NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg and Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Störe.

In total there are about 100 discussants. Among them are politicians, researchers and representatives of business and media, for example.

The Kultaranta discussions are a tradition that President Sauli Niinistö started. Niinistö held the Kultaranta discussions for the first time in 2013.
