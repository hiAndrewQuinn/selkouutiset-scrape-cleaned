# Sunday 13.11.2016 (radio)

In France people remembered those who died in terror attacks a year ago. There has been an earthquake in New Zealand. The artist Niillas Holmberg is Sámi of the Year. The Finnish dog Rokka fights for elephants.

## France's memorial event

![Bataclan concert hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9289768)

The Bataclan concert hall has been repaired, and there was a memorial concert there on Saturday. Photo: Juha Nurminen / Yle

In France people have remembered those who died in terror attacks a year ago. Then the terrorists attacked different parts of the capital, Paris. They killed 130 people.

On Saturday evening there was a memorial concert at the Bataclan concert hall. The concert hall was one of the places the terrorists attacked. Pop singer Sting performed at the memorial concert.

On Sunday French President François Hollande unveiled memorial plaques. There are memorial plaques in the places the terrorists attacked. The plaques say what happened at the place in November 2015.

There has been an earthquake in New Zealand. The centre of the earthquake was about 100 kilometres from the city of Christchurch. There is no information about the damage.

The New Zealand authorities have issued a tsunami warning, because the earthquake may raise high waves at sea. The authorities have advised people on the coast to move further away from the sea.

## Sámi of the Year

![Niillas Holmberg](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9033471)

Open image viewer

Niillas Holmberg. Photo: Mira Rauhala / Yle

The artist Niillas Holmberg is Sámi of the Year 2016. Holmberg has worked for Sámi culture and the rights of the Sámi.

Holmberg makes music and writes books in the Sámi language. Holmberg's books have been translated into many languages, and they make Sámi culture familiar to others too.

The Sámi are the only indigenous people in the whole area of the European Union. They have lived in Lapland for longer than others.

The Sámi live in Lapland in the areas of Norway, Sweden, Finland and Russia. In Finland about 10,000 Sámi live. The Sámi language is related to the Finnish language.

## Ivory dog Rokka

![Toni Lahtinen and Rokka examining a container from a Chinese company.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9287123)

Open image viewer

Rokka is searching for illegal ivory. Beside it is its owner, crime investigator Toni Lahtinen. Photo: Yle Kuvapalvelu

The Finnish dog Rokka has become a film star in Africa.

Rokka is an ivory dog. It can smell ivory. That is why it has been able to help the Tanzanian police.

The Tanzanian police are looking for criminals who kill elephants and sell ivory. In this work Rokka's sharp nose has been a big help. Rokka smells ivory in places where the police have not found anything.

The dog Rokka is now famous. It has even appeared in the documentary film of film star Leonardo DiCaprio.

Finns can follow the work of Rokka and its owner Toni Lahtinen on television. The TV series "Ivory Dog" comes on TV1 on Tuesday evenings, and the episodes of the series can also be watched on Yle Areena.
