UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres. Photo: AOP

The UN, that is, the United Nations, Secretary-General Antonio Guterres is visiting Russia.

Guterres has talked with Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov. He will also meet President Vladimir Putin.

Guterres said that right now the most important thing is to help people get out of the Ukrainian city of Mariupol. Russia has bombed Mariupol a lot.

There are still thousands of people in Mariupol whom Russia is not letting leave the city. They do not have enough food, water or medicines.

The UN Secretary-General will also travel to Ukraine.

Russia attacked Ukraine 2 months ago.

## Queues for passport applications

![Fingerprint scanner in use at the service point of the Pasila police station building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8534613)

Open image viewer

Photo: Helsinki Police Department

Applying for a new passport can take a long time.

During the corona time, Finns often did not need a passport, because they did not travel abroad. Now travel is increasing again, and many find that their passport is no longer valid.

The Helsinki Police Department has up to 800 customers every day who apply for a new passport. Via the internet it is very difficult to find a free appointment time.

In Helsinki, efforts are being made to ease the situation. Customers can, for example, book appointments for the weekend too.

However, some customers do not wait for a free appointment via the internet. They go to the police station to queue. The waiting time can, however, be long.
