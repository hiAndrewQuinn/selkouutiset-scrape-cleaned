## Sweden's parliamentary elections

![The Swedish parliament building in Stockholm in evening lighting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5616,h_3159,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1005815631b4f2cb5604/1662734264)

The Swedish parliament building in Stockholm. Photo: imageBROKER / AOP

In Sweden it takes several days to get the result of the parliamentary elections. Parliamentary elections were held in Sweden on Sunday. The result is so close that the winner of the election is not yet known. Counting all the votes takes a few days.

Between the two (2) competing blocs there was a difference of only one (1) seat on Monday. If the result stands, the right will win over the red-green left by one (1) seat.

The results are preliminary. The counting of votes in the Swedish parliamentary elections is still continuing.

## Niinistö to Queen Elizabeth's funeral

![Queen Elizabeth's coffin has arrived at St Giles' Cathedral.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4614,h_2598,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1006798631f46c107bf4/1662994541)

On Monday Queen Elizabeth's coffin was taken to St Giles' Cathedral in Edinburgh. Photo: James Whatling / MEGA

President Sauli Niinistö will take part in Queen Elizabeth's state funeral next Monday in London. President Niinistö will attend the funeral together with his spouse Jenni Haukio. Queen Elizabeth died last Thursday.

Britain's new ruler, King Charles III, has begun visits to the different parts of the United Kingdom. The visits are an old tradition.

On Monday King Charles travelled to Scotland. In Edinburgh he took part in the procession in memory of Queen Elizabeth. On Tuesday King Charles will travel to Northern Ireland. On Friday King Charles will visit Wales.

## Municipalities save energy

![Signposts in front of the road leading to the former town hall of Haukivuori, which belongs to Mikkeli.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_461/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-963637629764d54ec2b/1654089094)

Open image viewer

Municipal buildings include, for example, schools, libraries, offices and many other spaces. Photo: Markku Tuhkanen / Yle

Municipalities are saving energy this winter. Municipalities, for example, switch off street lights and lower the temperatures of buildings. 70 percent of municipalities say that they may lower the temperature in municipal buildings. Municipal buildings include, for example, schools and libraries.

The Association of Finnish Municipalities has asked Finnish municipalities how the energy crisis affects municipal activities. Almost all municipalities have a plan to reduce energy consumption.

More energy is wanted to be saved than before, because the price of energy has risen and because energy may become scarce.

## Tyre checks

![Tyre check, in which the depth of the tread of a car's tyres is measured.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2267,x_0,y_653/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1006502631ef48b42f3f/1662973162)

Open image viewer

The depth of a tyre's tread is measured with a small gauge. Photo: Ulla Malminen / Yle

Autumn has begun and car tyres are being checked again in Finland. The Car Tyre Association, Liikenneturva and the Police are organising tyre checks in 5 cities this week.

The aim is to remind motorists that the condition of tyres must be monitored. Worn tyres must not be driven on. Many motorists do not notice that a tyre is worn too much.

About 350,000 cars in Finland have bad tyres. This has been noticed in the tyre checks that have been carried out for a long time.
