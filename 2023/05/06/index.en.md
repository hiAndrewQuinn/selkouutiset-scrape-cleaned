## Charles III

![Charles in a royal robe in church.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4500,h_2519,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-110961364562c15356cb/1683369127)

Charles III. Photo: WPA-Pool/ MEGA

In Britain Charles III has been crowned king. Charles received the Crown of St Edward at Westminster Abbey church in London. The crown was placed on Charles's head by the Archbishop of Canterbury, Justin Welby.

There were about 2000 guests at the coronation. Also present were President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio. President Niinistö says on Twitter that he congratulated King Charles and Queen Consort Camilla on behalf of Finland.

After the coronation, King Charles travelled in golden horse carriages to Buckingham Palace. There the king greeted people from the palace balcony.

There were many people on the streets of London to watch the king's celebration procession. The police arrested some people who protested against the coronation and the monarchy. The celebration continues in Britain tomorrow, Sunday.

## Greens' leadership contest

![Green Party election balloons.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_560/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10941606429db57b670c/1680464767)

The Green Party's chair changes in June. Illustrative image. Photo: Sakari Piippo / Yle

Only 2 candidates have signed up for the Green Party leadership contest so far. The MPs who want to become the Greens' chair are Saara Hyrkkö from Uusimaa and Sofia Virta from Varsinais-Suomi.

Candidates for the Green Party leadership contest must sign up by next Monday at the latest.

The Green Party chair is changing because Maria Ohisalo is not continuing in the post. The Greens will elect a new chair in Seinäjoki in June.

## Battle of the Towers

On Saturday, birds were spotted, that is, identified, in a competition. In the Battle of the Towers competition, bird towers competed on where people identify the most bird species.

The purpose of the friendly competition is that people become enthusiastic about birdwatching.

The weather is warming. The weather will also be dry in the coming days.

Later next week the temperature will rise to over 15 degrees in Southern and Central Finland.
