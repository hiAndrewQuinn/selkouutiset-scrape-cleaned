## Corona is now among young adults

![A restaurant terrace full of people in central Mikkeli.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2108,h_1186,x_891,y_811/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81804060c0697983876/1626350722)

People on a restaurant terrace. Photo: Juho Liukkonen / Yle

The number of coronavirus infections in Finland is growing. The National Institute for Health and Welfare says that coronavirus infections have been growing strongly for 3 weeks already.

Most of the new infections have been found in young adults under 30. THL says that the infections spread when young people meet each other in bars and at parties.

Most of the new infections are of the delta variant of the coronavirus.

Although the number of infections has grown, the number of corona patients in hospital is about the same as before.

Today 324 new coronavirus infections have been recorded. There are about 40 corona patients in hospital.

## The USA paid the first child benefits

![Woman and child.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83158060ee8845cdcb6/1626245346)

Open image viewer

Samira Gharaei received child benefit for the first time on Thursday for her 4-year-old daughter Nelli. Photo: Hans Peter Dhuy

In the USA child benefit was paid to families with children for the first time today. Child benefit was paid to as many as tens of millions of families with children.

Children under 6 get up to 300 dollars a month, and older children 250 dollars a month.

Paying child benefit is President Joe Biden's project, with which he wants to reduce child poverty.

## Air-source heat pumps are selling well

![A user adjusts the temperature of an air-source heat pump.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3705,h_2084,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83204660efdd6275f70/1626332576)

Open image viewer

An air-source heat pump works with a remote control. Photo: Minna Matintupa

The heat has increased sales of air-source heat pumps. An air-source heat pump is an efficient way to cool a home or cottage.

Last year more than 80,000 air-source heat pumps were installed in Finland. This year the sale of the devices may grow by as much as 20 percent, the Finnish Heat Pump Association estimates.

Sales are currently being slowed down by a shortage of installers of air-source heat pumps.

## Potatoes grow in warm weather

![Freshly harvested potatoes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10888831)

Open image viewer

Early potatoes. Photo: Silva Laakso / Yle

Early potatoes have grown bigger than usual this summer. In shops there are, for example, early potatoes of the Timo and Jussi varieties, which are already bigger than the old potatoes from last year.

The new potatoes have grown well, because the summer weather has been so warm. The warm summer nights have especially helped the potatoes grow.

## Sea water at a record high

![Young people going swimming.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5258649)

Open image viewer

Sea water is now at a record high temperature. Photo: YLE

On the Gulf of Finland the sea water temperature has risen to a record high.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that the daily average temperature was 25.2 degrees and the highest temperature was 26.6 degrees.

Also in the Bothnian Bay the surface water was 7 degrees warmer than usual at the beginning of July.
