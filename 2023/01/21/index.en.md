## Sweden and Turkey's relations

![Two men. One has a book in his hand that is burning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688135)

The far-right politician Rasmus Paludan burned the Quran in front of the Turkish embassy in Stockholm. Photo: Reuters

Relations between Sweden and Turkey are now bad. One reason for this is a demonstration that was held in the Swedish capital, Stockholm, on Saturday.

Turkey was angered because the far-right politician burned the Quran, the holy book of Muslims, at the demonstration. He burned the Quran in front of the Turkish embassy.

Turkey earlier demanded that Sweden prevent the demonstration. Turkey also cancelled the visit of the Swedish Defence Minister to Turkey.

The visit was meant to discuss Sweden's NATO application. Sweden has applied for membership of the NATO defence alliance, but Turkey has not yet approved Sweden for NATO.

The matter also interests Finland, because Finland and Sweden have applied for NATO membership together. Finland and Sweden can join NATO only if all NATO countries approve them. Turkey is one of the NATO countries.

## Fire in Rovaniemi

Police do not suspect that the Rovaniemi fire was a crime. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

In Rovaniemi in Northern Finland 1 person has died in a fire. The fire broke out on Friday in a flat in a small block of flats.

Rescuers found 2 men in the flat. One of the men died and the other was seriously injured.

The police say that the case was not a crime.

## Falling down in the street

![A person who has fallen on the pavement lies on the ground on their side.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-6393557f63364e0b11/1475753040)

The streets are now slippery. Many people fall and hit their head on the icy street.

Fortunately the injuries are not often serious. Chief physician Ivan Marinkovic says that in some cases one must still go to a doctor.

It is worth going to a doctor if, after hitting the head, one is unconscious for a while or memory is lost. Unconsciousness and memory loss may be signs that the brain has been badly jolted.

It is good to ask a doctor's opinion whenever a child hits their head.

## Avalanche in Tibet

People have died in an avalanche in Tibet in Asia. Almost 30 people died in the avalanche.

The avalanche happened earlier this week. Snow slid down the mountain at a place where there is a tunnel exit.

Because of the avalanche, people were trapped in cars and could no longer get out. The search for people has already been stopped.

## The weather

Frost weather continues in Finland. On Sunday the temperature is between 3 and 8 degrees of frost across the whole country.

Cloudiness increases on Sunday in Southern and Central Finland, but the weather is fair, that is, no snow falls.

Also on Monday it is fair and frosty. After Monday the frost weakens and rain approaches Finland.
