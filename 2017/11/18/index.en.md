# Saturday 18.11.2017 (radio)

Western Metro traffic has started. Police have found a drug containing dangerous carfentanil. The Parliament House is open this weekend. At the start of next week the weather gets a little colder.

## The Western Metro has started

![A metro arrives at the station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9937605)

Open image viewer

The new Western Metro interests passengers. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Traffic on the Western Metro has started. Passengers can now take the metro from Helsinki to Espoo.

Until now, the end station of the metro was in Ruoholahti in Helsinki. Now the metro continues from Ruoholahti to Matinkylä in Espoo.

There are 8 new metro stations in total. On the Helsinki side, the new stations are Lauttasaari and Koivusaari.

The Koivusaari metro station is the only metro station in the world that is under the sea. The Koivusaari metro station also has the longest escalators in Finland. They are about 76 metres long.

On the Espoo side there are 6 metro stations. The most passengers are expected at the Matinkylä and Tapiola stations. About 30 0000 passengers are expected to use each of the two stations every day.

The Western Metro has cost over one billion euros. Construction was expensive, for example, because the metro was meant to be safe. The metro has been built so that passengers can easily get out from under the ground if there is an accident in the metro tunnel.

Metro traffic started in Helsinki 35 years ago. At first, the metro ran only between Rautatientori and Itäkeskus.

## Police found a dangerous drug

![Extremely dangerous carfentanil stretched with fairy sugar.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9935990)

Open image viewer

The drug contains fairy sugar and carfentanil, which kills. Photo: Police

Police have found a drug containing carfentanil in Eastern Finland. Carfentanil is a really dangerous substance.

Carfentanil is up to 10 000 times stronger than morphine. Just about 0.03 milligrams of carfentanil is enough to kill a person.

Carfentanil can also get into a person through the skin. A person can die if they touch or breathe in carfentanil.

Police have now found a drug that contains carfentanil and ordinary powdered sugar. That is why the drug looks and smells like brown powdered sugar. However, the drug can kill a person.

## The Parliament House is open to the public

![The Parliament House renovation is finished.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9803993)

Open image viewer

The Parliament House has been renovated to a fine condition. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

The Parliament House is open this weekend.

People can visit the Parliament House on Sunday as well, from 11 to 16. The Parliament House is in central Helsinki.

The Parliament House was completed in 1931. It was reopened this autumn after a long renovation.

The Finnish parliament, the Eduskunta, works in the Parliament House.
