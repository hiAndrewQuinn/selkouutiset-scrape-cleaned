# Tuesday 29.3.2022 (radio)

Ukraine proposes that Ukraine could be a neutral state. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy demands tougher sanctions against Russia. Food prices are rising. Olli Rehn is the Finns' favourite to be the next president.

## Negotiations between Ukraine and Russia

![Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan hosted negotiations in Istanbul.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4570,h_2570,x_0,y_368/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9347856242b7f39d3d4/1648539780)

Ukraine and Russia negotiated today, Tuesday, in Istanbul, Turkey. The negotiations lasted 4 hours.

In the negotiations, Ukraine proposed that Ukraine could be a neutral state. This means that Ukraine would not join NATO and no military bases would be built in Ukraine.

Ukraine says that a full ceasefire must first come into effect in Ukraine. Ukraine also wants security guarantees. Ukraine's negotiators tell about Ukraine's proposal.

Russia promises to reduce military activity near Kyiv.

## President Zelenskyy's speech

![A firefighter walks in front of a destroyed government building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_527/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9349396242e6bd3547b/1648565583)

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy demands tougher sanctions against Russia. Zelenskyy spoke to the Danish parliament. He said that the West must stop buying Russian oil.

Zelenskyy said that Russia carried out a missile strike on the city of Mykolaiv in the morning. At least 7 people were killed in the strike.

Zelenskyy said that the siege of the city of Mariupol is a crime against humanity.

## Food prices

![Packets of porridge flakes in a shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_208/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-927799622f2e6219192/1647259926)

Packets of porridge flakes in a shop. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Food prices are rising quickly in Finland. Food prices may be rising faster than ever before during the time Finland has been in the European Union. This is what Pellervo Economic Research, PTT, says.

PTT forecasts that food prices will rise by 11 percent this year. Food prices are rising quickly because Russia started the war in Ukraine. PTT says, however, that food prices had already risen before Russia's attack on Ukraine.

## Stora Enso

![Stora Enso's logo at the gate of the Helsinki office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-922460621f2cc53598a/1646225229)

The forestry company Stora Enso plans to sell 4 paper mills.

The plan concerns the Anjala paper mill in Finland and mills in Sweden and Germany. In future, Stora Enso wants to focus on packaging, for example.

Stora Enso currently has 5 paper mills, where a total of about 2,200 people work.

Bank of Finland Governor Olli Rehn is the Finns' favourite to be the next president.

A Yle poll says that 20 percent of people support Olli Rehn as president. In second place in the poll is Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. The third most popular is Prime Minister Sanna Marin.

The next presidential election in Finland is in 2 years' time.
