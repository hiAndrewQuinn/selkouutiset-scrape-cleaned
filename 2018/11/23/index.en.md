# Friday 23.11.2018 (radio)

Female genital mutilation. Finland's population is over 5.5 million. Artist Reidar Särestöniemi. A Clear Speaker has been chosen. The weekend is sunny.

## There are many mutilated girls and women in Finland

![Graphic showing types of female genital cutting](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10521528)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics, FGM

Health authorities have studied how many girls and women in Finland have had female genital cutting.

There are about 10,000 of them in Finland. Most of them originally come from Somalia or Kurdish areas.

In addition, about 3,000 girls or women living in Finland may be at risk of being subjected to genital cutting. Genital cutting means mutilating the genitals.

In Finland, mutilating girls' genitals is a crime. Girls are sent abroad for the procedure.

Finland wants to prevent the mutilation of girls.

## Finland's population is over 5.5 million

![A small baby wrapped in a blanket.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4702,h_2645,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5144165bb608c9a150b/1538656483)

In Finland more people die than are born. Photo: Unsplash

Finland's population has been counted. Finns now number 5,521,533.

Finland's population is growing, because more people move to Finland than move away from here.

Fewer people are born in Finland than die here.

## A painting by Reidar Särestöniemi

![Reidar Särestöniemi: Prohibition and Longing, 1967.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10522663)

Open image viewer

Reidar Särestöniemi's painting Prohibition and Longing. Photo: Arto Liiti / Särestöniemi Museum

Reidar Särestöniemi is a famous Finnish artist. Särestöniemi's paintings are rarely for sale.

Yesterday there was an art auction in Helsinki. There the Särestöniemi painting Prohibition and Longing was sold.

The painting depicts the love relationship between Reidar Särestöniemi and the poet Yrjö Kaijärvi.

At the auction the buyer paid 80,000 euros for the painting.

## Matti Rönkä, known from TV, received an award

![Yle news presenter Matti Rönkä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10044862)

Open image viewer

Matti Rönkä reads the Yle news on television. Then Matti of course has a suit on. Photo: Outi Kuitunen / Yle

Yle TV news presenter Matti Rönkä has received the Clear Speaker award.

Every year the Hearing Association chooses one well-known Finn who speaks clearly. The Hearing Association thanks Matti Rönkä, because he speaks calmly and clearly.

In Finland there are many people whose hearing has deteriorated.
