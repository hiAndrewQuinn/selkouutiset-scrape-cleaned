## Swedes vote

Swedes have voted in the election on Sunday. By Monday morning at the latest, it will be known how many seats the various parties get in the Swedish parliament.

The parties may find it difficult to agree on Sweden's new government after the election. The situation is difficult, because the other parties do not want to cooperate with the Sweden Democrats.

The Sweden Democrats do not want immigrants in Sweden. Immigration has been an important theme in the Swedish election.

## Health technology is developing

![Simon Kos.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10393104)

Open image viewer

Director Simon Kos. Photo: Toni Määttä / Yle

In the coming years, a lot of new technology will come to the health sector. This is what Simon Kos says. Kos leads Microsoft's health technology. He visited Finland at the beginning of September.

According to Kos, doctors and nurses can save a lot of time with the help of new technology. A computer can, for example, examine X-ray and MRI images very quickly.

According to Kos, writing notes takes too much time for doctors. That is why an AI device is being developed that listens to the conversation between doctor and patient and makes notes from it. The doctor only has to approve the document.

Virtual glasses can also save time and money, Kos says. Virtual glasses are already in use in Australia. When a nurse goes to a patient's home, the nurse and the patient put on virtual glasses. Then they connect with a doctor, who may be far away. However, the nurse and the patient feel as if the doctor is in the same room.

People will still be needed in the health sector in the future, Kos says. The machine saves time, but it cannot comfort or take responsibility.

## Birds migrate

![Geese on migration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10362667)

Open image viewer

Migratory birds leave for the south for the winter. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Many migratory birds are still in Finland, because the weather has been warm.

The bird enthusiasts' organisation BirdLife Finland says that the birds' migration has been slower than usual for autumn.

Migratory birds fly from Finland to the south for the winter. The birds leave for the south when the weather gets colder.

## The weather is getting colder
