# Friday 18.12.2020 (radio)

The STM expects a vaccine in Finland next week. The strong growth of the coronavirus epidemic may have stopped. Finnish parents are exhausted. Ateneum and Kiasma are being renovated.

## Coronavirus vaccinations

![vaccine needle](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11704056)

Open image viewer

Coronavirus vaccine. Photo: Brian van der Brug / EPA

The first batch of coronavirus vaccine may come to Finland perhaps as early as next week, says the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health.

The first batch holds about 10,000 vaccine doses. That is enough to vaccinate 5,000 people.

Head of department Tuija Kumpulainen of the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health says that the next batch of vaccine is expected in Finland in the days between Christmas and New Year.

Finland aims to start vaccinations together with other EU countries straight after Christmas, that is, on 27 December. The first vaccines are planned to be given to healthcare staff.

The vaccine used is the Pfizer and Biontech vaccine, which has already been given to people in Britain and the United States, for example.

The strong growth of the coronavirus epidemic in Finland has perhaps stopped, says the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare. The number of infections has also fallen a little in the Helsinki area

In the last two weeks, a little over 5,800 infections have been found in Finland. That is about two hundred fewer infections than in the previous two weeks.

THL says that in the last 24 hours, 354 new coronavirus infections have been reported across the whole country. There have been 5 new coronavirus deaths.

## Parents' exhaustion

![A tired mother hugs her baby.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7290055f8414d5c34ca/1602491637)

Open image viewer

Finnish parents are at the top of the exhaustion comparison. Photo: Janne Langen / Yle

A new study says that Finnish parents are exhausted.

The researchers compared the wellbeing of parents in 42 different countries. In the comparison, Finnish parents were 7th most exhausted. The most exhausted parents were in Poland. The least exhausted parents were in Thailand.

The study showed that in individualistic countries such as Finland, parents are more exhausted than in communal cultures. The researchers say that, for example, in Finland people think that a person is responsible for themselves, and so they try to cope alone. In a communal culture, people act together and also carry responsibility for others.

## Art museums to be renovated

![The staircase of the Ateneum art museum](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4200,h_2363,x_0,y_217/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6271055e0b3a2668d8b/1577794140)

The Ateneum and Kiasma art museums in Helsinki will have to be renovated. Both museums will be closed during the renovation.

The roof of Kiasma will be replaced during next year and the year after. At Ateneum, the ventilation will be renovated during 2022.

Ateneum and Kiasma are museums of the National Gallery. Because of the renovations, the National Gallery is laying off about 230 employees.
