## Quarantine period

![Student Ida Kettuoja is in quarantine in Vaasa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7269885f7c7646c40a5/1601992332)

Open image viewer

In quarantine you stay at home. Photo: Anna Wikman / Yle

The quarantine period may become shorter for people who have been exposed to corona, that is, who may have caught corona infection.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare recommends that quarantine lasts 10 days, no longer 14 days as before. However, a doctor always decides on quarantine.

A person must stay at home when they are in quarantine. If the corona test result is negative after 6 days of quarantine, one can get out of quarantine. A negative corona test result means that the person has not become ill with corona.

The shorter quarantine period applies only to people who may have caught corona infection. The isolation period for corona patients does not change.

## Night work

![A person sleeps in bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_178/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-79352760740e2bea4d9/1627793777)

Open image viewer

Night work causes sleep problems. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Night work is harmful to health.

Night work causes sleep problems, for example. About 60 percent of people who work at night have sleep problems. This is what the Finnish Institute of Occupational Health says.

Night work may also be a risk of serious illness. A person may more easily get heart disease, breast cancer or dementia, if they do night work for many years.

Often an employee does night work for only a few years. Most night work is done by 35 to 44-year-olds. Night work is done, for example, in the social and health care sector, in industry and in the transport sector.

## Cottage loans

![A summer cottage by the water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5243,h_2949,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7768966033378376cac/1613969450)

The corona era has increased the popularity of cottages. Photo: Alamy / All Over Press

Many Finns have taken out a loan this year to buy a cottage.

By the end of June, cottage loans were taken out for 600 million euros. That is about 30 percent more than a year ago.

During the corona era summer cottages have been very popular.

June and July were warmer than usual in Finland. Temperatures were up to 5 degrees higher than usual. The sun also shone a lot.

The first week of August starts with more usual weather. The temperature is about 20 degrees in the south, elsewhere it is cooler. On Tuesday it rains in the east and the north.
