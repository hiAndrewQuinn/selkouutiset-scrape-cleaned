# Monday 8.3.2021 (radio)

A lockdown began across almost the whole country. Restaurants will also close their doors tonight. The number of passengers on public transport will be limited in Päijät-Häme and Pirkanmaa. Today's coronavirus figures. Several complaints have been made about postponing the municipal elections. Yle has made a documentary series about Finland's coronavirus year.

## Lockdown

![School](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10925244)

Open image viewer

Upper secondary school pupils and second-level students have switched to remote teaching. Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

A lockdown that applies to almost the whole country has begun in Finland today. The lockdown lasts 3 weeks, that is, until 28 March.

During the lockdown, the upper grades of comprehensive school, upper secondary schools and vocational schools will be on remote teaching. Group hobby activities for people over 12 years old can be stopped. People may gather in groups of at most 6 at a time.

The lockdown is meant to help prevent the spread of the coronavirus.

## Restaurants closed

![Closed restaurant area in Tapiola, Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2670,h_1502,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6651995e987267be7b4/1587050695)

Open image viewer

Closed restaurant area in Tapiola, Espoo. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Restaurants will close their doors tonight. Parliament passed a bill earlier today that closes restaurants for 3 weeks.

Restaurants will be closed in 15 regions, that is, everywhere else except North Savo, North Karelia, Kainuu and Central Ostrobothnia.

However, restaurants may still sell takeaway food, that is, people can buy food portions and take them home.

## Restrictions on public transport

![Two passengers board a bus in Tampere.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6470046)

Open image viewer

People are boarding a bus in Tampere. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

The Finnish Transport and Communications Agency, Traficom, is planning restrictions on public transport in Päijät-Häme and Pirkanmaa.

The number of passengers on public transport can be limited, because the hospital districts have asked for it themselves.

The restrictions apply to buses and trains. The number of passengers on them may even be halved.

According to Traficom, the restrictions are meant to come into force this week.

## Postponing the municipal elections

![Municipal elections 2021 logo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11809667)

Open image viewer

Several complaints have been made to the Chancellor of Justice about postponing the municipal elections. At least 7 complaints have been made, says the Office of the Chancellor of Justice.

Over the weekend the parties agreed that the municipal elections will be postponed from April to June. The new election day is 13 June. The Finns Party was the only party that opposed moving the election day.

The municipal elections were postponed because of the poor coronavirus situation. Experts say that in the summer the situation may be better.

## Documentary about the coronavirus year

![Two nurses and a patient on a coronavirus ward in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11819309)

Open image viewer

Nurses and a coronavirus patient in Yle's documentary series A Tough Year. Photo: Tuomas Aro / Yle Kuvapalvelu

Yle has made a new documentary series about the lives of Finns during the coronavirus year.

The documentary series A Tough Year follows closely decision-makers, patients and relatives. It shows what work has been like during the coronavirus time, for example in health care and in the newsroom.

The documentary series has 3 parts. It can be watched on Yle Areena.
