## Finland's economy is growing

![Containers in a row at the HaminaKotka port in Mussalo, seen from the sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8854464)

Finland's economy is doing well. The European Commission predicts that Finland's economy will grow fast.

The Commission estimates that Finland's economy will grow by 3.3 percent this year. In the whole euro area, the economy will grow by only 2.2 percent. The euro area is made up of the countries whose money is the euro.

Because Finland's economy is growing fast, Finland's debt is no longer growing as fast as before.

Finland's economy did badly for many years. That is why Finns are happy that the situation is now better.

## The USA and China made deals

![Xi Jinping and Donald Trump at a press conference in Beijing on Thursday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9923271)

Open image viewer

Xi Jinping and Donald Trump met in Beijing on Thursday. Photo: Roman Pilipey / EPA

Chinese President Xi Jin-ping and US President Donald Trump have met in China's capital Beijing. At the meeting, the Americans and the Chinese agreed on big deals.

China plans to buy, for example, aeroplanes from the USA. China is buying 300 aeroplanes from the company Boeing. The price is 37 billion dollars.

The countries also made other deals. The deals are worth 250 billion dollars in total.

## The maternity package is 80 years old

![KELA maternity package box 2018](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9924050)

Open image viewer

The 2018 maternity package. Photo: Kela

The Finnish maternity package turns 80 this year.

In Finland, all mothers get a maternity package. The maternity package contains baby clothes and supplies that a mother can use to look after her baby.

The clothes and supplies come in a beautiful cardboard box. A small baby can also sleep in the cardboard box.

The maternity package is different every year. On Thursday it was announced what next year's maternity package will look like.

Next year's maternity package was designed by Robert Lönnqvist from Helsinki. The patterns on the package are animals, for example bears and elephants.

Lönnqvist also wanted animals in the patterns that are not found in Finnish nature. He hopes that Finland is an open country where not everyone has to be the same.

An exhibition called Baby in the Box opened at the Helsinki University Museum on Thursday. The public can see old maternity packages at the museum.
