# Monday 24.6.2019 (radio)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says that a war between the USA and Iran is possible. A new large power station is being planned on the Kemijoki river. Biodiesel is causing problems for agricultural machines and motorboats. The Pride event for sexual minorities has started in Helsinki. Domestic strawberries are starting to ripen.

## Iran and the USA

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto thinks that a war between Iran and the USA is possible. Haavisto says that events that grow into a war can start from any single matter.

Foreign Minister Haavisto hopes that Iran will still keep to the nuclear agreement. Many countries have signed the agreement, even though the USA has left it. In an interview with Yle, Haavisto said that Finland has close relations with Iran and that Finland has asked Iran to stay calm.

The Green party's Pekka Haavisto has been Foreign Minister for about two weeks.

The Kemijoki company is planning a new large hydropower plant on the Kemijoki river. The power plant will be built at Sierilä, about 20 kilometres from the city of Rovaniemi.

The first hydropower plants were built on the Kemijoki river in the 1940s. Since then salmon have not been able to get into the Kemijoki river to spawn. No proper fish ladders have been built on the river, along which salmon could get past the power plants.

Minister Sirpa Paatero says that it is possible to build more hydropower and at the same time bring salmon back to the Kemijoki river.

## Biodiesel clogs engines

![A man repairs a machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_183/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5709995d0539dcce1bd/1560623783)

Biodiesel is causing problems for the engines of boats and agricultural machines.

Bacteria in biodiesel grow algae in the tank, and the algae clog the engine filters. It is even dangerous if a boat's engine stops in the middle of the waves. Repairing the engines and cleaning the tanks is expensive.

Biodiesel is made from renewable raw materials, for example palm oil or restaurant frying fat. Oil companies mix it into ordinary diesel oil.

## The Pride event has started

![Pride Helsinki 2018](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5331,h_2999,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4961175b37abc80ea5c/1530375136)

The cultural event Helsinki Pride for gay, lesbian and other sexual minorities starts today, on Monday.

The event lasts one week. On Saturday a large Pride parade leaves from Senate Square. Last year 100,000 people took part in it.

The Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland is one of the official supporters of Pride this year.

## Strawberries are ripening

![Strawberries at the market square in Kuopio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10826333)

Open image viewer

Domestic strawberries are a sweet summer treat. Photo: Petra Husu / Yle

Domestic strawberries are starting to ripen.

Farmers say that the good weather, which has lasted a long time, has ripened the strawberries almost everywhere in the country. Customers can already pick strawberries themselves in the fields of Southern Finland.

Farmers say that the strawberries are now really sweet, because the days have been warm and the nights cool.
