# Thursday 4.5.2017 (radio)

Lavrov visited Finland. Nokia is cutting. French election debate. Prince Philip ends his royal duties. Farmers in the fields.

## Lavrov visited Finland

![Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov (right) and Finnish Foreign Minister Timo Soini (left) met in Moscow in June 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9593604)

Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov has visited Finland.

Lavrov met President Sauli Niinistö and Foreign Minister Timo Soini. During Foreign Minister Lavrov's visit they talked, for example, about the wars in Ukraine and Syria. The foreign ministers also talked about improving air safety over the Baltic Sea.

## Nokia is cutting workers

![Nokia's head office in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9594652)

Open image viewer

Nokia wants to save money and cut workers. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Nokia wants to cut 200 workers in Finland. Jobs are to be cut in Espoo, Oulu and Tampere.

Jobs are to be cut because Nokia wants to save money. Nokia has over 6,000 employees in Finland.

## The government met in Porvoo

![Emanuel Thelning's painting of the opening of the Diet of Porvoo in 1809.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9592946)

Open image viewer

In 1809 the Diet was held in Porvoo. It was an important meeting for the birth of Finland. Russian Emperor Alexander I was also present. Photo: Wikimedia Commons / Emanuel Thelning

The Finnish government has held a meeting in Porvoo today. The government meeting was held in Porvoo because Finland turns 100 years old this year. Porvoo was chosen because it is an important place for the birth of independent Finland.

The first meeting of the Diet was held in Porvoo in 1809. It was an important event for the birth of Finland.

The government meeting in Porvoo was also held in front of the public. Normally a government meeting is not public, and it is held at the Government Palace in Helsinki.

## Presidential candidate Macron won the TV debate in France

![On the left behind the table sit the female candidate, in the middle the male moderator, the female moderator, and on the right the male candidate.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9594036)

Open image viewer

Marine Le Pen and Emmanuel Macron debated on television. Photo: EPA/ERIC FEFERBERG

The French will choose a new president on Sunday. The presidential candidates Marine Le Pen and Emmanuel Macron debated on television yesterday for the last time before election day.

The candidates debated a lot about the economy, foreign policy and the fight against terrorism. Le Pen said that Macron is the candidate of the political elite.

Macron said that Le Pen spreads hatred and lies.

According to opinion polls, Macron won the debate.

## Prince Philip ends his duties

![Prince Philip of England.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9594746)

Open image viewer

Britain's Prince Philip turns 96 and ends his official duties. Photo: Will Oliver / EPA

Britain's Prince Philip is ending his official duties and retiring. Prince Philip turns 96 in June. He has worked as a prince alongside Queen Elizabeth for almost 70 years.

The British court said during the day that Prince Philip will end his official duties in August. Queen Elizabeth II will continue her work as before.

## Spring sowing has started

![Tractor in a field](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9595103)

Open image viewer

Tractors will soon be seen in the fields doing sowing work. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

Farmers have started spring sowing. Experts say that the sowing work is not yet late, even though the spring has been cold.

Farmers estimate that, for example, the first new potatoes may be eaten at the end of May. New potatoes are a great Finnish delicacy.
