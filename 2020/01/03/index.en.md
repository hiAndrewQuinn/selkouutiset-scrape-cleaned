# Friday 3.1.2020 (radio)

The United States killed an Iranian general. Finns think the president's term is now suitable. Many museums set a visitor record last year. Many waterbirds are spending the winter in Finland now.

## The death of the Iranian general

![Crowd carrying a painting that shows Qassim Suleimani.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_63/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6277545e0f243650d20/1578391902)

The United States has killed an important Iranian general, Qassem Suleimani, with a missile strike. The attack was made last night in the neighbouring country Iraq.

Suleimani was the commander of the special forces of Iran's Revolutionary Guard.

Iran says that it will take revenge for Suleimani's death.

In Iran's capital Tehran, tens of thousands of people have gone out onto the streets to protest against the United States.

## The presidential terms

![Survey on the length of the president's term in office](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11139439)

Open image viewer

Photo: Lasse Isokangas / Yle

Finns think it is good that the president's term lasts 6 years. Finns also think it is good that the same person can be president for only 2 terms in a row.

In a survey by Taloustutkimus, 85 per cent of Finns say that the current practice is good. Only 10 per cent of respondents want to shorten the length of the president's term.

President Sauli Niinistö suggested some time ago that the president's term should be shortened. He said that a better model is a term of 4 years, at most 3 times in a row, or a term of 5 years, at most 2 times in a row.

## The record year for museums

![Queue for the Ateneum Art Museum](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11139941)

Open image viewer

People are queuing for the Ateneum Art Museum in Helsinki. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Many museums set a new visitor record last year.

For example, the museums of the National Gallery, the Ateneum, Kiasma and the Sinebrychoff Art Museum had a record number of visitors last year, over 800,000. The 10 museum sites of the Finnish National Museum also set a new record, when they had over 760,000 visitors.

One reason for the museums' success is the museum card. 160,000 people bought the card last year. The card is an entry ticket to 300 museums around the country.

The director of the Ateneum museum, Marja Sakari, says that the popularity of museums is also due to people being tired of consuming things and now longing for spiritual values.

## Wintering waterbirds

![Swans on a frozen river in winter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10016739)

Open image viewer

Whooper swans. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

Many waterbirds have stayed in Finland for the winter, because the weather in early winter has been so mild.

The bird organisation Birdlife Suomi says that bird enthusiasts have started counting, to find out how many waterbirds there are.

The last count was made 4 years ago. At that time, about 175,000 waterbirds spent the winter in Finland. Birdlife says that this year there are probably more birds.
