# Monday 24.10.2016 (radio)

Peace marches for Syria have been held in different parts of Finland. France is clearing the Calais refugee camp. It is difficult for immigrants to understand a Finnish employment contract. Internet adverts annoy young people. Finnish singer Saara Aalto is doing well in Britain's X Factor competition.

## Finns' peace marches

![people with candles on the church steps](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9249511)

Participants in the peace march in Helsinki. Photo: Yle

Peace marches have been held in different parts of Finland. People have demanded peace in Syria.

The biggest peace march was in Helsinki. On Monday evening there is also a concert for Syria in Helsinki. With the help of the concert, money is collected for organisations that help Syrians.

The war in Syria has already gone on for almost 6 years. The situation is really bad, for example, in the city of Aleppo. Aleppo's residents have been left in the middle of war and bombing.

## The Calais refugee camp

![People with their suitcases queuing by the road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9248254)

Open image viewer

The residents of the Calais refugee camp are being moved out of the camp. Photo: Etienne Laurent / EPA

France is clearing the Calais refugee camp.

About 7,000 people have lived in the camp. They have wanted to get from Calais to Britain.

The residents of the camp are being moved to reception centres in different parts of France. In the reception centres they can make an asylum application.

1,200 police officers are supervising the clearing of the Calais refugee camp. The clearing will take many days.

## Immigrants' employment contracts

![SAK's premises.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9144638)

Open image viewer

SAK is Finland's biggest trade union organisation. Photo: Petteri Paalasmaa / AOP

Here in Finland it is difficult for many immigrants to understand a Finnish employment contract.

Finland's biggest trade union organisation SAK hopes that companies will make an English translation of the employment contract for immigrants. Even though the final employment contract is in Finnish, an English translation helps to understand it, says lawyer Anna-Liisa Häkkinen. She works in SAK's employment advice service for immigrants.

Häkkinen says that immigrants ask a lot about matters related to employment contracts. SAK started its advice service for immigrants in March.

## Internet adverts

![A young man uses a tablet computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7139313)

Open image viewer

Young people do not want adverts on the internet. Photo: Mikko Kuusisalo / Yle

Internet adverts annoy young people who spend a lot of time online.

Many young people use a program that blocks adverts. Young people think that the internet is nicer to look at without adverts.

The situation is difficult for media companies. They get money from adverts. An alternative to adverts is, for example, that the customer has to pay for the content.

Around the world, media companies have even closed their pages to people who have a program on their computer that blocks adverts.

## Saara Aalto's success

![Saara Aalto performed in the X Factor live finals on 22 October. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9247300)

Finnish singer Saara Aalto is doing well in Britain. Aalto is taking part in Britain's X Factor competition.

On Sunday Aalto again went through to the next round of the competition. Only 10 contestants were left.

The TV broadcasts continue so that in every broadcast 1 contestant drops out. Only 3 contestants make it to the final. The final is a little before Christmas.
