# Wednesday 20.6.2018 (radio)

The cut to holiday pay will show in the pay of state and municipal employees this summer too. The USA is leaving the UN Human Rights Council. The blueberry crop will be average across the country. Midsummer traffic will clog roads on Thursday. The Midsummer celebration is held in changeable weather.

## The holiday pay cut shows again in municipal pay

![graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10259960)

The cut to holiday pay will show in the pay of state and municipal employees this summer too.

Holiday pay is an extra bonus that is used in most jobs. The government's competitiveness agreement takes 30 percent of public sector holiday pay away.

State and municipal employees will get a one-off payment at the start of next year. It is 9.2 percent of the January pay. For example, a cook's pay is about 2000 euros. Even if the one-off payment of 9.2 percent is added, the cut is 250 euros a year.

## The USA is leaving the Human Rights Council

![USA's UN ambassador Nikki Haley](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10163845)

Open image viewer

USA's UN ambassador Nikki Hailey. Photo: Justin Lane / EPA

The USA is leaving the UN Human Rights Council. The USA's UN ambassador Nikki Haley said this at a press conference in Washington.

The decision is based on the council's attitude towards Israel. The USA says that the attitude is unfair.

The international community has criticised the USA's decision. Foreign Minister Timo Soini is sorry about the decision. Soini says that the USA was an important partner for Europe in the council.

## The blueberry crop will be average

![Blueberries in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9754659)

Open image viewer

Drought can make blueberries small. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The blueberry crop will be average across the country. The Natural Resources Institute says that a long drought can reduce the size of the berries. The crop may get worse, especially in Southern Finland.

On the other hand, the lingonberry crop will be plentiful in Southern Finland. Elsewhere in the country the lingonberry crop will be average.

## Midsummer traffic shows on the roads

![A line of cars on Highway 5.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9687991)

Open image viewer

On the straight stretch of Highway 5 at Lusi, there is always a jam at Midsummer. Photo: Heidi Sullström / Yle

Midsummer traffic will clog roads on Thursday. The exit roads from the capital region and the cities are congested.

There is congestion especially on Highway 4 north of Jyväskylä, on Highway 9 at Alajärvi and on Highway 5 north of Lahti and Mikkeli. The Traffic Centre estimates that the busiest time is between 4 pm and 6 pm.

Most roadworks will be paused over Midsummer. Work sites may still slow down the journey. Midsummer return traffic is busy on Sunday between 10 am and 10 pm.

## The Midsummer weather is changeable

![Rainfall over three days.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10265014)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

The midsummer celebration is held in changeable weather. On Thursday there will be showers in Southern Finland. In Central Finland it is sunny and dry.

On Midsummer Eve, Friday, it may be rainiest in the Ostrobothnia and Oulu areas. There may also be showers in the south. The forecast says the best Midsummer weather is probably in North Karelia and South Karelia.

On Saturday it is drier, and Sunday is dry, at least at first. There is wind at sea.
