# Tuesday 30.10.2018 (radio)

The number of animals has decreased. China again allows the use of endangered animals in science. Nobody survived the plane crash in Indonesia. New kinds of speed cameras are coming to Finland's roads. 3.6 million Finns will get tax refunds.

## The number of animals is decreasing

![Penguins in Antarctica.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10022176)

Open image viewer

Climate warming is destroying the penguins' habitat at the South Pole. Photo: Dean lewins / EPA

The number of wild animals on Earth has decreased quickly.

A report by the environmental organisation WWF says that in about 50 years the number of mammals, fish, reptiles, frogs and birds on Earth has already decreased by 60 percent. The number of animals has decreased most in South and Central America.

The decrease in animals is because their habitats are being destroyed. Habitats are destroyed by humans, who, for example, clear forests for agriculture. Fish species, in turn, are threatened by overfishing.

In Finland the situation of mammals is reasonable, but the situation of many birds is bad.

## China and endangered animals

![A rhinoceros.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10482206)

Open image viewer

There are a few thousand rhinos left in the world. Photo: Getty Images

China is easing its ban on selling endangered animals.

Selling parts of critically endangered animals has been banned in China since 1993. Now China allows them to be sold for scientific, medical and cultural purposes.

Chinese medicine is interested, for example, in parts of the rhinoceros and the tiger. But there is no evidence that parts of either animal cure any diseases.

It is feared that easing the restrictions will increase poaching of tigers and rhinos. There are fewer than 4000 tigers left in the world and a few thousand rhinos.

## The plane crash in Indonesia

![Belongings of the victims of the passenger plane crash have been found in the sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10482146)

Open image viewer

Things that belonged to the passengers of the crashed plane have been found in the sea. Photo: EPA

Indonesia has ordered an inspection of all planes that are the same as Monday's crashed plane.

The Lion Air passenger plane fell into the sea yesterday soon after take-off.

There were 189 people on the plane. The authorities say that everyone died in the crash.

## Speed cameras

![The first new-generation camera pole was installed on Highway 4 at Onkiniemi in Sysmä in October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10482487)

Open image viewer

The first new kind of speed cameras are coming to Highway 4. Photo: Markku Lähdetluoma / Yle

New, more effective speed cameras are coming for traffic monitoring.

The new speed cameras measure cars' speeds from as far as 150 metres away. One camera can also follow the speeds of 32 different vehicles in different lanes at the same time.

Installation of the new cameras will begin in the first half of next year.

Police say that in the coming years 500 camera poles will be put on new sections of road, with 150 surveillance cameras in them.

The old speed cameras will also stay in use.

## Tax refunds

![Tax decision](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9800080)

Open image viewer

Almost 3 billion euros of tax refunds will be paid this year. Nearly 3.6 million people will get a refund, the Tax Administration says.

Almost a million Finns will get a tax refund of over a thousand euros.

Tax refunds will be paid into accounts on 11 December.
