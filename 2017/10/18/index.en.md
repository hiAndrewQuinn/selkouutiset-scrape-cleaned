# Wednesday 18.10.2017 (radio)

A Communist Party congress has begun in China. Three people have been charged with preparing a terrorist crime. The Oulu police have found a large amount of drugs on criminals. New kinds of sleeping pills are being tested. All people are of the same race.

## Party congress in China

![The 19th congress of the Communist Party of China began today in Beijing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9888346)

More than 2000 people are taking part in the Communist Party of China's congress. Photo: Wu Hong / EPA

A Communist Party congress has begun in China. It is China's most important political event, because the Communist Party decides China's affairs. China's leaders are also chosen at the party congress.

The Communist Party's most important leader is now Xi Jinping. He is also the President of China. It is already certain that Xi Jinping will continue in both jobs, because the party congress wants it so.

The party congress is held every 5 years. There are more than 2000 participants at the congress.

## Charges for preparing a terrorist crime

![The ISIS flag](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8069447)

Open image viewer

The flag of the terrorist organisation ISIS. Photo: Mohammed Al-Mosuli / EPA

Three people have been charged with preparing a terrorist crime. They are Finnish citizens.

The accused may have been planning to go to Syria to fight in a terrorist organisation. They may also have raised money through financial crimes.

The authorities suspect that the crimes happened in 2012 and 2013.

The accused themselves say that they have not committed any crimes.

## Police found drugs

![Drugs seized by the Oulu police department.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9888808)

Open image viewer

The police found drugs in Oulu. Photo: Oulu police department.

The Oulu police have found a large amount of drugs on criminals.

The police found 30 kilos of amphetamine. The police suspect that the criminals brought the amphetamine to Finland last summer.

The police also found more than 8 kilos of hashish and a kilo of marijuana on the criminals. The police suspect that the criminals brought the drugs from Sweden.

The police say that the drugs were worth about 1.6 million euros.

## New sleeping pills are being tested

![ A sleepless man in bed at night. An alarm clock and a glass of water on the bedside table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5251387)

Open image viewer

Sleep problems are common. Photo: YLE / Arja Lento

Many people sleep badly. As many as a million Finns suffer from sleep problems.

Now new kinds of sleeping pills are being tested. They improve the quality of sleep. The medicines do not make your memory worse.

A patient may not need to use the new kinds of sleeping pills for long. With the help of the new medicines, sleep problems may end completely.

This is good news for people who now need a sleeping pill every night.

The new kinds of sleeping pills may come to pharmacies in 2 or 3 years.

## We are all the same race

![A researcher standing at a table outside holds a meter on the wrist of a seated test subject and writes down the reading. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9885693)

Open image viewer

A researcher measuring skin tone. Photo: Tishkoff lab

People cannot be divided into races by skin colour. All people are of the same race.

Researchers have studied the human genes that affect skin colour. The genes are the same all around the world.

Researchers say that human skin colour has always varied. At the beginning of human history, there were light-skinned people in Africa and dark-skinned people in Europe.
