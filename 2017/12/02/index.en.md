# Saturday 2.12.2017 (radio)

Social assistance. The President's question hour. The US tax reform. A Christmas calendar. The weather is getting colder.

## December's social assistance may change

![Social assistance](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9382062)

Open image viewer

December's social assistance may change. Photo: Yle/Ronnie Holmberg

Kela has problems paying social assistance. The amount of money in December's social assistance may change.

Kela has had to change the payment decisions of many applicants. They had to be changed because Kela did not take tax refunds into account. You get a tax refund if you have paid too much tax on your income.

Social assistance becomes smaller because of the tax refund. The tax refund will arrive in your own bank account next week on Tuesday.

## The President answered citizens' questions

![Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9957544)

Open image viewer

The President of the Republic answered questions in a live radio programme. Photo: AOP

The President of Finland has answered citizens' questions today. People could talk with the President of the Republic, Sauli Niinistö, in a live radio programme.

The President was asked, for example, how young people could be prevented from becoming excluded from society. The President answered that young people must not be left alone. "We must help if someone is left alone," President Niinistö said.

## The tax reform moves forward in the United States

![A protester's sign in front of the US Capitol](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9959982)

Open image viewer

The protester's sign says that the tax reform must be stopped. Photo: EPA-EFE/MICHAEL REYNOLDS

In the United States, President Donald Trump and the President's party, the Republicans, have succeeded in one goal. The US Senate has approved a big tax reform. The tax reform is an important matter for President Trump.

It is not yet certain whether the reform will happen. If the reform happens, Americans will pay less tax on their income and companies will also pay less tax.

Critics say that the reform only benefits rich people.

## How can a bear sleep all winter?

![A bear builds up a layer of fat before going into hibernation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9395200)

Open image viewer

A bear sleeps all winter. It must not be disturbed. Photo: Tuomo Björksten / Yle

Yle's science desk has its own Christmas calendar. Every day until Christmas Eve, the science desk publishes an interesting science question.

Today's question is: how can a bear sleep all winter? It is possible because the bear's metabolism is shut down. That means that in winter the bear does not eat or drink anything, and it does not poo anything either.

You can find the science Christmas calendar on Yle's website, the address is yle.fi.
