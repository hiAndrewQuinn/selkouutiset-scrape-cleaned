## World champion Kalle Rovanperä

![Kalle Rovanperä celebrates the world championship.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4067,h_2287,x_196,y_224/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1015598633921c3955f9/1664694361)

Finland has been successful in motorsport. Finland's Kalle Rovanperä is the new rally world champion.

Rovanperä won the New Zealand Rally on Sunday. After the win he has so many points in the World Championship that the title is certain. There are 2 more competitions left in the World Championship, but no one can overtake Rovanperä any more.

Rovanperä is the youngest rally driver to have won the world championship. Rovanperä turned 22 years old yesterday, Saturday.

The last time a Finnish rally driver won the world championship was 20 years ago. Finland's Marcus Grönholm was world champion in 2002.

## Unrest in Indonesia

![A woman walks next to a destroyed police car at a football stadium in Malang, Indonesia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_95/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10155916338f2fb9de13/1664676890)

Traces of unrest at a football stadium in Indonesia. Photo: Sandi Sadewa / EPA

In Asia, in Indonesia, people have died in unrest. The unrest began at a football match.

At least 125 people have died. The number of dead may grow, because there are many injured.

The unrest began when many thousands of people rushed onto the pitch after a football match. They supported the team that lost the match.

In the end the police fired tear gas onto the pitch, and panic broke out in the crowd. Many were trampled underfoot, as everyone tried to leave the stadium at the same time.

Indonesia's president says that the incident must be investigated carefully.

## Brazil's presidential elections

![A man plays a drum at a supporters' event for Brazilian presidential candidate Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva in Rio de Janeiro.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_73/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1015500633852c2de828/1664638099)

These Brazilians hope that Lula da Silva wins the presidential election. Photo: Débora Agostini / Yle

Presidential elections were held in Brazil, in South America, on Sunday.

Opinion polls predict that the candidates who get the most votes will be the current president, Jair Bolsonaro, and the former president, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.

Bolsonaro is the candidate of the far right. Lula is the candidate of the left-wing Workers' Party. Lula has gained more support in the opinion polls than Bolsonaro.

If none of the candidates gets over 50 percent of the votes, a second round of the presidential election will be held in Brazil 4 weeks later.

Brazil's election interests many people, because Brazil is the largest country in South America. It is also important for the whole region's economy.
