# Monday 15.8.2022 (radio)

Food prices rise quickly. Inflation continues in autumn as well. The war in Ukraine may last a long time. Prime Minister Sanna Marin met the prime ministers of the other Nordic countries. The police arrested suspected cutters of the train cable. Weather forecast.

## Food prices

![A woman stands and holds a shopping basket in a shop, showing coffee, toilet paper and lasagne sheets.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8688,h_4887,x_0,y_224/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96935462a7206db356b/1655120307)

Food prices are now rising quickly. Photo: Antti J. Leinonen

The rise in food prices continues fast. Prices of food and drinks rose in July by over 12 percent from July last year.

The biggest price increases were for coffee, fish, flour, potatoes and eggs.

Coffee has become about 46 percent more expensive in a year. Flour and fish have become almost 40 percent more expensive in a year.

## Inflation

![Picture from a nuclear power plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4743,h_2655,x_0,y_494/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98540762da910e5fe73/1658492389)

If the Olkiluoto 3 power plant produces electricity well in the winter, it will ease energy supply. Photo: Lucas Ekblad / Yle

Inflation, that is, the rise in prices, is now fast. Inflation has been this fast last in the 1980s.

Yle has asked economic experts how inflation will continue. The experts estimate that inflation will continue fast also in the autumn. The outlook for next year has also worsened because of the energy crisis.

It is not yet known how electricity and natural gas will be enough in the winter. Uncertainty about energy production raises the price of energy. That is why inflation may be fast also next year.

## The war in Ukraine

![Defence Forces commander Timo Kivinen speaks into a microphone while sitting in a panel discussion at Kultaranta on 13.6.2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3564,h_2004,x_1338,y_219/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96925962a7047fea421/1655467530)

Open image viewer

General Timo Kivinen. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

The war in Ukraine may continue for a long time. The reason is that neither side of the war, Ukraine or Russia, is reaching its goals. This is how the commander of the Finnish army, that is, the Defence Forces, Timo Kivinen, assesses. Kivinen does not believe the war will spread outside Ukraine.

Kivinen spoke on Monday at the opening of the national defence course.

Defence Minister Antti Kaikkonen also spoke at the event. Kaikkonen said that Finland may be a NATO member by the end of the year, even though negotiations with Turkey are difficult.

## Meeting of the Nordic prime ministers

![Sanna Marin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_114/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99167462f0e06854a63/1659955993)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Photo: Aalto Puutio / Yle

The Nordic prime ministers met on Monday at an informal meeting in Oslo. Prime Minister Sanna Marin took part in the meeting from Finland.

The main topic of the meeting was defence cooperation in the Nordic countries. Finland and Sweden will soon join the military alliance NATO. The other Nordic countries are already NATO members.

German Chancellor Olaf Scholz also visited the meeting of the Nordic prime ministers.

## Cable theft in Lempäälä

![Railway bridge in Lempäälä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4242,h_2386,x_0,y_54/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99171062f0f0ba37250/1659957648)

Open image viewer

The suspected vandalism that disrupted train traffic happened at the bridge in Lempäälä. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

The police have arrested two people for cutting the railway cables.

The police suspect that the people arrested cut the railway cable in Lempäälä last week. Train traffic between Tampere and Riihimäki stopped for many hours then.

The police identified and found the suspects with the help of video recordings.

The weather is really warm this week.
