## Presidential poll

![Pekka Haavisto and Alexander Stubb side by side. The Finnish flag can be seen in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1317,h_740,x_240,y_140/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116170464e7182255b98/1703140471)

Pekka Haavisto and Alexander Stubb are the most popular presidential candidates. Photo: Yle

The presidential election may be even. This is what Yle's new survey says.

In the presidential poll, people were asked whom they intend to vote for in the presidential election. Most support went to Pekka Haavisto and Alexander Stubb.

Among all the candidates, Haavisto's support was about 30 percent and Stubb's support about 20 percent.

If Haavisto and Stubb are against each other in the second round of the presidential election, they would get roughly the same number of votes.

In the presidential election, a second round is held if none of the candidates gets over 50 percent of the votes in the first round of the election.

The presidential election in Finland will be held at the beginning of next year.

## Interest rates

![General view – ECB head office in Frankfurt, Germany.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3936,h_2214,x_0,y_363/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89288261bb0fd8e030f/1639649459)

The ECB head office is in Frankfurt, Germany. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

The European Central Bank, that is the ECB, is raising interest rates again. The ECB said on Thursday that it is raising its key interest rates by 0.25 percentage points.

With interest rates the ECB fights against inflation, that is, the rise in prices. The ECB has already been raising interest rates for about a year.

The ECB's interest rate decisions have also affected the interest rates of many home loans. The interest rate of many home loans has risen, and therefore paying the loan is more expensive than before.

In Finland, economists already hope for a lower interest rate. The Finnish economy has slowed down, and for example the construction industry has come to a standstill. That is why the Finnish economy does not need to be cooled down with interest rates, the economists say.

## Parental leave

![Two adults with three children in front of a trampoline look toward the camera.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3826,h_2152,x_10,y_254/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117059764ff6370e9819/1694458901)

Open image viewer

In Finland it is hoped that fathers also use parental leave. Photo: Patrik Molander / Yle

Men still use parental leave less than women.

Parental leave was reformed a year ago. The aim is that fathers stay at home to take care of children more often than before.

However, Kela's information shows that men use only a small part of parental leave.

Researchers, however, say that it is still too early to say how successful the reform of parental leave has been. People's attitudes change slowly.

In South and East Finland a lot of rain has fallen.

In many places more than 30 millimetres of rain fell in 1 day.

The most rain fell in Tohmajärvi. There, more than 50 millimetres of rain fell in 24 hours.
