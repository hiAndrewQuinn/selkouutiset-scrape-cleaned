# Thursday 4.1.2018 (radio)

Bank strike. Power cuts. Young people and older people. Jari Sillanpää. Hair business in Turkey.

## The bank strike continues tomorrow

![Strike guards in front of Danske Bank.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10005328)

Open image viewer

Many banks were closed today. The strike guards are wearing orange vests. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

Many banks were closed today because of the strike. The branches of Nordea, Aktia and Danske Bank were closed, and they will be closed tomorrow too. The bank employees are on strike because the employees and the employer are arguing about doing weekend work.

Bank employees were already on strike for two days last week.

## Still power cuts in Kainuu

![A snowy forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10002024)

Open image viewer

Snow cannon snow is heavy. It presses trees onto power lines. Photo: Loiste Sähköverkko Oy

In Kainuu in Eastern Finland, there are still a thousand homes without power. The power cuts have continued for more than five days already.

The electricity company Loiste has been doing repair work, but conditions are difficult in places. The electricity company has asked the Defence Forces for help with moving about in the terrain.

There is still a lot of snow cannon snow on the trees, and the branches of the trees bend onto the power lines. New faults keep coming to light all the time.

More snow is also coming to Kainuu.

The rescue service reminds everyone that they should now look after their neighbours in the areas with power cuts. Hyrynsalmi has also prepared for emergency accommodation. Many ordinary people have also offered help.

## Young people and older people live together

![A nurse and an elderly person.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9934844)

Open image viewer

In a service home in Helsinki, older people and young people live together. Photo: Heikki Haapalainen / Yle

In Helsinki, shared living between older people and young people is being tried again.

The service home located in Laajasalo is looking for two residents under the age of 25. They only pay a small rent for their own studio flat.

In addition to the rent, the young people must spend time with the older people, dating and doing things together.

The same service home looked for young people for similar living two years ago. Then more than 300 young people wanted to move into the service home.

## Favourite singer goes to court

![Jari Sillanpää](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9833749)

Open image viewer

Jari Sillanpää is suspected of four crimes. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

The popular entertainer Jari Sillanpää is going to court.

He is accused of four crimes. The crimes are drink driving, a drug use offence and two traffic offences.

The police suspect that Sillanpää drove a car in Helsinki in September under the influence of a drug. Metamphetamine drugs were found in his car.

## Many hair transplants are done in Turkey

![American Keith after a hair transplant. Keith has to wear a bandage for some time and wait for his hair to grow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9995487)

Open image viewer

Keith travelled from the USA to Turkey and got a hair transplant. Photo: Sanna Raita-aho / Yle

Again to Turkey. Turkey has become a popular destination for people who want a hair transplant. In particular, many people come to Turkey from the Middle East for hair transplants.

In Turkey a hair transplant is cheap. Turkey hopes that various health treatments will attract tourists to the country after the unrest of recent years.
