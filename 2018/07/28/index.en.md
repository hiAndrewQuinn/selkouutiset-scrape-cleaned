# Saturday 28.7.2018 (radio)

Forest fires continue in Sweden. This weekend the World Rally Championship rally is held in Jyväskylä, and the Kuninkuusravit harness racing event is held in Rovaniemi. Clouds covered the lunar eclipse in different parts of Finland. The sun sets again across Finland.

## Forest burns in Sweden

![Ground fire in Ljusdal, Sweden.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10321207)

Open image viewer

Forests are burning in different parts of Sweden. Photo: Maja Suslin / AFP

The forest fires continue in Finland's neighbouring country, Sweden. The fire has burned forest in different parts of Sweden for over 2 weeks.

Sweden has never before had forest fires this big. The summer has been dry and hot in Sweden. That is why the fire has spread quickly.

Sweden has received help for the firefighting from many countries. A group of Finnish firefighters also arrived in Sweden on Saturday. They will help with the firefighting for at least a week.

## Rally cars compete in Jyväskylä

![Ott Tänak in flight.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10327435)

Estonian Ott Tänak leads the rally in Jyväskylä. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

Big car races are being held in Jyväskylä in Central Finland this weekend. Rally Finland is part of the World Rally Championship for rally cars.

Estonian Ott Tänak led the rally on Saturday. Tänak and the other drivers have attracted many spectators along the roads of Jyväskylä.

The rally continues on Sunday. The World Rally Championship rally was first held in Jyväskylä as early as 1951.

## Harness horses compete in Rovaniemi

![Saaga S](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10324015)

Open image viewer

Harness horses compete in Rovaniemi. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva.

Harness racing takes place in Lapland this weekend, when the Kuninkuusravit is held in Rovaniemi. At the races, horses compete to see which horse is the fastest.

Mares, that is female horses, and stallions, that is male horses, compete in their own categories. The fastest mare is this year's queen, and the fastest stallion is this year's king.

The Kuninkuusravit is an important event for horse breeders. Everyone hopes that their own horse will become king or queen. In addition, the winner gets a lot of money.

## The lunar eclipse went behind the clouds

![red moon](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10325060)

Open image viewer

The red Moon went behind the clouds in Helsinki. Photo: Rolex Dela Pena / EPA

On Friday evening there was a total lunar eclipse in the sky. The Moon was in the Earth's shadow. That is why the Moon looked red.

Unfortunately, there were also clouds in the sky in different parts of Finland. That is why the lunar eclipse was visible in Helsinki for only a few seconds.

In many places, however, the lunar eclipse was visible, and people took a lot of photos of the lunar eclipse. You can see photos of the lunar eclipse on Yle's website, at yle.fi.

## The midnight sun is over

![Čuvges ijat nohket Sámis](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10325076)

Open image viewer

Lapland's summer night is starting to get shorter. Photo: Maiju Saijets / Yle

The midnight sun is over. The sun sets again across Finland, including right in the far north, in Utsjoki in Lapland.

Utsjoki's first dark night is short. The sun sets on Sunday at 00:30, but it rises again at about 2 am.

The midnight sun began in Utsjoki on 16 May. The sun stayed in the sky for over 2 months.
