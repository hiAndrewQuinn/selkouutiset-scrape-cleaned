## Finland's NATO membership

![Man sits in a YLE studio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3084,h_1734,x_53,y_661/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1002968631302a213093/1662203069)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto was a guest on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme on Saturday. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

Yle has interviewed Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto about Finland's NATO membership. Finland will perhaps become a NATO member in a few months.

Haavisto said that Finland takes part in the defence of all NATO countries. Haavisto said that Finland gets all the rights in NATO, and Finland takes care of all the obligations in NATO.

Haavisto said that Nordic cooperation will become closer when Finland becomes a NATO member. Finland will also take part in defending NATO elsewhere too, if Finland is needed.

## Mikhail Gorbachev's funeral

![Mikhail Gorbachev's coffin is carried on display in Moscow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3238,h_1821,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1002973631306f586079/1662191439)

Open image viewer

Mikhail Gorbachev's funeral was held in Moscow. Photo: Maxim Shipenkov / EPA

The last leader of the Soviet Union, Mikhail Gorbachev, has been buried in Moscow. A memorial ceremony for Gorbachev was held in the morning at the House of Unions in central Moscow.

Thousands of Russians came to say goodbye to Gorbachev. Russian President Vladimir Putin did not take part in Gorbachev's funeral.

Gorbachev died last Tuesday. He was 91 years old.

## Difficulties for entrepreneurs

![Pastry counter in a café.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_756/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97330562b1d53bb03e9/1656494971)

Open image viewer

Entrepreneurs' costs have risen quickly. That is why prices must also be raised. Photo: Sofia Herrlin / Yle

The war in Ukraine and the energy crisis make the situation of many entrepreneurs harder. Entrepreneurs are people who have their own company.

Entrepreneurs, for example, have raised prices and started to save energy, because electricity and many goods keep getting more expensive. This is what a survey by the Finnish Entrepreneurs (Suomen Yrittäjät) organisation shows. It was made for entrepreneurs.

Over 30 percent of entrepreneurs say that they have raised prices. Over 30 percent of entrepreneurs say that they have saved energy. A company can, for example, lower the temperature so that energy is saved.

About 30 percent of entrepreneurs say that their company's profitability has worsened.

## Peace march in support of Ukraine

![People taking part in the peace march at Senate Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-100305163134fcc5a6d1/1662210089)

Open image viewer

About 1,500 people took part in the peace event at Senate Square. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

On Saturday a peace march in support of Ukraine was held in Helsinki. Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto and Ukraine's ambassador Olga Dibrova spoke at the event.

Ambassador Dibrova said how the war has destroyed churches, hospitals and schools in Ukraine, as well as homes. Dibrova also said that Ukraine needs military aid so that the war can be brought to an end.

The peace march ended at Senate Square in Helsinki, where there were, for example, music performances. About 1,500 people took part in the peace event.
