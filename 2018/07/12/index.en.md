# Thursday 12.7.2018 (radio)

Police have arrested former Helsinki drugs police chief Jari Aarnio again. Finns spend less money on summer holidays than other Nordic people. Foreign visitors wonder about Finnish tap water at the youth World Championships in Tampere. Croatia has reached the football World Cup final.

## Aarnio was arrested

![Jari Aarnio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10300704)

Open image viewer

Former head of the drugs police Jari Aarnio. Photo: Miika Siltalahti / Lehtikuva

The police have arrested Jari Aarnio again. Aarnio is the former head of the Helsinki drugs police.

The police arrested Aarnio on Wednesday. The police suspect that Aarnio is linked to a murder case.

The murder happened 15 years ago. The police suspect that Aarnio may have known about the murder plan, but did not prevent the murder.

The police are now questioning Aarnio. On Saturday it will be decided whether Aarnio is held in custody.

Two years ago Aarnio was sentenced to 10 years in prison for drug crimes. It was big news, because Aarnio had been the head of the drugs police before.

Aarnio appealed the sentence, and about a month ago he was allowed home to wait for the court's decision. Now Aarnio is back in police hands.

## Athletes get tap water

![Tap water station at the Tampere World Championships](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10300983)

Open image viewer

A Bahamian athlete fills a water bottle. Photo: Henriikka Hiilloskorpi / Yle

Foreign visitors wonder about Finnish tap water at the youth World Championships in Tampere.

The young athletes are to be shown how plastic waste can be reduced. That is why they have been given only one drinking bottle. The bottle says in English "drink tapwater", which means "drink tap water".

At the Tampere athletics stadium there are taps, from which the athletes get water for their bottles. For some athletes, tap water is a completely new thing. Many have never drunk tap water before.

The young athletes wonder how clean and fresh water can come from a tap. Foreign visitors think Finnish tap water tastes good.

## Croatia reached the World Cup final

![Croatia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10300305)

Croatia's footballers celebrate reaching the final. Photo: EPA

Croatia has reached the football World Cup final. Croatia's place in the final was confirmed when it beat England 2-1 on Wednesday.

Small Croatia is the surprise of the World Cup. Croatia will play France in the final.

On Sunday we will find out whether the world champion is France or Croatia this time.

## The summer heat record was set

![Weather picture: in Lapland the temperature rose to +30 degrees in places today.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10302184)

Open image viewer

Thursday was a hot day in Lapland. Photo: Yle

Finland now has hot summer weather. It is hottest in the far north.

In Utsjoki in Lapland, a new record high for this summer was measured on Thursday. In Utsjoki it was 30.3 degrees.

On Wednesday in Sodankylä in Lapland it was 30.1 degrees. The last time there were temperatures above 30 degrees was 3 years ago, in July 2015.

At the weekend the heat will move from the north to the south, and in the south temperatures above 25 degrees can be measured.
