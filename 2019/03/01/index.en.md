## Things are bad at an Attendo nursing home

![Attendo nursing home in Utajärvi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10668347)

Open image viewer

There are problems at the Attendo nursing home in Utajärvi. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

The care company Attendo is in difficulties again because of an elderly care home.

The authorities decided that Attendo may no longer provide care at the nursing home in Utajärvi. The elderly have felt unsafe at the home, because there have been too few carers. There were faults in the distribution of medicines and in all the care.

The municipalities are now taking care of the home's affairs themselves.

## Insurance company Fennia is laying off workers

![Fennia's office in Kuopio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7898809)

Open image viewer

The insurance company Fennia has too many workers. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

The insurance company Fennia is laying off many workers.

The co-operation negotiations concern more than a thousand people. Of them, perhaps 100 will lose their jobs.

Workers are being laid off because there are too many of them. Fennia bought the insurance company Folksam.

## Vocational students are satisfied

![Vocational training](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6804106)

Open image viewer

Students studying vocational subjects are satisfied with their studies, a survey says. Photo: Yle

Students who study in vocational education are satisfied with their studies.

Students were asked about their education when they were near the end of their studies. 85 percent of students said that they are satisfied with the education. The most satisfied are students whose mother tongue is something other than Finnish.

The teaching has been reformed. Students spend a lot of time at workplaces learning. In addition, you can apply for vocational education at any time, because you can do your studies at your own pace.

## Refugee spying

![Woman in front of a banner.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6085963)

Open image viewer

Practitioners of the Falun Gong religion believe that the Chinese state follows them in Finland. Photo: Yle

In Sweden, a court has jailed a man who may be guilty of refugee spying.

That means that the man in Sweden has followed people who criticise the administration of their own home country. The man then gave that information to that country. Swedish authorities do not say which country the man may have spied for.

## Relay in the ski competition

At the ski world championships, the men's relay was skied today. The Finnish team was 4th in the competition.

On the final straight, a French skier overtook Finland. Norway won, and Russia was 2nd.
