## The Pandora Papers

![Gennadi Timtšenko and Vladimir Putin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2929,h_1648,x_0,y_92/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8618546159d0ccdc67c/1633351669)

Gennadi Timtšenko belongs to the innermost circle of Russian President Vladimir Putin. Photo: Yegor Aleyev / AOP &amp; Sergey Guneev / EPA

A historically large data leak tells about tax haven companies.

Tax havens are countries where taxes can be avoided. For example, the Cayman Islands and Bermuda are tax havens.

The documents tell how property and income have been hidden.

Over 200 Finns have companies in tax havens. The so-called Pandora Papers reveal that many of them have criminal backgrounds.

Journalists of Yle's MOT team have also investigated the revelation.

Hundreds of foreign politicians are involved in tax havens. Among them are dozens of former or current heads of state. No Finnish politicians can be found in the Pandora Papers.

The papers also contain a lot of new information about the activities of the inner circle of Russian President Vladimir Putin. They include the businessman Gennadi Timtšenko, who also has Finnish citizenship.

## Corona infections

![Vaccination sign near the Ratina vaccination point](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2857,h_1607,x_0,y_147/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8593476152f3a8329ab/1632826344)

Open image viewer

The corona situation has again begun to turn for the worse. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

The number of corona infections has turned upward in Finland. Before this, the number of corona infections had been falling from the end of August.

The Institute for Health and Welfare says that the incidence rate is now almost 118. In the previous 2-week period the figure was about 110.

The incidence rate tells the number of disease cases per 100,000 inhabitants over a 2-week period.

There are 136 people with corona in hospital care, that is 14 more than on Friday. Of these, 28 are in intensive care, that is 4 more than on Friday.

## Tynkkynen's fines

![Sebastian Tynkkynen waits for trial with a mask on his face](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-85635661497d156a8a3/1632206180)

Open image viewer

MP Sebastian Tynkkynen. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

Finns Party MP Sebastian Tynkkynen has been sentenced to a fine. The reason is incitement against a group of people.

Oulu District Court ordered Tynkkynen to pay 70 day-fines.

The verdict concerns Tynkkynen's texts on Facebook during the municipal election campaign 4 years ago. The texts dealt with the alleged behaviour of immigrants and asylum seekers.

Tynkkynen has previously been sentenced 2 times for incitement against a group of people.

## Animal welfare reports

![dog](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5888955d722b29961de/1567763418)

Open image viewer

Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

Animal welfare reports have increased this year. In particular, reports have been made about the violent treatment of dogs.

About 8,000 animal welfare reports came to the attention of Finland's animal welfare authorities last year. In 2019 the figure was about 6,500.

Over 90 per cent of the reports concern pets, most often dogs and cats.
