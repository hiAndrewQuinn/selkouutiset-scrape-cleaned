# Sunday 25.9.2016 (radio)

Experiences of mediation in crimes are good. Many elderly people are financially exploited. Helping makes you happy. Fish can be heard singing in the sea.

## Mediation in crimes

![Helsinki prison.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7949771)

Open image viewer

Often the criminal themselves wants mediation. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Mediation in serious crimes has been tried out in Finland. Mediation means that the person who committed the crime meets the victim of the crime or the victim's family members. Professional helpers are always present at mediation too.

Serious crimes are, for example, rape and assault. In mediation, a common question is why the crime happened. The person who committed the crime often wonders whether they can somehow make up for the crime.

Experiences of mediation are good. After mediation, the victim of the crime can better move on with life. And the person who committed the crime is able to stop committing crimes more easily if they take part in mediation.

Because of the good experiences, the mediation work is planned to continue if money is found for the work.

## Exploitation of the elderly

![An older person takes money from a wallet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9190587)

Open image viewer

Sometimes children take all of an elderly person's money. Photo: Yle

Many elderly people are financially exploited. Often the guilty person is the elderly person's own adult child. They may take money and property from the elderly person.

In bad cases, the adult child moves in with the elderly person and lives a bad life there. The elderly person has to pay for everything, and may even be hit. Often the reason is alcohol.

The Suvanto association helps elderly people who are exploited. The association advises people to report exploitation, for example, to the police or to a municipal social worker. The authorities can appoint a guardian for the elderly person, who takes care of the elderly person's affairs.

## Happiness

![Emma Seppälä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9185738)

Open image viewer

Emma Seppälä studies what happiness is. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

People look for happiness in the wrong places. This is what Emma Seppälä, who studies happiness, says. Emma Seppälä lives in the USA, but Seppälä's grandfather was Finnish.

People think that money makes you happy. But when a person gets money, they want more of it. A person runs after the wrong kind of happiness, Seppälä says.

Real happiness comes from life having a purpose. A person finds happiness when they decide to help and encourage others.

Seppälä also says that a person must know how to calm down. You must not take work matters home. The Finnish way of calming down in the sauna and in nature is good, researcher Emma Seppälä says.

## The song of fish

![Batfish (Platax orbicularis). ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9187041)

Open image viewer

Fish are not always quiet. Photo: Alexander Vasenin / CC BY-SA 3.0 / https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Did you know that sounds can be heard in the sea too? Under water, you can hear, for example, the sounds of fish.

Australian researchers have recorded the song of fish in the sea. The researchers identified 7 different choirs. They sang in the morning and in the evening.

You can get to know the song of fish on the Yle News website at yle.fi/uutiset.
