Sanna Marin and journalists in the radio programme Prime Minister's Interview Hour. Photo: Stina Virkamäki / Prime Minister's Office

Prime Minister Sanna Marin is worried about the price of electricity. People are paying big electricity bills, but energy companies are making profits.

That is why the government is preparing a way to tax the profits of energy companies. In this way the state can get tax revenue, which can be used to support electricity users.

Marin spoke about this in the radio programme Prime Minister's Interview Hour.

## Protests in China

![A large crowd and many police officers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12682009)

Open image viewer

Protest in Shanghai. Photo: Hector Retamal / AFP

Chinese people have protested against coronavirus restrictions. There have been demonstrations in the cities of Beijing and Shanghai, for example.

Protesters have even demanded that President Xi Jinping resign. It is not common in China for people to criticise the president.

Chinese people are angry, because China's leaders have ordered millions of people into quarantine because of coronavirus infections. Whole cities have been closed.

China's leaders want to prevent coronavirus infections completely. However, the number of coronavirus infections in China has grown this autumn.

## Weekend traffic accidents

![Side of a police car with the word Police on it.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_570/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-101827063403b725239e/1669795506)

Open image viewer

Traffic accidents have given the police a lot of work. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

Many accidents have happened on the roads this weekend. At least 5 people have died in the accidents in total.

In Loimaa, 2 young people died when their car left the road. The car hit a storage building and caught fire.

In Kouvola, a car hit a motorhome that was travelling in the same direction. The car caught fire, and its driver died.

In Pello in Lapland, a collision killed 2 men.

## First Advent Sunday

![Four candles. One of them is burning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12682014)

Open image viewer

The first Advent candle has been lit. A new Advent candle is lit every Advent Sunday. Photo: Frugan/Mostphotos.com

On Sunday the first Advent Sunday was celebrated. The waiting for Christmas begins then.

The first Advent Sunday is an important tradition for many Finns. For example, more people go to church than on an ordinary Sunday.

There are 4 Advent Sundays before Christmas in total. So the waiting for Christmas lasts about a month.

## The weather

The new week brings no changes to the weather.
