# Friday 21.9.2018 (radio)

Parliament has confidence in Foreign Minister Timo Soini. Move tests continue in schools. Dairy products may be good for your health. An autumn storm is coming to Finland on Saturday.

## Foreign Minister Soini

![Timo Soini](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10416979)

Open image viewer

Foreign Minister Timo Soini. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Parliament has confidence in Foreign Minister Timo Soini. Parliament voted on the matter on Friday. Soini got the support of the government parties.

The opposition parties did not give their support to the Foreign Minister. In the opposition parties' opinion Soini has acted wrongly when he has opposed women's right to abortion abroad. The opposition parties say that women's rights are an important part of Finland's foreign policy.

The government parties have more members in Parliament than the opposition parties. That is why Soini won the vote in Parliament.

## Move tests

![Move test](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9208373)

Open image viewer

The Move test measures fitness. Photo: Aatu Itkonen/Yle

Move tests continue in schools. The Move test measures children's fitness.

The Finnish National Agency for Education says that the test is safe. The agency asked experts to look into the safety of the test. The experts say that the Move test is safe for healthy children.

The safety of the Move test began to be looked into after a pupil had a seizure during the test in Espoo. The pupil was taken to hospital, where he died.

## Dairy products

![Milk is poured from Arla and Valio cartons into the same glass.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10401972)

Open image viewer

Dairy products may be good for your health. A new study says that dairy products perhaps reduce heart disease and strokes.

The health effect of dairy products was seen in people who had dairy products at least 2 times a day. According to the study, for example milk and yoghurt are healthy.

The researchers do not know for sure why dairy products are healthy. The reason may be that milk has a lot of potassium, calcium and magnesium. They lower blood pressure. Yoghurt also has healthy lactic acid bacteria.

The researchers say that more research on the matter is still needed.

## Autumn storm

![Autumn storm at Nallikari in Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9745266)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ilkka Uusitalo / AOP

An autumn storm is coming to Finland on Saturday.

Yle meteorologist Matti Huutonen says that the storm wind may be dangerously strong. The wind speed may be as much as 20 metres per second. The storm may cut off the electricity in many homes.

The storm will also be felt at sea. The ferries to Sweden may have to travel more slowly than usual. Viking Line and Tallink Silja promise to tell customers if there are changes to the timetable.
