# Tuesday 19.10.2021 (radio)

Recommendation for a third corona vaccine. Corona has moved heart operations. The Välkky technology warns of game animals on the road. It is now lingonberry season. The weather.

## Third corona vaccinations

The Institute for Health and Welfare recommends a third corona vaccine for everyone who has turned 60 years old.

A third dose is also recommended for risk groups.

The vaccine is also given to everyone who is in institutions and care homes, regardless of age.

A third dose can be given when at least six months have passed since the second dose.

About 77 000 people have already received a third dose.

## Moving heart operations

![Surgeons at work in a hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3372,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-20865154be49823d0c0/1421756929)

HUS has had to move heart operations, because there are so many corona patients in the hospital. Photo: Yle/Sofi Nordmyr

More and more heart operations have had to be moved in Uusimaa. The reason is that there are more corona patients in hospital than before.

HUS's chief medical director Jari Petäjä says that last week a record number of heart operations were moved, 10 in total.

At HUS, 55 patients are in hospital care because of corona, 13 of whom are in intensive care.

HUS's information says that 77 per cent of corona patients have not received a corona vaccine.

## Välkky warning system

![Ilkka Pirttimaa, technology director, InnoTrafik](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4928,h_2772,x_0,y_254/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-868209616d11807f551/1634641151)

Open image viewer

The warning system also makes it possible to lower the speed limit at risk times, says InnoTrafik's technology director Ilkka Pirttimaa. Photo: Birgitta Vuorela / Yle

New technology is being used to try to reduce animal accidents on roads.

The Välkky warning system warns of the movements of game animals.

Välkky warns with light at risk times and when the system has detected an animal. The light is directed by remote control.

The speed limit can also be lowered when the risk of an accident is high.

The system is being tested on main road 19 at Seinäjoki airport in South Ostrobothnia. InnoTrafik, the ELY Centre and the Finnish Wildlife Agency are taking part in the trial.

There are also ordinary traffic signs on roads that warn about animals. However, not all drivers pay attention to them.

Statistics Finland says that more than 14 000 game animal accidents happened last year alone.

Game animals are moose, reindeer and roe deer.

## Lingonberries

![Two red lingonberries on moss, with stalks around them. Picture taken on 15 October 2021 in Rengo, Hämeenlinna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-868783616e85ec40b04/1634633295)

Open image viewer

Now it is worth looking for lingonberries in the bog. Photo: Kati Turtola / Yle

There are plenty of lingonberries this autumn. Lingonberries grow in bogs.

Lingonberries can be picked until snow covers the tussocks. Lingonberries do not spoil in the cold. Frost makes the lingonberry even juicier and softer.

If you forget berry picking in autumn, you can also collect lingonberries after the snow has melted.

Only 10 000–30 000 kilos of lingonberries end up for sale each year.
