# Radio | Clear News | Saturday 7.10.2023

Fighting between Israel and Hamas. Autumn storm. Interior Minister Rantanen. The weather.

## Israel and Hamas

There is fighting in Israel. The Palestinian militant group Hamas has attacked Israel.

Many rockets have been fired at Israel from the Gaza Strip area. The Gaza Strip is on the border between Israel and Egypt.

The Palestinian militant group Hamas says that it fired the rockets. Many Israelis have been killed and injured in the rocket attacks.

Israel has in turn started rocket attacks on Gaza against Hamas. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu says that Israel is now at war.

European countries do not accept the Hamas attack. Finnish President Sauli Niinistö and Foreign Minister Elina Valtonen also condemn the attacks on Israel.

Relations between Israelis and Palestinians have been bad for a long time. The last time the Palestinian militant group Hamas and Israel fought was in 2021. The war then lasted 11 days.

## Finns in Israel

The Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs has given advice to Finns who are in Israel. About 1000 Finns live in Israel.

The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says that Finns should follow the instructions of the Israeli authorities. They should also make a travel notification to the Finnish authorities.

## Autumn storm

![Water from the storm has risen onto the bike path in Pikku Huopalahti, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_352/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-118276665211fda5fdde/1696669818)

Open image viewer

The autumn storm brought water to the streets. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

There has been an autumn storm in Finland. The wind has blown hard and rain has fallen in different parts of Finland.

The autumn storm cut the electricity for many homes. Between Friday and Saturday night, as many as 30 000 homes were without electricity.

The autumn storm has also knocked down trees. Rescue services have cleared trees away from the roads, for example.

Because of the autumn storm, ferry trips have also been cancelled. The cancellations have affected ferry traffic between Finland and Sweden and between Finland and Estonia.

## Interior Minister Rantanen

![Interior Minister Mari Rantanen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5202,h_2926,x_0,y_333/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1182800652140226fa17/1696677991)

Interior Minister Mari Rantanen praises the Finnish police. Rantanen trusts the police in the fight against street gangs and drug criminals.

This week the Finnish police stopped an attempt by Swedish criminals to bring drugs into Finland. In Interior Minister Rantanen's opinion, the police showed Swedish criminals that they should not come to Finland.

A lot has been said in Finland about Swedish criminal gangs. Finland wants to prevent them from starting to bring drugs to Finland too.
