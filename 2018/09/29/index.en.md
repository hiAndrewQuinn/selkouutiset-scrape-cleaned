# Saturday 29.9.2018 (radio)

An earthquake and a tsunami struck Indonesia. There is a severe storm in Greece. The governor of the Bank of Finland estimates that mortgage interest rates will start to rise in a year. Forest improves learning.

## Earthquake in Indonesia

![Damage caused by the tsunami.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10430330)

The earthquake and tsunami also destroyed houses. Photo: BNPB Handout / AOP

In Indonesia, in Asia, there has been an earthquake. The earthquake also caused a tsunami, that is, a really big wave.

At least about 400 people died because of the earthquake and tsunami. Several hundred have been injured.

It is difficult for rescuers to get help through, because the earthquake cut roads and bridges. Electricity and phones also work poorly in the area.

This time the earthquake and tsunami struck the island of Sulawesi, which is part of Indonesia.

Indonesia is in an area where earthquakes are common. Indonesia last had an earthquake in August.

## Mortgage interest rates

![Olli Rehn.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9764727)

Governor of the Bank of Finland Olli Rehn. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Here in Finland, mortgage interest rates will start to rise in a year's time. This is what Bank of Finland governor Olli Rehn estimates.

Rehn says that it is good for people to make sure that their finances can withstand an interest rate rise. Now interest rates are low, but a mortgage must be managed also after the interest rate rises.

Many Finns have a mortgage, and a large part of their salary goes to paying off the loan.

## Learning in the forest

![Mahnala environmental school in Hämeenkyrö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10401709)

Open image viewer

There is a short distance from Mahnala school to the forest. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

Forest improves learning. This has been noticed at Mahnala school in Hämeenkyrö. The school has held lessons in the forest for over 20 years.

Studies show that the forest calms the brain. The teachers and pupils of Mahnala school also say that learning and remembering are easier in the forest than in the classroom.

In the forest, pupils get to explore the environment themselves. When children do things themselves and move more, they also learn better.

Doing things together in the forest also develops children's social skills. They get to know each other better, and bullying decreases.

In many schools people are thinking about how phones affect learning. At Mahnala school phones are not a problem. In the forest, pupils do not even remember their phone.

No wonder the nearby forest is an important place for the pupils of Mahnala school.
