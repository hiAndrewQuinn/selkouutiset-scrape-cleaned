Prime Minister Petteri Orpo has visited Ukraine.

In the morning Orpo met President Volodymyr Zelensky. Orpo said that a new aid package is coming to Ukraine from Finland. It is already Finland's 18th aid package to Ukraine.

Orpo also visited Irpin near Kyiv. Houses that Finland has given have been erected there for people who have lost their homes.

## School health care

![A sign reading school nurse and school counsellor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3767,h_2119,x_0,y_177/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6513975e665225ec68e/1583764254)

Open image viewer

A pupil cannot always see the school nurse when they want to. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

It can be difficult to get an appointment with the school counsellor or the school nurse in lower secondary school.

A new school health survey shows that 7 percent of 8th and 9th graders in comprehensive school could not see the school nurse when they tried to.

It is even harder to get to see the school counsellor. 10 percent of 8th and 9th graders could not see the counsellor when they wanted to.

If a young person does not get help when they want it, they may not seek help again, says Marke Hietanen-Peltola, chief physician at THL.

The queues in school health care are caused, for example, by the corona years. During the pandemic queues formed, and they are still being cleared.

## Children's hobbies

![Davyd Balberov plays football on a pitch with other children.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5470,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-952730627a2cd84742b/1652176277)

HJK football club has noticed that families' finances have tightened. Photo: Kristiina Lehto / Yle

Families' money worries are also visible in sports clubs. Hobby clubs say that many families now have to think about how to pay for their children's hobbies.

For example, the Helsinki football club HJK says that it has a fund from which low-income families can apply for support for their children's hobby fees. Three times as many grants have now been applied for compared with before.

The Helsinki Figure Skating Club says that fewer children are coming to skating schools than before. There are also fewer skaters in many hobby groups than before. At least some have had to stop because of prices.

## Police crackdown

![Close-up of a traffic sign marking a pedestrian crossing. Blue sky in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5536,h_3102,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1095337642c26a57f5d5/1680615386)

This week the police are intensively monitoring traffic at pedestrian crossings. The police are monitoring in particular whether vehicle drivers comply with the pedestrian crossing rules.

The police are also watching how pedestrians, cyclists and electric scooter riders behave in traffic.
