# Saturday 28.3.2020 (radio)

Uusimaa has been isolated from the rest of Finland. Restaurants and cafes will be closed. Health care professionals will have a work obligation. Metsähallitus closed nature sites. Summer time starts on Sunday.

## Uusimaa is now isolated for three weeks

![Police and conscripts at a quiet checkpoint on the Lohja and Salo border on the Ykköstie road at junction 20, on Saturday 28 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11280850)

Police officers and conscripts at a checkpoint on the Lohja and Salo border on the Ykköstie road. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The isolation of Uusimaa has begun. Parliament has approved a law that restricts movement across the Uusimaa border. Police and conscripts are monitoring the Uusimaa border on the main roads and on trains.

You may not leave Uusimaa to go, for example, to a summer cottage in other parts of Finland. The restriction is meant to slow down the spread of the coronavirus.

You may cross the border only if there is a really good reason. You may cross the border if you have to go, for example, to work, to a doctor or home. Conscripts may also cross the border to go home on leave.

The isolation of Uusimaa lasts 3 weeks.

## Restaurants are being closed - many restaurants are already closed

![The dining room of the La Famiglia restaurant in Flamingo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6546145e7205f1568be/1585148426)

Open image viewer

Restaurants may not have customers, so that the coronavirus does not spread. You may sell food to take away. Photo: Eleni Paspatis / Yle

Parliament has approved a law that all restaurants and cafes must be closed because of the coronavirus.

Restaurants may sell food to take away, but customers may not sit in the restaurants. The order does not apply to staff canteens at workplaces. They may continue to operate.

It has been difficult to make a law to close restaurants. The law will come into force perhaps on Monday. Many restaurants have already closed their operations or their customer areas.

## Metsähallitus closed excursion sites

![Day hut.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8752494)

Open image viewer

Day hut. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

Metsähallitus has closed popular excursion sites, because too many people have come to them.

Many people are now visiting nature sites, because all events and hobbies have been cancelled because of the coronavirus.

Metsähallitus has closed, for example, excursion huts and bird towers, so that too many people do not gather in them. The nature centres of the national parks have also been closed.

## Health care professionals have a work obligation

![A nurse operates a breathing machine at the Meilahti tower hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11275156)

Open image viewer

Hospitals may need many workers if many people fall ill with the coronavirus. Photo: Anni Reenpää / Lehtikuva

Parliament has decided on a work obligation in the health care field. This means that doctors and nurses can be ordered to work instead of their other jobs or from retirement.

Health care students, for example medical students or nursing students, can also be ordered to work.

Hospitals may need many workers because of the corona epidemic.

## Summer time starts on Sunday

![Clock being changed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11032026)

Open image viewer

Remember to change your clock to summer time, that is, one hour forward. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Next night, Finland and the rest of Europe switch to summer time. The clock is moved one hour forward during the night between Saturday and Sunday.

In Finland, the switch to summer time officially happens at 3 o'clock in the night. At home you can turn the clock forward already in the evening.
