# Wednesday 16.1.2019 (radio)

The British Parliament rejected the withdrawal agreement. A customs duty may be charged on British goods. The most asylum seekers came to Finland from Iraq. Coal use decreased. Bankruptcies increased. It will snow a lot on Thursday.

## The British Parliament rejected the EU withdrawal agreement

![The British Parliament met on 16 January.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10599762)

Open image viewer

432 MPs voted against the Brexit agreement. Photo: PARLIAMENTARY RECORDING UNIT / EPA

The British Parliament has rejected Britain's EU withdrawal agreement. More than 400 MPs voted against the agreement. A little over 200 MPs supported the agreement.

If there is no agreement, the situation is difficult for the EU and for Britain.

Britain may be able to ask for extra time for the exit.

## Customs duties could raise the prices of British goods

![The flags of Britain and the EU in front of the Parliament building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10596754)

Open image viewer

Britain's EU exit is a complicated matter. Photo: Andy Rain / EPA

Many goods for sale are imported to Finland from Britain. If Britain does not have a withdrawal agreement, customs duties will be charged on the goods. For example, food, medicines and car parts will become more expensive when they are imported to Finland or to other EU countries.

Many British goods may not be bought if the goods become more expensive because of customs duties.

## Most asylum seekers came from Iraq

![Evitskog reception centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9844077)

Open image viewer

About 1,000 deportation decisions were made. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

About 2,500 asylum seekers came to Finland last year.

The most asylum seekers came from Iraq, Russia and Somalia.

About 4,500 asylum applications were submitted. About 1,000 deportation decisions were made. The most deportation decisions were made for Iraqis.

## Use of renewable energy is growing

![The chimney of the Rauhalahti power plant](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8447444)

Open image viewer

Carbon dioxide emissions have decreased as the use of renewable energy increases. Photo: Enja Heikkilä / Yle

The use of coal decreased slightly last year. Coal's share of district heating was 20%. Natural gas's share was 13%. Natural gas's share grew slightly from the previous year.

The share of renewable fuels in heating also grew slightly, to 36%. Carbon dioxide emissions have decreased as the use of renewable energy increases.

## Bankruptcies increased

![BR Toys shop in the Kamppi shopping centre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10582758)

Open image viewer

BR Toys went bankrupt in January. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The number of bankruptcies has increased. Over 2,500 companies went bankrupt last year. The number of bankruptcies grew by 17% from the previous year.

Bankruptcy means that a company runs out of money and its operations end.

## Poor driving conditions in Southern Finland

![Cars in snowfall](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10580609)

Open image viewer

As much as 20 centimetres of snow may fall.  Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

A lot of snow will fall during the night in the southern part of the country. Driving conditions will be really poor in the south on Thursday morning. The snowfall continues during the day. As much as 20 centimetres of snow may fall. In the central part of the country it will be cloudy and dry on Thursday. In Lapland a little snow will fall.
