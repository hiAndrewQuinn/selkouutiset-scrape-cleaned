## Corona pass

![EU corona certificate visible in Omakanta in Vantaa in October 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_296/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8678336169921e09e8b/1634309752)

The corona pass is the EU corona certificate. You can download it from Omakanta. Photo: Timo Jaakonaho / Lehtikuva

Parliament has decided that the corona pass will be introduced in Finland. Wide corona restrictions may not be needed if people have a corona pass.

A corona pass can be required in places where many people come. For example, restaurants, concerts, sports facilities and museums can require a corona pass.

The corona pass is the EU corona certificate, which you get from Omakanta.

## In Italy, corona pass at workplaces

![Police and demonstrators clash in Rome.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3072,h_1728,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8666386166fb364d32c/1634139034)

Open image viewer

Some Italians oppose the corona pass at workplaces. There have been demonstrations against the corona pass in Italy. Photo: Massimo Percossi / EPA

Italy has introduced a corona pass at workplaces. All employees must have a corona pass. That means that an employee must have corona vaccinations or a fresh corona test.

If a person does not have a corona pass at work in Italy, the person can be laid off, that is, they must leave their job.

Italy is the first EU country where you must have a corona pass at work.

## Corona vaccinations and corona patients

![Nurse Evelin Prees treats a corona patient in protective equipment in the intensive care unit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_188/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8669256167ffb84b94c/1661955395)

Open image viewer

A nurse treats a corona patient in the intensive care unit. Photo: Arash Matin / Yle

In Finland there are still many people who have not taken a corona vaccine. 75 per cent of Finns over 12 years old have received full protection, that is, 2 corona vaccinations.

The number of corona patients in hospitals is growing. There are over 190 corona patients in hospitals. Of them, 36 patients are in intensive care.

People who have no corona vaccination are now ending up in hospital. A corona vaccination protects well against serious corona disease.

## Gambling company Veikkaus

![Slot machines in a row in a dim gaming hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3427,h_1928,x_567,y_428/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-86542961643200866ed/1718361974)

Open image viewer

Slot machines have been reduced by about 40 per cent. Photo: Anne Savin / Yle

The gambling company Veikkaus is reducing its employees. Veikkaus says that the work of up to 190 people will end at Veikkaus. The reason is that Veikkaus's operations have been reduced.

Gambling has decreased by about 40 per cent. Game machines in shops have been reduced. Veikkaus is also closing about 15 gaming halls.

Finland has reduced Veikkaus's gambling operations so that gambling harms decrease.
