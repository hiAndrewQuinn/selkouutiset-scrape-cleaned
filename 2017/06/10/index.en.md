# Saturday 10.6.2017 (radio)

Jussi Halla-aho is the new chair of the Finns Party. Timo Soini led the Finns Party for 20 years. Finland's 100th anniversary celebrations have been held in Estonia. Men are not allowed to sit spread out on buses in Madrid.

## Jussi Halla-aho

![Jussi Halla-aho at the Finns Party congress in Jyväskylä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9662371)

The Finns Party has got a new leader. On Saturday the Finns Party chose Jussi Halla-aho as its new chair.

Jussi Halla-aho opposes multiculturalism. He does not want immigrants to come to Finland. Halla-aho hopes that Finland will leave the European Union.

Halla-aho's election brings problems for Finland's government. The Finns Party is in the government together with the National Coalition and the Centre. They disagree with Halla-aho on many matters. The chairs of the government parties will discuss on Monday whether they can continue co-operating.

Jussi Halla-aho now works in Brussels. He is one of Finland's representatives in the European Parliament.

The Finns Party chose a new chair because the former chair, Timo Soini, no longer wanted to continue in the post.

## Timo Soini

![Timo Soini, serving his last day as chair of the Finns Party, gave the opening speech at the Finns Party congress on Saturday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9662085)

Former chair of the Finns Party Timo Soini. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Former chair of the Finns Party Timo Soini is a politician whom every Finn knows.

Soini led the Finns Party for 20 years, and he made the Finns Party a big party. Soini's biggest election victory came in 2011. At that time the Finns Party got more than 30 more MPs in Parliament, that is the Finnish Parliament, than before.

55-year-old Soini is now Finland's Foreign Minister.

## Finland's celebrations in Estonia

![Saara Aalto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9546340)

Open image viewer

Saara Aalto also sang in Tallinn on Saturday. Photo: Sini Liimatainen / Yle

Finland's neighbour Estonia has held Finland's 100th anniversary celebrations. In Estonia there were many different events on Saturday.

The main event was in the Estonian capital, Tallinn. A big concert was held in Tallinn's Freedom Square, with Estonian and Finnish musicians performing.

Finland's President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio also travelled to Tallinn on Saturday and took part in the Finland 100 celebrations.

Finland became an independent country 100 years ago, in 1917.

## Madrid's men on the bus

![A man sitting at a bus stop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9661870)

Open image viewer

Men are not allowed to sit spread out on buses in Madrid. Photo: AOP

Another news item, from Spain. In the Spanish capital, Madrid, men are no longer allowed to sit spread out on a bus. The reason is that a man often takes the seats of 2 people when he sits with his legs wide apart.

Some Madrid men do not like it that men are told how they must sit. However, the city of Madrid plans to ban men's wide sitting position next on the metro too.
