# Thursday 19.11.2020 (radio)

The number of coronavirus infections is growing in Finland. Finland is continuing its entry restrictions. There is good news again about coronavirus vaccines. Storm Liisa raged on Thursday. On Friday the wind will weaken and the weather will get colder.

## The coronavirus situation in Finland

![Director Pasi Pohjola of STM, strategy director Liisa-Maria Voipio-Pulkki and chief physician Taneli Puumalainen of THL at the STM and THL coronavirus situation briefing in Helsinki on 19 November 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11656851)

The number of new coronavirus infections is growing in Finland.

On Thursday, 351 new coronavirus infections were reported. That is more than ever before in one day.

About 20,000 coronavirus infections have been reported in Finland in total. Experts are afraid that the coronavirus situation in Finland may get worse quickly.

There are also more coronavirus infections among older people. That is a bad thing, because the coronavirus is dangerous for older people.

The coronavirus has also spread at workplaces. Experts say that workers must remember to keep safe distances and good hygiene also in break rooms and coffee rooms.

## Entry restrictions

![Passengers departing at Kemi-Tornio airport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5780,h_3251,x_0,y_27/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7376025fa143a7cf85b/1604404551)

Finland is continuing its entry restrictions. The government decided that the restrictions will continue until 13 December.

People cannot come freely to Finland from European countries where there are more coronavirus infections than the allowed limit. The limit is 25 new coronavirus cases per 100,000 people in 2 weeks.

The limit is strict. In practice, it means that travel from the rest of Europe to Finland is cut off. Only commuting traffic from Estonia and Sweden is allowed without quarantine.

Finland's coronavirus situation is the best in Europe. That is why the government is trying to use the restrictions to stop the coronavirus from spreading from abroad to Finland.

## Coronavirus vaccine test results

![The AstraZeneca vaccine is being tested at the mAbxience laboratory in Garin, Argentina.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11613763)

There is good news about coronavirus vaccines again.

The test results of the coronavirus vaccine from Oxford University are good. Oxford University is developing the coronavirus vaccine together with the drug company AstraZeneca.

This is already the third coronavirus vaccine with good test results. Experts estimate that coronavirus vaccines will come into use next year.

## Storm Liisa

![The picture shows a tree knocked down by storm Liisa in Luvia on 19 November.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11656686)

Open image viewer

Storm Liisa has knocked down trees. Photo: Jenni Joensuu / Yle

There was a storm in Finland on Thursday. The storm was named Liisa.

The storm wind has blown hard, and storm Liisa cut the power to thousands of homes.

The storm also damaged the railway in Central Finland. Because of this, part of the train traffic has been cut off, and train passengers have been carried by bus.

There has also been a lot of rain in storm Liisa. In Kokkola, for example, more than 5 centimetres of rain fell in one day. In the north it has snowed.

## Friday's weather

On Friday the weather changes. The wind weakens, and at the same time the weather gets colder.

On Friday during the day there will be snow showers in the south and middle of Finland. On Friday evening the rain stops, and the weather gets colder still.
