# Saturday 15.12.2018 (radio)

Some of Trafi's electronic services have been opened again. The Finnish police have arrested a dangerous criminal. Operation Snowflake has started on the roads. Finns drink a lot of filter coffee.

## Trafi's services have been opened

![New cars at a car dealership in Vantaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10537509)

Trafi's electronic registration services are important for car sales. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

Some of Trafi's electronic services have been opened again. The services have been closed for almost a week.

Trafi is the Finnish Transport Safety Agency. Trafi's electronic service was closed because people's information was not safe there.

Now registration services, for example, which are important for car sales, have been opened again. Some of Trafi's electronic services are still closed.

## Criminal caught

![Photos of wanted persons published by the National Bureau of Investigation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9973825)

Open image viewer

The police are looking for these people. Kellokumpu (top left) has now been caught. Photo: National Bureau of Investigation

The Finnish police have arrested a dangerous criminal. The police caught Jani Kellokumpu in Orimattila, in Southern Finland. Swedish police have also been looking for Kellokumpu.

It is known that Kellokumpu has killed one person. Now he is also suspected of being responsible for the death of one person in Northern Sweden.

The police caught Kellokumpu after a chase. First Kellokumpu fled from the police by car. Then he ran into the forest.

Kellokumpu's arrest succeeded because the Finnish and Swedish police cooperated well.

## Operation Snowflake has begun

![Members of the Automobile Association pose for the camera at the start of Operation Snowflake in Kemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10556513)

Open image viewer

Northern Finland's Operation Snowflake began in Kemi. Photo: Antti Ullakko

Operation Snowflake has begun on the roads. The Automobile Association organises the operation every Christmas.

The idea of the operation is that drivers get help if they have problems with their car during the journey. Volunteer helpers give the help.

Drivers can order help by phone. Drivers may need help if, for example, they run out of petrol in the middle of the journey.

Operation Snowflake continues until 1 January.

## Filter coffee is popular

![The first commercial coffee filter registered by Melitta Bentz in 1908.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7839906)

Open image viewer

This is where the history of filter coffee began in 1908. Photo: Melitta Zentralgesellschaft mbH &amp; Co. KG

Finns drink a lot of filter coffee. 57 percent of Finnish coffee drinkers want only filter coffee.

Coffee expert Karoliina Mäkelä says that filter coffee is easy to make. That is why a lot of filter coffee is drunk.

The coffee machine came into every Finnish kitchen 50 years ago. In a coffee machine, coffee is made with the help of a filter bag.

Filter coffee was already invented 110 years ago in Germany. Filter coffee was invented by Mrs Melitta Bentz.

Bentz wanted to offer good coffee to her neighbour. That is why she put coffee grounds into blotting paper and the blotting paper into a tin can, in the bottom of which she made holes. Then she poured water through the blotting paper.

The idea was so good that Melitta Bentz founded a company in 1908. Even today many Finns make filter coffee with Melitta filter bags.
