# Wednesday 5.2.2020 (radio)

President Sauli Niinistö condemns racism and anti-Semitism. The government plans to change family leave. A coronavirus patient has been allowed out of Lapland Central Hospital. Wednesday was Runeberg Day.

## The President condemned racism

![President Sauli Niinistö spoke at the opening of the parliamentary term in the plenary chamber of Parliament in Helsinki on 5 February 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11193558)

President Sauli Niinistö condemns racism and antisemitism, that is, anti-Jewish hatred.

Niinistö is worried that racist speech can also be heard in Finland. The President says that there is no place for racism and anti-Jewish hatred in Finland.

Niinistö spoke about the matter in Parliament, where he opened the parliamentary term. Parliament officially began this year's work on Wednesday.

## More family leave is coming

![A man carries a baby in his arms in a stairwell.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11185775)

Open image viewer

The government hopes that fathers spend more time with their children. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The government intends to change family leave. The government's goal is that fathers spend more time with their children than they do today.

That is why the government is planning that there will be more family leave. Both parents, that is, the mother and the father, will get about 7 months of family leave.

One of the parents can, however, give 69 days of their own family leave to the other parent. In addition, the person giving birth gets a month of leave before parental leave.

If the plan becomes reality, family leave will change next year or later.

## A coronavirus patient left hospital

![Lapland Central Hospital in Rovaniemi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11183230)

Open image viewer

Lapland Central Hospital in Rovaniemi. Photo: Laura Haapamäki / Lehtikuva

Finland's only coronavirus patient has left hospital.

The patient is a Chinese woman who came on holiday to Finnish Lapland. The woman ended up at Lapland Central Hospital when she caught the coronavirus.

The hospital said on Wednesday that the woman has recovered. She no longer has any symptoms of the coronavirus and she is in good condition. That is why she was allowed to leave hospital.

The woman comes from the Chinese city of Wuhan. The new coronavirus began to spread from Wuhan in December.

About 25,000 people have caught the coronavirus so far. Over 400 people have died because of the coronavirus.

## Runeberg Day was celebrated

![The statue of national writer Johan Ludvig Runeberg in Pietarsaari, where the family lived for years.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6389745e3aae6d8724d/1580905816)

Runeberg Day was celebrated on Wednesday. Runeberg Day is always on 5 February, because it is the birthday of the writer Johan Ludvig Runeberg. Runeberg was born on 5 February 1804.

Johan Ludvig Runeberg is Finland's national poet. For example, the words of the Finnish national anthem are from Runeberg's book The Tales of Ensign Stål. The name of the song in Finnish is Maamme and in Swedish Vårt land. Runeberg wrote in Swedish.

On Wednesday the Runeberg Literature Prize was also awarded. The prize this year went to Ralf Andtbacka. The prize has been awarded every year for over 30 years. The prize is 20,000 euros.
