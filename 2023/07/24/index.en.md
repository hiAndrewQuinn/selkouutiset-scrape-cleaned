Forest fires in Greece. Finnish travel agencies. Criminals' text messages. Weather.

## Forest fires in Greece

![Aerial view of an urban area. Thick dark smoke rises from the horizon.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4996,h_2810,x_0,y_520/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114683364be9b931f306/1690213794)

The forest fires in Greece continue.

New forest fires have also started. For example, several thousand people have been moved to safety from the island of Corfu because of the forest fires.

The worst situation is on the island of Ródos. The fire has been burning land, forest and houses on Ródos for many days. As many as 30,000 people have been moved away from the fire on Ródos.

Fires are now spreading easily in Greece, because the weather is dry and very warm. The temperature has risen to over 40 degrees.

## Finnish travel agencies

![People at an airport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4561,h_2565,x_0,y_233/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114642364bd3960c7e3c/1690122680)

Many Finnish tourists are now coming back from Ródos. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

The forest fires in Greece also affect trips to Greece. Finns take many holiday trips to the Greek islands.

Because of the forest fires, travel agencies are now restricting trips from Finland to Greece. Some travel agencies still fly to Ródos, but do not take tourists close to the forest fires.

Some travel agencies have cancelled flights to Ródos at least until Tuesday. Their planes fly to Ródos empty, so that they can bring tourists back from Ródos to Finland.

Travel agencies are assessing the situation day by day.

## Criminals' text messages

![A fraudulent message sent in Posti's name on a mobile phone screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_946,h_532,x_0,y_142/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114610164baa03d8b891/1715950940)

Open image viewer

This link should not be opened. Photo: Juha-Petri Koponen / Yle

Posti says that its phone number has been misused.

Criminals have sent people text messages asking for a customs fee. The criminals' text messages have come in among Posti's real text messages.

The criminals' text messages contain a link, through which the customer can confirm the customs fee. In this way the criminals try to get people's bank details.

Posti, phone companies and authorities are looking for a way to stop the criminals' text messages.

If a customer gets a text message about a customs fee, they must not open the link before they check the matter with Posti. The customer can call Posti's customer service or go to the Omaposti online service.

If a customer has opened the link and given their online banking credentials through it, they must contact their own bank and the police.
