# Saturday 1.4.2023 (radio)

Parliamentary elections. NATO membership. NATO flag. Spring migration of birds. The weather.

## Parliamentary elections

![Voters in voting booths in a school gym hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690124)

Voters in voting booths. No one may look at who another person is voting for. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

The parliamentary elections are held this weekend. Election day is now Sunday.

On Saturday the candidates have still been meeting people in different parts of Finland. On election day, Sunday, election campaigns are already banned.

Voters can also look for a good candidate in Yle's election compass. It can be found at the web address yle.fi.

Polling places open on Sunday morning at 9, and they close on Sunday evening at 8 p.m.

On election day the voter can vote at the polling place that is written on the voter's notification. The voter's notification has come by post.

In the parliamentary elections 200 MPs are elected. They will decide on Finland's affairs during the next 4 years.

## NATO membership

![Picture from a Turkish-language online newspaper.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690126)

Open image viewer

An announcement of the approval of Finland's NATO membership was published in the official Turkish newspaper. Photo: Tommi Pylkkö / Yle, screenshot from the official Turkish newspaper

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan has confirmed the law by which Turkey approves Finland joining the NATO defence alliance. The Turkish Parliament approved Finland's NATO membership on Thursday.

Turkey was the last NATO country to approve Finland's membership. Now all NATO countries have approved Finland's NATO membership, and Finland's NATO membership is certain.

Finland applied for NATO membership last year, in May. With NATO membership Finland wants to improve its own security. There is war in Europe for the first time in a long time, because Russia has attacked Ukraine.

## Spring migration of birds

![Spring migration of geese.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10159379)

Open image viewer

Migratory birds spend the winter in the south and return to Finland in spring. Photo: Timo Leponiemi / Yle

The spring migration of birds from the south to Finland has slowed because of wintry weather. This is what the bird organisation BirdLife Finland says.

In the spring bird migration there has been a pause of about a week because of snow and frost. Many migratory birds had already reached Finland, but because of the bad weather they turned back towards the south.

BirdLife Finland says that the situation is not rare. Weather often affects the progress of the spring bird migration.

## The weather

The weather on Sunday, election day, is clear. Only in the east may a little snow fall.

The temperature in the afternoon is between +3 and -3 degrees. Because of the north wind, however, the temperature feels colder.

On Monday it is dry, that is, no rain will come. The sun shines across most of the country.

On Monday too the temperature in the daytime is around zero. Nights, however, are cold across the whole country now.
