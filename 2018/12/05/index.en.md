# Wednesday 5.12.2018 (radio)

Police are investigating sex crimes in Oulu. In Finland there have been 2 attacks on buses. An adult from Espoo got measles. The game company Supercell is setting up a coding school in Finland. You can follow the Independence Day events on TV, radio and online.

## Sex crimes

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Police are investigating sex crimes in Oulu, in Northern Finland. There are 3 cases.

What the cases have in common is that the victims are children, girls of lower secondary school age. The possible perpetrators of the sex crimes are men with a foreign background. They came to Finland as asylum seekers or refugees.

## Bus attacks

![A bus driver drives a car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10372325)

Open image viewer

The driver was the target of an attack on bus 2. Photo: Ville Honkonen / Yle

In Finland there have been 2 attacks on buses. One case was in Uurainen and the other in Loimaa.

In both cases a passenger attacked the bus driver in the middle of the journey. The aim was to make the bus leave the road or move into the wrong lane. The driver and the other passengers managed to prevent the accidents.

In both cases the attacker had a foreign background.

## Measles

![An influenza vaccine is injected into an arm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9909372)

Open image viewer

There is a vaccine against measles. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

Measles has been found again in Finland. An adult from Espoo got the illness on a trip abroad in Asia.

The patient is probably not going to infect other people with measles, because almost all people in Espoo have protection against measles from vaccines.

Last week a child got measles in the municipality of Luoto, in Ostrobothnia. In Luoto people have feared that measles will spread, because many residents do not have vaccine protection.

In Luoto only 75 percent of residents have got vaccines against various diseases, because many families do not want vaccines for their children. Elsewhere in Finland 95 percent of residents have got vaccines.

Finland is now considering how to make sure that all families take the vaccines. Minister Annika Saarikko has set up a working group to think about the matter.

## Coding school

![Code on a computer screen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10533695)

Open image viewer

More coders are needed. Photo: Kare Lehtonen / Yle

The game company Supercell is setting up a coding school in Finland, where programming is taught.

The coding school lasts 3 years. The school has no timetable. The pupils build their own programs.

There is already a similar school in Paris and in Silicon Valley, in the USA.

## Independence Day

![Jussi-Pekka Rantanen, Ella Kanninen and Sami Sykkö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10542172)

Independence Day is celebrated on Thursday.

The festive church service is at Helsinki Cathedral, and the army's Independence Day parade is in Mikkeli. You can watch both events on TV1 and Areena.

The biggest Independence Day party is in the evening at the Presidential Palace. You can follow the Palace party on TV1, Areena and Radio Suomi.
