## Bow killings in Norway

![Police on a street in Norway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4280,h_2408,x_840,y_704/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-86670961679198141c3/1634194654)

Police on a street in Norway. Photo: AFP / Lehtikuva

Norwegian police are investigating a serious act of violence. A Danish man shot several people with a bow on Wednesday in Norway.

5 people died and 2 were injured when the man shot them on a street in the city of Kongsberg. Norwegian police arrested the man. Norwegian police say that the archer is a person known to the police.

It is not yet known why the Danish man shot people on the streets of the Norwegian city of Kongsberg.

## Cuts in the culture sector

![Antti Kurvinen and Sanna Marin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12143702)

Open image viewer

Antti Kurvinen and Sanna Marin. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle and Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

There has been a quarrel inside the government about grants for the culture sector. The Ministry of Education said on Tuesday that grants for the culture sector are being cut, that is, reduced by 43 million euros. The grants are being reduced for culture, art, science and youth work.

Prime Minister and SDP chairperson Sanna Marin says that the cuts to the culture sector must be cancelled. Marin says that the culture minister, Antti Kurvinen of the Centre Party, told about the cuts too early. Marin says the matter is still open in the government.

The Minister of the Interior and chairperson of the Greens, Maria Ohisalo, also says that grants must not be cut from the culture sector.

## Third corona vaccination

![Corona vaccination sign near the Ratina vaccination point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2857,h_1607,x_0,y_147/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8593476152f3a8329ab/1632826344)

Open image viewer

Corona vaccinations are now easily available. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

In Finland there is discussion about a third (3rd) corona vaccination. The Institute for Health and Welfare THL said on Thursday that information on the need for a third corona vaccination will be available next week.

THL also said that the effectiveness of two (2) corona vaccines has not decreased in Finland. The vaccine still protects well against serious corona disease.

THL says that it is important to take corona vaccinations. Without corona vaccinations a person will almost certainly catch corona now or later.

## President Niinistö's exposure

![President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö speaks into a microphone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8455546124da9380e5e/1630050356)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

President Sauli Niinistö has been exposed to the corona virus, that is, he has been close to a person who has a corona infection.

Niinistö met the President of Latvia on Wednesday, who was later found to have a corona infection. President Niinistö remains in voluntary quarantine.

Niinistö was to meet the new President of Estonia, Alar Karis, on Thursday, but the visit was cancelled, because Niinistö has been exposed to the corona virus.
