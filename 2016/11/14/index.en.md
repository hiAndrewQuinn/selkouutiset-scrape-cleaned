# Monday 14.11.2016 (radio)

The EU is waiting for Donald Trump's foreign policy lines. Many law students do work without pay. Supermoon. Birds nesting.

## The EU discussed the United States

![Flag of the European Union.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8385719)

Open image viewer

The EU is waiting to see what kind of new foreign policy lines will come from the United States when the new president takes office. Photo: Frederik von Erichsen / EPA

The foreign ministers of the European Union have discussed in Brussels the victory of Donald Trump as the new President of the United States. The message was that the principles of the EU do not change because power changes hands in the United States.

Nothing is yet known about Trump's foreign policy. In his campaign speeches, for example, Trump said that the United States may not support its NATO allies without conditions.

Trump has said that he wants good relations with Russia. The EU says that this does not affect the EU's sanctions against Russia. The EU, for example, bans the financing of Russia's major banks.

## Law students do work for free

![Finnish law books side by side](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8956175)

Open image viewer

Many law firms have students working for free. Photo: Nina Keski-Korpela / Yle

Many law offices have taken on law students to work without pay.

Almost a hundred students answered the survey of the Finnish Bar Association. Half of them had done unpaid work or had been offered it. The work has been full-time, independent and demanding.

Ira Hietanen made a report for the Finnish Bar Association on students' work. The report says that unpaid work is mainly offered by private law firms outside the capital area.

The Finnish Bar Association condemns unpaid work, which is often called an internship.

## A supermoon is a rare phenomenon

![Graphic of the supermoon.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9272783)

Open image viewer

A supermoon is a rare phenomenon. You have to wait for years for the next supermoon. Photo: Mitja Sirola / Prisma / Yle

The Moon is now bigger than it has been in 68 years. The Moon is now what is called a supermoon. The Moon looks about 14 per cent bigger and 30 per cent brighter than at its smallest.

Unfortunately, most of Finland is very cloudy, and so the Moon cannot be seen. In the west and south-west of Finland, and in some places from Ostrobothnia to Kainuu, the cloud cover may break up.

The supermoon phenomenon happens when the Earth, the Moon and the Sun are in the same line and the Moon is closest to the Earth at full moon.

The last time the full moon was this close was in 1948. The next time for a supermoon is in November 2034.

## Birds have brought nesting forward

![Nuthatch and great tit](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8807433)

Open image viewer

There has been a significant change in the nesting times of many birds. Photo: Kirsti Metsävainio, Aavasaksa

Common bird species in Finland nest earlier than before. Especially the great tits and treecreepers that spend the winter in Finland nest early. Many species that spend the winter near Finland also nest early. Such species include the blackbird and the fieldfare.

The researcher says that the change in birds' behaviour is already significant. Now more information is needed on how the start time of nesting affects the nesting result.

Birds have brought forward the start of nesting, because the average spring temperatures have risen.
