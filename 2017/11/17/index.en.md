# Friday 17.11.2017 (radio)

A meteor in Lapland. The Western Metro is setting off. Nurses face violence. Many job vacancies. Finnair is going to court. Isis has been driven out of Iraqi cities.

## Light ball in the Lapland sky

![Light phenomenon in the sky, filmed in Lohtaja.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9935400)

Open image viewer

Photo: Johannes Karhula

A bright light appeared in the Lapland sky on Thursday. It was probably a meteor, a shooting star. It was like a fireball.

The meteor flew through the atmosphere at a height of over 100 kilometres. Bangs could be heard during the light phenomenon.

The light may also have been some device, for example a part of a satellite. The light could be seen from far away. There is a video of the light phenomenon on the Yle News website.

## The Western Metro begins

![Metro trains at Matinkylä station in Espoo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9925614)

Open image viewer

The Western Metro at Matinkylä station in Espoo. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The Western Metro is setting off on Saturday. It runs from Ruoholahti in Helsinki to Kivenlahti in Espoo. Yle is taking part in the historic opening of the Western Metro.

You can watch the first journey on the Western Metro on Yle Areena on Saturday morning. Yle will follow the celebrations online, on television and on the radio during the morning.

## Nurses are bitten and hit

src="https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5732818"

Open image viewer

Nurses face violence. Photo: YLE

Last year, 80 percent of elderly care nurses faced violence. This is according to a new survey. More than 2000 employees and supervisors responded to the survey.

Nurses are, for example, bitten, hit and spat on. An expert says that violence is linked, for example, to memory illnesses.

## Industry needs more workers

![A welder at work.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9878834)

Open image viewer

A welder at work. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / AOP

There are more job vacancies than before. In the summer and autumn, over 34 000 jobs were open. The number is 12 percent higher than a year ago.

Industry and mining need more workers. However, companies say that it is hard to find workers for every second job. There are many such jobs in construction.

## Finnair's compensation may be too small

![Finnair logo on the tail of an aircraft.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9845256)

Open image viewer

Finnair is going to court. Photo: Russell Hart / AOP

The airline Finnair is going to court. This is because Finnair has problems paying compensation.

Finnair is accused of paying too little compensation, and customers' rights have suffered.

Finnair pays compensation when a flight is late or a flight is cancelled because of a technical fault. According to the accusations, Finnair pays too little compensation. Finnair disagrees.

People complained about Finnair in 2016 as much as 70 percent more than in 2015.

## Isis lost the city of Rawa

![Iraqi army soldiers were briefed at the Habaniya base in Anbar province, Iraq, on 25 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9936087)

Open image viewer

Iraqi army soldiers. Photo: STR / AFP

The Iraqi army says that it has taken the city of Rawa from the terrorist group Isis. Rawa is on the border between Syria and Iraq. It was the last city that Isis held in Iraq.

The Iraqi army also says that its troops "fully liberated Rawa and raised the Iraqi flag over its buildings".
