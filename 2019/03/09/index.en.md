## Prime Minister Juha Sipilä admitted that sote was too big a package

![Juha Sipilä on Ykkösaamu, 09.03.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10681188)

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä admits that too big a reform was attempted all at once with the sote reform. Sote is the reform of social services and health services.

The government resigned on Friday because the sote reform failed. The government is now a caretaker government. It has the same ministers as before, but it cannot make political decisions.

In the morning, Sipilä was interviewed on Yle TV1's Ykkösaamu. Sipilä said that he may no longer stand for the leadership of the Centre Party. Whether he stands depends on the election result.

The support for the Centre Party in Yle's poll was only 14.1 percent.

## Immigration divides Finns

![Customer preparing food](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10639118)

Open image viewer

The Local Government Development Foundation asked for opinions on immigration in February. Photo: Juha Kivioja / Yle

Attitudes to immigration divide people in Finland. The Local Government Development Foundation asked for opinions on immigration.

47 percent of respondents had a positive attitude to immigration. 41 percent had a negative attitude. The majority wanted to reduce immigration from the Middle East and Africa.

Attitudes to immigration are most positive in the Helsinki region. Supporters of the Greens, the Left Alliance and the Centre Party also have more positive attitudes than others.

The most negative attitudes are among Finns Party supporters. 96 percent of Finns Party supporters opposed immigration.

## There may be new observations about North Korea's missiles

![South Koreans watching television at a railway station in Seoul on 7 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10681199)

Open image viewer

North Korea may be preparing to launch a missile or a satellite.

The preparations are visible in satellite images. For example, the British broadcaster BBC and the news channel CNN report on the satellite images. Activity has been observed at Sanumdong, where most of North Korea's ballistic missiles are.

Earlier this week it was reported that North Korea has started repair work on the missile launch pad.

## Barbie is 60 years old

![The iconic Barbie doll celebrates her 60th birthday on 9.3.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10681068)

Open image viewer

Barbie is still popular, although she has also been criticised a lot. Photo: Jason Szenes/EPA

The Barbie doll celebrated her 60th birthday on Saturday.

About 58 million Barbie dolls are sold every year. The dolls are sold in 150 countries. More than a billion Barbie dolls have been sold in total.

The doll came onto the market at the New York Toy Fair in 1959.

Before the Barbie doll, only baby dolls were on offer. Barbie's inventor, Ruth Handler, wanted to make a different kind of doll. She invented a doll that gives little girls roles other than that of a baby doll's mother.
