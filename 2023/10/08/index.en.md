# Radio | Clear News | Sunday 8.10.2023

Fire in Vantaa. Fighting in Israel. Earthquake in Afghanistan. The weather.

## Fire in Vantaa

![Police are investigating the fire site in Vantaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_321/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1182945652266f874e69/1696753510)

There has been a bad fire in Vantaa. The fire started in a flat in an apartment block in Hakunila, Vantaa, during the night between Saturday and Sunday.

Five people died in the fire. They were from the same family.

The mother of the family and 4 children died in the fire. The children were under 10 years old. The father of the family survived.

The police say that the fire may have started in the sauna. There was extra stuff in the sauna, which may have caught fire.

The authorities are investigating the fire. The police estimate that no one has committed a crime.

The fire has shocked people in Vantaa. The city of Vantaa is offering crisis help to people who want to talk about the event.

In Finland it is rare for so many people to die in a fire.

## Fighting in Israel

![A member of Israel's rescue services is putting out a car fire in Ashkelon, Israel.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5148,h_2895,x_0,y_184/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-118278965213bef83f04/1696705726)

The fighting between Israel and the Palestinian militant group Hamas has continued.

Hamas attacked Israel on Saturday. It fired rockets from the Gaza Strip into Israel. Israel has since fired rockets at Gaza.

Many people have died in the rocket attacks. There are several hundred dead in both Israel and Gaza. There are many thousands injured.

Hamas is a Palestinian militant group. It now controls the Gaza Strip on the Mediterranean coast, on the border between Israel and Egypt.

Israelis and Palestinians live in the same areas. They have long argued about who the areas belong to.

Religion is also part of the dispute. Most Israelis are Jewish and Palestinians are usually Muslim.

Hamas is an Islamist militant group that fights against the Jewish state of Israel. For example, the European Union and the USA say that Hamas is a terrorist organisation.

## Earthquake in Afghanistan

![Children sleeping outside in Afghanistan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5108,h_2873,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-118291465223cd15891a/1696742704)

Many people have died in the earthquakes in Afghanistan. More than 2000 are dead.

More dead may still be found in the ruins of the houses. There are also thousands injured.

There were several earthquakes in western Afghanistan on Saturday. The strongest earthquake was in the Herat area.

## The weather

![Monday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11829996522c7f968749/1696778259)

Over the weekend, snow has covered the ground in different parts of northern and eastern Finland.

Up to 27 centimetres of snow was measured in Pelkosenniemi in Lapland.

At the start of the week the weather is cold. In the south the temperature does not rise above 10 degrees and in the north it stays close to zero.

At night the temperature falls below freezing in a large part of the country.

On Wednesday new rain will come to Finland and more snow will fall in Lapland.
