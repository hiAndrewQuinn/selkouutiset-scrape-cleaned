## Violent incident at an elderly care unit

![Kurjenmäkikoti 2](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5032,h_2831,x_0,y_262/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8513736138a05beb719/1631101047)

A violent incident has happened at an elderly care unit in Turku. The violent incident happened on Tuesday evening at the Kurjenmäki hospital area.

1 person died in the case and 1 person received hospital treatment. They were residents of the elderly care unit.

The perpetrator of the violence is also probably a resident of the elderly care unit. He or she has been moved elsewhere.

The police are investigating the case.

## Winter flu season

![A young person sneezes into a disposable tissue.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_287/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6548535e7238b70fe75/1584590708)

The flu season is approaching. People usually get the flu in winter. Symptoms of influenza include, for example, high fever, cough and runny nose.

This winter the flu season may be severe. Last winter, corona restrictions also prevented influenza. If the corona restrictions end soon, influenza can spread again.

The best way to prevent influenza is vaccination. Influenza vaccinations usually start in November.

## Yle Kielikoulu

![Screenshot of the Yle Kielikoulu website.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12090665)

Open image viewer

Photo: Screenshot of the Yle Kielikoulu website

Yle Kielikoulu is being renewed. New support languages have come to Kielikoulu.

At Yle Kielikoulu you learn Finnish or Swedish with the help of TV programmes. The programmes are watched with subtitles.

Finnish-language programmes have Finnish subtitles, and Swedish-language programmes have Swedish subtitles.

If a subtitle has a difficult word, the viewer can get a translation for the word. The viewer can choose for themselves which language the translation is in.

Finnish-language subtitles can be translated into 13 support languages. New support languages are now included. They are Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai and Polish.

Swedish-language subtitles can be translated into 25 support languages.

Information about Yle Kielikoulu is at yle.fi.
