# Thursday 11.8.2022

Finland has banned about 30 Russian aircraft from entering Finland. The fire in Porvoo has been put out. Fewer moose are hunted than a year earlier. The weather is warm.

## Russian aircraft

Finland has banned a total of about 30 Russian aircraft from entering Finland. The reason is that the EU banned all Russian aircraft from entering the EU area, when Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

Finland has banned flights, for example, because the aircraft has been owned by a Russian person. There may also have been a Russian passenger on the plane who is on the EU sanctions list. Finland also bans a flight from entering Finland if the flight is ordered by a Russian person.

## The fire in Porvoo

![Old Porvoo after the fire, charred planks on the street, roofs gone from the buildings.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_279/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99280762f4bda3c342f/1660206656)

Old Porvoo after the fire, charred planks on the street, roofs gone from the buildings. Photo: Reeta Crémin / Yle

A fire broke out in Old Porvoo on Wednesday evening. The fire started in a restaurant. Nobody was injured in the fire.

The fire has now been put out. The building was badly damaged in the fire. It is not yet known why the fire broke out.

Old Porvoo is the oldest district of the town of Porvoo. In Old Porvoo there are many old wooden houses.

## Moose hunting

![Moose at the Ranua wildlife park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3968,h_2232,x_0,y_696/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98936662e8f3b6bd7b2/1697699629)

Open image viewer

Moose at the Ranua wildlife park. Illustrative image. Photo: Marjukka Talvitie / Yle

Hunters may shoot fewer moose than before.

In the autumn and winter hunting, a total of about 36,600 moose may be shot. In last autumn's hunting about 42,000 moose were caught.

The Finnish Wildlife Agency is reducing moose hunting in many places, because there are fewer moose than last year.

Moose hunting starts in October in a large part of Finland.

There is more blue-green algae in the sea and lakes now than before. There is more blue-green algae than before, because the weather has been warm.

There is more blue-green algae in the lakes than usual at this time of year.

There is also more blue-green algae in the sea than last week. On the coast, however, blue-green algae has been seen a lot only near Rauma. In the open sea there is a lot of blue-green algae in some places.

On Friday the weather is sunny in almost the whole country. In the morning a little rain falls in Central Finland.

On Friday in Southern Finland there is a heatwave. In some places it is almost 30 degrees warm. Elsewhere in the country it is not quite as warm. In Northern Finland the temperature is between 13 and 19 degrees.
