## Situation in Sudan

![People at an airport. A cargo aircraft is visible in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_415/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11031206445f01d69dfe/1682305155)

The situation in Sudan remains unsettled. 10 Finns have been evacuated from Sudan, that is, brought to safety.

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says that 10 Finns have been brought outside Sudan. Children are among them. Haavisto says that there are still at most 5 Finns in Sudan.

EU countries have brought more than 1,000 citizens of EU countries out of Sudan. In Sudan the RSF forces have been fighting against Sudan's army for over a week.

## Shortage of early childhood education professionals

![Touhula day-care centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_107/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1086668641454a7c9270/1679054095)

Open image viewer

Big cities need many trained workers for early childhood education. Photo: Kamal Afarin / Yle

The biggest cities in Finland need thousands of workers for day-care centres in the coming years.

Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Turku, Tampere and Oulu have published a joint press release on the matter. The cities are worried about the sufficiency of the workforce. The cities need over 9,000 university-educated professionals for early childhood education by 2030.

The cities cannot comply with the Early Childhood Education Act if they do not get thousands of new workers. The new law says that by 2030, 75 percent of day-care centre workers must have a higher education degree in the field.

## Foreign tourists

![Skiers on the slopes of Yllästunturi on the side of Ylläsjärvi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5470,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-110308664455372d0876/1682265369)

Tourism is busy in Lapland. Photo: Antti Ullakko / Yle

Fewer foreign tourists come to Finland than before. The number of tourists has not returned to the old level after corona.

Last year foreigners made 4.1 million trips to Finland. That is only about 50 percent of the number of tourists in 2019. That is why less money came to Finland from tourists than before.

Tourists brought about 1.7 billion euros to Finland. In 2019 tourists brought almost 3 billion euros to Finland. Only in Pirkanmaa and Lapland has the number of foreign tourists risen.
