# Sunday 2.2.2020 (radio)

The government will not step into negotiations between employers and employees. Three Finns have got out of Wuhan in China. Many elderly people get a brain injury when they fall. Seven Brothers has turned 150 years old.

## The Prime Minister's interview hour

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin before the broadcast of Yle's Prime Minister's interview hour at the Prime Minister's official residence in Kesäranta in Helsinki on 2 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11188962)

Sanna Marin on the radio programme Prime Minister's interview hour. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The government will not step into negotiations between employers and employees. This is what Prime Minister Sanna Marin says.

New collective agreements are being negotiated in many sectors now. A collective agreement decides, for example, on wages.

Prime Minister Marin understands that nurses, for example, want more pay. However, the government does not want to influence the negotiations, Marin says.

Sanna Marin answered journalists' questions on the radio programme Prime Minister's interview hour. Marin was on the programme for the first time now. Marin became prime minister in December.

The Prime Minister's interview hour is broadcast on the radio about once a month. The first Prime Minister's interview hour was broadcast as early as 1962.

## Finns out of Wuhan

![The Finns who were helped out of Wuhan are believed to have been on this plane that arrived at the Istres air base on Sunday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11189018)

Open image viewer

This aeroplane brought citizens of various countries from Wuhan to France on Sunday. Photo: Clement Mahoudeau / AFP

Three Finns have got out of Wuhan in China. They were on the flight from Wuhan to France.

There were citizens of 30 countries on the flight. The Finnish authorities helped the Finns to get on the flight.

Citizens of different countries are now being brought out of Wuhan because of the coronavirus. The new and dangerous coronavirus began to spread from Wuhan in December.

## Elderly people's falls

![An elderly woman at a gym.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11164579)

Open image viewer

Good fitness prevents elderly people from falling. Photo: Vicky Kasala / AOP

Many elderly people get a brain injury when they fall. This is what a new study shows.

In 2017 about 2,600 elderly people were treated in hospital for a brain injury after a fall. People aged at least 80 were included in the count.

Elderly people's brain injuries are increasing because the number of elderly people is growing. Exercise is the best way to prevent elderly people's falls, the researchers say.

## Seven Brothers turns 150

![Still from the film Seven Brothers from 1939.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3072,h_1728,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6154315ddbc518cdcef/1574683984)

Open image viewer

Seven Brothers has also been made into a film.

Aleksis Kivi's book Seven Brothers turned 150 years old on Sunday. Seven Brothers was published in 1870. It is the first important novel in Finnish.

Seven Brothers is a dear book to Finns. The brothers fight and drink, and they find it hard to learn to read. The book has a strength that Finns like today.

That was not so 150 years ago. Then Seven Brothers received a lot of criticism. Many people thought that a good writer writes about the Finnish people beautifully.

Later Finns have understood how fine a book Seven Brothers is. Today Aleksis Kivi is Finland's national writer.
