## Assistants for severely disabled people

![Eveliina, who has an intellectual disability, sits in a wheelchair in front of an easel, with her mother Sari in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5789,h_3256,x_0,y_332/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-920001621795e977735/1645713864)

Eveliina Huhta-aho needs an assistant. Huhta-aho enjoys painting. Her mother Sari Jalava assists Eveliina Huhta-aho. Photo: Terhi Liimu / Yle

Municipalities are pressuring disabled people and their relatives to become employers of assistants. This is what Yle's MOT programme's study shows. The law says that municipalities must arrange an assistant for severely disabled people.

Municipalities have started to favour the practice in which a disabled person hires an assistant themselves. In that case, the disabled person is the employer. Responsibility for paying wages and for workplace safety passes from the municipality to the disabled person who needs an assistant.

Senior legal counsellor of the Parliamentary Ombudsman **Tapio Räty** says that the situation is problematic. The purpose of the law is not that a disabled person must be the employer of an assistant.

## Bombing of Lviv

![The location of the city of Lviv in Ukraine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12408444)

Open image viewer

Lviv is close to the Polish border. Photo: Rea Lemström / Yle

Russia has fired missiles at the city of Lviv in western Ukraine.

Ukrainian authorities say that at least 7 people were killed and 11 injured in the Russian missile strikes. Ukrainian authorities say that 4 missiles hit the Lviv region.

Lviv is close to the Polish border. Russia has not previously struck Lviv much. The city has mostly been out of the way of bombing.

## Bombing of Kyiv and Kharkiv

In recent days Russia has increased strikes on the Kyiv region, in the capital of Ukraine.

Russia has also shelled the city of Kharkiv heavily with artillery, for example.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy says that in 4 days, 18 people have died in Kharkiv and more than 100 have been injured due to Russian shelling.

## Peace event in Senate Square

![A sea of people holds up a huge Ukrainian flag in Senate Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_128/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-943150625d408602926/1650278602)

Open image viewer

People held up a large Ukrainian flag in Senate Square. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

An event for peace in Ukraine and the world was held on Monday in Helsinki's Senate Square. The event began with the ringing of peace bells.

The event included an ecumenical prayer and a demonstration in support of Ukraine. Musician Maija Vilkkumaa performed at the event. Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto gave a speech.

Thousands of people gathered in Helsinki's Senate Square to support Ukraine.

## Riots in Sweden

![Burning cars on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4406,h_2478,x_0,y_222/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-943132625d29c6390a6/1650272773)

Open image viewer

Rioters have set fire to cars, for example. Photo: Lehtikuva

Violent riots have continued in Sweden. On Monday night, rioters set fire to cars and a school in Malmö, for example. Rioters also threw stones at police.

Riots have taken place in various parts of Sweden on many days already. In the riots, 26 police officers and 14 other people have been injured. About 20 police cars have been damaged.

The Swedish police say that the situation is serious.
