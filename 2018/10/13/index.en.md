# Saturday 13.10.2018 (radio)

3 people died in a car accident in Northern Finland. The Green League chair's sick leave continues. Hunting moose has started across Finland. Lynx hunting will be reduced.

## Traffic accident in Kalajoki

![A passenger car on its roof in a ditch.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10455587)

A serious car accident has happened in Northern Finland. 3 people died and 1 person was injured in the accident. Everyone was young.

The accident happened in Kalajoki in North Ostrobothnia. The car drove off the road during the night between Friday and Saturday.

The police say that the driver may have caused the accident. The driver was driving too fast. The speed of the car was at least 150 kilometres per hour.

The police are continuing to investigate the accident.

## Aalto's sick leave

![Green League chair Touko Aalto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10455593)

Open image viewer

Green League chair Touko Aalto. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

The sick leave of the Green League chair continues. On Saturday the party said that the chair's sick leave will continue until mid-November.

Chair Touko Aalto went on sick leave in September. Aalto then said on Facebook that he cannot keep going with heavy work without a break. Aalto hoped that coping at work and burnout would be talked about openly in Finland.

## Moose hunting

![A moose killed on the ground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10455600)

Open image viewer

Moose hunting has started again. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Moose hunting started this weekend across Finland. The authorities have given almost 55,000 hunting permits. With one permit you may shoot 1 adult moose or 2 young moose.

Moose are hunted, for example, for road safety. In Finland there are over 1,800 moose collisions a year. Moose collisions are dangerous, because a moose can weigh up to 700 kilos.

The number of moose is also growing near cities. That is why hunters also move in city forests.

However, hunting must not cause danger to people. The leader of the hunt must make sure that the hunt goes safely.

## Lynx hunting

![Two lynxes in a snowy forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5303369)

The authorities have also granted permits for lynx hunting this winter.

Lynx hunting will be reduced, because there are fewer lynxes than before. There are now about 2,000 lynxes in Finland.

The authorities have given permission to shoot 205 lynxes outside the reindeer herding area. Last year the authorities gave permission to shoot 404 lynxes.

The authorities do not set exactly how many lynxes hunters may shoot in the reindeer herding area in the north. The reason is that lynxes eat reindeer.

The lynx is the only big cat in Finland's nature. A lynx can weigh over 20 kilos. In the past people hunted lynxes, because the lynx's fur was valuable.
