# Wednesday 11.10.2017 (radio)

Floods continue in Southern Finland. Spain demands an explanation from Catalonia about the declaration of independence. UN: Myanmar is preventing the return of the Rohingya population. Girls took over leadership roles around the world today. Niklas Herlin has died.

## Floods worsen in Southern Finland

![Traffic jams and flood water on the Turku motorway.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9877155)

Open image viewer

Flood water caused traffic jams on the Turku highway. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

In Southern Finland the floods have worsened during Wednesday.

There have been serious traffic jams on the Turku highway, because a lot of water has risen onto the road near Espoo.

The Turku highway is one of Finland's busiest motorways. A lot of water has also risen onto the road in Länsi-Vantaa and in Sipoo.

In Riihimäki, water from the river has flooded into the cellars of apartment blocks.

The floods may continue until the weekend.

## Spain demands answers from Catalonia

![Spanish Prime Minister Mariano Rajoy](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9877105)

Open image viewer

The Spanish government held an emergency meeting early on Wednesday under Prime Minister Mariano Rajoy. Photo: Cesar P. Sendra / EPA

The Spanish prime minister still wants to hear whether Catalonia has declared itself independent.

The Spanish prime minister says that he has asked the regional government of Catalonia about the matter.

The Catalan regional government announced yesterday that it is postponing the declaration of independence and wants to negotiate with the Spanish government first.

Spain intends to take countermeasures if Catalonia declares itself independent.

Catalans voted in the referendum more than a week ago that they want Catalonia to be independent. However, only 40 percent of Catalans took part in the vote.

## UN: Myanmar is trying to prevent the return of the Rohingya population

![Sleeping Rohingya refugees.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9877698)

Open image viewer

Hundreds of thousands of Rohingya have fled the atrocities of the Myanmar army. Photo: Abir Abdullah / EPA-EFE

Myanmar is trying to drive Rohingya Muslims permanently out of the country, says the UN.

The UN says that the Myanmar army has systematically terrorised the Rohingya population. The Rohingya have said that soldiers have carried out, among other things, mass killings.

More than half a million Rohingya have fled to Bangladesh since August.

According to the UN, the Myanmar army has laid mines on the Bangladesh border, so that the Rohingya cannot get back home, to Rakhine province.

## On Girls' Day, girls took over

![Girls fanning themselves on Girl Takeover Day](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9872135)

Open image viewer

Girls around the world showed today that they are good leaders. Photo: Plan International Suomi

Today Girls' Day has been celebrated in different parts of the world.

The children's rights organisation Plan has organised the Girl Takeover event on Girls' Day, in which girls have held leading positions in society.

![Women make up 6.2% of the chief executives of the world's 500 most influential companies.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9870025)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

In Finland, girls have been leaders, for example, at the game company Supercell and in the SM-liiga ice hockey league. Also today a young girl has been deputising for Prime Minister Juha Sipilä.

The aim of the event is to remind people that many girls are discriminated against around the world. At the same time, the aim is also to highlight the strength and possibilities of girls.

## Niklas Herlin, from a powerful family, has died

![Niklas Herlin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9877113)

Open image viewer

Niklas Herlin made a long career in Finnish media. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Book publisher, journalist and non-fiction writer Niklas Herlin has died.

Herlin died suddenly at the age of 53.

Herlin was one of Finland's richest men, because he belonged to the family that owns the lift company Kone.
