## Technology industry pay dispute

![Riku Aalto, Industrial Union.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4117,h_2316,x_0,y_212/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6274905e0deb8dc0664/1577971307)

Chair of the employee organisation the Industrial Union, Riku Aalto. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

No agreement has been reached on pay rises in the technology industry.

The technology industry includes many sectors, for example the electronics and electrical industry, metal products and information technology.

About a 6 percent pay rise over 2 years was proposed for the technology industry. The employers were ready to accept the proposal, but the Industrial Union, the employee organisation, did not accept it. That is why the first strike in the technology industry may begin as early as next week.

## Pandas at Ähtäri zoo

![A panda bear in a tree.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688358)

Open image viewer

Pandas thrive well in Finnish weather. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The future of the pandas at Ähtäri zoo is uncertain.

The zoo intends to send the pandas back to China. The reason is that the zoo does not have enough money to care for the pandas.

The Finnish state planned 5 million euros of aid for the pandas at Ähtäri zoo, but the plan was abandoned.

China rented the pandas to Finland in 2017, in the year of Finland's centenary of independence. There are still 10 years of the rental period left.

Ähtäri zoo will decide within a month what it will do with the pandas.

## Petrol price

![Fuel is pouring from a petrol pump nozzle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8222815)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yahya Arhab / EPA

The price of petrol has risen again. At many petrol stations a litre of petrol again costs over 2 euros.

In December the litre price of petrol fell below 2 euros. Otherwise petrol has cost at least 2 euros during the Ukraine war.

The war in Ukraine began last February, when Russia attacked Ukraine.

![Electric car charging at a car dealership.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5172,h_2909,x_24,y_216/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9440386260226722608/1650469288)

The number of electric cars in Finland has grown. Last year the number of electric cars doubled.

At the end of the year there were 45,000 electric cars on the road. A year earlier there were 23,000 electric cars.

## Health centres' phone service

![Eksote's phone service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7829676048b300dc7da/1615377901)

Open image viewer

Photo: Kare Lehtonen / Yle

There is congestion in the phone services of health centres in Uusimaa. Customers may have to wait for a callback for as long as several weeks.

In West Uusimaa, for example in Espoo, there were as many as 4,000 customers in the callback queue in January. There are almost 3,000 customers in the queue in Central Uusimaa.

The situation is very bad, because the law says that customers must be able to get in touch with a health centre within one day.

## The weekend weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688359)

Open image viewer

The weather at the weekend is very variable.

On Saturday snowfalls move across the whole country. Snow accumulates 1–5 centimetres.

On the coast the temperature is at zero and the rain may be sleet. Otherwise there is a little frost.

On Sunday the rain clears and the weather is fair and sunny.

On Monday new snow and sleet falls on Finland.
