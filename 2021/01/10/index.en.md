## Finns and the corona vaccine

![A corona vaccine bottle in rubber-gloved hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3543,h_1993,x_0,y_78/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7602515ff83a7d26c8c/1610715577)

Open image viewer

Across the whole world 71 percent of people are fairly sure they will take the corona vaccine. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

An international survey shows that Finns have a positive attitude towards the corona vaccine.

81 percent of Finns say that they will certainly or possibly take the corona vaccine.

People from 32 countries were asked whether they intend to take the corona vaccine.

People in the big Asian countries are very keen to take the vaccine. In Vietnam, China and India more than 90 percent of people are ready to take the corona vaccine.

The survey found that in France, Germany, Serbia and Croatia there are the most people who may not take the corona vaccine.

Worldwide, 71 percent intend to take the corona vaccine for certain or possibly.

## Corona vaccinations in Finland

![Minna Bonn giving a corona vaccine to Teemu Pöytäkangas. The vaccination event interested the media. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3330,h_1873,x_0,y_172/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7590855ff323d45174b/1609770592)

Open image viewer

About 11,000 vaccines have been given in Finland. Photo: Roy Fodge / Yle

Corona vaccinations in Finland are going more slowly than health authorities estimated in advance.

About 11,000 vaccines have been given in Finland.

Vaccinating is slow, because there is only 1 vaccine that Finland can use. A second vaccine may soon be available for use.

But the health authorities say that vaccinating will still continue slowly.

## Indonesian plane crash

![Rescue workers examine plane parts found in the sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11730365)

Open image viewer

Rescuers are examining parts that have been found in the sea. They are parts of the missing plane. Photo: Mast Irham / EPA

Yesterday there was a serious plane crash in Indonesia.

The plane took off from Jakarta, the capital of Indonesia, but soon it disappeared from radar.

The authorities say that the plane dived into the sea.

The black boxes of the plane have been found.

It is still not known why the plane dived into the sea. The reason may become clear when the black boxes have been examined.

There were 62 people on the plane in total. The plane was on its way to the Indonesian island of Borneo.

## A fell story

![Cameraman Teemu Liakka with his camera in a snowy landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3648,h_2052,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7602035ff81c4799733/1610095798)

Open image viewer

The film was shot by Teemu Liakka. He filmed the film for 2 years. Photo: MRP Matila Röhr Productions

A Finnish film called Tunturin tarina (A Fell Story) will soon come to cinemas. It tells about nature and animals on the fells, in Finnish Lapland and even further north in the Arctic Ocean.

In the film the main roles are played by, for example, the fell owl, the golden eagle, the reindeer, the bear and the fox.

The film was shot over 2 years.

In the film you can see what nature and life are like on the northern fells in different seasons.

The landscapes can be absolutely incredible.
