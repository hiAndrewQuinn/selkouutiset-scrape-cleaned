# Saturday 11.3.2017 (radio)

There have been hundreds of demonstrators at Railway Square in Helsinki. The Finnish Lutheran Church is discussing marriages of gay couples. Lavis is a new Finnish workout. There is good skiing weather in the north.

## The Railway Square demonstrations

![People at Railway Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9504844)

Open image viewer

There were a lot of people at Railway Square in Helsinki on Saturday. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Demonstrations have continued at Railway Square in Helsinki. But on Saturday the situation was calm.

There has been a demonstration by asylum seekers at Railway Square for many weeks already.

On Saturday a lot of other people also came there. Many wanted to support the asylum seekers. Others disagreed. They said that the asylum seekers' demonstration must be ended.

The asylum seekers are demonstrating at Railway Square because they are disappointed with the decisions of the Finnish Immigration Service. Many asylum seekers have received a decision that they may not stay in Finland.

## Marrying gay couples

![Archbishop Kari Mäkinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9504492)

Open image viewer

Archbishop Kari Mäkinen believes that in the future the church will also marry gay couples. Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

The Finnish Lutheran Church is discussing marriages of gay couples. Some of the church's priests want the church to also marry gay couples. Some priests oppose it.

The head of the Finnish church, Archbishop Kari Mäkinen, hopes that the church will start marrying gay couples too. But the archbishop says that the church must first make a joint decision on the matter. Marrying gay couples in church is not yet possible, the archbishop says.

Marrying gay couples is being discussed in the church because Finland has a new marriage law. The new law says that gay couples can also get married. It means that a man can marry a man and a woman can marry a woman. Gay couples can get married at the register office, but not yet in church.

## The Finnish lavis workout

![Leading lavis is fun, because exact moves have not been set.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9501538)

Open image viewer

In lavis, music familiar to Finns is played. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

At the weekend many people do exercise. One popular hobby is zumba. In it the workout is based on Latin dances, or South American music.

Now a Finnish alternative to zumba has been invented. Its name is lavis. In it the workout is based on dance pavilion dances, which are familiar to Finns. Finns like to dance, for example, humppa, jenkka and the waltz.

Fitness instructor Tiina Makkonen says that a lot of people come to lavis classes. They like lavis because lavis is based on Finnishness, Makkonen says.

## Good skiing weather

This weekend it is worth going outdoors too. The weather is beautiful. The sun is shining in different parts of Finland.

There is also good skiing weather in Northern Finland. It is only a few degrees below zero, but there is a lot of snow. In the north there is as much as a metre of snow.
