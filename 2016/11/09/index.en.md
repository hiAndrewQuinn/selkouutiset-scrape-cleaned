## Donald Trump is the next president of the United States

![Vice president Mike Pence and president Donald Trump at the election night party after the result was announced.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9281460)

Open image viewer

Future vice president Mike Pence and president-elect Donald Trump celebrated the victory at the election night party in New York. Photo: Shawn Thew / EPA

Businessman Donald Trump has been elected as the Americans' new president. The Republican candidate Trump beat the Democratic candidate Hillary Clinton in the US presidential election.

Trump has given a victory speech. He said that all Americans, Republicans and Democrats alike, must unite. Without uniting, Americans are not one people, Trump said.

According to Trump, he has a big economic plan that will double the economic growth of the United States.

To the international community Trump said that the United States will treat all countries fairly.

## Trump has caused many kinds of reactions

![Hillary Clinton's supporters at an election night party in New York.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9280883)

Open image viewer

Supporters of the Democratic candidate Hillary Clinton were tense during the vote count at an election night party in New York. Photo: Andrew Gombert / EPA

Donald Trump's election as president has caused reactions around the world. Many say that Trump's election is a risk. The reason is that nobody knows how Trump intends to use power.

Critics say that Trump is not a politician but a rich businessman and reality TV star, and he has no experience of, for example, foreign policy.  Many are also worried about human rights and minorities.

Russia is pleased that Trump won. The European Union, or EU, has invited Trump to a summit. A representative of the Indonesian Muslim council fears that tensions will grow between the United States and the Islamic world.

The President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö has congratulated the new US president. Prime minister Juha Sipilä says that Trump's election was a surprise, but he had nevertheless prepared for it.

Many Americans living in Finland are disappointed by Trump's victory. They hoped that a Democrat would win. Many also hoped for the first woman president for the United States.

## Finns oppose education cuts

![Infographic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9280151)

Open image viewer

More than 1,000 people answered the Science Barometer 2016 survey in July and August. Photo: Yle News Graphics

Finns do not accept cuts to education. The Science Barometer 2016 asked Finns about their attitude to science and research. The respondents trust them and believe that they benefit society.

The people who answered the survey think that education cuts are a short-sighted solution for state savings. The respondents trust that education and research keep Finland competitive.

Medicine, technology and the environment interest people most. Entertainment interests people less than in the 2013 survey. Trust in parties and parliament is weak.
