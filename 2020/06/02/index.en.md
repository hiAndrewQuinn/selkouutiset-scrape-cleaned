# Tuesday 2.6.2020 (radio)

A large plywood factory in Jyväskylä may be closed. Visits to doctors have decreased. The housing market is picking up. People can take part in church services again. Weather forecast.

## Jyväskylä plywood factory

![UPM flag against the sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_205/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5394585c505365e49a6/1548768146)

The flag of the forest company UPM. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

The forest company UPM wants to close a large plywood factory in Jyväskylä. About 170 people work at the factory. They will lose their jobs if the factory is closed.

The Jyväskylä plywood factory is the oldest plywood factory in Finland. UPM says that the factory cannot survive in competition, because plywood is made more cheaply abroad. For example, new plywood factories are being built in Russia.

UPM will start co-operation negotiations (yt-neuvottelut) with the workers at the Jyväskylä plywood factory about the situation.

## Doctor's appointments

![Stethoscope.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10850467)

Open image viewer

It has become possible to get to a doctor faster than before. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Finns have visited a doctor much less in the spring than before. Only about half as many people have visited health centres, that is, 50% of the usual number.

Because of the corona situation, people have not wanted to move around much. It has become possible to get to a doctor faster than before, because only a few people have wanted to go to a doctor.

Dental visits have also decreased in the same way. It has also become possible to get to a dentist faster.

## Housing market

![Two men at an apartment viewing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11372353)

Open image viewer

Apartment viewing. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The housing market has picked up after the corona break. Estate agents say that the housing market clearly picked up in May. At the end of May, about 8% fewer apartments were sold than usual.

In April the housing market was much worse, but it picked up in May.

Estate agents say that apartment prices have stayed the same.

## Church services

![Altar and altarpiece of Oulu Cathedral, and stained glass windows.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9795062)

Open image viewer

Oulu Cathedral. Photo: Wasim Khuzam / Yle

Finns can go to church again. The corona restrictions were eased on Monday, and people can take part in church services again. In small churches the number of people can be limited, so that everyone has safety distances. Holy Communion may also be held in churches.

The coronavirus spreads easily through droplet infection, and droplets can fly when a person sings. Therefore, less singing takes place in churches.

Parishes still also show church services online. They can be watched at home, if you do not want to go to church.
