## Helicopter crash in Ukraine

![Two firefighters pull a hose next to debris after a helicopter crash.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3516,h_1977,x_0,y_524/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106066463c7c90ab8ec5/1674037607)

The Interior Minister's helicopter crashed into a residential area. Photo: SERGEY DOLZHENKO/EPA-EFE

A disastrous helicopter crash has happened in Ukraine. The helicopter was carrying Ukraine's Interior Minister and other Interior Ministry officials.

The helicopter fell onto a residential area near Kyiv on Wednesday morning. Ukraine's Interior Minister and 17 other people died in the crash. More than 20 people were injured. The helicopter fell onto a kindergarten. That is why children also died and were injured.

It is not yet known why the helicopter carrying Ukraine's Interior Minister crashed.

## Disability services act

![Markus Lohi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4999,h_2811,x_0,y_141/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10345356373bdfd1ac73/1668529909)

Chair of the Social and Health Committee Markus Lohi. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Parliament may deal with the disability services act even before the election break. Last week the Constitutional Law Committee said that the government's proposal must be changed a great deal. That is why it seemed that Parliament would not have time to deal with the disability services act before the elections.

Chair of the Social and Health Committee Markus Lohi says that disability organisations are worried about the matter. They hope that the disability services act will be dealt with quickly.

That is why Parliament is hurrying the disability services act. Because of this some other laws may have to be postponed.

## Technology sector labour disputes

![The Industrial Union's building in Hakaniemi, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5987,h_3368,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89756561cdbd562cc38/1640873415)

Open image viewer

The Industrial Union's office is in Hakaniemi, Helsinki. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

The National Conciliator has started mediation of the collective agreement dispute in the technology industry and the chemical industry.

The employees' trade unions, the Industrial Union, YTN and Pro, issued strike warnings on Tuesday. The dispute is especially about wages.

The Industrial Union wants pay rises for employees as large as in Germany. In Germany the pay of metal and electronics industry employees will rise by a total of 8.5 percent this year and next year.

## VR's new sleeping cars and carriages

![Illustration of a three-person sleeper compartment.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2999,h_1687,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106053463c7a38b61b85/1674028068)

Open image viewer

This is what the new sleeping cars look like inside when the carriages are completed. Photo: Škoda Transtech Oy

The train company VR is buying more sleeping cars and carriages. VR is ordering 9 sleeping cars and 8 carriages from Škoda Transtech.

The carriages cost about 50 million euros. VR will receive the new carriages at the end of 2025.

VR is acquiring more sleeping cars and carriages because overnight train travel has increased a lot.

Night trains run from the southern Finnish cities of Helsinki, Turku and Tampere to the northern Finnish cities of Oulu, Rovaniemi, Kemijärvi and Kolari.
