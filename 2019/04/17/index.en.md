# Wednesday 17.4.2019 (radio)

The ministry is in a hurry with the social and health care reform. Researchers propose a big tax reform for Finland. Alcohol is not healthy even in small amounts. Easter traffic starts on Thursday.

## Social and health care reform is urgent

![Practical nurse washing dishes at home, a 95-year-old veteran sits in the kitchen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10734175)

Practical nurses are needed, for example, for home care of the elderly. Photo: Isto Janhunen / Yle

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health says that the social and health care reform must be made. Sote means social and health services.

The Finnish government tried to carry out the social and health care reform but did not succeed. That is why the government resigned shortly before the elections.

The ministry, however, says that the social and health care reform is urgent. The reason is that the number of elderly people in Finland is growing, and there will soon be far too few practical nurses and social workers.

## Proposal for a tax reform

![Traffic jam in the city.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10405769)

Open image viewer

The researchers propose that emissions be taxed more heavily. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Researchers propose a big tax reform for Finland. A research group at the Sitra fund proposes that wage taxes be cut and environmental taxes be raised.

The researchers say that such a tax reform can bring more jobs. The economy grows if wage tax falls and people have more money to spend from their wages.

At the same time the environment can improve if emissions fall. Emissions from industry and traffic warm the climate. That is why, in the researchers' opinion, emissions must be taxed more heavily

It is a proposal from the researchers. It is not certain whether the parties can agree on such a reform in Parliament.

## Effects of alcohol

![Brandy glass.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9224112)

Open image viewer

Alcohol never promotes health. Photo: AOP

Alcohol is not healthy even in small amounts. Until now researchers have thought that a small amount of alcohol protects against, for example, heart attacks and strokes.

A new study, however, shows that this is not so. Other reasons explained why some people have a heart attack and some do not.

Small amounts of alcohol gave no protection at all. The risk of heart symptoms and stroke began to grow straight away when alcohol use increased.

## Easter traffic

![VR train on the track.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9688684)

Open image viewer

Many trains are completely full on Thursday. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Easter is at the weekend, and many Finns travel, for example to visit relatives. Many people have 4 days off work, from Friday to Monday.

Easter traffic starts on Thursday. VR says that a large part of Thursday's long-distance trains are already sold out. Especially full are the trains leaving Helsinki towards the north. There is still space on the long-distance buses.

Monday is also a busy day, because many people return home then.
