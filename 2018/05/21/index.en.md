# Monday 21.5.2018 (radio)

Many young people are getting summer jobs. Fatal road accidents have decreased. The new US ambassador arrived in Helsinki. The royal wedding gathered a big TV audience. It is now as warm in Finland as in Spain.

## Summer jobs

![An ice cream seller makes an ice cream cone](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5245816)

Open image viewer

Companies are hiring summer workers again. Photo: Yle

More young people than before are getting a summer job this summer.

A survey by SAK says that 75 percent of companies are hiring young people for summer jobs this year. That is a little more than last year.

Last year, the public sector offered the most summer jobs. This year, industry is hiring the most summer workers.

More summer jobs are on offer because companies' finances have improved.

## Fatal crashes

![A three-car crash that happened during a chase on Hailuodontie in Oulu on 19.4.2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10167814)

Open image viewer

Fatal crashes have decreased on Finland's roads. Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

Fatal crashes in road traffic have decreased.

The Finnish Crash Data Institute says that between January and April there were 50 fatal crashes in road traffic. A total of 57 people died in the crashes. 6 of the dead were pedestrians.

Young people under 25 caused a total of 7 fatal crashes in the first part of the year. That is the smallest number in 5 years.

Fatal crashes on Finland's roads have decreased since 2016.

## The ambassador

![The new US ambassador Robert Pence introduced himself to the media in Helsinki on 21 May](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10215127)

Open image viewer

The new US ambassador to Finland, Robert Pence. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The new US ambassador to Finland has arrived in Helsinki. Ambassador Robert Pence will hand over his credentials to President Sauli Niinistö on Thursday.

The new ambassador Robert Pence is a businessman who has funded conservative election campaigns in the United States.

The United States has not had an ambassador in Finland for about a year and a half, that is, since Donald Trump started as president.

The wedding broadcast also gathered a big audience in the United States. Over 29 million Americans watched the wedding.

## Summer weather

![A three-day temperature map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10215229)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Weather

It is now as warm in Finland as in Spain. In Spain, though, there are thundershowers now and then, but here it is dry, says meteorologist Seija Paasonen.

The weather will be sunny all week. It will be warmest at the beginning of the week, Seija Paasonen says.

On Monday, the heatwave limit of 25 degrees was passed, for example, in central Finland. Seija Paasonen says that similar temperatures will be measured in the southern and central parts of the country on Tuesday too.

On Wednesday it will be a little cooler and there may also be showers. After that it will be sunny and warm again.
