# Thursday 24.12.2020 (radio)

Trade deal. Christmas traditions in the coronavirus time. Christmas post. The Good Christmas Spirit collection.

## EU and Britain agree a trade deal

![Boris Johnson](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11714612)

Open image viewer

Britain's Prime Minister Boris Johnson held a press conference in which he spoke about the agreement between the EU and Britain. Photo: GB POOL / EBU

A deal has been reached on trade relations between the EU and Britain.

Britain left the EU last January. However, free trade between Britain and the EU ends only now, at the turn of the year, that is, in a week. The agreement concerns the time after that.

The President of the European Commission, Ursula von der Leyen, says that the agreement is good, fair and even-handed.

## Corona shows in the Christmas celebrations

![The proclamation of Christmas peace in Turku's Old Market Square. Because of the coronavirus situation, the traditional event was held without an audience.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11714584)

Turku proclaimed Christmas peace, but this time without an audience. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

Finland has quietened down for Christmas. Christmas is traditionally celebrated, but because of the coronavirus restrictions many things are also different from usual.

Many people have stayed at home for Christmas this year, even though they would usually go to celebrate Christmas, for example, at their grandparents'. Because of this, there has been less Christmas traffic on the roads than usual.

![A Santa Claus waves from a computer screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_398/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7567755fe1aeed47d33/1608627123)

Open image viewer

In the coronavirus time, Santa Claus can make a remote visit. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

In Turku, Christmas peace was proclaimed at noon, as it traditionally is. This time, however, no audience was let into the Old Market Square in Turku. It was possible to listen to the proclamation on the radio, for example.

Christmas services in churches have been cancelled this year, or they are held without an audience. Usually many people attend the services, especially on Christmas Eve. Now people can take part in Christmas services remotely on parishes' websites.

The coronavirus epidemic has also changed Santa Claus's work. This year Santa Claus may not visit homes as before. However, he can come into the yard and wave from behind a window, send a video greeting, or make a remote visit over the internet.

## Lots of Christmas post was sent

![Christmas cards on a tray.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8552776)

Open image viewer

Finns sent more Christmas cards this year than last year. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finns sent a lot of Christmas post this year.

About 18 million Christmas cards were sent this year. That is about 20 per cent more than last year.

The Post has also delivered a record number of parcels, about 7.7 million in total.

## Good Christmas spirit for thousands of families

![The logo of the Hyvä Joulumieli collection](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5472524)

Open image viewer

The logo image of the Hyvä Joulumieli (Good Christmas Spirit) collection. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

The Hyvä Joulumieli (Good Christmas Spirit) collection went very well this year. People donated more money to the collection than the organisers had expected.

The Hyvä Joulumieli collection raises money for poor families with children. With the collection money, 50-euro gift cards for food shops are bought for the families.

The aim of the collection was to hand out 21,000 food gift cards this year. However, so many donations came in that gift cards were received by 25,000 families in total.
