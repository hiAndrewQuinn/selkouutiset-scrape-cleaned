# Wednesday 1.3.2023 (radio)

Effects of the bus strike. Nurse shortage. Finland's Nato membership. Hungary. The weather.

## Effects of the bus strike

![Empty bus stops.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689513)

Open image viewer

Few buses were visible at Elielinaukio in Helsinki on Wednesday morning, when the bus drivers' strike began. Photo: Petteri Juuti / Yle

The bus strike has begun. The strike is by the bus drivers, who stopped work early on Wednesday morning.

The bus strike has made it harder for people to get around, especially in big cities.

For example, in the Helsinki region most buses are not running. In Tampere the strike has stopped perhaps more than half of bus traffic.

Bus drivers are away from work until next week's Friday, if no solution is found to the labour dispute.

Bus drivers want changes, for example, to working hours. The drivers say that they are in too much of a hurry. The drivers do not even have time to go to the toilet.

On Yle's website at yle.fi there is information about how the strike affects bus traffic in different cities.

## Nurse shortage

Open image viewer

There is a shortage of nurses in Finland. Photo: Arash Matin / Yle

Finland needs many more nurses.

The pension institution Keva estimates that Finland now needs more than 16,000 new nurses. In addition, about 9,000 new practical nurses are needed.

Keva estimates the need for workers on the basis of the number of training places, open jobs and people retiring.

## Parliament and Finland's Nato membership

![Session hall of Parliament, with MPs present.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689516)

Open image viewer

Parliament approved Finland's membership of the defence alliance Nato on Wednesday. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Parliament has approved Finland's Nato membership. Parliament, that is, Finland's parliament, voted on the matter on Wednesday.

Finland is now ready to join Nato, as soon as all Nato countries have approved Finland's membership.

28 Nato countries have already approved Finland's Nato membership. Approval is still needed from Turkey and Hungary.

Parliament decided last May that Finland would apply for membership of the defence alliance Nato.

The reason was Russia's war in Ukraine. Finland does not want to be left alone if Russia's attacks on neighbouring countries continue.

## Hungary and Finland's Nato membership

![Hungarian parliament building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689517)

Open image viewer

The Hungarian Parliament has started to deal with Finland's Nato membership. Photo: Peter Lakatos / EPA

The processing of Finland's Nato membership application has begun in Hungary. The Hungarian Parliament began the processing on Wednesday.

The Hungarian Parliament is also dealing with Sweden's Nato membership application at the same time. Finland and Sweden applied for Nato membership at the same time.

It is still unclear when the Hungarian Parliament will approve Finland and Sweden joining Nato.
