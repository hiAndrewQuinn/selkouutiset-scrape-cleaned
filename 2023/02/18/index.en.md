## Munich Security Conference

![Sanna Marin at the security conference in Munich.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4677,h_2630,x_0,y_84/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107469363f0a657980e9/1676715648)

The Munich Security Conference began on Friday in Germany and continues until Sunday, 19 February. Decision-makers and experts from around the world are at the conference. In the picture is Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Photo: EPA-EFE

Finland has told Turkey and Hungary that Finland would prefer to join Nato together with Sweden. This is what Prime Minister Sanna Marin said at the Munich Security Conference in Germany.

President Sauli Niinistö has also assured that Finland is applying to Nato together with Sweden. Of the Nato countries, only Turkey and Hungary have not yet approved the applications of Finland and Sweden.

The Munich Security Conference was developed during the Cold War, after the Second World War. During the Cold War the USA and the Soviet Union and their allies were opposed to each other.

## Fertility treatments

![A tank of a fertility clinic, in which, among other things, frozen ovarian tissue is stored.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8576541)

Open image viewer

A tank of a fertility clinic, in which, among other things, frozen ovarian tissue is stored. Photo: Juha Kokkala / Yle

Fertility treatments may decrease, because the government changed Kela reimbursements at the start of the year. Kela reimbursement can no longer be obtained for fertility treatments done by a private doctor.

This may increase queues for fertility treatments in public health care.

In Finland 5 percent of children are born with the help of fertility treatments. Fertility treatments mean treatments in which germ cells, that is, egg cells or sperm, are handled in a laboratory.

In Finland fertility treatment is received by male-female couples, female couples and women who wish to have a child alone.

## Bears' winter sleep

![Wide-awake bears on Högholmen. The bears have just woken up from their winter sleep. It is snowing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2043,h_1149,x_0,y_127/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107469763f0abeb2bd5d/1709982704)

Open image viewer

In recent years the bears at Korkeasaari Zoo have most commonly woken up from their winter sleep in February. Photo: Annika Sorjonen / Korkeasaari Zoo

The bears at Helsinki's Korkeasaari Zoo have woken up from their winter sleep. The zoo's 22- and 17-year-old female bears went into winter sleep in November.

The bears did not wake up even once during their winter sleep this winter.

During winter sleep the bears lost about 50 kilos, that is, about 25 percent of their weight.

## Winter storm

![Snow drift at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10055042)

Open image viewer

Snow drift at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Archive photo. Photo: AOP

The strong snowfall in southern Finland at the weekend hinders air traffic. Flights may be delayed at Helsinki-Vantaa, Turku and Tampere-Pirkkala airports.

Clearing wet snow from the runway and from the wings of aircraft is slow.

Snow has also been forecast for other airports in Northern Europe.
