# Saturday 17.4.2021 (radio)

Vocational schools. Remote teaching. Prince Philip. Wildfires. Weather forecast.

## Studying for a trade

![There are students in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11629412)

Open image viewer

Cookery students make a dish. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

Remote studying can delay graduating into a trade. Because of the corona situation, there has been less face-to-face teaching at upper secondary schools and vocational schools than before.

In remote studying, you cannot learn all skills. For example, cookery students need practice in a restaurant, and laboratory technician students need practice in a laboratory.

Now many vocational school students have not been able to do work placements. Some of them may not graduate this spring.

## Corona support for schools

![Jussi Saramo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11889510)

Open image viewer

Education Minister Jussi Saramo was a guest on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme on Saturday. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The corona situation has made studying harder in all schools. Education Minister **Jussi Saramo** says that schools need extra support. The support can be used to add, for example, extra help lessons and small-group teaching.

Education Minister Saramo says that it is important to help students quickly, so that the bad effects of the corona situation do not grow.

Next year, schools may need support of more than 100 million euros.

## Prince Philip was buried

![Prince Philip](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_94/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-775220602d2e015765a/1613573749)

Open image viewer

Prince Philip (10 June 1921 – 9 April 2021). Photo: All Over Press

**Prince Philip** has been buried in Britain. The funeral was held at Windsor's St George's Chapel. Because of corona, only 30 guests were allowed. The guests were close relatives of Prince Philip.

Prince Philip was given a ceremonial funeral. It is a smaller event than a state funeral.

Prince Philip died last Friday. He was 99 years old.

## Wildfires start easily

![Burning dry lawn is a common hobby in spring.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6639410)

Open image viewer

A fire can easily spread if the grass is dry and it is windy. Photo: Vesa Vaarama/Yle

There have been several wildfires in Finland on Saturday. Many wildfires started when people burned, for example, brushwood or dry grass in their yards.

Some of the fires spread to outbuildings and houses.

A grass fire warning has been issued for many areas in Southern Finland. A grass fire warning means that the grass can burn easily. That is why open fires are not allowed.
