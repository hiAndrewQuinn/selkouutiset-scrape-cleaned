# Sunday 22.1.2023 (radio)

Handover to Turkey. Turkish demonstrations against Sweden. Shooting in the USA. Chinese New Year. Weather.

## Handover to Turkey

![Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan speaks. The Turkish flag can be seen in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3816,h_2146,x_7,y_162/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-966477629f4ae162f30/1654606950)

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan demands that Finland and Sweden hand over many people to Turkey. Photo: Stephanie Lecocq / EPA

In the autumn Finland agreed to one Turkish extradition request. Finland decided to hand over 1 man to Turkey.

Finland handed over, that is, gave to Turkey, a man who has been sentenced in Turkey for robbery. So the man is a criminal.

Finland has handed over a total of 3 people to Turkey since 2019. However, Finland has not handed over people who have been sentenced in Turkey for terrorism.

Turkey has said that the handovers and Finland's NATO application are connected. Finland applied for NATO membership together with Sweden last May.

Turkey has demanded that Finland and Sweden hand over many people. Turkey says that it will not approve Finland and Sweden for NATO if the handovers do not proceed.

Finland and Sweden can join the NATO defence alliance only if all NATO countries approve them. Turkey is one of the NATO countries.

## Turkish demonstrations

![A group of men. One has a Swedish flag in his hand that is burning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688172)

Open image viewer

Demonstrators burned a Swedish flag in Turkey. Photo: Erdem Sahin / EPA

Demonstrations against Sweden have been held in Turkey. There have been demonstrations in Istanbul and Ankara.

Many Turks were angered when the far-right politician burned the Quran in Sweden on Saturday. The Quran is the holy book of Muslims.

The man burned the Quran in Stockholm in front of the Turkish embassy.

In Turkey demonstrators burned the Swedish flag.

## Shooting in the USA

A serious shooting has happened in the USA.

At least 10 people were killed in the shooting. In addition, many people were injured.

The shooting happened in the city of Monterey Park in California.

The shooter moved in an area where the Chinese New Year has been celebrated.

The police have not yet given exact information about the shooting.

## Chinese New Year

![Many people on a Chinese street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688174)

Open image viewer

Chinese people are now travelling a lot, because corona restrictions have ended. Photo: Caroline Brehman / EPA

In China the New Year is again being celebrated on a big scale after the corona era.

Chinese people can now travel freely, because the corona-era travel restrictions have ended. That is why many Chinese have travelled to visit friends and relatives to celebrate the New Year.

In the Chinese calendar the year of the tiger ended on Saturday and on Sunday the year of the rabbit began.
