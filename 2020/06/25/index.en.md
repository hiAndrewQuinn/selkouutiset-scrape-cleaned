The corona situation in Finland is calm, says Taneli Puumalainen, chief physician at the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Opening restaurants has not increased corona infections in Finland. Restaurants reopened at the beginning of June, but the number of corona infections has not grown much.

On Thursday 5 new corona infections were reported. In total, about 7,200 corona infections have been found in Finland.

## Meat consumption

![Raw chicken on a table, with potatoes, onions and tomatoes around it.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10111617)

Open image viewer

Chicken is growing in popularity at Finnish dinner tables. Photo: CC0

Finns eat less pork than before. In 2019 Finns ate 5% less pork than in 2018.

At the same time, more chicken is being eaten than before. However, chicken is still not eaten in Finland quite as much as pork.

The information comes from a new statistic by the Natural Resources Institute Finland.

## Alcohol sales

![Alko's spirit shelves in the Alko shop of Sokos in Kuopio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10839622)

Open image viewer

Finns think it is good that only Alko may sell strong alcoholic drinks. Photo: Petra Husu / Yle

Most Finns think that Finland's alcohol rules are good. A new survey shows this.

Almost 60 per cent of the survey respondents think the rules are good. Only about 30 per cent of respondents think the rules are too strict.

For example, Finns think it is good that strong alcoholic drinks are sold only at Alko. Finns also want to keep the age limits for buying alcohol.

However, there are differences between men's and women's opinions. More men want to make the rules easier. Women do not want changes to the alcohol rules.

## App for learning Finnish

![Duolingo is a free app for learning languages. Users can compare their progress and compete in results with their friends.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9486551)

Open image viewer

The Duolingo app can now also be used to study Finnish. Photo: Juha Hintsala / Yle

Finnish can now be studied in a new way. A student can download the Duolingo app on their phone. Duolingo now also teaches Finnish.

Duolingo is a free app. With it you can study many different languages. Duolingo has over 300 million users around the world.

Duolingo's users have wished that Finnish would also come to Duolingo. Here in Finland, Duolingo helps immigrants who want to learn Finnish. In addition, there are people abroad who are interested in the exotic Finnish language.

## Hot weather

![Two construction workers with helmets on their heads.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6706042)

Open image viewer

Work strains the body more when it is hot. Photo: Terri Niemi / Yle

Thursday has been the warmest day of the summer so far. This summer's heat record is now 33.5 degrees.

Experts remind people that there must be breaks at work in such hot weather. There must be a 10-minute break every hour if the heat is over 28 degrees.
