The Finnish government is arguing about a bill that concerns the Sámi Parliament.

The Centre Party disagrees with the other government parties about the bill. The dispute is disrupting the government's work.

The law on the Sámi Parliament determines, for example, who may vote in Sámi Parliament elections. Matters concerning the Sámi are dealt with in the Sámi Parliament.

The Sámi are Europe's only indigenous people. Sámi live in the north. In Norway and Sweden too, the Sámi have their own Sámi parliaments.

![Sanna Marin and Kaja Kallas.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4256,h_2394,x_0,y_22/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-103423063736b176cab7/1668508530)

Finnish Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Estonian Prime Minister Kaja Kallas. Photo: Raul mee / Lehtikuva

The prime ministers of Finland and Estonia have met.

Finnish Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Estonian Prime Minister Kaja Kallas met in Helsinki on Tuesday.

The prime ministers discussed, for example, Russia's war in Ukraine. Prime Minister Marin said that helping Ukraine is important for Finland and Estonia.

Marin also said that cooperation between Finland and Estonia will strengthen when Finland becomes a member of the NATO defence alliance. Estonia is already a NATO country.

## World population

![People standing side by side in the metro.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7412034)

Open image viewer

There are now 8 billion of us. Photo: Yle / Kirsi Crowley

There are now 8 billion people in the world. The number of people passed 8 billion today, Tuesday.

The population is growing quickly, especially in Asia and Africa. The most inhabitants are in China, but perhaps as early as next year India will have even more inhabitants than China.

Kamomilla Ilmonen, an expert at the Family Federation of Finland, says that everyone must have the right to decide for themselves how many children they want. However, there are about 250 million women in the world who want contraception but cannot get it.

## Weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12677415)

On Wednesday the weather is dry. However, cloudiness is heavy over a large part of the country.

The weather gets colder. The temperature on Wednesday is between -2 and +2 degrees. In Lapland the frost is 5 to 15 degrees.

Towards the end of the week the weather gets colder in the south too. The temperature is below freezing in a large part of the country. At least at night there is frost across the whole country. In Lapland the frost may rise to as much as 20 degrees towards the end of the week.
