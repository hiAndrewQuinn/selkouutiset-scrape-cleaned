## Potassium iodide tablets

![Packets of potassium iodide tablets at a pharmacy counter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_186/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9204196218b7d361745/1645787313)

Open image viewer

Potassium iodide tablets are only taken if the authorities order it. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

The Finnish authorities have given new instructions on the use of potassium iodide tablets.

Potassium iodide tablets are used in a situation where there is a risk of radiation. Potassium iodide tablets protect against radiation. Potassium iodide tablets are only taken if the authorities order it.

The new instructions no longer recommend potassium iodide tablets for people over 40 years old. The instruction now is that households buy one iodine dose per person for storage for each person aged 3 to 40.

Healthcare will get potassium iodide tablets for children under 3 years old.

Many Finns have bought potassium iodide tablets because of the war in Ukraine. Russia has also bombed Ukraine near nuclear power plants, that is, atomic power plants.

However, the authorities say that Finland is safe. If an accident happens at a Ukrainian nuclear power plant, Finland is so far away that no large amounts of radiation will come here.

## Mental health problems among young people

![Young woman looking out of a window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3556,h_2000,x_225,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-76478160096e11a0567/1611230801)

Open image viewer

Many young people are lonely. Photo: SUPERREEL PRODUCTIONS / DocPoint

Mental health problems among young people have increased.

The war in Ukraine and the corona pandemic have increased young people's worries. Young people are also often lonely.

Young people also do not know where they can get help. Young people say that they do not get help when they need it.

The information comes from the Mental Health Finland association. It says that young people are contacting them more than before.

The Mental Health Finland association supports young people in a chat, and through courses and events.

## Hydrogen aircraft

![Hydrogen aircraft concept based on today's kind of aircraft, with hydrogen tanks in the rear of the aircraft behind the cabin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1471,h_828,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-101817663401a6e1ee77/1665145497)

Open image viewer

A hydrogen aircraft is environmentally friendly. Photo: Airbus

People want to stop emissions from aircraft.

Today aircraft release carbon dioxide into the air, because their fuel is made from oil. Carbon dioxide warms the climate and speeds up climate change.

That is why aircraft are being designed that run on hydrogen fuel. A hydrogen aircraft releases only water vapour into the air. That is why it is environmentally friendly.

Around the world, technology needed for hydrogen aircraft is being researched. International flight emissions are to be stopped by 2050.
