# Saturday 4.2.2017 (radio)

Antti Rinne continues as leader of the SDP. In the USA a judge decided that the travel ban on Muslim countries is temporarily not to be followed. Almost 30 countries have skiers taking part in the Finlandia ski race. Now people are eating Runeberg tarts. Next week will be cold.

## Rinne continues as leader of the SDP

![Antti Rinne at the SDP party congress in Lahti on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9440938)

SDP party leader Antti Rinne. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Antti Rinne continues as the leader of the SDP, that is, the Social Democrats.

The SDP holds its party congress in Lahti at the weekend. Rinne was chosen as party leader at the congress. There were 3 candidates.

Antti Rinne has been the leader of the SDP since 2014. Rinne is 54 years old. He has a legal education, that is, he is a lawyer. Rinne has previously worked in trade unions for employees.

The Social Democrats, the SDP, are a left-wing party. It is one of Finland's big parties.

## The dispute over the travel ban continues in the USA

![Donald Trump presents an entry ban he has signed at the White House.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9442210)

Open image viewer

Trump's orders are causing a dispute. Photo: Mandel Ngan / AFP

In the USA the dispute continues over President Donald Trump's order that bans citizens of 7 Muslim countries from travelling to the USA.

Judge James Robart has decided that the President's order is temporarily not to be followed. In the judge's view, Trump's order is illegal.

President Trump is of the opinion that the judge is wrong. The President says that the judge's decision must be cancelled.

Trump's order banned citizens of Iran, Iraq, Libya, Somalia, Sudan, Syria and Yemen from travelling to the USA. Now many airlines say that they will again start carrying citizens of these countries to the USA too.

## The Finlandia ski race

![Volunteers clearing the Finlandia ski trails in Tapanila in January 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9423663)

Open image viewer

Volunteers get the Finlandia ski trails in shape. Photo: Yle / Mikko Tuomikoski

The Finlandia ski race started on Saturday in Lahti.

In the Finlandia ski race, skiers ski different distances. This year a 100-kilometre race was also organised, because independent Finland turns 100 years old. More than 400 skiers took part in the 100-kilometre celebration trip.

Many skiing enthusiasts take part in the Finlandia ski race. This year there are skiers from almost 30 countries. The Finlandia ski races have been held for more than 40 years.

## Now people eat Runeberg tarts

![Runeberg tarts.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9440879)

Open image viewer

Jam and a sugar ring decorate a Runeberg tart. Photo: Olli Koski / Yle

On Sunday Runeberg Day is celebrated. The hero of the day is the writer and Finland's national poet, Johan Ludvig Runeberg. He lived in the 1800s.

On Runeberg Day Finns eat Runeberg tarts. A Runeberg tart has raspberry jam on top, and around the jam is a ring of sugar.

Runeberg's wife, Fredrika Runeberg, baked quite similar tarts more than 150 years ago. The tarts got their name after the writer Runeberg.
