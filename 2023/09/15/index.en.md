## Ban on Russian cars

![A car parked on a partly cloudy autumn day in front of the Niirala border station in Tohmajärvi, North Karelia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_293/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117271665044a1ee61a2/1694780305)

Cars with Russian number plates can no longer cross the border into Finland. Photo: Ari Haimakainen / Yle

Finland is banning Russian passenger cars from entering the country.

The ban comes into force quickly, already at midnight on Friday. After that, a passenger car will no longer be let into Finland if it has Russian number plates.

However, the ban does not apply to citizens of EU countries who live in Russia.

The ban reduces border traffic between Finland and Russia. In this way the aim is to reduce the circumvention of sanctions.

The European Union has imposed sanctions, which for example restrict trade with Russia. The reason for the sanctions is Russia's war in Ukraine.

## Swedish king's celebrations

![Swedish Queen Silvia and Carl XVI Gustaf sit side by side on golden chairs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11725486504289e34aae/1694771862)

Open image viewer

King Carl Gustaf and Queen Silvia. Photo: Claudio Bresciani / Lehtikuva

Sweden has celebrated King Carl Gustaf. He has now been king of Sweden for exactly 50 years.

Carl Gustaf became king of Sweden on 15 September 1973. The king was then 27 years old.

The king's celebrations have been held at the Stockholm Royal Palace all day on Friday. Also Finland's President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio have taken part in the celebrations.

Finland and Sweden have close relations. Finland was part of the Swedish kingdom for about 600 years.

King Carl Gustaf has often visited Finland. Next week the Swedish royal family's visits to Finland will continue.

Carl Gustaf's daughter, Crown Princess Victoria, visits Finland next Wednesday and Thursday.

## Libya floods

![A view of a city devastated by the flood.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_700/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117175365029d0ab1f96/1717407706)

Open image viewer

The floods caused a lot of destruction in Libya. Photo: Mohamed Shalash / EPA

In Libya, in North Africa, it is unclear how much destruction the floods have caused. This is what the UN, that is the United Nations, says.

The UN estimates that almost 300 000 children are suffering from the destruction in eastern Libya.

The cause of the floods was a storm that hit Libya on Sunday. The storm broke dams, and therefore water rushed onto the streets of the cities.

Many thousands of people have died in the floods. In addition, many Libyans are without a home.

## Food prices

![The shopping basket contains beer, a coffee pack, an energy drink can, a ready-meal box, a milk carton and a pack of sanitary pads, among other things.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5822,h_3274,x_6,y_518/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11719296502d92c56668/1719924643)

Food has become more expensive this year and last year. Photo: Janne Körkkö / Yle

Prices have risen in Finnish grocery shops. Food is now about 15 percent more expensive than at the beginning of last year.

The prices of different products have risen at different speeds. Dairy products have become more expensive by over 20 percent, but beer, for example, has become more expensive by only 6 percent.

In recent months the rise in food prices has, however, slowed down a little.
