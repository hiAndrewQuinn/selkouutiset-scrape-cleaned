## Flood disasters in Europe

![People wade down the street in flood water up to their armpits.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83237660f06937e09b4/1626369121)

The flood disaster in Europe continues.

On Saturday it was reported that more than 150 people have died because of the floods. Most of the dead are in Germany.

People died when many cities were under water. The water also destroyed houses.

In Germany, Belgium and the Netherlands many have lost their homes. Rescue workers are moving people to safety.

![Map of areas of Germany and Belgium where it is flooding because of heavy rain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12023687)

There is a lot of flood damage in Germany, Belgium and the Netherlands, also called Holland. Photo: Harri Vähäkangas / Yle

The German government promises aid money to the flood area. The residents of the flood area need support, because they have to rebuild their houses.

The cause of the floods is heavy rain. It has rained so much that the water in many rivers is at a record high.

## Corona vaccinations without a booking

![A nurse gives a corona vaccine to a patient at Energia Areena in Vantaa. 26.5.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4256,h_2394,x_0,y_293/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81128660ae19058da10/1622032119)

Corona vaccination is also possible without a booking, for example in Espoo, Tampere and Oulu. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

In some Finnish cities, residents can now get a corona vaccination without a booking too. This is possible, for example, in Espoo, Tampere and Oulu.

Helsinki also tried on Saturday how corona vaccinations go without a booking.

About 64 percent of the people in Finland have had the first corona vaccination. About 27 percent of residents have had both corona vaccinations.

## Muslim pilgrimage

![In the picture are men in white robes bending down. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11877762)

Open image viewer

Friday prayer in Mecca during the corona era. Photo: AOP

The Muslim pilgrimage has started in Mecca, Saudi Arabia. Saudi Arabia has set corona restrictions for the pilgrimage.

Only about 60,000 Saudi Arabian residents may take part in the pilgrimage. Participants must be healthy, and they must have a corona vaccination.

This is already the second year that the pilgrimage is smaller than usual because of corona. Usually about 2.5 million Muslims take part in the pilgrimage.

## Storm Riikka

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12025146)

Open image viewer

Riikka brings thunderstorms, and after them the weather gets cooler. Photo: Yle

Storm Riikka arrives in Finland on Sunday.

Riikka moves across Finland. It brings thunderstorms and strong wind to Central Finland and Eastern Finland.

Riikka cools the air. The temperature falls to even below 20 degrees.

In Lapland there are no thunder showers. It rains all the time on Sunday. The temperature in Lapland is only about 15 degrees.
