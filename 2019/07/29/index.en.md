In Moscow, Russia, there have recently been many opposition demonstrations. The opposition opposes the administration of President Vladimir Putin.

## Humanity uses up natural resources too much

![Garimpo da Fofoca gold mine in the Amazon rainforest in Brazil, about 450 kilometres south of the city of Manaus, in 2007.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5650313)

Open image viewer

In Brazil, rainforest is being cut down because gold is being dug from the area. Photo: Raimundo Valentim / EPA

Today is Earth Overshoot Day. That means that humanity has now used up the natural resources that the Earth's ecosystem has produced for this year.

Researchers calculate that humanity uses natural resources 1.75 times more than the Earth produces.

Earth Overshoot Day has never before been this early.

In Finland, the overuse of natural resources shows in nature, for example. When trees are cut down in a forest, birds and berries disappear. They do not survive in a forest where there are no trees.

## Police investigate serious sexual crimes

The police are investigating serious sexual crimes in Helsinki.

The police say that a 64-year-old man may have committed several sexual crimes whose victims are children. There are at least 12 victims. They are all girls under 18 years old.

The police published the man's picture on their own website. The police believe there are more victims.

The man may have committed crimes for over 13 years.

## Tigers have increased in India

![Tiger in the national park in Bhopal, India.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10897426)

Open image viewer

This is an Indian tiger. Its other name is Bengal tiger. This tiger is in a national park. Photo: Sanjeev Gupta / EPA

In India people are happy, because there are again many tigers in the country. India now has almost 3,000 tigers. 15 years ago there were only 1,400 of them.

Tigers are protected in India. They have their own safe areas.

Killing a tiger is a crime in India. Still, poachers kill them.
