# Tuesday 14.8.2018 (radio)

A motorway bridge collapsed in Genoa. The largest police crime trial in Finnish history began. Vantaa continues its trial of anonymous job applications. Vienna is the most liveable city in the world.

## Bridge collapse in Italy

![Collapsed bridge](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10352479)

Open image viewer

About 100 metres of the motorway bridge collapsed. Photo: Luza Zennaro / EPA-EFE

A motorway bridge has collapsed near the city of Genoa in Italy. At least 30 people have died in the collapse, says Italy's interior minister.

A 100-metre section fell from the bridge. There were perhaps about 20 cars on the bridge at the moment it collapsed.

The bridge was built in the 1960s. It was repaired the year before last.

Authorities suspect that there were structural defects in the bridge.

## Police chiefs in court

![Helsinki Police Department chief Lasse Aapio being interviewed by the media during a preparatory hearing at the Helsinki District Court on 14 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10351861)

Open image viewer

Journalists surrounded the current head of the Helsinki Police Department, Lasse Aapio, when he arrived at the district court. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

A rare trial has begun at the Helsinki District Court. Several former and current police chiefs are charged with official misconduct.

Those charged include, for example, former Police Commissioner Mikko Paatero, former head of the Helsinki Police Department Jukka Riikonen, and the current head Lasse Aapio. Former head of the Helsinki drugs police Jari Aarnio is also charged.

The charges concern the registration of informants at the Helsinki Police Department. The prosecutor says that the police chiefs have not supervised enough how the police use informants. The prosecutor demands conditional prison sentences or severe fines for the police chiefs.

The police chiefs say that they have not committed official misconduct.

## Anonymous job search

![A job seeker browses services online.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10029607)

Open image viewer

The purpose of anonymous job search is to give different applicants an equal chance of getting a job. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

Vantaa intends to continue the trial of anonymous recruitment.

Anonymous recruitment means that details such as the applicant's name, age, gender and ethnic background are removed from job applications. Only information about the applicant's skills remains, that is, information about education and work experience.

The purpose of anonymous recruitment is that different applicants have an equal chance of getting a job.

Vantaa already tried anonymous job search last spring.

## Vienna is the most liveable

![A traditional tram on the Schwedenbrücke bridge in Vienna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7662647)

Open image viewer

A traditional Vienna tram on the Schwedenbrücke bridge. Photo: Gaetan Bally / EPA

The Austrian capital Vienna is the most liveable city in the world.

Australia's Melbourne had won the international comparison for 7 years in a row, but now it lost to Vienna and fell to second place. The third most liveable city is Osaka in Japan.

A British research institute compared 140 big cities. The cities were compared, for example, on healthcare, cultural life, education and environmental issues.

Only one Nordic city made it into the top ten: Copenhagen in Denmark.

At the bottom of the comparison were Damascus, the capital of Syria, Dhaka in Bangladesh and Lagos in Nigeria.
