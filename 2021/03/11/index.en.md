# Thursday 11.3.2021 (radio)

## More mask requirements on transport

![Passengers wearing face masks in front of the Itäkeskus bus terminal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11672354)

Open image viewer

Mask requirements are coming to transport in the Helsinki region. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Mask requirements are being introduced on transport. The mask requirement on VR's trains came into force today, Thursday. People over 12 years old must wear a mask on trains, unless health reasons prevent it.

A mask requirement will also come to Helsinki region transport on Saturday.

On HSL's buses, metro and trams in the Helsinki region, masks must be worn from Saturday onwards.

## Coronavirus infections

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin comments on the meeting in front of the Säätytalo in Helsinki on 25 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11810934)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin's government met on Thursday at the Säätytalo in Helsinki to discuss the coronavirus situation. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Coronavirus infections still continue to grow a lot. On Thursday 720 new coronavirus infections were found in Finland. The coronavirus situation is difficult especially in the Helsinki and Uusimaa region and in the Southwest Finland region.

The number of coronavirus patients in hospitals is also growing. There are 260 coronavirus patients in hospitals. Of these, 41 patients are in intensive care.

On Thursday evening the government met again at the Säätytalo to discuss the coronavirus situation. The government is considering what measures must be taken in Finland so that the spread of the coronavirus can be stopped.

## Travel restrictions continue

![The Border Guard carries out border checks in Tornio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-76681660114531f0e4c/1611745233)

Open image viewer

Border guards at the border in Tornio. Photo: Antti Ullakko / Yle

Finland will continue travel restrictions. The restrictions will be extended by about a month.

People may come to Finland from abroad only for a special reason. Only from a few countries is it possible to come to Finland normally.

At the eastern border, the opening hours of the border crossing points will be shortened. The border crossing points will be kept open only during the day, so that health care has time to test those crossing the border.

## Astra Zeneca vaccine is being studied further

![Astra Zeneca coronavirus vaccine vials.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_173/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-779980603e1b48c6be6/1614683116)

Open image viewer

Astra Zeneca coronavirus vaccine. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Denmark, Norway and Iceland are suspending vaccinations with the Astra Zeneca coronavirus vaccine. The vaccine may have caused side effects for some people.

Some people have been found to have blood clots. However, it is not known whether they are caused by the vaccine or by something else. Authorities are investigating the matter.

Austria, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania and Luxembourg have also stopped using the Astra Zeneca vaccine.

It is also being examined in Finland whether the vaccine has side effects.

## Weather

Snowfall will come to Finland from the south-west on Thursday evening.

On Friday it will snow in the southern and central parts of the country. In the south a lot of snow may fall, about 10 centimetres.

On Friday the temperature is between +1 degree in Southern Finland and -8 degrees in the north.
