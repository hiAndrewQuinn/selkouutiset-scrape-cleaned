# Sunday 13.5.2018 (radio)

Few unemployed people go to health checks. Mother's Day has been celebrated on Sunday. A knife attacker struck in Paris. Israel won the Eurovision. The heat threshold has been broken almost everywhere except Lapland.

## Only 10 percent of unemployed people have had a health check

![Stethoscope.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10018942)

Open image viewer

Only one in ten unemployed people goes to a health check, even though they have had the right to it for 7 years. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Only a few unemployed people use their right to a free health check. It is estimated that only 10 percent of unemployed people have visited a nurse's appointment. Unemployed people have had the right to a free health check for 7 years already.

An authority must direct someone to a health check when a young person has been unemployed for more than half a year, and a person over 25 has been unemployed continuously for more than a year. However, the assessment of health and ability to work can often be postponed for many years.

An unemployed person can book an appointment with a nurse on their own initiative.

## Medals were handed out again on Mother's Day

![President Sauli Niinistö (left) greets Aila Fager at the national Mother's Day celebration in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10202779)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö greets Aila Fager at the national Mother's Day celebration in Helsinki on Sunday. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Mother's Day has been celebrated on Sunday. At the national celebration, President Sauli Niinistö handed out medals. 30 distinguished mothers received a medal.

They are educators, social influencers and local activists. Among them are single mothers, mothers of large families, foster mothers and support mothers, and mothers of children with special needs.

Statistics Finland says that there are almost 1.6 million mothers in Finland. Last year almost 20,500 women gave birth to their first child.

Mother's Day is celebrated around the world, but the date of the day varies.

## A knife attacker struck in Paris

![Officials at the scene of the knife attack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10202651)

Open image viewer

Officials at the scene of the knife attack. Photo: Geoffroy Van Der Hasselt / AFP

A knife attack was carried out in Paris on Saturday evening, and it is being investigated as a terrorist attack. 2 people died and 4 were wounded in a restaurant area near the opera. One of the dead was the attacker, whom the police shot.

The attacker was of Chechen background, and he was on the authorities' watch list. The terrorist organisation Isis announced that it was behind the attack. More than 240 people have died in France in Isis attacks over the last 3 years.

In Indonesia, at least 11 people have died and 40 have been wounded in bomb attacks on 3 churches. The authorities say that the attackers were members of one family.

## The heat threshold was broken almost everywhere

![Sun worshippers at the Kumpula outdoor pool](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10202823)

Open image viewer

People enjoy the sun at the Kumpula outdoor pool in Helsinki on Sunday. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The heat threshold was broken on Sunday across the whole of Finland except Lapland. The highest temperature of the day was measured in the afternoon in Kouvola, at 27.7 degrees.

The Mother's Day heat record was also broken. In 1996, 26 degrees was measured in Salo and in Lapinjärvi.

Tomorrow, Monday, the 30-degree mark may be broken in Southern and Central Finland.
