## Youth violence

![Infographic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1122,h_629,x_0,y_239/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88621661a62474994c9/1638278673)

Photo: Jari Pelkonen / Yle

Violent crimes by young people have become more common.

More and more violence where the perpetrators are under 15 years old comes to the attention of the police. The number of crime suspicions for under-15s has more than doubled since 2014.

Statistics show that the most common crimes among young people are assaults.

Researchers do not know why violence by under-15s has increased. The researchers say that the matter must be investigated thoroughly.

## Corona passes at workplaces

![The text of the EU corona certificate is visible on the screen of a mobile phone in a hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88146361975e37a8418/1638428487)

Open image viewer

The government is preparing to extend the corona pass to workplaces too. Photo: Tiina Kokko / Yle

A large part of MPs support the use of the corona pass at workplaces.

About 120 MPs answered Yle's survey. Of them, 70 supported the use of the corona pass at workplaces, and just over 30 opposed it.

The MPs of the National Coalition Party supported the corona pass the most, while the Finns Party were the strongest against it.

The government is preparing to extend the corona pass to workplaces.

At the moment, for example, a restaurant may ask customers for a corona pass, but not its workers.

## Air travel

![Finnair Airbus A350 XWB](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4213,h_2372,x_0,y_191/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4099895943d7142a21c/1497618282)

Open image viewer

Air passengers have returned to Finnair. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The airline Finnair says that its passenger numbers have grown.

In November Finnair had almost 490 000 passengers. That is more than 12 per cent more than in October. Compared with November last year, the growth is more than 470 per cent.

Finnair says that the strict travel restrictions of many countries still affect the number of air passengers.

## Euro banknotes will be renewed

![A series of euro banknotes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10409107)

Open image viewer

Photo: European Central Bank

The European Central Bank plans to renew euro banknotes.

There will be a design competition for the banknotes, and Europeans can say what they think of the plans.

The look of the new banknotes will be known by 2024.

The current euro banknotes have pictures of bridges, windows and gateways.
