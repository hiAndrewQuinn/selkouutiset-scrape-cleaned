## Budget proposal

![Finance Minister Matti Vanhanen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5340,h_3004,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6980845f08597515f63/1594382830)

The proposal for the state's budget for next year is ready. The budget shows how much money the state collects and spends.

Finance Minister, that is, Money Minister Matti Vanhanen presented the budget proposal on Wednesday.

Vanhanen said that Finland must stop the second wave of corona. Finland's money will run out if the corona situation becomes as bad again as it was in the spring.

The budget proposal does not raise the wage tax of an ordinary Finn. However, for example alcohol and tobacco will become more expensive, because their tax is planned to be raised.

The whole government will deal with the budget proposal in September.

## US presidential election

![Joe Biden and Kamala Harris debated in Houston, Texas, on 12 September 2019 after the debate of the Democratic presidential candidates. At that time Harris was still campaigning for her own candidacy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11489661)

Joe Biden and Kamala Harris. Photo: Robyn Beck / AFP

The USA, that is, the United States, is preparing for the presidential election.

The Democratic Party's presidential candidate Joe Biden has chosen a vice-presidential candidate. Biden's candidate is Kamala Harris.

Harris is a 55-year-old politician, and she has a legal education.

Harris is the first Black woman to become a vice-presidential candidate of a major party in the USA.

Harris is known for being good at speaking and debating. It is estimated that Harris will be a good help to Joe Biden in the election. Biden, of the Democrats, is trying to win the election against the Republican candidate, that is, the current president Donald Trump.

Biden will be officially chosen as the Democratic presidential candidate next week. The US presidential election will be held in November.

## Moomin anniversary exhibition

![Moomins 75, National Museum](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11487797)

Open image viewer

The Moomin anniversary exhibition starts at the National Museum. Photo: Jussi Mankkinen / Yle

The Moomins turn 75 years old this year. Tove Jansson's first Moomin book was published 75 years ago, in 1945.

The Moomin anniversary exhibition is at the National Museum. The exhibition opens in Helsinki on Friday.

The National Museum's exhibition respects the values of the Moomin books. That is why the name of the exhibition is Courage, Love, Freedom. These are important values in Jansson's books.

The Moomin anniversary exhibition has been built so that it can also be taken abroad. If, for example, Asian tourists cannot come to Finland because of the corona, the exhibition can be taken to Asia.
