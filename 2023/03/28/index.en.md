# Tuesday 28.3.2023 (radio)

Hungary ratifies Finland's NATO membership. Home voting is becoming more common. Maintenance charges rise. Police carry out intensive traffic monitoring. Pollen season approaches. Weather forecast.

## Hungary, Finland and NATO

![Hungarian parliament in session, interior picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4860,h_2733,x_0,y_27/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1086795641475496dd2a/1679062448)

The Hungarian Parliament finally voted with a clear majority to approve Finland's NATO membership. The vote had been expected for many months. Photo: KCS Presse / MEGA

The Hungarian Parliament has ratified, that is, approved, Finland's NATO membership.

Parliament voted on the matter on Monday evening after a short debate. 182 MPs voted for Finland's membership. Only 6 MPs opposed it.

Finland is still waiting for ratification from Turkey. Sweden is still waiting for ratification from Turkey and Hungary.

## Home voting

![Sinikka Frang from Lahti votes at home in the 2023 parliamentary election. Election official Niko Niemi holds ballot papers in his hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10909286421811d43a27/1679917814)

Open image viewer

Home voting in Lahti in the 2023 parliamentary election. Photo: Markku Lähdetluoma / Yle

There are more and more home voters.

For example, in Lahti more than 120 city residents will vote at home in these parliamentary elections. That is almost half more than in the last municipal elections.

The growth is partly because there are more older residents in home care.

In the last parliamentary election, in 2019, there were more than 10,000 home votes.

A caregiver who lives in the same household can also vote together with the home voter.

Advance voting for the parliamentary election ended today, on Tuesday. The actual election day is on Sunday, that is, 2 April.

## Maintenance charges

![Apartment blocks in an urban setting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2267,x_0,y_405/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-108623264131825dffec/1678973269)

At the general meetings of many housing companies, a decision must be made on a noticeable increase in the maintenance charge. Photo: Anna Polo / Yle

Housing companies intend to raise maintenance charges noticeably this year, according to a survey by the Finnish Real Estate Federation.

Housing companies estimate that maintenance charges will be raised by 8 percent on average compared with last year.

The charges are going up most in Helsinki and Turku, almost 10 percent. In Jyväskylä the increase is maybe 3 percent and in Lahti 4 percent.

## Intensive monitoring

![Police monitor and advise road users. This driver received a Liikenneturva leaflet and good-day wishes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8909244)

The police intensively monitored traffic at the weekend. Photo: Susanna Pekkarinen / Yle

The police monitored drunk driving in an intensive monitoring operation at the weekend.

In the monitoring 36,000 breath tests and 120 quick drug tests were carried out. 122 drivers were caught, of whom 88 are suspected of alcohol and 34 of drugs.

In the monitoring 17 people were found. A wanted notice has been issued for them.

## Pollen

![A man blows his nose, with falling catkins in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12370990)

Open image viewer

The pollen season begins in Finland when the wind brings pollen from the south. Photo: Jürgen Fälchle / Alamy / AOP

The Natural Resources Institute Finland forecasts that birch, spruce and pine will flower moderately this year.

People allergic to birch should prepare for a hard May, because birch flowering is abundant everywhere except the northernmost Finland.

Birch flowering in southern Finland usually starts at the turn of April and May. However, birch pollen can reach Finland from the south already in mid-April.

## The weather

![Finland's weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690055)

Photo: Yle

On Wednesday snow generally falls in the eastern and northern parts of the country. Especially in the east, and from south-eastern Finland to eastern Lapland, more than 20 cm of snow may fall.

In the south and west the weather on Wednesday is mostly fair, that is, it does not rain.

The temperature in the south is at zero, in the north about -5 degrees.
