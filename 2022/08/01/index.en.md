## Parental leave

![An adult holds a baby by the hand. Two hairy adult hands hold a baby in their lap.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5217,h_2934,x_3,y_539/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9365536246b04006d8e/1695214806)

We hope that both parents use parental leave equally much. Photo: Jasmina Kauta / Yle

Parental leave has been reformed. The reform came into force on Monday.

Parents who have a child get parental leave. During parental leave a parent can be away from work and look after the child, because they receive parental allowance.

The reform aims to increase equality. That is why both parents get 160 days of parental leave.

It is hoped that both parents use parental leave equally much. That is why a parent can give the other parent at most 63 days of their own parental leave.

Kela has made a plain-language brochure for families with children. The brochure has a lot of information about parental leave. The brochure can also be found on Kela's website.

## Ukraine's grain

![Port of Odesa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_160/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98877162e7780cec8b6/1659346055)

Open image viewer

The port of Odesa is important for grain shipments. Photo: Stella Pictures / AOP

Ukraine is again shipping grain on the Black Sea. The first grain ship left the port of Odesa on Monday.

Ukraine's grain ships have not been able to sail on the Black Sea since Russia attacked Ukraine. Russia's war in Ukraine has already lasted over 5 months.

Ukraine and Russia agreed in July that grain shipments on the Black Sea can start again.

The agreement is important, because Ukraine produces and exports a lot of grain. Many countries need grain from Ukraine.

## Firefighters to Greece

![Rescue chief Peter Johansson of the East Uusimaa rescue department packs things for the trip to Greece.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_215/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98874762e76e844c18d/1659334417)

Open image viewer

Rescue chief Peter Johansson packs equipment for the trip to Greece. Photo: Nina Svahn / Yle

On Monday firefighters left Finland for Greece. They are going to help put out the ground fires.

There are big ground fires in Greece, and the fire spreads quickly in hot weather. The temperature has often been over 40 degrees.

Greece hopes that the ground fires will not cause as much damage this summer as last summer. That is why it has asked for help from abroad to put out the fires.

England is the women's football European champion.

In the final of the European Championship, that is, the last match, England beat Germany 2-1.

The final was played on Sunday evening in London. There were over 87,000 spectators at Wembley Stadium.

There have never been so many spectators at a European Championship final before, not even in the men's European Championships.
