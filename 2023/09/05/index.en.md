## Veikkaus

![Veikkaus and lotto coupons at a kiosk counter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3780,h_2126,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7113235f43ba8d2d6ed/1598274267)

Veikkaus games are familiar to many people. Photo: Kiira Ikävalko-Lindqvist/Yle

Gambling company Veikkaus intends to reduce its staff. Jobs may end for as many as over 200 employees.

Veikkaus is also planning to reduce the number of gambling venues. In addition, it is being assessed whether the Tampere casino will be closed.

Veikkaus faces big changes. The reason for the changes is that gambling is to be opened up to competition in 2026.

Then other companies will also get the chance to organise gambling online. Now only Veikkaus may organise it.

## Construction industry

![Two people stand on a bare concrete floor at a construction site. Apartment blocks can be seen in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3965,h_2222,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116761264f6f6db243d2/1693907337)

Open image viewer

Construction has decreased in Finland. Photo: Juha Kokkala / Yle

The government is looking for ways to help the construction industry. The construction industry has problems because the building of homes has decreased a lot in Finland.

Now a working group has been set up in Finland to assess the situation. The working group must also assess how the problems of the construction industry affect the whole Finnish economy.

The construction industry says that construction will decrease quickly throughout the autumn.

## Food poisoning in Mikkeli

![A pile of tortillas.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5306,h_2985,x_0,y_182/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80806160a51180ce224/1693926010)

Open image viewer

The problems in Mikkeli may have been caused by tortillas. Illustrative image. Photo: Yle / Mikko Koski

Authorities have reported new information about the food poisoning in Mikkeli. Over 800 people had symptoms of food poisoning in Mikkeli schools in August.

The cause of the symptoms may have been tortilla wraps served at school lunches. The authorities do not yet know what was wrong with the tortilla wraps.

However, nobody had to go to hospital because of the food poisoning.

## Yle Language School

![Swedish-language journalist Jonatan Pedersen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1893,h_1064,x_6,y_5/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-111460264635a8e3a2da/1684233023)

Open image viewer

Translator Jonatan Pedersen runs Yle Language School.

Now, more about Yle Language School. Yle Language School offers a fun way to study Finnish.

At the language school, Finnish is studied by watching Finnish-language TV programmes.

The programmes also have Finnish subtitles, where the viewer gets a translation of difficult words.

The viewer can choose which language the translations are in. There are 14 languages.

In addition, Yle Language School has exercises. The language school is free.

Yle Language School can be used with an app or in a browser. More information is at yle.fi/kielikoulu.

## Floods in Greece

![Cars in flood water at a street corner in Pelonia, Greece.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_466/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116795764f7457b00c35/1693926915)

Open image viewer

Water has covered streets in Greece. Photo: Anastasia Karekla / Lehtikuva

There are floods in Greece. At least 1 person has died in the floods.

The cause of the floods is heavy rain. So much rain fell that streets went under water, and flood water has carried cars away.

In recent weeks there have been big forest fires in Greece. More than 20 people died in the fires.
