The summer holiday season is now in full swing.

Hiking and camping in Finland are of interest to foreigners. At the same time, there are many changes to the state's hiking spots.

Now we will tell you more about the hike.

## Camping and backpacking are popular

Camping is a lifestyle that attracts with its freedom. Most Finnish cities have camping areas.

Camping, motorhomes and “van life” are growing in popularity. Van life means, for example, converting a van into a mobile home.

Fewer cabins in camping areas are being booked now than before.

Finnish hiking and camping areas are increasingly attracting foreign tourists. The proportion of foreigners has been increasing every year.

They appreciate that you can go to Finnish campsites without making a reservation. This means you don't have to plan your trip in advance.

Foreigners also say that a hiker sees a lot. Then you can also see nature, not just cities.

## National parks and hiking areas

![Firewood on the campfire bench. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_625/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-16585806a297f829a194/1781105149)

In Finland, Metsähallitus manages all national parks. Metsähallitus also provides free hiking services.

Metsähallitus has now reduced maintenance of rest areas because there is not enough money. For example, many places no longer have firewood.

The Finnish Hiking Association warns that nature is suffering. People are collecting firewood from forests and walking outside hiking trails.

Some camping sites may later be transferred to the care of municipalities.

## Bush parks

![Motorhome in the parking lot. Forest behind.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4616,h_2596,x_0,y_58/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1294327665979948fc1b/1717140699)

Bush parks are becoming increasingly popular.

For example, bush camping means camping with a motorhome somewhere other than a camping area. Such places include beaches, forest edges, and rest areas.

Not everyone likes bush parks. Tourists sometimes cause disturbances and leave litter in the area.

In Finland, everyone has rights. This means that temporary residence and overnight stays are permitted wherever movement is permitted.

Your stay must not cause disturbance or harm to others or the environment.

However, movement in protected areas may be restricted.
