# Monday 6.4.2020 (radio)

There are more than 100 coronavirus infections in three Finnish municipalities. A coronavirus vaccine has been tested on volunteers. There has been congestion at the Uusimaa border. The winter has been hard for the pups.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare THL has reported on coronavirus infections in Finnish municipalities.

Infections are clearly most common in the capital region. Most coronavirus infections are in Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa. In all other cities there are fewer than 100 infections.

## Congestion at the Uusimaa border

![A police car in the picture at sunset.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11280687)

The police are monitoring crossing of the Uusimaa border. You may cross the border only if you have a good reason. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

On Monday morning at the Uusimaa border, people had to wait as long as 45 minutes. The Helsinki Police Department says that almost 13,800 cars drove to the checkpoints in the morning. Yesterday, on Sunday, 169 cars were turned back at the Uusimaa border.

The Uusimaa border has been closed because of the coronavirus.

## Predators eat Saimaa ringed seal pups

Finally, there is a story about the Saimaa ringed seal.

The winter has been hard for the Saimaa ringed seal pups. There is little snow, and a pup on the ice can be seen from far away. Predators are dangerous for the pups.

On the ice of Saimaa, 9 dead pups have already been found. A predator has killed 6 pups. In at least 3 cases the predator has been a fox.

White-tailed eagles can also kill pups. About 500 pairs of white-tailed eagles nest in Finland. White-tailed eagles do not yet nest on Saimaa, but there may be white-tailed eagles on their migration route there.

Researchers suspect that more than 20 percent of the pups die. Perhaps as many as 40 pups die.
