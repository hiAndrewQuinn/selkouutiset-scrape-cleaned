## Racism and Finns

![The Finnish flag flutters on a flagpole.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_254/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-109371864299d750639e/1687524993)

Finns have different opinions about racism. Photo: Tiina Grönroos / Yle

Finns' attitudes to racism now divide them a lot, says a new Yle survey.

About half of Finns say that Finns do not take racism seriously enough. About half disagree. They say that Finns take racism seriously enough.

Women and men often think about the matter in different ways. About 60 percent of women say that racism should be taken more seriously. Only about 30 percent of men say so.

Supporters of the Finns Party think about the matter differently from supporters of other parties. Only 2 percent of Finns Party supporters think that racism should be taken more seriously in Finland.

## Rent increases in Helsinki

![Apartment blocks in Jätkäsaari, Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_10,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98931562e8dc3c83f00/1659428335)

There will be big rent increases in the city's rental flats. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There will be a big rent increase in Helsinki city's rental flats at the start of next year. The city will raise rents by 10 percent on average. In some of the city's rental flats the increase may be smaller, and in some it may be bigger.

The biggest increases could be several hundred euros a month. That is a problem, because many people with low incomes live in the city's rental flats.

The city is making big rent increases because the city has a lot of debt and interest rates are rising. The city needs money so that it can pay the debts and the interest on the debts.

92,000 people live in Helsinki city's rental flats.

## The temperature of the seas

![All cod.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2045,h_1147,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-839025319739aa96cc/1645800597)

Cod have moved north. Photo: Peter Östlund

The temperature of the seas is rising quickly. The Atlantic Ocean is now record-warm in some places.

This is a big problem for sea animals. For example, many fish cannot live in water that is too warm, because there is no food for them in warm water.

Some fish have moved north, because the water is colder there. For example, cod and mackerel are fish that have moved closer to the North Pole.

The seas are warming because human activity has changed the climate.
