# Tuesday 26.3.2019 (radio)

Police suspect a man of assaulting an election candidate. Finns have many problems caused by gambling. Unemployment has decreased. The search for the victims of the African storm continues. The EU Parliament wants to give up changing the clocks. WWF has published a food recommendation.

## Police suspect a man of assaulting an election candidate

![Finns Party election sweets at the Korso country market in Vantaa on 24 March](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10706492)

Finns Party election sweets at the Korso country market in Vantaa on 24 March. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Police suspect that an election candidate for parliament was attacked in Helsinki.

The Left Alliance's parliamentary election candidate Suldaan Said Ahmed was at an election event in Helsinki's Itäkeskus yesterday, when an unknown man pushed her in the chest and insulted her.

The police suspect the man of minor assault and defamation. The police do not know who the man is. The police are searching for him.

At the weekend, Finland's Foreign Minister Timo Soini was almost hit at an election event in Vantaa. Soini's party is the Blue Future.

![café](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5020375b6adca89516a/1533730033)

The number of unemployed decreased in February. Statistics Finland says that at the end of February 246,000 unemployed people were looking for work. The number was 30,000 fewer than in February last year.

## Rescue work after the African storm continues

![Field destroyed by Cyclone Idai.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10707354)

Open image viewer

Destroyed farmland in the Tica area. Idai wreaked havoc in the city of Beira and nearby villages. Photo: Tiago Petinga / EPA

Rescue work and the search for the victims of the African storm continue. The storm hit Mozambique and its neighbouring countries more than a week ago.

More than 700 people are dead. The number of dead may be much higher. The UN says that thousands of people are still in mortal danger.

Almost a million people lost their homes. They will need help for a long time.

## Europe is abandoning summer and winter time

![Hands holding an alarm clock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10707712)

Open image viewer

Photo: Sebastien Nogier / EPA

The European Parliament supports abandoning summer and winter time.

Now the time is changed twice a year, by one hour forwards or backwards.

Using summer and winter time will be stopped in 2 years at the earliest.

## WWF published a food recommendation that helps the climate

![vegetable salad with falafel patties](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10706797)

Open image viewer

The climate impact of vegetarian food is smaller than that of meat dishes. Photo: AOP

The World Wide Fund for Nature, WWF, has published a food recommendation.

WWF says that the diet helps to stop global warming and is good for nature.

The recommendation says that a person should eat at most six eggs a month and red meat once a week. They may drink one glass of milk a day.

It is best to eat the most vegetables, Finnish fish and chicken. Finns now eat 20 percent more meat than 20 years ago.
