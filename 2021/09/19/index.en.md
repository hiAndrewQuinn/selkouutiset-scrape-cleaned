## Space tourists return

![Civilian crew on board a SpaceX rocket before the launch of the flight.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-85426361428f3245eef/1631752380)

Open image viewer

4 space tourists spent 3 days in space. Photo: AFP / SpaceX

The historic space flight has returned to Earth.

The flight was the first space flight to carry only space tourists. No professional astronauts were on board.

The SpaceX company's spacecraft had 4 space tourists on board. They orbited the Earth at an altitude of about 600 kilometres.

The spacecraft landed in the Atlantic Ocean near Florida on Sunday morning, Finnish time.

## Film award for Finland

![Film director Khadar Ayderus Ahmed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11969296)

Finnish film director Khadar Ayderus Ahmed has won an award. He won the award at the Toronto film festival in Canada.

Khadar Ayderus Ahmed received the award for the film Guled & Nasra. The film was given the Amplify Voices award, which promotes diversity in film art.

Guled & Nasra is a Somali-language film, and it was shot in Africa, in Djibouti.

## Singing as treatment

![Parkinson's patients' singing group](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10523196)

Open image viewer

Parkinson's patients' singing group in 2018. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

Singing can help people who have voice problems because of Parkinson's disease. This is shown by a University of Tampere study.

According to the study, singing makes the speech of Parkinson's patients clearer. The loudness of speech also increases.

The study followed Parkinson's patients who sing in a choir. Singing together was a good form of treatment for them, in addition to speech therapy.

Parkinson's disease often causes voice problems.

## Japan's elderly

![robot and elderly person](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10196435)

Open image viewer

Photo: Karsten Hohmann / Yle

Japan has a record number of elderly people.

Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs says that there are now over 36 million Japanese who are at least 65 years old.

About 30 per cent of Japanese are elderly. That is more than anywhere else in the world.
