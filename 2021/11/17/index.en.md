# Wednesday 17.11.2021 (radio)

There are many new corona infections. Estonia is putting up barbed wire on its eastern border. Lunch meals can teach people to eat vegetarian food. The Presidential Independence Day reception honours nurses. Weather forecast.

## The corona situation

![Health workers in Tornio take a corona test from a person crossing the border.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8407816113bb7a2a6e7/1628740347)

Many new corona infections have been found in Finland. More than 1150 corona infections were reported on Wednesday.

Most corona infections are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district. The second most corona infections are in North Ostrobothnia.

About 300 corona patients are in hospital care. 38 corona patients are in intensive care. The number of corona patients is growing quickly, especially in Uusimaa.

## Estonia prepares for the migrant crisis

![Polish border guards put up a barbed wire fence on the border against Belarus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12164031)

Open image viewer

Here Polish soldiers are putting up a barbed wire fence on the border between Poland and Belarus. Estonia also plans to put up a barbed wire fence on the border against Russia. Photo: Artur Reszko / EPA

The Estonian army is quickly calling up about 1700 reservists for military exercises. The reason is the migrant crisis in Belarus.

In the exercises, the reservists will put up 40 kilometres of barbed wire fence on the border between Estonia and Russia.

A barbed wire fence has already been built along part of Estonia's eastern border. A new barbed wire fence is now being put up in areas where illegal migrants could come into Estonia.

## Lunch meals and vegetarian food

![Workplace lunch](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5274156)

Open image viewer

Lunch meals teach people to eat healthily. Photo: YLE / Eijaleena Martikainen

Lunch meals at workplaces teach people to eat healthily. At the University of Helsinki, researchers have started to study how vegetarian food can be added to the lunch restaurants at workplaces and schools.

The researchers say that many large kitchens cannot yet cook vegetarian food well. That is why many vegetarian dishes do not taste good to people.

In the University of Helsinki study, new recipes have been made that suit large kitchens. The goal is that vegetarian food will become tasty in lunch restaurants. Then people will get used to eating more vegetarian food.

## The Presidential Independence Day reception

![President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11684290)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio at last year's reception at the Presidential Palace. Photo: Yle

The Presidential Independence Day reception is a smaller event than usual this year. Only about 200 people are invited to the reception. The reason is the corona situation.

The Presidential Independence Day reception is the President of the Republic's reception, which is held on Independence Day. President Sauli Niinistö says that this year the reception honours health care workers.

Niinistö says that the corona situation may still change how the event is arranged. Yle will show the Presidential Independence Day reception live on television on 6 December.
