# Tuesday 26.12.2017 (radio)

In some places, Christmas return traffic drove in bad weather. There is a severe shortage of blood donors. Northern lights may be seen at night. The Junior Lions' World Championship games begin. A Finnish Thing: the audience's favourites.

## Christmas return traffic drove in bad conditions

![Cars in traffic in snowy weather. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9994369)

Open image viewer

Driving conditions on St Stephen's Day were bad in places. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

On St Stephen's Day many Finns have come back home after celebrating Christmas.

The Christmas return traffic on the roads got busier after midday and quietened down again in the early evening.

The return traffic was made harder by bad driving conditions. During the day a rain area moved across Finland towards the east, and in the evening a new rain area came from the south-west. Driving conditions were bad in places in the eastern and central parts of the country.

There were a lot of cars on the roads, but no bad traffic jams formed. That is because many people are also on holiday during the days between Christmas and New Year, that is, until the New Year.

## The Red Cross needs blood donors

![Blood donation at the Blood Service office in Jyväskylä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9967937)

Open image viewer

Finland needs 800 blood donors every weekday. Photo: Eveliina Matikainen / Yle

The Finnish Red Cross urgently needs blood donors.

The Finnish Red Cross Blood Service is short of blood of almost all blood groups. Blood of the blood group O- is needed most.

You can go and donate blood on weekdays between Christmas and New Year. Blood donation points are open in over 20 towns. You can check the places and times in more detail on the Red Cross Blood Service website.

Finland needs 800 blood donors every weekday. Then there are enough blood products for all patients.

## Northern lights can now be seen in the north

![Blue and violet can be seen in the northern lights soon after sunset and in stronger auroral storms.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9166770)

Open image viewer

Northern lights may be seen in the coming days. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

In the coming days, northern lights may be seen in the night sky.

Forecasts say that the solar winds have become faster. They can cause northern lights in the central and northern parts of the country.

The weather forecast says that the cloud cover in the north will break up. That improves the chances of seeing northern lights.

## The junior ice hockey World Championships begin

![Miro Heiskanen and Eeli Tolvanen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9982545)

Open image viewer

Miro Heiskanen and Eeli Tolvanen play in the Junior Lions. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

The junior ice hockey World Championships begin today, Tuesday, in the United States. Finland is represented at the championships by the Junior Lions, that is, young players under 20.

Finland is one of the favourites before the championships.

Two years ago the World Championships were played in Finland. Then Finland won the world championship. But a year ago Finland's games ended already in the relegation round.

Finland's first game is on Tuesday evening against Canada. The game starts at 11 pm Finnish time. You can watch it live on TV2.
