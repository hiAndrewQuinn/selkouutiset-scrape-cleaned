## The Greens on cannabis

![Greens' party congress.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_100/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-852713613c5c20a6f24/1631345798)

Open image viewer

Cannabis was also discussed at the Greens' party congress. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The Greens want cannabis to be allowed in Finland. The Greens held their party congress on Sunday.

Cannabis is currently an illegal product in Finland. The law says that cannabis is a narcotic that may not be used or sold.

In the Greens' view, the use and sale of cannabis must be allowed. Then the state could also collect taxes from the sale of cannabis, the Greens say.

## Elections in Norway

![Erna Solberg](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11876423)

Open image viewer

Opinion polls say that Prime Minister Erna Solberg could lose the election on Monday. Photo: Valda Kalnina / EPA

Norway, Finland's neighbour, has parliamentary elections on Monday.

Opinion polls say that the Prime Minister of Norway could change after the election. Norway's current Prime Minister is Erna Solberg. Solberg is from the conservative party of the right.

However, in the opinion polls the most support has gone to the left-wing Labour Party. Norway's next prime minister may therefore be the leader of the Labour Party.

## Film award for Finns

![Wheelchair-bound Petri Poikolainen gets help before a scene.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1686,h_948,x_86,y_91/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-852039613a6bd8c0b63/1631218762)

Open image viewer

Actor Petri Poikolainen (in the middle). Photo: Niina Virtanen

A Finnish film has received an important award. The film won the prize at the Venice Film Festival in Italy.

The title of the film is *Sokea mies joka ei halunnut nähdä Titanicia*. It won a competition at the Venice Film Festival in which the audience chooses the winner.

The film is about a man who is blind and paralysed. He cannot move. However, the man gets help from others when he wants to set off because of love.

The film's director is Teemu Nikki. The man in the film is played by the actor Petri Poikolainen. Poikolainen is also really blind, and he cannot move because of an illness.

The film *Sokea mies joka ei halunnut nähdä Titanicia* is now also showing in Finnish cinemas.

## Rovanperä's rally win

![Kalle Rovanperä drives at the Greek WRC Rally.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_344/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-852896613df83f07981/1631451233)

Open image viewer

Kalle Rovanperä was the fastest at the Greek WRC Rally. Photo: imago images/PanoramiC/ All Over Press

Finnish rally driver Kalle Rovanperä has won a big rally competition. Rovanperä won the Greek WRC Rally, which ended on Sunday.

After the win in the Greek WRC Rally, Rovanperä will fight for medals in the whole WRC series. Rovanperä is now 4th in the rally WRC series standings.

The next race in the series will be run in October in Finland, in the Jyväskylä area.

In Western Finland the weather turns dry, that is, the rain stops. At the same time, however, the wind strengthens.

The temperature in Finland during the early part of the week is between 5 and 15 degrees.
