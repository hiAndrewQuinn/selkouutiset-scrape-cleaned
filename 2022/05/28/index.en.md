## The timetable of the NATO application

![Pekka Haavisto and Antony Blinken.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4166,h_2343,x_313,y_194/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9612336291b545a0c53/1653716396)

Finland's Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto and USA's Foreign Minister Antony Blinken. Photo: Nicholas Kamm / AFP

Finland's Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto is worried about the timetable of Finland's NATO application.

Haavisto told about the matter in the USA, where he visited on Friday. Haavisto talked with USA's Foreign Minister Antony Blinken.

Finland and Sweden have applied for membership of the military alliance NATO. However, Turkey opposes their membership.

Turkey thinks that Finland and Sweden protect Kurdish terrorists. Finland and Sweden must settle their disputes with Turkey, so that the handling of their NATO applications can begin.

Finland hopes that the disputes with Turkey can be settled before the NATO summit. The summit is at the end of June.

The travel industry expects a busy summer.

Finns intend to travel a lot in their own country, even though there are now few corona restrictions on trips abroad.

The travel industry says that Finns, for example, rent summer cottages in their own country. New experiences and interesting things to do are also hoped for from trips within the country.

Finland plays in the final of the ice hockey World Championship on Sunday, that is, the last game.

Finland's team, the Lions, reached the final when it beat the USA on Saturday. Finland won the game 4-3.

The final is very important for Finns now, when the ice hockey World Championship is in Finland. The medal games are played in Tampere.

The final is played late on Sunday evening. Then it will become clear whether Finland becomes world champion.

## Clear Language Day

![Clear-language novels on the shelf of the Seinäjoki City Library.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9771154)

Open image viewer

Many famous books have also been made into an easy-language version. Photo: Hanne Leiwo/Yle

Today, Saturday, is the International Clear Language Day. Clear Language Day is celebrated on 28 May.

Clear language is easier than standard language. Clear language is needed so that everyone can get information. It is estimated that 750 000 people in Finland need clear language.

Yle makes news in clear Finnish and also in clear Swedish. Yle's news in clear Swedish can be found on Areena, and the radio programme is called Yle Nyheter på lätt svenska.

Newspapers and books are also published in clear language. Selkosanomat is a news paper and Leija is a clear-language magazine.

Clear-language books are about many different themes. Last winter, for example, the first clear-language book about dinosaurs was published.

The author of the dinosaur book, Maija Karala, says that science belongs to everyone. That is why she wanted to write about dinosaurs in clear language.

Happy Clear Language Day to everyone!
