# Sunday 9.5.2021 (radio)

Mother's Day. Grandparents. Corona vaccinations. Traffic accident in Sastamala. Train travel to the European Championship matches. Weather.

## Mother's Day

![President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_294/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-804120609789d4add3c/1620543997)

President Sauli Niinistö congratulated mothers. Photo: Jon Norppa / Office of the President of the Republic

Mother's Day was celebrated on Sunday.

President Sauli Niinistö congratulated mothers in a video. The president shared the video on Twitter.

Niinistö said that mothers are important for the whole of Finland. Mothers build the future of Finland together with their children.

President Niinistö is also honouring mothers this year. 29 mothers will receive a medal of honour from the president. Because of the corona situation, the medals will be handed out to the mothers later.

## Grandparents

![Seija Vahvelainen hugs her granddaughter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4373,h_2460,x_0,y_164/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8027916093a12d37f8d/1620287970)

Open image viewer

Seija Vahvelainen often looks after her grandchild Varpu. Photo: Hanna Terävä / Yle

Grandparents look after their grandchildren more than before.

In 2007, less than 30 percent of grandparents thought that looking after grandchildren is the duty of grandparents. Ten years later, almost 50 percent of grandparents thought so.

Nowadays there is no longer a big difference between grandmothers and grandfathers. Grandfathers also look after many grandchildren.

Corona vaccinations are progressing well. About 35 percent of Finns have already received a corona vaccination.

## Traffic accident

![Close-up of a police uniform.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2016,h_1134,x_0,y_69/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8031456093e16ca0c91/1625127777)

Open image viewer

The police suspect that the cause of the accident was speeding. Photo: Johanna Manu / Yle

There has been a serious traffic accident in Sastamala. 3 people died in the accident. In addition, 3 people were seriously injured.

The accident happened in an overtaking situation, in which 2 cars collided with each other. After that, a motorcycle also crashed into the cars.

The accident happened in Sastamala on Saturday evening. It is 50 kilometres from Sastamala to Tampere.

## European Football Championship

![Allegro train in St Petersburg.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11220955)

Open image viewer

Maybe you can get to see Finland's European Championship matches in St Petersburg by train. Photo: Kimmo Brandt / AOP

The government intends to open train traffic to Russia for the time of the European Football Championship. Because of the corona, trains do not currently run to Russia.

The European football championship is played in June and July. Finland plays in a group whose matches are in St Petersburg.

The government plans for Finns to be able to travel by train to watch Finland's matches. The government may decide on the matter on Wednesday.

## Weather

![Weather map: the weather warms in the coming week, from Tuesday onwards the temperature rises widely to summer levels.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11922969)

Open image viewer

The closest to heat-level temperatures is expected on Wednesday in the west. Photo: Yle Sää/Matti Huutonen

In Southern Finland the week starts with rainy weather. In Northern Finland Monday is a dry day, and no rain falls there.

On Tuesday the rain moves north, and warm air comes to the south. On Tuesday the temperature in Southern Finland is about 20 degrees.

On Wednesday and Thursday the weather warms up further. In Southern Finland and Central Finland the temperature is over 20 degrees.
