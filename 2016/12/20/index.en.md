## The Berlin attack

![A picture of central Berlin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9363297)

Open image viewer

The attack happened at a Christmas market. Photo: EPA

A serious attack has happened in Germany's capital Berlin. 12 people died when a lorry drove into people. About 50 people were injured.

The police say that the lorry drove into people on purpose. This happened on Monday evening at a Christmas market in central Berlin.

The police arrested a man near the scene. The police suspected that the man is the attacker. On Tuesday the police said that this is not certain. The man may not be the attacker after all.

The police investigations continue.

## Saara's plans

![Saara Aalto at a press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9364348)

Open image viewer

Saara Aalto in Helsinki on 20.12.2016. Photo: Ville Vedenpää/Yle

Singer Saara Aalto has told about her plans. Aalto came to Finland from Britain on Tuesday. In Britain, Saara Aalto came second in the X Factor music competition.

Saara Aalto will soon start making a new album. She will also perform together with other X Factor stars in different parts of Britain.

Saara Aalto will also perform in Finland. At Easter she has a concert at Helsinki Areena.

Fans can see Saara already next week in Oulu. On Wednesday there is an event at Oulu's Market Square where Saara performs. At the event she will get a 5000-euro grant from the City of Oulu. Saara Aalto comes from Oulu.

## Lending workers

![A ship at a harbour quay](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5318268)

Open image viewer

The port of Loviisa. Photo: Yle

In Finland, a new kind of cooperation is being tried at the port of Loviisa. There, 2 firms lend workers to each other.

The goal is that workers have work also in bad times and the firms have enough workers in busy times.

The cooperation works well in Loviisa. The firms are close to each other, and the workers always come to work in the same area.

For the workers, the exchange is voluntary, but most of them have joined it.

## Cruises on icebreakers

![Tourists boarding the tourist icebreaker Sampo in Ajos, Kemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8639089)

Open image viewer

The icebreaker Sampo. Photo: Risto Koskinen / Yle

In the north, on the Bay of Bothnia, cruises are organised on icebreakers. Big icebreakers interest, for example, Asian tourists.

On the Finnish side, the icebreaker Sampo has carried tourists for 30 years already. Nowadays the ship is always full.

Now icebreaker cruises are also starting on the Swedish side. There too, tourists from Asia are expected for the icebreaker.

## Christmas peace in traffic

![On the left the police reindeer Artturi, Lapland police chief superintendent Rauno Pätsi, and on Santa Claus's right Trafi director general Kari Wihlman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9364509)

The police reindeer Artturi and Santa Claus were also there when Christmas peace was declared in traffic. Photo: Raimo Torikka / Yle

Christmas peace has begun in Finland's traffic. The police declared Christmas peace on Tuesday in Rovaniemi. The police hope that everyone follows the traffic rules, so that traffic flows safely.

Santa Claus and the police reindeer Artturi were also at the event.
