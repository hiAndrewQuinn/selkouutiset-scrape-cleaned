# Monday 26.12.2022 (radio)

Police are investigating the fire at Rautjärvi church. Floods in the Philippines. The storm is still disrupting traffic in the USA. It will snow in southern and central Finland on Tuesday.

## Rautjärvi church fire

![Aerial photo of Rautjärvi church on a summer day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4052,h_2280,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105292163a8166ee7613/1671967145)

The police have given a few more details about the fire at Rautjärvi church at a press briefing. Rautjärvi church in South Karelia was completely destroyed by fire on Sunday.

It is not yet known where the fire started. The fire spread quickly.

The police suspect that someone deliberately set the fire. The police are investigating the church fire as aggravated arson.

The police confirm that someone had put ropes or cords on the church's side doors. Someone had probably put the ropes there so that it would be difficult to get out of the church.

No one was injured in the Rautjärvi church fire.

## Floods in the Philippines

![Philippine coast guard rescuers evacuate a child from the flood area.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2000,h_1125,x_0,y_34/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105297263a931d4ce919/1672032912)

More than 46,000 people have been moved out of the way of the floodwater in the Philippines. Photo: Philippine Coast Guard

More than 46,000 people have been moved out of the way of the floodwater in the Philippines. At least 11 people have died and about 20 people are missing.

It has been raining a lot in the Philippines for many weeks already.

Water has risen into villages and onto roads, among other places near the towns of Ozamiz and Oroquieta. Telephone connections are down. People have been rescued from house roofs.

## The storm in the USA

![People cross the street in Mile End, a district of Montreal, Quebec.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_527/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105285663a6c9ad2b5f7/1671875082)

Open image viewer

There has been a winter storm in North America. This picture is from Canada. Photo: Andrej Ivanov / AFP

In the USA the storm is still disrupting traffic.

On Sunday, more than 2,800 flights were cancelled in the USA because of the storm. On Christmas Eve about 3,500 flights were cancelled, and on the day before Christmas Eve almost 6,000 flights. Buses have also run late and services have been cancelled.

In many US states the authorities have said that people should stay at home.

As a result of the winter storm, at least 32 people have died in the USA in 9 different states.

The weather on Tuesday is cloudy.

In the south and central parts of the country it snows in many places. The temperature varies between 0 and -15 degrees.

Driving conditions will become very poor at night because of snowfall in the regions of Uusimaa and Kymenlaakso.
