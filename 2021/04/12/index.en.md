## Corona vaccinations

![Syringes used for corona vaccination at a vaccination point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_278/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7913106066c59b74eff/1617348264)

In Lieto, vaccinations of people over 70 are being completed. Illustrative image. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Corona vaccinations are progressing well in Finland. About 20 percent of Finns have already been vaccinated against corona at least once.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that more corona vaccines will come to Finland later in the spring than before. In May, 300,000 corona vaccines will arrive in Finland every week.

All risk groups will perhaps receive the corona vaccination by the middle of May. After that, vaccinations for people of working age will begin.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare estimates that by early July, everyone who wants a corona vaccination will have received the first dose.

## Face-to-face teaching

![Katriina Raine teaches an expression lesson remotely.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11835344)

Open image viewer

Remote teaching has continued for a long time in many schools. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Face-to-face teaching is starting again in many schools.

Many lower secondary schools, upper secondary schools and vocational schools have given students remote teaching because of corona. So students have stayed at home and studied through a computer.

Now students can go back to school for face-to-face teaching.

## Ramadan

![On a sales table there are baklava pastries. Behind the table stand two men who work at the bakery.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11877684)

Open image viewer

During the fasting month, food is eaten only after sunset. Baklava is eaten at the evening meals, for example. Photo: AOP

The Muslim fasting month of Ramadan begins.

In Finland, Ramadan begins tomorrow, Tuesday. Ramadan ends on 12 May.

Ramadan involves fasting. During the fast, nothing is eaten or drunk while the sun is up.

Many Muslim leaders around the world say that the corona vaccine can also be taken during the fast. Taking the corona vaccine does not break the fast.

After Ramadan, the Eid al-Fitr festival is celebrated. In Finland, Eid al-Fitr is on 13 May this year.

## Heat record

![People outdoors in the harbour of Lappeenranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5254,h_2955,x_0,y_25/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-79367560742f9b405eb/1618227171)

Open image viewer

Monday was a warm day. Photo: Kalle Purhonen / Yle

A spring heat record was measured on Monday. The record is 17.1 degrees. This spring's heat record was measured in Parikkala, in Eastern Finland.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that the all-time heat record for April is 25.5 degrees. It was measured 100 years ago in Jyväskylä.
