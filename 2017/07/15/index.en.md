# Saturday 15.7.2017 (radio)

Paavo Väyrynen is aiming to become a presidential candidate. Exactly one year has passed since the coup attempt in Turkey. Leo-Pekka Tähti has won a world championship gold medal. The weather gets colder next week.

## Paavo Väyrynen

![Paavo Väyrynen intends to run in the presidential election through a supporters' association.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9725140)

Paavo Väyrynen is aiming to become a presidential candidate. Väyrynen has been part of Finnish politics for over 40 years. For example, he has been a minister many times.

Väyrynen is a member of the Centre Party, and last year he founded the Citizens' Party. The Citizens' Party wants Finland to leave the euro area.

In the presidential election, however, Väyrynen intends to be the candidate of an electoral association. The association can be founded if it gets 20,000 supporters.

## Turkey

![Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9718331)

Open image viewer

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan. Photo: Daniel Kopatsch / EPA

Exactly one year has passed since the coup attempt in Turkey.

A year ago, a group of army soldiers tried to take power in Turkey. They were of the opinion that Recep Tayyip Erdoğan is a bad president. Erdoğan intends to end democracy, they said.

The coup attempt did not succeed. Erdoğan's opponents were arrested, and Erdoğan continued as President of Turkey.

After that, President Erdoğan has been looking for those to blame. More than 50,000 people have been arrested in Turkey. Thousands of soldiers and police have been detained.

In addition, about 140,000 people have lost their jobs. In Erdoğan's opinion, they supported the coup attempt. For example, many teachers, judges and journalists are now without work.

Many Turks think that the search for those to blame has gone too far.

## Leo-Pekka Tähti

![Leo-Pekka Tähti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9725059)

Open image viewer

Leo-Pekka Tähti won the 100-metre wheelchair race at the World Championships in London. Photo: AOP

Finland's Leo-Pekka Tähti has won a gold medal in the World Para Athletics Championships in London. Athletes with disabilities take part in the competition.

Tähti won the 100-metre wheelchair race. Tähti's wheelchair broke during the race, but he came first to the finish line.

This was the third time Tähti became world champion. He has also been European champion many times. Tähti has also won gold medals at the Paralympics, the Olympics for athletes with disabilities.

Last winter Leo-Pekka Tähti was chosen Athlete of the Year 2016. Tähti is the first athlete with a disability to be chosen Athlete of the Year in Finland.

## The weather in the coming weeks

![People out walking in fog at Eiranranta, Helsinki, 12 July 2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9725206)

The weekend is warm in Finland, but next week the weather gets colder again.

Weather forecasts say that the summer is colder than usual also in the coming weeks. Although it is really hot in Southern Europe, hot air does not come as far as Finland.
