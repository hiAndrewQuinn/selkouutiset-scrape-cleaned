## Number of young people

![Several young people stand in a group. Various lights in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_504/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-79870460856d04e70ef/1673503675)

The number of under-15s in Finland falls by a hundred thousand by 2035. Photo: Tanja Heino / Yle

The number of children and young people is falling in Finland faster and more than has been estimated. Only in about 20 municipalities does the number of under-15s grow by 2035.

Regional researcher Timo Aro calculated the development of the number of under-35s. The background was Statistics Finland's population forecast.

Aro says that the result surprised everyone.

The ageing of Finland's population has been known. However, the speed of the change keeps accelerating all the time. The shortage of labour gets worse in the 2040s.

The number of children and young people decreases for the first time in 250 years. That is the time from which there is population data. In Finland fewer children are born than at any time since the famine years of the 1860s.

## Climate conference

![Journalists work in the press facilities of the Glasgow climate conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_134/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-874065617f71ae7f77b/1635742272)

The Prime Minister of Britain warns that the fight against climate change will fail if the Glasgow climate conference does not succeed. Boris Johnson says that the countries that are most responsible for climate change are still not doing enough climate action.

Prime Minister Johnson arrived in the morning at the international meeting in Glasgow, Scotland. There a mid-term review is being made of how the goals of the Paris climate agreement of 2015 are being achieved.

The current decisions lead the Earth toward a warming of a little over 2 degrees from pre-industrial times.

However, the warming must be stopped at 1.5 degrees.

## Explosion in Varkaus

![Traces of a fire on Kauppalankatu in Varkaus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12168598)

An explosion-like fire happened on the second floor of the apartment block. Photo: North Savo Rescue Department

One person has died in an apartment block explosion in Varkaus in North Savo. The explosion happened late on Sunday evening.

The police say that the person who died caused the explosion themselves. However, it was not about explosives.

4 people were taken to hospital care from the building. They were lightly injured.

## Remote work and sleep

![Toes of a sleeping person.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11331721)

Open image viewer

Expert employees have slept better when they have been able to work remotely. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

Remote work has made expert employees sleep better than before. In the morning it has been possible to sleep longer, because no time has been spent on commuting.

The Finnish Institute of Occupational Health made a survey on the matter. 42 per cent of experts assess that sleep has improved during remote work.

Women's sleep had improved more often than men's.

On average the respondents slept 7 hours. However, about 20 per cent still slept less than 6 hours.
