# Tuesday 22.1.2019 (radio)

Visas may have been granted on false information. France and Germany made a friendship agreement. Greenland is melting quickly. E-books are popular. Hard frost continues in Northern Finland.

## Visas may have been granted on false information

![Schengen visa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8649372)

Open image viewer

Finnish authorities suspect that visas to Finland have been granted in Turkey on the basis of false information. Photo: schengenvisainfo.com

Finnish authorities suspect that visas to Finland have been granted in Turkey on the basis of false information.

The authorities suspect that 3 employees are involved in the case. They work at the Finnish embassy in Ankara, the capital of Turkey.

The employees may have granted hundreds of visas to Turkish people that contain false information. Someone who would not otherwise get a visa may also have received one on false information.

## France and Germany are friendly countries

![Macron and Merkel sit behind a writing desk and hold a document in their hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10608379)

French President Emmanuel Macron and German Chancellor Angela Merkel signed the agreement. Photo: Friedemann Vogel / EPA

France and Germany have made a new friendship agreement.

French President Emmanuel Macron and German Chancellor Angela Merkel signed the agreement today. France and Germany have had a similar agreement for almost 60 years.

The countries cooperate. For example, France and Germany want Europe to have its own common army.

## Greenland is melting quickly

![An iceberg in front of a Greenlandic village.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10304789)

Open image viewer

A melted iceberg drifted out to sea in front of a village in Greenland. Photo: Magnus Christenssen / EPA

American researchers say that Greenland is melting faster than previously estimated.

Bigger and bigger pieces keep breaking off Greenland's glaciers. The pieces melt in the Atlantic Ocean.

Researchers say that meltwater raises the sea level a great deal. The rise in sea level is dangerous, for example, for islands in the Pacific Ocean.

## Tomorrow there will be only a little frost in Southern Finland

![Ice crystals on a window and a sunset behind them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10608592)

Open image viewer

There is a lot of beauty in nature in winter. Photo: Risto Koskinen / Yle

Today, Tuesday, the weather was cold. There was hard frost across the whole country.

Because of the hard frost and the drifting snow, trains had difficulties. Trains were late and many train services were cancelled.

Tomorrow, Wednesday, Southern Finland will be cloudy, and the frost will be only a few degrees.

In Northern Finland the sun will shine and the frost will be over 20 degrees. .
