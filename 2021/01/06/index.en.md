## Corona vaccine

![Corona vaccine doses on a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_264/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7555905fdbef4de00ed/1609786470)

The European Union intends to also start using a second corona vaccine.

The European Medicines Agency suggests that the EU gives a marketing authorisation to the corona vaccine of the American drug company Moderna. The European Commission decides on the marketing authorisation.

The President of the Commission, Ursula von der Leyen, promises that the Commission will make the decision quickly.

When the Moderna corona vaccine gets a marketing authorisation, it will also be sent to Finland. However, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare estimates that at first only small amounts of the Moderna corona vaccine will be available.

At the moment the Pfizer and BioNTech corona vaccine is used in EU countries. The vaccinations started after Christmas.

## Keskisuomalainen

![The front page of the Keskisuomalainen newspaper online.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10933534)

Open image viewer

Photo: Antti Seppälä / Yle

The newspaper Keskisuomalainen turns 150 years old this week.

Keskisuomalainen is the oldest Finnish-language newspaper that is still published. It first came out in Jyväskylä in January 1871.

The oldest newspaper in Finland at the moment is Åbo Underrättelser. It is a Swedish-language newspaper from Turku.

## World Championship bronze

![Russia's Roman Bychkov tries to keep Anton Lundell away from goalkeeper Jaroslav Askarov.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11725520)

Open image viewer

Finland's team captain Anton Lundell (in blue) at the Russian goal. Photo: Codie McLachlan/Getty Images

Finland has been successful in ice hockey. Finland won a bronze medal in the World Championships for men under 20 years old, that is, the world championship competition.

The Finnish team, Nuoret Leijonat, beat Russia in the bronze medal match.

Finland won the bronze medal match 4-1. Finland's goal scorers were Anton Lundell, Mikko Petman and Juuso Pärssinen.

So Finland came 3rd. The gold medal went to the USA. The USA beat Canada in the final match.

The junior ice hockey World Championships were held in Canada.

## Epiphany

![Christmas trees in a rubbish shelter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8575012)

Open image viewer

On Epiphany it is time to take the Christmas tree to the rubbish. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

In Finland Epiphany was celebrated on Wednesday. Epiphany is the last Christmas-time celebration.

Many Finns collect their Christmas decorations away on Epiphany or soon after Epiphany. In many homes the Christmas tree is also thrown away right on Epiphany.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11725859)

Open image viewer

On Friday it is dry and frosty. Photo: Yle

Epiphany was celebrated in beautiful winter weather. Across the whole country it is frosty, and in the south too there is snow.

On Thursday more snow will fall in Southern Finland. Because of the snowfall, the driving conditions on main roads may be bad.

On Friday the snowfalls in the south end, and across the whole country it is dry. The frost also continues across the whole country.
