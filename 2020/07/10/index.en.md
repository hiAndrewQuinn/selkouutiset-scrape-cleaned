The Ministry of Education is carrying out a review of S2 teaching in schools. It is good to check the tax percentage if the corona crisis changed the work situation. Hypo estimates that the Finnish economy will shrink. There was an error in the law entrance exams.

## S2 teaching

![Pupils doing tasks at a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10412004)

Open image viewer

Teaching of the Finnish language is to be improved. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

The Ministry of Education is carrying out a review of S2 teaching in schools. In S2 teaching, Finnish is taught as a second language to pupils whose mother tongue is not Finnish.

There are shortcomings in S2 teaching. The amount and content of teaching are not the same in all schools. That is why the situation of S2 teaching will be examined.

In addition, the Ministry of Education is ordering municipalities to provide S2 teaching already in day care centres.

Over 35,000 comprehensive school pupils study Finnish as a second language.

## Taxation

![Tax office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10945241)

Open image viewer

Tax matters can be handled at a tax office or on the internet. Photo: Linus Hoffman / Yle

Many people's work situation has changed because of corona. In that case it is good to apply for a change in taxation as well.

Many people have had less work than usual during the corona era. That is why they have also received less pay. In that case it is worth recalculating the tax percentage, so that too much tax is not paid.

Many have also worked from home. In that case some expenses can be deducted in taxation. Such expenses may come, for example, from work equipment and internet connections.

Many employees will find out after the summer whether work continues as normal again. At the latest then, it is good to check the tax percentage as well. Tax matters can also be handled online at omavero.fi.

## The Finnish economy

![Shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10666648)

Open image viewer

Hypo forecasts that the economy will grow when people start spending money again. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The Finnish economy will shrink this year. This is the estimate of the credit institution Hypo.

Hypo estimates that the Finnish economy will shrink by 7 per cent this year. The economy will shrink a lot because of the corona crisis.

However, Hypo forecasts that the Finnish economy will grow already next year. Hypo forecasts growth of 4 per cent for next year.

According to Hypo, the economy will start to grow when people start spending money again. That will happen if there is no second corona crisis in Finland and no more unemployed people, Hypo estimates.

There was an error in the law entrance exam. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There has been an error in the university entrance exam. The error concerns the field of law.

The points in the entrance exam were calculated wrongly in an automatic check. Now the error will be corrected, and the points will be calculated again.

However, no applicant will lose the study place they have already been told about.
