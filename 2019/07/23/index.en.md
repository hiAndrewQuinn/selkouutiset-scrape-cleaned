# Tuesday 23.7.2019 (radio)

Boris Johnson is becoming Britain's new Prime Minister. Employment is now improving slowly. A serious traffic accident happened in Peräseinäjoki. The traffic statistics for June are grim. The raspberry harvest is going to be very good.

## Boris Johnson is becoming Britain's Prime Minister

![Boris Johnson giving a speech.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10888358)

Open image viewer

Boris Johnson is becoming Britain's new Prime Minister. Photo: Neil Hall / EPA

In Britain the Conservative Party has got a new leader. He is the former Foreign Secretary and Mayor of London, Boris Johnson.

Johnson is also becoming Prime Minister. He has promised to take the British out of the EU at the end of October, even without an agreement.

Johnson won two thirds of the party members' votes. He clearly beat his rival candidate, Jeremy Hunt.

Johnson will be officially appointed Prime Minister on Wednesday. Before that, the current Prime Minister Theresa May will submit her resignation to Queen Elizabeth II.

The outgoing Theresa May congratulated Johnson on social media.

## Employment is improving slowly

![A man works in a factory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10824119)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Employment is now improving slowly. Statistics Finland says that the employment rate trend in June was 72.5 percent. The trend is a figure from which fluctuations have been removed.

In practice the employment situation has stayed the same all year so far.

The unemployment rate was 6.5 percent in June, the same as in May.

Even so, there were 16,000 fewer unemployed people than in June last year.

## There was a traffic accident in Peräseinäjoki

![The accident site in Peräseinäjoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10889527)

Open image viewer

There is a bend at the accident site where the speed limit is 60 km/h. Photo: Anne Elhaimer / Yle

A serious traffic accident happened in Peräseinäjoki in South Ostrobothnia on Tuesday morning. 11 people were involved in the collision between a minibus and a passenger car.

It was a head-on collision, and the speed was high.

Several people were seriously injured and some slightly. Thai berry pickers were on board the minibus. Many ambulances and a rescue helicopter were needed at the scene.

The driver of the passenger car is suspected of aggravated drink driving.

## Many people have died in traffic this summer

![A lorry passes the crash site, where one person died and three were seriously injured in a violent head-on collision between two passenger cars on 13 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10836838)

A lorry passes the crash site, where one person died and three were seriously injured in a violent head-on collision between two passenger cars on 13 June. Photo: Kalle Parkkinen / Lehtikuva

More people died in traffic in June than at the same time last year. S

tatistics Finland says that 24 people died and 504 were injured in traffic. There were 15 more deaths than in June last year. There were 36 fewer injured.

Most of the people who died were travelling by passenger car.

## The raspberry harvest is good

![Close-up of a raspberry](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9755339)

Open image viewer

The raspberry came through the winter and flowering well and is now producing a good harvest. Photo: Heli Pirinen

The raspberry harvest is going to be very good. The Finnish Fruit and Berry Growers' Association says that the raspberry came through the winter well. The flowering this summer also succeeded.

In addition, a good harvest is expected for highbush blueberries. The picking of highbush blueberries is starting across the whole country.

The Natural Resources Institute Finland (Luke) has estimated that the wild bilberry harvest will be average at best. Highbush blueberries and wild bilberries are different species.

A good harvest, however, is expected for lingonberries.
