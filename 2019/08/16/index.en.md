# Friday 16.8.2019 (radio)

Alcohol and tobacco use among young people. SDP MP Hussein al-Taee. Fast food restaurants and plastic. A good summer for butterflies. The weather warms up.

## Sober young people

![A young woman drinking beer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5906914)

Open image viewer

Young people now drink less alcohol than 20 years ago. Photo: Antti Karhunen / Yle

Finnish young people have been asked in a study how they relate to alcohol and tobacco.

40 per cent of girls say that they do not drink alcohol or smoke tobacco at all.

43 per cent of boys say that they do not drink alcohol or smoke tobacco at all.

They are sober. A sober person does not drink alcohol at all. The number of sober young people has fallen a little.

The situation is still better now than, for example, 20 years ago. Back then there were far fewer sober young people.

## SDP does not punish al-Taee

![MP Hussein al-Taee in Parliament on 23 July 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10890098)

Open image viewer

Hussein al-Taee is an SDP MP. Photo: Kimmo Hiltunen / Yle

The SDP parliamentary group will not punish MP Hussein al-Taee.

Today the SDP parliamentary group discussed writings that al-Taee had posted on social media before he became an MP. He wrote unpleasantly, for example, about Sunni Muslims, Jews and gay people.

The old writings came to light after al-Taee had been elected as an MP.

## Fast food restaurants reduce plastic

![Plastic straws](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10923983)

Open image viewer

Fast food restaurants are reducing the use of plastic straws, for example. Photo: Tapio Termonen / Yle

Fast food restaurants are reducing the use of plastic.

For example, drinking straws and cup lids are kept hidden behind the counter. If a customer wants a straw and a lid, they must ask for them.

Hesburger restaurants have tested paper straws. Paper straws can be recycled.

## Butterflies have had a good summer

![Small tortoiseshell butterfly on a flower](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5603307)

Open image viewer

There have been many small tortoiseshell butterflies this summer. Photo: Karoliina Kantola / Yle

There have been many butterflies this summer.

The summer has been warm and rain has fallen. Day-flying butterflies have found food easily.

They have also easily found a mate, and then many eggs have been laid.

In Finland there have been many butterflies of kinds that were previously seen much further south. Climate change is bringing new butterfly species to Finland.

## The weather warms up at the weekend

![Threshing wheat in Kontiolahti on 23 September 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9849599)

At the weekend it is warm and not much rain falls. Farmers can go to the grain fields to thresh. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

At the weekend the weather warms up. Warm air comes to Finland from the south.

Tomorrow, Saturday, it may rain at times in the west and north. In the east the sun will shine.

The temperature may be over 20 degrees.
