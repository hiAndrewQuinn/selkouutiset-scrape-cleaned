# Sunday 22.12.2019 (radio)

Finns keep to the road speed limits. Two children were brought from the al-Hol camp in Syria to Finland. Paperwork after a relative's death is being reduced. The chief executive of a big company is still almost always a man. The shortest day of the year was dark and rainy.

## Speeding is decreasing

![Speed camera by the road](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10125050)

Open image viewer

A pole camera, that is, a "speed camera", measures car speeds. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finns drive over the speed limit less and less each year.

This year, at the pole camera, that is, the "speed camera", about 6 per mille of cars have been speeding. Four years ago the figure was 13 per mille. The police say that speeding has decreased because speeds can be monitored effectively with pole cameras.

There are big differences in the speeding measured by pole cameras. In Siuntio in Uusimaa there is a pole camera that measures speeding for 10 per cent of cars. At some poles, on the other hand, less than half a per mille of cars speed.

## 2 children from al-Hol to Finland

![Unknown woman and child photographed at the al-Hol camp in Syria in February 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11120762)

Open image viewer

A woman and child at the al-Hol camp. Photo: Fadel Senna / AFP

2 Finnish children have been brought to Finland from the al-Hol camp in Syria.

Children under 7 were brought to Helsinki-Vantaa airport late on Saturday evening. The airport was guarded more closely than usual, and the children were kept out of the media's sight. They are now in the care of social and health authorities.

The authorities do not say more about the children to the public. The reason is the children's safety and privacy.

## Paperwork after a death

![A small porcelain angel placed sitting in a gap between the bricks of a memorial wall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11132360)

Handling the affairs of a deceased relative sometimes takes a long time. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Dealing with a relative's death involves a lot of paperwork, that is, many documents must be obtained.

Relatives must, for example, sort out the deceased's money affairs, such as paying bills, taxes and bank accounts. Relatives must be listed and their identity proven, and the inheritance must be divided.

Now the authorities, parishes and banks are trying together to make relatives' work easier. The reform may be finished in about three years.

## The chief executive of a big company is almost always a man

![Forest group Stora Enso's new chief executive will be the Swede Annica Bresky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10988712)

Annica Bresky. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

The top executive of a big Finnish company is still almost always a man.

Of the chief executives of Finland's 40 largest companies, only 2 are women. They are Annica Bresky, chief executive of the forest company Stora Enso, and Satu Huber of the pension company Elo. One reason is that women study maths and technology less than men do. Companies may not try hard enough to get women into top jobs. And women themselves may not want to be top executives as much as men do.

More than half of the heads of HR and communications at big companies are women.

## Light is starting to win

![Wet window pane](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2462,h_1385,x_0,y_4/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6208695def89079b1c4/1575979480)

Today, Sunday, was the shortest day of the year, that is, the winter solstice.

The sun was above the horizon in Helsinki for about 5 and a half hours, and in Rovaniemi for a little over 2 hours. Across the whole country there were thick clouds and it rained, so the day was very dark. It was a little brighter where there is snow.

The day will stay short for a few days and then slowly start to lengthen. The northern hemisphere is heading towards spring.
