## Interpellation on immigration

![Riikka Purra speaks in Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_522/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8592896152e43f8d044/1632825440)

Finns Party chair Riikka Purra. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

The Finns Party is tabling an interpellation to the government. The Finns Party asks the government about immigration and the costs of immigration.

The government has proposed easing immigration. The Finns Party says that the party does not accept that immigration is increased.

An interpellation means that a party that is not in government asks the government a question, which the government must answer. After the government's answer, Parliament votes on whether the government may continue its work.

## Hen houses

![Hens in an enriched-cage hen house in Somero. The lighting is dim, and the hens are in green cages.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-85852761507095a88ad/1632662496)

Open image viewer

Hens in an enriched-cage hen house. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

There are still many hen houses in Finland where the hens are in cages. Such hen houses are *enriched-cage hen houses.*

In Finland about half of the eggs come from enriched-cage hen houses. That is more than in the other Nordic countries. The situation will change in a few years. The S-group shops and the K-group shops will stop selling eggs from enriched-cage hen houses within 5 years. That is why the hen houses must change their operations.

In new hen houses the hens are free. Hen farms do not like the change. Changing hen houses is expensive, and raising hens in new kinds of hen houses is more expensive than in cages.

## Electric cars

![Picture from a car shop, in the foreground a light-coloured hybrid car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5574660)

Open image viewer

Hybrid car in a car shop. Photo: YLE / Kalle Niskala

Electric cars are increasing more slowly in Finland than in other Nordic countries. Of the new cars put into use in Finland in September, about 30 per cent were fully electric or hybrid cars.

In other Nordic countries electric cars are bought much more. In Norway almost all new cars, about 95 per cent, are fully electric or hybrids.

In Sweden and Denmark about half of the new cars in September were fully electric or hybrid cars. *Hybrid car* is a car that runs on electricity and petrol.

## Choral singing

![Chamber choir Kampin laulu](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5026,h_2827,x_0,y_157/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8612266156d804c57d7/1633081399)

Choirs can again practise singing. The gathering restrictions have ended, and choirs may again gather to sing.

Over 200,000 Finns take part in choral singing as a hobby. Many choir conductors have been without work.

Now thousands of choir singers and choir conductors are happy, because choirs can again practise and perform.
