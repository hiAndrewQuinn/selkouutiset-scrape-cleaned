# Friday 9.12.2016 (radio)

The prime ministers of Finland and Russia have met in Oulu. A mother and 3 children died in a fire in Helsinki. This year's useless thing is the plastic bag. Finnish singer Saara Aalto competes in the final of Britain's X Factor competition on Saturday.

## Medvedev and Sipilä

![Meeting 09.12.2016 Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9344741)

Prime ministers Dmitri Medvedev and Juha Sipilä in Oulu. Photo: Yle

The prime ministers of Finland and Russia have met in Oulu, in northern Finland.

Prime ministers Dmitri Medvedev and Juha Sipilä discussed, for example, a new telecommunications cable. The cable could perhaps be laid along the northern sea route from Asia to Europe. If the cable is built, telecommunications between Asia and Europe will become faster.

In Prime Minister Medvedev's opinion, the cable plan is interesting. If the cable is built, it can be of benefit to many countries, Medvedev said.

Finland is really interested in the new cable. Prime Minister Sipilä said that Oulu is the right place to discuss telecommunications. There are many telecommunications firms in Oulu.

## The Helsinki fire

![Firefighters in front of a block of flats in Vuosaari early on Friday morning, 9 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9342482)

Open image viewer

Firefighters in Vuosaari. Photo: Yle

There was a bad fire in Helsinki on the night between Thursday and Friday. A mother and 3 small children died in the fire. The family's father was working a night shift at the time of the fire.

The fire was in Vuosaari in Helsinki. The fire destroyed a flat on the 6th floor of a block of flats. There was no smoke alarm in the flat.

Fire chief Kimmo Tuominen says that the firefighters were at the scene quickly. The fire did not spread to other flats.

Such a big fire is not common in Finland. It is rare for many people to die in one fire.

## The useless thing of the year

![Different plastic bags.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9342463)

Open image viewer

Finns use 300 million plastic bags every year. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

This year's useless thing has been chosen. A useless thing means a pointless item that is not really needed. This year's useless thing is the plastic bag.

The magazine Suomen Luonto chose the useless thing. The magazine got over 500 suggestions from readers. The magazine chose the plastic bag as the useless thing, because plastic does a lot of harm to the environment.

The magazine Suomen Luonto says that 300 million plastic bags are used in Finland every year. That means that every Finn uses 55 plastic bags a year. In addition, Finns use 900 million thin plastic bags every year.

## Saara Aalto

![Saara Aalto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9336345)

Open image viewer

Saara Aalto visited Helsinki on Monday. Photo: Timo Korhonen / AOP

Finns are excited about Britain's X Factor music competition. The competition's final is on British television on Saturday. The Finnish singer Saara Aalto is also taking part.

Many Finns are following how Saara does in Britain. Saara Aalto visited Helsinki on Monday and performed for her fans in Helsinki's Senate Square. The performance was filmed for the X Factor programme.
