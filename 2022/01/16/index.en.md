## Omicron shows in workplaces

![A postal sorter on a delivery round by bicycle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5359,h_3014,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-77814160365e334c337/1640636122)

The omicron variant of the coronavirus is raging in workplaces.

Yle's round of calls to large employers says that more employees than usual are sick. Many have also had to go into quarantine because of exposure.

Work tasks can still be managed. However, the coronavirus is delaying, for example, postal deliveries and threatens to close construction sites.

The Institute for Health and Welfare says that the biggest peak of corona infections is a few weeks away.

## Climate change threatens the winter Olympics

![A cross-country skiing race.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10126698)

Open image viewer

The winter Olympics were held in Sochi, Russia, in 2014. Photo: Valery Sharifulin / AOP

Organising winter sports competitions may be difficult in the future because of climate change.

Yle has looked into the rise in temperatures in 21 cities where winter Olympics have been held before. The survey shows that many old Olympic cities will no longer be able to host winter competitions in 30 years' time. Every third former Olympic city will then be too warm for organising winter competitions.

Rising temperatures cause a lack of snow. Because of this, ski sports in particular are hard to organise. For example, in Sochi in Russia, Sarajevo in Bosnia and Herzegovina and Oslo in Norway, it may no longer be possible to hold ski races at Olympic level.

Yle's survey is based on research by the University of Waterloo.

## Novak Djokovic not allowed into Australia

![Novak Djokovic plays tennis.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3509,h_1973,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90084861dea4e45ba11/1641981191)

Open image viewer

Novak Djokovic will not play in the Australian Open tennis tournament this year. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

The men's tennis number one player, Novak Djokovic, is being deported from Australia. Djokovic lost a court battle over his Australian visa and has to leave the country.

Serbian Novak Djokovic came to Australia just under 2 weeks ago. He was supposed to take part in the Australian Open tennis tournament. However, the authorities cancelled Djokovic's visa, because he has no corona vaccinations.

The Australian Open tennis tournament starts in Melbourne tomorrow. Last year Djokovic won the tournament.

## Many complaints about gyms

![A woman squats in a gym with weights behind her neck. The picture is motion-blurred.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_232/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90019361dc305aa5e41/1641820436)

Open image viewer

Consumer advice has received many complaints about gyms. Photo: Jaana Polamo / Yle

There have been more complaints about gym operations during the corona time than before.

Consumer advice usually gets about 300 complaints a year about gym operations. In 2020 there were about 450 complaints.

Customers have experienced, for example, pressure to join the gym as members.
