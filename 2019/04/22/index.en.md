# Monday 22.4.2019 (radio)

The Sri Lankan government blames a local radical Muslim group for the terror attacks. TV comedian Volodymyr Zelenskyy has won the Ukrainian presidential election. Indoor air problems cause great harm to tenants. Domestic animals also suffer from being overweight.

## Sri Lanka suspects Muslim terrorists of the attacks

![Rescue workers, police, investigators and chaos in a church.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10750194)

Rescue workers in Sri Lanka. Photo: EPA

The Sri Lankan government blames the local radical NTJ Muslim group for Sunday's terror attacks. A government representative said that the suicide bombers were probably Sri Lankans.

At least 24 people have been arrested because of the attacks. In the capital Colombo a new explosion happened today, when police tried to remove an explosive from a car.

In the series of terror attacks at least 290 people died, and 500 people were injured. The attacks were made on churches and luxury hotels. No Finns have been found among the victims.

The Finnish Foreign Ministry says that unnecessary travel to Sri Lanka must be avoided. Finland changed its travel advice together with the other Nordic countries.

The recommendation is valid for at least a few days.

## Volodymyr Zelenskyy won the Ukrainian presidential election

![Volodymyr Zelensky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10750373)

Winner Volodymyr Zelenskyy held a press conference at his campaign office on Sunday. Photo: Genya Savilov / AFP / Lehtikuva

In Ukraine, TV comedian Volodymyr Zelenskyy has been congratulated on his election victory in the presidential election. Zelenskyy received over 70 percent of the votes.

Current President Petro Poroshenko has admitted defeat. The official election result will come only just before May Day.

Zelenskyy has a legal education. He promises to start reforms, for example in healthcare.

Zelenskyy also says that he wants to speed up the Minsk agreement. The Minsk agreement aims to end the war in eastern Ukraine.

## Indoor air problems are unreasonable for tenants

![Mould and indoor air problems](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5432121)

Open image viewer

Evidence of an indoor air problem falls to the tenant. Photo: Yle

The Consumers' Association says that indoor air problems cause tenants unreasonable situations.

If an indoor air problem is suspected in a flat, the tenant has to prove it.

However, there are months-long queues for the municipal inspector. A private study is expensive, and not all tenants have money for a study.

A tenant has the right to terminate the rental agreement if living there causes a danger to health.

## Domestic animals also suffer from being overweight

![A horse eats dry hay.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10353542)

Open image viewer

Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

Obesity and diabetes also threaten domestic animals and pets. Veterinarian Jaakko Väyrynen estimates that perhaps as many as half of dogs may suffer from being overweight.

Pets have the same lifestyle diseases as humans. The diseases include, besides diabetes, joint problems, urinary tract problems and heart problems.

Pets get too many treats and too little exercise. Pets in apartment blocks and in city centres are fatter than elsewhere.

Horses also suffer from being overweight. A horse spends a large part of its time eating. In addition, dry hay contains a lot of sugar. That is why horses need a lot of exercise.
