# Monday 1.5.2017 (radio)

May Day was celebrated in quite beautiful weather. May Day Eve went fairly calmly. Parties have organised May Day celebrations. Mobile devices disturb people's concentration and rest.

## Helsinki's May Day

![The Students' Union singers perform on Ullanlinnanmäki as part of the traditional May Day celebrations in Kaivopuisto, Helsinki, on 1 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9590128)

In Helsinki people sang on Ullanlinnanmäki on May Day. Many had student caps on their heads. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

On Monday May Day was celebrated.

May Day was quite sunny in a large part of Finland. In Helsinki, for example, people enjoyed May Day treats outdoors on Ullanlinnanmäki and in Kaivopuisto.

On Ullanlinnanmäki people also listened to choir singing, when the Students' Union singers performed. Singing has been held on Ullanlinnanmäki every May Day for about 60 years.

## Young people's May Day Eve

![May Day celebrations were coloured by water and sleet showers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9589869)

Open image viewer

May Day Eve was celebrated in Helsinki in cold weather. Photo: Tuomo Björksten/Yle

May Day Eve was celebrated quite calmly. The police estimated that one reason for this was the cold weather on May Day Eve.

In Helsinki, however, the police had to clear Kaivopuisto late on May Day Eve. There were thousands of young people in the park. Some of the young people fought, stole and set fire to portable toilets.

During the May Day night in Helsinki an operation was carried out that helped young people. Help was given, for example, to young people who had drunk too much alcohol. Almost 600 young people got help.

## The parties' May Day

![SDP chairman Antti Rinne at the Social Democratic Party's May Day event in Tampere on 1 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9590061)

SDP party leader Antti Rinne spoke in Tampere. Photo: Kalle Parkkinen / Lehtikuva

On May Day, political parties have organised May Day celebrations.

May Day is an important celebration for left-wing parties. At the May Day celebrations of the Social Democrats and the Left Alliance, the Finnish government was criticised. The left-wing parties are not in the government, and in their opinion the government is making bad economic policy.

The party leader of the Social Democrats, that is the SDP, Antti Rinne said that the government has not succeeded in reducing unemployment. Rinne spoke in Tampere, where the SDP had its May Day celebration.

![Left Alliance chairman Li Andersson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9590177)

Left Alliance party leader Li Andersson demanded that the minimum wage be raised to 10 euros an hour. The minimum wage is the lowest possible wage. Andersson spoke at the Left Alliance's May Day celebration in Jyväskylä.

## The harms of mobile devices

![Amber Case](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9587889)

Open image viewer

American researcher Amber Case visited Helsinki. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

Phones and other mobile devices disturb people. It is hard for a person to concentrate or rest when a device keeps notifying them of, for example, Facebook messages. That is why people are nervous.

Researcher Amber Case says that the solution to the problem is calm technology, in English "calm technology". It means that technology works in such a way that it does not disturb a person all the time.

If notifications come to the device, for example as colours, they disturb a person less, Case says. She gives an example of old technology: traffic lights work well, but they do not stop a driver from concentrating on driving.
