## Finland expels one Russian diplomat

![The gate of the Russian embassy on Tehtaankatu in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6320370)

Finland is expelling one person working at the Russian embassy. Photo: Yle

Many countries are expelling Russian diplomats. The expulsions show solidarity with Britain.

Finland is forcing one person working at the Russian embassy to leave Finland. Many other EU countries are also expelling Russian diplomats.

The United States is ordering 60 Russian diplomats to leave the country.

Britain accuses Russia of trying to murder a former spy in Britain with nerve gas.

## The police reveal the locations of the cameras

![A speed camera (a "tin policeman") by the road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10125050)

Open image viewer

This kind of automatic speed camera is called a "tin policeman". Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

In Finland, the police intend to reveal some of the locations of the "tin policemen", that is, speed cameras, to motorists.

The police will reveal the locations of 25 cameras at a time. There are 120 cameras in total in operation.

The police will publish the locations of the cameras online, on the Traffic Situation map service. The location of the cameras will also show on a car's satellite navigation, if it has traffic information updates.

The purpose is to improve road safety.

## The winter flu season is now ending

![A man blows his nose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9995827)

Open image viewer

This winter the flu season started as early as January, and it is still going on. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The worst flu time of the winter is now over. Many still have the flu, but not many new virus infections are found any more.

This winter the flu has spread over a long period. Two viruses were around: first the B virus, and then the A virus.

## Family and friends are important to many

![Lake landscape](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9757482)

Open image viewer

For many Finns, their home municipality is an important part of their self-image. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finns' self-image has been studied in a large study.

Family, friends and relatives are important to a large part of us. People close to us have a big effect on the picture we have of ourselves.

Many also consider their home municipality an important place, and it affects us a lot.

Political opinion and the economic position of one's own family are not important to many Finns.

## The director of the National Ballet has been relieved of managerial duties

![Kenneth Greve photographed in 2013.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10133859)

Open image viewer

Director Kenneth Greve has used inappropriate language, the dancers of the National Ballet say. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Director Kenneth Greve may no longer hold managerial posts at the Finnish National Ballet. The National Ballet relieved Greve of his managerial duties today, on Monday.

Many dancers of the National Ballet have said that Greve has behaved badly. He has used inappropriate words when talking about the dancers' appearance and private lives.

The National Ballet will get a new director in August. The change of director was already decided earlier.
