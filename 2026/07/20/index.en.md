## Norway fire

The next topic is the Norwegian fire.

A large fire was extinguished in Norway over the weekend.

A fire broke out on Friday in a row house area in Drammen, near Oslo.

More than 100 homes were destroyed in the fire. About 400 people were evacuated, but no one died in the fire.

The fire was Norway's largest in over 100 years. The fire was extinguished with the help of helicopters.

The extinguishing of the large fire in Norway has continued throughout the weekend.

Authorities will begin investigating the cause of the fire once the scene calms down and cools down.
