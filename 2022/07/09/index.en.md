## Petrol price

![Close-up of petrol pumps.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_570/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-921606621cc5916970b/1649159322)

Open image viewer

The price of petrol has fallen a little, but is still high. Photo: Mimmi Nietula / Yle

The price of petrol has levelled off. Petrol is still expensive, but this week the price has fallen by about 20 cents.

The prices of petrol and other fuels have fallen a little, because fuels no longer need to use as much renewable fuel as before. Renewable fuel is environmentally friendly, but expensive.

The government changed the rules for renewable fuel, so that the price of petrol does not rise further.

Petrol has become more expensive this year, because there is a war in Ukraine. At the moment the price per litre is well over 2 euros.

## Sanctions

![euromaster, price increase, summer tyres, winter tyres, studs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_247/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9408526254356e1f98d/1649685930)

Open image viewer

For example, tyres are expensive, because the import of tyres from Russia has ended because of sanctions. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

More than half of Finns think that sanctions against Russia should be tightened. 54 percent of Finns want more sanctions, even though it raises prices in Finland.

For example, the European Union has imposed sanctions against Russia because of the war in Ukraine. The sanctions prevent trade with Russia, for example.

About 30 percent of Finns think that the current sanctions are enough. Only 5 percent of Finns want to reduce sanctions.

The information is from a survey by Uutissuomalainen.

Finns' help for Ukrainian refugees has decreased. Now in the summer Finns have given less help than in the spring.

At the Helsinki aid centre it is said that food and hygiene products always run out. About 600 refugees visit the Ukrainian aid centre every week.

## Spanish bull run

![People running in front of bulls in Spain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10876893)

Open image viewer

Bull run in 2019. Photo: Jim Hollander / EPA

The bull run has again been held in Pamplona, Spain. In it, angry bulls are let onto the streets and people try to run away from them.

This year at least 7 people were taken to hospital.

Bull runs have been held in Pamplona for 800 years already.

## Pollen season

![Timothy grass in a field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4530075a429d7f4bd92/1514315286)

Open image viewer

Now allergic people can get symptoms from timothy pollen. Photo: Yle/Derrick Frilund

The pollen season continues. Pollen comes into the air when plants flower.

Now the timothy grass has begun to flower, and the flowering of grasses also continues. The flowering of trees and bushes has already ended.

Many people are allergic to pollen. Common allergy symptoms are, for example, a runny nose and stinging eyes.

Information on the pollen situation is on the website norkko.fi.
