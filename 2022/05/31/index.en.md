## The EU and Russian oil

![A red-hulled oil tanker in an aerial photo. The ship appears to be moving to the upper left in the picture on a sunny day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12432318)

Open image viewer

Transport of Russian oil to Europe by ship will end. Photo: Paul Martens / EPA

The European Union wants to stop using Russian oil.

The leaders of the EU countries have now agreed on how the EU will reduce buying Russian oil.

The EU intends to stop shipments of Russian oil by ship to EU countries. In this way most of the EU's oil purchases from Russia will end.

The oil pipeline from Russia to Europe will still remain open. The reason is that Hungary still needs Russian oil.

The EU does not want to buy Russian oil, because Russia uses the oil money for the war in Ukraine.

Prices are rising at a fast pace.

In the euro area prices rose by about 8 per cent in May from last year. The euro area consists of countries whose currency is the euro.

Prices are rising because of the war in Ukraine. Especially the prices of energy and food have risen, after Russia attacked Ukraine.

## Sentences for street gang leaders

The court has given sentences to the leaders of a street gang from Helsinki. The street gang is known as 47 or Kurdish Mafia.

Milan Jaff was sentenced to 10 years in prison. Yahue Mahdi Mohamud, also known as Cavallani, was sentenced to 9 years in prison.

The sentences came because the men shot towards a detached house. They tried to kill members of a street gang from Espoo.

Milan Jaff and Cavallani are also known from rap music, in which a criminal way of life and violence are admired.

## Electric treatment for Alzheimer's

![An elderly person in a nursing home.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9099535)

Open image viewer

Alzheimer's disease destroys memory. Photo: AOP

New information has been obtained about Alzheimer's disease. Alzheimer's disease takes away the patient's memory.

A new Italian study says that electric treatment can help Alzheimer's patients. In the study, small amounts of electricity were led into the patients' brains every 2 weeks.

After electric treatment the patients remembered words more than 2 times better than patients who did not get electric treatment.

In the autumn electric treatment will also start to be tested in Finland. At Kuopio University Hospital it is being studied whether electric treatment works better if the patient gets it on consecutive days.

If electric treatment works, it may become a good and cheap treatment for Alzheimer's patients.
