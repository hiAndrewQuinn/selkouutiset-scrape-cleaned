# Wednesday 27.6.2018 (radio)

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä has given Parliament a prime minister's statement about the postponement of the social and health care reform. Finland's health care is the best in the world. The new National Conciliator is Vuokko Piekkala. Sanoma is buying a majority of the Finnish News Agency. Now it is time to pick your own strawberries.

## Sipilä gave a prime minister's statement on the social and health care reform

![Prime Minister Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10276574)

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä has given Parliament a prime minister's statement. The statement was about the social and health care reform, or sote, being moved to the start of 2021. That is a year later than planned.

A prime minister's statement brings current topics to Parliament for discussion. The discussion was lively, and the opposition criticised the government a lot.

There was no vote of confidence in the government.

## Finland's health care is the best in the world

![An empty ward at Pohjois-Kymi Hospital in Kouvola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10250735)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle / Kari Kosonen

A large international study says that Finland's health care is the best in the world. Health care is of higher quality and fairer in Finland, Switzerland and Iceland than in other countries.

Care is most expensive in the USA, but it comes only 29th. In Finland, health care costs 10 percent less public money than in Iceland and 40 percent less than in Switzerland.

The results were published by the science journal The Lancet.

## Vuokko Piekkala was appointed National Conciliator

![Vuokko Piekkala](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10276292)

Open image viewer

The new National Conciliator Vuokko Piekkala. Photo: Aarne Ormio / Church image bank

The new National Conciliator is Vuokko Piekkala. The Government appointed Piekkala to the post on Wednesday.

Piekkala, the labour market director of the Church's employer organisation, has earlier been a lawyer and head of negotiations at the Association of Finnish Local Authorities. The previous conciliator, Minna Helle, left in the middle of her term to become labour market director at Technology Industries of Finland.

4 people applied for the post of National Conciliator.

## Sanoma bought a majority of STT

![STT logo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10276387)

Open image viewer

Sanoma is buying a majority of STT, the Finnish News Agency. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

Sanoma Media Finland is buying a majority of the Finnish News Agency. Sanoma publishes, for example, Helsingin Sanomat. Most newspapers and local radio stations, as well as Yle, use material from STT. Helsingin Sanomat will also become an STT customer again.

Sanoma has already been the biggest owner of STT. It has owned 33 percent of STT. After the deal it will own 75.4 percent.

The Finnish News Agency has had a lot of financial difficulties.

## Strawberries are cheapest if you pick them yourself

![A strawberry picker](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10275082)

Open image viewer

Photo: Riina Kasurinen/Yle

Picking strawberries yourself has become a trip to the countryside. People who pick their own also save money. The price per kilo is about one or 2 euros cheaper than if you buy the berries.

Picking your own started in the 1990s. Farmers could not get enough workers for their fields, and the strawberries had to be sold. During the recession, people who picked their own wanted to save money.

Now many people make strawberry picking into a summer outing. Farmers say that many grandparents, for example, come to the fields with their grandchildren.
