# Thursday 19.1.2017 (radio)

Work and studying are important for asylum seekers. A hotel has been buried under snow in Italy. In Iran, a block of flats caught fire and collapsed. The police can protect a person who gives evidence in court. Finland's national food is rye bread. Luksia's video blog continues.

## Preventing crime

![An asylum seeker uses a mobile phone in a corridor](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9365710)

An asylum seeker does not feel well if they have nothing to do. Photo: Kay Nietfeld / EPA

Work and studying are important for asylum seekers. They are also the best way to prevent crimes by asylum seekers.

Experts think that the reason for crimes by asylum seekers may be that asylum seekers have nothing sensible to do.

Experts talked about preventing crime on Thursday.

## Accident in Italy

![hotel buried in snow](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9411821)

Open image viewer

Not everyone has got out of the hotel that was buried under snow. Photo: Italian rescue service

A hotel has been buried under snow in Italy. The hotel is in the mountains. Snow slid down the mountain onto the hotel.

In the hotel there were customers and guests who have not got out from under the snow. Rescuers have worked all day searching for people under the snow.

The accident happened in Central Italy, where there have also been earthquakes recently. Now a very large amount of snow has fallen in the area.

## Accident in Iran

There has also been a serious accident in Iran.

A fire started in a block of flats, and the building collapsed. The building had 17 floors. The accident happened in Iran's capital Tehran.

At least 30 firefighters died in the accident. They were putting out the fire when the building collapsed. Dozens of people were injured.

## Protecting witnesses

![Prosecutors in a courtroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8201970)

Open image viewer

It is important that a witness dares to speak in court. Photo: Miia Roivainen / Yle

The police can protect a person who gives evidence in court. This has been done in Finland for about 2 years, and the experiences are good.

A witness is protected if there is fear that criminals want to harm them. It is important that people dare to tell about crimes.

Detective Chief Inspector Sanna Palo says that a witness can get a new name and a new home. The witness's home can be guarded with the help of technology.

## Finns' rye bread

![Fresh rye loaves in a bakery.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5453472)

Open image viewer

This is Finland's national food. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

Finland's national food is rye bread.

About 50,000 Finns took part in the vote in which the national food was chosen. Dark rye bread was the winner of the vote.

Second in the vote came Karelian stew. It is a meat dish.

Third in the vote came the Karelian pie. Its crust is usually rye, and inside it there is rice porridge.

Karelian stew and Karelian pie come from Karelia, on the border of Finland and Russia.
