## Corona vaccinations of nurses

![A nurse helps an elderly person who is eating at a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11724018)

Open image viewer

People who live in care homes are weak and frail. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

The discussion on corona vaccinations for nurses continues.

Now the Helsinki region's corona group proposes that corona vaccinations for nurses be made mandatory in care homes. Residents of care homes have already received corona infections from nurses who have not been vaccinated against corona.

According to the Helsinki region's corona working group, the law must be changed so that nurses of weak and frail patients must have corona vaccinations.

President Sauli Niinistö has met the new President of Estonia.

Estonian President Alar Karis visited Finland for the first time. Niinistö and Karis discussed relations between Finland and Estonia at the Presidential Palace.

President Karis praised Finland's corona measures. He said that Finland has fought corona better than Estonia.

Alar Karis is 63 years old. He started in the post of President of Estonia about 2 weeks ago.

## Sami languages week

![Sami flags flying](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12057849)

Open image viewer

The Sami also have their own flag.

This week the Sami languages week is celebrated.

The week is celebrated in Finland, Norway and Sweden. In all these countries there are Sami people who speak Sami.

There are 9 Sami languages. In Finland 3 Sami languages are spoken. They are Northern Sami, Inari Sami and Skolt Sami.

There are only a few Sami speakers left. That is why during the Sami languages week they want to bring the Sami languages to the fore.

During the language week, distance teaching of Sami languages is organised online. The teaching suits pupils, students and everyone who is interested in Sami languages.

Remote Sami language lessons can be found at the address saamenetaopetus.com.

## A foreign-language name in job search

![Job search service on a mobile phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-7593658a6f4bc78aab/1487336695)

Open image viewer

Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

A name is important in working life. A name can affect whether an applicant gets a job.

It is said that in Finland it is difficult for an applicant to get a job if they have a foreign-language name.

Yle is now collecting people's experiences of how a foreign-language name has affected job search.

If you have experiences of the matter, you can answer the survey at yle.fi.
