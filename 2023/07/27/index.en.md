## Bird flu

![Foxes in cages at a fur farm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3643,h_2049,x_4,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114840064c266abab907/1690462069)

In Finland they are trying to stop bird flu from spreading. In nature, bird flu has already killed many birds.

Bird flu has now also been found on fur farms. That is why fur animals have to be put down. The bird flu virus has reached the fur farms from birds.

Earlier, authorities ordered that hens must be kept apart from wild birds. Now they also remind people that cats and dogs must not get near dead birds.

So far, bird flu has not spread to people.

## Interest rates

The European Central Bank, that is, the ECB, is raising interest rates. On Thursday the ECB said that it will raise its key interest rates by 0.25 percentage points.

With interest rates, the ECB is fighting inflation, that is, the rise in prices. The ECB has already been raising interest rates for about a year.

The ECB's interest rate decisions have also affected the interest on many mortgages. The interest on many homes' mortgages has risen, and paying off the loan is more expensive than before.

Few children are born in Finland.

This year, about 21,000 babies were born in the first half of the year. Last year, in the first half of the year, more than 1,000 more children were born.

However, Finland has gained new residents from abroad. About 28,000 people moved to Finland from abroad in the first half of the year.

Most people who have come to Finland are Ukrainians. Many immigrants have also come from Russia, the Philippines and India.

## Sleepyhead Day

![Tom Pippingsköld holds a Sleepyhead sign on the shore of the old town of Naantali.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_453/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114803164c20ccc45bf6/1690438935)

Open image viewer

This year's official Sleepyhead, Tom Pippingsköld. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

Sleepyhead Day was celebrated on Thursday. The Sleepyhead Day celebration was in Naantali.

At the celebration it was announced that this year's official Sleepyhead is Tom Pippingsköld. He is the managing director of the shipping company Finnlines.

A Sleepyhead means a person who sleeps for a long time. The custom is that the official Sleepyhead is thrown into the sea in the morning in the harbour of Naantali.

Sleepyhead Day was already celebrated in Naantali in the 1800s. The official Sleepyhead was first chosen in 1958.

You can watch on Yle Areena how this year's official national Sleepyhead was thrown into the sea.
