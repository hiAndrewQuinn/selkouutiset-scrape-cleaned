# Saturday 11.2.2017 (radio)

Haavisto for the presidential election. Underage asylum seekers. 112 Day. The föhn wind.

## Pekka Haavisto wants to run in the presidential election

![Pekka Haavisto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9455201)

Pekka Haavisto received great support in the 2012 presidential election. Now he wants to stand again. Photo: Petteri Paalasmaa / AOP

MP Pekka Haavisto is ready to become the Greens' presidential candidate. Haavisto told the Ykkösaamu programme on TV1 that he can stand in the presidential election.

Haavisto's presidential candidacy will be discussed tomorrow, Sunday, at the meeting of the Greens party.

Haavisto was also a presidential candidate in 2012. Then he was very popular. Pekka Haavisto received more than a million votes. However, he lost to Sauli Niinistö, who became president.

## Researcher: young asylum seekers are an opportunity

![A young person does homework at a table, sneakers in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9444836)

Open image viewer

Young asylum seekers are an opportunity for Finland, says the researcher. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

The researcher wonders what people in Finland think of underage asylum seekers, that is, those under 18 years old, who have come to Finland alone. The researcher Anna-Kaisa Kuusisto-Arponen says that people often see young asylum seekers as a threat.

According to Kuusisto-Arponen, that is not true. She says that underage asylum seekers are an opportunity for Finland. They are ordinary children and young people.

Anna-Kaisa Kuusisto-Arponen says that Finland must integrate underage children and young people well. She has studied for many years children and young people who came to Finland alone as asylum seekers.

Almost 1,600 underage asylum seekers received asylum or a residence permit in Finland last year.

## 112 Day was celebrated in Finland

![A trial of the emergency alert system on Yle's TV channels on 11 February at 11.20.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9455266)

Open image viewer

On TV, the publishing of emergency alerts was tested on 112 Day. Photo: Yle

Finland has had 112 Day on Saturday. 112 is the emergency number in Finland, which you can call when you need the police, an ambulance or the fire service. 112 is the emergency number in all EU countries.

112 Day aims to improve people's safety. It is hoped that people will think about, for example, ways of preventing accidents. In addition, people are guided to act correctly in emergency situations.

## The weather is warming up quickly

![Icy artwork](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7888576)

Open image viewer

The start of the week is warm and sunny because of the föhn wind. Photo: Seija Annala

The weather will soon warm up really quickly. Yle's meteorologist Seija Paasonen says that at the start of the week it may be up to 5 degrees warmer across the whole of Finland.

The weather is warming up because of the föhn phenomenon. Warm air is coming to Finland from the coast of the USA. The warm air comes over the mountains of Norway, and then the föhn wind is created.

It is believed that the temperature will stay above zero in southern Finland at least until Wednesday. After that the weather will cool down in the south too.

Winter has been so warm that there is very little ice on Finland's sea areas. Icebreakers are needed at present only in the Bothnian Bay.
