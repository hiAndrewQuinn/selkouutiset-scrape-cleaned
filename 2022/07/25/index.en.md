## Social benefits

![Kela sign on an outdoor wall](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6781135ec2889fce4b2/1589808229)

Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

Many social benefits go up in August. Benefits rise by 3.5 percent.

For example, the labour market subsidy and the basic daily allowance rise by about 26 euros a month. The full national pension rises by about 24 euros a month.

Social benefits are increased because of inflation. Inflation, that is, the rise in prices, is now fast, and with social benefits you can now buy less than before.

Kela's costs grow this year by about 90 million euros because of the extra increases in social benefits.

## Russian tourist visas

![Visa application.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1198,h_674,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-33944459143c52a1964/1494498420)

Open image viewer

A Russian tourist needs a visa when they come to Finland. Photo: Kari Kosonen/Yle

Russians are applying for tourist visas to Finland less than before. Far fewer applications are coming now than before the corona time.

In Finland, however, they are considering whether to stop giving tourist visas to Russians completely. Many other EU countries do not give tourist visas to Russians because of the war in Ukraine.

In Parliament, at least the biggest parties think that tourist visas should be banned for Russians.

Russia attacked Ukraine 5 months ago.

## Firefighters to Greece

![Along the landscape there is a sea and a shoreline. Thick smoke rises from the horizon.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5110,h_2874,x_10,y_532/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98586562dcd418a4113/1658639844)

Open image viewer

Finns are leaving to put out the ground fires in Greece. Photo: Manolis Lagoutaris / AFP

Firefighters are leaving Finland for Greece. They are going to help put out the ground fires.

There are big ground fires in Greece, and the fire spreads quickly in hot weather. It is forecast that the temperature in Greece will rise above 40 degrees this week.

Greece has asked for help to put out the ground fires, and many countries intend to give help.

The first Finnish firefighters leave for Greece at the beginning of August. In total about 50 firefighters leave Finland for Greece.

## Jaakko's cold stone

![Beach sand toys.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3237,h_1821,x_221,y_390/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83312460f4378d27fe0/1717752990)

How will it go for the swimming waters on Jaakko's day? Photo: Heli Kaski / Yle

On Monday Jaakko's day was celebrated. Jaakko's day is on 25 July.

An old tradition says that Jaakko throws a cold stone into the water. That is why the swimming waters get colder and the weather cools down. Autumn comes closer.

The Finnish Unscientific Society has organised a fun Jaakko's day event at the Helsinki market square. The society's members threw a cold stone into the sea at the market square.

After this we will see whether the summer's hot weather ends.

## The weather

And on Tuesday the weather is rainy. The rain area comes from the west and moves across Finland towards the east.

In the west the temperature stays below 20 degrees on Tuesday. In the east the temperature is over 20 degrees, before the rain area has moved as far as the east.

On Wednesday there will be rain showers in different parts of the country, and later in the week the weather cools down across the whole country.
