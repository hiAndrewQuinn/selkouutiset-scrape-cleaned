## Russian attacks in Ukraine

![Firefighters putting out burning cars in Kyiv](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2048,h_1152,x_0,y_92/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10189026343e2ed140f2/1665393477)

Rescue work under way in Kyiv after the attack in Ukraine. Photo: Ukrainian state emergency service / EPA

Russia has carried out attacks against Ukrainian cities. People have died in the attacks.

Russia has also fired missiles at Ukraine's capital, Kyiv. There have been explosions right in the centre of Kyiv.

Russian President Vladimir Putin says that the attacks are a response to the explosion on the Kertš Strait bridge. Russia blames Ukraine for the explosion.

The Kertš Strait bridge connects Russia and the Crimean Peninsula, which Russia has taken from Ukraine. The explosion on the bridge was a loss for Russia.

## Autumn corona season

![Infectious diseases chief physician Asko Järvinen on Yle's Ykkösaamu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5984,h_3366,x_0,y_244/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88502561a1ea6b402b3/1638001470)

Infectious diseases chief physician Asko Järvinen. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

Here in Finland the autumn corona season has begun.

Infectious diseases chief physician Asko Järvinen says that the number of corona infections has risen in Southern Finland for 3 weeks now.

However, there are few serious corona cases, because most Finns have had corona vaccinations.

## Word of the year

![Three women at the front of a hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12655237)

Open image viewer

Leena Nissilä, director of the Centre for Domestic Languages, (in the middle) gives the award to Minna Mäkinen of the Suomi.fi editorial team (on the right). Photo: Henna Kara / Selkokeskus

The winner of the Word of the Year competition, that is, the Clear Language competition, has been chosen. The Clear Language of the Year is the online editorial team of the Digital and Population Data Services Agency.

The Suomi.fi online editorial team of the Digital and Population Data Services Agency has made guides for different life situations. On the Suomi.fi service there is information about, for example, marriage and divorce.

Customers get information from the guides easily and in simple language. That is important in difficult life situations.

This is what Minna Karhunen, chief executive of the Association of Finnish Local and Regional Authorities, says. She chose this year's winner in the Clear Language of the Year competition.

The competition was organised by the Centre for Domestic Languages.

## Aleksis Kivi Day

![Statue of Aleksis Kivi in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7838881)

Open image viewer

Photo: Seppo Sarkkinen / Yle

On Monday it was Aleksis Kivi and Finnish literature day.

Aleksis Kivi lived in the 1800s. He wrote, for example, the book Seven Brothers. Seven Brothers came out about 150 years ago, in 1870.

Many people think that Finnish literature starts with Seven Brothers. That is why Aleksis Kivi is Finland's national writer, and Aleksis Kivi Day is a flag day.
