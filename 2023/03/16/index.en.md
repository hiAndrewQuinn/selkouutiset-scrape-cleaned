## Threat of a train driver strike

![A white-green train at a railway station, a woman is getting on the train.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_620/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1011194632c04a39101a/1663829493)

Open image viewer

Rail traffic may stop next week because of a strike. Photo: Kiira Ikävalko / Yle

A strike is about to begin in rail traffic next week. The locomotive drivers intend to go on strike, that is, stop work, on Monday.

During the strike all of Finland's rail traffic will stop, that is, commuter trains, long-distance trains and freight trains will not run.

There is a dispute in the sector especially about the costs of parental leave. If no agreement is reached during the end of the week, the rail strike begins on Monday.

## Cleaners' strike

![Cleaning tools: a bucket, cloths, a floor dryer and cleaning products.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3121,h_1755,x_0,y_303/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-935326624414ddd47f0/1727426130)

Photo: Elina Rantalainen / Yle

A strike has already begun in the property sector.

2,500 cleaners and property maintenance workers stopped work on Thursday. They are on strike until Saturday.

Because of the strike, for example, trains and buses are not cleaned as well as usual.

The cleaners started the strike, because they demand more pay. If the pay dispute is not settled, an even bigger strike will begin in the property sector next week.

## Interest rates

![A woman at a speaker's lectern.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689895)

Open image viewer

The President of the European Central Bank, Christine Lagarde, spoke about new interest rates on Thursday. Photo: Lehtikuva

The European Central Bank, that is, the ECB, is raising interest rates again. The ECB said on Thursday that it is raising the key interest rate by 0.5 percentage points.

With interest rates, the ECB fights against inflation, that is, the rise in prices. The ECB says that inflation is now Europe's biggest economic problem.

The ECB's decision may also raise the interest rates on housing loans. When interest rates rise, people have less money left for big purchases. That is why the rise in prices may slow down.

## Niinistö in Turkey

![Pictures of the Turkish and Finnish presidents, with a Nato flag in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689889)

Open image viewer

Turkish President Recep Tayip Erdogan and Finnish President Sauli Niinistö will meet on Friday. Photo: EPA-EFE, Andrej Cukic / AOP and Silja Viitala / Yle

President Sauli Niinistö is visiting Turkey. On Friday Niinistö will meet Turkish President Recep Tayip Erdogan.

Erdogan may tell Niinistö that Turkey approves Finland's joining the defence alliance Nato. Finland also needs Turkey's approval so that Finland can join Nato.

Finland applied for Nato membership last year. The reason was Russia's war in Ukraine. Finland does not want to be left alone if Russia's attacks on neighbouring countries continue.
