# Sunday 6.8.2017 (radio)

Finns like meat. Professors propose service vouchers for health care. Finland has good weather for tourism.

## Eating meat

![Pork belly and vegetables on a plate.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8973686)

Open image viewer

Finns eat a lot of meat. Eating vegetables has also increased. Photo: Olli Koski/Yle

Finns like meat.

Health experts have said that Finns must eat less meat. However, over 40 per cent of Finns eat meat almost every day. The figure is higher than before.

Men eat more meat than women. In the experts' opinion, it is unhealthy to eat more than half a kilo of meat a week. However, many men eat more meat.

The situation for women is better. About 70 per cent of women eat at most half a kilo of meat a week.

Although Finns eat a lot of meat, they also eat more vegetables and fruit than before. However, only a small share of Finns eat as many vegetables as experts recommend. The experts say that you should eat half a kilo of them every day.

## The social and health care reform

![A doctor walks along a health centre corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9759211)

Open image viewer

New health services have been planned for many years already. Photo: Mikael Kokkola / Yle

The discussion about the social and health care reform continues. The Finnish word "sote" means social services and health services.

Now professors at Aalto University have made a proposal on the social and health care reform. The professors propose service vouchers.

The professors' proposal is that the health centre doctor decides what treatment the patient needs. After that, the patient can use a service voucher to buy the treatment wherever they want, the professors propose.

One goal of the social and health care reform is that patients can choose for themselves whether they go to a health centre or to a private clinic. The idea is that the price is the same for the patient.

The new health services will probably begin in 2020. Health services must be reformed so that there is enough money for good services in the whole country in the future too.

## Finland's tourist weather

![Juha Kauppinen from Kajaani in the yard of his summer cottage. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9262950)

Open image viewer

Is Finland a cold or a fresh tourist country? Photo: Laura Tolonen / Yle

Finland has good and varied weather for tourism. This is what Finnish people in the tourism industry think.

The weather is an important part of the package when they sell Finland abroad. For example, at a travel fair in the Arab country of Dubai, people became interested in Finland's fresh and wet summer.

The seasons in Finland are also different. In winter the weather is clearly different from summer. In many other countries it is not like that.

Tourism experts hope that Finns will not always complain abroad that Finland's weather is really bad. Finns do not notice that it is impolite to complain about Finland's rain in a country where people have perhaps waited half a year for rain.
