# Sunday 2.4.2017 (radio)

The Finnish government wants unemployed people to be able to move to where the jobs are. A bad bus accident has happened in Sweden. People have died in a landslide in Colombia. Children's food must have soft fats.

## Workers moving

![Moving boxes are carried on a hand truck.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8029139)

Sometimes you have to move if you want a job. Photo: Seppo Sarkkinen / Yle

The government is planning that a worker can get support if they find a job far away. The government is planning, for example, that a worker can get support for moving and bigger travel allowances than now.

In Finland there are 100,000 open jobs and 300,000 unemployed people. That is why it is important that unemployed people can move to where the jobs are.

## Bus accident in Sweden

![Rescue authorities at the accident site between Sveg and Fågelsjö in Sweden on 2 April. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9542897)

Rescue workers next to the crashed bus. Photo: Nisse Schmidt / TT / Lehtikuva

A bus accident has happened in Finland's neighbouring country Sweden. At least 3 people died in the accident. About 30 people were injured, some very badly.

There were about 50 schoolchildren and almost 10 adults on the bus. The schoolchildren were 8th graders. They were on their way to ski at a ski resort.

The accident happened on Sunday morning, when the young people were sleeping on the bus. The bus drove off the road and fell over into a ditch. The cause of the accident is not known yet.

## Landslide in Colombia

![Soldiers rescued victims of the landslide in Colombia](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9542821)

Open image viewer

Earth, mud and rocks slid over the city of Mocoa. Photo: EPA

A natural disaster has happened in Colombia in South America. More than 250 people died in a landslide. There are many hundreds of injured.

There was very heavy rain in the disaster area. That is why water rose quickly in many rivers at the same time. There was so much water that earth, mud and rocks began to move. The result was a landslide that buried a big part of the city of Mocoa.

The disaster happened at night. Many people fled the landslide in their nightclothes. They did not have time to take anything with them from their homes, which were buried under the landslide.

Many people went missing in the landslide. Many of them may have died.

## Children's food

![A child admires treats on a sweets shelf in a shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7853605)

Open image viewer

Sugar is not good for a child's health. Photo: Sini Salmirinne / Yle

Healthy food is important for children. A new study says that you can see from a child's blood values what the child eats.

A child's blood values are worse if the child child eats a lot of sugar and hard fats. Hard fat is found, for example, in meat and fatty dairy products.

If a child eats fibre and soft fats, the child's blood values are better. Fibre is found, for example, in dark bread. Soft fats are found, for example, in vegetable oils and fish.

Researcher Taisa Venäläinen says that low-fat products are not healthy for children. Low-fat products have only a little fat. Children should use, for example, vegetable margarine that has at least 60 percent fat.
