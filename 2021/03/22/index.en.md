# Monday 22.3.2021 (radio)

Corona restrictions are being planned for public transport in the capital region. The judges for the Finlandia Prizes for literature have been announced. The sun will shine this week.

## Public transport in the capital region

![HSL buses at Rautatientori.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3241,h_1823,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7336635f929d08547ae/1604391765)

The number of passengers on public transport in the capital region may be limited because of corona. Photo: Kristiina Lehto / Yle

Restrictions are being planned for buses, metros and trains in the capital region because of corona. The number of passengers may perhaps be reduced on some routes. In the future, half as many people may be allowed on some trains and buses as now.

Restrictions may come in for the morning and afternoon services, for example, because there are many passengers on them.

Authorities are considering public transport restrictions during this week. The restrictions may come into force at the end of this week or at the start of next week.

Oulu City Hospital has had many corona infections before. A total of 18 people have died of corona in the past month. There have been 118 infections.

Now there are only a few new corona infections at Oulu City Hospital. One ward still has patients who are in quarantine.

## Street dust

![A street sweeper washes and cleans a street in Toukola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_287/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6528095e6b2e5cefe22/1584083058)

Open image viewer

Street dust is in the air most in March and April, but the situation can change quickly depending on the weather. Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

There is a lot of street dust in the capital region now. Because of this, the air quality is worse than usual.

Street dust can cause a runny nose, coughing, and itchy throat and eyes. Street dust is especially harmful for people with asthma, small children and old people.

## Finlandia Prizes for literature

![Director Zaida Bergroth.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_216/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7213925f687b1fe5e9e/1616412430)

Open image viewer

Film director Zaida Bergroth gets to decide the Finlandia Prize for fiction. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

The Finnish Literature Foundation has announced the judges for the Finlandia Prizes for literature.

This year, film director Zaida Bergroth will choose the winner of the Finlandia Prize for fiction. Deputy CEO of Miltton, Katri Makkonen, will decide the Finlandia Prize for non-fiction. Non-fiction writer Perttu Pölönen will choose the winner of the Finlandia Prize for children's and young people's literature.

The Finlandia Prizes for literature will be awarded in December. The prize is 30,000 euros.
