# Saturday 9.6.2018 (radio)

The chairmen of the Centre Party and the National Coalition Party stay in their jobs. HIV tests are being offered at the eastern border. The president moved to Kultaranta for the summer. Finnish sauna day was celebrated on Saturday. Strawberries ripen for Midsummer.

## Orpo and Sipilä continue as party chairmen

![Petteri Orpo and Juha Sipilä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10243756)

National Coalition Party chairman Petteri Orpo and Centre Party chairman Juha Sipilä have been given another term. Photo: AOP

The party congresses of the Centre Party and the National Coalition Party continued on Saturday. The chairmen of both parties were given another term.

Juha Sipilä has led the Centre Party for 6 years. Petteri Orpo was chosen to lead the National Coalition Party 2 years ago.

The Centre Party congress is in Sotkamo, and the National Coalition Party congress is in Turku.

## HIV tests are offered at the eastern border

![Condoms at a factory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10238662)

Open image viewer

Condoms protect against sexually transmitted diseases. Photo: EPA

HIV testing is offered at 3 eastern border stations in Finland. The Finnish Red Cross and Hivpoint are on duty at the Imatra, Vartius and Niirala border stations on Saturday and Sunday.

The hospital districts on the eastern border have recorded a large number of HIV infections compared with the rest of Finland. Most of those infected are men over 60 who caught the infection on the Russian side.

The HIV test is free, and you get the result in 1 minute. In addition, the organisations hand out condoms and information about safe sex.

## The president's family moves to Kultaranta

![The president's summer residence, Kultaranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5457835)

Open image viewer

The president's summer residence Kultaranta is in Naantali. Photo: Jyrki Valkama / YLE

The family of Tasavalta President Sauli Niinistö has moved to their summer residence at Kultaranta.

The city of Naantali held a reception at the harbour. At the same time, President Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio met the public.

Kultaranta in Naantali has been the president's summer residence since 1922. The public can get to know the Kultaranta park area on Friday evenings. Guided tours are also organised in the area.

## Finnish sauna day

![A woman in a sauna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10010940)

Open image viewer

Sauna bathing in Finland may be added to UNESCO's list of intangible cultural heritage. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finnish sauna day was celebrated on Saturday. At the Helsinki Railway Square there was a Finland Saunas event. 9 saunas were brought to the square.

Representatives of the Sauna to UNESCO project from across Finland were also there. The Ministry of Education and Culture proposes that sauna bathing be added to UNESCO's list of intangible cultural heritage. UNESCO is the cultural organisation of the United Nations, that is, the UN.

In the autumn, a Finland Saunas day will be declared. Then it is hoped that as many people as possible will have a sauna in Finland at the same time.

## Strawberries will be ready for Midsummer

![Strawberries ripening at the Mattila organic farm in Nurmijärvi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9746670)

Open image viewer

Strawberries will be ready to celebrate Midsummer. Photo: Tuomo Björksten/Yle

Strawberries will ripen by Midsummer. The harvest is ripening earlier than last year, when the main harvest only ripened in the early part of July. The success of the main harvest still needs rain and about 20 degrees of warmth. The strawberry fields in Southern Finland and on the west coast need rain the most.

Frost on the ground has not yet harmed the strawberries. Strawberries can be protected with fleece. However, problems may come if night frosts continue for a long time.

The strawberry season is very short this year.
