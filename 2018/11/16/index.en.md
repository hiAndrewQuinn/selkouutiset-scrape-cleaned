## Few young people in Finland

![Street view](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10511122)

Open image viewer

This year is already the eighth such year in Finland in which the birth rate falls. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

According to Statistics Finland, there are now very few young people in Finland.

In Finland there may be 760,000 young people under 15 in total in 2030. The calculation is based on birth rates staying at the current level.

In the 2050s the number of young people may fall to below 700,000. Still in the 1970s there were a million under-15s in Finland.

Finland's population may turn to decline in 2035. This year is already the eighth such year in Finland in which the birth rate falls.

## Free flu vaccination for children

![A child is given a nasal spray.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10493838)

Open image viewer

A child can have the flu vaccine as a nasal spray instead of an injection. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

This autumn, for the first time, all children under 7 will get a free flu vaccination.

Vaccinating children is useful. When a child is healthy, the whole family stays healthy.

For young children influenza can be a serious illness. In addition, an ear infection or sometimes even pneumonia can come as a complication of influenza.

According to a study, vaccinating under-7s is a very effective way of reducing influenza in general.

## A very old great tit was found in Finland

![Great tit](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10129864)

Open image viewer

A great tit's life has many threats. It has to survive the frost, predators and accidents. Photo: Jouko Tykkyläinen

Bird enthusiast Christer Kalenius has been very lucky.

The oldest great tit in Finland, found by Kalenius, is almost 13 years old. Kalenius ringed the great tit in summer 2004. The bird was then one year old.

About 12 years later Kalenius caught the same bird in a net. At the time of the finding, the great tit was just over 12 years and 10 months old.

About 250,000 birds are ringed in Finland every year. Ringing is a good way to find out the age and movements of wild birds.

The oldest bird in Finland has been 36 years old.

## The sun will shine soon

The weather has recently been grey and damp. On Saturday the weather changes. Dry air flows into Finland and the sun begins to shine.

In the area stretching from the southern part of Finland to Western Lapland the weather is partly sunny.

The wind gusts across the whole country, and especially in Lapland the gusts are strong.
