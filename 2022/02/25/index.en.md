# Friday 25.2.2022 (radio)

War in Ukraine. People are fleeing the country. Niinistö and Zelenskyy. The Eurovision Song Contest. Weather.

## Russia's military actions in Ukraine

![Russian armoured vehicles on a train.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1079,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9204126218b5cbc1f2b/1645786766)

Image released by Russian authorities of Russian armoured vehicles on a train just before the attack on Ukraine. Photo: EyePress News/Shutterstock/All Over Press

Fighting in Ukraine has intensified. Russian forces have already advanced into the northern parts of the capital Kyiv. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy accuses Russia of attacks on civilian targets.

The mayor of Kyiv says that the Ukrainian capital has moved to the defence phase. Britain says that the country's main cities are still under the control of Ukrainian forces.

## Ukrainians are fleeing

![Traffic leaving Kyiv on Thursday morning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3535,h_1988,x_8,y_95/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-919569621725046c2f6/1645791350)

Open image viewer

Thousands of Ukrainians are trying to flee the country to neighbouring countries for safety. Photo: Pierre Crom / Getty Images

The UN refugee agency says that at least 100,000 Ukrainians have fled their homes. Thousands of people have travelled to the neighbouring countries of Moldova, Romania and Poland.

Already on Thursday, almost 11,000 Ukrainians crossed the border into the neighbouring country Romania after the morning attack. Some of them went on to Hungary.

The roads out of Ukraine are full of cars.

Finland, too, is preparing for the arrival of refugees. For example, the Turku reception centre can accommodate just under 00 people right away.

The Finnish Red Cross also has other premises for temporary accommodation.

## The presidents talked

![Volodymyr Zelensky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4129,h_2319,x_0,y_260/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89677361caeb49878e3/1640688712)

Open image viewer

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky. Photo: Johanna Geron / AFP

President Sauli Niinistö has spoken by phone with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky.

Niinistö said that Finland is giving almost 15 million euros of extra support to Ukraine. The support is given to a fund that supports Ukraine's resilience, that is, its ability to endure. Money is also given for humanitarian aid.

Niinistö said again on the phone that Finland strongly condemns Russia's military actions in Ukraine.

## Russia and Eurovision

![Eurovision winner, Italy's Måneskin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5258,h_2958,x_0,y_550/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80982560aa059bc6689/1621755390)

Open image viewer

Last year Italy won the Eurovision Song Contest. Photo: Robin Van Lonkhuijsen / EPA

Finland's broadcasting company Yle is appealing to the European Broadcasting Union EBU because of the Eurovision Song Contest. Yle hopes that the EBU will exclude Russia from the Eurovision Song Contest.

Yle does not intend to take part in the competition in Turin, Italy, if Russia is taking part.

Yle says that Russia's attack on Ukraine goes against all the values that Yle and other European public broadcasters represent. Sweden's public broadcaster SVT asked the EBU about the matter yesterday morning.

Finland's representative for this year's Eurovision will be chosen tomorrow, Saturday, in the final of the New Music Competition. The broadcast will be commented in easy language for the first time, on Yle Areena. The broadcast starts tomorrow evening at 9 p.m. on Yle Areena and Yle TV1.

## Weather

Dry air will flow into Finland for Saturday. Tomorrow, Saturday, is a little colder, and on wide areas it is a sunny day.

The temperature in the south is between 0 and -4 degrees, and in the north it is 6 to 12 degrees below zero.

During the night towards Sunday, snowfalls move east in the north. The day is dry, fairly sunny and windy.
