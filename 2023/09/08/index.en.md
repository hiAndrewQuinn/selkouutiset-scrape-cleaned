## Confidence in the government

![Prime Minister Petteri Orpo gives an opening speech in the plenary session.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3984,h_2241,x_0,y_9/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116829864f86679ac3df/1694000844)

Petteri Orpo's government continues, because it has the confidence of the majority of parliament. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The government has won parliament's confidence.

Parliament voted on Friday on whether it trusts the government. The government won the vote.

106 MPs trust the government. 65 MPs from the opposition parties voted against the government.

In addition, the MPs of the opposition party Centre voted blank, that is, they did not take a position on the matter.

The opposition wanted a vote on the government's confidence. The reason was the recent racism debate.

The old racist writings of some Finns Party ministers have angered many people.

Prime Minister Petteri Orpo's government can now continue its work, because it has the confidence of the majority of parliament.

## Industrial orders

![Empty plastic hand sanitiser bottles on the way to a filling machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2700,h_1519,x_0,y_215/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6589975e80a38620cb2/1585492181)

Industry is doing badly. Statistics Finland says that in July industry received 15 percent fewer new orders than a year earlier.

Orders decreased most in the chemical industry. It received over 20 percent fewer new orders in July than a year ago.

Industrial orders have been decreasing in Finland for 6 months already.

## Floods in Hong Kong

![A woman walks along a street that is covered by water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5962,h_3353,x_0,y_148/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116933364faf0b042bf8/1694185160)

There are floods in Hong Kong in Asia. At least 2 people have died because of the floods, and there are many injured.

The cause of the floods is heavy rain. The rain has brought so much water onto the streets of Hong Kong that traffic in Hong Kong has been in chaos.

More rain has fallen than ever before in the measurement history. Measurement of rainfall began in Hong Kong about 140 years ago.

## Protective fence for Havis Amanda

![Illustration of a cylinder-shaped protective net around the Havis Amanda statue.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3543,h_1992,x_0,y_94/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116923464face4b51b50/1694158665)

Open image viewer

This is what the Havis Amanda protective fence looks like, if it is carried out. Photo: Aaro Sariola / WSP

The famous Helsinki statue Havis Amanda, also known as Manta, is in the news again. On Friday it was reported that a steel fence is to be built around Manta.

The steel fence will be built when Manta has been repaired. Manta was taken away from the Helsinki Market Square when repair work started in May. Manta will return to its own place next year.

The aim is that the steel fence will protect Manta. Finns have a habit of climbing on Manta when it is May Day or when Finland becomes, for example, the world champion in ice hockey.

Manta, which is over 100 years old, may be damaged if climbing on it does not stop. That is why it is protected with a fence, which costs about 135 000 euros.
