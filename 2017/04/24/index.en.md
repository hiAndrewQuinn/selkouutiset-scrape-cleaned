# Monday 24.4.2017 (radio)

Emmanuel Macron and Marine Le Pen got through to the second round of the French presidential election. The ice on Lake Näsijärvi melted at the weekend. Nowadays older people also order food from a restaurant to take home. The Little Lions have won the silver medal in the ice hockey World Championship.

## The presidential election continues in France

![Macron and Le Pen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9579317)

Open image viewer

France's next president will be Emmanuel Macron or Marine Le Pen. Photo: AOP

The French have voted in the presidential election. On Sunday it was the first round of the election. The most votes were won by Emmanuel Macron and Marine Le Pen. They will go on to the second round of the election.

Emmanuel Macron got the most votes on Sunday. He got about 24 percent of the votes.

Macron supports European cooperation. He wants France to stay in the European Union. Macron also wants borders in Europe to stay open.

Marine Le Pen got the second most votes in the first round. She got about 22 percent of the votes.

Le Pen does not want immigrants in France. In Le Pen's opinion, jobs, housing and social benefits in France belong to the French. Le Pen does not like the European Union either. She wants the French to vote on whether France leaves the EU.

The second round of the election is in 2 weeks' time. Then it will become clear whether France's next president is Macron or Le Pen.

## The ice left Lake Näsijärvi

![Ice-free Lake Näsijärvi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9579107)

Open image viewer

There is no more ice on Näsijärvi. Photo: Milka Koskinen / Yle

Spring is coming to Finland too, although slowly. One sign of spring is that the ice on the lakes melts.

At the weekend the ice left Näsijärvi. Tampere, for example, is on the shore of Näsijärvi. In the area it is said that spring begins when the ice on Näsijärvi has melted.

Spring has not always begun on Näsijärvi this early. For example, in 1876 Näsijärvi was frozen almost until Midsummer.

## Older people buy takeaway food

![Food portions on a table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9156039)

Open image viewer

Restaurant food can also be bought to take home. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

Finns often get food from a restaurant to take home. Takeaway food now interests older people too. For example, it is reported from a Chinese restaurant in Kajaani that older people come to collect takeaway portions.

85-year-old Saimi Tikkanen from Kajaani says that takeaway food brightens life. Tikkanen sometimes goes to get food from a Chinese or Japanese restaurant. Takeaway food is a good option, because one portion is enough food for many days, Tikkanen says.

## The Little Lions won silver in the World Championship

![The Little Lions advanced to the semi-finals.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9574023)

Open image viewer

Finland's Little Lions, that is the under-18 ice hockey team. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

The Little Lions have won the silver medal in the ice hockey World Championship. Finland's Little Lions, that is the under-18 players, lost the final match on Sunday.

In the final Finland played against the USA. The USA scored 4 goals in the game, but Finland only 2. The USA won and is this year's world champion. Finland was world champion last year.
