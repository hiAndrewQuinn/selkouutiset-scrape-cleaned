## Wille Rydman

MP Wille Rydman is leaving the National Coalition Party's parliamentary group.

Rydman also gives up the posts that the National Coalition parliamentary group nominated him to, such as committee seats.

The chair of the National Coalition Party, Petteri Orpo, says that the reason for Rydman's departure is a lack of trust. The parliamentary group does not trust Rydman. The reason is that Helsingin Sanomat reported earlier this week that MP Ville Rydman has behaved inappropriately towards underage girls.

## Employment

![TE Office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7404535faa971c771d2/1605017520)

The employment situation has improved. The employment rate has risen and unemployment has fallen.

In May there were 68,000 fewer unemployed people than in May last year. In May there were 227,000 unemployed Finns.

Many new jobs are part-time or fixed-term. The unemployment statistics show no signs of a downturn, even though in Finland there has been fear of an economic downturn.

## Illnesses of the corona time

![A man blows his nose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6151165dd92fa744685/1576160788)

Open image viewer

Although there was corona disease in Finland, there was not much influenza in Finland. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The corona time has reduced other respiratory diseases.

The Institute for Health and Welfare says that last year, in the influenza season, there was no influenza epidemic in Finland. There was influenza only in individual people, but influenza did not spread much.

Bacteria that cause common intestinal infections were also less common than usual last year.

## Helsinki-Vantaa airport

![Passengers queue at the airport for a security check.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5388,h_3030,x_0,y_67/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96835962a33010f210d/1654862062)

Open image viewer

Helsinki-Vantaa airport now has one terminal from which all passengers depart. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

A big reform was carried out at Helsinki-Vantaa airport on Tuesday. The check-in and security check for all flights are now in one place.

Earlier Helsinki-Vantaa airport had 2 terminals. The terminals have been combined and all passengers now come to one terminal.

The change is part of the renewal of Helsinki-Vantaa airport. The airport will be expanded, that is, more space will be built there.

## Summer solstice

![Evening glow and lake landscape on Päijänne.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6976805f070c8b2a970/1594300978)

Open image viewer

North of the Arctic Circle the sun does not set. Photo: Miia Maria Lahti / Yle

Today, Tuesday, is the summer solstice. In the northern hemisphere it is the longest day of the year. North of the Arctic Circle, for example in Lapland, the sun does not set at night.

In Southern Finland the length of the day on Tuesday is about 19 hours.

On Wednesday the day starts to shorten again.

## The weather

The weather is warm over wide areas. Only in the north is it cold.

On Wednesday there will be showers in the central part of the country. In the north cloudiness will increase and at times a little rain will fall. The temperature is between 14 and 22 degrees. In North Lapland between 5 and 10 degrees.
