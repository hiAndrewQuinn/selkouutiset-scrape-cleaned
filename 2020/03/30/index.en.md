## 13 people have died of the coronavirus in Finland

Health authorities say that there are now more than 1,300 coronavirus infections in Finland.

These are infections that have been confirmed by a test.

Only people who have serious symptoms can get a test. Some people do not get any symptoms at all, even though they have an infection.

The coronavirus has spread across the whole of Finland.

13 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus. There are 49 people in intensive care in hospitals.

## There is congestion at the Uusimaa border

![Police checkpoint on main road 4 at the Orimattila junction.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11281819)

Open image viewer

The police check at the border whether a person has a good reason to cross the border. Photo: Nina Svahn / Yle

The border of the southern part of Finland, that is, the Uusimaa region, has been closed for 3 weeks.

A person may move across the border only if there is a good reason, for example, you may cross the border to go to work.

In the morning there was a heavy traffic jam at the Uusimaa border.

Many people drove to work by car, and the police stopped all people at the border. The police checked whether everyone had a good reason to drive across the border.

Employers must write a permission note for the employee.

The police say that in the morning there was too much traffic at the Uusimaa border.

The police say that everyone must now think about whether it is absolutely necessary to go to work across the border.

If you absolutely have to go to work, it is a good idea to leave early.

## A modern sawmill is being built in Rauma

![Metsä Fibre's new pine sawmill in Rauma.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7942,h_4467,x_0,y_12/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6591125e81a2a582abe/1585554218)

Metsä Group is also building a new factory. It will come to this area in Kemi .  Photo: Metsä Group

Now a good economic news story.

The Finnish company Metsä Group is building a new sawmill in Rauma, in Western Finland.

The sawmill will be the most modern in the whole world. Construction will start during the spring.

The sawmill may be ready in 2022.

When the sawmill is ready, it will give work to 100 people.

In addition, 500 people will get work when outside companies do some work for the sawmill.

## The Moomins will speak Northern Sami

![The second season of Moomin Valley.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8000,h_4500,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6464985e54c52abce64/1582614363)

The Moomin Valley TV series will soon become a special version.

In the new version, the Moomins speak the Northern Sami language.

Here, Moomintroll speaks Northern Sami.

Finland has 3 official Sami languages. They are Northern Sami, Inari Sami and Skolt Sami.

Moomin Valley in Northern Sami will come on television in April.
