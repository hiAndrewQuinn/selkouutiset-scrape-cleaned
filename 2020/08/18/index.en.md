# Tuesday 18.8.2020 (radio)

A record amount of new wind power is planned for Finland. Big supermarket chains continue to offer hand sanitiser to customers. Printed letter post will not be delivered on Fridays until the end of October. Sales of paracetamol and dexamethasone have been restricted. 2 men have been arrested because of the car bomb in Tornio.

## New wind power is being built

![Three wind turbines.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11499021)

Open image viewer

More wind power is being planned or built in Finland than ever before.

A record amount of new wind power is planned for Finland. For example, in Central Finland there are 20 wind power parks in progress.

Finland still needs more energy that does not produce emissions into the environment. In addition, the technology has developed. That is why wind power can also be produced well inland.

However, building wind turbines affects nature. For example, in Central Finland some of the wind power may be located in a so-called quiet area. A quiet area has not had sounds caused by human activity.

## Hand sanitiser is still available in shops

![Hand sanitiser being taken from a pump bottle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_144/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6995715f0efe85a1d01/1595522886)

Open image viewer

The distribution of hand sanitiser in shops continues. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

The biggest supermarket chains in Finland continue to offer hand sanitiser to customers. During the corona time, the chains have handed out hand sanitiser in shops. About 100,000 litres of free hand sanitiser has been given to customers.

Hand sanitiser dispensers and bottles appeared at shop doors in the spring. Lidl and the S Group say that consumption has fallen a little during the summer.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare hopes that shops will continue to hand out hand sanitiser. Using hand sanitiser prevents ordinary stomach bugs and flu from spreading, as well as corona.

## Letter post is still not delivered on Fridays

![A postal worker delivers post by bicycle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3012,h_1694,x_0,y_264/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6560755e74d41c67230/1584714961)

Open image viewer

Post is not delivered on Fridays. The practice is valid until at least the end of October. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Posti will continue the so-called reduced delivery until at least the end of October. Printed letter post is not delivered at all on Fridays.

Reduced delivery began in early June. Posti can sort the post faster when there is no delivery on Friday.

Parcels and newspapers that Posti delivers are still delivered on Fridays too.

## There are medicine restrictions in pharmacies

![Pharmacy](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4470,h_2514,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6594705e821597922e3/1585584147)

Open image viewer

You can no longer get a large amount of paracetamol from pharmacies. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health has restricted the sale and use of 2 medicines. These medicines are paracetamol and dexamethasone.

The Ministry wants to make sure that there is enough medicine during the corona pandemic.

The anti-inflammatory drug dexamethasone may be obtained with a prescription in an amount of at most 3 months at one time. The pain and fever medicine paracetamol may be bought in at most the size of the largest pack.

The restrictions are valid until the middle of January.

## Arrests over a car explosion in Tornio

![The rental car used by the Border Guard was parked by the side of the street on Aarnintie in Tornio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3866,h_2175,x_367,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7049285f2942b8bc3e4/1596544738)

Open image viewer

The rental car was parked by the side of the street on Aarnintie in Tornio. The car was used by the Border Guard. Photo: Juuso Stoor / Yle

2 men have been arrested for blowing up a car in Tornio in Lapland. The Lapland District Court ordered the men, aged 38 and 20, into pre-trial detention.

They are suspected of criminal damage and an explosives offence.

2 weeks ago in Tornio the rear of a car exploded when there was a bomb under the car. The van was rented, and it was used by the Border Guard.

Nobody was injured in the explosion.
