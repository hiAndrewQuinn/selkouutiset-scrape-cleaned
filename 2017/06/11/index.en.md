# Sunday 11.6.2017 (radio)

Finland's government will discuss on Monday whether it can keep working after the change of chair in the Finns Party. Nils Torvalds is the presidential candidate of the Swedish People's Party, RKP. There are parliamentary elections in France. When must you address someone with the formal "te" in Finnish?

## The government's situation

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä says that on Monday it will become clear whether the government can continue working. The government has 3 parties: Sipilä's own party, the Centre, the National Coalition and the Finns Party.

The Finns Party chair changed on Saturday. The new chair is Jussi Halla-aho, who does not want immigrants in Finland. He also hopes that Finland will leave the European Union.

The Centre and the National Coalition disagree with Halla-aho on many matters. That is why the leaders of the government parties will discuss co-operation on Monday.

## The Swedish People's Party's presidential candidate

![MEP Nils Torvalds](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9663093)

RKP presidential candidate Nils Torvalds. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Swedish People's Party, RKP, has chosen its presidential candidate. The candidate is Nils Torvalds.

Torvalds is one of Finland's representatives in the European Parliament. He is 71 years old.

The presidential election will be held in January next year. It is fairly certain that the election will be won by the current president, Sauli Niinistö, who wants to continue as President of Finland.

## The French election

![President Emmanuel Macron and his wife Brigitte Trogneux cast their votes in Le Touquet in northern France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9663448)

There was an election in France on Sunday. The French have now voted in the parliamentary election. This was the first round of the parliamentary election, and the second round is in a week's time.

The election is important for France's new president, Emmanuel Macron. Macron needs the support of Parliament when he wants to change France's policy.

Opinion polls predict that Macron's party will do well in the election. Many French people are satisfied with the new president. Macron began as President of France in May.

## Formal address

![A shopkeeper hands a customer a packaged product at the service counter of a supermarket.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5288658)

Using the formal "te" is difficult in Finnish. When must you say "te" to one person?

The matter is difficult for many immigrants who study Finnish. But Finns too find it hard to decide whom they address with "te", and when.

Many people think that older people are addressed with "te". But it is hard to decide who is old. That is why many Finns avoid the problem. Many have heard, for example, the question in a shop: "Mitä saisi olla?" (What would you like?). The salesperson simply leaves out the word "teille" or "sinulle" from the question.

In Finnish the formal "te" is used much less than, for example, in Russian, French or German. In Finland it is good to remember that speech can be polite even without the formal "te". Eye contact and gestures tell a lot.
