Inflation, that is, the rise in prices, is speeding up in Europe.

In July inflation in euro countries is 8.9 percent. In June inflation was 8.6 percent.

Prices are now rising fastest in Estonia, where inflation is 22.7 percent. In Finland inflation in July is 7.9 percent, calculates the EU's statistics office Eurostat.

The price of energy has risen especially. Also, for example, food, alcohol and tobacco cost more than before.

## Flat prices

![Apartment block in Kruununhaa in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82750560dd97fbd39bc/1625135997)

Open image viewer

In Helsinki the prices of flats in apartment blocks have even fallen. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Statistics Finland reports on flat prices. In the spring the prices of older flats rose more elsewhere in Finland than in big cities.

In big cities the prices of older flats rose by 1.4 percent. In Helsinki and Vantaa the rise in prices stayed under 1 percent. In Tampere, however, flat prices rose by 4.8 percent.

Elsewhere in the country the prices of older flats rose by 2.3 percent.

## Driving

![Petrol prices on Neste's digital display.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98781262e282de978ab/1659011966)

Open image viewer

Many Finns drive less by car than before. The reason is that petrol is expensive.

A survey says that 44 percent of people drive by car only for important trips. Only 25 percent of people drive by car as much as before.

The people who answered the survey spend on average 330 euros a month on driving.

There are fewer petrol stations in Finland than before.

There are 700 petrol stations in Finland. In addition, there are over 1100 unmanned fuel stations in Finland. You can get petrol at an unmanned station, but no other services.

The number of petrol stations has been falling all the time since the 1990s.
