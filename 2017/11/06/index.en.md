# Monday 6.11.2017 (radio)

The US defence secretary is visiting Finland. International media have received secret information about tax havens. Many municipalities give old people anti-slip grips for their shoes free of charge. More than 230 bears have been hunted in Finland this year. On Monday, Finland celebrated Swedish Day.

## Mattis in Finland

![ Defence Secretary James Mattis and Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9918460)

Defence Secretary James Mattis and President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The US defence secretary is visiting Finland. Secretary James Mattis met President Sauli Niinistö on Monday.

Niinistö and Mattis held talks at the Presidential Palace. They also met journalists.

Secretary Mattis said that the USA's relationship with Finland is now stronger than ever before. Mattis also congratulated Finland, because independent Finland turns 100 this year.

## Tax havens

![On Tuesday 6 April 2016, someone had written the word "tax parasite" in chalk in front of Nordea's bank branch on Aleksanterinkatu in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8793276)

People can get angry if they suspect a company of having links to a tax haven. Photo: Yle

International media have received secret information about tax havens.

For example, many companies move business and money to countries where they may not have to pay taxes at all. Such countries are called tax havens.

The new information helps to find out how companies avoid taxes. It may be legal, but it always reduces some country's tax income. Sometimes it is illegal, for example corruption.

The new information also tells about politicians' links to tax havens. No important Finnish politicians are found in the information.

## Anti-slip grips for old people

![An anti-slip grip on a table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9918631)

Open image viewer

The anti-slip grip is attached to a shoe. Photo: Hannu Harhama / Yle

Winter is coming, and many old people are afraid of falling. An old person can be injured very badly if they fall on an icy street.

Many municipalities want to help old people. They give old people anti-slip grips free of charge. The anti-slip grips are attached to shoes. The idea is a bit like winter tyres on a car.

It pays for municipalities to give anti-slip grips to old people free of charge, because that way they can reduce medical costs.

## Bear hunting

![A bear.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9642017)

Open image viewer

The number of bears has grown. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

More than 230 bears have been hunted in Finland this year. That is more than 50 bears more than last year.

It has been possible to hunt more bears than before, because the number of bears has grown. Before this year's hunt began, there were about 1700 bears over one year old in Finland.

Most bears were shot in Eastern Finland.

## Swedish Day

![Artist Tove Jansson photographed in 1991.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7402578)

Open image viewer

Tove Jansson wrote the Moomin books in Swedish. Photo: Seppo Sarkinen / Yle

On Monday, Finland celebrated Swedish Day. On that day, the Swedish language, which is Finland's second official language, is celebrated.

Swedish Day, in Swedish Svenska dagen, is always on 6 November. The day has been celebrated for more than 100 years.

About 300,000 Finns speak Swedish as their mother tongue. Many famous Finns are Swedish-speaking. For example, Tove Jansson wrote the Moomin books in Swedish.
