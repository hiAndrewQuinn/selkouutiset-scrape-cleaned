# Wednesday 12.5.2021 (radio)

Finland's economic growth speeds up to 2.6 percent this year. Those who got the AstraZeneca vaccine will be given a second dose of the Pfizer-BioNTech vaccine. The Tax Administration has given extra time to fill in the tax return. Estonia's grave peace may be suspended. The Savonlinna opera festival is cancelled.

## Finland's economy recovers towards the end of the year

![A 50 euro banknote.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_101/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-5946257a31b7ce9022/1470307299)

The Ministry of Finance has published a new economic forecast.

Finland's economic growth speeds up to 2.6 percent this year. Worldwide recovery and the easing of the corona pandemic are helping the economy.

The ministry estimates that Finland's economy will return to normal during next year. The deficit of public finances may still remain until the middle of the decade.

The forecast is based on the assumption that the corona pandemic will be over in the autumn.

The European Commission has raised its forecast for economic growth in the euro area. The Commission estimates that the economy will grow by 4.3 percent this year in the euro area. The previous forecast was 3.8 percent.

## AstraZeneca is replaced by the Pfizer vaccine for the second dose

![A nurse gives a young woman a Pfizer corona vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-79504660781a01cb3c9/1619062882)

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare has issued a new vaccination recommendation.

Those who received the AstraZeneca vaccine will be given a second dose of the Pfizer-BioNTech vaccine. There are about 100,000 of them. The recommendation applies to people under 65.

Denmark, Norway and Germany, among others, have also decided this.

Combining vaccines interests people all over the world. The AstraZeneca vaccine can cause a rare risk of blood clots.

## The Tax Administration extended the time to submit the tax return

![A person looks at the vero.fi website on a computer screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2991,h_1682,x_112,y_15/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7368125f9d36951db80/1666765174)

Open image viewer

The deadline for submitting the tax return was extended to Friday. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The Tax Administration has given extra time to fill in the tax return. More than 1.5 million people get extra time.

Last night there was a fault in the Tax Administration's OmaVero service. Some customers could not fill in their tax return by the deadline.

The deadline for the return was extended by 3 days, to Friday.

The Tax Administration estimates that most customers have been able to submit their tax return normally.

## Estonia's grave peace may be suspended temporarily

![TV news image of the wreck of M/S Estonia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5938195d8b428bbe219/1662375194)

Open image viewer

The car ferry Estonia is protected by grave peace. Now it is going to be temporarily suspended for research. Photo: TV news

The government proposes that the grave peace of the passenger ship Estonia be temporarily suspended by law. This would make it possible to research the causes of the sinking of the Estonia.

The aim is to study a hole in the wreck. The hole came to light in autumn 2020 from film material. The investigation is being carried out mainly by Estonia and Sweden.

The Estonian ferry sank in September 1994 on its way from Tallinn to Stockholm. 852 people died in the accident, most of them Swedes.

## The Savonlinna opera festival is cancelled

![Olavinlinna](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4593,h_2584,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7017115f184fb1d3aa0/1595429253)

The opera performances of the Savonlinna Opera Festival are cancelled because of the corona epidemic. Karita Mattila's gala concerts are also cancelled, for example.

The opera festival was also cancelled last summer.

In Savonlinna, all other concerts and the Timo Mustakallio singing competition are planned to be held in the summer.
