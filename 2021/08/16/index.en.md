## Afghanistan

![Taliban fighters in Kabul on 16 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12060616)

Taliban fighters in Kabul. Photo: EPA

In Afghanistan the Taliban movement has taken control of the whole country. The Taliban have taken, for example, the presidential palace in the capital, Kabul.

President **Ashraf Ghani** left for exile on Sunday, that is, he travelled out of Afghanistan.

There is chaos at Kabul airport. Very many people are coming to the airport who want to leave Afghanistan. The USA and other countries have been flying their own citizens out. The USA has also flown Finns out of Kabul.

The USA and Germany have sent soldiers to guard Kabul airport.

## Municipalities' climate emissions

![A wind turbine in Hailuoto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8192,h_4608,x_0,y_755/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81646760bb89df57200/1622904105)

Open image viewer

Electricity is obtained from wind turbines, which produce no emissions. Photo: AOP

Municipalities' greenhouse gas emissions have decreased. The Finnish Environment Institute says that municipalities' greenhouse gas emissions fell by over 7 percent last year compared with the year before.

The emissions fell because the use of wind power increased, and therefore the use of fossil fuels decreased. The year was also quite warm, and therefore less electricity production was needed than before. Driving also decreased because of the corona situation.

Greenhouse gases are gases that cause the Earth to warm.

On Monday 451 new corona infections have been reported in Finland.

Corona infections have grown a lot in recent weeks. In the last 2 weeks there have been almost 3,000 more new infections than in the previous 2 weeks.

About 70 percent of Finns have received one corona vaccine. Almost 43 percent of Finns have received both corona vaccines.

## Train traffic

![Pendolino on the railway track.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10366536)

Open image viewer

Train travel has grown, and therefore train services are being increased. Photo: Soile Laaksonen / VR Group

The railway company VR is increasing long-distance train services. VR is adding 26 train services on routes from Helsinki to Turku, Tampere, Joensuu and Lappeenranta.

Because of the corona situation, VR reduced train services last year. Now people are moving about more again, and VR is adding trains to traffic.

Trains will speed up in the autumn. By then the summer track works will be done, and the trains can run faster again.
