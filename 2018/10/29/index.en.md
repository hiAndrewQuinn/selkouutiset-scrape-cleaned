## Merkel is giving up the party chair

![Angela Merkel](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10481390)

Open image viewer

Angela Merkel has been the leader of the CDU for a very long time. Photo: Clemens Bilan / EPA

A big change is happening at the top of politics in Germany. The chair of the German CDU party, Chancellor Angela Merkel, has said that she no longer wants to be the party chair.

Merkel has been the leader of the CDU since 2000. Merkel's CDU party suffered a big defeat at the weekend in the state election in Hesse. Its sister party the CSU also suffered a bad defeat in the Bavarian state election two weeks ago.

But Merkel still wants to be Chancellor, which means she wants to lead the German government. The Chancellor's term ends at the end of 2021. Merkel has been Chancellor for 13 years already.

## Löfven's attempt to form a government did not succeed

![Stefan Löfven](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10480752)

Open image viewer

Stefan Löfven stopped trying to put together a new Swedish government. Photo: Janerik Henriksson / AFP

Putting together a Swedish government is still very difficult. Today the chair of the Swedish Social Democrats, Stefan Löfven, said that he will not continue trying to form a new Swedish government.

Löfven said that he did not get enough support to put together a government. Löfven is now already the second party chair who has not managed to put together a government.

## Ohisalo does not want to lead the Greens

![Maria Ohisalo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10480747)

Open image viewer

Maria Ohisalo said that she does not want to become party chair. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The new chair of the Greens will not be Maria Ohisalo. The Greens' vice-chair Maria Ohisalo said today that she does not want to become chair.

According to Ohisalo, the Greens need a chair who is well known and can convince people that it is worth voting for the Greens in the parliamentary elections.

## Nordea: interest rates will rise in spring

![Floor plans of flats.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10288017)

Open image viewer

Nordea says that mortgage interest rates may rise as early as spring. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The big bank Nordea estimates that mortgage interest rates will start to rise next spring. Nordea says that Finland's most common mortgage rate, the 12-month Euribor, will turn positive in spring.

If you have, for example, a mortgage of 120,000 euros and the interest rate rises by one percentage point, you may have to pay tens of euros more every month. Nordea says that now is a good moment to check whether your own money matters are in order, that is, whether your own finances are balanced.

## Summer and winter time may continue

src="https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6116413"

Open image viewer

Clocks may be changed to winter time and summer time for many more years. Photo: YLE

Changing the clocks to winter time and summer time may continue for many more years. Austria, which holds the EU presidency, has proposed that EU countries take more time to stop changing the clocks.

Until now it was thought that changing the clocks would end as early as next year. But some countries think that is too fast. It is said that, for example, air traffic needs more time for the change.
