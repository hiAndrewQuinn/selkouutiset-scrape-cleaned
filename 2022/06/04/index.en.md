The school year has ended. On Saturday about 25 000 new upper secondary graduates put on their graduation caps.

About 40 000 people graduate from vocational institutions. The exact number will be known at the end of June.

On Saturday 61 000 children finished comprehensive school.

The end of school has again been celebrated normally after 2 corona years. Schools have held spring celebrations, and at homes graduation parties and other graduation celebrations have been held.

## Defence Forces Flag Day

![Parade at the Defence Forces flag festival on 4 June 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4920,h_2767,x_0,y_102/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-965259629b3ee55e553/1654341472)

The national Flag Day parade of the Defence Forces was held in Helsinki. Photo: Lehtikuva

Today, Saturday, the Defence Forces Flag Day is being celebrated. After two years of corona break, the national Flag Day parade was also held. The parade was held in Helsinki.

A total of more than 1600 people took part in the parade, from the land forces, the navy and the air force, and from veterans' and national defence organisations.

Vehicles, aircraft and helicopters were part of the Flag Day parade.

## President Niinistö's speech on Flag Day

![President Sauli Niinistö spoke at the Flag Day parade review of the Defence Forces at Senate Square in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4494,h_2527,x_0,y_351/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-965248629b2ca1bafb0/1654336718)

President Sauli Niinistö gave a speech to the troops of the Defence Forces Flag Day parade.

Niinistö said that Finland is always itself responsible for defending Finland. Niinistö said that this is also the case in the future, even though Finland joins the military alliance NATO and Finland gets support from other NATO countries.

President Niinistö reminded that the world situation is uncertain, and that is why one's own army is very important for Finland.

## Long-lasting corona disease

![Start frame for long covid](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1280,h_720,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7441415fb683c66acbb/1605796950)

Open image viewer

Corona testing. Photo: Yle / Taisto Lapila

More long-lasting corona disease is being diagnosed in Finland than before. The diagnoses of long-lasting corona disease have quadrupled this year.

Last year long-lasting corona disease was diagnosed in 2 500 patients in Finland. This year long-lasting corona disease has already been diagnosed in 11 000 patients. The reason is that corona infections have increased, and that doctors now recognise the symptoms of long-lasting corona disease better than before.

The causes of long-lasting corona disease are not known. Research is being done in Finland to find out the causes of long-lasting corona symptoms.
