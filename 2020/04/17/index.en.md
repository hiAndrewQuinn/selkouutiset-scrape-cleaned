# Friday 17.4.2020 (radio)

People's ability to cope is to be supported in the middle of the corona crisis. The education minister hopes for school meals also for distance-learning pupils. Thousands of people have already been laid off. Calls to emergency centres have decreased. Road surfacing started earlier than usual.

## The Finland Works campaign

![Sanna Marin and Sauli Niinistö, who took part by remote connection.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11311143)

President Sauli Niinistö and Prime Minister Sanna Marin opened a new campaign to support citizens. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The Government has started a campaign to support Finns in the middle of the corona crisis. The campaign aims to strengthen the community spirit and the ability of Finns to cope.

The campaign, called Finland Works, was opened in the afternoon by the President of the Republic, Sauli Niinistö, and Prime Minister Sanna Marin.

Several civil society organisations are part of the campaign. They provide people with information and ways to deal with the different feelings that the corona crisis causes.

At the opening of the campaign, President Sauli Niinistö once again appealed to Finns to follow the instructions that have been given because of the coronavirus.

## School lunches for distance-learning pupils

![School food on a tray.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_439/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6372925e33e8d9a817d/1586964044)

School lunch. Photo: Marie Söderman / Yle

Education Minister Li Andersson says that municipalities must organise school meals also for pupils who are doing distance learning. Andersson says that not a single child must be left hungry because of the corona epidemic.

A survey by Yle shows that now, in the exceptional circumstances, school meal practices are very different.

About half of the municipalities organise school meals also for pupils who are doing distance learning. More than half of the municipalities, on the other hand, do not organise school meals at all, or food is offered only to some distance-learning pupils.

## Layoffs

![An OAJ poster with the texts "peace at work, please" and "a layoff is paralysis".](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7644614)

Open image viewer

A layoff means unpaid compulsory leave. Photo: Petra Ketonen / Yle

More than 140,000 people in Finland have now been laid off from work, says the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment. In April, new layoffs have started at a rate of about 6,000 a day.

A layoff means that the employer orders the employee to take leave for some time. No wages are paid to the employee for that time.

Employees have been laid off most in Uusimaa, about 53,000.

Among occupations, most laid off are sales staff and shopkeepers, and restaurant and large-scale catering workers.

## Emergency centres

![An emergency centre operator with the logo of the emergency centre organisation on her arm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10643721)

Open image viewer

Emergency centre operator. Photo: Salli Koivunen / Yle

Fewer reports are now coming to emergency centres than before the corona crisis.

Normally about 7,700 reports come to emergency centres a day. Now there are about 800 fewer reports a day.

For example, reports of falls have decreased. On the other hand, more reports of breathing difficulties are made than before.

## Road surfacing

![A machine spreading new asphalt.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10859970)

Open image viewer

A machine spreads new asphalt on the road. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

Road surfacing work has started in Southern Finland.

The surfacing work has started earlier than usual, because the winter was mild.

The road surfaces will be renewed this summer over a distance of about 4,000 kilometres.
