## Coronavirus tests may give wrong results

![Coronavirus testing at Mehiläinen's drive-in station in Tapiola, Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2814,h_1583,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6555835e739a115e6b5/1584634466)

The coronavirus is detected from a nasopharyngeal or throat swab. The biggest uncertainties in testing relate to sampling. Photo: Janne Järvinen / Yle

Some of the coronavirus test results may be wrong. Especially among negative test results, up to half may be wrong.

The errors are usually caused by problems in taking the sample. The timing of the test is also important.

The most reliable result is obtained when the test is done soon after becoming ill. If the test is done too early or too late, the virus will not be found.

## Refugee children will soon arrive in Finland from Greece

![Refugee children without a guardian.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11324882)

Open image viewer

Refugee children living without a guardian travelled from Greece to Germany on 18 April. Photo: Orestis Panagiotou / EPA

The first refugee children will arrive in Finland from the camps in Greece at the end of spring. The Finnish Immigration Service says that children can also be brought from Cyprus, Malta and Italy.

In February the Government decided that Finland will receive 175 asylum seekers from the Mediterranean area who are in a vulnerable position.

Primarily, single-parent families and children without a guardian will arrive in Finland.

## Kela has a new benefit for parents

![A man on his way to a Kela office in Itäkeskus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11324623)

Open image viewer

Epidemic support is paid during the exceptional conditions, that is, for at least the period between 16.3 and 13.5.2020. If the exceptional conditions end earlier, the payment of the support also stops. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Kela is starting to pay a new benefit because of the emergency situation.

Parents who look after a child at home because of the corona epidemic may receive epidemic support. The condition is that the parents have had to take unpaid leave from work.

In addition, people who have been sent into quarantine because of arriving from abroad may receive support.

The support is temporary, and it can also be applied for retroactively. The amount of support is just over 700 euros a month.

## Corona shows in housing

![A woman napping on a sofa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11289497)

Open image viewer

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Almost three out of four Finns are managing their housing costs despite the corona crisis. This is what a survey by the banking group Nordea shows.

One in four says that the situation is now harder than before the crisis. Young people, families with children and those who rent are especially experiencing problems.

6 percent of respondents have applied to their bank for a repayment holiday on their mortgage because of the crisis.

## Now is the time to control invasive species

![Giant hogweed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9714374)

Open image viewer

When you are removing giant hogweed, protect your skin with clothing. In sunny weather, giant hogweed can cause burns on the skin. Photo: AOP

It is worth controlling harmful alien plant species in spring. An alien species is a plant that is not originally native to a particular area.

People move alien species from one place to another, by accident or on purpose. An alien species can harm other species.

For example, giant hogweed is easiest to get rid of while the stands are still small. Controlling the species requires protecting yourself. Giant hogweed can, together with sunlight, cause burns to the skin.

Other harmful species include, for example, Himalayan balsam, large-leaved lupin and rugosa rose.

Control of alien plant species must begin before the seeds ripen.
