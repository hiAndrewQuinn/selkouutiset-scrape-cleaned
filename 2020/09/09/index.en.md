# Wednesday 9.9.2020 (radio)

More than 300 people have been exposed to corona in Mikkeli. Corona has slowed down the economy. More and more children in Finland are overweight. The Moria migrant camp in Greece has been destroyed in fires - nobody was injured.

## There are many corona infections in Mikkeli

More than 300 people have been exposed to corona in Mikkeli in Eastern Finland. Not all the exposed people have been reached yet.

The Chief Medical Officer says that new contacts keep coming to light all the time. In the area, 21 coronavirus infections have been found in a few days.

In the whole of August only 5 infections were found.

The infections are linked to the ice hockey team Mikkelin Jukurit. In the Jukurit organisation, a total of 20 coronavirus infections have been found.

The team, the coaches and other staff are in 14 days of quarantine.

A large part of the people who caught the infection have been moving around actively at a time when the disease can spread. The whole centre of Mikkeli was a corona exposure area at the weekend.

In the whole of Finland, 93 new corona infections have been reported today. About 10 cases were missing from yesterday's figures.

## Many young people have been laid off

![A customer steps into the Helsinki Employment Service Point at the TE Office in Pasila.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2683,h_1509,x_0,y_151/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7114415f44a29cae2c1/1598333724)

Corona has slowed down the economy. The layoffs have hit people in their prime working age. Among them are many young workers.

In the first half of the year there were almost 700,000 unemployed job seekers in the labour market.

Now the layoffs have clearly decreased. For example, young women are getting back to work in service sectors.

The situation in industry is still uncertain, however. Layoffs may still turn into dismissals.

## Child overweight is becoming more common

![Child overweight, girls and boys.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11533200)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

More and more children in Finland are too heavy.

The number of overweight children and young people has multiplied over the last decades. This is told in a report by the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

Of the boys, 27 percent were at least overweight last year. The number of overweight girls was 17 percent.

The biggest causes of overweight are the wrong food and eating too much soft drinks and sweets.

Overweight among children and young people is more common in rural municipalities than in cities.

## The Moria camp was evacuated because of the fires

![Migrants at the Moria camp on the island of Lesbos.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11534556)

Open image viewer

Conditions in the Moria camp have been miserable. Its capacity was only 3,000 people. However, there were 13,000 people there. Photo: Manolis Lagoutaris / AFP

A state of emergency has been declared on the Greek island of Lesbos. The state of emergency is because of fires at the Moria migrant camp.

The Moria camp was completely destroyed in bush fires in the early hours of the morning. About 13,000 people lived in the camp.

Everyone was brought to safety, and nobody was injured. Now the camp residents are on the hills near the camp.

The authorities are now trying to find out whether the fires were set on purpose.
