# Saturday 15.5.2021 (radio)

Parliament has ended the debate on the recovery package. There are 192 new coronavirus infections in Finland. The police ended a party of young people in Lahti. China's rover has landed on Mars. Behm celebrated at the Emma gala.

## Recovery package

![Parliament's support package at work.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5355,h_3012,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-806546609f92d8dd35d/1621070678)

Finns Party MP Sebastian Tynkkynen spoke for more than eight hours in Parliament's debate on the recovery package. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

Parliament has ended the debate on the EU recovery package. The debate ended during the night. The Finns Party MPs used many long speeches, and that is why the debate lasted many days.

Parliament's next plenary session is next Tuesday. Parliament will probably vote on the recovery package on Tuesday.

The government wants Finland to approve the recovery package. The opposition party, the Finns Party, opposes the recovery package.

The EU recovery package is 750 billion euros. The European Union intends to give money to EU countries that have suffered from the coronavirus.

## Young people's party

![Police car in Helsinki. 3 March 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4256,h_2394,x_0,y_193/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7809226040a878075dc/1644921076)

Open image viewer

Police car. Illustrative image. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The police ended a party of about 200 young people in Lahti. The young people were partying at the Lahti harbour on Friday evening. There were young people, for example, on the terraces of closed restaurants and on the roofs of buildings.

At least 1 assault also happened in the area. In Lahti there is a restriction in force that bans gatherings of more than 6 people. The reason is the poor corona situation.

## China's Mars rover

![A rocket with a flame tail rising into the sky.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11782359)

Open image viewer

Landing on Mars is part of China's ambitious space programme. The Long March carrier rocket that carried Tianwen-1 left Wenchang in China last July. Photo: EPA

China's rover has landed on the surface of the planet Mars.

The rover is a robotic vehicle. The rover has 6 wheels, with which it moves on the surface of Mars. The rover runs on solar power.

The rover collects and studies rocks on the surface of Mars. The rover also searches for water on Mars.

## Emma awards

![Behm performs at the Emma gala.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_155/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-806489609eab5d12392/1621011324)

Open image viewer

Behm is clearing the table at the Emma gala. Photo: Julius Konttinen / Emma 2021

On Friday evening, Finnish musicians were awarded at the Emma gala.

The musician Behm received the most Emma awards. Behm received 7 Emmas in total. No one has ever received this many Emmas before.

Behm was artist of the year and newcomer of the year. The audience also thought that Behm was the best Finnish artist of the year.
