# Monday 3.12.2018 (radio)

The government is lowering the tax on new cars. Jenni Haukio's Independence Day dress is made of birch. Honours are awarded on Independence Day. Helsinki region transport received an award on International Day of Persons with Disabilities.

## Car tax

![New cars at a car dealership in Vantaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10537509)

Open image viewer

Tax on new cars. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

The government is lowering the tax on new cars. The lower tax came into force on Monday. The price of a new car in the middle price range falls by about 1000 euros.

The change in tax is linked to the measurement of car emissions. In September, emissions began to be measured in a new way. It showed bigger emissions for cars than the old way of measuring. That is why taxes rose.

Now the government is correcting the tax tables so that the car tax is as big as it was before September.

## Haukio's dress

![Emma Saarnio (left) and Helmi Liikanen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10536523)

Open image viewer

Emma Saarnio (on the left) and Helmi Liikanen (on the right) have designed Mrs Haukio's dress for the Independence Day party. Photo: Eeva Suorlahti

The party at the Presidential Palace is held on Thursday. The Palace party is the most important event of Independence Day.

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio welcome guests at the Presidential Palace. This year the theme of the party is the environment.

Mrs Haukio's dress also fits the theme. The fabric of the dress is made of birch. The fabric is handmade.

The designers of the dress are students at Aalto University. Helmi Liikanen has designed the fabric of the dress, and Emma Saarnio has designed the model of the dress.

The designers say that the cooperation with Mrs Haukio has gone well. Haukio got the dress on Friday.

The model and colour of the dress are still a secret. When the TV broadcast of the Palace party begins on the evening of Independence Day, the secret will be revealed.

## Honours

![Commander's badge of the Order of the White Rose of Finland, first class.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10536629)

Open image viewer

The name of this honour is the Commander's badge of the Order of the White Rose of Finland, first class. Photo: Studio Fotonokka Oy

Honours are also awarded on Independence Day. President Sauli Niinistö has granted about 3800 honours this year.

Many of the recipients of the honours are decision-makers in business, politics and science. The Pro Finlandia medals are given to people in art and culture.

This year 12 people get the Pro Finlandia. Among them is the translator Kersti Juva. She has translated into Finnish, for example, the book series The Lord of the Rings.

## Award for HSL

![A tram drives in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10228767)

Open image viewer

Tram announcements are important for visually impaired people. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

On Monday the International Day of Persons with Disabilities was celebrated. HSL, that is, Helsinki region transport, received an award on the Day of Persons with Disabilities.

HSL received the award for the announcements on trams and buses. The announcements tell visually impaired people where the tram or bus is going. The announcements came to the trams in the summer.

The award was given by the Helsinki Disability Council.
