# Tuesday 29.11.2016 (radio)

Finnish children's maths skills are falling. Police will again search for drugs with dogs at festivals. A bank starts renting out electric cars. Good experiences from asylum seekers' work placements. Tax refunds are being paid to Finns.

## Children are worse at maths

![A blackboard at Tikka school](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9322293)

Open image viewer

Children's maths skills are getting weaker. Photo: Tenho Tornberg / Yle

Children are worse than before at doing maths calculations. A new study says that Finland has dropped from the top countries in science skills to a shared 5th place. In maths alone, the drop is even bigger, and Finland is now in shared 13th place.

The researchers are worried, because Finnish children have usually been good at maths and science.

Boys' skills in particular have weakened. Girls have overtaken boys both in maths and in science.

## Drug dogs will be used at festivals again next summer

![Riesa is meant to be trained as a drug dog.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8794312)

Open image viewer

The police have a lot of drug dogs. Photo: Jaana Polamo / Yle

The police are getting more rights to search for drugs with the help of a dog. The police may use a drug dog, for example, at rock concerts and other events, if a new law is approved.

Under the new law, drugs may be searched for with a dog in a festival area or in a market square where a concert is being held, but not on just any street.

The police stopped using drug dogs at festivals 3 years ago, because the law was unclear at that time.

## A bank rents out electric cars

![An electric car](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9320359)

Open image viewer

OP rents electric cars to its customers. Photo: OP Group

The big OP bank firm is trying out renting cars. OP says that the firm will start renting electric cars to its customers. The rent for a car varies from 500 euros to 1500 euros a month. The price depends on the car model, the length of the contract and the kilometres driven.

The cheapest car is a Renault electric car, and the most expensive is the luxury car Tesla.

The trial is starting in the Helsinki region.

## Asylum seekers' work placements were a success

![Wissam Hanna has been very happy with his work placement at Veli Mark Oy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5438584)

Open image viewer

Asylum seekers' work placements have been a success. Photo: YLE

Asylum seekers have eagerly taken part in work placements. The Finnish Red Cross is happy with the work placement, or TET, trial that was organised in the Turku region. There, 400 asylum seekers got to know Finnish working life. The asylum seekers learned the language and the rules of Finnish workplaces, for example in construction work, restaurants, shops and other customer service jobs.

The placements have lasted 1-3 weeks.

Because the TET placements were a big success, they now want to try them in all of Finland.

## Finns are getting tax refunds

![A back tax bill, coins and a calculator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9283777)

Open image viewer

The Tax Administration is returning overpaid taxes to people. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finns are getting money back from the state today. The Tax Administration is paying back 2.3 billion euros' worth of overpaid taxes to people.

Tax refunds are being paid to a total of 3.4 million taxpayers, that is, people who have paid taxes on their work and other income.

If the Tax Administration does not have a person's account number, the refunds are paid through the OP banks.
