## Travel to Finland

![A luggage trolley is pushed at an airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5106,h_2872,x_0,y_207/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83539260fe72e740d0c/1632151261)

Now you can always get into Finland with a certificate of corona vaccinations. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Travelling to Finland from abroad is now freer than before during the corona era.

If a traveller has a certificate of corona vaccinations, they can come to Finland from any country.

From the European Union area, you can also get into Finland without a corona vaccination certificate, if the traveller gets corona tests in Finland.

However, from many countries outside the European Union, you cannot get into Finland at all without a corona vaccination certificate.

So everyone whose corona vaccinations are in order can get into Finland. However, the Russian Sputnik corona vaccine is not one of the vaccines that are approved in Finland.

## A corona pass is being planned

![A traveller shows a digital corona pass to an official.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11938884)

Open image viewer

A corona pass has already been tested in some countries. Photo: Ramon van Flymen / EPA

Experts support introducing a corona pass in Finland.

A corona pass can be used, for example, to get into various events or restaurants.

The idea is that the corona pass shows corona vaccinations, corona tests, or that the holder has already had corona disease.

The use of corona passes is being planned in many European countries.

## Fire at the Pori Art Museum

![Police tape at the Pori Art Museum on the morning after the fire](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83533960fe4e442fd4d/1627279056)

Open image viewer

The fire did not cause serious damage to the Pori Art Museum. Photo: Jere Sanaksenaho / Yle

There has been a fire at the Pori Art Museum. The fire started during the night between Sunday and Monday.

The police suspect that someone set the fire on purpose. The fire started in a lorry that was next to the museum.

The fire spread to the museum's roof, but the museum building did not burn. Artworks were taken out of the museum to safety.

The Pori Art Museum says that the artworks were not badly damaged. They suffered mainly from smoke and extinguishing water.

## London floods

![A London taxi drives on a flooded street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6048,h_3402,x_0,y_236/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83534060fe4e7ab8929/1627279008)

Open image viewer

Water flooded the streets of London on Sunday. Photo: Justin Tallis / AFP

There have been floods in London, the capital of Britain. Water rose onto the streets of London on Sunday after heavy rain.

Because of the floods, streets and metro stations were closed. Many cars were left in the flood water, and roofs of houses collapsed in the heavy rain.

Also, 2 hospitals suffered from the floods. However, nobody died because of the London floods.
