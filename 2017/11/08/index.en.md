# Wednesday 8.11.2017 (radio)

The European Union wants to reduce pollution from cars. The air in India's capital Delhi is really dirty. Young Finns have good knowledge about society. Edible flowers are Finland's new export product.

## An attempt to reduce pollution from cars

![Car traffic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9920615)

Open image viewer

Cars release carbon dioxide into the air. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The European Union wants to reduce pollution from cars.

The European Commission proposes that carbon dioxide emissions from cars be reduced. Carbon dioxide is harmful to nature, because it warms the climate. The Commission proposes that carbon dioxide emissions from new cars be reduced by about 30 percent between 2021 and 2030.

The goal is that Europeans drive cars that pollute less.

## Delhi's air is dirty

![An Indian man has gone for a morning walk in the Lodhi Garden in Delhi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9921567)

Open image viewer

In Delhi it is a good idea to use a face mask. Photo: RAJAT GUPTA / EPA

In India's capital Delhi, people are suffering from pollution. There is 70 times more pollution in Delhi's air than the World Health Organization thinks is safe.

The air is so dirty and unhealthy that the authorities are trying to protect children. Because of the pollution, Delhi's schools were closed on Wednesday. Children are also not allowed to play outside.

Delhi's residents can fall ill because of the pollution. If a person breathes dirty air for years, they can get, for example, a heart disease or a lung disease.

Pollution gets into Delhi's air, for example, from cars, factories and coal power plants.

## Young Finns know about society

![A police car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9916533)

Open image viewer

Young Finns trust the police. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Young Finns have good knowledge about society. This comes out in a study that included more than 20 countries. Finnish eighth-graders know better than young people in many other countries how society works.

In Finland, young people also trust society. In Finland, young people trust the police and the army the most.

But young Finns are not very active. For example, young people do not want to take part in demonstrations.

## Flowers to eat

![Cornflowers suitable for eating.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9920722)

Open image viewer

Delicious cornflowers. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

Edible flowers are Finland's new export product. For example, the Kotipelto market garden grows cornflowers and violets, marigolds and geraniums that are exported abroad.

The first load of flowers has just left for Singapore. Before this, flowers have already been exported to Hong Kong and Sweden.

The Kotipelto market garden has got help from Finnish head chefs in building trade relations abroad.

The work is made easier by the fact that there are flights from Helsinki all around Asia. But the flowers must be packed well for the long journey. They must stay good until they reach the plates of Singaporean food lovers.
