# Friday 31.5.2019 (radio)

The new government's programme is ready. The compulsory education age is rising. There has been strong wind in Northern Finland. At the weekend, students who have finished their studies are being celebrated. The first Finnish strawberries of the summer are ripe.

## The government programme is ready

![Antti Rinne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10811219)

Open image viewer

Antti Rinne said on Friday that the government programme is ready. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

The government negotiations have gone well. This is what the leader of the negotiations, Antti Rinne, says.

Rinne said on Friday that the government programme is ready. The government programme says what the new government intends to do.

Rinne will tell more about the government programme on Monday. Before that, the parties still intend to agree on ministerial posts. The parties must agree which ministerial jobs each party gets in the new government.

5 parties are taking part in the government negotiations. Antti Rinne is leading the government negotiations, because he is the SDP party leader. The SDP is the largest party in Parliament.

## Compulsory education is extended

![Ninth grade pupils in an English lesson, teacher Katariina Pekuri, Riihikallio School, Tuusula, 15.1.2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_368/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5366975c3ef330d1911/1547629430)

Open image viewer

Nowadays only basic school is compulsory for everyone. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

The compulsory education age is rising.

Antti Rinne says that the government negotiations have agreed to extend compulsory education. This means that everyone must go to a vocational school or upper secondary school after basic school.

In the future, textbooks will be free for upper secondary school students and vocational education students.

It is not yet known how soon the compulsory education age will rise.

## Wind caused damage

![Trees fallen in the late May storm in Kemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10810559)

Open image viewer

Wind knocked down trees on Friday. Photo: Risto Koskinen / Yle

There has been strong wind in Northern Finland on Friday.

The wind has knocked down trees and torn roofs off. The wind has also cut off power, and many homes have been without electricity on Friday.

The sea level has also risen in the north. In Kemi, for example, the seawater has been more than a metre higher than usual.

## Many have graduated

![A student receives a certificate.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8024448)

Open image viewer

Many will receive a certificate this weekend. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

At the weekend, students who have finished their studies are being celebrated.

More than 80,000 students are taking a vocational qualification. This spring more than 25,000 new matriculated students also graduate.

Yle's vocational school machine tells who has graduated in a trade. Yle's matriculation machine tells the names of the new matriculated students.

Both machines can be found on Yle's website.

## Strawberries ripen

![General view](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4949,h_2784,x_0,y_255/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5670475ceeb2b4a1a6e/1559147366)

Open image viewer

Strawberries are already available for the weekend celebrations. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

The first Finnish strawberries of the summer are ripe. They have ripened in grow tunnels. A grow tunnel protects strawberries from the cold.

The first open-field strawberries will be available in mid-June, if the weather is warm. Open-field strawberries grow outdoors in strawberry fields.

It is still hard to predict how big the strawberry harvest will be for the whole summer.
