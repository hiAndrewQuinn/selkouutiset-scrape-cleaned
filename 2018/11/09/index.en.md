# Friday 9.11.2018 (radio)

Interference with air traffic. Tax matters can be handled online. Fathers take short paternity leave. The weather stays grey.

## Russia may have interfered with navigation devices

![The warning issued on 6.11.2018 by ANS Finland, Finland's air navigation service, about GPS interference over a wide area was the first of its kind in Finland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10499355)

Open image viewer

Aircraft were warned that their GPS devices may have interference. Photo: Tapani Leisti / Yle

Yle reported today that Russia may have interfered with satellite positioning systems in Finland.

This means that GPS devices in aircraft may have had interference. The interference may have happened when the military alliance NATO held a war exercise in the northern parts of Norway, Sweden and Finland.

Finnish authorities have not said anything about the interference possibly coming from Russia.

Norwegian authorities have said that similar interference has come from Russia before.

## Tax matters can be handled online

![Login page of the Tax Administration's OmaVero site on a mobile phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10500143)

Open image viewer

Tax matters can be handled on the Tax Administration's online service. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Every Finn can now handle many tax matters online.

Today the Tax Administration opened the new OmaVero online service. In the service you can order a tax card, make a tax return and pay taxes.

From there everyone can look at all their own tax matters.

## Fathers take short paternity leave

![A father and child.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9828503)

Open image viewer

A father can take 54 working days of paternity leave. Photo: AOP

In Finland a father can take paternity leave when a baby is born into the family.

The rules for paternity leave were changed a few years ago, so that fathers can take more paternity leave. But this has not happened.

Fathers have added only a couple of days to their paternity leave.

Fathers can take 54 working days of paternity leave. Usually fathers take 36 working days of paternity leave.

## Hunters are shooting many white-tailed deer

![White-tailed deer and fawn.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10304630)

Open image viewer

A white-tailed deer can have 3 fawns at once. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

There are now many hunters in the forest. This autumn hunters are trying to shoot many white-tailed deer.

In Finland there are really a lot of white-tailed deer.

A white-tailed deer can have 3 fawns at once. That is why the number of white-tailed deer remains large.
