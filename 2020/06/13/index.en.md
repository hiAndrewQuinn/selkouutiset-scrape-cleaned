# Saturday 13.6.2020 (radio)

The Finns Party and the Centre Party held meetings on Saturday. There are few new corona infections. People may eat unhealthily because of stress. It is hot at the weekend.

## Finns Party meeting

![The picture shows the book Totuus kiihottaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11394707)

Parties have held meetings on Saturday.

At the Finns Party meeting, they talked, for example, about a book that was published this week. The book was published by an organisation that is close to the Finns Party.

The book contains ideas that insult women. The author of the book, Jukka Hankamäki, harshly criticises, for example, Finnish women who date a foreign man. Many people are angry because of the book.

Finns Party leader Jussi Halla-aho said at Saturday's meeting that publishing the book was a mistake. Halla-aho fears that the Finns Party will lose supporters because of the book.

## Centre Party meeting

![Katri Kulmuni](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11400906)

Centre Party leader Katri Kulmuni. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

The Centre Party also held a meeting. At the meeting, Centre Party leader Katri Kulmuni talked about a scandal because of which she had to resign from her job as a minister.

The reason for the scandal was a presentation training course that Kulmuni took. The bill went to the state. The matter caused such a big scandal that Kulmuni resigned as minister.

Kulmuni said on Saturday that the experience was hard. However, she wants to continue as Centre Party leader.

## Work stress and food

![A challenge to eat an 80 cm wide pizza in half an hour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9769111)

Open image viewer

Fat tastes good to a person who is stressed. Photo: Elisa Kinnunen / Yle

Work stress may be the reason why a person eats unhealthily. Stress and shift work show in people's diets.

Researcher Katri Hemiö says that because of stress, a person wants, for example, fatty fast food.

Many men also eat sweets, in addition to fatty food, when they are stressed. Men do not eat vegetables when they are stressed.

Women also eat fatty and sweet foods when they are stressed. But women do not stop eating vegetables, even when they are stressed.

## Hot weekend

![Young people jumped into the water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11400995)

Open image viewer

Finland's summer is beautiful. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Saturday has been a really warm day. Over a large part of Finland it has been hot, with at least 25 degrees.

In Kankaanpää, this summer's new heat record was measured. The temperature was 27.7 degrees.

The sunny and warm weather continues on Sunday as well. The sun's UV radiation is strong, so people outdoors should protect their skin with clothing or sunscreen.
