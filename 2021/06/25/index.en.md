## Midsummer

![A Finnish flag flutters.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_150/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6732375eb7aa5b00565/1607238475)

The Finnish flag flutters on the flagpole all through Midsummer. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Midsummer is now being celebrated in Finland. Friday is Midsummer Eve and Saturday is Midsummer Day.

Many people have travelled from the city to their summer cottages. There was a lot of traffic on the roads, but no serious accidents happened on Midsummer Eve.

The return traffic from Midsummer is reduced by the fact that many start their summer holiday at Midsummer. That is why they stay at the summer cottage for longer.

Midsummer is also the day of the Finnish flag. The flag is raised to the top of the flagpole on Midsummer Eve at 6 pm and is lowered only on Midsummer Day at 9 pm. Midsummer night is the only night when the flag is flown.

Concerts, for example, are also being organised at Midsummer in other cities.

## Midsummer magic

![Adder's tongue leaf in the palm of a hand](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82523660d45d6ad98c0/1624530350)

Open image viewer

Will the adder's tongue flower on Midsummer night? Photo: Tuija Veirto / Yle

Midsummer is a time of magic. Midsummer magic is done with plants.

In the old days Finns believed that plants have strong magical power precisely at Midsummer. The magical power of plants brought people love and health.

Finns believed, for example, that the adder's tongue flowers only on Midsummer night. That is why its magical power was very strong.

If a person found an adder's tongue flower, they learned, for example, to understand the language of animals and to be invisible. The task was, however, difficult, because the adder's tongue does not really flower, not even on Midsummer night.

Nowadays Midsummer magic is a fun game for many people. Midsummer magic is done on social media, for example.

## Midsummer weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11998253)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

The weather is warm at Midsummer. The temperature is between 20 and 25 degrees. In Lapland it is colder.

However, the weather is unstable, that is, showers fall in different parts of Finland. Showers and also thunder showers come especially in the south and east.
