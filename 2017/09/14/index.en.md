## Kidnapped Finn freed in Afghanistan

![Kabul](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9629278)

Open image viewer

The woman was kidnapped from this house.  Photo: Jawad Jalali / EPA

A Finnish aid worker has been freed in Afghanistan. The 37-year-old woman was kidnapped in Kabul in May.

The attackers abducted the woman from a flat where a German aid worker also lived. The German woman and the guard of the flat were killed in the attack.

No more detailed information has yet been received about the kidnappers.

## Finland did not pay a ransom for the woman

![Foreign Minister Timo Soini](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9813435)

Open image viewer

Foreign Minister Timo Soini Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Foreign Minister Timo Soini says that Finland did not pay money to the kidnappers. In Soini's opinion, paying money only encourages new kidnappings. Finnish authorities helped in freeing the woman, but no more details of their actions are being given. An international criminal investigation into the woman's kidnapping is under way.

Soini has spoken with the woman on the phone, and the woman is safe. She is well, but it is not yet known when the woman can return to Finland.

## A big military exercise began in Russia and Belarus

![a helicopter and soldiers on a beach](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8995723)

Open image viewer

There are many soldiers in the Zapad exercise.  Photo: Aleksei Druginyn / Ria Novosti / EPA

The joint military exercise of Russia and Belarus has begun. The military exercise is called Zapad, and it lasts for a week. Russia says that 13,000 soldiers are taking part in the exercise, but in reality the number is much bigger. There may be over 100,000 soldiers.

Russia's neighbouring countries, for example Lithuania and Poland, do not like a big military exercise being held at their borders. The military exercise strains relations between Russia and EU countries, which are already bad.

## West Metro test run failed

![Cables at the Aalto University metro station of the West Metro in Otaniemi, Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9485230)

Open image viewer

The electricity failed during the West Metro test run.  Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The West Metro is in trouble again. Metro trains were tested on Thursday, but the test run had to be stopped. The electricity did not hold up during the test run. The test run also made other metro traffic difficult, and traffic had to be stopped.

Service between Helsinki and Espoo should have started a long time ago, but there have been many problems in building the metro line.

Now passenger service will perhaps start in October.

## Finns eat more vegetables and fruit

![Food is taken from a buffet line onto a plate.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9770776)

Open image viewer

Finns are putting more vegetables on their plates.  Photo: Wasim Khuzam / Yle

Finns now eat more healthily than before. Yle got a list from big retail companies and researchers of food items whose consumption has fallen or risen.

Finns now eat more vegetables, fruit and nuts than 5 years ago. Less meat and fatty sausages are eaten. Eating sugar is also decreasing, but more pick-and-mix sweets are being sold.
