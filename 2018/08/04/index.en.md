# Saturday 4.8.2018 (radio)

Fierce heat waves in Spain and Portugal. Survey: The rich get richer, the poor get poorer. Fines for a headscarf in Denmark. Arrests at the Pride parade in St Petersburg. UN: North Korea continues its nuclear weapons programme. Lightning rarely kills anymore.

## Fierce heat waves in Spain and Portugal

![People cooled off in a fountain in Madrid's Rio park on 3 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10337945)

In Madrid, the capital of Spain, people cooled off in a fountain, Photo: Pierre-Philippe Marcou / AFP

It is now really hot in Spain and Portugal. Three people have died in the hot weather in Spain.

The temperature is about 40 degrees. In Portugal it may be as hot as 47 degrees at the weekend.

Finland's health authorities say that it is not a good idea to travel to Spain and Portugal now. Severe heat can be dangerous for example to small children, old people or sick people.

## Survey: the rich get richer and the poor get poorer

![A man counts his little money.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10273574)

Open image viewer

Finns believe that life for poor people will get harder in the future. Photo: AOP

Finns believe that the differences between poor and rich people will grow in the future.

In the survey, more than 50% of Finns said that the situation of the poor will get worse and the situation of the rich will get better during the next 10 years.

Finns think that life will become harder especially for the poor, the unemployed, old people and those who live in the countryside.

## Fines for a headscarf in Denmark

![Women wearing niqabs in Germany.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10062334)

Open image viewer

In Denmark, a woman who wore a niqab has been fined for the first time. Photo: Boris Roessler / EPA

In Denmark, a woman has been fined because she wore a niqab veil in a shopping centre.

Denmark's new law says that a veil that covers the face must not be worn in a public place. The niqab and the burka are banned in Denmark.

The woman was fined 130 euros. The police came to the scene because another woman had tried to take the niqab off the woman.

## Arrests at a Pride event in Russia

![a rainbow flag in the foreground, in the background a building at the edge of Palace Square](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10338092)

Open image viewer

Pride activists gathered in Palace Square. Photo: Kerstin Kronvall / Yle

In Russia, at least 27 people have been arrested at a Pride event in St Petersburg.

A Pride event defends, among other things, the rights of sexual minorities, such as homosexuals.

The event did not get permission from the authorities. About 60 people took part in it.

## UN: North Korea continues its nuclear weapons programme

![North Korean leader Kim Jong-un](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10254225)

Open image viewer

North Korean leader Kim Jong-un has said that the country will give up its nuclear weapons programme. Photo: Kevin Lim / EPA-EFE

North Korea is still trying to develop nuclear weapons, says the UN.

According to the UN, North Korea continues its nuclear weapons programme because the sanctions put on North Korea do not work.

Because of the sanctions, for example oil must not be sold to North Korea. But North Korea has got oil without permission, says the UN.

North Korea has earlier signed a declaration in which it promises to end its nuclear weapons programme.

## Lightning rarely kills in Finland

![lightning in the sky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10337922)

Open image viewer

In the first days of August there has been more lightning than in the whole of July. Photo: AOP

The police suspect that two men died from a lightning strike in North Karelia yesterday. The lightning had struck a tree.

A lightning strike kills only rarely in Finland. The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that on average one Finn dies from a lightning strike every other year.

About 60 years ago, 10 people died from lightning strikes in Finland every year.
