# Tuesday 11.12.2018 (radio)

Aptitude tests are to be brought back to vocational schools. The number of drowned people has grown. Finns eat meat in restaurants. A new party has been formed in Finland. Kimi Räikkönen's biography is breaking records.

## Aptitude tests for vocational training

![practical nurse training](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9902929)

Open image viewer

Aptitude tests are to be brought back to practical nurse training. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

The aim is to bring back stricter student selection to vocational training.

Teachers at vocational schools have said that, for example, people who are not suited to the fields have been admitted to practical nurse training and security training. This is because since 2016 applicants could not be screened out with aptitude tests as they could before.

The Ministry of Education and Culture now wants to bring back aptitude tests for practical nurse training, the security field and the logistics degree programme. The reform is intended to be carried out during this government's term.

## Drowned

![A sign warns about weak ice.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6438045)

Open image viewer

Think carefully before going out onto the ice, warns the Swimming Instruction and Lifesaving Association. Photo: Yle

This year more people have drowned in Finland than last year.

Preliminary information shows that between January and November, 90 people drowned. That is 11 more than a year ago.

The Finnish Swimming Instruction and Lifesaving Association says that during the autumn at least 16 people have drowned.

## Finns' favourite foods

![Mashed potatoes, a steak and salad on a plate at lunchtime.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10074210)

Open image viewer

Steak-style meat patties are growing in popularity. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

Chicken and hamburgers are Finns' favourite foods in restaurants.

A trend survey of tourism and restaurant services says that 15 percent of restaurant guests order chicken or other poultry dishes as their main course.

14 percent of restaurant guests order hamburgers, and 10 percent order pizza. However, the popularity of hamburgers and pizza has fallen a little.

The popularity of steak-style whole-meat patties has grown the most, to 10 percent. Tourism and restaurant services say that when the economy is doing well, people order whole-meat patties instead of minced-meat patties.

5 to 6 percent of restaurant customers choose a vegetarian dish.

## New party

![Torch march of the Finns First movement in Oulu on Independence Day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10548521)

Open image viewer

The Finns First movement organised a torch march in Oulu on Independence Day. Photo: Timo Heikkala / Lehtikuva

A new party has been entered in the party register. The name of the new party is Finns First.

The party demands, for example, that Finland leave the EU, close the borders and bring back the markka as Finland's currency.

There are now 18 parties in the party register in total.

## Kimi Räikkönen's biography

![Kimi Räikkönen and Kari Hotakainen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3232,h_1818,x_120,y_664/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5035445b7566ecbf4dd/1534420799)

Formula One driver Kimi Räikkönen and author Kari Hotakainen. Photo: All Over Press

Formula One driver Kimi Räikkönen interests Finns. Kimi Räikkönen's biography has now sold more than any other book in the year it was published.

The book The Unknown Kimi Räikkönen by author Kari Hotakainen was published in August. More than 170,000 copies of the book have already been sold.

Before Räikkönen's biography, J.K. Rowling's book Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix had held first place since 2004.
