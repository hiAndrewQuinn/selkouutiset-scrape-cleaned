# Friday 17.7.2020 (radio)

Children liked the exceptional circumstances. Sales of used cars are growing. Raimo Sailas has died. The pea harvest is very good. The weather stays warm.

The TV Clear News has a summer break. It will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Children coped well in the exceptional circumstances of spring

![Child at home with books.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11350620)

Open image viewer

In distance school, children studied at home. Lessons were held by video link. Photo: Heidi Paal

Children adapted well in Finland to the exceptional circumstances and to distance school. The children's organisation Unicef has asked Finnish children what they liked about the exceptional circumstances in the spring.

Many children liked being at home a lot with their family. The children also liked that they could sleep more, and that life was calm, because hobbies had ended.

According to the children, what was bad about the exceptional circumstances was that they could not meet friends, grandparents or teachers.

## Sales of used cars grew a lot

![Car trade went quiet in autumn 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10581842)

Open image viewer

Many people buy a used car, perhaps for a summer holiday trip. Photo: Ensio Karjalainen / Yle

Finns have bought used cars much more than before. Car dealers sold over 56,000 used cars in June, that is 13% more than a year ago.

The sales of new cars have fallen a lot because of the corona situation, but the sales of used cars have increased.

Because of the corona situation, many Finns do not want to go on a bus or a train with other people, for example. Finns are also travelling a lot in their own country now.

## Raimo Sailas has died

![Raimo Sailas.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11452807)

Open image viewer

Raimo Sailas, 13.9.1945 – 17.7.2020. Photo: Kimmo Brandt / AOP

Raimo Sailas, former state secretary of the Ministry of Finance, has died. Raimo Sailas was well known in Finland. He worked at the Ministry of Finance for over 40 years.

Raimo Sailas was an economic expert. When Finland's economy was doing very badly in the 1990s, Raimo Sailas said where Finland must save.

Raimo Sailas was 74 years old.

## Peas are good

![An open pea pod in a hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_400/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6929045ef379ec227b9/1593016238)

Open image viewer

The pea harvest is bigger this summer than ever before in Finland. Photo: Rebecka Svedberg / Yle

The pea harvest is big in Finland this summer. The peas have grown well, and many peas are coming to sale. More peas have been grown than before.

The grain harvest is normal this year. The grain harvest is, however, smaller than last year, but it is enough for Finns.

The rye harvest falls by 50% from last year. The wheat harvest falls by 20%. The oat harvest is the same as last year.
