# Thursday 28.3.2019 (radio)

There is a shortage of workers in many professions. The peak of the influenza epidemic is over. The new maternity package has 63 products. WWF has opened an adder camera. Tomorrow is the warmest day of spring.

## Many fields need more workers

![Professions at the top of the pyramid have the biggest shortage of workers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10711172)

Professions at the top of the pyramid have the biggest shortage of workers. Photo: Aapo Parviainen / Yle

There is a shortage of workers in many professions.

The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment's profession barometer says that at the beginning of the year there was a shortage of workers in 54 professions. A year ago there was a shortage of workers in about 40 professions.

There is a shortage of workers especially in health and social care. For example, there are not enough speech therapists and doctors.

In the near future, more cleaners, building painters, restaurant workers and earth-moving machine drivers will also be needed, for example.

## The influenza epidemic is starting to ease

![woman sneezing into a tissue](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10710716)

Open image viewer

This winter's influenza epidemic was milder than in previous years. Photo: AOP

The peak of the influenza epidemic is now over.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that the number of influenza cases has turned downwards in different parts of the country. It also seems that the epidemic was not as bad as in recent years.

This year's influenza vaccine gave protection against 4 different viruses. The vaccine protected children under 6 years old especially well. It did not protect the elderly as well.

About 20 percent of Finns took the influenza vaccine this year.

## The maternity package is higher quality than ever

![all the clothes in the maternity package](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10710867)

Open image viewer

The maternity package contains many clothes and supplies for the baby. Photo: Annika Söderblom / Kela

This year's maternity package has been unveiled.

The new maternity package has 63 products. The box contains clothes and various supplies for the baby. The box can be used as the baby's first bed. This year special attention has been paid to the products in the maternity package being of good quality.

The maternity package is Kela's maternity benefit for a woman who is pregnant. Mothers can choose whether they want the maternity package or 170 euros in money.

## Life of adders as a live broadcast

![WWF adder live](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10711902)

Open image viewer

An adder basks in the sun in WWF's adder camera. Photo: WWF

The nature conservation organisation WWF's website now lets you watch the lives of adders in a live broadcast.

WWF has taken the camera close to the adders' winter nest in Southwest Finland. The camera films a place where the adders usually enjoy the spring sun.

The adder camera will be in place for about a month.

The adder is Finland's only venomous snake. WWF hopes that the adder camera will ease people's prejudices and fears of the adder.

## Tomorrow is a warm day

![Weekend temperature forecast](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10711054)

Open image viewer

On Friday it is warm, but then the weather cools again. Photo: Yle Weather

This spring's heat record will be broken tomorrow, forecasts Yle's meteorologist Joonas Koskela.

A temperature of 12.9 degrees was measured today in Jomala, on Åland. Joonas Koskela believes that tomorrow, Friday, the record will be exceeded somewhere in Southern Finland.

The warm weather will not last long, however. The weather cools already at the weekend.
