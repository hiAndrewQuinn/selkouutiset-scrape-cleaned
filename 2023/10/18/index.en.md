## Data breach case

![Aleksanteri Kivimäki in Western Uusimaa District Court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_142/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107903763fe132fe9c43/1697617751)

Aleksanteri Kivimäki is accused of hacking. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

The prosecutor has decided to bring charges in a big data breach case. Aleksanteri Kivimäki will be charged with, among other things, hacking and extortion.

The prosecutor says that Kivimäki hacked the psychotherapy centre Vastaamo and stole information about Vastaamo's patients.

The prosecutor also says that Kivimäki published patient information on the internet. Kivimäki demanded money in order to stop publishing the patient information.

That is what the prosecutor says. Kivimäki himself says that he has not committed a crime.

The police investigated the hacking for a long time and caught Kivimäki in February this year. The trial against Kivimäki will begin later in the autumn.

The Vastaamo hacking case is unusually big in Finland's criminal history.

There are more than 30 000 victims of the hacking. More than 20 000 of them filed a crime report.

## Ahtisaari's funeral

![The graves of Urho Kekkonen and Mauno Koivisto at Hietaniemi Cemetery in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5194,h_2921,x_0,y_218/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1188105652fd8889c2c0/1699540699)

Hietaniemi Cemetery also has graves of presidents. Photo: Markku Pitkänen / Yle

The funeral of President Martti Ahtisaari will be held in Helsinki on 10 November.

The ceremony at Helsinki Cathedral begins at 13:00. After that President Ahtisaari will be buried at Hietaniemi Cemetery.

Other Finnish presidents are also buried at Hietaniemi Cemetery. Presidents Urho Kekkonen, Mauno Koivisto and Risto Ryti are buried at Hietaniemi Cemetery.

People can also follow President Ahtisaari's funeral on television, on the radio and on Yle Areena.

President Ahtisaari died on Monday. He was 86 years old. Ahtisaari was President of Finland from 1994 to 2000.

## Memorial for the victims of the terror attack

![Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson wore the colours of Sweden around his neck in Brussels.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1188106652fd973a0f65/1698854158)

At the memorial, Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson had the colours of Sweden. Photo: OLIVIER MATTHYS/EPA-EFE

In Belgium the victims of the terror attack on Monday have been remembered. 2 Swedes died in the attack.

A memorial for the terror attack was held today, Wednesday, at the scene of the attack in Brussels. The prime ministers of Sweden and Belgium also took part in the event.

In the terror attack on Monday, two Swedish football fans were shot. The shooter wanted to kill specifically Swedes. The shooter was a supporter of the terrorist organisation Isis.

At Enontekiö airport in Lapland, on Wednesday morning, the temperature was measured at minus 17.2 degrees. It is the coldest temperature measured so far this autumn.

On Thursday too the weather is cold and dry. It is sunniest in the west and in the north.
