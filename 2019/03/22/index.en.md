# Friday 22.3.2019 (radio)

The customs laboratory has found listeria bacteria in frozen foods. Finland is taking part in Sweden's military exercise in Lapland. About 60 people died in a bus accident in Ghana, in Africa. Britain's EU exit, that is Brexit, is postponed. Kurds have celebrated the new year.

## Listeria bacteria in frozen foods

![Culturing bacteria in a laboratory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10701792)

Open image viewer

The customs laboratory tests food products. Photo: Customs laboratory

The customs laboratory has found listeria bacteria in frozen foods. The listeria bacteria was in fruit, berries and vegetables that were imported to Finland from abroad as frozen foods.

However, there was so little listeria bacteria in the frozen foods that shops were allowed to sell them. Listeria bacteria is removed from food when the food is heated. Rinsing with water is not enough.

Elderly people and expectant mothers are the easiest to make ill with listeria bacteria.

The customs laboratory found listeria bacteria in fruit for the first time.

## Military exercise in Lapland

In the north there is a military exercise. Sweden is organising a military exercise, in which other countries also take part. Finland is also taking part in the exercise.

The main location of the exercise is northern Sweden. Swedish and Finnish military aircraft are also practising together on the Finnish side.

Military aircraft will fly in the Lapland sky during the exercise. The training flights continue until next Wednesday.

## Bus accident in Ghana

![Street view in Ghana.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9817865)

Open image viewer

There is a lot of bus traffic in Ghana. Photo: AOP

A serious road accident has happened in Ghana, in Africa. About 60 people died when 2 buses collided with each other.

The number of dead may rise, because some of the injured are in very bad condition. There are about 30 injured.

The accident happened at night. In the collision one of the buses caught fire. There were about 50 passengers in each bus.

## Brexit is postponed

![Britain's Prime Minister Theresa May at a press conference in Brussels on 21 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10701575)

Open image viewer

Britain's Prime Minister Theresa May. Photo: Emmanuel Dunand / AFP

Britain's EU exit, that is Brexit, is postponed.

Britain's Prime Minister Theresa May asked the European Union for more time to arrange the exit.

The EU leaders gave more time, until 22 May. The condition is that the British parliament approves the withdrawal agreement next week.

If parliament does not approve the withdrawal agreement, the extra time ends on 12 April.

The intention was that Brexit would happen already at the end of March. However, British politicians cannot decide how Brexit should be handled.

## The Kurdish new year

The Kurdish new year has begun. Kurds celebrate the new year at the spring equinox. Then the day becomes longer than the night.

The spring festival is also the national day for Kurds. Kurds celebrate it in different countries. Finnish Kurds too have celebrated the new year and the Kurdish national day.
