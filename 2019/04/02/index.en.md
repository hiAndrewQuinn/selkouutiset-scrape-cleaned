# Tuesday 2.4. (radio)

Britain's Brexit problems continue. More homes are being sold in Finland. There are fewer prisoners in Europe. Finland does not have enough doctors. The police are checking car speeds. Helsinki's city bikes are the most popular in the world.

## Britain's Brexit problems continue

![Michel Barnier](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10718494)

Open image viewer

Michel Barnier. Photo: Stephanie Lecocq / EPA

Britain's exit from the EU without an agreement is very close. This is what the EU's chief negotiator, Michel Barnier, says. Last night the British Parliament again rejected 4 options for how Britain leaves the EU. Parliament may also vote for the 4th time on the withdrawal agreement that the British government negotiated with the EU. If Parliament does not accept the agreement, Britain must leave without an agreement after 10 days. That is a bad option for the economy of Britain and Europe.

## There are fewer prisoners in Europe

![Prisoner looks out through a barred window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8275583)

Open image viewer

Photo: Sini Salmirinne / Yle

There are fewer prisoners in European prisons than before. The number of prisoners began to fall in 2012. The Council of Europe says that in Europe there are 102.5 prisoners for every 100,000 inhabitants. In Finland the number of prisoners fell by 10 percent, which is the fourth largest fall in the whole of Europe. Even more than in Finland, the number of prisoners fell in Romania, Bulgaria and Norway.

## Finland needs doctors

![Stethoscope in a doctor's hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4113,h_2314,x_0,y_244/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-65475580de70b87954/1529046208)

Open image viewer

Photo: Alex Proimos

Finland does not have enough doctors. The biggest shortage of doctors is in Kainuu. There should be 20 percent more doctors there than there are now. The Finnish Medical Association says that it is hard to attract doctors who have lived their whole lives in the city to work in the countryside. Across Finland more than 200 more full-time doctors are needed.

## Helsinki's city bikes are popular

![City bike in the Helsinki region. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10718841)

Open image viewer

Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Helsinki's city bikes are the most popular in the world. About 50 cities from different parts of the world took part in the international comparison. The comparison counted how many times one city bike is ridden in one day. The number of trips was highest in Helsinki. City bikes can be used in Helsinki and Espoo. City bikes can be used if you register as a user. You also have to pay a little for use.
