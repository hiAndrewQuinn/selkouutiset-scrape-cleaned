# Wednesday 8.4.2020 (radio)

The government has a supplementary budget. Stockmann goes into corporate restructuring. Roma National Day. Don't go into the Easter rush at the shops. Weather.

## The government made a supplementary budget

![Minister of Education Li Andersson (left), Minister of Finance Katri Kulmuni, Prime Minister Sanna Marin, Minister of the Interior Maria Ohisalo and Minister of Justice Anna-Maja Henriksson at the press conference on the government's budget frame negotiations in Helsinki on 8 April 2020. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11298527)

Open image viewer

The government announced the supplementary budget on Wednesday. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The government has made a supplementary budget because of the coronavirus situation. The state will give companies about one billion euros so that they can continue their operations.

The state will give 600 million euros for protective equipment, medicines and hospital equipment in hospitals. The state will give municipalities about 550 million euros for the costs of the coronavirus situation. The state also gives support, for example, to culture, education and border control.

The supplementary budget is more than 4 billion euros in total.

## Stockmann goes into corporate restructuring

![File photo of the Stockmann department store in central Helsinki on 1 February 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11293774)

Open image viewer

Stockmann's famous department store in Helsinki. Photo: Yle News / Mark B. Odom

The department store company Stockmann is going into corporate restructuring. Stockmann has had a difficult time for a long time. Few customers visit department stores. The coronavirus situation has made Stockmann's situation harder.

Corporate restructuring means that a company agrees with its creditors on how the company's operations will be improved, so that the company can keep operating.

The aim is that the company's finances will get better. On Monday Stockmann asked to be allowed into corporate restructuring. Today, on Wednesday, the Helsinki District Court decided that Stockmann may go into corporate restructuring.

## Roma National Day

![Roma and Finnish flags flutter in a yard. In 1971 the first international Roma congress was held in London and the Roma flag was adopted. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1980,h_1114,x_0,y_102/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6626185e8d0a2638cff/1586301599)

Open image viewer

The international Roma flag. The flag has a red wheel. Photo: Vesa Moilanen/ Lehtikuva

Today, Wednesday 8 April, is Roma National Day.

It has been celebrated in different parts of the world. The purpose of Roma National Day is to highlight Roma culture and the Romani language. The first Roma people came to Finland in the 1500s.

About 10,000 Roma people live in Finland.

## Don't go into the Easter rush at the shops

Tomorrow is Maundy Thursday, which starts the Easter weekend. There may be a rush at the shops, because many people go to buy a lot of food for Easter.

Because of the coronavirus situation, it is bad if many people are in the shop at the same time. It is a good idea to go to the shop early in the morning or late in the evening, so that there are not many people there. It is a good idea to go to the shop alone, but not, for example, with your family.

You must not try fruit and vegetables with your hands in the shop, so that the coronavirus does not spread.

Hands must be washed before you go to the shop, and hands must be washed when you come home from the shop.
