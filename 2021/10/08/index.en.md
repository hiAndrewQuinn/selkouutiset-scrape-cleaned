## Nobel Peace Prize

![Both Nobel Peace Prize winners, Maria Ressa and Dmitri Muratov, in a portrait photo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5670,h_3189,x_0,y_381/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8645266160194223828/1633688208)

This year the Nobel Peace Prize goes to 2 journalists.

The Peace Prize goes to Filipino journalist Maria Ressa and Russian journalist Dmitri Muratov.

The Nobel committee told about this on Friday in Oslo.

The Nobel committee says that Ressa and Muratov have worked for freedom of speech. They want everyone to have a chance to get correct information.

Maria Ressa says that without facts nothing is possible. Journalists must defend the truth, Ressa says.

Ressa and Muratov are from countries where the work of a journalist is difficult and even dangerous.

Dmitri Muratov is the editor-in-chief of the Russian newspaper Novaya Gazeta. He wants to remember those Novaya Gazeta journalists who have died because of their profession.

## Elokapina demonstration

![Elokapina demonstrating at the Government Palace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_403/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-86447961600fa3cedab/1724847673)

Open image viewer

The demonstrators want the government to declare a climate emergency. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

There has been a demonstration in Helsinki. The demonstration was in the centre of Helsinki, at Senate Square.

The demonstrators blocked the entrance to the Government Palace, where the Finnish government works.

The demonstration was organised by the environmental movement Elokapina. Elokapina demands that the Finnish government takes climate change seriously and declares an environmental emergency.

The Elokapina movement has organised demonstrations for 10 days now.

This time there were about 200 demonstrators. The police asked them to leave, but not everyone obeyed. So the police carried them onto buses and took them away from the place.

The police arrested about 50 demonstrators.

## Start of the skiing season

![At Ruka, a ski slope is being made from stored snow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4624,h_2601,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-861967615ab5034ac0a/1633334587)

The ski slopes have been made from last winter's snow. Photo: Ensio Karjalainen / Yle

The skiing season has started. The ski centres Levi and Ruka opened their ski slopes on Friday.

The ski centres were able to open the slopes with the help of last winter's snow. Levi and Ruka stored snow last winter, and now the snow has been spread on the ski slopes.

The ski centres expect several hundred customers at the weekend.

## The weekend's weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12136493)

The weekend's weather is changeable.

On Saturday there will be only a little rain, but the weather is cloudy and foggy. In Northern Lapland it may snow or sleet on Saturday, and the driving conditions are poor.

On Sunday there will be rain, especially in the north.

The weekend temperature in the south is over 10 degrees. In Lapland it is colder, and the temperature stays below 10 degrees.
