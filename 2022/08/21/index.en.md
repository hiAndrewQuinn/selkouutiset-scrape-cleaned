# Sunday 21.8.2022 (radio)

A dispute in the care sector was mediated. New grain ships left Ukraine. There was a deadly attack on a hotel in Somalia. The police monitor pedestrian crossings. The weather is rainy in the east, the sun shines in the west.

## The nurses' dispute

![Millariikka Rytkönen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2575,h_1448,x_214,y_181/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99185462f1273d7a738/1659971806)

Chair of the nurses' union Tehy, Millariikka Rytkönen. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

A solution to the nurses' dispute was sought on Sunday.

The nurses' unions warned this week that nurses may go on strike at 2 hospitals at the beginning of September. The nurses will go on strike if the dispute is not settled. The nurses want more pay.

The National Conciliator says that it is really difficult to find agreement in the nurses' dispute. Mediation continues on Tuesday.

## Grain ships from Ukraine

![harvesting](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12571901)

Open image viewer

Grain was threshed in Kharkiv at the end of July. Photo: AOP

On Sunday 4 ships full of food left Ukraine. Turkey reports this.

In total 31 ships carrying grain have already left Ukraine. The ships have left since Russia and Ukraine agreed on grain shipments in July.

Ukraine produces a lot of grain. Ukrainian grain is important for many countries in the world.

## Attack on a hotel in Somalia

![Somali security forces in the hotel area where the Islamist attack began on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_413/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99729563015bc57408f/1661033575)

Open image viewer

Somali security forces in the hotel area where the Islamist attack began on Friday. Photo: Said Yusuf Warsame / EPA

In the Somali capital Mogadishu, terrorists attacked a hotel on Friday. The attack ended on Sunday. Somalia's security forces fought against the terrorists for about 30 hours.

An official says that at least 21 people died in the attack and almost 120 people were injured.

## Pedestrian crossings

![A police officer watches traffic near a pedestrian crossing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1893,h_1065,x_13,y_230/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8455706124dcda43608/1629806991)

Open image viewer

A police officer watches traffic near a pedestrian crossing. Photo: Johanna Manu / Yle

From Monday the police are monitoring across the whole country that it is safe for people to cross the pedestrian crossing. The police monitoring lasts 2 weeks.

The police observe what cars and trams do at the pedestrian crossing. A motorist must give room when a person walks across the pedestrian crossing.

On average 20 pedestrians die in traffic every year. About 10 percent of all traffic fatalities are pedestrians. Often an accident happens when a pedestrian crosses a pedestrian crossing.

On Monday it rains in Eastern Finland, Kainuu and in places in Lapland. In Western Finland the sun shines in many places.

It is warm on Monday in the daytime, 20...25, in Lapland 15...20 degrees.

In a large part of Finland it is quite windy.
