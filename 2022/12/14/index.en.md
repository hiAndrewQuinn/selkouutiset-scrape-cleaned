## Emergency departments at hospitals

![Grey building with a sign reading "Tays päivystys Acuta".](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_480/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1047955639986e9c4646/1672146563)

For example, in Tampere there is a bad queue at the emergency department. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

Emergency departments at hospitals have become badly overcrowded in various parts of Finland. The reason is a shortage of nurses and care places. There are not enough places for patients to continue their treatment, for example on hospital wards.

That is why many patients have to wait a long time in emergency departments. In some hospitals, new patients cannot always be taken into the emergency department.

The hospitals are worried about the situation. The situation may get worse at Christmas, because wards have to be closed because of a shortage of nurses.

## Adequacy of electricity

![Power lines at the Merikoski power plant in Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1043798638d927f4059d/1670222547)

Open image viewer

It is good to save electricity now whenever possible. Photo: Taneli Kärki / Yle

The adequacy of electricity in Finland is now on the edge. Almost all the electricity that Finland is able to produce is being used.

Cold air has increased electricity consumption. The new Olkiluoto 3 nuclear power plant is not yet in operation. Wind turbines do not produce much electricity, because the weather is calm.

The grid company Fingrid says that it is worth saving electricity now in homes, companies and everywhere.

## Children's fitness

![A boy makes his way through an obstacle course of hula hoops in a school gym lesson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_122/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1047854639960f4ee6bb/1670996320)

Open image viewer

There are big regional differences in comprehensive school pupils' fitness. Photo: Svetlana Lazarenka / Alamy/All Over Press

The fitness of many comprehensive school pupils is poor. This is what a new study says. About 40 percent of comprehensive school pupils have fitness so poor that it may make it hard to cope with everyday life.

There are regional differences in young people's fitness. In big cities young people's fitness is better than in the countryside. The reason may be that big cities have many hobby and exercise opportunities.

In cities it is often easy to join hobbies. Cities also have more friends with whom you can do hobbies.

## Rising prices

![Contents of a grocery bag on the table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10423386388ada7a79c2/1669901816)

Open image viewer

For example, food has become much more expensive this year. Photo: Isto Janhunen / Yle

Inflation, that is, the rise in prices, has accelerated further. Prices rose by more than 9 percent in November compared with last November. This is what Statistics Finland says, which monitors price changes.

As late as October, the rate of price rises was a little over 8 percent.

The biggest increases have been in electricity, housing loan interest rates and fuel prices. Food prices have also risen quickly.

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio have received traditional Christmas greetings at the president's official residence at Mäntyniemi.

The president's family received a Christmas tree from forest students, for example. The president's family also received a traditional Christmas catch from Korppoo.

The boys' choir Cantores Minores sang for the president. The Lucia maiden and the Lucia choir also sang for President Niinistö and Mrs Haukio.
