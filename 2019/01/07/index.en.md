# Monday 7.1.2019 (radio)

Children's ability to concentrate is worse than before. New recruits started military service. An aircraft ran off the runway in Savonlinna. Bad weather again prevented the search for the missing on a Norwegian mountain. The presidential couple makes a state visit to China.

## Children's concentration has weakened

![A mobile phone in a child's hand at school](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4594,h_2584,x_0,y_237/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5321655c18e523f2707/1545135667)

Children use many different smart devices both at home and at school. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

Teachers say that pupils' ability to concentrate has weakened.

In a Yle survey, more than 80 percent of teachers say that pupils' ability to concentrate has weakened over the last 5 years.

Teachers say that the weakening of the ability to concentrate is perhaps because pupils use many different smart devices.

More than 400 comprehensive school teachers from various parts of Finland answered Yle's survey.

## The army got new recruits

![Recruits in the back yard of the barracks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9388562)

Open image viewer

12,500 recruits started their basic training period in the army. Photo: Tiia Korhonen / Yle

New recruits started military service today.

Recruits are men and women who start the basic military training. The basic training lasts 8 weeks. After that they take the military oath and the recruits become soldiers.

Today about 12,500 new recruits started in the army. About 500 of them are women.

Finnish men are obliged to take part in national defence, for example in the army. For women, the army is voluntary.

## An aircraft ran off the runway

![Raf Avia aircraft stopped off to the side of the runway at Savonlinna airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10585324)

Open image viewer

The Latvian Raf Avia aircraft ran off to the side of the runway when it tried to land at Savonlinna airport. Photo: Accident Investigation Board Finland

An aircraft has run off the runway at Savonlinna.

The aircraft of the Latvian company landed at Savonlinna airport in the morning, when it ran off the runway to the side.

There were only 3 crew members on board, but no passengers. Nobody was injured.

The Accident Investigation Board is investigating what caused the aircraft to run off the runway.

## Bad weather prevented the searches again

![Map graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10585133)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics

In Norway the weather was too bad today to search for the missing Finnish skiers. Bad weather has prevented the searches on many days already.

3 Finnish men and 1 Swedish woman disappeared in Norway last week, on Wednesday. A large avalanche occurred in the area at the same time.

The Norwegian police believe that the missing people were buried by the avalanche and have died.

The police say that the 29-, 32- and 36-year-old Finnish men and the 29-year-old Swedish woman were buried by the avalanche.

## Niinistö makes a state visit to China

![The presidents of Finland and China with their spouses met in Helsinki in April 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10584440)

Open image viewer

The presidents of Finland and China met in Helsinki in April 2017. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / EPA

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio are making a state visit to China. The visit is next week and lasts 2 days.

President Niinistö will meet China's President Xi Jinping in Beijing next Monday. The presidents will discuss, for example, relations between Finland and China, relations between great powers and other international topics.

Niinistö will also meet China's Prime Minister and the Chairman of the National People's Congress. Niinistö's programme also includes, for example, a seminar where climate change will be discussed.
