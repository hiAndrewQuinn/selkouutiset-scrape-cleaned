The Finns Party's Jussi Halla-aho wants to be a presidential candidate. The Finns Party will decide on the party's presidential candidate in August.

Jussi Halla-aho is now the Speaker of Parliament, that is, he leads Parliament's work. Previously he was the chair of the Finns Party.

Presidential elections will be held at the beginning of next year. Many candidates are taking part in the election, for example Pekka Haavisto and Olli Rehn.

Haavisto belongs to the Greens, and he is a former foreign minister. Rehn's party is the Centre Party, and he is the Governor of the Bank of Finland.

## Social media

![A mobile phone in a user's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3596,h_2021,x_0,y_218/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10805406401d8df2bf06/1740321087)

Photo: Larry W. Smith / EPA

Social media is a problem for many young adults. This is what a new study by the University of Oulu says.

In the study, about 70 per cent of young adults felt that using social media was problematic. In the young people's view, social media took too much time, for example.

In the researchers' view, social media use was problematic for 10 per cent of the young people in the study. Social media disturbed these young people's everyday life and well-being.

The researchers say that problematic use of social media increases, for example, loneliness and depression among young adults. Problematic social media use was highest among young women.

## Bicycle thefts

![A parked electric bicycle at a bicycle park in central Jyväskylä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5302,h_2982,x_0,y_48/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1136295649d8488833e3/1688044774)

Open image viewer

Thieves want expensive electric bicycles. Photo: Jaana Polamo / Yle

A lot of bicycles are stolen in the summer. Thieves are now especially interested in expensive electric bicycles.

The most bicycles were stolen in 2020. Since then, the number of bicycle thefts has decreased, but the monetary value of the damage has risen.

Thieves are now taking electric bicycles that cost up to 5,000 euros. Expensive electric scooters also interest thieves.

## Wildfires in Canada

![Aerial image of a wildfire in Canada.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_83/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113927264a75787bf480/1688688909)

Open image viewer

A fire in Canada has destroyed a lot of forest this summer. Photo: Lehtikuva / AFP / Audrey Marcoux / SOPFEU /

In Canada there have been big wildfires this year. A record amount of land and forest has burned.

So far, more forest has burned in one year than ever before. Firefighting work has also cost a record amount.

The wildfire season in Canada is only halfway through. Canada's forest service is worried, because warm and dry weather continues in Canada. That is why many wildfires may also start during July and August.

On Saturday showers will form in the southern and central parts of Finland. Thunder also occurs.

In the northern part of the country it is dry, that is, it does not rain there. The temperature is between 15 and 20 degrees.

On Sunday the rain stops, and in a large part of the country it is dry. However, the temperature stays between 15 and 20 degrees.
