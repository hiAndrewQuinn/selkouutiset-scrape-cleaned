# Sunday 20.11.2016 (radio)

The state apologises to children. Children's Rights Day. A bad train accident in India. The Swedish police want stricter alcohol control on ships. The Christmas street was opened.

## The state apologises to children

![Family and basic services minister Juha Rehula.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9305178)

Open image viewer

Minister Juha Rehula made the state's apology on Sunday at Finlandia Hall in Helsinki. Photo: Yle

Family Minister Juha Rehula made the state's apology to all the Finns who were treated badly by Finnish child welfare.

The apology is based on a study that looked at children's experiences in institutions from the 1930s to the 1980s. According to the survey, the children experienced physical, mental and sexual violence.

## Children's Rights Day

Today, Sunday, is International Children's Rights Day.

The UN Convention on the Rights of the Child came into force in Finland exactly 25 years ago. The aim of the convention is to guarantee health, education, equality and safety for all children.

## A bad train accident in India

![The accident site near the city of Kanpur on Sunday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9304993)

Open image viewer

The accident site near the city of Kanpur on Sunday. Photo: STR / EPA

In India more than 100 people have died in a train accident. More than 150 have been injured.

The accident happened early on Sunday in the north Indian state of Uttar Pradesh, when a passenger train derailed. The cause of the accident is not known.

Train accidents are common in India, and the cause is often human error or old equipment.

## The Swedish police want stricter alcohol control on ships

![Beer tankard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8897783)

Open image viewer

According to the Swedish police, too much alcohol is drunk on Swedish ferries. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The Swedish police want Swedish ships to follow the country's alcohol laws also in international traffic.

According to the Swedish police, violence and sexual offences are a problem on Swedish ferries. The police want stricter alcohol control for them. According to the police, the ferry companies sell too much alcohol to customers.

## The Christmas street was opened

![Aleksanterinkatu with Christmas lights.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7646058)

Open image viewer

Aleksanterinkatu with Christmas lights. Photo: Yle

The wait for Christmas is starting all over Finland, as Christmas streets and Christmas markets were opened in the cities at the weekend.

In Helsinki the traditional Christmas street, Aleksanterinkatu, was officially opened in the afternoon, when Santa Claus lit the Christmas lights.

Santa Claus also visited Tampere on Sunday.
