# Wednesday 31.3.2021 (radio)

The debate about movement restrictions continues in Finland. Gyms will close completely in Helsinki, for example. Grocery shops want to avoid the Easter rush. The weather is dry and sunny.

## Movement restrictions

The debate about movement restrictions continues in Finland.

The government proposed movement restrictions last week. The government wants to restrict people's movement, so that the spread of the coronavirus slows down.

The government's proposal says, for example, where people may move and with how many other people they may be.

The Constitutional Law Committee of Parliament now says that the government proposes bans that are too strict.

In the opinion of the Constitutional Law Committee, the government's proposal is also unclear. It is hard for people to know what is banned and what is not. That is why, in the Constitutional Law Committee's view, the proposal must be changed.

The government now intends to withdraw the proposal on movement restrictions because of the opinion of the Constitutional Law Committee.

## Sports facilities

![Empty gym.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7848806050bffb136ec/1615904960)

Open image viewer

Gyms are closing. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

Gyms and other sports facilities will close completely, for example in the Helsinki area, because of the corona situation.

Until now, sports facilities have been allowed to stay open if there were at most 10 customers there at the same time.

However, tomorrow, Thursday, sports facilities will be closed completely, and they will stay closed for 2 weeks.

However, activities for small children may still be organised in the facilities.

## Grocery shops

![A customer carries a shopping basket in a grocery shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-774562602b9bbdbb620/1674474276)

Because of corona, it is not worth going shopping at the busy times. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Easter will be celebrated next weekend.

Grocery shops hope that not all customers come to do Easter shopping at the same time. For example, Thursday is a popular shopping day before Easter.

Because of corona, customers should avoid crowds. That is why many shops have extended their opening hours. In addition, shopping can be done online.

In the shop, customers must remember the corona guidelines. Masks and safety distances improve safety during Easter shopping too.

On Thursday, the weather will be dry across most of the country, which means no rain will fall. However, there may be snow showers in Eastern Lapland.

In the southern and central parts of the country the weather is sunny, and in the north it is cloudier. The temperature is between +2 and +10 degrees.
