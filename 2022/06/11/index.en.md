European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen travelled to Ukraine on Saturday. She met Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

## Corona medicines

![Molnupiravir is a corona medicine launched by the pharmaceutical company Merck. Molnupiravir pills.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5499,h_3078,x_0,y_229/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-878174618bf31d6715c/1642524929)

Open image viewer

Corona medicines have been developed in many pharmaceutical companies. Photo: Merck Sharp &amp; Dohme Corp

The first corona medicines may come to Finland already in July. This is what the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health says.

At first, corona medicines will come to Finland for a few thousand patients. Corona medicines are given to patients for whom the coronavirus is very dangerous, for example because of some illness.

Treatment with corona medicines must be started as soon as possible after a corona infection. Some corona medicines are taken only once, but some corona medicines the patient must use for some time..

## UV radiation

![A woman spreads sunscreen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9046795)

Open image viewer

A hat, clothes and sunscreen protect against the sun's UV radiation. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

The Finnish Meteorological Institute warned on Saturday about strong UV radiation from the sun. Strong UV radiation is in the south and central parts of the country.

It is good for people to be careful about strong UV radiation. UV radiation increases the risk of skin cancer. Clothes and sunscreens protect against UV radiation.

The time of strong UV radiation in Finland often starts about 2 weeks before Midsummer, that is, right about now.
