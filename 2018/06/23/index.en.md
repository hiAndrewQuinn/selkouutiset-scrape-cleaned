# Friday 22.6.2018 (radio)

Power cuts have hit different parts of Finland. Prime Minister Juha Sipilä is taking part in a meeting of EU leaders in Brussels. New potatoes have come to the shops early this year. Midsummer magic may bring love.

## Wind cut the power

![Power line.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10167211)

Open image viewer

Strong wind can cause damage to power lines. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Power has been cut in different parts of Finland. Many thousands of homes have been without power on Friday, which is Midsummer Eve.

The power went off because of the strong wind. On Friday the wind has been so strong that it has knocked trees onto power lines.

The wind also disrupted train traffic. Train traffic between Iisalmi and Kajaani in Eastern Finland was cut for a while, because the wind knocked trees onto the railway line.

## Sipilä travels to an EU meeting

![Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10268641)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Finland's Prime Minister Juha Sipilä will meet other EU leaders on Sunday. Sipilä is taking part in a meeting of EU leaders in Brussels.

At the meeting, the leaders will discuss immigration and refugees and prepare for an EU summit. The summit will be held next week.

Immigration is a current topic in Europe. Some EU countries want to reduce immigration.

Chancellor Angela Merkel of Germany hopes that EU countries will agree together on how immigration and refugee matters are handled.

## Early potatoes are a summer treat

![Early potatoes are taken from the field to washing and cold storage, from where the potatoes are packed for customers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9739509)

New potatoes have come to the shops early this year.

The start of summer was warm and sunny. That is why potatoes were lifted from the fields about 2 weeks earlier than usual.

Finns are happy that there are new potatoes on the Midsummer dinner table. New potatoes, that is early potatoes, are a treat of early summer.

## Midsummer magic brings love

![7 kinds of flowers on a pillow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10268774)

Open image viewer

Flowers ready for the Midsummer meal. Photo: Kaisa Siren / AOP

For Finns, Midsummer is a time of love. People were looking for a partner long before Tinder.

An old way to find a partner was Midsummer magic. The bright Midsummer night was a good time to do magic.

The most common Midsummer magic was this: Gather 7 different kinds of flowers and put them under your pillow on Midsummer night. In your dream you see whom you will marry.

Another way was to put a birch whisk from the sauna under your pillow. The branches of the whisk must not be tied together. The whisk also brought the future spouse into your dream.

On Midsummer night it was also worth looking into a well. The image of your future spouse appeared in the water of the well. The magic worked, at least if you looked into the well naked.

It is worth trying. Maybe some magic still works today. If you can't be bothered to do magic, ask Tinder who the right one for you is.
