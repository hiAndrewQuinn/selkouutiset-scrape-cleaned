# Saturday 19.8.2017 (radio)

The knife attack in Turku was perhaps terrorism. The attacker chose women as victims. Finland mourns the victims of the stabbing. The leaders of many countries have expressed their condolences.

## The knife attack in Turku was perhaps a terrorist attack

![An armed police officer on guard in the Turku market square on Friday 18 August 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9785584)

The police were on guard in the Turku market square on Friday. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

The police suspect that yesterday's knife attack in Turku was a terrorist attack. The police are now investigating the attack as murders and attempted murders committed with terrorist intent.

The police suspect that the stabber, that is, the person who carried out the knife attack, is an 18-year-old Moroccan citizen. He came to Finland a year ago and applied for asylum here.

The stabber was arrested yesterday when the police shot him in the leg. The stabber is now being treated in hospital.

The police have also arrested 4 other men who are Moroccan citizens. The police are investigating whether the men were involved in the stabbing in some way. In addition, an international arrest warrant has been issued for one person.

## Most of the victims of the stabbing were women

![Map graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9786007)

Open image viewer

The stabber fled from the Turku Market Square to Puutori, where the police stopped him. Photo: Google, Yle

On Friday afternoon the stabber attacked people in the centre of Turku. He stabbed a total of 10 people, of whom 2 died. In addition, 8 people were injured.

It looks as if the stabber chose women as victims. Both of the dead are women, and 6 of the injured are also women. The men who became victims were stabbed when they tried to help others or stop the stabbings.

Among the injured are 1 Italian, 1 Swedish and 1 British citizen; the other victims are Finnish.

## The president also brought flowers to the scene of the killings

![Sauli Niinistö lays a wreath.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9786036)

Many flowers and candles were brought to the scene of the killings at the Turku Market Square. President Niinistö also brought flowers. Photo: Markku Sandell / Yle

Today flags are flying at half mast in Finland because of the stabbings in Turku.

Flying a flag at half mast means that the Finnish flag is first raised to the top of the flagpole, but after that it is lowered a little and left to fly at half mast.

People have also brought a lot of candles and flowers to the Turku Market Square. The candles and flowers have been placed at the spot where the first stabbings took place. President Sauli Niinistö also brought flowers to the scene of the killings.

A moment of silence will be held tomorrow, Sunday, in memory of those killed in the stabbing. It will be held in the morning at 10 o'clock and it will last one minute.

## The attack attracted attention around the world

![A flag at half mast, Turku Cathedral in the background. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9785677)

Open image viewer

In Turku and elsewhere in Finland, the victims of the stabbing were remembered by flying flags at half mast. Photo: Yle

The leaders of many countries have expressed their condolences because of the stabbings in Turku.

Condolences have been expressed, for example, by the President of France, the Prime Ministers of Sweden, Estonia and Denmark, and the President of the EU Commission.

The media of many countries have reported on the stabbings. It has been in the news, for example, in the United States, Japan and India.
