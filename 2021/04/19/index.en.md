# Monday 19.4.2021 (radio)

Restaurants reopened. The rise in rents has stopped. Kindergartens are short of substitute staff. The corona situation is easing. Weather forecast.

## Restaurants reopened

![Turku neighbourhood restaurant Arvo's restaurateur Tommi Avola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2266,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-796223607d88de23bb6/1618839995)

In the worst corona areas, restaurants may be open during the day. Photo: Ari Welling / Yle

Restaurants and cafés have been allowed to open again across Finland. Restaurants and cafés had been closed in the worst corona areas.

However, restaurant opening is limited. In the worst corona areas, the sale of alcoholic drinks must stop as early as 5 pm. Bars will close at 6 pm, and food restaurants will close at 7 pm.

Not all restaurants will open, because they are not allowed to be open in the evenings. Being open only during the day is not profitable for every restaurant.

## The rise in rents has stopped

![Property sale, empty flat, rental flat](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11046609)

Open image viewer

Not every rental flat has found a tenant. Photo: Karoliina Marttala / Yle

Rents for flats have stopped rising in almost all cities. Rents have not risen because renting has decreased and more rental flats are available than before.

Because of remote work, people now want bigger flats, and renting two-room flats, for example, has decreased.

In university towns, rental flats have been left empty. Studying has moved online, and students have stayed in their home towns.

## Kindergartens are short of substitute staff

![Kindergarten children on a walk. 25.3.2021](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4256,h_2394,x_0,y_279/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-788084605c4625ca7b7/1616660581)

Open image viewer

The workload of kindergarten staff is heavy and there is a shortage of substitutes. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

Many kindergartens are short of substitute staff. Because of the corona situation, substitutes are needed more often than before.

If a kindergarten worker has flu-like symptoms, for example, they must stay away from work. If a kindergarten worker may have been exposed to the coronavirus, they must stay away from work. During the corona time, staff have more absences than before, and substitutes are needed more often.

However, because of the corona situation, it is hard to get substitutes for kindergartens. Some substitutes are afraid, for example, that they could catch a corona infection at the kindergarten.

## Corona situation

![A nurse takes a corona sample.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_78/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-775816602e919ef2b2f/1613729485)

The corona situation is improving in Finland.

On Monday, 156 corona infections were reported in Finland. Half of them are in the Helsinki and Uusimaa area.

On Monday, 4 new deaths caused by the coronavirus were reported.

At least one dose of the corona vaccine has now been received by 1.3 million Finns, that is, about 24 percent of all Finns.
