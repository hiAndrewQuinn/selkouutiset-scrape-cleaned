# Wednesday 3.5.2017 (radio)

The tyre maker Nokian Renkaat is building a new factory in the USA. The food company HKScan is cutting jobs. Apartment prices and rents have risen in Uusikaupunki. Flying cars are coming. There is good outdoor weather in the coming days.

## Nokian Renkaat's factory

![A tyre being checked at the Nokian Renkaat factory in Nokia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9394050)

Nokian Renkaat makes tyres. Photo: Jussi Mansikka / Yle

The tyre maker Nokian Renkaat is building a new factory in the USA. Nokian Renkaat is a Finnish company that makes tyres for passenger cars, lorries and machines.

Nokian Renkaat is building a factory that will give jobs to about 400 people. Construction of the factory in the USA starts next year, and work at the factory starts in 2020.

Nokian Renkaat's new factory costs about 330 million euros.

## HKScan's jobs

![HKScan products](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9592319)

Open image viewer

HKScan makes meat products. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The food company HKScan is cutting jobs. In Finland, 60 workers will lose their jobs.

Abroad, work ends for 100 workers. HKScan also operates in the Nordic countries and the Baltic countries.

Jobs are being cut in HKScan's offices and management.

HKScan makes meat products, and it has over 7,000 employees in different countries in total.

## Uusikaupunki housing

![A worker on the assembly line at the Uusikaupunki car factory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9523163)

Open image viewer

There is plenty of work at the Uusikaupunki car factory. Photo: Valmet Automotive

Living has become more expensive in Uusikaupunki. Flat prices have risen, and the rents of many rental flats are now much higher than before.

Flat prices and rents began to rise about a month ago. At that time the Uusikaupunki car factory said that it is hiring many new workers.

Many people are moving to the town who have got a job at the car factory.

## Flying cars

![flying car](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9591972)

Open image viewer

Airbus is making a system that can both drive and fly. Photo: Airbusgroup

Flying cars are already being sold around the world. New models will come in the next few years.

Flying cars are starting to be technically so good that they can soon be put into use. There are still many problems, however, for example in traffic safety. Flying cars are also still very expensive.

The aircraft maker Airbus presented a completely new kind of system at the Geneva motor show. The system includes a capsule that can fit 2 passengers. The capsule can be attached to different devices, so that it becomes a car or an aircraft. When a passenger reports a trip to the system, the system sends a suitable device. The capsule can also be attached to a train, for example. Airbus estimates that the system will be ready for use perhaps in about 10 years' time.
