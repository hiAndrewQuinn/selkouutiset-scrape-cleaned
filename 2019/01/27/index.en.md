# Sunday 27.1.2019 (radio)

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä supports an entrance test for asylum seekers. People have died in a bomb attack in the Philippines. A dam accident happened in Brazil. This winter's new frost record is 38.7 degrees of frost. Events were cancelled on Sunday because of the frost.

## Interview with the Prime Minister

![Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10593193)

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä was interviewed on the radio on Sunday. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä supports an entrance test for asylum seekers. In Sipilä's view, an asylum seeker must know how to behave in Finland.

In Finland it has been proposed that asylum seekers be given an entrance test for Finland. The reason for the proposal is the sexual crimes in Oulu and Helsinki, whose perpetrators probably have a foreign background. No decision has yet been made on an entrance test for asylum seekers.

Prime Minister Sipilä was interviewed on Yle Radio Suomi on Sunday.

## Bomb attack in the Philippines

![Traces of the bomb attack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10616271)

Open image viewer

A bomb exploded in a church on Jolo island. Photo: AFP

A bomb attack has happened in the Philippines, in Asia. More than 20 people died and more than 80 people were injured in the attack.

The attack happened on Jolo island, where 2 bombs exploded in the capital city. The first bomb exploded in a church, and the second bomb exploded outside the church. The bomb attack happened in the morning, when there were people in the church.

It is not yet known who carried out the bomb attack.

## Dam accident in Brazil

![Rescue work is under way near the city of Brumadinho.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10615446)

Open image viewer

Mud covered large areas after the dam of the mine broke. Photo: PAULO FONSECA/ EPA

In Brazil, in South America, people have died in a dam accident.

At least 40 people died in the accident. There may be more dead, because almost 300 people are still missing.

In the accident the dam of a mine broke, and a lot of mud got into the environment. The mud covered houses and roads.

Rescuers have been searching for the people who disappeared in the accident. However, the rescuers had to stop the search when the authorities warned that the mine's dam could break in another place too.

## New frost record

![Close-up of snowy moss](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10605974)

Open image viewer

Photo: Heini Holopainen / Yle

A new frost record for this winter was set over the weekend.

The new record is 38.7 degrees of frost. It was measured early on Sunday morning in Sodankylä, in Lapland.

Finland's coldest frost was measured 20 years ago. Then, in Kittilä in Lapland, it was 51.5 degrees of frost.

## Events were cancelled

![A cold frosty day in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10616020)

Open image viewer

Because of the hard frost, it has been difficult to hold events outdoors. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The hard frost has affected Sunday's events in different parts of Finland.

In Kuopio, the Frost Festival was cancelled. It is a park event that is held outdoors. Kuopio residents could not celebrate the Frost Festival, because there was too much frost.

In Seinäjoki, the harness racing was cancelled on Sunday. There was too much frost for the horses and their drivers.

Early in the week the hard frost will ease, which means the bitter cold will end.
