# Thursday 15.10.2020 (radio)

The number of coronavirus infections is growing. The government has given guidelines because of the coronavirus. The government proposes law changes concerning taxis. The film Aurora won 7 Jussi awards.

The number of coronavirus infections continues to grow. In total there are about 13,000 coronavirus infections in Finland.

THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, said on Thursday that there are 241 new coronavirus infections.

Most of the new coronavirus infections are among young people.

## Coronavirus guidelines

![Education minister Li Andersson, science and culture minister Annika Saarikko and family and basic services minister Krista Kiuru at a government press conference in Helsinki on 15 October 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11596731)

The Finnish government has given guidelines because of the coronavirus. For example, the government recommends remote work across the country.

The coronavirus epidemic is now in an accelerating phase in many parts of Finland. That means the coronavirus is spreading quickly in these areas. Uusimaa is one such area.

The government recommends that people do not hold private events of more than 20 people in areas in the accelerating phase. Private events are, for example, parties held at home.

The government also recommends that group hobbies be stopped for now in areas in the accelerating phase. For example, team sports should be stopped.

So the government has given recommendations. Local authorities in each area make the more detailed decisions.

## Taxi law

![A taxi in Helsinki in July 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_419/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6976885f070c960505e/1594297852)

The government wants the taxi sign to become compulsory. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The government proposes law changes concerning taxis.

The government wants taxis to state their prices more clearly than now. All taxis must have a taximeter or another device that can be used to monitor taxes.

The government also wants taxi drivers to have to take a test. In addition, a taxi must have a taxi sign with a light on the roof.

The government gave its proposals to Parliament on Thursday. Parliament decides whether the laws are changed. The government's aim is that the law changes come into force at the beginning of next year.

## Jussi awards

![Car journey scene from the film Aurora.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10617569)

Open image viewer

In the film Aurora, a wife is sought for Darian. Photo: Dionysos Films

The Jussi awards for the film industry have been given out. The awards were given out on Wednesday in Helsinki.

The Jussi award for best film went to the film Aurora. It tells the story of a young woman, Aurora, who lives in the north, in Rovaniemi.

In the film, Aurora promises to find a wife for the Iranian Darian, because Darian needs a residence permit.

The film Aurora won 7 Jussi awards in total. For example, Mimosa Willamo, who plays Aurora, won a Jussi award.

The director of the film, Miia Tervo, also won a Jussi. Tervo is herself from Rovaniemi.

The Jussi awards for the film industry are given out every year.
