# Tuesday 30.4.2019 (radio)

Government parties answered Antti Rinne's questions. Stockmann begins co-operation negotiations. Companies demand climate decisions from the EU. Japan gets a new emperor. Now May Day is being celebrated.

## Rinne received answers

![Government explorer Antti Rinne at a media event in the State Hall of Parliament on May Day Eve](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10761586)

Antti Rinne is acting as government explorer. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Parliamentary parties have given answers to government explorer Antti Rinne's questions. SDP chairman Antti Rinne says that the answers are businesslike and good.

Rinne still asks for more information about the parliamentary parties' opinions on European, climate and economic policy. Government explorer Rinne estimates that government negotiations can be started next week.

## Stockmann cuts jobs

![Stockmann department store in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10760782)

Open image viewer

Stockmann is looking for ways to reduce its losses. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The department store company Stockmann begins co-operation negotiations. 160 employees may lose their jobs. Stockmann made a loss of about 20 million euros in January to March.

Stockmann says that costs must be reduced and the department store must be renewed, so that the business can be made profitable. Department store salespeople will not be cut.

## Companies demand climate decisions

![Factory area.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8361427)

Open image viewer

Oil company Neste is one of the signatories. The picture shows Neste's refinery in Porvoo. Photo: Ismo Henttonen / Neste Oil

More than 50 big European companies are demanding climate decisions from politicians.

The companies have sent a letter to the leaders of the EU countries, in which they demand the goal of a carbon-neutral Europe by 2050. The companies want to be sure that it is worth using money on clean technology.

From Finland the letter has been signed by, for example, the lift company Kone, the oil company Neste and the electricity producer Fortum.

## Japan gets a new emperor

![Akihito attends a ceremony in Tokyo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10760960)

Open image viewer

Naruhito became emperor in 1989. Photo: EPA

Power is changing hands in Japan. Emperor Akihito has today given up the duties of emperor. Akihito is 85 years old. He is giving up power for health reasons. Akihito has been Emperor of Japan for 30 years.

Tomorrow, Wednesday, Akihito's son, Crown Prince Naruhito, will become the new emperor.

## May Day celebrations have begun

![Unto Seppänen statue wearing a cap on May Day in Kouvola](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10762356)

Open image viewer

Statues are also decorated with caps in cities other than Helsinki. In Kouvola the Unto Seppänen statue was given a cap. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

Today and tomorrow May Day is being celebrated. For many people the May Day celebration began at 6 pm, when a cap was put on the head of the Havis Amanda statue in Helsinki.

Statues have also been given caps in other cities. The weather on May Day is cool. It does not bother many people. On May Day people are in a festive mood. Spring begins with May Day.
