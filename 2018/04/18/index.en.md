## Construction sector strike

![Blocks of flats under construction.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10143151)

Open image viewer

The strike may slow down construction work. Photo: Kari Kosonen / Yle

A series of strikes starts in the construction sector on Thursday. The strikes last 2 days, and there will be strikes over 4 weeks.

The strikes will stop work in 7 construction industry companies. Workers plan to stop work at, for example, Rudus, Betonimestarit and Parma.

The strikes affect the making of concrete and concrete elements. That is why work on building sites may be delayed.

There is a dispute about pay in the construction sector. But the employers' and workers' organisations are continuing negotiations.

## Elections in Turkey

![President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10166194)

Open image viewer

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. Photo: Office of the President of Turkey / EPA

Turkey will hold elections in June. On the same day Turks will choose the parliament and the president. Election day is 24 June.

President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan announced this on Wednesday.

After the elections the president's power will grow. Last year Turkey had a referendum on the president's power. In the vote Turks wanted more power for the president. The change will come into force once elections have been held in Turkey.

## A woman's death

![An old woman in a wheelchair near a bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9907936)

Open image viewer

Many elderly people get care at home. Photo: Pixabay

The police in Helsinki are investigating a sad case. An old woman probably died at home, but nobody found her for many weeks.

The woman was a client of the city's home care. In the end a home care worker went to the woman's home because home care could not get in touch with the woman.

The city's home care is now investigating why nobody visited the woman for a long time. The police are also investigating the matter.

## The Seri support centre

![The door of the Seri support centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10165350)

Open image viewer

The Seri support centre helps rape victims. Photo: Yle

The Seri support centre works at the Women's Hospital in Helsinki. It helps victims of sexual crimes.

At the support centre a rape victim gets help from a doctor, a psychologist and a social worker. The service is free.

The Seri support centre has served residents of the Helsinki region for 10 months. There have been about 280 clients.

From May the Seri support centre will serve the whole Uusimaa region.
