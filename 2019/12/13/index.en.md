# Friday 13.12.2019 (radio)

The Conservative Party has won the British election. Electric cars are cheap to run. Nurses' right to prescribe medicines is expanding. Lucia brings light to the dark winter.

## Conservatives won in Britain

Prime Minister Boris Johnson's Conservative Party has won the extra parliamentary election in Britain. The party gains a majority of MPs in the British Parliament.

Boris Johnson says that Britain will leave the EU at the end of January.

The Labour Party, led by Jeremy Corbyn, lost the election. The Labour Party loses many seats in the British Parliament. Jeremy Corbyn says that he will step down as Labour leader before the next election.

## Electric cars are cheap

![An electric car's charging plug is in the car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10746458)

An electric car is charged with a power cable. Photo: Kalle Mäkelä / Yle

An electric car can be cheaper than an ordinary petrol or diesel car. An electric car is more expensive to buy than a petrol or diesel car.

Finnish researchers have calculated that an electric car is cheaper when it is used for a long time, for example 5 to 8 years.

An electric car is also good for the environment, because it has no exhaust emissions. Researchers say that Finland needs electric cars so that emissions can be reduced.

## Nurses' right to prescribe medicines expands

![A nurse handles a prescription at a medicine cabinet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5100082)

Open image viewer

Nurses can prescribe medicines to a limited extent. Photo: YLE

Nurses' right to prescribe medicines will expand at the start of next year.

From January, nurses will be allowed to prescribe medicines for, for example, coronary artery disease and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, or give a vaccine against tick-borne encephalitis. Nurses are already allowed to prescribe some medicines.

The aim is for patients to get treatment faster, because nurses can, for example, prescribe a repeat prescription for a medicine that the patient has received before.

## Lucia received a candle crown

![Lucia maiden of 2019 Sara Ray.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11116179)

Open image viewer

Finland's Lucia this year is Sara Ray. Photo: Henrik Schutt

Lucia Day was celebrated on Friday.

Finland's Lucia was crowned in Helsinki Cathedral. A candle crown was placed on Lucia's head. After that, Lucia walked through the centre of Helsinki in a big procession.

Finland's Lucia this year is Sara Ray. She is 24 years old and from Ähtävä.

Lucia is important to Swedish-speaking Finns, that is, Finland-Swedes. For example, Lucia celebrations are held in many Swedish-speaking schools.

During the winter, Lucia visits care homes, for example, and brings joy to the elderly. It is said that Lucia brings light to the dark winter.
