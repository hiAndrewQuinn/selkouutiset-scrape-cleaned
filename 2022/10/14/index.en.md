## Food prices

![Packets of porridge flakes in a shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_208/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-927799622f2e6219192/1647259926)

For example, semolina and flour have risen a lot in price. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The rise in food prices has speeded up to a new record. In September food was almost 15 percent more expensive than in September last year. Still in August the rise in food prices was under 13 percent. Flour, eggs, semolina and butter have risen the most in price.

Many things have become more expensive, but food has become especially more expensive. Experts estimate that the rise in food prices will speed up further towards the end of the year.

Statistics Finland also monitors the rise in prices of many other things. This is called inflation. Inflation monitoring includes, for example, the prices of food, petrol, clothes and services. Inflation in September was over 8 percent.

## Swedish government

![Johan Pehrson, Jimmie Åkesson, Ulf Kristersson and Ebba Busch walk side by side.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-102109363495a8971743/1665751774)

Details about the country's new government have been given in Sweden. This was said by Ulf Kristersson, leader of the Moderate Party, whose task has been to form the government.

The new Swedish government will include the Moderate Party, the Christian Democrats and the Liberals. That means that Sweden will have a right-wing government.

The Sweden Democrats will not get into the Swedish government, but the party will get many things into the government programme that are important to the Sweden Democrats. The Sweden Democrats want to reduce immigration, for example.

The Swedish Riksdag will vote on the new government on Monday.

## Corona situation

![Close-up of the door of the reception at Padasjoki health centre. A poster next to the door reads Corona vaccination point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4027,h_2266,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99789363046bc0166b4/1675253861)

Open image viewer

Corona vaccination and having had the disease give good protection against serious corona symptoms. Photo: Petri Niemi / Yle

The corona situation in Finland is fairly calm. There are about 1000 corona patients in hospitals.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, says that there is quite a lot of corona going around, but the symptoms of corona are much milder than at the start of the corona epidemic.

Many Finns already have corona antibodies, because many have had corona. Many also have had vaccinations. These give good protection against serious symptoms. THL estimates that the corona situation will not get worse especially in the autumn.

Many school pupils start their autumn holiday on Friday. That is why many families spend the autumn holiday next week.

The airport company Finavia says that there are crowds at Helsinki-Vantaa airport this weekend, because many Finns are leaving on holiday trips.

In Finland, spa and hotel holidays are popular during the autumn holiday. Many also spend the autumn holiday at their summer cottage. For some school pupils the autumn holiday is only the following week.
