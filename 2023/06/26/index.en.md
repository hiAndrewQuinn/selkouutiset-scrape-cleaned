The situation on the Finland-Russia border. Greek election. Helsinki Pride. Everyone's rights. The weather.

## Calm on the border

![Elina Valtonen greets the German foreign minister.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4500,h_2531,x_0,y_148/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1134410649947a03960d/1687766992)

The border between Finland and Russia is calm, Finland is not threatened by anything. This is what Finland's foreign minister Elina Valtonen says.

Valtonen commented on Finland's security because of the events in Russia. At the weekend, Wagner soldiers rose in rebellion against Russia's military leadership.

The leader of the Wagner soldiers, Jevgeni Prigožin, wanted the leaders of the Russian army to be replaced. The reason was disputes about how Russia's war actions in Ukraine should be led.

The rebellion ended quickly, but the situation in Russia is confused. Finland's foreign minister Valtonen says that Finland is following the situation in Russia closely.

## Greek election

![Kyriakos Mitsotakis stands on the speaker's podium.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4775,h_2685,x_68,y_13/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113432064988b38ca068/1687748381)

Kyriakos Mitsotakis won the Greek election. Photo: Lehtikuva

Parliamentary elections have been held in Greece.

The winner of the election is the New Democracy party. Its leader is the Greek Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis.

New Democracy got over 40 per cent of the votes in the parliamentary elections. The party gets more than half of the seats in Parliament.

Greece's previous parliamentary elections were held only a month ago. Then no party got a majority in Parliament.

## Helsinki Pride

![Pride flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4987,h_2805,x_12,y_442/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-113351464938532911c2/1687389629)

Helsinki Pride week has started. Helsinki Pride is a big human rights event. Its theme is the rights of sexual minorities.

The main event of Pride week is on Saturday. Then the Pride parade is held. Thousands of people walk from Senate Square in Helsinki to Kaivopuisto.

There are also Pride events elsewhere in Finland this week. Gay people and other sexual minorities celebrate at Pride events in Kuopio in Eastern Finland and in Kokkola in Western Finland.

## Everyone's rights

![A berry picker picks blueberries with a berry comb.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9999826308b3bf11cee/1661514958)

Open image viewer

Berry picking, for example, is part of everyone's rights. Photo: Juulia Tillaeus / Yle

Metsähallitus has told about a new word. The new word is everyone's rights.

Previously, Metsähallitus spoke about everyman's rights. The new word *everyone's rights* promotes equality, Metsähallitus says.

*Everyone's rights* means that in Finland everyone may use nature. For example, people other than the forest owner may also pick berries in the forest.

Metsähallitus looks after forests whose owner is the Finnish state.
