# Thursday 22.8.2019 (radio)

There are big forest fires in Brazil, in South America. EU citizens may soon get a free electronic visa to St Petersburg. Many children experience racism in Britain. Swedes have reduced flying. Friday is a rainy day.

## Brazil's forest fires

![Traces of ground fires in Mato Grosso state, Brazil, on 20 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10932640)

Open image viewer

Fire has destroyed a lot of rainforest in Brazil. Photo: Rogerio Florentino / EPA

In South America, in Brazil, there are big forest fires. The forest is burning in various parts of the Amazon region.

It is said that the Amazon rainforests are the lungs of the world. Climate change will speed up if the Amazon rainforests are destroyed. That is why the Amazon forest fires worry people in many parts of the world.

Brazilians have also set forest fires on purpose. They want more fields for soya and more pasture for cattle. Brazil sells a lot of soya and beef abroad.

Brazil's President Bolsonaro has been criticised around the world. In the president's view, rainforest may be burned for farming.

## St Petersburg visas

![People boarding the Allegro train at Helsinki Central Railway Station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4383,h_2465,x_0,y_260/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5787365d344234ac7be/1563707485)

Finns make many trips to St Petersburg. Photo: Jari Pussinen / YLE

Travel for EU citizens to St Petersburg will become easier. From the beginning of October, EU citizens can get an electronic visa for St Petersburg, which is free.

This was announced by Russian President Vladimir Putin, who visited Finland on Wednesday.

The new visa allows travel to the city of St Petersburg and its surroundings. The free visa is valid for 8 days.

## Racism in Britain

![Schoolchildren in London in their school uniforms](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10932682)

Open image viewer

Schoolchildren experience racism in Britain. Photo: AOP

Many children experience racism in Britain.

In Britain, children from Eastern Europe in particular are bullied. The information is from a study by a British university.

Almost 80 per cent of the schoolchildren who took part in the study experienced bullying. The children said that they are bullied because of their nationality, appearance or way of speaking.

Almost 50 per cent of the study's participants said that racism has increased in Britain since the Brexit vote. The vote was 3 years ago. At that time more than half of Britons voted for Britain to leave the European Union.

## Swedes' air travel

![A Norwegian plane takes off from Arlanda airport, with SAS planes in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10730244)

Open image viewer

Air travel is decreasing in Sweden. Photo: Johan Nilsson / EPA

Swedes have reduced flying.

In July this year Swedes flew 4 per cent less than in July last year. Within Sweden's borders, air travel fell by 11 per cent.

Many Swedes are reducing flying because of climate change. Aeroplanes use a lot of fuel, which warms the climate.

At the same time, Finns' air travel has grown.
