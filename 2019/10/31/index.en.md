# Thursday 31.10.2019 (radio)

Cuts will reduce slot machines. A Norwegian billionaire bought Tjäreborg. Boeing planes are banned from flying. The ice winter has begun.

## Veikkaus cuts slot machines

![A woman plays a gambling game. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10930590)

Open image viewer

A woman plays on a gambling slot machine. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Veikkaus is reducing gambling slot machines in shops and petrol stations.

Veikkaus will reduce 3500 slot machines next year. By 2025, as many as 8000 gambling slot machines may be reduced.

Right now, Veikkaus has about 18,500 gambling slot machines in total. The machines are in kiosks, shops, restaurants and petrol stations.

Veikkaus is also starting to require players to identify themselves. Players must identify themselves from the beginning of 2021. Identification prevents, for example, minors from playing

## Norwegian billionaire bought Tjäreborg

![Petter Stordalen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11043489)

Open image viewer

Petter Stordalen. Photo: Janerik Henriksson / EPA

The travel agency Tjäreborg is getting new owners.

Norwegian billionaire Petter Stordalen is buying Tjäreborg together with a Swedish and British investment company

In addition to Tjäreborg, the deal also includes the travel agency Ving in Sweden, Spies in Denmark and the airline Thomas Cook Airlines Scandinavia.

The travel agencies were part of the deal because their parent company, the British Thomas Cook Group, went bankrupt in September.

Tjäreborg is the 3rd largest travel organiser in Finland. It sells about 185,000 trips a year. Tjäreborg arranges trips especially to holiday destinations in the Canary Islands, Greece and Turkey.

Tjäreborg's new owner Petter Stordalen also owns hotels. For example, he owns the Kämp chain in Finland.

## Boeing planes banned from flying

![Ryanair's Boeing 737NG.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11044771)

Open image viewer

Boeing 737 NG Photo: Uwe Deffner / Alamy

The aircraft manufacturer Boeing has banned its planes from flying.

Boeing has banned 50 planes from flying, which are of the 737 NG model. The planes are banned from flying because cracks have been found in their structures.

Finnair does not use this aircraft model. Norwegian uses it, but its planes are so new that they do not need to be checked.

## The ice winter began in the Gulf of Bothnia

![Frozen Gulf of Bothnia at Kiviniemi in Oulu, 27 December 2015.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8555979)

The sea freezes in the frost. Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

The ice winter has begun in the Gulf of Bothnia. In some bays of the Gulf of Bothnia near the border between Finland and Sweden there is already an ice cover, the Finnish Meteorological Institute says.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute has today published the first ice maps of the winter.

Ice expert Jouni Vainio says that the actual freezing of the seas, however, may only begin at the turn of November and December, in the archipelago of the northern part of the Gulf of Bothnia.
