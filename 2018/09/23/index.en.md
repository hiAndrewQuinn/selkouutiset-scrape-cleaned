# Sunday 23.09.2018 (radio)

The police operation in the Turku region has continued. The valuable church of Kiihtelysvaara has burned down. Finns make more trips abroad than before. Sunday was the autumn equinox. Unusual chocolate flavours are made in Finland.

## Valuable church burned down

![A firefighter puts out the ruins of Kiihtelysvaara church.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10418901)

The fire burned Kiihtelysvaara church completely. Photo: Heikki Haapalainen / Yle

The valuable church of Kiihtelysvaara has burned down. Kiihtelysvaara is in Joensuu, in Eastern Finland.

The police say that someone perhaps started the fire on purpose. The fire burned the church completely.

Kiihtelysvaara church was a wooden church. It was about 270 years old.

## Police operation continued

![Investigators and police in the Turku archipelago on 22 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10418712)

Open image viewer

A large number of police took part in the police operation. Photo: Kirsi Kanerva / Lehtikuva

The big police operation in the Turku region has continued.

The reason for the operation is that the police suspect money laundering. With money laundering, criminals try to hide that the money was obtained through crime.

The target of the operation is a firm whose name the police have not yet revealed.

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä commented on the operation in a radio interview. Sipilä said that the operation is unusually big.

The Prime Minister also said that the police will tell how the operation is progressing. That is not the politicians' job.

## Travel abroad is increasing

![tourist](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10418682)

Open image viewer

Many Finns spend their winter holiday abroad. Photo: Juho Tuomisto / Yle

Finns make more trips abroad than before. For example, the travel agency Tjäreborg says that travel abroad has increased by almost 80 percent over the last 10 years.

Finns like to travel to a place that is familiar and safe. Next winter Finns will travel a lot to the Canary Islands, says the Association of Finnish Travel Agents.

## Summer ended

![söderfjärden cranes](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10397178)

Open image viewer

The sun is now fleeing from the north. Photo: Antti Haavisto/Yle

Today, Sunday, is the autumn equinox. Today day and night are equally long. The length of day and night is 12 hours everywhere on Earth.

Here in the north the autumn equinox ends the summer. In summer the day is longer than the night. After the autumn equinox the night is longer than the day.

The night gets shorter all through the autumn until about Christmas.

*(There is a mistake in the news item. The night does not get shorter in autumn. The night gets longer all through the autumn. Sorry.)*

## Unusual chocolate flavours

![Taiga chocolate's fish chocolate](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10414566)

Open image viewer

The firm Taiga chocolate makes fish chocolate. Photo: Merja Siirilä / Yle

Unusual chocolate flavours are made in Finland. A chocolate firm from Vaasa makes, for example, reindeer chocolate and fish chocolate. Fish chocolate has dark chocolate and dried fish.

The Vaasa firm took part in a food event in Hong Kong in August. Its products won a prize because they were among the best products at the event.

The head of the firm says that people wondered about the fish chocolate, but they still dared to taste it. And when they tasted it, they liked it. The Asians thought the Finnish fish chocolate was good.
