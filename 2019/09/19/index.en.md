# Thursday 19.9.2019 (radio)

The Navy is getting new warships. A new international border crossing will be built in Parikkala. Boys should also be given the HPV vaccine. Child and maternal mortality in the world has fallen.

## New warships for the Navy

![Artist's impression of a Pohjanmaa-class vessel](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10979308)

The Navy is getting 4 new ships. Photo: Finnish Defence Forces

Finland is buying new warships.

The government has decided to buy 4 new warships for the Navy. The ships will replace 7 old vessels, which will be taken out of use.

The new ships will be built in Finland at the Rauma shipyard. Building the ships may bring work for about 2,000 people.

The ships cost 1.3 billion euros. The ships are due to be ready by 2028.

## New route to Russia

![Parikkala border station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10978102)

Open image viewer

There is now a temporary border crossing in Parikkala. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

A new international border crossing will be opened on the Finland-Russia border.

The new border crossing will be between Parikkala in Finland and Syväoro in Russia. The new border crossing is planned to open for passenger and freight traffic in 2024.

At the moment, there is a temporary border crossing in Parikkala. That means that you need a special permit to cross the border there.

The new border crossing will be opened in Parikkala, because the government wants to strengthen the vitality of eastern Finland and the Savonlinna area.

## HPV vaccine for boys too

![The HPV vaccine is given in the upper arm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10351199)

Open image viewer

Today the HPV vaccine is only given to girls. Photo: AOP

The HPV vaccine should also be given to boys. Health authorities want the HPV vaccine to be given to boys as early as next autumn.

The HPV vaccine protects against many cancers. The HPV vaccine protects against cervical cancer and penile cancer, for example.

At the moment the vaccine is only given to girls. About 70 percent of girls get the HPV vaccination.

Tuija Leino, chief physician at the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, says that if the vaccine is also given to boys, vaccinations could prevent perhaps about 300 cancers a year

## Child and maternal deaths are falling

![A child in a parent's arms.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10846571)

Open image viewer

Child mortality has fallen, thanks to vaccines and better hygiene, for example. Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

Child mortality has fallen around the world, says the UN children's agency Unicef.

Child mortality has fallen by more than 50 percent in just under 30 years. In 1990, more than 14 million children under 15 years old died in the world. Last year about 6 million children died.

Child mortality has fallen thanks to vaccines and ever better hygiene, for example.

Maternal mortality has also fallen. In 2017, a little under 300,000 mothers died in different parts of the world. That is 35 percent fewer than in 2000.

Maternal and young child mortality is highest in sub-Saharan Africa.
