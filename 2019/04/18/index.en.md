# Thursday 18.4.2019 (radio)

Estonia has a new government. A bus accident happened on the Madeira holiday island. Valmet Automotive is hiring more employees. Easter brings changes to traffic. Easter has good weather for outdoor activities.

## Estonian government

![Jüri Ratas at the speaker's podium.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10746613)

Estonian Prime Minister Jüri Ratas. Photo: Toms Kalnins / EPA-EFE

Finland's neighbour Estonia has a new government. The Estonian Parliament approved the government, whose prime minister is Jüri Ratas.

Ratas of the Centre Party was also the prime minister of the previous government. The Fatherland party and EKRE also joined the new government.

Some Estonians do not like the fact that EKRE got into the government. EKRE opposes immigration, for example.

The Estonian parliamentary elections were in early March. The winner of the election was the Reform Party, but it was not able to agree on a government with the other parties.

## Madeira accident

![Bus that left the road on a slope.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10746043)

Open image viewer

The bus drove off the road and fell down a hill. Photo: AFP

A serious bus accident has happened on the Madeira holiday island.

29 people died when a tourist bus drove off the road and fell down a hill. Almost 30 people were injured.

The bus was carrying a German group, and the dead tourists were Germans.

The accident happened 12 kilometres from the capital of Madeira, Funchal. Madeira belongs to Portugal.

Valmet Automotive plans to hire more employees. It will hire about 1,000 new employees this year.

Employees are needed for the car factory in Uusikaupunki and the battery factory in Salo. The battery factory in Salo opens this year.

Valmet Automotive also hired 1,000 employees last year.

## Easter exceptions

![Autoliitto road service text on the back of overalls](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6426217)

Open image viewer

The telephone number of the Autoliitto road service is 0200-8080. You can get help from that number if problems arise in Easter traffic. Photo: Miki Wallenius / Yle

Thursday has been a busy day on the roads. Many people have travelled to celebrate Easter. There is also a lot of traffic on the roads on Monday, when people return home.

Autoliitto's Operation Pajunkki helps motorists who have problems in Easter traffic. Help can be obtained by calling the Autoliitto road service.

Bus and train timetables have many changes at Easter. Trains follow Sunday timetables on Friday and Monday.

Many grocery shops are open at Easter. Shops nowadays decide for themselves whether they are open or closed on holidays.

Banks and post offices are closed at Easter.

It is good outdoor weather on the Easter weekend.
