## Students' depression

![A young girl or woman sitting on the floor at a sofa table in the living room. Depression among university students has increased. A new study says that university students have more anxiety and depression than other Finns.

The reason is the corona situation. There are no shared events, and lectures are held remotely. Students are often alone. That is why difficulties in studying easily arise.

About 30 per cent of students experience symptoms of depression or anxiety.

## Ship accident

![Several rescue units are at the scene off the south coast of Sweden where a cargo ship capsized.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89152261b7630d37b28/1639408541)

Open image viewer

A Danish cargo ship capsized in a collision. Photo: Johan Nilsson / AFP

A ship accident has happened in the Baltic Sea. Two cargo ships collided during the night off the south coast of Sweden.

One cargo ship was Danish and the other cargo ship was British. The Danish cargo ship capsized in the collision. At the same time 2 people who were operating the Danish cargo ship disappeared. They have not been found at sea.

The authorities are investigating why the Danish and British cargo ships collided in the Baltic Sea. The authorities say that the reason may be drunken navigation, that is, the captain of one of the ships may have been drunk.

## St Lucia's Day

![Crowning of Finland's Lucia 2021, Tilde Polviander, in Helsinki Cathedral.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4416,h_2484,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89153661b76948399ad/1639410034)

Open image viewer

Finland's Lucia is Tilde Polviander. She was crowned by Mika Salminen, director of THL. Photo: Yle / Marcus Rosenlund

St Lucia's Day was celebrated on Monday. Finland's Lucia was crowned in Helsinki Cathedral. A candle crown was put on Lucia's head. Because of the corona situation, the traditional Lucia procession was not held.

Finland's Lucia this year is **Tilde Polviander**. She comes from Mariehamn in Åland.

Lucia is important to Finns who speak Swedish, that is, Finland-Swedes. For example, Lucia celebrations are held in many Swedish-speaking schools. During the winter Lucia visits, for example, care homes and brings joy to older people.
