# Thursday 20.9.2018 (radio)

There is a shortage of workers in many fields. The volcano Katla is waking up in Iceland. The Redi shopping centre has opened in Kalasatama in Helsinki. The Hunger Day collection has begun.

## Shortage of workers

![A man works on a building site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10291772)

Open image viewer

Workers are needed on building sites. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

There is a shortage of workers in many fields.

For example, more workers are needed in the construction industry. In construction, site supervisors and construction engineers are needed most.

There is also a shortage of workers in the care sector. The care sector needs more nurses and doctors. The biggest shortage is of speech therapists.

The information is from Finland's Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment.

## Katla volcano

![Katla volcano.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10414250)

Open image viewer

Katla last erupted in 1918. Photo: AOP

The volcano Katla is waking up in Iceland.

Volcano researchers say that Katla is releasing carbon dioxide into the air. It is a sign that Katla may erupt.

The researchers say that Katla's eruption will be big. On top of Katla there is as much as 600 metres of ice. When the eruption melts the ice, the water hits the volcano's hot lava. Then there is an explosion, and for example ash spreads far.

The researchers cannot say when Katla will erupt. Katla last erupted 100 years ago.

## Redi shopping centre

![Pia Svensk, shopping centre director, Redi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10413014)

Open image viewer

Pia Svenska is the director of the Redi shopping centre. Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

A new shopping centre has opened in Helsinki. The Redi shopping centre is in Kalasatama.

You can get to Redi easily by metro. Kalasatama metro station is on the 3rd floor of the shopping centre.

Redi has restaurants and cinemas. One special feature of Redi is a park. It is on the roof of the shopping centre.

Redi also has Finland's first food waste shop, WeFood. The food waste shop sells, for example, surplus food from shops. That way the food gets used and does not end up in the bin. WeFood's profit goes to aid work.

Kalasatama is a new district in Helsinki. Kalasatama is being built at a fast pace. High tower blocks with hundreds of flats are also coming to the area.

## Hunger Day collection

![Journalist Minna Rinta-Tassi as a collection box collector.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9833470)

Open image viewer

The Hunger Day collection can be seen on the streets. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

The Hunger Day collection has begun. The money from the collection goes to aid work.

This year money is needed in Bangladesh. There is a big refugee camp there, and the camp has a Finnish Red Cross hospital. The camp's residents are Rohingya Muslims who have fled from Myanmar.

The Finnish Red Cross organises the Hunger Day collection every year in September. The Hunger Day collection can be seen on the streets when volunteers collect money in red collection boxes.
