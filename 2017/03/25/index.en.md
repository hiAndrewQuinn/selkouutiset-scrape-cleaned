# Saturday 25.03.2017 (radio)

On Saturday there is the Earth Hour event. The European Union has turned 60 years old. Last year's best Finnish film is "Hymyilevä mies" (The Smiling Man). Summer time begins on Sunday. Sunday is a warm and windy day.

## Earth Hour

![](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5257265)

Open image viewer

The environmental organisation WWF organises the Earth Hour event. Photo: WWF

On Saturday Earth Hour is held. It is a climate event during which the lights are turned off for one hour, at 20.30. The lights go off, for example, at Helsinki's Finlandia Hall, at Jyväskylä's Vesilinna and along the Turku riverside.

The organiser of Earth Hour is the environmental organisation WWF. It hopes that Earth Hour will make people think about the environment. One theme this year is food. For example, raising meat puts a strain on the environment.

Earth Hour is held all over the world. The lights go off, for example, on the Eiffel Tower in Paris.

## The European Union

![Juha Sipilä signing a declaration in Rome on Saturday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9530550)

Open image viewer

Finland's Prime Minister Juha Sipilä also took part in the EU celebrations in Rome. Photo: Tiziana Fabi / AFP

The European Union has turned 60 years old. The development of the European Union began when 6 countries signed the Treaty of Rome in 1957. Today 28 countries are part of the European Union.

The leaders of the EU countries celebrated the union's birthday on Saturday in Rome. Leaders of all EU countries took part in the celebration, except for British Prime Minister Theresa May. May did not take part in the celebration, because Britain plans to leave the EU.

## Jussi awards

![Jarkko Lahti receiving the Jussi statuette for best actor for the film The Smiling Man](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9530063)

Open image viewer

Actor Jarkko Lahti received a Jussi award. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The best Finnish films of last year have been chosen. They were given Jussi awards. The Jussi is Finland's Oscar.

Last year's best Finnish film is "Hymyilevä mies" (The Smiling Man). The film tells about a real event in 1962. At that time boxer Olli Mäki got to box for the chance of becoming world champion. Mäki failed and lost, but it was the happiest day of Mäki's life. The reason was love.

"Hymyilevä mies" won 8 Jussi awards in total. The awards were given, for example, to the film's director Juho Kuosmanen and to actor Jarkko Lahti, who plays Olli Mäki in the film.

## Summer time

![The picture shows the change to summer time with clock hands. At three in the early hours of the night the clocks are moved forward by an hour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7891779)

Open image viewer

Sunday morning comes quickly, when the hour between 3 and 4 o'clock is skipped. Photo: Anssi Leppänen / Yle

Summer time begins on Sunday.

During the night between Saturday and Sunday, the clocks are moved forward by an hour. The hour between 3 o'clock and 4 o'clock is skipped. Because of this, night trains and night buses are an hour late on Sunday morning.

The change back to normal time, that is winter time, takes place on the last Sunday of October.

## Winter weather

![Cloudy weather](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9529734)

Open image viewer

Winter is gradually leaving. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Sunday is a warm and windy day. In the south the temperature may rise to about 10 degrees, but because of the strong wind the air does not feel warm.

Yle's meteorologist Kerttu Kotakorpi says that next week the temperature will fall again and the weather will still be quite wintry.
