# Tuesday 6.8.2019 (radio)

Veikkaus is taking a break from advertising. India has removed important rights and freedoms from the Kashmir region. China warns Hong Kong demonstrators. Most tax refunds have been paid into bank accounts. The grain harvest will be good across Finland.

## Veikkaus

![Veikkaus advertisement at the Kaari shopping centre in Kannelmäki, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10910489)

Veikkaus advertisement at the Kaari shopping centre in Kannelmäki, Helsinki. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Veikkaus is taking a break from advertising gambling. Until the end of September Veikkaus will advertise only the big jackpots of Lotto, Eurojackpot and Viking Lotto.

Veikkaus's advertisements have been criticised a lot, for example on social media. The critics have said that aggressive marketing of gambling is not a good thing. Veikkaus has an exclusive right, that is, a monopoly, to organise gambling in mainland Finland.

Veikkaus's managing director Olli Sarekoski admits that, for example, the advertising of Toto, that is, trotting betting, has not been good.

## Kashmir

![Pakistani protesters burn a doll representing India's Prime Minister Narendra Modi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10909375)

Open image viewer

In the Pakistani part of Kashmir, the measures of the Indian government are causing anger. Photo: Amiruddin Mughal / EPA

Relations between India and Pakistan are worse than ever.

India and Pakistan have quarrelled over the Kashmir region for decades. Kashmir is a mountainous area, one side of which belongs to India and the other side to Pakistan.

Now India has removed important rights and freedoms from Kashmir. Pakistan has condemned India's decision.

India and Pakistan both have nuclear weapons.

## Hong Kong

![Demonstrators in the streets of Hong Kong on 5 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10910252)

Open image viewer

Demonstrators protected themselves from tear gas in Hong Kong on 5 August. Photo: Miguel Candela / EPA

China criticises the Hong Kong demonstrators harshly. A Chinese representative says that the demonstrators should not think that China is weak. China warned that violent criminals will be brought to court.

The demonstrations in Hong Kong have continued for a long time. Hong Kong is part of China, but it has its own constitution. The demonstrators defend Hong Kong's right to decide many things itself.

The police have used tear gas and rubber bullets against the demonstrators.

## Grain harvest

![Rye field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10910041)

Open image viewer

Rye is already being threshed in Southern Finland. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The grain harvest will be quite good across Finland. In Southern Finland the harvest may be up to 30 per cent better than usual.

In Southern Finland the rye has ripened and is already being threshed. In Central Finland and Northern Finland, threshing, that is, gathering the harvest from the fields, will perhaps begin next week.

## Tax refunds

![Fifty-euro banknotes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_94/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4582135a62220ef3083/1581840559)

The timetable for tax refunds has been brought forward compared with before. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

A large part of the tax refunds have been paid today, Tuesday.

Tax refunds are now paid earlier than before, and the payment day is not the same for everyone. About 80 per cent of employees or pensioners will receive their tax refund this month or next month.

People who paid too much tax last year will receive tax refunds.
