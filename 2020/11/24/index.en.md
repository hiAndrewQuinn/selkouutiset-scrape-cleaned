## The serious coronavirus situation in the capital region

![A sign at the swimming hall in Vaasa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6309185e1dbca39030d/1665671229)

The swimming halls in the capital region will probably be closed. Photo: Antti Haavisto / Yle

The coronavirus situation is now serious in Helsinki and the capital region.

The cities of the capital region will decide on new coronavirus restrictions on Thursday.

They may be strict.

All public events will probably be banned.

All public places, for example libraries, swimming halls and sports halls, will probably be closed. Upper secondary schools and vocational schools will move to distance teaching.

More details about the restrictions will be given on Thursday.

Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa will decide on the restrictions together.

## Everyone must follow the coronavirus guidelines

![A safe-distance sign at a Citymarket store during the coronavirus pandemic in 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6944215ef9d132d9e69/1593431290)

Now everyone must remember safe distances and masks. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

Health authorities now urge all residents of the capital region to follow the coronavirus guidelines.

Everyone must avoid meetings and must keep their distance from other people.

Everyone must also wear a mask.

The coronavirus is spreading quickly in the capital region and in Uusimaa.

There are many infections, for example, in care homes for older people and in accommodation for homeless people.

## Young people may have come through the coronavirus time well

![The picture shows people's shadows drawn on the ground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11568280)

Open image viewer

A survey showed that young people may have come through the coronavirus time quite well. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Young people may have come through the coronavirus time quite well. Young people were asked how the ending of hobbies has affected them.

40 per cent answered that it has affected them negatively. 45 per cent answered that it has not really affected them in any way.

Young people have liked spending more time with their family.

According to young people, the hardest thing has been not knowing how long the coronavirus restrictions will last.

## Sanna Marin on the list of influential women

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin (SDP) answers journalists' questions on her way to the government negotiations at the House of Estates in Helsinki on 3 November 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11629002)

Open image viewer

BBC has put Sanna Marin on the list of influential women. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The British broadcaster BBC has put Finland's Prime Minister Sanna Marin on its list of influential women.

The BBC makes a list of 100 women who are inspiring and influential.

Sanna Marin is a new woman on the list. She is praised, for example, because she has managed the handling of the coronavirus crisis well in Finland.

There are many women from different fields on the list.

New women on the list also include, for example, the Belarusian opposition leader Sviatlana Tsikhanouskaya and musician Angélique Kidjo from Benin.
