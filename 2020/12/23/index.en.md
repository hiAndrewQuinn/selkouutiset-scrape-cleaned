# Wednesday 23.12.2020 (radio)

Coronavirus vaccinations. Christmas at cemeteries. The Hursti Christmas aid. Proclamation of Christmas peace. Christmas weather.

## Coronavirus vaccinations

![Close-up of the Pfizer-Biontech coronavirus vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_287/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7572125fe31f7eaed3d/1608720313)

Open image viewer

Coronavirus vaccine. Photo: ullstein bild - Sven Simon / All Over Press

Doctors are encouraging Finns to take the coronavirus vaccine. The Finnish Medical Association, Lääkäriliitto, says that it is good for Finns to get the coronavirus vaccination.

It also helps with the treatment of other illnesses. At the moment many other illnesses cannot be examined and treated in hospitals in time, because there are many coronavirus patients.

If Finns take the coronavirus vaccine, time is freed up in hospitals to treat other illnesses.

## Cemeteries at Christmas

![Candles and lanterns at the Kalevankangas cemetery in Tampere at Christmas 2001.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1937,h_1090,x_0,y_233/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4519325a3a618ab34f5/1513775674)

Open image viewer

A candle is lit at the cemetery in memory of a loved one. Photo: Arja Lento / Yle

More visitors than usual are expected at cemeteries this Christmas. Because of the coronavirus situation, many Finns are spending Christmas at home.

People are not travelling abroad or to relatives at Christmas. Christmas churches and Christmas devotions are not being held. A visit to the cemetery is an important part of the Christmas atmosphere for many Finns.

Crowds may come especially to the big cemeteries.

## The Hursti Christmas aid

![Hursti Aid handed out Christmas food bags at Helsinki's Rautatientori.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11713437)

Open image viewer

Heikki and Sini Hursti handed out Christmas food bags at Helsinki's Rautatientori. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Coronavirus restrictions have prevented the traditional Hursti Christmas celebration this year. The Christmas celebration has been held for poor and lonely people in Helsinki for over 50 years.

The celebration could not be held this year because of the coronavirus situation.

However, **Heikki** and **Sini Hursti** handed out 2,000 food bags today, Wednesday, to people on low incomes at Helsinki Central Station.

## Proclamation of Christmas peace

![The Brinkkala house in Turku's Old Market Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3278,h_1844,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7569355fe1f357d0b23/1608643485)

Open image viewer

The Brinkkala house in Turku. Photo: Ari Welling / Yle

The Christmas peace will be proclaimed tomorrow, Thursday, without an audience. The Christmas peace will be proclaimed at 12 noon from the balcony of the Brinkkala house in Turku.

Because of the coronavirus situation, people cannot come to watch the event in Turku. The area is fenced off.

Yle will broadcast the proclamation of Christmas peace live. The broadcast starts on Thursday on TV1 and Radio Suomi at 11.55.

## Weather

![Weather map for Thursday 24.12.2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11714378)

Open image viewer

Almost the whole of Finland has snow at Christmas. Photo: Yle

The Christmas weather is wintry in almost the whole of Finland. At night it snows in many places.

On Christmas Eve, Thursday, it also snows in the central and northern parts of the country. In southern Finland there may be some showers of water or sleet. On the coast the temperature is a little above zero. Elsewhere there is frost.

After Thursday the weather gets colder across the whole country.
