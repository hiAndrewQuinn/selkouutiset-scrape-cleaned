Salman Rushdie recovers. The student meal gets more expensive. The athletics European Championships begin. Clear News returns to television. The weather warms up.

## Salman Rushdie recovers

![The author Salman Rushdie speaks at a lecture event.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-848835947b45eb466a/1660324622)

The author Salman Rushdie. Photo: EPA/Helmut Fohringer.

New information has been received about the condition of the author Salman Rushdie. Rushdie is already doing better.

Rushdie is no longer on a ventilator, and he is already able to speak. Rushdie is still in hospital in the USA.

Rushdie was taken to hospital on Friday, when a man stabbed him at a literary event in New York. The police arrested the man.

The man is 24 years old. He supports extremist Shia Muslim movements.

Many Muslims were angry at Rushdie, when he wrote the book The Satanic Verses. The book came out over 30 years ago.

In the view of many Muslims the book mocked Islam. The religious leader of Iran ordered Muslims to kill Rushdie.

## The student meal gets more expensive

![At the school lunch of the Kulosaari comprehensive school, pork and beef stew with paprika and wholegrain wheat was served on 19 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-77790760361604098bb/1616385050)

Open image viewer

Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The price of student meals rises at universities. The price of a student meal rises by 50 cents in October.

The reason is inflation, that is, the rise in prices. The prices of food products have also risen a lot, and that is why preparing meals is more expensive than before.

The student restaurant of the University of Helsinki says that a 50 cent rise in the price of a student meal is not enough. In the student restaurant's view a 1 euro rise is needed.

## The athletics European Championships begin

![Oliver Helander throwing the javelin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2465,h_1386,x_0,y_199/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98594462dd13dc80ad3/1658655780)

Open image viewer

Javelin thrower Oliver Helander cannot take part in the European Championships because of an injury. Photo: Getty Images

The athletics European Championships, that is, the European championship competitions, begin on Monday. The competitions are held in Munich, Germany.

A lot of Finnish athletes are taking part in the European Championships. About 70 athletes from Finland take part in the competitions. Finns are waiting for a medal, for example in the men's javelin throw.

On Sunday, however, bad news came. Javelin thrower Oliver Helander said that he cannot take part in the European Championships because of an injury. Helander is one of Finland's best javelin throwers.

The athletics European Championships continue for the whole week. The last competition day is Sunday 21 August.

The weather is warm early in the week. In many places there is even a heatwave, that is, the temperature rises over 25 degrees.

Tuesday is the warmest day of the week. In the south the temperature may rise close to 30 degrees.

Warm and even hot weather may continue until Friday.
