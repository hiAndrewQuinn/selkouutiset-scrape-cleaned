# Thursday 23.03.2017 (radio)

There has been a terror attack in London. The Council for Mass Media has investigated a case in which the Prime Minister lost his temper with Yle. There are queues at the service points of the Finnish Immigration Service. Chinese President Xi Jinping is coming to visit Finland. A baby is due in the Swedish royal family in the autumn.

## The London terror attack

People have left flowers at the site of the terror attack. Photo: Pasi Myöhänen

A terror attack has taken place in London, the capital of Britain. Four people died in the attack. There are many injured. The attack happened on Wednesday in central London.

The perpetrator of the terror attack was a British man. He drove a car into people and then tried to get into the Houses of Parliament. The man killed a police officer who tried to stop him. After that, other police officers shot the man.

The police are investigating whether the man had accomplices. The terrorist group Isis says that it carried out the attack. There is no certain information about this yet.

## The Prime Minister and Yle

![Atte Jääskeläinen and Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9526706)

Open image viewer

Yle News director Atte Jääskeläinen and Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. Photo: Yle / AOP

In Finland people are talking about the relationship between Prime Minister Juha Sipilä and Yle.

The Council for Mass Media has investigated a case in which the Prime Minister lost his temper with Yle. The council's job is to defend journalists' right to write freely.

Prime Minister Sipilä did not like a news story that told about a company owned by the Prime Minister's relatives. Sipilä sent a great many messages to Yle about the matter. In the opinion of the Council for Mass Media, Sipilä tried to influence Yle's news. That is wrong, and that is why the council gave the Prime Minister a reprimand.

The Council for Mass Media says that Yle also acted wrongly. Yle corrected the news story when the Prime Minister lost his temper. In the council's opinion, there was no reason to correct the news story.

The freedom of newspapers, radio and television is very important in Finland. Politicians must not decide what is reported in the news.

## Queues at the Immigration Service

![People queuing for the Finnish Immigration Service](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9525250)

Open image viewer

People are queuing at the Finnish Immigration Service's Helsinki service point. Photo: Yle

There are queues at the service points of the Immigration Service.

In Helsinki, for example, people come to queue for a queue number early in the morning. The customer needs a queue number to be able to get in to the service point.

The Immigration Service has more work than before, because it now handles all matters concerning residence permits for foreigners. Previously the police also handled these matters.

The Immigration Service says that customers should book a time on the internet.
