# Tuesday 5.4.2022 (radio)

Government budget talks. NATO survey of MPs. Teachers' strike. Bicycle thefts. Weather.

## Government negotiations

![Anna-Maja Henriksson, Emma Kari, Sanna Marin, Annika Saarikko and Li Andersson spoke to the media on 5 April before the negotiations.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5380,h_3026,x_0,y_560/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-937935624beefc77ff5/1649143745)

Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The government has held negotiations on state finances. The government planned how the state will use money in the years 2023–2026.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the war in Ukraine is increasing state spending. The government will use more money for Finland's defence and security.

In addition, Finland may use 780 million euros next year for the costs of Ukrainian war refugees.

The government also agreed on savings. For example, the building of new roads will decrease. In addition, Finland will give less aid to developing countries than was planned earlier.

## NATO survey of MPs

![Finnish soldiers running in an exercise.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_526/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-932300623b3b05a9d22/1648048989)

Open image viewer

Finland has taken part in many NATO exercises, but Finland is not a NATO member. Photo: Jonathan Nackstrand / AFP

Many MPs are already ready to give their own opinion on the military alliance NATO.

Because of the war in Ukraine, Finland is now talking about whether Finland should join NATO.

When Yle asked MPs about this, 112 MPs answered the question. There are 200 MPs in total.

71 MPs were of the opinion that Finland must join NATO. 6 MPs opposed it.

Although Finland is not currently a NATO member, Finland cooperates a lot with NATO.

Schools are closed on Wednesday and Thursday in 4 cities. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

Schools are closed this week in some cities, if the teachers' strike begins.

Schools are closed for 2 days in Turku, Jyväskylä, Oulu and Rovaniemi.

Schools will be closed on Wednesday and Thursday, because the teachers are then on strike, that is, they are not working.

Next week the strike is in Kuopio and Tampere, if agreement on teachers' pay cannot be reached before then.

## Bicycle thefts

![Bicycles in the storage of a housing company.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7892059)

Open image viewer

Thieves often visit bicycle storage rooms during the quiet winter time. Photo: Yle

A lot of bicycles are stolen in Finland.

Bicycle thefts are often noticed in the spring like this. Thieves visit the bicycle storage rooms of buildings during the quiet winter time.

Expensive electric bicycles in particular interest thieves. Electric bicycles are now very popular.

Because of thieves, many housing companies have bought new kinds of locks and cameras for their bicycle storage rooms.

Chief Superintendent Pälvi Suokas says that it is always a good idea to file a police report about a bicycle theft. It is easier for the owner to get the bicycle back if they have exact details and identifying marks of the bicycle.

## The weather

![Weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12391909)

Open image viewer

A low-pressure system still affects the weather.

On Wednesday it will snow in the central and northern parts of the country. In the south the weather is dry and the sun appears in places. The temperature is a little either side of zero.

On Thursday new rain will come to the south. The rain is at least partly snow.
