## Finland's competitiveness

![Olli Rehn.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1049732639d7b7baa978/1671265826)

Governor of the Bank of Finland Olli Rehn. Photo: Yle

An expert says that new pay rises do not endanger the competitiveness of Finnish work. This is what the Governor of the Bank of Finland, Olli Rehn, says, whom Yle interviewed.

Many new collective agreements are valid for 2 years. Rehn says that a 2-year agreement brings a balance between people's purchasing power and the competitiveness of Finnish work. Rehn says that it is important for Finland to take care of the competitiveness of Finnish work and of companies.

The Governor of the Bank of Finland, Olli Rehn, also estimates that the European Central Bank will raise interest rates again at least in March.

## Estonian parliamentary elections

![The building of the Estonian parliament, Riigikogu, on Toompea hill in Tallinn.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2048,h_1152,x_0,y_368/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7583795fec659ce782d/1609328122)

Open image viewer

The Estonian parliament building, Riigikogu. Photo: Rain Kooli / Yle

Parliamentary elections were held in Estonia on Sunday. The polling stations closed at 20:00. Almost 50 percent of Estonians voted in advance.

Before the election, research firms asked Estonians which party they intended to vote for. Pre-election surveys show that the reform party of Prime Minister Kaja Kallas may be the election winner. It may get about 30 percent of the votes.

The first results of the Estonian parliamentary elections will come on Monday.

## Protecting the seas

![A shoal of goldfish in the Red Sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4528305a3e702f3333e/1514041508)

The UN negotiated for 15 years on the protection of the oceans. Photo: Mohammed Al Momany/ Robesus Inc.

The UN has reached agreement on the protection of the oceans.

30 percent of open sea areas will be turned into protected areas by 2030. The aim is to protect the sensitive ecosystem of the seas.

The UN member states have negotiated for 15 years on the protection of the open seas. That is why it is a big thing that an agreement on protection has now been reached.

## Attention deficit disorder ADHD

![Sign with ADHD.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2952,h_1660,x_0,y_950/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-43129059ca55270f031/1506432361)

Open image viewer

The symptoms of ADHD are recognised better than before. Photo: Most Photos/DOC_PhotoStock

Diagnoses of ADHD, that is, attention deficit disorder, and drug treatment for ADHD have become common quickly in Finland.

Kela says that 10 years ago about 12,000 Finns received medicine reimbursements for ADHD medicines. Last year more than 80,000 Finns received medicine reimbursements for ADHD medicines.

ADHD symptoms have not increased, but the symptoms of ADHD are recognised better than before.

Aircraft engines are developing. Test flights with hydrogen engines have been made in Europe and the USA this year.

The tests showed that hydrogen is suitable as fuel for aircraft. The aim is that the first passenger flights with hydrogen aircraft will be made in Europe in about 2 years.

The Finnish airline Finnair is also taking part in developing new hydrogen aircraft.
