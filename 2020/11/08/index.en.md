# Sunday 8.11.2020 (radio)

Joe Biden is the next president of the USA. Joe Biden wants to unite the people. There are more than 400 new coronavirus infections in Finland. Today, Sunday, is Father's Day.

## Joe Biden is the next president of the USA

![Joe and Jill Biden greet the crowd after the victory speech.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4896,h_2754,x_0,y_168/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7396005fa757d4d213a/1604802628)

The new president of the USA is Joe Biden. Biden's election victory was confirmed on Saturday.

Joe Biden is a 77-year-old Democrat. He is the 46th president of the USA.

Donald Trump still disputes Biden's victory. Trump claims that there was fraud in the election, i.e. the election was not honest.

## Kamala Harris is the first woman to be US vice president

![Kamala Harris cheering.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11636616)

Open image viewer

Kamala Harris. Photo: AOP

Joe Biden's vice president is Kamala Harris.

55-year-old Harris has previously been, for example, a prosecutor and a senator. Kamala Harris is the first woman to become vice president of the USA. She is also the first African American vice president.

In her speech Harris said that the choice is an example for girls all over the world. She also said that democracy must be defended every day.

## Biden receives congratulations

![President Sauli Niinistö speaks at a media meeting on the result of the US presidential election at the Presidential Palace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11636908)

President Sauli Niinistö speaks at a media meeting on the result of the US presidential election at the Presidential Palace. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Leaders of many countries have congratulated Joe Biden on his election victory.

President Sauli Niinistö and Prime Minister Sanna Marin have also congratulated Biden. Niinistö said that Biden's victory will not change the relationship between Finland and the USA much.

The British Prime Minister Boris Johnson has also sent Biden a congratulatory message, for example. Johnson said that the USA is Britain's most important ally.

There are 412 new coronavirus infections in Finland.

The figure includes the infections of Saturday and Sunday. The figures were not reported on Saturday because of a technical fault.

In total, Finland now has almost 17,800 coronavirus infections.

## Father's Day

![The Finnish flag on a pole.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11060883)

Open image viewer

Father's Day is a flag day. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

Today, Sunday, is Father's Day.

Father's Day is an official flag day. On Father's Day, fathers are remembered, for example, with a card, and fathers and children spend time together.

There are almost 1.3 million fathers in Finland and over 530,000 grandfathers.
