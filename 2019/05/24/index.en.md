# Friday 24.5.2019 (radio)

A Yle journalist met Finnish women at an Isis camp in Syria. Finland does not intend to help Isis women return to Finland. Britain's Prime Minister Theresa May is resigning. Young people have protested against climate change. Kela is starting a phone service in Somali. Finland's ice hockey team has made it into the World Championship medal games.

## There are Finns at the Isis camp

![Finnish women from an ISIS camp in Syria.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10799396)

Open image viewer

Finnish women at an Isis camp in Syria. Photo: Yle

There are also Finns in the Isis camp in Syria. A Yle journalist met Finnish women at the Al-Hol camp.

The women said that there are 11 Finnish women and 33 Finnish children in the camp. The women also said that they want to return to Finland.

Al-Hol camp has more than 70,000 people in total. Many are family members of Isis terrorists. The families were taken to the camp when the Isis terrorists lost the battles.

## Finland will not help the Isis women

![Interior Minister Kai Mykkänen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10719267)

Open image viewer

Finland's Interior Minister Kai Mykkänen. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Finland's Interior Minister Kai Mykkänen says that Finland will not help Isis women return to Finland.

In Mykkänen's view, it must be investigated whether the women have taken part in terrorism. Mykkänen hopes that the women will be sentenced in international courts.

However, Finland is studying how it can help the Finnish children in the Isis camp. The children may be brought to Finland, if that also suits the children's mothers.

## Britain's May resigns

![Theresa May](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10800159)

Open image viewer

Britain's Prime Minister Theresa May. Photo: Tolga Akmen / AFP

Britain's Prime Minister Theresa May is resigning. May will stop being prime minister this summer.

May has failed in handling Brexit. Parliament has not approved May's plan for how Britain will leave the European Union.

The British voted for an EU exit, that is Brexit, already 3 years ago.

Young people have protested about climate change in many countries. Young people want decision-makers to do more work against climate change.

There were young people from over 100 countries taking part. In Finland, young people took climate messages to politicians who are negotiating a new government.

## Finland made it to the medal games

![Harri Pesonen making a mask](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10799519)

Open image viewer

There was speed in the match between Finland and Sweden. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The Finnish ice hockey team, the Lions, has won an important victory. The Lions beat Sweden in the ice hockey World Championships on Thursday.

The victory means that Finland gets into the medal games. The medal games include the 4 best countries.

Finland plays Russia on Saturday. If Finland wins, the Lions will reach the final match.

On Sunday it will be decided which country is the ice hockey world champion this year.
