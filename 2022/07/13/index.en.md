The situation at hospitals is difficult in summer also for other reasons. For example, there are too few summer substitutes in hospitals.

## Equal Finland

![Female manager in an office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5878120)

Open image viewer

In Finland women have good opportunities, for example in working life. Photo: Yle

Finland is the world's second most equal country. Only in Iceland are women and men more equal than in Finland.

This is what the World Economic Forum's report says. Almost 150 countries are included in the report.

The report assesses equality between women and men, for example in education and the economy. The report says that the corona pandemic has slowed the improvement of equality in the world.

The most equal countries in the world are in the North. The 3 most equal countries are Iceland, Finland and Norway. Sweden is in 5th place.

## Skin cancer

![Sunbathing and especially burning the skin predispose to melanoma.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6556606)

Open image viewer

Finns' skin tolerates the sun poorly. Photo: Pekka Sipilä / Yle

In Finland and other Nordic countries there is a lot of melanoma, that is, skin cancer. Every year almost 2000 Finns are found to have melanoma.

Many Finns have fair skin, which tolerates the sun's radiation poorly. A tanned skin, however, is considered beautiful by many Finns.

A survey by Finnish cancer organisations says that over 60 percent of Finns think that tanned skin looks good. That is why many stay too long in the sun. The risk of melanoma increases especially if the skin burns in the sun.

The good news is that melanoma treatment is good nowadays. Over 90 percent of patients are still alive 5 years after melanoma was found in them.
