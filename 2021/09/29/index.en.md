# Wednesday 29.9.2021 (radio)

Backlog in cancer treatment. Libraries in crisis. Prices of winter tyres and flats. Corona restrictions in Sweden. Weather.

## Cancer

![Entrance of the cancer centre at Päijät-Häme Central Hospital in Lahti](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4029,h_2266,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7719326022721321d72/1612870295)

Many people avoided going to the doctor for fear of corona infection. That is why cancers have gone undiagnosed. Photo: Petri Niemi/Yle

The backlog in cancer treatment has been cleared this autumn. The backlog arose in the corona pandemic, when people avoided all unnecessary travel.

In many hospital districts, more new cancer cases than normal have now been diagnosed.

Preliminary figures show that last year 1,200 fewer new cancers were diagnosed than the year before. Last year was the first corona year.

The decrease shows that fewer diagnoses were made during the pandemic than usual.

## Libraries in crisis

![A woman fills a bookshelf in a library.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1488,h_837,x_0,y_88/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-856153614878d592643/1632139637)

Open image viewer

Finnish libraries were important to people during the corona era. Photo: Hämeenlinna library

Libraries are a good help in crises.

Last year libraries were important to people and to society as a whole, because they helped the ability to withstand crises. This comes out in a report by the regional state administrative agencies.

The libraries' online services in particular were important during the corona era.

The report says that libraries and municipalities must develop their preparedness for crises in the future too.

## Winter tyres

![Car tyres being changed at a car service station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11625249)

Open image viewer

Winter tyres must now be paid more than last winter season. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Winter tyres cost more than usual this autumn.

Tyre companies say that prices may be 5–10 per cent higher than last autumn.

There is too little freight traffic in the world. That causes delays in goods transport and price rises. The prices of raw materials, such as rubber and steel, are also high.

## Flat prices

![Flat viewing in Katajanokka in Helsinki on 17 March 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11603188)

Open image viewer

Flat sales are now brisk and flat prices have risen. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Flat prices have risen a lot over the year in the big cities.

Statistics Finland says that in Turku old owner-occupied flats became up to 8.5 per cent more expensive. In Helsinki flat prices rose by 8 per cent, and in Tampere by just over 7 per cent.

Across the whole country, old owner-occupied flats were on average 4.4 per cent more expensive in August than a year earlier.

## Corona restrictions in Sweden

![Stockholm's Sergels torg, with few people on a summer evening](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_155/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83325060f5346f7e7f9/1626682543)

Open image viewer

Sergels torg in Stockholm was also quieter than normal during the corona restrictions. Photo: Daniel Ivarsson

In Sweden all corona restrictions were removed on Wednesday.

In restaurants there are no longer, for example, opening hour restrictions. Tables may again seat a normal number of people.

Nor is the number of participants at gatherings any longer restricted.

In addition, the recommendation on remote work was removed. Only people's personal restrictions remain, that is, hand washing and staying at home if they have symptoms.
