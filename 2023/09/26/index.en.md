## Protests by workers' organisations

![Workers march out of their workplace and stand outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3888,h_2187,x_0,y_716/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1177075651297b2e1b74/1695717546)

Protests by workers' organisations against the government have begun. Workers have, for example, marched out of workplaces.

Protests will be held for 3 weeks in different parts of Finland. Today, Tuesday, there have been protests in Northern Finland, and tomorrow, Wednesday, they will be in Western Finland.

The reason for the protests is that the workers' organisations do not accept the government's plans. The government intends, for example, to make it easier to dismiss employees and to weaken earnings-related unemployment benefit.

## Turkey and Sweden's NATO membership

![Turkish President Erdogan speaks at the UN General Assembly behind the speaker's podium. He has raised his hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3514,h_1976,x_0,y_42/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1174333650a3f070df0c/1703696801)

Open image viewer

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. Photo: EPA-EFE

Turkey will accept Sweden's NATO application if the sale of American military aircraft to Turkey proceeds. This is what Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan says.

Turkey wants to buy F-16 aircraft from the USA. The USA has not yet agreed to sell them to Turkey. Therefore Turkey hopes that the deal will succeed with the help of Sweden's NATO membership.

Sweden applied for membership of the NATO defence alliance last year at the same time as Finland. In the spring of this year, Turkey approved only Finland's NATO membership.

However, all NATO countries must approve new members of the defence alliance. That is why Sweden cannot become a NATO member before Turkey approves it.

## Construction of homes

![A builder drilling a wall while wearing a safety harness on a balcony without a railing at a construction site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5765,h_3243,x_0,y_144/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117271865044bbf6e606/1694971653)

Open image viewer

There are fewer new construction sites than before. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

The building of homes is decreasing. The construction industry estimates that construction of new homes will start this year at half the level of last year.

Last year the building of 37 000 homes was started, and this year only 16 000 homes will be started.

Especially apartment-block flats are being built little this year. It is not worth building homes, because there are few buyers. Many do not want to take a home loan, because loan interest rates have risen.

## Lightning

![Lightning strikes in the sky among the treetops.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1909,h_1077,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115285364d05efa62b9a/1691380980)

Little lightning was seen in the summer this year.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that about 73 000 lightning strikes were observed in Finland in the summer. Usually about 130 000 lightning strikes are observed in summer.

There were thunderstorms this summer too, but they produced little lightning.

## The weather

The weather continues warm.

Wednesday is dry in almost the whole country, meaning it does not rain. In the evening, however, rain spreads to Finland from the west.

The temperature on Wednesday is 13–18 degrees. After Wednesday too, temperatures are higher than usual for the end of September.

The weather only cools on Sunday, when October begins.
