## Trust in the police

![Senior constable Joonas Baarman watches as a police car drives to an alarm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6993575f0ebfd3cdc85/1594802226)

The police get good ratings from Finns. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

Finns trust the police.

A new study says that over 90 per cent of Finns trust the police. In Finland the police are trusted more than in many other countries.

Trust in the police has, however, fallen a little. Now 91 per cent of Finns trust the police, but just 2 years ago 95 per cent of Finns trusted the police.

Finns also think that the police treat people fairly.

Finns do, however, assess critically the police's attitude towards minorities. Only 53 per cent of Finns think that the police treat customers with a foreign background in the same way as other Finns.

## A corona vaccine is being developed

![Patient being given a measles vaccination.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10730858)

Open image viewer

Many hope that soon there will also be a vaccine against corona. Photo: Lukas Schulze / AOP

Efforts are being made to develop a vaccine against the coronavirus. If the vaccine works well, a person will not get sick with corona.

The first corona vaccine has already advanced to the final test. The test involves the corona vaccine of an American pharmaceutical company.

The test involves 30,000 people. The test starts in a week.

This vaccine was tested for the first time in the spring. Then 45 people received the vaccine. 50 per cent of them had small side effects from the vaccine. Now we are waiting to see how well and safely the American company's vaccine works in the final test.

Many other pharmaceutical companies are also developing a corona vaccine. Over 20 corona vaccines are already being tested around the world. However, the other corona vaccines have not yet advanced to the final test.

Experts cannot yet say when the first corona vaccine will be ready.

## Slot machines open

![Close-up of a gambling machine, with a woman's hand on the button.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10930570)

Open image viewer

Slot machines are, for example, in shops. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Slot machines are open again. Slot machines were closed because of the corona crisis.

Many safety measures are still followed. Only some of the slot machines are switched on, and Perspex screens have been built between the slot machines. This ensures safe distances.

Slot machines also open later than before the corona crisis. Slot machines now open only at 11 am.

## The weather warms up

The weather warms up at the weekend.

On Friday the temperature is already at least 20 degrees in almost the whole of Finland.

On Saturday and Sunday the temperature is over 20 degrees. In Southern Finland the temperature may be as high as 25 degrees.
