# Wednesday 13.4.2022 (radio)

The government released its security report. Finland's NATO decision may come within weeks. The food basket is still cheaper in Finland than in Sweden and Norway. The tobacco law is being tightened. Weather forecast.

## The government's security report

![Interior Minister Krista Mikkonen, Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto and Defence Minister Antti Kaikkonen presented the government's current security report in Helsinki on 13 April 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5342,h_3004,x_0,y_490/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9419466256ba79e4e67/1649851112)

Interior Minister Krista Mikkonen, Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto and Defence Minister Antti Kaikkonen presented the government's security report. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The government has released a report on the change in Finland's security environment.

The report describes how Russia's attack war in Ukraine is changing Finland's security situation. The report also describes different options that Finland has in this new situation.

The report paves the way for a decision on whether Finland will apply to become a member of the military alliance NATO.

Parliament will begin discussion on Finland's security situation and the report after Easter.

## Finland's NATO decision

![Sauli Niinistö spoke to the media at the Presidential Palace on Thursday 10 March 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_142/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9264046229e5e76bb0a/1647765032)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

President Sauli Niinistö hopes that Parliament will deal with the security report thoroughly, but quickly.

The report does not go into more detail on the disadvantages of possible NATO membership and Russia's reactions. However, Niinistö says that Parliament will go through the matter thoroughly when it hears various experts.

Niinistö believes that Finland's NATO decision will be ready well before NATO's summit meeting in Madrid in June.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin also says that the decision on applying for NATO membership will be made within weeks.

## Food prices

![Shopping on a supermarket conveyor belt.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5917125)

Open image viewer

Shopping at the supermarket checkout. Photo: Yle

The price of food has stayed the same in Finland over the past month. Russia's war in Ukraine is not yet visible in food prices.

Yle has compared the price of food in Northern Finland, Northern Sweden and Northern Norway. The same 14 products were collected into the food basket in each country.

The food basket is cheapest in Finland and most expensive in Norway. In Tornio in Finland the food basket cost about 39 euros. On the Swedish side, in Haparanda, the basket cost about 6 euros more, that is, about 45 euros. In Alta in Norway the food basket price came to 60 euros.

## Tobacco law tightened

![Packed tobacco and a law clause.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9088682)

Open image viewer

The tobacco law is being tightened from the start of May. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka, photo AOP

The tobacco law is being tightened.

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health says that the tightening aims to reduce the appeal of tobacco products, especially in the eyes of young people.

For example, logos will disappear from tobacco product packaging. The new law also bans smoking on playgrounds and public beaches.

The amendments to the Tobacco Act will enter into force at the beginning of May. However, the requirement for uniform packaging will only enter into force in May next year.
