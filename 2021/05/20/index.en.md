# Thursday 20.5.2021 (radio)

Finns feel that life is safe. The corona epidemic is not over yet. Before the municipal elections, Yle is organising the Clear Debate. It will rain on Friday.

## Safe Finland

Finns feel that life is safe.

Almost all Finns think their own life is safe. In addition, 90 percent of Finns think that Finland is a safe country.

The information is from a report that the government published on Thursday.

The report also says that the corona crisis has affected Finns' sense of safety. A big worry is how children and young people will get through the corona crisis.

## Finland's corona situation

![Ouluhalli corona vaccination point, with people and staff.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11914494)

Open image viewer

Already 40 percent of Finns have received a corona vaccination. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

The corona epidemic is not over yet in Finland. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare said on Thursday that the decrease in coronavirus infections has stopped.

On Thursday, 259 new coronavirus infections were reported. No new corona deaths were reported.

Corona vaccinations are progressing well. About 40 percent of Finns have received the first corona vaccination.

By the end of July, a corona vaccination will have been offered to all adults who want it.

## Clear Debate and panel

Before the municipal elections, Yle is organising a clear-language election debate, the Clear Debate. The Clear Debate also includes a panel that assesses politicians' speech.

Politicians from different parties take part in the Clear Debate. Ministers and MPs are included. They try to talk about politics in easy Finnish. The task of the panel is to assess how well they succeed. The panel includes researchers, teachers and students of Finnish.

The Clear Debate will be shown on TV1 on Friday 4 June. Later, the Clear Debate can be watched on Areena.

## Friday's weather

Still the weather forecast.

On Friday there will be rain in most of the country. In the east there will also be thunder.

Colder air is flowing in from the north, and in Lapland some of the rain is sleet. In the east the temperature may still rise to 20 degrees in places.

At the weekend the rain moves north. On Sunday the weather is already quite dry in a large part of the country.
