# Tuesday 1.5.2018 (radio)

May Day has been celebrated in cold weather, and the parties have given May Day speeches. Fake medicines will be stopped from getting into pharmacies. The United States is postponing import duties on steel and aluminium.

## May Day went as usual, but in cold weather

May Day celebrations in Finland have mostly gone happily, without any serious incidents. The police have looked into various scuffles and disruptive behaviour.

In addition to drunk drivers, there have been many drug drivers on the roads during May Day.

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio received the traditional May Day music greetings at the Presidential Palace. The event was indoors because of the rain.

## May Day is a time for political speeches

![The workers' May Day parade in Helsinki on May 1, 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10185977)

On May Day, politicians have once again given speeches.

The governing parties and the opposition parties have disagreed in the traditional May Day speeches. The opposition said that the government is the least competent in Finnish history and that its policy is hostile towards women.

Representatives of the government thanked the government for its economic policy and said that in 3 years 90,000 Finns have found work.

## Fake medicines will be stopped in pharmacies

![Medicines seized by customs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10161144)

Open image viewer

It is rare for fake medicines to end up in a pharmacy in Finland. Customs often finds fakes. Photo: Customs

Fake medicines will begin to be stopped from getting into pharmacies.

Each medicine package will get its own identifier. The code of the identifier will be scanned, that is, read electronically, before the medicine is given to the customer. The system checks that the medicine is genuine and that it is in the national database.

The system will be in force across Europe by February next year at the latest. It is not yet known whether the change will raise the prices of medicines.

In Finland fakes have been found in a pharmacy only 2 times. Most fakes are sold online and on the street.

## The United States postponed import duties by a month

![Donald Trump](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10152872)

Open image viewer

Donald Trump. Photo: Jim Lo Scalzo / EPA

United States President Donald Trump is postponing import duties on steel and aluminium. The European Union, Canada and Mexico get one more month to negotiate about the duties with the United States. The duties were supposed to come into force on May Day.

The EU has received a temporary exemption from the duties. The duties make it harder for the steel industry to export to the United States.

President Trump has justified the duties on the grounds of United States national security. Jobs have been lost in the steel industry, and Trump promised jobs back during his election campaign.

The European Commission demands a permanent exemption for the EU from United States import duties. In the Commission's view, the duties cannot be justified on grounds of national security.
