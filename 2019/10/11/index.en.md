# Friday 11.10.2019 (radio)

The UN criticises Turkey for its attack on Syria. The Nobel Peace Prize goes to Ethiopia. An Iranian tanker exploded. An award for good cancer care goes to Turku. It will rain only a little at the weekend.

## Turkey continued the attack

![Photo taken from the Turkish side looking towards Syria, where smoke can be seen rising from the town of Tal Abyad.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11015385)

Smoke is rising from the town of Tal Abyad. Photo: Bulent Kilic / AFP

The UN criticises Turkey for its attack on northern Syria. The UN Security Council has held a meeting because of Turkey's attack.

The Security Council says that Turkey must stop the attack on Syria. The Security Council says that the attack makes people's suffering worse and increases the number of refugees. Finland also says that Turkey must stop the attack.

Turkey has also struck Kurdish targets at night. Civilians have also died in Turkey's attack. A great many people have fled from Turkey's attack.

## Nobel Peace Prize to Ethiopia

![Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11016398)

Open image viewer

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed. Photo: Michael Tewelde / AFP

The Ethiopian Prime Minister **Abiy Ahmed** receives the Nobel Peace Prize. The prize is awarded by the Norwegian Nobel Committee.

The Nobel Committee says that Abiy Ahmed has built peace and international co-operation. The committee thanks Ahmed for doing a lot of work to improve relations between Ethiopia and its neighbour Eritrea.

The Nobel Committee says that Abiy Ahmed has also improved democracy in Ethiopia, because he has released political prisoners and removed censorship of the media.

The Nobel Peace Prize will be given to Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed in December in Oslo.

An explosion has happened on an Iranian oil tanker, that is, a tank ship. The explosion happened in the Red Sea off Saudi Arabia. The tanker caught fire and oil has leaked from the ship into the sea.

The cause of the explosion is not known. The explosion on the Iranian oil tanker may worsen relations between Iran and Saudi Arabia.

## Turku University Hospital given an award for cancer care

![Turku University Central Hospital](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9786962)

Open image viewer

Turku University Central Hospital, TYKS. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Turku University Central Hospital has received an international award for good cancer care. The award is a recognition that cancer treatment in Turku is very good.

Turku University Central Hospital is the second hospital in Finland to receive the recognition of European cancer hospitals. Previously, the HUS Cancer Center in Helsinki received the recognition in Finland.
