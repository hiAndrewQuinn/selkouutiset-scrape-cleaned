# Saturday 17.11.2018 (radio)

Many people have disappeared in the California fires. The Redi shopping centre has not got as many customers as it expected. Premature babies' day was celebrated on Saturday. Finns have had luck again in the Eurojackpot game.

## The California fires

![Damage from a ground fire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10511011)

Open image viewer

Fire is destroying California. Photo: AOP

Large fires continue in California, USA. Over 70 people have died in the fires, and over 1,000 people are missing.

Most of the missing are from the town of Paradise and its surroundings. The fire has burned the town completely.

In many California towns the air is full of smoke. That is why many schools are closed.

President Donald Trump will visit residents of the fire area and firefighters on Saturday.

## The Redi shopping centre

![Construction site of the Redi and Majakka buildings.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10474323)

Open image viewer

The Redi shopping centre is in Kalasatama, Helsinki. Photo: Timo Korhonen / AOP

Helsinki's new shopping centre Redi has not got as many customers as it expected. Redi opened in Kalasatama in Helsinki in September.

Redi has almost 200 shops. Many shops have problems, because there are few customers. 5 firms have already closed.

Professor Pekka Mattila estimates that people have not yet got used to using Redi's services. Redi is not yet part of the residents' everyday life.

Mattila also says that Redi lacks a large hypermarket. For example, families with children want to buy everything they need from 1 big shop.

Redi may still succeed, Professor Mattila says. The Kalasatama area is growing all the time.

## Premature babies' day

![A premature baby's feet](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10339008)

Open image viewer

A small premature baby needs a lot of care. Photo: AOP

Premature babies' day was celebrated on Saturday. A child is premature if they are born before the pregnancy has lasted 37 weeks, or if they weigh less than 2,500 grams.

Premature babies are remembered in purple. Purple stands for sensitivity. For example, the Ylistö bridge in Jyväskylä, the Kuopio City Theatre and the Lahti radio masts were lit with purple light on Saturday.

This year the theme of premature babies' day is family-centred care. Studies show that a premature baby does better if the parents take part in caring for the child in hospital.

## Eurojackpot millionaires

![Eurojackpot coupons at the Prisma in Loimaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10192425)

Open image viewer

Eurojackpot is a European lottery. Photo: Mari Kahila / Yle

Finland has gained 2 new Eurojackpot millionaires. On Friday a player from Espoo won 18 million euros and a player from Kajaani won 3.8 million euros.

Eurojackpot is a European lottery. It started in 2012.

In total Finland already has 59 Eurojackpot millionaires. The group is large, because many big wins in Finland have gone to groups of many players.
