# Tuesday 9.1.2018 (radio)

Finns want to go on holiday to somewhere warm. Lake fish cannot be had in shops because of the ice situation. Snow cannon snow has caused a lot of destruction in Eastern Finland. 100 years have passed since the Finnish Civil War.

Luxury is now wanted on holiday trips

Finns are now eagerly booking trips. Finns want to go on a holiday abroad in spring or summer.

In January, a lot of package trips are sold, that is, trips whose price includes a flight and a hotel.

The travel agency Aurinkomatkat says that Finns now want luxury on their holidays. That is why many book a 4- or 5-star hotel for their holiday.

In winter Finns want to travel to the Canary Islands or to Thailand. In summer, Finns' favourite destination is Greece. Turkish and Tunisian tourist destinations also interest Finns.

There is a shortage of Finnish fish

![A net under the ice.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8715939)

Open image viewer

Ice fishing is difficult because of the poor ice situation. Photo: Antti Pylväs / Yle

There is a shortage of domestic lake fish in shops. Little fish gets to the shops, because fishermen cannot get to the lake to fish because of the ice situation.

This winter there have not been hard frosts, and that is why many lakes have not frozen properly. There may be a little ice on the lake, so you cannot get out fishing by boat. However, the ice is not thick enough, so you cannot walk on the ice to fish either.

In shops, the price of foreign fish goes up when there is a shortage of domestic fish.

Snow cannon snow has caused a lot of damage

![Kainuu snow cannon snow power cuts](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10009339)

Open image viewer

Snow cannon snow is hard snow that builds up on trees. Photo: Kainuu Rescue Service / Loiste Sähköverkko Oy

Snow cannon snow has caused extensive destruction in the forests of Eastern Finland and Kainuu. Forest owners are estimated to lose millions of euros because of the snow cannon snow damage.

In the forests of Kainuu, for example, snow cannon snow has broken trees, perhaps as many as tens of thousands of cubic metres. The full extent of the damage will only become clear in spring.

Many trees have fallen onto power lines. That is why many homes have been left without power.

Snow cannon snow is hard snow that builds up on trees. It is so heavy that the tops and branches of trees can break under it. This winter, snow cannon snow has knocked trees onto power lines. That is why power has been cut off in many homes.

Commemorations are held for the Finnish Civil War

![The Whites preparing for war, Lammi Civil Guard members.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9961280)

There was a civil war in Finland in 1918. Photo: Yle archive

It is 100 years since the Finnish Civil War. The Civil War of 1918 will be remembered during the year in many different events in different parts of the country.

For example, a commemoration will be held in Tampere in February. At the same time, an opera about the Finnish Civil War will premiere. The opera is called Veljeni vartija.

A commemoration will be held in Parliament at the end of January. At that time there will also be a moment of silence in memory of the victims of the Civil War. The secretary general of the anniversary year, Pekka Timonen, says that the event will stress the importance of democracy and social unity.
