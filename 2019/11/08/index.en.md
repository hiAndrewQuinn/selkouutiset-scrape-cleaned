# Friday 8.11.2019 (radio)

Youth unemployment is falling slowly. Unemployment benefits and social assistance may be late because of the postal strike. Disney's film Frozen II, which also tells about Lapland, had its premiere in Hollywood. An unknown painting by Helene Schjerfbeck will be shown at the Ateneum Art Museum. Järvenpää's Citymarket was chosen as the best grocery store in the world.

## Youth unemployment is falling slowly

![two construction workers carry an iron bar at a building site](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10918292)

Open image viewer

Employment is no longer growing in the construction industry. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment forecasts that employment will not improve as quickly as the government wants. The government is trying to get 75 percent of Finns aged 15 to 64 into work.

Unemployment has fallen a lot in Finland in recent years. Now 6.6 percent of Finns are unemployed.

Youth unemployment has not fallen as much as for other Finns. There are still a third more people under 25 unemployed than 11 years ago.

## Kela's benefits may be late

![A man opens the door of Kela.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10140741)

Open image viewer

Kela advises people to apply for benefits in the online service during the postal strike. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

People may get unemployment benefits and social assistance late, when the postal strike begins. Kela says that payment of benefits may be late, because Posti does not deliver customers' applications on time. Kela hopes that people will fill in their applications on the internet.

Customers may also receive late the letters that Kela sends about the payment of benefits. However, the benefit decisions are visible in Kela's online service on the internet.

The postal strike begins on Monday and lasts 2 weeks.

## Lapland's nature and people in a Disney film

![Per-Olof Nutti, Aili Keskitalo, Åsa Larsson Blind and Tiina Sanila-Aikio, Sami representatives.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11058310)

Open image viewer

Sami representatives on the red carpet in Hollywood: Per-Olof Nutti, Aili Keskitalo, Åsa Larsson, Tiina Sainila-Aikio and their daughters. Photo: Siv Eli Vuolab / Sámediggi

The animated film Frozen II has had its premiere in the United States.

The film shows Lapland's nature and Sami culture. The Walt Disney company made an agreement with the Sami of Finland, Sweden and Norway about the film. In the agreement, Disney promised to respect Sami culture. A group of Sami advised the film's makers.

Sami were guests of honour at the premiere in Hollywood. Tiina Sainila-Aikio, from Inari, said that the film was more beautiful than she could have dreamed.

Disney is also making a Frozen II film in the Sami language.

## Unknown Schjerfbeck shown in Helsinki

![The Girl from Barösund (1885–1890). Private collection. Helene Schjerfbeck](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1969,x_0,y_618/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6098615dc55a82c667e/1573807542)

Open image viewer

The Girl from Barösund. Photo: Ateneum

A painting by the artist Helene Schjerfbeck, which was not known before, will be shown at the Ateneum Art Museum in Helsinki.

The painting is called The Girl from Barösund. It is owned by a Finnish private person. The owner of the painting has said that Schjerfbeck visited his family and gave the painting as a gift in the late 1800s.

## Järvenpää's Citymarket the best in the world

![Markku Hautala](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11058385)

Open image viewer

Shopkeeper Markku Hautala. Photo: Citymarket

Järvenpää's Citymarket has been chosen as the best grocery store in the world.

The award was given by the international retail research organisation IGD in London. The competition compared a thousand shops in various parts of the world.

Järvenpää Citymarket's shopkeeper Markku Hautala says that the award came from good quality and from a sushi restaurant inside the shop.
