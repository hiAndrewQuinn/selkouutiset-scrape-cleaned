# Saturday 7.10.2017 (radio)

The rotavirus vaccine has worked well. Fewer large shipments of alcohol are caught by customs than before. A lot of people moved to Åland last year.

## The rotavirus vaccine has worked well

![A small child is on the floor in front of the television.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9723710)

Open image viewer

In Finland all children are vaccinated against rotavirus. Photo: Tiia Korhonen / Yle

Small children very rarely have to go to hospital in Finland because of rotavirus. Before, rotavirus was a common reason for hospital care of small children.

Rotavirus causes a stomach illness, which spreads easily. It causes diarrhoea, vomiting and fever in a child.

Rotavirus has decreased quickly in Finland, because since 2009 all children have received a vaccine against rotavirus.

The National Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, says that the rotavirus vaccine has been very effective. Hospital care caused by rotavirus has decreased by 93 percent among children under 5 years old within 5 years.

THL calculates that the vaccines have prevented about 2,200 serious cases of illness each year.

## Customs' alcohol checks are decreasing

![A man pulls beer trolleys behind him.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9853125)

Open image viewer

Many Finns buy alcohol from trips to Estonia as souvenirs. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / AOP

Customs checks the import of alcohol less than before. Checking has decreased, because customs' funding has been cut.

Before, customs had 7 people who checked the import of alcohol in Helsinki's harbours. Now there are fewer of them, so, for example, fewer intensive check raids are carried out than before.

Between January and June, customs caught 18 alcohol shipments, which were moved to tax assessment. Just a couple of years ago, over 500 shipments a year were moved to tax assessment.

An alcohol shipment is moved to tax assessment if customs suspects that not all of it is meant for personal use. Then tax may have to be paid on the alcohol.

## Åland gained new residents

![Åland](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9567231)

Open image viewer

The Åland island group is part of Finland.

A record number of people moved to Åland last year. More than 1,000 people moved to Åland.

Of the movers, 700 speak Swedish as their mother tongue and about 100 speak Finnish. More than 200 movers speak something other than Finnish or Swedish as their mother tongue.

About 30,000 people live in Åland in total.

## Weather turns rainy

![Weather map for three days.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9871412)

Open image viewer

In the north it is dry at first, while it rains elsewhere in the country. Photo: Yle

A lot of rain will fall in the coming days.

Yle meteorologist Kerttu Kotakorpi says that the first rain front will arrive in Finland on Saturday evening. At that time, the most rain will fall in South-Western Finland.

After that the rains move east. On Sunday the most rain will fall in the south-east. Perhaps up to 30 millimetres of rain will fall, Kerttu Kotakorpi says.

In the north it is dry at first, but after the weekend the weather will also turn rainy there. In the middle of next week it may also snow in Lapland.

Kerttu Kotakorpi says that the coming days will also be windy.
