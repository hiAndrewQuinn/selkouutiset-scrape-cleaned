# Monday 20.1.2020 (radio)

More Iraqis are applying for Finnish citizenship than before. Norway's right-wing populists are leaving the government. Billionaires have ever more money compared with others. In Lapland there is already almost a metre of snow in places.

## More and more Iraqis are applying for Finnish citizenship

![Asylum seekers](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10601748)

Open image viewer

Asylum seekers. Photo: Milla Vahtila / Yle

More Iraqis are applying for Finnish citizenship than before. Last year about 1,600 Iraqis applied for Finnish citizenship, whereas in 2018 Iraqi citizens submitted fewer than 1,000 applications.

People who came to Finland in 2015 can now apply for Finnish citizenship. In 2015, over 32,000 asylum seekers came to Finland. A large part of them were Iraqis.

Last year fewer than 2,500 new asylum applications were made in Finland. The most new asylum seekers came from Russia and Turkey.

## Norway's right-wing populists are leaving the government

![Siv Jensen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6330435e25a1b5f0b55/1579524670)

Open image viewer

Siv Jensen, the leader of the Progress Party, has served as finance minister. Photo: Cornelius Poppe / EPA

Norway's right-wing populist Progress Party is leaving the government. The departure is linked to the family of an Isis fighter who was brought to Norway from Syria.

The Progress Party does not accept that a Norwegian-Pakistani woman was let into Norway from the al-Hol camp last week. The woman has been married to 2 Isis fighters. Her child is seriously ill.

The Progress Party said that only a sick child may come to Norway. The woman did not agree to this.

The woman, a five-year-old boy and a three-year-old daughter came to Norway on Saturday. The woman was arrested at the airport in Oslo. The authorities suspect that the woman has taken part in the activities of a terrorist organisation.

## The number of billionaires has doubled

![Oxfam](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10088600)

Open image viewer

The aid organisation Oxfam is mapping inequality. Photo: Andy Rain / EPA

There are ever more super-rich people in the world, that is, billionaires. The number of billionaires has doubled over 10 years. Now there are 2,150 of them in the world.

The civil society organisation Oxfam has calculated that billionaires have more money than 60 per cent of the rest of the world's people. The 22 richest men in the world own more than all the women in Africa put together.

## In places in Lapland there is almost a metre of snow

![A person with a snow shovel next to a car](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_381/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4611155a73095e6a8c5/1517488599)

Open image viewer

In places in Lapland there is almost a metre of snow. Photo: Christoffer Westerlund / Yle

A lot of snow fell in Lapland at the weekend. In some places more than 20 centimetres of snow has fallen.

The most snow fell in Posio, 23 centimetres. In Tornio and Ranua 19 centimetres of snow fell.

The most snow in the whole of Finland is in Sodankylä. The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that in Vuotso in Sodankylä there is 97 centimetres of snow.

Only a centimetre less snow, that is 96 centimetres, is in Naruska in Salla. A snow depth of 95 centimetres has been measured in Poka in Kittilä.
