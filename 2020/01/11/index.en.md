# Saturday 11.1.2020 (radio)

Iran admitted that it shot down the Ukrainian plane. Finland is looking for ways to reduce emissions from traffic. Almost all children in Finland take part in some hobby. Oman's ruler Qaboos bin Said has died.

## Iran shot down the Ukrainian plane

![Rescue staff around the wreck](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11150617)

Open image viewer

Wreckage at the Ukrainian plane crash site. Photo: Abedin Taherkenareh / EPA

Iran admits that its army shot down the Ukrainian passenger plane on Wednesday. Iran says that the plane was flying near a military base and that the Iranian army thought it was a missile.

The Iranian army and Iran's state leaders ask the relatives of the victims for forgiveness. They say that the army made a mistake when it shot down the passenger plane.

There were 176 people on the Ukrainian plane. They all died.

## Reducing emissions by intervening in driving

![An electric car in the foreground and a petrol car behind it.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4242,h_2386,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6220605df247ed10742/1576159543)

Open image viewer

Car charges are being considered as a whole. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finland is looking for ways to reduce emissions from car traffic. On Saturday, the permanent secretary of the Ministry of Transport and Communications, **Harri Pursiainen**, was a guest on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme.

Pursiainen said that emissions can be cut most effectively if the state intervenes in the costs of car use.

New taxes or other charges based on emissions may come in for cars. Permanent Secretary Pursiainen says that more electric cars need to be sold, because electric cars do not produce emissions.

## Finnish children do a lot of hobbies

![A child jumping on a trampoline](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10859983)

Open image viewer

Jumping on a trampoline is effective exercise. Photo: Rosa Huuska / Yle

A very large number of Finnish children do some hobby. 90 per cent of children and young people have a hobby. A very large number of children do sport. Popular sports are cycling and jogging.

Newer hobbies whose popularity is growing are fishing, circus tricks, stick horse exercise, and jumping on a trampoline and bowling.

Besides sport, other popular hobbies are, for example, computer and console games, reading, outdoor activities, and video and photography.

## Oman's ruler Qaboos bin Said has died

![Sultan Qaboos bin Said Al Said at the opening of the annual session of the country's Consultative Council in November 2009.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6634597)

Open image viewer

Sultan Qaboos bin Said Al Said at the opening of the annual session of the country's Consultative Council in November 2009. Photo: Hamid Al-Qasmi / EPA

The longest-serving leader in the Arab world, the ruler of Oman, **Qaboos bin Said**, has died. He was 79 years old. Sultan Qaboos ruled Oman for almost 50 years. He did a lot of work for peace in the Middle East.

Qaboos bin Said is also remembered for helping to free many hostages who were kidnapped in the Middle East region. For example, he may have helped when two Finns were kidnapped in Yemen in 2013.

The new ruler of Oman has been chosen: **Haitham bin Tariq**, a cousin of Qaboos bin Said and the Minister of Culture.
