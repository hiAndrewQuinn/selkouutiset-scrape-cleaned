# Radio Clear News Sunday 13.8.2023

Shop workers. Finns Party presidential candidate. Summer theatres. Weather.

## Shop workers

![People shopping at the Sello shopping centre in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5987,h_3361,x_0,y_125/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114680964be8bff76c7e/1698658474)

Shopkeepers are afraid that in future it will be difficult to get workers for shops. The reason is that the government intends to reduce housing benefit, for example.

Many shop workers have a low wage, and they receive housing benefit. Many shop workers do part-time work.

Shopkeepers are afraid that without housing benefit, workers will not be able to live in big cities. Living there is often expensive.

That is why it may be harder to find workers for shops in big cities.

The government also intends to reduce other support that many part-time workers receive. The government wants people to go into full-time jobs.

## Finns Party presidential candidate

![Jussi Halla-aho gives a speech.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115597664d8ac5505b76/1691921527)

Halla-aho gave a speech. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

**Jussi Halla-aho** is now the official presidential candidate of the Finns Party. The Finns Party chose Halla-aho as its presidential candidate at the party congress in Tampere.

After that Halla-aho gave a speech.

Halla-aho said that it is good for the president to bring up the problems of ordinary Finns.

Halla-aho also warned that Europe is too dependent on China's industry. In his view, Europe must be more independent.

In his speech he also criticised the media, that is, the news organisations. In Halla-aho's view, some news organisations support left-wing parties.

According to Halla-aho, that is why they have attacked the Finns Party.

The presidential election will be held in January 2024.

## Summer theatres

![Juha Varis on the stage of the Löytänä summer theatre, telling the audience about the play.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_92/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114029664ababbecd5c2/1688972334)

Summer theatre in Viitasaari. Photo: Jussi Lindroos / Yle

Summer theatres are doing better than a year ago.

Last summer there were fewer audiences at summer theatres than usual. Some people were still afraid of the coronavirus.

They therefore did not dare to go to the theatre.

Summer theatres say that this summer there has again been a large audience.

There are more than 600 summer theatres in Finland. In summer theatres the actors are usually amateurs, not professionals.
