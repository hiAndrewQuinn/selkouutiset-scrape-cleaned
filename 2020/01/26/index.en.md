## Finnish researcher: The coronavirus does not spread easily during the incubation period

![A fully protected man at a self-service machine in a shop in Wuhan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11176358)

A Finnish expert says that transmission of the coronavirus during the incubation period is not likely. This is what leading expert Jussi Sane at the National Institute for Health and Welfare says.

The Chinese health minister said on Sunday that the coronavirus spreads during the incubation period. At that time a person does not yet know that they have the virus. The minister also said that the virus's ability to spread is growing stronger.

Jussi Sane says that the coronavirus incubates for about 2 to 12 days. The disease spreads most easily when a person already has symptoms. Catching the disease from a person without symptoms is less likely.

At least 56 people have died from the coronavirus in China. About 2,000 infections have been confirmed.

## Concrete industry is to become carbon neutral

![Concrete waste at a demolition site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_398/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6330235e259e18c7edd/1579523701)

Open image viewer

Most construction waste is concrete. Photo: Jaana Polamo / Yle

The use of concrete in the world is growing a lot. For example, many bridges, pillars, stairs, floors and streets are made of concrete.

Now there is a wish to make concrete part of the circular economy and to get concrete to bind carbon.

Cement is used in making concrete. The production of cement causes perhaps 5 to 8 per cent of all the world's carbon dioxide emissions. Crushed concrete, however, binds carbon dioxide.

The goal of the Finnish concrete and cement industry is to be carbon neutral by 2050.

## Divorces of parents of young children are common

![A woman takes a ring off her finger.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10731331)

Photo: AOP

Parents of young children are divorcing more often than before, the Family Federation says. Reasons for divorce include, for example, less time together, tiredness, busyness and quarrels.

Statistics show that the risk of divorce is greatest when the marriage has lasted 3 to 4 years. After that the risk gets smaller.

In Finland divorces are clearly more common than the European average. About 25 per cent of first marriages end in divorce in Finland.

## Bladder wrack has many healthy substances

![Bladder wrack belt in the Baltic Sea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3072,h_1728,x_0,y_461/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-27070856af3323c47f5/1614090448)

The wracks were renamed a few years ago. Before that, the bladder wrack was known by the name bladder seaweed. Photo: Camilla Gustafsson

There is more to eat in the Baltic Sea than fish.

At the University of Turku, researchers are studying the antioxidants in bladder wrack. An antioxidant is a substance that prevents oxidation. The former name of bladder wrack was bladder seaweed. It grows in the Baltic Sea.

The health effects of bladder wrack have already been studied elsewhere in the world. Now it is being studied in Finland.

Bladder wrack contains a lot of healthy fibres and vitamin C. Bladder wrack is also rich in minerals, especially iodine. Finns get too little iodine from their food.

Bladder wrack is at risk if the Baltic Sea is doing badly. Bladder wrack does not cope in an over-fertilised sea.

One way to stop the Baltic Sea from becoming over-fertilised is to eat its cyprinid fish. For example, one bream meal removes a kilo of blue-green algae.
