# Wednesday 3.7.2019 (radio)

Daily life in elderly care homes has not yet improved. June was record-breakingly warm. Finns want to go on holiday to the warmth of the south. A Finnish passport is good for travelling.

## Elderly care homes

![A nurse escorts an elderly person.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6722383)

Open image viewer

Many care homes have too few workers. Photo: Yle

Daily life in elderly care homes has hardly improved. Daily life in care homes has stayed fairly much the same, even though at the start of the year there was a lot of talk about the need to improve the quality of elderly care.

The nursing trade unions Tehy and Super say that the problems are the same as before. For example, care homes have too few workers.

Minister of Family and Basic Services Krista Kiuru says that the staff ratio, that is, the minimum number of nurses, may be raised perhaps next autumn.

## A record-warm June

![The thermometer rose above 35 degrees in France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10852260)

Open image viewer

June was warm all over the world. Photo: Damien Meyer / AFP

This year's June was record-breakingly warm. It was the warmest June in the whole measurement history.

The average temperatures in Europe in June were more than 2 degrees higher than usual, according to the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts. This is due to hot air that came from the Sahara desert to Southern and Central Europe.

June was also the warmest June ever in Finland and across the whole world.

## Travel

![Madlen Muscat and her son beside a canoe](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10857894)

Open image viewer

Maltese Madlen Muscat brought her son on a cottage holiday to the shore of Lake Saimaa. Photo: Kalle Purhonen / Yle

Many tourists who are fleeing the heat in Europe have now come to Finland. For example, many tourists from Italy and Germany have come to the Lake Saimaa area.

In Europe the temperature rose to over 40 degrees last week, so these tourists are not bothered by Finland's cool summer weather or showers. Finns, on the other hand, are bothered.

Travel agencies say that Finns are now eagerly looking for holiday trips to the warmth of the south. Finns start looking for trips to the south as soon as the weather turns rainy, the travel agencies say.

Many have left booking a holiday trip until the last minute. People wanted to wait first and see whether Finland would have the same heatwave as last summer. Now that it looks like no such heat will come, people want to go on holiday to the sunny beaches of the south.

## Influential passports

![A Finnish passport in a person's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10831155)

Open image viewer

A Finnish passport. Photo: Yle

A Finnish passport is among the most influential passports in the world.

A global passport comparison says that a Finnish passport gets you into 187 countries. South Korean and German passports also get you into just as many countries.

The most influential passports are the passports of Japan and Singapore. They get you into 189 countries.

The Afghan passport is the weakest. It gets you into only 25 countries without a visa.
