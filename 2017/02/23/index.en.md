# Thursday 23.2.2017 (radio)

Kesko is closing over 50 grocery stores. The Social Democratic Party does not yet have a presidential candidate. Finn Antti Karppinen has won a big photography competition. The One Million Birdhouses campaign succeeded.

## Siwa and Valintatalo shops are being closed

![The Siwa logo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9385502)

Open image viewer

Siwa got a new owner last year. Photo: Jarkko Heikkinen / Yle

The retail company Kesko is closing over 50 grocery stores. The shops are Siwa and Valintatalo stores.

Because the shops are closing, Kesko is laying off workers. Over 200 people will lose their jobs. Kesko is trying to find other work for them.

Kesko bought Siwa and Valintatalo last year. Already then Kesko said that maybe not all Siwa and Valintatalo stores could continue operating.

## The SDP is looking for a presidential candidate

![Jutta Urpilainen arriving at a plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9442464)

Open image viewer

Member of Parliament Jutta Urpilainen. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The SDP, or the Social Democratic Party, does not yet have a presidential candidate.

Many Social Democrats hoped that Member of Parliament Jutta Urpilainen would agree to be the presidential candidate. But on Thursday Urpilainen said that she will not run. Urpilainen said that she needs time for her family. Urpilainen is the mother of a small child.

The SDP needs its own presidential candidate, because it is one of the big parties in Finland. Finland has a presidential election next year.

## A photography win for Finland

![A boy above the clouds looks at his paper boat beside a stream.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9475632)

Open image viewer

Antti Karppinen's photograph Endless. Photo: Antti Karppinen

Finnish photographer Antti Karppinen has won a big competition. Karppinen got a gold medal in a competition called the World Photographic Cup. The award was given to Karppinen on Thursday in Japan.

Karppinen took part in the category where illustration pictures compete. He won the competition with the photograph Endless, in Finnish Loppumaton.

The photograph shows Karppinen's own son. He has angel wings. Water flows behind the boy, and it looks as if the water's flow continues forever.

Antti Karppinen has received a lot of feedback about the photograph. The picture has touched, for example, people whose own child has died.

## One million birdhouses

![A birdhouse in a tree.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8750389)

Open image viewer

A new birdhouse. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

The One Million Birdhouses campaign has reached its goal. The goal was to build a million birdhouses.

The birdhouses are meant to help birds. The birdhouses help birds that build a nest in a hole in a tree. Nowadays it is difficult for them to find a good nest hole, because old trees have been cut down from the forests.

The One Million Birdhouses campaign started a year ago. During the campaign a large number of people have built birdhouses.
