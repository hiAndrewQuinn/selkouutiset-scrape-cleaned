# Monday 28.10.2019 (radio)

The European Union is giving Britain more time for its EU exit. Winter speed limits come into use in traffic this week. Smartwatches are selling a lot. Helsinki is the second venue of the 2022 ice hockey World Championships. Some e-scooters are also in use in winter. The coming days are cold.

## Britain's EU exit is postponed

![View of the Parliament building in London.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11040213)

Open image viewer

View of the Parliament building in London. Photo: Neil Hall / EPA

The European Union has made new decisions about Britain's EU exit. The European Union is giving Britain more time for its EU exit.

The plan was that Britain would leave the EU already at the end of October. Now Britain gets 3 more months. The EU is ready to wait for Britain to leave the union at the end of January.

This is already the 3rd time that Britain's EU exit has been postponed. People have been trying to bring about the EU exit for a long time. The British voted for the EU exit already 3 years ago.

## Winter speed limits come into use

![A changed winter speed limit sign along main road 5.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9901759)

Open image viewer

On country roads, the speed limit in winter is usually 80 kilometres per hour. Photo: Niklas Gabrielsson / Yle

Winter speed limits come into use in traffic this week.

On motorways, the speed limit drops to 100 kilometres per hour. On some motorways, the speed limit may still be higher in November, if it is light and the driving conditions are good.

On country roads, the winter speed limit is 80 or 70 kilometres per hour. The 100 kilometre speed limit stays if the driving directions of the road are separated, for example by a barrier.

The speed limits change from Tuesday. Winter speeds are in use across the whole country by the end of the weekend.

## Smartwatches sell a lot

Smartwatches are popular.

This year, twice as many smartwatches have been sold as last year. By the end of September, smartwatch sales had grown by more than 90 percent.

For many buyers, a smartwatch is a sports watch. The watch is used to track their own movement and fitness.

## E-scooters in use in winter too

![E-scooters](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10852494)

Open image viewer

E-scooters. Photo: Markku Pitkänen / Yle

Some e-scooters are also in use in winter. E-scooters are in many cities.

At least 2 companies intend to rent out e-scooters in winter too. There are fewer e-scooters in use in winter than in summer, however.

The weather also affects the rental situation. If a lot of snow falls, the e-scooters may be collected off the streets.

## The weather is cold

The coming days are cold.

During the day, the temperature is below zero across almost the whole country. At night there is frost across the whole country.

Snowfalls will come in various parts of Finland.
