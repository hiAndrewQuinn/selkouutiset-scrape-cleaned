## Gaza

More help has been taken to Gaza. The help has been transported to Gaza through Egypt.

This time 17 lorries got into Gaza. On Saturday 20 lorries took food, water and medicines to Gaza.

The help is still not enough, however. Gaza lacks everything, because Israel has closed the borders of Gaza. Gaza is on the Mediterranean coast, on the border between Israel and Egypt.

The war in Gaza began when the Palestinian militant group Hamas attacked Israel from Gaza about 2 weeks ago. Many thousands of people have died in the war.

The King of Norway, Finland's neighbour, has a coronavirus infection.

King Harald is 86 years old. He is being treated at home.

The king is on sick leave until the coronavirus symptoms stop. During the king's sick leave, Crown Prince Haakon is carrying out the king's duties.

## Food robots

![Salen food robot in Tampere.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5177,h_2912,x_0,y_18/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11288196488380f21d2e/1686649003)

Food delivery robots are becoming more common in Finland. Food delivery robots already deliver grocery shopping to customers in 9 cities.

The S Group retail group says that robot deliveries will continue in winter too. Deliveries are possible because the food robots get winter tyres.

Finns like food robots. Many help if a food robot has difficulty continuing its journey.

## Bad news

![A man sits on a rock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_204/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-118598265290aaa7a46d/1697962210)

Open image viewer

Psychologist Juho Mertanen. Photo: Mikko Koski/Yle

Constant bad news can wear a person down. This is what psychologist Juho Mertanen says.

In recent years bad news has been coming all the time. The news has been about the coronavirus, Russia's war in Ukraine and the crisis in Gaza.

Social media adds to the flood of information. A person may get the feeling that they are drowning in bad news.

Bad news often also tells about things that a person cannot influence themselves. We may get the feeling that we do not control our own lives, Mertanen says.

That is why we sometimes need rest and the feeling that life is under control. Psychologist Mertanen recommends that we sometimes look more closely and notice the small everyday things that make us feel good.

When Mertanen needs a good moment, he goes to the sauna or plays with a child. Noticing good moments takes care of the mind, says psychologist Mertanen.
