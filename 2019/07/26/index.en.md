# Friday 26.7.2019 (radio)

Too many people die in road traffic in Finland. Hospital emergency departments are crowded. There are few electric buses in Finland. Javelin thrower Jorma Kinnunen has died. The weekend is hot.

## Many people die in traffic

![Car accident in Nakkila.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10722560)

Too many people die in road traffic in Finland.

The EU countries' goal is that in 2020 half as many people die in road traffic as in 2010. For Finland the goal is that next year there will be at most a little over 130 deaths.

Finland is unlikely to reach the goal. Last year more than 230 people still died in road traffic in Finland.

## The emergency departments are crowded

![Hospital emergency departments are becoming crowded in different parts of Finland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10894143)

Open image viewer

Hospital emergency departments are becoming crowded in different parts of Finland. Photo: Simo Pitkänen / Yle

The emergency departments of hospitals are crowded. There are often too many patients in the emergency department.

Doctors are afraid that patients' safety suffers because of the crowding. Patients have to wait a long time, and the staff are tired.

Most patients come to the emergency department in the evening and at weekends, when health centres are closed. However, many patients also come during the day, because it is hard to get an appointment at a health centre.

Many people think the problems would decrease if more doctors were added to health centres. More doctor posts and better salaries are needed, so that young doctors want to come to work at health centres.

## There are few electric buses

![HSL's Linkker electric bus at Rautatientori in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10893541)

Open image viewer

An electric bus at Rautatientori in Helsinki. Photo: Timo-Pekka Heima / Yle

There are only a few electric buses in Finland so far. Electric buses are in Helsinki, Turku and Tampere.

Finland's neighbouring countries Sweden and Norway have bought more electric buses. It is important for them that the buses do not pollute the environment.

Finland is following how the technology of electric buses develops. Electric buses cost more than ordinary buses. Charging equipment is also expensive.

Still, the number of electric buses is growing in Finland too. In the autumn and winter almost 40 new electric buses will come to the Helsinki region.

## Javelin thrower Jorma Kinnunen has died

![Jorma Kinnunen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10895604)

Open image viewer

Jorma Kinnunen. Photo: Lehtikuva / Jorma Pouta

The famous Finnish athlete Jorma Kinnunen has died. Kinnunen was a javelin thrower. He died at the age of 77.

Jorma Kinnunen's best period was 30 years ago. Kinnunen won a silver medal at the Mexico Olympics in 1968. He also set the world record in javelin throw in 1969.
