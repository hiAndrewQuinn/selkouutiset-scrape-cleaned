# Saturday 13.01.2018 (radio)

There is now flu in almost all of Finland. Many cleaners are immigrants. In Saudi Arabia, women have been allowed into a stadium to watch football for the first time. In Lapland, people fear collisions with elk. Last year's best TV programmes have been given awards.

## Flu

![A man blows his nose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9995828)

Open image viewer

The symptoms of flu are worse than the symptoms of an ordinary cold. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There is now flu in almost all of Finland. Flu is a worse illness than an ordinary cold. Flu often includes, for example, a high fever.

Doctor Hanna Nohynek says that this winter there will perhaps be the most flu at the end of January and the beginning of February. Nohynek is a chief physician at the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

Many Finns get a vaccine against flu every year. Usually the vaccine gives good protection, but perhaps not this winter. Many people have received a vaccine that does not include the flu virus that is common now.

## Cleaners

![A cleaner mops](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9497703)

Photo: Elisa Kinnunen / Yle

Many cleaners are immigrants. Already 20 percent of cleaners have an immigrant background. The newspaper Karjalainen writes about this.

The number of immigrants is also growing in other service sector jobs. There are also many immigrants in the construction and transport sectors. In these sectors, wages are often low.

## Women in Saudi Arabia

![People in the football stands cheer and wave the team's colours. Among the men there are women in black abaya robes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10021224)

Open image viewer

There are now women in the stands of football stadiums in Saudi Arabia too. Photo: STR / EPA

The position of women is changing in Saudi Arabia. Now women have been allowed into a stadium to watch football for the first time.

The position of women is changing because Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman is reforming Saudi Arabia. The crown prince is worried about what other countries think of Saudi Arabia. He wants to show that Saudi Arabia is a modern country. However, men still decide women's affairs.

## Danger of elk collisions

![An elk killed in a collision.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5006761)

Open image viewer

This elk crashed into a car. Photo: Yle

In Lapland, people fear collisions with elk. In an elk collision, a car crashes into an elk. It is dangerous because the elk is a big animal.

There are a lot of elk in Lapland this winter. They look for food near the roads, because there is now thick snow on the ground. Often the elk are in a big group.

The winter holiday season starts in February. Then many tourists travel on the roads of Lapland. Not all tourists are used to elk, and so the danger of elk collisions grows.

## Venla awards

![Leea Klemola got three Venlas for the programme Myrskyn jälkeen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10021225)

Open image viewer

The director of the series Myrskyn jälkeen is Leea Klemola. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Television programmes have been given awards. The best programmes and performers of the year got Venla awards on Friday evening.

The TV series "Myrskyn jälkeen" ("After the Storm") got the most awards. It got 3 Venla awards in total. For example, it was last year's best Finnish drama series.

"Myrskyn jälkeen" is about Helsinki in the near future. In the series, a storm has turned the lives of the people of Helsinki upside down. "Myrskyn jälkeen" was shown on the channel Yle TV1.
