# Friday 14.5.2021 (radio)

The situation in the Middle East is restless. Inflation is speeding up in Finland. Corona home tests go on sale. Cherry trees are blossoming in Finland. The weather cools down a little.

## Middle East

![Rescue workers search the ruins of a building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2835,h_1595,x_0,y_146/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-806395609e7e61aa883/1620999882)

This building was destroyed in an Israeli air strike. Photo: Mustafa Hassona / AOP

The situation in the Middle East is restless. Many strikes have been carried out in the region, and people have died in the strikes.

Israel and the Palestinian militant group Hamas are facing each other. Israeli military aircraft have bombed Hamas, and Hamas has fired rockets at Israel.

On Friday it was reported that about 10 Israelis and more than 100 Palestinians have died in the strikes.

The UN Security Council is holding a meeting on Sunday because of the situation in the Middle East. The UN, that is, the United Nations, hopes that the strikes in the Middle East will stop.

## Inflation

![A car being filled with fuel.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6973405f05e4074390d/1594222221)

For example, the price of petrol has risen over the year. Photo: Elisa Kinnunen / Yle

In Finland inflation is speeding up. Inflation, that is, the rise in prices, has been slow in recent years.

However, in April this year prices rose by about 2 percent compared with April last year.

Prices of petrol and other fuels have risen in particular. Tobacco and train tickets have also clearly become more expensive.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that there is 1 new corona death in Finland. A total of 931 people have died in Finland because of corona.

There are 154 new coronavirus infections. The total number of all coronavirus infections in Finland is almost 90,000.

## Home tests

![Corona virus home test kit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-806216609e578ed57cf/1620989897)

Open image viewer

Corona home test kit. Photo: Veikko Eromäki / Yle

Corona home tests are going on sale in Finland too. Lidl stores have started selling corona home tests in Finland as well.

In the home test, the sample is taken with a swab from the nose, and the result is ready in 15 minutes.

Later, corona home tests will also be available at pharmacies. Sales of home tests at pharmacies will start by the end of May.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare reminds that a corona home test is uncertain. For example, you cannot end quarantine on the basis of a home test.

## Cherry trees

Warm weather has made cherry trees bloom in Finland too.

For example, the Roihuvuori cherry blossom park in Helsinki is now a sea of flowers.

Many people gather in the park to admire and photograph the pink flowers of the cherry trees.

## Weather

The weather cools down a little. At the weekend the temperature is about 20 degrees in a large part of the country. It is colder in Lapland.

On Saturday there will be rain and thunder showers in the southern and central parts of the country. However, at the weekend it will be dry in a large part of the country, meaning no rain will fall.
