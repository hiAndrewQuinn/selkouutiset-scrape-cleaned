# Saturday 30.3.2019 (radio)

Consumer loans will become cheaper next autumn. Taxis have been checked in Uusimaa. The health trend can also be seen in the strength of alcohol. Summer time starts at night.

## Consumer loans become cheaper

![Woman takes a loan on her phone](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6602637)

Open image viewer

Consumer loans will become cheaper next autumn. A consumer loan is a loan used to buy things or trips, for example.

In September a new law will come into force. Then new quick loans and other consumer loans will get a strict interest rate cap. The interest may be at most 20 percent a year.

The law also regulates other fees for consumer loans.

## There are shortcomings in taxis

![Taxi meter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10697919)

Open image viewer

Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

Many shortcomings have been found in taxis.

Authorities together carried out a taxi control operation in Uusimaa. More than 100 cars were checked. More than 60 taxis had shortcomings.

The most common shortcoming was that the permit holder's details, the driver's name or the prices were not clearly visible. In addition, 3 cars did not have a taximeter. 2 of the drivers were wanted by the police. In one car the driver was suspected of gross drunk driving.

All taxis had a valid taxi permit.

The checks were mainly made near Helsinki-Vantaa airport and in Espoo. The operation had 10 police officers and 5 tax inspectors. Representatives of Traficom, the Finnish Transport and Communications Agency, were also present.

The joint check was the first after the taxi law reform. The checks will become a permanent practice in different parts of Finland.

## Low-alcohol beers are interesting

![Miika Korpela, restaurant manager of Teerenpeli in Kamppi, pours beer from the tap.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10707888)

Open image viewer

In Kamppi, Helsinki, the restaurant manager of Teerenpeli, Miika Korpela, says that Finnish microbreweries have slowly woken up to the trend of low-alcohol beers. Photo: Annika Martikainen / Yle

The health trend can also be seen in the strength of beer.

The popularity of beers with little alcohol keeps growing. The strength of low-alcohol beer, called "number one beer", is 0.6 - 4.4 percent.

The Finnish Brewers' Association says that sales have risen by about 8 percent. Beer makers have noticed the popularity. That is why they are improving brewing technology and developing new number one beers.

The change can also be seen in restaurants. Customers look for taste experiences, not so much for getting drunk.

## Summer time starts

![Sculpture made of clocks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10707706)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ian Langsdon / EPA

Summer time starts the night before Sunday. Clocks are moved one hour forward at 3 at night.

The European Commission has proposed that moving clocks be stopped. MEPs have decided that the changing will end after 2021. EU countries must decide whether they move to permanent summer time or winter time.

Until then clocks will be moved in March and October.
