# Sunday 29.1.2023 (radio)

Corona era. Healthcare tasks. Accidents in Pakistan. Bronze medal in ice dance. Weather.

## Corona era

![Washing hands in a toilet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4677,h_2631,x_0,y_263/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6454615e4fa9e05b33d/1582279243)

Corona has changed Finland in many ways. Finland's first corona infection was confirmed exactly 3 years ago.

Because of corona, Finns' hygiene has improved. People wash their hands more than before, and in shops customers use hand sanitiser.

Corona also changed Finns' behaviour at workplaces. Employees, for example, do not come to work if they are ill.

In leisure time nature has become important to people. Many began to move about in nature during the corona restrictions.

During the corona era families also began to spend more time together. Many noticed that happiness cannot be bought.

## Healthcare tasks

![A person types on a computer, close-up of hands on a keyboard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5004795)

Open image viewer

A nurse's working time is wasted if she has to do office work. Photo: Yle

Changes to healthcare tasks are proposed. A working group has considered how the shortage of healthcare workers can be eased.

The working group says that working time is wasted in healthcare. Nurses also do work tasks that do not require healthcare training.

In some hospital districts tasks are already being shared in a new way. Nurses' tasks have been given, for example, to secretaries and patient transporters.

The working group's proposals will be described in more detail in mid-February.

## Accidents in Pakistan

Two serious accidents have happened in Pakistan in Asia. Many people died in the accidents.

At least 40 people died in a bus accident in western Pakistan. The bus plunged off a bridge and exploded.

A sad boat accident happened in north-west Pakistan. At least 10 children died when a boat capsized. The boat was carrying schoolchildren who were on a trip.

## Ice dance bronze medal

![A woman and a man with medals around their necks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688375)

Open image viewer

Finland's Julia Turkkila and Matthias Versluis won bronze medals. Photo: Tomi Hänninen - Chilipictures

Finland has won a medal in the figure skating European Championships. Juulia Turkkila and Matthias Versluis came third in ice dance and brought Finland a bronze medal.

The medal is rare. The last time Finland won a European Championship medal in ice dance was 28 years ago.

![Ice dance couple skating.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688374)

Finns are especially happy because the medal came at a home competition. The figure skating European Championships were held this time in Finland, in Espoo.

## The weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688376)

Open image viewer

On Monday rain comes to Finland. In the south it rains and there is sleet, elsewhere snow falls.

Snow falls most in Central Finland. Up to 20 centimetres of snow may accumulate. Driving conditions are poor.

In the central and northern parts of the country the snowfall continues on Tuesday. In the south and west the rain ends on Tuesday and the sun may shine.

The temperature is a little above zero in the south, and in the north there is a little frost.
