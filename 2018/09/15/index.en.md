# Saturday 15.9.2018 (radio)

Things are going badly for the clients of the church's diaconal work. Energy drinks may perhaps be banned for children. Hurricane Mangkhut is travelling from the Philippines to Hong Kong. Storm Florence in the United States is weakening. The warm autumn has slowed the autumn migration of birds

## Diaconal work clients

![Diaconal workers Johanna Lehmusmies and Päivi Isomäki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10405011)

The church's diaconal workers are worried about growing income differences in Finland.

The diaconal workers say that things are going badly for their clients. When the money is not enough for everything, many have to choose whether to use the money on food, medicine or paying the rent.

The diaconal workers also say that there are many families where a cycle of social exclusion began in the years of the recession. Now the cycle of exclusion is already continuing in the 3rd generation.

The church's diaconal work offers help to poor and sick people.

## Energy drinks

![Energy drink cans.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6760970)

Open image viewer

There are many different brands of energy drinks. Photo: Stiftung Warentest

The government may ban energy drinks for under-15s.

The matter was raised by Minister of Agriculture and Forestry Jari Leppä and Minister of Family Affairs and Social Services Annika Saarikko. The ministers have asked the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare to do a study on how energy drinks affect the health and well-being of children and young people.

The study will be ready in the autumn. After that it will be decided whether the law must be changed so that energy drinks are banned for under-15s.

At the moment energy drinks are not recommended for children and young people, but selling them is not banned.

Energy drinks usually have a lot of caffeine. Because of that the drink is not good for children.

## Mangkhut

![cars drive on a seafront street in water](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10406719)

Open image viewer

The typhoon brought a flood onto the streets of Manila. Photo: Mark R. Cristino / EPA

Hurricane Mangkhut hit the Philippines last night.

At least 12 people have died in the storm. It is believed that the number of victims will still rise.

The storm brought heavy rain and floods with it. In the northern part of the island of Luzon the storm caused great destruction in rice and maize fields.

The storm is next travelling to Hong Kong.

## Florence

![Trees fallen onto a road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10406494)

Open image viewer

Storm Florence knocked trees onto roads. Photo: Jim Lo Scalzo / EPA

In the United States storm Florence is weakening, but it is still pouring with rain.

So far at least 5 people have died in the storm.

An evacuation order has been given to about 1.7 million people in South and North Carolina and the state of Virginia. Almost a million households are without electricity.

## Autumn migration

![Chaffinch](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5235701)

Open image viewer

Chaffinches have started their autumn migration. Photo: Yle / Jari Flinck

The autumn migration of small birds is in full swing.

Yesterday in Rääkkylä in North Karelia about 140,000 small birds were seen migrating. Among the migrants were, for example, chaffinches, greenfinches and wagtails.

Because of the warm autumn, many birds have set off on migration later than usual.
