## Confidence in the economy

![Shop](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10592457)

Open image viewer

Many Finns trust the economy and plan to buy new things. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finns are trusting the economy again. Many Finns believe that their own finances and also the finances of the whole of Finland are now going in a good direction.

The change has happened quickly. In the spring, at the worst time of the corona crisis, Finns trusted the economy very little.

The change shows in the fact that many Finns now plan to spend money on new things. Many also plan to take out a loan and buy a new car or a flat.

The information is from a study by Statistics Finland.

## Corona infections

![Passengers wearing masks at Helsinki-Vantaa airport on 17 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11466873)

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare has said on Monday that there are 5 new corona infections in Finland. In total there are about 7,400 corona infections in Finland.

There are no new corona deaths. So far, 329 people have died in Finland because of corona.

## Border control

![A Finnair aircraft at Helsinki-Vantaa airport in Vantaa on 3 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11463493)

Open image viewer

Travellers must stay at home in quarantine for 2 weeks if they return from Austria, Slovenia or Switzerland. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

There have been changes to Finland's border control on Monday.

Finland has tightened border control between Finland and 3 European countries because of the corona. The countries are Austria, Slovenia and Switzerland. In these countries, corona infections have increased.

Finns can still return home from these countries. However, the authorities recommend that they stay at home in quarantine for 2 weeks after the trip. This is to stop the spread of the coronavirus.

Traffic between Finland and many European countries is already free, because the corona situation has improved. If the corona situation gets worse again in some country, Finland will tighten border control, as it is now doing for traffic from Austria, Slovenia and Switzerland.

## Drunk drivers in the summer

![A breath test check in Helsinki on Midsummer Day. The number plate in the picture has been blurred.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10270940)

Open image viewer

A breath test shows quickly whether the driver has been drinking alcohol. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

The police are looking for drunk drivers in traffic this week. The police look for drunk drivers with breath tests, which show whether the driver has been drinking alcohol.

The police look for drunk drivers especially in places where there are many summer cottages. In the summer there are usually more drunk drivers on the road than at other times.

## Sunset in Utsjoki

![A midnight sun night in Rovaniemi in June 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6955455efd6c4eb5e48/1593671544)

Open image viewer

The midnight sun in Utsjoki is now over. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

The sun has set in Utsjoki for the first time in more than 2 months. Utsjoki is the northernmost municipality in Finland.

The sun last set in Utsjoki on 16 May. So the sun stayed in the sky for 72 days.

The night in Utsjoki is still short. The night between Sunday and Monday lasted less than an hour.
