# Friday 24.2.2017 (radio)

Vegetarian food is popular. New jobs are being created in the service sector. Birch allergy can be treated in a new way. It will rain next week.

## Vegetarian food is popular

![Plant-based foods, or vegan products, on a shop shelf at the Pirkkala Citymarket.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9475911)

Shops already have a lot of vegetarian food. Photo: Anne Savin / Yle

Finns eat vegetarian food more than before. Nowadays about 30 percent of Finns eat a vegetarian meal at least once a week.

Many people nowadays think that vegetarian food is healthier than meat. Many also think that animals do not do well when they are raised for meat. That is why they choose vegetarian food.

The food industry has also started to make more vegetarian food. New fashionable foods are pulled oats and härkis, or fava beans, which have a lot of protein. Earlier only meat dishes had a lot of protein.

## There is work in the ICT sector

![Server.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9183747)

The information technology sector is growing. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

There is good news from service sector companies. The service sector is growing and new jobs are being created in the sector. In the service sector there is enough work for, for example, IT experts.

Jukka Leinonen, head of the telecommunications company DNA, says that the ICT sector is growing well. ICT, or information and communication technology, brings work to many.

Expert services are also growing. For example, there is enough work for designers, because a lot is being built in Finland now.

## A new treatment for birch allergy

![Birch catkins](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6086984)

Open image viewer

Many people are allergic to birch pollen. Photo: T. Säntti / Yle

Birch allergy can be treated in a new way. The new treatment helps people who are not helped well enough by ordinary allergy medicines.

The new treatment is desensitisation therapy. It helps a person tolerate birch pollen better.

Annika Kupari from Kokkola became interested in the new desensitisation therapy, because in it the medicine is given by mouth. The old way to give desensitisation therapy is to inject the medicine into the patient with a needle.

Kupari's 8-year-old son Niko has a bad birch allergy. When the birch blooms in spring, Niko has had to stay inside for long periods. Now Kupari hopes that the new treatment will help her son Niko.

## The winter holiday in Central Finland started

![Children skiing on a field in Saarikylät, Kangasala](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8712145)

Open image viewer

At the weekend there is good skiing weather, but next week it will rain. Photo: Jukka Töyli / Yle

The winter holiday has started in many Finnish schools. Next week there is a holiday, for example, in schools in Tampere, Jyväskylä, Mikkeli and Vaasa.

In schools in Southern Finland the winter holiday is already ending, and in schools in Northern Finland the winter holiday starts only in a week.

Yle meteorologist Toni Hellinen says that next week the winter holiday will be spent in quite rainy weather. The weather changes on Tuesday, and after that there will be above-zero temperatures in almost the whole country. On Wednesday and Thursday it will rain in different parts of Finland.

The best place to ski is Lapland. It is below zero there all the time.
