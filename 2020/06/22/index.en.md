# Monday 22.6.2020 (radio)

The Coast Guard's sunken boat will be raised this week. Work pressure has increased in workplaces. Alcohol is the most expensive in the EU in Finland. There is a shortage of new bicycles in shops.

The TV Clear News has a summer break and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## The Coast Guard boat is being raised

![Sunken patrol boat off Loviisa at sea on Sunday 21 June 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11411192)

The patrol boat sank stern first. The bow stayed on the surface. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The Coast Guard's sunken boat will be raised. The Accident Investigation Board will raise the boat perhaps on Tuesday or Wednesday, if the weather at sea is good. Once the boat has been raised, it can be examined.

The Coast Guard boat sank in the Gulf of Finland on Saturday. One person died in the accident. The Accident Investigation Board has already examined the accident site and interviewed the coast guards who were rescued from the boat.

The Accident Investigation Board is investigating why the Coast Guard boat probably hit a rock and sank.

## Many people are busy at work

![Nurse leaning on her hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6540564)

Open image viewer

The risk of work exhaustion has doubled in female-dominated sectors. Photo: Tiina Karjalainen

Work pressure has increased in workplaces. Employees feel pressure and strain especially in female-dominated sectors, that is, workplaces where many women work. Many employees say that there are too few employees, so work feels hurried and heavy.

In female-dominated sectors, employees feel serious work exhaustion twice as often as before, according to Statistics Finland's working conditions survey.

Female-dominated sectors include, for example, social and health care work, many customer service jobs and teaching.

## Alcohol is the most expensive in the EU in Finland

![Hands holding beer mugs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9742565)

Open image viewer

Alcohol is the most expensive in the EU in Finland. Tobacco also costs a lot in Finland. Photo: Wasim Khuzam / Yle

Many things are more expensive in Finland than in other EU countries.

Eurostat, the statistics office of the European Union, has compared prices in different countries. Alcoholic drinks are the most expensive in Finland among EU countries. Alcoholic drinks cost about 90% more in Finland than the EU average.

Food is about 20% more expensive and non-alcoholic drinks about 25% more expensive in Finland than in EU countries on average. Tobacco costs almost 35% more in Finland than the EU average.

## Many bicycles are bought

![Road bikes at a bicycle shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11395193)

Open image viewer

The corona spring inspired people to cycle and buy bicycles. Photo: Markku Sandell / Yle

Finns have bought many bicycles in the spring. Because of the corona situation, many hobbies ended in the spring. Many people started to take up cycling.

Bicycle shops are short of some bicycle models, because bicycles are still being bought in large numbers.

Bicycle companies have difficulty making enough bicycles, because there is a shortage of materials and parts. The corona situation around the world has caused difficulties for many companies and factories in getting parts and materials.
