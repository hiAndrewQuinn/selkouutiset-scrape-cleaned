# Monday 5.6.2017 (radio)

In Britain London Bridge is open again. Helsinki may become the world's busiest passenger port this year. The teaching of small immigrant children is changing in Helsinki. For Finns the best holiday month is July. In Lapland, people are skiing at the Saariselkä ski centre at the weekend.

## London Bridge

![London Bridge](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9648715)

London Bridge is open again. Photo: Will Oliver / EPA

In Britain London Bridge is open again. London Bridge is a famous bridge in central London.

The bridge was closed at the weekend because of the terror attack. Although people can again cross the bridge, the security of the area is being closely monitored.

The terrorists attacked on and near London Bridge on Saturday. The terrorists killed 7 people. About 50 people were injured.

## Helsinki's port

![Cruise ships at Helsinki's south cruise harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7304770)

Open image viewer

Helsinki is a popular passenger port. Photo: Jyki Lyytikkä / Yle

Helsinki may become the world's busiest passenger port this year.

The number of passengers has grown in Helsinki's port for many years already. A lot of passengers are also expected this summer.

Last year 11.6 million passengers used Helsinki's port. However, even more passengers used the port of Dover in Britain. In Dover there were 12.2 million passengers.

It is very possible that the number of passengers at Helsinki's port will rise above that of Dover's port this year.

## Teaching immigrant children

![Pupils in a primary school classroom, with a globe in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5302691)

Open image viewer

An immigrant child learns Finnish faster in a class that also has Finnish-speaking pupils. Photo: Yle

The teaching of small immigrant children is changing in Helsinki.

The separate groups for immigrant children in the first and second grades will be closed next autumn. After that they will be in the same classes as Finnish-speaking children.

At Meri-Rastila school they have already tried out how well the children's shared teaching groups work. There it has been noticed that immigrant children learn Finnish better among Finnish-speaking children.

That is why Helsinki is ending the separate classes for small immigrant children in other schools too.

## July is the holiday month

![A man lounging on the grass.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8978835)

Open image viewer

A Finn in July. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

For Finns the best holiday month is July.

Last year more than 30 percent of workers were on holiday in July. In June and August more than 10 percent of workers were on holiday.

The popularity of August holidays has grown. Last year there were already a little more workers on holiday in August than in June.

## Skiing in Lapland

![Saariselkä, Kiilopää, early June 2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9650689)

Open image viewer

Saariselkä in early June. Photo: Antti Mikkola / Yle

The summer holiday season has started in an unusual way in the north. In Lapland the Saariselkä ski centre is open at the weekend for skiers. There is still a lot of snow on the ski slopes.

A lot of foreign tourists come to Lapland in June. They want to hike in Lapland's nature. Now skis and snowshoes are offered to tourists at Saariselkä, because the ground is still covered in snow.
