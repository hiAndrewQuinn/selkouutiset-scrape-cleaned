# Wednesday 22.12.2021 (radio)

Restaurant corona restrictions are tightened. The use of the corona pass is partly suspended. Corona vaccinations are being stepped up. The Christmas traffic has started. A white Christmas is coming to the whole country.

## Restaurant restrictions

![Family and basic services minister Krista Kiuru at the government press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_318/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89533961c2d2886ce98/1640239945)

The government has decided on many new restrictions, with which it tries to curb Finland's difficult corona situation.

For example, restaurant restrictions are tightened in a large part of the country. From Christmas Eve, restaurants must close their doors at 22.00. Closing cannot be avoided with a corona pass.

From next Tuesday, restaurants must close their doors already at 18.00. Food restaurants may stay open 2 hours longer with a corona pass, but they may not sell alcohol either.

## Corona pass put on the shelf

The government has also decided that the use of the corona pass is partly suspended. The suspension applies, for example, to large mass events where people do not have their own seats.

The government recommends that places with a low risk of infection can still remain open. Such places include, for example, libraries, museums, theatres and events where people have their own seat.

In the end the regional state administrative agencies decide which places may remain open.

Restrictions are being tightened because there are many corona infections in Finland, and there are many corona patients in hospitals and in intensive care. Today almost 2900 new corona infections have been reported.

Corona vaccinations are to be stepped up.

Vaccinations will also start to be given to all 5 to 11-year-old children. Until now the age limit for vaccination has been 12 years. Younger children have been able to get the vaccine if they belong to a risk group.

From now on, laboratory nurses, practical nurses and basic nurses may also give vaccinations.

## Christmas traffic has started

![Police car next to the road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7706876)

Open image viewer

The police are monitoring Christmas traffic. Photo: Ville Välimäki / Yle

Christmas traffic has started today.

However, the busiest day of traffic is tomorrow, Thursday. The Traffic Centre estimates that traffic will be busy from around midday onwards.

The police say that during Christmas traffic they will especially monitor drunk driving, driving speeds, overtaking and driving distances.

## Snow also to the south

![Map of Finland's weather](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12244582)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

A white Christmas is coming to the whole country, says the Finnish Meteorological Institute.

Tomorrow it will snow across the whole country. In the south, however, the snow cover will remain thin.

In the southern and middle parts of the country it is a few degrees of frost. In the north the temperature is between about -10 and -16 degrees.
