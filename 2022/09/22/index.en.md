## Russian demonstrations

![Riot police lead a demonstrator away.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_135/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1011126632bcc0f908c2/1665915332)

Police detained demonstrators in Russia. Photo: Alexander Nemenov / AFP

There have been demonstrations in Russia.

There were demonstrations on Wednesday in many Russian cities. Police detained about 1,500 demonstrators.

The demonstrations began after President Vladimir Putin's speech. In the speech Putin said that Russia is calling conscripted Russians into the army.

Russia intends to call 300,000 people into the army. Russia wants more soldiers, because its war in Ukraine continues.

## Applicant numbers for nurse training

![Nurse Tuula Sorvari works in an intensive care ward.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4700,h_2644,x_0,y_294/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8669156167fc72404c6/1634893130)

Open image viewer

A nurse at work. Photo: Arash Matin / Yle

Young people are not interested in the care sector. There are fewer applicants for nurse training at universities of applied sciences than before.

This year there were about 2,500 applicants for nurse training. 2 years ago there were 1,200 more applicants.

The situation worries the universities of applied sciences. Poor pay in the care sector may be one reason why young people are not interested in the field.

## Detur's customers' problems

![Tourists on the beach in Antalya.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9032846)

Open image viewer

Turkey is a popular holiday destination. Photo: Tolga Bozoglu / EPA

Finnish and other Nordic tourists have had problems in Turkey.

The tourists' trips were arranged by the travel agency Detur. However, Detur has not paid Turkish hotels for the tourists' rooms. That is why the hotels have demanded that tourists pay for the rooms again.

Detur says that the travel agency is being sold. That is why it has payment defaults.

The Consumer Agency is investigating the situation.

A fire broke out on an oil ship on Thursday. The ship was at sea between Estonia and Finland.

No one was injured in the fire.

The fire was on the oil ship Alexia, which is 250 metres long. Alexia is on its way from Russia to Turkey.

## Para School Day

![Schoolchildren try playing wheelchair basketball.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10793894)

Open image viewer

Schoolchildren play basketball in wheelchairs. Photo: Jarmo Nuotio / Yle

Some schoolchildren are getting to know para sport this week. In many Swedish-speaking schools there is a Para School Day this week.

Schoolchildren get to try for themselves what para sport, that is, disability sport, feels like. For example, they play basketball in wheelchairs and try what it is like to play football when you cannot see anything.

When schoolchildren get to know para sport, they learn that there are many kinds of people.
