# Wednesday 14.4.2021 (radio)

The Astra Zeneca vaccine will not be given to people under 65. Restaurants open on Monday, but restrictions continue. Finland offers to host a summit between the USA and Russia. The Rauma shipyard has received a ship order.

## The Astra Zeneca vaccine

The Astra Zeneca corona vaccine is still not being given to people under 65 in Finland. The authority says that vaccinations will continue for people over 65.

The Astra Zeneca vaccine may cause blood clots. However, blood clots are very rare. Blood clots have been found in young people.

The Astra Zeneca vaccine has not been given to people under 65 for many weeks.

Restaurants and cafés may open their doors on Monday, after a closure that has lasted three weeks. However, serving alcohol must stop at 5 pm. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Restaurants will open again on Monday.

However, restaurants will also be strictly restricted next week. Yle's information says that in some regions, the sale of alcohol must stop at 5 pm. Bars must close at 6 pm, and food restaurants at 7 pm. These restrictions will apply at least in Uusimaa and Southwest Finland.

The government has not yet decided on new restaurant restrictions. The government is negotiating, among other things, restrictions on restaurant opening hours on Wednesday evening. A decision will be made later this week.

Restaurants have been closed for 6 weeks in a large part of Finland.

## A summit?

![Former US Vice President Joe Biden and Russian Prime Minister Vladimir Putin met in Moscow in 2011.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11883309)

Finland is ready to host a summit between Russia and the USA.

The Office of the President of the Republic told Iltalehti that Finland can host a summit between Vladimir Putin and Joe Biden. Finland has told the USA and Russia that the summit could be held in Finland.

Biden has proposed a meeting with Putin somewhere in a country. Nothing has yet been decided about the summit.

Putin met former US President Donald Trump in Helsinki in 2018.

## Rauma shipyard

The Rauma shipyard is building 2 new ships.

A shipping company from Tasmania has ordered 2 new car ferries from the Rauma shipyard. The Rauma shipyard will start building the ships next spring.

The Rauma shipyard has now ordered 4 ships in total. Building the ships gives work to a large number of people.
