# Friday 30.12.2016 (radio)

Prison sentence for an assault at the station square. The number of people killed in traffic accidents has risen. Party support. Dangerous fireworks.

## Assault at the station square brought two years in prison

![Jesse Torniainen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9379719)

Open image viewer

Jesse Torniainen was sentenced to prison for an assault that led to a death. Photo: Yle

The Helsinki District Court has given its ruling on the assault at the station square in September.

Jesse Torniainen, a member of the neo-Nazi Resistance Movement, was given a two (2) year prison sentence for aggravated assault. The court says that a jump kick to the victim's chest knocked the victim over and caused a skull injury. The victim later died of the injuries.

The District Court rejected the charge of aggravated manslaughter.

The prosecutor is not satisfied with the ruling and intends to appeal to the Court of Appeal.

## More and more fatal crashes are caused by drunk drivers

![A car that crashed after running off the road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8207387)

Open image viewer

The share of drunk drivers in fatal crashes grew in 2015. Photo: Keijo Salokangas / Yle

281 people died in traffic accidents last year. That is 39 more than in 2014.

Most of the deaths happened in motor traffic. In accidents involving light traffic, 30 pedestrians and 29 cyclists died.

Serious drunk driving has increased. Last year a drunk driver caused every fourth fatal crash. In 2014 a drunk driver caused every sixth fatal crash.

## SDP support is highest in the year's last poll

![Graphic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9379328)

Open image viewer

This is what Yle News' last support poll of the year looks like. Photo: Taloustutkimus / Yle Uutisgrafiikka

The political year ends with the SDP at the top of the support poll. Taloustutkimus has made a survey for Yle News. In it, the SDP's support is 21.2 per cent.

The survey now measures, and in early next year it will measure, support for the parties in the municipal elections.

In the survey the Centre Party is in second place. Its support is 20.3 per cent. The National Coalition Party's support is 17.4, the Greens' 13.3 and the Finns Party's 9.4 per cent. The Left Alliance's support is 7.6 per cent, the Swedish People's Party's (RKP) 4.9 and the Christian Democrats' 3.6 per cent.

The municipal elections will be held in April.

## Fireworks have dangerous instructions

![firework package](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9379105)

Open image viewer

Many firework cakes have a sticker that advises pointing the firework the wrong way. Photo: Suomen Ilotulitus Oy

Dangerous fireworks have been sold in shops.

The Safety and Chemicals Agency, Tukes, warns that many fireworks have wrong instructions. For example, Suomen Ilotulitus has imported firework cakes with an incorrect instruction. If the instruction is followed, the firework flies towards the shooter and the audience.

Incorrect instructions are found at least on the products Finlandia, Paratiisin valloitus, Maanjäristys, Megabosse and Superbosse. The cakes have an extra yellow sticker that says THIS SIDE TOWARDS THE AUDIENCE. The sticker, however, is stuck where the firework must be pointed towards the sky.

Tukes says that the faulty products must be returned to the place of purchase. About 300 faulty products have been sold.
