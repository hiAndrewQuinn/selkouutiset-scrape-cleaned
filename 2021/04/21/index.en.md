# Wednesday 21.4.2021 (radio)

Corona restrictions are being lifted little by little. A 16-year-old is suspected of stabbing at Helsinki railway station. Fewer roads are being repaired than last summer. Summer cottages are being rented at a record pace. The weather.

## Lifting corona restrictions

![Annika Saarikko, Maria Ohisalo and Sanna Marin on the steps of the Säätytalo as the government's mid-term retreat begins on 21 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_117/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-796995607ff28fbcaba/1620287581)

The government has announced the lifting of corona restrictions.

The government's plan is that group hobbies for children and young people outdoors can start as early as this month. Group hobbies for adults outdoors can start in May.

Libraries and museums will also open in May. In June, gathering restrictions and restaurant restrictions will be eased.

At the turn of May and June, restrictions on border traffic may also be lifted. First, movement will be eased for people who live in the north, near the Swedish and Norwegian borders.

Corona restrictions are being lifted a little at a time. They will only be lifted if the corona situation does not get worse.

## New vaccination guidelines

![Astra Zeneca vaccine bottle.18.3.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3736,h_2102,x_0,y_41/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78592460545a6e33c3a/1616141024)

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare has issued new guidelines on corona vaccinations. Now people aged 65 to 69 can choose whether they take the Astra Zeneca or the Pfizer corona vaccine.

Many people do not want the Astra Zeneca vaccine, because it has been suspected that a few people may get a blood clot from it.

The Astra Zeneca corona vaccine is given in Finland only to people over 65.

## Railway station stabbing

![Helsinki Central Railway Station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11892629)

Helsinki Central Railway Station. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The police have been investigating the stabbing that happened yesterday at Helsinki Central Railway Station.

There was a brawl at the station. A 24-year-old man was stabbed to death in the brawl.

Yesterday the police said that several minors are suspected of murder. Now only one suspect remains. The suspect is a 16-year-old boy, whom the police caught yesterday.

## Road repairs

![Asphalt road being patched.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6418265e43ee613556b/1581511722)

Open image viewer

Asphalt is being patched. Photo: Jarkko Heikkinen / Yle

Fewer roads will be repaired this summer than last summer. About 40 percent less repair and resurfacing work will be done than last year.

About 2,500 kilometres of new surfacing, that is asphalt, will be laid, compared with about 4,000 kilometres laid last year.

Most of this summer's resurfacing work will start in May. Road works will be carried out on about 500 road sections in various parts of the country.

## Renting summer cottages

![Summer cottage in Asikkala.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_573/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6715255eb286807eb42/1588758240)

Summer cottage by the lake shore. Photo: Juha-Petri Koponen / Yle

Finns are renting cottages for the summer at a record pace.

Lomarengas Oy says that 30 percent more of its cottages are booked now than at this time last year, and 50 percent more than the year before last.

For example, there is strong demand for seaside cottages in Southern Finland.
