# Wednesday 9.12.2020 (radio)

The work of Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto has been investigated. On Wednesday 490 new coronavirus infections were reported in Finland. Estonia is closing schools because of the coronavirus. Finland is introducing the Finentry app for travellers. The weather is cloudy and dry.

## The Foreign Minister's actions

![Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto on his way to be heard by the Constitutional Law Committee of Parliament in Helsinki on 14 January 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11688084)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The actions of Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto have been investigated. The Constitutional Law Committee of Parliament, that is, of the Finnish Parliament, finished the investigation on Wednesday.

According to the Constitutional Law Committee, Haavisto does not need to be charged in court. The Constitutional Law Committee says that Haavisto's mistakes were not so big that he must be taken to court.

The investigation is related to the al-Hol camp in Syria. The camp holds family members of Islamic State terrorists, including Finns. That is why in Finland there has been an argument about whether Finland should help the Finns at al-Hol or not.

Foreign Minister Haavisto has made some decisions in the matter. Now it has been investigated whether Haavisto's decisions broke the law.

The whole of Parliament will still discuss the matter on Friday.

## Estonia closes schools

Estonia, Finland's neighbouring country, is closing its schools because of the coronavirus.

Schools will close on Monday. Estonian pupils will have a longer Christmas holiday than usual.

The number of coronavirus infections began to grow in Estonia at the end of October. Now Estonia already has more new coronavirus infections than Finland.

## An app for travellers

![A sign with coronavirus guidelines in the passport control hall at Vaalimaa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5124,h_2882,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7513795fce35b2bd5c1/1607350150)

Open image viewer

It is now quiet at the Finland-Russia border because of the coronavirus. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

Finland will soon introduce a new Finentry app. The app will make it easier to come to Finland.

For example, the border between Finland and Russia is closed because of the coronavirus, but more than 1,000 people cross the border every day. They are, for example, lorry drivers or travellers who have a residence permit in Finland.

With the Finentry app they can book a time for a coronavirus test in different parts of Finland. So a traveller can get a coronavirus test in the place where they are going.

The coronavirus test is important for travellers. If the coronavirus test shows that the traveller does not have the coronavirus, the quarantine period gets shorter.

The Finentry app will be introduced at the Finland-Russia border perhaps at the end of January. It is planned to be introduced at Helsinki airport in December.
