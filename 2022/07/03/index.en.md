## Healthcare

There is now a severe shortage of workers in social and health care. Not enough substitutes have been found, and hospitals have closed wards more than in previous summers.

For example, the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district reports that probably only urgent care will be arranged.

There is also a shortage of workers because during the corona pandemic not everyone took holidays.

## Drones

![Joby Aviation's eVTOL aircraft, designed for taxi flight operations. The device's test flight in California on 6.7.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8148,h_4583,x_0,y_692/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-932655623c48a467df2/1648118211)

Open image viewer

The American company Joby Aviation intends to carry passengers by air taxi between airports and competition venues at the Paris Summer Olympics in 2024. Photo: Joby Aviation

Drones may already fly regularly in Central European cities in the coming years. Drones, that is, small multicopters, can carry goods and people.

Drones run on electricity, so they are an environmentally friendly option for many transports.

Next year a new regulation of the European Union comes into force. It makes automatic control of drones possible.

The EU is studying with test flights how the traffic of drones and ordinary aircraft will be controlled together. In Finland the matter is being studied in Oulu, Tampere and the capital region.

## Cinemas

![grey and bluish seats in a cinema hall](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_650/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-932761623c638886419/1648126484)

Empty cinemas are now history. The corona pandemic has eased, and people are going to films again. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

Finns have returned to cinemas after a quiet start to the year.

Statistics show that last year fewer people went to the cinema than ever. However, last month as many people went to the cinema as in June 2019.

People also look for experiences in theatres that they do not get if they watch a film on the home sofa.

People also come to the cinema to cool down when it is hot outside.

## Prenuptial agreements

![A prenuptial agreement can also be made during the marriage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7807744)

Open image viewer

A prenuptial agreement can also be made during a marriage or registered partnership. Photo: Antti Pylväs / Yle

The popularity of prenuptial agreements is growing. Nowadays over 30 percent of married couples make a prenuptial agreement.

With a prenuptial agreement the spouses' property, or part of it, is excluded from the marital right. In a marriage or a registered partnership the spouses have a marital right to each other's property.

Without a prenuptial agreement a couple's property is divided equally when the couple divorces or one of them dies.

About 22,000 marriages are concluded a year. At the same time prenuptial agreements have been drawn up just over 8,000 a year.

## Dry terrain

![Metsähallitus's restoration burning, that is, a controlled forest fire.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4320,h_2430,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82524060d45e01013fa/1656499657)

Open image viewer

A forest fire can start even from something small, for example from a cigarette or a match. Photo: Ari Lahtinen / Metsähallitus

The terrain in Finland is dangerously dry. The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that thunder showers alone are not enough to wet the dry terrain.

A forest fire warning is in force across the whole country.

It is possible that the forest fire warning will remain in force for many more days if proper rain does not come.
