Olli Rehn and Marja Nykänen are the new directors of the Bank of Finland. A researcher says that parents should read aloud to small children. A year of mourning has begun in Thailand. There are many cheap immigrant barbers in the Helsinki area. Soon you can grow truffles in your yard.

## Rehn and Nykänen to the Bank of Finland

![Marja Nykänen and Olli Rehn ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9230187)

Marja Nykänen and Olli Rehn have been chosen as directors of the Bank of Finland.

Olli Rehn is currently Finland's Minister of Economic Affairs, and Marja Nykänen is deputy director at the Financial Supervisory Authority, which supervises banks. Rehn and Nykänen will start at the Bank of Finland in February.

A new minister will be chosen for the government in Rehn's place. The Centre Party will choose the new minister in a couple of weeks.

## Read to your child!

![A young person leafs through a book.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5315661)

Open image viewer

Good reading skills are important in life. Photo: Yle

A researcher says that mothers and fathers should read more to small children.

Nowadays only 25 percent of parents read aloud to their children every day. Researcher Päivi Heikkilä-Halttunen says that young people in Finland are now worse at reading than before. If a child does not read, they are more easily excluded from society as an adult than a child who reads a lot.

## A year of mourning began in Thailand

![A crying woman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9230326)

Open image viewer

A woman mourns the king. Photo: Mika Mäkeläinen / Yle

A year of mourning has begun in Thailand because of the king's death.

The government of Thailand hopes that the people will not hold parties for at least a month. The mourning period will probably reduce consumption and quieten the entertainment industry. Finland's foreign minister Timo Soini says that Finnish tourists should also respect the Thai mourning period and behave calmly.

King Bhumibol's coffin was taken from the hospital to the royal palace in Bangkok. The coffin was escorted by his son the prince and by Buddhist monks. The king died yesterday, Thursday, at the age of 88.

## Price competition among barbers

![Arkan Ashraf, owner, Salon Holiday](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9217097)

Open image viewer

Akran Ashraf. Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

There are many new immigrant barber and hair salons in the Helsinki area. They have got a lot of customers because of their cheap prices.

Akran Ashraf cuts men's hair for 8 euros. He says that a cheap price is important to customers.

Many old barber shops think that the new barbers' prices are too cheap. Because of the tough price competition, some barbers have had to close.

## Truffles from your own yard

![Truffle is grated onto food](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9216936)

Open image viewer

You can grate truffle on top of food. Photo: Samuli Holopainen / Yle

Soon you can grow truffles in your home yard.

The truffle is the most expensive edible mushroom in the world. In Juva, in Eastern Finland, there is a research centre that studies and grows truffles. Next spring the Juva truffle centre will start selling tree seedlings with truffle mycelium added to their roots. When you plant the tree in your yard, truffles will also start to grow from its roots about 5 years later.

Truffles usually grow in the forest, under the ground. People search for them with the help of a dog or a pig. The animal smells the truffle under the ground. A kilo of truffles costs many thousands of euros.
