## Finland's corona situation

![A worker wearing a face mask and rubber gloves fills a syringe from a corona vaccine bottle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5261,h_2959,x_10,y_244/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89309061bb3e5959452/1639661356)

The corona situation in Finland is easing. There are fewer corona patients in hospitals and in intensive care than before. Corona deaths have also turned to a decline.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that there are now under 500 corona patients in Finnish hospitals. Of them, 15 are in intensive care.

More than 600 corona deaths were registered in April, but under 100 in May.

Corona infections are still many, however.

THL is expanding its recommendation on corona vaccinations. THL now recommends a fourth corona vaccination for people aged 70 and over who are at risk of getting a serious form of corona disease.

## Party support

![Party support poll](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1682,h_946,x_192,y_69/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96345262974394c39a9/1654140703)

The National Coalition Party is still the most popular party.

In Yle's opinion poll people were asked which party they support. About 26 per cent of the respondents supported the National Coalition Party.

The SDP was second most popular. Its support was almost 19 per cent.

The support for the Finns Party was about 15 per cent and for the Centre Party about 12 per cent. Of the respondents, 9 per cent support the Greens and 8 per cent the Left Alliance.

## Russia banned Yle's website

![Yle's flags flutter, with Yle's link tower in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4679,h_2631,x_883,y_351/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6338955e283c1e68d9c/1655270720)

Open image viewer

Yle's link tower in Western Pasila, Helsinki. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

Yle's website can no longer be accessed in Russia.

Russia has blocked access to Yle's pages, because Russia's communications supervisory agency has added the Yle.fi pages to the register of banned sites.

The register of banned sites also includes, for example, Helsingin Sanomat and Ilta-Sanomat, the Danish newspaper Politiken, the British broadcaster BBC and the German Deutsche Welle.

Access to the sites has been blocked because they report on Russia's war in Ukraine.

## Britain celebrates the queen

![Queen Elizabeth II](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2662,h_1490,x_0,y_55/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9643046298a4fac61a2/1662695020)

Britain is celebrating Queen Elizabeth. Elizabeth has now been in power for 70 years. The reign is being celebrated for 4 days.

The celebration began today in London with a military and music parade. Tomorrow a thanksgiving service will be held at St Paul's Cathedral, and on Saturday a concert by Buckingham Palace. On Sunday millions of Britons will go to parks for the queen's celebration picnic.

Queen Elizabeth II is now 96 years old. She ascended the throne at the age of 26 in 1952.
