# Saturday 20.1.2018 (radio)

There has been a bus accident in Turkey. The Kemi Snow Castle has opened. Youth lasts longer today than before. Downhill skater Mirko Lahti won a junior world series race. The freezing weather will end next week.

## Bus accident in Turkey

![Rescue authorities at the scene of the accident in Eskisehir, Turkey.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10032954)

A bus drove into trees in Turkey. Photo: Emrah Yasar / EPA

There has been a bad bus accident in Turkey. At least 11 people died and more than 40 people were injured.

The accident happened early on Saturday morning. The bus was on its way from Ankara to Bursa when it crashed into trees.

Most of the bus passengers were on a skiing holiday. They were on their way to a ski resort.

It is not yet known what caused the accident. The police have arrested both of the bus drivers.

This is the 23rd time that a big snow castle has been built in Kemi.

## A long youth

![Young people at a colour festival.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10032264)

Open image viewer

Youth lasts a long time today. Photo: Marton Monus / EPA

Youth lasts longer today than before. This is what British researchers say.

Youth begins at puberty, and today puberty begins 4 years earlier than before. The researchers say that today puberty begins already at the age of 10.

Puberty begins earlier because nutrition has improved. During puberty, a person grows into a biological adult.

The researchers say that youth also lasts longer. Today youth ends only at the age of 24, which is 5 years later than before.

Youth ends later because young people study for longer and have children later than before. The brain also still develops after the age of 20.

However, the researchers remind that young people must not be treated as children.

## A Finn won

![downhill skating st. paul](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8706132)

Open image viewer

Downhill skating is very fast. Photo: EPA

A young Finnish athlete, Mirko Lahti, has done well. Lahti is a downhill skater.

Lahti won the first race of the junior world series. The race was in Saint Paul, in the USA.

In downhill skating, the skaters go down a race track at high speed. The speed can be more than 80 kilometres an hour.

Mirko Lahti also competes in the men's series. Lahti started the men's race well and got into the final race. On Sunday we will see how well Lahti does in the men's final.

## The weather changes

![People in a snowstorm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10032982)

Open image viewer

The snowy weather will continue for a few more days. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The cold weather will end next week. On Monday and Tuesday there will still be frost in the whole country, about minus 15 degrees in the south and as much as minus 30 degrees in the north.

On Wednesday the weather will get warmer, and on Thursday the temperature will be above zero in almost the whole country. It will also rain in southern Finland.
