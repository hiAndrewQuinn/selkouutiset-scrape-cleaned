## Food prices

![Grocery shop in Stockholm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4096,h_2304,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10859236412c91c52202/1679657393)

Food prices have fallen for the first time in a long while. Photo: AOP

Food prices have turned downwards after a long rise.

Last month, that is in April, food prices fell 1.2 percent from March. During the month, food prices fell more than at any time since 2019.

Food is, however, much more expensive than a year ago. Since April last year, food prices have risen by almost 14 percent. However, the pace of price rises has slowed.

## Government negotiations

![Government formateur Petteri Orpo at the House of Estates.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4999,h_2811,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1112539645de235885e0/1684127682)

Government negotiations continued on Monday. Government formateur, the National Coalition's Petteri Orpo, says that savings are being negotiated carefully. Orpo says that the questions about savings in social and health care and in their structures are more difficult.

Orpo says that the new government programme will be ready in June, at the latest before Midsummer.

Experts estimate to Yle that the government may make cuts, for example to the index increases of benefits and to housing allowance.

## Turkish presidential elections

![Opposition candidate in the Turkish presidential elections Kemal Kılıçdaroğlu giving a speech.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2696,h_1516,x_0,y_85/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-111362464617f409ea57/1684111350)

Open image viewer

Leader of the Turkish opposition Kemal Kılıçdaroğlu. Photo: Manuel de Almeida / EPA

Votes have been counted in the Turkish presidential elections. The elections were held on Sunday.

Turkey's current president Recep Tayyip Erdogan led the vote count on Monday during the day, but Erdogan did not get half of the votes. That is why there will be a second round in the Turkish presidential elections.

Erdogan's rival, opposition leader Kemal Kılıçdaroğlu, also said on Monday during the day that there will be a second round in the Turkish presidential elections.

## Finns turned back at the Russian border

![Traffic coming from Russia at the Nuijamaa border station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5706,h_3209,x_0,y_614/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1034189637361ba8c490/1699799093)

Open image viewer

Russia has turned back EU citizens at the border. Photo: Kalle Purhonen / Yle

Russia has turned back Finns at border crossings in Southeast Finland. Russia has turned back a few dozen EU citizens in a few days.

Many people from Southeast Finland have gone to Russia to fill up their tanks and shop. Russian authorities have annulled Finns' visas.

A visa is often needed for a business trip, for example. Russian border guards may have started following stricter instructions on checking visas. Previously Finns were also able to go shopping with business trip visas.

## Midnight sun

![A landscape where the sun shines near the horizon over a river bay on a summer night. The trees have leaves and the grass is green.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_169/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6955465efd6c4f93d27/1684138947)

Open image viewer

In the north the sun shines in summer also at night. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

In Northern Finland the midnight sun begins on Tuesday. That means the sun shines continuously for over two months.

The next time the sun sets in Utsjoki will be on 28 July.

The boundary of the midnight sun runs north of the Kemi–Kuusamo line.
