## Elisa's telephone sales staff have lied

![Elisa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11164468)

Open image viewer

Elisa's telephone sales staff have lied to customers. Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

Telecom operator Elisa's telephone sales staff have lied to customers.

The sales staff have lied when they sold faster speeds for broadband connections.

The salespeople told the customer that neighbours had complained that the internet was slow. Then the salespeople offered faster internet.

In reality, nobody had complained. The salesperson had made up the story himself.

Telecom operator Elisa says that the salespeople have used this sales trick for a year.

Elisa apologises for the fact that the telephone sales staff lied

## The EU is planning a fund for airlines

![A Thomas Cook plane at the airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_186/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5931475d88271c610f0/1569204229)

Open image viewer

Over 30 airlines have gone bankrupt in recent years. Photo: All Over Press

The EU is planning a new rule for airlines.

The EU says that airlines must have a fund in case of bankruptcy. If an airline goes bankrupt, the fund will pay compensation to the people who have bought a flight ticket.

Over 30 airlines have gone bankrupt in recent years. Bankruptcy means that the company runs out of money and can no longer continue its operations.

## A new virus is spreading

![A closed fish market in Wuhan on 12 January.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11164479)

Open image viewer

The Chinese suspect that the virus is spreading from the fish market in the city of Wuhan. Photo: Noel Celis / AFP

International researchers estimate that there are many people in China who have a new virus infection.

China says that 50 people have fallen ill. International researchers, on the other hand, say that perhaps as many as 1,700 people have fallen ill.

The new virus causes symptoms similar to pneumonia.

The World Health Organization warns that the virus is spreading.

There are also people who have fallen ill with this disease in Thailand and Japan.

2 people have died in China.

## Britain is preparing for Brexit

![The British flag flutters in front of Big Ben, which is surrounded by scaffolding.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_206/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6309815e1dcc1d5954f/1579011213)

Britain leaves the European Union on 31 January at 23:00. Photo: AOP

Britain is preparing for Brexit, that is, leaving the European Union.

Britain leaves the EU on the last day of January at 23:00. Britain is celebrating the departure in many ways.

For example, it is issuing a 50 pence commemorative coin.

The coin says: Peace, prosperity and friendship with all nations.
