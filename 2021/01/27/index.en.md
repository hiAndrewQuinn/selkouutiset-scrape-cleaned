# Wednesday 27.1.2021 (radio)

Finland has tightened passenger restrictions. Finnair requires proof of a corona test for flights to Finland from abroad. Corona infections are growing in Finland. More than 100 million corona infections have already been found in the whole world. The weather turns colder and it snows.

## Strict restrictions for passengers

Finland has tightened restrictions on passengers. The new restrictions came into force today, on Wednesday.

The restrictions concern the arrival of foreigners in Finland. For example, commuter traffic to Finland almost completely ends.

Only workers whose work is essential, for example health care workers, may come to Finland. Construction workers, however, cannot now cross the border into Finland.

A corona test is to be given to everyone who can still get into Finland. A corona testing point can be found, for example, at all the Helsinki passenger ports.

Finland is tightening restrictions on passengers because of the corona virus variant. Finland does not want the virus variant to come to Finland with passengers. The virus variant spreads easily to people.

The restrictions will last for a month, that is, until 25 February.

## Finnair requires a corona test

![In the picture a flight attendant works on a Finnair flight AY 435 from Helsinki to Oulu in September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7187735f5f8d5138960/1600172549)

A passenger can only get onto a Finnair flight to Finland if they have a certificate of a corona test. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The rules are also being tightened for air traffic.

From Thursday onwards a passenger must have a certificate of a corona test if they travel to Finland from abroad with Finnair.

Finnair requires a negative corona test result from all passengers, including Finnish citizens. Without a certificate the passenger cannot get onto a Finnair aeroplane.

The new rules only apply to Finnair flights from abroad to Finland. A certificate is not needed on Finnair's domestic flights.

THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, follows the number of corona infections in Finland.

The number of corona infections is growing in Finland.

On Wednesday almost 500 new corona infections were reported.

In the last 2 weeks about 800 more corona infections have been reported than in the previous 2 weeks.

On Wednesday 5 new corona deaths were also reported. A total of 660 people have now died of corona in Finland.
