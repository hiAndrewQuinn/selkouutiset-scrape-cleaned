## Corona restrictions on the Russian border

The border from Russia to Finland opened when corona restrictions ended. Photo: Kare Lehtonen / Yle

Russia has ended corona restrictions at the Finnish border. Russians can again travel to Finland without corona restrictions.

Finland ended corona restrictions at the Finland–Russia border already at the start of July.

Traffic on the Finland–Russia border grows as corona restrictions end. Russians come to Finland again, for example to shop.

In the near future it will become clear how soon and how much traffic at Finland's eastern border grows.

## Finland's NATO membership

![Jens Stoltenberg holds Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership applications in his hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4346,h_2444,x_0,y_258/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9570556285da8530efe/1668670850)

Open image viewer

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg received Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership applications in May. Photo: Johanna Geron / EPA

Half of NATO countries have confirmed Finland's and Sweden's NATO memberships.

15 NATO countries have already approved that Finland and Sweden join NATO. There are 30 member countries in NATO.

All NATO countries must approve Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership for the membership to take effect.

Finland and Sweden decided in May to apply for membership of the defence alliance NATO. Finland and Sweden decided on the matter after Russia attacked Ukraine.

## Housing fair

![A housing fair area in Naantali.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4333,h_2437,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98223062d02d7c147be/1657810385)

Open image viewer

Houses in the Naantali housing fair area. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

The housing fair has started. At the housing fair Finnish construction is presented.

This year the housing fair is in Naantali. There are 36 new homes in the fair area, which guests can visit.

The themes of the housing fair this year are happiness and respect for nature. The houses are on Luonnonmaa island in the middle of nature. The president's summer residence Kultaranta is also nearby.

The Naantali housing fair continues for a month. Guests can get to know the homes in the fair area until 14 August.

The housing fair is held every year. The first housing fair was held in 1970.

## Walrus

![A walrus lies on a beach between boats.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12537842)

Open image viewer

The walrus rested on the Hamina beach. Photo: Kymenlaakso Rescue Department

A walrus has visited the Hamina beach. The case is very rare, because the walrus is not one of Finland's animals. Walruses live, for example, in the Arctic Ocean.

The walrus perhaps got lost and swam many hundreds of kilometres through the Danish straits to Finland. The walrus was in poor condition, and attempts were made to help it. For example, a veterinarian came to the scene.

On Friday afternoon the walrus perked up and swam back out to sea, but later it came back close to the shore. The police say that people should not go near the walrus.
