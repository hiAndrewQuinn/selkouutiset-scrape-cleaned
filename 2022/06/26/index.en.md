# Sunday 26.6.2022 (radio)

Midsummer traffic. Midsummer celebrations. People with partial work capacity. Ukraine. Heat.

## Midsummer return traffic

![People queue to get on buses.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_213/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97462662b85b4bb3100/1656249314)

Return traffic from Midsummer has been busy on Sunday. Many long-distance trains and buses have been full.

Track work has seriously delayed train traffic on the main line on Sunday. The reason is that the track work at Toijala was not finished on time. That is why trains could not run between Tampere and Toijala.

People had to be transported by buses. Thousands of passengers had a long wait for their train journey.

## Midsummer went well

![Midsummer cottage atmospheres 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6008,h_3379,x_8,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97446462b5c31c6f6fb/1656080347)

Midsummer has gone quite calmly in Finland. For example, the police in Eastern Finland say that Midsummer Day, Saturday, was calmer than Midsummer Eve, Friday. The police in Western Uusimaa say that the number of tasks was as many as usual for a weekend.

There have been no serious problems. For example, the Midsummer festivals went well.

The Coast Guard in Western Finland says that for the Coast Guard, Midsummer was busy. The Coast Guard had 15 sea rescue incidents at Midsummer. During Midsummer 5 people drowned.

## People with partial work capacity

![Jyri Lähteenmäki arranges dishes going into the dishwasher in the restaurant's dish room.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2726,h_1533,x_0,y_175/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-864347615ff354bd5c4/1633678806)

Open image viewer

Työkanava helps people who have difficulty getting jobs into work. Photo: Silva Nieminen / Yle

In the autumn a new company starts in Finland that brokers work to people with partial work capacity. The company is owned by the Finnish state. The company's name is Työkanava Oy.

The company employs unemployed people with partial work capacity. People with partial work capacity can be, for example, disabled people, people with long-term illnesses, or people who have had a crisis in their own life.

Työkanava starts brokering work next autumn. Työkanava's customers can be companies, the state, municipalities and private individuals.

## Ukraine

![Rescue workers descend from a crane onto an apartment block destroyed by bombing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7999,h_4499,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97459862b8266619d6e/1656235751)

Rescue workers descend onto an apartment block that was hit by a missile. Photo: Roman Pilipey / EPA

Russia has fired missiles at Ukraine's capital Kyiv. At least 1 person died and 5 people were wounded when Russia fired 4 missiles at Kyiv on Sunday morning.

At least one missile hit a residential building. There may still be people in the ruins of the building.

Russia has not fired missiles at Kyiv for 3 weeks. Ukraine estimates that Russia fired missiles at Kyiv again, because Russia perhaps wants to intimidate Ukraine before the NATO summit, which starts on Tuesday.

## Hot weather

![Monday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12511198)

Open image viewer

The temperature on Monday is over 30 degrees in many places.

Finland now has heat, that is, very hot weather. The UV radiation from the sun is strong. That is why skin must be protected from the sun. The Finnish Meteorological Institute forecasts that the heat will continue in Finland next week too. Temperatures will rise to about 30 degrees in many places.
