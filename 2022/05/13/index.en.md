## Foreign comments on NATO

Foreign countries have commented on Finland's NATO application.

On Thursday it became clear that Finland is applying for membership of the military alliance NATO. Then President Sauli Niinistö and Prime Minister Sanna Marin said that they support the application. The decision on the application will be made in the coming days.

Russia does not like Finland's decision. Russia intends to strengthen the border against Finland. Russia may bring radar equipment to the border, for example.

Germany and other big NATO countries promise to support Finland during the application period. Britain promises to support Finland even militarily, if Finland is attacked.

The NATO country Turkey's President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan thinks it is a mistake if Finland and Sweden are taken into NATO.

Sweden is also considering a NATO application. Sweden's Foreign Minister Ann Linde says that Finland's decision affects Sweden too.

Finland and Sweden began to consider NATO membership when Russia attacked Ukraine.

## Food prices

![Coffee packets of 450 and 500 grams](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3999,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90644061eff7484cc65/1643116413)

Open image viewer

The price of coffee has risen a lot. Photo: Petri Kivimäki / Yle

Food is getting more expensive in Finland.

Pellervo Economic Research estimates that the price of food will rise by 11 per cent this year. Prices have last risen this fast about 60 years ago.

The prices of coffee and fish have risen the most. Edible oils and margarines have also become much more expensive.

The reasons for the price rises are the corona crisis and Russia's war in Ukraine. Ukraine is an important producer of grain and vegetable oils, but because of the war, food transports from Ukraine do not work.
