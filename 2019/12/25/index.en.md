# Christmas Day, Wednesday 25.12.2019 (radio)

Christmas has been spent calmly in Finland. The situation is getting worse on the Syria–Turkey border. Christmas waste is good to sort. The weather is getting colder.

Christmas has been spent calmly in Finland. There have been no serious accidents or crimes. The police say that Tuesday, Christmas Eve, was calmer than a normal weekend.

Most Finns spend Christmas with family or friends. For many, the Christmas celebration begins on Christmas Eve, after the Christmas peace has been declared in Turku. Christmas Eve and Christmas Day are a time of quiet and rest for many Finns.

## Situation in Syria

![Residents inspect the air strike area on the outskirts of Jubass village on 24 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11134422)

An air strike caused destruction in Idlib province on Tuesday. Photo: Muhammad Haj Kadour / AFP

The situation in Syria has got worse. It has got worse in the area close to the Turkish border.

Russia carried out an attack on the area on Tuesday. 8 people died in the attack. Among them were 5 children.

The attack happened in Idlib province. It is the last area that the Syrian rebels control.

Russia carried out the attack because it supports the Syrian government. The two countries have recently been bombing Idlib together too.

The fighting between the Syrian government and the rebels has continued for years. Many Syrians have fled the country because of the fighting.

Syria's neighbour Turkey says that new refugees are now coming from Syria because of the fighting in Idlib. Turkey warns that it cannot handle the refugee situation alone.

## Sorting Christmas waste

![Junk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11128507)

Open image viewer

Christmas decorations can be used for many years. That saves money and nature. Photo: Heikki Haapalainen / Yle

A lot of waste is created in homes at Christmas time. It is good to sort the waste before taking it to the waste containers.

Expert Hanna Tukiainen gives advice on sorting Christmas waste. She works at the Helsinki Region Environmental Services.

Tukiainen says that, for example, glass candle lanterns must go into mixed waste. The glass of candle lanterns does not fit in glass recycling.

Gift wrapping paper does not fit in paper recycling either. It usually contains dyes, tape and stickers. That is why it must go into mixed waste.

Christmas decorations are also usually mixed waste. If more than half of a decoration is metal, it belongs in metal recycling.

The best way to reduce waste is to save decorations and paper for next Christmas.

## The weather in Finland

![Frozen reed bed](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10659901)

On Friday there is frost across the whole of Finland. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The weather is getting colder. On Thursday, the second Christmas day, that is St Stephen's Day, is spent in frosty weather across much of Finland.

On Friday there is frost across the whole country. In the south it is under 5 degrees of frost, and in Lapland it is even over 20 degrees of frost. Because of the cold north wind, the weather feels very cold.

Happy rest of Christmas!
