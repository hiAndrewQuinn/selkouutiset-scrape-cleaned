# Tuesday 21.2.2017 (radio)

Slippery conditions have surprised people and hospitals. Norovirus is spreading in different parts of Finland. A new language minority has been born in Finland. Norppalive is coming this year too.

## Slippery conditions surprised hospitals

![walking in slippery weather.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9470831)

Open image viewer

Many people have fallen in the slippery weather and ended up in hospital.  Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

It is now so slippery outside that you can easily fall and get hurt. Many people who have slipped have come to hospitals at least in the Helsinki region, in Rauma and Pori in Satakunta and in Lahti in Päijät-Häme.

Because of the slippery weather, hospitals have become crowded. You may now have to wait a really long time for treatment. There are long queues in laboratories and X-ray departments.

In the Helsinki region, for example, people are advised to use anti-slip grips on their shoes or to wear studded shoes.

## A bad stomach bug is spreading in Finland

![a woman vomits into a toilet bowl](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8925812)

Open image viewer

Norovirus is a stomach bug that can make you vomit badly.  Photo: Mari Kahila / Yle

Many people in Finland now have a stomach bug called norovirus. When a person has norovirus, their stomach hurts, they feel sick and they may vomit.

Norovirus spreads easily, for example by shaking hands, from a table surface or from a toilet. That is why you must wash your hands properly, for example in the toilet and before you start eating.

In addition to washing your hands, you can use hand sanitiser. It helps with cleaning. If you get norovirus, it is important to drink enough. Then it is good to eat especially soup.

## It is worth speaking Finnish to an immigrant

![Course](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6310075)

Open image viewer

A Finn must try to speak Finnish to an immigrant and not English.  Photo: Yle

There is now a new language minority in Finland that has been born from immigration. The common language of the new language minority is Finnish, but the language has been learned only as an adult. The Finnish language is important to learn, because it helps people integrate into Finland.

An expert says that Finns often make a mistake when they speak with immigrants. Finns often speak English to immigrants. In the expert's opinion that is a bad thing.

The expert, professor Kirsti Siitonen, says that an immigrant must practise how Finns speak Finnish. That works by listening to a lot of Finnish.

When a Finn speaks to an immigrant, in Siitonen's opinion they must speak Finnish and say things simply.

## Seals will be watched again in May

![A Saimaa ringed seal on a rock](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8864147)

Open image viewer

Last spring the seals' life was followed in a live broadcast on Norppalive, which became really popular. A seal also visited the rock and was given a name: Pullervo.  Photo: WWF

This year too you can follow the life of the Saimaa ringed seal on the Norppalive broadcast on the internet. The environmental organisation WWF will again put a web camera at Saimaa in a place where the seals visit.

You can follow the seals' life in a live broadcast. It was really popular last year. The Saimaa ringed seal is a really rare seal in the world. There are now about 360 of them.
