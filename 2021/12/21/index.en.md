# Tuesday 21.12.2021 (radio)

Corona infections. Queues for corona tests. Shop mask recommendation. A new nuclear reactor. The shortest day of the year. Weather forecast.

## Corona infections

![A corona vaccine being given in an arm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_78/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-869391616ff23d94ec1/1634810273)

Corona vaccines are the best way to prevent corona infections. Photo: Yle/Simo Pitkänen

Corona is now spreading quickly. On Tuesday more than 2000 new corona infections were reported.

One reason for the situation is the Omicron variant of the corona virus. It catches easily and spreads quickly.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare estimates that Omicron may already cause more than 50 per cent of new corona infections in the Helsinki region.

The corona vaccine protects against the Omicron variant less well than against earlier variants. However, protection against Omicron is good after 3 corona vaccinations.

In Finland about 16 per cent of people over 12 have had 3 corona vaccinations. However, most people over 80 have already been vaccinated against corona 3 times.

There is a queue for corona tests in the Helsinki region. Free test times are only available at the end of the week.

The Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district says that there are 32 000 orders in the corona test queue. In recent days more than 10 000 tests have been done every day.

## Shop mask recommendation

Shops hope that customers will wear a mask.

Customers of shops should wear a mask whenever they are in a shop. It is good to wear a mask in all shops across the whole of Finland.

The strong mask recommendation for shops is because the corona situation has got worse.

## A new nuclear reactor

![A large turbine hall at Olkiluoto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12207183)

Open image viewer

Olkiluoto's 3rd nuclear reactor will be completed 12 years late. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

Finland is getting more nuclear power.

A new nuclear reactor was started up at the Olkiluoto nuclear power plant during Tuesday night. It will start producing electricity in January. After that it will produce 14 per cent of Finland's electricity.

The construction work on the reactor took a long time. It was completed 12 years later than planned.

The new nuclear reactor is Olkiluoto's 3rd nuclear reactor.

Finland has 5 nuclear reactors in total. Besides Olkiluoto, there are nuclear reactors in Loviisa.

## The shortest day of the year

![The church rooms of Utsjoki on a cold winter evening.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9946419)

Open image viewer

Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Tuesday was the shortest day of the year, that is, on Tuesday it was the winter solstice.

In southern Finland the sun was in the sky for only a few hours. Right in the north the sun does not rise at all at this time of year.

After this the day slowly gets longer. The journey towards spring and summer begins.

## Weather

On Wednesday in the northern part of the country it is cloudy with sunny spells. In the south and middle of the country the cloudiness varies. The weather is dry.

The frost is 10 to 20 degrees. The wind, however, makes the frost bite more.

On Thursday the weather warms, that is, the frost weakens. On Thursday there will also be snowfall.
