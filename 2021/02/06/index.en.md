## Shortage of students in construction

![Construction site of the Turku Market Square car park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4418,h_2485,x_0,y_7/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7298445f86b647786aa/1602664168)

Because of the corona situation it is hard to get onto construction sites, even for practice. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

There is a shortage of workers in the construction sector. Too few new students come into the construction field. Construction does not interest many young people.

The shortage of workforce may get worse. The corona situation has made studying construction harder. Students have not been able to, for example, be at a work site learning or show a demonstration of their work.

Starting work has also become harder in construction.

## Ski shops are busy

![Kari Jokelainen shows skis to a customer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11773027)

Across the whole of Finland there is now a proper winter, that is, a lot of snow and frost.

Winter sports equipment is selling very much now. For example, shops are short of skis with a grip base and of women's cross-country ski sets. Skis with a grip base are popular, because you do not need to wax them.

Skis, snowboards and skates are also being bought and sold a lot on online flea markets.

## Stricter rules at the Swedish border

![A nurse in protective clothing in front of a corona testing container.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11777089)

Open image viewer

Corona testing point at the border between Tornio and Haparanda. Photo: Juuso Stoor / Yle

Travel from Finland to Sweden has been tightened today, on Saturday.

Sweden now requires a negative corona test result from passengers who come to Sweden. The change restricts people's movement, so that the mutated coronavirus does not spread between the countries.

The test requirement does not apply to children under 18 or to people who live in Sweden.

## Day against female genital mutilation

![Graphic of different female genital mutilation practices.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10521528)

Open image viewer

Different methods of genital mutilation. Female genital mutilation is a harmful procedure. In Finland it is a crime. Photo: Yle News graphics, FGM

Today, 6 February, is the day against female genital mutilation.

Female genital mutilation, that is, the mutilation of the genitals, is a crime in Finland. The mutilation causes serious health risks.

Genital mutilation can make childbirth harder. Many doctors and nurses say that it is hard to talk about this in health care. Genital mutilation must be told to doctors already when you are expecting a baby.

## Sámi national day

![Sámi flag](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10531015)

Open image viewer

The Sámi flag. Photo: Ville Fofonoff / Yle

Today, 6 February, is the Sámi national day.

Nowadays about 60 percent of Sámi live outside the Sámi homeland, in different parts of Finland, or in Norway, Sweden and Russia.

The Sámi are the only indigenous people in the European Union area. The position of the Sámi was written into the Finnish constitution in 1995.
