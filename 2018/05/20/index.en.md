# Sunday 20.5.2018 (radio)

Many people work overtime. Car inspections are changing. The trade war between China and the United States is easing. Today was Remembrance Day for the fallen. The weather is getting warmer.

## Overtime is worked in many fields

![Lassila &amp; Tikanoja snow mover](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6176973)

Open image viewer

If there is a lot of snow, snow plough drivers have to work a lot of overtime. Photo: Lassila &amp; Tikanoja

Many workers have to do a really large amount of overtime. In some jobs so much overtime builds up that the working week gets one whole extra day.

Yle found out in which jobs the most overtime is worked. Drivers of work machines, machine fitters and machine repairers do the most overtime, according to statistics from Statistics Finland. In addition, for example sales directors and lower secondary school teachers may do a lot of overtime.

## Car inspections are changing

![An inspector checks the underside of a car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10210909)

Open image viewer

At the inspection, for example the tread of the tyres is checked. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Car inspections change from today. The time between inspections gets longer for cars under 10 years old.

If you buy a new car now, you only have to take it for its first inspection after 4 years of driving.

 In addition, more parts of the car are checked at the inspection. For example, brake fluids are checked.

## China and the United States reach agreement on tariffs

![Huge rolls of steel, a man wearing a helmet walks between the rolls.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10129509)

Open image viewer

A Chinese steel factory Photo: Linfeng/ EPA-EFE

China and the United States have agreed that they will give up their plans to put import tariffs on each other.

 China promises to buy considerably more American products and services.

 The preliminary agreement reduces the threat of a trade war between the countries.

## Today has been a flag day

![President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö laid a wreath at the Hero's Cross at Hietaniemi cemetery in Helsinki on Remembrance Day for the fallen, Sunday 20 May](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10214465)

Open image viewer

The President of the Republic laid a memorial wreath at the Hero's Cross in Helsinki today.

Today was Remembrance Day for the fallen.

On this day, all Finns who have died in wars, in peacekeeping tasks or in prisoner-of-war camps are remembered.

In addition, an event was held in Turku to honour the memory of the victims of the Finnish Civil War.

Archbishop Kari Mäkinen and Finland's state leaders lit candles for the victims of the civil war and the prison camps. They also laid flowers at the memorials of the Reds and the Whites.

## The snow melted ahead of time

![bird cherry in bloom](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10211786)

Open image viewer

Tomorrow, Monday, may be a really warm day. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Now there is no snow anywhere in Finland any more.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute said today that the snow melted ahead of time this spring. In northernmost Lapland, the snow has usually melted only in mid-June.

Warm air is now flowing into our country from the west.

Tomorrow, Monday, the daytime temperature will again rise close to the heatwave limit in southern and central Finland. The heatwave limit is 25 degrees.
