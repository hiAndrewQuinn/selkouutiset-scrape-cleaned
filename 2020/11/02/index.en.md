# Monday 2.11.2020 (radio)

A storm wind has blown in Finland on Monday. The coronavirus has spread only a little in Finnish workplaces. On Monday 109 new coronavirus infections were reported. The last Mannerheim Cross knight has died.

There is a strong storm in Finland. The storm moves across Finland during Monday.

In the storm there is a strong wind. The wind has knocked trees onto power lines, and the electricity has gone off in thousands of homes.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute has warned of strong wind across almost the whole of Finland.

## The coronavirus does not spread at workplaces

![Shop assistant at the checkout behind a plexiglass screen that protects against droplets.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2658,h_1495,x_0,y_100/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6622405e8c2b466d0a6/1586244550)

Workplaces protect employees from the coronavirus. The picture shows a shop assistant behind a plexiglass screen. Photo: Sari Ursin / Yle

The coronavirus has spread only a little in Finnish workplaces. This is what the Finnish Institute of Occupational Health says.

Perhaps only 10 percent of coronavirus exposures have started at workplaces. In exposure, a person has been close to another person who has the coronavirus.

The director general of the Finnish Institute of Occupational Health, Antti Koivula, says that Finnish occupational safety has worked well at workplaces during the coronavirus crisis too. Occupational safety is used to look after safety at workplaces.

However, coronavirus infections have been found in sectors where some employees live in shared accommodation. For example, in agriculture and construction there is shared accommodation for workers who come from far away.

In Finland one new coronavirus death was reported on Monday. In total, 359 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

On Monday 109 new coronavirus infections were reported. A total of 16,400 coronavirus infections have been found in Finland so far.

Worldwide, the number of coronavirus deaths is growing. Across the whole world, more than 1.2 million people have already died because of the coronavirus.

## Mannerheim Cross knight has died

![Tuomas Gerdt.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1500,h_844,x_0,y_120/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6143505dd6926ee2a26/1574343402)

The Finnish Army held a day of mourning on Monday. The Finnish flag was at half-mast on army buildings.

With a day of mourning, the army honoured Tuomas Gerdt, who died on Sunday.

Gerdt was 98 years old. He was the last Mannerheim Cross knight who was alive.

The Mannerheim Cross was the highest honour that a Finnish soldier could receive during the Second World War. Gerdt was given the rank and decoration of Mannerheim Cross knight in 1942.

There were 191 Mannerheim Cross knights. Now they have all died.

The Mannerheim Cross knights were Finnish war heroes. President Sauli Niinistö also sent a message of mourning on Twitter because of Tuomas Gerdt's death.
