# Friday 18.8.2017 (radio)

Many Somalis are unemployed. In Finland, residential areas are peaceful, the police say. Entrance exams are changing. Two terrorist attacks were carried out in Spain. On Saturday it will rain.

##

## A violent attack in Turku

Several people were stabbed in Turku. Photo: Laura Laine

A violent attack happened in Turku in the afternoon. Several people were stabbed. The police shot one suspect and arrested him. Because of the attack, the police increased surveillance at railway stations and at Helsinki-Vantaa Airport. The police also checked trains and buses leaving Turku.

## Many Somalis are unemployed

![Sagal Mohamud Ali](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9783170)

Open image viewer

Sagal Mohamud Ali hopes that young Somalis will get jobs. Photo: Petra Ristola / Yle

Many Somalis living in Finland are unemployed. Half of working-age Somalis are without work. There is now special concern about employment for second-generation Somalis born in Finland.

Sagal Mohamud Ali, chair of the Somali Association of Western Finland, says that people still have prejudices about young Somalis. It looks as if Somali women find work more easily than men.

## Finland has no problem suburbs

![Map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9780427)

Open image viewer

In Sweden there are 23 residential areas with serious problems. Photo: Yle News Graphics

In Finland there are no difficult residential areas or problem suburbs, the police say. It is different in Sweden. In Sweden, the police say that the country has 23 residential areas with a lot of problems. In those areas there are many criminal gangs, and the police cannot bring them under control.

In Finland, areas have been built with different kinds of homes and therefore also different kinds of residents.

## Entrance exams are changing

![A student photographed in Porthania at the University of Helsinki. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9455059)

Open image viewer

Next year it will be possible to get into higher education without an entrance exam. Photo: Sari Gustafsson / Lehtikuva

Entrance exams for universities and universities of applied sciences are changing in Finland. Already next year, universities and universities of applied sciences will choose a large share of their students on the basis of grades. After upper secondary school or a basic vocational qualification, you can apply for further studies with your certificate.

So you no longer necessarily need to study for entrance exams. The higher education institutions decide for themselves how they choose students.

## Two terrorist attacks in Spain

![people and candles](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9784836)

Open image viewer

A minute's silence was held in Spain. Photo: Quique Garcia / EPA

A minute's silence was held in Spain today. It honoured the people who died in two terrorist attacks. In Barcelona, a driver drove a van onto a pedestrian street. At least 13 people died and over a hundred were injured. The victims were from many different countries.

In the town of Cambrils, the police stopped a similar attack, but six people were still injured.
