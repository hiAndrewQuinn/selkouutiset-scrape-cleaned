# Thursday 15.4.2021 (radio)

The number of new corona infections is falling. The Ruisrock music festival is cancelled because of corona. Lidl is removing gaming machines from its shops. New word explanations help people follow the municipal elections. Sunny weather continues through the end of the week.

## New corona infections

![Mika Salminen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11568262)

Open image viewer

Mika Salminen, Director of the Health Security Department at THL. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, says that there are 289 new corona infections in Finland.

The number of corona infections is no longer growing as quickly as it was some time ago.

Director Mika Salminen of THL is pleased that the number of new corona infections is falling. In particular, corona infections among young adults have fallen.

Salminen says that the corona restrictions are working well. There are fewer corona infections when people's contacts decrease.

However, Salminen warns about May Day. If people celebrate May Day as normal, there will be more corona infections. May Day is already about 2 weeks away.

## Ruisrock is cancelled

![A huge crowd at the Puerto Rican Bad Bunny's gig at Ruisrock. Bad Bunny stands on stage in front of the audience with one hand raised.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_52/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5761165d2330ec87938/1580652559)

Ruisrock was last held in 2019. Photo: Nelli Kenttä, YleX

The Ruisrock music festival is cancelled this summer. The reason is corona.

This is already the second time Ruisrock has been cancelled because of corona. Last summer's festival was also cancelled.

Ruisrock is an old music festival. It was held for the first time as early as 1970.

## Gaming machines out of Lidl

![A woman plays games of chance. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10930590)

Open image viewer

Gaming machines can be a problem for the player. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

Lidl is removing gaming machines from its shops. There are about 250 Veikkaus gaming machines in Lidl's shops. Lidl will remove the gaming machines during the summer.

Gaming machines can be a problem for some people. They may lose money, because they cannot always stop playing.

## New word explanations

![Pictures of four women.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11884451)

Open image viewer

Students and researchers from the University of Helsinki wrote word explanations for the Clear News audience. Photo: University of Helsinki / Yle

There will be municipal elections in Finland in June.

Before the elections, Clear News will organise a debate in plain language, called the Selkotentti. In the Selkotentti, politicians try to speak so that everyone understands.

However, the municipal elections include difficult words. What are, for example, a municipal council and a municipal board?

Now a group of students and researchers from the University of Helsinki have written word explanations for the Clear News audience. The word explanations can be found on the Clear News website and Facebook page.

The word explanations also help viewers follow the plain-language election debate, the Selkotentti. The Selkotentti will be shown on TV1 on 4 June. It will also be available on Areena.

Sunny weather continues through the end of the week too.

At night there is frost again across the whole country. On Friday during the day, the temperature will rise above 10 degrees in the south and close to 10 degrees in the north too.

At the weekend the temperature may reach over 15 degrees in places. Sunday may be the warmest day of the week.
