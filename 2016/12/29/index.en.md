# Thursday 29.12.2016 (radio)

Jari Aarnio has been sentenced to 10 years in prison. Mika Lintilä is the new Minister of Economic Affairs. Female doctors treat elderly people differently from male doctors. The Saimaa ringed seal needs snow.

## Jari Aarnio was sentenced to prison

![Jari Aarnio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9377556)

Open image viewer

Jari Aarnio. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

The former head of the Helsinki drug police, Jari Aarnio, has been sentenced to ten (10) years in prison. The Helsinki District Court found Aarnio guilty of five (5) aggravated drug offences and 17 other crimes.

In addition, Aarnio will get a three (3) year prison sentence, which the Court of Appeal had already given earlier. So in total the prison sentence is 13 years. Three of Aarnio's accomplices have also been sentenced to prison. The accomplices are not police officers.

Most of the crimes are linked to the import of six (6) barrels of hashish in 2011–2012.

The prosecutor is satisfied with the District Court's ruling, because Aarnio was found guilty of most of the charges.

## Mika Lintilä is the new Minister of Economic Affairs

The Centre Party MP Mika Lintilä has been appointed as the new Minister of Economic Affairs. Lintilä took the ministerial oath on Thursday.

The previous Minister of Economic Affairs, Olli Rehn, is moving to become a director at the Bank of Finland.

Mika Lintilä is an MP for the Vaasa electoral district in his fifth (5th) term.

## A doctor's sex may affect the treatment of a patient

![A doctor uses a keyboard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9105400)

Open image viewer

A study made in the USA says that patients treated by female doctors die or return to hospital care less often than patients of male doctors. Photo: Eeva Kuivas / Yle

Female doctors perhaps treat elderly people better than male doctors.

A US study says that fewer elderly people treated in hospitals died when the doctor was a woman rather than a man.

The probability that a female doctor's patient would die was four (4) per cent lower than that of a male doctor's patient.

The difference is perhaps because female doctors are more empathetic, that is, more compassionate than men. Women may also perhaps follow treatment instructions more carefully than men.

A Finnish expert says that the situation may be the same in Finland too. However, it cannot be known, because the matter has not been studied in Finland.

## The Saimaa ringed seal needs snow and wind

![Saimaa ringed seal](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9377421)

Open image viewer

A seal's nest is formed from snow, when the wind piles up snowdrifts on the shores of the islands. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The Saimaa ringed seal needs nesting material. Nesting looks bad, because the ice on Lake Saimaa is weak and there is hardly any snow. The seal needs snow and wind, because the seal gives birth to its pup in a snowdrift.

The ringed seal gives birth at the turn of February and March. One nest needs 24 cubic metres of snow.

In winters with little snow, volunteers have shovelled snowdrifts for the seals. The volunteers have even considered using snow cannons if not enough snow falls. That would, however, be a big job.

At the moment only perhaps 360 ringed seals live in Saimaa.
