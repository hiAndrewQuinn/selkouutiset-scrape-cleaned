# Thursday 6.4.2017 (radio)

The municipal elections will be a tight race. Valmet is looking for workers for its car factory in new ways. The usual monthly salary of Finns is 3,000 euros. New arrests in Russia. The Finnish school in Kos sends greetings to 100-year-old Finland.

## The big parties go into the municipal elections level

![Election posters in Kaustinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9548699)

Open image viewer

The municipal elections are held on Sunday. Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

The municipal elections are becoming a tight competition between the parties. In Yle's poll, the popularity of the 3 biggest parties is almost level. The National Coalition Party, the Centre Party and the SDP all get about 18 and a half percent support. The most popular party in the poll is the National Coalition Party. The Centre Party is slightly ahead of the opposition party SDP.

An election expert says that the result of the elections can be different from the poll, because many people make the final decision on whom to give their vote only on election day. Almost 40 percent of people could not yet say in the poll which party they will vote for in the municipal elections.

The municipal elections are held on Sunday.

## Valmet's recruitment truck looks for workers for the car factory

![Valmet Automotive's recruitment tour in the Mikkeli market square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9547765)

Open image viewer

The recruitment truck travels around Finland looking for workers for the car factory.  Photo: Esa Huuhko / Yle

Finland now needs many car builders. Workers are being looked for all around Finland for Valmet's car factory in Uusikaupunki, because the factory needs 1,000 new workers. New workers are needed because the factory is starting to make new Mercedes-Benz cars.

The factory presents its work with the help of a special truck. The recruitment truck stops in 8 cities.

## An ordinary Finn earns 3,000 euros

![Fifty-euro notes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9182998)

Open image viewer

The average salary in Finland is 3,000 euros.  Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finns earn almost 3,000 euros a month. Statistics Finland says that the biggest salaries are in the information and communication sector, about 4,000 euros a month. Journalists and IT experts work in the information and communication sector.

The smallest salaries, on the other hand, are in the hotel and restaurant sector, where a worker usually earns a little over 2,200 euros a month.

## New arrests in Russia

![Guards in the Moscow Metro](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9547066)

Open image viewer

New arrests have been made over the metro attack.

More people have been arrested in St. Petersburg who are suspected of taking part in the metro attack. The Russian authorities say that the suspects come from Kyrgyzstan, that is, from the same place as the actual perpetrator of the terror attack. Eyewitnesses say that the police arrested 4 young men.

The police say that at least 1 explosive and other objects that may be connected to the terrorist act have been found in house searches.

14 people died in the metro attack, and 1 of them is the suspected perpetrator. 50 people were wounded in the explosion.

## The Finnish school in Kos greets 100-year-old Finland

![A Finnish school pupil in Kos.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9549087)

The Finnish school of Kos is in Greece. Photo: Kos Finnish school

A large number of Finnish children and young people live in different parts of the world, and they study the Finnish language and Finnish culture in Finnish schools.

Now that independent Finland turns 100, the Finnish schools send greetings to Finland on video.

The newest greetings come from the Finnish school in Kos, Greece.
