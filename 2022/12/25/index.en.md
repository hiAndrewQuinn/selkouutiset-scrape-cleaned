# Sunday 25.12.2022 (radio)

Rautjärvi church destroyed in fire. Christmas celebrations. Pope's Christmas speech. Christmas collections. Weather.

## Rautjärvi church destroyed

![Aerial photo of Rautjärvi church on a summer day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4052,h_2280,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105292163a8166ee7613/1671967145)

Rautjärvi church was a wooden building. It was completed in 1881.  Photo: Samuli Rahikainen

Rautjärvi church in South Karelia has been completely destroyed by fire.

The fire broke out in the morning during a service. There were more than 30 people in the church, and they were brought safely out.

There were many fire engines at the scene, but the fire could not be put out.

The police say that signs of a possible crime have been found at the scene. The police are investigating the fire as aggravated arson.

Rautjärvi church was a large wooden cross-shaped church. It was completed in 1881.

## Christmas celebrations

![Christmas decorations on a kitchen table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_247/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105277863a5b17865c31/1671803736)

Otherwise Christmas has passed mostly calmly in Finland. The rescue service reports that larger accidents have been avoided.

On Christmas Eve there were a few fires, but nothing serious happened to people.

There have been fender-bender collisions and cars driving into ditches in traffic, but as far as is known no one has been seriously injured.

For the police the Christmas night was busy because of many domestic call-outs.

The Southwest Finland Police say that a record audience was present at the Christmas peace proclamation in Turku yesterday, Christmas Eve. There were about 15,000 people in the Great Square.

## Pope Francis

![Pope Francis' Christmas greeting.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4242,h_2386,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105295763a85f7e49133/1671979187)

Open image viewer

The highest spiritual leader of the Catholic Church, Pope Francis, in his traditional Christmas Day speech hoped that the senseless war in Ukraine would end. Photo: AFP

The leader of the Catholic Church, Pope Francis, has given the traditional Christmas greeting. The Pope gave the Urbi et orbi, that is, the blessing to the city and the world, from the balcony of St Peter's Basilica in the Vatican.

In the Christmas greeting the Pope took a direct position on the war in Ukraine. The Pope said that the war is senseless. Russia launched a major offensive on Ukraine in February this year.

Tens of thousands of people gathered in St Peter's Square to listen to the Pope's Christmas greeting and blessing.

In addition, at the Christmas night Mass the Pope condemned people's never-ending thirst for riches and power.

The war in Ukraine and rising prices are visible in aid organisations' Christmas collections.

A round of calls by Yle shows that many have made donations later than before or with less money. .

Even so, Finns' desire to help at Christmas has remained.

There are many families in need of help at Christmas. Charities say that the need for help in Finland has increased.

## The weather

![Finland's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12686853)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

On St Stephen's Day cloud cover increases slightly.

The weather warms up, and snow showers spread to Lapland. In the west light snow may fall, and freezing rain is also possible.

Driving conditions will become poor in the north.

On the coast the temperature rises to zero, elsewhere there is frost of 2 to 10 degrees.
