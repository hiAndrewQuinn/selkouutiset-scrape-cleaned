## Restaurant industry

![Cook of a cargo ship at work in the ship's kitchen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96745562a1a973164b4/1654761902)

Open image viewer

Restaurant industry needs more workers. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

The restaurant industry has a shortage of workers. For example, in the Helsinki area there are too few restaurant workers. Some restaurants are even closed, because they have no workers.

The reason for the situation is the corona time. Because of corona, restaurants were closed, and many restaurant workers were left without work. That is why many changed fields.

It is estimated that during the corona time as many as 20,000 workers left the restaurant industry. Many have not returned, even though the restaurant industry is doing better again.

## French election

![French parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8678449)

Open image viewer

Archive photo of the French parliament. Photo: Yoan Valat / EPA

The French people have voted in the parliamentary election. The first round of the French parliamentary election was held on Sunday.

The result was even. The centre-right alliance and the left-wing alliance got equal amounts of votes. Both got about 25 percent of the votes.

The result is difficult for President Emmanuel Macron. Macron leads the centre-right alliance.

The president needs the support of a parliamentary majority in order to carry out his own policy.

The second round of the French parliamentary election is next Sunday. Then it will be clear whether Macron's alliance gets a majority in parliament.

## Uusikaupunki car factory

![A car body on a conveyor belt.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5779,h_3251,x_0,y_316/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-418684598306fe501ec/1509461563)

Open image viewer

The Uusikaupunki car factory offers jobs to many. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

The Uusikaupunki car factory is laying off workers. About 300 workers will lose their jobs.

In addition, the factory is temporarily laying off workers. During a temporary layoff, workers have no work tasks and they do not get paid.

Negotiations about jobs began at Valmet Automotive's Uusikaupunki plant in April. The plant does not need as many workers as before, because production has decreased.

## Girls' World Championship ice hockey

![Finland celebrates Canada's win in the group stage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96907162a6a8cd783db/1655089478)

Open image viewer

Finland's under-18 women's ice hockey team. Photo: Lehtikuva

Finland is in the medal games of the girls' ice hockey World Championships. Finland plays the bronze medal game of the under-18 women's world championships.

Finland played Canada in the semi-final. Canada beat Finland 2-1. Tilli Keränen scored Finland's only goal. Finland's best player was goalkeeper Emilia Kyrkkö.

Because Canada beat Finland, Canada goes to the World Championship final. Finland plays Sweden in the bronze medal game.
