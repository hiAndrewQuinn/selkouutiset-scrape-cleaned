# Monday 29.4.2019 (radio)

The causes of the Kuopio bus accident. SDP MP Hussein al-Taee apologises for old writings. More food is being sold abroad from Finland than ever before. The maritime traffic control centre prevented 18 shipping accidents last year. The Avengers film has earned over a billion euros in 5 days.

## "Drivers' health must be monitored"

![The bus in the Kuopio accident under technical investigation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10758938)

The driver's illness was one reason for the Kuopio accident. Photo: Antti Karhunen / Yle

Investigators of the Kuopio bus accident have told about the causes of the accident.

The accident happened last summer. The bus fell from a bridge onto the railway, and 4 people died. The passengers on the bus were Finns living in Sweden.

The investigators say that one cause of the accident was the driver's long-term illness. He had problems with blood sugar, and he had driven a long distance. The bus type was also quite unfamiliar to the driver.

The investigators of the Kuopio accident say that bus drivers' health must start to be monitored more closely.

## Al-Taee apologises

![Hussein al-Taee](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10759655)

Open image viewer

Hussein al-Taee. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

SDP MP Hussein al-Taee apologises for his old writings on social media.

Al-Taee says that the writings that have been spread recently are genuine. Al-Taee says that the writings now shame him. In them he insulted, for example, gay people, Somalis, Jews and Sunni Muslims.

Hussein Al-Taee's writings have also been noticed in Israel, for example.

Al-Taee got into Parliament in the elections, but before that he worked at the peace organisation CMI. Al-Taee's party, the SDP, says that the party nevertheless trusts him.

## Clean food interests people abroad

![Vegan ice cream being made for export to Germany.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10744408)

Open image viewer

Pine-flavoured vegetable ice cream is sold to Germany. Photo: Kari Ikävalko / Yle

Food is now being sold abroad from Finland more than ever before.

Especially a lot of Finnish food, for example organic ice cream, is sold to Germany. A small ice cream factory called Suomisen maito makes organic ice cream near Turku and sells it to Germany.

Organic means that the food is produced so that nature and animals suffer less. All Finnish food is advertised as clean. Clean food interests people abroad.

## Maritime traffic controllers prevent shipping accidents

![Marko Jokinen watches a radar screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10753233)

Open image viewer

Marko Jokinen monitors ships. Photo: Dani Branthin / Yle

The maritime traffic control centre prevented 18 shipping accidents last year.

Maritime traffic controllers can see on a computer all the ships that sail in Finnish sea areas. The controllers warn ships if they drift off a safe route and towards a shallow reef. Dangerous situations last year occurred on the open sea and in the archipelago, in summer and in winter.

Almost 30,000 ships sailed in Finnish sea areas last year.

## Avengers film breaks records

![Scarlett Johansson as Natasha Romanoff and Jeremy Renner.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10760170)

Open image viewer

From the film Avengers: Endgame. Photo: Walt Disney Studios

The American adventure film Avengers: Endgame is drawing huge audiences around the world. Tickets worth over one billion euros have been sold for the film in 5 days. It is a world record.

In Finland too, almost 95,000 people watched Endgame over the weekend. That is a lot, but still fewer than watched Aku Louhimies's The Unknown Soldier in its first weekend.

The Avengers films are based on Marvel Comics comic series. A total of 22 films have already been made.
