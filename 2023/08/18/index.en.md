## Electricity problems

![Loviisa nuclear power plant](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6563585e778155d66cd/1584890254)

Loviisa nuclear power plant. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

There will be problems with Finland's electricity production in the near future. That is why the electricity price may vary a lot.

There are three problems.

The first is that on Friday night a fault occurred in one reactor of the Olkiluoto nuclear power plant. The reactor therefore does not produce electricity. The fault is being repaired

The second problem is that the Loviisa nuclear power plant is being serviced. That is why the Loviisa nuclear power plant produces less electricity than normal.

The third problem is that less electricity than normal is coming to Finland from Sweden, because the energy companies are doing maintenance and repair work.

The electricity price may rise and fall quickly in the near future.

## Coronavirus vaccine

![An elderly man is being vaccinated.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-960295628e059985933/1654146961)

In the autumn people aged 65 and over will receive the coronavirus vaccine. Photo: Paulus Markkula / Yle

Older people will again be given a coronavirus vaccine in the autumn.

Health authorities recommend that the coronavirus vaccine be given to people aged 65 and over. In addition, the coronavirus vaccine will be given to risk groups.

A new form of the coronavirus is spreading in the world. Health authorities say that the new form is no more dangerous than the old forms. It is still being monitored.

The new form of the coronavirus is spreading in Israel, Denmark and the USA at least.

The new form of the coronavirus is, however, not the reason for Finland's coronavirus vaccinations.

## Mikkeli school food

![A tortilla portion on a pupil's lunch tray.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_239/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80806260a511816cecb/1693906591)

Open image viewer

In the picture, a tortilla and vegetable filling. Photo: Yle / Mikko Koski

In Mikkeli more than 400 pupils have had symptoms of food poisoning. The symptoms were caused by school food.

On Wednesday the pupils ate tortillas with a vegetable filling. The same food was eaten in 14 schools.

The tortillas came from the EU area. The vegetable filling was made by one kitchen in Mikkeli. There were more than 4,000 portions.

Some pupils said that the food tasted strange, maybe of soap.

The pupils' symptoms include stomach ache, nausea and headache, for example. Some also have a fever or diarrhoea.

Nobody has had to go to hospital because of the symptoms. The authorities are examining the food that the pupils ate. They are trying to find out what the cause of the symptoms is.
