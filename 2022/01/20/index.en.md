# Thursday 20.1.2022 (radio)

The corona epidemic is levelling off a little. 6 million corona tests will be bought for schools. Russia is holding big naval exercises in several sea areas. Winter has been good for Finnish shoe factories. Weather forecast.

## Corona situation

![Pasi Pohjola, Liisa-Maria Voipio-Pulkki and Otto Helve at a press briefing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90406661e91ba846ede/1642667067)

The corona situation was reported on Thursday at a press briefing of the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

A slight levelling off has been observed in the corona epidemic. The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health says that corona measures and restrictions have perhaps slowed the growth of infections.

However, a lot of corona infections are still happening. On Thursday 8,400 new corona infections were reported. There are actually more corona infections, because not everyone can be tested.

The government says that corona restrictions may perhaps be reduced after mid-February, if the corona situation eases then.

## Corona tests for schools

![Corona home testing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_208/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90215661e18163b5349/1642169106)

Open image viewer

A child is doing a home test. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

The National Emergency Supply Agency will procure about 6 million corona home tests for schoolchildren.

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health and the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment have given the National Emergency Supply Agency the task of procuring corona tests for schoolchildren. The tests may cost up to 10 million euros.

The first corona tests will reach schools in about 3 weeks. The idea is that schoolchildren can do corona tests at home every week.

## Big naval exercises in Russia

![The Russian warship Nastoichivy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3070,h_1725,x_0,y_321/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-29870057a0aae7b0611/1470307585)

A large number of ships and soldiers take part in Russia's naval exercises. Photo: EPA/Stefan Kraszewski

Russia is holding large military exercises in several sea areas.

Russia's Ministry of Defence says that the military exercises will be held in the Atlantic, the Pacific, the Arctic Ocean and the Mediterranean.

More than 140 warships, about 10,000 soldiers and more than 60 aircraft take part in Russia's military exercises. Russia's naval exercises will be held in January and February.

## Winter boot sales

![A shoe in a sole-moulding machine, where a rubber sole is cast onto it.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5817,h_3272,x_0,y_439/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90154361e0295e6b8d6/1642087521)

Open image viewer

Winter boots are being finished here at the Finnish Kuoma factory in Kuomiokoski. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Winter has been very good for Finnish shoe factories. Many winter shoes have been bought, because winter began early. Across the whole country there has been snow and frost for a long time. Because of corona restrictions, people also spend a lot of time outdoors.

Winter shoes have become scarce in shops. Finnish shoe companies have received many extra orders from Finnish shops and from shops in other Nordic countries.

Shoe companies have postponed the making of summer shoes to later, so that they can still make more winter shoes.

## Weather

Across the whole country it is frosty on Friday and mostly dry, that is, it does not rain or snow.

In the east it still snows in the morning. Otherwise the weather on Friday is mostly dry. In the west and in Lapland it is clear in many places. Elsewhere it is cloudy or partly cloudy. The temperature varies between -1 and -10 degrees.
