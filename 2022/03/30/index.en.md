# Wednesday 30.3.2022 (radio)

The court rejected the charges against Päivi Räsänen. More than 4 million people have fled Ukraine. Interest in foreign trips is growing.

## Päivi Räsänen

![Päivi Räsänen at Helsinki District Court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1687,x_0,y_107/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90595261eea3c217d74/1643029768)

Päivi Räsänen at Helsinki District Court. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

The court has rejected all the charges against MP Päivi Räsänen.

The prosecutor asked the court to fine Räsänen. In the prosecutor's view, Päivi Räsänen was guilty of incitement against a population group. In the prosecutor's view, Räsänen's writings and speeches insulted homosexual people.

Helsinki District Court decided that Räsänen's writings are not hate speech. Päivi Räsänen did not commit a crime. However, the court assessed that some of Räsänen's speeches insulted homosexual people.

Russia has heavily bombed cities in Ukraine. This is what Ukrainian authorities say. Ukraine says that, for example, the cities of Nizhyn and Chernihiv have been bombed.

In the Donetsk region in eastern Ukraine, Russia is shelling in almost all cities. Ukraine says that Russian troops hit a Red Cross building in the city of Mariupol.

## Trips abroad

![A woman takes a photo with her phone in front of the Hagia Sophia mosque in Istanbul.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4520,h_2542,x_0,y_33/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9349946242efb5df303/1648553985)

Many people plan to travel abroad in the summer. Travel companies say that people are planning more trips than before. Because of corona, people have travelled only a little over the past 2 years.

In the summer, people travel, for example, to the Mediterranean and to big cities in Europe. In many countries there are fewer corona restrictions than before.

Travel companies say that Russia's attack on Ukraine has only a small effect on people's travel plans.

## Boat exports

![Boats being built at a boat factory](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2500,h_1406,x_0,y_133/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7534155fd3711d7831d/1607693572)

Last year, more boats were exported abroad from Finland than ever before.

The boat industry organisation Finnboat says that the export value of boats rose by 55 percent. Last year, a total of nearly 13,000 boats were exported to about 50 different countries.

Sweden bought the most Finnish boats.
