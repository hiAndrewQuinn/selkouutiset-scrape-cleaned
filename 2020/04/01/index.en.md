# Wednesday 1.4.2020 (radio)

Finland is adding more corona testing. Restaurants will close on Saturday. The coronavirus situation is bad in Spain. March was warm in Finland.

## Finland is adding testing for the coronavirus

![Workers at PerkinElmer Wallac's Turku site. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2723,h_1532,x_0,y_140/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6599025e833beaaa518/1585659375)

Corona test kits are being packed at the PerkinElmer Wallac factory in Turku.  Photo: Paula Collin / Yle

Finland is doing more coronavirus tests than before.

Family and Basic Services Minister Krista Kiuru says that in future many more tests will be done in Finland than before.

Right now, 2,500 samples are being tested a day. In future, at least double that number of tests will be done.

## Restaurants will close on Saturday

![A restaurant table at La Famiglia in Flamingo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_328/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6546155e7205f1a4720/1729265526)

Open image viewer

Restaurants must close on Saturday 4 April. Photo: Eleni Paspatis / Yle

Restaurants and bars will close at the weekend because of the coronavirus. Restaurants must close on Saturday 4 April. The government's order is in force until the end of May.

There may not be customers inside the restaurant, but food may be sold to take away from the restaurant. The order does not apply to restaurants at workplaces, or to canteens at schools and hospitals, for example.

Many restaurants and cafes are already closed.

## The coronavirus is tormenting Spain

![Two health care workers stand in front of a hospital in Madrid.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11272455)

Two health care workers stand in front of a hospital in Madrid. Photo: Pierre-Philippe Marcou / AFP

In Spain, more people are dying of the coronavirus than before. Authorities say that over 860 people died of the disease in one day. This is a record number.

In total, more than 9,000 people have died of the coronavirus in Spain. There are more than 100,000 infections.

## Mood improves in nature

![A sunny pine forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6578575e7c6e05d1d7f/1585214781)

Open image viewer

Outdoor exercise has positive effects on mood. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

At the University of Tampere, researchers are studying what people's everyday life is like now during the corona epidemic.

Researcher Kirsi Salonen says that outdoor exercise is important. Going outside makes a person feel better. Mood may improve already just by looking out of the window at home.

Outdoor exercise is now even more important than before, because many other things are banned.

In Finland it is easy to go into nature. Studies show that stress decreases in nature.

## March was warm

![A windsurfer surfs on Kallahdenselkä in March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11284065)

Open image viewer

A windsurfer surfs on Kallahdenselkä in March. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

In Finland, March was warmer than usual.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that the average temperature in March in the south was almost +3 degrees. In Northern Lapland the average temperature was -6 degrees. The average temperature in the southern and central parts of the country was unusually high.

The highest temperature in March was measured in Hirvivaara in Mäntsälä. There, on 26 March, it was warm at 12.6 degrees.

In Lapland there was an unusually large amount of snow in March.
