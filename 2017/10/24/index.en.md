# Tuesday 24.10.2017 (radio)

A court has started hearing the case of a car attack that happened in Helsinki in the summer. The UN has promised help to Rohingya refugees. The weather is now colder than usual for this time of year. The old Puhos shopping centre in East Helsinki has become a lively bazaar, but it may be demolished. The Finnish school in Rethymnon sends greetings to Finland on its 100th birthday.

## Court hearing on the car attack

![The accused at the Helsinki District Court on Tuesday 24 October 2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9897719)

Open image viewer

A man went on trial for driving into people. Photo: Kai Jaskari / Yle

A court has started hearing the case of a car attack that happened in Helsinki in the summer.

In July, a man drove a car into people in the centre of Helsinki. One person died and 5 people were injured.

The prosecutor says that the attack was murder. The prosecutor thinks that the man's act was brutal and cruel. The prosecutor wants the man to be punished for the worst crime, murder.

The man himself says that he did not kill anyone on purpose. That is why the man thinks that the act was not murder.

The court will continue hearing the case next week.

## Help for refugees

![Rohingya.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9854208)

Open image viewer

Rohingya refugees get help from the UN. Photo: Kirsi Crowley / Yle

The UN has promised help to Rohingya refugees. The UN, or United Nations, is giving 300 million euros in aid.

Almost a million Rohingya have fled from Myanmar to neighbouring Bangladesh.

Myanmar is a Buddhist country, but the Rohingya are Muslims. That is why other people in Myanmar do not like the Rohingya.

Myanmar's army has attacked the Rohingya, because it thinks that the Rohingya are dangerous Muslim extremists. The UN is investigating the army's actions.

## Cold weather

![a frozen flower](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9384790)

Open image viewer

This autumn is cold. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

It is now colder in Finland than usual for this time of year. In Southern Finland, for example, the temperature is about 5 degrees lower than usual.

There has been frost at night all over the country. In Helsinki, for example, there have been night frosts, although usually the lowest temperature is zero.

In the far north, there have been as much as 15 degrees below zero at night.

## Puhos

![The Puhos shopping centre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9894232)

Open image viewer

At the Puhos shopping centre you can get, for example, halal meat. Photo: Kari Ahotupa / Yle

Puhos is an old shopping centre in East Helsinki. In recent years it has become a bazaar. Puhos has small shops run by immigrants.

But the future of Puhos is uncertain. The old shopping centre may be demolished.

## Greetings from Rethymnon

![Women in fancy dress costumes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9897210)

Open image viewer

Pupils of the Finnish school in Rethymnon, Crete. Photo: Yle

A large number of Finnish children and young people live in different parts of the world and study the Finnish language and Finnish culture in Finnish schools.

Now that independent Finland turns 100, the Finnish schools are sending greetings to Finland on video.

The newest greetings come from the Finnish school in Rethymnon, on Crete.

You can watch the greetings from the Finnish school in Rethymnon on the Clear News website at yle.fi/selkouutiset, in the Finland 100 section. The greeting is also on the Clear News Facebook page.
