# Wednesday 5.10.2022 (radio)

Journalists' trial. Department store fire. Fake police. Home care for the elderly. The weather.

## Journalists' trial

![Man surrounded by journalists.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12650674)

Open image viewer

The trial against journalists interests many people. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

In court an unusual criminal case is being heard. In the Helsinki District Court, punishment is demanded for 3 journalists.

The journalists made a news story about the army's Signal Test Centre. The news story was in Helsingin Sanomat in December 2017.

The prosecutor says that in the news story the journalists revealed a security secret. That is why he demands punishment for the journalists.

The journalists say that they have not committed a crime. They say that the news story did not contain secret information.

The trial interests many people, because it relates to freedom of speech. The journalists' work is to monitor decision-makers and tell everyone about matters. However, the army may have information that is secret.

The trial continues next week.

## Department store fire

![Sokos in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4849,h_2728,x_135,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10025406311b4fdb6198/1662106560)

Open image viewer

Sokos department store in central Helsinki. Photo: Jorma Vihtonen / Yle

The cause of the fire at the Sokos department store in central Helsinki has been found. The cause of the fire was a fault in a technical device.

A fire broke out in the department store last Friday. No one was injured in the fire.

However, smoke and extinguishing water caused damage indoors. That is why the department store was closed until Tuesday morning.

## Fake police

![Man holding a phone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11839830)

Open image viewer

A real police officer does not ask for bank details on the phone. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The police have exposed fake police officers. Fake police officers have cheated people out of money.

The fake police especially called elderly women. They lied that they were police officers and asked people for their bank details.

Using the bank details, the fake police stole over 700,000 euros from people in total. The crimes took place in 2021 and 2022.

## Home care for the elderly

![Basic carer Minna Kumpumäki on a home-care visit to a client.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4607,h_2591,x_66,y_1153/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7146965f4f959fd837c/1642154239)

Open image viewer

Home care workers have a lot of work. Photo: Tanja Kröger / Yle

Lack of time is a problem in home care for the elderly. This is what a study by the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare shows.

Usually half an hour is reserved for home care. In half an hour a carer has time to give food and medicines, and hardly anything else.

The institute's statistics show that a frail elderly person gets less care time in home care than in a service flat.

The institute's research professor Timo Sinervo says that home care workers are exhausted. They feel that they are in too much of a hurry and have too much work.
