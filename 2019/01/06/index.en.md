# Sunday 6.1.2019 (radio)

Finns want to influence the election most on the care of the sick and elderly and on the exclusion of young people. Finland has won gold in the Under-20 Ice Hockey World Championships. The search for the Finnish skiers in Norway could not be continued. Broken Christmas lights should be recycled.

## Finns have said which important election themes matter

![Ballot paper in an envelope](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9702868)

Finns want to influence the election most on the care of the sick and elderly and on the exclusion of young people. The Local Government Development Foundation carried out a study on election themes for next spring's parliamentary elections.

The respondents say that unemployment, education, energy issues and the position of the poor are also important themes. In addition, the respondents want to influence the state's economy and climate change.

Civic activism, development aid and alcohol harms were the least important themes. People now want to influence the composition of the government much more than in the 2007 parliamentary elections.

## Young Lions become world champions

![The Young Lions celebrate](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10583785)

Open image viewer

The Young Lions won a gold medal at the World Championships. Photo: AOP

Finland has won gold in the Under-20 Ice Hockey World Championships. The world championships were played in Vancouver, Canada.

Finland beat the USA 3-2 in the final. The hero of the match was 17-year-old Kaapo Kakko, who scored the winning goal about a minute and a half before the end of the game.

The gold was the 5th world championship for the Young Lions. The previous championships were from 1987, 1998, 2014 and 2016.

President Sauli Niinistö and his wife Jenni Haukio sent a congratulatory letter to the players. Prime Minister Juha Sipilä also sent a congratulatory message.

## The search in Norway could not be continued

![Helicopter in the sky above Tamokdalen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_529/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5348395c30e2e3e0611/1546707770)

The search for the Finnish skiers in Norway could not be continued on Sunday. The local police said that the weather was too bad for search flights.

The situation will be assessed next on Monday morning.

3 Finnish men and 1 Swedish woman disappeared on Wednesday. A large avalanche occurred in the area at the time of the disappearance. The authorities believe that the missing people will not be found alive.

The searches were suspended on Friday because of difficult weather.

## Christmas lights do not belong in the landfill

![A string of candle lights from the 1970s.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9935646)

Open image viewer

A string of candles like this does not belong in mixed waste. Photo: Tarja Nyyssönen / Yle

Sunday was Epiphany. In many homes, Christmas decorations are collected away on Epiphany.

Epiphany is a Christian feast day that ends the holy days of Christmas.

If Christmas lights have broken, they should be recycled. Even a small amount of electrical waste and electronic waste can be recovered as raw materials. The most important raw material is copper. Plastic and wood parts are also recovered.

In addition, Christmas lights contain harmful substances such as flame retardants. That is why Christmas lights do not belong in the landfill.

Christmas lights and seasonal lights belong to the SER recycling points for electrical and electronic waste. There are hundreds of them in different parts of Finland.
