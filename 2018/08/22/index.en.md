## Niinistö and Putin met

Finnish President Sauli Niinistö met the Russian President Vladimir Putin today.

The presidents met in Russia.

Russian President Vladimir Putin said that relations between Finland and Russia are good.

Finnish President Sauli Niinistö said that he wanted to talk about environmental issues in the Arctic regions and about air safety over the Baltic Sea. They also talked about Syria and Ukraine.

## Dog attacks are being discussed

![Dog biting](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10356170)

Open image viewer

Finland is talking a lot about dogs now. Photo: pixabay

A lot is being said in Finland about attacks by dogs. A dog can attack a person or another dog.

The Kennel Club is an organisation for dog owners. The chairman of the Kennel Club, Harri Lehkonen, says that a dog owner must control the dog in every situation.

Lehkonen says that some dog breeds are such that not everyone can own them.

It may be good to first look into what kind of person intends to get a dog. This is what Harri Lehkonen, chairman of the Kennel Club, says.

## In Finland bridges do not collapse, says the Finnish Transport Agency

![The old Mansikkakoski railway bridge crossing the Vuoksi river.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10362906)

Open image viewer

The Finnish Transport Agency says that bridges in Finland do not collapse. Photo: Vesa Winberg / Yle

There are many bridges in poor condition in Finland.

In Finland, bridges are supervised by the Finnish Transport Agency. The agency looks after over 15,000 bridges. Of these, 600 are in poor condition.

The Finnish Transport Agency says that bridges in Finland do not collapse, even though they are in poor condition.

If a bridge is in poor condition, a weight limit is put on it. Vehicles that are too heavy may not drive onto the bridge.

## The hospital sells births

![Delivery room waiting for a woman giving birth.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10364457)

Open image viewer

Foreign mothers can come to Lappeenranta hospital to give birth. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

In Lappeenranta in Eastern Finland, a hospital is starting to sell births. That means a foreign mother can come to Lappeenranta hospital to give birth.

A birth costs about 7,500 euros. The hospital believes that Russian mothers want to come to Finland to give birth.

The Lappeenranta hospital needs more women to give birth there. Otherwise, the maternity ward could be closed.
