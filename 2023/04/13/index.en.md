## Parliament's opening ceremony

![President Niinistö speaks to MPs in the Parliament chamber.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690418)

President Sauli Niinistö spoke at the opening ceremony of Parliament. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The new Parliament has started its work. Parliament is Finland's parliament.

The parliamentary elections were in early April. Today, Thursday, the MPs gathered for the formal opening of Parliament.

President Sauli Niinistö spoke at the event. The President reminded that Finland is now a member of the NATO defence alliance.

We are no longer alone, Niinistö said. NATO brings security to Finland. But it also brings responsibilities and duties.

In addition, the President said that MPs must remember the future of young people. Climate change, for example, requires action.

Finland's Parliament has 200 MPs. The MPs' most important work is to make Finland's laws. A law can come into force only if Parliament approves it.

## Warships' visit

![Warship in Helsinki harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690420)

Open image viewer

A NATO ship in Helsinki harbour. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

Two NATO warships are visiting Helsinki. The visit began today, Thursday, and continues until Sunday.

The ships are from Germany and Portugal. They first train with Finnish ships in the Gulf of Finland.

After the exercise the ships are in Helsinki harbour. However, the public cannot visit the ships.

This is the first joint exercise of warships since Finland became a NATO country. Finland officially joined NATO in early April.

## Popular first names

![A baby in a maternity hospital bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690421)

Open image viewer

Many Olivias and Leos were born in 2022. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

Statistics on last year's popular first names have been completed. Olivia and Leo are the most popular names among Finnish-speaking children born last year.

The most popular names among Swedish-speaking children are Saga and Oliver, and among children speaking other languages, Sofia and Adam.

A child's name can now be reported to the Digital and Population Data Services Agency online. That speeds up the handling of the matter.

## Ostrobothnian rivers

![A road under water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690422)

Open image viewer

Flood water covered this road in Ostrobothnia in spring 2021. Photo: Jarkko Heikkinen / Yle

Dangerous floods are unlikely to come to Ostrobothnia this spring.

Because of night frosts the snow has melted fairly slowly, and no new snow or rain has fallen.

That is why the water volume of the Ostrobothnian rivers has grown calmly.

Spring floods are common in Ostrobothnia, because there are many rivers in the area and the land is flat.

## The weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690424)

Open image viewer

On Friday the weather is sunny and dry. The temperature is mainly between 5 and 10 degrees, in the south-west of the country it is a little warmer.

The dry weather continues at the weekend too, no rain falls. The temperature rises to close to 10 degrees across much of the country at the weekend.

The nights are still cold. There are frosts at night also in the south.
