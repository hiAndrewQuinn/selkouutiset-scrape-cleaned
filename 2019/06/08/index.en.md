# Saturday 8.6.2019 (radio)

A hospital district may not have put all its purchases out to tender. The Blue Future party chose its chairman. There are many delays in air travel in summer. The USA and Mexico reached an agreement. There has been a lot of lightning.

## Possible errors in HUS tendering

![Laboratory sample tubes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9987961)

Open image viewer

DNA analyses have been bought from a private company without tendering. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Finland's largest hospital district may have made purchases without tendering. Public bodies must put their purchases out to tender in the EU. Inspectors say that in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district there are unclear purchases, perhaps worth tens of millions of euros.

For example, DNA analyses have been bought from a private company without tendering. The authorities are investigating whether the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district has broken the law.

## Kari Kulmala became chairman of the Blue Future party

The new chairman of the Blue Future party is **Kari Kulmala**. Kari Kulmala was elected chairman at the Blue Future party congress. Kari Kulmala is a police officer by profession.

The previous chairman of the Blue Future party was **Sampo Terho**. He no longer wanted to be chairman. The Blue Future party did not win a single seat in Parliament in this spring's parliamentary election.

## There is a lot of air travel this summer

There are delays in air traffic in Europe in the summer. A lot of flights are flown in Europe this summer. Flights from Finland may have to go around air traffic jams. Then the flight time gets a little longer. For example, there are a lot of aircraft in the airspace of Germany and France this summer. There may be delays in air travel in the summer.

## USA and Mexico agree on border security

![Lorry queue](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10815476)

Open image viewer

The USA will not put extra tariffs on Mexican products, because an agreement was reached on border issues. Photo: EPA

The USA and Mexico have agreed to limit immigration. Mexico is guarding the border more closely, so that Mexicans cannot go to the USA without permission. The USA says it will send asylum seekers back to Mexico. The USA threatened to put tariffs on Mexican products if no agreement was reached on guarding the border.

## Heat and lightning

![Lightning in the sky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10792685)

Open image viewer

There was a lot of lightning on Saturday. Photo: Leonid Tit / AOP

Hot weather, that is, heat, has continued in Finland today. There have been many thunderstorms and lots of lightning in the middle of the country and in Lapland. The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that lightning has struck the ground about 3,000 times. Hail has fallen in Eastern Finland. There have been severe hailstorms between Joroinen and Varkaus, and in the Kesälahti area. The thunderstorm has also caused power cuts. The heat will continue on Sunday. There will also be thunderstorms and hail on Sunday.
