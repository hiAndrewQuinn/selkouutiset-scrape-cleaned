# Sunday 15.1.2023 (radio)

Police operation in Juupajoki. Nepal air crash. Finnish families. Storms in California and the Philippines. The weather in Finland.

## Police operation in Juupajoki

![Many police cars in front of a house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12687900)

Open image viewer

Police surrounded a house in Juupajoki on Sunday. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

The police have had an unusual operation in Juupajoki.

The police surrounded a house where there was a man with a gun. The man is suspected of a crime.

The operation began on Sunday morning, and the police caught the man on Sunday afternoon.

The police say that no one died or was injured. The man was alone in the house.

Juupajoki is less than 60 kilometres from Tampere.

## Air crash in Nepal

![People and a fallen aircraft.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12687902)

Open image viewer

Many people took part in the rescue work after the air crash. Photo: Bijaya Neupane / EPA

In Nepal in Asia there has been an air crash. About 70 people died in the crash.

A Yeti Airlines plane crashed to the ground near the city of Pokhara. It came from Nepal's capital, Kathmandu.

There were 72 people on the plane, almost all of whom died. Among them were also foreigners, but no Finns.

Nepal is in the Himalayan mountains, where flying is difficult. It is not yet known what caused Sunday's crash.

## Finnish families

![A small child's hands take support from a birch-bark basket. In the background there is a toy box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97142362ac3e25c2bd0/1712041381)

Photo: Susanna Pesonen / Yle

In Finland most children grow up in families where the parents are married. Statistics Finland says that about 60 percent of children grew up in a married couple family in 2021.

The number is, however, smaller than before. As recently as 30 years ago about 80 percent of children grew up in a family where the parents were married.

The number of cohabiting-couple families is therefore growing. In a cohabiting-couple family the parents are not married. There are also more single-parent families than before.

## Storms in California and the Philippines

![A man and a woman clean mud off a yard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12687901)

Open image viewer

It has rained in California for more than 2 weeks. Photo: Peter Dasilva / EPA

Storms are causing destruction in different parts of the world.

In the storms in California in the USA about 20 people have died. The heavy rains have continued in California for more than 2 weeks.

Because of the rains, floods are feared in California. Authorities have warned millions of people of the possibility of flooding.

Also in Asia, in the Philippines, there have been heavy storms. About 30 people have died in the storms.

The storms in the Philippines began already at Christmas. Many Filipinos now live in evacuation centres, because the storms have broken houses.

## The weather in Finland

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12687899)

Open image viewer

Rainy weather continues in Finland on Monday.

The temperature is a little above zero in a large part of the country. That is why the rain is mainly water or sleet.

Only in Lapland is there a little frost and the rain is snow. In part of Lapland it may snow on Monday as much as over 10 centimetres.

On Tuesday the weather clears, that is, the rain stops. The weather also gets colder, and in Northern Finland there is a little frost. In Lapland there is frost in places of over 10 degrees on Tuesday.
