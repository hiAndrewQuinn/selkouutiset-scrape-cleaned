# Tuesday 28.1.2020 (radio)

Waste gas in Lappeenranta is to be turned into fuel. Finnair is cancelling some flights to China. Young graduates are finding work well. Korkeasaari has been given permission to keep a Saimaa ringed seal. It was very cold night in Lapland.

## Fuel from waste gas

![Finnsement.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9912990)

Open image viewer

The Finnsement factory in Lappeenranta. Photo: Kari Saastamoinen

A special industrial project is being planned in Lappeenranta.

Lappeenranta–Lahti University of Technology and a group of Finnish companies are investigating whether fuel can be made from the emissions of factories.

The aim is to make fuel from the waste gases of cement and chemical factories. This carbon-neutral, synthetic fuel could be used, for example, by lorries, ships or aeroplanes.

The university has been studying the matter in a laboratory for a couple of years already. Now they are studying whether production of synthetic fuel can be expanded so much that it pays off economically.

## Finnair's flights to China

![Signpost at an airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10357671)

Open image viewer

Finnair is cancelling some of its flights to China. Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

Finnair is cancelling flights to China. Finnair says that it is cancelling 3 weekly flights to Beijing and 2 to Nanjing.

The cancellation of flights is due to China banning Chinese group trips abroad. With the ban, China is trying to stop the spread of the coronavirus.

Most of Finnair's flights to China, however, continue as usual.

## Jobs market for graduates

![A young doctor writes a prescription on his computer in a health centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5684094)

Open image viewer

There is a great shortage of doctors. Photo: YLE

Young graduates now have a great demand in the jobs market. Statistics Finland says that as many as 90 per cent of young graduates get a job straight after graduating.

Work can be found especially in the social and health care sector and in the technology sector.

In many fields, students are recruited to work from universities even before they have had time to graduate. For example, engineers, business graduates and doctors are recruited to work well before they graduate.

## The Saimaa ringed seal at Korkeasaari

![A veterinarian tries to feed a pup.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10892948)

Open image viewer

Veterinarian Anu Korpikallio tries to feed the pup. Photo: Petri Vironen / Yle

The Korkeasaari zoo in Helsinki has been given permission to keep a Saimaa ringed seal. The permission was given by the Centre for Economic Development, Transport and the Environment of South-West Finland.

At the Korkeasaari wildlife hospital, a Saimaa ringed seal has been treated since last summer. Now the seal is healthy, but it can no longer be returned to Saimaa. It could pose a disease risk to other seals.

The Saimaa ringed seal is a protected animal. That is why Korkeasaari needed a special permit to keep the seal.

A new cold record has been set again in Lapland.
