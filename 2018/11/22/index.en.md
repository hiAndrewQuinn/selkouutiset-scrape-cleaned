## There may be enough timber to cut in forests

![A forest machine's harvester head cuts a tree trunk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9752280)

Open image viewer

Cutting of forests may not need to be limited so much, because forests grow well. Photo: Esko Puikko / Yle

Finland's forests bind more carbon dioxide than was known. New forest grows faster than before. Forests grow well because they are looked after well. Climate warming also increases forest growth. Cutting of forests may not need to be limited so much, because forests grow well.

## Orkla is buying Kotipizza

![Kotipizza restaurant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5416459)

Open image viewer

The Kotipizza chain has over 280 outlets in Finland. Photo: YLE

The Norwegian company Orkla is buying the Finnish Kotipizza. Kotipizza is the largest pizza restaurant chain in the Nordic countries. Orkla is offering 146 million euros for Kotipizza. Orkla, from Norway, has previously bought Panda's confectionery factory and the Taffel crisps factory in Finland.

## Drought reduced the grain harvest

![Rye field](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8233073)

Open image viewer

Rye harvest collapsed from over 110 million kilos last year. This time only 41 million kilos of rye was harvested. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

The grain harvest has been small. Drought harmed farming on many farms last summer. The drought harmed farms in Southern Finland most of all. Especially little rye was harvested this summer. The grain harvest this autumn is 2.7 billion kilos. The harvest was just as small often in the 1960s.

## A government still has not been formed in Sweden

![Annie Lööf](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7470694)

Open image viewer

According to Lööf, the parties have major disagreements on work and housing policy. Photo: Sveriges Riksdagen

It is very difficult to form a new government in Sweden. The leader of the Swedish Centre Party, **Annie Lööf**, has not managed to put together a new government. Lööf is giving up the post. Lööf was already the third person who tried to form a government in Sweden. Sweden's parliamentary elections were held at the start of September.

## Lack of snow harms tourism in Lapland

![Aki Holck's sled dogs give tourists a proper ride. The lack of snow harms the passengers no more than the dogs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10517290)

Open image viewer

The Lapland winter tourism season is about to start. Tourism companies have to find things to do without snow. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

There is a shortage of snow in Lapland. The lack of snow harms tourism. A dog team cannot pull a sled because there is no snow. At a reindeer farm, preserved snow is spread on the ground so that the reindeer can pull a sleigh.

A company that offers snowmobile safaris is having a hard time. People have had to be refunded, because the snowmobile safari could not go ahead.
