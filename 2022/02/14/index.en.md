## Ukraine

![A soldier walks in a muddy trench in a light green camouflage uniform, with an assault rifle on his back and a helmet on his head.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6605,h_3715,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91356562052539838b7/1644504541)

Ukraine wants to negotiate with Russia in the coming days.

Ukraine is asking for negotiations to begin with Russia and with the Organization for Security and Co-operation in Europe.

The purpose is to ask why Russia has placed troops on Ukraine's borders.

Ukraine's foreign minister says that Russia has not replied to the official request for clarification that was made on Friday. The request concerned Russia's military activity near Ukraine and on the Crimean peninsula.

Russia does not want Ukraine to join the NATO military alliance.

## G7 countries

![Group photo from the G7 meeting in Cornwall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5200,h_2918,x_0,y_424/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-872454617952c954497/1635342277)

People around the world fear that Russia may attack Ukraine as early as this week.

Finance ministers of the G7 countries warned Russia today. The countries say that "massive" economic sanctions will be placed on Russia if it attacks Ukraine.

The G7 group also promised to support Ukraine's economy.

The G7 group includes Italy, Japan, Canada, France, Germany, Britain and the USA. These countries are the seven most developed economies in the world.

Russia was part of the group in 1998–2014, when the group was called G8.

## Guardianship

![Hands of a middle-aged person and an old person.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10404679)

Open image viewer

A guardianship authorisation is worth making as soon as a memory illness begins. Photo: Pixabay

More and more Finns are making a guardianship authorisation. The reason can be, for example, a memory illness.

More guardianships are being made, because the population is ageing.

At present about 85,000 guardianships are in force. Of these, 15,000 are authorisations.

Most are still traditional guardianships. In these the district court appoints a guardian. The guardian can be, for example, a relative.

## Vaccinations in Sweden

![Maija Ranta at her desk at the Limingantulli corona vaccination point in Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_302/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89718861cc45938def6/1640777224)

Open image viewer

In Sweden a fourth vaccination is recommended for people over 80. Photo: Timo Sipola / Yle

Sweden recommends a fourth corona vaccine dose for people over 80. A fourth dose is also recommended for those who live in a care home or get home care.

The Public Health Agency of Sweden justifies the vaccination by saying that age weakens the immune system. Then the response to vaccines and the protection they give get weaker.

Sweden's state epidemiologist Anders Tegnell says that the extra dose strengthens protection.

The fourth dose can be given at the earliest four months after the previous one.
