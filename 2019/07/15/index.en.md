## End-of-life care

![An elderly person lies in bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9521718)

Open image viewer

Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

There are shortcomings in end-of-life care. People who are dying soon need end-of-life care.

The National Institute for Health and Welfare (THL) has examined how well end-of-life care for elderly people works in Finland.

According to THL, there is not enough end-of-life care for elderly people who want to live at home until the end. Often elderly people have to go to hospital also in the last months of their lives.

THL says that elderly people must be cared for better as death approaches. That is why more nurses are needed in home care. In addition, all home care workers need training in end-of-life care.

Finns trust science. Over 90 percent of Finns trust science and researchers.

Finns trust science clearly more than religion. Only 17 percent of Finns trust religion if they feel that religion and science are in conflict.

## Names

![A baby being baptised](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10111433)

Open image viewer

Photo: AOP

Children are getting longer names than before.

A new naming law came into force at the start of this year. A child can now get up to 4 first names. A child can also get a combination of surnames, for example the surname of the mother and the father.

By the end of May about 1 percent of children had received 4 first names or a combination of surnames.

On the Clear News website there are videos that explain what the new naming law says about first names and surnames.

There is a big change in the Bond films. In the next Bond film, Agent 007 is a woman.

British newspapers report that Lashana Lynch plays Agent 007. She is a Black British actress.

In the film the new female agent's name is not Bond. But she takes Agent 007's place when James Bond retires.
