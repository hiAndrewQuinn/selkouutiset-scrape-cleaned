## Memory diseases

![Two women walk in the rain on a bridge.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10414012)

Open image viewer

Memory can be affected by diet and exercise, for example. Photo: Timo Sihvonen / Yle

Lifestyle has a bigger role in the onset of memory diseases than has been known until now.

A new, large Finnish study shows that healthy lifestyle habits help to prevent memory diseases. Changing one's lifestyle can also delay the onset of a memory disease.

Memory can be affected by diet and exercise, brain training and relaxation, for example. Social activity and brain training are also important.

Every year about 15,000 people in Finland fall ill with a memory disease. The disease is often noticed only when the changes in the brain are already advanced.

## HIV infections

![In treating the HIV pandemic, medicine has achieved several major successes over the past ten or so years.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11371323)

Open image viewer

The red ribbon is the international symbol of HIV and AIDS awareness. Photo: Original image: AOP, image manipulation: Otso Ritonummi

The number of HIV infections in Finland has grown a lot. This year more HIV infections have been reported than in many years.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare (THL) says that about 240 infections have been detected in Finland this year. Last year there were 152 infections.

More than half of the infections have been reported in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district.

## Chinese Covid restrictions

![People in Beijing queued for a coronavirus test on Tuesday, 26 April 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4403,h_2476,x_0,y_306/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9466106267edab1f34d/1650978655)

People in Beijing queued for coronavirus tests last April. Photo: Mark R. Cristino / EPA / AOP

China is easing coronavirus restrictions in many cities.

For example, in the capital Beijing, people will in future be able to enter shops and the metro without a coronavirus test. In Shanghai too, people can use public transport and enter parks without a coronavirus test. Coronavirus restrictions have also been eased, for example, in Shenzhen and Canton.

In China there have been large protests against the country's strict coronavirus measures.

However, the number of coronavirus infections in China has grown to almost record levels.

## Iran's morality police

![A protester holds a clump of hair in her hand. The Iranian consulate building can be seen in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_239/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1043577638c6e4000462/1670147741)

Open image viewer

There have been big protests in Iran for women's rights. Photo: Sedat Suna / EPA

Iran intends to end the activities of the morality police. The Attorney General of Iran told Iranian media about ending the morality police.

The Attorney General also said that Iran is reviewing an old law that requires women to wear the hijab headscarf.

There have been big protests in Iran, after 22-year-old Mahsa Amini died in the autumn in the hands of the morality police. The morality police arrested Amini because she had put on the hijab headscarf incorrectly.

The morality police have operated in Iran for almost 20 years. It has patrolled the streets and monitored, for example, how people dress and behave.
