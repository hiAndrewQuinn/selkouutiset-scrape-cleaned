## Morocco earthquake

![A man carries a child in Morocco, the house has collapsed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4091,h_2301,x_0,y_370/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116998664fd7f437667b/1694335361)

Open image viewer

A man carries a child in Morocco, the house has collapsed. Photo: Lehtikuva

More than 2000 people have already died in the Morocco earthquake. An expert estimates that there may be many more dead people. At least over 2000 people have been injured.

Rescuers are searching for people in the rubble of houses. The earthquake badly destroyed villages in the mountains near the city of Marrakesh. In the city of Marrakesh, too, many houses collapsed.

The earthquake happened in Morocco late on the night between Friday and Saturday.

## Russian construction work

Satellite image of the Russian armed forces' equipment depot in Petroskoi. Photo: Google Earth / Maxar Technologies

Russia is carrying out construction work at military sites that are close to Finland's border.

For example, 3 new halls have been built at the Alakurtti garrison. Military equipment is stored in the halls. Russia is also building at the Petroskoi equipment depot.

Defence Minister Antti Häkkänen says that the construction work is normal activity for Russia. Häkkänen says that Russia's construction work does not pose a threat to Finland.

## Heat pumps

![The outdoor unit of an air-source heat pump on the side of a detached house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3185,h_1791,x_1,y_909/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10355816376539da0154/1668700022)

Open image viewer

The outdoor unit of an air-source heat pump on the side of a detached house. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

People in Finland buy a lot of heat pumps. Last year, people in Finland bought more heat pumps than people bought in other European countries. 21 countries were included in the comparison.

Last year, on average about 69 heat pumps were bought for every thousand Finnish homes. Almost 200 000 heat pumps were bought in Finland last year. A heat pump can heat or cool a home.

## Hunting of forest grouse

![Black grouse feeding in a birch tree.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7082375f364c265d592/1597394134)

Open image viewer

Black grouse feeding in a birch tree. Illustrative image. Photo: Tanja Heikkonen / Yle

Forest grouse may be hunted from this Sunday onwards. Birds may be hunted in a large part of Finland for 2 or 3 months.

The Finnish Game Centre says that in some places there are now many forest grouse. In some other places there are only a few birds.

Forest grouse include, for example, black grouse and capercaillie.

Tomorrow, Monday, the weather is dry across the whole country. In Lapland it is clear, that is, there are not many clouds in the sky.
