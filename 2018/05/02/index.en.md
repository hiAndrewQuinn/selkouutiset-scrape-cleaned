# Wednesday 2.5.2018 (radio)

The prosecutor wants punishment for 3 police chiefs. Many people are breathing dirty air. Food exports from Finland to Asia are growing. At the weekend the weather will be beautiful and summery.

## Police chiefs

![From the left: Police Chief Mikko Paatero, Helsinki Police Department Chief Lasse Aapio and Helsinki Police Commander Jukka Riikonen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9885425)

Mikko Paatero, Lasse Aapio and Jukka Riikonen are the police bosses the prosecutor wants punished. Photo: AOP and Yle

The prosecutor wants punishment for 3 police chiefs. The prosecutor says that the chiefs did not supervise the work of the drug police.

In the prosecutor's view, the drug police did not manage the register of informants properly. Informants are people from whom the drug police get information. In the prosecutor's view, they may be in danger because of the poor work of the police. That is why the court should punish the police chiefs, the prosecutor says.

The police chiefs themselves think that they have not committed a crime.

## Air pollution

![People in Beijing wear breathing masks to protect themselves from smog.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8494109)

Open image viewer

In Beijing, for example, the air is often dirty. Photo: Mika Mäkeläinen / Yle

Many people are breathing dirty air. The World Health Organization, WHO, says that 90 percent of people in the world breathe air pollution.

For example, cars and factories pollute the air everywhere in the world. Indoor air can also be dirty, if a family uses coal as fuel for cooking, for example.

Air pollution causes heart and lung diseases in people, for example. WHO estimates that about 7 million people die every year because of air pollution.

Air pollution is the biggest problem in Africa and Asia.

## Food exports

![eggs](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10184532)

Open image viewer

Finnish food is clean. Photo: Petra Ristola / Yle

More food products are being exported from Finland to Asia than before.

For example, the company Munax will start exporting eggs to Japan and Hong Kong.

Asians are interested in Finnish eggs and other food products, because they are clean. They do not contain salmonella or other dangerous bacteria.

Finnish food products also do not contain antibiotics, because in Finland antibiotics are not added to animal feed.

## Weather

![spring sun shining](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10158566)

Open image viewer

The weekend may turn out beautifully summery. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

Summer may start at the weekend.

Yle meteorologist Toni Hellinen says that warm air will flow into Finland on Saturday. On Sunday the weather will get warmer still.

In Southern Finland the temperature may rise close to 20 degrees. Far in the north the temperature at the weekend is about 10 degrees.
