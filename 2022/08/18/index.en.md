## Fingerprints

![A hand in a rubber glove turns a fingerprint powder brush on a wall with fingerprints.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11841835)

Open image viewer

Fingerprints are being examined. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

The fingerprint data of passports is wanted to be used in criminal investigations.

The Ministry of the Interior is investigating whether the police can use the fingerprints in the passport and identity card registers in crime prevention.

The police say that several serious crimes could be solved if the data is given for the use of criminal investigators.

The registers contain the fingerprints of about 3 million people.

The report will be given to the Parliament's Administration Committee in the coming weeks.

## Refrigeration equipment

![A storage area for products that need refrigeration, on the right fat packages, on the other side of the aisle ready meals, at the end of the aisle some customers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99502862fb85509f727/1660650968)

Open image viewer

Refrigeration equipment uses a lot of electricity. Open ones use more than closed ones. Freezer cabinets usually have a lid or door. Photo: Saara Hirvonen / Yle

In Finland the retail sector uses about 3–4 percent of all electricity. Most electricity is used for lighting and refrigeration equipment.

In many supermarkets there are tens of metres of open refrigerated shelves. They usually have ready meals, meats and dairy products.

Harri Heinaro, an expert at Motiva, the state's sustainable development company, recommends closed shelves. They can use about 40 percent less electricity than open shelves.

The biggest chains in the retail sector are S-Group, K-Group and Lidl. They say that the use of doors and covers is a recommendation in all shops that are being renovated.

Renewal will still take years.

## Corona and cortisol

![Corona home test showing a positive result.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3024,h_1701,x_0,y_1454/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98198662cff047c14e4/1657794732)

Corona illness is long-lasting for many people. Photo: Antti Seppälä / Yle

The amount of the stress hormone cortisol in the body can predict who will get prolonged corona disease. A US study tells about getting so-called long covid.

In the study, those who had got prolonged corona disease were compared with control groups.

In prolonged corona disease the blood cortisol level was about half lower than in the control groups.

## European Championship gold

![Wilma Murto celebrates.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4865,h_2736,x_0,y_101/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99582162fd7003eefc9/1660776476)

Open image viewer

Wilma Murto jumped European gold in the pole vault. Photo: Filip Singer / EPA

Still a happy sports news story.

Finland has got a new European champion in athletics. Wilma Murto won the pole vault European title in Munich late on Wednesday.

Murto won gold with a result of 485. Murto's previous Finnish record was improved now by 13 centimetres.

Murto's winning result of 485 is a European Championship record and the second best result of this year in the whole world.

Wilma Murto's win is historic: the last time a Finnish woman won European gold in athletics was in 1994.
