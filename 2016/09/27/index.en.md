# Tuesday 27.9.2016 (radio)

Finland has helped in the investigation of the Malaysian plane crash. A Member of Parliament is suspected of a crime. The police want the founder of MV-lehti remanded in custody. Clinton beat Trump in the election debate.

## Secret missile tests carried out in Finland

![Ukrainian workers collecting parts of flight MH17 at the crash site in Ukraine on 20 July 2014.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8161754)

Finland has helped to find out how the Malaysian plane was shot down. Missile tests have been carried out in Finland that have helped to find out how the Malaysian plane was shot down in Eastern Ukraine.

The investigators believe that the plane was shot down with Russian missiles. The research was done in Finland because the Defence Forces have the same BUK missiles. The National Bureau of Investigation has helped Dutch investigators in the criminal investigation. It is perhaps about examining explosive residue. The authorities do not want to say more about the research or when it was done in Finland.

At the time of the accident, the Malaysian passenger plane was flying from Amsterdam towards Kuala Lumpur. The accident happened in 2014, and almost 300 passengers died in it. Over half of the dead were Dutch.

## A Member of Parliament is suspected of a crime

![Teuvo Hakkarainen speaks in Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8536935)

Open image viewer

Member of Parliament Teuvo Hakkarainen speaks in Parliament.  Photo: Yle

Finns Party MP Teuvo Hakkarainen is suspected of a crime. The police have investigated the MP's Facebook post in which he accused Muslims of being terrorists. Hakkarainen wrote his message right after the terrorist attack in Nice.

The police say that Hakkarainen is suspected of a crime called ethnic agitation. The police think that Hakkarainen's post labelled a bigger group of people than was perhaps intended, but the threshold for a police investigation was crossed.

The matter now goes to the National Prosecution Authority. It considers charges, that is, decides whether the suspected crime goes to court.

## The police want the founder of the controversial MV-lehti remanded in custody

![Ilja Janitskin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9194906)

Open image viewer

The founder of MV-lehti, Ilja Janitskin Photo: Yle

The police and the prosecutor demand that the founder of the controversial MV-lehti be remanded in custody. The founder of MV-lehti, Ilja Janitskin, is suspected of many crimes, such as ethnic agitation and aggravated defamation.

Janitskin has not wanted to come to Finland voluntarily to sort things out, so the prosecutor is seeking to have him remanded in custody.

Many criminal complaints have been made about MV-lehti, because its stories often lie and insult people. MV-lehti opposes immigration.

## Clinton beat Trump in the election debate

![Donald Trump and Hillary Clinton stand behind lecterns](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9194824)

Open image viewer

Trump and Clinton met face to face for the first time.  Photo: Justin Lane / EPA

The US presidential candidates have debated on television for the first time. The Democrats' candidate Hillary Clinton and the Republicans' Donald Trump clashed hard. Clinton accused Trump of racism and avoiding taxes. Trump said that Clinton has been a bad secretary of state and is not fit to be president.

In a poll by 2 American television channels, over 60 percent of viewers said that Clinton was better than Trump in the debate. Only 27 percent of viewers thought Trump was better.

Experts say that both candidates were fairly careful. Trump at times lost his temper and often interrupted Clinton.
