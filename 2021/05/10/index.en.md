# Monday 10.5.2021 (radio)

Vartiainen and Sinnemäki have equal support for mayor of Helsinki. There are 143 new coronavirus infections. Norwegian returns to Finland in June. A whale got stuck in the Thames.

## Helsinki mayoral race

![Mayoral candidates Nasima Razmyar, Juhana Vartiainen and Anni Sinnemäki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11917119)

Mayoral candidates Nasima Razmyar, Juhana Vartiainen and Anni Sinnemäki. Photo: Yle

Yle has published a poll that measures the support of candidates for mayor of Helsinki.

Juhana Vartiainen of the National Coalition and Anni Sinnemäki of the Greens are almost level. Vartiainen's support is 19 percent. Sinnemäki is supported by 18.7 percent of respondents. Nasima Razmyar of the SDP has 14.5 percent support.

A new mayor of Helsinki will be chosen after the municipal elections. The post of mayor of Helsinki goes to the party that wins the municipal elections in Helsinki.

A mayor is a politician who leads the city. The current mayor of Helsinki is Jan Vapaavuori of the National Coalition.

## Corona infections

About a third of Finland's population has received a corona vaccination. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

143 new coronavirus infections have been found in Finland.

Over the course of 2 weeks there have been about 2,800 infections, which is almost 900 fewer than 2 weeks earlier. The total number of coronavirus infections in Finland is almost 90,000.

In Finland there are 2 new deaths caused by corona. In total there are now 924 corona deaths. There are 121 people in hospital care, of whom 26 are receiving intensive care.

Almost 35 percent of the population has now received the first dose of the corona vaccine.

Norwegian's passenger aircraft has been parked at Helsinki-Vantaa over the winter. The last scheduled flight on the aircraft was flown in August 2020, when it stopped in Trondheim. The aircraft returns to service at the end of June. Photo: Jari Tanskanen / Yle

The Norwegian airline Norwegian is starting flights from Finland again.

Norwegian will start flights from Helsinki-Vantaa at the end of June to about 20 destinations in Europe. Most of the flights go to holiday destinations in Spain, Greece and Croatia. Norwegian also flies from Helsinki to Oulu and Rovaniemi.

## A whale in the Thames

![Pilot whale.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5214381)

Open image viewer

In the picture is a pilot whale. Pilot whales usually live in the North Atlantic. Photo: Public domain

In England a small whale got stuck in the Thames river on Sunday. The whale got caught in a narrow lock of the river.

The whale is probably a pilot whale, which normally lives in the North Atlantic. Rescue workers freed the whale from the lock. After that, the whale was moved to a safer place.

The whale is injured. A veterinarian is assessing whether the whale can be released to freedom.
