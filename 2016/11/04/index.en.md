# Friday 4.11.2016 (radio)

6 books are competing for the Finlandia Prize. The medicines agency Fimea wants the right to close a pharmacy. The French police have taken down a migrants' tent camp in Paris. Abba is going to be brought back from the past with the help of virtual reality. All Saints' Day is celebrated on Saturday.

## The Finlandia Prize

![The nominees for the Finlandia Prize for literature.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9272606)

Open image viewer

The nominees for the Finlandia Prize for literature. In the picture from the left: Jukka Viikilä, Emma Puikkonen, Riku Korhonen, Peter Sandström, Tommi Kinnunen and Sirpa Kähkönen. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

On Friday it was announced which books are competing for the Finlandia Prize this year. The Finlandia Prize is Finland's most important literary prize.

6 books and their authors are competing for the Finlandia Prize. The themes of the books include, for example, relationships and history. Among the nominees is, for example, Sirpa Kähkönen, who is competing for the Finlandia Prize for the 4th time.

The winner of the Finlandia Prize is chosen by one person. This year journalist Baba Lybeck will choose the winner. She will announce on 24 November who wins the Finlandia Prize.

The Finlandia Prize is worth 30,000 euros.

## Fimea

![The Renkomäki pharmacy](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9271099)

Open image viewer

The Renkomäki pharmacy in Lahti. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

The medicines agency Fimea wants the right to close a pharmacy. Nowadays Fimea can only withdraw a pharmacy's licence, but the police decide on closing the pharmacy.

This is being discussed now because the Renkomäki pharmacy in Lahti may have sold wrong medicines. It has sold stronger and milder medicines than the doctor has prescribed.

Fimea thinks that it should have the right to close a pharmacy in cases like this.

## Tent camp

![Tents side by side on a littered street. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9272240)

Open image viewer

A tent camp in Paris. Photo: Yoan Valat / EPA

The French police have taken down a migrants' tent camp in Paris. The camp had been built without permission. There were almost 4,000 people in the camp. They are being taken to reception centres.

The French government has decided to move the migrants off the streets and take them to reception centres. Last week the French police cleared the migrant camp in the town of Calais.

## Abba

![The members of Abba in tight stage costumes on a smoky stage. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9273893)

At the Abba museum in Stockholm you can see the members of Abba as wax figures. Photo: Nora Lorek / EPA

Then a news item from the world of pop music.

The Swedish pop band Abba is going to be made familiar to today's young people with the help of digital technology. Virtual reality will bring Abba back from the past.

Abba made music 40 years ago. The members of Abba compare the new project to a time machine.

Abba's success began in 1974, when it won the Eurovision Song Contest. Abba then performed the song Waterloo.

## All Saints' Day

![A grave candle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8552009)

Open image viewer

On All Saints' Day candles burn on the graves. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

All Saints' Day is celebrated on Saturday. On All Saints' Day Finns remember dead family members and friends. For example, candles are taken to graves.

On All Saints' Day many shops are open, because nowadays shops can decide for themselves when they are open. There are changes in public transport. For example, Helsinki Region Transport follows Sunday timetables on All Saints' Day.
