# Monday 27.1.2020 (radio)

The forest industry strike has begun. Many Chinese people have cancelled their holidays in Lapland. Thousands of pre-birth child welfare notifications are made in Finland every year. Dry January is good for health. Many people visited the Schjerfbeck exhibition at the Ateneum.

## Forest industry strikes

![Strike guard at the gate of the UPM Kaukas mill in Lappeenranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11176854)

Strike guard at the gate of UPM's Kaukas mill in Lappeenranta. Photo: Lauri Heino / Lehtikuva

A big strike has begun in the forest industry. In total about 17,000 employees are on strike.

The strike is due to last 3 weeks. Dozens of paper and pulp mills are closed during the strike.

The strike is due to the fact that employers and employees have not managed to agree on working hours and pay. Negotiations continue again tomorrow.

## Chinese holidays in Lapland

![Asian tourists are not bothered by the crowds at tourist sites.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10648286)

Open image viewer

Chinese tourists visiting Santa Claus Village on the Arctic Circle. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

Many Chinese people have cancelled their holiday trip to Finnish Lapland this winter. About a thousand Chinese are cancelling the trip or moving it to another time, for example to next summer.

The cancellations are due to China having banned group trips abroad. With the ban, China wants to stop the spread of the coronavirus.

In Rovaniemi, for example, February is the busiest tourist month for Chinese people. Up to 10,000 Chinese tourists visit the city at that time.

Chinese people are an important group of visitors for Lapland. They spend much more money than other tourists.

## Pre-birth child welfare notifications

![A baby's feet](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9946279)

Open image viewer

Sometimes a child needs child welfare right after birth. Photo: AOP

Thousands of pre-birth child welfare notifications are made in Finland every year.

A pre-birth child welfare notification is made even before the child is born. It is made if there is a suspicion that the child will need child welfare right after birth.

The reason for the notification is often the mother's or both parents' use of intoxicants, or mental health problems. The notification is usually made by an authority such as the police or a child health clinic.

## Dry January

![Alcohol-free drink](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5645703)

Open image viewer

During Dry January you can make a cocktail with juice, for example. Photo: YLE Etelä-Karjala

Many Finns are spending Dry January now. Dry January means that during January no alcohol is drunk at all.

About 13 per cent of those Finns who otherwise drink alcohol spend Dry January every year.

Dry January can bring many kinds of benefit, says research professor Pia Mäkelä of the National Institute for Health and Welfare. For example, blood pressure can fall, sleep quality can improve and the mind can be refreshed. Dry January can also do good for relationships, and you can also save money during it.

## Ateneum's Schjerfbeck exhibition

![Helene Schjerfbeck at the Ateneum](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3144,h_1769,x_0,y_688/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6271085e0b3abc900a7/1577794359)

The Helene Schjerfbeck exhibition at the Ateneum Art Museum in Helsinki has been very popular.

The exhibition opened last November and it ended yesterday.

On average over 3,000 people a day visited the exhibition. That is a new record.
