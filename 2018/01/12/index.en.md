# Friday 12.1.2018 (radio)

The network company Nokia is cutting jobs. The Finnish economy is growing. A lot of housing is being built in Finland. Queen Elizabeth has given a rare interview. Young people are misusing energy drinks.

## Nokia is laying off workers

![Nokia's head office in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8791622)

Open image viewer

Nokia is now in the telecommunications field. Photo: Markku Ojala / AOP

The network company Nokia is cutting jobs. Nokia is cutting 80 jobs in Finland.

Earlier, Nokia estimated that it had to cut almost 200 jobs. In co-operation negotiations between Nokia and its employees, the number could be reduced. Some employees were found a new job within Nokia.

In addition, Nokia helps employees find new work, if they now lose their job.

## The economy is growing

![A man welds a steel beam.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6687460)

Open image viewer

The metal industry is doing well. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

The Finnish economy is growing. The Finnish Mortgage Association says that the economy is now growing quickly.

The growth is fast especially in the metal industry and the forest industry. The tourism sector is also doing well.

The Mortgage Association estimates that the growth will continue for many years yet.

## Homes are being built

![The construction site of the central library in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9781606)

Open image viewer

Many new homes are being built. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

A lot of housing is being built in Finland. Construction is brisk, because the Finnish economy is growing. Especially apartment blocks are being built more than at any time in decades.

Building housing affects rents, for example. There are so many new homes that rents may not rise as fast as before. That is good news for many tenants, because rents have risen faster than prices in recent years.

The information comes from an institute called Pellervo Economic Research.

## Queen Elizabeth

![Queen Elizabeth](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9686161)

Open image viewer

Queen Elizabeth II of Britain. Photo: Pool / EPA

Britain's Queen Elizabeth has given a rare interview to the BBC. The Queen spoke about the coronation held in 1953. Then the young queen received the symbols of power, for example the crown.

Now, 65 years later, Queen Elizabeth says that the crown was heavy on her head. It weighed a lot. The old carriages were also uncomfortable, the Queen says. Elizabeth went to the coronation in gilded carriages pulled by horses.

## Energy drinks

![Energy drink cans](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10018413)

Open image viewer

Energy drinks are not suitable for children. Photo: Juulia Tillaeus / Yle

Young people are misusing energy drinks. In Finland, young people have been taken to hospital because of energy drinks. Abroad, young people have even died.

Energy drinks contain a lot of caffeine. That is why they affect the heart. A large amount of caffeine can cause a heart attack.

Paediatrician Timo Sillanpää says that children should not drink energy drinks at all.

Young people often drink energy drinks because they do not sleep enough. Many play on the computer all night and stay awake with the help of caffeine.
