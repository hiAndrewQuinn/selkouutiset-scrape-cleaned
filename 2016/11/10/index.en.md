# Thursday 10.11.2016 (radio)

Donald Trump has met Brack Obama. There have been demonstrations in the USA because of Trump's election victory. A Finnish courtroom is a matter-of-fact place. Woollen socks are being knitted for veterans. A really large amount of snow has fallen in Stockholm.

## Trump and Obama

![The White House lit up at night](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9284618)

President Obama and future president Trump met in the White House, where the US president lives. Photo: Michaekl Reynolds / EPA

Donald Trump has met president Barack Obama in the White House. This was the first time that Trump and Obama met one-on-one.

Trump of the Republican Party will become the new president of the USA, because he beat the Democrats' Hillary Clinton in the election.

Trump will start as president of the USA on 20 January.

## Demonstrations in the USA

![A demonstrator with a sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9283456)

Open image viewer

A demonstration against Trump in New York. Photo: Alba Vigaray / EPA

Some Americans are angry because of Donald Trump's victory.

People demonstrated against Trump, for example, in New York and Washington. They are angry with Trump because Trump has spoken badly about, for example, Mexican immigrants. During the election campaign Trump said that he will build a wall on the border between Mexico and the USA so that illegal immigration will end.

It may take a long time before Americans forget the quarrels of the election campaign.

## A Finnish courtroom

![A judge behind a desk in a district courtroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5717188)

Open image viewer

You must be matter-of-fact in court. Photo: YLE Tampere

A Finnish courtroom is a matter-of-fact place. People there do not behave the same way as in American TV series.

A judge must not be emotional. Judge Asko Nurmi says that an emotional judge is as bad a thing as a drunk surgeon.

Others must also behave properly in court. For example, you may cover your face only when photographers are present. As soon as the photographers leave and the trial begins, the hood or newspaper must be taken away from in front of the face.

## Veterans' woollen socks

![Finished veteran socks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9269991)

Open image viewer

Woollen socks for veterans. Photo: Jarkko Heikkinen/Yle

Many Finns are now knitting woollen socks for veterans. The idea was born on Facebook.

The veterans were soldiers in the Finnish army more than 70 years ago, when there was a war in Finland. The last veterans are already really old.

Jaana Willman, the founder of the Facebook group, says that the gift of socks is meant to honour the veterans. The goal is that all veterans get woollen socks next year, when independent Finland turns 100.

## Stockholm's snowfall

![A man skis on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9283724)

Open image viewer

A skier got along well in Stockholm. Photo: Claudio Bresciani / EPA

A lot of snow has fallen in Stockholm, the capital of Sweden. More snow has fallen than ever before in November.

Because of the snowfall, Stockholm's roads and streets were blocked on Thursday. Snowploughs could not clear the snow from the streets, because there were many cars on the streets that had got stuck in the snow.

The authorities said that Stockholm residents should stay at home if possible. On Friday it will no longer snow.
