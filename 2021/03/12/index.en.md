# Friday 12.3.2021 (radio)

The number of coronavirus patients in intensive care is growing. There will also be a mask requirement on public transport in Tampere. Brazil has many coronavirus deaths. Teemu Pukki is the English Championship's player of the month. At the weekend the temperature in the south is above zero.

## Coronavirus figures

![A patient in intensive care in France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11558692)

The number of coronavirus patients in intensive care is growing.

There are now about 50 coronavirus patients in intensive care in Finland. In total, more than 250 coronavirus patients are in hospital care.

On Friday it was reported that there are about 700 new coronavirus infections in Finland and 3 new coronavirus deaths.

About 10 percent of Finns have been vaccinated against the coronavirus at least once.

Finland must reassess the order in which coronavirus vaccinations are given. This is proposed by Juha Tuominen, chief executive of the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district.

In Tuominen's view, people living in areas with a lot of coronavirus also belong to risk groups.

There are many coronavirus infections, for example, in Itäkeskus and Vuosaari in Helsinki, especially in families who speak a foreign language.

In Tuominen's view, there is a great need for coronavirus vaccinations in areas like these.

More mask requirements are coming to transport. A mask requirement begins on Monday on public transport in the Tampere region.

On public transport in the Helsinki region, the mask requirement begins now, on Saturday. Passengers must wear a mask on buses, the metro and trams.

The mask requirement already applies on VR's trains.

## Brazil

![A worker in protective clothing with an umbrella in hand walks past the graves of coronavirus patients in Manaus, Brazil.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11833747)

Open image viewer

Graves of coronavirus patients in Manaus, Brazil. Photo: Michael Dantas / AFP

Brazil, the largest country in South America, is suffering from the coronavirus.

Last week more than 10,000 Brazilians died because of the coronavirus. Now more than 2,000 people are dying per day.

Hospitals no longer have room for all coronavirus patients. Coronavirus patients have died while waiting for an intensive care place.

However, Brazilian President Jair Bolsonaro does not want coronavirus restrictions in the country.

## Pukki

Finnish footballer Teemu Pukki is doing well in England.

Pukki is the English Championship's player of the month. Pukki scored 7 goals in February.

Pukki plays for Norwich. Norwich lead the Championship, and they have good chances of getting promoted to the Premier League.

On Saturday it is cloudy across the whole country.

Snow falls in the central part of the country and in Lapland, and in the south rain or sleet falls.

On Sunday temperatures above zero will be measured in almost the whole country.
