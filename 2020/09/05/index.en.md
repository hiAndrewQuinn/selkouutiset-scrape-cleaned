# Saturday 5.9.2020 (radio)

The chairperson of the Centre. The party congress of the National Coalition. Corona infections in Finland. The forest company UPM. Rainy weather.

## The Centre's new chairperson is Annika Saarikko

![Annika Saarikko, elected chairperson of the Centre Party, at the party congress in Oulu on 5 September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11529558)

Annika Saarikko is the new chairperson of the Centre Party. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The Centre Party has elected a new chairperson.

The new chairperson is Annika Saarikko. She was the favourite in advance.

Annika Saarikko won by a wide margin against Katri Kulmuni, who was the chairperson of the Centre Party before.

Saarikko got over 1,100 votes and Kulmuni over 770 votes. Katri Kulmuni was chairperson for only one year.

Annika Saarikko is the Minister of Science and Culture in the government.

![Petteri Orpo was re-elected chairperson of the National Coalition at the party congress in Pori at the Isomäki ice hall on Saturday 5 September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11529246)

There is another party congress this weekend. The opposition party National Coalition also chose its chairperson.

The National Coalition had only 1 candidate, so it was clear that Petteri Orpo would continue as party leader.

Both the Centre and the National Coalition held their party congress with special arrangements because of the corona.

Some of the participants at the party congress took part by remote connection.

Today, 36 new corona infections have been reported in Finland.

In Kainuu in Eastern Finland, about 200 people are in quarantine because of a wide corona exposure.

In Kuhmo, many people were exposed to the coronavirus last week, and since then a total of 25 corona infections have been found.

## Cost competitiveness

![UPM CEO Jussi Pesonen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11529165)

Open image viewer

Jussi Pesonen was interviewed by Yle today. Photo: Leila Oksa / Yle

Jussi Pesonen, the head of the forest company UPM, explained today why UPM decided to close a big factory in Finland.

In an interview with Yle, Pesonen said that the company has only one reason to keep factories running.

The reason is cost competitiveness.

Jussi Pesonen, the head of the forest company UPM, said that the world needs a lot more paper than Finland can make.

Factories must be efficient, and the costs of making paper must be competitive, Pesonen said.

Pesonen thinks that in Finland industry is no longer understood. He means that governments in Finland cannot make decisions that are good for industry.
