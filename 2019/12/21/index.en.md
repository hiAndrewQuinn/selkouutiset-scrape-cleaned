# Saturday 21.12.2019 (radio)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin hopes for a calmer time in politics. Christmas is a hard time for a person with an eating disorder. Finnish filmmakers are interested in Chinese money. Film locations in Finland were sought for Hollywood films. Shop workers are doing more shift work and part-time work.

## Marin hopes for a calmer time in politics

![Sanna Marin on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme on 21.12.2019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11131594)

Sanna Marin. Photo: Yle

Prime Minister Sanna Marin hopes that a calmer time will begin in Finnish politics after the autumn turmoil. Marin was interviewed on the TV programme Ykkösaamu.

Prime Minister Marin criticised the National Coalition Party's remarks on the matter of the Finns in the al-Hol camp. Marin said that the National Coalition does not put children's interests above everything else. Marin also said that in a democracy the government does not make child welfare decisions; authorities make them.

## Dieting talk off the Christmas table

![Christmas casseroles](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_360/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6256025dfcdcbe4bf57/1576852710)

Christmas is a hard time for a person with an eating disorder.

Food is an important part of celebrating Christmas: relatives meet and eat together. Even if a person has anorexia or another eating disorder, they may want to take part at the Christmas table. However, they do not want others to pay much attention to what they eat.

Many people feel anxious if the table talk is full of calories and dieting.

## Chinese money for a Kaurismäki film

![Actors Anna-Maija Tuokko and Chu Pak Hong dance in the film Master Cheng.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11131452)

Actors Anna-Maija Tuokko and Chu Pak Hong in the film Master Cheng. Photo: Jouko Piipponen / Marianna Films

Finnish filmmakers are interested in co-operation with China.

Director Mika Kaurismäki has already made a film partly with Chinese state money. The film is called Master Cheng, and it is about a Chinese chef who moves to Lapland and falls in love.

The Chinese state only gives money to films that do not criticise Chinese politics. Director Mika Kaurismäki says that he is not worried about Chinese censorship or propaganda. Kaurismäki says that he only makes films that he stands behind himself.

## Hollywood films look for locations in Finland

![Group photo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6253395dfc9f6fd6b3b/1576837434)

Open image viewer

Hollywood people in Vuokatti in Kainuu. Photo: Timo Sihvonen / Yle

A group of American filmmakers from Hollywood has been looking for filming locations in Finland.

This week the group has visited Kainuu, Oulu, Turku and the Helsinki region, for example. In Oulu the group was interested in the blue light of the afternoon. In Vuokatti the group praised the snowy trees and the scenery.

Foreign filmmakers are attracted to Finland, for example, by getting back 25 per cent of the money they spend on filming in Finland.

## Shift work increases in shops

![Portrait](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_333/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6226285df3a2c665a37/1576248048)

Open image viewer

Grocery shops now do millions of hours more work every year than before.

Shop opening hours were freed up 4 years ago, and since then shops have offered more work. Part-time work has grown faster than full-time work

There is also more shift work, because many shops are open in the evening, at night and on public holidays. This Christmas, shops are open again more than last Christmas. About 60 S Group shops are open almost all the time, 24 hours a day.
