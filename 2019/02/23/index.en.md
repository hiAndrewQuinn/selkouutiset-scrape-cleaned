# Saturday 23.2.2019 (radio)

Sote reform. The situation in Venezuela. The ski world championships. Pollen. Warm weather.

## The progress of the sote reform is uncertain

![Parliament building](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10556058)

Open image viewer

MPs will go on their election break in three weeks, because the parliamentary elections are held in April. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Politicians in Finland disagree about what will happen to the government's sote reform. Sote means social and health services.

The government's proposal still needs to be changed, because it is not in line with the constitution.

Some politicians say that Parliament still has time to deal with the changes. Others say there is too little time.

MPs will leave Parliament for the election break in three weeks.

## The situation in Venezuela is tense

![Venezuelans remove security fences set up by the country's army in the border town of Ureña on 23 February 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10660255)

Open image viewer

The Venezuelan army has set up barriers at the Colombian border. Photo: Hector Perreira / EPA

In South America, the situation in Venezuela is tense.

Ordinary citizens are trying to bring food and medicine from Colombia to Venezuela. Soldiers of Venezuela's army are stopping them, because the country's president does not want any help from abroad.

The soldiers have used tear gas against people.

## Norway won 2 gold medals

![Iivo Niskanen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10660289)

Open image viewer

Iivo Niskanen had to ski very hard, because he fell during the competition. Photo: Lehtikuva

In the ski world championships, competitors skied the combined events today.

In the men's competition, Finland's Iivo Niskanen was 4th, even though he fell. However, he caught up with the other skiers in the leading group. The men's competition was won by Norway's Sjur Röthe.

In the women's competition, the best Finn was Krista Pärmäkoski. She was 8th in the competition. Norway's Therese Johaug won the women's 15-kilometre competition.

## There is a lot of pollen in the spring

![spring birch branches](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10564536)

Open image viewer

The elongated catkin of the birch has many names. It may be a catkin or a bud. Photo: AOP

This spring there is a lot of tree pollen in the air. Pollen causes unpleasant symptoms for people who are allergic.

Last summer was hot in Finland, and that is why the birches and alders are flowering very much this spring. The birches and alders only flower later in the spring.

But pollen is already coming to Finland from abroad. In Central Europe it is now so warm that the trees are flowering. The pollen of the trees comes to Finland with air currents.
