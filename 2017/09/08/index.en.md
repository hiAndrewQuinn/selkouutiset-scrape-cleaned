## Return flights from regional airports

![A demonstration against the return of asylum seekers at Helsinki-Vantaa airport on 4 July 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9821434)

Open image viewer

A demonstration at Helsinki-Vantaa airport in July. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The police want to move return flights of asylum seekers from Helsinki-Vantaa to other airports.

People sometimes organise demonstrations when they oppose sending asylum seekers back to their home country. Big demonstrations disturb other passengers and flights at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. That is why the police are moving flights to smaller airports that have less traffic.

For example, this week one return flight left at night from Lappeenranta to Afghanistan. The flight took 9 young Afghan men to Kabul who were not given asylum in Finland.

## Earthquake in Mexico

![Patients and doctors outdoors in a hospital yard during the earthquake in Villahermosa, Mexico, on 8 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9822491)

Open image viewer

Patients are treated outdoors after the earthquake. Photo: STR / EPA

There has been a strong earthquake in Mexico. The earthquake had a magnitude of 8.2, and it happened at sea 100 kilometres from the coast.

According to the first reports, at least 20 or 30 people have died. There are probably more victims, because apartment buildings have collapsed.

In 1985 Mexico had an almost equally strong earthquake. Thousands of people died in it.

## Crash often caused by a sudden illness

![Rescue vehicles at the crash site in Paukarlahti in April 2016](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9821254)

Open image viewer

A crash site. Photo: Yle

Today the cause of a bad crash is often a sudden attack of illness or suicide. Researchers estimate that as many as half of fatal crashes are like that, for example in Eastern Finland.

However, the police and doctors cannot always be sure what caused a crash. A sudden illness may not show up in an autopsy, and not everyone who commits suicide leaves a message.

## Spede made people laugh for 50 years

![spede](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9818340)

Open image viewer

Pertti "Spede" Pasanen.

A book has been written about the life of Pertti Pasanen, or Spede.

Spede made Finns laugh for over 50 years. He made dozens of films and many television series. Finns liked the Uuno Turhapuro films the most. Film journalists in the media did not understand Spede, and they criticised Spede's films and TV programmes harshly. Many younger makers of TV programmes have learned a lot from Spede.

Spede Pasanen died 16 years ago.

## Solar storm made northern lights

![Northern lights in Rovaniemi in autumn 2016](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9133856)

Open image viewer

These northern lights were photographed in Rovaniemi in autumn 2016. Photo: Jussi Perkkiö

A solar storm was noticed in Finland on Friday night.

A magnetic storm from the sun sometimes disrupts satellite connections and electricity distribution. There were no such disruptions in Finland.

Because of the strong solar wind, however, there were northern lights in the sky all over the country. In a large part of the country, however, they stayed behind clouds. There will quite certainly be northern lights on two more nights, until Sunday.
