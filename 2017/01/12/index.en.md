# Thursday 12.1.2017 (radio)

There has been a winter storm in Finland. Chinese tourists can pay with Alipay in many places in Finland. Primary schools are thinking about when pupils may use their phones. There are many rats around the statue of Aleksis Kivi at Railway Square in Helsinki. Sulkava's first block of flats will be finished in the summer.

## Thursday's winter storm

![The winter storm is a reason for joy for keen surfers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9401133)

This surfer was happy about the winter storm. Photo: Yle

There was a winter storm in Finland on Thursday. The wind blew really hard.

At sea, the wind caused trouble for ships. Some ships were late on Thursday.

A record wave also formed at sea. It was 15 metres high. The wave was as high as a building with 5 floors. The wave was seen south of Åland.

On Thursday evening the wind was already weaker and the winter storm was over.

## Mobile payments by Chinese people

![Alipay at the Arctic Circle Pajakylä in Rovaniemi ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9397526)

Open image viewer

Chinese people can already pay with Alipay in many shops in Lapland and Helsinki. Photo: Raimo Torikka / Yle

Chinese people are interested in Finland. Many tourists come to Finland from China. Lapland and Helsinki are Chinese people's favourite places in Finland.

In Finland, people hope that the Chinese enjoy their stay and spend a lot of money. That is why in many shops and hotels you can now also pay with Alipay. Alipay is a mobile payment that Chinese people use a lot.

Many Chinese people are used to paying with their phone. They are happy when they notice that they can pay with Alipay in Finland. Jing Yang from Beijing was surprised by an Alipay sign at Santa Claus's workshop in Rovaniemi. She will buy at least reindeer antlers and a reindeer hide in Finland.

## Primary school pupils' phones

![Children play in the courtyard of Snellman school in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8624085)

Open image viewer

Exercise and play are part of primary school pupils' break time. Photo: Petteri Juuti / Yle

In many primary schools, pupils are not allowed to use their phones even during breaks. The schools want children to move and play together.

However, last year the Finnish National Board of Education gave an instruction that phone use cannot be completely banned in schools. Now schools are thinking about how to solve the matter.

Not all pupils want phones to be used completely freely, because a phone can also be used, for example, for bullying.

## The rats of Railway Square

![rat, helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9012369)

Open image viewer

Rats at Railway Square in Helsinki. Photo: Ella Mikkola/ Yle

Railway Square in Helsinki has a rat problem. There are many rats around the statue of Aleksis Kivi because someone brings them food.

But the rats make a mess of the area. That is why the City of Helsinki wants the rats gone from the Aleksis Kivi statue. The city may decide already in January what to do with the rats.

## Sulkava's block of flats

![Sulkava's first block of flats will be finished in July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9399842)

Open image viewer

Sulkava's first block of flats will be finished in the summer. Photo: Jukka Partanen

One more news item from the small municipality of Sulkava. A new era begins in Sulkava when the municipality's first block of flats is finished in the summer.

The building has 4 floors and a lift. Until now, Sulkava has had only one lift, which is in the municipal hall.

The people of Sulkava are proud of the municipality's first block of flats. Fewer than 3000 people live in Sulkava.
