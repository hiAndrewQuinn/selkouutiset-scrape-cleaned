# Saturday 11.5.2019 (radio)

The Ombudsman for Children says that the children of Finnish foreign fighters must be brought to Finland. Russians are applying for many visas to Finland. Timo Soini is writing his next book. The number of barnacle geese is rising again. The ice hockey World Championships have started.

## Ombudsman for Children: Isis children must be brought home

![Professor of social work Elina Pekkarinen, Ombudsman for Children from 1 May 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10675413)

Ombudsman for Children Elina Pekkarinen. Photo: Jouni Koutonen / Yle

The new Ombudsman for Children says that the children of Finnish foreign fighters must be brought to Finland for safety.

Ombudsman for Children Elina Pekkarinen says in a newspaper interview that, for example, the children of Finnish Isis fighters have the same right to protection as other Finnish children.

The Finnish Security Intelligence Service says that at least 80 people have travelled from Finland to the conflict areas of Syria and Iraq. About 30 of them are children. Children have also been born in the area who have a link to Finland.

## Russians apply for visas

![Visa application at the Finnish Consulate General in St Petersburg.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10534482)

Open image viewer

Russians are now applying for Finnish visas at a record pace. Photo: Kari Kosonen / Yle

Russians are now applying for many visas to Finland.

The Lännen Media reports that, for example, about 63,000 visa applications were submitted in April to the Finnish Consulate General in St Petersburg. That is 37 per cent more than in April last year.

Russians are now also applying for many visas to other EU countries. The number of applications has grown, for example, in the Czech Republic, France and Spain.

## Timo Soini writes a book

![Timo Soini](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10778705)

Open image viewer

Timo Soini plans to write a new book. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / Yle

Foreign Minister Timo Soini has told about his plans for the future.

Soini says that he plans to write a book about populism. The book is due to be ready this year.

Populism is a way of doing politics. A populist puts forward strong opinions and big promises. With these, the populist tries to win the people's favour.

Soini is a former leader of the Finns Party. In recent years he has been in the Blue Future party.

Soini said in April that he is leaving politics. He will carry on as Foreign Minister until a new government is formed in Finland.

## Barnacle geese are on migration

![Barnacle goose sightings this spring in the Oulu region.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6099773)

Open image viewer

Barnacle geese are endangered. Photo: Tapio Kostet / WWF Finland

A lot of barnacle geese have been seen in Finland this week. The environmental organisation WWF says that more than 100 barnacle geese have already been counted in the Oulu region.

The birds have stopped in the Oulu region just to rest. They are on migration from Greece to Lapland in Norway, where they breed.

Still in the early 2000s, fewer than 10 barnacle geese a year were seen in Finland. Now, for many springs in a row, more than 100 birds have been seen in the Oulu region.

## Ice hockey World Championships have started

![Kaapo Kakko](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10778730)

The ice hockey World Championships started yesterday, Friday, in Slovakia.

Finland's first match was against Canada. Canada was the favourite, but Finland surprised everyone and won the match 3-1.

Today, Saturday, Finland is playing against Slovakia. The game starts after 9 pm.
