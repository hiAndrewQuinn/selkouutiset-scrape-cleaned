# Friday 24.3.2017 (radio)

Arrests have been made in Britain because of the London terror attack. It is hard for a person with an intellectual disability to find paid work. Volunteers sewed clothes for premature babies. The price of a driving licence has fallen. A tent of asylum seekers was set on fire.

## Police are investigating the London terror attack

![Three women light candles](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9527910)

Open image viewer

Londoners lit candles in memory of those killed in the terror attack. Photo: Facundo Arrizabalaga / EPA

The British police have arrested people because of Wednesday's terror attack in London. The police have arrested 9 people in total.

The police are also asking the public for tips, so that the background of the attacker can be found out.

A 52-year-old British man killed 4 people near the Houses of Parliament in London on Wednesday. The police shot the attacker.

The police are now finding out whether the man acted alone or whether he had helpers.

## Riikka's dream job was found in a restaurant

![Riikka Lumme at her workstation in the kitchen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9524105)

Open image viewer

Riikka Lumme got a real paid job about 4 years ago. Photo: Kari Kosonen/Yle

It is hard for people with intellectual disabilities to find a real paid job.

Finding work is difficult, because employers are suspicious. Employers think that people with intellectual disabilities are not able to do a real job.

In many municipalities, the only option for people with intellectual disabilities is open work, which is unpaid. About 2,000 people with intellectual disabilities do open work.

Riikka Lumme from Lappeenranta is lucky. She also did open work before, but about 4 years ago she got a real paid job in a restaurant. Riikka says that she is now in her dream job.

The Finnish Association for Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities says that perhaps about 500 people with intellectual disabilities have a real paid job.

## Tiny clothes for tiny babies

![Baby Salli.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9521523)

Open image viewer

Salli got new clothes. Photo: Yle

A large pile of tiny clothes has been donated to the Helsinki Women's Clinic. The clothes are for premature babies.

Premature babies are children who have been born early. They have been born before the mother's pregnancy has lasted 9 months.

Premature babies are often so small that it is hard to find suitable clothes for them.

More than 70 volunteers in different parts of Finland have sewn clothes for premature babies since last autumn. Almost 2,000 garments have now been donated to the hospital.

## You can get a driving licence more cheaply than before

![Night driving practice in a simulator](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9526824)

Open image viewer

Jukka Varis is practising driving in the dark in a simulator. Mikko Nuutinen gives advice.

The price of a driving licence has fallen over the last couple of years.  The driving licence for cars, that is the B licence, is now a couple of hundred euros cheaper than in autumn 2015.

The price drop is due, for example, to driving schools now trying out new ways of teaching. For example, more and more theory teaching is given on the internet.

Earlier driving experience can also lower the price of a driving licence.

## An asylum seekers' tent caught fire

![The tent of asylum seekers protesting was an attempted arson early on Friday morning at Helsinki's Rautatientori.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9528308)

Open image viewer

A hole burned in the protest tent of the asylum seekers. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The police are investigating a fire that broke out early on Friday morning in an asylum seekers' tent at Helsinki's Rautatientori.

The police suspect sabotage, that is, that someone set the tent on fire on purpose.

One person was injured while the fire was being put out.
