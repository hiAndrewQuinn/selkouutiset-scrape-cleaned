# Thursday 11.4.2019 (radio)

Young adults are satisfied with their own health. The British police have arrested the founder of WikiLeaks, Julian Assange. The army has taken power in Sudan. Support for the Finns Party has risen further.

## Youth health

![Vegetables in a basket.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9831586)

Open image viewer

Young people do not eat enough vegetables. Photo: Helena Lappeteläinen / Yle

Young Finnish adults are satisfied with their own health. Many under-30s think that they have good health and a good life. The information is from a new study by the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare.

The study also shows, however, that young adults under 30 do not always have very healthy lifestyles. For example, about 50 percent of young men and 35 percent of young women are overweight. Young people do not eat enough vegetables, and they sleep too little.

## WikiLeaks founder was arrested

![In the picture Julian Assange is in a police car. He is showing a thumbs up with his right hand. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10734691)

Open image viewer

Julian Assange in a police car. Photo: Reuters

The British police have arrested the founder of WikiLeaks, Julian Assange. WikiLeaks is a website that publishes secret information.

The police arrested Assange at the Ecuadorian embassy in London. Assange had been in the embassy for 7 years. The police arrested Assange now because Ecuador withdrew Assange's asylum.

Assange fears that Britain will extradite him to the USA. There he could be sentenced for data breaches.

## Sudan's president resigned

![Omar al-Bashir ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10733448)

Open image viewer

Omar al-Bashir. Photo: Ashraf Shazly / AFP

The army has taken power in African Sudan. The army forced President Omar al-Bashir to resign.

People have celebrated the president's resignation. The president is suspected of genocide and war crimes. Omar al-Bashir has been president of Sudan for 30 years.

The army intends to hold power for 2 years. After that elections will be held in Sudan.

## Election day approaches

![Party support measurement April 2019](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10732219)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

The Finnish parliamentary elections are on Sunday. Yle's new poll shows that the elections will be exciting.

Yle asked Finns at the start of April which party they support. No party got more than 20 percent support.

The largest support is for the SDP, that is, the Social Democrats. 19 percent of respondents support the Social Democrats. That is, however, less than in the March poll.

Support for the Finns Party has grown. The Finns Party's support is now 16 percent.

The Finns Party is the second largest party in the new poll. The National Coalition and the Centre have fallen behind the Finns Party.

The support figures for all parties can be found on Yle's website.

Yle's election programmes are at yle.fi/vaalit. The plain-language election debate is also included.
