## Support for nuclear power in Parliament

![Olkiluoto nuclear power plant seen from above](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3565,h_1998,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98272862d17399004b5/1666959601)

The Olkiluoto nuclear power plant area. The newest nuclear power plant is Olkiluoto 3, which is on the right in the picture. Photo: Lucas Ekblad / Yle

Most MPs support adding more nuclear power in Finland. Yle has asked MPs whether more nuclear power is needed in Finland.

There are 200 MPs in Parliament. At least 110 MPs support adding nuclear power, which is a clear majority.

Only 8 MPs oppose adding nuclear power. 11 MPs could not say what they think about the matter. 71 MPs did not answer Yle's survey.

Support for nuclear power has grown quickly because of the energy crisis. Finland must itself produce more energy without emissions.

## Russian attacks on Ukraine

![Smoke rises from the city of Kyiv.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1677,h_943,x_0,y_173/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1027554636004deb2427/1667237138)

Open image viewer

Several missiles hit Kyiv on Monday. Photo: Vladyslav Sodel / Reuters

Russia has carried out dozens of missile strikes on Ukraine on Monday. Ukraine says that Russia fired more than 50 missiles at Ukraine.

Many missiles went to the capital, Kyiv, but also to other Ukrainian cities. Russia fired missiles at power plants and water plants, for example.

Russian attacks cut off electricity and water supply for many Ukrainians. Ukrainians are repairing the damage so that water and electricity can be restored.

## Grain exports from Ukraine

![Ships anchored off Istanbul.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4860,h_2735,x_0,y_508/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1027091635e91eb7c63a/1667142219)

Open image viewer

Grain ships anchored off a port in Ukraine. Photo: Erdem Sahin / EPA

Russia has withdrawn from the grain export agreement for Ukraine. Russia withdrew from the agreement at the weekend. The agreement gave Ukraine the chance to export grain abroad.

Ukraine is a big grain exporter. Ukraine's grain deliveries are important for the food supply of many countries. If grain deliveries cannot be exported, famine may increase, especially in poor African countries.

The price of grain has also started to rise immediately, because grain exports from Ukraine are becoming more difficult.

Experts say that Russia withdrew from the grain export agreement because Russia wants to put pressure on the West. However, several grain ships were still able to leave Ukraine on Monday.

## Result of the Brazilian presidential election

![Brazil's incoming president Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva gives a victory speech surrounded by supporters at an election event in São Paulo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_122/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1027139635f2cf119f97/1667200230)

Open image viewer

Brazil's new president Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva thanked voters when he won the election. Photo: Nelson Almeida / AFP

The president of Brazil, the largest country in South America, is changing. The left-wing Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva has won the Brazilian presidential election.

He received about 51 percent of the votes in the second round of the election. That means that Lula defeated the sitting president, Jair Bolsonaro.

Leaders of many countries congratulated Brazil's new president, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, on Monday.
