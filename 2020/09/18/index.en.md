# Friday 18.9.2020 (radio)

Corona infections are growing in Finland. The coronavirus has affected Finland's economy. Estonia bans the sale of alcohol at night because of corona. Lapland's autumn colour trips have started. Damage from storm Aila is still being repaired. The weather is beautiful at the weekend.

## Corona infections in Finland

![People wearing face masks at Helsinki railway station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11520981)

Open image viewer

Face masks slow down the spread of the coronavirus. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen/Yle

The number of corona infections is growing in Finland. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, says that the number of infections has clearly grown.

During the last 2-week period about 580 new corona infections have been found. That is 240 more than in the previous 2-week period.

In Finland there are almost 9,000 corona infections in total.

## Economic figures from the corona spring

![View from the top of Saana fell](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11520481)

Open image viewer

Tourism to Finland has decreased because of the corona. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The coronavirus has affected Finland's economy.

For example, the tourism sector has suffered a lot from the corona. In April, May and June about 90 percent fewer tourists came to Finland than a year earlier.

Finns have also sold fewer goods and services abroad than a year ago. During the spring and early summer, exports abroad fell by almost 20 percent.

## Sale of alcohol in Estonia

![A bottle of hand sanitiser on a bar counter](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_450/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7086785f39069a67395/1598527205)

Open image viewer

The sale of alcohol at night will stop in Estonia for a while. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Estonia bans the sale of alcohol at night. The ban starts in a week, and it will last for at least a month.

The reason for the ban is the coronavirus. Estonia has many new corona infections, and the Estonian government wants to stop the spread of the coronavirus.

During the ban, alcohol may not be sold in bars, hotels or nightclubs in Estonia between midnight and 10 o'clock.

## Autumn colour trips in Lapland

![Autumn colour traveller Antti Paavilainen with his sons Ossi and Topi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_322/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7205945f646258a711d/1600414370)

Open image viewer

Autumn colour trips are now also interesting for young families. Photo: Annu Passoja / Yle

The autumn colour trips in Lapland have started. Tourists are coming back to Lapland to admire the colours of autumn.

Corona has also changed autumn colour tourism. Usually groups of pensioners go on autumn colour trips, but now pensioners are not travelling because of the corona.

Now there are many younger tourists in Lapland. Families with children and couples who cannot get south because of the corona have set off on autumn colour trips.

It is said from Lapland that the autumn colours are especially beautiful this year.

## Damage from storm Aila

![A branch brought down by the wind in Kaivopuisto in Helsinki on 17 September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11550478)

Open image viewer

Traces of storm Aila in Helsinki. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The damage from storm Aila is still being repaired. Storm Aila raged in Finland on Thursday.

The storm knocked down trees and cut off power to thousands of homes. Storm Aila also disturbed traffic.

The repair work continues at the weekend.

The weekend weather is beautiful across a large part of Finland. In the south it does not rain, and the sun may shine. In the north, however, there will be showers, and the weather there is windy.

The temperature may rise to 15 degrees in the south. In the north it is colder.
