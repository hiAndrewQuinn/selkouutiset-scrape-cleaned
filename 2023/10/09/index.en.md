## Israel and Gaza

![Fire and smoke over Gaza City in the early morning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_390/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-118290765222aeb47f85/1696738326)

An Israeli air strike on Gaza lights up the night sky. Photo: Eyad Baba / AFP

More than 1200 people have already died in the violence between Israel and Gaza. Thousands of people have also been injured.

The Palestinian militant group Hamas started rocket attacks on Israel early on Saturday. Hamas fighters also broke through from the Gaza Strip into Israel.

Israel has carried out air strikes on Gaza and called up 300 000 reservists. Israel has also imposed a full siege on Gaza. This means, for example, that electricity and water are cut off in the area and deliveries of food and fuel are blocked.

About 2.3 million people live in the Gaza Strip.

## Finnair cancels flights

![A Finnair Airbus A350 plane lands at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5759,h_3239,x_0,y_299/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114576964ba4715aab3a/1720679031)

Open image viewer

Finnair is cancelling flights to Tel Aviv. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

The airline Finnair is cancelling flights to Tel Aviv in Israel this week. Finnair also says that it will not use Israeli airspace.

The Finnish travel agency Toiviomatkat says that it currently has dozens of tourists on a tour of Israel. There are 80 Finns in total in Toiviomatkat's 2 groups.

Toiviomatkat says that it now depends on Finnair, because the Finns can get home. Originally they were due to return to Finland on Friday.

## The Hakunila fire

![A teddy bear and a memorial candle next to the wall of a house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3024,h_1701,x_0,y_2268/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11829856522af8a94ccf/1696772175)

Open image viewer

The mother and 4 children died in the Hakunila fire. Photo: Saara Hirvonen / Yle

The authorities are continuing to investigate the fire in Hakunila, Vantaa.

A fire broke out in a flat in an apartment block on Sunday night. The mother of the family and 4 children died in it. Only the father of the family survived.

The Accident Investigation Board of Finland, Otkes, says that the fire started in the sauna. Otkes says that there were also smoke alarms in the flat, but it is not yet known whether they worked.

## The President's salary

![Sauli Niinistö speaking.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4987,h_2805,x_0,y_404/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116328664eb5e8765fd3/1695056947)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö has been paid a smaller salary, because he wanted it that way. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The next president of Finland will get a much bigger salary than the current president, Sauli Niinistö.

The new president will get 160 000 euros a year, while Niinistö's salary has been 126 000 euros a year. Niinistö's salary is smaller because he asked for a lower salary in 2013.

The president does not pay tax on the salary.

A new president will be elected in Finland next year. The first round of the election is in January.
