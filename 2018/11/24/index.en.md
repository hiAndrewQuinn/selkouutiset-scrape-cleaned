# Saturday 24.11.2018 (radio)

Four Finns died in a plane crash in Zimbabwe. There were riots in France. The Centre opened its election campaign. Running shoes should not be too soft. There is less youth violence in countries where children are not disciplined.

## Finns died when a small plane crashed in Zimbabwe

According to local media, the crashed plane was on its way from Buffalo Range airport to Victoria Falls. The plane dived into the ground in a mountainous area in Masvingo province. Photo: Bekithemba Dube / AFP

Four Finns have died in a plane crash in Zimbabwe.
A small plane fell in a mountainous area on Friday morning.
There were four Finnish passengers on the plane, and the pilot. Everyone died when the plane fell. It is not yet known why the small plane fell in Zimbabwe.

## There were riots at the protests in France

![Demonstration in Paris](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10524521)

Protesters oppose increases in fuel prices and the French president's tax policy. Photo: Christophe Petit Tesson / EPA-EFE

A big demonstration in France has turned into riots. About 30,000 people gathered in the French capital, Paris. Protesters oppose increases in the fuel tax and France's economic policy. The demonstration became violent.

Protesters have, for example, set rubbish bins on fire on the streets of Paris. The police have used tear gas and water cannons against the protesters.

## Researchers: avoid running shoes that are too soft

![A Nike running shoe made of tennis ball material, in the Variety vintage shop in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10312584)

Open image viewer

This running shoe is made of tennis ball material. The shoe has to flex. A sole that is too thick is not good. Photo: Kari Ahotupa / Yle

New information has been gained about running shoes. Finnish researchers have noticed that a running shoe that is too soft is bad for the foot when a person runs. If the running shoe is too soft, the runner stiffens the foot and the foot becomes strained.

Many new running shoes with thick, soft soles have come into the shops. Researchers say that a sole about 2 centimetres thick is good.

## Physical punishment increases violence

![A person shakes a child](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9090182)

Open image viewer

In countries where children are not hurt, there is less violence among young people. Photo: Yle

Youth violence is less common in countries where the law bans physical punishment of children. Physical punishment of children is also banned in Finland.

According to the study, countries where children may not be disciplined are safer for children to grow up in. In countries where discipline is banned, boys and girls face much less violence than in countries where physical punishment is allowed.
