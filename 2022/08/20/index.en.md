# Saturday 20.8.2022 (radio)

Money minister Saarikko supports lowering the VAT on electricity. Young people drink less alcohol than before. The waterfowl hunt has begun. Topi Raitanen won the European title in the steeplechase. The weather is dry.

## Annika Saarikko

![Annika Saarikko in a television studio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_290/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99702663008a9331c54/1660979977)

Annika Saarikko in a television studio. Photo: Sakari Piippo / Yle

Money minister Annika Saarikko says that it may be worth lowering electricity taxation.

In Saarikko's view, however, there is a danger that electricity companies will not lower the price of electricity, even if electricity taxation falls. Saarikko says that the tax cut must reduce people's electricity bills.

Electricity is now really expensive. In winter electricity is probably even more expensive than now.

## Young people and alcohol

![Customer at Alko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4993,h_2809,x_120,y_631/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-77706760339bcd38d67/1613995539)

Open image viewer

Alcoholic drinks at Alko. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Young people are drinking less and less alcohol than before. Underage young people drink much less alcohol than 20 years ago.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare (THL) says that young people want to do well, for example at school and in hobbies. That is why many young people do not drink alcohol. It is also harder for young people to get alcohol than before.

## Hunting season

![A hunting dog carries a waterfowl in its mouth.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_509/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99642562ff22b20f03d/1660887900)

Open image viewer

A hunting dog carries a waterfowl in its mouth. Photo: Tanja Heikkonen / Yle

Waterfowl hunting has started. The hunt started on Saturday at noon. Waterfowl may be hunted until the end of December.

The Finnish Wildlife Agency hopes that the hunters' catch is often mallard, teal and goldeneye. There are many of these birds.

On Saturday the bear hunt also began.

## The athletics European Championships

![Topi Raitanen celebrates the European title.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4150,h_2334,x_0,y_151/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-997013630077a1f3fcd/1660975065)

Open image viewer

Topi Raitanen. Photo: IMAGO/Eibner/ All Over Press

Finnish athletes did well on Friday at the athletics European Championships in Munich, Germany.

Topi Raitanen won the European title in the 3,000 metre steeplechase. Kristiina Mäkelä got silver in the triple jump. Mäkelä jumped a new Finnish record.

Finland now has 3 medals from the European Championships. Wilma Murto won the pole vault European title earlier.

On Friday there was thunder and a lot of lightning in Finland. On Friday 34,000 lightning strikes hit the ground or water in Finland. This is the largest number since 1988.
