# Thursday 11.2.2021 (radio)

Metsä Group is building a new factory in Kemi. Over 700 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus. The court has given a verdict in a case where lies were told about the death of an Iraqi man. There are more problems in home care for elderly people than before. Beautiful winter weather continues.

## Metsä Group's factory

![A new Metsä Fibre bioproduct factory is planned for the Pajusaari factory area in Kemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6292205e15f7e8f1b74/1578498266)

Metsä Group is building a factory like this in Kemi. Photo: Metsä Group

Metsä Group is building a new factory in Northern Finland. The factory will be in Kemi.

Metsä Group is a forest industry company. In the new factory it will start to make pulp from wood, which can be used to make paper and cardboard.

The factory will also start to make new kinds of bioproducts, for example gas. The factory will also produce electricity.

The new factory is important for Northern Finland. It will bring many new jobs to the area.

The new factory is big. It will cost Metsä Group 1.6 billion euros. The factory will be ready in 2023.

## Coronavirus deaths in Finland

![Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11780252)

THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, collects the information on coronavirus deaths in Finland. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

A total of 708 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus. On Thursday, 2 new coronavirus deaths were reported.

Old people in particular have died from the coronavirus. The average age of those who died is 82 years. Almost all of them had some serious illness, for example a heart disease or diabetes.

## Verdict on a false death

![The accused and legal aides at Helsinki District Court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11731037)

Open image viewer

The court dealt with the case of the Iraqi man in January. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

The court has given a verdict in a case where lies were told about the death of an Iraqi man.

The man's daughter and the daughter's former husband were sentenced to almost 2 years in prison.

They lied to Finnish authorities that the woman's father had been killed in Iraq. Before that, the father had not been granted asylum in Finland.

That is why the European Court of Human Rights ordered that Finland must pay compensation to the daughter.

The truth, however, was that the man had not died. That is why the daughter and the daughter's former husband have now received prison sentences.

## Problems in home care

![Basic carer Minna Kumpumäki on a home care visit to a client.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_556/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7147005f4f95a1a70ad/1609260956)

The workload in home care has grown. Photo: Tanja Kröger / Yle

There are more problems in home care for elderly people than before. This is what Yle's investigation says.

3 times more reports of problems are made than in 2016.

The problems are increasing, because elderly people in poorer health are being cared for at home than before. The workload is growing, but there are not enough carers.

Finland is now planning a law reform that will improve home care. The goal is to make sure, for example, that there are enough carers.
