## Strike in the private social and health sector

![Three children swinging on a seesaw on a sunny spring day in a day-care centre yard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6050,h_3390,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-110800964525c45289b0/1684751034)

The strike is in private care homes and day-care centres. Photo: Elina Ervasti / Yle

A strike begins in the private social and health sector. The strike begins on Tuesday at 5 private day-care chains and 26 private care homes for the elderly. Strike day-care centres are in different parts of Finland. Strike care homes are in Häme and Pirkanmaa.

The strike lasts 2 days at day-care centres. The strike lasts 5 days at care homes. Because of the strike, private social service companies are hiring temporary workers for care homes and day-care centres. Supervisors, that is, bosses, also help with many tasks.

The reason for the strike is a pay dispute. Workers in the private social and health sector demand that they get the same pay as workers on the public side.

## Government negotiations

![Petteri Orpo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5116,h_2875,x_0,y_338/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1117289646b0d29a3727/1684737376)

Government negotiations are led by National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The government negotiations continue. The 4th week of the government negotiations has started. The government is being formed by National Coalition chairman **Petteri Orpo**.

Orpo says that the biggest and most difficult matters are left in the government negotiations. They are economic matters, climate and immigration policy, and social and health matters.

The National Coalition, the Finns Party, the Swedish People's Party and the Christian Democrats are taking part in the government negotiations. Petteri Orpo estimates that the government negotiations will continue into next week too.

## Sick leave in the municipal sector

![A close-up of a signpost in the corridor of Päijät-Häme Central Hospital. Signs in different colours.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4027,h_2266,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1100927643f8a996186d/1681885918)

Open image viewer

Corona increased sick leave in the municipal sector. Photo: Petri Niemi / Yle

Sick leave in the municipal sector increased a lot last year compared with the year before. Municipal sector workers were off work due to illness last year for over 20 days on average. That is 4 days more than the year before.

Sick leave in the municipal sector increased among both men and women and among workers of all ages. The Finnish Institute of Occupational Health estimates that the reason is the corona situation.

The coronavirus increased sick leave last year.

## Construction companies' offers

![New apartment blocks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97886362c5994bee853/1721980804)

In many cities a lot of new flats have been built. Now housing sales have slowed down. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Housing sales have slowed down a lot. About 70 percent fewer housing sales have been made this year than last year.

That is why construction companies have also started making various offers. For example, construction companies can pay part of the buyer's loan interest or housing company charges. The buyer can benefit from a construction company's offer by up to 20,000 euros.

Construction companies have a lot of money tied up in new buildings. That is why construction companies are trying to find ways to get people to buy ready-made flats.
