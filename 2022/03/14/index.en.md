## Support for NATO

![Lead image.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12357310)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle, graphics: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

Support for the NATO military alliance has risen in Finland. This is what Yle's new survey shows.

62 percent of respondents think that Finland should join NATO. 16 percent of respondents do not want Finland to become a NATO member.

Men support NATO more than women, but now more than half of women also want Finland in NATO.

The survey shows that support for NATO has grown during the war in Ukraine. Russia attacked Ukraine on 24 February.

In the view of many, Finland is safer if it becomes a member of the NATO military alliance.

Finland does a lot of cooperation with NATO nowadays, but Finland is not a NATO member.

## Asylum seekers

![Yasin Omer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_3,y_93/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-927291622c84ff4b97b/1647084871)

Yasin Omer. Photo: Pekka Koli / Yle

Already 2.5 million people have fled Ukraine because of the war.

EU countries are quickly giving residence permits to Ukrainian refugees. The aim is for the asylum system to work, even though there are millions of new refugees.

Other asylum seekers wonder at the better treatment of Ukrainians.

For example, Somali Yasin Omer says in a Yle interview that nobody has said welcome to him the way they have to Ukrainians. Omer fled from Somalia to Finland because of the war 7 years ago.

Yasin Omer has not yet received asylum in Finland. Now Omer believes that he has a chance of getting asylum. Because of the war in Ukraine, Europeans now understand what war is, Omer says.

There are still many new coronavirus infections in Finland.

Over the weekend almost 27,000 new coronavirus infections were found. The figure includes only coronavirus infections that have been found with an official test.

Over the weekend there have also been 64 deaths linked to corona.

There are about 860 patients in hospital care because of corona.

## Weak ice

![Sign reads: Beware of weak ice.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9322453)

Open image viewer

Photo: Kalle Niskala / Yle

The ice is already weak. This is the warning from the Finnish Environment Institute.

The ice may still be thick, but it cannot bear heavy weight. Even 50 centimetre thick ice may be so weak that you cannot walk on it.

The cause is the sun. It can make the ice so soft that it breaks under foot.

Places where water flows under the ice are especially dangerous.
