## NATO debate

![A referral debate was held in the plenary session of Parliament on 16 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5236,h_2945,x_0,y_545/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9555296282107725eac/1652781044)

NATO is being debated in Parliament. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

NATO is being discussed in Finland's parliament, the Eduskunta. After the debate Finland is ready to apply for membership of the military alliance NATO.

Sweden, Finland's neighbouring country, is also applying for NATO membership. Finland and Sweden will submit their membership applications at the same time. That will happen in the coming days.

Finland and Sweden want to join the military alliance NATO because Russia has attacked Ukraine. NATO membership is, in the opinion of the leaders of Finland and Sweden, the best way to look after security.

There are now 30 member countries in the military alliance NATO. They help each other if any member country is attacked.

## Corona in North Korea

![In the foreground a massive flag of North Korea. In the background people who took part in a parade.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12447268)

Open image viewer

Corona may have spread also at parades. Photo: EPA / KCNA

Corona is spreading in North Korea, in Asia. More than a million North Koreans have already caught corona and about 50 people have died of corona.

The residents of North Korea have not received corona vaccinations. The World Health Organization, China and Russia have offered corona vaccines to North Korea, but North Korea does not want to accept them.

North Korea has tried to prevent corona by closing the country's borders completely.

## Ukraine's Eurovision win

![People standing on a stage with Ukrainian flags in their hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_410/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-95521462803ae605edf/1652570959)

Open image viewer

Ukraine's Kalush Orchestra. Photo: Marco Bertorello / AFP / Lehtikuva

The Eurovision song contest interested Finns this year too. The final on Saturday got more than 2 million TV viewers.

The Eurovision win went to Ukraine. Ukraine received a record number of votes from TV viewers across Europe.

Ukraine's representative Kalush Orchestra thanked the viewers and said that the win is important for Ukrainians in the middle of the war.

The band is sure that the Eurovision can be held next year in a new and happy Ukraine.

## Hanami festival

![People admiring cherry blossoms in Roihuvuori Cherry Tree Park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_232/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9553316281049884783/1652622622)

Open image viewer

Roihuvuori Cherry Tree Park. Photo: Ilkka Loikkanen / Yle

The cherry trees are blooming again in Roihuvuori in Helsinki.

In Roihuvuori there is a Cherry Tree Park and a garden in Japanese style. There are about 315 cherry trees there in total.

The hanami festival has been celebrated in the Cherry Tree Park every spring since 2008. Hanami is Japanese, and it means viewing flowers.

Tuesday is a chilly day across the whole country. The temperature is under 10 degrees across the whole country.

Cloudiness is mostly heavy, the sunniest place is on the Bothnian Bay coast.
