# Wednesday 26.9.2018 (radio)

Yle has asked the parties for their opinions on immigration. Russian Prime Minister Dmitri Medvedev has visited Finland. Finnish young people drink less alcohol than before. The autumn wind is now strong.

## Parties answered about immigration

![Party leaders gathered in a picture with a woman dressed in a burqa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10423542)

Party leaders answered questions about immigration. Photo: Yle Uutisgrafiikka

Yle has asked the parties what they think about immigration. The parties were asked 10 questions about immigration.

Yle asked, for example, whether the burqa should be banned in Finland. Almost all parties think that people may dress as they wish. That is why the burqa should not be banned.

Only the Finns Party and Blue Reform want to ban the burqa. In their opinion, the burqa harms women's rights. Blue Reform is a party that split from the Finns Party last year.

The other questions and the parties' answers can be found on Yle's website, at the address yle.fi.

## Medvedev visited Finland

![Prime Ministers Dmitry Medvedev and Juha Sipilä on 26 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10424926)

Open image viewer

Russian Prime Minister Dmitry Medvedev and Finnish Prime Minister Juha Sipilä on Wednesday in Helsinki. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Russian Prime Minister Dmitry Medvedev has visited Finland. Medvedev met Finland's Prime Minister Juha Sipilä on Wednesday.

The prime ministers discussed environmental matters, for example. At the prime ministers' press conference, Medvedev said that Russia is interested in Finland's expertise in waste management.

Finland and Russia plan to clean up the Krasny Bor landfill together. The landfill is in Russia, near the city of St Petersburg. The landfill contains dangerous problem waste.

## Young people drink less

![Four-litre beer bottles in shops.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10112498)

Open image viewer

Alcohol no longer interests young people as much as before. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finnish young people drink alcohol less than before.

The World Health Organization, WHO, studied the alcohol use of 15-year-olds in Europe. The study period was from 2002 to 2014.

During the study period, the number of Finnish young people who drank alcohol every week decreased. Likewise, the number of Finnish young people who got drunk decreased.

## Food is getting more expensive

![PTT predicts that the prices of meat and dairy products will rise by over 20 percent by the end of the year.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10266028)

Open image viewer

Prices are rising in the grocery store, PTT predicts. Photo: Hanna Othman / Yle

The price of food is rising. The research institute PTT predicts that the price of food will rise by almost 2 percent this year. Next year food will get a bit more expensive, by just over 2 percent.

All important products are becoming more expensive. For example, the prices of dairy products and meat products will rise. Vegetables are also getting more expensive because of last summer's drought.

## The wind is strong

![Wind sock in strong wind in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10414043)

Open image viewer

Autumn is a windy time. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

A strong wind blew in Finland on Wednesday. The wind knocked down trees and cut off electricity. Thousands of homes were without electricity on Wednesday.

The strong wind continues on Thursday. The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that the wind speed may at times be as much as 20 metres per second.
