Parliament has dismissed **Tytti Yli-Viikari** from the post of Director General of the National Audit Office. Parliament's decision was unanimous, that is, no MP opposed the dismissal.

## Murder on the Viking Sally ship

![The defendant stands surrounded by media at the courtroom door.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2460,h_1384,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81002460ab46745b438/1621841902)

Open image viewer

Journalists outside the courtroom. The court case interested the media a lot. Photo: Tuomas Rimpiläinen / Yle

The court has acquitted a Danish man who was accused of murder on the Viking Sally ship. The charge concerned events more than 30 years ago.

In 1987, a 20-year-old German man died and a young woman was injured on the Viking Sally ship. The man may have been murdered and the woman may have been an attempted murder victim, but no one was found guilty. The case then attracted a lot of attention in the media.

The Danish man who was now accused in court was on the ship when the murder was committed. The court says that there was no evidence of the man's guilt. The court acquitted the man of the charges.

Finns have taken out many new mortgage loans. New mortgage loans worth over 2 billion euros were taken out in May.

The Bank of Finland says that mortgage loans have been taken out exceptionally much. Such a large amount of mortgage loans was last taken out 13 years ago, in the summer.

Mortgage loans have also been taken out actively this year throughout the spring. Finns now also take out longer mortgage loans than before.

## Blue-green algae situation

![Blue-green algae at the Huhtiniemi beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82373860d0673a18675/1624270951)

Open image viewer

Blue-green algae on the shore. Blue-green algae is not always visible this well. Photo: Kare Lehtonen / Yle

Lots of blue-green algae may come to the waterways this summer too. The weather has been warm. That speeds up the blooming of blue-green algae.

The Border Guard saw the first blue-green algae blooms of the summer in the northern Baltic Sea at Midsummer.

The Environment Centre says that blue-green algae may come especially to the eastern part of the Gulf of Finland. Blue-green algae is poisonous.
