# Friday 14.12.2018 (radio)

The Greens and the Finns Party. Reducing traffic emissions. Measles in Tampere. Matti Kassila has died.

## Haavisto: the Greens will not join a government with the Finns Party

![Pekka Haavisto](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10554357)

Open image viewer

Chairperson of the Greens Pekka Haavisto. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The chairperson of the Greens, Pekka Haavisto, does not want to join a government with the Finns Party. During the day Haavisto corrected what he had said in an interview with the Maaseudun Tulevaisuus newspaper. It was reported that, in Haavisto's opinion, the Greens could be in the same government as the Finns Party. Many Green politicians wondered at Haavisto's opinion.

Later in the day Haavisto wrote on Twitter and Facebook that the Finns Party's current line does not give the Greens the chance to be in the same government.

## Politicians have doubts about cutting emissions

![Exhaust pipe of a diesel car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10136704)

Open image viewer

Politicians suspect that the ways to cut traffic emissions are too harsh. Photo: Ina Fassbender / AFP

The new proposals of experts on cutting traffic emissions make many politicians doubtful. In the experts' new report, it is proposed, for example, that the sale of petrol and diesel cars must be ended in 2035.

Politicians think, for example, that the goals are too harsh for people. Tightening the taxation of fuel also makes them doubtful. Politicians also want more calculations to be made of the effects.

The expert group of the Ministry of Transport and Communications published the report on Wednesday.

## Measles found in Tampere

![A child who has measles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10532230)

Open image viewer

Measles causes a red rash on the skin. Photo: AOP

At least three adults have got measles in Tampere. The authorities say that the infections happened at the Mass of the Catholic Holy Cross parish on 25 November.

People who attended the Mass are asked to contact the emergency department Acuta in Tampere. Two measles patients have been vaccinated, but one has not.

Measles is a disease that spreads very easily. It does not spread if a person has been vaccinated against measles. Measles causes a high fever and a rash on the skin. Measles can lead to serious after-effects.

Nowadays measles is quite a rare disease in Finland.

## Director of the Inspector Palmu films has died

![Director Matti Kassila at home in Rajatorppa, Vantaa, in 2011.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10554245)

Matti Kassila was the director of Finns' favourite films. Photo: AOP

Film director Matti Kassila has died. Matti Kassila's career began as early as the 1940s. He managed to direct dozens of films.

![Actors Leevi Kuuranne, Leo Jokela, Matti Ranin and Joel Rinne in the roles of the characters in the film Inspector Palmu's Mistake.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10555182)

Open image viewer

For example, Inspector Palmu's Mistake was a very popular film. Photo: Oy Suomen Filmiteollisuus / Yle Kuvapalvelu

Finns remember Kassila especially for many of the Inspector Palmu films. They are still very popular. Many also remember other Kassila films, for example Radio Makes a Breakthrough, August and The Red Line.

Kassila was awarded the Jussi Award for film many times. He also received the State Prize for Film Art and the title of professor. Matti Kassila lived to be 94 years old.
