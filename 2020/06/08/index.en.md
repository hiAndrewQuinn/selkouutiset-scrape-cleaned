# Monday 8.6.2020 (radio)

Matti Vanhanen is becoming Finland's new finance minister. The coronavirus has already killed over 400,000 people in the world. Finland and Estonia established diplomatic relations exactly 100 years ago. The quality of Finland's bathing waters is good.

Matti Vanhanen is becoming Finland's new finance minister.

On Monday the Centre Party decided to propose Vanhanen for the job of finance minister. In the current government, the post of finance minister belongs to the Centre.

The post became free when the Centre Party chairman Katri Kulmuni resigned from the post of finance minister on Friday. Kulmuni resigned because she got caught in the middle of a row. The row arose when Kulmuni bought public speaking training at the state's expense.

The finance minister, that is, the money minister, manages the state's economic policy. The job is especially important now, when the corona crisis is burdening Finland's economy.

Matti Vanhanen is becoming the new finance minister, but he has been in politics for a long time. The 64-year-old Vanhanen is now Speaker of Parliament, that is, he leads the work of Parliament. He has also previously been Prime Minister of Finland.

## The number of corona victims

![Bodies of corona victims outside a hospital in Brooklyn, New York, on 20 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11370494)

The coronavirus has already killed over 400,000 people in the world.

About 25 per cent of the deaths have happened in the USA. Now the coronavirus is spreading especially quickly in Brazil and India.

In Finland, the coronavirus has killed 323 people in total. On Monday the Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, said that there have been no new deaths.

The coronavirus is no longer spreading quickly in Finland. THL said on Monday that 20 new coronavirus infections have been found. In total, about 7,000 Finns have caught the coronavirus infection.

## Diplomatic relations between Finland and Estonia

![Flag of Estonia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5735954)

Open image viewer

Diplomatic relations between Estonia and Finland turned 100 years old on Monday. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä

Finland and Estonia have celebrated a joint national day on Monday. The countries established diplomatic relations exactly 100 years ago.

Diplomatic relations meant that Finland recognised Estonia's independence. Finland was the first country to recognise Estonia's independence.

No big celebrations were held to mark the anniversary because of the corona situation.

## Finland's bathing waters

![Ivan Holster does a bomb jump into the water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4616,h_2596,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5684185cf7b1117cf4f/1718019569)

Finns can swim in clean waters. The European Environment Agency says that 90 per cent of Finland's bathing waters are excellent or good.

There are about 300 public beaches in Finland. Only at 2 beaches is the water quality poor.

The European Environment Agency's report monitors how many bacteria there are in the bathing waters. In particular, intestinal bacteria are studied, which can get into bathing waters from waste water.
