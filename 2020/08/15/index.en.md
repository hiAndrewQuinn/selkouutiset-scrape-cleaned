## Many people are thinking about buying homes

![Floor plan of an apartment at a flat viewing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9578839)

Open image viewer

People who are buying their first own home are especially applying for housing loans now. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Finns are again eagerly applying for housing loans.

Banks say that in March and May the corona crisis made the housing market quiet. However, the number of loan applications began to grow in May, and the growth continues. There have been up to 25 per cent more applications than a year ago.

Banks say that it is now especially people who are buying their first own home who are active.

## Final-year students are moved to distance teaching

![A student studying maths at Kuopio Lyseo upper secondary school](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_429/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6325495e22f38a4a79a/1579351515)

Open image viewer

Distance teaching for final-year students is meant to secure the autumn matriculation examinations. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

Many final-year students are again moving to distance teaching.

For example, Helsinki recommends that upper secondary schools move their final-year students to distance teaching. This is meant to secure the autumn matriculation examinations.

Distance teaching for final-year students is also being planned in other big cities around the country.

Final-year students will move to distance teaching a couple of weeks before the autumn matriculation examinations. The first exam day of the autumn matriculation examinations is in the middle of September.

## Dozens go missing every year

![A group of people's shadows.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11382782)

Open image viewer

There are 400 people in the police's information system who are missing. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Dozens of Finns go missing every year.

The police receive about 1,000 to 2,000 reports every year of people who have gone missing. However, most of the missing people are found already a few hours later.

About 20 to 40 people go missing each year and are not found.

Chief Superintendent Tero Haapala says that there are a little over 400 people in the police's information system who are currently missing.

## Constant dieting can make you gain weight

![A woman weighs herself on a scale.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9392568)

Open image viewer

A researcher says that a person of normal weight has no need to diet. Photo: AOP

More than 60 per cent of Finnish women and more than 40 per cent of men have at some point tried to lose weight. However, a new study shows that if a person's dieting attempts keep failing, the risk of gaining weight grows. The risk of getting type 2 diabetes may also grow.

Researcher Laura Sares-Jäske says that when a person diets, the body tries to slow down weight loss. Because of this, a person gains weight back more easily, and perhaps even more than what they weighed when they started dieting.

The researcher says that especially a person of normal weight should avoid pointless dieting attempts.

Tomorrow it will be dry in almost the whole country. In Northern Lapland, however, there may be showers of water, and on the fells of Käsivarsi there may be sleet showers at night.

In the southern and central parts of the country the temperature is 18 to 26 degrees, in the north 6 to 18 degrees.
