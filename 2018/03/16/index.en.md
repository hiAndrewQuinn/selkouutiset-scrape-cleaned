## Upper secondary students' poor Swedish

![Upper secondary students in the matriculation exams.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6800708)

Open image viewer

Swedish is no longer a compulsory subject in the matriculation exams. Photo: Yle

Upper secondary students' Swedish has got worse.

Most Finnish-speaking upper secondary students no longer take the Swedish exam in the matriculation exams. The Swedish exam was compulsory until 2004.

Poor Swedish skills make studies after upper secondary school harder, because Swedish is part of studies at higher education institutions.

## Helsinki is considering plain language

![Suldaan Said.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7841907)

Open image viewer

Suldaan Said Ahmed made an initiative about using plain language. Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

The City of Helsinki may start telling about the city's decisions in plain language too.

This week, an initiative, that is a proposal, about using plain language was made in the Helsinki City Council. In the council, politicians decide on the city's affairs.

The initiative was made by Suldaan Said Ahmed of the Left Alliance. Many politicians from other parties supported the initiative.

The initiative says that every Helsinki resident must get information about the city's decisions and activities. That is why the city must tell about its decisions in plain language too.

The initiative also says that the city must tell about important decisions in the largest foreign languages too.

The city will look into the matter, and it will be decided later.

## An accident in the USA

![The collapsed pedestrian bridge at Florida International University](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10120400)

Open image viewer

A new pedestrian bridge collapsed on top of cars. Photo: Joe Raedle / AFP

An unusual accident has happened in the USA. A pedestrian bridge collapsed onto a road, and cars were trapped under the bridge. At least 6 people died.

The bridge was new. It was not yet in use.

The bridge was built with a new technique. Parts of the bridge were made elsewhere, and they were brought ready to the building site. That is why the bridge was finished quickly.

The bridge was in Miami, next to Florida International University. The bridge was built especially for students. The idea was that students could get safely from the university to student housing over a big road.

## The position of the Finnish language in science

![Course books in the Tritonia science library in Vaasa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9302375)

Open image viewer

A science book is often in English, even though the writer is Finnish. Photo: Jussi Laine/Yle

Is science needed in Finnish? Yle News asked professors of the Finnish language about it.

The professors are worried that many Finnish researchers write only in English. The professors say that, for example, the vocabulary of Finnish will not develop if Finnish is not used in science. It is also important that all Finns get information about the results of science.

Finnish researchers write in English, for example, in the fields of technology and medicine. That way they get readers from many countries.
