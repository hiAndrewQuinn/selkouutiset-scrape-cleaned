# Saturday 22.4.2017 (radio)

President Tarja Halonen asks for the forced deportations of asylum seekers to be reconsidered. 600 marches for science around the world. Is it safe to dye your hair? Streets are being washed in cities. Musician Pekka Myllykoski has died.

## Halonen: A pause in forced deportations

![President Tarja Halonen's opening speech at a seminar at the University of Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9161593)

Open image viewer

President Tarja Halonen. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

President Tarja Halonen says that Finland must stop the forced deportations of asylum seekers to countries where there are conflicts. Halonen is talking about Syria, Afghanistan and Iraq.

Halonen says that the Finnish authorities must go and see what the situation in the conflict countries is. After that, Finland can think about forced deportations again.

Päivi Nerg from the Ministry of the Interior says that Finland gets reliable information about the situation in the conflict countries all the time. Based on the information, the authorities and the courts have decided that Finland can send people back to Afghanistan and Iraq, but not to Syria. The police obey the decisions of the courts when they send back people who do not want to leave Finland.

## Marches for science around the world and in Finland

![Demonstrators and signs](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9577423)

Open image viewer

March for science in Sydney, Australia. Photo: DAVID MOIR/EPA

Today, Saturday, there are more than 600 marches for science in different parts of the world. There was also a march for science in Helsinki, with hundreds of people taking part. Researchers, students and other friends of science wanted to show that they are on the side of science, against false beliefs and lies.

The Helsinki march ended at Senate Square. After that there were lectures at the University of Helsinki, which everyone could go and listen to. At the lectures, researchers talked, for example, about the brain and the universe.

The first marches for science were organised in the USA.

## Is hair dye safe?

![Hair is being dyed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9515704)

Open image viewer

Hair is being dyed. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

Many people wonder if it is safe to dye your hair.

Some researchers think that hair dyes may cause cancer. But this has not been proven; the matter needs to be studied more. The World Health Organization WHO also says that there is no reliable estimate of whether you can get cancer from hair dyes. The EU says that it is better if people under 16 do not dye their hair, to be on the safe side.  At least hair dye increases a person's chemical load.

Finns dye their hair at home more than other Europeans.

## Dust off the streets

![A street sweeping machine is coming](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9577303)

Open image viewer

The sand is brushed from the street. Photo: Sakari Partanen / Yle

In spring, city streets are washed. The sand that was spread on the streets in winter, when it was slippery, is collected from the streets. Now the sand and dust fly in the air and people breathe the dust.

The sand is collected and it is used for construction work. The sand cannot be spread on the streets again, because it is dirty. Washing the sand is expensive.

## Pekka Myllykoski has died

![Pekka Myllykoski](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9577442)

Open image viewer

Pekka Myllykoski. Photo: Jukka Ritola / Lehtikuva

Musician Pekka Myllykoski has died. Myllykoski died of cancer on Saturday morning. He was 57 years old.

Pekka Myllykoski was the singer of the country band Freud Marx Engels & Jung. Myllykoski also wrote lyrics for the band's songs.
