# Saturday 27.5.2017 (radio)

Sipilä does not want to change the government programme. There are problems in the work trials of asylum seekers. Food waste goes on sale. A Finnish short film is competing in Cannes.

## The government programme will not change

![Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9635303)

Open image viewer

Juha Sipilä. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä says that the government programme will not be changed, even though the leader of the Finns Party is changing.

The three government parties agreed on the government programme 2 years ago. The government programme lists the big and important things that the government plans to do during its 4 years.

In 2 weeks, Jussi Halla-aho may be chosen as chair of one of the government parties, the Finns Party. He has criticised the government's policy towards immigrants.

Jussi Halla-aho tells Sipilä that he does not demand changes to the government programme. Halla-aho demands that the government follows its own programme.

## Problems in work trials

![The agreement defines the work trial as &quot;unpaid activity under the reception centre, comparable to work and study activities&quot;.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9630544)

Open image viewer

An agreement about a work trial.

There are many problems in the work trials of asylum seekers, experts say.

Yesterday Yle reported on a restaurant where all the employees are asylum seekers and nobody gets paid. They have done unpaid work for many months.

The purpose of a work trial is that asylum seekers can get to know Finnish workplaces for about 2 weeks. If the work trial lasts many months, it becomes an official employment relationship. Then the company must pay the asylum seeker a wage.

## Food waste cheaply

![bread bread shelf grocery shop](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7044198)

Open image viewer

Bread can usually still be eaten even if the "best before" date was yesterday. Photo: Marjukka Talvitie / Yle

The association Finn Church Aid is setting up a shop in the Helsinki area that sells food waste.

Food waste means food that is thrown in the bin. It may be, for example, flour whose "best before" date has already passed but which can still be used. It may also be restaurant food that was made too much of, so that not all of it is sold during the day.

Finn Church Aid's shop plans to sell food waste at as much as 70 percent off. Finn Church Aid will use the shop's profit in developing countries.

## The short film Katto in Cannes

![Teppo Airaksinen and Melli Maikkula](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9635655)

Open image viewer

Screenwriter Melli Maikkula and director Teppo Airaksinen in Cannes. Photo: Juulia Klemola

The Finnish short film Katto (The Roof) is at the Cannes Film Festival in France. Director Teppo Airaksinen's film is competing for the prize for best short film. Katto got into a competition that about 5000 films tried to enter.

The film Katto is about a man who has got divorced. Many Finnish short films have attracted interest abroad in recent years. Making films is expensive, but in a short film you dare more to take risks, experiment and play, experts say.

On Sunday we will find out whether the film Katto wins the Palme d'Or for short films.
