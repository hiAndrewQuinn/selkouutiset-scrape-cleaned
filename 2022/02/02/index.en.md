## Corona restrictions

![Sanna Marin at the door of Säätytalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4876,h_2742,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90962361fa7186316a6/1643806567)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin's government discussed corona restrictions on Wednesday. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

Corona restrictions are being eased further.

The government decided on Wednesday that restaurants can stay open longer from the middle of February.

From 14 February, all restaurants may stay open until midnight. Alcohol sales must stop an hour earlier, at 23:00.

All restaurant restrictions will end at the beginning of March, if the corona situation improves. Then nightclubs can also open again.

The government also recommends that corona restrictions on events be lifted from 14 February. The government hopes that sports and cultural events can again be held normally. The authorities in different regions decide on this.

There are already over 2000 corona deaths in Finland.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare said on Wednesday that a total of 2012 deaths are linked to corona.

There were over 8000 new corona infections on Wednesday. Over 500,000 corona infections have been confirmed in Finland in total.

## Parliament

![Speaker of Parliament and president.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12299314)

Open image viewer

Speaker of Parliament Matti Vanhanen and President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Finland's Parliament has started its work for this year.

President Sauli Niinistö opened the Parliament's session on Wednesday. Speaker Matti Vanhanen also spoke at the event.

The most important work of Parliament is to decide the content of Finland's laws. A law can come into force in Finland only if Parliament approves it.

Sales of hybrid cars have grown in Europe. A hybrid car can run on both petrol and electricity.

Last year, hybrid cars were sold for the first time as many as diesel cars. Last year about 20 per cent of new cars registered in Europe were hybrids.

Still, petrol cars are sold the most.

## Dance house

![The Erkko hall of the Dance House has seats for 700 spectators.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4790,h_2689,x_0,y_300/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90836661f6ecb0144a0/1643572654)

Open image viewer

The Erkko hall of the Dance House holds 700 people. Photo: Tuomas Uusheimo

A new Dance House has opened in Helsinki. The Dance House is at Kaapelitehdas in Ruoholahti.

The Erkko hall of the Dance House is the largest performance space for dance in the Nordic countries. The hall holds 700 spectators. Large international shows can also be staged in the hall.

In the Dance House, circus can also be performed as well as dance.
