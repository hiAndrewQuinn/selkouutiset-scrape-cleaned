# Thursday 6.6.2019 (radio)

The President has appointed the new government. The number of drug deaths has grown. In France the Normandy landings have been remembered. Finland's bathing waters are clean. The temperature rose above 30 degrees.

## New government

![Antti Rinne's government in a group photo on the steps of Säätytalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10819539)

There are 19 ministers in the new government. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

Finland has got a new government.

President Sauli Niinistö granted the resignation of Juha Sipilä's government in the afternoon. After that the President appointed the new government.

The new government includes 5 parties, namely the SDP, the Centre, the Greens, the Left Alliance and the RKP. The new prime minister is Antti Rinne.

There are 19 ministers in total. Of them, 11 are women and 8 are men.

## Drug deaths

![A drug syringe on a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9913558)

Open image viewer

The number of drug deaths has grown across Europe. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Drug poisonings are a common cause of death among young men in Finland. Among men under 40, drug poisonings are the 2nd most common cause of death, according to a new report from the European Monitoring Centre for Drugs and Drug Addiction.

Relative to population, Finland reports the 7th highest number of drug deaths in the EU.

The total number of drug deaths has grown across Europe over the last 5 years.

## Normandy landings

![Emmanuel Macron and Theresa May in Normandy, France, on Thursday 6 June.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10818782)

Open image viewer

French President Emmanuel Macron and British Prime Minister Theresa May attended a memorial event for the landings in Normandy. Photo: Philippe Wojazer / AFP

The Normandy landings have been remembered in France. The landings took place exactly 75 years ago.

On 6 June 1944 more than 150,000 Allied soldiers landed in Normandy on the French coast. At that time Nazi Germany occupied France.

On the first day of the landings more than 4,000 soldiers died and more than 5,000 were wounded. Thousands of soldiers on the other side, that is Germany, were also killed and wounded.

The Normandy landings had a major impact on Germany losing the war, and the Second World War ending.

## Bathing waters

![Seelia Rahkola doing the splits on a beach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10819099)

Open image viewer

Seelia Rahkola went swimming at Pyynikki beach in Tampere. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

Finland's bathing waters are of good quality.

The European Environment Agency has studied water quality at public beaches. The report says that more than 90 per cent of Finland's bathing waters are of excellent or good quality.

Finland has 300 public beaches. Only 1 beach was rated as poor.

## Temperature record

![Blue sky, sun and clouds.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10786511)

Open image viewer

It will be hot tomorrow too. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

Today a summer temperature record was set. The temperature rose above 30 degrees in many places.

For example, 30.9 degrees was measured during the day in Kokemäki. Over 30 degrees was also measured in Jämsä, Tampere and Alajärvi, for example.

It is very warm tomorrow too. The Finnish Meteorological Institute warns that tomorrow there may be sultry heat across the whole country.
