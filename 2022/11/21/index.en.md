# Monday 21.11.2022 (radio)

Indonesian earthquake. Finnair jobs. The price of coffee. Marin's portrait. Weather.

## Indonesian earthquake

![Collapsed house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12679778)

Open image viewer

Many houses collapsed in the Indonesian earthquake. Photo: EPA

An earthquake has struck the island of Java in Indonesia. More than 160 people died in the earthquake. Several hundred were injured.

The figures may still rise as rescue work progresses. Rescue workers may find more people in the houses that collapsed in the earthquake.

Finnish President Sauli Niinistö has taken part in the mourning of the Indonesians. Niinistö sent a message about this to the President of Indonesia, Joko Widodo.

## Finnair jobs

![Finnair plane taking off from the airfield](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2875,h_1617,x_703,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1005590631b1ba07bfb1/1701790760)

Open image viewer

Photo: Tuomas Rimpiläinen / Yle

The airline Finnair is cutting jobs.

Finnair is ending 90 jobs in Finland. In total, Finnair is ending 150 jobs.

Jobs are being reduced in Finnair's offices. For example, jobs for managers and experts are being reduced.

Finnair says that it is reducing jobs because of the situation in Russia. Finnair's aircraft can no longer fly over Russia.

Finnair is also planning dismissals of cabin staff. However, no decisions have yet been made on this.

## The price of coffee

![Freshly roasted coffee beans spinning on a cooler at the Lehmus Roastery coffee roaster in Lappeenranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3664,h_2061,x_1,y_381/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90264061e531de4864c/1642411598)

Open image viewer

The price of coffee on the world market has started to fall. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

The price of coffee is falling around the world.

Coffee has already been very expensive for some time. Now its price on the world market is falling.

The world market price of coffee affects shop prices slowly. Finnish coffee roasters say that in Finland the price of coffee will only start to fall after many months.

Finns still drink a lot of coffee, even though coffee is now expensive.

## Marin's portrait

![Meeri Koutaniemi stands next to the portrait of Sanna Marin. In the picture, which is in red and gold tones, Sanna Marin looks up and to the side.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_351/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1036856637b45c5b0d63/1669023527)

Open image viewer

Photographic artist Meeri Koutaniemi has made a new portrait of Sanna Marin. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

A new portrait of Prime Minister Sanna Marin has been unveiled in Tampere. The portrait will go to the Tampere city office building.

Sanna Marin was previously the chair of the Tampere City Council. Marin's portrait will go to the same place as the portraits of earlier chairs.

However, Marin's portrait is a new kind. It is not a painting, but a photograph. Sanna Marin herself wanted the portrait to be made by the photographic artist Meeri Koutaniemi.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12679779)

Open image viewer

Monday has been a cold day. In Sodankylä in Lapland, a frost of 26 degrees was measured.

In the south, in Uusimaa, driving conditions are bad, because 3 to 5 centimetres of snow has fallen.

On Tuesday there will be dry frosty weather across the whole country. The sun may shine in places in the west and north of the country.

The temperature on Tuesday is between -1 and -5 degrees, and in the north between -10 and -25 degrees. On Wednesday the frost weakens slightly in the north.
