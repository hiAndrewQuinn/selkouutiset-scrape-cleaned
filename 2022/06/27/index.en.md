Trains are running again. Train traffic was disrupted for a long time, because the track work lasted longer than expected.

Track work was done at the Midsummer weekend, when there is little train traffic. The work was supposed to be finished on Sunday afternoon, before the Midsummer return traffic starts.

However, the track work continued until Monday afternoon. Bridge work near Tampere in particular took longer than expected.

Trains were delayed by up to 15 hours. There were several thousand passengers on the trains.

## Niinistö's meetings

![Photo combination: Sauli Niinistö, Tayyip Erdogan, Magdalena Andersson, Jens Stoltenberg](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97508462b9a286de760/1656333062)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö, NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg, Swedish Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson and Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. Photo: Eetu-Mikko Pietarinen / Yle

Finnish President Sauli Niinistö and Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan will meet on Tuesday.

The presidents will meet in Spain. The meeting is arranged before the NATO defence alliance meeting starts in Spain.

Also Swedish Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson and NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg will take part in the meeting.

The subject of the meeting is Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership applications. Finland and Sweden decided in May to apply for NATO membership.

Turkey does not want Finland and Sweden in NATO. Turkey says that Finland and Sweden protect Kurds, whom Turkey considers to be terrorists.

Turkey is a NATO member, and all NATO countries must approve new members of the defence alliance.

## The Norwegian shooter

![Two people lean on each other with a Pride flag over one shoulder; flowers have been laid in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4128,h_2322,x_0,y_430/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97460362b829e599bcd/1656236736)

Open image viewer

People have brought flowers and rainbow flags to the shooting site in Oslo. Photo: Martin Solhaug Standal / EPA

In Norway it is time for the court to decide whether the man who shot people in the Norwegian capital Oslo at the weekend will be held in custody.

The man shot people in and near a gay bar. 2 people died in the shooting and about 20 people were injured.

The shooter is suspected of murder and terrorism. The shooter is a Norwegian citizen. He was born in Iran.

Because of the shooting the Oslo Pride parade was cancelled. At the Pride parade people celebrate the human rights of gay people and other sexual minorities.

## Helsinki Pride

![Image showing Jenni Haukio at the Independence Day reception at the Presidential Palace in 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11821168)

Open image viewer

Mrs Jenni Haukio is the patron of the Pride event. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Helsinki's Pride week started today, Monday. The theme of Pride week is the rights of sexual minorities.

Helsinki Pride is Finland's biggest human rights event. This year the patron of the event is Jenni Haukio, the spouse of President Sauli Niinistö.

The Helsinki Pride parade is held on Saturday. After the Oslo shooting, special attention is being paid in Helsinki to the safety of the parade.
