# Saturday 21.5.2022 (radio)

Talks with Turkey. Russian natural gas. Marriages. Storm in Germany. The weather in Finland.

## Talks with Turkey

![Recep Tayyip Erdoğan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4979,h_2800,x_0,y_518/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9583046288c8c25da75/1653218655)

Turkey's President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. Photo: Stephanie Lecocq / EPA

Finland's President Sauli Niinistö and Turkey's President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan have spoken on the phone. The presidents talked about Finland's NATO application.

Erdoğan has also called Sweden's Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson. Finland and Sweden applied together for membership of the military alliance NATO this week.

Turkey has said that it does not want Finland and Sweden in NATO. Turkey thinks that Finland and Sweden protect Kurdish terrorists.

Turkey is a NATO country, and that is why it can prevent Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership. NATO can take in new members only if all member countries approve of it.

President Niinistö told President Erdoğan that Finland does not accept terrorism. The talks with Turkey continue.

Speaker of Parliament Matti Vanhanen says that Finland's NATO membership is certain. Vanhanen does not believe that Turkey will prevent Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership.

Vanhanen reminds that accepting new member countries has always taken a long time in NATO.

Speaker Vanhanen was in a TV interview on Saturday morning.

## Russian natural gas

![Gas station in Räikkölä in Imatra on 12 May 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4665,h_2624,x_0,y_411/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-95766262874787d7532/1653032960)

Open image viewer

Gas station in Imatra. Photo: Lehtikuva

Natural gas no longer comes from Russia to Finland.

Russia stopped gas imports to Finland on Saturday morning. The reason is that Finland refuses to pay Russia for gas in roubles.

However, Finland is getting enough gas. Gas is now coming from the Baltic states.

Finland is also renting a ship that stores liquid gas. The ship is probably in use already next winter.

## Marriages

![A wedding couple walks hand in hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12453098)

Open image viewer

The number of new marriages is decreasing.

Finns are getting married less than before. Last year the fewest marriages were concluded in Finland in more than 100 years.

The meaning of marriage in Finland has changed. Nowadays many young people think that a relationship does not need to be confirmed by marriage.

In recent years there have been fewer wedding celebrations than usual also because of corona.

## Storm in Germany

![A fallen tree in Helsinki's Central Park](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_479/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80801160a50728b9e93/1621430313)

Open image viewer

A storm wind can tear trees out of the ground. Illustrative picture. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

There has been a strong storm in Germany. Because of the storm at least 1 person has died and 40 people have been injured.

The storm has torn off roofs and pulled trees out of the ground. In the city of Lippstadt even a church tower collapsed.

The storm has caused damage worth millions of euros.

## The weather

In Finland the weather is dry, that is, no rain will fall. In Lapland there may nevertheless be rain showers.
