# Monday 9.11.2020 (radio)

The coronavirus vaccine is progressing. THL gave guidelines for the pre-Christmas party season. The Finns Party is rising and the National Coalition Party is falling in Yle's poll. Estonia's interior minister is resigning because of the Trump storm.

## Coronavirus vaccine development is progressing

![BioNTech building in Mainz, Germany.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4669,h_2626,x_0,y_292/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7399065fa94016916ef/1604927626)

German pharmaceutical company BioNTech. Photo: Ronald Wittek / EPA

The pharmaceutical companies Pfizer and BioNTech have said that they have made progress in developing a coronavirus vaccine.

The companies announce that the vaccine is effective. The vaccine has prevented the development of the coronavirus disease by 90 percent. These results are from phase 3 trials.

More than 40,000 people in 6 countries have taken part in the vaccine trials. The trials show that the vaccine has not been dangerous to health.

Pfizer says that it can manufacture 50 million vaccines by the end of the year. During next year, 1.3 billion vaccines will be produced.

## THL advises pre-Christmas party-goers

![Dancing on a restaurant dance floor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5189244)

Open image viewer

It is good to keep safe distances at pre-Christmas parties. Dancing with guests is not safe because of the coronavirus. Photo: Heli Sorjonen

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare has given safety guidelines for the pre-Christmas party season.

THL says that pre-Christmas parties should only be held in groups of fewer than 10 people. This avoids all the members of a workplace or hobby group falling ill at the same time.

THL also advises keeping the music at a level where pre-Christmas party guests do not need to speak loudly.

## The Finns Party rose, the National Coalition Party fell

![October 2020 party support.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11637693)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ilkka Kemppinen / Yle

The Finns Party has strengthened its position as the most popular opposition party. This is shown by Yle's party support survey.

The Finns Party's support is now 19.5 percent.

The most popular party is still the prime minister's party, the SDP, with 21.2 percent support.

The support of the opposition party, the National Coalition Party, has fallen and is now 16.6 percent.

The support of the government party, the Centre Party, has risen and is 12.4 percent. Of the other government parties, the Greens' support is 10.8 percent, the Left Alliance's 7.7 percent and the Swedish People's Party's 4.3 percent.

The Christian Democrats have risen to 4 percent. 1.6 percent of respondents would vote for the Now movement.

## Estonia's interior minister resigns

![Mart Helme](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11637939)

Open image viewer

Estonian interior minister Mart Helme. Photo: Valda Kalnina / EPA

Estonian interior minister Mart Helme is resigning from the post of minister.

Minister Mart Helme of the right-wing populist EKRE party questioned Joe Biden's victory in the US presidential election yesterday. Helme received a lot of criticism for this.

Finance Minister Martin Helme has said that the election was "clearly fraudulent".

Estonian President Kersti Kaljulaid says that the comments are an attack on Estonian democracy and on its most important alliance.

Interior Minister Helme said that the reason for his resignation is lies told by Estonian media.

Mart Helme drew attention last year when he called Finland's Prime Minister Sanna Marin a "shop girl".
