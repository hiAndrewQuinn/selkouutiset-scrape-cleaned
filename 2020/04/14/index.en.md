# Tuesday 14.4.2020 (radio)

Protective equipment. The isolation of Uusimaa. Graduation celebrations at educational institutions. Corona restrictions in different countries.

## Finland received protective equipment

![A nurse standing in front of a window in protective equipment.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_264/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6636135e8f388024c0e/1586444487)

Open image viewer

There is a serious shortage of this kind of protective equipment in Finland. Photo: Lehtikuva

There is a serious shortage in Finland of protective equipment, which many employees need because of the coronavirus, for example in hospitals and care homes.

Finland received protective equipment from China today. Mouth and nose masks came from China on a cargo plane.

The protective equipment will be tested first, so that it is known whether it is really good.

Finland may get more protective equipment from China perhaps tomorrow.

## The isolation of Uusimaa ends on Sunday

![Conscripts and police at a checkpoint on the Uusimaa and Southwest Finland border on main road 52 in Raasepori.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11303702)

Open image viewer

The border of Uusimaa has been closed for more than 2 weeks. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Finnish government will announce tomorrow whether the isolation of Uusimaa will be extended.

The government receives information from health authorities about the state of the corona epidemic in Finland.

Then the government decides whether the isolation of Uusimaa will be extended.

The original plan is that the isolation of Uusimaa ends next Sunday. It looks as if the isolation will end as planned.

The number of coronavirus infections is growing in Uusimaa. However, health authorities say that the situation is under control.

## Graduation celebrations can be held over a remote connection

![Coffee cups on a party table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9648173)

Open image viewer

This spring, graduation celebrations must be held differently from usual. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Schools have received instructions on how they can hold celebrations for their students.

The instructions apply to vocational educational institutions and upper secondary schools.

Educational institutions will send certificates by post to students who graduate into a profession or get their upper secondary school certificate.

Educational institutions may hold a graduation celebration over a remote connection on Saturday 30 May.

Educational institutions may later hold a joint celebration.

It could be, for example, in August, if at that time it is allowed to hold celebrations with many people.

## Many coronavirus infections among Somali speakers

![Somali-language text saying coronavirus information in Somali.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11268634)

Open image viewer

Yle also has corona news in Somali. Photo: Maiju Hakalahti / Yle

In Helsinki, coronavirus infections are increasing especially among Somali-speaking people.

More than 1,000 infections have been found in Helsinki. Almost 200 of them are in Somali-speaking people.

The City of Helsinki is considering, together with the Somali Association, how information can be better reached to Somali-speaking people.

The City of Helsinki is also trying to find out why the infections are spreading.

The coronavirus restrictions must be taken into account, for example, at the celebration at the end of Ramadan.

On the Clear News website you can find information about the corona in different languages.

Information about the coronavirus in different languages: https://yle.fi/uutiset/3-11268719.
