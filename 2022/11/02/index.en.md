## Cyber attacks

Cyber attacks on Finland from abroad have increased. For example, there have been many denial-of-service attacks on companies' websites. Cyber attacks have increased by about 30 percent during the Ukraine war.

Companies' employees are also being sent more scam messages than before. A message can look like an ordinary message from another employee of the company. The aim of the cyber attackers is for the employee to download, for example, spy software onto the company computer.

Experts say that many cyber attacks most likely come from Russia.

## Finland's NATO membership

![Pekka Haavisto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_228/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1028538636268a15dabc/1667394072)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto spoke at a press conference of the Nordic Council. Photo: Sakari Piippo / Yle

Hungary will approve Finland's NATO membership. This is what Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto said on Wednesday.

Haavisto spoke about the matter at a press conference of the Nordic Council. Haavisto said that Hungary will confirm Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership in December.

President Sauli Niinistö has also spoken with the Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orbán by phone. Niinistö says that Finland can trust that Hungary will approve Finland's membership of NATO.

Of the NATO countries, only Turkey and Hungary have not yet approved Finland's and Sweden's NATO applications.

Mette Frederiksen was moved when the left-wing alliance won the parliamentary election. Photo: Nikolai Linares / EPA

The left-wing parties' alliance has won the Danish parliamentary election.

The vote was close. The left-wing parties' alliance won 90 seats in parliament. The Danish parliament has 179 seats, so the left-wing parties' alliance won the election by a majority of only 1 seat.

The left-wing parties' alliance has been led by Danish Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen. Because Frederiksen's alliance won the election, Frederiksen now starts to form a new government in Denmark. Frederiksen says that she will invite parties from outside the left-wing parties into the government.

## Taxi licences

![Taxis at Tampere railway station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_131/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7226415f6c569b9420b/1600935794)

Traficom cancelled the licences of more than 2,000 taxi drivers. Photo: Miikka Varila / Yle

The Finnish Transport and Communications Agency Traficom has cancelled the taxi licences of more than 2,000 taxi drivers.

Most of the licences, about 1,500, were cancelled because the drivers lacked the qualifications for taxi driving work. Taxi drivers must complete an entrepreneur's exam. The exam had to be completed by April last spring at the latest.

A little over 500 taxi drivers' licences were cancelled because the drivers did not have a Y-tunnus. A Y-tunnus is a number that companies must have.
