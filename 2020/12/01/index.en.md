# Tuesday 1.12.2020 (radio)

THL is worried about the spread of coronavirus infections. Coronavirus vaccinations for Finns may start after the new year. Young adults have got excited about advent calendars.

## Coronavirus infections

![Anna Katz, project manager at THL, at the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare press briefing in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11674570)

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare is worried about the growth of coronavirus infections in Finland.

Today, 550 new coronavirus infections have been confirmed in Finland. In the last two weeks, almost 6,000 infections have been reported. That is almost 3,000 more than in the two weeks before that.

THL project manager Anna Katz says that infections have also been found in small places, so the disease is spreading across the whole country.

Katz is also worried that the number of hospital patients has grown a lot in recent days. There are now about 180 coronavirus patients in hospital care. About 20 of them are in intensive care.

Anna Katz says that about 20,000 coronavirus samples are being tested each day. The coronavirus is found in a little under 3 per cent of the samples.

25 per cent of coronavirus infections are found in people whose first language is something other than Finnish, Swedish or Sami. THL says that at the top of the infection statistics are people whose first language is, for example, Russian, Somali, Arabic or Kurdish:

## Coronavirus vaccines

![A vial of the Biontech coronavirus vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3071,h_1727,x_0,y_48/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7449985fbb35e7344af/1606104691)

Vaccinations against coronavirus for Finns may start perhaps at the beginning of next year.

Coronavirus vaccines have been developed all over the world. The first vaccines are already ready, and they are waiting for permission to be sold from the European Medicines Agency.

Finnish experts say that the first permissions to sell may be received around Christmas. After that, it will still take a couple of weeks before the vaccinations can start.

Experts say that almost all Finnish adults may be vaccinated by next summer.

## Advent calendars

![A cosmetics advent calendar at the Sokos store in Kouvola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_234/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7481935fc5eac70eb62/1606806435)

Behind the doors of this advent calendar there is cosmetics. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

Today is 1 December, and many children have opened the first door of their advent calendar in the morning. The advent calendar is used to count the days to Christmas.

Nowadays many adults also buy their own advent calendar. Young adults in particular buy adult advent calendars.

Adult advent calendars may contain, for example, coffee, crisps or beer. They may also contain small things such as cosmetics or hair accessories.

Terhi-Anna Wilska, professor of sociology, compares adult advent calendars to adult colouring books and friendship books. She says that the people who buy them are people who want to hold on to childhood and youth for a little while longer.
