# Friday 9.10.2020 (radio)

The World Food Programme has won the Nobel Peace Prize. New coronavirus infections have been found again. In Ostrobothnia, gatherings of more than 10 people are banned because of the coronavirus. People must change their diet if the Earth is to be saved. The weather is rainy at the weekend.

## Nobel Peace Prize

![A child is in a woman's lap.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11197260)

Open image viewer

The World Food Programme has taken aid to crisis areas. Photo: Dai Kurokawa / EPA

The winner of the Nobel Peace Prize has been chosen.

The winner of the Nobel Peace Prize this year is the World Food Programme. It is part of the UN, i.e. the United Nations.

The World Food Programme gets the peace prize because it tries to reduce hunger in war areas. The goal of the World Food Programme is that hunger is not used as a weapon.

The World Food Programme helps millions of people every year. It has also succeeded in working effectively during the coronavirus crisis.

The winner of the peace prize was chosen by the Norwegian Nobel Committee. It announced the winner on Friday in Oslo.

## Restrictions in Ostrobothnia

![The doors of the University of Vaasa are closed again and teaching is given remotely.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_253/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7258495f76fc53e9dfc/1601633491)

Open image viewer

Vaasa University students have had many coronavirus infections. Photo: Sebastian Björklund / Yle

Gatherings of people are being restricted in Ostrobothnia in Western Finland because of the coronavirus. The coronavirus has spread quickly in Ostrobothnia.

Gatherings of more than 10 people are banned in Ostrobothnia. The ban also applies to events held outdoors. The ban starts on Monday, and it is valid until the end of October.

The coronavirus began spreading very quickly from student events at the University of Vaasa.

## Food production

![food in the fridge](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_325/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7161695f561f31b62f6/1599479888)

Open image viewer

Meat and milk production puts a strain on nature. Photo: Markku Pitkänen / Yle

People must change their diet if the Earth is to be saved. This is what the World Wide Fund for Nature, WWF, says.

Food production puts a strain on nature. Nature becomes poorer when forests are cut down for agriculture. Meat production in particular puts a lot of strain on nature.

WWF says that official food recommendations do not take nature into account well enough. Food recommendations tell people what a good diet is.

WWF says that people should eat more vegetables and less meat and dairy products. Then food production puts less strain on nature.

The weather at the weekend is rainy.

On Saturday it rains in the north. In the south it is dry on Saturday, i.e. no rain falls. At times the sun may even shine.

On Sunday it rains in Southern Finland and Central Finland.

The temperature on Saturday is between 9 and 14 degrees. On Sunday it is a little cooler.
