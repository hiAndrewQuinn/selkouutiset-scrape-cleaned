# Friday 29.9.2017 (radio)

Chinese people can now buy Finnish food from an online shop. Rohingya refugees drowned in Asia. Russian opposition leader Alexei Navalny has been arrested. A documentary film has been made about the story of Nokia's mobile phones.

## Chinese online shop

![pirkka, fazer and paulig](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9857695)

Open image viewer

Finnish food products interest Chinese people. Photo: AOP and Fazer

Chinese people can now buy Finnish food from an online shop.

The Finnish K Group sells food in the Chinese Alibaba online shop. Chinese people can buy, for example, Fazer and Paulig products online.

Chinese people want to buy healthy food. For example, Finnish mueslis and berries interest Chinese people. Chinese families usually have only one child, and parents want to buy the best food for their only child.

In China there are many more online shop customers than in Finland. For example, the Alibaba online shop has 466 million active buyers.

## Rohingya refugees

![Rohingya refugees in a boat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9859120)

Open image viewer

Rohingya refugees at sea. Photo: Kirsi Crowley / Yle

Rohingya refugees have drowned in Asia. The refugees were on their way from Myanmar to Bangladesh.

More than 60 people drowned in the sea when the refugees' boat capsized. There were children among them.

In total more than 500,000 Rohingya have fled Myanmar in recent weeks, because Myanmar's army has attacked the Rohingya. The UN, or United Nations, says that it is a question of ethnic cleansing.

Myanmar is a Buddhist country, but the Rohingya are Muslims.

## Arrest in Russia

![Alexei Navalny and his family surrounded by police.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9712194)

Open image viewer

Alexei Navalny in the middle of police officers. Photo: Maksim Shipenkov / EPA

Russian opposition leader Alexei Navalny has been arrested. Navalny is an opponent of President Vladimir Putin.

The police arrested Navalny when he was about to leave home for an election event. The police think that Navalny's election events are illegal because he has not asked for permission for them.

Navalny wants to be a candidate in Russia's presidential election in March. The Russian authorities say that Navalny may not take part in the election.

## Documentary film about Nokia

![Nokia mobile phones in Mika Ollila's collection.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9526310)

Open image viewer

Old Nokia phones. Photo: Tero Ylioja / Yle

The Nokia mobile phone is perhaps the most famous Finnish product.

Nokia's phones conquered the world in the 1990s. Nokia was the world's largest mobile phone maker for more than 10 years.

Now the director Arto Koskinen has made a documentary film about Nokia's mobile phones. The film is called Nokia Mobile - the story of the mobile phone.

Koskinen said on Yle's morning TV that the Nokia phone was part of Finns' identity.

In the 2010s Nokia's success ended, and Nokia decided to sell its mobile phone business to Microsoft.
