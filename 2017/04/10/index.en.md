# Monday 10.4.2017 (radio)

New decision-makers for the municipalities were voted in the elections. The Greens were the biggest winner, the Finns Party the biggest loser. Jan Vapaavuori will become Helsinki's first mayor. A big tobacco company now says that tobacco is deadly.

## Election victory for the Greens

![Green Party chair Ville Niinistö celebrates the election success after returning to the Greens' election night party at the Tavastia club in Helsinki on the day of the municipal elections, 9 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9557449)

Finns voted in new decision-makers for the municipalities in Sunday's municipal elections.

The National Coalition Party got the most votes, the Social Democratic Party the second most, and the Centre Party came third.

The biggest win in the elections went to the Green Party. The Greens' share of the votes was the party's record, and the share grew by 4 percentage points from the last municipal elections. The Greens were popular, for example, in cities where there are many students.

The biggest loss went to the Finns Party.

## Of 9,000 decision-makers, 2,000 are entrepreneurs

![A ballot paper is being stamped](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9557763)

Open image viewer

A stamp on the ballot paper. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

In the elections, 9,000 decision-makers were chosen for the councils of 300 municipalities. Almost half of them got into the municipal council for the first time. There are more men than women in the councils, and the average age of the councillors is 50 years.

In the new municipal councils there are many entrepreneurs, that is, people who have their own company. 2,000 entrepreneurs were elected. Many teachers, doctors and nurses were also elected. Well-educated people vote more often than people with little education, unemployed people and immigrants. Well-educated people vote for other well-educated people.

In these elections, 60 percent of Finns over 18 who have the right to vote voted.

## Vapaavuori will become mayor of Helsinki

![National Coalition Party chair Petteri Orpo congratulates the party's top vote-getter and mayoral candidate Jan Vapaavuori at the party's election night event on the day of the municipal elections at restaurant Maxine in Helsinki on 9 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9557348)

Jan Vapaavuori (left) and Petteri Orpo. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Jan Vapaavuori from the National Coalition Party got a record number of votes in Helsinki: 30,000. The National Coalition Party is again the biggest party in Helsinki. Jan Vapaavuori will almost certainly become Helsinki's mayor, that is, the head of the city.

The Green Party also competed for the post of mayor of Helsinki. Anni Sinnemäki of the Greens got the second most votes in Helsinki, about 9,000 votes.

## Philip Morris: Tobacco is dangerous

![Ash from a cigarette is flicked into an ashtray.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8921035)

Open image viewer

Tobacco can cause lung cancer. Photo: Seppo Sarkkinen / Yle

The big tobacco company Philip Morris now says that tobacco is dangerous, you can die from it. Philip Morris no longer tries to claim that it is safe to smoke tobacco.

The company is developing new tobacco products in which the tobacco heats up and makes vapour, but it does not burn and makes no smoke. Philip Morris says that in that way tobacco is safer.

Tobacco companies have had to pay big compensation to people who have got lung cancer from tobacco and have sued the tobacco companies.

Finland is trying to get rid of tobacco completely by 2030.
