# Friday 19.5.2017 (radio)

Organic farming is growing in popularity. Nurses are often ill. Onni looks for snus smugglers. The heatwave limit was broken. Finland will host the Ice Hockey World Championships.

## There is now a record amount of organic fields

![Spring sowing](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9620747)

Open image viewer

Organic farmer Tommi Hasu sows broad beans.  Photo: Yle

Organic farming is growing in popularity. This year over 10 percent of Finland's fields are already farmed organically. That is a new record.

This year new organic fields have appeared especially in southern Finland.

Organic farming means farming in a natural way. It does not use expensive chemicals.

The growth in the popularity of organic farming is because of money. In organic farming the subsidies are bigger and the costs smaller than in ordinary farming. In addition, the farmer now gets a good price for organic products.

Organic farmer Tommi Hasu says that around the world there is strong demand for, for example, organic grain. Because of that, a record high price is now paid for it.

## Nurses are often ill

![a woman taking her temperature](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9505983)

Open image viewer

Nurses are ill more than other municipal employees. Photo: AOP

Nurses' sick leave has increased.

Registered nurses, practical nurses and home helpers were ill clearly more last year than other municipal employees on average, says the Finnish Institute of Occupational Health.

For example, in home care, employees may be on sick leave for as much as a month a year.

The least sick leave was among, for example, managers, doctors and teachers

## Onni is Finland's first snus dog

![A black Labrador retriever and snus tins](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9606917)

Open image viewer

Onni's job is to find snus smugglers. Photo: Customs

Customs has got a new employee. The Labrador retriever Onni is the first snus dog in Finland and maybe in the whole world.

Onni works at the port. Its task is to find big loads of snus that people try to smuggle into Finland.

Snus is a product made from tobacco. You may bring it into Finland only for your own use, at most 1 kilo.

Last year about 3500 kilos of snus were brought into Finland illegally.

## Friday was the first hot day of the year

![People sunbathing on the rocks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9032051)

Open image viewer

Heatwave temperatures were measured in southern Finland. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

On Friday the heatwave limit was passed for the first time this year. Heatwave temperatures were measured in southern Finland.

For example, in Lammi in Hämeenlinna 27 degrees was measured in the afternoon. It was also hot, for example, in Mäntsälä and Mikkeli.

In Finland the weather is called a heatwave when the temperature rises above 25 degrees.

It gets cooler for the weekend. In southern Finland the temperature is about 20 degrees on Saturday too.

## Finland hosts the Ice Hockey World Championships

![Lions fans at the 2013 World Championships.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9623323)

Open image viewer

The 2022 Ice Hockey World Championships will be played in Helsinki and Tampere. Photo: AOP

The ice hockey world championship will be played in Finland in 5 years' time.

The International Ice Hockey Federation says on its Twitter account that Finland has been chosen as host country of the men's Ice Hockey World Championships in 2022.

The World Championships will be played in two different cities, Tampere and Helsinki.
