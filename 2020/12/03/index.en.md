# Thursday 3.12.2020 (radio)

Coronavirus vaccinations in Finland may start early next year. Britain and Russia will start coronavirus vaccinations in the coming days. The number of coronavirus infections in Finland is growing. The popularity of libraries' electronic materials has grown during the coronavirus time.

## Coronavirus vaccinations in Finland

![THL chief physician Taneli Puumalainen at the STM and THL coronavirus situation briefing in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11678953)

THL chief physician Taneli Puumalainen spoke about coronavirus vaccinations on Thursday. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Coronavirus vaccinations will start at the beginning of next year. This is the estimate of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL.

On Thursday THL said that healthcare workers may be vaccinated in January. Healthcare workers must stay healthy so that healthcare works.

Older people and risk groups may be vaccinated in February. Risk groups include people who have some underlying illness, for example severe asthma.

Other adults will be vaccinated against the coronavirus during the spring. Decisions on vaccinating children will be made when there is more research data.

Finland intends to give the vaccination to everyone who wants it. The vaccination is free.

## Coronavirus vaccinations abroad

![A flu vaccine being given.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11650518)

Open image viewer

Russia and Britain intend to start coronavirus vaccinations soon. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Coronavirus vaccinations are already about to start abroad.

In Moscow, the capital of Russia, coronavirus vaccinations will start this Saturday.

Large vaccination centres are being opened in Moscow. First, teachers, social workers and doctors will be vaccinated.

Britain has said that it will start coronavirus vaccinations next week. Britain will first vaccinate the nurses and residents of care homes.

## Coronavirus infections in Finland

![A patient in intensive care in France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11558692)

Open image viewer

Some coronavirus patients need intensive care. Photo: Caroline Blumberg / EPA

In Finland there are fewer coronavirus infections than in many other European countries. However, the number of coronavirus infections is growing quickly.

In the last 2 weeks, over 6,000 coronavirus infections have been reported in Finland. That is 2 times as many as in the previous 2 weeks.

On Thursday, 540 new coronavirus infections were reported.

Hospitals have a lot of work, because many coronavirus patients need intensive care. The hospitals are worried about whether there will be enough intensive care places.

## Libraries' e-materials

![Screenshot of the ellibs e-library website.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2600,h_1463,x_0,y_85/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6700135eaab9274ad5e/1588246995)

The popularity of libraries' electronic materials, that is, e-materials, has grown during the coronavirus time.

The popularity of electronic books and magazines grew a lot last spring, when the libraries were closed because of the coronavirus. The popularity of electronic materials grew by as much as 250 per cent.

Customers hope that more electronic e-materials will be added to libraries too. Nowadays more electronic books and magazines are on sale than in libraries.

Now an e-library that works across the whole country is being planned for Finland. If such an e-library is set up, a large selection of electronic books will be available to all Finns.
