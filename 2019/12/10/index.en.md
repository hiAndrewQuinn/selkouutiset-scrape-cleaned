# Tuesday 10.12.2019 (radio)

Parliament has voted on a new prime minister. The Nobel Peace Prize has been awarded in Oslo. There is a shortage of coders. Lingonberries may lower blood pressure.

## Parliament voted on the prime minister

![Sanna Marin after the prime minister vote in Parliament, at a press conference on 10 December 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11111185)

New Prime Minister Sanna Marin (SDP). Photo: Yle

Parliament has voted on the new prime minister.

She is the deputy chair of the SDP, Sanna Marin. Of the MPs, 99 voted for Marin and 70 voted against. 30 MPs were absent.

![Parliament voted for SDP deputy chair Sanna Marin as Finland's prime minister in the plenary session of Parliament in Helsinki on 10 December 2019. At the plenary session, the President's letter about the candidate for prime minister was announced as received, and a vote on the prime minister was held.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11111137)

Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö has granted the resignation of Antti Rinne's government. Rinne's government had been in power for only half a year. Most of the ministers will continue in Marin's government. Former prime minister Antti Rinne is no longer in the government.

The new prime minister, Sanna Marin, is 34 years old. She is the youngest prime minister in Finland's history. Marin is also the youngest prime minister in the world currently in office.

Marin believes that citizens' trust in politics will be restored through actions.

## Nobel Peace Prize has been awarded

![Queen Sonja, Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed, King Harald, Crown Prince Haakon and Crown Princess Mette-Marit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_393/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6208865def8f2f8059f/1575980973)

Open image viewer

Queen Sonja, Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed, King Harald, Crown Prince Haakon and Crown Princess Mette-Marit. Photo: Terje Pedersen / EPA

The Nobel Peace Prize was awarded in Oslo, Norway. The Peace Prize went to Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed.

The prize is a recognition of the peace agreement that ended the war between Ethiopia and Eritrea. In his acceptance speech, Ahmed also thanked Eritreans.

The Peace Prize ceremony took place at Oslo City Hall. The Norwegian royal family was also present.

The prize has not received only praise. In Ethiopia, the prime minister is accused of being authoritarian. In addition, political violence has increased in Ethiopia.

People in Norway have also protested against Ahmed's prize.

## Shortage of coders

![Coding at a Code Academy training course in Kuopio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11064547)

Open image viewer

There is a shortage of coders at software companies. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

Software companies need more workers. There is a shortage of coders, for example. A coder writes instructions for a computer.

Coders are being sought through, for example, continuing education and retraining. Universities and universities of applied sciences do not produce enough skilled people.

The Finnish federation for telecommunications and IT says that about 40,000 workers will be needed within 5 years.

Banks, insurance companies, and metal and forestry companies also compete for workers.

Many companies hope that work-based immigration will ease the shortage of coders.

## Lingonberry lowers blood pressure

![Lingonberry](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11051604)

Open image viewer

The health effects of lingonberries are being studied a lot in Finland now. Photo: AOP

Drinking lingonberry juice lowers blood pressure if it is raised. Lingonberries also improve the functioning of blood vessels.

This is what a doctoral dissertation at the University of Helsinki shows.

Lingonberry juice also prevented low-grade inflammation. Cranberry juice and bilberry juice did not have as clear effects.

Researcher Anne Kivimäki says that lingonberry juice does not replace medicine. However, it is a good addition to the diet.
