# Tuesday 31.1.2023 (radio)

Industrial Union strikes begin. The majority of MPs support selling wine in grocery shops. Fewer housing loans were taken out than before. A bomb attack killed many people in Pakistan. The weather is cloudy.

## Industrial Union strikes

![Industrial Union chair Riku Aalto and Technology Industries of Finland managing director Jarkko Ruohoniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3984,h_2241,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106466663d3835dc8567/1674809928)

Industrial Union chair Riku Aalto and managing director of Technology Industries of Finland Jarkko Ruohoniemi. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The Industrial Union's strikes begin on Wednesday in the technology industry.

National Conciliator Anu Sajavaara has not given a new settlement proposal in the labour dispute. The Industrial Union and the Technology Industries of Finland employers disagree on pay rises. Mediation continues on Friday.

People go on strike on Wednesday at dozens of workplaces in the technology industry. The strikes last until Friday. The Industrial Union rejected the settlement proposal in the technology industry dispute last Friday.

Next week the strikes may spread to other sectors. For example, the Service Union PAM has announced strikes in shops.

## Wine in grocery shops?

![Wine glass.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4160,h_2340,x_0,y_2263/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106603463d7dc933865b/1675091123)

Yle has asked MPs for their opinion on wine in grocery shops.

A majority of MPs support approving the sale of wine in grocery shops. 133 MPs responded to the survey. 69 MPs supported selling wine in grocery shops

Selling wine in grocery shops gets the most support among MPs of the National Coalition and the Finns Party.

## Housing loans

![Estate agent's sign in front of a flat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3460,h_1946,x_3,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1016939633d5d336cc8b/1673530560)

People in Finland took out housing loans from banks worth 1.1 billion euros in December. This is 730 million euros less than a year ago.

The Bank of Finland says that the average interest rate on new housing loans was 3.3 percent. The interest rate rose compared with November.

## Pakistan bomb attack

![Pakistani security personnel guard in front of an ambulance.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_956/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106581563d79dda27ccd/1675075151)

Open image viewer

Photo: Bilawal Arbab / EPA

A bomb attack killing many people was carried out in Pakistan on Monday. The police say that at least 100 people have died and at least 225 people have been injured. A large part of the victims of the attack are police officers.

The bomb exploded in the city of Peshawar. The bomb attack took place at evening prayer time in a mosque. Over 300 people were praying in the mosque when a suicide bomber detonated the bomb. The mosque collapsed on the people.

The weather on Wednesday is cloudy across almost the whole country. In Lapland it snows lightly in places. In Southern Finland too it may snow a little.
