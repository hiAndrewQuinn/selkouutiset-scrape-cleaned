## Alcohol policy

![Wine bottles on a shelf at Alko](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_653/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-76290560019de426e40/1610719106)

Open image viewer

Wines stay at Alko. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Alcohol policy has been discussed in the government talks.

Yle's information says that there will be no big changes in alcohol policy. Grocery shops are not allowed to start selling wine. In the future too, wine and strong alcoholic drinks can only be bought from Alko.

In the government talks, the National Coalition and the Finns Party were of the opinion that grocery shops could also be given permission to sell wine.

However, the Christian Democrats opposed this. That is why selling wine in grocery shops is not going to be allowed.

## Inflation

![Different coffee packets on a supermarket shelf.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3949,h_2221,x_3,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90264661e531ea07044/1642411630)

Open image viewer

The price of coffee has fallen a little. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

Inflation, that is, the rise in prices, has slowed down. In May, prices were 6.8 per cent higher than a year earlier.

Inflation has not been this low for over a year. For example, in April, prices were almost 8 per cent higher than a year earlier.

The slowing of inflation can also be seen in the price of food. Food no longer rises in price as fast as it did still in the winter.

The good news is that the price of some foods has even fallen. For example, coffee and salads were cheaper in May than a year ago.

Silvio Berlusconi's funeral has been held in Italy. Berlusconi was a former Italian prime minister.

Berlusconi's funeral was held on Wednesday in Milan. The day was a national day of mourning in all of Italy.

Silvio Berlusconi died on Monday. He was 86 years old.

## Joensuu murder case

![Police press conference in Joensuu about the murder suspicion of a four-year-old child. The police told about the Joensuu case on Wednesday. Photo: Lucas Holm / Yle

The police have given information about the Joensuu crime case. A 4-year-old boy died in Joensuu last week.

The police suspect that the child's mother and the mother's man murdered the boy. They have been jailed.

The police say that many injuries were found on the dead child. The boy had bruises and burns.

The death of a small child has shocked many people. On Sunday, a memorial service was held in Joensuu church, and many people came.

The police do not yet give more detailed information about the case.
