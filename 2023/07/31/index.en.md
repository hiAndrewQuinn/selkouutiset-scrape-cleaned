## Economy

![Kristian Simppeltoinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5987,h_3367,x_0,y_176/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114677664be85332ffe9/1690208140)

The economic situation of Finns is slowly starting to improve. People may soon have more money to buy goods and services.

The economy is improving because wages are rising and at the same time the rise in prices is slowing down.

Almost all prices have risen very quickly for a couple of years. Now statistics show that the rise in prices may finally be slowing down.

For example, petrol is already much cheaper than a year ago.

## More police

![Police vehicles and a police motorbike at a police operation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4167,h_2343,x_0,y_219/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114870364c368c20317c/1690528020)

Open image viewer

The government wants police to have more time for important tasks. Photo: Jyrki Ojala

The government plans to increase the number of police in Finland. The government wants 8,000 police in Finland. Today there are fewer than 7,500 police.

The government also plans to move some tasks away from the police.

Today, for example, police take drunk people to the cells and guard security in court. The government thinks someone else could do such tasks.

The aim is that in the future the police will have more time for example to investigate crimes and to monitor traffic.

## Demonstrations

![A person dressed in white stands with one foot on the Swedish flag and the other on the Danish flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5291,h_2976,x_14,y_216/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114966764c67915a2ae1/1691503203)

Denmark and Sweden are considering whether they can ban the burning of the Quran at demonstrations.

In both countries, protesters have recently burned Qurans.

Many Islamic countries are angry at Denmark and Sweden. Burning the Quran insults the religious feelings of their citizens.

The situation is difficult for politicians. In Denmark and Sweden, the law gives wide freedom to express different opinions. In general, opinions may also be expressed even if they insult the feelings of other people.

Freedom of expression is an important value in both countries. It is difficult for politicians to restrict it.

## Mushrooms

![A mushroom picker in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_418/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7055315f2ab9faca2c9/1690802264)

The chanterelle is a delicious edible mushroom. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Mushrooms grow again as autumn approaches. In some places in Finland there are already many mushrooms.

For example, in the forests of Central Finland there are many chanterelles and other edible mushrooms too. In Southern Finland there are no edible mushrooms yet.

The amount of mushrooms is affected by rain, heat and the condition of the forests.
