# Radio. Clear News. Sunday 3.9.2023

Demonstration against racism. Problems in the construction industry. Mikkeli's bishop. Serious disease in hares. The weather.

## Demonstration against racism

![Demonstrators at the Töölönlahti event park listening to speeches and music.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5496,h_3091,x_0,y_272/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116690064f49c4e400d1/1693752451)

The demonstration was peaceful, even though many people took part. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

There has been a big demonstration against racism in Helsinki. About 10 000 people took part in the demonstration.

The demonstration went peacefully. The programme included speeches and music.

Many demonstrators were angry at Finland's new government. In Finland a lot has been said about the old racist writings of some Finns Party ministers.

The demonstrators said that the government must protect the equality of everyone. They reminded that human rights belong to everyone.

## Problems in the construction industry

![Two construction workers take a break at a work site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5987,h_3361,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1100831643ead140e6b5/1733984110)

Open image viewer

New construction sites are now being opened only a little. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Things are going badly in the construction industry. The building of new homes has decreased.

Fewer homes are being built, because there are also fewer buyers of homes than before.

People do not have money to buy a new home, because many prices have risen and life is expensive. A home loan also becomes expensive, because interest rates have risen.

The construction industry estimates that bankruptcies will increase and many construction companies will have to stop operating. If the construction industry slows down, the whole Finnish economy will suffer.

Mari Parkkinen has been ordained into the post of bishop in Mikkeli Cathedral. Parkkinen is the new bishop of the Mikkeli diocese.

The bishop leads the church's work in the diocese area. The Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland has 9 bishops, 3 of whom are now women.

In addition, the Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland has an archbishop, who leads the work of the whole church.

## Serious disease in hares

![A dead young hare lies on the ground next to the steps.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98460962d90637e22d8/1658390151)

Open image viewer

A dead young hare. Photo: Risto Koskinen / Yle

Hares have died in Finland from the coccidiosis disease. Coccidiosis does not spread to people.

The disease spreads among animals through faeces. Coccidiosis always kills a hare that gets infected.

Coccidiosis has killed hares this summer, especially in the Kuopio area.

Before a hare dies, it screams, lies on the ground and kicks its legs. Many people have been shocked when they have seen a hare die.

Calling the emergency centre is the best solution if a person sees a sick hare that is suffering. A dead hare can be put in two plastic bags and then in mixed waste.

## The weather

![Weather maps for the start of the week, 4.9. to 6.9. During the week, the weather turns dry across the whole country.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116687664f483b43be7f/1693746143)

The new week begins dry in Southern Finland, that is, the rain stops.

In Northern Finland there will be rain on Monday, and on Tuesday there will be rain in the central parts of the country. After that it will be dry across the whole country, and the sun will also shine over a large part of the country.

During the start of the week the temperature is between 15 and 20 degrees. It is a little colder in Lapland.
