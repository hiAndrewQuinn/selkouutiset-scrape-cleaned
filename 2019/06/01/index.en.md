# Saturday 1.6.2019 (radio)

School-leaving celebrations were held on Saturday. This spring's matriculated students were born in 2000. Some foreign workers can now change field more easily. Marriages of under-18s are now banned completely.

## School-leaving celebrations

![Police monitored the revellers in Kaivopuisto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10811734)

School-leaving celebrations were held on Saturday.

About 560,000 basic school pupils went on summer holiday.

More than 80,000 young people graduated in a trade, and there are more than 25,000 new matriculated students.

The police will monitor children's and young people's celebrations at the weekend. The police will visit places where young people meet, for example parks and school yards.

The police hope that parents will agree on rules with their children before the celebrations.

## Generation Z

![Marianna Juvani](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10811755)

Open image viewer

Marianna Juvani is one of this spring's matriculated students. Photo: Antti Kolppo / Yle

This spring's matriculated students were born in 2000. They are said to be Generation Z. Generation Z includes everyone who was born in the late 1990s or the early 2000s.

The world of Generation Z is digital. Young people were born into a world where everything happens online.

Young people of Generation Z are worried about climate change. They fear that the earth's climate will get too warm. That is why many young people of Generation Z, for example, do not get a driving licence and a car.

Young people of Generation Z are also well behaved. They use alcohol and tobacco less than young people did before. Young people's crime has also fallen.

## Foreign workers

![Reinforcing a concrete element.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6207614)

Open image viewer

Changing field is now easier in some cases for foreign workers than before. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

There has been a small change to the Aliens Act. The change came into force on Saturday, that is, on 1 June.

The change concerns foreigners who work in Finland. Some foreign workers can now change field more easily.

A worker can change field more easily than before once they have worked in Finland for at least one year on a worker's residence permit. Then the TE Office no longer assesses whether there is a worker in Finland for the worker's new job.

The change concerns some dozens of foreigners a year.

## Marriages of minors

![A man slips a wedding ring onto a finger](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7461073)

Open image viewer

The Marriage Act has also changed. The law changed on 1 June.

The law changed so that marriages of under-18s are now banned completely. Under-18s may no longer marry even with a special permit.

Until now an under-18 has been able to get a special permit for marriage. The special permit was given by the Ministry of Justice. Few special permits have been given in recent years.

The law changed because Finland wants to protect children.
