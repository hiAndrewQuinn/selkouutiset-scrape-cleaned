Evacuation of Finns. Scam messages. Athlete of the Year. Dead small birds. The weather

## Evacuation flights

![A young woman with a hat on her head looks at the camera at the airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1186347652a2b2c46298/1697262526)

Javiera Kortelainen came from Israel to Finland on an evacuation flight. Photo: Ilkka Loikkanen

Finland has evacuated, that is, brought people to safety from Israel to Finland. An evacuation flight landed in Helsinki this morning. There were about 150 people on the flight.

In addition, about 30 Finns were evacuated on a flight that landed in Stockholm in the early hours of the morning.

Evacuation was necessary because the militant group Hamas attacked Israel from the Gaza area a week ago. After that, Finnair and many other airlines stopped their flights to Tel Aviv.

There are still Finnish travellers in Israel and the Gaza area, says Foreign Minister Elina Valtonen on Yle's Ykkösaamu. Valtonen says that Finland will not, however, arrange any more evacuation flights.

## Scam messages

![A close-up of a mobile phone and fingers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_125/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-110369764477493aaa80/1682404585)

Open image viewer

The police warn about scam messages. Photo: AOP

The police warn about scam messages that look as if they come from the tax authority.

The police have received reports of several cases where people have received a text message. The message looks as if it was sent by the Tax Administration's customer service, Omavero.

The message contained a link, where people were asked to enter their online banking codes. When people entered the codes, they ended up in criminals' hands, and after a while the bank account was empty.

The police remind people that online banking codes must never be typed into any link.

## Athlete of the Year

![Wilma Murto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5790,h_3256,x_0,y_29/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-116496864ef6683ce291/1693410976)

Pole vaulter Wilma Murto is Athlete of the Year. Photo: Getty Images

Pole vaulter Wilma Murto is Athlete of the Year. Murto received the award yesterday, Friday, at the Finnish Athletics Federation gala in Helsinki.

Murto has had a good season. For example, she jumped to bronze at the World Championships this year in Budapest and was the indoor European champion in Istanbul.

Wilma Murto also won the Athlete of the Year award last year.

## Dead small birds

![Two dead waxwings.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1374,h_772,x_0,y_83/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-118622565293e88c974b/1697201837)

Open image viewer

Lots of dead small birds have recently been seen on the streets of Helsinki.

Dead small birds have been spotted, for example, in Alppiharju, Kruununhaka and Kalasatama.

Juho Leppänen of the Helsinki Region Ornithological Society says that the birds are probably waxwings or coal tits. The birds have probably flown into a window, and that is why they died or were stunned.
