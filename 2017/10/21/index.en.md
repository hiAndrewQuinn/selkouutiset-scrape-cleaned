# Saturday 21.10.2017 (radio)

The Spanish government is reducing Catalonia's autonomy. There have been demonstrations in Tampere. Finns have done well at the world championships of vocational skills.

## The Catalonia crisis

![The Prime Minister behind a desk](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9894957)

Prime Minister Mariano Rajoy leads the Spanish government. Photo: Carlos Hidalgo / EPA

The political crisis in Catalonia, Spain, continues. The Spanish government plans to limit Catalonia's autonomy. The government announced this on Saturday.

Autonomy, or self-government, means that Catalonia can decide many things itself. Although Catalonia is part of Spain, it has decided itself, for example, about Catalonia's schools and health care. Catalonia also has its own police.

Now the Spanish government will start to decide Catalonia's affairs. The reason is that Catalonia has planned independence.

Some Catalans want Catalonia to leave Spain and Catalans to set up their own state. The Spanish government says that the plan for an independent Catalonia is illegal.

## The demonstrations in Tampere

![Neo-Nazis of the Finnish Resistance Movement gather at Laukontori square in Tampere on 21 October 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9894878)

Neo-Nazis demonstrated in Tampere. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

There have been demonstrations in Tampere. There were 2 big demonstrations in Tampere on Saturday.

There was a neo-Nazi demonstration at Laukontori square. The demonstrators belonged to a group called the Nordic Resistance Movement. It is a racist group.

Another group demonstrated at Keskustori square. The group is called Tampere Without Nazis. It wanted to oppose the racist group's demonstration.

The demonstrations went peacefully. There were a lot of police in Tampere. The police made sure that the different groups stayed in different places.

## Finnish world champions of vocational skills

![Emeliina Papinniemi world champion vocational skills competition WorldSkills](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9892905)

Open image viewer

World champion in the pastry chef category Emeliina Papinniemi. Photo: WorldSkills

Finns have done well at the world championships of vocational skills, the WorldSkills competition. The competition was held in Abu Dhabi. Finland got 3 gold medals and 1 silver medal.

The gold medal winners are Matias Korri, Sofia Sundqvist and Emeliina Papinniemi. They are new world champions. Korri was the best in aircraft maintenance, Sundqvist in the practical nurse category and Papinniemi in the pastry chef category.

Emeliina Papinniemi was the best Finn at the competition. She got more points than the other Finns. Papinniemi is the first Finn to have won an international pastry competition.

Papinniemi graduated from the Saimaa vocational college Sampo 2 years ago. Now she is working and at the same time studying for a journeyman's qualification as a pastry chef.

A pastry chef makes fine pastries and treats. Papinniemi says that pure Nordic flavours are now trendy around the world.
