# Sunday 6.6.2021 (radio)

Youth celebrations. Encouragement to work in small places. Ice hockey world championships. Para athletics European Championships. Weather.

## Young people celebrate eagerly

![The police fined young people and ordered them to pour alcoholic drinks onto the ground at the school end-of-term weekend in Hietaniemi, Helsinki, on 5 June 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11967952)

Police with young people at Hietaniemi in Helsinki. Photo: Seppo Samuli / Lehtikuva

Young people have been celebrating the end of school this weekend. Young people have gathered in different parts of Finland.

During the night between Saturday and Sunday the police were busy. The police, for example, took alcohol away from young people.

In addition, the police cleared places where there were big groups of young people. For example, in Helsinki the police cleared the Hietaniemi beach and Sinebrychoff park.

## Young people are to be encouraged to rural areas

![Aerial view of the village centre of Pelkosenniemi. Pyhätunturi is visible on the horizon.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3319,h_1867,x_0,y_171/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-802108609152d304791/1620136901)

Open image viewer

There are many small places in Finland where, for example, more doctors are needed. Photo: Antti Mikkola / Yle

Prime Minister Sanna Marin supports the idea of encouraging young people to work outside the big cities.

One possibility is, for example, that a doctor does not have to repay the whole student loan if they go to work in an area where there are too few doctors.

For example, in Finland's neighbouring country Norway this is already done. Prime Minister Marin thinks the idea could work in Finland too.

## Finland in the ice hockey world championship final

![Jussi Olkinuora tries to stop the puck at Finland's goal. In front, Markus Eisenschmid and Ville Pokka are battling.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4904,h_2759,x_0,y_284/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81650060bbad5994299/1622913093)

Open image viewer

Finland's goalkeeper Jussi Olkinuora stopped Germany's attacks. Photo: GINTS IVUSKANS/AFP / Getty Images

Finland plays in the final of the ice hockey world championships late on Sunday. In the final, that is, the final match, Finland plays against Canada.

Finland reached the final when it beat Germany on Saturday. Finland beat Germany 2–1.

The hero of the Finnish team was goalkeeper Jussi Olkinuora. Olkinuora stopped Germany's attacks at Finland's goal.

Finland won the previous ice hockey world championships. Then too Finland played in the final against Canada. Now Finns are waiting for Finland to become world champions this time too.

The ice hockey world championships are played in the Latvian capital Riga.

## Finland's disabled athletes did well

![Leo-Pekka Tähti in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4034,h_2269,x_0,y_208/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81621060ba46ff8c7ce/1622820642)

Open image viewer

European champion Leo-Pekka Tähti. Photo: Jaakko Stenroos/All Over Press

Finland has done well at the para athletics European Championships. The European Championships for disabled athletes ended on Saturday in Poland.

Finland won 8 medals in the competitions. 5 of them were gold.

The best Finnish athlete at the European Championships was Leo-Pekka Tähti. Tähti won 2 gold medals in wheelchair racing.
