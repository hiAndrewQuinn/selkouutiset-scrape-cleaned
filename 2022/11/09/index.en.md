## Tax information

![Ilkka Paananen speaks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-863888615ed032b0b26/1633603713)

Supercell founder Ilkka Paanen earned about 59 million euros last year. Paananen paid about 22 million euros in taxes. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

The Tax Administration has published last year's tax information.

The tax information shows how much income Finns have and how much tax they pay.

Last year many high earners came from the games industry. For example, the founders of the games company Supercell still earn tens of millions of euros.

Among high earners there are more young people than before. For example, many of the successful people in the games industry are fairly young.

High earners are also almost always men. Among the 10 highest earners in Finland there is not a single woman.

Experts think it is good that tax information is not secret in Finland. Tax information shows who pays taxes for the good of society.

## Help for Ukraine

![Krista Mikkonen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4928,h_2772,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1022382634ee3ab550e9/1666114537)

Open image viewer

Interior Minister Krista Mikkonen spoke about Finland's aid to Ukraine. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

Finland has sent more help to Ukraine. Finland has sent generator sets to Ukraine, for example.

Ukrainians do not have enough electricity, because Russia has destroyed a large part of Ukraine's energy system. The situation is bad, because in winter energy is also needed for heating.

Minister Krista Mikkonen says that Finland intends to continue helping Ukraine. Mikkonen estimates that Ukraine will need help for a long time yet.

## Young people's well-being

![Saaga Salonen has suffered from distance learning during the corona time. Salonen photographed at home studying.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3617,h_2034,x_1103,y_1613/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-839506610d1122e7d95/1634095000)

Open image viewer

Distance learning is lonely. Photo: Tuomo Björksten/Yle

Young people's well-being got worse during the corona time. This is what the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says.

During the corona time young people were lonely. Many young people had a feeling that they were not part of a bigger group.

Especially young people who started studying during the corona time were lonely. Because of distance learning, they had nothing in common to do with other students.

## Meta layoffs

![Facebook's new logo after it changed its name to Meta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2584,h_1453,x_0,y_142/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-873251617afd8f7d973/1635453011)

Open image viewer

Meta logo. Photo: Justin Sullivan / AFP

The American technology company Meta is cutting jobs. Meta plans to lay off as many as 11,000 employees.

Meta owns the Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp applications. During the corona time Meta did well and hired thousands of new employees. Now Meta admits that the growth was too fast.

In addition, the general economic situation is now bad. That is why Meta is also doing worse than last year.
