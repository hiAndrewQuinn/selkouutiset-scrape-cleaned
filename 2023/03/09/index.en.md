## Russian air strikes

![Russian strikes on Ukrainian cities on 9 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1082776640991c0c1c95/1678348893)

Russia carried out dozens of air strikes in various parts of Ukraine last night. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Russia has again carried out dozens of missile and drone strikes on several cities across Ukraine.

The Ukrainian army says that Russia fired about 80 missiles, of which Ukraine's air defence shot down 34.

According to Ukraine, strikes have been carried out on Kyiv, Kharkiv and Odesa, for example.

Deaths have been reported at least in Kyiv and Lviv. The strikes have also caused widespread power cuts.

The Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant in southern Ukraine also was without electricity for a short time.

The President of the Republic, Sauli Niinistö, is currently visiting the USA.

Today he has visited the capital, Washington. There he has met, for example, American senators.

The President's Office says that Niinistö's meetings have discussed, for example, Finland's Nato membership and European security.

Tomorrow Niinistö will visit the Norfolk military base.

## The mood of conversation in Parliament

![Krista Kiuru speaks in the chamber.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5748,h_3233,x_0,y_450/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106451163d290861cde4/1734185046)

Open image viewer

Family and Basic Services Minister Krista Kiuru spoke at question time in Parliament in January. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

MPs think that the mood of conversation in Parliament has worsened.

Yle asked MPs about the matter. 139 answers were received to the survey.

More than 50 percent of respondents said that the mood of conversation has worsened in this parliamentary term. This is what especially the SDP's and the Centre's MPs think. On the other hand, more than 50 percent of the Finns Party's MPs said that the mood of conversation has not changed at all.

MPs say that the sharpening of the atmosphere shows, for example, in shouting in the chamber during plenary sessions of Parliament.

## Youth violence

![Map of Finland with three places marked: Oulu, Siilinjärvi and Helsinki. In Oulu on 8.3 a robbery and aggravated assault by young people, in Siilinjärvi an attempted murder on 4.3 and in Helsinki an attempted murder on 9.3.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1049,h_590,x_0,y_515/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10830416409d52cad513/1678369087)

More and more young people carry bladed weapons.

The police say that young people carry, for example, knives, folding knives and brass knuckles. According to the police, some of the young people's parents accept the carrying of bladed weapons.

The Children's Ombudsman, Elina Pekkarinen, says that young people carry bladed weapons for self-defence reasons. However, possessing a bladed weapon in a public place is a crime.

In recent days several violent incidents have happened in Finland in which young people have used bladed weapons.
