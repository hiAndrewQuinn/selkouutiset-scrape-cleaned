# Wednesday 3.4.2019 (radio)

Advance voting for the parliamentary elections has started. Election advertisements have been vandalised in Jyväskylä and Tampere. The death toll in the Kitee traffic accident has risen to four. Brunei is introducing sharia law. Schoolchildren hope for more time to be with friends.

## Advance voting for the parliamentary elections has started

![Election flag](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10704928)

Open image viewer

Advance voting has started. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

Advance voting for the parliamentary elections has started today, Wednesday.

You can vote in advance until next week's Tuesday, that is, until 9 April. Election day is next week's Sunday, 14 April.

There are over 900 advance voting places in Finland in total. Voting places are, for example, in municipal and city halls, libraries, shopping centres and post offices. You can also vote in advance abroad.

You can vote in advance if you do not want to or cannot vote on election day. You do not need to give any reason for voting in advance.

Election advertisements of candidates in the parliamentary elections have been broken and vandalised in Jyväskylä and Tampere, at least.

In both cities advertisements have been broken in many places.

The police are investigating the matter.

## Fourth death in the Kitee traffic accident

The accident happened shortly after 2 am at night on Route 5, a couple of kilometres south of the municipal border between Kitee and Tohmajärvi. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10715552)

A young woman who was seriously injured in the traffic accident in Kitee has died. Photo: Hannu Rissanen / Lehtikuva

A woman who was injured in the traffic accident in Kitee in North Karelia has died.

The East Finland Police say that an 18-year-old woman from Joensuu died yesterday, Tuesday, at Kuopio University Hospital. She was the driver of the other accident car.

In the head-on collision between a car and a minibus that happened on Sunday night, 3 people died straight away.

The police have said that the man who was driving the car was drunk and was driving far over the speed limit.

## Brunei introduces sharia law

![Sultan of Brunei Hassanal Bolkiah](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10710960)

Open image viewer

Sultan of Brunei Hassanal Bolkiah. Photo: EPA

In Southeast Asia the state of Brunei is introducing strict sharia law. For example, homosexuals face the death penalty, which will be carried out by stoning. A thief may have a hand cut off.

The UN and human rights organisations, among others, have criticised Brunei's plans very harshly.

Brunei, which is on the island of Borneo, is a rich oil state. More than 400,000 people live there.

## Schoolchildren want to spend time with friends

![Jamal Ogbu (left) and Ville Schneider at break at Viikki Normal School.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10720208)

Open image viewer

Seventh-graders Jamal Ogbu and Ville Schneider from Viikki Normal School in Helsinki want time to be with friends. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

Schoolchildren hope for more time to be with friends. A schoolchildren's survey made by the National Agency for Education shows that the schoolchildren who are most satisfied with their lives are those who have friends.

Schoolchildren also want to spend their free time at home.

Boys' favourite hobby is football and girls' favourite hobby is horse riding. However, only 15 percent of the children and young people who answered want more hobbies.

Schoolchildren also want to influence their schooling. More than half answered that they want to change things.

95,000 schoolchildren answered the survey.
