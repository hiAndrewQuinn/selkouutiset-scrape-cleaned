# Monday 18.2.2019 (radio)

The Foreign Ministry does not know whether there are Finnish ISIS fighters held as prisoners in Syria. 120 workers will be hired for the kindergartens in Jyväskylä this winter. There are dangerous particles in the smoke from fireplaces and sauna stoves. Pupils in Southern Finland are on their ski holidays. Stray dogs have come to Imatra from Russia.

## "No sympathy for ISIS fighters"

![ Timo Soini](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10635862)

Open image viewer

Timo Soini. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The Finnish Foreign Ministry does not know whether there are Finnish ISIS fighters held as prisoners in Syria.

Kurdish soldiers have taken about 800 foreign prisoners. They have been members of the terrorist organisation ISIS. There are also wives and children of ISIS members in the refugee camps. The USA demands that Europe take in its own citizens and bring them to court.

Perhaps a few dozen people have left Finland for Syria or Iraq and joined ISIS. Foreign Minister Timo Soini says that he has no sympathy if there are Finns among the ISIS prisoners who want to return to Finland.

## 120 new jobs in Jyväskylä kindergartens

![Children's feet under a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8063439)

Open image viewer

Jyväskylä is hiring permanent workers for kindergartens. Photo: Titta Puurunen / Yle

Jyväskylä will hire 120 new permanent workers for children's kindergartens this winter. The jobs will be in four kindergartens, two of which are still being built.

The city of Jyväskylä says that more daycare places are needed, because there are fewer unemployed parents of small children than before. Also, children's right to daycare is wider than before.

Jyväskylä has 50 kindergartens, and about 1,200 people work in them.

## Only clean and dry wood in the fireplace

![A fire starts in the fireplace.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10648391)

Open image viewer

It is nice to watch a fire. Photo: Lauri Rautavuori / Yle

Unhealthy substances spread into the air with the smoke from fireplaces and sauna stoves. They can cause cancer and other diseases.

For example, the air in one district of Lahti was measured, and many unhealthy particles were found in it.

In Finland, they are now trying to develop sauna stoves in which the wood burns better. In addition, people are being advised how wood should be burned. The smoke is not so dangerous if you burn only dry and clean wood in the fireplace, and no rubbish.

## Ski holiday started in fine weather

![Skiers on a ski lift.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10651777)

Open image viewer

Ski centres are busy now. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

Pupils in Southern Finland are on holiday this week.

The ski holiday has started in sunny and warm weather. This winter there is no need to go to the north to ski, because there is also enough snow in the south.

For example, in Kirkkonummi the ski centre season started already in November, when it was frosty and snow fell almost a month earlier than last winter.

## Stray dogs from Russia

![A wild dog pack in Svetogorsk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10363986)

Open image viewer

Dogs from Svetogorsk last summer. Photo: Kalle Schönberg / Yle

Packs of stray dogs have come to Imatra in Eastern Finland from Russia.

People have reported dog packs to the police many times this month. The police say that the packs usually have 7 or 8 dogs. Stray dogs may not be a danger to people or pets, but they should be avoided, especially if you are walking your own dog.

The dogs have apparently come from the town of Svetogorsk across the border, which is only a few kilometres from Imatra.

If the police catch the dogs, they will probably put them down. Russian stray dogs are sometimes aggressive, and they may spread diseases.
