## Birth rate

![A newborn baby sleeping in a bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5394,h_3034,x_0,y_472/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-3106575808e4218cb65/1534956641)

The birth rate has turned to decline again in Finland.

During the corona years the birth rate turned to rise after several years of decline. The rise has now broken off during the early part of the year, and fewer babies are being born again.

The Family Federation says that people are postponing starting a family, because economic prospects are uncertain.

For example, the price of petrol, mortgage interest rates and employment prospects affect whether people want to have children.

## Care queues

![intensive care hospital bed](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11579237)

Open image viewer

Intensive care hospital bed. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

Queues for care have grown longer in all hospital districts in Finland.

The Institute for Health and Welfare THL says that at the end of April about 150,000 people were waiting for access to non-urgent specialised health care. About 13,000 of them had been waiting for care for over half a year.

The longest waiting times at the start of the year were in the North Savo hospital district. The shortest waiting times were in the Kymenlaakso hospital district.

Many young people have today been told about their study place for next autumn. In the upper secondary joint application almost all applicants got a study place.

A total of 76,000 applicants applied to upper secondary schools, vocational schools and other education, of whom 91 percent got a study place.

Young people must apply for upper secondary education after comprehensive school, because compulsory education ends only at the age of 18.

## New fish species

![Tubenose goby and another alien species, the slender shrimp, in a white dish.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_669,h_375,x_0,y_12/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97095262aae929a6c53/1655372979)

A new fish species has been found in the Gulf of Finland.

The new fish species is the tubenose goby. It is a small fish that lives at the bottom of the sea.

The tubenose goby comes from the Caspian Sea, from where it has spread through rivers and canals. The first observation of the new fish species was made in the eastern Gulf of Finland in May.

The tubenose goby is harmless to people, but it is not yet known how it affects other plants and animals in the sea.
