# Thursday 1.4.2021 (radio)

The Finns Party is the most popular party. The government wants to change the order of vaccinations. Over a million corona vaccine doses have been given in Finland. There is a big corona risk in the construction industry. Many applicants for universities. The weather is dry and sunny.

## Party support

In Yle's new opinion poll, The Finns Party is the most popular party.

The second most popular party is the National Coalition Party. The SDP is third. The support for each of the three parties is just under 20 percent.

Yle's poll asked which party people intend to vote for in the municipal elections. The municipal elections will be held in June.

## Order of vaccinations

![A nurse who is preparing Astra Zeneca vaccine at the Porvoo vaccination centre. 18.3.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6016,h_3384,x_0,y_498/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78564760534b7ff2b74/1616071673)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The government wants to change the order of vaccinations.

The government proposes that first the corona vaccine is given to members of risk groups and to people who treat corona patients.

After that, vaccinations are given to people by age order. Vaccinations start with the oldest people.

The government also wants to increase vaccinations in areas with more coronavirus. At the same time, vaccinations would be reduced in areas with little coronavirus.

No changes have been approved yet.

## Construction industry

![Construction workers installing concrete barriers for a road block on Valtaväylä 3 on 27.3.2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4405,h_2478,x_0,y_227/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6588665e7f4aa7a4d7b/1585403488)

Construction workers are now at the greatest risk of catching corona.

This is according to a new Finnish research report.

The report lists 25 occupational groups that are at the greatest risk of catching the coronavirus.

At the top of the list are many construction trades. These include, for example, construction workers, painters and electrical installers.

## Universities

![A woman works at a table in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11360485)

Open image viewer

The two-hour medical field exam had 12 different sections and 120 questions in total. More than 7,000 applicants took part in the first stage of the entrance exam. Illustrative image. Photo: Anni Reenpää / Lehtikuva

More people have applied to universities than last year.

In total, almost 158,000 people applied to universities of applied sciences and universities. That is more than 6,000 more than last year.

At universities, the most popular fields are medicine, agriculture and forestry, and service fields.

At universities of applied sciences, it is hardest to get into dental hygienist and dental technician training, and into the natural sciences.
