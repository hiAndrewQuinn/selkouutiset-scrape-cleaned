# Sunday 21.10.2018 (radio)

The dispute between the United States and Russia over nuclear weapons. There will be a strike in Finland tomorrow. Caring for pets costs money. There is strong wind at night.

## The United States and Russia are arguing over a treaty

![Donald Trump of the United States and Vladimir Putin of Russia met in Helsinki on 16 July 2018.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10314716)

The presidents of the United States and Russia met in Helsinki in July. Photo: Anatoli Maltsev / EPA

The United States and Russia are arguing about the nuclear weapons treaty. The treaty bans medium-range nuclear missiles.

The countries signed the treaty 30 years ago. The treaty ended the arms race between the countries.

Now the United States says that it will withdraw from the treaty. The United States says that Russia is breaking the treaty and is making weapons that are banned.

Russia threatens to take revenge if the United States withdraws from the treaty.

Experts say that Russia is no longer such an important opponent for the United States. China, on the other hand, is a big and important opponent.

## A 2-day strike starts tomorrow

![A strike guard armband.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10454200)

Open image viewer

A strike guard is an employee who makes sure that the situation stays calm during a strike. Photo: Timo Jaakonaho / Lehtikuva

In Finland there is a strike tomorrow, Monday, and the day after tomorrow, Tuesday.

10,000 employees are on strike.

Some employees on strike make food in day-care centres, schools and care homes. Children may need to bring packed lunches to day-care and school.

Some employees on strike also work in libraries, swimming halls and sports facilities.

## A lot of money is spent on caring for pets

![Animal carer Mari Weiden prepares the cat Niisku for an operation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7843216)

Open image viewer

Today a vet can carry out a complicated operation on a pet. Photo: Pertti Huotari / Yle

Finns spend a lot of money on treating sick pets.

Veterinary medicine has developed. Today a vet can perform a difficult operation on a pet. That is why the costs of caring for pets have grown, even though there are as many pets as before.

Only a small part of pet owners have taken out insurance for their pet. Most pet owners pay the treatment costs themselves.

If a pet is seriously ill, its treatment can cost many thousands of euros.

## It is windy at night

![A man on the seaside rocks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10455975)

Open image viewer

Tomorrow night there will be a very strong wind at sea. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

There will be strong wind tomorrow night. The wind starts to pick up already in the evening.

There will be storms at sea, and on land the wind will be gusty in Southern and Central Finland. The wind may cause power cuts.

The wind will calm down tomorrow.

Tomorrow, Monday, the sky is clear and the weather is dry.
