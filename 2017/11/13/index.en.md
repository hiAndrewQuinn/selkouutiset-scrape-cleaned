# Monday 13.11.2017 (radio)

Many young people follow a special diet. An earthquake caused destruction in Iran and Iraq. A Finnish citizen is also running for president of Somaliland. Finland's independence is celebrated with a commemorative coin. Greetings from the Finnish school in Croydon.

## Young people's special diets

![Sanni Issakainen, Julia Ikonen, Pinja Leppänen and Anni Tirkkonen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9928777)

17-year-olds Sanni Issakainen, Julia Ikonen, Pinja Leppänen and Anni Tirkkonen all follow a special diet.  Photo: Anu Rummukainen / Yle

Many young people follow a special diet. According to a new study, about 20 percent of young people follow a special diet.

The most common reason for young people's special diets is illness. If a person has, for example, diabetes, coeliac disease or allergies, they cannot eat just anything.

The second most common reason for a special diet is weight control.

Especially among boys, a special diet is often also because of sports.

The study was done at the University of Eastern Finland. Data was collected for it from studies of young people's health habits over almost 40 years. More than 43,000 teenagers have answered the studies.

## Earthquake in the border region of Iran and Iraq

![A map of Iraq.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9928001)

Open image viewer

The centre of the earthquake was on the Iraqi side, near the Iranian border.

The earthquake in Iran and Iraq has caused great destruction.

More than 400 people died in the earthquake. More than 6000 have been injured. The number of victims may still grow.

The earthquake caused landslides, broke houses, and cut roads and electricity. More than 70,000 people need emergency shelter, says an Iranian aid organisation.

The earthquake happened in the border region of Iran and Iraq. There have been more than 100 aftershocks.

## Somaliland's presidential election

![Faisal Ali Warabe.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9927867)

Open image viewer

Faisal Ali Warabe. Photo: Yle

People in Somaliland have voted today in a presidential election.

There are three presidential candidates in total. Two of them have links to Finland.

One of the candidates is the former speaker of Somaliland's parliament, Abdirahman Irro. He used to live in Finland.

![Abdirahman Irro.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9927865)

Open image viewer

Abdirahman Irro. Photo: Yle

Faisal Ali Warabe, on the other hand, is a Finnish citizen. His UCID party was founded in Helsinki in the early 90s.

Somaliland declared itself independent from Somalia already in the 90s, but other countries have not recognised its independence.

## A commemorative coin for Finland's 100th birthday

![A commemorative coin struck in honour of Independence Day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9928555)

Open image viewer

Finland's commemorative coin is a 20-euro silver coin. Photo: Suomen Rahapaja Oy

100-year-old Finland gets a commemorative coin. The commemorative coin is a silver coin.

On one side of the coin there is a short text from Finland's declaration of independence in Finnish and Swedish. On the other side there is a picture of the Heimola house, where Finland's declaration of independence was signed on 6 December 1917.

The commemorative coin will be released in two weeks.
