# Thursday 16.2.2023 (radio)

Mediation in the dock workers' pay dispute will be held on Friday. Postal loaders are on a solidarity strike. The Clear Language Centre hopes for clear language from candidates in the parliamentary elections. The weather is cloudy.

## Dock workers' strike

![In Vaasa harbour, containers are inside a fenced area.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5460,h_3071,x_393,y_665/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107307563eca5fc4dc36/1704744571)

Mediation in the dock workers' pay dispute continues tomorrow, on Friday. The National Conciliator says that other pay disputes will also be mediated on Friday.

The AKT's harbour strike began on Wednesday. Because of the strike, ships are not loaded or unloaded in the harbours. That is why there are fewer goods in shops than usual.

About 9,000 workers are on strike in total. The workers want more pay.

## Postal solidarity strike

![A woman bends down and measures a large postal parcel.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1024,h_576,x_0,y_82/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106609463d81eb9308a9/1675108147)

The Postal Industry Union PAU has also started a strike. The post loaders are on strike, because they support the strike of the Automobile and Transport Workers' Union AKT. PAU's solidarity strike continues until next Tuesday, if the AKT strike does not end before Tuesday.

During the postal strike, letters and some newspapers may be delivered up to 2 weeks late. Postal parcels are delivered at least 5 days later than usual.

## Immigrants in schools

![Pupils follow the teacher at the front of the class.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4879,h_2744,x_0,y_75/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106973563e24fc39b913/1675777784)

In Finland there are about 20 schools where the mother tongue of most pupils is something other than Finnish or Swedish. Most of these schools are in the capital region.

In about 80 schools more than 30 percent of pupils are S2 pupils. An S2 pupil's mother tongue is something other than Finnish or Swedish. In most Finnish schools, fewer than 10 percent of pupils are S2 pupils.

## Clear language in the elections

![Close-up of the cover of the Selkosanomat magazine after the redesign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_516/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-883856619f6acd2a203/1638522958)

The Clear Language Centre wants the candidates in the parliamentary elections to speak clear language. The Clear Language Centre says that as many as 750,000 people in Finland find clear standard language too difficult. Politics and elections must be talked about in easy Finnish, says the Clear Language Centre.

The Clear Language Centre suggests that the election candidates make a video in clear language.

Advance voting in the parliamentary elections begins on 22 March. Election day is Sunday, 2 April.

The weather is cloudy. In the west snow falls in places, and in the south also sleet. On Friday daytime only a little snow or sleet will fall.

The temperature is mostly between +2 and -5 degrees. In Lapland it may be a little colder in places.

Driving conditions are poor in Eastern Finland, Central Finland and Uusimaa, and in places in North Ostrobothnia.
