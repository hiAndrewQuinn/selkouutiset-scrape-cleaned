# Saturday 24.4.2021 (radio)

Budget framework talks. Credit rating. May Day. The corona situation. The weather.

## The government continues economic talks

![Sanna Marin talking with Maria Ohisalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7984466082ca47e23ec/1619184703)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin (SDP) talks with Interior Minister Maria Ohisalo (Greens). Photo: Jussi Toivanen / Prime Minister's Office

The government has continued talks on Finland's economy. The talks are called the budget framework talks, or kehysriihi.

The talks were supposed to take place on Wednesday and Thursday, but they have dragged on. The talks are difficult. In the budget framework talks, the government agrees how much money will be spent on public spending in the coming years.

The governing parties have different opinions on how to improve Finland's economy and, for example, employment. The government's budget framework talks will continue on Sunday too.

## Finland has a good credit rating

![The office of the credit rating agency Fitch Ratings in New York.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4738,h_2665,x_0,y_267/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6351605e2c082d30735/1707557159)

Open image viewer

Fitch predicts that Finland's economy will grow by 2.3 percent this year. Photo: Andrew Gombert / EPA

Finland's economy is valued. The international credit rating agency Fitch has again assessed Finland's economic situation. Finland gets the credit rating AA+. That means Finland's economy is stable.

The credit rating agency says that Finland's economy will stay stable because Finland has good social security, flexible labour markets, and because Finland has not needed many corona restrictions.

Finland's economy will also stay stable because Finland does not depend on tourism.

## Restaurants on May Day

![Sparkling wine being poured into glasses](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5857754)

Open image viewer

Corona restrictions are spoiling restaurants' May Day. Photo: Yle

Restaurants are waiting for May Day. Restaurant companies with many restaurants have received many bookings for May Day lunches.

However, May Day will not be as good for restaurants as usual. Because of corona restrictions, customer numbers and opening hours have been limited.

For example, many nightclubs will not open, because in many areas restaurants may only stay open until 11 pm. Normally, restaurants are busy on May Day.

## The corona situation is improving

![Corona vaccines waiting on a table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_208/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-791673606c23a559084/1617700205)

Open image viewer

Corona vaccines ready. Photo: Pasi Takkunen / Yle

About 1.5 million Finns have now received the coronavirus vaccination. About 27 percent of Finns have received the vaccination.

On Saturday, 263 new corona infections were reported.

The corona situation is improving. The number of infections is falling. Over 2 weeks there have been about 4,000 corona infections. That is about 2,600 fewer infections than during the two weeks before.
