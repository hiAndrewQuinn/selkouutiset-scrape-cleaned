## The unclear situation in Bahmut

![Destroyed Bahmut.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11170956469c559b4813/1684655919)

Bahmut has been destroyed in long battles. Photo: Sputnik / AOP

The situation in the battles for Bahmut is unclear. Russia may have won the battle for the city of Bahmut in Eastern Ukraine.

The Russian mercenary army Wagner says that it has captured Bahmut. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyi said on Saturday evening that Bahmut may no longer be under Ukraine's control. Ukraine's deputy defence minister said on Sunday that Ukrainian troops have partly surrounded Bahmut.

Ukraine and Russia have been fighting over Bahmut since last August. There is no certain information about the situation in Bahmut.

## US aid package for Ukraine

![US President Joe Biden with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2800,h_1575,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11171096469d6918a753/1684661382)

The USA is giving more military aid to Ukraine. The USA is giving Ukraine more ammunition, artillery and armoured vehicles. The aid package also includes training for Ukrainian soldiers.

The value of the aid package is almost 350 million euros. The aid package was announced by US President Joe Biden at the meeting of the leading industrial countries, the G7 countries.

Biden said that the USA will help Ukraine defend itself, recover from the war and rebuild the country.

## Family leave

There are still big differences between sectors in paid family leave. Photo: Jasmina Kauta / Yle

There are still big differences between sectors in paid family leave.

Yle has looked at over 30 collective agreements to see what was decided about family leave in the spring bargaining round. For example, paid family leave for many people with higher education got longer. Also in female-dominated sectors, paid leave for the other parent got longer.

In contrast, in male-dominated sectors with low-educated workers, there were not many changes to family leave. For example, in the agreements for construction, lorry and technology industry workers, almost no changes were made to the parental leave amounts.

## Memorial Day for the Fallen

![President Sauli Niinistö laid a wreath at the heroes' memorial on Memorial Day for the Fallen at Hietaniemi Heroes' Cemetery in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4823,h_2712,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11171086469d5ad925b3/1684657620)

Today, Sunday, Memorial Day for the Fallen has been celebrated. The day is a flag day.

On Memorial Day for the Fallen, Finns who have died in Finnish wars or in peacekeeping duties are remembered. During the day, for example, memorial church services were held. Wreath-laying was held at the Heroes' Cemetery in Lappeenranta.

In Lappeenranta, heroes' funerals were also held for 48 deceased people whose remains have been found in Russian territory. So far, 850 unidentified war heroes have been buried in Lappeenranta.
