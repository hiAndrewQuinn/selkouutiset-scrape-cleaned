# Wednesday 22.03.2017 (radio)

The car factory in Uusikaupunki starts making a new Mercedes-Benz car model. No bad Brazilian meat has been found in Finland. Street dust bothers people in spring. Many countries are short of water. Greetings from the Finnish school in Vancouver.

## The car factory in Uusikaupunki

![A worker on the assembly line at the Uusikaupunki car factory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9523163)

The Uusikaupunki car factory makes Mercedes-Benz cars, that is, Mersus. Photo: Valmet Automotive

The Uusikaupunki car factory has got work. It is starting to make a new car model of the German Mercedes-Benz.

Production of the new cars starts during 2018. The Uusikaupunki car factory says that it needs 1,000 new workers in the coming months.

The Uusikaupunki factory already makes some Mercedes-Benz car models now. The owner of Mercedes-Benz is the car company Daimler. Daimler has been happy with the work of the Uusikaupunki car factory.

## Brazilian meat

![Red meat at a meat counter in a shop in Porvoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9522490)

Open image viewer

No bad meat from Brazil has been found in Finnish shops. Photo: Stefan Härus / Yle

No bad Brazilian meat has been found in Finland. That is what Evira says, the agency that monitors food safety in Finland.

At the weekend it was reported that Brazilian companies have sold bad and old meat abroad. Meat has also been brought to Finland from Brazilian plants that have quality problems. However, the amount has been very small, Evira said on Wednesday.

## Spring street dust

![Street cleaning with a vacuum sweeper in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9504055)

Open image viewer

Street cleaning has already started. Photo: Markku Ojala / AOP

Street dust bothers people in spring.

Sand is spread on roads in winter, when the roads are slippery. When the snow melts, the sand rises into the air. It gets into people's noses, throats and eyes.

The sand also contains bacteria, mould and metal dust that comes from cars. Street dust is dangerous for people who have a heart disease or a lung disease.

In Helsinki, street cleaning has already started. A breathing mask also helps, but for sick and old people wearing one is too hard.

## World Water Day

![Two women and a girl collecting water from a small, dry sand pit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9522909)

Open image viewer

Women are collecting water. Photo: AOP

Many people have too little water. For example, many African countries suffer from drought.

The water shortage gets worse as the number of people grows. By 2040, perhaps as many as 600 million children will suffer from water shortage.

In Finland there is enough clean water for everyone. On Wednesday World Water Day was celebrated.

## The Finnish school in Vancouver

The newest greetings come from the Finnish school in Vancouver, Canada.
