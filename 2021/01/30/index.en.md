# Saturday 30.1.2021 (radio)

Municipal elections. AstraZeneca. Corona in Finland. Garden bird-spotting. The weather.

## The important themes of the municipal elections

![Built-up area sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11269970)

Open image viewer

Municipal elections will be held in Finland in April. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

Finns think that health and work are the most important matters in the municipal elections.

The Foundation for the Development of the Municipal Sector asked Finns' opinion on important election themes. At the top were health services, elderly care and employment. Other important themes are the finances of the home municipality and the safety of the local area.

On the other hand, for example, municipal mergers, immigration or cultural services are not considered important matters in the spring municipal elections.

Municipal elections are held in April.

## The AstraZeneca vaccine

![AstraZeneca vaccine bottle](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_287/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-76649960102b03796ca/1612710208)

Open image viewer

The AstraZeneca corona vaccine. Photo: Sven Simon / AOP

The EU Commission has given a marketing authorisation for the new corona vaccine.

The European Medicines Agency approved the drug company AstraZeneca's vaccine yesterday. Right after that the EU Commission gave the vaccine a marketing authorisation in the EU area.

Chief physician Hanna Nohynek of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that the new vaccine will come to Finland at the beginning of February. Nohynek says that in February perhaps about 200,000 doses of the new vaccine will come to Finland.

The EU and AstraZeneca have already argued in advance about deliveries of the vaccine. The drug company has said that it is not able to deliver the vaccine as much and as quickly as the EU wants.

## Finland's corona figures

![Corona testing in Tornio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7608655ffc573a0c10b/1612375860)

Open image viewer

Corona testing point in Tornio. Photo: Juuso Stoor / Yle

The coronavirus epidemic is speeding up in Finland.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that in the last two weeks almost 1,300 more infections have been reported than in the previous two weeks. Today, Saturday, almost 500 new infections have been reported.

A little under 145,000 people in Finland have so far received the coronavirus vaccine.

## Garden bird-spotting

![A great tit eating at a bird table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8763947)

Open image viewer

A great tit at the bird table. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

This weekend the birds in your own garden will be watched again.

The bird organisation BirdLife Finland arranges the traditional garden bird-spotting, which lasts the whole weekend. In the event, birds are watched for one hour in your own garden or in some other place. Observations are reported on the BirdLife Finland website.

## More snow

![A snowplough truck ploughs the road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6067440)

Open image viewer

A snowplough ploughs the snow. Photo: Yle

Today it has snowed across almost the whole country and driving conditions have been bad.

Tomorrow, Sunday too, it will snow in many places during the day. In the south and east the rain may stop from time to time and the sun may also show itself.

The temperature is between -4 and -12 degrees.
