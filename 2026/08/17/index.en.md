## Moped rally

Then we'll talk about the moped riders' protest.

Moped enthusiasts demonstrated in Helsinki over the weekend.

Hundreds of moped riders gathered at Kansalaistori on Saturday. Motorcyclists were also present.

The protest was sparked by police action in Tuusula a week ago. The police chased a young moped rider and drove him into a ditch with their car.

Moped enthusiasts demonstrated in Helsinki over the weekend.

The goal of the protest was to improve relations between the police and moped enthusiasts.

## African swine fever

Then news about the fight against African swine fever.

Conscripts can provide assistance in combating African swine fever.

The Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry has agreed on the matter with the Finnish Defence Forces, i.e. the Finnish Army.

For example, conscripts can monitor an infected area or search for dead wild boars in the forest.

Conscripts can help in the fight against African swine fever. However, no request for help has yet been made.

African swine fever has been found in wild boars in southeastern Finland near the eastern border.

Pigs and wild boars die from the disease, but it does not infect humans.
