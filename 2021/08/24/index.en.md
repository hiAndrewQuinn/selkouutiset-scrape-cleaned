# Tuesday 24.8.2021 (radio)

Finland has evacuated more people from Afghanistan. The restaurant and tourism sector has a labour shortage. The employment rate has risen. Shopping centres are doing well. The Paralympic Games have begun. The lingonberry harvest will be plentiful.

## Evacuations from Afghanistan

![Evacuation operation at Kabul airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12069915)

Open image viewer

Evacuation operation at Kabul airport. Photo: Defence Forces

Finland has evacuated more people from Afghanistan. Embassy staff and family members have been taken out of Afghanistan.

Among them there are also about 40 people from the EU delegation, says Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto.

Finland does not intend to evacuate Afghans who have worked for Finland through subcontractors. The reason is that the time limit for evacuations and the resources are not enough.

The extremist organisation Taliban does not agree to moving the deadline for evacuations from Afghanistan, says a representative of the organisation.

Finland has evacuated more than 200 people from Afghanistan.

## Labour shortage

![A restaurant with chairs on tables.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12028824)

Open image viewer

A lot of workers left the restaurant sector because of the corona lockdown. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finding workers is difficult in the restaurant and tourism sectors and in construction. Many changed field because of the lockdown measures of the corona pandemic.

It is also difficult to get workers for health care.

In a Statistics Finland survey, employers estimated that more than 50 percent of the jobs in April to June were hard to fill.

Employers' organisations say that education, labour immigration and better housing policy help the labour shortage more than pay does.

## Employment

![TE Office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_287/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7404265faa9712e0202/1605018827)

Open image viewer

The employment rate has risen to the same level as before the corona era. Statistics Finland says that employment grew and unemployment fell in July.

The employment rate in July was 72.7 percent.

There were 5,000 fewer unemployed people than in July last year.

## Shopping centres and cafés

![Shopping centre Ainoa in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-795784607ab4d3a310d/1618750684)

Open image viewer

Shopping centres were busy in the spring. Sales volumes and visitor numbers clearly rose in April to June.

The Finnish Shopping Centre Association says that visitor numbers rose by as much as 24 percent from the same time last year. Total sales grew by more than 20 percent.

Sales at cafés and restaurants rose by almost 90 percent from last spring.

The Tokyo Paralympics have started today.

162 countries are taking part in the Games. In total, 4,500 athletes will be seen at the Games. Finland's team has 16 athletes in 7 sports.

Finland is aiming for 7 medals. At the Rio Paralympics it got 3 medals.

## Lingonberry harvest

![Nico Kauppila reaches for a lingonberry.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7285295f804fa519cdd/1602244671)

Open image viewer

Many people feared that drought would take the lingonberry harvest. However, the harvest will be plentiful. Photo: Juuso Stoor / Yle

The autumn lingonberry harvest will be plentiful, says the Natural Resources Institute Finland.

The hot summer was hard for lingonberries, especially in dry areas, but pollination succeeded better than average. Because of the heat, there were many pollinating insects on the move.

Lingonberries withstand drought better than, for example, blueberries, whose harvest was smaller than normal.
