# Saturday 19.2.2022 (radio)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto spoke about the EU's reactions to Russia. There is a mobilisation in Eastern Ukraine. Horn protests in Finland. Teaching emotional skills at school. Weather.

## Pekka Haavisto

![Pekka Haavisto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_144/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91358862053550088d7/1645349699)

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto (Green League). Photo: Anders Wiklund / AFP

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says that the European Union's response will be quick, strong and coordinated, if Russia attacks Ukraine.

Haavisto said at the Green League's party council that Russia wants a return to a time that no longer exists. Thinking in spheres of influence does not belong to today, Haavisto said.

Russia does not want the NATO military alliance to expand east.

German Chancellor Olaf Scholz warns Russia that an attack on Ukraine would be a serious mistake. Scholz said at the Munich Security Conference in Germany that an attack would have large political, economic and geopolitical consequences.

## Mobilisation

![Map of Ukraine, showing the capital and the areas held by separatists in Donetsk and Crimea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12315692)

Open image viewer

Photo: Tommi Pylkkö / Yle

The leaders of the separatist areas in Eastern Ukraine are calling on all reservists to register. Several news agencies report on the matter.

The call has been made by the separatist leaders of both the Donetsk and Luhansk regions.

Donetsk separatist leader Denis Pushilin said that the reason for the so-called mobilisation is the increased threat of fighting.

The pro-Russian separatist areas of Donetsk and Luhansk consider that they are independent "people's republics".

## Horn protests

![Horn Protest 4.0 demonstration, organised by the grassroots movement "Stop Punishing Motorists!", arriving in front of Parliament House in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-917460620fc679ba88d/1645201089)

The horn protests are against the high price of fuel. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The horn protests have continued in Finland on Saturday. The protests are against the high price of fuel.

There have been protests in Helsinki, Jyväskylä, Kuopio, Lappeenranta, Pietarsaari and Kajaani.

Convoys of cars have sounded their horns and driven slowly in the city centres. The demonstrations went ahead without disruption.

## Teaching emotional skills

![Empty classroom at Ilmarinen comprehensive school in Lieto.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2200,h_1238,x_0,y_113/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6552185e733ec789d7b/1584611895)

Open image viewer

Illustrative image. Photo: Kalle Mäkelä / Yle

In Ylöjärvi in Pirkanmaa, emotional skills and interaction skills are taught in comprehensive school. These skills are a separate subject on the timetable.

The Finnish Mental Health Association says that the subject should also be added to the national curriculum. Studies and surveys say that teaching emotional skills in comprehensive school can reduce bullying.

In Ylöjärvi they teach naming and recognising emotions, and managing them. The teaching also includes skills of listening to others, waiting for one's turn to speak, and belonging to a group.
