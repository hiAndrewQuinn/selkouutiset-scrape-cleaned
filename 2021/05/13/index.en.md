## Parliament

The debate about the EU recovery package continues in Parliament.

The situation in Parliament, that is, the Finnish parliament, is unusual. Parliament has been debating the recovery package for many days already, and the debate has continued into the night as well.

The speakers are from the Finns Party. They do not accept the EU recovery package. That is why they continue their speeches, so that Parliament cannot yet vote on the recovery package.

The EU recovery package is 750 billion euros. The European Union wants to give money to EU countries that have suffered from the corona. The aim is to get the European economy moving.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare has said that there are 262 new coronavirus infections in Finland.

About 2 million Finns have already received a corona vaccination. That means that almost 37 percent of Finns have been vaccinated against corona at least once.

## Ascension Day

![Porvoo Cathedral](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6562745e7637fecfbc0/1584806042)

Ascension Day is a church feast day. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

Ascension Day has been celebrated in Finland.

Ascension Day is one of the Christian feast days. Ascension Day is celebrated 40 days after Easter.

In Finland, Ascension Day is a public holiday. That means that many people have a day off in the middle of the week.

On Ascension Day many workplaces and schools are closed.

The Muslim festival Eid al-Fitr has also begun. Muslims celebrate Eid al-Fitr when the fasting month of Ramadan ends.

Because of the corona, many Muslims celebrate only with their own family. Eid al-Fitr continues until Saturday.

Tens of thousands of Muslims live in Finland. However, the exact number is not known.

## Flower Day

![Spring flowers in an allotment garden.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6657975e9ae561c997d/1587209622)

Around Flower Day, Finland's nature begins to bloom. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

Thursday has also been Flower Day. Flower Day used to be an important feast day for school leavers.

On Flower Day, school leavers gathered outdoors for a shared celebration and enjoyed the arrival of summer.

The celebration on Flower Day was especially important in 1848. At that celebration, the song "Our Land" was performed for the first time, and it later became the national anthem of Finland.

The weather on Flower Day this year has been warm and sunny. The temperature has been about 25 degrees in a large part of the country.

During the rest of the week the weather cools down a little, but the temperature is still about 20 degrees. Rain showers are also possible later in the week.
