# Friday 10.2.2023 (radio)

Help for Turkey. Russian strikes on Ukraine. EU support for Ukraine. Outage of the Omavero service. The weather.

## Help for Turkey

![A forklift driver packs goods into a container.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688934)

Open image viewer

The Finnish Red Cross is sending 3 lorry loads of aid to Turkey. Photo: Marko Melto / Yle

The death toll from the earthquake in Turkey and Syria is rising. On Friday it was reported that about 23,000 have already died.

The earthquake happened in the southern part of Turkey, near the border with Syria, on Monday.

Aid organisations say that the situation in Turkey and Syria is really bad. Children in particular need help in the cold weather.

The Finnish Red Cross, or SPR, is sending aid to Turkey. SPR is taking blankets, mattresses and sleeping bags to Turkey, for example.

Three lorry loads of goods are leaving Finland for Turkey. The first lorry load left for Turkey on Friday.

## Russian strikes

![A destroyed house.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688935)

Open image viewer

Russian strikes have caused destruction in Ukraine. Photo: Yasuyoshi Chiba / AFP

Russia has carried out air strikes on Ukraine. Russia has fired missiles at various parts of Ukraine.

Russia also struck the Ukrainian capital Kyiv with missiles. Residents of Kyiv are looking for shelter in metro stations.

Some of the Russian strikes hit energy plants. That is why there have been power cuts in Ukraine.

Russia's war in Ukraine has lasted almost a year. Russia attacked Ukraine at the end of last February.

## Support for Ukraine

![Sanna Marin and Volodymyr Zelenskyy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688936)

Open image viewer

Sanna Marin and Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Brussels. Photo: EPA-EFE

The European Union's support for Ukraine continues. This is what the leaders of the EU countries say, who met in Brussels on Thursday.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy also took part in the meeting in Brussels. Zelenskyy asked the EU countries to give more weapons to Ukraine.

Ukraine has hoped for fighter jets, for example, that is, military aircraft suitable for air combat.

Finnish Prime Minister Sanna Marin believes that in the future EU countries will also discuss sending fighter jets to Ukraine.

However, Marin reminded that Finland is a neighbour of Russia. That is why Finland must also look after its own defence and think carefully about what weapons Finland can give to Ukraine.

It is also reported that during the weekend tax matters cannot be handled online. The Omavero service is out of use until Sunday evening.

Nowadays many people handle their tax matters online. The Tax Administration's Omavero service is popular.

## The weather

![Weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688938)

Open image viewer

At the start of the weekend cold air flows into Finland. On Saturday the temperature is below zero.

In the daytime the weather is dry, that is, without rain. The sun shines on Saturday in almost the whole country.

On Sunday the weather is mild in the west. The temperature rises to as much as 6 degrees above zero.

In the east the temperature stays below zero on Sunday too. In places it is 6 degrees below zero.

No severe frosts are coming at the beginning of next week either.
