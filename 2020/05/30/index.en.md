# Saturday 30.5.2020 (radio)

Finns can travel within their own country again. Greece is starting to let tourists into the country. School end-of-term celebrations were held on Saturday. A lot of mosquitoes may come to Lapland this summer. The weather is warm.

## Domestic travel

![The picture shows a view of Hanko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11374529)

Open image viewer

There are many good travel destinations in Finland. The picture shows the town of Hanko. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finns can travel within their own country again, that is, to different parts of Finland. On Friday the government said that travelling within Finland is already safe.

In the spring the government asked Finns not to make unnecessary domestic trips because of the corona. At that time it was feared that the coronavirus would spread with travellers.

Now the government says that domestic trips do not spread the coronavirus. However, people must follow the corona guidelines on trips too. Good hand hygiene and safety distances are important.

## Greece

![A restaurant worker measures the distance between tables in Athens.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11367677)

Open image viewer

Restaurants in Greece also keep safety distances. Photo: Kostas Tsironis / EPA

Greece is starting to let tourists into the country.

Greece will open in 2 weeks. Greece has been closed because of the corona.

On Friday Greece said which countries tourists may come from. One of the countries is Finland.

So Finns may soon be able to travel to Greece. However, the Finnish government says that it is not yet a good idea for Finns to travel abroad.

## End-of-school celebrations

![Schoolchildren collected their certificates in Helsinki on 30 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11377221)

Open image viewer

Pupils received their certificates this year without big end-of-school celebrations. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

School end-of-term celebrations were held on Saturday.

Because of the corona, no big celebrations have been held. In many schools the celebrations have been moved to the autumn.

On Saturday, celebrations were held only in small groups or with the help of a remote connection.

This spring, 25,000 new matriculation students graduated and 47,000 students graduated from vocational training.

Comprehensive school pupils got their summer holidays after an unusual spring term. Because of the corona, most pupils received remote teaching for about 2 months.

## Mosquitoes

![Mosquitoes on a person's skin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10338360)

Open image viewer

Mosquitoes suck blood. Photo: AOP

A lot of mosquitoes may come to Lapland this summer. Researcher Jukka Salmela estimates this.

A lot of mosquitoes are coming, because there was a lot of snow in the winter and it is now damp in Lapland.

Mosquito larvae can do well in damp nature. The larvae develop in puddles.

The larvae will still develop in Lapland for a couple of weeks. They will become mosquitoes a little before Midsummer.

Small mosquitoes can be a big nuisance for people. They suck people's blood. If there are many mosquitoes, it is hard for people to be outside.

## Weather

The weekend is warm. On Saturday the temperature in Southern Finland was about 20 degrees, and on Sunday it will be a little warmer still.

On Monday the temperature in Western Finland is even over 25 degrees. In Eastern Finland it is cooler, and there the temperature may be below 15 degrees on Monday.
