# Wednesday 18.9.2019 (radio)

The opposition is unhappy with the budget proposal. Alcohol sales have fallen. Niinistö will meet Trump. Rinne met Macron. Saarikko had a baby.

## The budget does not please the opposition

![Budget books in the plenary session of Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10628788)

Open image viewer

Budget books in Parliament. Photo: AOP

The opposition parties are unhappy with the government's budget proposal. The opposition parties are the parties that are not in the government. The government published the budget proposal yesterday.

The opposition parties say that the state does not have enough money for all the things in the budget.

The leader of the National Coalition, **Petteri Orpo**, says that the government's employment measures are not credible. The leader of the Finns Party, **Jussi Halla-aho**, the largest opposition party, says that the government is making a mistake if it sells a lot of state property.

## Alcohol sales fell

![Shelves of beer and cider in a shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10067486)

Open image viewer

More alcohol was sold the summer before last, because it was so warm. Photo: Annika Holmbom / Yle

Alcohol sales in grocery stores have fallen. In the early part of the year, that is January to August, about 2 percent less alcohol was sold in grocery stores than last year.

Sales fell especially in the summer. Last year the summer was very warm, and then a lot of beer, cider and long drink were bought. This year, not as much alcohol was sold in the summer.

The grocery trade estimates that passenger imports of alcohol from Estonia will increase. Imports may increase because Estonia has cut its alcohol tax, and alcohol is cheaper there than before.

## Niinistö will meet Trump

![Donald Trump and Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9781869)

Open image viewer

Trump and Niinistö will meet in October in Washington, USA. Photo: AOP

President **Sauli Niinistö** will meet the President of the USA. Niinistö will meet President **Donald Trump** on 2 October in Washington, USA. The presidents will discuss co-operation between Finland and the USA and improving security in Europe and the Arctic region.

President Niinistö will also travel to the USA next week for the UN General Assembly and will meet the UN Secretary-General António Guterres.

## Rinne met Macron

![Emmanuel Macron](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10755524)

Open image viewer

Rinne met Macron in Paris. Photo: Ian Langsdon / EPA

Prime Minister **Antti Rinne** is visiting France. Rinne met France's president, **Emmanuel Macron**, in Paris, at the Élysée Palace today.

Rinne and Macron discussed EU matters, such as the EU budget negotiations, climate action, defence co-operation and Brexit.

## Saarikko had a boy

![Annika Saarikko](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10976407)

Annika Saarikko (Centre) now has two sons. Photo: Laura Ukkonen / Lehtikuva

Centre minister \*\*Annika Saarikko \*\*has had a baby. Saarikko gave birth to a boy last night. Saarikko says that the birth was hard and took a long time.

Annika Saarikko went on parental leave a month ago. She will return to her job as Minister of Science and Culture next August. Saarikko already has a son, who has now got a brother.
