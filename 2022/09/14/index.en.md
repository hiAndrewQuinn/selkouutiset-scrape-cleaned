# Wednesday 14.9.2022 (radio)

A court banned nurses' strikes in intensive care wards. OP forecasts that house prices will fall. People try to avoid food waste. Queen Elizabeth's coffin is in London. The weather is rainy.

## Nurses

The Helsinki District Court bans nurses' strikes in hospitals' intensive care wards. Yle's information says that the court bans strikes in intensive care wards in the hospital districts of Southwest Finland, North Ostrobothnia and Kanta-Häme.

Hospital districts previously demanded that the court ban nurses' strikes. The hospital districts say that people will die if nurses go on strike in hospitals' intensive care wards.

![Food waste on a plate.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_420/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98952362e91d7760b93/1659444850)

About 75 percent of Finnish people believe that less food goes into the bin from them than before. A large part of people also try to live in the future so that food waste does not occur. Food waste means that good food goes into the bin.

The Consumers' Association asked people's thoughts about food waste. The survey says that most food goes into the bin from young adults.

## Queen Elizabeth's coffin

![Elizabeth's coffin is on display at St Giles' Cathedral in Edinburgh.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1731,h_973,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1006834631f584c3ea5e/1662998665)

Open image viewer

Elizabeth's coffin is on display at St Giles' Cathedral in Edinburgh. Photo: Jane Barlow / AFP

In Britain Queen Elizabeth's coffin is now in London. The coffin was moved to London from Edinburgh in Scotland on Tuesday.

In London the queen's coffin is on display in Westminster Hall. People can go to say goodbye to the queen, who died last week. The British government says that as many as 750,000 people will come to see the queen's coffin.

People have to queue for a long time to get close to the coffin. The queue can last as long as 30 hours. The queue can be 8 kilometres long.

Queen Elizabeth will be buried next Monday.
