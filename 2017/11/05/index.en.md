# Sunday 5.11.2017 (radio)

Puigdemont surrendered in Brussels. Bad drunk drivers at the weekend. Nightmares. Kamu, run by people with intellectual disabilities, has become a popular restaurant in Hamina.

## Puigdemont surrendered to the police

Police have arrested the former regional leader of Catalonia, Spain, in Belgium. Carles Puigdemont was arrested in the morning, when he and 4 other Catalan leaders went to the police.

Spain has wanted them to be arrested. Spain wants to bring Puidgemont and the others to court.

Puidgemont has tried to make Catalonia an independent state. Spain is angry with Puidgemont about it.

Puidgemont has been in Belgium for about a week. He travelled to Belgium after Spain dismissed Catalonia's regional government.

## Many drunk drivers at the weekend

![A police officer breathalyses a driver](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9436193)

Open image viewer

One drunk driver blew 4.4 per mille into the breathalyser.  Photo: Kalle Schönberg / Yle

The police have caught many drunk drivers at the weekend. The police caught drunk drivers who were driving cars while very drunk.

Many had a blood alcohol level of more than 2 per mille. For example, in Janakkala in Häme, a drunk driver was so drunk that he drove the wrong way on the motorway. The drunk driver thought that he was driving south towards Vantaa. But he was driving north towards Tampere.

In Rantasalmi in South Savo, the police stopped a man who was driving a car while extremely drunk. The man blew into the police alcohol meter, or breathalyser, and it showed 4.4 per mille.

## A large study on nightmares

![A man lies in a ball pit with a sleep mask on his face.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9683298)

Open image viewer

In a nightmare you can experience terrible and very strange moments.  Photo: Kalle Mäkelä / Yle

Many Finnish adults have a nightmare at least once a month. A new study says that about half of adults have a nightmare every month.  A nightmare is a bad dream that causes anxiety.

According to the study, having nightmares increases as people get older. Hundreds of thousands of Finns have nightmares every week.

The researcher estimates that people have nightmares when not everything is well in their life.

The new study looked at nightmares over a period of 40 years. Tens of thousands of Finns took part.

## Restaurant run by people with intellectual disabilities is popular in Hamina

![Jussi Hurttila](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9917341)

Open image viewer

Kamu, a restaurant of people with intellectual disabilities, operates in the Hamina fortress.  Photo: Juha Korhonen / Yle

Next, we tell you that a restaurant of people with intellectual disabilities in Hamina has become really popular. The restaurant Kamu was set up in Hamina four years ago so that people with intellectual disabilities could get work.

More than half of Kamu's workers have intellectual disabilities. They serve customers, wait tables, bring food, clean and help in the kitchen. But they do not cook the food.

Kamu wants to influence what people think about people with intellectual disabilities.
