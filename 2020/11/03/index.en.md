# Tuesday 3.11.2020 (radio)

There has been a terror attack in Austria. Last year's tax data has been published. A new November heat record has been measured in Finland. The US presidential election can also be followed on Yle's channels.

## Terror attack in Austria

![Memorial event](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11628805)

Open image viewer

The President and the Chancellor of Austria visited the site of the terror attack. Photo: AOP

There has been a terror attack in Austria. The terrorist killed 4 people and wounded many.

The terror attack happened on Monday evening in the Austrian capital, Vienna. The terrorist shot people on the streets of central Vienna.

The police shot the terrorist. He was 20 years old and a supporter of the terrorist organisation Isis. The terrorist probably acted alone.

The terror attack has shocked Austrians. The Austrian government decided that there will be a 3-day period of mourning in the country because of the terror attack.

Finnish President Sauli Niinistö and Prime Minister Sanna Marin have also expressed their condolences to Austrians.

## Last year's tax data

![Natalia Salmela](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4752,h_2673,x_0,y_245/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6516205e6761117f8d0/1583834095)

Natalia Salmela earns well from writing blogs. Photo: Tiina Leinonen

Last year's tax data has been published. The tax data shows how much money Finns have earned and how much tax they have paid.

The highest-earning Finns are entrepreneurs and managers. The entrepreneur Ilkka Brotherus had the highest income in Finland last year, about 66 million euros.

The income of Petri Järvilehto, director of the game company Seriously, was the second highest. Järvilehto earned over 30 million euros.

New kinds of professions can also be found among the high earners. For example, the YouTuber Lauri Puttonen earned over 230,000 euros last year. Puttonen makes videos for YouTube in which he opens packages.

Blogger Natalia Salmela also earned a lot last year. Salmela's income was almost 170,000 euros.

## November heat record

![Autumn yellowed leaves in a puddle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5760,h_3240,x_0,y_585/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7337125f92ac1b390e5/1603448630)

A new November heat record has been measured in Finland. At Pori airport, during the night between Monday and Tuesday, it was 14.7 degrees.

The previous November heat record was measured 5 years ago. Then 14.3 degrees was measured in Kemiönsaari.

## USA's presidential election

In the USA, i.e. the United States, a president is elected. The election is held in the USA on Tuesday, i.e. in Finnish time during the night between Tuesday and Wednesday.

The election can also be followed on Yle's channels. Yle's broadcast of the election starts during the night between Tuesday and Wednesday at 2:30 and ends on Wednesday at 11.

The broadcast can be followed at yle.fi, on Yle Areena, on TV1 and on Radio Suomi.
