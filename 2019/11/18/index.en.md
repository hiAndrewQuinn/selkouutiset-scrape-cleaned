# Monday 18.11.2019 (radio)

Negotiations on the postal strike continue. Finns use antibiotics less than before. Back pain is common nowadays. The weather continues quite warm.

## Postal strike

![Heidi Nieminen (left), chair of the Posti and logistics sector union PAU, and Tuomas Aarto, managing director of the service sector employers Palta ry, continued mediation of the postal dispute in Helsinki on 17 November under the leadership of the National Conciliator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11073363)

Representatives of the employees and the employer negotiated on Monday. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

Negotiations on the postal strike continue. The negotiators are trying to end the strike.

This week, many support strikes will also begin, if the postal strike does not end. Workers in other sectors want to support the postal workers with support strikes. The support strikes will, for example, stop freight traffic on the railways.

If the postal strike continues for another week, more support strikes will come. For example, a support strike will stop the buses of the Helsinki region transport next Monday. However, no new support strikes will come if the postal strike is ended.

The postal strike negotiators say that they have moved forward a little. A proposal for an agreement may soon be ready. The negotiators need to agree, for example, on pay.

The postal workers began the strike a week ago. Because of the postal strike, letters and parcels are now moving very slowly.

## Antibiotics

![Excessive use of antibiotics weakens their effect.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10312131)

Open image viewer

It is not good to use antibiotics needlessly. Photo: Rolex Dela Pena / EPA

Finns use antibiotics less than before. Antibiotic consumption has decreased by 5 percent a year for many years already.

Elsewhere in Europe, antibiotic consumption has not decreased. Antibiotics are used just as much as before.

When antibiotics are used a lot, bacteria learn to withstand them. That is why the number of bacteria that antibiotics no longer work on is growing. In Finland there are fewer of them than elsewhere.

## Back pain

![A man holding his back](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10037725)

Open image viewer

Photo: AOP

Back pain is common nowadays. There is back pain already among young people.

The reason for back pain is that people sit too much in a bad posture. Phones and computers are the cause of sitting in a bad posture. People also move too little.

However, back pain can be prevented. Chief physician Jari Ylinen says that good muscle fitness helps keep the back in good condition. He especially recommends training at the gym.

Chief physician Ylinen says that the sleeping position is also important. The back must be straight in bed. That is why, in Ylinen's view, it is good if the mattress is not too soft or too hard. A good mattress is medium-firm.
