# Thursday 15.6.2017 (radio)

The workers at the Turku shipyard marched out of the workplace. The fire in London is being investigated. Using a mobile phone abroad is getting cheaper. Mrs Jenni Haukio gave an award to the Finnish schools. The Finnish school in Basel sends greetings to 100-year-old Finland.

## Workers at the Turku shipyard

![Meyer's Turku shipyard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9413544)

Open image viewer

At the Turku shipyard, relations between the workers and the employer are now bad. Photo: Meyer

The workers at the Turku shipyard have marched out of the workplace. The workers left the workplace on Thursday. They are off work for the rest of the week, until Friday.

The workers are angry. They say that the mood at the workplace is bad. In the workers' opinion, they are watched and monitored too much.

Ships are built at the Turku shipyard. The shipyard has over 1,500 workers. About 1,000 workers marched out of the workplace.

## The fire in London

![Grenfell Tower burns](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9671476)

Open image viewer

Many people died in the fire on Wednesday. Photo: Adrian Dennis / AFP

The fire in London is being investigated in Britain. A tall apartment block burned in London on Wednesday.

On Thursday afternoon the authorities said that at least 17 people died in the fire. More dead people will almost certainly be found. There were perhaps several hundred people in the building when the fire started.

The fire was not a surprise to the residents of the building. They have been worried about whether a bad fire could happen in the building.

## Mobile phones abroad

![Couple with their mobile phones in Paris's Parc de la Villette.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9436545)

Open image viewer

Many people cannot be on a trip without their phone. Photo: Etienne Laurent / EPA

Using a mobile phone abroad is getting cheaper. The customer no longer has to pay an extra fee if they use the phone in the European Union area.

Although calls, messages, internet browsing and data transfer are getting cheaper, the customer must not use the phone too much.

If a customer uses a mobile phone a very great deal in the EU area, they must pay an extra fee.

## Award for Finnish schools

![Child in a Finland T-shirt being interviewed in front of the Finnish flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9468699)

Open image viewer

A pupil at the Finnish school in Dubai. Photo: Yle

The Finnish schools have received an award. The name of the award is Finn Abroad of the Year.

Mrs Jenni Haukio gave the award on Thursday. Haukio met teachers from the Finnish schools at the president's summer residence in Kultaranta.

There are Finnish schools in many countries. They teach the Finnish language and Finnish culture to Finns living abroad, children and young people. Finns living abroad are Finns who live in other countries.

Finn Abroad of the Year was chosen by Suomi-Seura. It is an organisation that takes care of matters for Finns living abroad.
