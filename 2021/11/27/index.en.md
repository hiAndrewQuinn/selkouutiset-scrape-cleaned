## A new corona variant

![Asko Järvinen, senior physician in infectious diseases, on Yle's Ykkösaamu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5997,h_3373,x_0,y_121/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88502261a1ea671d460/1638001865)

Senior physician in infectious diseases at HUS, Asko Järvinen, on Yle's Ykkösaamu. Photo: Benjamin Suomela / Yle

There is only little information about the new variant of the corona virus. This is what the senior physician in infectious diseases of the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district, Asko Järvinen, says.

The virus variant was found in southern Africa. It is not yet known what kind of protection the vaccines give against the new virus variant.

Passengers are being checked at Helsinki-Vantaa airport because of the new variant of the corona virus.

The authorities ordered that everyone arriving from southern Africa must have a corona test. Passengers must also stay in quarantine.

## Perttu Nousiainen under travel ban

![Former chair of the board of the Youth Foundation Perttu Nousiainen (right) at the custody hearing at the Pasila police station in Helsinki today.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_335/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88506461a21345381a6/1638011773)

Open image viewer

Former chair of the board of the Youth Foundation Perttu Nousiainen (right) at the custody hearing at the Pasila police station in Helsinki today. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The Helsinki District Court has ordered that former chair of the board of the Youth Foundation, Perttu Nousiainen, may not travel. The order will also be monitored.

The police demanded that Nousiainen be kept in custody. The district court rejected this demand.

Perttu Nousiainen is suspected of 7 crimes in total. He is suspected, among other things, of aggravated fraud.

The police arrested Nousiainen late on Wednesday, when Nousiainen came to Finland from Switzerland.

The National Bureau of Investigation is investigating deals that were made at the Youth Foundation. The former managing director of the Youth Foundation, Aki Haaro, is also suspected of crimes.

## Iivo Niskanen won at Ruka

![Iivo Niskanen celebrates his win at the Ruka race.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2470,h_1383,x_0,y_205/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88510061a2311e3b788/1638019417)

Open image viewer

Iivo Niskanen celebrates his win at the Ruka race. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

Iivo Niskanen won the cross-country skiing World Cup race at Ruka in Kuusamo. Niskanen won the men's 15 kilometre classic technique race by 8.3 seconds ahead of the Russian Aleksei Tshervotkin. In the race, third was the Russian Alexander Bolshunov.

Niskanen has now won the 15 kilometre classic technique race at Ruka for the 4th time. Niskanen also won the race in 2019, 2016 and 2014.

Krista Pärmäkoski was 5th in the women's 10 kilometre classic technique race.
