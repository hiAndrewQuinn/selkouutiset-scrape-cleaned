# Friday 8.5.2020 (radio)

Finland's economy is shrinking a lot. Clear language is often needed. Fewer marriages were held than before. The air in cities has become cleaner.

## Finland's economy is shrinking a lot

![Vesa Vihriälä's report was handed over on 8 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11342498)

Open image viewer

The group of economic experts is led by Vesa Vihriälä. Photo: Laura Kotila / Government Communications Department

Experts have calculated how much Finland's economy is shrinking because of the corona situation.

Experts say that Finland's economy will shrink by 5–13% this year. The economy will shrink so much that some companies will not survive. Companies will go bankrupt, which means that their business will end. Companies need support.

Experts say that because public debt is growing a lot, taxes in Finland must be raised and spending must be cut. For example, public services may have to be cut.

More resources are required for authorities and municipalities, that is, opportunities for clear language. The Clear Language Advisory Board says that nobody must be left out of information.

## Clear language is needed in crises

![Kela's logo in a window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10286679)

Open image viewer

Kela is also a member of the Clear Language Advisory Board. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Because of the coronavirus, Finland is in exceptional conditions. New information, instructions and recommendations keep coming all the time. Not everyone understands general-language instructions. About 750,000 people in Finland need clear language. Many of them belong to the risk group for the coronavirus. The advisory board says that instructions in clear language are very important.

Clear language is also needed in crises that may come later. Clear language is always needed when things are told that affect everyone's lives.

The Clear Language Advisory Board has 50 members. Members include, for example, Kela, the Tax Administration, many organisations, educational institutions and Yle.

## The number of new marriages fell

![A bridal couple, Haapaniemi shop, crinoline, Jyväskylä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5580704)

Open image viewer

Wedding decoration. Photo: YLE Keski-Suomi / Nina Moisio

Fewer people are getting married in Finland than before. About 22,300 marriages were held in Finland last year. There were 1,500 fewer marriages than the year before.

Marriages in which the spouses are of the same sex were held just under 400. The most marriages were held in August.

The average age of women was 32 years and of men 34 years.

## The air in cities has become cleaner

![A summery Esplanadi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10188271)

Open image viewer

Traffic has decreased and the air is cleaner. Photo: AOP

The air quality in cities has improved in Finland.

For example, the amount of nitrogen oxide in the air has fallen by half. Nitrogen oxide comes from car engines. The air quality has improved because traffic has decreased because of the corona situation.

The amount of road dust in cities has also fallen by half compared with usual.
