# Tuesday 24.3.2020 (radio)

The government intends to close restaurants and isolate Uusimaa. The President and Mrs Ahtisaari have the coronavirus. The Finns Party withdrew the interpellation. The Olympics will be postponed by a year. WWF put a camera on the osprey nest. Egenland asks for travel tips.

## More movement restrictions

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin on her way to a meeting of the parliamentary parties about the coronavirus at the Säätytalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11273196)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The government intends to tighten movement restrictions for Finns. The restrictions are meant to stop the spread of the coronavirus.

The Uusimaa region is going to be isolated. Uusimaa will be isolated because it has the most coronavirus infections.

The government also wants to close cafes, restaurants and nightclubs.

Parliament still has to discuss the new restrictions. All parties say that they support the new restrictions.

## Coronavirus infection

![Martti Ahtisaari](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11272533)

President Martti Ahtisaari. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The President, Martti Ahtisaari, has been found to have the coronavirus. The Office of the President of the Republic says this. The infection was confirmed yesterday.

At the weekend it was reported that Mrs Eeva Ahtisaari also has the coronavirus.

In Finland, almost 800 coronavirus infections have now been found. .

## Interpellation

![Ville Tavio ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11272658)

Open image viewer

Chair of the Finns Party parliamentary group Ville Tavio. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The Finns Party is withdrawing the interpellation that it submitted to the government 2 weeks ago. The interpellation is being withdrawn because of the coronavirus situation.

The interpellation was about the government's policy lines on asylum policy and the migrant situation on the Greek and Turkish border. The government was due to answer the interpellation tomorrow.

## The Tokyo Olympics

![People in Tokyo protested against the Olympics on Tuesday. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11273417)

Open image viewer

People in Tokyo protested against the Olympics. Photo: Kazuhiro Nogi / AFP

The Olympics are being postponed because of the coronavirus.

This year's summer Olympics will be postponed by a year. So they will be held in summer 2021.

The Olympics were originally supposed to be held this summer in Japan's capital, Tokyo. They were due to start on 24 July.

## On the osprey's nest

![A pair of ospreys at their nesting place.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10746112)

Open image viewer

WWF's camera followed the osprey nesting last spring too. Photo: WWF Luontolive

The nature conservation organisation WWF has started a live stream from the osprey's nest.

The osprey is a large bird of prey. It lives in lake areas and on the sea coast.

The osprey's nest is still empty, but WWF expects residents there very soon.

If a really fun place or event comes to mind here in Finland, the travel programme Egenland would like to hear about it. Egenland especially wants to hear about places that not everyone may know yet.

You can tell your travel tip on the form, which you can find on the Egenland website at yle.fi/egenland.
