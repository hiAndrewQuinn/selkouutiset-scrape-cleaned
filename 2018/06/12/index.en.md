# Tuesday 12.6.2018 (radio)

The leaders of the USA and North Korea have met in Singapore. Municipal workers are doing worse than before. People should be careful of angry gulls. Suomenlinna will remain a free destination for trips in the future.

## Trump and Kim met

![Donald Trump and Kim Jong-un shake hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10250357)

Kim Jong-un and Donald Trump met in Singapore. Photo: Kevin Lim / EPA

The leaders of the USA and North Korea have met in Singapore. US President Donald Trump and North Korean leader Kim Jong-un talked about nuclear weapons.

Kim promised to work to get nuclear weapons removed from the Korean peninsula. Trump said that the USA will stop military exercises in South Korea.

The political situation on the Korean peninsula has been difficult for a long time. The USA has supported South Korea since the Korean War. The Korean War was in the 1950s.

In recent years the USA and South Korea have been worried that North Korea has developed a nuclear weapon.

Now people around the world hope that the meeting between Trump and Kim will calm the situation on the Korean peninsula.

## Municipal workers are doing badly

![An office worker.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6880052)

Open image viewer

Municipal sector workers are stressed. Photo: Yle

Municipal workers are doing worse than before.

A new study from the Finnish Institute of Occupational Health says that municipal workers are very tired. Many also enjoy their work less than before.

Almost 2,500 workers from more than 30 municipalities took part in the study. The workers were followed from spring 2016 to the end of 2017.

The Finnish Institute of Occupational Health says that the well-being of municipal workers at work has worsened, because of the health and social care reform, for example. It has brought uncertainty into workers' lives.

## Gulls are now angry

![A gull by the river.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7956286)

Open image viewer

A gull may be angry when it guards its chicks. Photo: Sami Tammi / Yle

People should be careful of angry gulls now, the City of Helsinki warns.

In Helsinki there are many gull nests on the roofs of apartment blocks. The gull chicks have now hatched, and the gull parents are watching the chicks closely.

The gull chicks do not stay in the nest, but move around the surrounding area. If people accidentally get too close to the chicks, the gull parents may become agitated and make mock attacks on people.

The city says that more than a thousand pairs of gulls nest on the roofs of apartment blocks in Helsinki.

Gulls are protected during the nesting season. That is why people must not kill gulls.

## Suomenlinna will not have an entry fee

![The tip of Kustaanmiekka at Suomenlinna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10200125)

Open image viewer

Suomenlinna is an interesting destination for trips. Photo: Jaana Sormunen / Yle

Suomenlinna will remain a free destination for trips in the future.

There will be no entry fee, because Suomenlinna is not only a tourist destination. It is one district of Helsinki.

Suomenlinna is a popular destination for trips. Many foreign tourists also visit Suomenlinna.

About a million visitors come to Suomenlinna every year.
