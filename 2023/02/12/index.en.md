## Ukrainian children taken to Russia

![Ukrainian rescuers work in a residential building in the rubble.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106609963d895e555ba2/1675138816)

Open image viewer

Russia's attack on Ukraine has continued for almost a year. Photo: Sergey Kozlov / EPA

Since the start of the war, Russia has taken thousands of Ukrainian children to Russia. Some children have, for example, been adopted in Russia.

This came out when Yle investigated the fate of the children. Ukraine says that at least 16,000 children have been forcibly moved to Russia.

Moving and adopting children from occupied areas is a war crime. Ukraine's chief prosecutor has started to investigate child abductions.

## Earthquake in Turkey

![Rescue workers search for survivors in the middle of the rubble of a building in the city of Jindayris.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_533/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107142463e6359b5fe7e/1676087743)

Open image viewer

Rescue workers are searching for people in the rubble. Photo: Rami Al Sayed / AFP

More than 34,000 people have died in the earthquakes in Turkey and Syria. The number may still rise, as more information about the victims is obtained.

In Turkey almost 30,000 have died. In Syria more than 4,500 have died.

At least 4 people have been found alive in the rubble of buildings in Turkey. The earthquake happened last Monday.

## Parties' investment assets

![The word "Börs" is written on the wall of the stock exchange building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_406/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7521475fd0781119489/1607498127)

Open image viewer

Some parties have investment assets, for example in stock exchange shares. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Yle has investigated the parties' investment assets. Many parties have investment assets, that is, for example, shares and fund units. The largest investment portfolio belongs to the SDP. The value of the SDP's investment portfolio is almost 7 million euros.

The second largest investment portfolio belongs to the Finns Party. The value of the Finns Party's investment portfolio is 4 million euros.

The third largest investment portfolio belongs to the Centre Party. The value of the Centre Party's investment portfolio is about 700,000 euros.

The value of the RKP's investment portfolio is about 250,000 euros. The National Coalition Party has no investment portfolio.

## Eating vegetables and fruit

![K-Citymarket Ideapark sells Finnish cucumbers and tomatoes in the vegetable shelves on 18 January 2023.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1152,h_648,x_0,y_83/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106052363c7a264ae570/1674027690)

Open image viewer

Sales of cucumbers, tomatoes and lettuce have decreased the most. Photo: Hanne Leiwo / Yle

Finns are buying fewer vegetables and fruit than before. The reason is rising prices.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, is worried about the situation. THL's research manager Satu Männistö says that already a few years ago only 15-20 percent of Finns ate enough vegetables and fruit.

The large shop chains Kesko and S-Group have said that the sales of vegetables and fruit have decreased, now that prices have risen. The sales of cucumbers, tomatoes and lettuce have decreased the most.

## Sign language day

![A woman who uses sign language signs with her hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5616,h_3159,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5708645d039f97c104b/1560518609)

Open image viewer

There are two sign languages in Finland. Photo: Valeria Borovskaya/Yle

Today, Sunday, sign language day has been celebrated. There are 2 sign languages in Finland. They are the Finnish sign language and the Finland-Swedish sign language.

The Finland-Swedish sign language is an endangered language, because it has few users.

Fewer than a hundred people use the Finland-Swedish sign language.
