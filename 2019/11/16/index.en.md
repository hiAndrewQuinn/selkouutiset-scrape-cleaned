# Saturday 16.11.2019 (radio)

Finns have celebrated success in football. Many trade unions have announced that they intend to support the postal sector strike. Li Andersson continues as chair of the Left Alliance. Ethiopia's coffee forests may save coffee plantations.

## Celebrating football

Finns have celebrated success in football.

Finland will qualify for the final tournament of the men's European Football Championship for the first time. The European Championship will be held next summer.

Finland's place in the European Championship was certain after Finland beat Liechtenstein. The important match was in Helsinki on Friday evening.

The match ended 3-0. So Finland scored 3 goals. Finland's first goal was scored by Jasse Tuominen. Teemu Pukki scored 2 goals in the match.

Many Finns watched the exciting match together. For example, 5000 people were at Helsinki's Kansalaistori square. Almost as many people were at the Keskustori square in Tampere.

After the win, the celebrations began. In Helsinki, people celebrated at Kauppatori.

## Support for the strike

![umbrella with the word "strike" in big letters and other strikers](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11069572)

Open image viewer

The postal strike began on Monday 11 November. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Postal workers get support from workers in other sectors. Postal workers are on strike because they have a dispute with the employer, for example about pay.

Many trade unions of the employees have announced that they intend to support the postal sector strike. The trade unions intend, for example, to stop freight traffic in ports and on railways.

Support measures will start if the postal sector strike continues. On Sunday, negotiations will continue to try to end the postal strike.

## Chair of the Left Alliance

![Li Andersson in Kuopio on 16.11.209.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11072656)

Li Andersson continues as chair of the Left Alliance.

The Left Alliance's party congress elected the chair on Saturday in Kuopio. Andersson was the only candidate.

Andersson has been chair of the Left Alliance since 2016. She is 32 years old and comes from Turku.

The Left Alliance is now in the government. Li Andersson is the Minister of Education in the government.

## Ethiopia's coffee forests

![A landscape in Ethiopia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11071201)

Open image viewer

Coffee forest in Ethiopia. Photo: Liselott Lindström / Yle

Many dangers threaten the world's coffee plantations. Climate change and plant diseases may destroy coffee bushes.

On coffee plantations, the hereditary factors of the bushes, that is, their genes, are very similar. That is why plant diseases can spread easily.

Researchers say that help may be found in Ethiopia, in Africa. In Ethiopia there are wild coffee forests.

The trees in a coffee forest have many different genes. That is why plant diseases cannot kill all the trees at the same time.

Coffee forests may hold a rescue if plant diseases start to destroy coffee plantations.
