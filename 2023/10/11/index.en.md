## Finns out of Israel

![Elina Valtonen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_138/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-118467865266db06a36b/1697017315)

Foreign Minister Elina Valtonen. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Finnish authorities have decided to help Finns who are in the crisis area in Israel.

The authorities are arranging a flight that will take Finns out of the crisis area. The flight will be arranged in the coming days.

The flight is voluntary for passengers. The passengers pay for the flight themselves.

There are still more than 170 Finns in the crisis area who need help arranging their return journey. This is what Finland's Foreign Minister Elina Valtonen says.

Valtonen estimates that the crisis in Israel may still get worse. That is why it is important to help Finns leave Israel.

The Palestinian militant group Hamas attacked Israel on Saturday. Israel has carried out counterattacks on Hamas's area, Gaza. More than 2200 people have already died in the crisis.

## Aid package for Ukraine

![Antti Häkkänen at the Kouvola security conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6038,h_3396,x_0,y_89/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-117322765081378c6129/1695028255)

Finland is sending more defence equipment to Ukraine. Now help is being sent worth 95 million euros.

After the new aid package, Finland has sent defence equipment to Ukraine 19 times.

Defence Minister Antti Häkkänen says that the aid packages are worth 1.4 billion euros in total.

Russia started the war in Ukraine last year, in February.

## The Afghanistan earthquakes

![People gathered in the shelter of a rock formation in Afghanistan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_70/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11829096522357df1dad/1696740873)

Open image viewer

Women and children are suffering in particular from the earthquakes in Afghanistan. Photo: EPA

Many women and children have died in the earthquakes in Afghanistan.

The UN children's agency Unicef says that more than 1000 people have died in the earthquakes in total. 90 percent of them are women and children.

There were earthquakes in Afghanistan at the weekend and again on Wednesday morning. The strongest earthquakes have struck near the city of Herat.

## Finnish Teacher of the Year

![Satu Lahtonen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3791,h_2132,x_0,y_1298/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-118471865267a73e753f/1697020666)

Open image viewer

Teacher Satu Lahtonen. Photo: Satu Rakkolainen-Sossa

The Finnish Teacher of the Year 2023 is Satu Lahtonen. The Association of Finnish Teachers and the Finnish National Agency for Education announced the choice on Tuesday in Helsinki.

Satu Lahtonen was chosen as Finnish Teacher of the Year because she has promoted the teaching of the Finnish language. Lahtonen has, for example, made materials for teaching Finnish.

Lahtonen's materials are well suited to students who are starting to study Finnish. They are also suitable for students who are learning to read and write.

Many people know Satu Lahtonen from her YouTube videos. In the videos Lahtonen teaches the basics of the Finnish language.
