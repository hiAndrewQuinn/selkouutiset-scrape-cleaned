# Sunday 8.7.2018 (radio)

Rescue work has begun in the caves in northern Thailand - the first boys are already safe. The employment rate of people of retirement age has risen steadily in the 2000s. There are more white-tailed deer in Finland than ever before. The Navy's 100th anniversary.

## The Thai cave rescue has  begun

![An ambulance leaving the mouth of the cave.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10294522)

According to information on Sunday evening, 4 boys are already safe. They were taken by ambulance to hospital. Photo: Lillian Suwanrumpha / AFP

In northern Thailand, the first boys have been brought safely out of the cave. 12 boys and a football coach have been trapped in the cave for over 2 weeks. By Sunday evening, 4 of the boys had been brought to safety. 

 The operation was in a hurry, because heavy rain is coming. The rescue work may still take time, but so far it has gone faster than expected. The operation was stopped in the evening for about 10 hours, because the oxygen tanks along the diving route must be filled again. 

The water level in the caves has been lowered. Part of the way can be walked. The rest of the way must be dived. 

In total, 90 divers are taking part in the operation. 50 of them are foreigners and 40 are Thai.  

## Many people of retirement age work

![An employment contract and a pen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9762821)

Open image viewer

More and more people work, at least for a while, even though they are already retired. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Most Finns leave working life as soon as the law allows it. Most leave work at 63. 

But the retirement age has risen and is still rising because of new laws. For many, the retirement age has risen to 65, and for example the unemployment pension was abolished. 

On the other hand, more people than before want to keep working, at least part-time. The Finnish Centre for Pensions says that the employment rate of people of retirement age has risen steadily in the 2000s. Continuing to work is most common in expert jobs. Women often work in care, and men in transport.   

## There are a lot of white-tailed deer

![A white-tailed deer fawn looks straight at the camera](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10292800)

Open image viewer

The first white-tailed deer were brought to Laukko Manor in Vesilahti in 1934. Today hundreds of deer live on the manor's land. The manor's owner Jouni Minkkinen lifted this fawn out of the way of a lawnmower. Photo: Jouni Minkkinen

There are more white-tailed deer in Finland than ever before. There are almost 100,000 of them. They may all be descendants of one and the same male.

The first white-tailed deer were brought to Laukko Manor in Vesilahti in 1934 and more in 1948. The deer also got the name "Laukko deer" from the name of the manor. The deer were brought to the manor from the state of Minnesota in the north of the USA. 

The deer were brought to Finland because there were no longer any roe deer or forest reindeer in Finland. So Finnish Americans wanted to give their old home country a new game animal. 

Only 5 deer survived the difficult ship journey. 1 of them was a male.

## The Navy turns 100

![Missile boat Tornio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10048747)

Open image viewer

Missile boat Tornio Photo: Lehtikuva

The Navy is celebrating its 100th anniversary in Turku on Sunday and Monday. On the bank of the Aura river you can see all the Navy's classes of ships. There will be a parade on Monday.

Boat traffic on the Aura river has been restricted because of the celebrations.
