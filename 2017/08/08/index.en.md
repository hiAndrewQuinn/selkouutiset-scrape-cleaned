# Tuesday 8.8.2017 (radio)

Fewer Estonians are working in Finland. Many health centre doctors work part-time. Koskenkorva will soon be sold in the United States. The rescue services have had a record number of snake alerts. Greetings from the Finnish school in Kiel.

## Estonian workers

![Estonian painter Jan Kaljura at a building site in Järvenpää.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9763331)

The number of Estonian workers in Finland will fall in the coming years, says a researcher.

Keiu Telve, a researcher at the University of Eastern Finland and the University of Tartu, says that the level of wages in Estonia has risen. Because of this, the number of Estonian workers in Finland may even halve.

The researcher says that at the moment perhaps about 20,000 Estonians work in Finland. Estonians in Finland work especially in construction, but also as doctors, in service jobs and as cleaners.

Most Estonians work in the Helsinki metropolitan area.

## Health centre doctors

![A doctor writes a prescription.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6193071)

Open image viewer

Part-time work interests especially young doctors with small children. Photo: Tiina Karjalainen / Yle

Many health centre doctors work part-time. A survey by the Finnish Medical Association says that 20 per cent of health centre doctors work part-time.

It is especially young doctors with small children who want to work part-time.

Doctors who, for example, do research alongside their ordinary work also want part-time work.

## Koskenkorva

![Bottles of Koskenkorva at Alko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9763523)

Open image viewer

Koskenkorva is going to be sold in 10 states in the United States.  Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The traditional Finnish spirit Koskenkorva will start being sold in the United States. Koskenkorva can be bought in the United States from the turn of August and September, says the manufacturer Altia.

Koskenkorva vodka is going to be sold in 10 US states. Sales will start first in California.

Koskenkorva is sold in about 30 countries. Altia says that Koskenkorva is its most important export brand.

## Snakes

![A grass snake photographed among rocks. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9763179)

Open image viewer

The grass snake is not venomous. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The rescue services have had a record number of alerts because of snakes this summer.

By the beginning of August, the rescue services had been called out because of snakes about 35 times in total. That is as many as usually in a whole year.

Snakes have been removed, for example, from homes, balconies and terraces.

Most have been grass snakes. The grass snake is not venomous.

There have been the most snake call-outs in western Uusimaa, the Turku region and Helsinki.

## Greetings from Kiel

![Pupils sing at school in Kiel, Germany. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9763203)

Open image viewer

Pupils of the Finnish school in Kiel sing a song of congratulations.  Photo: Yle

A large number of Finnish children and young people live in different parts of the world and study the Finnish language and Finnish culture at Finnish schools abroad.

Now that independent Finland is turning 100, the Finnish schools are sending greetings to Finland on video.

The latest greetings come from the Finnish school in Kiel, Germany.
