# Tuesday 4.8.2020 (radio)

Schools start with classes in person. 2 new corona deaths have been reported in Finland. The number of comprehensive schools is decreasing in Finland. Almost 2 million Finns received tax refunds on Tuesday. The weather is clearing up and warming.

TV Clear News has a summer break and it will continue on 17.8.
Radio Clear News is made every day, also in the summer.

## In-person teaching in schools

![In many schools, in-person teaching will return next week. In the picture, pupils of Otalampi school in a history and social studies lesson. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11477982)

Pupils return to school next week. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

Schools start next week. Teaching starts as in-person teaching, that is, the pupils go to school.

However, the schools are also preparing for distance teaching. They can quickly switch to distance teaching if the corona situation gets worse.

In the spring, school pupils received distance teaching because of the corona. The National Agency for Education says that distance teaching worked quite well.

The National Agency for Education also says that school leaving ceremonies and upper secondary graduation parties will be held at the end of August. The celebrations could not be held at the usual time in the spring because of the corona.

2 new corona deaths have been reported in Finland. In total, 331 people have died of corona in Finland.

On Tuesday 17 new corona infections were also reported. There are about 7,500 corona infections in Finland in total.

## Comprehensive schools are decreasing

![Pohjois-Ritaharju school in Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11240895)

Open image viewer

The number of comprehensive schools is decreasing, but their size is growing. Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

The number of comprehensive schools is decreasing in Finland. The National Agency for Education estimates that in 20 years' time there will be more than 30 per cent fewer comprehensive schools than now.

The number of comprehensive schools is decreasing because the number of children is decreasing. In 20 years' time there will be more than 100,000 fewer children of comprehensive school age than now.

In addition, the size of the schools is growing. Small schools are being closed, and schools with many pupils are what remain. This is also an attempt to save money.

## Tax refunds were paid

![Tax return](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10965642)

Open image viewer

Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The Tax Administration paid tax refunds on Tuesday. Almost 2 million Finns received tax refunds. The Tax Administration paid the money directly into people's bank accounts.

The Tax Administration gives money back to people who have paid too much tax. Some people will get their tax refunds later in the autumn.

If a taxpayer has been told about a tax refund but the money does not arrive in their account, it is good to check the matter. This can be done, for example, on the website Omavero.fi.

There are many reasons why tax refunds do not arrive. The reason may be, for example, that the Tax Administration does not have the taxpayer's correct account number.

## The rain stops

![a child throws water on another child on the beach](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11417773)

Open image viewer

Summer comes back at the weekend! Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

The weather is clearing up, that is, the rain stops.

On Wednesday it will only rain in Northern Finland. Elsewhere in Finland it is already sunny.

The weather also gradually warms up. At the weekend it may already be a heatwave, that is, the temperature is at least 25 degrees.
