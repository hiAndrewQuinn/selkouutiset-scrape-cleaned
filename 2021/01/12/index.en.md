# Tuesday 12.1.2021 (radio)

A lot of snow fell. Herd immunity may not happen this year. One billion euros of corona support was distributed. You must identify yourself on slot machines. The weather gets colder.

A lot of snow fell in Finland on Tuesday. A great deal of snow has fallen in Southern Finland.

Driving conditions have been really bad on many roads. Cars have crashed and cars have slid off the road. Trains and buses have been late.

The wind has also been strong. The heavy snowfall has also caused power cuts. More power cuts may come as more snow falls.

## Coronavirus

![A person being vaccinated](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_99/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7593465ff46547346bd/1609852337)

The World Health Organization, WHO, says that herd immunity against the coronavirus will not be reached yet this year.

During the year there will not be time to make so much vaccine and vaccinate so many people that the spread of the coronavirus stops. That is why corona restrictions must still be followed this year and we must be careful.

The WHO is also worried about new forms of the virus that spread quickly.

## Corona support

![An empty restaurant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_574/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7109205f412be61e71c/1598107451)

Restaurants, hotels and tourist service companies have suffered a lot from the corona situation. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Business Finland, which helps companies, gave about one billion euros in support last year to companies whose business is harmed by the corona situation. In the last year more than 20,000 projects received corona support.

Most corona support was given to the trade sector, industry, and tourism companies and restaurants.

Many companies are using corona support to develop digital solutions and services.

## Slot machines

![A man putting a Veikkaus card into a slot machine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10606902)

Open image viewer

You identify yourself at a slot machine with a Veikkaus card. Photo: Veikkaus

Playing on slot machines changed today, on Tuesday. Now you must identify yourself at slot machines.

Identification applies to Veikkaus slot machines in restaurants, shops and petrol stations. For example, minors cannot play slot machines.

Identification reduces problem gambling. A person can, for example, ask to be banned from playing.

## The weather

On Wednesday it will still snow in the south. There is a lot of frost in a large part of Finland.

Snowfall continues on Wednesday in Southern and Western Finland. Elsewhere the snowfalls will weaken during the day. In the north the weather is dry and clear. In Eastern Finland too the clouds will decrease.

In the south-west and in the south it is frost of 5 to 15 degrees. Elsewhere there is a lot of frost. For example, in Eastern Finland there is frost of about 25 degrees and in Lapland about 30 degrees.
