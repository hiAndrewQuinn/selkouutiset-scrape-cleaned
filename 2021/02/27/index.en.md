# Saturday 27.2.2021 (radio)

Rules for a ban on going outside are being prepared at the Government Office. Ministers are standing in the municipal elections. MP Ano Turtiainen has been expelled from the Finns Party. Rally legend Hannu Mikkola has died. The thickness of the sea ice varies a lot.

## A ban on going outside is being prepared

![Anna-Maja Henriksson visited Yle's Ykkösaamu programme on Saturday, 27 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_203/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-779229603a04782b3ce/1621068407)

Justice Minister Anna-Maja Henriksson confirms that rules for a ban on going outside are being prepared at the Government Office.

Henriksson said on Yle's Ykkösaamu programme that the coronavirus situation is difficult and that the worst must be prepared for. The Ministry of Justice is not yet part of the preparation.

A ban on going outside could be, for example, a movement restriction in some area. In Europe, for example, there have been very broad bans, where people could only go to the shop.

Henriksson says that she does not wish for such bans in Finland.

## Ministers want to sit on the municipal council

![Counting advance votes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11812744)

Open image viewer

Counting advance votes in the 2017 municipal elections. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

All government ministers except one are standing in the April municipal elections. The only exception is the Green Party's Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto.

After more than 10 years, the prime minister is also a candidate. Sanna Marin is the SDP's candidate in Tampere.

Ilkka Ruostetsaari, professor of political science at the University of Tampere, says that there are many reasons for ministers to stand. A minister can keep in touch with voters on the municipal council. At the same time, a high-profile and famous candidate collects votes for the party.

In addition, the municipality has a connection to the top decision-makers of the country through the minister member of the council.

Ruostetsaari also says that the minister's job is temporary and Finland's electoral system is person-centred.

## Ano Turtiainen was expelled from the Finns Party

![MP Ano Turtiainen in Parliament on 24.2.2021](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-77821660367671548d1/1614182019)

Open image viewer

Ano Turtiainen attended the plenary session of Parliament on 24 February. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

MP Ano Turtiainen has been expelled from the Finns Party. Party secretary Simo Grönroos said on Twitter that the reason for the expulsion is activity that has harmed the party.

Turtiainen had already been expelled earlier from the Finns Party's parliamentary group.

Turtiainen was elected as an MP from the Finns Party's lists in the 2019 parliamentary elections.

## Rally legend Hannu Mikkola has died

![Rally world champion Hannu Mikkola in 2002.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2408,h_1355,x_0,y_90/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7791986039d4005459a/1614402595)

Hannu Mikkola's active career ended at the 1993 Suur-Ajot in Jyväskylä, at the age of 51.

Rally legend Hannu Mikkola, a Finnish motorsport figure, has died. Mikkola was 78 years old, and he died of cancer.

Mikkola was the world champion in 1983.

Mikkola was a professional rally driver in 4 different decades. For example, he won the Jyväskylä Suur-Ajot 7 times and the Finnish championship 2 times.

## Sea ice is already weak in the south

![Footprints on the ice.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7860146)

Open image viewer

Going onto the ice is starting to become dangerous in the southern parts of the country. Photo: Kalevi Pitkäkangas / Yle

The thickness of the sea ice varies a lot.

There are dangerous patches of open water on the shore ice of the Archipelago Sea, the western Gulf of Finland and the capital region.

The Western Finland Coast Guard advises people to stay off the ice in Southern Finland. The sunny weather at the weekend will weaken the ice further.
