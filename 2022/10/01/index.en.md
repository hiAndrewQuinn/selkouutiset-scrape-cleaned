# Saturday 1.10.2022 (radio)

President Niinistö's comments on the war. Demonstrations for Iranian women. Fire at Helsinki's Sokos department store. Northern lights. First snow.

## The president's comments on the war

![Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3717,h_2090,x_0,y_30/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-101530863372277b0c28/1664557737)

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö does not believe that a war will break out between Russia and the Western countries. Niinistö commented to Yle on Russia's war in Ukraine.

Russian President Vladimir Putin said on Friday that Russia is fighting a war against the whole West. Putin especially means NATO, the defence alliance of the West.

Niinistö estimates that Putin's speech is meant for Russians. Putin wants to justify to Russians why Russia is calling men into the army.

Niinistö estimates that Finland is not under any threat right now. It is good for Finland to follow closely what is happening in the world. However, there is no reason to be afraid, President Niinistö says.

## Demonstrations for Iranian women

![Demonstration against the actions of the Iranian regime in Oulu on 1.10.2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1278,h_720,x_0,y_150/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1015428633829c878676/1664625296)

Open image viewer

Demonstration in Oulu on Saturday. Photo: Yle

Demonstrations in support of Iranian women have continued in many countries. In Finland too there was a demonstration on Saturday, when demonstrators gathered in Oulu.

The demonstrators want to support Iranian women. The demonstrations began when a young Kurdish woman died in the hands of Iran's morality police. The morality police thought that the woman's hijab was not on her head correctly.

After the incident there have been demonstrations around the world as well as in Iran.

## Fire at Helsinki's Sokos department store

![Fire engines ready to leave in the fire station hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_863,y_849/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89685561cb08ba40e05/1640696194)

Open image viewer

The fire was extinguished quickly. Photo: Ari Haimakainen / Yle

The Sokos department store in central Helsinki was closed on Saturday.

There was a fire in the department store on Friday evening. The fire was put out quickly.

No one was injured in the fire. However, smoke and extinguishing water caused damage. That is why the department store remained closed on Saturday.

There is also an S-market in the same building. It has been open as normal.

## Northern lights

![Northern lights shine green over Saimaa in January 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_508/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90229061e2761f013b9/1697191039)

Open image viewer

The reason for the northern lights is the solar wind. Photo: Kalle Purhonen / Yle

Beautiful northern lights may be visible in the sky at the weekend. Northern lights are created when the solar wind hits the Earth's atmosphere.

This weekend the solar wind is strong. That is why more northern lights are visible than usual.

Northern lights are usually seen in the north, but this weekend there are northern lights in the sky of Southern Finland too.

The best time to watch the northern lights is the night between Saturday and Sunday, the Finnish Meteorological Institute says.

## The weather

![Weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12646637)

On Friday the autumn's first snow fell. In Puolanka in Northern Finland 2 centimetres of snow fell.

At the weekend a little rain may fall. Rain showers will come in the north.

In the south the weather warms a little and the sun may show itself.

The temperature on Sunday is between 4 and 14 degrees.
