## Balcony safety

Then there is the topic of balcony safety.

A major balcony inspection project has begun in Finland. It could take several years.

The Ministry of the Environment wants municipalities to inspect the safety of balconies throughout Finland. This inspection is being carried out in Satakunta.

The reason for the inspections is accidents that have occurred on balconies.

It could take several years to inspect all balconies.

A balcony can be dangerous if, for example, the support bracket for the panel or glass is worn out. The protective panels on old balconies can also break easily.

## Apple harvest

Finally, the topic is the apple harvest.

Many home gardens are ripening a lot of apples this summer.

For example, in the Kupittaa allotment garden in Turku, the trees are sagging under the weight of apples.

There are so many apples in some places that there is not enough time to collect them all and they spoil. Many people have to take their apples to the bio-waste.

The apple harvest in home gardens is bountiful in many places this year.

The harvest is good because the apple trees bloomed well in the spring and there has been a lot of rain.
