# Sunday 17.1.2021 (radio)

Municipal elections. Pave Maijanen. Trust in news. The weather gets colder.

## Voter turnout in the municipal elections

![A voting slip being stamped](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9557763)

Open image viewer

An election official stamps a voting slip. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

The parties believe that the corona crisis may increase Finns' interest in voting in the municipal elections. The party secretaries say that the exceptional conditions have increased people's interest in public decision-making.

Municipal elections will be held in Finland in April. In the municipal elections, the decision-makers are chosen for the municipal councils.

In the previous municipal elections 4 years ago the voter turnout was about 59 percent. For example, the party secretary of the Centre Party, Riikka Pirkkalainen, believes that voter turnout may now rise. People have been at home a lot because of the coronavirus, and municipal elections are exactly about matters of home and everyday life, Pirkkalainen says.

The opposition parties, the National Coalition and the Finns Party, on the other hand, believe that voter turnout will rise, because people are worried about the economy of the state and the municipalities.

## Pave Maijanen has died

![Pave Maijanen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8465524)

Open image viewer

Pekka Juhani Maijanen, also known as Pave Maijanen. Photo: Minna Matintupa / Yle

Yle's Radio Finland channel has today remembered the musician Pave Maijanen. Maijanen died yesterday, on Saturday, after a serious illness. He was 70 years old.

Pekka Juhani Maijanen, also known as Pave Maijanen, was a musician, singer and producer. He had a long career, first in many rock bands and then as a solo singer. Some of Pave Maijanen's best-known songs were, for example, "Take Care of It" and "Would You Leave".

In 1992 Pave Maijanen was Finland's entry in the Eurovision Song Contest. In 2012 he received the Iskelmä Finlandia Prize.

Maijanen's music and interviews can be watched and listened to on Yle Areena. He will be remembered on TV2 next Friday.

## Finns' trust in news

![The Pasila link tower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8775530)

Open image viewer

Yle's link tower in West Pasila in Helsinki. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Finns trust the news of Yle and the Finnish News Agency STT.

In a new survey 80 percent of people say that they trust Yle's television and radio news and the news of STT.

70 percent trust the news of MTV. About 25 percent trust the news of the evening tabloids.

Tomorrow it will be cloudy and in places a little snow may fall. The frost is between two and fifteen degrees.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that from Wednesday the frost may get sharper again.

More snow may also come. On Wednesday and Thursday in the southern and central parts of the country, snow may drift as much as 20 centimetres.
