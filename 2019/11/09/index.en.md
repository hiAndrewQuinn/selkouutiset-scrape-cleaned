# Saturday 9.11.2019 (radio)

Big EU countries want the Union to have a strong authority to stop money laundering. It is exactly 30 years since the fall of the Berlin Wall. ## A new authority to stop money laundering is wanted for the EU

![Malta](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11060614)

Open image viewer

Malta's and the EU's money. Photo: Lino Arrigo Azzopardi / EPA

The big member countries of the European Union want to set up a new, strong authority to stop money laundering.

Money laundering means making money earned through crime look legal. Many European banks have been caught because criminals' money was laundered through them.

For example, Germany, France and Italy say that the supervision of money laundering must be centralised in an authority shared by the whole EU. The countries say that the member countries' own authorities are not always strict enough with banks that move very large sums of money.

## Celebrations in Germany

![The Berlin Wall fell in 1989](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11032204)

Open image viewer

People climbed onto the wall exactly 30 years ago. Photo: AOP

In Germany, people are celebrating that the Berlin Wall was torn down 30 years ago.

The wall divided the city of Berlin from 1961 to 1989. The wall separated communist East Germany from capitalist West Germany.

The fall of the wall is a big thing also because the wall fell without violence. Not a single shot was fired.

## Industrial Union breaks off negotiations

![Ski wax tins on a production line.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10480972)

Open image viewer

Ski wax is made in the factory. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

The Industrial Union is ending the collective agreement negotiations with the employers in the chemical industry.

The Industrial Union, which represents the workers, does not accept that the employer wants to stop collecting union membership fees from people's pay. Today, companies take the union membership fee from the employee's pay and pass it on to the Industrial Union.

The Industrial Union says that it will not go back to the negotiating table until the employer withdraws the proposal.

## Poll: Jussi Halla-aho the best party leader

![Jussi Hall-aho in Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11052488)

Jussi Halla-aho. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Almost a quarter of Finns think that the Finns Party's Jussi Halla-aho has succeeded best in the job of party leader.

In the Uutissuomalainen poll, the second place went to the Left Alliance's Li Andersson, who was seen as the best party leader by 16 percent of respondents. The National Coalition's Petteri Orpo was seen as the best by 8 and the SDP's Antti Rinne by 6 percent of respondents.

## Many employers, not a problem

![Collecting food at the Citymarket in Easton](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4951,h_2785,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5832205d5276930b75a/1565685428)

Many temporary workers work for several companies at the same time. Of the employees of temporary work agencies, 20 percent have two or more employers.

Temporary work agencies say that working for many companies is not a problem. Some employees think it is more flexible when there are many employers. There may be more job offers, and it is easy to accept the ones that suit them best.

Young people and students especially value choice and flexibility, says the managing director of the temporary work agency, Matti Kariola.
