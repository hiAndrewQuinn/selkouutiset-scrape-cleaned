# Tuesday 23.8.2022 (radio)

The President on Russia. The National Coalition Party on NATO bases. Employment. Rosatom and Fennovoima. Arctic foxes. The weather.

## The President at the ambassadors' days

![President Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4896,h_2753,x_0,y_207/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-998031630492ae685f6/1661244183)

Niinistö said that there are no prospects of building blocks for a fresh start with Russia. Photo: Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö says that the extent and duration of the effects of Russia's war of aggression are hard to exaggerate. Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

Niinistö spoke at the ambassadors' days in Helsinki.

In Niinistö's view Russia has made it clear that security is the most important goal of Finland's foreign policy.

Niinistö said that the defence alliance NATO brings security that Finland otherwise does not have. Finland has submitted an application to NATO.

Niinistö said that not much is left of Finland's earlier relationship with Russia.

## The National Coalition Party on NATO

![Kai Mykkänen gives a speech.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4619,h_2598,x_0,y_310/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99799563047fbcdc79a/1661239388)

The National Coalition Party wants to deepen Finland's NATO alliance. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

The opposition party, the National Coalition Party, wants permanent NATO troops and NATO bases in Finland.

The National Coalition Party also supports conscription for women. In the National Coalition Party's view this way the best talents are obtained for the right tasks.

The chair of the National Coalition Party's parliamentary group, Kai Mykkänen, hoped in Lappeenranta that all parties could decide on NATO matters together.

## Employment

![A construction worker walks on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-792146606da6f6a43d5/1617802170)

Employment has improved. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Employment development remained strong last month.

Statistics Finland says that the employment rate was 73.7 percent in July. There were 50,000 more employed people at work than a year ago.

The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment says that at the end of July there were about 270,000 unemployed job seekers. That is 50,000 fewer unemployed than a year earlier.

## Rosatom and Hanhikivi

![Artist's impression of Fennovoima's Hanhikivi 1 nuclear power plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3990,h_2244,x_8,y_269/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-916744620e64b86bb68/1645110556)

Open image viewer

Fennovoima's Hanhikivi nuclear power plant plan in an artist's impression. Photo: Fennovoima Oy

The Russian state nuclear power company Rosatom is seeking billion-euro compensation from Finland. Rosatom is seeking compensation of about 3 billion euros from the energy company Fennovoima.

Fennovoima's nuclear power project was cancelled in the spring, because Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

Fennovoima is seeking about 2 billion euros in compensation from Rosatom in court. The reasons are, among other things, delays in the power plant project.

The Hanhikivi 1 nuclear power plant was meant to be built in Pyhäjoki in North Ostrobothnia. At the moment the site has ready foundations, service spaces and living spaces for over 1000 people.

## Arctic fox breeding

![Arctic fox in the snow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4955,h_2780,x_0,y_411/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-949165626fd01cc9421/1651495019)

Open image viewer

Arctic fox in the snow. Photo: FLPA/Shutterstock/All Over Press

The highly endangered arctic fox has bred in Finland for the first time in over 25 years.

The arctic foxes were spotted in Lapland in den checks in the fell area of Enontekiö. Now an arctic fox pair and 3 cubs were seen.

There may be only 10–20 arctic foxes in Finland.
