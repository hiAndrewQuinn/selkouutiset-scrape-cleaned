## Air passengers were tested

![Two passengers walk with their luggage at Helsinki-Vantaa airport on 7 August 2020. They are wearing face masks in front of their faces.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1384,h_779,x_203,y_294/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7064895f2e70454b317/1607756526)

Passengers at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Photo: Arttu Timonen / Yle

A wide coronavirus test was done on the passengers of an aircraft at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Passengers who came from Bucharest on Friday evening were asked to take a corona test.

The test was voluntary, but everyone on the aircraft was recommended to take it. About 60 passengers went for a corona test. About 40 passengers did not go to be tested.

Finland may do more tests on passengers who arrive in Finland. The tests may also be compulsory, so that a passenger can get into Finland.

## Serious boating accident in Hanko

![Aerial view of Hanko.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3876,h_2180,x_0,y_484/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7032285f210c3250f33/1596001888)

Open image viewer

Hanko is a coastal town in Western Uusimaa. In Hanko there are often boating events in summer. Photo: Linus Westerlund / Yle

There has been a boating accident in Hanko. One person died and one person was injured when two boats crashed in front of Hanko on Saturday.

One of the boats was a racing boat that took part in a racing boat event in Hanko. The other boat was an ordinary pleasure boat. The people, one of whom died and one of whom was injured, were in the pleasure boat.

The police are investigating why the two boats crashed in Hanko.

## Parties are looking for candidates for the municipal elections

![A voting slip is put into a ballot box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10996379)

Open image viewer

A voting slip is dropped into a ballot box. The municipal elections will be held in April. Photo: Tuomo Rintamaa / Yle

The parties in Parliament have started to prepare for the municipal elections. There are municipal elections in Finland next year, in April.

The general secretaries of the parties say that because of the corona situation it has been hard for parties to find candidates for the municipal elections.

In the municipal elections, the number of candidates affects how well a party does. That is why the parties want to find many good candidates for the municipal elections.

## Forest sales picked up

![Forest to be cut down in the Ämmänvaara outdoor and hiking area in Kemijärvi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11443577)

Open image viewer

Forest sales have picked up, especially in Lapland. Photo: Tapani Leisti / Yle

Forest sales have picked up this year. Forest sales have been made 9 per cent more across the whole of Finland than a year ago, in January to June.

In Lapland, 50 per cent more forest sales have been made than last year. Forest prices have also risen more in Lapland than in the rest of Finland. In Southern Finland, forest property prices have even fallen in places.

In January to June, deals were made for more than 2,000 forest properties in Finland.
