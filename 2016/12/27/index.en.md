# Tuesday 27.12.2016 (radio)

Changes are coming to the lives of the unemployed next year. An air crash on Christmas Day is being investigated in Russia. Many shops were open every day at Christmas. Attempts are being made to shorten long sick leaves. Cheetahs may die out.

## Changes to unemployment benefits

![Joensuu TE Office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9268345)

Open image viewer

The unemployed are interviewed every 3 months. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Changes are coming to the lives of the unemployed next year. For example, unemployment benefits will get smaller. It is also harder for an unemployed person to refuse a job than before.

The changes are being made because the unemployed are to be got back to work faster.

If unemployment continues, the unemployed person is interviewed every 3 months. In the interview, the plan for finding work is checked.

## Russian air crash

![Soloist Vladislav Golikov at a concert of the Red Army Choir.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9372960)

Open image viewer

Many members of the Red Army Choir died in the air crash. Photo: Juri Kochetkov / EPA

An air crash on Christmas Day is being investigated in Russia.

For now, the authorities have banned flights on planes of the same model as the crashed aircraft.

A Russian passenger plane fell into the Black Sea on Christmas Day. There were 92 people on board. Everyone died.

Most of the aircraft's passengers were members of the Russian army choir, that is, the Red Army Choir.

The plane fell while it was on its way from Russia to Syria. The Red Army Choir was due to perform in Syria for Russian soldiers.

## Shops open at Christmas

![A woman at a supermarket milk shelf.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9207310)

Open image viewer

This year you could get into the shop on Christmas Day too. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Many shops were open every day at Christmas.

Shops can now decide for themselves when they are open. This has been the case since the beginning of this year. This was the first Christmas when shops tried out their opening hours.

The shopkeeper Pasi Hentilä says that there were enough customers on Christmas Day too. Many other shopkeepers agree, and they intend to keep their doors open next Christmas too.

## Attempts to shorten sick leave

![Paramedic Timo Åhs](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9363795)

Open image viewer

Paramedic Timo Åhs first returned from sick leave to office work. Photo: Minna Rinta-Tassi / Yle

Attempts are being made to reduce long sick leaves.

For example, an employee can be given tasks that make it easier and quicker for them to return to work from sick leave.

Paramedic Timo Åhs says that he hurt his back and had an operation on it. After the sick leave, Åhs did office work for 2 months, because paramedic work is physically heavy. However, Åhs got the same pay as usual.

Such an arrangement benefits everyone. The employee loses less money when the sick leave is shorter. Companies also save, because less working time is lost.

## The poor situation of cheetahs

![Cheetah](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6688380)

Open image viewer

The cheetah is the fastest land animal in the world. Photo: Lukas Coch / EPA

A new study says that cheetahs may die out. If that happens, no cheetahs will be left at all.

The cheetah is a cat animal. It runs faster than any other animal. In the wild there are still about 7,000 cheetahs.

The cheetahs' situation is bad, because people hunt the same animals that cheetahs hunt. There is a risk that space and food will run out for cheetahs because of people.
