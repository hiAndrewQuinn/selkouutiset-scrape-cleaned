# Friday 19.6.2020 (radio)

EU countries are negotiating a corona crisis aid package. No new corona infections were found in Uusimaa on Thursday. A Finnish conductor is getting a Paris orchestra. Finland is now celebrating the midsummer festival, Juhannus.

## EU meeting

![Charles Michel and European leaders hold a meeting by video link.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11410238)

Open image viewer

EU leaders had a video meeting. Photo: Olivier Hoslet / AFP

EU country leaders have started negotiations on the European Union's finances. The negotiations began on Friday with a video meeting.

EU leaders are negotiating, for example, a big aid package to repair the damage caused by the corona crisis. The European Commission has proposed that the aid package be 750 billion euros.

Many countries want changes to the Commission's proposal. Finland, for example, wants the aid package to be smaller than 750 billion euros.

## Few corona infections

![Meilahti hospital area.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11269522)

Open image viewer

Meilahti hospital in Helsinki. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The corona situation in Finland is good.

In the Helsinki and Uusimaa region, no new corona infections were found on Thursday.

Since the end of February, corona infections have been found every day in Helsinki and Uusimaa. Thursday was therefore the first day without new corona infections.

On Friday, 2 new corona infections were reported.

Across Finland, just over 7,100 corona infections have been found so far. The number of corona deaths has not grown in Finland. There have been 326 deaths.

## Finnish top work in Paris

![Klaus Mäkelä Classical Trancelations](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9124254)

Open image viewer

Conductor Klaus Mäkelä. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

The famous Paris orchestra is getting a Finnish conductor. The French Ministry of Culture says so.

Finnish conductor Klaus Mäkelä will start leading the Paris orchestra in autumn 2022. The job will last 5 years.

Klaus Mäkelä is 24 years old. He already has a fine international career. This year Mäkelä starts work as a conductor in Norway.

## Celebrating Midsummer

![Sauna stove and sauna whisk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10896664)

Open image viewer

Many people spend Midsummer at their summer cottage. Photo: Laura Mainiemi / Yle

Finland is now celebrating the midsummer festival, Juhannus.

Midsummer Eve has passed calmly. Because of corona, no big festivals have been organised. Many people are spending Midsummer at their summer cottages.

Midsummer Day is on Saturday. Then trains and buses run on Sunday timetables. Many grocery shops are open all Midsummer weekend.

The Midsummer weather is beautiful. In a large part of Finland it is sunny and really warm. In Southern Finland there is a heatwave, that is, the temperature is at least 25 degrees. Only in Lapland is it a bit colder.

Saturday is Midsummer Day. Before that there is still Midsummer night.

Midsummer night is a time of magic. If you put 7 different flowers under your pillow, you will see your future partner in your dream. Finns at least used to believe this.

Happy Midsummer to everyone!
