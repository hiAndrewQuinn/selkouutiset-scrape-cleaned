## Ukrainian children

![A small Ukrainian child eats at a railway station in Poland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5114,h_2876,x_0,y_356/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9403936252abc929c49/1649593407)

The number of Ukrainian refugees is growing. The situation of Ukrainian children is worrying, especially around the world.

The children's agency Unicef says that almost 5 million Ukrainian children have had to flee. That means that about 60 percent of Ukraine's children have had to leave their own home.

Russia attacked Ukraine at the end of February. Since then, a total of 11 million Ukrainians have fled, more than 4 million of them abroad.

## Finns' opinions of Russia

![Putin looking stern in the background, Russian flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5382,h_3028,x_0,y_1389/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-93711062485a956948a/1648963242)

Open image viewer

Finns like Russians, but not Putin. Photo: Asar Studios / AOP

Finns' opinions of Russia have changed because of the war in Ukraine. This is what a new study shows.

Most Finns now think that Russia is a danger to Finland. Over 80 percent of Finns say that Russia is a significant military threat.

According to Finns, the problem is Russia's leaders, not ordinary Russians. In the study, almost 60 percent of Finns think that Russians are pleasant people.

## Grey labour

![A cleaner mops floors on company premises.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11159696)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle

There is a lot of grey labour in Finland. Grey or black labour means workers from whom no taxes or social security contributions are paid.

Often grey labour comes from abroad. In authorities' inspections, about 20 percent of workplaces had foreign labour that did not have the right to work. The situation is worst in the construction sector.

## Popular names

![A baby's little hand in the palm of a mother's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_141/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6576295e7b6a792bda7/1585147433)

Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

Olivia and Leo are now popular names. Last year about 300 girls were named Olivia and almost 400 boys were named Leo.

Popular girls' names last year also included Lilja, Eevi, Sofia and Venla.

Popular boys' names also included Eino, Oliver, Elias and Väinö.

The most popular name for girls in Swedish-speaking families last year was Saga. The most popular Swedish-speaking boys' name was Liam.

## Summer speeds

![Summer speed limits are being changed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7900854)

Open image viewer

Speed limits change this month. Photo: Timo Ikonen / Yle

On Finland's roads, the switch to summer speeds is taking place gradually. The switch began today, Tuesday.

Summer speeds come into use in different areas when the weather and driving conditions are good enough.

The aim is that summer speeds are in use across the whole country by the end of April.

## The weather

The weather continues dry on Wednesday and in many places sunny. In the south the temperature rises close to 10 degrees, and in a large part of the country it is above 5 degrees.

Cloudiness will gradually increase from the west, and on Thursday rain will arrive in the south and central parts of the country. Driving conditions are poor in places on Thursday.
