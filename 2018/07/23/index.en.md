## Prostate cancer was given drug treatment

![Doctors give radiotherapy to a prostate cancer patient in France.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9651593)

Open image viewer

Doctors operate on prostate cancer. Photo: AOP

New information has been gained about prostate cancer in men. Prostate cancer is the most common cancer among Finnish men.

A new Finnish study says that cholesterol medicine may also help in treating prostate cancer. Cholesterol medicine is meant for treating the fat values in the blood.

Almost 160 men took part in the study. The men had their prostate removed because of cancer. Before the operation, the men took cholesterol medicine.

The researchers noticed that the growth of prostate cancer slowed down with the help of cholesterol medicine. It is good news for men. Every year about 5000 men get prostate cancer in Finland, and about 800 men die of prostate cancer.

## People want services across Finland

![Mail at a service point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10313467)

Open image viewer

Finns want services to remain everywhere. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

It is important for Finns that people live in all parts of Finland. That is why Finns want services to work in small municipalities too.

Almost 80 percent of Finns think that the state should support services in small municipalities. Most residents of the Helsinki region also want the state to give money to small municipalities.

The information comes from a new Yle survey.

## Blue-green algae is spreading in the waters

![blue-green algae porridge in the sea](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10314532)

Open image viewer

Blue-green algae grows well in hot weather. Photo: Jaani Lampinen / Yle

There is now a lot of blue-green algae in the sea and in lakes. Blue-green algae grows well because the summer is hot.

Blue-green algae is actually a blue bacterium. A blue bacterium can contain different poisons.

If blue-green algae gets onto a person's skin or into their mouth, the person may become ill. Blue-green algae can cause skin symptoms, vomiting and headaches, for example.

It is never a good idea to swim in water when there is a blue-green algae warning at the beach.

## The heat continues across Finland

![Sun shines in Vaasa on a record heat day](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10310737)

Open image viewer

Hard sunshine is a strain on small children and the elderly. Photo: Tuomo Rintamaa / Yle

The heat has lasted a long time in Finland, meaning the temperature has been over 25 degrees.

The heat is a strain especially on the elderly and small children. Hospitals have treated patients who have had sunstroke or heatstroke.

The heat is not ending yet. The weather forecast does not promise any cooler days.
