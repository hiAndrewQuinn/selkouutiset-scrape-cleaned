## Russia celebrated its navy

![ships sail past an opened bridge](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9748633)

Open image viewer

Russian navy vessels sailed in a parade along the Neva River. Photo: Alexander Zemlianichenko / EPA

Russia has celebrated Navy Day. This time, the main event of Navy Day was in St Petersburg.

On the Neva River there was a large naval parade with dozens of ships, submarines, aeroplanes and helicopters. About 5000 soldiers also took part in the parade.

The main attraction of the parade was the world's largest nuclear submarine, the Dmitri Donskoi.

## The coast guard's summer is busy

![Lieutenant Santeri Laitanen signals a boat to stop for a spot check.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9748199)

Open image viewer

The coast guard's duties also include monitoring boating safety Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

Summer is a busy time for the Gulf of Finland coast guard.

For example, this summer the coast guard has been needed to help when more canoes than usual have capsized and boats have collided off Helsinki.

The Gulf of Finland coast guard has about 1000 sea rescue missions each year. Most of them are in the summer.

The weather has a big effect on the number of rescue missions. In good weather, many boaters go out to sea, and then there are also more rescue missions than in bad weather.

Lieutenant Commander Oiva Juntunen is glad that nowadays almost all boaters wear life jackets.

## A good lock can stop a bike thief

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9747357)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics

If you own a bicycle, it is worth buying a good lock for it. Otherwise the bike may disappear with bike thieves.

The police say that, for example, many cable locks are easy for a bike thief to cut with tools. Cutting a chain lock is harder, but a thief can succeed with that too.

In the police's opinion, an arched U-lock is perhaps the best protection for a bike. If you lock the bike with a U-lock from the top of the frame to, for example, a railing, it is really hard for a thief to cut the lock.

Most bikes are stolen in the summer. The police say that in Helsinki, thieves take a lot of bikes, especially of the Jopo brand.

## Summer still continues

![Lightning](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5391987)

Open image viewer

In southern Finland there may be thunder on Monday. Photo: Yle

August begins the day after tomorrow, on Tuesday, in warm weather.

Yle meteorologist Joonas Koskela says that temperatures are between 20 and 24 degrees in almost the whole country. Only in northern Lapland is it chilly, with the temperature below 10 degrees.

The weather early in the week is also changeable. Sometimes it rains and sometimes the sun shines. Joonas Koskela says that in the southern part of the country there may be rain and thunder on Monday. On Tuesday the rain moves north.
