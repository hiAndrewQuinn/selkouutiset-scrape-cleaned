# Saturday 28.1.2017 (radio)

Citizens of many Muslim countries may not travel to the USA. Finnish tennis player Henri Kontinen has won an important match in Australia. Among the 100 best players in the history of the North American ice hockey league NHL there are also 2 Finns. The Moomins will not move from Naantali to Helsinki.

## Trump closes the USA to Muslims

![Donald Trump.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9429886)

Open image viewer

President Donald Trump. Photo: Olivier Douliery / EPA

US President Donald Trump does not want people from Muslim countries to come to the USA.

The President has ordered that the USA will not accept Syrian refugees until further notice.

Citizens of 6 other Muslim countries may not travel to the USA either. The countries are Iran, Iraq, Libya, Somalia, Sudan and Yemen. The ban lasts 3 months.

Already on Saturday there were many passengers who could not get into the USA because of the ban.

President Trump says that the ban is important for US security. Trump does not want Islamist terrorists in the USA.

## Tennis player Kontinen succeeds

![Peers, Kontinen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9429740)

Open image viewer

Henri Kontinen and John Peers on a tennis court in Australia. Photo: EPA

Finnish tennis player Henri Kontinen has won an important match.

Together with Australian John Peers, Kontinen won the doubles at the Australian tennis tournament. In doubles, 2 pairs play against each other.

Kontinen and Peers competed against the Americans Bob and Mike Bryan. The Bryans are brothers. They have won many tennis competitions before.

Henri Kontinen is the first Finn to win the doubles of a Grand Slam tournament. The Grand Slam tournaments are the world's 4 most important tennis competitions. One of them is the Australian Open tennis tournament.

## Kurri and Selänne were honoured

![Jari Kurri Teemu Selänne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9429365)

Open image viewer

Jari Kurri and Teemu Selänne. Photo: AOP

The North American ice hockey league NHL turns 100 years old. The NHL was founded in 1917.

For the jubilee year, the NHL has published a list of the best players. The list has the NHL's 100 best players from the last 100 years.

There are also 2 Finnish ice hockey players on the list. They are Jari Kurri and Teemu Selänne. Kurri played in the NHL until 1998. Selänne's NHL games continued until 2014.

## The Moomins stay in Naantali

![At Moomin World 2015](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8320994)

Open image viewer

Moomin World in Naantali. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

One more news item about the Moomins. The Moomins will not move from Naantali to Helsinki.

In Helsinki, people have hoped to get the Moomins to the capital. But the head of Moomin World, Tomi Lohikoski, says that Moomin World stays in Naantali.

In Naantali the Moomin park is in the middle of beautiful nature, as Tove Jansson wanted. Jansson wrote the Moomin books.

Director Lohikoski also says that Finland does not need a second Moomin park. Naantali is only 180 kilometres from Helsinki. That is a short journey, for example, for Japanese tourists who like to visit Moomin World.
