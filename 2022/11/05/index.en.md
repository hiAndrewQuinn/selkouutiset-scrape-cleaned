# Saturday 5.11.2022 (radio)

Finns prepare to save. Finland needs more police with an immigrant background. On All Saints' Day people remembered loved ones who have died. Möpsö and the puppies are the stars of the popular Pentulive. The weather.

## Saving

![Electricity, electricity consumption, extension cable.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10805411)

Open image viewer

About half intend to save on electricity and energy consumption. Photo: AOP

Most Finns intend to save during the next year.

In the Local Government Development Foundation's study, about 1,000 people were asked about their plans to save. About 70 percent of respondents said that they have to save on something at least.

About half intended to save on electricity and energy consumption. Respondents over 60 years old in particular intended to reduce their electricity consumption.

About 40 percent of respondents intended to save on holiday trips, restaurant services and food shopping. Young people in particular intended to save on food and clothing shopping and restaurant visits.

About 25 percent of respondents said that they do not need to save on anything.

## Shortage of police

![A police officer drives a car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_380/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1019046634404e841b9d/1666091029)

Open image viewer

Good language skills are an advantage in police work. Photo: Arash Matin / Yle

There is a shortage in Finland of police officers with an immigrant background. More Swedish-speaking officers and women are also wanted in the police.

The number of people with an immigrant background and people who speak foreign languages is growing quickly in Finland. The National Police Board says that it is good for the police to represent the whole population. Then all population groups feel that they can and dare to approach the police.

It is also an advantage in the police's everyday work if a police officer has good language skills and understands different cultures.

## All Saints' Day

![Grave candles on a grave](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8417449)

Open image viewer

On All Saints' Day candles are taken to graves. Photo: Nina Keski-Korpela / Yle

All Saints' Day has been celebrated today.

For many Finns the day is quiet and devout. On that day loved ones and dear people who have already died are remembered. On All Saints' Day candles are often taken to the graves of loved ones.

All Saints' Day is also a popular church holiday.

On Yle's website or on Yle Areena you can once again follow the life of dog puppies.

In Yle Tiede's Pentulive this time the dog Möpsö and her puppies are followed. The mother, Möpsö, is a Shiba Inu. She gave birth to three puppies the day before yesterday, that is, on Thursday.

Pentulive follows the life of Möpsö and the puppies until the puppies are handed over to their new homes, that is, for about 8 weeks in total.
