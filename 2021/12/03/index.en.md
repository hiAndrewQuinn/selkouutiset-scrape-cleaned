## Corona pass for passengers

All passengers arriving in Finland must show a corona certificate, says the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health. If the certificate is missing, the passenger is directed to a corona test.

The Government has given a new decree on the matter, which will come into force tomorrow. This is an attempt to stop the spread of the Omicron variant of the corona virus.

A second Omicron case has been confirmed in Finland. The infection was found in the Varsinais-Suomi hospital district.

The first Omicron infection was confirmed yesterday in the HUS area. Both infections come from Sweden.

## School threats

![A finger pointing at the TikTok logo visible on a mobile phone screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3405,h_1915,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7506935fcb1935f2d1b/1657709031)

Photo: Hayoung Jeon / EPA

In various parts of Ostrobothnia, several school threats have been made during the week. Behind the threats there may be a challenge that is spreading on social media.

The police have received information that a challenge is spreading on the video service TikTok, which urges people to make a school threat.

The police say that the latest cases are in Kokkola in Central Ostrobothnia and in Seinäjoki in South Ostrobothnia. In both cases, minor suspects have been caught.

The police remind that a threat always leads to a preliminary investigation and a crime report for an illegal threat.

## Birth rate

![A child plays with a lorry in a playground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11272828)

Open image viewer

Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The birth rate rose a little in Finland last year. The birth rate is a figure that tells how many children on average are born per 100 000 people during a year.

The number of children per woman last year was 1.37, when it was 1.35 the year before.

Among highly educated women the birth rate turned to growth, after a fall that lasted more than 10 years. The birth rate grew the most among women aged 30–39.

The birth rate rose only among highly educated women.

This is what Statistics Finland says.

## Thin ice

![A man breaks the edge of the ice with ice picks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11839742)

Open image viewer

The ice is still thin. It is not worth going there. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Many people have been in danger on thin ice.

The Finnish Swimming and Water Rescue Federation says that dozens of near-miss situations have happened.

In November 7 people drowned, and at the start of this month 2.

From January to November, almost 150 people have already drowned in Finland in total.

## Weather

![A hard frost at Ruka on 28.11.2021](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88526161a35a5f68626/1638095487)

The weather in the coming days is very cold across the whole country. In Lapland the frost may be as much as 40 degrees on Sunday.

The weather gets colder on Saturday and Sunday, when Arctic cold air mass comes to Finland.

In the middle of Finland the frost is about 25 degrees, and in the south 15 degrees.

Because the wind is biting, you can add 5 to 10 degrees to the frost figures.
