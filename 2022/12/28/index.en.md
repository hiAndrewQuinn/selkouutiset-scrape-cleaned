## Natural gas storage ship

![LNG ship Exemplar arrives at the port of Inkoo on 28.12.2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5820,h_3273,x_0,y_558/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105366563ac3ffb78861/1672402617)

The natural gas storage ship secures the supply of gas in Finland. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

The storage ship for liquefied natural gas has arrived in Finland. The ship arrived on Wednesday at the port of Inkoo in Uusimaa.

The ship's cargo is liquefied natural gas. The ship will stay at the port of Inkoo, where it works as a natural gas store.

More liquefied natural gas is brought to the ship by gas tankers. The liquefied natural gas is turned into ordinary natural gas, which is fed into Finland's natural gas network. The natural gas storage ship secures industry's access to gas.

## Olkiluoto 3

![Picture of a nuclear power plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3887,h_2187,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98273462d173dfd94ac/1657894068)

Open image viewer

The Olkiluoto 3 nuclear reactor is producing electricity again. The reactor may start regular electricity production in March. Photo: Lucas Ekblad / Yle

The new reactor 3 at the Olkiluoto nuclear power plant is producing electricity again. The reactor has been out of operation because of problems.

Energy company TVO is testing the new nuclear reactor's operation. The reactor should have been put into use long ago. However, the start-up of the reactor has been delayed many times, because there have been problems with the construction and technology of the reactor.

The new estimate is that the new Olkiluoto reactor 3 will start regular electricity production in March.

## Willow tits

![Willow tit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2100,h_1181,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-55176573ef5bf94378/1672846928)

Open image viewer

The willow tit has been a really common bird in Finland. Photo: Risto Salovaara / Yle

Now, about birds. Logging has reduced the number of willow tits. Researchers at the University of Oulu have been following willow tits for about 50 years.

Willow tits need old rotten trees as nesting trees. If there are no suitable trees, the willow tit cannot nest. A few decades ago the willow tit was a really common bird in Finland. Nowadays there are quite few willow tits.

Researchers say that the biggest reason for the decrease in willow tits is logging.
