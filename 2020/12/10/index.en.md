# Thursday 10.12.2020 (radio)

There has been a disturbance at the Olkiluoto nuclear power plant. The court has remanded 3 boys suspected of murder. There are more coronavirus infections. The corona van is sharing coronavirus information in Vantaa. On Friday it will snow in the south.

## Disturbance at the nuclear power plant

![Olkiluoto 3 nuclear power plant](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4784,h_2691,x_0,y_52/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5978065d9c8cd9f1823/1573223366)

The Olkiluoto nuclear power plant area is on an island in Western Finland. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There has been a disturbance at the Olkiluoto atomic power plant, that is, the nuclear power plant. The disturbance happened on Thursday afternoon.

Teollisuuden Voima, that is, TVO, says that the disturbance was not a danger to people. TVO owns the Olkiluoto nuclear power plant.

TVO says that the cause of the disturbance may have been a fault in the reactor water cleaning system. However, no dangerous radiation got into the environment from the nuclear power plant.

The Olkiluoto nuclear power plant is in Western Finland, in Eurajoki, near Pori.

## Murder in Helsinki

![Candles at the murder site of a 16-year-old boy in Koskela, Helsinki, on 9 December 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5079,h_2857,x_0,y_573/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7526155fd1232c1356b/1611037861)

Open image viewer

People have put candles at the murder site. Photo: Markku Pelkonen / Yle

The court has remanded 3 boys, aged 16, in Helsinki.

The police suspect that the boys committed a murder. They may have murdered a boy who was also 16 years old.

The murder happened a week ago on Friday, but the dead boy was only found on Monday. Construction workers found the boy's body when they came to work.

The murder happened near Koskela Hospital, where new blocks of flats are being built.

The police say that all the boys knew each other. The police also say that the act was brutal.

## Coronavirus information in Vantaa

![Antton Ahonen and Markus Helanen of the City of Vantaa's sports department share coronavirus information and masks in Hakunila.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11688423)

Open image viewer

The corona van is sharing coronavirus information in Vantaa. Photo: Miika Koskela / Yle

The City of Vantaa is sharing information about the coronavirus in a new way.

In Vantaa there is a corona van, that is, a delivery van that shares coronavirus information in 9 languages.

Immigrants' own communities are also taking part in the work of the corona van.

The Vantaa corona van is part of the City's "Neuvova" project.

Project expert Aadan Ibrahim says that the coronavirus information has been well received, for example, in religious communities. They have limited the number of people at events, and, for example, Friday prayers are no longer held, says Aadan Ibrahim.
