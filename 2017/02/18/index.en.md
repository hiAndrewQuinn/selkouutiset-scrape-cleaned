This year European leaders are interested in what US policy is like under President Donald Trump.

US Vice President Mike Pence said at the conference that the military alliance Nato is important to the USA under Trump too. But the USA wants the other Nato countries to also spend enough money on weapons.

## Falls among the elderly

![A woman carrying a handbag walks down the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5491873)

Open image viewer

Good shoes are needed if you do not want to fall in winter. Photo: YLE

There is a wish to reduce falling accidents among the elderly. Every year many elderly people fall and hurt themselves badly. The UKK Institute says that about 30 percent of these injuries can be prevented.

In a UKK Institute project, elderly people were asked about things that can increase the risk of falling. The researchers wanted to know, for example, whether the elderly person's home has rugs or cords that the person can trip over. The risk factors were fixed quickly. If needed, the elderly person got advice from a public health nurse and a physiotherapist.

The UKK Institute estimates that in this way it is possible to prevent thousands of falls every year and save over 100 million euros a year.

## The Lahti World Championships

![A shop window with a World Championships 2017 theme, optician Mäkelä, Lahti](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9450520)

The Nordic Ski World Championships start in Finland next week. The host city is Lahti.

The opening ceremony is on Wednesday. President Sauli Niinistö will speak at the ceremony, and there are over 1000 performers. The opening ceremony can also be watched on television.

700 athletes from 60 countries take part in the Lahti World Championships. They compete in skiing and ski jumping. The competitions end on 5 March.

## The elderly person and the dog

![A woman and a dog in a snowy landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9420723)

Open image viewer

It is nice to be outdoors with a dog. Photo: Anu Pöntinen / Yle

A dog is a good friend for an elderly person.

An elderly person moves and goes outdoors more if they have a dog. That is why a dog improves an elderly person's health. For example, dog owners do not need to see a doctor as often as others.

A dog also reduces an elderly person's loneliness. A dog is good company at home, and outside the elderly person gets to chat with other dog owners. That is why an elderly person has less depression and anxiety if they have a dog.

Because dogs are a great help, Finland will start training support dogs for the elderly. A support dog's task is to keep the elderly person active. Studies will soon start at the University of Eastern Finland for people who want to learn to train support dogs.
