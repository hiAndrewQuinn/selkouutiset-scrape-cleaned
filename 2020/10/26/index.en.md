# Monday 26.10.2020 (radio)

The Vastaamo data breach worries many people. President Sauli Niinistö has also taken a position on the data breach. There is 1 new coronavirus death in Finland. A state of emergency has started in Spain because of the coronavirus.

## The data breach worries many people

![The image shows the Vastaamo website on a computer screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11605240)

Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The police are still investigating the data breach that was carried out on the psychotherapy centre Vastaamo.

A thief took Vastaamo's patient data, and he has published it on the internet. The thief demands money so that he stops publishing the patient data. The police say that nothing should be paid to the thief.

Many Vastaamo patients are worried because of the data breach. It is frightening if you do not know what will happen to your own patient data. Patient data contains things that patients want to keep secret.

Dr Meri Larivaara of the association Mieli says that everyone should seek help if the situation makes them anxious. For example, Mieli's own crisis phone line is always open.

Mieli is an organisation that promotes mental health. The organisation's web address is mieli.fi.

The authorities have also opened a website with instructions for victims of the data breach. The website's address is tietovuotoapu.fi.

## The President took a position on the data breach

![The image shows Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11612517)

Open image viewer

The President gave an interview to Yle on Sunday. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö has taken a position on the Vastaamo data breach.

Niinistö says that the data breach concerns all of us. We all understand how bad it feels for the victims.

Niinistö also says that people must be critical. Everyone must reject information that has been obtained through a crime. It is good to close the internet right away if you come across information that has been obtained by stealing, Niinistö says in an interview with Yle.

## THL gave the new coronavirus figures

![The Meilahti hospital area of the Helsinki University Hospital (HUS) in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11270148)

Most coronavirus infections are in the Helsinki area. The picture shows Meilahti hospital in Helsinki. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

There is 1 new coronavirus death in Finland. In total, 354 people in Finland have died because of the coronavirus.

In addition, there are 122 new coronavirus infections in Finland. THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, gave the new figures on Monday.

The coronavirus has spread especially in the Vaasa, Helsinki and Central Finland areas.

## Spain has a state of emergency

![A nurse dressed as the Grim Reaper protests in Barcelona.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11601470)

Open image viewer

Nurses protested in Spain for better working conditions. Photo: Lluis Gene / AFP

A state of emergency has started in Spain because of the coronavirus. During the state of emergency, people may not, for example, go outside at night.

However, Spain's borders are not closed, and the state of emergency does not apply to the Canary Islands.

The state of emergency lasts at least 2 weeks, but it may continue much longer.

The reason for the state of emergency is the spread of the coronavirus. More than a million coronavirus infections have already been reported in Spain.
