# Tuesday 28.8.2018 (radio)

Children in day care can be sick a lot. The Court of Appeal is considering dissolving a neo-Nazi organisation. A toy smuggler is accused of taking away aid money. A baby panda was born at Korkeasaari.

## The cycle of illness among day care children

![Girl blowing her nose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8621469)

Open image viewer

Day care children can be sick as much as 100 days a year. Photo: Päivi Meritähti / Yle

Flu viruses are spreading again and causing sick leave at workplaces, schools and day care centres.

Day care children can be sick as much as 100 days a year. The youngest children are sick the most.

Paediatric specialist Tero Kontiokari says that many children are taken back to day care too quickly after an illness. A cycle of sickness can start if a child is brought back into care when they are not yet fully healthy.

Parents rush too much, because they cannot stay home to care for a sick child. Parents cannot be away from work for many days.

## Neo-Nazi organisation in the Court of Appeal

![Neo-Nazi Nordic Resistance Movement marched in the centre of Turku on Saturday 18 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10373177)

Open image viewer

The neo-Nazi Nordic Resistance Movement marched in Turku on a Saturday a little over a week ago. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The Turku Court of Appeal is considering this week whether to dissolve the neo-Nazi organisation.

The Pirkanmaa District Court already decided last November that the neo-Nazi organisation Nordic Resistance Movement must be dissolved. The court said that the organisation's activity is illegal. For example, it uses violence, spreads hate speech and violates other people's human rights.

The organisation appealed the ruling to the Court of Appeal.

The Court of Appeal will deal with the matter this week. A decision will be reached within about a month.

## Toy smuggler in the district court

![Toy smuggler Rami Adham in the Helsinki District Court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10373536)

Open image viewer

Toy smuggler Rami Adham in the Helsinki District Court. Photo: Irene Stachon / Lehtikuva

A trial against Rami Adham has started in the Helsinki District Court. Adham is known as Syria's toy smuggler, because he took toys to children who were living in the middle of the war in the city of Aleppo.

The prosecutor demands a prison sentence for Adham for about 20 offences. The prosecutor says that Adham has, for example, collected money for Syria without permission. The prosecutor says that Adham has put aid money into his own account and bought himself a cottage, for example.

Adham says that all the money has gone to Syrian aid projects.

## Baby pandas at Korkeasaari

![A baby panda in a keeper's hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10373611)

Open image viewer

The baby panda at Korkeasaari still needs its mother's care. Perhaps in September or October it will start moving around on its own outside the nest. Photo: Mari Lehmonen / Korkeasaari Zoo

A baby panda has been born at Korkeasaari Zoo. The birth of the cub is a very happy event, because the panda is a critically endangered species.

The new cub is a male. It was born in early July. The exact birthday is not known, because the mother hid the cub for several days.

The last time a panda was born in Korkeasaari was 7 years ago.

Pandas come from Central Asia, from the lower slopes of the Himalayas. Only fewer than 10,000 pandas live in the wild.
