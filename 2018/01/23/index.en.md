# Tuesday 23.1.2018 (radio)

SAK demands that the activation model for unemployment benefits is cancelled. There was a serious dangerous situation at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Finnish children eat too few vegetables. Hundreds of people are waiting for an organ transplant. This winter's cold record was set in Muonio.

## A demonstration and strikes against the activation model

![Representatives of SAK unions at a union meeting at the Helsinki Exhibition and Convention Centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10036344)

Representatives of SAK unions discussed the activation model for unemployment benefits at the Helsinki Exhibition and Convention Centre. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The workers' central organisation SAK wants the government to cancel the activation model for unemployment benefits.

SAK is organising a demonstration against the activation model at the Senate Square in Helsinki. The demonstration is on Friday next week, 2 February.

The Construction Trade Union and the Finnish Transport Workers' Union AKT are organising strikes on the same day.

The activation model means that an unemployed person gets less support if he or she does not actively look for work. In the activation model, an unemployed person must, within 3 months, work for a few days or be in training. Otherwise the support gets smaller.

A large share of Finns also think that the activation model is bad.

## A dangerous situation at Helsinki-Vantaa airport

![A Finnair plane at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10036393)

Open image viewer

A Finnair plane at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

There was a serious dangerous situation at Helsinki-Vantaa airport on Tuesday morning.

Air traffic control gave a passenger plane permission to go onto the runway. However, at the same time there were 3 work vehicles on the runway, which were removing snow. However, the plane did not crash into the work vehicles and nobody was hurt.

There were 60 passengers on the plane. It was on its way to Riga, the capital of Latvia.

The Safety Investigation Authority is investigating the dangerous situation.

## Eating vegetables is learned already at daycare

![a child eats vegetables](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9910867)

Open image viewer

It is good to eat vegetables many times a day. Photo: AOP

Finnish children eat too few fruits, berries and vegetables.

The recommendations say that it is good for children under school age to eat vegetables 5 times a day. On average, however, they eat them only 2 or 3 times.

Now separate food recommendations have been made for children under school age for daycare centres, clubs and preschools. The goal is to teach children to eat healthily and in a varied way

## Hundreds of people in the queue for organ transplants

![Organ transplant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9466285)

Open image viewer

Last year more than half of organ transplants were kidney transplants. Photo: AOP

Almost 400 organ transplants were done in Finland last year.

More than 50 percent of the organ transplants were kidney transplants. The second most common were liver transplants.

In Finland, for example heart and lung transplants are also done.

At the turn of the year, more than 500 people were waiting for a new organ.

Not everyone gets a new organ in time. Every year as many as 10 percent of patients die because they do not get organ transplant surgery in time.

## A new cold record was set in Muonio

![The picture shows a weather map with temperatures.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10035507)

Open image viewer

The night between Monday and Tuesday was cold in the whole country. Photo: Yle

Last night night a new cold record for this winter was set.

In Muonio, in north-western Lapland, it was minus 34.6 degrees last night.

This winter's previous cold record was minus 33.5 degrees. It was measured on 7 December in the village of Väylä in Inari.
