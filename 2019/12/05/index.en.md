# Thursday 5.12.2019 (radio)

Support for the Finns Party continues to rise. The SDP's party executive proposes Antti Rinne as government formation negotiator. Researchers have developed a contraceptive pill that needs to be taken only once a month. A memorial is to be built for President Mauno Koivisto.

## Party support

Support for the Finns Party is higher than ever before. In Yle's poll, 24.3 per cent of respondents support the Finns Party.

The second most support goes to the National Coalition Party, with 18.6 per cent.

At the moment, the Greens are the most popular government party. Their support is 13.9 per cent.

Support for the SDP, the prime minister's party, is now just over 13 per cent. The SDP's support has fallen by more than 4 percentage points since the parliamentary election.

The Centre Party has lost the most support. The Centre Party's support is now 10.6 per cent.

SDP chair Antti Rinne is the government formation negotiator. The parliamentary groups of the parties chose Rinne as government formation negotiator, as the SDP proposed.

Rinne's aim is for the government programme to be ready on Sunday.

The SDP's party council will choose the candidate for prime minister on Sunday evening.

At least Sanna Marin, Minister of Transport and Communications and deputy chair, and Antti Lindtman, chair of the parliamentary group, want to become prime minister.

## Once-a-month contraceptive pill

![Contraceptives](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10278961)

Open image viewer

Pills, condoms, contraceptive implant, coil. An e-pill taken only once a month could be a solution to forgotten pills. Photo: AOP

Researchers have developed a contraceptive pill that is taken only once a month.

The pill stays in the stomach for several weeks. Hormone is released all the time, which prevents pregnancy. The British broadcaster BBC reports this.

The pill is designed so that it withstands stomach acids.

Millions of women around the world use contraceptive pills. The problem is that taking the pill can be forgotten.

The new e-pill has already been tested on pigs. American researchers hope the pill can be tested on people within a few years. So the pill will not go on sale for a long time yet.

## Memorial for President Koivisto

![Mauno Koivisto memorial book.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9631313)

Open image viewer

President Mauno Koivisto died on 12 May 2017. He served as President of the Republic of Finland from 1982 to 1994. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / AOP

President Mauno Koivisto is to get a memorial in Helsinki.

The memorial will be unveiled in 2023. By then it will be 100 years since Mauno Koivisto was born.

The Prime Minister's Office has set up a committee for the memorial. The committee is led by former prime minister Paavo Lipponen.

The committee will organise a design competition and choose the makers of the memorial. The committee will also decide where the memorial will be.
