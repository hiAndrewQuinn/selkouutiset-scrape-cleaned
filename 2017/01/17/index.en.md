# Tuesday 17.1.2017 (radio)

Asylum decisions. Brexit. A serial drowner. A nightclub shooter. A power cut in Amsterdam. Collecting health data.

## Asylum decisions will not change

![The entrance of the Finnish Immigration Service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9097101)

Open image viewer

The Finnish Immigration Service will not start making asylum decisions in a new way.  Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

There will be no changes to how asylum cases are decided in Finland. The Finnish Immigration Service has examined whether asylum decisions must be made in a new way. The agency examined security in Afghanistan, Iraq and Somalia.

The agency thinks that security has not changed much. The agency now says that no changes need to be made in asylum decisions. Some changes, for example in Iraq's security, are already taken into account.

## Britain plans to leave the EU's free trade

![British Prime Minister Theresa May](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9408741)

Open image viewer

British Prime Minister Theresa May has told how Britain plans to leave the EU.  Photo: FACUNDO ARRIZABALAGA / EPA

Britain is leaving the EU's single market. The single market means that EU countries can trade freely with other EU countries.

British Prime Minister Theresa May said this when she spoke about Britain leaving the EU. However, May wants to keep good trade relations with the EU.

## A serial drowner was sentenced in Finland

![Pekka Seppänen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9408702)

Open image viewer

The police have issued a wanted notice for the serial drowner. If you see this man, call the emergency centre.  Photo: Eastern Finland Police

A Finnish man is going to prison for a long time for a rare crime. The man got a prison sentence of almost 15 years.

The court case has been named the serial drowner case. The man killed friends by drowning them. In addition, he tried to drown other friends.

People are also puzzled by the court case because the man disappeared after the sentence. The police issued a wanted notice for the man.

## Turkey arrested the nightclub shooter

![Security camera image of the attacker in front of the nightclub.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9382857)

Open image viewer

The Turkish nightclub shooter killed 39 people.  Photo: DHA / EPA

Turkey says that it has caught the Istanbul nightclub shooter. The shooter was caught in Istanbul late yesterday.

According to the authorities, the man has confessed to the act.  The man is from Uzbekistan.

The attack on the Reina nightclub in Istanbul was carried out on New Year's Eve. The shooter killed 39 people.

## A big power cut in Amsterdam

![Morning traffic in Amsterdam.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9408020)

Open image viewer

The power cut mixed up traffic in Amsterdam.  Photo: Marcel Antonisse / EPA

There was a big power cut in Amsterdam in the morning. Hundreds of thousands of homes were without electricity.

The cause of the power cut is not known. A large part of the electricity came back during the morning.

The power cut stopped public transport in Amsterdam. Cold weather is suspected as the cause of the power cut. In cold weather a lot of electricity is used.

## A data store is being collected on health

![A nurse looks into a patient's ear.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9407356)

Open image viewer

Care gets better if a lot of information is gathered from patients.  Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

There is now a wish to collect a lot of information about Finns' health. The information is wanted so that health care can get better.

People's health information is put together into databases, or data stores. Now the information is collected in hospitals. In the future, people can also send information themselves, for example about their own blood pressure.

Big databases help to give everyone exactly the right care. Today, big databases help, for example, in treating heart diseases.
