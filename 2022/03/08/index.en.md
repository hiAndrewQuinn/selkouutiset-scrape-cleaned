# Tuesday 8.3.2022 (radio)

A survey of MPs on NATO. Help for Ukraine. Ukrainian refugees. International Women's Day. Weather.

## MPs and NATO

![In the chamber of Parliament a vote was held on 10 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_348/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89050761b347ad1fc76/1639139802)

MPs work in Parliament. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Many MPs have not yet decided whether they support Finland's membership of the NATO military alliance.

When Yle asked MPs about the matter, most MPs did not answer the question at all.

58 MPs supported NATO membership. 9 MPs did not want Finland to become a NATO member.

Finland's NATO membership has been talked about a lot since Russia attacked Ukraine in February. In the view of many, Finland is safer if it is part of the NATO military alliance.

At present Finland is not a NATO member, but Finland cooperates with NATO.

## Help for Ukraine

![The Eastern Uusimaa ambulance stands in its garage waiting for a shipment to Ukraine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3699,h_2080,x_0,y_100/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9237326221dd1a516f6/1646386625)

Open image viewer

This ambulance will soon leave for Ukraine. Photo: Itä-Uudenmaan pelastuslaitos

Finland is sending help to Ukraine. There is war in Ukraine, because Russia has attacked Ukraine.

Finland is sending 15 ambulances and 2 fire engines to Ukraine. They are not new, but they are still in good condition. Some of the ambulances also have good equipment.

The ambulances and fire engines will be driven to Ukraine within a week.

In addition, rescue supplies, for example helmets and chainsaws, are being taken to Ukraine.

## Ukrainian refugees

![Two women eat at a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12349209)

Open image viewer

Ukrainian refugees at Kalle Åberg's home in Inkoo. Photo: Yle

The Ukrainian refugee crisis is getting worse. Already more than 2 million people have fled Ukraine because of the war.

Finland, too, is taking in refugees from Ukraine. The authorities have added places in reception centres. There are now more than 3,000 free places.

However, many Ukrainians are going to stay with relatives or acquaintances.

For example, farmer Kalle Åberg has invited to his home Ukrainians who worked on Åberg's farm in the summer.

Some women and children have come to Åberg's home. However, not everyone wants to leave Ukraine and leave their home to robbers, Åberg says.

## International Women's Day

![Customers buy flowers on Viru Street in Tallinn on International Women's Day, 8 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1620,h_911,x_0,y_170/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-78222060461ec685fd7/1615209030)

Open image viewer

In Estonia, flowers are often bought for women on Women's Day. Photo: Priit Mürk / ERR

On Tuesday International Women's Day has been celebrated.

Women's Day was born in the world at the beginning of the 1900s. Then, on Women's Day, it was demanded, for example, that women also get the right to vote in elections.

Later the UN, the United Nations, declared International Women's Day. International Women's Day was celebrated for the first time on 8 March 1975.
