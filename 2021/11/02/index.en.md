## UN climate conference

![Participants in the climate conference in Glasgow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4460,h_2508,x_0,y_17/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-874099617fa159e298c/1635754458)

Participants in the climate conference in Glasgow. Photo: Dominika Zarzycka/NurPhoto/Shutterstock/All Over Press

The UN climate conference has agreed on preserving forests. Over 100 countries have agreed that the destruction of forests will end by 2030.

Large countries are part of the agreement, for example Brazil, Russia, China and the USA. Finland is also part of the agreement.

Forests prevent the warming of the climate, because forests bind carbon dioxide from the air. That is why the UN climate conference has agreed on preserving forests.

## Finland's energy production

![Wind turbines in the Reposaari wind park.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3565,h_2005,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7102575f3e7272e9d66/1601614472)

Open image viewer

Wind turbines in Reposaari. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

More and more energy is produced in Finland from renewable energy sources. Statistics Finland says that last year already more than half of Finland's electricity was made with renewable energy sources.

Renewable energy sources are, for example, hydropower, wind power and wood.

Hydropower and wind power were used in Finland much more than before. Wood was used less than before.

## Car charging

![Electric car charging point](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-852297613b28f9bf028/1631267167)

Open image viewer

An electric car charges at a charging point. Photo: Seppo Ahava / Yle

Charging points for electric cars are being built in many housing companies. The Real Estate Federation has asked housing companies about this.

More than 40 per cent of housing companies say that they intend to build charging points within 5 years. About 15 per cent of housing companies have already built charging points for electric cars.

The state supports the building of charging points. The intention is to have many charging points for electric cars in Finland. Sales of electric cars have also increased this year.

## Corona situation

![Signs saying corona testing and corona vaccination.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_432/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83470860fa6770086ea/1629100681)

Open image viewer

There are still quite a lot of corona infections. Photo: Yle/ Isabella Lindell

On Tuesday over 800 new corona infections were reported in Finland. Three new corona deaths were reported.

The Institute for Health and Welfare says that the incidence of corona disease has only decreased a little. In 2 weeks there have been almost 7 700 new infections. The number is about 50 fewer than in the previous 2 weeks.

The number of vaccinated people is rising slowly. About 79 per cent of Finns over 12 years old have received 2 corona vaccinations.
