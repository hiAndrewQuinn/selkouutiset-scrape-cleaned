## Delayed degrees

![A student boy on the internet at a school computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3839,h_2160,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-109302564269febd63e7/1680252994)

Students in many fields never graduate and get their degrees done.

In universities and universities of applied sciences there are fields where only about 20 percent graduate in the target time. Some do not graduate soon even after the target time.

At university the target time is exceeded in computing, telecommunications and natural sciences. In computing and telecommunications only just under 24 percent graduate within 7 years.

Target times are exceeded in the same fields also at universities of applied sciences.

The reasons include, for example, quick employment, motivation problems and the difficulty of studies. In addition, many students have to work to make a living.

The level of education in Finland has practically not risen in 20 years.

Finland has fallen behind other industrial countries.

## Gas storage

The gas storage of the European Union countries is at a record full level.

European gas storage was about 57 percent full the day before yesterday.

The amount of gas in storage affects electricity bills: if the storages run low and the price of natural gas begins to rise, the price of electricity rises. The effect is also seen in Finland.

## Carbon sinks

![A red forest machine fells trees in tall pine forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1296,h_729,x_0,y_57/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1083281640ac8c81b977/1678428632)

Open image viewer

Carbon sinks shrink when forest is cut down too much. Photo: Jani Aho / City of Tampere

Finland's forest carbon sink is still on course to become too small. A carbon sink means that, for example, a forest collects and stores carbon dioxide. Carbon dioxide causes the warming of the climate.

Finland has difficulties achieving the European Union's climate obligations. The current obligation period lasts until 2025.

## Reporting channels

![Sign reading: Construction site. Entry forbidden to unauthorised persons.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_125/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1097834643649c74648c/1681279708)

About 20 percent of Finnish companies still do not have a mandatory reporting channel. This is what a survey by the Central Chamber of Commerce says.

At the start of the year a new law came into force. All companies with at least 50 employees must have a reporting channel. Through it employees can report shortcomings or misconduct. The report can be made anonymously and safely. The legislation ensures that reporting causes no trouble for the person reporting.

Two years ago the reporting channel was in use in about half of companies.

The warm and dry spring weather is becoming cooler.

On Friday it is mostly sunny and warm. In the north the weather cools and is cloudier. The temperature varies between 10 and 18 degrees, in the north it is cooler.

Between Sunday and Monday night a rain area will arrive in the central and southern parts of the country. In the Ostrobothnia region it may snow, in the south it rains.
