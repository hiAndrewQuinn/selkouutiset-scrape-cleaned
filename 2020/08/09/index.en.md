# Sunday 9.8.2020 (radio)

Finland has tightened quarantine guidelines. Finns want to get the corona vaccine. There are long queues at the Finnish Immigration Service. On Sunday flags were flown for Tove Jansson and Finnish art. The weather.

TV Clear News has a summer break. It will continue on 17.8.
Radio Clear News is made every day, also in the summer.

## No work or school during quarantine

A passenger arrives in Finland. Two people are there to meet the passengers and give instructions about quarantine. Photo: Arttu Timonen / Yle

Finland has tightened quarantine guidelines for passengers who come to Finland from abroad. The new guidelines say that adults may not go to work during quarantine, and children may not go to school or day care.

If a person comes to Finland from a country that is not recommended for travel, they must be in quarantine for 14 days.

The Communicable Diseases Agency may order a person into official quarantine if the person does not follow the quarantine guidelines.

## Finns want to get the vaccine

![A close-up of a nurse's gloved hand giving a vaccine injection into a man's hairy upper arm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11136428)

Open image viewer

A coronavirus vaccine may be ready this year. Photo: Sabastien Nogier / EPA

Most Finns want to get the coronavirus vaccine. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare has asked Finns whether they will take the vaccine once it is ready.

70 per cent of Finns say that they will take the corona vaccine. If a large part of people take the vaccine, the coronavirus cannot spread.

There is no vaccine yet, but researchers are developing it. The coronavirus vaccine may be ready at the end of this year.

## Long queues at the Finnish Immigration Service

![The door of the Finnish Immigration Service office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10908912)

Open image viewer

The Finnish Immigration Service has offices in different parts of Finland. Photo: Lotta Sundström / Yle

The service of the Finnish Immigration Service is busy. There has been a queue of many months for the Finnish Immigration Service's services because of the corona situation. There are also fewer staff working than before.

Appointments can now be booked 3 months ahead, that is, for November. The situation is hard for many people who are moving to Finland.

For example, you may not get a bank account or home insurance before you register with the Finnish Immigration Service and get a Finnish personal identity code.

## Flags flown for Tove Jansson and art on Sunday

![Tove Jansson](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9942151)

Open image viewer

Tove Jansson was born on 9 August 1914 and died on 27 June 2001. The picture shows Tove Jansson in 1982. Photo: Pekka Elomaa

On Sunday, for the first time, the flag has been flown in Finland for the birthday of the writer and visual artist **Tove Jansson** and for Finnish art.

The Ministry of the Interior has recommended that Finns raise their flags today.

Tove Jansson was a writer, painter and comic artist. She is especially remembered for the Moomins: the Moomin characters and the Moomin books.
