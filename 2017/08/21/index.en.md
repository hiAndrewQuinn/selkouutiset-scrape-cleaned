# Monday 21.8.2017 (radio)

New information about the perpetrator of the Turku stabbing. Because of the Turku case, many politicians are demanding an intelligence law quickly. A total solar eclipse is being seen in North America.

## New information about the main suspect in the Turku stabbing

![The memorial site for the victims of Friday's stabbing attack in Turku, at the Turku market square on 19 August. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9788111)

The police are asking for a total of five perpetrators to be remanded in custody on suspicion of the Turku stabbings. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The police are asking for the main suspect in the Turku stabbings and four other suspects to be remanded in custody. The district court will decide on remand tomorrow.

The main suspect in the stabbings is 18-year-old Moroccan Abderrahman Mechkah. He had applied for asylum in Finland but did not get it. After that he had appealed against the negative decision.

Ten people were stabbed in Turku on Friday. Two of them died. Two victims are still in intensive care in hospital.

## The Security Intelligence Service had received a tip

![The Finnish Security Intelligence Service, or Supo ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9536170)

Open image viewer

The Security Intelligence Service had received a tip about the main suspect in the Turku stabbing. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The Security Intelligence Service says that it received a tip about the main suspect earlier. According to the tip, the man seemed radicalised and he was said to be enthusiastic about extremist thinking. However, the tip did not include information about any plan.

The Security Intelligence Service says that it receives a lot of similar tips. All tips are checked in order of importance.

## Politicians are pushing for an intelligence law

![A server.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9183747)

Open image viewer

It is believed that an intelligence law will help in preventing violent attacks. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Many politicians are now demanding that Finland must quickly get a new intelligence law. An intelligence law means that the Security Intelligence Service and the Defence Forces can examine, for example, which country someone in Finland calls or sends email to.

The argument for the intelligence law is that intelligence may help to prevent, for example, terrorist attacks. Because of the intelligence law, Finland's constitution must also be changed. That is why dealing with the intelligence law takes a long time. In Finland, Parliament decides on changing laws.

## The sun goes dark in North America

![A total solar eclipse](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9789377)

This is what a total solar eclipse looked like in Indonesia in 2016. Photo: EPA/ANDRE ADRIAN

A total solar eclipse is happening right now in North America. The solar eclipse is the widest in a hundred years. A solar eclipse happens when the Moon passes between the Sun and the Earth.
