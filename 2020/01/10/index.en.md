## SDP's and the Centre Party's support rose

In the January support poll, the SDP and the Centre Party gained more support.

Yle's January party support poll shows that support for the SDP and the Centre Party has risen. That means the main parties of the government now have a little more support.

In the support poll the SDP and the Centre Party are in 3rd and 4th place.

In the support poll the most popular party is the Finns Party. It is an opposition party. In the poll the second most popular party is the National Coalition Party. It is also in opposition.

## Finland is trying out fighter jets

![Eurofighter plan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5018995b69f4d4850b7/1533670702)

Open image viewer

The Eurofighter Typhoon is a joint European fighter. Germany, Britain and Italy make fighters together. Photo: Peter Brogden

Finland intends to buy new fighter jets. The Finnish Air Force is now trying out different fighters.

There are 5 options. Two of them are American, one is French, one is Swedish and one is a joint European fighter.

Air Force fighter pilots fly each manufacturer's fighter for about a week.

First the pilots try the joint European Eurofighter fighter. Germany, Britain and Italy make them together.

The Finnish government will decide next year which fighter will be chosen.

Canada's Prime Minister says that it may have been a mistake. Canada thinks the matter must be investigated carefully.

## Peru protects an Inca city

![Machu Picchu, Inca city in Peru](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11150717)

Open image viewer

Machu Picchu is an ancient Inca city in Peru. Photo: EPA / Ernesto Arias

South American Peru intends to plant a million trees.

The trees will be planted at Machu Picchu, an ancient Inca city. It is a world-famous sight.

Peru believes the trees will protect the area from bushfires and mudslides.

Heavy rain, bushfires and drought are wearing away the historic area and its structures.
