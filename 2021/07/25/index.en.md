## Bus accident in Croatia

There has been a serious bus accident in Croatia. 10 people died in the accident. There are 45 injured.

In the accident the bus drove off the motorway and overturned. There were about 70 people on the bus.

The bus came from Germany, and it was on its way to Serbia and Kosovo. A lot of Kosovars live in Germany.

## Storm in China

![Meteorological map showing the typhoon making landfall on the east coast](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1024,h_576,x_0,y_128/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83525360fd4cb74b45c/1627213075)

Open image viewer

Meteorological map of Typhoon In-Fa on the coast of China. Photo: NASA Earth Observing System Data and Information System

A storm has hit the east coast of China. The storm has been named In-Fa, and it is a tropical hurricane, that is, a typhoon.

The typhoon has brought heavy rain and strong wind. The storm area includes, for example, Shanghai, China's biggest city.

Traffic was stopped in the storm area. Ships, aeroplanes and trains have not been running.

In addition, outdoor events were cancelled. The authorities asked people to stay indoors during the storm.

The typhoon also brings rain to Central China. Rain is falling in the same area where it rained heavily earlier. Previous water damage is still being repaired in the area.

Actor and theatre director Anu Panula has died. Panula was 64 years old. She lived in Tampere.

Panula founded the La Strada theatre in 2009. It is Finland's first theatre for people with developmental disabilities. La Strada operates in Tampere.

## Record-hot summer

![People playing beach volleyball.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_258/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83176960eed2203adfa/1626264876)

Open image viewer

People enjoyed the summer at the Matinkylä beach in Espoo on 14.7.2021. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The Finnish summer is hot. At many measuring stations it is now the hottest summer in over 100 years.

For example, in Kaisaniemi in Helsinki such high average temperatures have never been measured before as now. Measurements in Kaisaniemi began already in 1882.

This summer the longest heatwave in Finland has also been measured. In Anjala in Kouvola there was a heatwave for 31 days in a row. In a heatwave the temperature is over 25 degrees.
