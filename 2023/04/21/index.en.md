# Radio Clear News Friday 21.4.2023

Meeting on helping Ukraine. Finland's weapons aid to Ukraine. Cleaning robots. Foreign upper secondary students. Eid al-Fitr celebration. The weather.

A meeting has been held in Germany where arms aid for Ukraine has been discussed. More than 50 countries took part in the meeting.

US Defence Minister Lloyd Austin opened the meeting. He said that support for Ukraine will not end.

Austin said that an important theme of the meeting is, for example, supporting Ukraine's air defence.

## Arms aid

![A conscript on field exercises at Niinisalo in June 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_653/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81483260b77cc412c0d/1622637856)

Finland helps Ukraine with weapons and other defence equipment. Photo: Kasper Heimolehto/Yle

Finland is giving more weapons aid to Ukraine. The value of the new aid package is 78 million euros.

Finland is sending defence equipment for the training of Ukrainian soldiers. The European Union is training Ukrainian soldiers in Poland.

This is the 15th time that Finland has given defence equipment to Ukraine. The total value of Finland's aid packages is 990 million euros.

## Cleaning robots

![A cleaning robot moves along a shop aisle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4618,h_2593,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1100517643e5aca8b0eb/1681808519)

Open image viewer

A cleaning robot washes the floor. Photo: Johanna Lehtola / Yle

A cleaner's work is changing. In the future a cleaner's work mate is often a robot.

Cleaning robots are already in use, for example, in factory halls and in some shops. There the robots clean floors.

A cleaner has time to do more difficult tasks when a robot washes the floor. That is important, because there are too few cleaners.

In the coming years the shortage of cleaners will only get worse, as many cleaners retire.

## Upper secondary students

![A foreign-language short-syllabus matriculation exam was held in the gym of Kallio upper secondary school in Helsinki on 18 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7855856053424f1656c/1653213365)

Open image viewer

Upper secondary students in the matriculation exam. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Upper secondary schools in small municipalities have also taken in students from abroad.

Last autumn about 140 foreign students started their studies at one of Finland's small upper secondary schools. Most of the students came from Asia.

The students have learned Finnish already before they came to Finland.

In the future the number of foreign students will grow. It is hoped that they will stay in Finland to work after their studies.

## Eid al-Fitr celebration

![Pastries for Eid.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_125/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-948792626eb628b387d/1651422846)

Open image viewer

Eid al-Fitr celebration includes good food. Photo: John Palmen

The holy month of Ramadan for Muslims ends today, on Friday.

During Ramadan Muslims have fasted, that is, they have not eaten or drunk during daylight hours.

Ramadan ends with the Eid al-Fitr celebration. The celebration can last 3 days.

Muslims celebrate Eid al-Fitr together with relatives and friends. During the celebration children often receive gifts.

## The weather

![Weather map for Saturday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11026186442a875ad541/1682090196)

On Saturday in the southern and central parts of the country it is sunny and warm. The temperature varies between 12 and 18 degrees.

In the north it is colder and in places a little snow falls.

On Sunday the weather gets colder. In the northern and central parts of the country the temperature is between 0 and +5 degrees, in the southern part between +5 and +10 degrees.
