# Tuesday 21.1.2017 (radio)

Success in the Pisa study. A new name for the maternity package. Young people see the future as dark. Training for assistants of disabled people. Tickets for Cheek's concert ran out at once.

## Pupils are good at solving problems

![A schoolchild does homework.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9659468)

Open image viewer

Finnish pupils did well in the Pisa study again. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finland has placed among the ten best in the Pisa study, which measures success at school.

The Pisa study measures pupils' skills in mathematics and reading, among other things, and in solving problems.

Finnish young people placed seventh in the new study. So they have good conversation skills in situations that involve problems. Schoolchildren from many countries took part in the study.

South Korea, Canada and Estonia, among others, placed better than Finland.

## The maternity package may get a new name

![Maternity package 2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9494505)

Open image viewer

The contents of the 2017 maternity package. Photo: Kela

Kela, the Social Insurance Institution of Finland, has started a campaign to look for a name for the maternity package. The maternity package contains baby clothes and care items. Every mother gets it for free.

Kela is considering whether the name of the maternity package should take into account all families better than before, and whether the current name discriminates against fathers.

The name of the maternity package is written into law. Parliament decides on changing the name.

Name suggestions can be sent until the end of the year.

## For young people, Finland's future is dark

![The legs of two young women.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9642223)

Open image viewer

Young people's own future looks good. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Young people see Finland's future as dark. This is revealed by a fresh study.

According to the study, only about 20 percent of young people view Finland's future optimistically. About 200 young people took part in the study.

However, the study revealed that most young people see their own future as good. The results of the study are used to try to influence decision-makers and how society develops.

## Personal assistants will get training

![Disability symbol.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9087637)

Open image viewer

There are about 25 000 personal assistants in Finland. Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

Personal assistants of disabled people will get their own professional training. The qualification will come into force at the start of next year.

After this, personal assistant will be a protected job title. This means that only people who have completed the qualification may use the title.

About 25 thousand personal assistants work in Finland.

## Cheek's gigs sold out at once

![Cheek at the press conference on 15.11.2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9932516)

Open image viewer

Cheek is stopping in style. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

Tickets for rap artist Cheek's final concert sold out in less than a minute in the morning. The organiser announced a second concert straight away, because demand for tickets was high. Tickets for the second concert have also already sold out.

Cheek will perform for the last time in Lahti next August. Cheek said last week that he is stopping. According to Cheek, stopping was the only way to get rid of the stress.
