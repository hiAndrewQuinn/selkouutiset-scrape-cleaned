# Friday 9.3.2018 (radio)

In different parts of the world, people are pleased that the leaders of North Korea and the USA plan to meet. The Turku knife attacker understood what he did. In Finland, parents help children with homework less than in many other countries. The Paralympics have begun in South Korea. There is nice weather for outdoor activities at the weekend.

## Trump and Kim will meet

![People watch a television broadcast in Seoul, South Korea, reporting that North Korean leader Kim Jong-un has sent an invitation to a meeting to US President Donald Trump through South Korea.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10108654)

Around the world, people are pleased that the leaders of North Korea and the USA plan to meet. For example, the European Union and China say that the meeting is a positive thing.

North Korean leader Kim Jong-un invited US President Donald Trump to a meeting. Trump accepted the invitation. The leaders will meet during the spring.

Trump and Kim plan to talk about nuclear weapons. The USA is angry with North Korea because North Korea has done missile tests. The missiles can carry nuclear weapons over long distances.

North Korea has promised to stop the missile tests so that the meeting can succeed.

## The Turku knife attacker understood his act

![A picture of the Turku knife attacker on 18 August 2017. Police officers have surrounded a man lying on the ground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10108919)

The authorities have given more information about the Turku stabbing. A Moroccan man is accused of the stabbing.

The court now says that the man was criminally responsible. That means that the man understood what he did.

The Turku stabbing happened last summer. Then Abderrahman Bouanane stabbed 10 people, 2 of whom died. Bouanane is now accused of murders.

## Parents' help with homework

![A schoolchild doing homework.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9659468)

Open image viewer

Finnish children often want to do their homework without their parents' help. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

In Finland, parents help children with homework only a little. Parents spend less time on children's homework than in many other countries.

The information is from a new study that included 29 countries.

Only 5 per cent of Finnish parents help children with homework for at least an hour. That was the least of all the countries in the study.

The study also says that in Finland there are many children who do not even want help from their parents with homework.

## The Paralympics began

![Finland's Paralympic team at the opening ceremony.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10110668)

Open image viewer

Finland's team at the opening ceremony of the Winter Paralympics. Photo: EPA

The Paralympics have begun. The Paralympics are the Olympics for disabled athletes. Now it is the turn of winter sports.

The Winter Paralympics are held in Pyeongchang, South Korea. The Winter Olympics were also held in the same place in February.

The opening ceremony of the Winter Paralympics was on Friday. Snowboarder Matti Suur-Hamari carried Finland's flag at the opening ceremony.

A total of 13 Finnish athletes are taking part in the Winter Paralympics.
