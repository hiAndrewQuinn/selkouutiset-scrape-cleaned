## No evidence found of President Trump's plotting with Russia

![Special prosecutor Robert Mueller.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10638614)

Open image viewer

Special prosecutor Robert Mueller. Photo: Michael Reynolds / EPA

No evidence has been found of cooperation between US President Donald Trump and Russia. This is what the report of special prosecutor Robert Mueller says. Attorney General William Barr has presented the report to Congress.

President Trump is satisfied with the report. According to Trump, the report clears him of all charges.

The US Democratic Party disagrees. The Democrats say that the attorney general is not impartial.

## The military junta is strong in the Thai election

![Phalang Pracharat party](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10704801)

Open image viewer

The party supporting Thailand's military junta is about to win the parliamentary elections. Photo: Lillian Suwanrumpha / AFP

Parliamentary elections have been held in Thailand. Thailand is ruled by a military junta, and the party supporting it probably won the election.

More than 90 percent of the votes have been counted, and the party leads by about half a million votes.

Prime minister will probably continue to be General Prayuth Chan-ocha. Opinion polls predicted that the opposition would win the election.

Thailand's previous parliamentary elections were eight years ago.

## A passenger plane had to land on a Pacific island

![Map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10492092)

A passenger plane that was on its way to the USA had to land in New Caledonia. Photo: Yle News Graphics

A passenger plane has had to land on a Pacific island because smoke came from its cockpit. The plane landed at the airport of the capital of New Caledonia.

The plane was on its way from Melbourne in Australia to Los Angeles in the USA. There were 256 passengers on board.

The passengers got out of the plane safely.

## Income level affects voter turnout

![Election flag](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10704928)

Open image viewer

People with high pay vote more than people who earn little. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

People with high pay vote more than people who earn little.

A University of Helsinki study says that in the last parliamentary elections, those doing physical work and those on low incomes voted the least. The people who voted the most were those with high incomes and highly educated people.

For example, only just over half of waiters voted, while the voter turnout of doctors was over 90 percent.

## Mako sharks are threatened with extinction

![Cartoon picture of a slender shark.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10705267)

Open image viewer

A mako shark can grow 4 metres long and weigh 600 kilos. Photo: NOAA

The fastest shark in the world is threatened with extinction. The number of mako sharks in the Atlantic has fallen by 60 percent in 75 years. A mako shark can swim at up to 75 kilometres an hour.

Mako sharks are fished in many countries for their meat, and also just for their fins. However, no upper limit has been set for mako shark fishing.

Of all the 58 known shark and ray species in the world, 17 are at risk of becoming extinct. Sharks and rays are close relatives.
