## Job search

The first news item is about job searching.

Unemployed people will have a new obligation. The unemployed person must make a short introduction online. In the introduction, the job seeker tells who they are and what kind of skills they have.

The presentation must be on the authority's website from the beginning of September. Only a small number of unemployed people have made the presentation.

The purpose of the presentation is to make it easier for employers to find good employees than before.

Unemployed job seekers will now have to submit an online introduction. Job seekers will not be penalized if they fail to submit an introduction.

## Eppu Normal

Next up, music news.

The band Eppu Normaali will play their last gig this weekend. Eppu Normaali was founded in Ylöjärvi 50 years ago. Eppu Normaali is one of the most popular rock bands in Finland. The band has sold over 1.5 million records.

The members of Eppu Normaal announced last fall that the band would stop touring.

The band Eppu Normaali will play their last gig in Tampere this weekend. The band's last gig will be on Saturday at Ratina Stadium.
