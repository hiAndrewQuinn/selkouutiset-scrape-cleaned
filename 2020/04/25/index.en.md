## The corona epidemic will continue for a long time

![German doctor taking a corona test at a drive-in testing station in Halle on 27 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11315161)

Now everyone must go for a corona test if they have any kind of symptoms. Photo: Ronny Hartmann / AFP

Health authorities say that the coronavirus will spread in Finland for a long time yet.

The peak of the corona epidemic is still ahead. That means that there is still a long time until the number of coronavirus infections starts to fall in Finland.

Health authorities say that if symptoms appear, everyone must go for a corona test. You can go for a test even if the symptoms are mild.

The health authorities have changed their strategy.

Earlier it was thought that the best way to keep the epidemic under control was to restrict people's movement.

Now it is thought that it is best to test as many people as possible.

When it is known how many people have already got the virus, it is easier to lift the restrictions of everyday life.

## How does the corona affect men and women?

![A waiter attaches a notice to a restaurant window saying it has closed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4288,h_2412,x_0,y_221/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6681095ea2fc749b714/1587740022)

Open image viewer

The closing of restaurants affects women's jobs. Photo: Martin Divisek / EPA

The coronavirus affects women's and men's lives in different ways.

In terms of health, the biggest difference is that a man who has got the coronavirus is more likely to die than a woman who has got it. Researchers say that there may be many reasons for this.

In terms of the economy, the biggest difference is that the coronavirus affects jobs where there are many women.

For example, many women work in restaurants, hotels and shops.

Now those businesses are doing badly, and many women may lose their jobs.

## Finns have faith in their own finances

![People enjoying the weather in Haukilahti, Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4041,h_2273,x_0,y_423/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6639355e944c65b14d9/1586779388)

Open image viewer

Finns trust their own finances, even though the coronavirus is affecting many things in the economy too. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Life now looks bleak because of the coronavirus.

However, Finns trust their own finances and their future.

Yle asked Finns whether they are worried about losing their job or their income.

More than half of the people answered in the survey that they are only a little worried or not worried at all about their own finances.

Almost 1,500 people answered the survey. According to the survey, women are more worried about their own finances than men.

Only a small share of the respondents had lost their job.

Tomorrow, Sunday, there will be dry weather in Southern Finland.

In Northern and Eastern Finland there may be snow showers.

The wind will weaken. During the day the temperature will be between 5 and 8 degrees.

At night there will be frost.
