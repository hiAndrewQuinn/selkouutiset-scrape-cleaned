## Strike at ports

![In the foreground a man, below whom cargo containers can be seen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689136)

Open image viewer

Goods do not move in ports when the dock workers are on strike. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

The strike at Finnish ports has continued on Monday. The strike stopped the freight traffic of the ports last week.

The dock workers are on strike. During the strike the workers do not work, and the dock workers' job is to load goods onto ships and unload goods from ships.

The reason for the strike is a pay dispute. On Sunday a compromise proposal was made, but according to the dock workers the proposal did not promise a big enough pay rise. That is why the strike in ports continues.

The dock workers' strike will be negotiated again next Wednesday. If freight traffic is cut off for a long time, fruit, for example, may run out of shops.

## Clear Stories

![Drawing of two children.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689132)

Open image viewer

Photo: Niina Väänänen

Yle's children's programme Pikku Kakkonen has made stories in clear Finnish. The Clear Stories will come to Yle Areena on 10 March.

Riikka Tuohimetsä and Nora Lehtinen have written 10 clear stories. They tell about ordinary things, for example about friends, games and the birth of a little sister.

The Clear Stories suit everyone for whom ordinary stories are difficult. Clear Stories can be listened to, for example, in families where Finnish is being studied.

The narrator of the Clear Stories is actress Riikka Papunen. Papunen reads the stories slowly and calmly.

## Bears

![A dead bear and a man.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689137)

Open image viewer

A special permit is needed to hunt a bear. Photo: Ari Haimakainen / Yle

There are fewer bears in Finland than before.

The Natural Resources Institute estimates that there are at most about 1,900 bears in Finland. That means there are now 20 percent fewer bears than last year.

The Natural Resources Institute says that bears have been hunted more in recent years than before.

The bear is a protected animal, and that is why a special permit is needed to hunt a bear. The bear hunting season is in autumn.

## The weather

![Weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689138)

On Tuesday it is dry all over the country, that is, it does not rain.

In the north, the east and on the west coast the sun shines. In the south and Central Finland it is cloudy.

In the east and north there is frost of 10-18 degrees, in the south and west 5-10 degrees.

Wednesday also becomes a frosty day. On Wednesday afternoon there is frost of between 10 and 20 degrees in a large part of the country.
