# Monday 11.1.2021 (radio)

29 new mutated coronavirus infections have been found in Finland. Corona tests at fur farms are being expanded. A new rapid corona test was developed in Estonia. VR has reduced train services. A snowstorm makes traffic difficult.

## Dozens of the new virus variant are already in Finland

29 new coronavirus infections caused by the mutated coronavirus have been found in Finland.

In total, 49 such infections have been reported.

18 have been found in people who have come to Finland. 8 got the virus from them in close contact. The origin of 3 cases is being investigated.

Of the cases, only 2 are the South African virus type and the rest are the British virus type.

The mutated coronavirus can spread faster than before.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare recommends that passengers from Britain, Ireland and South Africa to Finland are tested twice.

## Fur farms are being examined

src="https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5287872"

Open image viewer

Mink cages.

Intensive corona testing at fur farms is expanding in Finland.

In December corona tests started at all mink farms. There are about 200 farms.

During the early part of the year, testing will also start at raccoon dog farms. There are about 100 of them.

Corona outbreaks have been found on mink farms in the Netherlands and Denmark.

Most of Finland's fur farms are located in the Ostrobothnia regions.

## A new rapid corona test was developed in Estonia

![New type of corona test developed at Tallinn University of Technology](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2048,h_1152,x_0,y_157/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7602475ff83753cdd91/1610102824)

Open image viewer

The Estonian rapid test also uses a smartphone. Photo: Jan Erik Kooli / Yle

In Estonia a new type of rapid test has been developed for corona testing. It gives a result in 15 minutes.

The test also shows how much virus there is in the sample.

The test uses, among other things, artificial material and a sample solution. They are connected to a smartphone with the help of a small add-on device.

The test was developed by Tallinn University of Technology. The test is still waiting for approval from the Estonian health authority.

## VR cut weekday trains

![Tampere railway station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6991705f0dae8725054/1594732282)

Open image viewer

Weekday timetables changed on Monday morning. Photo: Miikka Varila / Yle

The railway company VR has reduced train services because of small numbers of passengers.

Most services were cut between Helsinki and Tampere. The interval between trains grew to 2 hours in the morning and in the evening.

The cut applies to weekdays from Monday to Thursday. The trains between Helsinki and Turku will also be reduced.

The cuts do not apply to commuter traffic in Southern Finland.

VR says that it can quickly add trains if the number of passengers grows.

## Heavy snow is falling, and driving conditions are bad

![A car behind a wet windscreen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10647554)

Open image viewer

Driving conditions are really bad in the coming days. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The snowfall area arrives in South-West Finland late at night. It spreads to Southern and Western Finland too.

All through Tuesday and even on Wednesday there will be strong snow drifts. The wind between east and south-east is strong.

Traffic weather is even very bad in the rain area. For example, the Helsinki rescue service urges people to avoid driving in the city in the coming days.

Elsewhere in the country too, driving conditions are bad.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that the snowfall at the start of the week is exceptionally heavy. Bending trees may cause power cuts.
