## Accident in Italy

![A cable car crashed to the ground near the peak of Mottarone, and it was very high above the ground when it fell.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11944997)

Open image viewer

A cable car fell to the ground in Italy. Photo: EPA

A serious accident has happened in Italy. The accident happened on Sunday near the Alps mountains.

More than 10 people died when a cable car fell to the ground. The cable car route runs between the city of Stresa and Mount Mottarone.

The cable car was high in the air when it fell to the ground.

The cause of the accident is not yet known.

The ice hockey world championships began this weekend. The ice hockey world championships are in Latvia.

Finland has now played 2 games. On Saturday Finland beat the USA 2–1.

On Sunday Finland lost to Kazakhstan. Kazakhstan beat Finland 2–1.

The ice hockey world championships continue for another 2 weeks.

## Clear Speaker of the Year

The Clear Speaker of the Year award winner will be revealed in the coming week. The award is given for work done for clear language.

The organiser of the competition is the Clear Language Advisory Board. The board's panel has chosen 3 candidates for the final competition.

We will now tell you why the panel chose these candidates.

![People studying nursing around a patient bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11944894)

Open image viewer

Soilimaria Korhonen (in the middle) teaches clear-language communication to social and health care students Marika Suvinen and Mira Flang. The patient actor is Iasmina Robot. Photo: Jade Jimenez

The first candidate is Soilimaria Korhonen. Korhonen is a teacher at Savonia University of Applied Sciences.

Korhonen has brought clear-language studies to the social and health sectors. For example, nursing students at Savonia learn to serve customers better with the help of clear language.

The second candidate is the National Institute for Health and Welfare, THL. THL also gives information about corona in clear language. The THL website has correct information that everyone can understand.

![Woman at a desk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11195128)

Open image viewer

Ann-Lis Fredriksson reads the news in Clear Swedish. Photo: Yle

The third candidate is Yle News in plain Swedish. Listeners can hear the news in easy Swedish on the radio and online. Yle News in plain Swedish is an important news programme, for example, for people with intellectual disabilities and older people who speak Swedish.

Next Friday it will be revealed which of the final candidates wins the Clear Speaker of the Year award.
