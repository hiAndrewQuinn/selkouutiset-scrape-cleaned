# Friday 27.3.2020 (radio)

The movement restrictions in Uusimaa affect conscripts' leave. More than 1,000 coronavirus cases have been found in Finland. Nokian Tyres is laying off more than 1,600 people. The British Prime Minister, Boris Johnson, has been found to have the coronavirus.

## Closing Uusimaa and the conscripts

![Conscripts in their barracks room.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11256361)

Open image viewer

Recruits who started in January, in their barracks room in Helsinki. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The borders of Uusimaa may close at midnight or on Saturday.

The movement restrictions in Uusimaa prevent conscripts from getting home on leave.

Conscripts may not travel from Uusimaa on leave to other parts of Finland. Conscripts who live in Uusimaa also may not travel home from elsewhere in Finland.

For example, at the Dragsvik garrison in Uusimaa there are hundreds of conscripts whose home is in Ostrobothnia. Dragsvik has the only Swedish-speaking unit in Finland. About 800 conscripts serve at the garrison.

Other activities will be arranged for conscripts who cannot spend their leave at home. They will be isolated from the other people at the garrison.

## More than 1,000 coronavirus cases in Finland

![Emergency department of the Kuopio University Hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_151/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6559145e74b6cb9606d/1584711681)

The emergency department of the Kuopio University Hospital. Photo: Anna Ronkainen / Yle

By Friday morning, a total of 1,025 COVID-19 cases had been found in Finland. Most of the cases found in Finland have been mild. There are many more cases in Uusimaa than elsewhere in Finland.

There are 108 patients in Finland right now who need hospital care. Of them, 32 are in intensive care.

7 people have died of the coronavirus in Finland.

## Nokian Tyres is laying off

![Studded tyres, Nokian Hakkapeliitta 7.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9229114)

Open image viewer

The co-operation negotiations on Nokian tyres have been concluded. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Nokian Tyres is laying off more than 1,600 people because of the effects of the coronavirus. The layoffs will be carried out during this year.

The length of the layoff varies, but it is at most 90 days.

The reason for the layoffs is the uncertainty in the tyre market caused by the coronavirus pandemic.

## Sweden tightens the ban on gatherings

![Press conference.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11274728)

Open image viewer

The public health authority holds a press conference on the coronavirus situation in Sweden every weekday. Photo: Daniel Ivarsson / Yle

Sweden is banning gatherings of more than 50 people because of the coronavirus. The ban comes into force on Sunday. Until now, gatherings of more than 500 people were banned in Sweden.

If the ban is broken, the consequence can be a fine or even up to half a year in prison.

## Boris Johnson has the coronavirus

![Boris Johnson](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11163854)

Open image viewer

British Prime Minister Boris Johnson. Photo: Chine Nouvelle / AOP

British Prime Minister Boris Johnson has been found to have the coronavirus. He announced this on Twitter.

Boris Johnson says that he has had mild symptoms. His coronavirus test was positive.

Johnson is now in isolation. He still plans to lead the British government.
