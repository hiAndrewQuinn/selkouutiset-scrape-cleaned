# Tuesday 6.12.2016 (radio)

Finland celebrates its independence. The police are monitoring demonstrations. Over 1,000 bags of food were handed out to the poor. Finnish young people's science skills are getting weaker.

## Finland celebrates Independence Day

![The Palace reception's offerings from last year.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9323665)

Open image viewer

The Palace reception is the highlight of Independence Day. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Finland is celebrating its independence today. Finland has been an independent state for 99 years. The celebration began with the traditional flag raising on Tähtitorninmäki hill in Helsinki. During the day, a national military parade was also held in Kajaani. 1300 soldiers and 90 vehicles took part in the parade.

The highlight of Independence Day is the President of the Republic's reception at the Presidential Palace. Every year the Palace reception gathers a record-sized audience in front of Yle's television broadcast.

On Independence Day, many Finns have also received a decoration from the president.

## Many demonstrations in Helsinki

![Members of the Nordic Resistance Movement in Hakaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9337135)

Open image viewer

The Nordic Resistance Movement's demonstration march. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Not everyone is celebrating Finland's independence in a good mood. Many demonstrations have been held in Helsinki. There are at least 10 demonstrations.

The far-right march set off from Hakaniemi in the afternoon and about 200 people are taking part in it. 2000 people are taking part in the counter-demonstration of anti-fascists and anarchists.

The police have prepared for rioting. Extra police officers have been brought to Helsinki from elsewhere in Finland. In total, there are hundreds of police officers keeping order on the streets and squares.

Last year the police arrested over 130 demonstrators.

## Food was handed out to the poor

![Heikki Hursti's Independence Day celebration in Hakaniemi, Helsinki. The picture shows a food queue.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9337129)

Open image viewer

Over 1000 people got a bag of food in Helsinki. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

The poor have had their own independence celebration in Helsinki. Charity worker Heikki Hursti has handed out over 1,000 bags of food to the poor. Hursti says that the situation of poor people in Finland has become worse than before. In the food queue there were many people who said that their money is not enough for food.

Heikki Hursti and his parents have handed out food to the poor for a long time.

## Young people's science skills are getting weaker

![Children doing maths homework.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9322985)

Open image viewer

Finnish young people are no longer as good at science. Photo: Markku Ojala / AOP

Finnish young people's success at school is still falling. The new PISA school study says that 15-year-olds' skills have fallen a lot in science. The result has fallen because now there are many more pupils who are really bad at science.

The researchers are worried, because girls are clearly better than boys at science. However, boys apply to technology and science fields, even if girls are better than them.

In maths, skills have stayed the same. In reading, Finnish young people are still among the best in the world.

Finland is the fifth (5th) best when all the results are added together. The result is the worst ever for as long as the PISA study has been done. Over 70 countries take part in the study.
