# Saturday 23.9.2017 (radio)

In Finland there is debate about whether the president must have been born a Finnish citizen. Laura Huhtasaari is the Finns Party's presidential candidate. Germany has a parliamentary election on Sunday. Immigrant children learn to read and write as well as Finnish children. The weather is warm.

## The president's birth requirement

![The Presidential Palace and the Cathedral.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9641821)

Open image viewer

Today you can become president only if you were born a Finnish citizen. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

In Finland there is debate about whether the president's birth requirement should be removed. The birth requirement means that only a person who was born a Finnish citizen can become president.

Many think that the requirement discriminates against immigrants. For example, politicians such as Nasima Razmyar or Ozan Yanar cannot run for president because they have an immigrant background. They were not Finnish citizens when they were born.

Removing the president's birth requirement has been proposed by MP Pilvi Torsti. Many others also think that the requirement is old-fashioned. The matter is decided by Finland's parliament, the Eduskunta.

## The Finns Party's presidential candidate

![Laura Huhtasaari](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9848199)

Open image viewer

Laura Huhtasaari is the Finns Party's presidential candidate. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The Finns Party has decided who is the party's presidential candidate. The candidate is Laura Huhtasaari.

Huhtasaari is the vice-chair of the Finns Party. Huhtasaari does not want many immigrants to come to Finland. She also opposes Islam.

The presidential election will be held in January. There are many candidates in the election.

The current president Sauli Niinistö also wants to continue in the president's job. Opinion polls predict that Finns will re-elect Niinistö as president.

## Election in Germany

![German Chancellor Angela Merkel spoke to the press at the end of the G20 meeting in Hamburg on 8 July.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9830260)

Open image viewer

German Chancellor Angela Merkel. Photo: Daniel Kopatsch / EPA

Germany has a parliamentary election on Sunday.

Opinion polls predict that the Christian Democrats will win the election. The leader of the Christian Democrats is Angela Merkel.

Merkel is also the Chancellor, which means Germany's political leader. If the Christian Democrats win the election, Merkel will continue as Chancellor.

The German election also interests other European countries, because Germany is the largest country in the European Union.

## Learning to read and write

![A Syrian family.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9841636)

Open image viewer

Parents Salah Bitar and Marina Bsis, children Muhammed Bitar and Zamzam Bitar. Photo: Janne Ahjopalo / Yle

Immigrant children learn to read and write as well as Finnish children. This has been studied by Riitta Sikiö.

Sikiö says that immigrant children learn better if their parents encourage them.

In Joensuu, Yle met the Syrian Bitar family, in which the children are encouraged to learn. The whole family is learning Finnish together. The parents go to Finnish courses and the children are at school.

It is fun that we are learning the same things, says the mother of the family, Marina Bsis.
