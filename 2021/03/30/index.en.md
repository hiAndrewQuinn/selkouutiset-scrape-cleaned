# Tuesday 30.3.2021 (radio)

Corona vaccines effectively reduce corona infections. In a week's time, more than a million Finns will already have received the corona vaccine. House prices are moving in different directions in different parts of Finland. The Suez Canal is open again. On Wednesday the sun will shine in the south and it will snow in Lapland.

## How well corona vaccines work

![Pfizer-BioNTech (Comirnaty) corona vaccine in Lappeenranta, 17.3.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4073,h_2291,x_0,y_153/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7853036051f55067f11/1619015655)

Corona vaccines give protection against the corona disease. Photo: Mikko Savolainen / Yle

There is new information about corona vaccines. A new study says that corona vaccines effectively reduce corona infections.

An American study included healthcare workers who received the corona vaccine. The first vaccine prevented 80 percent of corona infections, and the second vaccine prevented 90 percent of corona infections.

The corona vaccines also gave protection against variants of the coronavirus. That is good news, because the variants spread easily and have quickly spread across the world.

## Corona figures in Finland

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare has reported that there are 4 new corona deaths in Finland. A total of 826 people have died of corona in Finland.

There are 420 new corona infections. About 77,000 corona infections have been found in Finland in total.

Corona vaccinations are progressing in Finland. About 860,000 Finns have been vaccinated against corona at least once.

Mia Kontio, an expert at the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, says that a large number of corona vaccines will arrive in Finland this week.

Kontio believes that in a week's time, more than a million Finns will already have received the corona vaccine.

House prices are moving in different directions in different parts of Finland.

House prices are rising in big cities. In the Helsinki area, flats were 3.4 percent more expensive in February than a year earlier. House prices also rose in Tampere, Oulu and Turku.

Elsewhere in Finland, house prices have fallen a little.

## The Suez Canal

![Rescue operation to free the ship at Suez continues with the help of dredgers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11858862)

Open image viewer

Traffic stopped in the Suez Canal when a large ship got stuck in the canal. Photo: EPA

The Suez Canal is open again. However, there is congestion on the canal. Many ships are waiting to be able to pass through the canal.

The cause of the congestion is an accident that happened a week ago. Then a ship more than 400 metres long got stuck in the Suez Canal. That is why other ships could not get into the canal.

On Monday the ship was freed, and traffic is working again.
