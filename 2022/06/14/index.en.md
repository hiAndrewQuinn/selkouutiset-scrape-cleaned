## Rising prices

![Customer shopping in a grocery store.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11568170)

Open image viewer

Prices are rising in grocery stores too. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Prices are rising fast.

In May, prices were 7 percent higher than a year ago. Still in April, the rise in prices, that is inflation, was under 6 percent.

The last time prices rose this fast was about 30 years ago.

Especially petrol has become much more expensive. The reason is Russia's war in Ukraine.

Inflation already affects household finances. For example, electricity and food are now clearly more expensive than a year ago. Russia's war in Ukraine also raises the price of food.

Summer's sowing work is done.

Farmers have already sown almost all cereals and other crops. Barley and rapeseed are still being sown in part of Finland.

Cool weather has slowed down farmers' work. For example, cereal grows a little later than usual now. The difference is about a week.

## Girls' Lions' World Championship bronze

![The Girls' Lions celebrate bronze.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2400,h_1350,x_0,y_159/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96953862a8176ca91de/1655183283)

Open image viewer

The Girls' Lions celebrate World Championship bronze. Photo: Lehtikuva

Finland won bronze in the girls' ice hockey World Championships. The under-18 women's world championships were played in the USA.

Finland beat Sweden in the bronze medal game. The game ended 3-0. The hero of the game was Sanni Vanhanen. She scored all of Finland's goals.

Finland's goalkeeper Emilia Kyrkkö also played well. She did not let the puck into Finland's goal even once. Kyrkkö was chosen as the best goalkeeper of the World Championships.

So Finland finished third in the tournament. Gold went to Canada and the USA got silver.

This was the Girls' Lions' third World Championship bronze medal. The previous bronze medals came in 2011 and 2019.
