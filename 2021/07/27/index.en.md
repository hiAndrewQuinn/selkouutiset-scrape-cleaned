## Employment improves

![A family at a restaurant table. A waiter sets a dish on the table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3072,h_1728,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83550660fea5cdd9b72/1627301402)

There are jobs in the restaurant sector, for example. Photo: Daniel Dal Zennaro / EPA

Employment has improved. Employment tells how many people have a job.

In June there were over 120,000 more people in work than a year ago. In particular, there were more women in working life.

So many Finns have found a job after the difficult corona year.

However, not everyone has been as lucky. Things have not gone as well for many long-term unemployed people, that is, for those who have been without work for at least a year.

In June there were over 110,000 long-term unemployed people. That is almost 40,000 more than a year ago.

The data are from Statistics Finland.

## Finns trust the economy

![TVs for sale on display in a household appliance shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_258/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83417360f81d07e07e1/1626886496)

Finns trust the economy.

Finns estimate that their own financial situation is now good. Many intend to buy various goods, for example home appliances. This is what Statistics Finland says.

Finnish companies also believe that the economic situation is developing well. The Confederation of Finnish Industries says that industry has received many new orders. The economic situation of the service sectors is also improving, as corona restrictions are being removed.

## Finns' holidays in Åland

![Cyclists in Järsö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3754,h_2112,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83506060fb1176962c3/1628603711)

Open image viewer

In Åland you can go on cycling trips, for example. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

The favourite holiday destination of Finns is now Åland. Because of corona, Finns are now holidaying more in Finland than abroad.

Åland is a Swedish-speaking island group that belongs to Finland. Åland has autonomy, that is, the people of Åland decide on Åland's affairs themselves.

The nature of the Åland archipelago attracts tourists. Tourists go on cycling trips in Åland, for example.

Also, ship routes now bring more tourists to Åland from the rest of Finland. Some of Helsinki's ships stop at Åland for the day and do not continue as far as Stockholm. Travel between Finland and Sweden has decreased because of corona.
