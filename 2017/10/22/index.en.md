# Sunday 22.10.2017 (radio)

The police have received reports from people who have been called by scammers. There have been big demonstrations in Sweden against sexual harassment. A buyer paid 140,000 euros for a letter that was written on the Titanic. Clear News on the radio turns 25.

## Beware of scam calls

![A phone call.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9401152)

Open image viewer

If someone asks you for your online banking codes on the phone, they are not a real police officer. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

The police have again received reports from people who have been called by scammers.

The scammers lie that they are police officers or bank employees. They ask people for their online banking codes. The codes are supposedly needed because someone has broken into the bank account and the money in the account is in danger.

The truth is that the scammers themselves try to steal money with the banking codes.

The police remind people that real police never ask for banking codes on the phone.

## \#metoo demonstrations in Sweden

![#metoo written on a woman's hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9894309)

Open image viewer

#metoo campaign: many women point out that they too have experienced sexual harassment. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

There have been big demonstrations in Finland's neighbouring country Sweden. The demonstrations were linked to the \#metoo campaign, whose theme is sexual harassment.

The \#metoo campaign has spread on social media around the world. The campaign opposes sexual harassment, for example ugly and dirty talk.

Young women experience sexual harassment the most. The campaign reminds people that sexual harassment is always wrong.

## A really expensive letter

![Alexander Oskar Holverson with his wife Mary Alice on the deck of a ship in December 1911.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9893137)

Alexander Oskar Holverson died in the Titanic disaster. His wife Mary survived. Photo: Henry Aldridge &amp; Son / AOP

An expensive letter has been sold in London.

We reported on Friday that a rare letter would be sold in Britain at the weekend.

The letter was written on the Titanic in 1912. American Alexander Oskar Holverson wrote it the day before the Titanic sank into the sea. The letter was found when Holverson's body was lifted out of the sea.

Now the letter has been sold. More was paid for the letter than expected. The buyer paid 140,000 euros for the letter.

## 25 years of Clear News on the radio

![Plain-language radio news 25 years.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9873946)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics

Finally, we can tell you that Clear News on the radio turns 25. The first Clear News was broadcast in October 1992.

At first, the biggest group of Clear News listeners was abroad. Finns living abroad wanted to hear news from their old home country in easy Finnish. For example, many people whose families come from Finland live in America.

Today, Clear News is listened to most in Finland. For many students of Finnish, Clear News is a good way to follow Finnish news. That is why immigrants are a big group of Clear News listeners. Many Finnish-speaking elderly people also listen to Clear News, because they like its slow speaking rhythm.

Today you can also follow Clear News on television and online. But it all started on the radio 25 years ago. Clear News thanks its listeners for the years together!
