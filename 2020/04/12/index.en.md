# Sunday 12.4.2020 (radio)

Fewer than 70 new coronavirus infections have been found in Finland. People have crossed the Uusimaa border without a reason. The Pope wants the debts of poor countries forgiven. The weather is rainy.

## Easter has lowered the number of confirmed coronavirus infections

![THL coronavirus information posters on a billboard at the Columbus shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4721,h_2656,x_364,y_546/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6592405e81c379c9487/1585565094)

In Finland, 69 new coronavirus infections have been found in a day. On Saturday there were many more new cases in THL's statistics, 136.

However, this does not mean that the spread of the disease has slowed down. A sick person may not go for treatment, because it is Easter now.

THL's chief physician Hanna Nohynek says that for all diseases, there are fewer cases during the holidays than on weekdays.

## People have crossed the Uusimaa border without a reason

![Police checking drivers at the Uusimaa border.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11300059)

Open image viewer

Police officers are checking access permits. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

The police say that many people have driven across the Uusimaa border by car without a reason. Most of the offences have happened in the evening and at night. People have crossed the Uusimaa border during Easter, for example, to meet friends.

On Saturday the police gave 17 fines to people who drove across the Uusimaa border for no reason.

The police hope that drivers will no longer drive across the Uusimaa border for no reason. The purpose of the restriction is so that the coronavirus does not spread. The Uusimaa border is closed until 19 April.

## The Pope calls for solidarity

![The Pope standing in the empty St Peter's Basilica.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11303766)

Open image viewer

This year the Pope held the Easter mass in an empty St Peter's Basilica. Photo: Andreas Solaro / AFP

Pope Francis says that the debts of poor countries must be forgiven because of the coronavirus. The Pope also said that the countries of Europe must help each other. He appealed for countries to stop wars and fighting.

The Pope said that he is thinking of the people who are suffering because of the coronavirus.

Pope Francis held the Easter mass at St Peter's Basilica, where there were no people. People watched the mass online.

Usually the Pope gives his Easter address from the balcony of St Peter's Basilica. Last year about 70,000 people listened to the Pope's greeting.

## Snow and sleet are falling in the south too

![Skiers on the bank of the Ounasjoki river in Rovaniemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11303261)

The weather in Finland is rainy.

On Monday, 10 centimetres of snow will fall in Kainuu and North Ostrobothnia. Snow is also falling in the capital region, so it may not be a good idea to change to summer tyres on your car yet.

At the start of next week the weather is cold. In the middle of the week the weather will get warmer.

On Wednesday and Thursday, it may be 10 degrees warm in Southern Finland.

There is still a lot of snow in Lapland. In Kilpisjärvi there is up to 1.2 metres of snow, and more snow will soon fall.
