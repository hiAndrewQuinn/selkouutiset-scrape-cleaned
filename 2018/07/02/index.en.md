# Monday 2.7.2018 (radio)

Hundreds of applications for taxi transport licences have already come to Trafi. The number of payment default entries has grown again. The housing market for students is crowded. Pregnant women in Finland smoke more than elsewhere in Europe. Jehovah's Witnesses may lose their exemption from military service.

## The first new taxi licences have already been granted

![Taxis](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10284508)

Hundreds of applications for taxi transport licences have already come to Trafi. The Finnish Transport Safety Agency Trafi granted the first new taxi licences on Monday.

The applicant gets the information by email and can start driving straight away. Besides the transport licence, a taxi driver must have a driver's permit. The vehicle must also be registered for taxi use.

## Payment default entries are increasing

![In the picture, scissors cut the corner of a credit card. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6384226)

Open image viewer

Having many debts is behind many payment default entries. Photo: Anssi Leppänen / Yle

The number of payment default entries has grown again. An entry goes into a person's credit record if payments or loans are left unpaid for a long time.

Suomen Asiakastieto says that there were almost 380,000 default entries at the end of last month. The number has grown every year since 2009. Registered people have 15 payment defaults on average.

But the real problem is having many debts. The same people have many kinds of loans and credit. Many people with too much debt take new credit when they try to solve their debt problem themselves.

A payment default entry stays valid for 2 - 4 years. Payment default entries make it harder, for example, to get a rented flat or insurance.

## The housing market for students is crowded

![A young person's moving load in the back of an estate car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7443514)

Open image viewer

Many cars carry a young person's things on the way to their first own home. Photo: Maarit Piri-Lahti / Yle

July is a busy time on the student housing market. The reason is that the results of the joint application to higher education are now known.

For a long time the trend has been that students want to move into a studio flat. A year ago, students were moved over to get general housing allowance. Students' housing allowance became year-round, and for many it increased the amount of the allowance. Low-income students who live alone benefited the most.

That is why shared flats have become even less common. Shared flats have even started to be turned into studio flats.

You are most likely to get a student flat if you are ready to move into something other than a studio in the city centre.

## Pregnant women in Finland smoke more than other Europeans

![A woman smokes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10232381)

Open image viewer

In Finland, expectant mothers smoke more than the European average. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

Finnish mothers smoke during pregnancy clearly more than other European mothers. In Europe on average 8 percent of pregnant women smoke. In Finland 15 percent smoke.

Across the whole world, only 2 percent of pregnant women smoke.

## A working group considered the exemption of Jehovah's Witnesses from the army

![A recruit's bunk and military badges on a jacket sleeve.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9454533)

Open image viewer

Jehovah's Witnesses are currently exempt from military service. That may change. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

A working group of the Ministry of Defence proposes that the exemption of Jehovah's Witnesses from the army should end. But the working group proposes a transition period. The working group thinks that all religious communities must be treated equally in law.

Jehovah's Witnesses were exempted from military service in 1985.
