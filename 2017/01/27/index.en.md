# Friday 27.1.2017 (radio)

The police removed thousands of people from Finland. People who are not suited to the care sector are studying to become practical nurses. YouTubers' videos interest young people. A Särkänniemi dolphin died in Greece.

## The police removed thousands from Finland

![Hayder Al-Hatemi at the Metsälä detention unit 23.1.2016](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9426170)

Hayder Al-Hatemi waits at the Metsälä detention unit.  Photo: Retu Liikanen

In 2016 the police removed a record number of foreigners from Finland. The police removed almost 6,700 people because many asylum seekers did not get permission to stay in Finland.

Most people left the country themselves, but the police escorted almost 1,700 people out of Finland. When the police escort a person, the police take the person onto a plane and always travel all the way to the person's home country. For example, last year the police escorted 1,400 Iraqis to Iraq. They had got  a negative asylum decision.

Sometimes the police have to use handcuffs if a person is taken back to their home country by force. However, this is rare.

## Not everyone is suited to being a practical nurse

![An elderly person and a carer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9396703)

Open image viewer

People who are not suited to the care sector have been trained as practical nurses.  Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Many people who are not suited to the care sector are studying to become practical nurses. The students have problems with skills, health and, for example, the wish to study care.

That is why there is a wish to make it harder to get into nursing schools. A new test is being planned for practical nurses, which tests, for example, maths skills. There is also a wish to have more interviews with students, so that the schools know whether a person is suited to being a nurse. Now many unemployed people are forced into nursing school even though they may not be suited to the care sector.

About 30 practical nurses lose their licence to do their work every year because they have problems, for example, with alcohol.

## YouTubers are popular

![Finland's YouTube ambassadors.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8856901)

Open image viewer

Young people often make YouTube videos about fashion and beauty.  Photo: Retu Liikanen / Yle

There are already more than 500,000 YouTubers in Finland. YouTubers make videos about their lives for the YouTube video service. For example, video diaries and videos where technical experiments are done are popular.

Every year Finland holds the Tubecon event, where fans can meet YouTubers. For many young people, they are big stars, like film actors.

Last year, Tubecon had more than 18,000 visitors. This year the event will grow even more.

## A dolphin died in a water park

![Särkänniemi's dolphin Delfi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9428141)

Open image viewer

The male dolphin died of a heart attack in Greece.  Photo: Särkänniemi

A former dolphin of the Särkänniemi amusement park in Tampere has died in Greece. The 37-year-old male dolphin maybe died of a heart attack.

In the autumn, 4 dolphins were moved to a Greek water park because it was decided to close Finland's only dolphinarium. Animal protection activists demanded that the dolphinarium be closed.

At first the dolphins had their own trainers with them in Greece, but the workers returned to Finland in November.
