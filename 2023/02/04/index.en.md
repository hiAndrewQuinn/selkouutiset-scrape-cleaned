## Loviisa nuclear power plant

![Fortum's Loviisa nuclear power plant in spring 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_478/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/14-svyle-284044571e1a12b5e76/1651232978)

Loviisa nuclear power plant. Photo: Yle/Stefan Härus

The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment proposes that the Loviisa nuclear power plant gets an extension. The ministry proposes that the Loviisa nuclear power plant may continue operating for 20 years longer than previously agreed.

The operating licences of the Loviisa nuclear power plant are now valid until about 2030. If the nuclear power plant gets 20 years of extension, nuclear power, that is, atomic energy, can be produced in Loviisa until 2050.

The Finnish government will decide on the matter in about 2 weeks.

The Loviisa nuclear power plant produces about 10 percent of Finland's electricity. The owner of the Loviisa nuclear power plant is the energy company Fortum.

The nuclear power plant was built in Loviisa over 40 years ago. The nuclear power plant is about 90 kilometres from Helsinki.

## French police and a Finnish hacker

![A man's face and a sign reading Vastaamo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688819)

Open image viewer

25-year-old Julius Kivimäki may be the perpetrator of the Vastaamo data breach. Photo: Europol, Juha Kemppainen / Yle, graphic: Aalto Puutio / Yle

New information has been obtained from France about how the French police arrested a Finnish man on Friday. It is suspected that the man has carried out a data breach in Finland.

A French news site says that the police caught the man by chance. On Friday police went to a flat where domestic violence was suspected.

The police did not see signs of violence, but found a Finnish man in the flat. It turned out that the Finnish police had ordered that the man must be arrested.

The Finnish police suspect that the man broke into the information system of the psychotherapy centre Vastaamo a few years ago. The man stole patients' medical records and may have tried to extort money using the information.

## Emma awards

![Singer Bess in a party outfit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688832)

Open image viewer

Bess received 4 Emma awards. Photo: Niki Soukkio

The Emma music industry awards were presented on Friday. The most Emma awards went to singer Bess.

Bess, that is Essi Launimo, received 4 Emma awards. Bess was, for example, Artist of the Year, that is, the best performer of the year, and Bess's song Ram pam pam was Song of the Year.

The Emma awards have been given for 40 years already. The awards are presented at a big music-industry event, the Emma Gala.

## Forest fires in Chile

![Burnt forest and burnt houses.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688831)

Open image viewer

The fire has spread in Chile from the forest to houses too. Photo: Javier Torres / AFP

In South America, in Chile, there are big forest fires. The fire has burned almost 50,000 hectares of forest.

In addition, the fire has burned houses, and many people have died.

The forest fires began on Wednesday. The cause of the forest fires is extreme heat.

## The weather

Snow falls across Finland on Sunday. In the daytime a little snow falls in the west. Across the whole country there is a little frost.

On Monday the frost weakens. In the south and west the temperature may rise a little above zero. Monday is fair across a large part of the country, so snow no longer falls.
