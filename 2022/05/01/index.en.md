May Day. Dangerous situation in Helsinki. Political May Day. Job search. Eid celebration. The weather.

## May Day

![Partygoers gathered at Senate Square in Helsinki on May Day Eve after the Manta cap ceremony.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_285/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-948653626da5c5303aa/1651353084)

May Day was celebrated again together, not remotely. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The May Day celebrations have continued on Sunday. May Day is always on the 1st of May.

May Day is a festival for Finns of spring, for upper secondary school graduates and for workers.

This year Finns have celebrated May Day lively.

On Saturday, May Day Eve, the parties went on late into the night, because restaurants were open again. During the corona time, May Day could not be celebrated in restaurants.

The police say that May Day was lively in different parts of Finland now.

## Dangerous situation in Helsinki

![Car of the driver who drove into people with its front smashed on Pohjoisesplanadi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5808,h_3267,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-948589626d674b878bb/1651338423)

Open image viewer

A man drove a black Mercedes. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

In Helsinki, a man disturbed the May Day Eve by driving a car at high speed in the city centre. The man drove on Pohjoisesplanadi, where there are always many people on May Day Eve.

On Pohjoisesplanadi the man crashed into another car. 2 other people were injured in the crash, besides the man. In addition, the man hit many cars, and they got dents.

The man is 33 years old. It is suspected that he endangered the safety of many people. The police are investigating the case.

## Political May Day

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin surrounded by people from Turku and security guards. In the foreground a woman is shaking hands with the Prime Minister.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3192,h_1792,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-948738626e69a51a2b0/1651488289)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin at the SDP May Day event. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

The party leaders have also given speeches this May Day.

For example, the chair of the SDP, Prime Minister Sanna Marin, spoke in Turku. Marin said that Finland's security and the message of peace are now important. Marin referred to Russia's war in Ukraine.

In the May Day speeches of the opposition parties, the government has been criticised. For example, the chair of the Finns Party, Riikka Purra, said that government spending has grown too much and taxes are too high. Purra demanded that taxes be lowered. She spoke in Helsinki.

## Job search

![A form for earnings-related unemployment allowance is being filled in.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4470,h_2514,x_0,y_864/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6999965f10638921bbe/1620050667)

Open image viewer

It is hoped that the unemployed will find work more easily with the help of the new model. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Changes to the job search for unemployed people will come into effect from Monday.

In the new job search model, unemployed people will be kept in contact more than before. The job seeker will meet an official every 2 weeks during the first 3 months.

The government believes that this way the unemployed will find work better.

## Eid celebration

All kinds of delicacies belong to the Eid celebration. Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

Muslims in Finland are getting ready to celebrate Eid. Eid is celebrated when the fasting month of Ramadan ends.

This year Eid is now at the beginning of May. Muslims celebrate it with family. Good food belongs to Eid, and children at least get presents.

Here is the weather forecast.

Monday is a windy day. Water and snow showers will come to Central and Northern Finland. In Northern Lapland it can snow more than 10 centimetres.
