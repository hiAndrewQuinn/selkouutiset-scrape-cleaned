# Tuesday 7.2.2017 (radio)

Inflation is speeding up. Finland and Estonia together in arms deals. WWF has published a meat guide. The frost requires layered clothing.

## You get less for a euro than before

![A euro coin on top of the EU flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7723080)

You can now buy less with a euro than before. Photo: Oliver Berg / EPA

The prices of goods and services have started to rise in Finland. People are talking about inflation speeding up. You now get less for one euro than before.

For example, with ten euros you get less petrol for your car than before. So travelling is more expensive than before.

Vehicle tax, hospital fees and, for example, the fees of dentists have also risen. But wages have not risen.

Experts have noticed the rise in prices. However, they are not yet worried.

## Finland and Estonia buy weapons together

![South Korean K9 Thunder tracked self-propelled howitzer during an army training exercise at the Rovajärvi firing range in Rovaniemi on 24 November 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9446550)

Open image viewer

This is what a self-propelled howitzer looks like. It is almost like a tank. Photo: Jouni Porsanger / Lehtikuva

The Finnish and Estonian armies intend to acquire weapons together. Finland and Estonia intend to buy a large number of self-propelled howitzers, that is, guns, from South Korea. Estonia intends to acquire at least 12 self-propelled howitzers. It is believed that Finland will buy many more of them.

The Finnish army has been preparing a large artillery deal for about two years. The self-propelled howitzers are not new but used. They look like tanks with a large gun.

The South Korean self-propelled howitzers can fire about 40 kilometres. They can move at a speed of 70 kilometres an hour.

## A meat guide helps with food choices

![Elk meat at a shop meat counter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6887319)

Open image viewer

According to WWF, elk meat is worth eating. Photo: Yle / Matti Myller

The nature organisation WWF wants Finns to eat in an environmentally friendly way. WWF has published a meat guide. The meat guide helps Finns make food choices that are good for the environment.

According to WWF, the best meat choices are game animals. They have lived freely in nature. For example, the Finnish elk, the roe deer and the white-tailed deer are good meat choices.

According to WWF, it is also worth eating organic meat. Organic animals have been able to move freely, for example, and have been outdoors a lot.

WWF says that some meat should be swapped for vegetables.

## The frost requires the right clothing

![Mari Mannila and her son Olli at the Haukilahti beach in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9446996)

The day is getting longer in the south by five minutes and in the north by ten minutes every day. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

It is now really cold winter weather in Finland. Southern and central Finland are being warned of hard frost this week.

In frost, the right clothing is important. The right clothing in frost is layered clothing. That means putting on many layers of clothes.

Even though winter weather is cold, the amount of light increases quickly. In southern Finland the day is now getting longer by about five minutes a day. In northern Finland the day is getting longer by about ten minutes a day.
