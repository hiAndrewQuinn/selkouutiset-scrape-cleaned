# Thursday 16.4.2020 (radio)

The coronavirus has reached care homes. There are already more than 2 million coronavirus infections in the world. Aeroplanes and trains are bringing protective equipment from China to Finland. Denmark has celebrated the 80th birthday of the Queen. The thickest snow cover of the winter fell on Helsinki.

## The coronavirus has got into care homes

![Rollator in a corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10639106)

Open image viewer

In Helsinki's care homes, 48 elderly people have already died of the coronavirus. Photo: AOP

The coronavirus continues to spread in Finland's care homes.

In Helsinki's care homes, a total of 48 residents have so far died of the coronavirus. Just last week there were 9 deaths.

In Kiuruvesi in North Savo, a total of 11 elderly people have now died of the coronavirus in care homes. 9 elderly people died earlier, and 2 died last night.

## More than 2 million coronavirus infections already

![A coronavirus sample waiting for laboratory testing at the drive-in testing station in Espoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11307560)

More than 2 million coronavirus infections have now been confirmed in the world. The most infections are in the United States, more than 600,000.

In Finland, about 3,370 coronavirus infections have now been confirmed. In total, 75 people have died of the coronavirus here.

## A train also brings protective equipment from China

![A container train from China being unloaded at the Vuosaari harbour on 15 April 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6644995e96cd414fc10/1586941744)

Open image viewer

A container train has come from China to the Vuosaari harbour. Photo: Ilkka Klemola / Yle

Finland has received more protective equipment from China.

A cargo plane brought protective gloves and respirators from China to Finland yesterday. The National Emergency Supply Agency says that the next equipment shipment may come perhaps at the weekend.

Protective equipment is also being brought from China to Finland by train. The first batch came by train to the Vuosaari harbour in Helsinki yesterday.

About 50 freight containers fit on one train. So a train can bring many more goods at once than an aeroplane. The journey of the train from China to Finland takes a little under 2 weeks.

## Denmark celebrated the Queen

![Danish Queen Margrethe](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11309167)

Open image viewer

Queen Margrethe waves from the door of Fredensborg Palace. Photo: Steinar Gestsson / AFP / Lehtikuva

Today in Denmark, the birthday of Queen Margrethe has been celebrated. Margrethe turned 80.

Because of the coronavirus, the queen was celebrated remotely this time. At exactly 12 o'clock, Danes went out onto their balconies or into their yards and sang the traditional birthday song to the queen.

The song was conducted in a live TV broadcast by Phillip Faber, the conductor of the girls' choir of the Danish Broadcasting Corporation.

The queen thanked them for the performance and said that the song went straight to her heart.

## A winter snow record in the capital region

![Warning of poor driving conditions on the Turunväylä road in Vihti during the morning sleet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11307635)

Driving conditions were poor in Southern and Western Finland today. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

In Southern and Western Finland, snow has been blowing today.

During the night and morning, many centimetres of snow fell. For example, in Kaisaniemi in Helsinki, this winter's snow record was measured.

The snowfall also made traffic more difficult. The Traffic Management Centre says that several traffic accidents happened in Southern Finland.

Tomorrow, snow will fall in Northern and Eastern Finland.
