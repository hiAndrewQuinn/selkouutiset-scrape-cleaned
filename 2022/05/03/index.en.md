# Tuesday 3.5.2022 (radio)

The strike has closed schools and day-care centres. Germany promises support to Finland and Sweden. The Finnish army is practising. Farm spring work is late. The weather.

## Strike in the municipal sector

The strike in the municipal sector has closed services in different parts of the country.

More than 80 000 municipal sector workers started the strike this morning in 10 different towns. There are workers from many different occupational groups on strike, from teachers to bus drivers and culture sector workers.

The strike harms families with children the most, because many schools and day-care centres are closed. Many parents of small children have stayed at home to work remotely, taken time off or asked grandparents for help.

The strike in the municipal sector lasts one week. The workers are on strike because the negotiations on a new collective agreement are stuck. The dispute is especially about wages.

Germany promises to support Finland and Sweden when they make decisions about security and possible NATO membership.

German Chancellor Olaf Scholz says that the countries can rely on German support also during the time when the NATO membership application has been submitted, but membership has not yet been confirmed.

Finnish Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Swedish Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson were visiting Germany today.

## The army practises

![A Hornet fighter of the Air Force takes off from Pirkkala airfield.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6448485e4e57e8262b8/1582194855)

Large military exercises are now being held in Finland.

The Army has a large international military exercise in Satakunta. There are 3400 soldiers and 450 vehicles taking part.

Troops from Britain, Latvia, Estonia and the United States also take part in the exercise.

The Air Force also has a large exercise. About 30 aircraft take part in it, taking off from Rovaniemi and near Kuopio at Siilinjärvi.

The planes fly in daylight and at times very low. They can be seen and heard widely in the northern sky.

## Spring sowing

![A tractor sows oats in a field. This year spring sowing can be started already around May Day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6108261)

Open image viewer

A tractor sows oats in a field. Photo: Yle / Jarkko Riikonen

The start of spring work is late on many farms.

Pro Agria says that the spring work is late, because there was a lot of snow in the winter and April was cool. Farmers have had to wait for the snow to melt and the fields to dry.

The first spring sowing has been done in Southern Finland and South Ostrobothnia. The first to be sown are spring wheat, oats, faba beans and peas.

At the moment it looks like sowing work will really get going in the middle of May.
