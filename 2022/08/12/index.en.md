## The Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant

![Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant in Ukraine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99229262f32f5e0b923/1660104730)

The safety of the Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant worries people in the middle of the war. Photo: Chine Nouvelle / AOP

Russia's war in Ukraine also affects the safety of nuclear power plants, that is, atomic power plants.

Near the Ukrainian Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant there has been fighting and shooting with artillery. Around the world people fear a nuclear disaster if the power plant is damaged in the war.

The International Atomic Energy Agency says that it must be allowed to inspect the situation at the Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant.

Russia took over the Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant in March. Russia is carrying out rocket strikes from the power plant area elsewhere in Ukraine.

The Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant is the biggest nuclear power plant in Europe. The power plant's employees are Ukrainian.

## The number of carers in elderly care

![A carer helps an elderly person eating at a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11724018)

Open image viewer

Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

In Finland people are discussing the number of carers in elderly care.

A new law comes into force next spring. It stipulates that there must be 7 carers per 10 elderly people in elderly care facilities.

Now decision-makers are considering whether the law could perhaps come into force only later. The reason is that there are too few employees in the care sector.

Basic services minister Aki Lindén wants to especially consider the content of the law. In Lindén's view the shortage of carers can be eased, for example, by hiring more care assistants.

## Junior ice hockey World Championships

![Finland celebrating Kasper Puution's goal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2102,h_1182,x_250,y_124/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99332162f55ea699046/1660247841)

Finland's young players beat Czechia in a World Championship game. Photo: Pasi Mennander / Finnish Ice Hockey Association

Finland has played well in the under-20 ice hockey World Championships. The world championships are in Edmonton, Canada.

Finland beat Czechia on Thursday. The game was exciting. Canada was already leading 2-0 before Finland got the game properly going.

When the regular playing time ended, the game was tied 3-3. After that Finland scored a goal in the shootout, that is, Finland beat Czechia 4-3.

Earlier this week Finland beat Latvia. Finland's next game is on Sunday. Then Finland plays against Slovakia.

The under-20 ice hockey World Championships continue for the whole of next week.
