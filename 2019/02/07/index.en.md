There have been problems in homes for people with intellectual disabilities. The SDP is Finland's most popular party. More foreign tourists visit Finland than before. Airports in Europe had more passengers last year than ever before. Menstruation gets its own emoji.

## There have been problems in homes for people with intellectual disabilities

src="https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5729682"

Open image viewer

There have been problems in homes for people with intellectual disabilities. Photo: YLE / Susanna Pekkarinen

There have been problems in homes for people with intellectual disabilities. Disability organisations say that there have been problems in many municipalities.

For example, in Rovaniemi in Lapland, many people with intellectual disabilities have moved away from Esperi's care home. The families of people with intellectual disabilities say that there are too few employees at the Esperi care home. There are also problems with the medication of people with intellectual disabilities.

There have been similar problems in elderly care homes.

## The SDP is the most popular party in Yle's support survey

![Parliament House](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_628/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4798895ad9e63bdbb7f/1524229751)

Open image viewer

Parliamentary elections will be held in April. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä/ Yle

A new survey of the support for Finnish parties has been made.

Yle's survey says that the SDP is the most popular party. The support for the SDP, that is the Social Democrats, is now about 20 percent.

The National Coalition gets the second most support. The support for the National Coalition is about 17 percent. In third place is the Centre, whose support is under 16 percent.

The support for all of the 3 biggest parties has fallen. The support for the National Coalition and the Centre is the lowest for a long time.

Among the other parties, the support for the Finns Party has risen the most. The support for the Greens has also risen.

The parliamentary elections are in April. The support figures for all parties are on Yle's website. The website is at yle.fi.

## More foreign tourists visited Finland in December than a year ago

![Santa Claus walks in a winter landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10562085)

Open image viewer

Lapland is a popular tourist destination in December. Photo: AOP

More foreign tourists visit Finland than before.

In December, the most tourists visiting Finland came from Britain. The second largest group was Russian tourists. More people also came to Finland from Asia than a year ago.

Most of the foreign tourists went to Lapland.

## Airports in Europe are busy

![Heathrow terminal](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10587421)

Open image viewer

Heathrow Airport is the busiest in Europe. Photo: Mark Richards / AOP

People are travelling by plane more than before.

Airports in Europe had more passengers last year than ever before. More than 2.3 billion passengers used the airports.

The busiest airports in Europe are London Heathrow, Paris Charles de Gaulle and Amsterdam Schiphol.

## Menstruation gets its own emoji

![Menstruation emoji](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10635944)

Open image viewer

The menstruation emoji is a drop of blood. Photo: Plan International

Menstruation gets its own emoji.

An emoji is a picture that is used, for example, in social media posts. The menstruation emoji is a drop of blood.

The children's rights organisation Plan says that the menstruation emoji is a good thing. In Plan's view, menstruation is a natural part of a woman's life, and one can talk about menstruation without shame.
