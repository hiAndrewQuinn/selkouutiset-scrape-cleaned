# Wednesday 22.5.2019 (radio)

The government negotiations need more time. The number of families is falling in Finland. Advance voting for the EU elections has ended. Summer jobs also require a tax card. There have been thunderstorms in Finland.

## Government negotiations

![Antti Rinne being interviewed by the media at Säätytalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10795015)

Antti Rinne told journalists how the government negotiations are going. Photo: Lauri Heikkinen / Prime Minister's Office

The government negotiations need more time. SDP party leader Antti Rinne is leading the negotiations.

Rinne says that the negotiations may need 2 more days. Rinne's plan was that the negotiators would finish the work by Friday.

The negotiators are trying to agree on Finland's new government. They must agree on what the new government will do and what the government will spend money on.

Rinne says that discussions on economic and tax matters are still ongoing.

5 parties are taking part in the negotiations. If the negotiations succeed, they will join the government, and Antti Rinne will become Finland's new prime minister.

## Families

![A father and child on the Seurasaari bridge.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10795411)

Open image viewer

There are fewer families with children in Finland than before. Photo: AOP

The number of families is falling in Finland. Last year the number of families fell by 2,800. This is according to Statistics Finland.

There are also fewer families with children than before. Only the number of childless couples in Finland is growing. There are now more couples in Finland who have no children at all.

## EU elections

![A polling place for advance voting in the EU elections.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10794072)

Open image viewer

Advance voting for the EU elections has ended, but the actual voting day is on Sunday. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Advance voting for the EU elections ended yesterday, Tuesday. About 900,000 Finns voted during advance voting.

The actual election day is Sunday, 26 May. The EU elections are held in all European Union countries.

Citizens of EU countries elect representatives to the European Parliament. The Parliament has over 750 representatives. Finns elect 13 representatives to the European Parliament.

## Tax card

![Tax card, withholding tax percentage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10006997)

Open image viewer

The tax card shows how much tax must be paid. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Summer jobs also require a tax card. The Tax Administration reminds young people that an employee must have a tax card even for short jobs.

The tax card no longer has to be on paper. An employee can show the tax card, for example, from the screen of their phone.

It is worth showing the tax card. If the tax card is not shown, 60 per cent tax is taken from the wages.

You can find information about tax matters online. Verokampus.fi is the young people's own information bank on tax matters.

## Thunderstorm

![Lightning in the sky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10792685)

Open image viewer

Lightning and rainfall are part of a thunderstorm. Photo: Leonid Tit / AOP

There were thunderstorms in Finland on Wednesday. In Central Finland the thunder was severe.

For example, in Jyväskylä rainwater rose onto the streets. Water also got into many buildings. For example, water got into the Torikeskus in Jyväskylä.
