# Thursday 15.9.2016 (radio)

Finland's economy is growing, but slowly. Many Europeans do not trust vaccines. Flowers that you can eat are now exported from Finland to China. Old people are happy. The northern lights are bright this autumn.

## A weak economy

![Two-euro coins](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9169849)

Open image viewer

Finland's economic situation is weak. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Finland's economy is growing, but slowly. This is the forecast of the Ministry of Finance, which handles the state's economic policy.

Although the economy grows a little, Finland's state debt also grows. At the end of next year, Finland will already have 111 billion euros of debt.

Finland's economic situation will stay weak in the coming years, the Ministry of Finance estimates. But it believes that unemployment will go down.

## Important vaccines

![vaccine](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8849443)

Open image viewer

Vaccines can prevent many diseases. Photo: Yle

Many Europeans do not trust vaccines. Many wonder whether vaccines are safe. A new study says that the French doubt the safety of vaccines the most.

Doctor Hanna Nohynek says that in rich Europe, people do not see diseases. That is why they wonder whether vaccines are safe and whether it is worth vaccinating children.

In poor countries, people see children die of diseases that can be prevented with vaccines. That is why vaccines are trusted more in Asia and Africa than in Europe.

## Tasty flowers

![Verbena](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9169935)

Open image viewer

Verbena is a beautiful flower that also tastes good. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

Flowers that you can eat are now exported from Finland to China. The first flowers left for China about 2 weeks ago. They went to chefs in Hong Kong.

The chefs were satisfied. The flowers survived the long flight well. They were beautiful and tasted good.

The Finnish Kotipelto garden grows clean and tasty flowers. You cannot eat ordinary flowers from a flower shop, because they have various chemicals in them.

## Happy old people

![Old people laughing](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9147386)

Open image viewer

Age brings happiness. Photo: AOP

Old people are happy, says an American study. The older a person is, the happier they are.

Adults under 40 feel the most stress and depression, and people over 90 feel the most happiness.

The researchers say that age brings wisdom and happiness. It may be due to the physiology of the brain. Old people's brains may not react to negative things as much as young people's brains.

## Bright northern lights

![Northern lights blaze at Olkkajärvi.Northern lights blaze at Olkkajärvi in Rovaniemi in autumn 2015.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9166792)

Open image viewer

On a dark evening, the northern lights can be seen well. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

The northern lights are bright this autumn. Northern lights are created when small particles come from the sun towards the earth.

Northern lights can now be seen in Northern Finland almost every night if there are no clouds in the sky. The northern lights can be seen really well in autumn, when it is otherwise dark and the ground is black at night.

Many tourists also come to Lapland to watch the northern lights. Tourists come from around the world, even from America and Australia.
