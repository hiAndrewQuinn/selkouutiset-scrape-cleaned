## Queues at corona tests

![Mehiläinen's drive-in coronavirus testing point in Tapiola, Espoo, on 19 March. A worker takes a test from a person sitting in a car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11473412)

Taking coronavirus test samples in the drive-in lane, where you can drive a car. Photo: Janne Järvinen / Yle

Coronavirus testing has got congested in many places in Finland. Then it can take many days to get a corona test. You may also have to wait many days for the test result.

There are queues at corona tests because more people are coming to be tested than before. Tests are now done for everyone who has flu symptoms.

Finland may need to get more people to do tests and examine samples, so that people can get tested quickly and get the result quickly.

## Face mask recommendation for Turku

![People on a bus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4312,h_2426,x_0,y_579/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-6068457bed3533e7bb/1571835947)

Open image viewer

Turku recommends that it is a good idea to wear a mask on the bus when schools start. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The city of Turku has given people a recommendation to use face masks. The city of Turku recommends that it is a good idea to wear a mask when travelling by bus. The mask recommendation starts in Turku next week, when schools start.

Turku is the first city in Finland to give a recommendation to use face masks.

Next week the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health may also give recommendations on the use of masks in the whole of Finland.

## Help from Finland to Beirut

![Tents for use as workspaces and accommodation, and information technology, are being sent to Beirut from Finland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11483449)

Open image viewer

The aid supplies were packed at the Finnish Red Cross logistics centre and sent to Beirut by plane. Photo: Pelastusopisto

Finland is sending help to the disaster area in Beirut. A huge explosion happened in the port of Beirut on Tuesday. At least 149 people died and thousands of people were injured in the explosion.

On Friday, first-aid supplies and operating theatre supplies were sent from Finland to Beirut by plane.

Finland is also sending a group of people to Beirut who will help with rescue work.

## Drought makes the harvest worse

![Rapeseed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10793470)

Open image viewer

A rapeseed field. Photo: Ville Toijonen/Yle

The drought in early summer will make this summer's harvest smaller. In early summer very little rain fell and many plants grew slowly.

Farmers say that the drought in early summer made the growth of cereals and oil crops especially worse. Oil crops are, for example, rapeseed and turnip rape, from which vegetable oil is made.

More than 80 per cent of farmers say that the drought has caused problems for farming.

## The weather

The weather on Saturday is warm. During the day it is dry in the south and west of the country, that is, it does not rain. In the east and in the north there may be showers of rain.

The temperature on Saturday is up to 28 degrees in the southern parts of the country. In the north too it is a heatwave, that is, the temperature is over 25 degrees in places.
