# Radio Clear News Sunday 18.6.2023

New ministers. War in Ukraine. Kultaranta talks. The weather.

## New ministers

The parties of the new government have decided who will become ministers.

The chair of the National Coalition, Petteri Orpo, is prime minister, because the National Coalition won the elections.

The chair of the Finns Party, Riikka Purra, is finance minister.

The foreign minister is the National Coalition's Elina Valtonen, and the defence minister is the National Coalition's Antti Häkkänen.

The new government will have 19 ministers.

Four parties will be in the government: the National Coalition, the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats.

## War in Ukraine

![Armoured howitzer firing with its barrel pointed upwards, in the middle of a bushy meadow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3720,h_2092,x_0,y_52/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1131585648ea9f806991/1687071384)

Many Russian soldiers have been killed or wounded in Ukraine at the weekend, as the Ukrainian army attacks in southern Ukraine.

Many Ukrainian soldiers have also been killed or wounded. Ukraine is trying to get back its own areas, which Russia has taken.

Fighting in the southern part of Ukraine is fierce, and many soldiers are being killed and wounded in it.

Finland and other European countries have decided to give more weapons to Ukraine.

Europe wants to help Ukraine, because Ukraine is defending its independence and its own territory.

## Kultaranta talks

![President Sauli Niinistö on the A-studio broadcast at Kultaranta, in a granite castle &quot;paradise&quot;, discussing with journalist Petri Raivio around a garden table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_45/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96956262a82605ef86d/1687153468)

Open image viewer

Photo from last year's Kultaranta talks. Photo: Jani Saikko / Yle

President Sauli Niinistö is hosting the traditional Kultaranta talks on Sunday and Monday.

At the event, decision-makers and experts discuss Finland's foreign policy and security policy. The media reports on the discussions.

The name Kultaranta comes from the fact that the talks are usually held at the president's summer residence, which is called Kultaranta. Now it is being renovated.

That is why the talks will be held this year in Helsinki, at the Presidential Palace.

The Kultaranta talks were first held as early as 2013. President Niinistö is holding the talks for the last time, because next spring Finland's president will change.
