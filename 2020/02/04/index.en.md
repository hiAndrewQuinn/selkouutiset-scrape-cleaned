# Tuesday 4.2.2020 (radio)

Municipalities warn of redundancies and layoffs. Parliament has returned to work after the Christmas break. Vaccinations against chickenpox have been more popular than expected. Finns are going to car dealers in Sweden. Many birds have stayed in Finland for the winter.

## Municipal redundancies

src="https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5367630"

Open image viewer

Many municipalities have to look for savings.

Municipalities have to make more employees redundant and lay them off this year than usual.

The municipal organisation Kuntatyönantajat says that municipalities and joint municipal authorities may have to make over 750 employees redundant this year. In addition, perhaps over 8,000 people will be laid off.

Kuntatyönantajat says that larger redundancies in municipalities were last made during the recession years of the 1990s.

The redundancies and layoffs are due to the poor economic situation of the municipalities.

The estimate by Kuntatyönantajat is based on a survey that it carried out among municipalities in December.

## Parliament has started work

The Speakers of Parliament who will continue are Juho Eerola of the Finns Party, Matti Vanhanen of the Centre Party and Antti Rinne of the SDP. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Parliament has returned to work after the Christmas break.

At the first plenary session of the spring, Matti Vanhanen of the Centre Party was chosen as Speaker of Parliament. Antti Rinne of the SDP and Juho Eerola of the Finns Party were chosen as deputy speakers.

The official opening of the spring session, that is, the opening of the parliamentary term, is tomorrow. The parliamentary term is opened by President Sauli Niinistö.

## Popularity of chickenpox vaccinations

![a girl being vaccinated](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9663096)

Open image viewer

A girl is being vaccinated. Photo: AOP

Schoolchildren have taken vaccinations against chickenpox more than was expected.

The chickenpox vaccination became part of the national vaccination programme in September 2017. Since then, the vaccine has been offered free of charge to children under 12 years old.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that almost all primary school-age children now have protection against chickenpox.

It is important to get protection against chickenpox while a child. If someone catches chickenpox as an adult, the disease is often more serious.

## Used cars from Sweden

![car](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10921547)

Open image viewer

Finns are going to car dealers in Sweden. Photo: Marko Väänänen / Yle

Finns bought a record number of used cars from Sweden last year.

About 26,000 used cars were brought to Finland from Sweden last year. The cars are being bought mainly by Finnish car dealers.

A car can be bought in Sweden more cheaply than in Finland, because the exchange rate of the Swedish krona is low.

## Birds wintering

![Blackbird](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2100,h_1181,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-1829754d0a5fab8d61/1422960159)

Open image viewer

In Espoo the blackbird has already started nesting. Photo: Yle/Risto Salovaara

More birds than usual have stayed in Finland for the winter, says the bird organisation Birdlife Suomi.

This year 165 bird species have already been recorded. Especially more waterbirds than usual have stayed here to winter.

The birds have stayed here because the winter has been warmer than usual in most of the country.

Birdlife Suomi says that in Espoo the blackbird has already started nesting.
