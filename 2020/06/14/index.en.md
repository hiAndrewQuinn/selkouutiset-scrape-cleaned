# Sunday 14.6.2020 (radio)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin thinks the Emergency Powers Act has been necessary during the corona era. Finland has reported 17 new corona infections. China fears a new wave of corona. Many people drowned in the spring. On Sunday, Wildflower Day was celebrated.

## Marin assesses the Emergency Powers Act

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin in an STT interview at the Prime Minister's official residence in Kesäranta, Helsinki, on 25 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11401285)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the Emergency Powers Act has been necessary during the corona era.

The Emergency Powers Act gives the government power to make big decisions quickly. It has been important, as the government has fought against corona.

The Emergency Powers Act was brought into use in March. Marin says that the Emergency Powers Act may be abandoned perhaps very soon.

Sanna Marin said this on the radio programme Prime Minister's Interview Hour.

The number of corona infections is no longer growing quickly. Finland reported on Sunday that there are 17 new corona infections.

On Sunday, 1 new corona death was also reported. The coronavirus has now killed 326 people in Finland in total.

## New corona cases in China

![People had their temperature measured in front of the Xinfadi wholesale market on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11400790)

Open image viewer

China fears that corona will come back. Photo: Greg Baker / AFP

China fears a new wave of corona, because there are new corona infections in the country.

On Sunday China reported 57 new corona infections. That is the highest number in one day since April.

New corona infections have been found in Beijing, the capital of China. The infections have come from a market where meat is sold.

Now the markets, schools and nurseries in the area have been closed. Movement of people has also been restricted in part of Beijing.

## Many drowned in the spring

![People walk on thin ice on the shore of Pyhäjärvi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7801582)

Open image viewer

Going out on thin ice is dangerous. Photo: Jukka Töyli / Yle

During the spring more people have drowned than usual.

In total, 40 people drowned from the start of the year to the end of May. That is twice as many as in previous years.

One reason for the situation is corona. Because of corona restrictions many sports places were closed, so people spent a lot of time outdoors. For example, in April 19 people fell through the ice and drowned.

Many more men have drowned than women.

## Wildflower Day

![Rowan tree blooming in the yard of the Oiva art school in Seinäjoki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10240099)

Open image viewer

The rowan has beautiful flowers. Photo: Hanne Leiwo/Yle

Wildflower Day was celebrated on Sunday. The goal of Wildflower Day is for people to learn to know nature.

This year the theme of the day is the rowan. It is a tree that has white flowers in June and red berries in autumn.

Wildflower Day excursions were cancelled this year because of corona. Instead, people have got to know yard plants. The rowan also grows in many Finnish yards.
