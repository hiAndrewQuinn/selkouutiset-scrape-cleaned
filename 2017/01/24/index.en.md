# Tuesday 24.1.2017 (radio)

The transport reform collapsed. Trump bans abortion counselling abroad. Finland has many space companies. A new guideline for school meals.

## Car taxes will not be changed

![Cars for sale in the Delta-auto hall in Konala, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9415637)

Open image viewer

Car taxes will not be abolished.  Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

A big transport reform has been stopped. Minister of Transport and Communications Anne Berner decided this together with the Prime Minister. At the same time it was decided that no changes will be made to car taxes.

Berner wanted to completely abolish the car tax that is paid on new cars. At the same time, a new road fee for drivers was being considered.

The proposal badly confused car sales. Car dealers are happy that car taxes will not be changed.

## Trump banned abortion counselling abroad

![Donald Trump in the White House on 22 January. Trump spoke at the swearing-in ceremony of his staff. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9419478)

Open image viewer

US President Donald Trump.  Photo: Andrew Harrer /EPA

The new President of the United States, Donald Trump, bans organisations from giving abortion counselling abroad. The President's order applies to American organisations that get money from the USA. The organisations are now in a difficult situation, because they have a lot of activity abroad. The organisations accuse the US President of violating women's health and rights.

Opponents of abortion have praised Trump's strict line.

## A Finnish company wants to take people to space

![Timothy Kopra floats in space outside the international space station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8914256)

Open image viewer

There is a wish to offer space trips to Finns.  Photo: NASA

New space companies have been set up in Finland. The companies are still small, but they have a lot of know-how in space technology.

For example, a new Finnish company wants to offer space trips. One of the company's owners is Peter Vesterbacka, the founder of the games company Rovio. Vesterbacka thinks it is wrong that only 600 people have been to space. He says that the company plans to take the 1st person to space already next year. Later the plan is to arrange more space trips.

## Fake police officers took a lot of money from elderly people

![Police badge card](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9028348)

Open image viewer

A real police officer must have a badge.  Photo: Police website

Fake police officers have cheated people in the Helsinki area. Young men have got a total of tens of thousands of euros by pretending to be police. The victims have been elderly people who have been followed, for example, in shopping centres in Vantaa. Later, people have been tricked into giving their bank card details and PIN codes when they were told about a police criminal investigation. At least 15 scams have come to light.

## More vegetables on schoolchildren's plates

![Courgette and minced meat bake and vegetables on a plate](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9420042)

Open image viewer

The school meal recommendations were renewed.  Photo: Kati Rantala / Yle

Schoolchildren should have peace to eat. A new recommendation, or guideline, on school meals has been published in Finland. The recommendation says that a schoolchild must have time to eat in peace, at least half an hour, or 30 minutes.

Red meat, that is beef and pork, must be reduced in school food. Berries, fruit and vegetables must be increased.
