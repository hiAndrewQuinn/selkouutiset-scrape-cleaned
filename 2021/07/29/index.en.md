## First Olympic medal

![Matti Mattsson cheers and holds his hands up in the water to celebrate bronze at the Olympics.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2722,h_1531,x_0,y_705/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83649261020f81b087e/1627525178)

Swimmer Matti Mattsson won Olympic bronze. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

Finland has won its first medal at the Tokyo Olympics.

Matti Mattsson was 3rd in the men's 200 metre breaststroke. So he got the bronze medal. At the same time he swam a new Finnish record.

Mattsson's Olympic bronze is Finland's first swimming Olympic medal in 25 years.

## Kalajoki forest fire

![Aerial photo of the Kalajoki wildfire area, July 2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83579360ffd218a011d/1627378292)

Open image viewer

Thick smoke rises from the Kalajoki forest fire. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

In Kalajoki in North Ostrobothnia a big forest fire is being put out.

The fire area is over 300 hectares. It is the biggest forest fire in Finland since the 1970s.

Putting out the fire has been difficult, because in windy weather the fire has spread by crown fires, that is, from one treetop to another.

The rescue department says that residents of the nearby area must prepare for evacuation, if the fire still spreads.

The extinguishing work will continue at least over the weekend.

The number of corona infections in Finland is growing quickly. Today 765 new infections were recorded, that is, over 100 more than yesterday.

Health authorities say that infections are now especially among young adults. Infections spread in restaurants, at parties and at various public events.

The chains of infection have also grown bigger, and tracing is harder than before.

## Toni Wirtanen awarded

![Toni Wirtanen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5017,h_2822,x_0,y_110/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8367746102a85b3fe00/1627564189)

Open image viewer

Toni Wirtanen. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The Juha Vainio Award is given this year to Toni Wirtanen. Wirtanen is the singer, guitarist and songwriter of the band Apulanta.

The Juha Vainio Award is 6,000 euros. The award is given to meritorious domestic songwriters and lyricists.

The award criteria especially praise the song "Valot pimeyksien reunoilla", written by Toni Wirtanen.

## The sun set in Nuorgam

![Bright nights end in Sápmi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10325076)

Open image viewer

Lapland's beautiful midnight sun is over for this summer. Photo: Maiju Saijets / Yle

The midnight sun has ended in Lapland for this summer.

In Nuorgam, the northernmost village in Finland, the midnight sun ended last night. A little over half an hour after midnight the sun set briefly below the horizon. Before that, the sun had last set in Nuorgam in the middle of May.

Elsewhere in Lapland the midnight sun ended earlier. For example, in Inari the sun already set last week on Friday.
