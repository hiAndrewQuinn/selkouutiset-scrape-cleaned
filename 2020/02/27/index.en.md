# Thursday 27.2.2020 (radio)

Finland has prepared for coronavirus cases. Many countries are limiting events. The Rauma shipyard got a big order from Australia. People are retiring later than before. The weather is dry and clear.

## There is no coronavirus epidemic in Finland

![Minister of Finance Katri Kulmuni, Minister of Family and Basic Services Krista Kiuru, Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Minister of Social Affairs and Health Aino-Kaisa Pekonen at the government press conference on the coronavirus situation assessment.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11231032)

Open image viewer

The government held a press conference about Finland's preparations for the coronavirus. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

The government has been discussing the effects of the coronavirus on Finland.

The government says that the coronavirus is not a big worry in Finland. There is no coronavirus epidemic in Finland. It is not easy to catch the coronavirus in Finland.

The government says that health centres and hospitals are ready to treat people if people catch the coronavirus in Finland. Hospitals have a lot of protective equipment and medicines in stock.

Two coronavirus cases have been found in Finland. One case was found yesterday, Wednesday, and the other case was found at the beginning of February.

The government has set up a working group that follows the coronavirus situation in Finland and elsewhere in the world.

## Schools closed and events cancelled abroad

![The Kaaba in Mecca.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8334918)

Open image viewer

The Kaaba in Mecca, Saudi Arabia. Photo: Amel Pain / EPA

In many countries there are now restrictions to stop the spread of the coronavirus. For example, Japan is closing schools. Universities have been closed in China and the USA. Big events, for example concerts or sports competitions, have been cancelled in Italy.

Saudi Arabia is banning foreigners from going on pilgrimages to Mecca. Iran is cancelling Friday prayers in Tehran.

In Finland, events have not yet been cancelled because of the coronavirus.

## The Rauma shipyard got a big order

![Opening ceremony at the Rauma shipyard.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10973487)

Open image viewer

Work at the Rauma shipyard. Photo: Rauma Marine Constructions

Australia is ordering two passenger ships from the Rauma shipyard. The ships will be built so that they are finished in autumn 2022 and 2023.

Building the ships in Finland at the Rauma shipyard will give work to about 3,500 people. The ships will run between Devonport in Tasmania, Australia, and Melbourne.

The Rauma shipyard has received many orders in recent years.

## The retirement age is rising

![A retired couple picks cherries.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9601862)

Open image viewer

Fewer people retired last year than the year before. Photo: AOP

People are retiring later than before. Last year in Finland people retired at an average age of 61.5 years. That is more than 2 months later than the year before, that is, in 2018.

Fewer people retired last year than the year before. People work for longer than before. The retirement age has risen a little every year.

Finland has carried out a pension reform, the aim of which is that people work for longer and the retirement age rises.
