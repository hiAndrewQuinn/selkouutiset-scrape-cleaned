## Finland received coronavirus vaccines

![Two people lift a box out of a bigger box](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_267/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7574995fe7024d1af29/1608974990)

The first coronavirus vaccine delivery arrived in Finland. Photo: EPA / Nicolas Maeterlinck

Finland has received the first coronavirus vaccines. The first batch contains almost 10,000 vaccine doses.

The vaccines are transported to big hospitals in different parts of Finland.

The health authorities do not say exactly how the vaccines are transported or where they are stored.

Criminals may be interested in robbing the vaccines.

Vaccinations will start in Finland tomorrow.

The first to be vaccinated are nurses and doctors who treat coronavirus patients.

Many other European countries also received coronavirus vaccines today.

At least Sweden, Norway, France and Spain received vaccines. All EU countries received the same coronavirus vaccine.

Vaccinations are due to start in EU member states tomorrow, Sunday.

At first, fairly few people will be vaccinated in all countries. Each person must be given 2 vaccine doses.

The EU has so far approved only 1 drug factory's coronavirus vaccine.

## Christmas food is being thrown away

![Christmas table](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9371167)

Open image viewer

Many people buy too much Christmas food. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

At Christmas Finns eat a lot of traditional Christmas food.

But often there is too much food.

A survey showed that more than half of Finns throw Christmas food into the bin after Christmas.

Respondents said that there was too much food on offer. Many also said that they got tired of Christmas food.

There were also other reasons why food is thrown away. Some said that Christmas food is too filling.

Some said that ready meals come in packs that are too big.

## The weather warms up in the south

![map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11715295)

Open image viewer

Today there has been hard frost in Lapland.

At Kevojärvi in Utsjoki the frost was 40.1 degrees today.

Tomorrow, Sunday, the frost will ease, that is, the weather will warm up in southern Finland.

In Lapland there may still be hard frost.

In the western part of the country it may snow. Elsewhere it is dry, that is, nothing falls.
