# Saturday 6.10.2018 (radio)

Climate experts are worried. Companies are training immigrants. A minister has become worried about school lunches. An opera star has died. Roads may be slippery.

## Climate experts are worried

![Helsingin Sanomat newspaper clipping from 1978.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10411404)

Open image viewer

The newspaper Helsingin Sanomat reported on climate warming already in 1978. Photo: HS.fi / aikakone

Climate experts around the world say that now all people must pay attention to the warming of the planet's climate. The world's climate experts are at a meeting in South Korea.

The day after tomorrow, on Monday, the climate experts will publish a report.

In the report the climate experts say how much and how quickly the planet's climate is warming.

The climate experts also say how much greenhouse gas emissions must be reduced. When people and states reduce greenhouse gas emissions, the climate warms more slowly.

## Companies train immigrants

![The municipality of Paltamo hopes that supporting summer employment will also help young people find work in the area in future.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10285756)

Open image viewer

Big companies train immigrants as workers. Photo: Lucas Holm / Yle

Big companies in Finland are training immigrants as workers. The companies train workers for fields where there is a shortage of workers.

The Ministry of Employment is also involved in a large project. The goal is that 2,500 immigrants get a job.

There are now 1,000 people in the training.

## Minister became worried about pupils' eating habits

![Agriculture Minister Jari Leppä at SuomiAreena in Pori.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10309994)

Open image viewer

Minister of Agriculture and Forestry Jari Leppä became worried about pupils' eating habits. Photo: Juha Kivioja / Yle

Minister of Agriculture and Forestry Jari Leppä wants to find out why pupils do not eat school lunch.

In an interview today, Minister Leppä said that he is worried about pupils' eating habits.

A study revealed that many lower secondary school pupils do not eat at school at all.

The minister also wants to find out whether a snack should be served at school.

## Opera star has died

![Montserrat Caballé.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10443338)

Open image viewer

Opera star Montserrat Caballé died at the age of 85. Photo: Nina Parikka / Lehtikuva

The Spanish opera singer Montserrat Caballé has died. She was a famous opera singer all over the world.

Caballé's career lasted 50 years. She performed at big opera houses.

Montserrat Caballé also performed pop music in opera style.

## Roads may be slippery

![Ice on the surface of the road.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10443261)

Open image viewer

At night it is so cold that the water that has collected on the road freezes. Photo: Riina Mäentausta / Yle

Motorists should be careful during the weekend. The road may now be surprisingly slippery.

In Central and Eastern Finland, many minor car crashes happened this morning. The roads were slippery, because water had collected on the road surface.

Next week the weather may turn warm again. A jet stream may bring real summer warmth to Finland.
