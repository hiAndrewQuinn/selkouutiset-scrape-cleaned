# Friday 2.12.2016 (radio)

A large share of Finns do not know which party they will vote for. Formula driver Nico Rosberg is quitting racing. Sámi proverbs can now also be read in Japanese. Head lice are a common problem in daycare and at school.

## Party poll

![Party support graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9328886)

Open image viewer

The SDP, the Centre Party and the National Coalition Party are the biggest parties. Photo: Yle News graphics

A large share of Finns do not know which party they will vote for. In Yle's new poll, over 40 percent of respondents did not know which party they support.

About 21 percent of respondents supported the SDP, that is, the Social Democrats. The SDP is an opposition party, which means it is not in the government.

The other big parties got almost as much support.

The Centre Party's support was about 20 percent, and the National Coalition Party's support was about 19 percent. The Centre Party and the National Coalition Party are in the government.

## Rosberg quits

![Nico Rosberg celebrated the world championship.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9319539)

Open image viewer

Nico Rosberg became world champion last weekend. Photo: AOP

Formula driver Nico Rosberg is quitting racing. Rosberg told about it on Facebook.

Nico Rosberg became world champion last weekend. Rosberg's win was secured in the Abu Dhabi car race.

Many were surprised that Nico Rosberg is quitting racing already now. Rosberg says that the world championship was a dream, after which it is good to stop. Rosberg wants to spend more time with his family.

Nico Rosberg has represented Germany in car racing, but he is also a Finnish citizen. Nico Rosberg's father is the Finnish Formula driver Keke Rosberg. He was world champion in 1982. Nico Rosberg's mother is German.

## Sámi proverbs

![Sátnegirjjit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6726279)

Open image viewer

The Sámi language is related to the Finnish language. Photo: Yle Sápmi

Sámi proverbs are spreading around the world. Now Sámi proverbs can also be read in Japanese. Earlier, a book of them has also been made in Norwegian, English and German.

Sámi proverbs tell what the life of the Sámi has been like in the north. The Sámi have lived close to nature. They have herded reindeer and moved from place to place. This shows in the Sámi language.

The Sámi have lived in Lapland longer than any other people. They are the only indigenous people in the European Union area.

The Sámi live in Lapland in the areas of Norway, Sweden, Finland and Russia. About 10,000 Sámi live in Finland. The Sámi language is related to the Finnish language.

## School lice

![louse parasite](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5328483)

Open image viewer

A louse is really small and it has 3 pairs of legs. Photo: AP Graphics Bank

Head lice are a common problem in daycare and at school. Lice spread from one person to another and cause itching of the scalp.

The problem is big, because not all parents tell the daycare or school that there are lice in the family. Not everyone follows the instructions. The instructions say that the whole family must be checked for lice.

Lice can be removed with lice shampoo.
