# Thursday 7.12.2017 (radio)

Wind turbines cause fewer bird deaths than was thought. Politico believes that Jyrki Katainen has a lot of influence. A flag thief was on the move in Lauttasaari in Helsinki. TV viewers voted for the most beautiful dress at the Independence Day ball. Greetings from the Finnish school in Australia.

## Wind turbines and bird deaths

![A wind turbine](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9811050)

Open image viewer

A wind turbine's rotor is dangerous for birds. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Wind turbines cause fewer bird deaths than was thought. Kalle Meller, a researcher at the Natural History Museum, says that perhaps a couple of birds a year crash into the rotor of each wind turbine.

When wind turbines began to be built in Finland, people believed that there would be many more bird deaths.

Researcher Kalle Meller says that, for example, small birds usually fly so high that they rarely hit the turbines' rotors.

## Politico magazine assesses Jyrki Katainen

![Jyrki Katainen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9642760)

Open image viewer

Politico magazine thinks that Jyrki Katainen has a lot of influence on Europe's future. Photo: Yle

The specialist politics magazine Politico writes that the Vice-President of the EU Commission, Jyrki Katainen, is one of the most influential people in Europe.

Politico has made a list of people who, in the magazine's opinion, will have the most influence on Europe's future next year. Jyrki Katainen is 7th on the list.

Politico writes that Katainen has a big role in the EU Commission. Katainen is responsible for, for example, EU defence cooperation and completing the economic and monetary union.

## A thief took Finnish flags

![Finnish flags flying on poles.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6405527)

Open image viewer

25 Finnish flags disappeared from flagpoles in Lauttasaari in Helsinki. Photo: Yle

A large number of Finnish flags were stolen in Lauttasaari in Helsinki on Independence Day. A total of 25 flags were stolen from the flagpoles of different housing companies, says a caretaker from Vattuniemi property maintenance.

The flags were taken at dusk. It is not known who took the flags and why.

The housing companies can report the theft to the police. Stealing or desecrating a flag can be punished with a fine.

## The most beautiful dress at the Independence Day ball

![Minttu and Kimi Räikkönen at the Independence Day ball on 6.12.2017](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9965446)

Open image viewer

Minttu and Kimi Räikkönen at the Independence Day ball. Photo: AOP

The Independence Day celebration programmes gathered a large TV audience. 3.6 million people watched the independence celebration on Yle's channels.

TV viewers were most interested in the President's reception on the evening of Independence Day.

This year Finns could vote for which of the guests had the most beautiful dress. In Yle's online vote, the dress of Minttu Räikkönen got the most votes. Minttu Räikkönen is the wife of racing driver Kimi Räikkönen.
