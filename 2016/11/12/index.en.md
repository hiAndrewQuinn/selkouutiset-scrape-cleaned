# Saturday 12.11.2016 (radio)

Minister Kai Mykkänen is worried about US environmental policy. Father's Day is celebrated on Sunday. The harms of sitting can be reduced easily at the workplace. It is worth skiing at the weekend; next week it may already rain.

## Climate policy

![Kai Mykkänen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9289216)

Open image viewer

Kai Mykkänen is Finland's Minister for Foreign Trade and Development. Photo: Yle

Minister Kai Mykkänen is worried about US environmental policy. US environmental policy may change when Donald Trump starts as president, Mykkänen said in a television interview on Saturday.

During the election campaign Trump said, for example, that he wants to reduce the money for climate protection. Trump may also want to increase the use of oil and coal.

However, minister Mykkänen says that it is not yet known what Trump's climate policy will finally be like.

The countries of the world are trying to reduce the use of oil and coal so that climate warming can be stopped.

## Fathers are celebrated on Sunday

![father of the year 2016 Jussi Hartikainen kuopio](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9287918)

Open image viewer

Jussi Hartikainen is Father of the Year. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

Father's Day is celebrated on Sunday. Around Father's Day the Father of the Year award is also given. This year the award is given to Pasi Uhtakari and Jussi Hartikainen. They have worked for the good of families.

Jussi Hartikainen lives in Kuopio. In his free time he helps families who are having a hard time because of the parents' divorce. In addition, he works as a sports coach for children and young people. Hartikainen himself has 3 children.

Father's Day is celebrated in many countries, but at different times. In Finland, Sweden, Norway, Iceland and Estonia Father's Day is celebrated on the second Sunday of November.

## Get up from your chair!

![A young woman sits on a chair with her hands on her knees.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7754709)

Open image viewer

It is good to get up from your work chair every half hour. Photo: Katja Oittinen / Yle

Sitting is unhealthy. It is harmful to health if you sit at work all day. Too much sitting increases, for example, the risk of heart disease.

A new study says that the harms of sitting can, however, be reduced. It happens easily. A person just has to get up every half hour and walk for 3 minutes. Then, for example, blood pressure goes down.

Light muscle training is also a good way to interrupt sitting. Stretching your muscles next to your desk is good for your health.

A person can choose for himself whether he walks or trains for 3 minutes. The most important thing is to get off the chair every half hour.

## People are already skiing in the south

![A ski track in Helsinki's Central Park, near the Pasila pedestrian and cycle bridge on 11.11.2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9288133)

A skier in Helsinki's Central Park. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

There are now good opportunities for skiing in Southern Finland. For example, there is a good ski track in Helsinki's Central Park. There are also tracks in Vantaa and Espoo.

It is rare that the tracks are in such good condition already in the middle of November. It is worth skiing at the weekend while the beautiful winter weather continues.

Next week the weather will get warmer. The precipitation will no longer be snow, but rain or sleet. The good skiing conditions will probably end.
