## Space

![Massive galaxy cluster SMACS 0723 (Webb telescope's first official colour image).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4537,h_2552,x_0,y_750/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98074162cca5e2361e8/1657630801)

Massive galaxy cluster SMACS 0723. Webb telescope's first official colour image. Photo: NASA

Completely new information is now being obtained from space.

The first scientific results of the Webb space telescope have been published. Webb was launched into space at Christmas.

The picture shows thousands of distant galaxies and light from more than 13 billion years ago. The objects are almost at the edge of the universe. Images have never before been obtained from so far away in space.

Most important, however, is that from now on images will be obtained continuously. The images tell about the development of stars from birth to death. The images show exoplanets, that is, other stars than planets that orbit the Sun.

The images also give information about the planet Earth.

## Air traffic

![Passengers queue at the airport for a security check.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5388,h_3030,x_0,y_67/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-96835962a33010f210d/1654862062)

The growth in passenger numbers shows in the security check at Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Air traffic in Finland is picking up again at great speed.

A total of 6.6 million passengers travelled through Finnish airports in January–June. The passenger number has risen by 520 percent since early 2020.

The passenger number at Helsinki-Vantaa airport grew by almost 580 percent. A total of 5.5 million people passed through the airport.

However, there were still few transfer passengers, because Finnair's Asian traffic has fallen drastically.

## Euro and dollar

![Series of euro banknotes.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10409107)

Open image viewer

The euro has not been this weak in 20 years. Photo: European Central Bank

The euro's exchange rate has weakened to the level of the dollar. This last happened in 2002.

Compared to the dollar the euro has weakened by 15 percent since the start of the year. The fear of recession in the euro area is weighing on the euro's exchange rate.

European stock exchanges were also falling.

## Dance halls

![Dancing at the Ilosaari dance hall in Joensuu in the early 20th century.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2400,h_1350,x_0,y_382/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-786765605883dc032d3/1616414225)

Dancing at the Ilosaari dance hall in Joensuu in the early 20th century. Photo: North Karelia Museum

There is a wish to develop the Finnish dance hall into a tourism product. This way foreign tourists can also be brought to dance halls.

Finnish dance hall culture is internationally unique.

For example, the Ministry of Education and Culture has a development project for dance halls.

The corona time has been hard for dance halls. Dances could not be arranged, and there was no income. Still the property payments must be paid. In addition, the leaders of dance halls are getting older and there are fewer volunteers. Marketing is also often weak.

Dances are a traditional way to raise money for various associations, especially in the countryside. Often the dance hall is run by a sports club.
