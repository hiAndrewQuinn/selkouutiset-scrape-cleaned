# Saturday 25.2.2023 (radio)

Sami Parliament Act. Vastaamo data breach. Substitution treatment. Spring monitoring. The weather.

## Sami Parliament Act

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689341)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin in the Yle TV studio on Saturday. Photo: Sakari Piippo / Yle

Prime Minister Sanna Marin is disappointed, because the reform of the Sami Parliament Act did not succeed. The reform fell on Friday, because some parties opposed it.

Marin's government's aim has been to promote the rights of the Sami. However, the government parties have quarrelled about the reform of the Sami Parliament Act, and the government party Centre has opposed the reform.

The aim of the reform was to increase the right of the Sami to decide on their own matters. The Sami Parliament Act determines, for example, who may vote in Sami Parliament elections.

The task of the Sami Parliament is to ensure that Sami culture survives in Finland. The Sami are an indigenous people, and about 10,000 Sami live in Finland.

## Vastaamo data breach

![A man's face and a Vastaamo sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12688819)

Open image viewer

25-year-old Julius Kivimäki may have been behind the Vastaamo data breach. Photo: Europol, Juha Kemppainen / Yle, graphics: Aalto Puutio / Yle

New information has been obtained about the data breach at the psychotherapy centre Vastaamo.

A man who may have carried out the data breach has been brought to Finland from France. The French police arrested the man at the start of February.

The man is a 25-year-old Finn. He is now in Vantaa prison.

The police suspect that the man broke into the information system of the psychotherapy centre Vastaamo a few years ago. The man stole people's patient records and may have tried to extort money using the information.

The case is big, because the patient records of more than 33,000 people were stolen in the data breach.

## Substitution treatment

![In Helsinki, next to the Harju youth centre, intravenous drugs are used. Open image viewer

Drug syringe. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Deaths of young people from drugs can be reduced with substitution treatments. This is how Medical Director Margareeta Häkkinen assesses it.

Today substitution treatments are not given to under-18s. Medical Director Häkkinen says that there is no medical obstacle to substitution treatments for young people.

In substitution treatment the patient receives strong medicines, so that they can stop using drugs.

## Spring monitoring

![A flower and a frog in the sun.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12689342)

Open image viewer

Spring monitoring involves observations of plant and animal species. Photo: Anna Sirén / Yle

The Nature League's spring monitoring begins this weekend.

In the spring monitoring, over 8 weekends it is studied how Finland's spring advances. Observations are made of more than 40 plant and animal species.

Everyone can take part in the spring monitoring. You can report your own observations on the spring monitoring website at kevatseuranta.fi.

The Nature League is an environmental organisation which organises nature camps for children, for example.

## The weather

Sunday is a sunny frosty day. There is frost in the daytime of between 5 and 10 degrees. In Lapland it may snow.

On Monday the weather warms up. A foehn wind blows from the west and brings warm air to the whole country. The temperature in Western Finland is as much as 5 degrees above zero.
