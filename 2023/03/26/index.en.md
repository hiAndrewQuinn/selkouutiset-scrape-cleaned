## Helsinki shipyard

![Aerial photo: Helsinki shipyard](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5791,h_3257,x_0,y_100/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7505385fca35c450da4/1607087796)

Open image viewer

Helsinki shipyard. Photo: Janne Järvinen / Yle

A buyer may have been found for the Helsinki shipyard. The Helsinki shipyard has built ships, for example, that suit the northern sea areas.

The Helsinki shipyard is looking for a new owner, because it is doing badly. The reason for the problems is that the sale of ships to Russia has stopped because of the war in Ukraine. European countries do not trade with Russia, because Russia has attacked Ukraine.

Now the Canadian shipyard company Davie is interested in the Helsinki shipyard. Davie builds special ships, for example icebreakers. That is why the Helsinki shipyard suits it well.

In the coming weeks it will be confirmed whether Davie buys the Helsinki shipyard.

## Children's heart operations

![Doctor in a children's hospital corridor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11212540)

Open image viewer

Helsinki has a good children's hospital, but there are far too few intensive care nurses there. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

Child patients from the Helsinki region will start to be operated on in Denmark.

In Denmark heart operations can be done for Finnish child patients. In Finland there is a queue for children's heart operations, because there are too few intensive care nurses for children.

## Summer time

![Long-distance train at the station platform.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11303417)

Open image viewer

At some stations the clocks went wrong when the change to summer time happened. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Summer time has started. The clocks were moved one hour forward early on Sunday morning.

Summer time confused the railway stations' clocks on Sunday. At some stations the clocks stayed on winter time and were an hour late.

At some stations the clocks were right, but the wrong departure time was shown for the trains.

However, the trains ran correctly.

## Advance voting

![Voters and election officials at a polling place.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12690023)

Open image viewer

It has been busy at advance voting places. Photo: Päivi Rautanen / Yle

Already one million people have voted in advance in the parliamentary election.

Advance voting can still take place on Monday and Tuesday. Advance voting places are, for example, in libraries and shopping centres.

At an advance voting place the voter must have an identity document with them. The voter can choose at which advance voting place they vote.

The actual election day for the parliamentary election is Sunday, 2 April. On election day the voter must vote at the polling place that is written on the voter's notification. The voter's notification has come by post.

At selkokeskus.fi there is more information about the elections in plain language.
