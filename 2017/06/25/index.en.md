# Sunday 25.6.2017 (radio)

The Muslim Eid al-Fitr celebration has begun. People died in Pakistan when an oil tanker overturned and caught fire. A Chinese village was buried under a landslide. A district heating pipe broke in central Helsinki. Virtual reality glasses bring variety to elderly people's lives.

## Ramadan has ended

![The sports field of the Oulunkylä sports park filled with people taking part in prayer](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9689188)

In Oulunkylä in Helsinki, over 600 Muslims prayed together when Eid al-Fitr began. Photo: Yle/ Toni Määttä

It is now a time of celebration for Muslims. The Muslim fasting month Ramadan has ended. During Ramadan, adult Muslims do not eat or drink during the daylight hours.

When Ramadan ends, the Eid al-Fitr celebration begins. It lasts 3 days. Good food, gifts and visits are part of the Eid al-Fitr celebration.

Eid al-Fitr began with a time of prayer. Muslims also prayed together in different parts of Finland. For example, over 600 Muslims prayed together in the Oulunkylä sports park in Helsinki.

## An oil tanker accident in Pakistan

![A map of Pakistan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9689050)

Open image viewer

An oil tanker caught fire in the Bahawalpur area in Pakistan. Image: Yle News Graphics

There has been a bad accident in Pakistan in Asia. About 150 people died when an oil tanker overturned and caught fire. Many people were injured.

The accident happened in Bahawalpur, in eastern Pakistan. The oil tanker overturned on the road, and many people came to the place. They wanted to collect the oil that was flowing out of the truck.

But the oil tanker caught fire and exploded. The fire was big. Its cause was perhaps a cigarette.

## A landslide in China

![A map of China.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9688558)

Open image viewer

The landslide happened in the village of Xinmo in the Sichuan region. Image: Yle News Graphics

There has also been a bad accident in China. The accident happened in the Sichuan region. There, the village of Xinmo was buried under a landslide.

Earth and rocks slid down from the mountainside onto the houses. At least 60 homes were destroyed.

![People at the scene of the landslide.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9688619)

Open image viewer

Rescuers are looking for people under the piles of earth. Photo: AOP

Rescuers have found about 10 dead. Over 100 people are still missing. They are being searched for under the piles of earth.

Experts say that the cause of the landslide was perhaps rain.

## Hot water on the streets of Helsinki

![Steam on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9689095)

Open image viewer

There was a lot of steam in central Helsinki when a district heating pipe broke. Photo: reader's photo

There was an unusual situation in central Helsinki on Sunday morning. A district heating pipe broke. Hot water and steam got out of the pipe onto the street. It looked as if there was a lot of fog in the centre.

The hot water also disrupted traffic. The water brought gravel and small stones onto the streets. Some buses and trams also ran on diversion routes on Sunday.

## Virtual reality glasses and the elderly

![virtual reality glasses](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9672082)

Open image viewer

Grannies on a virtual trip. On the left, Eila Harju. Photo: Ville Välimäki / Yle

Virtual reality glasses are also suitable for elderly people. Virtual reality glasses bring adventures to elderly people who can no longer get far from home.

Students from Häme University of Applied Sciences took virtual reality glasses to the elderly people at a service centre and showed videos of seas and the northern lights. All the elderly people wanted to try.

The virtual reality glasses took Eila Harju on a diving trip in the sea. She saw unusual fish. It felt as if I was there myself, a satisfied Harju said after the virtual trip.
