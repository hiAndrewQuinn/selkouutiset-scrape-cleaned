## The order of corona vaccinations

![Eira-Mailis Harmanen received the coronavirus vaccine in Helsinki on 1 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7682566017dcdbba454/1612537664)

Open image viewer

Corona vaccinations started with older people. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Finland is considering the order of corona vaccinations.

First, people over 70 and risk groups are vaccinated. Risk groups include people who have an illness.

Now people are considering how vaccinations will continue after the older people and risk groups have been vaccinated. One option is that more vaccinations are given in areas where there is a lot of corona.

Health authorities will consider the order of corona vaccinations next week. Decisions will be made in April.

So far, about 15 percent of Finns have been vaccinated against corona at least once.

## Bicycle thefts

![The front wheel of a bicycle on a lock.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6856194)

Open image viewer

Only the front wheel was left when the thief took the bicycle. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

Many bicycles are stolen. Police figures show that last year almost 24,000 bicycles were stolen in Finland.

Because of bicycle thefts, insurance companies paid 11 million euros in compensation last year.

## Earth Hour event

![Yle's link tower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5343577)

Open image viewer

The lights of the Pasila link tower will be switched off during Earth Hour. Photo: Seppo Sarkkinen / Yle

Earth Hour is held on Saturday evening. The event is held in various parts of the world.

During the event, people switch off their lights for one hour. This is how they show that they are worried about climate change.

Many companies also take part in Earth Hour. For example, Yle will switch off the lights on the Pasila link tower.

Earth Hour is usually on the last Saturday in March. The event is organised by the World Wide Fund for Nature, WWF.

## Summer time

![Clock face](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10042296)

Open image viewer

At 3 o'clock on Sunday morning, the clocks are moved forward by one hour. Photo: Anssi Leppänen / Yle

Summer time starts on Sunday. The time is moved forward by one hour early on Sunday morning.

Officially, summer time starts on Sunday at 3 o'clock. Then it is 4 o'clock summer time.

Summer time lasts until the end of October. Then the time is moved back by one hour.

Summer time starts and ends at the same time across the whole European Union. However, EU countries intend to stop changing the clocks in spring and autumn. It is not yet known when that will happen.
