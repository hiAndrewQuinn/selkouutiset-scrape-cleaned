EU countries' foreign ministers have visited Ukraine's capital, Kyiv. This was the first time that representatives of all member countries met outside the EU.

In Kyiv the foreign ministers met Ukraine's president Volodymyr Zelenskyy. The meeting discussed the European Union's support for Ukraine.

Finland's Foreign Minister Elina Valtonen says that the visit showed how strong the EU's support for Ukraine is.

## Nobel Prize in Medicine

![Smiling Katalin Karikó and Drew Weissman in white lab coats.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1023,h_573,x_0,y_21/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1180108651aadd8cc2de/1696584819)

Open image viewer

Katalin Karinko and Drew Weissman work at the University of Pennsylvania in the USA. Photo: University of Pennsylvania

This week it is announced who will receive the Nobel Prizes. Today, Monday, the Nobel Committee announced the winners of the Nobel Prize in Medicine.

The winners are Hungarian Katalin Karinko and American Drew Weissman. Thanks to the research of Karinko and Weissman, new kinds of corona vaccines were quickly put into use when corona began to spread.

The Nobel Committee says that the work of Karinko and Weissman has saved the lives of millions of people.

The Nobel Prizes are presented to the winners in a ceremony in December.

## Forged cards

![A cook lifts a large metal item into a trolley.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_429/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99243562f37711efb3d/1660122994)

Open image viewer

A hygiene passport is needed, for example, in the restaurant sector. Archive image. Photo: Marianne Mattila / Yle

The police suspect that forged hygiene passports and occupational safety cards have been sold in Finland. The police also suspect that the perpetrator is a teacher of immigrants.

The teacher may have sold several hundred hygiene passports and occupational safety cards that were not real. Cards were sold even though the buyer did not take part in the training or pass the test.

The police consider the crime especially unpleasant, because forged cards were sold to new immigrants. The police remind that new immigrants need correct information about how things work in Finland.

## Heat in Spain

![People on a beach. A large group sits under sunshades in a dense group.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7745,h_4356,x_0,y_49/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114475664b7dab320aa5/1689770792)

Open image viewer

The heat in Spain just continues. The photo is from July. Photo: Lorena Sopena / AOP

Unusual heat continues in Spain. After a hot summer, the temperature is still high.

On 1 October the temperature in Spain was as high as 38 degrees.

Spain's Meteorological Agency says that the warming of the earth's climate is already visible in Spain.

In the future, summers will be even hotter and the heat will continue far into autumn.

## The weather in Finland

![Tuesday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1180224651adef5bb0a4/1696259882)

Here in Finland, on Tuesday, showers will fall in the eastern part of the country and in Lapland. Elsewhere it is dry. In the west the sun can also shine.

The temperature in the north stays below 10 degrees. In the southern part of the country the temperature may still reach 15 degrees on Tuesday.

Towards the end of the week the weather cools also in the south, and temperatures stay below 10 degrees across the whole country.
