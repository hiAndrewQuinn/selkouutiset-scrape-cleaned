# Sunday 17.6.2018 (radio)

The future of the international system is being discussed at Kultaranta. Mines in the Baltic Sea are at risk of rusting. Frost destroyed strawberry blossoms. Bathing water in Southern Finland is warmer than usual. The weather at Midsummer may be chilly.

## Discussions at Kultaranta

![President Sauli Niinistö and UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres shake hands at a meeting on UN reform in New York on Monday 18 September.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9838710)

UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres and President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: UN / Lehtikuva

The Kultaranta talks, hosted by President Sauli Niinistö, have started. This year the topic is the future of the international system.

The main guest this time is UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres. He will give the closing speech of the talks tomorrow, Monday.

The Kultaranta talks are about foreign and security policy. The talks are held at the president's summer residence in Naantali.

This is the 6th time the event has been organised.

## Baltic Sea mines

![Sea mines on the deck of a minelayer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10250682)

Open image viewer

Sea mines on the deck of a minelayer. The picture was taken in 1943. Photo: Heinrich Hoffmann / AOP

There are still many mines in the Baltic Sea that date from the war.

At the start of this month, about 90 sea mines were cleared from Finland's sea area. The same number of mines was cleared from Estonia's sea area a short time ago.

During the war, perhaps about 60 000 mines were laid in the Gulf of Finland, says marine biologist Anu Lastumäki of the Finnish Environment Institute. Lastumäki is taking part in an international study that is examining the effects of the mines on the Baltic Sea.

The mines on the seabed are already so old that they are at risk of rusting through. If a mine rusts through, TNT explosive gets into the sea. TNT is poisonous.

The study will examine how the explosive that escapes from the mines pollutes the sea and its animals.

## Frost damages strawberries

![Strawberry blossoms.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10258552)

Open image viewer

Strawberry blossom. Photo: Anja Hiltunen / Yle

Part of this summer's strawberry crop has been destroyed by frost.

There have been several frosty nights in early summer, meaning the temperature at ground level has fallen below zero. In many places, frost has destroyed up to 80 percent of the strawberry crop.

The damage has happened, for example, in Suonenjoki in North Savo, where a lot of strawberries are grown. For example, the flowers of the popular Polka strawberry variety have been almost completely destroyed by frost on some farms.

## Bathing water temperature

![A child plays in the water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10203548)

Open image viewer

It is nice to play in the lakes of Southern Finland. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

In Southern Finland, bathing water is a little warmer than usual for mid-June.

In the south and west of the country, the surface water temperature of the lakes is about 15-19 degrees.

In the north, on the other hand, lake water is a little colder than usual. In Lapland's lakes, the surface water temperature is about 8-14 degrees.

## Rain in Midsummer week

![Rain falling.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9682826)

Open image viewer

Several rain areas will reach Finland next week.

The weather turns rainy and windy.

The Finnish Meteorological Institute says that several rain areas will reach Finland next week. There will be thunder showers in North and East Finland at least. On Tuesday it will also rain in Southern Finland.

At the moment it looks as though Midsummer will also be cool and rainy.
