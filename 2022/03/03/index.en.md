## Niinistö to Washington

![Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8174,h_4588,x_0,y_170/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9187436214cc6680c1a/1645530511)

President Niinistö is travelling to Washington. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finland's President Sauli Niinistö will meet US President Joe Biden. The presidents will meet on Friday in Washington.

Niinistö and Biden will discuss Russia's attack on Ukraine. They will discuss European security and the cooperation between Finland and the USA.

President Niinistö said on Thursday that rapid changes are taking place in Finland's surroundings.

Russia attacked Ukraine a week ago. Russia wants to prevent Ukraine from joining the NATO military alliance.

It is estimated that more than a million people have already fled Ukraine because of the war.

## Paralympics

![The Beijing Paralympic flame is lit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4389,h_2468,x_0,y_663/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-922714621f6cf13f514/1646226699)

The Paralympic flame is burning in Beijing this time. Photo: CHINE NOUVELLE/SIPA/Shutterstock/All Over Press

Russian and Belarusian athletes are not allowed to take part in this year's Paralympics. The Paralympics begin on Friday in Beijing, China.

The International Paralympic Committee did not give permission to Russian and Belarusian athletes to take part in the Beijing Games because of the war in Ukraine.

Para-athletes compete in the Paralympics. The Beijing Winter Paralympics last until 13 March.

## Corona patients

![Two corona patients in an isolation room, with a nurse examining the other patient.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2806,x_10,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-88951361b0af6316d97/1638969772)

There are about 700 patients with a coronavirus infection in hospitals. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The National Institute for Health and Welfare (THL) has reported on Finland's corona situation.

THL says that there are still many corona patients in hospitals. There are about 700 patients with a coronavirus infection in hospitals.

THL recommends that all adults take 3 corona vaccinations. Corona vaccinations prevent very serious corona disease well.

## Party support

![Petteri Orpo, chair of the National Coalition, on a television broadcast.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_384/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90693161f1417164b62/1643201815)

Open image viewer

National Coalition chair Petteri Orpo. Photo: Sakari Piippo / Yle

The National Coalition's support has risen. This is what Yle's opinion poll shows. In it, people were asked which party they support.

The biggest party in the poll is the National Coalition. Its support is over 22 percent.

The second biggest party is the SDP, whose support is over 18 percent.

The Finns Party is in third place. The party's support is over 15 percent.

So the National Coalition clearly gets more support than the other big parties.

Researchers estimate that competition between the parties will decrease, as politicians focus on the Ukraine crisis.

The support figures for all parties are at yle.fi.

## Weather

On Friday the sun shines across the whole country. However, colder air flows in from the north.

The temperature is above zero only on the south and west coasts. Elsewhere it is 1 to 8 degrees below zero.

Pavements may now be very slippery. On Saturday more snow falls, but only in the north.
