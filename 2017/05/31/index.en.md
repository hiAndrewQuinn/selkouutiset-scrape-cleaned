# Wednesday 31.5.2017 (radio)

The government is having trouble with the alcohol law. The police suspect discrimination crimes in the Defence Forces. VR angers disabled people. Bears want to have cubs now.

## The alcohol law is in trouble

![Strong long drinks on a shelf at Alko in Helsinki. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9641442)

Open image viewer

Long drinks like these may also go on sale in grocery shops if the alcohol law is approved after all. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Finland's new alcohol law is in big trouble. The government has tried to change the rules about what kind of alcohol can be sold, for example, in grocery shops. But a bad dispute about the reform broke out in the government parties.

The dispute is about whether grocery shops can sell, for example, strong beer and long drinks with a strength of 5.5 percent. Now they can sell, for example, beer with a strength of 4.7 percent.

Many people want the sale of alcohol in Finland to be freer than now. Others oppose, for example, strong beer coming into shops. An alcohol researcher says that if strong beers and long drinks come to grocery shops, people's health will get much worse.

## The police suspect discrimination crimes in the Defence Forces

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9447338)

Open image viewer

The police are investigating suspected discrimination against Finnish-Russian dual citizens.  Photo: Yle News Graphics

The police suspect that people working in the Defence Forces have discriminated against, that is, treated wrongly, dual citizens. The police are investigating two cases in which a crime may have happened. The cases concern Finnish-Russian dual citizens.

In one case it is suspected that a Finnish-Russian dual citizen was discriminated against when they applied for work in a restaurant in an army area. The person was told that they could not be given work because of their dual citizenship.

In the other case a Finnish-Russian dual citizen had almost started a job in which they would have had access to the Defence Forces' information system. But the job was blocked when the person's dual citizenship became known.

## VR's changes worry disabled people

![Disability parking sign.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9087637)

Open image viewer

Disability organisations are worried about VR's services, which are hard for disabled people to use.  Photo: Risto Degerman / Yle

Disabled people are worried about the changes made by the train company VR. For example, from next month you can no longer buy a train ticket on local trains in the Helsinki area. You can buy a ticket, for example, from a ticket machine. Blind people say that they cannot use the machine.

Disabled people with mobility difficulties are also worried about VR's services. Disabled people say that they cannot get into, for example, VR's new restaurant cars.

## Bears are moving around a lot now

![Bear.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9642017)

Open image viewer

Bears are on the move now and can be seen even near houses.  Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Bears are moving around a lot now because they are looking for food and a place to live. In addition, bears are now in heat, which means they want to have cubs. Bears may now be seen near areas where people live.

There are many of them especially in eastern Finland. It is estimated that there are now about 2000 bears in Finland.
