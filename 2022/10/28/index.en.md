Swedish Prime Minister. Psychotherapy centre Vastaamo. Combustion engine cars. Air ticket prices. Weather.

## Swedish Prime Minister

![Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson and Finnish Prime Minister Sanna Marin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_136/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1026775635bd0ed53d1d/1666961743)

Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson has visited Finland. In Helsinki he met Finnish President Sauli Niinistö and Prime Minister Sanna Marin. They talked about Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership and about Ukraine. Prime Minister Kristersson said that he will meet the Turkish Prime Minister in early November in Turkey. Turkey has not approved Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership.

## Psychotherapy centre Vastaamo

![Vastaamo logo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5195,h_2922,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7676376013b40835829/1611904070)

Open image viewer

Photo: Lehtikuva

The National Bureau of Investigation suspects a Finnish man of hacking the Vastaamo psychotherapy centre. The suspect is a 25-year-old Finnish man who is abroad. A European arrest warrant has been issued for the man's arrest. Two years ago it was reported that the data of over 30,000 clients had been stolen from the Vastaamo psychotherapy centre.

## Combustion engine cars

![Exhaust pipe of a diesel car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10136704)

Open image viewer

Photo: Ina Fassbender / AFP

The European Parliament and the EU Council have agreed on a ban on the sale of combustion engine cars. New combustion engine cars may not be sold in the EU area after 2035. Combustion engine cars will be replaced by electric cars. The purpose of the ban is to reduce car emissions and slow down global warming.

## Air ticket prices

![Scheduled domestic flights from Joensuu airport go only to Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12535388)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jarno Artika / Pohjois-Karjalan maakuntaliitto

Air ticket prices will rise in the coming years. Experts estimate that the price of one flight will rise by at most a few tens of euros. Air ticket prices will rise because renewable fuel is used in aircraft. It is more expensive than ordinary fuel. People are also flying much more than during the corona pandemic.
