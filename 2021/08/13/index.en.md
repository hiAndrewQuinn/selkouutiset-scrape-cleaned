## The Koskela murder case

![Candles and flowers at the Koskela murder site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_141/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7528905fd2055696827/1613658337)

People took flowers and candles to the Koskela murder site. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

A court case about the murder of a young boy has continued in Helsinki.

A 16-year-old boy was murdered in Koskela in Helsinki in December. Three boys of the same age are accused of the murder.

The boys have been given a psychiatric examination during the spring and summer. The people who carried out the examination say that the boys understood what they were doing. That is why they can be sentenced to punishment.

The court will announce the punishments on 3 September. The boys are waiting for their punishment in prison.

The case has been talked about a lot in Finland, because the act was brutal. The boy died when the other boys assaulted him for many hours.

## Masks on Finnair flights

![In the picture a flight attendant works on a Finnair flight AY 435 from Helsinki to Oulu in September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7187735f5f8d5138960/1600172549)

Open image viewer

On a Finnair flight everyone must have a good mask. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Finnair no longer accepts cloth masks on flights.

Passengers must have a surgical mask or an even more effective FFP2 or FFP3 mask. The order comes into force on Monday.

Finnair passengers must wear a mask for the whole flight. A passenger may take the mask off only when they eat or drink.

## Corona vaccinations

![Sakari Siika-aho (left) gets his first corona vaccine from nurse Maija Tilli, Jätkäsaari vaccination point, Helsinki, 19.6.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5980,h_3364,x_0,y_28/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82317760cdc3f2d05f5/1624179127)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

In Finland corona vaccinations are being considered.

An expert group is considering at the end of August whether it is good to give corona vaccines 3 times. Nowadays the corona vaccine is given 2 times.

Mika Rämet, director of the Vaccine Research Centre, hopes that the matter will be decided only later in the autumn. By then many will already have received 2 corona vaccines.

It is not yet known when Finland will decide whether Finns will be given a 3rd corona vaccine. It is also possible that the 3rd corona vaccine will be given only to risk groups, that is, the elderly and the sick.

In some countries it has already been decided to give residents 3 corona vaccines. However, the Director-General of the World Health Organization hopes that rich countries will not yet start a 3rd round of vaccinations, so that there are enough corona vaccines for poor countries too.
