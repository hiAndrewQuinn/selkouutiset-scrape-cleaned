# Wednesday 29.4.2020 (radio)

Schools may open. Corona infections in Finland. Health centres. Finnair's problems. The weather.

## Primary schools may open on 14 May

![Empty classroom.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11328822)

Open image viewer

The schools have been closed for 6 weeks. Photo: Marja Väänänen / Yle

The Finnish government must decide whether the schools will open before the end of this spring.

The schools have been closed for 6 weeks.

According to the original decision, they will remain closed until 13 May.

Yle has been told in advance that primary schools will probably open right after that, that is, on 14 May.

Upper secondary schools and vocational educational institutions will probably remain closed.

The health authorities said today that there is not a single child in Finland who has been taken to hospital because of the corona.

Symptoms in children have been mild.

This information has been an important reason why primary schools can be opened.

## 206 people have died of corona in Finland

There are now officially more than 4,900 coronavirus infections in Finland.

206 people have died because of the coronavirus.

Most of the infections are in people around 50 years old.

## You can go to a health centre

![Tuusula health centre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_48/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6445675e4d433bb2a98/1582354092)

Open image viewer

Doctors are worried because sick people do not dare to go to the health centre. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

At health centres, doctors are worried.

They are worried that sick people do not dare to go to a doctor because of their illness, because they are afraid of a coronavirus infection.

People do not dare to go to the health centre, or they think that they do not want to bother anyone now because of their own illness.

The doctors say that this is not the way to think.

At health centres, doctors and nurses do have time to treat patients.

Customers are looked after at health centres in such a way that there is no need to fear a coronavirus infection.

Doctors remind people that an illness can get worse if you wait too long before going to a doctor.

## Finnair is in trouble

![Captain (left) and first officer in the cockpit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11189001)

Open image viewer

Finnair will have to lay off all its employees. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The airline Finnair is in trouble.

Finnair says that it is losing 2 million euros every day, because many flights have been cancelled.

Finnair estimates that air traffic may perhaps start to increase in July.

Finnair has already announced that all employees will be laid off. The layoff may have to be continued.

Many airlines are in difficulty now.

Norway's Norwegian is almost bankrupt.

## It is cool on the eve of May Day

Cool air is coming from the north to Finland.

Tomorrow, Thursday, the weather will be partly cloudy or cloudy.

In places there will be showers of rain or snow.

During the day the temperature is above zero, but at night the temperature will be below freezing.
