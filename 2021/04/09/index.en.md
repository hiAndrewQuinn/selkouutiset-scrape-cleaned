# Friday 9.4.2021 (radio)

Prince Philip has died. Lifting corona restrictions may start as early as this month. The number of corona infections has fallen. The early voting period for the municipal elections is longer than usual. Flags fly for Agricola and the Finnish language.

News of sorrow has been announced in Britain. The Duke of Edinburgh, Prince Philip, has died. Philip was 99 years old.

Philip was the husband of Queen Elizabeth. Philip and Elizabeth had been married for more than 70 years. By birth, Philip was Prince Philippos of Greece and Denmark.

Philip was in hospital a short time ago because of heart problems.

## Lifting corona restrictions

![Sanna Marin and Henrik Haapajärvi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_192/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-792774606ffa0aed7bb/1617951331)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin spoke about lifting corona restrictions. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Sanna Marin has spoken about plans to lift corona restrictions.

Lifting the restrictions may be able to start as early as this month.

Basic schools, upper secondary schools and vocational schools may perhaps start face-to-face teaching during this month.

Restaurants may also be able to open their doors, but opening hours and numbers of customers will still be limited. Other restrictions will be lifted when it is seen how the corona situation develops.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin says that the corona situation is still serious. Restrictions can only be lifted if the number of corona infections falls. If the disease situation gets worse, restrictions will be tightened.

## Finland's corona situation

![A person going into a corona vaccination place. There is a poster on the door that says corona vaccination point.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3884,h_2185,x_0,y_407/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7862586054be84d2374/1616169018)

Open image viewer

More than a million people in Finland have received the corona vaccine. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

The number of corona infections in Finland has fallen a little in recent weeks.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that over the last 2 weeks about 6,800 infections have been reported. That is almost 2,700 fewer infections than during the 2 weeks before.

Today about 420 new infections and 2 new deaths have been reported.

More than a million people have now received the corona vaccine.

## Early voting for the municipal elections

![Municipal elections 2021 logo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11809667)

Open image viewer

There are more than 1,000 early voting places for the June municipal elections. The Ministry of Justice says that there are more than 900 places in Finland where you can vote early. There are about 100 early voting places abroad.

The early voting period is now also longer than usual. Early voting in Finland starts at the end of May and lasts 2 weeks.

The actual election day for the municipal elections is 13 June.

## Day of Agricola and the Finnish language

![Picture of Mikael Agricola's Abckiria.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5099672)

Open image viewer

Mikael Agricola's Abckiria was the first book in Finnish. Photo: Yle

Today the day of Mikael Agricola and the Finnish language has been celebrated.

Mikael Agricola is considered the father of the Finnish written language. Agricola published the first book in Finnish in 1543. The book was called Abckiria.

Mikael Agricola's Day is a flag day.
