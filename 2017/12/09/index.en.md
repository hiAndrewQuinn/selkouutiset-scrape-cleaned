# Saturday 9.12.2017 (radio)

2 people died in the Gulf of Finland when a pilot boat capsized. Iraq's Prime Minister says that the war against the terrorist organisation Isis has ended in Iraq. People hope for longer rehabilitation for aphasia patients. A time capsule was built in Kuopio.

## A pilot boat capsized

![Coast Guard divers prepare to turn the pilot boat over in the Gulf of Finland on 8 December.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9969415)

The pilot boat capsized, and here people are trying to turn it over. Photo: Finnish Border Guard

There has been an accident in the Gulf of Finland. The accident happened at sea near the town of Porvoo.

In the accident, a pilot boat capsized. There were 2 workers in the boat. Both died.

A pilot's job is to steer or guide ships into port. Pilots know the difficult sea routes of their own area better than the captains of foreign ships.

It is not yet known why the pilot boat capsized.

## Iraq defeated Isis

![Iraqi troops on their way to fight Isis near the town of Rawah on 25 November.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9969651)

In Iraq, the war against the terrorist organisation Isis has ended. Iraq's Prime Minister Haider al-Abadi announced this. The Prime Minister said that the Iraqi army now controls the whole territory of Iraq.

Isis took over large areas in Iraq in 2014. The Iraqi army has taken the areas back. Iraq has received support in the war from other countries, for example the USA.

## Rehabilitation helps with aphasia

![The communication folder of a person with aphasia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9966547)

Open image viewer

Aphasia is a language disorder. Photo: Petra Ristola / Yle

People hope for longer rehabilitation for aphasia patients.

Aphasia is a language disorder that adults get. Because of aphasia, it is hard for a person to understand and produce language.

In Finland there are tens of thousands of people who have aphasia. The cause of aphasia can be, for example, a brain injury from an accident. The most common cause of aphasia is a disorder of blood circulation in the brain.

Docent Kati Renvall says that patients' rehabilitation often ends too early. Rehabilitation lasts a year or 2 years, but that is not always enough.

Renvall studies, for example, how a person learns to use abstract words in rehabilitation. For example, words like "lovely" or "awful" help a person talk about feelings and opinions. Usually in rehabilitation people practise only concrete words, for example "food" or "doctor".

Docent Kati Renvall is one of the researchers who have written the first Finnish non-fiction book about aphasia.

## Things for the people of the future

![A time capsule.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9969091)

Open image viewer

The time capsule was closed on Friday. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

A time capsule was closed in Kuopio on Friday. It is made of steel and wood.

The time capsule contains objects, pictures and writings from today's Finns. The idea is that people will open the time capsule in 100 years, when Finland turns 200.

The people of the future will find in the time capsule, for example, a memory stick, a CD and a baseball cap. We do not know what people will think of such things in 100 years.
