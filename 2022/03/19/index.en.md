# Saturday 19.3.2022 (radio)

Peace talks for Ukraine. An Estonian researcher on how long the war will last. Music. Minna Canth. Weather.

## Ukraine

British intelligence says that Russian forces have advanced only a little in Ukraine this week.

At the same time Russia has heavily shelled cities that it has besieged. In the port city of Mariupol Russian forces have advanced to the city centre.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy demands that wide-ranging peace talks with Russia be started.

In a TV speech Zelenskyy said that now is the time to restore Ukraine's territorial integrity. Otherwise Russia's losses will be so great that Russia will need many dozens of years to recover, Zelenskyy said.

The director of the Estonian Foreign Policy Institute, Kristi Raik, also warns about unconventional weapons. These include, for example, biological weapons.

An Estonian researcher estimates that the war in Ukraine will last a long time.

The director of the Estonian Foreign Policy Institute, Kristi Raik, says that Russia's military actions may become even more brutal. At the same time the risk grows that Russia will also use unconventional weapons in Ukraine.

Kristi Raik says that Finland's NATO membership would be as logical a step as Finland's joining the European Union. Finland joined the EU in 1995.

Raik was interviewed on Yle's Ykkösaamu.

In Raik's view, the security situation in the Baltic Sea would improve if Finland and Sweden joined NATO.

## Music

![Singing together sessions in the Minnansali hall of the Main Library have been very popular.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10588153)

Open image viewer

Singing together in the library. Photo: Esa-Pekka Autio

Finnish research shows that music can help healing. Music rehabilitates, for example, people who have brain damage.

The effect of music on the brain has been studied with new imaging methods. Music can repair nerve connections and increase blood circulation in the brain.

With the help of music, strong painkillers have been reduced after surgery.

Music also helps with memory illness. Singing together in particular can support memory function and mood in the early stages of the illness.

## Minna Canth

![The Minna Canth Kuopio exhibition opens at the Kuopio Block Museum.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10772702)

Open image viewer

Minna Canth was born on 19 March 1844 in Tampere. Photo: Kuopio Museum of Cultural History

On Saturday Minna Canth and Equality Day were celebrated. It is 178 years since Canth's birth.

Canth is the first woman to have her own flag day in Finland. Canth was a writer, social activist and journalist.

Canth wrote, among other things, about the position of women and the poor in society.

In addition, today Library Day has been celebrated.

## Weather

Sunday is going to be the warmest day of spring so far.

The temperature rises, in many places as far as Southern Lapland, above 10 degrees. In the west of the country it may be 15 degrees.

In Northern and Central Lapland it is cloudier, and in places it snows.

Early in the week sunny and warm weather continues.
