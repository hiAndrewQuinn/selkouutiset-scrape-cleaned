# Wednesday 7.9.2016 (radio)

Three hours of exercise for children. Asylum appeals cause a court backlog. Kyyjärvi wants to keep its asylum seekers. Protecting the humpback whale. Veera the dolphin becomes leader of the pod in Greece.

## Three hours of exercise for children

The authorities have published new recommendations, that is, guidelines, on children's exercise. The recommendation says that small children under eight years old must move for at least three hours every day.

The exercise must also be varied. It must include light exercise but also heavy exercise. Sitting still for a long time is a bad thing.

## Court backlog from asylum appeals

![Law books.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8561396)

Open image viewer

The court may have to make 10,000 decisions on appeals against asylum decisions.  Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Asylum seekers have appealed a lot of asylum decisions to the court. It is estimated that the court may get as many as ten thousand appeals. That means that ten times more appeals come to the court than last year.

The court rejects many appeals. The court estimates that 13 percent of the appeals are such that there is a good reason for the appeal. Then the Immigration Service must make a new decision on asylum.

## Kyyjärvi does not want to give up its asylum seekers

![Line manager Tarmo Pölkki (left), Mohammadnadir Amiri, Mohammadnabi Nazari and purchasing manager Timo Noponen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9136858)

Open image viewer

In Kyyjärvi, people praise the municipality's asylum seekers for being hard-working and polite.  Photo: Sanna Savela / Yle

The small municipality of Kyyjärvi in Central Finland has started to defend the municipality's reception centre. In Kyyjärvi, a large list of names, that is, a petition, was collected for Minister of the Interior Paula Risikko. In the petition, almost half of the people of Kyyjärvi say that the reception centre must continue its work.

At first many did not want a reception centre in Kyyjärvi, but soon people started to like the asylum seekers. People in Kyyjärvi say that the asylum seekers are hard-working employees, polite, and bring a lot of joy.

## Protecting the humpback whale is succeeding

![A humpback whale jumps out of the water, a mountainous shore in the background. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9147018)

Open image viewer

A humpback whale can be 18 metres long.  Photo: Octavio Aburto / Greenpeace / EPA

Protecting whales in the world's seas has brought good news. For example, there are more humpback whales in the world than before, because the protection has succeeded.

There are now so many humpback whales that the United States no longer protects them. But the United States does not give permission to kill humpback whales.

A humpback whale can be 18 metres long. It was endangered for many decades, because many whales were killed.

## Särkänniemi's Veera became the leader

![Särkänniemi's dolphins in Greece.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9127519)

Open image viewer

Särkänniemi's dolphin Veera became the leader of the whole dolphin pod at the Attica zoo.  Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

The Särkänniemi dolphins that moved to Greece have now been able to swim together with the zoo's own dolphins. The dolphin trainers were nervous about whether the dolphins would fight over who is the leader of the pod. But everything went well, and the leader of the pod is now Veera. She is the oldest in the dolphin pod, 38 years old.

The trainers say that the age difference between the dolphins was clear. The zoo's own dolphins are much younger than the Särkänniemi dolphins.
