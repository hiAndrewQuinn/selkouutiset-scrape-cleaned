## The talks ended without an agreement

![US President Donald Trump and North Korean leader Kim Jong-un strolled in the garden between the talks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10666162)

The meeting between the USA and North Korea ended early and without results. Photo: Saul Loeb / AFP

The talks between the leaders of the USA and North Korea have ended in Hanoi, Vietnam.

The talks ended early, without an agreement. Donald Trump and Kim Jong-un were to hold a signing ceremony, but it was cancelled.

North Korea was ready to dismantle some of its nuclear weapons, but not the ones that the USA would have wanted. North Korea wanted all sanctions to be lifted. Trump did not agree to this.

Trump said that the talks will continue, even though they have now ended without results.

## A lot of bullying happens in hobbies

![child playing football](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10664920)

Open image viewer

A lot of bullying is encountered in children's and young people's sports hobbies. Photo: AOP

Bullying is common in sports hobbies.

31 percent of children and young people have experienced bullying, discrimination or inappropriate behaviour in a sports hobby. Over-15s experience more bullying than younger ones.

Bullying is most common among boys and in teams. Ethnic and gender minorities have the hardest time. Bullying in sports hobbies often means belittling, mocking or excluding someone from the group.

The study was made by the Finnish Youth Research Society.

## Elina Pekkarinen is the new Children's Ombudsman

![Elina Pekkarinen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10666788)

Elina Pekkarinen is becoming the new Children's Ombudsman. Photo: Trond H. Trosdahl / Lehtikuva

The new Children's Ombudsman is Elina Pekkarinen. She will start the job at the beginning of May.

Pekkarinen is currently a fixed-term professor at the University of Turku. Pekkarinen has a doctorate in social sciences.

The duties of the Children's Ombudsman are currently being carried out by Tuomas Kurttila.

## Escape rooms have serious shortcomings

![Corridor of an escape room](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10643098)

Open image viewer

One fifth of Finland's escape rooms have serious safety shortcomings. Photo: Elise Tykkyläinen / Yle

Finland's escape room games have many shortcomings. In total, serious safety shortcomings were found in 19 escape room games. The Ministry of the Interior says that leaving an escape room has not been safe enough.

The ministry checked the safety of a hundred escape rooms. About half of them had minor shortcomings in how safely people can get out.

An escape room is an adventure game in which players try to get out of a locked room within a time limit. They try to get out of the room using reasoning and cooperation.

Escape rooms began to be inspected in Finland after 5 young people died in a fire at an escape room in Poland in January.

## Flags fly on Kalevala Day

![The Finnish flag flutters on a flagpole in Salo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10502579)

Open image viewer

On Thursday flags have been flying in honour of Kalevala Day. Photo: Yle

On Thursday, Kalevala Day was celebrated. It is the day of Finnish culture, and it is celebrated in honour of Finland's national epic.

Elias Lönnrot dated the preface to the first version of the Kalevala 28 February 1835.

Kalevala Day is also an official flag day.
