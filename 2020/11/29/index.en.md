# Sunday 29.11.2020 (radio)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin hopes that people will spend Christmas in a small group. There are 322 new coronavirus infections in Finland. The coronavirus situation has got worse in Pirkanmaa. The first Sunday of Advent has been celebrated in Finland. The week starts in rainy weather.

## The Prime Minister comments on the coronavirus situation

![Prime Minister Sanna Marin arrives for the government negotiations at the House of Estates in Helsinki on 25 November 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11665888)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin has commented on Finland's coronavirus situation.

In Marin's view, it is good that Finland has noticed how serious the coronavirus situation now is. Coronavirus restrictions have been brought in in different parts of Finland. Marin says that the coronavirus situation may still get even worse.

The Prime Minister hopes that people will spend Christmas only with a few family members or friends, not in a large group. Marin says that everyone must also remember safe distances and face masks if they meet older people at Christmas.

Journalists interviewed Marin on Sunday in the radio programme Prime Minister's Interview Hour.

## Coronavirus infections in Finland

There are 322 new coronavirus infections in Finland. Most infections were found on Sunday in the Helsinki and Uusimaa area.

The total number of coronavirus infections in Finland is almost 25,000.

The coronavirus situation has got worse in the Tampere area, that is, in Pirkanmaa.

A lot of new coronavirus infections have been found. For example, on Friday 39 new coronavirus infections were found. That is more than ever before in one day.

Now new coronavirus recommendations are coming to the Tampere area. It is not good to hold events with more than 10 people, and for example indoor sports facilities and swimming halls should be closed.

The new coronavirus recommendations for the Tampere area, that is, Pirkanmaa, start on 2 December and end on 22 December.

## The first Sunday of Advent

![Candle and advent](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6602913)

Open image viewer

The first Advent candle was lit on Sunday. Photo: Yle

The first Sunday of Advent has been celebrated in Finland.

The first Sunday of Advent starts the Advent time, that is, the waiting for Christmas. On the first Sunday of Advent, the first Advent candle is lit in many homes.

There are 4 Sundays of Advent before Christmas. That is why there are 4 Advent candles in total.

Many Finns go to church on the first Sunday of Advent. This year many have followed the Advent celebration online, because there may not be large crowds in churches because of the coronavirus.

The week starts in rainy weather. In the south it is raining, and in the north it is snowing.

The temperature is around zero. In the south it is a few degrees above zero, and in the north it is a little below zero. At night there may be frost in the whole country.
