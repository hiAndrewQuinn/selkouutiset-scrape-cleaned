Train strike begins. Bank merger in Switzerland. Minna Canth's day. Weather forecast.

## Train strike begins

![Train from Espoo centre towards Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_208/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10469936397399216625/1671005676)

The strike by train drivers begins on Monday. The strike will stop passenger train traffic across all of Finland from 1 a.m. in the morning.

All long-distance and commuter passenger train services will be cancelled. VR is cancelling services one day at a time. For example, now on Sunday VR is cancelling Monday's services and on Monday Tuesday's services.

This will continue until the end of the strike.

No end date has been announced for the strike. The strike will end when an agreement is reached. Negotiations will continue already on Monday.

The parties were still negotiating today, on Sunday. The negotiations were led by National Conciliator Anu Sajavaara. However, no agreement was reached.

## Bank merger in Switzerland

Switzerland may consider nationalising Credit Suisse, in whole or in part, to rescue it. Then the state could take the bank into state ownership or acquire a significant number of shares in the bank. Photo: LEHTIKUVA / AFP

In Switzerland a major bank merger, that is a combining of banks, is being negotiated. Switzerland's largest bank, UBS, may buy Credit Suisse bank for about one billion dollars.

Credit's reputation has suffered, because it has made losses on loans worth billions of euros.

The bank has also been suspected of money laundering. In money laundering, property that has been acquired in a criminal way is made to look legal.

If the merger happens, it will be the biggest bank merger in Europe for a long time. The previous financial crisis was in 2007–2009. The crisis started in the USA.

There has been talk in Europe of a new banking crisis. Bank of Finland Governor Olli Rehn says that the threat of a crisis in Finland and Europe is now small. The banking sector is in strong shape, and banks now have strong ability to pay.

## Minna Canth and equality

![Minna Canth's Kuopio exhibition opens at the Kuopio district museum.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10772702)

Open image viewer

Minna Canth wrote plays, novellas and short stories. She was also a businesswoman. Photo: Kuopio Museum of Cultural History

On Sunday Minna Canth's Day was celebrated.

Minna Canth was a Finnish writer, journalist and social activist. Canth was born in 1844.

Canth's life's work has had a big effect on Finland becoming a pioneer of equality and education.

Canth is the only Finnish woman who has her own established flag day. Minna Canth's Day is also the Day of Equality.

This year in October, Miina Sillanpää's day will also become an established flag day. Sillanpää was born in 1866, and she was the first Finnish female minister.
