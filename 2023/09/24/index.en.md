## Sick leave

![A woollen sock peeks out from under a blanket on the leg of a person lying on the sofa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6093,h_3427,x_0,y_428/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6700805eaacaa706d90/1725092141)

Some people lie that they have the flu. Photo: Jarkko Riikonen / Yle

Yle has asked Finns about their sick leave. Some people lie about the reason for their sick leave. People tell their boss or doctor that, for example, they have the flu, even though it is not true. The flu is an easy reason for sick leave.

Instead of the flu, the reason for a short absence may be, for example, mental health reasons. If an employee lies about the reason for sick leave, the employee may get a warning from the employer.

The government is planning a change in the law, so that the first day of sick leave may in future be an unpaid day. The aim is that people do not take unnecessary sick leave easily. Many people do not like the government's plan. The danger is that a person goes to work while sick, if they do not get paid for a sick day.

## Mink farms

![Minks in cages at a fur farm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7408,h_4167,x_7,y_527/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115665864db31c3becb6/1692094791)

There are about 120 mink farms in Finland. Photo: Janne Järvinen / Yle

The authorities are inspecting all mink farms in Finland. The mink farms are being inspected before the influenza season begins in Finland, if possible. The authorities fear that people's flu may start to spread to minks. Then the virus may become dangerous to humans.

The influenza season in Finland is often in October and November. All mink farms may not be possible to inspect before then. There are about 120 mink farms in Finland.

Migratory birds will soon leave Finland. That may ease Finland's bird flu situation.

## Young people's crimes

![Young people standing on a street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3693,h_2077,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114901964c3d164b9445/1723638857)

Young people's crimes have increased this autumn. Photo: Joel Peltonen / Yle

Young people's crimes have increased now this autumn. The Helsinki police say that young people's crimes have increased, now that summer has ended and schools have started.

The police say that violent crime and robbery by young people have increased in a few years.

The police are worried that young people's crimes are increasing. In particular, the police are worried that crime by young people under 15 years of age is increasing.

## Finland's largest pumpkin

![Five gigantic pumpkins in a row are looked at by an audience.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3855,h_2169,x_784,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1176138650ef8332abd7/1695479959)

Open image viewer

Big pumpkins took part in the big vegetables competition. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Finland's largest pumpkin weighs almost 500 kilos.

The big vegetables competition was held in Mäntsälä this weekend. The competition was won by a pumpkin that weighed 487 kilos.

Big pumpkins can weigh hundreds of kilos. The world's largest pumpkin has weighed over 1200 kilos. It was weighed in Italy in 2021.
