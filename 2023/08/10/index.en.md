## The situation in the technology industry

![Device in a laboratory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5887,h_3311,x_0,y_416/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-112588564806f47d4b50/1686138758)

For example, the electronics industry is part of the technology industry. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

The situation of technology industry companies is getting worse in Finland. The volume of technology industry production has not grown at all this year.

The value of new orders in the technology industry fell by 5 percent in April to June compared with April to June last year. Still in early in the year, that is, in January to March, the value of new orders was 12 percent higher than at the same time last year. That is why it looks like the growth of the technology industry is stopping and may turn into a decline.

The technology industry is Finland's biggest export sector. That is why its situation affects the whole Finnish economy a lot.

Branches of the technology industry include, for example, the electronics industry, machinery and metal products industry, design and consulting, and information technology.

## The situation in the whole industry

![Cracker furnaces at the Borealis factory area in Borgå.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5409,h_3042,x_0,y_424/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-106921163e106fcc16fa/1675693596)

Orders have fallen the most in the chemical industry. Photo: Frida Frankenhaeuser / Yle

The economic situation is getting worse in other industrial sectors too. Orders in the whole of Finnish industry fell by more than 12 percent in June compared with June last year.

Orders have fallen the most in the chemical industry and the paper industry. Orders in the chemical industry fell by 28 percent, and orders in the paper industry fell by 24 percent.

For the whole first half of the year, that is, January to June, industrial orders fell by almost 7 percent compared with January to June last year.

## School absences

![A music upper secondary school classroom, where there are also screens between the desks.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2268,h_1266,x_0,y_1559/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-89243561b9e0f437ad2/1639572496)

Open image viewer

Absences worry principals. Photo: Raila Paavola / Yle

Principals of comprehensive schools are worried about pupils' absences from school. More than 43 percent of comprehensive school principals say that absences are a big problem. Absences from school are a problem when the same pupil is absent a lot.

The principals estimate that the reasons for frequent absences are mental health problems, heavy use of digital devices and changes in family situations.

Finnish principals were asked about school absences in a survey carried out by Lastenklinikan kummit (the Children's Clinic Godparents), the Finnish Principals' Association and MTV News.

## Blue-green algae

![Blue-green algae in shallow water on the shore.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5349,h_3008,x_0,y_160/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114815064c22160b9020/1719233896)

Open image viewer

Blue-green algae can look like this. Photo: Paula Collin / Yle

There is less blue-green algae than usual now. The Finnish Environment Institute, Syke, says that this week there have been fewer sightings of blue-green algae than is usual at this time of year.

There is less blue-green algae because the winds have broken up blue-green algae blooms in the sea and in lakes.

There is some blue-green algae, but no very heavy blue-green algae blooms have been observed anywhere, for example.
