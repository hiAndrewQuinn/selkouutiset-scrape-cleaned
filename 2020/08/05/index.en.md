## The Beirut explosion

![Aerial photo of the explosion site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11479584)

Open image viewer

The explosion happened in the port area of Beirut. Photo: AFP

A really big explosion has happened in Beirut, the capital of Lebanon.

The explosion happened on Tuesday evening in the port of Beirut. The explosion happened in a building where almost 3,000 tonnes of ammonium nitrate had been stored. Ammonium nitrate is a chemical that is used, for example, to make fertilisers and bombs.

More than a hundred people have died in the explosion and thousands have been injured, says the Lebanese Red Cross. As many as 300,000 people were left without a home.

The Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs says that one Finn was also injured in the explosion. In addition, the Finnish embassy was badly damaged.

## The corona situation in Finland

![A sign says there is a coronavirus testing point at the airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11476934)

The number of coronavirus infections is also growing in Finland. In the last day, 29 new infections have been found. In total, more than 7,500 infections have now been found.

The number of infections in Finland has almost doubled during the week, that is, it has grown by nearly 100 per cent from the previous week. In the last 7 days, 98 new infections have been found in total. During the 7 days before that, 53 new infections were found.

In total, 331 people have died of the coronavirus in Finland.

## The price of face masks

![A woman wearing a respirator at Ruoholahti metro station in Helsinki on 4 August](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11480538)

Open image viewer

A face mask can prevent the spread of the coronavirus. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, will say tomorrow whether it intends to recommend face masks to stop the spread of the coronavirus.

If Finland gets a recommendation on face masks, following it could cost 100 euros a month. Face masks cost about one euro each.

Estimates say that a person uses more than 100 masks a month if they go to work and use the masks correctly. The masks are disposable, that is, the same mask may be used only once.

A face mask covers the mouth and nose, and that is why it can stop the spread of the coronavirus.
