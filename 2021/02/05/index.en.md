# Friday 5.2.2021 (radio)

THL has said in what order risk groups will be vaccinated against corona. Corona virus variants are spreading around the world. Runeberg's Day was celebrated on Friday. At the weekend it is sunny and cold.

## The order of corona vaccinations

![Erkki Partanen received a corona vaccine in Helsinki on 1 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7682606017dcdd9e499/1630559484)

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, has given more information about corona vaccinations.

THL said on Friday in what order vaccinations will begin for under-70s. Vaccinations start with risk groups.

The corona vaccine is given first to risk groups who have a very high risk of getting serious corona. They include, for example, people with cancer.

After that other risk groups are vaccinated, for example people who regularly use asthma medicines.

Municipalities organise the corona vaccinations. Municipalities also tell residents where and when vaccinations are given.

THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, has said that 401 new corona infections have been found in Finland. In total there are almost 47,000 corona infections in Finland.

## Corona virus variants

![Corona testing guidance at Helsinki-Vantaa airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11757376)

Open image viewer

Attempts are being made to stop the spread of corona virus variants with travel restrictions. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Corona virus variants are spreading around the world. Variants have also been found in Finland.

About 200 corona infections have been found in Finland that were caused by a corona virus variant.

The most common corona virus variant found in Finland is the British one. The British variant spreads easily and quickly.

In Finland there are, however, fewer corona virus variants than in many other countries. Finland has strict travel restrictions so that variants do not come to Finland with passengers.

## Runeberg's Day

![A statue of national writer Johan Ludvig Runeberg in Pietarsaari, where the family lived for years.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6389745e3aae6d8724d/1580905816)

Open image viewer

Photo: Raila Paavola / Yle

Runeberg's Day was celebrated on Friday.

Johan Ludvig Runeberg is Finland's national poet. Finland's national anthem, Maamme, is a poem by Runeberg.

Runeberg lived in the 1800s. He was Swedish-speaking and wrote in Swedish.

Runeberg's Day is celebrated on 5 February, because it is Runeberg's birthday. Runeberg's Day is a flag day.

On Runeberg's Day in Finland people eat Runeberg tarts. Roughly similar tarts were already eaten by Runeberg 170 years ago.
