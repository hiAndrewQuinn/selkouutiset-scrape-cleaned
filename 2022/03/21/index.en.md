# Monday 21.3.2022 (radio)

More than 9,000 Ukrainians have applied for asylum in Finland. In Ukraine, the city of Mariupol has been almost completely destroyed. Many open jobs are being filled with temporary agency workers. Stockmann is selling its Helsinki department store. The market share of Finnish online shops has grown. The weather is warm for spring.

## Ukrainian refugees

More than 9,000 Ukrainians have applied for asylum in Finland.

The Finnish Immigration Service says that most of them are applying for temporary protection in Finland. In reality, more than 9,000 people have come to Finland from Ukraine.

Not all Ukrainians apply for protection. That is why nobody in Finland knows exactly how many Ukrainians have come to Finland.

## EU and Ukraine

![Josep Borrell](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2929,h_1644,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91965362173e5941bbd/1645690514)

The EU's foreign policy representative Josep Borrell spoke on Monday at the EU foreign ministers' meeting. Photo: EPA-EFE/All Over Press

The EU's foreign policy representative **Josep Borrell** says that Russia's attack on Mariupol in Ukraine is a major war crime.

Borrell spoke at the opening of the EU foreign ministers' meeting. Borrell said that Russia is destroying everything in Mariupol and bombing people so that people are dying.

The city of Mariupol has been almost completely destroyed. About 300,000 people are still in the city, and they have not been able to leave it.

## Temporary work agencies

![In the picture are construction workers in central Helsinki in October 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4500,h_2531,x_0,y_77/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7340945f955588a567d/1636459224)

Open image viewer

For example, a lot of temporary labour is used in construction. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

More and more open jobs are being filled through staffing firms, that is, with temporary agency workers. About 30 percent of jobs are offered through staffing firms. This is what Yle's MOT programme's survey says.

The MOT programme studied job advertisements on the public employment service's website. Construction companies and industry in particular use temporary agency workers.

The use of temporary work is increasing. About 50 new temporary work agencies have been established in Finland during the year.

The most free jobs in Finland are for nurses, salespeople and restaurant workers.

## Stockmann department store company

![Stockmann's building in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_383/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83467760fa610e853ad/1627021648)

Open image viewer

The Stockmann department store was designed by architect Sigurd Frosterus.  Photo: Alamy / All Over Press

The department store company Stockmann is selling the building of its Helsinki department store. The Stockmann department store in Helsinki is the most famous department store building in Finland. It was completed in 1930.

Stockmann is selling the department store building to the earnings-related pension company Keva. The purchase price is 400 million euros.

Stockmann is selling the department store because Stockmann has been doing badly. However, Stockmann will continue its department store business in the building under a lease agreement.

## Online shops

![The online shops of the K and S groups on a screen. Apples.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4030,h_2267,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7227035f6c6afbcfd39/1600940942)

The market share of Finnish online shops has grown.

Last year, 64 percent of Finns' online shopping was done in Finnish online shops. Five years ago, the share was 57 percent.

Finns are buying more from Finnish online shops than before. During the corona time, older people in particular have started shopping in online shops.
