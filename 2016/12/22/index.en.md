## The government presented the health and social services proposal

![Tipotie health and social services centre sign](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9193122)

In 2019 everyone can choose for themselves which health and social services centre they want to go to. You cannot change centre until a year has passed since your first visit. Photo: Terri Niemi / Yle

The government has drafted a bill on the health and social services reform, known as sote. Sote means the reform of social services and health care.

When a person falls ill, he or she can choose which health and social services centre to get treatment from. These include private and public sote centres and the centres of some organisations. The price of basic care is the same everywhere.

Responsibility for the centres lies with the regions. For example, the capital Helsinki is organised by the Uusimaa region. There will be 18 regions. The regions make sure that services are available to everyone and everywhere in Finland.

The reform is meant to start at the beginning of 2019.

The opposition says that the reform will transfer taxpayers' money to large health companies.

## The National Police Board wants to shut down the neo-Nazi organisation

![Police Commissioner Seppo Kolehmainen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9369356)

Police Commissioner Seppo Kolehmainen says the assessment that the Nordic Resistance Movement is illegal is based on many factors. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

The National Police Board says that the neo-Nazi organisation Nordic Resistance Movement should be shut down. The National Police Board says that the reason is, for example, the organisation's violent activity.

Police Commissioner Seppo Kolehmainen also says that the Resistance Movement is trying to overturn the social order.

The hearing of the case in court will perhaps begin in the first half of next year.

## Fish is on the Finnish Christmas table, even though it is expensive

![Pieces of salmon on crushed ice](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9367970)

Open image viewer

Finns eat a lot of fish at Christmas. Many are ready to pay a high price for Christmas fish. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

Norwegian salmon is Finland's most popular Christmas fish. Norwegian salmon is farmed fish. Its price changes almost every day. The Norwegian capital Oslo has a salmon exchange, which sets the price of salmon. The price of salmon is about 25 per cent higher than a year ago.

Many Christmas tables also have whitefish, which is farmed on the west coast of Finland and in Åland. Whitefish is also a little more expensive than a year ago. Herring is eaten most in Finland at Christmas and Midsummer.

Many are ready to pay a lot for fish just because of Christmas.

## Frost Santa and Santa Claus bring joy

![Graphic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9368169)

Open image viewer

We have gathered the most important differences between the traditional Frost Santa and Santa Claus. Photo: Hilppa Hyrkäs / Yle Uutisgrafiikka

In Finland Christmas is already at the door. At the same time, in Russia people are waiting for the country's biggest celebration, New Year. The best party guest for Russian children is Frost Santa. The Finnish Santa Claus has also been warmly welcomed in the capital, Moscow.

Not all Russian children know that Santa Claus comes from Finland and Lapland. For example, Maria from Moscow said that Frost Santa was born in Russia and Santa Claus in America. Liza believed that Santa Claus lives in Lapland, which is an island.

Russian Frost Santa differs from the Finnish Santa Claus. Frost Santa brings presents to children only at New Year. What then is common? Frost Santa knows. He says that the task of both is to bring joy and happiness.
