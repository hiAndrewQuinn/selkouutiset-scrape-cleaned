# Tuesday 28.2.2023 (radio)

Bus strike begins tonight. Vastaamo data breach. Fixed-term electricity contracts. Eastern border fence. Defence Minister changed. The weather.

## Bus strike

![Bus with a route display that says "Not in service".](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5557018)

Open image viewer

The bus strike will stop especially local transport in big cities. Photo: YLE/ Juha Kivioja

A strike in bus traffic begins tonight.

The strike will stop, for example, a large part of the local transport in big cities. For example, in Helsinki and Tampere it is estimated that a large part of bus trips will not run.

The strike is to last until next week's Friday.

Mediator Jukka Ahtela gave a compromise proposal for the labour dispute late in the afternoon. The workers' organisation AKT did not approve the proposal.

## Vastaamo

![Psychotherapy centre Vastaamo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4470,h_2514,x_148,y_1201/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7354795f996f1ee6db2/1605690427)

Open image viewer

The data breach at the psychotherapy centre Vastaamo became public in autumn 2020. Photo: Jorge Gonzalez / Yle

The West Uusimaa District Court has jailed a man who is suspected of the data breach at the psychotherapy centre Vastaamo.

The man is a 25-year-old Finn. He is suspected, for example, of aggravated extortion and aggravated data breach. The man himself says that he has not committed these crimes.

The information system of the psychotherapy centre Vastaamo was broken into a few years ago. The thief took the patient records of more than 33,000 people. The information was published on the internet, and the thief also tried to extort money using it.

## Fixed-term electricity contracts

![Tampere electricity company's bill and cash.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4980,h_2795,x_0,y_520/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1031127636a54c435bde/1731581377)

Open image viewer

Many households have an expensive, fixed-term electricity contract. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

The Energy Industries says that tens of thousands of households have an expensive, fixed-term electricity contract.

About 57,000 households pay more than 30 cents per kilowatt hour for electricity. About 150,000 households pay 20-30 cents per kilowatt hour for electricity.

Most of the fixed-term contracts are two-year contracts,

The number of contracts was investigated in a survey which the Energy Industries made for electricity companies.

## Eastern border fence

![A forest machine clears trees in a snowy landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_360/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-107884263fde5134d0fc/1677584395)

Open image viewer

In June there will be a 3-metre-high fence here. Photo: South-Eastern Finland Border Guard

The building of a fence on the border between Finland and Russia has begun. A stretch of about 3 kilometres of fence is being built in Imatra.

The work started yesterday, when trees were felled on both sides of the Imatra border crossing point.

A 3-metre-high fence will rise on the border, and a technical monitoring system will be in it. A road for maintenance work and border patrols will also be built next to the fence.

This first test fence is to be ready by the end of June.

Finland's Defence Minister has changed.

Mikko Savola has asked to resign, and Antti Kaikkonen has returned as Defence Minister.

Savola was Kaikkonen's substitute, because Kaikkonen was on parental leave for a couple of months.
