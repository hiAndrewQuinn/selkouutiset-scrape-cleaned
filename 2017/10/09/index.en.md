# Monday 9.10.2017 (radio)

The Finnish presidential couple is having a child. The health and social services reform is moving forward. Wave power is being developed in Finland. Aleksis Kivi Day is celebrated on Tuesday.

## Family news

![Jenni Haukio and Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9873540)

Jenni Haukio and Sauli Niinistö are expecting a baby in February. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The Finnish presidential couple is having a child. The baby of President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio will be born in February.

Sauli Niinistö and Jenni Haukio got married in 2009. They say that they have long hoped for a child.

The baby is Haukio's first child and Niinistö's third child. From his first marriage the president has 2 sons, who are already adults.

The Presidential Palace says that the birth of the child will not affect the president's work. This is the first time that a baby has been born in Finland to a president who is in office.

## Social and health care reform

![Health care staff by a surgical robot.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9850845)

Open image viewer

There have long been attempts to reform health services. Photo: Markus Sjöberg / Yle

The social and health care reform is moving forward. The reform concerns social services and health services.

The government's new plan includes, for example, customer vouchers. A customer can go to, for example, an eye operation anywhere in Finland and pay for the operation with a customer voucher. A customer voucher can only be used for important treatment, not, for example, for cosmetic surgery.

The aim of the social and health care reform is that a person can choose for themselves where they get treatment. They can choose whether they want to go to a public or a private health centre. People will be able to choose their own health centre in 2021.

The health services are to be reformed in Finland so that there is enough money for good services all over Finland in the future too.

## Wave power

![Wave power plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9871431)

Open image viewer

A wave power plant is being assembled in Järvenpää, to be taken to Portugal. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

Wave power is being developed in Finland. The idea is to turn the movement of sea waves into electricity. It happens in a wave power plant.

Finns want to sell wave power plants to many countries. A wave power plant is already being assembled in Järvenpää, to be taken to Portugal. Negotiations are also being held with many other countries.

Wave power is a good way to make electricity. It does not pollute nature, and the waves of the sea do not run out.

## Aleksis Kivi Day

![Aleksis Kivi statue in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7838881)

Open image viewer

Aleksis Kivi is Finland's national writer. Photo: Seppo Sarkkinen / Yle

Aleksis Kivi Day is celebrated on Tuesday.

The writer Aleksis Kivi lived in the 1800s. He wrote, for example, the book Seven Brothers.

Aleksis Kivi is called Finland's national writer. Therefore Aleksis Kivi Day is also the day of Finnish literature.

Aleksis Kivi Day is always celebrated on 10 October, because it is Kivi's birthday. On Aleksis Kivi Day, flags are flown.
