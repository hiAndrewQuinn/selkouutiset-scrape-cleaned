# Wednesday 4.1.2017 (radio)

Errors in tax cards. MP fined for hate speech. Clothes are expensive in Finland. The vaccine works half as well for old people as for young people. Pensioner Raimo Virkkunen measures the ice. The frost is biting.

## Errors in tax cards

![Pre-filled tax return.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9323705)

Open image viewer

A new tax card always comes at the turn of the year. Photo: Yle

About 300,000 Finns have received a tax card with the wrong tax percentage.

The cause of the error is in the tax authorities' computer programme. The authorities are sending a letter to those Finns who have received the wrong tax percentage.

Most tax cards are correct, because 4.7 million of them were sent in total.

## MP fined for hate speech

![Finns Party MP Teuvo Hakkarainen in court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9386781)

Open image viewer

MP Teuvo Hakkarainen (right). Photo: Antti Seppälä / Yle

MP Teuvo Hakkarainen has been fined for hate speech on the internet. Hakkarainen was fined about 1,000 euros.

The court sentenced Hakkarainen for a Facebook post that mocked and threatened Muslims.

Finns Party MP Teuvo Hakkarainen told the court that he did not know that the law forbids inciting hatred against a group of people.

## Clothes and restaurants are expensive

![Sale.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9385834)

Open image viewer

You can get clothes a bit cheaper in a sale. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Clothes and restaurant food are expensive in Finland. They are more expensive than in other countries that use the euro.

Over the last ten years, especially the price of food here has risen more than in other euro countries. Shoes cost on average 120 euros in Finland, and 100 euros in other euro countries.

It is more expensive here because wages and taxes are higher than in other countries. Finland also has less competition between companies than other countries.

## The vaccine protects children better than the elderly

![A woman is given a flu vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9291998)

Open image viewer

A vaccine does not protect 100 per cent against flu. Photo: Juha Korhonen / Yle

The flu vaccine does not protect old people as well as young people.

Many people over 65 catch flu even though they have had the vaccine. The vaccine reduces the flu risk of people over 65 by only about half this winter. The poor effect is caused, for example, by the fact that a person's own resistance weakens with age. The vaccine protects small children much better.

## The ice measurer

![Raimo Virkkunen measures the thickness of the ice at Pyhäselkä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9380457)

Open image viewer

Raimo Virkkunen. Photo: Heikki Haapalainen / Yle

Raimo Virkkunen, a pensioner from Joensuu, measures the ice on the lake every winter. Virkkunen goes to the lake three times a month, makes a hole in the ice and measures how thick the ice is.

Virkkunen sends the information to the authorities. This way Finns know when it is safe to go onto the ice of lakes and seas, for example to fish, ski or drive a car.

The environmental authorities have such helpers in different parts of Finland.

## Record frost

![A frozen flower.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9384790)

Open image viewer

The frost creates art. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

On Wednesday and Thursday the weather is really cold. In the north there is hard frost. In Inari it was 39.8 degrees of frost during the day.

In the south there is less frost, but the air feels really cold because the wind is strong.

People usually get used to cold weather in about a week. After a week the body no longer freezes as easily, and cold weather no longer stresses you as much.
