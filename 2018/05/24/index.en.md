# Thursday 24.5.2018 (radio)

Finland's greenhouse gas emissions decreased. Job placement services are going to be reformed. The wilderness economy has great potential for growth. English is the most popular foreign language in comprehensive schools. Nature is now bone dry.

## Greenhouse gas emissions decreased

![cars on Länsiväylä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10219241)

Open image viewer

More biofuels than before are used in traffic. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finland's greenhouse gas emissions decreased last year, says Statistics Finland.

Total greenhouse gas emissions were about 56 million tonnes of carbon dioxide. Emissions fell by almost 5 percent compared with the year before.

Emissions decreased, for example, because more biofuels than before are used in traffic.

## Job placement may move to private companies

![A person looking for work on a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6795376)

Open image viewer

The reform of employment services is due to start in 2020. Photo: Yle

There are plans to let private companies handle more job placement than before.

The reform of employment services is due to start in 2020, at the same time as the regions start their work. The idea is that the regions can outsource employment services to private companies, even completely.

Private job placement has been tried in different parts of Finland in recent years. Job placement companies have been paid a fee if they manage to find jobs for unemployed people.

However, it is not known whether private companies manage to find work for unemployed people better than TE Offices.

Parliament will vote on the reform in the summer.

## The wilderness economy hides growth potential

![A hunter near a bog pond next to trees in Kuusamo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9463055)

Open image viewer

A hunter on the shore of a bog pond in Kuusamo. Photo: Ensio Karjalainen / Yle

For the first time, the size and growth prospects of the wilderness economy have been studied in Finland. The study believes that the wilderness economy has great potential for growth.

Business linked to the wilderness economy includes, for example, hunting, fishing, and watching and photographing large predators.

There are about 800 wilderness tourism companies in Finland. The wilderness economy provides jobs especially in remote areas

## English is still pupils' favourite language

![An English lesson.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5452871)

Open image viewer

Most Finnish children start studying English already in primary school Photo: Yle

English is the most commonly studied foreign language in comprehensive schools, says Statistics Finland.

Last autumn term, 71 percent of primary school pupils and almost all lower secondary school pupils studied English.

18 percent of primary school pupils and 93 percent of lower secondary school pupils studied Swedish as a foreign language.

## You must now be careful with fire

![aerial view of a forest burning](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10221424)

Open image viewer

A peat bog is burning in Kaustinen. Photo: Jyri Jäntti / Rescue Department of Central Ostrobothnia and Pietarsaari

Nature is now bone dry, that is, really dry in a large part of the country. Nature is dry because the weather has been mostly dry for a long time and only a little rain has come.

The rescue department says that you must now be really careful when you handle fire. The rescue department says that it is better not to light an open fire at all. You must also be really careful if you light, for example, a charcoal grill, or even just throw a cigarette butt on the ground.

Nature is so dry that a small spark can start a fire, which spreads easily in dry terrain.

A forest fire warning is now in force in almost the whole country.
