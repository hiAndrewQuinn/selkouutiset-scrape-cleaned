# Radio | News Week in Clear Finnish | Sunday 11.2.2024

Presidential election result

This week's News Week is about the presidential election.

Finland's new president is Alexander Stubb. He beat Pekka Haavisto in the second round of the presidential election. Now we tell about the results of the second round and the winner of the presidential election.

## Second round

Finns have chosen a new president. The new president is Alexander Stubb.

Stubb won the second round of the presidential election. Election day was Sunday.

There were 2 candidates, Alexander Stubb and Pekka Haavisto.

Stubb got about 52 percent of the votes. Haavisto got about 48 percent of the votes.

Stubb was the National Coalition Party's presidential candidate. Pekka Haavisto was the presidential candidate of a voters' association, which also got support from the Greens.

Stubb and Haavisto got to the second round of the presidential election, because they received the most votes in the first round 2 weeks ago.

Finland's next president, Alexander Stubb, is an experienced politician. He has held many important posts.

Stubb has been, for example, Prime Minister and Foreign Minister of Finland. He has also been Finland's representative in the European Parliament.

Stubb speaks many languages. In addition to Finnish and Swedish, he speaks English, French, German and Italian.

Stubb is 55 years old. Stubb's partner Suzanne Innes-Stubb is from England. The family has 2 adult children.

The presidential election interested Finns a lot. About 71 percent of the people who have the right to vote in the presidential election took part in voting.

The election interested people because the president leads Finland's foreign policy.

In the middle of the world's crises and wars, Finns want a president who takes care of Finland's security.

Finns have trusted President Sauli Niinistö. Now Finns hope that the new president will continue Niinistö's work.

The topic of News Week was the second round of the presidential election.

Alexander Stubb received the most votes. He will become Finland's next president.

The Finnish president changes on 1 March. Then the president's power and responsibility will pass from Sauli Niinistö to Alexander Stubb.
