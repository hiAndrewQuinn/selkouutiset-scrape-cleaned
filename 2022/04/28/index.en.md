## Protection of Ukrainians

![Ukrainian civilians on a bus in Zaporizhzhia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_7870,h_4426,x_0,y_61/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-94460062617d4856cfd/1650556283)

Open image viewer

Already 20,000 refugees from Ukraine have applied for temporary protection in Finland. Photo: Roman Pilipey / EPA

Finland has granted temporary protection to 13,000 Ukrainian refugees.

The Finnish Immigration Service says that almost all decisions have been positive. A small part of the applications have lapsed, because the applicant has withdrawn the application.

After Russia's attack, more than 20,000 applications for temporary protection have been submitted in Finland.

Russia attacked Ukraine on 24 February.

## Ukrainian students

![Students at Tampere University on 4 September 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11569969)

Open image viewer

Finnish universities are taking in Ukrainian students. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Ukrainian refugees will get the opportunity to study at Finnish universities.

The Ministry of Education says that it supports Ukrainians' studies and guidance with separate funding.

The ministry estimates that 2,000–4,000 students can be taken on for studies.

## Home tests

![Home test kits hang on pharmacy racks. For sale are ovulation, urinary tract infection, drug, H. pylori and coeliac home test packs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_232/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9465716267e30367462/1650976340)

Open image viewer

Health home tests at a pharmacy. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

More and more home tests for measuring health are coming all the time.

At home you can take a test, for example, for sexually transmitted diseases, coeliac disease or high cholesterol.

The results can be used in public healthcare. In this way, laboratory work can be reduced.

A home test is also handy if the journey to a laboratory is long, as, for example, in Northern Finland.

## Rainforests

![Trees and other vegetation in a rainforest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9548705)

Open image viewer

Amazon rainforest in Brazil. Photo: Ralf Hirschberger / EPA

Last year a large amount of tropical rainforest was destroyed. 11.1 million hectares, that is, 111,000 square kilometres, were destroyed.

The amount can be compared like this: forest is destroyed in an area the size of a football pitch every 6 seconds.

Most of the destruction happened in Brazil, where natural forests were cut down over 15,000 square kilometres.

## Frogs

![Common toad](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6680939)

Open image viewer

Common toad. Photo: Kalevi Keskinarkaus

Now it is the frogs' spawning time.

Frogs thrive especially in small ponds where there are no fish. Biologist Antti Koli says that sound is the best way to identify the different frog species.

In Finland, 3 frog species live in the wild: the common frog, that is, the brown frog, the moor frog, and the common toad, that is, the warty frog.

The call of the brown frog is a low croak. The common toad also croaks, but its call is shorter and sharper.

The call of the moor frog is a kind of bubbling.

![May balloons at Tammelantori in Tampere.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3024,h_1701,x_0,y_1167/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-800628608bb013ad601/1619767396)

On May Day the weather is not very spring-like or warm. Photo: Maria Salovaara / Yle

The weather for the May Day weekend is cold, the Finnish Meteorological Institute says. Snow and rain are forecast for Finland, and they may continue for the rest of the week.

The highest temperature of the day is around 5 degrees. Towards the end of the week the temperature will not rise above 10 degrees.
