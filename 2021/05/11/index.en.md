# Tuesday 11.5.2021 (radio)

There has been a devastating school shooting in Russia. Yle's election compass has opened. Parliament votes on the EU recovery package on Wednesday. The weather is warm.

## School shooting in Russia

![Ambulances on the street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4641,h_2611,x_0,y_326/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-804728609a392183062/1620719975)

In Russia a young man shot people at a school.

At least 7 children and 2 adults died in the shooting. There may be more victims. There are also many injured.

An official says that there was only 1 shooter. He is a 19-year-old man. The shooter was arrested.

The shooting happened in Kazan, in central Russia.

## Yle's election compass

![Yle's municipal election compass shown on a phone screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8041946097eeec592ac/1622701493)

As a new feature in the election compass, every candidate has been asked three themes that are especially important to them. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Yle's election compass is now open online.

In the election compass you can get to know the candidates in the municipal elections. More than 15,000 municipal election candidates from different parts of Finland have answered the compass's questions.

The election compass has 26 questions or statements that you can answer. After that, the election compass tells you which candidates think in the same way as you.

## Recovery package

![Parliament building.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_573/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-780487603f6c9081ff6/1614769389)

Open image viewer

Parliament building. Photo: Lotta Laakso/Yle

Parliament votes on the EU recovery package on Wednesday. The recovery package could fail if a large enough part of the MPs do not support the recovery package.

The government wants Finland to approve the recovery package. The government needs support from the opposition party, the National Coalition.

The EU recovery package is 750 billion euros. The European Union wants to give money to EU countries that have suffered from the coronavirus.

## Astra Zeneca

![Astra Zeneca vaccine bottle and vaccine syringe.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4837,h_2721,x_0,y_529/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8043406099055756fdb/1620641255)

The European Union is demanding more corona vaccines from the pharmaceutical company Astra Zeneca. The EU demands that Astra Zeneca must send 120 million doses of vaccine by the end of June.

Astra Zeneca earlier promised the EU many vaccines, but the company has not kept its promise. The EU has started a lawsuit against Astra Zeneca.

## Heat record

Tuesday has been the warmest day of the spring.

The highest temperature was measured in Hyvinkää and Vantaa. It was 25.1 degrees warm. So this was the first hot day of the year.

The weather continues warm in the southern and central parts of the country. In Lapland it is cool at first, but there too the weather will warm up during the rest of the week.
