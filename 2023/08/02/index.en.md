## Bird flu and hunting

![Map showing the infection zones set up because of bird flu by region: Southwest Finland, Satakunta, South Ostrobothnia, Ostrobothnia, Central Ostrobothnia, Uusimaa, Kanta-Häme, Päijät-Häme and Pirkanmaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1079,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114751264c0cf82ebd8f/1690357789)

The Finnish Food Authority recommends restrictions on hunting waterfowl. Waterfowl must not be hunted in areas where bird flu has been found or where a large number of birds have died.

In addition, gulls and crows must not be hunted at all for training dogs. Flu viruses can survive also in frozen meat.

Meat from game birds that has not been cooked must not be given to dogs. If a dog or a cat has bird droppings on it, the pet must be washed carefully.

Bird flu does not easily spread to people.

## Taxi Act

![Taxi stand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4170,h_2345,x_0,y_260/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10452796391b02a0b522/1685530538)

The Taxi Act is going to be changed. The aim is that it will be easier to get a taxi than now, for example in the countryside. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

The Taxi Act is being changed again. The work is due to start in September. The government programme says that taxi services must be made better than now.

In 2018 the licensing system and the obligation to be on duty were removed from the law. Taxis have been able to operate anywhere and whenever they want.

When there is no obligation to be on duty, it has been difficult to get a taxi in small towns and in the countryside.

## Earth Overshoot Day

![A parent and child walk down an aisle of Verkkokauppa.com.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98167262cee840d3c0b/1657728393)

Open image viewer

Humanity has used more this year than the Earth can produce. Photo: Aalto Puutio / Yle

Today is World Earth Overshoot Day. People on Earth have used up in 7 months the natural resources that are meant to last the whole year.

Overshoot causes, for example, climate change and loss of nature, which weakens the functioning of nature.

Finland's Earth Overshoot Day was much earlier this year, already at the end of March.

If everyone lived like Finns, 3 and a half Earths would be needed. This is how Sitra, which studies the future, among other things, calculates.

## Trump's new charges

![Donald Trump points his finger at the camera.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2550,h_1434,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115056264ca0f2fc1286/1690963821)

4 new criminal charges have been brought against Donald Trump, the former president of the USA.

Trump is charged, for example, with conspiring against the US government. He is also charged with manipulating witnesses, that is, steering them.

The special prosecutor of the Department of Justice says that Trump and Trump's allies had a plan to undermine the peaceful transfer of power. The aim was to keep Trump in the presidency, even though he lost the presidential election.

In January 2021, Trump's supporters attacked the Capitol building, when Congress was about to confirm Joe Biden's election victory.

Trump wants to become president again and leads the Republicans' presidential race by a wide margin.
