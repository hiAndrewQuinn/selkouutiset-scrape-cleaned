# Thursday 27.10.2022 (radio)

There is a lot of coronavirus infection. Fewer babies were born than ever before during the statistics period. People estimate that the economy will develop badly. The weather is cloudy.

## Coronavirus

There is again a lot of coronavirus in Finland.

THL says that during October about 12,000 new corona infections have been recorded in Finland every week. There are more corona patients in hospitals than before.

Only a few people, however, have a serious form of the disease. The reason is that a large part of people have taken corona vaccinations. The vaccination gives good protection against serious corona disease.

## Babies

According to preliminary statistics, just over 34,000 children were born from January to September. This baby boy was born at the start of September. Photo: Esa Huuhko / Yle

In early in the year fewer people were born in Finland than ever before since 1900. Between January and September about 34,000 children were born. Over 3,500 fewer children were born than in the same period last year.

At the same time more than 46,000 people died in Finland. This is the largest number since 1945.

Finland's population grew by almost 11,000 people. The reason is that people moved to Finland from abroad.
\_ Correction note: 30.10. at 15.00 The correct number of children born, which is 34,000 children and not 3,400 children, has been corrected in the text.\_

## Economic development

![People at the checkouts of a grocery shop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_521/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-98165762ced8449682a/1664256865)

People do not believe that the Finnish state's economy is developing in a good direction. People also doubt that their own finances are going in a bad direction. People now have very little money to buy various goods.

Companies too think that finances will go badly in future.

The survey was carried out by the Confederation of Finnish Industries, EK.

## Emigration

![A Finnair plane takes off from the airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2875,h_1617,x_703,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1005590631b1ba07bfb1/1701790760)

Open image viewer

Taxation does not affect people's decisions to move. Photo: Tuomas Rimpiläinen / Yle

Finland's high taxation has only a small effect on whether Finns move abroad. Research says that people do not make the decision to move based on taxation.

Researchers say that taxes do not need to be lowered because of emigration from Finland.

Fewer than 10,000 people move abroad from Finland every year. Finns move most to the other Nordic countries and to other Western European countries.

## Weather

The weather is cloudy. In South Finland and Central Finland it rains now and then. In the north it also snows.

On Friday during the day the temperature is +5 to +12, in the north between 0 and +5 degrees.

On the roads the driving conditions are poor in many places in the north. The reason is snowfall or sleet.
