## Corona increases mental health problems

![People on Aleksanterinkatu in Helsinki in July 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_131/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7013135f17c27c55643/1623402060)

Open image viewer

The corona situation has increased anxiety. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

The corona situation has increased anxiety among Finns. More calls come to crisis helplines, and more messages come to online support services than before.

Many Finns are worried about their own health and the health of their loved ones. The fear of whether their own job will continue and of their family's financial situation has also grown among many Finns.

Because the corona situation is changing people's everyday lives, mental health problems may grow a lot.

## Restrictions for passengers from Skopje

![A border guard wearing protective equipment was there to meet passengers arriving in Finland from Skopje in North Macedonia.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11500879)

Open image viewer

A border guard wearing protective equipment is at Turku airport to meet passengers who are coming from Skopje. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Passengers on flights from Skopje to Turku must take a corona test before the trip. Turku and the airline Wizz Air have agreed that passengers must have a negative corona test result so that they can get on the flight to Turku.

A lot of passengers with coronavirus infections have come to Turku from Skopje in North Macedonia.

Finland may ban flights from Skopje to Turku, because there have been so many corona infections among the passengers.

## Problems with Gmail

![A man walks past the Google logo with a mobile phone in his hand.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2048,h_1152,x_0,y_35/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5767735d26bfda3438d/1562820650)

Open image viewer

A fault in Google's services also affected the use of Gmail in Finland. Photo: EPA/BORIS ROESSLER

On Thursday the IT company Google had faults in its services around the world. Many Finns also noticed problems with the Gmail email service.

Because of the fault, at times it was not possible to send email with Gmail, or it was not possible to add an attachment to an email.

There were also faults in Google's Drive service. For example, in the Drive service you can write and save texts. The faults in Google's services were most common in the morning.

## Waterfowl and bear hunting started

![Waterfowl hunting catch.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7422808)

Open image viewer

A waterfowl hunter's catch. Photo: Mari Nupponen / Yle

The hunting of waterfowl, that is, in practice ducks, started today, Thursday. About 100,000 Finns take part in duck hunting.

The Finnish Game and Fisheries Research Institute recommends that Finns hunt those ducks that are plentiful. Such common duck species are the mallard, the tufted duck and the goldeneye.

Bear hunting has also started on Thursday. More than 380 bear hunting permits have been given this year. Thousands of hunters take part in bear hunting.
