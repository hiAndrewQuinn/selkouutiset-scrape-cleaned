# Thursday 3.6.2021 (radio)

There was a demonstration by the cultural sector in Helsinki. Finland's corona situation is good. THL has issued new corona guidance. Director Juho Kuosmanen's film has been selected to compete at the Cannes Film Festival. The weather is sunny and warm.

## Cultural sector demonstration

![Satu Linnapuomi, theatre director, speaks into a megaphone on the steps of the Parliament House](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81514160b8a45aeca86/1622713522)

Cultural sector demonstration in front of Parliament House. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

There was a big demonstration in Helsinki. The demonstration was on Thursday in front of Parliament House.

About 1,500 people from the cultural sector took part in the demonstration. They were angry because they have not been able to organise cultural events during the corona time.

Demonstrators demanded more support for the cultural sector. They also demanded that cultural events be allowed to be organised again.

Culture Minister Antti Kurvinen spoke to the demonstrators. The minister promised more support for the cultural sector.

## Finland's corona situation

![Director Pasi Pohjola of the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health, strategy director Liisa-Maria Voipio-Pulkki and Otto Helve, chief physician at THL.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11896201)

Liisa-Maria Voipio-Pulkki (in the middle) at the press briefing on Thursday. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Finland's corona situation is good.

Coronavirus cases have decreased across the whole country. In particular, cases have decreased among young people.

Director Liisa-Maria Voipio-Pulkki from the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health said this on Thursday.

Voipio-Pulkki says that the corona situation has moved in a good direction because people have changed their habits. People now have far fewer contacts with other people than usual.

On Thursday it was reported that there are 143 new coronavirus infections in Finland.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, has issued new corona guidance.

THL says that people can meet at home without a mask after they have received 2 corona vaccinations.

However, THL recommends that everyone still uses a mask, for example in shops and buses, also after vaccinations.

## Finnish director succeeds

![Director Juho Kuosmanen and actor Seidi Haarla during filming.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11963467)

Open image viewer

Director Juho Kuosmanen and actor Seidi Haarla, who plays in the film Compartment No. 6. Photo: Sami Kuokkanen / Aamu Film Company

Finnish director Juho Kuosmanen is succeeding. Kuosmanen's new film has been selected to compete at the Cannes Film Festival. The film gets into the most important competition section at Cannes.

The name of Kuosmanen's film is Compartment No. 6. It is about a Finnish student girl and a Russian labourer who travel by train in Russia. The story of the film is from Rosa Liksom's book.

The Cannes Film Festival is in July. The film Compartment No. 6 will come to cinemas in the autumn.

The weather continues sunny and warm across the whole country.

On Friday the temperature rises to around 25 degrees in a large part of the country. It is cooler on the coasts. In Northern Lapland there may be rain showers.

Warm weather continues at the weekend. End-of-term school celebrations are held on Saturday in beautiful weather, when the temperature is about 25 degrees.
