## Armed forces' celebration day

On Sunday the armed forces' celebration day was celebrated, that is, the Defence Forces' flag celebration.

The flag celebration parade was in Jyväskylä this year. More than 1000 soldiers took part in the parade.

Military aircraft were also seen in Jyväskylä. For example, the armed forces' Hornet aircraft flew in the sky.

On the armed forces' celebration day, President Sauli Niinistö reminded that Finland is now a member of the defence alliance NATO.

Finns defend Finland together with the other NATO countries, the president reminded. In Finland the president is also the commander-in-chief of the armed forces.

The Defence Forces' flag celebration is held on 4 June, because it is the birthday of Marshal Mannerheim.

Mannerheim led Finland's army during the Second World War about 80 years ago. Mannerheim was born on 4 June 1867.

## Sweden's NATO membership

![NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg, in front of whom are microphones.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_570/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1123235647890fd5cd29/1685623076)

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg. Photo: EPA-EFE / Hanna Johre

Sweden's NATO membership may come true soon. That is how NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg assesses it.

Stoltenberg says that Sweden may become a NATO member before NATO's next summit. The summit is in July.

Stoltenberg met Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan on Sunday. Turkey has not yet approved Sweden in NATO.

In the meeting Stoltenberg said that Sweden has taken Turkey's demands into account. For example, Sweden has changed anti-terrorism laws and given permission to sell weapons to Turkey.

That is why it is time for Turkey to approve Sweden's NATO membership too, Stoltenberg said. Sweden applied for membership of the defence alliance NATO at the same time as Finland, but in spring Turkey approved only Finland's NATO membership.

All NATO countries must approve the new members of the defence alliance.

## Accident in China

A serious accident has happened in China.

People died and went missing in a landslide. There are about 20 dead and missing in total.

The landslide happened in a mountainous area in Sichuan province. The cause of the landslide may be that a lot of rain has fallen in the area.

Chinese authorities say that the accident happened early on Sunday morning.
