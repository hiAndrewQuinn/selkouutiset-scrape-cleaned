# Wednesday 4.10.2017 (radio)

The police have received many reports about fake police officers. Finland does not interest the staff of the European Medicines Agency. Cabonline is hiring more taxi drivers in Finland. Finns have made many trips to Southern Europe. The Helsinki Ferris wheel will keep running.

## Fake police officers

![The image shows a phone adviser with a hands-free headset.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5472193)

Open image viewer

The caller is not always a police officer, even if they claim to be. Photo: Yle

The police have received many reports about fake police officers. This year more than 500 reports have come in.

A fake police officer is not a real police officer, but presents themselves as one. A fake police officer tries to swindle money from people.

For example, a fake police officer calls an elderly person and asks for the elderly person's online banking codes. With the banking codes, they manage to steal money from the bank account.

The police remind everyone that a real police officer never asks for banking codes on the phone.

## The EU Medicines Agency

![European Medicines Agency in London.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9856774)

Open image viewer

The EU Medicines Agency is now in London. Photo: Ian Tuttle / AOP

Finland does not interest the staff of the European Union Medicines Agency. More than 70 percent of the staff intend to resign if the agency moves to Helsinki.

The EU Medicines Agency is now in London. The agency has to move, because Britain is leaving the EU. The agency has about 600 staff.

The staff of the Medicines Agency do not want to move to any Eastern European city. The staff hope that the agency moves to Amsterdam or Copenhagen, for example.

## Taxi drivers are being hired

![Taxis in a queue in front of the Oulu University Hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9790187)

Open image viewer

There is work for taxi drivers. Photo: Wasim Khuzam / Yle

The Swedish taxi company Cabonline is hiring more drivers in Finland.

Cabonline bought Finnish taxi companies in the summer. Kovanen, Mankaan Taksi and Inva-Taksi now belong to Cabonline. They are hiring 100 new drivers.

Cabonline believes that there will be enough work for the new taxi drivers.

## Finns' trips to the south

![Sunbathing, and especially burning the skin, exposes you to melanoma.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6556606)

Open image viewer

Finns fled the cold summer to Southern Europe. Photo: Pekka Sipilä / Yle

Finns have made many trips to Southern Europe this year. The summer was cold and rainy in Finland, and that is why many people travelled abroad.

For example, in July Finns travelled south clearly more than in July last year. The number of trips to Southern Europe grew by over 60 percent.

In July, Finns made 230,000 trips to the Mediterranean, for example. That is a new record.

However, Finns travel most to neighbouring countries, Estonia and Sweden.

## The Helsinki Ferris wheel

![Tour boat in Helsinki south harbour](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7304820)

Open image viewer

The Helsinki Ferris wheel is right in the city centre. Photo: Jyki Lyytikkä / Yle

Another happy news item from Helsinki. The Helsinki Ferris wheel will keep running. It may turn in central Helsinki for at least another 6 years.

The Ferris wheel is near Helsinki's market square and the Presidential Palace. Many tourists also want to ride the Ferris wheel, because from it you can see over central Helsinki.
