# Saturday 14.5.2022 (radio)

Finland, NATO and Putin. The SDP's NATO position. Youth centres. Eurovision. The weather.

## Niinistö and Putin

![President Sauli Niinistö.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3995,h_2247,x_5,y_167/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-954998627f59f1cfd4c/1652513526)

Open image viewer

Republic's President Sauli Niinistö on Ykkösaamu. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

Republic's President Sauli Niinistö has spoken on the phone with the President of Russia, Vladimir Putin.

President Niinistö said that Russia's demands and the major attack on Ukraine have changed Finland's security environment.

President Niinistö told Putin that Finland will decide to apply for membership of the military alliance NATO in the coming days.

Niinistö says that the discussion was direct and clear. Avoiding tensions was considered important in the call.

The call was made at Finland's initiative.

## Putin warns

![Russian President Vladimir Putin took part in a meeting in Moscow on Thursday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5233,h_2943,x_0,y_225/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-95101262740625ee885/1651771008)

Open image viewer

Russian President Vladimir Putin. Photo: Mikhail Klimentyev / AOP

Russian President Vladimir Putin warned President Sauli Niinistö in the phone conversation, according to the Russian news agency Ria.

Putin said that changing Finland's foreign policy line can weaken relations between the countries.

Giving up the policy of neutrality may be a mistake, Putin noted.

Putin also stressed to Niinistö that Finland is not facing a security threat.

## SDP's party council

![Prime Minister and SDP chair Sanna Marin speaks at the SDP May Day event at Vähätori in Turku on May Day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_273/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-948736626e6947d2a7c/1651405958)

Open image viewer

SDP chair Sanna Marin. Photo: Roni Lehti / Lehtikuva

The biggest party, the SDP, supports Finland's membership of the military alliance NATO. The SDP's party council voted on the matter at an extraordinary meeting.

The council says that the security environment of Finland and Europe has changed significantly. That is why the SDP gives its support to the state leadership, so that negotiations on membership can begin.

## Youth centres

![Billiards at the youth centre in Pernio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3093,h_1739,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90393461e8348fc77b8/1642607853)

Open image viewer

Billiards at the youth centre in Pernio. Photo: Lassi Lähteenmäki / Yle

In Finland there are a little fewer youth centres than before. In 2015 there were about 960 youth centres, last year only about 790.

Many old youth centres have been left in areas where there are no longer as many young people.

Youth centres are important for young people. They are equal places to spend free time.

There are always safe adults present there too.

## Eurovision

![The Rasmus at Eurovision rehearsals.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1279,h_719,x_0,y_53/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-953742627cb3a5e373e/1652339677)

Open image viewer

The Rasmus's song is Jezebel. The band performs fourth in the final. Photo: Nathan Reinds / EBU

The Eurovision final is contested tonight in Turin, Italy.

Finland's representative is The Rasmus.

The final starts at 22 Finnish time. The live broadcast can be followed on Yle's channels.

25 countries compete in the Eurovision final. Of them, 20 were chosen in the semi-finals on Tuesday and Thursday.

In addition to them, the 5 biggest funding countries take part directly in the final: the host country Italy, Spain, Germany, France and Britain.

This year Ukraine is the big favourite to win the Eurovision.
