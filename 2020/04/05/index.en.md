# Sunday 5.4.2020 (radio)

The coronavirus situation in Finland. Remote work is increasing. Many people do not need a car now. Municipalities are refunding day care fees. Pentti Linkola has died.

## The coronavirus situation in Finland

![Cartoon of a group of viruses.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11281227)

Open image viewer

An artist's drawing of the coronavirus. Photo: Lubos Paukeje / AOP

The number of coronavirus deaths in Finland has risen by three.

28 people have now died in Finland from the disease caused by the coronavirus. There are 209 patients in hospital care. Of them, 76 patients are in intensive care.

Just over 1,900 coronavirus infections have been confirmed in Finland.

## Remote work is increasing

![A woman writing on a laptop.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11196395)

Open image viewer

Remote work can be done, for example, on a computer. Photo: Tuija Veirto / Yle

Because of the coronavirus situation, a very large number of Finns are working from home, that is, they are on remote work.

More than a million Finns are doing remote work. Many of them want to continue remote work even after the coronavirus situation.

The research company Taloustutkimus asked Finns whether they like remote work. More than 300,000 Finns said that they want to do more remote work even after the coronavirus situation.

## Many people are not using a car now

![Cars in the yard of a detached house in Vehmaa.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5402,h_3039,x_0,y_279/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6398095e3c20d8af2fb/1581147111)

Open image viewer

If you take a car out of traffic use, you save money. Then you may not drive the car. Photo: Minna Rosvall / Yle

Many Finns have taken their car out of traffic use. If you do not use a car, you can take it out of traffic use. Then you do not have to pay the car's insurance premium or the vehicle tax.

About 100,000 Finns took their car out of traffic use in March.

Many people do not need a car now, because movement has been restricted because of the coronavirus situation.

## You can get an exemption from day care fees

![A day care child and a day care worker in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4608,h_2592,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6521335e68bdfddb5f5/1586062551)

Open image viewer

If you look after a child at home for a long time, you may get the day care fee back. Photo: Meri Remes / Yle

Municipalities do not charge families day care fees if the child does not go to day care because of the coronavirus situation.

The Association of Finnish Local and Regional Authorities, Kuntaliitto, says that many municipalities have asked Kuntaliitto for advice on how to pay day care fees back to families. The municipalities want to refund the fee.

Different municipalities may have different ways of refunding the day care fee. Usually, you get an exemption from the day care fee if the child has been away from day care for a long time.

## Pentti Linkola has died

![Pentti Linkola.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11293174)

Open image viewer

Pentti Linkola 1932-2020 Photo: Juha Metso / AOP

Nature conservationist and bird enthusiast Pentti Linkola has died.

Pentti Linkola was also a fisherman and a writer. Pentti Linkola is remembered for wanting to protect nature. For example, Pentti Linkola founded the Nature Heritage Foundation (Luonnonperintösäätiö), which buys old forests and protects them.

Pentti Linkola often said that people must protect all plants and animals on the Earth, and that people must not be more important than nature. Pentti Linkola was 87 years old.
