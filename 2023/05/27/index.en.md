## Government negotiations

![Petteri Orpo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3048,h_1714,x_1215,y_788/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1120582647216d7ac6af/1685211914)

Government negotiations continue. That is what the leader of the government negotiations, Petteri Orpo, said on Saturday.

Government negotiations can continue normally on Monday, because the parties have agreed on their disputes about immigration matters. In particular, RKP and the Finns Party have disagreed about immigration.

4 parties are taking part in the government negotiations. They are the National Coalition, the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats. Petteri Orpo hopes that these parties will get the new government ready during June.

National Coalition chairman Orpo leads the government negotiations, because the National Coalition won the election in early April. If the negotiations succeed, Orpo will become Finland's new prime minister.

## Turkish elections

![President Erdoğan's election poster in Istanbul.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5698,h_3205,x_0,y_555/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11199636470469c0db34/1685253904)

Recep Tayyip Erdoğan's election advertisement. Photo: Grigory Vorobyev / Yle

Turkish people vote in the presidential elections tomorrow, Sunday.

It is the second round of the elections. The first round was held 2 weeks ago.

Turkey's current president Recep Tayyip Erdoğan and the opposition candidate Kemal Kılıçdaroğlu have got into the second round. It is estimated that Erdoğan has good chances of winning the election.

However, some Turks are tired of Erdoğan taking all the power for himself. The opposition candidate Kılıçdaroğlu promises Turks more freedom.

Erdoğan's supporters and opponents are competing hard for the election victory. Experts say that Turkey has split into 2 parts.

The German band Rammstein is performing in Helsinki at the weekend. Rammstein is known all over the world.

Rammstein's show can be seen at the Helsinki Olympic Stadium on Saturday and Sunday.

Rammstein's performances are known for being big and spectacular. For example, fire is an important part of Rammstein's show.

The World in a Village festival is held in Helsinki this weekend. This time the festival is at Suvilahti in Helsinki.

The festival offers concerts, art, discussions and programmes for children. The festival's performers come from different countries.

The World in a Village festival was held for the first time in 1995.

## The weather

![Sunday's weather map.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-112062164722b0ae8062/1685203747)

On Sunday rain comes to Finland. It rains in the west and in the north. Elsewhere it is dry.

In rainy areas the temperature is only about 10 degrees. In the dry areas of the east and south the temperature is up to 18 degrees.
