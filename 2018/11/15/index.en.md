## The Brexit deal is being opposed

![Theresa May](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10510507)

Open image viewer

British Prime Minister Theresa May. Photo: AFP

British Prime Minister Theresa May is in trouble because of Brexit. Brexit means Britain's exit from the European Union.

The British government approved a plan for the withdrawal agreement on Wednesday, but already on Thursday ministers resigned from the government because of the plan.

The British Parliament is also arguing about the deal. Parliament will probably decide in December whether it approves the deal.

The most difficult issue in the withdrawal agreement is how the border with Ireland, an EU country, can be kept open. The matter is very important for Northern Ireland, which is part of Britain.

The British decided in a referendum in 2016 that the country would leave the EU. The intention is that the exit will take effect next year.

## Sipilä believes in the deal

![Juha Sipilä](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10502662)

Open image viewer

Finnish Prime Minister Juha Sipilä. Photo: Antti Aimo-Koivisto / Lehtikuva

Finnish Prime Minister Juha Sipilä believes that the British Parliament will approve the EU withdrawal agreement. In Sipilä's view the plan for the deal is good.

Brexit affects Finns too. It is important for Finland that, for example, free trade between Britain and the EU continues after Britain leaves the EU. If Finnish goods have to pay customs duties in Britain, the operations of Finnish firms will suffer.

## No microplastic in tap water

![Water is poured into a glass.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6829865)

Open image viewer

Finnish tap water is safe. Photo: Yle

There is only a little microplastic in Finnish tap water. Microplastic means very small plastic particles. Most microplastic comes off car tyres.

Researchers studied the tap water in the Helsinki, Tuusula and Nurmijärvi areas. The results show that water purification works well in Finland.

However, the authorities remind people that water was studied in only part of Finland.

## Support families are needed

![Women walking on a field path, with a dog in the foreground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10405224)

Open image viewer

A support family can, for example, go out with a young person. Photo: Pixabay

Vantaa is looking for support families for young migrants. A support family is important for an underage migrant who has come to Finland alone.

A support family can be an ordinary family. The family just needs to have time for the young migrant. The family can, for example, go out or cook together with the young person.
