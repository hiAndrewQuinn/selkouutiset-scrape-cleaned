# Monday 18.1.2021 (radio)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin and President Sauli Niinistö demand the release of Aleksei Navalnyi. Raising the age of compulsory education brings extra costs for municipalities. The second phase of the corona vaccine has started in Finland. Topelius Prize and Arvid Lydecken Prize.

## Aleksei Navalnyi was arrested straight away in Moscow

![Aleksei Navalnyi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11742556)

Russian opposition leader Aleksei Navalnyi on a bus at Moscow airport on 17.1. 2021. Photo: Kirill Kudryavtsev / AFP

Prime Minister Sanna Marin demands on Twitter that the Russian opposition politician Aleksei Navalnyi be released immediately.

Navalnyi was arrested yesterday in Moscow, when he returned from Germany after treatment. An attempt was made to kill Navalnyi with a nerve poison in August.

Marin wrote that Russia must investigate Navalnyi's poisoning and protect the rights of the opposition.

President Sauli Niinistö told Helsingin Sanomat that there are no grounds for the arrest. Niinistö said that he has approached Russian President Vladimir Putin on the matter.

Navalnyi's press secretary wrote on Twitter that Navalnyi's court hearing began in the morning at a police station on the outskirts of Moscow.

The Russian prison service accuses Navalnyi of having broken the conditions of an earlier suspended sentence.

Aleksei Navalnyi is calling on Russians to go out onto the streets to protest against the authorities.

## Free upper secondary school costs municipalities a lot

![Teppo Hynnä from Pori playing on a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2304,h_1296,x_0,y_155/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7565615fe0adc06153d/1608560166)

Open image viewer

The computers of free upper secondary school cost municipalities a lot. Photo: Kasper Heimolehto / Yle

Raising the age of compulsory education brings extra costs for municipalities. Next autumn the age of compulsory education rises to 18 years.

In the autumn almost 60,000 students will get free education in upper secondary school or vocational school.

Municipalities will have more to pay, for example, for learning materials. In many schools other teaching will have to be cut, unless the municipality pays the costs.

The biggest purchase in upper secondary schools is computers. The computer must be such that it can be used to take the matriculation examinations in 3 years' time.

The giving of the second stage of the corona vaccine has started in Finland. These vaccines are Pfizer and BioNTech vaccines.

At least in the Helsinki and Uusimaa hospital district the giving of the second doses started on Monday.

The first-stage doses have been given since the end of December. Vaccines were given especially to health care staff. The vaccine needs 2 doses, between which there must be 3 to 4 weeks.

The Pfizer and BioNTech vaccine gives about 95 percent protection when both doses have been given. The vaccine protects against serious forms of the coronavirus disease.

## Literary prizes for two

![Meri Luttinen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11628810)

Open image viewer

Debut author Meri Luttinen. Photo: Riikka Vaahtera/WSOY

The Topelius Prize for youth literature has been received by Meri Luttinen. The prize was given for the debut novel The Eye of the Storm (Myrskynsilmä).

The Arvid Lydecken Prize for children's literature has been received by Sari Peltoniemi for the work I'm Escaping From Here (Täältä minä karkaan).

The prizes are awarded by the Association of Finnish Youth Writers.

The value of both prizes this year is the same as the year number, that is, 2021 euros.
