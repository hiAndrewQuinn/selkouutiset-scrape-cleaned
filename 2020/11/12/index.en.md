# Thursday 12.11.2020 (radio)

Finland's coronavirus situation is better than in other European countries. The widow's pension is to be reformed. This autumn 3,500 new matriculated students will graduate. The Estonian film Viimeiset (The Last Ones) was shot in Finnish Lapland.

## Finland's coronavirus situation

Finland has succeeded well in the fight against the coronavirus. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, says that the coronavirus situation in Finland is better than in other European countries.

However, the coronavirus situation is different in different parts of Finland. Coronavirus infections are especially found in big cities.

THL said on Thursday that there are 197 new coronavirus infections in Finland.

## The widow's pension is being reformed

![An elderly person pulls a wheeled shopping bag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11436231)

Open image viewer

The widow's pension is important for many widows. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Family pensions are being reformed in Finland. Family pensions include, for example, the widow's pension. The widow's pension is paid to a widow, i.e. a person whose spouse has died.

Today a widow receives the widow's pension for as long as she lives. However, the new plan is that a widow will receive the widow's pension only for 10 years after her spouse's death. The aim is that the new rule applies to widows who were born in 1975 or later.

Many people criticise the reform. For example, carers are worried about the position of widows. A carer may have looked after a spouse for many years, and after the spouse's death the widow's pension is important for the carer.

Many think that a good thing in the new plan is that the widow's pension can also be paid to a cohabiting partner. Today the widow's pension is paid only to a married spouse.

## This autumn's matriculants

![Matriculation cap.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_52/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4899995b114e4eef41c/1527860864)

On Thursday the names of the new matriculants were announced. This autumn 3,500 new matriculants will graduate.

The names of the new matriculants can be found in Yle's matriculation machine. It is at yle.fi.

In total, more than 46,000 students took part in the autumn matriculation exams. Most of them will only graduate in the spring.

## An Estonian film from Lapland

![Veiko Õunpuu, Estonian film director.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3936,h_2214,x_0,y_160/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7223355f6b36e35f5b1/1600862050)

The director of the film Viimeiset is the Estonian Veiko Õunpuu. Photo: Antti Lähteenmäki / Yle

A new Estonian film has been shot in Finnish Lapland. The name of the film is Viimeiset.

The film is a Lapland western. In the film, reindeer herders and the mining industry face each other. An accident happens underground, but work in the mine continues.

The director of the film, Estonian Veiko Õunpuu, says that an environmental disaster is a topical subject.

The film is in Finnish, and most of the actors are Finnish. Viimeiset is, however, Estonia's Oscar nominee in the best international film category.

The film Viimeiset comes to Finnish cinemas on Friday.
