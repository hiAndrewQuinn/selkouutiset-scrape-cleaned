# Saturday 13.7.2019 (radio)

Monitoring procuring is difficult in Finland. Strawberry farms do not have many Finnish pickers. The Central Chamber of Commerce warns companies about fraudulent emails. The residents of New Zealand have started to give up their own guns.

## Procuring is hard to monitor

![A man hands money to a woman.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10876527)

Monitoring procuring is difficult in Finland. This is what experts interviewed by Yle say. Procuring means profiting financially from another person's prostitution.

Every day at least hundreds of people in Finland sell sex. Selling sex is not illegal in Finland, but procuring is. For example, Sweden has had a complete ban on buying sex since 1999.

Procuring is often linked with human trafficking. This week a criminal case was reported in which there was a sex service website on the internet. 3 Finnish men are suspected in the case, which involves human trafficking.

Procurers can also direct sex workers from abroad.

Over 100 million euros a year moves through the sex trade in Finland.

## Strawberry farms cannot manage without foreign workers

![Strawberries being washed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10873341)

Open image viewer

Photo: Kati Ala-Renko / Yle

Strawberry farms do not have many Finnish pickers. The farms have to use foreign labour. Most pickers currently come from Ukraine, Russia and Belarus.

For example, in Ukraine the average wage is about 200–300 euros. On a berry farm in Finland a picker can earn the wage of 2 years in one summer. On the best day a picker picks 100–200 kilos of strawberries.

Agriculture needs 10,000–15,000 seasonal workers in Finland every year. Most of them work on berry farms.

## Fraud messages are circulating in email again

![A man and a computer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10865446)

Open image viewer

Photo: AOP

The Central Chamber of Commerce warns companies about fraudulent emails. The messages ask people to make payments, or they try to phish for login details.

The messages have arrived especially at the accounts departments of companies.

The messages seem more believable because the sender is marked as a familiar person. Messages may come, for example, in the name of senior management.

Fraudsters are active especially in summer, because companies may have new employees.

## New Zealand is cutting down on guns

![Memorial service for the Christchurch attacks at the Al Noor mosque.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10704269)

A memorial service for the victims of the Christchurch attacks at the Al Noor mosque. Photo: Mick Tsikas / EPA

The residents of New Zealand have started to give up their own guns. This is a buy-back programme. Its purpose is to get rid of semi-automatic firearms across the whole country.

Giving up guns is a result of the mosque shootings in Christchurch in March. At that time 51 Muslims died. After the attacks New Zealand began to tighten its gun laws at great speed.

There are more than 200 gun buy-back events around the country. The first of these was held on Saturday at the site of the shooting in Christchurch.
