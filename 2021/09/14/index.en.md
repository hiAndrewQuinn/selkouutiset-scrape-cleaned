# Tuesday 14.9.2021 (radio)

The government is easing restaurant restrictions. Finland's economy is growing fast. Temperatures above 50 degrees are becoming more common on Earth.

Restaurants say that the government's decision is bad. In the restaurants' view, the restrictions must be lifted completely.

## Finland's economy

![A person carrying a shopping basket on the escalator of a shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_148/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-771075601f8c19c2ef4/1734432105)

Finland's economy is growing fast. So says Pellervo Economic Research. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Finland's economy is now growing fast. So says Pellervo Economic Research.

Pellervo Economic Research estimates that Finland's economy will grow by 3.7 per cent this year and by 4 per cent next year.

In March, Pellervo Economic Research estimated that Finland's economy would grow by 2.5 per cent this year and 3.5 per cent next year.

Finland's economy is now growing faster than usual. The growth may slow down, because many workplaces have too few employees.

## Heat

![A person lies on concrete under a fountain.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4464,h_2511,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-85349661407c9e042d7/1631616277)

Temperatures above 50 degrees are now recorded on Earth more often than before. This is what a report by the British broadcasting company BBC shows.

In 2010–2019, a temperature above 50 degrees was measured in the world 26 times a year. This is almost twice as many as in 1980–2009.

At the beginning of the 1980s, temperatures above 50 degrees were only in the Middle East and the Persian Gulf area. Later, temperatures above 50 degrees have also been in North Africa and in the Californian area of North America. This summer the temperature also rose close to 50 degrees in Canada and Southern Europe.
