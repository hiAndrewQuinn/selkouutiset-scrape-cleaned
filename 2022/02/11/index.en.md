## Olympic gold

![Iivo Niskanen celebrates the gold he has just won in the 15 kilometre classic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_383/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-91389162062f2fcc1cf/1644584508)

Finland has won its first gold medal at the Beijing Winter Olympics.

Iivo Niskanen won the men's 15 kilometre classic cross-country race. For Niskanen this medal is already his second at these Games, because on Sunday he won bronze.

Iivo Niskanen has now won 3 gold medals at 3 different Olympic Games.

## Restaurant restrictions

![A waiter starting to pour beer into a glass.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-779927603dfbc1c1116/1636604776)

Open image viewer

From Monday restaurants may stay open until midnight. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Restaurant restrictions are being eased on Monday. The government has decided that restaurants may stay open until midnight.

The easing applies to both food restaurants and bars. However, the number of customers is still limited.

If the corona situation in Finland keeps easing and hospitals are not overloaded, the restrictions can be dropped completely at the beginning of March.

The restrictions cannot be lifted with a corona pass.

The interest organisation for tourism and restaurant services, Mara, demands that all restrictions be cancelled immediately. Mara plans to complain about the matter to the Parliamentary Ombudsman.

## Evacuation from Afghanistan

![People queuing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_978,h_550,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8468256127ac96b0f3c/1629990083)

People queued for transport planes at Kabul airport last August. Photo: the interviewee

Finland has evacuated more people from Afghanistan.

In the evening, 37 people were brought to Finland from Afghanistan. In addition, 10 more people will come to Finland in the coming weeks.

Those evacuated include, for example, former employees of Finland's embassy in Kabul.

In August the government decided that a total of 300 people will be evacuated from Afghanistan to Finland.

## Women's sports, men's sports

![Gym training.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10984766)

Open image viewer

Women do group exercise more than men. Photo: Ralph Lueger / AOP

The Ministry of Education and Culture has published a report on equality in sport and exercise.

The report says that taking part in sports is very gendered, that is, women and men do different sports. The split in hobbies starts already in youth.

Men take part in team ball sports more often than women. Motor sport, chess and tennis are also men's sports. Group exercise, dance and horse riding, on the other hand, are women's sports.

Both sexes go jogging and cycling equally often.

The reasons for quitting a hobby are also gendered. Boys quit because they get bored with the sport. Girls quit, for example, because they do not like the coach, or because they want to concentrate better on their studies.

## Saturday's weather

On Saturday the weather is dry in a large part of the country. In the south and west the sun may also shine. In central parts and in southern Lapland a little snow may fall, however.

The daytime temperature is between -4 and +1 degrees, and in the north between -5 and -15 degrees.
