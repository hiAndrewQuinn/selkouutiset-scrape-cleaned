# Thursday 2.5.2019 (radio)

Police are investigating MP Hussein al-Taee's writings. Central medians are a good means of reducing traffic accidents. Music affects the ripening of cheese, say the Swiss.

## Hussein al-Taee

![Hussein al-Taee](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10760510)

Hussein al-Taee. Photo: Yle

News about MP Hussein al-Taee continues.

The police are looking into whether a criminal investigation will be started into al-Taee's writings. Al-Taee mocked Jews, Somalis, Sunni Muslims and gay people on Facebook. The writings are from between 2011 and 2015.

Al-Taee is now on sick leave. He was taken to hospital on Tuesday. This is what the SDP, al-Taee's party, says.

Al-Taee was elected to Parliament in the April elections. At that time there was no information about al-Taee's writings.

Al-Taee has asked for forgiveness for the writings. Al-Taee says that he no longer thinks in the same way.

Al-Taee is a 36-year-old peace mediator and politician. Al-Taee's family comes from Iraq. The family came to Finland when al-Taee was 10 years old.

## Central medians on roads

![Central median in Simo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5612375)

Open image viewer

A central median is an effective way to prevent serious accidents. Photo: YLE / Sauli Antikainen

A good means of reducing traffic accidents is a central median. A central median separates the driving lanes and prevents cars from colliding with each other.

Up to 80 percent of road traffic deaths can be prevented if roads have central medians.

However, central medians have been built on only about 300 kilometres of road.

Building central medians is difficult in Finland, because the roads are narrow. It is expensive to build a central median and widen the road at the same time.

## Music for cheese

![Emmental cheese.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6239346)

Open image viewer

Emmental cheese gets a strong flavour from hip-hop music. Photo: Christoph Ruckstuhl / EPA

Finally, something about cheese. The Swiss know how to make delicious cheeses.

Now the Swiss have tried a completely new method: they have played music to the cheeses during production.

Emmental cheeses matured in a cellar. Some of the cheeses listened to music, some listened to a humming sound, and one cheese matured in silence.

After half a year the cheeses were ready.

First, food technologists measured the cheeses. They said that the strongest flavour and richest aroma were in the cheese that listened to hip-hop music during ripening.

After that a group of chefs tasted the cheeses. In their opinion too, the hip-hop cheese was the best.

The Swiss say that the sound waves of music affect the cheese.

The study has interested people. Many have asked when they can buy blues cheese or AC/DC cheese.
