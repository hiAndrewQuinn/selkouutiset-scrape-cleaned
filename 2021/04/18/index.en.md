## The corona situation is easing

The corona situation is improving in Finland. In the last two (2) days, fewer than 500 corona infections have been found in Finland, that is, about 250 infections a day.

Over the last two (2) weeks there have been 4,800 infections, that is more than 3,000 fewer infections than in the two (2) weeks before.

The number of corona infections is now at the same level as it was at the start of February.

## More to correct in tax returns

![Tax return](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-808815902f8e5d7110/1548411548)

Open image viewer

The tax proposal must be returned in May. Photo: Mika Hokkanen / Yle

Finns have more to correct in their tax returns this year than before. Because of remote work, many Finns can, for example, claim a home office deduction. A home office deduction can be claimed if more than half of the working days have been remote work.

Other work costs can also be deducted in taxation. For example, internet subscription fees can be deducted if the internet is needed for work.

Tax proposals must be returned in May.

## Oats are growing in popularity

![Ripe oats in a field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5377,h_3025,x_0,y_260/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6850515ed7b6a025fba/1591195333)

Open image viewer

Oats. Photo: Alamy

Oats are growing in popularity. Oats interest many food manufacturers. The use of oats in food grew by 30 percent last year compared with the year before.

Besides food, oats have also begun to be used in cosmetics. Xylitol, which protects teeth, can also be made from the oat husk.

Oats are the second most popular crop in Finland. Oats do well in Finland. Oats are exported from Finland especially to Central Europe – to Germany, Belgium and the Netherlands.

## The USA and China discussed climate issues

![John Kerry speaks at a climate conference in Portugal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11748902)

Open image viewer

The USA's climate envoy John Kerry. Photo: Antonio Cotrim / EPA

The USA and China have discussed the climate crisis. The USA's climate envoy \*\*John Kerry \*\*has visited China. He met China's climate envoy **Xie Zhenhuan**.

After the meeting, the USA and China published a joint statement. The statement says that the climate crisis must be addressed, because the climate crisis is a serious and urgent matter.

The US envoy's visit to China has increased hope that the USA and China can cooperate on climate issues.
