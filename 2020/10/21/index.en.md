# Wednesday 21.10.2020 (radio)

In Finland more than 14,000 coronavirus infections have now been recorded. The flu season will start soon. The Helsinki shipyard has received a big ship order. There has been a big fire in Turku. The weather is rainy.

## Coronavirus infections

The total number of coronavirus infections in Finland is now over 14,000.

The number rose above 14,000 on Wednesday, when 222 new infections came. Most new infections were in the Helsinki and Uusimaa areas.

On Wednesday 4 new coronavirus deaths were also reported. A total of 355 people have died in Finland because of the coronavirus.

## Influenza

![An arm being given a vaccine injection.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7045765f278b78c9a33/1596427190)

There is a vaccine against influenza, but there is not yet a vaccine against the coronavirus. Photo: AOP

The flu season will start soon. Influenza causes symptoms similar to a common cold, but the symptoms of influenza are worse. Influenza often includes, for example, a high fever.

Vaccinations against influenza have also started. People should book a vaccination appointment. General vaccination events are not being held because of the coronavirus.

The influenza vaccine is important this year, for example for the elderly, because a person can catch influenza at the same time as the coronavirus. There is still no vaccine against the coronavirus.

## Ship order for Helsinki

![Arctech shipyard in Hietalahti, Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10847752)

Open image viewer

Ships will also be built at the Helsinki shipyard in the coming years. Photo: Mårten Lampen / Yle

The Helsinki shipyard has received a big ship order. The Helsinki shipyard is building a cruise ship.

Building the ship brings work for 700 shipbuilders. The value of the ship order is 150 million euros.

The Helsinki shipyard is building the ship for Swan Hellenic. It is a company for which the Helsinki shipyard is already building 2 ships.

Construction of the third (3rd) ship may start perhaps next summer. The ship will be ready in autumn 2022.

## Fire in Turku

![Fire on Pansiontie in Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4341,h_2442,x_0,y_454/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7327595f9035ff1bdbf/1603286584)

Open image viewer

Because of the fire there was a lot of smoke in the air. Photo: Arash Matin / Yle

There has been a big fire in Turku. The fire broke out on Wednesday in an industrial building near the port of Turku.

Because of the fire there has been a lot of smoke in the area. That is why residents of the area were warned about the fire. Residents were asked to close their windows and ventilation.

Because of the fire, many roads were also closed in the area. Firefighting work continues late into the night.

On Wednesday it was not yet known why the fire started. The police will start to investigate the cause of the fire when the firefighting work is over.

The weather is rainy. Rain falls across the whole country. In Lapland it snows, and in the southern and central parts of the country there is sleet or rain.

In the south it is getting warmer, and the temperature is over 10 degrees. By Thursday only Lapland will have frost.

Roads may be slippery because of the rain.
