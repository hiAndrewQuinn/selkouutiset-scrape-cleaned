# Monday 14.10.2019 (radio)

Syrian army comes to the aid of the Kurds. Economics Nobel Prize goes to three people. Better internet connections for train passengers. President Niinistö goes to the enthronement of Japan's new emperor.

## Syrian forces come to the Kurds' aid

![Turkish army moves equipment to the front.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11019491)

Open image viewer

The Turkish army has moved more equipment to the Kurdish area of north-eastern Syria. Photo: Sedat Suna / EPA

Units of the Syrian army have arrived near the Turkish border. The Syrian army is helping the Kurds against Turkey's attack. The Kurds have asked Syria for help, because Turkey has continued its attack on the Kurdish area.

The EU criticises Turkey for the attack. The foreign ministers of the EU countries say that the attack makes the peace process in Syria harder and causes a lot of suffering for ordinary people.

## Economics Nobel goes to poverty researchers

![The Swedish Central Bank's economics prize, a medal in memory of Alfred Nobel.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11018462)

Open image viewer

The economics Nobel Prize is a medal with a picture of Alfred Nobel. Photo: Yle graphics

The economics Nobel Prize has been awarded to three poverty researchers. The economics Nobel Prize goes to the Americans **Abhijit Banerjee** and **Michael Kremer**, and the French-American **Esther Duflo**.

They have studied how to reduce poverty. For example, why many children learn well at school or do not learn at school. The research helps to identify the causes of poverty.

Abhijit Banerjee, Michael Kremer and Esther Duflo will share the economics Nobel Prize. The prize is about 800,000 euros. It is awarded by the Swedish Central Bank.

## Better internet on trains

![People on a train.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5989645da018d029f66/1570773516)

Open image viewer

The internet connection easily drops on a train. The connection is also slow. Photo: Riina Kasurinen/Yle

The train company VR wants to improve internet connections on trains. Internet on trains works through the mobile phone network. The connection works badly.

Mobile phone network companies must build fast internet connections near the railway lines next year. Mobile phone network companies do not want to build a new network, because building a network is expensive.

VR has invited the mobile phone network companies to a meeting to discuss how trains can get a better internet connection.

## Niinistö and Haukio to Japan

![Sauli Niinistö and wife Jenni Haukio look at each other.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_21/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4918085b1b469b6b161/1528514272)

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio. Photo: Pelle T. Nilsson / AOP

President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio will take part next week in the enthronement ceremony of Japan's new emperor, **Naruhito**. Japan's previous emperor, **Akihito**, gave up the throne in the spring. Akihito's son Naruhito became the new emperor.

The enthronement ceremony will be held in Tokyo, the capital of Japan, next Tuesday. President Niinistö and Mrs Haukio will then take part in the official celebrations and a celebratory meal at the Imperial Palace of Japan.

On the Japan trip, President Niinistö will also meet Japan's Prime Minister **Shinzo Abe**. Mrs Jenni Haukio will visit the Moomin theme park in the town of Hanno.
