# Thursday 26.7.2018 (radio)

Groundwater. Forest fires in Sweden. Suicides. Taxi drivers. Weather information.

## Water must be used sensibly

![Tap water being poured into a glass of water.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10323448)

Open image viewer

Water must be used sensibly when it is dry. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

Many water utilities are asking Finns to use tap water sensibly.

Finland has had a very dry summer. The drought has affected groundwater areas. There is much less water in the groundwater areas than usual.

Now, for example, it is not worth washing a car for no reason. You can also use water sparingly in the shower.

## Finland offers help to Sweden

![Firefighting helicopter in Ljusdal](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10323572)

Open image viewer

Sweden has received help from many countries to put out the forest fires. Photo: Jonathan Nackstrand / AFP

Finland is offering Sweden help to put out the forest fires.

Finland has a rescue team ready, which includes 35 firefighters. If Sweden asks Finland for help, the team is ready to leave within a day. Fire engines and fire pumps, for example, will go with the team.

In Sweden there are many rescue workers from other countries helping to put out the forest fires.

## Most suicides happen in Lithuania

![Angel statue in a cemetery.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9634351)

Open image viewer

In Finland, 13 out of every 100 000 people take their own lives. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

In European countries the number of suicides has been studied. In Finland it was previously thought that a very large number of suicides happen in Finland.

Statistics show that Finland is not the country where the most suicides happen.

The most suicides happen in Lithuania. In Lithuania, 30 out of every 100 000 people take their own lives. In Finland the figure is 13.

The fewest suicides happen in Southern Europe.

## There is a shortage of taxi drivers

![Taxis at a rank.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10130309)

Open image viewer

Taxi companies can hire hundreds of drivers. Photo: Mikko Koski / Yle

There is a shortage of taxi drivers. Many taxi companies say so.

Companies in the capital region can hire hundreds of new drivers.

Finland's taxi law changed at the start of July. Taxi companies have noticed that because of the change in the law, many taxi drivers have stopped working. The drivers believe that the change in the law will reduce their earnings.
