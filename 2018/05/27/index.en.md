# Sunday 27.5.2018 (radio)

The Archbishop has said goodbye. Chinese tourists are expected at Saimaa. There may be only a few mosquitoes this summer. Denmark is celebrating the 50th birthday of the Crown Prince.

## The Archbishop hopes the church will change

![Archbishop Kari Mäkinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10223571)

Archbishop Kari Mäkinen retires at the beginning of June. Photo: Kalle Mäkelä / Yle

Archbishop Kari Mäkinen hopes that the church's position on the blessing of gay couples will change.

Mäkinen says that the church must correct the rights of gay couples to be blessed and to marry. He believes that the change will happen, and he hopes that it happens as soon as possible.

In the morning, the farewell mass for Archbishop Kari Mäkinen was held in Turku Cathedral. Mäkinen retires at the beginning of June. Espoo Bishop Tapio Luoma has been chosen as the new Archbishop.

## Chinese tourists are expected at Saimaa

![A summer view from Saimaa to the port of Lappeenranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9755114)

Open image viewer

Lappeenranta on the shore of Saimaa is a beautiful summer town. Photo: Kari Kosonen / Yle

Perhaps thousands of Chinese tourists are expected in the Saimaa area this summer. The Chinese became interested in the Saimaa area when it was presented at a travel fair in Shanghai.

Already 4 Chinese tour operators have said that they will start package tours to Saimaa. In addition, more than 10 tour operators are preparing to start trips to Saimaa, says the GoSaimaa company.

Groups of Chinese tourists will come to Lappeenranta, Imatra, Mikkeli and Savonlinna. The Chinese programme includes, for example, forest trips, eating smoked whitefish cooked over a campfire, boating, and sauna in a smoke sauna.

From Saimaa, the Chinese groups will continue their journey to St. Petersburg, Helsinki, Lapland or Stockholm, for example.

## A dry spring threatens mosquitoes

![A mosquito sucking blood from a finger.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10202253)

Open image viewer

The weather over the next few weeks will decide how many mosquitoes there are this summer. Photo: AOP

There may be fewer mosquitoes this summer than usual. Insect researcher Reima Leinonen says that the weather over the next couple of weeks may decide how many mosquitoes there will be this summer.

Mosquitoes develop in damp hollows. It takes about 2 or 3 weeks for a larva to develop into an adult mosquito. If the weather is dry, the hollows also dry up, and then the mosquito larvae die.

May has been warm and very little rain has fallen, so it now looks as if there will be fewer mosquitoes than usual, insect researcher Reima Leinonen says.

## Denmark celebrates its 50-year-old Crown Prince

![Danish Crown Prince Frederik and his spouse Crown Princess Mary in a carriage on their way from Amalienborg Palace to Christiansborg Palace for the Crown Prince's 50th birthday reception.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10225222)

Crown Prince Frederik and Crown Princess Mary travelled in a carriage to the birthday party for Frederik. Photo: Bax Lindhardt / EPA

Denmark has celebrated the birthday of Crown Prince Frederik. Frederik turned 50.

On Saturday Queen Margrethe held a party for Frederik, to which relatives and friends, Danish politicians and royalty from other European countries came. On Sunday a people's festival was held in Copenhagen.

The prince's milestone birthday has been celebrated in Denmark all week. In different places, events were organised, such as the Royal Run race, in which the Crown Prince and his family also took part.

Frederik will become King of Denmark after Queen Margrethe.
