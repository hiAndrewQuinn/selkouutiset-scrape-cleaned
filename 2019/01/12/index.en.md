# Saturday 12.1.2019 (radio)

Police suspect new sexual crimes in Oulu. President Sauli Niinistö is shocked by the suspected crimes in Oulu. In Helsinki, metro traffic from Lauttasaari eastwards is closed for the whole weekend. The best TV programmes of last year have been awarded.

## New sexual crime cases in Oulu

![Main police station in Hiukkavaara, Oulu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10593648)

The Oulu police are now investigating sexual crimes. Photo: Timo Heikkala / Lehtikuva

Police suspect new sexual crimes in Oulu, in Northern Finland. Men with a foreign background may have committed the crimes last summer. The victims are girls under 15 years old.

The police already began investigating similar sexual crimes in Oulu last year. The police say that now different crimes and different perpetrators are being investigated. What they have in common is that the perpetrators have a foreign background.

Interior Minister Kai Mykkänen commented on the events in Oulu in a TV interview on Saturday. Mykkänen proposes new ways to prevent crimes by foreigners in Finland. The Interior Minister is responsible for police matters and immigration.

Mykkänen said that the authorities are studying whether, for example, the Citizenship Act can be changed. One proposal is that a foreigner must accept Finnish values when applying for Finnish citizenship. Such a value is, for example, sexual equality between women and men, the Interior Minister said.

## Niinistö was shocked by the crimes in Oulu

![Sauli Niinistö, President of the Republic](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10453327)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

President Sauli Niinistö is shocked by the suspected crimes in Oulu. The president is sad, because the victims are children.

Niinistö says that everyone in Finland must remember that other people must not be harmed.

However, we must solve the problems and give immigrants the opportunity to live in Finland, President Niinistö says.

## Metro traffic in Helsinki is closed

![Metro at Matinkylä station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10013336)

Open image viewer

Metro traffic is closed across almost all of Helsinki over the weekend. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There are big changes to the metro traffic in the Helsinki region over the weekend. The metro only runs between Lauttasaari and Matinkylä over the weekend.

The metro does not run between Lauttasaari and Vuosaari, or between Lauttasaari and Mellunmäki. Metro traffic from Lauttasaari eastwards is closed for the whole weekend. Metro trains are replaced by buses.

Metro traffic is closed because of technical work.

Mikko Kekäläinen and Susanna Laine host the programme Half Past Six. Photo: Ilmari Fabritius / Yle Kuvapalvelu

The best TV programmes of last year have been awarded. The awards were handed out at the Venla gala on Friday. Awards were given in many different categories.

Yle's Couch Potatoes won the award for best entertainment programme. The programme features ordinary people who watch and comment on TV programmes.

The audience also voted for their favourite programme. Most votes went to Yle's programme Half Past Six. It is shown every weekday evening at 18.30 on TV1, and the programme can also be found on Yle Areena.
