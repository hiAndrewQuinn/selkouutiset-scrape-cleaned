# Sunday 21.7.2019 (radio)

Two people died on Route 6. The crayfish season has started. Emissions trading works. Ukraine holds parliamentary elections. Portugal is putting out forest fires. Bathing water has warmed up.

## Two people died in an accident on Route 6

The accident happened in Luumäki. Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

A serious traffic accident has happened in South Karelia. Two people died when a lorry crashed into a passenger car on Route 6. The accident happened at night in Luumäki. The passenger car stopped on the road. Two people got out of the car. The lorry hit them. There was one person inside the car. He was injured.

## Emissions trading works

![Smoke rises into the sky from the chimney of a German sugar factory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6389870)

Open image viewer

Companies have to pay for emissions. Photo: Armin Weigel / EPA

Emissions trading has started to work. Emissions trading works because the prices of emission allowances have risen. When emissions cost more, companies reduce emissions. In many European countries less coal is burned in power plants. More natural gas is burned, because natural gas produces fewer emissions.

## Parliamentary elections in Ukraine

![An older woman lets a young boy put her ballot paper into the ballot box in Kyiv](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10887738)

Voting took place in Ukraine on Sunday in the parliamentary elections. The parliamentary elections were held in Ukraine because the new president of Ukraine, **Volodymyr Zelenskyy**, dissolved parliament. In Ukraine's old parliament the majority opposed the new president. The new president called new elections.

## Forest fires in Portugal are being put out

Forest fires are raging in the Castelo Branco area. Photo: Kalevi Rytkölä / Yle

There are large forest fires in Portugal. A lot of firefighters are putting out the forest fires in Portugal. The army is helping the firefighters. People have been taken to safety. Roads have been closed. The forest fires are in Central Portugal. A strong wind is spreading the fires. Putting out the fires is difficult. There is intense heat in the area.

## Crayfish fishing begins

The crayfish season has started in Finland on Sunday afternoon. The crayfish catch will be good or quite good. Crayfish can be caught straight away in the waters of Southern Finland. In Northern Finland the crayfish catches are small at first.

In Finland signal crayfish and noble crayfish are caught. Every year 2–3 million signal crayfish are caught. A million noble crayfish are caught.

## Bathing waters are warming

![Lohjanjärvi shore in Vohlois.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10878534)

Open image viewer

The waters were cold for many weeks, because the air was cold. Now the waters have warmed up. Photo: Paula Tiainen / Yle

It is really warm in Finland. The warm weather is warming lakes and sea water. In many lakes the water temperature is 20 degrees. Sea water has also warmed up on the shores. The water temperature is now the normal water temperature for July.
