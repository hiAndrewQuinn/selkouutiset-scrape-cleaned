# Saturday 8.10.2016 (radio)

Many Americans are angry at presidential candidate Donald Trump, because he has spoken insultingly about women. Many Haitians have fallen ill with cholera. People in Vaasa want the Vaasa Central Hospital to keep its wide emergency care. Is the maximum age of a human being 115 years?

## Trump and women

![Donald Trump](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9218241)

Donald Trump. Photo: Jim Lo Scalzo / EPA

Many Americans are angry at presidential candidate Donald Trump. The reason is Trump's opinions about women.

On Friday the Washington Post newspaper published a video from 2005. In the video Trump talks insultingly about women. Trump says, for example, that he has touched women.

Trump's own party, the Republicans, is also angry. The party no longer wants Trump to take part in an important Republican event on Saturday. Some even ask whether Trump can continue as the Republican presidential candidate.

Donald Trump has commented on the matter on Facebook. "I was wrong and I apologise," Trump says.

There are presidential elections in the USA in November.

## Haiti and cholera

![In Haiti, Hurricane Matthew caused strong floods and at least 21 deaths. Flood waters have damaged roads, and crossing the streets is difficult. Picture from Grand Goave, Haiti, 5 October 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9212370)

Because of the storm, dirty sewage water has got into the drinking water in Haiti. Photo: EPA/Orlando Barria

The situation in Haiti is difficult. There was a strong storm in Haiti this week. About 900 people may have died in the storm.

Now Haiti has a new problem, cholera. It is a serious illness. Cholera is often caused by dirty water. In Haiti, dirty sewage water has got into the drinking water because of the storm. Some people have already died of cholera.

The UN, that is, the United Nations, says that 350,000 Haitians need help. The USA is already sending help to Haiti. Haiti is south of the USA, in the Caribbean Sea.

## Vaasa and emergency care

![According to the police, up to 10,000 people were there.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9218493)

10,000 people defended the Vaasa Central Hospital. Photo: Tuomo Rintamaa / Yle

There has been a big event in the city of Vaasa. About 10,000 people took part in the event. They defended the Vaasa Central Hospital.

People in Vaasa want the Vaasa Central Hospital to keep its wide emergency care. Then you can always get help from doctors in many specialist fields at the hospital.

Finland is planning to reform health services. Now it looks as if wide emergency care will end at the Vaasa Central Hospital.

## Humans and maximum age

![Emma Morano](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9217557)

Open image viewer

116-year-old Emma Morano is currently the oldest person in the world. Photo: Antonio Di Marco / EPA

How old can a human being live? Researchers disagree about this.

Researchers at the American Albert Einstein University say that the maximum age of a human being is 115 years. Medicine can slow down ageing, but almost nobody lives to be over 115.

Some researchers do not believe the Albert Einstein University study. Some researchers think that in the future people may live much longer than now.

So is medicine the only way to raise human life expectancy? The Frenchwoman Jeanne Calmet lived to 122. She died in 1997. Calmet's tips for a long life were olive oil, port wine and chocolate. Calmet ate almost a kilo of chocolate every week.
