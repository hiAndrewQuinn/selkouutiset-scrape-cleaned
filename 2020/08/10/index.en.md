# Monday 10.8.2020 (radio)

The coronavirus has not stopped construction work in Finland. There are 17 new corona infections and 2 new corona deaths in Finland. Young people think it is important to succeed in working life. The autumn hunting season has started. The weather is dry.

TV Clear News has a summer break and it will continue on 17.8.
Radio Clear News is made every day, also in the summer.

## Construction work continues

The coronavirus has not stopped construction work in Finland.

Construction companies say that the corona measures have slowed the work down only a little.

Construction companies try to stop the spread of the coronavirus in many ways. At the work sites, hand hygiene and safe distances are looked after.

Workers often also have more eating spaces than before, and they eat at different times.

The corona measures have also reduced other illnesses and sick leave.

However, some construction sites had problems in spring, when Finland's borders were closed because of the corona. Some foreign workers could not get to Finland.

Now the situation is normal again. About 20 per cent of construction workers are foreigners.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, reported on Monday that 2 new corona deaths have occurred in Finland. In total, 333 people have died of corona in Finland.

THL also reported 17 new corona infections on Monday. In total, about 7,600 Finns have got the coronavirus infection.

## Young people and working life

![A man looks out of a window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10182462)

Open image viewer

Succeeding in working life is important for young people. Photo: Andriy Bezuglov / AOP

Young people want to succeed in working life. This is what a new study says.

In the study, 80 per cent of young people said that succeeding in working life is important.

Pay and interesting work tasks are important to young people at work. For young men, a good salary is especially important. Young women want work tasks that they think are important.

Young people hope that school will tell them more about working life than it does now.

About 9,000 young people took part in the study.

## Bird hunting

![A shot wood pigeon.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11486898)

Open image viewer

A hunter has shot a wood pigeon. Photo: Tarja Nyyssönen / Yle

Hunting has started. The autumn hunting season began on Monday.

Hunters are now shooting birds. Wood pigeons are hunted in the whole of Finland.

Geese are also hunted. Hunters may shoot barnacle geese and Canada geese in fields. Outside fields, goose hunting starts later.

Barnacle geese may be hunted only in coastal areas near the sea.
