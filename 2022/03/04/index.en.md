## Ukrainian refugees

![Refugees boarding a train in Chelm, Poland.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_79/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-92301262205a1c58d94/1646287552)

Ukrainian refugees at a train station in Poland. Photo: Bartlomiej Wojtowicz / EPA

Many Ukrainians have left to flee the war. Russia attacked Ukraine more than a week ago.

The International Organization for Migration estimated on Friday that more than 1.25 million Ukrainians have fled Ukraine because of the war. Among them are about 500,000 children.

The EU has decided that Ukrainian refugees get temporary residence and work permits in all EU countries. They do not need to apply for asylum.

The USA, too, is giving permission to live and work to Ukrainians who are in the USA. In the USA there are tens of thousands of Ukrainians who cannot return to Ukraine because of the war.

## Access to information in Russia

![Smoke rises from a Russian armoured vehicle destroyed by Ukrainian forces by the road in the Luhansk region, 26.2.2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3500,h_1968,x_0,y_364/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9231566220981e6602a/1646303349)

Open image viewer

Russian media finds it hard to report on Ukraine's war. Photo: Anatolii Stepanov / AFP

In Russia it is hard for people to get correct information about the war in Ukraine. Now the situation is becoming even harder than before.

Russia's parliament has approved a new law. The law bans spreading information that the Russian authorities consider false information about the army.

If someone breaks the law, they may get a long prison sentence. The prison sentence can be up to 15 years.

Because of the new law, it is even harder for Russians to get information about the Russian army and the war in Ukraine.

## Number of moose

![Moose walking in the forest in Inari.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4285,h_2401,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90273961e550c8bc1f7/1644412434)

Open image viewer

Moose in the forest. Photo: Petri Timonen / Luke

There are fewer moose in Finland than before.

Around the year 2000 there were more than 150,000 moose in Finland. Now there are about 80,000 moose in Finland. This is the estimate of the Natural Resources Institute Finland.

The moose is Finland's most important game animal. More than 100,000 hunters take part in moose hunting every year.

Hunters are now allowed to shoot fewer moose than before, because the number of moose has decreased.

## Spring pollen season

![Alder catkins in Joensuu in March 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10119691)

Open image viewer

Alder is often the first tree to flower in spring. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The spring pollen season begins in the coming weeks.

Trees and shrubs are not yet flowering in Finland, but allergic people may already get symptoms. Alder and hazel are already flowering in Central Europe and in southern Sweden. From there the wind can bring their pollen as far as Finland.

Spring is a difficult time for everyone who has an allergy. Common allergy symptoms include, for example, a runny nose and itchy eyes.

Information about the pollen situation is on the website norkko.fi.
