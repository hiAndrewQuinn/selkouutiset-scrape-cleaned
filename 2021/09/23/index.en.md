Climate change and floods. Services for disabled people. Lifting of corona restrictions. The state of the Baltic Sea. Weather forecast.

## Climate change and floods

![Feet crossing a pedestrian crossing in the rain, wearing yellow rubber boots.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3868,h_2176,x_8,y_8/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6417425e43df523a538/1581506651)

Open image viewer

Rain and floods may increase because of climate change. Photo: Eleni Paspatis / Yle

Climate change may increase rain and floods a lot in Finland. This is what a report made by the Finnish Climate Panel shows. Rainfall may increase a lot over 30 years.

The Climate Panel says that people must prepare for floods in construction and in rescue plans. For example, drinking water may be spoiled in floods. The greatest flood risk is in Pori and Rovaniemi.

The Climate Panel says that people must start preparing quickly for climate change and the flood risk.

## Services for disabled people

![Päivi's electric wheelchair in a storage shed](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9497345)

Open image viewer

An electric wheelchair is an aid for a person with a physical disability. Photo: Kimmo Hiltunen / Yle

There are big differences in services for disabled people in different areas of Finland. The Finnish Disability Association says that in some municipalities possibly unlawful decisions have been made about services for disabled people.

For example, a decision on services for a disabled person may have to be waited for too long. Social workers who handle matters for disabled people can be hard to reach.

Services for disabled people include, for example, assistive device services, personal assistance, transport services and rehabilitation.

## Lifting corona restrictions

![Empty stands at the Olympic Stadium.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2000,h_1125,x_0,y_238/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7431715fb3e64aa7a52/1605626642)

Open image viewer

Corona restrictions have hindered, for example, the organising of events. Photo: Akseli Valmunen

Corona restrictions may be lifted in Finland perhaps at the end of October or the beginning of November. The Ministry of Health and Social Affairs and the health agency talked about the corona situation on Thursday afternoon.

The corona situation has improved a little. The number of vaccinated Finns is growing. Already 67 per cent of Finns over 12 have received 2 corona vaccinations.

Corona restrictions can be lifted when 80 per cent of people over 12 have received the vaccine.

## The state of the sea areas

![The Selkämeri sea area off Kallo island](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8011630)

Open image viewer

The state of the Selkämeri is poor. The situation in the Baltic Sea may improve. Photo: Kati Rantala / Yle

The condition of the Baltic Sea has improved slightly. The state of the Baltic Sea worsened about 6 years ago. At that time a lot of salty water came into the Baltic from the North Sea.

The Finnish Environment Institute says that the Baltic Sea may be doing a little better again.

In the other sea area, the Selkämeri, the situation is still poor. The Environment Institute says that in the deep parts of the Selkämeri the oxygen situation is getting worse.
