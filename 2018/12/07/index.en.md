# Friday 7.12.2018 (radio)

Swastika flags. A TV audience of millions. Party support. Bus disruptions.

## Investigation into swastika flags on Independence Day

![Police stopped the neo-Nazi "Towards Freedom!" march in Hakaniemi in Helsinki on Independence Day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10543659)

The appearance of swastika flags at the protests has caused an uproar in Finland. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The Finnish police are investigating the uproar over swastika flags on Independence Day. Yesterday the police decided to take swastika flags away from protesters in Helsinki.

Swastika flags are symbols of the Nazis. The police say that, at least for now, they will not give the flags back to the protesters. The authorities are investigating whether the users of the flags have committed a crime.

## A large audience for the party broadcasts

![Krista Paasi at the Independence Day reception at the Presidential Palace in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10542997)

Open image viewer

The handshake at the Palace party again interested a TV audience of millions. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The Independence Day party broadcasts on Yle's TV channels reached more than 3 million Finns this year. The figure includes the Palace party, the military parade, the "Towards the Palace party" programme and the Palace after-party programmes.

The most viewers were watching the TV broadcast of the Palace party on TV1, just before the 8:30 news. At that time about 2.5 million people were following the party. Following the Palace party on the internet clearly increased compared with last year.

The live Palace party broadcast was started 570,000 times on Yle Areena. Last year the same number was 260,000.

## Green Party support rises

![Party support estimates](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10541221)

Open image viewer

Support for the Greens has risen since the party got a new chairperson. Photo: Yle News Graphics

Support for the Greens has grown. The Greens elected Pekka Haavisto as their chairperson about a month ago. Haavisto has been in politics for a long time. Now it has been studied for the first time how the choice of the new chairperson has affected support for the Greens.

Support for the Greens started to grow right away. Support has grown by 2.6 percentage points and is now 13.9 percent. According to the study, the biggest party is still the SDP, then the National Coalition Party and the Centre Party. The Greens are fourth.

## Investigation into bus disruptions continues

![A bus driver drives a car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10542578)

Open image viewer

Attempts were made to disturb the bus driver's driving in Loimaa and in Uurainen. Photo: Stefan Paavola / Yle

The police are continuing their investigation of the bus incidents. The police suspect that two men tried to affect the driving of a bus in different parts of Finland at the beginning of this week. In Uurainen in Central Finland, a male passenger had grabbed the driver during the journey. In Loimaa in Southwest Finland, another man had done the same.

Both men are about 30 years old and are foreign nationals. They come from different countries. According to the police, there is no link between the acts.

At the moment the police do not suspect terrorism. However, the police suspect the men of another crime and demand that the men be jailed.
