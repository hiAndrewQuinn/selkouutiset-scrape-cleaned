# Monday 7.12.2020 (radio)

The number of child welfare notifications is growing. Finland's new coronavirus figures. Finns want to buy domestic food. The new-style palace celebrations reached a mass audience.

## Child welfare notifications

![A child looks out of a window.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4567,h_2569,x_0,y_471/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6615835e8745804ff59/1604930862)

The number of child welfare notifications has grown during the coronavirus time. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Many municipalities say that the number of child welfare notifications has grown during the coronavirus pandemic. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare and the Central Union for Child Welfare asked municipalities about child welfare.

More than 40 per cent of municipalities say that child welfare notifications increased during the autumn compared with the time before the coronavirus. The police and schools have made the most new child welfare notifications.

The municipalities say that the number of child welfare notifications has grown perhaps because, for example, parents are tired, families have money worries, or there are problems with children's schooling.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare has reported the new coronavirus figures.

There have been 9 new coronavirus deaths in Finland.

There are 250 new coronavirus infections. More than half of the new infections have been found in Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa.

More than 200 coronavirus patients are currently in hospital care.

## Domestic food

![Close-up of a shopping basket with purchases at an S-market.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_619/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6645945e96eb4b72c98/1586950611)

Open image viewer

Finns want to buy domestic food. Photo: Eleni Paspatis / Yle

Finns are buying more domestic food than before.

Sales of domestic food have grown during the coronavirus time, because people are now baking and cooking at home more than before.

For example, the S Group's shops say that sales of domestic food have grown by about 6 per cent. Lidl, on the other hand, says that domestic products are being sold about 15 per cent more than last year.

Grocery shops say that people are buying even more fresh domestic meat, potatoes, root vegetables and vegetables, for example.

The shops say that Finns want to buy domestic food because they value its purity and good quality. In addition, they want to support Finnish work.

## Palace celebrations 2020

![The Biolapset children's group performing in the Atrium of the Presidential Palace at the Independence Day celebration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11684482)

The Biolapset children's group performed at the Presidential Palace on Independence Day. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Office of the President of the Republic

Yle's Independence Day broadcast from the Presidential Palace interested the audience. On average, more than 1.5 million viewers watched the Palace Celebrations 2020 broadcast.

Because of the coronavirus pandemic, the palace celebrations were different this year than usual. There were no guests at the palace, but a group of Finnish artists were there. They performed music and dance, for example. In addition, during the evening, Finns from different parts of the country were met.

Last year, on average about 2.3 million people watched the palace celebrations on television.
