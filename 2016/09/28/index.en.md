# Wednesday 28.9.2016 (radio)

A Russian missile brought down the Malaysian passenger plane. Finland's competitiveness has got worse. There is a shortage of doctors and builders. Noise harms a child's speech development. Israel's former president died.

## A Russian missile brought down the Malaysian plane

![The wreck of a Malaysia Airlines Boeing 777 in a field in Eastern Ukraine in July 2014.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8377251)

The Malaysian passenger plane fell into a field in Eastern Ukraine in July 2 years ago. Photo: Anastasia Vlasova / EPA

A Russian missile brought down a Malaysian plane in Ukraine 2 years ago.

An international team of criminal investigators says that the plane fell because it was hit by a Russian Buk anti-aircraft missile. The missile was launched in Eastern Ukraine from an area that was held by pro-Russian rebels.

The international team of criminal investigators, led by the Netherlands, has investigated the crash of the Malaysian passenger plane. Over 100 criminal investigators took part in the investigation.

Almost 300 people died when the Malaysian passenger plane was shot down in July 2014.

## Finland's competitiveness has got worse

![Finland's competitiveness comparison](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9195367)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle News Graphics / Rea Lemström

The World Economic Forum has made an international comparison of competitiveness.

The number one country in the comparison is Switzerland. The best of the Nordic countries is Sweden, which is 6th.

Finland is 10th in the comparison. That is Finland's worst ranking in 10 years.

The comparison is based on statistics and interviews with business leaders.

## There is a shortage of doctors and builders

![Stethoscope](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9076203)

Open image viewer

There is now work for doctors and other health care professionals. Photo: Eeva Kuivas / Yle

In Finland, there are many fields where there is a shortage of workers.

The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment's occupational barometer says that there is a shortage especially of doctors and other health and social care professionals. There is also work now for various construction professionals.

There is also a shortage of telephone salespeople and sales representatives. For example, there are over 10,000 vacancies for sales representatives in the whole country.

On the other hand, it is now really hard, for example, for artists, journalists and secretaries to find work.

## Noise can harm a child's speech development

![A boot in the hallway of a daycare centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8657783)

Open image viewer

There can sometimes be a lot of noise in a daycare centre. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

Noise is a risk to a child's speech development. It is hard for a child to recognise differences in letters and syllables if there is noise in the background.

A new study shows that, for example, pallo and pullo or tuuli and tuli sound the same if there is a lot of background noise in the room.

The study was done at the University of Oulu. 2-year-old children took part in it. In children of this age, language develops fast.

In children's everyday life, noise comes especially in daycare centres.

## Israel's former president died

![Shimon Peres 1923-2016](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9196668)

Open image viewer

Shimon Peres died at the age of 93. Photo: Ronen Zvulun/EPA

Israel's former prime minister and president Shimon Peres has died. Peres died of a brain haemorrhage. He was 93 years old.

Peres's career in politics lasted almost 70 years. In  1994, he received the Nobel Peace Prize for his work in the peace negotiations between Israel and the Palestinians.

World leaders have expressed their condolences on Peres's death.
