## Stopping a young moped driver

First, news about the police.

A high-ranking authority is investigating police actions in Tuusula.

Police violently stopped a young moped driver on Saturday. The incident is seen in a video that was filmed by bystanders.

A police car wedged a moped into a ditch. The moped crashed into a fence and a steel container.

The police's action was perhaps too dangerous, as there was a fence and a container next to the road. The moped rider was only a young boy.

A high-ranking authority is now investigating the police's actions.

The actions of the moped driver are also being investigated.

The moped was missing a license plate and was perhaps going too fast.

The moped driver was only slightly injured, but there was a risk of serious injury in the situation.

## Clothing returns to online stores

Now for the news about clothing returns from online stores.

Finns return approximately 30 percent of clothing purchases.

These are returns of clothes from the women's clothing store Pappu.

Papu's owner **Tuomo Saarni** says that returning one garment costs the company up to 14 euros.

That's why returns are annoying for small online stores.

Finns return approximately 30 percent of clothing purchases from online stores.

The reason for the return is often the wrong size.

Another reason is that the garment doesn't feel or look good when you put it on at home.

Clothing store Papu wants to reduce returns. Tuomo Saarni says that images and product information need to be improved.

## European Athletics Championships

Finally, sports news.

The European Athletics Championships, or European Championships, begin today.

The competition will be held in Birmingham, UK.

Over 60 Finnish track and field athletes will participate in the games.

Hammer thrower **Silja Kosonen** says that in practice the hammer has sometimes flown well and sometimes poorly.

Now it feels like the throws are working.

*"In training, sometimes I've flown less and then more again. Now it looks quite promising, what has started to be lightened up here, it feels quite good."*

The European Athletics Championships start today.

The competition lasts 7 days, until Sunday.
