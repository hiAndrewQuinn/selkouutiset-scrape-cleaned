The first week of the government negotiations is over.

The leader of the government negotiations is the chairman of the National Coalition, Petteri Orpo. Along with the National Coalition, the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats are taking part in the government negotiations.

## Milan Jaff

![Milan Jaff in court.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_189/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10202636347bb906e73e/1683285534)

Milan Jaff, the leader of a Helsinki street gang, has received a new punishment. The court sentenced Jaff to 1 year and 8 months in prison.

The punishment was for 2 rapes. The second rape was a child rape, because the victim was only 13 years old.

Milan Jaff has already received a 10-year prison sentence earlier. That punishment was for violent crimes.

## Parkinson's disease

![Hospital research equipment.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_393/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-953799627cbc7163044/1683299323)

Open image viewer

Hospital research equipment. Archive picture. Photo: Arash Matin / Yle

Researchers at the University of Helsinki have received new information about Parkinson's disease. Parkinson's disease causes, for example, tremor and eventually makes moving difficult.

Until now it has not been known what causes Parkinson's disease. Now researchers at the University of Helsinki have found out that the cause of Parkinson's disease is often a bacterium.

If a person gets bacteria of the desulfovibrio group from the environment, they may get Parkinson's disease. The new information may help the treatment of Parkinson's patients.

Britain is preparing for a coronation.

The new king, Charles III, will be crowned on Saturday. The coronation ceremony will be held in London, at Westminster Abbey church.

2000 guests have been invited to the coronation. Among them are also President Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio.

The British coronation ceremony can also be followed in Finland. The broadcast starts on Saturday at 11.45 on Yle Areena and on TV1.

## Drought in Southern France

![Abandoned boats at the bottom of a dried-up lake.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5412,h_3030,x_0,y_563/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11093056454dfc9494da/1712635431)

Open image viewer

There has been no rain in Southern France for a year. Photo: Sebastian Nogier / EPA

It is very dry in Southern France.

That is why the French government bans the sale of swimming pools in the southern part of the country. There has been no rain in the area for a year.

Because of the drought, people must save water. Washing cars and watering lawns have also been restricted, for example.

## The weather

In Finland the weather is colder than usual. The temperature will not rise above 10 degrees on Saturday, even in the south.

Saturday starts sunny, and during the day cloudiness increases. The weather is dry, though, so no rain falls.

On Sunday the weather starts to warm, and in the middle of next week the temperature will rise to about 15 degrees in the south.
