# Monday 17.2.2020 (radio)

There will be no strikes in the property services sector. A winter storm cut the power to many homes. There will be no headscarf ban in Swedish schools. It will soon be 80 years since the end of the Winter War.

## The property services sector avoids strikes

![A cleaner mopping the floor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10291830)

Many kinds of work are done in the property services sector, for example cleaning. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

There will be no strikes in the property services sector. The strikes were supposed to start tomorrow.

The strikes have been cancelled, because a new collective agreement has been made in the property services sector. The agreement raises wages in the property services sector by 3.3 percent over 2 years. Wages will rise by the same amount as in many other sectors.

In the property services sector, for example cleaning and property maintenance work is done. The strikes have now been cancelled in many hotels, for example.

## The winter storm cut power

![Rescue workers wade through flood water in Nantgarw.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11212143)

Open image viewer

Storm Dennis brought flood water to the streets of England. In Finland the storm was not as strong. Photo: Neil Munns / EPA

There has been a winter storm in Finland. The storm brought strong winds and rain. The storm winds blew most strongly during the night between Sunday and Monday.

The storm knocked down trees and cut the power. More than 16,000 homes were without power on Sunday evening. However, the repairs went quickly.

The storm came from the Atlantic Ocean. Before the storm came to Finland, it hit Britain, for example. There the storm was stronger than in Finland.

In Britain the storm was named Dennis. It caused floods, and the flood water covered houses and cars. 2 people died in Britain because of the storm.

## Headscarves are allowed in Swedish schools

![Muslim women on a street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10355416)

Open image viewer

Photo: AOP

There will be no headscarf ban in Swedish schools.

The Swedish authorities have decided that schoolgirls may wear headscarves. This is part of freedom of religion, the authorities say. Many Muslim families want girls to wear headscarves.

In many Swedish municipalities there has been discussion about a headscarf ban. For example, in December the municipality of Skurup decided that pupils and staff at schools may not wear headscarves.

Now the Swedish authorities say that wearing headscarves in schools cannot be banned.

## Flags for the end of the Winter War

![A Finnish flag on a pole.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11060883)

Open image viewer

The flags will fly on 13 March, when 80 years will have passed since the end of the Winter War. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

The Ministry of the Interior wants to remember the end of the Winter War.

The Winter War ended 80 years ago, on 13 March 1940. That is why the Ministry of the Interior recommends that flags are flown in Finland on 13 March this year.

The Winter War was part of the events of the Second World War. The Winter War was between Finland and the Soviet Union. The most important area of the communist Soviet Union was Russia.

The Winter War lasted 105 days. It ended with the Moscow Peace Treaty. In the peace treaty the Soviet Union got 10 percent of Finland's territory. More than 400,000 Finns fled from the areas and had to find a new home elsewhere in Finland.
