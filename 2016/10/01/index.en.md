# Saturday 1.10.2016 (radio)

It is now easier than before to get a fixed fine. Loneliness is the root cause of many problems. The church wants to help asylum seekers. A giant footprint has been found in a desert.

## New limits for fixed fines came into force

![A police surveillance camera.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9204106)

Open image viewer

The police also monitor speeds with technology. There are post cameras, or "tin police", on many roads. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

A fixed fine is now given more easily than before. The new guidelines came into force on Saturday.

The police give a fixed fine if you are going seven (7) kilometres an hour over the speed limit. Before, you got a fixed fine if you were going eight (8) kilometres an hour over the speed limit.

The police will start to monitor speeds more effectively in risky places. Risky places are, for example, near daycare centres and schools.

The amount of the fixed fine is fixed, that is, the same for everyone.

## Loneliness is the root cause of many problems

![Juho Saari](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9185513)

Open image viewer

Professor Juho Saari from the University of Tampere knows what the dark side of Finnish life is like. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

Loneliness is the cause of many problems in Finland. Professor Juho Saari says that almost one in five Finns feels lonely. That is almost 20 percent of Finns.

Loneliness is also a big social problem. Loneliness can be the cause, for example, of a substance abuse problem, being overweight or depression. The professor says that loneliness is a so-called root cause.

For many, loneliness starts already in childhood. As an adult, for example, getting work can be hard because of loneliness.

The researcher says that society must also take care of lonely people. The professor is especially worried about lonely adult men who are not in work, education or a relationship.

## Bishops want to help asylum seekers

![Bishops of different dioceses in the picture.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9196433)

Open image viewer

The bishops are thinking about what role the church has in helping asylum seekers. Photo: Kalle Mäkelä / Yle

The bishops say that the church cannot close its door to asylum seekers who have got a negative asylum decision.

Yle News asked the bishops of the Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland about the matter.

The majority of the bishops say that the church wants to help people. But the church cannot offer people actual asylum, because that is the task of society. The bishops say that the church can help asylum seekers, for example, together with the authorities.

## A giant's footprint was found in the Gobi Desert

![A smiling researcher lies next to a raised, fossilised footprint. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9204199)

Open image viewer

Professor Shinobu Ishigaki from Japan shows how big the dinosaur's footprint is. Photo: Okayama University

A big fossilised footprint has been found in the Gobi Desert in Mongolia. The dinosaur's footprint is one of the biggest footprints of all time. The print is over a metre long and almost 80 centimetres wide.

The print was found in a layer of earth that is about 70 million years old. At that time, the Gobi was not yet a desert but a mud plain. The sole of the foot left a print in the mud that was like a mould.

The researchers believe that the giant animal was a titanosaur. The animal was perhaps bigger than a big plane, a jumbo jet, and weighed over 70 tonnes.
