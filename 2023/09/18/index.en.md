## Cheating of Kenyan students

![Scholarship document made by the Uasin Gishu county for a TAMK nursing student. According to it, the students had a full scholarship paid by the county.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2324,h_1307,x_0,y_360/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-11717976502b55937b3a/1694676995)

Open image viewer

According to this document, a Kenyan county paid the students' studies in Finland. Scholarship documents were made for TAMK nursing and physiotherapy students. Photo: screenshot of an internal corruption report of the Kenyan county Uashin Gishu. Photo: screenshot of an internal corruption report of the Kenyan county Uashin Gishu

Yle's MOT programme has found out about the deception of Kenyan students. Kenya deceived several hundred Kenyan students into going to Finland for healthcare training.

At least 2 500 students have come to Finland through Kenya's contract training.

Finnish authorities and educational institutions believed that Kenyan counties pay for the studies in Finland. In reality, the students paid thousands of euros to Kenya.

For example, 2 students paid about 10 000 euros into the account of Uasin Gishu county.

It was meant to pay the first semester fee, flights, insurance, residence permits and half a year's rent.

However, the students had to pay everything themselves.

Officials of Uasin Gishu lied that students easily get nursing work in Finland and can send money to their families in Kenya.

Now the students have a lot of debt. In Finland they work, for example, as food delivery drivers or cleaners.

## Integration of immigrants into work

![Postman Hameed Shinwari on his delivery round in West Pakila, Helsinki, 21.5.2021.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5766,h_3243,x_0,y_297/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80931060a78c68ca548/1621601682)

Open image viewer

Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

Immigrants get work in Finland best when they ask employers directly for work.

Getting work through acquaintances is also common, according to a study by the Institute for Health and Welfare (THL).

Only a few immigrants get jobs through the TE Office or through private employment agencies.

THL investigated the employment of people who moved to Finland in 2018 and 2019.

Making an emergency report will become easier at the end of 2025.

Then an emergency report can also be made with real-time text input, says the Emergency Response Centre Agency.

Text input helps people who have difficulty hearing or speaking. Now they have been using the emergency text message.

In real-time text input, text is passed between the reporter and the emergency centre letter by letter.

## E-cigarettes

![A person wearing a hoodie blows smoke into the air.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6039,h_3398,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115674164db4ba320ff9/1692093941)

Open image viewer

THL proposes raising the minimum age for buying tobacco products. According to THL, raising the age limit to 20 years may reduce children's opportunities to get e-cigarettes. Photo: Chicken Strip / Alamy/All Over Press

A lot of e-cigarettes are sold on the dark, that is illegal, market. E-cigarettes, that is vapes, are popular among children.

Yle's investigation shows that vapes are sold, among other places, on social media and on the messaging app Telegram.

Minors also sell vapes to other children.

Many children have already become addicted to nicotine, that is, they are dependent on nicotine.

On the dark markets, e-cigarettes are sold that contain much more nicotine than the law allows.
