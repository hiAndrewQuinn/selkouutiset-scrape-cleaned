## The worst train accident in Taiwan's history

![A train derailed near the city of Hualien in Taiwan.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11868877)

A train derailed near the city of Hualien in Taiwan. Photo: Reuters TV

About 50 people have died in a train accident in Taiwan. The crash happened between the capital, Taipei, and Hualien.

Some of the carriages got stuck in a tunnel. There were almost 500 passengers on the train.

The train accident is the worst in Taiwan's history. The accident may have been caused by a lorry that slid onto the track from a construction site.

## Carer's leave may come into force next year

![A man receives cytostatic treatment at Oulu University Hospital.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8481014)

Open image viewer

Until now, employers have also been able to grant leave to care for a family member. Now, however, the right is coming into law. Photo: Niko Mannonen / Yle

Next year, it may be possible to get leave from work to care for a family member at the end of their life.

A family member may be able to be away from work if they take part in the end-of-life care of a person who is dying. Short-term leave can also be taken if you take a close relative to the doctor.

The new leave totals 5 days a year. No pay is received for the leave period.

The reform will be added to the Employment Contracts Act. The aim is for carer's leave to be in use from August next year.

## Pilot training decreases

![Two people studying to be pilots in a flight simulator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11862084)

Open image viewer

Two students training to be pilots in a flight simulator. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

Fewer professional pilots are being trained in Finland. The number of training places will shrink in the coming years.

Air travel is recovering more slowly than was expected. That is why fewer new airline pilots will be hired.

However, there may be a pilot shortage in Finland in the coming years, because training is decreasing.

There are 1,400 airline pilots in Finland. About 70 percent of them have been laid off.

## Young men's faith is more common than before

Today Good Friday has been celebrated.

According to the Bible, Jesus was crucified on Good Friday. The day of Christ's death is the only day of mourning in the church year.

The liturgical colour, for example on church textiles, is black.

A study says that belief in the Christian message of Easter has increased among men under 30.

The result was a surprise to researchers.

In 2011, 14 percent of young men believed in Jesus's resurrection. The year before last, it was 30 percent.

Still in the early 2000s, 62 percent of Finns believed that Jesus rose from the dead. In 2019, only 32 percent still believed it.

Women under 30 are especially critical.

Traditionally, women have been more religious than men, but among young people the situation has turned the other way round.

## Western Europe's record heat fades next week

![Two young people jump from a harbour pier into the sea in Copenhagen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11868966)

Open image viewer

It was already very warm in Denmark on Tuesday. Photo: Liselotte Sabroe / AFP

The weather in western Europe has been unusually warm this week.

In Paris, France, 25.6 degrees was measured on Wednesday. That is the highest March reading in the city's measurement history.

After the weekend, temperatures in western Europe will fall by as much as 20 degrees.
