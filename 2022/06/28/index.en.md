## Niinistö's negotiations

![Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5119,h_2879,x_0,y_426/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97504262b99780895b4/1656330266)

President Sauli Niinistö has negotiated in Madrid. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

President Sauli Niinistö has negotiated with Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan.

Swedish Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson and NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg also took part in the negotiations.

The negotiations took place in the Spanish capital Madrid. In Madrid the NATO defence alliance holds its meeting.

In the negotiations, Finland's and Sweden's NATO membership applications have been discussed. Turkey has wanted to talk also about its own security concerns, before it approves Finland's and Sweden's accession to NATO.

Finland and Sweden decided to apply for NATO membership already in May. The matter has not moved forward because of Turkey.

## Russian missile strike

![Firefighters and other rescue personnel move in the ruins of a shopping centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1574,h_885,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-97519762ba7ec7a96c4/1656389405)

Open image viewer

Russia destroyed a shopping centre in the city of Kremenchuk in Ukraine. Photo: Ukrainian rescue service

Russia has fired missiles at a Ukrainian shopping centre.

About 20 people died in the strike and several dozen people were injured. They were ordinary Ukrainians, not soldiers.

More dead and injured may be found when rescuers examine the ruins of the shopping centre.

The strike happened in Kremenchuk in Central Ukraine on Monday. Russian missiles hit the shopping centre when it was full of people.

Russia's strike has been condemned around the world. For example, the UN, that is the United Nations, says that in war, attacks must not be made against ordinary people.

Russia started the war in Ukraine over 4 months ago. Russia attacked Ukraine in February.

![Close-up of petrol pumps.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_570/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-921606621cc5916970b/1649159322)

The price of petrol fell a little last week. Before that the price of petrol rose for a long time.

Petrol cost on average 2 euros and 55 cents last week. That was 2 cents less than a week earlier.

The price of diesel, however, also rose last week. Diesel cost 5 cents more last week than a week earlier.

## Berry harvest

![Pickers in Yrjölä's strawberry field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2267,x_0,y_422/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-82514260d43857aaffb/1624521591)

Open image viewer

Strawberries are ripening now in warm weather. Photo: Miikka Varila / Yle

Strawberry season has started. Strawberries are ripening quickly now, because the weather is warm.

It is expected that this summer's strawberry crop will be good, that is, a lot of strawberries will be picked.

Also wild berries can be picked in large amounts this summer. Bilberry and lingonberry have flowered normally. There have been many cloudberry flowers.

If the weather is right, a lot of berries can develop from the flowers. Last summer drought reduced the bilberry crop.
