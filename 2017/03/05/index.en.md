# Sunday 5.3.2017 (radio)

Matti Heikkinen won bronze in the 50-kilometre race in Lahti. Finnair is cancelling many flights. Timo Soini will not continue as chair of the Finns Party. There has been a rush on Yle's election compass. Criminals carry out data breaches in many ways.

## Finland won bronze in the 50-kilometre ski race

![Matti Heikkinen celebrates. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9492785)

Finland's Matti Heikkinen rewarded the crowd in the last event of the World Championships, the men's 50-kilometre race. Finland got five medals in total at the championships. Photo: Tomi Hänninen

Finland has won its fifth (5) medal on the last day of the Lahti World Championships.

Matti Heikkinen took bronze in the 50-kilometre cross-country race. Gold went to Canada and silver to Russia.

About 180,000 tickets were sold for the championships, when the goal was 250,000.

## Finnair is cancelling flights

![A Finnair passenger plane at Helsinki-Vantaa Airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9492574)

Open image viewer

The aviation labour dispute and the strikes supporting it are keeping many Finnair planes on the ground. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Finnair is cancelling many flights because of the aviation labour dispute and its supporting strikes.

Finnair says that it has to cancel five (5) one-way flights on Sunday and 17 on Monday because of the dispute over working conditions.

The aviation union's next industrial action and supporting strikes start on the night between Sunday and Monday. The stoppage in security checks and ground services lasts from three o'clock until nine in the morning.

## Timo Soini will not continue as leader of the Finns Party

![Timo Soini](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8999098)

Open image viewer

Timo Soini has said he will announce "around spring" whether he will still run for chair of the Finns Party. On Sunday Soini announced that he will not continue as chair. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

Timo Soini will not continue as chair of the Finns Party.

Soini writes online that he will no longer run. The new chair of the Finns Party will be chosen at the party congress in Jyväskylä in June.

Soini has led the Finns Party for 20 years.

Defence Minister Jussi Niinistö and parliamentary group chair Sampo Terho plan to say on Monday whether they will run. Member of the European Parliament Jussi Halla-aho has also said that he may be interested in the chair's job.

## There has been a rush on Yle's election compass

![Municipal elections 2017 Election gallery](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9464619)

Open image viewer

Characters from the series Kätevä emäntä encourage candidates to make election videos. Photo: Yle

A rush of municipal election candidates has caused congestion on Yle's election compass. The election compass is a web page where a voter can look for a suitable candidate.

By Sunday afternoon, over 13,000 candidates had used the election compass.  Sunday was the last day when candidates could upload their own videos to the service.

The election compass is meant to open to the public at the end of next week. The voting day of the municipal elections is 9 April.

## Cyber criminals are cunning

![Illustration: corporate espionage graphic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9489069)

Open image viewer

Data security is also tested with social hacking: people are fooled, for example, with a disguise and a cover story. Photo: Yle News Graphics

Criminals can break into companies' data in many ways. One way is to sneak into the company's premises. A criminal can use a disguise and pretend to be there on legal business. Cyber crimes take advantage of people who are trusting, helpful and careless.

Last year the police got about 1000 reports of online crimes, or cyber crimes.

There are also companies that offer data security to others. A security service tests companies' data security and makes different test attacks. With them you can find the weak points in the company's operations.
