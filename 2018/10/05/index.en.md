## Nobel Peace Prize awarded

![Denis Mukwege and Nadia Murad.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10441485)

Open image viewer

Congolese doctor Denis Mukwege and Iraqi activist Nadia Murad will receive this year's Nobel Peace Prize. Photo: AFP and EPA

The Nobel Peace Prize this year goes to the Congolese doctor Denis Mukwege and the human rights activist Nadia Murad.

Both have worked especially to help war victims who have suffered sexual violence. Nadia Murad herself is a victim of rape.

She escaped the sexual slavery of the Islamic State forces. Murad has spoken publicly about her experiences as a captive of the organisation.

Denis Mukwege is a Congolese gynaecologist who has helped victims who have experienced violence. According to the Nobel Committee, Mukwege and Murad have courageously opposed and exposed war crimes.

The prize is very prestigious, and it is awarded every year in Norway.

## Few opponents of abortion

![Opinions on abortion.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10439435)

Open image viewer

Photo: Yle / Uutisgrafiikka

According to a Yle survey, there are very few opponents of abortion in Finland. According to the survey, a large part of Finns have a liberal attitude to abortion.

About 1,100 people responded to the Yle survey.

71 percent of the people who answered the survey accept abortion. 24 percent allowed abortion under certain conditions. A condition may be that the woman has been raped or that the mother's health is at risk.

Three percent do not accept abortion at all.

## A man confessed to setting the church on fire

![Smoking ruins of the Kiihtelysvaara church.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10420078)

Open image viewer

The church burned completely. Photo: Petri Lassheikki / Yle

A young man has admitted that he set the Kiihtelysvaara church on fire about two weeks ago.

Kiihtelysvaara is located in Joensuu, in Eastern Finland. The fire burned the church completely.

The man used flammable liquids to burn the church. The man has told the police about the events in detail. The police arrested other people too, but they have been released, and the police no longer suspect them of the fire.

The Kiihtelysvaara church was a wooden church. It was about 270 years old.

## Winter has come to the north

![Skiers at the Ruka ski resort in Kuusamo on Friday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10441613)

Open image viewer

Skiers at the Ruka ski resort in Kuusamo. Photo: Harri Lindfors / Lehtikuva

Winter has come to Northern Finland. In Kuusamo you can already go downhill skiing. There is artificial snow on the slope, but real snow fell on the day the slopes opened.

According to the Finnish Meteorological Institute, as much as 5-10 centimetres of snow may fall in Lapland.

The snow will not melt away in a day, but will probably stay at least through tonight.
