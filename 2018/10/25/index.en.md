# Thursday 25.10.2018 (radio)

The government is trying to settle a dispute over the dismissal law with the trade unions. The chair of the trade union Akava is being criticised. Finland plans to ban marriages of minors completely. Lappajärvi is celebrating world champion Petra Olli.

## Dispute over the dismissal law

![Minister of Employment Jari Lindström, Prime Minister Juha Sipilä and Minister of Finance Petteri Orpo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10476082)

Ministers Jari Lindström, Juha Sipilä and Petteri Orpo told journalists about the meeting with the leaders of the trade unions. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

The government is trying to settle the dispute over the dismissal law. The government met the chairs of the trade unions on Thursday.

The trade unions got a new proposal from the government on how the dispute can be solved. The trade unions plan to comment on the proposal on Friday.

The dispute between the trade unions and the government began when the government proposed a new dismissal law. The government's idea was that small firms could dismiss employees more easily.

But the trade unions got angry about it. They have even organised strikes because of the dispute.

## The chair of Akava

![Sture Fjäder](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10216812)

Open image viewer

Akava chair Sture Fjäder. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / AOP

The chair of the trade union Akava, Sture Fjäder, is being criticised. The reason is Fjäder's opinion on immigrants' pay.

On Wednesday Fjäder suggested that an immigrant could be paid a lower wage than a Finn if he or she has no education. It is a way to help an immigrant get work, Fjäder said.

Akava is an organisation for highly educated people. Many in the organisation are angry about Fjäder's proposal. It is estimated that chair Fjäder may even be fired.

## Marriages of minors

![married couple hand in hand](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9577189)

Open image viewer

Soon nobody under 18 will be allowed to get married. Photo: AOP

Finland plans to ban marriages of minors completely. Minors are people under 18.

Nowadays minors can apply for special permission to get married. For example, last year the authorities received 11 applications. 7 applications were approved.

Often the person applying for special permission is a 17-year-old girl who wants to get married because of religion or pregnancy. People under 17 have only rarely been given special permission.

The government wants to change the law so that it is no longer possible to apply for special permission. Parliament, that is the Finnish parliament, decides on changing the law.

## A world champion is celebrated

![Wrestler Petra Olli](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10210553)

Open image viewer

Wrestler Petra Olli. Photo: Elina Niemistö/Yle

Lappajärvi is celebrating a new world champion. Wrestler Petra Olli comes from Lappajärvi.

Olli won World Championship gold on Wednesday in Hungary. Olli is the first Finnish female wrestler to be world champion.

The residents of Lappajärvi are proud of the world champion. The municipality of Lappajärvi plans to hold a big party for Olli.

Olli will also get a present, but the municipal manager of Lappajärvi cannot yet say what the present is.
