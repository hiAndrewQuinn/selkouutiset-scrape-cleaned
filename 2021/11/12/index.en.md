Turkey is preventing migrants coming from the Middle East from getting into Belarus. Turkey has agreed on this with the EU.

Turkey is a country through which migrants have come to Belarus. In Belarus the authorities have sent them to the Polish border.

Poland says that Belarus is deliberately sending people towards the European Union. The European Union thinks that Belarus is trying to disturb countries that have criticised Belarus's poor human rights situation.

## Travel in Europe

Travel in Europe has become easier since the corona pass was introduced.

Before the corona pass, travelling in Europe was very difficult because of the coronavirus.

In addition to the corona pass, some European countries also require other information and forms.

That is why it is important to find out the requirements before the trip.

The requirements of different countries can be asked, for example, from the airline or a travel agency.

## Paternity leave

![A father and son on a bridge.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10795249)

Open image viewer

Half of the male respondents felt that they had very or fairly good opportunities to influence the length of their working day. Illustration photo. Photo: AOP

Fathers in Finland use more than half of their paternity leave.

Kela says that last year fathers used about 66 per cent of their paternity leave.

That means that one father was on paternity leave for an average of 35 weekdays.

In Finland fathers may be on paternity leave for at most 54 weekdays. During this time Kela pays them paternity allowance.

## The weather

On Saturday the weather is largely cloudy, and in places a little snow or rain falls.

The temperature on Saturday is between 0 and 3 degrees in a large part of the country. In Lapland it is about 10 degrees of frost.

In the western and northern parts of the country the roads may be slippery because of ice.
