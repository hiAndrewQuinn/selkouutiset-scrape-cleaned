# Sunday 6.10.2019 (radio)

Flu vaccinations start later than usual. The police are worried about big speeding. Lake Inari is one of the most beautiful places in Europe.

## Flu vaccinations

Flu vaccinations start later than usual in Finland this year.

![Flu vaccine packaging box.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10475794)

Open image viewer

Flu vaccine. Photo: Matti Myller / Yle

The vaccinations are late, because the vaccine was ready later than usual.

Finland has ordered 1.8 million doses of the flu vaccine. Adults get the vaccine as an injection, but small children can also get the vaccine as a nasal spray.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare recommends that mass vaccinations in municipalities start after the middle of November. The flu epidemic usually starts after the turn of the year.

## Speeding

![Police monitor traffic.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10321295)

Open image viewer

Police stepped up traffic monitoring in the Oulu area last night. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The police are worried about big speeding on main roads. The police say that speeding is already crazily high.

For example, last night the police arrested several young drivers who were driving at very high speeds.

The worst speeding was by a young man in Liminka near Oulu. He drove at 190 kilometres per hour, even though the speed limit in the area was 100 kilometres per hour. The young driver was only 17 years old. He had got his driving licence with a special permit.

In Haukipudas, the police caught a young man who was riding a motorbike. He drove at 155 kilometres per hour in a roadworks area, even though the speed limit was 60 kilometres per hour.

Near Tampere, in Nokia, a young man was caught who drove at 165 kilometres per hour, when the speed limit was 80 kilometres per hour.

## Lake Inari

![Ukonsaari island in Lake Inari in the winter polar night landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11007389)

Open image viewer

Ukonsaari on Lake Inari in the winter polar night. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

The American news website CNN has listed the 20 most beautiful places in Europe. The first place on the list is Lake Inari in Finnish Lapland.

CNN writes that Lake Inari is a centre of Sami culture and a paradise all year round.

Because of the dark and snowy winter, Lake Inari is the perfect place to watch the northern lights. In autumn, in the autumn colours, the shores of Lake Inari glow orange. In summer you can swim in the cool water of the lake, CNN writes.

Besides Lake Inari, the list of Europe's most beautiful places includes, for example, the Lofoten islands in Norway and Lake Vättern in Sweden.
