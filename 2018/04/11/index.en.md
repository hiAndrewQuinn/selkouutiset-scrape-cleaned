# Wednesday 11.4.2018 (radio)

The government has finished its talks on the state economy. An app called Noodi has been made for young people to help them find a profession. A full-size model of the Tampere tram has been made. Young people are being encouraged to become birdwatchers.

## The government found agreement on the state economy

![The government's budget framework talks continued on Wednesday morning at Kesäranta in Helsinki on 11 April.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10154429)

The government's economic talks were held at Kesäranta in Helsinki. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

The government has negotiated on the state economy for the next few years on Tuesday and Wednesday.

On Wednesday afternoon Prime Minister Juha Sipilä said that the parties have reached agreement on all the big issues. The government will tell the public about them early in the evening.

The government parties have decided, for example, that employment offices will get more money. The activation model of unemployment benefits will be changed so that education is accepted as activity more widely than until now.

## An app helps with choosing a profession

![NOODI](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10151530)

Open image viewer

The Noodi app can be downloaded for free. Photo: Markku pitkänen / Yle

A mobile phone app has been made for young people to help them find a profession.

The app helps find exactly the kind of information about studying and working life that young people need. The app called Noodi tells, for example, how much an aircraft mechanic is paid, whether there are jobs available and where you can study for that profession. The app was made by the Me Foundation. It first asked young people what kind of information they need.

The Noodi app uses information from Statistics Finland and it can be downloaded to a phone for free.

## A wooden model of the Tampere tram

![A model made of plywood shows what the tram coming to Tampere will look like.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10154877)

Open image viewer

A model of the Tampere tram. Photo: Timo Nykyri / Yle

A full-size model of the Tampere tram has been made.

In Tampere, 15 residents were chosen by lottery to go to Oulu to see the future tram. The Tampere trams are being designed in Oulu. The model is built of plywood, but you can see from it what size and shape the tram will be. Residents were pleased that there is a lot of space in the tram. It is, for example, wider than the Helsinki trams.

Tram tracks are being built in Tampere right now, and the tram will start running in '21.

## Young people are encouraged to spot birds

![Olli Keskikallio holding a goldcrest that is going to be ringed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10152691)

Olli Keskikallio is holding a goldcrest. Photo: Paula Tiainen / Yle

Young people are also being encouraged to become birdwatchers.

Tringa, the association of birdwatchers in Helsinki, held a camp for young people in Hanko. 8 boys aged about 15 took part. The boys watched migrating birds with binoculars and watched when adults ringed them. The boys took photos and made videos for the Internet. They saw many water birds, chaffinches and wagtails, for example, which were migrating from the south to Finland for the summer. The boys counted and wrote down how many species they saw, or spotted.

14-year-old Olli Keskikallio said that a birdwatcher learns something new all the time. Nobody knows everything.
