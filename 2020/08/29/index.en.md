# Saturday 29.8.2020 (radio)

On Saturday final-year students were celebrated. New fighter jets cost a lot. Finland's Nature Day. The Venetsialaiset party ends the summer cottage season. On Sunday there will be showers of rain.

## Spring's final-year students were celebrated

![matriculation cap](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3822,h_2150,x_0,y_836/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6727655eb5404130e7a/1588942773)

The matriculation celebrations were moved from spring to the end of August. Photo: Jani Aarnio / Yle

Today, on Saturday, the final-year students of the spring have been celebrated in Finland. Matriculation celebrations could not be held in spring because of the corona situation. Many upper secondary schools moved their matriculation celebrations to the autumn.

There have been special arrangements at the matriculation celebrations. For example, at many schools the number of participants was limited so that people can keep safe distances.

In some places matriculation celebrations were not held at all, because corona infections have grown again.

## Fighter jets cost a lot

![Candidates for Finland's new HX fighter project.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10984492)

Open image viewer

Candidates for Finland's new fighter jet. Photo: AOP, image editing Juha Rissanen / Yle

New fighter jets may cost Finland more than the money Finland has set aside for the fighter jets. Finland has asked for offers for 64 fighter jets from 5 different companies.

There have been two rounds of offers. The price from all the companies in both rounds has been more than 10 billion euros, which Finland has set as the limit.

The third and final round of offers will be next year. At that point Finland will decide which fighter jets Finland buys, how many, and how much Finland pays for them.

## Finland's Nature Day

![Black chanterelle](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9832903)

Open image viewer

Black chanterelle is a good edible mushroom. Photo: Tommi Parkkinen / Yle

Today, on Saturday, Finland's Nature Day is celebrated. There are various events on Finland's Nature Day.

For example, the Sleep outdoors campaign encourages people to sleep outside for the night, for example in a tent. On Finland's Nature Day, hoisting the flag is also recommended.

Today is also the national mushroom day. The Martha Organisation encourages Finns to pick and eat mushrooms.

## The Venetsialaiset party ends the summer

![Light show of fire figures by the water in a light show of the Venetsialaiset.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10943055)

Open image viewer

Light shows of the Venetsialaiset by the water. Photo: Markku Saari / Yle

This weekend the party that marks the end of the summer cottage season and the boating season is celebrated. The party is called Venetsialaiset. Now it is the last weekend of August and the summer ends.

Many people light bonfires and lanterns on the shore. They look beautiful as the evening gets dark. In some places there are fireworks.

## The weather

On Sunday the weather is partly cloudy or cloudy. Mainly in the south and southeast of the country, and in the north, there will be showers of rain.

The temperature on Sunday is between 15 and 20 degrees, and in the far north between 10 and 15 degrees.
