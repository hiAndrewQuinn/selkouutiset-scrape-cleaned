## Explosion on the Kertš Strait bridge

![Partly collapsed bridge. A fire burning on the bridge in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1500,h_843,x_0,y_400/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-101837563413e4e479f5/1665220605)

Open image viewer

A large explosion took place on the Kertš Strait bridge on Saturday morning. Photo: Ukrainian security service

There has been a large explosion on the Kertš Strait bridge. The Kertš Strait bridge connects the Crimean Peninsula and mainland Russia.

The Kertš Strait bridge is an important supply route for Russia to Crimea, which Russia has occupied. The bridge also has a railway track. Because of the explosion, a large fire started on the bridge. Part of the bridge collapsed. Traffic on the bridge stopped.

Ukraine's army perhaps organised the explosion. Damage to the Kertš Strait bridge is a big loss for Russia. Russia built the bridge just over 4 years ago.

People in Ukraine are happy that the bridge was damaged. Ukraine has not said whether Ukraine organised the Kertš Strait explosion.

## Financial difficulties at the Turku and Rauma shipyards

![Sheds and an industrial hall at the Meyer shipyard area in Turku.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5175,h_2912,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9631796296fc1fb3bb6/1654062248)

Open image viewer

Turku shipyard. Photo: Jari Hakkarainen / Yle

The Turku and Rauma shipyards have financial difficulties. Helsingin Sanomat reports that the Turku and Rauma shipyards have asked the state for emergency funding.

The Turku shipyard is negotiating for up to 200 million euros of state funding. Last year the Turku shipyard made a loss of 17 million euros, so the shipyard has big financial difficulties.

The Rauma shipyard has already received emergency funding in the summer. Helsingin Sanomat reports that the state has given the Rauma shipyard a loan of 40 million euros.

## Mushroom harvest

![A big porcini mushroom in the forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5043285b7bbf4128b15/1534836692)

Open image viewer

Porcini is a delicious edible mushroom. Photo: Ilona Laurikainen

The autumn mushroom harvest is different in different parts of Finland. In Lapland the mushroom harvest has been really good.

In the Helsinki region and in South-Eastern Finland the mushroom harvest has been really bad. There are almost no mushrooms. The reason is that the late summer was hot and dry in the south and east.

Mushroom expert Pertti Salo says that it is bad for nature if there are no mushrooms. Conifer trees need mushrooms. It is not yet known how conifers are affected by there being no mushrooms in Southern Finland this autumn.
