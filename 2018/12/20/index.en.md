# Thursday 20.12.2018 (radio)

Finland wants to reduce emissions faster than has been agreed. The popularity of non-alcoholic drinks is growing. In Vuosaari, shared scooters will be tested next summer. Father Christmas and Father Frost exchanged gifts. A white Christmas may come to the whole country.

## Emission cuts

![Juha Sipilä spoke to the media after the climate debate of the party leaders of eight parliamentary parties in the State Room of Parliament in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10566110)

Finland wants the EU to reduce emissions faster than planned. Finland intends to push the matter when it becomes the EU presidency country next summer.

Eight parliamentary parties have considered climate policy together.

The parties want the EU to be carbon neutral before 2050. Carbon neutrality means that the EU produces only as many emissions as, for example, forests can absorb.

The parties also want to cut Finland's emissions. The goal is that by 2040 Finland removes more greenhouse gases from the atmosphere than it produces.

## Non-alcoholic drinks

![Cans and bottles of non-alcoholic beer in a shop's cold cabinet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10017747)

Open image viewer

Sales of non-alcoholic beer have grown quickly. Photo: Petri Aaltonen / Yle

The popularity of non-alcoholic drinks keeps growing.

Finland's biggest breweries say that sales of non-alcoholic beers have grown by 70 percent since last year. Sales of non-alcoholic drinks have grown by the same amount, for example, in S Group restaurants.

Because non-alcoholic beers, wines and drinks now interest Finns, the drinks selection has also grown quickly.

Non-alcoholic drinks are bought especially by the parents of families with children and by pensioners.

## Scooters

![A man and a scooter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10483294)

Open image viewer

In Vuosaari you can try a city scooter next summer. Photo: Lauri Eriksson / HSL

In Helsinki, shared scooters will be tested next summer.

The Helsinki Region Transport, HSL, will test city scooters in Vuosaari in East Helsinki. Vuosaari was chosen as the test site because there are not yet any city bikes there.

Next summer, people from Vuosaari will be able to rent an ordinary scooter for their use, which moves forward when you push it with one foot. You can also rent a scooter that runs on electricity. The maximum speed of an electric scooter is 25 kilometres per hour.

## Father Christmas and Father Frost

![Russian Father Frost and Finnish Father Christmas met at the Nuijamaa border station in Lappeenranta.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10566719)

Finland's Father Christmas and Russia's Father Frost have met each other at the eastern border.

Father Christmas and Father Frost met in Nuijamaa. From there they went together to the Christmas market in Lappeenranta.

Father Christmas and Father Frost also gave each other Christmas presents.

## White Christmas

![snow situation](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10564782)

Open image viewer

There is quite little snow across the whole country at the moment. Photo: Yle

Almost the whole country will have a white Christmas, says the Finnish Meteorological Institute.

In Lapland there may be up to 30 centimetres of snow at Christmas. Elsewhere in the country there may be up to 20 centimetres of snow.

There is the least snow on the west coast. In some places there may be no snow at all, the Finnish Meteorological Institute says.

It is also frosty across the whole country at Christmas, but it gets milder on St Stephen's Day.
