The war in Ukraine continues.

Evacuations also continue. Ukraine's president says that in recent days about 125,000 Ukrainians have been evacuated, that is, taken away from the war zone.

In Ukraine's capital Kyiv, preparations are being made for a siege. The city of Kyiv has, for example, stored food.

If Russia besieges Kyiv, that is, prevents travel into the city and out of it, life in Kyiv will become difficult.

Ukraine's neighbouring country Moldova needs help. Moldova is a small country, but already 270,000 Ukrainian refugees have come there.

Moldova's foreign minister says that the situation is really difficult. Moldova no longer has places for new refugees.

## The Winter War

![View from the edge of Mikkeli market square at the end of the Winter War, at the present pedestrian street.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4974,h_2778,x_0,y_2176/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-927296622c87fcd605e/1647085636)

Open image viewer

Mikkeli during the Winter War. Photo: SA-kuva

One more piece of history: Finland's Winter War ended exactly 82 years ago. Peace was made on 13 March 1940.

The Winter War began at the end of November 1939. Then the Soviet Union attacked Finland. The Soviet Union was a communist state, the biggest part of which was Russia.

The peace of the Winter War was harsh. Finland lost 8 percent of its land to the Soviet Union. 400,000 Finns lost their homes and fled elsewhere in Finland.

Many Finns remembered the Winter War when the war in Ukraine began.

## Pollen season

![Birch pollen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4539235a4ba7ea537cc/1617542947)

Open image viewer

Birch pollen. Photo: Risto Salovaara / Yle

The spring pollen season will begin soon. Pollen comes into the air from trees and shrubs when they flower in spring.

Many people are allergic to pollen. Common allergy symptoms include, for example, a runny nose and itchy eyes.

Alder flowering may already begin in Finland in March. Birch flowering may begin perhaps in April.

It is estimated that this year there will not be as much birch and alder pollen as a year ago.
