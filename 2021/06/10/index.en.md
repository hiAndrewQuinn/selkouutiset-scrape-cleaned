# Thursday 10.6.2021 (radio)

There are fewer coronavirus infections than before. The National Coalition Party is the most popular party. It is easier to get to vote by wheelchair than before. Left Alliance chairperson Li Andersson returns as Minister of Education at the end of June.

## Corona

In Finland there are fewer coronavirus infections than before. On Thursday there are 93 new infections. Almost everywhere in the country there are only a few new infections.

In Southern Finland there are more coronavirus infections than elsewhere. However, in the south too there are fewer infections than before.

The capital region has moved to the corona acceleration phase. This means that the coronavirus epidemic has calmed down. For example, restaurants will soon be allowed to stay open longer than at present.

Also in the whole world the number of new coronavirus infections is falling. The number of infections is falling the most in India. In India there used to be a very large number of coronavirus infections.

Party support measurement from May 2021. The National Coalition Party is the largest party, with the Finns Party and SDP behind.

The National Coalition Party is the most popular party in Finland. In Yle's poll, support for the National Coalition Party is 19.6 percent.

The next most popular party is the Finns Party. Support for the Finns Party is 18 percent.

In third place is Prime Minister Sanna Marin's party, the SDP. Support for the SDP is 17 percent. The support of all of the 3 largest parties has fallen a little.

The next are the Centre, the Greens and the Left Alliance. The support of these parties has risen a little.

The support figures of all parties are on the website yle.fi.

Municipal elections are on Sunday.

An authority has examined how well people in wheelchairs can vote. The examination says that it is easier to vote by wheelchair than before.

There are more accessible voting booths at the polling places than before. In many places people in wheelchairs can vote without help.

The Left Alliance is changing its minister at the end of June.

Left Alliance chairperson Li Andersson is returning to be Minister of Education. MP Hanna Sarkkinen will start as Minister of Social Affairs and Health.

Li Andersson had a child in January and she has been on maternity leave.
