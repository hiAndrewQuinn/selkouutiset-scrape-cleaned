## Minister spoke about corona vaccinations

![Krista Kiuru visited Yle's Ykkösaamu on Saturday 21 November 2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_523/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7447545fb8cfdbd3ddc/1605947822)

Minister Krista Kiuru. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Finland is ready to vaccinate all people over 12 against corona 3 times. Minister Krista Kiuru said this on Saturday.

Kiuru said that Finns will only be given a 3rd corona vaccination once experts recommend it.

In the view of the vaccine expert group, it is still enough, at least for now, for everyone to be vaccinated 2 times. Experts will assess during the autumn whether a 3rd corona vaccination is given to elderly people and other risk groups.

The Institute for Health and Welfare said on Friday that a 3rd corona vaccination can already be given to immunocompromised people. Immunocompromised people include, for example, many cancer patients. 2 corona vaccinations do not give them enough protection against corona.

## Vaccinations are progressing

![BioNTech-Pfizer's Comirnaty corona vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3843,h_2162,x_0,y_150/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-844874612241338c336/1629634921)

Corona vaccinations are progressing well in Finland. About 66 per cent of people over 12 have already received 2 corona vaccinations.

The government intends to end corona restrictions when 80 per cent of people over 12 have received 2 corona vaccinations.

Corona vaccinations give good protection against the corona disease.

## Demonstration against corona vaccinations

![Demonstration against corona measures](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5130,h_2886,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8555486145eb1139817/1631972160)

Open image viewer

Demonstrators on the steps of Parliament House on Saturday. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

There has been a demonstration against corona vaccinations in Helsinki.

More than 1,000 people took part in the demonstration. They demanded that corona vaccinations for children be stopped.

The demonstrators also opposed other corona measures, for example corona tests.

Demonstrators came to Helsinki by bus from different parts of Finland. For example, demonstrators came from Turku and Tampere.

The police say that the demonstration went calmly.

Similar demonstrations were held on Saturday in various parts of the world. Demonstrations were held in over 100 cities.

## Requests for help from Afghanistan

Finland has received about 8,000 requests for help from Afghanistan.

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto said this in an interview with Helsingin Sanomat.

Afghans have sent requests for help to Finland for various reasons. Some feel they are in danger. Some have lost their jobs.

Finland brought more than 400 people from Afghanistan to Finland when the Taliban took power in the country.
