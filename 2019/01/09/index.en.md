# Wednesday 9.1.2019 (radio)

Too few vegetables are eaten. Bullying leads to mental health problems. Municipal mergers have brought only a little benefit. Boaters were helped more than before. The EU is taking in a migrant ship. It will snow in the north on Thursday.

## Too few vegetables are eaten

![Oat porridge](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10588833)

Open image viewer

Porridge is healthy. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finns' diet needs to be improved, says a study. Too few vegetables, fruit and berries are eaten. Many Finns get few vitamins from their food. Many men eat too much red meat.

Hard fat, for example butter, is also eaten a lot. Experts say that vegetable fat is good, for example vegetable margarine or vegetable oil.

## Bullying can lead to mental health problems

![A young boy sits on the floor with his hands over his face.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10426567)

Open image viewer

Bullying must be noticed. Photo: Jurgita Vaicikeviciene / AOP

Bullying causes mental health problems for young people. Bullying causes, for example, anxiety and depression. A doctor must know if the cause of mental health problems is bullying. Otherwise the doctor cannot help properly. Bullying happens in schools, for example.

## Municipal mergers have brought little benefit

![Coats of arms of municipalities in the hallway of a municipal building in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9301080)

Open image viewer

Municipalities have their own coat of arms. Photo: Mauri Ratilainen / AOP

The benefit that was hoped for has not come from municipal mergers. Municipalities have been merged, but there has been only a little benefit from the mergers. Savings were hoped for from the municipal mergers. No savings have come, says a study from the University of Eastern Finland.

## The tasks of the Sea Rescue Society increased

![The rescue vessel Rautauoma of the Helsinki Sea Rescue Society returns to its home port in Helsinki's Jollas.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10270334)

Open image viewer

The rescue vessel Rautauoma of the Helsinki Sea Rescue Society in Jollas, Helsinki. Photo: Ari Mölsä / Yle

Boaters needed more help last year than the year before. The Sea Rescue Society helped about 2,000 boaters last year. 500 more boaters needed help than the year before.

The Sea Rescue Society helps a boater if there is a fault in the boat and it cannot be sailed. The Sea Rescue Society can tow the boat to the shore.

## Passengers of migrant ships get ashore

![Asylum seekers arrive in Italy by ship.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9614290)

Open image viewer

Asylum seekers arrive in Italy. (Archive photo.) Photo: Orietta Scardino / EPA

Eight EU countries will take in migrants from ships near the island of Malta. The migrants have been waiting in Maltese territorial waters, because the EU countries have not given the ship permission to come into port. Two ships have 49 migrants. The EU countries have agreed that eight countries will take in the migrants.

## Snow made traffic difficult

![A tractor doing snow work.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10586746)

Open image viewer

It will snow in the north and in the middle of the country on Thursday. Photo: Sirkka Haverinen / Yle

A lot of snow fell in Southern Finland last night. The weather conditions were poor. Flights were cancelled. On Thursday it will snow in Northern Finland, and in the evening it will snow in Central Finland. In Southern Finland it will be dry on Thursday. It is freezing across the whole country.
