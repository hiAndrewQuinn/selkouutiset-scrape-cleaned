# Tuesday 2.10.2018 (radio)

The trial of police leaders has begun. More than 1,200 victims of the earthquake and tsunami have been found in Indonesia. The death of a school boy after the Move test was caused by an illness. Wednesday is a strike day. Clear News's new Asioilla series examines whether offices provide service in clear language.

## Police leaders in court

![Former Police Commissioner Mikko Paatero (front right) and lawyer Jarkko Jaatela, retired Helsinki police commander Jukka Riikonen (back right), Helsinki police chief Lasse Aapio, who has been suspended from his post (back, second from right), and former head of the Helsinki drugs police Jari Aarnio (back, fourth from left) at the Helsinki District Court on 2 October.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10434183)

Police leaders in court. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

An unusual trial has begun in Helsinki. Five police leaders are accused in court. Some of them are no longer in office.

The prosecutor demands that the police leaders be punished for abuse of office. In the prosecutor's opinion, the police leaders did not supervise well enough how the police used information sources. Many of the police's information sources are criminals who give the police information about crimes.

The police leaders say that they have not committed abuse of office.

## Indonesia's victims

![Rescue workers search for victims in the rubble in the city of Palu on Sulawesi island on Tuesday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10434196)

Rescue workers are searching for people in the rubble of houses. Photo: Hotli Simanjuntak / EPA

More victims of the earthquake and tsunami have been found in Indonesia. More than 1,200 have already died.

The number of dead may still rise. Rescue workers have not yet been able to reach all the earthquake areas.

The earthquake was on Friday on the island of Sulawesi, which is part of Indonesia. The earthquake also caused a tsunami, whose waves were up to 6 metres high.

## A school boy's death

![Move test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9208375)

Open image viewer

The Move test measures children's fitness. Photo: Aatu Itkonen/Yle

The death of a school boy after the Move test has been explained.

The boy died after the test, because he had a rare illness. The boy had had the illness since birth. The boy's family did not know about the illness.

The boy was 13 years old. He had a seizure after the school's Move test. The test measures children's fitness. The boy died in hospital.

## Strike day

![Fried Saarioinen liver casseroles on the factory production line.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7969977)

Open image viewer

Some food factories will stop on Wednesday. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

Wednesday is a strike day. Many workers will not work on Wednesday, because they oppose the government's plan. The government intends to make it easier to dismiss employees in small firms.

The strike shows in many ways. For example, it stops factories. The strike also shows in shops, because some products may run out.

In addition, it is good to remember that not all bus, metro and tram services may be able to run.

## Asioilla series

![Young woman in an open-plan office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10403761)

Clear News's new Asioilla series examines whether offices provide service in clear language. Clear-language service is important so that all customers can deal with matters independently.

The Asioilla series tests clear-language service in many different offices. Parts of the series can be watched on the Clear News website and on Yle Areena. The series is presented by Nasib Naseri.
