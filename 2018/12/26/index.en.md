# Wednesday 26.12.2018 (radio)

Food aid was needed at Christmas too. A gift bought from an online shop can be returned within 2 weeks. Blood donors are needed. In Sweden, the Gävle straw goat is still intact. There was a white Christmas on Mars too.

## Food aid at Christmas too

![Bread queue in Myllypuro.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4756,h_2675,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5334715c20aab84fef9/1545644787)

The bread queue in Myllypuro. Photo: Mikko Ahmajärvi / Yle

Food aid has also been given in Helsinki at Christmas. Volunteers handed out free food parcels in Myllypuro in Helsinki on Christmas Eve.

People need food aid more than before. For example, in Myllypuro food aid has been handed out 5 days a week this year. Earlier it was handed out 3 days a week. This year more than 250,000 food parcels have been handed out in Myllypuro. That is 100,000 more than last year.

Bread queues are longer than ever in many other cities too.

## 2 weeks to exchange a gift

![Christmas gift wrapping paper in a bin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10571314)

Open image viewer

Sometimes a gift has to be exchanged or returned to the shop. Photo: Heikki Saukkomaa / Lehtikuva

After Christmas many people want to exchange goods they received as gifts. For example, a shirt may be the wrong size, or a book has already been read.

Goods can be returned to an online shop within 2 weeks. Other shops are not obliged to take goods back. However, most shops exchange goods if the receipt is kept.

Fewer goods are returned to online shops in Finland than in many other countries. About 10 percent of clothing purchases from Finnish online shops are returned, and fewer of other goods.

## Blood donors are needed

![Matti Matinmikko gives blood](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7600040)

Open image viewer

Matti Matinmikko gives blood in Rovaniemi. Photo: Jarmo Honkanen / Yle

Blood donors are needed between Christmas and New Year.

The Finnish Red Cross Blood Service reminds people that hospitals need blood on public holidays too. Blood stays good for only five days after it has been donated. In Finland blood is collected every working day from about 800 volunteer donors.

This year blood donation points have been open on St Stephen's Day too. Some of them are open again on New Year's Eve.

## Gävle straw goat still intact

![The Gävle straw goat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10542055)

Open image viewer

The straw goat on the market square in Gävle, Sweden. Photo: Albin Bogren / Visit Gävle

In the city of Gävle in Sweden a big straw goat still stands in the market square.

The straw animal statue is built in Gävle every year at the beginning of December. It is meant to stay in the square until the new year. However, someone often sets the straw statue on fire. In the 2000s the statue has burned 12 times. The straw goat last burned 2 years ago.

The Gävle straw goat has been built for 52 Christmases already.

## A white Christmas on Mars

![The picture shows a crater covered by ice, which is 82 kilometres in diameter.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10571334)

Open image viewer

Ice on Mars. Photo: ESA/DLR/FU Berlin

The camera of the European Space Agency, ESA, has sent a picture from the planet Mars showing a lot of ice. It looks as if Mars also has a white Christmas.

The camera of the ESA space probe took a picture of Mars of a glacier that is about 80 kilometres long and almost 2 kilometres thick.

ESA launched the probe into space in summer 2003, and its journey to near Mars took half a year.
