# Wednesday 1.3.2017 (radio)

Skier Iivo Niskanen is world champion. The new marriage law came into force. Cities may get a new rainwater fee. Donald Trump hoped for cooperation. Tuulikki Pietilä's art is on display in Helsinki.

## Iivo is world champion

![Iivo Niskanen celebrates 15-kilometre classic World Championship gold in Lahti.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9486228)

Iivo Niskanen. Photo: Lehtikuva

Iivo Niskanen is the world champion in skiing.

Niskanen won the 15-kilometre race at the Lahti World Championships. Niskanen was clearly the fastest. He skied 18 seconds faster than Norway's Martin Johnsrud Sundby, who came second.  Finland's other skiers were in places 7, 10 and 23 in the race.

Iivo Niskanen's mother Tarja Niskanen said that Iivo wanted to be world champion already as a little boy.

Iivo Niskanen's gold medal is Finland's third medal at the Lahti championships. Niskanen already has one bronze medal, or 3rd place, from Lahti.

## Female couples and male couples can get married

![A couple's hands wrapped around each other in their lap on a sofa](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9485673)

Open image viewer

A married couple can now be of the same sex. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

The new marriage law came into force today. It means that a same-sex couple can get married. Gay and lesbian married couples have the same rights as married couples of different sexes.

The authorities say that dozens of female couples and male couples will be married in Finland this week.

Same-sex couples cannot have a church wedding in Finland; instead they are married at the local register office.

## Cities are thinking about a rainwater fee

![Sewers must be in good condition in spring.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9485853)

Open image viewer

Photo: Kari kosonen/Yle

Many cities are planning a new rainwater fee.

When it rains in a city, the water flows away from yards and streets into the sewer. The city builds the sewers and keeps them in good condition with tax money.

Nowadays a city can also collect a new rainwater fee from residents. It is, for example, 20 or  100 euros a year per detached house.

## Trump proposed reconciliation

US President Donald Trump. Photo: Jim Lo Scalzo / EPA

In the USA, President Donald Trump gave a speech in which he proposed reconciliation and cooperation. In a poll by the TV channel CNN, almost 80 percent of viewers thought the speech was good.

In the speech Trump wanted Americans to solve problems together. He was ready to make compromises.

Many Democratic Party politicians also praised the speech. But some asked whether Trump was genuine, or whether he will speak differently the next day.

## Pietilä's art at the Ateneum

![Tuulikki Pietilä: Early Morning (Archipelago 17) (1976) and City VIII (1975).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9471411)

Tuulikki Pietilä's works Early Morning and City VIII. Photo: Ainur Nasretdin / Finnish National Gallery

The Ateneum museum in Helsinki has an exhibition of the artist Tuulikki Pietilä.

Pietilä was an important Finnish graphic artist. She studied a lot abroad and knew how to use all printmaking techniques.

Finns remember Pietilä best for living together with the artist Tove Jansson. The women's love story lasted many decades. Tove Jansson's Moomin books have Too-ticky, whose model was Tuulikki Pietilä.
