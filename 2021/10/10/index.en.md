## Electricity price

The electricity price has risen quite a lot this year. Photo: Ari Haimakainen / Yle

The electricity price has risen this year. The electricity price also varies more than before. Experts say that this winter the electricity price may have hard price spikes, that is, the price may rise a lot at times.

The electricity price in new electricity contracts has risen by about 45 per cent since the end of last year. The price of exchange electricity has doubled in a year.

The electricity price rises because the water reservoirs of hydropower plants have become smaller and fossil fuels have become more expensive. A lot of electricity is also sold to international electricity markets.

## Austrian chancellor's resignation

![Austrian Chancellor Sebastian Kurz.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1024,h_576,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8649616161e50300b38/1633805610)

Open image viewer

Sebastian Kurz.

The prime minister, that is the chancellor, of Austria has resigned. Chancellor **Sebastian Kurz** resigned because he is suspected of corruption.

Austrian authorities suspect that Kurz and 9 other politicians may have given state money to a newspaper so that the paper would write positive stories about them.

The matter has caused a big uproar in Austria, that is, it is talked about a lot there. Because of the uproar, Sebastian Kurz decided on Saturday that he would resign from the post of Austrian chancellor.

## Lotta Svärd 100-year anniversary mass

![The spouse of the President of the Republic, Jenni Haukio, gave a sermon at the Lotta Svärd 100-year celebration mass in Helsinki Cathedral.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_244/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-8650216162b11d68790/1633857879)

Open image viewer

Jenni Haukio gave a sermon at the anniversary mass. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

On Sunday an anniversary mass of the Lotta organisation was held at Helsinki Cathedral. It has been 100 years since the Lotta Svärd organisation was founded.

The Lotta organisation was a voluntary, unarmed national defence organisation of women and girls. It operated from 1921 to 1944. About 240 000 women and girls took part in its activities.

The President **Sauli Niinistö** and Niinistö's spouse Mrs **Jenni Haukio** also took part in the 100-year anniversary mass of the Lotta organisation at Helsinki Cathedral. Jenni Haukio gave the sermon, that is, the speech, at the mass.

## Aleksis Kivi Day

![Esko Salminen as Juhani in the play Seven Brothers at Turku City Theatre in 1975.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12053947)

Open image viewer

The story of Seven Brothers has also been performed in theatres. This picture is from Turku City Theatre from 1975. Photo: Antero Tenhunen / Yle

Today, Sunday, the day of Aleksis Kivi and Finnish literature has been celebrated. Aleksis Kivi was Finland's first professional writer. He wrote about 20 books.

The best known book by Aleksis Kivi is the novel Seven Brothers. It was published in 1870.

On Sunday there has also been flag flying, because Aleksis Kivi Day is a flag day.
