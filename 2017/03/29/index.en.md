## Supo: Finland is spied on more and more

![The front door of the Security Intelligence Service in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9536045)

Open image viewer

According to Supo, the terror threat in Finland is raised. Photo: AOP

Foreign states have increased spying in Finland.

The Finnish Security Intelligence Service Supo tells about threats to Finland's security in a report that was published on Wednesday.

According to the report, spying has become more aggressive than before in recent years.

Spies try to form relationships especially with young people who are close to politicians or other decision-makers.

Spying in computer networks especially has increased.

Supo also says that the threat of a terror attack is still raised in Finland.

## Turkey is building a wall on the Syrian border

![A Turkish soldier patrols the border wall between Turkey and Syria in Kilis.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9535928)

Open image viewer

A Turkish soldier patrols the border wall. Photo: Murad Sezer / AFP

Turkey is building a wall on the Syrian border because it does not want to let the extremist group Isis or Syrian Kurds into the country.

The wall is over 300 kilometres long, and it is already almost ready.

There are almost 3 million Syrian refugees in Turkey.

A human rights organisation says that Turkish forces have killed people who try to flee from Syria to Turkey.

## More and more people speak Arabic

![An underage asylum seeker.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8795776)

Open image viewer

More and more people in Finland speak Arabic, because many asylum seekers have come to the country from Syria and Iraq.  Photo: Yle

Arabic has become the third most common foreign language in Finland.

At the end of last year there were about 22,000 Arabic speakers.

Of foreign languages, Russian is spoken the most and Estonian the second most. Last year there were more than 350,000 speakers of foreign languages in Finland in total .

## Britain handed in its letter of withdrawal

![Britain's EU representative Tim Barrow handed his country's letter of withdrawal to the President of the European Council, Donald Tusk.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9537238)

Britain has handed in its official letter of withdrawal to the EU.

Last year Britain decided in a referendum that it will leave the EU.

Now Britain and the EU begin to negotiate how the exit happens and how much it costs Britain.

## Voting in the municipal elections began

![Prime Minister Juha Sipilä (Centre) has just voted in advance in the municipal elections at the Post House in Helsinki on 29 March 2017. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9536216)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Juha Sipilä voted in advance and also urged others to use their right to vote. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

Advance voting in the municipal elections began in Finland on Wednesday.

In the municipal elections, you can vote in advance until 4 April.

Every municipality has at least one polling place where everyone who wants to can vote in advance.

The actual election day is 9 April.

The residents of municipalities vote on which politicians are chosen for the municipal council. In the council, politicians decide on the important matters of the municipality.

## The birth rate is falling - the peak year in Finland was 1947

![Pensioners exercise in a swimming pool.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9503653)

Open image viewer

Seniors today are active people. Photo: Kaisa Siren / AOP

The most Finns were born 70 years ago, in 1947. Then more than 108,000 children were born in a year. They are called the baby boom generation.

70-year-old Finns are now much healthier and more educated than their own parents were at the age of 70.

On the other hand, last year a record low number of children were born in Finland.

In 2016 only about 52,500 babies were born in Finland, according to preliminary data from Statistics Finland. In 2015 too, fewer babies were born than before.
