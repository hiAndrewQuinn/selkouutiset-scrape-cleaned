School skiing holidays start on Monday. There are 3 weeks of skiing holidays. The skiing holiday season is started by schools in Southern Finland.

Many families have gone to ski centres, for example in Lapland. Ski centres in Southern Finland are also busy.

Experts say that it is good to spend the skiing holiday only with your own group. For example, it is not good to be in the restaurants of ski centres in the evenings, so that the coronavirus does not spread.

## Water pipes

![A workman melts water pipes frozen in the frost.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7058221)

Open image viewer

A workman is melting water pipes frozen in the frost. Photo: Mika Kanerva

Warming of the weather in Southern Finland can again break water pipes in houses. There has been a lot of frost and there may be ice in the water pipes. When the ice melts, it expands and can break the pipe.

For example, in Helsinki a water pipe broke in an old apartment block on Saturday. A lot of water flowed into the flats and the cellar of the building.

The rescue service says that pipes often break in old buildings when the weather warms.

## Saimaa ringed seal

![A Saimaa ringed seal pup peeks out from behind a snowy islet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2696,h_1517,x_0,y_282/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7718226022537863f02/1612862641)

Open image viewer

A Saimaa ringed seal pup peeks out from behind a snowy islet. Photo: Jouni Koskela

This winter is good for the nesting of the Saimaa ringed seal. A cold and snowy winter is good for the seal.

At the end of February or the beginning of March, the Saimaa ringed seal digs a nest in the snow. The Saimaa ringed seal gives birth to its pups in the nest.

Winters are not always good for seal nesting. Now the winter is really good for the seal, because there is a lot of snow.
