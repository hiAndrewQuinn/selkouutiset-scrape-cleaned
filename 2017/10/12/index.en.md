# Thursday 12.10.2017 (radio)

In Yle's poll, Sauli Niinistö is the clear favourite in the presidential race. The Foreign Ministry is inviting Turkey's ambassador to a discussion because of a Finnish journalist's sentence. The man suspected of the Turku stabbing spree has been ordered to a mental state examination. Finns remember The Unknown Soldier best from the books they read at school. The Finnish school in Barcelona sends greetings.

## Sauli Niinistö is clearly ahead in the presidential race

Yle's next presidential polls will be published in November and January.

President Sauli Niinistö is the clear favourite in the presidential election. This is what Yle's opinion poll says. 76 percent of respondents support Niinistö. In second place is Green candidate Pekka Haavisto, with 14 percent support. The other candidates had 1 - 3 percent support.

The first round of the presidential election is in January.

## Foreign Ministry invites Turkey's ambassador to a discussion

![Journalist Ayla Albayrak.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9878062)

Open image viewer

Ayla Albayrak Photo: Juha Heikanen / Yle

The Finnish Foreign Ministry is inviting the Turkish ambassador to a discussion. The reason is that a Finnish journalist has been sentenced to prison in Turkey. President Sauli Niinistö and Foreign Minister Timo Soini have agreed on the discussion. President Niinistö is also sending a letter to the Turkish president, Recep Tayyip Erdoğan.

Ayla Albayrak was sentenced in Turkey to about two (2) years in prison, because she worked in the Kurdish area of Turkey. Albayrak currently works in New York.

## Turku stabbing suspect ordered to a mental state examination

![candles at the Turku market square](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9879345)

Open image viewer

The Turku market square filled with flowers soon after the stabbing spree in August. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

The district court has ordered the Moroccan man suspected of the Turku stabbing spree to a mental state examination. The district court says the man asked for the examination himself.

Abderrahman Bouananea is suspected of two (2) murders committed for terrorist purposes. He is also charged with eight (8) attempted murders. The stabbings took place on 18 August.

The police preliminary investigation of what happened is still ongoing.

## Finns remember The Unknown Soldier from school

![Pentti Siimes (Corporal Määttä), Åke Lindman (Sergeant Lehto) and Kaarlo Halttunen (Private Oskari Rahikainen) in the film The Unknown Soldier.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8817906)

Open image viewer

Kaarlo Halttunen, Åke Lindman and Pentti Siimes in Edvin Laine's film The Unknown Soldier from 1955. Photo: Suomen Filmiteollisuus Oy, Edvin Laine.

Väinö Linna's The Unknown Soldier is the book that Finns remember best from school.

Yle News asked Finns to recall books that were read in Finnish lessons. Almost 270 replies came in.

For many respondents, the fates of the soldiers have stayed in their minds. Reading the book as a teenager could, however, feel shocking.

Väinö Linna's The Unknown Soldier was published in 1954. The book tells of a machine gun company's experiences in the Continuation War.

## Greetings from the Barcelona Finnish school

A large number of Finnish children and young people living in different parts of the world study the Finnish language and Finnish culture at Finnish schools.

The latest greetings come from Spain, from the Finnish school in Barcelona.
