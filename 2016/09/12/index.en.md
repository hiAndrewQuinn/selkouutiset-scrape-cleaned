# Monday 12.9.2016 (radio)

The police have arrested 2 Iraqi men in Kajaani who may have killed a man. Finns are worried that too few children are born in Finland. Hedgehogs are already preparing for winter. Students in Luksia's integration training are making a video blog.

## Crimes in Kajaani

![Police cars near the Otanmäki reception centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9161246)

Many police officers were seen in Otanmäki, Kajaani on Monday. Photo: Jonna Karjalainen / Yle

The police have arrested 2 Iraqi men in Kajaani. The police suspect that the men have committed 2 crimes.

It is suspected that the Iraqis killed a man in Kajaani and stole the man's property. In addition, they may have hit another man and stolen his phone.

The crimes took place in Otanmäki, Kajaani, where there is also a reception centre. The men are asylum seekers.

About 120 asylum seekers live in the Otanmäki reception centre. They do not accept what the Iraqi men did. The asylum seekers also want to take part in the grief of the dead man's relatives.

## Few babies

![Baby](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9098176)

Open image viewer

Finns think that too few children are born. Photo: Yle

Finns are worried that too few children are born in Finland. In a survey by Taloustutkimus, 70 percent of those who answered said that more children should be born.

Finns are older than before nowadays when they have their first child. That is why there are also fewer large families.

Finns think that working life must be flexible if more children are wanted. That means, for example, that the father or mother of a small child can work part-time. Then the family has more time.

## Hedgehog

![Sonja the hedgehog was found curled up in a ball on the lawn.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9152986)

Open image viewer

If a hedgehog comes to lie in a yard, it needs help. Photo: Antti Pylväs / Yle

Hedgehogs are already preparing for winter. A hedgehog is in hibernation all winter, which is like a deep sleep. A hedgehog must be healthy and fat when hibernation starts.

A hungry or sick hedgehog may need help from people. Seppo Pörhö from Oulu has looked after hedgehogs as a hobby for many years. He nurses a sick hedgehog back to health, so that the hedgehog can return to nature.

If a hedgehog is not fat enough before winter, it can get a place to hibernate in a little hut in Seppo's yard.

Seppo Pörhö has good advice for everyone. If a hedgehog lies in the middle of a yard, it needs help. Hedgehogs know how to come to people to get help, Seppo Pörhö says.

## Luksia's blog

![Students in Luksia's integration training.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9070922)

Open image viewer

Students in Luksia's integration training are making the video blog "A year in integration training". Photo: Luksia

At the Luksia adult education centre in Espoo, students in integration training also study digital skills. They take part in the Osallisena verkossa (Taking part online) project, which the European Social Fund supports.

The students, for example, make a video blog about their own integration training. The students have now finished the 1st part of the blog. You can watch it on Yle's Clear News website.

A new video blog is coming once a month.
