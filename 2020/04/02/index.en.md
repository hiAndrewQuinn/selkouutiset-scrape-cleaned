# Thursday 2.4.2020 (radio)

Ferry passengers are being sent to quarantine. Most people are coping well with the corona restrictions. In Spain, the coronavirus is making unemployment worse. A record number of people are applying to universities.

## Quarantine for ferry passengers

![The newest terminal at the Länsisatama harbour.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10396660)

The Länsisatama terminal (archive photo). Photo: Ulla Malminen / Yle

If a person comes to Finland by ferry, they now have to go into quarantine. The authorities are checking passengers at the ports more closely than before.

If a passenger has symptoms, a corona test is done on them. If there are no symptoms, the passenger goes into quarantine for 2 weeks.

Passengers may not use public transport. They must go into quarantine by taxi or in their own car. In quarantine, people usually stay at home.

About 200 passengers a day have now come to Finland by ferry.

The quarantine applies to all people who come to Finland from abroad.

## A survey on the corona crisis

![A passenger's feet in the empty aisle of a bus.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11286892)

Open image viewer

Many young adults say in the survey that they do not cope very well with the exceptional conditions of the corona crisis. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The Taloustutkimus research company has carried out a survey on what people think about the corona crisis.

More than 50 percent of people say that the corona crisis has changed their life only quite a little. Only 1 percent of respondents think that the corona crisis is really terrible. Women think this more often than men.

Many young adults say that they do not cope very well with the exceptional conditions. Young people find various restrictions harder than older people do.

## Unemployment in Spain

![A Red Cross worker prepares hospital beds in a temporary hospital in Barcelona.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11288381)

Open image viewer

A Red Cross worker prepared beds in a temporary hospital in Barcelona on Wednesday. Photo: Susanna Saez / EPA

In Spain, a large number of people have become unemployed. In March, over 300,000 people registered as unemployed. This is the largest number in a single month ever.

In Spain there are now 3.5 million unemployed people in total.

In Spain there has been a state of emergency because of the coronavirus since mid-March. More than 10,000 people have already died of the disease in Spain.

## Many applicants for universities

![The library of the University of Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11236665)

Open image viewer

About 32,500 applicants are aiming for a study place at the University of Helsinki. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

A record number of people are applying to universities and universities of applied sciences this spring. There were almost 152,000 applicants.

Many applicants will not get to study. There are 46,600 study places in total.

More than half of the new university students are now chosen for the first time based on their certificates. The other students are chosen based on an entrance exam. The entrance exams are meant to be held at the end of May or at the beginning of June.
