## Marin's resignation

![Medium close-up of Sanna Marin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3984,h_2241,x_0,y_83/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1095750642d2fb189b87/1680687066)

Open image viewer

The new chairman of the SDP will be elected in September. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

Prime Minister Sanna Marin is giving up the post of SDP chairman. Marin held a news conference on Wednesday morning. Marin said that she will stop her work as SDP chairman after the party congress in September.

Marin says that her 4-year term in government has been exceptional and hard. Marin's government term has included the corona crisis and Russia's attack on Ukraine.

Marin's party, the SDP, came 3rd in the parliamentary election. That is why also Marin's work as Prime Minister ends. The new chairman of the SDP will be elected at the party congress in September.

## Drunk driving by a former editor-in-chief of HS

![Kaius Niemi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2812,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1021742634d2cb3275c0/1666002544)

Open image viewer

Former editor-in-chief of Helsingin Sanomat, Kaius Niemi. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

A former editor-in-chief of Helsingin Sanomat has been sentenced for gross drunk driving.

Editor-in-chief Kaius Niemi drove a car drunk in a car park in November. That is why he resigned from the job of editor-in-chief.

Helsinki District Court sentenced Niemi to heavy fines. Niemi must pay 24,000 euros in fines.

## Payroll problems at wellbeing services counties

![Cash money.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_570/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1031128636a54c4c0283/1683080425)

There have been many errors in payroll. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

There have been problems with payroll at the wellbeing services counties during the early part of the year. The activities of the wellbeing services counties started at the beginning of the year. The staff of the wellbeing services counties then came from many different organisations.

For example, in the North Ostrobothnia wellbeing services county there have been thousands of payroll errors. For example, wages have been paid incorrectly.

The problems have been fixed by hand.

## Denial-of-service attacks

![Screenshot of the Ministry of Defence homepage under a denial-of-service attack.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1014,h_568,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-939872625006f074614/1649413005)

Open image viewer

A denial-of-service attack can stop a website from working completely. Photo: Screenshot www.defmin.fi

A denial-of-service attack has been made on Yle's online services. The cyber attack took place on Tuesday. Yle fended off the effects of the denial-of-service attack. That is why the attack was not visible to users of the online service.

On Tuesday there were also denial-of-service attacks in Finland on the online services of Parliament, the research company VTT and Helsinki Region Transport.

A denial-of-service attack means that someone disrupts a website so that the website does not work well.

In future passengers will be taken by bus from Tampere and Turku to Helsinki-Vantaa airport. Photo: Timo Valtteri Sihvonen / Yle
