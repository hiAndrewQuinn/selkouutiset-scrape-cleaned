# Saturday 2.1.2021 (radio)

So far 50,000 corona vaccines have arrived in Finland. All university students now get the same health care. The police will start to fight human trafficking with a new special unit. On New Year's Day about 14,500 emergency calls were made in Finland. The Finnish Orthodox Church has changed its administration.

## 50,000 corona vaccines have arrived in Finland

So far 50,000 corona vaccines have arrived in Finland. In most areas the vaccinations will get going on Monday.

On Saturday 201 new coronavirus infections have been reported in Finland.

During the last 2 weeks, about 3,750 infections have been recorded. That is 1,900 fewer than in the previous two weeks.

During the last 1 week, about 1,800 infections have been recorded. That is about 200 fewer than the week before.

## YTHS is now also at universities of applied sciences

![YTHS sign of the Student Health Care Foundation in Tampere](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10777618)

Open image viewer

University students and students of universities of applied sciences are now equal in student health care. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

All university students now get the same health care.

Now students at universities of applied sciences also get the services of YTHS, the Student Health Care Foundation. Before this, only university students got YTHS health care.

There are now 270,000 customers.

Fees also change. Every student must pay the university student health care fee separately.

Before, the fee was collected together with the student union membership fee. The fee must be paid by the end of January.

## The police special unit investigates human trafficking

![A staged victim of human trafficking, a sex worker, in a hotel room, Helsinki, 24.7.2020.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11479572)

Open image viewer

The picture is an illustrative picture, with an actor appearing in it. Photo: Jari Kovalainen / Yle

The police will start to fight human trafficking with the help of a new special unit. The unit has about 20 police officers.

The group aims to fight human trafficking, forced labour and sexual exploitation.

These crimes bring criminals tens of millions of euros in dirty money.

In Finland, the number of human trafficking victims is estimated to be as many as thousands.

## The emergency centres were busy on New Year's Day

![A fire engine drives on a road on an emergency run in the middle of a snowstorm.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5657,h_3182,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6346645e2ab07e4af84/1639584585)

On New Year's Day about 14,500 emergency calls were made in Finland. The calls were made between the evening of New Year's Eve and the end of New Year's Day.

95 percent of the calls were answered in less than half a minute.

A year earlier there were 148 fewer calls. Then 99 percent got an answer in less than half a minute.

About 7,300 rescue tasks were passed on to the authorities. That is almost 100 fewer than a year earlier.

## Orthodox parishes were merged

![Uspensky Cathedral](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10353728)

Open image viewer

Uspensky Cathedral in Helsinki. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

The Finnish Orthodox Church has changed its administration. The change is the biggest in a long time.

Many parishes have been merged at the start of the year. For example, the new Helsinki parish now also includes the parishes of Lahti and Hämeenlinna.

The new Helsinki parish works in an area of 46 municipalities.

The number of parishes fell from 21 to almost half of that. Finland now has 12 Orthodox parishes.

The total number of members of Orthodox parishes is about 59,000.
