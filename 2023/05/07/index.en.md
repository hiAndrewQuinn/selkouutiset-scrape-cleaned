# Radio Clear News Sunday 7.5.2023

Car sales. Norwegian salmon. Bird competition. Heat in Southeast Asia. The weather.

## Car sales

Car sales are quiet. Illustrative image. Photo: Antti Haanpää / Yle

People are now buying fewer cars than before. The car industry predicts that this year almost as few new cars will be bought as in the recession years of the 1990s. People have less money than before, because prices have risen.

A new car costs almost 40,000 euros on average. People now buy cheaper and older cars than before. The car industry's estimate says that the average price of a car sale is about 8000 euros.

## Norwegian salmon

![Rainbow trout fillets at the fish counter of a Prisma in Kuopio.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1919,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-105570363b55c092bea4/1672830141)

Open image viewer

Salmon is now expensive. Photo: Antti Karhunen / Yle

Norwegian salmon now costs a lot in Finnish shops. Salmon costs much more than in the autumn. The price of Norwegian salmon per kilo at the store's fish counter can be as much as 40 euros, when in the autumn a kilo of salmon cost less than 10 euros.

During the early part of the year people have bought less fish than before. Fish traders estimate that in the summer salmon will again cost less than now.

## Results of the Battle of the Towers

![Bird tower in an autumn forest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4028,h_2265,x_0,y_420/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10189536343eaabeddec/1665395475)

Open image viewer

Bird tower. Photo: Kirsi Lönnblad / Yle

On Saturday there was the Battle of the Towers bird competition. Over 3,000 people from different parts of Finland took part in the competition. More than 300 bird towers were involved.

The most different bird species were identified in Siipyy, in Kristinestad. There people saw a total of 108 different bird species.

During the competition, all participants saw a total of over 200 different bird species.

The rarest bird was the rusty blackbird. The bird was seen in Kokkola. The rusty blackbird has been seen in Finland only 10 times before.

## Heat in Southeast Asia

![A group of people walks on a sandy road, carrying sacks on their heads and in their hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2047,h_1149,x_0,y_274/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10978136436347181c07/1681274064)

Open image viewer

It is now very hot in Southeast Asia. Illustrative image. Photo: EPA-EFE

The weather in Southeast Asia is very hot.

In Vietnam a temperature of 44.1 degrees was measured on Saturday. This is a new record in Vietnam. Authorities have said that people should stay indoors. There have been few people on the streets of Hanoi, the capital of Vietnam.

In Thailand the temperature on Saturday was 44.6 degrees and in Myanmar 43.8 degrees. In India and Bangladesh too the temperature is much higher than usual for this time of year.
