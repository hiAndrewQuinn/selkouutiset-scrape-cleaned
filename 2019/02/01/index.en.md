# Friday 1.2.2019 (radio)

Voluntary returnees. Elderly care. The self-determination of the Sámi. The dangers of ice fishing. More snow is coming.

## Finland gives money to voluntary returnees

![An undocumented asylum seeker sits on a bed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9539344)

Open image viewer

Finland gives money to people who return to their home country voluntarily. Photo: Carolus Manninen / Yle

In Finland there are many thousands of people who do not have a residence permit. They have received a negative asylum decision. Finland wants them to return to their home country.

Finland has raised the amount of money a person gets if they return voluntarily. Asylum seekers whose home country is Iraq, Afghanistan or Somalia can receive the largest amount of support.

Finland hopes that a bigger sum will tempt people to return.

## Elderly care is being improved

![An elderly person drinks their coffee. A juice glass and a plate of sandwiches are also on the table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7378591)

Attempts are being made to improve the care of the elderly. Photo: Jyrki Lyytikkä / Yle

Politicians, representatives of care companies and representatives of carers held a meeting today where they talked about the care of the elderly. At the meeting, everyone agreed on the matters.

The companies will make sure that care homes really have enough carers.

In addition, many other things were agreed that will improve the care of the elderly.

## The UN criticises Finland

![Petra Biret Magga-Vars and Ulla Magga](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10561304)

Open image viewer

The Sámi Parliament decides on matters concerning the Sámi of Finland. Photo: Vesa Toppari / Yle

The UN Human Rights Committee criticises Finland.

The committee says that Finland has violated the rights of the Sámi. The violation happened when a Finnish court decided differently from the Sámi Parliament.

The Sámi Parliament had decided that it would not accept some people as candidates in the elections. The court decided to accept the candidates.

The Sámi have self-determination. The Sámi Parliament is the highest political body of the Sámi of Finland.

## An ice fisher must be careful

![Ilkka Sjöman ice fishing](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10619527)

Open image viewer

Ice fishing is fishing for fish through a hole in the ice in winter. Photo: Antti Eintola / Yle

In winter many Finns like to go ice fishing, that is, fishing on the ice.

A hole has to be made in the ice. Traditionally the hole is made with an auger that is turned on the ice. Now there are also augers that run on electricity.

Doctors warn that you must be careful with an electric auger. It can get caught and cause a hand injury.

## More snow is falling

![Eeva Korhonen keeps the yard clean with her own hands.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10626654)

Open image viewer

Eeva Korhonen shovels snow. Photo: Petri Vironen / Yle

There is really a lot of snow in Southern Finland. There has not been this much snow for many years.

At the weekend more snow will fall in Southern and Central Finland. A dense snowfall, that is a snowstorm, starts on Saturday evening.

Up to 20 centimetres more snow may come. There is also a strong wind.
