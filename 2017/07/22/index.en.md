# Saturday 22.7.2017 (radio)

Electricity can be used to make food. Finns have a lot of debt. Finland now has a national cat. Even tractors have beauty contests.

## Electricity can make food

![A small spoonful of light brown grit.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9735566)

Open image viewer

This is what the finished product looks like on a spoon in the VTT laboratory. Photo: Lappeenranta University of Technology

Finnish researchers have developed a method that produces food without farming. Food is made in a laboratory from only air and sunlight. Carbon dioxide and hydrogen, which is made from electricity, are enough to grow the food. The electricity is obtained from the sun.

The researchers have already made the first batch of single-cell protein. It can be made into food for people and feed for animals.

At the moment only one gram of product is produced in two weeks. In the future the method may be one solution to the world's hunger problem. The method was developed by Lappeenranta University of Technology and the Technical Research Centre of Finland, VTT.

## Finns have a lot of debt

![Mortgage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9639448)

Open image viewer

Many people do not calculate how much their loan costs rise when interest rates go up. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Finns have a lot of debt today. The Bank of Finland says that the amount of consumer credit grew by more than 5 percent in May. About 25 percent of Finns have some consumer credit. Consumer credit is often used to buy, for example, a car, a trip abroad, a boat or some household appliance.

Many Finns are more in debt than they themselves know. Difficulties will come for them when interest rates on loans rise again in the future. Interest rates have been very low for a long time, close to zero.

Also in old housing companies there is a lot of debt, which is due to repairs. Residents must take the repair debt into account when they plan their own finances.

## The European cat is Finland's national cat

![Five-year-old Mesi is a purebred European cat.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9734563)

Open image viewer

Five-year-old Mesi is a purebred European cat. Photo: Jouni Immonen / Yle

The Finnish Cat Association has declared that the European cat is Finland's national cat. The European cat was declared the national cat in Hämeenlinna, at the exhibition of the Finnish Pedigree Cat Association. The European cat is an original, traditional and vital Finnish cat breed. Registered European cats are related to ordinary Finnish cats.

The breeding of European cats is concentrated in the Nordic countries, especially Finland. In Finland about a hundred (100) new kittens are born each year. The European cat has managed to be protected from the influence of other breeds.

The choice is part of the programme for the Finland 100 anniversary year.

## Beauty contest held for tractors

![Old tractors from the tractor beauty contest in Tiistenjoki, Lapua.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9735227)

Open image viewer

At the tractor beauty contest you can see many old, fine machines. Photo: Matti Rinnankoski

In Lapua in South Ostrobothnia a beauty contest for tractors has been held. Exactly one hundred (100) tractors are on display in the contest, to honour the anniversary year of independence. The event was organised by the Tiistenjoki youth association in Lapua. It wants to bring activity to the small village.

New and old tractors are on display. The winner will be announced later.
