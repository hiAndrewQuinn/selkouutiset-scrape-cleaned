## The EU's climate package

![EU climate targets press conference in Brussels.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3898,h_2193,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83195060ef07bda191d/1626277905)

The EU Commission told about the climate package on Wednesday. Photo: Dursun Aydemir / AOP

Wednesday has been an important day for the European Union's climate policy.

The EU Commission published a large climate package. The climate package tells how the EU intends to prevent climate change.

The EU's goal is to reduce the use of coal and oil, because they heat the climate. For example, aircraft and ships must use more fuels that have not been made from oil.

The climate package also affects directly the lives of ordinary people. The aim is, for example, that new petrol cars will no longer be sold after 2035.

In industry the climate package tightens emissions trading. That means that pollution costs factories more than before. That is why it is worth it for them to get technology that pollutes less.

Next, the EU countries and the European Parliament will begin to negotiate about the climate package.

All corona certificates can now be found in the Omakanta service.

Now the EU corona test certificate is also in the Omakanta service. The address is kanta.fi.

If a Finn has had coronavirus disease, a certificate of it will also come to the Omakanta service.

Already in June the European Union coronavirus vaccination certificate was introduced in Finland. Information about coronavirus vaccination comes to the Omakanta service at the latest 5 days after the vaccination.

People need corona certificates when travelling abroad.

## Record-long heat spell

![A heat day was spent at Marjaniemi beach in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_234/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83168760eeaad40496c/1626688391)

A new weather record has been set in Kouvola.

Wednesday was the 27th consecutive hot day at the Anjala measuring station in Kouvola. At the same measuring station temperatures over 25 degrees have been measured every day for 27 days already. That is a new record.

Yle meteorologist Matti Huutonen says that the record may still improve. The heat continues until Sunday, and in Kouvola there may be more than 30 consecutive hot days.

Matti Huutonen also says that the hottest day of the week is Thursday. The temperature may be as high as 33 or 34 degrees.

Next week, however, the weather will change. The temperature drops to close to 20 degrees.
