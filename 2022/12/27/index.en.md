## Consumer confidence in the economy

![An electricity bill and cash.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4629,h_2591,x_0,y_96/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1031124636a54c294783/1671528338)

Open image viewer

Finns' confidence in the economy is getting worse. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

Consumer confidence in the economy has fallen very low. Statistics Finland says so. In December, consumer confidence fell lower than ever before during the measurement period. Measurements have been made since 1995.

Confidence in one's own finances has got worse in particular. 38 percent of Finns think their own finances are worse than a year ago. 84 percent of Finns think Finland's economy is worse than a year ago.

53 percent, that is, more than half of Finns, believe that Finland's economy will get even worse.

## Intestinal diseases

![Intestinal diseases and diet.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/17-1152954577da6a066c/1415019997)

Open image viewer

Not all intestinal diseases can be cured, but treatment can ease the symptoms.

Intestinal diseases are increasing in Finland. For example, in the 1980s about 3,000 Finns had colitis. Last year almost 57,000 Finns had colitis. Kela's statistics say so.

The reason for the increase in intestinal diseases may be that general hygiene has improved. Hygiene cannot be made worse, but more is known about intestinal diseases than before.

The symptoms of intestinal diseases can be eased with treatment.

## Calls to the emergency number at Christmas

![The general emergency number 112 taped to the side of a black rubber boat of the rescue service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4096,h_2304,x_5,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1004340631878e4c2173/1703666662)

People called the emergency number, for example, because their general condition got worse. Photo: Heli Kaski / Yle

Finns called the emergency number a little more at Christmas than last Christmas. A total of over 20,000 calls came to the emergency number on Christmas Eve and Christmas Days.

Over 1,000 more calls came to the emergency number than last Christmas.

The most common reasons for calling the emergency number at Christmas were a person's general condition getting worse, falls or breathing difficulties.
