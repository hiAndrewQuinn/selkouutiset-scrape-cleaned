## Regional elections

![A person examines regional election adverts in a snowy landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90246361e43844d2d21/1642346701)

On Sunday there is voting in the regional elections. Polling places open at 9 am and close at 8 pm.

In the elections, new regional councils are elected. The regional councils lead the wellbeing services counties. Finland will have 21 wellbeing services counties.

Each regional council decides on the social and health services of its own wellbeing services county, that is, for example, where the health centre is or how the elderly are cared for.

A wellbeing services county contains many municipalities. Finnish municipalities are often so small that they cannot organise good services alone.

The exception is Helsinki. It still provides the services itself. That is why there are no regional elections in Helsinki.

## Nurses' pay

![Nurses prepare to go into the isolation rooms of corona patients.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4989,h_2800,x_0,y_208/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-90016761dc25d5e039b/1649733752)

Nurses in Finland have a small salary.

The economic organisation OECD has compared nurses' pay in different countries. The comparison shows that nurses in Finland earn less than in many other countries.

A nurse's pay in Finland is lower than in the other Nordic countries. In Finland a nurse earns an average of 3,200 euros a month. In Denmark the pay is 4,300 euros, and in Iceland as much as 5,000 euros a month.

In France nurses earn about as much as in Finland. In Eastern Europe nurses' pay is lower.

## Women in the military

![Conscripts of the Armoured Brigade during a drinks break.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4624,h_2601,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-83665961027143de80e/1627550186)

Men have to do military service in Finland, but for women military service is voluntary. Photo: Timo Leponiemi / Yle

Some Finns support conscription for women. The information comes from a study made for Helsingin Sanomat.

About 30 per cent of Finns want women to do military service too. Today, military service is compulsory only for men.

About 40 per cent of Finns do not want military service to become compulsory for women.

Women can already join the military now, if they themselves want to. For example, last spring about 1,700 women joined the military voluntarily.

The weather warms on Sunday. The frost weakens, and the temperature rises a little above zero in a large part of the country.

The coming week also starts with mild weather. In the south there are about 3 degrees above zero on Monday, and in Northern Lapland about 3 degrees of frost.

The start of the week is windy, but little precipitation will come.
