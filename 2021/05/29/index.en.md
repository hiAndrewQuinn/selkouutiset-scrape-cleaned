## Belarus

![Sauli Niinistö visited Yle's Ykkösaamu show in Pasila on 29 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5000,h_2813,x_0,y_180/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81296960b1efa7dcc4f/1623911384)

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Silja Viitala / Yle

President **Sauli Niinistö** criticises the hijacking of an aircraft by Belarus. Last Sunday Belarus forced an aircraft of a European airline to land in Belarus.

In an interview with Yle, President Niinistö said that Belarus's actions are a big exception to normal practice. Passenger air traffic has previously been able to operate in peace.

Niinistö says that Belarus's actions weaken international trust in the country.

## University researchers

![University of Eastern Finland](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6240,h_3510,x_0,y_341/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6451755e4e943a84392/1582208184)

Open image viewer

Sign of the University of Eastern Finland. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

Universities are worried about the employment of young researchers. In the framework budget session, the government decided that research funding granted by the Academy of Finland will be cut by 35 million euros in 2 years' time.

University rectors say that the smaller funding reduces the work of young researchers.

At the same time a gap is forming in the generation of researchers, because only some young researchers receive funding.

## Victims of the civil war

![Two rows of small wooden coffins](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-81300960b22e5bbd214/1622290126)

Open image viewer

50 coffins at Heinola cemetery. Photo: Laura Rautavuori/Yle

Victims of the civil war have been reburied in Päijät-Häme. At Vierumäki, 50 people who fought on the Reds' side in the Finnish civil war had been buried in a mass grave.

The grave was in an industrial area. The remains were moved, and they were buried at Heinola cemetery on Saturday.

The intention was that the remains would also be identified. However, identification was cancelled, because it was too difficult and expensive.

## Golden Venla awards

![The band Biolapset performing at the Presidential Palace Atrium at the Independence Day celebration.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11684482)

Open image viewer

The band Biolapset performed in the Linnan Juhlat programme. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Office of the President of the Republic

Yle's programmes have won many awards in the Golden Venla competition for television programmes.

The best sports and events programme was Yle's Linnan juhlat. The best entertainment programme was Sohvaperunat. The best news and current affairs programme was the documentary series Logged in, which followed the lives of unemployed young men. The best children's and young people's programme was Moominvalley. The performer of the year was Aki Huhtanen from Eränkävijät ("Backwoods Wanderers").

In total, 13 Golden Venla awards were given to Yle's programmes.
