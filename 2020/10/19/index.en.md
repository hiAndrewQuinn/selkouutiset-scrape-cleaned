# Monday 19.10.2020 (radio)

Family poverty worries children. Coronavirus infections in Finland. Explosives disappear from building sites. Winter tyres are being changed now.

## Children of low-income families feel anxious

![A young person climbing stairs.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2805,h_1578,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7166125f5788f3373be/1684758344)

Many children of low-income families are worried about their family's situation. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Children from low-income families feel more anxiety than other children. They also feel that they are worse off than other children.

As many as 80 percent of children from low-income families say that their family's financial situation worries them. Children from low-income families also have worse opportunities for hobbies than other children.

The coronavirus situation has added financial problems in many families. The child welfare organisation Save the Children says that the coronavirus situation may increase inequality in Finland.

## The coronavirus situation in Finland

![Covid-19 virus, 3D model.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_10000,h_5625,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7301565f871b0fea9c4/1603029246)

On Monday 131 new coronavirus infections were reported in Finland. Infections have decreased since last week. No new coronavirus deaths are known of.

In Vaasa the coronavirus has spread to residents and staff at a care home. There are 10 residents in the care home. Eight of them have a coronavirus infection.

Coronavirus infections have also increased in the army, i.e. the Defence Forces. Most of those who have fallen ill are conscripts.

## Explosives disappear from building sites

![Blasting site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2989,h_1681,x_0,y_177/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7308585f89460613d5c/1602831951)

On building sites, explosives are used to blast rock.  Photo: Lauri Miettinen / Yle

Explosives, such as dynamite, are stolen from building sites in Finland. Last year the police found over 300 kilos of explosives that criminals had stolen from building sites.

Perhaps many more explosives are stolen from building sites. Construction companies may not report all explosive thefts to the police, because they perhaps do not monitor the storage of explosives enough. That is why they may not want to report to the police if explosives go missing.

The law does not require building site explosive thefts to be reported.

## The winter tyre season begins

![A pair of new studded tyres.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4937,h_2777,x_0,y_486/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5215385be1af53ac8e7/1541517240)

Open image viewer

These winter tyres are studded tyres. Winter tyres can also be friction tyres, i.e. tyres without studs. Photo: Petteri Sopanen / Yle

The first snowfalls of winter are overloading tyre companies. Winter tyres are now being fitted to cars.

The law on winter tyres has changed. The winter tyre season is from November to March. The new law says that a driver must use winter tyres if the road is slippery.

Summer tyres may be used if the conditions are really good. For example, in autumn or spring the conditions may be good. It is good to keep winter tyres on the car for the whole winter.
