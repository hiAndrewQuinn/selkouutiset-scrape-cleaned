Finland is the ice hockey world champion. The members of the European Parliament have been elected.

First, some happy sports news:

Finland is the ice hockey world champion. Finland beat Canada in the final match.

Finland scored 3 goals in the game, but Canada only one. Finland's best player was Marko Anttila. He scored 2 goals for Finland.

Marko Anttila, or "Mörkö", is now a hero of the Finns.

## EU elections in Finland

![Henna Virkkunen, Petri Sarvamaa and Sirpa Pietikäinen celebrating at the National Coalition's election night party in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10802949)

The European Parliament elections were also held on Sunday. Citizens of the EU countries have elected new representatives to the European Parliament.

Finns elected 13 representatives to the European Parliament.

The National Coalition got the most votes in Finland. The National Coalition will get 3 representatives in the European Parliament. The National Coalition is a party that supports cooperation between EU countries.

The Greens' support in Finland grew, and the party got the second most votes. The Greens will get 2 representatives in the European Parliament. In the election campaign the Greens talked a lot about climate change.

Five other Finnish parties will also get representatives in the European Parliament. The SDP, the Finns Party and the Centre will get 2 representatives each. The Left Alliance and the RKP will get 1 representative each.

## EU elections elsewhere in the EU

![German Greens' lead candidate Sven Giegold celebrates at the election night party in Berlin after the first exit polls were published.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10802758)

The European Parliament elections were held in all the countries of the European Union. There are 28 EU countries.

In many EU countries there are parties that do not want a strong union. These populist parties say that the EU has, for example, let too many asylum seekers into Europe. For example, in France the populist party of Marine Le Pen won the election.

In the biggest EU country, Germany, the election result was different from France. In Germany the Green party won the election. Environmental issues are important to it.

The election results from all EU countries will be known on Monday.

## European Parliament

![Members of the European Parliament voted in plenary on 6 February.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10063488)

The European Parliament has 751 representatives in total.

The population of an EU country decides how many representatives the country gets in the European Parliament. The largest EU country, Germany, gets 96 representatives in the Parliament. The smallest countries, for example Estonia, get 6 representatives.

The European Parliament has important tasks. It makes EU laws and decides on EU funds.
