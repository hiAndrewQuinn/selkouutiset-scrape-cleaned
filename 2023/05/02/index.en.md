# Radio Clear News Tuesday 2.5.2023

Government negotiations. Suspected crime in construction. Manta repair. The weather.

## Government negotiations

Government negotiations have started. In the government negotiations, they are trying to put together a new government.

The leader of the government negotiations is the chairman of the National Coalition, Petteri Orpo, because the National Coalition is now the largest party in Parliament. The National Coalition won the Parliamentary elections a month ago.

The National Coalition is negotiating a new government with 3 other parties. They are the Finns Party, RKP and the Christian Democrats.

These parties must agree on many things before a new government can be put together. The parties disagree on immigration, for example.

The parties must also agree on which ministerial posts they get in the new government.

If the negotiations succeed, Petteri Orpo will become Finland's new prime minister. Orpo hopes that the new government will be ready in June.

## Suspected crime in construction

![A sign reading: Construction site. Entry forbidden to unauthorised persons.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_125/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-1097834643649c74648c/1681279708)

Problems have been found at construction sites. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

The police have told about serious problems in the construction industry. The police suspect that foreign workers in the construction industry have been treated badly.

The police say that perhaps several dozen workers were lured to Finland from Estonia and Latvia. The police suspect that the workers did not always get paid in Finland. In addition, the workers may have been treated violently.

Finnish and Estonian police have investigated the matter together. The authorities demand punishment for 11 people.

## Manta repair

![Hundreds of revellers around Havis Amanda.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3936,h_2214,x_0,y_328/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9178486211f48d6210d/1645348478)

The famous Havis Amanda statue, also called Manta, is being repaired. Manta is in Helsinki's Market Square.

Still at the weekend, students put a student cap on Manta when Finland celebrated May Day. Tomorrow, Wednesday, the statue will start to be moved so that it can be repaired.

Manta is already 115 years old. On May Day and in other important situations, people climb on Manta. People come to Manta when Finland wins a world championship or the Eurovision, for example.

The area around Manta is also being repaired. The sea lion statues that are around Manta are also going for repair.

Manta will perhaps come back to Helsinki's Market Square in a year.

On Wednesday the cloudiness varies. Mostly it is dry.

The temperature in the north of the country is between -2 and +5 degrees. In the south and central parts of the country the temperature is between +5 and +11 degrees.

On Thursday cold air comes to Finland. The temperature is below 5 degrees in most of the country.

In the south and central parts of the country there will be snow showers on Thursday.
