# Friday 18.11.2016 (radio)

Finnish football player Roman Eremenko has been given a competition ban. The number of carers will not be cut in elderly care. A new, dangerous drug has appeared in Finland. New words keep being added to the Finnish language.

## Eremenko's playing ban

![Roman Eremenko in the Finnish national team shirt.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9301300)

Finnish football player Roman Eremenko has been given a competition ban. The ban lasts 2 years, so Roman Eremenko may only compete on football pitches again in autumn 2018.

Eremenko received the competition ban because he has used cocaine. It is a drug, and its use is banned.

Roman Eremenko is a very famous player. He is an important player in the Finnish national team.

Eremenko has played professional football in Finland, Italy, Ukraine and Russia. Now Eremenko's team is the Moscow club CSKA.

Eremenko's father Alexei Eremenko also played football. He moved from Russia to Finland in 1990. Roman was 3 years old at that time.

Roman Eremenko's brothers, Alexei junior and Sergei, also play football. The Eremenkos are Finland's most famous football family.

## Carers for the elderly

![An elderly person being fed at a service centre](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7591090)

Open image viewer

The number of carers for the elderly is not being saved on. Photo: Raila Paavola / Yle

The number of carers will not be cut in elderly care.

The Finnish government planned to cut the number of carers to save money. Now the government has withdrawn the plan. That means there must still be 1 carer for every 2 elderly people.

The government's plan to cut the number of carers received a lot of criticism. Now the government is looking for other ways to save on care.

## A new drug

![White powder in a small freezer bag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9301957)

Open image viewer

Drugs are dangerous for users. Photo: AOP

The authorities have found a new kind of drug in Finland. The drug is very dangerous. Five people have died because of it in half a year.

Professor Ilkka Ojanperä says that the new drug is a stronger substance than morphine. The name of the substance is U 47 700. Drug users have been ordering it from the internet.

The new drug can, for example, stop breathing. Then the user dies, if he or she does not get help straight away.

## New words

![Players of Pokémon Go.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9238934)

Open image viewer

Here we are poketing. Photo: Ghadi Boustani / Yle

New words keep being added to the Finnish language.

Minna Haapanen from the Centre for Domestic Languages told Yle's morning TV about this year's new words.

According to Haapanen, "Brexit" is an interesting word, because it has come into many languages. It means Britain's exit from the European Union.

New words are sometimes easy. At least players of the Pokémon game know what "pokettaminen" is, that is, playing Pokémon Go.

It is harder to know what "sipsikaljavegaanit" eat. They are not interested, for example, in whether "nyhtökaura" (a pulled-oat meat substitute) is healthy food or not. The main thing is that it is tasty and that no animals have been killed for it.
