Finland's immigration policy may tighten. That is how National Coalition chairman Petteri Orpo assesses it.

## Possible strikes in the health and social sector

![Two children play in a sandpit at a day-care centre on a sunny spring day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6007,h_3380,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-110800664525c4343bcf/1686574706)

A strike may close day-care centres next week. Illustrative image. Photo: Elina Ervasti / Yle

A strike may begin in the social and health sector next week. The strike starts next week on Tuesday, if no solution is found to the pay dispute before then.

The strike affects some private day-care centres and care homes for the elderly. The strike does not concern the whole social and health sector.

Workers intend to go on strike, that is, stop work, at about 40 day-care centres and about 30 care homes. The strike lasts a few days.

The reason for the strike is a pay dispute. Workers in the private social and health sector demand that they get the same pay as workers on the public side.

For example, the pay of a practical nurse is about 200 euros less in private companies than on the public side. The money on the public side comes from taxes.

## Yle Language School

![Translator and journalist Jonatan Pedersen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1893,h_1064,x_6,y_5/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-111460264635a8e3a2da/1684233023)

Open image viewer

Jonatan Pedersen says that users like Yle Language School.

In Yle Language School you learn Finnish by watching Finnish-language TV programmes.

The programmes also have Finnish subtitles. The viewer gets a translation for difficult words.

The viewer can choose for themselves which language the translations are in. There are 14 languages, for example English, Arabic and Ukrainian.

Yle Language School offers translations based on the viewer's level of Finnish.

The tasks also suit the level that the viewer has chosen. The tasks relate to the programme's text.

Translator Jonatan Pedersen coordinates the Language School. He says that Yle Language School is free for users.

Pedersen says that users have given positive feedback about the Language School. In users' view, it is nice to use the Language School.

You can use the Language School with an app or in a browser. More information about the Language School can be found at yle.fi/kielikoulu.
