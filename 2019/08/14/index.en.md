# Wednesday 14.8.2019 (radio)

The Ministry of Finance's budget proposal is ready. Child patients have been operated on with the help of robots in Helsinki. Thousands of court hearings are cancelled every year in Finland. Panama disease threatens the world's bananas.

## Budget proposal

![Minister of Finance Mika Lintilä (centre right) at the information event on the 2020 budget proposal negotiations in Espoo on 13 August.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10922467)

Minister Mika Lintilä told journalists about the budget proposal. Photo: Martti Kainulainen

The Ministry of Finance's budget proposal is ready. The budget shows how the state collects and uses money.

In the budget proposal, the state's expenditure next year is 57 billion euros.

More money is promised for universities, for example. Small pensions are also going to be raised.

Income taxes will not change much, but harm taxes will be raised. That means that, for example, petrol and tobacco will become more expensive.

The government will deal with the budget proposal in September. After that Parliament decides on the budget.

## Robot operations

![New children's hospital](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10403485)

Open image viewer

After the operation, the children were cared for in the New Children's Hospital in Helsinki. Photo: Kimmo Hiltunen / Yle

In Helsinki, new kinds of operations have been done on child patients. Surgeons operated on 5 children with the help of robots.

The children were operated on at Meilahti hospital. After that they were cared for at the New Children's Hospital in Helsinki.

Doctor Niklas Pakkasjärvi says that robotic surgery is very useful. The surgical wounds are smaller and there is less pain after the operation. The children also heal faster than after an ordinary operation, Pakkasjärvi says.

## Court cases

![Courtroom](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6062504)

Open image viewer

A court hearing is cancelled if the parties do not come to the place. Photo: Antti Eintola / Yle

Thousands of court hearings are cancelled in Finland every year.

Often the reason is that people cannot be bothered to come. A court hearing cannot go ahead if, for example, a witness is missing.

When a court hearing is cancelled, money is spent for nothing. The suffering of the crime victim also continues when the handling of the case is delayed.

At the start of this year, however, a new rule came into force: a court may deal with some criminal cases even if the accused, that is, the possible perpetrator of the crime, is not present.

## Bananas

![Bananas](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10919962)

Open image viewer

Cavendish bananas grow on banana plantations. Photo: AOP

The world's bananas are in danger. The reason is Panama disease. It is a plant disease that destroys banana plantations.

Until now Panama disease has not infected the Cavendish variety, which is the most common on banana plantations. Now, however, Panama disease has changed, and it infects Cavendish bananas too.

It is worrying that Panama disease has spread as far as Latin America. A large part of the world's bananas grow there.

Researchers are now thinking about how the banana can be saved. The banana is the world's most popular fruit.
