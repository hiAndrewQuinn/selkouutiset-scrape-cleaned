# Thursday 28.1.2021 (radio)

Cities in the Helsinki area open hobby spaces for children and young people. Corona infections are growing. The government wants to slow down the rise in electricity transmission prices. Actress Tiina Rinne has died.

## More corona infections are coming

![A sign of the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, THL, in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11760661)

The number of corona infections is still growing in Finland.

In the last 2 weeks about 1,000 more corona infections have been reported than in the previous 2 weeks.

On Thursday over 400 new corona infections were reported. In addition, it was reported that there are 4 new corona deaths. A total of 664 people have died of corona in Finland.

## Children's hobby spaces open

![Young people playing basketball in a sports hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11662848)

Open image viewer

Children and young people can again take part in hobbies in the Helsinki area. Photo: Janne Lindroos / Yle

The cities in the Helsinki area will open hobby spaces for children and young people next week. Helsinki, Espoo and Vantaa told about this on Wednesday.

The cities' decision means that under 20 year olds can again do hobbies indoors. There has been a break of more than 2 months in hobbies.

However, the corona guidelines must still be remembered in hobbies, for example washing hands and keeping safe distances.

## The rise in electricity transmission prices will be slowed

![Men installing an underground electricity cable.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10909730)

Open image viewer

Electricity cables are being dug into the ground. Photo: Markku Rantala / Yle

The government wants to slow down the rise in electricity transmission prices. The government proposes that electricity transmission prices may not rise by more than 8 percent a year.

Electricity transmission means carrying electrical energy to customers along electricity networks. Transmission companies charge customers for the transmission, for example households. Many customers think the charges are too high.

In addition, the government is giving the transmission companies more time to make sure electricity gets through, for example by digging electricity cables into the ground. The transmission companies must finish the work by the end of 2036. The work is expensive, but electricity cables work more reliably than power lines that are in the air.

## Tiina Rinne has died

![Actress Tiina Rinne](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11756755)

Open image viewer

Actress Tiina Rinne. Photo: Anne Hämäläinen / Yle

Actress Tiina Rinne has died. Rinne was 91 years old.

Tiina Rinne had a long career as an actress. She started working as an actress in 1950. Rinne acted in the theatre and on television.

Many Finns remember Tiina Rinne from the TV series Kotikatu (Home Street). Rinne played Maija Mäkimaa in the series. Rinne was part of Kotikatu for as long as the series was made, 17 years in total.

Tiina Rinne came from a theatre family. Both of Rinne's parents were actors. Many of Rinne's relatives also chose acting as a profession.

## More snowfalls come to Finland

On Friday snowfalls will spread to Finland from the east. At the weekend it will snow across the whole country.

The temperature is below freezing across the whole country. In the north the frost is 10 to 20 degrees. In the central and southern parts of the country the frost is less.
