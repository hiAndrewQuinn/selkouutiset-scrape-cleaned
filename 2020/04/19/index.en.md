## Finland needs money

![Coins on top of the Finnish flag.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9772443)

Open image viewer

The state always has debt. The state never pays all of its debt off. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The Finnish state has to borrow much more money this year.

The Finnish state has to spend a lot of money because of the corona epidemic.

For example, the state supports companies that are now doing badly. A lot of money also goes to treating sick people.

The Finnish state always has debt. The state never pays off the whole debt.

In recent years, however, the situation has been fairly good, and the Finnish state has not needed to borrow very much more money.

Now the state suddenly needs more money.

## Sometimes a priest behaves badly

![Harri Kekäläinen, priest.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3648,h_2052,x_0,y_798/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6618135e8ae1d31153c/1586160494)

Open image viewer

A priest can sometimes behave badly. Photo: Ronnie Holmberg / Yle

The Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland sometimes has to step in on the behaviour of priests.

Yle investigated what the church has criticised priests for.

Many cases are ones where a priest or parish priest has behaved inappropriately at work.

For example, a priest has used rude or strange words at a funeral.

In addition, there have been situations where it has been difficult for a priest to work with other parish employees.

The Diocesan Chapter criticises a priest if he behaves badly in his work.

The Diocesan Chapter can also criticise or pass some kind of sentence if a priest has committed a crime and been sentenced in court.

## Migratory birds are awaited

![Barn swallow.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9698865)

Open image viewer

The barn swallow has a forked tail. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Migratory birds are already eagerly awaited in Finland.

This week the weather was cold and wintry. A cold wind came from the north. The migratory birds stayed waiting for better weather.

Next week the weather will warm up in Finland.

Bird experts believe that the migratory birds are now flying to Finland.

In Southern Finland, white wagtails and a few barn swallows have already been seen. They are already real signs that summer is coming.
