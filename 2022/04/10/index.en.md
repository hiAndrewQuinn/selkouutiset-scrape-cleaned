## Marin comments on labour disputes

![Nurses' demonstration and procession in Helsinki on 9 April. People in the procession marching.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_348/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-9402266251600e8cb5d/1649500290)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin hopes that the municipal sector labour disputes will be resolved.

Because of the labour disputes there are even strikes in Finland. During a strike, employees do not work.

The biggest strike at the moment is the nurses' strike. About 25,000 nurses are on strike in different parts of Finland. They demand more pay.

Marin does not take a position on how much nurses' pay should be raised. In a radio interview, the Prime Minister reminded that resolving labour disputes is not the government's job.

## Support for Ukrainian refugees

Ukrainian refugees are receiving a large international aid package. 10 billion euros has been collected for the refugees in an international campaign.

Leaders of various countries and many famous performers have taken part in the campaign, for example Elton John and Annie Lennox.

The organisers of the campaign were the European Union and Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau.

## French presidential election

![There are many people queuing at a polling station and returning ballot papers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6720,h_3780,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-94036762529dc24703b/1649581709)

Open image viewer

French people voting. Photo: Sebastian Nogier / EPA

Voting has been held in France in the presidential election. Today, Sunday, was the first round of the presidential election.

There are 12 candidates in the French presidential election. If none of them gets more than 50 percent of the votes in the first round, a second round of the election will be held 2 weeks later.

In France it is predicted that a second round will also be needed in the election. Experts believe that the current French president Emmanuel Macron and the far-right candidate Marine Le Pen will reach the second round.

There has been a fire in Juuka in eastern Finland.

The fire started in a flat in a care home. An elderly resident of the flat died in the fire.

Smoke spread from the fire to neighbouring flats. 2 residents were moved to safety.

The police are investigating why the fire started.

## Palm Sunday willow switchers

![Helsinki children switching for Palm Sunday on 10.4. 2022.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_535/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-940353625291f0b7b4d/1671547537)

Open image viewer

Switchers in Helsinki. Photo: Sasha Silvala / Yle

Today, Sunday, Palm Sunday has been celebrated.

The Finnish Palm Sunday custom is switching (virpominen). The switchers are children. They are often dressed as Easter witches.

The switchers decorate willow twigs, with which they switch relatives and neighbours. When a switcher has touched a person with a willow twig and wished them good health, the person gets, for example, a chocolate egg.

Palm Sunday is celebrated one week before Easter.
