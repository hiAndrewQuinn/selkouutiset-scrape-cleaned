# Tuesday 22.12.2020 (radio)

The Finnish government has issued a decree on coronavirus vaccinations, and vaccination can begin in Finland. Helsinki's former head of narcotics police, Jari Aarnio, has been sentenced to life imprisonment for murder. Unemployment grew clearly last month. The coronavirus has reached Antarctica.

## Vaccinations will start soon

The government has issued a decree on coronavirus vaccinations. That is why voluntary vaccination can start in Finland.

The European Commission gave yesterday the marketing authorisation for the Pfizer and BioNTech vaccine in the EU.

Vaccinations are planned to start on Sunday after Christmas.

First, healthcare and social welfare staff will be vaccinated. After that, people over 70 will be vaccinated. In the third stage, people who belong to risk groups because of illnesses will be vaccinated. In the last stage, the rest of the population will be vaccinated.

Finland is set to receive over 18 million vaccine doses. That is enough for 9 million people.

Finland's first delivery of vaccine contains 10,000 doses.

A so-called booster shot must be taken after the first coronavirus vaccination. It must be taken within 3 weeks.

## Jari Aarnio sentenced to life imprisonment

Helsinki's former head of narcotics police, Jari Aarnio, has been sentenced for murder. The Helsinki District Court sentenced Aarnio to life imprisonment for a murder committed 17 years ago.

The verdict says that Aarnio knew about the murder plan but did not warn the victim. He also did not tell other police officers about the information.

The District Court rejected the charge of murder against the former crime boss Keijo Vilhunen. The reason was insufficient evidence.

4 men have previously been sentenced to life imprisonment for the murder of the Swedish-Turkish Volkan Ünsal in Vuosaari, Helsinki.

Jari Aarnio's defence lawyer, Riitta Leppiniemi, says that Aarnio is shocked by the verdict. Aarnio is appealing the verdict to the Court of Appeal.

## Unemployment has grown

![TE Office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_70/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7404275faa97133b632/1605018472)

Unemployment grew clearly last month. Statistics Finland says that the unemployment rate was 6.9 per cent in November. That is one percentage point more than a year ago.

The unemployment rate for under-25s rose by almost 5 percentage points from a year earlier. The unemployment rate for young people was 16.5 per cent in November.

There were 315,000 unemployed jobseekers at TE Offices. That was 86,000 more than a year ago.

## The coronavirus has reached the last continent

![Map of Chile.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11110194)

Open image viewer

Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

There is no longer a single continent on Earth where there is no coronavirus. The coronavirus arrived last in Antarctica.

In Antarctica, 36 Chileans have tested positive for the coronavirus. The infections are linked to a Chilean army research station.

Chile has 13 active bases in Antarctica. There is no permanent population there.
