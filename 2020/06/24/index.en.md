# Wednesday 24.6.2020 (radio)

Parliament discussed immunity from prosecution. Unemployment has increased. Finnkino is opening cinemas. Finns are more critical of gambling than before.

TV Clear News has a summer break and will continue on 17.8.
The radio Clear News is made every day, including in summer.

## Parliament discussed immunity from prosecution

![Juha Mäenpää in Parliament.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11415230)

Open image viewer

Juha Mäenpää, a Finns Party MP in his first term, compared asylum seekers with invasive species in a speech he gave in Parliament last summer. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

MPs have discussed in Parliament the immunity from prosecution of MPs.

An MP can be prosecuted in court only if a large part of the MPs give permission for it. The Prosecutor General has asked MPs for permission to bring charges against MP Juha Mäenpää. A charge means that a punishment is demanded for Mäenpää.

Mäenpää is a Finns Party MP. The police suspect Mäenpää of inciting hatred against a group of people. Mäenpää compared asylum seekers and invasive species in Parliament. An invasive species is a plant or animal that is not originally from the area.

MPs will vote on Friday on whether Juha Mäenpää may be prosecuted.

## Unemployment has got worse

![TE Office.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6499675e5fc2b0df1da/1583335708)

Open image viewer

At the end of May, 433,000 people were registered as unemployed job seekers at TE Offices. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

There are many more unemployed people than before. In May there were 46,000 more unemployed people than in May 2019.

There were 150,000 more people on temporary layoff than a year ago.

There are 56,000 unemployed people under the age of 25. That is 28,000 more than last May.

May was the third month in a row when unemployment rose a lot.

## Cinemas are opening

![Finnkino cinema.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5184,h_2916,x_0,y_97/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6119965dcd73819e7e8/1573745677)

Open image viewer

At least one cinema will be open in all Finnkino locations on 1 July. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finnkino is opening some cinemas.

A cinema is now open in Tennispalatsi and Itäkeskus in Helsinki, and in Kinopalatsi in Turku. Next week at least one cinema will open in every town that has a Finnkino cinema.

Showings start at different times, so that the cinema does not get crowded. It is worth buying a ticket in advance from the online shop.

Cinemas have been closed because of the coronavirus.

## Finns are more critical of gambling than before

![Slot machines.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11054433)

Open image viewer

In 2019 there was an outcry over the marketing of Veikkaus, and a change in how gambling machines are placed was demanded through a citizens' initiative. Illustrative image. Photo: Retu Liikanen / Yle

Finns think about gambling more critically than before.

People's attitudes towards gambling were a little negative in 2019, when in 2015 attitudes were a little positive. Young people in particular think about gambling more critically than before.

Gambling has been talked about a lot lately. There was an outcry last year over Veikkaus' advertising.

Last year it was also demanded that there be fewer gambling products, for example in grocery stores.
