# Tuesday 3.4.2018 (radio)

There are shortcomings in the working conditions in restaurants. Ethnic profiling is common. Tammerkoski rapids were emptied because of bridge work. There is a lot of birch pollen. Spring is late.

Restaurants received many complaints about working conditions

There are shortcomings in the working conditions in restaurants. For example, occupational health care is not always arranged for restaurant employees. The kitchen may be too hot or the floor may be slippery. Wages are not always paid well enough, or employment contracts have not been made correctly.

Authorities inspected many restaurants. Only a few restaurants were in order in every way.

Ethnic profiling is common

![Police](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10142402)

Open image viewer

Ethnic profiling is forbidden in Finland. Photo: Emilia Malin / Yle

Many immigrants face ethnic profiling in Finland. Many people said in a study that police officers, security guards or shop assistants often watch people who look foreign closely.

People said that especially the actions of security guards are often unpleasant. Ethnic profiling is forbidden in Finland.

Tammerkoski rapids were emptied

![Tammerkoski has been emptied of water because of the renovation of Hämeensilta bridge.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10142481)

Open image viewer

The rapids without water look exciting in the centre of Tampere. Photo: Jussi Mansikka / Yle

A big repair project has started in the centre of Tampere. The Tammerkoski rapids have been emptied of water, because Hämeensilta bridge is being repaired. The bridge is 90 years old. The bridge's stones will be taken down and then put back in their places. The work will take 18 months.

Tammerkoski has been emptied of water, because repair scaffolding is being built under Hämeensilta bridge. At the same time, rubbish is being collected from the bottom of the rapids.

Many people think that the rapids without water look exciting in the centre of Tampere.

There is a lot of birch pollen

![Birch flowers. This picture was taken in early summer.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9143044)

Open image viewer

There are many birch catkins now. Photo: AOP

The birch pollen season will be difficult for allergy sufferers this spring. There are many birch catkins now, and the pollen comes from them. There are many catkins on birches that grow in warm places in yards and residential areas.

Native birches usually start to flower in early May. Before that, birch pollen comes to Finland from abroad on the winds.

Spring is coming slowly

![Park scenery at Ruskeasuo, Central Park, Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10123752)

Open image viewer

The last time March was colder was five years ago. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Spring is late. Spring is coming slowly, because the weather is colder than usual. March was 2 to 5 degrees colder than usual in a large part of Finland.

According to the Finnish Meteorological Institute, spring is about a week or two behind its normal schedule.
