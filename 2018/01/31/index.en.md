# Wednesday 31.1.2018 (radio)

US President Donald Trump wants to keep the Guantanamo prison camp running. Some electrical devices are not safe. A new kind of X-ray machine has been put into use in Turku. Posti is bringing parcel lockers to ordinary buildings. A film about Cheek has come to cinemas. Because of snowfall, fewer trains will run tomorrow.

## Trump wants to keep Guantanamo running

![A satisfied Trump](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10051533)

Open image viewer

Doland Trump. Photo: Win McNamee / Epa

US President Donald Trump wants to keep the Guantanamo prison camp running.

Trump has signed an order that new prisoners can be brought to the prison.

The previous US president, Barack Obama, said that the Guantanamo prison must be closed.

Many countries are worried that the USA treats prisoners badly at the Guantanamo camp, which is in Cuba.

## Safety problems in electrical devices

![A smartphone and a charger.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10040347)

Open image viewer

A faulty electrical device can get hot. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

Some electrical devices in shops are not safe.

The Finnish Safety and Chemicals Agency says that last year it removed more than 150 electrical devices from shops because of safety problems.

Faults are most common in transformers and LED lamps. A faulty electrical device can cause a fire.

## A new kind of X-ray machine was put into use

![Tyks's new imaging device that reduces the radiation dose.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10048450)

Open image viewer

You can stand in the new kind of X-ray machine. Photo: Nadine Karell

A new kind of X-ray machine has been put into use at a hospital in Turku. The patient gets less X-ray radiation from it than from old machines.

You can stand in the new kind of X-ray machine.  Doctors say that the pictures are then better.

Turku's new X-ray machine also takes three-dimensional pictures of the patient.

## Parcel lockers are coming to residential buildings too

![A Posti employee at a parcel locker.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9593261)

Open image viewer

People can collect their own parcel or send a parcel from a parcel locker at any time. Photo: Posti

Posti is bringing parcel lockers to ordinary buildings.

New parcel lockers are coming to 500 buildings. Many more parcel lockers are also coming to shopping centres.

People can collect their own parcel or send a parcel from a parcel locker at any time.

You can also order food from a shop to the new parcel lockers.

More parcel lockers are being added because people nowadays order a lot of things from online shops.

## The Cheek film tells about a popular rapper

![Antti Holma raps in the role of Cheek in the film Veljeni vartija](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10051534)

Open image viewer

Actor Antti Holma raps in the role of Cheek in the film Veljeni vartija. Photo: Helsinki-filmi/Veljeni vartija -elokuva

A film about Finland's most popular rap singer, Cheek, is finished. It will be shown in cinemas for the first time today, Wednesday.

The name of the film is Veljeni vartija (My Brother's Keeper). It tells about Cheek and the brother of Cheek, or Jare Tiihonen, Jere.

The film tells what the brothers were like as children and how Jare became the popular rapper Cheek. The film also tells about the fast-paced life of a pop star.

## Snowfall affects train traffic

![a train at a station](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9496964)

Open image viewer

Train traffic is being reduced in Helsinki because of snowfall. Photo: Varpu Helpinen / Yle

Because of snowfall, fewer trains will run tomorrow.

Trains will be reduced in Helsinki and its nearby cities.

The train company VR says that train services are being reduced so that the tracks can be cleared of snow from time to time.
