# Sunday 18.11.2018 (radio)

President Donald Trump thinks Finns look after forests well. Parish elections were held on Sunday. Latvia has celebrated the 100th anniversary of its independence. The Arabic-language Crisis Line has received many customers.

## Trump

![A man in a baseball cap and a man in uniform walk, with a burnt car behind them.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10513700)

President Trump visited the Californian fire area. Photo: Genaro Molina / EPA

President Donald Trump thinks Finns look after forests well. That is why there are no forest fires in Finland, Trump says.

Trump spoke about the matter when he visited the Californian forest fires. In Trump's view, poor forest management is the cause of the Californian fires.

Trump said that California should take an example from Finland. Finns clean up and rake the forests, so that fires do not start.

Trump said that he has spoken about the matter with the Finnish president.

President Niinistö says that he has told Trump how Finland's forests are monitored. We did not talk about raking, Niinistö says.

The matter has been made into humour on social media.

## Parish elections

![-](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10494260)

Open image viewer

Parish elections choose the parish decision-makers. Photo: Anne Elhaimer / Yle

Finland had parish elections on Sunday.

In the elections, the decision-makers of the parishes of the Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland were chosen. They decide, for example, how the parish helps people and what clubs the parish organises.

The Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland has 400 parishes. A church member belongs to the parish in whose area they live.

## Latvia

![Latvia 100 illumination](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10513795)

Open image viewer

The Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs in Helsinki was lit up in Latvian colours in honour of Latvia's celebration. Photo: Maxim Fedorov/Yle

Latvia has had a celebration day on Sunday. Latvia has celebrated the 100th anniversary of its independence. Latvia became an independent country in 1918.

Finnish President Sauli Niinistö also took part in the celebration in the Latvian capital, Riga. Niinistö laid flowers at the Freedom Monument and watched the military parade.

Latvia is one of the Baltic countries. Estonia and Lithuania are its neighbours.

## Crisis line

![The website of the Arabic-language crisis line on a phone screen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10510716)

Open image viewer

The Arabic-language crisis line can be called on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday at 11-15, and on Wednesday at 17-21. The number is 040 195 8202. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The Arabic-language crisis line has received many customers. The Finnish Mental Health Association opened the Arabic-language crisis line last March. Since then about 400 calls have come in.

Many people call the Arabic-language crisis line because of anxiety or depression. The head of crisis line operations, Susanna Winter, says that many also want to talk about asylum matters.

The reason is that an asylum seeker is often alone with their problems. An asylum seeker does not want to tell relatives in their home country about their problems. They do not want the relatives to worry.

Young people also call the Arabic-language crisis line a lot. Young people want to talk about, for example, the differences between their parents' home country and Finland.
