# Radio Clear News Saturday 5.8.2023

Finnish children in al-Hol. Medicine waste. Desertification. Scout jamboree. Weather.

## Finnish children in al-Hol

![A person guards people waiting in a queue.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2999,h_1686,x_0,y_173/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10391946380427c7b7d0/1691243345)

The al-Hol camp last November. Photo: Ahmed Mardnli / EPA

Several Finnish children still live in the al-Hol camp in Syria. About 10 Finns live in the camp, most of whom are children.

The Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs is not currently planning to bring the Finnish children home. This is mainly because the children's mothers do not want to have anything to do with Finnish authorities.

The al-Hol camp holds family members of Isis terrorists. Conditions in the camp are harsh.

26 children and 9 adults have previously returned to Finland from al-Hol.

## Medicine waste

![A red-haired hand is putting a medicine blister pack into a medicine waste bin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5400,h_3037,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115082464ca5cf60e4be/1690985200)

Open image viewer

Old medicines must be returned to a pharmacy. Photo: Joel Peltonen / Yle

A lot of medicine waste is left over in Finnish homes.

The University of Eastern Finland says that about 5.6 million medicine packages are returned to pharmacies every year. Most of them are prescription medicines.

If a medicine is not used and it expires, it is medicine waste.

Old medicines must not be thrown in the rubbish or flushed down the drain, but must be returned to a pharmacy.

## Desertification

![The dry bed of a river, with a bridge visible in the background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4500,h_2531,x_0,y_164/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-114780564c1179352697/1713761304)

The dry bed of a river in Northern Italy in March. Photo: Piero Cruciatti / Stella Pictures

A large part of Southern Europe is threatened by desertification. Desertification means the soil drying out and its quality getting worse.

Markku Kanninen, emeritus professor of forest sciences at the University of Helsinki, says that climate change is pushing the desert climate of North Africa towards Southern Europe.

The most serious situation is in Spain. The UN says that up to 75 percent of Spain's land area may become desert.

## Scout jamboree

![A young teenage girl.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_900,h_506,x_0,y_299/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-115243964ce375734f26/1691236400)

Open image viewer

16-year-old Lotta Timonen says that Finnish scouts have coped well with the extreme heat in South Korea. Photo: Lotta Timonen

The hot weather is hampering the scout jamboree in South Korea.

More than 40,000 scouts from different parts of the world are at the camp. About 900 scouts from Finland are taking part.

During the camp the temperature has been as high as 38 degrees. In the camp hospital hundreds of young people have been treated who got symptoms because of the heat.

The camp began last Tuesday and it is due to end a week later, on Saturday. However, the scouts from Britain and the USA have already said that, because of the extreme heat, they are going home now. The Finnish group, on the other hand, is still doing well and intends to continue at the camp.
