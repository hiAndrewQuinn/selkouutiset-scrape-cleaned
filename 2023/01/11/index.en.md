## Ukraine

Ukraine hopes for more modern weapons.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy says that Russia is planning a big attack on Ukraine. That is why Ukraine needs new weapons.

Many countries are now considering whether they will give battle tanks to Ukraine. Giving battle tanks to Ukraine has also been discussed in Finland.

However, Finland wants first to see what the big countries do. Ukraine has hoped for battle tanks from Germany, for example.

The war in Ukraine began last February, when Russia attacked Ukraine.

![Bottles of spirits on an Alko shelf.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5192,h_2921,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-77707060339bceaf2e8/1613995644)

Sales of alcoholic drinks at Alko fell last year. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

There has been a change in Alko's sales. Sales of alcoholic drinks fell last year.

In 2022 Alko sold 80 million litres of alcoholic drinks. That is 10 percent less than in 2021.

There was also a change in the sales of wines last year. For the first time in a long while, Alko sold more white wines than red wines.

## Interrupting military service

![Conscripts practising in a row at the Rissala garrison.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10931139)

Open image viewer

Some young people interrupt military service for health reasons. Photo: Sami Takkinen / Yle

Some young people interrupt their service in the army. Those who interrupt are about 15 percent of young people who go into the army.

The reason for interrupting is often an old injury or overuse injury. The reason may also be psychological, for example anxiety or depression.

Interrupting often happens at the start of military service. That is why the army has lightened the start of training.

Military service lasts about half a year. Military service is compulsory for men in Finland and voluntary for women.

If a young person does not want to go into the army, they can do civilian service. Civilian service can be completed, for example, in a hospital.

## California storms

![A road that has collapsed.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12687695)

Open image viewer

Storm rains have also broken roads. Photo: STELLA Pictures/ddp/abaca press

In California, USA, there are severe storms. More than 10 people have died in the storms.

The storm has brought heavy rain. Water has risen onto the streets.

Storm winds have also knocked down trees. Trees have ended up on the roads and they hinder traffic.

In California a new storm is already expected. In the mountains maybe over a metre of snow will fall.

Experts say that the storm is a sign of climate change. Because of climate change, California has storms in winter, but in summer it is dry and hot.

In Finland the weather is very rainy, windy and warm.

A rain area moves across Finland. On Thursday snow still falls in the east, otherwise only a little rain falls during the day on Thursday. On Friday new snowfalls may come to Eastern Finland.

The temperature rises above zero almost everywhere in the country and the roads are slippery.
