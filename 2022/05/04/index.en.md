## Sanctions on Russia

![Ursula von der Leyen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3835,h_2157,x_0,y_18/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5750905d1c8494cac72/1562150155)

The President of the European Commission, Ursula von der Leyen, told about new sanctions on Russia. Photo: Clemens Bilan / EPA

The EU Commission is proposing new sanctions on Russia.

The Commission proposes that imports of Russian crude oil be stopped within half a year, and imports of oil products by the end of the year. The Commission also wants more sanctions on Russian banks.

Personal sanctions will also be expanded. For example, officers of the Russian army and people suspected of war crimes in Ukraine will be added to the sanctions list. The news agency AFP says that the head of the Russian Orthodox Church, Patriarch Kirill, will also be added to the sanctions list.

The sanctions will come into force if the EU countries approve them unanimously.

## Britain's support

Britain's Defence Minister promises Britain's support to Finland and Sweden if they decide to apply to join the military alliance NATO.

Defence Minister Ben Wallace says that Britain will help Finland and Sweden if they become the target of Russia's actions during the NATO process.

Wallace has visited Finland today. He and Defence Minister Antti Kaikkonen went together to Satakunta to watch the large international military exercise of the land forces. Troops from Great Britain, Latvia, the United States and Estonia also take part in the exercise.

## Will to defend the country

![A reserve group under the guidance of an instructor.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_387/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-922127621e1f89efe30/1646141573)

Reservists practise local defence. Photo: Ari Haimakainen / Yle

The will of Finns to defend their country is now high.

In a study by the Confederation of Finnish Industries, EVA, 85 per cent of Finns say that they are ready to help Finland's defence in the best way they can.

36 per cent of women and 76 per cent of men are also ready to defend Finland with a weapon in hand.

Russia's attack on Ukraine has increased Finns' will to defend the country.

There are now many people signing up for voluntary national defence training, and thousands of new members have joined the Reservists' Association in the early part of the year.

## Medals for mothers

![First class medals of the Finnish White Rose to be given on Mother's Day.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1346,h_757,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6729155eb55579ebfe8/1588942289)

Accomplished mothers receive the first class medal of the Order of the White Rose of Finland with gold crosses. Photo: Samuli Miettinen / MLL

This year 30 mothers will receive a medal on Mother's Day.

The mothers receive the first class medal of the Order of the White Rose with gold crosses. They receive the medals because they have done well as raisers of children.

President Sauli Niinistö will hand out the medals on Sunday at the Mother's Day celebration in Helsinki.
