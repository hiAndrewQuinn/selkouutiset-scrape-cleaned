## The strike at sawmills and plywood factories ends

![ Jyrki Hollmen, labour market director of Metsäteollisuus ry (back left), National Conciliator Vuokko Piekkala and first vice-chair of the Industrial Union Turja Lehtonen (back right) start negotiations at the National Conciliator's office in Helsinki on 10 February 2020. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11200861)

An agreement has been reached in the labour dispute at sawmills and plywood factories. At the same time, an agreement was also made in the labour dispute in the carpentry industry. The agreements cover 9,000 workers. Wage increases are about 3.3 percent, and the extra hours, the kiky hours from the competitiveness pact, are removed from the agreement.

The strikes at sawmills and plywood factories end now, because an agreement was made in the labour dispute.

## Water flooded the streets in the storm in Finland

![The Rauma river floods at the Rauma canal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_285/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6459165e51d78545151/1582421971)

The storm has cut power during the night in Southern Finland and Southwest Finland. Trees fell on power lines. The wind blew hard on Saturday evening and during the night.

It also rained a lot. In many seaside towns, sea water rose into the streets. Many rivers and streams flooded, because it rained a lot.

## Sandstorm stopped flights in the Canary Islands

![A tourist dries herself at a beach in Puerto del Rosario.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11223646)

A sandstorm in the Canary Islands has made air travel and the trips of Finnish tourists difficult. Because of the sandstorm, planes could not take off or land on Tenerife and Gran Canaria on Saturday and on Sunday morning.

The Canary Islands are a popular holiday destination in winter. Many Finns and other Nordic people go there.

Sometimes sandstorms come to the Canary Islands from Africa's Sahara.

## Origin labels on food will be more detailed

![Marjamaa strawberry jam jar](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9402116)

Open image viewer

Strawberry jam must say which country the strawberries have grown in. Photo: Jari Tanskanen/Yle

The origin labels on food are improving. A new law says that packages must clearly state which country the product's main ingredient comes from.

The main ingredient means the ingredient that makes up more than half of the food, that is, more than 50%. For example, strawberry jam must say which country the strawberries were imported from. Rye bread must say which country the rye flour comes from.

The new EU rule on package labels will come into force at the beginning of April.

## On Shrovetide people go sledding and eat buns

![Children sledding on a hill in Laajasalo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8655266)

Open image viewer

There is very little snow in Southern Finland, or no snow at all. Photo: Yle

Today, Sunday, Shrovetide is being celebrated. On Shrovetide people sled down a hill on a sledge or a toboggan and eat pea soup.

On Shrovetide people also eat Shrovetide buns. Cream and almond paste, or cream and jam, are put between the bun. Shrovetide is celebrated for two days, on Sunday and also on Tuesday.

In Southern Finland it is not possible to sled down a sledging hill this year, because there is no snow. Small hills have been made with artificial snow in Southern Finland too.
