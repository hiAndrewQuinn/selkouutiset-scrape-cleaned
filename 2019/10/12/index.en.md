# Saturday 12.10.2019 (radio)

Civilians are in trouble in Syria. A strong typhoon has hit Japan. Labour Minister Timo Harakka promises action on getting over-50s into work. Moose hunting has started.

## Civilians' suffering in Syria

![Smoke rises from the city of Ras al-Ain in northern Syria on 11 October 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_419/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5994585da1a8c8e54aa/1570875737)

Turkey says that it has captured the Syrian town of Ras al-Ain, where significant fighting has taken place in recent days. The picture is from Friday, 11 October. Photo: Ozan Kose / AFP

In north-eastern Syria, civilians are in an even worse situation than before. The Kurdish administration of the area says that almost 200,000 people have fled their homes. The reason is a military operation that Turkey started on Wednesday.

At least 20 civilians have died in northern Syria. On the Turkish side, the authorities say that 17 people have died because of shells fired from Syria.

Turkish media says that Turkish forces have advanced even deeper into the Kurdish area of north-eastern Syria. The news agency Reuters says that forces led by Turkey have reached the town of Ras al-Ain, on the Syrian side of the border. However, Kurdish fighters deny this.

## Typhoon hit Japan

![Combined picture showing a street view of Tokyo's popular Harajuku shopping area on Friday evening, 11 October (upper picture), and Saturday morning, 12 October (lower picture).](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11017651)

The combined picture shows a street in Tokyo's Harajuku shopping area on Friday evening, 11 October (upper picture), and on Saturday morning, 12 October (lower picture). Photo: Odd Andersen / AFP

A strong typhoon has hit Japan. Millions of people have been told to leave the path of the hurricane.

The typhoon has caused strong winds and heavy rain. Because of the rain there have been floods, landslides and power cuts.

At least 1 person died in the city of Ichihara when strong wind destroyed a car.

Parts of Tokyo, the capital, have been evacuated. In Tokyo it has rained heavily and the wind is strong. Train stations and shops have been closed, and flights have been cancelled.

The typhoon is the strongest hurricane in Tokyo in over 60 years. In Tokyo, perhaps as much as half a metre of water may fall in one day.

## Labour Minister Harakka on getting over-50s into work

![Timo Harakka on Yle's Ykkösaamu on the morning of Saturday 12 October 2019.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11017628)

Open image viewer

Labour Minister Timo Harakka. Photo: Ykkösaamu / Yle

Labour Minister Timo Harakka promises measures that will get jobs for people over 50. He proposes a bigger wage subsidy for over-50s. A wage subsidy can be paid to a company that hires an unemployed person.

The employment rate of people over 55 is clearly lower than for others. Labour Minister Timo Harakka says that older people are treated clearly worse in the labour market than younger people.

## Moose hunting has started

![A moose killed on the ground.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10455600)

Open image viewer

Moose hunting starts on Saturday. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Moose hunting has started.

About 50,000 moose may be shot in Finland. That is 8 percent fewer than last year. In total, about 60,000 white-tailed deer may be shot.

Moose may be hunted until the end of the year. After that, moose may still be hunted for 2 weeks, but a dog may no longer help.
