## Monkeypox

![Monkeypox virus test.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4000,h_2250,x_0,y_411/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-960252628df76d4c680/1653472281)

Photo: Davor Puklavec / Lehtikuva

The first monkeypox patient has probably been found in Finland.

The patient has blisters on the skin and a high fever. He is being cared for at home in the Helsinki region.

The patient caught monkeypox while he was travelling in Europe. Monkeypox has been found in different parts of Europe in recent weeks.

Monkeypox is most common in Africa. People usually get monkeypox from wild animals, but now it has started to spread from person to person.

However, monkeypox does not spread easily. Usually monkeypox also gets better by itself in a few weeks.

## US shooting

![People hugging each other outside.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4388,h_2468,x_0,y_110/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-960064628ddb37b5c91/1653556813)

The shooting shocked people. Photo: Brandon Bell / AFP

There has been a shooting at a school in the USA.

More than 20 people died when a shooter attacked a school in Texas. Among the dead are 19 children.

The shooter was an 18-year-old man. The man died when the police shot him. The police say that the man acted alone.

President Joe Biden is sad because of the death of the children. Biden thinks that the USA's gun laws must be tightened, so that buying guns becomes harder than now.

## Talks in Turkey

![Flags of Turkey and NATO.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9429611)

Open image viewer

Flags of the military alliance NATO and Turkey. Photo: Benoit Doppagne / EPA

Representatives of Finland and Sweden negotiated in Turkey on Wednesday about the military alliance NATO. Finland and Sweden have applied for membership of NATO, but Turkey opposes the countries' membership.

Turkey thinks that Finland and Sweden protect Kurdish terrorists. Finland and Sweden must settle their disputes with Turkey, so that the handling of their NATO applications can get going.

## Young people and working life

![Two work trainees at a construction site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5333968)

Open image viewer

Good work colleagues are important for young people. Photo: Sami Tammi / Yle

Finnish young people are afraid that working life is hard.

In a new survey about 30 per cent of young people are afraid that working life is too hard.

Young people want to get into a field where working hours are not too long. Young people also hope for good work colleagues and managers.

The respondents of the survey were lower secondary school pupils and upper secondary level students.

## The weather

![weather map](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12462044)

Open image viewer

Wednesday was this year's first heat day.

The temperature rose above the heat limit, that is, above 25 degrees. In Northern Finland, in Oulu, as much as 25,4 degrees was measured.

On Thursday the weather changes and a lot of rain falls in the southern and western parts of the country.

In the east and in the north the weather continues sunny and warm.

In the rain it is warm at 10-15 degrees, and in the east and north the temperature is close to 20 degrees.

On Friday new rains will come to Finland from the east and the weather is cool.
