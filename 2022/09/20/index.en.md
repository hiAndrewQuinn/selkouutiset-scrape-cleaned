# Tuesday 20.9.2022 (radio)

Handling of the budget. Left Alliance voting behaviour. Errors in passport shipments. Potato harvest. Vesa-Matti Loiri's funeral. Weather forecast.

## Budget handling

![Yellow budget book on an MP's desk in the Parliament chamber.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_628/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-863605615da96410c34/1638225739)

Parliament is dealing with the budget for almost the whole autumn. Photo: Petteri Bülow / Yle

Parliament has started handling next year's budget. The government's budget proposal is over 8 billion euros in deficit, that is, the state spends more money than it takes in.

State spending next year is about 15 billion euros higher than this year. A big reason is that the funding of social and health services is moving from the municipalities to the state. Defence spending also grows.

Parliament will handle the budget for almost the whole autumn.

## Left Alliance's voting behaviour

![Picture of the Riksdag voting board, with 109 yes votes, 38 no votes, 1 blank vote and 51 absent.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4362,h_2453,x_0,y_310/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-101002163287ba4f0fc4/1663597541)

Open image viewer

The patient safety act was approved by 109 votes to 38. Photo: Lehtikuva

The voting behaviour of the Left Alliance's MPs worsens the Left Alliance's relations with other parties.

5 Left Alliance MPs voted against the patient safety act on Monday. That is why the other government parties are angry with the Left Alliance. For example, the SDP and RKP demand that agreed matters are kept to in the government.

The leadership of the Left Alliance's parliamentary group is also angry that some of the party's MPs did not follow the party line in the vote. The Left Alliance's parliamentary group will deal with the matter at a meeting on Thursday.

## Error in passport shipments

![Many passports in a row.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_573/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-80194560912c88a3f19/1622687833)

Open image viewer

The mailing machine managed to pack over 500 passports wrongly. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

An error has happened in the police's passport shipments. The police have sent the passports of the wrong people to over 500 Finns.

The cause is an error in the use of the mailing machine. The mailing machine has packed passports into the envelopes of the wrong people.

About 100 people managed to receive the wrong person's passport. The error was noticed, and the police will call back the remaining 400 passports. They will be sent again to the right recipients.

## Potato harvest

![Potatoes in boxes in a potato field.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_82/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-857108614b22ee9f2ec/1643898947)

Open image viewer

Rain has worsened the potato harvest, especially in the Ostrobothnia region. Photo: Vesa Grekula / Yle

The potato harvest is worse than expected. In places the potato harvest is up to 30 percent smaller than last autumn. This is what the agricultural expert organisation ProAgria says.

The potato harvest was supposed to be good. However, it has rained a lot in August and September, especially in Ostrobothnia.

The rain has worsened the growth of the potatoes.

## Vesa-Matti Loiri

![Black-and-white picture of Vesa-Matti Loiri at his 70th birthday party, on stage behind a microphone.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3869,h_2176,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-100972163283c0341b09/1663582119)

Open image viewer

Vesa-Matti Loiri (4. 1. 1945 – 10.8.2022) was a versatile actor and musician. Photo: Jarno Kuusinen / All Over Press

The artist **Vesa-Matti Loiri** has been buried. Loiri's blessing ceremony was held on Tuesday afternoon at Johannes Church in Helsinki. The blessing was conducted by Bishop Teemu Laajasalo.

Many musicians and athletes had been invited to the funeral. The spouse of President Sauli Niinistö, Jenni Haukio, also took part in Vesa-Matti Loiri's funeral.

Prime Minister Sanna Marin and Minister of Science and Culture Petri Honkonen laid the government's wreath.
