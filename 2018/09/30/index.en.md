# Sunday 30.9.2018 (radio)

More victims of the earthquake and tsunami have been found in Indonesia. Repairs have been made after the storm in Greece. Finnish young people have done well in the Euroskills competitions. In Hämeenlinna it has been possible to reduce the smoking of expectant mothers. The week begins in autumn weather.

## Indonesian victims

![Earthquake damage in the city of Palu on the Indonesian island of Sulawesi.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10431320)

The earthquake and tsunami destroyed the city of Palu in Indonesia. Photo: STR / EPA

Indonesia has reported new figures for the victims of the earthquake and tsunami. More than 800 have died, and thousands are injured.

Authorities estimate that the number of victims will still rise. Rescue work is still ongoing. Victims are being searched for, for example, in the rubble of houses. Rescue workers are getting help from the army.

The earthquake was on Friday on the island of Sulawesi, which is part of Indonesia. The earthquake caused a tsunami, that is, big waves. The waves were up to 6 metres high.

## Greek storm

![Storm damage in Kalamata.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10431000)

Open image viewer

The storm brought wind and rain to Greece. Photo: Jari Niemelä / Yle

Repairs have been made after the storm in Greece. The storm struck Greece on Saturday.

The storm was not as bad as feared. However, heavy rain covered streets with water and filled homes with water.

Rain in Greece continues, and the rain may hinder agriculture. For example, olives and figs ripen in Greece at this time of year.

## Euroskills competitions

![A technician measures voltage.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10029496)

Open image viewer

Young people competed in vocational skills in Budapest. Photo: Pekka Tynell / Yle

Finnish young people have done well in the Euroskills competitions. Euroskills is the European Championship for young people's vocational skills.

25 Finnish young people took part in the competition. They are Finland's top specialists in their own trades. At the European Championships, Finns won 5 medals and 6 awards.

The Euroskills competitions were held this time in the Hungarian capital, Budapest.

## Expectant mothers smoking

![Woman smoking.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10232381)

Open image viewer

Quitting smoking is difficult also for an expectant mother. Photo: Nella Nuora / Yle

Expectant mothers smoke less in Hämeenlinna than elsewhere in Finland. The reason is a campaign by the Hämeenlinna child health clinics, called "Smoke-Free Babies".

The campaign started last year. Before the campaign, about 14 percent of mothers smoked during pregnancy. Now only 4 percent of expectant mothers smoke.

An important part of the campaign is peer support. Mothers have supported and encouraged each other on the campaign's Facebook page.

Firms are also part of the campaign. Expectant mothers have, for example, received discounts in some shops when they have succeeded in quitting smoking.

The Hämeenlinna campaign now interests many cities that want to reduce mothers' smoking during pregnancy.
