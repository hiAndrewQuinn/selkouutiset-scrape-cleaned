Most Finns do not believe that relations between Finland and Russia will improve soon. This is what a new survey says.

About 75 percent of Finns think that relations between Finland and Russia will not improve in the next 10 years.

Over 1000 Finns answered the survey of the Municipal Sector Development Foundation.

## Attack on Salman Rushdie

![Salman Rushdie stands in the spotlight against a dark background.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4248,h_2389,x_7,y_442/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99380762f685981336f/1660323323)

Open image viewer

Salman Rushdie. Photo: Olivier Douliery / AOP

The author Salman Rushdie has been attacked. Rushdie was badly injured and is in hospital.

Rushdie was attacked on Friday in New York. The attack happened at a speaking event, where Rushdie also appeared.

The attacker is a 24-year-old man. He attacked Rushdie and stabbed him with a knife. The police arrested the man.

Over 30 years ago Rushdie published the book The Satanic Verses. The religious leader of Iran was of the view that the book mocks God. He ordered Muslims to kill Rushdie.

## Explosion in Vantaa

![Police sweep broken glass in the yard of an apartment block in Vantaa after the explosion accident.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5180,h_2913,x_0,y_157/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-99389562f78a3b9f83c/1660390025)

Open image viewer

The explosion happened in an apartment block flat in Myyrmäki, Vantaa. Photo: Esa Syväkuru / Yle

On Saturday there was an explosion in Vantaa. The explosion happened in an apartment block flat in Myyrmäki in Vantaa.

One person was injured in the explosion. They were taken to hospital.

The police say that the explosion may have been caused by chemicals. The chemicals exploded when they were being handled in the flat.

The police are still investigating the matter.

The situation of women in Afghanistan has worsened since the Taliban came to power. Photo: EPA

The extremist movement Taliban has prevented a women's protest in Afghanistan.

Several dozen women gathered in the Afghan capital Kabul. They demanded bread, work and freedom.

Taliban fighters drove the women away and beat them. The extremist movement Taliban took power in Afghanistan a year ago.

## Drought in Europe

![A sea of fire in the forest in the dark, tree trunks visible against the flames.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-12572469)

Open image viewer

Fire is burning land and forest in France. Photo: Gironde fire brigade / EPA

Many European countries are now dry. Dry weather has caused a lot of problems.

There is a big ground fire in France. The ground fire is 40 kilometres wide. Firefighters have come from many European countries.

In Britain people must reduce their use of water, because the country has not been this dry for 50 years. In many places people may not water gardens or wash cars.

## The weather

Sunday is a warm day in Finland. The temperature is 20–28 degrees in almost the whole of Finland, only in Lapland it is a little cooler.

Sunday is also dry, no rain falls. The sun shines in a large part of the country.

On Monday the weather warms up also in Lapland. The start of the week is warm in the whole country.
