# Thursday 1.8.2019 (radio)

The cause of the Tampere plane crash is not yet known. Timo Kivinen is the new commander of the Finnish Army. In the Netherlands a new law bans covering the face in public buildings. In July there were fewer hot days than usual.

## Tampere plane crash

![Pirkkala plane crash](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10902984)

The plane crash happened at Tampere-Pirkkala airport. Photo: Marjut Suomi / Yle

The cause of the Tampere plane crash is not yet known.

A plane crashed on Wednesday in Tampere. One person died in the accident.

A small plane fell to the ground at Tampere-Pirkkala airport. The pilot of the plane died. He was alone in the plane.

The pilot's flying studies were still unfinished, but he already had permission to fly alone. The pilot was about 50 years old.

## The Army's new commander

![Lieutenant General Timo Kivinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10902355)

Open image viewer

Commander Timo Kivinen. Photo: Martti Kainulainen / Lehtikuva

The Finnish Army has got a new commander, that is, a new head. The new commander is Timo Kivinen.

President Sauli Niinistö appointed Kivinen to the post of commander in June. Kivinen started his work as commander on Thursday.

Kivinen says that the army must also prevent new kinds of hybrid threats. For example, artificial intelligence changes military operations.

Kivinen, who is 59, has been in military service for a long time, over 40 years. For example, he has been on peacekeeping duty in Bosnia.

Kivinen has also studied in Britain and the USA. As well as Finnish and Swedish, he speaks English and Russian.

## The Dutch burqa ban

![A Muslim woman in Rotterdam, the Netherlands, wearing a niqab that partly covers her face.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10903133)

Open image viewer

Outdoors on the street, you may also cover your face in the Netherlands. Photo: Robin Utreht / EPA

In the Netherlands a new law bans covering the face in public buildings. Faces must not be covered, for example, in schools, health centres or offices.

In addition, the law bans covering the face in public transport, for example on buses. However, a person may cover their face outdoors on the street.

The Dutch law bans covering the face with any kind of clothing or helmets. However, people in the Netherlands are talking about a burqa ban, because women may no longer cover their faces with a burqa or niqab in public buildings.

Many other European countries also have a burqa ban. Covering the face is banned in Belgium, Bulgaria, Austria, France and Denmark.

## July's heat

![Summer landscape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10892450)

Open image viewer

In July there were really hot days, but few. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

In July there were fewer hot days than usual. A hot day is one where the temperature is at least 25 degrees.

This year there were 13 hot days in July. That is 3 days fewer than usual.

Although there were few hot days, record temperatures were set in July. Last Sunday in Porvoo it was 33.7 degrees, and in Kaisaniemi in Helsinki it was 33.2 degrees.
