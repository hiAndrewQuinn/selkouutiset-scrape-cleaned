The recommendation on remote work ends. A research group says that a face mask protects against corona. Housing sales have still been busy. A new boat route is opening in Finland.

## The recommendation on remote work ends

![K-Group, YIT, Rovio and Nordea](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11473224)

Open image viewer

We asked ten large employers when they will return to the offices. Photo: Yle

For several months the government has recommended that people do a lot of remote work. The aim has been that the coronavirus does not spread. The recommendation on remote work ends this weekend.

Yle asked big employers about remote work.

For example, the City of Helsinki is calling its employees back to the office right at the start of next week. Nordea and Kesko say that employees have already returned to the offices at the start of the summer. Employees at F-Secure and Rovio will continue remote work for a few more weeks.

Ilmarinen and YIT have not yet decided whether remote work will continue.

Employers say that they have had many good experiences from remote work.

## Face masks are useful

![A woman with a mask on her face.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11378885)

Open image viewer

The researchers' analysis shows that the protection from the masks is great. Photo: Esko Jämsä / AOP

A Finnish research group says that a face mask protects against the coronavirus. The help from a face mask is significant, because a mask lowers the risk of getting sick by more than 30 per cent.

The results of the study also show that a face mask does not have any big harm. People also get used to face masks.

International researchers will look at the Finnish study later.

## Corona did not stop housing sales

![New apartment blocks are being built in the centre of Joensuu.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4035,h_2270,x_0,y_485/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6647195e97dd3327211/1587010967)

Open image viewer

New apartment blocks are being built in the centre of Joensuu. Photo: Jouki Väinämö / Yle

Just as many flats were sold in June as in June last year. So the coronavirus has not reduced the number of housing sales. There were 30 per cent more housing sales in June than in May.

Flat prices rose by less than one per cent in the year in the capital region. In the rest of the country, prices fell by 4 per cent. Flat prices fell the most in Eastern Finland.

## The Kimola canal opens

![The Kimola canal](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6981105f085e5b226df/1594384023)

Open image viewer

The Kimola canal makes boating possible between Lake Pyhäjärvi and Lake Päijänne. Photo: Pyry Sarkiola / Yle

A new route for boats opens in Finland on Monday.

The name of the route is the Kimola canal. Along the Kimola canal you can get by boat from Lake Pyhäjärvi to Lake Päijänne. The canal is 5.5 kilometres long.

Before, logs were transported along the Kimola canal. Now the canal has been changed so that you can sail a boat along it. Construction started in summer 2018.

The Kimola canal has a 70-metre-long tunnel that goes through the rock. Such a boat tunnel is rare in the whole world.
