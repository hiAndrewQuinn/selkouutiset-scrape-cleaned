## Entry restrictions

![Tallink Silja Europa arriving from Tallinn at the Helsinki West Terminal on Sunday 20 October. The Estonian police found two dead young adults on the ship at the port of Tallinn on Sunday morning.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11028702)

Commuting from Estonia to Finland is becoming easier. Photo: Mikko Stig / Lehtikuva

Finland is making it easier to enter the country. Restrictions on entry will be eased from next Monday.

People may come to Finland freely from EU countries if the person coming has received full vaccine protection against corona. People may also come to Finland if they have had corona less than half a year before entering the country.

Easing the restrictions makes commuting from Estonia to Finland easier, for example. Leisure travel will also be freed.

The restrictions are being eased because Finland's corona situation is better. Today it was reported that 55 new coronavirus infections have been found in Finland.

The Pfizer-BioNTech coronavirus vaccine. Photo: Kristiina Lehto / Yle

The National Institute for Health and Welfare recommends coronavirus vaccination for children and young people who belong to a risk group.

Coronavirus vaccination is recommended for children and young people aged 12-15 who have, for example, a serious heart disease or asthma that requires continuous medication.

Children and young people are given the Pfizer and BioNTech coronavirus vaccine.

## Shortage of carers in elderly care

![A carer helps an elderly person who is eating at a table.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11724018)

Open image viewer

There is a serious shortage of carers across the country. Photo: Antro Valo / Yle

There is a big shortage of carers in elderly care.

There is a shortage of carers across the country. Hundreds of summer jobs have been left empty, because not enough practical nurses and registered nurses have been found for the work. Permanent jobs are also open.

For example, in Jyväskylä there are 15 permanent places open in home care for the elderly. In addition, 40 substitutes are still needed for the summer. However, workers have not been found.

## Laila Hirvisaari

![Author Laila Hirvisaari in 2011.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11986830)

Author Laila Hirvisaari. Photo: Markku Ulander / Lehtikuva

Author and professor Laila Hirvisaari has died. She was 83 years old.

Hirvisaari was famous for her historical novel series and plays. She wrote 41 novels and many plays.

More than 4 million copies of Hirvisaari's books have been sold in Finland.

## Cloudberry harvest

Cloudberry flower. Photo: Yle

This summer's cloudberry harvest may be a good one.

The Natural Resources Institute says that cloudberries are now flowering on many bogs where there are usually no cloudberries. If the flowering succeeds, the harvest will be good.

The cloudberry harvest was also good last year.
