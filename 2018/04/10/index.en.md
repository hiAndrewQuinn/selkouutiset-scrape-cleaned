# Tuesday 10.4.2018 (radio)

The use of coal will end in 2029. The government is planning how to spend money in the next few years on Tuesday and Wednesday. Midwife Elina Botha knows how to calm a crying baby. Not all small children sit safely in the car on short trips.

## Coal ban in 2029

![Operations manager Jari Laine working at the Suomenoja power plant](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10148935)

Finland will ban the use of coal after 2029.

The government parties have agreed on the coal ban. The government also agreed that an energy company will be paid support if it gives up coal four years earlier, in 2025. Support is paid if a company switches from coal to wood fuel or new technologies such as ground-source heat.

Today a lot of coal is used for heating, for example in the Helsinki region, Turku, Vaasa and Jakobstad.

## Training to fill open jobs with workers

![National Coalition Party chair Petteri Orpo, Centre Party chair Juha Sipilä and Blue Reform chair Sampo Terho at the government's budget framework talks at Kesäranta in Helsinki](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10152568)

Today, Tuesday, and tomorrow, Wednesday, the government is planning what tax money will be used for in the next few years.

The government's most important goal is employment. There are many unemployed people in Finland, but also open jobs. The government wants, for example, to increase training so that a worker is found for every open job.

The government may also give more money to support people who do not have a home. More support may also be given to people who have a very large amount of debt.

## A baby's crying can be calmed

![A baby swaddled in a pink swaddle.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10151686)

Open image viewer

The baby has been swaddled, or wrapped in a cloth. Photo: Marko Melto / Yle

Midwife Elina Botha knows how to calm a baby who cries a lot.

Some babies cry often and for a long time, even if there is no clear reason for the crying. Elina Botha advises swaddling the baby, or wrapping it in a cloth. Then the mother or father takes the child in their arms so that the baby is lying on its side. Then the child is rocked and is allowed to suck, for example, a dummy. You can also play a hissing sound to the child, so-called white noise.

Elina Botha says that this way the child feels a bit like it did in the mother's womb.

## A small child in a car seat even on a short trip

![A police officer looking at car seats.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10151347)

Open image viewer

The police check car seats. Photo: Toni Pitkänen / Yle

A surprising number of small children do not travel safely in cars.

This was found out when the police checked cars in day care car parks in Eastern Finland. About 25 per cent of small children were not in a car seat in the car. Some of the children sat on the car seat with only the seat belt on. Sometimes the child was in the front seat on the mother's lap.

Many parents said that there was no car seat because the drive to day care was short. Some also said that they did not have time to put the child in safely because they were in a hurry.
