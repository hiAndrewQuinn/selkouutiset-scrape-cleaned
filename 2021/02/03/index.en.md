# Wednesday 3.2.2021 (radio)

The AstraZeneca corona vaccine will be given in Finland only to under-70s. The President opened the parliamentary session. In Tampere a court is examining war crime charges. The frosty weather continues.

## AstraZeneca corona vaccine

![AstraZeneca medicine box in the hand of a vaccinator.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4718,h_2654,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7679856014709d98e47/1612013185)

Photo: AFP / Lehtikuva

Finnish authorities recommend that the AstraZeneca corona vaccine is given only to under-70s.

The AstraZeneca corona vaccine may come to Finland perhaps next week. The EU gave it a marketing authorisation last Friday.

2 other corona vaccines are already in use. They are also used to vaccinate elderly people.

It is estimated that in Finland all elderly people will have been vaccinated against corona at least once by the end of April.

The corona vaccine is given 2 times.

## Finland's corona figures

![THL sign](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11760661)

Open image viewer

THL, the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare, follows corona infections in Finland. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that 265 new corona infections have been found in Finland. In total there are about 46,000 corona infections in Finland.

On Wednesday it was also reported that there was 1 new corona death. A total of 685 people have died of corona in Finland.

## Opening of Parliament

![President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_2878,h_1619,x_0,y_31/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6558035e74973a51530/1584731761)

Open image viewer

President Sauli Niinistö. Photo: Juha Kivioja / Yle

Finland's parliament, the Eduskunta, has started its work for this year.

President Sauli Niinistö opened the parliamentary session on Wednesday. The President said that Finland has succeeded well in fighting corona.

Niinistö admits that the corona restrictions are heavy. Even heavier, however, is becoming ill with corona or losing a family member or friend because of corona, President Niinistö said.

At the event, apart from the President, there was only Speaker of Parliament Anu Vehviläinen. MPs were not present because of corona. They followed the event online.

## Trial for war crimes

![Accused Gibril Massaquoi](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4032,h_2268,x_0,y_378/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-769287601a58f7ddea7/1612339476)

Open image viewer

A Sierra Leonean man is accused of war crimes. Photo: Matias Väänänen / Yle

An unusual trial has begun in Tampere.

In the court there is a man who is accused of war crimes.

The Sierra Leonean man has lived in Finland for more than 10 years, but he may have committed crimes in Liberia about 20 years ago. In African Liberia there was a civil war at that time.

The prosecutor says that the man killed and raped ordinary people during the war.

The man himself says that he did not commit these crimes.

The court will probably give its verdict in the autumn.
