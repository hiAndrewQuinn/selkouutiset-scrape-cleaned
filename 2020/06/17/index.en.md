# Wednesday 17.6.2020 (radio)

Homicides. Finland's corona figures. New corona infections in Beijing. Norwegian flights. Fishing management fee. The weather.

## Many homicides in Finland

![Police cordon tape.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11405233)

Open image viewer

Many murders, killings and attempted killings have been committed in Finland during the first part of the year. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

The police say that many homicides have been committed in Finland during the first part of the year.

Early in the year more than 200 homicides were committed. The number is the highest in more than 10 years.

Homicides are murders and killings, or attempts at them.

The police say that the use of alcohol and drugs is often linked to homicides. Sometimes relationship problems also lead to violence.

This spring there have also been many cases where the perpetrator had mental health problems.

Since yesterday, 5 new coronavirus infections have been found in Finland.

The last corona-related death in Finland was reported on Sunday.

## Strict corona restrictions in China

![Sellers at a wholesale market in Beijing.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11402839)

Open image viewer

Chinese wholesale markets sell a lot of different food. Photo: Adrian Bradshaw / EPA

Chinese authorities are worried about new corona infections.

In the capital, Beijing, 140 infections have been found in a week.

The authorities are trying in every way to stop the virus from spreading.

Beijing's schools and universities are closed. Most domestic flights in China have been cancelled.

Beijing residents have been ordered to stay in the city.

The authorities believe that the corona infections have spread at wholesale markets where food is sold.

## Norwegian starts flying again

The planes of the airline Norwegian will start flying again. Norwegian is opening routes from Helsinki to Stockholm and Oslo.

Norwegian is opening over 70 flight routes.

The routes have been closed because of the coronavirus pandemic.

## Fishing inspectors keep watch

![Fisherman shows his fishing licence on his mobile phone to an inspector.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11008528)

Open image viewer

This picture is from last summer. A fishing inspector checks from an app on the phone whether the fee has been paid. Photo: Mika Moksu / Yle

Fishing has been popular this summer.

Metsähallitus reminds people that fishing supervision will be increased from this week on many lakes and rivers.

A fishing inspector may ask for the fishing management fee.

Every adult must pay the fishing management fee if they fish with a lure or with nets or traps, or catch crayfish.

The fee can be paid with a phone app.

The weather is warm.
