# Saturday 1.7.2017 (radio)

Estonia now holds the presidency of the European Union. The Pride parade interested people in Helsinki. There are many music events this weekend. There is a big religious event, the Summer Services, in Pori.

## Estonia holds the EU presidency

![The Estonian band Metsatöll performed at the opening ceremony of Estonia's EU presidency in Tallinn on Thursday.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9700535)

There was a big celebration in Estonia when Estonia started its EU presidency. The Estonian band Metsatöll also performed at the celebration. Photo: Petri Raivio / Yle

Estonia now holds the presidency of the European Union. Estonia started the presidency on Saturday, the 1st of July.

The task of the presidency country is to prepare and lead the negotiations of the EU countries. In the near future, the EU countries will negotiate, for example, about the Union's asylum system.

Because of Estonia's important task, travellers' passports are now checked at Estonia's borders more often than usual. There are also more checks in the ports of Tallinn and Helsinki.

Estonia holds the presidency of the European Union until the end of this year.

## Pride

![The Helsinki Pride parade in Helsinki on 1 July 2017.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9701036)

Open image viewer

The Pride parade went peacefully. Photo: Emmi Korhonen / Lehtikuva

There was a colourful Pride parade in Helsinki on Saturday. About 35,000 people walked through central Helsinki. They demanded equality for homosexuals and other sexual minorities.

The Pride parade was part of Helsinki Pride week. Many different events have been organised during the week.

![Mr. Gay Finland Rami Kiiskinen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9698847)

Open image viewer

Mr. Gay Finland Rami Kiiskinen. Photo: Taavi Tihkan / Mr.Gay Finland

During Pride week, for example, Mr. Gay Finland was chosen. The competition was won by Rami Kiiskinen. He is half Estonian. Kiiskinen's mother tongue is sign language, because both of Kiiskinen's parents are deaf. Rami Kiiskinen wants to remind people that among homosexuals there are also many minorities, for example refugees.

## The Summer Services

![The Summer Services in Tornio photographed from the air.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8997509)

Open image viewer

The Summer Services have really many participants every year. This photo is from last year. Photo: Uula Kuvaja / Yle

The biggest event of the weekend is a religious event. It is called the Summer Services.

This summer the Summer Services are held in Pori, in Western Finland. Over 70,000 Laestadians go to the Summer Services.

Laestadians belong to the Finnish Lutheran Church, but they think about some things differently from other Finns. For example, they do not accept contraception. There are many big families with many children at the Summer Services. Laestadians say that children are a gift from God.
