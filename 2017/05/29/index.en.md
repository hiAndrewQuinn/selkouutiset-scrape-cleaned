## Head of news Jääskeläinen leaves Yle

![Atte Jääskeläinen.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9534036)

Open image viewer

Atte Jääskeläinen is stopping his work as head of news at Yle. Photo: Derrick Frilund / Yle

Yle's head of news is changing.

Head of news Atte Jääskeläinen said on Monday that he is leaving Yle.

There has been a lot of talk in Finland about Yle and head of news Jääskeläinen. It has been said, for example, that Jääskeläinen let the prime minister influence the content of the news.

The matter has been talked about a lot, because the freedom of newspapers, radio and television is very important in Finland. Politicians must not decide what is said in the news.

Jääskeläinen hopes that the situation calms down and Yle can work in peace again.

## A Finnish short film did well

![Teppo Airaksinen and Melli Maikkula](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9635655)

Open image viewer

Director Teppo Airaksinen (right) at the Cannes Film Festival together with writer Melli Maikkula. Photo: Juulia Klemola

A Finnish short film has done well at the Cannes Film Festival. The short film "Katto" (The Roof) got a special mention from the jury. It means that the judges thought "Katto" was good, even though it did not win the short film category.

5000 short films tried to get into the Cannes Film Festival. Only 9 short films got in. The judges chose a Chinese short film as the winner.

The director of the film "Katto", Teppo Airaksinen, was pleased. It was great that "Katto" was chosen for the festival and also got an honourable mention, Airaksinen said.

## Nominate the Plain Speaker of the Year

![A man and a woman holding a certificate and flowers.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9629297)

Open image viewer

The Satakunta Hospital District got the Plain Speaker of the Year prize in 2015. Riikka Törnroos and Olli Wanne received the prize. Photo: Kaisa Kaatra/Finnish Centre for Easy Language

The Plain Speaker of the Year will be awarded again in the autumn.

The Plain Speaker of the Year prize is given to a person who has promoted communication that is easy to understand. Then all people get information about things and culture in easy Finnish.

The prize can also be given to a working group, a media outlet or, for example, a government agency.

This year the prize is given by the Easy Language Advisory Board.

You too can nominate someone for the prize. The last day to make a nomination is 25 August. You can make a nomination on the website of the Finnish Centre for Easy Language at selkokeskus.fi.
