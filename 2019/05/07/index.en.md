# Tuesday 7.5.2019 (radio)

Government negotiations begin. There are problems with home care for the elderly. Start-up companies pay lower wages in Finland than in other Nordic countries. The popular Norppalive has started again.

## Government negotiations

![Government explorer, SDP chairman Antti Rinne, holds a press conference about the government exploration at Parliament in Helsinki on 7 May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10771734)

Antti Rinne (left) told about the parties' discussions. Photo: Roni Rekomaa / Lehtikuva

Government negotiations begin. SDP chairman Antti Rinne will say on Wednesday which parties will start negotiations on the government.

Rinne has met the leaders of the other parties. The party leaders' talks ended on Tuesday. Now Rinne is assessing which parties are best able to cooperate.

The SDP is leading the government negotiations, because it won the elections in April. The SDP is now the largest party in the Finnish Parliament.

## Home care for the elderly

![Practical nurse at an elderly person's home](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10734232)

Open image viewer

Practical nurse with an elderly person. Photo: Isto Janhunen / Yle

There are problems with home care for the elderly, because there are too few carers.

Sometimes a carer does not come to see an elderly person at the right time, and the elderly person may be left without medicine.

Sometimes a security guard or a taxi driver comes to see an elderly person. This can happen, for example, when an elderly person presses the alarm button of the personal alarm.

Security guards have even changed elderly people's nappies.

Yle's MOT programme investigated problems with home care for the elderly. You can watch the programme on Yle Areena.

## Start-up companies' wages

![Elina Arponen, CEO, Quicksave](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5056,h_2844,x_0,y_134/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-5607205ccfe2d721b55/1557127961)

Open image viewer

Quicksave is a start-up that develops games. Photo: Markku Pitkänen / Yle

Start-up companies pay lower wages in Finland than in other Nordic countries. A start-up means a young company with some new idea.

A new survey shows that the highest wages are in Danish start-up companies. Wages in Norway and Sweden are also better than in Finland.

One explanation is that the working week of start-up companies is shorter in Finland than in other Nordic countries. In a Finnish start-up, work is done 42 hours a week. In Norwegian start-up companies, work is done as much as 49 hours a week.

## Norppalive

![Saimaa ringed seals lounge on the islets during moulting time in May.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10219342)

Open image viewer

A Saimaa ringed seal rests on a rock. Photo: Petri Vironen / Yle

The popular Norppalive has started again. Norppalive is an online broadcast that people can follow on the internet.

The Norppalive camera films a shore rock on the shore of Lake Saimaa. The seals often come to rest on the rock.

The first Norppalive was in spring 2016. More than 6 million viewers have followed Norppalive so far.

The star of Norppalive is Pullervo. It was the first performer on Norppalive, and since then the camera has filmed it every spring.

The Saimaa ringed seal is a rare seal species. They live only in Lake Saimaa in Finland.

There are about 400 Saimaa ringed seals. Last winter a lot of Saimaa ringed seal pups were born, more than 80.
