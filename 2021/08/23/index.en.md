# Monday 23.8.2021 (radio)

Finland has evacuated more than 140 people from Afghanistan. Every third stock exchange company director has a criminal conviction. Economic shocks may come if people do not prepare for climate change. More people have been vaccinated against corona in Eastern Finland than in Western Finland.

## Evacuations from Afghanistan

![People are waiting to board an aeroplane at Kabul airport.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4720,h_2655,x_119,y_145/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-844564611fae05078d5/1638877240)

Finland has evacuated more than 140 people from Afghanistan. Over the weekend Finland evacuated over 60 people from the capital, Kabul.

Among them are people who have worked for Finland. Some of them have worked for the EU, for example.

Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto said that Finland has now got about half of those being evacuated out of Afghanistan. Among them are many women and children.

Evacuations from Kabul are difficult, and time is running out. Civilian aircraft do not fly from Kabul.

The extremist movement Taliban now controls the whole of Afghanistan. The Taliban took the capital, Kabul, the weekend before last.

## Criminal backgrounds of stock exchange executives

![Many domestic listed companies' boards still have no women members.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5357562)

Open image viewer

Photo: YLE/ Pekka Sipilä

Every third stock exchange company director has a criminal conviction.

This is what a broad study by the University of Oulu says. It studied Finnish and Swedish data.

Board members of stock exchange companies, and, for example, auditors, also have payment default entries and criminal convictions.

These include, for example, repeated drunk driving, leaving the scene of an accident, violent crimes and even prison sentences.

The most unusual case was a Swedish company. Its managing director and all of its board members had a criminal conviction in their background,

The study says that people do not always dare to ask about or check the backgrounds of executives. In recent years the situation has improved. Attitudes in working life have changed.

## Climate change and the economy

![Fortum's Suomenoja district heating power plant.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_8192,h_4608,x_0,y_422/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-763500600594159ee38/1633084989)

Climate change may cause a big shock to the economy in the coming years. This is how economists assess it in an interview with Yle.

Experts say that climate change changes, for example, the obtaining of raw materials, taxation, prices and the financing of states.

Economics is now looking for ways to measure and anticipate the effects of climate change. Economic shocks may come if people do not prepare for climate change.

More people have been vaccinated in Eastern Finland than in Western Finland.

The biggest differences between the regions are about 10 percentage points.

At the moment the most people have been vaccinated in East Savo, and proportionally the fewest in South Ostrobothnia. The national average has been exceeded in all regions of Eastern Finland.

THL's leading expert Mia Kontio says that the explanation lies in the population structure. There are more elderly people in Eastern Finland than in Western Finland.

Attitudes to vaccines do not show in the statistics, although they have divided Finland before.
