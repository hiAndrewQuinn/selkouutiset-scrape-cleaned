# Wednesday 22.7.2020 (radio)

Prime Minister Sanna Marin is pleased with the results of the EU meeting. Minister Matti Vanhanen assesses that Finland succeeded well in the EU negotiations. The corona situation in Finland is calm. The weather is rainy.

TV Clear News has a summer break and it will continue on 17.8.
Radio Clear News is made every day, also in the summer.

## The Prime Minister's comments on the EU meeting

![Sanna Marin](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11460622)

Open image viewer

Prime Minister Sanna Marin met journalists on Wednesday. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Prime Minister Sanna Marin has commented on the results of the EU meeting. At the meeting, EU leaders agreed on an aid package that will help the economies of EU countries after the coronavirus crisis.

In Prime Minister Marin's opinion, it is also important for Finland that the European economy gets going again.

Marin said that Finland succeeded well at the meeting. At the meeting it was decided to reduce the support in the aid package and to increase the loans, which the EU countries must pay back. Finland was one of the countries that feared that the support would become too expensive.

750 billion euros will be shared out to EU countries from the aid package. Half of the sum will be shared out as support and half will be shared out as loans.

## Other comments on the EU meeting

![Minister of Finance Matti Vanhanen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5228,h_2941,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6980835f085974aed1f/1594382990)

Open image viewer

Matti Vanhanen is Finland's Minister of Finance, that is, the money minister. Photo: Berislav Jurišić / Yle

Finland's money minister Matti Vanhanen assesses that Finland succeeded well in the EU negotiations.

At the meeting, EU leaders also agreed on the budget of the European Union, that is, how the EU will spend money in the coming years.

Vanhanen is pleased, for example, that in the negotiations Finland got more support for the countryside of Northern Finland and Eastern Finland.

The opposition parties have criticised the government because of the EU negotiations. They say that Finland's payments to the EU are too big.

There are 27 countries in the European Union. Finland is one of the countries whose EU payments are bigger than the EU support they get.

## The corona situation in Finland and the world

![A woman going to a corona test station in Florida.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11456595)

Open image viewer

A woman is going to a corona test in the USA. Photo: Joe Raedle / AFP

The corona situation in Finland is still calm. In Finland, 11 new corona infections were reported on Wednesday. There are no new corona deaths. So far, 328 people have died in Finland because of corona.

In the world, the corona situation is worse. Almost 15 million people in the whole world have already got a corona infection. More than 600,000 people have died because of corona.

The most corona infections and corona deaths are in the USA. On Tuesday, President Donald Trump said that the corona situation in the USA will probably get even worse.
