## Over 100,000 children live in poverty in Finland

![A pot of porridge on the stove, and porridge being put on a plate](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7703475)

Open image viewer

The poor can often afford to eat only porridge. Photo: Jarkko Heikkinen / Yle

Child poverty is increasing. A new study says that 10 percent of families live in poverty, which means they have too little money to live on. In total, over 100,000 children live in poverty in Finland.

Poverty has increased because parents are without work, less help is available from the authorities and fees have gone up.

The researchers consider poverty among families with children a big problem, because child poverty still affects a person's life later as an adult.

## Sing and learn Finnish

![Nälkämaan laulu was in the 1975 school songbook Tie musiikkiin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8932808)

Open image viewer

Songs teach Finnish well. Photo: Hanne Leiwo/Yle

You learn Finnish by singing. A new study says that Finnish words and other important things stay in the mind better with the help of songs. In the researchers' opinion, music helps memory. People who cannot read also learn the language with the help of songs. Singing helps so much in learning a language that it should be used more in teaching. The study says that students and teachers think singing is a good way to study Finnish.

## Schools lack digital devices

![Pupils of Marttila school learning coding in September 2016.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9194480)

Open image viewer

Children are learning coding. Photo: Pasi Takkunen / Yle

There are too few digital devices, that is, smartphones and computers, in schools. Teaching was reformed in Finland in the autumn, and schools must now teach children more than before about using new smart devices, computer software and digital services.

In a Yle survey, almost 70 percent of schools say that there are too few digital devices. Schools have to use the children's own devices, if the pupils have enough devices.

## The president of the Philippines talks about his killings

![Rodrigo Duterte](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9332492)

Open image viewer

The president of the Philippines Rodrigo Duterte. Photo: Eugenio Loreto / EPA

The president of the Philippines says that he has killed drug criminals himself. President Rodrigo Duterte told business leaders that he killed criminals when he was still working as a mayor. The president wanted to show the police that the police can also kill criminals.

The president's words are causing confusion in the Philippines. Some politicians say that the president must be removed because of his wild talk.

## Finland's president got a Christmas ham and a tree

![Jenni Haukio and Sauli Niinistö receiving Christmas greetings in 2014.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9350503)

Open image viewer

The President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio. Photo: Yle

The President of the Republic Sauli Niinistö and Mrs Jenni Haukio have once again received Christmas presents. The traditional Christmas greetings were given to the presidential couple at the official residence Mäntyniemi.

Lucia maiden Ingrid Holm delighted the presidential couple with her singing. Forestry students brought them a Christmas tree.

The president also got a Christmas ham and Christmas fish. Animal and nature protection organisations also gave organic vegetables to the presidential couple.
