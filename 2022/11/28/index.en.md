## Finns' confidence in the economy

![A person pays for purchases with a card terminal.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_6000,h_3375,x_0,y_309/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7892686060491621bf5/1616924014)

Finns think it is a bad time to buy goods, a car or a home, because the economy is uncertain. Photo: Henrietta Hassinen / Yle

Finns' confidence in the economy has worsened a lot. Finns have record low confidence in their own finances. This is what a new survey says. Expectations for Finland's economy are also weak.

Finns' expectations of the economy began to fall when Russia attacked Ukraine in March. Because of the war, prices are rising and there is a lot of uncertainty in the economy.

Many Finns feel that now is a bad time to buy goods, a car or a home. The confidence of companies in the economy has also fallen in many sectors.

## Coronavirus protests in China

![Demonstrators holding blank white sheets of paper during a protest in Beijing against China's Covid policy.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5315,h_2989,x_0,y_246/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10401756384535bed2f7/1669616548)

Protest in Shanghai. Photo: EPA-EFE

In China the police have increased security measures because of the protests. In recent days there have been protests in China, because Chinese people are fed up with long coronavirus restrictions. China has had strict coronavirus restrictions and movement restrictions for 3 years already.

In recent days there have been protests in various parts of China, for example in Shanghai, Beijing and Wuhan.

The police have arrested protesters. The British public broadcaster BBC says that Chinese police also arrested a BBC journalist who was following the protests in Shanghai.

Finland's Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto visited the Ukrainian capital Kyiv on Monday. Foreign ministers from the Nordic and Baltic countries took part in the visit.

The foreign ministers discussed helping Ukraine in Kyiv. Foreign Minister Pekka Haavisto says that Finland has good capacity to take part in the rebuilding of Ukraine.

Ukraine is afraid that Russia is preparing new missile strikes on Ukraine. The Ukrainian army says that, for example, a Russian warship that can fire missiles has arrived in the Black Sea

## Ukrainian refugees in Finland

![Towels ready for refugees arriving at emergency accommodation.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_4031,h_2268,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-10202456347baa3a59f3/1665645773)

Open image viewer

Finland can accommodate at least 10,000 Ukrainian refugees. Photo: Jarmo Nuotio / Yle

Finland can accommodate more than 10,000 Ukrainian refugees in the winter. This is what the Finnish Immigration Service Migri says. Finland can also arrange more places for Ukrainians if more places are needed.

Migri has put out to tender many places in Finland where accommodation can be arranged for Ukrainian refugees.

More than 44,000 Ukrainians have applied for temporary protection or asylum in Finland this year. About 500 people have arrived per week in the autumn. The Ministry of the Interior estimates that next year perhaps 30,000 to 40,000 people will come to Finland from Ukraine.
