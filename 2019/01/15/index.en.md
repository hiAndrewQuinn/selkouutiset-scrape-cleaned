# Tuesday 15.1.2019 (radio)

Nokia is starting co-operation negotiations in Finland. Online shopping from China keeps growing all the time. The maternity package is being criticised. Less than 70 percent of Finns now belong to the Evangelical Lutheran Church. Ice in Antarctica is melting faster than ever.

## Nokia is cutting jobs

![Nokia logo](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10596916)

Open image viewer

Nokia has a cost-cutting programme that applies to all units around the world. Photo: Jussi Nukari / Lehtikuva

Nokia is starting co-operation negotiations in Finland, that is, negotiations with employees. The aim of the negotiations is to cut 350 jobs this year.

The plan applies to all of Nokia's locations in Finland, but not to the Oulu factory. Nokia now has about 6,000 employees in Finland in total.

Nokia says that the cost-saving programme is also going ahead in other countries.

## Online shopping from China keeps growing

![General view](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5472,h_3078,x_0,y_376/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-4708705aa68e326ede7/1520865379)

Open image viewer

A lot of small goods are ordered from Chinese online shops. Photo: Mikko Koski/Yle.fi

Finns are ordering more and more goods from Chinese online shops. The growth has been many tens of percent in recent years.

Domestic online shops say that the competitive situation is unfair. No value added tax is charged on packages from Chinese online shops if the packages are worth less than 22 euros.

Cheap goods from China will remain outside value added tax for almost 2 more years.

## The origin of maternity package products is criticised

![Contents of the 2018 maternity package.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10596463)

Open image viewer

Contents of the 2018 maternity package. Photo: Ari Lahti / AOP

The civic organisation Finnwatch criticises Kela for the way it tenders the products of the maternity package. The organisation says that many of the products in the package are made in poor conditions.

A large part of the maternity package products are made in China. Products are also made in Pakistan, India and Bangladesh, for example.

Finnwatch says that Kela's tendering emphasises a low price and not responsibility.

Kela says that it plans to improve the social responsibility of the maternity package purchases.

## Less than 70 percent of Finns belong to the church

![The slogan "What good do you believe in?" on an advertising banner for the church elections in front of the Helsinki Cathedral at Senate Square on 28 October 2014.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7610347)

Open image viewer

Helsinki Cathedral during the church elections. Photo: Teemu Salonen / Lehtikuva

For the first time, less than 70 percent of Finns now belong to the Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland.

By the end of last year, a little over 3.8 million people belonged to the church. That is 69.7 percent of the whole population.

The figure is based on preliminary information from the Church Research Institute and Statistics Finland. The membership of the Evangelical Lutheran Church has fallen quite steadily over the last 10 years.

## Antarctica is melting dangerously fast

![Penguins in Antarctica.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10022176)

Open image viewer

Penguins in Antarctica. Photo: Dean Lewins / EPA

In Antarctica and its surroundings, the ice is melting faster than ever before.

Researchers say that the ice in Antarctica melted 6 times faster the year before last than 40 years ago. The current melting rate will raise the sea level by many metres over the coming centuries.

Earlier studies already say that the sea level could rise by 1.8 metres by 2100. It could put many coastal cities under water. Millions of people live in them.

If all the ice in Antarctica melted, the sea level would rise by 57 metres.
