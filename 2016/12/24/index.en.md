# Friday 23.12.2016 (radio)

Police shot the Berlin attacker in Italy. Disabled people and elderly people may get a personal care budget. More and more drugs are being sold online. Many Finns travel today to spend Christmas, for example with relatives or at a summer cottage. There are more security measures than usual when the Christmas peace is declared on Saturday in Turku.

## Police shot the Berlin attacker in Italy

![Crime investigators collect evidence at the shooting site.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9371528)

Open image viewer

Police collect evidence in Milan. Photo: Daniele Bennati / EPA

The perpetrator of the Berlin terror attack, Anis Amri, has died. Police shot him in Milan, Italy.

Amri came to Milan by train, and at the station police asked him for an identity document. Amri pulled out a gun, and the police shot him. One police officer was wounded.

The Tunisian Anis Amri deliberately drove a lorry into a Christmas market in Berlin, Germany, on Monday. 12 people died and dozens were injured.

## Personal care budget

![Raisa Tickle with a guide dog in the corridor of the Finnish Association of the Blind.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9371918)

Open image viewer

Raisa Tickle and her guide dog. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

When the health and social services reform, that is, the sote reform, comes into force, elderly people and disabled people may get a personal care budget.

This means that an elderly or disabled person can use a certain sum of money on services. For example, a blind person can buy car rides or home help from whoever they want.

The amounts of the care budget are decided, for example, by a social worker.

## Drug trade is easy online

![Tor network](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9364689)

Open image viewer

On the Tor network you can act anonymously. Photo: Eetu Pietarinen / Yle

More and more drugs are being sold online.

Drugs are easy to buy over the internet. They are sold on the Tor network, where people can act anonymously. For example, on a website called Silk Road, almost 100 per cent more drugs are on offer now than a year ago. New sellers have come to the site.

The police know about online drug trades and monitor them. Sometimes the police pose as a drug buyer and catch the seller when he or she comes to deliver the drug.

## Super train and lots of other traffic

![Car exhaust in freezing weather](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9209579)

Open image viewer

Many families travel to grandma's for Christmas. Photo: Kaisa Siren / AOP

There is a lot of traffic on the roads today, Friday, as many people leave the Helsinki area to spend Christmas elsewhere in Finland. The most cars have been on the move between 3 pm and 6 pm. The weather is good across the country, but in some places the road may be icy and slippery.

On the railways a record-long train runs from Helsinki to Rovaniemi. It is more than 400 metres long and has 1,500 passengers on board. The train will arrive in Rovaniemi before midnight.

## Christmas peace from Turku on Christmas Eve

![The Christmas peace was declared again from the balcony of the Brinkkala house, on Turku's Old Market Square.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6131338)

Open image viewer

The Christmas peace is declared on the Old Market Square in Turku. Photo: YLE / Jouni Koutonen

The Christmas peace is declared in the traditional way in Turku tomorrow, on Christmas Eve, at 12 o'clock.

The police say that there are more security measures than usual on the Old Market Square in Turku. The reason for them is, among other things, Monday's attack in Berlin. However, the police say that there is no clear threat in Turku, and people do not need to worry.
