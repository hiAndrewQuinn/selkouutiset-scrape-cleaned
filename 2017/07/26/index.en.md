# Wednesday 26.7.2017 (radio)

Olkiluoto's new nuclear reactor may start producing electricity at the end of 2018. The Russian president is visiting Finland on Thursday. In Sweden, many women freeze their own eggs so that they can become pregnant when they themselves want to. Polar bears at the Ranua zoo got snow in the middle of summer.

## The Olkiluoto nuclear reactor

![Olkiluoto 3](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9674468)

Open image viewer

Olkiluoto's new reactor may produce electricity next year. Photo: Joona Haarala / Yle

Olkiluoto's new nuclear reactor will probably start working next year. The reactor may produce electricity at the end of 2018.

The reactor is badly late. It was supposed to be ready already in 2009.

The new reactor is the third reactor at the Olkiluoto nuclear power plant site. There are already 2 old reactors in the area.

Environmental organisations have been worried about the safety of the new nuclear reactor. The authorities say that the reactor is safe, even though there have been problems with its construction work.

Nuclear power is produced in 2 places in Finland. One is Loviisa in Southern Finland, and the other is Olkiluoto in Western Finland.

## Putin's visit

![Sauli Niinistö and Vladimir Putin.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9739719)

Open image viewer

The presidents Sauli Niinistö and Vladimir Putin will meet on Thursday. Photo: EPA / Alexei Druzhinin / Sputnik / Kremlin Pool

The Russian president Vladimir Putin is visiting Finland on Thursday. He will meet President Sauli Niinistö in Savonlinna. The presidents will hold talks at the Hotel Punkaharju.

One reason for Putin's visit is that independent Finland turns 100 years old this year. Putin wants to congratulate Finland, which has a shared history with Russia.

Finland became an independent country in 1917. Before that, Finland had been part of Russia for about 100 years.

Russia took Finland from Sweden in 1809. Before that, Finland had been part of Sweden for several hundred years.

## Freezing eggs

![Pipette and test tubes](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9716799)

Open image viewer

In the laboratory, people can also be helped to have children. Photo: AOP

Many women wonder when they are too old to have children. Even if a woman is healthy, at about 40 years old it is already hard for her to become pregnant.

In Finland's neighbouring country, Sweden, there are already many women whose eggs have been frozen. This is done to make sure that a woman can become pregnant when she herself wants to.

Freezing is done at a clinic, where eggs are stored at minus 200 degrees. Freezing costs about 4,000 euros, but the woman's employer can pay for the treatment.

In Sweden there is now discussion about whether a young woman will feel that she must postpone pregnancy if her employer pays for the freezing treatment.

## Snow for the polar bears

![Polar bear plays in the snow at Ranua zoo.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9741967)

Open image viewer

Happy polar bear in Ranua. Photo: Ranuan Seudun Matkailu Oy

One more news item from the Ranua zoo. The zoo's polar bears have got snow in the middle of summer.

The snow is a gift from Ruka ski resort. 5 lorry loads of snow were taken to Ranua from the ski resort's store.

The inventor of the snow idea was a child who visited the zoos.
