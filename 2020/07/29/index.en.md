# Wednesday 29.7.2020 (radio)

Finns are ready for new corona restrictions. There is summer flu going around in Finland. The Saarioinen factory was damaged in a fire. The price of petrol will rise on Saturday. The pilgrimage to Mecca has begun.

TV Clear News has a summer break. It will continue on 17.8.
Radio Clear News is made every day, also in the summer.

## Finns are ready for new corona restrictions

![A bottle of hand sanitiser and a reminder about safe distances in the entrance hall of the Sohvi restaurant in Jyväskylä.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3840,h_2160,x_0,y_192/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6880165ee0b363ef808/1591785446)

Finns are not afraid of new corona restrictions.

Yle has asked Finns how they feel if restrictions have to be brought back in Finland in the autumn.

Finns say that they can cope with new restrictions too, if they come. Pensioners cope best with new restrictions, and young people, 20-year-olds, the least.

## Summer flu going around

![A young person sneezes into a disposable tissue.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5568,h_3132,x_0,y_287/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6548535e7238b70fe75/1584590708)

Open image viewer

Summer flu spreads when people have started to move around more again. Photo: Tiina Jutila / Yle

There is summer flu going around in Finland. The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that Finns have been diagnosed with a cold and sore throat, which are caused by a rhinovirus.

Rhinoviruses often spread in late summer and early autumn.

The Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare says that if you get flu symptoms, it is a good idea to call the health centre. Then it may be a good idea to go for a corona test.

## Fire at the Saarioinen factory

![A fire broke out at the Saarioinen factory.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3272,h_1841,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7034595f2159d879faf/1596021268)

Open image viewer

The fire broke out in the morning at the Saarioinen factory in Valkeakoski. It took many hours to put out the fire. Photo: Marko Melto / Yle

A fire has damaged the factory of the food company Saarioinen in Valkeakoski. A fire broke out at the factory on Wednesday morning.

Production at the factory has stopped, because the fire damaged the factory. The Saarioinen factory in Valkeakoski makes ready meals, for example pizzas, meat pies and Karelian pies.

It is not yet known when the factory will start working again.

## Fuel prices rise on Saturday

![A price sign at a petrol station.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11469835)

Open image viewer

Petrol prices on the display of a petrol station. On Saturday the prices will rise.

The prices of petrol and diesel will rise on Saturday. The prices of petrol and diesel will rise at the start of August, because the fuel tax rises then in Finland.

Petrol stations are busy now at the end of the week, because many Finns are going to fill up their tanks before Saturday.

Petrol stations say that there is plenty of petrol for everyone, even though a lot is being filled up at the end of the week.

## The Muslim pilgrimage has begun

![Hygiene workers clean the Masjid al-Haram mosque while Muslim pilgrims pray in front of the holy building of the Kaaba.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5120,h_2880,x_0,y_264/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-7032095f20a5dede952/1595975394)

Workers are cleaning the Masjid al-Haram mosque. Muslim pilgrims pray in front of the holy building of the Kaaba. Photo: AFP / Lehtikuva

The Muslim pilgrimage has begun in Mecca, Saudi Arabia.

Because of the corona situation, only 10,000 people may take part in the pilgrimage. Usually about 2.5 million Muslims from different parts of the world take part in the pilgrimage in Saudi Arabia.

Because of the corona situation, pilgrims, for example, may not touch the stone of the Kaaba.
