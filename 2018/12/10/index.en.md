# Monday 10.12.2018 (radio)

The Sitoumus 2050 website gives advice on reducing your carbon footprint. Supo kept its assessment of the terrorist threat unchanged. Young people may be able to drive light cars as early as next year. Rock musician Ismo Alanko will become an honorary doctor. Water damage at Finlandia Hall.

## Smaller carbon footprint

![Front page of the Sitoumus2050 service.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10546723)

Open image viewer

The Government Office published the Sitoumus2050 service today, Monday. Photo: Sitoumus2050, Yle News Graphics

A website focused on fighting climate change has been opened for Finns. In the service you can make your own plan for halving emissions, that is, your carbon footprint.

The service calculates the user's carbon footprint, and after that it suggests different ways to reduce it. For example, eating domestic vegetables, berries and fish is an easy way to reduce emissions.

The service is called Sitoumus2050. The Government Office says that there is no similar service anywhere else.

## Terrorist threat unchanged

![Antti Pelttari](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10546897)

Open image viewer

Head of the Security Police Antti Pelttari. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

Supo, the Finnish Security Intelligence Service, has assessed the threat of terrorism in Finland.

Supo says that the threat of terrorism in Finland is at level 2, that is, at the same level as before. Supo uses a scale with 4 levels.

Supo says that terrorist networks are trying to find more supporters in Finland too.

Supo works against terrorism. To fight terrorism, Supo, for example, monitors about 370 people.

## Young people behind the wheel of light cars

![A young boy drives a car.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10283945)

Open image viewer

Matias Malin got to test a light car in Pori last summer. Photo: Mari Kahila / Yle

Young people may be able to drive light cars as early as next year. The idea is that 15 to 17-year-old young people can drive light cars with a moped licence.

A light car is a passenger car whose speed is limited. A light car can drive at most 45 kilometres per hour.

If Parliament approves the law proposal on light cars, the law is meant to come into force as early as next November.

## Ismo Alanko becomes an honorary doctor

![Ismo Alanko rehearses with the Radio Symphony Orchestra at the Music Centre.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6371250)

Open image viewer

Ismo Alanko has had a long career as a musician. Photo: Yle

Rock musician Ismo Alanko will be made an honorary doctor of the University of Eastern Finland in June. An honorary doctorate is a title that does not require an academic doctoral degree.

In its justification, the University of Eastern Finland says that Alanko's music has influences from the cultural heritage of the whole world. In addition, Alanko's way of performing belongs to the shamanistic tradition, in which magic and science are one.

In June the University of Eastern Finland will award a total of 12 honorary doctorates. In addition to Alanko, the title will be given to, for example, former Governor of the Bank of Finland Erkki Liikanen and emeritus bishop Wille Riekkinen.

## Water damage at Finlandia Hall

![Fire engines in front of Finlandia Hall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-10547028)

Open image viewer

Hot water flowed into the basement of Finlandia Hall. Photo: Vesa Moilanen / Lehtikuva

There has been water damage at Finlandia Hall in Helsinki.

Hot water has flowed into the basement of Finlandia Hall. About 100,000 litres of water got in.

The water comes from a district heating pipe. The pipe broke when excavation and drilling work was being done outside Finlandia Hall.
