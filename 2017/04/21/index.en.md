# Friday 21.4.2017 (radio)

The rush of applications for basic social assistance has eased. There are more errors than before in asylum decisions. Houses in Vantaa are heated with waste. You may soon be able to fly directly from China to Lapland. Tampere's Vapriikki museum has objects from the Forbidden City in Beijing.

## The rush at Kela is over

![The Kela logo on a wall.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9327503)

Open image viewer

Basic social assistance is applied for from Kela and from the municipalities. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The rush of applications for basic social assistance has eased. Kela can now process the applications in seven working days, as the law requires.

Kela had a big rush at the beginning of the year, when handing out basic social assistance partly moved from the municipalities to Kela. People had to wait for the money too long.

## Errors in asylum decisions

![The building of the Supreme Administrative Court on Fabianinkatu in Helsinki.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6639919)

Open image viewer

You can appeal against asylum decisions to the administrative court. Photo: Yle

The Finnish Immigration Service makes more errors than before when it processes asylum applications.

This year the Immigration Service has to process again 4 percent of negative decisions, because there are errors in the decisions. The year before last there were errors in less than half a percent of the decisions. The errors were found in the administrative court, where people appeal against negative asylum decisions.

There are more errors, for example, because the Immigration Service is in a hurry and has a lot of work. Many new people work at the agency, and their co-workers have to teach them.

## Homes in Vantaa are warmed with waste

![A waste bunker, photographed from behind glass](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9573287)

Open image viewer

Vantaa's waste-to-energy plant. Photo: Riina Virtanen (work experience pupil) / Yle

Blocks of flats and terraced houses in Vantaa are heated with waste. The mixed waste bags of 1.5 million people are burned at Vantaa's waste-to-energy plant.

When the waste is burned, district heating is made for Vantaa's houses. Thanks to the waste-to-energy plant, the price of district heating in Vantaa has fallen 10 percent in two years.

## Soon directly from China to Lapland?

![Buses waiting for Christmas tourists at Rovaniemi airport](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9369272)

Open image viewer

Tourist buses at Rovaniemi airport at Christmas time. Photo: Vesa Vaarama / Yle

You may soon be able to fly directly from China to Lapland.

Finland and China have agreed that flights between Rovaniemi and Beijing are allowed four times a week. It is not yet known whether some airline wants to start the flights. Nowadays only Finnair flies between China and Finland, from Helsinki.

This winter there have been many more Chinese tourists in Lapland than before.

## The Forbidden City on show in Tampere

![A piece of jewellery from China's Forbidden City.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9572200)

Open image viewer

A piece of jewellery from the Forbidden City. Photo: Vapriikki Museum Centre

Tampere's Vapriikki museum has an exhibition about the Forbidden City in Beijing.

24 Chinese emperors lived in the Forbidden City over 500 years. The Tampere exhibition has the emperor's throne and other objects, clothes, jewellery and art. The exhibition also has everyday objects, for example dishes.

Objects from the Forbidden City are now in Northern Europe for the first time.
