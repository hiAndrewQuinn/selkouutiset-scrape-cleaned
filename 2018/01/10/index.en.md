# Wednesday 10.1.2018 (radio)

The government wants to tighten unemployment benefits again. The police are investigating a data breach at a Finnish forestry company. A measles epidemic has broken out in Sweden. The number of refugees has fallen a lot. Shops are trying out smart shopping trolleys. The Radio Symphony Orchestra is starting its own Beethoven festival.

## Unemployment benefits

![a form and a pen](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-9936530)

Open image viewer

Unemployment benefits are to be tightened. Photo: Ismo Pekkarinen / AOP

The government wants to tighten unemployment benefits. The government wants unemployed people to apply for at least one job every week. Otherwise they can lose their unemployment benefit for 60 days. The conditions of unemployment benefits were last tightened last week, when the so-called activation model came into force.

## Data breach

![The keys of a computer keyboard spell the word breach.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5448272)

Open image viewer

A data breach may have happened at a Finnish forestry company. Photo: YLE Uutisgrafiikka

The police are investigating a data breach at a Finnish forestry company. Someone may have gained access to the computers of the Metsä Group company and taken information from them.

Metsä Group published its results yesterday, just to be safe. The company wanted to publish the information early, so that a possible data thief could not misuse it.

## Measles epidemic

![Measles vaccine.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-7823802)

Open image viewer

Vaccination protects against measles. Photo: Reuters

A measles epidemic has broken out in the city of Gothenburg in Sweden. As many as 22 people have already fallen ill with measles in a month. Doctors believe that more patients will come, because measles spreads very easily.

The symptoms of measles include high fever, runny nose, cough and a rash. Measles can also cause serious complications, such as pneumonia. Of Swedes, 98 percent have either had measles or been vaccinated against it.

## Migrants

![Asylum seekers arrived on the Greek island of Lesbos on 9 March.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-8905695)

The number of migrants and refugees who have come to Europe has fallen a lot. Last year, about 170 000 refugees came to Europe. That is 50 percent fewer than the year before. Then about 360 000 migrants and refugees came.) European countries now take in fewer migrants than before.

## Smart shopping trolley

![The smart shopping trolley has a checkout terminal and a barcode reader.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-6487736)

Open image viewer

The shopping trolley gives advice to the customer in the shop. Photo: Kalle Heikkinen / Yle

Shops are trying out new kinds of smart shopping trolleys. Smart shopping trolleys have a tablet computer. The smart shopping trolley helps find the product the customer is looking for.

With the tablet on the trolley you can also search for recipes. The device then shows which ingredients are needed for the food and where they can be found in the shop. The trolley knows where in the shop it is, and can guide you in the right direction. The smart shopping trolleys were invented by a Finnish company. It also sells them abroad.

## Beethoven festival

![Portrait of Beethoven](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-5799206)

Open image viewer

Beethoven composed over 200 works. Photo: Public Domain

The Radio Symphony Orchestra is starting its first festival of its own today. The theme of the festival is the composer Ludwig van Beethoven.

Beethoven was a German composer who lived at the turn of the 1700s and 1800s. He is one of the most famous composers in the history of music. He composed more than 200 works.

The Radio Symphony Orchestra's Beethoven festival lasts 2 weeks. The festival concerts can be listened to on Yle Radio 1 and on Yle Areena.
