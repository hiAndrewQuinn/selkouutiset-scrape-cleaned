## Corona infections

![Corona vaccination](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_3000,h_1688,x_0,y_66/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-842438611a6d8e83ae0/1629969003)

The number of corona infections in Finland is slowly decreasing.

Last week just over 3,100 new infections were confirmed. That is about 750 fewer infections than the week before, say the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health and the Institute for Health and Welfare.

Now the infection is affecting under-50s who have not been vaccinated against corona.

About 65 per cent of people over 12 have now received 2 vaccine doses.

## Climate change

![Chimneys against the evening sky](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1600,h_900,x_0,y_85/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-84029361123c4aa2c67/1709205464)

Open image viewer

Chimneys of a coal power plant in China. Photo: AOP

The corona pandemic has not slowed climate change.

The World Meteorological Organization, WMO, says that the slowdown in economic growth and the corona restrictions have not reduced emissions.

WMO says that emissions are growing again, and that meeting emission targets is not even close. The goal of the Paris climate agreement is to limit global warming to 1.5 degrees.

## A new kind of visa

![Passport control point sign at Suomenlinna.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_5275,h_2967,x_0,y_272/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-6615955e8748002f944/1605791218)

Open image viewer

The government wants to make work-related immigration easier and faster. Photo: Mårten Lampén / Yle

The government proposes changes to the Immigration Act. The proposal includes a new kind of D-visa, which is intended to make work-related immigration easier and faster.

The D-visa is at first proposed only for specialist experts and growth entrepreneurs. With a D-visa you can be in Finland for a year

The bill now goes to Parliament for consideration. It is intended to enter into force next spring.

## Drivers' strike

![Between the Maistraatintori and Kyllikinportti tram stops there is almost a line of sight. ](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,dpr_1/f_auto/13-3-11483636)

Open image viewer

Tram stop in Helsinki's Länsi-Pasila.  Photo: Paula Tiainen / Yle

In the capital region trams and the metro are still out of service tomorrow.

The trams and the metro will stand still for 2 days, Thursday and Friday, because of the drivers' protest. The drivers are protesting against the city's plans to set up a company for metro and tram traffic. They fear that the working conditions in the new company will be worse.

The drivers return to work on Saturday morning.

## Space tourists

![Civilian crew on board a SpaceX rocket before the launch of the flight.](https://img.img-cdn.yle.fi/crop_extract,w_1920,h_1080,x_0,y_0/crop_fill,w_767,h_431,ar_16:9,dpr_1/f_auto/39-85426361428f3245eef/1631752380)

Open image viewer

The first space tourists set off on Thursday morning. Photo: AFP / SpaceX

History has been made in space tourism. In Florida, USA, a rocket carrying only civilians was launched into space in the morning.

The Space X company's craft has 4 passengers, but no professional pilots at all.

The spacecraft orbits the Earth at an altitude of about 600 kilometres. The flight lasts 3 days. After that the craft lands in the Atlantic Ocean.
